# IDENTIFICATION OF ANTI-CYCROBIAN ACTIVITY OF THE EXTRACT OF THE LEVELS OF THE JUSTICIA CF. COLORIFYERA IN CULTIVES OF STAPHYLOCOCCUS SPP. AND PSEUDOMONAS SPP.

It has been proven that plants belonging to the genus Justice have an antibiotic phytotherapeutic effect, so the interest arises to evaluate if plants of the same generated that exist in Arauca have the same behavior. Although they are frequently used, there is little information that validates their antimicrobial potential. This study evaluated the antimicrobial effect of an etanolic extract of Justice leaves cf. colorifera, traditionally used in medicine in Orinoquía and in some parts of Colombia, by using the cultivation of bacteria and the application of the technique for antibiogram Kirbi. With the results it is expected to generate a mathematical model of the observed effect. The study methodology involves the process of obtaining the extract, bacterial culture and susceptibility tests of the extract dissolved in dimethylsulfoxide. Staphylococcus and Pseudomonas were used provided by the laboratory of reproductive biotechnology and different concentrations of the extract were evaluated. 6,000,600,6 ppm.
