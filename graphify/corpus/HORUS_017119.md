# BIODIRIGID OXANDRA LONGIPETALA (ANNONACAE) FRANKING USING ANTI-FUNG TESTS IN VITRO

It is estimated that phytopathogenic fungi cause about 70% of the diseases affecting crops.1 Annually, these organisms are responsible for up to half of the losses of food production worldwide.2 In search of sustainable use of native species of the Colombian Andes, the antifungal capacity of 107 plants, collected in 11 monitoring plots located in different elevational gradients, was evaluated.The etanolic extract of Oxandra longipetallase was highlighted by its inhibitory activity at 500 ppm on Fusarium oxysporum (57.1%) and Botrytis cinerea(49.1%), fungi of high agro-industrial interest3. Following a biodirected fractionation of the extract of O.longipetala, 16 fractions (F1F16) of increasing polarity were obtained. F2 and F3 fractions have been inhibited by F. oxysporum (500 ppm), with a percentage of 63.14% and 67.95%, respectively.
