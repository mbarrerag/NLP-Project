# CHAPTER 5. FOOD AND HEALTH NUTRITION AS ALTERNATIVE FOR SUSTAINABILITY OF HUMAN LIFE IN PLANET

This chapter reflects on the need to take care of human health through a healthy diet that does not impact the health of the planet and takes a look at the environmental problems generated by traditional food practices and the agro-industrial food system and proposes to consume healthy diets from sustainable agricultural systems, reflecting on the diet and planetary health, highlighting the need to provide food education in educational environments to improve human health and health on the planet
