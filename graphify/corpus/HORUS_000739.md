# Emerging Trends in Retail Analytics: A Bibliometric Analysis of the Last Decade

Retail analytics has become a transformative force that leverages data-based knowledge to optimize operations, customize customer experiences, forecast demand and improve supply chain efficiency. This study provides a comprehensive bibliometric analysis of 563 documents indexed in Scopus, which profile the evolution of retail analytics over the past ten years. Key findings include 131 emerging issues grouped into 13 central trends. The analysis highlights the growing application of artificial intelligence, machine learning and big data to drive decision-making, improve profitability and improve competitiveness in the retail industry. This paper addresses critical questions of "what", "where", "when" and "who" in retail trade analytics research, identifying areas of innovation and future growth, especially in predictive analysis, customer knowledge and optimization of business operations
