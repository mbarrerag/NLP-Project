# INDIGENOUS SCHOOL FOOD, NASA COMMUNITY EXPERIENCE OF THE RESGUARD OF TOEZ, CAUCA, COLOMBIA

This chapter presents the experience of school feeding in the Nasa indigenous community, in the indigenous reserve of Toez, in Caloto, Cauca, Colombia, as a result of the characterization of the Nasa people's school feeding, following the collection of information at the Tóez Ethno-Educational Educational Institution (IEET). It describes the aspects that converge around school feeding, both in the institutional sphere, and in the area of alternative strategies proposed by the Nasa indigenous community and that favor the path towards food autonomy and sovereignty and food and nutrition security
