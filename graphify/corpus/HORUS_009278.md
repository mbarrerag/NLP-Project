# A MODEL OF MADUREMENT FOR STRATEGIC PLANNING OF PRODUCTION SYSTEMS.

To formulate successful strategies is a challenging issue and highly relevant for companies. In this book the reader will find a theoretical and conceptual journey around business strategy as a whole, with a special emphasis on the strategy aimed at production/operation systems, called manufacturing strategy. This work is the result of an investigation. Therefore, the approaches are solidly supported in scientific literature and evidence is presented from real companies. In particular, a maturity model is provided that has been tested following the scientific method to formulate the manufacturing strategy, specifically from the paradigm of research-action, with empirical evidence that allows to move from theory to practice. The knowledge presented here is relevant both to professionals in the companies and to researchers interested in the field of study, so an effort was made to guide the content that could satisfy both spheres, although aware that language requirements on both sides are often very different
