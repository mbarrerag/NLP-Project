# Modeling of large deposits of the Myocenes of epithermal type and gold porphyry of the Metalogenic Belt of Cauca of Colombia using simulations Monte Carlo

The Calogenic Belt of Cauca (CMC) is an interandin area located along the fault zone Cauca-Romeral of Colombia. The CMC is composed of a group of twelve magmatic-hydrothermal deposits of Au-Ag-Cu of the Late Miocene, located between the Western Cordillera and Central of Colombia. Besides being a region widely developed in exploration and mineral exploitation, with known Au endowments of more than 1700 tons, the CMC represents an exceptional metalogenetic laboratory for modeling mineralizations of the Andean type from a calco-alkaline source with high reasons of Sr/Y. Although the efficiency processes in magmatic-hydrothermal deposits have been extensively studied with a geochemical approach, such as the transport of ions and halogens, the higher volume of oxygen and the sulfur content, among others, applying the quantification and modelling of these efficiency processes have been discussed at present.
