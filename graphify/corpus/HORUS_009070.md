# VALUATION OF GALVANIZE RESIDUE BY IMMERIATION IN HEAT FOR THE SYNTHESIS OF OXIDS OF FE-ZN ACTIVE IN PHOTOCATICAL DEGRADATION OF COLORANTS

The process of the hot immersion galvanizing industry generates a large amount of toxic effluents due to its high metallic load, especially Fe and Zn [1], this because the end of the useful life of a stripping bath is when the saturation point of Fe (~150 g/L) or Zn (160-200 g/L) is exceeded [2]. This way an effluent commonly composed by HCl around 8-10%, salts such as FeCl2 and ZnCl2, Fe+2 ions, Zn+2 and Fe+3 traces are obtained. In addition, there are also different metals formed in trace levels such as Cr, Co, Ni, Al, etc. [3]. The final disposition of these effluents has become a problem of environmental and economic type of work. Therefore, the various alternatives of the gases have been investigated to recover these residues.
