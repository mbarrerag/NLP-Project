# Pediatric abdominal physical examination

Abdominal pathology represents an important diagnostic challenge in all age groups, especially in paediatric patients. The limited availability of new knowledge in the literature, a phenomenon supported by a continuous conceptual extrapolation of adults to paediatric patients, and by the lack of knowledge of their pathological, anatomical and semiological particularities, among others, leads to erroneous assessments and inaccurate diagnoses. This article presents a review of the integral pediatric abdominal physical examination, in the context of surgical and non-surgical abdominal pathology, highlighting its differences with that of adults, the importance of an adequate approach to pediatric patients and an exhaustive anamnesis that favors the validity of the findings, as well as addressing the clinical relevance of certain signs to physical examination, the usefulness of semiological techniques in abdominal examination and finally, some considerations of abdominal masses and ostomy examination for a consequent timely diagnosis and treatment
