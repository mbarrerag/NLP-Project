# Evidence-based clinical standard: diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of meniscal lesions patients at the National University Hospital of Colombia

This book represents the Clinical Standard based on evidence: diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of the patient with meniscal lesions in the National University Hospital of Colombia. This ECBE is part of a process of standardization of health care, taking into account the best evidence, available resources and interdisciplinarity, with the purpose of generating a comprehensive approach that improves the outcomes of patients and optimizes the use of resources at the hospital level. The importance of the standardization of care of patients with this condition is recognized, due to its importance for the institution and for the comprehensive care of the patient with this condition.-- Contravest
