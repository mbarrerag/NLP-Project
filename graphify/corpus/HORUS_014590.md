# Classification technologies for imaginary speech brain signal through spatial functional data

The present work aims to classify the thought of the five vowels of the Spanish language measured through electroencephalograms in 21 electrodes around the Broca area of the brain into 23 individuals. For this purpose, the framework of functional spatial data was used, considering each EEG measurement a continuous curve in L2 and performing functional kriging, several images were constructed to apply machine learning and deep classification techniques. Finally, both classification routines achieved an average accuracy of more than 91% for each individual, it is necessary to take into account that each individual must have his own sorting mechanism
