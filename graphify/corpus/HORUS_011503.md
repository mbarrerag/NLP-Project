# DEFINITION OF NUTRITIONAL REQUIREMENTS

OBJECTIVE Comprehensive health care for the enteral food therapy of newborns is fundamental: it is a goal of singular importance in the context of timely, safe and quality care. This comprehensive care should include the provision of a nutritional medical therapy that provides the tools and supports required to care for premature newborns. Thus, this text aims to respond to challenges that arise in clinical practice and that are analyzed in the context of clinical care and the academy in order to meet the nutritional needs of premature newborns. In 10 chapters, this book elaborates on key aspects of clinical practice and responds to the academic and research needs that arise in the Neonatal Intensive Care Units. In short, this work constitutes a rapid consultation document for health professionals, teachers and students who want to complement what has been learned in the book Parental Nutritional Therapeutics. The objective of this chapter is to systematically describe the nutritional needs required by newborns to ensure their development and growth in hospital centers
