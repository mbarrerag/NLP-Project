# CREATING A DIGITAL PROJECT FOR PREVENTION OF COVID-19 FOCUSED ON LATINO POPULATION

COVID-19 pandemic has generated a negative impact at economic and social levels. Furthermore, the health system has presented an insufficient response to the pandemic, leaving populations from rural areas without essential coverage of education, prevention, detection and treatment of the disease. This negative impact increases in population who lack a health literacy level enough to understand evidence, or those who do not speak English on US territories
