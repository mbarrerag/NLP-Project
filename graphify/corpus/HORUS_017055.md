# Solubility and solution thermodynamics of acetaminophen in (ethanol + propylene glycol + water) ternary mixtures.

The solubility in acetaminophen (ACF) equilibrium was determined at five temperatures from (293.15 to 313.15) K in ternary mixtures {ethanol (EtOH) (1) + propylene glycol (PG) (2) + water (3)}. From the variation in solubility with temperature, the apparent thermodynamic analysis of dissolution was performed in all ternary mixtures. Additionally, thermodynamic quantities of mixture and solutation were calculated, as well as the specific volume apparent to saturation of ACF. The solubility of ACF expressed in molar fraction (x4) increases by increasing temperature and also increases with the increase in the proportions of EtOH and/or PG. This varies from x4 = 4.56 × 10–3 in the mixture 0.10,10-0.80 (in mass fraction) to x4 = 6.63 × 10–2 in the mixture 080,10-0,0,10 to 298,15 K.
