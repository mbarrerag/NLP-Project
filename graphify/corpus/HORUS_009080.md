# BIOTECHNOLOGIC POTENTIAL OF GULUP ENTRYTH HONGOS (PASSIFLORA EDULIS F. EDULIS; PASSIFLORACEAE) POTENTIAL BIOTECHNOLOGICAL OF PURPLE FRUIT PASSION ENDOPHYTIC FUNGI (PASSIFLORA EDULIS F. EDULIS; PASSIFLORACEAE)

The evidence shows that all plants coexist with endophytes, indicating that they play a fundamental role still by diluting.The objective of this work was to evaluate the biotechnological potential of endophytes from Pasiflora edulis f. edulis, in two aspects: as an action promoting plant growth and as controllers of the pathogen Fusarium oxysporum. An in vitro phase was used in which antifungal activity was studied in dual culture, finding that the fungi UNE075 and UNE004 managed to inhibit the radial growth of the pathogen by up to 67.63 % and 63.89 % respectively. Additionally, by action of volatile organic compounds on F. oxysporum, it was observed that the highest percentages of inhibition were given by the fungi UNE075 (33.78 %) and UNE098 by the highest effect (32.32 %).
