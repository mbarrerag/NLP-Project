# Health promotion in the working places of the Parties

After reviewing the sources referred to, it can be concluded that the work environment is a key environment to promote health because they highlight work as a fundamental dimension for the well-being of people and to promote healthy habits and highlight positive aspects such as interaction, solidarity and personal and professional development. Work contributes to the development of life projects and creativity and provides economic income that allow workers access to housing, education, health services, etc. In Latin American countries, work is indispensable for the reach of the quality of life and health of people, because it is associated with social protection systems. According to (24), the main functions of promoting health of health workers are: promoting styles, behaviours, healthy habits. Duggdill and Springett (25) state that health promotion was introduced initially in the 1960s as part of the so-called, then, occupational health programs, to improve the health conditions of workers. Duggdill and Springett (25) state that health promotion of workers is more oriented to the individual needs of the workers than the overall ones of the organization.
