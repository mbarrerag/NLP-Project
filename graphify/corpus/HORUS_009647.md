# Methodology to identify and characterize areas sown in potato in the department of Cundinamarca from the integration of optical and radar images.

Identifying areas cultivated in potatoes in Colombia would allow better programming of planting periods, decreasing the oversupply that reduces the price to producers. Optical images allow identifying cultivation areas, but the presence of clouds is a limiting factor that can be countered through the method of fusion with radar images, which complements the missing information. Based on the above, a methodology was developed to identify and characterize areas sown in potatoes in Cundinamarca, integrating Sentinel 1 and 2 images. As reference data, potato batches were georeferred from the first semester of 2022 in Villapinzón and Lenzázque. Images of Sentinel 1, Sentinel 2 and Sentinel 2 were processed plus vegetation spectral indices. The fusion was performed by analysis of main components (ACP) and a multitemporal image was constructed with the first three components of the selected images. The overall difference was applied to random forests (BA) and support vector machines (MVS).
