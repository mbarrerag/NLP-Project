# OFFLINE APPLICATION IN HEALTH EDUCATION FOR AFROCOLOMBIAN COMMUNITIES

Background and meaning: Innovation through the use of the Information and Communication Technology (ICTs) is an important tool in data management and communication, to address actions through education in the health environment1. The contents of the app were built with the participation of the community and its validation. This prototype will be incorporated into the public health plan of the municipality. Conclusions / implications: The use of technology as a tool for prevention and promotion in health, based on the active participation of the community from the identification of needs to the construction of the prototype, facilitates the appropriation of knowledge in the communities, given the adaptation that requires from the cultural and recognition of its environment
