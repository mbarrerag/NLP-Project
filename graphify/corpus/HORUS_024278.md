# ASSESSMENT OF ANTI-INFLAMATIONAL ACTIVITY OF EXTRACT ACUOSUM, BUTANOLIC FRACTION AND COMPOUNDS IDENTIFIED ON LEAFS OF LIGULARIS JUSS PASSIFLORA.

The inflammation comprises a series of physiological processes of adaptive response to an injury of a tissue by an external or own agent.Inflammation processes allow the isolation and direction of the immune response, and the regeneration of the affected tissues. Therefore, the affected tissues present a marked loss of functionality, which is the product of the multiple events that occur, either acutely or chronicly.These events include: cellular damage, vasodilatation and increase of capillary permeability, cellular adhesion, release of proinflammatory substances that allow the recruitment of cells of the immune system, the destruction of external agents by phagocytosis, removal of cellular residues and generation of a protein network to recover tissue.1 The genus Passiflora has been studied in the modulation of inflammatory processes in different pharmacological models2. This genus is widely presented as anti-minous studies in Colombia, with more than 167 species, 48 of them located in the Andean region, besides being plants with importance in agricultural and ornamental fields.3 One of the most representative species in Colombia is Passiflora lgularis, commonly known as a grantilidal activity, of the opyranoside (6) and ligularoside A (15) could be the compounds responsible for the activity detected in the crude extract and in the butanolic fraction. This contributed to the pharmacological characterisation of the leaves of P. ligularis with a view to the exploitation of this crop by-product
