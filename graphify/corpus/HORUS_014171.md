# Life Skills in First-Semester University Students of the National University of Colombia and the University of Rosario

The skills for life are constituted as determining elements for the adaptation of the individual to the environment in which he or she develops. His or her evaluation in the academic field is indispensable for the timely management of situations that can generate difficulty preventing an optimal performance and preventing the appearance of alterations in the health and well-being of the students. In this sense, the present research proposal aims to propose a program of skills for life mediated by technologies for students of first semester of the National universities of Colombia and El Rosario through a mixed approach
