# DEGRADATION OF VIOLET CHRISTAL PRESENT IN RESIDUAL WATERS USING A COMPOSITED NATURAL MINERAL OF HERRO OXIDES AS A CATEGOTER

One of the main problems associated with the rapid growth of the textile industry, is the high amount of dyes deposited in the water bodies, many of which have carcinogenic effects, cause eutrophication and disturbances in the aquatic life.One of the strategies to address this problem, is the use of natural minerals based on iron oxides to carry out the Fenton process, which have become an efficient alternative to carry out degradation processes, because its cost is very low and its abundance is relatively high in soils, mines or even as a residual product of the process of steelworks, which makes the cost/benefit ratio to be favored. In this research, with the aim of valorizing a natural mineral based on iron oxides, from Acerías de Paz del Río in the department of Boyacá, Colombia, it was carried out to reaction such material as catalyst in the process of Fenton versus a model used in degradation reactions, such as Violet Crystal (CV).
