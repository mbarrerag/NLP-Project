# Adherence of reinforcement steel coated with paint and Aloe vera gel extract in lime mortar.

In the development of projects of reinforcement and rehabilitation of historical buildings, it has been seen the need to use the lime mortar, given its chemical and mechanical compatibility with the original mortars. However, due to the requirements of resistance and ductility it is necessary to use the steel reinforcement in these mortars to ensure good structural behavior. Given the high porosity of the lime mortars and the low protection that it offers to the steel reinforcement against the corrosive agents present in the environment, recently the use of corrosion inhibitors based on plant extracts such as the Aloe vera gel is found to be resistant to the adhesion, however, it has not been studied whether the application of this type of protection affects the adhesion of the steel. According to the above, this research studies the influence of a coating based on Aloe vera gel extract mixed with a enamel type paint based on water in the behavior to the adhesion. In the mainly experimental investigation, tests were carried out of the materials and evaluation of the properties of the mortar such as the fluidity and resistance to the compression.
