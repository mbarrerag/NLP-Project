# PRIMARY ATTENTION IN BUCALYCOMMUNICATIVE HEALTH. COMMUNITY EXPERIENCES DURING PANDEMIA BY COVID-19

The pandemic by Covid-19 demanded that the processes of formation and care in oral health be attached to this new reality. Among the different strategies to dynamize these processes is virtual communication, which was placed in community activities. Thus, the interaction between students and teachers of dentistry and phonoaudiology of the National University of Colombia and four educational institutions from different rural regions of the country continued around the care of oral and communicative health of teachers and students of these institutions
