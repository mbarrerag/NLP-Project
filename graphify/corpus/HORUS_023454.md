# NEUROLOGICAL EXPRESSIONS OF CHILD BALANCE IN PRIMARY PEDIAtric ATTENTION AND IN CHILDREN WITH DISABILITY.

The main concepts of neglect (MN) are presented in a critical analysis of academic and scientific literature. This is the most frequent form of child abuse and what most harm it produces in child development is potential and often fatal, and can pass unnoticed in health services. Its fundamental basis is a deficit in the quality of the primary link. The oversight negligence can occur clinically as accidents, including brain trauma, poisoning or deaths in children. Nutritional negligence can be presented as a medro failure. The first expression of MN is the alterations in child development. The most common form of neglect in health care of MN is that occurs within neurological care in children. It is presented primarily as a lack of adherence to the therapeutic plan. The local profile of MN is different from that described in other countries of higher economic development. The intervention of MN is multilevel and includes support for families, the approach and management of trauma, accompanied by public health interventions, Triple P, Early Start, Nurse Family Partnership, among others.
