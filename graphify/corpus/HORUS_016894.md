# DIGITAL HOLOGRAPHIC MICROSCOPY EXPERIMENTS IN IMAGEJ

An ImageJ plugin that allows the realistic simulation and real-time reconstruction of digital holographic microscopy experiments is presented. Its performance is verified with a phase-only resolution test target, showing accurate simulations and reconstructions
