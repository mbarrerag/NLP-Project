# DIDACTIC STRATEGIES FOR MINIMATING THE IMPACTS OF COVID-19 IN PRIMARY BASIC EDUCATION FROM A MULTIDISCIPLINARY PERSPECTIVE

One sector of the population that was most affected by the COVID-19 was the population of school-age children, because social isolation emerged as one of the various strategies to reduce its impact. However, this isolation, although effective from an epidemiological point of view, strongly impacted the children of school age, because their learning and well-being is strongly based on this social contact. Considering the above problem, and seeking to contribute from the academy to the solution of the same, this work was developed, which seeks to strengthen the capacities of public institutions of primary basic education in the city of Medellín (Colombia), before the COVID-19, through the development of personal protection elements and didactic strategies based on Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) and Transmedia. In this sense, this work has as central focus the presentation of the main results and impacts generated by the implementation and development of a didactic strategy that allows contributing to the teaching (in primary basic education) of self-care and care of the other
