# The creation of the Governorate of Antioquia: political culture and population in the second half of the 16th century

The aim of this monograph is to study the way in which the constitution of the governorship of Antioquia was carried out during the second half of the 16th century. The creation of this entity framed within the territory known at the time as _x0093_Between the Two Rivers_x0094_ was subject to the disputes that the governors and some neighbors of the provinces of Popayán and Antioquia mainly and, to a lesser extent, also with the interference of the governor of Cartagena
