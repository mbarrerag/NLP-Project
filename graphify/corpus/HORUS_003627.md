# FRAGILITY OF THE MAJOR ADULT AND THE CHRONIC INSTRUMENTS

This chapter addresses the process of skin aging and the risks inherent in the aging of other organs and functions that result in skin lesions. To this end, the risks arising from the decreased capacity for mobilization, loss of continence, stability, cognitive system, chronic health conditions, and healing process are addressed. Likewise, care is given to the skin of the elderly and the management of skin lesions
