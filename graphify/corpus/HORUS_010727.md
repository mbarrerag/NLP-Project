# CHARACTERIZATION OF INDUSTRIAL EFLUENTS OF GOLD LIXIVATION WITH CIANURO IN MINING ENTABLES OF THE DEPARTMENT OF CALLS

In order to establish a baseline on the quality of the waste water from the process of leaching gold with cyanide in mining stalls of the department of Caldas, the characterization of its effluents was carried out monitoring the parameters set out in Resolution 0631 of 2015 of the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development (T, pH, COD, BOD5,TSS, SS, G & A, Phenols, ASMB, HTP, P-PO43-, P, N-NO3-, N-NO2-, N-NH3, N-CN¿, Cl¿, SO42-, S2-, As, Cd, Zn, Cu, Cr, Fe, Hg, Ni, Ag, Pb, Total acidity, Total Alkalinity, Calcic Durability, Total and Color). Six sampling methods were performed, in a period of six months with monthly samplings, directly in the descales of the extraction process of gold before these systems were converted to the bodies of surface water.
