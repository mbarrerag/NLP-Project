# DATA QUALITY, MACHINE LEARNING AND STATISTICS

With the popularization of data-based disciplines, the use of data for problem solving has become increasingly popular, which has brought with it a transformation of statistics and Machine Learning, making them transversal skills in any area of knowledge. However, this whole boom has also generated a lack of rigorousness in the implementation of methodologies and techniques, which has revealed a number of problems within which we place the quality of data. With this work we aim to show the quality of data as a relevant research area both in statistics, as in Machine Learning. Showing in the first instance the importance of addressing the quality of data with rigorous processes, then offering a historical context on data quality research and finally leading to a more technical knowledge about how to structure data quality processes in real projects
