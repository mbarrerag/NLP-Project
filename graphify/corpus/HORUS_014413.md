# Evaluation of the properties of air permeability and durability of concretes added with limestone and calcined clays.

In this research, the properties of permeability to air and water were evaluated, the resistance to the penetration of chlorides, electrical impedance, porosity, density, compressive strength and elasticity module of five concrete mixtures added to calcined clay and limestone in different proportions (M01) to (M05) and a reference mixture without any addition (MR).All the mixtures were designed by the Engineer Luis Felipe Salazar (Salazar Mayorga, 2023), member of the research and analysis group directed by the Engineer Juan Manuel Lizarazo Marriaga, as a basis for the conduct of a series of research on cements added to calcined and limestone. All the mixtures have the same water / cement ratio equal to 0.50 and the calcined clay used is of low gradation, with a caolinite content equal to 40%. The cement used as reference is Alion brand of high early resistances (ART) because it is a cement with a minimum of additions and very close to what is considered as a polyctland cement. The probetas were cured and tested to the University of the early and tested to the age (90 to the baseline). The porosity of the added mixtures was lower than that of the reference mixture except for the M01 mixture which presented a slightly higher value. As regards the properties related to durability, the permeability to air and water was evaluated, the resistance to diffusion and migration of chlorides and the electrical impedance. The totality of the mixtures presented low permeability to air. It was shown that the addition of calcined and calizated clays, as expected, effectively decreases the permeability to air due to a refination in the structure of pores of concrete. The optimal content of the additions corresponds to the M02 mixture with 20% of calcined clay and 10% of limestone. The permeability to water of all the mixtures was evaluated as low. It was not possible to establish a reliable quantitative content between the mixtures due to the deficiencies in the quality of the epoxy paint used M05 resistance which distorted the results by allowing the flow of water not to be unidirectional.
