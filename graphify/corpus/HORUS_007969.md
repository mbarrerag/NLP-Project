# Pedagogical liaison strategy for continuing technical to vocational training

The project aims to propose a learning environment aimed at developing an action plan as a centre of interest to create training transit opportunities for students based on the problem of breaking the learning continuity between technical students and graphic design professionals at the University of Colombia (IUDC) educational institution located in Bogotá Colombia. Taking as a theoretical reference the significant learning and motivation as a learning axis, for the application of a learning environment by scenarios that allows to demonstrate with results such as the link, interest and motivation are fundamental pillars for the educational process within the institution and thus to propagate more spaces like this and to replicate them for other careers within the same institution.
