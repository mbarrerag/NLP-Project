# PRELIMINARY CONSIDERATION OF ELECTROCHEMICAL OXIDATION OF DILUTED EFFLUENTS POLLUTED WITH CIANURO. EFFECT OF THE OPERATION CONDITIONS AND CHARACTERISTICS OF EFLUENT

In this study, a preliminary study of OE was conducted as an alternative treatment for synthetic CN solutions with concentrations between 100 and 1000 mg/L. The experiments were carried out in an electrochemical cell (0.2 L), continuously shaken, operating in discontinuous mode (30 minutes), at 20°C. Two electrodes of vertical graphite (anode) and aluminium (cathode) plates connected in monopolar arrangement to a DC power supply, operating in galvanostatic mode were used. To study the effect of the operating variables on the EO were systematically varied: pH, NaCl concentration, distance between the electrodes (DE), agitation rate (¿) and current density (J).
