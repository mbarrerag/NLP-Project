# PRELIMINARY CONSIDERATION OF ELECTROCHEMICAL OXIDATION AS ALTERNATIVE TREATMENT FOR EFFLUENTS POLLUENTED WITH CIANURO PROVIDING FROM THE GOLD LIXIVATION PROCESS

The gold mining around the world is carried out using cyanide leaching solutions as this substance, when forming a cyanoauric complex, allows the extraction of gold from the rock. As a collateral effect, the extraction process generates effluents contaminated with cyanide and other substances associated with the mining of the extraction site. In this research, the Electro Oxidation is studied as an alternative for the treatment of waste water from the cyanide-contaminated gold leaching process and the effect of the presence of anions, metals and metaloids of mineral effluents (v.g., Cl¿, SO42¿, Cu and Zn which are typical of these processes) on the conversion of cyanide, the removal of DQO and the specific energy consumption was analyzed. An electrochemical discontinuous reactor was used when with stainless steel electrodes, operating in galvanostatic mode, and synthetic solutions of cyanide to strongly alkaline conditions (pH = 13).
