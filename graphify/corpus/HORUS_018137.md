# CHAPTER 16 CLINICAL CASES

The nutritional care process (pan) is defined by the Academy of Research on the Nutritional Care Process (ncpro) as a systematic approach that aims to provide high quality nutritional care.1 The suggested bread in the clinical care of the pregnant woman consists of four distinct and interrelated steps (The Nutrition Care Process (ncp), 2019): 1. Nutritional evaluation: includes the food or nutritional history; biochemical data, medical tests and procedures; anthropometric measurements and physical findings focused on nutrition. 2. Nutritional diagnosis: determination of nutritional status that will result in an intervention. 3. Nutritional intervention: directed at the root cause or etiology of the nutritional problem. 4. Nutrition monitoring and evaluation: is the final step of the process, which allows to determine whether the patient has reached or is advancing towards the planned goals.
