# INTERGENERATIONAL MEMORY OFTHEARMED CONFLICT IN THE MUNICIPALITIES OF SAMANÁ AND MARULANDA (CALDAS-COLOMBIA)

Colombia has had several cycles of violence. During the second half of the last century, between the years 1946 and 1958, there was a bloody bipartisan violence that ended with a agreement between the two main political parties. After that, there was a transition to a violence of rural guerrillas, to which the paramilitary groups were added; an era known as the internal armed conflict. Some guerrillas demobilized in the 90's, the paramilitary groups in 2005 and the main guerrilla in 2016. Since that date there has been a reorganization of the residual and emerging armed actors, and although in some places the resumption of war activity has been seen, in others there is a forceful de-escalation of the war. Taking into consideration that several generations have lived during the armed conflict, a collective and individual memory exercise was carried out; from which the actors, the content, the direction and the intention of the intergenerational memory about the armed conflict in inhabitants of the Encimadas and Montebonito corregimientos, of the municipalities of Samaná and Marulanda were traced. The collective component was made by constructing timelines in focus groups, the individual one using biographical diaries and the intergenerational one with the analysis of the material collected and grouped by generations. The reference to the affectation was found due to the conflict of two or three generations of the same family, associated with it the rupture of intra-family ties, especially between grandparents and grandchildren. Explanatory interpretations with particular nuances were found for each generation. The contribution of this type is reiterated memory in the construction of identity and collective meaning in societies
