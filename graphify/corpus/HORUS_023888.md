# Evaluation of concrete durability with fly ash incorporating fine recycled aggregates.

In Colombia and the world, the use of the fine fraction of recycled aggregates derived from concrete (RCA) is a highly restricted practice due to the negative effect that they have on mechanical properties and durability. Although it has been concluded internationally that satisfactory resistances can be obtained using these aggregates, the effect of these on durability properties, specifically those related to corrosion due to chlorides, has not been studied extensively. In this research, concrete mixtures were made with 0%, 20%, 60% and 100% replacement of RCA aggregates and fly ash as an additional cementant in order to evaluate the physical properties, mechanical performance, chloride permeability and corrosion of the reinforcing steel with electrochemical techniques at different ages. The results indicate that despite some properties being negatively affected by the inclusion of recycled fines, the fly ash is a highly effective mitigatinger to the penetration of long-term chlorides and the use of recycled aggregates does not significantly increase the risk of corrosion of the steel reinforcement despite its high alkalinity. Based on these results, an analysis of concrete construction codes was carried out
