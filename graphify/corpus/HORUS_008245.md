# THE TERRITORIES OF THE ANTUM OF SUPIA IN THE TIMES OF THE GENERAL MARIN RAMON.

The text gives an account of the political changes and changes in the administrative nature of the former Catón de Supiía, between the birth of Ramón El Negro?Marín, in 1855 in Marmato and his death, in Honda (Tolima), on 10 December 1923. In those nearly 70 years, where Marmato, Supia and Riosucio were part of different political-administrative formations as subdivisions of the then State of Cauca until 1905 when they were incorporated into the Department of Caldas. It is realized this dynamic as the process of configuration and consolidation of its territorial urban system
