# THE QUALITY OF TEACHING AND LEARNING IN OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY PROGRAMS

Teaching-learning processes are important in the training of occupational therapists, in the development of graduates and in the evolution of the profession, therefore it is necessary to know and deepen about their quality and their understanding in some countries of Latin America. Critically analyze the quality of teaching-learning in occupational therapy programs. The epistemo-methodological proposal is related to a qualitative type with an epistemological perspective based on critical hermeneutics. It consists of a collective case study in which qualitative techniques were used, such as the critical analysis of the documentary and in-depth interviews with the educational community participants. Results and or practice implications. It is found that the quality of teaching and learning processes among these programs, is linked as a right to the occupation of educational actors, who must recognize the historical journey they have made as a profession, as well as their development upon their academic processes regarding teaching and learning. Occupation and human participation are recognized as rights of educational actors. In addition, there is evidence of a different perspective towards the quality of education from a productive perspective towards one that takes into account coexistence and justice, and the integration of biomedical and social humanistic knowledge
