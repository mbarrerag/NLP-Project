# Identification of a parasporin candidate protein of Bacillus thuringiensis.

Parasporins are a group of proteins with anticancer activity, derived from proteolytic excision of Cry proteins synthesized by Bacillus thuringiensis. Previous work of our group has identified the presence of protein candidates for parasporins in two native strains of Bacillus thuringiensis. Indeed, the evaluation of 115 strains native to Bacillus thuringiensis of the Biopesticides strainer of the Instituto de Biotecnología of the National University revealed the presence of proteins without hemolytic or insecticide activity and with cytotoxic activity against PC-3 cell lines (prostate cancer) and MDA -MB231 (M breast cancer) in two strains (Cepas 68 and 79). Due to the anticancer potential of these proteins, in this project we propose to identify one of these proteins candidates to parasporins, which could be promising in the search of new alternatives for cancer treatment
