# Community justice for peasants. Case study towards constitutional recognition

This work, framed in the line of the sociology of law, aims to demonstrate the existence of a community justice, typical of the peasant communities of the Guamoco region, located in the southwest foothill of the Serrania of San Lucas, at the intersection of the municipalities of El Bagre and Nechi, department of Antioquia, and Santa Rosa del Sur and Montecristo, department of Bolívar; corollary of its peripheral position regarding the centers of distribution of economic, social and cultural resources on the one hand, and the armed conflict of which has been historical scenario, on the other hand. To do this demonstration, first, the historical configuration of the subject in key of the state presence limited to the extractivist and military fields is described; second, identifying the legal systems that arise as a result of that material impossibility that the communities have access to the justice of the State; and third, analyzing the origin of the peasant justice, and describing their mechanisms of intervention in the community conflicts.
