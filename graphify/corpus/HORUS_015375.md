# Diagnostic exercises in the museum sector: 12 years of learning about what we call the Antioquia Museum Network 2010-2022

The following written work presents the three moments that make up the work exercise of degree of the Master in Museumology and Heritage Management of the National University of Colombia. The first part, product of a collaborative internship with Luz Daniela Ortíz, in the Museo Fiestas del Maíz, in the Municipality of Sonsón, Antioquia. To evaluate and propose museum improvements and design execution phases and sources of financing. The second part, a professional commission of research, design and production of the exhibition “Gente del Maíz”, with emphasis on the curatorial processes, for the University Museum of the University of Antioquia, in Medellín. And the third, the conceptual work dedicated to learning around the Network of Museums of Antioquia (the department with more museums of Colombia according to the Colombian Museum Information System- SIMCO), based on the definitions of Museums, Networks and systems; the application of the participatory diagnostic tools and the analysis of information to generate alternative management proposals that take over the territory and the community to which the museum owes, as its centre. .
