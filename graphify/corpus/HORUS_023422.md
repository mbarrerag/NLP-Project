# EVALUATION OF THE EFFECT OF ANTIMICALROBIAN COMPOUNDS IN MICROORGANIMS CAUSEN OF DETERIOR IN FRESH FRUIT OF UCHUVA

The effect of three antimicrobial compounds in the vapour phase (2-nonanone, cinamaldehyde and essential oil of oregano) on the growth and activity of the Botrytis cinerea mold was previously determined to be the main cause of deterioration in uchuva fruits (Physalis peruviana L.) from Boyacá. A first assembly was made at cooling temperature evaluating concentrations 1 g and 0.1 g of each compound by 2000 cm3 of head space. The second assembly was performed at ambient temperature of reference (25 °C) evaluating concentrations 0.1 g, 0.05 g, 0.001 g and 0.005 g for 2000 cm3 of head space. The cinamaldehyde was the compound with the highest antimicrobial capacity to control the growth of Botrytis cinerea in fresh fruits of uchuva
