# ANALYSIS OF SCHOOL ERGONOMY FROM THE OPTIONAL THERAPY PRACTICE IN A PRIMARY SECOND COURSE OF AN OFFICIAL EDUCATIVE INSTITUTION OF BOGOTA FOR POINTIALIZATION OF SCHOOL DEEMPNIGHT.

The interaction between the human being and the educational environment, in which he develops various school activities, self-care and recreation, provides possibilities for learning and development. This is why the use of educational furniture shows opportunities to promote the performance of the school in the relationship between the student, his school demands and the context. Faced with these needs, the practice of the National University of Colombia proposes to explore the issue. OBJECTIVE Analyze the School Ergonomics in a course so that opportunities are provided to improve the performance of the school children through the evaluation, analysis and application of strategies that have an impact on postural hygiene, support in the literacy and care of children. MATERIALS AND METHODS The approach of research is mixed, instruments: participation in the classroom and adapted version to evaluate the school environment, form of inspection of study posts and school environment. RESULTS The recognition of school ergonomics in the educational community and in the family environments, makes part of the collective achievement of integral well-being and participation in the development of school-related changes and school environment
