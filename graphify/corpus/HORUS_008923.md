# Technical feasibility of the application of electrocoagulation as a treatment for the removal of mercury in drinking waters of the Bagre, Antioquia

The present research paper aimed at analysing the electrochemical process in the removal of mercury from simulated waters at concentrations evidenced in human consumption waters of the municipality of Bagre, Antioquia. The methodology was based on the optimization of an electrocoagulation cell with the establishment of a physical chemical scenario with optimal pH conditions, current density, the material used in the slaughter electrodes and the time required in order to improve the efficiency of the process. The results showed that the electrocoagulation cell present in the laboratory of the National University of Colombia, achieved an efficiency of 99,64-99,77% of Hg removal, with the use of aluminium electrodes, which can be considered as a useful procedure in the removal of this type of metals present in contaminated waters. .
