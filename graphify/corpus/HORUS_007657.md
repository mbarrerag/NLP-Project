# Characterisation of secondary metabolites with anxiolytic activity in the extract of roots of uchuva (Physalis peruviana) by liquid chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry (LC-MS)

The presence of anxiolytic activity in fruits of the solanaceae family, and some of them in Uchuva, has been reported. It is expected to be a fractionation of different parts of this plant to look for compounds that have been reported as anxiolytics using the LCMS technique
