# Magdalena River and its basin: towards environmental and territorial sustainability

The Magdalena River, the _x0093_rio de la Patria_x0094_, has a singular importance for Colombia: it has played and continues to play a key role in the configuration of the national territory due to its location, extension, physical complexity and diversity of the territories it connects. Its basin, which covers 19 departments, 724 municipalities, and 80% of the population of the country, contains the majority of the economic activity of the country, representing a contribution of 85% of the national GDP. The fishing production of the basin represents 70,671% of the total continental fishing, while the energy production is in values of 95% for the thermal energy, and 70% for the hydroelectric one (Cormagdalena, 2007; AUAP, 2013).
