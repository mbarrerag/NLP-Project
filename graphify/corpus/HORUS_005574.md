# GERIATRIC PALIATIVE CARE IN DEMENCIA ADVANCED DEMUNCIA

As the population ages, the increase in the prevalence of neurodegenerative diseases generates enormous concern for global public health.1,2 Many of these conditions are associated with the commitment of different cognitive domains and functionality, which was previously known as dementia, but is currently called major neurocognitive disorder (CND).2---4. Worldwide, during 2021, about 55 million people suffered this condition, but it is estimated that it will affect between 131 and 152 million people by 20501--3. By this date, 20% of the global population will be over 65 years old, and is estimated that the NMD will affect 40% of this group
