# PACKAGE LEAFLET OF METHODOLOGIES FOR DETECTION AND MONITORY OF VASCULAR MARCHITEZ BY FUSARIUM OXYSPORUM F.SP. CUBENSE REAZA 4 IN COLOMBIA BANNUM CULTIVES

The banana is currently the non-traditional agricultural product with the greatest export in Colombia, its production is threatened by the entry into the country of the fungus Fusarium oxysporum f.sp. cubense Raza 4 (FocR4T). Although currently a large number of techniques are used for the detection of diseases in plants (serological, molecular and biomarker-based methods), most require the destruction of samples, are delayed, expensive, require highly qualified personnel and generally cannot give results in real time. It is for these reasons that it is proposed to use methodologies based on properties or stress of the plant, such as the spectroscopy of reflectance and hyperspectral images for the detection and monitoring of FocR4T in Colombian banana crops. Recently, it was possible to develop a methodology to detect and discriminate plants in the pathosystem tomato-Fusarium as a model of study using reflectance spectroscopy and hyperspectral images.
