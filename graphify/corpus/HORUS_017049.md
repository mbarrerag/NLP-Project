# Effect of the application of a psychological intervention protocol on the management of an injury, resilience and adherence to rehabilitation by an athlete with acute injury: case study.

The participation in different sports activities brings benefits for the health and well-being of sportsmen. However, accidents and sports injuries can occur, with possible negative consequences on mental and physical health, besides economic repercussions, on the professional status, and on the perceptions that have of pain or the future, among others (Mohammed et al., 2018; Prieto et al., 2015; Reina Mencias, 2018). Therefore, the evaluation and psychological intervention of the injured athletes is indispensable, as well as the adoption of a multidisciplinary vision, with a more inclusive approach, in order to provide valuable information and strategies that will be useful for the sportsman's rehabilitation process (García-Mas et al., 2014; Ortín Montero et al., 2010; Salim et al., 2015). Describe the effects of a psychological intervention program on the management of an acute lesion, the resilience and adherence to the rehabilitation process of a sportsman (García-Mas et al., 2014; Ortín Montero et al., 2010; Salim et al., 2015). of its totality was in accordance with the 7 phases of functional sports rehabilitation, the type of injury and the individual process of the athlete. The training in the different techniques proposed by the protocol positively influenced the motivation, contributed to decrease the perception of pain and the adherence to rehabilitation was maintained during the process
