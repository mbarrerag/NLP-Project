# Evaluation of corrosion resistance due to carbonation in concretes with polyland cements added with limestone and calcined clay.

The kertland cement, added with limestone and calcined clay (LC3), is a material capable of developing mechanical properties comparable to ordinary politland cement (OPC), of allowing the obtaining of a dense and rich microstructure in aluminates that improves the resistance of concrete to the attack by chlorides and the silica alkalic reaction (RAS), and brings environmental, technical and economic benefits to society. In this study, the resistance to corrosion due to the carbonation in concretes manufactured with LC3 cements found that this was directly proportional to the clinker factor of cement, particularly in accelerated condition. LC3 cements were formulated by adjusting their SO3 content by evaluating the hydration process in cement paste. The carbonation tests were executed by means of accelerated methods in paste, mortar and concrete, and also developed tests in natural condition. Also, the effect of the carbonation on the resistance to the mortar compression, finding a good performance for cements with higher clinPupil.
