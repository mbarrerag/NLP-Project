# ANEMIC SINDROME

This third edition of Pediatrics, Diagnosis and Treatment is structured into three sections: Basics, Clinical Specialities and Surgical Specialities. It presents current and accurate information for the understanding of healthy children and adolescents in their stages of growth and development; it also allows for a deeper study of clinical management and guidance when they are in disease. It has 165 topics distributed in 36 chapters that cover the fundamental aspects of daily practice
