# CHAPTER 16 PRINCIPLES FOR TRANSITIONAL CARE OF THE CAREER OF THE PERSON WITH CRONE DISEASE

The chapter conceptually addresses transitional care as a concept of intervention in illness based on the theory of transitions of Afaf Meleis, This chapter presents a novel approach to care of the caregiver of persons with ECNT, based on the theory of middle range of transitions proposed by Afaf Meleis. This framework allows to move from a mechanistic paradigm in which the caregiver is conceived as a care provider that requires instruction towards one focused on the caregiver as a subject that also requires care
