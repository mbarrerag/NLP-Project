# Development of a methodology to define the DevOps process and its implementation in the information technology industry

In a business environment where technology is essential, the Information Technology (IT) industry is constantly looking for ways to speed up the delivery of quality software. In this context, DevOps has emerged as a key approach, fostering collaboration between development and operations teams, process automation and continuous delivery of software. This master's thesis aims to develop a comprehensive methodology for defining and implementing DevOps in IT organizations. Through a comprehensive review of literature and analysis of best practices, a guide is presented that covers from DevOps's foundations to its practical application in business environments. .
