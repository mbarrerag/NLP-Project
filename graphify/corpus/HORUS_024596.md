# THE SOCIO-CULTURAL APPROACH OF READING AND WRITING: SOME IMPLICATIONS IN SUPPORT OF STUDENTS OF MINORIZED COMMUNITIES IN UNIVERSITY

This article reviews some of the main approaches from which reading and writing have been conceptualized and their pedagogical implications. The assumptions of the grammar, communicative, cognitive and sociocultural perspectives are reviewed. In the second part, it explores the sociocultural perspective and some of its contributions to work with indigenous students, Afro-descendants and poor municipalities at university
