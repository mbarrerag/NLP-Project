# Resist the limit. Transforming a mission into a multi-ethnic reservation in the Colombian Orinoquía 1950-2020.

This project seeks to describe the process that a group of indigenous families and communities belonging to various ethnic groups experienced during the formation of the La Pascua (La Primavera, Vichada) Reservo as a political-administrative entity that grouped them together and now configures them as a social and political body recognized by the Colombian State. This initiative is part of the PhD Thesis Project "Resisting to the Limit. Transforming a Mission into a Multi-Ethnic Reservo in the Colombian Orinoquía 1950-2020." After having obtained an ethnographic description of the current reservation and making a historical reconstruction of the migrations of the families that make up it, this project seeks to support the realization of the last part of the research that consists in the description of the configuration of these indigenous families and communities as an indigenous territory legally recognized by the State under the figure of Resguardo
