# ATI-FIBROXANTOMA: CASE SERIES

atypical fibroxantoma (FXA) is a tumor that appears in elderly patients, with an antecedent of photoexposure, with a higher incidence of cases in the head and neck. There are no epidemiology-logic figures regarding incidence in Colombia; however, the incidence of FXA in Spain has been reported to be 0.59 cases/100,000 inhabitants; therefore, it is recognized that it is a tumor lesion of low incidence (1). The objective of this study is to report three cases of patients who presented with atypical fibroxantoma in different anatomical locations and who received surgical management with broad local resection and MOHS surgery
