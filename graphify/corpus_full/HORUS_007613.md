# Obtaining a fusion protein for the administration of peptides in tumor cell lines

Lectins of different origins have been a promising tool in cancer treatment due to the cytotoxic effect which includes the suppression of cell multiplication and the induction of cell death in different tumour cell lines, and have been considered as recognition molecules due to specific glycosylation patterns expressed in tumor cells. On the other hand, biomolecules as peptides obtained from animal-based poisons are cytotoxic in tumour cell lines of different origin. Thanks to technological advancement, it is possible to express recombinant lectins and peptides, as well as to enhance biological activity by designing peptides or the expression of fusion proteins, where lectin acts as a vehicle directed and fused to a peptide that potentiates antitumor activity. The aim of this project is to develop a targeted drug supply system based on a lectin of animal/vegetal or fungal origin and a cytotoxic peptide derived from poison that has antitumor activity against prostate cancer, cervix, breast cancer or stomach cancer
