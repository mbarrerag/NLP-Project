# Evaluation and pedagogy in higher education

Until 2022, there are no researches on the pedagogical performance of the academic fellows in the country. The Faculty of Sciences of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters, links as fellows students of master's and doctorates to guide courses in physics, in the modality of workshops or laboratory, aimed at young people who pursue basic sciences and engineering careers. The research in reference characterizes the pedagogy and evaluation strategies most recurrent in the academic fellows, in the period 2012-2015
