# DEVENING MARXISM IN COLOMBIA (1919-1949)

The article proposes to expose the evolution of Marxism in Colombia between 1919 and 1949. It shows that the world economic and political context, such as Latin America, and the dynamics of the country during this time made possible the emergence of Marxism in the determined forms that it acquired, as well as Marxism itself represented the response to the historical needs of Colombia at its determined time. For this, it exposes the evolution of Marxism in Colombia in two of the forms it has acquired: I-political-insurrect Marxism (1919-1930), II-Associative-Reformist Marxism on the popular front (1930-1949).
