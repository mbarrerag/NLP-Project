# MICROBIOLOGICAL AND MULECULAR DENTIFICATION OF BACTERIES ASSOCIATED TO THE RIZOSFRESH OF FARNESIAN VACHELLIA (FABACEAE) IN A REGION OF THE FOREST OF TROPIC SECO IN PEACE,

The composition of bacterial microorganisms associated with the rhizospheric is essential for plants to overcome adverse environmental conditions, allowing their survival and/or adaptation to ecosystems such as the tropical dry forest. In this research, the identification of bacteria associated with the Vachellia farnesiana (Fabaceae) rhizosphere was carried out in a region of the tropical dry forest of the municipality of La Paz-Cesar, using microbiological cultures and molecular methods from soil and root samples taken from the plant's rhizosphere. Microbiological results allowed to obtain five isolates with different morphotypes and analyses of the sequences of the 16S molecular marker, allowed to classify isolates within the Enterobacterial order, belonging to the Enterobacterial and Serratia genera. These bacterial communities associated with the Vachellia farnesian rhizospheres may be playing a fundamental role in nitrogen fixation processes and could open new strategies for recovery by biotechnology, of highly intervention soils through anthropogenic activities such as livestock and mining in the tropical dry forest. Key words: Bacterias, molecular identification, 16S, tropical forest sequence
