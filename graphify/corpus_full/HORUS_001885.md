# COMPARISON OF DIFFERENT SPARTIAL FILTERS FOR IDENTIFICATION OF THE GRAY MATTER IN BRAIN IMAGING

to compare different spatial filters for the identification of grey matter in brain images, in order to improve contrast in images and facilitate their interpretation in medical diagnoses. ten spatial filters were selected that were implemented and evaluated using a graphical user interface (GUI) in Matlab. Filters include from grayscale conversions to more complex filters such as the Gaussian and focus filter. The efficiency of each filter was measured through the correlation coefficient between the pixel intensity curves generated by the filters and an ideal model curve. the medium nonlinear spatial filter was the one that showed the highest correlation coefficient (0.8974), indicating that it was best approximated to the model curve ideal for the differentiation between white and grey matter. In contrast, the relief filter (embosss) showed the lowest coefficient (0.6972), suggesting that it was not suitable for tissue identification. the median filter proved to be the most effective for the identification of gray matter in brain images.
