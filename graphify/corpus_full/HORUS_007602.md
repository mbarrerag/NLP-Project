# Design of a study on the effect of climate variables on the Colombian stock market

Several studies have reported an effect of climatic variables on the behavior of financial markets. For example, it was found that sunny days affected the returns of Dow Jones, NYSE/AMEX (Saunders 1993). This relationship presumably occurs because the climate affects the emotions of the brokers and investors on the same day. Studies have concentrated in countries of the northern hemisphere and there has only been a study for the torrida area. It is sought to design a project to establish if there is a similar relationship for the Colombian case. The interest is that, since there are no seasons, a current confusion factor is reduced for the northern areas that must make additional steps to deseasonalize the data. Under these premises we set out to carry out the research project with Hermes code 61826. Given the times, scopes and commitments proposed in it, it is necessary to have support staff for the development of research and academic activities, since it is a teaching day project and does not have alternate sources of funding
