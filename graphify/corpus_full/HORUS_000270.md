# Genetic variants of casein and β-lactoglobulin in the cattle Lucerne and its association with milk quality

The objective of this research was to evaluate the variation in genes CSN1S1, CSN2, CSN1S2, CSN3 and BLG in the Lucerne breed (LUC).For this purpose, 94 animals LUC and 10 animals Hartón del Valle (HDV) were genotyped using the GGPTM Bovine 150 K chip, which has four NPS in the CSN1S1, 14 in the CSN2 gene, two in the CSN1S2, 12 in the CSN3 gene and 11 in the LGB gene. From which the haplotypes and protein variants of the αS1-CN, β-CN, αS2-CN, α-CN and β-LG were reconstructed the genotypic frequencies of the most important variants between sexes and generations.The variables BFFinberg were evaluated and the values of wFST and FIS for each of the NPS. The values of LD* and the haplotipical frequencies for the cases were estimated and NS2 were measured. These results can be used in assisted selection programmes aimed at improving milk quality and processing traits
