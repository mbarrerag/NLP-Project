# RECOGNIZED AS MUCH RECOGNIZED. THE PURE CONCEPT OF RECOGNITION IN THE CHAPTER OF THE PHENOMENOLOGY AUTHORITY OF HEGEL SPIRIT.

The dialectic of the pure concept of the recognition of the chapter of the Self-consciousness of the Spirit unfolds the series of intersubjective conditions and behaviors from which the reciprocal experience of recognition is achieved among the self-consciousnesses. Con-form to the narrative structure of the Spirit's Phenomenology, the pure concept corresponds to the point of view of the "we" on the nature that has the encounter for the self-consciousness that, in what way are you immediate, are certain of themselves only. In this work we propose to address the practical-ontological significance inherent in this encounter and to explain the moments through which it leads to the experience of reciprocal recognition, as the only experience that corresponds, in the whole of the Spirit's Phenomenology, to the model of the recognition achieved
