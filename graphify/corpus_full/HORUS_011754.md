# CHAPTER 1. ONE TENTH OF THE PROGRAMME ALL TO BE LEARNED: MASTERS BELONGING MASTERS

All of us have become one of the most important teacher training programs in Latin America, a reference. This chapter presents the essential aspects through which the program has passed during its ten years of existence. For this, we analyze its official documents, related research and impact assessments available. It is highlighted that the program has had at least three stages: the beginning, between 2011 and 2014; the reengineering, between 2015 and 2018; and the consolidation, between 2018 and 2021. Each of these stages is marked by important changes in the administrative, pedagogical and conceptual fields
