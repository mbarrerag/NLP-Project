# POROSITY INFLUENCE ON ADVISORY OF BASIC RED TEXTILE COLORANT 46 (RB-46)

Textile dyes are dangerous because, in addition to increasing the values of COD in natural waters, many are toxic (such as azo dyes), cause allergies and are related to certain types of cancer.3 Adsorption is an efficient way of removing organic pollutants in aqueous environment. For this, highly porous materials are typically preferred due to the classic concepts of the phenomenon. In fact, materials of high surface area and high microporosity are almost always sought. In this work, the actual influence of the area and the porosity of a set of solids on the adsorption of a cathionic azoic dye (basic red 46: RB-46) in aqueous medium are studied
