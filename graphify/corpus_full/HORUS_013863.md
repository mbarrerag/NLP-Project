# ATTENTION AND CARE PRACTICES FOR DISEASE MANAGES TRANSMITTED BY VECTORS IN THE SOUTH OF THE AMAZON DEPARTMENT

The diseases transmitted by vectors (VTEs) constitute an important public health problem in the department of Amazonas due to the high prevalence of vector insects and difficulties for access to medical care, especially for remote indigenous communities. To reduce the incidence of TVEs it is necessary to create public health policies that integrate the knowledge of Western medicine (biomedical) and traditional medicine, in order to achieve a more complete attention that is articulated to the socio-cultural conditions of the area. In this project it is sought to identify local knowledge about TVEs, through workshops, semi-structured interviews and surveys to the inhabitants of an indigenous community in the south of the department of Amazonas, the people who exercise traditional medicine and the health assistant. This knowledge will be analyzed from different angles: (1) insects involved in their transmission and their ecology; (2) symptoms, measures of self-care and prevention and (3) plants used for treatment and forms of preparation. In addition to the collection of these data, taxonomic and phytochemical characterization of plants used for the treatment of the associated TVEs.
