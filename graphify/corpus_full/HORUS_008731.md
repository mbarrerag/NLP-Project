# CESAR PLANARIAS: A CONNECTION WITH THE DEVELOPMENT BIOLOGY FROM THE WALK

The planaries are widely used in Regenerative Biology given their high capacity to regenerate different tissues, process regulated by multipotent stem cells called neoblasts. This characteristic makes them a valuable model organism in Development Biology, since it allows to study cellular and molecular processes involved in the establishment of the bodily axes after the tissues suffer some damage. This organism has been used in the course of development and morphophysiology? at the National University of Colombia, La Paz Headquarters, which seeks learning based on projects and focused on observation, description and experimentation with a local organism. During the course specimens were collected in La Mina, Cesar, in the Badillo River. We measured physicochemical parameters of the local ecological niche in order to extrapolate these conditions to the laboratory and ensure good follow-up of the crop. We made the identification of species, from the morphological characterization, internal and molecular characteristics.
