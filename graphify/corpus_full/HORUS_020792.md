# Environmental analysis of the agricultural border in the Colombian Orinoquía

The agricultural frontier is a dynamic transition area, socially defined according to the biophysical characteristics of the environment and the forms in which the societies relate to it. Nevertheless, the institutionality in Colombia defines it as a border that divides the areas destined for the development of agricultural activities, of the areas that by legal criterion are excluded or conditioned for those purposes. Particularly, in the Colombian Orinoquia, watershed that has a tropical climate of monomodal pluvial regime, mountainous and plain reliefs, and soils with low fertility, mostly oxysoles, where it dwells a great biological and cultural diversity, that for hundreds of years has determined the use of the soils of the region.
