# BIODIGISTORIC PACES FOR THE TREATMENT OF ORGANIC WASTES OF THE SAINT PAUL AGRICULTURAL STATION (RIONEGRANTOIOCIA)

The purpose of this research was to assemble biodigestora bales, as a technique for the treatment and integral use of organic residues of livestock activities (birds and pigs), and of plant origin, generated in the agricultural station San Pablo, of the National University of Colombia Sede Medellín. An experimental design was carried out 22 taking as reference the type of residue (bird and pig style) and size of the mold (750 and 125 litres), with 4 treatments and three replicas. The physical and chemical composition, besides the monitoring to height (H) and temperature (T°C) weekly. The results obtained currently show that the 125 liters bales present better progress in the decomposition of the organic material than the 750 liter bales.
