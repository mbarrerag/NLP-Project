# EPYLOGUE: A. A MAP WITH MANY NINE AND LINES. B. A RECOGNIZED BY ITS URRICLE. C. BIBLIOGRAPHY

Carlos Niño Murcia (Ubaté, 1950) is an architect who can be seen from different prisms. This research, the result of a work supported in various interviews conducted between 2016 and 2020, provides some light on the professional, academic and human trajectory of a professor at the National University of Colombia
