# How do you respond to PHOTOSELLECTIVE AND SOMBRIO BAD IN SEMICONTROLATED CONDITIONS?

In agriculture meshes are used to improve plant characteristics and to limit income and manipulate the behavior of pest insects. We analyzed the response of 300 females of Frankliniella occidentalis (Thysanoptera: Thripidae) of 4 days old, obtained from ornamental cultures of the Bogotá Sabana, to the effect of commercial meshes of 50 mesh OptiNet® and Aluminet®. It was used 50mesh-Anti-Insect® as control and a semi-controlled system with chrysanthemum flowers, separated by each mesh and located in rows of three stems at distances of 90, 113, 137 and 162 cm of thrips, which was always performed at 10hr. Three repetitions were performed for each mesh with a time of evaluation of 24 hours/repeat, with mostly cloudy days. Of the females released were recovered N=45 using OptiNet®, N=48 with Aluminet® and N=34 with Anti-Insect®. Aluminet®.
