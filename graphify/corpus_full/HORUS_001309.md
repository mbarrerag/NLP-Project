# PREADULT LIVING CONDITIONS DURING SOCIOPOLITICAL TRANSITION IN FLOORED CEMETRY (500-400BC), SUPE VALLEY, PERU: CHILDHOOD MORBIDITY AND SOCIOPOLITICAL CHANGE IN CENTRAL PREHISTERIC ANDES

Through a detailed evaluation of non-specific stress markers in 67 individuals (47 under 8 years), this study investigates the health conditions of preadult individuals in the Cementerio La Quebrada de Chupacigarro (QCC), on the north-central coast of Peru, during the transition between the Middle and Late Formation periods (500-400 BC), a critical time for the sociopolitical processes of the region. Our objectives are to determine (i) the nature of preadultial morbidity and mortality, (ii) periods of greatest vulnerability of the population, and (iii) the etiology of the disease. (iii) etiological factors associated with the health-disease processes in this context of demographic pressure. The results show a high prevalence of stress markers related to preadult anemia, with a strong selective mortality in individuals <3.5 years of age, and recurrent periods of anaemia in adults as well as recurrent periods of adult morbidity. These findings indicate exposure to diseases due to overcrowding, lack of sanitation and malnutrition, supporting the idea of a precarious condition of cavalleys of about 500-C
