# Factors and strategies for the participation of parents in the operation of School Feeding Programs. Comparative study of Peru, Brazil and Colombia

Colombia is in the stage of implementation of a rural SEAP model, which aims to give priority to the participation of communities in the operation of the program, to facilitate social control, monitoring and thus promote local trade in the regions with the aim of offering a more equitable SEAP, which contributes to food security and to guaranteeing the human right to food.This gives parents and other actors of the educational community the opportunity to implement the SEAP.However, it is necessary to ask how to achieve an effective participation?, a question that becomes a guide of this research.To give a detailed analysis of the factors and strategies, both in Peru and Brazil, considered effective to expand the participation of the educational community in the execution of the SEAP. This with the intention of giving light and contributing to the implementation of the SEAP in Colombia. Some factors that influence the implementation of the SEAP were identified in the form of the SEAP and the other factors that affected the SEAP. These strategies are developed in each of the four main factors identified that influenced four types of actors mainly: participation of the school community; articulation and coordination between public and private sector actors; committed CAE members and sufficient specialized staff. .
