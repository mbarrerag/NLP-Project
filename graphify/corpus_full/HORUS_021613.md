# Factors that determine the acceptance of virtual classes in students of the engineering faculty of the National University of Colombia.

The importance given to information and communication technologies (ICT) in the education sector has been growing considerably. Different technological mechanisms focused on better support for teaching and learning processes are increasingly visible. Virtual classes are part of these technological mechanisms and due to the health crisis that arose in 2020 by the SARS-COVD-2 virus and strategies to prevent contagion such as compulsory confinement, most educational institutions opted for this modality in order to ensure that students continue to receive appropriate and timely classes. Taking into account this situation, this work focused on identifying, through the UTAUT2 theory, the factors that determine the technological acceptance of virtual classes for engineering students at the National University of Colombia. To achieve this, an online survey was used that responded to a total of 174 students, and the data were analyzed under the PLS-SEM procedure, where it was observed that the determinant factor of acceptance of virtual classes is the hedonic motivation
