# Taste changes in the downside of the Quarks in U(1)’x models.

In this project a detailed study of the standard model is carried out, in which the Lagrangiano fermionic, bosonic and Yukawa blocks was taken into account for the description of the respective interactions that occur in nature, and subsequently with the use of the spontaneous break of the symmetry and the mechanism of Higgs to explain the obtaining of the mass of the elementary particles. In addition, it is analyzed that when rotating the Lagrangiano fermionic, in this case the Quarks sector, and to take it to the base mass state appears the matrix of mixture (CKM) that explains the changes of flavor [ 1 ], and also the new Jarlskog invariant that is a measure of the violation (CP) [2].
