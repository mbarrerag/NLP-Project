# Eetane: Digital platform for Amerindian languages

The aim of this proposal is to complement and strengthen an international project approved by the Modern Endangered Archives Program (MEAP) of the University of California (UCLA) with the creation of a web platform (Eetane: Digital Platform of Amerindian Languages) that will serve as a node of a network of digital archives from around the world with collections of Amerindian languages to support local initiatives of cultural and linguistic revitalization.The proposal that was approved by the MEAP/UCLA (Hermes 57968) is focused specifically on works of preservation of analogous audio files and does not include dissemination activities, such as those proposed here.The proposed objectives are compatible with those of the approved international project and have the support of the MEAP.The Eetane platform is based on the work advanced by the research group Ethnology and Linguistics of the Amazonia (A1 Ministry of Economic Affairs) and the institutional repository of the UNAL. The Magütá people of the Maloca of the Amazon Sea, which is part of the infrastructure of the Laboratory of Languages and Symmetric Dialogue. Eetane means _x0093_something brilliant that rises, expands and attracts people_x0094_. This initiative is part of the purposes of the UNESCO declaration of the Decade of Indigenous Languages (2022-2032), of the Ten-Year Plan of Native Languages of Colombia (Ministry of Culture of Colombia, 2022) and of the provisions of Law 1381 of 2010 on the protection of native languages, which assigns to public universities a role in the conservation, preservation and protection of native languages
