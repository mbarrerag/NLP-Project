# Combination of actions between reactive take-off changers and compensations in emergency situations close to the collapse of tension.

The stability of tension in the power systems is a subject that is still the subject of research. The collapses of tension generate great economic and social losses. To avoid them, the operator has several control actions that improve stability. Sometimes, it is necessary to apply two actions in a combined way in critical situations of the network to restore the values of tension. Although in the literature the use of actions has been investigated individually and also in a combined way, little has been studied on the combination of transformer take-changers and reactive compensations. In this work, an automatism is proposed that combines these two actions in a coordinated way and makes decisions based on indicators such as the margin of load. The automatism is implemented in the power systems IEEE 14 nodes and 39 nodes with critical scenarios. To validate their performance, the actions are evaluated individually and combined to compare both options. The results show that the use of individual actions is not enough in some cases to recover a proper operation of the network, while the combined actions are able to achieve it, moreover, obtaining improvements of up to 18% of the margin of load, in comparison with the other ones
