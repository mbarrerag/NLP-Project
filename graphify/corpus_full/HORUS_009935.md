# Computational analysis of influenza A hemagglutinin of pandemic lineage viruses in Colombia

Although the wide circulation of influenza virus (VI) A has been demonstrated in pigs and humans in the world and in Colombia, there are few studies that have characterized the biological aspects of these viruses. Therefore, this work was based on the computational analysis of the hemagglutinin surface protein (HA) of the pandemic strains of circulating VI in the country, and its relationship to a change in the biological properties of these. This work was developed in three chapters, in the first one characterized the genomic composition of HA, followed by the identification of mutations in this protein and its possible contribution to the spread of these viruses in the region. The last two chapters, corresponded to the structural analysis of the HA and its interaction with cellular receptors. Overall, it is suggested that the I32V, P84S, S204T, E375K mutation among others, could have contributed to the fitness and the establishment of these viruses in the current populations in Colombia. The most relevant mutations (E375K and S163N) were related to the increase in virulence and the ability to evade the immune response.
