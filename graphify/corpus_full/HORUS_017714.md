# Relationship between HPGD expression levels and T lymphocyte density and phenotype in tumour tissue of patients with prostate cancer and its association with biochemical recurrence.

In this project it was proposed to determine the relationship between the levels of expression of 15-Hydroxyprostaglandine dehydrogenase (HPGD) with the density and phenotype of T lymphocytes (LT) in the prostatic tissue of patients with prostate cancer (CaP) and its possible association with biochemical recurrence (RB). It is a retrospective analytical study, in tissues included in paraffin of patients with Cap treated at the National Institute of Cancer (INC) between the years 2007 to 2013, intervened with radical prostatectomy (PR). The expression of HPGD was analyzed by means of qRT-PCR and immunohistochemistry. The population and phenotype of LT (peripheric and tumor infiltraters (TIL) was determined by immunohistochemistry through the use of specific antibodies for each marker. Tests of χ2 or Fisher's exact test were applied to determine statistically significant differences between variables analyzed with a p -0-0.05). The expression of HPGD in patients in non-tumurous prostatic tissue was found in high frequency at low level (0,00). further tests are needed to establish a better phenotype of cellular populations and a larger population number
