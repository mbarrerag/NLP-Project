# Peirce and grothendieck on mathematical cognition: A merging of the pragmaticist maxim and topos theory

Around the problematics of "mathematical cognition, " some Peircean and Grothendieckean tools are presented (Pragmaticist Maxim and Topos Theory), which help to assess the diverse perspectives and strata which enrich our understanding of mathematics
