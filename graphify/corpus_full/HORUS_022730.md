# Characterization of soil moisture dynamics in agricultural areas of Colombia

ABSTRACT The seasonal dynamics of soil moisture are influenced by different environmental factors, such as topography, soil physical and chemical conditions, plant cover type and climate classification. Data from 105 agrometeorological stations of the IDEAM network, distributed in Colombia, were studied, with records from January 2001 to April 2020. A nonparametric test of Spearman ranges correlation was used to evaluate the relationship between soil humidity and atmospheric variables. Simultaneously, the behavior of seasonal dynamics was analyzed, as well as its interaction with atmospheric, physical soil moisture and plant cover variables. The results showed that soil humidity is significantly influenced by the frequency that by the intensity of precipitation, this variable had a seasonal behavior, similar to that of precipitation. The physical variable texture was closely related to the behaviour of soil surface humidity (<10 cm of depth). In addition, a response of surface humidity to the physical conditions of the soil, topography and the availability of plant cover was evidenced; at a higher depth the humidity has a lower influence of the surface humidity
