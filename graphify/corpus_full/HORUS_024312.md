# From the research programme to the research activities of the research seminar on education in the Antarctic University 1999-2001

This chapter on the history of a movement that became a network describes the origins of the movement through some of its founders, the values and principles that established the foundations of the movement, identity symbolism and the logic of its regional and national meetings. It also reveals the different confluences that led to the creation of the first seedbeds of research, its institutional networks and the constitution of the Colombian Network of Semilleros de Investigación
