# The educational measure as a sanction in the SRPA, its effectiveness from a socio-educational perspective in Bogotá

With the validity of Law 1098 of 2006 - Code on Children and Adolescents, Colombia is developing the System of Criminal Responsibility for Adolescents (SRPA), which includes among its approaches to guarantee by the different State entities related to SRPA, the fulfilment of the rights, rehabilitation and resocialization of adolescents between 14 and 18 years of age who have committed a crime, with the aim of not punishing them as an adult, but recalling that they are still minors who have not yet completed their training. Therefore, the intention of national and international entities that propose the protection of the child will be observed in this document, identifying in this regard the application of the pedagogical measure as a sanction mechanism for adolescent offenders of the Criminal Law. .
