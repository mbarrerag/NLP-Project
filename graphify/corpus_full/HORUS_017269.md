# DISTRIBUTION AND BIOGEOGRAPHY OF THE DOPHOPRIED SCARE (COLEOPTERA: SCARABAEIDAE) OF COLOMBIA

The union of the endocoprid beetles is represented by about 80 species belonging to seven tribes within the subfamily Scarabaeinae and Aphodinae. Although for the case of the subfamily Scarabaeinae has been made a relatively recent review of the delimitation of their species and their geographical distribution, for the case of the species belonging to the subfamily Aphodinae, their diversity is only beginning to be known and there are many information gaps regarding their geographical and altitudinal distribution. In the next work we present the known distribution in Colombia of the species of the gangmium of the endocoprid beetles, with special emphasis on the species of the subfamily Scarabaeinae of which is counted with the greatest information of updated species, which allows to make an approach to their biogeographical characterization also taking into account their biological and behavioral characteristics and their phylogenetic relations. The analysis of the results allowed to identify groups of species with broad geographical and altitudinal distribution as well as groups of species with restricted distribution of certain geographical and altitunal species, which are considered to be the same species
