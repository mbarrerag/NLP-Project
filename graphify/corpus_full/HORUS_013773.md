# Alonso Restrepo Moreno: life and work of an anti-Ocoquean intellectual of the 20th century.

This research proposes to do a biographical, prosopographic and intellectual study of the life and work of the anti-Oqueño physician and inelectual Alonso Restrepo Moreno (1893-1955), whose life took place almost all in the city of Medellín, in the exercise of his medical profession, his scientific works, his critical activity in the press and his literary production. His personal files and documents, scattered in different repositories of Medellín, allow a study of the different facets of his participation in the cultural, scientific and political life of his time. Alonso Restrepo Moreno was a very enlightened man, virtuous writer, indefatigable and philanthropic critical concerned with the health and well-being of the society in which he lived. Many of the problems he tried to publicly repair and denounce still persist; for this reason, and for the little attention paid to him by later generations, the legacy of this physician and intellectual remains in force and awakens interest for current historiography. Our prosographic approach will show the insertion of his life and his work into a broad political, scientific and cultural context
