# Music for piano by Luis A Calvo: Critical edition of 4 adaptations for Álvaro Bedoya Sánchez's solo guitar

This work aims to show the process of elaboration of the critical edition, of four adaptations of the guitarist Álvaro Bedoya de la Música del famoso compandero santandereano Luis Antonio Calvo, originally written for Piano. From the collection, analysis and convergence of different documents that provide information of biographical, aesthetic and musical type, relevant for the assembly and interpretation of the work, of one of the most important Colombian composers of the first half of the twentieth century. It will have different sections within which they can be found. A brief review of the most important events of the life of the composer. A chapter, on the appearances of his “polyphatical” artistic personality, which aims to show the main compositional influences and to place in a correct way his musical style. To finally, a review of the adaptations made by Álvaro Bedoya Sánchez, with the aim of making a few notes to the edition, which allow to correct possible errors of notes or chords in its elaboration, and which in some way do not reflect the genuine musical ideas of Calvo. brief review of the most important events in the composer's life.
