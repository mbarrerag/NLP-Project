# Strategy for teaching-learning of exponential function and logarithmic function from the use of virtual tools.

This document presents a strategy for the teaching of exponential and logarithmic functions from the implementation of some Virtual Learning Objects (OVA), which were posed from real and semi-real situations in the context of ninth-grade students, from situations where students can enhance the processes of reasoning, modelling, communication and problem-solving, making use of virtual tools which were presented through the application of the compound interest generated in educational credits and models of exponential population growth taken over a time interval, starting from the Covid 19 contagions in our country
