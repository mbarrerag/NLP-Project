# Social competitiveness and school health: from indicators of taxation to effectiveness

Although references to the importance of addressing health in the school environment can be traced to the 18th century, the evolution of this field has been contradictory.While academic literature reports the progressive evolution of school health objectives, in principle focused on aspects related to infrastructure, then on hygiene and prevention, until reaching an emphasis promoting the promotion of the well-being, human development and quality of life of schoolchildren; in the Colombian school environment, health has been restricted to conceptions linked to organic functioning and responsibility for its approach is usually delegated to health sector agents. Whether it is the normative mandate that states that children and adolescents are subject to special protection, in view of the traditional belief that children are the future, or by virtue of the ease of viewing problems in (or accessing) captive population, much of the efforts in promotion and prevention have concentrated school; however, most of these actions are marked by a strictly biomedical approach, such that in addition to the good place they represent in many cases to educational institutions, is quite limiting in relation to the quality of the promotion and prevention of life
