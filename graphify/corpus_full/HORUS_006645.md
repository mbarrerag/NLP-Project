# Analysis of the reexpression of financial statements of the 2021 term under the regulatory framework for government entities. Case study

The Accounts General of the Nation, regulatory entity in the accounting matter of the public sector in Colombia, issued Resolution 533 of 2015 by which the regulatory framework applicable to government entities was incorporated into the Public Accounting System. In this regime, the objectives of financial information were established the accountability, decision-making and control, taking a fundamental role for users. However, in order for the financial information to meet its objectives and be useful for users, it must be free of material errors and, in the case of identifying them, it must reexpress the financial statements. The objective of this research is to analyze the effect of the reexpression of the financial statements under the regulatory framework for the government entities against the objectives of the financial information, for users of this type of information. Therefore, a case study is carried out in the Rural Development Agency, the government entity that reexpressed the financial statements of the validity 2022 due to material errors.
