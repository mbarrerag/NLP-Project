# ADAPTATION OF PROJECT GUIDELINES IN WITHDRAWAL AND MANAGEMENT OF ASBESTUM UNDER THE PMBOK GUIDE

Asbestos or asbestos is a material that has been used highly in the construction sector, so there are many components of this material worldwide. However, since 1977 the World Health Organization has determined that asbestos is a type 1 carcinogen, because of this, a material that for its time was a response with different qualities and low cost, today is considered as a major global problem that needs to be removed. One way to do this is by developing a strategy from the direction of projects for the removal and eradication of asbestos present in different constructive systems in the world. For these reasons, from the experience of good practices in institutions in Spain that have been responsible for its withdrawal, the integration of activities is being developed to lead a project for removal and management of asbestos with the knowledge and key components of the PMBOK® guide (Project Management Body of Knowledge) to contribute to this type of project. The results of this work aim to strengthen the processes of management of projects applied to the removal and management of asbestos, allowing from any PMBOK science transfer to take place and to manage it from a way of common vocabulary for this type of project
