# Changes in the models of territorial occupation based on road infrastructure in Funza, Mosquera and Cota 2000 - 2020

The purpose of this work is to identify and explain the transformations of the models of territorial occupation based on road infrastructure in the municipalities of Mosquera, Funza and Cota between 2000 and 2020. To this end, the relations between space production and the role of road infrastructure in urban assemblies are reviewed, precisely how in urban government from the space conceived through instruments of territorial planning and the influence exercised by road infrastructure have generated changes in the land use and vocation of municipalities, also in relation to metropolitan interactions and dynamics of urban economy. .
