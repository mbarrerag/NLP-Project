# Rated moment-resistant beam-column connections for tubular steel profiles

In the construction sector the use of steel has increased, a trend that includes the implementation of structural tubular profilery. Although this industrial alternative is widely available, there are no studies on its application within a seismic resistance system to ensure its correct behavior, specifically in moment-resistant porticos. Therefore, it is necessary to investigate the use and behavior of these profiles and the appropriate and practical way to connect them to ensure good behavior of the structural elements and connections (viga-column) to form porticos. In this study two connections were described as screwy viga-column between tubular profiles, with the aim of providing the industry with alternatives to apply steel órticos resistant to moment. In this process the compliance of all the regulatory requirements for the connections within these systems was ensured. The work was developed in two main areas: the experimentation in laboratory to establish a reference behavior, and the numerical simulation, to offer alternatives of mechanical behavior in the elastic and inelastic range of a connection through analysis of the terminal elements method without compromising the economic viability. Each process was described in detail, and presented in the numerical connections to be used in order to determine the to form frames. In this study, two metallic connections between tubular sections were qualified to provide the industry with valid application cases and assurance correct implementation of elements such as momentum-resistant frameworks. Throughout this process, compliance with all regulation requirements for connections within systems was assumed. The work was conducted along two main models: laboratory experimentation, aimed at establishing a baseline behavior, and numerical simulation, which provided alternatives for evaluating the physical being was present for future applications, then alternative models, without finding economic functionality
