# ELEMENTS OF LANDS AND THEIR APPLICATIONS IN BIOMIMMESIH

This study presents the chemical and mineralogical characteristics as well as the main deep seabed deposits such as phosphates of rare earth elements and their application to Biosynthesis, particularly in medicine, bionics and robotics. For this, TSEs are part of the need to have new sources of mineral resources with selective processes in which green chemistry and biotechnology are fundamental, to achieve and carry out sustainable and long-term mining operations
