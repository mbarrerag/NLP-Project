# Policy analysis of food labelling in Colombia as a tool for the implementation of the right to food.

The objective of this research was to determine how the existing normivity in Colombia on general and nutritional food labelling contributes to the protection of the Human Right to Food and Adequate Nutrition (DHANA). Recently, the implementation of renewed normivity related to frontal labelling of nutritional information in food has been discussed in Colombia; different bills have been established that raise the need to thoroughly review the relevance of the legislation in the light of the results of scientific evidence, the epidemiological profile of the country, the educational level of the population, and therefore, of its degree of understanding, under the perspective of progressiveness in the guarantee of the right to food. Through a mixed methodology of documentary review and field research, the legislative framework related to the topic, the object and field of application, the technical content, the administrative provisions and technical annexes of the regulation has led to the qualification of food and the right to food. On the other hand, semi-structured interviews were carried out from the institutions and from civil society with the aim of knowing their perceptions regarding the compliance of DHANA as it exists through the labelling of food
