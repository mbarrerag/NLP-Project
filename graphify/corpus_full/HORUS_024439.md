# METHODOLOGY BASED ON MULTICRITERIAL ANALYSIS FOR THE ASSESSMENT AND PRIORIZATION OF AREAS FOR METALOGENICAL RESEARCH

The process of selecting areas to advance metalogenic research has been advanced in the project Meta-genic Mapa de Colombia based on the analysis of variables such as geology, geophysics, geochemistry, metallurgical and social aspects.Although it is an organized and concerted process, it lacks systematicity and standardization in terms of the evaluation of the variables involved, being a mainly qualitative process. With the aim of quantifying and standardizing this process it is proposed to develop and implement a methodology based on multicriteria analysis and the hierarchical analytical method (AHP) to evaluate and prioritize areas for metallurgical research. The use of these techniques allows hierarchical and weightierate the variables to consider (geology, structural geology, tectonic, geochemistry, geophysics, metallurgy) from a high to a very low point of view, assigning them a weight consistent with their influence on the probability of occurrence of a certain type of mineralization and/or mineral deposit. The methodology proposed together with the spatial integration of these variables in a GIS will generate the map of areas discriminated in high to low priority ranges.
