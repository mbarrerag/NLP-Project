# A OFFLINE APP COULD IMPROVE THE SEXUAL HEALTH OF THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC COMMUNITIES

In one of Colombia’s poorest and most violent areas, a group of researchers used digital technologies to share sexual health information among young people in the Colombian Pacific. Their main objective is to generate health education strategies through something obvious, but rare: to listen to the community
