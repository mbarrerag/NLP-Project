# Policies to mitigate the impact of the entry of electric vehicles in the Colombian electricity sector.

In this work, a study of the impacts in the Colombian electricity sector due to the entry of electric vehicles is carried out, this type of studies are required due to the absence of a comprehensive policy around these. The objective is to propose strategies to mitigate these impacts in the country, one of the inputs for this is the identification of energy policies at the international level that can be implemented in Colombia. To achieve this, the work is articulated taking into account 4 fundamental aspects: type of charge of electric vehicles, demand for electricity in the Colombian system, energy market and energy policies; therefore, it is necessary to describe and understand these 4 topics in the national territory, compared to what is established internationally. In relation to the current state of charge of electric vehicles in Colombia, 3 levels of load are handled: slow load, semi-fast load and fast load; which handle different power values, so they require to be regulated to give reliability to the supply.
