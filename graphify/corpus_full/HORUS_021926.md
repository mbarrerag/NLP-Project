# Identification of stages to be optimized in biomass gasification for sustainable energy production through the application of life cycle exergetic analysis (LCA).

The method of Exergetic Analysis of Life Cycle (ELCA) is implemented, from cradle to door, to evaluate the use of coffee stems from the department of Caldas - Colombia, as fuel in a gasification process that uses fixed bed technology. This biomass has been used for generations as fuel in hand wood stoves is different coffee farms, a practice that has been gradually replaced due to the polluting nature of the gases generated and the harmful effects evidenced in health. The process of gasification of biomass was simulated in Aspen Plus® and in which, a composition of the gas synthesis was obtained according to experimental and theoretical results found in the literature and that includes H2 (17.07 per cent), CO (18.62 per cent), CO2 (15.66%), CH4 (5.37%) and N2 (43.28%) when it was used by heating of 6.119 kJ/Nm3.
