# Improvement and creation of laboratory practices for the Mechanical Solids subject as support in the teaching-learning process

The subject Mechanical in Solidos of the programs of Civil and Agricultural Engineering of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá, has a practical component that facilitates the learning of the linear and elastic behavior of deformable bodies submitted to different types of loads. This practical component is formed by nine laboratory tests, which are described in the text Guidelines of Laboratory of Solid Mechanics, published in 2022 by the same institution. A diagnosis of each of the laboratory practices related to the text mentioned above, identifying actions that lead to reduce uncertainty about six of them. Likewise, three new practices on topics not experimentally addressed in the course of Solids Mechanics were created. Thus, the devices used to measure displacement and of training were renewed, with the resources available in the Laboratory of Structures of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá. For all the experimental practices of developed laboratory were determined the mechanical properties of the materials of the test probadas, and the devices used to measure displacement and of training were implemented, and the experimental model of which are performed nine repetitions of each practice were compared. on topics not experimentally addressed in the Mechanicals of Solids course. In this way, the connection between theory and practice was strengthened, with the resources available at the Structures Laboratory of the National University of Colombia in Bogotá campus. For all the laboratory practices developed, the mechanical properties of the materials of the test species were determined, the services used to measure displacements and strategic were renewed, and an experimental model was implemented in which nine repeats of each practice were carried out.
