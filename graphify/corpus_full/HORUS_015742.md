# Videocapillaroscopic pattern in healthy volunteers from a Colombian population.

Capillaryoscopy is a non-invasive procedure that allows to evaluate capillaries in the ungular capillary bed, its main indication is the evaluation of Raynaud phenomenon in patients with suspicion of scleroderma and other rheumatological diseases, findings such as abnormal forms, number of decreased capillaries, areas of loss of capillaries, giant capillaries and microhemorrhagias indicate pathology of microcirculation and a scleroderma pattern. Previous studies describe these abnormalities in healthy subjects. There are no reports of findings in healthy population in Colombia. A descriptive cross-sectional morphology study was performed using a DinoLite® device to increase 200x in 101 healthy volunteers using the definitions of the EULAR group of microcirculation study. A capillary density of 8 capillaries/millimeter, 99.52% of capillaries had normal morphology, 0.48% abnormal.
