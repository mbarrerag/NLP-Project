# Design of an experimental facility for the study of the thermochemical conversion of residual biomass using fixed bed technology.

The design of a facility for the thermochemical use of residual biomass was addressed in this paper. The residual biomass with moisture content of less than 60% was selected, with greater energy potential and with availability for its thermochemical valorization in the experimental installation. Of the biomass with the greatest practical energy potential, the oil palm cousk and coffee husk were used for the design of the experimental installation. A one-dimensional model was developed for biomass gasification using reaction kinetics with H2O and CO2. The model was used to determine the biomass mass flow, reaction agent and products of the experimental installation through the simulation of different reactor geometries and operating conditions. The reactor design contemplated a diameter of 3 in with a total height of 1.16 m for which detailed engineering and a basic design for auxiliary systems was developed
