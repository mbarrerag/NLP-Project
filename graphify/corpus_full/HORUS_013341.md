The bi-bilitary sphingids or colibrí butterflies (Family: Sphingidae) are nocturnal moths recognized as one of the most important pollination vectors in nature, particularly in the Neotropical forests. In the world, there is recognition of approximately 1400 species, of which 408 have been reported in the Neotropics and 56 in the Amazon. In spite of the great diversity of this group in the world, and of the relevance of their intimate interaction with tropical plants, there is a great emptiness of knowledge about their ecology and the relationship with ecosystems in our country and in particular in the Colombian Amazon.
