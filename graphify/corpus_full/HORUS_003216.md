# TRAFFICTS IN THE CONTEMPORARY THEORY OF ARCHITECTURE IN LATIN AMERICA.

The theory of architecture today is redefined on the basis of contemporary problems of the world. The delimitation of the disciplinary autonomy, the answers to climatic imbalances, the interaction in exercises of community social order, the participation without discrimination of gender, age or ethnicity, the action of collective interdisciplinary and multigenerational groups, the immersion in digital environments, are posing challenges around the current formulation of the architectural theory. In the scenarios of the current Latin American intellectual discussion, questions arise: how are the proposals and advances of the theory of architecture in the continent considered? Is it possible to establish patterns or theoretical tendencies characteristic? Who are the theoreticians, the schools or the identifiable groups? What are the centers of thought where the theories are formulated? The notion of architecture, in the course of the twenty-first century, has been displaced from the closed, canonical, from the disciplinary autonomy, dilating its field of action, its scales, on environments, geographies, territories, landscapes, communities. Lameneutica, as method of interpretation of signs, has provided alternatives that are adapted to the particularities, its local and its local architecture
