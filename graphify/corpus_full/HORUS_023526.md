# CHAPTER 1 THE RESULTS OF AGRO-INDUSTRIAL CHANGES: SUBPRODUCTS AND WASTES OF AGRICULTURAL ACTIVITY

During the transformation of raw materials from agriculture into value-added products, waste and by-products (rys) are generated and often converted into waste, which are usually disposed of by garbage dumpers, without prior evaluation of their potential for exploitation. This chapter presents a vision of the generation of rys in the agro-industrial chain in a clean production model, with emphasis on two phases or stages: 1) harvesting or harvesting of raw materials, and 2) processing or processing of raw materials and valuing of residues as ingredients for the food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic industries, and animal feed. It highlights the issue of rys from the most representative agro-industrial chains in Colombia, describing the fruit and other plants, sugar cane, rice, corn, wheat, oil palm and coffee, emphasizing the use of these materials, not only for the generation of energy in the thermal stations of factories because of their calorific power, but in other important applications
