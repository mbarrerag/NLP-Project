# LATIN AMERICA IN WAR AGAINST DRUG: A MULTIDIMENSIONAL LOOK AT A GLOBAL PENOME

Fifty years after Richard Nixon’s famous declarations of June 17, 1971, the war on drugs in Latin America is a total social event with various manifestations in local, regional and national contexts. The analyses gathered in this book – produced by academics linked to study centers in Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, Germany, France and the United States – examine the place of the war on drugs in social and political interactions. The authors, without ignoring the historical and political weight of the United States in drug war issues in the region, focus their attention on Latin America.
