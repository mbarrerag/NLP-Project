# Methods of reducing the dimension of variables for the classification of sample expression data in unit cells.

The study of unit cell expression data has been growing in recent years given its great usefulness, since it allows understanding the functioning of biological systems at the molecular level. These data are very extensive in computer terms so it is important to use a method of reducing the appropriate dimension to interpret and visualize the information. Currently, there are several methods and algorithms that perform this work. However, they lack good results or strong theoretical support. By means of simulations, the most popular methods are compared, analyzing their strengths, weaknesses and limitations. A method of dimension reduction based on a mixed linear model is proposed, trying to capture all the important information for single cell RNA sequence data. In addition, a methodology is proposed that is particularly easy to implement, which allows highlighting the influential genes of a biological process. This methodology is implemented in oligodendrogliomas data, showing 3 metabolic pathways that can help to understand the cellular heterogeneity of this type of tissue
