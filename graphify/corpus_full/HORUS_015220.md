# HOW A COUNTRY IS ARMED GEOGRAPHS AND PEOPLE OF COLOMBIA

If there is an image that summarizes Colombia, it is the idea of diversity in singing to natural scenarios and social groups that inhabit it, which have created imaginary ones that point more to divergence than to unity, giving form to a national culture that is escapular in the midst of the richness of local expressions of all kinds. Despite all centrifugal cultural forces, beyond urban polycentrism, there is a unity represented in the State, centralist and unifying with its national symbols and rivers. In Colombia of clear polycentric tendency, institutions unite us above the plurality
