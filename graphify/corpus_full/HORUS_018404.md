# PREVENTION OF THE CONTAGIUM OF SAR-COV2 IN HEALTH WORKERS

The number of cases since its identification in December 2019 has increased rapidly to become a pandemic and to declare itself an international health emergency (Sarvepalli, 2020). Efforts to contain the ravages of this disease represent a challenge for the health systems of the affected countries, as the economic resources, availability of different treatment options, personal protection equipment and professionals are limited in the face of the demand for care (Sarvepalli, 2020; Woolley et al., 2020)
