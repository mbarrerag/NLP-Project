# INFLUENCE OF ENVIRONMENTAL FACTORS ON THE RICHANCE AND ABUNDANCE OF ADVICE IN THE VERDE SPACE OF THE AMAZONIC CITY OF FLORENCE, CAQUETA, COLOMBIA

In an increasingly urbanised world, urban green spaces are key sites for maintaining biodiversity in cities. In this study, we evaluate the relationship of the richness and abundance of the bird community with environmental variables in 18 green spaces of the Amazon city of Florence, Caquetá, Colombia. Within the environmental variables we choose the local characteristics of the sampling sites as well as the characteristics of the adjacent urban matrix. We recorded a total of 60 native bird species and an exotic species, Columba livia, which represented 20.2% of the total abundance. We found that the abundance of shrubs inside the green spaces had a positive relationship with the abundance of native birds. Also, the percentage of the woody vegetation coverage of the adjacent urban matrix had a positive association with the richness and abundance of native birds. On the other hand, the abundance of Columba livia was positively related to variables associated with urbanization, such as the percentage of waterproof vegetation. Based on our results, we propose some actions such as the increase of structural complexity of vegetation within the green spaces, through the sowing of native shrubs.
