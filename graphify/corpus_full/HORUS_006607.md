# Asbestos, the killer fiber

Asbestos is a group of six naturally occurring fibrous minerals that, due to their heat and corrosion resistance properties, has been used to produce a wide variety of products, mainly construction materials, friction products, thermoresistant textile materials, packaging and coatings. Exposure to this material represents a serious risk to health, as it can produce diseases such as asbestos, lung cancer and mesothelioma, among others. Thus, its use is increasingly regulated and has even been banned in about 70 countries
