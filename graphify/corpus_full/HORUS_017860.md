# INDICATORS OF A PALIATIVE MANAGEMENT PROGRAMME IN COLOMBIA: RETROSPECTIVE FOLLOW-UP

To compare the monitoring of palliative care indicators (CP) of a program implemented in Bogotá, Colombia, between March 2019 and February 2020. Descriptive, comparative, retrospective study developed based on the indicators of a CP program. The sample corresponded to a cohort of 391 people. They were considered indicators: frequency of hospital events, days of hospital stay, admissions to intensive care unit (ICU), emergency consultation, opioid consumption, frequency of use of professional services, pain, well-being, quality of life, satisfaction and use of financial resources. Less frequency of hospital events, use of intensive care, consultation to emergency services (p < 0.01), increased consumption of opioids in the last month of life (p < 0.01) and lower expenditure of financial resources (p < 0.01) were reported in users of the CP program compared to users susceptible to CP.
