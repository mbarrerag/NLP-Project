# Bioclimatic evaluation and optimization of the Non-Centrifugal Cane Sugar (NCS) Factory in Caparrapi, Colombia.

The production of panela in Colombia is an important economic and social factor for the 350,0000 peasant families that continue to operate in this activity. In Colombia, the implementation of industrial factories, called Central, has tried to improve the economy of workers who exercise these activities without technicalization. So far there are no bioclimatic studies applied to this type of agro-industrial facilities. Thus, the objective of this study was to develop a computational model that represented the real environment, in the specific case of the Caña Honeyhouse, in order to predict their environmental behavior under different scenarios. The results showed temperature levels suitable for the hours of 7 am and 9 am, but with high relative humidity, favoring the condensation of water vapour, dripping and the proliferation of fungi. At 2 pm, the results showed high temperature values, generating thermal stress in workers, due to the nature of the work activities in the Central. The alternatives proposed show an improvement of the Central environment, however, it is necessary to couple other measures to reduce thermal stress of the operators
