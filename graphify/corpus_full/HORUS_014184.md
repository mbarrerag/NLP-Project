# Geomechanical laboratory testing for sand production characterization using 3D-printed core analogues

Sanding, while detrimental due to causing equipment erosion and posing environmental hazards, offers benefits like enhanced fluid conductivity and increased productivity. It occurs under conditions such as mechanical failure, erosive forces, and adequate flow energy for sand transport. The triggers for sanding include drilling, cementing, completion practices, stress conditions, fluid invasion, formation weakening, pressure changes, depletion, water saturation, and cyclic processes. Any single factor may suffice to initiate sand production. Hence, comprehending sanding entails examining various physical mechanisms concerning the interaction between fluids and rocks under reservoir conditions. This article presents an innovative study focused on evaluating and understanding the effect on the sanding of the following components: stress state anisotropy vertical to horizontal, stress distribution around hole, and formation strength and weakening.
