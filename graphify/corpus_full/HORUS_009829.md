# ON VEECH GROUPS OF INFINITE SUPERELLIPTIC CURVES

Considering a meromorphic map $f:\mathbb{C}\to \widehat{\mathbb{C}}$ (called Weierstrass map) whose simple zeros are the points $z_{1},z_{2},\ldots$, which has associated to the infinite superelliptic curve $S(f):=\{(z,w)\in\mathbb{C}^2:f(z)=w^n\}$ with $n\geq 2$. With this setting, in this talk, we provide a characterization of the Veech group of $S(f)$ in terms of certain matrices in ${\rm GL}_{+}(2,\mathbb{R})$, which permute the zeros of the map $f$.
