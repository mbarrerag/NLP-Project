# The Problem of Works of Art and Artistic Creation in Virginia Woolf's Novels and Rainer's Poemaries Maria Rilke

In the following work I explain how Rainer on Maria Rilke and Virginia Woolf understand artistic creation from the formal characteristics and reflections on this subject that appear in their literary works. Research is an exercise of comparative literature, which puts into dialogue works written in different languages and different literary genres. In the first part of the thesis, I contrast the poetics of Al lighthouse and New poems, with emphasis on how the plastic arts influenced the development of these artistic proposals. In the second part, I analyze in a comparative way Between acts and Elegías de Duino, starting from the hypothesis that both seem to represent a questioning of the carefully ordered form around a characteristic centre of Al lighthouse and New poems.
