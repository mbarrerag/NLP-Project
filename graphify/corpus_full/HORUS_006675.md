# Tools for spatial-temporal analysis of climatic and spectral data as a basis for climate characterization and modelling and indirect estimation of productive parameters in avocado cv. Hass

The avocado cv. Hass has experienced a growth in the demand worldwide, which has generated an increase in the last years of the cultivated areas. This fruit tree from Colombia has been admitted in 18 countries of the world, so the area harvested has increased in recent years. However, phenomena such as water scarcity, variability and climate change and the presence and dispersion of pests have posed challenges for the establishment of a sustainable avocado agroindustry in our country. In this context, the main objective of this research work was to implement tools of spatial data analysis temporal for the climatic and spectral characterization of the areas producing avocado cv. Hass.
