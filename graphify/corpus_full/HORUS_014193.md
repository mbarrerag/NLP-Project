# Incorporation of game-based learning into courses in the System Engineering and Computer Engineering program.

Engineering education includes the training of professionals who use their ingenuity together with methods, techniques and tools to solve problems with a global and optimal look, focused on the preservation of life and the harmonious interaction of the human being with his environment [1]. This entails great challenges in this discipline, among which are highlighted: (1) solution of problems that afflict society for its systematic solution, (2) transformation of knowledge from the theoretical to the practical to provide society and (3) efficient and ethical use of the resources and means available with which it is counted for the solution of problems of the environment. In addition to these challenges in the training of engineers, another important element in the engineering programs at national and global level is the fact that the teaching-learning processes are mainly in charge of _x0093_ingenieros educativos_x0094_, that is to say engineering professionals who opt in their professional exercise by the teaching of universities, providing their experience and knowledge in the solution of problems specific to their disciplinary area, but do not have training in key subjects such as didactic and pedagogy in higher education [2]. It is our interest from this proposal to incorporate game-based learning as a didactic contribution to the process of teaching-learning in systems engineering, seeking benefit for: (1) students by incorporating into the classroom instruments that enable them to achieve meaningful learning in a playful environment and (2) teachers of software programming and engineering courses by providing them with a strategy for the use of games in their classes, as well as a repository of games to facilitate their incorporation and replication at the university level
