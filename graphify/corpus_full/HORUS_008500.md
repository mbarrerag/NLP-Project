# Who is social housing coming to in Bogotá 2004 – 2019?: the problem of access to the social housing supply in Bogotá

This work aims to identify the results of access to social housing by the families who demanded the right to housing in the city of Bogotá during the period from 2004 to 2019, within the framework of the Constitution and Colombian laws, national and district policies of financing and subsidy for the acquisition of housing of new social interest. . This work used qualitative and quantitative tools that involved the analysis of regulations, official documents and government programs, both at the national and district level, as well as the official information related to subsidies and social housing projects. The period of study covered from 2004 to 2019, since during this period significant regulations were established in the Territorial Planning Plan (POT) that influenced the generation of social housing supply. In addition, a strong impetus in the implementation of programs and projects of the POT during this period has not been achieved.
