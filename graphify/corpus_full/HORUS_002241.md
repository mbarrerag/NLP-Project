# Probabilistic multisector and multihazard climate-related disaster risk assessment as a tool for adaptation decision-making: application in Colombia

Nowadays, there is a growing need for multihazard risk assessment because not only the built environment but also critical sectors like agriculture and the natural environment can be affected by various hazards. Furthermore, the necessity to quantify the risk due to climate change requires expanding the catastrophe risk assessment methodology to incorporate deep uncertainty. This chapter presents the application of this enhanced methodology in a multihazard risk assessment for Colombia, encompassing tropical cyclones, floods, droughts, landslides, and wildfires across the country, while also incorporating climate change. The model is multisectoral, covering housing, infrastructure, crops, and ecosystem services, and expresses risk using the same catastrophe risk metrics. This integrated assessment contributed to the update of the National Risk Atlas and justified the risk management and adaptation investments included in Colombia’s long-term climate strategy (E2050) by applying the Risk Control Engineering methodology to optimize the implementation of risk management and adaptation actions
