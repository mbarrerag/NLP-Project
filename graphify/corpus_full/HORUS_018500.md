# PARTICIPATIVE CONSULTATION: THE GREAT CHALLENGES OF THE ANALYSIS OF NORMATIVE IMPACT AND ARTIFICIAL INTELIGENCE

To meditate on the quality of the standards is to reason about the quality of democracy that in turn implies reflecting on the quality of the consultation and the means to improve it and make it effective, reflection in which it is pertinent to determine: What opportunities for improvement exist in the Analysis of the Normative Impact that can be implemented through the use of cognitive technologies derived from Artificial Intelligence? What are the benefits of the implementation in real environments of Artificial Intelligence as a principle of good practice of governance and access to participation in the processes of Normative Impact Analysis?, questions against which it can be anticipated that Artificial Intelligence is a powerful instrument of participation in the Analysis of Normative Impact
