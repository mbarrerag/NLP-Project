# COMPARISON OF MULTITIVARIES OF STUDIES

The importance of the normal multivariate distribution lies in a number of pro-pity associated with it and that is used as a assumption in many statistical analyses. Nowadays, there is a lot of statistical evidence that allows to evaluate the multivariate normality of the data, however, there are not many studies that compare the test power of these tests. In this study Monte Carlo simulation was used for the comparison of two multivariate normality tests that are: MardiaDos test and Energy test, these tests were applied to a data set whose sample size is small that comes from a contaminated multivariate normal distribution, and as a comparison criterion the test power was used. For the study, data from a normal contaminated distribution with dimension 2, 3 and 5, sample size n = 10, 20, 30 and 40, and contamination level p = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4, every 500 times, were simulated to finally find the test power of the tests in question. From the study it was found that, in all the scenarios involved, the Energy test is more potent than Mardia's test
