# Method for classification of small-scale agricultural crops using deep learning techniques

Approximately 75% of the total agricultural area belongs to small farmers, being essential for local food supply. However, common challenges include the lack of precise characterization of crops and the limited detailed information in the productive areas. Intelligent Agriculture, which uses advanced technologies such as Non-Cropped Air Vehicles (VANTs) and computer vision, offers solutions; however, its lack of accessibility excludes 94% of small farmers in Colombia. This work addresses the need to propose a method of classification of small-scale agricultural crops using deep learning techniques. A DCI Mini 2 SE, accessible in the market, is used to capture images in San Cristobal, a rural area of Medellín, Colombia, with the objective of identifying green or branch onion crops, foliage and areas without cultivation.
