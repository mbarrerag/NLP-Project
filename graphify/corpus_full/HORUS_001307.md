# The Commission has also taken note of the information provided by the Commission in its communication on the implementation of the measures to combat fraud and to promote the free movement of workers.

The Program on Man and the Biosphere recognizes the natural richness of the archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina and declares it in 2000 Seapower Biosphere Reserve; later, in 2005, this is declared as a marine area protected by the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development. Between 2017 and 2018, within the framework of the Seapower scientific expeditions, crustacean coral remains and soft bottoms were collected. Representatives of 17 families were collected, on the island Cayos de Serranilla (ICS) and the island Cayos de Albuquerque (ICA). The results show a greater richness in ICA, with 46 species of 31 genera, while in ICS only recorded 37 species and 27 genera. In total, 67 morphoespecies of decapods are recorded in the two cayos, a contribution is made in the knowledge of 26 new registers for the Archipelago and six new ones for Colombia.
