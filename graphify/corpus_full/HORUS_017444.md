# THE MICROCREDIT: EFFECTS ON THE MICROEMPRESES OF THE BEAUTIFUL SECTOR OF BOGOTA

Microcredit was created as a tool to contribute to the economic development of people through the financing of productive projects with the objective of reducing poverty. To determine the effects of microcredit on microentrepreneurs to understand if it meets its purpose. The multiple case study was used for micro-enterprises in the beauty sector in Bogotá, applying semi-structured interviews. Access to micro-credit does not promote the growth of the company. Additionally, it is observed that assertiveness for the improvement in the economic situation of micro-enterprise with the funds obtained depends more on the good planning and use of the resources made by micro-entrepreneur. Micro-credit has many limitations due to the difficulty of its access, and requires that the entrepreneur have good planning and knowledge in financial matters
