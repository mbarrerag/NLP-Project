# STRUCTURE DESIGN FOR CIVIL ENGINEERS AND CONSTRUCTOR ARCHITECTS

In order to contribute, from teaching and research, to the efficient use of wood and aimed at all those who wish to design structures of wood and laminated wood Glulam, we propose this text that is based on recent research and norms. This book is concerned with presenting the steps required to properly calculate and dimension a straight or curved structural element of solid wood, of Glulam laminated wood or a wooden board derived from wood, requested in bending, in cutting, in torsion, in compression or in traction. As far as possible, the practical calculation is emphasized with various numerical examples with more complete details than that requires an inexperienced designer, is therefore encouraged his careful study. The book would be a guide text for students of civil engineering, construction and architecture and for civil engineers of structures and architects of wood structures and construction structures
