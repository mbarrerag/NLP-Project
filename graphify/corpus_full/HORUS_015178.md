# Criminal liability of legal persons in Colombia: effects to be considered from international experience.

Traditionally, the doctrine, law and jurisprudence in the criminal field have recognized the impossibility of penalizing legal persons, under the maximum criminal society, causing the absence of a system of full criminal responsibility for legal persons in countries such as Colombia. However, the conditions in the globalized world have generated new doctrinal, legislative and jurisprudential positions that consider legal persons to be capable of being responsible for serious violations of legal assets relevant to the administration of justice, which warrants a response from the most radical sanctioning structure, that is, criminal law, since the precariousness of administrative sanctions and the need to adopt criminal liability regimes applicable to non-natural persons have been noted. Thus, countries such as Chile and Peru have opted for the construction of own systems of criminal responsibility for legal persons that allow the development of the market and, in turn, the protection of the essential legal assets of society, adapting to the experiences of those territories
