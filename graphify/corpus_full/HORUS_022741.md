# MODELATION OF TOPICS FOR IDENTIFYING PATRONS IN THE SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH OF COVID-19

"We present a model of topics based on the method of latent assignment of Dirichlet (LDA), with the aim of examining patterns in the scientific research of Covid-19, taking into account the publications indexed in the database PubMed. 4928 scientific summaries published during the first half of 2020 are taken. An LDA model is adjusted using two topics. The first topic corresponds to risk factors, severity and mortality due to viral infection, while the second one to the impact of respiratory infections on public health. The proposed classification provides a global vision on the two research trends present to the date when the analysis takes place. Additionally, the results indicate that the application of the proposed methodological approach provides a way to address and make the bibliographic revision more efficient in the academic context."
