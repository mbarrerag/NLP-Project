# JULIATION, A CASE FOR RELATING FROM HEALTH AND SAFETY AT WORK

The book Advances in Research at the Faculty of Nursing is a publicity commitment in the framework of the celebration of the 100 years of the Nursing program of the Faculty of Nursing of the National University of Colombia. Its objective is to contribute to the visibility of the works of the research groups of a call made in 2019 and consolidated in 2020 and the social impact of these. As a result, the research experience with impact on scientific knowledge in the research groups was left. Ten of the groups participated in this opportunity through their researchers who are part of the teaching and student team of the Faculty of Nursing and graduates of the undergraduate and postgraduate programs (specializations, masters and doctorate)
