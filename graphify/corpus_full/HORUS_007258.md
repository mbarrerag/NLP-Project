# Transformation of social representations of environment into university students: Case study in a course of fundamentals of ecology

This work arises from the interest in generating educational alternatives that integrate environmental aspects to environmental education; at an instant in which it is essential to restore harmony between the human and the natural. The document develops a study on the changes in the social representations of the environment of the students in an interdisciplinary course of undergraduate studies of “Fundamentos de Ecología”. In addition, the approaches to environmental education of the participants of the subject are identified, as well as the realization of a descriptive study of the dynamics and problems of the Forest Protection Reserve Eastern Forest of Bogotá. The methodology allowed the implementation of workshops in a subject of undergraduate studies, which facilitated the reflection of the impacts generated by the human influence on the environment. The analysis of the different activities promotes the approach to environmental education, aligning itself with previous research and with the theoretical framework developed in this work.
