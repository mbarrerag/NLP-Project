# ECO-HYDROLOGY FUNCTIONING OF COLOMBIA PARARMS. HYDRICAL BALANCE, PERFORMANCE AND HYDRICAL REGULATION

Ecohydrology is a new science that integrates ecological and hydrological processes, in other times it is stopped. In general terms the hydrological functioning of ecosystems and function of its ecological dynamics, specifically of the vegetation and in this way explains its behavior against variables of climate, vegetation, soils and the anthropic activities is determined.During the years 2006 to 2019 a research program related to the ecohydrology of the moors, in the north of the Andes, with the objective of characterizing the ecohydrological functioning of the ecosystems of paramo and identifying and quantifying the variables that determine that operation was carried out.To this end all the variables of the water balance were measured in seven hydrographic basins located in five moors in Colombia (Guerrero, Belmira, Chingaza, Romerales-Berlin, and Chili-Barragán), as are the vertical and horizontal precipitation (or contributions of the fog), the interception of the precipitation by the moor vegetation, the spatial-temporal magnitudes of the soil humidity in each paramo and the flow. Likewise, the hydrophysical properties of the small, frequent and low intensity events), as well as the frequency of fog events, and the high humidity of the soils, almost permanently, are the determinant variables of the ecohydrological functioning of the wasteland ecosystems in Colombia, and responsible for their high water performance. This study contributes significantly to the understanding of the ecohydrological functioning of the wasteland ecosystems in Colombia and provides scientific evidence and information that support this operation
