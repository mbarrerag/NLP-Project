# WAYS IN THE TERRITORY OF THE GREAT CALLS

The article offers a historical review of the routes and roads in the Great Caldas. The roads are seen as a reference heritage, cultural and identity, so that historical processes such as the colonization of the Andean Central Range -which includes migrations and population-, as well as the expansion and consolidation of Colombian coffee-growing, among others. Thus, the authors analyze the beginnings and transformations of these routes to consider, in the framework of a process of modernization of the infrastructure, the construction of roads, railways, tunnels and air cables. They also develop a regional historical approach, with sources, which are subject to bibliographic review, and include official documents, chronicles, cartographies and travel accounts, among others
