# Variation in the diversity and abundance of medium and large mammals between two localities of the Colombian Middle Magdalena with different degree of transformation

The diversity of medium and large mammals was characterized in two localities, Cimitara and Yondó, of a transformed landscape of the Colombian Middle Magdalene. During the years 2016, 2017 and 2020, the area was visited to perform the samplings between January and May. The methods of phototrampeo and sampling by distances with linear transects were used. They were recorded in total 28 species. The diversity between localities was compared and the richness was estimated by fragment, the dissimilarity by pairs of fragment and the population density for Ateles hybridus, Cebus versicolor and Alouatta seniculus, to later relate these results to variables of the landscape previously measured. The richness of habitats, area and the presence of floodable areas were the variables that were related in a significant way to both wealth and dissimilarity. The results of the density of each species were compared to the relationship between localities and the difference was found in the density for A. hybrids in 2016 and 2020, and for C. versicolor in 2016. The density for A. hybrid and the relationship between the situation and the area of each species and the area. bus versicolor, and Alouatta seniculus were estimated, and then these results were related to previous measured landscape variables. Habitat richness, area, and the presence of flood zones were the variables significantly related to both richness and dissimilarity. The density results for each species were compared to verify if there were changes between locations, and significant differences were found in density for A. hybrids in the years 2016 and 2020, and for C. versicolor in 2016. Density for A. hybridus was positive related to area and isolation distance, and negative related to distance to the Serrania de San Lucas.
