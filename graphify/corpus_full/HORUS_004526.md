# CONVEGN: ASCOLTARE I TONI DELL'INCONSCIUM

Music is inanalizable.Music is real, impossible to reach/play.That's why it is impossible to play or interpret a musical work twice in the same way, just as it is impossible to hear the same thing twice, hence the clinic is that space where you only hear once what the subject says through his rhythms, in other moments it will be to listen to other rhythms
