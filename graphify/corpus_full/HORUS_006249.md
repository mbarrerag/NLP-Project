# Evaluation and antigenic analysis of field viruses as candidates for updating the serological diagnosis of Influenza in pigs in Colombia

Influenza A virus (VIA) in pigs causes respiratory infection and is associated with Porcino Respiratory Complex, generating economic and health impact. In Colombia, the limited information on the VIA in pigs has led to the use of international reference strains in serological diagnostic tests. The representativeness of these strains in the Colombian context is uncertain, which affects the accuracy of the national diagnosis. Therefore, the objective of this study was to characterize molecular and antigenically VIA pigs present in the country, to choose representative strains and to verify their usefulness as antigens in serological tests. Through approximations based on sequence and various molecular methodologies, 38 VIA from eight regions of the country, detected between 2008 – 2021. Seven viral candidates were chosen, which were evaluated by means of the proposed Hemaglutination Inhibition tests (HI).
