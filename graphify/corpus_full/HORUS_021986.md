# Teaching the mechanisms of evolutionary change using the game as a didactic strategy in seventh grade students.

The present report presents the design and implementation of a teaching strategy based on the game methodology to promote learning of the mechanisms of evolutionary change in seventh grade students. Thus, the games Do the evolution! and Evolution Jeopardy were applied in a group of 21 students at The Victoria School. Through the approach of action research, data were collected through the perception of students using a Likert scale and the application of a written test presented by two populations; the first, a group that studied the topic in a regular way; the second, a group that did the same, this time with the inclusion of the game as a complement and axis of teaching-learning. Analysis of the results showed that the game increases motivation in students, favors the learning process and is displayed as a significant learning experience in the classroom
