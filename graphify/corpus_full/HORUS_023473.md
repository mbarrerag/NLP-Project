# VACUNES IN THE ADOLECENT

One of the pillars of preventive medicine in adolescence is vaccination, and each visit to a health centre must be seen as an opportunity to update and complete the vaccination scheme. Vaccines must be offered to adolescents to prevent infections that have a high incidence or lethality during this stage of life, as well as to strengthen the schemes administered in early childhood and prevent diseases of the adult age. Vaccines contribute to reducing school absenteeism and to strengthening the health care of this population. Furthermore, maintaining high vaccination coverage in adolescents favours immunity from herds. The chapter presents aspects related to the epidemiology of immunopreventable diseases in adolescence, the main vaccines and the vaccination schedule in adolescents are presented
