# POTENTIAL BACTERIES FOR BIOMINERALIZATION PROCESSES BY MICP AND MIPP MECHANISMS

Biomineralisation consists of the secretion of metabolic products by microorganisms, which interact with metallic elements in the environment, to form minerals. This phenomenon has several applications, such as the strengthening of concrete, the production of nanoparticles or in processes of bioremediation of toxic metals. These mechanisms reduce the bioavailability of these elements in soils and waters, thus protecting human health. Among the processes of biomineralization by micro-organisms are MICP (Microbial Induced Carbonate Precipitation) and MIPP (Microbial Induced Feasibility Phosphate Precipitation). These consist, respectively, in the formation of carbonates and insoluble phosphates.
