# PRIORITISING RESPIRATORY SYNCYTIAL VIRUS PREVENTION IN LOW-INCOME AND MIDDLE-INCOME COUNTRIES

Respiratory syncytial virus is a major cause of lower respiratory tract infection morbidity and mortality in children globally, causing 3·2'3·6 million hospitalisations and more than 100 000 deaths annually in children younger than 5 years, 99% of which occur in low-income and middle-income countries (LMICs).1 Despite being identified 65 years ago, at the same time as poliovirus, there is no widely available and affordable preventive strategy for respiratory syncytial virus
