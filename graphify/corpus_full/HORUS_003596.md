# AIR-BASE ANALYSIS OF FOOD

Acidity in food can be determined by volumetric evaluation with a basic reagent, which is usually sodium hydroxide. The result is expressed as the percentage of the acid predominant in the material. As part of quality control analyses, the determination of acidity for dairy foods, juices (juices), fruit nectars, among others, is important
