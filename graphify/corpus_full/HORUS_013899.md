# Pedagogy, Territory, Memory and Reconciliation: experiences of peace in Putumayo.

Pedagogy, territory, memory and reconciliation: experiences of peace in Putumayo is a pilot project that seeks to contribute to the construction of reconciliation processes based on reflections and actions that contribute to the internal, spiritual and social healing of the territory, taking as a basis the pedagogization of the report of the Commission for the Clarification of the Truth, Coexistence and Non-Repetition: There is a future, if there is truth, in rural areas of Lower and Middle Putumayo. The pedagogization of the final report seeks to strengthen the organizations and key actors in the territory, in order to promote the joint dialogue that allows reflection and deepening in a culture of peace that has been promoted by reconciliation and the resolution of conflicts from peaceful solutions, in which the human rights and the life of those who live in Putumayo and the country are respected. Also, this project seeks to promote and make visible the processes of endogenous memory that have allowed the confrontation, re-signification and exigibility of guarantees for the non-repeakability of the forms of violences lived in the framework of armed conflict. position of Professor Patricia Sierra
