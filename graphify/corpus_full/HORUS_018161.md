# HOW ARE VAS AND BISHES RELATING?

The number of chicken and egg meats in Colombia has increased annually, becoming the most consumed animal protein in the country. This growth trend has been seen in the world for several years, which has boosted the search for innovations to make the production process more efficient. One of them has been the use of additives that promote growth in the diet of birds. In the 1940s it was discovered that the use of products from the fermentation of Streptomyces aureofaciens improved the development of birds for production. These discoveries joined others of the same type, where they found benefits in the use of small doses of antibiotics to animal feed. It was not until the 1970s when the use of antibiotics that were also human in use began to be regulated in European countries, and later in the 1990s, in the face of the imminent risk of inducing microbial resistance in animals and humans, the first prohibitions of these substances began. Currently, WHO considers antibiotic resistance as one of the main public health problems to be addressed, which has led to the prohibition of the use of antibiotic growth promoters in various countries
