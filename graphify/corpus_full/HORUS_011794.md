# SEPTEMBER 2021: THE ISLAMIC TRIUNF OF THE TALIBANS

The Taliban's victory in Afghanistan, after the departure of U.S. troops, results not only in major transformations on Afghan society, but also in a radical change in the geostrategic action of the powers with interest in Central Asia. Understanding the latest violence in Afghanistan involves going back to the origin of the Taliban phenomenon, to the political alliances that were created since before the Cold War, to the presence of Al Qaeda and to the intervention of the United States after the attacks of 11 September 2001. This chapter is closed, for the time being, with the failed attempt of the Western powers to transform a territory characterized by intertribal struggles, systemic corruption and the return of the Taliban to the power of a weak State, but with important international support that involve changes in world geopolitics
