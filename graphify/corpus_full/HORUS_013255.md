# Facial analysis of the closed lagoon system Laguna Seca, in the Paramo Parque Nacional Chingaza (Colombia) and its link to environmental variability

The Chingaza mánamo is part of one of the ecological and environmental systems exclusive of the Andean neotropical and intramontane zone. The Laguna Seca, is one of the inherited lagoon systems of the parame due to the events of the Late Pleistocene.The latter is an organic and ecologically neutral.The latter is an organic and ecologically neutral.The latter is a small source of input, allowing the conservation of sediment and a continuous record since the 16.329 years BP to the present.This study seeks to identify environmental conditions and changes related to the evolution of the climate system from a detailed facial description and the sum of mineralological, isotopic and geochemical analysis reported in Colombia for the Quaternary. The analysis thus shows that the sedimentological changes combined with their chemical traits are proxies that respond to environmental changes, being of great use for studies of the natural and inter-state of the mountains. ed us to identify 8 climatic zones under conditions of drain-humidity, cold and heat that mostly represent stadial and interstadial events of global size reported in Colombia for the Quaternary. In this way, it is new that sedimentological changes, together with their chemical paths, are proxies that respond to environmental changes, being very useful for paleoenvironmental studies of high mountain paramunas areas
