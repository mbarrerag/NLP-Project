# Composition of fatty acids in fruits of Ocotea caudata (Nees) Mez (Lauraceae) from Colombia

In the family of Lauraceae, the genus Ocotea presents species as sources of wood for construction, woodwork, biofuel, disinfection, cosmetics and for the treatment of various diseases. Ocotea caudata has few phytochemical and pharmacognostic studies: this work is the first to study the fruits of this species. The fruits of O. caudata (Nees) Mez of Colombia were investigated for their oil content. The extraction with heptane of the oil of the fruits of O. caudata yielded 54.9 % of total lipids and the composition of fatty acids was determined. The analysis of GC-FID (Gas Chromatography Flame Ionization Detector) of the oil indicated that the lauric acid is the most abundant fatty acid in the oil (51.7 %, C12). In addition to the lauric acid, the oleic acid (23.6 %, C18:1) and palmic acid can be considered the most important acid content in the oil (51.7 %, C12).
