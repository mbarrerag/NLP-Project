# Physical and chemical evaluation of guava germplasm fruits preserved in Agrosavia, Colombia

Abstract The guayabo (Psidium guajava) is a species of economic importance in different countries of the world, especially by the production of edible fruits, its high vitamin C content and its malleability for agroindustrial transformation. The Colombian Germoplasma Collection of Guayaba conserved in Agrosavia protects the variability of this species and some related, however, partial studies limiting the potential of its use. The objective was to evaluate and characterize the physical and chemical variables of the fruit in search of identifying accesses with attributes of agroindustrial interest. Twenty 29 descriptors (17 quantitative and 12 qualitative) were evaluated in fruits of the 41 accessions that make up the collection. The information obtained was subjected to an analysis of main components and multiple correspondence.
