# THE ART IN WAR. THE PLASTIC LOOK AT BELIC CONFLICT

Analysis of some works of visual or plastic whose content focuses on military, military and military actions, revealing that what is now understood by art has been present in human history since ancient times for different purposes: to exalt the values of war, to glorify heroes and to recognize victories as a documentary footprint, sometimes to express criticism, to denounce devastation and to show that one of its purposes is the humanization of the world
