# Small non-flying mammals of the Rio Blanco Reserve and contribution of localities for the identification of ecological patterns in the Colombian Andes

A list of species of small non-flying mammals (PMNV) is presented in three locations in the Central Colombian Andes between the 2500 and 3500 in the Rio Blanco reserve, Caldas, Colombia. In addition, a compilation of the studies of ecology and distribution of small mammals in the Colombian Andes is made in order to provide inputs for the future identification of patterns over different intervals
