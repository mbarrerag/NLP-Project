# The changing male voices: pedagogical, musical and identity perspectives on the Bogotaan choral scene

This document presents an analysis of choral work with male changing voices in the Bogotaan choral scene from three perspectives: pedagogical, which determines what is being done in the musical technical area with young people in the city; musical, where the work of choosing repertoires, characteristics and selection criteria is explored; identity, investigating the importance of creating subjectivities and identities, inclusion and new masculinities in choral activity. Through the analysis of five semi-structured interviews carried out to five choral directors of the city of Bogotá, analysis is carried out and preliminary diagnosis of the situation in the city with the aim of continuing research and contributing to the improvement of choral exercise with changing voices. .
