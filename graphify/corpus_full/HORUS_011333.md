# The Commission has also taken the view that the Commission is not in a position to adopt a decision on the application of Article 93(2) of the Treaty.

Cerebrovascular disease is one of the most relevant causes of morbidity and mortality in the world. Recently, it has been found that the activation of autophagy virus has a dual effect in acute cerebral ischemia scenarios; and it has been reported that liver X receptor agonists (LXR: from the groin, Liver X receptors) exert a neuroprotective effect associated with their ability to modulate autophagy
