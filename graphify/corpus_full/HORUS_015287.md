# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE ACUICO PRODUCTIVE SYSTEM IN THE MUNICIPLE OF CHIMICHAGUA, DEPARTMENT OF THE CESAR

The department of Cesar is recognized for its river wealth; however, the aquaculture production of the department is estimated between 2000 and 5000 tons/year, according to data released between 2015 and 2020. On the other hand, the National Agricultural Survey of 2019 indicates that in the Cesar there was a take-off of 393 upa that carry out aquaculture, thus positioning the department in the 15th place among the Colombian departments that carry out aquaculture. Currently there is no real information on the number of upa with aquaculture in the municipality of Chimichagua, however, since it is related to the Cenagoso de la Tesasa complex and identify that the artisanal fishing and aquaculture in the municipality are the livelihood of the population. Characterizing the aquaculture production system of the municipality of Chimichagua. For this description is a survey that is only the productive one year that is used in the year 2022 to 34 producers of the Cenagoso de la Tapa.
