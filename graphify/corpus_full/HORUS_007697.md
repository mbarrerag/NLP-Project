# Teaching of the Architectural Project. The Project of Formal Structures in the Open Workshop

The teaching of the architectural project is a subject that is subject of successive actions chained: a critique, a rereading and interpretation and a reformulation. The initial critique began from putting under the magnifying glass the general understanding that the teaching of the project is based on the transmission of the experience in the realization of projects of some teachers who taught a trade. The teaching based on professional practice, immediacy, the original technical solution, etc. The counterproposal aimed to address in a direct and first way the disciplinary questions of the architecture and the project that constitute its meaning, essence and autonomy. The teaching then passed from the resolution of problems and professional questions that burden the architects daily in their offices, from Palladio, to expand the reflection on the questions that make a project not only part of a tradition, but that is a way of knowing that it will then face and build a reality. One of the results is the teaching of projects based on reflection on formal structures. It is to address the fundamental problems of the project under a basic foundation but rather than to the circumstances and external coefficients (programme, form of the construction, form of the project is based on the design)
