# - Development of methods for the determination of elements of the platinum group, through the technique of atomic absorption spectrophotometry applied to the characterization of minerals established as strategic for the development of the Colombian energy mining industry.

Colombia, endowed with rich biodiversity and an abundance of natural resources, occupies an enviable position in the global context. Among its most valuable assets, strategic minerals, whose existence and exploitation are fundamental pillars for the economy and progress of the country. Within this group, platinum and its derivatives, such as palladium, rhodium, osmium, rutenium and iridium, play a crucial role in various industries and sectors, offering Colombia significant opportunities and facing challenges of equal magnitude. This importance of strategic minerals for national development is framed in the government policies outlined in the National Development Plan 2022-2026 "Colombia World Power of Life" and in the guidelines established by the Board of Directors of the National Mining Agency ANM through Agreement No. 01 of 2023. This vision anticipates the growing relevance that these minerals will acquire in the next decades, which has prompted the realization of studies to determine the metallic potential of the country.
