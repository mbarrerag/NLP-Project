# Analysis of the variation in the threat of flooding due to climate change

This research addresses the problem of floods in the Quebrada Olivares de Manizales through a prospective analysis of the variation of the threat, considering the effects of climate change. To achieve this objective, the performance of General Circulation Models contained in the CMIP5 in the emulation of historical climatic patterns of the basin. Through the generation of synthetic series of precipitation and temperature, together with the application of the technique of reduction of scale k-NN Bootstrapping, futures plausible in the basin were projected. These projections are essential for the evaluation of alternatives of adaptive planning in response to narratives of increase of rainfall in the region. In addition, the research included the evaluation of hydrological models and the generation of flow series, which allowed to determine the variation of the area prone to floods.
