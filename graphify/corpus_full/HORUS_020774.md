# GENDER AND ENVIRONMENTAL RELATIONSHIP AND ITS CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE FIELD OF MEMORY STUDIES

The references to memory and historical memory are abundant in recent literature of the social sciences. These terms refer to a field of studies developed in the last four decades focused on producing heterogeneous versions of the past, which seek to privilege the views of subordinate actors or victims of social and armed conflicts. In its effort to make visible the historical experience of contemporary subjectivities, the environmental has become a topic of interest. Therefore, the reflection presented here gathers elements that can integrate the studies of memory to deal with the interaction between human communities and the ecosystems that support them, based on the consideration of the environment from a gender perspective. This deliberation also arises from a field experience in which the environmental conflict by extraction of hydrocarbons was analyzed in the Lipa region, Arauca, Colombia, where it was found that oral history and testimonies were the main sources of information on the transformation of local ecosystems and the means of life of people
