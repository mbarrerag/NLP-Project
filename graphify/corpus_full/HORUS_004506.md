# MUTANT CORRESPONDENCES.

The expansion of the metaverse brings with it a sinumer of paradoxes that show from a heterogeneity in its own definition and practice, to the uses and appropriation of these digital ecosystems and their relationship with contemporary art practices. These experiences are inserted as a metaphomatic category to the incunatable, located in what some of them will have a period of time as the technosocial history of virtualization processes. As a phenomenon, the metaverse must be studied from a type of interpretative flexibility in coherence with the primary stage of development and expansion, integrating various actors that postulate different scopes and interests
