# Proposal for a market for complementary services in Colombia to facilitate the integration of large-scale renewable energy sources

The world's electricity markets encourage their energy policies and transitions towards the use of renewable energy sources such as wind and solar, however, given the variable and sometimes uncertain nature of these sources, it is necessary to adapt the power system in order to guarantee continuity of service at all times, which is possible through complementary energy services. This work proposal seeks to propose a complementary service market mechanism that responds to the operation of the Colombian interconnecting system with the large-scale integration of non-conventional wind and solar energy sources, which are subject to intermittance and uncertainty in their forecasts. .
