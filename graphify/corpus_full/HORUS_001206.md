# E-Sport OF THE PROFESSIONAL CONSTRUCTION PROGRAMMES. EVOLUTION OF THE PROFESSIONAL PROFILE OF THE CONSTRUCTION ARCHITECT OF THE NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA, MEDELLIN, 1968-2023

This chapter examines the evolution of the construction programme of the National University of Colombia, Medellín Headquarters, through three distinct historical stages: from its foundation in 1968 to 1992; the period of reform "Mockus" between 1992 and 2008; and the stage of permanent updating initiated during the rectory of Wasserman Lerner (2008-2012). The analysis focuses on curriculum transformations, with special attention to the professional profile of the Construction Architect, structure of the curriculum and its adaptation to the global context. It highlights how the principle of flexibility implemented in the last decade has provided more frequent updates, responding to contemporary demands. Research reveals the progressive consolidation of construction as an interdependent discipline in AECO (Architecture, Engineering, Construction and Operations), whose curriculum evolution reflects both the epistemological changes of discipline and the transformations of the socio-economic and technological context. This historical-curricular journey allows us to understand the foundations of the current program and project its future development in an increasingly complex and interconnected professional environment
