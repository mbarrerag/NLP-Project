# INTERNATIONAL SEMINAR ON INNOVATION IN UNIVERSITY DIDACTICS (SEIIDUN): SCIENTIFIC AND SOCIALLY RESPONSIBLE TEACHER TRAINING STRATEGY

In today’s world, it is necessary to reach more people with quality education, transforming teaching methodologies and the academic training of students and teachers. It is an opportunity to train teachers from science didactics with a rehumanizing and socially responsible profile. The International Seminar on Innovation in University Didactics (SEIIDUN) is proposed as a scenario for reflection, creativity and scientificity on university didactics. In its three versions, the concepts of didactics, evaluation, virtual education have been addressed and educational strategies have been shared in various disciplines, reaching more than 500 Latin American teachers and students. The application of the seminar as a strategy for reflection and qualification of teaching work has allowed significant changes that are reflected in the results evaluated by students, which account for the use of innovative teaching strategies in the classroom, in the evaluation, in the use of digital tools to promote synchronous and asynchronous meetings and the recognition and appreciation of the student as a person, as a human being
