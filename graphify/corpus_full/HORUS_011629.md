# STATUS OF THE ART OF RESEARCH IN DISABILITY AND PHYSICAL ACTIVITY IN SOUTH AMERICA.

From the understanding of the social/environmental partner model of disability, where this is conceptualized from the relation of the subject with the environment, is found the concept of adapted physical activity; a multidisciplinary field that from different perspectives seeks to achieve the full participation of persons in situations of disability in the practice of physical activity and sport, both from the promotion, as in the adaptation and modification necessary to achieve an active participation. The objective of this research was to carry out a review of the publications made in the last 10 years, faced with disability and physical activity related to the barriers and facilitators for its practice in persons with disabilities (PcD) in countries of South America, including Argentina, Brazil, Bolivia, Colombia, Chile, Ecuador, Peru, Paraguay, Uruguay and Venezuela. A narrative review was carried out in the databases EBSCO Host, Science Direct, Scielo, Scopus, Pubmed, Discoveror UNAL and Google Scholar.
