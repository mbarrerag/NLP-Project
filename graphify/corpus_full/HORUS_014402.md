# Performance of buildings in reinforced concrete porticos based on their nonlinear dynamic response to synthetic accelerations representative of the seismic threat.

This paper proposes a methodology developed based on the collection of existing techniques, which allows to determine the seismic performance of buildings in terms of damage and probability of collapse, with applicability in structures of portals resistant to reinforced moments in concrete. The procedure starts from representing seismic action by means of simulated synthetic acelerograms using a finite fault stochastic method, which are used to evaluate the nonlinear chronological response of buildings required in the calculation of total seismic damage, estimated by means of weighting of a cumulative local damage index. This work seeks to verify the fulfilment of the implicit objective of performance of the NSR-10 regulation, established as the protection of the life of occupants, projected through the prevention of structural collapse
