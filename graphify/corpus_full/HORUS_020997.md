# Peace in the perspective of women: analysis of the scope and challenges of the peace initiatives of FARC ex-combatants in rural and urban contexts.

The armed conflict in Colombia has been a complex and persistent phenomenon that has left its mark in the recent history of our country through various forms of victimization to civil society. However, Colombia has not only been marked by war but also by abundant efforts in favor of the construction of peace, since, as Muñoz describes (2005), in our country _x0093_the peace initiatives are innumerable, probably the most numerous, comparatively speaking, with any other country of the planet_x0094_ (p. 257), and have put as protagonist the civil society but also to recognize the former combatants who have initiated processes of reintegration.These peace initiatives are configured as spaces for a new enunciation of the former combatants within society in which there can be both ruptures and continuitys regarding their logics of life, what happens, among others, for the maintenance or transformation of the gender relations that marked their life in the context of war, and thus, the project seeks to analyze the scopes and challenges that are evidenced in the peace initiatives created, accompanied and/or modeled by the women of rural in the process of re-incorporation, in 2016
