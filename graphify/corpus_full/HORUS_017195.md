# AN EXISTENCE RESULT CONCERNING A SEMIPOSITONE QUASILINEAR PROBLEM IN A BALL

The aim of this talk is to present a new result in partial differential equations concerning the existence of at least one positive, radial solution for a semi-positone quasilinear problem. Indeed, we studied the homogeneous Dirichlet problem in the unit ball in $\mathbb{R}^N$ $N \geq 2$ associated to the partial differential equation $-\Delta_\phi u=\lambda f(u).$
