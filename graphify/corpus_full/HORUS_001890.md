# Environmental reconstruction of the territory: a proposal for action to mitigate climate change. Case: Amazonian Piedmont of Valle del Guamuez, Putumayo

Climate change is a phenomenon with which humanity must interact with greater sensatitude and urgency, since some human actions have led to a major climate crisis today. Its recent breakthrough was not able to ignore the consequences of short, medium and long term eco-systems and ecosystems existing on the planet, as well as the consequences for human societies. Thus, this work aims to be a demonstration of the possibilities that have to be had to reverse the effects of climate change through local actions that articulate ecosystem and pedagogical approaches, and that contribute to new proposals for an integrated management of the territory from an environmental perspective, to advance an Environmental Reconstruction of the Territory. It will then be done a qualitative exploration to know the conditions presented by the Amazonic Piedemonte in the region of the Guamuz Valley and the local experiences of environmental governance, territorial management and eco-alphabetation that can be applied in the area to avoid, mitigate and reverse the harmful effects of climate change in that region fundamental to the world balance. The conditions in which the exploration and exploitation of natural goods, territorial management and the importance of human resources are taken (as well as is the crisis and the only a means of control). that contribute to new strategies for the integral management of the territory from an environmental perspective, to advance, in an environmental reconstruction of the territory. Thus, a qualitative exploration will be carried out out to understand the conditions of the Amazonian foothills in the Guamuz region and the local experiences of environmental governance, territorial management, and eco-literacy that can be applied in the area to avoid, mitigate, and reverse the harmonious effects of climate change in this region fundamental for the global balance. There will be a review of the conditions under which the exploration and exploitation of natural resources are carried out in the region, as well as the import of conservation and control of various types of extractive activities
