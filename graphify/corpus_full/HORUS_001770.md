# Effect of geographical and taxonomic biases on the assessment of the extinction risk of species of the genus Grallaria (Birds: Grallaridae) of the Colombian Andes

The study applied the IUCN Criterion B to estimate the extent of presence (EOO) and the area of occupation (AOO) in six species of the genus Grallaria. Using species distribution models (SDM) based on occurrence records, geographical and environmental biases were identified that could affect the accuracy of the projections. To minimize these biases, the records were filtered and refined in three stages, and the impact on the prediction of the climate niche was assessed using the Maxent algorithm. The results showed variations in entropy and prevalence metrics among the data sets, suggesting that the geographical and environmental biases have a small effect on the prediction of the distributions. In addition, the taxonomic complex of the Toroi rufo (Grallaria rufous) was explored. The results highlighted the importance of the new species, five of them restricted to Colombia. The evaluation of these five new taxonomic entities showed that three of them should be classified as threatened (VU or EN).
