# EXPERIENCES OF TRAINING RESEARCH AS DIFFERENTIAL FACTOR IN ENGINEERING ERWES

The Special Academic Practice (PAE), is an educational commitment of the National University of Colombia that allows students to participate in training research activities, projects and event organization. This work analyzes and reflects on the impact of this strategy articulated to the training research experiences proposed from the Seed of Research in Instrumentation, Control and Robotics Syncro of the Department of Electrical and Automatic Energy of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia. Medellín, as a key and motivating differentiator in the continuation of formal research activities, or studies at the master's and/or doctoral level. To validate this hypothesis, a survey is applied to graduates and graduates of Electrical Engineering and Control Engineering linked to SINCRo in the period 2013-2021
