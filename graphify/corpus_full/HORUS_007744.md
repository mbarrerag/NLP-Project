# --From the graphic reporting as art: Luz Elena Castro and how photography begins to be art in Medellín (1980-2003)

This research studies the way in which the graphic reporting is entered into the art field of Medellín during the 1980s _x0096_ 2003 from the case of Luz Elena Castro. This photographer and artist worked as a graphic reporter of the newspaper the world during the beginning of the decade of the eighties and, according to the sources consulted, was the first press photographer to participate in an exhibition room in the city, opening the way for an artistic medium little explored by those years. It is then sought to draw a retrospective of the exhibitions, institutions, actors and facts that were relevant for such admission. First, a retrospective of the work of Luz Elena Castro in contrast to the social and political context of the country during the two decades included, establishing a relationship between his photographs and the context of the art that emerge.
