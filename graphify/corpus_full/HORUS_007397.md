# Economic diplomacy and trade relations between Colombia and Venezuela 2010-2023

This paper analyses economic diplomacy and trade relations between Colombia and Venezuela during the period 2010-2023, in a context of political and economic fluctuations. The main objective is to assess how both countries have used economic diplomacy to balance their political and economic goals, while examining the evolution of bilateral trade. A mixed approach is implemented, combining qualitative and quantitative analyses. The qualitative analysis examines the impact of diplomatic and political decisions on bi-national relations, while the quantitative focuses on economic and trade indicators. Trade between Colombia and Venezuela has gone through phases of growth and slowdown, with diplomatic crises affecting economic cooperation, marked by the breakdown of diplomatic relations and border closure. Since 2022, an economic recovery has been observed with the reopening of borders, the restoration of diplomatic relations and the increase in trade. Bilateral agreements in trade matters, as well as in transport, investment and integration, have been fundamental to facilitate this normalization and strengthen relations between the two countries.
