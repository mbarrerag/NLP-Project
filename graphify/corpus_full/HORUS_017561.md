# EFFECT OF OSMOTIC DEHYDRATION ON THE CONVECTIVE DRYING OF TOMATO CHERRY (SOLANUM LYCOPERSICUM VAR. CERASIFORME)

An osmotic dehydration process was used as a pretreatment of convective drying. During osmodehydration, the following properties were evaluated: moisture in initial dry basis (HbsDo), water activity (aw), effective diffusivity (Deff), loss of water (PADo) and gain of solids (GSDo). Results showed that the Deff is independent of the characteristics of the osmotic dehydration.
