# COMPARISON OF SOME ESTIMATORS OF THE SURVEY FUNCTION UNDER ALTERNATIVE CENSURA TASKS

In the area related to failure data it is essential to estimate the survival function S(t). However, censorship rates, especially high ones, could compromise quality in the estimation of that function. In this work, three known strategies of estimation of survival function under different censorship rates and different sample sizes are compared via simulation. Such comparison will be based on metrics such as the Middle Quadratic Error (MCC)
