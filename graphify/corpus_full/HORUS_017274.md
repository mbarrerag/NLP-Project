# Resignation of the gaps in the resolution of the road infrastructure of Avenida Longitunal de Occidente in the city of Bogotá: Puertas del Sol, Villa María and Gaitana neighbourhoods in the town of Suba

This final master's work (TFM) focuses on the urban voids resulting from the resolution of the road infrastructure of Avenida Longitudinal de Occidente in Bogotá, based on a multiscale analysis of the object of study, the aim is to establish strategies of re-signification from the discipline of urban design, with which urban fragmentation and discontinuity will be reduced by the road reserve zone. To this end, the identification of the problems of the vacuum under the analysis of three scales is carried out, normative, urban approaches are re-established and the results of a participatory process are incorporated for the formulation of a proposal for intervention at metropolitan, zonal and local scale through three possible scenarios, in order to restructure the void in an axis of activity and urban integration for the city. .
