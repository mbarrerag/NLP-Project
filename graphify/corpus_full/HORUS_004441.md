# Currency and banking in Antioquia - Colombia- late 19th century

Abstract In this article, some elaborations are approaching the process in historical development of the monetary and banking scenario of the Province of Antioquia at the end of the 19th century. It is made transit through the places of monetary scarcity, the issuance of currency and the complicated networks of individual capitalists, which would mark the path of monetary and banking policy. In this exhibition, to an abstract degree, the understanding of currency and banking in the case that is called, beyond the functional character, helps to stop in the tendencies as constitutive of social ties. This immediately refers to the relationship between the issuance of private notes and the fiscal bill. This does not emerge suddenly, but corresponds to a process of historical transformation of social production
