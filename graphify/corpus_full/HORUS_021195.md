# APPROACHES AND METHODOLOGIES FOR ECONOMIC ASSESSMENT AND ASSESSMENT OF MODES AND URBAN-METROPOLITIAN TRANSPORT SYSTEMS IN COLOMBIA. CONCEPTUAL, METHODOLOGICAL APPROXIMATIONS AND APPLICABLE IN BOGOTA AND MEDELLIN.

The research seeks to develop methodologies and models of valuation and economic evaluation of the implementation and operation of mass transport systems, as well as of road, urban and metropolitan modes of transport.A few case studies will be carried out for the cities of Bogotá and Medellín Bogotá and Medellín on three specific researches.The first will develop a multidimensional methodological model of economic evaluation for the Mass Transport Systems of Passengers in Bogotá (Transmilenio and its metropolitan connections) and Medellín (Metro de Medellín operating metropolitanally).The multidimensional part of the idea to incorporate and identify the direct and indirect benefits that manifest themselves in the form of urban, institutional and transport impacts throughout the operating horizon of the systems.For this, it is necessary to propose a methodological model that identifies and values the benefits on these dimensions, applied comparatively for the systems of the two cities, and that allows to contrast them and evaluate them against their costs of investment and operation.The identification of both direct and indirect benefits, is based on the multidimensional case, since traditionally the economic evaluation of projects of these transport systems is made on the direct benefits of the transport of the users, both in terms of time savings and the transport. The identification and estimation of these polluting emissions, as well as their economic assessment, is a fundamental step in order to have a better diagnosis of the problem of air pollution and its different impacts, but to advance the measures required to mitigate or reduce them. Additionally, the traffic and transit of these vehicles generate other types of negative effects (externalities) on society, as is congestion and siniestality. In the case of the metropolitan region of Bogotá, the vehicles of the freight road have a very important participation of this set of externalities. It is expected that the economic valuation of externalities as inputs for subsequent road mode assessments in metropolitan Bogotá will be developed.
