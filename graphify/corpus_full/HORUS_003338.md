# COMMUNICATIVE DISEASE REQUIREMENTS FOR RELATIONSHIP WITH THE PATIENT'S FAMILY IN CRITICAL STATE

During nursing care it is important to establish interpersonal relationships with the patient's family to ensure that there is harmony between nursing and the person of care, in order to improve the quality of care in special contexts such as the Intensive Care Unit (ICU); therefore it is necessary to recognize the relational needs that surround it. To identify the communicative needs of nursing in the ICU that allow it to interact harmoniously with the patient's family in critical condition. Development: An integrative review was carried out. Among the databases consulted were PubMed, SciELO, Scopus, ScienceDirect, Cochrane and Ovid. The search terms were interpersonal relationships, nursing, family, critical care, and needs assessment. The data were analyzed by a comparison and identification of main findings.
