# Detection, location and attachment of a medium voltage line by means of artificial vision techniques

This thesis presents a methodology for the detection, segmentation and attachment of medium voltage power lines compact, oriented to improve robot autonomy in maintenance and inspection tasks. Using an approach that combines depth and color thresholds for segmentation and location of lines, the proposed algorithm offers a specific solution for the identification of compact electrical lines in various environments, this approach is combined with the detection of contours to perform instance segmentation, which allows to discriminate each of the electrical lines. For the subjection the problem of path planning is addressed, first, estimating the position and orientation of the electrical line in space, then moving the robot through three 3D points that depend on the initial conditions of the robot, and the orientation and position of the line. The proposed algorithm for the detection and segmentation of the line turns out to be comparable in terms of precision and performance with that of YOLov8 nano. In addition, the efficiency of the algorithm for the estimation of the position of the line in a practical environment is evaluated by means of the precision of the proposed 3D context of the final effect of a robot, obtaining an error of 3 cm based on the 3 cm and the difference of the segmentation, which allows to discriminate each of the power lines.
