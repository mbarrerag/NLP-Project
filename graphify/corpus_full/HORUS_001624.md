# Subword count on restricted Dyck words

This work focuses on the road count of Dyck by analyzing its subwords. To do this, we explore concepts such as generatric functions and successions such as Fibonacci, Lucas and Catalan. In addition, recursions and counting methods are used to approach the symbolic method, which facilitates the basic count of these subwords on the roads of Dyck. Conte successions are validated by programming in Mathematica®. Subsequently, the paths are restricted from the heights of the vertices of their valleys, defining them as restricted Dyck. Finally, generatric functions are obtained for subwords of length 2 and 3, and the results are displayed through symbolic programming in Mathematica® .
