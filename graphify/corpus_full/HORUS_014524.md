# EXISTENCE AND UNIQUENESS OF SOLUTION FOR SPECIAL NONLOCAL DIFFUSION MODEL WITH NONLINEAR FLUX

Using the Banach Fixed Point Theorem, we show the existence and uniqueness of solution for the following nonlocal diffusion problem with Neumann conditions [Formula presented] in which, we consider for the boundary conditions the border of a special domain ∂Ω [Formula presented], instead of the complement used previously by other authors. With this, we show the calculations are reduced in a satisfactory way and therefore, we have a new model which is a complement of a previous work where the authors use a linear flux. In this new model, the flux in the border is considered as no lineal. Finally, a comparison principle is considered
