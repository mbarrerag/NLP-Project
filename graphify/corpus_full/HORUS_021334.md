# Geophysical Network of the National University of Colombia - RGUNAL (Monitoring of environmental variables in Border and Andean headquarters from the Geophysical Network of UNAL _x0096_ An interdisciplinary research effort of intergroup and intersed cooperation)

The proposal COMPLETE WITH TABLES AND FIGURAS IS APPROACHED IN PDF DOCUMENT (This platform has limitations regarding the extension of paragraphs and inclusion of figures and tables) The installed capacity in 2022 of the Seismological Network of the National University of Colombia (RSUNAL) is nine (9) seismic stations (three of them with equipment of magnetothuric - MT and greenhouse gases GHG: CO2 and CH4), which are located in the Andean region; in addition there is a tenth station of MT and GHG in the Island of Marambio, Antarctica. Thanks to the management with the industry and resources of internal calls of the UNAL, for the beginning of 2023 the network will expand to reach 24 multiparametric geophysical stations (systems, MT and GHG sensors), besides two stations in Antarctica (Marambio and La Esperanza). This instrumental network transmits unique data in real time to a UNAL server in Bogotá, with the possibility to generate digital mirrors in all the case of UNAL.
