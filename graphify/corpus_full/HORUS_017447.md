# CRAZLE OF DISEASE FRONT TO DELIUM IN INTENSIVE PEDIATRIC CARE UNITY: SCOPING REVIEW

The comprehensive management of delirium has different components and the nursing role vis-à-vis this can be diffuse. To identify in the literature available the non-pharmacological nursing care for children hospitalized in the Intensive Pediatric Care Unit that present delirium. A strategic search was carried out in Web Of Science, Medline, Science Direct, Scielo, Virtual Library in Health, LILACS and Open Grey using the terms ¿nursing care?, did child OR children?, did delirium?, and did Pediatric Intensive Care Unit?. The extraction and analysis of the data was given through a matrix. 12 articles were identified that met the inclusion criteria and were classified in 4 categories according to the main intervention developed in the study: Research approach, comfort interventions, integral interventions, and educational interventions. The discussion: Nursing care versus the delirium includes preventive or curative measures that depart from the patient and extend to its environment and its family. The nursing interventions can be considered as interrelated among them
