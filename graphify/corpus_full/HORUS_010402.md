# INDIO-ALUMINIUM NITRURO (INALN): THE SOLLAR FUTURE CELDES

The semiconductor ternal alloy of Indian and aluminium nitride (InxAl1-xN) is attracting increasing interest in industry, due to its potential applications in optoelectronic and photovoltaic devices. Due to this, research on this interesting material has increased in the last decade, especially for its use in future applications in solar cells. In this book we present the chemical, physical, structural, morphological, optical and electrical properties of this semiconductor. The main contribution is to use various strategies to obtain it at a lower cost with respect to the epitaxial methods used in solar cells multiunion with record world efficiency. For this purpose, the material was obtained both by magnetron sputtering Radio Frequency and by sputtering Direct Current; by using a single target of InAl and at room temperature. Likewise, amorphic, polycrystalline and highly crystalline films were synthesized, allowing high degree of versatility in the proposed methodology
