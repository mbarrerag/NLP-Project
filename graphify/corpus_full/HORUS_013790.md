# Peace of Conflict Observatory: Peace initiatives from and for ex-combatants

The present project aims to continue the study of the peace initiatives that have emerged in Colombia as mechanisms of resistance to the Colombian conflict and as a means of peace-building. Thus, this observatory focuses on the population of former FARC combatants and the current initiatives that they have built based on the scope, threats and challenges they face in an institutionality like the Colombian one that, while it has mechanisms for their reintegration and reintegration, still continues to problematize this population group. To this should add the importance of putting research, academic and scientific efforts in these collective bets that mark the search for and realization of a peace that, sometimes seems elusive, and sometimes seems within our reach
