# TRANSNATIONAL CRIMINAL POLICY IN COMBATING ORGANISED CRIME: TRANSFORMATIONS OF THE CRIMINAL PROCESS IN CONTEMPORARY SOCIETY

"Transnational Criminal Policy in the Fight against Organized Crime" is a detailed and profound work that explores the transformations of the criminal process in contemporary societies. This book, written by Estanislao Escalante, deepens the relationship between criminal policy and the punitive instrumentalization of the criminal process, focusing on the so-called `war against transnational organized crime. Through a transdisciplinary analysis, Escalante addresses concepts such as the integral system of criminal law, the transnationalization of criminal law and the challenges of criminal investigation in this context. The work stands out for its focus on the evolution of organized crime and its impact on the judicial systems of both Spain, Colombia and Ibero-America, offering a unique comparative perspective. With its rich narrative and critical analysis, this book is essential for those seeking to understand the complexities of criminal law and criminology in the current global scenario, marked by unprecedented challenges in the fight against increasingly sophisticated and transnational forms of crime
