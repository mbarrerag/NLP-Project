# Probabilistic stability analysis of planned slopes in the University Commune of Manizales, Colombia

The main objective of this study is to estimate the safety factors and the probability of failure to plan in the slopes of the University Commune of Manizales (Colombia). Statistical processing of the data collected during 14 years of geotechnical studies is carried out. The infinite slope equation is applied in a Monte Carlo simulation to analyze typical entry scenarios such as cohesion, friction angle, water level and inclination of strata with ash presence and full anthropic. In the simulation, 5000 iterations are generated for each variable, resulting in the representation of a tool for detecting conditions that generate instability in the slopes of the commune. It is concluded that for acceptable safety factors calculated by deterministic methods, high levels of threat can be found in function of probability of failure, so probabilistic analyses are considered conservative and useful in early detection of disasters
