# Time-consuming devices. Time-tested questions as merchandise

Apparatus for time loss is an artistic inquiry into the use of time. The project proposes a series of artistic interventions that questions patterns of time use looking for a complex relationship with it, taking into account the possible folds that may appear when directing attention to what happens when the possibility of a time of its own or leisure time arises from the dynamics of pleasure, as opposed to what happens when time is understood as a commodity exchanged for other goods, this responds to linear time and has a close relationship with the dynamics of work. A critique of what has been standardized to use time has allowed to play from the absurd and contemplative to propose gestures around the ways in which we consume time as a finite resource. .
