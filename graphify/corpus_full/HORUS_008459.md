# USE OF TECHNOLOGICAL TOOLS: A CHALLENGE FOR DISEASE EDUCATION

Highlights: ICTs are one of the important tools in the teaching-learning processes in nursing, therefore, it is necessary to strengthen the competencies in digital skills of both teachers and students. Conclination by the pandemic accelerated further the development and implementation in learning scenarios. The use of ICT alone does not guarantee teaching-learning processes, it is necessary to encourage critical thinking and self-regulation. The future in technological advances represents important challenges for the training of nursing professionals, which will require provision and preparation in different educational actors. Since 2004, the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) has emphasized the importance of incorporating information and communication technologies (ICT) into the different teaching-learning processes, as a result of their rapid and growing development, as they are considered relevant as pedagogical complements at different levels of training, especially in higher education. For their implementation, it is essential to have sufficient tools for strengthening digital skills, establishing learning results, as well as the interest and motivation of the different actors, mainly students and teachers1
