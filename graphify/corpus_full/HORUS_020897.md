# Rebel cities: Bogotá and Cali. Right to the city, urban agriculture and social organizations, between 2004 and 2021

The expansion, urban densification and accelerated population growth are characteristic phenomena of the big cities of the country, specifically of Bogotá and Cali. These phenomena have caused consequences in the cities associated to poverty, which causes violence, environmental conflicts, urban displacements and hunger problems in the face of the precariousness of the labor relations, among other factors. This situation is complemented by the pandemic produced by the COVID 19 and the last social outbreaks by different organizations and social groups. Faced with the problem of access to food that causes food insecurity associated with hunger, the response of the social, popular and community movements, expressed during the last years, with neighborhood and collective expressions in the peripheries of the cities in this specific case Bogotá and Cali, is the construction of urban gardens and community spaces where other values and new forms of life related to the right to an environmental and human city, prevails the common goods. The above supposes the germin of an alternative proposal of ecological and sustainable city, where the population establishes a struggle for the defense of the common goods and the good to live. This perspective of city is opposed to the model of the capitalist ordeal and the previous urban model
