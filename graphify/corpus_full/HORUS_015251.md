# DEVELOPMENT OF A NEW MATERIAL WITH EXTERNAL RECOVERY OF A MONTMORILLONITE TYPE ARCHILLA ON A BIOPOLIMIT ON THE ASSISTANCE OF AGROINDUSTRIAL WASTES FROM THE REGION OF THE CAFETERO AXE (CASSORA OF VALENCIA ORANJA), FOR POOTENTIAL USE IN FOOD PACK

The decarbonisation of the economy, the adaptation to the climate crisis, the use of renewable sources and the urgency of adopting the circular economy find in the replacement of non biodegradable polymers a great challenge.The production of pectin from the orange shell valence to develop biodegradable materials of food packaging is the central object of this work.However, the use of biopolymers has been limited due to the poor mechanical and barrier properties, these properties can be improved by revising the external part with nanocomposites, such as nonoarchillas. Modern instrumental tools will be used for the characterization of the hybrid biopolymer and thus investigate the physical and mechanical properties of the new bionanocomposites. The potential for migration of the new bionanocomposito is tested using four food simulants and the evaluation of the presence of non-indentally added substances in the head space (GCMS) and dissolution (LC-MS)
