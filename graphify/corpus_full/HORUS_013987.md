# Obtaining extrudable material based on polylactic acid obtained from cheese serum with additive manufacturing applications

The production of filaments for use in additive manufacturing technologies is expected to expand due to the current boom of the 3D printing market, with FFF methods being melted and by deposition being the most widely applied and used at present. According to the 3Dnatives website, it is estimated that the market of the 3D printing industry should grow from 255 million to 1189 million in 2017 to 2024. Currently, the most used materials include a wide range of polymers being one of the most used PLA that is generally obtained from renewable sources such as corn starch. However, it is important to note that this type of PLA requires large tracts of land for its production and that for its subsequent process of synthesis or extrusion of the filament it is necessary to add up to 40% by weight of additives that can be dangerous to people. It is for this reason that the present research project uses PLA obtained from whey and tries to use plasticizers compatible with the PLA in concentrations not exceeding 20% by weight, which are also the least dangerous when used at the time of application
