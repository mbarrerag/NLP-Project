# Development of a portable kit for the teaching of cinematics

An experimental low cost kit has been developed using 3D printing techniques and Arduino plates, with the purpose of serving as a didactic tool for teaching kinematics. The modular design of 3D printed tracks allows to carry out various experiments, including the uniform rectilinear movement, the rectilinear movement uniformly accelerated and the free fall. The kit also includes motion sensors and an LCD screen to capture and visualize data in each experience performed. During the development process, special attention has been paid to three fundamental criteria: the reliability of the experiments around their application in courses of the academic medium, the ease to reproduce each of the components inherent in the kit and finally, the safety presented by the kit for the realization of each experiment, this in order to comply with the standards established by the Ministry of National Education for laboratories. .
