# TIME FOR STRATEGIC DECISION: CASE OF STUDY IN THE TEXTILE SECTOR

The objective of this research was to make an organizational approach at the time of the strategic decision based on a case study in a textile company in the department of Antioquia (Colombia), making use of the interfunctional integration within the process of development of new product. To this end, a methodology based on the knowledge construction process was used, carried out in which the tools of interviewing, observation process and revision of documents of the company were applied. It was evidenced, from the case, that the phenomenon of the moment of decision, seen from the organizational approximation of development of new product, involves elements such as interfunctional integration, quasi-technological trajectory, encounters, decisions of exploration and exploitation, the event of constructor, agency, joint work, organizational guidelines, information and the environment. It is appropriate to replicate these results in other organizations and industries, in order to adjust and strengthen them in the development of this case
