# OPTICAL PROPERTIES AND CONDUCTIVITY OF BIASED GAAS QUANTUM DOTS

We report the intraband optical absorption influence on the tunneling currents in biased gallium arsenide quantum dots. The energy levels and tunneling times are calculated using a complex eigenvalue formalism through an axisymmetrical two-dimensional finite element method.
