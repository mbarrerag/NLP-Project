# STATUS OF RESEARCH IN ELE DURING THE LAST TWO DECADE

Spanish as a Foreign Language (ELE) has experienced significant growth in the last two decades. Although this growth has been reflected in research products such as the articles published in academic journals, there are no current and comprehensive studies that account for the state of art in this discipline. In this study, 1110 articles published in academic journals between 2000 and 2023 were organized in a Knowledge Organization System and analyzed in the Vantage Point tool in order to account for the state of research in ELE through its chronological evolution, its thematic axes and its places of knowledge production. The results show that the development of ELE has been mainly given by the carrying out of studies and publications in Spain, with a strong predilection by the report of classroom experiences and case studies. This research is presented as a framework for understanding not only the evolution and dynamics of ELE academic production, but, perhaps more importantly, to reflect and project lines of research that respond to the local and international research needs and interests of the field
