# ANALYSIS OF THE MECHANICAL DEEMPERY OF MINERAL INDUSTRIAL USE IN COMMUNITY MINING AREAS OF THE VALLEDUPAN MUNICIPE

The objective of this study is to show the analysis of the mechanical performance of minerals for industrial use in mining community areas of the municipality of Valledupar, by means of the physicochemical, thermal and mechanical characterization of the materials present on the active exploitation fronts of the area under study. It is proposed to perform physicochemical, thermal and mechanical characterizations to compare the process variables associated with the distribution of particle size, limits of consistency and plasticity, elemental chemical composition, mineralogical composition and variation of the cooking temperatures of the test elements, with respect to the mechanical resistances of clay materials elaborated from the field operating conditions and controlled operating conditions in laboratory. It is concluded that as the cooking temperature increases and some process variables of the materials under study are adjusted, a significant increase in mechanical resistances and a decrease in the percentages of humidity of the finished products produced is generated
