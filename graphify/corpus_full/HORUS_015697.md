# BOGOTÁ-CUNDINAMARK METROPOLITIAN REGION: CHALLENGES FOR HABITAT

The rapid and disarticulated population growth of Bogotá and its neighbouring municipalities has generated various problems of mobility, environment, territorial planning, provision of public services and housing. The implementation of plans and projects in the newly created Metropolitan Region Bogotá-Cundinamarca (rmbc) has as one of its purposes to develop planning of processes related to habitat. The objective of this chapter is to contribute to this discussion. To this end, it identifies the challenges for the construction of an equitable and sustainable habitat in the rmbc, by reviewing the concepts of the region, the analysis of the relationship between the inhabitants and the main ecological structure, as well as the habitat-habitation pair. The study concludes that municipalities must strengthen the relationships between them and jointly plan the model of land occupation to ensure a territorial balance and an adequate articulation between activities and functions. This, without deepening the sociospatial se¬gregation already existing, looking for alternatives for the construction of housing, which have good habitability conditions and allow establishing a model of territorial equity
