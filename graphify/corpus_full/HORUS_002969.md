# EQUINE BUILDING FORCE DURING AN ARATE WORK IN ANIMAL TRACTION TESTS.

This work presents the instrumentation of sensors applied to horse throw tests during a plowing day. A module of measurement of body temperature (TC) was used in two horses pulling from a plowing cart, in turn equipped with a set of monitoring equipment of traction force and depth of chisel, to establish the influence of the operating variables on the physiology of the animals as an indicator of effort. The project has the endorsement of the committee for animal experimentation of the National University (CICUA). A control team based on the Arduino Mega technology was used as the control center of the system, in turn, the depth of the plow tool (cincel) was measured by means of an ultrasonic sensor HC-SR04, with ability to measure distances from 2 to 450 cm; for the thrust force during the shooting work was implemented a S type load cell with load capacity up to 1000 kg; the CT of the echins was individually recorded in each animal using two sensors DS18B20; the storage of the data was generated each ESP32.
