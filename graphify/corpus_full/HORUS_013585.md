# CHANGES IN THE QUALITY OF AIR ASSOCIATED TO THE TRAFFIC AUTOMOTOR, DURING COVID-19 ISSULATION IN SANTIAGO DE CALI, COLOMBIA

The temporal and spatial variation of motor traffic in the urban area of the District of Santiago de Cali and its relationship with the concentration of particulate matter, were analyzed during the first half of 2020. The data of motor traffic shared by the Secretariat of Mobility of Cali and concentration data of PM2.5 and PM10 measured by the Air Quality Monitoring System of the environmental entity DAGMA were used. The record of vehicle flows in the city, especially of motorcycles and automobiles was evaluated. The time behavior of all variables was analyzed. Since the study period includes the first period of isolation due to the pandemic by COVID-19, the analysis of traffic variables and pollutants was carried out for three sub-periods called: pre-insulation, isolation and post-insulation. The decrease of PM up to 69% during the isolation period compared to the previous period. It was found that there are two points in the city that show greater traffic and that record the higher concentrations of particulate material, the first located in the center and another in the south of the city.
