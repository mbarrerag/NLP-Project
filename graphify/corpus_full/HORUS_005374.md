# THE IMAGES OF THE FINDING WITH NUKAK: A VISITIONAL TEST ON THE LAST INDIGENOUS PEOPLE CONTAINED IN COLOMBIAN AmazONIA

The Nukak are the last indigenous people contacted in the Colombian Amazon. From the first encounters, the photograph was present, without any inquiry being made so far about these images, their contexts and evaluations. The visual monitoring reveals that contact with the Nukak is prior to 1988 and that after each record there are particular intentions or evaluations. Amazonia; Nukak; contact; photography; representations
