# REFLECTIONS ON BORDERBORDER REFLECTIONS. CHANGES AND PERMANENCES TO STARTS OF THE 21st CENTURY

This paper derives from the kind invitation to participate in the Second National Symposium of regional and international seminal geography or Espaço do novissimo seculo XXI: to Amazônia no centro do mundo. The invitation required a discussion around three questions: How to conceptually define the borders and regions in contemporary times?; What current theories are closer to the complexity of borders and regions?; and How to think of borders and regions from a methodological point of view, in view of the production of geographical knowledge? Responding to these three questions, this text aims to offer a position regarding the theoretical challenges in reading the borders today and is structured in the following separate parts: an analysis of the contemporary nature of borders; an examination of the evolution of the treatment of the concept by seeking permanences and novelties; a methodological evaluation for the contemporary examination of borders; and, finally, the conclusions
