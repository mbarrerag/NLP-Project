# Ethical-political CONFIGURATIONS OF SOCIAL WORK IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF PEACE IN COLOMBIA. A LOOK AT THE TRAYECTORY OF THE DEPARTMENT OF SOCIAL WORK OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY (2011-2022)

The present research proposal aims to trace the experiences, theoretical postulates, ethical-political positions, pedagogical and methodological proposals developed by the Department of Social Work of the National University of Colombia, in relation to the construction of peace, memory and psychosocial work in the midst of the continuity of socio-political violence. This, through a dialogue with those and managers of these initiatives that have taken place since the educational, research and extension practices, seeking reflective exercises and the contribution to current and upcoming processes, from a critical interpretation of the experiences. For the analysis of these contributions, a systematization of the experiences related to the topics stated, which have taken place in the last 11 years (2011 - 2022) and which have been agencyd by professors, active students and graduates of the Department
