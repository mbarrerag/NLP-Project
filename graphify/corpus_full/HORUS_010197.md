# Mentoring relationships between generations fosters reciprocity, growth, and innovation

Mentoring is a relationship in which a mentor assists the mentee's professional and personal endeavors by sharing experiences and expertise. Mentoring can include psychological and social support, such as active listening, care for the individual, acceptance, and encouragement. In this chapter, we describe three mentor-mentee experiences that occurred in Colombia, as well as the lessons learned, difficulties encountered, and challenges that served as a foundation for those who began as seeds to bear a fruit today. The mentoring process that we use is innovative because it extends beyond the academic profile to the personal, influencing the lives of those involved and strengthening the bond and our individual and collective capacities. This informal mentoring is based on professional development in nursing, with the goal of contributing to nursing excellence and leadership while benefiting the entire team.
