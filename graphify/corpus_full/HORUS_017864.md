# GASMS MODELS AS A ENTIRE ALTERNATIVE TO IMPROVE THE PROCESS OF RECOVERY OF CHURCHING INSTRUMENTS WITH CHROMODUR

Surgical instruments are used in surgical interventions and must be resistant to corrosion and wear to avoid contamination. To improve the mechanical properties of surgical instruments, a surface modification can be made by covering them with some material. In the coating process, several factors intervene that affect the characteristics of interest and traditionally the classic linear regression model has been used to explore these relationships. The variables of interest are random variables and do not always follow the normal distribution that is the statistical distribution assumed in the classical linear regression model. Using an appropriate regression model to study the surgical instrument coating processes is important. In this article experimental data obtained from the process of coating with chromoduro with surgical stainless steel needle holders are used. Experimental data were analyzed using linear models to study the effect of exposure time, current density and temperature on the average thickness of the coating of the needle holder. The final model had gamma response and the significant variables were time and density. Using this model, mathematical expressions could be constructed to estimate the mean and variance of the average thickness
