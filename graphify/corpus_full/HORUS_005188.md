# EFFECTS OF THE REPUTATION OF THE CENTRAL COLOMBIA BANK ON INTEREST TASS

We analyze the effects of the reputation and communication of the Central Bank of Colombia on the forecasting errors of the interest rate of monetary policy. We implement a methodology that calculates the forecasting error through expectations of the financial market for horizons of 1, 3, 6, 9 and 12 months. Then we construct different communication indicators of the Central Bank to estimate their effects on the forecasting errors. Econometric estimates allowed us to identify that the reputation helps to reduce the forecasting errors, both in the short and long term. The monetary policy news reduces the errors for the horizons of 1, 3, 6 and 9 months. Finally, we find that the clarity of communication and the number of words in the minutes only matters to 1 and 3 months. The main policy recommendation aims to consolidate the reputation of monetary policy and improve the channels of communication in order to influence more strongly the long-term expectations
