# Barrio Abajo in Barranquilla, aesthetic and sociocultural aspects of its gentrification

This is a critical investigation from a historical and aesthetic perspective, which links social and cultural elements to account for the production of space in the Barrio Abajo, in Barranquilla. Through a dialogue established between documentary and ethnographic sources, the text reflects back thoughtfully throughout the history of the neighborhood: from the distant nineteenth century hamlet to the blast of the barranquiller cultural heritage in which they intend to convert it today. In the framework of the reflection, there are critical discussions around the way in which the neighborhood has historically been introduced into the city project that sets up Barranquilla, drawing attention to its high doses of inequality and exclusion. .
