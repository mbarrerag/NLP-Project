# Development of an automatic control system for a prototype of cyclic pressure-change dehydration

Coffee is one of the main export products in Colombia, with about 540,000 families dependent on its cultivation, representing 11% of the country's agricultural GDP in 2020 (Pazmiño-Arteaga & Ruíz-Márquez, 2023). The quality of coffee, influenced by its genetic variety, geographical conditions, agronomic management and post-harvest management, is crucial to maintain its competitive price in the international market (Parra-Colonado et al., 2020).One of the most influential processes in coffee quality is post-harvest, specifically in the drying process. The systems of drying by cyclical pressure changes, alternating vacuum cycles and atmospheric pressure, have been proposed as a solution to improve the quality of coffee. These systems minimize the exposure to high temperatures and improve the uniformity of the drying, avoiding deterioration that affect the final quality of the product (Peñuela-Martínez et al., 2020).
