# ASSESSMENT OF BIOESTIMULATION AND BIOAMOTION IN THE TREATMENT OF BORKS EMPLOYING BIOPILE

During oil extraction, storage and processing, oil sludges are generated as oil sludges, residues whose physical-chemical characteristics depend on their origin. The sludges originating from heavy crudes, present high density and viscosity, and a low API (American Petroleum Institute). The sludges are subjected to various processes (e.g., centrifugation) to recover oil of commercial interest, removing the lightest fractions of hydrocarbons (HCs).
