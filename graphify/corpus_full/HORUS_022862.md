# PHONOTIPICAL VARIABILITY OF NATIATIVE BATATA GENOTIPES (BATATAS L.) LAM.] WITH PIGMENTED RAICES

The sweet potato [Ipomoea sweet potatoes (L.) Lam.] is one of the most productive crops in the world, of American origin, identifying two genetic groups, one of which originated in the north of South America in the territory of Colombia (Muñoz-Rodríguez et al., 2018). Its roots are desirable because they are useful in strategies to mitigate food insecurity and prevent diseases caused by vitamin A deficiency, various types of cancers and diabetes, thanks to their functional characteristics, since they have contents of carotenoids, anthocyanins, protein, crude energy, raw fiber, phenolic compounds, tocopherols, sugars, minerals, among other compounds that make it appropriate to include in the diet, especially of people in vulnerable populations with little access to quality food. In Colombia, despite having a large territory in the main Center of diversity of the species, it is little known and used, especially those with functional characteristics associated with pigmentated roots
