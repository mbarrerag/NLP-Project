# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE IMMUNOHISTOCHEMICAL EXPRESSION OF PROTEINS RELATING TO SOMATIC PRONOSIS MUTATIONS IN MIELODISPLASIC NEOPLASIA IN BIOPESIA OF OUSE MEDULA

Myelodysplastic neoplasms (MDNs) are a series of clonal disorders of hematopoietic cells defined by cytopenia, dysplasia of myeloid lines and a series of genetic abnormalities characteristic (1). The behavior is very variable, so there are multiple scales for prognostic stratification (2). More recently, the inclusion of several somatic mutations in these prognostic scores (3-5). Immunohistochemistry (IHQ) is a relatively fast, reproducible method, widely available and has already been explored as an indirect marker of the presence of these mutations in MDS (6-8)
