# Environmental justice, water and tourism: an analysis based on the distribution of environmental burdens and benefits between the permanent inhabitants and the floating population in the Golden Triangle of the West of Antioquia.

The human being uses and consumes the goods and ecosystem services that nature offers, increasingly intensely; where the water, fundamental element for the life and socioeconomic development of human beings, does not escape this reality. However, this utilization is not carried out in a uniform way among the inhabitants of a territory. This research analyzes a problem related to water in the municipalities of Sopetrán, San Jerónimo and Santa Fe de Antioquia, from the perspective of environmental justice.
