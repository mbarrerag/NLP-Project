# Marketing Challenges since the Emergence of the covid-19 Pandemic

This new issue of Innovar is the continuation of the result of the call for a special issue “Marketing and consumer behavior during and in overcoming a pandemic”, whose purpose was to provide the academic community of the disciplinary field of marketing a space to disseminate their research results related to marketing and consumer behavior during and in overcoming a pandemic
