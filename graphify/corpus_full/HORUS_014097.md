# Evaluation of explants for the in vitro propagation of vanilla rivasii (orchidaceae), as a strategy for mass multiplication and conservation in biogeographical chocó

The aim is to address in vitro cultivation as a strategy for the conservation and sustainable use of the species Vanilla Rivasii, which is provided as a promising with a high commercial value for the department of Chocó. This project seeks to standardize the protocol of micropropagation by explants (seed, axilar y Apicales), the field phase will be carried out in the municipality of Bahia Solano (Rio Valle), the laboratory phase will be carried out in the facilities of the Plant Biotechnology laboratory of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín. The experimental design will consist of a simple factorial arrangement, where two factors will be established: Factor 1 will include three disinfection methodologies and Factor 2 will include two types of explants. Experimental units will be distributed completely at random in the trial. This propagation route can provide options to mitigate the loss of this species, increasing the availability of seeding material and in the near future be able to establish plots in the hands of farmers which would help as a development strategy of the same communities that today are used as a source of income food, handicraft, gastronomy, and gastronomy among others
