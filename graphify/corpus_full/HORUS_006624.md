# Development of an optimal dimensional synthesis technique of flat mechanisms with rotational joints for motion generation

The dimensional synthesis of mechanisms is the first step in mechanical design, after the appropriate choice of mechanism in type synthesis. Dimensional optimization is defined from the initial design of the mechanism, the technique that is addressed for the initial design of the mechanism is the graphical synthesis by coupling curves as mentioned (R. L. Norton, 2011). This final mastery work focuses on the development of a computational technique for obtaining the optimal dimensional synthesis of mechanisms with rotational joints, applying an efficient mathematical formulation. The development starts with a systematic bibliographic search of the existing methodologies that apply to the synthesis of flat mechanisms. Subsequently, the software methodology for obtaining the algorithm design is applied; this is achieved by defining the client's requirements and giving more weight to the most relevant ones. Then, the individual architecture for each requirement is defined by uniting them in a single general architecture and bringing the algorithm to a process of codification and implementation in Matlab. Finally, two case studies are evaluated in the applied computational technique: the first case study is academic and consists of a mechanism of four bars that employs the objective ten positions for the generation of motion, and the analysis. on the development of a computational technique for obtaining optimal dimension synthesis of plant mechanisms with rotational joints, applying a efficient mathematical formulation that allows the addition of various parameters such as constraints, multi-objective functions, weight functions, together others.
