# The International Criminal Court: the inclusion of Ecocide as the fifth category of crime

The following work aims to analyze, not only the inclusion of ecocide as the fifth category of crime within the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court, but also to analyze the possible paradigm shift of extending criminal sanctions — which are now individual — towards the responsibility of societies such as national and international corporations and multinationals. That is, it seeks to move from an individual criminal sanction for those who cause serious damage to the environment — inefficient so far — to the criminal linkage of societies and corporations at both national and international levels
