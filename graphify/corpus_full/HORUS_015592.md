# MODEL OF BIRNBAUM-SAUNDERS RETURN IN THE PRESENCE OF NON-PARAM ́ETRIC EFFECTS AND NON-INFORMATIVE CENSURE

The main objective of this work is to provide a more flexible version of the regression modelLog-Birnbaum-Saunders under the presence of random and non-informational censorship, where it is assumed that the effects of some explanatory variables are nonlinear but their functional form is unknown.The flexibility provided by the model proposed lies in its ability to describe themedian distribution of life time by means of a set of covariates using a sum of arbitrary functions, whose functional form is estimated from data (observations), thus reducing the possibility of specification error, which allows for the estimation of parameters of robust form under the presence of extremes or outliers, including non-informative censored observations
