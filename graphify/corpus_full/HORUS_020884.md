# The National Learning Service (SENA) and training for work in the constitution of a National System of Public Tertiary Education in Colombia

SENA and training for work have been the basis of a government educational policy that prioritizes the massification of higher education at low cost and the adjustment of the education system with the objectives of development and economic competitiveness. This has resulted in the reproduction of persistent social inequalities in the sector and the lack of precision and conceptual ambiguity with which higher education modalities and institutions are valued. Therefore, the objective of the research is to evaluate the role of SENA in the framework of the constitution of a Public Tertiary Education System in Colombia, in order to resolve the ambiguity and lack of precision with which this institution has been incorporated into the Colombian higher education system. In this sense, the Research Group on Public Policies of Higher Education assumes the challenge of presenting a research consultation with the different actors in the sector for the formation of a National System of Higher Public Education integral, articulated and differentiated, guaranteeing quality and not reproducing the inequalities that characterize the field of higher education today. As a result of this academic exercise, it is expected to produce an article of research based on the collection and analysis of results of official and scientific data, the primary information and the documentary evidence of the public institutions
