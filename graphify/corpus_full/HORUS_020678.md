# CHARACTERIZATION OF ALTERNATIVE MINERALS IN DOMS AND PYROCLASTIC DEPOSITS IN DOMES AND PYROCLASTIC DEPOSITS OF PAIPA GEOTHRMAL AREA, COLOMBIA

The hydrothermal alterations are indicators of the interaction of hot fluids with the mineral components of the rocks that are altered, rebalanced and transformed into new sets of minerals whose formation temperatures are known; the study of the alteration minerals allows to identify active and fossil hydrothermal systems being a crucial input in the characterization of the areas with potential to use the heat stored in the rocks. In Colombia, the Geothermal Area of Paipa (AGP), in the department of Boyacá, is conceived as one of the most promising areas, and the present research also provides details on the mineralogy of alterations.
