# Identification of beta-lactam resistant bacteria in agricultural irrigation waters in Ramada, Cundinamarca.

This work was initially based on the physicochemical and microbiological characterization of agricultural water in the western area of the Cundinamarca Sabana, known as irrigation district the Ramada, where parameters were measured as pH, conductivity, temperature, dissolved oxygen (OD), chemical oxygen demand (DQO), biological oxygen demand (DBO5), total organic carbon (COT) and total suspended solids (S.S.T.) and microbiological parameters as total Coliform Total and Escherichia coli concentration were analyzed. The samplings were carried out at six different points of the Ramada, where the water collected was analyzed in the laboratory of Environmental Engineering of the National University of Colombia. It is worth mentioning that the microbiological parameters in the water samples were analyzed by two microbiological techniques; the first to establish a total population count (NMP/100mL) and to compare the results with the Colombian legislation in force (Decreto 1594 of 1984) to determine if it is suitable for agricultural irrigation, and finally the technique of membrane filtration (UFC/100 mL) to obtain the isolated colonies of bacteria. Similarly, the counts of bacteria such as E. coli were greater than 5000 NMP Log/100mL at site 4 and site 6 in both seasons.
