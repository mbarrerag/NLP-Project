# Health and safety at work in time of COVID-19

In 2019, when the first cases of the disease caused by SARS-CoV-2 were reported in China, the world thought that this would be something temporary and that it would not transcend the borders of Wuhan, a population where the virus originated. However, in October 2021, the pandemic has been among us for more than a year, has caused 4,899,169 deaths, and has changed the way of interacting, living and working of people. The difficulties inherent in a pandemic that just started and the fact of having little information about its origin, treatment and prevention affected especially the workers of the health sector. However, the panorama was gradually clarified, thanks to scientific research, confinement and certain guidelines emanated from the World Health Organization that, among others, insisted on the need to preserve the social distance, the constant hand washing and the use of tapabocas to prevent the contagion.
