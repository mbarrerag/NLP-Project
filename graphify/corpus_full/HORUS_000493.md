# Social determinants of the retardation in the size of children under the age of twelve of the indigenous indigenous council inga José Homero of the Municipality of Mocoa - Department of Putumayo

Social determinants of the retardation in size in children under 12 years old from the indigenous council Inga José Homero de la municipio de Mocoa - department of Putumayo. Characterize and analyze the social determinants identified by the community to explain the problem of the retardation in size of children under 12 years old from the indigenous council Inga José Homero de Mocoa, which contribute to proposing alternatives to overcome this problem in this community. A cross-sectional study was developed with mixed approach (quantitative and qualitative). From the quantitative approach was carried out the application of an instrument of information collection and the taking of anthropometric measures. From the qualitative approach, an in-depth interview was applied, which allowed to investigate and know the needs and problems of the population subject to study. The results obtained show a prevalence of retardation in height in children under 5 years of 15.4%, in those 5 to 11 years old, the delay in height was not identified, but if a risk to this of 32%.
