# ESTIMATION OF THE COST OF THE EQUITY FOR A FOOD ENTERPRISE: A VISION FROM THE VALUE OF PORTFOLIO

The cost estimate of equity has become a necessity for the investor by providing funds to a company, as well as for project appraisal and business decision making. The importance of this indicator drives the conduct of research that provide results that are realistically adjusted. In this context, the objective of this work seeks to innovate, applying portfolio management tools to determine the cost of own resources (Ke). To this end, a contrast is proposed between the Capital Asset Pricing Model International (ICAPM) and the implementation of the Black-Litterman model (BL). For the latter, the investor's expectations are incorporated considering three scenarios (optimist, moderate and pessimistic). Empirical evidence and the application of the Treynor index suggest that the rates estimated through Black-Litterman approach the market results, where the investor seeks the combination that maximizes profitability and minimizes risk
