# REVIEW OF THE MAIN THEORIC AND METHODOLOGICAL APPROACHES OF ENVIRONMENTAL GOVERNANCE AND THEIR CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE STUDY AND CONSERVATION OF THE SILVESTRE FAUNA

The in situ conservation of biodiversity is one of the main challenges that Colombia and the world currently have and is carried out with strategies that emerge from the local territories and that can be analyzed from the perspective of environmental governance (GA), understood as a modern approach that recognizes that the responsibility of conservation does not rest only on the government and its institutions, that at the local level, there is a network of actors that includes public, private, NGO, academic institutions and local communities, who autonomously or collaboratively also develop own conservation strategies. Precisely, the aim of this paper is to review theoretical and methodological approaches of the GA and its contributions to the study and conservation of wildlife through the secondary review and discussions with experts on the subject.
