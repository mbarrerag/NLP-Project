# Accounting practices of student chazas at the National University

This research seeks, starting from the realization of three monographs of degree, the consolidation of an exercise of memory of the daily work of the people linked to the processes of production and management of the chazas of the National University of Colombia Sede Bogotá. The proposal is aimed to make use of a methodology of mixed character that allows the elaboration of the following products: 1) report of characterization of the chazas in 2023; 2) a comic book; 3) photographic gallery. To this end it will be used: surveys and tools of geographical reference, diaries of field (for the elaboration of an ethnography) and focal groups (for the realization of workshops of photography). Students who will carry out their undergraduate work will be responsible for directing each of the stages of the research process under the coordination of a member of the recently graduated seeder of the sociology program and the supervision of the teacher who will lead the study. Likewise this research expects to be the opportunity to link four students of pregradation to research and to the semi-dlerant, to participate in the exercise of design and application of the different methodological instruments proposed, under the coordination of the forms of the last semesters
