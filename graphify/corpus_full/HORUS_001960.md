# Preconceptional experiences and care of traditional doctors and midwives Nasa from the indigenous reservation of Toribío, Cauca, Colombia

This research analyzes pre-conceptive care from the perspective of traditional care and midwives Nasa del Resguardo Indígena de Toribío, Cauca, Colombia, Colombia, under an intercultural approach. With a qualitative design, semi-structured interviews and a focal group with ancestral experts selected by chain sampling were conducted. The study focused on a territory with 5,961 inhabitants, where 50% of pregnancies are not planned and geographical and socioeconomic barriers persist to access Western health services. The results showed that Nasa pre-conceptual care integrates physical, spiritual and community dimensions, highlighting the importance of ancestral rituals and knowledge to balance the uterus and prevent complications. Traditional food, based on arracacha, banana and local proteins, aligns with key nutrients (iron, folic acid) recognized by biomedicine. However, young people underestimate these knowledge due to external influences, aggravating "disarmonies" reproductive.
