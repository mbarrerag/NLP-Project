# Variables of impact on the strategic outlook and knowledge management of public higher education institutions in the city of Manizales/Colombia.

Every organization, in search of optimizing processes and obtaining better results in the long term, implements different strategies in order that the fulfillment of its objectives is feasible, and that this entails lower risks. Concepts how knowledge management and strategic foresight promote the scope of achievement within the organizational systems. Based on this, this study was developed where, from validating the viability of these, perceived from the administrative staff of the Institutions of Higher Education -IES- of the city of Manizales, explicitly the University of Caldas, the Higher School of Public Administration and the National University of Colombia, different factors were established as the bureaucracy and economic limiting factors that directly affect the healthy development of these two strategic concepts. Taking as a basis a total of 580 possible participants, 195 of them belonging to the University of Caldas, 298 to the National University of Colombia and the remaining 87 belonging to the School of Higher Public Administration, all these cataloged as collaborators or administrative staff of these IES, selected randomly and of which 132 of these participated freely from the method of collection of selected information, with which each mechanism of careful validation of application of qualitative methodologies and ultimately establishes the results
