# Early adverse neonatal detachments associated with maternal colonization by Streptococo of group B in an institution of III level in Bogotá, 2019.

The strep of group B is the main causal microorganism of early neonatal sepsis. Intrapartum antibiotic prophylaxis based on rectal-vaginal screening of pregnant women is considered an effective strategy to prevent neonatal sepsis. The aim of this study is to determine the incidence of early adverse neonatal outcomes in children of mothers with GBS. Likewise, to estimate the prevalence of colonization by microorganism, adherence to screening and recommendations for intrapartum antibiotic prophylaxis according to the national guide
