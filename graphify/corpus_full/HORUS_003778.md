# LGBTIQ+ PERSONS IN THE SIGNATURES OF ACCOUNTING AND AUDIT IN COLOMBIA, PERCEPTIONS AND THEIR LABOUR

At present, the lack of attention to the subject of sexuality and gender in accounting research contributes to the reproduction of the notion that accounting firms are asexual places. Therefore, the study focuses on analyzing the perception of homosexual employees on aspects related to the management of their sexual identity in accounting firms in Colombia. The study is based on a qualitative methodology based on the conduct of a survey of 11 LGBTIQ+ accountants; however, all participants are homosexual men, it is not possible to find lesbian, transsexual, queer, transgender or intersexual accountants, who are undoubtedly present in the Colombian accounting work scenario, which is already a finding. The results show that perceptions are diverse at the personal, professional level and in the work context, most were focused on the persistence of barriers to the display of non-heterosexual identities as they are still considered taboo in many contexts, especially in traditional fields, such as the context of signatures. However, it is concluded that a strong movement against heteronormaticness in accounting is being generated, since, even today, there are some accountants who feel comfortable in their sexuality
