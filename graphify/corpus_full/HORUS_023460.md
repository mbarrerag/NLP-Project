# CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF THE ESSENTIAL TYPE OF PHILIFOLY TAGETES RECOLECTED IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The yield of extraction of essential oil was 0.11% (p/p). Twelve compounds (relative area = 0.1%) were identified. The main components found in the AE were stragol (19.8%) and transanetol (74.3%). The majority compound, trans-anetol, differs from the AE obtained from the same plant cultivated in Peru, where cis-anetol (68.2%) was reported as the main compound (3)
