# THE TRAVEL WORK OF THE BOGOTAH SABANA INDIANS IN THE CONTEXT OF COLONIZATION (SEGUNDITY OF THE XVIth AND FIRST YEARS OF THE XVIIth CENTURY)

The Sabana de Bogotá was transformed throughout the pre-Hispanic agricultural period (Herrera, Muisca Temprano and Muisca Tardío) by the construction of a hydraulic system of elevated fields and channels for cultivation, in order to transform a floodable space into a dynamic territory, seat for the moment of the Spanish conquest of the Zupazgo Muisca of Bogotá. The colonization process transformed this form of territoriality construction, based on water management, by introducing a different form of land tenure and by favouring other forms of production, based on the livestock and cereal cultivation. In this sense, the present presentation aims to show how the territory changed in the Sabana from the perspective of the muiscas, in the first half of the XVI century and early years of the XVII century, taking as a axis of the analysis the agricultural system of high fields of cultivation or cellones. It seeks to understand to what extent these ancient agricultural practices survived, of what way and in what spaces of the Sabana, in order to integrate into the new reality of colonization
