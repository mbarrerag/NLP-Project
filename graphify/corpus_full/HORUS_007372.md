# Freight trip generation analyses based on online sales in emerging economies

The study focuses on the impact of e-commerce on the generation of cargo travel in emerging economies. The main objective of the research is to predict the attraction of cargo travel derived from online sales, specifically in the context of electronic commerce B2C. For the generation of models of attraction of cargo travel, demographic and economic partner factors are analyzed, obtained through a survey conducted in a Google Form form that sought to collect data in the metropolitan area of the Aburra Valley. To estimate the attraction of cargo travel, the study developed linear regression models with the method of ordinary squares and a geospatial analysis of the data was carried out with interpolation methods available in geographic information systems (GIS). The most relevant finding of the models obtained is the relationship between the number of trips attracted and the characteristic of high revenues in the consumer. The results of the geospatial analysis showed the most attractive of cargo travel in the case of study, showing that they are the municipalities with the best socioeconomic behavior and that are in the area.
