# What to do with drug trafficking and illicit drugs in Colombia? Methodological elements for a State policy

The author, based on a re-issue of previous works, proposes methodological elements to achieve a democratic policy that will deal more lucidly with drug trafficking in Colombia. The author considers that it is necessary to distinguish three different but interrelated problems: the impact of drug trafficking on Colombian violence and democracy, the discussion on Colombia's prohibitionism and the degree of autonomy against the international regime of control of psychoactive substances. Based on these distinctions, the author concludes that Colombian policy against drug trafficking should seek both alternatives to prohibition (because the prohibition is an unjust and ineffective regime) and alternatives within the prohibition (since this regime will be maintained in the medium term, at least for drugs such as marijuana or cocaine)
