# DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION IN THE AULA FOR RISK MANAGEMENT IN PATIENT SAFETY: A PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE OF AUDIT IN NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The study presents an academic experience in time of pandemic COVID - 2019 in which a digital transformation was carried out for the teaching of risk management in patient safety., built with the ADDIE instructional model and application in the first semester of 2021. Sw intended to determine if with a Virtual Object of Learning (OVA) students identify the safety conditions in a fictitious hospital institution (Clínica Las Américas) and offer an improvement plan in terms of guaranteeing the quality of healthcare. 66 nursing students enrolled in the second academic period of 2020 in the Management in Health and Nursing course of the National University of Colombia, who - ́by conditions of the pandemic - continued to be enrolled in the subject for the first semester of 2021. The results indicate that the OVA designed with which the students performed a virtual audit of the institutional conditions, records of the clinical history and review of structural elements, process and result according to the administrative theory.With the qualitative perspective of the study was identified the reflection of the participants on the importance of the patient safety and the nursing professional role in the hospital environment.
