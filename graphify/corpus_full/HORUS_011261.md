# NUCLEAR-SYMPLE NUCLEAR POLYMORFISMS IN HORMONS ASSOCIATED TO MUSCULATE GROWTH IN COLOMBIAN CRILOL OVINS

Objective Summary. To determine Polymorphisms of Simple Nucleotide (SNPs) present in the genes of Growth Hormone (HC) and Growth Factor similar to In-sulin 1 (IGF-1) and its association with muscle growth in Colombian Creole hair sheep. A population of 100 sheep was selected, from three different regions: Interandin Valleys, Piedmont Llanero and department of Cordoba, subjected to different production systems. The identification of polymorphisms in genes was performed by means of the techniques of Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR) and of the Polymorphism Conformational Chain Sencilla (SSCP). The genotypes AA, AB and BB were identified for these genes.
