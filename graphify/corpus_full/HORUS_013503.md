# Spatial analysis model as an intervention tool for the comprehensive management of Acacia decurrens in the Tominé reservoir

It is of particular importance to optimize the comprehensive management of the colonization of A. decurrencies in the Unladen Operational Area (AOI) of the Tominé reservoir, since the broad knowledge of the ecology and dynamics of the invasion of this species, allows to clearly determine the factors that affect its expansion, which, in turn, determine the spatial distribution of the event and provide the ability to model the natural phenomenon in order to simulate it.
