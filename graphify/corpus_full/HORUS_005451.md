# NOVO REGISTRATION OF GUNNERO PUCCINIALS (PUCCINIALS) ON FUCHSIA HARTWEGII (ONAGRACEAE) NA COLÔMBIA

The family Onagraceae in Colombia records 59 species, among them Fuchsia hartwegii Benth. as native shrubby species registered in the low montane humid forests (bh-MB) of the Andes, in an altitude range, with pulsation between 1850-3200 m.s.l.l. native and introduced plants are parasitized by fungi, among them the Pucciniales (roys), which are among the most important plant pathogens.The plant material parasitated by a rust was collected in the department of Putumayo and was deposited in the Museo Micologico of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín-MMUNM without being identified the host and the rust that parasite it.The aim of this study is to present the first register of the genus Pucciniastrum on the software of Onagraceae, in Colombia. The sample was revised with the help of a stereomicrocroscope Carl Zeis Stemi 305, in order to find the spe of the rust present in the material and to perform the micropreparates in the laciliae, in the laciliae. Bosas, 17-28 x 12-20 μm; equalized wall, less than or equal to 2,5 μm of uniform thickness, hyalin; dark germination pores. Spermogonium, Ecium and Tellium not found in the material studied. In Colombia, the family Onagraceae was registered as the species Aecidium fuchsiae H. S. jackson & holway on fuchsia putumayensis munz and fuchsia sp.; for this reason, the rust P. circaeaeae is considered a new registry for the Funga of Pucciniales of Colombia
