# THE SUPPORT AULA AS A REVELATOR OF THE CHANGE OF EDUCATIONAL INTEGRATION TO INCLUSIVE EDUCATION IN COLOMBIA

This chapter proposes to analyze the transit of the language of "educational integration" to that of "inclusive education" through observation and semi-structured interviews about the device "Support classroom" and the newly installed figure of "Supporting profession". In language we want to mean a semantic code whose axis depends in a certain way on designing the fight against discrimination in school. In this sense, in the international context, two languages are detected to refer to the fight against discrimination in school, which are based on perspectives of analysis of different reality and which present different models of intervention. In the local context, in Colombia, both languages are mixed and do not appear as sufficiently differentiated references, derived from the international debate. With the notions of educational integration and inclusive education, does it seem that it is being spoken from different positions. The expression "educational integration" refers to a strategy that allows children and adolescents with special educational needs (NEE) to enter into regular education, without suffering any form of discrimination based on their limitations, and through the recognition and exercise of their rights as individuals and as citizens
