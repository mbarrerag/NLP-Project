# Socioeconomics Laboratory

The Socioeconomic Laboratory, as a research seedbed of the Research Group in Socioeconomics Institutions and Development, starts on the basis of its predecessors: The seedbed of Popular Economy, the Seed of Territory and the Seed of Macroeconomy Heterodox and Financialization (the latter, winner of the III meeting of semilleros of research organized by FENADECO). These seedbeds have sought to respond to the demands of the conjuncture of their moment, and also to serve as a discussion and study group regarding different approaches to the teaching of the areas that have composed their interdisciplinarity. Today, the Laboratory of Socioeconomics has functioned as the articulation of the and the members of the semilleros conformed inside the GSEID, in a non-institutional way thanks to the commitment of its students, graduates and researchers, who maintain a constant work around the political economy, public policy and fiscal (education, pensions, tax), the popular economy and rural socioeconomic forums.
