# Negative asymptotes in delay discuding and pattern-setting

This thesis goes into deepening the concept of self-control and behavioural patterns to evaluate the validity of a new mathematical model and the pattern establishment program proposed by Rachlin (2016) in four studies. The first two studies are with smokers, and the last two with people who do not do physical activity but would like to start. The sample for each study was 48, 2, 6 and 8 participants in order. Studies 1 and 3 evaluated the mathematical model to describe discount patterns where the immediate value is positive and the delayed negative value and increase patterns where the immediate value is negative and the delayed positive value. Studies 2 and 4 evaluated the pattern establishment program to reduce cigarette consumption and increase physical activity. The participants of studies 1 and 3 assigned values from -100 to 100 to consume a certain amount of cigarettes (1), or to perform some amount of physical activity (3), after some delay.
