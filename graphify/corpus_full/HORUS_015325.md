# Penitentiary system in Colombia

A brief overview of the recent history of the Colombian penitentiary and prison system is followed by an examination of the following points: recent history of Colombian prisons and legislation for Aboriginal population; the current structure of the Colombian penitentiary and prison system, including the number and type of facilities; structural and functional problems of the penitentiary system in Colombia; general procedures for criminal and labour assessment of the penitentiary population; the Social Support Network strategy; and penitentiary treatment programmes
