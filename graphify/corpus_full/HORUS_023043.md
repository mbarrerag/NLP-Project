# Study of the bio-precitation of cation by isolated ureolites of phincas of Santander, Colombia

Cadmium(II) is a toxic cation that produces serious health problems and bioaccumulates in cocoa seeds. This represents a problem for cocoa exporters in Santander (Colombia), since some soils in the area have high concentrations of metal, and should meet the limits of cadmium(II) in cocoa products imposed by the European Union in Regulation 1881 of 2006. Recently, the use of ureolytic bacteria by the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms (MICP, by its acronym Microbiologically Induced Carbonate Precipitation) as a measure to mitigate the availability of cadmium(II) in contaminated soils has been proposed. Thus, the objective of this research work is to isolate and characterize ureolithic bacteria resistant to cadmium(II) from cocoa soils in Santander, with potential to be used in metal mitigation applications on soils.
