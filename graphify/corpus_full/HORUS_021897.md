# Freedom and conscientious objection: legal, conceptual and contextual parameters aimed at maximizing their recognition and supranational and national protection.

This paper condenses the main approaches and results derived from the research proposal whose central axis investigates the legal, conceptual and contextual parameters that are in line with the purpose of maximizing the recognition and effectiveness of the rights to freedom of conscience and conscientious objection in the national and inter-American spheres. Taking as a reference the main doctrinal aspects, the normative and jurisprudential developments in the national legal system and the European and inter-American systems of protection of human rights, as well as the sources of interpretation more widespread in the orbit of the International Law of Human Rights, the central elements were auscultated around the essential content, the concept and the foundation of the rights mentioned, as well as the emblematic cases, assumptions and critical points in the recognition of the freedom of conscience and the objection of conscience.
