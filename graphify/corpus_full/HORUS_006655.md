# Analysis of the determinants that facilitate the acceptance and use of particular electric vehicles from the perspective of the automotive sector in Bogotá D.C.

At the global level, the environmental sector has gained relevance due to the effects of climate change becoming a concern for the states, since the ecosystems depend largely on the actions that can be developed with commitments such as the 2030 Agenda and the Paris Agreement. The transport sector with its traditional sources of supply is one of the most polluting when issuing toxic gases. By identifying this problem, it has sought alternatives that will reduce this negative environmental impact, being the energy transition a representative option that incorporates sustainable mobility with electrification. Colombia has adopted the global targets for the year 2030, so since 2013 has been incorporated this new technology in Bogotá D.C. being a long-term project that has been strengthened with measures such as the Law 1964 of 11 July 2019 marking a milestone in the electric mobility, promoting the acquisition of this type of vehicles. In relation, the present study analyzes the main determinants that would facilitate the acceptance and use of particular electric vehicles in Bogotá D.C., in order to find the recommendations that should be improved; it focuses on the automotive sector as a key actor of transformation and on the possibility of diversifying this market, using the qualitative approach, using the electric vehicles. d lience scholarship of climate change, coming a concern for the States, since ecosystems dependent widely on the actions that can be developed with commitments such as 2030 Agenda and Paris Agreement.
