# STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS CRITERIA FOR BUILDING BUILDING COMPUTER PROGRAMMES

This paper aims to show some typical examples that have been collected when reviewing the computational memories of different structural projects, so it is intended to present them to make known some erroneous criteria when performing structural models. Analysis of some computational memories has been carried out and cases have been identified as misuse of the concept of rigid diaphragm, confusion when using finite elements, confusion between the use of a column or wall element, misuse of elements for slabs and way of transmitting vertical loads. Technology has facilitated the execution of analysis and design procedures, as well as virtual simulations that allow structural engineers to visualize and analyze the characteristics of a project before its construction in an appropriate way. This helps to optimize designs and identify possible problems or improvements, saving time and resources. However, if these tools are not used in an efficient way, they will generate designs that are usually on the side of insecurity
