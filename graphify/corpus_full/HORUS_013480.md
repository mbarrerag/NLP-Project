# Paraprofessional women trained in psychological first aid in community intervention on gender-based violence and sexual violence

This research sought to create a record that would enable women leaders/social activists to be trained in the care of persons presumed to be victims of gender violence and sexual violence, through the application of techniques of psychological first aid with a gender and feminist approach. For this purpose, 3 moments of validation of the protocol were carried out. A pedagogical strategy was developed through Feminist Popular Education. The adaptation of the instruments of incomplete phrases and the Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI), were also created instruments of registers to meet the objectives. The Research Proposal from the systematization of the experience proposed by Oscar Jara (2018) aimed at describing and interpreting the results. Changes in the learning and attitude were evidenced by the participants. Finally, the aim was to carry out a training protocol as a product of this research .
