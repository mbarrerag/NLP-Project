# TERRITORIALIZATION TO TRANSFORT HEALTH IN PANDEMIA AND POSPANDEMIA

A contribution is presented for the reflection and construction of programmatic alternatives in this field, more than since the recommendation of an expert, we analyze first the accumulated problems that has evidenced the pandemic of the disease of the coronavirus (coronavirus disease 2019, covid-19) in the Colombian case, from an explanatory analysis that allows, in line with this, to construct some proposals for the period of postpandemia, but beginning already, in times of pandemic
