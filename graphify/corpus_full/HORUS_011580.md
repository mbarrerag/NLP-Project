# REUMATHOLOGY LABORATORY

The rheumatology laboratory is important because it helps diagnose rheumatological diseases, monitors the activity and response to treatment, and allows for prognosis. This chapter reviews the most commonly used laboratory tests in the study of patients suspected of rheumatologic disease and is grouped into general, immunological and special tests
