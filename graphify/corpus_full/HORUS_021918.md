# Development of a non-linear predictive control strategy and estimation of unknown entries with applications to biomedical processes.

The nonlinear systems represent, in many cases, an ideal in the field of control because they have the ability to represent with high delicacy the dynamics present in systems own of different fields; however, a difficult dynamic when it comes to accessing the benefits and details provided taking into account the complexity associated with these models. Now, to access these benefits, a common practice has been the linearization of these systems. This simple process is the design, but at the same time causes loss of properties or characteristics of the nonlinear systems, besides involving the limitation of the range of operation depending on the accuracy of the approximation. To solve these disadvantages, the present thesis proposes control schemes in MPC nonlinear so that the benefits associated with the realization of approximations in each step of time are accessed to the patients.
