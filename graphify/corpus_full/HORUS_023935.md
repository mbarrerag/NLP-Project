# DIFFERENTIAL OPERATION STUDIES OF EXIM TEST ITEMS

The differential functioning of the items affects the validity of the tests, while the probability of response depends on different variables to have the attribute being measured. In this sense, psychometry has studied the subject and suggested a series of procedures to find DIF in standardized tests. From the above, the work presents the DIF study carried out for each of the components of the basic engineering science examination -EXIM- that applies ACOFI annually. The work was carried out for the application of 2020 and a total of 850 students, being the women considered the focal group. Making use of the JASP 0.14.1 program and the DifR library in RStudio 1.4.17.17 was calculated the DIF using the logistic regression method. The results show the presence of both uniform and non-uniform DIF in three of the four components, being that of mathematics which shows the greatest number of items and that of Chemistry which does not present items with DIF. From the qualitative analysis of each question, some differences are evidenced in the management of concepts evaluated or in the cultural influence due to the gender role that facilitates the practical approximation to the resolution of the situations raised in the analysis
