# Transformation of the socio-territorial dynamics of the peasants in the San Cristóbal (Medellin) district, based on the planning and territorial planning processes between 1999 and 2023

The San Cristobal district is a metropolitan rurality that is defined by the constant interaction between the rural and the urban areas. The planning and territorial planning processes – Partial Plan Pajarito, Tunnel of the West and Autopistas al Mar 1- responded to urban needs, where the improvement of access routes and housing construction was prioritized. The application of ethnography as a method of knowledge, showed that these processes transformed the socio-territorial dynamics of the peasants, by encouraging the arrival of new urban inhabitants, attracted by the proximity of the co-ordination to the city of Medellín and the Metropolitan Area of the Aburrá Valley. This, together with structural problems of the peasant population such as their ageing, the disassociation of the young and the low profitability of agricultural production, have increased the subdivision of rural predoms, and decreased the availability of agricultural land. .
