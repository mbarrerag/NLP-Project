# Design of PEG-PCL-based copolymers for encapsulation of apollar drugs.

At present there are multiple molecules with pharmacological interest of lipophilic character that have low solubility in water and low bioavailability, for example, curcumin with antioxidant properties and cannabinoids such as cannabidiol (CBD) and Δ-9-tetrahydrocannabinol (THC) that interact with the endocannabinoid system. Different transport vehicles have been developed to address the previous problems, which have shown the ability to increase the bioavailability of the drugs, however, they present disadvantages mainly associated with their low capacity for drug loading and toxicity. For this reason, the development of a polymeric vehicle functionalized with fatty acids in this research is proposed.
