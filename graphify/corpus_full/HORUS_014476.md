# The African snail phenomenon is an opportunity to develop scientific skills among students in the 4th and 5th primary schools.

This research paper aims to show whether, when taking a problem of the school environment (the phenomenon of the Achatina fulica) scientific competences can be developed in students of the fourth and fifth grades of rural primary basic by implementing a didactic sequence based on the ABP methodology (Learning based on problems) the eight steps of Torp & Sage are used, (2007) in three didactic sequences that correspond to the three dimensions evaluated by the test we advance with knowledge Icfes: Comprehensive use of scientific knowledge, explanation of phenomena and inquiry.The design is developed in 10 steps; first the idea, which part of the approach to the reality of a phenomenon being investigated (The African snail), second the problem of research is posed, (how to develop scientific competences through the study of the African snail phenomenon?), third the contributions were made in the field as a approach to the reality of the problem, besides checking the feasibility; the fourth phase is oriented to the documentation of the topic by using a variety of information; in the fifth phase, the previous knowledge of the students was evaluated, within the instrument, the questions were established, which gave the sociodemographic information, important to guide to the topic. The triangulation of information contributes to giving greater credibility, verifying convergence and contrasting the results and findings that arise from the work with students and parents, the range of investigation is appropriate. The strategy is longitudinal, two working groups are taken, among them, are awarded positions as independent scientists taking ownership of the problem; in the process of applying the proposal it was demonstrated that the ABP methodology provides learning, autonomous, contextualized and relevant, and also enhances skills and values in students, the teacher is given the importance of mediating learning. The strategy highlights the empirical knowledge of parents by using the cryo duck as predator of the African snail and exterminating them at least from the garden of the farm and the surroundings of the house
