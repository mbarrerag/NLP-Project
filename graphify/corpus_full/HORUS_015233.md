# ASSESSMENT OF THE EFFECTS OF NERTERA GRANADENSIS IN A MURINO MODEL OF DISEASE OF ALZHEIMER.

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is the most common neurodegenerative disorder and mainly affects the elderly. In recent years, it has been observed that the prevalence of AD is increasing due in part to the extension of life expectancy of the population. There are currently no treatments that stop neurodegeneration, so it is important to find pharmacological alternatives that allow control of both symptoms and progress of the disease. In this research, the effect of the etanolic extract of Nertera granadensis on a murine model of Alzheimer’s disease was evaluated. For this, mice were induced to the Alzheimer’s model by intracerebral injection of streptozothocone and divided into three treatment groups that received oral (vo) vehicle, memantine (5 mg/kg) and etanolic extract of Nertera granadensis (300 mg/kg), respectively. All groups received treatment for 7 days. Learning and memory were evaluated in the water labyrinth test of Morris.
