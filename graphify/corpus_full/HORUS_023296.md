# VIOLENCE TO THE DISEASES IN THE CIVID SCENE

The pandemic of the COVID-19 has become a scenario for the generation or increase of violence towards nurses in and outside the workplace. Description of the problem or need: violence in the workplace is a pre-eminent phenomenon, which harms not only nurses, but also patients and the organization. Unfortunately, some consider it to be the very problem of health work, the phenomenon has increased with the pandemic. Added to this was the discrimination and social violence outside the workplace. Intervention, strategies or implemented practice: hospitals around the world have implemented mechanisms for the accompaniment of health teams, seeking to reduce the emergence and consequences of mental disorders generated by the pandemic and the violence it has unleashed within and outside health care institutions. However, in a scenario full of uncertainty, death and an intense physical and emotional workload, the emergence of violent episodes, mediated by feelings of impotence and frustration, both inside and outside the workplace, is more possible
