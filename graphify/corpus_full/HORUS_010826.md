# Pathways for tertiary and armed conflict in Colombia 2002-2019

The armed conflict in Colombia has been one of the causes of the institutional weakness and misery that the country is experiencing. This research studies the impact that investment in tertiary road infrastructure has had on 7 modalities of the conflict in Colombia from 2002 to 2019. For this purpose, a panel database was constructed containing the interventions carried out at the municipal level with resources from the General Budget of the Nation and the General Royalties System, and an econometric model was implemented using Instrumental Variables with Fixed Effects. The results of this empirical estimation show that interventions in tertiary roads have positive effects on the reduction of the intensity of the armed conflict. However, when estimating the impact by heterogeneous effects, it is evident that, for the departments of Antioquia, Cauca and Norte de Santander, interventions in tertiary roads increase the intensity of the conflict. The same occurs when there is presence of non-renewable resources in the municipalities. This work adds to the literature that studies the impact of rural roads on the armed conflict, and the results contribute to the evaluation of the effectiveness of existing investment programs and policies
