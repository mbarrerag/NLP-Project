# Analysis of fungal microbiome present in different types of cacaoter soils with cadmium in the municipality of Yacopí - Cundinamarca.

Cadmium (Cd) is a toxic heavy heavy metal that alters the growth and development of all the organisms present in agricultural systems. Cocoa plants accumulate Cd and there have been high concentrations in cocoa beans, which affects food safety and its marketing.The microbial soil community plays an important role in the nutrient cyclaje and in the bioremediation of soils contaminated with heavy metals.In this community, the fungi are highlighted, which establish mycelial networks that colonize the soil and exhibit tolerance mechanisms that allow them to survive high concentrations of the metal.However, few studies have evaluated the effect of the physicochemical properties of the soil and of the Cd on the fungal community present in cocoa soil.The aim of this study was to characterize the structural diversity of the fungal microbiomes present in soil and exhibit the upper levels of the soil and show tolerances that allow them to survive high concentrations of the metal.However, few studies have evaluated the effect of the physi chemical properties of the soil and the Cd Cd.
