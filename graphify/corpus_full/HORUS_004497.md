# BASIC NURSING CARE: SCOPE REVIEW PROTOCOL

To establish how basic nursing care is described in the literature between 2000 and 2023 Introduction: The need for exhaustive research to determine the most effective methods of health care due to the increasing demand for quality care caused by the global trend of population aging increase in chronic diseases and disorders related to lifestyles. The transformation of the organizational culture of health is crucial, and the participation of nursing is essential to guarantee the well-being and satisfaction of patients. It has been identified that basic nursing care practices are crucial to meeting objectives related to safety and quality in health care. The review aims to establish how basic nursing care is described and to improve understanding of its impact on various aspects of health care. A preliminary search was carried out that shows a growing interest in the subject since 2012. Inclusion criteria: The review will examine the literature describing basic nursing care. The review will follow JBI's scope review recommendations. A preliminary search was conducted in 2024, where some authors and expert reports dealing with this topic were identified. Once the protocol is validated, the literature search will be done in the databases: Embase, Science Direct, Pubmed, Ovid Nursing, VHL, Scopus, Wiley Online Library, and the Virginia Henderson repository. To identify gray literature, a search will be done on Google Scholar and web pages of associations or institutions that deal with the subject. 2 evaluators will independently review the titles and abstracts. Then the full texts and the relevant information will be presented as set out in Annexes I and II. nursing care, basic nursing care keys, and fundamentals of care
