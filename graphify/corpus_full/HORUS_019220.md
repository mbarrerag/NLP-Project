# Nanoencapsulation as a strategy to improve the delivery of medicines for the treatment of infectious diseases: an overview

Chemotherapy control is one of the main ways to combat infectious diseases, however, there are several limitations that put this strategy at risk, among which are: the small amount of drugs for clinical use, high costs associated with the way some drugs are administered or their chemical nature, high toxicity, the presence of drug resistant pathogens and serious side effects. As a result, alternative therapies arise as a fundamental requirement to improve the treatment of these pathologies
