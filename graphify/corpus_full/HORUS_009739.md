# DEVELOPMENT OF ANALYTICAL TECHNICALS FOR THE STUDIES OF CHARACTERISTICS IN ENVIRONMENTAL SAMPLES: TARGET ANALYSIS & SUSPECT SCREANING DOING SO OF THE LIQUID CRIMATOGRAPHY HOLDED TO THE MASS SPECTROMETRY OF HIGH RESOLUTION

In this occasion, within the Environmental Health line, the processes and work carried out for the development and validation of analytical methodologies for the monitoring, detection and quantification of environmental pollutants of pharmaceutical type in matrixes of samples of river fish are presented. As part of the process of optimisation of the extraction of the drugs, extractions were carried out at three levels of enrichment (25, 50 and 100 ng of compounds/g of matrix), with different solvents (mixes of acidified water with MeCN and/or iPrOH), variation in initial mass of the matrix (0.5 and 1.0 g), varying the types of extraction, and purification of the extracts. In this case, 5 different protocols for extraction of the compounds (QuEChERS and extraction supported by ultrasound) and variations in purification of the extract (QuEChERS, with/without freezing, centrifugal, reconstitution, filters) among others. This work allows to complete the successful validation of the extraction of the most commonly-type fish with monitoring, detection and quantification of the 47 compounds with limits of detection to fish at least 50 %. The validated method is generally highly sensitive, highly selective, reproducible and versatile when analyzing different families of compounds. It is relatively fast, simple and economical when processing samples. However, more efforts are needed to further develop techniques for analysing environmental samples that are consistent with environmental awareness, green chemistry and, moreover, safe for analysts. On the part of government entities, analytical developments that have less waste material, less consumption of toxic solvent, and less energy consumption, including less time for analysis, should be promoted more effectively
