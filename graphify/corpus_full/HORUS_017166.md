# ESTIMATES OF GENETIC PARAMETERS FOR WEIGHT WEIGHT IN RED-JOB Brahman USED TWO STATISTICAL ABORDAGES

The weight to the weaning is a characteristic of great biological and economic importance that must be included in the programs of bovine genetic improvement, considering both the direct genetic effect, and the maternal one. However, in small populations, the estimation of the genetic parameters can be limited, being important to apply methodologies that allow to minimize errors, obtain genetic values with greater precision and therefore a greater genetic progress. Compare the genetic parameters estimated for weight to weaning, using the methodologies REML and GIBBS in the breeding herd of red Brahman, of the Cotové Agricultural Station, of the National University of Colombia, Medellín. Information was collected between the years 2002 and 2021 of 728 animals, with 579 weights to weaning. An animal model was used taking into account as fixed effects: birth number, sex, year, age and time of weaning and the hered effects of the Rproject. The analyses were carried out with the methodologies REML, using the program WOMBAT and GIBBS, using the program GIBBSF90, the convergence was verified by the BOBS package of the analysis
