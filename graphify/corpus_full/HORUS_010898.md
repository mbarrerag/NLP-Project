# SELECTION OF DRYING TECHNOLOGY THAT MINIMIZES THE LOSS OF CAROTENOIDS, COLOUR, AND ANTIOXIDANT CAPACITY OF THE PEACH PALM (BACTRIS GASIPAES)

The peach palm (Bactris gasipaes) is a source of interest for carotenoids for the food industry, especially for the ß-carotene bioactive present in the fruit, which is considered a potential antioxidant. The proposal of this research is to retain ß- arotene in fruit through the evaluation of drying techniques: convective (CD), vacuum (VD), spray (SD), microwave (MD), and refractance window (RW). The ß-carotene retained in the fruit was evaluated by HPLC technique and upported with ORAC, DPPH, PT, and ABTS assays to evaluate the antioxidant capacity of the dried fruit in each of the rying techniques. The results revealed that 88.5% of the ß-carotene was preserved in the refractance window drying, while he other techniques yielded 11.44% dried by convection, 23.94% dried by microwave, 22.85% dried by vacuum, and 30% ried by aspersion.
