# Comparative assessment of In Silico predictive tools of variants of change of meaning in genes of interest in pharmacogenetics: bioinformatics and population analysis for the DPYD gene

A large part of the toxicity in cancer patients treated with fluoropyrimidine is due to the loss of the enzymatic activity of dihydropyridine dehydrogenase, caused by variants in the DPYD gene, so several studies have proposed the genotypification at the beginning of the treatment with these drugs. This study sought to determine the efficacy of different In Silico algorithms of prediction of negative meaning change variants in the DPYD gene, with the purpose of proposing a flow of evaluation based on the tools of annotation with high sensitivity and specificity in the prediction of variants of interest in pharmacogenetics in the Colombian population. For this purpose the comparative evaluation of tools of annotation In Silico in the DPYD gene based on the findings of a systematic review of scope, additional to a structural descriptive analysis, the variables of noAF, Eigen, Eigen, E-SPC, and Aventor were proposed. The study aimed to determine the effectiveness of different in silicatory algorithm and specific in predicting alternatives of interest in pharmaceutical, was less than 1%, which is lower than reported for Caucasian populations, and the most frequent was HapB3. The variant c.1127A>C was identified, and, according to annotation tools, could be harmonious; however, additional studies should be conducted to confirm the effect of the variant. Conclusion, dismissal being an initial computational analysis to identify variations in the DDY gene in the Colombian population, further investigation is required to validate the findings of this research. This could lead to the development of analysis workflows in pharmaceuticals tailored to the characteristics of the Colombian population
