# Macroricetous fungi: alternative biofactories of compounds with application potential in cosmeceutics and nutricosmeceutics.

In recent years, modern societies have become more aware of “natural” treatments, which has promoted the development of new products from natural sources, to which the cosmetic industry has not been alien. The use of biocomposites in cosmetic formulations has allowed the appearance of so-called “biocosmetic”, a term that includes two groups of special importance that are cosmeceutics and nutrocosmeeutics. The traditional and most studied source for the search for bioactives are plants, but the use of this natural resource has limitations such as slow growth and noticeable variation in the content and class of its bioactives, particularity not desired in the production of biocosmetics. Macrofungal biosinteticas can be grown quickly and in a cost-effective way, by applying biotechnological processes such as the fermentation in Líquido state, this natural resource has been used which can be placed as an alternative promising source for obtaining bioactives, with the advantage that they can be cultivated quickly and in a cost-effective way, by applying the updated vision of the natural product
