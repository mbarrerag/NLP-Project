# ATTENUATION

The aim of the work was to identify the effects of the presence of edge mangroves on erosion rates and to evaluate the mechanisms of dissipation of the wave. The erosion and accretion rates in coastal segments with vegetation and without vegetation were studied for 2.4 years and the rates of dissipation of the wave through pressure sensors arranged at different distances from the edge of the mangroves. The segments with vegetation reduced the rates of erosion between 3 and 15 times in the sites with the highest energy of the wave and experienced accretion in the site with the lowest energy.The coast segments with the highest area of Rhizophora mangrove reduced the rates of erosion and increased the coastal stability in greater proportion.The attenuation of the energy of the wave was explained by the height of the wave incident (28-76%)Do the presence of oysters? the density of the vegetation? the tide based (41-55%) and the presence of mannuffage and the mannager in the edge was also explained by the presence of the wave incident (28-76%)?
