# Study of the transformations of Colombian residual biomass inorganic material during combustion in fluidized bed.

The present project proposes an experimental study of the combustion of Colombian residual biomass in an experimental fluidized bed installation, in order to determine the transformations of the inorganic elements present in biomasses and their interaction with the material of the bed, which is silica sand. The methodology of the project is divided into three parts. The first consists of a review of literature and physicochemical characterization of residual biomass to determine the potential risk of agglomeration in fluidized beds. Additionally, fluid dynamic studies are carried out to estimate operational parameters of the experimental installation, such as fluidization speed, selection of particle size distribution, determination of the relation between ash and silica sand, and operating temperature. The second part consists of the setting up of the fluidized bed reactor that allows the execution of combustion experiments and the approach and execution of an experimental plan that takes into account methodologies used for the study of agglomeration.
