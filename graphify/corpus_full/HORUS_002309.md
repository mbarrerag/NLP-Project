# Indirect detection system of post-harvest pathologies in Glupa and avocado cv. Hass using RGB images, VIS-IR indexes and artificial intelligence

In Colombia, strategic crops such as the avocado cv. Hass and the gulupa constitute fundamental pillars for the supply of fresh fruit, generating foreign exchange and promoting the economic and social development of the rural sector. However, these crops face serious phytosanitary limitations in the post-harvest stage, where the losses due to diseases can exceed 25_x0096_30=8239;%, directly affecting the quality of the fruit, the profitability of the producer and access to demanding international markets. Among the main pathologies that compromise the integrity of these products are the anthranosis (caused by Colletrichum spp.) in gulupa and the rotting of the peduncle (associated with Lasiodiplodia theobromae) in avocado. Both diseases deteriorate the external appearance of the fruit, reduce its useful life in anaquel and cause high percentages of rejection in the points of commercial destination. In the face of this problem, the present project proposes a comprehensive solution based on the use of emerging technologies, particularly the artificial intelligence (IA) and the espectral advice. It is proposed the development of an automated system for the detection of epimetic damages. and seeks to strengthen the competitiveness of small and medium-sized producers, generating practical, replicable and adaptable tools to different productive chains of the country
