# INTRINGULIS OF SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

The two main challenges that humanity will face in the next century are associated with food security and climate change; and both are directly related to animal production systems, that is, animal protein production. However, these systems are at the center of discussions on the health of the planet and people, because the sustainability of these forms of production is constantly being questioned
