# The testimony of elderly victims of political violence in Colombia in processes related to moral formation. Participatory observation work in sixth grade of a Public College of Cundinamarca

The testimony of elderly victims of political violence in Colombia in processes related to moral training. Participatory observation work in sixth grade of a public school in Cundinamarca Alba Lucía Olarte Sánchez This paper collects the experiences and analysis of a participatory observation work of pedagogical nature, based on the use of testimonies of victims of political violence in Colombia, focusing on testimonies of elderly victims, to address contents related to the training in moral matters of sixth grade students in high school. The research question was: How do testimonies of victims of violence, used as a pedagogical resource, contribute to the moral training of students and how this can be evidenced in the context of training? Thus, this work with testimonies sought to promote a critical and reflective awareness of the reality of political violence in Colombia, as well as to promote empathic attitudes towards the victim population, mainly, the population of the third age. The proposed activities to students tried to dialogue with the characteristics of childhood, seeking an emotional approach to the stories and the characters of the testimonies.
