# EFFICIENCY AS A MEASURE OF THE DESIGN OF COLOMBIAN AGRICULTURAL COOPERATIVES

The associated working cooperatives, which belong to the Colombian agricultural sector, contribute sig-nificatively to the solidarity economy, generate two jobs and GDP growth. However, their performance is little studied. The objective of this study is to analyze what are the factors that determine the efficiency of these organizations during the period 2003-2015. The methodology used is the nonparametric DEA technique in two stages. The results indicate technical efficiency of 21% in average, with an annual growth trend of the number of efficient organizations. Finally, the management of the administrators and the increase of associates are considered determinant factors of efficiency
