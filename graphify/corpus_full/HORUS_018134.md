# THE EXPERIENCE OF ART, ART IN EXPERIENCE.

The chapter gives a historical account of what has been understood by the concept of art at every moment, from the first aesthetic manifestations of man to the present, to highlight the transformation of aesthetic expressions and to show that the common factor is the vital experience that is experienced when one comes into contact with art and that they enrich human consciousness
