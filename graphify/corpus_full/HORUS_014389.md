# Evolution of pain and presence of comorbidities of the patient with rotator cuff tear.

The study aims to describe the evolution of pain and functionality in patients with conservative treatment for tearing of the rotator cuff in the first assessment, at 3 and 12 months of follow-up. A single-center, descriptive study was conducted, with prospective data collection. 83 patients with a mean age of 60 years were evaluated, 75.9% of whom were women. It was found that women had a lower score on the OST scale and greater pain compared to men. A significant decrease in the ANA was observed three months after the initial evaluation in patients with partial rupture with rotator cuff syndrome, but at 12 months, the pain returned to baseline levels. Multivariate analysis found that patients with decreased shoulder abduction and female gender were more likely to have a poor outcome after 12 months. Overall, most patients had a favorable response with conservative pain management and functionality at 3 months, but no significant changes were presented at 12 months
