# Dynamic capabilities for transforming business models into a digital disruption context: case for corporate finance services

The business models of the traditional financial institutions are in a context of digital disruption, to address this type of contexts the entities can develop dynamic capacities, which can be described as the ability to create new value in changing conditions from the reconfiguration of resources. However, these companies face challenges in the development of these capacities. Highlighting these challenges, there is a shortage of empirical research associated with how companies develop dynamic capacities and their application in different industries. Therefore, this project seeks to address the question: How do dynamic capacities that allow the identification of opportunities and threats to transform the business model of providing services for corporate finance in Colombia in a context of digital disruption? Through a qualitative approach, the current practices of the corporate finance business model of a Colombian financial institution were assessed, contrasted with the best practices identified in the literature. Based on this analysis, specific recommendations were issued to improve the capacity of identifying threats and opportunities in the business model were improved.
