# STRENGTHENING WOMEN AS A POLITICAL SUBJECT IN THE TRUTH Sclarification COMMISSION AND IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF PEACE IN COLOMBIA.

Based on the study field that intersects feminist and gender studies with the participation of women in peace-building, this chapter addresses participation in the scenarios that are opened up by the Peace Agreement, specifically in the Commission for the Clarification of Truth, Coexistence and Non-Repetition (CEV), as a dimension of women's political exercise. To this end, the chapter presents recommendations to the CEV regarding the participation of women in the various processes, activities and actions it undertakes in the remaining areas of its mandate, in such a way that this contributes to enhancing their status as political subjects and, consequently, as citizens
