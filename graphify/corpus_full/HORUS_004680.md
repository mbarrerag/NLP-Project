# RESIDENT IN PATIENT SAFETY. QUALITY ASSESSMENT OF METANALYSIS

To evaluate the quality of meta-analysis that review patient safety when residents participate in patient care compared to specialists. Material and methods: In May 2023 a systematic meta-analysis review was carried out using the search terms "residents and patient safety" in the PubMed,Embase databases and with these Spanish terms in Lilacs, without filter by publication date. The meta-analysis finally selected were qualified with the AMSTAR 2 evaluation tool. Three meta-analysis were extracted for qualitative analysis. The rating with the AMSTAR 2 evaluation tool found that the general confidence of its results was critical. The met-analysis evaluated were consistent in reporting on the longest operating time of residents. Also on patient safety when the resident performs surgery. Evaluation with the AMSTAR 2 evaluation tool showed that the general confidence in the results of meta-analysis was critically low
