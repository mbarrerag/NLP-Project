# Teaching and learning strategies for the integration of natural sciences, mathematics and language in primary basics: Case study.

The educational context requires a transformation in more efficient teaching and learning processes in different areas, therefore, managers and teachers must seek different strategies that allow contextualization of knowledge. This leads to the realization of research that directly involve the student triad, teacher and know so that from them the ideas and changes that are required arise, is how the didactic proposal arises whose aim was to design and implement teaching and learning strategies that favor the integration of natural sciences, mathematics and language in primary basic through a case study. The methodology was implemented in the La Felisa Educational Institution, co-ordination of the municipality of La Merced initially with a group of 7 primary basic teachers and finally achieved the linkage of much of the teaching plant including secondary and middle basic. The different teaching and learning strategies were oriented through the use of graphic organizers, construction of learning guides, role play, incorporation and prioritization of learning in study plans, area plans and use of platforms, continuous training where the model New School and the moments that guide the All to Learning program were oriented by turning the learning into a learning tool that allows to replicate and reflect and apply to the teaching opportunities, even to the development and improvement of platforms, constant training where the new school model and the environment were combined with the different moments of learning and learning
