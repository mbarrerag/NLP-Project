# DEVELOPMENT OF A PYTHON IMPLEMENTATION FOR SPACE VARIABILITY MAPEUM IN DIGITAL AGRICULTURE

To develop a portable geographic information system with the use of specialized modules for data analysis in the Python ecosystem and to compare its results with the geostatistical engine SAGA GIS to validate the system. The application GeoStatLiteUN v2021.6.1 was developed implementing the specialized modules: SciPy v1.20.3, NumPy v1.20.0, statsmodels v0.12.2, PyKrige v1.6.0, Matplotlib.pyplot v3.4.2, seaborn v0.11.1 and Tkinter of Python (Python 3.9.5) in a script that, under the logic of the geostatistics, automated repetitive procedures to analyze spatial variability (inside and outside of the lots) by means of the ordinary Kriging interpolation, whose execution allows users to process and analyze spatial data and build spatial variability maps in an agile way. GeoStatLiteUN when tested, it showed that the theoretical results are valid if you take into account the similarity of the results of SAGG and GIS for the same
