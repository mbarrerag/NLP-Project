# COORDINATION COMPOUNDS OF CU (II) WITH GLICINE AND DICARBOXYLIC ACIDS AND THEIR POTENTIAL ANTI-ACTIVITY.

The diseases caused by bacteria represent a high risk to the health of all people. Antibiotics have been for many years the methods to combat these microorganisms, but each day there is a greater number of these that are resistant to antibiotics, including those of wide spectrum. The coordination compounds that exhibit antibacterial activity have allowed to search for new alternatives to reduce the incidence of diseases caused by these microorganisms. In this work were obtained the compounds of coordination of Cu(II) with glycine and oxalic and itachonic acids. As copper precursor trihydrate copper nitrate was used and synthesis was made at 80°C using magnetic agitation and reflux. The compounds were characterized by thermal techniques (TGA) and spectroscopic (FTIR, DRX,UV-VIS). The compounds show a thermal stability around 200°C and have crystalline structures with possible porosity, as seen in the results of X-ray powders.
