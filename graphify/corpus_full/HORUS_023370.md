# THEAPOCUTIONAL THERAPEUTS IN THE EDUCATIONAL CONTEXT: A COMMUNITY OF LATIN AMERICAN PRACTICE.

The Occupational Therapeutics are being required to promote the participation and performance of infants and adolescents in the educational context. This document gives an account of the advances generated by an online community of practice called Occupational Therapy in the School (TOE) of Latin America and the Caribbean, which emerged in 2020 within the framework of the COVID-19 pandemic, as an independent group of Latin American Occupational Therapeutics interested in sharing experiences and professional development in the field of education. Problem: How has the development of the Latin American TOE Practice Community been? Understand the panorama of the work of occupational therapy in countries that make up the TOE Latin American practice community. Qualitative and documentary research, using as source a document made available to members of the community of practice, which completed topics on the panorama of the practice of occupational therapy in education in their countries. Data has been analyzed according to the Analysis of Content. Results of advances or finals: Currently 11 countries participate in TOE Latin America, of which 10 completed the document used in this research and 9 of them gave the total of information
