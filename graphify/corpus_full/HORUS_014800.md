# INTERNATIONAL RULES FOR THE NEUROLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION OF THE LESSONS OF THE SPINAL MEDULA: THE ASIA 2019 CLASSIFICATION

The international standards for the neurological classification of spinal cord lesions (ISNCSCI), arise from the need to establish a common language in the initial evaluation and follow-up of the patient with spinal cord injury (SML).The 2019 version incorporates new concepts about the non-spinal cord injury deficiencies and partial preservation areas in incomplete spinal cord injuries.This classification allows for standardization of physical examination, which increases reproducibility between different examiners and long-term follow-up.It also facilitates the establishment of treatment and prognosis
