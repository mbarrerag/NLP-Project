# NEUROPATOLOGY FORENSE

In 2018, while I was studying the first year of medical residency, Professor Nelson Ricardo Téllez proposed me to participate in a project: the creation of a book on forensic neuropathology, as a result of the processing of specimens preserved in the pathology service of the Regional Bogotá of INML and CF For many years the forensic pathology service of the Regional Bogotá of the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences has presented a systematic cluster of neuropathological specimens originating in the context of legal medical necropsies, through the project systematic documentation in Forensic Neuropatology? Was it proposed the systematic documentation of specimens and macroscopic images of neuropathological specimens of forensic origin (brain and spinal cords), it was intended to take advantage of the enormous amount of neuropathological material from legal medical necropsies as study material in addition to the textbook and atlas to allow access to the students of different levels and from the various health professionals interested in neurosciences reference material for learning of the most frequent injuries
