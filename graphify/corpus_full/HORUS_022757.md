# Three moments of the coronavirus era: obey, indignant and revolt

Just as there is an instant to see, a time to understand and a moment to conclude in Lacanian theory, there is also an instant to obey, a time to be indignant and a moment to revolt. Lacan introduces these logical times to think the place of the Other. In this work, other logical times will be introduced to think about the place of the coronavirus in our reality. Coronavirus and its pandemic have caused a series of subjective responses to anguish such as obedience and indignation; the proposal in this work is that there is another way, a way out of that anguish through the act of revolting with others
