# Competition, transmission and impact: a look from the telecommunications market Latin american

Given the sectoral and microeconomic nature of competition policy, the impact it may have on macroeconomic aggregates is generally not considered. Although there is sufficient research on the subject, it is difficult to consider the causality of competition policy from a particular market, impacting a sector and thus the economy. A possible transmission channel of this policy is presented, as well as an empirical evaluation through a panel data model with instrumental variables with data from Chile, Colombia, Mexico and Peru for the period between 2009 and 2022, using information referring to the mobile internet market and the telecommunications sector. A positive relationship between competition and growth is found, although it is not significant given some limitations in the estimation
