# Deep learning model for automatic generation of synthetic antimicrobial peptides with specific functionalities.

Antibiotic resistance has become one of the biggest health problems worldwide in recent years, causing direct effects against health and the economy. A special type of short proteins, called antimicrobial peptides, is taking on great relevance in research to combat this problem, mainly due to their antibiotic benefits. There are different methods for the search for new antimicrobial peptides, among them the use of machine learning techniques that allow to reduce costs and time of search, compared with traditional bioprospecting techniques. In this line, in this work, a method for the generation of synthetic sequences of antimicrobial peptides with specific functionalities using a neural network with a conditional GAN architecture and recurrent cells is proposed to be evaluated from a proposed validation strategy that focuses on measuring the quality and diversity of anti-micron synthetic sequences generated.
