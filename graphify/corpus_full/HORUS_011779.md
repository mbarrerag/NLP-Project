# ECOLOGY RESPONSIBILITY IN THE TEACHING OF ARCHITECTURE

The presentation describes some projects developed in the courses of projects of the basic cycle at the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters Medellín, in which attempts were made to incorporate different topics linked to systemic sustainability, through the dialogical chain between the natural world and that artificial. Projects on different scales have been addressed and experiments have been developed that incorporated reflections linked to the issues related to the increase of population and the inhuman growth of cities, territorial destruction, the scarcity of resources and climate change. The pedagogical cavilations have determined us to continue incorporating, outside and within the university world, the concepts of sustainable architecture, and to reflect on new territorial transformations not only in function of the human being, but of all living beings, and of the global ecosystem. The surveys regarding the transformation of territorial portions have followed patterns of adaptation to the answer, of concatenation with the natural systems and -in urban context - have pointed towards systems of pedestrian connections, to the incorporation of natural elements in the city, to the humanization of the neighborhoods, to the participation of the community in the project decisions
