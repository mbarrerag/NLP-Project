# ESTIMATION OF LODO LESS TERMS BY MEASURE OF FACTURES BY MATHEMATIC MODELATION AND VALIDATION BY A PHYSICAL MODEL

The loss of fluid can occur through fractured formations or in permeable areas, especially when there are low pressures. This event increases the non-productive times in drilling operations, which increases costs. This generates the need to understand the interaction between the drilling fluid and the fractures present in the formation. To do this, a methodology is proposed that has two approaches, one numerical based on rheological models and balance of the movement equation, and one physical based on the scaling theory. For the numerical model, equations are proposed that allow to calculate the velocity profile according to the properties of the fluid and fracture geometry. For the physical model, real rock samples are taken, the fracture is constructed with a controlled width and geometry, through which a mud is circularized, whose properties were scaled and determined in laboratory. The results of the physical and numerical model were compared for different reologies and fracture widths, concluding that the mathematical model does represent the phenomenon of losses especially for low viscosities.
