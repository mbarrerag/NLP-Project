# PHYSICAL CONDITIONS OF EQUITMENT UNDER SACRIFIED IN A COLOMBIA BENEFIT PLANT

It is common that animals in the pre-slaughter are subjected to unnecessary mistreatment with the argument of immediate death that awaits them. To favor their welfare and impact on products derived from the benefit of equidae, the management of animals should avoid causing stress or affecting their physical integrity during the process. Characterize the physical conditions of equidae subjected to slaughter in a benefit plant in Colombia. 160 animals were evaluated individually during their stay in the pens. A questionnaire and topographic map were used to identify physical alterations. Animals were selected in a systematic random way.
