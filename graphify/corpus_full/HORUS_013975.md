# Climate change and environmental problems in the southwestern region of Antioquia

Climate change increases the vulnerability of natural and human systems to disasters such as floods, landslides, droughts, forest fires, cyclone surges, ocean acidification, among others. In the worst scenario, the collapse of the ecosystem could have devastating consequences for human well-being. In Antioquia, one of the main consequences is the transformation in the dynamics of natural ecosystems. It is estimated that in Colombia an annual average of 170,000 hectares of forests was lost between 2010 and 2020 due mainly to deforestation, which is linked in some way to climate change. To assess the risk of exposure to climate change, indices such as the Vulnerability Index and the Risk Index to Climate Change are used. According to a report on Climate Change Vulnerability and Risk Analysis in Colombia, Antioquia presents a medium risk index. Biodiversity and hydroresource present a high risk and high vulnerability field. For the south-west subregion, the Risk Index associated with climate change effects is 0.187, which is considered a medium risk.
