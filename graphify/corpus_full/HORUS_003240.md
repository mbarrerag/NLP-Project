# EDUCATION IN ENGINEERING, EXPERIENCE, RESULTS AND GOOD PRACTICES FROM THE CASE OF THE CONSULTORY Commission ON THE FACULTY OF MINES OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

Since the first academic semester of 2023, the Center for Development and Innovation (CDi) of the Faculty of Mines of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Medellín, has been developing the Consultorio CDi, a project through which undergraduate students of different engineering programs carry out a special academic practice (PAE) in which they implement a basic consultancy service to attend cases or consultations presented by Entrepreneurships of Graduates of the UNAL, micro, small and medium enterprises (Mipymes), Entities Without Lucro's Aim (ESAL), and Social or Community Organizations. In this process, the teams of students, in their role as consultants, are accompanied by tutors, teachers and/or graduates of the Faculty of Mines who guide the process to maintain the technical level of the case topics being addressed, and also have permanent support by the CDI to facilitate the proper implementation of each moment of the basic consultancy process established. With this experience of active learning, students put into practice the knowledge acquired in their training process, while strengthening the cross-cutting skills such as team work and critical thinking, and contribute to the strengthening of the consulting structure, and other aspects of the CDP
