# IDENTIFICATION OF THE NAMNAT OF TRICHOMONS VAGINALIS A ESSENTIAL LINE IN ENERGY METABOLISM

Introduction The parasite Trichomonas vaginalis[1] causing non-viral STDs of higher incidence, presents resistance to current treatments, to identify control strategies, analyze the energy metabolism of NAD+. In this work an experimental approach was developed by the production and enzyme evaluation of recombinant protein. Methods The amplification of the coding region of the candidate for NMNAT (XP_001584530.1) building pETSUMO-tvnmnat. The expression of the recombinant was evaluated by SDS-PAGE and immunoblot, subsequently purified by IMAC and its enzyme activity was verified by the generation of NAD+ in trials coupled to ADH [2,3]. Results and Conclusions The NMNAT of Trichomonas vaginalis (24 kDa) was identified as a key protein in the metabolism of NAD+ by means of enzymatic tests coupled. To this, the cloning of the enzymatic region in the PET-SU vector was performed. Results and Conclusions The recombinant plasmids were isolated and verified by PCR and DAG+ by DAD+ by means of enzymatics
