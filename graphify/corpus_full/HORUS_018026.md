# The Judicial Construction of the Stare Decision in Colombia (1991-2018)

This article maintains that the stare decision, understood as the obligation of the judicial precedent to establish and resolve a dispute in Colombia, was established through the internal and external battles of the Constitutional Court to enforce its decisions and the effects of these. In the course of these clashes with other legal and political actors, the Court favoured the development of a dogmatic more conceptual than casuistic for the understanding of the precedent. This penetrated the political sphere and was erected as a tool of judicial decongestion, ratifying the constitutional precedent as a mandatory source of law and extending the scope of the precedent to the jurisprudence of the other high courts
