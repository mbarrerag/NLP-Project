# Weaving/weaving absences: textile choreographies that weav/weaving individual and collective bodies

This is a post-humanist feminist agenda research in which I examine, and sometimes I believe, spaces in which knowledge (textile concepts and materialities) is recognized and promoted as collective acts. And that besides de-centering the gaze of the human, calls us to look as part of an assembly of relationships and affectations in which the textile is produced. This is a production that is text and is textile. It is a textus. It links various stories of absences caused by unexpected deaths of loved ones or by the absence of ourselves, which with the textile doings we take care of undoing, doing and remaking. Along this textus you will find a tangle of textile women who in dialogues count our pains, our broken material and the way we transform them. You will also find the collected in full-text boxes of textiles that help me to revisit, reflect and question the antagonism of extremes to propose them as tensed threads with which we create something new.
