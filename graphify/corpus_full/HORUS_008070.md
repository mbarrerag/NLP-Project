# Moral agency and abuse. Comparison of narratives on harm experiences in abused and untreated Colombian adolescents

This research compared the constructions of a moral agency between Colombian adolescents who were ill-treated (n=30) and non-distreated (n=30), as well as between adolescents under 11 to 13 years of age (medium=12.2) and adolescents aged 15 to 17 years (medium=15.6) in relation to their experiences of interpersonal conflict. Through a semi-structured interview, 120 narratives were collected on situations in which they were victims or perpetrators of harm. ANOVAS and MANOVAS were used to evaluate differences between groups in severity measures, facts and interpretations, presence, relevance and narrative contents, as well as judgments and moral justifications, considering the condition, age, sex and perspective (with perspective as a repeated measure). In general, significant differences were found in the constructions of a moral agency according to the condition of the participants.
