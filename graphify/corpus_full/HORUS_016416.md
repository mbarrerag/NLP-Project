# THE RIGHT OF CHILDREN AND ADOLESCENTS 30 YEARS AFTER THE 1991 CONSTITUTION: PROGRESS AND PERSPECTIVES

In this article, I respond to the following concern: what are the advances and prospects in the legal protection of Colombian children and adolescents, 30 years after the promulgation of the 1991 Constitution? And in order to resolve it, I will study the emergence, conditions and concept of constitutionalization of the right of children and adolescents, and then carry out a legislative and case law review of the legal regulation for Colombian children and adolescents during these 30 years. After that, I will finally conclude with the progress and prospects regarding the effective recognition of the active ownership of children and adolescents in Colombian territory
