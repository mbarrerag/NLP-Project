# Critical analysis of a telepresence training process of the biology subject in students of higher education.

This inquiry aims to identify the factors that influenced the learning of concepts and the development of scientific skills among students of the undergraduate course of Biology at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá when, for reasons of public order and compulsory isolation measures, the institution had to adopt the distance training model. To identify the factors that interceded in the training process, the guidelines established by the Ministry of National Education for remote vocational training, as well as the technique of critical analysis, were followed by a review of the curriculum of the subject, then the documentation for the development of competencies, accompanying technologies and the didactic strategies that were implemented by the course director was evaluated; finally, the achievement of the achievements achieved by students was assessed by analyzing the final projects that they delivered as academic product at the end of the semester. The work concludes with an overview of the problems experienced by students during the adoption of the new modality. Recommendations are also provided to improve the teaching-learning process given the case should continue with the form of telepresence
