# HEALTH AND SAFETY AT WORK: APPROXIMATIONS, PERSPECTIVES AND REFLECTIONS

The work as a cross-cutting axis in the economic and social development of life is a necessary process that involves the transformation of the environment and its resources for the search for the collective and individual well-being of the human being. However, this search represents, in turn, a contradiction that implies facing challenges with progressive effects, which will determine the state of health of the working population. Recent transformations in the world of work demand a harmonious relationship between the work process and health and require social actors (government, academia, society) to investigate and take measures to adapt to the health challenges generated by the work, which requires greater evidence from the scientific field that contributes to guaranteeing the workers' extra-laboral and individual conditions. Being multidisciplinary, health and safety at work allows scientific evidence through the study of variables from different points of view, which generate a combination of knowledge, important input for the processes of adaptation to the health requirements generated by the work dynamics, from an interdisciplinary approach that aims transdisciplinary. This document includes three sections that integrate conceptual aspects, experiences of scientific, theoretical and technical processes developed by each of the authors, with the purpose of avoiding the adaptation of the work to the world
