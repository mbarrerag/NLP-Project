# Factors determining the success and sustainability of Shared Bike Systems

Although many urban centres have been adopting shared bicycle systems (CBS) with the intention of providing benefits to the population in terms of more sustainable and easily accessible transport, few justify precisely and quantitatively why the implementation of these systems would be favourable and what objectives they intend to achieve, contributing to the indefinite sale of the idea that these systems are beneficial without rigorous metrics supporting it, allowing comparisons to be made between different BCSs, or assessing how much they favour the environment and are being used. This research aims to identify key factors that influence the success and sustainability of global shared bicycle systems, through empirical analysis and the application of regression models, in order to contribute in the formulation of policies that maximize environmental and social benefits, as well as in the making of decisions justified in concise quantitative evidence
