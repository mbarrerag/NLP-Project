# Design of a medium of cultivation from vinaza and sugarcane honey for the production of single cell protein Candida utilis

In this final mastery work, the design of a culture medium for the production of single cell protein waduro (SCP, single cell protein) from yeast Candida used using vinaza and sugarcane honey as substrate. All developed was focused on its application in the food industry. For the design of the culture medium, an experimental design of 2 factors was performed to evaluate the effect of the concentration of vinaza and sugarcane honey on the cell concentration of the culture medium, the purpose of this work was to find a culture medium whose composition produced a high cellular concentration by statistical analysis. In the results a medium composed of 45 g/L of cane honey and 16% v/v of vinaza. In addition, a kinetic study was carried out for cell growth, where the concentration was measured in the medium, the SCP produced and the concentration was calculated in the cell time (in) was found.. For the design of the culture medium, a 2-factor experimental design was carried out to evaluate the effect of the concentration of wine and suggest cane molasses on the cell concentration of the culture medium, the purpose of this work was to find a culture medium which was composition produced a high cell concentration by means of a statistical analysis. The results showed a medium composed of 45 g/L of suggestive molasses and 16% v/v of wine. With this medium, a SCP concentration of 15.706 g/L with a protein content of 56.17% and a cell concentration of 5.413*108 fromlls/mL was acquired.
