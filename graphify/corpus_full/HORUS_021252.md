# Maintenance equipment Thermocycling reference LightCycler ® 480. Roche brand

Currently, most research in the fields of biology and particularly in viriology, immunology, nutrition, is based on molecular tests. Through these, pathogens can be detected, pathogenic loads can be established and gene expression can also be seen. For this, teams called real-time thermocyclators are used. the lightcycler® 480 reference thermocyclator team. Marca Roche, has been used for the last 10 years to analyze thousands of tests and has contributed to the development of research within the Postgraduate in Health and Animal Production of the FMVZ. Many scientific publications have been supported by the results obtained with processes in this team.
