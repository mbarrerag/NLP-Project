# TRENDS IN RESEARCH AND TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT IN THE PRODUCTIVE EACHINERY (AMERICANA L.)

The avocado is a growing crop in the world due to the increase in consumption of this fruit. Therefore, the research in this productive chain has increased, in technical topics, seeking increase of yield, decreasing losses from pests and diseases and generating processes that allow to maintain the quality of the fruit over time. However, research and development, as well as studies of current trends in other areas of the chain, are unknown. The objective of this article was to know the trends in research and technological development in the productive chain of avocado. For this it was carried out an exercise of technological scanning, using bibliometric and patentometric techniques with information from the databases Scopus and Lens.org, the information collected was refined and analyzed. It was found that the countries with the greatest number of scientific publications were United States, United Kingdom and Netherlands, with weaknesses in the collaborative work between countries. The most productive authors are persons linked to the academy, the magazine Acta Horticulturae presented a high number of publications. The topics of cosmetics and pharmaceutical were found to be relevant to the scientific sector and considered to be relevant to this productive chain, due to the dynamics of the most productive market
