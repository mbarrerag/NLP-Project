# SONGS AND ADVANTAGES

The book collects collaborative research with the community Carijona de Puerto Nare (Guaviare). Local knowledge about birds, identification, ecology, uses, meanings, and the academic gaze of ornithology was put into dialogue. The collective research process was illustrated and photographed, and the local and academic knowledge is presented species by species. A map of the path of the Loro lagoon, where the community can develop ecotourism activities, is attached. At the end there is a guide for identifying the identified species
