# High-range coal liquid phase exfoliation for graphene oxide.

This document is the result of the work of obtaining graphene oxide using as material of starting carbon semi anthracite from the San José mine of the municipality of Boavita (Boyacá). The objective of the project is to evaluate the obtaining of graphene oxide by means of the method of liquid phase exfoliation using high range coal as raw material. Two variables are taken into account: the size of the starting particle (greater than 0.15 mm, 0.15 mm to 0,05 mm and less than 0.05 mm) and the removal of mineral matter by means of a basic washing of the samples. A protocol of grinding and selection of particle sizes is designed, then the basic washing of the samples is carried out, the process of oxidation by means of the Modified Hummers Method, to finally proceed to make the exfoliation in liquid phase (LPE) using as surfactant the Pluronic 123.
