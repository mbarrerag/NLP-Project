# Cost of nursing care for people with venous vascular ulcer

Abstract Introduction: The care of venous vascular ulcers has been considered a public health problem due to its high incidence and social and economic impact. To determine the direct costs of nursing care in people with venous vascular ulcers attended by a health service provider in the city of Bogotá. Observational, descriptive, retrospective study, carried out between 1 January 2019 and 31 January 2020; which was based on the review of clinical histories of people with venous vascular ulcer. The sample was made up of 52 clinical histories, selected by a nursing assessment instrument. The variables analyzed were age and sex, using descriptive statistics and cost analysis. The predominant sex was female (69.24%) and the average age of 64 years. The direct cost was 70.109.504.76 COP and the average estimate 1.348.259 COP, value that included the cost of dressings, elastocompression, cleaning and consultation. The costs in the care of venous vascular ulcers are immersed in the care packages of care, which are not explicitly included
