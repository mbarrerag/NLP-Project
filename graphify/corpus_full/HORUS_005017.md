# Seismic attenuation identification of geological fault lubrication areas associated with fluid migration: example case of Valle Medio del Magdalena

The Middle Valley of Magdalena (VMM) is a sedimentary sub-surface, with a complex tectonic context and geological history, which has produced an intense deformation and fracture. There are several faults in the region that could present current activity promoted by fluids that can migrate through the subsoil, from natural and anthropic sources. The seismic attenuation in the region could show anomalies by heterogeneities and fluids in the subsoil in interaction with the faults. We performed calculations of intrinsic seismic attenuation, dispersive and total, with 1653 seismic events between 2016 and 2022 for a selected area. We can see a domain of intrinsic seismic attenuation over the dispersive. The distribution of values is presented in maps from different years. Calculations of intrinsic, scattering, and total sixmic attenuation were made using 1653 sixmic events between 2016 and 2022 for a selected area. The results show a domain of intricate over scaling sixmic attention. The distribution of these values is presented in maps for the different years. Additionally, a geological profile modeling was carried out for the Trasandean sixmic line ANH-TR-2006-4A, to be integrated with the sixmic attenuation results.
