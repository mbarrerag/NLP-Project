# The consequences of mining extractivism on the food sovereignty of peasant households in the Loma district (Cesar, Colombia) during the period 2010 - 2022

This research project arises from the need to contribute to the understanding of the consequences of mining extraction in the food processes and the construction of food sovereignty in peasant households, which is framed within the context of the technocratic approach to rural development during the period 2010 _x0096_ 2022. This research seeks to determine the consequences of mining extraction in the Food Sovereignty of peasant households in the La Loma region, which has as its main economic activity coal mining, located in the municipality of El Paso (Cesar, Colombia) based on the characterization of the role of mining extractivism in the framework of the Tecnocratic Approach to Rural Development in Colombia during the period 2010-2022, later, to identify and characterize the food transformations promoted from that approach in peasant households from the correction where research is carried out; to establish the relationship of consequence in the food sovereignty of peasant communities. It is expected that this research will be a contribution to understanding the technocratic approach to rural development in food extraction and destruction contexts that separates peasant communities from their means of production directly affecting their own cultures
