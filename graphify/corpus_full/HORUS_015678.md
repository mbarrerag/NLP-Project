# VALUE ADDED VAT REFORM SCHANGES: REDISTRIBLE AND FISH IMPACTS

This book compiles the reflections of academics from the most prestigious universities in the country, the recommendations of public policy aimed at addressing the fiscal challenges that the country is facing in order to achieve a more efficient and progressive system. The book is aimed at those who make decisions, academics, analysts, students and those interested in improving the Colombian state’s fiscal policy. The chapter under my own authority is in particular responsible for analyzing the possibilities of reform of VAT and its impacts both redistributive and fiscal. It is important to note that personal and corporate income taxes also have various problems that need to be corrected, however, the interest in VAT arises from the high debate that it raises in public opinion, and the possibility of building mechanisms that prevent its regressivity and at the same time minimize the tax spending produced by its current structure
