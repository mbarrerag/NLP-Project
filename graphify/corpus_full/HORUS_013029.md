# Collective action and community rural tourism in the Province of Alto Ricaurte, Boyacá

This study aims to establish the collective action capacity of rural communities in the Alto Ricaurte, department of Boyacá, in relation to rural community tourism (TRC), as an activity that contributes to the sustainable development of this territory. It is an approach that starts from the collective action, adopted as a strategy for the organization of self-management, based on decisions and arrangements related to cooperation. The contribution of this work, in general way for the TRC, consists of indicating how from the different levels of collective action it is possible to re-signify the value of the community to transform the needs into actions of self-management, and in seeking to overcome the contradictions that exist in the same community, product of external and internal dynamics, and cultural factors. Hence, the development of the TRC is provided through cooperation arrangements in social environments of pugnacity, hostility and conflict within the same communities. This, involves the work of understanding of the TRC understood as a generator of the advantages of its own work in local communities, an aspect that has not been sufficiently investigated in the literature. For the case of the High Ricaurte, the existing organizations of the community, the collective, the vels of collective action, the value of the community can be re-identified, not only transforming needs into self-management actions, but also in seeing to overcome, through cooperative arrangements, the social environment of fighting, hostility, and conflict within the same communities. This involves a deep work of understanding, where RCBT constitutes a sector that boots the integration and use of local communities’ own work, which has not been successfully investigated in previous literature.
