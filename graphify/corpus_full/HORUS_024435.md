# CHANGES IN THE STATIONALITY OF LLUVIA SIGNED IN THE PEDOGINETIC PROCESS OF MIOCEAN MEDIUM PALEOSUELS IN TACOA, COLOMBIA

The sale, one of the most important fossil sites of the average Miocene at a global level, resides in the paleosols of the Honda Group in the Tatacoa (Huila, Colombia).The characteristics redoxi morficas of the grey soils of the Formation La Victoria (~13.6 to ~13.1 Myr) and the red color of the soils of the Formation Villavieja (~13.1 to 12.0 Myr) register a change in the humidity regime, of paleosuelos predominantly humid (in a seasonally flooded wetland, correlated with the Pebas System) to paleosuelos subject to an alternate regime of humidity and drought. Low values of Eh and pH values between alkaline and alkaline in paleosuelos of La Victoria are interpreted from the association of pedogenic clays (flation < 0.2 μm) due to the presence of the soil, the soil of the most mild and the products of this series of meteorization, along with the high concentrations of basic cations (Ca2+, Mg2+, Na+)
