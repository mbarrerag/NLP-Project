# Comparative analysis of the results of geotechnical modelling of the excavation of the colon theatre and the results of the instrumentation installed in situ.

This grade work develops the analysis of a deep excavation in three dimensions for the case study “Expansion of the Colón Theater”, by which the finite element method is implemented through the software MIDAS GTS NX, in order to establish geotechnical criteria and validate the behavior of the soil in study. Subsequent to the bibliographic review and collection of geotechnical information, the Mohr Coulomb constitutive model is selected, whose parameters were defined by analysis of information from the study of the project soils. In the model assembly the existence of structural elements involved and their soil interaction – structure was considered. The results of lateral deformation accumulated in the analysis sections were compared to those reported by the in-situ installed inclinedmeters, in this way it is possible to calibrate the model until obtaining the most approximate behavior to what was presented in field during the stages of the construction process.
