# Modeling the impact of road extreme management on pollution using dynamic systems

We present a mathematical model based on dynamic systems that evaluates the ecological and economic impact of road maple management on the ecosystem service of pollination. Our model takes into account the nature of the ecosystem adjacent to the roadside, the type of road associated with the edge, and its physical and ecological composition. It also considers different changing factors related to the shape maintenance is carried out, such as cutting height, maintenance period, frequency and scope, biomass extraction, and pollution caused, among others
