# DETECTION AND CRITICAL TREATMENT MEASUREMENT BARGES: A SCOPE REVIEW

to identify nursing strategies for the detection of delirium and barriers for its measurement in the intensive care unit (ICU). revision of scope based on the methodology of the Joanna Briggs Institute and the PRISMA-ScR protocol, by searching six databases with the terms "delirium", "diagnosis", "nursing", "critical care" and "critical care" protocol, and the extraction of SUMARI software. 43 studies were selected for analysis and categorization in two emerging topics: "Frequent tools for the detection of delirium" and "Barreras" reported by nursing staff for the assessment of delirium." Conclusions: there is a wide variety of validated and standardized tools for the detection of delirium in intensive care. However, their use is limited in many cases due to the different barriers of the patient, the evaluator and the multidisciplinary team. Therefore, it is important to train health personnel to be familiar with the delirium, its detection and its use in many cases because of different barriers of the patient, the evaluation and multidisciplinary equipment
