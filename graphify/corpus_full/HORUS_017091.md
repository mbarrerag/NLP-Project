# Strengthening the playful dimension: A journey through acquired physical disability.

Within the emotional and bodily path, which remains after having acquired a physical disability, we find that the concept that has of the body, experiences a modification, since the subject is dwelling a body that has transmuted and must start an atypical path to continue habiting it, which results in re-signifying the perception of its body image, since this aspect is strongly impacted in this unexpected event called: acquired disability. The process of reconstruction and/or transformation of its concept of body image is accompanied by new ways to feel and live its body and by different ways to weave affective and social relationships that continue to contribute to the personal construction and make possible a basis for the people who arrive and will arrive at this same path. The journey that will be carried out, places the ludic dimension and the strengthening of this, as a route to explore to accompany the encounter with the new perception of the body image present and acquired. There is a strong interest in strengthening that human dimension to identify the important aspects that impact positively in the body recognitions, this work will be displayed during the research. This research seeks to find possibilities that we can approach to the concept of the present and the present and acquired body., release, gather and build together ways to continue to know the infinity of the body and all the places that we can inhabit with it when life decides to transform us.
