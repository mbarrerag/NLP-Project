# Efficient strategy for improving the capabilities of large language models (LLMs)

The large language models (LLMs) have been consolidated as a milestone in the field of artificial intelligence and natural language processing, but their large scale implementation is limited by the need for high computational resources. This work proposes that from a base model, they explore and combine techniques of processing and careful selection of data, training and adjustments in architecture, in order to improve the efficiency of models in environments with limited resources and on a delimited knowledge basis. The methodological approach included the definition of criteria for the development of reliable data sets, controlled experimentation with different configurations and the systematic evaluation of the resulting variants in terms of capacity, versatility, response time and security.
