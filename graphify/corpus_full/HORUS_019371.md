# Symptoms of the lower urinary tract, falls and fractures in adult men: Associations and clinical outcomes. Systematic literature review and metaanalysis.

The symptoms of the lower urinary tract (STUI) are frequently present in the adult population. Frequent trips to the secondary bathroom to the symptoms of emptying and filling in patients with comorbidities and elderly involve the danger of loss of balance, falls and serious injuries. This can impair the subsequent motor function, independence, quality of life and survival in the elderly. The objective was to evaluate the association between the presence of urinary symptoms in the elderly and the risk of falls and fractures by systematic review and metaanalysis to establish possible causal relationships. We carried out a systematic search of the literature of articles indexed in databases (PubMed, Central Cochrane, LILACS and EMBASE).We performed metaanalysis of fixed effects of probabilities between urinary symptoms and falls. Of 45 studies found 15 articles from 8 countries were included in the systematic review, two of them related the presence of urinary fractures and symptoms and no study evaluated the impact of the treatment. 3 studies were metaanalysized, achieving a relationship in the presence of urinary symptoms and falls (OR 2.10, 95% CI)
