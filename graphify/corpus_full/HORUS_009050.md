# BASIC ASPECTS OF JOINT LINE MODELS WITH CONTINUOUS RESPONSE. APPLICATIONS IN R.

The aim of this mini-course is to motivate the use of the mixed regression model and recognize in it a valuable tool in the generation of knowledge from complex data, such as longitudinal data and repeated measurements. In addition to showing applications to real data, aspects related to the mathematical and statistical theory of these models are described
