# High density vertical interconnection systems: a reinterpretation of the spatial interconnection concept from the matbuilding model

This final master's work reveals a systematic procedure for the approach of interconnected spaces of high density in vertical from the reinterpretation of the morphological and qualitative principles of the mat-building reworked or transgressed under the precept of a vertical notion of the collective and individual space, since, in the models mat-building is recognized a systematic procedure due to its configuration from projectual operations such as: “decentrate, fractionate and break”, which, in conjunction and simultaneously, enable multiple routes, visual, combinations and continuous interconnections, so that, it is possible to create collective spaces to enhance the event understood as encounters and places of introspection, through the multiple spatial interconnections, which, leads to the operational development of a “collective fact and an anonymous or private fact”, but also, establishes relations with the immediate environment and with the parts that compose the project as a coherent whole. The above establishes a counterposition to the current configuration of buildings thought as isolated planes that tend to segregate the individual in spaces with a poor response to the needs of a diverse inhabitant.
