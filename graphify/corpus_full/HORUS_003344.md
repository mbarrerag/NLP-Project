# Duffing oscillator coupled to a linear oscillator: Analytical solution

The Duffing equation has established successful applications in modeling different physical phenomena, involving reinforcing springs, beam buckling, nonlinear electronic circuits, among others [1]. Given a physical system with associated potential energy, its dynamics in the proximity of a potential minimum can normally be approximated by a linear oscillator. The oscillator's restorative force coefficient, includes the curvature of the potential well, comprises the first term of the Taylor series in which it can develop the positiondependent force acting on the aforementioned physical system. Taking more terms of the series increases the definiteness of the approximation. The following term of the Taylor series, which presents qualitative changes, is the one that goes with the cube of the position (while the quadratic term only alters the equilibrium position). Narasimha [4] summarized the general equations governing the dynamics of a thin wire. The midpoint of a vibrating wire,with its ends embedded, can be described as a one-dimensional effective oscillator. It was shown that for this oscillator, the cubic term should not be neglected under resonance conditions.
