# Manufacture and characterization of intermetallic systems of the rare earth-based RT2AI10 family using flow technique.

When dealing with experimental work in condensed matter physics or material science, it is essential to have reliable, carefully designed and manufactured samples, so that they exhibit the different physical phenomena that are desired to be studied, such as: magnetism, superconductivity, thermoelectricity, heavy fermions, quantum criticality, among others. This grade work focuses on the adaptation and implementation of a space in the laboratory of the NGF, for the manufacture and structural characterisation of monocrystals of the family RT2Al10(R: rare soil, T: transition metal) using an experimental technique called flow technique, with the purpose of achieving samples of a high quality, to then measure its structural and compositional characteristics, giving way to a new line of research in materials science at the National University of Colombia - Bogotá Headquarters, in which in future it will be possible to study electronic, magnetic and thermal properties derived from the degrees of electronic correlation and other physical phenomena that occur in this family of compounds; and that they are at the forefront of research in new materials, specifically in the line of quantum materials
