# Understanding the collective reintegration of FARC-EP ex-combatants from the perspective of social worlds

In this article I analyze the process of collective reintegration of former members of FARC-EP from the perspective of social worlds.The term social worlds arises as a sociological perspective developed by the School of Chicago, which understands the processes of social change through the understanding of the interdependences and shared schemes between different social groups that transform themselves through new forms of communication and relationation.For this, I will explore the contributions of former members of FARC-EP to the peace process in Colombia and the implications that their interaction with other social worlds has in their proper social, political and economic reintegration. Under a multi-situated ethnography, during 2018 and 2019 in three territories of Colombia (Tavigua Bonita, Llano Grande and San José de León), I will explore the local practices of the former members of FARC-EP and their relationship with national, international and surrounding communities.For this research I conducted 58 interviews with former members of FARC-EP and 12 neighboring communities, which were complemented by the participatory and daily processes of field.
