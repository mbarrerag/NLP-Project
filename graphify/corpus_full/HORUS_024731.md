# Prognosis of mortality rates in Colombia: an approach based on functional data analysis.

This project uses functional data analysis to model and forecast mortality curves in Colombia, in particular, using the methodology proposed by Hyndman and Ullah in 2007, which employs adjusted models on functional curves with temporary dependence in order to understand the mortality trend and their respective forecasts. The annual series of mortality curves by age and sex for Colombia from 1950 to 2019, which have been constructed mainly from the information available on the Latin American Human Mortality Database page (“LAHMD”). The projection window of mortality curves is 10 years. The analysis is carried out in a disaggregated manner between men and women, and in a totalized way. Hyndman and Ullah methodology offers multiple advantages, allowing to control defects implicit in the initial information, provides forecasts that show the level of mortality for any age, offers projections that are closer to figures reported by official entities compared to projections obtained through the classic methodology of Lee and Carter, among other advantages
