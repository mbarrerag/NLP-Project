# The emergence of inclusive innovation systems: contributions to their understanding from agent-based modelling.

Inclusive innovation systems are intended to contribute to the reduction of social exclusion through innovation. Understanding how this type of systems works is of great interest in the research agendas of the countries of the Global South, given the high indices of social problems that characterize the region. One way to contribute to this understanding is to explain how these systems emerge; this purpose is addressed in this thesis in an evolutionary way, through the Agent-Based Modeling. A model was created based on theoretical bases on systems of innovation, inclusive innovation, new agents and their relationships, learning processes, knowledge, directionality, innovation capabilities and capabilities for inclusion; relevant elements in inclusive innovation systems. The model was validated in the agricultural sector of the municipality of La Unión, Colombia and allows to simulate different scenarios representing conventional innovation systems and different configurations of inclusive innovation systems.
