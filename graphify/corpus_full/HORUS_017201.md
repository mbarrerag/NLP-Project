# Pedro Nel Gómez: a look at the body through the fresco mural (1935-1954)

This research study studies the artistic gaze of the muralist antioqueño Pedro Nel Gómez (1899-1984) through the representations of the body present in his works between 1935 and 1954, years that comprise his first two great muralist ensembles after returning to Colombia in 1930: the mural series of the Municipal Palace of Medellín (present-day Museo de Antioquia) and that located in the Aula Máxima of the National University with headquarters in the same city. Specifically, of the previous pictorial collections, the corresponding representation of women, rural workers and urban workers is included, groups widely portrayed in the work of Gómez with which it held a particular proximity due to the sociability of the works as a political and educational face, areas of their professional performance equally addressed for this occasion. The iconographic and iconological analysis of the images in the murals, is also taken from the review and investigation of the written primary sources that account the author of the works together with the places and production periods behind it. Consequently, interviews, correspondence, notes of conferences and other material related to Pedro Nel Gómez, are also taken from the articles of the press referring to the Colombian urban workers, groups widely carried in Gómez's work, with what he was particularly close due to the socialities resulting from his faces as a political and university professor, areas of his professional performance also addressed for this occasion. The iconographic and iconological analysis of the images that make up the murals treated, is complemented with the review and investigation of primary written sources that account for the author of the works along with the places and periods of production being them. Therefore, interviews, correspondence, literature notes and other archive material related to Pedro Nel Gómez a included, as well as magazine and press articles of the time referring to the Colombian art and culture
