# Coca, territory and health: Medicinal uses of Erythroxylum species in a rural community of Santander (Colombia).

A ethnobotanical study was carried out between 2022-2023 in a peasant community resident of the region of Capitanejo, Santander (Colombia). to characterize the use given to the coca plant (Erythroxylum sp) in the region. a bibliographic review was made, visits were made to the region, samples were taken of the plants, open interviews were conducted. The species used in the region was found to be E. novogranatense var. novogranatense. The forms of use are: chewed and mixed with lime, infused and macerated with alcohol for topical use. There is another form not used in the region, about which they know, coca flour. Among the main reasons of use was found: to increase energy in the work and vitality, improve the mood, reduce the insomnia, calm the anxiety, manage osteomuscular pains, improve the health of the bones and as food. There is a high probability that this is a habit transmitted from pre-Columbian times in this region: probably, the form of use of coca comes from syncretism among the indigenous population who lived in the region
