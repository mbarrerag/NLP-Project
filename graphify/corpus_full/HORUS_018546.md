# TRANSCULTURAL ADOPTION OF THE COLOMBIAN VERSION OF THE SOLIDAD SCALE OF UCLA IN PATIENTS WITH CANCER DIAGNOSTIC

Undesired loneliness is a construct that has been related to negative outcomes in different aspects of health, including those of patients with cancer. UCLA's loneliness scale is a widely used instrument in the context of health research to measure this construct, and in Colombia version 03 of this instrument is not translated, nor adapted transculturally. Transculturally translate and adapt the UCLA loneliness scale, version 03 for use in cancer patients in Colombia. The translation and transcultural adaptation process followed the methodology proposed by the EORTC group, with the aim of achieving semantic equivalence with the original version of the scale available in English. A pilot test was conducted with 20 Colombian patients diagnosed with different types of cancer, who attended the National Cancer Institute of Colombia. There were no changes nor in the translation of instructions nor in the response options. In the reconciled version, the UC1 item was modified.
