# Hand controlled prosthesis with EEG signals

In this paper, it is described how brain waves produced in the left lateral lobe of the human brain and detected with a NeuroSky MindFlex atrial were used to voluntarily control a hand prosthesis. In the first instance, the signals EEG (electroencephalographic) are detected by a BCI (Brain Control Interface) and after being analyzed with the help of an ARDUINO microcontroller its frequency spectrum is divided into Alpha, Beta, Theta and Gamma ranges. As a result of EEG analysis, it is found that voluntary blinking mainly affects the power values related to the alpha band, such that this frequency band is finally used to voluntarily direct and controlled the abduction and adduction movements of the prosthesis fingers
