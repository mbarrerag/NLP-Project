# Opportunities in the use of blockchain technology for the energy market in Colombia.

The trust, reputation and authority are attributes that characterize the mediating institutions in the exchange of goods and services between companies and individuals. Centralised entities such as banking, financial institutions, public instrument offices, clearing houses, among others. These entities apply rules and protocols of exchange recognized by the agents with whom they interact, keep records of transactions, and in some cases, can serve as guarantors for the fulfillment of agreements between different parties. Particularly in the energy sector, the development of distributed energy generation technologies, leveraged by the competitiveness of renewable energy sources, has created a strong pressure for the highly regulated and centralized energy markets, allowing effective exchange between prosumers and energy service companies, encouraging competition, transparency and flexibility. However, the control, monitoring and reconciliation of these exchanges presents a challenge for traditional institutions. It is in this sense that the distributed registration technology (distributed Led Technology) offers solutions for validation, control of origin, registration and integrity so that such transactions can be carried out routinely
