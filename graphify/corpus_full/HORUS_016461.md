# RESEARCH IN COMMUNITY REHABILITATIONBRD: CONSTRUCTED CAMES TO ENHANCE THE OBJECTIVES OF THE CONVENTION ON THE RIGHTS OF PERSONS WITH DISABLED PERSONS

In a diverse and stressful scenario, community-based rehabilitation (RBC) can be understood as a work proposal linked to the daily life of people in different areas of construction, use of knowledge and innovation, with new practices that can permeate public policies. As the RBC is a strategy for the implementation of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and is a political practice insofar as it provokes transformations in the areas of daily life of the people participating in it, research favours ethical reflection that implies the recognition of the other as a subject. This chapter aims to advance in the reflection on the contribution of RBC research to the commitments of the Convention and to local development
