# Context analysis of crimes of sexual violence in transitional justice processes.

This paper aims to highlight the importance of contextual analysis of crimes of sexual violence in the current transitional justice process in Colombia and to propose an appropriate strategy for the application of this analysis of context based on perspectives and tools to assume judicial work and investigations, in a different way from how it has been carried out in the past, such as interdisciplinarity, intersectionality and non-hegemonic feminism. To this end, the main national and international background of the concept is presented, a diagnosis of the previous exercises of context building, the challenges that left such exercises, and the opportunities that the Special Jurisdiction for Peace has, for a better application of the contextual analysis based on the new proposals presented
