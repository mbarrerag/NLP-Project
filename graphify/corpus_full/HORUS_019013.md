# ACHOUSE AND PROXIMITY AS POSSIBLE INTERRELATION IN OCCUPATION TELETERACY: EXPERIENCE OF PRACTICE WITH CHILDREN AND CHILDREN OF THE CHILD BORROWING BODY OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

This article proposes a reflection based on the experiences of a group of students of Occupational Therapy during their practice in the area of education, in dialogue with the bets of the ethical perspective of Lévinas and the construction of horizons of meaning in the educational field, which challenge understandings of education as an instrument for the mere learning of skills and concepts that are installed in the subjects. The practice was carried out with two groups of children of the Kindergarten of the National University of Colombia during the first semester of 2021, in the modality of occupational teletherapy. It is emphasized in the process of constitution of spaces of learning and reflection on the professional activity in the school, in times of social distancement by the pandemic of COVID-19, and in other ways to build relationships based on proximity and the welcome in teletherapy
