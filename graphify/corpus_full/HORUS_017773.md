# Effects of music therapy on subjective well-being in family members who have been victims of enforced disappearance

This research took place between November 2021 and March 2022 in this one carried out a process of musicotherapeutic with a virtual approach, which had a total of 15 sessions that were developed with the participation of a family victim of enforced disappearance. The research process was carried out with a narrative design of case study and aimed to describe the effects that music therapy has on the subjective well-being of family victims of enforced disappearance. Among the main findings we found that music therapy served to improve the mood of the user, having a positive impact on the affective aspect of subjective well-being. In turn, the self-esteem of the use improved due to the development of experiences that expanded her self-knowledge and her understanding of the internal and external world.
