# Democracy in Latin American education: The contribution of the democratic exercise of parents in the political socialization of the Sorrento I.E.D. school, Bogotá, D.C., Colombia.

This text is the result of the research “Democracy in Latin American education: The contribution of the democratic exercise of parents in the political socialization of the Sorrento I.E.D. school, bogotá, d.c., colombia” carried out at the master's degree in latin american political studies (maepla) of the national university of colombia. The integrality of the document is about the case study of the educational institution, which aims to determine the political socialization of parents in relation to democratic trends in Latin America, based on a study from a qualitative approach, where the use of bibliographic tracing techniques, focal groups and conversational; similarly, the use of hermeneutics as a form of analysis supported by Atlas Ti software is made. Among the main results we can say that for this case of study the participation of parents of families works as a mechanism to convenience, which does not allow effective political socialization; a question that is not far from Latin American processes where parents depend on institutional structures and participation is low, a question that only increases in scenarios of violation of rights
