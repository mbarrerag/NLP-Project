# “For the defense of life and health”: The case of the Popular Network of First Aids (REDPAS) in the framework of the Social Starburst of 2021 in Colombia

In the last cycles of social protests in Latin America and with the increase of the violent state response, different forms of collective action are being consolidated to provide attention in physical and mental health to the protestor. Since there are no investigations that offer interpretative keys around these forms of collective action developed to try to solve health problems-disease-care-discontinuance-death in the framework of social protest and violence triggered, especially in Colombia, before a poor and even absent health system, it was sought to understand this fact, through the case of the Popular Network of First Aids (REDPAS) in the city of Bogotá during the Social Strike of 2021.
