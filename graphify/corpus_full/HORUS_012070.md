# Identities and imagined communities: the investment of Colombian English teachers in their professional development

The professional development of English teachers responds to individual needs and social discourses on teaching, learning and using the language. This case study explored the factors that increased or decreased the active and continuous participation of nine Colombian English teachers in professional development programs. It was found that English teachers invest in their professional development if they can develop three imagined identities — competent English speakers, experts in English teaching and competent ICT users — and join the imagined community of “bilinguals.” The journey to imagined identities and the imagined community is full of ambivalences generated by the socio-political context of their work and the educational linguistic policies of the country
