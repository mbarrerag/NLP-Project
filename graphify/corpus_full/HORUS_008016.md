# SPACE ANALYSIS OF THE ECOGEOGRAPHY DIVERSITY OF MELOTHRIA PENDULA L. FOR IN-SITU AND EX-SITE CONSERVATION IN MEXICO

Melothria pendula L. is a species that is used as food and medicine in Mexico and has different names, being the most common sandiita. It is a plant that, although not in cultivation, can be an alternative for food and nutritional security at local and regional level. The objective of this study was to characterize ecogeographically the known populations of sandiita in Mexico, determining its adaptive range and possible sites for in situ conservation and its collection for ex situ conservation. To this end, a set of 1,270 registers of M. pendula were consolidated from the presence of herbaria. The generation of adaptive scenarios was possible by means of the development of a map of ecogeographical characterisation of the territory (ELC), with a previous determination of the abiotic variables of greater influence in the distribution of the species. Of a total of 179 variables bioclimatic, edifica and geophysical analyzed categories, 11 were important for the distribution of the species, which were related to the temperature of the coldest fourth species, annual precipitation and some variables of monthly precipitation, texture and characteristics of the soil, altitude and solar radiation
