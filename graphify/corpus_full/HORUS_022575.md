# The Leadership Schools in Sovereignty and Food and Nutrition Security: a scenario for building food citizenship?.

Food Citizenship is understood as a theoretical and epistemological commitment in construction for Colombia, which presents conceptual background of authors like Gómez-Benito & Lozano among others, which have contributed to the idea of understanding it as a concept that could be a point of union among other more well-known concepts such as Food and Nutritional Security (SAN) and Food Sovereignty (SoA). Therefore, the CA is related to the ways in which both urban and rural citizens access a healthy and quality food supply, which also generates processes of mobilization and social organization to achieve it, and exercises their food, cultural and ancestral preferences, being informed about what is healthy from the nutritional and productive field. In turn, it recognizes the conditions in which the agri-food chain develops as a means of production and distribution of food, in which it can present social and environmental inequalities, and therefore act coherent in the exercise of its food practices in the private and public sphere, generating impact in the governance of the food system.
