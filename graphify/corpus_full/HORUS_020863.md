# YOUNG SMBRATING HUERTS SECKING COMMUNITY

This project of joint extension, is a theoretical commitment - practical to strengthen the community cultural initiatives agroecological and the forms of youth organization present in the municipality San José de Manizales. In an exercise that strengthens the activity with the young people of the municipality of the Foundation _x0093_Comunativa Huertas Urbanos_x0094_, while involving young university students of the National University of Colombia, as collaborators of a community process that contributes to their integral formation and to the connection of the University with the social environment of the city. The process is posed through a methodology of popular education about agroecology and concrete generation of urban gardens. From the interest of developing jointly a praxis with critical view to the current urban conflicts and betting on the collective social construction of future scenarios of spatial justice and defense of the territories of the difference from the Sanecology urban group.
