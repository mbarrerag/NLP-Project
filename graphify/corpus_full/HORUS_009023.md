# Fly to the noroeste amazonico.

Aviation is an activity that profoundly transformed the life of humanity. Despite its rapid development, Vaupés, a distant region on the border of Colombia and Brazil occupied mostly by indigenous people, only enjoyed the benefits of late air connection. The study of printed and visual sources allows us to conclude that the flights not only allowed the arrival of goods to sustain extractive activities, but also the work of evangelization of Catholics and Protestants and that additionally generated new population flows and effects on native populations. Even today the airway remains the best means of communication with the Vaupés in the two countries, but also the most expensive and a means to continue with the extraction of other raw materials
