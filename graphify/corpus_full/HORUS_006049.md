# Analysis of radiodynamic characteristics in images of F-18-FDG PET and Multiparametric Magnetic Resonance as predictors of response to neoadjuvant chemotherapy treatment in breast cancer

The purpose of this study was to evaluate whether the radiomic characteristics derived from F-18 FDG PET images and the ADC maps obtained by magnetic resonance (RM) are able to predict the complete pathological response (pCR) to neoadjuvant chemotherapy (QNA) in patients with locally advanced breast cancer. 30 patients without prior treatment submitted to PET with F18-FDG and RM were included in this study. The specimens of the pretreatment biopsy were used to extract the immunohistochemical characteristics of the tumor. The histopathological results of the post-QNA resected tumor were used to classify between PCR and No-pCR. 1702 radiomic characteristics and 4 by biopsy were extracted. The reproducibility before intra- and inter-observer variability was evaluated using the intraclass correlation coefficient ICC>0.7%. The reduction of the dimensionality was performed by selection of minimal redundancy and maximum relevance.
