# PEACH PALM FRUIT (BACTRIS GASIPAES) PEEL AS A SOURCE OF PROVITAMIN A CAROTENOIDS.

The total carotenoid content (RV = 836.5 μg/g DW; YV = 75.0 μg/g DW; CV was below 9% in all measurements) and Provitamin A activity (RV = 33.1 μg RAE/g DW; YV = 3.9 μg RAE/g DW) were higher in red variety than in yellow variety. The main Provitamin A carotenoids in both varieties were: b-carotene, g-carotene, Zg- carotene, 13-cis-b-carotene, d-carotene, 9-Z-b-carotene, and a-carotene. Lycopene was also detected but it does not contribute to the provitamin A activity. The drying of B. gasipaes peel allows for a food additive with agroindustrial potential to reduce the environmental impact, which could be incorporate to fortify different foods
