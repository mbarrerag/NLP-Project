# Manufacture of a chocolate (Theobroma cocoa) with partial replacement of cocoa butter by Copoazú fat (Theobroma grandiflorum)

The aim of the study is to develop an innovative formulation that can partially replace cocoa butter in the production of chocolate without altering its structure. To achieve this, the fatty substitute extracted from the fruit of Theobroma grandiflorum, known as Copoazú. Its physical and chemical characteristics are similar to those of cocoa butter, decisive factors in the final structure of chocolate.
