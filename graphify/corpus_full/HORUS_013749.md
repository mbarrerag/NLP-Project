# Wear resistance of coatings (Ti,Cr,Al)N-Cu produced by the co-sputting technique magnetron reactive

In this research work, coatings of (Al,Ti,Cr)N were synthesized with different copper contents by means of the technique of co-sputting magnetron reagent and the technique of high power impulse (HiPIMS) deposited on steel substrates AISI 4340 and WC-Co. The effect of coatings on mechanical and tribological properties was evaluated by varying copper contents between 0 and 1.74 at. % Cu, for co-sputting, and between 0 and 4.48 at. % for HiPIMS. In addition, the chemical, structural and morphological composition was increased on the properties of the coating. The chemical composition revealed a quasi-stequiometric coating. The XRD diagrams showed a structure type B1, FCC-NaCl with preferential growth in orientation (111) for both techniques. Overall, the rate of wear in air decreased by 22.77 to 29.35% on WC-Co and 4340 respectively on MS and on oil, almost 100% for 4340 and 84% for WC-Co. The resistance to polarisation of coatings deposited by HIPIMS increased by 49.73 to 29.73 % compared to those deposited by MS on 4340 and in a unit on WC-Co.
