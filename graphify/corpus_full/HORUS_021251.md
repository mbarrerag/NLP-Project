# Cytomegalovirus interactions - periodontal bacteria and their effect on periodontal ligament fibroblasts and gingival fibroblasts

The global prevalence rate of periodantitis is generally high and varies depending on each region, due to the fact that it is determined by the increase in age and income of each country, however, global distributions up to 44% (Nazir et al, 2020). On the other hand, the IV National Study of Oral Health - ENSAB IV reported that 62% of the Colombian adult population has periodontitis, plus, the increase in the extent and severity of this disease has a direct proportional relationship with age, this pathological condition is expressed increasingly from age 18 to 79 years old (IV National Study of Oral Health, 2014). In addition, according to the DANE projections from 1985 to 2018, a trend in population ageing has been observed, where the population aged over 59 years has an annual growth of 3.5% (Situational Hall of the Adult Population, the highest Ministry of Health and Social Protection, Ministry of Health, and Social Protection, Office of Social Promotion, 2018). This means that, in addition to the fact that a large percentage of Colombians suffer periodtal disease, there is a high probability that this percentage continues to increase in the next years (Situal to the population, in the population, in the age group). Additionally, periodontitis is directly related to systemic diseases such as cardiovascular disease, diabetes, preterm birth, low birth weight, preeclampsia, obstructive respiratory disease, kidney disease, cancer (oral, esophageal, gastric, pancreatic, pulmonary and prostate), arthritis and alzhéimer. Additionally, the control and treatment of periodontal disease improves the signs and symptoms of arthritis, controls the glycemia levels in type 2 diabetes and promotes good results in the treatment of kidney disease (Nazir, 2017; Ramos-García et al., 2016; Nakanishi et al., 2020). Therefore, this pathological condition in oral cavity has direct effects on general health
