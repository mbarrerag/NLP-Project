# Plecturocebus caquetensis habitat preferences (Order: Primates) in the Andean Amazonian foothills south of the Cauca department.

The primates use space and their times differentially to adapt to changes of natural or anthropogenic origin. The species Plecturocebus caquetensis, in a critical state of extinction, inhabits fragmented forests of the Amazonian Andean region to the southwestern Colombia. The southwestern zone of its distribution (Bota Caucana), protects one of the priority areas for the connectivity of the habitat, where it is necessary to know the structure of vegetation and habitat usage trends to inform decisions of management and conservation in situ. The use of habitat of a group of Plecturocebus caquetensis and its relation with attributes such as the availability of resources and structure of vegetation, in Piedmont, Cauca. The group of P. caquetensis survives in interventional habitats of temporarily floodable and non-inundable type. The period of greater supply of resources during the dry season in August and September and its period of low supply of resources is in October and November. The group was 97% of the time in the in the inundable habitat and 3% in non-influentable habitatss.
