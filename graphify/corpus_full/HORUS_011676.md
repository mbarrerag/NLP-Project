# TERRITORIAL TRANSFORMATION OF THE MIGRANT.

The Venezuelan migration has had effects both among the families who have experienced the migration of one of its members, as in the cities where these people are heading towards. Thus, the objective of this article is to analyze the territorial transformations experienced by Venezuelan migrants during their period of residence in the city of Medellín, Colombia. The research was qualitatively oriented and uses a hermeneutic method for the analysis of interviews to Venezuelans based in Medellín. It was found as results that Venezuelans have experienced a territorial transformation manifested in their spatiality, their temporality and their social relations.
