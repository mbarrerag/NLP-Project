# Structural behaviour of walls in post-sense masonry in clay block.

The masonry as a structural system represents one of the oldest and least costly construction techniques that can be used, however, its fragile fault mode, and its low performance in the face of stress stresses, makes it necessary to complement its system with the use of reinforcing steel, thus providing it with greater ductility and better behaviour before failure. Although only the use of steel seems sufficient, in some cases it is not, since the compression forces that the masonry can withstand are wasted. Thus, the behavior of the masonry wall was studied with a certain pre-compression before the service loads, for which experimental tests were carried out that allowed estimating the behavior of the post-enhanced masonry walls in clay block, and based on these to perform a numerical simulation. The above tests consisted in failure of 3 post-sensed walls and a reinforced wall in monotonic load, and on the other hand two post-ensensate walls in cyclical load. Finally, as a result, a greater load capacity could be evidenced in the post-enhanced walls and in the cycles of histeresis and the linear capacity was removed
