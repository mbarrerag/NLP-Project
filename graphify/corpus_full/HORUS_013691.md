# Singularity in the building of the Governorate of Risaralda. Works of Laureano Forero

This research analyzes the building of the Government of Risaralda and identifies, the methods and devices that gave it a singular character. Due to the management of the material used, the forceful expression of its carrying structure and the relations that it establishes with the immediate environment, result of projectual and material decisions of the architect Laureano Forero, the building has acquired a particular image in the city of Pereira, which connects it with Brutalism. With the purpose of making a critical analysis of the building, three approaches strategies were established that respond to the most representative characteristics of this, such as belonging to the group of the early work of the architect, being a governmental building made by contest and, being an exponent of Brutalism. Each approach strategy allowed to select some projects, which were studied and compared with the building of the Government of Risaralda through the characteristics of analysis, which, allowed to identify the methods and devices, which gave it a singular character to the building.
