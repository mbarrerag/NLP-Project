# Suicide in contemporary discourse

This paper is concerned with the palpable tension between new representations of suicide which seem to put aside taboos, and even incite them, and a great commitment to the prevention of the phenomenon. The thesis is divided into two main sections that ask themselves, the first, why the discourse pushes death? By analyzing the subjectivity of the epoch that goes from thinking the social bond in the capitalist discourse, to reading suicide in the coordinates of the operations of alienation and separation. The second, asks what are the causes of the failure of the institutional commitment for the prevention of suicide? By taking account of its epistemic, economic and political budgets, in which the place of the symptom is unknown, as the expression of the subject, as objector of the discourse. With the support of the theory and the psychoanalytic clinic, it reflects on the place of suicide from the contemporary to the present state, arriving at a controversial, inclusive synthesis that “suicide hysterizes the discourse”. place of the synptom like a subject’s expression, as an objector of the discourse.
