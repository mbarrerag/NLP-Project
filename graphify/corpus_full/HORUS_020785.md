# Methodological proposal for the assessment of the significance of environmental impacts in the context of prior consultation in environmental licensing processes in Colombia

The preliminary consultation is a mechanism of participation and a fundamental right for the cultural, social and economic protection of ethnic peoples, besides being a constitutional obligation in the field of environmental licensing in Colombia that must be implemented in accordance with the uses and customs of each ethnic group. The lack of normativeness that strictly and clearly regulates this right, has restricted it to developing meetings for the verification of requirements and to doing the writing of the record, denying in most cases a binding, effective and real participation to the communities, as evidenced by the analysis of the consultative processes developed by the ANLA in the last 10 years. Taking into account the above, the effectiveness and normative compliance of the process of consultation prior to environmental licensing, using the methodology of complex networks in two projects with a muisca indigenous council of the municipality of Soacha and the project of construction and operation of the plant of waste water treatment of Canoas, showing that this methodology facilitates the connection by recognizing the knowledge of the communities, being the basis of environmental evaluation of the projects and the recognition of the impacts through the causal relations.
