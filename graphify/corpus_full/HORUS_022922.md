# Model for the analysis of the assessments of a technology product on an electronic commerce platform.

According to Nasdaq, 2020 is currently increasingly being bought by more people through online trading platforms, and in turn every year more companies migrate their activity or at least a part of it to e-commerce, because of this there is a lot of information available from buyer opinions, about the degree of satisfaction obtained after having acquired the products, which in some cases are not being exploited (Mosley, 2020). As a result, this paper proposes a solution through the implementation of statistical algorithms, which allow interpreting human language quickly, efficiently and identifying the feelings underlying each of the comments, so that companies can act quickly to meet the needs of final consumers. In addition, different approaches are addressed to classify the opinions and to be able to determine the degree of polarity of these same ones, to achieve this, different scenarios are posed with different types of pre-processing and evaluating different types of feelings
