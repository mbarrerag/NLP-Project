# DESIGN OF A SERIES GAME FOR THE LEARNING OF GENDER CRICK THEMATICS IN MINES FACULTY.

The purpose of this study is to develop and validate a game that addresses gender gaps, a problem that is relevant at a global level that significantly impacts education and STEM areas, using serious games as a teaching-learning tool. A bibliographic review is started to identify concepts, elements and variables that contribute to the existence of the gender gap in society and educational environments, with a particular focus on training and understanding of these disparities in STEM areas among university students of the School of Mines. Then, it proceeds with the design of the game. Are you able to design the game using a specific methodology for the design of serious games, which consists of three phases: predesign, design and testing. The testing phase involves evaluation surveys designed to identify and validate the learning provided by the game. The results obtained from these evaluations will be presented in this document
