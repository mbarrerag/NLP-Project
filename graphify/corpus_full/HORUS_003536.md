# CULTURE, TERRITORY AND MANAGEMENT SCENARIES IN LOCAL URBAN AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT

the center for thought in culture, territory and management (cpctg); the research groups in the Management of Artistic Expressions, Memory and Creation; in regional studies, society and culture; the unesco chair for heritage management of the national university of colombia, manizales; in conjunction with the Autonomous University of Zacatecas (Mexico); the national autonomous university of honduras; CEIPA Powered by Arizona State University of Sabaneta (Antioquia), share this research work on knowledge about Governmental, Private and Community Cultural Management with narratives of experiences of cultural organizations. Likewise, it presents the work of the School Itinerant of initiatives related to the Integral Management of Heritage and the dissemination of historical memory, which allows contributing to processes of recognition and recovery of traditional memory in times of peace building from the 6th cultural management; which incline in a transformative action of and in the territories. The contents of this book, today in the eyes of readers, were socialized at the International Meeting of Culture, Territory and Management in Urban and Rural Local Development held from November 4 to 8 of 2023 in Maniza
