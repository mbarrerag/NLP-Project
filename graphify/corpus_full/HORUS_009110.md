# ANTI-NIVULANT PROFILE ON CD-1 RATONES OF NEW ISOBENZOFURAN DERIVATE DESIGNED BY VIRTUAL CRIBATE

In this study, the anticonvulsant activity of the isobenzofuranone derivatives IBF-0, M-125 and M-120, selected from virtual screening against the Nad1.2 voltage-dependent sodium channel, in the models of maximum electroshock, Pentilentetrazole-induced seizures (PTZ) and electrical stimulation threshold of 6 Hz in male and female CD-1 mice (n=6/gender) were evaluated. The compounds were initially evaluated at dosing intervals between 100 and 600 mg/kg according to the response obtained, finding a protective effect dose-response with the compound IBF-0 in the electroshock model in male mice. Likewise, the IBF-0 DE50 in this model was determined by means of an up-down methodology in order to reduce the number of animals employed (n=12), obtaining an acute IBF-0 value in the model of electroshock in male mice.
