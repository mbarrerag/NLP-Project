# SEPARATION

Cuniculus papa is a terrestrial rodent that inhabits the neotropic forests of central and South America. The objective of this study was to evaluate the effect of sperm separation on the quality of C. pasa cryopreserved semen. Sixty frozen semen straws were thawed. One aliquot was subjected to sperm separation by centrifugal separation with density gradient of one layer (SLC) and the other aliquot remained as a control group with non-separate semen. Spermatic mobility and kinetics were evaluated by means of a computerized system of sperm analysis (CASA), the functional integrity of the membrane by hypoosmotic test (HOST) and the spermatic morphology with tinction with eosine-nigrosine characteristics. The adjustment of linear models and comparison of means by Tukey was performed. SLC separation produced a higher proportion of mobile and linear spermatozoids, while control showed superior values for the curviline speed (p <0.05).
