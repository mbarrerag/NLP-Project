# Modeling, simulation and redesigning of a dish-stirling application engine to cover the energy requirement of 1 kw.

In order to reduce CO\textsubscript{2} and generate electric power, the Stirling engine coupled to a solar concentration of thermal power that allows to supply the dependence of fossil fuel, whose performance is by means of external combustion, where its most important virtue is the simplicity of its creation, its limited contribution to air pollution and it is reliable to name that the Stirling engine can carry out operation with any type of fuel, including solar radiation. The Stirling engine has one of the highest performance of a thermal machine, however, they show challenges that need to be addressed if you want to obtain a design with high handling in the same way, optimization in the design should not exceed certain maximum prices of the group.
