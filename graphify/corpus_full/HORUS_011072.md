# Bodies by ground or two cuts in the substance of the world

This thesis began as an inquiry around a pattern that perceived violent and hierarchical reciupion in a number of works of art. In developing research I called this pattern Cartesian spatial binarism. I understood that it is a representative device of long duration. This device constructs, from technologies of the gender, the gender difference maintaining the hierarchical opposition man/woman. Cartesian spatial binarism, by force of repetition, becomes an optical unconscious that directs our gaze. The Cartesian spatial binarism is a structure of vertical and horizontal coordinates -two cuts in the substance of the world-, which orders the world; it locates and spatializes human, events and things. The vertical co-ordinated is presented and understood ideologically as the place of power, of the agency, of the masculine and of the human thing. The horizontal binarism is understood as a location of the impotence, the subservitive, of the feminine and of the almost human thing, the inhuman and less than human.
