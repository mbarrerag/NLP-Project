# SQUIRLING AND NOISE STAY IN THE OURSES OF BOJAYA'S SURVIVORS

The Bojayá Massacre (Chocó) is not just a memory: its survivors still feel the consequences of this crime on hearing loss, psychological disorders, and small metal pieces embedded in their body that would increase their exposure to toxic metals such as lead and mercury. Health problems that have not been addressed more than 20 years after the fact
