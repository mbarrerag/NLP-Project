# Development of an extrusion process without added starch, case: Fruit snack

This work aimed to develop an expanded fruit snack, without added starch, taking into account the current market trends, the voice of the consumer and the engineering of processes. To determine the signals of the market and the voice of the consumer were identified the leading products in the cities of Manizales and Ibagué and the motivations, preferences and perception of consumers in how much expanded snacks. Subsequently, the expanded leader snack was quantified the determinant parameters of quality. Based on the information of motivations, preferences and some properties of characterisation of the leading product, it was applied from a methodology developed by the research group Food-fruits, which resembles that some authors call resistant engineering and functional properties, to develop a snack that met these expectations. The expanded ones were manufactured in several stages. First they were selected as basic matrix of the product to the fruit flour, the infused the fruit. quently, the leading expanded snack was quantified in terms of quality determinants. Based on the information on motivations, preferences and some characterization properties of the leading product, reverse engineering was applied to develop a snack that met these expectations. The expanded snacks were manufactured in a severe stages. First, green banana flower was selected as the basic product matrix for its natural resistant start content and functional properties, and mango and passion fruit concentrates were used as flavorings. After severe pilot extrusion tests, battles of an expanded pure fruit snack with the disabled physicochemical and textured character were obtained.
