# BASIC GUIDE TO VALIDATE HEALTH AND SAFETY INDUSTRY AT WORK

To speak of health and safety at work does not only mean recognizing it as a technical discipline that seeks to prevent workers from suffering accidents and diseases resulting from their occupation; this is imposed as one of the most important areas of study in the scientific field, able to grant the necessary tools for workers to understand and adapt to the social, economic and labour challenges of today, through the approach of paradigms that, combined with the wealth left by scientific experience, maintain a favorable state of health and well-being and generate knowledge
