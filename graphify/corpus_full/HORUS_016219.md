# RESISTANCE, YOUTH AND MEMORIA: AN ANALYSIS OF THE PARTICIPATION OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY IN RECONCILIATION IN THE CESAR. EXPERIENCES FROM THE SALON DE CLASSE.

Support for the consolidation of peace agreements and processes in the territory, in partnership with the United Nations Verification Mission and the Seed of Cultural Prisma. The contributions we have made to the implementation of the peace agreement, as a researcher and leader of the Seed of Cultural Prisma, are aimed at involving students in actions and processes that impact local and national life. Through responsible social militancy, citizenship training actions, the assessment and dissemination of the Human Rights, the importance of peace and leadership training activities. This idea goes in harmony with the proposal of a territorial laboratory classroom, involving us as an academic community in the social, economic and cultural processes that are shaping and defining the present and future of Cesar and the Caribbean region.
