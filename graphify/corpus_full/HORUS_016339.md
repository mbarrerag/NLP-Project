# Spatial-temporal analysis of forced displacement in Colombia.

In Colombia, the phenomenon of Internal Forced Displacement (IDP) has left 8,231,887 victims at 2022, compared with the DANE population projected for the same year, which would account for 16.5% of the total population (DANE, 2022), placing the country in group 5 of the countries with the largest displacement in the world (IDMC, 2021). This document carries out a spatial and temporal analysis at the municipal level that reveals that there are 3 main types of municipalities in the face of displacement: low, medium and high incidence, each of these groups characterized in particular with different determinants related to the presence of the State, natural resources, illicit economies and conflict depending on the state of progress of the phenomenon
