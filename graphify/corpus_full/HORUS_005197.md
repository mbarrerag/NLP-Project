# PARASITIONS TO THE SCOPE OF ALL: AVAILABLE DATA STRATEGY IN A COLLECTION

Biological collections have been a fundamental tool in the development of biology throughout history. However, do they face a great challenge today: making information accessible to maximize its use and impact. The biological collection Grupo Estudio Relación Parísito Hospedero(Study) of the National University of Colombia has the philosophy of providing open data to build science.
