# Dynamic inclusion of decision-makers' preferences in a multi-objective genetic algorithm using an SID

This paper proposes a methodology that allows a decision maker to include the preferences that he experiences on a problem, using a system of Inference Diffuse in a genetic algorithm of multi-objective optimization. This methodology is achieved through the design of a method called Genetic Algorithm Multi-objective with Inference System of Differing Preferences (AGMOSIPD). This algorithm is a technique of incorporating a priori preferences that offers to the decisionor a stage of initial learning where, through obtaining a set of solutions through Monte Carlo simulation, he can construct his preference structure by designing a Difuse Inference System (SID). Subsequently, the SID is incorporated into a genetic algorithm through a restriction to direct individuals towards the area of the Pareto border most preferred by the decision maker and offer a reduced set of alternatives. This work is performed in 60 cases of test involving 6 problems and 2 genetic algorithms, the graphical results are presented, the obtaining of efficient solutions and the solutions obtained by AGMOSIPD are compared with the solutions obtained through the optimization of the problems of test in a genetic algorithm without the most efficient cases.
