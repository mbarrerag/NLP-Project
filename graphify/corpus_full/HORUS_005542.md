# Analysis of social inequalities in patients with gastric cancer in Quito (1996-2015)

People in low socio-economic positions become ill, suffer and die from gastric cancer in greater proportion and in worse circumstances than people in other socioeconomic positions. This situation is presented worldwide and is shown of the unfair differences within societies, since it occurs in function of unequal distribution of income, wealth and power. This also violates the fundamental right of people to enjoy the highest level of health without any distinction. Therefore, it is a public health problem that requires attention. In this sense, this research analyses the different ways of developing, suffering and dying from gastric cancer in Quito in the period 1996-2015, to understand the social inequalities in this type of cancer that constitutes one of the first causes of morbidity and mortality as well as one of the most lethal types of cancer. For this purpose, a model of analysis was constructed that took as a basis the transdisciplinary proposals of Naomar Almeida-Filho, and its model of articulation of the components of inequities in health, which rescues the importance of the recognition of the subject and his capacity of social agencies.
