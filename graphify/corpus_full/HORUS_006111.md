# Experimentation as a strategy to strengthen the learning of inorganic chemical reactions

In this work, a didactic strategy was implemented through experimentation based on the use of elements of everyday life, to improve the learning of inorganic chemical reactions with 12 students of ninth grade of the High Nubia Educational Institution located in rural area of the municipality of Anserma, Caldas. Initially a diagnostic test (pretest) was applied consisting of 23 questions, 14 multiple selection and nine open questions, to know the presbers and conceptual difficulties presented by the students, faced the understanding of the chemical reactions. Then, four guiding guides based on the new school model were designed and applied, in the C moment that corresponds to the exercise is where the experimental practices were developed, under an investigational approach with materials of everyday use, each practice designed addresses a different problem situation, besides taking into account the metacognitive approach of each student, at the moment of carrying out the reflection after each practice carried out. Finally, the postest was applied, in order to evaluate the learnings acquired by the students and in this way to evaluate the effectiveness of the experimental work with everyday reactions materials. questions, to know the pre-knowledge and conceptual difficulties that the students present, regarding the understanding of chemical reactions.
