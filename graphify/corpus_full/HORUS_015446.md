# ELIBITATION OF PATOGENSI-related enzines IN LEGIMOUS CYCHONESE BY USE OF INDANOIL TYPE ELIBITORS

Summary The enzymes related to pathogenesis (ERP) accumulate in plants during and after infection with pathogens and have an important role in defense, either by hydrolysing the cell wall of the pathogen or by catalysing reactions in the biosynthesis of antimicrobial metabolites; the production of the ERP may be stimulated by the use of naturally or synthetically sourced chemicals (elicitors) (indanoyl derivatives).The activity of the ERP has been correlated with the resistance to fungal diseases.In this work, the activity of the ERP in several legumes has been evaluated as a result of treatment with indanoyl-type derivatives.The catalytic activity was dependent on the species, the post-elicitation time, and the structure of the indanoyl derivative.Introdução The search for new alternatives to combat phytopathogenic micro-organisms has been evaluated in several years and no longer exclusively focuses on the random synthesis and optimization of biocidal chemicals.The knowledge of the interaction between the enzymes and the metabolites of the plant and the pathogene, has allowed to design new active molecules and plants. ). Elicitores protect crops by a non-biocidal mechanism of action involving induction of ERP. Among these, the indanoyl derivatives (aromatic analogues structurally related to phytotoxin coronatine), has been developed. In this work the elicitation of ERP in several legumes was evaluated as a result of treatment with indanoyl derivatives. Material and methods 1. Indanoyl derivatives: Indanoyl derivatives were prepared from 2-carboxycinamic acid, by hydrogenation reactions and intramolecular cyclation of Friedel-Crafts. The product, 1-oxo-4-indanoyl carboxylic acid, was transformed into different derivatives by condensation reactions with alcohols and amino acids, and aldolic condensation of the phenylamone (PAL), quintinate and entacterase
