# ¿CHANNEL CARE AND TRANSNATIONAL CARE TREATMENTS?

In this conference I addressed what I call transnational care approaches, a theoretical category that I have developed from a long research agenda around migration and care. In this exercise, I put into perspective the notion of global chains of care, to complex the analysis of the trajectories of those who migrate and care and their place in the transnationalized care regime?
