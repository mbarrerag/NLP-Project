# Proposal for a model for developing leadership competencies in heads and potential leaders to improve organizational performance. Case study: retail supermarket chain

Leadership is a complex phenomenon that has been studied from various theories and approximations, influenced by historical and cultural changes in the organizations, however, in different approaches, has been studied to increase performance, competitiveness and sustainability. The approach of leadership competencies differs from traditional leadership approaches by focusing on what the leaders know, know how to do and want to do to achieve their objectives, this approach is based on the concept of competencies proposed by David McClelland in 1969, which refers to the observable characteristics that underlie superior and effective performance in a particular context (Contreras, 2008). These competencies can be developed even in adultity (Boyatzis, 2011), however, they require a high degree of motivation and non-linear experiences to achieve a sustainable change. In this master's job, it is sought to identify and diagnose the leadership competencies for the organization under study, considering their challenges, with the aim of proposing a development plan that can be implemented later. The type of research is descriptive and transversal, the population selected is formed by heads of point of sale of a model of the Colombian.
