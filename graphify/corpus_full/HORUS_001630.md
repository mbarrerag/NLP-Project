# Barbiturates associated with torture in homicides in Bogotá. Case series 2020-2023

In the forensic community of the Pathology and Toxicology Groups of the city of Bogotá, there was an increase in the positive toxicological results for barbiturates in cases of homicide with signs of torture addressed by the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences (hereinafter INMLCF). Since the detection of these substances and their relationship with torture was an unusual event, a study was necessary to document the above. A retrospective, observational study of descriptive and cross-sectional type was carried out based on information provided by the National Group of Information Technologies and Development of INMLCF and by the Coordination of the Pathology Group of the Regional Bogota of INMLCF, compiling 22 cases studied. The frequency of the use of barbiturates in the context of torture, the sociodemographic characteristics of the victims, the lethal and non-letal lesions, the circumstances of the death with a human rights focus, as well as the routes of administration and the concentrations of barbiturates found for each of the cases.
