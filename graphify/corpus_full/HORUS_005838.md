# Generation of green hydrogen from oil production water

The main objective of this study is to evaluate the effect of oil content of oil production water of oil fields on the production of green hydrogen by electrolysis in the presence of carbon quantum points (CQD). To this end, various electrochemical techniques were used, such as linear scanning voltametry (LSV), cyclic voltametry (CV) and potentimetry, to identify the effect of crude oil during hydrogen production. The results show that the use of CQD affects the pharadic efficiency, which increases from 78% to 83% with the incorporation of CQD. In the presence of CQD, the effects generated by the presence of oil are inhibited at low concentrations of oil. On the contrary, the production of hydrogen increases by 10.0% (0,1 ml/min) with a Pharadaic efficiency of 83% and an efficiency of medium cell by the irrole, compared to the record obtained with the maximum concentration of crude emulsion (400 mg/ L). 1000 bblopd. This alternative offers a theoretical potential for hydrogen production of approximately 7.6 kW, which represents a promising opportunity for practical deployment in the field
