# Monitoring of seismic activity in Colombian territory using deep learning.

The sysmological networks, whether global, regional or local, aim to monitor seismic activity. This implies the detection of seismic events and the determination of their location (latitude, longitude, depth and time of origin) with an acceptable level of uncertainty.We apply these steps in three seismic networks automatically.A regional seismic network (Colombian-CM Seismological Network, separation between stations ~ 100 km), and two local and temporal networks (separation between stations ~ 10-30 km) in the north of South America: The local seismic networks of the Middle Valley of Magdalena (VMM) and the Andes of the Caribbean-Mérida (YU).For this, it is necessary to process the continuous data of multiple stations to detect and to target seismic phases (usually body waves).In many networks, this process leads to an analyst that, visually examining the traces, determines the time of each wave to a station.
