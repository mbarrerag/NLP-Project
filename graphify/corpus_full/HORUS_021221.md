# Use of lignocellulosic agroindustrial waste as sorbent materials in water and gaseous effluent purification

Adsorption is a phenomenon of surface that takes place in the interface either solid-liquid or solid-gas, in which the molecules or ions of a substance of interest are fixed on the surface of the adsorbente due to different forces of attraction and chemical affinity. Therefore, the adsorption is the enrichment of one or more components in an interfacial layer as a consequence of the asymmetry of the forces that exist in all interfaces. According to the nature of the forces involved in the adsorption process, the functional and chemical characteristics of the internal adsorbing are mentioned, depending on the weaker ones (Van forces of the Waals) or they involve chemical links (link forces) respectively. However, the phenomenon of adsorbation is favoured or disadvantaged by other factors other than the fluid interaction surface, in which the textured properties of the adsorbing process are specific, the porous structure and the particle diameter of the adsorbate influence on the external and internal mass transport phenomena and the dynamic fluid behavior of the system.
