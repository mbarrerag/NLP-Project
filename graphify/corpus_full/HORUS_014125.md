# EME-CAF: Mass Spectrometry in Environmentally Contaminants Pharmaceuticals: analysis of active pharmaceutical ingredients, metabolites and degradation products as environmental pollutants.

In Colombia, freshwater bodies have a fundamental role because of their ecological, economic and recreational importance. These ecosystems suffer significant impacts associated with anthropogenic activities leading to water pollution.The impact of the presence of pharmaceutical products, or more specifically, active pharmaceutical ingredients (IFA) best known for their acronym in English as API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients), in both waste water (AR) and natural waters (AN) is a common and permanent problem in Colombia and in general throughout the world. APIs mainly refer to the active principles of medicines for animal and human use, which are manufactured, distributed, marketed, consumed and/or administered in Colombia in a prescribed or free-selling manner in homes, clinics, hospitals, veterinary, centres of animal and human care, among others.In the presence of sunlight and the action of some aquatic organisms or bacteria, the APIs can be metabolized.In general, such metabolites are less dangerous as they are normally a form of detoxification of organisms to release from foreign substance. However, what can be less harmful and what is not used for a human product. However, there are few specialists in the operation of LC-MS equipment in the country. This is why this project aims to carry out analytical developments for the detection, monitoring and quantification of APIs using the LC-MS, in addition to implementing a support methodology, which allow for reliable detections and quantifications through the LC- according to Diodes and other analytical techniques existing at the Faculty of Sciences of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín. This is expected to combine capacities of extension and research laboratories to implement the technique and that can be offered as part of the laboratory service. At the end of the project, the specific technique of LC-MS validated and the support technique implemented for API analysis in various types of natural water and wastewater samples should be available
