# Ecological restoration in rural territories: a comprehensive analysis from the approach of the socio-ecological systems in the upper area of Montes de María (Bolivar).

Eco-restauration has been placed on the agendas of countries as a strategy for sustainable development and climate action. To achieve the achievement of restoration goals and objectives, governments, cooperation agencies and private companies have implemented programmes to encourage initiatives led by entities of various kinds, aimed at meeting these goals in different socio-ecological scales and scenarios. From this perspective, ecological restoration is posed from the global sphere and its local scale landing is determined by the socio-ecological and territorial dynamics that characterize a given geographical space. This research seeks to understand from an integral and socio-ecological perspective, the articulation of ecological restoration in a rural setting in Montes de María (Bolivar), based on the need to contribute in the construction of governance models for restoration that are structured from a territorial perspective capable of articulating with local development claims.
