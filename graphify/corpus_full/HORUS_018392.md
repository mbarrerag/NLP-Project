# BOGOTÁ: A TRHANSITE LENT TO MODERNITY

The Bogotá text: a slow transit towards modernity is the narrative of a city built through dissimilar rhythms; initially with a rather accompanious and rugged start that defined its unique place in the Hispanic American urban context. Situation that, at the beginning of the twentieth century, changes drastically, understanding accelerated modernization processes that demarcate its dizzying consolidation as the main axis of national urban development. The facts here treated should be understood as an invitation to reflect on the gigantic sum of urban experiences that give meaning to Bogotá, addressed all through chapters between which the reader will be able to find the transit of the Baroque Bogotá to modern city, the conformation of the space and public opinion in Bogotá of the xviii century and the fear as an area of urban administration in contemporary Bogotá. Thus, it offers a wide-spected look that aims to recognize from early expressions of modern urban culture to possible political and administrative transformations of end century related to the demographic changes that happened two decades before.
