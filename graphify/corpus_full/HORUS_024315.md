# STRUCTURE OF PEOPLE AND POLYZING STAFF OF CATASETUM OCHRACEUM IN THE FILLIONES OF THE Occidental CORDILLIER (THE VORAGINE, COLOMBIA)*

Catasetum ochraceum is a terrestrial orchid with discontinuous distribution in Colombia and Venezuela. A population was monitored for four years in La Voragine, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. Pollination observations were made and a census was made of the individuals of the population. Catasetum spp is known to be pollinated by male euglosins bees, so to identify local pollinators we found traps with 1-8 cineol and methyl salicylate attractants to collect bees with catasetum pollinaries. A total of 50 individuals were registered, measured and mapped to follow them from 2016 to 2020. During the observation period, no marked plant produced fruits and 22 plants survived until the end of sampling. As for the collection of bees, 18 specimens of eight different species were registered: Eulema nigrita, Exaerete smaragdina, Euglossa desspecta, Eg. igna, Eg. modesta, Eg. typical, Eg. tridentata, Eg. population deceptrix and Eg. de polirix, Eg. lyopoda
