# ACTION AND PHASE JUMP ANALYSIS FOR LOCAL ERROR CORRECTIONS

The principles of the Action and Phase Jump (APJ) are presented with simulated and experimental data and the merit of this technique to estimate corrections is evaluated through observables like the beta-beating. Special emphasis is done in the action and phases estimates at the Interaction points where special techniques an instrumentation is required. Finally the effect of the uncertainties in the different observables are evaluated through different simulations
