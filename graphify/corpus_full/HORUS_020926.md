# Software Process Engineering

The growing demand for software and IT services has caused an excessive demand for skills and competences in the markets and has generated opportunities in emerging countries whose software industry has grown rapidly. Currently, the software industry can be considered a global market where it is required to possess extensive knowledge and skills from the staff, knowledge in numerous technologies that cover broad business domains and a constant motivation for innovation and continuous improvement. To address the challenges of software development, the area of knowledge of Software Engineering has been consolidated, which is a discipline that covers systematic study of software development and maintenance in a professional manner. The software engineering community has emphasized its interest in abandoning artisanal development techniques that focus primarily on program coding and replacing them with a rigorous approach based on engineering principles that cover all phases of the process from the specification of software requirements to the final delivery to the user.
