# Impact of territorial urban planning on the production of housing of social interest: application of the right to the city in the Medellín Housing Strategic Plan by 2030

The research work focuses on the impact of the Urban Territorial Planning on the Production of Social Housing in Medellín, the main objective was to analyze how territorial planning processes impact the implementation of the Urban Planning Plan of Medellín to 2030 (PEHMED) from the perspective of the right to the city. Through a qualitative methodology with a critical hermeneutic approach, three key themes are addressed: the characterization of the theoretical and methodological perspectives that guide territorial planning, the analysis of the implementation and monitoring processes of PEHMED (2030) from the municipal administration, and the interpretation of the tensions between territorial planning and housing policies in relation to social housing. The conclusions of the study highlight the challenges present in the realization of the right to the city, demonstrating the need for more inclusive and participatory policies that ensure equitable access to housing from the most vulnerable sectors. of the study highlight the challenges present in the materialization of the right to the city, evidenceing the need for more including and participatory policies that ensure equitable access to housing for the most vulnerable sectors.
