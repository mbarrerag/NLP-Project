# Implementation of a mathematical model to represent variations in the water quality of the Chinchiná river by potential discharges of sediments from the Cameguadua reservoir of the Caldas Hydroelectric Power Plant - CHEC

The conservation of water bodies has been one of the most relevant topics for sustainability in the planet given its importance for the regulation of natural ecosystems and ecosystem services of support, culture and supply provided to the different communities. Among other activities, the water has been used for the electrical generation facilitating the provision of this service to various populations. In Colombia, approximately 70% of the electrical energy is generated in hydroelectric plants, which, for the most part, operate by the accumulation of the water resource in reservoirs to lead it to generators and turbines and there transform the kinetic energy and potential of the water into electrical energy.
