# Sustainable mariculture: research for the development of marine production systems with an ecosystem approach

Sustainability is recognized as the main goal in the governance of aquaculture, therefore, practical demands and productive systems responsible in such a way that aquaculture is ecologically low impact, diversified and profitable. This project proposes the creation of a network for scientific research and development of technologies will be organised by the Brazilian authorities, the Brazilian authorities and the Brazilian authorities. To this end, the creation of scientific knowledge about the biology and ecology of promising species, is proposed to design and develop from applied engineering, aquaculture models that integrate different species within the same productive system.From the strategic areas of fundamental research, oceans and hydrobiological resources, as well as biotechnology, environment, agricultural sciences and bioeconomy, this proposal seeks to contribute to the diversification of aquaculture, as well as to the reduction of the environmental impact that this can cause, through the reuse of nutrients, bioremediation and generation of value by-products derived from the same system of cultivation. With a working team led by Professor Adriana Santos Martínez and formed by research groups of four headquarters of the National University of Colombia - UNAL, it is proposed to consolidate a network of cooperation between national academic institutions, the Spanish University and the Spanish University of Le sector. In order to continue the participatory processes, an extension project will be formulated to support the management of financial resources of the network in the medium term. Likewise, a research seedbed will be formed that uses in its methodology and didactic strategy the biointegrated productive systems as models of study in different scientific disciplines
