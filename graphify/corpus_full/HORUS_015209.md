# THE COMMITMENT AND PREVENTIVE CONTROL OF REFORM TO COLOMBIAN TAX CONTROL AND ITS RELATIONSHIP TO CONTINUOUS AUDITORY.

The recent approval of the reform to the Colombian fiscal control regime envisages the incorporation of continuous audit mechanisms, through what the legislative act 04 of 2019 and decree 403 of 2020 have called concomitant and preventive control, a concept criticized by some social actors as the return to the old practices developed by the control bodies, before the current Political Constitution of Colombia. On 14 and 15 November 2020, the second international congress and third national congress of online control and continuous audit, jointly with the Comptroller General of the Republic, was held at the National University of Colombia - Manizales headquarters, in which about 180 attendees from 40 municipalities of the country and 37 Comptrollers participated. In this space, the aim is to raise awareness and provide Colombian tax control actors with concepts and tools to incorporate information and communications technologies into government audit processes
