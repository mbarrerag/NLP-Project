# QUANTITY OF REZAGOS

Identifying the number of lags to be included in an autoregressive model, either under statistical theory or more recently under the paradigm of machine learning, is an open problem in research; since the computational cost of treating the number of lags as a hyperparameter and calibrating or estimating it from data is high. Therefore, it is sought to guide this search through exploratory statistics of the time series. This work explores different alternatives in literature, some of them of classical and highly studied use, along with more modern ones, in order to be able to give recommendations on the number of lags to include in a time series analysis
