# Influence of Co/Mn on the optical and morphological properties of nano ZnO structures manufactured by Co-Sputtering

In this work the effects of Mn and Co were studied on the morphological, optical and structural properties of thin ZnO films manufactured by the method DC Magnetron Co-Sputtering, modifying some parameters of synthesis on each sample, some as; concentration of Mn or Co, temperature of the substrate, use of substrate of different nature to that of the glass; such as Titanium and variation in the deposit time. Thus, from the characterization through microscopy SEM, FEMEM and AFM showed granular formation, the topography of the samples ZnO:Co constituted by small granules, where the higher concentration of the material deposited the granular nanostructures are larger and are defined by increasing the resolution of the micrography. On the other hand, for the samples ZnO:Mn the increase in the power of the Mn 3,0 and its output by 250. The optical characterization was made through the use of an atmospheric spectrophotometer Cary 5000 of the UV-VISNIR of high photometric performance in the range The second stage of the study is the first phase of the study, which is based on the following: staining coefficient (k), gap and an estimation of the thickness (d) of the samples were established. Characterization by X-ray diffration (XRD) and RAMAN specroscopy revealed the amorphous characteristic of the sample, which implies that the thin film does not present crystalline phases. Likewise, bands associated to the vibrational modes were identified, which are attributed to the E2 (high) and E1 (LO) modes and allow affering the presence of the ZnO phase
