# Keeping the woolly ring Leopardus tigrinus: a multifocal strategy for knowledge and conservation opportunities, in the municipalities of Tabio and Ubaté Cundinamarca

The Tigrillo lanudo Leopardus tigrinus, is the smallest wild feline in our country, in America has registered from Costa Rica to the north of Argentina, however, has few records in the central and northern part of South America; it is considered a species of low population density, remaining a high ignorance of aspects of its ecology. Added to this, at the global level and in Colombia, it is classified as a vulnerable species that despite this, in periurban areas the Tigrillo Lanudo has shown some degree of tolerance to human intervention, being registered in areas of forest crops of eucalyptus.
