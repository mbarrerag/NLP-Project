# Identification and prevalence of parasites of interest in public health, in irrigation waters and vegetables of the western savannah of Cundinamarca.

The water deficit resulting from population growth has resulted in the use of wastewater as an alternative for irrigation; in the case that occurs in the former district of La Ramada. Without the adequate treatment of the resource, this can mean exposure to pathogen parasites for man. Helmintos and protozoan have been confirmed in food and water used for irrigation. The objective was to identify and determine the prevalence of parasites of interest in public health, in irrigation waters and vegetables of the Western Savannah of Cundinamarca. 96 water samples and 120 vegetable samples cultivated in La Ramada were collected. The detection of protozoan removal in waters used direct centrifugation, DNA extraction and qPCR. The identification of parasites in vegetables used the technique of Matosinhos, the concentration of Formol-Eter and microscopy of Inmunofluorescencia. The sample of the test was evaluated for high removal of eggs of helmints, in two pilot systems of wastewater treatment: sequential discontinuous reactor (SBR) and the anaerobic filter of the discharge (FAFD).
