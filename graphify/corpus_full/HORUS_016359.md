# SOURCES OF INCERTIDUDE IN SPECTER MEASUREMENTS WITH CCD SPECTROPHOTOMETERS; CALCULATION OF DOMINANT WHERE LONGEST.

The use of matrix spectrophotometers is a method that is widely used in science and industry to obtain SPD spectral power distribution. From this, parameters that characterize, be it a light source or a surface, for example: the coordinates of color, color temperature, dominant wavelength, color reproduction index, etc. Uncertainty associated with the measurement of these parameters is crucial, whether it is to assess the conformity of a product intended to specific demands, or, whether it is to judge the consistency of measurements carried out in different laboratories. However, the measurement with these instruments contains a varied number of sources that contribute to the uncertainty of measurement. This document presents a description of the most important sources of uncertainty, with their corresponding mathematical modelling; in addition, the correlation between different sources of uncertainty is considered, which has a considerable influence on the estimation of the uncertainty of the final mesurando. On the other hand, the estimation of the chromaticity and the dominant wavelength with a measured spectrum of uncertainty is illustrated
