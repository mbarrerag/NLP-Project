# Geography of Colombia: from its territories

This book is an invitation to know, study and enter the geography of Colombia. Far from being a sum of knowledge that would exhaust the complex geography of the national territory, this book gathers the works of more than 30 authors, who, each from his experience, propose his readings of the national territory or of territories of local or intermediate scale. Through this polyphony, the reader goes on to elucidate the fundamental traces of the current territorial dynamics of Colombia. As a product of a research project of the Department of Geography of the National University of Colombia, in partnership with the University of Tolima and with the collaboration of the Institute of Regional Studies of the University of Antioquia, it is a reflection of the contemporary Colombian university geography: an open and updated geography that is interested in multiple, multi-scale, multidimensional and temporarily located territories. The book articulates chapters with a thematic approach, focused on the analysis of different dimensions of the national territory (historical, political, demographic, environmental, economic and social), and chapters with a territorial approach, that develop studies of problematized cases analyzed at local or regional level.- Contracadas
