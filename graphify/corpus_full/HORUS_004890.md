# Approach to a model of targeting vulnerable population with food insecurity in the Special District of Science, Technology and Innovation of Medellín, based on the Theory of Response to the Item (TRI)

This thesis is based on the approach of a model of targeting vulnerable populations with food insecurity in the Special District of Science, Technology and Innovation of Medellín, specifically in Comuna 2 – Santa Cruz. The main objective is to improve the selection process of beneficiaries in order to optimize the execution of public resources and improve the quality of life of people in situations of extreme poverty and food insecurity. The study uses the Theory of Response to the Item (TRI) to validate the relevance of the application of the Latin American and Caribbean Food Security Scale (ELCSA) in candidates for different social projects. Finally, logistic regression models are applied and the accuracy of these . is evaluated.
