# Parenting practices, maternal self-efficacy and emotional competence in preschool children

The emotional competence – fundamental in the development of preschool children – is influenced by various aspects of their social and family environment. Among these factors, parenting practices and maternal self-efficacy stand out for their potential impact on the development of these skills. In this context, it was proposed to explore this relationship, specifically when maternal self-efficacy acts as a mediator variable, so, a cross-sectional, quantitative study was carried out in which 235 mothers and their children, from 4 to 6 years, were involved, belonging to five district schools in the city of Bogotá. Self-informed questionnaires were administered to mothers and a test of emotional competence to children. The data collected were analyzed using Modelling of Structural Equations (SEM); advanced statistical technique that allows to evaluate the direct and indirect relationships between multiple variables. The results indicated a very significant association between parenting practices, specifically communication, with maternal self-efficacy and emotional competence.
