# Analysis of road accident risks for the community of the Engineering Faculty of UNAL headquarters Bogotá and prevention strategies through educational campaign.

The present project aims to address the problem of road accidents at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Bogotá through awareness-raising and education strategies. As a starting point, some figures have been considered at the national level, among which it is emphasized that the age group between the ages of 20 and 30 is particularly vulnerable to this problem, an age group representing the majority of the university community. Therefore, the need to implement a road safety strategy on the university campus, with the aim of preventing accidents and promoting a road safety culture, is justified. The campaign "UN Viaje Seguro" consists of two main elements: a practical component that includes activities designed to strengthen culture and promote safe behaviour, and a theoretical-academic component consisting of a booklet called "Guide for Safe Movement UN". This booklet will provide essential information on road signs, traffic regulations and safe behaviour patterns on the road, based on a diagnosis of road safety on campus. In addition, training and practical activities will be carried out in order to promote preventive driving skills in different types of vehicles. It is expected that this initiative can be used in other institutions of the country
