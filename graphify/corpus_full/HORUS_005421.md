# Humanization, ethical sensitivity and decision-making of health personnel in ICU

to interpret the relationship between humanization and ethical sensitivity with the decision-making of health personnel working in intensive care units (ICUs) in Bogotá and Cartagena. qualitative study of a comprehensive type. Three tools were used to collect information: semi-structured online interview, case analysis and the day-to-day participant-diary field observation. Initially, coding, categorization, layout and data grouping were carried out (deductive categorical analysis); then, analysis of the discourse and hermeneutics of ideas, identifying two emerging categories (inductive categorical analysis): Faces in the ICU and Cultivation of Professionalism.
