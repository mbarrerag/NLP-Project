# Business plan for public passenger transport services in non-motorized tricimobiles or with pedaling assisted in the Lili Valley sector, Cali.

A business plan model for public passenger transport service was developed in non-motorized tricimobiles or with pedalling assisted in the Lili Valley sector in Cali, based on a quantitative methodology with an exploratory and descriptive scope. In the framework of the implementation of Resolution 3256 of 2018 that authorized the provision of the service of bicycletaxi by existing informal operators. Thus, a study of the competitive sector and a DOFA analysis were carried out to formulate the strategies necessary for its formalization, with which they reach a competitive advantage in the sector. The strategies guided the development of market studies, business description, definition of services, marketing strategy, operations and administration, financial and risk viability. Among the main results it was found that the competitive public transport sector offers advantageous conditions for a formalized tricimobile operator. In addition, the impact of formalization would benefit the operator with the greater number of weekly trips and good image of the brand to the population. However, its capabilities to position itself in the sector should be adjusted.
