# Detection of genetic alterations in pediatric gliomas and association with clinical factors: Retrospective analysis of a population sample at the Hospital Fundación la Misericordia in the city of Bogotá, Colombia

To establish the presence of genetic alterations of the main routes of growth and cell proliferation in a pilot sample of Colombian pediatric patients with diagnosis of glioma by using techniques of molecular cytogenetics FISH and immunohistochemistry. Descriptive observational study: case series. place: fundación hospital la misericordia. Patients: Paediatric population with gliomas diagnosed between 2016 and 2020 in a reference hospital in Bogotá. 50 paediatric patients were analyzed by means of microarreglos of tissue and hybridization in situ with fluorescence (FISH) to detect the fusion alterations KIAA1549::BRAF, deletion of CDKN2A, amplification of EGFR, N-MYC, and codeletion 1p/19q. In addition, immunohistochemistry was used to evaluate the expression of the epigenetic mark H3K27me3.
