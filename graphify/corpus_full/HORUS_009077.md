# A LITTLE STORY, WHERE DO WE COME?

This paper opens the UX-UI table on the third day of CIDI4. It seeks to contextualize the origin of UX-UI design in the world and locate this origin in Colombia. It refers to the arrival of the Internet in the country, the methodologies used to create the first user interfaces, the first concepts and their application. It seeks to lay the basis for a self-knowledge of the making of UX designers in Colombia
