# Poetics from house to river. Constructive and spatial system to enhance the habitation of the Andesitos neighborhood of Neiva

In the city of Neiva currently building houses in the city center or on the periphery is the same as building on the river bank, usually use their same constructive, spatial and activity patterns without technical order, nor spatial control affecting urban civic dynamics, without recognizing from the qualitative and quantitative aspects of the place since implantation, grouping and typology. This final mastery work makes a journey of the true situations that are presented around the river with two dwellings, which are determinants to propose through a constructive and spatial system, an alternative way to build on the river of the water channel, seeking to recognize the poetic value of the natural tributary as an essential part to inhabit the city on the three scales. .
