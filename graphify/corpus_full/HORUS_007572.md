# Digital Maturity Model for Small and Medium Enterprises

The purpose of this research is to propose a model to measure the digital maturity of Colombian small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), which responds to the specific characteristics of this type of enterprises and to the conditions and dynamics of the national environment. Existing digital maturity models will be analysed with special emphasis on those that are oriented to SMEs, the main dimensions and scales of measurement will be identified and the necessary adjustments and adjustments will be made through the expert method. Subsequently, the model will be validated in a sample of regional SMEs using the multiple case study methodology. The application of appropriate methods to assess the level of digital transformation could contribute to the design of digital strategies aimed at improving the competitive performance of these companies in the market, under the perspective of efficiency and sustainability
