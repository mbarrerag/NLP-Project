# Utilization of a variety of Colombian Creole maize for use as an ingredient in the production of puff pastry

This work seeks to characterize a type of maize produced in Colombia to strengthen the use of indigenous varieties as a basis for technological applications that in the end allow to know more about the diversity and possible functionality of the corns criollos, and that strengthen the construction of strategies for the defense of the genetic heritage of the country and the defense of autonomy and food sovereignty. Initially, some physical and chemical characteristics of the corns will be determined and will be compared with those of the corns reported in the Table of Composition of Colombian Foods (TCAC) carried out by the Colombian Institute of Family Welfare (ICBF), to determine if it is similar to the maize previously characterized in Colombia. A storage in hermetic bag will also be carried out with a controlled temperature and different relative humiditys of storage, and will be evaluated the maize for 30, 60 and 90 days to evaluate the effects on the characteristics of the hermetic storage of the maize at different humiditys. Finally, the extraction of the modified starch from the maize will be carried out by the enzyme method, and will be performed a characterization where the technological properties of the modified starch will be established to be used as a substitute of fat in a mixed product and baked and baked into different types of the leaves
