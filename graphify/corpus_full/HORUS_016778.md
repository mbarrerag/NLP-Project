# STATUS OF EDUCATION ART IN GEOCIENCES IN THE NIVEL OF BASIC EDUCATION AND MEDIA IN COLOMBIA

The processes of education in geosciences are transversal elements for the implementation of global models of sustainable development, influencing the capacities for the recognition, appropriation, participation, management and decision-making of citizens; for this reason it is essential to advance efforts that allow to recognize the level of literacy or geoscientific knowledge in the population and to promote the link of these areas in the public discussion of nations, which, like Colombia, are in the process of development and present socio-economic problems of poverty, inequality and harmonious planning of the territory. In this sense, based on a survey directed to the national geoscientific community, the study of the legislative framework of Colombian education and the review of educational experiences carried out by international agencies was constructed a representative scheme of education in geosciences at the basic and middle levels of the country's education system, so that it was possible to recognize the deficits and meeting points between the global models of literacy in the field sciences and the contents proposed by the Colombian Ministry of National Education in the Basic Rights of Learning and the Active Secondary Model
