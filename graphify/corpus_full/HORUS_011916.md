# BIM METHODOLOGY CONCEPTS AND ITS APPLICATION IN VIAL INFRASTRUCTURE PROJECTS

This is a guide for students and those who, in general, make use of the BIM methodology (Building Information Modelling), which is experiencing an implementation not only in industry but in all construction sectors. The use of collaborative tools has made it possible to significantly improve work on countless consulting projects and/or infrastructure construction, which generates direct impacts on local and global economies, promoting profits and productivity. Road infrastructure is all that allows the operation of a country, a territory, a city, and is fundamental to generating exchanges of information, goods and/or services. The inclusion of this methodology in infrastructure designs, although not fully implemented, in projects where it has, led to significant advances in the development and execution of projects, achieving the optimization of resources that can be used in other phases of the same project. This document aims to update and specialize technicians, technologists and engineers, dedicated to the design and calculation of road infrastructure, focusing on the new methodologies and requirements that the construction sector demands, framed in the use and application of the BIM methodology
