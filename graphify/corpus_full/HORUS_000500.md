# Study of urban pollution through in-situ and remote sensing measurements, chemical analysis, and chemical transport model assimilation techniques

This chapter focuses on studying urban pollutants in Colombia's most populated cities by developing local instrumentation and implementing chemical transport models (CTMs). The instrumentation includes Scanning Depolarization Lidar System, MAX-DOAS and low-cost in situ devices. Additionally, particulate matter is collected for chemical and morphological analysis using different techniques to identify the main sources of emissions. The chapter aims to provide examples of the findings from a study that is set to establish a foundation for adapting chemical and transport models to complex topography, such as coupling WRF-Chem with LOTOS-EUROS and setting the basis for incorporating data assimilation techniques to enhance the models' predictive capabilities in tropical conditions
