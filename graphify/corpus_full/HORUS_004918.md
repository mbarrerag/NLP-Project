# Analysis of choice and invention in light of Gilbert Simondon's isodynamics

In this research, Gilbert Simondon's isodynamics and his analysis was based on the categories of choice and invention at different levels of being. It was found that it is feasible to establish analogies between physical-chemical, biological and psychic-collective functioning by means of choice. This is given as a possibility to opt for a path between several, and arises on the basis of the already individualized as well as the undetermined pre-individual energy. In the case of the living, the choice is an invention, that is, the result of an image cycle that articulates anticipatory, cognitive-perceptive and affective elements and occurs as an action that is sometimes novel for the becoming of the subject, and may even enable him to locate himself beyond his determinations. . In this thesis we depart from the Simondon’s isodynamis to analysis of the operations of choice and innovation in different levels of being
