# THE BENEFITS OF THE METROCABLE LINE K IN MEDELLIN - COLOMBIA

In 1995, the Medellin Metro system was inaugurated with two lines, the A line from north to south with 19 km, extended 2km further south in 2012 and the B line from west to east with approximately 6 km that are intercepted in the center of the city. Medellin is the second city of Colombia in a metropolitan area close to 4 million inhabitants at 2024. In the year 2004 the Metrocable or K line was implemented that connects an area of the northeastern slope with the A line of the Metro system. This Metrocable line has a length of 2.1 km and saves a elevation of approximately 400 m, was the first cable of this type used for mass transport and has served as a world reference for this type of facilities. As one of the main strategic projects contained in the 2004 Development Plan 2007 is the Integral Urban Plan (PUI) of the northeastern commune, proposed as urban planning strategy and tool for social transformation that has allowed the Metrocable project to become the center of a social, economic and cultural intervention process in that municipality.
