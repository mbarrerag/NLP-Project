# THE BIBLETICS HAVE VALUE: METHODOLOGICAL GUIDE

This book illustrates the way in which the research "Estudio de Valor Económico y Social del Sistema de Librás Públicas de Medellín" was developed, through the procedures, formats and structures suggested; in such a way as to facilitate the replication of this type of studies and their adaptation to the contexts and specificities of different public, cultural and/or librarian institutions or organizations
