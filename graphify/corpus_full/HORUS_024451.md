# WATERING EMOTIONS IN LEARNING: ANALYSIS OF INTERACTIVITY IN A HIBRED EDUCATIONAL ENVIRONMENT

The aim of this work is to interpret the emotional dimension in the attribution of meaning to a teaching and learning process from a cultural and linguistic constructivist socio-view in pedagogy. The interpretative approach was carried out from the hermeneutics and the model of analysis of interactivity, to a case study that takes place in a hybrid university environment. The contributions account of the inseparable relationship between emotional and cognitive processes thanks to the mediation of language; also differences and similarities were established to the emotional expressions that emerged both in the face-to-face and in the virtual environment. Finally, it was found that the meaning given by the emotional dimension to the joint activity is given in the cohesion that this generates among its participants. The text can be of interest to all those who have a relationship with teaching and learning in formal education at any of its levels, for researchers in social sciences who want to appropriate rigorous research processes in qualitative methodologies.
