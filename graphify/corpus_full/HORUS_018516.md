# THE PEOPLE OF THE WORLD CENTRE: CURATION OF HISTORY IN A LOVELY SOCIETY

This work seeks to contribute to the understanding of the culture and history of an Amazonian society, after its violent insertion into the extractive economy. It is believed that indigenous groups have tended either to assimilation or to the formation of ethnic enclaves. Here we ask the question in other terms: Could it be the culture of those Amerindian groups, which appears as distinctive and own, a dialectical reflection of the history of contact? The aim of this book is to investigate how an Amazonian indigenous philosophy appears today as its traditional culture? is related to the historical process of contact and to the construction of new forms of collective identity, as is manifested in the expression of people of the centre? The first part of the work has a wide range in ethnological and historical terms about the eight peoples of the people of the center.
