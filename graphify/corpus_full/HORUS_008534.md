# THE SINGLE STRESS METHOD -SSM- AS A HIGH VOLTAGE TEST PROCEDURE

The disruptive behaviour of insulating objects in high voltage systems is very important for engineering designs. For this reason, it is important to have reliable test procedures to obtain quality assessment of the insulations in terms of a breakdown voltage or a discharge probability function. However, in the study of configurations geometrically similar, it has been detected that methods recommended by the standards (Multiple Level Method ¿ MLM- and Up and Down Method ¿UDM-) are not sensitive to small variations in the electrode geometry. Therefore, the Single Stress Method ¿SSM- was proposed as high voltage test procedure. In order to check the Single Stress Method validity, the breakdown voltages obtained with the SSM and the conventional methods are compared, and their advantages and disadvantages are presented for air discharge studies
