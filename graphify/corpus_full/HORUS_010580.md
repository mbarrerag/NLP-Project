# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE DESIGN OF AN INDIUM FINISHED CELL (IN) AND ESTIMATION OF INCERTISM OF MEASUREMENT OBJECTED TO THE REPETIBILITY OF THE TRANSITION OF SOLIDIFICATION PHASE

A fixed point cell of Indian was designed and constructed at the National Institute of Metrology (INM) as part of the implementation of a thermometric pattern of the international temperature scale (EIT-90). To corroborate the correct functioning of the cell, measurement uncertainty due to the repeatability of the phase transition was estimated. It was found that this source represented 1.5% of the expanded measurement uncertainty, which allows the conclusion that no significant amounts of impurities were deposited during the cell's manufacturing process. A computational algorithm was implemented to determine the completion of the solidification process in an automated manner
