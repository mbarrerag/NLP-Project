# COSTS UNDER IFRS AND ITS IMPLEMENTATION IN SMEs

Sections 13.8 and 13.9 of IFRS for SMEs make a profound change in the process to be followed in determining the cost of the product, as a difference is made in the treatment to be given to fixed and variable indirect costs of production; it also incorporates into the system the concept of normal capacity, stabilizes the unit cost of production for levels not exceeding normal capacity, does not allow the use of the total cost technique to establish unit cost and makes the cost useful for decision-making; these reasons led to the completion of this text, which indicates the process to be followed in establishing the cost of production under IFRS when working with a manufacturing and process production order system
