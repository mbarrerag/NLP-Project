# Effects of cannabidiol phytocannabinoid on sodium excretion in Wistar rats

The elimination of sodium at renal level in the murine model, induced by CBD, is mediated by the binding of the ligand the phytocannabinoid with the receptors for cannabis. This interaction modulates the endocannabinoidoma at renal level, specifically in sodium reabsorption, modifying to some extent the rate of glomerular filtration. In reference to the above, this work aimed to identify the effects of the cannabinoid phytocannabinoid at different doses, on sodium reabsorption at renal level. 30 rats male Wistar, adults (3 months old), distributed in 4 groups, control group, vehicle, group to the os that were applied via intraperitoneal CBD in a dissolved olive oil of extra virgin at different doses (15, 30 and 50 mg/kg) are used prior to the sampling. The method used to take urine was spot collection, which consists in performing a tactile stimulus on the animal back, placing a dilutant vessel of 5 ml near the perinatal zone. The method used to collect urine was directly collected, which constitutes the sodium excretion at the renal level
