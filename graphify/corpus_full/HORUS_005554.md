# Factorial methods for the analysis of

In order to fulfil the constitutional mandate to ensure that the Colombian people have the tools to participate effectively in the construction of democracy, the team of the Corporation for Social Control - CONTRIAL, in the head of Dr. John Sudarsky, designed the instrument Barómetro de Capital Social (BARCAS). This instrument has been applied in four opportunities (1997, 2005, 2011, 2017) seeking to explain the transformations of social capital over time, as well as to account for the specificity of the cultural variations in the national territory. The items of this survey were grouped into categories that were called variables, dimensions and factors. This work seeks to identify the evolution of the main indicators of social capital in the four periods in which the BARCAS has been applied. The proposal of the work was aimed at identifying factors based on the dimensions defined in the original work of Sudarsky. For this purpose, different factorial methods were used, specifically the analysis of multiple correlations, the multi-factorial analysis and the dual factor analysis. As a result of this study, five factors were identified that were related to the concepts of political participation, the voluntary participation and the republican solidarity. that we were called variables, dimensions and factors.
