# CALIBRATION CURVE STRATEGY BY HYDRAULIC MODELATION

This study proposes a methodology to improve the accuracy of flow calibration curves, especially in mountain rivers, using a combination of field capacity or measurements and hydraulic modelling with IBER. The calibration curve is of great importance for water resource management, since it allows estimating flow rates according to measured levels. However, in rivers with high morphological variability such as mountain channels, these curves present significant uncertainties, especially in high levels. The methodology developed in this work integrates capacity data and hydraulic modelling to reduce this uncertainty, validating the model with empirical data and comparing the curves generated with those obtained exclusively from flow measurements. The results indicate that the inclusion of the hydraulic model significantly reduces uncertainty in the estimation of flow rates for levels higher than full bench levels, offering a more robust tool for water resource management and planning in the region
