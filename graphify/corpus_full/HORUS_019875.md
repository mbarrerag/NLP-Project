# PV Array Reconfiguration Based on Genetic Algorithm for Maximum Power Extraction and Energy Impact Analysis

This paper presents a reconfiguration procedure based on a genetic algorithm. The execution times obtained with the proposed approach validate its good performance compared with a traditional brute force algorithm. Finally, different shading patterns are considered in the simulations
