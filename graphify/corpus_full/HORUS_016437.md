# MODEL REQUIREMENTS AND CONTROLS IN THE COLOMBIAN ELECTRIC SYSTEM

The requirements of models and controls applicable in the Colombian Electrical System were presented in detail. It is part of the background of the energy matrix of Colombia that is currently dominated by synchronous generation and will be mobilized to a matrix with a significant component of non-conventional renewable generation. Under this expectation, it shows the need not only to maintain an adequate quality of the models of synchronous generators but also to have procedures to maintain quality models for non-conventional renewable plants and with this tend to obtain results closer to reality in the electrical analyses that are carried out to plan and operate the Colombian electrical system. It is shown as a procedure in which the generator agents interact through the development of field tests and surveying models complying with quality indicators, the Colombian electrical system operator that validates the compliance of requirements and carries out the monitoring in time, favors the maintenance of the quality of the models. It is also presented as through the proper adjustment and monitoring of the response to the controls, it manages the stability and quality conditions of the variables of the Colombian electrical system
