# INCOMOD TRUES OF GLOBAL PUBLIC HEALTH

The book Uncomfortable Truths of Global Public Health, like the David and Golliat of Gadwell, also relates some common challenges facing public health in a globalized context. It is a combination of theoretical and conceptual elaboration about these uncomfortable truths -which have become more relevant in the context of the pandemic-, complemented by stories of the intimacy of difficult public policy decisions in health, which were taken in Colombia between 2011 and 2018. The book allows understanding the interaction between global and local power, and shows how to influence international scenarios to facilitate debate and local decisions and vice versa.
