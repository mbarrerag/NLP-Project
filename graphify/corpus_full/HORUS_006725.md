# Parental coordination as an unnamed precautionary measure: A procedural instrument to protect the best interests of children and adolescents within custody and parental rights proceedings

We live in a society where the rates of violence between parents have been increasing, putting at risk the emotional, psychological, physical and economic stability of our children and adolescents, a situation that requires new legal tools to protect their fundamental rights, and that is why this final work suggests parental coordination as an unnamed precautionary measure in the processes related to custody and parental authority.
