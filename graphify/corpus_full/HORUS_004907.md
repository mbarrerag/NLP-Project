# CHARACTERISTICS OF COLOMBIAN CENTENARIANS: A ANALYSIS OF THE National Population and Living Census 2018

To explore the sociodemographic characteristics related to exceptional longevity in the older adult population of Colombia. Cross-sectional study using the National Population and Housing Census (CNPV) of 2018. Sociodemographic characteristics that best explain exceptional longevity in the Colombian centennial population compared to older adults were analyzed. in Colombia for 2018 there were 12,226 centennials and 584,754 elderly adults (60-99 years), for each centennial man there were 1,38 centennial women. The departments with the highest rates of centennials were Bolívar, Guaviare and Chocó. Sociodemographic characteristics had similar behavior in both groups, with the exception of civil status, educational level and labor activities. Global aging and in Colombia underscores the need for strategies to improve the quality of life of older adults. The similarities between Colombian centennials and at the global level suggest the importance of prospective research to influence public policy and the guidelines of the global health organization
