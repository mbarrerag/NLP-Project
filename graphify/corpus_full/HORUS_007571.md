# Readers and writers: an encounter between the inside and outside of the prison archipelago

The deprivation of liberty is part of the punitive practices that have been established as the main form of punishment since its introduction in the Western world at the end of the eighteenth century. For Foucault in Vigilar and Punishar, this form of punishment that gave materiality to prisons as places of imprisonment, involved the establishment of an entire prison archipelago of surveillance and punishment that exceeded the walls of prisons and involved the introduction of micro surveillance and punishment devices in places such as schools, hospitals and barracks. However, this form of punishment has had limits in its ability to impact the reduction of sentences, the reparation of victims and the resocialization of prisoners.
