# Obtaining recombinant fluorescent green protein and its polyclonal Igy antibody

The Fluorescent Green Protein (Green Fluorescent Protein, GFP) is widely used in in vivo and in vitro trials. Multiple variants of this protein have been generated to diversify its characteristics, such as GFP-enhancer (EGFP) which emits a 35-fold fluorescence signal compared to wild protein, being implemented as a fusion protein in localization and structural stability studies, among others. The detection of this protein and its variants can be direct or indirect, by using anti-GFP antibodies. Although the use of GFP is widespread and of obvious utility in research and teaching, the inputs for its study exhibit a high cost since they should be imported, constituting a limited resource in Colombia. This work reports the cloning and expression of recombinant protein 6xHisEGFP, whose purification was completed from the soluble and insoluble fraction of the heterologist system Escherichia coli by means of chromatography of immobilized metals and preparation electrophoresis, respectively.
