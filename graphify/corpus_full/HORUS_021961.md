# Environmental alterations caused by the presence of hydrocarbons by analysis of isotopic fractionation (-) in an aquifer in the Magdalena Middle Valley.

The isotopic fractionation studies (- ) allowed to identify the behavior and trends of dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) in the groundwater of the northern zone of the Magdalena Middle Valley basin, confirming that the sources provided by DIC can be differentiated. By complementing the analysis with 14C values, the evolution of the DIC in open systems and towards closed systems was discriminated against. However, due to the heterogeneity of the zone it is important to make local characteristics and given the mixtures of water produced by the complexity of the aquifer system, both surface and underground, the main but not unique sources of DIC are highlighted. Likewise, in view of the varied results, it cannot be confirmed that the area is suffering from an alteration, at an underground level, by contamination with hydrocarbons, which does not deny the presence of these, but rather supports the natural response of the groundwater (Aute x odón ón óneo)
