# Predicting health risks for people with obesity using machine learning techniques.

Obesity is a high-risk disease that is considered a public health problem, in Colombia about 60% of the population has poor eating habits and this influences other pathologies where intervening in time and avoiding an urgent hospitalization or a death is fundamental. This research tries to propose a model of machine learning, analyzing behavior patterns and different interactions with Colombian health systems, with the purpose of predicting an adverse event, the result is a model scalable to the systems and acceptable precision that will allow a greater understanding of the data and contribute to generating a more efficient preventive health policy
