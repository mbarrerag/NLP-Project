# UNAL Clear Language Thinking Center of the Research Group REPENSARELDERECHO

The proposed project focuses on the implementation of the "clear language" as a key tool to improve communication in the National University of Colombia (UNAL). This approach seeks to facilitate inclusive and participatory interventions, recognizing the importance of clear language in the legal and administrative field, where its absence can become a factor of exclusion (Perafán, 2021; Rosero, 2021). Originated in the United Kingdom, the clear language has evolved in Ibero-America, with notable examples in Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Chile and Colombia. In the latter, it is highlighted, after the promulgation of the Political Constitution of 1991, its developments in the public policy of relation with the citizen, through the National Policy of Service to the Citizen (CONPES 3785 of 2013), followed, with emphasis on the relation policy, by the promulgation of Law 1792 of 2014, known as _x0093_Law of transparency_x0094_ Regulates the right of access to public information (Congress of the Republic, 2014) and Law 2052 of 2020, known as _x0093_Law of streamlining of procedures_x0094_, with the objective of the public opinion. The proposal also considers empathy and intercultural dialogue as potential for communication strategies that are decidedly inclusive, allowing participation in decision-making affecting the university community.
