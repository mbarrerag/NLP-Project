# Thermal images and spectral responses to identify water stress conditions and nutritional status related to nitrogen in yellow potato diploid (Solanum tuberosum Phureja Group).

The yellow potato diploide (Solanum tuberosum Group Phureja) is susceptible to water deficit conditions, negatively affecting the yield potential. The climatic variability increases the frequency of drought, so it is necessary to generate strategies that allow to diagnose in time and thus mitigate the effects caused by the water stress in the crop. The objective of this work was to evaluate the use of thermal images and the spectral response to identify conditions of water stress and nutritional status regarding the N in yellow potato diploide (Solanum tuberosum Group Phureja) cv. Criolla Colombia under greenhouse. Tubres-seeds in bags with soil of seven liters of capacity watered every third day at the capacity of field until the beginning of tuberization 45 dds (days after sowing), subjected to two water regimes: i) continuous irrigation (CW) and, ii) water deficit by total irrigation suspension (SW) during 13 days, the doses of fertilization with N were 0%, 50%, 100% and 150% of the average dose used for the cultivation.
