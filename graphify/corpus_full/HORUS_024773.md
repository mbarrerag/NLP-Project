# THE METHOD OF MONTECATING IT IN A FINANCIAL MATEMATICS APPLICATION

We will show how through two mathematical models, one deterministic based on financial mathematics and another probabilistic or stochastic value based on the Monte Carlo method can be estimated the present net value of an investment project. When doing the inquiry of different methods of financial investment, the following variables must be taken into account: income, interest rates, production, costs, among others, which are not deterministic since, when using only a deterministic model, one does not take into account the fluctuations of these variables, therefore, a probabilistic model must be applied to take into account the uncertainty of the variables and thus be able to estimate the nature of the different factors that can affect them. Thus, to put into practice the different aspects mentioned above it is intended that, from some values given as costs, revenues and interest rates offered by some banks, is simulative to use the more than the Montecar method of investment and the law of large numbers, it can be estimated the present value of an investment and thus be able to perform a much more real financial evaluation.
