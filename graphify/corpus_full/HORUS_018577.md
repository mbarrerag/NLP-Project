# Performance of interoperability, scalability and communications safety of an electric power microgrid.

In Colombia, there is a low implementation of electrical microgrids with communications systems that address emerging technologies such as IoT and cloud, closely tied to the lack of knowledge of these and their behaviors within communications infrastructures for the electrical sector. This research study assesses the performance of communications from an electrical energy microgrid (MG). For this purpose, the three main characteristics in this communication are analyzed, namely interoperability, scalability and safety; from the establishment of a use case and its associated requirements for the performance of the designed tests. Indicators were created for each of these characteristics that allow to measure their performance, finding the elements where greater attention is required to improve the results obtained. From the calculated products, a total performance value of 75% is reached, which allows the conclusion that the proposed MG has a medium level, strengthened by the good performance of interoperability and scalability, although with notable improvements at the level of computer security.
