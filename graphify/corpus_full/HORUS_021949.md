# The quality of the commercial relationship between the supplier and the agricultural producer and its effect on technological innovation.

Agricultural producers face difficulties in accessing sources that promote technological innovation and lead to greater competitiveness. One of the sources of innovation available in the regional territory are commercial relations, through which the relational capital of agribusiness is used to generate new knowledge among the actors that are part of the supply chain, such as suppliers. However, this transfer of knowledge may be conditioned by the quality of the relationship between these actors. For this reason, it is necessary to study innovation from the relational marketing paradigm, with the purpose of investigating how the quality of the commercial relations with suppliers affects the level of technological innovation in the producer. To this end, a theoretical model is proposed between the quality of the commercial relationship and technological innovation, which is evaluated empirically from a sample of 250 producers. In addition, within the model the mediation effect of the collaboration is assessed and the moderate effects of some variables characteristic of the agricultural sector are explored; these procedures are carried out mainly with the partial square minimum effect (PLS-SEM) in models of structural equations.
