# VERTICAL COMPRESSION INDUCTED TO THE SOLUE DURING THE MECHANICAL PRESENCE OF SACCHArum SPPP. IN THE VALLE OF CAUCA, COLOMBIA

Characterize vertical forces transmitted to the soil by the agricultural machinery used during the mechanical harvest operation of Saccharun spp. (sugar cane) with self-voltage wagons on two floors of Valle del Cauca, Colombia, by developing an electronic device for the measurement of compression forces in the ground. The components used for the construction of the sensor were: a microcontrolling plate, an amplifier module of AD620 electrical signals, an LCD screen, a transducer of compression forces with nominal capacity of 10 Mg. The compression forces transmitted to the ground by the cane harvesting system with autovolteer wagons in two floors up to 50 cm depth and were related to the phenomenon of compaction through the apparent properties density in wet and mechanical resistance to penetration. It was found that the rear rim of the tractor transmitted the greatest compressive forces. At the depth of 15 cm the values were 120.42 kPa and 108.29 kPa, while at 50 cm were of wet density and 29.46 kPa. The efforts of the machinery resulted in the mechanical stress.
