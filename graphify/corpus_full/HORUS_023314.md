# ALERETRO-NOTES: A PROJECT FOR IMPROVING PUBLIC HEALTH NOTIFICATION AND DEMORING LABOUR OVERCOME.

Problem: In the pandemic for COVID -19, the labor overload affects health personnel by the emotional impact that implies to face complex and stressful situations that produce physical, emotional and psychological exhaustion, which causes the loss of the ability to face the daily work and personal load (up to the current and personal load).- The emotional impact of the health care workers in the institution is the result of the complex and stressful situations that produce physical, emotional and psychological exhaustion, which causes the loss of the ability to face the daily work and personal load.(4) Thus, the perception of occupational overload in health workers in the institution is increased in the pandemic and negatively impacts the reporting of events of public health interest (ESP) due to the increase in events to report. Implemented intervention: Through Research-Action-Partipartive-Partipartiveness-Participative (IAP) (5), as a methodology that seeks the production of pro-positive and transformative knowledge in the process of the collective construction of knowledge between different actors, the project was designed by the project AleRetro-notes. The project was formed by 8 students of nursing pregrad, one teacher, a teacher, 7 officials of the problem group of the institution's of 4. Socialization: carried out to the referents of the public institution, allowing feedback and commitment for the implementation of the project. Limitations, difficulties and/or successes: The software of the management dynamic was purchased from the company SYAC S.A, which represents limitation in the implementation of Alernotas in the short term by the cost generated. For the pilot test the strategy Retronotas-tips advanced with 9 events and the design of Alernotas with 8 events. The project is welcomed and is in the study phase for institutional implementation.
