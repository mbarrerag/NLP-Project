# Implementation of a comprehensive maintenance plan for a bottling plant

The present work is implemented in a company dedicated to the elaboration of non-alcoholic beverages, production of mineral waters and other bottled waters. The company has five bottling lines which produce approximately two million bottles daily. However, the plant was governed under a corrective maintenance, which generates unexpected production stops due to the failure of some component, delays in maintenance times, dead times due to lack of spare parts, low availability and reliability of the machines, and high maintenance costs. In addition, lubrication was carried out empirically based on the knowledge of the lubricating body, there was no continuous training plan or training, nor was there an established lubrication plan with which the type of lubricant applied, the quantity and frequency in which it was to be applied, which increased the probability of faults by lubrication and costs. It is the objective of this project to develop a diagnosis and to implement a comprehensive plan of preventive maintenance and lubrication for the lines of carbonated beverages in the plant. The methodology selected for the development of this project is the total production and costs.
