# Proposal for a training evaluation for derivatives: study of three cases in personalized education.

The evaluation for learning is a challenge in any teaching process, in many cases, the lack of knowledge of its characteristics and purpose makes it not carried out properly and I agreed to the context. In particular, during its classroom practices at the Academic Preparation Center Teaching and Tutoring (CPA-T&T), the author teacher identified the need to strengthen the process of formative evaluation, specifically for the thematic axis of derived in grade eleven, and taking into account the model of personalized education that underpins the academic processes in the institution. In response to the problem identified, five evaluation instruments were designed based on the analysis of theoretical supports established in the conceptual framework and practical supports resulting from both the characterization of their classroom practice, and from the interviews carried out to five teachers of the institution. The designs, were submitted to validation process by nine peer teachers related to the context, and for the analysis of results, were grouped into three cases of study.
