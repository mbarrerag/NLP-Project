# electrochemical electrode characteristics for hydrogen production

The project titled _x0093_Generation of materials for applications in the production and use of hydrogen as energy source_x0094_ with hermes code 57683 has as general objective _x0093_Theoretical studies, manufacture and characterization of materials for applications in the production and use of hydrogen as energy source_x0094_ and one of its specific objectives is _x0093_Manufacturing electrodes that can be used in the production of hydrogen and characterise its physical, chemical and electrochemical properties_x0094_. With this proposal we seek to strengthen the specific objective mentioned above. Especially, to emphasize the electrochemical characterization of the electrodes. The young headquarters of the Peace already has a potential to perform electrochemical characterization tests, however, it is necessary that the tests carried out be supplemented with other electrochemical techniques, with which the headquarters does not count.
