# Simulation of radiobiological effects in DNA induced by alpha particles of Ra223 using Geant4-DNA.

The direct effect of alpha particles generated from radioactive decay of Radio 223 to a DNA-B molecule (1ZBB) of the Protein Data Bank (PDB) as a biological endpoint was investigated using the Geant4-DNA extensions simulating the transport of primary alpha particles with 3-20 MeV energies and their secondary particles in liquid water to study the radiobiological damage in the form of SSB and DSB. Simulations were performed in a spherical water medium containing the DNA molecule, in which alpha particles were emitted isotropicly that radiate the volume of water evenly. It was assumed that energy depositions of more than 8.22 eV lead to ruptures of the DNA chain. In addition, the direct performance of SSB and DSB for alpha particles with different incident energies in terms of the LET were calculated. The results presented a reasonable agreement in terms of trend and value between DSB performance results of this work, other simulations and available experimental data.
