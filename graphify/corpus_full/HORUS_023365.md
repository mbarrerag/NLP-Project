# LIMITATIONS OF THE MINIMUM RENABILITY GUARANTEE OF PRIVATE PENSION FUNDS IN COLOMBIA

In individual funded pension schemes, profitability is a factor that influences not only the pension benefit but also the maintenance of the capital of pension funds. Some systems involve certain guarantee mechanisms such as the guarantee of minimum return on the investment of funds. This article analyzes the model used by the supervisory and regulatory authorities of private pension funds in Colombia to determine the compliance of the guarantee of minimum return. Based on the analysis, the limitations of this model are shown to provide protection to the resources of the participants. The model underestimates the minimum return required. Likewise, given the behavior of the flock that has characterized the returns, the way the model has been formulated finds it difficult to guarantee adequate returns as is the claim of its existence and does not protect against negative returns. It is suggested that a real guarantee should be established to provide protection to members in the investment processes of their pension savings
