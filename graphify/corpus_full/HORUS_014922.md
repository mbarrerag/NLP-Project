# Social energy and energy transfer in Colombia: from social practices to energy governance

The book, a research product, builds the thesis where the energy transition, specifically electric, is an opportunity for spatial, social and environmental justice in Colombia, insofar as current technology for its generation allows, in addition to the reduction of energy poverty, the democratization of energy by citizens. This opportunity, concretely defined in community energy, represents a new model of government of electricity energy that recognizes local energy practices of the inhabitants. This means greater appropriation and progress towards energy sobriety and other environmental sustainability actions, since it allows the generation of income through local business models based on the generation of self-consumption and sale of surpluses, and finally makes territorial development viable thanks to the rights and services that enable access to such resource. At present, in Colombia this possibility cannot be exploited by all populations, especially in rural areas where it would be of greater impact, as for this purpose local capacities are required that are not yet developed, and therefore, the book presents a geographical analysis of these capacities and a proposal of utility at local level with a view to advancing towards social business models in the context of Colombia
