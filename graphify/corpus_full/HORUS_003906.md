# ASSESSMENT OF THE TEMPERATURE PREFERENCES OF AEGGYPTI AEDES AND ALBOPICTS, ARBOVIRUS VECTORS IN LAITICS, AMAZONS

The increase in the average temperature of the planet has generated changes in the distribution of the vector insects, adapting to climatic variations and expanding to new regions. In the Amazon of Colombia, this has increased the cases of arboviral diseases (dengue, Zika, chikungunuña) transmitted by Aedes. aegypti and Aedes. albopictus. This study evaluated and compared the ranges of temperature preference of males and females between wild populations of Ae. aegypti and Ae. albopictus collected in November 2023 in Leticia, Amazonas. A bioassay in a thermocline with a temperature gradient between 19°C and 36°C. Fifty adult individuals of 3-5 days of age were introduced into males and females of each species and the distribution was counted after 1 hour = more than 1 hour. Replications with the thermocline on and off for females and males of both species. Results showed that the behavior of Ae aegypti of males and females found prefer the temperatures between 21-23°C and 27°C were established
