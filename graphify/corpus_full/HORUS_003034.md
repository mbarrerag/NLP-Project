# APPLICATION OF THE YOLOV 5 ALGORITHM FOR THE DETECTION OF MALEZAS IN THE MAIZ CULTIVE (ZEA MAYS L.) UNDER GREEN RESEARCH

Maize (Zea mays L.) is one of the most important cereals worldwide, to maintain crop productivity it is necessary to control weeds that compete for nutrients and resources, However, traditional methods of eradication often involve the use of agrochemical products, which involve important environmental problems so the implementation of technology that mitigates this impact is a challenge. This study presents a system of artificial vision based on the YOLov5s model (You Only Look Once), which uses a single convolutional neural network (CNN) to distinguish corn from four types of weeds. A mobile support structure was developed to capture images, which were later used to train the model. The trained model reached a mean accuracy (mAP@0.5) of 83.6% with a threshold of 0.5. For the class of maize, the system demonstrated a precision of prediction of 97% and a mAP@0.5 of 97.5%.
