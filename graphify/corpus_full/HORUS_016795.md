# A SHORT CHARACTERIZATION OF GAUSS PARAGRAPHS WITH APPLICATION TO VIRTUAL KNOT RECOGNITION PROBLEM

In this talk we explain a short soluction of the so well known Gauss paragraph problem by using the join operation given in [3]. Moreover, we introduce the concept of virtualization in the signed Gauss words category. We relate this concept with the solution of the Gauss word problem and the recognition problem for the case of classical knot diagrams with a single virtualized crossing
