# WALKING AWAY: ARCHITECTURE AND CHOFETER CULTURE

This book is presented as the official text for visitors of the academic program Campus Universitario (University Campus) ¿Paisajeando anando la arquitectura y cultura coffee maker?, part of the academic promotion of the National University of Colombia headquarters Manizales to the culture of innovation, which seeks to learn to value the uniqueities of landscapes as heritage, emphasizing the national and international dissemination of the Cultural Landscape Cafetero declared a World Heritage Site. Through this academic program, of experiential tourism, will learn to analyze the natural and cultural components that make up the Cultural Landscape Cafetero expressed in the modules: Coffee Culture, Vernacular Architecture and Gastronomy, as educational tools of direct learning that allow the attendees to live a training experience in the landscape. The three modules agree in highlighting the need of sensitive actions in specific fields, starting from the historical, biophysical, economic, political and cultural dimension, that allow to identify and give meaning to cultural landscapes
