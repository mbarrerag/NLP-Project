# Induced character in K-equivariant theory, wreath products and pullbacks of groups

Let G be a finite group and let X be a compact G-space. In this note we study the (Z+ × Z/2Z)-graded algebra (equation presented) defined in terms of equivariant K-theory with respect to wreath products as a symmetric algebra, we review some properties of FGq (X) proved by Segal and Wang. We prove a Kunneth type formula for this graded algebras, more specifically, let H be another finite group and let Y be a compact H-space, we give a decomposition of FqG×H(X×Y) in terms of FGq (X) and FHq (Y). For this, we need to study the representation theory of pullbacks of groups. We discuss also some applications of the above result to equivariant connective K-homology
