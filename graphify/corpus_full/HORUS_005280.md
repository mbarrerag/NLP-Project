# THE REFORM OF HIGHER EDUCATION: ESSENTIAL CONSIDERATIONS

In the public discussion of the reform of Law 30 of 1992, it is necessary to understand the characteristics of the social process of its reproduction. One objective of this brief article is to reveal this ontology. Another is to establish guidelines for an epistemology of the higher education reform that guides interventions aimed at closing gaps and reducing persistent inequality. In addition, it is discussed about aspects of the two laws that define the dynamics of higher education in the country: the Malthus Law and the Gresham Law. Finally, the principles for reflecting and understanding the meaning of the changes required in higher education are listed
