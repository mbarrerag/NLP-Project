# INTER-PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION: PERINATAL MATERN CARE (INfermeria) AND GENECOLOGY AND OBSTETRICIA (MEDICINE)

Interprofessional Education (ITE) is a student-centred teaching-learning strategy, which, as future professions, in medicine, nursing, among others; they need to work together to effectively address patient health situations. Evidence shows that skills such as teamwork and collaborative practice are not intuitive, nor are they learned through professional exercise; they need to be trained in the teaching process learning particularly when the student is in the training process. Thus, the World Health Organization (WHO) urges that education systems work on training strategies and it is in this context, where the concept of ITE arises as a necessary step for students and later health professionals to prepare for collaborative practice, indispensable for establishing teamwork, and thus to respond to health needs. Early start, with this strategy, hopefully before students initiate pre-graduate training practices, facilitates the development of basic collaborative work values within the interprofessional teams
