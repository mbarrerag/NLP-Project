# METHODOLOGICAL CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE CONSTRUCTION OF NEW KNOWLEDGE IN DISEASE

The methodological advances of the Research Group on Nursing Care and Practice, Family Health, Family Nursing and Health Measurement, based on the contribution of members as sole authors or participants of projects in the direction of undergraduate and graduate studies (specialization, master's and doctoral studies) or collaborative research, have allowed the study of some phenomena of interest for nursing discipline to be expanded. In this context, the purpose of this chapter is to present some research processes where a plot is constructed around nursing practice interests despite different perspectives and methodological routes, which have allowed to provide elements for the discussion of nursing phenomena. The chapter begins its journey by understanding the experience and meaning of menopause through the finding of units of meaning from Heidegger's phenomenological interpretation, recovering the voice of women. It continues with the prediction of the behavior of the sexual health of women with breast cancer and their sexual partner based on a structural equation approach. From the perspective of the quality of life of people in organ transplantation, the phenomenon was addressed based on the integration and consolidation of the results of primary studies (a critical analysis of the structure of care)
