# USE OF HIGH EFFECTIVE METHODS AFTER THE VOLUNTARY INTERRUPTION OF THE EMBARAZO IN PROFAMILY, 2015-2020

To describe the contraceptive methods reported by users of voluntary termination services (ive), before and after consultation, and to explore the association between sociodemographic characteristics and gynaecological background with the choice of contraceptives of high effectiveness after ive, with pregnancies of less than 15 weeks, in the department of Antioquia, Colombia, in the Profamiliar institution, between 2015 and 2020
