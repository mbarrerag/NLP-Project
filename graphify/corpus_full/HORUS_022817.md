# Strategy for the design of a microgrid focused on the supply of energy to critical loads under conditions of generation intermittance.

This document proposes a methodology for the optimal dimensioning of small-scale micro-grids made up of photovoltaic generation and battery storage assets using the Linear Programming with Enteros-Mixtos (MILP). The research is based on the case of a study of the city of Puerto Carreño in Colombia, for which its electrical, meteorological and economic parameters were modeled based on the free information published on the Internet. The methodology proposed in this document has a versatility to be used in any site provided that information of entry parameters is available and can be elaborated in even more detail to achieve a greater generalization. The results found with this methodology are those that minimize the investment and operation costs of the micro-networks of study in a period of one year for different investment values, installation areas and various techno-economic parameters of interest. Additionally, this research proposes a way to determine the necessary spare parts of the storage assets employed in the micro-network during the useful life of the project
