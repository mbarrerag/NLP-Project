# CONTRIBUTION TO THE TAXONOMY OF THE GENUS ARISTOLOCHIA (ARISTOLOCHIACEAE) IN THE STATE OF MATO GROSSO, WITH A NEW OCCURRENCE FOR BRAZIL

The work presents a key for identifying species of the genus Aristolochia L. (Aristolochiaceae) from state of Mato Grosso, Brazil, in addition to morphological descriptions for Aristolochia chiquitensis Duch., A. goudotii Shower.and A. hoehneana O.C.Schmidt considered new occurrences for the state, with A. goudotii being a new occurrence also for Brazil, in addition to taxonomic and phytogeographical comments and detailed photographs for the three species.The work was carried out between February and May 2021, with materials collected in the municipalities of Alta Floresta and Nova Bandeirantes, and analysis of specimens deposited in the Herbarium of the Southern Amazon (HERBAM), collected in the municipalities of Colíder, Itaúba and Nova Canaã do Norte, in the extreme north of Mato Grosso.The study morphology was carried out at the Plant Morphology Laboratory located at HERBAM, at the State University of Mato Grosso, Alta Floresta Campus, using a stereomicroscope and usual utensils.The identification of taxa, as well as the creation of the dichotomous key, were carried out by taxonomists.The present work expands from 22 to 25 species of Aristolochia in Mato Grosso, as well as from 83 to 84 species in Brazil, demonstrating the importance of field expeditions, botanical collections, and taxonomic studies for knowledge of the biodiversity
