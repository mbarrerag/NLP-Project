# QUIRURGICAL MANNEX OF BASOCELLLAR CARCINOME BASED ON THREE RISK GROUPS. EXPERIENCE IN A THIRD-LEVEL CENTRE OF COLOMBIA, 2008-2010

Basocellular carcinoma is the most frequent cancer of the skin, for its treatment it has been classified into two groups of recurrence risk. Describe the characteristics of primary tumors managed by conventional surgery, according to institutional guidelines, describing the findings of tumors with positive or recurrence margins and analyze the associated factors. A retrospective descriptive study that sought to relate the involvement of margins and recurrence, with the classification used in the National Cancer Institute of Colombia, by analyzing the demographic, clinical and histological variables of tumors managed with conventional surgery from 2008 to 2010. The time of tumor recurrence free was determined by the Kaplan-Meier method. 429 tumors were operated in 335 patients, allowing free margins in 96.5% of the resections based on the approach of three groups of risk. Of those with positive margins, 12 belonged to the group of high risk, 3 to medium or low risk. There were 5 recidivative groups, 3 to the group of local cancer, and 3 to the group of low risk
