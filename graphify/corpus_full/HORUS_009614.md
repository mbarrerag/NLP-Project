# EMERGENCY TECHNOLOGIES

The promulgation of Law No. 21.180 on Digital Transformation of the State has involved continuous modernization processes that have involved challenges in different areas, such as promoting a culture towards digital citizens and in turn the challenges in cybersecurity have required and will require efforts to coordinate between the public and the private for the appropriation of emerging technologies, generating preventive alerts and cybersecurity incidents. We must identify those technologies that have an impact on cybersecurity to incorporate them into the Digital Transformation Strategy at 2035. To do this, we must know the state of the art in relation to emerging technologies, establish future challenges, their domains and categories, in order to finally, through methodological analysis, propose incorporating their application and uses. IN more than 12 working meetings, held between 22 June and 30 November 2022, a team made up of 13 professionals from diverse formations, including lawyers, engineers, journalists, entrepreneurs, academics and others, achieved the result reflected in the chapter of the book
