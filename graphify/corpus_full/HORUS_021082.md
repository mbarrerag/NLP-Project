# Pernate connections of UHPC orthotropic plates: increased tensile strength in high-performance concrete elements

Ultra high performance concrete (UHPC) is a composite material with a remarkable capacity of self-compact and a high resistance to compression. The construction of precasts in UHPC, due to its exceptional properties, is considered as an interesting option in civil engineering projects. The development of practical and reliable methods for the design of connections with elements bolts in UHPC has been considered a crucial factor in future construction projects. At an initial stage of this research, the voltage concentrations and minimum distances from the center of the perforations to the edge of the union in the orthotropic connections of ultra high performance concrete UHPC. The voltage concentrations in pernate connections in orthotropic plates can be particularly high and can cause a fragile break with a decrease of the safety factors of the structure. The analytical solution proposed is based on the use of functions in the complex field and can be considered as an application of the theory of distribution of stresses in the anisotropic plates of Lekhnitskii. This analytical solution was validated experimentally, the need was determined in the first phase of the analysis
