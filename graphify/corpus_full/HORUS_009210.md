# GENETIC, MORPHOLOGICAL AND CHEMICAL DIVERSITY OF TITHONIA DIVERSIFOLIA COLLECTIONS (HEMSL.) A. GRAY FOR ANIMAL FOOD

Tithonia diversifolia is a shrub with a high potential for animal feeding, but has shown high variability in its agronomic yield, supply of nutrients and adaptation to specific production conditions. The aim of this work was to determine the genetic, chemical and morphological diversity of wild materials of T. diversifolia in Colombia and Mexico, and to asiperfilate genotypes highlighted for animal production.
