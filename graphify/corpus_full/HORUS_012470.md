# WAYS OF HONEY: LOVE OF GAULA AND THEIRS IN THE NETHERLANDS GOLD SIGNATURE

This paper studies the editions and reprints of the Dutch translations of the book of cavalry "Amadís de Gaula". Likewise, it is concerned with studying its reception and the impact on the cultural history that this book had during the seventeenth century, known as the Dutch Golden Century
