# Inter-faculty alliance for GHG reduction in cattle

In order to assess the effect of food supplementation on the nutrient balance (nitrogen (N), calcium (Ca), phosphorus (P) and potassium (K)) and emissions of carbon dioxide and methane (main greenhouse gases: GHG) by nursing cows in dual purpose systems, 81 cows managed under grazing will be selected to be distributed in nine farms of three regions (Caribbean, Orinoquia and Amazonia) (3 farms/region, 9 cows/fence and 27 cows/region) to which one of three dietary supplements will be randomly assigned: mineralized salt offered at will twice a day in the stable (SM); multinutrient block offered at will twice a day in the stable (BMN) and concentrated food (SC) offered in the stable twice a day at the rate of 0.5 kg/d/l milk response. The experiment will be performed in three consecutive 20 days each with three cows of each predium, the last three of which will be taken samples of the prairies, food supplements, faeces and urine for their chemical analysis
