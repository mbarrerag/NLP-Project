# THE CONFORM CONCEPT ON THE CARE OF DISEASE

ABSTRACT Introduction: For nursing, comfort is a goal of care in the multiple scenarios of disciplinary action, leads to the formulation of theories with holistic perspective and manages to apply comfort from a physical, psychospiritual, environmental and social perspective. To identify the attributes of the concept comfort understood by nursing in the different scenarios of care. Integrative review, with search strategy: Comfort? and nursing?, in the databases scopus, google academic, bvs, ebsco, cochrane, ovid and medline. The criteria of eligibility were: primary studies, to full text, published between 2009-2019, in Spanish, English and Portuguese. The prism diagram was used for the critical analysis of experimental designs, reviews and qualitative, the templates of the Critical Appraisal Skins Programme (Caspe) were used for the psychological and psychological aspects. For the other designs, the checklists of the Joanna Briggs Institute were applied, they were included 16 articles.
