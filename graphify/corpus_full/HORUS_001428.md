# SEMANTIC SEGMENTATION MODEL FOR SATELLITAL IMAGES BASED ON CONVOLUTIONAL NEURONAL NETWORKS FOR LAND COVERAGE CLASSIFICATION IN PARAMOS

The ecosystem of mooring is essential for water regulation and biodiversity conservation in mountainous areas. However, they face significant threats due to climate change and human activities such as agriculture, livestock and mining. The absence of clear delimitation and continuous monitoring systems of their coverage hinder their effective protection by highlighting the need to employ advanced digital techniques that provide information with high accuracy and updated.Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) have been positioned as promising tools for semantic segmentation of satellite images.This research sought to evaluate the performance of two models based on architectures of CNNs U-Net++ and DeepLabV3+ to classify the coverage of the land in the Tota-Bijagual-Mamapacha moor complex (TBM) ecosystems for the purposes of spatial mapping in Colombia, using Landsat 8 images from the period 2017 to 2019 and the national coverage tags of the IDEAM of 1.000.
