# IMPACT OF THE ECO-TENDING THE OUTER TO CONTRIBUTE THE AUTONOMY AND FOOD SOVEREIGNITY IN THE MUNICIPLE OF FUSAGASUGA

This initiative promoted by Tierra Libre, a social organization that promotes the defense of the territory, the local economy and permanence of the peasantry in the province of Sumapaz? Cundinamarca, through the Ecotienda La Huerta?, aims to break the logic of the conventional agrofood market, and generate a direct relationship between producers and consumers, favoring the commercialization of agroecological foods, within the framework of fair trade and the solidary economy (Free Land, 2020). Among the objectives set for this process that works since 2016, are identified relations between producer and consumer that have contributed to the social construction of this market in the city of Fusagasugá. The Huerta provides food that is produced locally by 30 farmers, family and community agriculture, consumers and tourists who prefer the consumption of food with differential quality (agroecological transition and artisanal production) in the municipality of Fusagasugá.
