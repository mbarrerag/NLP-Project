# Transformations of rural habitat in the 21st century in La Vuitrera, Palmira

The rural habitat of La Vuitrera (Palmira, Valle del Cauca) in the 21st century has been transformed, these changes in the territory can be framed within what some authors call “new rurality”. In dialogue with this concept and collecting the missing data by the local authorities (supported by phenomenology), it is sought to account for these transformations in the territory and their consequences (gentrification) from a new perspective, focused on the habitat and therefore more diverse. . The rural habitat of Valle del Cauca, Palmira, La Vuitrera in the XXI century has transformed. These changes in the territory are part of the concept of new rurality. Taking this into consideration, and with the information previously collected from field research, we are looking to discuss the consequences that these changes have led to such gentrification within the community
