# Entrepreneurship and sustainable development goals

As shown in ods 12 and 16, information, accountability and transparency are key elements of sustainability, as they involve the design and implementation of indicators, measures and reports to account for the commitment, and the degree of progress, of companies and governments to ods. Since accountability and transparency materialize through corporate accounting information, in this paper we ask ourselves: what role does corporate accounting information play in the search for ods? Therefore, the objective of this chapter is to present an overview and critical view of the role of corporate information in sustainability
