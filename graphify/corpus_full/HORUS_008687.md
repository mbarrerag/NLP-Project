# INTERRUPTION OF THE NIGHT INHIBE FLOWING AND MODULATION ACUMULATION OF CANNABINOIDS IN CANNABIS SATIVA L.

Cannabis sp. is a short day plant. Industrial flower production requires infrastructure to artificially extend the day and obtain good yields, with a high cost of production. Night interruption (night break, NB) is an alternative to the extension of the day, however, the control mechanism in Cannabis and the influence on the concentration of cannabinoids is unknown
