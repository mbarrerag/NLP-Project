# NUTRITIONAL MANNEX IN PATIENTS WITH SINGING IN PEDIATRIA: A NARRATIVE REVISION

Childhood cancer affects 400,000 children and adolescents in the world every year, being the second cause of infant mortality; it is estimated that up to 70% of paediatric patients with malignancy suffer malnutrition. The objective of this narrative review is to map the existing literature on nutritional management in pediatric patients with cancer, identify research gaps and provide an overview of current practices and areas requiring further study. Review following PRISMA-ScR methodology (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses extension for Scoping Reviews), which includes a systematic process for the search, selection and synthesis of relevant literature. Specific inclusion and exclusion criteria were used to ensure a comprehensive coverage of the topic.
