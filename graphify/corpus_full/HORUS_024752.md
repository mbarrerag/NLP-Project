# Rromanese Studies in Colombia: Brief Crytic Review to Establish a Panoramic

To present a critical review of Roma studies in Colombia, aimed at updating a state of art that will enable researchers interested in the Roma languages to understand the current situation in the field and to be able to propose possible lines of research
