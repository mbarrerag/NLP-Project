# Application of the design of experiments for the improvement of the production of export flowers

The main objective of this work is to show the improvement of the production of export flowers, for this purpose selects the rooting stage as the most significant of the process, with the help of techniques such as brainstorming, paret diagrams among other tools, from this selection the most significant varieties are defined to develop the analysis, which were florella and rony, due to their high percentage of losses and their high volume of production, once the object of study is identified, the technique of factorial designs is used and it is identified that the most significant factors in the losses of the stage are: the composition of the hormone and irrigation, for each one of them the "optimal" level is determined; obtaining the following results: reduction in the percentage of total losses with respect to the historical average of the varieties of 8 %, decrease the use of the hormone by 10%, do not use the slag as a means of cultivation, decrease the use of water by 5%, all of which implies annual savings of $41,337,417 in the analyzed stage. Therefore, it can be considered that the application of factorial designs in the improvement of the production of the other stages of flowers can be used (the production of the same process)
