# BIOPROSPECTION OF VEGETABLE SPECIES FROM COLOMBIAN ORINOCIA

The present study provides that, from the plant biological resource collected, the corresponding taxonomic and physicochemical characterization is initially carried out, as well as the obtaining of primary and secondary metabolites by various physicochemical techniques. Volatile fractions (VF), essential oils (AE) and plant extracts (EV) will be obtained by distillation, solid-liquid extraction and supercritical fluid. The metabolites obtained will be characterized and evaluated by various biological activity tests (antioxidant, antimicrobial, insecticide, antiparasitic, etc.), for the purpose of bioprospecting. Likewise, new materials and bioinsumes are planned, with various applications (fertilizers, biocontrollers, bioprotectors, biocatalizers), using as starting material VFs, EAFs and/or EVs
