# Hydroclimatic variability in the Sinu River Basin 1981-2020.

The study of hydroclimatic variability, the assessment of the hydrological impact caused by man and the determination of the water vulnerability of human systems is of great importance in the current climate change context; the design and implementation of adaptation or mitigation strategies should be based on the study of variables that may be affected, such as precipitation (P) and flow (Q). This study studies the variability of the hydroclimatic regime in one of the main basins of the Caribbean region of Colombia, the Sinu river basin, which has been subjected to a strong intervention process related to changes of coverage and use of the soil, and the construction and operation of the Urrá reservoir. The records of 46 pluviometric stations and 16 flow stations were used with at least 10 years of information and whose data are total or partial in the 1981-2020 interval. In addition, the analysis of the CHIRPS data base was used and the analysis applied methods of the rainfall field and has total coverage of the study area. The methodological process consisted in the application of appropriate statistical methods to characterize the variables, analyze their variability, detect the trends and evaluate the impact of the analysis of the applied (ENSO). As for the trends of rain, a significant and widespread decrease in rainfall was found in February, as well as an increase in water deficit in recent years. New indicators need to be designed and applied to assess the hydroclimatic vulnerability of hydroelectric production
