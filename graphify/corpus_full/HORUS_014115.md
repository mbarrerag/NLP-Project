# Diluting the limit between species of bees without stinger Melipona Illiger, 1806 (Apidae: Meliponini) from Antioquia

This research project focuses on elucidating the boundaries between the species of bees without sting of the genus Melipona present in the department of Antioquia.The difficulties in differentiating these species have affected their management, conservation and commercial regulation.The genus Melipona includes approximately 74 species, being the most diverse group of bees without sting in the Neotropical region, with 24 species reported in Colombia.Meliponas are key pollinators in natural and agricultural ecosystems, but the difficulty in their differentiation has led to their uncontrolled exploitation and errors in their identification, affecting their conservation. Recent studies suggest the existence of species not described and complex of cryptic species, which makes it more difficult to identify them accurately.The project has as general objective to clarify the limits between the species of Melipona in Antioquia using various sources of evidence, such as morphology, morphometry, DNA sequences and geographical distribution.The specific objectives include morphologically and with DNA sequences the species, analyzing the limits between them from an integrating perspective, and determine the species present through the review of biological collections
