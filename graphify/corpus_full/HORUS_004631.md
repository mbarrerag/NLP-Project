# VIII PHOTOGRAPHICAL CONNECTIONS. ON THE PHOTOGRAPHICAL IMAGE POSSIBILITIES IN GRAPHICAL DESIGN.

Photographic connections. On the possibilities of photographic image in graphic design is a chapter that deals with the photographic influence that the designer David Consuegra had in his academic formation and his professional working methodology that led him to propose strong connections between design and photography both in his curricular proposals at the School of Graphic Design, and in his written work in which he reflected on the trade and the profession of design. Photography allowed David Consuegra not only to use it as part of his work compositions for commercial purposes, but as a tool to support his research projects. Its coherence between thinking, doing and teaching graphic design, allowed his work to be a reference in many fields, identifying the importance of academic work in the training of professionals with a graphic and photographic sensitivity
