# Effect of the use of flexible barriers on the behaviour of a detritus flow applied to the upper basin of the Taruca gorge, municipality of Mocoa, using DEM

This study presents an analysis of what would have been the use of flexible barriers in the behavior of the detritus flow that took place in 2017 in the upper part of the Taruca basin, specifically in Mocoa. To carry out this research, a code was developed using the program "YADE". This code represents the fall of granular material along a predetermined distance of 2.5 km in the basin, with the aim of calculating the interaction between particles and barriers, having the volume of material involved. The formulation of the code was based on the Method of Discreet Elements (DEM), a numerical approach that allows the study of the behavior between particles by evaluating the individual interactions between them. This method provides a detailed representation of the particle dynamics, thus allowing a deeper understanding of the impact of the flexible barriers on the flow of debris in the study of interest. The code is specifically designed for the numerical simulation of the movement occurred in the coordinates corresponding to the upper part of the Mocoa basin, particularly in the Taruca.
