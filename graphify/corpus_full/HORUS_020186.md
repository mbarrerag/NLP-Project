# Miniature Fourier Lightfield Microscope

We report a miniature Fourier Lightfield Microscope, based on a cost-effective reversed surveillance camera lens and a contact field stop. The miniscope captures efficiently 3D information, in fluorescence or brightfield mode, in one single shot
