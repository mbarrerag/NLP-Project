# Burnout syndrome and the hybrid work model in administrative employees of a construction company in the city of Bogotá

The aim of the study is to analyze whether there is association between the burnout syndrome and the administrative workers with a hybrid work modality of a construction company in Bogotá. The study time began in October 2022 to November 2023, since the aim was to identify whether there is a relationship but not the effect. The approach of this research is quantitative in type, data were collected through questionnaires and analyzed on the variables to be calculated by means of a statistical analysis of relationships and results. Finally, it is non-experimental in type, it is not supported in the experiment, in this case, phenomena were observed in its natural environment and then analyzed without manipulation of the variables. According to the questionnaires applied to characterize both burnout syndrome and the hybrid model in the workers, the statistical results revealed the lack of relationship or dependence between these two constructs. The absence of a linear relationship between the perception of the hybrid model and the burnout syndrome suggests that they are not directly and proportionally affected by each other. .
