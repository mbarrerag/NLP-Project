# EXPERTS PARTICIPATING IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE CONSENSUS SECTION IV. DIAGNOSTIC CASES OF INFECTION BY SARS-COV-2/COVID-19

IV.1 What are the diagnostic tests to establish case confirmed by SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19? The recommended test for both epide-myological follow-up of the pandemic in each country, and for the eva- lation of patients in the diagnostic and eva- sion studies of interventions, is the test based on amplification of viral nucleic acids. In this case a real-time RT-PCR, based on TaqMan fluorescent probes. Although there are several protocols, from the first (Corman, 2020) re-carried by the Institute of Virology of Charité (Berlin, Ale-Mania) to those standardized in Thailand, Japan and China. The majority of countries have implemented the protocol that was designed by CDC (by its acronym Cen- ters for Diseases Control) framework, U.S. (US HHS, 2020). Both tests have demonstrated high sensitivity and specificity, it does not have cross reactivity with other coronavirus or with seasonal respiratory viruses, in addition can be used in any context1–3
