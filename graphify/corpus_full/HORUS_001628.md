# Second edition of the book: Practical Manual of Measures in Pediatric Radiology

The creation of this second edition arises from the need to update a work published in 2011, which compiled the key measurements for the practice of pediatric radiology. The daily challenges posed by this specialty due to changes in the proportion of anatomical structures and exponential growth of medical publications; motivated the renewal of the content. Through an exhaustive search of literature and following the experience of two subspecialists in pediatric radiology, the subjects to be incorporated were selected. New chapters were developed on generalities, central nervous system and Doppler. The chapters on head and neck, spine, thorax, abdomen and musculoskeletal system were significantly updated. The final result is a revised reference manual, designed to support the daily practice of medical professionals dedicated to pediatric care .
