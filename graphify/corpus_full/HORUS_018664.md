# OENOCARPUS MAPORA FRUIT OENOCARPUS MAPORA EXTRACTS EMPLOYING NATURAL EUTECTIC SOLVENTS

The extraction of bioactive compounds from natural sources generally requires the use of volatile organic solvents (DOVs), which are mostly relatively toxic and can leave residual substances in the extracts obtained. Considering that within the principles of green chemistry the use of safe and eco-friendly solvents is established, a new generation of green solvents has emerged in recent years as an alternative to DOVs. It is natural eutetic solvents [in English terminology: Natural Deep Eutectic Solvents (NADESs)], which are mixtures of primary metabolites (usually combinations of amino acids, sugars, organic acids and alcohols) that to certain molar relations generate viscous liquids with melting points inferior to those of individual components. In this work, six NADESs were prepared by means of heating to the bathroom, of equimolar mixtures of choline chloride (used as hydrogen bond acceptor) with six hydrogen bonding compounds.
