# USE OF GALBANIC LOSES AS A PRIME MATERIAL IN THE CERAMIC INDUSTRY FOR THE MITIGATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL POLLUTION

The galvanic process sludges due to their high content of Cr, Cu and Ni classified as hazardous waste, constitute a great environmental hazard. They are a byproduct of the galvanic industry for which there is currently no industrial exploitation, so they accumulate in safety cells. Galvanic sludge added in different concentrations (2%, 5% and 8% by weight) to clay of ceramic processing. The pieces are obtained by two methods of forming: dry pressing and extrusion. Two cooking cycles, maximum temperature of 1150°C are studied: Short 34 minutes; length 42 hours. The results show that as the percentage of sludge added to the ceramic piece increases, the resistance to the bending and linear contraction of the material is decreased. On the contrary, the absorption of water and the losses by calcination increases. The colorimetric coordinates for samples obtained by pressing in dry evidence, that at higher concentration of the mud the color changes from a cream tone to a green tone; whereas, in samples obtained by the extraction of the organic acid, the maximum amount of the organic acid is increased
