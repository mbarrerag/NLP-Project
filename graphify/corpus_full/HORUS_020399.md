# Manufacture of solar cells and estimation of their electrical parameters from their experimental characterization

Abstract In this research work, solar cells with Mo/CuInSe2/CdS/n+ZnO structure were deposited and characterized mentally by their I-V curve. From the results, the electrical parameters were estimated to be adjusted theoretically to the curve in order to determine the most important variables. The estimation was carried out using the models of a single diode and double diode and comparing the curve that is most appropriate. The variables estimated were photogenerated current (Iph), diode current in darkness (ls), serial resistance (Rs), parallel resistance (Rp) and factors of ideality of the diode (a1 and a2) according to each circuit model taken for optimization. The adjustment of current-voltage (I-V) and voltage-power (P-V) curves achieved with the double diode model indicates that in the solar cells manufactured the interfacial states are present in the p-n junction, which decrease the efficiency of the device
