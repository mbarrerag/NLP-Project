# USE OF COLOMBIA EVALUATIONS: AN EXPERIMENT OF INCIDENCY AND ACADEMIC COMMUNICATION BY POLICY PAPERS

The authors propose the development lived by the evaluation, its epistemological, theoretical and methodological controversies, and then analyze the autonomy of the evaluators and the use of their results in Colombian reality; in particular, the role played by the political paper/brief type documents
