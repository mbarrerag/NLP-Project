# Campesino Reserve Zone Head of the Orteza and San Pedro Rivers. A scenario of conflict between environmental, economic and agrarian rights.

The Caquetá department is seeking to establish a Peasant Reserve Zone (ZRC) involving the municipalities of Florence, La Montañita, El Paujil and El Doncello, with an extension of 161,000 hectares. The ZRC is moving with environmental regulations, requests for exploration and exploitation of hydrocarbons, as well as with the construction of a hydroelectric plant. The creation of the ZRC seeks to balance the balance against the different interests that exist within the territory, which have generated this conflict over land. Thus, the collision between constitutional rights to the healthy environment, economic development and progressive access to rural property is intended to overcome by carrying out a balancing exercise of principles, in order to limit none, but to give it its fair proportion, taking into account that it should be promoted by the effective enjoyment of rights by rural communities, who have lived inequitable conditions and inequality exacerbated by the armed conflict. Despite the fact that agrarian policy for the peasant communities has had normative and constitutional developments, these have not been sufficient to guarantee the effective enjoyment of rights, even the peace agreement proposes solutions for those communities to effectively access their rights
