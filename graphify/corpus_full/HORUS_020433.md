# Study of sediment transport in desiring tanks of small hydropower plants and recommendations for removal systems

This work analyzes the hydraulic behavior and transport of sediments of the desanrador tank of the Sancancio Plant of the Hydroelectric Plant of Caldas, CHEC, (this plant is catalogued according to its capacity, as a Small Hydroelectric Central - PCH), by means of the implementation of the numerical model 2D, Iber, version 2.5.2. The study part of the problem of progressive increase in the transport and production of sediments in rivers, which when entering the hydroelectric plants generate a erosive wear of the electromechanical equipment creating the need for structures of removal of more efficient sediments. The study was performed in two phases: field measurements and numerical modelling; in the first phase data were collected from different climatic conditions of a year, which were used in the second phase to model seven scenarios with hydrodynamic simulation for all flow and transport of sediments for the maximum flow. The results obtained presented a good adjustment between the measured data and the modelings with a relative percentage error for the speed between 0% and 25%, showing the maximum error in the lower speed zones, however, the trend of the speed in the data was the data was the analysis and the 2D numerical model, Iber, version 2.5.2. The study reviews specifically the hydrodynamics and efficiency of settling tanks as sediment removed removal structures in order to minimize erosive wear of the electronic technical equipment.
