# Spatial and temporal variability of solar radiation in Colombia 1997–2019.

In Colombia, solar radiation has been mainly studied locally. Some authors have studied solar radiation throughout the territory through interpolation of observations on the ground. However, these approaches have limitations related to the density of stations on the ground, the quality of observations and uncertainties associated with interpolation techniques. The ERA5-Land reanalysis of the ECMWF offers series of solar radiation towards the surface at a time and spatial scale of 0.1° grids, since 1980 for the whole globe. ERA5-Land allows to study solar radiation and other terrestrial variables using a set of data that is continuous in space and time. In this work, solar radiation from ERA5-Land was validated with time records of weather stations of IDEAM, distributed over the territory of Colombia. The variability of solar radiation time and daily, of ERA5-Land was characterized by the study of its daily and annual cycle; and its stochastic behavior was studied at these scales by calculating atmospheric indices of clarity and of clear sky.
