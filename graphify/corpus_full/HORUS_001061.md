# Dynamic analysis of sustainability in a market research company: a case study

This case study aims to analyze dynamically the sustainability of a medium-sized Colombian market research company (EIM), exploring from a theoretical-practical perspective how strategies employ sustainable dynamic capacities to generate competitive advantages. By using the methodology of system dynamic modeling, the problem is established, the dynamic hypothesis is formulated and a basic model of the operational structure is elaborated for the analysis of the organization. This enabled understanding how different strategies can drive stagnation or consolidation of the competitive position, taking into account the ability of the organization to respond to variations in market circumstances. Ultimately, it is concluded that strategies aimed at business sustainability have the potential to generate sustainable competitive advantages, if the capacities of monitoring, adaptation and transformation developed allow the company to properly manage market changes. .
