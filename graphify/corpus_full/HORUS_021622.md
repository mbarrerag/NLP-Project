# Regularized neural networks for the prediction of Colombian inflation.

For the financial sector, one of the relevant macroeconomic variables is inflation, which is generated by different pressures in the economy and its effects on it have been extensively studied. Monetary policy decisions that can control inflation have a direct impact on the economy and the financial sector, so in this sector there is a need to generate scenarios and inflation expectations that allow each of the responsible actors to make appropriate decisions in this sector. In this work, first, a background review is carried out in search of variables that can be provided in a forecast model for inflation, 24 variables were chosen from which seven were finally selected by a generalized additive model. A total of 100 models of multilayer perceptron neural networks were trained and evaluated with the aim of comparing the performance of three regularizers, LASSO (Least absolute shrinkage and selection operator), Ridge Regression and ElNet (Elastic Net), were performed a cross-validation of the models and final candidates were evaluated with the measure of the average quadratic error and coefficient of determination.
