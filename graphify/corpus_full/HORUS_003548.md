# COMPARISON OF MACHINE LEARNING STORIES TO SEARCH EVAPOTRANSPIRATION WITH UNCOMPLLETED DATA IN VALLE OF THE CAUCA

This study evaluated the use of different Machine Learning (ML) models to predict evapotranspiration (ET) in Valle del Cauca, Colombia, using incomplete meteorological data. Data from 32 Cenicaña stations were collected and several models were tested, including polynomic regression, multivariate regression, neural networks, vectoral regression of support and Random Forest. The selected variables were temperature, relative humidity, solar radiation and altitude. The results showed that the combination of mean temperature and solar radiation produced the best predictions, with correlation coefficients (R) above 0.9 and determination coefficients (R 2 ) above 0.8 in most models. Although ML models offer moderate accuracy and reduce the dependence of complete data, they do not replace high-precision field measurements of ET
