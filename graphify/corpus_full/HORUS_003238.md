# ANALYSIS OF THE CARBONATATION PROCESS OF A 3D PRESSED CONCRETE IN A HPC MIX, VARIATING PARAMETERS OF THE CONSTRUCTIVE PROCESS

The 3D printing of concretes has had a significant advance in recent years, cataloguing itself as an emerging and promising technology for many fields of knowledge. The durability performance of the concretes printed in 3D is decisive for its application and currently many authors recommend continuing to carry out in-depth studies in this field. In environmental terms, 3D printing of concretes is able to significantly reduce the carbon footprint that comes from the construction processes in the buildings, as well as the decrease in execution times and work risks in the works. Thus, this study will focus on evaluating the degree of carbonation of specimens of concrete printed in 3D, varying the constructive parameters: printing speed and type of nozzle in a HPC mixture. Through an accelerated carbonation chamber, the resistance of specimens to CO2 attack and their mechanical behavior will be evaluated.
