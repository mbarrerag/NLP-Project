# ADMINISTRATION OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS IN CIRUGIA ROOMS FROM A HUMANIZED APPROACH

The lack of safety of surgical care can cause considerable damage.In industrialized countries, significant complications have been recorded in 3-16% of surgical procedures requiring admission, with mortality or permanent disability rates of approximately 0.4-0.8%. Studies in developing countries indicate a 5-10% mortality rate in major surgery operations. At least seven million patients are affected by surgical complications each year, of which at least one million die during the operation or immediately thereafter. Technical guide Good practices for patient safety in health care.. In Colombia there is depersonalized and relegated care The results obtained seek to guide practices that are in keeping with reality, that favor safety and quality in the care of patients during the surgical process
