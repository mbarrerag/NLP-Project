# Clinical effectiveness and safety of pharmacological and psychological interventions for the treatment of social anxiety disorder in adolescents: Systematic review of literature with network metaanalysis.

To evaluate the clinical effectiveness and safety of pharmacological and psychological interventions used for the treatment of social anxiety disorder in adolescents. A systematic review of the literature was carried out with network metaanalysis, implementing a frequency model of random effects. The reduction of symptoms of social anxiety was evaluated as the standardized difference of means between the change of the score in the baseline to the follow-up evaluation. Partial and complete remission were evaluated as a dichotomous outcome based on the relative risk in the frequency of the event. The certainty of the evidence was evaluated with the CINeMA approach. After the identification of 9309 references in databases and complementary sources 25 randomized clinical trials were included. Most of the studies evaluated different modes of cognitive-conductual therapy, including individual, group, virtual, self-directed applications, among others. Among the pharmacological treatments, the use of venlafaxine ER, fluoxetine and paroxetine were identified. However, these studies included population of children, and presented high risk of sesgo, which resulted in the limited results.
