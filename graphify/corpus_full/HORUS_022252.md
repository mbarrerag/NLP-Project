# Keys to participatory high mountain land management in the Andes of Colombia

This work discovers some of the most sensitive aspects that should be taken into account when studying high mountain territories. Its findings around the main ecological structure, socio-economic, organizational and participatory, and educational aspects, become relevant contributions to its territorial planning. The experience with mountain communities in the Andes of Colombia, with their natural and cultural heritage, based on the dialogue of knowledge, is articulated with territorial and institutional perspectives around reconciliation proposals. The opportunity to innovate in a participatory way, within the framework of the post-agreement, allows to make some reflections in view of a territory unknown by many people, and thus to draw attention to key aspects for ecosystem and social well-being. We hope that by bringing people closer to the reality of high mountain territories, it will contribute to awakening greater sensitivity for national heritage.- Contracadas
