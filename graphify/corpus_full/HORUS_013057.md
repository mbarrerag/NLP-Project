# Teaching of the stequiometry through analogies specific to the socio-cultural context of students

Abstract In the teaching of chemistry, quantitative relations generate problems for the understanding and resolution of stoichiometric calculations, due to the difficulty in the application of concepts of reason and proportion, especially, and also to abstract representations and mental models to be performed. In this work a didactic strategy was implemented based on the use of analogies typical of the sociocultural context of students of the tenth grade of the Loperena National Educational Institution (Valledupar, Colombia), to facilitate the understanding and visualization of abstract concepts such as those that are part of the learning of stochiometry. The strategy starts with the identification of previous concepts; then continues, with a stage of structuring the analogies specific to the daily life of students; then, with a stage of development and application of class guides to establish links with the topic treated and, lastly, uses an evaluation instrument to assess the scope of the implementation of the strategy. The results revealed a significant improvement, not only in the processes of interpretation and resolution of hypothetical situations associated with the stochyometria, but also, in the motivation of students towards the strategy
