# Assessment and conservation of brick architecture built in Bogotá in the early 20th century

The brick has played a fundamental role in the construction development of the city of Bogotá. Thanks to the technological advances of the brick industry in the late 19th and early 20th century, this material was used as an expressive and ornamental resource in the facades of countless buildings that are still preserved and have a great heritage value. Nowadays, the brick in view is an essential part of the city’s architectural landscape and has contributed to consolidating the identity of various sectors. Thus, this work seeks to contribute to the conservation of the heritage built in bricks in view through the valuation of its ornamental pieces. Based on the study of some characteristic sections of the city and two buildings representative in a detailed way, it was possible to identify 12 types of ornamental pieces and to analyze the way in which they were used as a basic ornamental module in the configuration of various faça elements .
