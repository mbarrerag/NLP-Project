# LOCAL ADAPTATION TO NOVEL HABITATS AND CLIMATIC CHANGES IN A WILD TOMATO SPECIES VIA SELECTIVE SWEEPS

Adaptation to abiotic conditions often occurs by means of positive selection. In heterogeneous environments, however, plants may be strongly influenced by locally variable selection. This can lead to divergence of populations at key loci (Savolainen et al., 2013; Tiffin & Ross-Ibarra, 2014), and results in trade-offs where native alleles show a fitness advantage relative to foreign alleles (antagonistic pleiotropy) culminating in local adaptation (Kawecki & Ebert, 2004)
