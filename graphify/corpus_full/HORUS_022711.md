# Spectral noise reduction in hyperspectral images by discrete wavelet transform

Abstract The use of hyperspectral sensors has taken on importance in agriculture, due to their potential in crop plant protection management. However, these sensors are sensitive to the recording of spectral noise, which makes it difficult to apply effectively. Therefore, this work focused on the analysis of spectral noise present in a bench of 180 hyperspectral images of mango leaves acquired in laboratory, and the implementation of a noise reduction technique based on the discrete wavelet transform. Noise analysis consisted of identifying the most noisy bands, while the performance of the technique was measured with the PSNR and SNR metrics. As a result, it was determined that spectral noise was present at the ends of the spectrum (417-421nm and 969-994nm), while the Neigh-Shrink method reached a NRS of the order of 1011 with respect to the order of 102 of the original spectrum
