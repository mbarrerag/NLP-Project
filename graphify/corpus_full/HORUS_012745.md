# Factors that affect the improvement of the competitiveness of the offer continuing education: a Case study of three institutions of higher education in the District Science, Technology and Innovation of Medellin

The general objective is to evaluate the factors that influence the competitiveness of the Continuing Education offer in the Higher Education Institutions such. The methodology used was mixed, interviews were conducted with actors responsible for continuing education in the two Higher Education Institutions and the University, in order to obtain primary and secondary information, capture experiences and perceptions of the main actors involved. A quantitative methodology was used, which allowed direct exploration, analyzing qualitative data of content, narrative or discourse, to determine the factors that affect the generation of value and competitiveness of the Continuing Education offer. The results were a detailed exploration of the factors that influence the competitiveness of the two Higher Education Institutions and the University, as well as the analysis of the perception of the continuing education offer among the actors involved and proposals that add value to the competitiveness of the continuing education offer, to contribute to the improvement of its quality and relevance offered by the Higher Education Institutions, benefiting the academic community of the three institutions to be competitive in a changing educational environment
