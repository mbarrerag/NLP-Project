# Development of a clustered product of Uchuva (Physalis peruviana L.) with instant characteristics and potential antioxidant effect

The uchuva (Physalis perupviana L.), is an exotic fruit of the Andean region, which presents an increasing demand because it possesses aromatic characteristics and nutritional properties favoring its use as functional food. Colombia is among the main producers and exporters of exotic fruit in the world, mainly gulupa and uchuva, therefore, is the fifth fruit with the highest market after the banana in terms of export. The generation of new products, with innovative flavors and with better physical and chemical characteristics has allowed the development and implementation of new methodologies for its acquisition. An alternative to this context is the drying of the pulp in order to obtain a powdered product, preserving the properties of the fruit. Some drying methods are inappropriate because they strongly affect the sensory characteristics and nutritional properties of the fruits. The drying by aspersion, is a method used in the pulp of fruits that are sensitive to the heat, being its main advantages the high performance and reduction of the thermal damage.
