# TRADITIONAL MODELS VS MODELS OF EQUINE LEARNING: CHARACTERIZATION, ALERTS AND USE RECOMMENDATIONS

Machine learning techniques are becoming one of the most popular tools in the time series forecast. However, as M4 [1] skills show, they are not always the best option when it comes to forecasts and, at times, classical statistical models can be much more precise. In this work we present a guide on the use of the LSTM neural networks, which will provide recommendations and alerts on their use for time series analysis. In addition, we will identify scenarios in which machine learning models could be a good approach or when to use classic statistical models instead
