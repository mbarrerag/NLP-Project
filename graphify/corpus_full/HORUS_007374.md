# Complementary tests on connections with beam end plate I A composite column embedded

The construction systems with steel structure have the characteristic of being versatile and give the possibility of structural solutions for creative architectural requirements. In Colombia, the implementation of structures in steel is becoming more common and there are rules that regulate the materials used, the tolerances in their construction and their structural design. This document is a contribution to the development and proper use of this type of structures understanding that in Colombia it is usual to use concrete structures and there is a tendency to combine both structural systems. This latter is not a purely Colombian custom, large projects such as the Petronas Towers and the Khalifa Burj have shown that the combined structures are a viable way to obtain lightness and resistance. There are different ways to join steel beams to composite columns according to the type of link you want to generate.
