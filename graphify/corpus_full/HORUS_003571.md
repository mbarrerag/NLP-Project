# [Not Available].

Analyzing water insecurity (IAg), frequency of water supply (FSA) and food insecurity (AI) in Mexican households, addressing their social determinants and providing recommendations for public policies. Material and methods. The information of 28 500 households in the National Health and Nutrition Survey (Ensanut Continua 2020-2023) was analyzed. Scales of validated experiences such as HWISE and ELCSA were applied to measure IAg and IA, as well as an indicator on FSA, according to some determinants. 16 per cent of Mexican households experience IAg and 22 per cent experience moderate and severe AI. Only 34.7 per cent receive water 24 hours a day. The determinants of households most affected by IAg, IA and FSA are the worst conditions of well-being, being indigenous and when AI and IAg are together in households. It is essential to accelerate implementation and improve coverage of actions that impact positively on the access and availability of water and food for vulnerable people
