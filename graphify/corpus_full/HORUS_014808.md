# Neuromuscular profile of the lower train in cheerleaders of the city of Bogotá, evaluated by vertical jump analysis.

The porrismo has had an important global and local growth. The research in this population in favor of interventions to improve the performance and prevention of injuries are scarce.The objective of this thesis is to determine the neuromuscular profile of the lower train of bogotanese cheerleaders by vertical jump, and its relation to asymmetries, and describe history of lesions in the population.It is a transversal study of descriptive and analytical type.A survey was conducted to know history related to sports injuries.We performed countermovement jump tests (CMJ) and drop jump jump (DJ). 84.62% have presented sports lesions, the most frequently compromised areas were knee, ankle and shoulder.In the CMJ differentiated by gender there is statistically significant difference in the values of the variables JH, CMF, CMP, ECCB-RFD, ECCB, EccMF, EccMP, CMD.
