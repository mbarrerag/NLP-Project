# Resignation of cultural heritage in urban areas

The article investigates the importance of cultural heritage as an element of cohesion in communities settled in border territories. For this, three themes are addressed: the definition of heritage, the re-signification as a process of recognition of the realities of communities with their territory, and the border space as a scenario of convergence of various socio-territorial conditions. The results show how re-signification is an opportunity to reflect on the dynamics that operate in urban border contexts, such as the case of Usme and its urban-rural particularity. It is concluded that the construction of tools capable of intervening and managing the dynamics present in the border territories and communities is necessary
