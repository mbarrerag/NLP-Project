# CHALLENGES OF THE OCCUPATION THERAPY IN MENTAL HEALTH: REFLECTIONS FROM A EXPERIENCE OF DOCUMENTS

Reflecting occupational therapy in mental health from the teaching practice, with a qualitative and critical approach, allows identifying professional challenges in the usual contexts of individuals and collectives. Prioritizing the clinical context for care has conditioned the integrality and continuity of the processes. Occupational therapy in mental health must also pass through the usual contexts, in which people participate in occupations. This article presents the reflections made, around the challenges of occupational therapy in integral mental health care, from the teaching practice. These reflections are based on the use of qualitative techniques of analysis used by teachers in the psychosocial area between 2013 and 2020, among which is included the review and documentary analysis of the normative and conceptual framework, field journals, documents of systematization of experiences, participant observation and academic spaces of discussion. It is observed that occupational therapy in mental health in Colombia, has been structured mainly in clinical contexts, with a resolutive approach that frequently responds to individual needs related to the symptoms associated with the prevalent disorders. This institutionalization of practices limits the continuity of the processes.
