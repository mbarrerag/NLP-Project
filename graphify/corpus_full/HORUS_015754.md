# CHAPTER 1: THE AFFAIRS OF KNOWLEDGE ON THE VICTIMS OF ARMED CONFLICT

In this review of the literature about the victims of armed conflicts or military dictatorships, the emphasis has been placed on the peace agreement concluded between the government of Juan Manuel Santo and the former FARC guerrilla in 2016, the challenges that exist for its implementation in Colombia and the productions of the last twelve years in Latin America. Gaps of knowledge have been identified, among these, studies that deepen in the forms of expression of the victims as political subjects and actors protagonists of their destiny, or in the processes of emancipation from their victimization. Another identified vacuum is the situations of self-adscription in a kind of victim identity; understood as a field of verification of the subject that closes and impoverishes other possibilities of being, and the individual, social, political and cultural circumstances that can lead to attenuating, exiting or remaining in that victimistic circumstance
