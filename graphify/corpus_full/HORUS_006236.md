# Characterization of extracellular vesicles type exosomes with VEGF marker in a breast cancer model

The exosomes, a type of extracellular vesicle, emerge in physiological and pathological conditions as in breast cancer, playing a key role in intercellular communication within and outside the tumor microenvironment. To understand its function, it is essential to perform the physical and molecular characterization of its composition, as well as to establish its interactions in biological contexts. In this work, exosomes from the in vitro culture of the cell line of breast cancer MCF7 were obtained. In normoxia conditions, two commercial methods were evaluated, one based on chromatography of exclusion by size and one on targeted filtration. The exosomes of the same cell line were compared with those obtained by the traditional method of ultracentrifuge of differential chloride, and the exclusion chromography by size was selected as the method with greater performance, economy and accessibility for the purification of exosomes.
