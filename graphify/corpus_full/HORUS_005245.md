# HABITAT SUSTAINABLE NEW HOPE.

The group of architecture, environment and sustainability, of the National University of Colombia (UNAL), participates with the development of an architectural proposal, a mobile module for community activities, with the use of alternative energies and the use of drinking water, fulfilling the SDGs. It involves community pedagogy, food security and sovereignty, water purification, health brigades, and produces cartography for the improvement of the occupied territory. The problem detected: The Nueva Esperanza settlement, in addition to the deficiencies, in infrastructure, housing, employment, education, added to an economic and social marginality, does not have a physical habitable space, that allows to gather the community, to develop activities in search of improving the living conditions
