# Design of a computer model for intelligent information management in power transformers from sources of the Internet of Energy (IoE)

This research project presents a computational prototype designed to obtain value, optimize the useful life of the assets especially of the power transformers, through the integration of data and processes into an Internet energy ecosystem (IoE).The integration of concepts related to the management of assets in the transformers of power distribution networks in Colombia requires a structured methodology. It is proposed hybrid methodology that combines the approach of thought of design, with emphasis on the compliance of the standard of Asset Management (International Organization for Standardization, 2014) with the concepts of active management of the equipment.This is the result of the integration of the actives of the energy networks and standard technologies such as the CRISP-DM.The prototype seeks to characterize, understand the behavior and facilitate the interaction of the transformer of power as a physical productive asset of the electrical network, improve its management and safety.The Coresense device located in the assets of the Hydroelectric Center of Caldas of according to the criticality allows to the ability to collect data in real time on variables such as oil temperature, the hydrogen and the humidity of the power transformers. The technological platform acts as an ecosystem to process and store these data. The analysis of the information generates, allows indicators, allowing to be maintained and to be able an ecosystem based on the IoE concept, to manage the safety, reliability and useful life of the assets in an electrical distribution network (Iberdrola, 2024) . This research project presents a computational prototype designed to add value and optimize the livespan of assets, especially power transformers, through the integration of data and processes in an Internet of Energy (IoE) ecosystem.The integration of concepts related to the attachment management in power transformers in distribution networks in Colombia requires a structured methodology. A hybrid methodology is proposed that combines the design thinking approach, with an employment on competitiveness with the Entity management standard (International Organization for Standardization, 2014), along withtting-edge technique economy. Real-time data analysis and visualization enable the optimization of energy distribution, as decisions could be made by reducing potential equipment replacements or maintenance needs based on performance measurements, comparing them with international standards (EIMAC, 2024), and agreeing to the asset studied, determining operational scenarios that improve grid efficiency. In this way, the system could impact the reduction of maintenance, repair, and service interaction costs, as outlined in the operational requirements for distribution networks under Colombian regulations (Energy and Gas Regulatory Commission, 2018).
