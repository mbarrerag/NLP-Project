# Mercury detection in water based on an E-SMS optical fiber structure

Etched singlemode-multimode-singlemode (E-SMS) optical fiber structures are evaluated through the immobilization of a chitosan/Fe2O3 absorption layer for the detection of Hg2+ ions in water at concentrations as low as 0.001 μg/mL (1 ppb)
