# A STATE OF THE QUESTION ON RESEARCH FROM ARTS IN LATIN AMERICA

This conference presents the preliminary conclusions of the state of the research issue entitled “Policies and epistemologies of research from the arts.” Trajectories in Latin America, led by the speakers and funded by the Vice-Rectorship of Research of the National University of Colombia. Researchers ask themselves what the main trends of thought or debate have been about the meaning or place of research in artistic practices will be considered Grosso mode, the project finds that terms such as artistic research, research-creation, or artistic practice as research, despite their apparent similarity, point to different perspectives or constellations of thought and action on this problem. In the revised writings, arguments for artistic production as a legitimate form of research are predominant, as well as the claim of the arts in the university system and in those instances that validate scientific knowledge. To a lesser extent, there are arguments that call attention to the inter- or trans-disciplinary nature of research in the arts at present
