# POLYGESTA - Environmental Policy, Legislation and Management

At the global level, environmental pollution represents a growing concern, affecting not only ecosystems but also the economy and human health. In Colombia, the challenge is particularly acute, with studies that show alarming levels of pollution in aquatic and terrestrial systems, directly affecting the biodiversity and health of the population.Despite previous efforts through integrated pollution management, there are significant limitations in the effectiveness and implementation of public policies.In this context, the main objective of the project is to create the POLYGESTA Thinking Center for the study and analysis of Colombian Environmental Policy, Legislation and Management, with a particular focus on environmental pollution issues.This center seeks to be a place for collaboration between strategic actors of the University, Enterprise, State and Society, promoting the generation of specialized knowledge, intersectoral collaboration, citizen participation, and the development of more effective policies to address environmental challenges in Colombia.The POLYGES review think Center aims to be a key actor in the transformation towards more comprehensive and responsible environmental management in the country. Through the critical analysis of existing environmental public policies and the proposed new environmental strategies, Colombia will contribute significantly to the mitigation of environmental problems and the strengthening of strategic lines
