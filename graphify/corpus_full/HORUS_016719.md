# Description of the phonology of the variety of the language spoken in Anteta, Norte de Santander

The U’wa are one of the ancestral peoples with the greatest geographical dispersion in the Colombian north-eastern. At present they are located in the departments of Arauca, Casanare, Boyacá, Santander and Norte de Santander. Although the groups located in these departments are identified as U’wa, each one has different customs and speak different dialects. To date, linguistic descriptions of the varieties spoken in Boyacá, Arauca and Casanare have been made, but the varieties spoken in Santander and Norte de Santander have not yet been described. This work is pioneering in this sense, since to contribute to the preservation of the indigenous languages and the description of these, the purpose of this research is to analyze the phonelogical system of the Anteta variety spoken in Norte de Santander. This work uses central concepts of the phonology, such as phonema, phonelogical rules, sylaba and accent, from the theoretical perspective of the structural paradigm. Its approach is qualitative, so the methodology contemplated the implementation of data collection techniques as questionnaires, interviews and participant observation. The corpus collected in the field was transcribable, system and analyzed through the method.
