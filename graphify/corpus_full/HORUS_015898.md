# DIGITAL GOVERNMENT ENTERPRISE APP GENERATOR. A BPM BASED APPROACH

Serving the citizen through digital means is a priority for government entities, in Colombia in particular this purpose has been difficult to fulfill despite the extensive regulation and policies established for it. To build a tool that supports the digitization of formalities in the Colombian government, leveraging the Business Process Management - BPM approach and emerging interface construction technologies. Following the Design Science Research, we start from a problem to design an artifact that contributes to solve it. We designed a tool that uses a reference business process - R-BP and an interface generator that complies with the regulated implementation of formalities in Colombia. The flexibility of the proposed development is one of the fundamental quality attributes in this first approach to the solution; both at interface and business process level
