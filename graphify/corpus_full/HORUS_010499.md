# DOUBLE INJURY OF THE SPINAL MEDULA BY PROJECTIL OF FIRE ARMS WITH MIGRATION IN THE MEDULARY CANAL: REPORT OF A CASE

The surgical treatment of lesions by firearm projectile in the spine is controversial; however, neurological deficit is one of the criteria for its indication. Presentation of the case. 21-year-old man who entered the emergency department of a third-level hospital in Bogotá, Colombia, due to multiple wounds of firearms, paraplegia, hypoaesthesia in lower limbs and saddle, and inability to move, so thoracotomy and laparotomy were performed. In the absence of muscle strength and reflexes, grade A spinal cord injury was diagnosed according to the American Spinal Injury Association Impairment Scale (ASIA). At 12 hours after admission, the patient was taken to surgical extraction of a projectile (laminectomy and longitudinal hardotomy) without obtaining extraction, so fluoroscopy was used, in which it was observed that the cephal artery had migrated to the intervertebral space L4-L5.
