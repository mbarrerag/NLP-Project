# Strategy for monitoring aggressiveness for non-technical losses using energy balances

This work focuses on the design and implementation of an integral strategy for the targeting of non-technical losses in users of the electric system of Bogotá and Cundinamarca. An algorithm of balance sheet combination has been developed for the identification of circuit supplences, forming the basis of an effective and reliable approach to manage non-technical losses. Additionally, strategies for the optimization of resources in macromediation maintenance, prioritizing areas and clients with greater risk. In the course of the study, three successful cases of energy recovery were classified and identified, with a total estimate of 1.15GWh of CNR. These cases of success demonstrate the viability and effectiveness of the proposed strategy, underlining its potential positive impact in the management of non-technical losses. In addition, as an integral part of the strategy, three algorithms of machine learning were implemented for the classification of potential fertilators. This incorporation of advanced technologies significantly strengthens the ability of the system to identify and address proactively cases of non-technical losses, improving the efficiency of the inspection and monitoring process. In short, the work not only proposes an integral strategy, but also supports its effectiveness through cases of in-use and in-cases. The first and second of the first three cases of energy recovery were classified and identified, with a total estimated recovery of 1.15GWh of CNR.
