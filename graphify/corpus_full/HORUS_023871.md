# THE DISCREET OF FORIER AS A ANALYSIS TOOL IN STATISTICS

Fourier's discrete transform (TDF) is a tool for analysis of engineering and physics by which a signal of a discrete nature can be analyzed. Among the analyses that can be performed is energy, energy distribution in the different frequencies that make up the signal and others. By the properties presented by the Fourier transform can be used as a statistical tool to perform the analysis of discrete random variables. The TDF of the probability mass function of the discrete random variable corresponds to the so-called characteristic function. With the use of the properties of the TDF the moments of the random variable can be obtained, so that the TDF also corresponds to the moment-generating function. On the other hand, the effect of changing parameters of the mass function can be observed through the analysis of the TDF. In the case of the use of several statisticians for the same discrete variable, the TDF can be used to observe the advantages and disadvantages of these and thus be able to make the decision of choice of the statistician
