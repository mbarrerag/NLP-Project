# THE UNIVERSITY AND ARMED CONFLICT REPORT IN COLOMBIA: SIMETRIAS AND THEORY OF TWO DEMONIES

The aim of this article is to challenge some thesis formulated by the Commission for the Clarification of Truth, Coexistence and Non-Repetition (CEV), based on a critical analysis of the report on Universities and Armed Conflict in Colombia. In the first part of it, the right to truth is analyzed from a brief tour of international and national jurisprudence; in a second moment, and recovering the contributions of the Argentine sociologist Daniel Feierstein on memories and representations, the truth process is analyzed in relation to the multiple processes of memory construction and how these are generally reflected in the Commission's report; in order to finally formulate some critical approaches regarding the way the CEV has interpreted the armed conflict in Colombian universities
