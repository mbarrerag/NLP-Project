# DISTORSIONS OF THE CHRISTAL STRUCTURE OF THE OXIDE OF DONE ZINC BY MANDANESE BY MORPHOLOGICAL AND STRUCTURAL STUDIES

The distortions generated by the substitutional doping of manganese, replacing Zinc, in the crystalline structure of ZnO, have been studied. ZnO was prepared by chemical means (sol-gel citrate route), at various levels of doping. The structural study was performed by X-ray diffraction, showing distortions at the structural level, as the level of Mn in the ZnO increases. l morphological study was carried out by means of electron scanning microscopy (SEM) and transmission (TEM). The average size of glass and the shape of them is possible to visualize them with measurements of this latter technique
