# SOCIO-FAMILY IMPACT OF THE MECHANIUM ORDEN IN CAMPESINIC ECONOMIES

Implementing technological innovations generates alterations in the daily life of those involved in the production processes, so that the objective of this exercise was to examine the socio-family impact of mechanical milking on peasant dairy economies, where the use of this tool has increased in recent years. For this purpose, a study was carried out with 40 peasants in economic, family, human development and psychosocial aspects, expressed in 25 variables analyzed with a model of structural equations. The effects were explained by the three initial dimensions, discarding the influence of the psychosocial component. It is concluded that there is a working culture of the greatest possible effort that explains the perceptions of the peasants about this tool, focusing on the increase of income, the reduction of costs, the reduction of daily work in dairy production and the reduction of risks of labor disease. It is observed that there is a replacement of ancestral knowledge and the strengthening of social capital
