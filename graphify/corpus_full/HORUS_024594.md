# ACADEMIC SPACE CONSTRUCTION IN DISABILITY, ARMED CONFLICT AND PEACE CONSTRUCTION

Two academic spaces were created to serve as a scenario for addressing the relations between disability and armed conflict, as a central issue to contribute to their re-knowledge. With the determined desire to contribute to the construction of peace, from the Master's Degree in Disability and Social Inclusion and the Master's Degree in Design.When activating these thinking exercises, it was warned that women, men and other gender subjects of all ages and conditions occupy a position in the social world, narrate experiences, weave memories, plot stories or order projects, while, with different reflective tools, they combine cultural and political realities and reconfigure social and academic relations
