# Strengthening processes associated with metric thinking based on the problem approach in students of secondary school

The present research work aims to contribute to the development of processes associated with metric thinking in secondary basic students of the Archdiocesan Liceo of Our Lady, through the design and implementation of learning activities based on problem resolution, with a special emphasis on strengthening the processes of magnitude estimation, assessment of the range of magnitudes, and selection of units of measurement, patterns and instruments. The research is framed in a naturalistic paradigm with a qualitative approach of descriptive scope in which a design based on the grounded theory is used, implemented through the development of three phases: diagnosis, intervention or consolidation, and validation and verification. It is expected to show a significant improvement in the construction of concepts associated with metric thinking by students, and in a specific way, an improvement in the assessment of the measure and the ability to adequately select units of measurement, patterns and instruments of measurement. In addition, it is expected to show an improvement in the ability of students to apply metric thinking in problem resolution .
