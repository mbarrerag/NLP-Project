# Experimental characterization of atmospheric electric discharge parameters from electromagnetic field measurements and high-speed video recording in Manizales - Caldas.

The continuity in the characterization of the activity of rays and its parameters in Colombia is very important for the design of electrical systems and systems of protection against rays, and for the understanding of the phenomenon in tropical regions.For this, it is necessary to validate the information coming from the positioning systems installed in the country.The having adequate information directly affects the decision-making involved in the process of maintenance and expansion of the electrical infrastructure of the country, and in the protection of human and animal lives.The only way that exists to verify the performance of a system of location of discharges is through the systematic acquisition of redundant information based on the simultaneous measurement of electromagnetic fields and on the recording of videos or images in small geographic regions.It is so important that this aspect that if not having redundant measurements, it would be impossible to determine whether the value of the parameters of the discharges in a region or the variation of these is actually due to their natural behavior or to errors introduced in the results by a poor performance of the network of location of rays.The results obtained so far in the Colombian case have been based on the information of the systems of location with performances established from the theoretical criteria and only verified for the specific situations. This will allow to expand knowledge about the localization systems in tropical mountainous areas from the acquisition of electromagnetic field signals and simultaneous recording of videos. Given the experience collected in previous projects, it is expected that the quantity and type of samples acquired exceed the previous ones, leading the project to obtain more relevant results in the understanding of the lightning phenomenon in the country
