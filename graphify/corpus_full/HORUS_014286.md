# Allocation of revolving credit quota applied to new clients of a Colombian fintech.

The allocation of credit quotas is one of the major problems facing the financial industry in Colombia today. This thesis proposes a new model of allocation of credit quotas through the extraction of relevant financial information from historical records of credit applications and machine learning strategies. In particular, it proposes to estimate directly the credit quota segment from financial, sociodemographic and credit risk information of clients, in contrast to previous models that address the problem in two steps: 1) estimating the probability of non-compliance, and then 2) the credit quota with optimization. The information to adjust the learning models is extracted from historical credit approvals made by experts. The results suggest that the proposed model is more than the assigned performance of linear models of the art state and presents low levels of impact. The model was evaluated in credit data of a Colombian financial entity in the task of allocating quotas in microcredit.
