# Power conversion systems for increasing the efficiency in DC microgrids based on renewable sources

This thesis reports the contributions obtained during the development of the Doctorate studies in Automatic Engineering of the National University of Colombia. The thesis focuses on power converters for the processing of energy in microgrids of continuous current. The document contains six chapters; each chapter begins with a brief introduction of the specific topics; then, the methodology focuses on the analysis, design, modeling and/or control of the system. The chapters also present the validations of the proposed methodology. Finally, each chapter presents the conclusions on its topic. To validate the contributions of this thesis, specialized software and experimental validations are used to corroborate the design process of the power converters and their controllers. The DC microgrids formed by photovoltaic systems and batteries are the object of study of this thesis; therefore, the optimization of the energy flow in these microgrids of continuous current is allowed in these microgrids, the main area of research that is addressed by the design, modeling and control of the power converters that can be used with the photovoltaic panels or with the batteries as charge/ dischargers.
