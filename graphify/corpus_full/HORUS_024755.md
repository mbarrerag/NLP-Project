# Virulence, in vivo immune response and transcription of Mycobacterium tuberculosis genotype Beijing circulating in Colombia.

Tuberculosis (TB) remains a serious global public health problem. In Colombia, the most prevalent lineage is Euro-American. The increase in circulation of the Beijing genotype in the national territory, which is associated with drug resistance and hypervirulence causing the death of patients, is a research reason. The variant Beijing-Like SIT190 is the most prevalent in Colombia, followed by Beijing-Classic SIT1. Pathogenic and modulatory mechanisms are unknown. The present work compared the behavior of strains 323 (Beijing-Like) and 391 (Beijing-Clasic), in a model of pulmonary TB in Balb/c mice. The course of the disease was different for each group of animals, those infected with Beijing-Like had a mortality of 100% per day post-infection (PI), with high bacillary loads and massive pneumonia, while the total of those infected with Beijing-Clasic died towards the 60 PI day by pneumonia and necrosis
