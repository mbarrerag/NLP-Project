# Evaluation and adaptation of conventional overcurrent electrical protection schemes in distributed generation distribution networks.

This work aims to contribute in the search for answers to two questions about the inclusion of the distributed generation GD in the distribution systems used in Colombia: What is the impact it has on the performance of the traditional electrical protection schemes of distribution networks? and How can traditional electrical protection schemes of distribution networks be adapted to meet the new requirements? For this, in the 13 node test system modified to the voltage level 13,2 kV used in Colombia, with the electrical protections acting in a coordinated (selective) way, the effects of the penetration of GD are assessed, finding the increase of short-circuit current, the loss of selectivity between the protection of the circuit header and the protection fuses of the derived branches for GD of the synchronous generator type and the variation in the recirculation intervals. Based on the detected effects, adaptations such as change of equipment and elements, type of ground connection of the GDs and implementation of additional protection functions at the point of connection of the GDs and at the head of the circuit are proposed
