# The Cuba Quarter of the city of Pereira, a historic and urban landmark of the 1960s

This monograph focuses on the process of urbanization and consolidation of the Barrio Cuba in the city of Pereira, starting from the fact of its disordered population as a consequence of the internal migration that had been fleeing from the violence and urban war and was looking in the pereiran lands for a better place to live. In this sense the research aims to answer the following research question: How did the Barrio Cuba originate and build, during the accelerated urbanization that took place in the city of Pereira in the 60s? For this purpose, the reconstruction of the historical process of the Cuba neighborhood in the 60s was proposed as a goal, highlighting the importance of its origin in the city of Pereira, identifying the urban actors, the construction process and the development of the neighborhood, characterizing the type of infrastructure that developed. To achieve these purposes, a methodological route of qualitative approach was proposed, under the figure of the case study with a descriptive and exploratory scope and of documentary and hermeneutic type.
