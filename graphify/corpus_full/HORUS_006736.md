# Free access virtual applications for Mendeliana genetics teaching in basic education

The new technologies, today make a fundamental part of basic education in the teaching and learning of students in the areas of the exact and natural sciences. A didactic unit is designed that motivates the use or virtual platforms of free access, which were related to a humanistic model for the teaching of the Mendeliana genetics. The properly planned activities included games, workshops, interactive presentations, participation in blog, among others, dynamically and attractively for the student. The above, framed in a virtual classroom through the platform of Genial.ly, with videos complementary to the academic activities developed in a face-to-face. Students actively participated in the virtual classroom, of the different platforms for teaching and learning Mendeliana genetics, generating greater motivation for the concept of study .
