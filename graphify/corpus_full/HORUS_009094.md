# Bioprospecting in Colombia: antimicrobial activity of Eichhornia crassipes and Lemna gibba, as a model of analysis

The current development in search of new drugs that mitigate the high resistance of microorganisms to current medicines in the market, has led to the testing and use of natural metabolites as an alternative to this problem and among the species currently most investigated are plants; the use and conservation of natural species has undoubtedly had greater relevance in recent decades, highlighting the geopolitical, economic and social need to establish parameters for the management of species, genetic and biological resources of origin, the objective of this study is to analyze from bioprospecting, the use of buchote plants of water (Eichhornia crassipes) and water lens (Lemna gibba) in the search for antimicrobial substances. The methodology included the preparation of plant material, the obtaining of alcoholic extracts, the anti-microbial tests were carried out by the technique diffusion in disc and micro dilution to find the minimal concentration of inhibitive (MIC), additionally a theoretical exercise of bioprospection was carried out that includes the preparation of the plant material, the obtaining of the necessary alcoholic extracts, the analysis of application and of market and the tests and transfer of knowledge and technology can be demonstrated
