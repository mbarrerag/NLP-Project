# Frank Molano Camargo. History of garbage in Bogotá. Scobits, bacteria and dumps (1950-2003). Bogotá: Universidad de los Andes / Francisco José de Caldas District University, 2023. 279 pages.

History is built from traces, indications, traces that societies and individuals leave, consciously or unconsciously, some more lasting and recognized than others. Frank Molano’s book reflects on a type of footprints that has been incorporated into urban historiographical reflection of recent decades, but on which there is still much to say: the so-called garbage or waste. Being the residue of what a society produces, garbage has been seen in a residual way in historiography
