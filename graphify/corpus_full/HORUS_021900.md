# Analysis of the regime of liability of corporate administrators in situations of conflict of interest: A proposal to regulate business justice rule in Colombia.

This analysis deals with the liability regime of social administrators in Colombia, based on the study of the legal nature of conflicts of interest that arise during the execution of their actions and decisions in the light of the observance of the social interest. For this purpose, the first part of the study will introduce the context of the problem inherent in the system of responsibility of the social administrator, based on the treatment of conflicts of interest; in the second part, the notion of the rule of corporate discretion (business judgment rule) will be addressed in depth as a possible response to the problem initially raised, developed within Anglo-Saxon law and incorporated in Colombian corporate jurisprudence; finally, in the third and last part, the preceding analysis will be decanted in a proposal to reform the regime of corporate administrator responsibility, based on the corporate jurisprudence sub-rules present in Colombian law and in Anglo-Saxon law on the subject
