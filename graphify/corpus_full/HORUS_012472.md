# TERRITORIAL COLONIALITY AND CONFLICTIVITY: GLOBAL-LOCAL AMOUNTS IN ANDINA AmazONIA (COLOMBIA, ECUADOR, PERU AND BOLIVIA)

The Andean Amazon, and in general the whole Amazon, suffers an intense process of territorial coloniality of devastating dimensions for its territories and populations, and of significant consequences for the whole region, the planet and humanity. Never, in the history of humanity, in a period of time so short, has been devastated as much jungle as in the last two decades. The Andean Amazonia suffers today a 1492 recharged, since the ability of agency of the State and of the capital, to finally conquer it, is much more effective and covers a geographical magnitude far superior to that of the colonial period, to finally integrate these riches to the territorial disorder of life, produced by the modern-colonial world system, as we will see. What happened in the Andean Amazon is not new, but the result of a cumulative layer of coloniality and territorial conflictivity, which today have its maximum expression. This coloniality and conflictity advances from a great multi-scale articulation of processes that must be seen together to know the magnitude of what happens, its deep causes and devastating impacts.
