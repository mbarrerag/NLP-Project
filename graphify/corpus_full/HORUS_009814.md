# LEAD OF AGROPECUARY USES FROM SOUND TO MAYOR SCALE 1:25.00

This document was prepared with the aim of providing a guide for establishing and structuring at the national level the agricultural uses of the soil. It is important to note that the CORINE Land Cover Methodology is the official methodology at the national level for the development of land coverage maps.
