# Corporate governance factors contributing to the management of Mutual Investment Funds in Colombia

The Mutual Investment Funds (IMF), created in the 1960s, emerged as a mechanism to encourage investment in the public market of securities by workers, preserving the purchasing power of their wage savings. Despite their relevance, this figure has experienced a decrease. For its part, the Corporate Government (GC) understood as the set of structures and practices that guide and control organizations, is fundamental to ensure transparency, security and efficiency in management. Therefore, this work is aimed at analyzing the factors of the GC that can contribute to the management of the IMF, for which, first, the contributions of the development of the GC in organizational management are explored, starting from the review of academic literature and the progress of some supervisory entities. Secondly, it is carried out a financial and legal characterization of the IMF based on the current regulations and the financial data of the sector. In addition, semi-structured interviews were carried out with actors related to the sector. Consequently, it is concluded that the factors that contribute to the management of the IMF are those that are directed to regulate informal practices, that are advanced and generated by the financial data. organizational management. This paper seeks to analyse the key Corporate Governance factors that can enhance the management of MIFs. First, it examines the contributions of Corporate Governance developments to organizational management, drawing on a review of academic literature and the advances made by regulatory bodies. Second, it provides a financial and legal characterisation of MIFs based on current regulations and financial data from the sector. Additionally, semi-structured reviews were conducted with industry stakeholders to gain insight.
