# Interuniversity Seed for Policy and Digital Training

This project seeks to create an interuniversity Seed for policy and digital formation as part of the alliance between the Center for Thought for Political Education (CEP) of the National University of Colombia and the Center for Internet and Society of the University of Rosario - ISUR. This alliance, in particular through the Interuniversity Seed, will strengthen training processes of citizen political education from university areas and will allow the production of Open Educational Resources (REA) in a collaborative way on specific issues emerging from collaborative research. To this end, a cycle of four exploratory workshops will be carried out with agile methodologies of prototyping for the identification of the problems that will be addressed in the seedbed. The present project includes in its execution the design and implementation of a training cycle in Citizen Laboratories of Political and Digital Education for the members of the Semillero.
