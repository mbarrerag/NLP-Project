# THE TRIBUTATION LATER IN ITS CALCULATION. A SOCIO-AMBIENT ANALYSIS OF THE TRIBUTE BENEFITS TO THE CARBON MINERITY

The coal mining sector, an important link in the Colombian economy that enjoys extensive incentives in tax payments, is one of the main drivers of environmental degradation in the country. Thus, this article contrasts orthodox theoretical approaches from the view of Pigou with the ecological economy of Georgescu-Roegen and uses as a research strategy the case study proposed by Robert Yin to analyze the coal mining sector in the municipality of Cucunubá (Cundinamarca) and its tax benefits. As a result of the research, a reprimization of the economy is identified, representing for the community an economic and environmental risk, and for its shareholders an effective tax rate lower than the nominal rate, as well as social, economic, legal and environmental aspects that make visible the need for a restructuring of the benefits granted
