# School of Community Justice

The School of Community Justice of the National University of Colombia (EJCUN) as a research group carries out research, teaching and extension work aimed at strengthening community from the processes of social transformation and of self-management and peaceful conflict. It is composed of professors, graduates, interns and students of Law, Political Sciences and Human Sciences, as well as leaders and social actors. It is located as an academic entity of theoretical, practical and pedagogical production that seeks to generate knowledge from the exchange of diverse knowledge, the empowerment, peaceful management and the right to access to Justice, this as a response to the need for democratization of practices of administration of justice and law in Colombia. The EJCUN has carried out a wide-ranging work in various territories and communities at the national level, based on social pedagogy in issues such as reconciliation in equity, citizenship construction, management of community justice operators and social mediation, among others. This exercise has led to a continuous dialogue of the academy with many and varied voices of Colombian society, without the latter being simply object of study, but on the contrary has been relevant and legitimate actors in the field of community justice, social mediation, has been generated as a consequence
