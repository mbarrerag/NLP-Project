# OUR TIME. SUPERPOSITIONS BETWEEN PHYSICAL AND VIRTUAL SPACE

Being installed in contemporaryness is to inhabit the intertwining between the physical and virtual worlds, a threshold that defines today the social interactions. Environments like the Internet and lately the social networks, which for a couple of decades have enabled a virtual act, have initiated a new definition and affectation of the spaces of life by cohabiting and intermixing these two ways of inhabiting; are social networks a new form of urbanization, the architecture of how we live together? (Colomina, 2018, p. 19), question on which it is intended to reflect below for considering as an even invaluable phenomenon on the spatial projection mechanisms with which architecture can and must operate from now on, both in residential intimacy scenarios and in the scenarios of citizen collectivity
