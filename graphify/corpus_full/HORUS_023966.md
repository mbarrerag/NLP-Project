# METHODOLOGICAL TRENDS FOR THE SOCIAL INCLUSION OF TECHNOLOGIES APPROPRIATE IN RURAL AREAS

The objective of this article is to study the methodological trends at the global level of appropriate technologies (OT) developed in rural areas with social inclusion. A systematic review is carried out in scientific databases. The main methodologies (qualitative, quantitative and mixed), tools and approaches in rural processes with TA are identified. The main factors (social, technical, economic, environmental, institutional and cultural) and study variables in rural TA processes are also identified. The continents with the highest number of experiences in rural TAs are Africa and Asia. The energy sector associates 56.0% of the experiences detected
