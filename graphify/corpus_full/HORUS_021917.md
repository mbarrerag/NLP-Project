# Identification of factors to be improved to increase recommendation, NPS analysis for neutral customers from natural language processing.

This paper presents a methodology for identifying factors to improve to increase the intention of recommendation of the clients of a financial entity. 7,000 verbatims of the clients classified as neutrals were used in the survey net promoter score, which correspond to the information of the years 2019, 2020 and 2021. Processing of this information was carried out to obtain a structured corpus that represents the content of all verbatims. We applied analysis of feelings with the aim of analyzing the feelings underlying the verbatims of the clients, which allowed to conclude that in each of the years analyzed more than 80% of the clients had a negative feeling associated with their verbatim. Different algorithms were trained with the intention of finding the most relevant topics for the clients, the models trained were, latent allocation of dirichlet, latent semantic indexation and factoring of non-negative matrix, the models were evaluated using the approach of maximization of coherence, combined with human judgment to validate the topics in the context of the business. Among the models trained is the result of the maximum delay of dilettric, reaching the service and the analysis of the results
