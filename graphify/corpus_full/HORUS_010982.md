# MDMA as an agent of change: a theoretical review and proposal for phenomenological research on the conditions under which changes in experience lead to changes in character

We are interested in knowing under what conditions the temporary changes in experience lead to lasting changes in character. Traditionally, the definition and evaluation of personality have been left in the hands of psychology, but for these tasks this discipline has left aside the perspective of the subject of research or the nature of experience. We resort to phenomenology in search of elements that allow us to construct the definitions and the methods necessary to solve our question. We analyze the case of the use of MOTAPM to discover how these transient changes can lead to lasting changes. Finally, we formulate a proposal of research based phenomenally to a more complex exploration of our original question. .
