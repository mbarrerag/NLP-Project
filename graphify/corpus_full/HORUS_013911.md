# _x0093_Archifep_x0094_. Preservation, management and analysis of a FARC-EP file. Phase 1

This project seeks to preserve a digitized archive of the FARC-EP guerrillas to which we have had access and organize and analyze a selection of text, image, audio and multimedia documents of the same collection. Through the diagnosis of digital files and the historical characterization of information, a repository model will be designed and put to service, through a prototype of a web page that allows to explore the material. Through an interdisciplinary team will seek to ensure the preservation of a file that is at risk and that, moreover, is relevant for the analysis of primary sources of the FARC-EP. It opens new perspectives in the understanding of the Colombian armed conflict. This repository will allow to make historical analysis of the conflict, in an environment of digital humanities that serves as a platform for the public use of history in Colombia. It will create a research seedbed of peace building focused on the understanding of an armed actor, for the destigmatization of the figure of _x0093_enemigo_x0094_ in the Colombian academic field.
