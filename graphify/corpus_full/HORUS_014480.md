# Preparation of a virtual learning object for the development of mathematical logical thinking through chess in students of the Pio XII Educational Institution in Neira (Caldas).

The author proposes the construction of a virtual learning object that allows the teaching and learning of logical thinking – mathematical through the game of chess with the secondary students of the Pio XII Educational Institution of Neira (Caldas). Thus, the analysis of the shortcomings that students have in relation to the development of logical thinking processes is proposed, while being motivated by the learning of chess game. Mathematical logical thinking questionnaires are elaborated as part of the virtual learning object, in order to measure the development of this ability before, during and after implementation, using chess as an instrument. Although the proposal does not contemplate an in-depth intervention, some tests were carried out with students, which allowed the author to find improvements in the performance of students once the virtual learning object was applied. Finally, it is concluded about the relevance of chess and the implementation of virtual learning objects in the development of mathematical logical thinking, as it manages to generate a greater motivation in students by the area of mathematics
