# EFFECT OF LIGHT AND SOUND ON THE DEVELOPMENT AND COMPORTATION OF SEDA GUSAN (BOMBYX MORI)

The silkworm (Bombyx mori), is considered as the only domesticated insect and with standardized production systems. Although the synthience in vertebrates is accepted, there is still little evidence on the systems of perception in invertebrates. It is known that the light and sound are factors that condition the development, behavior and wellbeing in insects. Analyze the effect of photoperiod and sound, on the mortality and development of Bombyx mori. An experimental model was performed, with two replicates, for the variables change of bed (cc), time of moult and larval death in groups of 40 individuals (experimental units) to light and sound effects under equal conditions of management: ad libitum diet, (morera leaves - Morus alba) bed (base of sulphite), temperature (2325 °C) and humidity (60- 75%).
