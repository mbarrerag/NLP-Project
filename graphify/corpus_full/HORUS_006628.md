# Design of the lighting scheme of rooms and common areas of hotel, with circadian focus and prevention of JET LAG

The aim of this project is to compare the lighting design using the guide UL 24480 and WELL 2.0 in a technical and economic way, focusing on different specific areas of the hotel: rooms with their respective bathrooms, a conference room, a restaurant-bar and a corridor. The efficiency of the Circadian lighting is evaluated according to these different guides. Using the software DIALux, the illuminance data required by both the design guides and by the ANSI/IES RP-9-23 regulations are evaluated. For a design that is planned to be located in Colombia, it is relevant to stress the importance of the distributions of spectral potential for this type of designs and to evaluate the various areas individually for a correct lighting design. The results are presented by tables and graphs to corroborate the compliance of the guides and the regulations. The dimerization of each of the luminaires, and finally, a revision of the Twinmotion software is offered for a more commercial representation of the final product .
