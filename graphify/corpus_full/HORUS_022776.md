# AGENCY OF AUTHORITY IN WOMEN TO PREVENCE CARDIOVACULARY DISEASES, IN A BOGOTA UNIVERSITY

This study presents an analysis of the capacity of women to assume their self-care integrally and reduce the risk of presenting a cardiovascular disease. To evaluate the capacity of self-care agency in women for the prevention of cardiovascular disease, at the Manuela Beltrán University. Qualitative-quantitative study, cross-sectional. 41 women with non-probabilistic and non-random sampling participated as there was ease of sample access. The data collection tool was the Self-Care Agency Assessment Scale (ASA), to measure the individual's ability to perform the self-care agency's productive operations. The participating population showed prevalence in regular self-care agency (63 to 81 points), in accordance with the results obtained in the scale to evaluate the self-care agency. The health status of a person depends, to a large extent, on the care provided to itself.
