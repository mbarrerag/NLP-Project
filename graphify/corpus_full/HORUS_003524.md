# ARCHITECTURE OF INTERIOR AND EFFIROUS ARCHITECTURE

The architectural design of the food area of the Oviedo Shopping Centre is characterized by a sequence of patios in the middle of the commercial corridors, as well as balconies that surround them on the upper floors.The interior design project incorporates the nature elements of these courtyards, such as the central carob and the abundant surrounding vegetation, to create a fluid environment that takes advantage of natural lighting and ventilation
