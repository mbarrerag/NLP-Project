# PROPERTY IN TURN-GRADULED PBW EXTENSIONS

In this article we study the property of algebras that are twisted PBW extensions. We show that if R = p0Rp is a N-Graduated Noetherian algebra and A = (R)x1,...,xnIs a quasi-conmutatively twisted PBW extension graduated from R, then A satisfies if and only if R satisfies. We also give sufficient conditions for a twisted PBW extension to satisfy R
