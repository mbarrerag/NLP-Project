# THE PARADIGN SHIFT TOWARDS AN ADAPTATIVE ARCHITECTURE

THE PARADIGM SHIFT TOWARDS ADAPTIVE ARCHITECTURE Earth's population has increased 6 times during the last century: from 1.200 million people it has increased to 7.000 million. Never before the humanity had experienced such a significant and fast demographic growth, since 75% of the population live in cities. The disproportionate population's growth has triggered a predatory consumption of the planet's natural resources, an unrestrained demand and supply of products and services, increasing pollution and even the globe's temperature. Building's constructions represent 41% of the world's energy consumption (industry represents 31%, and transportation represents 28%), which means that is the human production with the highest energy use. Natural environment is changing as well. However, we continue occupying the earth applying the same criteria from hundreds of years ago: we build in a slow, rigid and heavy way, in an era that is clearly claiming more dynamism, adaptability and responsibility towards the environment. What is there to do about it? The current changing conditions of the world and societies make us to think about other constructive systems that may be more in accordance with time changes. Nowadays, it is necessary to have other constructive methods that can be flexible, light, and easy to transport and install, that use Eco-Friendly energy and materials. More than thinking about buildings as finished elements, we must think of constructions as organisms, systems that can adapt over time to social conditions and the environment. These insights make part of an investigation that this author has developed during several years, which are consequent from a couple of patents invented in Spain and Colombia, just like a light and adaptable system of constructions that have been introduced in his own company
