# EFFECT OF THE LOW POTENCE LASER IN THE OSTEOBLASTIC PROLIFERATION AND DIFFERENCE OF MESENCHIMAL CELLS.

BACK TO THESE: The photobiomodulation with low-power lasers (LBP) generates anti-inflammatory, analgesic and biostimulant effects, which increases the regener potential of tissues such as bone. This effect is mediated by the modulation of bone metabolic processes such as osteoclastogenesis. However, little is known about its effect on proliferation and osteoblastic differentiation, being several of the studies contradictory due to the great variability in the irradiation parameters used. Additionally, some studies attribute their photobiomodular effects during the absorption of light by photoreceptors internal electron transport chain, which induces mitochonddrial activation, generation of reactive oxygen species (ROS) and consequently, increase in the production of ATP. However, the specific role of the ROS in the photobiomodulative effects, in the photobiomodular absorption by photobiomodulation induced in osteoblasts, is still controversial. Morphological changes such as larger cytoplasms with abundant prolongations were evident in the irradiated cells at 940nm during the three times evaluated; compared to the control group.
