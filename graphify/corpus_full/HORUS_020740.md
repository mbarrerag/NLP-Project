# Towards a Poetic of Electric Light in Bogotá (1886-1930): Device and metaphor in the construction of a modern city

The topic of this research is the study of the role played by electric light as a physical and symbolic agent of modernity in Bogotá between 1886 and 1930, period known in the political history of Colombia as “conservative hegemony”. The main objective of this work is to analyze the function of metaphors of light in the construction of an imaginary of the Bogotan modernity during the period in question, with the aim of making visible the discrepancy between the processes of material modernization of the city and the spiritual conditions for the emergence of a modern culture proper in the capital. The document is divided into four chapters, composed by several sections in which the most outstanding scenes or moments of significance are collected, both of the metaphor of light in the general framework of the European Western tradition, and of the effects that brought with it the introduction and slow expansion of the electric light in the case of the Bogotan society. The conclusions of this research also suggest that the study of the metaphors of light in general constitutes a methodological strategy of research that allows to articulate, both in time and in space, the technological developments of the urban modernization with the processes of cultural change in the plane of ideas, practices and the practicalities, text is divided into four chapters, composed in turns of different sections, in which the most significant scenes or moments are collected, both for the fuction of “light-metaphors” in the general framework of the European Western tradition, and for the effects raised about the introduction and slow expansion of electric light in the case of Bogotá society.
