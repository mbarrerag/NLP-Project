# Effect of restricted suckling on growth characteristics in Colombian hair sheep

To evaluate the effect of restricted breastfeeding (RA) on growth parameters (adjusted weight (AP) and daily weight gain (GPD), body condition (CC) and FAMACHA® score (FA) during lactation and post-weaning in Colombian-haired sheep (OPC). 76 OPC with offspring, were assigned to one of two treatments. Treatment 1 (T1), lambs with continuous suckling (CA): 24 hours with the mother (n= 38). Treatment 2 (T2), restricted breastfeeding (RA), 10 hours (n=38). Measurements were made every 30 days from birth to 210 days with weaning at 90 days. BP, GPD, CC and AF were evaluated, the data were analyzed with a linear model of fixed effects and repeated measures and Tukey'Kramer test. BP before 60 days did not vary significantly between T1 and T2. After weaning and until the end of the experiment, the APS were significantly (p<0.001) higher in T2. The average pre-weaning GPD did not vary between T1 and T2 (p=0.053). The post-weaning GPD varied significantly (p<0.001) between T1 and T2 (82.38±17.4 and 123.95±20.6 g/day respectively). After weaning the CC was higher (p<0.001) in the lambs in T2. No differences were found in AF during lactation. After weaning, T2 presented a better rating (p<0.001). T1 and T2, showed the same performance at weaning. However, the differences from weaning to seven months of age were in favor of the lambs subjected to T2
