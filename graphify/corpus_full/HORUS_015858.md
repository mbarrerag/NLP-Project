# MORPHO-GEOMÉTERIC ASSESSMENT OF CORPORAL PROFILE IN THE ARABUCAN HORSE OF COLOMBIA

The geometric morphometry (MG), which gradually gains field in the zooethnological studies, allows the evaluation of the variables pure form.. When quantifying this parameter, the technique allows mathematically to analyze contours that, in the case of this study, can be applied to the profile, trait difficult to objective and much less quantified by other methods. The traditional morphometry uses the linear distances between two anatomical points, which are translated into figures in the paper, losing their relation to the biological form. The objective of the research was to study the cervical, dorso-lumbar and group profiles in the horse of the Arauca area (NE Colombia), by methods of MG, comparing the values between layers and zones.
