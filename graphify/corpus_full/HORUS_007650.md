# Strengthening research in Bioconservation and Replacement of Chemical Additives in Carnic and Aquaculture Products

This proposal aims to expand the technological capabilities to strengthen the research activities of the group of Science and Technology of Carnic and Aquaculture Products, linked to the Institute of Food Science and Technology of the Bogotá headquarters. Currently, this group supports the research activities of the postgraduate in Food Science and Technology, affiliated to the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences of the same headquarters, where research is carried out to strengthen the meat and aquaculture products sector in the lines of research Bioconservation of food, Inclusion of natural dyes as a supplement in the production of food of animal origin and Science and Technology of Carnic and Aquaculture Products that directly support the three lines of research of the postgraduate in Food Science and Technology (Food Quality Line, Food Processing Line, and Product Design and Development Line)
