# CLINKS WITH THE FUTURE INITIATIVES OF ACADEMIA FOR GOVERNMENT 2022 - 2026

The National University of Colombia promoted the Convergence for Colombia initiative: ideas from the university for constructive dialogues, within the framework of the decision of several public and private universities that pronounced themselves in the University Letter to the Colombian nation, on the occasion of the social protest initiated on April 28, 2021. To this end, several thematic discussion and proposal building tables were formed. Table 2 took an interest in working on the topic "The right to health and the health system and committed itself to contributing its conclusions to the process of articulation of university initiatives led by Ean University with the title "Pilas with the Future". The referent for the debate focused on the definition and criteria for guaranteeing the fundamental right to health, established by Statutory Law 1751 of 2015, and its implications for a reform of the health system and all those public systems and policies related to a broad sense of health, beyond the care of the disease. The synthesis of these discussions and conclusions include a diagnosis of the main problems, the main contents of the debate and the conclusions of this debate and ends with recommendations, highlighting certain differences
