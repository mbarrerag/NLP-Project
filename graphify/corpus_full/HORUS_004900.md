# LIBERALISSM AND RIGHTS: BETWEEN CIVILIZATORIAL CRISIS AND RADICAL CRITICS

Liberal political systems are in crisis. Certain positions attribute this crisis of liberal systems to the tearing apart of the mechanisms of integration and social mobility based on the search for equal opportunities, the exaltation of the ethics of work and the legitimization of hierarchies resulting from meritocratic processes
