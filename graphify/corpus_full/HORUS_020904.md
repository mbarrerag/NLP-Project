# Ethnopragmatic approach to the study of the greetings of the indigenous iku of the Arhuaco indigenous reserve of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta

This work aims to contribute to the theoretical and practical discussion of greetings from the description and analysis of the ethnopragmatic perspective of these community acts present in the community ik=649; located in the region of Moro't=649;rwa of the Sierra Nevada of Santa Marta. This is achieved through an ethnographic work consisting of interviews, participant and non-participating observation and field diary that allows obtaining the description of the greetings immersed in communicative interaction, of the rules that govern this interaction, as well as of the socio-cultural factors associated with this practice in the Arhuaca population. This research is an approach to the understanding of greetings within the indigenous community ik=649; of the Sierra Nevada of Santa Marta and, in relation, a contribution to the definition and delimitation of greetings as a subject of study
