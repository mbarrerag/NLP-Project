# Opportunities and challenges of phonoaudiology to move towards inclusive education in Colombia

Several studies have shown that, in the school environment, phonoaudiology has a significant impact on the detection, promotion and approach of the processes inherent in human communication as a basis for learning, participation and inclusion. Despite this, recent normative provisions in Colombia do not clearly define the work of this professional in this scenario. Taking into account the above, the objective of this article was to present a critical analysis of the role of the phonoaudiologist in the context of inclusive education in Colombia. In the first part of this article, the problems in the name of phonoaudiology and the relevance of his functions are analyzed. Then, the role of this professional in the educational context is addressed. In the last part, some actions are proposed to assert the role of phonoaudiologists in inclusion and equity in education in terms of removing barriers from the environment and strengthening of capacities according to the particularities of students
