# Analysis of the connection between astronomy and the curriculum in basic and secondary education within the framework of the Office of Astronomy for Education in Colombia (OAE)

The aim of this study is to analyse the teaching and learning processes and initiatives in astronomy and space sciences, and their relationship with the natural sciences curriculum. To this end, a survey was conducted aimed at a sample of 250 teachers of basic and middle education, associated with the Office of Astronomy for Education in Colombia (OAE). The results obtained were subjected to an analysis using unsupervised learning techniques, in particular the method of clustering by kmodes. This method is justified by its ability to examine and classify nominal, ordinal and categorical variables, which allows identifying common patterns and characteristics among the teachers surveyed. With the development of this work, it is sought to identify methodological aspects of teaching astronomy and space sciences in the Colombian educational context, as well as to recognize possible areas of improvement in the integration of these disciplines into the natural sciences curriculum. The results obtained can be of great relevance to the OEA and other educational institutions, by providing valuable information to strengthen teaching and promote more enriching and effective pedagogical practices in the field of astronomy. . fy methodological aspects of teaching astronomy and space sciences in the Colombian educational context, as well as to collect possible areas for improvement in the integration of these disciplines into the natural sciences curriculum.
