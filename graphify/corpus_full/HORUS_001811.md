# Budget planning of the operating expenses of the chambers of commerce in Colombia. Case of the Chamber of Commerce of Bogotá

This document analysed the elements that shape the budget planning of public resources with respect to operating costs in the Chamber of Commerce of Bogotá, with the aim of characterizing an efficient process from the theoretical approach of the forms of budgeting that have been developed in organizations with similar characteristics. The methodology used in this research is of qualitative approach, based on a case study of the Chamber of Commerce of Bogotá, which combines the exhaustive review of literature with semi-structured interviews and documentary analysis. The main result is the identification of various opportunities for improvement in the budget processes, mainly in terms of the strategic alignment of the budget with organizational objectives, efficiency in the management of public resources and the implementation of technologies that allow for more accurate monitoring. In addition, it is concluded that the CBC could benefit from greater flexibility in its budgetary projection to adapt to changes in the economic and regulatory environment.
