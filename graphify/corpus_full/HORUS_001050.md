# Effect of inappropriate medication deprescription in older adults: a literature review and meta-analysis of controlled clinical trials

Multipathology in older people is a risk factor for polymedication, medication cascade and potentially inappropriate medication. Deprescription is proposed as a strategy to reduce pharmacological burden and inappropriate medication. To establish the effect of deprescription on health outcomes of older persons as reported by the literature. A systematic and meta-analysis was developed until June 2020, using the databases MEDLINE via PubMed, LILACS, SciELO and Epistemonikos, following the PRISMA methodology. Experimental and quasi-experimental studies were included, in English and Spanish. Evidence levels and risk of bias were evaluated for each individual study. Reduction of potentially inappropriate prescriptions, mortality, falls and number of hospitalizations were evaluated in meta-analysis. A total of 1524 records were identified by database were assessed. 24 articles were included in the descriptive analysis and 14 in the quantitative.
