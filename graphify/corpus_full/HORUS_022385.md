# Methodological and relational conditions that favour the process of formulating local public competitiveness policies.

Competitiveness is a concept associated with the performance of the market activities of companies and territories, and has been adopted as a public policy approach for development, although academically there are discussions on its conceptual clarity or practical utility. This paper presents some methodological parameters to promote the development of local public competitiveness policies (PPLC) since its formulation. The analysis is based on a process of theoretical reflection and analytical observation of the development of the PPLCs in an intermediate city in the Andean region of Colombia. As a result, three fundamental parameters of formulation of PPCL associated with its nature, scope and interaction among its actors are highlighted
