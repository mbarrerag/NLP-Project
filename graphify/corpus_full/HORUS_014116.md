The phytoplasplaslas (Acholeplaplasmatals: Mollicutes) are pathogenic bacteria of plant floem plants and hemolymph of their insects vectors. The phytoplasplas are associated with diseases of imbalance in the risks of plant growth such as witch broom, elongation of intern, dwarfism, phylody and virescence, which affects the architecture and physiology of plants. In the world, the impact generated by the transmission of phytoplaslas by means of their vectors is worrying and critical, due to the economic losses in agricultural crops and the decrease in ecosystemic services, particularly in native plants.
