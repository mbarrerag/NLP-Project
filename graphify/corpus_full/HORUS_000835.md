# Citizen Science and Digital Data for Trends Analysis and Impact Assessment of Proplosis as an Emerging Plague in foliage Crops

Prodiplosis longifila is a plague of high economic relevance that seriously affects crops such as tomato and asparagus. However, its impact on foliage crops has been little studied, despite its increasing importance. This study addresses this gap by implementing and validating tools of digital epidemiology (ED) and citizen science (CC), which allow a participatory and dynamic approach to monitoring this pest. A trend analysis was carried out based on scientific publications, internet searches and interactions in social networks, with the aim of identifying the topics addressed, knowledge gaps and emerging areas of interest. Additionally, the impact of Prodiplosis on foliage crops was evaluated, describing its indirect effects and management strategies adopted by farmers through digital channels. The results show that digital tools such as analysis of trends in social networks, the study of web data, WhatsApp groups and digital platforms managed by producers are effective in identifying the distribution, importance and control strategies of Prodiplosis. The methodologies of ED and CC also revealed critical gaps in the knowledge about biology, ecology and management of this pest in ornamental crops, and the digital analysis are effective in terms of high performance
