# Social vulnerability and psychological resilience in young adults and young adults involved in an artistic Community network

In Latin America, unequal access to socio-economic resources and educational or labour opportunities generates social inequality (Capriati, 2015). The deterioration of living conditions, the increase in unemployment and informality result in few social opportunities for young people and their families. In Bogotá- Colombia, less than half of the young people have access to higher education and their employment rate decreased between 2016-2017 by 2.3 percentage points, reaching for 2017 by 53.9% (Alcaldía Mayor de Bogotá, 2019). In this context, the Artistic and Cultural Collective Abya Yala (Community Organization) identifies during ten years of work in training processes in community art, problems related to poverty, common crime, intra-family violence, gender violence, addiction to psychoactive substances and few opportunities for transformation of its inhabitants. For these young people the opportunities to empower and exploit their cognitive, mental, emotional and relational skills can be reduced to their creative, productive and social interaction lives, which other young people with greater opportunities (Capriati 2015), (Alcaldía Mayor de Bogotá, 2019). These factors and the low-cost projects can participate in their creative, productive and social interaction. The five groups represented conditions, fields, activities, social relations, adaptive psychological resources, risk factors and trajectories that were known throughout their life stories.
