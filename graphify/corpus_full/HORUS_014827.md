# Victor Uribe Urán. Fatal loves. Conjugal homicides, right and punishment at the end of the colonial period in the Spanish Atlantic.

Victor Uribe Urán has the merit of focusing on the judicial dimension of crimes, norms, discourses and legal, religious and social practices, as well as on the doctrines associated with crimes framed in marital violence and legal justifications and types of punishment, which helps to overcome the merely discursive (linguistic) aspects of the files, traditional emphasis of the historiography of marital violence. In his work he reveals how ethnic relations were established in the three societies covered by the book and how subordinate criminal subjects, gender roles, tensions and social hierarchies, as well as cultural values and concepts of honour are constructed. This work is highly recommended for those who have been the historians of crime and law in the colonial period, as well as for those who make history of women, especially from the standpoint of female transgression
