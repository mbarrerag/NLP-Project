# SOCIAL INNOVATION AS A RECONSTRUCTION INSTRUMENT FOR SOCIAL TEJIDE: A LOOK FROM EDUCATION AND CITIZEN PARTICIPATION

Social innovation is considered as one of the practices that civil society carries out to transform its realities in a disruptive way in a given territory, constituting an aspect that allows strengthening processes that place it in the generation of territorial peace and consolidate the reconstruction of the social fabric in areas affected by the Colombian armed conflict. The search for new languages that strengthen the social aspect allows, moreover, to achieve new approaches and structure strategies that conform to the particular environment to which it is intended to apply, thus achieving greater impact in terms of its implementation and results. Through a reflective analysis and assisted by the voice of different authors, the importance of the reconstruction of the social fabric in our country is evidenced and it shows how it is possible to carry out this reconstruction through education and citizen participation
