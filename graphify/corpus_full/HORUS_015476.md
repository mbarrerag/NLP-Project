# HEMATOPOYESS CLONAL OF INDETERMINATE PONTIAL

The Clonal Hematopoiesis of Indeterminate Potential (HCPI) better known as CHIP is a cancerization field model that has attracted the attention of scientists in the last decade. Partly this interest stems from the idea that these mutated clones give rise to different types of blood cells with altered functions that predispose to the development of the two main causes of mortality in the world; cancer and cardiovascular diseases. Both are considered chronic diseases, increase with age, are within the high cost account. This is how most somatic mutations in HCPI occur as a signature of aging and that the three mutated genes most often participate in epigenetic regulation
