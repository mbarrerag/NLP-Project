# ANALYSIS OF DETERMINANTS AND RISK MANAGEMENT IN INTER-PARATURE CROWDFUNDING

The objective of this study was to analyze the trend in research on the determinants and risk management in crowdfunding of P2P loans, in order to expand knowledge to investors, entrepreneurs and policymakers on this disruptive financing. A qualitative approach was used with a description of publications focused on credit risk. A bibliometric analysis of scientific production was then carried out at the WOS and Scopus bases. The bibliographic analysis was carried out with VOSviewer and RStudio platforms (library Bibliometrix and Biblioshiny), in which four current thematic clusters of research focused on knowledge production were identified. Based on the descriptive analysis, a theoretical approach was made with the most relevant findings.
