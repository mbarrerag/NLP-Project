# Design of a conceptual model for follow-up of agricultural startups in Colombia.

A start-up is an emerging, technologically based company that seeks rapid and scalable growth through business models focused on innovation. In Colombia, entrepreneurship in the agrarian sector seeks to guide the creation of startups with innovative, sustainable and scalable business models that respond to the needs of rural communities and promote the economic, social and environmental development of the country. The limited academic literature that documents how these agricultural startups have managed to identify and overcome barriers to entry and stay in the market is a challenge for academia, where, by method, entrepreneurs can be accompanied to succeed in consolidating and growing in an ecosystem where there is a lack of articulation between governmental institutions, the private sector, non-governmental organizations and the communities themselves are aspects that limit access to resources. To recognize the particularities of the agricultural sector, the financing and the proper functioning of networks of support and support to the entrepreneur, for the consolidation and growth of startups is key to the country. Thus, this research raises the identification of an accompanying route that, based on the identification of barriers and facilitators specific to the agrarian sector and its entrepreneurs, allows the creation, consolidation and growth of startups
