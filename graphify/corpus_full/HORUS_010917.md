# A critical approach to the use of cost-effectiveness evaluation for Vaccines inclusion in Immunization Programs: the case of pneumococcal vaccines in Latin America.

Worldwide, vaccination is considered a very successful and cost-effective public health strategy to prevent a wide range of communicable diseases, especially in children. New vaccines are available, but at higher costs than those originally included in the Expanded Programme of Immunizations (PAI) basic. Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) is a region with a great commitment to the inclusion of new vaccines, supported mainly by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), however, the budgetary impact of universal immunization remains a challenge for national IPPs. This research considered the positive and regulatory dimensions of health decision-making, particularly in environments with limited resources such as the LAC countries, to explore the key rationalities considered for public health investment decisions.
