# Experience of moments experienced by families of patients who were hospitalized in intensive care unit

The process of having a family hospitalized in an intensive care unit (UCI) is perceived by the family as a transit of emotions and changes over that certain period of time, and described in different moments and contexts. The hospitalization and the recovery and rehabilitation process is slow, difficult and requires adaptation to new realities that have been studied little qualitatively. In this sense, the objectives of this study were to describe the experience of the different moments lived by the families, to describe the emotions through which the family of patients passes after discharge and to know the role assumed by the family. To carry out the research was used the qualitative methodology of grounded theory, constructivist current of Katy Charmaz. The data collection was carried out through semi-structured interviews and daily written by 14 participants, in two moments, to present the discharge of the ICU and three months after it, to have a relationship with the institution. als of these studies are to describe the experience of families in different stages of the process; to describe the emotions families go through after differs, and ultimately, to characterize the role of the family in the care of these patients. To conduct the research methodology used a qualitative method known as surrounded theory. It is a constructivist approach by Katy Charmaz. Data were obtained through semi-structured interviews and diary written by 14 subjects at two points in time: at dispute from the ICU and at 3 months after discharge.
