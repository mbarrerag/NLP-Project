# INFLUENCE OF THE MARINE ECOSYSTEMS IN THE COSTER INUNDATION OF THE ISLAND OF SAN ANDRES

The island of San Andrés is the most populated of the Colombian island territory, its geographical location exposes it to hurricanes (King,2021), producing material, service and life impacts.The island houses a high coverage of corals and marine pastures that provide the service of coastal protection against the waves, but these have degraded in the last decades product of climate change, extreme events and anthropic pressures (Roldan,2021). Thus, it is sought to know the influence of the health state of the coral ecosystems and marine pastures in the flood level during medium and medium-extreme events. Hydrodynamic models of the waves and run-up in the NE of the island of San Andrés by means of sequential coupling of the models SWAN 2D and XBeach 1D, calibrated by November 2019 and validated by March 2018 and November 2021. Then, the response of the coastal flood in 2 scenarios, means and means-extreme, for 3 states of the medium where the health generated by the ecosystems, related to their health status, optimal processes and deterioration is considered. The presence of infragravitatory waves requires other considerations and configurations within the SWAN model, especially inside the reef lagoon, being information not transferred to the XBeach model and that are relevant in the analysis of coastal flooding (Rijnsdorp,2021). Although the health relationship or structural complexity is direct with roughness and friction (Carlot,2023), the spatial variation of the coverings, roughness and friction factors is relevant given the complex morphology of the island and its dependence on the conditions of the swell (Navarro,2021) (Masashi,2023).
