# NECROPSY TECHNOLOGY IN BIG ANIMAL

The aim of this work is to develop a protocol of the technique of necropsy in large animals, considering the collection, packaging and sending of samples to complementary laboratories. Although necropsy is the post-mortem examination of an animal, the best specimen for the diagnosis of veterinary pathology, when available, is the living animal that is dying, that is, an animal in terminal state of the disease. The necropsy technique is accompanied by the correct description of macroscopic lesions, the collection, conservation and adequate packaging of samples for laboratory purposes. Together, necropsy and the timely referral of samples to the laboratory contribute to the definitive diagnosis of the cause of disease and/or death of the animal or the problem of the health of the herd. In conclusion, necropsy together with histopathological analysis and complementary laboratory tests are useful tools and necessary to establish the definitive diagnosis of disease or death of an animal or group of animals of production
