# Trophic aspects of five ictic species of a sewage ravine (Amazonas, Colombia)

To provide basic information on the life history of some of the many small fish found in the streams of the sewage forests in the Colombian Amazon near Leticia city, this paper describes the trophic aspects of five species of fish. Various fishing gears were used for the capture of the specimens, sampling was carried out in the afternoon and at night, with an average duration of five hours. We studied the stomach contents of four caracids: Ctenobrycon hauxwellianus (Cope 1870), Moenkhausia melogramma Eigenmann 1908, Tetragonopterus argengenteus (Cuvier 1816), Hemigrammus bellattii (Steindachner 1882) and Bujurquina cyclide cf. pilgrimagebunda (Eigenmann 1922). Most of the species were generalists in their food strategy, but M. melogramma had a great preference for aquatic invertebrates, and Bujurquina cf. pilgrimage ate ate aquatic invertebrates and fish
