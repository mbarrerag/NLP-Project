# Evaluation of the anticorrosive and microstructural properties of structured nano coatings of (Ti, Cr, Al, Si) N deposited by co-sputting technique

Corrosion is an electrochemical phenomenon responsible for premature deterioration of a large part of mechanical components, conducts a large number of investigations in this field in order to reduce the losses caused by this phenomenon. One of the areas in which it is investigated with great interest are the coatings deposited via PVD. Because these show an excellent mechanical resistance, besides exposing other properties of interest dependent on its chemical composition, there may be as many types of coatings as mixtures of elements occur, but only with the correct selection of elements and parameters of deposition is possible to obtain an optimal result in its characteristics. A coating of type nitride is that in which nitrogen is present, moreover, about 50% in its composition is this same.
