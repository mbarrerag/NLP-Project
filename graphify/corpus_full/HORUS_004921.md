# Study of the formation of L-Tyrosine-derived azacyclophans and their interaction with zinc

This study studied the synthesis of azacyclophanes derived from L-tyrosine using the synthesis strategy called “Synthesis assisted by hydrogen bonding”. The results show that the synthesis strategy allows for the acquisition of pentacyclic azacyclophans symmetrical by reactions of 2 components and asymmetrical by reactions of 3 components. This synthesis strategy allows for the acquisition of symmetric benzyl cyclophanes with substitutions on nitrogen by reactions of two components and asymmetrics by reaction of three components. It should be mentioned that sterical effects and acid-base interactions can influence the course of the reaction for obtaining the macrocyclic product or linear oligomers.
