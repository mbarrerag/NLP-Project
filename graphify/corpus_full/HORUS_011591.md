# CORPORATE GOVERNMENT AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT: A BIBLE ANALYSIS

In the last two decades, the social and environmental policies of public organizations and institutions have become an element of pressure of the multiple interest groups, a situation that leads to corporate governance and sustainable development being fundamental to maintaining optimal economic and social performance in the organizational field. The non-implementation of sustainable processes in companies can negatively impact the profitability of these and their reputation can be severely affected. In view of the above, the purpose of this research was to review the scientific contribution based on scientific mapping techniques on the topic corporate governance and sustainable development. To achieve this objective, a literature review and bibliometric analysis of the related documents in Scopus for the last two decades (2000-2020) was developed. This exploration allowed to show how corporate governance and sustainable development impact on the finances of the organizations, and how the dissemination of sustainability reports allows for creating trust links between the board and the different interest groups.
