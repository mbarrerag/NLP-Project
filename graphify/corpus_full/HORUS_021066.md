# Structure and implementation of a workshop pilot for the internal maintenance of laboratory equipment at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Medellín Headquarters

This proposal seeks to implement a strategy of maintenance of laboratory equipment in the Medellín Headquarters of the National University of Colombia. Currently, the Laboratory System of the Medellin Headquarters has 146 laboratories formally created and registered in the Hermes Information System, which provide services to 5 faculties: Architecture, Science, Agrarian Sciences, Human Sciences and Mines, according to the three missionary purposes, teaching, research and extension. These laboratories have associates about 10860 teams that require preventive and corrective maintenance in order to ensure their proper functioning and useful life time (Hermes Source). Due to financial limitations, a large part of these teams cannot be intervened without maintenance and leading to the generation of maintenance plans that cannot be implemented. As a strategy that allows to overcome this situation, from the Laboratory Management of the Medellin Headquarters it is proposed to carry out the structuring and implementation of an auxiliary task that can be performed by an internal maintenance workshop pilot, starting from programs in the Hermes information system, carrying out maintenance of small and medium format teams, seeking to increase the coverage and scope of the maintenance of each place of the students.
