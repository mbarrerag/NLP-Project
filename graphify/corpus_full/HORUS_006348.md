# Development of biological strategies for the management of rice tamo for biofertilisation purposes in the department of Tolima

The most used practice for the management of residues of rice cultivation is open field burning, which not only alters the soil ecosystem in all its components, but produces considerable amounts of greenhouse gases and significant losses of valuable nutrients for the establishment of new crop cycles.However, the progressive loss of organic matter in the soils of the tropics and the low rate of assimilation of fertilizers mainly nitrogen, undermine the sustainability of the crop. Under this context, the biodegradation of the rice size in situ turns out to be the most successful strategy from the point of view of ecological, economic and sustainability of the crop in the long term. However, its high content of the microbial co-incules with hydrolytic potential, as well as the constant fluctuations in the climatic conditions make this process less efficient and profitable in the field. Therefore, strategies were developed to optimize the biodegradation of the soil in the soil of Trichoderma commercial, and in situ, by means of production of the microbial co-incules of the microbial and biocontrol potential. The results also contribute to the sustainability of cultivation and minimize the ecological and environmental impact of traditional agronomic practices of fertilization and burning of open-fielded soil, giving greater competitiveness to rice growing plants in Colombia .
