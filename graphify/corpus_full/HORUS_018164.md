# CRITICITY MATTER TO PRIORIZ THE MANAGEMENT OF EQUIPMENT IN THE LABORATORY OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY MANUFACTURE PROCESS

The proposed strategic tool determines the criticality of the equipment: machines, instruments, systems and existing processes. The methodology and tool to prioritize the management of the equipment of the Manufacturing Process Laboratory is based on the principles, concepts, theories and guidelines of the integral management of assets with missionary functions that facilitate analysis for the allocation of human, technical and economic resources; the effectiveness of the functioning of the equipment; the reliability and security of the assets, in the three missionary axes: teaching, research and extension
