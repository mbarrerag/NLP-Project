# Budget impact analysis of Empagliflozin Vs Metformin, Glibenclamide, Sitagliptin and patients with type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Colombia

The objective of this grade work was to perform a analysis of the budgetary impact of the use of Empagliflozin versus Metformin, Glibenzlamide and Sitagliptin in patients with DM2 in Colombia. The complications of cardiovascular disease had a direct impact on the costs of treating DM2, this due to the additional cost paid for therapies that make a treatment based on a glycocentric and non-cardial metabolic approach.
