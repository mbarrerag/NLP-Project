# Assessment of the nutritional potential of tropical shrub species for feeding traspatium pigs.

The objective of the investigation was to evaluate the food potential of tropical shrub species in the diet of traspatio pigs in the canton Chone, Manabí, Ecuador, where the parishes Santa Rita, Boyacá, Ricaurte, Eloy Alfaro, San Antonio and Convento were considered. To comply with the above, first a characterization of the pig feeding systems used by the producers of traspatio pigs was carried out, then the acceptability of the use of four foliage flours of tropical shrub species was determined: Morera (Morus alba), Moringa (Moringa oleifera), Chaya (Cnidoscolus aconitifolius), and Peregrina (Hibiscus rosesinensis), in the pig feeding, and finally the inclusion of chaya flour at different levels (0, 10, 20 and 30%) in the diet for the feeding of mixed pigs in the growth stage was demonstrated that the management of the traspatio pigs feeding systems was performed in an empirical way leaving to a factor that affected by the production and profitability. source)
