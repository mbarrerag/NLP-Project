# BUCAL PROTECTOR IN PLAYERS OF BALONCHES, RUGBY AND FUTOBOL. QUALITATIVE STUDY IN A COLOMBIAN UNIVERSITY

The high prevalence of dental trauma during contact sports practices led the Association Dental American (ADA) to recommend the use of oral protector; however, the perception and low risk of these additives leads to not using or using any inappropriate. It has been reported that bucal protectors generate protective neuromuscular effect, improvement of player performance (function and strength) and greater security and confidence, especially when they are manufactured in a personalized way. As reasons for the non-use of oral protector has been posed: discomfort, pressure, difficulty to breathe or communicate (talk, gesticular, voicing), acquisition costs and non-compulsory. In this regard, studies in Colombia are scarce. Understand the perception that have basketball, rugby and football players from a Colombian university in relation to the presence of the oral protector. MÉTODOS: Qualitative study with a theoretical-methodological approach. In this regard, studies in relation to the use of oral protector in basketball, rugby and soccer players. They took part in 10 soccer players from the university selection (6 men, 4 women)., basketball and rugby) has a close relationship with the idea of physical risk, possibility of trauma or previous traumatic experience; it is recognized its use as functional but unattractive preventive addition. Knowledge is built through experiences between players of the same sport or different sports, dialogue with health professionals or qualified personnel. Determinants for use are: comfort and adaptability of the player during the practice of sports, poor communication, the cost of personalized oral protector with quality standards. Participants recognize the importance of minimizing injuries or traumas at the stomatognatic level and improving playability developing strategies with the use of oral protector. The perception about oral protector in football, basketball and rugby players is built in a multifactorial way, highlighting the idea of risk by contact during practice, the preventive characteristics of the addition, sports values, sports performance (individual and group goals), comfort, adaptability and individual disposition
