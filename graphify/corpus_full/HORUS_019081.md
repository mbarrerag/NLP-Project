# Soil interaction structure in tablestacles.

This final mastery work proposes an incremental method of interaction soil structure for the design of the tablestacas that includes the evaluation of the ultimate capacity and analysis of the deformations caused by the excavation of the ground. The analysis of fault, or of ultimate capacity, serves to pre-dimension the structure; that is to say to evaluate the depth of hincado and the rigidity to the bending of the tablestaca. On the other hand, the analysis of the deformations is done by means of the interaction procedure soil structure of Professor Zeevaert (1980) for each of the stages of the excavation. The method that is proposed takes into account that each stage of the excavation generates in the soil that is at the front of the tablestaca a discharge, and with this, a decrease of both the effort of soil plasticification and its rigidity. This fact leads to the obtaining of greater deformations and to that greater anchor lengths than those commonly used in the construction of these structures.
