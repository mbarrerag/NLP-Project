# Sustainability and alternative measures for the artesanal fishing in the Pacific: torramients, strategies and exploitation.

This book relates part of the experiences, applied research processes, lessons learned and articulated actions; which were possible through the development of the research project Strengthening artisanal fishing activity in the Colombian Pacific towards sustainable use of the resource. Tumaco BPIN 2020000100068. The project was implemented by the strategic alliance called Strengthening the Territory from the Institutions of Higher Education (IES) between the Bogotá, Medellín, Palmira and Tumaco headquarters of the National University of Colombia and the University of Nariño, and financed by the National Bank of Investment Programs and Projects through the General System of Regalías (SGR). this project was won by the biennial plan of convenings no. 2/890 For the creation of a list of project proposals eligible for strengthening institutional and research capacities of public higher education institutions, through the participation mechanism No. 1 of the ministry of science, technology and innovation (minsciences)
