# Excomborare

Exccomborare is the birth of the future Mactriz. The book collects the forces of investigation-creation of the EXCOMBORARE gesture, forces and images that appear from drifts made in the city of Cali, especially in the area of St. Nicholas. The demolition-construction and the scrap, which every day happens in ancient buildings, reveal the strength of the future Mactrix. In the space of action, emerges, under the rays of an ellipsoid light, the presence of the actress Maar, who with her voice dissects texts as vestigiums of a theatre. The expression "excomborare" is a displacement, a vulnerable action and an indergroud that destroys and reconstructs, produces an erasure, an overflow, a teluric movement and a special narrative of the debris that make part of the action alive.
