# Enlightenment of a chapel: Mystic and spirituality.

The project developed consists of the interior and exterior lighting design of the Cristo Maestro chapel of the National University of Colombia. A clear concept was designed and put into the design of lighting, responding to the historical, cultural and religious importance of space, all of this with the help of the review of references and background. A concept specifically designed for the place being illuminated is given, the light is related to God, because although for believers, although God does not see it is known that it is there, it is appreciated and felt all that he does; also the light, although it is not always possible to see its origin, it is possible to observe and feel, what this generates in the spaces and how it can cause sensations with an appropriate use of this. The current regulation for the space to be designed in a specialized lighting software (DIALux), following the specific guidelines of the RETILAP and the CIE 150, besides using a BIM modeling software (Revit) was designed model 3D. Finally, a lighting design is delivered that is not only technical and normatively correct, allows to give the observer a clear sense of the place is being illuminated
