# Design and structuring of indicators for demand management and energy performance at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters, under the parameters of ISO 50001.

This document shows the behavior of the energy demand of the Universidad Nacional Sede Bogotá, where it is intended to identify the impact on the energy performance of the final uses of energy and the activities that are carried out in the buildings 453 of Engineering Classrooms, the building 500B Historical Central Archive and the building 861 Uriel Gutiérrez, under some criteria that were defined throughout the study. Based on the information collected during the semester that included this development, some indicators were proposed with follow-up in certain periods, which result in establishing mechanisms and strategies to promote energy savings and help manage demand in the buildings in question, applicable to other buildings with these same parameters
