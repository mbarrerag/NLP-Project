# UnPROPRIATE DRIVING OF PROFESSORS AND DISEASE STUDENTS: A SCOPE REVIEW

Introduction: Inappropriate conduct in the academic field of both teachers and nursing students may have a negative impact on the professional life of graduates, on patients, the profession, institutions and society in general. To identify the research that has been developed around the inappropriate conduct of both professors and nursing students in higher education centers, the issues of interest on this subject and the findings of these researches. Scope review using the Prisma methodology. This type of research is scarce, a total of 16; 3 of them explore inappropriate behavior in professors. Research comes mainly from Uni dos States. Inappropriate conduct in research in the classroom, in clinical practice and in common scenarios is studied. Among others, it asks about types of behavior, attitudes to these behaviors and their management. The review proposes recommendations for monitoring these behaviors in universities, rescuing the importance of the role of professors as role models and codes of ethics for the appropriation of ethical behaviors by students
