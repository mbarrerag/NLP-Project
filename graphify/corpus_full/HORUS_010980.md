# SYNTHESIS OF REGID SPUMES AND FLEXIBLE POLYURETANS WITH PRODUCTS REFURED BY CHEMICAL DEPOLIMERIZATION VIA GLYCOLISIS

Polyurethane (PU) is one of the most consumed plastic materials in the global market because it has a wide range of high-performance properties. Due to its large volume of production, PU generates a large amount of waste around the planet, so its waste is an important environmental problem to treat, which motivates the inclusion of various methods for the use of PU foam residues, of which glycolysis is highlighted. In this work, the synthesis of rigid and flexible PU foams with products obtained by glycolysis of both foams. The foams developed are subjected among other tests to FTIR, TGA, SEM, mechanical tests of compression and density. The recovered polyol maintained its degradation temperature similar to virgin polyol. Obtaining a conversion of 0.86 for flexible and 0.74 foam glycolysis for rigid foams, both at 4 hours and 30 minutes of reaction. The recovered flexible polyol was used for the manufacture of flexible and rigid foams
