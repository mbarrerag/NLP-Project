# Articulated domains on health professions: an approach from physiotherapy work in hospital institutions

This work is the result of a qualitative research that seeks to reveal the logics of domination articulated on occupational physiotherapy professionals, in the hospital institutions, starting from the developments of post-structuralist feminism and the matrix of oppression. It was counted with the participation of physiotherapists linked to hospital institutions of the city of Bogotá, understanding these places as spaces in which spatial-gender relations are configured between different professions of the health sector. Therefore, this research seeks to understand as the articulation of dominations on the profession, determines the living conditions of the workers of physiotherapy and the relationships between professionals of the health area, the care as work and activity of the feminized professions in the sector, as well as the historical construction of the health disciplines in the field of the hospital. The contributions that this academic work carries out are given by means of the experiences of the workers of physiotherapy. s work and the activity of the womanly professions in this sector, as well as the historical construction of the health disciplines based on gender. The contributions of this academic work are given through the experience of the physiotherapists and from there, its build a knowledge which is located around the management of the “feminization of the work” from the voices of the people who perform these works in the hospital environment.
