# Evaluation of the modified cassava starch in the quality and sensory acceptance properties of a fermented soy beverage

The soy drink has been compared at nutritional level with cow's milk, including possesses certain nutritional components is superior due to its low fat content, without lactose, nor cholesterol. However, it lacks good sensory characteristics such as: low viscosity, characteristic flavor to bean and content of non-digestible oligosacarides. Thus, there are several researches related to the reduction of these defects by means of fermentation with addition of probiotics. Likewise, research has been carried out to improve viscosity, water retention and texture of the drink with the addition of stabilizers and/or thickeners. On the other hand, modified yuca starch has a wide variety of applications in different food matrices, as in meat products, dairy, breadmaking and in plant products, however, its application in the different matrices depends on the type of modification. The modification in starches can be enzymatic, physical and chemical type.
