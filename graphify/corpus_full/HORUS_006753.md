# Cartographies of and for peace: the peaceful route of women and their national mobilization to Cauca in 2015

This research presents an analysis of the national mobilization of the Pacific Route of Women towards the department of Cauca-2015, in key of gender, peace and spatialities. Through the voices and experiences of three of its members, narrative and visual reconstruction of the mobilization are reconstructed. Their accounts allow to recognize that in these spaces catalogued as off-page, important transformations are developed on different scales, innovative ways of making peace are constructed, and the bodies are appropriated for resistance, acuerpamiento and incidence. The practice of national mobilization demonstrates that there are “other ways” of making peace, through joy, non-violence, art, symbols, performance, granularity and party.
