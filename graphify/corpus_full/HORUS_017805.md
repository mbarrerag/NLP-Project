# Systemic design and analysis of a self-manageable backhaul network in star topologies and ring for rural connectivity in Caldas

In this article, a methodology for designing a wireless telecommunications network is validated to extend rural connectivity in Andean areas of Colombia within the framework of the post-conflict. The methodology presented allows the design of different types of network according to geographical information of the urban-rural area of the municipality of Marulanda, located in the region of Caldas, Colombia. Internet connectivity will be delivered through a self-manageable telecommunications technology known as WiBACK, which will support future 5G communication technology. Thus, the advantages and disadvantages of two network topologies are compared and a projection of the technological components for the implementation of the network is presented. The results of the research will design the roadmap and the comparative evaluation of the scenarios of expansion of the Colombian broadband access network in post-conflict scenarios
