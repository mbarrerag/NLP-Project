# Perception of patients on pre-anesthesia evaluation and the role of anesthesiologist in a level III hospital in Bogotá Colombia.

Anesthesiology is an area of knowledge of which most patients have little knowledge, in many cases they do not know the importance of this branch in the medical-surgical act. During the pre-anesthesia evaluation the anesthesiologist-patient relationship should be established, explaining what the anesthetic act consists in, solving doubts and optimizing the clinical state of the patient. Objective Knowing the perception of patients on the pre-anesthesia evaluation and the role of anesthesiologist in a third-level hospital in the city of Bogotá. Materials and methods This is a descriptive study. Through self-careed surveys, demographic variables were explored, the perception of patients regarding the role of anesthesiologist and the pre-anesthetic evaluation was investigated. The surveys were carried out at the end of the pre-anthetic evaluation, they excluded the incomplete records Results 173 complete surveys were obtained, mostly female residents in Bogotá, with academic bachelor level within the most frequent sociodemographic variables.
