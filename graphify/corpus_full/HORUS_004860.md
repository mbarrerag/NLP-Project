# Introduction to Python for GEOCIENCES

What is a new book by Python? And in Spanish? Although there are texts available, and many examples on the web that serve to learn individually, this book has a specific objective: it is an introduction to programming with Python for the beginner in computing. Many of the available books focus on the capabilities, characteristics and structure of Python codes, but they do not contain a guide for the reader to learn how to program. This book is at the same time an introduction to Python and a guide to how to program. It is a book suitable for the geoscientific expert who wants to enter into programming, the newly graduated professional who seeks to apply computational methods in his work, or the undergraduate and graduate student who feels deficiencies in computational aspects, and who does not find applied courses that can be useful in the development of his studies and his future career. There are few references with these characteristics, and in Spanish the availability is even less. This text seeks to cover the demand of the geoscientific public in Latin America, and the large number of students of undergraduate and postgraduate in Geology. The objective of Py is to be used.. The book approach comes with my prejudices. I learned to schedule late in my career, after finishing my undergraduate degree in Geology. Python is an object-oriented language, unlike Fortran (F77/F90), the language I first learned during my graduate studies. Therefore, the book has a more classic approach, with little emphasis on object orientation. This book includes Python scripts (archives containing code to be run in Python), and Notebooks or JupyterNotebooks that replicate all examples and most figures in the book. These files are available in the supplementary material of the book, and can be accessed through github
