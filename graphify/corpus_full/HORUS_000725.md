# Methodology for the determination of water quality parameters in lymphatic bodies using multispectral drone images and computational learning

The use of multispectral remote sensors coupled to unmanned aircraft represents an agile and economical alternative for the estimation of water quality parameters. However, in tropical areas and in the Colombian context, knowledge about its modelling by machine learning algorithms remains limited. This study aims to develop a methodology to evaluate water quality in lentical bodies using multispectral images obtained with UAS and three key parameters: turbidity, phosphates and nitrates. A DJI M300 RTK dron was used with the RedEdge Micasense P sensor to generate multispectral orthoimage on two water bodies in Cundinamarca, Colombia. In addition, 30 spatialized samples of the above parameters were collected. The results showed the spatial characteristics of water and vegetation, and by means of the partial minimum squares (PLS) regression of the water quality, the most variable in the prediction was determined. Subsequently, three automatic learning algorithms were developed and compared: SVR, RFR and RBM.
