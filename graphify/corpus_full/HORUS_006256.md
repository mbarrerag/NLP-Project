# Performance and energy efficiency in electric vehicles using an induction engine through active disturbance correction and optimal control strategies

Energy efficiency in the present day is of great importance in society and its energy transition engine proposals, including the topics of vehicle technology. As regards electric vehicles, a critical challenge lies in addressing the issues of autonomy. To address this concern, it becomes imperative to optimize several components and associated strategies for these vehicles. A critical component is the electric motor, and the induction motors are widely used for their profitability and well established techniques. Despite their advantages, the induction engine also experiences intrinsic losses, which requires an improvement in performance that can be achieved with control strategies. This research adopts a modified control approach based on active rejection of disturbances to address uncertainties and complex dynamics, together with optimal control concepts for the efficiency requirements of the induction engine. The modification implies that it includes a weighting in the rejection of disturbances, develops a cost-weighted function and adjusts all the parameters of the controller considering metaheuristic techniques.
