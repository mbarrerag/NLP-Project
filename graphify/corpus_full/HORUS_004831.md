# ORANJA (CITRUS SINENSIS [L.] OSBECK) MANUAL TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THEIR CULTIVE

This document is the result of the Subproject on Innovative Technologies for the Integral Management of Mango, Orange and Mandarin Crops in Production Areas of the Department of Cundinamarca, developed within the framework of the Agricultural Industrial Corridor CTA-2, Project on Research, Development and Technology Transfer in the Agricultural and Agro-Agricultural Sector, with the aim of improving the entire department, Cundinamarca, Centro Oriente, signed by the Government of Cundinamarca, through the Secretary of Science, Technology and Innovation; the mayor of bogota, through the district secretariat for economic development; the national university of colombia; and the Colombian Agricultural Research Corporation (agrosavia, formerly Corpoica). The Agricultural and Industrial Technological Corridor CTA-2 is financed with resources from the Science, Technology and Innovation Fund of the General System of Regalias
