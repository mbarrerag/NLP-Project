# Allocation of scores in standardized examinations using compatible neuronal networks and psychometric matching techniques: Case Study Knowledge 11 in Colombia

Standardized examinations are valuable tools to evaluate objectively both the cognitive and non-co-giv characteristics of a specific population. To construct measurement scales that accurately reflect the constructs that these examinations seek to evaluate, the theory of response to the item (TRI) is commonly used as a statistical technique. However, the TRI presents limitations when its assumptions are not fulfilled, compromising comparability over time and between subpopulations. This grade work is intended to develop an innovative methodology that uses Artificial Neuronal Networks (RNA), specifically through AutoEncoders (AE), to preserve the advantages of the TRI and to apply it even when its assumptions are not met, seeking even to improve the quality of adjustment and prognosis. The research is based not only on the analysis of the review Knowledge 11 applied in the years 2018 and 2019, during the A and B calendars in the country. Results were obtained that in some cases exceed the performance of a classic model of the TRI, such as the logistic model of 2 parameters (2PL).
