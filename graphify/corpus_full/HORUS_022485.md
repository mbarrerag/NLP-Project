# WITTGENSTEIN LAW FROM THE PERIFERENARY

Through a critical examination of the essays published in Ideas and Values about Ludwig Wittgenstein's philosophy, the paper seeks to establish how rich our assimilation of the thought of one of the great philosophers of history is, whether such a work shows an evolution along the become of the magazine and whether the examination I am doing here can give us some indication of our philosophical development. Finally, some brief considerations are made about what it means to make philosophy from the periphery and some possible advantages that it has, as well as to point out the need for a more collective work
