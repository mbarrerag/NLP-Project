# Passiflora ligularis Juss: chemical and pharmacological studies of a plant with therapeutic potential

This book is the result of interdis-ciplinary research around the leaves of Passiflora ligularis (Granadilla) for medicinal purposes, between the research groups Bioactive Principles of Medicinal Plants of the Department of Pharmacy of the National University of Colombia, Study and Exploitation of Natural Products Marines and Fruits of Colombia of the Department of Chemistry of the National University of Colombia and the Research Group in Phytochemistry of the Pontifical University Javeriana, with support from the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation of Colombia. Along these pages, some of the most outstanding results and advances of these research groups in terms of chemical characterisation of the leaves of this species emphasizing flavonoids and saponins, to the study in silico, in vitro and in vivo of their pharmacological activity, mainly as hypoglycemia and ant inflammatory, to the development of precise, accurate and reproducible analytical methodologies of this species, giving as a result of the full characterization of the optimized extract obtained as an active promising ingredient for the development of pro-ducts of phytotherapeutic, currently a stage of progress
