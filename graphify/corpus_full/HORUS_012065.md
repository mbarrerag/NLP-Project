# USE OF THE REASONABLE VALUE IN THE POST-TERM MEASUREMENT OF ASSETS UNDER IFRS. SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA.

The adoption of IFRS has indicated that fair value measurement (VR) of assets and liabilities would increase the relevance of information and its usefulness in investment decision-making. However, the application of VR in fixed assets has not been common in companies and presents challenges and problems in its application. Empirical research on the implementation of IFRS is growing, so it would be useful to carry out a review of literature that allows for the systematic revision of academic perspectives and findings on the use of VR in the subsequent measurement of fixed assets. Thus, the aim of this article is to systematize academic arguments and empirical evidence on the choice of VR, under IFRS, in the subsequent measurement of fixed assets, based on a systematic review of literature. To this end, the empirical work published in journals indexed in the Scopus, WOS, Redalyc and Scielo bases, between 2010 and 2020, was addressed. With the application of a protocol of incorporation and exclusion, 33 empirical works were selected and analysed, systematizing theoretical perspectives, methodological designs and findings, allowing to define some of academic research perspectives and to be addressed in this context
