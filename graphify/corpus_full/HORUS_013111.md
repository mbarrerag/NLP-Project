# Design of a project for pedagogical mediation that contributes to the development of mathematical competences in early childhood

This research paper arises in the face of the problem of teaching mathematics through traditional methods, where the student learns to memorize mechanically and routinely; in addition, to this is added the context so complex that the communities of rural areas in Colombia live, where they do not have many educational institutions and the few ones that exist, do not provide a quality education, especially for children of early childhood, whose bases are indispensable for their learning in later years. It is for this reason that, in this project, a project for teaching mediation is designed based on didactic strategies, which contributes to the development of numerical and geometric thinking in children of 4 and 5 years of age belonging to the Foundation Love, Grace and Bread, of the neighborhood Granizal in Bello – Antioquía; for this purpose, the Method for Natural Learning of Mathematics proposed by Professor Carlos Díez is implemented. Through the use of concrete material, and the implementation of the activities of the conteo and grouping of quantities, the children demonstrated an improvement in the relationship between the number graph and the quantity it represents, showing the effectiveness of the method. .
