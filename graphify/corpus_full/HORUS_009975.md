# Rethinking corruption in Colombia: the phenomenon of the capture and reconfigured co-opt of the State

Abstract The article seeks to identify, from a socio-legal perspective, how corruption was not exhausted with the end of the armed conflict, but rather remains a structural issue in socio-political relations in Colombia. In this regard, it is intended to establish the need to study new dimensions of analysis and approaches to definitive or structural solutions to problems parallel to internal war, rooted in the sociocultural links associated with the phenomenon of corruption. The latter is based on the socio-political and socio-economic relations of the population, in the face of the rupture and lack of trust between individuals and theirs with State institutions; this divorce promotes new forms of violence linked to the capture, co-optation and reconfigured co-optation of the State
