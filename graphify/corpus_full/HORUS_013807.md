# Design of a strategy of cooperation between laboratories that enhances the use of innovative simulation technologies for the development of collective and transformative interventions in the management of knowledge in health.

The scope of this proposal is framed in the design of a strategy of cooperation between the simulation laboratories of the Faculty of Nursing Bogotá Headquarters, the Technology laboratory for education and innovation of the Faculty of Engineering of Bogotá headquarters, and the Image and Modeling laboratory of the Faculty of Engineering and Architecture of Manizales Headquarters, who through the formation of this alliance seek to enhance the use of innovative simulation technologies that integrate advances in science in engineering and virtual experimentation in health areas for the development of collective and transformative interventions in the management of knowledge in the different areas and in the timely development of a product of virtual reality for the simulation of nursing procedures
