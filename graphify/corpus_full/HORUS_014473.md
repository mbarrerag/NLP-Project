# Study of the reduction of overadjustment in residual neural network architectures ResNet in a pattern classification scenario.

Artificial neural networks are a technique of machine learning inspired by the biological functioning of neurons, currently they support much of the so-called Artificial Intelligence. Despite their remarkable evolution these algorithms present the problem of overadjustment, "memorization of the training data", which decreases the ability to generalize. In this work, the overadjustment in a pattern classification scenario was studied and a method was determined to solve the problem. This study was carried out for the residual neural architecture (ResNet) and sustained in the analysis of the mathematical properties of the function represented by this structure, in particular the continuity of Lipschitz. The validation of the method was carried out comparing its performance with the conventional techniques of reducing overadjustment: the regularization L1, L2 and Dropout. Variing the depth of the network were carried out two experiments of classification with the data sets Digits and Fashion of MNEST. The analysis of the method was also carried out by means of analysis of conventional data and 3 sets of synthetic data. In addition, two experiments were carried out that included adverse images.
