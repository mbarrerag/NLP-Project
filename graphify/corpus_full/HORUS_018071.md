# CLINICAL STANDARDS BASED ON EVIDENCY: FUNCTIONAL DIAGNOSTIC AND INEGRAL REHABILITATION OF THE PATIENT WITH RACHYMEDULARY TRUMMY IN THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL

The rachimedal trauma (TRM) is a term that encompasses all traumatic lesions that affect the different structures of the spine at any of its levels (osteoligamentosas, cartilage, muscular, vascular, meningeas, root and spinal).The most frequent complications after the RMT include the loss of motor, sensory, intestinal and bladder functions, although complications have also been described as spasticity, neuropathic pain, autonomic dysreflection and depression.The objective of this Clinical Standard based on Evidence (ECBE) is to generate recommendations regarding functional diagnosis and integral rehabilitation of adult patients with TRM attended at the National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN), in order to optimize the quality of care provided to these patients as well as to improve their quality of life. This EST is also aimed at developing a flow diagram of functional diagnosis and integral rehabilitation of adult patients with TRM attended at the URN in any of its modes of health care provision: outpatient care or by telemedicine. This EST is an institution that is located in the general room, or in the hospital
