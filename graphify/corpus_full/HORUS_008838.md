# PHONOTYPICAL PLASTICITY IN RANA ANDINA DENDROPSOPHUS MOLITOR RENACUJES: AN ASSISTANCE USING CTMA

The organisms are constantly pressured by environmental stressors, being the adaptation one of the possible answers for their survival. Along the Andes mountain range, there are variations in ecosystems that exert pressure on species, especially those with a wide geographical distribution. These species develop conditioning processes that generate phenotypes capable of tolerating the specific conditions of each zone, what is known as phenotypic plasticity. This study analyzes the phenotypic plasticity of thermal tolerance, based on the maximum critical temperature (Ctmax), at different rates of warming in renacuages of the frog altoandina Dendropsophus molitor. This species is a heliothermic amphibium characterized by living high Andean wetlands and by having a phenotypic notoric plasticity among its populations.
