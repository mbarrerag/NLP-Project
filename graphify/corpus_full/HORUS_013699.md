# Nothing by magic, all by neighborhood art: territorial bets for the management of the soil protection by risk: the case of the upper basin of the Fucha river

This research presents the analysis of the strategies of management and social production of the habitat that are developed in the soils of protection by risk of the basin of the high river Fucha, for this purpose a approach was carried out from the methodology of the Observatory of Environmental Conflicts that included the documentary review of the current policy, interviews and field visits for the analysis of actors and the milestones that lead to the communities organizing and intervention of the soils of protection by risk as an exercise to guarantee their right to the City and to inhabit the hills in a sustainable way. It was found that there have been two ways for the implementation of alternative strategies, one, through the tables of consultation and the pressure of the inhabitants to be used to destine public resources and another, through the self-management of the own leaderships. In both cases, agroecological gardens, ecological restoration, terrace and interventions in public space with light materials such as strategies of social appropriation, mitigation and adaptation to risk.
