# ALTERNATIVE FOR SELECTION OF SARS-COV-2 BY RT-QPCR COPED TO SYBR GREEN

Introduction The exponential increase in cases of contagion with SARS-CoV-2 worldwide shows the importance of early detection to decrease the rate of progression of contagion. To perform large scale screening and detect cases quickly, a low cost diagnostic test is necessary. Methods RNA samples of patients positive for SARS-CoV-2 were retrotranscribed by RT-PCR and subsequently detection of the genes RdRp, E and RNP was performed with the first ones reported by Corman et. al. (2020) using a conventional qPCR system coupled with SYBR® Green. Amplifiers were observed by electrophoresis in polyacrylamide gel. Results and conclusions The concentration and ringing temperature of the first ones for detection of the virus SARS-CoV-2 using a response volume of 10μL. The efficiency curves show a linear behavior and the fusion curves have a single signal corresponding to the amplicon expected for each of the gene steps.
