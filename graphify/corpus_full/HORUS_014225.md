# Developing skills for strengthening the self and the role of persons with disabilities as a strategy for their personal empowerment and integration into the working environment: Building an inclusive future

The persons in condition of disability (PCD), have been stigmatized throughout the history in our society, creating a gap both economic and social that has prevented the access of this population to education, to the work environment and to the economic development. This project aims to develop labor competences for the strengthening of the being and of the doing in PCD, as a strategy for their personal empowerment and their integration into the work environment. This initiative has been the result of the articulated work of the National University of Colombia and the Vita Nova Association, NGOs that work for the PCDs in the city of Medellín. The development of the project will be based on the information of the diagnosis carried out by the Vita Nova Association and consists of three phases, namely: 1) Training of student leaders in university extension processes with PCD. These students will learn about the labor skills and the working methodologies adjusted to the needs of this population and will be those that will later accompany the processes of mentoring with the PCD participants in the project. 2) In the second phase, they will start the activities of mentoring with the emotions in the development of the soft skills, through methodologies of the experiential workshops and will be able to create the the topics addressed in the project and a video on entrepreneurship management for PCD, translated in sign language and with subtitles
