# BEHAVIOR OF WELDED BOLT SHEAR CONNECTORS SUBJECTED TO CYCLIC LOADS

Welded bolts shear connectors in composite steel deck diaphragms are occasionally used in some countries in Latin American for low-rise and middle-rise buildings, as a replacement of the steel headed stud anchors. Experimental and numerical simulations on push-out specimens were performed to study the behavior of welded bolts shear connectors subjected to fully reversed cyclic load. The focus was the behavior under low-cycle fatigue that occurs in structures subjected to seismic events. Due to the nature of the cyclic loads, the shear connectors will be subjected to alternate loads. Reversal-cycle load resulted in a reduction of about 40% in shear welded bolts capacity and 90% in slip capacity compared with the static results. shear connector, welded bolts, low-cycle fatigue, finite element analysis, composite structures
