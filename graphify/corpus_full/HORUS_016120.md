# Electromagnetic and thermal simulations of a prototype portable shelter against indirect atmospheric electrical discharge

This research aims to perform electromagnetic and thermal simulations of a prototype portable shelter against rays to determine the risk factors for the user, based on the IEC 60479-2 standard. Colombia is one of the countries in the world with the highest rates of occurrence of lightning and victim impacts by this phenomenon, especially in its armed forces. Electromagnetic and thermal simulations of the prototype are performed in the software simulation CST Microwave Studio®. The results show the induced pass voltages and energy levels reached by the user, depending on the resistance of the soil and the distance of location of the prototype from the impact site of the beam. It is concluded that the shelter prototype was not designed to withstand a direct lightning impact; it only mitigates the effects by passing voltages in the face of an indirect lightning impact
