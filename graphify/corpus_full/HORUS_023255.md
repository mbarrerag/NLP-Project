# Colombian investment regionalism, an anti-Oqueño approach.

Understanding the dynamics of regionalism in the national context allows defining the mechanisms that can facilitate investment in Colombia. These mechanisms are the necessary tools to address this issue within the negotiations of current entrepreneurs, whether national or international. Focused on the anti-Oqueño institutional framework, the determination of which aspects rooted in the culture of the region and the way in which its organizational structure is found impact on the decision-making of investment in the region. The region’s economic plurality allows a good development of flow of operations in the region, which consolidates with good social indices and good projections of the economy. The incidences of regional support in business have relevance, but within the reality of the negotiations, the importance of other determinant variables in decision-making as aspects of price and quality, where the first plays an important role in the process. The investment proposal in Antioquia goes beyond the attractive factors of the region, requires a detailed study of the participants of the negotiation, where the internal assessment has good basis for identifying and controlling the execution of a correct decision
