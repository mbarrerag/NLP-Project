# INFECTION CONTROL MEASURES IN HEALTH INSTITUTIONS IN THE CONTEXT OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIA

The group of health workers includes all persons who take action to promote health and who provide services in institutions from promotion, treatment, rehabilitation, management and administration. It also includes support services personnel such as transportation and feeding in outpatient, hospital and family care settings. (2) Studies in health workers have identified that an increased risk of acquiring the infection is related to factors such as inadequate infrastructure, inadequate personal protection elements (or incorrect use), and physical and mental overload by direct care of people with COVID-19, coupled with the absence of environmental control and engineering measures in work environments (3, 4)
