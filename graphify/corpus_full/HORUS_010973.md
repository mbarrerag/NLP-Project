# Study of the in vitro survival behaviour of SiHa cells irradiated with a BQT-HDR source of IR-192.

In this study, the survival of the SiHa cell line was evaluated when it was irradiated with a radioactive source of Iridio 192 (Ir-192), for this purpose the following methodology was proposed and developed. In the first phase, quality controls were carried out associated with the activity of the source, transit times and verification of the dose to be delivered. The activity of the source was verified by obtaining an error of 0.9104% and the transit time with an error of 3.15%, in addition to verifying and correcting a subdosing of 15% of the dose at the time of irradiation of the cell cultures. In the second phase of the methodology, a cell survival curve was obtained from the MTT test, for different irradiated doses, obtaining a decreasing exponential behavior according to what was shown in the literature; clonogenic tests were also performed to determine the fraction of the survival of the cells, but the results were not conclusive. In the third and last phase, cell cultures were irradiated with two of the most used irradiance patterns in the brachytherapy procedures of the National Cancer Institute.
