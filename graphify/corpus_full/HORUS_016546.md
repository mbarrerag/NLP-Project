# LOCAL SCIENTIFIC REVISATIONS ATTRACTED TO THE SUCCESS OF THE EXITS

The production of scientific knowledge is an important activity for the development of countries, and the dissemination of this knowledge, through specialized journals, is a characteristic of the university of the 21st century. In the Latin American context, the vast majority of scientific journals are of a public nature, edited by local universities of each nation (Pire, 2015). Despite the greater visibility of American and European scientific journals, regarding the visibility of Latin American scientific journals, local scientific journals may have a greater approach to local problems and communities, facilitating the development of research lines that are not of interest in international contexts, but that are relevant to the country where the journal is located. They are also part of the strategy of sovereignty of scientific knowledge, with respect to the science market (Gerding & Gerding, 2020)
