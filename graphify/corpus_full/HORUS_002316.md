# Exploration of Maillard's reaction for the obtaining of flavorings usable as salinity highlighters

At present, a branch of the food industry that has experienced a great growth is the flavoring sector; this sector has presented a series of challenges, such as the creation of characteristic flavor profiles through Maillard's Reaction (RM). The purpose of this research is to explore and analyze the various flavor profiles that originate through this reaction, additionally, its possible application as salinity highlighters will be analyzed. The first stage of this research is to obtain different flavor profiles by modifying various conditions of Maillard's Reaction, for example, the type of essential amino acid according to its radical chain, the types of reducing sugars used, the pH fixed and the reaction time. The objective of this stage is to achieve an optimization that guarantees the reproducibility and minimizes the formation of acrylamide. Once the optimization is achieved, the amino acids will be replaced by vegetable proteins, which will be a diverse source of amino acids with the lateral chain of interest. The objective of this stage is to achieve an optimization that ensures the chemicality of the formation of acrylamide.
