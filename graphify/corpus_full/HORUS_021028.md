# Study of vernacular architecture of Yuca hill in the municipality of Sutamarchán, with a view to its conservation and protection

In the village Resguardo Bajo and Valle de Santo Ecce Homo, of the municipality of Sutamarchán in the department of Boyacá, has been identified a group of thirteen vernacular dwellings in the vicinity of the Convent of saint Ecce Homo, which are grouped in the vicinity of the road that leads to the urban town of Sutamarchán. By this way, have also been identified, the ruins of the village of Indios de Yuca, a human settlement of Prehispanic origin of which there are currently few vestiges, represented in a few tapial, foundations, and also the vestiges of the ancient royal road of Santo EcceHomo. In this small portion of territory formed by the previously named paths, there is still, a community that continues to live proudly in its homes on land, cultivating in this place with unique climatic characteristics of a high Andean dry enclave, and that strives to establish a balance between its productive activities, its community life and the landscape. It is undeniable that this singular place of work counts with historical significance due to its relationship with its commercial activities and its unique activity
