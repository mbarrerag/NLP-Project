# THRIPS SAMPLE (THYSANOPTERA: THRIPIDAE) IN ROSE CULTURES EXPORT: SPACE ANALYSIS, PREDICTION MODELS AND DECISION-MAKING

Thrips (Thysanoptera: Thripidae) are key pests in ornamental crops due to aesthetic damage caused by feeding and oviposition processes in plants, such as by actionable quarantine measures in destination countries. Infestation analyses, plant-insect relationship, spatial and temporal dynamics of species in crops are fundamental to MIP decision making. A model of direct sampling analysis with different sample size for thrips in a commercial rose crop with two varieties is presented. Of a weekly sample size of 6.8% (N=34/900) of the total production/greening tables, each with three plants, was managed to adjust to the half based on population density differences allowing, however, to obtain a predictive model of infestation and of present-day models of infestation and relation to the damage. A high-aggregation areas of thrips were identified as donor focal points to recipient greenhouse areas, which allowed to estimate population dynamics of thrips in cultivation, valuable information as part of an early warning system were found to target the Franklini strategies
