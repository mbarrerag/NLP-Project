# WORK DISEASE: CHALLENGES OF CARE

The nursing of the work has an area of action that can be developed from the promotional approach based on the premise that workers are healthy people who will develop an activity. Within the health and safety at work, nursing has a leadership role that aims at promoting health and well-being, preventing the events associated with the work and when these events occur, rehabilitating them and even contributing to the palliation of those events that fail to have favorable resolution for the worker
