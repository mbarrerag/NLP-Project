# Model of the background and consequences of the Actor Engagement in Higher Education Institutions

The present doctoral thesis proposes and validates a model of background and consequences of the Actor Engagement in Higher Education Institutions. For this purpose, a review of the literature was carried out in which the origin, definitions and theoretical evolution of the concept were identified. Similarly, its possible background and consequences were established. The variable Quality of the Relationship and Loyalty were defined as the variable consequence of the Actor Engagement. The methodology applied in the research was divided into three stages. In the first, the exploratory factor analysis was used which sought to identify the dimensions and preliminary structure of the constructs for the three instruments. In the second, the confirmatory factorial analysis was applied using the technique of the Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) to assess the validity and reliability of the factors. Finally, the PLS-SEM technique was applied to validate the structural model and its predictive capacity. In addition, an innovative methodology of weighting based on the perception of each actor on the importance of its relations with other actors was implemented by the data of the LSD and the LSD. divide into three stages.
