# Single-shot Noise Reduction of Phase Maps through Phase-preserving Sliding Window Spatial Filtering

A single-shot procedure for coherent noise reduction in phase maps from digital holographic microscopy is presented. The method is supported in a multi-step spatial filtering which, unlike existing Fourier-based filtering methods, preserves the phase integrity
