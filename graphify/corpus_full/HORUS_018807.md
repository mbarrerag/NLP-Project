# CHAPTER 5: APPROPRIATION FOR TECHNOLOGICAL AND INNOVATION MANAGEMENT IN MILITARY INSTITUTIONS

The previous chapters offered conceptual frameworks in technology management and contextualized the role of technology in the ff. mm. in Colombia. This chapter shows, initially, the results of the characterization of technological management and innovation processes in various entities belonging to the Defense Sector, and especially the National Navy. This field work is key to understanding the real situation that is in the context analyzed. Subsequently, and as learning from the initial phase, with the aim of achieving greater institutional identity with the role of technology in the organization, an analogy with the naval is proposed for strengthening the appropriation of technology and its management within the arc. Finally, a systemic conceptual model was generated for the strategic interpretation of technology management and innovation
