# JULIATION, A CASE FOR RELATING FROM HEALTH AND SAFETY AT WORK

This research, carried out from the Health and Work Research Group of the Faculty of Nursing, has allowed to contribute to the visibility of a matter of interest and determinant for the quality of life of the people during and after the end of the working life. The above considering that, in the world scenario, a demographic change is experienced according to which it is expected that by 2050 the population of over 60 years of age will go from 10% to 21%. This creates new challenges for the social security systems. From this phenomenon, the countries of Latin America and the Caribbean are not exempt, since it is expected a generalized growth in the region [1]. Thus, the ageing of the population is also reflected in the work scene and the area of health and safety at work cannot be excluded from this reality. Thus, it requires the development of research that allows to guide actions in organizations founded on the needs of the workers and that they account for designing programs promoting healthy and quality of life environments according to the life cycle in which they are located. Colombia is in a demographic transition that characterizes the population aging situation.
