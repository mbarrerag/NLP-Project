# Contributions from the Social and Solidarity Economy to the construction of the Buen Vivir Campesino. Experience of the Women’s Committee in Inzá – Cauca

This research responds to the analysis of the contributions of the Social Economy and Solidarity in the construction of the Buen Vivir in Inzá – Cauca, starting from understanding the Buen Vivir (Campesino) as an alternative proposal to rural development constructed by rural women from the political bases of the Women's Committee of the Peasant Association of Inzá-Tierradentro: Food Sovereignty, women's political culture and peasant feminism. An area of impact for the Women's Committee has been the Social and Solidarity Economy, which has transformed into a Women's, Peasant, Social and Solidarity Economy, and which contributes to the construction of the Good Living in three dimensions: in their personal life (personal dimension), in the life of the community and the peasant territory (community dimension), and in the national and international political bets that position the peasant movement (global dimension) seeking to transform the social, economic and political order that keeps them in exclusion, discrimination and invisibilization. .
