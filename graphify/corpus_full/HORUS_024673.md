# PERFORMANCE PARAMETERS IN GALLINS AND FOOD POINTS RECEIVED FROM DIE CONTAINING COLOMBIAN MAIZE AND MAIZE IMPORTED FROM THE UNITED STATES

This study assessed possible differences in zootechnical yield and egg quality parameters in laying hens and fattening chickens fed with diets formulated with maize produced in Colombia or imported maize from the United States. For the trial with laying hens, 80 24-week-old Hy-Line Brown hens were used and randomly distributed into two treatments, each with 40 individually caged birds. The diets contained 56.7% of maize and adibitum was provided for 20 weeks. For the trial with fattening chickens, 352 single-day fed Ross 308 AP males were used and randomly distributed into two treatments, with 8 replicates of 22 birds each. The diets contained 51.0 per cent (initiator) to 59.6 per cent (finer) of maize and ad libitum were provided for 35 days.
