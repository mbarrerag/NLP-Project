# DATABASES FOR PROPHANS

This book does not aim and is not, a material of high theoretical depth, but rather hopes to serve as a help and foundation to students who start in these subjects or to the graduates of careers whose main components do not focus on these topics, but that in real life they must face problems that involve the construction and operation of database systems. The proposed approach resembles a workshop course, but based on clearly exposing the concepts, which must be internalized as necessary condition to move to higher level competencies.
