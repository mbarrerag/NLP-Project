# 6.BIOCLIMATICS AND ROOMABILITY

ABSTRACT CHAPTER 6 BIOCLIMATE AND HABITAT In 2015, the National University of Colombia Headquarters Medellín participated in the prestigious international university competition for sustainable housing called Solar Decathlon, an edition of Latin America and the Caribbean 2015, promoted by the United States energy department and in partnership for this edition, with the Ministry of Housing of Colombia and the Mayor of Santiago de Cali, who sponsored the construction of 15 prototypes of sustainable housing finalists in the "Sun Villa" in Santiago de Cali, the venue of the event. The University obtained fourth place with the prototype of sustainable housing UNSOLAR developed as a research applied by an interdisciplinary team of teachers and students of the faculties of mines and architecture. This chapter 6 of the book "A SOLAR Prototype of Sustainable Social Housing" summarizes the ideas with which one of the competition's tests was faced: Bioclimatic and habitability, which consisted in explaining the response of the sustainable urban master plan (developed in the test: urban design and affordability), to topics such as solar incidence, natural ventilation, circular metabolism and environmental recovery of intervention
