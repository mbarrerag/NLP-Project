# Dynamic burden of proof in the non-contractual liability of the State.

The classic burden of proof instructs the parties to prove the factual assumption of the rules containing the legal effect they seek, in order to avoid a decision contrary to their interests. However, despite being the regime that has dominated the Colombian legal scene for decades, cases were eventually presented in which, in accordance with this criterion, it was not possible for the party to provide the proof to be assessed by the judge, since it was the counterpart who had custody. Timily, and by the hand of the jurisprudence of the Council of State, the theory of the dynamic burden of proof, established as a distribution in the evidentiary burden, appeared in the procedural context. With the entry into force of the General Code of Procedure, the theory was elevated to legal standard, as a reflection of judicial activism and the search aimed at satisfying the principles of equality between the parties, the right of effective judicial protection and the primacy of the substantial law over formal law. However, being of exceptional application, it is necessary to indicate which criteria are the judge to apply the dynamic burden of proof in judicial proceedings, and specifically, in those in which the source of the extra-contractual responsibility is discussed (seed of the State)
