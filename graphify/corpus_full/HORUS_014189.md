# Contributions to strengthening cultural practices of Afro-descendant and indigenous communities in the Sanquianga Subregion _x0096_ Gorgona on the Pacific coast, from an approach to Environmental Rights and Territorial Peace

During the last three decades, the populations that inhabit the rural-coastal zone of the departments of Cauca and Nariño have suffered the impacts of the armed conflict, drug trafficking and various forms of dispossession that, together with the historical exclusion of systems of oppression that have impoverished and racialized, have also subjected them to a permanent violation of rights.The continental, coastal and maritime zones that these populations recognize as their territory, have been called Natural National Parks Sanquianga and Gorgona Island, within the framework of a normative development that conceives the environment fragmented from a modern rationalist and patriarchal logic.By their strategic location have been transformed into conflict zones, due to the presence of illicit economies and armed groups of all kinds that undermine the integrity of ecosystems and are imposed through practices of denial of rights to the communities, allowing in a deep way their well-being, knowledge and cultural practices, and their quality of life
