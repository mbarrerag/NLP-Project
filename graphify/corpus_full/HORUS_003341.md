# PHITOCHEMIC TAMIZAGE AND ANTIOXIDANT AND ANTI-CYCROBIAN PAUJIA GUAYABA (EUGENIA CF. CRIBRATA MCVAUGH), A FRUTA PRESENT IN ARAUCA (COLOMBIA)

The species Eugenia cf. cribrata is a plant that is present in the department of Arauca and in the region of the Colombian Orinoquía, has few researches on its chemical composition and its potential biological and pharmacological properties.The present research proposes the preliminary chemical study and the evaluation of the antioxidant and antimicrobial potential of the pulp and seeds of E. cf. cribrata. For this purpose the etanolic extract of pulp and seeds, by maceration, with a yield of 3.2% and 3.7%, respectively.The preliminary chemical analysis tests of both extracts showed the abundant presence of flavonoids, polyphenols, glycosides, saponins and reductive sugars, and in less the presence of other type of compounds such as, for example, coumarins.The evaluation of the antioxidant activity of the pulp extracts and seeds against the radical of DPPH at 100 μg/ml. The sample of the following was shown in the following studies: 95.26, 94.51 and 93.59% for gallic acid, being very similar values. The antimicrobial activity of the seed extract was evaluated against the bacteria Staphylococcus aureus, Enterococcus faecium, Escherichia coli, Pseudomonas aeruginosa and yeasts Candida albicans, C. tropicalis, C. glabrata and C. auris, with higher antibacterial activity, with percentages of inhibition of growth between 64 and 100%, compared to antifungal activity, which did not exceed 60% inhibition in any of the cases
