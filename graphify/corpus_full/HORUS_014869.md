# CONTEXT OF THE PLATANO MARKET IN GARIA Montes

addresses the geographic contextualization of one of the subregions with potential for growth of banana agro-industry in the country: Montes de María, in which is evidenced the need to implement a transformation model based on best practices that contributes to the productive inclusion of the department of Sucre, presenting the historical context, the situation of armed conflict in which this territory has been involved, in addition to its main advantages at the geographic level (as it is a strategic corridor of access to the region from other areas of the country), its ecological component (the use of its soils) and the territorial and productive characterisation of two of its municipalities: Ovejas and Chalán
