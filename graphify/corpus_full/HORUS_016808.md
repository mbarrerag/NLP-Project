# DETERMINATION OF THE AMOUNT OF SUBSTANCE OF POTASSIUM HYDROGEN PHTHALATE FROM THE THEORETICAL COULOMETRIC TITRATION CURVE

National Metrology Institutes certify the potassium hydrogen phthalate (KHP) as a reference material for acid-base titrations and pH buffers [1]. They use coulometry as a potential primary method for quantification and certification of this analyte [2]. The coulometric titration of KHP involves three stages: a) the titration of acidic impurities in the initial dissolution, mainly carbon dioxide; b) the titration of 99.8% of the KHP; and c) the neutralization of the remaining analyte [2]. The usual method to calculate the amount of substance involves the non-linear regression (NLR) of an empirical function (eWf) to experimental data [3]. In this work, we propose an alternative way to compute the amount of substance: the theoretical coulometric titration curve (TC), which not only calculates the amount of KHP directly but also estimates the amount of carbon dioxide solubilized, and the acid dissociation constants of CO_2 and KHP. We performed 47 coulometric titrations of KHP of two different purities. The amount of substance for the KHP from Merck EMSURE® Reag. Ph Eur (99.78%) was 4.88084 mol·kg^(-1) (SD= 0.00265), and for the KHP from K-34 CCQM (2016) (99.97%) [4] was 4.89088 mol·kg^(-1) (SD=0.00342). In both cases, the value was 0.11 % lower than the one calculated with the eWf. This result can be explained considering that the eWf calculates only the endpoint time, and it cannot distinguish between KHP and carbonic species. Hence, the amount of substance is given as total acidity. On the contrary, the TC estimates the amount of CO_2, HCO_3^- and KHP, being possible to report the result as entirely KHP. Finally, the TC has the advantage of calculating the acid dissociation constants of CO_2 and KHP, founding values with low percentage error from the data available in the literature [5,6]: for CO_2 constants; the differences are -14.2 and 0.2 %, and for the second dissociation constant of KHP the difference is only 0.2 %. The first dissociation constant of KHP was not estimated because it has low statistical significance in the model. The results presented before allow us to propose the TC as an alternative model for calculating the KHP amount of substance
