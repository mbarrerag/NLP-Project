# RESILIENCE IN THE SINDEMIC SCENARIA OF COVID-19: FOR THE PURPOSE OF HEALTH TRANSITIONS AND TRANSFORMATIONS

From different perspectives, we reflect on the resilience in the syndemic scenario of the COVID-19: regarding transitions and transformations in health.. This text raises the need to take advantage of this pandemic crisis to generate sustainable transitions towards transformational changes in resilient health systems. Based on existing experience and publications, lessons learned from the current pandemic, given human and life-related fragility, uncertainty and social response capacity, are highlighted. There is a need for greater knowledge of these public health phenomena, greater governance in policy formulation to prepare, direct and manage health systems in response to these crises
