# HEALTH IN TERRITORIES STRENGTHENING COMMUNITY PARTICIPATION IN THE INCLUSION

Primary care in health, interdisciplinarity and community participation in identifying needs and formulating improvement strategies are issues that are gaining a lot of relevance in today's society. This article aims to describe the variables that influence social inclusion in Cundinamarca from the perspective of three social actors to identify the axes in which work should be done in terms of the implementation of the Public Policy in social inclusion. The critical-social approach was applied for this reason the data is collected through discussions and interviews in which 14 members of the community participated (leaders of the village, parents and rural teachers). The topics addressed were disability, social inclusion, and barriers to access to inclusive education. The analysis focuses on the categories of analysis of social determinants of health and the concepts surrounding disability. The results describe the distal determinants such as the conditions of the context, access to health, employability, social practices, access to education.
