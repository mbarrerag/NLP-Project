# Butterfly diversity (Lepidoptera: Hesperioidea-Papilionoidea) in two areas of the department of Vaupés, Colombia

Summary In some places in Colombia there are still gaps in the knowledge of fauna, specifically of the lepidopterological diversity, due to its distance and limited access, such as the indigenous reserves of the Amazon region. The aim of this work was to characterize the diversity of day butterflies in two areas of the department of Vaupés corresponding to the indigenous communities of Puerto Inayá, Wasay, Puerto Paloma and Villa Fatima and in two climatic seasons. The samplings were carried out in the winter season, July to September 2017, and in the summer season, January to March 2018. In each community the organisms were collected for five days between 7:00 and 18:00 hours with jam and traps van Someren Rydon (VSR) using two types of bait (fermented fruit and broken fish) in places vertically distributed in three strata (sotobosque: 1m, intermediate stratum: 5m and canopy: 10m). 2,199 specimens of 272 species and 123 subspecies, 6 families and 18 subfamily, were reported the total distribution of two new records and three spatial expansions were recorded in the country
