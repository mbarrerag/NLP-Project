# AUDIT INTERVENTION - VERBALE EARLY IN FIRST INFANCE TO THE LIGHT OF AUDIT AND COMMUNICATIVE HEALTH

Summary This document, born from the academic and professional exercise in the Central Military Hospital, the Sanitas University Foundation and the National University of Colombia. It is strengthened by the conjuncture of the health, social and economic emergency due to the global pandemic of Sars-Cov2. The aim is to reflect towards traditional treatment models vs. models centered on the social environment, in the care of infants with cochlear implants at an early age, which has a close correlation with the normization in communicative auditory health. The methodological approach was reflective and analytical to know and understand the normative guidelines and the care actions from the teleassistance as a response to the recommendations of the World Health Organization, the International Monetary Fund and the World Labour Organization that harmonize with the law 1419 for Colombia that regulates the care of patients by teleconsultation. The review of the literature is made using the PubMed base, with terms of association and filters to find the publications of greater evidence. Of these, 4 support the (re)habilitation focused on the family and the social environment of the patient.
