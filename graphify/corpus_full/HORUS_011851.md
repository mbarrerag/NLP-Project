# INCLUSION OF THE GENDER PERSPECTIVE IN THE SCHOOL AND BEST RESEARCH LINE/S

The National University of Colombia, from the National Directorate of Digital Strategy (DNED) and the Division of University Laboratory (UNALab), propose a framework for action to manage data and information to reflect the actions carried out on the impact of digital transformation with a gender focus on the contribution to reducing gender gaps. This framework includes the definition of structured tools for data collection with a gender focus, reliable recording of information and access to data, by the areas responsible for carrying out the measurements. Two fundamental axes were defined with which reflection is initiated with regard to digital transformation with a gender focus. These are: the visibility of academic actions; and the definition of actions necessary for the recording of measurable data, according to the pillars of gender and digital transformation. The latter leads to the definition of indicators, which allow for better decision-making.
