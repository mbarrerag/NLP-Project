# Functional Urban Areas in Colombia: Space Interdependencies for the Optimum Applicability of Local Economic Development Policies

This research characterizes and discusses the concept of Functional Area Colombia and the system defined by the Organisation for National Cooperation and Economic Development OECD applied to the Colombian case and, contrasts it, with the study of the Mission of the System of Cities elaborated by the National Department of Planning DNP and the figure of territorial planning corresponding to the Metropolitan Area. The methodological approach is carried out in two ways, on the one hand, a spatial analysis is carried out through the identification of clusters or clusters and hot spots by means of three indicators: the measure of multidimensional poverty, the index of fiscal performance and the measurement of the integral municipal performance; this result is intersected with the location of the AUF and is analyzed through the concept of experts in the question, as well as through a discussion group. On the other hand, both the results of the identification of the AUF in the country, by the study of the OECD and the spatial analysis carried out in this study, the most important form of the adoption of experts in the figure is broken down.
