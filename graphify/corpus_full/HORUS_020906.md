# Quality Assessment of Public Space at the Barial Level in Manizales - Colombia

In urban planning, the benefits in the provision, use and maintenance of Public Spaces (EP) are widely recognized, therefore, the evaluation of the quality of the EP is an integral part of the management of this, which is why the underlying need for a measurement arises, since, the quality without EP does not provide benefits and without evaluation cannot measure the quality of the EP nor determine its contribution to the improvement of the quality of urban life. For the evaluation, there are methods of quantitative and qualitative type, the former are designed for the instruments to be applied by specialists and in the data collection is used for a considerable time; the latter serve to explore and understand the meaning that the individuals or groups attribute to a problem through words or descriptors, however, the use of text for the description of the quality of the EP may require users to read, interpret and visualize or imagine the situation, which is limited and is a possible source of bias, while the use of numbers facilitates understanding when it comes to decision making or prioritisation of investments according to the condition of the EP. This project aims to develop a quantitative type method for the assessment of the EP. identification of funding opportunities, programming for maintenance and prioritization of interventions
