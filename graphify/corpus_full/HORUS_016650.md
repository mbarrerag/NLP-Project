# Processes of rural land transformation in the Province of Sabana Centro 2000 – 2019. Evaluation of occupation and coordination in territorial planning

Suburbanization, as a territorial phenomenon in the province Sabana Centro, is analyzed in this document from an urban government perspective that considers the territory's legislation as a national legal basis for the regulation of the rural component. From there, theoretical scenarios are opened that analyze the processes of metropolitanization and urbanization in the rural territorial planning from the perspective of the state and the market, as main axes in the urban-rural contexts. Based on the understanding of the normative and academic panoramas in the matter, an analysis is proposed of the expressions that have transformed the territory, based on the instruments of territorial planning of the municipalities that make up the province Sabana Centro. This shows the relationship between the normative provisions and the realities that confront the territories, in addition to the consequences arising from the administrative dislocation and its governance, which limit the understanding of the territory and originate actions and decisions fragmented from within the Sabana Centro progional and authorities against planning and territorial planning. .
