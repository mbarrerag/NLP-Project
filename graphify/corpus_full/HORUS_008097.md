# Development of mycelium-based biomaterial and wood waste for the construction field

This research evaluates the development of a biomaterial based on mycelium and wood waste, as an initiative towards the mitigation of the environmental degradation generated by the construction field, responsible for 37% of the total CO2 emissions. The mycelium is the vegetative structure in the form of a network of fungi decomposing lignocellulosic compounds, through enzymes that excrete the cell wall of its filaments called hyfas, which break the polymers of the substrate in which it is found in molecules of easy absorption, generating a natural binder species. Due to this capacity, the mycelium has been of great research interest in the development of materials with application in various fields, such as art, architecture, construction, among others. For this case, samples are prepared with a substrate formed by wood waste, obtained from the local wood industry. The sample cultured is a factor of acoustic absorption and percentage of water.
