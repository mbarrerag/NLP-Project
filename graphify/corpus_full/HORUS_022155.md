# Survival at 3 years, costs and associated factors in patients over 65 years of age who have undergone cholecystectomy in Colombia: a retrospective national cohort.

Retrospective study of cohorts based on data of administrative claims that uses the basis for the study of the sufficiency of the UPC of the Ministry of Health and Social Protection of Colombia and the RUAF for deaths. The main outcome was survival at three years after surgery, additionally mortality was evaluated at 30 days and one year, as well as overall costs.
