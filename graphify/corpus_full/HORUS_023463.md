# ASSESSMENT OF MICROSTRUCTURAL MASES OF PLATE CHARINE ADDITIONED WITH PROTEINS, EMULSIONANTS, HYDROCOLOIDS AND ALLMIDON

This work evaluated the behavior of masses of banana flour Dominico Hartón (Musa Paradisiaca L.), by incorporating proteins (Winter powder), emulsifiers (Fat Acids of the Tartaric Diacetyl Acid (DATEM), hydrocholoids (Goma xántan) and corn starch, by means of a microstructural characterisation compared to wheat flour control. The microstructural characterization showed a new network of proteins in the added flour, which were non-existent in native flour, these changes are due to the presence of the protein and starch added with the intention of improving their technological behavior
