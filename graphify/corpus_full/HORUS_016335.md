# ADOPTION OF THE ROLE AND SURVEILLANCE OF THE FAMILY CAREER OF THE CRONE PATIENT: RELATING FACTORS

The family caregiver faces transition processes due to changes in his life, the role of a healthy person, to be a caregiver of a person with chronic disease (EC) to whom he should learn to administer medicines. The objective of the work was to describe and correlate the sociodemographic, care, overload and adoption of the general role and in the use of medications in family caregivers of people with EC. A descriptive, analytical, cross-sectional study carried out in Bogotá, (June 2021 - March 2022). 321 family caregivers of people with EC, recruited in a convenience sampling. Obtaining the information was done by telephone call and e-mail, and the administration of the instruments: A characterization card of the GCPC-UN-D diad, ROL-M instrument, ROL instrument and the carer's burden scale of Zarit. Family caregivers take care of their loved ones with highly dependent and multipathological EC. Caregivers present a satisfactory role and a basic adoption in the role of use of medications.
