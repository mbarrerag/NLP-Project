# Robust multivariate regression: a focus for high dimension data

The multivariate multiple linear regression is a statistical technique widely used to model the relationships between several variables response and several predictor variables. Traditional methods based on verisimilitude can produce very misleading results in the presence of atypical values. In this work, we propose two robust methods of multivariate regression designed to handle data with high dimensionality: one based on the MRCD estimator, a robust estimator of location and dispersion for data with high dimensionality; and another focused on reducing the dimensionality of the problem by using the ROSPCA methodology. Through simulations, we evaluate the robustness and efficiency of the estimators obtained, the ability of the methodologies to properly classify observations into contaminated data sets, and the computational cost. An application with real data illustrates the use of the proposed methodologies. .
