# Cauchy's problem associated with a fifth-order dispersive disturbance of Benjamin's equation.

In the context of fluid electrodynamics, the following equation was deduced: $u_t + u_{xxxxx} - u_{xxx} + \sigma\, u_{xxu_x=0$, where {sigma$ is called Hilbert's transformed]. In this paper, the Cauchy problem associated with this equation was studied, obtaining results of local well-proposed in the following cases: first, taking an arbitrary real initial data in the periodic space of Sobolev $H^s (T)$, when $s>3/2$, and second, when the initial data belongs to $L^2 (R)$
