# Evidence-based clinical standard: diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the thyroid node patient and differentiated thyroid cancer at the National University Hospital of Colombia

This book presents the Clinical Standard based on evidence: diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the patient with thyroid node and differentiated thyroid cancer at the National University Hospital of Colombia. This ECBE is part of a process of standardization of health care, taking into account the best evidence, available resources and interdisciplinarity, with the purpose of generating a comprehensive approach that improves the outcomes of patients and optimizes the use of resources at the hospital level. The importance of standardization of care of patients with this condition is recognized, due to its importance for the institution and its comprehensive care.-- Contracadas
