# Development of tools for the registration and monitoring of SUDS: Case study flood-watered corks located on 132nd Street with Avenida Ciudad de Cali, in the city of Bogotá

Within the framework of the implementation of Urban Systems of Sustainable Drainage (SUDS) in the city of Bogotá, the SUDS Network identified that there were no tools to monitor and monitor the operation of these structures, which would allow the detection of preventive and corrective maintenance needs. For this reason, this project carries out the development and evaluation of tools to improve the registration and monitoring of the SUDS by using applications based on Geographic Information Systems (GIS). Two independent tools were generated, consisting of advanced forms of Field Maps and Survey123 Connect, of which simplified use tools were obtained, focusing on the use in the field by technical staff who do not have expertise in the SUDS. These tools were implemented during the monitoring of the operation and maintenance of a floodable cork located in the City of Cali with street 132, from which design recommendations and monitoring frequencies were issued. .
