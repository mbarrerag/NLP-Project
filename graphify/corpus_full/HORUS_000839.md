# PARTICIPATION AND DISCRIMINATION BY GENDER IN THE WORLD OF WORK

This research proposal should focus on patriarchal culture and its impact on the labour market. As a result of women's struggles during the 19th and 20th centuries, an equal standard has been achieved, but it is not applied in practice. Work aims to reveal the roots of these inequalities, which are caused by imbalances in the labour markets and which are to be manifested in the shaping of the law and its implementation. Indeed, in the second half of the 20th century, the great female revolution began. One of the most significant manifestations of this revolution is the incorporation of large numbers of women in the world of work, which was a shock to the production structures that originally, the first industrial revolution, were designed for male labour.
