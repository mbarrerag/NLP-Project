# Update to the development plan and requirements foreseen in previous applications - Step 1

This project improves the teaching, research and extension processes handled by the Laboratory of Electrical Machines and Measurements (LMME), by acquiring voltmeters and ammeters for test consoles 1, 2, 3 and 4. These instruments are essential to visualize the voltage and current values of the variable sources in DC in the process of manufacture, thus ensuring the proper functioning of laboratory practices. Additionally, progress is made in the commissioning of workstations 1, 2 and 3 with the necessary inputs to install and operate the torque and encoder sensors that were acquired in previous calls and are available in the laboratory
