# Comprehensive reparation for victims of forced displacement in Bogotá (2012-2020)

This final work of Mastery has as objective to analyze how the rights to a decent housing were restored and to an adequate habitat of the victims of forced displacement through the allocation of housing units during the district administrations Bogotá Humana (2012-2016) and Bogotá Mejor Para Todos (2016-2020). For this, the application of a methodological strategy of qualitative character around two cases of study: the Project of Housing Plaza de la Hoja and the Project of Housing Paths of the Sierra. To analyze these two case studies, it is part of the framework concept associated with the integral reparation of the victims of the armed conflict and its two approaches: the restorative and transformative reparation.
