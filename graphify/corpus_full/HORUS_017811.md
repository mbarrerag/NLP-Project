# Influence of the slope of the soil on the indicators of manual citrus harvest in the agricultural sector in Caldas-Colombia

Summary In Colombia, the agricultural sector has difficulties in integrating technologies, due to the difficulties of topography, which is very characteristic in the area of the Colombian mountain ranges that pass through the country's main agricultural departments and the limitations of human capacity; the process of collecting citrus fruits has traditionally been done by hand, employing thousands of people who do not achieve significant yields, thus causing an increase in production costs with an impact on the harvest indicators, measured in terms of quality and productivity. This study aimed to determine the impact of the conditions of the slope of the ground on the indicators of quality and indicators of effectiveness, efficiency and losses, used to evaluate the productivity of the orange harvesting process, in a case of study in the Department of Caldas-Colombia and to identify opportunities for improvement of the process. The information on the ground was carried out on orange producing farms with different slope of land classified in four categories. The results were able to identify statistically significant associations between the indicators of efficiency, effectiveness and loss and conditions of the slope of the field.
