# EDUCATION FOR CONSUMER RESPONSIBLE AND SAFETY OF MEDICINAL HONGOS WITH POTENTIAL IN MENTAL HEALTH: A DRAFT RESEARCH-ACTION-PARTICIPATION IN THE COMMUNITY OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY, HEADQUARTERS

It is recognized that various factors, ranging from genetic to economic and emotional, can affect the mental health of people, especially in moments of significant transition in life. Colombia faces additional challenges in mental health, including the risk of drug shortages and an increase in depression and anxiety during the pandemic. In this regard, this research project addresses mental health in the community of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters, based on participatory action to involve the university community in identifying and addressing these problems, with the main objective of implementing an educational strategy that promotes the responsible and safe consumption of medicinal fungi with mental health potential in the target community. To achieve this, specific objectives have been established that include the characterization of the population of fungal consumers, the analysis of their experiences, the analysis of the composition of the most relevant fungi, both marketed on campus and consumed in a therapeutic way, and the design and evaluation of the educational strategy. The participatory action methodology will be used throughout the process to ensure the active and equitable participation of all those involved. This seeks to address a public health problem by providing education and awareness about the potential use of mental fungi
