# Regularization of artificial neural networks for the classification of images of diabetic retinopathy.

Diabetic retinopathy (RD) is a retinal pathology caused by diabetes, and is one of the main causes of blindness worldwide; early detection is paramount in order to prevent its progression in the patient. There are several methods for early diagnosis, among these, it has been shown that convolutional neural networks (CNN – Convolutional Neural Networks) are suitable for the analysis of this phenomenon, contributing to the early diagnosis of this disease. In addition, deep learning techniques (DL – Deep Learning) have been used, the models proposed in the literature have been focused on the stages of preprocessing, extraction and selection of image characteristics; however, these models may have overadjustment (Overfitting) and the use of regularization techniques to control it has not been considered. Thus, in this work, a technique of conceptual and experimental improvement of the neural networks has been proposed, the selection of five techniques of regularization on five pre-trained deep learning models and through the analysis of metrics (precision, Recall, F1 score) is determined for the use of a technique of neuro-adjustable improvement
