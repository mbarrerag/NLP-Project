# Haematological Responses to Hypoxy Canotage Practice

Because the canoeing in the country has been of short practice there has been interest to characterize haematological discipline. To determine the response of haematological and anthropometric variables generated by the practice of canotage in hypoxia. An observational cross-sectional range was performed by means of the method of re-inhalation of carbon monoxide to estimate the haematological variables as concentration of Hemoglobin [Hb], Hematocrit (Hct), total mass of Hemoglobin (Hbt). The previous variables were conjugated to estimate the volume of erythrocytes (VE), volume of plasma (VP) and volume of blood (VS). Anthropometric variables of Muscular Mass (MM) and % muscular tissue (TM) were determined by analysis of four components; the statistical descriptive response, mean values, standard deviation estimating the coefficient of Spearman and p. Place: Participants in the nautical mode located in the urban park Simon Bolivar were applied, with the variables of the MM. xia. A cross-sectional observational study was covered out, where the carbon monoxide re-inhalation metod was applied to estimate the haematological variables and four-component analysis for the anthropoometric variables, descriptive statistics were applied throughout frequency tables, measurements of trend. central estiming the Spearman coefficient and p value (alpha of 0.005). Place: It took place in the city of Bogotá, in the caneing leaving located in the Simón Bolívar metropolitan park. Population: canoe athlets between 14 years and 26 years old. other countries in Latin America and Europe
