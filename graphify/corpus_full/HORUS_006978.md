# The role of sexuality in older people’s lives: a multi-method analysis from Colombia

There is a significant research gap concerning older people’s sexualities, particularly in middle-income countries. When included in the literature, their experiences are portrayed simplistically, and without considering the particularities of their identities and circumstances. The purpose of this chapter is to consider the importance that older individuals in Colombia place on sexuality from a gender perspective and considering underlying inequalities. To do so, a multi-method approach is proposed that includes a statistical analysis of inequities with multivariate logistic regression models from the National Survey of Health, Wellbeing and Ageing (SABE) 2015 (n=26,694 aged 60 or plus) as well as a qualitative study based on 39 semi-structured interviews with older people in Colombia. Results show that, people with a better socioeconomic status and higher level of educational attainment report a greater importance of sexuality. Furthermore, although the SABE estimates that for 73 per cent of males, sexuality is a relevant aspect of their lives, compared to 28 per cent of females; the analysis shows that the importance of sexuality among older individuals does not diminish; rather, it is redefined because sexuality is assessed multidimensionally and is susceptible to gender and socioeconomic inequalities. The values learned early in life, the transformation of ideas about sexuality, physical changes, health conditions and diseases, and recent sexual practices and experiences are all variables that influence perceptions of sexuality either negatively or positively. This study is a key contribution to the scholarship on sexuality and ageing from a gender perspective in middle-income countries
