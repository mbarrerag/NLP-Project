# TO THE EDITOR

The aim of our study was to develop predictive models to determine the normal/expected CAHI in healthy children. This approach may be used to identify children with CAHI values outside of the range expected for the physiologic effects of age and altitude above sea level, regardless of whether they were associated with PB or not. Although we acknowledge the limitations of using current CAHI and PB definitions at high-altitude, we feel our results may contribute to optimize testing and treatment of hypoxia, reoxygenation, apnea, and arousals, all of which are increasingly recognized in children living at high altitudes
