# Effect of foliar and edaphic silicon application on nutritional status of flower stem and post-harvest rose (Rosa x hybrida L.) cv. ‘Brighton’

The rose is considered the main exportable crop in cut flowers worldwide, where Colombia is located as the second producer and marketer country of this flower. Silicon (Si) is considered an element for plants thanks to the tolerance under stress conditions, and for increasing the yield of some crops. Recently a possible effect of Si on mineral nutrition has been reported, however, little research has been developed to explain these events. Therefore, it was intended to evaluate the effect of the exogenous application of three fertiliser of Si in a foliar and edaphic way on the post-harvest life and nutritional status of rose.
