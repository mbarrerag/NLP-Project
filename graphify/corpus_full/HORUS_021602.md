# Evaluation of the condrogenesis mediated by conditioned means of mesenchymal stem cells derived from adipose tissue versus plasma rich in platelets in osteochondral knee defects: an experimental model in rabbits.

The lesions of the hyalin cartilage cartilalage showed whether they are traumatic or degenerative, they result in condrale or osteocondraal defects that do not scar with new hyalin cartilage. For this reason, the regenerative therapy has been investigated only, with special attention to the secretoma of mesenchymal stem cells (MSC). This study aims to evaluate the condrogengenesis of platelet-rich plasma (PRP) supplemented with means of the mesenchymal stem cells derived from adipose tissue (AD-MSC-CM) in a model of osteocondral defect (DO) of knee in rabbits. a phase study is proposed, a pilot phase in which the experimental model is established first standardizing the surgical technique with six pieces of the cadvericas, then the method of administration of treatment with two rabbits New Zealand with survival. The first is treated only PRP administered in an infiltra phase, the second phase is treated with PRP + the following experimental model: ED-AC-MSCSC-MS-MS-CM-CM. The first is considered, a study is considered, seven days, none of the rabbits showed any adverse effect other than a mild limp. They also did not show important signs of pain, discomfort or distress so there was no need to apply end-point criteria. Despite the differences between species, the approach of an DO in rabbits allows to evaluate the principle of treatment to be applied as an effective cost model, which will allow to analyze the chondrogenic capacity of the PRP and AD-MCS-CM. The PRP is a good strategy that serves as scaffolding for the delivery of growth factors and also acts synergistically with AD-MSC-CM. We should clarify that the results regarding chondrogenesis should be analyzed by means of a histopathological study in the following phases of the study and after having passed 12 weeks. Despite having performed a xenogenic implant we did not observe any type of adverse event in the rabbit treated with AD-MSC-CM, possibly indicates that these are safe in application in subsequent studies
