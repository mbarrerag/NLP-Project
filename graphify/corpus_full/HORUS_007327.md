# The Orchestra Rhythm in Bogotá between 1935 and 1950

This research analyzes the trajectory and relevance of the Orchestra Ritmo in Bogotá between 1935 and 1950, highlighting its role in the capital music scene of the time. Through the review of 412 scores preserved in the Rother-Batuta archive of the National Library of Colombia, the repertoire of the orchestra is reconstructed, composed mainly by international genres such as foxtrot, bolero, samba and rumba. The research reveals that, unlike other national orchestras such as those of Luco Bermúdez and Pacho Galán, the Orchestra Ritmo focused on satisfying the cosmopolitan tastes of the Bogota elite, moving away from a nationalistic discourse. In addition, the study highlights the importance of radio in the dissemination of the music of the Orchestra Ritmo, with its participation in stations such as La Voz de la Víctor and Nueva Granada.
