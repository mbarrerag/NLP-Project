# CHAPTER 3: TRAINING AND FRONTIING TO GET OUT OF VICTIMMATION: THE CHARGES OF THE VICTIMS OF THE ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA

The public eruption of the subjects with the name of "victims" is recent in Colombia. The war had led us to name them with other labels: displaced persons, disappeared, kidnapped, killed, etc., depending on the consequences that civilians had experienced in the midst of crossfire and the disputes between the armed groups. The homogenization of each of these experiences with the name of "victims" does come to us because of the processes of democratization, the positioning of humanitarian discourse and the defense of human rights in the international context, besides the normative character around the different peace agreements with paramilitary and guerrilla groups, at the national level. However, one of the characteristics of the different governments that have happened in these decades in Colombia has been the inertia, which has been the indifference to the events that occurred to thousands of victims related to the armed conflict.The armed conflict has been lived, on the screens of their television sets, by an important part of Colombians inhabitants of the big cities.
