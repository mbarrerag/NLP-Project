# PROPOSAL FOR PHYSIOTHERAPEUTIC TREATMENT IN THE PHYSICAL DISACONDITIONING ACQUIRED IN THE INTENSIVE ADULT CARE UNIT

It is estimated that approximately 13 to 20 million people annually worldwide require life support in intensive care unit (ICU) Adult due to present risk factors such as: prolonged immobilization (made by critical health status, complicated surgery, severe trauma or oxygenation compromises), administration of sedatives or medicines that negatively affect muscle fibers, hypermetabolic and catabolic state (sepsis, shock, multiorgan failure), hyperglycaemia and mechanical ventilation; which can develop multisystemic alterations that are expressed with clinically significant muscle weakness, altered cardiac response to mild stress, orthostatic hypotension, alteration of cough mechanism, diaphragmatic weakness, ulcers by pressure and insulin resistance, these are some of the manifestations of the Physical Decomposition Syndrome (SDF) and additionally have been observed. Studies of prospective cohort in patients of the ICU have been evaluated in Spain (28%), compared to a low level of comorbidities and showed alterations in physical function and independence with prevalence of 43%2 and incidence of 48.6%8, and have been observed in the following studies of mortality (28%).
