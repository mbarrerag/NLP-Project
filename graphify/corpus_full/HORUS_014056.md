# Urban uses of sacred plants in therapeutic and ceremonial contexts in the debate on public policies against drugs: the case of the Muisca community in the town of Suba (1995-2023)

The use and consumption of enteogens (understood as the main type of substances modifying the consciousness used in contexts of shamanic societies and grouped in plants such as Yagé, Psilocybe fungi, Catus San Pedro and Peyote, among others) is considered a custom of universal and historical nature among ethnic peoples in continents such as America, Asia, Africa and Europe, both in their ancestral practices and in their contemporary western use, being these differentiated from other psychoactive substances by their ritualistic and cosmogonic character, as well as by their privileged use in ceremonial contexts under the category of _x0093_sacred plants_x0094_ and therapeutics recently subscribed to the world of science as psychology and medicine (generally grouped under the category of _x0093_psychodelicos_x0094_sepsocietal, so that their relevance is observed, on the one hand, at the global level, according to the world report on drugs 2023, because of the renewed interest in the use of enteogens (which is the general grouped under the category of _x0093_psicodetalicos_x94_____ S, 2019).
