# NOTES ON THE ECONOMY OF MISSIONS IN THE FRONTERIZED REGION OF THE COLOMBOUS-BRASILEN VAUPES, 1914-1975

This chapter provides a comparative picture between Colombia and Brazil of the economic activity, operation and financing of missions on the border between 1914 and 1975
