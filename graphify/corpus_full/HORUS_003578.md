# HYDROGEN PEROXIDE DECOMPOSITION IN PRESENCE OF CATALASS

The breakdown of hydrogen peroxide occurs according to the reaction shown below: 2H2O2 does 2H2O + O2 (g) The reaction is bi-molecular, but behaves as a reaction of order one. In the presence of catalysts, the rate of decomposition eaction increases. One of the methods to follow the development of the reaction is to determine the amount of oxygen produced during the reaction. As a gas is collected, it is necessary to consider that this type of assembly requires the use of sealed and special care systems to avoid leakage, especially if dangerous or irritant gases such as chlorine (Cl2) are present. In this practice, gaseous oxygen is to be generated. Detachment and gas collection will be used as a way to determine the gas generation rate, which allows learning how to use the method of gas collection on water to make stoichiometric calculations. In addition, some factors affecting the action of the catalyst, such as temperature increase, thermal denaturation and chemical denaturation, will be studied
