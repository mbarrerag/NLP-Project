# EXPERIENCE OF INFORMAL CAREERS IN COLOMBIA: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND METASINTHESS

Caregivers have a particular way of living the experience of caring, adapting to the health system that does not recognize them as caregivers, and that downloads in them tasks for which they are neither prepared nor accompanied in their trajectory. To integrate the findings of primary studies into a qualitative meta-synthesis to describe the experience of informal caregivers in Colombia. Systematic review of qualitative literature or qualitative metasynthesis. Systematic search in metasEARCHERS including descriptors: caregivers, experience, qualitative research, Colombia. The selection of studies was made based on the PRISMA methodology and the COREQ tool, available in the EQUATOR network. 20 qualitative primary studies were included, they were codified into 250 nominal codes, classified into 6 central categories: relationship between the day and their context, touching background to strengthen spiritually, delivery, overprotection and self-limitation, key moments to adapt to care, relationship with itself, health system, policies and institutional support. Caregivers share the experience of illness together with their loved one. The health system needs of the disabled need to be transformed to meet the needs of the needs of the sick in place of the disabled
