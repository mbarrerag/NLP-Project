# FOOD OF THE GOVERNMENT AND INVOLVEMENT IN MATERNAL HEALTH

The main reasons for the deterioration of maternal health are: One of the fundamental elements of maternal health care is adequate and healthy nutrition. During pregnancy, energy needs increase. To supplement these new demands, in addition to the establishment of an adequate diet, depends on the physical, social and economic access to nutritional foods of quality and sufficient quantity for pregnant women. Documentary review of scientific articles on maternal health and nutrition, in English, Spanish and Portuguese published in electronic databases between 2015 and 2021. Nutrition of pregnant women must be understood as a set of eating behaviours, attitudes and practices, as well as knowledge. Food practices must be interpreted in the light of the factors that condition them, sociodemographic, environmental, cultural and family characteristics could be determinants of this matter. The nutritional status of women before and during pregnancy is a determinant of risk for maternal morbidity, pregnancy duration and complications of childbirth. Maternal malnutrition, caused by insufficient or inadequate nutrition, is one of the main causes of maternal health deterioration
