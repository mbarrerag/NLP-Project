# Muscle atrophy parameters as predictors of clinical outcomes in patients in Intensive Care Unit.

The atrophy involves structural and functional alterations related to the production of muscle strength and movement. This condition has been reported as the main reason for the presence of generalised muscle weakness; it reflects the severity of the disease and can have a profound impact on the clinical results in the short and long term.The following were presented: extension of the time with ventilatory support, increase of the stay in Intensive Care Unit (ICU), decrease of the functional status, extension of the mortality figures, among others. To determine if the parameters of muscular atrophy by ultrasound predicting early muscle weakness, morbidity (days in Mechanical Ventilation (VM), days free of VM and days of stay in ICU), and mortality to 28 days in patients of the ICU of the National University Hospital of Colombia. Type of study: Observational study type prospective cohort. Patients older than 18 years with medical or surgical pathologies, requiring mechanical ventilation for more than 24 hours. Subjects with comorbidities that prevented the evaluation of muscular strength were considered were recorded as demographic variables, diagnosis, and risk factors for muscular weakness were included. Muscle ultrasound may be considered as a tool for early monitoring of muscle mass loss.
