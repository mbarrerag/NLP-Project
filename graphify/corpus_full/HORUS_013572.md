# On the Galois theories and categorical aspects of strongly normal extensions

The differential algebra studies differential equations from an algebraic point of view. It was introduced by Joseph Ritt, who stated that an algebraic-differential structure (ring, field, algebra) is an algebraic structure together with a finite set of derivatives. Subsequently, the notion of Strongly Normal Extension was introduced by Kolchin [11] and more recently by Jerald Kovacic [4,5].
