# Parallax VISIONS ON COLOMBIAN URBANIZATION: A CRITICAL BALANCE OF THE LAW OF THE FOREIGN URBANA LAND GEOGRAPHIES

The geographical research on the processes of Colombian urbanization, carried out in the country, has multiplied its presence at a great pace, at least for a decade. However, before the proliferation of post-gradual research, Colombia was a sort of urban laboratory explored and exploited with assiduousness by researchers and researchers who were part of urban geography schools with a long tradition in their contexts of origin: the United States, the United Kingdom and above all, France and Germany. In fact, the geographical analysis of such an atypical capital city in the continent as Bogota was fundamental for the production of the concept of Latin American city present in some of those.
