# Strengthening the technical and technological infrastructure of La Paz Headquarters laboratories.

The present project seeks to strengthen the network of laboratories at La Paz Headquarters, which are part of the National Laboratory System (SNL), by acquiring furniture for the storage of equipment, reagents, materials, and for student lockers to improve the operation and procedures of teaching, research (semilleros and research groups) and extension activities, of biological and chemical properties analysis laboratories (LAPBQ) and of physics and prototyping (LFP). It is also hoped to strengthen the network of laboratories at La Paz Headquarters, by diagnosing the existing needs in quality management, environmental management and safety and health, processes and operating procedures for teaching, research and extension activities, in order to start implementing standards related to good laboratory practices, for quality purposes, by hiring three student assistants, to support the diagnosis to be led by a professional woman employed by the Academic Directorate through the Component 607-C4 project. Strategies for dissemination and appropriation of the results of the project will be implemented with members of the university community using the laboratories of La Paz headquarters
