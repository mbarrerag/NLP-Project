# MECHANOUANTIC STRUCTURE STUDY AND MOLECULES SPECTER CAPSULED IN FULLERENOS.

The encapsulation of molecules within structures such as fullerenes or clatrates has opened new opportunities to explore the properties of host-host systems, with potential applications in nanoscience and nanotechnology. The nature of the interactions of the encapsulated molecule with its environment can modify the energy structure of the isolated molecule and mix the vibrational, translational and rotational modes that were decoupled in the gaseous state thus appearing a new set of energy levels. One of the most striking effects is the loss of the degeneration of the lower excited rotational state in the systems H 2@C60, HD@C60, HF@C60 and H2O@C60 suggesting the rupture of the icosopheran symmetry.[1] Here we present theoretical studies of the endohedrales H2O@C60 and HF@C60. Based on the calculations of the spectroscopy of these systems we propose different models to include the anisotropia of the confinant environment.
