# Odontologic emergencies and emergencies

Despite advances in science, the comprehensive dentist and health services will continue to be confronted with clinical behavior and management of endodontic and dental emergencies. Notable advances in the different areas of knowledge have allowed the production of a large amount of information. Therefore, the creation of this electronic book in a simple-formual format based on the best evidence available especially for dental service providers, legislators and clinicians of the emergency service. The purpose of this text is to present to the different groups of people involved in the topic of emergency and dental emergencies, formal guidelines or explicit criteria (guides of management) (with the best evidence available)
