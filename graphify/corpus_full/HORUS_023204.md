# Diversity and gene flow in four Colombian turtle populations of Podocnemis vogli (Testudines: Podocnemididae)

The turtle Sabanera Podocnemis vogli is a species restricted to the sheets of the Orinoco basin in Colombia and Venezuela. Its populations have decreased and disappeared in some places due to the illegal exploitation and alteration and destruction of its habitat due to human activities. This species has not been evaluated globally by the International Union for the Conservation of Nature (IUCN), but it is categorized into minor concern (LC) at regional level and is regulated under appendix II of the CITES convention. With the objective of analyzing its conservation status using molecular tools for the first time, 19 microsatellite loci were evaluated by means of the technique of cross amplification, as well as sequences of the mitochondrial gene (mtDNA) Cyt b, in five populations located in Puerto Carreño (Vichada), Paz de Paz polítbolte (Casanare), Puerto López (Meta), Unillanos (Villavice, Meta), San Martin (Meta) and 26 individuals in captivity in the Tropical Biology Station Roberto Franco (EBTMF).
