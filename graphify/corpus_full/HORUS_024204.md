# FOR EQUITATIVE ASSESSMENTS IN MERITOCRATIC PROCESS

The classic idea of standardization in the application of tests used for evaluation in different applied areas of psychology sought to carefully control the conditions, both content and form. This can be understood from two perspectives: 1) the prevailing idea of equal treatment for all, as a way of guaranteeing equality between the evaluated and 2) the need to control all variables that could affect evaluation. In practice, this translates into the preparation of the same questions or tasks for the evaluated and under conditions of equal application for all, as a way of ensuring that differences in results between the evaluated can be attributed to differences between them, in what it wants to be measured, not to other variables. This chapter discusses this idea and shows the need to reflect it in favour of seeking inclusive and equitable assessments. The approach from universal design with use of technology can provide options with that objective
