# Teaching rational numbers in response to inclusion and diversity from universal design for learning (DUA)

The degree work focuses on applying the principles of Universal Design for Learning (DUA) to improve the teaching of rational numbers in sixth grade of the Educational Institution Villa del Socorro in Medellín, integrating these principles with the guidelines of the Ministry of National Education and Decree 1421 of 2017 to focus on inclusion and diversity. The problem lies in the persistence of traditional methods in mathematics teaching, despite new technologies and tools. The mixed methodology combined qualitative and quantitative analysis, beginning with a diagnosis of students to design a contextualized didactic intervention. The results show that the DUA approach facilitates inclusion and improvement of understanding of rational numbers, providing multiple forms of involvement, representation, action and expression. The study highlights the importance of an inclusive and adaptable educational environment, using interdisciplinary activities and diverse resources to develop mathematical competencies in everyday contexts. .
