# Articulation and efficiency of the various forms of property derived from the delimitation of the Black Line contained in Decree 1500 of 2018: conservation of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta

The issues on land ownership in Colombia are one of the most problematic factors of the country. The wasteful goods in Colombia are those real estate by nature that are owned by the nation and lack economic exploitation by a private individual, these lands cannot be acquired by the original acquisition modes that are prescription and occupation, are those that, as Velázquez Jaramillo (2014) says, are expecting a settler for the first time. Although they cannot be acquired by the original modes, they can be awarded to natural persons, community enterprises and peasant cooperatives, according to what has been established for each region or municipality by the Institute of Rural Development, which applied until 2015, now they are awarded by the National Land Agency; they can also be awarded to public law entities for works with social interest purposes (Velásquez Jaramillo, 2014). The right of dominion has been permeated by the social changes and the decline of the original ideas of the legislative that conceived it at the time.
