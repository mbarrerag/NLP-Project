# Quantum algorithm for the reconstruction of spin states a medium.

The estimation of quantum states is an important task that is used in multiple quantum information protocols. This work is considered a family of unitary evolution operators dependent on a parameter (two parameters) that allow the estimation of a spin component (all spin components) of a two-tier system. The quantum tomography transfer function (qTTF), which corresponds to the trace of the inverse of Fisher's information matrix, is used to quantify the estimator's performance. In this work, the qTTF is optimized for the two estimators. The minimum qTTF of a parameter model is reached when the power of interlacing of the associated unit operator is maximum. The two models are simulated in an IBM quantum processing unit. While the implementation of the model for estimating a component works satisfactorily, the model for the total estimation of the spin shows large errors due to the associated circuit depth
