# Photograph and anticipation

The research-creation proposal that we present part of the research question: How do the narratives that we imagine daily to anticipate what has not yet happened, that is, to the future, can be structured as a photographic project? This question arises from the interest that we have in the "Semillero of visual narratives" to explore, from the photographic image and visual narrative, the topic of "anticipation." To address the research question, we have proposed a collective work of reflection and discussion around the topic pointed out, from this point of view, to have a theoretical and conceptual basis that allows the development of individual photographic projects. Specifically, this proposal starts from the hypothesis that the narratives that we create mentally and imaginaryly from anticipation can materialize in a visual narrative project via photography. This means that, it is possible to construct visual narratives based on the mental narratives that we imagine daily when we seek to anticipate the future
