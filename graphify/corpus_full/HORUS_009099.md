# L?NVENZIONE DI UNEROTICA SOLVERSIVA DEL CORPO (the invention of a SUBVERSIVE AEROTIC OF THE BODY)

There is an illusory but legalistic practice of calling oneself clinical in certain psi practices, believing that being clinical is diagnosing with manuals and intervening with protocols and guidelines. But the clinic is to accompany the other from a listening to his suffering, from his discomfort, is to listen to the pathos of the subject, not standardized criteria that become fixed disorders. The clinic is what in one subject can follow the path of his desire, inquiring in himself through the listening of another. Psychoanalysis allows the rewriting of the destiny of a subject from a reading of his place in the unconscious Other, from the fatality of destiny to a knowing how to do with that destiny to come
