# CHECK AND TEXTUAL COHESION PROBLEMS AND ITS EFFECTS ON GLOBAL COMPREHENSION PROCESS

This research investigated the relationship between problems of textual coherence and cohesion and performance in the global textual understanding. The methodology was mixed: quantitative-descriptive of quasi-experimental design while the hypothesis was tested from an experiment with 3 population and qualitative groups, since some erroneous answers from the reading test of students with good academic averages are interpreted with the content analysis methodology. Statistical results show that the problems of coherence and cohesion do not affect the overall understanding in students of higher grades such as those of grade 11 or university, but yes for students of lower grades (5o). Qualitatively, it is evident that certain errors that are presented in constant form in the responses of good students may be induced by the characteristics of the text
