# Method of compression of image files using deep learning techniques.

In recent years, internet traffic has been dominated by applications related to image and video files, especially content streaming services and video distribution applications on demand. More than three quarters of total internet traffic correspond to image and video files. That these tasks are as efficient as possible has a direct impact on the user experience and the quality of the service provided. Preserving the quality of this user experience is the main objective in the development of these compression systems, as well as the point where these systems can most weaken. That is why minimizing the distortion or loss of information generated in the process of compression of a file is a priority and a matter that has tried to address from various perspectives and methods throughout history. This work focuses on those recent proposals where deep learning or Deep Learning plays a major role in this process, proposing a neural network-based method to address the problem of image file compression, showing the research carried out, the development of the method and its testing
