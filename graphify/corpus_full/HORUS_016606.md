# VALIDATION OF BESTEST IN POPULATION OF COLOMBIAN ADVANTAGES

Aging and comorbidities affect the cognition and sensoriomotor system of people. Loss of balance is one of the main causes of falls in older adults, so monitoring their functional performance is important. The equilibrium evaluation system identifies risk factors for falls. Identifying the psychometric properties of validity and reliability among evaluators and estimating the optimal cut value of the BESTEST to differentiate between adults who have or do not risk of falls. Observational study of validity and reliability in which 221 adults between 50 and 80 years of age participated able to walk, with or without assistive device, independently for six meters and to perform tasks of balance without fatigue. Sociodemographic, clinical and history of falls were investigated. A significant correlation was found between the equilibrium system and the scale of confidence in daily activities (r=0.47, p<0.01), an internal consistency suitable for the overall test (Cronbach alpha of 0.90)., a sensitivity of 78% and a breakdown of 66%.
