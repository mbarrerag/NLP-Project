# Music therapy for the management of behavior in dental care of children and adolescents with motor deficiency of cerebral origin

The Motor Impairment of Cerebral Origin (IMOC) is a non-progressive disorder caused by lesions in the immature brain, which generates difficulties in the transmission of signals to the muscles, affecting its functioning and movement. This research addresses the influence of the musicotherapy in the management of the behavior of patients with IMOC during the dental prophylaxis procedure in the Oral Health Service of the HOMI Foundation Hospital Paediatric of Mercy, located in the city of Bogotá. The study was carried out jointly with the aspirant to the pediatric stomatology and Orthopedia Maxilar. The qualitative component included focal sessions with a case study approach, direct observation through the odontological consultations. A mixed research design, called exploratory mixed methods, was used, which allows the integration of qualitative and quantitative data in a sequential manner.
