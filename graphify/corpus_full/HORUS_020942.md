# Biology and ecology of Frankliniella panamensis and Frankliniella occidentalis (Thysanoptera: Thripidae) in a rose crop for export from the Bogotá Sabana: contributions for surveillance and decision making for the phytosanitary management of thrips in ornamentals

The doctoral thesis approved by the Faculty Council and entitled "Biology and Ecology of Frankliniella panamensis and Franliniella occidentais: contributions for its surveillance and control in cut flower crops in the Bogotá Sabana, Colombia " proposes a research work in greenhouses of commercial production of cut flowers for export in the Bogotá Sabana, the area that represents 79% of ornamental production in Colombia and the second in export for the world. The proposal consists of obtaining a baseline knowledge on the biology and ecology of two species of thrips considered as pest of flower crops in Colombia, which correspond to Frankliniella occidentalis (Pergande, 1895) and Frankliniella panamensis Hood 1925 (Thysanoptera: Thripidae). F. occidentalis is a species cosmopolitan in the world, well adapted to crops in greenhouse, but with little information of the biology and ecology of the populations in Colombia. F. panamensis is a species of the Neotropic, of quarantine importance for the floricultor business.
