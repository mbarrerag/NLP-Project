# CHAPTER 6: THE ROLE OF COLOR IN THE QUALITY OF FOOD

Color is a determining quality factor for the choice of fresh or matured meat. This characteristic is used by consumers as an indicator of freshness and hygiene, as there are colors that indicate deterioration, such as brown discolorations of meat kept in storage for long periods of time, green rings that are generated due to bacterial growth or roasted meats that in the oven do not brown. The reaction that the consumer has to a meat cut is conditioned by the sensation experienced when perceiving its color. Thus, it can reject it without considering other characteristics, or accept it, going to consider the goodness of others (ternity, juices and taste).The color is influenced by the interaction of many factors: the genetics of the animals, the pre and postmortem environmental conditions, the chemical of the muscle, the factors related to the processing of the meat (packed, distribution, storage, presentation) and, finally, the preparation for the color (Mancini and Hunt, 2005).
