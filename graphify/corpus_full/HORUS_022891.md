# LATIN AMERICAN INTERREGIONALISM AND CARIBBEAN - EUROPEAN UNION TO THE END OF POST-HEGEMONY.

This chapter explains the meaning and characteristics of the present inter-regionalism between the two regions.The central argument is that, built through interactions and initiatives from each region, the interregional relations Latin America and the Caribbean-European Union involve in the contemporary global space both the regulation of economic exchanges between the countries of the two regions, and the management of political affairs
