# INFLUENCE OF THE SPATIAL COHERENCE PUMP LASER ON PCF SUPERCONTINUUM GENERATION

The impact of spatial coherence on supercontinuum generation in photonic crystal fibers is experimentally investigated using a rotating ground glass diffuser within a two-lens configuration, exploring variations in spectral width and envelope shape
