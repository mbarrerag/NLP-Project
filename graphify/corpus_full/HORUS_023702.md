# ENZIMATIC BUSCEMENT: CONTROL AND APPROACHMENT OF ANTIOXIDANTS FOR APPLICATION IN MINIMUMLY PROCESSED FRUIT AND HORTALIZES.

The book addresses the theme of the use of agroindustrial waste in both the food and non-food sectors. The result of this exhibition is reflected in this document, which from the curricular perspective proposes the Agroindustrial Engineering program of the Faculty of Engineering and Administration of the National University of Colombia - Sede Palmira. This document is structured into nine chapters, organized from a priority approach to understanding the outcome of the production chain, towards decanting in various alternatives specific to the use of the byproduct. Chapter 4 What is enzyme management: control and use of antioxidants for the application of minimally processed fruits and vegetables? by Prof. Saul Dussán Sarria, deals, from an academic perspective, with the oxidation of MP vegetables that causes tissue obscuration, control with antioxidants and a review of the most important research in this regard
