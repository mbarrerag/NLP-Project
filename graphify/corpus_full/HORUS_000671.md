# On the RBV(p,α)0([a,b]) variation space as the dual space of L(p,α)([a,b])

In this paper we study some properties of the space RBV(p,α)0([a,b]). Among other, we proved that the dual space of L(p,α)([a,b]) may be identified with the space RBV(p,α)0([a,b])
