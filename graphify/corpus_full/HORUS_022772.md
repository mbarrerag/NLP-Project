# Some articles for the production of obedience: from the small Glossary of Acaemical Anti-TELECTISM

The institution of the university sees itself, with increasing insistence, as an organization that, when it does not form labour for the labour market, is engaged in the production of goods and services that integrate themselves to the pace of the knowledge economy. The ideological basis of these changes has been, as predicted by Bill Readings three decades ago, the emptying of the political and cultural content of the university, which in turn has allowed its management at all levels.The new academic jargon has been introduced into the university as a Trojan horse: it is composed of terms that seem harmless, even reasonable, but contain an army of values that transform from the root the concept of education at all levels.The current expansion of economic thinking has ended up turning the businessman into the ethical model by excellence, and his ideology has penetrated deep into the consciousness of all, including academics and intellectuals.The main motor of human actions is, according to the prevailing ideology, the individual benefit; the competition is the principle that governs social relations.The small-scale vision of the small-scale glossary that only the abstract is strengthened here is concentrated in terms of the university
