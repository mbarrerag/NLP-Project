# Evaluation of a theology program in virtual modality at the University Foundation Biblical Seminary of Colombia (2022)

This research evaluates the virtual theology program of the FUSBC based on a contextualization of theology and pedagogy in Latin America, particularly the theology of the integral mission and critical pedagogies, and a conceptualization of virtual education, exploring the definitions of Simonson and García Aretio. The subjects were evaluated The church and community development, Introduction to the New Testament, Christian Theology I, Life Cycle and Ecclesial Administration, through interviews with students, teachers and mentor, and the researcher’s own observation. They were established as criteria for evaluation versus the content: the type of material employed in the course, the structure of the course in the platform, the depth of the course and the strategy for delivering content; and with respect to the teaching and learning relationship: the interaction of the material with the syllabus, the interaction of the teacher with the student, the interaction of the student with the other students and the interaction with the evaluation.
