# Determination of in-vitro conditions for the expansion of memory T Stem cells for cell adoption therapy.

The adoption therapy of cells has the potential to increase antitumor immunity, by modifying in-vitro cells to expand lymphocytes that recognize and attack the tumor. The functional capacity and survival of cells transferred to the patient will depend on memory subpopulations that are expanded in the laboratory, so obtaining poorly differentiated memory cells is desirable. The main objective of this work was to determine an in-vitro expansion strategy of CD4 stem T lymphocytes of human memory. Starting from virgin cells stimulated by a polyclonal agent and adding different combinations of cytokines of the common gamma family, it was found that the combination of IL-7, IL-15 and IL-21 or IL-7 and IL-21 were the cocktails that produced a greater number of cells with memory phenotype stem, measured by flow cytometry. In addition, through the measurement of membrane proteins and insyllic analysis, a close relationship of the Tstem lymphocytes of memory was established with the memory of lymphocyte-ts, which we consider to be the best infernometic processes
