# Development of a digital scientific literacy card on teaching renewable energies with a CTSA approach for middle school students

This grade work has been developed with the purpose of producing a digital literacy card on renewable energy education with a CTSA (Science, Technology, Society and Environment) approach to middle school students (grades 10° and 11°), which was tested with a pilot group of students from the San Rafael Arcángel College of the city of Neiva-Huila. The digital booklet is the result of research focused on analysis, understanding and interpretation as it is framed in the collection of data for its elaboration by containing an original creative development and taking into account a pedagogy oriented towards middle school students. It also serves as a guide tool for teachers in the teaching process – learning content in subjects such as renewable energy, uses and applications; moreover, to create awareness on current topics of environmental and scientific relevance, facing the various risks of transformation and evolution facing society, and which are related to the energy and environmental crisis.
