# Strengthening sustainable management of tropical dry forest and livelihoods in the municipality of Becerril (Cesar)

The department of Cesar in Colombia houses important ecosystems such as dry forests, tropical forests and wetlands, as well as areas of great cultural and ecological importance.However, it faces serious environmental problems such as soil degradation, deforestation and unsustainable agricultural activity.These problems have caused the loss of ecosystem services and biodiversity, negatively affecting the quality of life of people in the region.In addition, their situation has been aggravated by the armed conflict and the presence of illegal armed groups, which has led to the loss of livelihoods and forced displacement.After the peace agreements with illegal groups, the peasant communities are returning to the territory but face a lack of institutional support and present many basic needs unsatisfied.The project aims to generate socio-ecological information and biological diversity of the guáimaro to strengthen initiatives in progress through the sustainable management of the tropical dry forest (in which the latter is used to improve the livelihoods of local communities, productive processes and biodiversity conservation in the municipality of Becerril (Cesar).This project is developed around the community project in question, in which the area of the forest is used (the wild) and in which the forest is used to improve a species. The project, the identification of areas of interest for the study of the guáimaro, the preparation of nurseries focused on the guáimaro and the socialization of the results with the community. It is expected that this interdisciplinary proposal from the academy, in partnership with local actors and the National Institute of Biodiversity: Humboldt Institute, in addition to supporting the training of graduate and undergraduate students, will achieve improvement in the quality of life of the inhabitants of the region through good management of natural resources and the strengthening of an environmentally sustainable livelihood
