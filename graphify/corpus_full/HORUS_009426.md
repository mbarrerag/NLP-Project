# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF A FRACTION WITH ANSIOLITIC ACTIVITY BEGINNED FROM THE VALERIAN VALERIAN RAIT RUIZ & PAV. (CAPRIFOLYACEAE)

The chemical characterization of the dichloromethane fraction of roots of Valeriana pilosa was performed. Preliminary analysis detected iridoids and terpenes. Using Nuclear Magnetic Resonance and Mass Spectrometry, two iridoids were characterized, one of them not previously reported, a phytosterol, a lignane and three valepotriate degradation products
