# Measurement of physical processes in education as a condition of potential to improve quality of education

This symposium presents three works in the area of psychological and educational measurement, the first corresponds to the analysis of an instrument to measure the teaching performance by students making use of the Model of Graduate Response of Samejima. A second work on the measurement of memory and attention in primary children making use of the cognitive diagnostic model and, finally, the measurement of one of the processes that has been pointed out has greater association with learning: motivation, this instrument is analyzed with Classical Theory of Test and Theory of Response to the Item. The symposium shows the research work that contributes to the development of the fourth objective of the Sustainable Development Goals, (SDG) that is expressed by the quality education, and that seeks to guarantee an inclusive and equitable education of quality and promote opportunities for lifelong learning for all. (United Nations, 2018 p. 27). This objective is composed of 10 specific goals, the first goal is to ensure that all girls and all children complete primary and secondary education, which must be free, equitable and of quality and produce results of learning relevant and effective (United Nations, 2018 p. 27). This goal is presented by the three goals and higher employment is also intended to ensure that all the last part of primary and secondary education is completed
