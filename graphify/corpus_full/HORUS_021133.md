# Digital three-dimensional model and technical specifications for a Guadua cover. Case study, multisport plate of the centrality of the Vereda Mazotes of the Municipality of Cocorná

The use of environmentally friendly materials, which are typical of some territories and are easy to manage and repair by the inhabitants, is a priority objective in the projects of habitability in rural areas. In the centrality of the village of Mazotes in the municipality of Cocorná, there is a sports board that requires a cover to improve its use by the community and there is also an important presence of Guadua and a tradition of use among the inhabitants of the sidewalk. This becomes a potentiality and an opportunity to improve the conditions of the infrastructure, using vernacular materials that are easily appropriate by the community and allow for its maintenance in time, in this way its sustainability is guaranteed
