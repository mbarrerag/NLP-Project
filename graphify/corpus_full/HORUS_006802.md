# The hidden workshops of the city: reproduction of urban space and care in urban planning of Medellín (2005-2020)

This research explores what is the organisation of social and spatial reproduction of the city, from care and urban planning. It investigates the condition of women in their urban daily life and how it relates to the planning exercises that have been developed in the city. The above is investigated in a specific context, between 2005 and 2020, period in which urban planning in Medellín has had a significant boom and a much more marked political exercise, in the same way as the years in which the information of the censuses and statistical surveys manage to portray with greater precision what happens with women in the city. Through the study of the labour market of women in Medellín, of the elements that determine the organization of urban care and the provisions of the territorial planning plans, it is found in the theories of social reproduction and the critical-feminist geography a dialogue necessary to understand the economic burdens of the workplace and the care of women.
