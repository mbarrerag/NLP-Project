# INCIDENCE OF EARLY WITHDRAWAL OF SUBDERMICAL IMPLANTS BOGOTÁ, COLOMBIA.

To establish the incidence and causes of early withdrawal of subdermal contraceptive implants in women of childbearing age. A mixed observational study in a service provider in Bogotá between June and December 2016 in a cohort of women who decided to implant subdermal contraceptives. The outcome variable was the time of withdrawal of the implant during the first six months of use and a phenomenological qualitative hermeneutic study was conducted with focal groups to establish the causes of early withdrawal. The risk of withdrawal was calculated by means of incidence density and qualitative analysis using Stata 13 and Invivo respectively. The median age was 23 years. During the first six months of follow-up, 107 women of 1782 women decided to make the removal of the subdermal device for an incidence density of 3.17 withdrawals for 10,000 days of use (accumulative incidence: 6.0%).The causes of withdrawal most frequently stated were: alteration of the menstrual cycle, headache and weight increase.The similar groups revealed the need to improve the quality of the consultation performed to all women before the implantation are affected by the early removal
