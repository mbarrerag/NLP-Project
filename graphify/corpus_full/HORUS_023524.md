# PREDITION OF DIVORCE BY INTELLIGENT TECHNICAL MEANS

The aim of this paper is to estimate the relationships between the most important variables that can lead to divorce. Weka machine learning and data mining platform is used, which uses the classification algorithm (J48), which is of behavior similar to a Bayesian algorithm. Initially, an existing database is modeled, with 173 records and 55 variables, of which 54 are independent and a dependent variable (divorce). Independent variables that produce a moderate influence on the dependent variable are selected. Additionally, a second reduction of variables is performed through a statistical selection process. As a result, the four most influential variables are identified with a 100% success when a partition percentage of more than 90% is used. It is concluded that through the decision tree, the most important variables that can cause a divorce process can be observed clearly and concisely
