# Economic evaluation of procalcitonin as a guide to antibiotic therapy for acute respiratory infection in adults in a referral hospital in Colombia 2019. Cost effectiveness analysis.

Acute respiratory infection represents a high burden of disease for hospital institutions, this pathology is associated with a high consumption of antibiotics due to its difficulty in the etiological diagnosis. The evidence in Procalcitonin on the other hand, suggests being a safe and effective biomarker as a guide for antibiotic treatment in acute respiratory conditions. To determine if the use of procalcitonin as a guide for antibiotic therapy is Effective Cost in Acute Respiratory Infection in a referral hospital in Colombia for the year 2019. Integrative study, type Economic Evaluation, as a source of effectiveness a systematic review of the literature was carried out and as a source of costs was performed a cost per type, with a final analysis using a model of simulation of Decision Tree.
