# VALIDITY AND CONFIDABILITY OF THE TINETTI SCALE FOR COLOMBIAN POPULATION

The falls, are the fourth geriatric syndrome with higher prevalence, increase morbidity and mortality, decrease functionality, and prematurely increase the income to geriatric households. The alterations of the march and balance are the main factors that generate them, with a range of adjusted values of relative risk between 1.2-2.2 and 1.2-2.4, respectively. The scale of Tinetti of march and balance has proved to be a valid and reliable tool for the evaluation of mobility (r 0.74-0.93), moreover, it has a high inter-observer reliability (0.95). To validate the Scale of Tinetti, from its original version, for the use in Colombian population. The scale of Tinetti in its Spanish version was applied by two senior evaluators, residents of three geriatric institutions of the city of Medellín and two municipalities of the Valley of Aburrá with different degrees of functionality in the march and in the balance.
