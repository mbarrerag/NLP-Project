# BIBLE STUDIES ON STRATEGIC PLANNING AND THEIR INFLUENCE IN SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT FROM THE PERSPECTIVE OF HIGHER EDUCATION.

The contributions from the higher education field to the topic of sustainable development have been made since the 1970s, mainly from the summits, global declarations and treaties established in the United Nations Organization, where the concept of sustainable development was incorporated in various fields and for the case of higher education was not the exception. Strategic planning in higher education institutions (HEIs) is due to variations in budgets, technological and demographic changes, globalization and the obligation to account to public sector bodies. In this sense, the aim of this work is to show an overview of strategic planning in HEIs in favour of sustainable development from the literature indexed in the Scopus® database for the period 1992 to 2022. For this, documentary research was carried out using the bibliometric study and considering the variables of strategic planning, institutions of higher education and sustainable development. The results show a trend to increase in publications, with the majority being scientific articles concentrated in the area of social sciences.
