# Local mechanisms of acupuncture (acupuncture) a vision from molecular biology.

The approach of Western Medicine to the Acupuncture to elucidate its mechanisms of action dates back to the 70s. Although much progress has been made to date, the little dissemination about “how” exerts its effects the Acupuncture has favored a tortuous relationship with modern medicine. This brief work focuses on contributing to the dissemination of its mechanisms of action, specifically describing what we know today about the point of acupuncture, from structural morphology to its physiology
