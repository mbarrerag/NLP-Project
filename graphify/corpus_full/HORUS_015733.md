# : CHAPTER IX INEGRAL CARE OF THE PERSON CONCERNED BY TUBERCLESIS

Chapter This chapter addresses the different ways of providing treatment to people diagnosed with tuberculosis, explains the strategies for comprehensive care for people affected by tuberculosis in vulnerable populations affected by tuberculosis, takes into account the ethnic, gender and differential approach, considers priority intervention activities in populations affected by tuberculosis and belonging to vulnerable groups and explains the importance of follow-up through the information system of the Tuberculosis Programme. Records are addressed for patient follow-up through the Information System of the Tuberculosis Programme, works on the rights and duties of users, an important aspect for work is the educational process aimed at the person affected by tuberculosis. Likewise, emphasis is placed on the comprehensive care of the person affected by tuberculosis in the home. It is reiterated in the main activities of tuberculosis prevention, taking into account the Tuberculosis Control Programme: in the Hospital; individually. At the end of the article, the corresponding bibliographic references are given
