# The montage in the writer's house: composition, analysis and experience at Casa García Márquez, Rogelio Salmona, Cartagena, 1991-1996.

This research addresses the route in architecture, postulating its importance not only as a subproduct of design, but as a project in itself. Through the use of "mountage" as an analytical tool, this thesis allows to understand the composition of the architectural project as parts that are independently accommodated and related through the route. Likewise, it shows how strategically arranged spatial fragments can evoke emotions and meanings. This approach allows to understand the experience of the journey beyond a simple physical displacement, as a practice deeply linked to knowledge, based on the interaction with the environment, the moments of reflection and the memories it generates. For this research it is taken as a case study the Casa García Márquez in Cartagena (1991-1996), projected by Rogelio Salmona, which illustrates in an exemplary way the composition from fragments, the flexibility of the route and the narrative construction of the ascending route. .
