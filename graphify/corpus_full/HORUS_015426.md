# Increased production of biological production of biological probabilities by supplementation with inulin

The design of probiotic food matrices requires in the first instance the production of microbial biomass in sufficient quantity, in such a way that it manages to remain viable during the application of the operations necessary to obtain the final product. The objective of this research was to seek better yields in the production of probiotic lactic biomass of the Lactobacillus rhamnosus strain by supplementing with inulin. The probiotic strain was inoculated in MRS broth and in the final stage of exponential growth, inulin was incorporated in a concentration of 10g/L. The kinetic parameters in the growth curve of L. rhamnosus were evaluated, by adjusting the data of Cologne Forming Units (UFC) to the kinetic model of Gompertz. The parameters of latency (¿) and generation time showed a decrease of 15% and 21% respectively, while the growth rate increased by 26%, for fermentation with the addition of inulin comparatively with the non-supplemented.
