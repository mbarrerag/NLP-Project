# Assessment of the effects of seismic waves on the local dynamic response in the northern Bogotá basin.

The effects of seismic waves on the local dynamic response of the sedimentary basin of the north of the city of Bogotá were evaluated, using models of unidimensional dynamic response (1D), which evaluate the effects on the soil response due to the cutting waves that propagate vertically from the rock bed to the surface and two-dimensional models (2D), which take into account the variable stratigraphy of the soil in depth and the conditions of lateral containment of the lake deposit by the presence of the Eastern Hill and the Suba Hill. For the dynamic response analyses the computer programs DEEPSOIL (1D) and MIDAS GTS NX, two-dimensional computation program applied to the Geotechnical Engineering using the finite elements method of the data used by the University of the Eastern Information System, the EBAS of the City of Bogotá were used for the geotechnical analysis and the results of laboratory tests carried out for different engineering projects
