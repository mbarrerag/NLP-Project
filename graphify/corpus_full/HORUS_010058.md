# ANALYSIS OF MAIN COMPONENTS (ACP) FOR RANGE WITH REGARD TO THEIR LIFE QUALITY

It is essential that, for the development of any society, there be an adequate standard of quality of life that allows citizens to make steady and sustained progress over time. In order to evaluate this, the ACP is used to calculate an objective index based on information related to mortality, birth, access to basic goods, education and economic growth, which generates an order of priority among a group of countries analyzed
