# phenological type-state chart in Hass avocado for the town of Roldanillo, Valle del Cauca

This project is funded by the Science, Technology and Innovation Fund of the General System of Regalías of the National Planning Department and aims to benefit producers in the areas of the hillside of Valle del Cauca. It is aimed at increasing sustainable competitiveness in agriculture of the hillside of Valle del Cauca, through research and development processes in the different links of the productive chain, ranging from the initial stage of cultivation to the agro-industrial stage of the three selected fruit trees: pineapple md-2, avocado Hass and mora of Castile. -- Contracadas
