# World trip: what is built with a backpack. Educational experiences and meaningful learning in non-school environments

This research aims to carry out a process of systematization and case analysis, based on learning experiences and knowledge that are built individually and collectively, during land travel around the world, in people known as backpackers; with the purpose of demonstrating the importance of learning outside the classroom by putting this into discussion with the Colombian educational system, with special emphasis on the level of higher education. . In the present investigation, it is intended to carry out the process of systematization and case analysis, based on learning experiences and knowledge that are built individually and collectively, during trips by land around the world, in people known as backpackers; with the aim of demonstrating the importance of learning outside of school, putting this into discussion with the Colombian educational system, with special emphasis on the level of higher education
