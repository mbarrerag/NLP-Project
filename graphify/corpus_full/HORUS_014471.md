# Strengthening processes associated with metric thinking from problem solving in eleventh-year-old students.

This paper seeks to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with metric thinking in students of eleventh grade of the Departmental Educational Institution Miguel Samper Agudelo, Guaduas - Cundinamarca, based on a series of learning activities based on problem solving. The approach of the work is qualitative in nature with a descriptive scope, in which it is also sought to investigate emerging skills in relation to the object of study. The methodological design is based on the theory of design where the development and experience gained in each activity has an impact on the continuous improvement of subsequent activities. Among the results achieved are advances in the following: comprehensive approach to magnitude (identification of the magnitude susceptible of measurement in an object, recognition of the number and unit ratio to indicate a measure, the appreciation of the range of a magnitude); optimization of processes and refinement of measuring instruments (selection of instruments and processes suitable in the approach of strategies to capture measures, capture of continuous measures based on discretization strategies); critical perspective of the measure (discovering the social background of measurement, decision-making, relation of measurement with other fields of mathematics and processes taken between other fields of mathematics)
