# Extinctive Limitation of Actions in the Insurance Contract and the Fiscal Liability Process in Colombia

The link between insurance companies and the processes of fiscal responsibility in Colombia, is the subject of important legal discussions between the insurance and reinsurer sector, the Comptroller General of the Republic and the jurisdictional authorities, when we refer to the term of limitation of the insurance contract, and the determination of the period to be taken into account by the legal operator when linking the insurer to the tax process. This, due to the absence of specific regulation or, in its absence, due to the lack of case law that unifies the variety of interpretations that have arisen on the subject.
