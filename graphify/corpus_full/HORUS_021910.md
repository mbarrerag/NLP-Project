# Air pollution by particulate matter in an urban and mountain territory. Case study Valle de Aburá, Colombia.

The particulate material is a polluting aerosol constituted by microscopic particles that represent a risk to health by intrusion into the respiratory system and even the bloodstream.The characterization of their emission sources, monitoring their presence in the urban atmosphere and understanding of the phenomena that facilitate their concentration or dispersion are vital for the management of air quality.In tropical cities these phenomena involve greater atmospheric dynamism in the daytime scale, where solar radiation determines very strong convective conditions.Now, when these tropical cities are contained by a mountain topography, the orographic forcing of the winds, the gradients of temperature and pressure, and the physical barrier of the mountains increase the complexity of the study of air quality and usually represent unfavourable conditions for the dispersion of the pollutants.
