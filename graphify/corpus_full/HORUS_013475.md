# Feminist and popular education to be and do(se) in the world: pedagogical practices of the Xisqua Educational Caracola during 2020-2022 in Bogotá D.C.

The valuable act of teaching and learning is not given merely in the classrooms; in the neighborhoods arise organizational processes that have been realized with a commitment to construct other possible realities from education, assuming it as the path or tool for social transformation. In the town of Bosa, in Bogotá, the community organization Caracola Educativa Xisqua developed processes of Feminist Popular Education since 2017, and was consolidated as a vital space for encounter, dialogue, affections and learning among women. The purpose of this qualitative research is to recognize and analyze the pedagogical practices of the Xisqua Educational Caracola together with the transformation scenarios that has been developed from popular and feminist pedagogies in the town of Bosa in Bogotá, during the period 2020-2022.
