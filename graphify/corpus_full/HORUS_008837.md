# PLASTICITY IN MAXIMUM CRITICAL TEMPERATURE, FRONT TO DIFFERENT CLIMATE CHANGE SCENIUM IN THE SPECIES GASTROTHECA AFF. RIOBAMBAE (ANTURE: HEMIPHRACIDAE)

Climate change can be due to the increase in environmental temperature by factors mainly of anthropic origin. In this context, understanding the thermal biology of organisms with a high potential affectation at high temperatures, such as high Andean amphibians, is crucial to determine efficient and effective management plans. In this sense, the Gastrotheca aff. riobambae frog, is distributed in mountainous areas of southern Colombia and northern Ecuador, since the tropical andes are zones, the different scenarios propose hyperthermias never previously recorded after the emergence of the Andes. The objective of the study was to evaluate the variation of the maximum critical temperature (CTmax) of Gastrotheca aff. riobambae tad., simulating potential hyperthermia scenarios, due to the global climate change. Three experiments were carried out submitting five renacuages to 0.25°C x min. 0.5°C x min., 1.0°C x min.
