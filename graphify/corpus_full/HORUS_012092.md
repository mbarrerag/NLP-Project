# Proposal and Experience of Integral Training of Human Resources for the Protection of Life from the Promotion and Prevention of Disease

The article describes a proposal and an experience that is made with students of Nursing and Nutrition careers who take the line of deepening in health promotion and prevention of the disease.The proposal is an attempt to construct alternative processes to recreate the academy through the integration of the three basic components of the teaching process—learning: theory, methodology and practice.Theoretically, the thematic axis revolves around the processes of health promotion and disease prevention with a focus of complexity and integrality.It is also contemplated, as part of the analysis, the historical-social, the social relations and the economic,political, environmental and cultural contexts of the population groups.In the methodological, it is proposed that the master classes be changed by spaces of exchange of knowledge.The preparation and development of each session is carried out by a group of students, which with sufficient anticipation prepares the session.Each group should achieve: recreate, analyze concepts, looking for depth in the topic.They must, in turn, propose and develop activities that lead to the motivation, creation, participation and commitment of all the students
