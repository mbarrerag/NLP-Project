# THE SPECIAL EDUCATION CENTRE FOR THE PAEDIATRIC HOSPITAL OF MERCY

It shows some contributions that the Paediatric Hospital of Mercy has made to the University in its relationship, in particular through the program of Occupational Therapy, the care of children with limitations; it tracks the scope and limitations of the model of rehabilitation applied to special education between the decades of the seventies and the nineties, as well as the incursion that from there was made in the late eighties and early nineties in the new perspectives that were configured towards educational integration as horizon of action in the national territory
