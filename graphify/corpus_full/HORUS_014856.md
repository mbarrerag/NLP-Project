# INCIDENCE OF ORGANIZATIONAL CLIMATE AND QUALITY OF LABOUR LIFE IN ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE

The results of the study show that the quality of life is relevant to the commitment of the workers to the tasks, and therefore consider that change can have positive consequences. In the research carried out by García y Forero (2016) on change and quality of life, it is clear that these variables have a positive correlation. Similarly, it is concluded that the quality of work life is relevant in the commitment of the people to carry out the tasks, as well as the definition of goals and objectives that are reflected in the productivity and the willingness of the associated climate in the process of change, but that the three studies do not report the variable relations of the individual, but that the results of the evaluation of the different aspects of the work and the results of the study are not the most important
