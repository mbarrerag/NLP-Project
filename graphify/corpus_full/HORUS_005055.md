# A LOOK AT THE THEORY OF VIRTUAL NUDE

In this talk we will do a theoretical review of the concept of virtual knots from a combinatory perspective. We will show how some concepts and results of classical theory of knots could extend to this "new" theory. We will focus on the group of a virtual knot, give some examples and some recent results
