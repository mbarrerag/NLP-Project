# Biotechnology for agroecological production and transition in coffee and cocoa production systems in peasant communities of Valle del Cauca and Nariño

The agroecological transition is a complex and profound process that interweaves in the territorialities of the communities, articulating in a multiscale and multidimensionality. Within the different scales we find: the farm, the community, the co-ordination, the municipality, etc.; and in the different dimensions with: social, economic, technological, cultural, political, ecological, sacred, among others.This transition implies a change of the modern hegemonic cultural model, where capitalism with its strategies of power has sought to impose an agrarian geopolitics protected in technical-political rationalities and hegemonic discourses that are presented as absolute truths, such as agronegotiation, the green revolution and the agroextivism.This is why, the agroecological transition implies an ethical, epistemic, ontological, political and political dispute in which the symbolic order and culture take on great importance, being a way to re-sign and evaluate local and community knowledge from the complementarity between theory and praxis
