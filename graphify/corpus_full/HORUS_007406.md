# Adjustments to human talent management processes in the implementation of teleworking in Wingo airline

After the pandemic of the COVID-19 many companies have adopted the teleworking, which has opened new opportunities to improve labor and capitalize the benefits that this work modality offers. This study aimed to analyze the opportunities for improvement in the processes of human talent management in the airline Wingo. A qualitative study was carried out that included a review of the theoretical framework and interviews with leaders of the business management and the area of human resources, in order to align the theory with the actual practice. This analysis revealed differences between current practices and ideals according to the theory, which provides the opportunity to identify areas of improvement. From the interviews, challenges were highlighted as the need to establish clear policies, provide training, promote effective communication, promote teamwork and carry out a permanent monitoring, which will help to maximize the benefits of the telecommutation. In addition, strengths were identified, such as the flexibility of the company, job satisfaction and the capacity of innovation.The results and conclusions suggest that it is essential to establish a process of continuous improvement, focused on the development of technological competences, the standardization of practices and greater support to the leaders in the management of the human talent.
