# FOOD INSURITY IN THE RURALITY OF THE COLOMBIAN ORIENTAL REGION

the 2015 ENSIN reveals that more than half of the Colombian population (54.2%) is in food insecurity in the household (INSAH); it is higher in areas with lower population concentration (59.0 %), less urbanized and dispersed areas of the country related to poverty. to analyze the nutritional situation and food security in rural households with children under 5 years of age in the eastern Colombian region and to identify the prevalence of coping strategies. the study is observational of descriptive type and cross-cutting cut derived from the ENSIN 2015. A sub-sample of 487 households was obtained applying criteria of geographical area, region and age less than 5 years of age belonging to the rural area of the eastern Colombian region. Univariate and bivariate analyses were carried out. 45.79 % of households are shaped by the region of 2 to 4 people; in health insurance, 72.3% were employed by the secondary system; 32.2% and 79.7% of households did not have access to water and sewerage services respectively. The prevalence of food insecurity represents 61.8 %; in total proportion of the rural households was 53%
