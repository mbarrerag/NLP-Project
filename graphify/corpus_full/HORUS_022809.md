# Anatomical-radiological correspondence of the cinemadegluion: Practical guide for students.

The swallowing is a complex process that has been a topic of interest in the clinical field, the kinedeglution as a diagnostic examination has been the gold standard for its description and management. The recognition of the anatomical elements that participate in the process of swallowing, is a fundamental factor for its interpretation. The observational and association skills must be developed during academic training and improved during professional exercise. For this reason, a practical guide is made for students of speech and medical specialties that relate to the performance and interpretation of the examination, starting from the correspondence of the anatomical elements located in images of bodies with radiological images at rest and at different times of the swallowing.
