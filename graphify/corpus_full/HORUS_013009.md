# Changes in intra- and extra-laboral working conditions in the rural sector in Vélez, Santander

In recent years, Colombian rurality has had multiple transformations due to phenomena such as globalization, political violence, the emergence of armed groups, agrarian reforms and policies; which have generated variations in the intra-labor and extra-labor working conditions of the peasants living and working in the rural sector.The aim of this article is to describe the changes in the intra- and extra-labor working conditions experienced by rural workers in the village of Peña de San Vicente in Vélez, Santander in Colombia and the way in which they have influenced their living conditions.Research of a qualitative-descriptive approach, semi-structured interviews and participant observation, the analysis of the results had as reference the categories of Pierre Bourdieu in his theory of rural sociology, allowing to show an economic crisis in the areas of Vélez Santander given by the changes in the economic dynamics and globalization that have affected the conditions of work in the rural sector. .
