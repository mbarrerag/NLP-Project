# IMPORTANCE OF THE BLAND HANDS IN THE TOURISTICAL COMPANY.

Soft skills play a very important role in the tourism industry, and are considered fundamental requirements for the successful management of these organizations. In this context, the question arises: What soft skills are identified in the management of the tourism sector companies in Manizales? The methodology used was empirical in nature, and interviews were conducted with 102 managers in the Manizales sector, as well as nine teachers in the Professional Training in Tourist Administration program at the Catholic University of Manizales. During this process, a hierarchy of soft skills was established and it was observed that teamwork, leadership and communication skills were perceived as the most fundamental to their work performance. The results obtained were contrasted with different research, which revealed that there are multiple ways of hierarchizing, evaluating and grouping these skills. The evaluation model used allowed to characterize the soft skills of managers, and complementary factors that were also identified that were relevant to addressing soft skills as an integral element in personal and business management, in conjunction with technical, conceptual or design skills
