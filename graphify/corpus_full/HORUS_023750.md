# CHARACTERIZATION OF DELIUM PRESENCE IN A UNITY OF PEDIATROTIC INTENSIVE CARE OF THE CITY OF BOGOTÁ, COLOMBIA

The delirium is considered an acute cerebral dysfunction characterized by an alteration in the state of consciousness given by a fluctuation of the mental state, lack of attention and the inability to receive, process, store or remember the information.1 The true prevalence of the delirium in the pediatric population is not well documented, as well as the diagnosis and frequency in this population.The purpose of this study was to apply the scale of detection PSCAM-ICU per nursing to determine the presence of delirium in critical pediatric patients. Observational study of cross-sectional cut performed in the second trimester of 2021 in the pediatric intensive care unit of a third level hospital in the city of Bogotá, Colombia. A total of 52 patients between 6 months and 5 years of age were included after the signature of the consent by their accupunctive patients. The data were analyzed in the software SPSS V28 through relative and absolute frequencies for categorical variables and measures of central tendency and dispersion in numerical variables.
