# Policy evaluation for the motorcycle sector

The motorcycle is a means of transport that generates significant impacts on contemporary urban dynamics, especially in developing countries. At the socioeconomic level, it is constituted as an important tool of mobility and inclusion, particularly for limited resource populations that face restrictions on access to public transport or the acquisition of cars. Its preference is based on operational characteristics as versatility and efficiency in travel times. However, its predominance in the fleet, including Colombia, generates significant externalities in terms of pollution, congestion and traffic risk, requiring evidence-based public policy interventions. This research develops an innovative methodology for the evaluation of urban transport policies from the perspective of complex systems, with emphasis on contexts where the motorcycle has strong influence. The methodological framework integrates the model based on data oriented agents with the human behavior model CONSUMAT and the framework of Institutional Analysis and Development (IAD). This integration provides a solid basis for the assessment of technical and social aspects of transport systems, establishing seven systematic phases that ensure their replication in different urban contexts.
