# FINANCIAL DESIGN OF MULTINATIONAL

The purpose of this research is to carry out a review of the literature on the evolution of research in the financial performance of multinationals, using bibliometric and network analysis, in an attempt to map this area of knowledge and identify emerging research trends. Likewise, this article allows to establish the research flows that emerge today, on the subject studied. From 687 recovered records of Web of Science and Scopus, during the period 2000-2021, a scientific mapping was carried out to identify the documents, authors, journals, institutions and countries most relevant in the field, in addition, through a network analysis the main groups of documents and the research addresses that are being assumed. Four main areas emerging from the literature were identified: Financial Decommission of the 1NMNE that carry out activities of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), impact of the exploitation and transfer of the competitive advantages of the NMs are established, relationship of the diversification of countries of the NMNE and finally, advantages of the countries in which the NM is located, versus financial performance.
