# ENVIRONMENTAL AND TOURISM SUSTAINABILITY OF THE GREAT RIVER CULTURE ITINERARY OF MAGDALENA

The present presentation presents the preliminary results of the research Contribution to the construction of the environmental and tourism sustainability component of the project to include the Rio Grande de la Magdalena in the UNESCO World Heritage List in the category of Cultural Itinerary, which constitutes the first phase of a project that aims to contribute to the identification and documentation of the heritage values of the Rio Grande de la Magdalena (RGM) in the environmental field, as an input to support the request for incorporation of the river into the UNESCO World Heritage List in the category called Cultural Itinerary. To this end, it was sought to know the current state of natural resources and to analyze the hydrosocial conflicts present in the territories of this basin, especially those related to tourism activity, in order to propose preliminary guidelines for its sustainability and effective management. The working team is made up of a group of women affiliated to the Santo Tomás University, members of the research group Centro de Altos Estudios en Cuencas Hidrográficas (CAECHI), composed exclusively of women
