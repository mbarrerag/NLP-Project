# Improvement of resistance to WC-Co coating cavitation through control of thermal projection process parameters by High Velocity Oxy-Fuel (HVOF).

In this research, the relationship between the resistance to the cavitation of coatings based on WC-Co and the projection parameters of the HVOF technique was studied. The coatings were manufactured with the DJH-2600 gun which works with hydrogen and oxygen as combustion gases. The projection process was automated. Three repetitions were obtained for each projection condition to ensure the repeatability of the experiments. The resistance to cavitation was evaluated by means of the indirect test of the ASTM G-32 standard as well as the roughness of the coatings, surface morphology and microstructure by means of SEM and optical microscopy respectively. The layer porosity was measured by means of digital imaging treatment. The analysis of the phases present in the coating was carried out by XRD. Subsequently, the data obtained were correlated with the projection parameters to give the best recipe. It was found that the parameter that had the greatest effect on all the evaluated properties was the total combustion flow (FTC), while the combustion ratio (RC) did not have a statistically significant effect on the levels evaluated in the experimental plan
