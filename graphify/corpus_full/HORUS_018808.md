# CASE OF STUDY: TECHNOLOGICAL MANAGEMENT AND INNOVATION IN THE NATIONAL ARMED OF COLOMBIA (ARC)

The first is the conceptual scope of a methodology, its components and characteristics, which includes the organizational elements of the arc and the proposal to appropriate the gti in the ff. mm., based on its organizational strategy. Subsequently, the dynamicization of the functions of the gti within the framework of the map of processes of the arc as a methodological proposal for technological management and innovation is defined. In this sense, a characterization is made for each of the subprocesses proposed and its relationship with the processes of the Colombian National Navy. Finally, the proposal for the training of personnel of the Colombian National Navy for the proper development of the elements associated with technological management and innovation is included. This proposal is framed in four phases that will allow the development of capacities of gti in the arc, such as: awareness-raising, diagnosis, development and evaluation
