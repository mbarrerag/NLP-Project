# EXERCISES OF DESIGN AND LOCALIZATION OF REELS

This document brings together the main parameters and processes in the geometric design of pathways that a student of a course of pathways must know. The text must be developed individually by each of the students of the respective course, with the orientation, monitoring, control and evaluation of the teacher in charge, who must present the concepts and explanations necessary in the classroom. In each chapter exercises are formulated with field data, so that each student will perform the calculations and relevant plans in a logical and orderly manner in the corresponding phases of design and location of a road project. For this purpose nine chapters are determined: zero line and preliminary layout; surveying and transfer of height; horizontal geometric design; overwidth; empalme equation; vertical design; peralte; rasante and cubication portfolio. For each exercise, the parameters, formulas and specifications determined for this type of projects within the national territory of Colombia, based on the Manual of Geometric Design of Roads of the National Institute of Vias (Invias). This text was elaborated from the results of teaching and research in the area of Topegráficas and Vias de Ingenías de Lónicas
