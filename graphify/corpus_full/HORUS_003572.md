# DEVELOPMENTS IN CAPITAL STRUCTURE LITERATURE: A SYSTEMATIC REVISION

The study of the financial structure of a company is valuable for the generation of models that seek the formation of an adequate capital structure that results in the efficient use of resources and in a balanced relationship between the level of indebtedness, financial risk and the cost of capital. This study identifies the most influential publications in the evolution of the field of capital structure, the most cited publications, focus of knowledge and its disaggregation themes. Additionally, provide a structure categorizing the publications according to their seminal, structural or recent contribution. The methodology was composed by a bibliographic search in the academic databases Scopus and Web of Science (WoS), the structural analysis of the bilibliographic network through the software Tree of Science (ToS) and the analysis of clusters with Gephi software. The results show that while the postulates of the theory of Modigliani and Miller represent the fundamental study base, there is no universal hypothesis valid for the formulation of a capital structure. On the other hand, the topic of corporate risk was identified as that of greater scientific debate in recent decades, with the greater and more determinant structure of publications and analysis of various types
