# Validation of analytical tools for monitoring food quality

The quality of the food is defined as the aptitude for the consumption of a product, or the fulfilment of the requirements necessary to meet the needs and expectations of the consumers. A typical tool for the evaluation of the quality of the food is the use of methodologies of sensory analysis on the finished product in order to establish if the food possesses the necessary attributes that guarantee that the people will acquire it. However, the sensory analysis involves expensive, dispersed and that require an amlio training and rigorous to panelists. Currently, we are looking for analytical instrumental tools that allow an objective evaluation of the quality of the product. In this proposal we will take as a reference matrix the honey of bees, in consideration to that its quality attributes are fundamentally related to organoleptic properties. By here the obtained profiles are linked sensory work that can be attributable to a description of the aroma or taste of a product, respectively.
