# LEGAL, HONORARIAS AND COSTS TO THE INTER-AMERICAN SYSTEM OF HUMAN RIGHTS

Promoting a case before the Inter-American Human Rights System can generate a high cost, due to the procedural times and the costs required. Non-governmental organizations and litigants who are involved in conflicts in international courts are aware of this situation, as are the institutions of the Inter-American System. This research article aims to study the way in which legal services and costs are established before the Inter-American Human Rights System. For this purpose, a qualitative method was used, for which the documentary and case law analysis was used. It was concluded that there are criteria on the part of the Inter-American System to establish the fees and costs incurred when accessing the system
