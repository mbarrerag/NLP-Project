# Forensics: text and atlas

Through hundreds of images selected from thousands of photographs that have served to document, over the past 10 years at least, thousands of cases of legal medical necropsies, and that have been considered representative of brain injuries most frequent in forensic medical practice in Colombia, this book and atlas offers a very complete view of the morphological alterations associated with trauma or diseases of legal medical interest as findings to explain death or the conditions associated with it. As far as possible, the images have been accompanied by brief explanations, which complement what has been wanted to illustrate, including the non-infrequent practice of errors in the preservation or processing of specimens and not forgetting the normal anatomy for the better understanding of injuries. The book is the result of the systematic effort of a team of work committed to appropriate knowledge for all, a paradoxical gain that bequeaths the history of violence in Colombia.-- Contracadas
