# REGISTRATIONS ON THE PRESENTION OF ANOPHELES (KERTEZIA) IN COLOMBIA

The secondary information was reviewed and records were documented to update the data on the presence of subgenus species in Colombia, providing information on the geographical distribution for the species and reviewing the authors who provided the records. Additionally, field collection material was reviewed and deposited in the MEFLG. Records of the presence of 7 species were found for Colombia: An. homunculus, An. bambusiculus, An. phallidatus, An. lepidatus, An.neivai, An. crusii (s.l.) An. bolivensis. In addition to this last species, the occurrence of the other two species of the bolivensis complex is recorded based on molecular evidence. Records indicate the presence of the subgenus in 16 departments. For 5 species, available information from the region genetic code of bars was found with 83 records from specimens from different regions of the country
