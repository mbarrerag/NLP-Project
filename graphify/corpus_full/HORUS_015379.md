# Study of the photocatalytic activity of nanostructured and modified TiO2 electrocatalysts in hydrogen production.

The results complement the state of art of the synthesis of this type of nanostructures, establish relationships between the morphological characteristics obtained and the variables of synthesis. These relationships allow to establish zones of synthesis where the morphology of the nanotubes can be controlled. The results of treatments that modify the crystalline structure of the nanotubes and their surface are discussed. The effect of the thermal treatment on the evolution of the anatase phase of the rutile in the nanotubes of TiO2 is discussed. A protocol is established to modify superficially the nanotubes of TiO2 with nickel nanoparticles, by means of electrodeposit. For each treatment, a morphological, crystalline and elemental characterisation of the samples is performed. The electrochemical characterization of the synthesized photoelectrodes and the effects of the nanostructures on their photoelectrochemical behavior is performed.
