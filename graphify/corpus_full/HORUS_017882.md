# Target modeling for strategic business and IT alignment.

The gap between the development and analysis of business and IT strategies, in practice and literature, affects the way IT uses strategies to define and prioritize resources for IT projects, initiatives and operations. Closing this gap helps to better support future developments, changes in operation and new projects and initiatives within business and IT units, ensuring an analytical basis for decision-making. We present a business/IT/early requirements management model that supports i) prioritization and definition of requirements related to business and IT strategies, ii) analyzing how these requirements can be associated with new and old systems, and iii) IT/SI operation. The proposed model and process allow to build a model of strategic business goals and IT/SI that can be analyzed and applied in the IT governance model to support the functional alignment of business/IT strategies. The model was built while a case study was carried out in a Colombian university applying Design Science Research. The model was subsequently evaluated using qualitative and quantitative methods, with experts within the evaluation case study and external professionals of the academy and the industry
