# PEDAGOGOLOGICAL TRENDS FOR STRENGTHENING HANDS AND LISTENING FOR SINGLE DOCUMENTS IN PRE-SCHOOL, THROUGH DIGITAL TOOLS

The aim of this review article is to identify the approaches to learning developed on strengthening the teaching process of the English area in preschool, especially speech and listening skills, aimed at teachers, based on a Virtual Learning Environment (AVA) as a tool for the media in the field of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT). Among the main findings is that teaching of English in preschool should focus on developing basic communication skills such as listening and speaking through interactive activities and the use of visual and auditory resources and that the role of the teacher is definitely fundamental in this context, since his knowledge and creativity directly impact on the effectiveness of learning; that is how the game emerges as a crucial tool for teaching English, providing a playful environment that facilitates significant and natural learning. Finally, it emphasizes the need to incorporate in educational institutions, from the initial level, training programmes for teachers to develop and improve their skills in the management of digital technologies and to replace classic models of teaching the language
