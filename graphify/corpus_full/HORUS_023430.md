# Generation of trajectories for a 3GDL robot applied to the cutting in textile fabrication.

In the execution of activities for textile clothing, it is necessary to take raw materials and transform them into parts. Usually the fabric is the raw material that composes the majority of the final product, whose first stage of manufacture is the cut. Many producers in Colombia use as a tool a manual cutter that allows to divide the fabric, arranged in multilayered layout, in order to obtain the desired volume of parts. When performing the cut in manual form, problems arise related to the availability of labor, human errors and other eventualities that can negatively affect the process of textile cutting. With the aim to address the first layer of automation of the process without needing to change the tool of confection, in this document the approach of an error planning of trajectories that must follow the textile cutter oriented by a cartesian robot of 3 degrees of freedom (GDL). It generated flat contours in the working space using cubic interpolation and parametrization of functions can be considered, in order to abstract trajectories in the operating space in function of time.
