# INDEX OF RESILIENCE AND EFFECTIVENESS OF DISASTER RISK MANAGEMENT

An integrated disaster risk management involves then disaster risk reduction and adaptation to global climate change considering economic, social, and environmental issues. These require a suitable strategy for both vulnerability reduction and resilience improvement. This chapter demonstrates how best to evaluate resilience employing a way to gauge the performance and effectiveness of disaster risk management, employing a transparent, robust, and representative indicator just like the Disaster Risk Management Index, DRMi. This indicator has demonstrated to be easily understood by public policymakers at the national, subnational, and concrete levels after its broad use in Latina America and the Caribbean region. Herein it is presented a briefing of the calculation methodology of the DRMi, using the fuzzy sets¿ theory as a tool, thanks to the requirement to accommodate data with incommensurable units or information that only is evaluated using linguistic estimates, and the results of the evaluation of disaster risk management performance'and resilience'of 26 countries
