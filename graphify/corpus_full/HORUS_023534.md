# Analysis of demographic dynamics in the West of Antioquia, area of influence of the Aborra – Cauca Road Connection and the Autopista al Mar Uno project (1985-2018).

The construction and operation of the road development projects generate environmental impacts on the demographic component that have not been considered, measured, evaluated in magnitude and importance in the Environmental Impact Studies -EIA-. In order to verify, analyze and confirm this assertion, this research was carried out. The impacts on the demographic component and on the territorial resources were studied in three municipalities of the West Anti-Oqueño: San Jerónimo, Sopetrán and Santa Fe de Antioquia in the period 1985-2018, derived from the construction and operation of two road projects: Conecto Vial Aburrá - Río Cauca, which began its construction in 1996 and entered into operation in 2006 and Autopista al Mar 1, consisting of the extension of a second tunnel and two more lanes of the Connection Vial Aburra - Río Cauca. The information of the last four population censuses -1985, 1993, 2005 and 2018 was used for the analysis of the variables of the population, which can be correlated and established that the construction of the Vial Connection Aburrá – Rio Cauca, orcasion direct impacts in the demography, activating, activating processes, activating and other variables. as well as cultural and political. The result obtained draws attention to the contents of Environmental Impact Studies, their temporal and spatial scope in retrospective and prospective terms and their importance for decision-making regarding environmental management as a whole
