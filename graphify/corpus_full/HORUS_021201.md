# Browser role in caring for person with cancer under cancer treatment

The impact of cancer is growing steadily in many low- and middle-income countries. An understanding of the relevance of this disease and of intervening urgently against it is considered a priority.It is necessary to strengthen prevention, early detection and control activities, as well as the adoption of a new comprehensive approach.The problem is based on identifying the burden of cancer in the world and in Latin America, the threat is increasing: the number of people, families and affected communities is increasing and a cause of under-privileged poverty is making economic development difficult in many countries. Likewise, the costs of health care, and the increasing interest in innovative programs that link the central role of the patient in the management of his disease from the stages of promoting health, prevention and early detection.These programmes are expected to provide patients with the knowledge, skills and confidence to address the problems related to the disease and to prepare them for participation with health professionals and the health system. In addition, it is expected to link students of different levels of pre-graduate training and consolidate the research network in care for the cancer patient in Colombia, which consists of a process during which individualized support is provided to patients, families and caregivers to overcome the difficulties of different health systems and facilitate timely access to health care services and at the psychosocial level. This process is guided by a nursing professional, called _x0093_nermero navigador_x0094_, who has specific clinical knowledge in the area, is assertively communicating and able to use the nursing process to provide education and resources by facilitating informed decision-making, and ensuring quality care during all phases of cancer. It is the objective of the programme to provide input in generating the patient navigation programme, through a diagnosis of the burden of symptom and self-management behaviour of patients; the design of the pilot programme in line with the needs of the Colombian context; the determination of the acceptability and feasibility of the programme with the precision of barriers and facilitators for its implementation in cancer care centers
