# Application of the technique, which uses the accumulation flow, to determine sites where new landslides may occur, on slopes adjacent to the Central Transversal of the Pacific (Tadó – La Virginia Sector)

This academic work applies, with the support of a Digital Elevation Model – DEM, and Geographic Information Systems-GIS, the accumulation flow technique in the slopes adjacent to the Central Transversal del Pacifico-TCP road corridor, Tadó – La Virginia sector, to identify landslide site associated with anthropic causes and flow accumulation; achieving the objective of locating possible sites where land use and a greater accumulation flow are determining factors in the occurrence of new landslides. This product must serve to attract the attention of the reader, who is looking for valid reasons to request an early review of the issue before the and take control and prevention measure before the landslide events in the TCP
