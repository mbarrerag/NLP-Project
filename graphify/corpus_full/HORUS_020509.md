# Catalytic oxidation in heterogeneous medium of an azoic dye using the bicarbonate activated peroxide system

One of the recalcitrant pollutants that generate high impact on water bodies are synthetic colourants, especially those containing in their molecular structure azo groups, due to which they prevent photosynthetic processes, reduce dissolved oxygen concentration and affect the dynamics of the aquatic system. Given the complex chemical structure and molecular size of the dyes, most of these compounds are not eliminated by conventional methods for water treatment. Red allura, also known as red No. 40 or E129, is an azoic colouring widely used in the food industry to impart colouring in ice creams, soft drinks, sweets and bakery products. Its molecular formula is C18H14N2Na2O8S2 and is characterized by being a stable molecule, with a mean life of 33.6 h under direct fotolysis (UV).
