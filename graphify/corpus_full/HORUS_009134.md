# METHODOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT TO GET A COCAINE IN-HOUSE REFERENCE MATERIAL FOR FORUM APPLICATIONS IN THE SDG FRAMEWORK.

A methodology was developed to obtain a reference material in-house¿ (RMih) of cocaine from a sample seized, taking into account different international guidelines, as well as the principles of Green Chemistry and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). To carry out this, extraction and purification procedures were evaluated as (i) column chromatography (CC) and (ii) a sequential strategy of extraction with supercritical fluids (SFE)-extraction with pressurized liquids (PLE). Likewise, the parameters of solubility of Hansen (HSP) were used, in order to select the best green solvent to be used in PLE. On the other hand, the characterization of both starting material and the material obtained in the different techniques of extraction and purification mentioned, through GC-MS and GC-FID. Considering the above, experimental designs were evaluated to determine the optimal working conditions to be used in PLE, being the variable response to analyte.
