# HOMOORFISM SPACES BETWEEN UNABLE FOUR

In this work we use a useful relationship between the tetrade representations category and the representation category of a certain associated carcaj, which allows us to apply linear algebra techniques in the explicit obtaining of homomorphism spaces between the types of incompatible representations corresponding to the postprojective component of Auslander-Reiten carcaj associated with the problem of the four subspaces
