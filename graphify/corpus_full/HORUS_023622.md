# LOAD OF THE CAREER AND RESILIENCE OF DISEASES IN DOMICILIARY ATTENTION DURING PANDEMIA COVID-19

The need for home care services especially for older adults is increasing, and in times of pandemic make formal caregivers the support of this care bringing them to the burden of caregiver. Describe the burden of the caregiver and resilience of nurses who provide care in home care programs during the pandemic. Quantitative, descriptive, cross-sectional study, carried out in the Home Care Health Provider Institution in November 2020. The population was made up of 20 nurses. The sampling was non-probabilistic by convenience. The Resilience Scale of Wagnild, & Young, and Caregird load of Zarit was used. For the analysis of the data, IBM SPSS Statistics 22, of which was obtained descriptive and inferential statistics, as means, standard deviations, frequencies, percentages and Fisher test. 94% of formal caregivers were women, was evidenced intense burden in 20% of caregivers and low level of resilience in those caregivers living with their siblings, and a high level of resilience in caregivers living alone, increased in the conclusion
