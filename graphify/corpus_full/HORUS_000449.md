# REPLY OF IMPEDANCE OF FIVE AMOUNTS FE70Nb10B20 AND [(Fe50Co50)75B20Si5]96Nb4 OBTAINED BY THE RESONANCE OF A SMALL SONENOID

In this paper, we present a study of the longitudinal impedance response of samples of metal glass Fe70Nb10B20 and [(Fe50Co50)75B20Si5]96Nb4 by using the resonance of a small solenoid. The longitudinal impedance measurements as a function of frequency were performed in the range of 0 < f < 30 MHz for magnetic fields of HDC = 0,5, 10, 20, 30 and 40 Oe at room temperature. The curves obtained present increases in the amplitude of impedance peaks around the resonance region of the solenoid. Based on this, an equivalent circuit and polynomic adjustment generated by artificial intelligence (AI) is proposed to model the impedance response of these systems
