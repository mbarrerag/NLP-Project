# INFLUENCE OF MINERALIZATION IN MECHANICAL PROPERTIES OF YARAGUA MINES IN BURITICÁ ANTIOQUIA, COLOMBIA INCLUENCE OF MINERALIZATION ON MECHANICAL PROPERTIES OF THE ROCKS OF THE YARAGUA MINE IN BURITICÁ - ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA

A geomechanical characterization of a tunnel for the exploitation of gold was performed in the Yaraguá mine (Continental Gold Limited) in the Municipality of Buriticá (Antioquia). The characterization process included the classification of the rocky mass through the classification systems RMR and Q, analysis of thin sections, tests of P wave velocity, simple point load and resistance to uniaxial and triaxial compression. The rocks analyzed were classified as basalts, andesites, diorites and gaps, all with minerals such as magnetite, carbonates and pyrite. In the tests of wave velocity, the rocks reached 70% of the theoretical velocity, showing continuity indices that are in the range from strong to intermediate. The uniaxial compression resistance in the range of 42 MPa and 134 MPa. The behavior of the rocks under the loads in the triaxial compression test shows a fragile tendency to fragile-ductile and with maximum resistance in the range of 130 MPa to 150 MPa.
