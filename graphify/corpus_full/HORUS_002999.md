# ECOSOPHY: A RELATIONSHIP WITH BIODIVERSITY.

Our planet speaks to us in tongues of fire, it is a fact that every day biodiversity decreases, and that our way of life is at risk. From my artistic practices I try to solve the question that we all ask ourselves: What to do in this situation? To do this I involve elements of science, aesthetics and spirituality, thus composing an echosofia, that is, a way to relate to the beings and forces of our planet, remembering always that it is alive because it is diverse
