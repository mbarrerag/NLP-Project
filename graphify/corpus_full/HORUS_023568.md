# Need for training in research in design posts

This quantitative research is part of the question as to whether designers are training to take on research as an activity in the daily professional exercise. The aim is to highlight this gap in undergraduate training and propose research training as a necessary resource to address the new challenges of design. Nine curriculum programs of visual and graphic design were taken in Colombia and their courses were classified by thematic areas, and then focused on those courses oriented to research training. The findings show the absence of the cross-cutting research component for design careers
