# QUANTIFICATION AND MITIGATION OF THE ACRILAMMID OCCUPATIONAL FORMED BY THE REACTION OF MALILLARD IN PANELA DE CUNDINAMARCA

The panela is a product of tradition and basic of the family basket, with a per capita consumption and significant productions in Colombia, elaborated by means of a process of evaporation at high temperatures, in which the formation of acrylamide in the Maillard reaction is given origin; the acrylamide studied as a possible carcinogenic compound in humans. Therefore, the chemical characterization regarding acrylamide in panela in the department of Cundinamarca and the reduction of it by means of recommended practices is derived in this research project. Initially for the research methodology, samples will be collected from a honey plant referred to by Fedepanela, followed by a conditioning and pretreatment of the panela and then continued in three phases: 1. Identify the process stage with greater formation of acrylamide in aplumida panel and block in the honey plant, 2. Conduct a study of stability of the acrylamide in the panela and 3.
