# Super-critical Hardy-Littlewood inequalities for multilinear forms

The multilinear Hardy-Littlewood inequalities provide estimates for the sum of the coefficients of multilinear forms T ∶ lnp1 × ⋯ × lnpm → R (or C) when 1/p1 + ⋯ + 1/pm < 1. In this paper we investigate the critical and super-critical cases; i.e., when 1/p1 + ⋯ + 1/pm ≥ 1
