# History of SENA in the department of Huila (1958-1978)

This research paper describes the birth of SENA in the Department of Huila and the comprehensive vocational training provided by this entity, as a response to the demands of training for work by the productive sectors between 1958 and 1978. For the collection of the data, a survey was applied in its semi-structured interview modality to officials, instructors and apprentices who had a significant participation in this period of growth, development and institutional consolidation in the city of Neiva; then the information obtained in triangulation was contrasted with the written sources consulted (Regional Council Acts, texts and institutional archives among others.) The results indicate a clear correlation between the growth and development of the entity with the development of the qualified workers demanded by the productive sectors of the Department of Huila. This shows the flexibility and adaptability of the different forms and training methods used by the entity in response to the national, regional and union development policies in the labour and education sector, which support and direct the institutional mission of the entity through comprehensive vocational training and its objectives, aimed at being, knowing and learning the Colombian worker-apprentiz.
