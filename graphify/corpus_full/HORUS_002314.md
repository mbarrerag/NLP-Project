# Design of optical system for meniscus leveling in the calibration of glass material and metal volumetric containers.

_x0095_ The calibration of volumetric instruments (pipettes, burets, test pieces) critically depends on the correct identification of the meniscus of the liquid to ensure metrological traceability. Traditional methods depend on the visual perception of the laborator, introducing significant uncertainties (*Purata-Sifuentes et al., 2018*). This project proposes the design of an automated optical system that uses artificial vision and image processing algorithms (Canny, Hough) to detect the meniscus with adequate precision (Wang et al., 2024), reducing human errors and complying with international standards
