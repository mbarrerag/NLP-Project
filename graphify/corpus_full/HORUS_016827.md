# DISTRIBUTION OF HEMATOLINPHOID NEOPLASIES IN ADULTS OF A THIRD-NIVEL INSTITUTION OF BOGOTA FROM A REGISTRATION OF THE MEDULA STUDIES OF THEIR PATOLOGY LABORATORY

Of 1133 records, 402 had a final diagnosis of hematolymphoid neoplasia; the mean age at diagnosis was 57.8. The distribution of the entities was as follows: 27.9% Mature B neoplasias, 22.9% Plasma cell neoplasms, 12.2% Acute Myeloid Leukemias, 9.7% Acute Lymphoma/Able Lymphoma, 9.5% Chronic Myeloproliferative Neoplasias (other than Chronic Myeloid Leukaemia), 5.7% Chronic Myeloid Syndromes, 3.9% Hodgkin Lymphoma, 2.5% Matured T Neoplasia, 1.7% Chronic Myelodysplastic Neoplasia/Myelodysplastic Syndromes, 8 (2%) Chronic Myeloid Leukaemia and 8 (2%) Other entities
