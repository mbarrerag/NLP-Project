# RELEVANCE, EVOLUTION AND TRENDS OF Entrepreneurship OVERVIEW A REVISION OF LITERATURA IN FINANCE

The objective of this article is to present a review of literature that shows the relevance, evolution and trends on scientific production related to business survival identified in the Web of Science and Scopus databases between 1973 and 2020. For the records obtained, a network analysis was carried out supported by graph theory, applying tools such as R, bibliometrix and Gephi that allow to identify the importance of the topic and build the theoretical evolution of its approach using the algorithms of the Tree of Science tool (TOS), with which seminal, structural and trend documents are identified, through clusterization analysis. The results show four subareas of survival analysis: from entrepreneurship, strategies and the influence of innovation, the variables that influence it and the type of output in the stock exchange
