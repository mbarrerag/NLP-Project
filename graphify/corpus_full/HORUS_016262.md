# Recovery of zinc from mining waste by biolixivation by acidophilic bacteria and electro-acquirement.

This work presents the results obtained for the recovery of Zn, by means of processes of biolixivation/biooxidation and electroobtivation, from the mining waste of the La Gavia mine, located in the municipality of Riosucio, Caldas, Colombia. This mining company is dedicated to the exploitation of gold, and in it there is produced large residual batteries with a significant content of secondary minerals, where the sphalerite is highlighted, one of the main menas of Zn. The biolixivation was carried out in the presence of pure and mixed crops of strains of A. ferrooxidans, L. ferrooxidans and A. thiooxidans is produced, with the aim of evaluating its capacity and use in the leaching process. The results at laboratory scale of the adaptation process showed the faculty of these microorganisms to survive in complex environments. The formal trials allowed to know the kinetic of the process, in function of Eh, pH, biomass and Zn+2. The results of the results of the micro-organs of the process were confirmed in the mixing crop, thus, the higher percentage of the solvent and the Zn and the solution, the higher percentage of Zn The adaptation of the strains, compared to mineral samples with high levels of toxic metals. Therefore, it was concluded that biolixivation assisted by acidophilic bacteria is an effective process for the recovery of material of interest (Zn in the case of this thesis) from mining waste, besides being friendly with the environment
