# CHARACTERIZATION OF HIGH PERFORMANCE, USING AUTOMATIC LEARNING, IN BANKS OF GERMOPLASM IMPROVING ANALYSIS OF CULTIVES AND SIDE PARIENTS: CASE OF FRIJOL AND FORRAJERO MANI

A better knowledge of the accesses conserved in the banks of germoplasm would contribute to improving the agri-food systems, since a precise characterization allows to identify and describe the main attributes, as well as the similar or dissimilar traits that discriminate the accessions.The use of proximal sensors and analysis of digital images have increased considerably in the platforms of phenotyping of high yield in the improvement of crops. Standardizing processes of high yield characterization of the wild relatives of the crop and of the indigenous races of the agrodiversity is of importance in the banks of germoplasm. In this study the objective was to characterize relatives of cultivation and wild of forage mani (Arachis spp) and frijol (Phaseolus spp.) of the bank of germoplasm of the Alliance Bioversity and CIAT using high yield phenotyping (MethodoLOGIA: the phenotyping unit of the forage (Arachis spp.) and 16 accessions of the frijol, in the process of regeneration of the fields of the regeneration of the stations of the Bioversity Alliance and the localized. The variables were selected using the ML random forest algorithm and the classification of the species was performed with what was observed in the confusion matrix. These analyses were performed both to the fenomic descriptors, as well as to the traditional descriptors and the combination of both. The characterization was performed using traditional and traditional fenomic descriptors.
