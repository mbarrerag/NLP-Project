# Selection of gasification techniques for paneling rod bagasse for thermal or electrical energy production using process optimization.

Colombia is one of the largest panel producers in the world, a sector very important for the country since it links more than 20,000 families, so it is indispensable to carry out studies to improve the profitability of this agro-industrial sector. Gasification of the bagasse of paneller cane, residual of the productive process, can allow the most efficient use of this and meet the requirements of thermal and electrical energy of the process. In this work, a methodology is implemented to evaluate different alternatives of use based on biomass gasification. This methodology involves the strategy of collecting the bagassess from the trapiches to the location of the transformation site, evaluating the configuration of gasifiers that allow obtaining a synthesis gas with high energy, minimizing the costs of processing. Initially, the study area is characterized, determining the location and production of each gas trapiche. Subsequently, the possible routes of collection and its optimization are established using the heuristic technique 2-opt and Matlab® software. The simulation of gasification systems is made using Aspen Plus® V.10.0.
