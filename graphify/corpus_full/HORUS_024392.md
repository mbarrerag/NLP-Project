# Parametrization of columnary arrangements in volcanic rock: cases of study in COLOMBIA.

The formation of patterns is a natural property of nonlinear dynamic systems, these systems tend to self-organisation in spatial-temporal patterns, whose study can give us an idea of the underlying physics. Columnar disjunctions are one of the examples of most fascinating patterns of nature. Around the world are countless examples of rocky massifs and deposits with diaclases organized in columns and polygons. Observations, both qualitative and quantitative, of thirteen lava outcrops with columnar disjunction in Colombia. The configuration of the diaclases in these rocky massifs allowed the identification of colonnata and entabladura faces, and by describing the morphological traits presented by the columns could be inferred that the predominant cooling mechanism in the formation of the columns corresponds to the cooling assisted by cracks.
