# IDENTIFYING THE RELATIONS OF A NETWORK OF LOCAL ACTORS FOR PREPARATION IN THE CHAIR OF COLOMBIAN STUDIES

This article seeks to identify the characteristics of the relationships of the network of actors involved in the stage of preparation for disaster management. The case study is the city of Manizales, in Colombia. A description is made by applying the methodolog ́ia of social networks to evaluate the relations between the actors. The results obtained allow to show a network that has established processes, where information is shared among all the participants, has formal channels of communication on and the entity that represents the local government is the central actor and most relevant. The indicators calculated show that the network has 61% of the possible connections between the actors, in which each actor has, on average, 5.5 links established with the other actors that constitute the network.
