# Performance analysis of DESeq2 for detection of differentially expressed genes for genomic sequencing data.

The RNA sequencing methodologies have greatly accelerated the understanding of biological processes at the molecular level in different organisms. However, these methodologies are costly, leading to high-dimensionality data sets with reduced sample sizes. DESeq2 is currently one of the most used methodologies for differential expression analysis, and despite having a high fexibility in terms of its hyper-parameters, in most cases it is used with predetermined parameters. This work analyses two important elements of this methodology: performance is evaluated when the counts follow a Poisson distribution instead of negative Binomial and is shown as the sensitivity of the method increases with this distribution.
