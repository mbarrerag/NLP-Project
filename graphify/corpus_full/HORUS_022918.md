# EXACTITY OF THE QUICK TEST FOR DIAGNOSTIC INFECTION BY SYPHILIS IN EMBARAZED WOMEN

Every year, 350,000 adverse perinatal results attributable to syphilis are recorded in the world. The reduction of the prevalence of syphilis in pregnant women and the prevention of mother-to-child transmission is a priority in health. Evaluating the diagnostic accuracy of rapid tests for the detection of syphilis infection in pregnant women in middle- and low-income countries. A systematic search was made of the literature in MEDLINE, Embase, CENTRAL, LILACS, WHO-ICTRP and Web of Science, without restriction of language or type of publication. Diagnostic accuracy studies were included that recruited pregnant women in middle- and low-income countries. Only studies were included in which all participants were tested index (fast test) and the standard of reference (treponemic test and non-trepoemic). The study protocol was recorded in Prospero CRD42018107533. 11 studies with 19,291 women who analyzed the operational performance of nine types of tests (quick test) were included.
