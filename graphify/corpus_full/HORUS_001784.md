# The confluence between Popular Action and the Urban Project

This final master's degree study studies the impact of the use of Popular Action, as a constitutional mechanism in Colombia for the defense of collective rights, as a tool of citizen participation within the planning process and management of urban projects. To develop the work, two cases of study are taken, based on two judicial processes of Popular Action in the city of Bogotá, which allow to analyze the citizen participation, incidentally promoted by Popular Action, to solve the research objectives raised. In the end, it is deduced, within the conclusions and recommendations, the importance of validating scenarios of tangible citizen participation for the management of urban projects, beyond a requirement of law, that allow to propose consensual solutions for urban management, reducing conventional management times. .
