# DIAGNOSTIC LEARNING IN STATISTICAL INTRODUCTORY COURSES IN SEMIPRESENTIAL PROGRAMMES

This research investigates the following question How do some educational resources contribute to the diagnosis of learning in introductory statistical courses in a semipresential modality program? For some experts the diagnosis is a primary practice of approach that involves discovering aspects related to the cognitive part, the conditions and the aptitude of each student and the group, approach that will allow the teacher to base the development of the learning process. Likewise, it is considered as a serious articulated process and with coherence in its concepts and arguments. The pedagogical diagnosis is an officialized and accepted practice by teachers and tutors at different educational levels. However, the vision of the diagnosis and its significance is usually limited to the application of an instrument at the beginning of the course to describe difficulties and cognitive strengths of the students, ignoring essential aspects and the scope of the diagnosis in education. This study is carried out by the University Institution Antonio José Antonio Camacho de Santiago de Cali, the research and the research that uses the four teachers who provide the 15th degree of statistical in the Occupational Health program, a program that is offered in the semipresential modality, the analysis of the resources proposed by the Department of the Basic Sciences and other specialists. The Commission has also been able to assess the impact of the new approach on the educational diversity of the educational sector, so that they do not emphasize only the cognitive part
