# BIODIVERSITY OF MARINE CRUSTACES

Crustaceans are one of the most abundant groups, they are third after insects (1,020.007 species) and arachnidae (112,201 species). 66,914 species have been described and are the most abundant group in the marine environment. Decapods are the best represented order worldwide with 14,861 species. At the world level, 50 orders are known and in Colombia there is a record of a few, such as Copépods, Ostracodes, Cirripedia, Tantulocarida. Malacotraca are recorded as Stomatops (bravos or squilas shrimps), Peracarida (Ampopod, Isopode, Cumacea and Tanaidacea) and the largest rebistro of species correspond to Decapods with more than 600 species
