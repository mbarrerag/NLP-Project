# Coffee consumption patterns and their potential for change in the university community

The present research proposal addresses the patterns of coffee consumption that the community has within the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Bogotá, along with its possibilities for change through participatory actions within the campus. All in order to improve the health conditions of the different groups and reduce informal sales within the university space. Three (3) consecutive phases are proposed within a research with descriptive-comparative scope, where a first census of the actors who market coffee, in their different presentations, will be carried out within the university. In addition, coffee families who have links with the university will be identified at this stage. Subsequently, and with the characterization, a survey will be carried out to measure the forms, traditions, attitudes and practices of consumption of: administrative staff, students and teachers. The sample will be determined using a stratified random sampling taking into account the mentioned groups and their academic unit. Finally, actions will be carried out where the community will actively participate in order to promote the sustainable economy and the specialty coffee culture. This series of actions will be made looking to impact consumption patterns and, therefore, a measure of impact will be carried out after each implemented action.
