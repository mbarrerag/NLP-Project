# Analysis of the genomic diversity of the Pirarucu (Arapaima spp.).

The Pirarucú or Paiche (Arapaima spp.) is one of the largest fish in South America, emblematic of the Amazon region, which plays a special role in ecosystems and presents a great economic, nutritional and cultural importance, which makes it a relevant resource that requires attention and management. However, in Colombia it has been little studied and the population genetic diversity and structure that presents is unknown, which hinders not only its effective use, but its adequate risk conservation and protection in natural conditions. In addition, the Pirarucú is considered of great aquecultural potential for the country and from which already there are commercial productions, which is expected after several studies to be extended to other areas of potential for aquaculture production in Colombia, including the Valle del Cauca, where there are animals in cultivation conditions, without knowing their origin and possibility of crossing with other populations of the country. For this purpose, the objective of this work is to characterize the genomic diversity of the Pirarucú (Arapima spp.) in the basin of the Putumayo river and the Lake Tarapoto in the Amazon, and their relationship with animals of the Cauctario in the Vallectá.
