# LEARNING REMEDY: TRAINING OF DISCLOSURED AND ACTIVIZED PERSONS IN THE RATIONAL USE OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS

The project seeks the training of divulgators in a population of adolescents between 15 and 17 years interested in health sciences. For this purpose, we contacted the Institution of Distrital Education (IED), COLEGIO EL SALITRE - SUBA located in the UPZ 27 (Suba) chain of the town of Suba. The IED will support the execution providing pedagogical spaces during the stages of the project. A population of 150 students of the ninth grades will be beneficiaries; in turn, 20 students will generate tools for social appropriation of health information aimed at the locality. The tools will answer the lack of access to critical and precise information about the Rational Use of Medicines (URM) and the problems around the concept that are of interest the health of the Colombian population. Thus, it seeks to identify members of the community with interest and projection in this area to train and conscientize on the problematic of access to medicines in Colombia. The design of the first stage is the design of sessions and dissemination material focused on socializing the concept of URM, through the study of the productive chain and its conditions of execution in Colombia.
