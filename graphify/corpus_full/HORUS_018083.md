# Evidence-based clinical standard: diagnosis and treatment of the patient with systemic lupus erythematosus at the National University Hospital.

Systemic lupus erythematosus is a chronic disease, of autoimmune origin, can affect any organ, an adequate and early process of diagnosis and classification is fundamental, produces serious sequelae, generates irreversible damage and can compromise the life of the sufferer. It is estimated that the incidence is 0.3 to 31.5 cases per 100,000 people per year and the prevalence is 3.2 to 517.5 cases per 100,000 people. The comprehensive care of these patients from diagnostic support to treatment involves multiple medical specialties. Standardizing the diagnostic process and treatment is of supreme importance. This Clinical Standard based on Evidence, aims to identify the indications for the diagnosis and comprehensive treatment of patients with lupus attended at the National University Hospital, has as target population the adult patients with Lupus attended in external consultation and hospitalization, is aimed at professionals involved in the care of patients with lupus.
