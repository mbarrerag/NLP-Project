# CONCEPTIONS ON THE ROLE OF HUMAN BEING IN CLIMATE CHANGE.

In this work, we investigated the conceptions of the human being as an outstanding factor in climate change (CC) (anthropogenic global warfare, AGW). By approaching the student to the scientific controversy, the AGW theories and the natural one of the CC were contrasted to review the change of conceptions in this regard. The results showed that the intervention helps to construct critical sense and scientifically literate about the climate, offering a contribution to the training of science professors
