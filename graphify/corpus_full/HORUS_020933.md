# Effects of official speeches on urban modernization on cultural practices of the Afro-descendant population of Tumaco between 1979 and 1986

The 1980s in Tumaco brought political, economic, social and cultural transformations to its population, due to the State's efforts to modernize the city as a result of the effects of the 1979 earthquake. These discourses affected various cultural and daily practices of the population by materializing in public policies of urban reorganization. This research seeks to analyze the effects of official discourses on the modernization of the city on the cultural practices of the Afro-descendant population in Tumaco between 1979 and 1986, through the theory of Stuart Hall's Cultural Studies and using a qualitative methodology with a deductive method. This produced a series of syncretisms and tensions between traditional cultural elements and customs of African descent and the new needs derived from a modern and planned state organization that can be seen reflected in the visit of Pope San Juan Paul II to Tumaco in 1986
