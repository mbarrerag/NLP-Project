# interstitial lung Disease severe in a patient with antisynthetase syndrome by anti

Due to the introduction of new diagnostic tests, it is possible to detect the disease earlier and to determine the best treatment strategy. However, in our country the availability of these tests is limited. We present the case of a patient with antisynthetase syndrome associated with anti-PL7 antibodies in whom, thanks to the early identification of these antibodies, timely initiation of treatment for the disease was achieved
