# FREQUENCY OF DELIRIUM IN OLDER ADULTS IN INTENSIVE CARE UNIT

To describe the frequency of delirium in older adults hospitalized in an intensive care unit. Cross-sectional observational study, convenience sampling. Population: 74 hospitalized adults over 60 years of age. the nursing delirium detection scale (nu-desc) was applied. The analysis was conducted using relative and absolute frequencies for categorical variables, as well as measures of central tendency and dispersion in numerical variables. The elderly patients were characterized by being mostly men 57% (n= 42), respiratory and cardiac admission morbidity, with suspicion of delirium according to the NuDESC scale of 54% (n=40) and categorized according to the score of the Apache scale for the probability of mortality, finding that patients who presented delirium 26% (n=65) had 85% mortality. The prevalence of 54% of delirium in the elderly in critical condition, and predisposing factors such as chronic conditions, age over 60 years, and risk of death due to critical illness. The implementation of the NuDESC scale enables the identification of acute behavioral changes and the timely implementation of strategies to prevent and reduce the frequency of delirium
