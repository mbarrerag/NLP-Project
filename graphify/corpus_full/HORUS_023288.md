# ASSISTANCE AND MANAGEMENT OF COMMONS. A COMPRENSION FROM SOCIAL WORK

The patrimonialization and management of commons are analytical categories that allow us to understand in the social sciences and, specifically in the discipline of Social Work, the consolidation of the natural will, identity and the sense of community among human beings that interact in the territories. First, patrimonization is a process linked to the consolidation of social and symbolic capitals product of exchanges - interested or disinterested - of goods and services meaningful to individuals and groups, through which they build alliances of kinship, neighbourhood and friendship. The valuation of practices, objects and values is historically consolidated in the territories as a result of binding histories between people that assign value to cultural and natural goods. Second, the management of commons results from the transformations in the modern society of individuals that resist the imposition of a heritage vision from the monumentalist perspective, tangible and material. Faced with the exclusion of large social sectors and the loss of value of popular traditions, the subjects delimit dynamics of collective heritage, from social and community action, around objects objectified intergenerally through memory
