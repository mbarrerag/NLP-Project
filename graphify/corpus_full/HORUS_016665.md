# VALIDITY AND CONFIDENCE OF THE SPANISH VERSION OF THE SCALE OF PERCEPTION AND SATISFACTION WITH THE DISEASE CARE (CUCACE) IN COLOMBIA

The quality definition of quality, when applied to nursing care, is multidimensional and complex [2]. Many studies do not even attempt to define quality. This implies that doing so limits its meaning and that any definition necessarily reflects a value judgment on the part of the author. Quality is often associated with a degree of conformity with current standards [3]. This indicates that quality can be defined in various ways in relation to the research instruments used. It can be defined as the characteristics of the environment in which nursing care is provided and as the types of processes and activities that nurses perform when providing care to patients. The evaluation of the quality of nursing care, from the perspective of the profession, involves services rendered, i.e., nursing processes and activities [4]. Objective Evaluating the validity, reliability and applicability of the nursing care satisfaction scale (CUCACE) in a hospital of IV level of care in Colombia.
