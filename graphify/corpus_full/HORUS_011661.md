# Sufficiency and social sustainability of the COLOMBIAN INDIVIDUAL AHORM PENSIONAL SYSTEM

The Faculty of Law, Political and Social Sciences and the Center for Thought in Health of the National University of Colombia have focused their academic efforts to study the reality and perspective of the pension phenomenon, under the legal, economic, actuarial, demographic and social scenarios. To this end, researchers and academics, national and international, were invited to participate in this process. They addressed, for their analysis, some of the most relevant topics on the configuration, development, achievements and inadequacies of the two pension regimes that structure the dual or parallel pension system in Colombia. In the writings, analyses and answers to some questions (considered), either under the configuration of structural reforms, or, by reforms of a parametric nature. This text has, as its central purpose, to reflect from the Academy and contribute to the open and public debate, in Colombian society, on the approaches and proposals to overcome the precarious situation of the social security system, full of uncertainties, with new risks coming from globalization, and the incorporation into the citizen life of the technologies that accompany the fourth industrial revolution, and demand new forms of social protection and of overcoming the gender gap
