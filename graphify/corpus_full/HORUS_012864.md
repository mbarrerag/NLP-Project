# Factors determining career choice: analysis applied to applicants and students in the first semesters of higher education programs

The choice of a responsible professional career favors adaptation and permanence in higher education, as well as labor market insertion; elements that, together with others, account for the relevance of a higher education program. The objective of this article is to determine the main motivations for career choice by applicants and students of education programs. These motivations are identified from surveys of applicants to undergraduate academic programs offered by the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Medellin and Universidad de Antioquia. Additionally, the data collected through a questionnaire filled out by first semester students of the Institución Universitaria de Envigado are analyzed. Finally, it is identified that, in relation to the data analyzed, there are practical implications for the generation of predictive models as an extended structure of the information systems of the Ministry of National Education that allow supporting the analysis of the relevance of Higher Education programs
