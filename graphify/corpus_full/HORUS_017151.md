# ASSESSMENT OF THE ANTI-PARASIS EFFECT OF THE PAPAYA (CARICA PAPAYA) SEED IN EQUINES

The effect of the papaya seed (Carica papaya) as an antihelmintic has been evaluated in other species, where it has been proven its effectiveness. To evaluate the antiparasitic effect of the papaya seeds on the gastrointestinal parasites in horses. Coprological examination was carried out at six horses (3 males and 3 females) to determine the number of eggs per gram of feces (parasitic load) using the McMaster technique, the animals had an average age 6.1 years (range 1-16 years) and were located in a farm of the corregimiento of Santa Helena Antioquia, in permanent grazing with kikuyo meadows (Cenchrus clandestinus). 8 grams of papaya seed were supplied and dehydrated, by oral work in single dose mixed with 200 g of concentrate. The seeds were dehydrated at the same time and exposed to the environment in a laboratory room (not to the sun) for seven days.
