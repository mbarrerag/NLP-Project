# Anthropic permanence strategies in the midst of the spatial competition between tourism and agriculture. Case, vereda El Espinal

Anthropic strategies within the framework of spatial competence - between second-residence tourism and agriculture - are a central issue in the promotion of rural well-being. However, the management of natural resources and culture must be agencyd inclusively in order not to put at risk future generations. Academic initiatives undertaken in the Near West, through projects of Solidary Extension, and materialized in graduate research such as the _x0093_Evaluation of the multifunctionality of agriculture of the Systems of Traditional Production of the Near West of Antioquia-Colombia_x0094_ support the institutional mission and support integral training outside and within the class classroom; as well as, to the close links with various sectors of the region for territorial union. Thus, the present study seeks to contribute to the deductive reasoning of residential tourism in Santa Fe de Antioquia from the context of the determined (vered the Espinal) and to take into account the innovative methodology to allow responsible operationality of industries (non-agricultural activities) and agroecosystems (agricultural activities) without threatening the well-being of the environment and of the associations.
