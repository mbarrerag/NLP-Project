# ECOSYSTEM OF DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION INTERSEDES GOVERNANCE, EXPERIENCES AND STALENT IN DIGITAL CULTURE

Technological adoption remains a driving force of change at the global level, and education is not an exception. It is recognized that higher education institutions, when they find themselves at a turning point, see in digital transformation an opportunity to transform the educational model, allowing the adoption of disruptive changes (Jiménez and Andrés, 2022).Under the leadership of Rector Dolly Montoya, the National University of Colombia (unal) has taken the challenge of leading an ambitious digital transformation project that covers all its headquarters.This process involves an organizational change driven by the integration of digital technologies, with the aim of improving and innovating in the processes, which in turn requires a cultural change.In addition, initiatives have been generated to undertake innovations in pedagogical, research and project and process management areas, with the aim of strengthening the relationship of the one with the territories of the country and the world (Herrera and Pérez, 2023).The mission of the one goes beyond academic training; it seeks to create integral, responsible and autonomous citizens, oriented to be agents of change with ethical and social awareness
