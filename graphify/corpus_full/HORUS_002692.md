# Museums and ceramics: Contemporary views of a tradition

The present Degree Work groups the three components that require the Master in Museumology and Heritage Management to obtain the title of Magister. Each document is the result of diverse experiences around multiple museum reflections. The first component is a conceptual essay entitled Museums and ceramics: Contemporary views of a tradition. Case studies of the exhibition “The mud has a voice” of the Museum of Antioquia, the Museum of Ceramics of El Carmen de Viboral and the Museum of Ceramics of Barichara, where I focused on the emergence of these three exhibition spaces as meeting points between artistic and artisan ceramics and their respective role in different museum spaces. Through the case study some categories of enunciation and analysis are launched to understand the insertion of these practices and the knowledge of there derived in the museum field.
