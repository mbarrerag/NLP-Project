# DYNAMIC OF AGROECHOSYSTEMS UNDER THE APPROACH OF SOCIOECHOLOGIC SYSTEMS. CASE OF STUDY: A HYDROGRAPHIC FOURTH OF GREAT RIUM AND CHICO RIUM

The individual and/or collective decision-making processes related to agricultural production, which interact with the natural environment over time horizons that extend from one day to several decades, have an impact on the dynamics of agroecosystems and the provision of ecosystem services. Agroecosystems can be analyzed through three subsystems that interact: decision subsystem, operational subsystem and biophysical subsystem. This article proposes the analysis of the dynamics of agroecosystems under the socio-ecological systems approach in the Río Grande and Río Chico Hydrographic Basin located in the Colombian Andes, strategic in the provision of drinking water for the Metropolitan Area of the Aburrá Valley, the second largest populated center in the country. The analysis of the dynamics of agroecosystems was carried out through the identification of the variables of each subsystem and its interrelations based on a systematic review of literature and an analysis of the trajectories of the socio-ecological system of the basin, its socio-economic, productive and environmental characteristics.
