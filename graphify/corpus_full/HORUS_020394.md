# Bromological characterisation of fruit residues

ABSTRACT In Colombia, a large amount of fruit residues are currently generated. These cause a negative environmental impact due to their high organic load. However, this type of residues present in their compound structure that can be used in order to reduce the environmental impact and obtain an added value. The aim of this study was to characterise bromatologically sixteen residues of fruit processing in Colombia, in order to propose a possible use in the food industry. The bromatory characterisation of the residues was made by quantifying dry matter (ASTM E1756-08), ash (ASTM E1755-01), proteins (Kjeldahl method), gross fats (Official Method AOAC), total dietary fiber (AOAC 993.21) and carbohydrates. It was found that the seeds of grapes and guanabana, lulo skin and vat of tree tomatoes have the highest dietary fiber content (AOAC method), gross fats (AOAC) and total dietary fiber (AOAC) and carbohydrates.
