# Snooping through the windows. Watched for violence in six Colombian fiction films

This research explores the look and the point of view in six Colombian fiction films: Rodrigo D. no futuro (víctor gaviria, 1990), la playa d.c (juan andrés arango, 2012), la vendadora de rosas (víctor gaviria, 1998), la tierra y la sombra (césar acevedo, 2015), agarrado pueblo (carlos mayolo, luis ospina, 1977) and coraje (libia stella gómez, 2024). The five-motive análises represent the protagonist’s gaze, the representation of the lack of future for children and young people, the representation of the female character, the look at the impact of families and the way of representing violent death; it leads us to an analysis of the way Colombian filmmakers have taken to see their surrounding reality (Aragón de la vención, de las án contigos las úrgidas de se llán. way in which Colombian filmmakers have given to see their surrounding reality. Other aspects are also addressed such as: the poetry of each filmmaker, the ethics involved in focusing on the victims of violent events and the way in which our films are viewed in other scenarios, specifically in European funds and festivals.
