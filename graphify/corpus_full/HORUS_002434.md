# OCCUPATION TERAPEUTS PRACTICES IN HIGHER EDUCATION

The article presents experiences of occupational therapists in inclusive higher education, carried out at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia and systematized from records that document them, in dialogue with the author's experience. Four practice areas are identified: in the administrative direction, in the accompaniment of students, in university policies and in the observatory of inclusion. The Vice-Deanship of Welfare at the Faculty of Medicine at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia is an administrative and managerial position that has been assumed since the late 1980s by occupational therapists. Since 2004, there is a significant professional participation in programmes aimed, especially, at the accompaniment of applicants and students with disabilities and which eventually resulted in an institutional policy. The creation of the Observatory of Inclusion has been a scenario of developing the functions of occupational therapists and a key strategy to influence the daily life of different groups that demanded that their voices and feelings be received at the university. In addition, some approaches regarding occupation in the university context are synthesized, a topic that several occupational therapists have given importance
