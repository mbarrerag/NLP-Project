# Analysis of the river dynamics of the Guamal River in order to assess and propose flood mitigation works for the municipality of Guamal, Department of Meta

The Guamal River is part of the most important tributaries of the Meta Department. Its great water potential has been a key source of development for the populations that are settled on its banks. However, historically it has represented a significant threat to the inhabitants of the area due to its tendency to overflow and to produce river floods. With the aim of proposing and evaluating different solutions that allow to counteract the floods produced on a specific sector located in the vicinity of the urban district of the municipality of Guamal, in the development of this document the results of a detailed study of the hydraulic behavior of the river to the Guamal river has been presented, which has focused on the estimation of its geomorphological, hydrological and river parameters and on the evaluation of its hydrodynamic conditions through the two-dimensional hydraulic model IBER.
