# Approach for analyzing landslide and torrential flow susceptibility conditions in relation to landscape evolution in the northern Colombian Andes

The analysis of the conditions of susceptibility due to landslides and torrential avenues is an important topic to develop different strategies that allow to improve the territorial order. These processes are recurrent in the Colombian Andes affecting people every year in addition to generating economic losses. The analysis of the landscape requires the quantification of the topographic characteristics. This study uses drainage networks as the way in which the morphogenesis is connected with the morphodynamics. A set of 25 morphometric indices and tools of spatial analysis were used to characterize 168 watersheds of the northern Colombian Andes, in order to identify anomalous patterns in their distribution and examine them in function of the inventories of landslides and torrential avenues collected.
