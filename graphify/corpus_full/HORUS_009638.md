# THE EXPERIENCE OF THE BODY, CREATIVITY AND CO-CREATION IN PANDEMIA TIMES.

The arts of the body as a celebration of life and encounter is a work that brings together the research results of various Latin American, European and Asian academics around the body, as a scenario of disciplinary tensions that contribute to the epistemological understanding of arts-based research, research-creation and semiotic-sensitive research
