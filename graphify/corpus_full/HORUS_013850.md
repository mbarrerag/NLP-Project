# Imagine being a link: phagiosimbiosis

Imagine being a link: fagiosimbiosis is a research-creation project around the re-signification of interspecies relationships using symbiosis as a concept and metaphor, promoting life practices with which everyone can build a relationship from affections and not from the utilitarian. This project makes use of the intersections between feeding, personal narrative, pedagogy, biology and performance
