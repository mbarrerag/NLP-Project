# MICROBIOLOLOGICAL AND MOLECULARY TECHNICALS FOR SP SP DISALMONE IDENTIFICATION IN THE ADVICE INDUSTRY: A SCOPE SYSTEMICAL REVIEW

A systematic review of the diagnostic methods described in the literature was carried out for the identification and characterization of species of the genus Salmonella. The review was carried out using publications from 2015 to 2021 recorded in the database of NCBI/PubMed, ScienceDirect, Scopus, SciELO, Google Scholar and Springer, applying the PRISMA methodology. 61 articles were analyzed, grouping data according to the country of origin, sample type, technique used, microbiology protocol employed and genes used in molecular tests. Microbiology protocols are based on pre-enrichment tests in peptonic water and enrichment in tetrationate (TT) and Rappaport-Vassiliadis (RV), especially with mues-tras of feces or environmental samples, to subsequently perform the isolations in selective and differential media
