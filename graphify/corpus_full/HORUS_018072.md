# THE ARBOLOCO, ENVIRONMENT AND CONTEXT

The different ecosystems and the search for ecological support of a country or region are an important part of the preservation of natural resources of forest and fauna origin, with which nature has endowed the populations that live them with exceptional environments. It is surprising how many of these resources, represented by species already biologically classified, are about to become extinct or no longer exist; however, with their resilience, nature fights the adversity of human aggression. Still, there are other species that have not been discovered and others that have not yet been given a scientific name
