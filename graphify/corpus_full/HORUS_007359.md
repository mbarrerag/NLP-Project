# Family relations and filio-parental violence: narratives of three women in the city of Bogotá, 2024

This research analyzes the meanings of family relations and filio-parental violence, based on the narratives of three women residing in different localities of the city of Bogotá in 2024. It was developed from a qualitative approach, based on the postulates of social constructionism, the systemic perspective and the theory of Coordinated Management of Meaning (CMM), as well as the semi-structured interview, the field diary, the genogram, the map of networks and the letters. The narratives of Laura, Martha and Cecilia allow to understand the meanings attributed to family relations with their children, their experiences of violence, and the crucial role played by the support networks —families, social and institutional — in the way in which they face violence and reconstruct their relationships. Through their narratives, it is understood how the dynamics of power, gender roles and cultural expectations have co-constructed their relationships and identities.
