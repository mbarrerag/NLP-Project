# Appearance Validation of a Water Insecurity Scale: Qualitative Assessment in Risaralda, Colombia, as Part of a Multicentric Latin American Study

To validate the appearance of the scale of insecurity of the water (Household Water Insecurity Experiences Scale, HWISE) in a low-resource population in Colombia, as part of the Multicentric Latin American study. The qualitative validation of the translation of the HWISE scale was carried out through focal groups with women located in rural and urban areas of the Department of Risaralda. In accordance with the inclusion and exclusion criteria of the main guide of the study, women beneficiaries of the modalities of care of the Food and Nutritional Security programme of the Government were summoned. Using a non-probabilistic sample, women from 12 to 15 women were invited to participate by focus group. In total, 4 focal groups, two by population, were realized, a total of 40 women. Categories and emerging issues are revealed that allow to know if the instrument of the scale of perception of insecurity translated into Spanish maintained the concept of questions and key words to obtain the data sought. women beneficiaries of the care modalities of the Food and Nutritional Security program of the Government “Risaralda without Hunger” were summoned. Using non-probability sampling, 12 to 15 women per focus group were invited to participate. In total, 4 focus groups were held, two per population, a total of 40 women. Emerging categories and themes are reviewed that allow us to know if the water insecurity perception scale instrument translated into Spanish languages the concept of the questions and key words to obtain the data sought. It is then found that the review of the translation allowed under understanding of the questions and key words in agreement with their original in the English language
