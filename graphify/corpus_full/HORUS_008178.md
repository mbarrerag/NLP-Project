# In-silico study of the interaction between insulin glargine and the hybrid IR/IGF1R receptor

Insulin glargrigin binds to IR-IGF1R family receptors, including the insulin (IR) and IGF1 (IGF1R) receptor that regulate metabolism, division and cell differentiation. The cells also express hybrid IR/IGF1R receptors, which have been associated with mitogenic effects in vitro. Although there is considerable information on the structure and mechanism for the activation of IR and IGF1R receptor receptors, little is known about the recognition and activation of hybrid receptors. By means of In-sili approach, propose a hypothetical structural model of the hybrid receptor IR/IGF1R and study the glargine-receptor interaction in that model.
