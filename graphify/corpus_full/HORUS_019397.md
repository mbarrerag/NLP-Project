# COMPARISON OF SOME METHODS FOR THE MODEL OF PROPOSAL COX RISKS FOR INTERVAL CENSURE DATA

The data with censorship at interval are common in several areas of knowledge, such as: epidemiology, finance, demography, medicine, among others. They occur when the event of interest, the time of failure, is not exactly observed, but is within some interval of the observation time. Often in this situation a imputation of the data is made that are not exactly known. Some of the multiple imputation methods proposed in the literature are the algorithm PMDA (Poor Man's Data Augmentation) and the algorithm ANDA (Asymptotic Normal Data Augmentation), which allow to estimate the parameters of the proportional risk model of Cox using classical estimation methods.There are also alternative methods to make these estimates, such as the ICM algorithm (Iterative Convex Minorant) and a Bayesian approach, which do not perform imputation of the data with censorship at interval.In this work a comparison was made via simulation of the estimators of the parameters of the Cox model produced by each of the above mentioned methods.The results showed that in general terms the MCI values and the high approach was not higher
