# POLQUETES (ANNELIDA) OF THE ISLANDS CAYES OF ALBURQUERQUE AND SERRANILLA CAYES, RESERVE OF THE SEAFOWER BIOSPHOR, CARIBBEAN COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

The polychaetes are the most abundant marine invertebrates, with a valuable contribution of biomass to the Trophca chain and to the balance of ecosystems. In the Colombian Caribbean, the areas with the greatest information deficit on marine biodiversity are the islands. In order to know the communities of polychaetes of the Seapower Biosphere Reserve, in the Colombian island Caribbean, studies were carried out between 2017 and 2018 in the Cayos de Serranilla (CS) and Cayos de Albuquerque (CA) islands to generate an inventory of polychaetes of these two areas. Using dredges, net, autonomous diving and by apnea it collected in both cayos islands revealed a total of 30 families, 152 genera and 226 species. Families with the highest number of species were Syllidae (56 species), Eunicidae (23 species), Spionidae (16 species) and Sabellidae (14 species). The present list contains 86 new registers of genera and 172 species of polychaetes in the Colombian Caribbean
