# Publishing action research by teachers of english

Action research (AR) by teachers of English is regarded as an option to motivate prospective teachers and practitioners to examine and systematise their teaching practice. Its contributions to teachers' professional development are also highlighted in different fora. However, the results of their studies are not always present in specialised publications, such as academic journals. With the purpose of making their voices visible, the journal Profile: Issues in Teachers' Professional Development, edited at Universidad Nacional de Colombia, welcomes contributions by action researchers whose reports can get published together with other kinds of research in the areas of English language teaching and teacher education. This chapter depicts an ethnographic case study that focused on the experiences of authors writing AR for publication, the difficulties they found during the process, and the impact that both doing AR and publishing has had on them and on their professional development. The results of the study show that although authors gained knowledge in academic writing for publication, there are implications concerning the roles action researchers, teacher education programmes, and editorial teams play in disseminating AR via academic journals
