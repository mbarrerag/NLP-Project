# WOMAN IS SCIENCE

Throughout our history on Earth, human beings have done amazing things in the search for the secrets of nature, venturing from the depths of the sea to outer space. Science has been an essential tool to be able to answer many of these questions and today remains a key piece for the development of society; in it, women have played a fundamental role. In this book we will take a tour of the lives of ten Colombian and ten Latin American scientists, you will know about their areas of research, the achievements they have achieved and what their motivations have been, which are of great inspiration for many more girls to walk the fascinating path of science
