# TERMOHALIN STABILITY AND STRUCTURE OF THE WATERS OF THE GERLACHE STRATEGY

The knowledge of the vertical structure of the water column allows us to understand the mixing processes has the distribution of water masses and currents.The present study contributes to the identification of the water layers?the thermohalin components of the stability and the vertical fine structure of the Gerlache Strait.The region is located west of the Antarctic Peninsula? between the Danco coast and the Palmer archipelago? framed in the 64° S - 65° S and 61° W - 64° W. The information 0.25x0.25 monthly and annual of the World Ocean Atlas (WOA18) was used to analyze the variables Temperature (T¿ ° C) and Salinidad (S) of the climate period 1981 ¿ 2010.The distribution of the Superficial Temperature of the Sea (TSM) and the Superficial Salinity of the Sea (SSM) was described in the uppermost part of the Türth and south of the Strait (E) The vertical stability (SSM) was assessed in the central part of the Herup and the central part of the Helocline. The N values showed that the mixing processes are mainly developed at the surface level in summer and to a lesser extent in winter. The fine structure in the Gerlache Strait showed above 60 m absolute stability (EA) did below 60 m prevail the stability of Convection by Layers (CC) observing a stratification by Saline Fingers (DS) from 300 to 325 m and from 375 to 400 m in some stations. It is suggested to analyze the vertical structure of the water column with information in situ that allows to detail the dynamics of the currents and the distribution of the water masses in the Gerlache Strait
