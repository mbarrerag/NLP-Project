# Effect of changes in land cover on direct potential recharge in the Free Aquifer of the Aburra Valley

It is of great importance to understand the effects of changes in land coverage on direct potential recharge (RPD), especially in areas where accelerated urban growth and pressures are presented by changes in land cover. In this study, the effects of changes in land coverage were analyzed (the RPD Free Aquifer of the Aburrá Valley, an aquifer located in an urban environment, with important anthropic interventions and rapid urban growth, located in the Andean region of Colombia. To represent the changes in land coverage, coverage maps were generated from the classification of satellite images using the Random Forest algorithm, four (4) scenarios were defined for the years 1990, 2010, and 2020, the classification model was meticulously calibrated and validated, achieving the Kappa coefficients above 0,87 in all scenarios.
