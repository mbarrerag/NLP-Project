# OF JERACKIC ADMINISTRATION TO EFFECTIVENESS

The research reverses a great interest for researchers, academia and organizations, allowing the design, construction and implementation of a more reliable administrative model, making the authority and its different components in the administrative process, to maintain causality with the conditions of openness, flexibility and adaptation that suggest all the resources of an organization in the twenty-first century; as well as redefining some variables and procedures that through the TOC process (theory of contrasts theories of restrictions), lead to the effectiveness of the institutions. From the novelty, this work proposes the dimensionality of a new organizational model, that highlights the integrality of organizational decisions, and allows to establish guidelines of improvement in the use of the resources of the organizations, whose primary purpose is to increase profitability. It also expects to conform to the considerations of the most relevant elements of judgment to improve the management and decision-making process in the organizations, so that its results are more critical, reliable and effective. The usefulness, although a lot evident, refers initially to the configuration of a system that is more suited to the companies today and to the improvement of their processes of planning; it will also allow us to have the greater sense of internal decisions
