# The rhetoric of emotional capitalism: Speech by Colombian political youtubers in the context of the 2016 Plebiscite for Peace

This doctoral thesis is the result of the study of five Colombian commentators who used Youtube as a platform for the visibility of their assessments of the Plebiscite for Peace, 2016. The concept of emotional capitalism is the backbone of this thesis, which is joined by a series of notions and theoretical perspectives of the Social Sciences that contribute to the strengthening of an inter- and transdisciplinary approach that understands the discourse contained in the audiovisual productions of these opinion makers as a socio-cultural expression of a strongly emotional rhetoric. This supported the elaboration and application of a methodological proposal in which the interview of the chosen youtubers is combined, with the analysis of content and the multimodal analysis to a total of 58 videos in which the intention of these digital speakers is evidenced by the attention and provokes reactions in potential audiences interested in consuming public information related to the aforementioned situation 2016.
