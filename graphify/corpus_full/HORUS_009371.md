# TOWARDS THE IMPLEMENTATION OF LEARNING RESULTS IN REGULATE RESULTS OF ENGINEERING. CASE OF STUDY: SEGNAL PROCESSING

In recent years, the curricula of the subjects that are part of the engineering programs worldwide have migrated from a content-based approach to another based on competencies. More recently and in the particular case of Colombia, it has evolved to a learning results-oriented approach. One of the pedagogical challenges at present, has to do with how to implement, monitor and evaluate these Learning Results, in the framework of the development of a regular subject of an undergraduate program. This work discusses the preliminary results of the application of two strategies in the course of Signal Processing, mainly taught to the Electrical Engineering and Control Engineering programs, in the School of Mines of the National University of Colombia, Medellín. These two strategies correspond to the development of laboratories at home, which are mandatory in nature, and to the approach of challenges, which are offered as an optional activity in the subject.
