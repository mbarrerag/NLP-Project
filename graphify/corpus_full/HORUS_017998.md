# METHODOLOGY FOR ROSE-BURDER ESTIMATION OF PARAMETERS IN MATHEMATIC MODELS OF BIOTECHNOLOGIC PROCESS

Biotechnology can provide solutions to current problems through the generation of economically viable, environmentally sustainable and socially responsible industrial processes. Mathematical models describing these types of processes often present challenges such as non-linearity and lack of representation of the phenomena involved, overparametrization, uncertainty and lack of information in experimental data, non-convex optimization problems for their tuning, among others, which may negatively affect the quality of the mathematical model. This paper proposes an extended methodology using established but not used methods as a whole for robust estimation of parameters in mathematical models of biotechnological processes that considers data cleaning, structural identification of parameters, suitability of experimental design, uncertainty in experimental data, descriptive quality and predictive quality of the mathematical model.
