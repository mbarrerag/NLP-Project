# Automatic creation of investment portfolios using financial analytics.

In this work, a prototype simulation is presented to evaluate different strategies of Algorithmical Commerce in the Colombian financial market; this in order to analyze if it is possible to incorporate this type of strategies by investors. To construct strategies, different types of Artificial Intelligence models are made use, such as neural networks, random forests and logistic regression, which predict the trend of the price of the following day. These predictions are transformed into signals of purchase and sale of the actions that allow the daily configuration of the portfolio. The different simulations allow to establish the strategy that achieves the highest profitability for the investor, which in the scenario of this work consists of the selection of 11 actions and a different type of model for each asset according to its best predictive performance. The different simulations allow establishing the strategy that achieves the highest return for the investor, which in the strategy proposed in this work consists of the choice of 11 actions and a different type of model for each asset according to its best predictive performance.
