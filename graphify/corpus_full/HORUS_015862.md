# EFFECT OF THE BLOOD POINT ON CRUTCHED CRIME WITH ANGUS AND HOLSTEIN, IN ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA, ANTIOCIA, PRODUCTION SYSTEM

The White breed Orejinegro (BON) is a Creole breed with a high level of adaptability to the South American tropics. It is used in Colombia mainly in cross-breeding programs for meat and milk production. There are also cattle breeds that use it pure for meat production purposes. This research aimed to evaluate the effect that different proportions of the breed BON has on weight at birth (PN), in cross-breeding with animals of the breeds specialized in meat and milk production, Aberdeen Angus and Holstein respectively. A total of 64 registers of births reported between the years 2014 and 2022 were analyzed. All corresponded to offspring born in the Paysandú Agricultural Station, located in Medellín Antioquia, owned by the National University of Colombia. This indicates that for every 1% increase in the blood percentage of BON the PN decreased by 51 Grs. These results indicate that crossing with BON is an excellent alternative to decrease the weight at the birth of the offspring, without leading to productive deterioration of the production system, since the weights of the individuals were within normal ranges.
