# Immersion in the study of the different fields of medical physics

This research project has as main objective to strengthen the medical physics research seedling of the national university of Colombia, focused on the topics of radiation therapy, radiation protection, radiobiology and applied engineering in the science of materials. Medical physics plays a fundamental role in the application of physical technologies and principles in the field of health, especially in the diagnosis and treatment of diseases by ionizing radiation as well as in instrumentation for the quantification of ionizing radiation. The research is based on the immersion of students in an active and practical learning environment, where they can acquire theoretical knowledge and practical skills related to the medical physics at undergraduate and postgraduate levels. Through workshops, laboratory sessions and collaboration with professionals in the field, students will be introduced into the essential concepts of radiation therapy, radiation protection, radiobiology and applied engineering in the field of materials. The expected results include the formation of a group of students with a solid understanding of the fundamentals of medical physics and its applications in medicine. In addition, this research seedling is expected to generate projects and academic work related to the optimization of radiation therapy techniques, the development of radiation therapy and the applied risks
