# CORROSION OF STEEL BARS IN CONCRETEWITH RECYCLED AGGREGATE TREATED WITH DIAMMONIUM PHOSPHATE

This study focuses on a comparison of the corrosion kinetics of reinforcing steel in three types of concrete:conventional with natural aggregates, concrete with recycled aggregates from crushed concrete, and concrete with recycled aggregates from crushed concrete treated with diammonium phosphate (DAP). The literature shows that DAP improves the characteristics of recycled concrete aggregates and therefore the mechanical performance of concrete mixes is better, however, there is no clarity about the kinetics of corrosion in embedded steel. Three series of mixtures of cylindrical specimens with a water-binder ratio of 0.46 were prepared. Test specimens for each mixture were exposed to 3.5% NaCl aqueous solution, and the corrosion current density was measured using the resistance to polarization (Rp) technique. The corrosion kinetics results allowed establishing the minimum DAP concentration conditions to enhance the quality of the treated recycled concrete aggregates and prevent the deterioration of the reinforcing steel
