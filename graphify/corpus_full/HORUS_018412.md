# A pre-response model for the production of requirements in the context of software development.

The Eduction of Requirements (ER) focuses on identifying and characterizing stakeholders and their requirements. This activity can be challenging as the scope of the domain of the software product grows, generating errors and delays. The Natural Language Processing (PLN) is used to analyze, understand and generate natural language automatically. Software analysts use PLN-based approaches to improve the ER, making it more efficient and reliable. However, the scope of the domain and the limitation to understand the writing styles of the requirements documents generate important drawbacks for these approaches. In this Doctoral Thesis SQUARE (Scalable QEstion Answering for Requirements Elicitation by its acronym in English) is presented, a novel approach to improve the PNN-based approaches to ER based on Question-Response Systems (SPR), which includes a meta-restricted domain for ER. The PL-based approach to generate questions and answers related to ER. The SPRs are used to extract precise and concise answers to questions in natural language. The SQUARE model represents a contribution to the ER model
