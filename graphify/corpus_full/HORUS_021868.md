# Assessment of seismic threat at surface level in the city of Manizales incorporating detailed characteristics of rupture in the modeling.

The seismic threat is defined by the parameters of strong movement, in order to represent the intensity of movement in a given site of analysis. With this final mastery work, a seismic threat assessment was carried out at the surface level in the city of Manizales, based on a proposed methodology based on a rupture model, incorporating characteristics of rupture, normally excluded in classical methodologies, such as the distribution of the slip and the release of the moment. For this purpose, the software SISMAN LISA (G. Bernal et al., 2021) was used as a tool to perform the simulations at the rock level and at the surface level of earthquakes in the seismic event collection representing the seismic threat in the study area. Obtaining as a result maps of uniform threat of accelerations for different return periods and periods of vibration, along with threat curves and spectra of accelerations at certain points of importance in the city. Finally, when comparing the results obtained with results of previous studies made with probabilistic classic models, very similar maps of threat were obtained in their distribution and in the range of magnitudes taken
