# THE ROLE OF RENEWABLE ENERGIES IN DEVELOPING COUNTRIES.

Renewable energies, a term used mainly to refer to those contrary to fossil fuels (oil, coal, natural gas), have been booming in recent years because of their contribution to the environment. However, the relevance of these sources is also present in other contexts, as in the socio-economic case; and in these terms it is interesting to review their role in various aspects that can be problematic mainly in developing countries. An analysis is made of their inclusion as appropriate technology and object of interest in projects of community participation, in the relationship of electricity in the community, in the supply of water and in the generation of employment. It includes the study of specific cases such as solar cooking, the bottle of sunlight, and cases of successes of renewable energies as aid in improving the quality of life of the population
