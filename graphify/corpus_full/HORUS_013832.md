# Strengthening of the company for the production of Soap in the association Sol de Oriente del corregimiento San Diego, in Samana Caldas

The Sol de Oriente association, in the co-ordination of San Diego, in the Municipality of Samaná Caldas, was created approximately 12 years ago, especially by a group of mothers head of family and people victims of violence, of which approximately 90% of the population was targeted by the armed conflict, at different hands actors outside the law, such as the M-19, FARC, and ELN, this especially because of the strategic location of the municipality, very close to the department of Antioquia, and because of its extensive rural area. The Sol de Oriente foundation, has developed a business focused on the production of blue soap, with a great interest and enthusiasm of the partners. However, due to the rudimentary and little optimized process, the profits are minimal and the sustainability of the entrepreneurship becomes increasingly difficult. For this reason, this project proposes, as a work methodology, to generate strategies and methodologies for the optimization of the soap production process in the association Sol de Oriente in Samana, in order to achieve this objective, to implement this methodology and to achieve the objective of the commercial development
