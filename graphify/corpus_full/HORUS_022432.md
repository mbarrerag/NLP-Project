# The Magdalena River Waterworthiness Recovery Project and Related Environmental Conflicts

This article aims to document the Magdalena River Navegability Recovery Project in its floodplain, highlighting possible ecosystem and social impacts, strategies of territorialization and generated environmental conflicts. The Magdalena River Flood-Serious Dynamics depend on the productive cycle of its flood plains, the physiology, behavior and reproduction of fish, the culture of artisanal fishing and the livelihoods of the communities there. This complex territory is the Navegability Project, with a strategy of improving logistical performance based on the multimodality of transport, the Public-Private Associations (PPP) and the exploitation of the advantages of river transport. All this, with the aim of optimizing the transport of hydrocarbons and minerals previously extracted for foreign trade. These works would generate serious impacts on the diversity and complexity of the river, as a result of the attempt to fix and stabilize an environment that is dynamic and changing, which reflects the lack of knowledge of the river and the lack of basin vision. All this can affect the coastal communities, especially the artisanal fishermen that have built their territories around the river, and their new resistance to the river
