# Evaluation of the protective immune response stimulated by epitopes B derived from Plasmodium yoelii MSP-1 protein optimized for binding to molecules of the major class II histocompatibility complex of mouse

The model of malaria murine has been widely used in preliminary protective trials with those proteins of Plasmodium yoelii that are homologous to the proteins of P. vivax, when evaluating possible candidates for vaccine. The MSP1 protein is the most abundant protein located on the surface of the merzoite and is involved in the initial binding to the membrane of the red blood cell during the process of erythrocytes invasion. In this study, as a proof of concept, the protective immune response of epitopes B of the protein PyMSP1-42 kDa with an artificial epitope for binding to H2-IEd was characterized by the epitopes B under a functional restriction analysis in silicus, finding two regions preserved from the fragment 33 kDa and 19 kDa; based on this analysis, the synthesis of sixteen peptides covering the whole of the preserved regions and, in vitro trials, 3 peptides with capacity of binding to erythrocytes, capacity of inhibition of the invasion and antigenic capacity were identified.
