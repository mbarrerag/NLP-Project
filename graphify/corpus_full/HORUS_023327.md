# CURRICULARY INNOVATION OF THE SYSTEM ENGINEERING AND COMPUTATION PROGRAMME OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

This document is the synthesis of the work developed during the Diploma in Curricular Innovation in Engineering offered by the UNAL of equal denomination.The proposal of curriculum innovation of the Engineering System and Computing (ISYC) program of the National University of Colombia (UNAL) involves a holistic vision based on the evolution of Engineering and the areas of Systems and Computing, as well as its importance for the sustainable development of Colombian society, it glimpses the training of high quality professionals whose capacities allow them to address the economic, social and cultural challenges of the local and global context. Likewise, it indicates the importance of the collective participation of the academic community in the reflection about the context, which is directed to the improvement of Education in Engineering, where the central axis are the aspects from the perspective of education and the curriculum. The main objective is to innovate the curriculum in alignment with national and international standards, with the participation of all actors of the academic community. [1] [2] [4].
