# Simón Bolívar: Liberator and legislator of the Americas. Bolivarian ideas for the formation of the legislative branch (1812-1830).

The Liberator Simón Bolívar is generally remembered for his military prowess, but not for his constitutional ideas. During the independence process, Bolivar conceived politics on two fronts: the military and the constitutional; within the latter he was erected in the Lawgiver of America. This process was one of those that were born during the era of the revolution, the two-fold enlightened and industrial revolution, as well as during the revolutions of the Atlantic world, so that its nature was labyrinthine: in that time of great changes, a crucible of various ideas about the State, the republic and the constitutions converged. The Liberator-Legislator, cosmopolitan man, naturally was influenced by various traditions of political thought (both ancient and modern), by his travels and his political experiences, whose lessons were the insummation of his constitutional ideas.
