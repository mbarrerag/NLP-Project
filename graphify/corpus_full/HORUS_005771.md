# MULECULAR BLV DETECTION IN BOVINS AND ASSOCIATION WITH SOME RISK FACTORS/PROTECTION IN RISALDA HATE (COLOMBIA)

Leukosis is a disease produced by an oncogenic retrovirus belonging to the family Retroviridae. Although it is known for its worldwide distribution because it has caused great economic losses, especially in milk raising, in Risaralda (Colombia) few studies have been reported on the disease. This is why the objective of this work was to determine the prevalence of the bovine leukosis virus and its association with some risk/protection factors in bovine herds in this Colombian department. During 2018, blood samples were taken from 145 individuals of six herds located in Risaralda. DNA was extracted from samples by salting out and PCR was performed to detect the DNA of the provirus.
