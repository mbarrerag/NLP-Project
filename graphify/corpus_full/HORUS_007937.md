# Effective measurement of participation: Instrument for environmental governance

The objective of this article is to present the proposal of the General Effective Participation Index (IGPE) as a strategy to measure the quality of participation in environmental planning instruments. The IGPE is derived from a relational system of variables associated with participation in environmental issues, composed of four teamed dimensions and 22 variables. These dimensions are based on the conceptual development of the Environmental Territorial Governance Model (GOTA), whose objective is to strengthen participation in environmental management. The measurement was carried out through a primary census-type information collection instrument, which collected 343 effective surveys. As a result of the measurement, the IGPE is determined in the four plans analyzed to be at a medium level. It is observed that the participation and the conditions for its activation are solid in the initial stages of the elaboration of environmental planning processes, but require adjustments in procedures that allow for monitoring and monitoring. Thus, the understanding of the quality of participation allows the environmental authority to make adjustments in the processes of implementation of the plans drawn up
