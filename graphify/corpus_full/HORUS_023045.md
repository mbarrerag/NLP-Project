# MODULATION OF DOLLAR AND TRPV1 IN DENTAL PULPA

Dental pain is the main symptom in which patients consult dental professionals and is classified as a public health problem. The pathophysiology of dental pain is not fully known and the involvement of odontoblasts during the transduction process through transmembranal receptors, within which transitory potential channels (TPR) are found. Several subfamily channels of these channels have been studied in relation to pain, mainly vanyloids channels such as TRPV1 and TRPV4 and TRPA1 and TRPM8 channels. The study of cellular and molecular mechanisms that contribute to pain are fundamental elements for the development of new analgesics
