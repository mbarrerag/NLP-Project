# Arthur Schopenhauer and the denial of the will to live

Since the publication of The World as Will and Representation (1818) several authors have emphasized that, throughout the fourth book, Arthur Schopenhauer develops an element that is extremely problematic within the framework of his philosophy, namely: the denial of the will to live. The purpose of this research is to clarify the significance of this phenomenon by studying in detail the exhibition held in MVR I and the volume of its complements, showing the scopes and problems involved in the ethics of Schopenhauer .
