# Educational actions for the closure of the gender gap in the INGREDIENCE OF HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS IN COLOMBIA

This research paper describes one of the actions carried out by the Center of Thought for the Strengthening and Empowerment of Colombian Women in STEM ¿ CPMC ¿ and the Colombian Association of Engineering Facultys ¿ACOFI ¿ in response to the challenges of education to reduce gender inequality in Colombia.[1][2] In the framework of the social and cultural perspective, the gender perspective has a high relevance, in which it is stated the need to provide the same development opportunities to each person in the areas of education, work and economy, as well as the opportunity to expand their possibility of participation in each of the different fields of society and to generate adequate spaces of interaction and communication. One of the most relevant actions to contribute to gender equality is the training and training of all actors of the academic community of the Engineering Faculty in the topics that circumscribe the gender perspective. Therefore, the CPCM formulated the Workshop of Women in Engineering Faculty in Colombia is empowered, leading and committed to contribute to the awareness of the contribution and contribution to gender equality, gender equity and the inclusion of engineering as a focal factor in the gender perspective
