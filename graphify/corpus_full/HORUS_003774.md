# Automation of budget processes for construction projects based on the integration of management methodologies, ERP software and database management using Power Query

According to the acceptance that BIM has had in the construction industry in Colombia, it is pertinent to study from which other areas can be optimized processes. The budget represents a critical line for the projects, since the accuracy of this depends on the fulfilment of the economic goals proposed. Some of the factors of inefficiency of this activity are the incorrect approach of the working methodology, databases of amounts and inaccurate prices and a disconnection with the other areas of the company which generates an inadequate use of the information and technological tools. The application of new technologies and collaborative work methodologies represents today an advantage for the construction sector as it implies the generation of a more automated, digital and planned work, this research develops a proposal of elaboration and management of budgets of construction projects, starting from the integration of a BIM model, where you can extract very successful construction times with an international codification, with ERP software (Enterprise resource planning) responsible for managing business resources, optimizing this integration by means of the database management with the work of an internal Power Query. and technological tools.
