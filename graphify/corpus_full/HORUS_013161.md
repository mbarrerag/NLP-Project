# HEARING A CALL FOR GENDER EQUITY IN THE RADIOLOGY PRACTICE

This letter to the editor calls attention to the fact that Colombia has a higher index of gender inequality than in the United States or other countries of the first world, in the 18 radiology programs of the country, four are directed by women and in another nine women have important managerial positions. Likewise, we show that in the Sub-Pestiality of Paediatric Radiology of the National University 64% of graduates are women
