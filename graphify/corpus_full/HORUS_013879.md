# Pensions, thinking of a better future

In the last 30 years, overall different dynamics have been created from which Colombia has not been external, such as the transformation of the demographic curve and high rates of informality.The first phenomenon may be due to the contraceptive methods, family planning, education and longevity of the people; the second one, for its part, is generated by the institutionality and by a technological context that grows more than what it integrates labor.These dynamics lead to an imperative need to modify the pension systems, issue that had recommended the World Bank since 1994 in its report _x0093_oldness without crisis_x0094_, calling for a greater saving and a change in the paradigm of the pension by division.Although in Colombia a change was created in 1993, this was given in the wrong direction by putting to compete the public system with the private one, problem that the government of today tries to solve. Thus it is like the answer of the government, that it has not mentioned, how to reform structurally the pension system in Colombia to guarantee its sustainability, stability, coverage and equity? Different institutions have begun to give their concepts about the pension reform that Colombia requires, facing the project of Reformed groups and that it is based by the national government. The importance of a constant institutionality over time (sustainable, not only financial, but also taking into account other additional variables, such as the time when the current policy will be maintained without needing to reform it again); coverage; and the need for equity in both people's income and gender (taking into account the role of care and imbalances in the labour market in this matter). This study is relevant, regardless of whether the proposal submitted by the Government is finally approved for various reasons. First, because work will begin as soon as possible on this proposal, especially to understand the technical bases of the Colombian pension system and similar countries, in addition to the proposals put forward by other institutions, so that audiovisual dissemination products, infographics and/or graphics that inform the population of what is happening in this matter and thus take a position on this matter, seeking a more healthy and informed democracy.
