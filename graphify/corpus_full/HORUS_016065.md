# ESSENTIAL OIL EXTRACTION OF LIPPIA ALBA: A DIRECT EXPERIMENTAL STRATEGY FOR CHEMICAL LEARNING

Chemistry is not an attractive science in the school environment, not only because of the difficulties of understanding conceptual contents, but because of its apparent lack of applicability in everyday life. This raises the need to implement methodologies that contextualize the contents with the sociocultural environment of the students. This research applies an experimental didactic sequence for teaching-learning of organic functional groups, taking as central axis the extraction of the essential oil from the Lippia alba plant (Alivia-dolor). The strategy was applied in the Agricultural Educational Institution La Mina, located within the Kankuamo Indigenous Reguard, which has a model of own education, with focus on the mother nature; however, despite being in an environment full of natural and cultural richness, in practice, the same difficulties of the urban institutions in terms of teaching the sciences were presented. The work began with the realization of surveys about the use of the Alivia-dolor plant. Subsequently, it was consulted in the literature on organic compounds contained in the essential oil of this plant. Then, the didactic sequence was structured by means of collaborative work guides and the experimental development of the oil was applied
