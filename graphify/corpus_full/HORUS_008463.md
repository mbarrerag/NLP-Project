# Degradation of pharmaceuticals from municipal wastewater effects by ozone-based advanced oxidation processes

Due to the growing concern about the release of emerging concern pollutants (ECCs) in different water compartments, the need for effective and sustainable water treatment methods has become a significant issue. These CECs, which include several pharmaceutical active compounds (PhACs), personal care products and other pollutants, pose challenges to conventional water treatment processes.As a result, there is a growing need to implement alternative solutions to address this pressing problem and ensure the protection of our water resources.In this context, the application of advanced technologies such as water treatment processes by ozonation has emerged as a promising approach to addressing the removal of recalcitrant organic compounds, especially PhACs and safeguard the quality of our water sources.Ozonation stands out as a versatile and powerful technology capable of directly and selectively attacking organic compounds, especially under conditions of acidic pH.
