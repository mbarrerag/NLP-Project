# Project for the conservation and rehabilitation of the Pasaje Hernández urban complex

This work addresses as a case study the block of Pasaje Hernández, located the Historic Center of Bogotá, with the purpose of formulating strategies for its rehabilitation, starting from its understanding as an urban piece with heritage value and container of historically settled activities in the sector, some of which tend to disappear, while others currently cause the physical deterioration of the historical buildings, due to its intensive use. The study of the apple, composed of nineteen buildings built in different historical periods, allows to understand its transformation dynamics and its consequences on its conservation, identifying the Passage and the Hernández Building as the elements that morphologically fragment the colonial apple, becoming the cornerstone of the transformation of the apple and one of the first gestures of modernity in the city, associated with the commercial walk. Assuming in this way the apple as a heritage urban system, and as an indivisible part of the fabric from the Historical Center, the work proposes urban and management guidelines for the conservation and rehabilitation of the apple and its public space, based on the definition of intervention criteria that recognize its values from the cultural heritage, and is circumscribed in a reality as it is the cultural industry and its impact of the cultural modes of the cultural and its own. dynamics and its consequences about its conservation, identifying both Hernandez Passage and Building as the main elements that morphologically break up the colonial block. They become the keystone needed for the block transformation and one of the very first managements of modernity into the city: the commercial passage.
