# Mammals (Mammalia) from San José del Guaviare, Colombia

Abstract We present the results obtained during four years of mammal sampling in different plant coverages of the municipality of San José del Guaviare, Guaviare, Colombia. Between 2012 and 2017 we collected information on the diversity of mammals through sampling with fog nets, traps for small and medium mammals, trap cameras, direct observations, interviews and secondary information. We reported 121 species of native mammals for the municipality. We recorded a completeness of sampling of 64 % for bats, 60 % for small non-flying mammals and 100 % for medium and large mammals. We highlighted two geographic novelties for marsupials Marmosa waterhousei and Glironia venusta, and six novelties in bats Lonphorhina marinkellei, Phyllostomus latifolius, Glyphonycteris sylvestris, Glyphonycteris daviesi, Glossophaga commissari and Myotis keaysi. The wealth found is comparable with other long-term inventories of mammals neotropical, and presents the greatest diversity of species of bats in Colombia
