# Surgical management of erectile dysfunction and urinary incontinence in men treated with prostate cancer with radical prostatectomy: analysis of a retrospective cohort.

Context and Justification: In the literature it is reported that approximately 70% of patients treated with radical prostatectomy due to presenting prostate cancer, presenting erectile dysfunction and/or urinary incontinence; that they are sequelae that generate impact on the quality of life of patients. There are no regional (Latinoamerica) or national data on the number of patients presenting erectile dysfunction and/or urinary incontinence as sequelae and they are offered management of these sequelae. The knowledge of this information contributes to the evaluation in regional health that would allow identifying patterns of management of the sequelae of the surgical management of prostate cancer according to the characteristics of the Colombian population. To determine the number of patients presenting erectile dysfunction and/or urinary incontinence as sequelae of the management of prostate cancer with radical prostatectomy and receive management with implantation of ventricular prosthesis and/or artificial urinary sphincter, in a Colombian cohort. The information will be collected through a database of administrative data of patients belonging to the contributive regime in health from 01 January 2016 to 31 December 2019
