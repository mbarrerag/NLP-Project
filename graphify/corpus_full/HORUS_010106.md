# PROMOTING CIRCULAR ECONOMY IN AGRO-FOOD SYSTEMS THROUGH INSPECTS

The agrifood system faces challenges arising from its linear economic structure, which implies the need to make a transition to a circular approach. Insects represent an alternative to this transition in agrofood systems, due to their environmental and economic advantages as an animal production system, as well as their significant contribution to ecosystem services and to several of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) established by the United Nations. In addition, the use of insects as food has also demonstrated important social benefits in rural communities in the Global South. Although entomofagia has been an ancestral practice in many parts of the world, nowadays, the use of insects as human and animal food, as well as bioconverters, is experiencing rapid growth. It is essential to reflect on the use and production of insects to genuinely contribute to the sustainability of the global agrofood system. To do this, it is necessary to adopt an approach of use and production that encompasses economic, social and environmental dimensions, and adapts to different contexts. It is essential to reflect on the use and some of insects to contribute in these new areas of circular development at different levels.
