# Influence of microplastics in the community of benthic foreign from a historical perspective

The marine basins have received increasing quantities of plastic since the middle of the last century. The particles less than 5 mm are called microplastics (MPs), and recent studies have revealed that their presence has transformed the biogeochemistry and the biological structure of the seabed. To evaluate the changes in the time of the benthic foraminifers and their relationship with the presence of MPs, the composition of the community and the concentration of MPs using sediment nuclei of the Vásquez Sénaga, Cartagena, an open marine lagoon was evaluated. In addition, the increase of the total density was made with 210Pb in order to establish a temporary framework for this study, from 2021±0.31 to 1961±20.82. The MPs concentrations reached values of up to 5358 items/kg in the year 2021±0.31, and the overall increase of the total concentration was determined towards the sediments, which reflected overall patterns of contamination for this material. evaluate historical changes in benthic foraminifera and their relationship with the presence of MPs, the composition of the community and the concentration of MPs was evaluated using sediment from Ciénaga de los Vásquez, Cartagena, an open marine lake.
