# REINFORCEMENT LEARNING FOR BIPEDAL GAIT WITH MAX-E2 HUMANOID ROBOT

We consider the whole-body humanoid gait control model via reinforcement learning, where aninitial prede ̄ned parametric gait policy is optimized by employing the augmented random searchalgorithm realizing most of the experiments in simulation and verifying the results onto the realrobot. In our proposal, we take into account the reality gap problem, also known as Sim to realproblem, for the development of the experiments and the policy guaranteeing functional results inthe real robot employing a simpli ̄ed custom simulator. We show that our proposed learningalgorithm allows the humanoid robot MAX-E2 to walk e±ciently in virtual and real environments
