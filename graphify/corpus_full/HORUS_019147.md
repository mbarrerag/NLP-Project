# THE HONOR'S CARCEL: THE ZAWADZKY CASE

The chapter analyzes the importance of the culture of honor in the societies of the past. Based on the famous crime committed by politician and businessman Jorge Zawadsky in the early twentieth century, the network of legal arguments that defended crimes by honor is analyzed
