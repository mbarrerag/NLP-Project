# Energy conversion efficiency in thermoelectric junctions based on ceramic compounds p-Ca3Co4O9 and n-Nd2CuO4.

During the last decades an increase in the development of thermoelectric materials has been observed motivated by the need to find sources of energy non-pollutant, this taking into account that the thermoelectricity is a technology that allows direct conversion of heat into electricity and vice versa. One of the fundamental criteria for the practical application of thermal devices is its efficiency, which depends on the figure of merit, Z=PF/, this performance parameter is proportional to the power factor, PF=S^2/, and inversely proportional to the thermal conductivity,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,,, The maximum volume of the transport properties was calculated using the ionic substitutions in the base compounds, this was the optimal doping factor that increased its merit figure. The thermoelectric couplings were characterized by the determination of their efficiency as a thermal machine and the coefficient of performance as a solid state refrigerator. Using the solid state reaction method it was possible to prepare polycrystalline samples with a nominal composition of n-Nd2-xCexCuO4 (0,00<x<0.075). The Seebeck coefficient is negative in all temperature range studied, suggesting a conduction given by negative load carriers. The electrical resistivity was increased by the decrease of the temperature showing a semiconductive mechanism, its behavior is described by a driving mechanism given by small polarons, its magnitude decreased with the cerium content, at ambient temperature these values changed from 0.16 μcm to 4 m∫cm. The thermoelectric properties were evaluated by the sample of the PF2 thermoelectric power factor, which reached the near values of the cerium. The results allow to consider this type of ceramic, type p, as a useful material for the development of thermoelectric devices. The characterization of the thermoelectric module, as an electrical power generation device, was carried out in vacuum and according to the temperature temperature of both the thermal bath TC and the thermal source TH. Three values for ΔT between 0 and 6 degrees, namely 0.6K, 1.5K and 4.8K, as well as three values for the temperature of the thermal bath temperature TC, 293K, 293K, 270K and 251K, were selected in order to establish different thermal conditions under which the energy conversion power of the device could be studied in the configuration as a thermoelectric generator.
