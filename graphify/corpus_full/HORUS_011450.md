# DELETERIAL EFFECTS IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF CHILDREN IN THE CAUSE OF TEMPRARY SCHEDULE-SCHEME EXPOSURE: REVISION OF LITERATURA

Summary Since the 1990s, due to the increase in consumption of audiovisual material and particularly since the beginning of the confinement due to the pandemic of COVID-19, humanity has been more exposed to the use of screens, with children being a population vulnerable to environmental stimulation because they are going through critical periods important in their development. A search was carried out for literature in PubMed, Google Scholar and Lilacs. Applying the exclusion and inclusion criteria, 53 references were obtained from which the review was developed. It was found that excessive exposure to screens is harmful to children by producing language alterations, sociability, sleep-vigility cycle, limbic system, behavior and the dopaminergic mesolimbic system; which can affect their normal development, depending mainly on the age at the time of exposure, time and whether there is accompaniment or not. Key words: Children. Screening time. Childhood development. Neurodevelopment disorders. Language development. Sleep Disorders
