# Pedro Almodóvar persona artifice -The case of Talk to her (2002)-

This research seeks to relate the techniques of creating film characters with other components of film narrative, through the study of the characters of the filmography of the Spanish filmmaker Pedro Almodóvar. For this purpose, a detailed description of the film is made, and later the significance of the global themes of the film is established, as well as of the costumes. Finally, it traces the popular origin of Almodóvar’s work in traditional Spanish genres such as the spermpent, categorizing the characters according to their closeness as a comic or tragic essence. .
