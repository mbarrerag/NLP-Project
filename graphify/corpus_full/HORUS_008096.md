# Rack and Quandle Representations and Connections to the g-digroup Structure

In this research we study some algebraic properties of the rack and quandle structure as well as the representation theory of these objects. We establish a correspondence between the irreducible strong representations of a finite, connected rack with the irreducible representation of its finite enveloping group, which implies that the study of strong representations of a finite, connected rack can be approached through the representation theory of finite groups. Finally, we study the g-digroup structure and its connection to the rack structure
