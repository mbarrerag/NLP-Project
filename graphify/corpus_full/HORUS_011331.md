# 446. COLOMBIAN VEGETAL EXTRACTS WITH NEUROPROTECTOR ACTIVITY FRONT TO CERAMIDE, GLUTAMAT AND PARAQUAT

Alzheimer's disease (EA) and Parkinson's disease (EP) are neurodegenerative diseases characterized by neuronal death associated with the accumulation of cytotoxic polypeptides, oxidative stress, neuro-inflammation, among others. Previously we demonstrated that the activation of the hepatic X nuclear receptors (LXR), regulate the gene expression, promoting the elimination of protein aggregates, reducing inflammation, increasing neurogenesis, among others. Our research groups have carried out the search for plant natural products with neuroprotective activity in species of families of Basic Angiospermas characterized by their chemical diversity and biological potential of their constituents. Performing the search for plant extracts with agonist activity of LXR and evaluating their neuroprotective activity against the neuroprotective agents of the Zhxoids, as well as the serum and paraquat.
