# GOVERNANTS BEFORE THEIR JUDGES: ON THE OWN SIDE OF THE STATE

The chapter is devoted to the judicial management of the dark side of the State under the title "Governors" before its judges. It is a comparative and historical study that analyzes the great political processes and similar actions since the case of the Sicilian King CONRADINO Unfortunate in the thirteenth century, passing through CARLOS I of England, LUIS XVI and NAPOLEÓN of France, ITURBIDE of Mexico, SANTANDER of Colombia, ALFONSO XIII of Spain, the leaders of Nazism, the German RD Communists HONECKER and CEAUSESCU of Romania until the ex post management of the former Latin American dictators of the Cold War. While some of the convicted articulated a lot of criminal energy, others were more victims of new circumstances of power. Finally, the author debates whether criminal law is an appropriate method for the handling of recent pasts more or less traumatic
