# DIFFERENCE TO OBSERVE DIVERSITY IN THE WALK: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA.

In the current educational context, teachers need to adapt their pedagogical practices to respond to the growing diversity that characterizes the school. This article of bibliographic review contributes to transforming the discourse and teacher-training that have been developed in the management of such heterogeneity. The corpus includes more than 50 sources selected through a systematic search methodology in databases such as Redalyc, Taylor & Francis, Dialnet and Scopus, based on thematic rele-vanence, methodological quality and the current state of studies. The article focuses on differentiation as a strategy to address the diverse needs, interests and learning styles of students. The aim is to offer a critical analysis of what has been researched in the last decade about differentiation in education, explaining its definition, interpretation and application in different contexts. The findings reveal different interpretations, methodological approaches, practical strategies and associated challenges. In conclusion, differentiation is presented as a theoretical-practical posture that offers a valuable framework for responding to the diversity present in the classroom and promoting learning processes
