# ANALYSIS OF TRANSCRIPTOMA BY RNA-SEQ: ACTUAL AND PERSPECTIVE APPLICATIONS

Advances in next generation sequencing techniques and bioinformatics have allowed the study of the complete transcriptoma of a set of cells or even a single cell. This masterful presentation will present general concepts about the study of transcriptomas by RNA-seq sequencing, analysis flows, examples of current applications and perspectives
