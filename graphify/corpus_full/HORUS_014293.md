# SEIR model with compartments for the propagation of Covid-19 in the Nariñense Pacific.

SARS-COV2 is a virus that has spread throughout the world and has generated a global health crisis. The aim of this work is to propose a modified SEIR model to describe the dynamics of the spread of COVID-19 in the Nariñense Pacific from the data provided by the National Institute of Health (INS). Computerly experiments are carried out for the modified SEIR model with what is expected to estimate some parameters of the model and propose the modelling of possible scenarios of the spread of the virus
