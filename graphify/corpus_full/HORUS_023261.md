# PRICE IN THE CONNECTS OF THE MAIN THREE CENTRES OF HYDRO-ELECTRIC GENERATION OF ECUADOR: COCA CODO SINCLAIR, PAUTE-MOLINOS AND MINAS-SAN FRANCISCO

The electrical systems of Colombia and Ecuador are interconnected in order to take advantage of the complementarity between the sources of electricity generation to reduce costs and risks.The three main hydroelectric generation plants of Ecuador are Coca Codo Sinclair (1500 MW), Paute-Molinos (1100 MW) and Minas San Francisco (1500 MW).This work characterizes the temporal variability of precipitation in the watersheds of these plants.Using daily precipitation data from the CHIRPS database for the period 1981 to 2020, the daily, monthly and annual rainfall series added spatially in each basin were calculated.The regime was characterized using the mass curve of the annual cycle in daily resolution.The results show that the Codo Sinclair basin has an annual average rainfall of 1948 mm and a non-seasonal regime, while the basins of Paute-Molinos and Minas-San Francisco are a long seasonality.The dry season is the same
