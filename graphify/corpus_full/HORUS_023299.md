# Pharmacoterapeutic follow-up by illness: an integrating review

: The increase in non-communicable chronic diseases has changed the epidemiological profile worldwide and nationally. Thus, a greater complexity of pharmacological treatments has been presented and, consequently, of polypharmacy. This situation is potentially insecure in so far as it generates problems related to drugs (PRM), such as drug interactions, therapeutic failure, among others, derived from multiple factors such as failures in pharmacological knowledge, therapeutic non-compliance, failures in the integration of the health care team. That is why nursing from its guiding, educational and integrative role, has a great field of action and relevance in this scenario, by carrying out an Integral Pharmacotherapeutic Follow-up by Nursing (SFTE) that allows to influence from several fronts to mitigate the occurrence of MRI
