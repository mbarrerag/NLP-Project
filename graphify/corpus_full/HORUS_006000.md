# New species and taxonomical notes on Bolivian Lamiinae (Coleoptera, Cerambycidae)

One new genus and three new species of Lamiinae are described from Bolivia: Leptocometes wappesi sp. nov. (Acanthocinini); Pentheochaetes pulcherrima sp. nov. (Acanthocinini); and Guarayo phantasmaticus, gen. nov., sp. nov. (Parmenini). The female of Periestola raphaeli is illustrated for the first time, and the differences compared with the male are reported. Estola basiflava is synonymized with Estola densepunctata. The alternative of couplet ‘36’ from the key to South American genera of Acanthocinini with erect setae on elytral surface is modified.
