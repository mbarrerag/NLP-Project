# Being an artist, woman and teacher: strategies for emancipation through artistic-pedagogical experiences in Medellín

The content of this thesis is accompanied and supported by the work of some female artists who, with their way of living emancipation and feminism, provided tools from both conceptual and artistic to the teacher, who found in their own strategies of emancipation the way of inhabiting the school settings from a political and ethical position in front of the construction of their being an artist-teacher and the collective work that integrated into each process with their students. .
