# REFLECTIONS ON THE EFFECTS OF THE COMPETITION DISTRIBUTION IN LAW 2080 OF 2021, TOWARDS A EQUILIBRIUM BETWEEN THE UNIFIED ROLE OF THE STATE COUNCIL AND THE PROTAGONISM OF ADMINISTRATIVE JUDGE

Law 1437 of 2011, did not represent a major change in the system of distribution of powers provided for in Decree No. 01 of 1984. However, judges were granted powers in the conduct of the proceedings to ensure effective access to the administration of justice, more speed and immediacy were introduced in the trials and special powers were established for the substantive resolution of procedural claims. The Full Division of Administrative Litigation and its sections were also entrusted with hearing disputes outside extraordinary and ordinary procedural channels which, because of their legal, social or economic importance, justify unifying jurisprudence and merit to be known for the high stay of the Court
