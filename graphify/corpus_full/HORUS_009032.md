# Nutritional differences between some agroecological and conventional products in Valle del Cauca.

The world is currently facing the challenge of developing food production systems that protect the environment and its resources, which establish a balanced relationship with those who work directly in their production. The present study aims to evaluate agroecological production systems in the Valle del Cauca, Colombia, in terms of the nutritional mass of its main products: egg, chicken and banana. The contents of moisture, protein, fat, ash and minerals, such as phosphorus and potassium, in eggs and chicken breast of agroecological, conventional and commercial systems, as well as the moisture, ashes, phosphorus, potassium, calcium and magnesium in bananas of these three origins were analyzed and compared.
