# REINCORPORATION IN KEY OF STRENGTHENING TERRITORIAL CAPACITIES FOR PEACE BUILDING: A LOOK FROM ACADEMIA

The article presents the analytical synthesis of a process of dialogue between the academic community of the National University of Colombia; the signatories and guarantors of the Peace Agreement in Havana, Cuba; and representatives of the Farc-EP and the Colombian State around talks, followed by an analysis of the process of reintegration to identify its scope and limitations, aimed at visualizing proposals for its strengthening in nine axes: understanding of reintegration; territorial approach in the implementation of reintegration; access to land; new geography of reintegration; security in the territories and protection of the lives of persons in the process of reintegration; productive projects, associativeness and link with community processes; strengthening of the capacities of the persons signing the Agreement; political incorporation; and new family, community and collective dynamics
