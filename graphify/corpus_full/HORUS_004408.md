# Evaluation of cedence and cutting resistance in soils from volcanic ash with different alfana contents

The volcanic ash deposits exposed to environmental conditions undergo a process of chemical meteorisation leading to the formation of minerals such as alophan, imogolite and haloisite; the presence of these minerals conditions the factory, structure and mechanical behaviour of soils derived from volcanic ash. In order to evaluate the physical characteristics, composition, behaviour of the cutting resistance and cedence of this type of materials in unchanged condition, soil samples were taken in the Municipality of Montenegro, Quindío. Chemical characterisation tests were carried out, their index properties were evaluated and the influence of the alophan in the geometic behavior of this type of soils were performed, in order to obtain the parameters of resistance and deformability of the soils derived from volcanic ash studied.
