# REGIONALIZATION OF Caudal-Duration Curves — FRENCH BASED ON THE INDEEX-FLOOD METHOD.

The present study applied the method of flow-duration-frequency curves developed by Javelle et al. (2001) in the region of Antioquia-Colombia, which consists of estimating the maximum flow rates associated with different return periods for different durations. It was possible to obtain equations that allow the estimation of the flow rate in function of the return period and the duration for each of the hydrologic regions of the study region. The results show good performance and are a potential tool for estimating the maximum flow rates for basins with little information
