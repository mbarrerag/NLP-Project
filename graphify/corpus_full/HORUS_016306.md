# EFFECT OF THE TERMS REFERRED TO IN THE TERMS ON THE PRODUCTION OF ULTITOL BY CARIBBIC MEYEROZYMA IN THE PRESENCE OF ACETICAL ACID

The production of xylitol by fermentation has become an attractive alternative due to the disadvantages in the chemical method, associated with costs and environmental impact, such as its high energy consumption, and the use of catalyst [1]. The biotechnological method may be a more economical and environmentally friendly pathway by valuing lignocellulose residues. However, the presence of inhibitors such as acetic acid, generated during pretreatment and hydrolysis processes [2] represents a stress factor to be overcome by yeasts.The activation of response to environmental stress in unicellular organisms may provide cross protection in future stress conditions of the same factor or other type [3].The main objective of this study was the evaluation of a culture strategy under suboptimal conditions of temperature over the production of xylitol by non-conventional yeasts in the fermentation of D-xylose in the presence of acetic acid.The identification of yeasts was made from sequencing and multilocus analysis of the associated domains D1/D2 of the rDNA region (LSU) and the 6th region of the LTS for the production of the Acetic acid. ass to the lithic action of the zimoliase
