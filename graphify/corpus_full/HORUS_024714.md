# Design of a didactic sequence for teaching the planetary model from gravitational forces to sixth grade students.

This paper shows the development of a didactic sequence for the explanation of the planetary model from the gravitational forces oriented to students of the sixth grade of the Liceo Francisco José de Caldas of the municipality of Nile, Cundinamarca. Likewise, the design and implementation of the activities developed from an epistemological approach, based on the construction of knowledge and logical reasoning with the purpose that the student obtains knowledge from his previous knowledge
