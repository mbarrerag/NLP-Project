# ESTIMATION OF AEDES AEGYPTI MOSQUITO PUPAE FROM METEOROLOGICAL VARIABLES USING THE ZERO-INFLATED NEGATIVE BINOMIAL MODEL IN THE DEPARTMENT OF CAUCA, COLOMBIA.

The Aedes aegypti mosquito is a vector of dengue, Zika, chikungunya and urban yellow fever viruses. The geographic range of this mosquito is associated with different factors such as temperature, which varies according to altitude. In Colombia, in approximately 35 years, this arbovirus vector has moved 102 meters in altitude. Under climate change scenarios it is expected that by the end of the century the temperature in the Colombian Pacific will increase by approximately 2.15°C and therefore the mosquito and the viruses it transmits will be found at higher latitudes and altitudes
