# Sustainable development and innovation of instruments and devices for experimental and research strengthening of laboratories with an emphasis on basic and applied sciences, implementing an eco-design model in interconnected laboratories through the cloud-IoT-5G

The project proposes a strategy of experimental strengthening of R+D+I through the cooperation of laboratories of the Bogotá headquarters in union with laboratories of the headquarters of Palmira. Likewise, it will be sought to continue extending this academic and experimental convergence to other headquarters of the university, through the implementation of an intersedial scenario of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. This scenario will allow to make new innovative developments through practices of online remote laboratories, accessing resources of the Laboratory Sala CAM - Laboratorio Fábrica Experimental of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia (Lab FabEx - UN). The proposed initiative seeks to capitalize the three decades of continuous transit of applied research instruments to consolidate technological experimental infrastructure, such as experimental machines and robust equipment of industrial typology (Machination Center and robots), all of which are tele-operable through the Robotic Integration and Automation Platform of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia (PRIA-UN). The platform is a tool of integration of technology enabled by flows of information in the cloud, facilitating the deployment of routines with a collaborative robotic approach, starting from the rapid prototyping and manufacturing by additive and subs, and substractives, cycles cycles of the construction. microbial consortium developed by the Agricultural Microbiology Group of the Institute of Biotechnology, generate interactions applicable to the context of the management of rice tam in the current climate crisis and fertilizers in Latin America. Likewise, other innovative developments in this project are the design and manufacture of Hykabai mini robot with reduced transit footprint, automated assembly of minidrone-UN with assistance of collaborative robotics in COBOTS environment, development of micro experimental factory for the manufacture of printed circuits, experimental application of automated engraving with ND:YAG laser, eco-friendly design for the measurement of GHG and mini instrumentation with a sustainability approach by recycling and reuse of electronic waste for laboratory practices with minimal energy consumption in the field of green chemistry and biotechnology, also developing a low-cost carbon footprint measurement platform in field and laboratory
