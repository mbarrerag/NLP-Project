# EFFECT OF NUTRITIONAL STRESS ON THE INVASIBILITY OF 3D HUMAN TROPHOBLAST CELULATIVE CULTIVES: FUNCTIONAL AND TRANSCRIPTIONAL CHARACTERIZATION.

The cells of one extravellous trofoblast are responsible for the formation of the placenta at the beginning of pregnancy, due to their ability to migrate and invade maternal deciduousness. The invasion of the trophoblastic cells is closely related to the nutritional factors present in the extracellular environment. In in vitro trofoblasto crops, it is known that the deprivation of bovine foetal serum (SFB) modifies the expression of proteins and affects cell proliferation, being an interesting model of study of tumour progression given the similarities with the behavior of cancer cells. Even more when it is made three-dimensional growth, where the disposition of the cells in a spheroid generates greater interaction between them, and a heterogeneous access to nutrients and oxygen. In this order of ideas, this work evaluated the ability of the trofoblasto in terms of nutritional stress in spheroid type cultures.
