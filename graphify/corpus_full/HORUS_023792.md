# KAWASAKI’S DISEASE

Kawasaki disease (EK) is an acute febrile disease and a generalized vasculitis that affects the medium- and small-sized vessels, especially the coronary arteries of children under 5 years of age., capable of producing aneurysms, short-term and long-term cardiovascular disease. Its diagnosis is based on clinical criteria with the help of some paraclinicals. Its treatment is mainly with immunoglobulin IV and ASA. Its prognosis depends on the involvement of the coronary arteries in particular with the development of the aneurysms
