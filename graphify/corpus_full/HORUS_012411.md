# Findings of optical coherence tomography of the macula and its clinical correlation with leukemias and lymphomas in the adult population.

leukaemia and lymphomas can cause significant changes in macula. Evidence is needed to understand the repercussions in ophthalmology and haematology. Optical Consistency Tomography (OCT) is a non-invasive image that details alterations in each layer of the retina, however, the literature lacks any studies guided by OCT in this population. To describe the macular alterations identified in OCT of patients with leukaemia and lymphomas, in adult population who attended the Ophthalmocenter Clinic and Eye Clinic in the period 2017-2022. a descriptive, retrospective study with patients from the clinical history database, in the institutions involved, with history of leukemia or lymphoma, who already had macula OCTs performed. a total of 139 eyes of 120 patients, with mean age of 42.2 years (DE=18.4).
