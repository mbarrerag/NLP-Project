# Numerical modelling of massive sand production during cold heavy oil production.

The cold production of heavy crude sand (CHOPS) is a single well technology that involves the deliberate start and maintenance of sand flow to wells using progressive cavity pumps (PCP) to produce oil at high rates with a subsequent high pressure gradient around the well. and improves the productivity of oil wells. CHOPS is a method of primary recovery widely used in the world as a cost effective and simple technology. The flow of foaming sand and the formation of worm holes are the main mechanisms of CHOPS, where the aggressive production of sand is the result of geomechanical problems such as the smoothness of the effort, the fault criteria, the pressure gradient, the erosion and the smoothing of the sand. The general objective of this thesis is to construct a numerical model to predict and explain the mass production of sand during the production of heavy crude oil. It integrates the fluid flow with the geomechanical and the erosion processes. pressure gradient, cohesion, the angle of internal friction and the regime of stress. Finally, a special case is constructed combining all variables and seeking to promote sand production with successful results
