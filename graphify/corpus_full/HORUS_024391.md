# MINERAL CHEMICAL IN THE COLOMBIAN MIDDLE EAST: GENESIS AND DEVELOPMENTS

Pegmatites and granitic intrusives from the Colombian East and belonging to the Mitú Complex were studied in order to understand the type of magma generator and to find the relationship between the presence of minerals carrying Nb -Ta reported by Bayona-Valderrama (2018) and their degree of magmatic fractionation. Quantitative analyses were carried out in apatite, biotite, garnet, microcline, muscovita and turmaline with EMMA electronic microsound. It was found with the results of biotite, garnet and turmaline that the rocks come from a peraluminumous magma rich in iron, which is proposed to be related to the Parguaza Event. In addition, the concentration of Ba in microcline, Na in muscovita, Fe and Mg in biotite and, Mn and Sr in apatito allowed to raise an evolutionary line where the samples carrying minerals from Nb-Ta were located and it was found that turmaline and the granate are minerals that can act as indicators of economic exploration for Nb-Ta
