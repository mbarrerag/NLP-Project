# Research methodology to establish the determinants of E-commerce adoption in Manizales SMEs. Application in the commercial sector

The aim of e-commerce in Colombia has been to increase significantly over the last few years. This research aims to identify the determinant factors that influence the implementation of e-commerce in the business environment, especially in small and medium-sized business enterprises in the city of Manizales. It starts with a contextualization of the problem, highlighting the strategic importance of e-commerce in the current business landscape. The theoretical and reference framework provides a solid basis, exploring the history of the Internet, the fundamentals of e-commerce, the theories of technological adoption and the determinant factors. Through the analysis of information from academic articles, research highlights the importance of e-commerce as a driver of the growth and development of the organizations. The methodological design details the type of research, the procedure and the instruments used to collect data. The analysis and discussion of results present key findings, such as the importance of the training of the staff, the business perception on the E-commerce, the relative advantage, economic and technological factors. These results provide a valuable perspective for local companies, highlighting benefits such as the reduction of the operational efficiency and expansion of the client base.
