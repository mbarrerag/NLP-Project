# 200 YEARS OF PEOPLE IN COLOMBIA

This publication is made up of 155 maps prepared from a database that includes more than 20,000 records, which are based on the compilation of censuses of the nineteenth, twentieth and twenty-first centuries. The reading and systematization of this information was the basis for the construction of cartography. Time and space are the two variables used to account for this purpose, which is committed to an interpretation of historical events from three forces: resources, institutions and technology. Thus, through an exercise of reading and statistical and cartographic inference, the reader will be able to find an explanation of how the configuration of twelve territorial frameworks of a subnational character has been traversed by the regions of the Amazonia, Orinoquia and the Coffee Axis, and the departments of Boyacá, Chocó, Cundinamarca, Huila, Nariño, Norte de Santander, Santander, Tolima and Valle del Cauca. Has several territorial management systems implemented by societies in their interest to manage resources?
