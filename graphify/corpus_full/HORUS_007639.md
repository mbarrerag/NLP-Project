# Inflation in Latin America: Analysis through a Panel-VAR

This project will analyze the effectiveness of the interest rate to control inflation in Latin American economies using a panel-VAR model in continuous time. The econometric model considered is suitable to study the problem under consideration since it allows modeling the endogeneity of macroeconomic variables, observing their interdependence between countries and giving a more realistic treatment to time series when treating them in continuous time
