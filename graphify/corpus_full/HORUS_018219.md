# TECHNOLOGY FOR THE CULTIVE OF MARACUA IN COLOMBIA (PASSIFLORA EDULIS F. FLAVICARPA)

This manual is aimed at producers, students, technicians and researchers and was designed with the purpose of providing technical support in relation to the establishment, agronomic management and marketing of the maracuyá. The monitoring and control of problems toosanitarios are focused on the integrated management of Plagas (MIP) with the use of bioinsumos and following the standards of Good Agricultural Practices (BPA). This document describes the generalities of the maracuyá and the field experiences collected over the last 12 years, through visits to the main areas producing maracuyá in Colombia, with emphasis on the department of Valle del Cauca. The visits were carried out through the project, which was developed by the National University of Colombia, Palmira and Agrosavia headquarters, with the support of the producers, marketers, exporters and processors of maracuyá. Likewise, the information available and exposed in this manual contributes to innovative agronomic practices for optimal productivity and which are the basis of an agriculture of precision to preserve resources, guarantee the sustainability and protection of the environment
