# USE AND PERCEPTION OF HEALTH PERSONNEL ON MOBILE APPLICATION FOR INTEGRATED GEORIC ASSESSMENT

There is little evidence to support the usefulness of mobile applications in the care of older adults. GeriatriApp is an application created for Android devices that seeks to facilitate the health professional to carry out comprehensive geriatric assessment (GIV) and integrates functional, nutritional, cognitive, emotional, pharmacological, fragility and sarcopenia evaluation. The objective of this study is to evaluate the perceived usefulness among users of the App at the time of making the IPV, as well as the effect on decision making and to evaluate possible differences between health personnel
