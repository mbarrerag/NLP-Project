# IMMUNOMODULATION OF THE PULP MICROENVIRONMENT

This presentation focuses on the potential application of immunomodulation for enhancing the prognosis in treating invasive cervical root resorption (ICRR) through vital pulp therapy. It investigates the utilization of antioxidants in reducing osteoclastic activity on dentin using an in vitro model
