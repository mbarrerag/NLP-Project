# Rational design and synthesis of heterocyclic derivatives, slurry and nitrogen with potential pharmacological activity on ion channels regulating nerve signals.

The ion channels have recently aroused the interest of study from pharmacology given their potential application as therapeutic targets in the treatment of various pathologies. In this measure a library of compounds of heterocyclic pyrazolic, thiazolidineonic, thiazepine and pyrimidine nucleus that could interact with molecular targets of this protein family was proposed; with special interest for this work the GABAA receptor and the voltage-dependent sodium channel NaV1.7. This library was subjected to a screening process using molecular docking using 3 programs (AutoDock4, AutoDock-VINA and DOCK6), in-silicon determination of the molecular descriptors of Lipinski bioavailability (ADME) and prediction of the toxicological properties, selecting the most promising prototypes and bringing them to the synthesis phase where the optimal conditions were also studied, as well as structural subtleties and autoactivity oriented to the improvement of the chemical processes that allowed obtaining with good yields, 4 series of compounds with a high promisological profile in the synthesis of the ionic channels diana
