# QUANTIFICATION BY ANALYSIS OF IMAGES OF SPANGOSPORES SUBTERRANEAN IN SUELE

The protista Spongospora subterranea is the pathogen of the soil that causes the powdery scab of the potato. It is a compulsory parasite that reproduces in roots and tubers, where it produces groups of zoospores enqueradas, cystosores, which pass to the soil to generate a new infective cycle. The prevention of this disease requires identifying and quantifying the cystosores of the soil. In order to automate the process, we digitally photograph cystosores of different crops under the same conditions, and characterize their size and shape as an area and circularity with analysis of images. The range of the area was 1086-2247 μm2 and the circularity was 0.824-0.886.
