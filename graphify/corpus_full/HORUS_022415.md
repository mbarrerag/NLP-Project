# Assessment of the physical vulnerability of structures impacted by landslides.

The physical vulnerability of structures is defined as the degree of damage or loss of an element. It is an adimensional value with a range of 0 to 1. In addition, it is a key element for the assessment of the risk to slopes of the slope, being this essential for the prediction of the consequences of a landslide. The present work, shows the design, construction and calibration of a model to laboratory scale that possesses the ability to reconstruct geometric parameters of the slope and the resistance of the structure for the estimation of the vulnerability of individual structures exposed to landslides. The model takes into account parameters of intensity of the landslides by means of the modified gradient Z proposed by Guimaraes-Silva (2015) and the resistance of the structures calculated by means of the methodology of Li et al. (2010) which was parametrized by means of the model T. The dimensioning of the model was made from the database of the slidings in Nova Friburg, Brazil, in January 2011 and the slidings of south Korea between July and August 2011.
