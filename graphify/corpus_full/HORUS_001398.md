# Consensuses and Dissents to Reform the Health System in Colombia

This paper analyses the consensus and dissent following attempts to reform the Colombian health system in the period 2010-2024. It is argued that the transformations in public policies are shaped by complex political processes in which different actors interact according to their interests, resources, roles, values and strategies to influence decisions. To this end, a qualitative case study was carried out that included analysis of multiple sources, such as parliamentary archives, draft laws, constitutional rulings, national press, public statements of actors and institutional resources online. It is concluded that the inquiry into the dynamics of agreements and divergences in health policies allows us to understand how changes are concrete or obstructed, either by validating certain interests or excluding others
