# THE DECLARATION OF THE RIGHTS OF THE CAMPESINATE AND THE RESOLUTION OF CONFLICT IN NATURAL AREAS PROTECTED IN COLOMBIA.

The environmental and conservation policies of natural ecosystems in Colombia, have sought to exclude from these areas a human population and its economic activities. In protected areas in Colombia (AP) they inhabit peasant communities, Afro-descendants and indigenous people who struggle to remain in their territories where they have built their life and culture.
