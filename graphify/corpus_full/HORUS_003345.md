# Generalized Kdv. analytical solutions

In this paper we explore new families of Kdv equation. We present single and multi-soliton solutions for these equations. Additionally, we utilize the Hirota bilinear form to derive further solutions for the generalized Sawada-Kotera equation. We also investigate a forced and damped version of the equations
