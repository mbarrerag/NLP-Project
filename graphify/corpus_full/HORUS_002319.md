# Implementation of Machine Learning and Deep Learning models for linear classification in animals of the Brahman breed

The project seeks to address the inaccuracies in the phenotypic assessments of Brahman cattle, which are currently based on subjective visual assessments, affecting the selection of breeding and genetic improvement. An automated system based on artificial vision and 27 specialized models of Machine learning and Deep learning, validated in contrasting tropical biomes (Cotové: dry forest; Montería: wet forest), will be developed in order to make more accurate and objective measurements based on images, improving the reliability of the linear classification of animals. This will allow identifying structural characteristics relevant for the selection of animals with better functionality and productive performance. The methodology includes a bibliographic review, manual image collection and labelling, preprocessing of images, model development and training, validation and evaluation using standard metrics, adjustment and optimization of models, and comparative analysis with manual methods. The project is expected to provide technological tools that improve the accuracy of the linear evaluations of Brahman animals, facilitating decision-making and contributing to the genetic improvement of the species, with a positive impact on the productive performance of the farms involved
