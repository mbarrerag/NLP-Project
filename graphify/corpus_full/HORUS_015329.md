# RULE MAP FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF ELECTROMOVILITY IN THE COLOMBIAN FERROFLUVIAL SYSTEM

Colombia aims to achieve migration towards more sustainable and competitive models for cargo and passenger transport, and for this reason the National Government has made efforts to prioritize and develop in the national infrastructure network iron and river projects that reduce polluting emissions and logistics and transport costs. As outlined in the National Development Plan 2022-2026 in its proposal for transformation No. 4 Is it a productive transformation for life and climate action? with the carbon-neutrality goal, through a fair energy transition and technological growth of transport. These initiatives seek to enhance Colombia's comparative advantages vis-à-vis the other countries of the region: a privileged geostrategic position, a wide network of navigable rivers and various areas suitable for the establishment of railway networks and intermodal corridors, which are key to improving connectivity between production centres, ports and consumption centres. In order to achieve the goal of migrating towards sustainable transport systems, which generate less negative externalities to the environment and reduce dependence on fossil fuels, the national government has diagnostic and planning instruments for medium-term transport and actions to be implemented
