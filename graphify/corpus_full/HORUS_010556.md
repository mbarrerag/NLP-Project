# Prototyping a Kundt tube under DIY strategy for the development of physical thought in middle education

This research presents a teaching proposal based on the design of the Kundt tube with sensors for data acquisition and materials of easy access as an ICT tool for the construction of knowledge in physics based on the socio-cultural theory of Vygotsky. It aims to show the importance of experimentation as an instrument that facilitates the learning of students, the potentialities of the Maker culture from the DIY philosophy as a teaching strategy through collaborative work and the advancement of students in the compression of concepts related to stationary waves during the implementation of the proposal. This work was developed in the educational institution San Ignacio de Loyola of the city of Medellin, with students from the sixth to the eleventh degree. The collection of information is carried out through different techniques and instruments such as questionnaires and observation of the participants. In relation to the results it was obtained that: the role of each teacher as a guide and mediator is fundamental, already for the employees, who were able to develop skills such as decision-making, formulation of questions on physics and appropriation of acquired knowledge. de Loyola educational institution in the city of Medellín, with students from six to higher grade.
