# Analysis and regulatory policy for the generators and network operators in connection with the incorporation of systems of energy storage in the national interconnected system

This research work establishes how energy storage systems are incorporated in electricity systems, developed through qualitative and quantitative analysis. The qualitative analysis defines the regulatory trajectory of case studies, European Union, Spain, United Kingdom, United States, Chile, China, Japan, Australia, and New Zealand; Another element of the qualitative analysis is the incentives for storage agents and, finally, the regulatory analysis of the requirements for the final disposal of batteries in four case studies, the European Union, Japan, the United States, and Colombia. The quantitative analysis is constituted by the free cash flow for a project with an energy storage system with 60 MW batteries as a transmission asset, in contrast, with a storage system of the same capacity associated with a 100 MW photovoltaic electric generation plant, in this project the free cash flows that the agent would have without incentives. With the incentives of Law, 1715/2014 are estimate. The results conclude that storage agents obtain incentives for supporting generation with non-conventional energy sources. Likewise, Colombian regulations encourage storage agents to participate in the market as generators
