# Ecology: Climate, soils, biogeography, climatic variability and climate change

This chapter addresses the ecological, edaphic and climatic requirements for optimal avocado production, particularly focused on cv. 'Hass'. Advanced data analytics tools explore insights into the biomes supporting avocado cultivation in traditional production regions in Mexico, Israel, Chile, USA, Spain, South Africa, Australia and New Zealand, along with detailed edaphoclimatic profles. Attention is also drawn to emerging avocado-growing countries such as Peru, Colombia, Ethiopia and Kenya. Climate variability and the potential impacts of climate change are discussed as critical factors infuencing avocado production systems worldwide. The contentious issue of water usage in avocado cultivation is also addressed, including conficts over this vital resource in certain production areas. Pressing challenges facing avocado cultivation are discussed, particularly sustainability and adaptation strategies required to face climate-variability and -change scenarios. This chapter synthesizes knowledge of avocado ecology and edaphoclimatic conditions and records efforts towards sustainable avocado cultivation under evolving environmental and commercial dynamics
