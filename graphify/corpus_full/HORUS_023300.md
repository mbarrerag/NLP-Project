# LANDS AND MAMPINGS IN THE WRITER: TEARS, MASS AND INTERTELLIONS

The purpose of this writing is to position the cultural, aesthetic, constructive meaning and toponymic effects that produce the terracotta colors by the predominant use of brick in the construction of the city of Medellin, reinforced by the benefits of this material ara its manipulation, installation, texture, economy and ease of achievement become the great aesthetic and colorimetric protagonist of this physical result with artistic characteristic features of Medellin.The naked brick, has been the great modeler of the aesthetic, decorative and colorimetric message that has given the iconic imprint to Medellin in key with its location in an intra-mountain valley delimited by two slopes that make more visible the predominance of brick.The eastern edge of the city as a geographical reference is linked to the research of the doctorate in arts in process focused on the aesthetic component that has as reference the intensive occupation of this sector product, ingenuity and construction has become a physical fact consolidated in the time that has had as an ally for its prevalence the brick as predominant material
