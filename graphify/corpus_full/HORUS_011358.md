# THE COLLECTION OF VOLCANIC ROCKS OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA-SEDE MEDELLIN UNIVERSITY

The rocks used in teaching acquire a lot of importance when organized and have associated information. The results of the acquisition, organization and classification of a set of hand samples and thin sections, used as support in the classroom, but with potential use in research, which houses the Department of Geosciences and Environment of the National University of Colombia-Sede Medellín. The samples, acquired by direct collection, purchase or donation, are cleaned, sequentially coded and classified to be then arranged in marked containers and with inner molds that protect them from mechanical interactions. For each sample the collection of associated information was made (procedency, location, lithological unit) and a 3D digital model was developed using high resolution photographs and photogrammetric techniques. The digital information is recorded in a database file of data that allows to make consultations and generate reports. The collection has 250 hand samples and 106 thin sections, distributed as well: 211 volcanic rocks, 35 alien materials, 4 plutonic rocks. The volcanic samples include 151 lavas, 52 volcanic rocks and 8 hydrothermal gaps, with basals and 106 sections
