# Study of microsclerotics formed by the entomopatogen fungus Metarhizium robertsii Mt004: Production, characterization and insecticide activity.

The conidios are the most commonly used fungal structures as an active ingredient for the development of bioplagicides. However, different problems associated with its mass production in solid fermentation, its large-scale handling, and the susceptibility to environmental conditions, resulting in low persistence in the field, have motivated research into the alternative use of other fungal propugules. Microsclerotia (ME) are propáulos with great potential for the development of more persistent and easy to produce bioplagicides, due to their characteristics of resistance, germination capacity and production of infective conienda of insects. Taking into account the above, the main objective of this work was to induce the production of microsclerotia with the Metarrhizium robertsii Mt004 strain and to study the process of formation, structure, composition and potential of the Mpacter. The ME showed the presence of intricated hyphas at the beginning of their formation, until forming a compact structure based on an amorphous extracellular matrix. In the compositional analysis of the structures the presence of trehalose was not detected, but if the presence of small concentrations of mannitol was confirmed; and the spectra of magnetic nuclear resonance suggested the presence of other carbohydrates that could serve as energy reserves to guarantee the survival of the ME. In comparison with the conidios, the MEs presented greater resistance to environmental stress conditions such as temperature and UV radiation. Similarly, the MEs were more stable than the conidias under storage conditions at different temperatures (8, 18 and 28 °C), remaining viable after being stored for 6 months at 8 °C, while the conidios did not germinate after 2 months of free-flow to any of the evaluated temperatures.The insectivative activity of the conidios produced by the MEs was evaluated using the diatraea saccharalis larvae as a model, with which the median efficacy was obtained 78% which suggested that the conidis produced by these structures were prouds. a simple, scalable, replicable and easy to control process, makes this technology ideal for the future development of a novel, efficient and highly technical and economical bio-pesticidal
