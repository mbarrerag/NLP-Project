# STANDARD CLINICAL BASED ON EVIDENCY: DIAGNOSTIC AND PATIENT TREATMENT WITH SIDE CUTHANIC REACTIONS OVER MEDICINES IN THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL

The RCAMs are a group of clinical conditions characterized by the alteration in the structure or function of the skin, mucous membranes or the skin annexes, induced by the exposure to a given drug. The RCAMs are the most common manifestation of adverse reactions to medicinal products (RAMs) that, according to the World Health Organization (WHO), as a harmful and unintentional response to a drug that occurs at doses normally used in humans for prophylaxis, diagnosis or treatment of a disease or for modification of physiological function. It is estimated that between 10% and 15% of the patients medicated develop a RAM. In the hospital area, it has been reported that the incidence of RCAM in patients hospitalized in developed countries varies between 1-3%, while the incidence in these patients in developing countries varies between 2-5%. In terms of the outpatient area, Chatterjee et al., in a study carried out in 27726 patients who attended in a period of one year the outpatient dermatology service of a university hospital in Calculta, India, reported the incidence of RCAM of 2.6 %.
