# SUSTAINABLE TECHNOLOGIES: ELECTRO-OXIDATION AS ALTERNATIVE FOR THE TEXTILE INDUSTRY

This study assessed the extent of electro-oxidation for waste water treatment in the textile industry. It focused on the elimination of persistent organic pollutants that contribute significantly to water pollution and can have toxic effects in humans and the environment. Electro-oxidation is presented as an efficient alternative to physical and biological treatment methods, offering advantages such as minimal generation of sludges and moderate operating costs. The study used stainless steel cathode and boron-doped diamond anode, to generate hydroxyl radicals that break down organic pollutants without generating unwanted by-products. The effectiveness of the process was measured in terms of color elimination, and decreased chemical demand for oxygen (COD) and total organic carbon (COT). The results indicated a significant reduction of pollutants and compliance with the regulatory standards for waste water. The research highlighted the importance of electro-oxidation in advancing towards sustainable development objectives and its potential to improve water quality in the textile industry. With its ability to treat effluents with high organic pollutants, electro-oxidation is a promising solution for industrial applications
