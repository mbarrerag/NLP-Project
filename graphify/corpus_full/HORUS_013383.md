# Evaluation and development of strategies for improving the quality of honey bee from production units in Casanare department

In the Department of Casanare honey production of bees has been recognized as a potentially sustainable productive alternative for the region. Recently, projects funded by the General System of Regalías have been established to promote the development of this productive sector. This is the case of the project “Strengthening the production chain of bees and beekeeping through social appropriation of knowledge in the department of Casanare, led by the National University of Colombia, which focuses specifically on the municipalities of the Piedmont subregion: Sácama, La Salina, Chámeza and Yopal. For the development of this project it is necessary to establish a baseline of the current state of the apiculture practices and the quality of the honeys obtained. In this final work the results of a previous study, carried out in the framework of the project, on the organization and association of the apicultures of the region and the micro-organisation of the practices were analyzed.
