# Construction of test benches for experimental measurement of the parameters of the dynamic model Drones - UAS (Unmanned Aerial System).

One option for automatic control of mechanical devices is to obtain the parameters that make up the equation that describes their movement. This is why, under the approach of Newton’s dynamic model – Euler applied to drones of maximum 25 kg, it is necessary to determine the inertial parameters for the complete system such as mass, dimensions, centre of gravity and moments of inertia, as well as friction parameters for brushless engines such as thrust force, torque and angular velocity. In this work, the design and construction of test benches is carried out that allow the measurement of parameters of the dynamic model of drones, starting from the identification of the parameter to be measured, devising measuring mechanisms that are feasible from the constructive, functional and economical point of view. The designs are designed with the premise that the tests or measurements are replicable and offer reliable results with the required accuracy for the tests with the drones and a low maintenance cost. Additionally, for the measurement banks of friction parameters, a data acquisition system is implemented under a working environment designed in AppDesigner of MATLAB, which allows the angular velocity equation to be determined by means of the angular velocity and velocity
