# Elements for an integral theory of the property

This work begins with the dogmatic reconstruction of the concept of land ownership, demonstrating that there is no univocal idea about this category, and then analyzes the historical evolution of land ownership in Colombia in order to identify what are called the great challenges of property in the XXI century. In order to address these challenges, an integral theory of property is proposed that resorts to paradigms of critical and complex thinking from the global south, from there, other contributions of environmental thinking are incorporated that allow to base and conceptualize property as an environmental right in a perspective of integrality. Finally, the recognition of traditional land tenure systems of peasant communities is proposed as an opportunity to advance in the realization of this idea of property
