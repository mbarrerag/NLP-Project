# Harnessing materials for national progress

The most relevant ideas of the conference delivered by Dr. Baker in 1976 are presented and in which he urges the people of the United States to make efficient management of materials and to develop new ones. Likewise, he compares the reality of our country with what Dr. Baker has exposed and calls for those who work in the area of materials to promote the rational use of our materials and develop those suitable to our needs, using our own resources
