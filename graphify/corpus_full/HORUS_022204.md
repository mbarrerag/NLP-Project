# Gravitational cosmological radiation in formalism 1+3 for the theories of gravity f(R).

The gravitational waves predicted by Albert Einstein [1] have taken on great importance since their detection in 2015 [2]. In addition to astrophysical sources, gravitational waves can also occur in cosmological scenarios and can serve as evidence to study the feasibility of modified gravity theories [3]. In the covariate and invariant formalism of gauge 1 + 3 [4], the propagation of gravitational waves is described by the electrical and magnetic part of the Weyl tensor, or equivalently by the shear tensor [5]. In this work a detailed calculation is made of the different equations governing the kinematic and dynamic quantities of formalism 1 + 3, then studying the theory of disturbances to reach the Onda equations for RG and for the modified gravity theories f(R). Solutions are found for the radiation and matter epochs in RG, and possible solutions of the wave field equations for the Hu-Sawicki model [6] are studied, following the proposals for the solution of field equations given in [7, 8]
