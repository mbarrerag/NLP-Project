# Strengthening the capacity of the Agricultural Business and Agricultural Economic Research Seed for the application and analysis of structural equation models (SEM)

The resources financed by this call will be used to train the members of the seedbed of research in agribusiness and agrarian economy in the application and analysis of statistical techniques in models of structural equations. This accompaniment will be done in a group and personalized way in each of the works carried out. This will enhance the competencies of the members of the seedbed in the analysis of data and improve the quality of the work carried out
