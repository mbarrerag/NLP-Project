# SUSTAINABLE BUILDING -REFLEXIVE - A POSSIBLE WAY.

This is a chapter on the futro of construction in Colombia. In this specific case, Sustainable Construction. This paper addresses energy, rainwater and its use in construction processes and facilities. It also publicizes initiatives in ecomaterials and shows projects designed and constructed with sustainability guidelines
