# COLOR ASSESSMENT BY COMPUTER IMAGE ANALYSIS TO DETERMINE THE QUALITY OF FOOD OF FOOD

The colour in meat determines part of the sensory quality, allows correlations with other physical and chemical indicators, and is an important acceptance factor for the consumer. There are three methods to measure it: sensory, instrumental and computerised image analysis, the latter includes measurements of colour, shape, size and texture, is not destructive and allows an analytical transformation in numerical values CIE L*a*b***, this method is more precise because it considers the complete surface of the meat. To determine the colour coordinates CIE L*a*b* for porcine lomo meat, using a system of computed image analysis, using a system of computed image analysis. A sample population of 80 pigs was defined by means of a test t, with a significance level of 0.05 and a statistical power of 0.9, using reference values of other published studies. The samplings were performed at the Beneficial plant of Medellín, L; the sample was performed at the Longissimus dorsi muscle sample sample of 2 cm of colour, 2 cm of thickness, the red tone of the muscle according to the CIE L*a*a*b*b*b*****. carry out further studies and compare the results with those obtained by other instrumental methods of analysis of colour in meat
