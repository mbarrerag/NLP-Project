# Development of an IoT Architecture for environmental monitoring: integration of open source technologies with application projection in the mining sector

This work details the design and implementation of an Internet of Things architecture focused on temperature and humidity monitoring. The strategic selection of technologies, such as Arduino, LoRa modules, DHT11 sensor and ESP32 microcontroller, is the basis of the system. It highlights the use of Python Flask to develop a user interface in the web application, thus ensuring an easy and accessible user experience. In addition, the implementation of modular algorithms and authentication measures to improve the scalability and security of the system is emphasized. The article concludes by highlighting the benefits and effectiveness of open source technology in the acquisition, processing and monitoring of data in different application scenarios. Suggestions are provided for future improvements, with special attention to the expansion of data visualization and environmental monitoring. The limitations and advantages of the system are addressed, highlighting its potential application in remote areas or with limited resources. It is expected that the results obtained in this work can be scaled up to the different stages of a small and medium scale mining project in which environmental parameters are required to guide decision-making, such as a working methodology that is within the scope of small natural resources
