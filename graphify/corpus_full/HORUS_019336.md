# RESEARCH TRENDS FOR ADMINISTRATIVE DISCIPLINATION: BASES FOR A CLASSIFIED SCHEME

Currently, there is a growing academic interest in classifying the trends of intellectual production of each discipline. For the case of administrative discipline, this challenge is even greater due to the complexity that emanates from the existence of different theoretical approaches to organizations and administration, such as organizational studies, critical studies of administration, organizational theory, administrative theory, etc., and the wide variety of research trends.This compartmentation, it is worth saying, has not received the full consensus of the academic community.For the above, the aim of this work is to propose theoretical bases for the creation of a classificational scheme of research trends in administrative discipline. For this, the study carries out a documentary review with analysis and conceptual synthesis and hermeneutic interpretation.Theoretical references: The above applies to various contributions of the general philosophy of science and the epistemology of the administration and some research trends of Colombian research groups in management selected from university rankings. Results and conclusions are obtained from the above two criteria for a classification of scientific trends in management, based on the possible objects of study and the epistemology: the organizations and the administrative types of research
