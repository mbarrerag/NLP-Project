# How to mitigate the COGNITIVE BODIES IN DIVERSATION PROCESS?

This exploratory type research addressed the cognitive biases presented in the design work teams during the diverging thinking phases (ideation and co-creation) the objective was to propose a tool to promote the recognition of these biases and thus reduce their impact on decision making. Taking as a conceptual framework of design for behavioural change, which is an area of knowledge in full development that part of the knowledge generated by the behavioral economy and is focused on the design of artifacts, goods, services and strategies, a selection of 14 cognitive biases was identified based on the concept of cooperative play and the elements of communication based on the work of Alexander Osterwalder and Stefano Mastogiacomo (2021). Subsequently, the idea of a table game was carried out where the participants were working on the identified selves, taking into account the idea of cooperative play and the elements of communication based on the work of Alexander Osterwalder and Stefano Mastogiacomo's analysis (2021).
