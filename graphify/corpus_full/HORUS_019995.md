# TOWARDS A NEW MODEL OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE PROCESS

In accordance with modern times and the recognition of the theoretical and practical advances of the administration, it must be accepted that it is necessary to review and adjust aspects of its foundations, proposals and developments, in order to legitimize new orientations to which the Administration and its components sustain the evolution in the immediate future. Although the administrative theories through the different critical studies have been contextualized, redefined and sometimes standardized in their practices and concepts for a better job, many issues have not yet been raised, one of them the Administrative Process. In this investigative exercise we want to demonstrate the existence of parallel, consistent and complementary elements to those already expressed by authors of the administration in past times and thus guide the administrative world to new models, which proactively promote new considerations and integrated and integrating elements. in the administrative exercise. The methodology used in this article is of a descriptive hermeneutical type, supported by primary sources, where there is a timeless journey of different authors and rapporteurs on the object of study, highlighting the contexts and subjects of the initially constitutive elements in the Administrative Process and its importance. to others of the same order to integrate a new conception and view of the administrative task. The results, conclusions and projections are evidenced in a new model of the process, in which new elements are integrated and related
