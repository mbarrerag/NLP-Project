# Clinical standard based on evidence. Diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the obese patient. Hospital Universitario Nacional de Colombia.

The National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN) together with the National University of Colombia has positioned itself as a center of reference for healthcare and academic, with the projection of forming clinics or centers of excellence. To achieve this objective, it is indispensable that it realize and adheres to its own clinical practice guidelines or clinical standards based on evidence (ECBE), which allows adequate and standardized care of patients applying the best available medical evidence. Obesity is a pathology of high prevalence in the general population, being one of the most frequent reasons for consultation of the endocrinology service. Given its impact on public health, its association with other chronic diseases not transmissible and the negative effect on prognosis and mortality of Sars-Cov-2 disease, the creation of the Clinical Standard Based on Evidence (ECBE) was considered a priority for the diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the patient with obesity in the National University Hospital. This ECBE was made following the “Methological Manual for the development of clinical standards based on evidence” developed by the Institute of Clinical Research of the National University.
