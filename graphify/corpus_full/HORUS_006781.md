# Dosimetric validation of stereotactic radiation therapy plans in lung cancer

This paper presents an analysis of the dosimetric aspects in treatment plans of CSR, woporal stereotactic lung cancer, performed using the Eclipse treatment planning system. These plans were calculated with the Analytic Anisotropic Algorithm and the Acuros XB algorithm, and delivered in the linear accelerator Halcyon de Varian. A study of experimental cases was carried out, using tomographic images of patients who have already received pulmonary SBRT. The study population consisted of tomographic images of pulmonary SBRT planning records of patients with early stage pre-work, treated between 2020 and 2023. Methods were evaluated and implemented that allow to decrease inaccuración that is generated in the clinical flow of treatments in the Radiotherapy Service of the Oncological Medical Institute S.A.
