# OBSERVATORY OF FINANCIAL AND BUSATIL LAW

With the introduction of disruptive technologies to the financial sector in the framework of the so-called fourth industrial revolution and the constant search for efficiency by the operators of the same, the use of new models and structures based on the opening of information has been promoted. This approach implies that customers, such as the owners of the information that today rests on financial institutions, can decide who uses it, and in this way access to other operators and industries is facilitated. This in turn promotes the creation of new competitive financial solutions that respond to the growing and diverse needs of consumers, increasing the inclusion or access to both financial and other products. Through comparative research, taking as a leading-edge basis in the implementation of Free Circulation of Information Models, the research project aims to draw on the positive and negative experiences of jurisdictions such as those of the United Kingdom, the United States and Mexico; in order to identify, analyse and evaluate potentially useful regulatory guidelines for being considered when considering the incorporation of Open Banking, Openfinance or Open Data models in Colombia.
