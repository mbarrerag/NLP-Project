# VALORIZATION BIOMASS FROM ROSES WASTES USING THE ORGANOSOLV PRETREATMENT PROCESS

Colombia is a country with a high agro-industrial capacity thanks to its climatic characteristics and natural wealth. One of the main markets at national and international level is the rose crop, this being the main exported specie. However, the rose production process, especially the post-harvest and unproductive cutting stages are the ones that generate the greatest amount of waste. Although a certain proportion of this biomass waste is used to produce fertilizers, the amount of this is so big that not all biomass is reused. For this reason, organosolv pretreatment process provide a solution to the excess of wastes by means biomass valorization, generating reducing sugars, organic acids, among others, which are promising chemical platforms or biofuels in the industry. The aim of this work was to use the organosolv pretreatment process to separate the lignocellulosic complex (cellulose, hemicellulose and lignin) of the roses stalks wastes. The type of solvent (ethanol: water (1:1), glycerol: water (1:1) and ethanol: water: glycerol (1:2:1)), the effect of temperature (130°C and 200°C) and the reaction time (30 and 90 min) on sugars and inhibitors yield (glucose, xylose, HMF and furfural) and lignin removed were evaluated using a multilevel factorial experiment design. The results show that these experimental factors induce several changes in the yield of the monomeric sugars and inhibitors evaluated and specially in the removal of lignin. Structural analysis, proximate analysis and ultimate analysis were carried out in order to know the composition of the untreated roses stalks. The yield of sugars and inhibitors of the pretreated fractions (solid and liquid fractions) were evaluated by HPLC. Acid soluble lignin content was quantified by UV-Vis spectrophotometry meanwhile the acid insoluble lignin content was determined by gravimetry
