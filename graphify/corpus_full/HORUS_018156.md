# Postural balance and spatial navigation in children between 7 and 10 years of age from a school in Bogotá: Correlational study.

The advance in understanding the body-mind relationship has allowed to recognize the influence of motor qualities in cognitive processes between them the spatial processing, however, little has been investigated in the subject matter of motor control and even less in the infantile population, vital stage with particular neural behaviors and in which is in constant exposure to learning processes that need to be potentialized.The objective of this research was to establish if there is correlation between postural balance and spatial navigation in childhood. A transversal design with potentially healthy children was proposed to those who applied tests of perspective and mental rotation as indirect indicators of spatial navigation, as well as the register of physical activity and motor development, in order to obtain the correlations by means of diagrams and nonlinear measurements, additional to the measurement of the variables of spatial conditioning.The description of the behavior of the main variables of general and segmented form results were performed in function of the age, sex, level of physical activity and later motor development
