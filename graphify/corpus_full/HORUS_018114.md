This discipline involves the conceptual basis with which the social reality is interpreted, understood and oriented in practice that is taken as a study object; moreover, sociology is the diverse unit of disciplinary perspectives on the social and human with which it manages to account for the integral and characteristic dynamics of the objecto that determines for its study; and, finally, the sociological deployment of sociology is consumed in the identification and passionate dedication -based on the conceptual orientation and the various disciplinary perspectives- of study objects of national interest. I express the above from its reverse: the fact that the sociological research only takes place in ethnographic descriptions when the functionality of the practice, of the devoid relationship of conceptual clarity, exhausts the perspective of the sociologist., III- the demand to investigate problems of national interest. With this exhibition I intend to show that Darío Mesa's intellectual work gives an account of a sociological display that clearly puts what is to be relevant in the formation and praxis of the sociologist in Colombia today
