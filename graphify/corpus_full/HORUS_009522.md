# WATER WAYS, ITS XIX AND XX. FROM WOOD ARMASON TO METAL STRUCTURE

In the region of Caldas, Colombia, in the second half of the 19th century, since the beginning of the occupation of waste, a network of roads began to be consolidated that had its origins in the Conquest expeditions. During the Republic, a set of wooden bridges were built on them that followed the dictates of the European tradition, forged around the so-called carpentry of arming, thanks to the work of local artisans who knew the materials and conditions of the place. However, with the arrival of the metal structures (rigid and hanging), such structures were quickly replaced, being forgotten or, in the worst case, being destroyed. This book gives detailed account of this process, which is part of the history of the technique in Colombia
