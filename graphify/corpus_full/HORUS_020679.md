# ANALYSIS OF THE ESTIMATES OF THE ALEATORY EFFECTS OF A JERACY MODEL WITH PRIORI DISTRIBUTIONS OF PESED COLES

The Bayesian hierarchical models are used in the modelling of data in different areas in which hierarchical structures are reflected through random effects. The probability distribution considered as a natural choice for the modelling of random effects is Normal. As a priori distribution for the scale parameter is regularly used Gamma-inversa (e, e) (IG) with very small e values and this selection has had criticisms, some authors comment that subsequent unstable distributions can be obtained, which causes the inference to be not robust. Distributions such as half-Cauchy, Beta2 climbing (SBeta2) and Uniform are considered as alternatives by various authors to model the scale parameter. In this research study was carried out the behavior of the estimates of the random effects of a hierarchical model with a Bayesian approach. Comparison of random effects distributed t-Student and scale parameter distributed half-Cauchy, SBeta2 and Uniforme. A simulation study was carried out to evaluate the behavior of the model's errors was performed. It was assumed to determine the results obtained, and under the different scenarios were assessed
