# REPOSITION OF THE ARCHITECTURE PROFESSION

In our step through the university, as future architects, we are encouraged to develop architectural and urbanistic proposals that respond satisfactorily to the needs of a client or a determined society, whether it is a family, a community or a city, and so we go through projects of different scales and complexities, always trying to create comfortable and quality spaces, that develop in the best way in all the aspects that make up the program: formal, environmental, urban, functional and technological. All the above responding to the specific context where they are implemented and to the follow up of the legal and regulatory aspects. It is assumed that, with the realization of these exercises of projectual processes, the passage through all the activities and academic discussions that make up the program, and that develop throughout the career, the new professionals come out with the basic and necessary competences to face their profession. Many of these architects that annually obtain their title of the more than thirty programs of architecture that we have in the different areas of the country, then decide to take post-graduates at different levels, whether it is specialization, master or doctoral, according to their interests and expectations, the demand of the medium and/or the labor possibilities that this offers. State and society of the importance of the architect's work. - Adoption of international models in the country, which do not adapt to our territories.
