# THE GENETIC HERITAGE OF COLOMBIAN CREOLE SHEEP BREEDS HERMAN REVELE

The SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus responsible for the COVID-19 pandemic has tested Latin American health systems, so it is imminent to carry out viral surveillance studies. and implement laboratory capabilities to detect imported cases and limit transmission. In Santiago de Cali, Colombia, 281,516 cases of COVID-19 were reported between March 2020 and September 2021, of which 400 complete genomes of SARS-COV-2 have been isolated and sequenced, in which our laboratory has participated. To carry out a genomic surveillance analysis of SARS-CoV-2 in Santiago de Cali. We have performed a phylogenetic and bioinformatics analysis to identify the viral strains of SARS-COV-2, applying mobile sequencing technologies, such as that of Oxford Nanopore, a fast and efficient method to evaluate the viral evolution in an outbreak. Our analysis showed the circulation of the variants of interest (VOI) lambda and mu, as well as the variants of concern (VOC) Our alpha, gamma and delta.
