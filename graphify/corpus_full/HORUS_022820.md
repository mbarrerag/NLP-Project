# Variety Catalog of Yuca, Cauca - Colombia

The Colombian Economic and Social Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (CATT) has been working on the evaluation of cauca cultivation in Latin America and South-East Asia. In Colombia, the Brazilian and Latin American countries have been able to study the processing of cauca starch from fresh roots. This work has been made possible thanks to the strategic alliance between CIAT (Colombia), Cirad (France), Universidad del Valle and Universidad Nacional (Colombia) and has been used to improve the extraction of cauca starch, as well as to ensure the quality of the final product (the sour starch). This work has been done in order to strengthen the value chain, strengthening the supply of citrus starch, vital for the production of pandeyuca and pandebono in the region, and has thus been able to produce a similar impact in Africa and Latin America, innovating in the panning.
