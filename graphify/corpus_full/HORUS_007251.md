# Simulation of residence time distribution in a hectic tank using CFD

In this study, the distribution of the time of residence (RTD) was analyzed in agitated tanks using simulation by dynamics of computational fluids (CFD). Tanks of 1, 5 and 100 litres were used, under different operating and geometric conditions, evaluating the impact of variables such as agitation speed, the position of the agitator and retention time, with the aim of studying the fluid dynamic behavior in these systems. The agitation speeds were 144, 246 and 399 rpm for the 1 L tank, 59, 89 and 142 rpm for the 5L tank, and 9, 14 and 22 rpm for the 100 L tank, obtaining numbers of Reynolds approximately 8000, 12,000 and 20000 respectively. The experimental results of RTD were obtained using red pigment as a tracer and an economic capture system, which were compared with the results of the CFD simulations, the validation of the numerical models showed a high agreement between the simulated and experimental results, especially when using the standard k-0 model, with mesh adjustments that optimized the accuracy of the simulations. er 144, 246 and 399 rpm for the 1 L tank, 59, 89 and 142 rpm for the 5 L tank, and 9, 14 and 22 rpm for the 100 L tank, obtaining Reynolds numbers of approximately 8000, 12,000 and 20000 respectively.
