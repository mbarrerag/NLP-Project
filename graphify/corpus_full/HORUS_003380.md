# DOCUMENTARY RESEARCH FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A EVIDENCE-BASED GUIDE TO SUPPORT THE CAREGIVE OF THE PATENT WITH APHASIA

UMN: Objective: To identify evidence-based phonoaudiological strategies that can be applied by caregivers of patients with afasia, guiding the communicative interaction between caregivers and patients in their day-to-day lives at home. A documentary search was carried out in 4 databases (PubMed, LILACS, MedLine and ASHA database) with the terms aphasia, caregivers, treatment, strategies and phonoaudiologists, in Spanish and English, between 2005 and 2023, obtaining 48 articles selected by their title, which were later analyzed by abstract, of them 34 articles that were read to obtain strategies and techniques that were later adapted and exemplified within the guide of follow-up to the patient with aphasia based on the evidence. 9 therapeutic strategies and 2 techniques were obtained, which were adapted and exemplified within the final guide, developing 3 to 5 activities for caregivers of patients with aphasia to perform in their day-to-day lives, thus enhancing the communication skills and contributing to the process of language recovery
