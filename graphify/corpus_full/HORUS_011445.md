# USE OF INSECTS AS ALIFICULTURE: EXPERIENCES IN COLOMBIA

The low-cost and low-cost farming sector is part of the local and regional development in emergency in countries of the global south. However, the food costs represent the most relevant item of the aquaculture systems, on any of its scales. Among the main causes are the dependence of imported raw materials, such as soy cake, corn, flour and fish oil, among others. These resources, are expensive and their production could be unsustainable over time. In search of food alternatives, several species of insects have been widely used, mainly as a protein source. Among the most widely used species are the black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens; BSF by its acronym in English), the domestic fly (musca domestica), the domestic cricket (Acheta domestica), the field cricket (Gryllus assimilis) and the agricultural worms (Tenebrio moor and Zophobas morio).
