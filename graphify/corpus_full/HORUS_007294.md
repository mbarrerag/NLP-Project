# Design of a pedagogical tool to promote the study and conservation of spiders present in the Cruz Verde moor, Bogotá

The moors are unique ecosystems that play vital functions for the planet, such as atmospheric carbon capture, water storage and maintenance of plant and animal species exclusive to these habitats. This project was carried out in the green Cruz moor, with a focus on spiders, organisms that often go unnoticed and generate fear in many people, possibly because of the lack of knowledge of their role in nature. The aim of this work was to design a pedagogical tool aimed at middle school students, in order to present the most representative spiders of the green Cruz moor, to highlight their ecological importance and to promote their conservation together with that of the ecosystem where they live. The project was developed by means of a mixed research methodology, based on the significant and structured learning in three phases.
