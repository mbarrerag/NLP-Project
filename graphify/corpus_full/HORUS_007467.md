# Radio channel modelling in Bogotá D.C. for 5G communications in millimetre wave frequencies

This doctoral thesis presents the results of experimental research aim at modeling the channel in a millimeter wave transmission in the city of Bogota. Divers Semiconductors transceivers are used as the main device for the measurement, operating in the 24 to 29.5 GHz band and in the 24 to 29.5 GHz band. The main device used for the measurements were Semiconductors transceivers operating in the 24 GHz to 29.5 GHz band and in the 57 GHz band, for which a test bench was designed and implemented with different hardware and software elements. Additionally, the results of measurements performed with commercial equipment in the 28 GHz band during the investigation period were located at the 5G Laboratory located at the Chile in Santiago de Chile University present. The results of measurements performed by Colombia National and internality in the 28 GHz band during the investigation period. The results allow to appreciate the propagation performance in frequencies of millimetric waves in bands of interest of 26 GHz and 60 GHz in outdoor and indoor scenarios of the National University of Colombia in Bogotá and in the frequency of 28 GHz in indoor and outdoor scenarios to the 5G Laboratory of the University of Chile
