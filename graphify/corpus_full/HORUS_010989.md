# Estimate and prediction of the costs of all residential blocks of the municipalities included in the National Population and Housing Census of Colombia (DANE, 2018) for the identification of real estate projects of the Success Group.

Based on data from the National Population and Housing Census, 2005 and 2018, the Large Integrated Household Survey (GEIH) and the National Household Budget Survey (ENPH) of 2018, the Microbusiness Survey of 2021 and the databases of the Success Group, this paper develops a methodology for predicting the costs of all residential apples in Colombia, combining data analytics techniques and supervised machine learning models for continuous variables, which will allow the design of scenarios for real estate investment projects
