# Nutritional evaluation and sensory acceptability of a candy with modified base formula from quinoa, peas and cocoa liqueur

This study evaluated a golosin with modified base formula, elaborated from quinua, arveja and cocoa liqueur, inspired by the traditional Colombian recipe known as "chucula". The general objective was to evaluate its nutritional composition and its sensory acceptability as an attractive and healthy alternative for consumers. The introduction highlighted the process of nutritional transition in Latin America, where the consumption of foods with high glycemic index, deficit of nutrients and products ready for consumption has affected the health of the population. In response, the revaluing of ancestral and traditional foods has been promoted to improve the nutritional quality. The "chucula" was presented as an appropriate content of proteins, fiber and carbohydrates, and obtained high approval in the sensory analysis. The connection with the tradition and ancestral culture was an important factor in its acceptance.
