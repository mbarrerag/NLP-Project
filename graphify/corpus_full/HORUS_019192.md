# PALATIONS AND CHARGES

This book analyzes the results obtained by more than 26,000 students from the National University of Colombia who have presented the SABER Pro tests during the period 2016 to 2020. The main purpose of this document is to analyze the gender gaps, type of admission, stratum and type of college in cross-cutting or generic competencies (quantitative reasoning, critical reading, citizen competencies, English, written communication) and disciplinary or specific competencies for all the headquarters, faculties and curriculum programs of the National University of Colombia. Likewise, it analyses comparative performance in some of these competencies in the Admission Examination with the performance obtained in the SABER Pro test in order to determine the effect of the University Experience in the development of these competencies. Finally, the results found here can be used as a roadmap for the implementation of future academic improvement actions in the National University of Colombia. Throughout the document, the reader will find a series of graphic information with which he can interact according to his interests and even, with interactive maps to establish some interesting relationships on the scores that help him/herself in the different competencies
