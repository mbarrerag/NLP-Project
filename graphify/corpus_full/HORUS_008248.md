# Human resource and geographical distance as barriers to access to health services, Caquetá, Colombia, 2020: quantitative analysis and from the perspective of health system actors

Abstract Introduction: Historically, access to health services has been measured through the rates of insurance of a population; however, this approach has been widely questioned as it does not provide effective access figures, considering that individuals face various barriers to access to health services, which are exacerbated in remote territories. to determine whether the number of health providers and professionals, as well as their geographical distribution, constitute barriers to access to health services in the department of Caquetá, Colombia; in turn, the perception of some actors of the system against these barriers is evidenced. a mixed descriptive cross-sectional study. In the quantitative phase, secondary data were collected through consultation in databases, and a system of distance/time travel measurement was applied; while in the qualitative phase semi-structured interviews were conducted with health system actors. of the 16 municipalities, three have barriers to access to health services, two by land and one by river.
