# Factors associated with intraoperative and postoperative complications in patients with pituitary adenomas who were taken to transsphenoidal surgery. Multicenter descriptive cohort study – retrospective

The hypophysiary adenomas are the tenth part of intracranial lesions, and correspond to 20 to 50% of intraselare lesions, which when causing symptoms of neuroendocrine disorder or local mass effect require surgical treatment, by means of endoscopic transsphenoidal endoscopic resection. The gold standard is the one that shows better results with respect to tumor resection volume and lower rate of complications compared to open surgery. Therefore, the histopathological distribution in the Colombian population is attempted in a multicenter descriptive retrospective study with multivariable analysis to demonstrate its association of pathologies and perioperative conditions for the development of trans and post surgical complications .
