# Analysis of the prescription for insomnia management between 2019 and 2021, in the primary care services of the SubRed Sur de Bogotá, a mixed study

In Colombia, insomnia is a common pathology that presents challenges in its treatment due to the lack of specific guidelines for the local population. The approach is typically based on cognitive behavioral therapy (TCC) combined with Z drugs. In this mixed study on the management of insomnia in patients aged 18 years and over the Integrated Subnet of Health Services South E.S.E of Bogotá, clinical histories were analyzed with specific diagnostics (codes ICD 10: G47.9 - G47.8 - F51.9).
