# Cost-Effective, DIY, and Open-Source Digital Lensless Holographic Microscope with Distortion Correction

A cost-effective, DIY, and open-source certifiable digital lensless holographic microscope (DLHM) is presented. The proposed microscope reduces the flaws regularly found when budget laser diodes and aspherical lenses are utilized in compact setups
