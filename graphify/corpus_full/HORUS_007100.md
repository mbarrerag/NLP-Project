# Acupuncture as auxiliary management of emotional stress in adults a narrative review

Emotional stress is a highly prevalent condition in the general population, with well documented serious repercussions for physical and mental health. It is of the utmost importance that health personnel can properly recognize this alteration, as well as identify their alarm signs to provide optimal management in accordance with the needs and risks of the patient. In this document a narrative review of the most relevant aspects in terms of identification, physiopathology and treatment of emotional stress from the perspective of conventional medicine is also proposed a comparison with Traditional Chinese Medicine, especially with acupuncture, with the aim of providing added value, in terms of results, improvement in the control of symptoms and decrease of side effects of pharmacological therapies
