# Theoretical analysis of the impact of emotions on the emergence of collective identity and political subjectivation in the liberal democracies of the 21st century

The liberal democracies today face various challenges to deal with phenomena such as the reception of migrants by the destination nations, the rise of political populisms, religious fundamentalisms, the demands for recognition of minority groups, among others. Most of these situations present a common source of motivation, are conflicts that are transversalised by analytical categories such as emotions, collective identity and political subjection, in that sense, from a post-foundational approach, the intention of this work is to expand the way of understanding these concepts, to point out their need for complementarity, to indicate that the processes of identification and de-identification have a background that comes from emotions, which motivate the construction of identities and processes of political subjection.
