# Genetic architecture of reproductive biology of Colombian yucca varieties resistant to vascular bacteriosis: the beginning of a participatory phyto-improvement program in the country

The yuca is a pillar crop for food security and sovereignty of many tropical countries. Although the yuca is highly tolerant to different conditions of biotic and abiotic stress, and presents a relative high adaptability to cultivation conditions, in order to reach the maximum agronomic development and meet the agricultural and hilly expectations it is necessary to develop varieties adapted to the needs of the peasants and adapted to particular conditions. The program of genetic breeding of cassava of the CIAT in partnership with Agrosavia has managed to develop varieties improved adapted to different conditions and with desired agronomic characteristics. However, it has been evidenced by some reports and visits to territories, especially in the Colombian Caribbean, that in many occasions these varieties are not highly accepted by the producers and are not those that are mainly allowed to be sown. Thus in the region of Montes de María the variety that is most cultivated is the so called _x0093_venezolana_x0094_ in spite of the fact that several varieties are developed for this area.
