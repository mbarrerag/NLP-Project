# The urban renewal project Ciudad Paraíso and its relationship with the Colombian urban development system

The Colombian urban planning system contained in the Law of Territorial Development (LDT) has established the treatment of urban renewal through the instrument of partial plan as a strategy to recover the areas that became enclaves of deterioration or urban voids in the process of transformation of some municipalities such as Santiago de Cali. The LDT establishes the principles of territorial planning in Colombia, which should be applied in all planning and urban management instruments. However, there are reasonable doubts that partial plans of expansion and urban renewal are being designed and implemented in disarticulation with the principles of urban land planning approved.
