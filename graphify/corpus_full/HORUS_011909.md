# More than just the RANKINGS: Is there research into artificial intelligence for sustainable development?

The production of international research, innovation and technology rankings allows us to have an overview of the capabilities and preparation of a country to produce and use scientific knowledge. This is the case of the Global Innovation Index at the level of national innovation systems and, in the case of artificial intelligence (ia), the ranking made by Oxford Insights (2021), which provides data on the use of information technologies in government, ia business initiatives and the availability of public data
