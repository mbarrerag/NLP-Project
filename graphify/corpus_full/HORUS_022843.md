# IMPACT OF MINERITY TO OPEN CIELO ASSOCIATED TO CONSTRUCTION NEARER COMMUNITIES

The present chapter addresses a reflection regarding open-pit mining for the extraction of stone materials and the impacts generated towards nearby communities from two aspects: the first is the phenomenon of wind-squeezing or abrasion and the second is the phenomenon of the heat island. For the determination of the corrosion, a wind profile was established in order to identify the direction and speed of the wind, later with the help of advanced electron microscopy sweeping, the microstructure of the facades of the houses facing north, in the direction of the quarry, and towards the south, in the opposite direction of the quarry. The phenomenon of the heat island is established by determining the surface temperature, through the use of satellite images type LANDSAT that with the help of geographically information programs, adjusted and rectified, the surface temperature of the study area was determined.
