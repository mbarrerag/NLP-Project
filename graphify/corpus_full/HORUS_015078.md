# Effect of abiotic variables on the hyporheic fauna of the Dagua River, Colombia

Abstract Introduction: The sediment fauna of the subsoil, where the exchange between groundwater and surface water, called +8220; hyporreica fauna +8221;, fulfills important ecological functions in rivers; however, it has not been sufficiently studied in Colombia. To identify the relations between hyporheic invertebrates and physical and chemical variables in the Dagua river, Colombia. In three field tours (between October 2017 and December 2018) in five sampling stations along the river basin, we performed perforations implementing the method of Karaman-Chappuis (sediment, water and invertebrates), and recorded the granulometry and organic matter in sediments; moreover, we measured nutrients, ions and metals in water. The stations in the area of headboard had substrates with thick sediments (gravity and stone), higher oxygen levels and lower temperature of water in the basin.
