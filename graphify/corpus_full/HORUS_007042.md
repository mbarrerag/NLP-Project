# University dropout in the Business Administration Program of the Faculty of Administration of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Manizales headquarters: Relationship to pedagogical practices

This study aims to determine the relationship between the main pedagogical practices and university dropout in the curriculum of Business Administration of the Faculty of Administration of the National University of Colombia, Manizales headquarters. To carry out this research, a tool for collecting data validated by expert evaluation and measured in reliability through the Cronbach Alpha, ensuring internal consistency of the questionnaire applied to students. A quantitative approach was used and analysis was carried out with SPSS statistical software, where frequency tables, bivariate correlations, heat maps and Chi-square tests were used to examine the relationships between pedagogical variables and dropout. The results revealed that pedagogical practices such as lack of clarity in instruction, lack of training feedback, and insufficient pedagogical adaptation to the individual needs of students have a significant influence on the dropout rate. In addition, students who experienced greater customization and focus in the evaluation showed a greater retention.
