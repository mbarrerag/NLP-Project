# An almost strong relation

Let μ be a strong limit singular cardinal. We prove that if 2μ > μ+ then the almost strong polarized partition relation (μμ+) → (μτ)θ for every ordinal τ < μ+ and for every θ < cf(μ). We obtain an optimal positive relation under 2μ = μ+, as after collapsing 2μ to μ+ this positive relation is preserved
