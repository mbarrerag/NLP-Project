# ANDROCENTRIC CANON IN THE SOCIOLOGY PROGRAMME OF NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA: TAX TO THE OVERCOME OF EPISTEMIC VIOLENCES

The disputes over the definition of canon in social theory have led to the consolidation of an androcentric disciplinary field that involves a series of voluntary forgetfulnesss of other voices, other problems, other methods to study society; these forgetfulnesss are a form of epistemic violence. This article starts from the definition of the classical canon and its androcentric character, and exposes the evidence of its application at the National University of Colombia. Below, we present several contributions of feminist criticism to the canon, to the erasure of the women it entails, and to the effects of its application in the academic and professional sociological field. Finally, we return to different proposals on how to destabilize the canon, how to rewrite the history of discipline against the erasure of women, and to rethink the sociology that we want to contribute to develop
