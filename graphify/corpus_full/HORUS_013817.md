# Improvement of the technical and technological infrastructure of the laboratories of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Manizales

The National University of Colombia has been, is and will continue to be an institution of teaching, research and development; it must therefore be attentive to the new requirements and guarantee the competitiveness that will enable it to develop these functions; in this sense, it is intended to support at least 9 laboratories of Manizales Headquarters, especially those that provide the teaching service. The support to the laboratories will be based on priority needs and projected according to a global vision of development of each laboratory and all laboratories as a whole. The results of the project will not simply be a sum of acquisitions (e.g. equipment and maintenance), but rather a progress of the project inherent to each laboratory. The project also contemplates the implementation of improvement plans based on the diagnosis of the laboratory system at Manizales Headquarters. As a result, the level of functionality of the laboratories and the laboratory system at Headquarters will be improved
