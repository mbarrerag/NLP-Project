# From Humor to Protest: An Analysis of the Oppositions Between Censorship and Freedom of Expression in South Park

This study explores how South Park addresses and represents the oppositions between censorship and freedom of expression. Using an approach based on the political economy of communication, the discourses present in key episodes are analyzed to understand the position of the series in relation to these issues. Through a qualitative methodology that combines hermeneutic interpretation and content analysis, it identifies how creators use satire and transgressive humor to question and challenge the rules imposed by corporate and social entities. The results reveal that South Park not only actively criticizes censorship mechanisms, but also promotes free expression as a fundamental value. Through its irreverent style, the series reveals the contradictions inherent in the restrictions of public discourse, encouraging a critical debate about the limits of expression in contemporary media culture. .
