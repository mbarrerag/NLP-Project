# MODEL OF JOINT ENTRY PROGRAMMING FOR GEOGRAPHICAL LOCATION OF SECURITY AND JUSTICE CENTERS IN BOGOTA COLOMBIA

A common problem of value chains that have after-sales service operations, as well as of the consulting, insurance and collection industry, is to determine the number of commercial agents that will have the task of carrying out or meeting a call demand from users, in this way, this article establishes the size of this work team in an insurance company that carries out portfolio operations, under the criterion of achieving a minimum staff cost. To this end, this article summarizes the activities carried out to achieve the objective of establishing the number of agents through three stages, which starts with a process of identification and characterisation of the operation of the portfolio management, finding the operations for each service that is performed, estimated the average time of each operation, as well as the demand for each type of service; after this, a linear programming model is constructed under the guidance of a staff programming problem with which a resource and demand analysis is carried out. Finally, from the analysis carried out, it was possible to determine the size of the workforce necessary to meet the projected demand, as well as to conclude with a synthesis of the size of the work team, cost of payroll and the capacity analysis required for this type of the company
