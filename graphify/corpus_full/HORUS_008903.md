# CHARACTERIZATION OF BLAND TECHNOLOGIES IN SMALL FINISH WINNERS OF THE NARBIUM, COLOMBIA DEPARTMENT

soft technologies are related to the management of information and knowledge that an organization implements to improve its performance. to characterize soft technologies adopted in small livestock farms in the department of Nariño, Colombia. a descriptive cross-sectional study was carried out in a sample of 62 small cattle ranches in the municipalities of La Llanada and Los Andes of the department of Nariño, Colombia; in which research was carried out on the use of soft technologies in the production system. 63% of cattle farmers had been carrying out bovine activity for more than 10 years, with herds oriented to double purpose, milk and meat with a participation of 52, 34 and 14%, respectively; and an average inventory of 4.5±3.2 animals. 18% of producers had technical records, while 10% had applied accounting records. 16% received technical assistance, but none had accounting or administrative advice. 45% of producers handled animals in batches, according to their physiological status, 16% identified animals individually and 5% weighed the offspring at birth and weaning.
