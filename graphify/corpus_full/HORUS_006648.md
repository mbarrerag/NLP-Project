# Characterization of legitimization and rationalization processes in software services implemented by successful product manufacturers in the massive mobile app market from 2008 to the present

This paper investigates the presence of institutionalization processes in the mobile applications market as a way of finding alternative mechanisms that, in future work, serve the adoption of information systems in organizations. It takes as a reference some examples of successful implementations in which a significant user base has decided to make use of specific applications for its particular tasks. Such a decision, which is mediated by the functional characteristics of the products, could be conditioned also by discursive elements that users access as they shape their usage intentions. Promotion of products by manufacturers, intervention of specialized means by product reviews and publication of comments by users using the applications, are some of the elements that may influence the decision to use or not a specific application.
