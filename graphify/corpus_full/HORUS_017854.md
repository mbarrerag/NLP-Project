# LSS AND I4.0 IN MANUFACTURER SMEs: A REVISION OF LITERATURA

To achieve improved productivity in the operations of SMEs through the use of methodologies that support them is the challenge of business leaders, for this reason research is undertaken to identify the studies that have been addressed on Lean Six Sigma (LSS) and Industry 4.0 (I4.0) in small and medium-sized manufacturing enterprises (SMEs) published between 2005 and 2020 in English as a preliminary input for the development of future research in the area. This article seeks to determine, based on a review of scientific literature, what trends, countries, authors and methodological factors of greater and lesser participation, through the analysis of the LSS, I4.0 and SMEs so that future research opportunities are identified
