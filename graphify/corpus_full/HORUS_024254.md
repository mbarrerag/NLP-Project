# Added value of regional climate modelling in areas characterized by complex terrain: precipitation over the Colombian Andes.

First, the value added (VA) of the dynamic scale reduction was investigated using simulated values of precipitation obtained with the regional climate model (MRC) RegCM4 (with two resolutions, 0.44 ° and 0.22 °) forced by two different global climate models (MGCs) (HadGEM2-ES and MPI-ESM-MR) of the project of intercomparison of coupled climate models (CMIP5) for the period 1981-2005, from the experiment CORDEX, with a focus on the Colombian Andean region. Different metrics of VA were used: mean precipitation pattern, annual precipitation cycle, Di Luca-Dioso formula and distribution of daily precipitation intensity. The comparison with the reference data CHIRPS showed that RegCM4 does not provide VA in the regions of complex topography (Colombian and Colombian races), however, in the flat regions of Colombia, VA was demonstrated for RegCM4. Second, the susceptibility of this model was analyzed in the choice of different resolutions, dimensions of the domain, MGCS and SPS5 was applied., when selected either EMAN, GAS, GFC, GLEO or TIE. EMAN and TIE were found to have the best performance together with the BATS surface scheme
