# • To take care of persons with pain and cardic insufficiency from the theory of adaptation to chronic health conditions

Introduction: about 85% of patients with heart failure report pain, this generates concern because, according to pain typology, this experience can affect the quality of life of patients and their caregivers. to design a nursing care strategy based on Callista Roy's theory of adaptation to chronic health conditions, to reduce the pain experience in people with heart failure. the empirical theoretical conceptual structure (EQF) of the selected mid-range theory was developed, to consolidate the concepts and propositions that generated the components or domains of the strategy. Subsequently, to construct the care of the strategy, a systematic review of the literature was carried out with search in the databases: ScienceDirect, Scopus and Medline, with the terms "pain", "heart failure", "heart failure", and "necessary care" and "necessity" were applied, and the evidence was organized according to the emerging domains of the TEC for this phenomenon. a strategy with care was designed that addressed the domains: 1) physiological, with evaluation of the symptom, exercise, weight control and painful sites; 2) self-giving, and role with the disease, and stress, and with the effect of anxiety, and stress reduction of anxiety
