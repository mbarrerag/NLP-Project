# SELF-COMPOSIVE NEGOTIATIVE APPROACH FOR COVID PANDEMIA -19

It is a text of comparative law between the legal systems of Germany and Colombia. It puts into perspective the importance of the contract as an intersubjective regulatory instrument and its objective and material relativity. This relativity of autonomy may result from the proof of an extraordinary and unpredictable circumstance that allows the application of the Theory of Unpredictability in Colombia, or the Decay of the bases of the legal business in Germany. It is then contracted to analyze contrastingly but also equivalently whether the Covid- 19 pandemic fits within the typical scope of application of these institutes. The affirmative response, then allows theorizing and analyzing, what judicial or transactional adjustments are or should be, for some of the most widespread contractual casuistics in the two countries.
