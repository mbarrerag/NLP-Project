# The “agriculture locomotive” and the populated centers in the municipalities of Puerto López and Puerto Gaitán (Meta), in the period 2010-2018

Since the fall of oil prices in the first decade of the 21st century, Colombia has undergone a transformation in the economic guideline that was promoted by the National Government, strengthening the agro-industrial sector through planning instruments of national scale that allowed the creation of the so-called “Locomotora Agroindustrial”. In this sense, the department of Meta -the main producer of hydrocarbons-, turned its economy to the agricultural potential that houses its territory, forging new attractive conditions for large-scale agro-industrial projects. This process brought with it multiple events, including a great labor demand from agro-industrial companies, and with this a new migration process towards the territories adjacent to the main agricultural and livestock production centers, which led to the emergence or spatial reconfiguration of populated centres from the new economic and social dynamics produced by the relationship of the communities with the enterprises.
