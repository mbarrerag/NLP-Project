# COMPLETE DOCTING APPROVAL OF ICT IN EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS GOVERNED WITH TECHNOLOGICAL TOOLS

The research sought to understand the process of appropriation of information and communication technologies (ICT) in schools equipped with technological tools, using the model of technology appropriation (MAT) and the Piagetian conception of analytical complexity. Focus groups were carried out with teachers from 82 public secondary education institutions. The resulting corpus was analyzed from the perspective of the grounded theory based on the levels of adoption, adaptation and integration of ICTs and tracing differences in the process due to urban or rural location. It is concluded that the do-cents appropriate ICTs when they prove that it is a useful tool for their work with students, either as a way to improve co-munition with them, as a way to improve their teaching activities, or as a way of motivating students in the medium or long term
