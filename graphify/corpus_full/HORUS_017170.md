# OF TRAINING IN ZOOTECNIA TO DOCUMENTATION AND RESEARCH IN GENOMIC DIVERSITY AND IMPROVING IN COLOMBIA

The object of study of animal husbandry are mainly domestic animals and to a lesser extent wild animals, in order to obtain benefit from them, mainly by obtaining products or services for the benefit of human beings. The current animal husbandry with the support of the formal, natural and social sciences seeks to obtain the maximum economic yield possible, guaranteeing the welfare of animals and the proper administration of resources under criteria of environmental sustainability, so it is based on some basic pillars of animal production, such as (1) Nutrition and animal feeding; (2) Genetics and improvement; (3) Reproduction; (4) Health; (5) Management; (6) Animal welfare; (7) Administration; (8) Environmental sustainability (Zambrano, 2019). Based on these pillars, animal husbandry has been responding to the challenges and demands of modern societies. However, the role it plays is still associated with the generation of knowledge and technological development, with the aim of increasing production and diversifying the supply of products and services of animal origin, but using less natural resources, ensuring the welfare of animals and protecting the environment, by means of sustainable intensification of the systems of production (Mahon et al., 2017)
