# Languages in education: contributions of a linguistic policy for the archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina

The Caribbean Headquarters of the National University of Colombia, located on the island of San Andrés, developed between 2011 and 2013, in coordination with the Institute of Vocational Technical Training (INFOTEP), the participatory construction of guidelines for a language policy for the archipelago department, under the mandate of the territorial government of the archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina, Colombia. Consultation, diagnosis, training, research and intervention in the community, allowed to define the problems arising from the presence of three majority languages in contact in the island territory: Creole, English and Spanish. This article aims to present the contributions that the policy guidelines made in the time towards the management of the languages in the formal education system of the islands
