# Economical differences on fragmentation in the care of patients with breast and stomach cancer in the contributory regime in Colombia.

Breast cancer and stomach cancer are pathologies of high incidence and mortality in Colombia. For treatment, the three pillars of treatment are required: cancer surgery, chemotherapy and radiation therapy. The lack of integration of the three pillars can lead to fragmentation of health care, this research measures the impact on total care costs. A retrospective study of cohorts of subjects included between 2013 and 2015 and a follow-up of 3 years was carried out. The subjects were identified by algorithms based on the registration of services and CIE-10 codes related to the types of breast cancer under study. The exposure was the fragmentation defined as the number of different IPS during the first year of survival that formed the patient's care network. The outcome was the sum of the costs of all services paid from the third payer's view and deflated to weights of year 2021. The impact of fragmentation on costs with multivariate models of linear regression using 95% confidence intervals from robust standard errors.
