# Genetic survey and variation of the populations of Sparisoma chrysopterum (Labriformes, Scarinae) in the southern and western Caribbean

Loro fish play a vital role in marine ecosystems, especially in coral reefs, a highly threatened and extremely important ecosystem because it houses about 25% of the diversity of marine life. These fish have become the objective of the artisanal fishermen of the Caribbean and their decrease in natural habitats has been remarkable. Despite the importance of these organisms, little is known about the populations in terms of their genetic diversity. The fisheries resources generally do not take into account the population structure nor local adaptations, instead they are considered, prematurely, panmistic stocks and, although the parrot fish are not established as a fishing resource, they suffer from this pressure.
