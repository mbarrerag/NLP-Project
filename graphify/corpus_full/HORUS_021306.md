# --Inter-agency management to contribute to the productive development of bamboo (Guadua Angustifolia Kuhnt) in the Cauca Valley.

The interest of the National University of Colombia on bamboo (Guadua Angustifolia Kunth) and its possibilities is reflected in the repertoire of courses offered and published studies, as well as in the development of events around the topic. In this field of study, disciplines such as engineering, industrial design and architecture find promising ground to implement actions or initiatives that contribute to strengthen the development of spaces and products using bamboo as raw material. As antecedents to this, in the Palmira headquarters it is necessary that in 2012 the Industrial Design program had the opportunity to develop academic projects in this field, thanks to the approach made with the NGO Fundación Bambúes de Colombia - FUNBACOL, company legally constituted at the beginning of 2008, located in the city of Buga. In that opportunity was achieved the recognition of processes related to the treatment of the guadua to convert it into raw material and were carried out explorations of products developed from bamboo and objetual proposals to implement in the Recreative Center of Tablons.
