# ANALYSIS OF SPACE DISTRIBUTION OF PAATONAL CRUCES APPLICATING A MODEL OF GEOGRAPHICAL ACCESSIBILITY. CASE OF STUDY: ADDED SANTANDER, MANIZALS (COLOMBIA)

The objective of this research is to develop a methodological proposal to determine areas of low pedestrian accessibility in the mobility corridor along Avenida Santander in the city of Manizales (Colombia) and, with this, to propose the insertion of new pedestrian crossings in that corridor. The model of transport offering of integral average accessibility is used. The results show areas of low pedestrian accessibility in the mobility corridor of Avenida Santander, leaving in evidence the lack of pedestrian crossings in those areas. It was proposed to insert new pedestrian crossings to improve accessibility. As a conclusion, it is established that the methodology presented can be applied as a territorial planning instrument to determine the conditions of pedestrian accessibility
