# Validation of the CVT-GOHISALO instrument on humanitarian workers, Colombia 2023

The definition of the concept of Quality of Working Life today is perceived in an imprecise way because it is related to multiple and complex variables such as satisfaction, motivation, work autonomy, improvement expectations, personal achievements, work benefits, among others, that make up and build the worker's job stability throughout its environment.The current and different researches, worldwide, have proposed instruments of measurement that mix variables as previously indicated to make an academic approach to the construct Quality of Working Life, describing it and adapting it for various work scenarios in order to have a more rigorous and precise approach to the conditions offered to the employees hired by the companies. This research had as a goal both the validation of content and the validation of appearance of an instrument that measures Quality of Working Life in health workers, focused on workers that are performed in humanitarian work in Colombia. A study of descriptive and transversal type, was included in the participation of 7 thematic experts and/or methodological to carry out the validation phase of the CVT GOHISALO instrument proposed for this study, two validation sessions and a discussion session for the validation of content, later a successful analysis of the evaluation of the evaluation of the project. . The definition of the concept of Quality of Work Life is currently perceived imprecisely, as it is related to multiple and complex variables such as satisfaction, motivation, work autonomy, expectations for improvement, staff achievements, work benefits, various other. These factors contribute to and share the job stability of workers within their environment. Recent global research has proposed measure instruments that combine the strengthened variables to provide an academic analysis of the construct of Work Life, describing and adapting it for various work opportunities and validated in order to increase and clarify understanding of the conditions offered to employees. An average Lawshe index of 0.85 was obtained for all items. For the application validation, a pilot test was applied to 35 humanitarian action workers, obtaining an average understanding of 99% for the instrument. The reliability of the instrument was determined using Cronbach's Alpha, resulting in an overall value of 0.968, similar to the results found in González-Baltazar's study. The results presented according values according to Ruiz Bolívar's relative magnitude scale, both for the instrument as a whom and for its 7 dimensions.
