# CURRICULARY CHANGE TO FRONT THE TECHNOLOGICAL CHALLENGES OF THE COLOMBIA CONSTRUCTION INDUSTRY

The changes that have been taking place in the AECO sector (Architecture, Engineering, Construction & Operations) due to the adoption of technology, are becoming increasingly important, requiring universities and higher education institutions to redefine the training and adaptation of teaching-learning processes in a dynamic and accelerated way with the aim of mitigating the business risk associated with the competences of human capital. Additionally, since the adoption of the training criteria around learning outcomes, education institutions have entered into a series of processes to adapt to these changes without having an adequate baseline. To this end, the creation of actions leading to the articulation of criteria for training at different levels of knowledge is an imperative that must be addressed as soon as possible. This project presents the technological gaps that are currently found among construction industry companies and higher education programmes that support them.
