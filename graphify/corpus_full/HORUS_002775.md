# NEW ZIRCONIUM-BASED METAL¿ORGANIC FRAMEWORK AS EFFICIENT VISIBLE-LIGHT PHOTOCATALYST IN AEROBIC OXIDATION REACTIONS

Oligo phenylenevinylenes (OPVs) structures have been defined as oligomers analogous to polyacetylene, in which any other diene unit in the polyene chain is converted to a benzene unit. The main interest in this type of organic molecules is attributed to its chromophore nature manifested in its high fluorescence that can be used for the development of chemical sensors, optical switches, markers in biological systems, bacterial infections photodynamic treatments, or as photocatalysts in environmental remediation due to its high capacity to generate reactive oxygen species in the presence of visible radiation. In this work, a new porous Zr based metal organic framework (OPV COO/Zr) was successfully synthesized using 4,4'--((1E,1'E)--(2,5 dimethoxy 1,4 phenylene)bis(ethene 2,1 diyl))dibenzoic acid (OPV) as the organic linker and its photocatalytic performance and stability were investigated systematically. The structure of the catalytic material was confirmed by X ray powder diffraction, infrared spectroscopy, N2 adsorption desorption measurements, UV Vis diffuse reflectance spectroscopy, SEM microscopy, and X ray photoelectron spectroscopy. The photocatalytic activity was examined in the photo oxidation of t anethole under visible radiation in the presence of molecular O2 as oxidizing agent. The reaction was monitored by GC, yielding as reaction products 1--(4 methoxyphenyl)propan 1 ol, 4 methoxybenzaldehyde and 2--(4 3 methyloxirane. The OPV COO/Zr MOF have good potential as catalysts to be used and recycled in the photo oxidation process of essentials oils
