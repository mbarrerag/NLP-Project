# HOW I LEARNED TO STOP PREOCUPARME AND LOVE THE INTERNATIONALIZATION OF THE

The internationalization of the curriculum (IdC) is often identified with student mobility. However, as I discovered when I began to deal with internationalization at the School of Engineering, Science and Technology, mobility is only an ingredient among many others, and it is not necessarily the most interesting or important. In this article I will report my experience, as a foreigner and novice, of being in charge of internationalization of the School. Throughout this paper I will reflect on how my experience has been and how it changed my vision as I talked with colleagues and learned more and more about the subject. The article is divided into three parts. Initially I will comment on the planning and execution of the internationalization plans of the curriculum for the various programs of the School of Engineering, Science and Technology of the University of Rosario, these were my first steps in the field of internationalization. The second part narrates my experience when participating in a program organized by UC Davis and Fulbright Colombia, this was the key moment for me as I understood the broad and interesting IdC. Finally, I will conclude by reporting my research experience in international collaborations
