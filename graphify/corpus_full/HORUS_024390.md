# EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS OF HYDROMETEOROLOGIC VEHICLES INVOLVING THE OCURRENCE OF TORRENTIQUE ADVANCES: A APPROXIMATION USING AUTOMATIC TREATMENT TECHNIQUES

The increasing volume of environmental and hydrometeorological data in a free way and at different temporal and spatial scales, has allowed an approximation to phenomena like torrential avenues through the use of machine learning techniques. From the identification of patterns or structures in variables that influence the occurrence of these natural phenomena, it is possible to recognize both the factors with greater incidence and to establish critical detonating thresholds. Due to the morphometric and hydrometeorological conditions that configure the Northern Andes, it is possible to study these processes in this area. The importance of establishing detonating thresholds is based on the possibility of establishing early warning systems, considering the great destructive potential of torrential flows, since they can remobilize large quantities of material, which is deposited with high speeds and impact, representing a latent threat to the communities located in the middle and lower parts of the basin, Molitrate of the Movable, which for this study identified torrential events between the years 1922 and 2021. easy-to-access variables that can be implemented in any area of interest, where data is usually lacking in situ
