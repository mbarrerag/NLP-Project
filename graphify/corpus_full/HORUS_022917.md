# EFFECT OF THE UNPROPROPRIATE TREATMENT OF HAPPY PATIENTS HAPPYLY QUICKED WITH PIELONEFRITIS BY EMPI ́RIC HAND WITH CEFUROXIMA, A COHORT STUDIO

Urinary infections are a frequent cause of hospitalization, context in which there is an increase in resistance of Escherichia coli. Evaluating the effect of inappropriate antibiotic treatment (not sensitive in vitro) in adult patients with pyelonephritis hospitalized in a third-level center who received empirical cefuroxime treatment during hospital stay and re-entered by pyelonephritis. A survival analysis was performed in a retrospective cohort using a propensity score based on covariables that predicted non-sensitivity and possible elements of confusion. As for re-entry, logistic regression and proportional risk models of results were used in weight-adjusted pseudopopulation.
