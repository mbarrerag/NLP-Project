# WELFARE OF FISH ANIMALS: GUIDE TO IMPLEMENT WELFARE IN FISHES IN CULTURE FARMS

Research is a fundamental tool for knowledge generation, especially when it is oriented towards the development of practical solutions to the problems facing the population. However, it is not enough to carry out research; it is crucial that the knowledge obtained is disseminated in a clear, accessible and understandable way for those who need it. Following the Arauca-Acuícola project, funded by the SGR for the department of Arauca, deep concerns arose about animal welfare in fish, the importance for fish and for business, the tools to measure well-being in fish grown for human consumption. In workshops and talks with producers, it was evident that they begin to have a favorable perception of the importance of implementing the welfare of fish, because they recognize that they are beings they feel, that they are intelligent and capable of expressing emotions. Therefore, this document aims to promote the social appropriation of the knowledge that exists about well-being, facilitating the understanding of the concepts and ethical principles that govern animal welfare in fish grown for consumption. In addition, practical elements are presented that are essential to properly evaluate the welfare of these animals in farming environments
