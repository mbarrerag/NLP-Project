# Territorial expressions of mourning in the Lower Attrate. Why are emotions important to the territory?

In a society like Colombia, where we have learned to naturalize multiple forms of violence, the territories have also become wounded bodies that need to be healed. In this context, and considering the partial implementation of the Peace Agreement signed in 2016, it is crucial to reflect on the role of emotions in spatial and affective relations that are established with the territories. Recognizing collective and territorial pain is the first step to transform it. This research explored the importance of emotions in the relationship between territories and conflicts, addressing the Territorial Expressions of the Duel as a key to understanding the cultural and spatial transformations in four territories of the Bas Attrate: Carmen del Darién, Marriaga, Triganá and Santa Maria de la Antigua del Darien. Navigating, walking, rolling and flying through these territories were forms of approach to try to understand them. The resulting cartographic exercises weaving a spatial and territorial narrative narrative through music and memory, drawings and imagination, food and daily practices of its inhabitants, condating both its physical forms and its cultural expressions, obvious in their architectures and landscapes.
