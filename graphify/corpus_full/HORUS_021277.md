# Study on the use of rigid polyurethane foam (PUR) waste generated in Valle del Cauca

The rigid polyurethane foam or PUR is a material used in various industries, including the cooling industry. In this industry, PUR waste is generated in processes such as initial foam density tests, foam leaks, accidental spills or low or quality-rejected equipment. Therefore, the present research proposal seeks to study alternatives for the use of the residue of rigid polyurethane foam, through a thorough analysis of the literature, its characterization and laboratory-level processing of cement mortars added to this residue. PUR residue will be analyzed by the application of chemical, mineralogical and environmental techniques; additionally, the mortars obtained at the laboratory level will be evaluated from the point of view of mechanical behaviour and environmental testing. All of the above, in order to determine which alternative would represent a good option for the use of the residue study from the technical and environmental point of view, also considering the Colombian context
