# Identification of determinants in the administration of resources and personal finances of rural women. Case of municipality of Chámeza, Casanare.

This study seeks to identify the determinants of the administration of resources and personal finances of rural women in a municipality historically affected by the Colombian conflict. To this end, a socio-demographic characterization of the population of rural women in Chámeza, Casanare will be carried out through the analysis of official documentation and primary information collection in the study area, and then those particular factors that, according to literature, could affect the management and empowerment capacity of rural women, taking into account the post-conflict scenario, will be evaluated. Finally, a series of public policy recommendations will be suggested that will serve as input for the design of municipal development plans, which seek to reduce the gaps and caraterized factors
