The convoked to answer this question were historians of Colombia and Venezuela, due to our knowledge and experience in the matter. As the months progressed, the preparation of the works, and despite the declines of some guests, arose the idea of widening the discussion around the history of the history of the Great Colombia. The historiographical concern that initially motivated the structuring of this book has interwoven with new proposals that address key problems of the period.
