# The use of mobility variables as a tool in identifying beneficiaries of public transport subsidies. Case study: SISBEN incentive in Bogotá.

In the framework of this work, a methodology was formulated for the identification of potential beneficiaries of the SISBEN incentive to public transport in the city of Bogotá, using a scheme in which the data are related in the basis of the SISBEN and the variables associated with the mobility characteristics of the inhabitants of the city. For this purpose, a methodology was used that involved the integration of variables from the Mobility Survey and the Multipurpose Survey of Bogotá to the base of the SISBEN IV. Thus, a database was formed that contained the socio-economic and mobility variables of the population in the base of the SISBEN, and on it a model of Gausssian finite mixture was applied for the generation of population clusters. As part of the results it was found that the most vulnerable cluster is integrated by the population that has the greatest travel times, which carries out a greater proportion of trips in informal transport, which has greater times of access from their home to TransMilenio stations, as well as greater times of access to green areas, cultural centres and ATMs among others.
