# Cohort study of patients with malignant neoplasms treated for acute respiratory infection (ARI) at the National Cancer Institute (INC) during the pandemic by COVID-19 May 2020-August 2021.

Cohort study of patients with malignancies attended by acute respiratory infection (ARI) at the National Institute of Cancerology (INC) during the pandemic by COVID-19 May 2020-August 2021 Introduction: Patients with malignant neoplasms are a high risk population. A comprehensive characterization of patients with cancer and acute respiratory infection (ARI) during the pandemic by COVID-19 is described and risk factors for invasive mechanical ventilation and death in this population are analyzed. A study of cohorte ambispective, of 644 patients with ARI and cancer from May 2020 to August 2021. Follow-up was performed 30 days after hospital discharge. Clinical, paraclinical, imaging and history of cancer were compared between survivors and non-survivors, and between positive and negative for COVID-19.
