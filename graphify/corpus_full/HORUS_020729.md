# In Boga

The drama of a presidiary, Fabio, now in freedom, who seeks to give meaning to his life and find a future, walking the Magdalena River from his city, Honda, in the department of Tolima, to the mouth of Bocas de Ceniza. Fabio tries to reconstruct the memories of his father dragged by a growing river. In documents treasured in an old trunk, Fabio will find signs and traces of some dreams not fulfilled by his father that will drag him to embark on his adventure. The history of the trip will show shamelessly the life of the coastal inhabitants immersed in a culture of political and social violence that rashes with life to take over the land, taking away also the life of the river that now more than sustains, drags death and hides bodies. Partly it is also the story of the river that has sustained life for generations, but also represents the oblivion of the State, by the non-gaughter, the Fabo inf. ance, drags death and hides bodies.
