# URBAN INFRASTRUCTURE SELF-DETECTION AND SEGMENT IN DRONE IMAGES AND SATELLITE USING ARTIFIC INTELLIGENCE

In recent years, the use of Artificial Intelligence in civil engineering applications, and in particular the neural networks known as Deep Learning, have improved the results obtained with heuristic algorithms. Parallel to this, the availability of drone images, and the decrease in the cost of satellite images, have made it possible to use them in many fields such as agriculture, security, disaster management, urban planning, logistics and the environment. This work describes the classification, detection, segmentation and generation as applications of artificial intelligence in aerial images. The work presents a flow for the obtaining, transformation and production of different types of data that support such applications. The architectures of neural networks for classification, and segmentation of aerial images are displayed and contrasted. Open data sets of images that support these applications are referenced. It also details specific applications such as extraction of transit signals, vehicles, swimming pools, roofs of buildings and streets in RGB images, merged, and thermal images obtained with drone or satellite images. Finally, it allows for the process to be used in the production of a GIS
