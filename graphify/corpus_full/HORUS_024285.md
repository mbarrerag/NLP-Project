# SOCIAL DISTANCE, DURATION, WITHDRAWAL, REPEAT AND INTERACTION

Social distance is an environmental factor that induces prosocial behaviors such as cooperation, which is concrete as the ordination that an individual performs in a group of people with whom he interacts. In the organization the position of each member is relative to the position of the others. The social distance variable has been exogenous in social discount procedures, but has not deepened in its determinants. The time spent in an interaction induces the organization of social distance. To analyze this hypothesis, an interaction time assignment task was used to carry out an interaction with a group of people with which each participant has previously interacted. The results show, with determining coefficients greater than 90%, that the time reflected in duration, wait or repetition explain the behavior of social distance management
