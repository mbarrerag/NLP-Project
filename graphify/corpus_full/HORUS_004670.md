# MONOLITS OF SOIL PERFILE AS A RESOURCE FOR THE SOCIAL APPROPRIATION OF KNOWLEDGE

The monoliths allow to show the soil as a component of the natural environment from their functions in nature, and those linked to the anthropic activities. In tropical areas the soils have a fundamental role in all biogeochemical cycles of the earth as the cycle of carbon, oxygen, nitrogen and phosphorus; fundamental in the production of food, the capture and storage of carbon and the maintenance of biodiversity and the provision of ecosystem services.Despite their importance most people do not have knowledge about them and their genesis which hinders their conservation.In order to get to awaken interest in the knowledge of the soil as a resource, a multidisciplinary team of soil scientists from the National University of Colombia is proposed to develop a collection of monoliths of soils.This academic initiative is aimed at teaching, disseminating and raising awareness about the importance of the soil as a natural resource not renewable, allowing for an approach to health and ecosystem services that it can provide.The monoliths are a pedagogical tool that allows to directly observe the properties of the soil, morphology, genes, classification and its current and potential uses of the soil, the city, for the city, eight, the city, the city, the city of the city of the city of
