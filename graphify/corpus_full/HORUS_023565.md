# NON-LOCAL OPERATORS ON PALTIC NUMBERS

Book aimed at students of late semesters of the mathematics career, master's or doctoral students, who want to start their research in the field of pádic numbers or non-Archimymedian analysis. Our aim is to show certain topics of interest in this topic, which will allow the student to understand and assimilate the techniques used to solve different problems related to pseudo-differenceal equations, mainly when working with quadratic forms. This is divided into six chapters, in the first, basic concepts of pádic analysis necessary for the development of the subsequent chapters. The second chapter, is dedicated to the classification of quadratic forms on the body of p-adic numbers. In the following two chapters, using different techniques, we find the fundamental solutions to the pseudo-difference equations associated with different pseudo-differenceal non-local operators.
