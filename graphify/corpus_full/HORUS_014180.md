# "YOUNG CAMPO: EMPROPTION AND SUSTAINABILITY IN RURAL COMMUNITIES"

The rural sector faces a significant challenge marked by the ageing of the rural population and the massive migration of young people to urban areas in search of economic opportunities. This phenomenon not only threatens the viability and sustainability of rural communities, but also generates an alarming decrease in the available labour force and limits generational shift in traditional agricultural activities. Furthermore, the lack of job opportunities and adequate training in the countryside motivates further encouragement of permanence, to encourage the engagement of young people with their communities of origin. The lack of opportunities aggravates the gap between the countryside and the city, therefore the importance in the search for balanced development of rural areas. In view of this problem, there is an urgent need to implement comprehensive initiatives and programmes that address these key areas. It is essential to root young people in the countryside and ensure the continuity of agricultural activities to ensure the future of these communities. The project "CAMPO JOVEN: EMPORATION AND SUSTAINIBILITY in rural communities" is born as a response to this challenge. It proposes a holistic approach combining the training in psychosocial skills, leadership, entrepreneurship and agricultural management through the necessary support of their own productive projects
