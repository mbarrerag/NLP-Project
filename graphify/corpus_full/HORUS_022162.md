# Design of a 3D electrohydrodynamic printing system to improve the resolution of printed scaffolds for bone tissue.

The osteoporosis is a skeletal pathology characterized by the loss of bone mass which, depending on the patient's risk, requires from pharmaceutical treatments to surgical interventions. The drugs are responsible for compensating for this bone loss, but they do not prevent the occurrence of a fracture. In view of the eventuality of a fracture, surgery is used where different types of biomaterials are manufactured bone scaffolds to replace the lost and/ or affected tissue. One of the common techniques for the manufacture of these scaffolds is the printing by extrusion, due to its ease of implementation. However, this technique has important limitations, in terms of resolution, referring to the diameter of the fibres and porosity. These characteristics are fundamental to mimic the property of a healthy bone tissue, what is desired for the patient's healing process to be in the desired way.
