# FUNCTIONAL DATA ANALYSIS: AN INTRODUCTION

Statistical methods for Functional Data Analysis (FDA) have seen an explosion of advances over the last two decades due to technological development that provides data with a continuous nature (functions, curves, images, surfaces, etc). These methods have been applied in biology, medicine, science, business, and engineering. The objective of this course is to provide an accessible introduction to this field
