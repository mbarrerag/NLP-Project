# NEONATAL ENCEPHALOPATIA BY COVID-19 WITH CONVULSIVE STATUS

ABSTRACT We present the case of a 6-month-old patient who presented a clinical picture of emesis, generalized tonic-clonic seizures and two cardiorespiratory stops, requiring orotracheal intubation and inotropic support in the NICU. A positive PCR test was obtained, acute viral encephalitis was diagnosed and management was initiated with intravenous antiepileptic, sedoanalgesia, inotropic support, intravenous corticosteroid, human immunoglobulin, N-acetylcysteine and thromboprophylaxis. Due to the atypical establishment of the COVID-19 infection in this age group, we discussed the presentation spectrum of viral encephaloitis in pediatrics and its challenging management.
