# Impact assessment of payment for environmental services through an eco-efficiency index in livestock farms in the La Vieja, Quindío and Valle del Cauca river basin, Colombia.

The Silvopastoral Systems (SSP) are a useful tool for the ecological intensification of livestock. This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of livestock conversion through indicators of Ecosystem Services (SE) at various scales. SE indicators of carbon storage and biodiversity conservation were analyzed from 104 farms that participated in a project of payment for environmental services (PSA) SE. From these 104 farms a sample of 20 according to types identified by conglomerate analysis were selected, for which synthetic indices related to soil quality, landscape changes and a socioeconomic indicator were constructed and evaluated, through procedures based on multivariate statistics. The soil index evaluated in 2003 showed better quality in the SE, in the SSP. The landscape index showed a high correlation with the SEs of biodiversity and carbon. Finally, an approximation was made to the concept of ecoefficiency integrating the different indices and indicators above and comparing the typologies, finding better performance of the indicator in those farms with higher values of carbon and landscape
