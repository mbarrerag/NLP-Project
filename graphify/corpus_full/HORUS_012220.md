# Educational proposal based on the postulate of Vygotsky: the area of near development, for the prevention of injuries associated with care, aimed at health personnel of the National University Hospital of Colombia

To design an educational proposal based on Vygotsky theory: next development zone, aimed at health professionals, in order to prevent the emergence of LRCs in hospitalized patients, the emergence of LRCL. Final work in an internship modality during the second semester of 2022 at the National University Hospital of Colombia. Outcome: Content creation taking into account the selected themes with the prevention of LRCs. To develop audiovisual material taking into account the prevention of skin lesions associated with care to be delivered to the HUN in order to be included in the training courses. With the development of the internship, an educational proposal based on a theoretical assumption (Vygotsky theory: next development zone) was designed to prevent skin lesions associated with care in patients with skin injuries, as well as to educate health personnel in the proper identification and treatment of the LRCs. .
