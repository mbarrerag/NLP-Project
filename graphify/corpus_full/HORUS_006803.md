# Interprofessional Health Education Experience: Description and Implementation at the School of Medicine and Health Sciences of the Universidad del Rosario, Colombia

According to Hugh Barr's approach (2009), interprofessional education (EIP) occurs when two or more professions learn with, from and on the other to improve collaborative practice, quality of care and services provided. This book is born from the memories and reflections of the EIP learning community in health, highlighting the complex process of developing an initiative involving seven undergraduate programs in the same activity. Here, epistemological approaches, learning results, instructional designs and curriculum integration converge, together with the experiences and challenges faced in implementing a pedagogical strategy that, by the end of 2023, had already sensitized more than two thousand students and about fifty teachers of the School of Medicine and Health Sciences of the University of Rosario, in the challenge of reconsidering the professional identity and defragmenting the care of patients, their family and their community.-- Contracadas
