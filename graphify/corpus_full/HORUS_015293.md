# TUBERCLESIS INFECTION CONTROL MEASURES: A COLLECTIVE CHALLENGES FOR HEALTH INSTITUTIONS

Tuberculosis is an infectious disease that affects health workers to a large extent as a result of its occupational exposure and the limited implementation of the control measures of infection. In this regard, the World Health Organization (WHO) emphasizes that tuberculosis is among the top ten causes of death and constitutes one of the main causes of death by a single infectious agent.In 2019 it was estimated that 10 million people worldwide were infected with tuberculosis and reported 22 314 cases of this disease in health workers.This shows that the number of cases of tuberculosis per 100 000 workers in the area of health was more than twice as high as in the general adult population [1].In Colombia, during the year 2019, 14 886 cases of tuberculosis were reported, which resulted in an incidence rate of 27.3 cases for every 100 000 inhabitants. Of the total cases, 318 (2.1%) were workers in the area of health.The Administration of Occupational Safety and Health (OSHA), states that there is a greater probability of infection by Mycobacterium tuberculosis in the health workers, which shows a high degree of flexibility in the organization
