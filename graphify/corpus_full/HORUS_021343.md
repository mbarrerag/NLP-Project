# Cinemaclubism and public training in the current urban scene: is a critical film culture possible?

The project aims to analyze the cultural activity of the cinema clubs and their ability to influence the formation of critical audiences in the current urban scene. To this end, the cities of Bogotá and Manizales have been chosen, as they are two of the urban centers of the country in which cinema clubs have gained greater strength in relation to the formation of new audiences, new tastes and new ways of seeing and appreciating cinema in the city. Based on the absence of public policies aimed especially at cinema clubs as a cultural activity, we seek not only to enrich the limited bibliography that exists on this phenomenon, but also to contribute to the visibility, interpretation and appropriation of cinemaclubism, taking into account aspects such as the validity, trajectory, theme and accessibility of these spaces, in order to clarify its impact on the formation of critical audiences, as an alternative way, or film, of making city through cinematographic appreciation and criticism
