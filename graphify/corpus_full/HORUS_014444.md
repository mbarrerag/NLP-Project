# Taxonomic characterization based on morphometric analysis of a fossil canide of the Late Pleistocene of the Soatá Formation.

Is it possible to identify a canide and its feeding through the fragment of a jaw? In order to extrapolate identity and diet from a canid fossil of the Late Pleistocene of Boyacá, a set of photographs of 1018 jaws of 33 modern species of canides with geometric morphometry was studied. Through statistical exploration of the patterns of Landmarks, it was explored in search of taxonomic and dietary signal by analysis of multivariate normality, PCA, M’Box, MANOVA, ANOSIM and PERMANOVA on the taxonomic and dietary groups, implementing at the end of the analysis linear, quadratic and distance discrimination of Mahalanobis. The sample has a non-parametric distribution, its statistical parameters are significantly different between groups, and exhibits a structure in the morphospace related to diet and taxonomy. The application of the discriminating analyses showed a diagnostic efficiency of 96% for complete fossil specimens, but with a strong tendency to decrease in material more fragmentary. The discrimination for high type of sample was very rare
