# Method for automated detection of defects in the production of alphajor steel sheets by means of artificial vision and deep learning.

The detection of anomalies is of great importance in the production of steel plates to ensure that the products do not have defects. In recent years several deep learning methods have emerged for the detection of defects on steel surfaces limiting themselves mainly to flat steel surfaces. In addition, the detection of anomalies based on deep learning remains a difficult task if not enough training samples are available, which usually happens in real world scenarios. As for textured steel plates, such as alphajor sheets, the availability of abnormal samples is low, since the productions are optimized to minimize the appearance of defects. Therefore, the main objective of this work is to determine an appropriate method based on deep learning, for the detection of superficial anomalies in textured steel plates. Several models were trained, which were compared in terms of the ability to segmentate and precision of classification. On one side, a pre-trained neural network was adapted to artificial defects in the images coming from a different production line, of which only the analysis of the free anomalies was made
