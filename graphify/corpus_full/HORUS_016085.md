# ADJUVANT TREATMENT AFTER RADICAL SURGERY FOR CERVICAL CANCER WITH INTERMEDIATE RISK FACTORS: IS IT TIME FOR AN UPDATE?

Cervical cancer is the fourth most common cancer in women worldwide. The preferred treatment for early stage cervical cancer is radical hysterectomy with pelvic lymph node assessment, and adjuvant therapy is suggested according to histopathological risk factors. A landmark study was published in 1999 that established 'intermediate risk' criteria for relapse, showing a benefit in recurrence free rate in patients that received pelvic radiotherapy
