# Determination of the feasibility of detecting trading strategies in the Colombian foreign exchange market using the information in the order book.

The detection of effective, cost-effective and efficient business strategies requires the identification of predictive patterns in the information provided by the market. Normally the market information is presented as a collection of time series of prices (opening, closing, maximum and minimum) and volume that are available with a particular granularity of time. This depends on the information provider, p. each transaction, every minute, every day, etc. depending on level access. In this work we have access to an information tool that is commonly not available: The Limit Order Book Information for the Colombian bulk currency market. The order book data provides a valuable source of information in the financial markets. For this reason, it is an excellent candidate for building new tools and business models. The representation of the order book is a branch still active in quantitative finance. This work addresses the problem of displaying financial data from the exchange currency to bulk of Colombia using two approaches: a heat map representation and a Haar. Representation based on wavelet to filter the noise of high frequency. This work addresses the large number of data from the Colombian market
