# EXPERIENCES OF WOMEN WITH WOMEN ' S STERILIZATION

To describe the experiences of women in Bogotá-Colombia with female sterilization (before, during and after implementation). Qualitative descriptive research; the information was collected through semistructured interviews, recorded, transcribed and subjected to content analysis. Twelve women who had different experiences from their approach to female sterilization up to several years after its implementation participated. These experiences were grouped according to the moments explored: in the past, did women approach the method by the need to adopt a good contraceptive; for decision-making influenced both personal and contextual aspects. The interviewees agreed to the method without sufficient education to make a true informed decision. In the period of time, the experiences differ according to the time of the procedure (postpartum or interval); they identified shortcomings in the process of the management of informed consent. In the following, women perceived changes in their health, especially in the menstrual pattern, some left pregnant and others reported repentance. The experiences of users of female sterilization, showed the need to strengthen the post-advertertal and post-advisership process
