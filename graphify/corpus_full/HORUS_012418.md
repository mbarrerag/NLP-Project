# Environmental changes in water and sediments by aquaculture in floating cages in Lake Guamuz, Nariño, Colombia

Aquaculture provides animal protein production in a cost-effective and sustainable manner, however, its production generates changes at ecological and environmental level. For this study, changes in water quality and sediments related to the cultivation of rainbow trout (Onchorchinus mykiss) were determined in the RAMSAR wetland - Guamuz Lake, in high and low rainfall, and six zones were compared with three replicas and three depths. Physical chemical parameters and nutrients in water and sediments were measured, carrying out analysis of variances and determining significant differences. The physical and chemical parameters of water presented less favorable values in the cultivation companies, unlike the different control points, mainly in the upper part of the lake where there is greater influence of anthropic activities, which showed that not only the aqueumatic processes alter water quality, but also other anthropic activities, but also in the values are in acceptable ranges for aquaculture and for the ecology of the lake.
