# CONCRETE WITH RECICLATED AGGREGATES

The recycling of concrete waste can help to conserve natural aggregate sources and, at the same time, reduce construction costs due to lower amounts of materials and transport.Produce recycled aggregates (ACR) has proved to be commercially viable and technically suitable for nonstructural applications.Recycling of concretes necessary for effective use of construction resources and environmental management.This research confirmed that thick ACRs can successfully be used as a total substitute for natural thick aggregates in concretes for airports.The main benefit of using TSMA was the increase in the workingability of concretes when compared with the concretes prepared following the NMP, as well as a slight increase in resistance.The use of post-Zolanical substitutions can be a strategy to increase both the working and the resistance of the material
