# RESEARCH CREATION WAYS

Research - art creation has put on the table new debates, challenges and ways of approaching that instance, allowing a knowledge that is not entirely rational, and that seeks constantly to go beyond the separation imposed between affection and knowledge, thus expanding, the panorama towards synergies, networks and networks that give place and independence to creation within and from bodies and relations in their respective context, nourishing from other places the processes of knowledge. Thus, it is proposed a journey through the research processes - creation of the professors of the National University of Colombia of the Master's Degree in Visual and Plastic Arts: Victor Laignelet, Mauricio Bejarano, Mercedes Angola, David Lozano, Mario Opazo and Diego Aguilar. Establishing different ways of addressing the various aspects that the research-creation contains, and exemplifying from the processes of the teachers, ways of crossing: allegories, practices, metaphors and ways of teaching from and outside the academy
