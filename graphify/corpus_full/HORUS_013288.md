# Physical activity, self-care, quality of life, comprehensive rehabilitation and social inclusion of former members of the security forces

Under the qualitative and descriptive research of the kind hermeneutic and inclusiveness, carried out to the population with disabilities of the Directorate of the Center for Integral Rehabilitation (DCRI), currently, the Directorate of Veterans and Inclusive Rehabilitation (DIVRI) of the Ministry of National Defence of the Republic of Colombia, ex-combatants of the armed conflict with physical disabilities participated in interviews where their experiences were reflected in the realization of physical activity, sport and its influence on the quality of life, self-care, comprehensive rehabilitation and social inclusion. The experiences of the interviewees allow us to ask what have been the successes, barriers, advances and what, still, is missing to be achieved and built in relation to the rehabilitation and social inclusion of the victims of armed conflict of the public force.
