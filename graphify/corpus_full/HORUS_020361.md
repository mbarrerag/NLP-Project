# News Caracol, News RCN and the media construction of geological and hydrometeorological disasters and risks

This paper analyzes the telenotitious coverage of risks and disasters of geological and hydrometeorological character in the central broadcasts of News Caracol and News RCN, the news of the highest audience in the Colombian television of open signal. In this type of investigations has been very little progress in the Colombian context. It is then a approximation to a sample between March 2017 and February 2018, based on an analysis of content in combination with approaches to disaster risk management, circulation of knowledge, the sociology of risk and other fields and subfields. This shows two news articles that deal much with emergencies and suffering, but very little about risks and prevention, something more notorious in News RCN than in News Caracol. It is also a qualitative analysis to a sample on the torrential avenue of Mocoa (31-03-2017) and the earthquake of Mexico (19-09-2017), with elements of the contract of reading of Elisha Verón, of the circulation of knowledge, of the communication of risk and of the studies of the relationship between communication and culture, among other fields and sub-camps. ches to disaster risk management, circulation of knowledge, sociology of risk and other fields and subfields.
