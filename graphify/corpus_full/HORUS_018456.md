# OECD Panorama on the Role of Oral Health in National Strategies and Policies for the Management and Control of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus: A Systematic Review.

Objective: To describe the role of Oral Health in national strategies and health policies for the comprehensive management and control of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (DM2) in the 38 member states of the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD). A systematic exploratory review was carried out, following the PRISMA-ScR guideline. Government reports, clinical practice guides, official documents WHO and OECD, and articles identified in PubMED and LiLACS until December 2020 were included. It was summarized: 1) Inclusion of Oral Health in national plans or clinical guidelines of DM2, 2) Dental benefits and coverage for DM2 and 3) Health indicators associated with DM2 control. 84 documents were included. 1) Oral Health is incorporated into the national plans of 22 OECD countries for DM2 control. 2) Of these, 8 guarantee dental care with high coverage for DM2. 3) OECD countries with high dental coverage present the best metabolic control indicators of DM2.
