# Development of hybrid material from Strapajo fibres (Luffa cylindrica spp.) as a support for transition metals in the removal of emerging pollutants in water

Industrial activities have increased the presence of emerging pollutants in water, which generate human and environmental involvement. Alternatives have been studied to eradicate them through methods such as adsorption, which uses an adsorption material as lignocellulose surface material to remove heavy metals from water. In this research work, the Luffa cylindrica spp. was used for pre-treatment called organosolv and a chemical treatment of selective oxidation by the system NaBr/TEMPO/NaOCl for the conversion of the OH groups to COOH groups in glucose. Subsequent, Cu and Zn were deposited on the surface of the Luffa through the methods of impregnation in wet monometallic and bimetallic type, with the result of the removal of the material and the concentration of the Pbsor. This research work, Luffa cylindrica spp. was used, which was subject to an Organosolv pre-treatment and a chemical treatment of selective oxidation by the NaBr/TEMPO/NaOCl system for the conversion of OH groups to COOH groups in the C6 of glycose. Subsequently, Cu and Zn were deposited on the Luffa surface through monometallic and bimetrolic wet impregnation methods, obtaining 5 impregnated materials.
