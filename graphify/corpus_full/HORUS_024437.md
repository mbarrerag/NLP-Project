# GEOETICS: A QUESTION OF ALL

The geosciences (or Earth sciences) are a wide set of scientific disciplines, basic and applied, whose objectives, methods and instruments are used by geoscientificians only to investigate the Earth system to understand its composition, structure, forces, processes, dynamics, cycles, resources, evolution, in different scales and spatial and temporal intervals. Geo is not simply the Earth, but rather specifically the place where humans live and where future generations will live. So, geoethics means responsibility towards the Earth and future generations. In particular, it is proposed an analytical approach to reality, focusing on the need to identify values on which to base the growing interaction between humans and the Earth system, it is asked to geoscientific people who assume the ethical responsibility to use their knowledge for the benefit of society. Humans are able to modify the natural environments, and by virtue of this prerogative they have an ethical responsibility towards the planet. In fact, the study and use of Earth resources, exploiting their resources, and intervening in natural processes are actions that involve the great responsibilities towards society and the environment, of which they should be a geoscience. preparation to bring science closer to society
