# ATMOSFERICAL CONVECTION IN COLOMBIA: RECENT DEVELOPMENTS FROM MAESTRY IN NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY METEOROLOGY

Colombia is in a privileged place for the study of atmospheric convection, due to its equatorial location and the great diversity of generating processes operating there. Considering the importance of convective processes for both human activities and the study of the dynamics of the terrestrial climate system, from the Research Group in Atmospheric Sciences of the Master's Degree in Meteorology of the National University of Colombia an effort has been initiated to improve the understanding of convective processes in this region of the planet. As a starting point a method of identification and follow-up of convective systems was developed from infrared satellite images. This study allowed to obtain a detailed description of the temporal distribution of convective events in the region, in addition to identifying places and processes of particular interest. In this presentation, a summary of the results of this initial study, of the open questions that emerged, will be made available and an overview of the most recent advances in research that are being carried out to address some of these open questions.
