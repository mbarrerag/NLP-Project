# Validation of predictive models of analysis of oil analysis data used for the decision making of maintenance in a steam turbogenerator.

This paper develops descriptive, categorising and predictive models of data analytics, in order to generate a decision-making tool based on the observations captured from different tests made on the oil used from a Siemens-branded steam turbogenerator from a paper industry. A database is structured with information collected over a period of six years (81 records). It has measurements of different properties of the lubricant, so 4 main variables are selected for analysis. The selected variables are the Total Acid Number (TAN), the percentage of dissolved water in the oil, the concentration of phosphorus in the oil and viscosity at 40°c. The models of hierarchical clusterization, time series, approximation by mobile means and control letters are implemented. Finally, the conclusions derived from the implementation of these models are presented
