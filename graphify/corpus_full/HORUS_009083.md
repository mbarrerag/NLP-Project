# ONLINE FERTILITY FORM FOR TROPICAL CULTIVES BASED ON PHYSICAL-CHEMIC ANALYSIS OF SOUND

The fertilisation in tropical crops implies being aware of the biological and chemical dynamism of the soil, as well as the stages of plant development and its requirements. This article exposes the design and operation of an information system for the interpretation of physical and chemical analyses of the soil with interaction to various databases, with the goal of formulating the online fertilization plans. For this purpose, the cassava culture was used as a base, and its phenology, performance and nutrient requirements were specified in the soil. The support software interacts with various databases, based on the relationship: plant-floor-water. The algorithm, flow diagram and pseudocode supported in different programming languages: HTML, PHP and JavaScript, housed in an application server, with a MySQL database system. The system is divided into four modules: 1. soil analysis; 2. fertilization requirements; 3. crop database (in progress); and, 4. tools and reports. Functional and non-functional tests were performed, allowing adjustments in the accuracy of the results and improvement during development and has been confronted against the expected level of precision, and accuracy
