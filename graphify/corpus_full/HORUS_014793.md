# Selection of a machine learning technique for the detection of Digital Financial Fraud focused on unauthorized or consensual transactions.

The constant evolution of technology, coupled with the cultural change of people, has allowed companies to provide multiple services on digital platforms by encouraging immediacy and easy access to services via the Internet. In focus of this work, digital financial services in Colombia have been well accepted in the market, allowing, for example, payments through electronic transfers in neighbourhood stores; however, in parallel to the increase in the use of digital financial services, frauds in digital transactions have also evolved and increased every day. In this work different machine learning methods are evaluated, with the aim of finding an appropriate model for detecting fraud in digital transactions, based on the quality of prediction and execution times. In addition, it focuses on the analysis of a set of real banking transactions already occurring, in order to detect them and eventually avoid them in future transactions. Four models used widely in the literature for the classification problem were implemented and compared: Logistic Regression; Random Forest; support vector machine (svm); and Neural Network. Finally, it is evident that an appropriate model for the detection of frauds is the SVM. (Text taken from the SVM)
