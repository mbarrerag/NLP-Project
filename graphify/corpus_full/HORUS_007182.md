# Characterization and analysis of Black, Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenqueras communities in grassroots organizations and community councils in Caldas: From the perspective of artificial intelligence

The problem that the research addresses is the deficiency in characterization and data of the communities Negras, Afrocolombianas, Raizales and Palenqueras of the department of Caldas, problem that makes it impossible to direct strategies that are in line with the specific needs that promote the integral development of these communities. The objective of this work consisted in designing a comprehensive methodological proposal that would allow to characterize the communities Negras, Afrocolombianas, Raisales and Palenqueras of Base Organizations and the Community Councils of the department of Caldas. The design allowed to identify 12 dimensions to take into account in the integral characterization for communities Negras, Afrocolombianas, Raisales and Palenqueras.
