# Fragility and clinical characteristics in very elderly patients with acute heart failure.

Fragility and clinical characteristics in very elderly patients with acute heart failure Introduction: The main cause of hospitalization in the elderly is acute heart failure, with high morbidity and mortality, mainly when it coexists with fragility, whose prevalence is up to 70%. The objective of this study was to determine the prevalence of this geriatric syndrome in very elderly patients hospitalized for heart failure. A descriptive cross-sectional study. Patients > 80 years hospitalized with diagnosis of heart failure were included for a period of 7 months. Fragility was sifted with the clinical scale of fragility, considering the fragile patients with a CFS > 5. Other geriatric syndromes were evaluated and a description of the clinical and sociodemographic characteristics of the population was performed. 123 patients were analyzed, the average age was 85.31 years (DE 3.86). The prevalence of fragility was 51.2% (IC95% 42% - 60%) was observed. Only 22 % were independent in their basic activities, they were fulfilled with the definition of polypharmacy, 35 % had a high load of comorbidness and 16.3 % was satisfied with the definition of disease
