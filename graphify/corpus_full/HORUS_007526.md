# CHAPTER V: A MUSEUM FOR GRAPHIC DESIGN

David Consuegra (1939-2005) is recognized as one of the pioneers and most outstanding representatives of graphic design in Colombia. He trained as a master in Fine Arts and graphic designer at the Universities of Boston and Yale, respectively. After his return to the country, he devoted himself tirelessly to promoting and strengthening graphic design as a discipline through his professional exercise, the development of editorial projects and his link with academia. In this volume of the collection, five professors of the School of Graphic Design of the National University of Colombia stand out his thinking, projects and proposals, forged over the more than twenty years that he was linked as a teacher of this institution. In addition, they explore his production from some of the less studied areas of his work, such as typography, illustration, comics, photography and museography. The legacy of this teacher, built at the National University, remains a living reference. His graphic and academic work constitutes a valuable cultural heritage in the fields of design and arts
