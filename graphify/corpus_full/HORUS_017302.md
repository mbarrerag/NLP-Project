# END PHENOMENOLOGY

As its title indicates it the subject of this article is that of the currentity of the "Fenomenology of the spirit" of Hegel It is assumed frequently that this current exists only if the idea is abandoned that it is in the system where the philosophy of Hegel finds its definitive truth. And, however, what from here results is rather a present forged precisely in its opposition to the system, and therefore a fragmentary current that only rests on isolated approaches. Thus, the phenomenology turns out to be current if, for example, they mobilize approaches of their first chapters in epistemological debates as the critique of the representativeistic model of knowledge, or if they put into play their thesis on recognition and forgiveness in specific issues of the ethics and the contemporary social philosophy, to cite only two cases. A comprehensive look at the project of the work and to its relation with the system in general is thus excluded from the entry, because it assumes that the performance of this text lies precisely in its assismatic character.
