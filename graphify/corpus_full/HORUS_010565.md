# SUSTAINABLE MULTICAMPUS: TOWARDS ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY - BOGOTÁ

The environmental sustainability of the National University of Colombia is marked by the environmental certification of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the United Nations Program, mainly in the components of clean water and sanitation; affordable and clean energy; industry, innovation and infrastructure; cities and sustainable communities; responsible production and consumption; action for climate, underwater life (Sedes Pacifico and Caribbean); life of terrestrial ecosystems and alliances.In this context, the University defined its environmental policy that seeks to promote an environmentally sound environment for the development of its educational and training mission, to protect its natural environment, to propose sustainable alternatives to solve the environmental problems presented in its spaces, as well as to include in a transversal way the environmental dimension in the processes of teaching, research, extension and administrative operation under the foundations of continuous improvement, prevention of pollution and in compliance with the applicable environmental legal requirements. The essential lines that guide the structure of action of the University for the development of its environmental policy are: Environmental Training, Environmental Management and the Management of Environmental Resources.
