# BUILDINGS OF NEOLIBERAL URBANIS IN LATIN AMERICA, REASON AS PARTICULAR REFUNDS

Contemporary forms of urban renewal, especially in Medellín with their dense and compact city model, generate displacement and relocation practices aimed at building works that are necessary to consolidate a scenario of neoliberal competitiveness, destroying political meanings of the territories and their inhabitants with very little possibility of democratic participation of their inhabitants, in this sense, a deliberate, neoliberal and authoritarian extraction of the inhabitants of the territories without possibility of consultation and consultation and without prior resettlement that restores their cultural, economic and collective rights and conditions
