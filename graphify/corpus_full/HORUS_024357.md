# Varieties Luisa

This digital text is composed of 7 chapters. In each chapter i exploin different creative processes from the game of words and images that complement this language. This project is a summary of some artistic tours that allowed me to experiment and talk about one of the issues that interests me the most: what is already done. With this technique, in its unfinished quality and in option to the original work, I was able to apply objects, places, means and uses already pre-established in order to order a deterritorialization of the (image and language) work of art, relocating it( o) in new spaces (trivial and everyday). This project is a summary of artistic paths that allowed me to experience and speak more about one of the technical issues that is pre-empted and in fact the original design
