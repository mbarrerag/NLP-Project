# Development of pilot project for the integration of Green Roofs as Urban Drainage Systems in the Unal

This project seeks to develop a pilot system of green roofs as a sustainable solution to improve water management and reduce surface runoff. Through the design, construction and evaluation of green roofs adapted to the climatic conditions of Bogota, it is intended to demonstrate its viability and effectiveness as an integral part of the urban drainage system. Research will contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to clean water, sustainable cities and climate action. The project will be developed in five phases. Phase 0 will focus on collecting relevant information about plants, substrates and geotextiles suitable for green roofs, as well as of water quality parameters. Phase 1 will include the design of the pilot system, selecting the most suitable plants and substrates and planning the monitoring system.
