# Support and strengthening of research projects in NEUROCIENCES (NEuro-degenerative diseases in the field of LONG COVID and Schizophrenia)

The Neurosciences Group UNAL, co-organiser and linked from its beginnings to the Master's Degree in Neurosciences of the University, carries out within one of its lines of research studies on Neurogenetics, which has allowed the identification and studies of genetic risk factors for neurodegenerative diseases and neurodevelopmental disorders, among others, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, Attention and Hyperactivity Deficit, Schizophrenia, Autism. Undergraduate and postgraduate students have carried out their undergraduate and master's or doctoral thesis work around this line, several with important recognitions and continuity of their career as researchers, accessing national and international doctorates. In addition, the pandemic of the COVID-19 their developments, particularly the Covid Largo (Long-Covid) with their sequelae at neuropsychiatric level, including neurodegenerative and affective disorders, pose the need and possibility of studies within the Neurogenetics line for patients of our population
