# HEALTH PROMOTION AND HUMAN RIGHT TO HEALTHFUL FOOD: EVIDENCE COMPILATION

The Department of Human Nutrition of the National University of Colombia seeks to contribute, from its missionary processes (docencia, research and extension), to the solution of the food and nutritional problems present in Colombia; therefore, within the curriculum of studies of the career of Nutrition and Dietetics, an optional subject of deepening called Promotion of Health and Human Right to a Healthy Food, whose purpose is to study the promotion of health as a fundamental component of public health and its application in the field of food and nutrition. In this sense, it is intended to encourage research and the autonomous work of the student in the study of national strategies and public policies that are in process of achieving its implementation and that seek to promote the human right to a healthy diet. In addition, in the development of the subject, it seeks to strengthen his skills in the search of scientific evidence relevant to these topics, the interpretation and analysis of the same. To achieve the previous purpose, at the beginning of the semester, it is developed a work organized by groups of professors and students, in which questions are raised on the subjects. Subsequently, during the development of the subject, these questions are answered from a literature and from a methodology. The most important of these are the following: the use of ultraprocessed food products (UCPs), imposed on sugary drinks and healthy school food environments. All these topics are addressed in a clear and simple way to facilitate the understanding of different audiences. Because of this, all people are invited to read the different reviews, as they constitute an academic input for informed decision-making
