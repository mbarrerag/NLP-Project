# CATALITICAL TRANSFORMATION FROM XILOSA TO FURFURAL USING A MODIFIED CAOLINIT

The sustainability of our civilization, which depends on the supply of energy sources, food and chemicals without compromising the environmental stability of our planet, is now urgent, becoming a major scientific, technological and social challenge. One of the most attractive concepts in chemistry for achieving sustainability is green chemistry. (1) Furfural, as a typical chemical derived from biomass, can complement fossil resources to produce resins, lubricants, adhesives and plastics. It is also the raw material for the synthesis of many valuable chemicals, including furfuryl alcohol, tetrahydrofuryl alcohol, 2-methylfuran and 2-methyltetrahydrofuran (2).
