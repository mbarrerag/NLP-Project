# Application of Blockchain technology in a prototype for handling complaints from Fusagasugá Family Police Station

The Blockchain is a technology famous thanks to cryptocurre, for its benefits such as the validation, syn and immutability of the records. This provides confidence among interested parties. Although this technology has significant potential, its implementation in the public sector has experienced a notable delay and delay. It is normal for many municipalities to manage their processes with information systems. However, the integrity and confidence in information can be questioned, which can lead to an increase in costs, decrease the well-being of the community and deteriorate the image of the community before the local government. The scenario of interest is the Commission of Family of Fusagasugagá, where its information system presents fallacia in different parts of its activity. This provides the opportunity for the construction of a prototype for the registration and monitoring of complaints with Blockchain technology. The methodology applied is the deductive one, with a focus on qualitative research. It was sought to understand the functioning from the general in-use to the organisation up to the level of its processes. This provides an opportunity for the construction of a prototype for the registration and monitoring and monitoring of complaints with Blockchain technology.
