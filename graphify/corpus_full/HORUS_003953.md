# Evaluation of the conservatism bias in leaders responsible for investment decision-making in the biofuel sector in Colombia

The conservatism bias is understood as a mental process in individuals cling to their previous views or forecasts, reason why the incorporation of new information is done slowly or null, which may lead to the decision-makers losing perspective and/or generating erroneous estimates. This grade work sought to evaluate the presence of the conservatism bias in a group of leaders of the biofuel sector in Colombia, responsible for the decision-making of investments within their organizations; the participating group was 45 individuals who worked in positions from coordination to management and directions and have an average experience of 15 years. For the measurement of the conservatism bias, an instrument was built that incorporated the experiment carried out by Ward Edwards and the diagnostic test of Michael Pompan, a series of demographic variables for the characterization of individuals, the bulging test of Frederick and a series of questions associated with the conservatism bias, in order to evaluate possible relationships.
