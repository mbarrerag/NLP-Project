# DEFINITION, DIAGNOSTIC DELIRISUM IN INTENSIVE CARE UNITS: A WATER REVIEW

The delirium is a cognitive disorder typical of the critical disease, it is linked to the increase of mortality, greater hospital stay, greater requirements of ICU, greater functional deterioration and need of rehabilitation units and chronic care. Identify and evaluate the evidence of the last 5 years regarding definition and diagnosis of delirium in adults hospitalized in ICU through a review of systematic reviews and metaanalysis.MATERIALS AND METHODS:A review Umbrella guided according to IBBB guidelines. The data were obtained Science direct, Web of Science, Scielo, CINAHL, PubMed, Virtual Health Library (BVS) and Cochrane Library. The last search was carried out on March 28, 2022. Cath revisions met the inclusion criteria, the definition of delirium from the patient and family perspective, prevalence risk factors and precision of the different scales such as CAM-ICU, ICDSC, PREDELIC and E-PREDILICOLRC are necessary to achieve good predictive values
