# INFLUENCE OF THE REQUIREMENTS FOR VEGETAL PARTY MATERIAL ON THE BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY OF EXTRACTS ENTRYING FROM FRUITS OF PERUVIAN PHYSALIS L.

The objective was to evaluate etanolic extracts obtained from Physalis fruits peruviana categorized according to the requirements of the Colombian Technical Standard (NTC) 4580 of 1999 (1), in order to study the influence of the category and the states of maturity on physical-chemical characteristics and biological activity. The extracts were obtained by a process of solid-liquid extraction, from fruits classified according to the NTC. The extracts were carried out dry and evaluated with some physical-chemical properties, yield and activity of in vitro inhibition of aamylase (2), the latter related to its antidiabetic activity. The extracts of fruit category II with maturity level 4 and 5 presented the highest yield and inhibitory activity in vitro of aamylase at a concentration of 10240 μg/mL.
