# CRYTIC THINKING AND SOCIAL SCIENCES IN COLOMBIA BEFORE THEIR INSTITUTIONALIZATION. PROPOSALS, TRAINING AND REPRESSION AT THE TENTH OF 1940

The origin of Social Sciences in Colombia has traditionally been located in the 60s, taking as a nodal point its institutionalization and professionalization in the University, although some background is recognized in the 40s. This linear construction of the history of the Social Sciences shows an evolutionary perspective, where the recent is always superior to the old, and does not investigate the reasons for the ruptures, apparent voids or discontinuities.Under the general budget of the profoundly political character of the university and the social sciences, this paper seeks to characterize two emerging proposals of critical social thought in the 1940s: is the proposal of a US sociology also from the application of Marxism to the continental reality, which Diego Montaña Cuellar poses as professor of the National University. The Indigenist Institute of Colombia ICC (1942-1948), led by Antonio García and Gregorio Hernández de Alba, where the possibility of social development and research that sought to articulate scientific knowledge with political action is converged. Subsequently, the work analyzes what happens with these proposals (and its authors) in the process of restoration of conservation (1948-1953), to show the conditions of social change
