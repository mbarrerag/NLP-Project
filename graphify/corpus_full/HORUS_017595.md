# INITIATION IN THE STUDY OF CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF THE GANODERMA LUCIDUM HONGS AND HERICIUM ERINACEUS BIOTECHNOLOGICALLY OBTENTED

The macromyceto fungi are characterized by having an important number of biological activities within which they are found antimicrobian, hypocholesterolemic, immunostimulant, antioxidant, among others. Triterpenes, phenols, flavonoids, proteins and polysaccharides, are some of the components responsible for such activities and their production is determined by different factors, at the biotechnological level, such as aeration, agitation, the composition of the culture medium, the use of elictors and the way in which the products developed based on these fungi are presented. The research around the effect of the variation of this type of factors on the composition of the biotechnological product and of course its biological activity, has been the approach of study of different researchers at the global level.
