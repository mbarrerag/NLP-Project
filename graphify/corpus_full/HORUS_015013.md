# MARINE EXTINCTION SEVERITY WAS DECOUPLED FROM ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE DURING OCEANIC ANOXIC EVENT 2

A prevailing hypothesis for many significantextinctions of marine fauna and attendant ecological disruptions in Earth history is an associationwith widespread marine anoxia. In Mesozoicstrata, marine anoxic episodes are recorded as sequences o laminated organic-rich black shalesrepresenting short-lived disruptions of the carbon cycle known as Oceanic Anoxic Events (OAEs) We determined the global extent of anoxic watersfor an OAE at the Cenomanian'Turonian boundary. termed OAE2. Our results are consistent with a global ocean predominantly anoxic but not euxinic before, during, and after OAE2
