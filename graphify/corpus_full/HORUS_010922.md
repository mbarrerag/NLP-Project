# Diversity of fungi and bacteria present in tropical forest soils of the Cordillera Occidental-Valle del Cauca in response to an altitude gradient.

A bacterial taxonomic characterisation (16S) and fungal (ITS) was performed using metabarcoding on an altitudinal gradient in the western mountain range of Valle del Cauca, Colombia, which included ecosystems of Andean Forests, Tropical Dry and Páramo in the localities of Laguna de Sonso (LS) (900 msnm), Yotoco (Yt) (1800 msnm), Bosque del Duende (BD) (2400 msnm) and Paramo del Duende (PDU) (3200 msnm).The physiochemical analyses showed soils with high organic matter (>10%), without problems of compaction, extremely acidic pHs (4.4) for areas with higher altitude, light and strongly acidic in low areas (5,5-6,1). 59 families of plants were identified in the sampling zones, being the most abundant Aaceae, Lauraceae and Fabaceae.The taxonomic allocations were dominated by the phyla Acidobacteriota (33,1%) and Proteobacteria (23,9%), for fungi, were the most abundant Acommodity, and hemoteria (110%)
