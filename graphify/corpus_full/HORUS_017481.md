# Implementation of a method for measuring value at corporate risk in the construction sector.

This paper presents the implementation of a model for the measurement of value at corporate risk, based on the company's financial statements affected by macroeconomic variables such as Interbank Rate (ITB), Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Consumer Price Index (CPI), among others. This quantification is made from the estimation of the profits at risk (EAR). The model is applicable to both financial institutions and non-financial institutions, so with the implementation of the R-language model, it is intended to provide companies with tools for risk analysis and decision-making based on them
