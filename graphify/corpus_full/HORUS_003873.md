# Comparative analysis of sustainability in conventional and agroecological production systems in the Uval village of Usme-Bogotá

This research project aims to carry out a comparative analysis of sustainability in farms with conventional and agroecological productive systems in the Uval valley (Usme-Boátán location), which contributes to understanding and evaluating this type of socio-ecological systems in a holistic way. The comparative analysis was carried out on categorical data that establish the potential effect that 21 attributes for four dimensions, can have on the sustainability of the territory studied from the characterization of farms with conventional systems versus agroecological systems. The obtaining of data was made from interviews and a semi-structured survey, followed by the categorical assessment of the attributes based on the codification of the surveys. The comparative analysis part of the categoric values by attribute, using qualitative and quantitative methods. The results showed that farms with agroecological systems have attributes that confer greater sustainability, in particular for the variables exchange of knowledge, soil conservation and marketing of products. To strengthen the agroecological systems in the future it is necessary to promote greater participation, strengthen the production of seedlings and seeds, and increase the productive diversification.
