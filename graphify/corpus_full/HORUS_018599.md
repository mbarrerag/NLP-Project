# Police Code: the new speech on the criminal subject and its security policy.

This work is part of the critical analysis of the national code of police and coexistence (Law 1801/2016), its background, the impacts and results four (4) years after its implementation in the country. It is developed from a mixed approach that, at the first moment, makes a theoretical approach to deviation as a strategy to strengthen social control and security. Likewise, a historical context is proposed in front of the institution of police, its conception and its most relevant aspects within the socio-political context of the country. Finally, a discussion is opened around the subject labeled of the theory of deviation, in contrast to the results obtained from the imposition of fines in these four years (2016 – 2020) for behaviors contrary to coexistence
