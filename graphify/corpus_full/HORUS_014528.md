# Hidden treasures: first record of Lejeunea adpressa Nees (Marchantiophyta, Lejeuneaceae) in mangrove forests, Pacific Colombia

We report the presence of the liverwort Lejeunea adpressa Nees 1845 in mangrove forests along the Pacific coast of Colombia. The species was observed on mangrove roots, possibly fallen from the trunk or from bromeliads that were abundant on the mangrove trees. The liverwort is a widely distributed taxon in South America, from lowland to the Andean region. The present observation is the first record of the species associated to mangrove forests, and in Colombia it is an expansion of its known range of distribution
