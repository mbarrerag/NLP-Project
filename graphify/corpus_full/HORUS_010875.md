# Plant management during early periods (Preceramic and Formative) in the savannah of Bogotá, Colombia: Analysis of starches in lithic artifacts

This grade study presents the development and results of the analysis of microrestes (botanques), contained in surface and interior of lithic artifacts used in the milling and maceration of plant products, coming from four archaeological sites of the south and west of the Bogotá savanna, which correspond to the Early, Late (Arcaic) and Formative Preceramic period, and which cover a chronology approximately between 9,500 and 2,000 years AP. For the analysis of archaeological pre-sustaining, historical and contemporary plants, the technique of identification and analysis of ancient starch was mainly used, for which the elaboration of reference plates of current starches, of twelve species of plants producing tubers, where important amounts of starch are stored.
