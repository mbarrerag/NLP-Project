# Feasibility of draft laws on Universal Basic Income in Colombia.

Estimates of the maximum fiscal cost are made on the possible implementation of the draft laws on basic income in Colombia, based in the Congress of the Republic of Colombia, during the period of the pandemic by the COVID-19, as well as on the impact of each of these on the rates of monetary poverty and extreme monetary poverty. In addition, an actuarial model was adapted in which the Universal Basic Income is considered as a monthly life annuity paid, in order to estimate the fiscal cost of each project, brought to present value, considering the mortality of the population, in two cases, population over 20 years of age and vulnerable population over 20 years of age. Likewise, annual costs were estimated, modeling the Basic Income as an annuality at a later date than one year
