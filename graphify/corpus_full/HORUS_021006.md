# Audiovisual Tutorials in Teaching-Learning Processes in a Teaching Laboratory in Earth Sciences: A path to new teaching niches focused on the study

The Petrology Laboratory of the Faculty of Mines (LPFM) is a site of intense academic work in subjects that are compulsory within the curriculum structure of Geological Engineering, Petroleum and Mines and Petroleum Engineering. Annually, 400-600 students, to a limited place in resources (physical space, number of microscopes, stereoscopes and samples) where 4 to 6 pre- and post-graduate subjects are offered, including, among others, Optical Mining,,,,, Ígnea, Metamorphic and Sedimentary, Vulcanology and Pedology) are included.
