# LOCATION OF EQUIPMENT FOR THE REGISTRATION OF AUTHOMOTORS AND DRIVERS FROM AN ANALYSIS OF INTEGRAL ACCESSIBILITY: CASE OF BOGOTA, COLOMBIA

The main objective of this research is to identify sectors in the city of Bogotá (Colombia) to locate new equipment for the registration of vehicles and drivers, based on their geospatial relationship with places of training of drivers, transport companies, and motor vehicle sales premises. Studies of location and characterization of coverage of these equipments are marginalized with respect to other types of insertion. Based on the calculation of the conditions of integral average accessibility, possible insertion zones are selected and their impact is assessed jointly in terms of spatial coverage and gradient of average travel time savings. Geostatistical models are applied based on the automotive transport networks. Attractive sectors of Bogotá are identified as Engativá, Fontibón and Puente Aranda. It is concluded that analysis of accessibility conditions facilitates the selection of potential insertion sectors. Key words: accessibility; transport; equipments; Bogotá; gradient; savings
