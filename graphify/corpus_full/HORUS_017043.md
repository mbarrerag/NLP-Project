# ARTIFICIAL ILLUMINATION IN COLOMBIA AND THE PARTICIPATION OF COLOMBIA NATIONAL UNIVERSITY

In Colombia, at the beginning of the millennium, a legal technical merger is proposed for the implementation of the FTAs agreed with other countries, giving rise to mandatory regulations to comply with the agreed commitments. In 2004, the first technical regulation for electrical installations was adopted, Retie (Ministry of Mines and Energy, 2021), which entered into force in 2005; in 2009, the first regulation on lighting and public lighting was adopted, Retilap (2021a) which entered into force in 2010. The document presents the participation of the National University in the development of lighting in Colombia showing the evolution until 2020.
