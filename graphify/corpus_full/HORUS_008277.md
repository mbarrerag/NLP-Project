# Perceptions of health service providers on the sexual health of older adults in Colombia

Abstract Introduction: Sexuality and sexual health of older adults is an invisible dimension in the provision of health services. Because of this, it is important to know the perceptions and difficulties of health personnel to provide this care. To identify differences and similarities in the perception of health service providers in Colombia about sexuality and sexual health in older adults. Five focus groups were held in Bogotá, Barranquilla, Cali and Medellín, where 19 health professionals from an institution providing sexual and reproductive health services participated. Health professionals recognize sexuality as an important aspect in the provision of health services in older persons, despite the current invisibility of the topic in medical care. They recognize the complexity of talking about sexuality in the medical-patient interaction, in part because of the lack of training of health professionals and because of the patients' own inhibitions. Social and theoretical obstacles persist to address the topics of sexuality in consultation, as well as gender differences in the treatment of sexuality and sexual health issues.
