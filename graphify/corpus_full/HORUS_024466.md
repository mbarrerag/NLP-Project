# POLICY AND POETICAL: CHECO ITSHISPANICITY AND THE LATIN AMERICAN PRAGA SCHOOL

The essay focuses on some episodes of the history of the dissemination of ideas of the Prague School in Latin America. After proposing a brief historical contextualization, the author describes the role of the Czech historian Oldich B?li in this process, in particular the contacts of B?li with academic environments in Chile, Cuba and Colombia, and their publications in these countries. Also presented are the contributions that two of his most outstanding students from Carolina University, who left their native country after 1968: Jarmila Jandová and Emil Volek, made to the intellectual exchange. In two exemplary editions, published in Bogotá, Jandová and Volek translated and commented for the Spanish-speaking reader the works of Jan Muka-ovský and other representatives of Czech aesthetic thinking.
