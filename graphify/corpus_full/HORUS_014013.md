# Application of DMSI (Dry mix/steam injection) methodology in the manufacture of alkalineally activated cement pastes - CAA.

Currently, one of the greatest interests of the human being is the ability to inhabit the outer space for a longer period, with the interest of investigating and gradually managing to migrate to another planet, such as Mars, due to the scarcity of resources that are presented on the earth.
