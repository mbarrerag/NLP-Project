# Validation of the square wave anodic stripping voltammetry methodology for cadmium quantification in Theobroma cacao L. beans.

Cadmium has a high toxicity for humans by being bioaccumulated and causing damage to internal organs, such as kidney, lungs and bones.In January 2019 the new regulation of the European Union (EU) No 488/2014 on maximum levels of cadmium in different types of chocolate and cocoa powder was entered into force.The main objective is to regulate the high levels of food safety in these foodstuffs.Therefore, the quantification of cadmium in cocoa beans has become a necessity for producers.They must guarantee high and constant quality electro-medical products, which meet the maximum levels of Cd2+ which vary between 0.1 and 0.8 mg kg−1 for the different types of chocolate and 0.6 mg kg−1 for cocoa powder.Considering the above, this study presents the development and validation of a reliable electro-analytic method for the determination of Cd2+ in cocoa by means of the volumetrical volume of redilution.
