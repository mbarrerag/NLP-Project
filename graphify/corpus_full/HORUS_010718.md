# A city model? Planning and Urban Development in Bogotá, The Case of the Chicó

This research addresses the transformation process of Chicó (Bogotá, Colombia) to become a sector of high density and mixed uses, based on the urban policies that were adopted by the Administrative Department of Planning and the subsequent District Secretary of Planning. These policies, contributed to the progressive densification and change of use of the sector, granting consecutively higher heights and densities without defining clear reasons that justify such deregulation. These measures, modified the living conditions of its inhabitants without just making changes to the original urban layout, leading to the conversion process of El Chicó (Bogotá, Colombia) to become a high-density and mixed-use sector, based on the urban policies adopted by the sector
