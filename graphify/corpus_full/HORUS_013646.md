# Play and punish

The project Play on and Punish is the result of a process of creation of two years during the master's degree in visual arts of the National University of Colombia. The proposal both conceptual and formal of the project seeks to make visible the ideas of the game and the punishment as forming elements from the early childhood of the individual to the adult age. Play and Punish poses two problems or central axes, the duality between the game and the punishment, education and work, the plastic commitment of the project focuses on choosing two key spaces in this formative process: the house and the college. Two institutions where these concepts are exemplified. Through two facilities these two spaces are constructed, a classroom of garden or primary school classes and the interior of a house or home configured as a room or room with a specific temporality: a mid-century house art. This text attempts to collect much of the creation process, the problematics, the sketches, the questions and hypothesis, at the time it describes the stages of the creative process: the behind of a camera of a difficult way to travel: an interior trip to the psyche.
