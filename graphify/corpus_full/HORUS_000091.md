# Quantification of reactive silica and alumina in calcined kaolinitic clays via arithmetic generation of PONKCS pseudo-structures

The determination of reactive silica and alumina fractions in calcined kaolinitic clays is crucial for optimizing their use as supplementary cementitious materials (SCMs). Traditional methods assess overall reactivity but do not distinguish between silica and alumina contributions. This study introduces an arithmetic Partial Or No Known Crystal Structure (PONKCS)-based approach to quantify amorphous silica and alumina in metakaolin. The method was validated using solid-state nuclear magnetic resonance under magic-angle spinning (ssNMR, MAS), inductively coupled plasma–optical emission spectroscopy (ICP-OES), and thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), showing that the amorphous content can be decomposed into separate silica and alumina contributions.
