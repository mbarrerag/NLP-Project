# Clinical impact of the use of Filmarray® multiple PCR panel; for pneumonia in patients hospitalized in Intensive Care Unit suspected of pneumonia.

At present, pneumonia continues to represent one of the main causes of infectious etiology both of morbidity and mortality affecting all age groups but with the greatest impact on the extremes of life. People with pneumonia diagnosis that require hospitalization for this cause, may also need admission to the intensive care unit in 10-30% of cases and is in this case that potentially increases the risk of mortality that could be 26%. The diagnosis of pneumonia remains a predominantly clinical construct with radiological component and that in a percentage not greater than 60% of cases it is possible to identify the etiological agent despite using tools such as grams and cultures of respiratory secretion, hemocultive and bacterial antigens.Considering that the recommendations of the management guides vary significantly in particular, the antimicrobians for the empirical management and therefore, the most commercial capacity could be used in the event of a delaying of the infectious agent involved in the disease process.In this sense, the tools used in the case of the diagnostics of the molecular biology and mainly the action of the polymers (in the case of a delay of the patient) Multiple CR for Pneumonia Filmarray®, to correlate the results of the panel with the results of the cultures and to evaluate the changes in the therapeutics with the results report. A paired retrospective cohort of patients suspected of pneumonia will be established and who have required management in the intensive care unit with invasive mechanical ventilation in a group of hospitals in Bogotá. Exposure will be considered for those patients who have undergone the multiple PCR panel for Pneumonia Filmarray®. Pairing 1 to 1 with patients not exposed (who have not been performed multiple PCR panel for Pneumonia Filmarray®) taking into account the date of admission, diagnosis of COVID-19 and hospital of management. For the sample calculation, an impact potential for antibiotic desiculation was taken into account thanks to the realization of the 15.8% panel with OR of 2.31 which allowed to calculate a sample of 294 patients, 147 exposed and 147 not exposed. Expected results: To determine whether the realization of multiple PCR panel for Pneumonia Filmarray® with a severe pneumonia impact has some usefulness as the duration of the hospital outcome and the relevant antibiotic
