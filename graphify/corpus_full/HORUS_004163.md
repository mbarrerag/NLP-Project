# GENETIC CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES FOR INSPECTS BASED ON CRYSPR-CAS AND THEIR APPLICATION IN CHAGAS DISEASE CAP 09.

CRISPR-based genome editing systems have revolutionized different fields of science, demonstrating great value in the genetic manipulation of insects whether for the study of basic or applied biology. In a short period of time, these genetic editing systems have been used for the study of functional genomics of different insects, including flies, stilts, bees or butterflies. Additionally, CRISPR systems have evolved to apply in other fields such as the detection of nucleic acids, the mapping of genomic sequences in vivo, the overexpression or reduction of specific transcribed ones, the development of efficient gene drives and other applications of synthetic biology that continue to be optimized in different laboratories around the world. With this chapter of the book, we explore the advances of CRISPR-cas systems for the genetic editing of insects, and discuss their potential application for the study and control of triatominos vectors of Chagas disease
