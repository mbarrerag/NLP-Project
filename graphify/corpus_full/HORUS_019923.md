# CAREERS IN CARE HEALTH: FROM THE THEORY OF OREM SYSTEMS

Education and promotion of health for the elderly and their caregivers, within the framework of the pandemic by COVID-19; can be addressed from different theoretical approaches of nursing. For the phase of health promotion in this project, the focus of the theory of nursing systems will be considered; specifically the sub - theory of educational support systems proposed by Dorothea Elizabeth Orem, because it has as fundamental pillar the importance of empowering people of their self-care capacities to increase control over the maintenance of their health status. Considering that the elderly people have been identified by the World Health Organization (WHO), as the population with the greatest probability of contagion and death by COVID-19. This situation has motivated the implementation of strategies that allow to reduce the risks of contagion in this population and its caregivers. Promote the self-care in the elderly caregivers in the municipality of Ibagué; through the implementation of an educational program, for the prevention of contagion with COVID-19. Observational, longitudinal, prospective study. Carried in phases, in this chapter is presented the first phase of support of elderly people in the educational support and the implementation of the system of the Ibaguillo. -19. The promotion of the care of contamination by the COVID-19 for adults who are institutionalized has been effective, thanks to the fact that the caregivers learned to use the instrument of early detection of signs and symptoms, previous workshop of evaluation of vital signs.
