# --BLINDEJE EN FACHADAS: APPLICATION OF THE SUPERSPONSE CAPS CONCEPT BY THE 3D BUILDING FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF RESISTENT GARITS IN THE BELLIC ATTACKS WITH CALIBER 9MM AND 5.56MM

The purpose of this application is to apply to the financial call proposed by the Faculty of Arts of the National University of Colombia for support and contribution to research projects. In this case the support would be directed to the research thesis with the title: BLINDAGE IN FACHADAS: APPLICATION OF THE CONCEPT OF SUPERPUEST CAPS BY THE 3D PRESSURE FOR THE BUILDING OF RESISTENT BUILDINGS IN BELLIC ATTACKS WITH CALIBER 9MM AND 5.56MM_x0094_, a research project that seeks to propose a system of shielding in building facades by applying the concept of superimposed layers and the use of 3D printing technologies. The objective is to evaluate, through simulations by finite elements, the ability of different combinations of materials to resist impact of 9 mm calibre project and 5.56 mm, with a view to establishing constructive parameters that can be replicated in risk environments.
