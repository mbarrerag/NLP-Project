# Study and revitalization of the Gallery (Market Square) of the Municipality of Pradera Valle del Cauca, for the strengthening of identity, appropriation and construction of social fabric in the territory.

In the heart of the municipality of Pradera Valle, an area marked by its history through the experiences of the armed conflict and prioritized by development programs such as PDET and ZOMAC, is located the Market Square henceforth, (City Gallery of Pradera), which represents much more than a simple place of commercial transactions for its inhabitants.This gallery, located in the central area of the municipality, about 20 years ago was an economic, social and cultural epicenter where various social actors such as farmers, traders and whole families, mostly women, whose lives have been intertwined with this space throughout generations.The gallery is a symbol of the identity and peasant root of the municipality, since in general terms, the producers and peasants of the rural sector market food with a weekly frequency (Clavijo, et al., 2023). In a territory that geographically encompasses 70% rural and 30% urban, the municipal gallery plays a fundamental role in the economic dynamics. It is a collection center for the products that grow in the rural area, allowing the popular economy to prosper and ensuring the supply of food for the entire population. However, it is a collection of the economic sector. the historical memory of the gallery and the peasant identity of the pradera, recognizing the challenges and experiences of the past, especially in a context where the social fabric is still fractured by the experiences of the armed conflict and where memory deepens the links of the network in view of a possible future
