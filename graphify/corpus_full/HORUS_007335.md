# Technological development based on a solar dehydration system for the generation of value in food produced by a vulnerable community in Santander

The Association of Apcultores of the Serranía de los Yarigíes (APISY) is a community located in the village of Las Clavellinas, in the municipality of Galán, Santander, whose subsistence is based on the commercialization of the fruit and vegetable products produced in their premises.However, they face problems arising from the high perecibility of their products due to their high moisture content which, together with the difficulty of fresh transport to the nearest urban centers, and the lack of accessibility to conservation mechanisms, causes a significant volume of losses that affects their economy and consequently their well-being.With the aim of addressing this problem, this work developed a system of passive solar dehydration as an alternative to conservation and value-generating to the vegetable foods produced by that community.
