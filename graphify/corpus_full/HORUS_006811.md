# CAMPISIN MARKETS AS A STRATEGY OF SUSTAINABILITY IN FOOD SYSTEMS OF CUNDINAMARCA, COLOMBIA

Contextualization: the sustainability of food systems has acquired great relevance in academic and political areas, highlighting the emergence of proposals that question the distribution of food on a global scale. In Colombia, the experience of the National Network of Family Agriculture-RENAF and its campaign I carry the Colombian countryside, which integrates peasant, ethnic and agroecological markets in several departments of the country is highlighted. Knowledge vacuum: lack of analysis of the potential of short marketing circuits in relation to sustainability, based on contextualized analysis frameworks from the perspective of the actors that make them. three surveys with mixed questions were used for the collection of information; also, a framework of sustainability analysis was built with twenty-five indicators, grouped in five attributes: self-management, stability, territorial dinamization and environmental care. The analysis of the indicators was analyzed, followed by a framework of sustainability analysis of the Cundinamarca Node, and their contribution to the sustainability of the food systems
