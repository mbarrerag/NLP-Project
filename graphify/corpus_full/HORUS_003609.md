# EFFICIENCY ESTIMATION OF FULLY DEPLETED NANO-WIRE RADIAL HETERO-JUNCTION SOLAR CELLS

Modeling semiconductor and opto-electronic devices is an area of research that has made great contributions to further the development of modern devices. A particular example is the study of nano-wire solar cells in the radial configuration in which the hetero-junction is formed by a core (n or p semiconductor) and a shell (p or n semiconductor), and for which theoretical models are scarce. Hence, in this work we focus on developing a theoretical model that allows the performance estimation of this type of solar cells. In addition, two examples of nano-wire hetero-junction solar cells (Si/InP and InP/GaAs) are discussed, demonstrating that efficiencies higher than 19% can be achieved, when their dimensions are optimized. Eventually, when the carrier recombination lifetimesare increased, this type of devices can become competitive with existing technologies
