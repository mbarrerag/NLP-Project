# Planning, environmental determinants and Territorial Planning Councils. Possible harmonies?

In Colombia, the processes of territorial and environmental planning and, with them, the processes of citizen participation have been structured as independent systems. This disconnection has an effect on the processes of environmental territorial governance by the disharmonization between the definition of environmental determinants and their incorporation into municipal territorial planning. With the implementation of the Environmental Territorial Governance Model-GOTA in the Jurisdiction of the Regional Autonomous Corporation for the Antioquia-Corantioquia Centre, it is possible to interpret that the Territorial Councils of Planning-CTP, by their capacity in the recognition of environmental problems and determinants and their role in the coordination of local planning, can become that link that weaves bridges between environmental and territorial planning, within the framework of the ordination powers of the Regional Autonomous Corporations-CAR and the municipalities. Hence, this instance of participation establishes a strategic node to consolidate multilevel governance. The theoretical approach of this article is framed in territorial studies based on a look at environmental territorial governance. At the methodological level, social mapping was used to analyze the knowledge of local actors on municipal environmental heritage
