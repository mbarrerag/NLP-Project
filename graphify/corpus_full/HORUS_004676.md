# Design and evaluation of a computer model based on deep learning for the prediction of road traffic in a segment of the road of the city of Bogotá

The congestion of traffic in Bogotá is becoming more and more serious, and is not simply a perception of the residents, since the city has maintained a prominent position in the congestion rankings worldwide. This gives the urgency to address and contribute in some way to this significant challenge. This study focuses on various aspects, from obtaining and analyzing data of average speed and precipitation in a road segment of Bogotá, to the development of a computational model aimed at predicting average speed. This approach aims to offer an initial and valuable perspective towards the solution of the traffic problem in the city. In the framework of this study, the design of an LSTM model using real high quality data from a road segment in Bogotá.
