# COLOMBIA: DANGERS AND PANDEMIA

In recent years, various explosive materials have been accumulating in Colombia, the effects of which have been expressed in recent months in the deterioration of democracy, violence and social exclusion. It is clear that this drift cannot be attributed solely or mainly to the pandemic. However, this fact may have had an impact; for example, by reinforcing the authoritarian tendencies of the current government. But the Covid crisis has also affected in a slightly more indirect and less visible way. I intend here to explore some of the mechanisms through which the specificities of the pandemic period contributed to the deterioration of democracy, the proliferation of violence, and an escalation of the dynamics of exclusion in the country
