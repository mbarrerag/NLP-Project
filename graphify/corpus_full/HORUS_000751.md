# Editorial

DYNA wants to contribute to the necessary reflection on the dramatic change that is taking place in the training and professional practice of engineers. In this issue we work on the role of mathematics in engineering. In the future, issues of the journal will be devoted to the other basic sciences. A fundamental reason for these reflections is that we will only achieve competitiveness in this global society of knowledge as we develop a better foundation, that is to say to the extent that we build on broader and more solid bases, that they can give us greater perspective. The message is that if we want to be competitive, if we want modern, productive, creative engineering we have to strengthen the mathematical training of our engineers
