# Issulation and Mollecular discrimination of microorganisms that induce the pre-meditation of carbonate from a colombia cacao region

Recently, the use of ureolithic bacteria has been proposed by the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms (MICP) as a measure to remedy soils contaminated with cadmium(II). The objective of this work was to recover and characterize micro-organisms MICP resistant to Cd(II) present in soils of Carmen del Chucurí, Santander. i)soil sampling in el carmen de chucurí, santander. ii) Insulation of micro-organisms MICP and resistant to cadmium(II). iii) Insulation of Gram. iv) Test ureasa qualitative (TSB with urea/red phenol as an indicator of pH change. v)Molecular discrimination by SSCP. vi) Analysis of crystals by DRX and stereoscopia. From soil samples of four different farms of the municipality of El Carmen del Chucurí, Santander, 42 micro-red micro-organisms were recovered from cadmium(II) and able to perform the MICP process.
