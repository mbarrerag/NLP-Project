# Changes and Continuities in Labor Relations in Times of Regression The Cases of Brazil and Colombia

Abstract This article analyzes the changes in the systems of labor relations in Colombia and Brazil, which, due to their labor structures, inheritance of slave systems and profound regional differences, allow us to analyze the contradictions capital-work in the context of central and central-left governments in the 21st century. For Brazil, after more than a decade of improvements, the shift to the right has generated serious setbacks in individual and collective labour rights. In the case of Colombia, although with greater continuity in neoliberal policies, the shift to the center gave a democratic space for conflict resolution, which closed with the election of an extreme right-wing government. In a context of deepening labour conflicts, the reinvention of trade unions is key to ensuring that there are no further losses in labour rights and to pressure for a new cycle of changes in the face of the possibility of a new political shift to the left on the horizon
