# Evaluation of corrosion resistance of TiB2 coatings deposited on Ti and Ti-6Al-4V obtained by the coaxial cladding laser technique.

Titanium is a highly used material in industry, a condition linked to its good properties, without any doubt this offers important advantages with respect to materials such as steel; thus the industries of greatest demand are aeronautics, medicine, armament industry and even in the sports field. In this research they synthesize metal-ceramic coatings (60% wt of Ti+ 40% wt of TiB2) by the technique of coaxial cladding laser on substrates of Ti grade 2 and Ti-6Al-4V at different levels of energy (600, 900 and 1000 W) and evaluate their electrochemical behavior in a solution of NaCl at 3.5% and at high temperature. The material is characterized by electron microscopy of sweeping, the electrochemical evaluation is done by means of electrochemical spectroscopy of impedance and tafel polarization, its structure is evaluated by diffraction of X rays. The corrosion at high temperature is assessed by means of the technique of cyclic oxidation.
