# Impact on the visibility and sales of the micro and small businesses in Manizales that use digital influencers as a strategy.

In a present where the use of social networks is growing more and more and the micro, small, medium and large companies are turning to use this channel to make their products known, there are multiple voices of experts and supposed connoisseurs who flood the web and the same social networks. This situation forces to listen first hand to those who have used these networks to boost their businesses, from what emerges the main objective of this document, which is to analyze the impact on visibility and sales that have 20 micro and small companies from various economic sectors of Manizales that implement strategies based on digital influencers. The results show that in fact the entrepreneurs claim that there is an increase in visualization and sales when using influencers, but in turn they have reservations. The respondents have clear that there must be some recruitment criteria that reflect the interests of the brand, but for some the growth when using digital influencers is only linked to the visualization and not to sales, which is linked to the lack of measurement that companies implement this strategy, because the majority uses perception as an indicator
