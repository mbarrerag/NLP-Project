# Study of breast tissue samples embedded in paraffin using fluorescence spectroscopy to support diagnosis in histopathology.

In this study, a study of embedded breast tissue in paraffin was carried out using the technique of Fluorescence Spectroscopic. An alliance was made with the Caldense Institute of Pathology, who provided the samples for the study. 56 breast tissue samples were measured, of which 495 fluorescence spectra were obtained and processed by an algorithm developed in the research group through Matlab, which normalized the data and generated the most relevant parameters of each spectrum, such as amplitude, area, FWHM, among others, in order to implement statistical models for the analysis of the data, within which the Analysis of Main Components (PCA), Conglomerates, K-medias and Discriminant Lineal Analysis (LDA), in order to identify the most representative variables that allow for the classification of tissue with normal or pathology. The fluorescence spectras had a marked variation between the two samples of each patient, the fluorophores present in the tissue, the Collagene, the Elastine, DH and Flavinas with high intensil and the patient. The results show that the study of breast tissue by fluorescence spectroscopy is a diagnostic support for the pathologist
