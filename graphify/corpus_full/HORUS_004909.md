# GETTING THE DEBT OUT OF THE HOUSE AND THE BODY

Analysis of context, reflections, findings and conclusions of a feminist study on the processes of indebtedness in households and its effects on women's autonomy, carried out between the summer of 2023 in Cali and the winter of 2024 in Barcelona. Two cities with different histories and characteristics that become a strategic place to take a feminist and intersectional look at debt
