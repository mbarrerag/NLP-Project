# Assessment of solutions based on energy storage systems to improve the quality of electrical energy service in Puerto Carreño, Vichada

This document presents an evaluation of solutions based on energy storage systems to improve the quality of electrical power service in Puerto Carreño, Vichada. The location and capacity of energy storage systems (BESS) or hybrid energy storage systems of batteries and supercapacitors (HESS) were determined. The solutions were developed in Python language, with the mixed whole linear programming model (MILP) implemented through the Pyomo library. The results of the implemented methodology minimized the investment costs of the proposed energy storage systems. .
