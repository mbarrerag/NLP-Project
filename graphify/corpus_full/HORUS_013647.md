# Pre-feasibility methodology of construction projects based on design thinking structuring a business model with emphasis on real estate asset management, applying a phase analysis methodology based on design thinking. Case study university residences May 10 at the Centro Urbano Antonio Nariño in Bogotá

This document shows the application and adjustment of the methodology of pre-feasibility of projects for the formulation of a business model with real estate emphasis based on Design Thinking, which was developed in the consultancy carried out by the National University of Colombia for CASUR in 2019. This methodology is applied to develop the pre-feasibility of a construction project, and to formulate a business model that allows the profitability of a heritage building that requires intervention, in order to validate the hypothesis that the methodology mentioned can be replicable in other similar buildings, to make profitable institutional assets. The methodology stands out for a logical and intuitive elaboration of each of the components of the pre-feasibility, which allows the development of several models and feasible scenarios that are evaluated with different criteria. It differs from the traditional pre-feasibilitys when it is based on the profitable management of institutional assets that cannot be sold, which is common and evident in the patrimonial buildings, whose maintenance and updating costs threaten its conservation.
