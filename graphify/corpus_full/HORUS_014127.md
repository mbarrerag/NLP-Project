# --Assessment of thermodynamic properties of pitaya shell surfaces as a potential raw material for the development of dietary supplements

This research proposal is focused on carrying out a thermodynamic characterization of the surfaces of vegetable layers rich in dietary fibers obtained from pitaya shells. To this end, the project focuses on several stages corresponding to (i) conditioning of the plant material, (ii) obtaining the pulpulent plant material, (iii) determining the granulometric properties of the pulpulent plant material and (iv) thermodynamic characterization of the surfaces of the tablets of the pitaya shell extracts. To this end, the project initially includes methods of cleaning, peeling and cryoconservation of the pitaya shell, and then proceeding with milling, extraction and quantification of the vegetable extract fibers. Once obtained the vegetable extract, this will be dried by convection and refractive window, to be later stamped by meshes of different openings. Subsequently, the obtained pulpulent material will be characterized granulometrically, to then design several prototypes of tablets (tabletas), where different superfibre thermodynamic models, such as de Young, Otrol and Foil are studied
