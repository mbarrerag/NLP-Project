# RELATIONS BETWEEN THE STATE AND THE ORGANIZATION OF TAX CONTROL

This article analyses from a historical perspective the evolution of fiscal control in the current Republic of Colombia. The investigation begins with the way fiscal control was exercised during the 18th century, highlighting that during the period of colonial domination control was exercised to guarantee the escape of the economic surplus through the fiscal route. In a second phase this control has a democratic component because it functions in a democratic regime. The modernization of this system originates in the first two decades of the 20th century and emphasizes the role played by the Kemmerer Mission. The administrative reform of 1960 articulates fiscal control with the discourse of development
