# Validation and protection of potential value intellectual property as a transfer mechanism to Spinoff Poporo Technologies project

Since 2014, the GFNUN has included market approximations and validations from early stages of research with the aim of identifying product acceptance/discarding, demand expectations, technical scope and iterations. The GFNUN has also been supported by DEB in the development of various activities, methodologies and events, among them: development of the Canvas methodology (2014), validations in Rueda Tecnova (2017), I-III Deminated Encounter (2015-2017), Technology Transfer Workshop _x0093_Train The Trainer_x0094_ Lund University (2016-2017) pre-validation in Expoconstruction (2019), training on Spinoff. This proposal seeks to continue the validation and protection of the intellectual property of the Gamma Ray Retrodispersion technology in metallic and polymeric materials
