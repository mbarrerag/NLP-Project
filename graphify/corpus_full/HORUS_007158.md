# Influence of atmospheric wind dynamics on PM10 and PM2.5 concentrations in the open-pit coal mining area of the department of Cesar

The coal mining area in the open sky of the department of Cesar, Colombia, presents a significant impact of the air quality of its populated centers due to the particulate material PM10 and PM2.5 emitted by the coal mines that currently operate there. In this research work the planetary boundary layer schemes YSU (Yonsei University scheme) and MYNN2 (Mellor-Yamada-Nakanishi-Niino 2.5) of the WRF model were validated and determined as the wind dynamics influences the concentrations of the particulate material that is monitored in the mining area. In the period of study patterns of winds dynamics at local scale were identified and the influence of regional and local winds on these routes was estimated, as well as the behavior of the winds at different heights, taking as a reference the planetary boundary layer; finally, the patterns of winds at local scale were identified in the period of study that could be implemented to forecast episodes of high concentrations of particulate material in the area. nd dynamics influence the concentration of particulate matter monitored in the mining area. Circulation routes of PM10 and PM2.5 particle matter were found in a neutral ENSO periodic and the influence of regional and local winds on these routes was estimated. In addition, the better of the windows at different levels was analyzed, taking as reference the planetary boundary layer; Finally, during the study period, local-scale wind dynamics were identified that could be implemented to forecast episodes of high concentration of particulate matter in the area.
