# From the Tulpa to the Milpa: reconciliation booklet

By 2020, during the pandemic, a strategy was being developed to pass on the ancestral knowledge of the villages and at the same time to highlight the environmental and cultural richness of rural communities. To this end, a booklet was created that, although designed for children, allows parents to interact with the material, since this information, in addition to information on the richness of fauna and flora, also contains important elements on the culture of the area, thus making the information reflected in it reach the whole area
