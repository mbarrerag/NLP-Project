# To Telthe Sea: Communication of Scientific Knowledge about Coastal and Island Communities from the Performing Arts

The public Policy of social appropriation of knowledge in the framework of the CTeI of Colombia (2021) invites you to advance initiatives of artistic creation that combine the recognition of the cultural diversity of communities and territories, the dialogue of knowledge between the different social actors and the visibility of the culture as a cross-cutting element in the scientific research. This article proposes a series of reflections on the challenges involved in creation in the terms highlighted by this policy, and it does so based on the analysis of the play The Other sea, a theater, music and dance production that deals with the problem of the decline of patrimonial knowledge and the increase of risk between peasant communities that inhabit two coastal and insular points of Colombia (Buenaventura and the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina). The analysis uses the conceptual perspective of science communication from the performing arts and is based on the methodology of systematisation of experiences with a critical approach proposed by Oscar Jara. The article concludes that the line proposed by this policy for artistic creation is viable, but it implies organizational and aesthetic decision-making, which include the integration of interdisciplinary and intercultural teams, the questioning of the budgets of the creative team of the cultural contexts treated and the use of innovative scenic resources that enhance elements of familiarity of potential audiences
