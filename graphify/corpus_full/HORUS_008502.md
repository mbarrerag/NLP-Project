# GENDER RESEARCH IN ACCESS, PERMANENCE AND STUDENT GRADUATION: CASE STUDY IN COLOMBIA

A study is presented that links Tinto's desertion theory (1975; 2017) with the methods for studying segregation of Fosset groups (2017) and an approach for minority analysis proposed by Kanter in 1977. The international and national context is presented, and a case study is then carried out in a higher public education institution in Colombia. A descriptive and explanatory analysis is made using secondary information sources, survival curves are constructed and a statistical classification model is estimated with dichotomous dependent variable. It was found that the persistence of women, even in careers with male bias is high and that gender barriers are not eliminated by increasing women's access to and participation in higher education
