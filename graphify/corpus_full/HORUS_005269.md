# CONDITIONS

Taking into account the potential of geopolymeric cements as an alternative to partially replace Portland cement, this article presents the analysis of the effect of the curing conditions of geopolymeric cements on their mechanical performance. The variables evaluated during the curing process were time, humidity and temperature. In addition, the behavior of these variables is described according to the curing methods that have been studied and evaluated so far in the geopolymeric cements with fly ash. To carry out the bibliographic review was used Scopus as the main search tool, starting from the key word geopolymeric cements. More than 90 relevant documents were identified after applying specific filters and reviewing their summaries. Finally, a detailed reading and analysis of the bibliographic data, which provided relevant information for the study topic.
