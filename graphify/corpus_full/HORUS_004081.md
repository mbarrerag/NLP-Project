# ASSESSMENT OF SUSCEPTIBILITY TO DISLIZED IN REGIONS WITH DISABLED DATA USED IN REMOTE SENSORS

Mass movements caused by rain are one of the most frequent causes of natural disasters on mountainous terrain. However, assessments of susceptibility of mass movements are often limited due to the lack of reliable observations. Due to this lack of data, especially in developing countries, remote sensors can be used for analysis of the susceptibility of mass movements. This study presents the application of a logistic regression model with remote sensor data to assess the susceptibility to mass movements in a basin in a territory far from the northern Colombian Andes; where on 18 May 2015 a storm triggered more than 40 mass movements and a debris flow associated with it subsequently. The methodology applied is based on tools of remote remote sensors of free access, as the study area is considered a data scarcity zone. The results show that free tools of remote sensors provide sufficient information to execute a model as a logistic regression and achieve a first successful approach to the map of susceptibility to mass movements for complex territories such as the study area. This suggests that the proposed methodology could be implemented in several regions with free access only based on similar features
