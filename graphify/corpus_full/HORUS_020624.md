# The Magdalena River and the Honda Port Complex 1745-1808

The document analyzes the role of the harbour complex of Honda, and of the Magdalena River, in the performance of the colonial economy between 1745 and 1808. The investigation maintains that the Magdalena River was the hinge that communicated to the mining regions of the province of Antioquia and the ports of the Caribbean with the agricultural-pecional zones of the high Magdalene and the textile zones of the eastern mountain range. To prove this was built a database using the manual books of alcabala, about 50,000 records of goods of the land were systematized and analyzed, managing to estimate the quantity of the goods of the land traded, their places of origin and their final markets of consumption. The the thesis supports that the villa of Honda was consolidated as the nucleus of a complex of ports, which facilitated the transport along the Magdalena River to communicate the zones producing goods of the land that circulated, mainly, in direction to the mines of flood of the province of Antioquia, ports of the low Magdalena and the Caribbean of New Granada, as well as to maintain part of the supply of the same harbour complex of Honda. . nes in the Antioquia province, Bajo Magdalena’s ports and the Caribbean, and to maintain the needs of supplying its own functioning
