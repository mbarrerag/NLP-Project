# An explanatory model of social, interpersonal and individual factors related to suicidal ideation in students of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá.

To explain by means of a model of structural equations, the effect that individual, interpersonal and social factors have on suicidal ideation, and the relationship between these factors. For the selection of participants in this study, two sampling methods will be used: quota sampling and convenience sampling. The instruments to be used will be: PANSI, ASSIST, Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, CES-D, MOS questionnaire of social support and ad hoc questions. The data collection process will be carried out through the Research Electronic Data Capture platform. Data analysis will be carried out based on the model of structural equations, multivariate statistical analysis technique, which is used in scientific research that aims to verify and evaluate multivariate causal relationships. Expected results: the theoretical model developed will allow a more complete and profound understanding of the factors that can contribute to suicidal ideation in university students, which may be useful for the development of prevention and treatment strategies
