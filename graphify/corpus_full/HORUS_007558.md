# Public health and medicine in Antioquia through the intellectual biography of scientist Alonso Restrepo Moreno, 1893-1955

This monograph proposes to make a biographical history of the doctor Alonso Restrepo Moreno (1893-1955), deepening in the study of his scientific production, his influence on the public health of Medellín and Antioquia from his exercise of medicine, as well as his connections and networks of exchange of knowledge with other scientists and institutions at national and global level. To this end, the sources of his personal archive and those that rest on the main archives of the city of Medellín will be consulted, in order to obtain a sufficient knowledge of the context in which Restrepo exercised his profession and, in this way, to explore his contributions
