# DURING 2020 and 2021

Porcine circovirosis is one of the diseases that most affects swine production in the world. The most common and expanded form of the disease is that caused by the pig circovirus type 2 (PCV2) that causes several syndromes that are already characterized. An important feature of this virus is the ability it has to generate new variants (genotypes) that can cause changes in the forms of presentation of the disease and effect on the efficiency of vaccines used for its control
