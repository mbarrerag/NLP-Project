# ASSESSMENT OF THE POTENTIAL CAPACITY OF SCAPHYTOPIUS SP (HEMIPTERA: CICADELLIDAE) TO TRANSMIT PHITHOPLAS TO QUERCUS HUMBOLDTII BONPL (FAGACEAE)

Quercus humboldtii is a species native to Colombia and is part of the urban wooded. It is affected by phytoplasms of groups 16SrI and 16SrVII, bacteria that generate progressive death and are transmitted by vector insects. The aim of this work was to evaluate the potential capacity of Scaphytopius sp. (Cicadellidae) to transmit phytoplasms. In 2019 individuals of Scaphytopius sp. were collected in oaks of three sites with contrasting landscape structure (park, vehicle separator and and andén) in Bogotá. The individuals were captured with Japanese umbrella and were evaluated for the presence of phytoplasms by PCR annis. From the individuals captured (park n=22, vehicle separator n=176), 41 were evaluated, and only 4 were positive for phytoplasms. The ability of transmission was evaluated with incristrial tests; they consisted of providing the insects with artificial food of sucrose in a way that when these were fed secretan saliva and with it was phytoplas, simulating what occurred in plants.
