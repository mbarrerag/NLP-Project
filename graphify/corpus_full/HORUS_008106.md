# Teaching strategies of violin teacher to increase motivation from meaningful learning at the basic level

This qualitative research consists of the autoobservation of the exercise as a professor of the researcher. The information observed concerning motivation is described, synthesized and catalogued in its three main types: intrinsic, extrinsic and aversive. For the case study, a selection of nine students in total of the current population and a theoretical framework prior to the collection of data will be analyzed later. Consequently, the violin teacher with students of basic level, a list of strategies, created and/or frequently used by the researcher in her classes, is proposed in order to increase the motivation, according to the classification stipulated, from the significant learning, being at the same time points of departure of a more dynamic instrumental pedagogy that allows the musical exploration of students from the beginning of their training. . The present quantitative review is described, and strongly considered as an autoethnography of the researchers exercise as a teacher. ining
