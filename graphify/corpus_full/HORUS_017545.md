# PROBLEM ON SELECTION OF USED SEMIINFINIT OPTIMINATION

In this paper, a method of mathematical optimization is used to solve the portfolio selection problem. It is posed as a semi-infinite programming problem (PSI). It is solved using a version of the stochastic method of external approximations in MATLAB. The method replaces the original problem with a finite sequence of optimization problems. The results are compared with other portfolio selection strategies such as that developed by Markowitz. Numerical experiments are carried out using five years of data from eight major Colombian companies listed on the stock market. The results indicate that the PSI portfolio is similar to the minimum variance portfolio and that both are diversified portfolios. The maximum yield is not a diversified portfolio and should be discarded. Although the portfolio is diversified, it presents a lower performance and expected return, but less risk than the minimum variance portfolio and the PSI. It is concluded that the proposed method provides competitive solutions compared with Markowitz's method
