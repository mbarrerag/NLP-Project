# Mitigating voltage imbalances in distribution electrical networks using synchro-converters as power supply of distributed generation.

This thesis develops a cascade control method for the integration of distributed generation to electrical networks, whose voltage is unbalanced. These networks are common in distribution systems since, it is usually found voltage imbalances caused by the presence of monophasic and biphasic loads, or monophasic generation photovoltaic that, unbalanced power consumption in the network. A modified model of Synchronverter (Syncro-converter) capable of synchronizing to a debalanced network, in order to deliver active and reactivate power to the network, and give support to voltage and frequency. After the syncro-converter connection stage, the percentage of imbalance by negative sequence presence in the common connection point (PCC) is decreased. The above is achieved by decomposing into symmetrical components according to the time, with special attention to the voltage calculated positive and negative sequences. The proposed control focuses on a flexible control of positive and negative sequences, and delivers power in both sequences with the aim of reducing the negative component in the PCC voltage each result is achieved by means of a single voltage factor of a positive and negative sequences
