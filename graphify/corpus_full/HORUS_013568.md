# Concentration of particulate material in the urban area of Medellín, based on solar photometer observations and air quality sensor

Currently there are techniques that allow to study optical and microphysical properties of aerosols of the column of atmosphere by modifying the radioactive field due to the characteristics of the atmospheric aerosol, also allow to determine pollutants that can affect the quality of the air. This study is based on the optical methods of solar photometry and laser diffraction, for the remote monitoring of the column of atmosphere and of the air layer at ground level. The data collection was carried out by a solar photometer of NASAAERONET to determine the optical thickness and monitor the entire column of the terrestrial atmosphere; and a portable laser equipment, Sensor of PurpleAir, which detects particulate material to measure pollution in the air by particles.
