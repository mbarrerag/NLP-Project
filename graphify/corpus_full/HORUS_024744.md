# Frequency, type and determinants of cognitive deficit in a Colombian cohort of patients with systemic lupus erythematosus.

The cognitive deficit belongs to neuropsychiatric syndromes associated with systemic lupus erythematosus. It requires diagnosis through specific neuropsychological battery. It affects the quality of life and predisposes to the development of dementia. In our environment the frequency of cognitive deficit as manifestation of neuropsychiatric LES is unknown. It is not known whether there are determinant conditions for this. To define the frequency and degree of cognitive deficit in a cohort of patients with SLE and describe associated factors. An observational cross-sectional study was performed with 94 patients with SLE. The presence of cognitive deficit was established through a screening test (MoCA test) and the diagnostic confirmation with a battery of specific neuropsychological tests in patients with the abnormal MoCA test. The quality of life was evaluated through the LupusCol form and the presence of depression with Beck's inventory. An exploratory analysis of the variables was performed and differences were established. A multivariate analysis was made to establish the variables of association with the abnormal tam.
