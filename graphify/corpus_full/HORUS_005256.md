# THE SIERRA NEVAD DE SANTA MARTA, A DYNAMIC TOPOGRAPH

The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta (SNSM) is a rocky massif isolated from the Andes mountain range. Its topography begins on the Caribbean coast until it reaches a height of more than 5,500 m above sea level, covering almost all the thermal floors. What geological processes allowed SNSM to have these characteristics? What makes this massif keep its height so close to the coast?
