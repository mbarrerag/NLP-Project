# TOWARDS THE CONCEPTUALIZATION OF HETERARCHICAL GOVERNANCE IN ALTERNATIVE AGROFOOD NETWORKS

In the analysis of the dynamics that are generated between the actors that constitute alternative agro-food networks to understand governance architecture that they establish for their operation, it is essential to go beyond the traditional analysis that involves the understanding of hierarchical and market relations, incorporating elements that explain the interactions that arise in these processes based on the coordination and cooperation capacities of the actors. Alternative agro-food networks are considered as collective actions that can be understood under the concept of heterarchy as a social structure. Conceptualization of the heterarchical governance was carried out using elements from the theory, and the analytical framework of governance was used on eight cases of alternative agrifood networks to characterize its existence, finding as common problems the feasibility of alternative mechanisms of production and consumption, and in some experiences, the organization for the management of the production and its sustainability. Regarding the variables, autonomy, coherence, and coordination is observed in all cases, as a highlighted by the research as strengths to successful collective actions that configurated of heterarchical governance
