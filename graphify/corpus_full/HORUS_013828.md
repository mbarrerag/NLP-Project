# Calibration and evaluation of drone images (UAV) for the study of spatial variability of crop nutritional status.

Colombia regulated policies associated with the multipurpose cadastre in order to have the country's real estate inventories updated through the use of tools and cartographic products, within which they must be guaranteed to have positional accuracy in relation to the scale of production, this in combination with multispectral cameras, remote technologies and satellite images, have been applied on multiple occasions within the agricultural sector, where a large amount of space can be captured in a very short time, facilitating processes that involve effort and monetary expenditure, as well as providing an analysis benefit for decision-making by the specialist. Therefore, in the following proposal, it will carry out the evaluation and calibration of sensors for the taking of images with UAV in order to determine the spatial variability of the nutritional state of crops
