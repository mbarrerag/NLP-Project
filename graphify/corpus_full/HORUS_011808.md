# ROLE OF INFRAMESENTRIC PARA-AORTIC LYMPHADENECTOMY RADICAL DURING HYSTERECTOMY IN PATIENTS WITH IB1 CHANGE IN NATIONAL CANCEROLOGY INSTITUTE BOGOTA, COLOMBIA.

ABSTRACT Introduction: Cervical cancer is a common pathology in developing countries. Radical hysterectomy is the standard for early stage management without a desire for fertility. Paraaortic lymphadenectomy as part of surgical treatment is controversial. The aim of this study is to determine the frequency of paraaortic ganglion involvement in a retrospective series of patients with stage IB1 cervical carcinoma (Figo 2009 classification) who were taken to radical hysterectomy plus pelvic and paraaortic lymphadenectomy at the National Cancer Institute during the period January 1 2009 to March 31, 2017. A descriptive, retrospective study. Clinical, operative and histopathological variables were described. The frequency of ganglion involvement at the paraaortic or pelvic level was determined, and concurrent. Univariate analysis was performed in the statistical software R Project version 3.6.0
