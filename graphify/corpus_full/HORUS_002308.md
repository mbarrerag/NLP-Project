# Residences Columbus: Look and Look

In the Mirador mirador mirador, I explore the relationship between intimate space and public space through the experience lived in the 403 apartment of the Residencias Colón building in Bogotá. Focused on the window as a physical and symbolic limit, the project analyzes how this element means between the domestic interior and the urban exterior, allowing a constant exchange of looks, sounds and presences. The window not only delimits the private, but also acts as a device for observation and contemplation of the urban landscape. Thus, the Residencias Colón building (first building on the 10th race), icon of the architectural modernization of Bogota, serves as an historical and contextual framework through an autobiographical approach that permeates all my experience. In which, I find how the window becomes a viewpoint that connects experiences and times. The research is structured in three axes: observation, contemplation and memory. Observation is addressed as an active practice that analyzes the urban landscape; contemplation, as a state of spontaneous pause that offers a respite to the accelerated pace of life; and memory, as a link between the history of the building and the city
