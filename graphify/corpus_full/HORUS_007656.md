# Study of starch obtained from the seed of the fruit of the umari (Poraqueiba Sericea) as a possible excipient for the pharmaceutical industry.

One of the areas of the country where there is a great diversity of native plant species corresponds to the Amazon region. An example of this is the fruit of the umarí (Poraqueiba sericea Tul.), a species that is not known in much of the Colombian territory since its cultivation takes place in this region, and of which it is expected that the starch obtained from the seed of the fruit could have a high potential for use in different industries of the country, especially in the pharmaceutical one. Therefore, the aim of this grade work is to study some of the physical chemical properties of the starch obtained from the fruit of the umarí (Poraqueiba sericea Tul.), which gives scientific basis for the future formulation and understanding of its possible functionality as excipient to be used in cosmetic or pharmaceutical formulations.
