# A TRUTH from ECOGENOETNOCIDIO IN BENNAVENTURA

As a contribution to the processes of truth, justice, reparation and non-repetition, this book aims to tell the story of structural violence suffered in Buenaventura from the perspective of the Black people and their territory. It is the result of the joint work of a research team of the Central University with the Association of Women and Men of Triana and other leaders, with whom a solidary experience of historical construction was developed. Based on this experience, the book proposes a biocentric shift in order to understand in depth the violence suffered by black communities and their ecosystems, as well as to subvert the current notion of Afro-repairs, through the term ecogenoethnocide. The introduction and its eight chapters of this book approach various aspects of the history of the Black people, their struggles against violence and racism, and the crimes committed against women, communities and the ecosystem. In addition, two dialogues on the construction of this black truth with their own leaders and victims are included. This book, in the last analysis, aims to be a contribution to the search for comprehensive justice for Black people in Colombia
