# COMPARISON OF TWO CONTROL CHARTS FOR MONITORING SKEW-NORMAL DISTRIBUTED DATA

In the context of Statistical Process Control (SPC), the quality of a process can be often expressed in terms of a single random variable, whose distribution must be checked for stability over time. Most of the research work about variable monitoring is based on the normal assumption. However, even when normality could be established, it would still be a mere assumption since data may actually exhibit some skewed pattern. In this work, two methodologies for monitoring skew-normal samples are reviewed and compared in terms of their average run length performance. Special attention is devoted to changes in the process mean level that are due to changes in the skew-normal shape parameter
