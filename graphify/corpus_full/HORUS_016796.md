# CORROSION AND WEAR RESISTANCE OF ALCRTIN-CU COATINGS DEPOSITED BY MIX PULSED DC AND RF REACTIVE MAGNETRON CO-SPUTTERING

Transition metal nitride (MeN) thin films have been widely investigated due to their physical and chemical properties. However, the industry is looking for enhancing the performance of coatings and it has been found that optimizing AlTiCrN coatings by adding soft metals attracts considerable interest in order to modify their structure and thus enhance their functional properties. In this work, AlCrTiN films with different Copper (Cu) contents were deposited on WC-Co and 4340 steel substrates through pulse-DC and RF reactive magnetron sputtering. The influence of Cu content was examined regarding the microstructure, morphology and corrosion wear resistance behaviour. The wear resistance of the films was investigated using a pin on disk tribometer and the corrosion resistance though out electrochemical impedance spectroscopy. The phase and microstructure of the film have been characterized through XRD and TEM. The morphology has been performed via a field emission scanning electron microscope (FE- SEM). Preliminary results showed that all the films exhibited a pronounced orientation and diffraction peaks crystalline Cu were not observed. The morphology exhibits a pyramid-shaped structure. The dry friction coefficient decreased a value of 0.6, corresponding to AlTiCrN film, to a low value of approximately 0.5 in the film with the highest copper content. Transmission electron microscopy confirms that copper is most likely to form on nanoclusters of composite coatings. The wear and corrosion mechanisms in the films will be discussed in this work
