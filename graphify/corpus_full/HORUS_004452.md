# PROCESS OF DESIGN, COMPILATION AND DATA PROCESSING IN CASE STUDIES

This chapter aims to explain the application of the methodology of the Ccaso Study (EC) for the construction of the chain of evidence linking the general context, theory and research problem with the final results of an investigation. Therefore, here are defined the strategies and tools used for the review of the previous theory, data collection, information processing and case presentation. In this order, this chapter describes the process of design, collection and processing of data with special emphasis on the design and execution of interviews and the processing of information with the use of ATLAS.ti software and examples taken from a doctoral research
