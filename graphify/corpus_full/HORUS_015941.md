# Preliminary evaluation of the safety of Bijao Leaf (Calathea Lutea) in Bocadillo Veleño as a contribution to Food Sovereignty.

In Colombia, the packaging and packaging of food, as a guarantee tool of safety, does not have a specific regulation on the use of plant materials traditionally used as food packaging, as is the case of the Bijao leaf traditionally used as a sandwich wrap. On the other hand, the designation of origin given to the “Velek Bocadillo” by issuing Resolution 37563 of 2017 of the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce supports the use of the Bijao leaf because it considers this to be a crucial element within the characteristics of the product and in the same way the Colombian technical standard NTC 5856:2011 that refers to the specifications of the guavadillo snack, indicates that for the traditional presentation of the Bocadillo the Bijao leaf can be used as a packaging of the product. It is then found a dichotomy between the regulation, standardization and the use of the Bijao leaf as a food packaging.
