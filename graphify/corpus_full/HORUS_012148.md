# CHAPTER 1 DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION: THE WAY TO THE CULTURE CHANGE OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The path to Digital Transformation in the world is a necessity that allows us to welcome the urgent changes that we need in the globalized and hyper-mediated societies of the Fourth Industrial Revolution. At the National University of Colombia, we have taken on a collective project for the integral transformation and sustainable development of the nation, as well as the reconstruction of the social fabric and the search for solutions to the problems of the country. At the National University of Colombia, under the leadership of the Rector Dolly Montoya, responses arise to the challenges facing Colombia. Education, science and technology are some of the pillars for the structural changes of the country in the face of the global needs and trends (World Economic Forum, 2022), since they allow the formation of integral citizens as agents of ethical and cultural changes, with social awareness and knowledge management. Reaching the goals and goals set by the challenges of the country implies a substantial change within the classrooms, that involves all actors, since finally we seek changes centered on the human being. Thus, digital skills and academic disciplines of science, technology, engineering and mathematics (CTIM or STEM by their acronyms in the National University) acquires relevance to driving the innovation processes within Colombia
