# TRENDS IN ACCOUNTING RESEARCH: BIBLE GRAPHICAL REVIEW

A categorization of the articles included in the research area was based on a review of content analysis. For the content analysis, the keywords, objectives and methodology were reviewed. This allowed to review in particular the topics developed in the sources consulted, the methods and techniques used, to finally recognize the contributions, recommendations and main results. The following sub-themes were identified: administration, economy, education, gender, epistemology of accounting and humanities
