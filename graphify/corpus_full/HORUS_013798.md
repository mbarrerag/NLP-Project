# ESTIMATION OF THE QUALITY OF CACOA ORGANISED BY FAMILY LINKING TO ASSOCAPADAYARI-SANTANDER ASSOCACYANIC ASSOCANYARI-SANTANDER ASSOCACYANIC ASSOCACYALY.

The economic value and acceptance of chocolate and its derivatives depend on several factors such as the taste and smell of cocoa, the aroma of cocoa beans depends on the genotype of cocoa, geographical origin, environmental conditions, state of maturity of the grains, and post-harvest processing (fermentation, drying and roasting).Although the department of Santander is considered the department with the highest cocoa production in the country, not all persons linked to this sector have the availability of resources to estimate the quality of their products, it is the case of families linked to the association of Asocapayari farmers, who grow cocoa as a means of survival, located in the municipality of Carmen del Chucurí-Santander. In this project, it is proposed to support these families, through the estimation of the quality of their cocoa products by means of the physiochemical and sensory characterization in cocoa liquor and cocoa grain, by analytical techniques such as gas chromatography, MIR spectroscopy, and electronic nose. By integrating the instrumental and sensory results, it is intended to obtain a tool that allows the producers in the field to estimate the quality of their cocoa grain, in a differentiated market value
