# Hemerography, graphics and peace in the beginning of the Colombian 20th century

The Colombian hemerography of the 20th century is characterized by its warlike character. Newspapers were a combat weapon in electoral campaigns, giving continuity to the war of the 19th century through politics. However, there was a part of the national newspaper, which was borne by the graph that proposed a different alternative: that of peace. these are el gráfico, cromos and universidad magazine. Magazines arranged for: cartoonists, painters, caricaturists, illustrators, photographers, etc. Through them another country is perceived, different from that of political confrontation, and that of social deprivations; lighter, more liveable and interesting. An art divorced from the State, which is not interested in the use of art for the development of national culture. What would have happened to Colombian art if the media that we have approached had not existed, that discovered, welcomed and recognized it? It is the question that haunts the spirit of this article
