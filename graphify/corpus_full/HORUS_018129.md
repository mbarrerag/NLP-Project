# Emerging properties of the opening of the chess game.

This work consists of two parts. In the first part, the formalism of entropy for nodal degree and the nodal strength in the theor ́a of complex networks were applied to the first chess movement to the Olympics of the FIDE, the international congress of Hastings and a historical compendium of games; for this purpose a representation of the opening of the chess game was constructed through a directed and valued graph, which allows to characterize emerging properties of the evolution of the strategies of the game. In the second part, a mechanical-statistical model was applied, combined with the entropies of nodal degree and nodal strength of the network to the opening of the great masters Anatoly Karpov, Garry Kasparov and Magnus Carlsen. With the measures of entrop ́as, relations were found between the first movement and the result of the games, relevant events in the history of chess, and the changes and fashion of the use of strategies.
