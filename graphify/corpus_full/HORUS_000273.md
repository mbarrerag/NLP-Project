# Agricultural engineering and the CONCIA 2025 Congress: 60 Years in Colombia Meeting the Challenges of Sustainable and Competitive Agro-Food Production

Agricultural Engineering originated with the creation of the first schools of agriculture and mechanical arts in the United States of America appearing as an academic program in 1862. At European level, in 1930, the “Commission Internationale du Génie Rural” was founded as the “Commission Internationale de Génie Rural” with the purpose of coordinating the work and development of Agricultural Engineering. In Latin America this program was established in the 1950s, parallel to the modernization of agriculture. The first School of Agricultural Engineering was created at the Technical University of Manabí-Ecuador in the year 1957. The origin of the first program of Agricultural Engineering in our country, dates back to 1956, when the National University of Colombia signed an agreement of academic cooperation with the University of Michigan, proposing in 1962 the creation of a five-year university-level program suggesting that the Faculty of Agronomy of the headquarters of Medellín will be responsible for promoting it and establishing it, given that it was one of the most experienced schools of the academic field in the teaching of Agricultural Sciences in Latin America. With the collaboration of FAO, the OAS, the Agricultural University of Peru plan, the University of Michigan, and professors of the National University of the Bogotá, Palmira and the Engineering. 716 (237 engineers and 488 engineers) who have graduated from the curriculum. The curriculum plan of Agricultural Engineering since its creation has been focused on the areas of water and soil engineering; agricultural mechanization; rural constructions; and post-harvest engineering of agricultural processes. The profile of the Agricultural Engineer is a professional with the ability to correlate the foundations of engineering to give optimal technical economic solutions to the needs of food production, which has the theoretical and practical preparation that provides him with the technical and scientific principles for his correct performance in research, consultancy, intervention, direction and administration of the activities and projects of the Agricultural Engineering, with criteria of sustainability. Several updates have been made to the curriculum of the programme in line with the processes of globalization and technological advances that allow for a better use of the resources, in which the function of the engineer is essential for the construction of the objectives of economic growth with equity and social justice, as targets of any development strategy., adaptation to climate change and environmental sustainability. Therefore, in the academic preparation of future professionals of agricultural engineering, consideration should be given to the social, market, professional practices and environmental preservation needs (economic, social and environmental sustainability), incorporating knowledge in precision agriculture (digitalization, automation and mechanization of agriculture);
