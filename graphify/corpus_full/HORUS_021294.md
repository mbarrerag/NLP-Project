# Coexistence in multi-ethnic communities adjacent to urban areas of the Colombian Amazon

With this research we seek to contribute to the expansion of the theoretical corpus on sociality, gender, and good living in multi-ethnic communities in urban transition in the Amazon Northwest. The purpose of this research will be to analyze native theories that in effective practice point to the search for good coexistence and, from there, to understand how people face social conflicts and their affects, especially those related to gender, in order to jointly propose strategies that improve coexistence, that besides being useful to propose gender arrangements within communities, they are also useful to negotiate models of own and intercultural attention with the institutions that are responsible for these issues. To achieve this objective, we propose the development of ethnographic and collaborative investigations (Guba & Lincoln, 2012; Heron & Reason, 1997), that privilege the forms of experiential knowledge and the relations of respect and cooperation between local and external co-investigators
