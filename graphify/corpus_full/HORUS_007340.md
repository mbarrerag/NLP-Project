# Guided tours for children's audiences in the art museums of the Cultural Apple of the Bank of the Republic: Analysis of their value chain and their contributions to the training of publics

The art museums are increasingly inclusive and open to all publics. Even with all the efforts and constant work from the cultural management to build accessible spaces, there are those who do not feel invited and refrain from visiting them. For this reason, the educational processes focused on children's audiences -without wanting to neglect other audiences - turns out to be a valuable tool to generate long-term links with these cultural institutions, being this one of the bases for the construction of current audiences and for posterity. This study focused on the case of the art museums of the cultural apple of the Banco de la República, institution that has been constituted as a reference in management for its tireless contribution to the cultural activity and protection of the Colombian heritage; actions that have built a solid trust on the part of its users. The present work had as a goal to identify and analyze the value chain after the guided visits to children's public and its contribution to the formation of these audiences, making a recognition of its good practices and the construction of significant experiences for its small users. This research has been used ethnographic methods and systematization of experiences to know the experiences made from the museums and the perceptions of children. ent and future audiences. This study focused on the art museums of the Bank of the Republic’s Cultural Apple, an institution that has become a reference in management due to its patterns contribution to cultural activity and the protection of Colombian heritage. These actions have built a strong sense of trust combined its users. The objective of this work was to identify and analyse the value chain best guide tours promoted at children and its contribution to audience development, improving best practices and the creation of meaningful experiences for its young visitors.
