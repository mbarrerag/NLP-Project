# organizational Performance of agroredes supply agri-food of the central region of Colombia

Food production in Colombia comes mostly from peasant economies. In order to improve supply, policies have been generated that promote associativity, their purpose is to harmonize demand and promote the integration of food organizations (agroredes). To ensure permanence in the agroredes market, the strategies must promote the strengthening of management through the generation of instruments that identify organizational strengths and weaknesses. The objective of this research was to analyze the organizational performance of agroredes in the central region of Colombia. Three phases were contemplated: (i) Characterize the agroredes, (ii) identify their level of organizational performance and (iii) develop strategies that increase their performance. The data was obtained from surveys carried out with the representatives of 60 agroredes in the central region (Bogotá, Cundinamarca, Boyacá, Tolima and Meta), with which the dimensions of: Administration, associativity, marketing, ICT management and logistics were evaluated. and storage of each organization. For the analysis, multivariate statistical tools of multiple correspondence (ACM) and cluster (AC) were used. The results presented by chapters show that half of the agroredes are formalized, and the majority with defined functions, but they need to consolidate their financial strategy. In addition, three groups of agroredes were identified based on their organizational maturity: 11 in organizational maturity, 43 in organizational opening and 6 in organizational development. The performance of the agroredes is in the consolidation stage. Associativity is the dimension with the best performance; while ICT management is the dimension with the greatest challenges. Through PESTEL and SWOT analysis, strategies have been generated that will allow agroredes to improve decision-making, better respond to changes and take better advantage of opportunities in the environment
