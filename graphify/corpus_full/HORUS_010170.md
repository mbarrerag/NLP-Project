# Effect of the application of potassium phosphite on metabolite biosynthesis during the key interaction (Dianthus caryophyllus L.) - Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi.

In this research, the effect of applying a solution to 3% potassium phosphite in the biochemistry of carnation roots during the induction of resistance to the pathogen Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi. Although the dissolutions of potassium phosphite have been used as fertilizers, systemic fungicides or natural defense activators, for the case of the cultivation of the carnation, their use has been limited and there are only some recent reports on their role as an inducer of resistance. Therefore, in order to deepen the processes involved in the action of this inductor, in this research, applying tools of metabolomic analysis not directed, were studied the metabolites that accumulate in plant roots by action of this resistance inducer and the expression of genes associated with some of the metabolites of interest, with prior transferability. For this, a live trial was carried out where the strategy for the application of the solution of phosphite in the soil was selected.
