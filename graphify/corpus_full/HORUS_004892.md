# PROGRESS OF SECOND GENERATION: BETWEEN GASPADISM AND SOCIAL REFORMS AS SETTINGS OF TRANSFORMATION.

The global crisis we have been seeing since 2007 and which is becoming more severe with the health emergency arising from COVID 19 in 2020 has opened important debates on whether the crisis is an expression of the general collapse of the system or simply a convulsive movement possible to adjust within the mainstream of the current social order. And, while there is no unified position between the different economic and political forces at global level in the face of the magnitude of the crisis and the paths for its re-solution, it is clear that both the orthodox and heterodox tendencies advocate for solutions of less or more serious within the contours of the capitalist social system.1 The context of the generation and the implementation of social reforms is proposed in the first and second stages of the current situation (Jimenez, 2023b).
