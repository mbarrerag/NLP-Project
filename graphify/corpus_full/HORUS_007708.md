# Barriers and opportunities for the implementation of emerging technologies in design teaching strategies in the industrial design and graphic design curriculums of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá

--In recent years, numerous studies have been carried out on the use of emerging technologies in education. The current research trends in the field of education have called attention to the inadequacy of studies that address the educational process in environments of extended realities (XR) from a perspective that considers user experience (UX). As XR technologies evolve and integrate into more everyday settings, their potential to transform educational experiences continues to grow, promising significant advances in how users interact, personalise and diverge in learning content.This project explores the integration of emerging technologies such as extended realities (RX) and Artificial Intelligence (IA) into design teaching strategies, focusing on how these technologies can enhance educational experience from the perspective of user experience design (UX).The commitment, motivation and outcomes of learning are examined, as well as the barriers and opportunities for their implementation in the curriculum of industrial and graphic design of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, and the challenges in the development of user interfaces (UI) will be based on the XR research. Ultimately, this project has the potential to position the National University of Colombia as a leader in the adoption of emerging technologies in design education, providing new knowledge and methodologies that can be shared with the academic and professional community at the national and international levels through publications and presentations
