# COVID-19 AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT IN LATIN AMERICA

This work addresses the unfolding of the pandemic by covid-19, both in economic reflection coma in the Latin American economy. Thus, it brings together the appreciation of Latin American researchers who share a common vision of the functioning of capitalism and the general outlines of the economies of the region, based in an important way on the combination of the structuralist perspective with the debates on financialization. The structure of this book organizes the chapters in three parts: 1) those who review the fundamental theoretical elements for the discussion of the outstanding tasks of Latin American development in a post-pandemic reconstruction scenario; 2) those who evaluate the concrete experiences of the historical present of some Latin American countries with an emphasis on financial reorganization; and 3) those who review the economic policy responses implemented after the pandemic in the region
