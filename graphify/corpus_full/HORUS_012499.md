# THE IVP FOR CERTAIN GENERALIZED DISPERSION OF THE ZK EQUATION IN THE CYLINDER SPACE

The paper establishes well-posedness for the Cauchy problem associated with the Zakharov-Kutnesov equation in the cylinder for certain generalized dispersion. The main ingredient in this work is a localized Strichartz estimate and a compactness argument
