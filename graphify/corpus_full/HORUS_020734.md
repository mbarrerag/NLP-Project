# Prefabricated construction system (off site), technical analysis to develop its use in building construction in Colombia. “Case study a project designed and built in the city of Bogotá”

The growing need for building is a constant reality of our society. The construction system of precasts is part of one of the alternatives that confirms the importance of the construction industry for the development of Colombia, taking into account that in the country the adaptation of systems is required increasingly industrialized with a decided commitment to innovation and the implementation of sustainable and technological developments. This grade work on the prefabricated construction system will be carried out in order to analyze this system from a project designed and constructed in the city of Bogotá as an alternative of use for the construction of new buildings in Colombia. To this end, a methodological approach of a quantitative and qualitative type is proposed. Initially, information is sought through surveys and interviews with construction companies that allow to see the knowledge, opinions or experiences that have on this topic, in the second place and with the design of case study, it is intended to carry out a comparative analysis between the industrialized, traditional and precasting system in a construction in the city of Bogotá and finally it is expected to establish a guide with the subject in depth and with the results adjusted to the constructive reality of the country that allows to provide its implementation in the great scale. cal approach is proposed.
