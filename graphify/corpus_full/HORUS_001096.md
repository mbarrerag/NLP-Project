# Effect of the use of garlic extract (Allium sativum) as a dietary additive in juvenile red tilapia hybrid

The objective of the study was to evaluate the effect of different levels of garlic (Allium sativum) on the diet of juveniles of red tilapia, on productive, physiological and muscle composition parameters, under normal conditions of cultivation and thermal stress. Two experiments were carried out. In the first, with a duration of 46 days, 81 individuals distributed in nine tanks (9 ind/tank) with three repetitions per treatment, under a water recirculation system. The juveniles presented a size and average weight of 14,783 ± 0.725 cm and 63,843 ± 3,067 g. They were distributed in three treatments: a control (0 g/kg of garlic extract) and two levels of inclusion of garlic (T1: 1 g/kg and T2: 2 g/kg). At the end of the study, they were sampled and slaughtered 4 individuals per tank (12 per treatment), to evaluate the productive, physiological and body composition parameters.
