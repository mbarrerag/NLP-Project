# Analysis of the sustainable VFAs production using anaerobic digestion through the biorefinery concept

The conventional anaerobic digestion (AD) is a widely applied technology for the generation of renewable energy in the form of biogas, from organic waste. The simplicity of the process and the existing microbial consortium allows to use different types of residues as substrate. Residues from different value chains (VC), for example, agricultural, agro-industrial and food, from different links. The AD has multiple metabolic pathways present at each stage of the process. Therefore, it has been demonstrated that the AD could be designed to produce mixed volatile fatty acids through the modified AD. The modified AD corresponds to the variation of the operating conditions of the process to promote specific metabolic routes. It was proposed the integration of conventional and modified AD as a sustainable pillar for the valorization of three residues generated in different VC links through the concept of biorefinery. A functional analysis was carried out from the input to determine the bottlenecks of the VC and the possible integration of the biorefineries. Likewise, the sustainability (considering the technical, economic, environmental and social dimensions) of different biorefinery scenarios was evaluated.
