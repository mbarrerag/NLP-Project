# THE WORKER WOMAN IN TIMES OF THE COVID-19?

In 2019, when the first cases of the disease caused by SARS-CoV-2 were reported in China, the world thought that this would be something temporary and that it would not transcend the borders of Wuhan, population where the virus originated. However, by October 2021, the pandemic has been among us for more than a year, has caused 4,899,169 deaths, and has changed the way of interacting, living and working of people. The difficulties inherent in a pandemic that just started and the fact of having little information about its origin, treatment and prevention affected especially the workers of the health sector. However, the panorama was gradually clarified, thanks to scientific research, confinement and certain patterns emanated from the World Health Organization that, among others, insisted on the need to preserve the social distance, the constant handwashing and the use of tapabocas to prevent the contagion. Thus, professors of the Faculty of Medicine, Nursing, Engineering (Bogota and Palmira), Arts, and Economic Sciences that, among others, present in this book generalities about the COVID-19, and their impact and challenges for the field of health and to be returned
