# CIMMENTATIONS: CONSTRUCTIVE PROCESS AND ITS PERFORMANCE DESIGN

Our book brings together academic and practical material essential for students from the first semesters of careers related to construction, especially on the construction processes and the design and execution of superficial and deep foundations. We design this book to facilitate access to information relevant to the execution of a construction project, show the step by step of the construction process and enrich the learning process with the introduction of an administrative component
