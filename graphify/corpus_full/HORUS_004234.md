# Assessment of sensory profile and degree of acceptability of mango residues (Mangifera indica) source of fiber as an ingredient in the formulation of beverages

The assessment of the sensory profile as the degree of acceptability of mango residues (Mangifera indicates), the production of mango shell flour and subsequently used as a source ingredient of fiber and antioxidants in the formulation of a drink based on mango and maracuyá. The first challenge facing the development of a fruit drink type blended with fiber is the evaluation of sensory acceptability. In parallel, the characterization of the flourmediate physicochemical parameters was performed, including pH, total solids (°Brix), water activity, color CIELAB (L*, a*, b*), dietary fibre (total, soluble and insoluble), phenol content and antioxidant capacity. The basic drink is characterized both physically and sensoryly, measuring pH, total solids, content of phenols and antioxidant capacity. In the sensory assessment, the formulation of preference of consumers and the sensory profile of a trained panel is determined. Subsequently, the mango shell flour is added to the basic drink selected in percentages of inclusion of 1%, 2% and 3% of the total dietary capacity.. It is subject to sensory evaluations of acceptance and is added in different proportions to a fruit drink mix. Flour characterisation is carried out out using physicochemical parameters, including pH, total solids (°Brix), water activity, CIELAB color (L*, a*, b*), dietary fiber (total, soluble and insoluble), phenol content and antioxidant capacity. The base drink is characterized by physiochemically and sensoryly, measuring pH, total solids, phenol content and antioxidant capacity.
