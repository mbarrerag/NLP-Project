# A proposal for classification of games for educational purpose for software engineering

ABSTRACT The games for educational purposes are also being used in different areas to complement traditional teaching-learning strategies. Software engineering oriented to the application of methods for the construction of high quality software products also incorporates these games in the training of their professionals. In order to provide a tool to teachers that guides their use in the teaching-learning process, different classifications of games are proposed. However, the existing classifications take into account who is targeted or what is their purpose, more are not included variables associated with the structure of the game as its materials or rules. In this article a classification of games for the teaching of software engineering is proposed taking into account both its purpose and scope and its play component. This proposal seeks to establish the key characteristics that any game should incorporate for the teaching of software engineering and to provide alternatives to teachers to incorporate this teaching tool into their training processes. The proposed classification was applied to a set of 17 games oriented towards the teaching of software engineering demonstrating its potential as a support tool in the analysis process, comparison and selection of games for the teaching of this discipline
