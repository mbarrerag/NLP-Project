# BIODETERIOR MECHANISMS AND IN VITRO CONCRETE METEORIZATION MEASURED BY ASPERGILLUS NIGER AND LEUCAENA LEUCOCEPHALA

Interactions between fungi and plants in structures generate instability to concrete by changing their chemical, physical, mechanical and mineralogical properties, which leads to a biodegradation, which are induced by processes of metabolic activity of these living organisms, when they produce organic acids. Moreover, the construction industry, in the processes of demolishing-constructing, affects the soil, not only in the processes of extracting raw material but also in the processes of disposal of the generated residues. These anthropogenic processes do not allow the use of soil in landscaping or agriculture generating ecological impacts on the soil. In this study, the production of organic acids was investigated by the symbiosis of the fungus Aspergillus niger and the Leucaena leucocephala plant In vitro.
