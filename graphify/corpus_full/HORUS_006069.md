# Methodological approaches to calculate the risk of both expected and unexpected losses in equity investments in the Colombian stock market

The document "Methodological Approaches to Calculate the Risk of both expected and unexpected losses in the Colombian Accionary Market" presents an applied research of a quantitative and exploratory nature. It focuses on the detailed analysis and the precise measurement of financial risks using methodologies such as Normal Delta, Historical Simulation, Montecarlo, Theory of Extrem and Escalated Values. The study seeks to provide robust estimates of the Value in Risk (VAR) and the Value in Conditioned Risk (CVAR) in the dynamic context of the Colombian Stock Exchange (MSCI COLCAP).
