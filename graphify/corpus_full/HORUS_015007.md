# EPIDEMIOLOGICAL CONTEXT OF THE APICAL PERIOD IN ASSISTANTS TO OdonTOSANITAS BOGOTÁ,2011-2016

Apical periodontitis (PA) as a prevalent disease connects oral health to the general state of the individual. It was intended to characterize in an integral epidemiological context the presence of PA in a population of a clinic of Odontosánitas in Bogotá. This cross-sectional study included retrospective documentary record of 3981 patients attending the endodontic service. Periapical condition; absence or presence of PA, was recorded clinically and radiographically. As sociodemographic and individual variables: sex, age, provenance, type of insure, type and location of tooth. The medical condition included presence or absence of systemic diseases, drug use and smoking habit. A total of 345 patients, with a prevalence rate of 43.6%. Unii/bivariate analysis, recorded prevalence. A multivariate model of multiple correspondences (CM), identified spatially, association and frequency of factors analyzed. A total of 378 PA assistants with a prevalence of PA of 51.6% [95% CI (46,5.6%)] and 48.98 years of age were identified as having a higher prevalence (ACM), with a higher association (40%) and a greater association (50%)
