# Decline in reading comprehension performance in fourth grade: a present phenomenon

Different studies have shown how the results in the area of language and in the understanding of reading show a tendency to decline in the fourth grade of primary basic, a phenomenon known as fourth grade decline, transit stage between learning to read and read to learn. This research has the interest to obtain information on the presence of this phenomenon in students from Bogotá. The reports of the students' notes of the subjects associated with Castellana Language, between 1 and 9th grade for the years 2016 to 2021 of 271 educational establishments, distributed in 508 places and formed by 654,642 students based on what was reported in the School Support System of the Secretariat of Education of Bogotá, a tool that brings together the academic reports of the students from some schools in Bogotá. The results indicate that there are statistically significant differences between grades and that there is a decline in performance from the first grades to the fourth grade, remaining stable for the next grades, but with results aimed at the average-low.
