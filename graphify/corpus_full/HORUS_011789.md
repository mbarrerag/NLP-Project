# ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL MEASURES AGAINST TUBERCLESIS

Administrative control measures are aimed at reducing the risk of occupational exposure of health workers to tuberculosis [1]. The aim is to reduce staff contact with the particulate pollutants of Mycobacterium tuberculosis, reduce intensity and mitigate the risk of tuberculosis transmission in the workplace [1]. According to the latest guide to oms on tuberculosis infection, prevention and control published in 2019, these measures include the following recommendations [1]. Recommendation 1. To remove people with signs and symptoms of tuberculosis, or with tuberculosis disease, from other people, to reduce the transmission of Mycobacterium tuberculosis to health workers, and to avoid the risk of infection to those attending these types of institutions. Recommendation 2. Separation/respiratory isolation of persons with infectious tuberculosis alleged or demonstrated to reduce the transmission of Mycobacterium tuberculosis to health workers or other persons present in health facilities. Recommendation 3. To initiate an effective treatment against tuberculosis immediately to reduce the transmission of Mycobacterium tuberculosis to health workers, persons attending health facilities or other health-care facilities should be assisted by the use of tuberculosis or by the use of the health care facilities
