# Urbanization and environmental valuation in the sub-basin of the Tunjuelo River

Urbanisation is a complex process involving different variables and actors, as a result, cities can provide goods and services to their inhabitants; but they are also the niche of discussion on the different impacts that this brings on the environment. In this context, it is proposed to analyze the impact of this process on the low sub-basin of the Tunjuelo river, understanding that its extension turns out to be one of the most significant for Bogotá and its role during the development of the city has been predominant. So, from the theoretical conceptions, the instruments and norms generated and the dynamics presented in the city and the country, an analysis is made of the application of the environmental assessment of this sub-basin and how timely it has been, given the transformations it has undergone .
