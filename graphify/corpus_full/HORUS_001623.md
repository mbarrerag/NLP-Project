# Implementation of valuation adjustments (XVA) an estimate for a recalculation of prices in exchange rate financial derivatives

The need to reconsider and quantify the valuation adjustments in derivative instruments better after the lessons learned from the last major financial crisis. They make this research emerge as an application carried out to the local level in Colombia, through the exchange rate. Thus, it is proposed to deploy an integrated methodology based on the combination of market models and simulations of Monte Carlo. The results evidenced and consolidated for XVA present non negligible impacts, which if not valued are translated directly into opportunity costs in the negotiation and additional risk exposure . There is a pressing need to reassess and more Accurately quantitative valuation adjustments in derivative instruments after the less have been learned since the last major financial crisis. This research requires to apply to the local context in Colombia, focusing on the foreign exchange rate and proposed an integrated methodology means market models with Monte Carlo simulations.
