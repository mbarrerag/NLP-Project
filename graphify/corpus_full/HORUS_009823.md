# Multivariate spatial analysis of enforced disappearance in the context of the Colombian armed conflict: department of Antioquia, 1961-2016

The problem of the violent territories in Colombia has been marked by the absence of state, the permanence of conflicts and violence, as well as by the environmental impacts caused by the exploitation of natural goods. The extraction of raw materials in the hands of various actors, including armed groups, combined with the precarious conditions in which the population lives, has led to a control of the territories and of the space through hostile practices and violations of human rights, for example, enforced disappearance. This research is carried out in this context, focused on carrying out a multivariate spatial analysis of the phenomenon of enforced disappearance in the framework of the Colombian armed conflict, specifically, in the department of Antioquia during the period from 1961 to 2016. The analysis developed seeks to understand how this crime behaves spatially and how it is related to factors of social vulnerability, extractive activities and the presence of armed groups. With the purpose of identifying areas with greater vulnerability to the analyzed crime, and possible areas of disposition of bodies (common pits, illegal cemeters, water bodies, among others), the analysis of spatial patterns, the spatial autocorrelation analysis and of correlations between the forced disappearance and the factors mentioned above, as well as the analysis of regression of local bodies, and the analysis of group, along with the precarious living conditions of the population, has led to control over the territories and space through hostile practices and violations of human rights, such as addressed disappeararances.
