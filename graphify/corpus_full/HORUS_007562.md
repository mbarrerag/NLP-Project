# ANALYSIS OF EXPERIMENTAL MODELS REPORTED FOR DETERMINATION OF ANTIMAL AID ACTIVITY OF VEGETAL SPECIES EMPLOYED IN TRADITIONAL MEDICINE: ASSISTANCE TO MALARIA DATABASE PLANT DATABASE COMMUNITY (MpDBc)

Malaria is an endemic disease in about 95 countries, predominantly in tropical and subtropical regions. Malaria is the subject of extensive attention as a global public health problem, but it persists endemicly in a large number of countries, with approximately 3.2 billion people at risk of malaria, according to WHO data (WHO 2019). The progressive resistance to current antimalarials is a serious problem that has arisen when dealing with malaria. Historically, drugs that have allowed the greatest progress in the fight against malaria have been obtained through substances of plant origin.
