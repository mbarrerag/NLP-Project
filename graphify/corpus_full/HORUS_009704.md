# COMPUTATION ELECTROMAGNETISM

Computer electromagnetism is the process that allows the estimation and analysis of intricate electromagnetic phenomena, which are applied in areas such as telecommunications, electromedicine, renewable energies, among others. This book illustrates this process as an effective approach to the study and understanding of the behavior of electromagnetic fields and their interactions. Thus, the reader acquires capabilities in simulation of computational models, as well as becoming expert in the design and optimization of electromagnetic devices and systems, allowing to develop innovative solutions for real world problems. These capabilities and experiences are being taken into account for professional opportunities in the current industrial sector
