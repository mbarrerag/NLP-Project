# PROMOTION OF TECHNOLOGIES APPROPRIATE TO THE PLURICULTURAL TERRITORY OF THE TRIANGULTURAL RISK DISTRITE OF TOLIMA

The large irrigation districts are systems that seek solutions of water supply and drainage for the improvement of agricultural productivity and generate benefits to the inhabitants of the areas of adaptation. However, they do not always take into account the local culture, the specific environmental conditions of the territory, nor the vernacular technologies of local use.The District of Irrigation Triangle of Tolima is a megaproject that has been planned for more than 50 years and that currently, despite not having been completed nor having been delivered to the users irrigation some 24000 hectares located in the multicultural territory of the municipalities of Coyaima, Natagaima and Purificación, in the Tolima, area of indigenous and peasant settlements. However, there are several aspects that need to be analyzed to understand the challenges and potentialities that the DRTT project imposes, especially factors related to the production systems and the possession of the land, this work proposes some technologies in the identification of the DRTT. The systems of production with the state of the local and rural areas can be strengthened, if the soil conditions are improved and the sustainable management of the natural resources is done
