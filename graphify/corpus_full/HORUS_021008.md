# Decolonization of writing centres

The writing centers (CE) are institutions that work for the communicative skills of students. These centers focus much of their activity in the research exercise and tend to adhere to approaches such as writing through curriculum and writing for disciplines. Thus, by their very structure and questioning the hegemonic principles that govern academia in general, it is necessary to question what is the lens from which the writing centers address communication processes and the production and reproduction of knowledge, specifically referred to historically excluded groups where the dominations and power structures are most strongly situated. In this regard, questions are raised about whether writing centers have worked and are recognized under an inclusive approach, which is to ask whether this conception has reinforced colonial ideas that have traditionally excluded minorities, or whether they have functioned as a regulatory center where the individual is most adapted to the demands of a duty to be linguistic, communicative and dominant scientist
