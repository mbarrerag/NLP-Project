# Reflecting on other practices in professional rehabilitation from the listening of voices and feelings of workers.

This document, called a polyphonic text, was co-constructed from the listening of voices and sentipensators of five colleagues and co-workers who have gone through professional rehabilitation processes as recipients of services. The purpose was to know their experiences in this regard, understand what this process meant, what they felt and what they thought, to then reflect on the other practices in professional rehabilitation, which challenge the dominant paradigms and promote the participation of the working population. Through another methodology, we talked affectively and we gave rise to a dialogue of knowledge among equals, being able to reveal that their voices have been silenced or left aside by favouring the voices of the authors and other subjects of power. In addition, we reflect that their experiences in professional rehabilitation were harsh and unfair; they involved pain, violence, discrimination and few opportunities for participation. Other practices in professional rehabilitation are related to an emancipatory rehabilitation and an ethics of listening, which invite to build heterarchic relationships among all the actors of the process, beturing their pain and understanding, through the establishment of professional opportunities
