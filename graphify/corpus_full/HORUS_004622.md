# REFLECTIONS ON COLLECTIVE HORIZONS AND EMMANCIPATORY PRAXIS IN THE EXPERIENCE OF THE NATIONAL NETWORK OF WOMEN EXCOMBATING INSURGENCY (COLOMBIA)

The political practices and collective actions of ex-combatants constitute a social and political phenomenon that faces serious difficulties in terms of social recognition, partly as a result of stigmatization of their experiences. In this context, this article seeks to propose a reading of the political practices of ex-combatants, demonstrating that they are not reduced to the passage of armed insurgency, but rather find collective channels after the departure of guerrilla groups
