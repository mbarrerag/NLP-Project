# Contribute to the development of an self-emulsifiable system for the release of naringenine

Naringenine is a flavonoid widely available in nature, present in fruits, vegetables, nuts, coffee, tea, red wine, among other diet foods. These matrices generally favor the absorption of the compound and allow high safe doses. Previous research have identified various therapeutic functions of narginine, including immunomodulation and antidiabetic properties, in addition to its therapeutic potential against inflammatory diseases such as sepsis, non-alcoholic fatty liver, arthritis, fibrosis and cancer. In the context of antidiabetic treatment, mechanisms related to Nrf2 and activation of the GMPc dependent on PKGI=945 have been elucidated; Naringenine is a promising candidate for the development of medicamners but has limitations as a low oral bioavailability and aqueous solubility due to its lipophilic nature. Therefore, there is a need to design an oral release system that improves the bioavailability of narin.
