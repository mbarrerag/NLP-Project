# Effect of iron content on deamined clays for the treatment of water contaminated with amoxicillin.

This research focused its attention on the synthesis and physicochemical characterization of the solids obtained from the modification of natural clays by delamination processes, and the subsequent obtaining of supported iron catalysts, to be used in the degradation of amoxicillin present in water. To obtain catalytic supports with optimal textureal and physical chemical properties, a natural sandonite clay from the Cauca Valley – Colombia was selected as starting mineral. This was modified using three methodologies of delamination and characterized by X-ray diffraction (DRX) and sortometry from N2 to 77K. While the catalysts were also characterized by reduction with H2 at programmed temperature. Achieving an increase in the surface area, good distribution of poro and high dispersion of the selected active phase (Fe: 1, 3 and 5%). The catalytic activity of the catalysts was evaluated in the degradation of amoxicillin present in water, followed by liquid chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry.
