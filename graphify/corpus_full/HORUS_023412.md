# INFLUENCE OF THE METHOD OF EXTRACTION IN THE ANALYSIS OF THE FOOD AROMA

The volatile compounds responsible for the aroma of fruits and vegetables are the secondary products of the different metabolic routes. While in fruits these compounds are released or increase their concentration during the maturation process, in plants they are released during cell disruption, usually from non-volatile precursors. When working with essential oils the method of extraction par excellence is distillation by steam trawling; however, to study the aroma of food products, the extraction method should be carefully chosen since on this depends the composition of the final extract. In this conference will be presented as an example the analysis of the aroma of two fruits of the Sapotaceae family, lucuma (Pouteria lucuma) and sapota (Pouteria sapota), compared to the analysis of the aroma of panela water. Emphasizing that the SAFE method (Solvent Assisted Flavor Evaporation) allows to obtain an extract of aroma close to natural because the sample is not heated; whereas the DES method (Destilination by steam-extraction simultaneous with organic solvent) is useful when the aroma is produced by thermal treatment
