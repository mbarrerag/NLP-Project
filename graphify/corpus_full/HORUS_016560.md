# Sowing affections in the territory

Red collects vibrations and resonaries with the continent as mother red earth in the diverse and plural south. Explores and expresses sentients with ecosystems, sowing and food, generating encounters, relations and exchanges. From a need for expanded writing explores relations between humans and non-humans to register in the territory. The ecosomatic gesture arises from the economic-social crisis during the special period of pandemic by Covid 19 and the social outbreak 28A 2021 in Cali. It seeks to make visible food insecurity, food shortages, hunger and social inequality. .
