# RETERRITORIALIZACY FRONTERS IN AMAZONIA

In 2019, in Puerto Nariño (Colombia), a picturesque town on the shores of the Amazon confluence with Loretoyacu, following the reconstruction of the truths about the armed conflict in the Colombian Amazon, a seminar-workshop was organized with indigenous peoples, in which they emphatically affirmed: "Do we not want to talk about environment, but about territory anymore?" This article deals with the relationship between long-term processes aimed at building the Amazonian territory in varied contexts of imperial struggle, the construction of the nation-state and the new contributions of indigenous peoples and communities
