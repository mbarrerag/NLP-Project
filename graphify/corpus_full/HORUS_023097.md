# TERRITORIAL INJUSTMENTS AND THE SEARCH FOR PEACE BUILDING. COLOMBIA: BETWEEN THE GEOGRAPHIES OF DISPOSAL AND EXPERIENCE

This paper is concerned with assessing some of the major territorial problems which the report seeks to solve in the light of the AFP and the progress or setbacks achieved with the implementation process under way. To advance in this direction, the paper proposes three sections. The first, “Disequalities and territorial injustices in Colombia: geografies of dispossession” proposes certain features of the Colombian spatial formation that are intended to be formed under effective implementation of the Haba-nah Agreement. Followed by “The Final Peace Agreement and the search for territorial justice” which presents some ideas that suggest that AFP is a political opportunity to move from a geografi ́as of dispossession towards a new geografi ́as of peace and presents a very succinct balance of what has happened in these five years. Finally, does the Geo-Grafi ́as of hope: communal arrangements for good living?
