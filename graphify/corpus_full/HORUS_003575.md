# REFLECTED SPACE: A PEDAGOLOGICAL-CURATORIAL PROPOSAL FOR THE UNIVERSITY TEACHING OF HISTORICAL MEMORY

The following pages are part of a greater approach to systematization of experiences, which seek to refer strategies of emotional education in relation to environments physical and evocative places of memory from exhibition splits. A work proposal configured as a model of Research of Aula Based on the Arts (IABA), developed in the context of the transition from the university course of Historical Memory Management to the research program of Itinerant School on places of memory. Research program to which it is intended to contribute from the activities of the Semester in memory, heritage and narration of the Research Group Management of Artistic Expressions, Memory and Creation (group attached to the Faculty of Administration of the National University of Colombia), especially those aimed at developing proposals of narrative pedagogies for the recovery of historical memory and local valuation of intra and extra class heritage making use of artistic interventions in non-conventional spaces
