# Transcribomic and physiological approach to the study of molecular mechanisms involved in the key interaction (Dianthus caryophyllus L.) – Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi

The vascular wilting caused by Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi (Fod) in carnation is undoubtedly one of the diseases with the greatest impact on the world flower-cultor sector. The molecular study of this plant-pathogen interaction will allow to propose new strategies for its early diagnosis, control in the crops and provide tools for the genetic design of varieties resistant to the pathogen. In this research is presented the first joint study of physiological parameters and of comparative transcriptomics using RNAseq in this system. From the physiological approach, it was found that the mechanisms deployed by the plants of the resistant variety in study, included stomat closure without affectation in the production of total chlorophyll, neither in the photochemical efficiency of the photosystem II or the production of biomass. Likewise, it was found that, at least in the varieties studied, the response of the foliar is a response to the potential resistance of RGA, the foliar temperature and the oxidative effect of the disease. in crops, and provide tools for the genetic design of varieties resisting to the pathogen.
