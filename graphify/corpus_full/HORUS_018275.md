# ART, MAPS AND RURAL MANAGEMENT: EXPERIENCE OF COLLECTIVE BUILDING OF A PARTICIPATIVE METHODOLOGY FOR THE UPDATE OF EOTS IN THE MUNICIP OF NATIONAL POUNDS

This article presents the collective construction of a participatory methodology for updating the Territorial Planning Schemes (EOT), from a case study in the municipality of Puente Nacional (Santander, Colombia). The experience reflects the tensions between what is posed to the territories from the institutional levels and the daily life of the communities, especially those who live, study and work in the field.
