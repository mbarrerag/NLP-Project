# What is at stake in the game? Continuities and transformations in the meanings involved in the construction of gender identity, socialized in the family environment through the game

This research aims to identify and analyze in two generations of six nuclear families resident in Bogotá of socioeconomic strata 3 and 4, the continuity and transformations in the meanings involved in the construction of gender identity, socialized through the game, in girls and boys from five to ten years in the family. For the development of this study, a constructive approach is adopted in dialogue with the proposal of gender performativity developed by Judith Butler; in this context, it is used as techniques of information collection: the story of life, the encounter at home, the semi-structured interview and the observation participant. As found in this research, it is identified that despite the fact that the games of girls and children in the family stage present profound changes in the technological advances, the meanings involved in the construction of gender identity socialized in two generations present subtle changes. This phenomenon is given by the permanence of gender fiction as a cultural brand that is incorporated into the bodies thanks to the devices of anticipation and repetition, through which the institutionalization of the female and male behavior is continuously updated. -structured interview and participant observation.
