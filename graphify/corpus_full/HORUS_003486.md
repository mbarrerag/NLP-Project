# Factors affecting staff turnover in the companies Herragro, Sicolsa and Toptec de Manizales: An approach from data analytics

This research examines the phenomenon of turnover of staff in three manufacturing companies in Manizales, using analytics data. The turnover of staff, understood as continuous replacement of employees, is a critical challenge that has a negative impact on productivity, costs and organizational stability. The study is based on the idea that data analysis can provide the companies studied with a greater understanding of the factors that influence the rotation, allowing to make strategic and informed decisions. The main objective of this study is to identify, through natural language processing techniques, the key causes of turnover in the companies analyzed. To do this, a mixed methodological approach is used, which combines the qualitative analysis of the interviews with the quantitative modeling of the data analytics, where 435 withdrawal interviews were processed between 2022 and 2023. The results conclude that the adoption of the analytics in the management of the human person will be able to develop, in addition to the expected factors of turnover and the employee cuts. more strategic and reported decisions.
