# Impact of technical studies on the scope, time and cost of public works contracts in the Department of Arauca, in force 2015 and 2016

This work aims to study the planning stage of the construction contract, emphasizing the management process of the technical studies, in order to determine how these affect the results of the scope, time and cost foreseen for the public works. To this effect, an analysis of the technical studies and the results of compliance with the scope, time and costs foreseen in 73 works contracts concluded by the Department of Arauca, during the 2015 and 2016 public tender. The results indicate that the studies and designs in 48.4% come from external professionals who have no contractual link with the administration, through the figure of donation, assignment or with unknown origin, which generates that 65.75% of the projects present alterations in their scope, 13.70 % delays in the delivery of the work and 5.48 % overcosts, figures higher than those achieved with the technical studies coming from a consultancy contract.
