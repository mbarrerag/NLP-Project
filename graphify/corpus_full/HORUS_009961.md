# Case study: The TERRITORIAL PRESENCE OF CRIME ORGANISED IN COLOMBO-ECUATORIAN FRONT, NARIGNAL AND PUTUMAY DEPARTMENTS (1990-2016).

In this chapter, the authors strive, from a geohistorical perspective, for an articulated understanding of organized crime with illegal economies, in correlation with the armed actors of the conflict and also articulating the border dynamics of the south, particularly with the Republic of Ecuador, in the departments of Nariño and Putumayo, with a special emphasis on the urban area of the municipality of Tumaco (Nariño). This analysis supports their empirical evidence in the application of methodological strategies derived from spatial analysis and thematic cartography, combined with the results derived from the interviews applied in the study region, without giving up establishing the necessary relationship with the national context of organized crime and the actions of the actors of the armed conflict
