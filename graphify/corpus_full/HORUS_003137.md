# ASSESSMENT OF DEPURATOR PLACE CARBONIZED AS NUTRIENTS SOURCES AND AGRICULTURAL AMENDMENT

Biosolids are a residue of wastewater treatment plants (PTAR) that have been used as a source of nutrients and for soil recovery due to their organic matter content and nutrients such as nitrogen and phosphorus, however, their direct application presents limitations due to the presence of pathogenic microorganisms and pollutants as heavy metals. By pyrolysis the biosolids are transformed into carbonized and the microbial load is eliminated and the pollutants are reduced or their release is therefore a potentially safer alternative for the use of these materials. Despite this, research on the subject is scarce therefore there is a need to deepen the impact that these carbonized have on the soils. Thus, the aim of this study was to evaluate the effect of the carbonised application of biosolids of the PTAR Salitre (Bogota) and the organically controlled soil.
