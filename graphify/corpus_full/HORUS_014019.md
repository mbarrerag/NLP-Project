# Trends in appropriation of Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence in undergraduate Engineering programs in Colombia.

The Education in Engineering faces several challenges in relation to the Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence between which they are stated: the digital divide with limited access to high speed connectivity and digital technologies in rural areas that limits the opportunities to use emerging technologies, the lack of training and adequate education in digital technologies and skills related to the good use of artificial intelligence tools, the insufficient investment in Research and Development R & D, the lack of knowledge and appropriation for the good use of emerging technologies in the academic community. The increasing dependence on digital technologies and Artificial Intelligence increases the exposure to cybersecurity risks and privacy problems and the need for strategies that integrate Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence for digital social inclusion as set out in the National Policy document for Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence in Colombia in the CONPES 3975 (National Council of Economic and Social Policy, 2019). To contribute to the solution of these problematic programs, this project has as general objective to characterize the appropriation of Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence in the Engineering programs in Colombia, which leads to the evaluation of the current status of digital inclusion, and teaching. integrate Digital Transformation and Artificial Intelligence into its curriculum structure
