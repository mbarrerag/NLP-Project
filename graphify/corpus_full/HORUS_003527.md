# Agroclimatic modeling of the water requirement of oil palm cultivation (Elais guineensis Jacq.) in the department of Cesar, Colombia

The limited availability of water in the department of Cesar (Colombia) and the high water consumption associated with the lack of knowledge of water requirements and low efficiency irrigation systems in the cultivation of oil palm (Elaeis guineensis Jacq.) have affected the inefficient use of water resources and the impact of crop productivity. The main objective of this work was to determine the water demand for oil palm cultivation in areas with homogeneous agroclimatic conditions in the department of Cesar. For this purpose, the Department's climate information was obtained through meteorological stations and satellite climate data. Also, the water demand for this crop was determined by obtaining the cultivó coefficient (Kc) in plants of two ages, 10 and 15 years, using the water balance methodology, obtaining a Kc of 0.91 and 0.84 respectively. As a result of the zoning, 10 agroclimatic zones were identified in the department, of which 8 were suitable for the cultivation of oil palm.
