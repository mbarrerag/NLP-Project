# Estimate of pore pressure from seismic speeds in the Magdalena Middle Valley Basin.

The basin of the Valle Medio del Magdalena presents zones of over pressure evidenced in the drilling of oil wells, in which to different formations and depths the behavior of the pressure is abnormal, which generates problems of instability, presence of gases, oil stains, water influences and differential pastes, giving as a consequence unproductive times and high operating costs. An important case in this basin is that of the Lizama 158 well, which, due to the overpressure generated an environmental emergency with affectation of the flora and fauna in an extension of 24 kilometers in the correction La Fortuna in the Colombian District of Barrancabermeja, in 2018. Predicting the overpressure from the identification of the anomaly in the P wave before perforcing the well is of vital error, not only in the lutistic intervals, but by the opposite, it is fundamental to analyze the overpressure in the calcareous formations, as the calizas and carbonates present in the formation The Moon, since with the conventional methods this has not been possible, because they apply only to the lutistic and arclicose formations. in the methodology of geo-pressure calculation, especially in the formation The Moon which is composed mostly of calcareous rocks, with intercalations of calcareous lutita, microtes, biomicrotes and lodolites in which the previous conventional methods do not have a good prediction
