# NATION AND RACIALIZATION IN THE ARCHYPIELAGE OF SAN ANDRES AND PROVIDENCE DURING THE FIRST YEARS OF THE NATIONAL INTENDENCE.

The process of nationalization that the Intendencia de San Andrés y Providencia experiences in the first half of the 19th century is explored, and as in this process of incorporation of this archipelago into the Colombian nation, racialized accounts of the island society are constructed, as well as inferiorizing visions of its capacities to promote the autonomous, political and economic development of its territory. In this sense, some categories are constructed by agents of the continental government that legitimize the political subordination process of the islanders and at the same time border the territory and make it an object of economic greed and seek to insert it into the national market.
