# Non-technical managerial skills required for the technically trained manager.

The non-technical management skills (HGNT) are one of the success factors for companies or their lack will lead to the failure of their managers. This research aimed to propose the managerial skills necessary to promote the success of a manager with technical training in small and medium enterprises in the cities of Bogotá and Medellín. The problem is that professionals with technical training face the consequences of not having developed the managerial skills not technical, the stronger the technical skills tend to weaken the non-technical skills such as communication, delegation and human management, which negatively affects the working environment and leads to the loss of time and money to the companies. The research had a methodological process of quantitative type, non-experimental design and descriptive type and was based on theoretical references for the identification of these skills. The data collection tools used were surveys and the target group of work with the purpose of classifying, selecting and proposing the necessary skills. The research found relationships between the characteristics of the managers, their living conditions and the labor needs for the development of non-technical management skills. One of the findings is that the interpersonal skills of the technical ones are most important to develop the training models
