The University City of Bogotá [CUB], of the National University of Colombia, is a living testimony of the architectural evolution of Colombia and, more importantly, of the conception of the first university city of the country. Although the CUB has been documented as a whole on several occasions and some individual buildings, there are buildings that still do not present specific and detailed studies, among them is the case of the Chapel Christ Master, which combines in meritorious form the technique and aesthetics, but has been partially forgotten in the documents of the architectural history of the city.
