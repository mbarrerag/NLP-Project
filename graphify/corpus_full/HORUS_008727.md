# Chemical study of cytotoxic compounds present in caimaron grape fruit (Purouma cecropiifolia)

The fruit of caimaron grapes (Pourouma cecropiifolia) was analyzed with a view to identifying the compounds with cytotoxic activity in relation to different human tumor cell lines. To this end, epicarp, mesocarpium and fruit seeds were analyzed separately, which were lyophilised and the corresponding residues were extracted with an aceton-water mixture (7:3, v/v). The extracts of each part of the fruit were subjected to partition with growing polarity solvents: pentane, dichloromethane, ethyl acetate, butanol and water, to obtain five subfractions in each case. In a preliminary study used the MTT method (modified) on the A549 line, it was identified that the fractions of ethyl acetate of the epicarp (Acetaptipul), as well as the fractions of ethyl of the mesocarpium were used as the main ethyl acetol (Acetaptopul), as the fractions of ethyl acid, as the cell, as the fractions of the HT-29, RKO and A549.
