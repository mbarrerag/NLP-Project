# LEARNING ABOUT CLIMATE ACTION, HAIR AND CARBON MARKET BY A SERIES - BONOPOLYC GAME

Climate change is a real problem that has not been fully aware of; global warming increases the temperature of the earth, alters the usual balance of nature, ecosystems and all forms of life.Governments, organizations, academia and society in general play a fundamental role in the purpose of mitigating this phenomenon and should be committed to all. In this context, the academy is called to take a leading role in the training of people aware of the problem, who can propose and implement concrete actions of solution. On the other hand, serious games are specifically designed games with a learning purpose, which allow the teaching of various topics while participants have fun and that present a valuable opportunity for training in topics such as climate change. This research proposes the design of a serious game focused on climate change training, footprint and carbon markets, which, moreover, allows to raise awareness about the actions of the organizations and show alternatives to mitigate the problem. For this, a methodology of serious games composed by three fundamental phases, which guides the designers in an iterative and friendly process.
