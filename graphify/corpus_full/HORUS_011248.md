# Molecular prevalence of the bovine leukosis virus in specialized dairy companies in Antioquia, Colombia

The milk production systems are a sensitive sector of the primary economy, frequently affected by pathogens that negatively impact productive parameters, being the bovine leukosis virus (BLV) one of these. In this study, the molecular prevalence to BLV was determined in the specialized dairy of Antioquia by means of the viral marker of the envelope gene (env). Blood sample was taken from 575 cattle from specialized dairy companies of Antioquia distributed in 53 herds, located in the three specialized milk production areas of Antioquia (north, east and valley of Aburra). DNA extraction was carried out by Salting out and a nested PCR was performed for detection of the env gene. The products were visualized in a 2 % agarose gel with GelRed as an intercalante. A molecular prevalence was found to BLV of 17.0% in animals and 71.7% in herds, with the Aborra Valley being the area where the highest rate of positive animals was obtained (21.1%), unlike the northern one that had the lowest animal (15.6%).
