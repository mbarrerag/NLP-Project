# CAPACITY OF FRONTMENT AND ADAPTING IN ADOLECTS WITH TYPE 1 DIABETS

type 1 diabetes mellitus is a disease that commonly occurs in adolescence. Likewise, people diagnosed with this disease experience daily stress in relation to the care and treatment they require and the deployment of adaptation and coping strategies. to describe the capacity to cope and adapt in adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus. quantitative study, descriptive cross-sectional cut. A non-probabilistic sample was performed to convenience that included 100 adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus diagnosis. The inclusion criteria that they had to meet were: age, period of care, care in two health-care institutions in the city of Manizales, signature of consent and informed consent. Sociodemographic variables were measured and the scale of the process was applied to face and adaption, abbreviated version in Spanish. adolescents with type 1 diabetes mellitus showed a level of coping and average adaptation of 55%.
