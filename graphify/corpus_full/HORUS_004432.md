# CHAPTER 1: ANALYSIS OF THE INNOVATION PROCESS IN LATIN AMERICA FROM METHODOLOGICAL COMBINATION OF THE MODELATION BASED ON AGENTS AND THE ANALYSIS OF SOCIAL NETWORKS

The formulation of public policies of science, technology, innovation and society (CTIS) in the context of Latin America (AL), considered a region of emerging economies (World Bank, 2019), assumes a leading role for economic growth and to ensure that developing countries are involved in global dynamics of a commercial, scientific, technological, environmental, multicultural nature, among others (Velasco, 2016).This context is also framed by constraints associated with meeting needs of a more basic level, most of these nations have important challenges in the areas of poverty, food, coverage of public services, transport, health, education, public order, among others (Cros Flores, 2017).Although these problems can be leveraged from the CTIS policies, they do not wait for their attention, conditioning the priorities of the political agenda of the nations.The decision makers are those who face this scenario (Bonvecchi and Scartascini, 2020). an appropriate combination and contextualized in AL. Subsequently, the discussion is presented as to the scope, limitations and recommendations for the application of the method, some cases of use of the proposed combination are presented and, finally, conditions are posed for a possible future work agenda
