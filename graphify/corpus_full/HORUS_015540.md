# Data analysis Pseudo-Panel applied to educational returns in Bogotá D.C.

This paper analyzes an alternative to overcome the main limitation of applying the data panel methodology, which is the absence of longitudinal data. In the 1980s, Deaton (1985) conceived the way to link the data panel methodology with the information collected through cross-sectional studies. The result was the pseudo-panel data methodology. This work aims to explore the pseudo-panel data methodology and be a guide for its application. The main conclusion of the work is that the estimator proposed by Verbeek and Nijman (1993) turns out to be the best estimator in terms of the average quadratic error. Finally, an application related to the estimation of returns to education in Bogotá is carried out with the information from the Great Integrated Household Survey (GEIH) carried out by the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE)
