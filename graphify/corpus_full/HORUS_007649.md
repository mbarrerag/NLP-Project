# Design of Strategies for Integrating Research into the Public Accounts Curriculum

This project aims to strengthen the research seedbeds in Public Accounts by integrating strategies that promote effective articulation between research and academic curriculum. It will focus on identifying tools and methodologies relevant in literature to incorporate research in an integrated manner into the Public Accounts program. In addition, the impact of research seeding on the development of critical and professional competencies of students will be analyzed, assessing how these experiences can enrich their academic training and preparation for the labor market. As part of the project activities, collaborations will be established to allow the strengthening of international academic networks and the expansion of methodological and theoretical perspectives of the research team, through research stays and presentation of results at events. Regular meetings will be organized among the project members to discuss progress, share results and explore new opportunities in accounting research. In this order of ideas, the semi-millero will seek to promote the active participation of students in academic outreach spaces, such as congresses and seminars, where they will be able to present their findings and receive valuable feedback. This initiative not only seeks to improve the communication and presentation skills of students, but also to position the research work in Publicloan in an international context, contributing to the enrichment of knowledge
