# SUSTAINABILITY VIEW FROM PHYSICAL INGENERY, A VISION FROM TERRITORIES IN THE FRAMEWORK OF REVOLUTION 4.0

SENA and the academic sector in general have worked hard on the third version of this event, continuing a high-impact cycle on technologies that contribute to sustainability, digitization and access to efficient mobility for all communities. This objective, which comes from the first version of the event, has proved to generate a very valuable social impact in order to achieve not only the objectives of the country's energy matrix but also to further promote activities that bring us closer to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals
