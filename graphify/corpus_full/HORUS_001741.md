# Effect of the progressive Fe-substitution in the A-site of the BaTiO 3 : Wet chemical synthesis, systematic characterization and photocatalytic performance

BaTiO3 is a ceramic semiconductor with a significant photocatalytic performance. However, it presents a wide bandgap energy, requiring UV irradiation for applications in pollutant degradation. Therefore, BaTiO3 materials with a progressive Fe-substitution by Ba were synthesized for the first time using a sol-gel method with subsequent calcination at 700 °C for 3 h. The systematic characterization revealed an exclusive insertion of Fe into the A-site, which led to a progressive consolidation of the tetragonal phase, a lower bandgap energy, and an increased ferromagnetic behavior. The samples exhibited the coexistence of Fe2+ and Fe3+ ions near the surface, suggesting the involvement of the Fe3+/Fe2+ transition in decreasing the band gap and preventing the recombination of photogenerated electron-hole pairs. Consequently, the progressive addition of Fe improved the photocatalytic performance for MB degradation under UV and simulated solar light, with a significant contribution from the •OH radicals in the photocatalytic mechanism. Thus, this study demonstrates that Fe-substitution at the A-site of BaTiO3 enhances the photocatalytic properties, making the resulting materials promising candidates for advanced water treatment
