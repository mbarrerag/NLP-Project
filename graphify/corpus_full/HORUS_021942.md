# Integrated management model for Caldas municipal music schools: towards valuation as a cultural good and systemic service.

The present final master's work consists of a research and development project for the municipal music schools of the department of Caldas, which seeks to answer the following research question: What elements needs a participatory management model with and for the municipal music schools of Caldas that strengthens them and consolidates them as a systemic good and service of cultural, social, political and economic value? To give answer, this research was carried out in the department of Caldas, with the participation of the directors and teachers of the municipal music schools of Aguadas, Anserma, La Dorada, Manzanares, Marquetalia, Páncola, Pennsylvania, Riosucio, Supia, Viterbo and in coordination with the coordination of the Departmental Plan of Music of the Department of Culture. Through the application of three methodological moments distributed in the following way: i) Technical review, ii) collection and analysis of data obtained through semi-structured interviews to the directors and teachers, which served to identify the capacities, limiting, projections and characteristics of the implemented management models of the schools, and iii) design and validation of the municipal model. In addition, network integration strategies and ongoing reviews are proposed on the processes that work best according to the municipality, users, their expectations and those of the school itself
