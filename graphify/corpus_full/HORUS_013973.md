# Custom system design and assembly for the characterization of polymeric supports for fabric engineering

By means of tissue engineering it is possible to assist medical treatments in patients who due to diseases or traumas present damage in tissues. For these therapeutic treatments, tissue engineering develops support structures with materials that favor cellular processes such as migration, adhesion, proliferation and differentiation, among others. Among the diversity of properties that must possess these supports, are the morphological and mechanical properties that are highly interdependent. Research has been developed that seek to study the relationship between the structure and the mechanical performance of these supports using conventional uniaxial stress testing machines. Such research univocally report the influence of the initial structure on the mechanical performance of the supports used in tissue engineering, which has led to adjustments in the processes of obtaining, seeking optimal manufacturing routes. However, we do not find report of tests of these supports that allow, during stress tests (i) characterize in situ the variation of their structure, (ii) mapear in situ the state of deformations, (iii) characterize the effect of the anisotropia of the structure in its structure, to establish the technical and deformation. To study the changes of surface texture using a confocal laser microscope, also of custom construction. Thus, this project seeks the development of a system with the following subsystems of test and analysis, all of them of custom construction at home: (i) machine that allows to perform tests of voltage optimized to the characteristics of the supports used in engineering of tissues, (ii) optical extensometer that allows, by conventional optical microscopy and image analysis system, to determine in situ the two-dimensional state of deformations in the support, with micrometric resolution, and (iii) confocal laser microscope that allows to study in high resolution the variation of surface texture of the support as a cause of the efforts applied in the stress test. For the development of the project, besides involving the students of research seedling, a strategic alliance has been formed between the groups of applied electronic and optical microscopy, both of the Department of Physics, the company Combustion Engineers and the Workshop of Mechanics Fina, of the Department of Physics.
