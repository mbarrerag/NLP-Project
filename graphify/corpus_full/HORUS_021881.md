# Evaluation of vessel location strategy, using compressed acoustic signals.

This master's thesis presents the implementation in simulated environment of a strategy of reduction of information and location of objectives, based on the processing of underwater acoustic signals using the methods of Matrix Pencil (MPM) and reverse time (TR). The analysis of the relationship between the reduction of data and the reliability in the reconstruction of signal provided by MPM for signals from real ships, taking into account parameters of processing time and signal correlation. The data reduction is carried out in order to optimally use the bandwidth of a wireless channel that facilitates communication between several sensors and a central node, in which the localization process is then carried out. Once the specific characteristics of the signals are determined and then the application of MPM, it is sought to use the information from each sensor to calculate the location of the objectives using TR. Likewise, the effectiveness of the method is evaluated considering variations in the characteristics of the reconstruction provided by MPM and taking into account random noise factors present in the process of signal propagation through the medium
