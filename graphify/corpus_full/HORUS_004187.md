# Vision of labour-educational policy from the right of the Kokonuko indigenous people - Puracé reserve in the Colombian Cauca

In this work, built on a dogmatic legal basis, supported by ILO Convention 169 (Convention on Indigenous and Tribal Peoples), the laws and decisions of the high Colombian courts that incorporate, develop and promote it within the Colombian legal system, it is analyzed how, despite its ratification since August 7, 1991, after 32 years, the government and the indigenous communities have not achieved labor agreements in the face of the links, stability and prerogatives of ethno-educators and/or dynamizers in the face of the administration of the SEIP (Indigenous Education System Propio). This anomia has forced indigenous peoples and, in particular, the Kokonuko people of the Puracé reservation in the Colombian Cauca to blame and inadvertise the patterns imposed by the national government that do not know their own. From this proposal that includes conceptual elements between decolonial thinking and multiculturality, there are suggested patterns of dialogue and consultation, which would allow removing obstacles to the proper implementation of the SEIP and to overcome tensions between the government and peoples facing the development of the work of the People-docent (Text from the source and from the legal relationship). guidelines imposed by the national government that ignore their own. Based on this proposal, which contemplates conceptual elements together which decolonial thinking and multiculturalism stand out, guidelines for dialectic relations and concertation are suggested, which would allow the removal of obstacles to the appropriate implementation of the IES andward off tensions between the governance and the people regarding the development of ethno-teaching work
