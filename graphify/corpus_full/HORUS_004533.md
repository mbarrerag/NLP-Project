# INTEGRATION OF HUMAN CAPABILITIES INTO ORGANIZATIONAL MANAGEMENT

How to integrate and develop talent in organizations continues to be a great challenge for labor relations units, every time turnover, attrition, rework, deviations in the organizational climate and culture, lack of relevance and belonging in the performance of roles and many other things increase, which are far from being explained in the theory and practices of each organization. Although many authors have developed human resource management models and have adjusted the Chiavenato model, the feed-provision subsystem (recruitment and selection) does not in itself guarantee the best provision to fill job vacancies. For this reason, and in view of the premises previously stated as obstacles to this process, it is necessary to recognize the importance and legitimacy that this type of practice gives to the future of organizations
