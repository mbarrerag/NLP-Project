# Analysis of the denaturation of the extension of the jurisprudence of the Council of State to third parties; related to the actions carried out by the administrative authorities.

The extension of case law was conceived as an effective mechanism to contribute to judicial decongestion and prevent unnecessary litigation in the jurisdiction of administrative litigation, to obtain recognition of complaints at the administrative headquarters, and to strengthen respect for judicial precedents by administrative officials of the State. However, these objectives and purposes for which the figure was designed have had certain drawbacks to their materialization, due to several factors not considered during their formulation and implementation, such as unjustified rigidity and other conduct carried out by administrative authorities. Thus, the present research work aims to determine whether the actions carried out by administrative activities at the administrative headquarters distort the purposes of the request for extension of State Council jurisprudence to third parties
