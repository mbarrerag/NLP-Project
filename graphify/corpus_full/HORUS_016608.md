# SEMIPARAMETRIC MODELATION OF CARRYOVER EFFECTS IN CRESSOVER DESIGNS WITH REPEATED MEASURES

In this work, a model for crossover designs is constructed with repeated measurements within each observation period by means of an extension of generalized estimation equations that includes parametric effects for design effects and nonparametric effects for time and carryover effects that the data might have. Splines approach nonparametric estimation, demonstrating theoretical and practical properties by means of simulation exercises on the usual models where the absence of carryover effects is assumed or modeled by usual regression. In theoretical properties it is demonstrated that the solution is obtained in a similar way to weighted minimum squares. Therefore, diagnoses of the model can be made similar to those of a classical regression and demonstrate its effectiveness in the application exercise on systolic arterial pressure data
