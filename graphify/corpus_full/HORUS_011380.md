# VALLEDUPAR, 2015-2016

This book presents the reflections of a group of professionals who took part in the studies and designs for the remodeling of the building, the surrounding areas and the assets of the Governorate of Cesar, carried out through the inter-administrative contract signed between the National University of Colombia and the Governorate of Cesar in February 2013. It also presents the results of urban and architectural designs that reflect the intention to strengthen the relationship between architecture and nature, to qualify the noble plane of the city, to renew the local symbols and to recover the public and private (institutional) space of universal access, for the development of collective and inclusive activities, which encourage the exercise and construction of citizenship that give meaning to the institutions of the State
