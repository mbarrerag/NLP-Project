# Konkreta. Web series of concerts of sound art

Konkreta is a research-creation project that seeks to make a television series for web with a quality that can be used on conventional television on the theme of sound art (8 chapters of 25 minutes each, although the duration can several after the collective process of design). Specifically, the format would be inspired by the tradition of television musical programs where presentations are combined with interviews and public participation but where the guest act would be a person with recognition in the field of sound art. Thus, the mentioned format is recontextualized using an artistic expression that has been considered as niche and difficult to access by the great public so it has little diffusion. Thus, a kind of virtuous circle would be formed where the device of sound art presentations would allow to experiment with the text from the visual and auditory to look for other forms and at the same time the format of television series would offer a window of listening to a non-massive sound genre and that has even come to be defined as anti-mous.
