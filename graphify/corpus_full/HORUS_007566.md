# Continuous time models with random coefficients: theory and applications in finance

The purpose of this research project is to extend the econometric model in continuous time with random coefficients considered by Tao et al. (2019) and Hoyos (2024) to a more general case where serial correlation is allowed in random coefficients. Likewise, to evaluate its application and performance in the area of finance. To do this, it is intended to propose a model considering a matrix of random coefficients that follows a first-order self-regressive process, derive a method of estimating the parameters of the model, study the performance of the method in finite samples using a Monte Carlo simulation exercise, perform an application in finance and compare the performance of the proposed model with alternative methodologies
