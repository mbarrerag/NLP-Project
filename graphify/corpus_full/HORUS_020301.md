# The monetary regime of inflation targeting and its impact on the functional distribution of income in Colombia

This research presents a reflection on inflation targeting monetary regimes and their virtual impacts on the functional distribution of income. For this, the post-Keynesian model of Rochon and Setterfield is taken as a starting point, which incorporates, on the one hand, an endogenous view of money, and, on the other, a Kaleckian perspective of distributive conflict. In this sense, the working hypothesis aims to indicate the regressive impact of monetary policies that take as their exclusive objective the containment of inflation through countercyclical measures. To give empirical consistency to the model, critical elements of it are exposed, which allow an adaptation to the economic structure of a country like Colombia, while suggesting an econometric estimation through VAR models (Vector autoregression)
