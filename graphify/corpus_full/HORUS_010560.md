# CLINICAL AND GENETIC APPROACHES TO NON-SYNDROMIC CLEFT LIP AND/PR PALATE ANDVAN DER WOUDE SYNDROME FROM THE EXPERIENCE WITH A DOMINICAN FAMILY

To describe clinical and genetic characteristics of non-syndromic cleft lip and/orpalate and Van der Woude Syndrome, based on the research experience with a Dominicanfamily aff ected with cleft lip and/or palate with autosomal dominant inheritance Methods : A Dominican family with cleft lip and/or palate with autosomal dominantinheritance is analyzed molecularly and clinically, by means of clinical evaluation, genealogyanalysis according to Bennett RL. et al., and, exome and complete genome analysis in trio(laboratory process performed by Centogen). A Dominican family with four generations aff ected by cleft lip and/or palate isdescribed. Exome and trio whole genome analysis was performed on the proband, femalewith left cleft lip and palate and lower lip pit, her carrier mother with palatal torus andhealthy father, under diff erential diagnosis of non-syndromic cleft lip and/or palate/VanderWoude Syndrome. Whole exome analysis did not yield positive results, whole genomeanalysis indicated loss of 1 copy of 10,349 kb with deletion of exons 1 - 3 of the IRF6 gene Conclusions : This is the fi rst report of a copy number variant with deletion of exons 1-3 ofthe IRF6 gene, variants in IRF6 have been associated with non-syndromic cleft lip and/orpalate and Van der Woude syndrome (diff erential diagnoses in the family)
