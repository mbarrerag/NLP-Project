# Progress in the research of intestinal bacterial populations of Cavia porcellus

The cuy (Cavia porcellus) is a small monogastric mammal of the family Caviidae originating from the Andes. This species is distributed in several countries of South America, where it has gained recognition for its aptitude as a species of promisiary and emerging livestock production. In Colombia, the breeding of the species is mainly located in the department of Nariño, where it is an important part of the culture, gastronomic diversity and economic sustenance of many families in both urban and rural sector.
