# PUBLIC ADMINISTRATION IN THE DIGITAL ERA — TRANSFORMATIONS, OBSTACLES AND OPPORTUNITIES

The introduction of new technology for document production transforms administrative work. The health crisis of 2020 has allowed an acceleration of the change of the mode of domination and production in the administrative function. Thus, after the bureaucratic domination that was installed at the beginning of the twentieth century, partially displaced by a domination of management type at the end of the same century, with digital technology a new domination, platform type, based on information is being imposed. It is aware of these transformations, it identifies opportunities, obstacles and risks linked to this transformation and concludes with considerations that relate the digitalization of administration to surveillance and democracy
