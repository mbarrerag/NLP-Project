# PROPERTIES OF SOLUTIONS: CONDUCTIVITY, ACIDITY AND BASICITY

In this chapter, the use of a simulator to address the subject matter of the dissolutions is proposed. The general objectives are: To review and understand some concepts related to acidic and basic solutions; to understand how the pH measurement of an aqueous solution is performed using a potentiometer and universal indicator paper; to understand how pH and pOH values are obtained; and to examine changes in electrolytic conductivity in different acid and weak bases solutions
