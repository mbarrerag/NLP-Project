# COLOMBIAN CONSENSUS OF NEUROENDOCRIN TUMORS INTESTINAL AND CARCINOIDE SYNDROME

intestinal neuroendocrine tumors (NETs) develop from specialized cells of the diffuse endocrine system and may have the ability to release, in an excessive way, serotonin and other vasoactive peptides, causing symptoms that are collectively called carcinoid syndrome.Intestinal NETs are heterogeneous in their biology and clinical course, so their histopathological diagnosis offers challenges for pathologists and the images that are required for staging and follow-up have technical particularities, together with a limited availability of some of them.In addition, due to their low incidence, the possibilities of clinical suspicion in primary care centers are diminished, often causing a delay in diagnosis. All these factors make the path for diagnosis and decisions about treatment complex.Objective: define the criteria for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of patients with secretive intestinal TNEs in Colombia.Methodology: a modified Delphi type was made. To this end, a panel of Colombian physicians, experts in the management of neuroendocrine neoplasias were selected, and analyzed, where the conclusions were obtained from the diagnosis of the patient were obtained
