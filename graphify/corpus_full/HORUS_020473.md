# Bees of the genus Eufriesea Cockerell, 1908 (Apidae: Euglossini): Update on presence and distribution in Colombia

In this research, the current state of knowledge of the genus Eufriesea was evaluated for Colombia, including the taxonomy of the group and the records of presence and distribution for the country were updated, through the search for recent scientific literature, the use of distribution databases such as GBIF, ScanBug and IdigBIO, as well as the review of the main entomological collections of the country. The results obtained propose a taxonomic key for the species found and with possible distribution in Colombia, as well as the first taxonomic keys for the groups of species are proposed.
