# HYBRID METHODOLOGY FOR SINTONIZATION OF STOECASTICAL GLOBAL OPTISMS WITH MATLAB-R

Metaheuristics are stochastic global optimization algorithms that could solve the problem of estocasting parameters efficiently and quickly. These optimizers contain hyper-parameters that control their behavior in the search space and therefore should be tuned to improve their performance. This paper presents a hybrid methodology of interconnection tuning of Matlab® and R® through the R.matlab and irace packages. As a case study, there is the problem of estimating parameters for a highly nonlinear xylitol bioproduction mathematical model with 5 states and 11 parameters. The performance of metaheuristics (i.e. genetic algorithms, particle swarm optimization and simulated tempering) is compared with the methodology proposed with respect to their default configurations. It was found that tuning significantly improves reproducibility and robustness in the solution of the optimization problem. Finally, guidelines are proposed for the tuning of stochastic global optimization algorithms in Matlab®
