# Urban renewal and public space: an analysis of individual accessibility

This thesis analyzes the transformation of accessibility to public space in two areas of Bogotá—the Third Millennium Park (former "Cartucho") and the José Antonio Sucre Park ("The Hippies") in Chapinero—in the context of urban renewal processes driven by competitiveness dynamics. Through a qualitative approach combining landscape analysis, interviews and documentary review, it examines how these changes have affected traditionally marginalized actors, such as street dwellers, waste pickers and sex workers. .
