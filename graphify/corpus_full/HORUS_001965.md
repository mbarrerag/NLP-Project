# Design of a digital marketing model for the postgraduate area, case: Universidad Nacional sede Manizales, postgraduates in administration

This paper proposes the design of an integral model of digital marketing aimed at the area of postgraduate courses in management of the National University of Manizales. The aim is to develop effective strategies that allow to increase the visibility, uptake and retention of students interested in postgraduate programs in administration, specifically focused on strengthening the digital presence of the university and improving the attraction of qualified candidates for its academic programs. To achieve this purpose, an analysis is carried out of the digital environment and the educational market in the area of postgraduates in administration. This analysis includes the identification of current trends and preferences of potential students, as well as the evaluation of online competence . This paper proposals the design of a comprehensive digital marketing model encouraged at the postgraduate area of management at the National University Manizales campus. The objective is to develop effective strategies to increase visibility, involvement, and retention of students inters in postgraduation management programs, specifically innovatively innovative leadership
