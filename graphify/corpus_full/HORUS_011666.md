# Inequality (en) and poverty, persistent problems in Colombia: reflections for an urgent agenda

Colombia is one of the most unequal countries in Latin America, which is at the same time the most unequal region in the world. Although this discouraging panorama is not new, it has taken a significant place on the national political agenda, awakening numerous expectations among the citizens who have been the losers of the time: the poor. This work investigates the challenges faced by the newly possessed government of Gustavo Petro to address the persistent conditions of inequality and poverty suffered by more than half of the population. It starts with the question of the challenges and governmental proposals to reduce inequality, while contextualizing the Colombian case in the global and regional socio-economic situations that affect the possibility of carrying out structural changes in social policy. Although this work recognizes a wide range of inequalities present in the territory such as gender, age, ethnic and racial gaps, it will nevertheless concentrate mainly on income inequalities. The analysis is carried out based on consultation of specialized sources on the subject and the follow-up to fluctuations in inequality in Latin America and the country in the last decade
