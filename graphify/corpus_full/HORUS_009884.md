# The mutation of the principles of state procurement by Colombian regulations Buy Efficient.

New information technologies have generated an evolution in the form of human interaction, both in the private and in the state sphere, in the latter, platforms have been standardized to guarantee the state purposes, with the particularity of allowing interaction with citizens, which is a gross way to be an advantage for the efficiency and transparency of public actions. And in terms of public procurement, one of the main needs of individuals is met, for this reason, the Colombian Agency Buys Efficient - the CCE- has established guides, terms and conditions, for users to learn, understand, and use platforms, even when these platforms present failures, protocols of indisponibility have been established. Hence, this research through first-level hermeneutics analyzes the evolution of electronic commerce, its principles, the implementation of these in the states of the region, and the role of CCE as an actor of the electronic state procurement in Colombia; together with this, the debates of interpretative theories of law, jurisprudence, norm and doctrine are examined to investigate whether CCE has exceeded the regulatory power, and has altered the principles that guide the public procurement in Colombia
