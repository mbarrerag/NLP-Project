# Changes to the audit report and expectations gap: an analysis for companies listed in Colombia

The recent financial crises and corporate frauds of recognised companies have increasingly affected the role of the auditor. The different interpretations of the limits and responsibilities of the auditor vis-à-vis fraud and what is transmitted in the audit report has generated a gap (breach) of expectations between what users expect from the profession and what auditors have as responsibility only; problem of great interest to various authors, academics and control entities. This work seeks to examine the effects that the changes in the audit report generate on the gap of audit expectations for companies listed in the Stock Exchange of Colombia. The audit plays a fundamental role in the credibility, confidence and quality of the accounting and financial information generated by this type of companies that are oriented to a large number of users. The above, makes this work an important contribution to the accounting discipline in the field of control, since despite the extensive literature that raises and analyzes the gap of expectations from its different perspectives at the global level, particularly in Colombia it is an area that will contribute to the initiation of more in-depth investigations in the relationship of the audit expectations gap and the modifications to the audit report introduced in this audit. the impact of the impact on the economy of the economy.
