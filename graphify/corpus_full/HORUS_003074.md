# Optimization of IDF CURVAS DERIVED FROM IMERG IN COLOMBIA

Advances in remote sensing technologies have increased the use of secondary information sources to generate hydroclimatological variable series, which is particularly useful to reduce the monitoring gap in remote areas. The satellite precipitation product IMERG V06B FINAL, with its high temporal resolution, is particularly suitable for hydroclimatological analysis requiring sub-diary scale series. In previous research, carried out by the same authors of this article, it has been observed that IMERG tends to underestimate the total intensity and precipitation of extreme precipitation events, as well as to show underestimations in the IDF curves for the precipitation intensities of shorter durations and overestimations for the larger durations, compared with the IMERG curves derived from the information recorded at the stations. This article expands previous analyses, with two objectives: i) improve the methodology of identification and comparison of extreme storms between the ground measurements and the IMERG product, and ii) implement a correction methodology to improve the adjustment of IMERG curves derived from IMERG, encouraging their application in Colombia.
