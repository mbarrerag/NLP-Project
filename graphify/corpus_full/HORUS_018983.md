# THE BOGOTAU, OR THE ENVIRONMENT EDUCATION, IN THE DAY OF ARCHITECTURE;

The article analyses the project of the Tropicario di Bogota, which recently won the World Prize ¿ Social Habitat and Development of the Pan-American Architecture Biennale in Quito, Ecuador, because it aspires to educate and promote environmental conservation through an experience of recognition and connection with the ecosystems it hosts. In fact, by penetrating the different spaces, visitors are informed about the environmental values related to the various types of Colombian ecosystems, with the ultimate aim of encouraging their conservation. Reflecting on the current environmental emergency, the project appears to be dense with a strong symbolic character. ¿A commitment to the construction of a culture of the landscape ¿, where, from the local, a message of global urgency is sent
