# A method for summary and rating of app store reviews (IOs and Android) about Claro APP using machine learning techniques

In recent years, qualitative text analysis has acquired significant importance, especially with the boom of techniques of machine learning, in particular, deep learning. This growth has been driven by the ability to process on graphics cards. A valuable source of free information for this analysis are the comments of application stores, where users share their opinions on applications and brands. However, these comments present a challenge due to their uncomplicated structure, which makes it difficult to perform simple automatic learning algorithms. In this study, this challenge was addressed by looking for a simple but reliable way to extract information from the comments in the applications stores of Google and Mac, specifically on the application of Claro. The aim was to obtain a summary for each comment, identifying the central idea (such as billing complaints) and the expressed feeling (positive, negative or neutral). To achieve this, a systematic review of the literature was carried out to identify the best techniques of abstract and analysis of feelings in comments. The aim was to select Chat GPT as a viable alternative and implemented a code in Python that integrated the summary and analysis of feelings using the API of Open AI and the tool.
