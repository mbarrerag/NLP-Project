# Effect on the voltage wave due to the integration of distributed generation into electrical energy distribution networks.

This paper studies the behavior of the voltage wave in a circuit of electrical energy distribution of the local network operator, in which different simulation cases that contemplate distributed generations are evaluated considering the current Colombian normivity. For this work, a methodology of analysis based on simulations in the ATPDraw software, is chosen, starting from the real parameters provided by the network operator for a distribution circuit in medium voltage. In addition, due to the large number of nodes that are held in distribution networks, the methodology of simplification of networks is then integrated to reduce the simulation times. Since the network operator must maintain the quality in the power at the different points of the distribution system, the analysis of the graphical results of the voltage wave in different distributed generation scenarios, flow of load in conditions of stability and of transitive faults, allows to evaluate the quality of the power and verify the dynamic behavior of the system by having new sources of energy that can change the configuration and current operation of the network. The tests carried out showed results in which the voltage profiles at different points of the circuit can favor the adjustment of the voltage, however, it is important to control the income of the distributed generations, which can already take advantage of the power system
