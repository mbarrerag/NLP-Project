# THE TRIPLE FRONTERA BETWEEN COLOMBIA, BRAZIL AND PERU IN AMAZONIA. PRACTICES, FLUJES AND INTERCHANGE WITHIN A TRANSFRONTERIZED URBAN SYSTEM

The text reflects on the geographical characteristics of the triple Amazon border between Colombia, Brazil and Peru, emphasizing the practices, flows and exchanges that are carried out within the cross-border urban system that links cities around the border
