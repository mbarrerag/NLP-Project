# Exploration and Assessment of Fungal Diversity in the vicinity of the Mitú-Cachivera community

The community of Cachivera, located in kilometer 6 of the way Mitú-Monfort, in the Amazon region of Colombia, is part of the Association of Traditional Indigenous Authorities Aledañas a Mitú (AATIAM).This area is one of the most biodiversive and culturally rich of the country's integration, and in turn, one of the least explored in scientific terms.The project proposes to strengthen the seedbed of research in fungal diversity through the recognition and documentation of the local funga, contributing to the scientific knowledge and well-being of the community through the sustainable management of natural resources. Colombia houses an exceptional biological diversity, many of them located in the Amazon region. However, the lack of research in this area, due to their difficult access and limited scientific intervention, has hampered the scientific knowledge about these key organisms for ecosystems. The fungi play vital roles as breakers, soil formers and symbiont practices in the trophic networks. In addition, they have a significant cultural and medical value for the indigenous communities of the Amazon, which reinforces the need to document and evaluate this unique biodiversity. The project will also be part of the global objectives of biodiversity conservation, aligning itself with the goals of the United Nations Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD), by promoting the sustainable use of biological resources, the conservation of biodiversity and the fair sharing of benefits derived from genetic resources
