# Comparative study of diagnostic methods for mixed linear models and generalized linear models.

Many phenomena of nature can be represented by statistical models satisfactorily and, to validate these models, diagnostic methods are very useful tools for the verification of good adjustment. The application of diagnostic methods is relatively simple for classical linear regression models, however the process is more complicated when considering more general models and with additional sources of variability, such as the mixed linear model or models with binary or counting response, such as generalized linear models and mixed generalized linear models, which generally require the use of more complex residual and sensitivity analysis techniques. This paper presents different strategies related to model diagnosis, introducing both classical approaches, which are commonly used, as well as the most recent approaches. The derived methodologies will be studied for mixed linear models, generalized linear models and mixed generalized linear models, emphasizing their use in different applications
