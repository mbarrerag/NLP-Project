# Analysis of the directional distribution of the wave energy during different climate conditions

The directional wave spectra during hurricane conditions are not well repre-sitted by numerical wave models such as WAVEWATCH III, limiting its capacity for the hyndcast and extreme wave forecast. This research has the intention to carry out a sensitivity analysis of the terms of entry of the wind using the numeric model WAVEWACTH III in the physics packages of the wave ST3/4/6 to identify the response of the wave spectra generated by hurricanes to changes in the value of the most critical parameters of each parameter. In addition, modifications in the directional dispersion functions of the current source terms based on observations during a regular storm case in the Caribbean Sea and the Gulf of Mexico. BETAMAX, ZALP and ZWND are the most sensitive parameters of ST3/ST4, and SINWS for ST6 are evaluated; however, their impact is not relevant in the direction and does not indicate in an unequivocal way the physical mechanisms that resolve the underestimation/overmation of the local/bottom wave. parameters of each parameterization. In addition, modifications in the directional spring functions of the current observation-based source terms are evaluated during a regular storm case in the Caribbean Sea and Gulf of Mexico.
