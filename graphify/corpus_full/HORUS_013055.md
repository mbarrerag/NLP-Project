# Depoliticization and consecration in The Battle and "That Woman." Walsh and antiperonism in the 1960s

ABSTRACT Through the analysis of The Battle and some characteristics of "A Woman"; together with some considerations about Walsh's literary trajectory and the Argentine and Latin American political context, this article aims to contradict the narrative -so widespread and popularized- of Walsh's "gradual" politicization, from the late 1950s to the late 1970s. Here it is argued that, during the 1960s, due to political disappointments and conditions in the Argentine cultural field, Walsh moves away from the political intent -in a strict sense- of his works, and his writing is "depoliticized" if compared to his previous works -as Operation Massacre- or later -as the Open Letter
