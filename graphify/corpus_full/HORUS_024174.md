# Neurophysiological Characterization of Small Fiber Function in Heterozygous Women with Fabry Disease

ABSTRACT INTRODUCTION: Fabry disease (EF) is a genetic disease, caused by the deficit of the alpha galactoidase A enzyme (+945;-Gal A), which causes the accumulation of glycosfingolipids in the tissues. Its clinical manifestations are variable. Studies in heterozygota women report the existence of neuropathic pain as a manifestation of small fiber neuropathy. To determine the presence of small fiber neuropathy in women heterozygota for EF, by means of the quantitative sensory test. 33 women heterozygota for EF and 33 healthy women, with similar demographic characteristics.
