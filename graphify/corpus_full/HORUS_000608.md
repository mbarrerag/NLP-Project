# Effect of Hyaluronic Acid on Adjacent Papilla to Single Implants in the Previous Zone

The aesthetic appearance around dental implants includes both dental restoration and soft tissue morphology, being the filling of the interproximal space by the important papilla. The purpose was to evaluate the effects within three months of the application of hyaluronic acid in the interproximal area adjacent to single implants in the previous sector. Five patients were included who had at least one papilla adjacent to a single implant in the upper anterior sector with volume deficiency, subclass b or subclass c, according to the classification described by Zucchelli et al. (2019). The application of 0.2 ml of reticulated hyaluronic acid (Hyadent BG-Regedent, BioScience GmbH, Dümmer, Germany) in the mesial papilla and distal during three trials, each with one week of interval was performed. A follow-up was performed at three months to evaluate the effects on papilla volume gain.
