# THEORICAL APPROXIMATION TO THE CONSIDERATION OF ARMED CONFLICT AND CIVIL WARS IN AFRICA

This article outlines the current phase of armed conflict in Africa, the combination of causes, focusing on grievance, greed, failed negotiations and bad governance, as well as the characterization of war economies. It also reviews theoretical and conceptual perspectives on civil wars in Africa
