# Development of critical thinking based on the appropriation and protection of El Dorado Regional Natural Park by ninth grade students of a rural educational institution

This research arises from one of the teachers' concerns, in how to get students to own their territory and generate strategies for their protection, through the development of critical thinking skills by having as a basis the socio-scientific issues. So, a didactic proposal is developed with the aim of strengthening some skills through the appropriation and protection of flora, fauna (insects) and water resources of the El Dorado Regional Natural Park in students of ninth grade of a Rural Educational Institution. The methodological design of the proposal is based on a qualitative paradigm and of descriptive scope, in which having the results of the Pretest on the identification of critical thinking skills through everyday situations taking into account the foundation of the Halpern test (Halpern, 2006); a didactic sequence is constructed with which is sought from a socio-scientific perspective such as recognition of the territory and conservation of the El Dorado Regional Natural Park that students develop some critical thinking skills through instruments such as environmental mapping, taxonomy guides and recognition of the fauna and flora, as well as pedagogical solutions from the territory, which are effectively recognised (in the context of the region and conservation). in which having the results of the Pretest on identification of critical thinking skills through everyday situations taking into account the foundation of the Halpern test (Halpern, 2006); didactic sequence is built with which it is sound from a socio-scientific perspective such as the recognition of the environmental territory and the conservation of the El Dorado Regional Natural Park that students develop some critical thinking skills through instruments such as environmental photography, taxnomy guides and recognition of the fauna and flora, as well as educational outcomes to collect the territory, which effectively and respond to the resolution of the environmental problems that arise in the region
