# Female voices, pedagogical, musical and cultural elements in the choral context of educational institutions in the city of Facatativá

This study examines the role of female voices by observing pedagogical, musical and cultural elements in the choral context of educational institutions in the city of Facatativá. It analyses the prominent role of women in school choirs, since it not only enriches the musical landscape, but also promotes gender equity and strengthens cultural identity. Pedagogical strategies are explored that promote women’s empowerment through choral music, as well as its impact on the personal and academic development of students . The present study examines the role of female voices by observing pedagogical, musical and cultural elements in the choral context of educational institutions in the city of Facatativá.
