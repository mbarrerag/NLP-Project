# Design of a collaborative cost reduction strategy for citrus producers in the Shoe Swamp

The cienaga de Shoesa, in the department of Cesar, is a region of citrivistic vocation where small producers face high logistic costs and low associativeness.This grade work proposes a collaborative strategy for the reduction of logistic costs in the supply chain of citrus, developed by means of a quantitative and participatory approach In the framework of the ECORZA project of the National University of Colombia, we applied surveys to producers to characterize the current situation, we used the method of Jerarchic Analytic Process (AHP) to prioritize collaborative logistic practices, and we implemented simulations in FlexSim to evaluate the operational impact of these practices.From this analysis, we selected two main strategies: the collaborative management of transport and shared storage. Two scenarios were compared: one based on the current model of individual operation, and another based on a collaborative model managed by a partnership. The results of the simulation showed a 25% reduction in total logistic costs, highlighting significant decreases in transport and storage. In addition, we observed an improvement in the operational efficiency and the use of the local infrastructure.
