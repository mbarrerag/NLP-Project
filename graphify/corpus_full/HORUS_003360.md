# DELIRIUM AND SYNDROME INTENSIVE CARE: A COHORT STUDIES IN THE UNITY OF ADULT INTENSIVE CARE

Background / Introduction: The Post Intensive Care Syndrome (PICS) is a phenomenon of great impact in critical patients. Complications of care in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) can be physical, cognitive and mental, affecting the quality of life of the patient, the family and the health system. Mental health problems include anxiety, depression, post-traumatic stress, sadness and delirium, the latter with acute onset and fluctuating course, characterized by reduced attention, impairment of memory, disorientation, and altered language and perception. Delirium contributes to cognitive deterioration (OR = 2.85, 95% CI: 1,10-7,38) and negatively impact the recovery of critical patients.General objective: To determine the association between the occurrence of delirium during stay in ICU and the Post Intensive Care Syndrome to the month and three months of discharge of the Unit.Methodology: The patient's prospective cohort study, which seeks to quantify the occurrence of the Post Intensive Care Syndrome at discharge, to month and to the psychiatric disorders Stairs.
