# Characterization and differentiation of Colombian honeys by application of sensory instrumental tools and physicochemical properties

Colombia is recognized in the world for its great biodiversity what generates high potentials of honey production, however, the importance that has been given to the apiculture is not remarkable until now. The study of the physical and chemical properties of honey is important to recognize the quality of this product.However, the lack of legislation has led to a high index of counterfeiting and a low verification of quality.
