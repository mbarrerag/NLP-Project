# Social protection floor in Colombia – reform pension for part-time workers

Discuss how the adaptation of Colombia, of recommendation 202 of 2012 of social protection floors, can affect part-time and additional workers as it moves away from the desire of the ILO and the UN in the search for decent work for all through non-contributory social security systems, which manage to have universal coverage in some cases. It is concluded that the Colombian social protection floors are a pension reform for part-time workers in Colombia, which creates a third pension regime that works in synergy in the general pension system, which does not have a change in the contributory pillar in which the Colombian system has passed, for which the coverage figures do not have a significant increase. So, it is time for Colombia to comply with international recommendations, and begin a migration to broad social security systems, through a pension reform that includes the non-contributory pillar within the general social security system, thus complying with the recommendation. of the ILO and decreasing the poverty figures like the countries of the region
