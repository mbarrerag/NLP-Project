# Analysis of a depropanizer column with internal energy integration of concentric configuration.

In this study, the thermodynamic performance of a concentric distillation column, with internal energy integration, was analyzed to separate the propylene-propane mixture, by means of the analysis of the second law of the column. Further, the hydraulic behavior was studied and the efficiency of a plate was determined in different sections of this type of columns by CFD. To achieve this, five specific objectives were established: 1) Selecting the type of configuration HIDIC concentric most suitable for the separation of the propylene-propane mixture by means of the analysis of the second law of the thermodynamics applied in the complete column considering the effect of the configuration on the generated entropy, the energy and exergity required for the separation. 2) Predicting the hydraulic behavior and the efficiency of the mass transfer in a selected plate of the HIDIC using the CF. 3) Identifying effects of the variables of design and operation of the column on the thermal performance, hydraulic and mass transfer in a plate of the column HIDIC. 4) To complete the objective three, three integrated dishes were taken, located in different places of the HIDIC column, one above, one in the middle and the other in the part below the column, which had three different conditions (Chapter 4), checking the influence of these initial and frontier conditions on the properties studied (height of the clear liquid and efficiency of the dish). In the fourth objective, it was possible to discriminate the exergetic losses by each component of the HIDIC column and compared with losses generated by the conventional column and the vapour recompression, obtaining better results in the HIDIC column (Chapter 5). To conclude, in the development of the last objective, a preliminary conceptual design was established by means of 5 general steps, with the principle of exergetic loss as the main basis of the design (Chapter 6)
