# THE TRANSFORMATION OF THE RESIDUAL SPACE IN PUBLIC PLACE OF QUALITY: UNDER THE LINES OF THE MEDELLIN METRE.

The article presents results of the research New approaches? developed by the research group Transepto, of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters Medellín- of which is part the degree work carried out by the architect Daniel Narváez and is a reflection on the possible and urgent ecological transformation of the residual spaces in our cities: specifically those spaces linked to the great works of road infrastructure like the viaducts, which in most cases are designed and constructed without taking into account their immediate context, neglecting, for example, the public space that surrounds them, becoming dark places with presence of garbage and crime, generating rejection by the inhabitants of the sector and the city. Thus, within the research several types of problems related to these urban areas were identified and analyzed and initial approaches were defined in order to generate project responses that would allow to take advantage of these places in function of the regeneration of the public space, of the redefinition of the natural landscape and of the functional programs that they can host
