# EFFECT OF BRASINOESTEROIDS AND PROMOTOR BASTERS FOR GROWTH OF PLANTS IN GROWTH AND PERFORMANCE OF SATIVE lactuca IN HYDROPONIA CONDITIONS

Lactuca sativa is considered the most important leaf vegetable and of greater consumption worldwide, thanks to its nutritional and medicinal benefits. It has been reported that the application of phytohormones, such as brasinosteroids (br) and bacteria promoting plant growth (bpcp) promote physiological parameters of growth and yield of plants.
