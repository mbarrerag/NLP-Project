# Strategy to enhance the technical and technological infrastructure of laboratories at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Palmira

The good state of physical infrastructure and technical equipment are fundamental factors to provide technical, scientific and technological support in order to guarantee the timely, efficient and effective attention of the demand of the services of laboratories that support and require teaching, research and extension at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Palmira. The deficiencies in physical infrastructure and equipment presented in laboratories are due to: 1. Buildings of laboratories not suitable for the development of their technical requirements related to good laboratory practices, biosafety, RETIE and resistance. 2. Restriction of resources for the implementation of strategies of support, improvement and maintenance of the physical infrastructure and specialized equipment in laboratories that guarantee a correct renewal, update and equipping of equipment for the timely provision of services in laboratories. The above indicates that there is a low renewal of these, finding the great majority of the equipment to the end of their useful life, applying the necessity of the technical services and applying the new development.
