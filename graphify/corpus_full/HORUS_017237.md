# ANALYSIS OF CAPITAL STRUCTURE IN THE MAIN CENTRO- AMERICAN BANK SYSTEMS FOR THE PERIOD 2015-2019

The purpose of this article was to identify the determinants of the capital structure for the banking system of El Salvador, Guatemala and Panama in the period 2015-2019, in which 68 banking institutions that report their accounting statements monthly to the local financial superintendencies were analyzed. To this end, a mixed methodological design was carried out, applying, through a review of extensive literature, a theoretical and empirical collection of the main postulates around the topic, in which also the evolution of each banking system and its structural capital behaviour was analyzed, through a quantitative approach. For this purpose, a descriptive data study was carried out, as well as the econometric development of a static panel data model with fixed effects. The results obtained allow identifying that the traditional determinants that explain the capital structure of non-financial firms are also significant in the banking context, and that their relationship with the debt capacity managed to conform to the approach of the theory of financial hierarchy
