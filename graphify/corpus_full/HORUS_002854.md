# ON NILCOMPACTIFICATIONS OF PRIME SPECTRA OF COMMUTATIVE RINGS

Given a ring R and one of its proper ideals S, we obtain a compactification of the prime spectrum of S through a mainly algebraic process. We call it the R-nilcompactification of Spec(S). We study some categorical properties of this construction
