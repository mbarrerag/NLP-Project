# Assessment of the potential use of steel aggregate stabilized with zeolite-based additive in road infrastructure.

This study allowed characterizing a material as steellurgical aggregate called Siderurgical Slag Diaco (ECSD) composed of electric arc furnace slags (EAF) and spoon furnace (LF). The possibility of stabilization of the material with different percentages of cement and additive based on zeolites to be used as construction material in layers of pavement structures was evaluated. In a first stage, the ECSD was characterized as aggregate according to national and international regulations for granular material. In general, the ECSD met the standards established by the specifications INVIAS and international references. In a second stage, the formulation and analysis of mechanical behavior of three mixtures composed by ECSD, hydraulic cement and additive based on zeolites. The mixtures were composed by 0%, 2% and 6% of cement in mass. The relation between additive and cement was expressed by 1/100. According to the specification for hydraulically linked mixtures (NF-14227), the mixtures were classified as C4/ and C5/6 according to the strength in compression and as type T2 and T4 of resistance in concrete (RT) was presented by means of resistance to the concrete (NC)
