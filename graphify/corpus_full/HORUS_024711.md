# GASTROINTESTINAL STROMA TUMOR IN SHEEP

The gastrointestinal stromal tumor (¿gastrointestinal stromal tumor¿, GIST) is a mesenchymal neoplasm originating from interstitial cells of Cajal, located between the muscular layers of the gastrointestinal tract. gastrointestinal.The tumor has been described in several animal species, but to date there are no reports in sheep. GIST should be considered a differential diagnosis of intestinal neoplasms in sheep
