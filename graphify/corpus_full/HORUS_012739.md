# Comparative analysis of the provision of medicines in public health programs for leishmaniasis, Chagas disease and Hansen's disease in Brazil and Colombia in 2022

Neglected diseases of infectious origin are defined by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) (2019) as “a group of parasitic diseases and other infectious diseases that are generally characterized by historically low investments in the pharmaceutical sector and that mainly affect populations living in precarious socioeconomic conditions”.Despite the high social impact of these diseases, research and development of new medicines that lead to new treatments by the pharmaceutical industry are low or null, as they do not represent favorable economic markets.This is related to the comprehensive approach to care for these communities, which must consider access to medicines.This study analyzes the supply of medicines within the scope of leishmaniasis, Chagas disease and leprosy programs;the three pathologies present in Brazil and Colombia.Objective: To carry out a comparative analysis of the supply of medicines in public health programs for leishmaniasis, Chagas disease and leprosy in Brazil and Colombia during the first half of 2022. A broad literature review was carried out in databases and official documents.Semi-structured interviews were carried out with key stakeholders, which were analyzed using the specialized software ATLAS.ti® according to the categories (criteria) of interest.Results and conclusions: The selection of medicines in both countries is guided by protocols or guides from the national Ministries of Health.In demand planning in Brazil, a greater number of actors participate compared to those who participate in Colombia, however, the same sources of information are used in both countries.In Brazil, 50% of medicines are acquired locally through bidding, while in Colombia 100% are imported and acquired through PAHO.Regardless of the country, monopolies or oligopolies can be observed, mainly from pharmaceutical companies of European or American origin.Brazil has a regulatory framework that seeks to develop scientific, technological and production capacity for medicines for diseases of public health interest;In Colombia, there is no evidence of a State policy on the subject.Neglected infectious diseases are defined by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) (2019) as "a group
