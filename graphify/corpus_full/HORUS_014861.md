# Effects of communication on disagreement of expectations: empirical evidence for Colombia

This thesis seeks to identify the effects of the communication of economic policy on macroeconomic expectations. In particular, it seeks to identify the effects of the communication of monetary and fiscal policy on the disagreements in consumer inflation expectations, expectations of the fiscal deficit and the expectations of the interest rate of monetary policy. To this end, indicators of disagreements in expectations, indicators of monetary and fiscal communication, as well as indicators of credibility and reputation of monetary and fiscal policy. The methodology used is to evaluate the effects of the communication through estimates OLS-HAC, GMM-HAC, GMM-WINDMEIJER and VAR (impulsus-response and breakdown of variance). The econometric results of the research indicate that the monetary and fiscal communication exerts important effects on the formation of macroeconomic expectations. Therefore, the main recommendation of economic policy suggests that the monetary and fiscal institutions should perform greater in order to make communication an important tool of economic policy that allows the conduct of macroeconomic expectations and as a result facilitates the achievement of the particular targets imposed by the economic authorities . The economic results of the research indicate that monetary and fiscal communication exports important effects on the formation of macroeconomic expectations. Therefore, the main economic policy recommendation is that the monetary and fiscal institutions must do more so that communication becomes an important economic policy tool that allows the management of macroeconomic expectations and, as a result, facilitates the conclusion of goals ordered by the economic authorities
