# Teaching strategy to develop the competition inquiry from Bioprospecting natural plant products.

According to the results of the standard test SABER 11 of 2020 applied by the ICFES and the data of the first simulation of 2021 carried out by the company Milton Ochoa to the students of the eleventh grade of the Anglo American College, a low level of performance of the students in the “inquiry” competition was evidenced. With the aim of improving this competence in the eleventh grade students of the school, in this work a didactic strategy related to the concept of Bioprospecting of natural plant products was designed, implemented and evaluated. For the design of this strategy three activities related to: to consult the students about their strategies of searching for information and socialize with them a series of tools that can support the process for the search for this one. In the second phase of implementation of the didactic strategy was carried out the selection of the natural plant product, the approach of a question and the space to answer this question (taking into account the selected product). Finally, the didactic strategy was evaluated from the verbal support of the work carried out by the students and the score obtained by the institution in the presentation of the test SABER 11 points. Finally, the didactic strategy was evaluated from 71st area
