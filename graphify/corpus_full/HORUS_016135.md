# Impact of categorization on the structure of concepts

This thesis is a critical and propositional analysis of the structure of concepts in the context of the philosophy of mind. To carry this out, it begins by making explicit what is understood by a structure of concepts. Subsequently, it proposes and justifies a set of requirements that must meet a structure for concepts to be considered viable; these requirements are based on the impact that categorization has on the structure of concepts. With this clear, it is passed to evaluate the structures for the most relevant concepts from the proposed requirements; the result of this evaluation is that none can be characterized as a viable structure for concepts. By way of conclusion, it proposes a structure for concepts that is viable according to the proposed and justified requirements throughout the the the thesis. .
