# VALORISATION OF AN IRON WASTE MINERAL AS A CATALYST IN THE DEGRADATION OF ACID FUCHSIN THROUGH A PHOTO-FENTON TYPE PROCESS

The catalytic activity of a natural mineral, considered a waste product of mining activity, was evaluated in the degradation of acid fuchsin, a dye used mainly in biochemical and microbiological laboratories in histological studies. The high content of iron phases, makes it promising for the Fenton reaction. Variables such as dye concentration, hydrogen peroxide concentration and catalyst load were analyzed, in addition to exploring the assistance of LED white light and the addition of oxalic acid to the system. In the reactions explored, it was found that the best working conditions are concentrations of 1g/L of solid, 0.30 M of oxidant and assistance of LED light, reaching a 90% of loss of color and higher proportion of degradation products thus making it a Photo-Fenton process. The addition of oxalic acid turns in a higher loss of color. Leaching results indicates that the catalyst is stable even under acid mild conditions, indicating the heterogeneity of the system
