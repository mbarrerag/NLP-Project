# EXCLUSION, DEEQUALITY AND FINANCING OF EDUCATION IN COLOMBIA.

The aim of this document is to analyse the general trends in pre-school, basic, middle and higher education in Colombia in terms of the progress made in guaranteeing the right, but showing the exclusions and inequalities that impede its full realization. This exercise will be accompanied by an analysis of budgetary trends, which reflect mixed state commitments regarding the importance that has been offered to basic education, neglect of preschool education, timid advances in secondary education and privatization trends in higher education.The analysis will be carried out for the last 20 years and will take as sources the most recent data presented by the Ministry of National Education, the National Statistics Department, the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit and the State University System.It will also use secondary sources that base its findings on the National Household Surveys conducted in the country.The analysis will show that education in Colombia has been configured in a field that reproduces exclusion and inequality relations that impede the effective enjoyment of the right for disadvantaged social classes, women, ethnic groups, sectors from rural sectors, persons with disabilities and the world of sexual and gender dissidencies.These groups, with the least and the least social education, have the least
