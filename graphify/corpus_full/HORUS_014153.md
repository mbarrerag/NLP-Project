# _x0093_ Sentient Centennial and Popular Memory: A Tribute to Orlando Fals Borda (1925-2008)

This project aims to demonstrate the validity of the thought of the teacher Orlando Fals Borda in the centenary of his birth. To this end, it is proposed to publish a text constructed collectively with: a) testimonies of the first graduates of Sociology and disciples of the teacher, who participated in the conversation held in Sociology in September 2023 and who analyzed the contributions of Fals Borda to the problem of scientific and cultural autonomy in Colombia with a decolonial perspective aimed at overcoming Eurocentrism; b) synthesis of recent research that illustrate the relevance of those called by Fals Borda as _x0093_people original_x0094_ with its founding values and its resistance processes: solidarity of the indigenous, the freedom of the black Maroons, the dignity of the mestizo peasants and the autonomy of the internal pioneer settlers; c) results of the work of field of students of Sociology, during the years 2022 and 2023, which applied the criteria defined by the teacher for the Research Participatory Action with the communities of the parish of the City of Palermo and the Eastern Cerros of Bogotá and with the inhabitants of Bortrá
