# Development of a booklet with analysis of common misconceptions and which endanger the electrical safety of people in distribution networks

Development of a letterbook with analysis of common misconceptions and which put the electrical safety of people in distribution networks at risk In the applications of electrical engineering, some errors have been passed from one generation of engineers to others without any major criticism, creating problems in the operation of projects, problems that can be from: short life of the equipment, interference and malfunctioning of these to dangers for the health or life of the operators, users and the general public. Faced with these difficulties, the companies request advice from the National University of Colombia. These requests have identified recurring cases of deficiencies in the interpretation of both circuit theory, and of the respective technical rules, so that it has been considered by the National University of Colombia, the Association of Egreados AIEEUN and some private companies, make short courses, workshops and didactic material that serve to mitigate this type of weaknesses in the interpretation of the rules and laws of circuits at professional level.
