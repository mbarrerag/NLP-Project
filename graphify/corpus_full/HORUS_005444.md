# WHERE THE INDIVIDUAL WAS, THE SELF MUST COME!

Nowadays, a myth runs through psychological faculties and institutions. The myth is about the individual. An individual that cannot be divided, which is independent of other things; every myth is based on a moment of origin. In recent years this has been an issue that has wanted to turn to the biological, or to be more concrete and precise: to the brain. This biological-cerebral ¿unit¿ that supposedly makes us think and behave in certain ways, is nothing more than another attempt to want to guide, orient and control. All this production with biological pretensions is nothing other than individualism that in recent decades has turned to the ideals of neoliberal capitalism
