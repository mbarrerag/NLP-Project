# Maintenance equipment Thermocycling reference LightCycler ® 480. Roche brand

"Today, most research in the fields of biology and particularly in viriology, immunology, nutrition, is based on molecular tests. Through these, pathogens can be detected, pathogenic loads can be established and gene expression can also be seen. > 65533; For this purpose, real-time thermocyclators are used. The reference Thermocyclator equipment LightCyclor ® 480. Marca Roche, has been used for the last 10 years to analyze thousands of tests and has contributed to the development of research within the Postgraduate Course in Health and Animal Production of the FMVZ. Many scientific publications have been supported by the results obtained with processes in this team. > 65533; Currently, the equipment is out of operation because the "unit of lampara" was damaged and it is necessary to change it.
