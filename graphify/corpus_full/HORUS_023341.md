# Transformation of the landscape, functional diversity and eco-hydrological functioning of natural and interventional ecosystems in Bolivia

The objective of this research was to identify and characterize the variables that determine the transformation of the landscape of the Tucumano-Bolivian forest, in order to evaluate the effects of this transformation on the ecohydrological functioning of these ecosystems in the Natural Area of Integrated Management Rio Grande – Valles Cruceños, Bolivia.
