# Strengthening Natural Products Research

The work in the area of natural products requires some equipment for daily use for the processing of plant material and the optimal development of bioassays. To do this, equipment such as rotoevaporators for constant use in the process of evaporation of solvents are required that are used in processes of extraction or purification of active proclipia of plant samples. In addition to extraction booths, to protect the health of researchers when working with volatile solvents and biosafety booths for bioassays. This together with support equipment such as autoclaves and furnaces, forms the basic equipment of the laboratory, which, in the face of constant use, are urgently rerouding corrective maintenance and some preventive maintenance to be able to continue with the execution of the projects and thesis in progress
