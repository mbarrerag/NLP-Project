# Fixed term deposit and benchmark bank indicator: analysis of interest rate migration in Colombia

This research analyzed the relationship between the reference interest rates most used in Colombia for the standardization of financial contracts and the economic stability of the country. Self-regressive vectors were used to evaluate the impacts of these rates, through the channel of transmission of monetary policy of bank loans on inflation for the period 2010-2019. The impulse-response functions and breakdown of the variance of models allow to reflect a better transmission in the prices to the consumer with the reference bank indicator (IBR) than with the fixed deposit rate (FMD)
