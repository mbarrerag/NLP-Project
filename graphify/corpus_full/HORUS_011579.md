# Statistical-Space methodology to establish the priority of intervention of the rural road mesh (MVR) of Bogotá D.C.

The city of Bogotá Distrito Capital has the particularity of owning within its territory soils of urban and rural type. The transport infrastructure (vias) of each of these types of soil presents unique dynamics and, as well, specific conservation needs (Rehabilitation and maintenance). This work seeks to propose a methodology to determine a priority value for intervention, particularly of the rural roads of the city, taking into account the dynamics specific to this rural area (population and rural society, topographic and accessibility conditions, economic production, environmental characteristics, legislation protecting ecosystems and areas of environmental importance, etc.) and, considering that the resources available in the district budget for road conservation are insufficient, a process of prioritisation is necessary for the selection of road segments of such a way that the resources that are invested maximize the benefit of the population based on different criteria of the technical, economic, social and environmental dimensions. ssary a prioritization process to select the roads in a way that the invested resources maximize the population benefit based on different criteria of the technical, economic, social and environmental dimension. Employing geographic information and using Geographic Information System (GIS), statistics and spatial analysis technologies a qualification or priority value for the rural roads of the city was obtained
