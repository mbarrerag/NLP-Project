# Characterization of physical and mechanical properties for the structural use of wood of the species “Acacia Mangium”.

The work consisted of the characterization of basic mechanical properties of interest for the use in structural elements in buildings of Acacia Mangium Wild wood coming from the Rio Sinú Valley in the department of Córdoba, in order to expand its presence within the construction industry in Colombia. For the determination of the different properties, a visit was made to the areas from which the wood logs were obtained to be studied, which were divided into two large groups, dried in oven and dried in the air, in order to be later characterized physically and mechanically. In the development of this research, the modulus of elasticity and resistance to bending, traction and compression parallel and perpendicular to the grain and cutting were evaluated. Additionally, densities and moisture content were determined. The results obtained allow to appreciate a good quality wood, which is suitable for its use as main material in the elaboration of structural elements in buildings.
