# CORPORAL LABORATORY / Moment Two: Experiences to build interaction on body knowledge from health.

This research project investigates the knowledge produced from the exploration of body practices present in the training of health professionals of the National University of Colombia; Continuing with the generation of a meeting space called Body Laboratory or "Corplaboratory" moment 2, where we want to explore, strengthen and nurture with areas of knowledge such as pedagogy, methodologies and tools for work and rhythmic body exploration based on research and pedagogical training, contributing to the analysis and reflections in relation to health and the training of professionals in this area; doing research from the experience and contrasting it with the existing theoretical and methodological review. From this research experience, it is intended to strengthen the knowledge and knowledge about the body, if not to learn about the research around body experimentation, the systematization of bodily experiences, the production of web, digital material and events resulting from the process and the interaction with participants in communities, that allow to know and recognize the visions of those working the body from different viewpoints and actions that are developed there. This meeting space for learning, part of the exploration, but seeks the construction and structuring of methodological tools (which allow the interaction of other professional sciences, that allow the interaction of pedagogy and social sciences)
