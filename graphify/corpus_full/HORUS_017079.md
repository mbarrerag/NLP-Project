# Teaching sequence for teaching - learning decimal numbers in primary in a context of remote classes.

Education in the world underwent a very strong change from moment to moment because of the presence of Covid-19. It generated that classes will be held over the Internet to continue the teaching-learning process that was brought with students. Initially, it was hampered because teachers did not have the appropriate tools and strategies for virtual education, nor did some students have resources and tools that would allow them to continue their educational process. For these reasons, this work presents the design, implementation and evaluation of a didactic sequence to work on the topic of decimal numbers, with fifth grade students under the approach of Teaching for Understanding, in a context of remote classes. It was intended to target students who did not have a good connection to the Internet and had few resources
