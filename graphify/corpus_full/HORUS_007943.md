# THE STORY WORKSHOP: EXPERIENCES, RECOGNIZED AND CONVERGING PROPOSALS BETWEEN COLOMBIA AND THE RIUM OF THE PLAT

This article presents three methodological perspectives for undertaking historiographical research in renewal. Based on the metaphor of the workshop, the authors reconstruct part of their trajectory as historians of history. First, an approach to the world of printed material is made on historical topics published in the Colombian xixth century, from a perspective that combines concern for materialities, formats and genres. Second, elements are posed that seek to put in dialogue the intellectual history and the history of the culture written as heuristic and analytical references to think a historiography beyond textual analysis. Finally, based on the case of the Río de la Plata, a series of arguments are presented to emerge from the nationalistic corse and to highlight the relevance of a transnational approach to historiography. The text provides elements for a collaborative agenda that decisively integrates the contributions of other sub-disciplines of history into historiography
