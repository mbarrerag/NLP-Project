# PEDAGOGATION, CREATION AND LIFE: TRAINING PRACTICES OUTSIDE SCHOOL

This book is the product of the research project Training practices outside school, which was proposed to elaborate a conceptual and methodological framework for the approach to these practices, as well as to investigate some of their main characteristics in the country. The book asks about the difficult relations between formal and non-formal education, about how these practices have contributed to the government of individuals and the population, about the type of subjects they have formed, about the degree to which these practices emulate or differ from classical school educational practices and, finally, about their social, political and cultural effects. Twelve cases of training practices outside of school are examined.
