# Tree-level recursive self-dual Yang-Mills and self-dual Gravity

This paper studies the quantitativeization of the Gauge theories by means of the formalism of Batalin-Vilkovisky in the language of the graduated differential varieties, introduced initially by Schwarz, Kontsevich, et. al. [AKSZ97], and some of the mathematical consequences of this language, emphasizing the fact that the geometric structure underlying a quantitative gauge theory is a simplistic variety graduated equipped with a homological vector field. These structures induce homotopic Lie algebras, such as the L_infinite algebras, so that the formalism of Batalin-Vilkovisky classical dispersion functions can be translated into a homotopic Maurer-Cartan theory that encodes the Gauge theory. The conceptual gain of this language in physics is described as the algebra L_infinite allows building of generatric functions of dispersal amplitudes at tree level, by means of the implementation of the Berends-Giele currents. We consider this approximation by calculating the currents of Berends of-Giele, the L_infinitive. V formalism is translated into Maurer-Cartan theory for a cyclic L_infinity-algebra that already collected all the information of the partnered gauge theory. The advantage of this language when describing the physics of particular models is that the L_infinity-algebra allows one to produce a generating function of tree-level amplitudes by directly implementing the so-called Berends-Giele currents. We tested this approach by explicitly calculating Berends-Giele currents from the L_infinity-structure of different themes, such as Yang-Mills theory, self-dual Yang-Mills, and self-dual Gravity, constructing the last one as the double-copy of self-dual Yang-Mills
