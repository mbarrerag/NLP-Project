# MALNUTRITION IN THE MAJOR ADULT

Malnutrition is a very prevalent condition in older adults, which involves a burden on health systems and society at large. It is associated with an increase in vulnerability, given the physiological deterioration related to age, reduced access to food of nutritional quality, food insecurity, comorbidities specific to old age, among multiple factors (1.2). It is important to recognize that malnutrition is a concept that must be understood both from an over-reported state, i.e. overweight, obesity and non-communicable diseases related to food, and from a state of deficit that refers to malnutrition, which may be given by underweight, micronutrient deficiencies and emaciation (3)
