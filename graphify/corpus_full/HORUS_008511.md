# Law on the rubble, buildings that fall, bodies that resuscitate

On October 12, 2013 the 6th tower of the Space building in Medellín collapsed. The cause of the collapse: the design did not meet the total requirements of the law and its regulatory decrees. Once the remaining towers were demolished, the ghost of the Space began to travel the world: sick buildings, Law 1796 of 2016, the National Register of Certified Professionals (UNPA) and the 10-year insurance. The Space is not a fallen building anymore, it is the representative of all buildings that could fall; it is the history of a world that collapses and the effort of engineers-sociologists to make new and increasingly durable arrangements. From scattered debris scattered in press notes, trades, plans, licenses and reports is rebuilt at different scales and times the most “exitous” case of a fallen building in Colombia.
