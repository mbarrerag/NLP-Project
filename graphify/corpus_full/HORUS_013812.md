# Energy Harnessing Vegetable Waste

This project seeks to support the implementation of the Seed called Immediate Characterization, Last, Calorific Power and pyrolysis profiles of vegetable residues, in order to evaluate the possibilities of energy use of vegetable residues. In this way, it seeks to achieve two fundamental purposes: one scientific and one academic. Scientific for the thermal characterization of vegetable residues and academic to initiate and encourage undergraduate students to research
