# Development of biodegradable films based on pectins extracted from coffee benefit by-products by acid hydrolysis

The use of the by-products of the coffee industry, such as the moth-based gew and pulp, is shown as an alternative for obtaining relevant compounds such as pectin, polymer widely used in the food industry thanks to its gelling property. The objective of this study is the development of biodegradable films based on pectin of these by-products, obtained by acidic hydrolysis with citric acid, reinforced by extract of coffee and bacterial cellulose.
