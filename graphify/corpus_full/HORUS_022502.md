# LIFE, SPACE, FEAR AND HOPE IN CIVID 19

In this special issue we compiled the results of the International Workshop on Cartographic Creation 2.5: Space Discontinuities in the New Pandemic Daily Events. On October 8 and 9, 2020, with the support of the Open and Distance University, we are from different places virtually. The speakers, the forum and the socialization of the results of the two workshops were broadcast live on YouTube. The experience led us to enter into the participatory methodologies in a virtual way, while allowing us to know different contexts and form of solidarity creativity to face these times of multi-crisis
