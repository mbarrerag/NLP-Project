# VALIDATION OF A AIRPORT OF SINGLE SINGLE VALIDATION IN STAFF AND SUPERCRIPTIVE FLOW

The three-dimensional numeric validation of a high-altitude cannexion spillway of prismatic circular section in supercritical regime is presented. The effects of turbulence and free surface variations are simulated in three dimensions using the model of realizable k-e turbulence and fluid volume (VOF). To simulate the high-alloy cannexion spillway, the Pseudo-Transient solution is used with the Pseudo-Transient method, which solves steady-state flows and accelerates the convergence of solution. Simulations show that the numeral model predicts experimental measurements of flow and variance of the free surface with good precision. Additionally, the results obtained are compared to the methodology of design proposed in the current local standard
