# PILLOT VALIDATION STUDY OF THE AGE ESTIMATION METHOD OF DEATH IN MOTHER ADULTS IN THE OSTEOLOGICAL GRANTED COLLECTION AND PRACTICAL APPLICATION IN A COLOMBIAN SERIES

The estimation of age at the time of death in mature individuals is very important, even more so in the context of historical series. Falys and Prangle proposed in 2015 a method from the sternal end of the collarbone, which makes the evaluation and scoring of three morphological criteria: topography of the surface, porosity and formation of osteophytes. The confidence of the method in individuals over 40 years of age in the Christ Church series in Spitalfields was 96.4%, but in other series it has been reduced. The process of mestizoje in Latin America and the almost complete absence of known sex and age series allow us to propose the Spanish series as key references for the validation of methods of estimation of the age of death to be applied in the undocumented Latin American series. The bone collection of the University of Granada, which comes from the San José Cemetery, whose individuals date from the 20th and 21st century, counts 237 individuals over 40 years of which, a sample of 50 individuals will be evaluated through the method of Falys and Prangle (2015).
