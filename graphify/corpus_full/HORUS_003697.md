# Organizational adaptability and superior performance: Dynamic Capacity Triad Model

ABSTRACT This study examines the relationship between business adaptability and superior performance. Researchers used Exploratory Factor Analysis and Kruskal-Wallis statistics to analyze data from a survey of 227 companies associated with a University Center. The study found that the three-pronged model of dynamic capabilities, including components Detection, Exploitation and Transformation, explains 78.38% of the variation in business adaptability. The findings of the study confirm the importance of detection, exploitation and transformation capabilities to maintain competitive advantage, as demonstrated by the superior performance observed by the organizations. The originality of the study lies in identifying superior capabilities associated with business adaptability in organizations in different sectors
