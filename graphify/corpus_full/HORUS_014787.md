# OBSTACLES IN THE LEARNING OF FRANCE: PERCEPTIONS OF MODERN LANGUAGE STUDENTS (UNIVERSITY ECCI).

This research paper details the perceptions of obstacles that arise in the teaching-learning process of the French language in a group of students from Modern Languages. For this purpose, we created a survey of opinions that questioned about the socio-economic, cognitive, psycho-affective, concentration and environmental barriers. 315 students from different semesters were questioned directly. Quantitative analysis of the data collected reveals that a relatively low number of students declare to be simultaneously and successfully carrying out their studies and paid work. About half of them claim to use appropriate learning strategies, communicate with difficulty and do not consider harming their learning of the French language with the use of virtual and electronic distractions in class. It is concluded that efforts must be made both individually and collectively to perfect the teaching-learning processes in the French language. Key words: foreign language; French; difficulty in learning; language teaching
