# AUTHORREGULATION IN THE LEARNING OF THE DIDACTIC PEDAGOLOGICAL UNIT OF DEPRESSION IN SIXTH SEMSTER MEDICINE STUDENTS OF THE HEALTHY UNIVERSITARY FUNDATION

This study considers that the development of self-regulation in students is necessary to promote meaningful and inclusive learning of knowledge, but these processes are often not facilitated, especially when theoretical knowledge is integrated with practical exercise, and therefore it is proposed that an innovative strategy be developed that stimulates self-regulation in students in a simulated clinical context, in this case applied to psychiatric semiology. Since this, under an exploratory study methodology, with a quantitative - qualitative mixed descriptive approach, the aim is to evaluate the self-regulation strategies used by students, from the undergraduate of medicine of Unisanitas, by creating a learning environment that integrates the competencies and evaluation established in the Educational Educational Unit of depression. The study concludes that, for both students and teachers, the exercise was useful and relevant when it allowed to show the self-regulatory process. It is also recognized that some discrepancies were presented in the evaluations of teachers and students who are more related to the lack of confidence of students in their own knowledge
