# Urinary lithiasis in the cadaveric donor: responses to urological penumbra from a bibliometric analysis

Abstract The available literature on the implications of urolithiasis in renal donation and transplantation (TxR) with cadaveric donor grafts is limited; the available information has special emphasis on the living donor. To expose the bibliographic production and clinical implications of urolithiasis in the donation process and TxR including the perspective with cadaveric donor grafts. Bibliometric analysis executed by a systematic search of the literature in Medline, Embase, SciELO, Cochrane Central, Google Academic and Web of Science using combinations of the terms MesH "urolithiasis", "kidney calculi", "ureral calculi", "tissue andgan procurement"""brain death", "living donors" and "kidney transplantation" It limited the search to articles published between the years 2000-2022. 23 articles were analyzed. 95.6% of them were published in English, 26% of them were published in journals of the quartile 1, only one third of them have relation to the donor's disease
