# Modelling of the cane bagasse combustion in a non-scented sugar production module

The non-centrifugated sugar (ANC) is a solid, non-refined sweetener with a high marketing potential due to its nutraceutical attributes. However, the production modules of ANC present problems of low thermal efficiency due to the empirical design of its components such as the combustion chamber causing the energy supplied by the fuel (cane bagasse) to be insufficient to meet the energy demand of the process. As a result, low combustion temperatures and high rates of emission of polluting gases are obtained. Consequently, to establish modifications and design criteria in these equipments, it is necessary to understand the phenomena of thermochemical conversion experienced by the fuel. Therefore, the development of a model of combustion of bagasse of cane from techniques of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) in a flat combustion chamber of fixed bed. The model was proposed from the balance of energy and mass in transient state for a heterogeneous system composed by two phases: gas and solid. It takes into account the processes of heat transfer, mass and chemical reactions derived from the processes of thermal degradation of the fuel.
