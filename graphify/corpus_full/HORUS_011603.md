# Study of the factors determining the choice of multimodal freight transport. Application to the Ferrean corridor Train del Carare.

The research carried out in this final master's degree work presents a technical application for the estimation of models of discreet choice through the development of declared preferences surveys that allow, finally, the construction of a logit model (modal choice) for the estimation of the potential of load capture of the Férreo Tren del Carare project. In Colombia, logistics corridors and multimodal projects are assessed and prioritized mainly through vehicle-based transport models as decision variable and utility functions with typical parameters of cost and time. Therefore, one of the most important contributions of this research is to estimate a model of discrete choice with the variables that affect the decision of a means for the transport of goods, which were identified through direct approaches with logistic representatives of companies of the Cundiboyacense region and, where they were determined as predominant – additional to the cost and time - the number of multimodal transfers, the reliability of the transport service, frequency of dispatch of goods and ownership of the transport fleet (third or own). The case study estimates a load uptake for the Carare project of the maximum load (40 to 45% regarding the viability of the transport service) taken (the potential to the transport fleet)
