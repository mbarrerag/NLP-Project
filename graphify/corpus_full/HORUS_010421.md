# Job dimensioning for an insurance sales channel based on a demand prediction model using machine learning techniques.

The present research thesis aims to propose a method for job dimensioning, based on a model of prediction of demand based on the history of the number of policies that arrive at the insurance issuing process. The interest of this research arises from a business problem in a Colombian insurer, therefore, for the study a set of data provided by that entity was used, which corresponds to the amount of policies that have been recorded for the solutions of people and for the different movements that a team of insurance experts executes in the process to issue policies, during January 2019 and June 2022. From this data set, pre-processing of the data is generated, followed by prediction of the number of policies for each solution and type of operation, using classical statistical models and machine learning. Finally, a comparison is generated between the models used, 5 in total for each of the 12 time series and evidence is generated that, from the metrics MSE, MAPE and RMSE, a winning model is generated for each of the series, allowing to suggest from these results of analysis to be taken into account in the projecting process
