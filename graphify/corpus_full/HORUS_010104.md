# DEGRADING MICRO-ORGANIMS OF LAMBDA-CIALTRINE AND METOMIL PROVIDENTS OF THE FRUGIPER SPODOPTER INTESTINE AND ITS POTENTIAL IN BIOREMEDIATION

Chemical insecticides represent the most widely used method of control against Spodoptera frugiperda worldwide, however, pesticide residues cause serious damage to human health and the environment. In addition, an increase in resistance has been observed in S. frugiperda in recent decades. For this reason, new alternatives are needed for integrated pest management in order to mitigate environmental pollution. It has been described that lepidopteral intestinal bacteria are capable of degrading pesticides, so they can be used as a potential biocontrol and for this reason were analyzed in this study
