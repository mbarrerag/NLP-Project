# Value transfers received by prescribers of medicines, services and health technologies in the Colombian health system. 2019-2023

In Colombia, mainly since 2019 began to operate the Registry of Value Transfers between health sector actors (RTVSS), including prescriptions of medicines, services and technologies in health. To present the data on value transfers received by prescribers of services, pharmaceuticals and health technologies in the Colombian health system between 2019 and 2023. Descriptive study. We sought information on the value transfers delivered to prescribers of services, pharmaceutical products and health technologies (recipient 01) reported in the RTVSS from 2019 to 2023, namely, amount of value transfers, number of prescribers that received them and number of entities that reported them. Searches were carried out between January 2 and 6 of 2024 and in December 13, 2024. The total amount of value transfers received by prescribers between 2019 and 2023 was higher value 717 790.26 USD, being 2023 the year with the highest amount (46 050 673.92 USD) and 2020 the year with the lowest amount of payment was (1643 plus 447.54 USD)
