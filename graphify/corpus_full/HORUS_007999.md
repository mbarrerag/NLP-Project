# The nightly boundary layer of the Aburra valley, a tropical urban area with complex topography

The atmospheric processes affecting the Nightly Limit Layer (NBL) raise unresolved questions, more critical in the case of urban, tropical and mountainous areas. This research examines the structure of the NBL in the Aburá Valley - Colombia, a tropical and urbanized region characterized by a complex topography. Here six methods were used to estimate the thickness of the NBL, considering as a reference the method of the minimum gradient of retrodispersion (based on ceilometer data). While all the methods contribute to the understanding of the NBL, it was found that Richardson's Critical Number equal to 0.5 is better suited to the reference method, at least for 2017, indicating that the peaks of the NBL were below the peaks of the surrounding mountains. These results provide technical arguments to be considered in the management of urban air quality in the Aburra Valley and other urban, tropical and mountain areas
