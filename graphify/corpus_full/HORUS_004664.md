# PROPOSAL OF THE FRENCH DICTIONARY OF FRANCE - SPANISH COLOMBIA

A considerable number of bilingual French-Spanish dictionaries take into consideration mainly Spanish from Spain and do not include too many cultural and intercultural information. It is for this reason that it was decided to propose a model of French-Spanish cultural dictionary from France. This is aimed at devising a structure that includes such information together. This paper describes the term of culture and sets out certain characteristics of the cultural content that are frequently presented in French-Spanish bilingual dictionaries. The purpose of this study is to elaborate on the methodology used in our study; this is descriptive and mixed in nature. It also indicates the way in which the sample of words was selected and the dictionary articles were written. Some of these were presented to a group of French students in order to know their views on the proposal. It is concluded that the model is positively valued by the students and that it is useful to find intercultural information about France and Colombia in a bilingual dictionary. Key words: bilingual cultural dictionary, cultural content, intercultural content, French Foreign Language
