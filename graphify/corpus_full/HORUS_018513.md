# 7TH INTERNATIONAL SYMPOSIUM ON DEEP-SEA CORALS

This book highlights a collection of research papers that were presented during the 7th International Symposium on Deep-Sea Corals (7th ISDSC). The symposium took place in 2019, in the city of Cartagena, Colombia. The hosting institutions were Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Universidad de los Andes and Lehigh University
