# Identification in the pre-anesthetic consultation of clinical factors associated with surgical cancellation in a University Hospital in the city of Bogotá. A study of cases and controls.

The cancellation on the day of surgery is frequent, generates costs, delays care and impairs the health of patients. In our environment, the capacity of the pre-anesthetic evaluation to identify the risk of surgical cancellation has not been evaluated. Our objective was to identify factors in the pre-anesthetic consultation able to predict the cancellation by medical cause. We conducted a study of Cases and Controls, we identified the outpatient procedures during 2019 at the National University Hospital of Colombia cancelled by medical cause where we compared the pre-anesthetic evaluation of the cancelled procedures and those that did not. We divided the sample into a training and validation set, and through a logistic regression model we identified factors associated with cancellation, the predictive capacity of the two data sets was evaluated. 390 patients were included, the medical cancellations represented 36.4% of the outpatient and surgical procedures. The vascular and maxillofacial surgeries used, the consumption of hypoglycants, antiaggregators and anticoagulants were identified as independent factors associated with cancellation (p <0.05).
