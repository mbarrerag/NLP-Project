# Corporate Entrepreneurship and its Relationship to Organizational Innovation

In modern organizations, Corporate Entrepreneurship, which is addressed from the perspective of Strategic Entrepreneurship, emerges as a source of renewal of the competitive advantage of organizations, through the transformation of management processes that allow the introduction of Organizational Innovations, by deploying, through a balance-sheet strategy, the exploration and exploitation competencies. This book exposes the relationship between Corporate Entrepreneurship and Organizational Innovation, analyzing human management practices oriented towards generating contribution opportunities and the role played by the exploitation and exploration competencies in transforming organizations that seek sustainable competitive advantages in the current turbulent business environment. Transforming the understanding of the human being within the institutions that carry out this type of practice is fundamental when dealing with this type of enterprise, since it allows the re-signification of the collaborators of the firms by transforming them from simple, easily disposable resources to making them the central axis of the entrepreneurship processes that are developed within the organizations
