# PROPERTIES OF THE HERRADURA AND BEAKER FUNCTIONS?

This talk will address the study of a version of Smale's horseshoe function and Baker's function, as well as its dynamic properties, from a topological perspective. Subsequently, the functions of Herradura and Baker will be defined based on the composition of two geometrical transformations defined on the unit square, denoted by Q. These transformations will induce a series of identifications at the Q border and will use the some notions about Riemann's Surfaces to study the geometric and topological properties of the quotient space generated by these identifications. Finally, some concepts of the ergonomic theory will be introduced to study the dynamics of the system of the horseshoe function
