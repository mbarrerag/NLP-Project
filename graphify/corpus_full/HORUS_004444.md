# Importance and implications of copper exploration and exploitation in Colombia in the context of the energy transition

The transition towards low energy supply and consumption in greenhouse gas emissions has become a goal for sustainable development at the global level. This implies an intensive and growing demand for minerals such as copper, given its fundamental role as an electric conductor. Recent projections indicate that the global demand associated with this transition would increase from 5% of total production of refined copper by 2020, to 10% in 2030 and thereafter to 15% in 2040. Colombia has a high potential as a major commercial dynamic linked to the energy transition. However, in the face of a productive scenario, it becomes crucial to sustainably manage the extraction and benefit of copper in order to minimize its environmental and social impacts, especially in a country with sensitive ecosystems and a large ethnic plurality as in Colombia. To do this, it is necessary to transfer and adjust the progress made in sustainability by leading countries in the industry as Chile and Peru, in terms of recirculation and benefit of copper in order to minimise its environmental and social impacts, especially in a country with a country with a sensitive ecosystem and a large industrial development and positioning as in Colombia. To do this, it is necessary to transfer and adjust the progress in sustainability made by countries leading in industry as Chile and in the gas sector, in terms of Chile and Peru, in terms of the recovery Belt and the existence of Occurrences and deposits of this metal in its territory. This represents a great opportunity for the country in terms of mining-industrial development and positioning in the global commercial dynamics linked to the energy transition. However, in a productive scenario, it becomes crucial to sustainable management the extraction and processing of copper to minimize its environmental and social impacts, especially in a country with sensitive ecosystems and a great ethnic plurality such as Colombia.
