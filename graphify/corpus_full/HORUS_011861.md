# REFLECTIONS IN THE FORESTRY SECTOR IN THE DEPARTMENT OF CALLS AS ALTERNATIVE FOR PEACE AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

This article shows how sustainable management of the forest sector can contribute to the peace process in the department of Caldas. Through meetings and interviews with the main forest actors, a vision of the current situation of the sector and its potentialities is obtained. It was concluded that strengthening governance can enhance and sustain this development through forest policy, incorporating spaces for formal participation and promoting projects such as zero deforestation, reduction of illegality, forest certification, and employment generation; the latter, taking advantage of the potential of 164 000 ha for forest plantations existing in the coffee region
