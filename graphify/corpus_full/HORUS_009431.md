# GENOMIC CHARACTERIZATION OF PASSIFLORA EDULISSF.

Abstract We use DNA sequencing associated with restriction sites (RAD-seq) to explore the genetic structure of Gulupa. Illumina NovoSeq6000 generated data from 111.6 million readings of 117pb. The analyses included quality control with Fastqc and detection of NPS and diversity indices with STACKS. The results obtained allow understanding the genome and enhancing the search for genes linked to pharmacological properties in this plant. Gulupa, genetic diversity, SNP, NGS
