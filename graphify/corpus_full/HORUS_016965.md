# marketing Strategy for the coffee sector in colombia in the u.s. market using strategic futures and Big Data

The coffee sector in Colombia faces challenges such as difficulty in maintaining high prices in an international market with a downward trend and intense competition between producing countries. These challenges might be causes of decreasing trends in Colombian coffee value, impacting severely its economy as it is a fundamental pillar. On the other hand, marketing joining with business strategy requires a prospective exercise since, in dynamic markets, managers must recognize the presence of interactions, looking forward and reasoning backwards to make optimal decisions. In this research, a marketing strategy is formulated for the Colombian coffee sector in US market (its main target market) to face the challenges through of the creation of future scenarios for the brand “Juan Valdez”. The study is based on the analysis of Big Data, the clustering of key variables for coffee consumption and the union of hypotheses resulting from Schwartz axes methodology. It is found that managers must ensure that Colombian coffee consumption in the US enters a new positioning, not only by quality differentiation, but also advancing in the pyramid of brand resonance, by establishing relationships of loyalty directly with the consumer. If Colombian coffee does not adjust the US consumer lifestyle, it might remain in an unfavorable scenario within a highly competitive market
