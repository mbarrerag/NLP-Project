# APPROPRIATION OF THE KNOWLEDGE OF MACHINERY DESIGN BY TRANSVERSAL INTEGRATION OF PROJECT ASSIGNATIONS

Summary In the department of mechanical engineering and mechatronics of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Bogotá, the appropriation of knowledge and capacities on machine design is carried out through a project (by team) that develops over several semesters and subjects, integrating students from different subjects and establishing different team roles throughout their execution. After having appropriate knowledge on static, dynamic, mechanisms, materials, resistance of materials and finite elements, the design process is started with a seminar on design methodology; later in Design of Machine Elements I, and parallel to the corresponding topics, a problem of real design is created conceptualizing its solution until obtaining definitive sketches; to define some of the design parameters of the product to be obtained a short research work in which the students are carried out in which they design non-standardized tests that lead to achieve the required ones. In Design of Machine Elements II they are worked together with the students of Machine Drawing, establishing roles of designers and designers in a way that the detailed design of the machine is carried out while the designers are responsible for designing the pieces and parts, obtaining the planes of manufacture by the components. which mechanical engineering can integrate to seek solutions to technical and/or social problems
