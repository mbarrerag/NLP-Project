# Digging architecture and other addition processes through subtraction: an application exercise of vacuum strategies in the former Moore tube factory

The word stereotomy is linked to a construction idea where, starting from the gradual removal of matter, a matrix mass can be transformed. This subtractive process and its possibilities constitute the main interest of this work on the premise that the void can be the structuring element of an architectural project. The exercise proposes four “strategies of emptiness”, which are projectual tools built thanks to the analysis of eight contemporary architecture projects where the subtractive operations are used explicitly and forcefully, and where the void is protagonist and related to the collective space. These projects are the theater Grand Poly in Shanghai toward, TEK in Taiwan of BIG, the Mediateca of Sendai of Toyo Ito, the Simmons Hall in Massachusetts and the Seona Reid in Glasgow both of Steven Holl and OMA the TGB in Paris, the House gives music in Porto and the Embassy of the Netherlands in Berlin.
