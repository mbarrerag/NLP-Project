# Development of pretreatments to improve the recovery of gold from refractory gold ores

Gold is a metal that thanks to its unique properties has been widely appreciated from antiquity to our days. In Colombia, gold represents one of the main raw materials of export and therefore, the processing of gold minerals contributes significantly to the national economy. Colombia has a great potential to increase its production of gold, since the geological events that occurred in what is today the Colombian territory were the propitious ones for the formation of numerous gold mineral deposits. Now, although Colombia excels as one of the most attractive countries to develop gold mining in the world, many of the mining projects that are now located throughout the national territory in stages of exploration, construction or exploitation, face technical challenges to make the extraction of gold from complex mineral deposits. Thus, an important challenge for the growth of gold mining in Colombia is associated with the development of new technologies and processes that allow to process the so-called _x0093_minerales of refractory_x0094_, which are all those minerals in which it is not possible to extract more than 80% of the gold present when it is processed by cyanurination (meth of mineral). This project seeks to carry out experimental research to develop new treatments that allow to improve the recovery of gold from various types of refractory gold minerals and that allows the decrease of the consumption of toxic chemicals like cyanide. All this is done to achieve the maximum use of the gold seen as a non-renewable resource and the care of the environment, positively impacting the national mining industry, making it more sustainable and efficient. The research proposed here corresponds to the projects of master's thesis of research of students of the Master's Degree in Engineering _x0096_ Mineral Resources of the University, where each student will study a type of refractoriety relevant to the Colombian mining industry (refractariness by telururos, refractariness by copper and refractariness by arsenic)
