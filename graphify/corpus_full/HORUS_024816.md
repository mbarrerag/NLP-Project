# Preparation and characterization of activated charcoal obtained from textile waste of denim.

The objective of this project was to evaluate the production of activated carbon, obtained from textile waste of blends composed of cotton, polyester and elastane by two methods, the first of which by chemical activation with phosphoric acid with pyrolysis at two steps, maintaining a temperature of 300°C for 2 hours and subsequent heating to 500°C for one hour and a second method of activation with a mixture of ferric chloride and zinc chloride at a temperature of 400°C for one hour. The carbons obtained were characterized by the presence of nitrogen fisssioning industry, electronic scanning microscopy, zero-loading point (pHpzc), infra-red spectroscopy and the Boeh method, and their ability to remove commercial aniline in the synthetic and real samples of a carbon-based wool efluent.
