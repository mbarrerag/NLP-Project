# TECHNICAL ACCEPTANCE OF MOBILE APPLICATION FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF MILK NEGOTIALS

The objective of this study was to evaluate the technological acceptance of a mobile application for the management of dairy businesses and identify the factors that influence the intention and frequency of use of these technologies in the dairy industry. For the evaluation, a technological acceptance model (TAM) was selected. A survey was carried out on 122 livestock entrepreneurs, the TAM was calculated by the partial minimum square approach and, for the frequency of use, an orderly logistic regression was used. The greatest influence found on the intention to use was due to perceived usefulness. The size of the business, moreover, significantly increased the perceived usefulness. On the other hand, the volume of milk production, the age of the livestock entrepreneur and his previous knowledge of mobile applications for the management of dairy businesses do not influence the perceived usefulness or ease of use. Also, evidence was presented of the influence that education has on the ease of use and the type of milking on the frequency of use. The information of this study would strengthen management capabilities in the dairy industry, favoring its business performance, which would allow the closing of technological gaps and address the market challenges presented by the sector
