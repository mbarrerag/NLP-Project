# Floor lighting design 2 intensive care unit and floor 3 operating room of a hospital.

This project presents the design under four standards and a comparative analysis of lighting for the two floor (UCI) and three floor (Operations Room) of a hospital, taking into account parameters such as horizontal illuminance level, vertical illuminance, cylindrical illuminance, illumination on the ceiling, illumination on the wall, maximum illuminance, uniformity, limit value of glare (UGR), minimum value of chromatic reproduction index (CRI), simulation height, indicators of rational use of energy efficiency and maintenance factor, to be compared with different national and international regulations, in order to know which standards have the highest demands in each of the parameters. To begin, normative reference data are taken on 24 spaces for the floor of Intensive Care Units and 17 spaces for three operating rooms, followed by designing in the Dialux software under the different legal frameworks of the UNE-EN -1244- 1,ANSI/IES RP 29-20, Lighting Guide 2:Hospitals and Health Care Buildings and NTC 6519-1, then they are evaluated and compared to know which standard of high standards of lighting has been implemented
