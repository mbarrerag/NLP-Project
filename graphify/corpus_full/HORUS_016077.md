# DIRECT EXTRANJOR INVESTMENT ATTRACTION FROM EMERGENCY COUNTRIES, THE CASE OF INDIA AND COLOMBIA

The presence of Indian capital companies in Latin America grows in a representative way in different industrial and service lines. However, the authors of this book have noted with concern how during the last five years the arrival of such capital flows to Colombia has lost dynamism, as well as impact on the generation of positive externalities related to chains of local industries, diversification of exportable production and supply and the formation of strategic associations that favor technology transfers. During that period, the distance from Colombian institutions and business with India increases, as well as the measured or limited interest of state and business actors in the Asian country is confirmed in the face of greater and better economic integration with Colombia
