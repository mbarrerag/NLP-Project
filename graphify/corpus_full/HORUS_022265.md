# Influence of space time variation of environmental factors on the accumulation of heavy metals in macroinvertebrates of the Bay of Buenaventura.

The estuary of the Bay of Buenaventura is impacted by residues of anthropic origin that arrive through the discharges of the main rivers of the region. Also by residues derived from mining and port activity. Considering this environmental problem, the main objective of this research was to determine how the environmental dynamics of the bay influences the structure and diversity of the macroinvertebrates community; as well as, in the processes of accumulation and bioaccumulation of heavy metals, Cd (Cadmium) and Pb (Plome), the maximum concentrations of the sediments and macroinvertebrates of this estuary. To address this, macroinvertebrates samples were taken in two areas of the bay during three different climatic periods, in 2018. Additionally, the samples of sediments and chemical data of the water were taken. The sample of the sediments and the ecological data of the water was calculated each. The highest content of Cd and Pb was evaluated, the most important. in sediments and in the muscle tissue of four macroinvertebrates species, C. arcatus, R. byrdi, S. aculeta and L. panamensis, species, according to the main, established for human consumption organisms (Crustaceans, Cd 0.5 mg/kg and Pb 1.0 mg/kg, fresh weight; Cephalopods, Cd 0.5 mg/kg and Pb 1.0 mg/kg, fresh weight), suggesting that there is no serious contamination by these two metals in the sediments of Buenaventura Bay. Among the environmental parameters that best explained the variability, both of the community structure, and of the accumulation of Cd in the tissue of macroinvertebrates, are: transparency, temperature, salinity and fine sand.
