# Reference values for the number of motor units (MUIX) and the motor unit size index (MUSIX) in a Colombian population of healthy adults.

The MUNIX is a technique that estimates the motor units with greater reproducibility, speed and ease than the previous MUNE methods. It is based on obtaining a potential motor (CMAP) and interference patterns by electromyography of surface. Few studies demonstrate its reference values, none applied to Latin American population. To establish the reference values of MUNIX and MUSIX in a sample of healthy adults, their relationship with anthropometric variables and behavior between muscles. Descriptive study, cross-section, with prospective data collection. Two intakes were performed for each muscle (AB, ADM, EDB, TA, IDF) not consecutive by the same evaluator following the updated protocol of registry of MUNIX (2018). A good correlation coefficient intraclass between the intakes of each muscle was established. For the analysis an average of the data was performed. The correlation of variables was performed by coefficient of correlation of Pearson and tau (560) of Kendall according to the type of distribution. ANUI was performed.
