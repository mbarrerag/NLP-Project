# DEVELOPMENT OF TECHNOLOGICAL COMPETENCES IN DOCUMENTS USED IN AN INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN MODEL

The methodology of instructional design to Analyze, Design, Develop, Imple-mentar, Evaluate (addie) has been widely used in learning training processes for the development of teaching materials and, since 2020, as a response to the conditions derived from the covid-19 pandemic. The objective of the research was to identify the form as an accompanying strategy designed from the adie methodology has an impact on the ICT competencies of teachers and on the generation of digital educational resources (RED) or virtual learning objects (OVA) in the areas of citizen competencies and socioemotional, his-toria and ethics skills, physical education and artistic education for basic and middle education in Colombia. 66 teachers participated with 49 projects of OVA, which produced 146 RED. A mixed study was proposed through a multiple integration design (MI) in which a quasi-experitional pre-post competence was combined with a single group of competences, analyzing the measures of an ICT competence instrument, and a systematic design of grounded theory, taking as corpus the portfolios of teachers during the process of development of their OVA and RED.
