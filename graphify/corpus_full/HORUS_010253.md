# PUBLIC DELIBERATION IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE RESPONSE TO PANDEMIA BY COVID-19: AN ANALYSIS OF THE EARLY RESPONSE IN COLOMBIA

An analysis of the decision-making process was carried out in the first months of the arrival of the COVID-19 pandemic in Colombia and in the months around the beginning of vaccination, in light of the concept of public deliberation and with emphasis on the decisions of the National Government related to the health sector. A qualitative analysis of content was carried out, using as a source documents from the web pages of the Presidency of the Republic of Colombia, the Ministry of Health and Social Protection and the Congress of the Republic and pages of social organizations, particularly representative of vulnerable populations and non-governmental organizations
