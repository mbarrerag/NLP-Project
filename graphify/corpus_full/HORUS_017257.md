# TRASSENTIAL INDIAGATION AS AN EVENING AND AS A CLARING

In the context of the uses made of phenomenology by other disciplines, the transcendental ca-racter of phenomenology seems to be lost. For this reason, this text clarifies the sense of transcendentality, focusing on the explorations of Husserl on transcendental logic.Husserl's research is bilateral: it investigates, not only logical ob- jettivity, but also on the subjectivity that makes logic In the phenomenological work, the clarification is itself an operation in which the same thing emerges that becomes clear: the thing itself is made emerge.The transcendental logic, far from being an epistemological foundation, in the traditional sense, of formal logic, is rather a clarification that reveals the meaning of this: therefore, it does not seem possible to de-strascendentalize phenomenology, as is intended in some uses that are made of it in other disciplines
