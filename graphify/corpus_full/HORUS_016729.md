# Evaluation of the ability to release dye from lactic acid and glycolic copolymer (PLGA) nanoparticles.

In this study nanoparticles were manufactured by nanoprecipitation, using PLGA, which has been studied for use in the controlled release of active substances.The objective of this research was to analyze the involvement of the molecular weight of the polymer (MOLECULAR PESSION 7-17 kDa, MOLECULAR PESSION 38-54 kDa) and the solvent variation - non-solvent on particle size and release profile in mineral oil (poco studied), these variables are relevant since the application given to the manufactured nanoparticles will depend on this.
