# Influence of macroeconomic fundamentals on the volatility of the forwards of the exchange rate in Colombia: a rapprochement with the GARCH-MIDAS model

This document examines the influence of macroeconomic fundamentals on the volatility of the COP/USD exchange rate forwards during the period 2004-2019. To this end, a conditional self-regressive model of variance with mixed data sampling (GARCH-MIDAS) is estimated, which allows for the joint incorporation of short-term daily movements and low-frequency macroeconomic variables. Results show that macroeconomic fundamentals are significant in the long-term volatility of the Forwards and that including low-frequency macroeconomic variables improve the forecasting capacity of the model for short- and medium-term horizons
