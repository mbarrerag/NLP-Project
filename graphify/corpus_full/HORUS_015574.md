# Evaluation of factors associated with cryotolerance, rédox state and the fertilizing capacity of the asnal semen, oriented to the conservation of Colombian donkeys (Equus asinus) criollos

Currently the breeds of asses are in serious danger of extinction, where there has been a substantial decrease in the size of the population. In Colombia it was reported that the population of asnoses criollos in the country for 2010 was 134251 animals and by the year 2015 there was a dramatic reduction to 75072 specimens. One of the consequences of the reduction of the population in the asses, has been given by the indiscriminate export of asne skins to China, for the production of a traditional medicine, which has led to a significant reduction in the population worldwide. Given this, the criopreservation is a technique that allows keeping cells at low temperature without losing their viability and usefulness, it is aimed to inhibit the metabolic activity of semen, guaranteeing its vitality and function through time, preserving it indefinitely. However, the fertility rates with chronic semen is affected by the decrease in the intensity and functionality of the insemination artificial factors. The semen, subject to the different alternatives of cryopreservation, was evaluated by the seminal plasma ratio, total antioxidant capacity (TAC), reactive species of oxygen (ERO) and fertilising capacity. For statistical analysis, generalised linear models (GLM) were adjusted and correlations and regressions were performed. Associations were found between the plasma content, TAC and EROS on the quality of fresh and defrosted semen of asses.
