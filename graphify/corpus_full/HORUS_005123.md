# Analysis of the changes and permanences of the cultural expressions of the natives of the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina that have shaped the root identity

This research sought to analyze the discourses of the leaders of the ancestral root people about their artistic cultural expressions and how these have been affected by the affirmative political actions that have led to the reconfiguration of the root identity in the archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina in recent years. Leading to the identification of changes and permanences that have had the main artistic cultural expressions around the root identity . This research thought to analyse the discourses of the voices of leaders of the ancestral Raizal community about their cultural artistic expressions and how these have been affected by the positive political actions that have led to the reconfiguration of the Rootal identity in the archpelago of San Andres, Providencia and Santa Catalina in recent years
