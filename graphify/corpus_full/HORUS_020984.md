# Critical factors of success in the implementation of business intelligence systems in public higher education institutions in Colombia.

In the current context of the information society, business intelligence (Business Intelligence BI) is a fundamental tool to support organizational decision-making, based on accurate and timely information. Although the rate of implementation of BI in public sector organizations is increasing, it is still a very low percentage, leaving a long way to go. This project aims to study and identify factors that would be critical for the success of the implementation process of BI systems in particular in the institution of higher education in the public sector. It is intended to carry out a literature review that will allow identifying the different typologies, characteristics, needs and effects of BI. Although the study approach is in the public sector, in the review we will seek to include and analyse the experiences of the private sector. Subsequently, we will seek to analyze the information needs of the institutions under study, as well as the way in which they have been addressed until now. This is in order to contrast the findings of the literature review with respect to the organizational reality
