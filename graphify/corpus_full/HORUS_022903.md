# INEMINATION OF ARGIFICIAL AND RURAL LEADERSHIP IN THE BOVINO AGRONEGOCY

The leadership is defined as the ability of a person to inspire, motivate and influence others a positive and lasting change that allows the construction of social fabric. Rural leaders have a responsibility aimed at seeking the improvement of the living conditions of the inhabitants of the countryside by generating new knowledge and the improvement of those preexisting ones aimed at optimizing the agricultural production. Artificial insemination in cattle is the technique by which we deposit a small dose of bovine semen in the reproductive tract (carpet body) of the female in order to reach a viable pregnancy. When we ensure as producers that semen used in in insemination is not only of good quality, but that it comes from a healthy bull and enhancer of the phenotypic characteristics of economic interest, we will have a product (neonate or newborn) that surely impact positively the finances of our bovine agribusiness. It is like this as the artificial insemination in cattle, combined with the principles of rural leadership, it becomes an interesting and useful tool to improve the family economy of small bovine producers.
