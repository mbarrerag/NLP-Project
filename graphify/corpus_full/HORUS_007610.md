# Production and characterization of red light emitters based on organic semiconductor compounds

The development of red light emitting diodes is sought by the high vacuum thermal evaporation method using organic semiconductor compounds. In this development, the way to improve the emission efficiency of the devices will be sought by optimizing the architecture of the devices and including the tunnel effect to improve the injection of load carriers to the device
