# _x0093_The Iku people of two Arhuacas communities in the Sierra Nevada: a gap between statistics and other realities_x0094_

During the semester 2023-1 and as a grade option in the academic program of Social Work of the Faculty of Human Sciences Headquarters Bogotá, the student Ferney Villafaña, belonging to the Arhuaca community has proposed to advance the project of the thesis denominino: _x0093_The Iku people of two communities Arhuacas of the Sierra Nevada: an abyss between statistics and other realities_x0094_, which seeks to analyze the demographic registry in two communities Arhuacas of the Sierra Nevada, through primary information with the application of a census, conversations and interviews, to later contrast the results with the current systems of the entities that allow to identify the percentage of registration and agreement, as well as to describe the advances, the setbacks and projections in the processes of identification, registration and update of the population information systems, the impact on the processes of public policies and own policies, as well as the access to health services, education and social programs. This research will leave as recommendations, some possible adaptations that institutions can make to overcome the barriers in the identification processes in the indigenous communities of the regions of the country and to guarantee the Colombian citizens, for the inclusion and for the inclusion of the same. The project aims to bring together research carried out by the National University of Colombia in the framework of the professional training of one of the members of the Arhuaca community (a student from the country with indigenous peoples) and to create spaces for the dissemination of knowledge through the socialization of the main findings of the study, and to enable them to be discussed with the community and to be translated into an instrument that proposes adjustments to the processes that the institutions are regularly carrying out to ensure that the participation of indigenous peoples is inclusive and active. In this regard, it is the beginning of a process of training in duties and rights that can in future pave the way for the definition and proposal of autonomous identification and registration mechanisms in communities that provide a way of materializing the approaches to public policies that have remained in most cases, such as the differential approach, the intercultural approach, the rights approach, the gender and intersectual approach
