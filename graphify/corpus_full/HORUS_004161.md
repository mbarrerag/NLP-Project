# THE AMBENCE OF AN INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK AS A KEY FOR UNDERSTANDING THE POLITICAL UNDER-REPRESENTATION OF WOMEN IN COLOMBIA

The article analyses the trends in political representation in Congress and at the subnational level (departmental assemblies and municipal councils), finding a low level of participation of women in these bodies of political representation, far from parity and in comparison with international experiences
