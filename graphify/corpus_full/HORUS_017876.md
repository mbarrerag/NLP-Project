# Effect of flower residues compost on some physical, chemical and biological properties of soil

Summary The objective of this research was to evaluate the effect of the application of flower compost on some physical, chemical and biological properties of the soil. The compost used was 100% residues of Dianthus spp., 100% sunflower and mixture (50-50 %), were incorporated into the soil (Tapthic hapludand) at doses of 8, 12, 16 kg.m-2 and a test without application. An experimental factorial design of repeated measurements over time was performed with two factors; the first corresponded to the compost type, the second to the doses. The apparent density, total porosity, organic carbon (CO), soil respiration and soil forming units of phosphate and nitrogen fixers bacteria were evaluated. In the results obtained, the apparent density of the soil was observed with the application of sunflower compost and dianthus at the maximum concentration of 16 kg.m-2 at the maximum concentration of soil and 25% at the initial concentration (1.14 g.cm-3).
