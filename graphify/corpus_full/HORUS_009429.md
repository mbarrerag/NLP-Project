# STUDY OF THE COSMETICAL POINT OF SUBPRODUCTS DERIVED FROM THE PROCESSING OF UCHUVA (PERUVIAN PHYSALIS) IN THE FRAMEWORK OF CIRCULAR ECONOMY

The development of an extract formulation from dehydrated fruits of Physalis peruviana is presented, which could be used as a functional cosmetic ingredient of a future product, starting from the characterisation of the extract and proving that its antioxidant activity is maintained in microemulsion
