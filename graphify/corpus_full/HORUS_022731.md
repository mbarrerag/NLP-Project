# Proposal for an empirical model to estimate the productivity of orange var. Valencia (Citrus sinensis L. Osbeck) in the lower Colombian tropics

ABSTRACT The response of citrus cultivation to the environmental supply determines to a large extent the speed and intensity of the ecophysiological processes of the plant, which affect the development and production of the crop. As main objective it was proposed to analyze the effects of the climatic conditions on the productivity of the agroecosystems citricultural of orange +8216;Valencia=8217; (Citrus sinensis(L.) Osbeck) previously typified in the department of the Meta-Colombia. In an observation window (years 2013-2015) the variables climate precipitation (PPT), maximum temperature (Tmax) and minimum temperature (Tmin), wind speed, relative humidity and solar brightness were analyzed. Using the model CropWat of FAO, the evapotranspiration of reference crop (ETo) was obtained, applying agroclimatic indexes. Using the statistical software STATGRAPHICS Centurion XVI v 16.2.04, an empirical model was proposed that relates productivity in function of agroclimatic indices, for the vegetative and reproductive phenological phases.
