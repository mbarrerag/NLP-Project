# BATTIAL MEGABENTS OF THE EXTREME THIS OF THE BAD CORDILLERY

The deep marine areas of the Colombian North Pacific basin, such as the lower batial zone (between 1800 and 3000 m of depth), correspond to areas with little national exploration on benthic megafauna, as is the case of the Cordillera Malpelo. In these marine elevations are rocky habitats of ecological interest, offering large extensions of heterogeneous substrates and geographical isolation zones, considered of interest as reservoirs of biodiversity where it is presumed to be very endemic. This area was evaluated in April 2022, during an exploration with a vehicle of remote operation (R.O.V) carried out in the Cordillera Malpelo, about 6 mn east of the Sanctuary of Flora and Fauna Malpelo, in which five video-transects radial, were realized, were identified 190 m deep. The video-transects obtained were analyzed for the interpretation of photograms and identification of the main Fauna groups present and visible with this technology. Knowing these communities that inhabit the batial zone of the Colombian North Pacific Basin allows to determine their biodiversity and ecological processes as an important component in the connection of the adjacent ecosystems not emerged from the East Tropical Pacific Corridor Marine Corridor CMAR
