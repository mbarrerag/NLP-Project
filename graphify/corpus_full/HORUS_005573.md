# FRABILITY SYNDROME IN ANCIANS: PRIMARY HEALTH ATTENTION APPROACH

In 1978, the General Assembly of the United Nations (UN) demonstrated an unprecedented demographic transition characterized by ageing societies, which led to the establishment of the World Assembly on Ageing (AME), whose objective was to launch an international programme for the construction of health policies with a positive differential approach to old age, two editions of which were held, Vienna (Austria) and Madrid (Spain) in 1982 and 2002, respectively.
