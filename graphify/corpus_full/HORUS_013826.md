# STRENGTHENING AND ARTICULATION OF A NETWORK OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY LABORATORIES SPECIALIZED IN GENOMIC SEQUENTIFICATION

Genomic sequencing is a laboratory method that is used to determine the genetic composition of a specific organism or type of cell. This method can be used to find changes in areas of the genome and help researchers understand how certain diseases are formed, such as cancer, as well as to diagnose and treat diseases. In animal and plant production, sequencing is also widely used to study the diversity of species and improve their production, resistance to adverse conditions and the quality of their products. This technique includes different steps, such as extraction, preparation of libraries, sequencing and bioinformation analysis. The creation of a genomic sequencing network at the National University of Colombia will allow researchers and students of the university to have access to advanced technologies and make the installed capacity of the four laboratories known by different university entities, becoming a network available for new research projects. The conformation of this strategy of cooperation will facilitate the development of research and innovation of the headquarters, favouring the training and appropriation of knowledge from researchers and teachers, influencing the training of pre-graduate and mainly post-graduate students.
