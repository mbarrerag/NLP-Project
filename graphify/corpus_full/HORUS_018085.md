# BIOMASSPOTENTIALAS A RENEWABLE ENERGY SOURCEIN COLOMBIA

Promoting the development of bioenergy is currently a global compromise. At a national scale, it translates into government policies and strategies, which constitute the basis for the implementation of sustainable projects that contribute to the decarbonization of the energy matrix. The first step for the development of bioenergy is the quantification and characterization of biomass from the point of view of its energy potential. In this framework, this study establishes the energy potential of the residual biomass generated by agricultural, agro-industrial and forestry processes in Colombia. For this purpose, information from secondary and primary sources was analyzed to determine the different biomasses generated from the processes and prioritize some of them Then, the theoretical energy potential of the prioritized productive sectors was calculated from the biomass¿ physicochemical properties. The real potential was evaluated considering the currently unused biomass, the logistics and the conditioning requirements for the gasification process. Finally, the potential by sector was calculated, obtaining a national estimate of 103153 TJ, and the possibility of synergies around the regional potential was visualized. These results were validated by representatives of unions, companies, and government entities, thus verifying a consolidated potential by sector
