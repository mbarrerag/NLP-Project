# PRACTICAL METHODOLOGY FOR TEACHING POTENTIAL VERTIERS TO PREGRADING STUDENTS

The aim of the paper is to present an innovative and practical methodology for teaching about power converters, integrating hardware and software aspects for undergraduate students. A pedagogical approach is introduced that combines the configuration of passive components for various types of converters with an embedded system that eliminates the need for low- or high-level programming. This strategy facilitates learning the theoretical and practical principles of power converters. The implementation of this methodology provides students with an enriched educational experience, allowing a deeper understanding of the role of power converters in integrating renewable energy sources and distributed generation systems. In conclusion, this proposal simplifies the process of learning power electronics for undergraduate students, improving their access to key concepts and their ability to work effectively with power systems. Key words: power electronics; power converters; embedded systems; practical education; hardware configuration
