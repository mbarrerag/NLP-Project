# Two beautiful people read to a bassinet

Every classical author is condemned to be read, among others, also by a caterva of assholes. But the two of us who will be dealing here, after having read the Russian critic Mikhail Bajtín without understanding anything, without contacting the thought of him at any time of his extensive and clever research, proceeded to write a book in which, with great lightness and ignorance, they insult from the title (vulgar, yellowish, telenovelese) the distinguished thinker whose unrivalled work revolutionized literary theory, and who marked a before and after in the twentieth century, since the renewing breath of his thought permeates the whole sphere of human sciences
