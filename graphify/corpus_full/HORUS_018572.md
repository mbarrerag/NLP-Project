# TEACHING METHOD/LEARNING APPLICABLE TO ELECTRIC ENERGY SYSTEMS

Contemporary methodologies are focused on students, who, in engineering careers, are often discouraged by the lack of integration of practical content with attractive and experiential methods. In this article, the design and implementation of a new teaching-learning method is presented. The method proposed here, called integrative knowledge practices and resource articulation, seeks to unify different areas of knowledge with laboratory resources, generating in the student a holistic vision of electrical energy systems. This is composed of three sections: Prelaboratory, Laboratory and Post-laboratory, including in each of several pedagogical moments that the student will perform based on techniques such as active learning, affective teaching and playful learning experience. It is intended to achieve a meaningful and motivating experience, with the use of videos, didactic games, short readings and activities that innovatively motivate students to continue their studies and to research more on acquired knowledge, emulated the situations that can be found in the productive sectors
