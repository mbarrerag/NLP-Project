# Association and diagnostic performance of TIRADS and ACAF/Bethesda compared to pathology report.

Although many thyroid nodule categorization systems have been proposed, none has validation studies in Latin American context. Given the promising usefulness, this study is conducted to evaluate the diagnostic performance of TIRADS and BETHESDA, with respect to the pathology of the surgical specimen. Cross-sectional study monocenter. Data collection from 2017-2021. Only included patients with reports of TIRADS, BETHESDA, submitted to thyroid surgery, with a confirmed pathology report. Calculation of cancer risk for each class of TIRADS and BETHESDA. Diagnostic value analysis with sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value (VPP) and negative predictive value (VPN); and performance by nodules size and age. 920 patients. Nodules were classified in TIRADS 1 6.3% TIRADIS 2, TIRDS 3 15%, TIRDS 4 of 33.7% and TIRADS 5 39.6%. The prevalence of malignity in each class was: 28%, 31%, 47.1%, 67.4% and 85.7% (p<0.05)
