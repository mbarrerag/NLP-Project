# Pd-based nanoparticles stabilized by N-Heterocyclic carbene ligands: Application in the catalytic transformation of N-containing molecules with D2.

Deuter compounds have aroused great interest in recent years due to their various applications. For example, they can be used in mechanical studies, to alter pharmacokinetic profiles and as internal patterns in different techniques coupled to mass spectrometry. Among the different methods reported for obtaining these molecules, we can highlight two: the Isotopic Hydrogen Exchange (HIE) reactions and dearomatization reactions using D2 as a reducing agent. These processes can be catalyzed using transition metals in both homogeneous and heterogeneous ways. In this sense, the metallic nanoparticles (NNPs) have appeared as a new catalysis alternative, especially in the activation of H-H and C-H bonds, due to their great advantages compared to the metals in their bulk formation. Thus, the N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N-N Siva, infra-red spectroscopy of total attenuated reflectance, magnetic resonance with solid-state aromatic and photoelectronic X-ray spectroscopy. It was observed that these NPMs were dispersed in organic and aqueous media due to the stabilization and coordination of NHC ligands to the surface, with sizes between 1.8 and 2.9 nm.. They were tested in dearomatization reactions and IHDs on different lipophilic substrates (pyridine, 2-methylpyridine, 2-(trifluoromethyl)pyridine, 2-phenylpyridine, 4-phenylpyridine) and hydrosoluble (L-phenylalanine, L-tyrosine and L-histidine). PdNPs were active in deromatisation reactions on Ppyridine and its derivatives, as well as with 2-phenylpyridine and its isomers. In addition, an unexpected isotopic change in the deromatisation reaction of the 2-phenylpyridine, with best results using D2 when compared to the PNPNHCs were found
