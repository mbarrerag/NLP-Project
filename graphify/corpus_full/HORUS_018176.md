# BIO BOYACA - ACTIONS FOR CONSERVATION AND SUSTAINABILITY.

Between November 2017 and March 2018, when the region and the country already was reporting the birth of Boyacá BIO, the 794th R & D Call was open. This initiative represented the first part of the Boyacá BIO Call for Action, which totals 24 projects. The complement is a set of 12 Innovation bets, subject to a publication similar to this. As regards R & D, we seek to contribute to the solution of departmental challenges about climate variability, conservation of strategic ecosystems, and sustainable uses of biodiversity. From those axes, groups and research centers belonging to public and private universities of the territory, in alliance with non-governmental organizations, municipal governments, autonomous corporations, and international academic institutions, postulated ideas that would evolve thanks to investments of departmental resources of Science, Technology and Innovation (CTeI), along with counterparts of the actors involved. The following pages expose wonderful results of 12 projects that give us a very good lead to days in which life is possible for all, and in which there are availability of water and food equipment.
