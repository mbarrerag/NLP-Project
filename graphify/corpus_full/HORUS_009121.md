# HETEROGENIZATION OF A-CIANOPHENIENVINILENE IN TIO2 NANOTUBOS FOR THE PHOTODEGRADATION OF CARMIN INDIGO WITH VISIBIL LIGHT

In this work, the 4,4'-((1Z,1'Z)-1,4-phenylene bis(2-cyanoeten-2,1-diyl)) dibenzoic acid (a-CNFV) was synthesized, which was chemically immobilized on the surface of TiO2 P-25 as commercial support and also on previously synthesized TiO2 nanotubes (NT).The hybrid systems a-CNFV/TiO2 P-25 and a-CNFV/TiO2-NT were characterized by UV-Vis spectroscopy, infrared, Raman, and thermogravimetry, and their textureal properties were studied using N2 adsorption isotherm.The evaluation of its activity as a heterogeneous photocatalystizer was studied in the degradation of the carbonic pollutant (IC) using molecular O2 and visible light under environmental pressure and temperature conditions (Figure 1).The results obtained allowed to observe that the free system a-CNFV degrade only 10% of the pollutant system at the end of 60 minutes of exposure, while the systems were demonstrated to P-CNFV2 and PCNV2 and reached by the PCNV2 and TIT2
