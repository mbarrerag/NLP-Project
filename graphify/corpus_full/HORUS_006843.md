# Strategies for integrating the buffer zone of the Regional Natural Park Forest of Galilee (Villarrica, Tolima) into the processes of territorial planning

This document describes the main findings of an exploratory research whose aim is to identify strategies for the integration of the community of the buffer zone of the Regional Natural Park Forest of Galilee to the processes of territorial planning, in order to contribute to the sustainable conservation of the Park. The analysis focused on the socio-ecological conflicts present in this area, examining and comparing the main conflicts of land use, as well as the evolution in the occupation of the territory and changes in plant cover between the years 2007 and 2023. Moreover, the perspectives of various social actors of the municipality or related to the topic of study were considered. For this, individual interviews and a workshop with inhabitants of the zone were carried out. This allowed to conclude that the main challenges correspond to the lack of formalization and ownership of the property, the conflicts by use of soil derived from administrative determinations that do not always contemplate the reality of the territory and the lack of institutional accompaniment in the face of the challenges that involve the sustainable development and protection of the protected area, which directly affects the socio-ecological conflicts. The above allowed us to conclude that the main challenges correspond to the lack of formalization and ownership of property, conflicts over land use derived from administrative determinations that do not always take into account the reality of the territorial and the law of institutional support in relation to the challenges involved in sustainable development and environmental protection, which has a direct impact on socio-ecological conflicts. In response to this, a Understand Environmental Management Plan for the buffer zone is proposed, which has 12 programs that correspond to strategies that seek to link the community to the planning processes involved, and give effect to the strategicity of the project
