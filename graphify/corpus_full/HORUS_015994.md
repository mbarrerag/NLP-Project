# Carnal Technopoetics: fictions, bodies and technologies in contemporary Colombian performance: The case of Praba Pilar and Nadia Granados or how to assemble a monstrous Latin-American cyborg

The present research proposes an analytical and speculative look of two series of performances by Colombian artists and activists Praba Pilar and Nadia Granados: La Iglesia de Nano, Bio, Info, Cogno and La Fulminante, respectively. As part of the postmodern cyborg tropo, a wide-ranging reading is carried out where the theoretical relations between bodies and technologies are problematic from different angles and backgrounds, taking as its main thread the artistic practices, discursive forms and theoretical reflections displayed in its performatic bets. The result is the assembly of the Latin American monstrous cyborg, an entity, between mythical creature and theoretical tropo, which embodies the possibility of a Latin-American perspective on technology and its expressive and political powers .
