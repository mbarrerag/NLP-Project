# Design and development of an interactive teaching tool for the education and promotion of breastfeeding

In Colombia, the practice of breastfeeding exclusively up to six months is reduced. In the Breastfeeding Research Seed and Child Feeding Seed, of the Department of Human Nutrition of the National University of Colombia, a project was formulated with the aim of developing a didactic tool for the promotion of breastfeeding during education and/or breastfeeding counseling to mothers and their families. Different methodologies were adapted and combined, such as the “double diamond” to structure the phases of the project, the PESTEL tool for the process of identifying and understanding the determinants of breastfeeding in Colombia, the tool for defining requirements and determinants of design and the technique of focal group to collect perceptions of the product and the analysis of satisfaction level. As a result, a pedagogical tool consisting of a model of simulation of the mammary gland that presents information on types of milk, sequence of production and output of milk, and types of nipples was obtained. It is concluded that it is possible to contribute to maternal infant health from the interdisciplinary work between the areas of health and design
