# Strategic decisions to invest in renewable energy for generators in a competitive electricity market.

This paper shows a model of strategic decisions of investment in renewable energy for generators in a competitive electricity market, from the perspective of complementarity of resources, diversification and strategic investment in an energy market with two energy sources: Hydraulic and Wind. This proposal develops a model of support for decision-making, so that an investor of electricity projects can choose in which technology to invest in its expansion, taking into account the flexibility and uncertainty in decision-making and the strategic behavior of competitors. This research uses the Options Game that combines the Real Options and the Theory of Games to fully consider the problem of investment. The model uses information of flows with high negative correlation with the Colombian electricity market wind.
