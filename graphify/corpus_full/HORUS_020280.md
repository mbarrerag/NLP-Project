# Analysis of strategy compliance through financial results with emphasis on financing decisions

This case study seeks to analyze whether the strategies expressed by Avianca were fulfilled through its financial results for the period 2015-2020 with emphasis on financing decisions, being this one of the distinguished airlines that provides air transport services for passengers and of cargo domestically and internationally, mainly in the national markets of Colombia. The case will be presented under a mixed qualitative-quantitative methodology. It is qualitative because a review of the management reports, annual sustainability reports, 20F SEC reports, financial statements and other available corporate documents will be carried out, as well as the available sources of financial analysis of Avianca by experts (financial analysts, investors, among others). others). And quantitative because the financial performance is analyzed for six (6) years. In conclusion, we observe that the strategies expressed by Avianca through the financial results are not fully met, for which since 2019 the Company has made a change in its strategies with a view to solving its financial difficulties with a re-profiling in its financing decisions, finally filing in 2020 for Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Law
