# Management system for tourism innovation in the Metropolitan Area of the South Center of Caldas: An approach based on ISO 56002 standard

The document aims to propose a management system for innovation in sustainable tourism in the Metropolitan Area Centro Sur de Caldas: a approach based on ISO 56002 standard, research is descriptive in type, initially the search for literature related to the management of innovation in sustainable tourism in the region is carried out using secondary sources. It emphasizes the importance of adopting a more systematic approach to innovation in the tourism sector, where the standard ISO 56002 can provide a useful framework for the development and implementation of a system of innovation management adapted to the particularities and challenges of the companies of the tourism sector of the mentioned environment. As results are elaborated fundamental documents for the implementation of the SGI as are the guidelines of the implementation plan, an instrument called “State of implementation of the SGI for a company or organization of sustainable tourism in the Metropolitan Area “Centro Sur” de Caldas: based on the standard ISO 56002”, a manual of the SGI, 4 guides called int) guide for the management of strategic intelligence in the tourism sector, b) guide for the management of the intellectual property in the tourism sector, c) guide for the effective collaboration in the management of the tourism sector, the management of the SGI, 4 guides m sector of the mentioned environment. As results, fundamental documents for the implementation of the IMS are elaborated, such as the guidelines of the implementation plan, an instrument called “State of the implementation of the IMS for a sustainable tourism company or organization of the Metropolitan Area ‘Centro Sur’ of Caldas: based on the ISO 56002 standard”, an IMS Manual, 4 guides called: (a) guide for the management of strategic intelligence in the tourism sector, (b) guide for the management of intellectual property in the tourism sector, (c) guide for effective collaboration in the management of innovation in the tourism sector, and (d) guide for the management of innovation ideas in the tourism sector
