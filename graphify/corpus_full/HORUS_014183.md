# Generational integration as a strategy for the recognition and appropriation of peasant environments in Roldanillo, Valle del Cauca

The project "General integration as a strategy for recognition and appropriation of peasant environments in Roldanillo, Valle del Cauca" is an initiative of joint extension led by the Faculty of Engineering and Administration of the National University of Colombia Sede Palmira, which seeks to contribute to the sustainable development and the construction of peace in the Buenavista area, through the intergenerational dialogue, the rescue of traditional knowledge and the strengthening of local capacities. The proposal arises as a response to problems identified participatoryly with the community, such as the intergenerational disconnection, the loss of ancestral knowledge, the weakening of rural economies and the degradation of local ecosystems. In response to this, it proposes three specific objectives: 1. To stimulate the oral transmission and practice of traditional peasant knowledge through intergenerational dialogues. 2. To generate spaces of community participation in food sovereignty, the conservation of biodiversity and the adaptation to climate change. 3. To support the participatory strategic planning of the community to promote co-management and sustainable development processes. To achieve them, it proposes a series of activities such as conversation, exchanges of seeds, community gardens, monitoring of biodiversity, strengthening of the team, and the development of teachers, and other projects. The proposal is an innovative and relevant commitment to articulate the university's training, research and extension functions around the challenges of sustainable rural development and the construction of territorial peace, recognizing and enhancing the knowledge and experiences of peasant communities as protagonists of their own good living
