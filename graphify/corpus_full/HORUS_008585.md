# MUSCULARY CONDITIONS

This article presents a computer application that uses artificial intelligence technology known as 3D vision. This application offers a simple way to allow the interaction of people with diverse muscular conditions with a computer. Despite the abundance of devices in the market able to detect movements and recognize gestures, there is a shortage of specific developments that facilitate access to and use of media and communication oriented to people with motor limitations. The results obtained from using this application indicate that it is a useful tool when integrated into a process of social inclusion, allowing people with diverse muscle conditions to enter more effectively into work and educational environments
