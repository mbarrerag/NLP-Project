# Use of digital narratives to promote empathy in second grade boys and girls of the Agustín Fernández College IED

The educational system in Colombia has undergone great transformations due to the pandemic against COVID 19, affecting the emotionality of children, as well as their academic performance and interaction processes with others. Therefore, this study focuses on promoting empathic experiences and management of emotions through the use of digital narratives in a group of 20 second degree children, ages between 7 and 9 years. It is a qualitative research framed in a methodological design of action research. It aims to measure the empathic response of children (as), based on some items taken from the “Escala of empathy for children and adolescents adapted for Costa Rica” Kathia (2011), which gave way to the elaboration and implementation of 5 workshops.
