# --CORRELATION BETWEEN FOUR QUARTERS OF ROTTORY LIMES AND THEIR FALL FOR FRACTURA

The following endodontics limas are instruments used during the biomechanical preparation. These instruments have the aim of eliminating microorganisms, kerl dentinary and in turn, widening the root canal to achieve desired conicity, and thus facilitate the filling. The fracture of instruments within the root canal can take place during the endodontic procedure and can be presented for two reasons mainly: torsion failure and fatigue by flexion or cyclic, but can also be presented as a combination of both. The fracture of manual slurry of stainless steel is in the range between 0.7 and 7.4%, while those of Níquel Titanium are reported in a range of 0.4 to 21% The ratio of the cut-off angle and the failure by fracture of the instruments will affect the prognosis of endodontic treatments. When we perform a treatment of endodontic primary we will have a prognosticity of 86% favorable, but when we have a history of fractured instruments the percentage of success will decrease.
