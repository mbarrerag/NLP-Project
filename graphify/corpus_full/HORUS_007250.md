# Characterization of the abrasive wear behaviour of three bodies in materials made additively from continuous carbon fibres

The aim of this master's thesis is to characterize the behavior to abrasive wear of three bodies in materials manufactured additively with continuous carbon fibers. To this, a tribometer of wheel rubber and dry sand under the ASTM G65 standard is used. The selected materials include Nylon and Onyx® reinforced with continuous carbon fiber, chosen for its mechanical properties provided by the printer manufacturer Markforged and for its wide application in the industry. A design of completely random test bodies (DCA) is carried out to analyze the results of the wear tests with respect to the orientation of the reinforcement fibers (0°, 90°, 45°-135°, isotropic and without fiber). It is observed that the addition of continuous carbon fibers significantly improves the resistance to wear of the reinforced probetas, regardless of their orientation, which is corroborated by the results of ANOVA and tests of the protetrical and T-test with Bonferroni correction. The study includes an evaluation of the hardness of the probetas, where it is shown that the functional fibers of the material contribute to the hardness, reducing the penetration of the probeta and the protea.
