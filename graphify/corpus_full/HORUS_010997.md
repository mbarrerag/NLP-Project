# MUTANT CORRESPONDENCES. INITIMOUS LANDSAGES

This project proposes an exercise of correspondences as an expanded creation practice in which visual, sonorous and textual assemblies are made possible; intimates connected in distant spaces, diverse bodies and images and confined stories. We are a multidisciplinary group of students and teachers from different fields of the Arts and Human Sciences -such as anthropology, cultural studies, cinema, sound art and plastic arts- that seeks to create dialogues between knowledge and neighboring makings. Thinking about the epistolary genre in an expanded sense, the group's research has allowed us to understand the possibilities that correspondence opens up as an exercise of common doing, as much as critical elaboration and expressive and sensitive practice on the present convulsion of which we are part.
