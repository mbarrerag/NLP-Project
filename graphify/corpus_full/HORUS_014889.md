# Analysis of the process of shaping hunger in the north-eastern region of Cauca.

The present research aimed to understand the processes of shaping hunger in the northeastern department of Cauca, the case of the municipality of Inzá. The method used is framed in the qualitative approach, from a decolonial epistemology and a relational ontology. The tools used for collecting information were documentary analysis, focus groups, interviews, participant observation and field journals. The historical territorial critical perspective, collective construction of the research group “Sociohistorical studies of health and social protection”, was used as a axis of analysis. It was found that the process of shaping hunger in Inzá arose in a network of conflicts and tensions between global territorialities, such as the agro-industrial complex, food regimes, the global agri-food system, the notion of Eurocentric and modernizing development, neoliberal policies and globalization; and local territorialities such as the peasant community, the native culture Nasa, the guerrilla, the paramilitaries, the Colombian National Federation of Coffee Growers, the Colombian State and its institutionality, the agents of drug trafficking, coffee associations, among others.
