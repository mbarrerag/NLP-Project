# POTENTIAL OF RESOURCES IN DETECTION OF CLANDESTIN PHOSES

In Colombia, the identification of mass graves has become a necessary task for the clarification of the truth of the violent conflict.The activity is performed manually in most cases; to identify burial sites is used as input the information provided by the victimists or the reports of relatives of the victims.However, these methods, besides vague, are extremely tedious and expensive.To increase the chances of success of this work, in other countries have been experienced search methods based on geophysical resistivity, magnetometry or radar of penetration of the soil.These methods can only be used in a delimited area, often small, besides presenting challenges if they are in places of difficult access such as swamps, swamps or in places where the conflict persists, which could imply that they are mined. Therefore, there is the need to implement methods that do not involve direct access to the field and that can cover large areas, speeding up the search and reducing the cost in time and in money.
