# Consequences of food aid on the food sovereignty of the indigenous community Emberá de Bajo Gitó – Risaralda

Food assistance is one of the strategies implemented to alleviate the problem of hunger. However, on many occasions, its application has not taken into account the cultural and food characteristics of the receiving communities, as is the case of the indigenous community of Emberá de Bajo Gitó. This research work aims to analyze the consequences of food assistance on the food sovereignty of the indigenous community of Emberá de Bajo Gitó, using a qualitative methodology. Over more than 60 years, the community has been the recipient of food from assistance programmes that do not include their ancestral knowledge or their food diversity. As a result, these programmes have generated a profound transformation in community food practices, affecting both the food sovereignty and, ultimately, contributing to what can be called a "food genocide." This phenomenon has not only impacted biodiversity, but has eroded ancestral knowledge about seeds, preparations and traditional utensils, affecting both the cultural identity and the food sustainability of the community. .
