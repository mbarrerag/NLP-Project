# Evaluation of the antiplatelet activity of compounds derived from caffeic acid obtained by semisynthesis.

Evaluation of the antiplatelet activity of the compounds derived from coffee acid obtained by semisynthesis Cardiovascular diseases remain the main cause of mortality in the world, therefore they are of great interest in public health.The development of new antiplatelet therapies present in the plant kingdom aimed at receptors and key signaling pathways in the maintenance of antiplatelet efficacy preserving hemostasis is a huge challenge for research.The objective of this study was to evaluate the antiplatelet activity of the compounds derived from coffee acid obtained by semisynthesis. To evaluate the antiplatelet effect, the technique of agregometry of light transmission (LTA) was used, with 21 ml of blood of healthy volunteers, following two phases: Phase 1 to perform a sifting of the antiplatelet activity of all compounds and phase 2 to perform the antiagrea concentration curves of 3 of the compounds of higher activity detected in phase 1. In phase I all compounds, tested to the initial concentration of the tamerite of 200 μg/ml, showed antiagres effects of the platelet, linked to the metabolic route of arachidonic acid, with particular interest being the compound 3-(3-hydroxyphenyl) ethyl acrylate, whose antiplatelet potency is comparable to that obtained with the source compound, caffeic acid, which gives it interest as a semisynthetic compound with potential antiplatelet effects.
