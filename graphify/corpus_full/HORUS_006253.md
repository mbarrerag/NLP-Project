# Study of the architecture of monitoring and supervision of Smart Grids technologies for implementation in renewable energy projects in the island of San Andrés

This thesis focuses on the design of a monitoring architecture proposal for Smart grids systems that integrate renewable energy projects. For which a study of three cases of use focused on the monitoring of some key characteristic of the electrical network, such as: the state of the network, the management of consumption and the management of energy. For them the relevant business chaos was established and, in addition, the technologies and information systems that were able to manage the flow of data present in the network, the model SGAM is used to ensure that the processes are not interrupted by having teams of different manufacturers (iinteroperability) or that the system presents vulnerabilities to attack of third parties in the communications network (cybersecurity). During the realization of the use cases, it was taken into account that the proposed architecture of this work was expected, the key performance indicators and the scenarios that could be achieved during the operation of the new system proposed for the electrical network of the island of use of San Andrés, finally took the three architectures product of the use cases and integrated into one, resulting in the proposal of the architecture of this work. .
