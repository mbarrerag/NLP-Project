# Obtaining Nanoemulsions from Longa L Turmeric and its Application in Aquatic Beverage

However, its solubility is low in water solutions limiting its applicability. This research developed a nanoemulsible oil in water (O/W) based on long-term turmeric oleorresin (ORC) to be applied to aqueous drink as a possible alternative to the use of artificial dyes such as tartrazine. Two types of nanoemulsion were formulated and prepared: Oleorresin (EORC) and commercial curcumin 75% (ECC).These were characterized by the percentage of retention of curcumin (CR), total phenol content (TPC); physical stability and particle size (TP) by electronic transmission microscopy (ETM).S stable nanoemulsions were obtained with TP of 10,201nm and 13.847 for EORC and ECC respectively.
