# Evaluation of the response of genotype to the environment in yield and quality of yellow potato diploide (Solanum tuberosum, Phureja Group).

The IGA is a theoretical model used to explain the differential response of genotypes to the environment. Several statistical methods have been developed to break down the phenotypic response based mainly on the general mean, on the effect of the genotype and its interaction with the environment, being treated environmental variance as a factor of confusion. However, the estimation of the environmental effect from the evaluated traits would establish a dependence on the genotype, resulting in a lower adjustment and predictive potential of the model. The integration of environmental covariates to models that associate edafoclimatic characteristics with the traits of interest, with the purpose of increasing the predictive potential and variance contained by the models has been proposed. The aim of this study was to evaluate the sensitivity of the traits of interest in crops of yellow potato diploid to environmental covariates, selecting the most relevant covariable characteristics as parameters in empirical models of multiple regression from environmental variance. The results showed a high variability of performance due to covariables of the hydro component, while the quality traits were mainly affected by the characteristics of the energy components and fisical models
