# IMPLICATIONS OF MINERITY TO OPEN HEADING ON FOOD AND NUTRITIONAL SECURITY IN CAMPESIN POPULATION. CASE: SIBATE CUNDINAMARK.

This chapter aims to describe the processes that the Nasa people in the Indigenous Reserve of Toez, implements in terms of school feeding and to analyze whether these emerging alternatives go beyond being a modern/colonial measure of food assistance to one that contributes to the guarantee of the human right to adequate food. To this end, the experience of school feeding is presented after the collection of information at the Etno-educational Educational Institution Tóez (IEET) in Caloto, Cauca. The experience presented below arises as a result of the characterization of the dynamics that occur around the school feeding of the Nasa people, after the collection of information by means of semi-structured interviews and observation participating with the educational community in the Educational Institution Etno-educational of Toez, (IEET), in the Toez Safeguard, in the municipality of Caloto, Cauca. Among the results were public and community strategies that are carried out around school feeding, as are the School Food Program directed by the Ministry of National Education (MEN) and the food supplement provided by the Association of CabilAC indigenous people (Cauca and go to guarantee)
