# ARCTERISTICS OF THE BUCAL HEALTH SERVICES OF THE CAMPESIN PEOPLE OF LAND-TOLIMA, 2023

The Colombian health system faces multiple challenges, including the gaps between the countryside and the city. These gaps reflect on one side, the inequalities in socioeconomic conditions and on the other, the territorial disparities in the availability and quality of health services.This situation is evidenced among the peasants of the co-organisation of Tierradentro, municipality of Lebanon, Tolima, who face multiple barriers to access to healthcare, a matter that affects their well-being and quality of life.OBJECTIVE: Characterizing the oral health services for the peasant population of the co-organisation of Tierradentro, municipality of Lebanon, Tolima METHODS: Qualitative research with an ethnographic approach. Its use of multiple investigative techniques. The semi-structured interview was applied to parents and community leaders, as well as to the health personnel of the local hospital and to a ¿dentista¿ of the region. The participation of the public observation was carried out in the dental health service of the hospital, in the health post of the correction and in the daily social dynamics, areas where information was obtained and organized in the field journal. The municipal development plan for the period 2020-2023 on health, education and culture, which includes the administration's presentation of the challenges in the provision of health services, proposes the development and strengthening of different strategies to reduce social gaps affecting the health of the population. No information is found on oral health services and no approach to rural care. Dental care services in this rural area are concentrated in the public supply in the town centre of this municipality. This implies the existence of multiple access barriers and, logically, a lag in the oral health of the peasant population of Tierradentro
