# ORGANIZATIONAL STUDIES IN COLOMBIA: A LOOK AT THE ACHIEVED COMMAND AND THE BUILDING WAY

The organizational studies (EO) are a field of knowledge of interdisciplinary nature. They emerge in the European context, between 1960 and 1980, as an alternative approach to the American gaze. From their origin, the EOs sought to claim the European academic community and scientific production, as well as their identity and problems related to the organizations. This, not only when considering the latter as a social fact, but also when studying the activity of organizing and becoming proper to the dynamics of being organized. Over time, the emergence of the EOs attracted the attention of academics beyond the European context, among them several Latin Americans. Some of them have been articulated with researchers linked to the European community; others have done so in different ways. The truth is that, in general, they managed to promote work in different countries of the region, among which Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Chile, Argentina, Ecuador, Peru and Venezuela are highlighted. In Colombia, they have emerged various contributions in relation to the study of the administration and organizations. These date back, in the beginning of the twentieth century; however, only by the end of 2016 a group of researchers formally managed by Organo. and the analysis of academic production in the field of administration. The main networks and academic events are described below and the doctoral studies are reflected in the consolidation stage of research in this field. Finally, the contents of teaching in administration and organizations are analyzed in the undergraduate and postgraduate programs offered by higher education institutions (HEIs) in Colombia.
