# Agro-industrial assessment of nutritional blocks for precision feeding of cattle with methane gas mitigation potential

The food agroindustry produces nutritional blocks for the cattle farming sector, as an option to improve the quality of the animal diet, its health and productive yields. However, in accordance with the demand for greenhouse gas emission reductions (GHV) by livestock, mainly methane gas (CH4), it is necessary for products to provide their nutritional benefits in balanced proportions for a precise diet, and also to contribute to the mitigation of climate change. This study was carried out at the campus of the International Center for Tropical Agriculture (CIT), Palmira, Valle del Cauca. Its objective was the evaluation of nutritional blocks for cattle in the stage of lifting. For the elaboration of the blocks were used: fruits of Enterolobium cyclocarpum (EC), leaves of Tithonia diversifolia (TD), pods (LP) and leaves of Leucaena leucophela CIAT20561 (LL). A commercial block (BC) was used for control. During the study, measurements were carried out during a period of 60 days, making data take-up every 15 days.
