# Longitudinal modeling of Mexican couples' migration to the United States and their fertility

Migration and fertility generate changes in the demographic dynamics of the countries of origin and destination, hence the importance of their study. This grade study analyzes the relationship between international migration to the United States and fertility in Mexican couples. The information comes from the Mexican Migration Project (MMP), which provides the history of household heads’ events. With the project’s data, the history of Mexican couples’ events is reconstructed because migration and fertility are family decisions. Through generalized estimation equations, the probability of the couple having a child is estimated, given that one or two spouses migrated and the probability that at least one of the spouses migrated given that they have children. Thus, longitudinal analysis is used in demographic data, which have been studied mainly with cross-sectional data.
