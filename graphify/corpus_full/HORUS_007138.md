# Analysis of the implementation of international air service agreements with Colombia in the development of its air connectivity

The present final master's work entitled Analysis of the implementation of international air services agreements with Colombia in the development of its air connectivity has as purpose the analysis of the air services agreements that Colombia has with other countries around the world, since the impact on international connectivity in the Colombian market, and the assessment of the access to air traffic of the participants in the agreements. For the development of this research, a general background analysis was made on the constitution of international agreements, which allowed to understand the scope and conditions governing the terms of exchange in the field of air transport, after an identification of the agreements signed by Colombia and the traffic rights that it has with the participating States. This information is support for analysing the relevant international routes in passenger traffic, and to assess the competitiveness of the market with the access of operators and their offer of chairs in the level of occupation of the most influential regions of international traffic with Colombia. This analysis highlights the importance of increasing the number of agreements with other States, making the adjustments, in other cases, and achieving a good degree of liberality in the terms of negotiation to strengthen the country's connectivity with the final economy. This information is supported for the analysis of the relevant international routes in international passenger traffic and then be able to evaluate the competitiveness of the market with the access of operators and their offer of seats in the Occupancy level of the most influential region of international traffic with Colombia. This analysis concluded on the importance of creating the number of agreements with other States or the necessary adjustments in other cases; and the degree of liberality in the negotiation terms to strengthen Colombian's connectivity in the world
