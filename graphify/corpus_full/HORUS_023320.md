# CONTENT DESIGN, CONTENT VALIDE AND FACIAL VALIDITY OF THE FAMILY CAREER'S ROLE ADOPTION IN THE ADMINISTRATION OF ROL-M MEDICINAL PRODUCTS

: Family caregivers of people with chronic illness are seen in the need to develop cognitive, emotional and social skills that allow them to respond to a number of instrumental tasks for which frequently it is not prepared, such as the administration of medicines (1). From Nursing there is recognized the need to address the different transitions of the human being among which is the adoption of the role of the family caregiver of chronic patient. This process of transition towards the role of caregiver takes greater relevance insofar as in recent years has increased the number of people with chronic non-communicable diseases (NS) with increasingly complex care needs (2). The presence of healthy practices allows the development of process indicators: Optimum role work (execution), Role organization, Giving sense to the exercise of role (3), operationalized through the Instrument of How to adopt the role of family caregiver of chronic patient (ROL)Is this method of managing family based on the knowledge of the concept of nursing derived from the Colombian context (4) and from which other concepts that can be derived that cover the routine work of family caregivers as it is the use of the care provider of medicines in the home
