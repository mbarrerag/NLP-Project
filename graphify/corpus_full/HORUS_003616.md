# EFFECT OF NI AND CE DOPANTS ON THE MICROSTRUCTURE AND ELECTROCHEMICAL BEHAVIOR OF THE YSZ SYSTEM

We present results for the deposition of coatings of zirconium stabilized with Yttria (YSZ) and doped nickel and cerium. The coatings were grown from an YSZ ceramic target symmetrically covered with metallic (Ni, Ce) pieces on silicon ang glass substrates, via the RF sputtering technique. The microstructure of the coatings was characterized by means of X-ray diffraction (XRD), the chemical composition was determined through X-ray dispersive energy (EDX) analysis, and the electrochemical response was evaluated via impedance (EIS). The XRD analysis showed that the coatings exhibited peaks belonging to the target material, dopant elements, and oxides . Electrochemical analysis indicated an increase in electric properties of the coatings grown with the addition of Ce and Ni
