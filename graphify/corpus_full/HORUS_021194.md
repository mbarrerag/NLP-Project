# Determination of reactive fractions of silica and alumina in metacaolin

The production of concrete manufactured with conventional portland cement (OPC), emits between 6-8% of carbon dioxide of anthropogenic origin. To reduce the impact of these emissions several strategies have been developed, including the use of alternative cementing materials such as the re-tweet. Pozzolanes are materials that by themselves do not present any cementing property, but that finely ground and in the presence of calcium hydroxide (Portlandita), react to produce hydration products similar to those obtained during the hydration of the OPC. The idea behind the use of the pozzolanes, is to take advantage of the production of Portlandite during the hydration of the OPC, since this apart from producing C-S-H which is the main product of hydration, produces in a secondary reaction portadite. This material if it helps to maintain the pH of the cement hydrated, does not contribute significantly to the resistance. However, if it is partially re-moved with the pozzolans, these can react with that Portlandite and generate phases that contribute to the resistance of the cement.
