# Characterization of the compound CsPbBr3:Ce synthesized by solid state reaction method

The compounds of type perovskita constitute one of the most exciting families of materials studied during the last decades, due to the diversity of physical properties attuned by compositive variations. Its characteristics have resulted in a wide range of technological applications in anodic elements for solid oxide fuel cells, highly active catalysts in electrochemical energy conversion reactions, substrates for high efficiency solar cells, piezoelectric or dielectric sensors, catalysts in high temperature reactive systems, magnetoelectric and magnetoresis devices, magnetic semiconductor and spintronic semiconductors with utility in dynamic access memories or ferroelectric and non-ferrous random access memories, hard disk reading-writing heads for the storage of high information capacity in magnetic support and devices for the polarization of the electron current, magnetic sensors, Bromurtronics, among others. Although the potential for the applicability of perovskits reaches areas such as medical physics, this perspective has not been sufficiently explored.
