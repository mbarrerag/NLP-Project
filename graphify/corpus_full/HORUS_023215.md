# The objective paradigm in the responsibility of banking institutions for electronic fraud: a look from the obligations of result.

The work proposes a development of critical and proactive research giving a different interpretation to the current position of the Supreme Court of Justice, in relation to electronic fraud events. The work develops the question: What should be the civil liability regime applicable to banking entities in electronic fraud events, in the development of irregular deposit contracts for savings account and current account in Colombia? And it offers as a hypothesis to the previous question: that in electronic fraud events the current jurisprudence of the Supreme Court of Justice provides that the damage suffered by the victim in irregular deposit contracts (current account and savings) should be borne by the bank, under the allocation of strict liability for created risk or social risk. The assignment of responsibility in this type of contracts is inapplicable, since these are presided over by the general regime of obligations, being the way of analysis for these events, the theory of the obligations of result.
