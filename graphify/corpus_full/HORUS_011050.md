# INTERNATIONAL PLENARY: HYDROGEN PROGRAMMES IN LATIN AMERICA

The European Commission presented the RepotEU plan [1], to speed up the transition to clean energy and to increase Europe's energy independence. In March 2023, it adopted stricter legislation to speed up the deployment of renewable energies to make Europe independent of Russian fossil fuels well before 2030. In the short-term measures defined in the RepotEU plan, new State partners providing reliable international renewable energy, or low carbon, are considered to be reliable partners in the production of renewable energy. Latin American countries are partners with great potential to produce and supply green hydrogen, thanks to the availability of significant renewable energy resources such as photovoltaic, wind, hydro and thermal energy to produce the electricity needed for electrolysis of water. All these options are currently explored and materialised in development projects and operation in Latin America. In this scenario, it was proposed to create export hubs in the Argent-de-Caribbean region
