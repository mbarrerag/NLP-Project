# Flexible progressive housing: For middle-income owners. Barrio Calderón Heredia 1st stage Pitalito Huila

When performing the qualified observation of the Calderón Heredia neighborhood, the current conditions of the housing are recognized, identifying the problems and deficiencies that prevent the growth realized to adapt to the changes in the needs of its inhabitants, maintaining the quality of the housing. It is the case of the lack of lighting and ventilation, the complete occupation of the property and the existence of closed spaces. To characterize precisely the current situation, a planimetric survey of the neighborhood is carried out, analyzing its occupancy and construction rates, simultaneously it reviews the municipal regulations, POT Agreement 023 of 2021 and are taken as reference of the current state of the neighborhood and the five houses that are being constructed by stages, in order to propose different alternatives in the buildings without building, implementing new concepts in this occupation process by stages.
