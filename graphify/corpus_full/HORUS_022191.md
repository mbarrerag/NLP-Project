# Teleworking, a new form of work organization with behaviors of cyber-bullying.

A public entity must be prepared for a prompt and timely response to labour disputes and cases of cyberbullying, have protocols that avoid job harassment and allow for the intersection of manifestations of persecution, abuse or discrimination against any teleworker, with the aim of having as key the identification and prevention for the development of an organisation that operates remotely or in isolation. (...) There is a need to develop public policies that reflect the mechanisms of prevention, ways of reporting, procedures by which the person who commits the conduct can be punished, but also repair the worker affected by the conduct. Likewise, it is necessary for public organizations to develop measures of prevention of occupational harassment, to consider on the part of their institutional offices of human resources possible practices that constitute labour harassment, regardless of whether it is through electronic mail, calls, instant work applications, video calls, etc. and in the provisions of the General Disciplinary Code to include a disciplinary type of occupational harassment, since although in the chapter of the prohibitions there are errors such as 2.
