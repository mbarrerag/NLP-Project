# GOVERNANCE AND STRATEGIC MANAGEMENT OF COMMUNICATIONS

This book includes aspects related to public policies related to communications in the Special District of Science, Technology and Innovation of Medellín in the period 2020-2024 and places the pillars of this policy and the developments of the programs implemented during the administration. It highlights the importance of the relationship of communications for citizen participation and the use of information and communication technologies to interact with communities from the state-municipality. On the other hand, it addresses the role of alternative, independent, community and citizen media (MAICC) in putting issues of citizen interest on the public agenda, as well as the construction of social fabric from communications with a democratic perspective
