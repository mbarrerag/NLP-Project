# Strategic content proposal for social networks Facebook and Instagram of the In Situ Museum of the Malaga Culture Foundation, located in Palmira, Valle del Cauca

The following degree work aims to know the characteristics that should be hosted by a communication strategy for social networks, with which to disseminate the activity of the Museum in Site of the Malagana culture in the municipality of Palmira, in the Valle del Cauca. For this reason a process of research has been carried out methodologically complementary for the analysis of the findings, starting from a documentary review of experiences of the mass organizations in the social networks Facebook and Instagram of organizations of international character such as the Museum of site and sanctuary Pachacamac in Lima, Peru, as well as the Museum of Gold Bank of the Republic of Colombia at national level, and finally the review of the platforms in the mentioned social networks of a local institution, the MUSA Archaeological Museum Headquarters Casa del Marqués, located in the city of Bogotá. From the results obtained in this investigation, the aim is to identify some of the main characteristics to contemplate in the creation of a strategic proposal of communications in which both contents are accommodated, as services adapted to the multiplicity of publics present in the social networks with the purpose of creating, maintaining and developing more effective and participatory ways of maximizing the access and with this, the knowledge. and finally the review of the platforms in the empowered social networks of a local institution, the MUSA Archaeological Museum House of the Marquis, located in the city of Bogotá, was carried out out. From the results obtained in this research, it is intended to identify some of the main characteristics to be considered in the creation of a strategic communications proposal in which both content and services adapted to the multiple audiences present in social networks are hosted in order to create, maintain and develop more effective and participatory ways to maximize access and with this, the knowledge of cultural heritage represented in the extraordinary researches preserved by the Museum in situation of the Malaga Culture
