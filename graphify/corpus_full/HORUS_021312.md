# Implementation of strategies to increase resilience to climate change and value aggregation to products derived from the cultivation of chili pepper (Capsicum annuum) in Caldas department

In the Institute of Biotechnology and Agro-industry until 2021, a fertiriego farm was used that is located in its facilities for testing crops of projects financed with international funds. Due to the good results obtained with this strategy that increases the resilience to climate change of such crops in conjunction with the application of good agricultural practices, it is proposed to implement in this plot a short cycle (ají) crop that its productive cycle starts 60 days after its transplantation and is maintained until 18 months later. In addition, the aji can be associated with other perennial crops that are managed in the region like avocado, providing benefits in terms of pest and disease control. This project will be carried out through the analysis of the soil-water-plant-climate relationships that allow to evaluate the productivity and quality of the aji. In addition, processes of aggregation of value to the fruit or its residues, with their corresponding economic and environmental evaluations. This latter, will be carried out through the analysis of life cycle (LCA) based on inventories based on the primary information obtained from the fertirigo trials. This information will be shared with the family farmers and agro-industrial companies that make part of the company of the Cata
