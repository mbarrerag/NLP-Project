# Characterization and study of devices based on ZnO:Co nanostructures for application in non-volatile memory using a transistor configuration.

In this work samples of thin zinc oxides doped with cobalt (ZnO:Co) were prepared by means of the method of cathodic spraying (DC magnetron co-sputtering), varying parameters of scnthesis such as the temperature of the substrate, deposit time and power of the whites. With the aim of identifying the effects on structural properties, morphology and electrical characteristics, measurements of X-ray diffraction (XRD), of electron scanning microscopa (SEM), of atomic force microscopa (AFM) and voltage measures (current curves IV) were performed. The synthesized samples were submitted to a process of reciding after a temperature of 473K during a time of 2 hours. The presence of the Wurtzite phase of the magnetic strength was found from the measurements of XRD. The sample was observed to be the size of the crystallites, the power of the whites and the deposit temperature.
