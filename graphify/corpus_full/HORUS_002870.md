# Formulation of productive projects for victims of the armed conflict in the municipality of Fómeque (Cundinamarca, Colombia)

This research addresses the question: How to formulate productive projects for victims of the armed conflict in the municipality of Fómeque, Cundinamarca? In this sense, it seeks to identify the problems in the formulation of these projects, develop strategies to overcome them and analyze their contributions to rural development. The municipality of Fómeque is classified as receiving victims, mainly of displacement, and to date does not have productive projects for formulated victims. However, the law of Victims and Land Restoration, Law 1448 of 2011 regulates the measures of assistance, care and integral reparation to the victims of the internal armed conflict in Colombia, in order to guarantee them access to justice, truth, reparation and guarantees of non-repetition. This implies that, in compliance with the law, territorial entities formulate productive projects for and with victims. To advance in this purpose and address the problems of the formulation of these projects, the present research proposes a methodology with differential and intersectional approach, through the formulation of semi-structured interviews and focal groups, which contribute to the diagnosis.
