# SOUND HUMIDITY IN THE GUERRERO PARAMO COMPLEJO, 1981-2020

The Páramo de Guerrero Complex (CPG) is one of the most involved environments in Colombia, mainly by agricultural and mining activities. These activities alter the plant cover and water quality, as well as its distribution in space and time, affecting both the valuable ecosystems of this zone, as well as the urban settlements that are supplied from its waters. In this work we analyze the spatial temporal variability of the volumetric humidity of the soil using data from the ERA5-Land reanalysis. This product has a spatial resolution of 0.1°, a temporal resolution of 1 month and comprises four layers of the soil: 1) 0-7 cm, 2) 7-28 cm, 3) 28-100 cm, and 4) 100-289 cm. For each layer, and each cell, we find a statistical description of the soil humidity and we evaluate the annual cycle, the interannual variability and the trends of the monthly average humidity. The results indicate the following: 1) The CPG is generally more humid in the north-eastern and south-western extremes, and less in the central zone; 2) The three layers of the upper and upper ones have the mean values and the pattern of the most distinct variability and the most distinct from the soil
