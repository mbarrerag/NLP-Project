# IMPROVING TECHNICAL AND TECHNOLOGICAL INFRASTRUCTURE OF THE LABORATORIES OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY - HEADQUARTERS 2023-2024

In order to improve the technical and technological infrastructure of the laboratories of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Sede Caribe, we apply to this call for funding the acquisition of furniture that meets technical and safety specifications for the storage of reagents, equipment and work areas; safety instruments for laboratories for the treatment of accidents by handling reagents, external maintenance services; acquisition of modern equipment; replacement of obsolete equipment; inputs and equipment necessary to carry out internal maintenance and transfer of the laboratory operating technician to CECIMAR (Santa Marta). These actions are of cross-cutting impact to laboratories of the Caribbean Headquarters and seek to improve the services offered for teaching, research and extension
