# The Relationship between Knowledge Management and Dynamic Capabilities

Abstract This article aims to discuss theoretically the relationship between knowledge management and dynamic capabilities, because of the little exploration that has taken place in the literature on strategic direction. This discussion is carried out around the theoretical approaches of the theory of signature, the Vision of the Firm Based on Resources (RBV) and the Vision of the Firm Based on Knowledge (KBV), from which it is argued that knowledge management allows the strategic renewal of the organizations. Therefore, this article exposes the influence of knowledge management on dynamic capabilities of detection, learning, integration and coordination. Finally, some empirical theoretical and practical implications are offered
