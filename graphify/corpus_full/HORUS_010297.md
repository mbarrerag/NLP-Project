# The Health Reform Project: A Reflection from the Academy

On 13 February, the National Government presented to the House of Representatives the articulated of the bill of Reform to Health in Colombia. The initiative seeks to improve and strengthen the General System of Social Security in Health (SGSSS) to guarantee the provision of services as a universal right, and with this, to recover control of public resources in this heading. Likewise, it proposes a health for life, through the implementation of a preventive and predictive system and the improvement of labor for health workers. Although it is not the only reform promoted by the Government, which has also opted to rethink the systems of pensions, labor, education and tax, the Health Reform has provoked intense debates, voices of support and bitter disputes by many sectors, associations of professionals of different specialties, associations of patients, institutions of health promotion (EPS), unions and population in general.
