# Domina PsoC5LP and Kodular: 11 projects to integrate hardware and mobile applications

Domina PsoC5LP and Kodular is a practical guide for integrating hardware and developing mobile applications. Through 11 projects, it explores the use of the PsoC5LP and Kodular microcontroller, combining theory, external devices and applied solutions to create innovative systems. The book is divided into two main parts: an introduction to the PsoC5LP and the development of applications in Kodular. Its chapters range from the basics to the implementation of practical projects. This edition updates examples and programming techniques, optimizing compatibility with new versions of software and development tools. It is aimed at students in electronic engineering, mechatronics and systems, as well as professionals in automation, telecommunications and embedded software, it is also useful for hardware design enthusiasts and programmers who seek to integrate devices with mobile applications in the need of advanced knowledge in programming. -- Contracadas
