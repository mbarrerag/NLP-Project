# Effect of a muscle therapy intervention to reduce work stress by promoting coping skills of formal care staff of patients with neuropsychiatric disorders in a residential care device

Given the psychosocial conditions of risk that formal caregivers of people with neuropsychiatric disorders face, this research was developed to determine the effect of a musicotherapeutic intervention on their level of stress. The present study has developed with mixed descriptive scope annuated with qualitative preponderance, with a sample of three workers of a social care device in the city of Bogotá, Colombia, who participated in a 14 session musicotherapeutic intervention in which information was collected through Sessional Records, Intralaboral and extralaboral Psychosocial Risk Assessment Questionnaires, Questionnaire for the Evaluation of the Pre- and Post-Intervention Stress and Psychosonic Indicators of Bonding Degrees. The findings of the quantitative branch indicate a reduction in stress symptoms in 2 of the 3 caregivers, passing from being in very high levels to high levels. The third caregiver increased her level of stress, going from not presenting stress to presenting a low level.
