# WORKING PLAN RESEARCH GROUP ON COLLECTIVE AND ENVIRONMENTAL RIGHTS _x0096_ GIDC: POPULARY ENVIRONMENT, ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE AND CLIMATE CRISIS

Environmental injustice arises from the persistence of an irresolute environmental conflict (including the climate crisis), in which some human beings unreasonably appropriate from what belongs to others (to many or to all) and, therefore, ignore, suspend, eliminate or violate the rights that lie at the head of subjects of law. Given this situation it becomes pertinent to address from a critical environmental theoretical perspective, the political legal issues related to the environmental and civilizing crisis, as well as its causes and consequences on ecosystems and cultures, To this end is guided the Work Plan of the Research Group on Collective and Environmental Rights - GIDAC, which seeks to develop various tasks to achieve a change of paradigm from critical environmental approaches and a broad understanding and a deep debate on the various environmental problems and conflicts, which it is necessary to overcome or resolve from the environmental participation, which must irrigate the different state, social and community bodies, where the movements and networks for the defense of the environment, contribute to the pervivence of present and future human life and other beings on the Earth. In such sense, a comprehensive and systemic understanding of the rights, the policy and construction, the society, the They do not constitute, nor force shirts and stagnant segments, but, on the contrary, they are conceived as interconnected elements that are analyzed integrally and systemicly. A second block is composed of a deepening of the diffusion of the new knowledge through three great exercises, i) increasing the spectrum of research products generated by GIDC, ii) increasing the activities of social appropriation of knowledge, through the support to the members of the GIDCA for participation in academic events (congresses, seminars, forums, etc.) either as attendees or as speakers and iii) developing own exercises of discussion of knowledge as dissemination events on environmental law issues, environmental policy, environmental conflict, environmental justice and analysis of the climate crisis. Finally, a third structural axis in the development of the activities proposed by GIDCA, has to do with increasing cooperation and publication with other related research groups, both from research groups belonging to the National University of Colombia, and some outside the University.
