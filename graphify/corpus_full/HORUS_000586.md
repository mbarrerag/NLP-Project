# Predictive analysis and development of a quantitative model for the study and segmentation of the pharmaceutical market for high cost pathologies

Currently, Colombia faces important challenges in its health system, especially in the efficient management of resources, coverage and administration of service providers. Within this context, the pharmaceutical sector, in charge of the marketing of medicines, faces specific challenges due to the limited coverage of the system, and the lack of robust information systems.To ensure adequate access to therapies according to the needs of the population, there is a need to analyze the information of the market of high cost medicines and the Colombian health system, with the objective of supporting the decision making in an analytical and informed way.This work proposes the application of automatic learning models and techniques of data mining to address those needs, focusing on the construction of a model of clustering inspired by the RFM methodology (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) (Hughes, 1996), adapted to the context of the pharmaceutical sector.For this, the adaptation allowed to capture the financial dynamics of the RFM model, replacing the dimension of recency by the days of inventory of each client and frequency by variations in purchases. Finally, the model trained allowed customers to be classified according to their historical purchasing behaviour and predict their possible future evolution, facilitating the identification of segments with different financial risk profiles. This classification offers a practical tool for making data-based business decisions, allowing the implementation of differentiated strategies for each group and optimizing the management of the high-cost drug market in Colombia. The methodology developed is scalable and can be adapted to other pathologies, thus consolidating a replicable solution for different contexts within the pharmaceutical sector. .
