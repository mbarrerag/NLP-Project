# Comparison of technical requirements for the connection of new wind and photovoltaic energy projects to electrical power systems.

In the world and Colombia in recent years, the need to include non-conventional sources of renewable energy into power systems has become a major challenge due to the limited experience and knowledge regarding the design, construction, operation and maintenance of these technologies. One of the important aspects to be taken into account for the inclusion of non-conventional generation systems is the assessment and analysis of the impact that the connection of a new plant can generate on the safety and reliable operation of a power system. For this reason, it is considered important to identify which countries are leading in the world in the development of this type of technology, in order to carry out the bibliographic review, first of the requirements of technical requirements set out in its network codes and second of the technical studies of proposals for connection of new plants to the power systems carried out by experts in the field. All the above in order to extract information from relevant aspects and conditions that must be taken into account for the implementation of this type of projects, which can serve as technical support to develop technical conditions related to the operation and design, construction of the regulation and generally for companies interested in the production of power
