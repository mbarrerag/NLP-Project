# Counting on generalized Fibonacci Objects

The general Fibonacci numbers play a significant role in the combination, appearing in the list of various combinatory objects. This thesis investigates the generalised Fibonacci numbers through the ECO method (Cyptollables), culminating in the introduction of a new class of words listed by these numbers, called generalized Fibonacci words. Using these words, we construct two additional families of objects: the generalised bar charts of Fibonacci and the generalised polytops of Fibonacci. The combinative wealth of these families is explored in detail. For bar charts, we present new results on their enumeration for various statistics. For polytops, we provide partial findings on their standard volume, their $f$-vector and structure, shed light on their interesting geometric and combinatory properties .
