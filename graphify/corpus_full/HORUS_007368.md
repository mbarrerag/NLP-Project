# Sociolinguistic aspects of bilingual education in the village of Paletón, Indian safeguard of Jambaló - Cauca. Description of a traditional model of its own

The aim of this research is to carry out a characterization of the sociolinguistic aspects of bilingual education imparted in the Educational Institution of Paletón us Ki’pnxi Kiwe del Resguardo indígena de Jambaló. An inquiry has been made with emphasis on teaching the nasa yuwe language, understanding that the pedagogic and methodological methods used in the Institution are linked to the cultural practices and traditions of the community, together with the search for a model of own education. In this study, priority has been given to a sociolinguistic perspective, focusing on the teaching of languages in their social and community context. This implies a deep understanding of how languages are used and valued in the daily life of the community. The collection and analysis of data has been carried out through the field work, surveys and interviews. To evaluate the current state of bilingualism in children attending the Institution was considered necessary to understand and reflect on the methodologies, the use and environments offered by the Institution for the languages of the community.
