# ETHICS AND VALUES: FUNDAMENTS, CHARACTERISTICS AND DEVELOPMENTS

L a éthética is an area of knowledge that aims to orient human action in a rational sense. It is stated that it had its origins in ancient Greece based on the thoughts and proposals of philosophers who sought to understand human action. Since the seventies and eighties of the twentieth century, and given the corruption scandals that come to light, it begins the concern for the effectiveness of education in ethical issues that were imparted to the present day. The present document aims to carry out a foundation of the main concepts associated with ethics and values, to show its evolution and its most important characteristics and applications, so that they can give clarity on concepts that are constantly used in different contexts
