# SUMAPAZ PEAMA: DEVELOPMENT OF ACADEMIC HANDLES IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF AUTENTIC LEARNING PROJECTS IN THE SWAPPAME

The Special Academic Admission and Mobility Program (PEAMA) Sumapaz of the National University of Colombia uses an active pedagogical variety of project-based learning (ABP). One of the pedagogical and theoretical foundations of the ABP that guide us is the description that constructivism of human learning makes, besides the idea of situated cognition (Brown, Collins and Duguid, 1989). The constructivist conception of learning includes that of learning as a process (Ausupuel, Novak and Hanesian, 1983; Piaget, 1999), learning in action (Brown, Collins and Duguid, 1989) and authentic performance in education (Ordognez, 2010). In our particular case, with this active pedagogical form we seek the learning of students who come from rural schools in Bogotá, starting from their participation in authentic collaborative projects in the capital district 20, Sumapaz. Specifically, we want students to consolidate academic skills poor developed in basic and middle education, as well as advancing in their careers, fulfilling the objectives of some of their basic subjects and their general project
