# FINAL DEBUS AND GRAPHIC --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

One of the biggest difficulties around the topic of representation in architecture lies in the fact of understanding it as an instrument that transcends the simple act of showing the idea, towards the conclusion of many studies on the drawing of architecture that indicate that the representation forms a complex part of the process of generation of the same; it will not be equally adequate any way of representing or any graphic style, to any architecture, since, considering the nature of the architectural representation, it is necessary to be aware that it is there where the important part of the capacity of renewal of this one lies. The drawing is assigned in a general way the role of communicating in the architecture; however, besides the communicative character, it is necessary to establish the moment in which this drawing has a reflexive character; that it contributes to the conception and that identifies what in the first instance would be the process of creation. This problem appears in the first phases of the project and is useful to generate and verify hypotheses, containing the formal intentions of the designer. It is from there that it takes control of the idea that will allow to carry out until the end; and it is through this reflective drawing that it thinks the problem of the architecture, differentiated from the act of representing, understanding of the representation and the fundamental think as an act of constant reflection on the present and past experience, individual and collective. Thinking through the graph requires the configuration of a way of operating through the manipulation of the conceptual elements of the form to achieve an understanding and interpretation of the world, through constructed and constructible facts; mental processes that should not be confused with the acquisition of a skill, or the development of a technical skill, in the sense that graphic thinking is not the way that is shown through a drawing, but how it is thought through the drawing itself, either using manual-instrumental means, or what constitutes an important search at the moment, through digital means, without these interfering in a pernicious way in creative work. In the present they join the world panorama of architecture, designers and offices that have transcended into the digital media for the exploration of ideas at the initial moment of the project; that which belongs to the realm of the intimate and which throws graphic expressions that usually contain the essence, not only of a particular project, but of the thinking and project strategy proper of an architect or of a collective, always legible part of the intimate and graphic that usually contains the essence of the process
