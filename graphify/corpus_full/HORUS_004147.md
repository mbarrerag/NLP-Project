# Juliet B. Wiersema. The History of a Periphery. Spanish Colonial Cartoon from Colombia's Pacific Lowlands. Austin: University of Texas Press, 2023. 168 pages.

The book by Juliet B. Wieserma, edited under the title that we could provisionally translate as History of a Periphery. Cartography of the colonial period on the Colombian Pacific, then arrives at a time when it is possible to speak of a certain maturity in the debates around cartography as a document for research in history. Within these debates, key questions have arisen, such as: what kind of political and economic projects can be read in the language of maps?, how are the social and ethnic tensions around the territory expressed through cartography?, what do the maps tell us about the construction of social hierarchies anchored in space?
