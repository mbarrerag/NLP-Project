# MANAGEMENT OF KNOWLEDGE IN THE RECONSTRUCTION PROGRAMME FOR SOCIAL TEJIDE IN POSCONFLICT AREAS IN COLOMBIA (CHOCO, SUCRE AND CALDAS). PART 2: STRATEGY AND PRACTICE COMMUNITIES

This study reveals the results and the relationship between the strategies and communities of practice that support knowledge management in the development of the research programme for the reconstruction of the social fabric in post-conflict areas in Colombia in the regions of Chocó, Sucre and Caldas. For this purpose, from a qualitative research, with a descriptive, explanatory and correlational study, a model of knowledge management evaluation is applied to 74 teachers of the program. The results show that the strategies are positively and significantly correlated with the communities of practice. It is also highlighted that important progress is being made in teamwork, consensus and participation. It is concluded that the results presented can serve as feedback for the development of the programme in other regions of Colombia
