# ARAUCA: A CUSTOMS JUSTICE SCHOOL FOR COLOMBIA.

This book gives an account of the contribution that we make from this team of the National University in the search for a kind of Hiawatha Sarareño, in a scenario of complex social conflict intensely impacted by the war and affected by the lack of power of the State to manage the disputes, to order the territory and to guarantee the offer of rights established in its laws. The work is organized in three parts. In the first, the scenario is described; in the second, the way the action and interaction of the School in the territory is realized; and in the third, the two main strategies are described
