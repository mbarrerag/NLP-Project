# Management and transformation in intermediate heritage cities. Honda case study, Tolima (1977 and 2010)

This work is focused on the impact of tourism as a tool of heritage management in the city of Honda, so the reference to the zone configured as historical heritage, is made simply to understand the dynamics with which the rest of the city has developed from the purely urbanistic point of view, after the implementation of Decree 1192 of 1977, which separates the historical center from the rest of the city, especially in the tourist and urban planning areas. This work proposes to identify the urban transformations that are presented from the incorporation of tourism as strategy of the contemporary management of the heritage in Honda, in two moments: 1977 and 2010, which correspond to the first declaration to its Historical Center and the exponential growth of the tourism in the city. To this, a cartographic analysis is carried out, which allows to analyze aspects like urban growth, uses of the soil, tourist market, among others. The analysis is complemented by the structure of property of the hotel, as well as the analysis of tourism has managed more strongly than any other form of heritage management.
