# IMPACT OF ENERGY TRANSITION IN SMALL NETHERLANDS NETWORK

The energy transition is a priority issue that concerns everyone, from large cities and their electricity-intensive sectors to small remote rural communities whose electricity supply is produced through decentralized generation systems that, in many cases, are partially or totally supplied with fossil fuels. In Colombia, more than 90% of these rural communities generate their electricity using diesel plants, which makes it relevant to the study of the energy transition in these communities. Thus, the objective of this research is to show the impacts of the energy transition in these communities and how, in the long term, the transition can guarantee access to reliable, affordable, sustainable and modern electricity for all. To achieve this objective, a mixed methodology was used that included field work and computer simulations. Through field work, multiple impacts of the transition to renewables were identified, for example, social impacts as far as better conditions for education, environmental impacts as reducing CO2 emissions and economic impacts as the possibility of developing new productive activities.
