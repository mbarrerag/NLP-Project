# PROSPECTS OF PUBLIC ACCOUNTING FRONT TO SUSTAINABILITY (CLAICON 2024)

This paper summarizes and analyzes the perspectives and challenges of public accounting in relation to the demands of information for sustainability. First, it takes a tour of international and then national trends in this field. It emphasizes the dual role of the State and the Public Sector in relation to sustainability: as a producer of public policies and as an entity that affects the environment, society and the economy in its operation. It presents the relationship between public policies in Colombia and the needs of accounting information
