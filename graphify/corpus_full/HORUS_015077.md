# Discreet event simulation and sensor emulation model to improve a rural transport route by reducing waiting times

This research is carried out with the aim of evaluating the potential of different tools provided by industry 4.0 in improving transport systems. The rural transport system was chosen between the municipality of Cajamarca and the management of Anaime (Department of Tolima, Colombia) because of its economic importance for the region. Two models are developed for analysis, the first describing the real system and the second adding sensors that generate and transmit information in real time to facilitate decision-making. This system is modeled in FlexSim software. The results show that it is possible to reduce waiting times by up to 60% when sensors are integrated into the transport routes and a logic of decision regarding the information they provide. It is concluded that including tools for the taking and transfer of information in real time together with support tools helps to reduce the waiting times of passengers
