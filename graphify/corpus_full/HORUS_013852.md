# Global solar radiation: data imputation to promote energy complementarity analysis in Colombia

The Colombian electricity sector has been transforming through the insertion of new energy sources to generate electricity, one of them is solar energy that is being promoted by companies interested in photovoltaic technology. The study in this technology is important for electricity generation in general and for the planning of the sector from the perspective of energy complementarity. Precisely in this last approach is where the project is located, we are interested to answer the concerns about the reliability of the electrical system when climatic phenomena occur like El Niño or to define whether the replacement or expansion of thermoelectric plants with renewable electrical generation systems is feasible. In this sense, some difficulties related to the basic information of renewable energy sources must first be solved from measured data, since these come from automatic weather stations administered by the Institute of Hydrology, Meteorology and Environmental Studies (IDEAM) and, in the study range (2005-2019), they have significant amounts of data lost. For this reason, the general objective of the project is to complete the sets of data of global solar irradiance to obtain time series that allow to develop analysis of energy complementarity in a next project. The filling of the technical methods will be carried out by the Spanish journal
