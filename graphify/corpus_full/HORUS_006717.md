# Formulation of a proposal for a stable base with foam asphalt on high mountain roads in Colombia, UF-5, project Cambao-Manizales

In Colombia, the high mountain areas, known as “pamos” are strictly protected by the National Government. However, they need to be communicated with the rest of the country. The construction and rehabilitation of asphalt roads requires a significant investment of economic and natural resources. In view of the above scenario, the formulation of a structural alternative for paving is proposed by using Stabilized Bases with Asfalto Espumado (BEAE), considering the case of study of the functional unit UF-5 of the Cambao-Manizales project in the Alto de Ventanas - La Esperanza area. This road connects the Colombian departments of Tolima and Caldas and crosses the paramo in the Nevado del Ruiz (area protected by the National Natural Parks Authority of Colombia). To this end, the author reviewed and analyzed previous studies on the design of pavements, including traffic and geotechnical data obtained from the Concessionaria and the construction company. The author then carried out a laboratory test programme on the untreated aggregates to propose a stabilisation with foam asphalt, looking for a less invasive structural paving alternative alternative for protected areas. de Ventanas - La Esperanza zone.
