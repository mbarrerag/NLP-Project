# NUMERICAL SOLUTION OF MAXWELL'S EQUATIONS APPLIED TO A TOROIDAL WAVEGUIDE

In this work, the synchrotron radiation in a toroidal vacuum chamber is calculated by varying the size of its cross-section a and the bending radius R, by including the parameter \epsilon=\sqrt{a/R}. Maxwell's equations are simplified by assuming that the characteristic frequency of the modes \omega \approx c/a\epsilon thus neglecting the higher-order terms in \epsilon. Frequencies and loss factors for the lowest modes in square and circular cross-section waveguides are calculated. Finally, based on our results, we define our coordinate system and solve Maxwell's equations by comparing the approximate results obtained with the precise onesobtained computationally
