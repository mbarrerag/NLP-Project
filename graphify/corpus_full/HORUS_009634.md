# Using a left ventricular assist device (Heart Mate III) as a target therapy in a patient with chronic heart failure due to Chagas disease Could it be avoidable?

Introduction Chagas disease is an infectious disease that can have chronic consequences such as heart failure (CI) and enlargement of the esophagus or colon. The HF can shorten people's lives and severely affect their quality of life. There are not many alternatives to treat patients with advanced heart damage. Recently, it has been proposed that the implantation of devices that provide mechanical circulatory support can help patients with advanced HF to improve their quality of life. Objective To analyze the case of a patient with untreated Chagas disease in a timely manner due to failures in primary care, the consequences on their health and the high cost of late treatment that could be avoidable. Materials and Methods Diagnostic methods demonstrated the damage to the functional and structural level of the heart, classified its affectation as an ICC, which led to the implantation of the technological medical device (MT). Results We present the results of the implementation of such a device (Heart Mate 3) in a 54 year old Colombian patient with advanced HF diagnosis (2 years earlier) as a consequence of chronic chagetic infection.
