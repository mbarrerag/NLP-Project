# DEVELOPMENT OF ANTI-HELMSICAL FARMATICS: UPDATE OF THERAPETIC CHARACTERISTICS AND DIANATES IN GEOHELMINTIASIS HANDLING*

An estimated 1500 million people worldwide are infected by some geohelminth. The decrease in the efficacy of antihelmintics used in the preventive chemotherapy strategy, reported particularly for the species Trichuris trichiura, motivates the search for other antihelmintics to confront this situation and avoid possible resistance to this group of drugs. This review focuses on knowing the current state of research in therapeutic targets and candidates for antihelmintics drugs reported in the literature from 2013 to 2021. 28 research studies were compiled that include developments from the in-silico phase to the clinical research phase. Most of the work is related to developments in the omic sciences; from correction in genome annotations to the identification of protein families fundamental to pathogenicity mechanisms. information concerning dianas and drug candidates, available in this review, is an approximation to further explore the possible applications and experimental development of these drugs for the control of geohelminthiasis
