# Laboratory design for teaching of Industry 4.0 technologies applicable to the Colombian energy sector.

Industry 4.0 has allowed a global transformation of the various production chains and impacted various social sectors. However, one of the great challenges generated for their adoption is the lack of trained personnel who have sufficient skills to manage technologies, thus generating a need that can be addressed through teaching strategies in higher education centers. Therefore, this article shows how from the Electrical Demand Management laboratory of the National University of Colombia – Manizales Headquarters, two boards were constructed with technologies of the 4.0 industry and laboratory practices were developed allowing students of the electric and electronic engineering careers, to gain knowledge in technologies such as the Internet of Things, cloud computing and data analysis, with a focus on its application in the Colombian energy sector
