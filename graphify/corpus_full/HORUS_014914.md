# Data-driven control of interconnected energy systems.

This research proposes several algorithms for the identification and control of electric microgrids based on the Koopman operator. The contributions presented in this manuscript focus on voltage control and reactive power. We have considered five control scenarios based on the Koopman operator: (i) A centralized algorithm that regulates the voltage of the micrornet without the need for information sharing and that uses MPC. (ii) a non-cooperative distributed control, with a consensus term on the constraints of the optimization problem, which regulates the voltage and is based on the model of the investors in the Koopman space (iii) a cooperative distributed control that uses the model of the microrred in the Koopman space, where the agents use the control signals taken by other agents to generate their own signals. Here, we identify the input matrices using data. (iv) A distributed control, which uses data to identify the system error, to design an ADMM-based algorithm. (v) an online controller based on data that regulates the microrred voltage. Also, an analysis of the system's own values and the measurement effects with noise
