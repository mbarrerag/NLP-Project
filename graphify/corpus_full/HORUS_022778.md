# LYCHID RESIDUE MANAGEMENT: ANALYSIS OF AVUS GENERATION, IN RESTAURANTS AND CAFETRIES OF THE FOURTH VILLAVICENCE COMMON (META, COLOMBIA)

In this article, we analyze the generation of used vegetable oil (AVU) in restaurants and cafes in 30 neighborhoods of the municipality 4 of Villavicencio Meta, and we propose alternatives for the proper management and final disposal of the residue, through an exploratory-descriptive research, which allows identifying areas of greater generation of the residue, forms of behavior in relation to the studied problem, quantity and frequency of generation and the habits of waste management, information that was collected with the application of a semi-structured survey composed of 18 questions
