# INSURANCE LABOUR IN A UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL OF BOGOTA

Although the concern for healthy work environments is not recent in nursing, it is a phenomenon that has been visible in an important way with the emergence of the Pandemia Covid, which affected the quantity and quality of nursing staff around the world. But this concern only increased due to the pandemic; the deficit of nursing staff and the increase in the tendency to leave the profession has been considered a problem of a world order. The aim of the study was to characterize the work environment of nurses who perform care functions in a University Hospital of the city of Bogotá. An exploratory descriptive study, which incorporates a correlation component, since it is intended to describe the characteristics of the work environment of nurses, as well as the nature and type of relationships that, from their perception, have these characteristics with some results of care in the patient, in the nurse and in the institution, from the application of the instrument ENLASA-Enfermería. ENLASA-Enfermería was answered by 50% of the nurses to whom the questionnaire was sent. Among the respondents were found that 67,3% were women. The study of nursing work environments based on the particularities of the Colombian context. Limitations: The participation of 50% of nurses in the institution under study is identified as the first limitation, which prevents the recognition of the findings as representative of the work environment of the nurses of the institution. As an anonymous questionnaire in which the service variable was not included, the interpretation of the results was limited because it prevented the recognition of differences in the environments by work unit. Additionally, it was not possible to access absenteeism data or turnover of the institution's staff, because they are not part of the regular indicators of nursing management
