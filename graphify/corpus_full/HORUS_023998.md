# SIMULATION OF CHANNEL MODEL COBERTURE IN THE FRENCH OF 26 GHZ FOR A 5G MOBILE NETWORK

The purpose of this paper is to present the research results in the comparison of channel models for 5G networks in millimetre wave frequencies, applicable in the city of Bogotá, Colombia, conducting simulations in the Xirio Online program. A detailed description of the simulation process is presented in the study scenario and the methods New York University, 3GPP TR 38,901, mmMAGIC, METIS and 5GCM are used in an urban area, obtaining results in one sector of a base station and three sectors of the station. Comparisons of methods observe identical behaviors in the results of simulations with adequate coverings to offer 5G mobile services
