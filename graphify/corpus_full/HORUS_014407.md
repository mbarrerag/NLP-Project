# Early detection of phishing using characteristics related to the affected brand.

Phishing is one of the cyber attacks suffered by users of transactional services via the Internet, although there is research focused on detecting phishing attacks and the literature shows results with high effectiveness in detection, these studies do not allow to emphasize at which stage of detection is acted. Taking into account the systematic review of literature previously carried out in Barreiro2022, a general updated description of phishing detection is presented, in this study it was identified that 83% of consulted literature focused on the mitigation phase, where the methodology works reactivated using static features that provide high precision but fail in the model over time. Thus, this document will detail the implementation of a computational model of phishing detection based on the extraction of characteristics of the affected brand, which allows to act in the phase of prevention of the attack.
