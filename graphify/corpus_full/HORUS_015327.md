# MARKS FOR COLLECTIVE ACTION IN FARC-EP EXGUERRILLERS: GRATA LAND AND PONDORS (2017-2019)

This work has been aimed at investigating the motivations to participate in the insurgency and in collective actions following the Peace Agreement-2016; in order to know continuitys and reconfigurations in frameworks for collective action of injustice, identity and effectiveness.
