# NUTRIENT REMOTION CAPACITY IN SYMPHOTIC WATER SYMPHOTES (CYANOBACTERIA) COLOMBIANAS

Treatments with microalgae have been proposed as promising alternatives for the treatment of waste water at low costs combined with oxygen production and reduction of carbon footprint as by-products of the process. The aim is to evaluate the removal of nitrates, phosphates and organic matter dissolved in synthetic residual water for the first time with high mountain cyanobacteria from Colombia. For this, the removal capacity of nitrates, phosphates and organic matter was determined by reactors in batch configuration with digestions over 4 days and quantifications by colorimetry for nitrate and phosphate ions and determination of DQO and DBO according to metods standard. By molecular analysis and morphological characterization it is necessary that the cyanobacteria correspond to Leptolyngbya frigida (LAUN55), Leptolyngbya borian (LAUN69) and Leptolyngbya sp (LAUN71).
