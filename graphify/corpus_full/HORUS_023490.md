# BLOOD AND MILK: DISCUSSION AND CATEGORIES ON RACIALIZATION. A RETROSPECTIVE READING

This is a research on race and milk in the context of early forms of racialization in the Iberian world during the 15th, 16th and 17th centuries. For this purpose, primary sources are discussed, a historiographic and theoretical panorama is presented. As a result, certain methodological aspects are proposed for discussion
