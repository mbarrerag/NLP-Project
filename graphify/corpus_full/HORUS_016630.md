# COMPARISON OF TWO CONTROL CHARTS FOR MONITORING INCREASES IN THE GUMBEL SCALE PARAMETER WHEN A TYPE-II CENSORING MECHANISM IS OPERATING

Two methodologies for monitoring the Gumbel scale parameter s in the presence of a Type-II censoring mechanism are addressed. The first one is the upper sided version of the control chart proposed by Guo & Wang that is proved to be effective for detecting unwanted drops in the Weibull shape parameter. The other methodology is the recently developed exponentially weighted moving averages (EWMA) control chart with reflecting barrier that is based on the chi-squared approximation of a pivotal quantity involving the maximum likelihood estimator of s. The performance of both the presented methodologies is compared via simulation for certain combinations of impacting factors
