# Macrocyclic synthesis, hydrogen bridge-assisted from benzylamines using two- and three-component reactions

The chemosensors are systems that transform a chemical phenomenon into an analytical signal measurable experimentally. This is achieved by generating a structural, electronic or photophysical change in a sensor molecule when it comes into contact with an analyte. The design and synthesis of new molecules that can be useful in the development of new chemosensors is of increasing interest due to the need to have systems that detect cations in aqueous media due to the increase of environmental problems on the presence of heavy polluting metals. On the other hand, Although in Colombia has advanced in the implementation of policies, programs and projects oriented towards an integral management of the water resource, even a large part of this resource is in state of pollution attributed to the industrial development, which, while improving the quality of life, causes a strong impact on the environment in general and on the aquatic ecosystems and human health in particular. The present proposal of research has been oriented towards obtaining new azaphanes that can be useful for the development of chemical sensors.
