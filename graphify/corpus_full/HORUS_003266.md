# Editorial 93 The importance of epistemology in research in administration and organizations

Innovating is recognized as a leading journal in the fields of social sciences, administration and accounting in Ibero-America. Unlike institutional journals or magazines, which disseminate the results of research and teaching carried out in certain faculties, universities or professional associations, this is a scientific publication and is therefore aimed at a wider disciplinary and research community. Hence, one of our main criteria for the publication of articles entering the evaluation process is the scientific validity of their results
