# DEVELOPMENT AND VALIDATION OF A ASSISTABLE CALIBRATE METHOD FOR REFERENCE SUPERFFICIES

The measurement and calibration of reference surfaces is important for precision engineering, metrology and optical systems. Therefore, many Metrology Institutes (INMs) around the world offer these calibration services. Mechanical, electromechanical and optical measurement techniques are usually applied, which reach uncertainties around micrometers. However, these techniques require equipment such as optical levels, electronic levels, interferometers, and autocoilers for measurements which is not affordable for many laboratories due to their high costs and maintenance. This work proposes an affordable and simple method that uses standard cylinders for measuring flatness in reference surfaces. The method was validated through comparison with the method "Moody" which uses electronic levels. The uncertainty of 3.3 μm obtained from the new method tests reliability and utility for laboratories with reference surfaces with lengths above 300 mm and precisions of planitude from grade 00 to grade 3
