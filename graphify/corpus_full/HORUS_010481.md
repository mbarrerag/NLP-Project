# SOCIAL DETERMINANTS ASSOCIATED TO FOOD AND NUTRITIONAL SECURITY IN HOUSES OF WOMEN EXCESSING ThOUGHTING GIRARDOT, CUNDENMARK (COLOMBIA)

The results of the National Nutritional Situation Survey (ENSIN) of the last 15 years show a marked increase in excess weight in adults. In 2015, 59.6% of women between 18 and 64 years old presented excess weight. The objective was to analyze the situation of food and nutritional security (SAN) in a group of households in the municipality of Girardot - Cundinamarca, based on the reference of the social determinants of the SAN and social equity. quantitative cross-analytical study carried out between 2019 and 2020.
