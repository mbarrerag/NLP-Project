# IMPACT OF URBANIZATION ON DIVERSITY AND FUNCTIONAL RAGES OF COLLETITED BISHES ON NATURAL PARKS OF THE VALLE DE ABURRA

Cities are complex ecosystems marked by fragmentation, heterogeneity and waterproof surfaces, which limits biodiversity. However, they can house a considerable diversity of organisms, including bees, crucial for pollination of ornamental and food plants. In addition, bees contribute to the diversity and resilience of urban ecosystems to human interventions. Cities favor the establishment of certain groups of bees, according to characteristics such as body size, nesting type, food preferences and sociability. The bees of the family Colletidae are solitary, small to medium size, and nest in the soil or in cavities in wood. Therefore, it is essential to analyze the biological traits and functional diversity of these bees, to design plans for the conservation of their diversity. In each of the six natural parks of the Metropolitan Area of the Valley of Aburrá, they were drawn transects for collecting bees using nets (Jamas) and took botanic samples and floral buttons of the species in which they were observed forage. The collected bees were washed with alcohol to separate the pollen, which were then processed with potassium hydroxide (Omas)
