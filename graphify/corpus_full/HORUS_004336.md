# MACHINE-LEARNING FOR DATA ANALYSIS OF AN ELECTROCOAGULATOR USED IN THE TREATMENT OF TEXTILE WASTE WATERS

In this work Machine Learning (ML) techniques were evaluated for the analysis of the performance of an electrocoagulator in the treatment of industrial textile wastewater. The importance of electro-coagulation (EC) as an efficient and less polluting alternative, compared to conventional methods, was emphasized in order to meet Goal 6 of Sustainable Development: Clean Water and Sanitation. In addition, the complexity of electrochemical processes and the need to optimize the design and operation of these systems using data-based models were emphasized. Concentration data of DQO, COT and colouring and operating time were used to train a classification algorithm that allowed to distinguish between optimal operation and inefficient operation. By including more than three operating variables, a dimension reduction algorithm was implemented that allowed to observe the relationships presented between the system variables. The study showed that the need for data standardization before applying the dimension reduction significantly improved the classification of the operation, reaching a 94% accuracy. This underscores the effectiveness of the ML tools in identifying inefficient operations and the decision making based on data to optimize the use of wastewater management
