# ECOLOGICAL URBAN TESSETURES

The article - the result of the research "Nuevos Enfoques" developed at the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia - describes the recent urban transformations of Medellin, the second Colombian city, which introduced a system of green corridors to foment active transport - on foot or by bicycle - the use of low-emission public transport, and a careful and respectful concatenation between living elements and public space, and hopes for its urgent and intelligent developments
