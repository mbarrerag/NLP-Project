# MITIGATION OF LABORAL RISK BY A SERIES-ASURATE GAME

Work risks are present in any working environment regardless of the economic activity that develops, all sectors of the economy and jobs present certain situations of danger to workers, equipment, facilities or the environment, although the potential for damage and the likelihood of materialization varies according to the particular conditions of each organization.A serious game is designed and developed with the aim of strengthening information about the risks present in daily work activities which seeks to make participants aware, strengthen and internalize the occupational risks to which they are exposed when developing the different work activities with the importance of mitigating and controlling these risks through the successful implementation of prevention measures. Finally, results and conclusions are shown on the knowledge acquired by the participants of the game in occupational risks and prevention measures
