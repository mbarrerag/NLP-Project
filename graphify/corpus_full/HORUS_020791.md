# The impact of relations with Venezuela on the construction of Colombia's national security strategy between 1999-2022

Colombia and Venezuela share unique historical links in the region; however, they have differences that have deepened in recent decades. One of them is presented in the field of security, which led in 2019 to Caracas being identified as a threat in the Colombian Defense and Security Policy. This is an unprecedented historical milestone, which represents an object of study unique to the characteristics in which the phenomenon develops. Despite its relevance, this has not been sufficiently explored in literature; the the thesis is intended to be a contribution to this problem. Therefore, the present work analyzes the impact of relations with Venezuela on the construction of the Colombian national security strategy. For this, the research makes a documentary exploration, a press review and applies interviews to experts. In the the thesis there is a process in which ideological differences are going to lead to mutual interferences, which have as a central point the internal armed conflict. This is the main cause of the mutation in the Colombian strategy so that the Bolivarian Republic is considered a threat.
