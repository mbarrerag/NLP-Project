# CASE REPORT: INFESTATION BY CLINOSTOMUM SP. IN PTEROPHYLLUM SCALARE AND USE OF PRATIQUANTEL AND PAMOATE OF PIRANTEL FOR CONTROL

ABSTRACT An unidentified parasitic agent was analyzed in a batch of Pterophyllum scalare obtained by the Laboratory of Ictiology and Ornamental Fish of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters. The batch of animals had as background had a gradual discharge in ponds of earth. With the passing of days, during the quarantine period in aquarium, a fraction of the lot, corresponding to the Koi variety, began to present abnormal structures white color in the fins; structures that had a slow spread towards the area of the crown. To determine the causal agent of this phenomenon, a fin sample was analyzed, identifying the presence of metacerarias of the parasite Clinostomum sp. With this information, an antiparasitic treatment was carried out using a drug intended for small species based on Praziquantel and Pamoate of Pirantel (Vermiplex of Laboratorios ZO). The drug was administered orally with a total dose of Praziquantel and 75 mg total in the water; achieving a total recovery of the affected animals by completely disappear in the zed, identifying the presence of metacercerariae of the parasite Clinostomum sp. With this information, an antiparasitic treatment was carried out using a drug for small species based on Praziquantel and Pyrantel Pamoate (Vermiplex® from ZOO Laboratories).
