# Obtaining graphene-CaO-P2O5-SiO2-TiO2 coatings, over TI6AL4V by electrolytic plasma for bone repair and regeneration.

In this project, an electrolytic plasma oxidation (EPO) was synthesized by means of an electrolytic plasma (EPO) acidification method, a bioceramic coating, composed of graphene-CaO-P2O5-SiO2-TiO2. This technique allowed the migration, diffusion and deposition of the constituent ions of the electrolyte elaborated, on the surface of Ti6Al4V, previously treated with anodized process. After this, thermal treatments were performed, at 400°C and 600°C, which, in conjunction with the different percentages of graphene used, promoted effects at the structural, morphological and chemical level in the coating. For the analysis and characterization of the synthetic coatings, techniques of electronic microscopy of Barryde (SEM), spectroscopate of energy dispersion of X-rays (EDS), Elemental Mapping, Diffraction of X-ray (DRX), Espectroscopia Confomic Raman, Mapeo Raman and measurement of the sesil got contact angle were used.
