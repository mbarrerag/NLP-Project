# THE ETHICAL CONCEPTION IN MARX

With the presentation I intend to give an account of the arguments I take up enough to show that Marx developed an ethical conception with which to understand and evaluate the political, social and economic dynamics of reality. This provides a philosophical and sociological foundation with which the debate with those who assume an immoral position in Marx is inclined with good reasons on the side that warns an ethical conception
