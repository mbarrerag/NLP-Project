# Sensor Based on U-Bent MMM Optical Fiber Structure for Detection of Mercury in Water

In this work, U-Bent multimode-multimode-multimode (U-MMM) optical fiber structures for the detection of Hg2+ ions in water are evaluated. The U-MMM, fabricated via the immobilization of a chitosan/Fe2O3 layer in a coreless fiber section, can detect the presence of mercury ions in drinking water at low concentrations, by tracking the wavelength response of the structure
