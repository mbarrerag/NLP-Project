# DISCUSSION: ENURERY AND ENCOPRESIS

Disorders of elimination are common in school age and the first medical contact related to sphincter control occurs with primary care professionals. Psychological reactions of shame, sadness and rabies are common in children with these disorders and families, which lead to problems in parenting. Comorbidity with psychiatric disorders, especially deficit of care and behavioural disorders, is also common. Pediatricians, general doctors and other primary care professionals should have the tools to carry out the initial assessment and approach presented in the chapter
