# Analysis of the drought phenomenon in Colombia

The results of the study of the drought phenomenon in Colombia are presented: the impact and impacts reported in historical documents of events that occurred in the past, mainly in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, and the description of the characteristics of this phenomenon described based on spatial distribution, intensity and frequency. For this characterization, the analysis of the Standardized Precipitation Index was carried out, which quantified the various intensities of the phenomenon and, based on the spectral analysis, explained its frequency. Also, based on the historical review of events that occurred during the last four centuries in what is currently the Colombian territory, the various forms of impact and the most affected economic sectors were identified. Although the events that were identified are few, there were cases of severe droughts with marked economic, social and political impacts such as the one of 1877-1878, the droughts of 1925-1930, and the one of the 40s of the 20th century.
