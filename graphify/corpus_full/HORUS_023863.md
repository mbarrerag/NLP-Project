# HIGH CUTS AND SOCIAL TRANSFORMATION. (DES) OBEDIENCE TO LEGAL ORDERS. WHAT HAPPENED AFTER THE JUDICIAL DECISION?

What happens after winning a dispute against some form of discrimination? What are the incentives or costs that induce the convicted to disobey a sentence? This innovative book analyses the experience of victims after obtaining a judicial triumph, received with high expectations and, in the hope that the orders issued will be effectively implemented. What implies, in most cases, the beginning of a long and painful renegade. To this end, different points of view will be proposed on how effective the right is to transform reality and the advantages of cost-benefit analysis will be taken into account as a tool to understand the rational action of the actors involved, as well as their weaknesses, due to ethical and cultural considerations in the perception of justice by society. The book offers the social movements and strategic litigants of different disciplines the possibility of determining the chances of still winning failure; and the Courts, judicial operators, means of communication and administrative personnel, to determine when a decision will be implemented or simply resisted
