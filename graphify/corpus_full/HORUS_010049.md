# COMPARISON OF A BAYESIAN APPROACH AND MACHINE LEARNING FOR THE ESTIMAL OF CRANEOPHACIAL MEASURES IN CHILDREN

The ability to predict craniofacial growth in children would allow better diagnosis and treatment in orthodontics. To this end, statistical models are used that consider the heterogeneity of individuals, such as Mixed Linear Models (MLM). In this work, two approaches to obtaining MLM parameters are compared by simulation of important craniofacial measures in practice. These approaches are of Bayesian and Machine learning nature. The strengths and limitations of both approaches are discussed. This study may have a significant impact on the development of diagnostic and treatment techniques in orthodontics
