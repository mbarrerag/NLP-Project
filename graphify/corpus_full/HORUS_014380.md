# Functional dissociations in children with deambulant cerebral paralysis who have been operated with selective dorsal rhizotomy.

The Cerebral Palsy (PC) is a disabling condition, chronic, non progressive and permanent, secondary to an injury of the brain fetal immature, which conditions limitations of motor predominance (alterations in movement and posture); clinically the most frequent primary manifestation is spasticity, and is the characteristic sign of motor alteration in the population with PC. Describe the differences between the results of functional tests applied before and after neurosurgical intervention with Dorsal Selective Rizotomy (RDS), in mobile patients with spastic PC operated between 2014 and 2022 in the Roosevelt Institute (IR), in addition describe the differences in the group of children with similar characteristics not operated. It is an observational, retrospective and descriptive study. It is reviewed the available IR databases for the selection of children with mobile PC operateds of RDS and the groups of children with similar characteristics. It is considered that functional tests such as GMFM-66, 6MWT, PEDI, the scale of the Ashworth and FMS, with the classification of the functional group operated ones in the group are functional.
