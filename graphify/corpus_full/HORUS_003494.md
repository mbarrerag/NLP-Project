# COST-EFFECTIVENESS ANALYSIS OF CLINICAL ADVANCEMENT IN THE EARLY PHASE VS. PHASE ESTABLISHED IN A COORTE OF PATIENTS WITH ARTRITIS REUMATOID OF A FOURTH-LEVEL HEALTH INSTITUTION IN COLOMBIA

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a high-cost disease, which allows classifying patients in early or established phase. The purpose of this work was to do a cost-effectiveness analysis, comparing both phases with patient data to a 6-month time horizon, from the perspective of the third payer. The population was delimited. The costs and effectiveness of each phase were estimated. An economic evaluation model of decision tree type was developed and the incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (RICE) was calculated with their respective sensitivity analysis, both deterministic and probabilistic. In terms of costs, it was found that, for an effectiveness in targets, the cost was 85% higher in the established phase than in the early phase. Likewise, for an effectiveness in non-target, the cost was 77% higher in the established phase than in the early phase. On the other hand, the effectiveness results were in favor of the latter compared to the established phase. With respect to the RICE, it was determined that the approach in early phase saves $2,326,389 Colombian pesos in the current phase
