# Functional and genetic characterization of a population of patients diagnosed with cerebral palsy and normal cerebral magnetic resonance imaging in an institution in Bogotá.

Functional and genetic characteristics of a population of patients with diagnosis of cerebral palsy and normal cerebral magnetic resonance in an institution in Bogotá In cerebral palsy (PC) there are usually structural cerebral abnormalities, however, up to 10% can be found normal cerebral resonance. This group of patients is poorly characterized. In PC, “red flags” have been described that indicate the need for a search for an underlying genetic etiology. Characterize a population of Bogotá with diagnosis of PC of dyskinetic, ataxic and hypotonic type and normal resonance. Perform a genetic panel for neurotransmitter disorders in this group of patients. Describe the brain resonance findings using the MRICS system. 195 patients with PC were included, 33 with normal cerebral resonance. Patients with normal resonance presented a better motor function than the group with abnormal GMFCS level IV and V resonance (70.59%, vs. 85.09, p =.04). No statistically significant differences were found in other variables. In 21 patients with normal brain resonance patients, patients with no pathogenic or probably pathogenic lesions were found
