# Luca Bullaro.The Urban Regeneration of Medellín.

In recent years Medellín has undergone an intense transformation of ur-49 bana and social that has had, outside and within Colombia, a media impact of great proportions, and that attracts an increasing number of visitors, curious and scholars from various parts of the world. This radical change can be compared to what it experienced Barcelona in the eighties, when Oriol Bohigas, responsible for urban planning of the Catalan capital, has conceived the slogan "Monumentalize the periphery": a motto that well matches the recent principles of urban planning of Medellín. The suburbs of the city are gradually modernising through a system of contemporary architectural works that, when they manage to link the different interests of citizens and visitors, multiply the possibilities of socialization of the inhabitants and improve the safety of the neighborhoods: as former mayor Sergio Fajardo, promoter of several important projects, is a simultaneous process of change of skin, reason and heart¿
