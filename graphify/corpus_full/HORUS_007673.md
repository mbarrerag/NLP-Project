# Characterisation of mixtures obtained in the fermentation ABE and in the flash distillation, to validate the results of the project: Production of solvents by intensified processes with fermentation and coupled separation

The production of butanol by fermenting route is a process with more than 100 years of history that, for several decades, was the main route for obtaining this commodity, but this process lost relevance to the development of the petrochemical industry (Moon et al., 2016). Currently, the need to develop environmentally friendly processes, within the biorefinerías scheme and under the concept of circular economy has aroused interest in fermentation ABE taking advantage of the natural capacity of the Clostredia family and the improvements that can be implemented by genetic engineering. The present project seeks to contribute to the development of a sustainable process for the production of butanol applying the fermentation strategy integrated with recovery of products by flash distillation, which has shown to be a valid option to give competitiveness to this process (Mariano et al. 2011). The project will be executed by students of the Bioengineering Seedlero (code 2943) who have been working approximately a year in the fermentation ABE.
