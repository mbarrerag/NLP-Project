# Music therapy for empathic communication between primary caregiver and pediatric oncology patient in palliative care

This research sought to promote an Empática Communication between the dyad composed by the Paediatric Oncological Patient and its Primary Caregiver in a context of Palliative Care in favor of the improvement of the Quality of Life of the dyad, through the four methods of Musicotherapy. The qualitative research process with design of case of phenomenological type was carried out in the Foundation Hospital de la Misericordia – HOMI, in the city of Bogotá, with a total of 10 participants in a work of 5 days, each one formed by a child between the 5 and 13 years that was referred to palliative care by his treating physician, and his main caregiver. The muscular therapy intervention was carried out by diads, and the number of sessions varied between 4 and 8 depending on the case, each with an approximate duration of one hour. The process was initiated in intrahospital modality; however, the fifth day was discharged and continued its process in the home way. menological case study design was carried out out at the Foundation Hospital de la Misericordia - HOMI, in the city of Bogotá, with a total of 10 participants in a work of 5 dyads, each one formed by a child between 5 and 13 years old who was referred to to to palliative care by his treating physican, and his main care.
