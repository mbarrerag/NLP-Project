# Mixed whole programming model in planning activities and staff in a student academic chapter

Abstract Context: Currently it is possible to find in various academic programs in engineering around the world multiple student groups, as is the case of the Institute of Industrial & System Engineers (IISE), whose purpose is to promote the formation and integration of its members with the industry and the work environment, by carrying out activities of different types and nature in a self-managed but empirical way and with little use of the few references in the literature about the topic. This article presents a mixed whole programming model that identified the parameters of capacity, repetitiveness, remuneration and temporality collected from the norms of evaluation of the student chapters of the IISE, with which it was possible to formulate a mathematical model with the aim of maximizing the score by optimizing the use of the human talent of the student group, taking into account the restrictions as to quantity and repetition of each type of activity to be performed.
