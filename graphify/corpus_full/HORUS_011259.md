# THE COLOMBIA FIRE

This book offers you the opportunity to explore the Colombian sky on your own. Follow it as a guide and discover the various celestial bodies of the catalogue of the famous comet hunter, Charles Messier, while admiring the incredible astrophotographs captured by the authors in the Tatacoa desert
