# MEMORY OF ARMED CONFLICT IN THE MUNICIPIO DE PORE BETWEEN 1986-2006

From a dialogic perspective, that is, from the voices of the victims of the armed conflict in Pore-Casanare, and from the narrative, aesthetics and theoretical conceptualization that comes from the interdisciplinary field of human and artistic sciences, the text entitled Memories en piedra is presented. From the armed conflict in the municipality of Pore between 1986-2006 before the Colombian State, its justice and reparation institutions for the victims of the armed conflict, the academic community and the protagonists of the memories here embodied, with the aim not only of describing and recording the memories of the victims in Pore, but also with the idea of setting a precedent that will help to build peace and not repeat violent events in the national context, and particularly in the Colombian Orinoquia; territory that has been flogled by violence for decades
