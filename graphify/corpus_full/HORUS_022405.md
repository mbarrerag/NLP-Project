# Assessment of methods to increase the stability of pesticide reference materials.

In the present investigation, the effect of the use of different strategies on the stability of selected pesticides in candidates for reference materials (MR) was studied. In the first part, the effect of additives on pesticides in calibration solutions was evaluated and in the second part, the influence of the use of additives, encapsulating matrices and drying processes was evaluated, on the stability of pesticide reference materials in avocado hass. For studies of pesticide calibration solutions, different methods were developed and validated through gas chromatography; subsequently, stability studies were carried out, which showed that the proposed stabilization strategies were effective, achieving stabilization of all pesticides that were unstable, which resulted in an improvement of uncertainty due to stability of up to 286 times. On the other hand, four different stabilization strategies on MR of pesticides in inclusive water catholophilised were used. The results showed that the use of antioxidants and preservatives, allowed to increase the useful life of MRs.
