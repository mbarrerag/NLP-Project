# Characterization of petrophysical properties through seismic geostatistical investment in coal formation, Llanos Basin, Colombia.

The predictive power of the petrophysical properties is systematically evaluated by means of elastic geostatistical investment to know the spatial disposition of the quality of reservoir in the subsoil, this technique has been implemented in an area of the Llanos Basin, Colombia, where there is a need to achieve a lesser uncertainty in the characterization of the Carbon Formation. For this purpose, acceptable quality data were used and among those that have 250 km2 of 3D seismic data and 117 vertical wells with effective porosity, gamma ray and face records, of which 17 have registers of density and speed of both P wave and S wave.
