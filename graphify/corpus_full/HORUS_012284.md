# Need for therapeutic endoscopy in intensive care patients in a Colombian institution.

Acute gastrointestinal haemorrhage is frequent in critically ill patients hospitalized in ICU, with an incidence of 5 to 25%, associated with an increase in morbidity and mortality. Endoscopic studies help diagnose, stratification and management of these patients, however, despite their frequent realization, they may be normal in up to one third of patients with suspected digestive bleeding. To determine the need for endoscopic therapeutic intervention in critically ill patients hospitalized in ICU with manifest gastrointestinal haemorrhage, or suspicion of gastrointestinal bleeding Materials and methods: It is an observational analytical study based on the results of gastrointestinal endoscopic procedures and medical records of patients over 18 years of age hospitalized in the intensive care unit of the National University Hospital of Colombia, who were performed gastrointestinal endoscopic studies, Results: 210 EVA were included 3,9%. The findings were the following: eroscopic esophagitis 20,5%, chronic gastritis 83,3%, erotic gastritis 36.2%, eroscopic duodenitis 12,8, and gastric ulcers Fortresses III
