# Mechanical characterization of adobe dies reinforced with guadua mat.

The guadua mat is a natural material, easy to obtain and low cost, which can be used for the reinforcement of adobe walls. This proposal would allow to preserve existing structures and thus avoid a greater environmental impact, product of reinforcement with synthetic or steel elements, which are not compatible with the soil, or even demolition as a result of post-sism structural failure. Thus, the aim of this work is to determine the viability of the use of guadua mat as reinforcement of Murets constructed in adobe for the improvement of its structural capacity.
