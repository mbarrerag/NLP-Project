# ANALYSIS OF ARTICLE 65 OF THE AMERICAN CONVENTION ON THE INVOLVEMENT OF THE JUDGMENTS OF THE IHRQ COURT BY STATES PARTIES

The guarantee of the democratic State of rights has as its essential function, among others, the protection of fundamental rights, which are continuously violated when the States parties to the American Convention on Human Rights, fail to comply with their international obligations, hence the failure to execute the sentences issued by the Inter-American Court of Human Rights (IACHR) against the States parties, has become the Achilles heel of the American Convention on Human Rights, because democracy is affected and a lack of justice for the victims is not being applied.From this point of view, it is intended to analyze how the presentation of the annual report of the Inter-American Court to the OAS General Assembly can be a mechanism for strengthening deliberative democracy and for the violation of the rights of the victims
