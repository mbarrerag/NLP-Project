# DECOLONIZING THE VEGETAL WORLD TO PARTICIPATE ARTISTICAL PRACTICES

Decolonizing the Plant World From artistic practices Conversation with Eulalia De Valdenebro and Roberto Barbanti Eulalia de Valdenebro, Artist-botanist, explores in his work a decolonial perspective on the relationship between human beings and the plant world through their bodies. In this conversation three of her artistic practices will be addressed: a critical appropriation of scientific botanical illustration and of herbarium specimens; the elaboration of a living sculpture in the city of Bogotá with species of vines native to the Andean forest; and a video performance made in the Colombian pamos where the artist turns the extractivist processes into an ecosophistical perspective.The colonial perspective in her works has two approaches, one refers to the relationship that we humans establish with the plant world, and the other refers to the place of artefacturing of the artist: woman, racialized insofar as "white" and heiress of an elite that has forged a deeply unequal and violent society in contemporary Colombia.Eulalia De Valdenebro University, Colombia
