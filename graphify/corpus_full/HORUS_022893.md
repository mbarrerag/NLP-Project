# A 1:1 ESC RATIO WITH THE PARAMO.

From an ecosophical and decolonial perspective, I criticize modern botanical episteme using as a method of research and expression, the artistic practice by which I make a map of tactile relationships at scale 1:1. In the work I present, I compare the dimensions of my own body with those of a Espeletia grandiflora (frailejon, emblematic plant of the high tropical mountain ecosystems or moors) by a geometric method developed from imprints of our bodies
