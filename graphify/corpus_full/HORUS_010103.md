# HYMENOPTERS AS BIOINDICATORS OF THE QUALITY OF THE ECOSYSTEM OF FOREST IN A STORAGE AREA (NEIVA, COLOMBIA)

The ecosystem of Tropical Dry Forest (BST) presents diversity of flora and fauna highly endemic, very affected by human activity. Colombia has presented a reduction of about 94% of its original surface. To observe the changes in the diversity of hymenopteras index along a conservation gradient, the Program Fibras, led by Research Institute of Biological Resources Alexander von Humboldt in agreement with Ecopetrol, established the biomonitoring of insects under different thematic lines, mainly with Malaise traps, carrying out sampling between October 2021 and March 2022, in three covers with different degree of disturbance in a fragment of BST located in Neiva (Huila), classified as: Secondary Forest (BS), Forest in Regeneration Process (BPR) and Perturbated Forest (BP), achieving a total of 6298 individuals, with a total of BIT. The entomological material was identified following taxonomic keys, additionally it has been complemented with molecular information according to the obtaining of DNA bar codes.
