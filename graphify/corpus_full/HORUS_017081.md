# Audiovisual autobiographies and life paths of adolescents and young people deprived of their liberty: from the knowledge of others to the learning of themselves

This research comprises and interprets the processes and life paths of four people – two teenagers and two young people – deprived of liberty, in the System of Criminal Responsibility for Adolescents (SRPA) of Colombia, from Audiovisual Autobiographies (hereinafter AA) produced by themselves. It is carried out on the basis of a qualitative methodology developed in two ways. On the one hand, from the Research Action (IA), for the production of the AA through transmedia resources adapted for this purpose; on the other, from the interpretive framework of Analogue Hermeneutic Cultural Analysis, which allows understanding and explaining the life paths manifested in moving images, music, recording styles and narrative. The findings are organized through the life paths studied, in the educational, social and cultural fields; operated in the dimensions of the concept of life process (a.g. from the system and from the perspective of conscience, in 1999 and in the concepts of life project, usually used in socio-educational intervention. s to understand and explain the costumes of life manifested in moving images, music, recording and narrative styles.
