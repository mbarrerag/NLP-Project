# DEVELOPMENT OF A TOOL CARD FOR THE FORMULATION OF A POMCA HYDROLOGIC COMPONENT

the ministry of environment, housing and territorial development published in 2010 the national policy for the integrated management of water resources (pngirh). It includes the formulation of the Plans for the Management and Management of Basins (POMCA) as a key element for the sustainable management of water and basic sanitation in the country. The main purpose of these plans is to support the planning of land use and the sustainable management of renewable natural resources. However, since the issuance of the PNGIRH, progress in the formulation of the POMCAS has been limited, due to the need to generate knowledge and the limitations in the information necessary to formulate such plans. To contribute to the generation of such knowledge, a box of hydrological tools was developed to support the formulation of POMCAS in its hydrological component and for the hydrological characterization of Colombian basins. In this work, the design, architecture, implementation and application of the toolbox in three basins (Rivers Humea, Pamplonita and Chinchiná), each one with different levels of availability of hydroclimatological data and physical characteristics is presented
