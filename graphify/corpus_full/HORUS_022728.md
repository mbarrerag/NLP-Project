# Study of the growth and development of the fruit of agraz (Vaccinium sure Swartz) under cold weather conditions

ABSTRACT Agraz (Vaccinium surare Swartz) is a wild species that grows spontaneously in the high Andean zone of countries such as Colombia and considered a fruit with growing demand. The objective was to carry out a study of the growth and development of the fruit of agraz under cold climate conditions. Fruits of adult agraz shrubs were selected in Chiquinquirá (Colombia); from day 30 after ante-harvest, 7 periodic samplings were carried out, physicochemical parameters were measured. It was determined that from flowering to harvest the fruit took 124 days and 817.5 degrees heat day. Agraz fruits in mature state presented an equatorial and polar diameter of 9.8 ± 0.11 mm and 10.3 ± 0.14 mm respectively; fresh weight and dry weight of the fruit, presented a sigmoid simple behavior, adjusting to a logistic growth model. Total soluble solids, total acidity and color index increased as a function of development. Breath decreased, although it had a peak in the middle of development.
