# History and Resistance: Pedro Luis Valencia Giraldo in the Context of Political Violence and Human Rights in Colombia, 1985-1990

This thesis addresses the private, public, political and academic life of Pedro Luis Valencia Giraldo, a purpose that allows exploring the tension between the different actors of the war, the dynamics of the armed conflict and political violence during the period 1985-1990. These years were characterized by the strong criminalization against leftist leaders and human rights defenders in Colombia, by agents belonging to State institutions, who, in the company of criminal organizations, persecuted people, social movements and political parties representing a political alternative in the country. .
