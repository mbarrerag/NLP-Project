# Simulation of the enzyme clarification process of glucose syrups produced by cellulase from agro-industrial waste.

In general, this research seeks to model and simulate the process of enzymatic clarification with pectinases and improve the traditional clarification processes used in glucose syrups produced from agroindustrial residues, allowing to improve the syrups produced by the group of Bioprocesses Research and Reactive Flows of the National University of Colombia (BIOFRUN). Glucose syrups are solutions rich mainly in glucose, produced by the acidic or enzymatic hydrolysis of starch, cellulose of agro-industrial residues and/or other materials. Through the pretreatments and hydrolysis, not only the glucose is released, they also remain in the solution residues of the plant cells, proteins, fats, oils, pectins and other dissolved and suspended compounds that give a cloudy appearance and opaque to the syrups produced. Depending on the uses of these syrups, these need to be treated with methods of clarification that remove or degrade these contaminants of the syrup. These methods present some drawback and the centrifugal, despite being simple, they present losses of syrup (40 50%) This research aimed to evaluate pectid enzymes applied at the time of making the glucose acid degraded and obtain an increase in their clarity. This research aimed at assessing pectidic enzymes applied at the time of making the clarification of glucose syrups produced from lignocellulose residues, finding the best conditions to perform this process and modeling it using research data. The first part of this work begins with a bibliographical compilation of the subjects related to this process, beginning with the history of glucose syrups, the raw materials from which they are obtained, their production and clarification methods, which is the pectin and the pectidic enzymes, previous studies for the clarification of juices and syrups, and the modelling. The second part of this research corresponds to the process of enzymatic clarification, which begins with the characterization of the raw material used for the production of syrup and the enzymes used for the clarification, finding properties such as the chemical composition of the raw material, kinetic parameters and the activity of the enzyme.
