# Characterization of a 3.5 kw generator operated with biogas and AGM battery energy storage.

The purpose of this experimental study is to characterize a 3.5 [kW] motor generator set that operates with biogas and two AGM batteries of 12 [V] and 300 [Ah] each. The maximum efficiency of the motor generator was 15,329 [%]. The batteries loads during the first 10 minutes showed a considerable increase in voltage. In turn, during the same time period, the load percentage increased dramatically. It is shown that the information of the discharge time provided by the manufacturer does not resemble the time measured in the study. The study concludes that the most efficient way to use a motor generator is to connect high loads without exceeding the maximum amperage indicated. It can also be concluded that, the consumption of natural gas was practically constant connecting high or low loads. It should be noted that the motor generator operated, without problems, with natural gas, even though it is designed to operate with biogas
