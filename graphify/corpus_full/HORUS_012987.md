# Validation of the scale of parenting, nutrition and mental health in a Latin American context

Objectives To present the process of creating and validating an instrument to detect problems in mental health, including parental experience, excessive use of screens and low physical activity, in children with excess weight. Methods A cross-sectional study was carried out and validation was carried out by judges. Subsequently, the instrument was applied, a factorial analysis was performed and the concurrent validity was explored. The sample was made up of 131 families of children and adolescents with an average age of 11.2 years. The population was selected from urban, semi-urban and rural areas of Colombia. Results The test t indicates that the items discriminate correctly and have an adequate level of difficulty. The scale showed a high-mediate reliability (Alfa=0.703), with an important correlation between scales such as CBCL in all its domains, which determines affective and behavioral pediatric symptoms, and scales of Zarit, PHQ-9 and PAFAS, related to caregiver overload, affective symptoms in caregivers and parental adjustment. Discussion Excess weight and alterations in mental health are problems of public health, so that the children require instruments of detection in mental health settings
