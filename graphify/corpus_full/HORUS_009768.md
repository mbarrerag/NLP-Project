# CUMARE (ASTROCARYUM ACULEATUM) AND NUKAK MATERIAL CULTURE: A VISUAL TEST ON USE AND ECOLOGY OF A SPECIES BETWEEN AN INDIGENOUS PEOPLE OF Amazonia

The extraction of fibers and their use in the Amazon is extensive. Far from being simple materiality, the material culture among indigenous peoples has symbolic associations and under situations of contact is one of the elements that is quickly affected or modified. Cumare is traditionally used by the Nukak to make objects or as a component of them. The extraction of fiber and the process to make cordería is a female labor. The exploitation reveals that the extraction respected their ecological conditions by not destroying plants and that now under the situation of forced displacement, this is an alternative of economic income that already compromises the source of the resource by overexploitation
