# Standardisation of an in vitro model with dentin barrier for the evaluation of the diffusion of substances in a culture of cells similar to human odontoblasts

In order to simulate the in vivo conditions in in vitro models, various methods of indirect exposure have been developed to assess the dentin-pulpary resistance to the diffusion of substances. However, it is not a technique that is commercialised, but it should be standardized in each laboratory for use in research and thus be able to evaluate how the presence of dentin affects the diffusion of molecules towards the pulp tissue. Therefore, the aim of this work is to standardize an in vitro model of indirect exposure with dentin barrier to evaluate the diffusion of different substances in a culture of cells similar to odontoblasts. It will be performed by positioning dentin discs attached to transwell inerts that will divide a superior compartment of one lower in the wells of a plate of 24 wells, where the active presence of cells similar to odontoblasts in monolayer is allowed. It will apply the monomer triethylglycol-dimethacrylate-like (TEGMA) and the flavonoids miricetin (MYR) and proantocyanidine (PAC) as the presence of the materials used during the process-adolblast and/adolin-adolentulence
