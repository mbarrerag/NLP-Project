# A NEW CONTRACT FOR SOCIAL AND LIFE REPRODUCTION

The pandemic of covid-19 transitioned from the upper classes to the lower classes, exacerbated inequalities between centers and peripheries, showed the inconvenience of the privatization of health as a commercial good, led to questioning central aspects of the development model and of economic and social policy. In the end, the pandemic uncovered a crisis of civilization. The health and political crisis makes us more sincere as it exposes the structural failures of our social construction and thus opens a debate on the necessary reforms to be undertaken and on the desirable transition towards a more inclusive and supportive society. Contributing to this debate is a challenge of this editorial project of the Network of Research Centers in Programmatic Alternatives of the National University of Colombia. The 23 works produced within 24 research groups and submitted to a collective debate make up this book, which is at the same time a call for a change of Rumbo: towards an inclusive, equitable and sustainable Colombia
