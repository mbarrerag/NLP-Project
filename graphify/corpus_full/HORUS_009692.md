# Methodology for optimisation of straight gear power transmission systems for vibration reduction.

This paper presents a viable methodology for the development of an analysis of a system of cylindrical gear transmission of straight teeth for vibration reduction, based on a case of scientific study reviewed and studied multiple times by different researchers to obtain the optimal parameters. The stages of the project were supported by tools of MATLAB (solution of differential equations and optimization libraries). . Initially a study was carried out on the dynamics of power transmission systems, reviewing type of models, mathematical approach, factors that generate vibrations and geometric parameters of the system that affect their mass, stiffness and damping. On the other hand, a review of the optimization process for vibration reduction was carried out taking into account objective function and restrictions approaches. A dynamic model was proposed by means of concentrated parameters, taking into account all its geometric characteristics, initial conditions and parameters in order to validate the rigidity and displacements with respect to a case of study.
