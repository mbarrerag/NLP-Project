# Mapping coverages in the Ciénaga Grande wetland of Santa Marta using polarimetric synthetic aperture radar data.

Wetlands are some of the most important ecosystems on the land and have been identified as natural solutions to the global water crisis. For this reason their monitoring is necessary, and for this task remote sensor data have been widely used. However, these ecosystems are difficult to map and classify due to their high degree of spatial and temporal variability, so uncertainties persist. The aim of this research was to evaluate the potential of techniques of polarimetric decomposition of synthetic aperture radar data (SAR) from band L in the extraction of thematic information in the bi-polar large bi-meta Marta wetland. To complete this first we obtained polarimetric de-scriptor de-sorts (CP), Touzi (TZ), Van Zyl (VZ) and Freeman-Durden (FD), which were used in a supervised classification scheme with the algorithm Allatorial Forests (BA). The results of the thematic accuracy assessment of classifications were then analyzed to estimate the contribution of polarimetric descriptors.
