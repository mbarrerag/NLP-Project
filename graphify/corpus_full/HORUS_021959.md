# Predict the progression of brain injury in multiple sclerosis by biomarkers in magnetic resonance imaging.

According to the WHO global health organization multiple sclerosis is the most common primary neurological disorder in young adults, sudden attacks without established temporal pattern that produce attacks on the nervous system and the formation of multiple lesions, this leads to typical disease symptoms such as loss of balance, muscle spasms, motor coordination problems among others, these symptoms generate disabilities that can cause deterioration of normal daily life. This disease presents a rather heterogeneous course that generates a challenge to exercise the medical control, personalized therapies, treatment and medication. Magnetic resonance imaging and its obtaining of quantified information with tools such as biomarkers of image in specific radiomy, open a door to examine the lesions of the nervous system almost in real time and this information is vital for the diagnosis of the disease and for its monitoring and control. This work presents the construction of a model of recovery prediction for each lesion with information obtained through the image biomarkers in specific morphology and analysis of texture
