# Transformative Reparation: on the Dilemmas of Reparation for Victims of Armed Conflict, Based on the Program of Administrative Reparation in Colombia

This paper analyses the right to reparation of victims of armed conflict, and the concept of transformative reparation within the framework of reparation programmes, with the aim of advancing a practical definition of transformative reparation. Thus, some clarifications of point of departure are made on reparation programmes as a mechanism of transitional justice. Later, the theory of social transformation and its relationship with transitional justice is elaborated. Subsequently, the definition of transformative reparation is analyzed, elements and criteria are proposed for designing a practical definition, which emphasizes the possibilities of implementation through a reparation programme. The last chapter, studies the Colombian Reparation Programme, which adopts the concept of transformative reparation in its legal framework. The programme is valued from the elements and criteria proposed in the definition of practical reparation, which also includes some reflections collected with two communities victims of armed conflict in Colombia: Reachuelo (Santander) and Liberia (Cundinamarca). the case of Colombia’s Administrative Reparation Program, which adopted the notion of transformational repair in its legal framework. The Colombian case is evaluated based on the elements and criteria proposed in the practical definition of transformational repair, and the findings of the field work’s carried out out in two communities that are victims of the armed conflict in Colombia: Riachuelo and Liberia. Some practical lessons are extracted to try to solve the dilemmas of reparation to the victims of the armed conflict
