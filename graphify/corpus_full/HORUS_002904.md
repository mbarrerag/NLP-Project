# VALUATION OF AGROINDUSTRIAL RESIDUES OF ARRoz IN THE SYNTHESIS OF SILICE MESSOPOROUS WITH POTENTIAL ENVIRONMENTAL APPLICATIONS

The production of rice (Oryza sativa) is one of the most important agricultural activities worldwide.This crop generates around 120 million tons of residues per year.In these residues the rice husk (CA), is composed of 50% cellulose, 25%-30% lignin and 15%-20% silica. [1] The presence of silicon makes it difficult to biodegradation for this reason, the AC is poured into the soil or burned in open field, process that releases pollutants such as SO2, NO2, CO and particulate matter PM10 and PM2.5, which significantly contributes to atmospheric pollution and serious health problems. [2] Therefore, it is of great importance to process this residue to transform it into materials of added value and achieve a circular economy based on the 3R (reduce, reuse and recycling). [3] The materials of mesoporous silica (MSM) during the study are highlighted by their large specific surface area, high pores volume, adjustable pores size and uniform morphology, these characteristics make them suitable for various applications in catalysis, adsorption and other fields [4]. Four signals at angles 2? corresponding to 2.4°, 4.2°, 4.9° and 22.1° characteristic of a mesoporous silica structure in the DRX. XRF revealed a silica purity of 96%, while nitrogen fissortion confirmed the presence of a porous structure, with a high surface area. The results obtained in this study demonstrate the feasibility of using agro-industrial residues such as rice husks for the synthesis of mesoporous materials
