# Identification and characterization of T-phases in Colombian territory from marine earthquakes located in the Pacific Ocean

The T phases are acoustic waves originated by the propagation of seismic energy from the seabed to the water column. These waves, when traveling through the SOFAR channel and colliding with the continental slope, have the capacity to generate phases Tp and Ts. This type of waves have been studied since the 1930s and are widely used to characterize the cortical structure, locate seismogenic sources and to improve the early warning systems of tsunamis in areas with little seismic activity. Due to all its possible applications and its limited knowledge in South America, this project focuses on identifying the T phases in the peaceful coast of Colombia, determining its physical properties, generation mechanisms and location point from earthquakes generated in the Zona de Cizalla de Panamá, registered in the National Seismo-Associational Network of Colombia (RSNC) and the TNC. A seismic catalog with marine events originated in the Panama basin, signals were processed and the theoretical travel times of the Tp and Ts phases were calculated.
