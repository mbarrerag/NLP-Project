# THE CENTRE AS A SPACE SOCIAL AND PHYSICAL SCENE. CONFLICT AND APPROVAL ON THE BOLIVAR, MEDELLÍN COLOMBIA PARK

The inquiry proposed by the Seed in Contemporary Urban Studies begins with a question: How is the daily space, in the urban central areas, defined, well-known, constructed from social practices? From this general framework is the study of the specific case of the Bolívar Park in the center of Medellín, as a possible scenario of conflicts and appropriations that stress the relations between the social space and the physical materialization of the city. With its realization, it seeks to contribute to an expanded understanding of aspects of urban relations that are found outside of the purely physical spatial logics, based on tools such as rapid ethnography and discourse analysis, which can redefine the traditional way in which the student of architecture approaches the study of urban events; as a result, it is expected to construct a double mapping by various means: descriptive, at the first, of the behaviors of actors in space and synthetic-comprehensive, at a second moment, of the aforementioned conflicts and appropritions that can be evidenced
