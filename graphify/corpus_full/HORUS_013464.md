# Futures in the Continuity Present

Too often it seems that we live in a continuous present, in the subsequent, at the moment that comes immediately after, in a presense and a imbebibeción of the ‘short term’. These perceptions are forms of blocking that tarnish the possibility of building sustainable futures in the dailyness of the present. This dissertation proposes an experiential, performatic and embodied turn of the future, as well as a pedagogy of time away from concepts such as utopia or dystopia and science fiction in favor of a conception of plural and multidimensional time, the stimulation of the notion of change and the transformation beyond the fantasy. The research offers an inter and transdisciplinary methodology itself called ‘Spective’, which fuses participatory research-action based on art with social foresight, and is based on the present four axes of action: decolonizing the future, the game, the experience and experience of the future, and the projection of the future from a collective time, the colonial past. This methodology has been applied in different contexts of environmental sustainability, building of peace and intangible cultural heritage from the specifics of the significance of the an experiential, performative and embroiled turns of the future, as well as a pedagogy of time away from concepts such as utopia or dystopia, and science fiction in favor of a conception of time as plural and multidimensional, stimulating the notion of change and transformation beyond fantasy.
