# HYMENOPTERA DIVERSITY: APRIDAE, MELIPONINI AND APANI IN CEMENTERIES OF THE SUR OCCIDENT OF COLOMBIA: A OPPORTUNITY TO IMPROVE OUR ECOSYSTEMIC SERVICES

The objective of the study was to investigate the biodiversity of the Tribe meliponini and Apini (Apidae) in cemeteries of the Valle del Cauca and Cauca, Colombia (Buga, Cali, Cartago, El Cerrito, Guacarí, Palmira-Santa Helena- Cerrito, Tulúa, Buenaventura, Tunía ¿ Cauca). A total of 1227 individuals of 131 nests were collected in 12 cemeteries. Each specimen was classified according to taxonomic keys and reviewed externally by observation through a stereoscope (Leica S8APO) in the laboratory of Parasitology, Immunology and Infectious Diseases (PARINEI). The specimens rest in the entomological museum CEUN-70 of the National University of Colombia-Sede Palmira.
