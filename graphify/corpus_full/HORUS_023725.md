# CHARACTERIZATION OF INTER-ORGANIZATIONAL RELATIONS: UNIVERSITY-EMPRESE, KEY STAKEHOLDERS FOR CO-CREASE VALUE

The purpose of the text is to characterize the relations presented in the inter-organizational university-entrepreneurial field, as part of a specific objective of a research to structure the dimensions and categories of that day, in a context where co-creation of value is promoted. The methodology is of bibliometric analysis using the Bibliometrix R Studio Cloud Package, whose search protocols were carried out in the Web of Science (WoS) and Scopus databases. The records were obtained using Gephi and dividing the documents into three categories: hegemonic, structural and recent. The results allowed visualizing four approaches, which are complemented by a study carried out in 2019 and which identified six clusters, keeping similarity in themes. The concussions revolve around the importance of investigating inter-organizational relations between the University and the Company as key stakeholders, as they are generators of co-creation of value in greater benefit of society
