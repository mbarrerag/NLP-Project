# Information architecture model for the monitoring of intercultural health of a Health Promotion Company.

For an indigenous community, health is understood as the product of the harmonious relations of the human being with itself, the family, the community and nature, which result from strict compliance with social behavior norms, as well as respect for the forces of nature and its elements. In these terms, intercultural health is attended by an Indigenous Health Provider Company (EPSI). Transactional data that currently stores an EPSI can be related to researching the intercultural health of indigenous peoples, this leads to having results in accordance with their culture, in order to take successful actions that impact positively on the state of health of the community. Based on the systematic review of the literature, the general objective of this thesis is to design a model of architecture of the information that allows the monitoring of intercultural health in a Health Promoter Company that provides assurance services to an indigenous community, for this purpose, the data structure of the Indigenous Health Promoter Company (EPSI) Anas Wayuuu, is identified the requirements and the data obtained around the different components of the results of the Model of the indigenous health that can be identified as the alternative health
