# Evaluation of the effect of cultivation conditions on the production of Penicillium sp. HC1 used in the degradation of plant residues of flower crops in Guasca – Cundinamarca

In recent decades, Colombia has positioned itself as a referent worldwide in the floricultor industry. However, this prosperous sector faces environmental challenges, being the generation of residues a constant concern. In context, the production of carnation waste in the country is 1500 kg/month of productivity per hectare, of which on average 10% is used in composting and the remaining 90% is generally given as animal feed, which has been questionable by magnifying the pesticides in the trophic chain. Thus, as the search for sustainable and efficient solutions for the degradation of plant residues has become a priority of this agricultural sector. An alternative that has been studied is the direct use of micro-organisms with lignocellulolytic capacity. Bioculture S.A. and the Institute of Biotechnology of the National University of Colombia – IBUN developed an inoculum based on the production of the product HC1 to accelerate the degradation of lignocellulósic material. This bioinsum presents challenges aligned to the production and quality of its conidia in submerged fermentation and therefore, the present work evaluated different conditions of cultivation on the production. The effect of three air flows in the fermentation process submerged in agitated tank was further explored, achieving a significant improvement in the product to 2.5 vvm.
