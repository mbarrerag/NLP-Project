# Determination of carbon footprint of three higher education institutions in the Aburá Valley

Indiscriminate greenhouse gas emissions into the atmosphere have serious environmental consequences due to climate change. Among the consequences of global warming can be listed: floods in coastal cities, forest fires and melting glaciers, among others. Every person or institution generates greenhouse gases resulting from the activities carried out in their daily lives or in the exercise of their functions. The development of this research project seeks to determine the carbon footprint of three higher education institutions in the metropolitan area of the Aburra Valley, and then to propose strategies that seek to mitigate their greenhouse gas emissions. Initially, variables describing the phenomenon are identified, then the most appropriate methodology is selected based on the analysis of some existing methodologies for the calculation of the institutional carbon footprint and finally a plan for the reduction of such carbon footprints is designed. Thus, the university community is sought to raise awareness about the impacts of global warming and to provide information to the competent agencies that facilitate them to implement relevant actions to reduce the gases emitted by the institutions involved in the study
