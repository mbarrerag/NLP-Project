# Study of victims of road accidents in the city of Quibdó department of Chocó in the period 2014-2023

Road traffic accidents are one of the main causes of death, disability and hospitalization of people worldwide. Approximately 1.3 million lives are lost each year as a result of these eventualities. Between 20 and 50 million people suffer non-fatal trauma, and many of them cause disability according to the World Health Organization (WHO 2022). The cost of road accidents affects not only victims and their families, but also the economy linked in terms of deceased, injured, disabled and loss of potential income. Considering the importance of this issue throughout Colombia plans are adopted in road safety, which includes the implementation of programmes, plans and strategies to reduce the number of fatal and/or serious casualties caused by road accidents. This project seeks to identify and analyze the measurement and distribution of road accidents in the city of Quibdó, in the Department of the Chocó between 2014 and 2023, investigating their possible causes, the elements involved and thus establish a diagnosis of the real and apparent causes of road accidents, which allows the hospital to provide mobility and transit of the city, in the context of the city, in the context of the development and strategies of the city of the city and in order to reduce its potential causes, to take care and to take care. safety plans are adopted throughout Colombia, which includes the implementation of programs, plans and strategies to reduce the number of fatalities and/or serious injuries caused by road accidents.
