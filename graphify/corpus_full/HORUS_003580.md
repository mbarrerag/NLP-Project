# COLOIDAL DISPERSIONS

Dissolutions differ from simply called mixtures because the latter are heterogeneous, i.e. their components are distinguished by the naked eye or by the microscope. Dissolutions are also different from compounds, since they have the constant composition ratio, while the solutions are not. Finally, the solutions differ from other types of mixtures such as colloids or colloidal dispersions, this, mainly, by the size of the dispersed particles: in colloids the size of the particles is between 10-5 and 10-3 cm. This practice seeks to illustrate, then, different aspects of colloidal dispersions
