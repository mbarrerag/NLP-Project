# ENERGY USE OF RESIDEL BIOMASS IN COLOMBIA. A OPPORTUNITY FOR TECHNOLOGICAL TRANSFER AND INVESTMENT.

Due to the signing of international commitments such as the SDGs, the link to the OECD and the ambitious update of the NDC 2020, in which the country commits itself to a reduction of 51% of its emissions by 2030 and carbon neutrality by 2050, the Colombian government has been leading a change in the regulatory framework that includes strict environmental policies, an energy transition law, a climate change law, tax incentives to motivate investment in projects of FNCER and support to innovation to contribute to the decarbonization of the National Energy Matrix and to the National Strategy of Circular Economy, where renewable gases play a fundamental role as set out in the roadmap of the Hydrogen and the structuring of the NAMA of Biogas. On the other hand, the geographic location and agricultural vocation of the country promotes the availability of residual biomass with a wide energy potential that guarantees not to compete with food security. In this presentation, the investment opportunities for the production of renewable gases based on material and energy balances in Colombia will be addressed.
