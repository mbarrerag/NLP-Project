# Effect of cementation on the one-dimensional volumetric behavior of a soil in the Colombian Orinoquía.

This research studied the effect of cementation, as a fundamental part of the soil structure, in the volumetric behavior of a tropical soil (non-lateritic) clay limo with high sand content from the eastern Colombian plains. The study included tests, of controlled load and with constant deformation rate, to evaluate the behavior of the soil in one dimensional compression in compacted and reconstituted state. To investigate the effects of the structure on the soil behavior, samples with different characterization of the soil were tested on the soil cycle (2%, 5% and 8% regarding the dry soil mass).The minimum curing time of 28 days was established based on the results of tests of resistance to the unconfined compression.The compressibility curves of the different soil mixtures – cement and that of the reconstituted soil allowed to determine the degree of structure of the soil samples.In the case of the mixtures of 2% of cement content, the destructuring was so high that its compressibility curves crossed that of the reconstituted soil, indicating a degradation of the material site of the reconstituted soil.In the soil, the reconstituted soil was shown a growth of the vertical index
