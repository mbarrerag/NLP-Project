# PROPOSAL FOR A CONDITION INDEX: A CONDITION FOR PUBLIC SPACE MANAGEMENT

There is significant evidence on the benefits of public space (EP), which are achieved only when it is of quality. Latin American intermediate cities do not have an instrument to facilitate decision-making for the evaluation of the quality of the EP, although it has been evaluated, in the Latin American context quality indicators predominate in capital cities (Páramo et al., 2018; Siemens AG, 2010). Quantitative and qualitative methods based on user perception have been used without considering measurement parameters according to the condition of the elements that make up their infrastructure. A Public Space Status Index (PEC) is proposed based on quantitative quality assessment methods that have been put into practice. The research is thus addressed: review of literature on EP management and quantitative methods, proposed by ICEP, validation of ICEP (Delphi method) and demonstration of its usefulness in Manizales (Colombia). The ICEP is based on the qualities and functioning of EP objects linked to a range of values from 0 to 6 according to the percentage of elements in operation and the presence or absence of maintenance.
