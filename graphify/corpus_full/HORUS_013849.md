# Observatory of State Responsibility

The work of the observatory is oriented in analyzing the state contract based on a series of cases of national importance due to their media, economic and social impact, as they are immersed in failures and defects in their different stages of development. For this purpose, a review of the pre-contractual, contractual and post-contractual stages, where relevant, of the following cases: Hidroituango, Reficar, Transmilenio, Ruta del Sol, Via al Llano, Tunnel de la Línea and Centros Poblados
