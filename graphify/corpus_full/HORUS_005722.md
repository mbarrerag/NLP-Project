# Comparative genomics study for the identification of biomarkers with probiotic activity in the bacterial genus Lactobacillus spp.

The comparative genomics allows to identify common and specific genetic elements of strains of microorganisms. In the case of the genus Lactobacillus spp, it allows the identification of traits associated with probiotic activity, through markers that provide safety information for the host, response to stress, adhesion capacity and antimicrobial and immunomodulatory activity. With the aim of identifying biomarkers of probiotic activity, were downloaded complete genomes of Lactobacillus spp from the database National Biotechnological Information Centre (NCBI).A gene prediction and functional annotation were performed to later group ortologists proteins.Considering the core-genome, 20 biomarkers were selected and their respective firsts were designed.For the amplifications of the biomarkers, DNA was extracted from probiotic and pathogenic strains, were performed. Individual PCRs for each gene and the obtained amplicons were confirmed by means of electrophoresis and sequencing.
