# Cost-effectiveness of ecological restoration projects oriented to water regulation. An approach from hydrological simulation.

This research evaluates the cost-effectiveness of ecological restoration projects with a focus on the ecosystem function of water regulation, as a contribution to the analysis and targeting of investments for the maximization of socio-ecological benefits in scarce resource contexts. It applies to the project “Mas Bosques para Medellín (MBPM)” taking as a case of study the San Pedro micro-basket (1.5 km2 of area), located in the co-regiment of Santa Elena, municipality of Medellín, Colombia. The coverage of the microbasket corresponds to forests and semi-natural areas, agricultural territories and artificialized territories, whose extension is a determining factor in the analysis of the hydrological response to 5 different evaluated scenarios. The cost-effectiveness assessment implemented includes the hydrological simulation of the 5 scenarios through the application of the hydrological model Water Evaluation and Planning (WEAP), in addition to the analysis of the economic investments made in the micro-basket between the period 2008 and 2019 oriented to the purchase of pre-concentives, the establishment and maintenance of forest arrangements.
