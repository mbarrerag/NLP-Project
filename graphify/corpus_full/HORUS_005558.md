# EXPLORATION OF THE VERTEBRAL COLUMN

An exhibition of aspects related to the problems of the spine related to definition, basic anatomy, epidemiology, clinical history (anamnesis, physical examination), red flags to be taken into account, exploration in special situations (neurological deficiency or spondylorarthritis) and aspects to be taken into account in some atypical situations is carried out
