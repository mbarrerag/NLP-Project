# Use of neon lines in the study of components present in SEDs of silent radio AGNs

The emission line of [Ne V] in infrared has shown to be a relevant indicator of the activity of an AGN by its high potential for ionisation. Likewise the analysis of the ratio [Ne V]/[Ne II], has allowed to distinguish between stellar environments and AGNs. Based on the above, this work sought to verify from a sample of silent radio AGNs (Galax Seyfert and QSO) taken from the CDS database and its SIMBAD service, that the estimates of the fraction of the AGN (fAGN ) from CIGALE, coincide with the tracers of the lines of the Neon in the infrared.
