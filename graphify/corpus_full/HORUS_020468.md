# Contribution to the study of biochemical resistance response induced by the use of biotic elicitors in the interaction Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi race 2 - carnation (Dianthus caryophyllus L.)

In this research, the biochemical changes generated in the roots of keylo plants (Dianthus caryophyllus L.) were studied during the application of an elicitor of biotic origin (eFod, acronym of the elicitor from Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi) with potential for the induction of resistance to vascular wilting caused by the fungus Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi. To this end, analytical tools were used that allowed to determine changes in the proteomic and metabolic profiles, independently in three scenarios: 1. by effect of application of the elicitor of biotic origin; 2. during infection with the pathogen; and 3. during elictation and subsequent challenge with the pathogen. It was determined that, the application of the elicitor subject to study, has a positive effect in the reduction of progress of the disease and its application affects phenomena related to the resistance in plants, such as changes in production/accumulation of compounds derived from the shikimal/phenylpropaide route, together with a regulation of the disease associated We used analytical tools that allowed to determine the changes in the proteomic and metabolic profiles of foundation roots. Three scenarios were evaluated: 1) effect of the application of the choice of biotic origin; 2) effect of the infection with the pathogen; and 3) effect of the application and subsequent challenge with the pathogen. We found evidence that the application of this manufacturer has a positive effect on reducing the progress of the disease and that its application effects phenomena related to plant resistance, such as as changes in the application/production of compositions related to the challenge/benefit
