# Effectiveness of CDK–SMS nursing intervention for adults with chronic kidney disease

The self-management of persons with chronic renal disease is an opportunity for approach; there is currently a nursing intervention in self-management that has been shown to be effective in various contexts. To determine the effectiveness of the intervention CKD SMS on self-management behaviors, therapeutic adherence and self-efficacy in comparison with usual care. A pragmatic, simple blind clinical trial with random allocation of participants. The study consisted of comparing the usual intervention with respect to the intervention CKD SMS. The outcome variables were the self-management behaviors, therapeutic adherence and self-efficacy. The population under study were adults, men and women with chronic renal disease in stages 1-4, who attended control of their disease in a PSUR in Bogotá. The data analysis was carried out through descriptive statistics and through the multivariate analysis; through the effective behaviors of self-management, the therapeutic adherence and the self-conformance of the study were controlled. 118 participants were involved, 59 in each group. Post-service intervention was obtained that the intervention was effective in terms of the effective self-management, the disease, the therapeutic approach, the therapeutic adherence and the self-advanisation and the impact of participants. Outcome variables were self-management behaviors, therapeutic adherence, and self-efficiency. The study population was adult men and women with stage 1-4 chronic kinney disease, which addressed the control of their disease in an IPS in Bogota. The study consisted of the comparison of the usual intervention with respect to the CKD SMS intervention.
