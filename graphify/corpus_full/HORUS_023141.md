# VALIDATION OF THE LATIN AMERICAN SPANISH VERSION OF THE SCALE VALIDATION OF LIFE IN DISEASES THAT THREAT LIFE - FAMILY VERSION CAREFUL (QOLLTI-F)

Measuring the quality of life of family caregivers of persons in palliative care is fundamental to document the impact of care on the caregiver. This study aimed to adapt and validate a Latin American - Spanish version of the QOLLTI-F scale in family caregivers of patients in palliative care. This study was carried out between May and November 2019 in Medellín, Colombia. It was developed in three phases: translation and semantic adaptation (n = 17), apparent and content validity (n = 21), construct validity, internal consistency (n = 208) and test-retest reliability (n = 35). The family caregivers of patients with stage IV cancer in palliative treatment participated. In translation and semantic adaptation some items were adjusted following the recommendations of the caregivers. An index Fleisest (1) was found in the model of the structure of the structure of the patient above 0,65 for the elements of the review by family caregivers. In the content validity, Fleiss Kappa oscillated between 0.54 and 1 for the care experts.
