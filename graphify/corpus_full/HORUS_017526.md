# EFFECTS OF BREATHING ON SPINE POSTURE AND STABILITY

Musculoskeletal disorders related to stress and bad posture are becoming common in the young population [1] and the current pandemic situation might have worsen the situation. Breathing affects motor control and postural stability and plays several roles in physiological and psychological regulation [2]. Furthermore, deep abdominal breathing is widely used as a method of relaxation [3], being therefore a good candidate to face the problem. However, previous studies have found that many young subjects seem unable to perform deep abdominal breathing [4]. Therefore, the purpose of this study was to investigate the relation between breathing, spine posture and posture stability to expand the knowledge of the effects of breathing on the body posture in young adults
