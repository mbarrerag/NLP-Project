# Clinical and epidemiological characteristics of the alleged acute sodium monofluroacetate poisonings, treated in the northern subnet of Bogotá, 2018-2021.

Sodium monofluoroacetate is a worldwide banned rodenticide for several decades due to its high toxicity, the diagnosis of acute poisoning is based on clinical presentation as there is no useful diagnostic test available in the hospital environment that would confirm it, to this is added the absence of a specific antidote for its management. In Colombia and other countries of South America this substance is manufactured and distributed illegally for domestic use, therefore its exact composition and possible additional components that may be present in products that are marketed as sodium fluoroacetate. Through this study it performed the clinical and epidemiological characterization of cases recorded as alleged sodium fluoroacetate poisoning in the E.S.E Subred Norte de Bogotá during the period 2018-2021 are unknown; also included those cases of intoxication by rodenticides where the substance involved was not clear, but which resulted in a clinical picture suggestive of exposure to sodium fluoroacetate. In total, 14 cases were obtained of the most of the female sex, with the majority of the patients aged between 23 and 14 years, which corresponded with a clinical picture suggestive of exposure to sodium fluoroacetate
