# Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP): Review of the judicialization of cases of _x0093_false positive_x0094_ as crimes against humanity in Colombia, with a view to contributing to the search for truth, justice and reconciliation.

The research proposal is based on a documentary and historical compilation of three main variables, their definitions and their doctrinal and jurisprudential considerations, which fall into special jurisdiction for peace, crimes against humanity and criminal policy within the framework of justice, truth and reparation and is intended to generate an analysis of the Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP) and its deployment in order to judicialize cases called _x0093_false positives_x0094_ taking into account its legal nature of crimes against humanity in Colombia, focusing on its contribution to the search for truth, justice and reconciliation
