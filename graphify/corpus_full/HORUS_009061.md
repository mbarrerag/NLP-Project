# Richard Rorty: The unfortunate articulation between liberal democracy and capitalism

Richard Rorty, from an anti-fundationalist perspective, proposes the objective of analyzing, in ethical-political terms, how to live better. To this, it proposes three main elements, namely, the priority of democracy over philosophy, the fight against cruelty and always maintaining open conversation. Rorty concludes that the best political model for a desirable society is liberal democracy. However, it maintains the articulation between liberalism and capitalism. Thus, it aims at a capitalism controlled by the State, more a welfare state. In this research, the coherence of the Rorty approach is analyzed. Specifically, it defends that, by maintaining the articulation between liberalism and capitalism, Rorty contradicts its own post-industrial principles. This, since capitalism weakens and destroys democracy, while it constitutes a rather cruel way of life.
