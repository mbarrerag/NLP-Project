# Paraphernalia of the Trappantock

The mental illness in general, and schizophrenia in particular, has been circumscribed to the scientific reading provided by medicine, an approximation conditioned not only by scientific precepts but also by the cultural, social, economic and even political characteristics dominant in the different stages of human history. In this sense the mental illness has been a dynamic and changing concept, permanently, through time. The term “crazy” used to name people with behaviors outside the “normal” framework has been lent for interpretations, stigmatizations and interventions that have evolved from the sacro-religious, the demonic, and antisocial, and in terms of treatment from the exorcism in the middle age to the containment and interventions, theoretically scientific, as invasive and lesive as the electroshuccs, lobotomy and extreme medication parallel with the stigmatization and social exclusion.
