# BIOCONTROL OF BACLRUS SP. EA-CB0959 ON FUSARIUM OXYSPORUM F. SP. CUBINSE (FOC R1) SEBASTIAN ZAPATA HENAO1, LIGHT EDITH ARGEL ROLDAN2, LILLENA MARÍA HOYOS-CARVAJAL3, VALESKA VILLEGAS-SCOBAR4.

Musaceae are a complex micro-ecosystem in which different niches are occupied by a wide variety of microorganisms, which is why biological control agents (BAC) and their active compounds are sought to limit the development of different pathogens of the culture, such as Fusa-rium oxysporum f. sp. cubense (Foc) causative agent of the sea- chitez by Fusarium, a disease that does not have effective control measures, except for phytoimprovement (Dita et al., 2018). Under this premise, the ability of isolated bacteria of Musa sp. to inhibit the growth of Foc breed 1 (R1) at the in vitro and in vivo level was evaluated
