# Analysis and contextualization of the chromatic fantasy and leak bwv 903 by Johann Sebastián Bach

This work aims to provide a brief study of the Chromatic Fantasy and Figue BWV 903 by Johann Sebastian Bach to the musical community and those who may be interested. Therefore, this paper developed a detailed insight about the content of this musical work, including contributions from the personal and subjective perspective from the author, encouraging to build a supporting text that will address the technical, structural, and musical complexity of this piece of great importance in the piano replicaire.
