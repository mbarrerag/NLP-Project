# INTER-NETHICAL RELATIONS IN THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN.

The aim of this article is to describe and analyze significant elements present in the relationships and interactions that occur between the Zenu indigenous and other surrounding populations, with an emphasis on the traces of what could be classified as "negative reciprocity." The methodological tools used for data collection were the interview and the participant observation, as part of a prolonged ethnography in the Zenu territory. The most relevant results of this study indicate that manifestations classifiable as jealousy/envy, which are explicitly present in inter-ethnic relations (between indigenous and white/mestiza population), extend many times to the relations between those there called "more Indians" and "less Indians." In the conclusions of this paper, ethnological reflections are re-eminent, which show how the feelings and emotions mentioned are fundamental elements of sociability, as they shape and reinforce local hierarchies, seeking to balance historical relations of oppression and disadvantage
