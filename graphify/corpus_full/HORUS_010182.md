# DESIGN OF THE NOISE DISCRIMINATION TEST (DHR) FOR COLOMBIAN SPANISH STAGE 1: DESIGN OF THE CONTRALATERAL AND IPSILATERAL NOISE TEST AND PILOTAGE IN NORMOUS SUBJECTS

Abstract / Abstract Context: A common manifestation of auditory processing disorder is the difficulty to understand speech in noise, condition presented in different age groups generating communicative disability. To design the first version and pilot the test of speech discrimination in noise (DHR) with counterlateral and ipsilateral noise, for Colombian population normoyente. Descriptive study for design and exploratory during piloting. The sample was 10 men and 10 women, between 18 and 50 years, without neurotological background. For statistical analysis the Inteligibility of stimuli was measured and three interrelated categories were established; Mode of presentation of stimulus (contralateral- Ipsilateral), Hearing (Right -Left) and Noise or Level of Signal Relationship (SNR) of -5dB and -10dB. The most intelibilities with -5 dB SNR obtaining very easy stimuli, the intelligibility with -10 dB NRS was 0.95 for words and 0.97 for those sentences.
