# Classification of Hypersurfaces in the Euclidean 4-sphere Satisfying □H=λH

Let M3 be a hypersurface immersed into the 4-dimensional Euclidean sphere S4⊂R5 and denote by H its mean curvature vector field in R5. This paper locally classifies those hypersurfaces that satisfy the condition □H=λH with λ∈R, where □ denotes the Cheng-Yau operator of the hypersurface. In particular, we prove that (open pieces of) totally geodesic great 3-spheres S3⊂S4 are the only hypersurfaces in S4 which satisfy the condition □H=0. We also obtain that (open pieces of) totally umbilical small 3-spheres S3(r)⊂S4, 0<r<1, and the Clifford torus S2(1/3)×S1(2/3)⊂S4 are the only hypersurfaces in S4 which satisfy the condition □H=λH for a real number λ≠0. This characterizes the Clifford torus S2(1/3)×S1(2/3)⊂S4 as the only non-trivial hypersurface in S4 with □H=λH for a real number λ
