# CO-CONSTRUCTION WITH COMMUNITY LEADERS AND HUMAN STALENT IN HEALTH OF A LINE OF APPLICATION FOR SEXUAL EDUCATION

Digital tools, Health education, Sexual health Introduction: Innovation through the use of information and communication technologies - tics, addresses public health actions for the detection and control of epidemic outbreaks of diseases, and health promotion (1), so it should take into account the social determinants of the community in which it will be applied for the improvement of the quality of life of these people. In accordance with the proposal of PAHO/WHO (2.3) on healthy environments, it is considered that it is at the local level that the social management of health promotion is made more effective, improving the community participation, cultural competence, the development of communication among people, and the involvement of social and health services towards families from a cultural and health promotion approach. Research study, participatory action, developed according to the stages proposed by Martin (4), which includes 1) diagnosis, 2) programming and analysis, 3) conclusions and proposals. For the first stage, semi-structured interviews were carried out to 16 community leaders and 18 people of human talent in health of the municipality of Guapi-Cauca, were made recordings and transcriptions, prior informed phase. The focus was on the subject of addressing the app, sexual health education for adolescents, whose content could be downloaded on mobile phones or computers, as devices for greater use and access by the young population of the municipality. Under a primary health care approach and the use of ICTS, the possibility is opened to make use of digital tools as a possible strategy for prevention and promotion in health, with regard to the needs identified in and by the same community. Technology and information tools in health are a possible solution to impact the barriers of care and reduce gaps for timely health care, being accessible and culturally adaptable. Limitations: Collection of data in a virtual way in pandemic situation by Covid-19, connectivity of participants restricted by availability of access to the internet and of time, aspects that influenced the progress of the project.
