# Design of a Mach-Zehnder Modulator Using Silicon-on-Insulator Technology and Its Application in Opto-Electronic Oscillators

In this paper, the design of a Mach-Zehnder modulator (MZM) is presented. The MZM consists of the following building blocks: Phase-shifters based on the carrier injection principle, compact Y-branches, and waveguide bends. Each block was individually characterized at the device-level, and the performance of the modulator was evaluated through circuit-level simulation. Furthermore, we propose an application of the MZM in an opto-electronic oscillator (OEO) and verify its functionality within the OEO system
