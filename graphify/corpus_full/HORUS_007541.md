The implementation of the project that is proposed from the laboratory of sanitary engineering represents a strategic opportunity to optimize and revitalize the use of a resource that is available in the University. The proposal of investment for maintenance and validation of the equipment allows to transform this equipment in the functional and efficient tool that is required in this case.
