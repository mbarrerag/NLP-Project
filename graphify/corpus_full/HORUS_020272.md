# The impact of behavioral factors on tax evasion in Colombia

tax evasion is a phenomenon that affects a large part of the developed countries and in developing at the global level, reducing the available income by States to be invested socially (Alm & Torgler, 2011). Consequently, there has been a great interest on the part of academia and government institutions to determine the causes of evasion with a view to generating a reduction in them or a mitigation of the negative effects of this phenomenon (Fernández-Cainzos, 2006), given that the development of an adequate study in tax matters could influence relevant tax planning, balancing the interests of society and the State (Gon-zález-Echavarría et al., 2019, p. 243). However, nowadays there is still no single formula that allows determining the reason for evasion; in fact, the causes are becoming more and more and are constantly changing in relation to changes in society
