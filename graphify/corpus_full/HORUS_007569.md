# Assistant student for the Research Project "Economic Assessment of the World Heritage in Colombia: the Cultural Landscape"

The economic development of a community is influenced by the values, beliefs, traditions and rituals (intangible cultural capital) that identify them as a group, as well as by the material elements and products of artistic and creative creations that represent them (material cultural capital); that is, that culture has a deterministic role in the processes of transformation and social progress, not only by the economic effects that it generates in terms of jobs, income flows and production, but also by being a useful and effective instrument to intervene and give solutions to different social and territorial problems. Thus, the putting into value of culture is unsusceptible for the planning, implementation and evaluation of public policies. Under these premises we proposed to carry out the research project _x009x009_Economic evaluation of the heritage of humanity in Colombia: the Cultural Landscape Cafetero_x0094_ (Hermes code 60244). Given the times, scopes and commitments proposed in it, it makes necessary to have support staff for the development of research, academic and administrative activities, since it is an important teaching day project and does not have alternative sources of funding
