# INTEGRAL MANDATE OF WASTES IN A PROPOSED ASSETS BUILDING COMPANIES

The increase in industrial activities has led to a greater production of solid waste, the poor management of these negatively impacts the environment and human health, therefore, there is a need to promote the correct management of waste at national, regional and local level.The aim of this study is to update the generation rates and identification of the problems associated with waste management, in order to be able to formulate a proposal for improvement for the Integrated Management Solid (non-hazardous) wastes that is carried out in two main headquarters of a company providing the public toilet service.In the first phase the general characteristics of the headquarters were compiled by means of a search on the official website of the company.In the second phase a diagnosis is made of the current situation of the management of the waste through field visits, processing of lists of check, photographic register, among others. Subsequently the characterization and quantification of the waste are executed following the guidelines of the manual for the Integrated Management of Wastes in the Borrá Valley and the technical norm ASTM D523192
