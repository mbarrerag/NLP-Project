# Analysis of the public policy of resettlement of population for high risk not mitigated in Bogotá, a perspective from the historical neo-institutionalism

This final master's degree work aims to present a descriptive analysis on the development of urban resettlement of population located in areas of high risk that is not mitigated during the period 1990-2023. In this regard, figures of the district administration indicate that from 1997 to 30 June 2023 there have been 26,173 cases of resettlement, where 9,540 families were identified and 24,843 persons in conditions of high risk not mi:gable (Open Data Bogotá, 2023). The resettlement of population by high risk not mitigated is carried out by occupation of areas identified in danger of flooding, overflow of water bodies, mass removal phenomena and torrential avenues. Conceptually, for the development of this final work, the theoretical framework called historical neo-institutionalism, using the following conceptual tools: critical situation, institutional development and dependence of the path, to explain the development of the resettlement of population located in areas of high risk not mitigated as one of the constituent axes of risk management policy.
