# PRONOSTIC OF MULTI-TISTED TIME SERIES USING VECTORIAL SUPPORT RETURN

The regression of vector support (SVR) has allowed to model and forecast time series in an optimal way, in cases when the assumptions of the classic models are not fulfilled or when it is more desirable to make use of supervised learning algorithms due to the nonlinearity of the time series in study. This work has been carried out mainly with univariate time series, where the results of the prognosis have been much more precise than when using classic models such as ARMA or ARIMA models and even when comparing it with other learning algorithms such as neural networks (comparison performed through the MAPE), due to the ability of vector support machines to do in the face of seasonality, atypical values and properly generalize the unseen data [1] in addition to preserving the autocorrelation structure of the time series
