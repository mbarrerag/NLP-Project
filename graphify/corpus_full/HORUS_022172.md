# Corrosion resistance at high temperatures of nanostructured coatings (Ti,Cr,Al,Si)N deposited with the cosputting technique.

The objective of this research is to synthesize and characterize quaternary and kinary coatings of the type (Ti,Cr,Al)N and (Ti,Cr,Al,Si)N varying the concentration of silicon in the system by using the technique of spraying HiPIMS. The coatings are deposited with approximate thicknesses of between 800 and 1000 nm. The coatings were deposited on two different substrates of steel 316L and hard metal K20. Once deposited the coatings, their elemental composition was measured by the use of X-ray fluorescence (XRF) and a disffraction pattern was obtained by means of X-ray diffraction (XRD) and a surface analysis was performed by means of the use of electro-scopic scanning microscopy (SEM) and a depositional measurement by means of the micro-scopicity by means of micro-scopic force (AFM). Once the coatings were characterized by cyclic corrosion to 800°C during 8 hours and the analysis of isothermic corrosion to 600°C during 100 cycles.
