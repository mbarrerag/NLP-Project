# ASBEST: CRITICAL PERSPECTIVES FROM PUBLIC HEALTH

In our country, there has been a growing interest in the health problems related to asbestos, which led to the prohibition of all its uses through the Law 1968 of 2019. From the National University of Colombia, the importance of contributing to debate and public action in this regard has been raised, taking as a starting point the signing of an agreement between the Institute of Health of Italy and the Institute of Public Health, to develop cooperation aimed at strengthening the dissemination of scientific knowledge for the decision making that involve society as a whole (Marsili, Comba, Bruno, et al., 2010)
