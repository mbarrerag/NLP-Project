# USE OF NEURONAL ARTIFICIAL MODELS FOR THE ELABORATION OF AMMONIAC EMISSION SCENARIES IN AVAICO GALPONES.

The poultry industry both worldwide and in Colombia has experienced an important growth and the environmental normivity in force requires control and decrease of ammonia emission within the sheds. From the deysections, ammonia variable can affect the birds and people generating health problems, as well as economic losses in this industry. The environmental conditions and variables of design and operation of the exploitation activities within the shed, influence the quantity of ammonia emission. In this work, a neuronal model based on artificial neural networks of multilayer fed forward and propagation of the error backward is used for the prediction of the emission of ammonia released into the air within the galpons for posture and fattening birds. The performance of the neuronal model was evaluated from the linear correlation coefficient of the R. For the training of the neural networks, a set of training based on the internal networks, based on the data base elaborated by systematic review of emissions in poultry galpons, located in countries of the tropic and the northern hemisphere with influence of climatic stations, for both the functional and postural plants, is constructed by means of 300 reports., proposals for networks of Multilayer Perceptron of Feedforward-Backpropagation, whose connections were represented using the sigmoid function as an activation function between the input neuronal layer and the intermediate neural layers, and the linear function for the output neuronal layer. The proposals of the network described, are conformed with various topologies based on architectures of intermediate neural layers and number of neurons in these layers, trained for twelve learning algorithms of Backpropagation. The process systematization was made from a computer code in M language, typical of the MATLAB® software, which incorporates a library contained in the Neural Toolbox of the same software, and which allows to implement the proposed models of the described network type
