# Clinical and economic links associated with adherence to pulmonary rehabilitation in patients with exacerbation of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease affiliated to the contributory regimen in Colombia

Introduction Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is considered a public health problem, which causes a large burden in terms of morbidity and mortality and economics. Efforts have been initiated to include pulmonary rehabilitation (RP) in the patient's care; however, the adherence to the RP and its association with different outcomes in the Colombian context is unknown. Objective Determine the association between adherence to the RP in survival, hospital readmission and costs of patients with COPD exacerbation affiliated to the contributory regimen in Colombia during the years 2013 to 2017. Methods Retrospective observational study of patients affiliated to the contributory regimen in Colombia between the years 2013 and 2017. Cohort was formed with older patients with exacerbated COPD, using an electronic algorithm based on codes. The adherence to the RP was evaluated taking into account the number of RP sessions billed at the observation time. The analysis of the RP was controlled taking into account clinical and sociodemographic variables such as age, sex, health promotion entity, and the comorbidity index of Charsson, department of origin. It was identified that adherence to RP has a variation in total costs billed at 4.57 million Colombian pesos (CI95 95% 0,60 – 1.4.6). Conclusion Low adherence to RP was identified during the study period. No association was found between survival and hospital re-entry at one year with adherence to RP. A variation in total costs billed at one year was identified. Adherence to RP can be evaluated according to the number of RP sessions held over a given period of time. Clinical variables should be included to reinforce the findings of this research, as well as their impact on both health and associated costs .
