# Photography by Photography: an Interpretation exercise: the joyous and hurt in a selection of photographs by Daniel Rodríguez

This work aims to understand the experience of a spectator when observing a photograph, for which images taken by the photojournalist Daniel Rodríguez will be used. Thus, when focusing attention on photography per se, this research differs from other studies carried out on the work of Rodríguez by stopping in the reflection on the relation of the subject with the image and not with its use as possible document of being inferred from it. The research was conducted from the reading of a selection of photographs from the aforementioned photographic background of Daniel Rodríguez. The analysis of these photographs was divided into La Bogotá voivo and La Bogotá dolida. Likewise, for each photograph two readings or categories were assigned: From my reading, corresponding to my personal voice and reading of each photograph, and From the theory, corresponding to the theoretical description of each photograph. Finally, and as a consequence of the study of these photographs, it was possible to reach the following: photography is not evidence from the real, it is pure speculation and fabulation . . Finally, from these same photographs it was possible to make a first approach to Daniel Rodríguez's view and reach a maxim, photography is not evidence of reality, it is pure specification and manufacture
