# CAPFITOGEN3, A BOX OF TOOLS FOR THE CONSERVATION AND PROMOTION OF THE USE OF AGRICULTURAL BIODIVERSITY

Since 2012 and with the sponsorship of the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture, the project Farmer's Pride (Horizon 2020 Framework Programme of the European Union), the University of Birmingham (UK) and the National University of Colombia have been implemented. A system of analysis of information on agrobiodiversity in the form of a computer toolbox called CAPFITOGEN, of which is presented in 2021 its latest version, CAPFITOGEN3, after 2 years of development on version 2.1. The form of toolbox and its dissemination through workshops training sessions, were the strategies chosen to make the transfer of technology involved in CAPFITOGEN more effective in terms of reaching the maximum possible audience. However, to achieve the highest degree of adoption, the strategy consisted in that the processes carried out by CAPFITOGEN for the users have been reduced to the daily problems at low cost of those people carrying out conservation activities in situ or ex situ or promoting the use of what is conserved in the germplas banks (the objective was clearly the wealth of the countries and the ten-years). At the regional level, five at the regional level and the rest at the national level, distributed over 19 different countries. At the regional level, the greatest adoption is evident in Latin America, South Africa, the Near East and Europe. Beyond the fact that coverage and adoption data are encouraging for a low-cost technology transfer programme, these results must be associated with simple aspects that should be taken into account in technological development aimed at the RFAA community: Is scientific bases easy to understand. In the presence of heterogeneous audiences, the concept that is to be transferred must be related to tangible and everyday facts that any technician, student or professional has been able to verify in their routine activity. Simplified processes. The complexity of certain scientific methodologies is inversely proportional to the willingness to adopt. Not always better. Facilitation of use. If technical-scientific bases are assimilated, adoption will be given sooner or later, provided that the appropriate means for the use of technology are provided. In the case of CAPFITOGEN, all processes are developed in the language of R. Understanding scheduled processes in R may require from days to months of training, depending on the initial degree of computer training
