# Factors associated with death and evaluation of genetic variants in adult patients with covid 19 who enter the critical care unit in the national university hospital of Colombia.

The mortality associated with infection by SARS CoV-2 varies in different geographical locations, finding in relation to variability in the population with respect to the prevalence of comorbidities, age, the immune response of the host, among others. Genetic variants of the virus with different phenotypes have been described, either by antigenic changes, transmissibility or virulence, and therefore have an impact on morbidity and mortality Objectives: To determine which factors are associated with death in patients with COVID 19 entering the critical care unit and to describe the frequency of variants of SARS CoV-2 in these patients. An analytical observational study, retrospective type cases and controls were performed to evaluate the association between clinical, paraclinic and sociodemographic variables and the outcome of death, in patients entering the critical care unit with diagnosis of COVID 19, in the first analysis of the first analysis of the first diagnosis of 2020 and August 2021.
