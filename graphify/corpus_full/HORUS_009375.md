# Quality of RESIDUAL WATER CONTENTS FOR INTEGRAL EVALUATION OF EFLUENTS OF THE GOLD LIXIVIATION PROCESS WITH CIANURO IN THE DEPARTMENT OF CALLS

The water quality indexes (ICA) are born as instruments to simplify the way in which the quality of the water resource is valued, condensing the physicalochemical parameters of water quality in compact form through a number, an interval or a descriptive category. In addition to the ICAs have developed, although with less diffusion, the ICARs of Residual Water Quality Index (ICAR). These have been applied to the evaluation of effluents of different types of industries and can be used as a tool for monitoring and management of the impact of the wastewater on the water resource. It is thus proposed the formulation of a ICAR basado in the Colombian environmental regulations. To calculate the ICAR the method of the arithmetic index weighted taking as reference the parameters of Resolution 0631 of 2015 of the Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development and its maximum permissible values in the municipalities of Marmato and Maniza is provided by the department of the water. CAR of 828.06, which indicates a very poor quality of the effluent. This encourages to seek treatment alternatives that take into account the characteristics of the effluents of the department. Finally, ICAR has allowed to evaluate the quality of the water integrally and plan mitigation actions.
