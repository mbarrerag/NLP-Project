# A contribution to understanding the innovation strategy with emphasis on exaptive innovation processes.

This doctoral research addresses the problem of the formation of innovation strategies from the decisions and actions that guide the innovation process, taking as a reference the evolutionary phenomenon of the exaptation, which is important for innovation because it is possible to find unforeseen applications to the products or technologies that dominate the company. Therefore, its general objective is to explain the formation of innovation strategies from the analysis of the innovative process as an exaptive phenomenon, to address this objective, a methodological triangulation is designed. Initially, from the review of literature, a conceptual model of formation of innovation strategies and a systematic review of the concept of exaptation and its relation to innovation is proposed. Subsequently, a theoretical model of exaptive innovation strategy is proposed, as a representation of the strategic system. Finally, and seeking to enrich the understanding of the phenomenon studied, an empirical analysis of the model is made in a multiple study of cases and is complemented by a comparative study of cases that contrasts the exaptation as an innovation strategy. It is concluded that the formation of exaptive innovation strategies is of a predominantly emerging nature, but also allows to be favored by the deliberate practices of R & D and innovation
