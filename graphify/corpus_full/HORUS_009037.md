# CO2 OXID ASSISTANCE AND EVALUATION AS CATALIZED IN GLYCEROL CARBONATE OBTAINATION

One of the most promising proposals is the production of glycerol carbonate by means of the direct carboxylation reaction, a reaction involving the contact between CO2 and glycerol by making use of solid materials with Lewis-type acid-base properties to activate CO2 and glycerol on its surface and catalyze the glycerol carbonate formation reaction. Considering the above, in this research a detailed study was carried out of CO2 activation on La and La-Zr oxides and its catalytic evaluation in the direct carboxylation reaction between CO2 and glycerol
