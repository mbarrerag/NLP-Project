# DETECTION OF SP. LEISHMANIA AND IDENTIFICATION OF SOURCES OF BLOOD INSURANCE IN THE COLOMBIAN AMAZON

The biodiversity characteristic of the Amazon region provides an ideal ecosystem for the establishment and proliferation of insects such as the phlebotominets vectors of Leishmaniasis. These zoonoses affected between 2007 and 2022 to 36,779 people in this region, where Amazonas and Caquetá provided more than 50% of the cases. In the Colombian Amazon circulates more than 30% of the fauna of phlebominets registered in the country, finding vectors of Leishmania panamensis, Leishmania amazonensis and Leishmania braziliensis. Although in some vectors blood traces of vertebrates have been recorded, as criteria associated with the identification of potential reservoirs, there is a vacuum of information for this region. The objective of this research was to identify the presence of Leishmania sp., with the molecular marker HSP-70N, besides the sources of blood intake, using the markers CytB and DNAr 12S, in phlebotominets of Amazon and Caquetá. 168 insects grouped in 10 generas and 26 species were analyzed, of which 61 individuals were in the females were previously identified. auraensis was detected as many as two sources of intake. This is the first approach to the study of insect interaction, food source and presence of Leishmania in the Amazon region where vertebrate DNA was detected in species with high epidemiological value and where the presence of Leishmania could also represent a risk of transmission
