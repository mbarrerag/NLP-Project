# Understanding the concept of function using graphing calculators.

One of the fundamental concepts in the study and understanding of mathematics and other branches of this discipline is function. The same historical evolution of the concept has proved complex, but more so its understanding within the classroom. Additionally, the classroom dynamics and didactics used in the classroom do not tend to favor the learning process of the aforementioned concept, constituting one of the real challenges of mathematical thought development that involves both teachers and students. This work aims to provide a didactic proposal, in which, through the use of a software of graphic calculator, the student is given the possibility of developing a meaningful learning that will facilitate a greater understanding of the concept of function, making use of different systems of representation, problem solving, and modelling of real-life situations
