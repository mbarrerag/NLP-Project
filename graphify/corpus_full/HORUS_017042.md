# ASSOCIATIVE WORK AS AN EMANCIPATOR PRACTICE AND CREATOR OF SOCIAL TREATMENT

This essay presents a historical account of the lines of association that were created in acis between 2012 and 2015. During that period, new guidelines were introduced with the aim of making a change of perspective and, to some extent, of reflecting on the role of the researchers of acis in their capacity as scientific diaspora. At the Board of Directors of that time, which I chaired, we unanimously decided to direct our activities and projects towards the local association environment. Consequently, we were able to involve new people and open our organization to new scenarios and professions that historically had been considered as outside our scientific work. As a result, new members converged within acis, social organizations, government organizations, and other local associations. To illustrate this strategic change, this reflection will present the acis Symposium 2015 entitled "How long will we have water in Colombia?" as well as other activities that developed in Geneva on cooperation and development issues
