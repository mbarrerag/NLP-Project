# Strengthening basic training capacities in cell culture

Currently, animal cell cultivation is one of the most widely used systems for the production of biopharmaceuticals, since its advantages have been demonstrated in comparison with other systems for the expression of recombinant proteins such as bacteria, yeasts or plant crops. The aim of this project is to familiarize seedbed members with basic cultivation techniques, which will enable them to improve their skills in order to encourage research in the area of animal cell-based biotechnology
