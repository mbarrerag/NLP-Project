# PROJECT, IMAGE, ALGORITME

Project, image, algorithm is a contribution to the construction of interdisciplinary knowledge at a time when contemporary challenges require a perspective that transcends traditional academic limitations. We start from the premise that current problems cannot be effectively addressed from a single discipline. This work brings together a diverse group of researchers and academics in an area of exchange that encompasses seemingly unrelated topics, such as images, objects, architecture, mathematics, the arts and communications. The proposal is that the axis that articulates all these themes is composed of three parts: the project, images and algorithms. The eleven chapters of this book are intrinsically linked - two through the project spirit. It is a question of contributing from Latin America to the project in a world today driven by algorithms and visualities in constant change
