# _x0093_ Characterization and valuation of vernacular housing in the north of the Chingaza-Cundinamarca National Natural Park, with a view to its protection and conservation_x0094_.

The _x0093_Cobijo_x0094_, the land inherited from generation to generation in peasant families, the farm_x0094_, the minifundio, have been for generations the places of convergence, productivity and sustenance of peasant communities that inhabit the rurality of the Colombian territory. The sense of inhabiting and producing a special space like the rural territory, is visually complemented by a changing natural landscape with activities, tasks, customs, oral traditions, usually acquired through the experience of generations in the territory over the years. These dynamics have directly impacted the form and spontaneity of the self-constructed buildings with materiality and oral and practical tradition.The owners and the community that influence the properties that are found in the vicinity of the Chingaza National Park, have disengaged themselves from these constructive traditions, the use of regional and original building sites materials, to give access to new techniques and constructions that are outside the landscape context, techniques as invasive and decontextualized as the concrete use of the rural areas, plus some of the high-valued housings, which have been built in rural areas
