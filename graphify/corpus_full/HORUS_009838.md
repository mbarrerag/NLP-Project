# REPORTED FREQUENCY OF PULPO-PERIODONTAL DISEASE BY HEALTH SERVICES RESOURCES IN COLOMBIA 2015-2022

To present the reported frequency of pulp and periodontal diseases based on the report of health service providers to the territorial entities in Colombia. A quantitative, cross-sectional and descriptive study was carried out, based on information obtained from the Colombian population of SISPRO, attended by all types of providers registered at the national level, according to the different levels of affiliation to the system and whose main diagnosis was periodontitis or some type of periapical pulp pathology. For the period 2015-2022 periodontal octopus diseases were generally reported at 57.7% for gingivitis, pulpitis of 15.8%, and periodontitis 6.3%, as well as pulpitis, necrosis and tooth loss were presented with a significantly higher frequency in the subsidized regime. The frequency of pulp and periodontal diseases from these reports are lower than those reported in previous national studies, the results should be interpreted with caution since they may be due to a positive impact of the interventions of the health system or may indicate deficiencies in the information systems
