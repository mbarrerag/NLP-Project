# VARIABILITY AND CLIMATE CHANGE: REALITIES AND CHALLENGES FOR PAPA PRODUCTION SYSTEMS IN COLOMBIA

As a basis for the search for solutions to adapt and mitigate the variability and climate change of potato cultivation in Colombia, institutions such as the Colombian Federation of Potato Producers (FEDEPA) administrator of the National Fund for Pope Promotion (FNFP) and the National University of Colombia are at the forefront of research to address these challenges, seeking innovative solutions that allow the sector to effectively face the new climate reality. In this document, we will discuss how these factors are influencing potato production in Colombia and explore adaptation and mitigation strategies available to producers
