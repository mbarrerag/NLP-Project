# PRONOSTIC INDICATORS FOR THE OVERVIVIVENCY OF POTROS PNEONATES HOSTILIZED IN THE BOGOTA SABAN: ANALYSIS OF BLOOD GAS, ELECTROLYTES AND METABOLITES

Neonate foals are susceptible to life-threatening diseases. For so-called, it is important to identify markers that allow predict the survival of these patients, and to initiate appropriate treatment for each case. This study was conducted at the Equina Foal Care Perinatal Center located in Cujicá, Cundinamarca. The clinical histories of 106 pyotrs who entered hospital were reviewed and analyzed between September 2019 and December 2020 to assess the predictive value of the parameters of blood gases and electrolytes versus the survival of hospitalized neonate foals. Different statistical tests were used such as the Kolmogorov Smirnov test, Mann-Whitney U test, binary logistic regression, chi-square test, odds ratio and analysis of the receptor operator characteristic curve (ROC). It was observed that the most common acid-basic disorder at admission to hospitalisation was metabolic acidosis (37/106), and the most frequent electrolyte imbalance was hypocalcemia (89/106).
