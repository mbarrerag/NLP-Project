# Research Seed in Entomology FCAB - 2024

Based on the Hermes 59847 project entitled _x0093_Chonta-Cao: Promoting inter-institutional collaboration and dialogue of knowledge for agroecological sustainability of chontaduro and cocoa in the coastal regions of Ecuador and Colombia_x0094_ will be collected and studied the insectile fauna of three farms with chontaduro crops in Lloró, Chocló. The students of undergraduate, graduate, graduate and expert participants of the Entomology Research Seed FCAB will be proposed and will develop short projects that allow the exploration, discussion, human resource formation and knowledge transfer among the participants of the Semillero. The aim of this proposal is to strengthen processes of analysis, discussion, recognition and evaluation of entomofauna of the municipality of Lloró, Chocó. It is expected to contribute to the formation of human resource in pre- and post-graduate laboratory based on analysis of biological samples traceability, its integrated taxonomic determination, discussion of aspects of biology, ecology and importance as a pest of the species, all the participation and the research is available
