# Context and prospects of carbon quantum dots applied to environmental solutions

Among the novel wave of nanomaterials, carbon quantum dots (CQDs) have shown excellent performance in a variety of applications, especially in the environmental field, because they can be a sustainable material from synthesis to application. This study aims to identify how CQDs can contribute to environmental welfare. First, we present a brief historical progress of CQDs focused on environmental technology development. Then, the generalities and trends of the CQDs’ synthesis and characterization are discussed. Next, the performance characteristics of CQDs’ applications in the context of environmental challenges are addressed. Finally, using several toxicity studies, the implications of CQDs on living beings are presented. CQD's synthesis processes increasingly focus on greener methods, which include using lower reaction temperatures and times, employing catalysts, utilizing water as the primary solvent, and repurposing waste materials as carbon sources. These strategies aim to produce high-quality CQDs while minimizing their environmental impact. CQDs can contribute to environmental well-being by improving various processes, such as water treatment, pollutant detection and degradation, and alternative energy production. By using CQDs, there is a valuable opportunity to enhance environmental technologies with respect to sustainability, process efficiency, and stability
