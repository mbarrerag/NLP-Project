# Modernization of the Colombian public university and American philanthropic foundations: the case of the University of Antioquia, 1956-1969

This research analyses the modernization process of the University of Antioquia, between 1956 and 1969. It also correlates the modernization process with the interaction between the University and the philanthropic foundations of the United States Ford, Kellogg and Rockefeller. First, it analyzes the history of the idea of university, making a historical journey of it as an institution and describing its changes through its relationship with modernity and modernization. Second, it examines the relations of the University of Antioquia with the philanthropic foundations of the United States. It identifies how each of these relations is conditioned by the uniqueness of the link. This fact allows to see that the establishment of these alliances took place in a field of diplomatic strategies, exchanges, appropriations and favors that were integrated into the modernization plans of the University, beyond the mere reception of donations. Third, it explores how the transformation of the academic and administrative spheres also led to consider the increase of the enrolment and administrative planning of a teaching plant that responded to the professional and research needs that the moment required. Third, it explores how the transformation of the academic and managerial areas led to consideration of created integration and administrative planning for a teaching facility that would meet the professional and research needs of the time. Finally, it is pointed out out how the construction of the University City responded to the integration, planning and rationalizing effforts of the University of Antioquia.
