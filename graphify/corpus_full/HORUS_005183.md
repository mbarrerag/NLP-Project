# HYPERALDOSTERONISM PRIMARY, A FRESH PATOLOGY AND INFRADIAGNOSIS: PRESENTATION OF A CASE

The primary hyperaldosteronism is a syndrome that is little recognized but prevalent, characterized by the independent and autonomous production of aldosterone, which contributes to the genesis of arterial hypertension and cardiovascular diseases. The case of a 57-year-old woman with a diagnosis of arterial hypertension for 12 years has been in management with three antihypertensives without achieving goals and finding hypokalaemia during a hypertensive emergency episode. Only after this event are studies indicated for primary hyperaldosteronism which lead to confirmation by selective catheterization of adrenal veins of bilateral aldosterone secretion, with excellent espond to the management with spironolactone. The lack of knowledge of primary care physicians of how to suspect and initiate the approach of this pathology has generated that less than 1% of the real cases are diagnosed at some time. This case aims to socialize the diagnostic and therapeutic approach of this pathology
