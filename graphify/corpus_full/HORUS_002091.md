# Proposal for the improvement of sustainability in its environmental, social and economic dimensions in the estates of 12 villages in the municipality of Vélez, Santander

The peasants themselves, in their daily work, develop activities and practices framed, in the agricultural and forestry sector, which provide them with an economic income for their livelihood and that of their family. These activities have developed from time immemorable, following the knowledge, guidelines and methodologies that the ancestors applied, that is, the knowledges went from generation to generation, to the present; however, the natural sciences from the different studies carried out, have managed to propose concepts such as that of the Sustainability, which cover a variety of topics that allow to understand how the smallest of the actions from the daily, they affect directly the balance between the environmental, the social and the economic. In this research, we have followed a mixed methodology, where a tool of own construction was applied and that follows guidelines of the qualitative and quantitative research, applied to the studies and the rural ones, is to the measurement of the process of transition to the sustainability, which allowed to observe very interesting results. territory is the most affected or diminished and the least contributes to the transition processes to sustainability in the search for balance between dimensions, since it was found that the farms of the sample do not have variety of species that contribute to the natural ecosystems; instead, it is the management of both organic and inorganic residues that contributes the most to the processes and development of the farms, since in the various climates of the municipality these residues are used for feeding the larger and smaller livestock species and the inorganic ones are reused, or are incinerated . Farmers, in their daily work, developed activities and practices framework in agriculture and forestry, which provides them with an economic income for their livinglihood and that of their generations, that is the result of their activity to develop since they are immemorial, following the knowledge, guidelines and methodologies that the consequences apply from generation, that is the only part of their knowledge, that is the territory is the most affected or diminished and that least contributes to the sustainability process. applied, following qualitative and quantitative research guidelines, applied to the farmers who were part of the sample, for a total of three-six (36) farm, in 12 villages of the municipality of Vélez Santander. One of the most significant results of the study is that the economic dimension is the one in which the least sustainable practices can be developed, taking into account that the income is insufficient to achieve significant progress in the development of actions that contribute to the balance between dimensions. In addition, it is identified that it is the social dimension in which the farmers are responsible for more practices and actions that contribute to the process of transition to sustainability
