# MATHEMATIC MODELATION OF INSULINOTHARAPY FOR THE CONTROL OF DIABETS

Diabetes is characterized by high levels of glucose in the blood due to low or lack of insulin production by the pancreas. The main treatment consists of the administration of artificial insulin, but this usually depends on the manipulation of the patient, which can lead to errors in the calculations of the insulin dose and affect the quality of life of the patient. In order to automate insulin therapy in diabetic patients, we propose a new model of optimal control for the continuous administration of insulin, considering specific characteristics such as: type of diabetes, eating habits and the role of the liver in maintaining glucose levels. The existence and uniqueness of the dynamic system solution describing the interaction between glucose and insulin is studied, as is demonstrated by the existence of optimal control. A numerical approach to control is then made by solving the associated nonlinear optimization problem. The potential of the proposal is evidenced in the study of three different scenarios combining type of diabetes that the patient suffers and feeding style
