# Bogota has syncope: adoption and significance of ska in the city

The ska is a music rooted to its place of origin: Jamaica. However, it is also a music that has acquired a unique personality in Bogotá, through the implementation of new interactions, circuits, symbols and consumption forms. Its sound transmits joy and identifies the members of the scene with a series of meanings related to the local environment, in dialogue with common struggles to other contexts. This research aims to contribute to the analysis of popular music and to how it relates to a whole community of people that attributes meanings to music and works to enable it to be consumed, referred here as a scene/circuit: bands, followers, vendors, designers of clothing, discs, graphic illustrators, etc. As a result, this study identified the sound and scenic dynamics that make ska a social practice that pursues not only ideals, but moves by a circuit that responds to the possibilities and needs of interaction, with the Bogotana scene a glocal character and a commercial response located in a musical spot of the underground (Text taken from the funte) Ska is a music attach to its place of org itself: s, graphic designers, etc. As a result, this study identified sound and scene dynamics that make ska a social practice that suits ideal, and moves through a circuit that relates to the possibilities and requirements for modern interaction, which gives the scene in bogota a glocal character and a located commercial response in the stain of underground music
