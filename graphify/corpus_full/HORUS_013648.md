# Low-cost BIM implementation protocol for the use and use of companies categorized as SMEs dedicated to the design of architecture projects

Over the last years, the BIM methodology (Building information modeling) has revolutionized the AIC industry (Architecture, Engineering and Construction) in the way in which the use of new computer technologies in favour of systematization in the development processes of a project is included. It is the large companies in developed countries that pioneered the adoption of this methodology; however, the affordability of BIM within small and medium-sized enterprises (PyME) has been limited due to multiple factors that hinder its easy adoption. This work seeks to establish a protocol of implementation of BIM methodology at low cost aimed at SMEs dedicated to the design of architectural projects, protocol that is the product of a structuring of an art state that collects definitions, trends, regulations and standards that revolve around the relationship between BIM and SMEs. In addition, a methodological proposal for implementation is presented where a new company, roles and tools are established, and a workflow of work; proposal that finally presents relevant conclusions and recommendations. .
