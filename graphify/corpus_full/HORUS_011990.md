# Total phenols and antioxidant capacity of Moringa oleifera leaf extract in three phenological states

Moringa oleifera is a plant species with multiple applications and important characteristics of medicinal and pharmacological use, contains compounds that confer antioxidant, anticancer and antiinflammatory activity, among other properties. The leaves are the most used structure and with the highest concentration of bioactive compounds that can be partially isolated in extracts for later uses. However, in Colombia the studies related to the species are scarce, which generated the need to evaluate the phenolic compounds and the antioxidant capacity of the leaves of this plant in three phenological states (J: young, M: mature, A: adult). The extracts were evaluated in terms of total content of phenols (CTF), flavonoids (CTFl), catechines (CTC) and the antioxidant capacity (DPPH, FRAP and ABSS).
