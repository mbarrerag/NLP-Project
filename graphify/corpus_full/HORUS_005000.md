# COBROAD OF WAY STATION AS A FUENT FOR THE FINANCING OF MASITIVE TRANSPORT.

A latent drawback in terms of the sustainability of public transport systems in Colombia is based on the sources of financing established by law, among which are the charges for on-road parking, for which a case study in the city of Santiago de Cali is structured a technical and financial model that allows to assess how effective this source of financing can be. For this purpose and based on the collection for three months of primary information by implementing a pilot test in the Regulatory Parking Area (ZER) San Antonio - Peñón, a multivariate regression technical model is designed and developed for the calculation of parking demand by KER, involving economic, transport and urban variables, and provides predictions for the analysis of a financial model for the economic use of KERs, based on the tariff and some assumptions of macroeconomic variables that includes capital and operational costs (CAPEX and OPEX), which, for the particular case of the city of Santiago de Cali, reflects distopia when considering this source of financing as a relief for the sustainability of the city's mass transport system
