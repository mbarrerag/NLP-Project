# --Strategies for the development of ethical competencies of the academic community of the Faculty of Mines, UNAL _x0096_ Medellín

In line with the approach still prevalent in most engineering faculties in the world, the curriculum profile of the Faculty of Mines is more oriented to the development of technical than humanistic, social and environmental competencies. Only recently, based on the declaration of the new graduation profile of the Faculty, was the institutional motto Work and Rightness reinterpreted as the commitment _x0093_with the training of dedicated and responsible engineers and engineers, who exercise their profession according to academic and technical standards of quality, aware that engineering constitutes a powerful factor of transformation of human communities and the natural environment_x0094_. Thus, in order to achieve a satisfactory balance between the competencies of the discharge profile, we believe it is appropriate to begin to develop a strategic vision of the problem posed by that declaration. This project seeks, through a methodology of strategic analysis, to make available to the university community of the Faculty of Mines a proposal to achieve the academic objective of ethical training of engineers, so that, in the personal and professional sphere, they are able to consciously and responsibly address the present and future problems of a planet subject to powerful economic transformations and to environmental transformations
