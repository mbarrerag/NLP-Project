# Effect of IGF2 on the DNA methylation panorama and the expression of mRNA associated with the HTR-8/SVneo cell line.

The process of placental implantation depends on a thorough regulation of the invasion of maternal deciduousness by the cellular subtype called extravellous trofoblast, the escape of the immune system of the mother and the remodeling of the local vasculature.These and other processes make the placental implantation significantly similar to the tumor invasion.The cancer cells owe their origin to a loss of the somatic cellular identity accompanied by the development of an aberrant phenotype that is reached as a result of the reactivation of embryonal development programs.It is believed that the loss of the cellular identity such as the reactivation of the development programs are due to a large extent to a loss of the genomic stability, which in turn depends on the panorama of methylation at the genomic level.It is known that the various extracellular stimuli are able to promote a remodeling of the laborous model of the somatic cells as well as the cellular transition line of the highly proliferative and invasive phenotype, which is due both to the stochastic activation of oncogenes, as to a response of the pulsupular and the phósuperal tumor migratory and invasive cell line, and these changes occur in parallel, and probably as a result of alterations at the epigenetic and transcriptional level
