# Indicators of integration of palliative care in heart failure: a Delphi study

Abstract Introduction: Heart failure, disease with high prevalence and mortality, presents low incorporation of palliative care (CP) despite its need. In Colombia, the lack of indicators to monitor the integration of CP in the treatment of heart failure makes it difficult to implement effective strategies. It is proposed to use a classification model to monitor the integration of CP in the Colombian health system. To agree on a set of indicators evaluated for their relevance and feasibility to monitor the integration of palliative care in heart failure in the Colombian health system. A Delphi consensus process was carried out in two rounds, including experts from different regions of the country. An online survey was used on nine indicators related to the integration of palliative care in cardiac failure in the Colombian health system. It was assessed in each indicator its relevance and feasibility. 17 experts from all regions of Colombia participated, 76% were physicians and 24% were nursing professionals; in its great majority (65%) belonged to scientific societies such as the Colombian Association of Palliative Care.
