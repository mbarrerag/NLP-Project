# The strategic policies of change in second generation progressisms and their impact on the popular movement. The cases of Chile and Colombia (2022-2025).

The first of December 2018 took possession as president of the Mexican republic Andrés Manuel López Obrador (AMLO) opening from the largest economy of the region a second cycle for Latin American progressism. In fact, after this victory come a series of electoral victories throughout and wide of the region: Alberto Fernández en Argetina (2019) Luis Arce en Bolivia (2020), Pedro Castillo en Perú (2021), Xiomara Castro en Honduras (2022), Gabriel Boric en Chile (2022), Gustavo Petro en Colombia (2022), Luiz Inácio _x0093_Lula_x0094_ Da Silva en Brasil (2023), and Bernardo Arévalo en Guatemala (2024). The comparison will be carried out through the methodology of processes and mechanisms where the episodes will be the periods of government from 2025 to 2025.
