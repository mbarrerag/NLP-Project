# Dynamic modelling of track-vehicle interaction in railway systems: effect of elastic properties of the track and structure

In the Medellin Metro, as well as in various railway systems around the world, the presence of defects has generated a high cost associated with the maintenance and replacement of components of the road. This problem has been addressed from different methods in the literature and in this work multibody simulations were carried out to perform the analysis of the elastic properties of the track. In this work was carried out the modeling of the CAF train of the Medellin Metro. For this purpose the identification of the main components that affect the dynamic performance of the vehicle and its mechanical and geometric properties. A complete model was implemented that included the elastic characterisation of the components of the track, and a discrete model was proposed that allowed to separate the rigidity of the pad and the ballast. With this model was carried out the analysis of the elastic properties of the track in the dynamic performance of the vehicle. The model of the CAF vehicle was performed using the measurements made for the characterization, the frequency of the evalutives of the vehicle, the acceleration of the vehicle in the given area, the distribution of forces, among others. The main components that affect the dynamic performance of the vehicle and its mechanical and geometric properties are identified, for the implementation of track model the elastic character of the track components is performed, and a separate model that allows separation the status of the branch and the ballistic property is proposed. With this, the analysis of how the elastic proposal address the dynamic performance of the vehicle is proposed. The CAF train technique was modeled and validated through different methods included previous reviews, the analysis of how the elastic address address in a specific area of the system, force distribution, among other.
