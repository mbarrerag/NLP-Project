# Curriculum Proposal for Computer Science for Primary Cycles I and II.

This research project aimed at designing a curriculum proposal for the Computer Sciences for cycles I and II of the Manuela Beltrán IED College. The main interest in teaching these disciplines from the first grades of school education is the need generated by the incursion of technologies into everyday life and the ease with which the youngest students adapt to them. Thus, from a qualitative research approach, a research process is developed that had as a strategy documentary research, which allowed to draw the path to follow traces of experiences and curricular proposals at local, regional and international levels, to contrast, analyze and evaluate them in order to find common elements on learning objectives and contents that allow to answer questions for what to teach? what to teach? and how to teach? In addition, the research was nurtured with the analysis of the experiences that the researcher had with students from 3rd and 4th grade of primary school in 2021, as well as with the analysis of surveys to families and teachers of the institution on the conception of computer sciences.
