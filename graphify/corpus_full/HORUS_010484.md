# DYNAMICS OF VACUUM IMPREGATION OF IRACA PALM BUDS

Although the buds of the iraca palm can be used as a source for human feeding, most of the information available is for the production of handicrafts. The response to the vacuum impregnation (IV) of fresh iraca palm buds (CFPI) was evaluated, with an isotonic solution of NaCl (0.6%). The response surface methodology was used, with a composite central design (a=1), considering the following independent variables: the diameter of the buds (10-15 mm), the time in the vacuum phase T1 (3-5 minutes) at vacuum pressure (4.1 kPa) and the time in the local atmospheric pressure stage (85.32 kPa), T2 (3-5 min). The dependent variables taken into account were volumetric fraction of impregnation in the vacuum stage (X1), final volumetric deformation (g), final volumetric fraction (X) and effective porosity (Ee).
