# Socio-sanitary responses from an indigenous community of Zio Bain del Putumayo (Colombia) to Covid-19

The experience of an indigenous community Ziomic Bain del Putumayo vs. Covid-19 was addressed to identify its forms of understanding and coping with the health emergency, the socio-sanitary responses, cultural knowledge, organization and relationship of health with the territory and environment. Semi-structured interviews of key actors were conducted for their practice and knowledge in the context of the pandemic, and participant observation of organizational processes in health was carried out. It was found that the effects generated by the pandemic in the community had a syndemic behavior, due to the convergence of precarious conditions of life, the threat of the new virus and the negative economic, social, political and cultural impacts, caused by the restrictions imposed officially as mechanisms to contain and mitigate the pandemic. In the community, socio-anitic responses of self-care were generated that had as a basis an active and integral view of health, based on the traditional management of spirituality, with the fluidity of the Yagé plant and the recognition of the Yai Bain as traditional authorities that guide the Good Living.
