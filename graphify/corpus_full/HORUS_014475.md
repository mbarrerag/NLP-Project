# Gamification as a mediation for the development of scientific skills and learning basic concepts of chemistry focused on the preparation of the test know 11.

The learning of chemistry has generally been considered as a problem for students of secondary basic, since they classify it as a very difficult or very boring matter. On the other hand, we have that it is one of the components evaluated in the test to know eleven and therefore it is important that as teachers we think of other alternatives to prepare students to face such examination, which has concepts and competences directly associated with chemistry. The development of this work, proposes to teach the basic concepts of chemistry to the students of the French Lyceum of Pereira, through the creation and implementation of a series of games, which will allow a better appropriation of the fundamental scientific competences for the evaluation 11, besides encouraging their critical ability, since the preparation of students goes beyond the memorization of formulas and concepts, it is more oriented to the strengthening of the competences. The above, it can be emphasized that the gamification is a different strategy, since the students can change the perspective of learning chemistry, because as they carry out the ludic activities in class, they can realize that they can learn by playing, which is totally out of traditional teaching
