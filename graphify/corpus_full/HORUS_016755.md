# GEOMECANICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF ACCESSABLE ROCO MACIZES OF THE CHILES VOLCAN (NARIÑO) AND ROCK MACIZON PARAMETERS.

The Volcán Chiles is an active stratvoltrican in the state of rest of the Cordillera Occidental, located in the collombo-Ecuadorian border within the southern volcanic segment of Colombia. The volcano has been constructed mainly by accumulation flows of andesítico-dacitic lava, and was affected by a large lateral collapse in the northern flank, which generated a subsequent avalanche of volcanic debris. Without excluding the magmatic activity, this collapse could have been triggered by various factors that affected the stability of the volcanic building. The geomechanical characterization of the rocks that constitute the Volcán Chiles was performed in five outcrops located in the southern flank applying, as the case may be, the System of Classification of the Macizo Rocoso (Rock Mass Rating RMR) or the index of Geological Resistance (Geological Strength Index GSI). In addition, these values obtained in the field were complemented with the results of other geomecan studies carried out in the area, which include the eastern and northern flanks of the volcano.
