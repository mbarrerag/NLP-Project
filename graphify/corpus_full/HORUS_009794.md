# EFFECT OF CLIMATE VARIABILITY ASSOCIATED THE SUR (ENOS) ASSISTANCE CHILD ON THE RANGE RESTAURATION TRAYECTORIES OF THE GREAT SCIENAGE OF SAINT MARTA

The hydrological rehabilitation seeks to generate adequate conditions of the soil regulators to promote the passive recovery of mangroves. However, climate variability can affect the fulfilment of rehabilitation goals. In this study long-term observations were used to determine the effects of climate variability associated with El Niño Southern Oscillation (ENOS) and hydrological rehabilitation in recruitment and mangrove coverage changes in the Santa Marta Grande Swamp complex. The hydrological rehabilitation and ENOS together explained 89%, 90% and 70% of the changes in mangrove coverage and the interstitial salinity in basin and river mangroves respectively. The climatic variability ENOS also explained the rates of change of the interstitial salinity in a reference basin mangrove (58%).The main measures of hydrological rehabilitation increased the coverage of mangrove trees during El Niño, while during La Niña the recovery of mangroves was delayed. In contrast, harmful hydrological rehabilitation measures increased the salinity during both phases of the ENSO.
