# THE MARIAN DEVOTION VIEW FOR LITERATURA: THE CASE OF OUR SIR OF THE ROSARIUM OF CHIQUINQUIRA. COLOMBIA IN THE XIXth CENTURY

The article aims to show how in the literature of the nineteenth century various visions were expressed about Marian devotion, particularly of the Virgen de Chiquinquirá. For this purpose, a literary corpus composed of more than half a hundred travel stories produced by both foreigners and Colombians, and more than 100 prose works, especially of the Costumbristas, were reviewed. In the literature consulted, general data on Marian devotion in the country were observed, as well as specific information on the devotion to the Virgen de Chiquinquirá, such as the history of the painting, the origin of devotion and practices such as pilgrimages. But also, and that is one of the relevant results, visions on devotion that fit with ways of seeing the wider world, among others, with readings on the Spanish colonial past, the conflict between a liberal modernity against traditions considered superstitious and questioning the religious as a Tara that conflicted with progress and civilization
