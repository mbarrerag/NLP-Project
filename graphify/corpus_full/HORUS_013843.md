# Bio-processing of agro-industrial waste to generate value-added products

Vinazas are a liquid by-product of the ethanol synthesis process derived from the distillation of cane juice. These vinazas are highly polluting organic residues due to their high level of organic matter, nutrients and biological weight. Their elimination and treatment are crucial to prevent harmful environmental effects on soil, water and air. Thus, research into the alternative use and remediation of vinazas is of the utmost importance, thus, the adaptation and growth of different microalgal microorganisms and bacterial cultures in means with vinazas, could reduce to a high degree the problem of contamination caused by the vinazas and could in turn generate products of high added value. The re-incorporation of the residues into the productive chain through the use of biological resources, contributes greatly to the understanding of the decontamination capacity of the microorganisms and their potential reach
