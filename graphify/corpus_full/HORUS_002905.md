# MG/AL AND CU-MG/AL OXIDS AS CATALIZERS FOR THE OBTENTION OF 1- BUTANOL TO ETANOL

The current production of 1-butanol is linked to the use of petroleum derivatives or to the ABE route [1]; however, in view of the need to reduce the consumption of fossil fuels and, due to the disadvantages associated with the acetone-butanol-ethanol route [1,2], the importance of seeking new alternatives for obtaining butanol is becoming ever larger. In this sense, the conversion of ethanol to 1-butanol using mixed oxides was studied experimentally and computationally. The catalytic activity of mixed oxides Mg/Al and Cu-Mg/Al was evaluated at temperatures of 250 and 300 °C. Thus, the presence of Al3+ in mixed oxide Mg/Al generates acid sites selectivity at 1-butanol of 11.7%, at 300 °C. On the other hand, the substitution of Cu2+ of ethanol in a structural way generates a conversion of 91% and a selectivity towards 1-butanol of 7.8% at 300 °C; while, at 250 °C this solid presents a better selectivity of the acid, the result (16.5 %).
