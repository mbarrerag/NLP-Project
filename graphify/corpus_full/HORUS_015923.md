# ECONOMIC REACTIVENESS OF MYPES INTEGRATION AND DEVELOPMENT OF NEW PERSONAL PROTECTION PRODUCTS IN PANDEMIA TIMES

A plan was proposed for the de-sufficiency of personal protection elements EPP for informal vendors at risk to the COVID-19, addressing the lack of biosafety protocols for use and production. Through a design-based process: 1. A PPF facial protection screen was developed, registered before the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce as industrial design. 2. Biosecurity protocols were designed, through visual scenarios based on the analysis of vendors' activities. 3. Biosafety and quality protocols were developed, for the reactivation of Mypes; seven were trained and three mypes were selected for the production of 200 EPP kits. 4. A campaign was designed to disseminate biosecurity protocols. 5. 200 KEPP were donated to the NGO El País Primero, who handed them over to beneficiaries and trained vendors in protocols. 6. This initiative prompted a spin-off, accompanied by Mentor UNAL. It is concluded that: I. Collaboration allowed to articulate knowledge around a common objective. II.
