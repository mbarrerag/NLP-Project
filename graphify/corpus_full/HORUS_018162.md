# ANALYSIS OF THE CURRENT MAINTENANCE SITUATION OF THE COLOMBIA S.A.S. DRINKS COMPANY

Drinks de Colombia S.A.S, is a bottling company that has five production lines. However, the plant is still governed under corrective maintenance, which generates unexpected production stops and high maintenance costs; besides not having a defined lubrication plan. Therefore, an analysis is carried out to know the current situation of the company and the possibility of implementing a comprehensive maintenance plan
