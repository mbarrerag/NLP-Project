# RESIDENT ASSISTANCE CHANGES: ORIGIN, EVOLUTION AND FUTURE

The purpose of this conference is to provide an introductory overview of resilient supply chains through a brief description of the origin, evolution, state of art and some possible future research work, which will enable us to motivate the study of this emerging line of research for the strengthening of supply chain management in the new generations of engineers
