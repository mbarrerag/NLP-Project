# Experiences of the therapeutic relationship in psychotherapy performed in different physical spaces: a qualitative approach

This research aimed to describe how the consultants perceive the impact of the space in which psychotherapy is performed on the therapeutic relationship, especially in verbal and non-verbal communication, in psychotherapy carried out in different physical spaces (consultatory, open air or in the spaces in which they took the virtual consultation). To this end, a qualitative methodology was used through semi-structured interviews to nine consultants of the Psychological Care Service of the National University of Colombia, taking as a starting point four categories for analysis (verbal language, non-verbal language, meaning of the space and physical characteristics of the space). It was concluded that the privacy affected the expression of oral content and the development of other aspects of the therapeutic relationship such as trust.
