# CLINICAL TRYING OF THE INSURANCE CARE IN THE CARDIAC ISCHEMIA

In Colombia, during the last three decades, the ischemic heart disease is the first cause of mortality. Nursing staff as a member of the multidisciplinary team is an important actor in the timely care of the patient with Acute Infarction of Miocardio, since it performs direct interventions from the first hours of the event until his discharge. However, the literature has reported that nursing care during the healthcare process is segmented by each care service and there are few that identify the interventions performed by nursing staff that impact on the health results. A quantitative study of descriptive observational type of retrospective type. Data were collected from 98 clinical histories of adults attended in a health institution specialized in cardiovascular care of the city of Bogotá, diagnosed with IAM during the period from July to December 2019, by means of a survey of the sociodemographic and clinical characterization elaborated by the researchers. A descriptive statistical analysis was performed with central trend measures in the SPSS version 25. The project counted with the ethical approval of the Shaio clinical foundation.
