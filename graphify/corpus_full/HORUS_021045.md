# High performance concretes HPC

The high-performance concrete HPC is one whose characteristic compressive strength is greater than 50 MPa. HPC requires special dosing and a specific mixing process. HPCs, thanks to their good mechanical characteristics, could be precast, connected with bolts and used in construction of buildings and bridges
