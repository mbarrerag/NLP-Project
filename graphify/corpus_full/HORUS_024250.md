# Single-pulse antenna design for applications of

This paper presents an RFID reading system for the detection and determination of the direction of motion of radio frequency identification tags (RFID) installed in vehicles. This system focuses on the adaptation of secondary radar technology, specifically, the application of monopulse techniques used in SSR (Second-hand Surveillance Radar) to the reading antenna. The monopulse antenna is integrated into passive RFID readers operating in the 915 MHz (Industry, Scientific and Medical) ISM band for Intelligent Transport Systems (ITS) applications. The system provides the direction of motion of an object with an installed RFID label, as well as the reading of the unique code of the label, through a single monopulse antenna and RFID interrogator and a phase detection system that filters signals received by the antenna and identifies the polarity change in signal voltage, allowing the determination of the direction of motion of the label.
