# DIALOGUES BETWEEN THEORY AND PRACTICE FOR THE TEACHING OF THE ARGUMENTARY TEXT IN HIGHER EDUCATION

The objective of this article is to publicize the dialogues between theories on academic writing processes, specifically of argumentative texts, and the reflections of teachers and tutors in relation to their own action in the Level-Level-Level-Lector courses of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters. The work is framed in the research-action in education and uses the dialogue between theory and practice through reflection in action and on pedagogical action. Discussion sessions between teachers, tutors and a session between teachers and tutors are analyzed. The result of the dialogues is that some strategies are used to develop writing as a process, to give rise to writing as a contextualized discursive activity and to enhance the role of argumentation in critical thinking processes. As a conclusion, we have found that to promote the understanding of what it means to elaborate a narrative text can be used strategies as well as to contrast the structure and purposes of argumentative texts with other types of texts (narrative, expositive), to read and discuss among peers, to confront voices (including one's own), to create publication spaces, to promote the use of different sources and to motivate writing from contemporary themes in relation to the country
