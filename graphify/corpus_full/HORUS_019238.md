# Automated etiological classification of macular oedema using Deep Learning strategy applied to images acquired by Macula OCT.

To develop a computational method based on the strategy of deep learning (‘Deep Learning’, DL) to perform an automated etiological diagnosis of macular edema (EM) based on the assessment of images acquired by optical coherence tomography (OCT), classifying them between diabetic macular edema (EMD), exudative macular degeneration (DMRE(e)) and secondary to vascular occlusions (EM 2a OVR). Development of artificial intelligence algorithm (IA) for the automated classification of retinial diseases using retrospective data. Participants: 1343 images of macula OCT were included, obtained from the database of patients attended by a Clinic of Oftalmology and free access databases, which were used to train and test an artificial intelligence model to detect MS and its etiological diagnosis. The images of OCT were marked and segmented manually by two expert ophthalmologists, labeling biomarkers (BMs) and classified them as a function of the corresponding disease (EMD, DMRE(e) and EMCD(e).. The sensitivity and specificity values for the classification of the three retinal exudative diseases and for normal images were comparable with the performance of an expert ophthalmologist, as reported in the literature. The proposed automated model with a DL approach can identify normal images with macular oedema from scans acquired by OCT of macula, and allows to classify its cause among the three main retinal exudative diseases with high accuracy and reliability
