# Physicochemical characterization of monomeric and dimeric synthetic peptides derived from Lactoferricin Bovine with proven anticancer activity.

According to WHO cancer is one of the main causes of death at the global level, with 10 million deaths in 2020. The therapies for the management against cancer are aggressive, not selective and produce adverse effects that affect the quality of life of the patient. This has generated the need to develop new treatments against cancer that are safe and effective. Currently the peptides have emerged as a therapeutic alternative for the treatment of diseases, however, because each peptide has unique physical and chemical properties, makes its analytical characterization represent great challenges, and this makes it a key stage in the development of medicines. In this work, analytical methodologies were developed and implemented following the recommendations of the USP and European pharmacopoeias for characterizing peptides using as an experimental model the anticancer peptides: RWQWRWQWR and (RRQWRFKKLG)2-K-Ahx, which are considered promising for the development of a broad-spectrum anticancer medicine.
