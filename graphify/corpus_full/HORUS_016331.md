# ADVICE ON THE READING OF SUBTITLES, PERCEPTION AND COMPRESSION OF AUDIOVISUAL PIECE BY OCCULAR MONITORING TECHNOLOGY

The pandemic by SARS-CoV-2 and the measures implemented for the reduction of contagion generated an increase in digital consumption worldwide in different languages, especially in virtual experiences of learning and entertainment, such as streaming platforms where there is high content of videos with subtitles that are seen by people of different nationalities, so it is essential to analyze how people perceive the information in this type of platforms and the use and understanding given to the subtitles present in this type of videos. This research used a mixed approach, whose objective was to analyze the perception and reading understanding of subtitles in audiovisual pieces, during the projection of a documentary with the help of the software of ocular tracking Sticky and a qualitative questionnaire. 64 people from 18 to 59 years of age participated, who processed the informed consent and after watching the videos, answered a validation questionnaire. Statistical analysis of the parameters of ocular tracking and information contained in the questionnaire was carried out. It is concluded that the subtitles in Spanish as mother tongue (L1) are a support for the competent and non-competent people (L2).
