# Classification of fungi by machine learning

This research aims to apply machine learning and data mining to identify whether different kinds of fungi (new or not) are beneficial (compensable) or harmful to humans. The methodology is based on the classification algorithm J48 (Weka) and the online database of fungi is used. The dependent variable (class) has two states (comestible or poisonous). 17 independent variables are considered, these include habitat, season of the year and morphological characteristics such as hat, bruises or bleeding, gills, stem and ring. As a result, it is found, with an effectiveness of 99.76%, that the most influential variables are: hat diameter, hat surface, hat color, stem thickness, stem surface and stem color. In conclusion, the methodology developed here allows identifying whether a fungus is poisonous or not with a 99.76% effectiveness
