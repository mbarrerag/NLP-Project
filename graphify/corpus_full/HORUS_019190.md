# COLOMBIAN BIOCULTURE: ANTROPOLOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVES

The Department of Anthropology of the National University of Colombia, founded in 1966, has already accumulated its history of fifty years. In its transfer it has welcomed academics and academics from the most varied profiles and areas of research. Its students have undoubtedly contributed to building the diverse knowledge that has emanated from it. This great plurality led to the adoption of this title for the work Colombian biocultural diversity: anthropological perspectives. Here are twelve chapters in which only 19 of the actors who have passed through the Department of Anthropology, from its shores, reflect the journeys and advances in the various areas that make up the discipline
