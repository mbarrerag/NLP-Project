# QUIMIOTERAPIA AND IMMUNOTHERAPY IN A TUMOR TRANSMISIBLE CANIN VENER (TVTC) FOR EXTRAGENIT PRESENTATION.

Anamnesulitis: male canine neutered with neoplasia in the face in the region of the zygomatic arch that appeared after an avulsive wound generated by another canine. Diagnostic aids: cytopathological study, histopathology and immunohistochemistry for interleukin six (IL6) before initiating treatment; haematological follow-up and evaluation of the neutrophil-lymphocyte ratio weekly. Cytologic and clinical findings: cytology of mass corresponded to transmissible venereal tumour of plasmocytoid type, T:3/N:0/M:0 extragenital presentation, concomitant with hemoparasitic disease Ehrlichia spp, neutrophil-lymphocytic relationship (RNL): 9,47. Current treatment approach: the patient receives 10 mg/kg/24h during 21days of doxycycline and immunomodulation with hexosamine of E. coli and Propinebacterium granulosum.
