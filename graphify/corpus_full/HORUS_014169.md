# Family Aquaponia as an alternative to sustainable production: a circular strategy to facilitate the energy transition in the municipality of Yotoco, Valle del Cauca.

The project _x0093_Acuaponia familia as an alternative to sustainable production: a circular strategy to facilitate the energy transition in the municipality of Yotoco, Valle del Cauca_x0094_, through the identification of community practices and the generation of sustainable productive alternatives, has its origin in the interest of a family of small producers who have found in the reuse of waste, the use of water and the combination of production systems, an alternative that allows to recirculate and to make the maximum use of the resources and ecosystem services available in their territory. However, this interest has not only been expressed in the interest of finding more profitable productive systems, but has incorporated in their exercise, the possibility to share and serve as a reference to their community of producer neighbours and include in their production alternatives the respect, care and conservation of the ecological resources and services that we obtain from them. Therefore, it has been defined as the general objective of the project the Co-create and implement a pilot system of production circular acuapnica as an alternative for small families producing in the municipality of Yotoco, generating a new productive alternative, that allows the care of the ecological environment, and mitigates. The role of associativeness and in this, identifying the valuable contribution that each producer can make, as well as the benefits that can be aspired to, is an exercise that requires dedicated work where perspectives the aquaponic can flourish as the benefit of the communities, the health and the environment of the region that impacts. This proposal, involves the sharing and circulation of comprehensive knowledge, from different perspectives and disciplinary areas, to contribute to the union of each region with the nation, recognition of wealth and biological and cultural diversity. In this sense it is proposed to generate productive referents, which through the Field Schools is sought to systematize and make visible the convergence and the encounter of knowledge (community, academia, private and public), good practices, better production methods and training processes, allow to transfer knowledge to the new generations, which in turn constitutes the main tool to promote the social integration of the community and that allows the continuity in the development of sustainable alternative practices that are expected to derive from the project
