# MUTUALIST MARKETING AS EMERGENCING STRATEGY. CASE OF THE PAPA DURING THE COVID PANDEMIA IN 2019.

This document presents the main drawbacks of the potato chain in Colombia during the pandemic and analyses one of the solutions applied. It studies the mechanisms of collaboration in marketing from the biological metaphor, as an emerging strategy to solve the problems of distribution of the potato chain. It highlights the emergence of the virus as a deconstructive event, in which, in addition to deliberate strategies, another emerging one is required to solve the economic and health problems arising from the pandemic situation. Finally, there are the emerging elements that favored this situation and some factors are proposed to strengthen mutual marketing as an alternative, in a deconstructor event
