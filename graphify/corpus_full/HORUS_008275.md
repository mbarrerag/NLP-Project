# Impact of COVID-19 on the clinical practice of phonoaudiology in Colombia

Abstract Introduction: Few studies describe how the pandemic by COVID-19 has affected the clinical practices of professionals in phonoaudiology, and there is even less knowledge of this impact in Latin American countries, such as Colombia. To describe the impact of the pandemic by COVID-19 on the exercise of clinical phonoaudiology, as well as the adaptations implemented to guarantee health care in Colombia. Cross-sectional study in which an online questionnaire was applied between January and February 2021 to clinical phonoaudiologists with experience in the area. The questionnaire asked about sociodemographic variables and categories such as clinical professional exercise and impact of COVID-19 on clinical practice. The results were descriptively analyzed and Chi2 of independence was applied to find possible associations. 9% of professionals lost their work due to the pandemic, and the remaining total, the adoption of 14 % opted for telehealth. There are restrictions of intervention, mainly in the protection and speech, as well as adaptations that seek to protect the health of the patient and professional.
