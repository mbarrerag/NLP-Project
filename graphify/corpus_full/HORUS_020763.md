# The commemorative space from the distance from everyday life: A project strategy for the installation of monuments

This final master's work aims to investigate a spatial configuration type to implant a monument so that its commemorative information, regardless of the characteristics of its content, can be presented to the viewer in a clear, paused, uninterrupted way. The argument presented throughout the research to reach this objective is the distance of the subject from the everyday world so that it can contemplate. To this, the research establishes a guide from a series of steps to follow, identified in five cases of study belonging to different periods and geographical places. This procedural guide develops from the exposition of the following steps: A. Identification of the cause of commemoration, B. Identification of the place of remembrance, C. Formation of the commemorative space. Within this last step are the following compositional parts defined by this research as conforming to the commemorative space: 1. Perimeter separator, 2. Alegamento, 3. Platform of approximation, 4. Borders, 5. Patio homogeno, 6. Cofre, 7. Solemn object. Finally, the research puts to test the proposed guide through a theoretical project implanted in the third millennium park of Bogotá, with reason of the construction.
