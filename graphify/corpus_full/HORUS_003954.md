# EXCRETES AND GUINE: taxONOMIC AND ECOLOGICAL ANALYSIS OF A CASE STUDY IN COLOMBIA

The vertebrate research is usually carried out with direct sampling involving the use of traps. Other indirect methods, such as trace observation, are little used, although easy, economical and with adequate interpretation offer valuable information. We propose a protocol of analysis of excreta and guano in order to guide researchers to obtain taxonomic and ecological information. This protocol includes details of sampling, photographic records, sample collection, information rescue, report of organoleptic characters, laboratory procedures, preservation, storage, assembly and conservation. We detail information to taxonomically determine the excreta and correctly interpret the information. We present a case study in Colombia with the details of each type of analysis. In conclusion, we highlight the importance of standardized protocols for the analysis of this type of sampling as a biological witness or voucher to obtain information on ecology, etology, habitat, trophic dynamics and age of certain species
