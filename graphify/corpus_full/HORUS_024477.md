# Cities and Biodiversity: Perception of Ecosystem Services at Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters

The study of ecosystem services (SE) in urban environments has had a great growth in recent years, due to its relationship with the well-being of citizens and the way they perceive and value nature. In this study, the perception of 674 people belonging to the National University of Colombia, Bogotá headquarters on urban ecosystem services (SEU) provided by the campus was analyzed, using semi-structured surveys. The responses were compared at the level of faculties and interest groups (students, teachers and administrative staff), identifying spatially the important areas on offer by SEU. The perception of the respondents between two scenarios was compared: prior to being informed about the concept of the SEU (a priori) and subsequent to this (a posteriori). 77.23% of the respondents did not know the term “ecosystemic service”; however, they recognized the benefits of nature in their daily living. Cultural and regulatory services received the highest evaluations (priorial recreation, physical and mental health, air quality) associated with the urban or wooded areas within the campus.
