# DENAMIC URBAN DENAMICS ASSOCIATED TO THE DEVELOPMENT OF NATIONAL CHARGE CONCESSIONS: THE CASE OF COLOMBIA

As part of the development of road concessions contracts in Colombia, variants have been built and put into operation to avoid the congestions that could be generated over some urban areas. In order to assess the dynamics of land use that were induced from these highways, the processes that allow to revise the 58 concession contracts signed since 1994 are presented in order to determine the variants implemented. Once identified, the criteria defined for selecting the area to be reviewed in each highway, which allowed to analyze the changes in land use. 38 variants located in 39 municipalities were demonstrated, of which 29 are in operation and on which areas of analysis were defined based on the available photographs. As special pilot cases, the variants associated with the urban helmets of Madrid, Cota and Cajicá were analyzed to which an assessment of the dynamics of land use was carried out; the results show that there was a significant increase in new residential and industrial land use in the areas adjacent to these highways, changing the conditions initially foreseen in the design stage
