# BIBLETICS HAVE A VALUE: A LOOK FROM THE SOCIAL RETURN OF INVESTMENT

This book describes the application of the Social Return to Investment (SROI) method, which is based on the assumption that, if the public library does not exist, citizens should search the markets for the services they offer and therefore assume a payment for their enjoyment. Thus, the investments made by the State for citizens to freely access libraries, is also translated into the perceptions of savings that they manifest, since it allows them to allocate their resources for the purchase of other goods and services, which are expected to improve their conditions and quality of life
