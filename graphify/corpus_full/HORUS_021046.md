# Borders in (de)construction

This project aims to study through theoretical-practical research the boundaries that define our daily life and that are present in the body, in space, in territory, in culture, in language and the incidence that these have in our body language, our expressions and the way we communicate. Likewise, it seeks to recognize the different mechanisms that we use to construct and deconstruct these limits and understand how these borders structure our life, our identity and our interaction with the environment. Through the use of different tools, both corporal, plastic, sound and technological, different situations will be proposed that derive from a no place, from blurred, blurred borders, which allow to generate different interactions between materials, performers and the public
