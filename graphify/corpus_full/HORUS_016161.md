# Post-graduate training and hegemônic modes of public policy analysis

ABSTRACT Objective Characterize the modes of teaching and researching public policies at the postgraduate level in Colombia. Methods Qualitative research of the type of documentary analysis. Initially, a general characterization of authors, theoretical approaches employed, thematic of interest, among others. At a second moment, and from the content of the academic programs of the postgraduates from where the analyzed thesis emerged, were identified central signifiers of the curricular discourse. Results 313 theses and 8 graduate programs in public policies were analyzed. It is emphasized a strong preference for the use of postpositivist approaches and problem-solving strategies; weak questionings to structural determinants of oppression, exclusion and exploitation, and maintenance of dominant relations in decision-making between political science/administrative researchers and subjects with other knowledge. Conclusions The findings of the study problematize the field of Public Policy Analysis, its epistemological positions and predominant methods, as well as the role of the analyst, inviting the reader to reflect on the need to engage in alternative critical approaches
