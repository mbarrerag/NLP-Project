# INFORMAL URBANIZATION, CONSTITUTIONAL SIGNATURE AND LEGAL ACTIVITY IN COLOMBIA

The article presents an analysis of the legal tension caused by informal urbanization in relation to property and housing rights in Colombia. In this dispute, the way Colombian constitutional jurisdiction has responded to the phenomenon is described. It is argued that the judge faces a difficult situation in these events, due to the absence of an effective public housing policy for the lower income sectors. This has led to judicial activism materialized in decisions that range from a strong intervention in the administrative functions of the government and the delay in the effective implementation of constitutional rights. In contrast, it seems that decisions that contribute to finding structural solutions to the problem are absent
