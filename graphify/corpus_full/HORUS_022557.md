# Some motivational factors in the learning of French language in the Universal Context

This research paper presents the perceptions of a group of university students about certain motivational factors that influence their learning of the French language. For this purpose, we write an opinion survey. This was completed by 315 students. The data were analyzed quantitatively. The analysis shows that a large part of the students appreciate the French language, study it to travel abroad and expand their job possibilities. They recognize that their teacher is not the center of the learning process. However, they consider that they should make more efforts to motivate them. For some, the attitude and behavior of classmates and the poor results obtained in the evaluations affect their learning. It is concluded that French teachers must create strategies to further motivate their students, develop a deep, autonomous learning and raise students' awareness of the learning techniques most appropriate for them
