# VOLTAGE CURVE TIME IN A C-PROCESS OF ANTI-ARTECI VORTIC PARES ANCIVALE

In this contribution, the electrical response of the creation-aniquilation of kinematic vortexes was studied in a superconductor film in the presence of a direct external current to a zero magnetic field. The film is in contact with different types of simulated materials through the deGennes extrapolation parameter; in the same time, it has a small metal region located at a distance from the center. We use the time-dependent Ginzburg-Landau theory to describe voltage variation as a function of time in a kinematic vortex creation-aniquilation process considering several positions of the metal defect and the deGennes parameter. Our research shows that critical currents, amplitude and frequency of oscillations in the curve depend strongly on the deGennes parameter and the defect position in the sample
