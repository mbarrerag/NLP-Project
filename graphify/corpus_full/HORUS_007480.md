# Experiences and discourses on the shared upbringing of children from five months to two years of age in Child Development Centres, Rionegro, Antioquia, 2022-2024

This research seeks to understand the affective, pedagogical and institutional dynamics that shape the shared upbringing of children between the five months and the two years in Child Development Centers (CDI) of Rionegro, Antioquia, from an interdisciplinary perspective that integrates contributions from psychoanalysis, sociology and pedagogy. To achieve this objective, a qualitative ethnographic approach was adopted, using research techniques such as semi-structured interviews, participant observation and focal groups. Eleven babies and their families participated in the research, three educational agents, two pedagogical assistants and three coordinators of the program. The theoretical framework is based on the contributions of the sociologist Gérard Neyrand with his critical contributions to the knowledge of the first childhood and the psychoanalytic theories of Wilfred Bion and Donald Meltzer, with special emphasis on the concept of "continent" emotional work. The findings of the research highlight the challenges in the articulation of roles among the actors involved, and reveal a disconnection in the satisfaction of the affective and cognitive needs of the children.
