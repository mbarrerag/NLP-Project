# Resource management model for project optimization integrating Artificial Intelligence techniques

The management of resources is a fundamental part of project management, since through it the managers, through planning determine beforehand what resources they require for the execution of a project, starting from a rigorous identification of needs and being quantified by means of the estimate of the budget required for its execution. In this case, since the budget is a limited resource, it is necessary to make precise formulations that allow the optimization of the capital and minimize the risk of incuring cost overruns or non-compliance with the objectives. This work applies techniques of data analytics and artificial intelligence to predict the total budget of research projects based on their own characteristics. Once executed the six selected models, Random Forest showed the best performance to predict the total cost of a project. The selected model is recognized as a strategic support tool that can be integrated for decision-making in the optimization of resources in projects. .
