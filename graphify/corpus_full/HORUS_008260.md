# SAFETY PROFILE AND FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO COMPLICATIONS OF TRANSVESICAL PROSTATECTOMY IN A HIGH COMPLEXITY HOSPITAL

The symptoms of the urinary tract low in population > 18 years may have a prevalence of up to 79%, being benign prostatic hyperplasia (HPB) the main etiology. Regarding open prostatectomy, there is the retropubic and transvesical technique, depending on its realization of the surgeon's experience, both with variable complications rates. Given the limitations in our health system, this surgery continues to be the recommended method for prostates > 80 g. Describe the safety profile of the transvesical prostatectomy technique in a IV-level hospital (high complexity) in Colombia. Retrospective cohort study. Inclusion criteria: patients taken to transvesical protectomy. Exclusion criteria: incomplete clinical histories for variable analysis. period: december 2016 to april 2022. Data reading: STATA version 13.0. The most effective univariate analysis and multivariate odectomy ratio was performed for variables and outcomes of interest. The median symptoms were considered p < 0.05
