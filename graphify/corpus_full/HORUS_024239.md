# TROTAMIND CULTURES: A CONTRIBUTION OF ICA AND BIOVERSITY-CIT ALLIANCE FOR AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH. HTTPS://HDL.HANDLE.NET/10568/115036

Scientific research and technological development is the creative work resulting from the collaboration of multiple institutions, which has existed for decades in a great synergy between the Colombian Agropecuario Institute (ICA) and the Bioversity-CIT Alliance. This collaboration has contributed significantly to the distribution and promotion around the world of genetic materials of rice, fodder, beans and yuca with better agronomic characteristics, and at the same time in the maintenance and conservation of the diversity of these crops. The records of the genetic improvement programs and the germplasm bank of the Alliance show that in collaboration both institutions have distributed about 400,000 genetic materials containing base germplasm, advanced lines, or improved varieties to various countries around the world, following the international normivity and phytosanitary certification issued by the ICA. The results show that the distributed materials have been requested by a great diversity of institutions in different countries and with multiple proposies in agronomic research and ingenatry. Finally, in this document we highlight the greatest achievements achieved by the Alliance and the ICA, which have contributed significantly with scientific research and food security at the global level
