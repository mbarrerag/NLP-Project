# NUTRITIONAL CARE PROCESS IN CANCER UNDER THE ENTRY OF ENTRY IN PERSON: HOPE PROCESS

Malnutrition is a common characteristic in cancer patients with a negative impact on the quality of life, which occurs in patients as a consequence of both the presence of the tumor, as well as of the medical and surgical treatments against cancer, which makes it necessary to manage a nutritional care process. To propose a process of nutritional care in cancer under the concept of center-based care in the person and the Model of the Picker Institute. This literature review was carried out following the methodology of Arksey and OMalley and the PRISMA guidelines, from which systematic searches were carried out in the databases PubMed, ScienceDirect and SciELO. The information was managed by means of tool promoted by Artificial Intelligence (IA) for systematic reviews of Rayyan literature. 886 articles were identified in an initial search that allowed selection of 8 articles in the period 2019-2022 that met the criteria established in this review, which allowed the description of the processes identified in terms of nutritional processes in cancer, based on the concept of diagnosis, based on the diagnosis of nutrition
