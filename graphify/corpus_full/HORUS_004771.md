# Astyanax caucanus: microsatellite loci development and population genetics in the Cauca River, Colombia

Astyanax caucanus is an endemic fish species to the Magdalena-Cauca basin in Colombia. It is considered a Least Concern species by the International Union for Conservation of Nature, and currently, it is not a fishery resource. Its fertilized eggs may drift up to 4–5 days before hatching and can be carried up to 340 km given the water velocity of the river. Although A. caucanus is listed as short -migratory species ( E ) and effective population sizes (N e > 1000), but also show evidence of bottlenecked populations and high values of the inbreeding coefficient (F IS ). Finally, these results are useful to understand the effects of other anthropic activities, besides fishing pressure on population bottlenecks found for other fish species cohabiting the area
