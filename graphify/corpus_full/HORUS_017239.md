# ENTRY IN CULTURE AND COMPASSIVE COMPETITION FOR INSURANCE EQUIPMENT: DEVELOPMENT AND PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT

Suffering is a reality shared by humanity, has important cultural connotations, and is especially relevant in the current globalized world.The cultural and compassionate competence allows nurses to recognize and alleviate suffering through interventions consistent with the culture of individuals, families and care contexts. Develop and evaluate the acceptability, feasibility and perceived effect of a training intervention in cultural and compassionate competence (ECCC) for nursing teams in hospitals. Method/s: A sequential mixed study (CUQQQQQQQQQ) in two phases: 1) A model of intervention (CUQU) which included a systematic integrative review of literature and qualitative descriptive study with nurses and nursing assistants from two hospitals in Bogotá, Colombia; 2) A quasi-experimental study (quan-CUAL) for preliminary evaluation (acceptability, feasibility and perceived effect) with nurses and nursing assistants in two hospitals in Bogotá. In all cases, informed consent was signed and confidentiality was guaranteed
