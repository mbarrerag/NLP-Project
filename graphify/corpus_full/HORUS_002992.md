# Evaluation of functional features of tropical dry forest species seeds

The dry tropical forest is one of the most transformed ecosystems in Colombia. The propagation of plant species is fundamental for its restoration, but the knowledge on the functional traits of the seeds and germination processes in native species is scarce and diffuse. In order to contribute to the conservation and propagation of the tree species of the tropical dry forest of the middle Magdalena, in this research were characterized morphological functional traits and germination of the seeds of eleven native plant species. The characterization included the detailed description of the morphoanatomical traits of the seeds; the identification of the traits that meet the greatest variation through an Analysis of Main Components (ACP); and the grouping of the species based on the similarity of their traits, by means of a analysis of conglomerates. In addition, five species were selected for evaluation of two pregerminative treatments (imbibition and scaification), and the increase of the species. In addition, three indicators were evaluated: percentage of germination, velocity and median of the mean germination time. Among the most relevant traits, the diameter and thickness of the testata, the viability, and the tolerance of the species to the three groups and the desicination. The classification included the detailed description of the morpho-anatomical treaties of the seeds; identifying the treaties that show the most variation using a Principal Component Analysis (PCA); and the specifics grouping based on the similarity of their trafficking through a cluster analysis. Besides, five species were selected to test two pre-germination treatments (imbibisation and exclusion), and the specific grouping based on the similarity of their treatment through a cluster analysis. Besides, five species were selected to test two pre-regulation treatments (imbibification and exclusion), to evaluate through three three indicators: germination percentage, speed, and average germination period.
