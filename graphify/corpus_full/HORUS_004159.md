# Losing is earning a little: Lessons from a living arrangement in COLOMBIA (1946-47)

In 1947, the first urban housing design competition for the middle class took place in Colombia, promoted by the Institute of Territorial Credit (ICT), a public institution created a few years ago to provide solutions in this area for all the cities of the country. Five proposals of six possible were awarded and one was disqualified for not literally fulfilling the established bases; however, it was the latter that finally materialized, paving the way for its authors to begin playing an important role in the technical experimentation of housing construction in the country
