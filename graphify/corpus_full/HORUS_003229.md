# EFFECT OF CO ADDITION ON THE STABILITY OF CATALIZERS OF OR SUPPORTED ON A CRIME FOR THE REFORM OF BIOGAS

The effect of adding Co (5 %m/m) to Ni catalyst (10 %m/m) supported on a mesoporous exmectite in the dry biogas reforming was evaluated. The experiments at 700 °C without pretreatment with H2 used a simulated stream of sulphur-free biogas (CH4/CO2/N2 45/35/20), ideal conditions for evaluating the anti-coke effect of Co. The addition of Co improved the catalyst’s reducibility to 98 %, reducing Ni reduction temperatures from 714 to 668 °C and decreasing Ni crystalline size, suggesting a greater metal-support interaction. Although both catalysts had a greater H2/CO ratio to 1 due to the high CH4 content in biogas, Ni-Co catalyst showed a catalytic conversion of approximately 5-7 % higher and a lower deactivation than Ni catalyst.
