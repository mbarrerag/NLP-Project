# ASSESSMENT OF THE PROXIMAL STRUCTURAL COMPOSITION BY FTIR-ATR OF THE CIB ASFALT IN FRESH STATUS USEFUL FOR PAVIMENTATION

This is an experimental work carried out using infrared spectroscopy in the mode of total attenuated reflectance (FTIR-ATR) and the conventional characterization provided in some tests of quality requirements, to evaluate Colombian asphalts fresh, under conditions of the state in plant, as raw material for the production of asphaltic mixtures for paving. This work considers the need to strengthen the road network that uses asphalt as construction material, mainly for flexible pavements, which has usually presented low durability and at the same time large investments, to provide the maintenance of what already exists and the implementation of new infrastructure developments, in the framework of a developing economy and with expectations for improving competitiveness
