# Management of post-vaccine musculoskeletal pain against COVID-19 with neurotherapeutic medicine: series of cases

The vaccination against SARS-Cov-2 has decreased the rate of infection, severity and mortality by COVID-19. Studies on the immune response to vaccines show an increase in inflammatory markers, some similar to severe COVID-19. Some patients generate the post-vaccine syndrome of COVID-19, it has been proposed that a persistent inflammatory response and vagular nerve dysfunction as an explanation. Neurotherapeutic medicine may be used for the regulation of the nervous system. Case 1: 52 year female patient with chronic pain with increased post-musculoskeletal pain against COVID-19. Case 2: 73 year old female patient with chronic pain in upper limbs subsequent to receiving vaccination against COVID-19. Case 3: 75 year female patient with onset of pain in the upper right limb, knees and lumbar area post-vaccture against COVID-19. After treatment with neuroltherapeutic medicine there was a decrease in the three cases. Ronic pain with increased muscularkeletal pain after the second dose against COVID-19.
