# AXES FOR A REFORM TO THE GENERAL SYSTEM OF SOCIAL SECURITY IN HEALTH OF COLOMBIA

The need to reform the General System of Social Security in Health has recently been discussed. However, despite the various initiatives before the Congress of Colombia, none of them have prospered. One of the apparent reasons for this failure is that the public debate on what changes are required is complex: it requires addressing issues of economics, law and ethics of care. Therefore, in this article, a comprehensive overview is presented based on four problems: (1) Health indicators (2) Normative contradiction (3) The financial structure and (4) The General System of Participations. Subsequently, proposed strategies for changes in the current health system are proposed that allow for balancing the ethics of care, protection of the fundamental right to health, and the economic and fiscal aspects of the system. The proposals focus on three main aspects, namely: (1) Different models of agreement with the territories; (2) Regulatory organization of the sector in a health code; and (3) Identification of the center for financing the health system
