# A two-dimensional model in finite elements for hydrodynamic simulation in regions near the coast.

This article presents the numerical solution, using the Finite Elements method, of the resulting equations when integrating the Navier-Stokes equations along the depth. Examples of academic interest and engineering interest are presented that show the benefits of the model and its numerical solution. Some of the most important restrictions presented by the model are also mentioned. Finally, some comments are presented concerning the complementation of the model, to cover a greater field of applicability, and referring to the state of progress in which this area of numerical techniques applied to the solution of problems in the area of hydrodynamics is found
