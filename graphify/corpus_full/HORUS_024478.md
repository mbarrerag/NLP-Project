# LAND RETURN FOR VICTIMS OF ARMED CONFLICT

Forced displacement and dispossession are at the heart of the dynamics of the armed conflict and the humanitarian crisis in Colombia. These phenomena have also affected women differently and disproportionately. In this context, women also face specific vulnerabilities factors that explain the current context, and in particular the forced displacement, which generate in them a differential, specific and acute affectation. Moreover, as survivors they are forced to face extreme material and psychological burdens, since they must assume abruptly different family, economic and social roles than those used. These findings have led the Constitutional Court to affirm that the situation of displaced women constitutes one of the most critical and alarming manifestations of the unconstitutional state of affairs declared in judgment T-025 of 2004 and that therefore they require specific measures of resolution by the competent authorities, which go through the inclusion of differentiated approaches that allow us to be able to respond to their particular conditions and needs. The first part is the present introduction and is concerned with showing the starting point of the approach developed throughout the text, which consists of noting that both the quantitative and qualitative magnitude of the conflict make restitution a major step as a measure of reparation, but that its realization faces multiple difficulties, in particular for women, who are exposed to conditions of exclusion and discrimination that are accentuated by the violence they are victims of in the context of the armed conflict. The second part develops the main obstacles that exist in Colombia to ensure the restitution of land to women who have been stripped of their property. The third part identifies key aspects of the design of public policy and makes a proposal on how they could be proposed or structured in order to ensure that their design, implementation and monitoring allow the effective realization of restitution for women
