# Parental role in new family organizations: homoparentality and single parenthood

This work has sought to investigate the paternal function in the new family organizations that have been established first and foremost in the contemporary era, namely the single-parent and homoparent family formations. It seeks to clarify what is sustained and what changes in these transformations or contemporary family arrangements. For this purpose, an initial journey is made by the father and his fall and by the Oedipus complex in culture, in myths and in some religions, and then to analyze fundamental aspects of psychoanalytic theory related to the structuring of the subject and linked mainly to the Edip Complex proposed by Sigmund Freud and to the respective elaborations of Jacques Lacan.
