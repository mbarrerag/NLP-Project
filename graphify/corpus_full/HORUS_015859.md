# URBAN- ARCHITECTONIC DESIGN OF CAMPUS AND ITS SIGNALIZATION

The headquarters signage system is anchored in cultural references, such as the Arhuacas backpacks, and in the concept of academic graffiti; moreover, it has the imprint of recognizing the campus as the headquarters of nature and life, articulated to the local culture, which makes it relevant to the community that travels through it. As for the work team that defined this signage, it was made up of: Miguel Rolando Ruiz, Alexander Encizo, Juan Sebastián Cuestas and César Puertas. Santiago Soler was in production, among others, and the National Director of Undergraduate Programs, Professor Diana María Farías, and Ivón Dorado, professor of ethnomatematics, was consulted
