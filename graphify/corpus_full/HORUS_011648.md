# CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT, WORK, AND HEALTH IN NON-ASSALARIZED WORKERS IN BOGOTÁ, 2013: A STUDIES WITH NEOMARXIST CLASS PERSPECTIVE

In this work, we sought to contribute to the knowledge of differential conditions of employment, work and health in urban non-wage workers through a neo-Marxist analysis of social class position in a sample in Bogotá. A quantitative methodology was adopted with secondary analysis of the survey. 766 people participated (M=45.1; D.E.=14.3; 46.2% women). The emerging position of non-wage-non-owner social class of means of production (33.9% of the sample) was identified. This class position presented, in general, more unfavourable conditions in employment, work and health compared to the rest of class positions. MANOVAS revealed a significant multivarizing effect of the position of non-waged social class in the assessed conditions. The findings present the relevance of the social class to public health research
