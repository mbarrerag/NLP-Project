# Compositional quality and concentration of omega-3 fatty acids (alpha-linolenic) and omega-6 (linoleic) present in bovine milk from three natural regions of Ecuador

The milk of cow is an important component in the human diet and one of its nutritional contributions is the lipid fraction formed by various fatty acids, among them, the linoleic acid (AL) of omega-6 family and the alpha-linolenic acid (AL) of omega-3 family, both structural constituents of membranes of cellular tissues and metabolic regulators. Due to its importance, the objective of this research was to determine the concentration of omega-3 fatty acids (alpha-linolenic) and omega-6 (linoleic) by means of gas chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry (GC-MS), in relation to the influence of the factors region (Costa, Sierra and Amazonia) and period (luvicious and dry) on the concentration of those acids. It was worked with 30 collection centers and was collected according to the LCL-INS-01 protocol. The compositional analysis was performed under the method ISO 9622-ALF 141/2013 /LCL-PE-01 and the analysis of the lipid profile using GC-MS.
