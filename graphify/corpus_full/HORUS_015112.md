# The concept of notation in Wittgenstein's Logical-Philosophicus Tractatus

In a posthumous note of 1929, Wittgenstein states that a logical notation is “the ultimate expression of a philosophical conception” (MS 105, 10–12). The thesis presents an investigation of the concept of logical notation in the Logical Tractatus-philosophicus (TLP) and an interpretative proposal of two aspects of the work that is consistent with that affirmation.
