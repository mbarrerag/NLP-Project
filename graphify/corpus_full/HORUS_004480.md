# OVERVIEW OF THE FAMILY CAREER IN COLOMBIA: EXPLORATORY SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

to describe the methodological trends, the populations studied and the future challenges presented in the literature on the overload of the Colombian family caregiver.Methods: Systematic exploratory review in which the databases PubMed, ScienceDirect, Lilacs, Cuiden, SciELO, EBSCO and BVS were consulted, specifically original articles published from 2016 to 2021.Results:In 20 arcles reviewed, a direct relationship was found between socioeconomic conditions and the caregiver's overload. Cultural contex and socioeconomic conditions are factors that influence the perception of the caregiver's overload.Conclusions: Nursing interventions directed at family caregivers are necessary to improve their perception of overload and consequently quality of life
