# EDITORS: GOMEZ-GUERRERO MANUEL EDUAARDO,GONZALEZ TEJADA, CATALINA. GAT GROUP, EAFIT -MARIN-CERÓN, MARÍA ISABEL. GAT GROUP, EAFIT - BETANCURTH-MONTES, LILIANA, GAIA GROUP, ANTIOCIA UNIVERSITY, NATIONAL UNIVERSITY, MINES FACULTY - RESTRESS

The geological heritage in Colombia as a line of action, dissemination and research in geosciences has been developing notably during the last twenty years, with special interest and contribution of research and academic groups, bringing with it innovative concepts such as geotourism, the global geoparks of UNESCO, geoeducation, geoconservation, among others, that have gradually come to revitalize the link between the local communities and their geological heritage, contributing also to the modernization of geoscientific thinking in Colombia. In particular, the concept of geoconservation invites us to think as citizens and as professionals in Earth sciences, on the need for conservation and sustainable use of those sites or geoambients that offer services or resources on which we depend directly as a society; or those geological traits that, due to their scientific importance, constitute the memory of the Earth, as estimated by the Digne Declaration of 1991. In this context, the work does Geoconservation in Colombia: Approximations around the theoretical and practical.
