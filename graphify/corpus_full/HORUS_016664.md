# Fruko and his Thess. A Studio of His Discography (1970-1980)

The study of the twelve long-lasting albums (LP) made by Fruko and his Theses in the 1970s, allows to reconstruct the history of the group and salsa in Colombia. They are also a sample of the processes of creation and musical production that the group achieved during these years. Also and especially, they are testimony of the evolution and consolidation of the characteristic sound of the group, which constitutes its main contribution to the development movement of salsa in Colombia in this period. Dúrate the period we consider, the orchestra Fruko and its Theses would become a school of arrangers, composers and interpreters and would be the platform for launching the most outstanding voices of salsa in Colombia as those of “Piper” Pepper, Wilson “Manyoma” and “Joe” Arroyo. . The study of the twelve LP (long Wilson-playing) records produced by Fruko and its Theses in the 1970s, is fundamental in the reconstruction of the history of the group and the salsa movement in Colombia
