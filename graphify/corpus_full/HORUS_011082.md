# Economical evaluation model for disposable masks N95 versus half-facial half-piece reusable elastomer masks with P100 interchangeable filters.

The pandemic by COVID-19 has had great impacts on our way of life, including the economic one. Given the shortage of protective equipment like masks N95, other equally effective equipment emerged as options, such as the elastomeric masks with interchangeable filters. However, it remains unexamined in depth to what extent one of the two options is most beneficial from an economic point of view. This research has as its main objective to compare the costs of both types of masks and to design a calculation tool for it. The study was a prospective, descriptive, cross-sectional, cost analysis, using in a theoretical model of use of masks N95 versus elastomeric masks P100.
