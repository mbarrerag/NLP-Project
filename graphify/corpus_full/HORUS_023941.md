# PHOTOSYSTEM ECOPHISIOLOGY

The study of physiological responses of plants to the environment (i.e., "ecophysiology") has attracted researchers from remote times, since ecophysiology studies provide the basis for scaling the physiological processes of plants from tissue to canopy, at ecosystem, region and global level. Ecophysiology is a science that allows decision-making for the proper agronomic management of crops in the face of both favourable and unfavourable conditions. Photoynthesis is the main driving force of life on earth, as it introduces the energy of sunlight into the biosphere and releases oxygen from water. Plants and some forms of bacteria capture the energy of light directly from solar radiation and use it for the synthesis of organic substances, in addition to producing basic raw materials from which other cell biomolecules are produced. Photoynthesis describes the process by which green plants synthesize organic compounds from inorganic raw materials (CO2 and H2O) using light (photons).
