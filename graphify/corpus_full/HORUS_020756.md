# Church of Santa Clara de Santafe of the New Kingdom of Granada. Symbolic power, mystical experience and rhetoric in the 17th and 18th centuries

This is a theoretical research that links social, political, theological and aesthetic elements around the Church of Santa Clara in the City of Bogotá. Here we analyze how a discourse was consolidated through space, the construction of a rhetorical language that exceeds the power of the word and is transferred to the multiple sensibility of the human being. Thus, this church is positioned as a building testimony of the evangelizing processes, by putting it in dialogue with the positions proposed in the counter-reform, which turned the arts into a persuasive support for the diffusion of the faith in Europe, and by extension, in Colonial America. In the framework of this research, the church and the elements that constitute it are put into dialogue with various disciplines to trace in it the consolidation of a rhetorical process that, from the language, is transferred to various systems of materialization, making the space an intertextual discourse. age, is transferred to various systems of materialization, making space an intertextual discourse
