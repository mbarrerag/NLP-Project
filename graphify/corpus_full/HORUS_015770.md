# HUMANIZATION, ETHICAL SENSIBILITY AND DECISION-MAKING OF HEALTH PROFESSIONALS IN INTENSIVE CARE UNITS OF BOGOTA AND CHARTERGENA

in the ICUs hard technologies generate impact and great demands in those in charge of the care of patients, who must perform a work of excellence, virtuous, agile, among other aspects. Currently the humanization in the provision of health services is an ethical imperative; it is also for ICUs, where to achieve that technological and scientific advances are focused on relatives and patients allows a more holistic and holistic care. Interpreting the humanization and ethical sensitivity relationship with the decision-making of health professionals in public and private intensive care units of Bogotá and Cartagena. qualitative study of a comprehensive type that used tools interview in semistructured line, the field journal and the participant observation. For the analysis of the information, the patient's coding and categorization process (deductive and inductive categorization analysis), a hermeneutic analysis of the professional, and content. This difference is important to materialize humanization in the Uci, taking into account the information cited by Dalla C, with greater ethical sensitivity, attention is focused on the person and on a relationship of trust. Limitations: Access to obtain data: There were difficulties in collecting information due to the limited availability of time for professionals to agree on interviews
