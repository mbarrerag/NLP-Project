# Flexion behaviour of concrete beams reinforced with polypropylene fibers subjected to cyclic loads.

The following research presented the results of the experimental study of flexion behaviour and energy absorption capacity of concrete beams reinforced with polypropylene fibers for four different fiber dosifications 1.8kg/m3, 4.5 kg/m3, 6.0 kg/m3 and 7.5kg/m3, two fibre-free control mixtures and two different sizes of thick aggregate (TMN, 12.7mm and 19mm) after being under two cyclic load patterns with 40 and 80 repetitions (C1 and C2) controlling in both cases the amplitude of the load, which was considered constant and equal to 65% of the average break-up module. For the established dosages and mechanical properties of the fibres used, the probes presented a softening behavior due to the sudden drop of the load after the fissureness, therefore the residual stresses calculated after the break-up and the tenacity were significantly affected by the application of external loads of cyclical nature as the probetas further loss of the load
