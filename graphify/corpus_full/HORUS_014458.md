# The bicycle as a contribution to the logistic efficiency of the cargo transport in the last mile.

Cities for economic development seek to transport goods more efficiently by increasing productivity and decreasing negative externalities; and cargo bicycles could be a good strategy to achieve this. The aim of this research is to estimate econometric models that allow representing the production and attraction of last mile goods that are susceptible to transport on cargo bicycles in urban areas. As a result, it is necessary to determine which are the most important variables for estimating production and attraction of last mile deliveries, to calculate the environmental and economic benefits, and to propose policies of the use of cargo bicycles for the distribution of goods in the last mile based on a case study in the city of Medellín, Colombia.
