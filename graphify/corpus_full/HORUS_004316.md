# Design and modeling of a vegetal biomass stove to improve efficiency

The wood stove is generally used for cooking food in rural areas, although stoves have been developed with new technologies such as energy and gas. Currently, the use of wood resources as biofuel is constant, to the point that the World Health Organization (WHO) states that the use of wood as a source of heat in traditional stoves is among the main causes of air pollution, causing 3.7 million deaths in 2012 in the world, from burning wood, emitting Particulate Material -PM- and greenhouse gases. Approximately in Colombia, by 2013, according to DANE -National Department of Statistics -1.6 million families used wood for cooking, where 1.4 million were families in rural areas and the remaining 200,000 lived in urban areas. The contribution of PM 2.5 (Material Particulated below 2.5 microns) by wood combustion is important because of its impact on air quality and human well-being, i.e., they are also responsible for respiratory diseases such as asthma, non-fatal heart attacks, among others, given from continuous exposure of these materials. The proposal is the design of a lower stove, with greater efficiency than any analysis, which is found in terms of the current ones
