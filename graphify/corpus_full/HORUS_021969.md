# Peptidic chimeras: a synthetic alternative to enhance antimicrobial activity of peptides.

The emergence of antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) is an alternative that has gained relevance since they have antimicrobial activity against a wide spectrum of microorganisms and anticancer against cell lines of different types of cancer through unique action mechanisms. One of the alternatives that allows to enhance the biological activity of APMs is the synthesis of peptides with biologically active peptides, which allows the acquisition of molecules that are not produced naturally and act through combined mechanisms of action generating increased selectivity and biological activity between 1.5 and 10 times. In this sense, the design of the chimeras can combine penetrating peptides in cells with biologically active peptides to generate hybrid peptides with dual function.
