# Determination of the mechanical characteristics of simple masonry dies reinforced with steel mesh and cross-sectional connectors subjected to axial load and diagonal tension.

This research evaluates and analyzes the behavior of simple masonry not reinforced and reinforced externally, constructed with horizontal drilling clay units. 13 simple masonry walls, mortar and masonry with mesh reinforcement, cutter and mortar connectors were tested at compression. 18 simple masonry walls, mortar masonry, mesh-reinforced masonry and cross-sectional connectors, and mesh masonry, connectors, mortar and fibers were tested at diagonal traction.
