# VARIABILITY IN NUTRITIONAL QUALITY AND PRODUCTIVITY OF GRAMINIES ADJUSTMENTED TO UNUNIFABLE ARABIAN SABANES, COLOMBIA

The objective was to evaluate the variability of parameters of nutritional quality and productivity of adapted grasses introduced into bank and low physiographics of floodable savannahs. Plant tissue samples were taken at 30, 40 and 50 days of age and a bromathological analysis was performed through Near Infrared Spectroscopy (NIRS). Subsequently, a main component analysis (ACP) was performed. The results showed that the low grasses present a higher concentration of digestible fractions, with lower fiber levels compared to the species of banks
