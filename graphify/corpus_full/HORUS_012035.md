# Power management in interconnected microgrids using D-ADMM.

The electrical microgrids are the result of the high integration of renewable energies into electrical systems and the constant effort to improve the quality, reliability and safety indices. The increase of these systems has resulted in interaction through interconnected systems. As a result of this interaction, the economic allocation of resources in charge of the energy management system has become a complex task, often solved with centralized optimization methods. This work presents a formulation of the energy management problem using AC power flow equations, to represent the behavior of any electrical system. In addition, a distributed optimization algorithm based on the method of alternating direction multipliers is proposed, which with the use of local estimators distributes the problem among all microgrids. Thus, partial centralization of information is avoided by eliminating unique points of failure and privacy violations. The algorithm is tested through three study cases that simulate different network and resource conditions. Similarly, convergence is verified using the primary and dual residuals of the problem raised
