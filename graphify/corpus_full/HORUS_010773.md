# SIMULTANEOUS AND NON-SIMULTANEOUS BLOW-UP FOR A DISCRETE MODEL OF A NON-LOCAL DIFFUSION SYSTEM

We study the following discrete model of nonlocal diffusion coupled system with p, q, r, s > 0. We analyze the conditions on the exponents p, q, r, s > 0 for the existence of simultaneous blow-up and non-simultaneous blow-up of solutions and the blow-up rate are analyzed. Finally, we show some numerical experiments which illustrate the obtained results
