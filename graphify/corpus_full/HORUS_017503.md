# Current status of research on gender and accounting in Colombia

This research seeks to analyze the current state of gender and accounting research in Colombia. From this consideration, it was necessary to identify the literature and the available documents on this subject, to examine the perspectives and visions used by Colombian authors in the production of studies aimed at gender and accounting. The methodology is qualitative with an exploratory and descriptive scope, focused on the method of content analysis and allowed to define three categories of study: history, research and profession. From the documents found it was concluded that women have historically been related to accounting, however, their professional abilities and the recognition of their rights have been unknown, so that from the accounting discipline transformative agendas are being led that allow to re-indicate the recognitions that have been stolen from women, among the strategies are the gender studies in accounting that have allowed to reflect multiple characteristics that help to understand the importance, presence and permanence of accounting women in the disciplinary transformation. In addition, there are limitations that prevent the evolution of variables, such as barriers of academic relations
