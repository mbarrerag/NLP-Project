# SPARTIAL URDER MODELS AND ASSOCIATION BETWEEN WAR AND LEUKEMIA.

The incidence rate in Colombia is 4 cases per 100,000 children under 14 years of age, and survival does not reach the levels of developed countries. The search for cluster rates of incidence and mortality due to acute pediatric leukemia and perinatal exposure to violence, poverty and other health quality variables may be factors that explain the pathology’s behavior in the country. The association of pe-rinatal exposure to collective violence, poverty and environmental conditions and the presence of cluster rates of incidence and mortality due to acute pediatric leukaemia in children born from the period 2002 to 2013 is explored. The spatial self-regressive model of Poisson-Hurdle found that incidence and mortality rates by LAC increase by increasing the incidence and mortality rate of IICA. The percentage of NBI and rurality decrease incidence and mortality rates by PAL. The percentage of health coverage increases the mortality rate by PLA. Armed conflict measured by AACI is a factor associated with the presence of acute Leukaemia
