# Emotions experienced by middle school students in physics and chemistry classes

Emotions in the science class play a fundamental role as mediators in learning, thus, have a significant impact on the behavior, skills, abilities and school context. Therefore, in this research, the emotions experienced by the middle students in the physics and chemistry classes of the Archidiocesan Lyceum of Our Lady (LANS) are studied in order to identify the most frequent emotions and the causes that generate them. In the research different sociodemographics of interest are involved such as age, sex, socioeconomic stratum, class type, type of subject, among others. The methodological development is framed within the quantitative model, under guidelines of a non-experimental, transsectual and correlational design, which allowed the creation and reduction of an instrument that was validated by experts and used to measure the frequency of emotions present in the students.
