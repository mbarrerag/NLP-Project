# Transforming life from home · Care practices and collective strategies for sustaining life developed by women in vulnerable neighborhoods Nueva Villa La Iguaná neighborhood study, Medellín

In this text I pay attention to the daily care relationships in two houses of the neighborhood La Iguaná, and in some houses I have inhabited. Human relations and more than human beings between people, with objects, spaces and other beings, mediated by the affections and memories that make us. Generating questions by the agencies present to care for, and naturalization of the actions that make up the care as “feminine” in order to situate the political and ethical of care, to recognize in the knowledge-housework other forms of knowledge necessary for life, that allow the recognition and legitimization of other knowledge. I do this from conversations with authors, women close to me, and from the experiences of life of Pilar and Nancy, two inhabitants of La Iguaná that allowed me to know with my body, my chamber and my libretto, their houses and their lives.
