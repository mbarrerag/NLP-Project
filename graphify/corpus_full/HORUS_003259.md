# OBTENTION OF RUMEX CRIPS ALMIDON AND BIOPLASTIC PRODUCTION

Rumex crispus L. is one of the weeds that can be most visualized in almost all landscapes of Colombia, this species is problematic for any agricultural productive sector. Due to the above, and taking into account the long time that it represents the degradation of plastic materials derived from oil and its high accumulation (Kings, 2013), the extraction of starch from the root of Rumex crispus and its subsequent use in the production of bioplastic. The plant material to be used were roots from mature plants of Rumex crispus collected in Centro Agropecuario Marengo -UN headquarters Bogotá is proposed as objective. To obtain the starch, the roots were washed and liquefy in relation to 100 g of root by 50 ml of distilled water, subsequently obtained by means of cloth, allowing the separation of residues, these samples were carried out by gravity sedimentation for 3 hours.
