# ASSESSMENT OF THREAT BY TORRENCIAL AVENCES IN THE DEPARTMENT OF ANTIOCIA TO CUENCA SCALE

The torrential avenues are highly destructive and frequent events in tropical environments and mountainous terrains such as Colombia, where the presence of human settlements located on torrential fans is common. This combination of factors creates a critical scenario, where torrential avenues are natural threats that must be properly understood and incorporated into the plans of territorial planning. The present study proposes a methodology for the prioritization of basins with susceptibility and threat to torrential avenues, with a regional approach that allows understanding and characterizing such events. The susceptibility of more than 3,000 basins in the department of Antioquia is characterized by factors such as morphometry, analysis of slope stability and probability of occurrence of precipitation as a trigger factor. The results show that close to half of the basins are torrential in nature, and 11% of them are in the highest category of threat. These critical basins must be prioritized to carry out studies in greater detail, allowing to define precisely the potential areas of impact and the associated risk level for the exposed elements
