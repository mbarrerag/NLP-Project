# EFFECTS OF EXPLOSIVE ARTICLES ON THE AUDIT HEALTH OF EXPOSED PERSONS IN ARMED CONFLICT

to describe the knowledge built regarding the effects of conventional and non-conventional weapons of war explosions used in various armed conflicts on the hearing health of exposed persons. systematic exploratory review of scientific production elaborated in the last 20 years, between 1999 and 2019. The identification of bibliographic references was carried out through the consultation in databases and search interfaces such as BIREME.BR, Oxford academic, EBSCOHOST, PubMed and Google Academic. A total of 1801 references were recovered and included qualitative and quantitative research, scientific reports and revisions of literature in the review. A total of 41 articles were selected for full text review. Qualitative synthesis was carried out by heterogeneity in the studies. there was a growing interest in the study of this problem through the period addressed, especially from English-speaking countries involved in international armed conflicts.
