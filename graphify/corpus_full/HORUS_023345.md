# PROPOSALS FOR TERRITORIAL MARKETING FOR THE PROMOTION OF THE CENTRE OF MEDELLIN AS A CULTURAL TOURISTIC DESTINATION BETWEEN YOUNG PEOPLE

Currently, the marketing or territorial marketing is a strategy that allows to take advantage of the historical social constructions in the territories, in order to improve their economic performance. Tourism is one of the activities that use this strategy most, since it is a suitable way of promoting the visit of foreigners and residents. In the case of Medellín, the center is the place where the city arose; however, this area suffers a physical and security deterioration, due to the criminal problem that is experienced there. In this order of ideas, the objective of this research is to determine territorial marketing strategies that contribute to the promotion of the center of Medellín as a domestic cultural tourist destination among young people from public universities. To this, the inductive method was used with qualitative and quantitative approaches and techniques of gathering information for analysis. It is concluded that territorial marketing allows to promote tourism in specific territories and, likewise, that university youth could see in the center of the city a new space of cultural leisure, although support from the mayor and other institutions is necessary to finance this activity
