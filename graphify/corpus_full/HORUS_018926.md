# EFFECTS OF PUBLIC EXPENDITURE CORRUPTION IN COLOMBIA DEPARTMENTS

This article is framed in the literature on corruption and its effects on the economy. In particular, it is analyzed the effect that corruption has on the public expenditures of the Colombian departments’ governorates for the period 2008-2018. To this end, a corruption indicator based on criminal sanctions against public officials is constructed and its impact on spending on education, health and infrastructure is verified through econometric models. The results show that the governorates with the greatest risks of corruption spend more on education and infrastructure and less on health.
