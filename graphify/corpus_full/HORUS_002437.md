# MIES CROWN HALL AMERICAS PRIZE: MEXICO PIGLIATUTTO¿, EN THE DAY OF ARCHITECTURE, TURIN, DECEMBER 2024. ISSN 2284-1369. HTTPS://ILGIORNALEDELLARCHITETTURA.COM/2024/12/07/MIES-CROWN-HALL-AMERICAS-PRIZE-MESSICO-PIGLIATUTTO/

The article, the result of the "Paisajes antrópicos" research analyzes some prized projects in the last edition of the MIES prize, which highlighted the work of young Mexican architects, who built two of the six finalist works: the extension of the Anahuacalli Museum, by Mauricio Rocha, who won the first prize, and the Guadalupe market, by Colectivo C733. Moreover, in the emerging category the recognition went to the landscape project of the Colosio dam, by Taller Capital. The three works are characterized by simplicity and relevance to the urban contexts, which are reflected in the choices regarding environmental comfort, flexibility of use, materiality, inclusion of the community in both conceptual development and maintenance of the interventions. the selected cities give more than they require: they place the bases for a more generous future for architecture, says Dirk Denison, director of the prize, which, in resonance with the famous affirmation of the new vanishing cities, has added to the public areas: they have thus created a more generous system for the future than the designers, which is the three more than they have been built
