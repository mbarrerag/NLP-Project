# USE OF GENETIC RESOURCES OF PASSIFLORA L. TO IMPROVE PRODUCTIVITY AND DISEASE RESISTANCE IN THE CULTIVE OF MARACUYA (EDULIS F. FLAVICARPA DEGENER PASSIFLORA) IN COLOMBIA

The maracuyá (Passiflora edulis f. flavicarpa Degener) is one of the main fruit species of the Neotropical region due to its high nutritional value as a source of minerals and vitamins. Colombia with about 10,000 hectares and a production of 175,000 tons is the second world producer after Brazil. Despite this potential, the crop is affected by multiple plant protection problems, such as the dryer caused by Fusarium solani, which imposes chemical treatments for its control.OBJECT: Estimating the effect of passiflora portianders (P. alata, P. edulis f. edulis, P. quadrangularis and P. maliconis). resistant to the drying plant in the vegetative growth of the cup and agronomically evaluated the productivity and quality of the fruit in the maracuyá injertado. MÉTODO: The study was carried out between 2019 and 2021 at the Agrosavia experimental station in Palmira, Valle del Cauca (Colombia) at 1032 m. n. m. under analysis of the blocks and 5 The porttainjerto P. maliformis (cholupa) showed the best averages of production in kg/plant (36.5) and yield in ton/ha (20.2), although without significant differences with control (maracuya without grafting; 36.4 kg/plant and 19.9 t/ha). Likewise, the porttainjertos with the highest survival percentage due to the involvement of F. solani were P. maliformis (87.5%), P. quadrangularis (87.5%) and P. alata (81.25%) at 15 months after planting. The cholupa presents a great potential as a porttainjerto for the maracuyá for its tolerance to the dry land and its agronomic behavior. These results are the basis for a maracuyá improvement program in Colombia that involves the use of grafting technique with a relative of the maracuyá so that producers can mitigate the effect of soil diseases
