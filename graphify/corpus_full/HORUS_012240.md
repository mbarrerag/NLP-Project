# The vegetation of the Colombian paramuna life region: with emphasis on the Eastern mountain range and the Central Andes.

With the purpose of achieving an approximation to the phytosociological order of the high Colombian mountain and contributing to the knowledge of the relations between the vegetation developed in the high Andean natural regions, the patterns of floristic composition of 1321 plots made in moors of the great mountain chains of the country were analyzed, contrasting it against 1317 units of vegetation described for the north of the Andes, evaluating its validity previously according to international nomenclature protocols. A consensus between the traditional phytosociological approaches classical and the application of automated classification methods allowed to identify 206 syntaxons of terrestrial vegetation and palustre in the Colombian moors (13 classes, 20 orders, 44 alliances, 129 associations), plus another 48 syntaxons of aquatic nature (4 classes, 4 orders, 5 alliances, 18 associations, 17 subassociations). These latter show wide regional distribution to cosmopolitan especially towards the mountain belts, while some specialized communities inhabiting parameral ecosystems.
