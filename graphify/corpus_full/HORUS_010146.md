# PLAYS ASSOCIATED TO PRODUCTION OF EXPORT LIRANCES IN ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA

The cutting lilies represent the fifth most demanded ornamental product worldwide, were introduced to Colombia in the middle of the year 2000 to diversify a market saturated with roses, carnations and chrysanthemums. The pests have been one of the most limiting plant protection problems in the production and export of the flower; they generate economic losses due to alterations in the quality of the flower and interceptions in the international market. It is important to establish integrated pest management programs that are efficient, economic and environmentally sound. The objective of this research was to determine pest species associated with the production of lilies and their percentages of infestation. The taxonomic identification of pests was determined by the Laboratory of Phytosanitary Diagnosis of the ICA Sectional ICA Antioquia. The percentages of infestation were obtained by means of a monitoring program, for 72 weeks, by direct count of the total number of individuals per unit of sampling, by bed, by block of production.
