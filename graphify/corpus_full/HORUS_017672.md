# Positive solutions for a semipositone φ-Laplacian problem

We prove an existence result concerning positive radial solutions for a nonlinear φ-Laplacian problem by using a shooting technique. The nonlinearity satisfies adequate conditions and has semipositone behavior. Our result generalizes a previous result in the literature
