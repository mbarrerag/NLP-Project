# PEACE, INSPECTS AND CIRCULARITY: MOSCA SOLDIER NEGRA (HERMETIA ILLUCANS) AS ALIMENT OF FOOD POINTS IN A COMMUNITY OF PEACE FIRE

In territories affected by violence and armed conflict in Colombia, agricultural alternatives are needed that facilitate the reintegration of armed actors and in turn are economically viable alternatives for rural communities. In addition, the expansion of the agricultural border and linear production practices generate environmental challenges. To address these challenges, it is proposed the implementation of sustainable productive alternatives for the production of animal protein in this type of territories immersed in a circular approach. Among these alternatives, it is emphasized the production of insects such as black soldier fly (*Hermetia illucens*) that from the degradation of organic residues generates protein in the form of larval biomass, to be used in the feeding of conventional animals, also generates a residue that can be used as organic fertilizer. This project aims to strengthen the initiative Insectos por la Paz, through the evaluation of the larvae of the black soldier fly as a food alternative for the production of chicken meat and the use of its residues as a fertilizer in the Old Territorial Area of Training and Reintegration (AETCR) Jaime Pardo Leal region in Las Colinas, Guaviare.
