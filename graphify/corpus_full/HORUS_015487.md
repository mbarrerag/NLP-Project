# Characterization of the clinical and pharmacological profile of patients with epilepsy in a state of detention treated in a third-level hospital in Colombia.

Title: The description of the clinical and pharmacological profile of patients with epilepsy in a state of confinement attended in a third level hospital in Colombia. The diagnosis of epilepsy is associated with an increase in mortality from 2 to 3 times with respect to the general population and confers a significant degree of morbidity. Its impact on vulnerable populations, such as the case of the prison population has been little studied in the world, in fact in Colombia is an unexplored topic, partly because health care does not usually concentrate in a single institution. It was considered relevant to propose and execute this research conscious of the lack of available studies, the need to characterize the population with epilepsy in prisons and the impact that this would represent on local and national health policies. Justification: Regardless of the reason for their detention, the patients deprived of their freedom are considered vulnerable, since they have been separated from their usual environment and lack of free will. The diagnosis of epilepsy in this context, is an additional factor that can deteriorate their quality of life if not guaranteed integrality in the care in patients, this implies not only access to the service, also opportunity to be attended by the appropriate professional, medicines and technology. The statistical analysis was performed in the program “R” version 4.0.4. An univivary analysis was performed of the sociodemographic, clinical and outcome characteristics of patients was performed. For quantitative variables, normal was evaluated by means of graphic methods with histograms, box diagrams and whisus and mustaches only. Since the quantitative variables did not present a normal distribution, the median and the interquartilic range were calculated for each of these. In case of the variable having a symmetrical distribution, the mean and standard deviation was calculated additionally and the mean and standard deviation.
