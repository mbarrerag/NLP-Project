# Pythagorean Boxes and Quotients of Gaussian Integers

We show that the right combination of Gaussian integers and stereographic projection leads very quickly to the classical four-parameter formulas parameterizing all primitive Pythagorean boxes. Suitable conditions for primitiveness are also established along with an easy algorithm to find such parameters
