# TOWARDS A RESEARCH AND EDUCATION STRUCTURE FOR THE PREVENTION OF ACCIDENTS BY INCIDENTS AND EXPLOSIONS IN COLOMBIA

The current situation of research and education in fires and explosions in Colombia was analysed from a science and engineering approach and recommendations were made on the fields to be developed to create an investigative and educational structure that supports efforts to prevent them. Given the risk of fires and explosions, most countries have encouraged the creation of research and education centres oriented to scientific development in this area. In Colombia, such infrastructure has a scarcely incipient development. Review of important aspects in fires and explosions such as statistical analysis, physicochemical characterization of flammable substances, chemical kinetics, combustion, simulation, thermal radiation, pyrolysis phenomena, smouldering, soot and smoke formation, experimental characterization, risk assessment, education and other specific aspects of explosions showed that in Colombia there is good development in the application of combustion and pyrolysis for commercial purposes, but without emphasis on fires and explosions. In the other areas there is a background of research specifically related to this field that needs to be strengthened. It is recommended that the development of curricular programs of post-graduate science and technology in this area be strengthened, as well as the characterization of utilization of flammable of flammable substances for the development of flammable purposes
