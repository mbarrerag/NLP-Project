# Assemblage of the zooplankton in a high Andean reservoir (Chivor, Colombia)

Reservoirs represent social and economic importance, since they offer services for nearby communities, among them they highlight the generation of electrical energy, storage of water for consumption and in some cases for irrigation. Several studies have demonstrated the importance of zooplankton in aquatic ecosystems based on its ecological role. Thus it is important to highlight the importance of broadening knowledge regarding the dynamics of this group, which leads to a closer approach to the behavior of the system. This study was carried out in the Embalse La Esmeralda (Chivor, Boyacá), where a sampling periodicity of 16 weeks was defined, in three sampling stations: Station 1 input or riberin, Station 2 or transition zone and Station 3 or dam area, each with three depths (surface, media, background), where samples of 30 L were taken.
