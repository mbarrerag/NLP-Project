# System for managing the risk associated with mass movements in road corridors in mountainous areas

This study proposes a methodology for managing risks by mass movements in road corridors in mountainous areas. The main objective is to develop an integral model that allows to assess the threat, vulnerability and risk by mass movements of the road corridor, at any stage of the life cycle of the project, allowing the user to visualize the risk scenario of the complete corridor or specific sections, according to the graphic capabilities of the geographical information system used. The system provides practical recommendations for both the reduction of exposure and the treatment of the threat. The method was applied in a segment of road in the Northeast Antioquia, between the municipalities of Vegachi and Remedios, where 69 sections distributed along the stretch were analyzed. The results of the evaluation showed consistency with the actual conditions of the road, and the numerical risk indicators allowed to issue practical management recommendations throughout the segment analyzed. The proposed management system from the most critical areas and the prioritization of interventions, optimizing resources and improving road safety.
