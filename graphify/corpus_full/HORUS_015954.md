Delirare evokes images of a mad man in a field without any discernible plan. Patients with symptoms compatible with delirium have been described throughout antiquity in medical writings. Hippocrates described patients with frenitis, a syndrome characterized by confusion and restlessness that fluctuated in an unpredictable and unsteady manner and is associated with physical disease, often a febril disease (Gunther, Morandi and Ely, 2008; Morand et al., 2008). Delirium is an acute variation in the mental state (braindriplessness), with a fluctuding course, characterized by lack of attention and disorganized thinking (Bourne, 2008).
