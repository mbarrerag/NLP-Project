# Climate change: perceived effects and socio-territorial adaptation practices. Zone 7, municipality of Envigado-Colombia

The world lived daily, with its environmental dynamics, is source of knowledge of the climate as perceived, known, understood and assimilated reality. The perception arises from the subjective experience with the place, so, from the relationship of the people with the place as a joint space of emotions, identity, knowledge and action, the territorial experience with the climate and the ability to transform and adapt to the environment through the territorial socio-practices is explored.The general objective of this work is to interpret the territorial practices oriented to the adaptation to the climate change in Zone 7 of the municipality of Envigado-Colombia, based on the effects perceived by its inhabitants.
