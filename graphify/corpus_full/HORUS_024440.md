# PALEOPROFUNDS FOR THE PLACING OF AURIFERS ASSOCIATED TO THE COLOMBIAN CENOZOIAN MAGMATICAL ARK

This work compiles information obtained from the project "Digital Foot of Minerals", and the physical-chemical characteristics of the fluids of three important mining districts of the country are evaluated.The mineralisations are related to intrusive bar formed in the last 24 Ma, which allows to compare the depths of emplacement and the degree of exhumation that have suffered the hydrothermal systems through time.The mineralizations associated with the district of La Llanada, Nariño, are related to intrusives of age between 23 and 21 Ma, whose reported fluids show an enrichment in CO2, low to moderate salinities and training temperatures between 320 and 360 °C. On the other hand, the mineralizations of Suarez - Buenos Aires, Cauca, are related to intrusives and porfiritic rocks of ages between 18 and 8 Ma, whose fluids present CO2 in the lower proportion, low to moderate salinities with some local cases of hypersalinity, and temperatures of formation in an interval of 230 to 350 °C.The mineralizations associated to the district of Marmate - Quinchía, whose fluidity in the Middle Cauca are linked, are More shallow hydrothermal systems, with which it is necessary to consider these data when comparing the gold footprint of grains located in contemporary paleoplacers with the lifting and its relation to their possible source area. In addition, the location of the deposits currently exploited within the evolution of hydrothermal systems through the bark and its explorational perspectives in depth is indicated
