# STUDY OF RELATIONS BETWEEN AUDIOOLOGICAL RESULTS OF PSYCOACUSTIC, ELECTROACUSTIC AND ELECTROPHISIOLOGIC IN CHILDREN OF CERO FIVE YEARS

The stimulation of audiometric thresholds in paediatric patients under five years of age, who do not have functional behavioural repertoire for adult-like audiological evaluation, such as comprehension and follow-up of instructions, discrimination, attention and imitation, is a challenge for the audiologist due to the different measurement scales in decibels that require complex conversions, the use of test batteries to evaluate complementary aspects at different levels of the hearing pathway, the need to cross-check results and the effects of maturation, growth, nutrition and stimulation. The evolution of the hearing pathway denotes internal processes of myelinization, observable, in communication and motor manifestations that are increasingly thinning to pass from hearing to listening. From the above considerations, the following research question arises: What are the qualitative and quantitative relationships existing in the results of psychoacoustic, electroacoustic and electrophysiological audiologic tests, in children from zero to five years of age, taking into account the different scales of decibels, HL, SPL and NHL?
