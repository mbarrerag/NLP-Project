# BIOGENIC SILICE AS SUPPORT OF ISLATED SATIZERS (SACS) FOR PENOL OXIDATION IN RESIDEL WATER

Sustainable development is one of the goals of Colombian agribusiness and to achieve it, the generation of knowledge and the development of technologies that contribute to the advancement of environmentally sustainable production systems is required. The paddy rice industry is an important part of the economy of the country, and within its processing, the result is 16-25% of residue of rice husks per kg of green paddy rice [1,2]. With a production for the year 2022 of 2.94 Mt in Colombia [3,4] it is 0,47-0.73 Mt of contaminant rice husks for the environment and for human health. On the other hand, the unique characteristics of the rice husk in comparison with other agricultural residues: high content of silica in the ashes (87-97 %), high porosity and surface area, reactivity associated to the silanol groups (hydrogenous, isolated, geminal, neighborhood and terminal) in the surface considered as the activity sites (2,3), which makes it a valuable biomass, and efficient use of organic products. ethyl Wet Peroxide Oxidation (10,11). In the framework of the studies of the CWPO of phenol (one of the most representative pollutants and model molecules in the oxidation tests) different materials have been evaluated, among them: zeolites and other mesoporous solids (12_x0096_14) This research aims to contribute to environmental management with the use of catalysts of isolated atoms-Fec SACs using as a silica support obtained from rice husk ash in the oxidation of phenol present in waters. Aiming at the principles of sustainable development, a sustainable production process is guaranteed and the hydric resource is managed, valorizing the shell produced in rice mills and obtaining new catalysts of isolated Fe atoms for the treatment of toxic organic compounds such as phenol
