# DETERMINATION OF SPACE-TEMPORARY AREAS OF TRANSFER OF LEGUMINOUS SEMILLS FORGETHER IN ARGENTINE FRONT OF CLIMATE CHANGE

The conservation of plant genetic resources for fodder is important for the sustainability of the livestock industry in view of the impact of climate change. The spatial approach to delimiting seed transfer zones (ZTS) from maps of ecogeographic characterization of the territory (ELC) is a novel approximation for collection and conservation activities in the present thinking on the ecological restoration of populations of native species affected by climate change in the future. Under this approach, a map ELC was constructed for two wild fodder species of the genus Stylosanthes SW. (Fabaceae) for the current bioclimatic conditions of Argentina, which was projected in two future climate change scenarios to define possible spatial ZTS for the time. Geolocations of the collection of germplasm Stylo BGCTES and of public data bases (GBIF, genesys and herbarias of national models) were constructed for selecting bioclimatic, edaphic and geophysical variables associated with the adaptation and distribution of species.
