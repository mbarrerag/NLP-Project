# Sound as an articulating tool of classroom experiences

How to generate spaces of awareness, exploration and creation through sound objects in the classroom? Without doubt, one of the essential starting points for making music is sound. However, this cannot be reduced to that produced by traditional musical instruments; almost any object that we find in our environment, by putting it into vibration, produces sound that can be transformed and projected individually and grouply, when it comes to interacting with others. Based on such premises, this document presents the analyses, reflections and thoughts arising from my own experience in the territories transferred throughout my life, connected with the Master of Artistic Education, to bring artistic knowledge to my place of work, the district school El Libertador, located in the town of Rafael Uribe Uribe, El Libertador neighborhood, of the city of Bogotá, Colombia and to put it into practice with the students of the five cycle and a course of the three cycle. There, through free sound explorations, the research based on arts (IBA) and in the form of dialogues, different sound transformations were obtained and other elements of communication, such as a way to develop ideas, and work, my life, connected with the Master's Degree in Art Education, to bring artistic knowledge to my workplace, “El Libertador”, a public school located in “Rafael Uribe Uribe” location, at “El Libertador” neighborhood, in Bogotá, Colombia. This practice was carried out with students from fifth cycle and one course of the third one. There, through free sound explorations, and considering the Arts-Based Research (ABR), and dialogues, different sound transformations and other communication elements were obtained to generate ideas, work on them, and materialize them within a collaborative, creative, free, autonomous, and empathology
