# PREVENTION PROGRAMME FOR PROFESSIONAL FUTBOLISTS FROM RISK STRATIFICATION

Football is one of the most popular sports, therefore, has been the object of research and scientific evidence regarding the different factors surrounding it. A relevant aspect are sports injuries, their epidemiology and prevention. In this regard, Vicente et al. (2019) suggest that both predisposed risks of injuries and injuries derived from professional sport require knowledge and specialized treatment of the sciences involved. Likewise, they suggest that scientific evidence collects publications referring to specific aspects of the professional practice of sport, its risks and injuries, but epidemiological studies oriented towards more effective preventive actions are not sufficient. Due to the number of individuals playing football in the world (about 250 million people), any reduction in the incidence of injuries will have a benefit on the health of the groups practicing this sport.
