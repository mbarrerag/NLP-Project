# Evaluation of palm oil phytonutrient extraction in the process of ethanol extraction refining in descending film contactors

The presence of free fatty acids in edible oils is unwanted, because they give it organoleptic characteristics that are not desired by consumers, by thermally degrading promote the browning of oil and by being consumed promote the appearance of cancer cells. Although the conventional refining process eliminates them, it does not eliminate precursors of carcinogenic molecules, promotes the formation of carcinogenic derivatives and thermally decomposes phytonutrients as antioxidants and vitamins. To avoid these drawbacks in sensitive applications, the UN Chemical and Biochemical Process Group developed a process to remove free fatty acids, eliminate cancer precursors, avoid the operating conditions that promote them and extract a part of the phytonutrients. In this project it is proposed to evaluate the effect of the conditions of extraction already established on the quantity of phytonutrients (vitamins and antioxidants) both in alcohol and in the refined oil. To this end, the new oil fatty acids will be affected by the conditions of extraction already defined conditions and will be characterized by the efluent phases of the phytonutrients, which will be evaluated with respect to the quality of the plant
