# ANALYSIS OF THE LABORAL AND HEALTH CONDITIONS BY PANDEMIA COVID-19 IN INFORMAL WORKERS IN BOGOTA

To analyze the implications of the pandemic by COVID-19 on the working and health conditions of workers in the informal sector in Bogotá. Hypothesis: The COVID-19 increased the inequalities and difficulties in the working and health conditions of workers in the informal sector. Cross-section with application of questionnaires Occupational History and Working Conditions, Employment and Health (CTESLAC) in December-2021. Sample composed of informal workers in public space of the localities with the highest concentration in Bogotá: Santa Fe, Kennedy and San Cristobal; with minimum age of five years and whose related main activity is the sale of food, varieties and clothing. Descriptive and differential analysis by subgroups of interest. 191 informal sellers participated in the area of performance of daily life activities, 29.8% varieties and 35.1% clothes; with current general age of 43.2 years, 16.8 years of exercise in informality and 78.5% belonging to one/two strata. At occupational level, between 12 and 16% show light to moderate difficulties in the area of performance of the daily life activities.
