# On justice: elements of proportionality and reciprocity in modern hegemonic theories of justice in law, economics and politics

The research critically questions hegemonic authors of social theory (Locke, Smith, Beccaria, Bentham, Maistre, Hume, Marx and Kropotkin) with respect to the elements of proportionality and reciprocity of these elements with the aim of using these elements for a new theory of justice
