# MERCURY ADVISORY ON LEVEL NIRRARY NANOPARTICLES SUPPORTED IN FIBRE OF FIQUE: CYNETIC AND ADSORRATION ISOTERM

The adsorption of mercury ions from an aqueous solution is studied on an adsor- bente (fibre with iron nanoparticles). The effect of pH, initial con-centering of mercury ions ([Hg+2]) and iron load (Fe % weight) on the adsorbing material were studied. From the experimental response surfaces obtained it is inferred that mercury adsorption predominates the mechanism of chemomercury over fissionation. Langmuir and Freundlich isotherms were used to describe the physical adsor-tion of mercury, however, they failed to adequately describe the majority of experimental isotherms obtained. It was established that the adsorption kineticsation of mercury on the surface of fibre with iron nanoparticles is described by a model of pseudo-second order involving the chemomercism (chemical reaction) as a mechanism of control of velocity, indicating that the adsorption process is irreversible.
