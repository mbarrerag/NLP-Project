# Construction and partial characterization of the mutant Mycobacterium tuberculosis in membrane conveyors CtpF and MmpL7, with vaccine potential.

In the search for new vaccines that prevent TB, several strategies have been used, among them, as recommended by WHO, the design and obtaining of mutants attenuated has aroused a lot of interest, since this type of strains has shown the advantage of a greater stimulation of the immune response, since it is a live attenuated bacteria. Once inoculated in the individual, the live vaccines have the possibility to continue to repel (Hernandez Pando et al., 2006), therefore, the active attenuated bacteria possess the phenotypic characteristics immunodominant of the wild bacteria, also producing the maximum effect, imitating the natural infection process. In this sense, for the case of Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb) is very relevant in a living vaccine the presence of immunomodulating antigens widely known as ESAT6, CFP10, Ag85, MPB64, MPB70 and MPB83. In this regard, for the case of the biological processes that lead to the survival of the tuberculous bacillus in conditions of infection is critical in the development of mutants attenuated Mbs. and with capacity to resist stress conditions (Jackson et al., 2007).
