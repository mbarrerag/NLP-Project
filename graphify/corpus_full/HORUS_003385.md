# THE PATIENT: CENTRAL ELEMENT IN THE TRAINING OF FARMACIA STUDENTS

The goal of the Farmacia course is to train professionals with knowledge and skills in the management of medicines. Hospital Pharmacy II is a practical subject that exposes the student to the hospital context. Hospital practice is a unique opportunity for students to strengthen knowledge, but the hospital environment also involves several challenges. This study seeks to identify elements necessary in the course design to enhance the experience and lead students to significant learning. qualitative study of type research-action. It was developed in five stages: (i) Initial evaluation; (ii) Initial modification; (ii) Impact assessment; (iv) Second modification and (v) Final impact assessment. The data were obtained from individual interviews and focus groups with students and field notes of the teacher. several difficulties were observed in the development of the course, associated with its practical nature and the characteristics of the students. The importance of having clear and consistent learning objectives was identified.
