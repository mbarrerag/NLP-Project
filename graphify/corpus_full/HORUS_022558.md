# Relationship between dispositional variables in relation to risky driving behaviours and the occurrence of traffic offences in a sample of Bogotan drivers

The psychology of transit has undertaken efforts to understand the psychosocial variables that are linked to the occurrence of traffic offences, understanding these as risk factors specific to the behaviors of the users of the route. Such risk factors have been in the public agenda of several countries that have joined the global strategies to counteract the fatalities in the transit. Hence the importance of approaching this topic and include in its analysis, variables such as the attitudes towards risk, perception of difficulty in driving or frequency to commit infringements. It was counted with the participation of 260 drivers, to which 5 instruments were applied to evaluate risky and unsafe behaviors in the route. With the data obtained, it was intended to establish relationships between the scores of the scales and variables as the occurrence of reported infringements, dividing the sample into groups of offenders according to the current categories of the National Transit Code. The results obtained indicate that there is no relation between the attitudes considered and the frequency of incurred in dangerous behaviors or to have more traffic infringements.
