# Study of the K} S-wave amplade near threshold in the D+ → K −K +K + Decay

The description of scalar mesons has been one of the challenges open in spectroscopia of light mesons in the last decades. In particular, the study of scalar states, such as the $f_{0}(980)$, has been of great interest given the little understanding that is held on these. In this context, the disintegrations to three bodies of enchanted mesons, studied by the use of the so-called analysis of the diagrams of Dalitz, have been presented as excellent laboratories to examine the nature of those states. A promising channel to carry out those investigations in the LHCb is the disintegration $rightarrow K-}KK, which can provide valuable information about the extent of the S wave in the system. This amplitude is crucial for the estimation of the fractions of disintegration and of the couplings of the Kū system.
