Introduction Congenital craniofacial syndromes have been widely described due to their precursor nature of alterations in the skull-cervic-mandibular system; specifically the malformations at the condyl-mandibular level, such as hypoplasia or aplasia of these structures, compromise one of the vital functions for the human being: feeding. With the aim of establishing a practice guide, clinical guidelines are proposed that promote the phonoaudiological practice based on evidence. Patients and methods A systematic review of the type of scoping review was carried out in the main biomedical databases, in search of scientific articles aimed at the evaluation of feeding and swallowing in infants with hypoplasia and condylo-mandibular aplasia associated with craniofacial syndromes. Results 37 articles of type randomized clinical trial, case study, ecological study, metaanalysis and systematic review, whose content is presented and organized within an evaluation format that seeks to obtain anatomofunctional information of the minor infants for the development of their clinical pathology were selected. Conclusions A small amount of parameters and dissemination of phonological evidence is presented and organized within a general evaluation of evaluation, which seeks to obtain an evaluation of the results
