# Influence of spatial and temporal distribution of precipitation cycles and other environmental factors on human morbidity and mortality leptospirosis in Colombia, 2007 – 2017.

The disease transmission is dependent on its excretion by animal reservoirs and the presence of an environment of humidity which allows the survival of the bacteria. A retrospective study was carried out to describe the sociodemographic and meteorological patterns of human leptospirosis reported to the National Institute of Health between 2007 and 2017 through SIVIGILA, to determine if there is a possible association between the occurrence of the cases confirmed by laboratory of leptospirosis and meteorological factors such as rain, temperature and solar radiation. The meteorological data were collected from the databases of the Institute of Hydrology, Meteorology and Environmental Studies (IDEAM). The analysis of the sociodemographic and meteorological variables was carried out by means of descriptive statistics and correlation tests of Pearson in Excel.
