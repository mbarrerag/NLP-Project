# Effects of the armed conflict on multidimensional poverty in the municipalities most affected by violence in Colombia

This document estimates the effect of violence associated with the armed conflict on multidimensional poverty in the municipalities of the southwestern part of the country, considering the prioritization framework of PDET municipalities. To this end, a panel that captures municipal information from the last 8 years prior to the signing of the Peace Agreement with the FARC-EP is carried out and a Municipalized Multidimensional Poverty Index - IPMM (adjusted for availability of municipal indicators) is constructed as a measure of multidimensional poverty. The results show a negative effect, of a greater accentuation of violence associated with the armed conflict on multidimensional poverty
