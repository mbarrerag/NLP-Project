# COMPOSITIONAL CHARACTERISTICS AND SHELF-LIFE OF INTERSPECIFIC HYBRID PALM OIL

The interspecific hybrid E. oleiferaXE. guineensis (OXG) has a high phytonutrient content provitamin A such as a- and ß-carotene and antioxidants compounds (vitamin E) such as a-, ß+¿- and d-tocotrienols, a-, ß+¿- and d- tocopherols (Vitamin E). Both groups of compounds have nutritional and bioactive potential for the food industry. The present study aimed at characterizing the phytonutrient content of hybrid crude palm oil and evaluate the stability and shelf life of these compounds under controlled storage conditions. Phytochemical compounds and oxidative index were evaluated by total carotenoids content, free fatty acid, peroxide index and p-anisidine index as an initial characterization. The results showed that hybrid crude palm oil has 540.53 ± 1.71 ppm, average content of total carotenoids, 2.33 ± 0.22 initial free fatty acids value, and no peroxide and p-anisidine index values were reported, which is among on the standard parameters for palm oil. The hybrid crude palm oil stored at 60°C reported the major stability index changes where a slightly decrease of total carotenoids content were evidenced, reaching values of 516.75 ± 8.38 ppm and significant (P<0,05) variations in peroxide index was noticed, reaching values of 57.77 ± 2.93 meq/kg when samples were storage for 5 weeks. Under this conditions, free fatty acids remained constant, nevertheless at 40 and 50°C, these variations decreased. These results evidenced the effect of temperature on hybrid palm oil parameters, moreover, these results allowed to consider future technological conditions in food processing and open the possibility to explore more about possible applications of this nutritional oil in food industry
