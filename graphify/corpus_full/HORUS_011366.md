# ♪ To stand up ♪ ♪ BOTTLES: ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ To ♪ ♪ To ♪

Activity aimed at fostering creativity, where engineering students aim to integrate their theoretical and practical knowledge into themes such as mechanical design, dynamics, materials science, machine elements, among other subjects of the discipline training of engineering, into a playful activity. The purpose of this activity is to open in the shortest possible time a bottle containing a gaseous drink that is in cold state, as far as possible at the same temperature of the liquid for all players; the glass container is of personal size with capacity 350 milliliters, where all players use the same type of bottle, which is located two meters away from the players and consuming the liquid of the drink. This activity is carried out to integrate the theoretical and practical knowledge developed in the disciplined subjects in the engineering programs, this activity will be carried out in an open space at the University of Antioquia
