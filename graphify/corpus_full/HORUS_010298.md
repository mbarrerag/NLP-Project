# Relationship between political systems and the POP index in 62 countries during the period 1970-2010

to determine the relationship of political systems and their trajectories, according to the classification of Navarro and Shi, with the COP index in 62 countries during the period 1970-2010. a retrospective ecological design was used. For this purpose, countries were selected that fulfilled at least three records of those classified in the C.O.P index at 12 years of age in the period 1970-2010. Once the sample of countries was defined, information was sought on the political trajectories of each country to characterize the political system and oral health conditions. The analysis of the information was carried out from crossing and triangular data of the presence of cavities in countries with each category of the political system or regime. the social and macrostructural political determinants that organize health systems influence the well-being of the population of a country.
