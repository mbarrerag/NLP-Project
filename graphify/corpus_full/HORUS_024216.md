# CARBON ENTERPRISES IN BOYACA AND CUNDINAMARCA STRATEGIC OR REACTIVE GERENCE?

They have designed and selected strategies with the intention of dynamizing their decision-making. However, this set of actions that the companies have adopted, due to the particular situation of the environment in which they are immersed, have focused on the problems of the day to day and in responding quickly to the cyclical challenges.
