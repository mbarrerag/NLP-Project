# Characterization of circulating immunosenescence and its correlation with lung function in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)

The increase in life expectancy and growth of population over 60 years of age in Colombia and the world, are the result of better socioeconomic conditions, advances in public health policies and decrease of births.However, aging carries different types of physiological deterioration, for example, it can affect the ability of the immune system to respond to infectious agents, as well as the ability of immunovigilance necessary to remove apoptotic and senescent cells that accumulate with age and that may be favoring the pathological remodeling and fibrosis in the tissues. As a result of the immune alterations associated with aging or immunosenescence, older adults may be more susceptible and vulnerable to respiratory infections and diseases such as Cronica Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) and idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) patients. The IPF is a progressive course disease, which occurs mainly in older adults and has a mean survival after diagnosis of only 3 years.
