# Transformers and processes of reconfiguration of the ways of living in territories with implementation of energy solutions in ZNI of Colombia: The case of the co-ordination of Isla Fuerte, Cartagena D. T., department of Bolívar and the sidewalk Buenavista Altorredondo, municipality of Paratebueno, Cundinamarca

This thesis addresses three transversal components to the modes of living, agency, transformation processes and reconfiguration processes in the modes of living that are based on adaptation processes. Each component interconnects itself by provoking successive configurations in a process of change and permanence. The thesis contrasts two cases that allow to distinguish the processes of agency, transformation and adaptation in two territories with geographical, cultural and organizational differences. The agency, is the first element reconfiguration of the modes of living, although in one of them it is presented as strong lines of escape that strengthen processes of reterritorialization, in the other it is presented as fragile lines of escape, whose forms of agency are incomplete and do not give way to reterritorialization processes. Between the actions of the inhabitants and the actions of their organizations, there is nothing that compensates for the escape, that is to say no decisions, nor action taken to avoid segmentity, since that inaction instead of uniting, separates, because what causes those types of escape, in which there is no arrojo -and takes away the willingness to act, to create a species, to develop a given and to develop their own territory instead of clarification processes, in the other it is present as fragile lines of escape, what forms of agency result in complete and do not give way to reterritorialization processes.
