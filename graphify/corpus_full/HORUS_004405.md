# Geostatistical assessment of compliance with the percentage of carbon recovery in the Annex - Cerrejon

The successful development of a mining project depends directly on the effectiveness in the estimates of reserves and in the quality of the mineral, which are generated from the geological information, is therefore that this research seeks to evaluate the volumetric differences between the actual extraction of the Annex regional pit and the estimated reserves, using geostatistical methods applicable in mining of coal mantles. Initially an exploratory analysis of data of the regionalized variables, ash, sulphur and thickness was carried out, where from histograms the spatial impact of the values was defined. The study was elaborated using the academic license of the Isatis Neo Mining software acquired by the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia. Then a structural analysis was carried out for the ash variable consisting of the creation of an experimental variogram adjusted to a mathematical model in which its dependence and continuity was interpreted, then the estimate was made using traditional techniques as the reverse of the distance to the square (IDC) and the geostatistic methods as the regular block (KOB) and Sequential Gaussssian (SSG) were compared from the estimates to yes, the analysis of the d variables, ash, sulphur and thickness, was carried out, where the type of spatial distribution of the values was defined based on histograms.
