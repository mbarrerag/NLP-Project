# PREPARATION OF QUANTICAL CARBON POINTS FOR TEACHING ABOVEMENT AND ENERGY EMISSION CONCEPTS

This paper presents an innovative didactic strategy implemented for students of basic chemistry in middle school with the aim of teaching abstract and hard to understand concepts in chemistry, in a more attractive and accessible way. The strategy is based on the use of practical experiences in the classroom, using nanotechnology as a differentiating tool. It focuses on the preparation of carbon quantum points through a simple but fascinating process, to experimentally address the concepts of energy absorption and emission. The strategy begins with the identification of necessary previous concepts and the introduction of new concepts related to carbon quantum points. It then takes place the development phase, where students follow a working guide to prepare quantum points and discuss the relationship between what is observed and the concepts learned. Finally, an evaluation tool is used to measure the results obtained, and it shows that the strategy has significantly improved understanding of concepts such as absorption and energy emission. Additionally, students have the opportunity to learn about nanotechnology and create structures with technological and environmental impact. It offers a refreshing and attractive approach to learning chemistry
