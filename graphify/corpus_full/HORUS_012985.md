# Regression models 2-Beta and 2-Beta binomial

This article proposes new parametrizations of beta and beta binomial distributions as functions of the parameters of mean and variance. Based on these new parametrizations, new models of linear regression beta and beta binomial are formulated assuming that real appropriate functions of mean and variance follow linear regression structures. These models were adjusted to actual data sets by applying Bayesian methods, using OpenBUGS software. The new models of beta regression were adjusted to the set of precision reading data of children with dyslexia and the new models of beta binomial regression applied to the set of absenteeism school data. In both cases, the results obtained by adjusting these models were compared with those obtained by adjusting the usual models of mean and dispersion beta regression and the models of mean and dispersion beta-binomial regression, respectively
