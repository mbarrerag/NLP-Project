# Financial planning and value generation: a literature review

This is documentary-type research, which explores through a literature review of fifty-six biographical references from the last fifteen years: the incidence between the value generation in organizations and the application of financial planning in the development of their short- and long-term activities. For that purpose, Scopus and Web of Science were the investigation’s citation database and using the corresponding methodology it was enable collect, sort out and characterize the selected references, As a result, it presents the conceptualization of the different research contributions, followed by the identification of the tools that financial planning has at hand for its long-term exercises and that are in trend, up to the enumeration of the effects that this financial exercise contributes to the generation of financial value to the organizations
