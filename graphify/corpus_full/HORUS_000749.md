# Rocks in fault zones

In civil infrastructure projects, where the geology and civil engineering must work together, characterizing and identifying units and processes of direct impact for the works, it is essential to establish, at a minimum, a common language that is understandable for the optimal development of the projects. The correct use of terminology for the processes and materials present in a zone of mechanical deformation (dynamic metamorphism), as well as the origin and part of their behavior are presented in this work. Terms or words as sheared rock, gaps, milonites, faults, etc., will be characterized and exemplified with places where the basic characteristics can be observed that help to define and understand them more easily
