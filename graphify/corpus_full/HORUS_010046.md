# BINARY TWO-DIMENSIONAL IMAGE CLASSIFICATION: TENSOR REGRESSION MODEL VS TOTAL VARIATION MODEL

Image processing, pattern recognition, and classification systems through images have become widely studied due to technological development. A frequent topic is the analysis of medical images, which consists of identifying regions of interest in the body that are relevant to specific clinical responses. This problem can be seen from the statistical point of view as a regression model where the exogenous variables are the brain images, and the patient's state is the response or endogenous variable. In such a way, the regression coefficients correspond to certain signals that activate in specific body parts. In the context of binary two-dimensional image classification, this conference introduces the tensor regression model and the total variation model, compares the results of their performance from a simulation study, and illustrates their application with a real example
