# EVALUATION OF THE EFFECT OF CONSUMPTION OF FORTIFIED YOUGURS WITH SOURCES OF VITAMIN AND OMEGA 3, ON CLASTIC BIOMARKERS OF CARDIOVOSCULARY DISEASE AND LEVELS IN VITAMIN BLOOD AND ADULT POPULATION

The food profile of Western culture is characterized by a high energy density, mainly due to high intake of sugar and saturated fatty acids. These dietary practices, combined with a low intake of food sources of essential fatty acids [AGE], fat-soluble vitamins, fiber and phytonutrients, have been linked as a risk factor for the development of cardiovascular diseases [ECV], which are the leading cause of death in the world (WHO, 2017). To improve the intake of AGE and vitamin E, one of the strategies is to vehiculate AGE in mass-consumption foods. A good alternative is to add or enrich products such as dairy derivatives with oils with a good nutritional profile. This work evaluated the effect of the consumption of two fermented dairy beverages fortified with ASI and AHP, on the lipid profile, APO A and B, and vitamin E, in individuals at risk of ECV
