# SIMULATION IN THE TEACHING OF DISEASE DURING PANDEMIA.

The keynote conference focused on the use of the pedagogical strategy of simulation in nursing teaching in the pandemic, where different modalities were used in different countries including research results of satisfaction, effectiveness in the use of these modalities. Finally, the experience in the simulation laboratory at the Faculty of Nursing of the National University of Colombia is presented
