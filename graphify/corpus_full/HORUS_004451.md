# EPISTEMOLOGICAL CONSIDERATIONS ON CASE STUDY FOR ORGANIZATIONAL RESEARCH

The objective here set out is to present the epistemological considerations that validate the methodology of the Ccaso Study in organizational research. To this end, this part of the book discusses the ontology of the organizational social and the relevance of the Ccaso Study methodology to the disciplines of that field of study. With the elements raised here, it is expected that the application of epistemological consideration will provide a solid support for the process of design, collection and processing of data from a case study and mark a logical, traceable and defensible path towards its results
