# CORROSION AND WEAR RESISTANCE ANALYSIS OF ALCRTIN-CU COATINGS DEPOSITED BY HIPIMS

Due to their physical and chemical properties, functional materials have been widely investigated in the last years. Adding soft metals have been shown to offer greatly enhanced mechanical and electrochemical properties. TiCrAlN-Cu coatings with different Copper (Cu) contents were deposited by HiPIMS on WC-Co and 4340 steel substrates. The influence of the copper content on morphology, structure and corrosion behaviour was investigated using a field emission scanning electron microscope (FE- SEM), X-ray diffraction (XRD), transmission electron microscopy (TEM) and potentiodynamic anodic polarization curves and electrochemical impedance spectroscopy and The wear resistance of the films was investigated using a pin on disk tribometer. The preliminary results indicated that the coatings are mainly composed of AlN and their structure has a pronounced orientation. In a 3.5% NaCl electrolyte, the corrosion resistance of the films increases as the copper content increases, then decreases with the addition of Cu up to a critical value. Transmission electron microscopy shows a low density of the coatings and a gap between columnar structures. The corrosion and wear mechanisms in the films will be discussed in this work
