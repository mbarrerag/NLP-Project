# PART I. ENVIRONMENT, HABITAT AND FOOD. ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY FROM A PROPOSAL FOR PUBLIC POLICY FROM THE CENTRE OF THOUGHT IN ETHICS FOR ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY.

The environmental problem that Colombia is facing is complex, historical and can be understood as a phenomenon that concerns multiple sectors of society, including academic, productive, educational, political, ethnic, community, among others, because it is recognized that the appropriation of nature to achieve economic growth is exceeding the capacity of load and resilience of the planet (WWWF,2015). The Center of Thought in Ethics for Environmental Sustainability from an interdisciplinary perspective STEAM+H, within its strategic objectives, during the first year of creation 2019, was proposed the formulation of two proposals of public policy the first is called: Proposal of Public Policy in Education Ethics for Environmental Sustainability (PPSEA). The proposal PPESA examines the educational guidelines defined in the US strategy for the strengthening and development of the science, technology, engineering and mathematics of STEM (2018), and promotes those that are applicable in the national context. Likewise, it studies the guidelines proposed by the Organization of American States in which additionally include some that involve strategies for the development of the arts STEAM (OAS, 2019). This perspective considers the ethics as a transversal element of the interaction in the national context. opportunities for improvement in order to strengthen public policy in this area
