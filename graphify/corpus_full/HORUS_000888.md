# IN MENT HEALTH WITH INTERCULTURE APPROACH

Mental health is a field of nursing action and interculturality is even more so. Providing evidence-based comprehensive care to the original or ethnic communities is among its functions and roles. To describe nursing perspectives in mental health with an intercultural focus in Latin American countries. Qualitative study, descriptive design, cross-sectional, the sample was selected for convenience. In-depth interviews, prior informed consent, were conducted until theoretical saturation and content analysis was performed
