# EVOLUTION OF THE METHODOLOGY OF MÉTRIC LEVANTATION. THREE CASE STUDIES 1995, 2006, 2022

The evolution of the processes of graphic documentation of the heritage also represents an advance in the study of the past. Through new techniques, technologies and equipment, it is possible to achieve more precise and complete results. This evolution is presented through three cases, realized in different decades, making a comparison between its objectives, methodologies, instruments and results. First, Cloister and Church of San Francisco in Villa de Leyva (1995), was developed by manual methods, implementing leveling of spaces and lateral measurements by summarization and diagonals, triangulating each zone for its graphic restitution. A two-dimensional planimetry was obtained to perform archaeological, historical, identification of lesions and materials in plans of qualification, diagnosis and the proposal of restoration implemented. Second, lifting of Lanceros in the Pantano de Vargas (2006), were used photográfi techniques, topographic measurement, drawing with levels and bindings on the sculptures, giving result, bidimensional planes and materials in plans of the whole and of the 15 sculptures. Second, the removal of Lanceros in the Pantano de Vargas (2006), were used as a means of identifying lesions, diagnosis and intervention.
