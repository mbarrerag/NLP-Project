# PROPOSAL FOR A CONCEPTUAL MODEL FOR TRAINING IN INNOVATION AND EARLY ANALYSIS STRATEGIES

The main objective of this study is to propose a conceptual model for the formation of innovation strategies, which recognizes an emerging strategy, in which the organization responds to the spontaneous emergence of innovation opportunities. The conceptual model is empirically analyzed in nine innovative companies in Colombia, seeking to identify the main components of the model in its innovation management processes. Empirical analysis shows that these companies have an important level of development of the elements of the conceptual model, highlighting the emerging actions they use as part of their strategic innovation process. Conceptual analysis shows that the innovation strategy has been studied mainly deliberately, so there are still gaps in the literature to understand the emerging form of this type of strategy. It is concluded that it is necessary to deepen the emerging innovation strategy to establish a theoretical basis for better understanding the formation of innovation strategies. Key words: formation of innovation strategies; emerging strategy; innovation management; innovative companies analysis; conceptual model;
