# Potential of the strategy marks territory for responsible tourism

The document carries out a theoretical inquiry and analysis in the concepts of land branding and responsible tourism in order to establish an integration between these concepts, resulting from identifying that the territory brand as a cultural product is constituted as a strategy at a territorial level with the potential to orient tourism to a responsible practice with the territories. Finally, guidelines are proposed to contribute and orient the process of land branding in a way that motivates responsible tourism .
