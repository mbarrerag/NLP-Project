# Analysis of the Radical Change party and its relationship with the political trajectories of Germán Vargas Lleras and Alejandro Char (1998-2023)

The relationship between political parties and leadership has not occupied a preponderant place within political studies. This master's thesis in Political Studies and International Relations contributes the analysis of the Radical Change party, characterizing it from its origin, its electoral results and its ideological position and in relation to the careers of two professional politicians who have had a remarkable performance as members of the party. It explores their role of leadership within CR and the possible tensions between them, both as part of the Colombian political class. Party and politicians have been able to remain in the electoral scene partly because of the relationship between them; politicians because they have found a structure in which to consolidate their political career and the party because they have found leadership and electoral flows important to remain in force. .
