# EVALUATION OF SIMULTANE MECHANISMS AND PHYSICAL CHEMICAL AND BIOLOGICAL INDICATORS DURING THE IMMOVILIZATION OF TOXIC METALS BY PRECIPTITATION OF MICROBIOLOGICALLY INDUSTRY INDUSTRY INDUSTRIAL CARBONATES

The MICP technology (Microbiologically Induced Carbonate Precipitation) allows the precipitation of toxic metallic carbonates (axis. Cd and Pb) mediated by bacteria thus decreasing the availability of these elements in the environment. Considering that the bacteria can interact in different forms with metals, the various mechanisms that can occur simultaneously with the MICP process have been little explored, particularly the processes of biosorption and bioaccumulation.In this sense, it is important to study the MICP process taking into account the extracellular and intracellular immobilization and the different interactions of the bacteria with the metal, in order to better know the contribution of each type of immobilization during the MICP process and thus understand and propose hypotheses related to the stability of the long term immobilization. To determine the contribution of other mechanisms (bioadsorption and bioaccumulation) during removal of the Cd(II) in solution, the distribution of the removed Cd was evaluated by bioprecipation and biosorption tests. 6 previously isolated strains were used, considering the removal capacity of Cd1 and its different gender. The evaluation of the mechanisms was carried out through the quantification of Pb(II) in 5 different fractions within the studied system (remnant Pb, biosorbed Pb, Pb adsorbed Pb adsorbed, Pb precipitated, and Pb bioaccumulated). These tests found that, under the selected cultivation conditions, the removal of Pb(II) in solution for two of the isolates is mainly given through the biosorption of the metal in the cell wall or in the exopolysaccharides producing these bacteria, and the percentage of the metal associated with the carbonated fraction is between 13,5 and 15.2%. For the other isolate, the percentage of Pb(II) removed associated with the carbonate fraction was also performed.
