# OUR URBAN HOUSE OF MONTAÑA

This book presents an analysis on the relations of the domestic architecture built between the 1950s and 1970s and the geographical characteristics of the city of Manizales. The work presents an important and novel examination on the organic link between the territory -the topos- and the different types of construction that were created in accordance with this. Particularly, the cases of the firms Vélez-Villegas, Gutiérrez Arango, Gómez Botero and Gómez-Ángel, who designed houses in the Manizale mountains under two typologies: to break the house to adjust it to the slope of the land from what arises the house-scale and the house-rampa-and regularize the soil to flatten the house of what results the house-podio-, The authors of this book investigate regarding the variations and invariant of these two types, that is to say, the structure that they share and that in what they differ.
