# Plan of action based on the 607-C4 component project for strengthening the technical and technological infrastructure in the laboratories of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Bogotá

The plan seeks to strengthen the technical and technological capacities of laboratories in order to benefit at least a total of 46 laboratories from Headquarters for the years 2023 and 2024, distributing resources in an equitable manner in order to meet the most felt and priority needs presented by both faculties and institutes. This proposal in its general form is presented and submitted to the Laboratory Committee of Bogotá Headquarters, and then the laboratory coordinators of Faculty and Institute carry out within their respective units the census of the most felt needs that are framed within the guidelines of the National Directorate of Research and Laboratories, and the prioritization of support is carried out to the budgetary availability. From the Faculty of the University of Bogotá, information is consolidated and the minimum criteria established in the call is carried out: _x0095_ Percentage of progress of the laboratory registered in HERMES at 100% _x0095_ To attend laboratories that have not been attended in the triennium 2019-2021 (as the plan is possible according to the number of laboratories of the Faculty or Institute) _x0095_ To strengthen the technological equipment and equipments for acquisition by the construction and construction and construction of the construction of the facilities
