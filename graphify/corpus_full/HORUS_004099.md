# Housing prices in Medellin: short-term or structural urban crisis?

The price of housing has been the subject of discussion and concern in Medellín in the 2023 electoral year. Although there are multiple arguments and even social representations about its causes and implications, they all point to it being a conjunctural, momentary problem restricted to the rental market. This article is based on the hypothesis that it is actually a conjunctural manifestation of a housing and urban crisis of a structural nature that has lasted for years and extends to all access circuits to housing. Thus, the objective of the article is to analyze both in an aggregate way and from spatio-temporal variations in the city of Medellín, structural and conjunctural components of the increase in new housing prices between 2004 and 2022. The temporal and spatial scope of general and particular structural movements of new housing prices in Medellín was identified, for different social groups and in Non-VIS and VIS housing segments, with notable implications for the middle and lower classes. This allows us to conclude that it is a structural urban and housing crisis tending to a growing socio-spatial elitization
