# Comparison of habitat preferences of Catasetum bicolor and C. ochraceum (Catasetinae: Orchidaceae): implications for its conservation

The Orchidaceae family is widely recognized for mutualistic relations with its pollinators, however, knowledge about this group of monocotyledones, the most diverse of the planet, is still limited. Here the ecological requirements were recognized and habitat preferences of Catasetum bicolor and C. ochraceum were modeled. Climate data, bioclimatic and presence records were used in a) Analysis of Main Components (ACP) to identify the most influential variables; and, b) Maximum Entropy algorithm (Maxent) in modelling of suitable habitats. In addition, the conservation status and possible priority areas of monitoring and conservation for these species were identified. Both species differ in their habitat preferences according to their altitude range, these variables were associated with the ACP (average, minimum, seasonality), solar radiation, and precipitation of the most dry and warmer areas.
