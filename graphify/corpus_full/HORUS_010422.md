# Predictive model of unbundling of guarantees of white line products over time.

At present, in a competitive and globalized context, it is essential that companies manage the quality of the products and services they market, since this has a high impact on the costs associated with the lack of quality and on the reputation of the brand vis-à-vis distributors, competitors and customers. In the market for the marketing of goods there is a legal requirement to provide assurance on the distributed products, which occurs from the moment the product reaches the consumer's hands and is subjected to normal conditions of use, until the period of time agreed by the company at the time of sale is fulfilled. It is essential for the team responsible for managing the quality to know the performance of what is being produced, so that estimation of the performance in the field is essential to take timely corrective action on the process or product. In this work, a predictive model of disaggregation of guarantees of white line products in the time that considers variable as the number of guarantees at defined time periods, sales and production that aims to provide information with which corrective actions could be made, effectiveness of actions already materialized, forecast of financial resources to respond for services in guarantee, performance of raw materials and suppliers, facilitating the value of the company, among other
