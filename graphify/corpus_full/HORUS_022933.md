# PERMEABLE COURSES: STOP ART AND SCIENCE IN DIALOGUE WITH THE WORK OF EULALIA DE VALDENEBRO.

Given the various manifestations of crises that go through ecosystems and infrastructures at a global level, the challenge of how to create vital connections with the environment is presented to us today urgent and ineluctable. Centuries of rapacious human intervention in biodiverse territories, the domination of urban centers over rural communities, together with the isolation of the academies of popular knowledge, have been forming a world that the thinker michel serres defined as a landscape made to scraps and made pieces. Counter this scenario and living in it, requires spaces of awareness, dissemination and dialogue that can cultivate a broad consensus on the value of biodiversity and incentivize practices that resume the broken link between the health of ecosystems and the well-being of the planet.As the Humboldt Institute proposes in its Operational Plan, so that the value of ecosystems is sowed in the social fabric that they lack strategies of vital connection with the various lives, beings and communities that we root on Earth, generating confluences between disciplines and communities that are often considered as separate.From between bodies, we develop creative methodologies to connect the arts and sciences, communities and society
