# ART AS A FORM OF KNOWLEDGE IN COLLABORATIVE PROJECTS WITH SOCIAL INCIDENCY. REFLECTIONS TO BE TAKEN FROM THE PROJECT MUSEUM + COMMUNITY OF THE ANTIOCIA MUSEUM (2011-2013)

The chapter proposes that art is a form of specific knowledge that provides ways of doing, experiences and methodologies in collaborative initiatives that seek transformation or incidence. From a long-term project called Museum + Community, led by the Museum of Antioquia between 2011 and 2013 is it in collaboration with Corporación Convivamos and other organizations, the author explains details of the initiative and analyses how from the discourses of organizations and institutions committed to the longings for social change in territories, memory or the repair of the social fabric, art is often seen as a technique at the service of the territories. However, from the practices and resources of the actors directly engaged in the project "Museum+Community", legacies of knowledge associated with artistic practices are glimpsed, particularly those who are committed to the experiences of being together. In the end, the text calls attention to the need to encourage the debate and critical training of artists in this type of practices, and in a story located of this type of initiatives, such that epistemological recognition of their contributions in Colombia to the profuso collection of initiatives of this type of this type of initiative is evident
