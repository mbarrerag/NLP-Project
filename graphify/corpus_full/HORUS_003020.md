# TOTAL PRODUCTIVE MAINTENANCE (TBM) IMPLEMENTATION IN PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION LABORATORIES FOR OPTIMISSING DEEMBORATION AND QUALITY AS IN ISO 17025:2017

The laboratories that belong to the professional educational context in engineering are important in the development of tools for learning, research and innovation. For this reason it is important that they maintain a high quality standard, so that the ISO 17025:2017 standard is used as a starting point for this work, which is an international standard for the operation of laboratories. The implementation of Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), widely applied in the industry, is proposed as an action to improve the efficiency, quality and reliability of laboratories; since this philosophy encourages the participation of all those involved in the good support of resources through different techniques, which coincide with the needs required by the standard. Thus, a group of projects based on the pillars of the TPM is implemented, taken as specific objectives that, if fulfilled, promise to improve the quality of the laboratory of machines and electrical measures of the National University of Colombia. The task is evaluated through indicators as the activities of each project are developed
