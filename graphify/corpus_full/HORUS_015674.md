# LESSON OF EXPERIENTIAL TEXTS: A ROLE WITH STUDENTS OF MINORIZED COMMUNITIES IN UNIVERSITY

An experience of research and didactic proposal is presented based on the work with indigenous students, Afro-descendants and poor municipalities of the National University. The experience in relation to the didactic processes is systematized to contribute to the understanding and production of experiential and autobiographical texts with these students
