# THE DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES OF INDUSTRY 4 OR AS ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITIES FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF GREEN SUPPLY CHANNAS

The current competitive dynamics require companies to develop capacities that enable them to implement environmental practices in their supply chains. This article aims to assess whether the digital technologies of Industry 4.0 facilitate the implementation of the environmental practices of the GSCM approach and the effect of the latter on organizational performance. The Model of Structural Equations of Partial Square Minimum (PLS-SEM) was applied and tested in a sample of 200 Colombian manufacturing companies. The results show that, at the general level, digital technologies of Industry 4.0 have direct and positive impacts on green supply chain practices and that these, in turn, have direct and positive impacts on economic and environmental performance. The main contribution of this study was that it allowed to identify digital technologies of Industry 4.0 as organizational capabilities relevant to the implementation of environmental practices in supply chains, as well as the improvement of organizational performance
