# Effect of the entry of biosimilars in the market of Colombian imports.

Chapter 1 presents the current regulations for the approval of biosimilars in Colombia, together with the history, Colombian regulations and procedures for obtaining the health registry. Chapter 2 presents the current situation of the biosimilar market, analysing the market in Europe, the United States and Colombia, analysing similarities and differences between regulatory agencies and identifying the approval criteria that are available in the national territory. Chapter 3 presents the detailed research methodology, using quantitative and qualitative research techniques that allow the analysis of databases presented by electronic portals or government entities. Chapter 4 presents the qualitative and quantitative results of the research, detailing the effects of the import of biosimilars, but also considering information from ports, tariffs, laboratories, among others, that support decision-making. Chapter 5 presents the conclusions, giving effect to the specific objectives
