# Musicotherapeutic process focused on the resilience of caregivers of children diagnosed with leukemia

This study presents the description of a musicotherapeutic process focused on the promotion of resilience (after the phase of evaluation in therapy some fundamental factors were identified that could promote the achievement of the general objective), based on theoretical-practical foundations of humanistic psychology, mindfulness and positive psychology; the above, thought as a form of accompaniment that contributed to the well-being, health and self-care, significant and lasting, of 6 primary caregivers of children diagnosed with leukemia.
