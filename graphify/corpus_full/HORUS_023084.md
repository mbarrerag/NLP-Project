# CULTURE, WORK AND HUMANISM

A way is proposed to humanize man based on the mediating role of ethics in the relationship between the society of humanist culture and organizations. For this, the most relevant features of the humanist perspective on organizations, the conception of society of humanistic culture and the mediating role of ethics between them are presented. This suggests a critical and optimistic understanding of the alienation of human beings following the establishment of man-organization, characteristic of the present time
