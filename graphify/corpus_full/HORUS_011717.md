# THE WATER MARKET IN COLOMBIA //AVOCATED MARKETING IN COLOMBIA

A review of the exports and prices of avocados in Colombia was carried out, in relation to free trade agreements concluded with different countries and communities over time. For statistical analysis of monthly prices per kilo of avocado in different market places in Colombia, time-saving models, such as the mixed linear model, were used over a period of nine years (from January 2013 to August 2022). An increase in the prices of avocados of the Hass variety was observed, while in the Paper and common varieties there was a reduction in marketing prices. Similarly, it was identified that in Bogotá the highest prices of this fruit were presented compared to other cities
