# PROTOCOL OF OBSERVATIONS IN THE MTEOROLOGICAL STATUTES

Information on atmosphere, weather and climate is determined by meteorological observations carried out in the network of meteorological stations, which must be carried out regularly, following rigorous procedures that allow compliance with international standards of quality. Meteorological observations are instrumental measurements and quantitative visual assessments of meteorological processes and phenomena. Observations of the state of the atmosphere in the surface layer allow establishing the climate of an area, the thermal regimes, the rain regimes, the winds and the humidity, as well as contributing to the diagnosis and the state of evolution of atmospheric time, for forecasting effects. Analyses of the results of observations in meteorology and climatology serve as a means to elucidate the causal connection of the meteorological and climatic phenomena and events under study with the consequences or effects on nature, populations, communications and productive sectors
