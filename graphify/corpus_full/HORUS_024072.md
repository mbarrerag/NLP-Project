# OF 2009 AND 1996

The book makes a critical assessment of the initial regime of the Civil Code, and of Law 1306 of 2009 which repealed it, but maintained the discriminatory forms of interdiction and disqualification, regime of guardianships and curators dating from Roman law; and then studies the new legal subsystem that grants it and guarantees full capacity to persons who, due to cognitive conditions, do not have the same behavior and interests as what are traditionally considered normal
