# Between the Cambodia Economy and the Flower Farmers' Association.

This article addresses the process of territorial reconfiguration of the San Juan village, in the municipality of La Unión, Oriente antioqueño, from the perspective of socio-spatial studies. This place, which had been characterized by the rhythms of peasant life, at least until the beginning of the year 2000, begins to be modified through the sale of land, the lease of farms or the modification of the peasant plot for the development of floriculture. This activity produces an expanded territorialization that integrates the spatial organization of the cultivation, the rhythms, times and paths of those who work there, some inhabitants of the sidewalk, others, of the urban centres of the region, which, along with the production cycles, end up altering the community life in the paths where it is installed, its landscape and its territorial dynamics
