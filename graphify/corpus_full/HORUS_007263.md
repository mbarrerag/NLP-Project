# Electronic and molecular structure of amyloid complexes Beta (Aβ) with Fe2+/3+ (Fe2+/3+-Aβ) associated with Alzheimer’s disease

The interaction between iron and the amyloid β (Aβ) peptide has been the subject of great attention in the research of Alzheimer’s disease (EA) due to its possible implications in the development of this pathology. While previous studies have investigated the preferences of coordination of iron and the Aβ1−42 peptide, the contribution of the peptide part within the stabilization of these systems has not been studied.
