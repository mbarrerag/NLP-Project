# PREVALENCE OF THE FINKELSTEIN AND TECHHOFF SIGNATURES IN A HEALTHY POPULATION

De Quervain's disease is frequent in the active working population and its diagnosis can be a challenge. Finkelstein and Eichhoff's maneuvers are semiological tests used for the diagnosis of this pathology, whose actual diagnostic performance is not clear. The aim of this study is to determine the prevalence of the signs of Finkelstein and Eichhoff in a population of healthy individuals of working age, seeking to determine the real value of a positive finding. It is a cross-sectional, descriptive study. The sample is made up of healthy individuals between 18 and 45 years old, male and female, who participate voluntarily. All participants were performed both maneuvers; the sign of Finkelstein had a prevalence of 33.3%; the sign of Eichhoff of 75.2 %. 65 patients participated and data were recorded of 129 limbs.
