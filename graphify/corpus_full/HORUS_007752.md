# Gonzalo Arango Arias (1931-1976).

- The intellectual biography of Gonzalo Arango is an investigative adventure through the Nadaista Archive donated in the 90_x0092_s to the Antioquia Room of the Public Library: from its manuscripts, mecanuscripts, articles in newspapers, photographs, an exhaustive search for primary source, as well as audiovisual, and conducting interviews with its contemporaries this writing project is presented in five parts. The first (1931-1949) presents its period of youth training in Andes and accounts for the bipartisan violence rural of the southwestern Antioquia; liberals in power: 7 April and Laureano Gómez. The second (1950-1958) portrays the intellectual evolution: his sentimental education, the institutions, friends, literary and political works, the urban history of Medellín years 50: Junín, the Metropol and the bookshop Aguirre; when withdrawing from the University of Antioquia decides writer; the social and urban transformation after Gaitan's death and the dictatorship of Rojas Pinilla (1958-1963) formulates the incident years of incidentialism with the head, signing and making manifest and making of the articles
