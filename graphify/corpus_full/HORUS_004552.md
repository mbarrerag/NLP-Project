# Proposal for process improvement in a dairy plant through digital processing

The dairy sector in Colombia has highlighted its quality, although the nature of its raw material can lead to the production of non-compliant products. A significant challenge lies in ensuring quality control throughout production processes, complying with the specifications required. Good manufacturing practices play a crucial role in defining the variables that must be controlled. However, the lack of effective tools to identify deviations, generate alerts and facilitate traceability processes compromises customer satisfaction. The purpose of this study is to identify opportunities for improvement and propose the implementation of a digital tool that optimizes this process and that are within the reach of all actors. To achieve this, an initial diagnosis is carried out through a survey aimed at key partners in the process. Subsequently, the most appropriate type of technology is selected to address the problem and a proposal for implementation is formulated involving the application of digital transformation. This approach seeks to strengthen quality control, identify possible deviations in a timely manner and ensure traceability, thus contributing to the continuous improvement and satisfaction of the client in the Colombian dairy industry. . is conducted through a survey directed at key process collaborations.
