# BREAST CANCER CLOSE TO RADIAL SCAR

breast cancer is the main cause of death worldwide, radial scar is a high risk lesion for the development of cancer, there is currently controversy regarding the treatment of these lesions. to review publications that evaluate and mensuren the presence of breast cancer in proximity to the site of percutaneous radial scar biopsy. Material and methods: systematic review in the database of PubMed, with the terms breast radial scar and neoplasms, looking for articles with unique diagnosis of radial scar by percutaneous biopsy and subsequent open biopsy with finding of malignant breast neoplasia, separated from the site of the first biopsy and confirmed by measuring the distance between the two lesions. 242 publications were found. Of these, 108 were excluded in the screening by title and summary, 28 of them because they are review articles. Two articles were excluded in German, five cases presentations, one letter and one comment. The others were excluded by not corresponding to the objective of the research.
