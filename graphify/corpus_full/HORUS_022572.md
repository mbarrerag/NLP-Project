# Visual and digital interpretation of remote sensor data for the identification of rotational and translational slides.

This thesis presents a method for the detection and location of mass movements when analyzing their distribution, pattern and recurrence. The proposed method for the detection of mass movements uses tools of geomatics in order to reduce times and facilitate the creation of inventories of mass movements. In this study, the visual identification of pictorial - morphological features in already identified landslides was carried out, and its subsequent use for the definition of criteria for classification of landslides in a semi-automatic method of classification based on geographical objects (GEOBIA). Qualitative patterns were identified that identify the pictorial - morphological features of landslides. Subsequently, the classification based on objects was made, generating the segmentation of the image followed by classification based on geographical objects identifying the coverings of the earth and landslides. From the qualitative patterns, objects classified as landslides were refined and classified by the use of the curvature of the ground in landslides of the translational or rotational subtype. The result was validated in terms of area between the polygons classified as sliding and an inventory of previous mass movements.
