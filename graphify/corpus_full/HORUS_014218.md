# The staging. Space and programmatic sequences

This research addresses the relationship between architecture and cinema as disciplines with similar procedures and structures. Assembly and composition take a decisive role in the development of artistic work. This approach to both arts will allow the architectural object (postgraduate building of Human Sciences) to be addressed as a device/artificial with its own movement. The cinematic concept of staging will allow understanding of the typologies and architectural elements such as ensembles and joints, applying an anatomical and fragmentary analysis of the building, which will allow to exemplify in a clear and concise way the qualities of the building as its simultaneity and multiplicity
