# The Maze of the Representation in the Literature of Jorge Luis Borges

The labyrinth of representation in the literature of Jorge Luis Borges This work explores, starting from five stories by the Argentine writer Jorge Luis Borges, the way in which representation is performed in the creation of literary images. These images involve the execution of metaphor as a narrative tool, with which it is possible to describe objects and phenomena in another impossible way. This is the case of the image of chance/infinite, which takes shape with the multiple images of labyrinths proposed by the author. It will be seen that this exploration contemplates relations with Jewish, Christian and Greek traditions, therefore mythological links are drawn. Finally, an effort is proposed to describe geometrically and spatially some of those labyrinths within the stories. .
