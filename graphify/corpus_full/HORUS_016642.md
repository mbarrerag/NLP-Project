# Standard Conjugated Models: A Shiny Application for the Teaching of Base-Sian Statistics

In a Bayesian Statistics course, it is interesting to study conjugated models and to have a later distribution belonging to a known distribution family. However, limited class times do not allow students to interact more dynamically with these models to better understand these concepts and see the effect of data and a priori distribution on subsequent distribution. Thus, it is sought through a Shiny application, to analyze three cases of the normal conjugated model
