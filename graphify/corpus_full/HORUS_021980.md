# Analysis of the risk of spread of feral pigs (Sus scrofa) to areas of porciculture production and protected areas in two natural regions of Colombia.

The pigs asilvestrado (Sus scrofa) act as reservoirs of different infectious diseases.There is the probability of transmission of these diseases between feral pigs and pigs of porciculture farms.This study aims to analyze the level of risk of dispersion of feral pigs in the Caribbean region and the Colombian Orinoquia and to identify which ecological and environmental factors would facilitate the dispersion of feral pigs (S. scrofa) towards protected areas of these two regions.For this we carry out an analysis of networks and functional connectivity of S. scrofa applying theory of electrical circuits and theory of graphs.This methodology assumes that the probability of risk of dispersion increases with: the presence of maroon pigs/feral; the location of traspatio properties; a lower resistance to movement; and a lower distance between the porciculture areas of traspatio.It identifies a high 24.41 % of the nodes evaluated with a high risk level of high or very high risk in the Caribbean region (of 1,598 nodes); and 25.23 % for the region of the Orinoquia, and other characteristics (of the main areas), in the areas of the regions and the highest areas of the highest
