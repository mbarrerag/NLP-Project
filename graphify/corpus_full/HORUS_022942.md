# INFORMATION MANAGEMENT OF HIDROMETRIC STATION MAINTENANCE

The electrical power generating companies have networks of hydrometric stations that collect hydrological data. These stations require both corrective and preventive maintenance by contracting companies. However, these contractors lack IT platforms within their management systems and carry out their activities using traditional procedures, represented in physical and digital reports, which make the task of producing reports available for effective decision-making. The aim of this study is to present the development of a computer solution composed of a web application and a mobile application for the management of hydrometric station maintenance activities. The mobile application allows maintenance data to be collected from remote sites, while the web application allows management of scheduled and completed maintenance requests. The results show that the proposed model offers benefits with respect to the availability of centralized information, data collection and transmission, monitoring of the status of maintenance and consulting of historical reports
