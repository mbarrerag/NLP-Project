# Development of an in vitro model of GANNS enzyme constitutive deficiency in human mesenchymal stromal cells by the use of CRISPR-Cas9.

The mucopolysaccharidosis Type IVA or Morchio syndrome is an autosomal recessive disease caused by deficiency of the enzyme N galactosamine -6-sulfatase (GALNS), necessary to break down the glucosaminoglycans (GAG) keratan sulfate (KS) and chondroitin sulfate (CS). Enzymatic deficiency leads to lysosome dysfunction especially in connective tissues rich in KS and CS as cartilage, cornea and heart valves. There is accumulated evidence in vitro and in vivo that addresses the physiopathological mechanisms that ultimately determine the clinical manifestations in patients affected, especially in osteochondral tissues. However, the difficulty in obtaining bone and joint tissues of affected patients has limited the study of early deleterious effects of the GALNS deficiency in the stromal mesenchymal cells.
