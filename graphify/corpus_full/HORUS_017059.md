# Evaluation through Clinical Simulation of the Development of Transversal Competencies in Medical Students of the National University of Colombia.

Evaluation through the Clinical Simulation of the Development of Transversal Competences in Medical Students of the National University of Colombia The current medical education at the National University of Colombia is based on a constructivist system in which the student, through an independent learning process, is responsible for building his own knowledge and the teacher becomes a guide to achieve this task. Therefore, as he advances in his learning process, he develops different professional competencies, specific type, specific to his profession or cross-sectional type, common to various professions of Health Sciences. The transversal ones include leadership skills, team work, problem solving, situational awareness and assertive communication, among others. It has been demonstrated that clinical simulation as a learning strategy, allows the progressive development of non-technical skills related to transversal competencies and there is evidence that these decrease the morbidity mortality of patients and reduce the medical error. This research will be carried out as a case study, with alternative approach and triangulation of data. It seeks to evaluate through the Clinical Simulation the development of transversal competencies for the care of the adult patient in Urgencies, in students of Pregraduation, and medical error.
