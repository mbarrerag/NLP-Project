# Fundamental requirements of the conditionality regime in the special jurisdiction for peace.

After the signing of the peace process between the Colombian Government and the FARC signed in November 2016, the Comprehensive System of Truth, Justice, Reparation and Non-Repetition was created, from which the Special Jurisdiction for Peace emerged as its component of Justice. Thus, this paper analyzed the debates associated with the Conditionality Regime; it appears that it emerges as the need to relax the punitive standards of the armed actors of the conflict and in contrast to this guaranteeing the realization of the rights and needs of the victims. Thus, the text makes a closer approach to the legal background on the aforementioned regime, analyzing experiences of previous peace processes erected in the Colombian legal system; a reconstruction of the jurisprudence of the Special Jurisdiction for Peace in its early years is carried out. It concludes with some reflections on the challenges of the aforementioned regime and decants the legal tensions to comply with the standards of restorative justice in the strict temporality of the Jurisdiction
