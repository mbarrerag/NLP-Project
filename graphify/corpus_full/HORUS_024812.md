# COMPARISON OF THE ADJUSTMENT OBTAINED BY THE LINE MODEL OF JOINT EFFECTS AND THE RE-EM TREE METHODOLOGY THAT INCORPOSES DECISION ARBOLES, CONSIDERING DATA WITH REPEATED MEASURES.

In this work, a comparison was made between the results obtained by making the adjustment of a linear model of mixed effects (MEM) (see Davis (2002)) and the methodology RE-EM tree proposed by Sela and Simonoff (2012) that combines the structure of the models of mixed effects for data correlated with the methods of estimation based on decision trees, considering ten simulation cases in which data sets were generated with repeated measures in the response of interest, based on the results presented in Posada (2012) and Pande et al. (2017), and the correlation between such repeated measures should be incorporated into the analysis. In the first case of simulation, based on Posada (2012), three models were adjusted for different scenarios: LME without interactions, LME with double interactions and RE-EM tree.
