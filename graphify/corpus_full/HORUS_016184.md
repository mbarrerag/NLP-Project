# METHODOLOGIES FOR THE TEACHING OF PALIATIVE CARE IN HEALTH AREA STUDENTS: EXPLORATORY REVIEW

the need for palliative care teaching to health professionals is becoming more prevalent in the world and in Latin America. Educators must recognize the advances for palliative care teaching through new forms of learning. to characterize the methodologies, contents and effects of palliative care teaching in health students in literature published between 2016 and 2020. a review of the type of literature was carried out. Articles published in journals indexed in the databases Medline, Cuiden, Lilacs, CINAHL during the period 2016-2020 in English, Spanish and Portuguese were reviewed. 25 researches were selected after the process of critique of the evidence. the included studies were mostly carried out with quantitative methodologies, in the United States and with populations of nursing students. The information extracted from the articles was organized in four topics: 1) educational courses in palliative care, 2) simulation strategies, 3) knowledge in palliative care of students, and 4) attitudes and emotions of students in palliative care. the evaluation of the importance of the articles were organized in four subjects: 1) educational courses in palliative care, 2) simulation strategies, 3) knowledge in palliative care of students, and the complete sample care populations
