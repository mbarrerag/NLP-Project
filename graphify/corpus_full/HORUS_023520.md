# Generation of SI/IT co-evolution mechanisms with strategy in a higher education institution (Case Universidad Nacional de Colombia).

The relationship between information technologies (IT) and business strategy is a topic that occupies the first places of interest both for entrepreneurs and academics. More so in a context in which technological developments and innovations become more and more relevant, significantly impacting the behaviour of organizations, as well as the emergence of new business models with digital base, structures in red, management of IT-mediated processes. This is how it is evident that it needs to achieve the adjustment between strategy and IS/IT is important for all types of organizations. However, in this work we pay attention to a particular type of organization, the university. Due to its nature and importance for the social impact that it has both in teaching, as in research and in the relationship with the community.
