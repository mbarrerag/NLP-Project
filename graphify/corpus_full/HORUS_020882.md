# Project formulation _x0093_PCCC as we go_x0094_ Information System, pilot in Manizales Caldas

The territory of the Coffee Cultural Landscape of Colombia requires for its management and sustainability to have a technological development based on software that allows to carry out monitoring, evaluation and monitoring of the indicators both cultural and natural existing in the management plan of the SCCP, that are agreed and exposed to the community and administrative, civil and academic institutions that intervene in the landscape. It is intended to determine the model of information _x0093_PCC system as we go_x0094_ required to characterize each of the variables that make part of the observation unit of the Coffee Cultural Landscape of Colombia; by means of a pilot in the municipality of Manizales (2 veredas); which will serve as a model to implement it in the departments of the SCCP (Caldas, Quindío, Risaralda and Valle) and in its 51 municipalities
