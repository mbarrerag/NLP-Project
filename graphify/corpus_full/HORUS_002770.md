# CHARACTERIZATION OF FRESH FLOWS BY SOME BISH GROUPS ( SUPPORT) IN SIX AREAS PROTECTED FROM THE VALLEY OF BLOWS

Urbanisation is an anthropogenic alteration of natural environments that has deteriorated and transformed these spaces into filters for different plant and animal species. Processes such as fragmentation, the decrease in the size of natural areas and the entry of exotic species, alter and modify the different communities and the way in which they interact. Therefore, the structure of the cities exerts an effect on the organisms present in the communities, benefiting some characteristics or traits that allow the subsistence of the organisms present in these. In the case of the bees, according to their diet, the availability of floral resources is a vital factor for their survival in a community. The aim of this study is to characterize the type of flora used and mostly frequented by some species of native bees present in protected areas of the Aburra Valley. For this, the bees that were visiting flowers, in a monthly sample made part of the plants that most frequently visited, the captured bees are extracted from the pollen that they carried and this was processed using standard palinological methods.
