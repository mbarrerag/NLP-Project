# Treatment of demodicosis and its beneficial anatomical and functional effect on the meibomium glands as evaporative dry eye management

The deterioration of the functionality of the tear film, secondary to the infestation by Demodex is a problem of frequent ophthalmological health in the general population, is therefore that to determine an effective management that eliminates the symptoms and that restores the function of the tear film is presented as an alternative to those patients who suffer from that condition. The idea is to establish an effective therapy that apart from eradicating the parasite Demodex folicullorum of the palpebral tissue, improves the quality of life of patients affected by dry eye syndrome of evaporative type. For this purpose a review of clinical histories of patients attended in the particular consultation of Dr. Marcel Yecid Avila who have received treatment with the topical gel and analyze the changes presented in the function of the tear film and in the structure of the meibomial glands is carried out.
