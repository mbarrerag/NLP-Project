# QUALITATIVE BEHAVIOUR OF THE SOLUTIONS TO A QUASILINEAR PROBLEM FROM A GENERALIZED POHOZAEV IDENTITY

We prove an existence result of positive radial solution for a nonlinear $\phi$-Laplacian problem by using shooting technique. Our nonlinearity satisfies adequate conditions and has semipositone behaviour. Our result generalizes a previous result in the literature
