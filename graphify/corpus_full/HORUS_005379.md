# Accidentally associated injuries of motocyclet in the department of the META in COLOMBIA between 2017 and 2018

According to WHO, each year approximately 1.35 million people die from road accidents in the world. This cause, specifically motorcycle driving, can cause secondary injuries of importance to public health given the conditions in occupational, social and family performance, becoming a burden for the health system due to the progression to chronic condition. to describe the most frequent physical injuries in the population of the motorcycle accidented Meta in 2017 and 2018. quantitative, descriptive, cross-sectional study based on data sources available in the 2017 and 2018 records of the Regional Board of Invalidity Rating of the Meta. 1114 records of road accidents on motorcycle during the period 2017-2018 were reviewed. 78.46% of the injured and injured were in male population, of which 61.58% presented single marital status, with an academic level of secondary education of 60.95%. 75.22% of accidents occurred in urban area, the most frequent fracture injury with 79.98%, area with higher impact of lower members, 40.48% and the most injured bone of the population, 17.24% of the injured population, with higher degree of secondary education
