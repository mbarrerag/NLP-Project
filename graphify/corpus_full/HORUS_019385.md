# INFECTION BY VIRUS OF THE HUMAN PAPILMOMY IN THE EMBARAZO

Human papillomavirus (HPV) infection is considered the most common sexually transmitted infection in the world with an estimated prevalence of 20% to 46%, can cause cancer of vagina, vulva, penis and anus, as well as some head and neck cancers, anogenital warts and recurrent respiratory papillomatosis
