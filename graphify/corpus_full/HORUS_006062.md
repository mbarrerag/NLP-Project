# Model 4D –Multicriterio/Multi-temporal/Multi-space – for impact assessment in infrastructure projects: case hydropower

In the literature, there are various tools and methodologies to evaluate and analyze the effects on the natural environment and surrounding communities with strategies such as expert consultation, simulation, content analysis and stakeholder surveys. These methodologies, usually carry out a unidirectional analysis in terms of the objectives that a project seeks to impact, that is, they focus on the approach that the project has with respect to the results that it intends to achieve. This work seeks to apply a model of impact analysis in hydroelectric projects that manages to include the social, environmental and economic axes and that also relates multicriteria, multidecisive, multitemporal and multispatial dimensions. By methodology, a filtered literature analysis with a PRISMA methodology, a system of impact classification, the application of analysis using the DEMATEL-WINGS model, making a matrix analysis of the complex system of impacts, actors and other dimensions, obtaining as an important contribution the criterion of influence, which is used finally for the assessment of impacts AHP together with the criteria of magnitude and temporality.
