# MOVEMENT COURSES-TERRITORIES: INDIGENOUS WOMEN AND RELATIONSHIP SPACE

For the development of the text, I present the following parts. In the first part, Mobile Worlds in Permanent Encounters: Female Bodies-Territories, I generally raise the territorial notions of indigenous peoples and interactions with the non-human, and I present the proposals and notions of indigenous women around body-territory. Subsequently, I focus on three perspectives: Multiple and merged Tempo-territorialities; living memoirs in spiral; and Interlinkages of Conviviality, which address interactions of the various beings, their territorialities and conviviality crossed by collective memories. Finally, in Posicando políticas de vida, I address some of the conceptual and methodological implications of the proposals of indigenous women in the form of conclusions
