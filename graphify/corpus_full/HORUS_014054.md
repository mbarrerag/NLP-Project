# Jurisprudential and theoretical analysis of the pronouncements of the Criminal Cassation Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice with a gender focus: 2018-2023

The project will be developed by undergraduate students of the Faculty of Law, Political and Social Sciences of the National University of Colombia. Additionally, the students of the project have the support of members of the research group INTERNATIONAL RED DE POLÍTICA CRIMINAL SISTÉMICA EXTREMA RATIO UN, which is made up of academics from the National University and other national and foreign universities. The project aims to analyze the changes in the jurisprudence of the Criminal Chamber of the Supreme Court of Justice with a gender focus since 2018 (since this year the first pronouncement on the subject was issued), which have most influenced in recent years in the field of criminal law, and to which there are no relevant and critical academic works. These changes have occurred in: (i) the resolution of cases of feminicide, in virtue of which they deal with the topic of historical violence against women and how this is revealed in cases where the death of a woman is caused by the fact of being married; (ii) in the gender approach applied to evaluate the evidence, especially in cases of sexual violence; (iii) the recognition of the sexual self-determination of women in the context of the application of marriage; and the Supreme Court of women
