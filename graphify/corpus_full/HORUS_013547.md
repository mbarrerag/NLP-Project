# Ethical factors in the control environment of the Internal Control System, as limiting in the exercise of professionals of administrative sciences in the fulfilment of their objectives

Internal control is a system developed through policies, procedures, mechanisms and methods with the purpose of helping companies to achieve their objectives, this by ensuring financial information, efficiency and effectiveness of operations and compliance with laws. Internal control allows the optimization of resources, contributes to the prevention or detection of fraud and strengthens decision-making based on efficiency; in this way, the non-implementation of this system or, a non-strict implementation and with failures, can prevent the company from achieving what is proposed and even reaching bankruptcy. Therefore, internal control has a series of limitations that affect the mission to exist; the casuistics are different, but highlight academic, ethical, knowledge, among others. This work is focused on analyzing the ethical factors of professionals that prevent internal control from achieving its objectives. These factors from the ethics were considered not only from a philosophical perspective but also from the COSO perspective.
