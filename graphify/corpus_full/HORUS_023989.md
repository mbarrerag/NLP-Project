# COMPUTATION DESIGN AND MODELATION OF A VENTILATION SYSTEM TO DEMOVE COVID CONTAGIUM RISK19 IN UNIVERSITY AULAS

The need for economic reopening is becoming increasingly evident, which implies, among other things, the reopening of educational centres. The recent recess that caused the Covid-19 pandemic has complicated the carrying out of activities with clumps in enclosed spaces, since they favor the high concentrations of aerosols in the environment that are strongly related to the contagion of the virus. In this chapter, the process of designing a ventilation system that decreases the likelihood of contagion of SARS-CoV-2 in enclosed spaces is proposed. The pilot plan studies a classroom located at the Faculty of Engineering of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá, which has insufficient air renewal rates for a safe return to classes. By theoretical calculations of air loads and flows and the implementation of commercial software, it was ensured that the system meets the standards and latest recommendations on ventilation for the reduction of contagion. As a result, a design of a local extraction system was obtained that allows a complete renewal of air from the enclosure every 10 minutes.
