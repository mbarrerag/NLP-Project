# The pension obligations of the UGPP: characterization of its accounting developments and its relationship with the financing of the Colombian public sector

In the last decades of the twentieth century the reforms of the State became the central theme of the international political agenda, promoting a private vision of the public and deepening the importance of the markets, institutions and financial elites in the operation of the economy. This ended transformed the pension systems based on schemes of intergenerational solidarity to systems of insurance. In this order, the processes of information and control, mediated by the accounting, carried out in the Colombian pension system, acquire an important role for the understanding of this system, hence that this research seeks to characterize the transformations that have occurred in the accounting treatment of the pension obligations of the nation since the year 2000 and understand how the accounting instruments have been used in the public sector entities to reproduce the processes of financing that have motivated the transformation of the pension administration of the Colombian State in recent years.
