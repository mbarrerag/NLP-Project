# CHAPTER 4: MOBILE ME BECAUSE I HAVE THE POWER TO PROJECT MY FUTURE: ADOPTION OF HEALTHFUL COMPORTMENTS IN CHILDREN

For more than a century the study of the behaviors of the human being has been of great interest especially for the human disciplines and of the health, because, depending on the actions and the decisions that the people assume in the context in which they develop, they will have a positive or negative influence for their health and well-being.The behavior is defined as the set of actions that are exposed and externalized by a person, and that in turn are influenced by the culture, the attitudes, the emotions, the individual and social values, the ethics and even by the genetics [1].Through these characteristics, the people or groups establish relationships with themselves and with their immediate physical and social context [2].The behaviors have a direct link with the health when they increase the vulnerability to develop a disease, by worsening an existing pathological state, or when, on the contrary, they exercise a reduction in the probability of sickening or contribute to the decrease of their progress
