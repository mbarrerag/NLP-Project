# SUSTAINABILITY OF ENERGY COMMUNITIES: PROPOSALS FOR MANAGEMENT AND ASSESSMENT OF COMMUNITY RENEWABLE ENERGY PROJECTS FOR STRONG, BOLIVATE ISLAND.

This article addresses the importance of renewable energies in the development of sustainable and participatory energy communities. A study is presented in Isla Fuerte, Bolívar (Colombia), where a portfolio of productive projects based on renewable energies was designed, using Design Thinking methodology in co-creation laboratories. Four viable projects were designed that will meet the needs identified by the community. Co-creation laboratories were identified as enriching and productive spaces, allowing the community to devise solutions for their problems. It emphasizes the importance of identifying potential actors and establishing responsible for the implementation of projects. This provides clarity in the course of action and facilitates the continuity of the projects of the created portfolio. A community management guide is also proposed that seeks to be replicable in future projects, establishing preliminary factors for their development and long-term sustainability. Finally, it emphasizes the relevance of actively involving the community in the implementation of projects in their territories, because their continued participation is fundamental to the viability and sustainability of the actions developed. It concludes that community projects based on renewable energy can meet the needs of communities if they are implemented adequately by the community, the collaborative approaches and the importance of the community
