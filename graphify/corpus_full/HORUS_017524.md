# Molecular coupling and three-dimensional modelling by homology of amentoflavone-derived flavonoids with avian influenza virus neuraminidases H1N1 and H5N1

Influenza A virus is responsible for avian influenza, pathological condition that affects mainly birds, horses and marine mammals, however, the subtype H5N1 has the ability to infect humans quickly, exposing them to a possible pandemic event. Therefore, the objective of this study was to perform the molecular coupling and three-dimensional modeling by homology of flavonoids derived from amentoflavone with the neuraminidases H1N1 and H5N1 of the avian influenza virus. Initially, the 3D structure of the neuraminidase H1N1 was obtained by homology. Subsequently, a molecular coupling of H1N1 with six ligands was performed (F36, Ginkgetin, 3S, 3S, 5S, 5S, 6S and 6R), and later H5N1 and the ligands F36, Ginkgetin, 5R and 6R. Finally, the complex obtained were subjected to an interaction analysis. The results also showed a relationship between the inhibitive activity and the H5N1 and the active ligands were replaced
