# EFFECTS OF THE COVID-19 PANDEMIA IN GROWTH AND RENTABILITY OF COLOMBIAN MANUFACTURER UNDERTAKINGS

The objective of this study is to analyze the impact of the pandemic effect of the IVO ID -19 on the behavior of growth and business profitability in Colombia. To achieve this, a method of analysis of financial data from 850 Colombian companies was used, using a panel data approach and applying the statistical model of fixed effects and first differences. The results obtained confirmed the relationship between profitability and business growth. Furthermore, no evidence was found of a co-dependency between these variables, since such a relationship depends on the particular aspects of each context. Both positive and negative impacts caused by the IVO ID -19 were observed in the variables analyzed. The post-analysis discussions coincide with other studies highlighting the correlation between profitability and business growth. It is also noted that while most of the impacts of this pandemic present similarities, they vary according to the specific context of each company. The magnitude of these impacts is related to the efficiency of the responses offered by governments as well as by the companies themselves. These factors influence the interpretation of the results and their implications.
