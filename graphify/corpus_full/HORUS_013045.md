# Peasant, no one in the folklore paradise of Huila

This journey around the peasant singularity, is a work based on practice, in which the uncertainty and the certainties stick and take off simultaneously to trigger the creative practice in scenarios where the “none” dialogues both splendid and miserable. A project that addresses the absurd as an event of meaning, where the vibrancy, prejudices and singularity of the peasant thought are scenarios of creation for the arts, particularly the ritual of the “tinto” and the cultural display of the “matachin” that surround the peasant subject in the “Paraiso folkloric of the Huila”, The villa of San sebastián of the silver. This research is an approach to the peasant subject without any transcendent desire. A critical comparative discourse that does not aim to solve the question for “the no one” but to confront it in order to understand its conflicts and collisions, not as an abstract exercise but situated in the coordinates of the art and in the sense of the paradoxical absurd.
