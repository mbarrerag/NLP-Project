# INVISIBILIZATION OPERATIONS: THE CASE OF THE SINGLE PORTAL OF THE COLOMBIAN STATE (GOV.CO)

This contribution aims to present interdisciplinary approaches to the regulatory activities of the State when communicating with citizens. We assume that such activities are associated with practices of invisibility and simplification of its recipients that show conceptions of citizenship and of the nation strongly limited that ignore the realities of the populations to which they are directed.To achieve this objective, we propose a multimodal reading of selected contents of the official page of the Government of Colombia (gov.co) using the concepts of what is invisibleization of the other subaltern, male or female, and of what is clear language?. We understand the page as a scenario where the public word is exercised, in particular, the digital communication between the State and the citizenry, and we investigate the ways in which its contents and messages involve simplification of the other through the use of images, texts and multimedia content. This chapter is divided into four parts. In the second part that follows this introduction, we present the theoretical reflections from the anthropology and the philosophical epistemology about the unequal performance of the interpretive work and the white identity of the different forms of the unequal distribution of the different forms of the power, which are divided into different forms. attention to the image of citizenship by the state administration with a focus on bureaucratic mechanisms of invisibility. Finally, we offer a set of conclusions derived from the analysis
