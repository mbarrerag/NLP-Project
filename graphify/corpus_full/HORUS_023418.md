# PH DETERMINATION METHOD REVISION IN PINTURAS TO WATER TYPE COLOMBIA EMULSION: METROLOGIC UPDATE

The paint industry is one of the most relevant markets in Colombia. One of the methods that allows to evaluate the condition and quality of the paint is the determination of pH. Therefore it is important to make an improvement and update of the method from a metrological approach. The determination of pH in emulsion type water paints, will follow the procedure as established in the NTC 624:1972 and ISO 19396-1:2017 and according to the parameters established metrologically varying the matrix and the quality of the sample with a pH meter combined immersion of glass membrane. According to the bibliographic review carried out, it is found that the Colombian technical standard of 1972 proposes a method of measuring pH different from international standards (ISO 19396-1:2017) and to the methods currently implemented by Colombian companies. Therefore, it is important to improve the implicit activities in measuring the pH value in emulsion type water paintsions that promote its reproducibility and eventually allow to provide the basis for updating the Colombian technical standard. From the metrological approach, it seeks to perform a study of variables that affect the measurement and evaluate the pH value of the other analytical methods
