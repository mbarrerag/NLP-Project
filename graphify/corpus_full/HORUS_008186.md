# Education as a strategy for preventing the use of psychoactive substances in minors: A proposal from the perspective of public health

This research reports the results of a qualitative study in which, through a process of research-action, proposals for intervention for the prevention of the consumption of psychoactive substances were developed in students of grades 9°, 10° and 11° of the IE León XIII located in the Municipality of Soacha.
