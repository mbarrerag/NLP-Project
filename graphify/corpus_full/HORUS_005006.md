# RISK FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO THE SUICIDE IN CHILDREN AND ADOLESSENTS IN THE PANDEMIA CIVID-19: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA AND METAANALYSIS 2020-2022

Children ~ and adolescents could present a greater risk of suicidal intent or ideation during the COVID-19 pandemic. To establish risk factors associated with suicidal intent and ideation in children ~ and adolescents during the COVID-19 pandemic. Systematic literature review and meta-analysis. The search was done in Pub-Med, LILACS, Science Direct, Scopus and Google Scholar from May to August 2022. Quality was evaluated with Newcastle-Ottawa and STROBE. Meta-analysis was done with the random effect model for dichotomous data. A relationship was found between depression and suicidal ideation and overall suicide attempt OR: 10.50 (9.90-11.14); I2 = 98%; OR: 7.31 (6.38-8.39); I2 = 0%
