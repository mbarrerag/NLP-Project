# Easy-to-ignition coal briquette combustion gases

The emissions of the combustion gases of the possible pollutants from the atmosphere from burning easy-to-ignition coal briquettes are analysed. The measured emissions were of smokes, CO, CO2, NOx and SO2. The fumes were measured qualitatively and their quality varied between excellent and smoke-free, which shows the good performance of the briquette relative to this parameter. As for the emission of gases, an increase is shown when the combustion is made in the open air compared to if it is carried out in a closed enclosure. In both cases, the emissions at a given time depend on the briquette layer being burned. The differences obtained in the emissions of SO2 lead to the conclusion that the briquettes do not have a good homogenization in terms of the materials used for the retention of these emissions. The quality of the surrounding air was measured for the case of the emissions of CO being found to burn in closed enclosure, which is where I know the greatest amount, the concentration of CO is below the limits allowed in the Colombian standards and the EPA (Environmental Protection of Time taken for exposure)
