# Model for the forecasting of demand for medicines for the care of diseases of public health interest

The effectiveness of programs aimed at mitigating events of interest in public health lies in the availability at national level of essential medicines. In its role as manager and administrator, the State has within its responsibility to ensure the accessibility of these medicines, in order to reduce disparities in health and improve living conditions. In order to ensure this availability, a management model for the forecasting of the demand for medicines from the territorial authorities has been designed. To this end, a comprehensive characterization of the supply chain of medicines was carried out, allowing to understand the flows of information and medicines, as well as to identify successful practices and challenges. Additionally, an ARIMA model (1,1.0) was applied to forecast the demand for medicines intended for the care of the Malaria P. Faciparum in the department of Chocó. This model was adjusted and verified through the methodology Box-Jenkins, providing a forecast for the year 2022 with an error of MAP to 19.2%.
