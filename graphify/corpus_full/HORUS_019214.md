# Properties of some orthogonal polynomial systems Sobolev in several variables.

In this paper we studied some algebraic and analytical properties of orthogonal polynomials in several real variables with respect to an internal product Sobolev continuous-discrete. We considered Sobolev polynomials on different domains, namely: a product domain; the unitary ball; the simplex; and the cone. Our main results consist of an iterative method of construction of orthogonal polynomials with respect to the internal product, properties involving its main part (continuous), a connection formula, and some results on partial differential equations. In order to illustrate our main ideas, at the end of this work we presented several numerical examples in two variables. In addition, we discussed some open problems
