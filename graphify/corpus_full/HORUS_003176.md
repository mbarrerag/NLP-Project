# Comparative analysis of the evolution in the structuring and financing of concessions in Colombia

Colombia has been historically lagging behind in road infrastructure, however since the economic opening of the 1990s concession projects have been structured in partnership with the private sector. In this work, variables of regulatory framework and governance and risk management have been analyzed - key elements to achieve the financial closure of infrastructure projects - in order to establish the advances and limitations that have been made over the years. In the execution of the first three generations of road concessions in Colombia there were some advances in regulatory and risk management matters, but it was definitely up to the fourth generation of concessions with the introduction of the project financing methodology that were adopted the best practices for the implementation of projects through PPP in the country. .
