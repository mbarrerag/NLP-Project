# Design of moulds for injection of plastics and metals using the topological optimization method

One of the main problems in the manufacturing processes of the injection mould lies in the attention to efficient production, which means shorter cycle and processing times, determined largely by cooling times, parts with precise and precise measures, dimensional and geometric tolerances, longer useful life of the mold, use of materials and the production of products at reasonable prices, which show a solid economy for the production of large series. In this work, a mold is developed by topological optimization, considering the thermal compensation, which means that, the deformations of the mold, caused by the thermal gradient and the closing forces of the mold that are cyclical, are minimized in the chambers of the mold (contact zone between the mold and the molten material in the filling process), in order to improve the tolerances of the part.The topological optimization method is based on the model of the material Solid Isotropic Material with Penalization (SIMP) and in the implementation of the linear sequential programming (SLP) method of mobile asymptoms (MMA).The topological optimization and objective function is formulated, considering a model of the thermal design and the structural design. t reasonable prices, which show a solid economy for large series production. In the present work, a survey optimization (OT) mold is developed, considering thermal compensation. Thermal compensation means that the mold-deformations, which is caused by gradient temperature and cycling injection forces in the mold, are minimal in the mold-cavity zones (contact zone between the mold and the material incorporated in the fill process), to improve piece tolerances. The OT design is based on traditional SIMP material model and SLP implementation, considering linear theory analysis. The domain design is considered as a 3D domain and the numerical implementation will be developed in a programming software *.m code (MATLAB implementation)
