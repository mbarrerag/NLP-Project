# Synthesis of adsorbent from microparticular by-products of quarry mining activity.

The activities of mining from quarry to open floor for the production of aggregates for the construction industry produce a microparticle by-product that until now do not present viable economic or technological applications.The distribution of particle size of this by-product prevents its application in the formulation of concretes.The lack of applications for this particulate material has led to its classification as solid waste, whose final disposition is stacked to open sky; due to this stacking, solid particles are easily dispersed by air and rainwater currents, impacting negatively water sources, soils, vegetation and urban establishments around the place of final disposal of the material.In view of the environmental problem generated by these solids and their final disposition, this research was set out to develop conversion processes that allow this residue to be transformed into materials with technological applications such as zeolites.The solid, usually called as a quarry or solid establishments produced in the grinding of quarry rock, is a mixture of clays, quartz, organic matter and glass pieces, plastics and metals.The use of this microparticulate in physical conversion processes will allow the ins to be used as an insorical. in the processes of removal of aluminium or silicon from the structure of the precursor
