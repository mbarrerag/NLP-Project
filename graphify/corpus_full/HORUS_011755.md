# A GREAT PROGRAMME ALL TO LEARN

This book, dedicated especially to the ten years of life of the All to Learn or Pta Program, consists of nine chapters, of which the first one aims to describe the main stages and transformations that the pt has had in this decade of implementation in the national territory. Each chapter is the product of reflections by a group of tutor teachers of the pt, who, in the process of accompanying other teachers in the classroom, have generated reflections on the practices of reading, writing and orality; the role of the learning communities (cda); the networks of teachers and the role of tutors in the educational contexts. The texts, resulting from an exercise of research and reflection, were selected through a process of accompaniment and review by the academic team that led the national call
