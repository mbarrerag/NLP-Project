# Development of a damage identification system for composite material structures based on FBG sensors with a focus on the development of digital twins

The current technological developments have generated a space conducive to the development of the concept of industry 4.0 where the integration of advanced production techniques and operations with intelligent technologies is considered [1]. In this frame of reference, developments in sensors, instrumentation, communications, robotics, artificial intelligence, analytics of data, internet of things (IoT), among others are taken advantage to obtain solutions that provide relevant information about the process in which they are applied and thus generate processes increasingly intelligent.For the systems of monitoring of structural health, there are developments oriented to intelligent systems, prediction of variables based on data, digital twins, among others, but given its current development of these strategies imply a way to still travel that allows to generate confidence in their use and robustness to cover different environmental and operational conditions. As a contribution to the solution of this problem, this project proposes as one of its objectives to develop and validate a system based on optical sensors (FBG) to use the structural health of composite structures with a view to their integration into the future development of digital twins. The project covers three main phases: design, simulation and implementation of materials. In the design phase, the optical monitoring strategies based on FBG will be used
