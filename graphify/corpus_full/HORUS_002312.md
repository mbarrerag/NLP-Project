# Calibration in the Cary 5000 spectrophotometer NIR region for microplastic analysis

The project aims to develop a preliminary calibration procedure in the NIR region for the Cary 5000 spectrophotometer, in order to exploit its potential in the analysis of materials of environmental interest through rapid, precise and non-destructive methods. The initiative will help to reduce the technical gap in optical characterization, ensuring more reliable and traceable measurements. For four months, adequate measurement configurations will be documented, the accuracy and reproducibility of results will be evaluated, and initial criteria will be established for the selection and configuration of optical accessories. The approach is focused on microplastic detection, strengthening laboratory capabilities in spectral analysis and laying technical bases for future research on emerging pollutants
