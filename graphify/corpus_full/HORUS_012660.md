# AI algorithms used for fault diagnosis of operating PV modules with thermal images

The present study has as its purpose the explanation of the evaluation of an AI application that allows the diagnosis of failures in PV modules in collection by means of thermographic images. That is, currently renewable energy systems represent a field of significant importance within Society, for this reason, research and studies are required to deploy more clearly the implementation of improvements and technological trends to contribute to society. The following article cites many authors who have played a significant role in the study of PV modules and who have also implemented systemic or software improvements to contribute with research to guide the innovation of these renewable energy sources
