# About Medical Education in Latin America

While the World Medical Association has established minimum quality standards for the training of general practitioners to enable them to acquire basic medical skills necessary to exercise their profession properly, it is also important to remember that psychosocial aspects influence the training processes of medical students. In this regard, the social responsibility of Latin American medical schools to train competent doctors as a contribution to reducing inequities in the region has been included in recent decades as a standard of quality in medical education
