# THE VICTIM OF ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA

Colombia has been experiencing armed conflict for more than five decades. During these years, various forms of violence have characterized the national scene, leaving millions of victims and becoming a constant in the life of Colombian society. Women have suffered with particular severity the conflict and its consequences. They have been victims of various forms of violence, some of which are explained by gender, reproducing the roles traditionally assigned to them. This document aims to demonstrate some elements that allow understanding of the situation of women victims of the armed conflict, documented in various human rights reports prepared by international human rights bodies, as well as by national organizations working for women's rights. For this, the article has three parts. The first one shows how the conflict has affected women, indicating; (a) the roles they have played; (b) the forms of violence they have been victims of; and (c) the effects that such violence have had on their lives. The second part shows the main barriers that women encounter in gaining access to their rights to truth, justice and reparation. Finally, in the third part, a brief overview and some conclusions are presented
