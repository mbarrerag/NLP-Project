# THE BOGOTAH SABANA IN THE 16th CENTURY: A RECONSTRUCTION OF THEIR TERRITORY THROUGH TOPONIMIA

In this paper, the advances of the ongoing research titled "Is the territory of the savannah of Bogotá from the word: an indigenous construction and Spanish reconstruction from the toponymy of the sixteenth century", whose aim is to explain how the territory of the Savana of Bogotá (a floodable plain) was understood by the muiscas, the indigenous group that inhabited it at the time of the Spanish invasion, through the toponyms, and how it was redefined and reorganized in the early years of colonization by the Spaniards. For this purpose, the colonial documentation of the second half of the sixteenth century and of the first decade of the seventeenth century, specifically to the processes associated with land disputes between the encomenderos, visits of the authorities of the Royal Audience to the peoples of Indians, and processes of demarcation of the indigenous reservations, were used. In these documents not only a large number of muisca toponyms, today completely forgotten, but also their geographical location according to the spatial references of the period, are shown how these geographical descriptions are being analyzed, with the aim of achieving a complete reconstruction of the geographical location and the different characteristics
