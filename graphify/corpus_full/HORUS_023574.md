# Reflections on the violence present in that silence of Roberto Burgos and El desbarrancadero by Fernando Vallejo

The thesis is a critical appraisal of two contemporary Colombian novels. The literary analysis, on the one hand, leads to determining that the silence of some of the characters of Burgos obeys a type of symbolic violence, with their respective psychological implications; on the other hand, it allows establishing that the insult of the narrator and main character of Vallejo seeks to conjure the evil that has always besieged him. The conclusions of that analysis highlight the critique of the novel of Burgos to the silence imposed by violence, as well as the need of the insult in the novel of Vallejo, which is an argumentive resource with possibilities to face all that harms life.
