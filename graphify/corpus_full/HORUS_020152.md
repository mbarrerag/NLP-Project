# Sustainability assessment of different biorefinery schemes to enhance the development of post-conflict areas in the Colombian context: The Montes de Maria case

The objectives of sustainable development (ODS) and the concept of sustainability have focused on specific topics such as poverty, quality of life, environment and society. Today, biomass has been used to implement an economic model based on renewable sources. Therefore, the objective of this work was to evaluate the sustainability of different small and large scale biorefineries to dilute a portfolio of products that promote technical, economic, environmental and social progress in the region of Montes de María, Sucre, Colombia. The knowledge of the region and the agricultural products offered by farmers were fundamental to define the raw materials of this work (i.e., banana and avocado). The alternatives of entrepreneurship and industrial processes considered marketable at different levels were generated as avocado oil, levulinic acid, banana flour, bio-mass and biogas.
