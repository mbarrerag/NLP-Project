# The butterfly tree as a strategy for teaching natural sciences at the Reggio Emilia school

This paper proposes the design of a strategy of teaching in natural sciences that encourages the use of the butterfly, allowing to mobilize the environmental education in the apprentices of the school Reggio Emilia looking to generate care and appropriation on the environment. To meet this objective, it was based on an initial diagnosis of the previous appropriation that the apprentices had on the site through the strategy of the speaking maps, it was continued with the design of the strategy based on the active and constructivist methodology on which the school is based and based on four of the principles of Marco Antonio Moreira in his proposal for Critical Significant Learning (ASC). As a main result of this work, it was possible to corroborate that this type of strategy is an efficient method of learning in the environmental field for apprentices, not only to generate awareness about a specific site, but on all those sites in which they share in their daily life. . This paper proposal the design of natural sciences teaching strategy that promotes the use of a butfly competitive, enabbling and educationally oriented, at the same end. awareness about a specific site, but also about all those sites in which they spend their daily lives
