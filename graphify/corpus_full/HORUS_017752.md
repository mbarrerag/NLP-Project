# COMPRESSIONS OF THE COMMUNITY IN THE OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY

This article identifies and analyzes the path taken in the ways of conceiving the community in the publications of the Revista Occupación Humana, between 1996 and 2021. As a methodology, a documentary review of the articles published in this journal and available online was carried out, guided by the presence of the words "community", "community", "community participation", "in Spanish", in the titles, abstracts and keywords. The documentary corpus was formed by 13 articles. Two ways of understanding the community emerged from the documentary review: the first, as a population group to intervene, and the second, in relational key. This article contributes to the understanding of the community in Occupational Therapy and contributes to local analyses and critics
