# SIMULATION OF AGRO-FOOD CHANGES FROM THE DYNAMIC APPROACH OF SYSTEMS: A SYSTEMICAL REVIEW OF LITERATURA

The objective of this work is to identify the conceptual bases and methodological elements to consider in the modeling of the dynamics of the agri-food supply chains, as well as the opportunities of research in the field of study. To this end, a systematic review of literature was developed that started from the definition of a search strategy which consisted in the construction of an equation based on the key words identified, and later to be entered into the databases of Scopus and Web of Science. In the review only articles in English language published in the period from 2010 to 2021 were considered. For the selection of the works to be analyzed, a review of the title and summary was carried out, verifying that these were related to the topic of study, then those published in high impact journals and with a higher level of citation were selected. For the information extraction, a matrix was made that contained aspects such as type of chain, class of model, approach to the approach of sustainability, key variables, parameters, main cycles of feedback and validation performed, scenarios analyzed, among other aspects. This study is selected, since the simulation of agro-food chains, given their characteristics and challenges, is today a growing field of work. In this way, the work provides important considerations to model this type of chains
