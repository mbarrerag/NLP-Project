# SUSTAINABLE ENERGY IN ISLATE AREAS OF LATIN AMERICA: REVIEW OF THE INTEGRATION OF THE WEF NEXUS APPROACH TO IMPLEMENT RURAL DEVELOPMENT

This study provides an exhaustive review of the approach to water-energy-food (WEF Nexus) in the context of Latin America, with an emphasis on rural development through the implementation of sustainable energy in remote areas. The WEF Nexus approach has been consolidated as an essential tool for managing resources in isolated and vulnerable areas. The energy situation in Latin America is contextualized and the methods and materials used in the literature review are detailed, highlighting knowledge about the integration of the WEF Nexus approach into rural development projects and its importance for addressing socio-economic and environmental challenges in isolated communities. It concludes by examining the diagnosis of WEF Nexus conditions in the non-interconnected areas of Colombia, underlining the need for holistic approaches and raising new questions for future research, including aspects of implementation and long-term assessment of the impact of these interventions
