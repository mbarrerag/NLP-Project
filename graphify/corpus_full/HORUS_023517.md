# ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYSIS OF TRANSFER OF TECHNOLOGY IN URBAN AGRICULTURE DEMTERIAL SPACE

In urban agriculture, part of the technology transfer is carried out through training workshops in demonstration spaces. Due to the heterogeneity of actors, it is necessary to generate transfer strategies that are in line with the characteristics and environment of the stakeholders. This research was developed in three demonstration spaces of urban agriculture previously established in the Colombian Agricultural Research Corporation -AGROSAVIA, located in the departments of Córdoba, Cundinamarca and Nariño in Colombia. The objective was to characterize these spaces at the economic, social, institutional and technological level and to analyze the perception of the actors on the workshops carried out between 2015 and 2016. Based on the analysis of 17 semi-structured interviews, 64 virtual surveys and the systematization of 2 collaborative construction workshops with external actors, a proposal was structured with a focus on the exchange of experiences aimed mainly at women (66%) and students aged between 18 to 28 years (30 per cent).
