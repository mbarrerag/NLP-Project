# COMPACT SELF-INTERFERENCE DIGITAL HOLOGRAPHIC MICROSCOPE BASED ON A BUDGET FRESNEL BI-MIRROR

A compact off-axis self-interference digital holographic microscope (DHM) is presented. Using a bi-mirror as the shearing element, this architecture enables single-shot spherical-aberration-free quantitative imaging for a fraction of the cost of similar DHM systems
