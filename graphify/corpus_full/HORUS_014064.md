# Management of the Justice Needs of the People Ette Ennaka: A Proposal for a Cultural Strengthening Route

The approach achieved between the research team and the Ette Ennaka people, through the experience of the seedbed called _x0093_Right and Justice of the Ette Ennaka people: experiences of the exercise of justice of 3 communities of the departments of Magdalena and Cesar_x0094_, developed in the framework of the Call for the Selection of Semilleros of Research 2022 of the Faculty of Law, Political and Social Sciences, allowed a general contextualization, but robust of the dynamics that, around the right of the people, weave in the own right. This process was also based on a line of work initiated by the Fourth Court of Decongestion Civil of the Specialised Circuit in Restitution of Lands of Santa Marta, in which it was decided in favor of the restitution of lands to the people in question and, moreover, it ordered a work plan of cultural strengthening in charge of ICANH and the University of the Magdalene, based on the visibility and treatment of multiple problems generated by external actors that were violently imposed, choosing in a change of the ancestral territory of the people and various problems related to the administration of the above mentioned. The essential basis of this School is in its worldview, in relation to Nara Yaao (the creator god of the Ette), customs, justice needs, capacity and potentialities identified by the community. Thus, this exercise aims to decant in the concreteness of a work plan for each community with which this process will be developed (the major Issa Oristunna reservation, the Itti Takke reservation and the Narakajmanta settlement). In order to generate and/or strengthen a self-recognition of community management capacities and, in parallel, this is put into practice through actions to manage conflict at the head of the community, to lay the foundations for what could become a School of Propio Law
