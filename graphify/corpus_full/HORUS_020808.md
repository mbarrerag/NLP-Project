# Campesino territoriality: absent in political projects in Eastern Antioquia

This article examines the functionalities traced for the rural area of Eastern Antioquia in the political projects of territorial intervention deployed in the last 20 years, based on a theoretical-methodological approach to territorial studies. For this, an approximation is made to the political stakes on a supramunicipal scale, to their approaches and to the functionalities assigned to fragments of this geographical space; finally, emphasis is placed on the changes and tensions produced. In this subregion, highly affected by the armed conflict in the 80s and 90s, a series of bets with a long-term vision are being implemented today, weaving multiple forms of territorial reduction, divisions that do not recognize peasant territoriality or their links with the territory. As a result, the overcoming of the armed conflict has meant a deepening of a model focused on agro-industry, energy extractivism, ecosystem services and what the subregion offers. This has resulted in a fragmentation based on an economic functionality, which has as an effect the direct or indirect denial of a peasant subject, his practices and knowledge, as well as his ways of intervening in the territory
