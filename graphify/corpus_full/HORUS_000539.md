# COMPARISON ANALYSIS OF THE STRUCTURAL PROPERTIES OF A WATER SDS MICELA AND THEIR INTERACTION WITH THE SOLVENT USING THE CHARMM36/TIP3P AND GROMOS53A6/SPC MODELS

The structural and dynamic properties of a sodium dodecyl sulphate (SDS) micella in water were evaluated by molecular dynamic simulations with two combinations of force fields: CHARMM36/TIP3P and GROMOS53A6/SPC. Properties such as the rotation radius, the surface accessible to solvent, the free-solvatation energy and molecular interactions between the hydrophilic part and water were determined. The results show that the combination of CHARMM36/TIP3P models predicts a higher number of hydrogen bonds and a stronger interaction between the sulfate group and water, while GROMOS53A6/SPC estimates a higher solvatization energy, indicating a high affinity of the micela for water. Both force fields adequately describe the micellar system; however, the combined CHARMM36/TIP3P models offer a greater accuracy in the description of molecular interactions
