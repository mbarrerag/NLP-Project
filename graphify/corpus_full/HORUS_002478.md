# MODELS OF GOVERNANCE IN SOCIAL, POPULAR AND SOLIDARY ECONOMIC ORGANIZATIONS: TRANSPARENCY AS A GERENCE STYLE

Social, popular and solidarity economic organizations (ESPYS) play an essential role in building inclusive and sustainable economies. These entities, characterized by their orientation towards collective well-being and solidarity, require governance models that reflect their fundamental values. The paper analyzes the main governance models in this type of organizations and proposes transparency as a key management style to strengthen their legitimacy and effectiveness
