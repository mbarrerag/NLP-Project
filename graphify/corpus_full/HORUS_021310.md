# Thinking the country: analysis and cartographic production of the potentialities of the Colombian landscape

The concept of landscape in Colombia has not developed beyond the recognition of international agents of a cultural framework common in a given territory, and the legislations that indirectly endorse its protection are limited to purely descriptive issues, understanding the landscape strictly as a geographical scenario and ignoring the symbolic, spatial, cultural, visual and economic conditions that span the term. That is, the landscape in our country has been limited conceptually by its indeterminacy and by the absence of theories that allow its semantic expansion, which has made it impossible to construct meaning at the local level and has relegated its appearance in the laws of the country to a mere formal question in the best of cases, or to a simple representation of polygonal traces of territorial planning maps. In this panorama, it is necessary to create methods that consolidate a strategy of treatment, protection and valuation of the landscape in the country, so the project Think the country, from the research group of spatial studies and the semillero homonym. To fulfill the proposed objectives, the project contemplates a methodological articulation of the functions that start from the simultaneousness of its components. That is, as we progress in the construction of the state of art _x0096_ that would comprise the field of research _x0096_, teaching processes would be advanced in the formation of students through chairs and the seedbed of research arranged for this purpose, favoring academic production through the realization of an academic event, writing of articles, presentation of papers and other associated products. In turn, preserving the interest of the members of the academic community, and adding them to the objective of managing alliances with external sectors and formulating projects, which would shape the triad between research, teaching and extension. In conclusion, the project starts from a conceptual and semantic inquiry around the landscape, places research hypotheses from the Semillero de Estudios Espaciales, and contemplates an adaptation of the cartographic method for academic (and artistic) production and results in the identification of potentialities in the territories
