# THE WORLD OF WORK AND THE HEALTH OF WORKERS IN THE POST COVID-19

The current situation resulting from the pandemic by Covid-19 has had a profound impact on the world of work, negatively affecting the majority of the working population in different dimensions: job losses; income decrease; intensification of work via telework or remote work; accentuation of forms of work via digital platforms; strengthening the triple burden for working women; loss of the limits between the work environment with the domestic one, as well as working hours with rest ones; physical and mental health problems related to these changes in the organization of work and deepening the inequalities in relation to the recognition of Covid-19 as a professional disease only for a certain working group, among other matters (Torres-Tovar, 2020).The circumstances of the world of work in recent decades, product of the implementation of neoliberal labour policies on the labor situation have been established as common the flexible, temporary, informal, that have determined precarized, unsafe and dangerous forms of work for the health and life of workers (Antunes, 2012).These dynamics of the labor processes in the context of the pandemic have intensified the labor situation.Considering of these dynamic forms and the post-life situation, the workers can be more dangerous and the workers can be affected by the effects of the effects of the
