# TECTONICS OF THE NICE COLOMBIAN

The complex tectonic history of the Colombian west is discussed through a structural cut from Medellín to the Serrania del Baudó. In the section, two major fault systems are observed. The fault system or shear zone of Romeral and the system of Dabeiba Pueblo rico, both constituents of limits of fundamental tectonic blocks. The system of faults of Romeral corresponds to a transpressive system with vergence to the W, formed by structures mainly Paleocenas with reactivations to the Miocenas E. The system of faults Dabeiba Pueblo Rico corresponds to the area of high strain possibly generated by the collision of the block Panama Chocó. This zone preserves Miocenas structures. To the east of this system a area of fold with truth to the E that functions as a local Mioceno foreland has been developed. In this sector also preserved direction structures possibly Paleogenes associated with a different tectonic event.
