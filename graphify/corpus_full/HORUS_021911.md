# Implications of relationation in transformative innovation.

This research analyses the characteristics of a network that promotes the formation of transformative innovation, with the aim of extracting the most important aspects that give strength to this type of formal and informal alliances and agreements, and in this way, to offer guidance on the capacities that must be developed to execute projects with this approach, so that they can be replicated in different contexts. Quantitative analyses of network metrics are presented, based on Network Theory. In addition, an instrument is proposed to measure technological and transformative innovation capabilities, which is used within a case analysis. It is also identified that the centrality metrics: medium degree, varied centrality, central intermediation and centrality of own vector, usually coincide with the actors with the best performance regarding the technological capabilities of linkage and diffusion, and also with the transformative capacities of generation of networks and expectations. It also identifies that technological capacities of appropriation and market, coupled with the transformative capacities of: autonomy, incidence and legitimacy, relational governance and scaling, are the most difficult to manage. Finally, it is expected to contribute to a much clearer conceptual scheme, taken from all the groups of interest
