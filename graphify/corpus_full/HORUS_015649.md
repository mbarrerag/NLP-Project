# Implementation, monitoring and analysis of a strategy for teaching natural sciences using ABI

The role of the teacher in the teaching - learning processes is fundamental, hence the relevance of the teaching training as pedagogical and didactic update for the teaching of the sciences. In response to this need, particularly in a group of teachers in the municipality of Guateque-Boyacá, a strategy of teacher training based on the learning based on research (ABI) was designed and implemented, with emphasis on experimentation for teaching on topics of flat geometry and measurement. This strategy was mediated by a process of action research, where the experience logbook became the tool of reflection of the pedagogical and didactic knowledge of the teachers involved. With the analysis of the information collected in the implementation, the levels of understanding and appropriation of the methodology were determined by the teachers and it was possible to conclude that the training strategy requires continuous follow-up processes to promote the transformation of pedagogical practices in the teaching of the sciences, and the necessity to further implement teachers based on their learning experience. .
