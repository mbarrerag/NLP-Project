# DISPARING RISKS. ACADEMIC AND PROFESSIONS DYNAMICS OF A FARC-EP ODDONHOLOG

The first step of the guerrilla movement was to create a communist government in Colombia. Its military actions were mainly carried out in rural areas, where health services are scarce. For this reason, one of the political strategies of this guerrilla group was to train its own staff on health issues, including dentists, and thus to provide care to its troops and to the civilian population living in the territories where it exercised political and military control. To characterize the process of academic training and professional practice of dentists inside the FARC-EP, and the processes of dental care to the members of this guerrilla group and to the civilian population. Qualitative research with an ethnographic focus on a history of life. It was worked with a person who was a guerrilla and dental practitioner within the FARC-EP. It was carried out in the area of the municipality, in which the latter was located. In 2016, this person joined the peace process between the national government and FARC-EP, returning to civilian life where he has found legal barriers and is not allowed to practice as a dentist. The process of academic training and professional practice of dentists was generated from the needs of FARC-EP to serve its own troops and the population of its influence. This process was developed with limited human and technical resources with an insufficient curriculum for comprehensive professional training. This was given in a scenario where the priority was to safeguard life in a context of war
