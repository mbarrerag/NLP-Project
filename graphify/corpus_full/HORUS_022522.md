# Effect of metformin and oral combinations on type II diabetes mellitus on the risk and prognosis of cardiovascular disease: systematic review.

Cardiovascular disease causes one in three deaths in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Current evidence on the effects of antidiabetes drugs to control and reduce cardiovascular complications is moderate and inconclusive. To determine the effectiveness and safety of metformin and combinations with I-DPP4 and I-SGLT2, administered orally, as a protective factor against cardiovascular diseases. A review was performed in the Medline, PubMed, and Cochrane Library databases using specific terms. The search was conducted in English and Spanish for the period 2010 to 2020. The quality of the studies and risk of bias were evaluated. 911 publications were found. After applying the inclusion and exclusion criteria, 13 documents were selected: 5 RTC, 2 observational studies and 6 other categories (three reports, two RTCs and one diabetes guide).Seven articles were selected for this review. This review provides an overview of the evidence available up to the moment on the efficacy and safety of the use of metformin alone or in combination of SGTL2 and I-DPP4
