# Archaeological tourism: the senses and the trajection. A study on the way to the Teyuna Archaeological Park - Lost City

Tourism is a complex economic axis that structures and mercantilizes territories until recently marginal. This transforms the territories accelerated, generating impacts –especially- on the local communities and nature. Such is the case of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta and the tourism that takes place in the Teyuna – Lost City Archaeological Park. This type of tourism is particular because it involves walking a territory to reach a destination, so the tour is part of the visitor experience where the encounter with its inhabitants and their environment feed the tourist experience. In this thesis is explored through the sensory geography and the identification of sensory landscapes, the perceptions of tourists and their own in the trajection of the road to Teyuna. The cultural and environmental impacts that leave the action of walking towards Teyuna are generally negative: the transformations of the environment, the overload and tourism and the economic inequality that is between the tourists and the ones in the journey of the road to Teyuna. The cultural and environmental impacts of walking to Teyuna, is an example of the traditional landscape, and the identification of sensory landscape, the perceptions of tourists and my own perceptions along the costumer to Teyuna.
