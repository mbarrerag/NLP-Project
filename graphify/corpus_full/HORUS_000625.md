# Method of self-learning based on machine learning applied to the diet of an individual with ulcerative colitis. Case study: patient Juan Pablo Aguirre Martínez

The Ulcerative Colitis (CU), is a non-inflamatory Intestinal Inflamatory Disease (III), chronic disease that affects approximately 1 in every 1000 people in the world. This autoimmune disease causes inflammation and ulcers in the colon, triggering symptoms such as diarrhea, bleeding, pain and fatigue, significantly reducing the quality of life. The severity may range from mild to severe, and may even be life-threatening. Although there is no cure, personalized nutrition is key in the management of symptoms. This work proposes a method based on self-learning techniques and Machine Learning to adjust the diet of individuals with CU, taking as a case study case case case the patient Juan Pablo Aguirre Martínez. The justification for this is based on the lack of previous literature on the application of Machine Learning systems to the diet of people with CU. The methodology includes the construction of a data set, review and evaluation of Machine Learning techniques. Precisely, the model’s ability to process temporary data and learn continuously, which led to the choice of the LSTM model. It is expected that the proposed method will contribute to the development of an innovative tool that it will improve the quality of people and the life quality of people and the environment and self-learning technologies and Machine Learning to adjust the diet of individuals with UC, using patient Juan Pablo Aguirre Martínez as a case study.
