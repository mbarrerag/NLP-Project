# Use of galvanic sludge as a raw material in the Ceramics Industry for the Mitigation of Environmental Pollution.

To take advantage of a polluting residue and mitigate the environmental pollution generated by it, sludges from Galvanic Process PTAR with high content of Cr, Ni and Cu were used in the industrial production of traditional ceramic products and at the laboratory level of inorganic pigments for this same industry. Parts for floors and processed walls were synthesized, by two methods used at the industrial level: dry pressing, where the ceramic pieces were sintered in a quick cycle of 34 minutes in a roller oven, and by the method of extrusion, sintered in a slow cycle of 42 hours in a tunnel kiln. Additionally, inorganic pigments were synthesized for the ceramic industry by means of two methods: the traditional mixing galvanic sludge with silica, calcining at 900°C, 1000°C and 1100°C and by the method of extrusion, slurrying out of the sludge with sodium silicate solution, calcining at 600°C, 700°C and 800°C.
