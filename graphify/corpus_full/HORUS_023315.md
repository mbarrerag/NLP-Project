# ANSIENCE, DEPRESSION, SOLIDITY AND ROL OF THE CAREER OF DISEASE PERSONS

The chronic diseases not transmissible (ECNT), generates losses in years of healthy life both in patients and in their caregivers due to constant limitations, some degree of disability and dependence to which the disease compromises them. The majority of patients with ECNT require constant support for the management of the disease and their self-care, which makes evident the need of a person who assumes the role of family caregiver. In this sense it has been documented that there are multiple changes faced by the family caregiver, with complex interactions with other significant individuals within their role, can experience negative feelings, in many cases lives family tensions, which can generate an overload of the tasks to be performed in the day to day, since it begins to assume the role of primary caregiver, which results in results such as deterioration of their quality of life, overload of care, among others. The objective of the study was to describe the levels of depression, anxiety and loneliness of the caregivers of people with chronic disease in the adoption of the role of the family caregiver. An analytical cross-section carried out in Bogotá Colombia between 2020 and 2021 with a sample of 505 main family caregivers of people with chronic disease. The prevalence of anxiety was very close to 42.3% reported in a previous metanolytic study. Limitations: The sample was collected in the middle of closures and isolation orders during the second and third wave of Covid-19 infections in the country. This can affect the results since the effects of the pandemic on the exacerbation of anxiety and depression, in addition to the loneliness resulting from social distancing, have been documented
