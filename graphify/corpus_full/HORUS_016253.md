# VALUATION OF THE PHITO-NUTRIENTS OF THE CROSS PALMA OIL OF THE INTER-SPECTIFIC HIBRIDE E.OLEIFERA X E. GUINEENSIS THROUGH THE USE OF MEMBRANES TECHNOLOGIES AS MECA- COMPOUND SEPARATION NISM: PHYSICAL CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION

Palm oil is one of the most marketed in the world, being an abundant raw material with nutritional potential of interest to the scientific community and the palm sector, especially the interspecific cross Elaeis oleifera x Elaeis guineensis, known as the OxG hybrid, which, in addition to having greater resistance to diseases, mainly Congollo (PC) and Marchitez Letal (ML), has shown better attributes in relation to its oil production and phytonutrient content, which have an important biological function and high nutritional value. These aspects, make the O×G hybrid a matrix of interest in study. The membrane technologies, on the other hand, are constituted as an emerging technology that allow for the separation of compounds processes. Part of this research is focused on the potential that this technology would have as a technique for the separation of phytonutrients from palm oil, specifically from the OxG hybrid. The objective of this study was to carry out a first stage of characterization of raw palm oil (APC) from the hybrid and establish a sample preparation methodology to use this technology
