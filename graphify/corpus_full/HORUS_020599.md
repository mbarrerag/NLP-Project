# Evaluation practices of students and teachers in a teacher training process: A case study in Colombian higher education

This case study aimed to describe the evaluation practices (PE) of a group of students and teachers in the subjects of the pedagogical component of the Bachelor's degree in Philosophy and Languages (English, French, German) of the National University of Colombia. This qualitative study adopted some orientations of the research model of critical ethnography in education proposed by Phil Carspecken (1996). In particular, it is based on interviews with teachers and students, for whose analysis the theory of the architecture of the internships (Kemmis et.al, 2004) was used. In this theory the practices were understood as a form of human activity, which is cooperative, socially established and involves forms of individual understanding.The analysis of the data provided by the students participating in the study allowed the identification of four types of evaluative practices (PE) of teachers of the curriculum of interest and two PE of them as students. As for the teachers, the following types of information were identified: PE in terms of qualification, EP in terms of the process, PE in terms of the students and PE in terms of their PE, PE of the students, the PE of the students, and PE of their own, and PE of their own instruments, and PE of the EP of the students. proposed by Phil Carspecken (1996). Specificly, this study was based on interviews to teachers and students, which were analyzed based on the theory of the practical architectures (Kemmis et.al, 2004).
