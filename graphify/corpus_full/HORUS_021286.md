# Functional analysis of genes associated with loss of susceptibility to Glucantime in Leishmania parasites (Viannia) braziliensis by gene editing by CRISPR-CAS9

Leishmaniasis, is a parasitic disease caused by a protozoan belonging to the genus of Leishmaniasis. Currently, leishmaniasis is considered a neglected tropical disease and a public health problem, adversely impacting health and quality of life in several developing countries. In Colombia, one of the countries that have the most active number of species of the circulating parasite, the disease affects vulnerable population, among them communities living in isolated rural areas in poverty state and military personnel. The first-line treatment are the pentavalent antimonies (SbV), of which there are two different formulations, Pentotam® (sodium stibogluconate) and Glucantime® (merdominal antimony).
