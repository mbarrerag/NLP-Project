# Increasing the visibility of the research group in agro-industrial processes through the publication and dissemination of scientific products

The research group in agro-industrial processes belongs to the Faculty of Agrarian Sciences, and has as its main objective to strengthen research in areas such as food science, food processing and agro-industrial processes. In this group, teachers and students from both the Faculty and the Institute of Food Science and Technology participate. Within the purposes of the group, it is increasing its visibility and its categorization, which is currently C, where promoting an increase in publications is one of the strategies that has been proposed. Currently, an invitation has been received for the publication of a book chapter, and also, there is an important advance in obtaining results for the publication of an article, however, in both cases it is necessary to obtain new results of laboratory analysis so that the works are robust enough to guarantee their evaluation in indexed journals. It is expected that the support offered by the faculty will allow consolidating these publications in order to improve the group's classification
