# SOLATING AND RADIATION IN DIFFERENT GEOGRAPHICAL AREAS OF THE VALLE DE ABURRÁ

The topographical relief of the Aburra Valley generates climatic conditions that vary throughout its territory, which are not taken into account in urban decisions. The objective of the research was to establish the differences of sun and radiation in different areas, to define factors that influence their variability, and to propose alternatives for different conditions. Nine blocks were selected and modelled on hillside and plain, in three strips of the valley, and simulations were made through computational programs to define microclimates in terms of sunning and radiation. It is concluded that: there are important climatic differences between the eastern, western and plain slopes; solar radiation in the tracks is reduced, according to their orientation and treeization; the municipalities to the south have better performance in attenuation of radiation; radiation in the west facades differs up to 29% and the streets in the east direction do the west have more time of solar radiation per day on average
