# Federalism in Colombia (1853-1886): a conceptual political history.

This paper analyses the political dispute over the meaning of federalism in Colombia, between 1853 and 1886. It sets out an interpretative framework according to which the concept is transformed into a “trialectic” process, which links the constraints of the cultural matrix, the processes of receiving discursive elements and political practices in search of hegemony. The conflict is shaped around two constants. First, the dispute by the holder of sovereignty and constituent power, resulting from regional interests and identities. Second, the thought scheme provided by the science of legislation, which evaluates the effectiveness of the institutions in function of the characteristics of the people who adopt them. Around them, new discursive elements emerge gradually that are articulated in the discourses in dispute. This perspective complements political history and conceptual history, re-establishing the representations that the actors of the time built from their own experience.
