# Parking planning on the main roads of the centre of Tumaco

The roads in the city centre of Tumaco are road congestion due to poor parking in them. Therefore, a strategy is proposed to promote the proper use of the roads. To this end, the demand for users was first characterized, the deficit of parking lots was determined with measurements of use, and the location of new off-road parking facilities and the appropriate signage on the road was proposed
