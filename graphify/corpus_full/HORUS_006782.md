# Identification of tectonic and structural features of the Tumaco basin base from the interpretation of potential field anomalies

In order to reduce the uncertainty related to the base of the Tumaco basin in the southern part of the Pacific Coast of Colombia, the identification of tectonic and structural features of the basin base was carried out from the interpretation of anomalies of potential fields and their integration with seismological information and solutions of focal mechanisms. The structural investment in 3D of the gravimetric residual shows the development of a sedimentary basin with orientation N33°E that deepens towards the south, where it reaches 9.5km of depth. The analysis of spectral correlation, indices of local favorability and application of techniques of enhancement to 43km2 of aerogravimetric and aeromagnetic data and its integration with the position of seismic events and focal mechanisms allowed to identify guidelines and structures in the basement, where the right lateral fault with orientation N73°E predominates, influenced by the subduction of the Nazca plate under South America mode (instead of the soil). of 9.5 km. The analysis of spectral correlation, local favourability indexes, and the application of enhancement techniques to 43 km2 of airborne gravity and airborne magnetic data, combined with the location of sixmic events and focal mechanisms, allowed the identification of lines and structures in the foundation. These structures are predominantly characterized by right-lateral failure with an orientation of N73°E, influenced by the subduction of the Nazca Plate beneath South America, and a change in structure orientation north of 3.4° latitude.
