# Valorisation of rice husks by thermocatalytic processing for the production of mesoporous amorphous silica

The following research focused on the systematic study of the effects of milling, screening, liffviation and thermal decomposition of rice husks for obtaining mesoporous amorphous biosilica amorfa. To this end, it was evaluated as the experimental factors of the previous treatments, determine the physical and chemical and thermal properties of the treated rice husk, as well as the physical and chemical properties of the obtained biosilica. The kinetic triplets of the combustion and the pyrolysis of the raw and treated husk are established, and the effect of a catalyst on the pyrolysis process was evaluated. It was determined that the particle size of the molated and sieved rice husk significantly affects the physical and chemical properties of the treated shell, making possible to classify some granulometric fractions in homogeneous groups. The model was based on the synthesis of the mesoporous biosilica of 99,45 ± 0.04 % of purity, 318 ± 10 m2 g-1 of surface area and 0,46 ± 0.01 cm3 g-1 of poresss. The kinetic triplets of combustion and pyrolysis of the raw and treated spindle were determined; in the same way, the effect of a catalyst in the pyrolysis process was evaluated. It was found that the particle size of the mature and Sieved rice spindle significantly affected the physicochemical and thermal properties of the trafficked spindle. It is possible to classify some granulometric fractions into homogenous groups. Mesoporous biosilica of 99.45 ± 0.04 % of purity, 318 ± 10 m2 g-1 effect area and 0.46 ± 0.01 cm3 g-1pore volume, was synthesized the from rice riding in HCl at pH 1.5.
