# THE PLUMUDA (SPPISTHONEMA SPP.), POTENTIAL FOOD OF THE NARIÑAN PACIFIC

Due to the opportunity to use low commercial value species for the development of innovative products, the high seasonal availability of two small pelagic species of the Colombian Pacific, the plumuda (Opisthonema spp.) and the carduma (Cetengraulis mysticetus), which have a high content and a very good quality of protein, as well as the presence of beneficial lipids for cardiovascular functioning and the central nervous system (Hleap et al., 2010), a project was proposed with the aim of developing a nutget type product based on the mixture of pastes obtained with the useful part of the plum and carduma
