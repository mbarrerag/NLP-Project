# Microbiological identification of some culturable native strains associated with the digestive tract in Panaque cochliodon

To characterize by microbiology the native strains associated to the intestinal tract of the species Panaque cochliodon. Three adult specimens captured in the Magdalena River that were transported and sacrificed under animal welfare regulations were used. Dissection of the intestinal tract was performed, obtaining samples for microbiological isolation, using culture media, purifying the microorganisms, performing metabolic biochemical tests and API 20E (Biomeriux) tests for their identification. Information on the microbial population structure was obtained, reporting the phylum Proteobacteria with the species: Pantoea sp, Erwinia sp, Providencia stuarti, Providencia alcalifaciens, Serratia ficaria, Citrobacter koseri and the phylum Firmicutes with the species: Bacillus sphaericus, Bacillus subtilis, Bacillus thurigiensis, Bacillus mycoides, Bacillus coagulans and Bacillus circulans. The predominant culturable microorganisms in P. cohliodon belong to the phylum Proteobacteria and the phylum Firmicutes
