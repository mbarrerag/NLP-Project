# On centralizers and pseudo-multidegree functions for non-commutative rings having PBW bases

In this paper, we introduce a notion of pseudo-multidegree function on non-commutative rings having Poincaré-Birkhoff-Witt (PBW) bases with the aim of establishing a criterion to determine whether the centralizer of an element has finite dimension over the non-commutative ring. We illustrate our results with remarkable families of algebras studied in the literature
