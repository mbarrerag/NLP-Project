# The public space as articulator of urban renewal in the sector of Bazurto, Cartagena de Indias

In the sector of Bazurmicto located in the city of Cartagena, since 1978, the central market of abastos of the city operates. It is bordered by the Ciénaga de las Quintas and close to the hill of the Popa, currently the sector is waiting to be intervened for the transfer of the market of the city and transform the space through a project of integral urban intervention, highlighting the sociocultural identity of the place. By the dynamics of the operation of an urban market in this sector, conflicts are presented that mainly affect the residents of the surrounding neighborhoods, the citizens, by problems in mobility, pollution of the environment, overcrowding of informal occupants of the public space, deteriorating the urban environment of Bazurto. In the present Final Work of Maestria (TFM) a proposal of urban renewal is established through the intervention of the public space that renews the sector in deterioration and potentials the urban attributes, socio-cultural and environmental of the place. environmental pollution, overcrowding of informal sales by occupying the public space and damage to the urban environment of Bazurto. In this Final Master's Project (TFM) an urban design proposal is established through the intervention of public space that renews the damaged sector and enhancements the urban, sociocultural and environmental attributes of the place. Through the principles of urban design of diversity, proximity, accessibility, permeability, sociocultural and environmental identity, an urban renewal project is formulated with the intention of revitalizing what exists and creating new spaces in the pursuit of well-being and quality of urban life, safeguarding the heritage of the cultural traditions of citizens. urban renewal, public space, urban life design, urban society of life, urban society of environmentality, urban life, safeguarding the heritage of the cultural traditions of citizens
