# FROM THE LITIC PANTS TO THE HOLOGRAPHY AND ELECTRONIC PANTS

From the bilitary screens to the holographic and electronic screens. Multi-medialities and hypertextual connections in Colombia. The following is a commitment to connect between my practical experiences as creator in the area of artistic technologies (more specifically as a filmmaker in electronic arts and in holography), with my research interests in the field of so-called medial archaeologies (with the concrete case of research of the paintings that are caveats in the Serranía of the Lindosa as screen), and finally of the profession as a teacher in media art at the National University of Colombia. The first element that is put into play is that of artistic creation, as a place of experimentation where the affective experiences that are put into value are conjured in the moment of working with materials that is their nature and origin. Experimentation that seeks to transform initial conditions and evidence and transmutate the materialities that enter into the world and configure new alterities in the social space.
