# Mineralological characterization of hydrocarbon impregnation levels in study areas of the Magdalena Valley and Caguán-Putumayo basins in Colombia

This study presents a spectral analysis of the mineralological characteristics of the sands bituminous in the basins of the Upper Valley of the Magdalena and Caguán-Putumayo, in Colombia. To characterize the samples and determine their mineralological and geochemical properties associated with the impregnation of hydrocarbons, analysis was carried out using spectroscopy VNIR-SWIR, applied to both hydrocarbon extracts and to samples of impregned rock. The results reveal a predominance of caolinite with variable crystalinity, as well as the presence of inter-strategic phases of illite-smetic and esmetic in the impregnated levels. The hydrocarbon was identified spectrally as asphalt and bitumen for the basins of the VSM and CP, respectively. It was evidenced that the hydrocarbide present in the samples tends to mask the spectral signals of the minerals of the clay in the range NIR-SWIR, making it difficult to identify precisely. However, after the extraction of the hydrocarbide, it was possible to detect minerals as canitite, esmetic and inter-strate.
