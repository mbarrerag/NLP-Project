# Opportunities and challenges for the modernization of hydroelectric power plants in Colombia.

Most of the large hydroelectric plants currently installed in Colombia have more than three decades of operation, which have fulfilled the useful life for which they were designed, have inefficient subprocesses, their systems, equipment and components have deteriorated, their technology in some cases is obsolete, they have high operating and maintenance costs, their efficiency, their reliability and their availability have diminished. This technical deterioration makes it necessary to implement modernization processes that allow to guarantee the recovery of their efficiency and their operational continuity in conditions similar or even better than the original ones. This modernization is possible and necessary in the current context of accelerated technological changes, where the technology own of a hydroelectric power plant can be again obsolete in a few years, moreover, allows to guarantee greater continuity in the long term, so it is necessary to foresee this type of processes in a frequent and planned way. In this study it identifies opportunities of modernization in this type of facilities for large hydroelectric plants in Colombia.
