# Gender inequalities in research and management positions: Associate Professors and Professors at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters (2015-2021).

This research is the result of a quantitative descriptive observational study, whose purpose was to characterize the gender inequalities present in research and in the management positions of professors and associate professors of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters, and to explore the relationship between these inequalities and health. To this end, a virtual and anonymous survey was designed and applied to a non-probabilistic sample of volunteers, which took as a reference a pre-pandemic period (2015-2019) and pandemic (2020-2021). Based on the results obtained, a statistical analysis was carried out in three phases (descriptive, bivariate and multivariate), whose discussion was focused on a gender and feminist perspective, taking into account the categories of social production and reproduction addressed from the sexual division of labour. Finally, a series of recommendations are proposed for the approach to this problem at the University, as well as guidelines for future research, which have an interest in further deepening on this topic
