# Building memory, repairing pain: collective reparation for the National Prohousing Central.

This work aims to analyze the understandings of damage and collective reparation, particularly in the process of reparation advanced with the National Prohousing Central (CENAPROV). At the first moment, a approach to transitional justice is made from the international level, making a particular analysis of the concepts of damage and reparation. At a second moment, a context is realized on the transitional reality in Colombia, referring to the mechanisms and conceptions of damage and collective reparation. Finally, at a third moment, an analysis is made of the diagnosis of damage of the National Prohousing Central and a presentation of some debates and recommendations on collective reparation is made in the case of political organizations such as CENAPROV
