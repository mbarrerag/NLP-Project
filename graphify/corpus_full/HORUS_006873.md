# Development of a BIM-based performance measurement system for social housing projects

This study addresses the lack of a clear measurement system to evaluate the performance in housing projects of social interest VIS, during the design stages. Through an exhaustive research, crucial criteria and indicators were identified to evaluate the success in construction projects, highlighting the lack of specific measurement practices during the design phase. The literature review and interviews with experts revealed essential criteria such as Cost, Time, Quality, Health and Safety, Environmental, Customer Satisfaction, among others. In addition, the use of BIM methodologies in housing projects was explored, analyzing the information generated and validating it through surveys to BIM Managers. This provided the conceptual basis for integrating BIM data into performance measurement. Thus, an effective strategy was proposed to use BIM data and BI technologies, developing a structured system with six success criteria and 17 performance indicators, automating the obtaining of data to improve efficiency and minimize resources.
