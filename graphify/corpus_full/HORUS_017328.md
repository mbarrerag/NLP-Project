# WHAT HAPPENS WHEN WE GET CONNECTED AT HOME

What happens when we connect at home emerged as an alternative to public and private institutional explanations and the permanent presence of the opinion of the specialists during the first half of 2020, when they felt called to present, describe and explain what would happen in the midst of the disconcerting technological content in trade, work, study, sex, medicine, sport, religion and entertainment. It emerged at that time and in the house because, while we heard these explanations from groups and officials, in the near experiences it seemed that we lived the first general quarantine, decreed in the Colombian cities, in a way very different from those announced in the official media landscape. In short, this research deals with those first months of the year 2020 in which, in the presence of the COVID-19, the technological agency, emphasized, in all forms of coexistence between living beings, both human and non-human, and the encounters and disagreements between living beings and what we call virtual things, objects, appliances or technology, was recognized, not only the devices and dogs, when they marked the course of cohabitation between living beings, both human beings and non-humans, and the meetings and disagreements between living beings and those who, and those who are not in which we The second part of the book is dedicated to reviewing and publishing the auto archives of two women, in collections made during several months of 2020, in which changes in the perception of the pandemic are noticed: one, entitled "Auto archivos en dispositivos personales", is the messages received, not sent, to two mobile phone devices that they decided to save, or at least not delete; the other, "Personal registers of mobilizations in Bogotá", is integrated by the photographs taken by one of these women in the numerous social mobilizations that took place in the city of Bogotá; in its streets, corners and parks, against the monuments and public conventional writings, and in which the marchant@s and Protestant@s created, produced and published images and letters counting on that they would be viewed from drones and helicopters. Therefore they filled the floor of the streets and avenues, of messages demanding rights and respect for all living beings, waters and lands.
