# Influence of water activity on oxidation of the inchi sacha oil (Plukenetia volubilis L.) formulated in oil - water - surfactant systems

The oils of plant origin rich in polyunsaturated fatty acids are of great interest in the pharmaceutical and cosmetic fields given the benefits they offer thanks to their composition. However, this type of compounds are susceptible to the effects of lipid oxidation, which compromises the stability and safety of the products in which they are included. For this reason it is useful to find strategies for their stabilization, which should be in tune with the trends in the development of products, such as the design of minimalist formulations. In this sense, this research is devolved by the decrease of the excipients in the formula by exploring the possible correlation between the water activity and the oxidation of the oil either as matter or in heterodispersive systems type emulsion, emulgel and bigel of similar composition whose water activities were dependent on the presence or non-glycerol in its composition. The results suggest that the activity of water to influence the primary and secondary oxidation of the oil especially to water activities less than 0.5.
