# Characteristics of the recommendations included in the clinical practice guidelines commissioned by the Ministry of Health of Colombia (2013-2021): a systematic review

In the last decade, and after publication of a methodological guide for the development of clinical practice guides (GPC), the Ministry of Health and Social Protection of Colombia (MSPS) commissioned the generation or update of a series of GPCs. However, so far there is no diagnosis of the characteristics of the recommendations made in those GPCs. Explore the characteristics of the recommendations included in the GPC commissioned by the MSPS between 2013 and 2021. A systematic review of Colombian GPCs published between 2013 and 2021. To be included, the GPCs had to report the certainty of the evidence and the strength of the recommendation in 70% or more of their recommendations according to the GRADE methodology. Information was obtained on the certainty, direction and strength of each recommendation.
