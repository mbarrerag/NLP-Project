# IV INTERNATIONAL RESEARCH CONGRESS IN DESIGN IV CIDI - ORGANISED AS THE COORDINATOR OF MAESTRY IN DESIGN OF THE FACULTY OF ARTS OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The IV International Congress of Research in Design: Horizont+es will be a space for reflection and academic exchange between researchers/as and interested in research in Design. In this opportunity it invites the design community especially to consider the projection and future of design both in Colombia and Latin America. The above, however, does not invalidate the possibility that they have to participate research-doers/as in the various fields of relation to design. To this end, this Congress proposes the following six thematic axes: social realities, public policies, citizenships, theory and history of design, systemic design, future and design (UX-UI). In the congress papers, posters, were developed at the workshop and proposals were presented for research that were being initiated, as well as some that were already completed. It was intended that the International Congress of Research in Design in its fourth version with realization between 4 and 6 October 2023, was a space for reflection and academic exchange of researchers and assistants around the projection in the time and future of the design, which was the purpose of receiving the title of HorizonDI + six
