# Prevalence and morphometry of the anterolateral ligament of the knee in a sample of human corpses in Colombia

The anatomy of the knee and its ligaments is clearly described in the literature; however, several studies have found new structures related to this joint, such as the anterolateral ligament (LAL) of the knee. The prevalence of this ligament is very variable worldwide and studies on the subject in Colombian population are scarce. Determine the prevalence of LAL and its morphometric characteristics in a sample of Colombian human corpses. Cross-sectional study in which the knee joints of 15 human corpses were dissected and analyzed. The specimens should not have previous dissections to guarantee the integrity of anatomical structures as ligaments and tendons, so that only 29 knees were included. Through the dissection the presence of the ligament was verified and the site of proximal and distal insertion was established, as well as its length and width. The prevalence of LAL was 24.13%, as this was identified in 7 of the 29 dissected knees (4 left knees and 3 right).
