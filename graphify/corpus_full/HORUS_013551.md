# In vitro evaluation of the effect of electrical stimulation on chondrocytes grown in a three-dimensional construct

The treatment of degenerative pathologies of the hyalino cartilage remains a clinical challenge. Biophysical stimuli such as electrical fields seem to be a promising non-invasive tool for the restoration of cartilage tissue. In this in vitro study, the effects of electrical fields coupled capacitatively with a voltage in the form of sinusoid wave of 50 V (corresponding to 7.7 mV/cm) and 100 V (corresponding to 8.7 mV/cm) were evaluated with a frequency of 60 kHz for 21 days, in the condrocitos grown in gelatine hydrogels. From the samples stimulated and controls, quantitative measurements of cell proliferation and detection of glycosaminoglycans were carried out.
