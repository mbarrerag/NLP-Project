# COLOMBIAN GUIDE FOR THE USE OF PROBIOTICS IN PEDIATRIC PATHOLOGIES

In the last 25 years, the use of probiotics has suffered an important metamorphosis thanks to the science ómico, which has generated a prolific scientific information and, consequently, an increase of its clinical use.Probiotics are defined as "living microorganisms that, when administered in adequate quantities, confer benefits to the health of the host".The relationship between intestinal microbiota and the health status of the people is every day proven, therefore, it is important to analyze from the scientific evidence which probiotics are useful in certain pediatric pathologies. To provide the medical community with an exhaustive analysis about the use of probiotics and establish recommendations for the pediatric population. Material and methods.
