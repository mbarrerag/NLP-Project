# Fertile Sumapazuelo

The seedbed J&D Solum belonging to the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences, Department of Agronomy, is a valuable research space in topics related to soil quality, spatial analysis and verification and development of methods for determining physical, chemical and biological properties of the soil as quality indicators.Sumapazuelo Fertil is a pilot project of the seedbed J&D Solum focused on the assessment of soil quality and on the verification and development of veracious methods for determining indicators. As the first stage of the project, we seek to establish the baseline in soil quality issues in a single demonstration farm of Sumapaz, which will provide us with essential information for the region, design a preliminary soil quality index, establish the spatial variation of some physical, chemical and biological properties and have a living laboratory to verify, adjust and validate methodologies. Aspects that will allow to strengthen the research capacities of the seedbed, and generate information to increase its visibility.
