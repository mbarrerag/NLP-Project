# Biobased plasticizer from agroindustrial residual streams.

The distillate of the deodorized soybean oil (SODD) is an agro-industrial residue that can be used as a raw material to obtain a wide spectrum of oleochemical products. In this direction, the selection of an oleochemical derivative with plasticising potential was initially carried out using a method of product design assisted by computer. This method allowed to make a selection between potentially useful molecules by evaluating different criteria associated with its performance as a plasticizer. The criteria selected were compatibility, efficiency, permanence, toxicity and cost. These criteria were predicted for the candidate molecules using group contribution methods and empirical correlations. As a result of the selection method, it was found that the epoxidated isobutyl esters can be promissory plasticizers for polymers such as PVC. Subsequently, the epoxides of the isobutyl fatty esters were produced experimentally using a two-step reaction process: sterification and epoxidation. In addition, a kinetic model of epoxidation of esters was obtained which were considered as isobutyl, and the result of selectivity was studied
