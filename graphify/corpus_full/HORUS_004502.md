# Carbon footprint mitigation through anaerobic digestion in a rural municipality of Colombia

The environmental challenges facing the municipality of Tumaco (Colombia) include the energy supply and disposal of organic wastes (RSO), especially those related to artisanal fishing (RP) and Fruit and Vegetable Waste (RFV). Thus, the population resorts to steady-state plants DIESEL and to the consumption of LPG, generating polluting emissions and affecting their health. In addition, the RSO contributes to the emissions of Greenhouse Gas (GEI), evaluated in terms of Carbon Foot (HC). This research focuses on taking advantage of the RP and RFP through the Anaerobic Digestion (DA) to obtain biogas as an energy source and reduce HC in the territory. Two systems are proposed: the current system (system 1) involving emissions from fuel burning and inadequate disposal of waste, and the proposed system (system 2) that uses the DA to produce biogas as a result of the RSO. The study covers five stages: (1) contextualization of the problem and collection of information, (2) calculation of the theoretical carbon footprint (HCT), (3) collection of samples, (4) evaluation of the measurement of the SID, and/IT5. The study strategy is based on the results of the study and analysis of the results of the study
