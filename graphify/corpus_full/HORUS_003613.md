# CORROSION RESISTANCE OF (TIALZRTANB)NX HIGH ENTROPY THIN FILMS COATINGS GROWN ON 304 SS

In this study, TiAlZrTaNb nitride thinfilms were deposited on 304 stainless-steel (304 ss) substrates by High-Power Impulse Magnetron Sputtering (HiPIMS) under the substrate bias from -50 V and working pressure from 0.5 Pa. The effect of corrosive electrolyte on anticorrosive properties were studied by electrochemical methods of the films produced. The microstructure, morphology and chemical composition of the coatings were analyzed by X-ray diffraction, Scanning Electron Microscopy and Energy Dispersive X-ray spectroscopy. The sample porosity and corrosion resistance were studied by electrochemical impedance (EIS) tests and potentiodynamic polarization employing a water, acetic acid and citric acid solutions. The preliminary results show that the films are polycrystalline and they have high corrosion resistance. The corrosion mechanism in the films will be discussed in this work
