# THE MANNEG OF PLAGS WITH SEMIOCHYMICS

Pheromones, especially sexual pheromones, are the semiochemicals most commonly used as biorational pesticides, and have become an environmentally friendly option as an alternative to the use of conventional insecticides for pest control. The practical application of these compounds has been a great success in the control and reduction of pest populations without effects on beneficial fauna in crops, thus providing sustainable and ecological pest management in crops of agricultural interest. Colombia is an agricultural country par excellence, has crops of high commercial and economic interest, whose products have great export potential. In our country, the use of techniques that employ biorrational pesticides for pest control is still very low, so the use of conventional pesticides remains predominant. It is possible to consider that research with semiochemicals has a great field of study and can be developed successfully, allowing incentives and implementation of the use of these biorrational pesticides as an alternative for the control of different pests of economic interest
