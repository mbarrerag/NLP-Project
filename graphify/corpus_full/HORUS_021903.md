# Transit of torrential avenues in mountainous terrain and tropical environments for threat assessment.

The torrential avenues are complex natural phenomena. As transitory processes, they are located between floods and mass movements. Due to this dualism, torrential avenues have been considered differently depending on the experience and training of the scientist or professional who study them. DesInvent, a database of disasters, reports that in Colombia there were 1,387 flows of channeled debris, floods of debris and sudden floods between 1921 and 2020. These events caused 3,332 fatalities and more than 1.1 million people affected. This work tries to analyze the transit of torrential avenues through various computational techniques, taking into account at the same time the effect of geomorphology on the understanding of the threat. The aim is to investigate various methodologies and compare them using validation criteria, as well as to investigate the influence of the concentration of sediments on the behavior of the flow and in the tool and parametrization of the simulation. Two case studies in the Colombian Andes are analyzed: the flood of debris occurred in Salgar, Antioquia, in 2015, which caused 112 slips caused by the rare occurrence of two storm, the instant flow and the parametrification of the storm
