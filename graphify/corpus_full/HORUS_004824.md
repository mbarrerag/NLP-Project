# Measurement of voltage and compression stresses under flight conditions, for the determination of the plane elongation profile on the Colombian Air Force T-90 Calima aircraft, using fiber optic sensors

The impact and importance of this project lies in that it allowed to obtain data on deformations in critical areas of planes of the aircraft T-90 Calima during in-flight tests through fiber optic monitoring systems Fiber Bragg Grating FBG, characterizing the mechanical behaviors of the material and calibrating the monitoring system to acquire data of elongation of the composite material of the aircraft. The critical points based on the results of the structural tests carried out by the manufacturer were selected, where two critical areas of deformations were found applying high load factors. Once the material and the optical sensors FBG were demonstrated to be the most active in the planes of the aircraft T-90 Calima.
