# EXPERIENCE IN REGENERATIVE COMPANY COMPANY CREPES & WAFLES, S.A.

Crepes & Waffles S.A. is a Colombian company that opened its doors in the year 1980. Its interest has not only focused on closing the social gaps, by creating employment opportunities for women mothers head of household and programs of access to health and housing for the workforce of the company, but also seeks mechanisms to help small agricultural producers overcome some of the conditions that have prevented them to put their collective experience and knowledge of the local conditions to service an agricultural system with sustainable and equitable bases. To this effect, in the company has created a program called _x0093_Compras Regenerativas_x0094_, a system of relationship with suppliers that make part of productive systems of family agriculture, in which the negotiation is linked to a program of conservation of the biodiversity in specific rural territories, counting on the support, both in formation in agroecology, and in organizational strengthening, of organizations based in the territories. The reason why the construction of a case study or teaching is chosen as the objective is the opportunity of learning and capitalization of knowledge, which is based on the company's situation. (Crepes & Wafles, 2022). The methodology followed for the collection of information, which is later to be the indispensable input for the construction of the case study or teaching, is the Systematization of Experiences. From a documentary research and the collection of data from primary sources, it will be achieved: _x0095_ Understand and improve one's own experience, both of the company and of the communities that have been working as providers, overcoming the descriptive and narrative, to move to critical interpretation. _x0095_ Generate an exchange of learnings from experiences based on a common question: aspects to improve and correct _x0095_ Dialogue with existing theory in aspects such as good living, rural development, among others, and propose contributions to disciplines. _x0095_ Constantly question about the own work to promote spaces for improvement
