# Morphological and molecular characterisation of bacteria associated with Capsicum spp. in the Cauca Valley-Colombia.

The cultivation of Capsicum in the Valle del Cauca in Colombia has a high agricultural and industrial potential affected by different biotic and abiotic factors; the bacteria can cause losses between 10 and 20 % and, under favorable environmental conditions for its development, up to 100 %. To address this problem the objective of this study was to characterize phytopathogenic bacteria and those with biocontrol potential of the previous ones. We collected affected plant material in 17 areas of the department, of which 69 isolates were obtained: 8 isolates were phytopathogenic Gram negative and 4 with positive biocontrol potential Gram. We performed the following characteristics: cultural, morphological, biochemical (API 20NE tests), pathogenic and molecular (region of 16S rADN, GyrB and GyrA), and we evaluated the biocontrol potential in vitro.
