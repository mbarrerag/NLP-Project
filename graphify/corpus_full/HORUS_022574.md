# The capacities of peasant women and their participation in the achievement of Food Sovereignty and SAN Genuine Vélez, Santander.

This research tries to show the historical invisibility that rural women have lived in Colombia, due to the triple role that they play in the care work as are the reproductive, domestic and productive jobs. Added to the multiple discriminations and violation of their rights, such as quality education, the scarce opportunity to work with fair remuneration, domestic violence by the Machista culture, the limitations of access to land ownership and credit, which have contributed to further accentuate the gaps of inequality and inequality in their territories. That is why it should be visible the contributions of women to the peasant economy, to the human right to adequate food, Sovereignty and Genuine Food and Nutrition Security in the country, the above from the capacities that they have developed, and in this way demonstrate their role as peace builders and subject to rights. The objective of this study was to analyze the capacities of the peasant women, which allow them to contribute to the achievement of the Sovereignty and the nutritional safety in the country. This is through their qualitative research through the technique of a life history of the women
