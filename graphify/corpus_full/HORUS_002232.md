# Challenges and perspectives for public policy in Latin America

In this final chapter, we aim to identify and propose what we believe to be five key challenges that could help to develop and consolidate the public policy field in Latin America. This chapter proposes ten key dimensions for understanding the specificities of the policy process in Latin American countries, with a view to identifying promising avenues for advancement. We include some concluding remarks from several authors in the analytical chapters of this Handbook. In light of the legacy left by H. Lasswell, which emphasizes the advancement of human dignity and democracy, we suggest to move forward in a coordinated manner at the regional level, with the aim of constructing a more robust, strategic, and transformative field for Latin America. We also suggest working towards a more relevant and active field of knowledge within wider international academic community, striving to make this professional field more robust, with a greater focus on protecting democracy, increase social justice, the reduction of inequalities and fostering positive impacts on people's lives
