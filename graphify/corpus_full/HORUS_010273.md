# Short-term photometry for the CHARACTERIZATION OF DISCONTINUITIES IN ROCKEN MACIZE

The Colombian topographical characteristics, with the presence of three main mountain chains, impose complex technical challenges for engineering and it is increasingly common the need for excavations in rocky massifs to generate tunnels, cutting slopes, foundation of bridges and extraction of materials for construction. From a technical and scientific point of view, the design of these works requires an adequate characterization of the rocky massifs in which they are located. First, it is necessary to have a geological model for engineering, based on which a geotechnical model is generated, which allows feedback and adjustment of the geological model. In this context, the characterization of rocky massifs, through the analysis of three-dimensional images, offers the possibility of systematic and reproducible collection of information, largely complementing the information collected with conventional techniques. The approach developed in this work, based on analysis of three-dimensional images and in the statistical management of its geological data, is innovative and relatively easy to apply in the practice of engineering in the country to make viable the probabilistic modeling responsible and explicit in the taluctituation model.
