# THE SIGNIFIED FOOD FOR MIDDLE EFFECTS NOT INSTITUCATED FROM BOGOTA, COLOMBIA

The book deals with research results of the Food and Human Nutrition research group of the Faculty of Medicine. The chapter is about a qualitative study on the meaning of food for older people strata 3, 4, 5 and 6 residents in Bogotá
