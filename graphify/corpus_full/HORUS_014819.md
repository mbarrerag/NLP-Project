# Adoption of e-commerce in the fruit and vegetable sector: An analysis in times of pandemic

The pandemic by COVID-19 prompted the adoption of e-commerce as a response to the decline in sales in organizations related to the value chain of the fruit and vegetable sector. However, there are challenges in the efficient use of e-commerce in this industry, so the objective of this research was to identify the level of adoption of e-commerce in organizations linked to the link of distribution of the value chain of the fruit and vegetable sector in Bogotá, Colombia, through the e-MICA methodology, to recognize the challenges of more competitive management in post-pandemia. It was determined that organizations make extensive use of technologies and present a degree of maturity of the medium electronic commerce, which requires improved interaction with the customer to promote their products. It was identified that the use of social networks and the creation of value content improves the marketing process. As limitations, the need to involve variables of greater complexity such as digital traffic and omnichannel presence is recognized, with the aim of explaining more deeply the factors associated with the adoption of e-commerce
