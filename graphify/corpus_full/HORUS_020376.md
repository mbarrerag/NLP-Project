# Housing series: appropriation, adaptations and transformations of a model, from Le Corbusier to Salmon

Since the first decades of the twentieth century, the repetitive, economic and standardized housing in the creation of a “for all” housing was a concern of the architects of the modern movement. The main interest of this research lies in revealing and studying the possible influence that the reflections, research and the development of housing in series carried out by Le Corbusier in the work of Rogelio Salmona, who was his collaborator for a few years. And, through the exploration and spatial recreation through spatial sequences of three early projects of Salmona: Experimental Houses in Pereira 1958, Urbanization San José in Pereira 1960 - 1961 and La Coruña neighborhood in Bogotá 1964-1965, identifying the processes, mechanisms and spatial devices in their models to allow, on the one hand, to continue revealing the impact that Le Corbusier had on its collaborators and, on the other hand, to demonstrate how from the residential models of the Swiss architect both theoretical and practical, they presented appropriations, transformations and/or adaptations as a response to the local context of the projects of its apprentices related to the geography, the climate and the modes of life of the inhabitants, whose results were to respond to the local conditions of the inhabitants. identify the processes, the mechanisms and devices in their models to allow, on the one hand, to continue developing the impact that Le Corbusier had on his collaborators and, on the other, to demonstrate how from the residential models of the Swiss architect, both theoretical and practical, approaches are present, transformations and/or adaptations in response to the local context of the learning projects related to geography, climate and the ways of life of the inhabitants, the outcome of which were projects with a clear notion of responding to the conditions of the landscape
