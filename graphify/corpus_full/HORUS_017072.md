# ASSISTANCE TO THE MULTIACTOR DESIGN OF PUBLIC POLICIES BOTTOM-UP.ANALYSIS COMPARISON OF SOCIAL INNOVATION EXPERIENCES FOR PEACE FROM ACADEMIA

In recent decades, the design of public policies has called for various disciplines for the generation of new ways of building knowledge aimed at meeting needs and opportunities. In Latin America, the multicultural/plurietnic character of societies has generated special interest in their design. The case study allows to locate experiences and make comparisons in complex and detailed readings of the phenomena present in the territories. Based on the comparative analysis of four experiences led by the National University of Colombia, with a focus on social innovation, the contrasts and nuances found in the interaction of local collaborative ecosystems are described. Complex thinking and the approach to social innovation are proposed, to understand the contributions to peace from the mediation of the academy in the interaction with local knowledge, fundamental in the consolidation of community and territorial processes, in order to establish recommendations to the multi-actor design of public policies in the post-agreement
