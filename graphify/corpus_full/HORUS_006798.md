# Development of a control strategy for tracking the paths of an unmanned aerial vehicle

Unmanned air vehicles (UAV, from its acronym Unmanned Aerial Vehicle) offer solutions to complex tasks in many engineering areas due to their maneuverability and portability. The vast majority of solutions are achieved by applying control strategies on the UAV in such a way that it can follow a path demanded by the task in question. The implementation of controllers for tracking trajectories generally requires a precise knowledge of the dynamics of the UAV through a mathematical model, which, despite being extensively worked in the literature, is not common that it contemplates the dynamics of the actuators, leaving the door open to possible failures or aspects to improve when implementing a control strategy in practical way. In addition to the above, the development and practical implementation of the control strategies in its early stages implies a high risk for the safety of both the personnel performing such implementation and the UAV in question, since a minimal error can trigger both the destruction of the UAV and a damage to the physical integrity of the operator.
