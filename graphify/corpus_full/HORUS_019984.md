# PSYCHO-OCIAL FACTORS INFLUENCE THE DECISION-MAKING OF WOMEN WITH CORONARY SYMPOMAS: A REVIEW SCOPING

The present exploratory review sought to analyze the literary production of relevant studies on psychosocial factors that influence the decision-making of women with coronary symptoms, through a planned and systematic strategy of literature search. It was identified that it is necessary to develop research that allows to evaluate the responses of women to coronary symptoms and to deepen the influences of psychosocial and interpersonal factors since the decision-making in health, mainly in adverse experiences in childhood, negative emotionality, partner violence and under social support
