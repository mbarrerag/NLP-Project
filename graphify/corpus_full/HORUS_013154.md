# Evaluation of total cyanide of yucca leaves (Manihot esculenta) and proposal of disposal methods in different varieties of Colombia 2023

The linamarin is a glucozysid cyanogenic which is present in yuca (Manihot sculenta C) whose breakdown by enzymatic hydrolysis reactions releases hydrocyan acid as a defense mechanism of the plant. It is known that the consumption of the tuberous roots of yuca in Colombia is abundant, as well as its production and versatility in terms of its cultivation. However, at nutritional level this food only contributes in contrast to the leaves that have a great contribution in proteins and minerals, but its content in linamarin is considerably greater than that present in the roots. In the framework of this research, an experimental analysis was carried out by means of a clearly descriptive methodology with the aim of investigating the concentration of cyanic acid hydro (HCN) in the yucca leaves of various varieties. The techniques of colorimetric analysis and spectrophotometric analysis (UV-VIS) were used, adapting and optimizing the standardized methodologies for the identification and quantification of HCN where values were observed in a range of 97 to 3936 mg HCN/kg of dry sample (ppm)., the linamarin content in leaves is considered to be a purely descriptive methodology to explore the concentration of hydrogen cyanide (HCN) in cassava leaves of various varieties. Colorimetric and spectrophotometric (UV-VIS) analysis techniques were employed, adapted and optimized standardized methodology for the identification and quantification of HCN. Results reviewed concentrates rating from 97 to 3936 mg HCN/kg of dry bases (ppm). Additionally, this study considered the removal of HCN from the leadings with the aim of promoting and adding added concentrations rating from 97 to 3936 mg HCN/kg of dry bases (ppm)
