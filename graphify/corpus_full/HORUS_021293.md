# Processing of wood and coffee-cleaning waste for the removal of two antibiotics into aqueous solutions

The recovery of waste is one of the strategies that proposes the reprocessing of waste materials, which allows to obtain a material of economic interest and in this way does not become a waste and remains in the economic chain. Therefore, in this project will be studied the thermochemical transformation of residues of the wood industry and the food industry (coffee bottle) for the obtaining of carbonous materials, activated carbon type, with potential use as adsorbents. This in order to reduce the amount of waste that go to bodies of water, municipal landfills or composters and that can potentially form greenhouse gases, like methane, in the processes of decomposition. The materials obtained will be evaluated in the removal of emerging pollutants of concern, known in English as pollutants of emerging concern (CEC). The CEC is proposed to the use of materials that are considered to be low concentrations in surface waters, and there is concern that these compounds can have a negative impact on living beings, especially in the long term. In general, these pollutants are not yet regulated. Among the CECtanes, the substances and products of personal care are considered
