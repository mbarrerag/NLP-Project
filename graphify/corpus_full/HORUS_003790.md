# VOTIMINATION OF A MODEL EXCESSING CEROS APPLICABLE TO TRIP CONTROL IN FRESH CULTIVES BY THE METHODOLOGY OF REPLY SUPERFICIES

In this work, two approaches are combined: the methodology of response surfaces and an overzero model (Poisson, Binomial Negative, Hurdle), with the purpose of determining the one that best fits counting data in experimental designs that have a high proportion of zeros, as is the case with the presence of trips in strawberry crops. The parameters of the proposed second order model are estimated by the Esperanza-Maximization algorithm (EM). Later, hypothesis tests are carried out, performing an equivalent to the variance analysis of the normal theory in order to make inference. In addition, confidence intervals are calculated for the estimated parameters and confidence intervals of prediction. Finally, the predicted response is optimized
