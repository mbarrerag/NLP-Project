# PEDAGOGAGES FOR THE COMPANIES OF THE MEMORY AND ACTION OF THE PACES.

This work presents the results of a pedagogical experience that had as aim to reflect on the educational blogs built in the optional-disciplinary subjects Management of Spaces of Memory and Culture of Peace, of the program of Cultural and Communicative Management, in the period 2022-1. These proposals were developed from the pedagogical model ¿in construction and permanent reflection? of the network of aulas-laboratorio of pedagogical innovation of the national university of colombia, headquarters of la paz. The methodological criterion consisted in planning jointly the programs of the subjects, implementing them and documenting them through the blog and allowing in their execution that the error emerge as soon as possible to evaluate and adjust during the module. Subsequently, a detailed analysis of the bitacoras and their annexes was carried out. Thus, the present document arises as a testimony of the intention and the first results of the institutional presence in the region. The analysis started from the following premise: the pedagogics for the memories and the peaces are still in the process of construction and, therefore, the ways of materializing them are different in the measure of integrating knowledge, wills, interests and experiences and experiences
