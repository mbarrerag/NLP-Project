# NUTRITIONAL STATE OF THE MAJOR ADULT TO A SUPPORT GROUP

To describe the nutritional status of retired older adults who belong to support groups in the city of Ibagué. A quantitative, descriptive, cross-sectional study was implemented during the period between September and November 2020. A simple random sampling was carried out with a sample of 80 older adults, who met the following inclusion criteria: to be retired and to be part of the support group. The measurement was carried out with the Mini Nutritional Assessment scale, which has a sensitivity of 96%, specificity of 98% and the pre-dictive value of 97% for malnutrition. The data were analyzed by means of descriptive statistics. It was found that the majority of retirees are well nutrids and none of them have a state of malnutrition. However, 11.25% of the participants in the study present a risk of malnutrition in the future. It was also shown that most retirees are overweight. The study allows to conclude that 70% of the participating population has nutritional status alterations, mainly in a noticeable increase in weight in relation to the prevalence of diabetes, which results in the chronic arterial hypertension
