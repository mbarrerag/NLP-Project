# Editorial: The Silver Jubilee of the Profile Journal

We are pleased to announce to our readers that in September 2024 we will celebrate the twenty-five years of the magazine. Profile was launched in 2000 as an annual publication. In 2008, the magazine became biannual and, since then, a new issue is published every January and July. The celebration of twenty-five years of publication not only gives us satisfaction for the achievements made; it also makes us feel that all the efforts to maintain the periodical publication of the magazine have been worthwhile
