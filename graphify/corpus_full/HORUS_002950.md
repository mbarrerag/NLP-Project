# SENSORICAL MEASUREMENT SYSTEM FOR IDENTIFYING ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS IN AGRICULTURAL MECHANISATION USED DEposiTIVE DEposiTIVE OF ARATE WITH TIRO EQUINS

In this study carried out on the farm, is Paysandú?, a system of sensarist measurement designed to identify and evaluate the environmental conditions during agricultural mechanization using plowing devices with shooting horses. The main objective is to optimize the performance and welfare of the animals by monitoring in real time various critical environmental variables. Specific sensors were implemented to measure relative humidity, ambient temperature and thermal sensation. The measured relative humidity had a maximum value of 53% and a minimum of 49%, with a percentage variation of approximately 7.55%. The ambient temperature recorded a maximum of 29.40°C and a minimum of 28.10°C, with a percentage variation of approximately 4.63%. The thermal sensation presented a maximum of 30.33°C and a minimum of 28.75°C, with a percentage variation of approximately 5.49%. These sensors were integrated into the plowing equipment, allowing the collection of data in real time while the animals performed their work in the field. The analysis of the collected data showed that variations in the ambient temperature and relative humidity can significantly influence the thermal sensation perceived by the animals.
