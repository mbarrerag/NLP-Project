# Impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on household food security: multicenter study in 11 Colombian cities

Objective A multicenter descriptive observational cross-sectional study was carried out, with the objective of analysing the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on food security, water security and coping strategies for Colombian households. Methods 1 524 households were surveyed, via telephone, of 11 capital cities, measuring food security, water security and implementation of coping strategies for food insecurity. Results 71.6 per cent of households were classified with food insecurity (95% CI 66,5-76.3). Mild food insecurity was 31.6 per cent (26.9-36.7), moderate 26 per cent (22.0-30.3) and severe 14.1 per cent (11.6-17.1). 16.6 per cent (12.7-20.0) of households were classified with water insecurity. 64.3 per cent (59,1-69.2) adopted some coping strategy with food insecurity.
