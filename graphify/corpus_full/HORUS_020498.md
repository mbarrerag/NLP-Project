# GUIDELINES FOR THE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF VIDEO-GAYED SERIES

In recent years, one of the most widespread topics in different application contexts is game-based learning through serious games that motivate users and, therefore, as their use becomes more widespread, it is desirable to better articulate serious games in the educational context, so that they enable learning and skills development defined in the curriculum and are designed with a focus on the user. In this article, a set of fifty and a guidance for the design, construction and evaluation of serious educational video games, including aspects related to usability, learning and play, is proposed as a contribution. The guidelines were used to inspect the video game lightbot, in order to determine the design aspects to be improved in this video game. The guidelines proposed in this work aim to be linked in the development process and methodologies for the construction of video games, with a view to promoting the generation of serious educational video games focused on the user, satisfactory and more relevant to the desired educational context
