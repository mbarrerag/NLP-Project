# INDICATIVE TRAILING - INTRODUCTION

This chapter plays a key role in the book, since given that it is an interdisciplinary work involving musicians, visual artists, philosophers, art historians, mathematicians and political scientists, it needs a fine fabric that makes visible/problematic the possible relationships between the different approaches of the chapters. In this sense, the chapter introduces the central problems that serve as a starting point for volume trials: definibility, categorization and representation and makes visible the connections that exist between the essays. That connection starts from the question about the possibility of capturing, "harving" the world in our different disciplines, and the need for certain characteristics of language that includes the presence of signs, processes, traces, marks, remains, etc
