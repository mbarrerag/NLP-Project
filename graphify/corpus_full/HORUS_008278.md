# Hand-eye coordination of a robotic arm using a stereo camera

This paper presents the results obtained after implementing an artificial vision algorithm developed in Python to estimate the position of an object by means of visual information of a stereo camera. The estimation of the position of the object is used by the controller of a robotic arm, to position it and hold the object; however, the robotic arm does not always accurately reach the expected point, therefore, this process was complemented by an algorithm of correction of the position based on the Gradient Decent optimization algorithm and the hand-eye coordination process that humans make. The position values are sent, via wifi, through the TCP/IP protocol and through sockets, to the robotic arm controller. The experimental results obtained show that, at higher resolution of the camera image, better is the estimation of the position of the object and, in general, with the corrected algorithm implemented, the distance between the final position of the robot and the position of the object does not exceed 10 mm
