# Narratives of public policy of citizen participation in the town of Engativá – Bogotá (2016 – 2023)

This study interprets the narratives in the public policy of citizen participation in the town of Engativá (Bogotá) in the period between 2016 and 2023 in light of the theory that in this field proposes Emery Roe and under the lens of the participatory analysis of public policy developed by Frank Fischer that relates citizens to decision-making in politics. In this sense, the dominant account of the policy is reconstructed, its counter-narrative and finally it does its own with the metanarrative, which allows proposing paths of future approach for citizen participation in the locality and in Bogotá. To this end, the collection of information with a qualitative approach is done in which interviews, group workshops and documentary analysis of writings of different origin constitute the source of information. In such approach, experiences are taken from local development plans, participatory budgets, the local citizen observatory of Engativá, the municipal employeehood and the need for institutional coordination, the district and some other relevant actors in the local citizen participation.
