# Re-enforcement of the memorial of the Central Building, the Plastic Arts Programme, the National Universe School of Columbia, the Medellin Headquarters, a fundamental contribution to the education of the people and the teaching of the arts in the country (1980-2010)

Within the framework of the line of work: History and cultural analysis of methodologies of teaching of the arts. Curriculum programs and uses of didactic resources only.This research is born under the hypothesis of making visible how through the evaluation of this subject it becomes aware of a teaching methodology - learning.At the beginning it was proposed to review, restore and classify an existing archive material of the subject in mension, carried out by the professors of the School and later reconstruct the memory of the pedagogical strategies, contained in that archive, developed during the decades of 1980-2010, that impacted the education in arts in the schools of art of Medellín. We also proposed to document the living memory of some professors and some graduates of the career through interviews and recorded conversations that enriched the archive and the existing memory. Finally we proposed to create an audiovisual document and a written material that documented the history of the program and the memory of the Central Workshop, to evaluate the generations of students and teachers of coming and coming arts, in our country and in the continent. The analysis of these memories helped us to find the pieces of a puzzle and the art of ours and the art of teaching, we could have been able to evaluate the main generations of the art. They contributed to the intellectual maturity of the city in relation to modern, postmodern and contemporary art. This pedagogical project of teaching and learning committed to its community, was built in time and space in company and permanent consultation with its main beneficiaries and context, and was subjected to constant reviews and tests by the academic community for four decades. Finally, it can be realized that the theoretical framework that accompanied the Central Workshop was dialogue with a methodology that is visualized today with what the artist Luis Camnitzer and María Tradón name as the Art Thinking, as a sharing of the basic concepts, the key moments of the process, the empowerment of the student under their individual or collective production, guided, motivated, accompanied and advised by a group of teachers, posing doubts, challenges, questioned realities, analyzing the context, succeeding in building a creation in Arts, particularly our program
