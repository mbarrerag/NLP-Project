# Strategies for using pronouns in/and in the oral communication of future FLE teachers

Generally speaking, French students with a foreign language (FLE), with different mother tongues (L1), present problems in the use of pronouns en/y, which are part of native French. Indeed, it has been observed, for example, that Colombian students of advanced French levels do not use pronouns en/y when communicating in French. Hence, the interest to answer the following research question: what strategies do students and recent graduates of the Bachelor's degree in Philology and Languages use: French of the National University of Colombia, in their oral communication, to carry out the communicative function performed by pronouns en/y in the French native language? This study is carried out in the framework of qualitative research, specifically, within the descriptive-explicative approach and addresses the theory of FLE and the communicative competence. The study population is therefore shaped by students and newly graduated from the aforementioned undergraduate programme that carried out their teaching practice in 2018 and 2019.
