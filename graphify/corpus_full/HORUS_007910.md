# Construction of a new olfactory psychophysical identification test for the population of Bogotá, D.C., of the National University Hospital of Colombia, between 2020 and 2022.

The olfactory disorders affect 25% of the population, their prevalence increases with age. Although common, they tend to be overlooked in the medical consultation. The available psychophysical tests are produced mainly by the United States and Europe, and their application is limited to countries that share their cultural background. to develop a psychophysical test of validated, rapid, cost effective and reliable olfactory identification for the Colombian population between 18 and 80 years. A olfactory test (POC) was designed with 12 odors recognizable by the general population, from a survey with Likert scale. It was applied in a fourth level hospital to people with and without olfactory alteration to obtain normal values and quantifying time of realization. The test was repeated between 30 and 120 days to a proportion of participants to evaluate its reliability, and against the Cross Cultural Smell Identification Test (CC-SIT) to validate it with the international standard.
