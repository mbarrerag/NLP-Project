# Methodology that considers Friedländer's pressure-time diagram as an indicator of an external demand due to an explosive charge, on a conventional reticular structure.

The main objective of this work is to develop a methodology that correlates the pressure-time diagram or known as the Friedländer diagram of an explosive charge as an external request on a reticulated structure. For this project it is considered that the structure is being affected by a chemical explosion, which, in attempting to balance with the surrounding air, creates a shock wave that could be visualized as a bubble that impacts the structure. According to the FEMA (FEMA, 2003) this wave starts at the peak and rapidly decreases thanks to the dissipation of energy and the divergence of geometry. A dynamic analysis of the structure was carried out through the STAAD.pro program, asig-nando the loads by means of the Time-History command.
