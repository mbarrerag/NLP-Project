# Study and analysis of extended violin techniques and their application in two works by Colombian composers of the 21st century

The violin is one of the instruments with the greatest study of “extended techniques”, which have been the product of the experimentation of 20th and 21st century performers and composers. While the performers form themselves within an educational model based on the 19th century European Conservatory, where the “noise” does not seem to have a place, the present composers use complex sonorities as part of their musical discourses. As a result of this difference, many questions arise about the educational model in which we form violinists in Colombia, and the presence of the use of techniques extended in the recent repertoire. In this work, a study of techniques that have been categorized as “extended technologies” and its use is questioned through the listening of two works of Colombian composers active in the 21st century. .
