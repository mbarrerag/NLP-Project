# Ethics, Bioethics and SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY FOR SCIENTIFIC METHOD AND PROGRESS OF SCIENCE

the scientific knowledge generated in the sciences starts from an unfinished cycle of search for creative and methodologically grounded answers, and approach of new questions; this in the framework of formal schemes that serve as scaffolding to the scientific method. a review of the literature published between 2010 and 2022 on ethics and bioethics in research and scientific integrity was made. The databases were: Pubmed, Lilacs, Redalyc and Scielo, Dialnet plus. Then a hermeneutic exercise was carried out that allowed reflections on the ethics of research, scientific integrity and ethos as researchers. the hermeneutic exercise allowed establishing two central categories for reflection: 1) Ethics of research as a cross-sectional process in research; 2) Scientific integrity as a meta-principle that underpins research. it is necessary to ensure rigorous research processes, with scientific integrity, with the gold stand scientific and the gold ethical standing, and in any case for the care and respect for the human and non-human rights that participate in research. It is important to recognize that the common research is not enough to fulfill the positive and to fulfill the objective and to the scientific integrity
