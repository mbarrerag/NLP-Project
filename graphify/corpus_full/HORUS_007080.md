# Contribution of short marketing circuits to social market construction and food autonomy: the case of the eco-shop La Huerta in Fusagasugá

Given the different disadvantages that are presented in the day to day of the producers for commercializing the foods that leave their farms, the Ecotienda la Huerta located in the municipality of Fusagasugá, is an alternative of local marketing of the organization Tierra Libre, which seeks to bring closer to the producers and consumers promoting the importance of fair and responsible consumption.This initiative promoted by Tierra Libre, social organization that promotes the defense of the territory, the local economy and permanence of the peasantry in the province of Sumapaz – Cundinamarca, through the Ecotienda “La Huerta”, aims to break the logic of the conventional agrifood market, and generate a direct relationship between producers and consumers, favoring the commercialization of agroecological foods, within the framework of the fair trade and the solidary economy (Tierra Libre, 2020). Among the objectives drawn for this process that works since 2016, the socio-economic and demographic relationships are identified, the forms of agriculture and the development of this market in the city of Fusagasugá.
