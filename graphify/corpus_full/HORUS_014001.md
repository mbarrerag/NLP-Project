# Evaluation of some physico-chemical properties of orange powder obtained by spray drying.

The project focuses on addressing the loss of physical-chemical and nutritional properties in food, result of drying at high temperatures. This process has been identified as causing the degradation of essential nutrients and bioactive compounds in food, which leads to a decrease in their nutritional and sensory quality. In this context, the research of drying alternatives, specifically the spray drying, which operates at more moderate temperatures and shorter times, is proposed, with the primary aim of preserving more effectively the nutrients and bioactive compounds. This is how the main objective of this project is to analyze and study the physical-chemical properties of orange powder obtained through a spray drying team. With the development of the project it is expected to identify the optimal operating conditions during the drying process by spraying that lead to the greater preservation of the properties of the orange powder, and will promote the innovation. In addition, it will seek to improve the laboratory practical skills and the written communication skills of the students participating in the project. This project is aligned with the following Sustainable Development Goals: Guarantee of consumption and sustainable production and will promote the industrial construction. In short, the project focuses on investigating the spray drying method in order to preserve the properties of agricultural foods, generating benefits in terms of food security and skills development for the students involved
