# VOLUME 1.CHAPTER 3: BEHAVIOUR OF WATER BUFALI.

The water buffalo is a globally distributed species that adapts to various climatic conditions. Although bubalin species are believed to have a high tolerance to tropical and cold environmental conditions, it is known that overexposure to high temperatures in combination with high relative humidity and direct solar radiation cause heat stress, while abrupt temperature decline, rapid winds and exposure to extreme weather changes can lead to cold stress.1 2. When animals face an environmental challenge they respond with a variety of related mechanisms that include physiological, biochemical, immunological, anatomical and behavioral changes. If this response is prolonged over time it can impact on the growth, reproduction, health status and overall decrease the productivity of this species.1 4. Therefore, the objective of this chapter is to report on the different thermoregulation mechanisms triggered by heat and cold thermal stress on buffalo productivity.1
