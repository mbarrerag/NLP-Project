# Inventory of emissions from mobile sources under Bottom - Up methodology for Bogotá, Colombia and analysis of supply scenarios for their reduction.

The air pollution in Bogotá is highly related to vehicles that are responsible for the highest percentage of pollutant emissions. Emission inventories are a tool for the identification of potential sources of pollution and the formulation of air quality management programs. This research provides for the updating for Bogotá D.C. of the inventory of emissions by mobile en route sources for CO, NOX, PM2.5, SO2, COV and CO2. A Bottom-Up approach is used based on the activity factor obtained from the city's transport planning model for peak hour at 18,600 road mesh links. The use of 1.770 emissions factors of COPERT V adapted to the typical traffic operation conditions and the technological distribution of the fleet. It is reported that light vehicles contribute with more than 70% of CO and SO2 emissions, and more than 50% of the emissions of COV and CO2 emissions. The SITP buses and intermunicipal transports contribute with 26% and 28% respectively, and also produce the highest PM2 emissions.
