# POTENTIAL BIOLOGICAL MECHANISMS AND EFFECTS OF VITAMIN D IN CIVID 19: AN NARRATIVE REVIEW

Recent studies have reported beneficial effects of vitamin D both in prevention and in the treatment of disease by COVID-19. This has been related mainly to its mechanisms of action within the renin angiotensin system (RA), the immune response and regulations of pro-inflammatory cytokines, which are important axes in the pathophysiology of COVID-19. The objective of this review is to elucidate the biological effects of vitamin D in relation to COVID-19 and to describe the biochemical mechanisms that explain the clinical outcomes in favor of vitamin D supplementation
