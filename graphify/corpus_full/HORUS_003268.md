# INTEGRATED SYSTEM FOR ANALYSIS AND INSPECTION OF SUBSTANCES AND GAS WITH NARICS AND ELECTRONIC LANGUAGES

Electronic languages and noses are specialized systems that are able to detect compounds in liquids and gases respectively thanks to the use of a sensor network, an acquisition system and a data analysis system that includes the use of artificial intelligence strategies, making them ideal as monitoring and quality control systems. Commercially, these systems are individually located and are still in development phase as it is possible to continue improving the results obtained with different pre-processing and data processing strategies. There are many processes that require the combined use of various senses to generate a more precise response, so the use of electronic languages and noses in a combined way can improve in certain processes the quality of the results obtained through data fusion strategies. As there are still few developments, this work presents a progress in the design and development of a prototype integrated system for the analysis and inspection of chemical compounds in liquids and gases using an electronic tongue and nose
