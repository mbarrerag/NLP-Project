# ASSESSMENT OF THE EFFECT OF AN ELECTRIC CAMP ESTIMULATION ON THE HIALINE CARTILAGE

The electrical fields have been proposed as a non-invasive alternative therapy to improve the repair of connective tissues such as hyalino cartilage, which is characterized by having a low capacity for self-regeneration when it is affected by degenerative lesions or pathologies. Since hyalino cartilage is an avascular tissue with low proliferative capacity, this work aims to evaluate experimentally the effect of the application of electrical fields on the cellular dynamics of the tissue in three different in vitro scenarios. First, the proliferation, cell death and molecular synthesis of monolayered chondrocytes were analyzed and were electrically stimulated. Second, a histomorphometric analysis was performed to cartilage explants that were stimulated with electrical fields. Finally, the effect of the electrical fields on the condrogenic differentiation of mesenchymal stem cells cultivated in injectable hydrogels was evaluated.
