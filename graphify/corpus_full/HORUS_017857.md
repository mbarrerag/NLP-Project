# TERRITORIAL ACCESSIBILITY OF TURISTIC ACCOMMODATION SERVICES IN THE REGION OF THE CAFETERO, COLOMBIA CULTUREL LANGUAGE

This research proposes the calculation of the territorial accessibility to the accommodation services of the Coffee Cultural Landscape (PCC), the coffee region of Colombia, based on the premise that the tourism potential of a region is proportional to the availability of services, equipment and accessibility conditions. Accessibility models supported by geostatistical calculations are applied that allow to identify population coverage. The results show that the departments of Caldas and Risaralda have the best conditions of accessibility to hotel equipment in the coffee area. However, the economic policies of these departments are focused on strengthening trade and industry, reducing importance to the need to strengthen the tourism sector, in which one of the indispensable characteristics is related to the quantity and quality of these. It is concluded that the main challenge to be faced in the CPC is to improve the operational conditions of transport infrastructures, especially in the Quindío department
