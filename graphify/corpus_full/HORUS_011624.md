# Design of scatters for bond tissue regeneration through diagnostic imaging and generative design

The engineering of bone tissues is responsible, among others, for understanding the structures of bones to try to promote their formation with scaffolds. The processes of making scaffolds with variable density are difficult for traditional manufacturing methods where holes located in structures are made with the aim of achieving imitation of bone architecture. In recent years, the Tissue Engineering has been benefited from the advances that have been made in the development of additive manufacturing, which allows the creation of complex geometries such as those of the scaffolds. To enter this method, the use of design based on diagnostic images is proposed. In this thesis a scaffold with variable internal density is developed, which can be carried out in its manufacture by additive manufacturing controlling external and internal geometry of the structure, porosity and pore size from diagnostic images
