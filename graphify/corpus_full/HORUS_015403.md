# EFFICIENCY OF WORK IN EQUIPMENT FOLLOWS THE LEADERSHIP TYPE BY A SERIES GAME

Teamwork, the quality of the people and the relationships that establish each other within the organizations are critical factors for the success and permanence of the time of the organizations (Vignolo, 2006). Teamwork is guided by a leader, is the person who takes the initiative to influence the other members achieving a motivated work and the scope of the proposed objectives. In the organizations there are different types of leaders and the importance arises to know and enhance the leadership styles of each member, with the aim of efficiently reaching the team work and allowing the ultimate goals to be reached with greater ease. The types of leadership presented with which efficiency is evaluated are: autocratic (authoritarian), democratic (participative) leadership and leadership of Laissez-Faire (abdicatoric). The serious game designed seeks to teach the participants different types of leadership presented in the team work and which of these games are most effective in solving problems to the internal organizations, allowing the leadership to help make decisions and the success of the organizations. Teamwork becomes efficient when different teams work under one objective, guided by some types of leadership that have a role in the organization's internal affairs, allowing leadership to help them to make decisions and to help them to make decisions and succeed
