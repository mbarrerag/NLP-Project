# ASSESSMENT OF DIFFERENT CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDELINES FOR DISLIpidITY MANNEE IN PRIMARY PREVENTION PATIENTS IN EXTERNAL CONSULTATION

cardiovascular disease is the main cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide.There are several evaluation scales to estimate cardiovascular risk and with this, make therapeutic decisions aimed at primary prevention.The objective of the study was to evaluate the agreement between the AHA/ACC, ESC/EAS, USPSTF and GPC guidelines in determining the cardiovascular risk and the decision to prescribe statins as primary prevention. observational analytical study of agreement. Patients between 40 and 75 years old without history of cardiovascular disease were included, who attended external consultation of internal medicine of the Hospital San José in the period January 2019 to January 2021.Eligible patients were applied to the Framingham, adjusted Framingham and SCORE scales to determine the correlation in the assessment of cardiovascular risk; and the AHA/ACC guides, ESC/EAS, USPSTF and GPC guidelines to evaluate the agreement in the decision to start statins as primary prevention. 288 patients, average age of 60.5 years, female gender found
