# Determination of potentially zoonotic agents present in domestic pigeon (Columba lvia) that inhabit places crowded by humans.

The common pigeon (Columba livia) is an invasive species, highly successful colonizing urban environments. It is common to observe it in parks, restaurants, amusement centers, even hospitals. Although measures have been proposed for its control, the reproductive success is too high for them to have strong results. On the other hand, this species of bird, is a proven reservoir of a variety of potentially zoonotic microorganisms. This study aims to analyze two university campuses Bogotá and Orinoquia, as well as some localities in the capital district, the city of Arauca and aguazul. Evaluating parasites present in blood, feces and also viruses with zoonotic potential such as avian influenza and west Nile. We hope that in the process of collecting samples and presenting results we will have citizen participation, and that even with them they propose strong control or prevention measures. It is well known that in many places it is the same citizens who oppose the control of pigeons. Participatory research must come to action. Its aim is to change attitudes and behavior of the community, aimed at preventing, controlling and/or eradicating the causes of the community
