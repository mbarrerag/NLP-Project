# Development of instrumental experimentation using cellular phone sensors, for Physical I subject

In this work, cooperative work guides based on instrumental experimentation were developed and implemented, using the smartphone sensors and the Phyphox application as a data collection tool. The focus was on improving the teaching-learning process of some physics concepts, such as gravity, the constant elasticity of a spring, the dependence of mass in the spring period and the dependence of length in the pendulum period; aimed at students of the physics I subject at the San Tarsicio College in Bogotá. The implemented methodology actively involved students in experimentation, seeking to optimize the results obtained. The guides designed were based on experimentation with materials available in the environment, taking advantage of the capabilities of smart phones to explore other functionalities that would facilitate the realization of experiments. Finally, a qualitative analysis of the perception of students versus experimentation and concepts addressed in the course .
