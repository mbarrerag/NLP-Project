# Return and multimodal learning to aid diagnosis in ophthalmology and histopathology

The main contribution of this thesis is the development of probabilistic machine learning models to support the diagnosis of diseases from medical information. We show how a probabilistic approach offers great versatility when taking advantage of all the information available on the objective task. Based on the mathematical formalism of quantum mechanics, we develop and apply learning models that allow us to manage the flow of information using density matrices in different ways. We develop mechanisms that can encode in a natural way not only categorical information, but also ordinal, and that can also merge different modes of information. In addition, we demonstrate that the models proposed are naturally interpretable, which allows and facilitates its application in sensitive domains such as medical applications. Precisely, in this work we test our models in specific tasks of diagnosis of eye diseases and prostate cancer.
