# THE TECHNOLOGICAL REVOLUTION IN THE QUANTIFICATION OF THE NATURAL CAPITAL FOR THE PROGRESS OF COLOMBIA

Satellite images and artificial intelligence (AI) are transforming the quantification of natural capital in Colombia and at the global level. Quantification of natural capital involves measuring the economic value of ecosystem resources and services, essential for sustainable development. This process is facilitated by technologies such as the Environmental and Economic Accounting System (SEEA) and advanced methods, but faces technical challenges due to the complexity and scale of ecosystems. Satellite images provide a comprehensive and long-term vision of environmental changes, even in remote areas, allowing monitoring key indicators such as air quality, water and land coverage. In addition, AI processes large volumes of data, identifies patterns and models future scenarios, improving decision-making in conservation, resource management and mitigation of climate change. In the Colombian context, characterized by its exceptional biodiversity, the integration of these technologies allows the assessment of problems such as deforestation in the Amazon, the state of mangroves and the impact of mining. These tools not only facilitate the understanding of ecosystems, but also highlight the economic potential of the country's natural heritage, placing it as a leader in global sustainability
