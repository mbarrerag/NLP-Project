# NUmeric SOLUTION OF BURGERS ECUIATION BY A FREE MALA METHOD

The Burgers equation is a nonlinear hyperbolic equation that its solution develops shock waves or discontinuities that occur in fluid dynamics and are described by equations such as Euler and Navier-Stokes. From a numerical point of view, such discontinuities in the solution and their evolution over time are not easy to reproduce by mesh-based techniques as they need to use riveted what is computationally expensive. To avoid these inconveniences it is proposed to consider the fluid under study as a particle system to track fluid movement, this implies using a methodology of non-mesh discretization of the differential equation. In this shawl the important concepts involving the method without mesh are presented as: the generation of the point system, search for neighbouring points, approximation of spatial derivatives by means of the method of minimal mobile squares and the solution of systems of ordinary differential equations resulting. As a method of application the Burgers viscose and non-viscose equation is solved. The numerical solutions are compared with the analytical solution and a convergence analysis of the method via numerical experimentation is performed
