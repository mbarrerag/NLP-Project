# THE FICTIONS OF NON-FICTION: ISCED, IMAGES AND SIGNATURES

The faces of Morpheus are a reflection on central concepts of non-fiction cinema, such as "image", "reality", "fiction", "objectivity", "subjectivity", among others. The book therefore proposes a semiotic concept to understand the genre while, from a philosophical perspective, it establishes connections between fictional cinema and non-fiction, characterizing the iconic traits and the effects on the interpretation of the reality on which the image works
