# PHYSICAL CHEMICAL AND NUTRICIONAL PROPERTIES OF INRIQUECIDE YOGURES WITH COLOMBIAN HIBRID PALMA OIL (OLEIFERA ELAEIS X ELAEIS GUINEENSIS) AS A SUBSTITUTE TO LACTURE FAST

The CVD represents a public health problem, according to the World Federation of Hearts, in 2015, CVD caused 17.5 million deaths in the year, in turn, the WHO reports that these diseases constitute 31% of all deaths recorded in the world (World Health Organization, 2017). It is worth noting that in recent years, the pandemic situation by COVID-19, showed that the population with CVD has the highest risk of mortality. The importance of food as a modifiable risk factor in the development of CVD has been documented, therefore, to mitigate it, the development of functional foods, can be part of the actions to favor cardiovascular health. Hence, the Colombian hybrid palm oil (AHP) by being a source of AGMI and vitamin E, is attractive for the design of foods with functional potential.
