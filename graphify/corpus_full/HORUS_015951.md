# PRODUCTION OF IGYS ANTI-DURPES AS A TOOL FOR IDENTIFICATION OF ANTAGONITS PEPTITES OF N-METHYL-D-ASPARTATE IONOTROPIC RECEPTOR (NMDA) CONTAINING GLUN2B SUBUNITY

Antibodies were produced against three NMDA-GluN2B receptor antagonist peptides in a chicken model. The immunoglobulins were removed and precipitation was performed from the egg yolk and purified by thiophilic chromatography. Their specificity and activity were evaluated by ELISA and dotblot assay
