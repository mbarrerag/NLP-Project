# IMPLEMENTATION OF A NEW CHARACTERISTICS SYSTEM FOR CONTACT TERMS BY REFUNDS AT THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF METROLOGY

The process of implementing a new contact thermometer calibration system based on the application of the proposed method in the International Temperature Scale 1990 (EIT - 90) in the sub-range between the triple water point (H2O, 0.01 °C) and the Indian solidification point (In, ~156,59 °C), and the sub-range between the triple water point and the tin solidification point (Sn, ~231,928 °C).From the design, manufacture and evaluation of an Indian enclosed cell at the National Institute of Metrology of Colombia (INM), a thermometric pattern was obtained to include the SPRT-type thermometer calibration service (Standard Platinum Resistance Thermometer) and Pt100 by the EIT method of use did 90 (commonly known as the Fixed Point method) within the offer of the Temperature and Humidity Laboratory of the Physical Metrology Subdirection. This type of services had not previously been offered by the HIT5 HIT system completed by the HIT network of laboratories
