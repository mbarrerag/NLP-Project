# On female waters: Experiences and reflections of being an indigenous Kankuama woman, Sexual and Reproductive Health, in the Kankuama Indigenous Reserve of the Sierra Nevada of Santa Marta

to describe and analyze the understandings, experiences and practices of care-care, in the cycles of menararchy and menstruation, sexuality, planning, abortion and conception, of sexual and reproductive health (SSR) of the indigenous Kankuamas who live in the Kankuamo Indigenous Reguard (RIK) of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta. immersion from a participating observation (to Tim Ingold), since 2015, to the different population and territorial realities that live in the Kankuamas indigenous and Kankuamo people in the Resguardo. The information was collected through the register in the field diary of the daily and informal conversations with the indigenous Kankuamas, besides implementing individual and collective interviews, meetings of Word Circles and workshops in SSR. the understanding of some care relations between the indigenous Kankuamas with the territory, the networks of affections, the community economy and the health models have made it possible to approach the way in which they experience and care-care their SSR. The Kankuamas use it mainly to purchase drugs or other hygiene products, pregnancy prevention and prevention of sexually transmitted diseases, and to have institutional medical assistance in cytology, family planning and preconception tests. although there are power exercises available to the different health models at the disposal of the Kankuamas indigenous women, their great capacities for self-management of their SSR are recognized. This is thanks to the understandings they have on their body, health, sexuality and reproduction, their affinities/distances with belief systems (identity), the availability of information and access or not to the different health models. This allows us to decide which one(s), when and why they will turn to certain models(s), and whether they articulate them or not, for the support of their health and/or the prevention or cure of
