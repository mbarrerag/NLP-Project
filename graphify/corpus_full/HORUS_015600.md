# INVOLVEMENT OF THE KNOWLEDGE AND PRACTICE OF PUBLIC HEALTH IN THE OCCUPATION THERAPY CURRICTIONS.

Introduction/justification: Public health as a field of knowledge and practice interested in the analysis and approach of the health of the populations and specific collectives has become an area of professional exercise of occupational therapists in recent years, offering possibilities for the scholars of the human occupation and the occupational performance to lead activities, programs and political initiatives in which the axis is the promotion of health and the prevention of the disease. In this perspective, it becomes a concern of the academy to explore the training that the occupational therapists have to respond to such challenges, emerging the concern of the extent to which these experts have received and handled essential notions typical of the health-disease world. In this perspective, the social determinants of health, the debates on health equity, Primary Health Care, basic principles of epidemiology, health promotion, and other topics that, in general, are of the public domain of other health professionals. There, the central question of this presentation and is to what extent the knowledge and practice of public health has permeated the curricula of the Oportional Therapy in the country, and what the topics of the Oportional Therapy have been incorporated into the public. There is a need to organize spaces in which we discuss how this knowledge would be integrated into the training of occupational therapists and to be aware of which public health is what we would like to claim in these dynamics
