# Unequality in the attention of the mother's cell in Bogotà: a cohorte study from 2017 to 2019

Breast cancer, Inequity, Quality Introduction: Social inequalities influence the health care of women with breast cancer, for whom it is the main cause of death in the world. The distribution by stages of breast cancer is considered to be the most disadvantaged groups of the population, probably related to the imbalance in the distribution of socioe conomic resources. Therefore the objective of the study was to determine the relationship of the social conditions in educational level, type of insurance, socioeconomic strata and occupation in equity in detection, diagnosis and initiation of treatment of breast cancer in a cohort of women in Bogotá. Analytical observational study of cohort, retrospective from the period 2017 to 2019. The index of the inequality in health (ICDS) shows the percentage of the distributive inequality in the opportunity in the screening (28.1%), diagnosis (25.3%) and the beginning of treatment of breast cancer (20.1%) according to the type of insurance in favor of the contributive regime
