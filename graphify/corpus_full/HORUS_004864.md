# PRACTICES OF THE AGRESTE.

This text emerges from a practice of movement and contact, Translating to writing the investigation of certain gestures, movements and modes of relationship that copy some feline relational gestures, the wild translation of the experience of movement shares the questions and encounters with less anthropocentric and civilized ways that seek to escape a certain modern notion of body that isolates it from its surroundings by separating it from that which can affect it
