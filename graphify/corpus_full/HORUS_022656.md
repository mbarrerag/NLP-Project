# Algorithmic negotiation of actions through deep reinforcement learning.

This grade work aims to explore the use of synthetic financial time series generated by a model of Adversary General Neuronal Networks (GAN) to train a Deep Learning Q algorithm. For strengthening to execute buying and selling actions for a standard & Poor’s 500 index securities market title. For the development of the work, the methodology CRISP DM proposed by IBM was used, understanding first the business and the theory necessary to develop the models, to continue exploring and knowing the available data that correspond to the objectives of the study. This developed a procedure for selecting fictitious series and for training an algorithm by reinforcement with these data. Kolmogorov - Smirnov metric is used as an essential component for training the GN networks. The results of the experiments are explained, and the difficulty in calibrating generative models of adversaries and agents trained by reinforcement is evidenced. Finally, the conclusions derived from the work and possible future research are presented
