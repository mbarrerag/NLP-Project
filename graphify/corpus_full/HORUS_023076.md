# ANALYSIS OF THE USE OF INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGIES IN RURAL EXTENSION IN THE PAMPLONA, COLOMBIA MUNICIPIO PHASE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

The objective of this research was to analyze the use of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) in the rural extension process in the strawberry production system in the municipality of Pamplona (Colombia). To this end, a sample of 85 producers and 18 extensionists of the municipality was defined. The characterization of both groups in relation to the use of ICTs through sociodemographic variables, identification, experience and limitations in the use of these technologies during the various activities of rural extension. The data were analyzed with descriptive and differential statistics. In addition, the significance tests were used, such as the Chi-square test, V de Cramer and Pearson classification, to examine the relationships between sociodemographic variables and the perspectives of the participants on the use of ICT in the agricultural decision making. The characterization allowed to identify that at the level of producers 83.3% are men and for extensionists the predominant age range of 41 to 60 years for producers and 26 to 40 years for extensionists.
