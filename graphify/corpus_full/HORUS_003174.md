# Strategy for psychological intervention in people who have survived eye injuries in social protest

The present work includes the design, application and analysis of a Strategy of Psychological Intervention with a narrative approach in people who survived Eye Injuries in Social Protest. The research process adopted a narrative hermeneutic research design to guide the analysis of the results, and it was based on the validation of a Guide of narrative interview to account for the event, and also counted on the application of the WHO Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SRQ). The registration of the sessions was carried out following the methodology of systematization of the experience of Oscar Jara and the identification of communicative acts of Teun A. Van Dijk. The analysis of the narrative hermeneutic followed the moments proposed by Marieta Quintero-Mejía, starting with Paul Ricoeur, with the structuring of the plot.
