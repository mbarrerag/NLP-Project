# SELECTION AND OPTIMINATION OF AN NADES IN THE EXTRACTION OF TOTAL FLAVONOLES IN UCHUVA CALIZ (PHYSALIS PERUVIANA L)

Seven natural euthectic solvents (NADES) related to 1:1 and ethanol (70%) were used in ultrasound-assisted extraction of total flavonols from the uchuva calyx. The best result in extraction of the total flavonol content (CTFl) was obtained with choline-glycerol chloride (8.72 mg ER/ g MS). With this solvent, the optimization of three extraction parameters was performed: time, % water in the NADES and M/S ratio (g Sample/ ml Solvent). The optimal conditions in the extraction process were 43.33 min, 41.51 % water and 56.36 g solvent sample/ml
