# CENTENARY MEMORATION, POLITICAL MANAGEMENT AND URBAN PROJECTS IN PEREIRA: 1947-1963

The article reconstructs the political dynamics that preceded and defined the organization of Pereira’s first centenary in urban planning. In the context of the modernization that the country experienced between the forties and sixties, the interests and proposals of the city formulated by the main public and private promoters of the commemoration are identified. Although it coexisted with the first steps of urban planning, the different projects were oriented by tension and collaboration between successive local governments and civic entities. The research is based on documentation from the Mayor’s Office, the Municipal Council, national and regional press
