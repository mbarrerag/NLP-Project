# DEVELOPMENT OF A REACTOR FOR THE AUTOMATED PRODUCTION OF SILVER FIELDS OF ZINC OXIDE BY PLASMA REACTIVE EVAPORATE TO BE USEED IN THE MANUFACTURING OF GREAT AREA SOLAR CELDS

This paper presents the development of an automated reactor for the production of thin zinc oxide films ZnO by the plasma-assisted reactive evaporation method ERAP, with properties to be used in different types of solar cells, including: inverted organic cells, Cu(In,Ga)Se2-based cells (CIGS), compounds-based cells with Kesterite structure and hybrid solar cells based on perovskit-structured compounds. The ERAP method has greater advantages than the conventionally used RF sputtering method, mainly in terms of production costs. The reactor includes a Knudsen cell-type Zn evaporation source implemented with an advanced design to improve homogeneity in large area sample thickness and additionally an electronic system that allows for accurate control of both the main deposition parameters and the process of growth of ZnO films automatically. It is expected that the contribution of this work will contribute to the development of a reactor with facilities to produce thin ZnO large area films that could be used in the industrial production of thin film-based solar panels
