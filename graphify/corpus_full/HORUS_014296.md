# Obtaining glycerol carbonate from glycerol and CO2 using La and La/Zr oxides.

In this research work, a review of the state of art on the direct carboxylation reaction between glycerol and CO2 was initially carried out for the obtaining of glycerol carbonate, covering aspects such as environmental problems, the nature of the catalysts used, kinetic and thermodynamic limitations. Subsequently, La and La/Zr oxides were synthesized by the conventional co-precipitation method and characterized by evaluating their physicochemical properties to be used in this reaction. Additionally, a study was carried out on the parameters that affect the reaction such as the effect of water, temperature, pressure, time, desiccant agent and catalyst mass. Finally, the results obtained illustrate that La/Zr catalyst is promisorium for the production of this compound of high industrial interest due to the correlation between its capacity to capture and release CO2 together with its catalytic activity
