# “Children, let’s read!” “What about us?” An inclusive reading plan proposal

This thesis proposes a reading plan for children’s literature for the third grade of primary basic, based on the Semilla Collection and based on concepts of literary theories of feminism and decolonialism. The object of study is the Semilla Collection, a selection of books of children’s and youth literature proposed by the Ministry of National Education (MEN). Hence, the six books that make up the final reading plan, from which a succinct analysis is presented. Among the findings, it is found that there is an effort by the MEN to expand the range of readings and be inclusive in terms of class and race, although it lacks books that address not only gender issues, but also women’s characters that question and subvert the place that has been attributed to them, men, children and girls in society. .
