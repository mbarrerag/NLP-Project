# Reduction of intrünseco noise of a device for the production of images by retrodispersion of gamma rays

In order to reduce the contrast and improve the capabilities of a gamma-ray retrodispersion imaging device, some changes are proposed aimed at reducing the intr > intr enseco noise of the device. The results obtained with the simulation suggest that the use of the proposed shielding system, together with the use of a 68 Ge radiation source, is able to significantly reduce the intr enseco noise of the device
