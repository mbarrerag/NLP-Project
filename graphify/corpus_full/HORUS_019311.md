# STRATEGIES FOR THE COLLECTION OF QUALITATIVE INFORMATION IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE PLAN OF EMERGENCY AND DISASTER IN THE MUNICIPLE OF TAUSA.

Emergency management is a topic of interest to the community in general and to governmental organizations, due to the risks existing in some geographical areas where, the population is exposed to natural disasters and catastrophic events. However, the local emergency vision depends on the real and potential risks perceived by the community and social leaders, which seek solutions and strategies to generate actions of prevention and mitigation against possible events of risk 1. In this sense, giving voice and participation to the community recovers special importance and the li-terature describes various strategies that facilitate the collection of qualitative information that involve communities such as video walks, community participation workshops and social mapping 2,3. This research project aims to demonstrate the application of strategies for the collection of qualitative information in the rural population of the municipality of Tausa Cundinamarca in the framework of a participatory action research. Research-Action-Participative that involves the simultaneity of the co-nocer process and to intervene, and involves the participation of the same people involved in the study program and action map. This methodology includes four stages, diagnosis and the preparation of the cam-po. They allow the graphic documenting of the natural and anthropic risks present in the municipality, allowing the analysis to be expanded in a more complete way. In addition, they have proved to be innovative for researchers and allow the approach in an assertive way with the community. Limitations: To achieve contacts with the inhabitants of the sidewalks, distance that has to be traveled between one sidewalk and another, difficulty to gather the inhabitants of the sidewalks in order to be able to develop the workshops
