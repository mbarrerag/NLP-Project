# Health and work conditions of physiotherapists in municipalities 4, 5 and 6 in Colombia

This study investigates the labor precarization of the physiotherapists in Colombia, focusing on municipalities of categories 4, 5 and 6. Law 50 of 1990, by relaxing the labour market, has increased instability in the health sector. Despite previous research, the specific conditions in these municipalities are not fully understood. The research focuses on the factors that determine the health and work conditions of the physiotherapists in these areas. It is justified by the need to address the particular challenges of these professionals and fill a gap of knowledge. The objectives include characterizing the municipalities, analyzing the profile of the physiotherapists and understanding their job challenges to propose improvements in the management of the human talent. The analysis, transversal, exploratory and descriptive, is based on an electronic survey that covers 13 domains: demographic information, academic training, job situation, working conditions, working hours, identity and autonomy, social support, mental load in the work, impact of the work, actions of promotion and protection of safety and health in the work, and experiences of harassment and violence.
