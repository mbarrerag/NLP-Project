# Durability and mechanical behavior parameters of the Compressed Land Block (BTC) in Colombia for the execution of reinforced masonry housing

This research addresses the process of normalization for the use of Compressed Land Block-BTC, in systems of structural masonry partially reinforced in low complexity buildings in the framework of the process of approval of exception regimes of the Permanent Advisory Commission for the Regime of Constructions Sismo resistant, The hypothesis seeks to demonstrate that soils in different conditions can have homogeneous results, if they are chemically stabilized and physically according to their characterization. To this end, three soils were selected: a high plasticity limus and two high plasticity clays, which were physically stabilized with river sands and peña and chemically adding 10% of cement to the dry mixture, then subjected to wetting and drying cycles, accelerated erosion test (SAET), immersion, apparent density, compressive resistance and resistance to flexure. The results were analyzed according to the international standards NZS 4298:20, used by the standard of the XP 13-901:2022 and UNE 41410: 2023, confirming the hypothesis when finding that the blocks of the 3 samples were considered as being at least in terms of durability according to the normative parameters, thus reached by the standard. -plasticity clays, which were physically stabilized with river and quarry sands, and chemically stabilized by adding 10% cement to the dry mix. Subsequently, they were subject to cycles of wetting and drying, accessed erosion testing (SAET), immersion, bulk density, comprehensive strength, and flexural strength tests. The results were analyzed according to international standards NZS 4298:20, AFNOR XP 13-901:2022, and UNE 41410:2023, confirming the hypothesis by providing the blocks from the three samples were released suitable in terms of durability based on on regulatory parameters.
