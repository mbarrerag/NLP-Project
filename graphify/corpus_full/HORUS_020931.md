# DYNAMIC OF SOME VIRUS RESPIRATORIES IN TWO PORTINARY GRANKS TECHNIFIED IN THE HIGH COLOMBIAN TROPIC

The porcine respiratory complex (CRP) is a multifactorial entity whose manifestation depends, among others, on environmental factors, biosafety levels and infectious agents present on farms and their interactions; within the latter, the viruses constitute a factor of the first order in the severity of their manifestation. Due to the important economic impact of the CRP for the pig industry, a study is proposed to establish which are the respiratory viruses present in two pig farms in Colombia, located at an altitude between 1500 and 2000 msnm. It will be taken as a differential factor between the two farms that have a circulation of the virus of the porcine respiratory and reproductive syndrome (PRRSV) and the other not. A study of the observational, stratified, prospective and comparative type of gas shall be carried out. The two farms handle a continuous flow production and samples shall be collected in four groups different from the productive cycle, thus: (i) pigs in pre-destetet-soil (PD), (ii) pre-ceba (PC: 7 to 9 weeks of age), (iii) ceba (CC: 14 to 15 weeks of age, bi- 15 weeks of age) and (iv, and (iv) (iv) will RT-PCR) (depending on the type of virus). After its detection, depending on the viruses found, these will be genotypified by Sanger sequencing in the Sequency and Molecular Analysis Service (SSiGMoL) of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá. From each farm will be collected climate information related to temperature, rainfall, humidity and photoperiod. Likewise, productive information will be collected as weight gain, food consumption, food conversion, percentage of mortality per stage. Differences will be established between the different groups evaluated both within the farm and between farms by descriptive statistics. Likewise, the behavior of each virus and co-infection present over time will be analyzed (evolution over time). The analyses of association between the climatic variables and presence of viruses as well as the productive variables will be analyzed by multiple correlation.
