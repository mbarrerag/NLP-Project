# Evaluation of the gold extraction process, open-pit gold, using life cycle analysis methodologies, the first law of thermodynamics and exergy. (Case study).

The 20th century was characterized by the economic growth of industrialized countries. It was maintained by the extraction and massive use of mineral resources present in the subsoil; situation that has not changed at present because of the latent desire for a higher standard of living and technological progress where the physical limitations of the planet could seriously restrict the world's economies, in fact, many mineral products like oil or copper already show signs of scarcity, consequently their prices are increasing considerably [1]. The Colombian mining sector characterized by the production of coal, gold, emerald, ferroniquel and construction materials; it is considered as an economic agent that drives development in the region, which requires strengthening of its policies and environmental responsibility. Therefore, this research aims to show the importance of implementing methodologies based on the logic of nature that allow to calculate the environmental impact of extractive activities, as it is the Auriferous mining to Open Sky, in an objective way; in order to measure the physical cost of a Natural Resource from a degraded state in the so-called Reference Environment, using the Exergy as a universal unit of measure
