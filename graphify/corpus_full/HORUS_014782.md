# SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION OF CELL METALS OF ALUMINIUM-SILICE OF OPEN POOR

AbstractDoes this work show the experimental results obtained from the manufacture and characterization of cellular metals of open or interconnected poro, whose base metal was an aluminium-silicon alloy for casting, using a technique of infiltration of removable fillers modified. As fill material was used high purity marine salt with three ranges of particle size.
