# Teaching unit for the study of atmospheric variables, a strategy for teaching-learning the concept of climate.

This research work focuses on the development and implementation of a didactic unit for the study of atmospheric variables, as a teaching strategy, the learning of the concept of climate, from the methodology of research-action. In order to achieve the objectives set out in this work, the project was developed in several phases. In the first phase, the relevant conceptual, theoretical and pedagogical references were studied and revised, as well as the objectives that were desired with the proposal. The second phase focused on the design of the didactic instrument of work; a tool that was implemented in a third phase, from which the results were obtained that were evaluated in the last phase of the process; showing that more than 70% of the students of grade 10 of the I.E.D. El Carmen included the concepts of climate, atmospheric time, weather variables, climate factors among others; as well as 60% of them strengthened their scientific and research skills and the characterization of the climate of the municipality of Guasca.
