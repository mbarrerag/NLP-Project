# Is the loss of CD44, low expression of E cadherin and high expression of Ki-67 in malignant equine melanoma related to their biological behavior?

ABSTRACT The melanomas in horses affect all races and although 90% of these tumors in this species are benign, 66% of them progress to malignant forms and can make metastases, which translates into economic losses for owners. For this reason, it is essential to investigate prognostic factors and establish their relationship with histopathological criteria.The objective of the work was to identify the expression of CD44, E-cadherine and Ki67 and to relate them to cytological and histopathological findings in melanomas horses in order to identify a possible relationship between the anatomo-clinical expression and biological behavior.From the laboratory of pathology of the Paulista State University "Júlio de Mesquita Filho", Brazil, 15 cases of equin melanoma, confirmed by cytology, histopathology (biopsy); the samples were analyzed according to criteria of cytology, Kipp7 and all micrometric criteria of "Breslow", subsequently the samples were submitted to the technique of immunohisochemical (IHQ) for S-100, CD44, E67 and ECAD5
