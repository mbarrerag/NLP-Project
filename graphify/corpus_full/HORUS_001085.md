# Chemical study of red macroalgae of the department Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina with potential use in the cosmetic industry

Exposure to solar radiation is a significant risk factor for human health, contributing to the development of diseases such as skin cancer. The search for effective methods of photoprotection has led to the development of products that protect the skin against ultraviolet (UV) radiation. However, the adverse effects of some of these products, such as allergic reactions and the accumulation of its components in the environment, have aroused interest in the development of safer and more sustainable natural alternatives. In this context, the natural products obtained from marine organisms, such as red algae, have been generated by their bioactive properties, especially in the solar protection. In this context, the red algae of the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina represent an unexplored resource that could offer new compounds with photoprotective capacity.
