# MATHEMATIC MODEL FOR BIOLOGICAL CONTROL OF THE COFFEE HYPOTHENEMES HAMPEI BROKE BY HORMIGAS

Coffee growing is an important agricultural activity worldwide and very influential in the economy of some countries, as is the case of Colombia, which occupies third place among the countries with the highest production, after Brazil and Vietnam. Unfortunately, both the quantity and quality of coffee beans are severely affected by the coffee drills Hypothenemus hampei (Ferrari) (Coleoptera: Scolytidae), a beetle of the size of the head of a pin, which penetrates the grains to feed on the endosperm and deposit their eggs to develop their life cycle. One of the ways to counteract this beetle is by biological controllers, such as the ants (Hymenoptera: Formicidae), some of which are characterized by inhabiting naturally the coffee trees and feeding on brocas in all their biological states. Given the above, a pre-predatory interaction between these two insects is described, using a mathematical model based on ordinary differential equations, where the state variables correspond to the bi-d, immature brocas and ants are determined by the theory of the diet
