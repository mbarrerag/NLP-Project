# A DOCTING EXPERIENCE OVER THE COMIC, GRAPHICAL DESIGN AND ILLUSTRATION

The present article has two parts. The first is an approach to the notion of academic comics, in addition to an exhibition of some key backgrounds of comics and graphic novels used for didactic and pedagogical purposes in the framework of edutainment. This support is the basis of a collaborative project carried out by several specialists of the National University of Colombia, who, based on a bioanthropological research carried out in the former "Poor Cemetery" of Bogotá, in 2015, adapted the results of the academic and scientific project to the language of comics. In this sense, the second part of the article describes the process of adaptation, development and production of the graphic novel "Memento Mori", and also includes some conclusions derived from the process of exchange and interlocution between anthropology, literature and graphic design
