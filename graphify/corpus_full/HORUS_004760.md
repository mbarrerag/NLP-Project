# Technical-economic evaluation of an electricity-based fuel production system

This Final Work evaluates the technical and economic feasibility of implementing an alternative production system of synthetic fuels based on electricity, in the Eastern Plains of Colombia. The case of study starts with an over-weighted crude deposit that, due to its low viscosity, requires to be diluted with nafta for its transport, increasing costs and carbon footprint. Additionally, non-commercial gas is produced with 50% CO2. Considering the availability of CO2 and the possibility of producing green hydrogen, a technical-economic solution was evaluated to produce a synthetic fuel with low carbon footprint. The evaluation includes three types of synthetic fuel: DME (Dimethyl ether), gasoline and Diesel, in order to determine which would have the lowest cost of production. The calculation of the Leveled Cost of Energy (LCOE) revealed that in all cases its value is higher than that of the fossil fuels.
