# Development led by multiplatform mobile application models from a set of heuristic rules based on preconceptual schemes.

The development approaches led by models (MDD) aim to increase the productivity of the development team and reduce the time of marketing of the software. Some authors propose MDD approaches for mobile multiplatform applications. Therefore, a systematic review of the literature is carried out in search of MDD approaches for mobile multiplatform applications, resulting in 39 primary studies grouped into 19 different MDD approaches. It is observed that 100.0 % of the approaches lack close to natural modeling languages, 36.8 % of the approaches lack design patterns and 84.2 % lack usability characteristics. In addition, 42.1 % of the approaches use obsolete programming languages as a result of automation. Therefore, an MDD approach for mobile multiplatform applications is proposed through the use of preconceptual approaches. These schemes allow to guarantee a close to natural modeling language, include design patterns and include usability characteristics. In addition, the UN-LEND specification language is completed as an intermediate model between the preconceptual and multiplatformaform applications. These schemes allow to ensure the use of a close to natural layout, design language and design design. As a result of this Master's Thesis, some proposals and future challenges are identified as: including Heuristics of Programador Experience (PX) in the resulting code; allowing transformations between code, UN-LEND and preconceptual schemes; including rules related to vectors, matrices and the achievement relationships of preconceptual schemes; and developing a compiler for the UN-LEND models
