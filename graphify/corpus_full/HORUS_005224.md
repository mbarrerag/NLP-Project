# CO2 AND CH4 DYNAMICS IN A EUTROPHIC TROPIC ANDERAN RESERVE

We studied the dynamics of methane (CH 4 ) and carbon dioxide (CO 2 ) in a eutrophic tropical reservoir located in the Colombian Andes. The temporal and spatial dynamics were addressed by sampling during six field campaigns carried out over a period of two years. We monitored the flows in the air-water interface, the concentrations of dissolved gases, the physical and chemical properties of the water column, the microstructure profiles of turbulence and the weather conditions. Throughout the study period, the reservoir was a persistent source of CH 4 to the atmosphere with higher emissions occurring in the region near the entrance. During the periods of low water levels, both emissions and surface concentrations of CH 4 were higher and more heterogeneous spatially. The CO 2 flows measured in the air-water interface changed direction depending on the time and location, showing an alternate capture and emissions by the surface of the water.
