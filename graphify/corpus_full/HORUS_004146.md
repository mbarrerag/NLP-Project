# PROPOSALS FOR THE PROMOTION OF HEALTH FROM ALIMENATTION AND NUTRITION FOR INDIGENOUS PEOPLES.

The text provides a context for the situation of indigenous peoples in Colombia and the actions that have been carried out by the State in the area of health and health promotion. The importance of the differential approach in promoting health is introduced. Based on this, a review is made of the work and proposals that have been made in the Specialization of Food and Nutrition in Health Promotion. Proposals have been made that incorporate the ethnic differential approach into a commitment to interculturality. It is evident that the authors have approached each other in a transparent and respectful manner to the communities, with the aim of contributing to the quality of life from actions based on food and nutrition. The health promotion strategies applied in the formulation of the previous proposals have included the creation of favourable environments, the strengthening of community action, the development of personal skills and health education. The methodology used is participatory planning, understanding participation as a collective construction, since it enables the community to express its needs and identify the most important problems by analysing their structure, origin and the real possibilities with which it has to deal with them
