# HEADQUARTERS IN THE UNIVERSITY CITY OF BOGOTA: READING OF A MANIFESTIED BUILDING

This article describes the conceptual determinants that guided the design and construction of the headquarters building of the Inter-American Housing Center - CINVA, inside the campus of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá (1953). In the first part, an explanation is made concerning the methodology and state of art of related research. Subsequently, it describes the way in which the activities of the Center began and that in the short term would condition its functional program, to give way to the narrative about the genesis of design with a brief relationship of those who integrated the team in charge. The discussion focuses on how the construction systems employed responded to a series of project premises explicitly described, but that did not have full continuity in the academic training of the fellows of the Center. The conclusions seek to highlight the way in which the conjunction of all these aspects allows to affirm that the headquarters of the CINVA is a manifest building that served as a reference to a current of technological exploration in the country during much of the second half of the twentieth century
