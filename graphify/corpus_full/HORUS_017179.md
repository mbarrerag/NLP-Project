# EVALUATION OF EMERALDS BY THEIR ORIGIN: MULTIVARIATE ANALYSIS - INFRARED SPECTRA

Colombian emeralds, from Palo Arañado (east emerald zone, Chivor district), and Santo Domingo, La Pava mine (western emerald zone, Muzo district), and Zambian emeralds were studied in order to differentiate emeralds by geographical origin, using Fourier Transform Infrared spectroscopy, Principal Component Analysis, clustering, and Partial Least Squares ¿ Discriminant Analysis. The original data were smoothed, and a baseline correction was made. Furthermore, seven extreme emeralds and one outlier were eliminated. The results showed that the wavenumbers with the most significant contribution to the first principal component were the most valuable to separate the groups of emeralds by geographical origin. This allowed the identification of discriminatory vibrational bands associated with the chemical composition of the emeralds. Complete discrimination of emeralds from Zambia and Santo Domingo was obtained, while the Palo Arañado samples showed only five individuals that were 100 % differentiable
