# A MINI-CODE OF DNA BARS FOR IDENTIFICATION OF CALLIPHORIDAE (DIPTERA) MOSCASES OF SUD AMERICAN NOROESTE

The flies of the family Calliphoridae are of considerable importance because of their proximity to human beings (Marshall, 2012). Some species are of medical, veterinary, sanitary and forensic importance; they are an efficient mechanical vector of pathogens (Greenberg, 1973), they produce myasis in vertebrates (Norris, 1965), they are the most important organisms in the calculation of the time of death in forensic entomology (Greenberg & Kunich, 2002) and have recently been proposed as bioindicators of the quality of habitat and degree of anthropopic intervention in processes of fragmentation and restoration of tropical forests (Cabrini et al., 2013; de Sousa et al., 2014; Mendes et al., 2021). Their appropriate practical use and applied depends essentially on a precise taxonomic identification. In this sense, the identification of species in the neotropical region 2008 is faced with serious limitations due to the inadequate availability of taxonomic tools (Clavs) and little staff trained for this task (Whitworth, 2010).
