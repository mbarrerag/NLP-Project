# The agrarian problem in Colombia and proposals for resistance from Latin American agroecology

The agrarian problem in Colombia and proposals for resistance from Latin American agroecology is a book of a scientific nature, which addresses a historical problem that has been in force for 530 years, which has not been resolved until now. The text provides a novel perspective of reading, understanding and transformation of reality, namely, rural development is not part of the solution and therefore the different ways of presenting it, of aggregating it and promoting it only exacerbate the problems that are intended to be solved with its prescriptionary. Agroecology is not conceived as a science, but as a flow that starts from the recognition of agroecology as a sum of unknown, disqualified knowledge, subject on some occasions to hegemonic discourses. Agroecology is a materialized flow from the social movement and from the accumulated thousand-year-old knowledge of people, tested in agriculture in the broad sense of that collective and ancestral practice. Agroecology, as an insurrection of the knowledges of people and against the reduccionism of industrial agriculture, runs the risk of being captured by the order of scientific discipline, established by means of a formal discipline
