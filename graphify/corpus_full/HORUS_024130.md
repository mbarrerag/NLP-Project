# QUANTITATIVE APPROXIMATION TO APPROVAL AS A VARIABLE OF RELEVANTIENCE IN PUBLIC SPACE INTERVENTIONS. CASE OF STUDY: URBAN MOBILITY CORREDITOR OF THE AYACUCHO TRANVIA*

The urban interventions in Medellín have generated debates about their relevance. The objective of this article is to find criteria for defining it in projects in public space, based on its appropriation. Public space is understood as a common good, where the concept of appropriation is not linked to property, since it is not possible to speak of ownership of a common good, therefore, monofunctionality is a way of owning it, for this reason the term urban diversity is used as a variable to interpret appropriation. The case study is presented the Urban Corridor of Mobility of the Tram of Ayacucho¿ in the city of Medellín, a consolidated sector that modifies its traditional dynamics. There, a method is applied that involves quantitative techniques to evaluate qualitative phenomena such as urban diversity, appropriation of public space, and define the relevance of interventions
