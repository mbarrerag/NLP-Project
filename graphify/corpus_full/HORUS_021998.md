# Curriculum design of a line of emphasis on science for middle education.

In this work, a curriculum proposal was designed for a line of emphasis on sciences as a strategy to improve the construct: attitudes towards the sciences and how stimulating factor of scientific vocations in middle education students. As part of the justification of the project, a diagnosis was made that took into account three aspects: negative trend of results in tests Knowledge 11, low percentage of admission to higher education of graduates and negative attitudes to the sciences of high school students, particularly middle school. To respond to this situation and to enhance scientific learning through the curriculum proposal, the evaluation was carried out: the educational flow CTI (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics), the disciplinary content and methodologies of international curriculum references such as the NGSS (Sets of Science of the Next Generation) and the DP (Diplomatic Programme) of IB (International Basketball). The study was determined by the ABI (Learning Based on Indagation) as the pedagogical model of the proposal with its methodologies 5E and ABPy (Aply based on Projects). The study is innovative in the sense that it seeks to transfer quality methodologies, to which only privileged sectors have access, to a public educational institution and of a female character, also attacking gender inequality in the sciences. It is expected that this proposal will be extrapolable to other official educational institutions and contribute to the improvement of the quality of education in science and as a strategy to increase the scientific vocations so necessary in the so-called era of knowledge
