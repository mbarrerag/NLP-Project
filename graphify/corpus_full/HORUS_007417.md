# HEALTH AND LONGEVITY: BENEFITS OF PULMONARY REHABILITATION IN TOLERANCE TO THE EXERCISE AND QUALITY OF LIFE IN ANCIANS WITH COPD.

COPD has a high impact on the health of the elderly patient. Pulmonary rehabilitation (PR) offers an opportunity to improve symptoms specific to lung disease. to evaluate the effects of a RP program on exercise tolerance, dyspnea perception, fatigue and quality of life in a group of COPD patients over 60 years of age in a high level of complexity hospital in the city of Bogotá
