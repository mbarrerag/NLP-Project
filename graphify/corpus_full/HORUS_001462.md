# THE ECONOMIC TRAINING OF THE FAMILY IN CENTRAL AND COLOMBIA TEXTS IN THE XV TO XXS: ANOTHER HOUSE, GOVERNMENT, CALL AND MORAL

From a family approach as a privileged instrument of formation and government in Western modernity, differences and regularities are examined in the prescriptions regarding the formation of economic subjects in texts addressed to the family in Europe and Colombia from the fifteenth to the second decade of the nineteenth century. These texts are part of three discursive events: the birth of the modern treaty of formation of the family in the fifteenth century; the Christianization and civilization of these treaties in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries; and the intensification of the economic formation of poor wives and families since the last decades of the eighteenth century.
