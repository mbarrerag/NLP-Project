# VERTICAL STRUCTURE OF THE WATERS OF THE GREEL ALTARCTIC

The aim of the work is to determine the components of the thermohalin stability and the possible mechanisms formation of the thin structure of the Gerlache Strait, located along the Antarctic peninsula between the coast of Danco and the archipelago of Palmer. In the study region, the highest Superficial Temperature of the Sea (TSM) is recorded in February, reaching 2.0 °C in the central west zone. During the month of October the lowest TSM with -1.84 °C at the entrance of the south-west zone ending the Bismarck Strait and starting the Gerlache Strait. The highest TSM are located in the central western region of the Gerlache Strait and the lowest in the northeast region. The month of September records the Superficial Salinity of the Sea (SSM) highest with 34.4 months in the central region east. In February the lowest SSM with a thickness of 33.19 in the south-west region (G1). During the course of the year the highest SSM is located in the north-eastern zone of the strait (G12) and the lowest ones in the south-west. The thermocline with a maximum thickness of the summer and central months (inth and summer with a thickness of Upper Circumpolar Deep Water modified (mUCDW) water mass between 250 and 500 m southwest of the Gerlache Strait. Longitudinal T-transects show the intrusion of the modification of the High Salinity Shelter Waters (mHSSW) between 300 and 500 m north-east of the Gerlache Strait. Considering that thermohalin stability (E) is almost equal to its haline component (E Is Is), it is proposed that the condition of total thermohalin stability of the water column is stable from 0 to 300 m and indifferent to depths greater than 300 m. The oscillation values of the frequency of Vaisala Brunt (N) indicate water mixing processes mainly at surface level, to a greater extent in the summer and lower winter seasons. Finally, as for the fine structure, layers above 60 m are characterized by absolute stability (EA), below 60 m stratification by Convection observed by Layers (CC) prevails, and only stratification by Deinos (Deinos) 325 mDS) is observed by some 350 m
