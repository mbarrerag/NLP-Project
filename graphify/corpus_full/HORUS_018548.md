# Lightweight and self-compating concrete design from expanded polystyrene.

Concrete is a material that is recognized and using very frequently in the construction of buildings, structures and civil engineering works in general, because it is a material that has the ability to take different forms, it is relatively economical, excellent qualities when resisting fire and great durability among others, although it is a material of quite qualities also has some deficiencies as they are: 1. Structures with excessive weight and volume. 2. Some works with great architecture, but a poor seismic behavior due to its great mass and weight. 3. High costs in structures of great height and lights due to which the sections of the structural elements have to be more concrete strength this considerably increases the weight of the structure and thus its ability to resist the seismic forces, equally raising the price of the construction. Due to these problematics the objective of this research is to evaluate by means of laboratory tests, the behavior of the mixtures of concrete is self-compating and lightweight concrete (with addition of polystyrene expands), in order to: 1. reduce the weight of the structures. 2. Improve its behavior to seismic loads. 3. reduce the cost in large works. 4.
