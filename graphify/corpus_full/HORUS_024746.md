# Pedagogical use of the media in science teaching: Speech on bacteria.

This research seeks to analyze the discourse of the news in the newspapers DNA and Publimetro de Bogotá, between the years 2017-2018, around the concept of bacteria and in which it seeks to corroborate the intentionality and the sense in which these news are presented, through the identification of the elements that constitute the concept of bacteria in the analyzed media, the scientific validity, the coherence and conceptual errors of the presented phenomena, and the identification of possible incidences in the scientific dissemination. Thus, this research was organized in four phases: theoretical-methodological design, reading and organization, analysis and interpretation, proposed for the pedagogical use of the media in the science classes, through which the discourse analysis was made and it was found that the concept of bacteria in the two newspapers analyzed was constituted by four elements: public health problems due to the threat of bacteria, scientific development for the control of bacteria, benefits of bacteria for health and nutrition, and advertising of products for the prevention of bacterial diseases.
