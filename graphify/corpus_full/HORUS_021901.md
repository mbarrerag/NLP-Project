# Trusted interests: protection of investors and comparison of investors with the equity market and investment trust.

In recent years and in the last decade, in Colombia, trusts have been promoted as alternatives to investment, with which some promoters of real estate projects of a productive nature such as hotel complexes, commercial centers and business parks seek to finance them; this, in exchange with investors, would be to award them a portion of rights over the revenues produced by them, which would eventually result in the generation of returns or losses on them. However, over time, socio-economic problems were evident that affected investors, due to the low dissemination of information about the projects undertaken, the use of the moneys collected for it, and, the financial and legal situation of these and their developers; topics that attracted the attention of the Colombian government and its supervisory body for these matters, the Financial Superintendence of Colombia. This entity, seeking to guarantee the rights of investors, produced its External Circular 024 of 2016, regulating specifically such type of business principles.
