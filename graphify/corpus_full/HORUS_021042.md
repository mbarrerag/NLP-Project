# Identification of key variables for achieving equitable assessments in meritocratic processes: Elements for a proposal

The Colombian State adopted meritocracy as a public policy for access to and promotion to career jobs. After more than three decades of having begun the implementation of merit competitions to provide positions in the different public entities of the country, it is necessary to ask whether the evaluations carried out in the framework of these processes have complied with the principles of equity and justice. Taking into account the ethnic and cultural diversity of a country like Colombia, it is necessary to review the way in which merit competitions for the selection of career officials have been developed and are currently being developed and to know if these processes are truly inclusive. In addition, it seeks to propose the incorporation of recent evaluation progress in order to ensure that the assessments respond to the needs of each population group involved in these processes, on the one hand, emphasizing the content of the instruments, and on the other, the conditions of application.
