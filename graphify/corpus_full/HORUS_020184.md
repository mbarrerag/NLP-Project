# Digital Lensless holographic microscopy based on an optical pick-up unit

Digital lensless holographic microscopy (DLHM) supported on an optical pickup unit (OPU) is presented. The whole OPU is utilized to replace the costly and fragile pinhole-based illumination in DLHM to image micrometer-sized specimens
