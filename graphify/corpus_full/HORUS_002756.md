# FUTURE PEDAGOGAGES: TO TALK CREATION PROCESS AS SPECULATIVE PRAGMATISM LABORATORIES.

The authors of the article, as teachers, reflect on the need to propose experiences in the classroom and future pedagogies based on perceptive exploration exercises focused on drawing and collective performative actions, in alliance with plants, in order to question the art system and its clichés. The purpose is to bring the reader closer to the creation processes as laboratories of speculative pragmatism, where communities of co-learning are created that restore links and assume art in its overflow and indiscipline to open possible futures. The authors conclude that speculative experimentation of anthropo-decent revaluations of value is at stake
