# --Ecology and cultural representation of the Borugo (Cuniculus paca): case study in the Santa Sofia reserve, Amazonas.

The aim of this project is to understand the relationship between the Borugo (Cuniculus papa) and the communities of the Santa Sofía and El Progreso reserves, from traditional ecological practices and knowledge, and as a starting point for community monitoring of the Borugo in the area. Specifically, the study seeks (1) to analyze traditional practices and knowledge of management, use and cultural importance of the Borugo and its relations in the territory (biological and cultural), (2) to estimate the abundance of the Borugo in the protection, and to project possible trajectories of the population of this species under different management actions. The initiative arises due to the proximity of the members of the working team to the local communities, a collective dream of conservation and food sovereignty, and to the concern for the nutritional transition that threatens the loss of cultural tradition.
