# NEONATAL COLRESSIS: NARRATIVE REVIEW OF THE LATIN AMERICAN SOCIETY WORKING GROUP ON GASTROENTEROLOGY, HEPATOLOGY AND NEONATAL COLRESTITION: NARRATIVE REVIEW OF THE LATIN AMERICAN SOCIETY WORKING GROUP ON GASTROENTROLOGY, HEPATOLOGY AND PEDIATRITION

Neonatal jaundice requires a differential diagnosis between non-conjugated or indirect hyperbilirubinemia and direct or conjugate hyperbilirubinemia, characteristic of neonatal cholestasis, which is always pathological. It is recommended that in every newborn of 15 days or more of life with jaundice, differential bilirubin is performed to detect it. Colestasis is defined by direct bilirubin > 1 mg/dl
