# Quantile Regression Models from a Bayesian Perspective: Sensitivity Analysis and An Application In Student Performance Data in Tests Know

Quantitical regression is a statistical method that allows to estimate different cut-off points or percentiles of a variable of interest, in contrast to traditional linear regression that focuses only on the conditional mean. This technique is valuable because of its ability to manage atypical values, model nonlinear relationships, deal with asymmetric data and evaluate the ends of distribution. In this work, we will focus on exploring quantum regression from a Bayesian perspective, performing a sensitivity analysis that encompasses different a priori distributions and verisimilitude functions. As a final result, a practical application will be carried out using data from the ICFES to determine the effect of sociodemographic factors on the score obtained by students from different regions of the country in the tests Know 11
