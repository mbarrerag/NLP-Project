# ROLE OF DISCUSSION MARCATORS IN THE READING COMPREHENSION

This article is the result of a study whose objective was to determine whether the absence of discursive markers, which are the structure of information and connectors, affects the level of reading comprehension. To this end, an experiment was implemented with students from the first semester of various academic programs of a Colombian public university using a quantitative-descriptive approach with a quasi-experimental design, through a reading comprehension test with a control group and an experimental one. The text was delivered to the experimental group without the markers. The data were analyzed based on a statistical test of research hypotheses. The results showed that the presence of this kind of markers does not affect the processing of literal or inferential information from the text; however, it does so with respect to the metallinguistic processing of the text, which implies a higher level of abstraction by the reader
