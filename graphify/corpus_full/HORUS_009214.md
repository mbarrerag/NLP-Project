# DIVERSITY OF THE BISH COMMUNITY AND ITS RELATIONSHIP TO THE FLORAL RESOURCE: A STRATEGY FOR CONSERVING URBIAN AREAS

Bees are very important for the ecology of many ecosystems, however, many species are in danger and many interactions between bees and vegetation of urban ecosystems are unknown, which become very important places for the conservation of these pollinators. This study describes and compares a set of data of bees present in six strategic urban ecosystems of Antioquia (three in the center and three in the periphery), the differences found (wealth and abundance), the methods of collection and a description of their visits. In each park or urban area a systematic sampling was carried out for 12 months. Where it was carried out trapping, capture and follow up of the visits of bees in four transects. To study the floral preferences of some bees, the family with greater abundance was Apidae, where they visited and counted the most of the plants with KOH. A total of 3522 individuals of 149 species and 46 genera were collected.
