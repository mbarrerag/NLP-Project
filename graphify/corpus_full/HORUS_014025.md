# Effects of public welfare policies on the Food Sovereignty of the indigenous community Emberá Canchidó - Risaralda

The indigenous community Emberá Chamì, located in the department of Risaralda, has been beneficiary of various types of humanitarian and assistance assistance aid, with the purpose of addressing the worrying indicators of hunger that affect this ethnic group. These aids have ranged from the direct distribution of food, which includes essential food rations and pay-for-work systems, to the support of community projects that are compensated with food and the implementation of food security initiatives based on technological packages that contemplate improved seeds, fertilizers, insecticides and agricultural tools. In this context, the main objective of this research project is to analyze the consequences of the assistance programs on the food sovereignty of the indigenous community Emberà. To carry out this analysis, a research methodology will be employed with a qualitative approach of particular ethnographic cut. This approach will be based on the realization of semi-structured interviews, focal groups and dialogues of knowledge with the Emberá population.
