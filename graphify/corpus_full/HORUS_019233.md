# PROGRAMME IMPLEMENTATIONS WHEREAS CAREERS®: THE DOCTION ARTICLES RESEARCH-EXTENSION

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), for Colombia in 2014, mortality figures indicate that 71% of these, considering both genders and all ages, are associated with chronic non-transmissible disease (NCHD) conditions [1]. The burden of NTCD is 81 % and reports high inequalities in health care [2]. Globally, this burden significantly affects the poorest countries and causes 80 % of deaths. The diseases that most burden in the world represent cardiovascular diseases, cancer, chronic lung diseases and diabetes mellitus [3]. In the regional context, the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) [4] reported that in Latin America chronic diseases cause 4.8 million deaths per year, of which one third cause deaths before age 70. Cardiovascular diseases, diabetes and cancer are positioned as chronic diseases with the highest morbidity and mortality. The epidemiological situation of chronic diseases in Colombia is not far from the above-mentioned scenario. In 2017, diseases in the circulatory system produced 30% of deaths and 16.7% of years of life lost [5]
