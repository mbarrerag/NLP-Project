# CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF ESSENTIAL WEDELIA CALYCINE PLANT OILS GROWING IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The characterization of the AEs was carried out by gas chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry. Two columns were used, with different stationary phases (polar and non-polar). The compounds were tentatively identified, when comparing their mass spectra (EI, 70 eV), with those reported in spectral databases (ADAMS, NIST, Wiley), and their linear retention rates with those reported in the literature (3). The majority compounds found were a-pinene (4-28%), sabinene (3-5%) trans-ß-caryofilene (3-14%), germacrene D (14%), carotol (13%), cariofilene oxide (6.7%) and spatulenol (6-7 %). The analysis of the main components (PCA) performed on the components of the AE showed the existence of two types of W. calycina, which may be due to differences in the environmental conditions in which plants grew, or their different phenological states
