# Ember Place and Resistance in Puerto Boyacá

In this article I analyze two processes of territorialization of an embera katío community on the urban edge and in the rural area of the municipality of Puerto Boyacá, from the categories of sense of place developed by Ulrich Oslender. From an ethnographic perspective that pays attention to the contrasts, solidaritys and daily conflicts that occur during the procedures of acquisition of land and formation of reserves, I explore how the territorialized spaces operate as scenarios of inclusion and domination through the categories of permitted Indian and subversive Indian. Finally, I explore the demands and ways of fact of the community emera katío and highlight the relationship between domination and resistance in the settlements that build and build them to the communities in conditions of forced displacement
