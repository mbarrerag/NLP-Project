# DISCUSSION AND PROPOSALS ON CONVERGING TECHNOLOGIES AND INDUSTRY 4.0

With the presentation of the International Mission of Sages in February 2019, once again the public debate was opened, especially in the academic field, about the relevance and practical utility of the projects of such characteristics. While, on the one hand, there are those who have expressed their satisfaction with this initiative of a prospective nature, on the other hand, there are those who, based on the little impact that the Mission had of the previous one of 1993, have expressed their disagreement with what they conceive as an exercise without real possibilities to affect the Colombian reality. However, and beyond the validity that concerns both poles of this discussion, it is necessary to appreciate that the launch of this new Mission of Sages involves another series of dynamics that it is not possible to leave aside, which are, in a strict sense, those that they have encouraged the National University of Colombia to join this commitment for the construction of a future for the country of the hand of science, technology, innovation and artistic and cultural creation. Indeed, far from reducing the question of giving credit or not to the work of the forty-seven wise ones summoned in this new version of the Mission, this House of Studies and the Castisian. To give effect to its participation, the National University of Colombia delegated to some of its most outstanding teachers the task of attending the events of the focus of the Mission of the Wise, listening to the opinions of its colleagues and, on this basis, consolidating critical contributions aimed at dynamizing and enriching the discussions led by the forty-seven experts appointed by the National Government. As a result of this effort, this document is the main reference point for the National Meeting of the Focus of Basic Sciences and Space of the Mission held in Manizales between 19 and 21 August 2019, it has the advantage of providing a collective, thought-provoking and powerful vision that puts into dialogue the past, present and future through creative reflection. We hope that this effort will open the doors to new and suggestive proposals that will place emphasis on the growth and prosperity of the Colombian population, and that, in turn, will be an incentive for the academy and the scientific community to continue working towards the country and its people
