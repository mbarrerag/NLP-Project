# RESOURCES, CONTRAINDICATIONS AND CONTROVERSIES OF PEDIATRIC HOSPITALIZATION IN PSYCHIATERIA.

Pediatric hospitalization in psychiatry has clinical and psychosocial risks for patients in their families and the staff in charge. Few studies have focused on the risks of dislocation and contagion.The recommendations to mitigate these risks are not feasible in the Colombian health system.It is necessary to know these risks to support therapeutic decisions in pediatric population with severe mental disorders, investigate actions to mitigate them and inform the actors responsible for health policies
