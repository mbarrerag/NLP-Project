# Design and development of new magnetic ceramic materials with spintronic properties

The complex compounds of perovskit type constitute one of the most exciting families of materials due to the wide range of technological applications that derive from their physical properties attuned to their composition. Among them, the possibility of generating new channels of transport of spin, besides the transport of electric charge, opens new perspectives within the spintronic industry. In this project we will focus on the double perovskit study with general formula A2BB'O6. An exhaustive theoreticalexperimental study of these compounds will be carried out, including structural, magnetic, semiconductor and thermophysical properties, correlated with ab-initio calculations in the framework of the Functional Density Theory and thermodynamic properties using the quasi-harmonic model of Debye. It is expected to find behaviors of a semiconductor ferromagnetic character with potential applicability in spintronic technology
