# Analysis of rheological behaviour and physico-chemical properties in salt water base drilling fluids under controlled laboratory conditions._x0094_

One of the most important aspects during drilling is the sludge or drilling fluid used and this is due to the multiple functions that it must perform, that is why, from this point on, it will depend in large part on whether the operation is successful or has additional setbacks and costs. The properties of a drilling fluid must be evaluated and defined based on the requirements of the well, and in order to minimize or prevent negative events occurring during drilling different compounds and additives are used that improve the properties that control the characteristics of the sludge. In this project, a study is sought from a base sludge to be made on salinity and the effect that it has on the main properties that influence during drilling. A salt water base drilling fluid is produced at different salt concentrations, which are analyzed by measuring different properties such as reology, filtration losses, chemistry and in general all those evaluated in the laboratory that can indicate and ensure an optimal drilling sludge. At the end, a report will analyze and relate the different sludges resulting from the different salt concentrations used, where their effectiveness and performance of each of the fluids is required, as well as an optimal water formulations
