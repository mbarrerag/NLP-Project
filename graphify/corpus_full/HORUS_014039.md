# Preventive maintenance of teaching and research equipment and purchase of variable volume micropipettes, for the biological process laboratory that provides services to the research group in Industrial Biotechnology and the curricular area in Biotechnology.

This project seeks to obtain resources that allow the hiring of preventive maintenance processes of the following equipment used for research and teaching: water purification system, five orbital agitators, four spectrophotometers, a vertical autoclave, two pH meters, two refrigerators, one ultrafreezer, two centrifuges, two laminar flow chambers, three magnetic agitators. Additionally, the purchase of four micropipettes of different volumes is required for research activities
