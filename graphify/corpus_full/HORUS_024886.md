# Physical and chemical structural changes of biomass during slow pyrolysis.

This research shows a new methodology for assessing the slow pyrolysis of lignocellulosic biomass using cellulose as a model compound. For this, cellulose pyrolysis was characterized through the Friedman method [1], and the kinetic models of Broido-Shafizadeh [3], Diebold [4] and Ranzi et al. [5]. Based on the Ranzi et al. model [5], a new kinetic model for cellulose pyrolysis was proposed considering the functional groups representative of volatile compounds produced during the reaction. The values calculated for the activation energy by this kinetic model are closely related to the values calculated by the Ranzi et al. model
