# GENOCIDE OF EX-INSURGENTS IN COLOMBIA OR THE STORY OF AN ETERNAL RETURN

Six years after the signing of the Havana Peace Agreement, academics from different universities take stock of the state of implementation of the most relevant aspects of the Agreement. Furthermore, they point out the open perspectives with the new government elected in 2022
