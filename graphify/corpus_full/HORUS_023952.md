# Catholic intellectuals, magazines and mass Catholicism. An intellectual history of secularization in Colombia, 1934-1957.

This work analyzes the intellectual dimension of the Colombian Catholic world between 1934 and 1957. For this general objective, it is part of identifying three facets in the mechanisms that from Colombian intellectuality were employed for the defense and promotion of Catholicism: the biographical-intellectual trajectories, the periodicals catholic and the realization of mass events. The work focuses its analysis on the trajectory of Gonzalo Restrepo Jaramillo (1895-1966) as a Catholic intellectual, and articulates that journey with the intellectual context of the period studied. It also explores the creation and transformations of the Javeriana Journal, visualizing its strategies in the context of a Catholic editorialism in its first two decades of circulation. It also raises the notion of mass Catholicism, in the study of Catholic congresses and other meetings that were carried out in the period. This exploration corresponds to an intellectual history of secularization, that is to say, an application of methodologies of intellectual history and of intellectuals to the process in which societies, including their religious components, adopt differentitive elements and complex in a modern context, which implies an approximation to the secularization from a more interdisciplinary perspective, that includes the Colombian perspective. Also, this work explores the creation and transformations of the Javeriana Magazine, and observations its strategies inside an Catholic publishing institution in the two first decades of circulation. Ultimately, its used the notion of mass Catholicism for the study of Catholic congresses and meetings. These three topics represent a the secularization’s intellectual history, this is an use of intellectual history methodology for the study of processes in which the same religious manifestations get made self modern and complex aspects. Therefore, this research collect some debates from religion sociology and Church Catholic American history. The following these are defended: there was a defense of Catholicism in Colombia capable of dialoguing and introducing aspects of modern culture and intellectualities
