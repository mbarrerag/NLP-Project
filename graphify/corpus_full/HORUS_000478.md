# Management tool for decision-making in the use of domestic wastewater in rural areas of Colombia from the context of circular economy

This research proposes a management tool for decision-making in the selection of technologies for the use of domestic waste water (ARD) in rural areas of Colombia under the framework of the circular economy (EC). In this proposal, the selection of alternatives through the application of the multicritical evaluation method (TOPSIS) is proposed, relating spatialized information of the area of study of interest, so that the results obtained can be represented cartographically through a Geographic Information System (GIS). The dimensions and criteria proposed for the development of the methodology were based on the conceptual and structural guidelines previously identified for Colombia; where the weighting of the dimensions proposed was established from the consultation to experts.
