# TEXTILE DOING TO INVENT LIFE IN THE ADD TO COLOMBIAN ARMED CONFLICT

This article displays three textile fabric creations carried out daily by women of rural origin who have been victims of the armed conflict in Colombia and who live in the municipalities of Bojayá, Quibdó and Sonsón. These activities were identified in the analysis of the textile biographies carried out on women belonging to handicraft groups and costumers of memory in each of the localities. We argue that these practices are forms of political resistance at the domestic level that accompany the collective work of which these women are part.
