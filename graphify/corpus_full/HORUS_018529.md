# Evaluation of the angiogenic potential of conditioned media obtained from mesenchymal stem cells derived from adipose tissue grown in a dynamic system in the healing of skin wounds.

Introduction The mesenchymal stem cells derived from adipose tissue (A-MSC) exercise their regenerative-reparative action by means of a mechanism of paracrine action produced by hundreds of trophic factors, components of the extracellular matrix, extracellular vesicles, etc., which are secreted to the extracellular environment.This secretive can be collected from the culture media as conditioned media (MC).The A-MSC MCs have a tissue effect similar to the cellular transplantation, which allows their use as a therapeutic alternative. Up to now, the A-MS MCs are obtained essentially in crops of two dimensions (2D) static in normoxia, however, to scale their production it is necessary to perform the cultivation in dynamic systems guaranteeing the conservation of their regenerative-reparative potential.The main objective of this work was to evaluate whether the scaling of the MCs production of the A-MS, by introducing two changes in the cultivation system (a dynamic three-dimensional system and hypoxia) it maintains the angigenic and functional capacity as an indirect measure of the conservation of its therapeutic potential. Functional. Obtaining MCs in dynamic 3D conditions in hypoxia can enhance their therapeutic effect on the closure of acute skin wounds
