# A GEOLOGIST PER MUNICIPALITY INITIATIVE: BRIDGING GEOLOGY AND GOVERNANCE FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT IN COLOMBIA

In Colombia, the unique and complex landscape, prone to natural hazards, as well as the wealth of natural resources, underscores the necessity for geologists in guiding sustainable development and mitigating risks. Recognizing this, there is a push for a law named "Un Geologo ́ por Municipio" (A Geologist per Municipality), which would ensure that each Colombian municipality has a geologist to oversee environmental and land-use matters, assess natural hazards, manage natural resources, and help in disaster planning and response. This initiative aims to improve the management of the country's geological resources, reduce disaster risks, and promote sustainable use of the environment. The proposed legislation is designed to make certain that every municipality can benefit from the specialized knowledge of geologists, thereby fostering informed decisionmaking for the welfare of the community and the country's development
