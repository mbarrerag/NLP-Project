# TECHNOLOGY IN ARCHITECTURE. A METHOD FOR KNOWLEDGE OF HISTORICAL BUILDING

This chapter presents a method that allowed to know the technology of a set of heritage buildings and to carry out analysis by comparison, that is, contrast the technological in several buildings of the same epoch, of the same region, of the same author, etc. After considering several methods of technological evaluation of constructive systems and to investigate the principles of architecture, the method consisting of several stages is defined. The first stage is the obtaining of information. To start the evaluation of the buildings it is necessary to have the historical information that will facilitate the process, since there are variables that can only be known through the historical study, process that basically consists in the application of the method of historical research. Then a scale of analysis is defined that depends on the information obtained in the previous phase; in this method a general scale is assumed. Subsequently, a tool is defined that allows to evaluate the factors to be evaluated with its variables and indicators, which must be based on the principles that govern the architecture and all its spatial or material elements. Here we will understand how the spatial cannot be detached from the technological. Subsequently, for the definition of some indicators and its form of application, it is used to determine the parameters defined in the regional constructions of the period, which are finally defined by the regional ones
