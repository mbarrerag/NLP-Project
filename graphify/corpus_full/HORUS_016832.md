# INFLUENCE OF TIXW INTERLAYER SINTERED BY SPUTER RF IN ADHESION OF THE DIAMOND LIKE CARBON COATINGS ONTO TOOL STEEL

Diamon Like Carbon or DLC coating has a disadvantage and that is low adhesion on different metallic substrates such as steels. This problem is mainly due two reason, the first is the difference in the coefficient of thermal expansion between the substrate and the DLC coating and the other is the high diffusion between of carbon and the substrate. With the aim of improving the adhesion of DLC coatings to AISI H13 tool steel, an interlayer will deposit of TixW. Titanium multilayer systems have been used as an interface in some investigations, showing good results in increasing the adhesion of DLC protective coatings and diamond coatings on metal surfaces, because Ti can form chemical bonds with Fe
