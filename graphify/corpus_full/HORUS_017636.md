# Mosaic of the center of Medellín. Subjectivities exhibited and inscribed in the urban practices of three places of overflow (1999-2019)

In this research we have drawn the purpose of elucidating the forms in which a subjectivities, which are inscribed and made visible in a singular urban practice, have been constituting and making explicit the overflows of the urban environments that surround three of the churches of greater symbolic relevance of the traditional center of the city of Medellín, namely: the church of La Veracruz, the church of La Candelaria and the Metropolitan Cathedral. These modes of constitution of these three places are addressed here starting from the premise that in each of them three sociospatial dimensions of the city enter into dispute: one that is narrated, one that is planned and another that is lived. To address this problem, we propose the notion of places of overflow as a methodological theoretical category for the renewed reading of complex urban phenomena and, from there, we propose a problematization of the forms of the city's own participation, in which in the last 20 years we have been talking, planning and renewing the Centre in key of its necessary “recovering”. From there, we make explicit the interpretative limits that offer both the planned dimension and the dimension of the city's narrated dimension. in dispute: one that is narrated, one that is planned and another that is lived. To address this problem, we propose the notion of overflowering places as a theoretic-methodological category for a renewed reading of complex urban phenomena. From there, we formulated a problematization of the homogenous ways in which the need for “recovery” of the downtown area has been discussed about, planned, and renewed. Then, we make explicit the interpretive limits that both the planned dimension and the narrated dimension of the city offer and focus our attention on presenting some of the ways with which it has been lived.
