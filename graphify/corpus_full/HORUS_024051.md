# Drug trafficking and culture: habitus and daily life in contemporary Medellín.

In this work, it is part of the idea that, with the drug trafficking incursion in Medellín, a reconfiguration of the ways of socially behaving and of expressing culturally many people in their daily lives has been given. Therefore, it is assumed that around these reconfigurations a set of representations has been constructed that assign meaning to the ways of conceiving life and inhabiting the city, a fact that makes many behaviors judge or interpret themselves as part of the reproduction of the life model that drug trafficking instituted. In this sense, it is analyzed how drug trafficking has influenced the reconfiguration of certain actors in the daily life, but that, today of being fed and updated through the circulation of global cultural influences. Thus, we show how many modes of behavior have been structured in the daily life of Medellín that are interpreted, but are labeled by the transformation of the phenomenon of drug trafficking. the reconfiguration of the habitus of certain actors in daily life, and these, as ways of thinking and acting, are structured on the basis of a particular worldview that gives means to numerous cultural expressions, that contain those less than some historical sediments through the costume of the drug trafficking phenomenon, then today they are fed and updated through the circulation of global cultural influences. Thus, we show how some modes of better have been structured in the daily life of Medellin are currently interpreted and judged through trafficking experiences, but this practice is not only attributable to drug traffickers
