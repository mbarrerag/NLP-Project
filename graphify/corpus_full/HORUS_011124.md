# IMPACT OF COVID-19 ON HEALTH WORKERS A LOOK FROM THE INEGRALITY OF THE OCCUPATIONAL DESIGN CONCEPT

Introduction/Justification: Health workers have been one of the groups most affected by the situation of COVID-19, with their health and well-being being necessary to address this crisis. Official statistics and the media have shown this impact based on the number of contagions and deaths of health personnel, but there is no mention of the consequences in other vital areas. Understanding that the occupational performance of this population may have been disturbed in its different areas, it is sought to identify how much this impact has been revealed in the occupational and health media. To recognize the impact on health and occupational performance that COVID-19 has generated on health workers and that it has been documented by the Colombian press. Method/Focus: This descriptive study examined journalistic articles on the websites of the most representative Colombian media. The findings were analyzed through a system of categories inspired by the social determinants of occupational health and performance concepts.
