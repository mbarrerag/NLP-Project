# LOVE, VUOTE AND THE FEMALE

In this way, the present work appeals to three meanings: love, void and feminine, three concepts that today allow to sustain and embrace that sort of perpetual revolution (Sauret, 2021) that is the subject himself, in a world that increasingly seeks to reduce us to individuals, simple living beings without desire, consecrated to hyperconsumption or hyperproduction. And more precisely, these meanings are not called only in this work to guarantee a place to this subversion of the subject, but Jairo Enrique Gallo Acosta connects them to weave with them precisely what is impossible to close, the hole in knowledge, which is the condition of social bond, what makes it possible and renews it, what allows to accommodate what has most singular everyone within the community
