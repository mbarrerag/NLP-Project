# What if we don't laugh at the river? Reflections on the main ecological structure and public space in regional planning for a sector of the middle basin of the Bogotá river

The public space and the main ecological structure are two important structures at the time of planning the planning, especially urban of a territory. However, these two structures have been dislinked and their complementarity has not been shown at the time of ordering and structuring a territory. For the case of study of this document, the river Bogotá is proposed, in part of its middle basin, as an articulating element between these two structures at regional level. Thus it is intended to achieve a reflection around the strong and coherent relationship between the main ecological structure and the public space for the territorial planning. The analysis is carried out from a multiscale territorial approach reviewing the behavior of different dimensions of development, then analyzing the relationship between these two structures and establishing problems and tensions that are presented in the study area and in the relationship between public space and the main ecological structure. To conclude the work, strategies and guidelines are established with the aim of balancing the tensions encountered, and to guide the articulation of these two structures at the time of planning a territory. The contribution of this analysis is to highlight the need to make a change in the urban planning approach, putting the environmental structure and the development centre, especially the water and the development. and the public space for spatial planning.
