# DIRECT STRATEGY FOR TEACHING MASOTERApia IN STUDENTS WITH VISUAL DISABILITY, PILOT STUDIES

There are different barriers in higher education reported by students with disabilities, as well as challenges related to the management of disability and the career requirements they are in. This requires a significant effort to compensate for the lack of adaptations, since students with disabilities report that they have difficulties in obtaining accessible or adapted learning materials. to design and implement a didactic strategy for teaching masotherapy in students with visual disabilities of the Fisiotherapy program. quasiexperimental study, pre and post with control group, which had a study group made up of 21 students, who received the didactic strategy designed and a control group of 26 students who received the traditional methodology. The intervention protocol employed within the study included the use of multisensory strategies and 5 explanatory videos with subtitles for population with auditory disabilities. Post-intervention did not show significant differences (Prueba U de Mann Whitney p=0.069 in post-market; p=0.76 in post-practic; among the groups, however, the two groups presented the multiple study strategies and p=0 and p=0 test)
