# Meanings attributed to sexual diversity in the family by social workers in socio-legal intervention scenarios in Bogotá D.C.

The research arises from the questioning of the sexual diversity in the family area in socio-juridical intervention scenarios. From a qualitative approach and exploratory scope, the study analyzed the meanings attributed to this diversity by seven (7) social workers linked to police stations and family defenders in Bogotá D.C. From the social construction, the technique used was the semi-structured interview, which allowed recognition of interpretations, tensions and challenges that face the everyday nature of their interventions when addressing problems related to the violation and threats of rights.
