# MACHINE LEARNING APPLICED TO THE ELECTION OF MODE WHEN TRAVEL IS CRIMINATED BY TIME

The transport planning requires modeling the choice of the mode of travel to predict demand and understand the causal variables. This modal choice depends on a large number of variables, so that often discrete choice models have been used, where the modes of travel are mutually exclusive and joint alternatives within different frameworks. The Logit Multinomial (MNL) model is the most used discrete choice model. However, the MNL has several limitations when assuming that the probability of each alternative is independent of the characteristics of the rest of the alternatives. On the other hand, there is evidence that the machine learning algorithms or Machine Learning (ML) (ML) machine learning) function optimally for the statistical approaches used for the modeling of choice of mode of travel, because these do not make drastic assumptions about the data studied and learn to represent nonlinear and, in general complex relationships. On the other hand, the strategy of applying a rationalization of the available road space for the private vehicle seeks that the drivers choose other alternatives to access to the urban centers due to the criminalization in time they perceive.
