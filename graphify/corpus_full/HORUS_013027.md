# "That strange thing we are." An aesthetic approach to the production of female transsexuality from the practice of street prostitution in Barbacoas

This thesis proposes an aesthetic and interdisciplinary approach to the forms of production of female transsexuality from the practice of street prostitution in the space of Barbacoas from a case study. To carry out this task, a description is made on the urban, historical and aesthetic conformation of the sector; the configuration of its streets as fields of expressive deployment and affirmative strategies of specific modes of female transsexuality. To understand in depth the experiences, the affirmations, the sensitive manifestations and the aesthetic behaviors of transsexuality, the forms of perception of itself and of presentation of itself in the particular case of a transsexual of prostitution in that public space are analyzed; the polymorphic relationship that establishes with itself, its link with different discursive-material productions, with varied conditions of truth and with different processes of identification and of social sense.
