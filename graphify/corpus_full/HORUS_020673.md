# Quality of life and nutritional status of the elderly institutionalized in a geriatric home in Bogotá

This research sought to identify association and quality of life and nutritional status of a group of elderly institutionalised in a Center for the Welfare of the Elderly located in the town of Antonio Nariño de la ciudad de Bogotá. The population studied were adults over 60 years old, who resided in this institution. A descriptive and cross-sectional type study was carried out, in which tools such as the generic Health-Related Quality of Life (CVRS) EuroQol-5D (EQ-5D) were applied.
