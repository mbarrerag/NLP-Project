# DESCRIPTIVE ANALYSIS OF THE USERS OF CONVENTIONAL TAXI AND AMOBILITY WITH DIGITAL PLATFORM IN MEDELLÍN, COLOMBIA

This article aims to carry out a descriptive analysis of taxi and car users or vehicle with digital platform (VPD), in Medellín, Colombia. For this purpose, a survey was conducted among 200 individuals. Prior to this, a focus group and a pilot survey were carried out to properly design the final survey. A socioeconomic analysis of users of modes of transport, travel behaviour, travel characteristics and choice of one way or another was shown. The most relevant finding is that users corresponding to the age group between eighteen to twenty-nine years prefer to use VPDs over the traditional taxi. It was also found that VPD users prefer them because they usually have rates more economical than taxi fares. The frequency of use of the two modes coincides, being this one time a week. Moreover, travel made by taxi users and VPDs are mostly solo. Finally, the possession of a car or motorcycle is not an impediment to make at least one weekly trip using either of the two modes in question, which is logical, considering that 2023 there is a restriction to circulate in car or motorcycle one week in the city of Medellín
