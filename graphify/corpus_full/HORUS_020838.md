# LOCAL INITIATIVES FOR THE REFUPERATION OF THE HUMEDALS OF THE TIWA HABILDE IN LATICIA, AMAZONS.

The community of the TIWA lobby is composed of people from nine ethnic groups (Murui, Yucuna, Tikuna, Bora, Inga, Cubeo, Tanimuka, Cocama and Miraña) coming from non-municipalized areas of the department of the Amazonas and the northern part of the Colombian Amazon, who have had to move from their territories because of the context of armed conflict and drug trafficking that occurs in these areas. They have settled in km 6 adjacent to the community of San José of the Via Los Kilómes de Leticia, and so far they are not under the figure of indigenous protection. That is why this lobby is considered a vulnerable community, because it does not count on the recognition nor the benefits and subsidies that the government provides to the ethnic communities. The project therefore contributes to the fundamental right that these indigenous communities have in relation to having a healthy environment.
