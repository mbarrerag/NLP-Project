# Study of the effect of climate on the production of a dairy herd from the dynamics of systems.

The influence of climate on grazing systems is a reality. However, given the multiple interactions between the different climatic variables: temperature (T, °C), solar radiation (Rs, W/m2), precipitation (Pp) and wind speed (Vv, m.s-1) and variables associated with herding herds (forage consumption, body temperature, among others), make their understanding and analysis in favour of seeking strategies aimed at sustainability complex. The aim of this work is to study the relationship, through a systemic approach that allows integrating the greater number of variables associated with the study problem. The constructed simulation model was formulated with primary (sensor) and secondary information (semi-structured surveys) of a bovine herd dedicated to milk production, located in the department of the Valle del Cauca.
