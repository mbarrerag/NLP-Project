# Evaluation of parameters of synthesis of metallic oxides as carriers of methotrexate on its potential activity as antineoplastic.

With the purpose of developing a material carrying the active substance methotrexate (MTX), antineoplastic belonging to the pteridine family, this project proposes the synthesis of particles type core-covered or core shell, with nucleus formed from iron oxides through the method of coprecipitation and subsequent functionalization or coating with MTX. There are reports that the use of MTX generates among others adverse side effects in tissues exposed to light and that, depending on the method of administration, the average bioavailability can vary from 33 to 77%. Therefore, it is interesting to generate a material that can be both carrier of the active principle, as it contributes to the better use, penetrability and/or bioavailability of MTX, trying to achieve with lower doses the same therapeutic effect and consequently, to reduce the adverse effects and its release to the environment after its excretion. The materials synthesized from the iron oxides, with and without coating agent (MTX), will be characterized and will be performed with the same therapeutic dose and minimal chem effects on the cell cycle.
