# Estimate of soil losses caused by water erosion in the Dagua River Basin, Colombia

The Dagua river basin, Colombia, is the most important source of water for the ecosystem of Valle del Cauca, however, due to poor agricultural practices has been affected by water erosion. The aim of this study was to estimate soil erosion in the Dagua river basin, using the universal equation of soil losses (USLE). The results show that most of the area presents erosivity that are among the 1000-5000 MJ.mm.ha-1, corresponding to low and very low categories.
