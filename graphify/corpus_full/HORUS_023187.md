# LABOUR MARKET WORK AND GENDER DISIGUALITIES.

Gender inequalities are heterogeneous in the world. In the case of Latin America, the systematic increase in female labour participation has contributed to greater economic independence, higher educational levels, and broader spheres of political participation. However, this increase in participation has had great obstacles in terms of employment opportunities and access to labour guarantees under decent conditions.Under this problem, the aim of this paper is to analyze the inequalities that occur in the field of work, and particularly the situation in the case of Colombia
