# Reduction of uncertainty in glucose monitoring for people with type 1 diabetes mellitus in the presence of asynchronous measurements and data loss.

The diabetes Mellitus type 1 is a disease in which the homeostatic system of glucose is interrupted due to an autoimmune response of the body where the beta cells of the pancreas are destroyed. The treatments available for this disease are the functional therapy of insulin and treatment by means of artificial pancreas. These two treatments have as foundation the continuous monitoring of the levels of glucose. Such monitoring is carried out by means of continuous monitors of glucose and/or glucometers. The continuous glucose monitor takes periodic measurements while the glucometer takes sporadic measurements synchronic and asynchronous. However, this monitoring is not necessary due to faults of the sensors, disconnection or bad manipulation of the user, generating uncertainty. To address this problem, in this work 3 techniques were designed to reduce the uncertainty in the monitoring of the levels of glucose. First it was proposed four instances of measurement (menor, higher, intermediate and null), in order to generate a mathematical, graphical and verbal representation of the problems presented in the measurement.
