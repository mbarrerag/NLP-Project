# Influence of iron oxide deposits on the resistance to hot corrosion of YSZ thermal barriers used in gas turbines.

The present research evaluated the effect of iron oxide deposits on the resistance of hot corrosion of the thermal barriers of Zirconia stabilized with Itria (YSZ) in the presence of vanadium oxide. In the experiments, YSZ samples were contacted with vanadium oxide (V2O5) at 900°C for 90 min with and without iron oxide addition. The test surfaces and cross section of the samples were evaluated by optical and electron scanning microscopy. The porosity of the cross section and the thermal barrier delamination was measured through digital imaging. Microstructural analysis was carried out by X-ray diffraction (DRX). The elemental chemical microanalysis was carried out by X-ray dispersive energy spectroscopy (EDX). Iron oxide was found to have no inhibitory or accelerator effect on the hot corrosion mechanism of YSZ given that it is dissolved by vanadium oxide once it reaches its melting point
