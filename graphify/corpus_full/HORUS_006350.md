# Analysis of patterns and trends in the use of drugs used in sedation and analgesia for the management of patients hospitalized in the ICU COVID of a third-level hospital in Bogotá before and during the first year of pandemic by COVID-19 (2019 – 2021)

The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic (COVID-19) represented a major challenge for global health systems, seriously affecting developing countries due to their limited resources. Approximately 5% of COVID-19 infections caused acute respiratory distress syndrome (C-SDRA), increasing the demand for intensive care and mechanical ventilation, which increased the consumption of sedatives and analgesics. This study investigates the use of medicines such as propofol, midazolam, fentanyl, among others, in a COVID ICU of a third-level hospital in Bogotá, before and during the first year of the pandemic, using an analysis of time series interrupted, finding that, from the data obtained, it is not possible to establish an association between the change in the levels and trends of the consumption of the drugs studied and the onset of the pandemic . of the studied drugs and the onset of the pandemic
