# PHYSIOTHERAPEUTIC PRACTICE IN THE INTERACTION WITH CHILDREN WITH HIGH NEUROLOGY RISK: REFLECTIONS, CHALLENGES AND USEFUL TELESALUDE TOOLS.

The chapter seeks, through a reflective look, to discuss some elements in physiotherapeutic practice that can guide decision-making during interaction with populations of children at high neurological risk and their families. The impact of developmental alterations, early intervention actions (it) and the role of physiotherapist in early intervention is discussed as a reference framework for the analysis of aspects such as evaluation, work with the family, environment and the use of telehealth that, with the support of emerging elements from the experiential, are proposed as of interest for professional practice and pose perspectives for the growth of the body of knowledge in this area
