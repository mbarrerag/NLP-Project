# PHOTOPERIODISM, PACIFIC INSTRUMENTS OF COLLECTIVE MEMORY AND CULTURE IDENTITY OF THE PEOPLE AWA IN THE NARRIÑO DEPARTMENT4

In Colombia, the indigenous communities have forged processes of resilience and cultural survival through peaceful means of collective memory, among which documentary journalism stands out as being suitable for portraying such scenarios. This article will develop three specific points: the first will explain cultural identity as a fundamental and human right in Colombia based on the aesthetic theory of Gadamer; the second will address the development of photojournalism as a peace instrument of collective memory and its impact on the protection of the right to cultural identity; and the third will address the study of the photojournalism process advanced by photographer Juan Manuel Barrero, regarding the collective memory that recreates identity uses and customs that form part of the resistance and protection of the right to cultural identity of the Awá indigenous community in the department of Nariño. This article is developed under a historical paradigm of a qualitative approach to the history of photography. It was intended to understand documentary journalism through the observation and interpretation of the historical memory of the armed conflict in Colombia, from the experiences of the Awá indigenous reservation, for which documentary photography has served as a peacemaking instrument and collective evoking the relevant approach
