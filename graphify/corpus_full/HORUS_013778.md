# Economic-legal analysis of medical-health liability in the decisions of the Council of State and Supreme Court of Justice in Colombia

Research problem: It is unknown whether the parallel application of medical-health liability produces significant differences in the judgements issued by the Supreme Court of Justice and the Council of State, and whether this influences the behavior of the actors of the General System of Social Security in Health (GSSS). Furthermore, no research has been conducted on how the sociodemographic characteristics of those involved and the types of events affect the results of judicial processes. Nor has it been determined which medical services and specialties are most affected by the demands of health responsibility. General objective: To compare the sociodemographic characteristics of the parties involved, the legal institutions applied and the compensatory results of judicial processes in the administrative and civil jurisdiction in Colombia, in relation to medical-health liability. This project involves empirical research covering the period from 2017 to 2022. The primary data used are judgements issued by the Supreme Court of Justice and the Council of State. Statistical tools will be applied to carry out a descriptive study of the judgments identified and analyze the association between sociodemographic, legal and economic variables
