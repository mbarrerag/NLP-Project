# Financial Innovation in the Digital Age: Exploring the Technical-Legal Dimensions of Embedded Finance

In Colombia, financial inclusion is a key challenge, as much of the population does not have access to traditional financial services. This project will focus on analysing and understanding business models that integrate embedded financial elements and evaluate their effectiveness in the Colombian context, since the incorporation of Embedded Finance has the potential to address this problem by carrying financial services through non-traditional channels. Research will focus on analysing how legal regulation and technical considerations impact financial inclusion in Colombia. To do this, the determinants of financial inclusion in Colombia are established and then analyzed with the legal-technical characteristics of Embedded Finance. The findings of this research could provide valuable information for regulators, financial institutions and companies seeking to expand their presence in the embedded financial market in Colombia, and contribute to the promotion of financial inclusion in the country
