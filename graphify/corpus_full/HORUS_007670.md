# Tomato assisted pollination (Solanum lycopersicum L) under cover: Effect on curing, yield and quality of fruit.

The tomato (Solanum lycopersicum L) is the most consumed vegetable in Colombia, mainly due to the availability of the product throughout the year, and the inclusion in the usual diet of Colombians The crop develops in two productive systems, open air or open field, greenhouse or protected system under cover, highlighting a transition in the last years, from open field to protected crop, in order to control some biotic and abiotic factors and facilitate the management of the crop and guarantee high yields and quality. However, the plastic cover conditions affect the wind which is the main yield factor, and the entry of pollinating insects that can replace this function is limited.
