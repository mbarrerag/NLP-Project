# REFLECTIONS ON THE ROLE OF PUBLIC POLICY IN THE TERRITORIAL APPROACH FOR DEVELOPMENT IN COLOMBIA

Rurality and territories play a central role in the development of our countries, and recognizing their importance challenges the definition and implementation of public policies. It is usually emphasized that a desirable route for the formulation of public policies is to launch governance processes in which civil society and its organizations are protagonists in the orientation of the development of their territories. The approach presented here considers that, in order to move towards successful strategies of governance, it is necessary to strengthen governance by the State, so that both are strengthened among themselves. Therefore, aspects that should facilitate this articulation of mutual convenience are examined
