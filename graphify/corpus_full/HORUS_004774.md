# Assessment of the spatial time estimate of recharge using a hydrological model using a multi-objective calibration incorporating remote sensor information

The recharge is the amount of water that feeds the groundwater systems, systems that supply approximately two billion people. For their estimation there are a variety of techniques among which is the hydrological model. In this research was carried out the implementation of the hydrological model TETIS to estimate the recharge of groundwater in the basin of the Lebrija river located in the Eastern Cordillera of the Colombian Andes. This is a tropical basin with an annual average precipitation of 1675 mm, which is presented in a mixed regime, with peaks at mid semester. The basin has a strong change of elevations from 4200 to 28 msnm at the point of delimitation. In the implementation was used information of area recorded by stations of the IDEAM.
