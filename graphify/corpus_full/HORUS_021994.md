# A mathematical model of diabetes based on stochastic differential equations.

Diabetes is now known to be one of the diseases with the largest number of patients in the world and its prevalence is increasing. Among the complications that can cause we find that it is a major cause of blindness, amputation and renal failure; therefore, it is considered a high-cost disease and a public health problem. In order to understand insulin-glucose dynamics, a model of Stochastic Differential Equations is proposed from the compartment method and its transition probabilities, in which random disturbances are considered to represent the variation in the levels of insulin production secreted from the pancreas or an external source, food intake and other physiological factors.
