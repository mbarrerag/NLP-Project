# PRESENTATION OF INSURANCE DOCUMENTS IN THE TORNEY TO TRAINING RESEARCH

The construction of disciplinary knowledge through research is one of the priorities of academic scenarios, in which the teaching role from teaching and training research contributes to the scientific development of nursing. To know the perceptions of teachers of the nursing faculty on training research. A qualitative approach study of descriptive type with content analysis technique based on inductive categorization of type. 28 participants of higher education of the nursing faculty were included, the information was collected through a written questionnaire. Three categories emerged: 1. Training research as an educational training process, to solve phenomena in practice 2. Training research in the generation of new skills in students 3. Training research in the generation of scientific knowledge of all actors. Training research must be consolidated in teachers and strengthen disciplinary knowledge, it is appropriate to put into practice strategies that allow the teaching staff to align conceptions and strategies that implement in the classroom. Key words: Qualitative research; education; nursing; research in nursing education
