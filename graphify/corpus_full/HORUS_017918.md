# Third United Nations Mission, Strategies for De-ploying It by Using the

The promotion of education and the formation of entrepreneurial competencies in universities is related to the third university mission, this mission focuses on the transfer of knowledge and is related to the construct called the entrepreneurial university. In Colombia, from Law 1014 of 2006 entrepreneurship is promoted from an integral human development approach that seeks to expand opportunities and generate actions for the well-being of the being. In order for universities to carry out this mission they must have dynamic capacities such as organizational learning. The chapter evaluates the organizational learning model and the dimensions that determine it such as training, organizational support, strategic clarity, organizational culture, sources of learning and relationships. Likewise, it evaluates possible variables of impact in the development of the third institutional mission (transfer knowledge) and results in entrepreneurship. The main results show aspects of the state of art of the entrepreneurial university and organizational learning, the proposal of the model of the measurement indicated and a pioneering proposal is made in this regard. The findings of this research allow university managers to evaluate the effectiveness of strategies for the development of the third mission based on their abilities and entrepreneurship
