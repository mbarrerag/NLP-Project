# Estimate of repeatability in dry fibre production and foliar emission in fique accesses belonging to the Agrosavia work collection

At the global level, natural fibres are important to combat the problems of contamination by synthetic fibres. Thus, it is again relevant to enhance the cultivation of fique for the manufacture of packaging and other biodegradable products at national and international level. It is reported fiber deficit between 7,000 and 10,000 t, in Colombia, the fique is of importance in the agricultural sector, since it is used for the packaging of products such as coffee, corn, cocoa and potato. However, there is not enough technical information regarding the cultivation and the genus Furcraea. The Colombian Corporation of Agricultural Research-Agrosavia, has a collection of fique work and related species formed by 148 accessions, of which 68 accesses were selected to estimate the genetic potential by means of the coefficient of repeatability of the production and percentage of dry fiber. The results of repeatability were obtained values of 0,29 and 0,14, for the production and percentage of fiber, respectively.
