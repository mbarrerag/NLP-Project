# ECONOMIC ASSESSMENTS IN SAFETY AND HEALTH AT WORK: CONCEPTUAL AND METHODOLOGICAL APPROXIMATION

The economic evaluation provides information for decision makers in public policy, since it helps to answer questions such as: is it worth investing in a program or any type of initiative, if compared to another alternative? Moreover, when resources are scarce, it allows to prioritize, since without a systematic analysis it is difficult to clearly identify all possible options and consider the point of view from which the problem is addressed and if it takes relevance, because without measurements it is difficult to estimate the magnitude of the consequences of an action. Thus, the economic evaluation is the tool that facilitates the process for making decisions. In the field of health there is economic evaluation of health technologies (eths s), which analyses an intervention, through production, synthesis or review of a wide range of scientific evidence and, moreover, systematically examines the characteristics of a health technology, its safety, efficacy and clinical effectiveness, costs, organizational implications, social consequences and legal considerations of its application (2).
