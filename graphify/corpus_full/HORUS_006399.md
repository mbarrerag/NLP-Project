# Compact Applicator Antenna for Superficial Microwave Hyperthermia

We present a microwave applicator aimed at the hyperthermia therapy of skin cancer types with emphasis on melanoma. Our design consists of a modified folded dipole on a reflector for a uniform and focused footprint on the therapy area. It affords low cost, low weight, compact size, and ease of handling, as required for integration in complex applicator systems. A Simulation and experimental validation campaign on a human phantom model demonstrate good performance in terms of power footprint and power delivery to the tissue under therapy
