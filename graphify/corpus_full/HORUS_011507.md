# INCIDENTY OF INSPECTIONS ASSOCIATED WITH PROVISIONS IN UNITS OF ADULT AND PEDIATERIC INTENSIVE CARE. COLOMBIA, 2012-2021

to analyze the trend of incidence rates of Device-Associated Infections in Intensive Care Units Adult and Paediatric in Colombia for the period 2012-2021. descriptive observational study based on trend analysis. The data of Device-Associated Infections (IAD) were taken from the reports of events published annually by the National Public Health Surveillance System (SIVIGILA). Descriptive statistics and trend charts were performed, the joint-point regression analysis was used to analyze the behavior of incidence rates. the incidence rates of reported IADs present a fluctuating trend in the period 2012-2021 in both adult and paediatric ICUs. The rate of ITS AC in adult ICUs shows a statistically significant decrease from 2012 to 2019, however, significantly increases between 2019 and 2021. In the pediatric ICU, the rate of AVN is significantly decreased in the period 2014 to 2021, as well as the rate of ITS AC is statistically significant from 2012 to 2019, but nevertheless, it does increase significantly between 2019 and 2021.
