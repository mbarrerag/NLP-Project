# CROSS-COOLED AND CO-GLOMERANTS FOR CIRCULARITY IN CONSTRUCTION. POOTENTIAL USE OF RCDS AND ALTERNATIVE CEMENTS IN SUSTAINABLE CONSTRUCTION MATERIALS

The development of construction materials that incorporate waste and develop under low energy consumption processes and greenhouse gas emissions has become a means of transition towards circularity and sustainability of the construction industry. The low cups of use of construction and demolition waste (RCD) as the materials from excavation or demolition have been influenced by the few implementation alternatives currently used. This presentation shows the mechanical performance and laborability of soil-cement mixtures and mortars made with excavation soils, fine aggregate of recycled concrete and a low impact cement of limestone and limestone clay known as LC3 (Limestone Calcined Clay Cement), giving the influence of factors such as the amount of cement, water or recycled aggregate in the performance of the mixtures.The analysis is shown through the compression resistance to 7 and 28 days of mixtures of soil-cement compacted and mortars with different levels of substitution of fine aggregate recycled, absorption, and the demand of water of the system, in the liquid and soil-cement through the proctor. Finally, a thermal analysis is shown
