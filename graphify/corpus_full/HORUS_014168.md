# Digital School for Environmental Justice: Digital content for strengthening Peasant Reserve Zones as territorial governance that builds peace and environmental justice

This proposal seeks to create digital pedagogical material to support the realization of the Digital School of Environmental Justice focused on the strengthening of the Zones of Campesino Reserve (ZRC) in three municipalities of Cundinamarca that belong to the Sumapaz Region (Vereda Chisacá, Sumapaz; Vereda San Jorge, Soacha and the ZRC in Cabrera, Cundinamarca).The project, aims to be an experience of co-creation, taking advantage, first, of the knowledge, expertise and trajectory of the Environmental Corporation Walking the Territory, in terms of leadership and implementation of educational processes that promote environmental sustainability and social organization environment to care for the environment and the communities that inhabit it. Secondly, the project seeks to articulate the experience of creating technological solutions through concerted and agile methodologies of the Innovation, Creativity and New Technologies Laboratory, in terms of incorporation and new technologies, of the Faculty of Engineering of the National University of Colombia. This is why flexible material is proposed that can be accessed via the internet but also that can be used offline by hosting storage devices such as USB, portable disks or computers
