# Variations of the mestizo house. Residential architecture of Borrero, Zamorano & Giovanelli, Cali 1953-1963

Mestigenje en la arquitectura is a way of naming the mixture of recognizable architectural structures that, convened by social, programmatic, technological changes, among others, provoke new spatial and formal results that contribute to the adaptation of the projects to the conditions of the site, the period and the particular context. This procedure took place in the city of Cali, Colombia, during the middle of the twentieth century, when the approaches of the modernity and the traditional architecture of the Valle del Cauca were combined by Borrero, Zamorano and Giovanelli (BZG) in unpublished versions of the house-patio. The firm BZG exemplifies this practice in its unifamily residential work projected between 1953 and 1963, composed of about thirty houses built in Cali. Although its work is one of the most relevant elements of the Colombian south-western, there are few exhaustive studies on its projects, beyond some notable works. This research seeks to expand the knowledge of the Firm by means of comparative analysis of some of its works based on the critical review of the original material found in public and private archives.
