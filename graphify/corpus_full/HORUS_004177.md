# Potential environmental risk assessment generated in traffic accidents involving liquid hydrocarbon transport vehicles, using free software tools

In Colombia, the transport of dangerous chemicals is carried out using land-load vehicles, which distribute these substances throughout the country. Among the dangerous substances of higher volume transported are the liquid fuels such as gasoline and the ACPM, which are carried from the substations or pumping stations to the stations of service located throughout the national territory. The terrestrial transport of these substances carries an inherent danger and risk, both for the transporters and for the communities and the surrounding ecosystem to the road corridors through which they transit these substances. In order to evaluate the possible risk and environmental impact that would result from an incident involving the spill of dangerous chemicals, in particular liquid hydrocarbons, a simulation study was carried out using free software tools. Initially, an analysis was made to identify the hydrocarbons that represent the highest risk, as well as the road corridors of higher accidentity. Subsequently, the selection of the free software tool was carried out to implement risk studies in the transport sector. national territory.
