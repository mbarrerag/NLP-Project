# Phase diagrams J1(r) and J1(?) of a superconducting film

In this contribution we use the Ginzburg-Landau theory dependent on time in the presence of currents a null magnetic field to investigate the dynamics of kinematic vortices in a superconducting sheet mesoscopic with a thin central pillar. Our study covers two cases: (a) a central pillar to which we vary its height, simulated by the parameter ¿ > 1;0, with a superconductor-vacuum interface in the entire sample, simulated with the parameter ¿ = 1;0; (b) a central pillar with a superconducting-superconducting boundary condition at greater critical temperature ?c, (? > 1;0); we also consider a homogeneous sample, i.e. without pillar ?= 1;0.
