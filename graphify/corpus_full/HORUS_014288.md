# Constructible sets in lattice-valued models.

We researched different constructs of the theory of sets in Residual Logic based on Fitting's work on the Krypke intuitionist models of the Theory of Sets. First, we consider constructible sets within valued models of the Theory of Sets. We present two different constructs of the constructible universe: L B and L B, and we demonstrate that they are isomorphous to V (universe von Neumann) and L (universe constructible of Gödel), respectively. Second, we generalize Fitting's work on the Krypke intuitionist models of the set theory using the residuated models of Krypke of Ono and Komori. Based on these models, we provide a generalization of the von Neumann hierarchy in the context of the Residued Modal Logic and demonstrate a translation of formulas between it and an appropriate valued Heyting model. We also propose a notion of universe of construtable sets in Residual Modal Logic and discuss aspects of the source. (Text from the original version)
