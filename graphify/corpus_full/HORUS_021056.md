# Community music therapy pilot project for the empowerment of the Asoquimbo community coordinators affected by the El Quimbo dam.

The socio-environmental conflicts, specifically the construction of dams in Colombia, have been studied over the last decade, mainly from the environmental, social and economic perspective. However, the impacts and/or damages generated by the displacement by development, from these, deserve a broader understanding and even a process of intervention from the cultural, psychosocial, affective and ruptured perspective of the social fabric of the communities, which allows to restore the community and organizational relations in the territory. In this context, the project aims to advance a research process and therapeutic intervention, which allows to validate the contribution of community music therapy to the empowerment of the organized community and in particular, as a pilot experience to the affected coordinators belonging to Asoquimbo, a peasant organization that carries out a process of territorial defense against the dam
