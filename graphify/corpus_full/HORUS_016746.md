# Identification of small molecules with potential use as pharmacological chaperones for the human enzyme N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulphatase (GALNS).

The mucopolysaccharidosis IV A (MPS IVA) is an autosomal recessive disease caused by the deficiency of the enzyme N-acetylgalactosamine-6-sulfate sulfatase (GALNS).The majority of mutations affecting this enzyme are of a wrong sense, affecting its fold and intracellular traffic.In this sense, the use of pharmacological chaperones (CFs) has been proposed as an alternative treatment.The CFs have the ability to improve the folding, avoid degradation and favor intracellular traffic of the mutated protein, recovering their biological function.Although these molecules have been evaluated in other diseases of lysomal deposit, their study in the MPS IVA is found in initial stages.With the aim of identifying new molecules that can be used as CFs, this study initially performed a computational analysis of the enzyme GALNS with natural and artificial substrates. All natural substrates were located at the bottom of the active cavity with the sulphate group intertating with the residuous catalytic (formaltyc) alternative for the MPS VAT
