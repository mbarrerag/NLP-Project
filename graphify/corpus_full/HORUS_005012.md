# POINTS FOR LIFE: INCENTIVES FOR THE USE OF ACTIVE AND MASITIVE TRANSPORT.

The main objective is to capture and understand the modes of transport employed by users of Manizales city, taking into account the age groups and socioeconomic strata, in each of the Transport Analysis Zones (ZAT), which will provide an initial vision of the community perception of sustainable mobility and the possible incentives associated with it. This study follows an approach that starts with a collection of information from existing databases such as the target source survey and the census carried out by the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE), culminating in the location of the sectors that are intended to be analysed in the next phase of the project that aims to find possible companies that could participate in the project. At the same time, a detailed analysis of the relevant factors will be carried out. In short, what is sought with this research is to discover the ZATs that generate and attract the highest percentage of private vehicle trips in order to guide the areas to intervene in the project as Palogrande commune
