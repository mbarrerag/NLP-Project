# EFFECT OF A COMBINED PROGRAMME OF PHYSICAL EXERCISE AND MANUAL THERAPY ON THE FLESH AND FLEXIBILITY OF THE ARTICLE-FLEXIBILITY OF MEN IN DEPORTISTS OF PARA-ATLETISM OF CAMPO. CASE STUDY

Throughout history, sport has been considered in different ways, from a simple practice to a tool of construction and change that within the disability processes has allowed to glimpse a broader perspective of disability, since it has developed as a strong area of integration and inclusion, adaptation and competition, emerging and recognising as a paralympic sport at national and international level, with specific and specialized practice connotations dedicated to the improvement and competence of a sports discipline Objective: To determine the validity of the content of a protocol of the physical condition of paralympic athletes practicing chair athletics in wheelchair, constructed from the protocol proposed by Parra in 2017, and the review in literature. For this purpose a validation was carried out through expert committee, under the approach of the Delphi method, and the Likert scale for its qualification, the statistical methods of kappa de Fleiss, W of kendall and Kappa de cohen was applied by the protocol of cohen by pair of evaluators, in addition to a descriptive analysis of each Flegenes related to the Flegens method, and the degree of the results of the results of 19 tests were selected to conform to the protocol. Control of the speed of movement or the position of the joint mentioned by Troy, Munce and Longworth, in 2014. Another test with considerable amount of grade at variance in the three items is the flow of flexibility and the flexi-test test. One of the limitations of this study was a number of low expert evaluators, which due to their different areas of expertise increased the heterogeneity of the evaluation, therefore, it is recommended to continue with the phase of the study where there is a greater number of experts that allows to perform a sub-grouping and in this way to perform a more detailed analysis, in addition to a possible piloting Implications: It allows the analysis, evaluation and adaptation of a method of evaluation consistent to the white population. Key words: Paralympic sport, athletics, athletics in wheelchair, physical condition
