# Evaluation of the NUCLEAR ITS2 REGION POTENTIAL FOR DIFFERENCE OF GENDER SPECIES FANNIA ROBINEAU-DESVOIDY, 1830 (DIPTERA: FANNIIDAE)

Fannia Robineau-Desvoidy, 1830 is the most representative genus of the family Fanniidae (Diptera: Calyptratae), with a wide geographical distribution. Given their preference for decomposition and excrement matter, they have a medical-health, veterinary and forensic importance.The identification of species of the genus has been based on external morphological characters of the males, which requires a technical expertise for their correct identification, while for females and immature states there are few or nonexistent morphological descriptions. Thus, molecular identification represents an opportunity to facilitate the identification of species and their conspecific.The identification based on DNA sequences, specifically the nuclear fragment internal spacer transcribed 2 (ITS2) has demonstrated its usefulness for the identification of some dipteros association. Thus, this study evaluated the potential of the ITS2 nuclear region as a complementary tool for the differentiation of ten species of the genus Fannia.
