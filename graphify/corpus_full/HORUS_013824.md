# Youth and Social Outbreak in Colombia: A Comparison of Youth Social Mobilization during the Social Outbreak in Medellín and Cali (2021)

Latin America witnessed several social mobilizations where the young people were protagonists from 2019 to 2022. In Colombia a protest was called on April 28, 2021 and the days extended until July 2021. These mobilizations attracted attention for the intensity, duration and quantity of people involved and are known as the _x0093_social impasse_x0094_. In the _x0093_social impasse_x0094_ youth groups participated.The youth is a heterogeneous sector and their participation in the _x0093_social instability_x0094_ of 2021 was through various organizational forms such as the traditional student movement, and other social sectors such as cultural organizations, football fans and _x0093_first lines_x0094_, among others.These protests developed throughout the country and wide participation and, above all, in the main cities such as Bogotá, Medellín and Cali.Although the epicentre of the mobilizations was Cali and in this city a political and social crisis was generated, in other cities like Medellín there was also a significant context of youth participation during the months of the __. The techniques of data collection are, first, the documentary review of the press coverage of traditional newspapers and community and alternative media that covered the mobilizations during the study period in the two cities; second, semi-structured interviews of young people from the student movement, popular sectors, cultural organizations, football fans and front lines in the two cities. The different techniques of data collection will allow for a triangulation of the information found to make a content analysis and identify the patterns and themes recurrent in the documents and interviews. The results will be presented, first, through a product of generation of new knowledge with the subjection of an academic article to an indexed journal; and, second, through a pedagogical experience with a proposal of subject that addresses in theoretical, methodological and empirical terms the study of the participation of young people in social movements
