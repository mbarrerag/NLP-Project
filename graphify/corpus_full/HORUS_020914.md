# --PLEBIO 2022-2023 working plan

Project 1. --analysis of content in community biocultural protocols. This project is within the lines of research of the PLEBIO group (Traditional knowledge protection systems) since the community biocultural protocols are tools used by indigenous, Afro, root and palenquer communities for the proper recognition of their ancestral knowledge, customs and way of life. Protocols arise on the initiative of communities as a means of protection against potential threats, especially those related to the misuse of their traditional knowledge and possible impacts that have projects of any kind that are to be developed on the territory that the communities inhabit (Shrumm & Jonas., 2012; Bavikatte et al., 2015). Several communities have adopted biocultural community protocols in the context of access to traditional knowledge and genetic resources and distribution of benefits, but the scope of protocols differs in legal and political contexts.
