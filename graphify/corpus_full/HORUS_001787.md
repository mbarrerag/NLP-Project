# Symphonic program and music conservatory of the Vermont Gymnasium School in Bogotá. Systematization of the experience

This research focuses on the process of creation, structuring and development of the Symphonic Program and the Conservatoire de Musique du Lycée Gimnasio Vermont de Bogotá. It approaches the experience of its educational community from a qualitative approach that gathers the voices of the protagonists of the transformation of the program, from a post-school activity to a complete symphonic proposal, articulated in the educational project of the institution. The study was carried out using information-gathering tools such as interviews, discussion groups and analysis of various documentary sources. This allowed to reconstruct a path that shows how music becomes a central axis which powerfully influences the formation of sensitive, creative and engaged human beings in their environment. This study contributes academically to the master's degree in the Symphonic Directorate of the National University of Colombia, proposing a rigorous, relevant and well articulated research of various documentary sources.
