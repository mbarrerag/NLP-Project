# Predictors of intestinal anastomosis failure in children: analysis of a retrospective cohort

The failure of an anastomosis intestinal data is an event of high morbidity in the pediatric population. Despite this, few studies in children aimed at evaluating potential risk factors for this outcome. An analytical observational study was carried out as a cohort retrospective in patients undergoing intestinal anastomosis between January 2017 and July 2023 in the HOMI Foundation (Bogotá, Colombia). Demographic data were described and possible risk factors for anastomosis failure were analyzed through a logistic regression model. 449 anastomosis was identified in 404 patients. The population was characterized by a high incidence of acute pathology of high complexity (41.87% ASA greater than III, 44.77% postoperative surveillance in ICU). In the multivariate analysis, the only variable that was associated with the greatest risk of anastomosis failure was the presence of laparostomy (OR 7.86, IC95% 2.099 to 29.427, p=0.002). A total of 449 anastomoses in 404 patients were identified.
