# ADVANCES IN PARTIAL DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS IN A PROBLEM INVOLVING PHI-LAPLACIAN OPERATOR

We prove an existence result of positive radial solution for the problem \Delta_\phi (u)=\lambda f(u) in a ball with homogeneous Dirichlet condition. In order to prove that, we use a shoothing method and our main tool is the use of Pohozaev Identity
