# DIRECT STRATEGY FOR DEVELOPING INDIAGATION COMPETENCE IN GRADE 11 STUDENTS FROM THE BIOPROSPECT CONCEPT

According to the results of the standard test SABER 11 of 2020 applied by the ICFES and the data of the first simulation of 2021 carried out by the company Milton Ochoa to the students of the eleventh grade of the Anglo American College, there is a low level of performance of the students in the competition inquiry. With the objective of improving this competence in the students of the eleventh grade of the school, in this work a didactic strategy related to the concept of Bioprospecting of natural plant products was designed, implemented and evaluated.In the design of this strategy three activities were proposed related to: To consult with the students about their strategies of search for information, socializing with them a series of tools that can support the process for the search for this one and carrying out the search for the information. In the second phase of implementation of the didactic strategy was carried out the selection of the natural vegetable product, the approach of a problem and the space to answer this question (taking into account the selected product). Finally, the didactic strategy was evaluated from the verbal support of the work carried out by the students and the score obtained by the institution in the presentation of the test SAFES was obtained in the IC year 11
