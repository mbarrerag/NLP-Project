# Climate Emergency in Medellín Commune 8: Case Study from Urban Political Ecology

In the context of the intensification of the effects of the climate crisis, cities appear as scenarios of vital importance both for the mitigation of Greenhouse Gases (GEI) and for the adaptation measures to climate change. However, these policies associated with climate change are mediated by a latent conflict between different actors and interests. The case of Medellin, especially in the upper part of Commune 8, in the last year is a notable expression of this conflict from the process of declaring the climate emergency to address the vulnerability and risk of communities settled on the slopes and affected by rainfall more and more intense. This grade work then seeks to approach the process of this declaration from the theoretical approach of Urban Political Ecology (UPR). The question that guides this work is: How has the process of declaring the climate emergency in Medellín been developed from the actors, interests, negotiations and confrontations in the upper part of the Commune 8? and to solve it will be based on a model of qualitative research through the case study and the documentary analysis.
