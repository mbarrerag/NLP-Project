# ANALYSIS OF PRONOASTICS IN A MODEL APC WITH BAJESIAN APPROACH FOR TUBERCLESITY IN COLOMBIA FOLLOWS THE AGREGATION OF THE PERIOD

Age-period-cohort models, also known as APC, are popular in epidemiology, as they allow to study mortality or incidence of diseases in order to see the effect of any of these three factors. These types of models face the problem of identification, which can easily be solved from a Bayesian perspective. For the work with this type of models, age groups are usually grouped into quinquenniums and periods in quinquenniums or annuities, and in relation to this latter factor, studies related to their aggregation have not been found in literature and how this may affect or affect the adjustments and forecasts made with this model. Thus, three APC models with Bayesian focus on tuberculosis mortality in Colombia are adjusted, each with three additions of different periods: annuities, trienniums and quinquenios to study the effect of these additions in the predictions that each model makes
