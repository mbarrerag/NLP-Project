# Space-time dynamics of the coverage of Tropical Dry Forest in the department of Valle del Cauca

The intensification of agricultural activity has been one of the main factors of degradation of the dry tropical forest (BsT) worldwide. In the Valle del Cauca there are few relicts with characteristics specific to a dry forest, therefore it was proposed to recognize the distribution area and the changes of coverage of the BsT between 1984 and 2014 in flat area of the department, reviewing definitions given by different authors, and using cartographic and satellite information. As results, it was observed that 80% of the fragments of BsT are outside the study area in mountain ecosystems, the maximum height was 1695 msnm to the suroccident of the study area and only 0.2% of the BsT is protected. Between 1984 and 2014, the natural areas were reduced by about 50%, showing the degradation process caused by the increase of the main factor of Dry Forest (TDF) and influence of the ecological restoration and monitoring of the BsT. . The intensification of agriculture has been one of the tropical area of the DDF. areas represent an opportunity to initiate processes of ecological restoration and monitoring of vegetation cover of TDF
