# PROPOSALS SUBMITTED: INNOVATION AND INVESTIGATION 2021. INNOVATION MODALITY

For this purpose, the Department of Education of the District (SED) and the Institute for Educational Research and Educational Development (IDEP) make available to the public the summaries of the proposals presented and enabled in the two categories: Innovation and Research. 87 proposals were awarded for the fifteenth edition of the Prize, 60 of which belong to the category of Innovation and 27 to the category of Research
