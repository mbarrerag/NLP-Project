# Search for compounds with possible inhibitory activity of enzymes of cosmetic interest from algae of the Colombian Caribbean.

The proliferation of marine algae in the reefs of the Colombian Caribbean has caused a loss in the biodiversity of these ecosystems. In order to make use of this biomass, this document investigated its potential in the production of cosmetics depigmentantes and anti-aging. This study is composed of five chapters that cover aspects such as bibliographic review, extraction of chemical compounds, analysis by means of magnetic nuclear resonance (RMN) and mass spectrometry (EM), evaluation of the enzymatic inhibitory activity, as well as a detailed chemical study of one of the most promising algae. The bibliographic review focuses on the background of the cosmetic industry in Colombia and the advances in anti-aging formulations. In addition, it deepens the characteristics and potential of marine algae as cosmetic ingredients. In the second chapter, the chemical assessment of different methodologies of extraction of brown and red algae is explored, and the chemical profile of selected samples using RMN was carried out. The third chapter focuses on the analysis of extracts using mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) with the aim of obtaining a detailed vision of the mobile and redweedness. The results highlight the extract of Dictyopteris justii as a promising candidate and highlight the need for further research in this field. .
