# Establishment of a protocol for total body irradiation with the technique of volumetric arcotherapy of modulated intensity.

In this work a TBI/VMAT protocol was established that required the previous construction of an anatomical simulator to characterize some of the stages of treatment, such as: 1) immobilization of the patient in a stretcher constructed for TBI/VMAT with the ability to rotate freely around its central axis, 2) obtaining diagnostic images of the patient's complete body by merging partial images, 3) planning of the radiation beams using multiple isocenters for the calculation of the dose absorbed by the patient's organs and tissues and, 4) checking the treatment before and during its application, using detector arrangements. In the protocol of TBI/VMAT developed in this work the required elements, such as a special stretcher and immobilization accessories, are established to implement the treatment in any radiation therapy center that has the clinical linear accelerator with the output of 6 V photons. It was determined that although the computed tomography team can make an acquisition of up to 140 cm without pause, when the height of a patient exceeds the maximum range of permitted irradiation without having to modify its position in the body, in our case 110 V. The verification of the planned treatment execution with the treatment delivered using measurements in semiconductor detector arrangements. By establishing as a criterion a maximum difference of 3\% in dose and 3 mm in position, it was obtained that only 2\% of the values are outside this range. In conclusion, this work allowed obtaining through an anatomical simulator a protocol for the first time implementation in Colombia of TBI/VMAT, which will serve as the basis for the future implementation of this technique in patients
