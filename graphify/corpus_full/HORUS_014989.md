# Traditional home gardens of Chocó, agroecological alternative for conservation of Agrobiodiversity, food sovereignty in the middle area of San Juan.

In the middle area of San Juan, the research work on traditional home gardens of Chocó was carried out. Agroecological alternative for the conservation of agrobiodiversity, and food sovereignty. In which it proposed among its main objectives to evaluate with agroecological criteria the structure, function and diversity of traditional home gardens and their contribution to local food sovereignty; and to evaluate the physicochemical properties of soils intervened by mining and their impact on food sovereignty. This research was carried out in phases: First stage documentation, was carried out under indirect observation, included an extensive bibliographic review and secondary information collection, and the study area (36 orchards) was selected. The second phase consisted of socialization to the community, through the local ethnic-territorial authorities, to obtain permits on the execution of the project. All the study was developed through the methodology of Participative Action Research (IAP). The third phase.
