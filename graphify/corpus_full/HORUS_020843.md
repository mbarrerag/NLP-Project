# STRENGTHENING ASSOCIATIVE PROCESS AND PRODUCTIVES OF SMALL AGRO-PECUARY PRODUCERS BY SOCIAL MANAGEMENT PRACTICES IN THE YOTOC MUNICIPLE.

The Project Strengthening of the associative and productive processes of small agricultural producers through social management practices in the municipality of Yotoco, through the identification of social practices, traditional knowledge, implementation of agroecological practices and the generation of sustainable productive alternatives, has its origin in the community interest to improve the management and development of good productive practices in the search for alternatives that strengthen the production systems and favor the exercises of articulation based on the transformation and design of products and services of high impact in the region. Therefore, it has been defined as general objective of the project to establish a model of production and transformation of goods and experimental services, through a participatory exercise and co-creation articulating different population groups and communities of the region of influence of the Municipality of Yotoco, generating sustainable productive alternatives that allow to improve the social, productive and commercial capacity. It has been considered for the achievement of this objective the participatory action methodology, rain of ideas, concept map, social mapping, DOFA matrix and some tools such as participatory planning, cost analysis, among others, in order to establish the region and criteria of priority of actions to optimize production, to improve the practices, improve the practices. The project is designed to promote the social integration of the community and to allow the continued development of sustainable alternative practices that are expected to result from the project
