# Challenges for the Reincorporation of the FARC-EP Ex-Combatants. Analysis Based on the Results of the City Census

Summary The Peace Agreements between the FARC-EP guerrilla ex-combatants and the Colombian Government promoted an innovative reintegration approach that challenged reintegration as a traditional perspective of the Disarmament, Demobilization and Reintegration (DDR) process. However, six years after the implementation of these agreements, various challenges have hindered an adequate reintegration process. Based on the results of the district census of signatories of the Peace Agreement residing in the city of Bogotá, interviews and four focus groups, the article analyzes the challenges that the signatories of the agreement have had to carry out a process that guarantees their adequate reintegration
