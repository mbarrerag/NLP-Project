# A NEW HIBRIDE IN PARABLECHNUM (BLECHNACEAE)

A new hybrid is described in the genus Parablechnum distributed to the south of the Andes of Colombia.It is discussed its morphological relationship with the parents and with other similar species. This is the first hybrid of Parablechnum recorded in the neotropical region
