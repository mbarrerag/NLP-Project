# CHAPTER 9 ARHUACAS WOMEN WEAVE AND CARE FOR THE ¿HEART OF THE WORLD¿

This book is part of the debate agenda on the topic of crafts, their nature, their forms of expression and the different challenges faced by ¿masters¿ holders of knowledge, techniques and practices inherent to their activities.Far from being considered an anachronistic situation, the proposed debate shows a movement towards valuing professional knowledge and identifies some of the strategies of resistance in relation to the production processes that result from the new configuration of the world of work.In an almost underlying way, the rescue of the knowledge present in the craft, at the same time that it appears as a movement towards the (re)appropriation of the good produced, denies the emptying of this knowledge and considers the indissoluble articulation between thinking and doing, the basis of crafts. The questions proposed in the different chapters are part of the contemporary socio-productive scenario.Marked by different dilemmas and challenges arising from ongoing transformations, this situation has produced changes in our way of life, many of them arising from profound changes in the world of work, both in nature and in the type of activity carried out.The changes that were already being implemented with the informational revolution, based on the contributions of microelectronics and information technology to the advancement of new technologies, deepened and were reinforced with the ¿news¿ conceived and conducted by central capitalist countries and global corporations that, with the ¿primary objective of valuing capital'1, intensify the use of digital technologies in the world of production and are at the basis of the so-called 4th Industrial Revolution
