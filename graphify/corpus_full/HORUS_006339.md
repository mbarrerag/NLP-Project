# Theoretical study of electronic transport through nanotapes

In this grade study, the probability of transmission through the nano-tapes of graphene of the type of chair and zig-zag is studied by varying its dimensions and reducing the system to a single degree of freedom by means of the decision process. Green functions and the Dyson equation are used to understand the movement of the electron in these structures. The practical implementation of the calculations of the probability of transmission, carried out in the Python programming language, reveals that the nano-tapes of graphene of the type of chair present a greater probability of transmission in their borders compared to the nano-tapes of the type zig-zag. These results contribute to the understanding of the transmission properties in nano-tapes of graphene with potential applications in nano-structured devices. .
