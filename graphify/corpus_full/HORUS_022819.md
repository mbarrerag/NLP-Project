# Development of a multicomponent wavefunction-in-DFT embedding methodology.

This thesis presents theoretical development, computational implementation and numerical applications of multicomponent methodologies that describe positron-molecule interactions.Theoretical developments combine wave function methodologies, such as the propagator theory for molecular orbitals of any particle, with procedures of the functional density theory (DFT) for electrons and positrons.The embedded scheme using projection operators of Manby et al. [J. Chem. Theory Comput., 8, 2564 (2012)] has extended to a multicomponental formulation.The extended wave function scheme embedded in DFT reduces the computational cost of positron bonding energies obtained with the third order propagator theory in the full base set limit without affecting its quality.The stability of new states linked to positrons with alcoxide and carboxilate anions is predicted with the extended embedded scheme. This thesis also contains the development of a new positron-electron correlation functional inspired by the electron correlation formulation of Colle-Salvetti.
