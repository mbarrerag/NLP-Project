# EVALUATION OF THE TRANSPORT OF TOXIC SUBSTANCES BY MINERIA IN MICROCUENCA WATER CLAR, MARMATO-CALDAS

The basic objectives of the modelling of a pollutant require explaining how it has reached a certain place and predicting how fast it moves through a compartment in the environment. Therefore, one can infer what changes this pollutant had in terms of its concentration and how toxic this concentration is at the destination place. The objective is to evaluate the spatial and temporal dynamics of heavy metals (Hg, Cr, Pb, Ni, Cd and Zn) and total cyanide generated by gold mining in the water basin Aguas Claras (Marmato, Caldas-Colombia) by developing and implementing a distributed pollution model
