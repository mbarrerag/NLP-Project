# Statistical-actual analysis of historical information of the regime except for the Colombian Military Forces

This grade work presents a statistical-actual analysis of the exceptive pension system of the Colombian Military Forces, which operates outside the General Pension System and is financed exclusively from State resources. Through a normative, demographic and financial perspective, the main components defining this regime are examined: retirement allowance, disability pension and survivors’ pension. The study systematizes the legal evolution from the 1991 Constitution to the current regulations, and analyses the role of the managing entities, such as the Military Forces Retirement Fund (CREMIL). Based on administrative data from 2008 to 2023, a descriptive analysis of the active and retired population is carried out, identifying trends in the number of beneficiaries and in the growth of the payroll. Subsequently, an actuarial projection of the pension liability for each type of provision is made under differentiated macroeconomic scenarios. The results show that the retirement allocation represents the largest proportion of the total liability, and that the regime presents significant challenges in terms of fiscal sustainability.
