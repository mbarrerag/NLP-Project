# Characterization of innovation in health and safety consulting companies at work in the city of Bogotá.

Companies that seek to be differentiated and increase their profitability tend to implement innovative products and services. Consultants are not the exception. However, there is a lack of knowledge about how innovation is developed for organizations that provide consultancy services, in a general and particular way, for the Colombian case in the consulting companies of safety and health at work. Due to the above, the present final master's degree work aims to characterize innovation in health and safety consultants at work located in the city of Bogotá, through the evaluation of the dimensions of intellectual capital and absorption capacity, being possible to identify how the consultants carry out their innovation and that could be improved, for which recommendations are put forward. To this end, a methodology was used based on a descriptive type study through a non-experimental design, with a mixed research strategy. Finally, the results obtained highlight the creation of spaces for consultants to exchange informal knowledge, the use of technologies that allow to collect the knowledge generated in each service and the creation of a record of excellence that allows to control the innovation activity and its results
