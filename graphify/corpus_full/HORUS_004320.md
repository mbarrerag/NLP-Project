# RESISTING, ILLICIT RELATIONS IN THE NEOGRANADIN CARIBBEAN TO THE FINAL OF THE XVIIIth AND XIXTH INICIES.

In the late 18th century and early 19th century, this paper seeks to show that the affections and their erotic expressions in that period constituted a way of resisting the colonization of the bodies, which were embedded in a single heteronormative Christian model of relationation. It will be done to the women with an intersectional look at the different strategies of resistance according to the quality, gender and ethnicity of the characters at a time when the Borbonic reforms and especially the Royal Pragmatic on Marriages of Desigual Linaje of 1776, prohibited the free expression of affections between women and men of the various states
