# Concretely prefabricated panels reinforced for a Social Interest Housing project (VIS) in Tocancipá, as an alternative to industrialised systems melted on site. Case study

This grade work is erected as a proposal from the fields of civil engineering and construction to make an analysis from the factors of planning of the work, constructive process and cost (direct and general) of the system of prefabricated panels in concrete reinforced as an alternative to the industrial systems of concrete in place. The interest of this research is to establish a necessary starting point to propose alternatives in the way in which it is constructed in the country; this with the objective of being able to generate urban development of housing of social interest (VIS), implemented on a large scale and of the lowest possible cost for the Colombian sector most vulnerable and that does not have access to the decent housing, in such a way that it serves as a solution to the crisis of habitability that has the country (DANE, 2021). To develop this research, it was based on a case study, where the results of direct and general costs, planning of work and constructive process of two units of analysis were compared to the project of industrialized in place (DANE, 2021). To develop this system, it is based on a prefabricated panels. To conclude the study of case, we found a construction and construction of two units: a project, a project of VIS designed as a ary start point to propose alternatives in the way it is built in the country; with the objective of being able to generate urbanistic developments of multifamily public housing (VIS housing), implemented on a large scale and at the low possible cost for the most vulnerable Colombian sector that does not have access to decent housing, in such a way that it services as a solution to the sustainability crisis that the country has (DANE, 2021).
