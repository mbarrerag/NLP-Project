# Perceptions of undergraduate medical students from the National University of Colombia on the change from face-to-face to virtual in anatomy and histology classes during the pandemic by SARS cov-2.

The present document aimed to understand the perceptions of undergraduate medical students about the change to virtual modality in the anatomy and histology classes of the National University of Colombia. It is important to establish that the pandemic by SARS-COV2 laid in the national and international environment an extraordinary health situation where government containment and prevention measures include social isolation, and consequently the interruption of classes. The study methodology was based on the mixed approach of research through the development of a descriptive exploratory cohort study, since the instruments implemented used the survey format initially to 6 students as a pilot test, and subsequently applied to 88 undergraduate medical students of the National University of Colombia of seventh semester. The results indicated that virtual education appears as an immediate response to the presented situation, because they indicated the possibility that students would continue the training process from their homes.
