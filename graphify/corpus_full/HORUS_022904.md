# AGRICULTURE IN THE CITY QUESTIONS AND RESPONSES ON URBAN AND AGRO-ECHOLOGICAL AGRICULTURE AND THE MARKETING OF THEIR PRODUCTS

Agriculture in the city. Questions and answers on urban and peri-urban agroecological agriculture and the commercialization of its productsIs it a publication aimed at agroecological producers in the city of Bogota? Its content is based on the knowledge and experience generated in the framework of the project "Urban and peri-urban agriculture [agroecological] with a differential focus on communities in Bosa, Ciudad Bolívar and Cerros Orientales de Bogotá." In five chapters, the theory and practice of urban and peri-urban agroecological production, its costs and the commercialization of its products are presented step by step. The document ends with a glossary aimed at providing greater clarity of the terminology of production and marketing in the AUPA
