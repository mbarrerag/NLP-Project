# Effect of biocarbon amendments on physical, chemical and Fitoabsorption properties of Cadmium in dissimilar soils sown with lettuce.

The biocarbon is currently a material of interest, rich in carbon that has potential properties for use as soil amendment and remediation of pollution with heavy metals. It is obtained by pyrolysis (thermal decomposition in absence of oxygen) from biomass residues. This research used biocarbons produced with palm rachis (BRP), palm cousk (BCP), tree pruning (BP), coffee pulp (BC) and rose stems (BTR), with the aim of characterizing them, evaluating them as amendments on the growth of lettuce (Lactuca sativa) and determining their effect on the physical-chemical properties of disimile soils (Ferralsol, Umbrisol, Andosoles and Tecnosol) contaminated with cadmium.
