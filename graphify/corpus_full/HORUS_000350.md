# Technological characterization of power generation and distribution in Colombia at the beginning of the 20th century: Manizales case

This article shows a characterization and description of the technical development that residential and industrial electrical installations had in Colombia at the beginning of the 20th century. The analysis focuses on the city of Manizales – Caldas given its role in the region by the coffee trade and because it constituted a point of commercial relationship within the country. Based on the analysis of historical documents, it was possible to reconstruct the context in which the installation and expansion of generation plants and transmission and distribution networks was developed, responding to the energy demand imposed by the commercial growth of the region. Among these elements, it shows the evolution of the technology of the facilities, as well as aspects of safety and maintenance that led to the consolidation, at the end of the same century, of the proposal and approval of laws that combined the experience of foreign professionals and the adaptation of knowledge to the local environment
