# Identification of Anopheles spp. (Diptera:Culicidae) present in endemic malaria transmission areas in Turbo, Antioquia and its infection with Plasmodium spp.

Abstract Malaria is a serious public health problem in Colombia, with Plasmodium falciparum and P. vivax as the parasites responsible for 97% of the cases. In 2019 the department of Antioquia presented 7076 cases of malaria, being the municipality of Turbo one of the most affected.
