# Professional and work situation of the Physiotherapist in high performance sport in Colombia.

The development and growth of physiotherapy at the global and national level has involved this profession to enter into less clinical and traditional areas, to which historically it has been linked, among them the sports area. The above is justified, among other aspects, by the increasing demand in these areas of professionals of the highly specialized movement. This rapid growth has led professionals to specialize and continue their training processes in a constant way and in new areas of professional exercise. In this context, the physiotherapy as a liberal and autonomous profession, which carries out its processes of self-regulation, has the constant need to know the professional and labor situation of its union in order to improve constantly. In Colombia, professional monitoring studies have been carried out in a general way for physiotherapists and in areas that are not the specific field of high performance sport, as for example in the area of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation. This means that there is a high degree of knowledge of the professional and work situation in the field of these highly specialized professionals.
