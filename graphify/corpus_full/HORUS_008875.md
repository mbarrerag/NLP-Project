# SOCIO-ECOLOGIC RELIANCE OF AGROECOSE UNDER ENVIRONMENTAL CHANGE SCENARIES

In the face of the processes of environmental change faced by agroecosystems, having indicators that facilitate the understanding of the resilience of agroecosystems through variables and guiding attributes is of great importance since it facilitates the decision-making oriented to strengthen resilience in the territories against scenarios of environmental and/or economic change. The index of resilience of agroecosystems against disturbances depends on the configuration of the variables that determine it.Knowing the state of resilience and understanding through the analysis of possible scenarios the response of the initial state of the variables against different disturbances contributes to the formulation of strategies of strengthening and prioritization of efforts to improve the states of resilience of agroecosystems in the territories.Based on the proposal of Suarez-Pardo et al. (2022) from an index of resilience in agroecosystems (ARI, by its acronym in English), this work analyzes the resilience of agroecosystems in different scenarios of environmental change disturbances (more specifically climate change and economic change).The scenarios are configured as the associated cases that reflect the magnitude of the disturbance under analysis of the new scenario
