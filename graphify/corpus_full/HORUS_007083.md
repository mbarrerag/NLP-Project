# Design of a functional baked product including flour and Sacha Inchi oil (Plukenetia volubilis)

The Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia volupilis) is an oil seed of great nutritional value. It extracts an oil rich in Omega-3 and obtains a pressed cake rich in protein and minerals, which can find a potential application in bakery and pastry products as cakes. In this study the effects of the substitution of wheat flour and vegetable fat, by flour and Sacha Inchi oil, respectively, on the parameters of quality of cakes were investigated. The properties of the smoothing of the formulations investigated are analyzed. The results show that as the content of the Sacha Inchi flour increases the specific gravity increases from 0,926 to 1,109, generating cakes of lower volume, with greater hardness, and less elasticity and cohesiveness. The color of the bark and the miga is darker, more red and less yellow in the cakes with the inclusion of flour and cake inchi.
