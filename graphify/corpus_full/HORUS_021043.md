# The Ominousness of Water

This work aims to investigate in the female rage from the relationship with the mother and the female lineage. This is a struggle that is carried to the dance floor and comes from an unrest that passes through generations. From there the question is articulated by voice, sound, space and body to denounce, protest and complain. Water appears in its different states in relation to the strength of the body and the presence. Water is sometimes serene, contemplative, welcoming, warm, deep. Sometimes full of ominousness, disturbing strangeness, sometimes violent, voracious, intempsive, sad, open, indomitable. There is a question to navigate those tides. The figure of the siren, seductive, attractive, enveloping, charming and at the same time strange, disturbing and monstrous. From there the voice, sound and image is investigated
