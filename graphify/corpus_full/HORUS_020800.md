# Characterization of the local emergency and disaster preparedness network in Manizales

In recent years, losses have been witnessed around the world due to different natural disasters (sequias, hurricanes, floods, earthquakes) and others caused by human action (wars, mass migrations, famines). The increase in these events has attracted the attention of various sectors, which highlight the importance of having different strategies of preparation to attend to the affected community, supported by several organizations and entities to achieve better results in the stages of response and repair. The main objective of the research is to identify the characteristics of the network of actors involved in the stage of preparedness for disaster management. It is taken as case study the city of Manizales. It is carried out a characterization by applying the methodology of analysis of social networks to evaluate the relations between the actors. The preparation processes are described and the participation of the actors in them is evaluated. Finally, the communication mechanisms used by the actors to relate.
