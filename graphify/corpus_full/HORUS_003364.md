# RESULTS OF THE USE OF ROSE PAST: A PRESENT APPLICATION

Abstract Introduction: neonates requiring hospitalization or clinical follow-up can receive up to 12 painful daily procedures. Indeed, they are able to perceive pain from very early gestational ages, but the immaturity of their nervous system makes it difficult to express with clear signs. Their difficulty in verbalization causes professionals to worry little about diagnosing and treating neonatal pain. The consequences of these nociceptive experiences can be devastating for neurological development, which according to the intensity and duration of exposure can cause neuronal apoptosis and decrease of synaptic connections. Applications using artificial intelligence can facilitate the measurement of neonatal pain, because, although many years ago it is counted with scales, they are not very used by the complexity of use in daily care routines. to describe the results of the use of Pain Rose: an application that measures neonatal pain. a secondary analysis of the results obtained in a case study and controls, which evaluated the diagnostic accuracy and the performance of the Premature Infant Pain Profile Revis web test was developed by the patient. mature Infant Pain Profile Revised (PIPP-R) with the EEG gold test during the observation of cases, (PIPP-R) had a correct classification against the pain diagnosis of 78.38 %, a sensitivity of 96.55 % and specificity 12,5 %. In both the observation of neonates as controls (PIPP-R) has a correct classification against the pain diagnosis of 56.76 %, a sensitivity of 57.14 % and specificity 55.56 %. When making an analysis of the use of the application and the classification of the intensity of neonatal pain as mild, moderate or severe, it is found that the application may overestimate the final score. Adjustments in the web application are required in a specific way, particularly in the second counter of facial expressions that contemplate the time of reaction of the evaluator. The application facilitates the use of diagnostic scales that evaluate neonatal pain. evaluating neonatal pain should be routine care that not only humanizes clinical care, but promotes neonatal neurodevelopment.
