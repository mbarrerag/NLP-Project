# MULTIOBDITIONAL OPTIMINATION OF THE SECUENTIAL COAGULATION/FLOCULATION-FENTON PROCESS FOR THE TREATMENT OF RESIDUAL INDUSTRIAL TEXTILE WATER

In this study, the sequential process Coagulation/Floculation-Fenton (C/F-F) was examined as an alternative treatment to the RNAD IT contaminated with black acid colouring 194 (NA194) generated in a textile plant located in the coffee region. The physicochemical characteristics of RNAD under study were obtained from the analysis of 13 samplings, of a point type, with a periodicity of 20 days, for 8 months, following the guidelines of collection, preservation, quantification, management and transport of samples according to ISO 5667 [3] and APHA [4]
