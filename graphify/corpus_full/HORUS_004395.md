# Fertilization with nitrogen and phosphorus in young agrace plants (Vaccinium sure Swartz) in nursery

Agraz or mortiño (Vaccinium surea Swartz), is a fruit tree native to high-andin zones, with potential for fresh or processed consumption. Current fertilization practices are performed empirically, due to the limited information reported.
