# Chronic poisoning by toxic metals in survivors of improvised explosive devices with incorporated fragments and their neoplastic potential.

Abstract Introduction: Improvised explosive devices are non-conventional weapons that can cause multiple injuries and leave squirts as foreign bodies that can contain toxic metals, with potential affecting the health of victims according to reports dated since 1977, which mention high mortality due to cancer and other symptoms compatible with chronic toxicity Objective: Describe the research results that report the chronic toxicity produced by metals in people who are victims of improvised explosive devices with internalized squirts, and their possible relationships with cancer. Systematic exploratory review of published and grey literature that was carried out between March and May 2021 in different repositories of theses and databases (Scielo, Pubmed, Academic Search Complete, JSTOR, Virtual Health Library, Freepdf, Google Academic, Open Grey); without temporal or geographical limit. Original articles of indexed journals or non-published final reports were included, corresponding to scientific research with full text, in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Of 56 documents evaluated, only three met inclusion criteria. All of these were written in English language. Only one Iraqi study was addressed civil metals and the other studies were not published
