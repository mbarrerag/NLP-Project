# VALIDATION OF A TAMIZAGE TO IDENTIFY DIFFICULTIES IN MATHEMATIC LEARNING AND LESSON-SCRIPT IN PRIMARY GRADE STUDENTS, USING THE WISC-IV TEST

Education, being a universal right, is a concern of international organizations, which propose policies that allow to expand coverage and guarantee the quality of education. In Colombia, the Ministry of National Education has the standards that guide what must be fulfilled in each school grade, however, if a student lacks skills such as abstract reasoning, spatial perception, attention or fine motorism, his or her learning process will be affected and with this the fulfillment of those standards.
