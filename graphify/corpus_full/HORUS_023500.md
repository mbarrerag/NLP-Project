# IN ADP-RIBOSIL POLYRESS (PARP) POLYSTIC POLICE SSP: AN ASSISTANCE

The poly (ADP-ribosyl) polymerases (PARP) are post-raducal regulatory enzymes, which transfer multiple units of ADP-ribosyl) poly (ADP-ribosyl) polymerases (ADP-ribosyl) polymerases (PARP) are post-raducal regulatory enzymes, which transfer multiple units of ADP-ribosone to accepting proteins, using the hydrolysis of the NAD. Its activity is relevant in various DNA repair systems; the PARPs have been extensively studied in multicellular organisms, on the contrary, in unicellular eukaryotics their study has been limited. The Leishmaniasis is a disease of high incidence in public health, increased in recent years by climate change, which favours the prevalence and wide distribution of the phlebotomian transmitting the parasite, Leishmania. To date, there has not been identified PARP in this protozoo. The chimyo-therapeutic treatments previously identified in the data bases of the National Centre, the data model of the National Data Center for the Bio-based, for the Bio-Iclo National Information, the obtained from LbPARP with HsPARP-1 and HsPARP-2 presented RMSDs of 0.653 Å and 0.401 Å respectively. Validations of the structures obtained were made with Ramachandran plot, of 89.37% for the model that took as template hsPARP-1 and 82.37% for hsPARP-2. The bioinformatics findings obtained establish the starting point to address for the first time, the experimental characterization of the candidates identified for PARP in Leishmania, pathogen of interest in current public health
