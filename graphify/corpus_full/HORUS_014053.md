# Territorial disputes and the resolution of inter-ethnic conflicts: A socio-legal analysis of the territories of peace.

The communities of the Pedregosa district, located in the municipality of Cajibío (PDET municipality and belonging to the plans to replace crops for illicit use), are currently recognized as subjects of collective reparation due to the crimes against humanity committed by the paramilitary group of the Calima block. Following the victimizing events, the communities of La Pedregosa (17 villages) together with the village of La Granja and La Aurelia and with the accompaniment of the community council, the Territorial Unit of Cajibío, have a proposal of territorial protection in the understanding that the presence of armed actors remains a reality in the territory.
