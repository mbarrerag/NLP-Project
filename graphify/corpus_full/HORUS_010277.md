# Lighting of the pedestrian routes, cyclopaths and termoletas of the Olympic Village of the municipality of Arauca.

The Olympic Village of the municipality of Arauca, currently has a problem of public lighting, causing a sense of insecurity in pedestrians that pass through it; for this reason a lighting design is carried out for the pedestrian zones, cycloruta and termoleta of the Villa; proposing a scene of functional lighting, attenuated functional lighting and architectural lighting, taking into account the projected occupation of the sector and under the compliance of the Technical Regulation of Lighting and Public Lighting RETILAP; thus seeking a saving in energy consumption and generating an attractive space for visitors and the community in general of the municipality of Arauca
