# VENEZUELA TO COLOMBIA MIGRATION.

This chapter analyses the political and economic conditions that have influenced Venezuela, after being the main country receiving a migrant population in South America in the twentieth century, to become an expelling country of its population in the twenty-first century. It reviews how this Venezuelan transformation changed the condition of Colombia that became a receiving country of Venezuelan nationals, after having been the country with the largest number of migrants to Venezuela. The migratory processes in both countries, although with unequal characteristics, have gone through economic and state crises that have directly impacted on the institution-nality.
