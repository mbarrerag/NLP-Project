# Pilot project of estimation of kiln emission factors for brick production in the jurisdiction of Sabana Center of the Regional Autonomous Corporation of Cundinamarca (CAR)

The absence of emission factors for brick kiln technologies in the Colombian context limits the knowledge on the impact of these sources on air quality. In this document, the emission factors of particulate matter (PM), sulfur dioxide (SO2), nitrogen oxides (NOx), chlorine halides (HCl) and fluoride halides (HF) for brick production kiln were estimated from measurements found in the records of Cundinamarca’s environmental authority (CAR), representing 32 kilns of the central savanna jurisdiction. These emissions reports were scored according to the US Environmental Protection Agency emission factor criteria, obtaining a rating of D (below average) without a control system and E (poor) for those with emission control systems
