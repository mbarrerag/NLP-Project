# 3. DETERMINATION OF SOIL PH AND SOUND INTERCAMBIABLE ACID.

Soil acidity is due to two types of protons found in this: those associated with soil colloids (organic matter and clays) by electrostatic unions and those that are easily dissociated (which are dissolved in the solution or water of the soil). Representation of ion exchange in the soil Measuring pH does not, then, mean quantifying all protons in soil dissolution or soil water, but they represent only a small part, very small, of the total protons in the soil, and many more are bound to the surface of colloidal particles (called interchangeable protons). Dissolved protons can be quantified by reading with potentiometry, while those that are bound to colloids can only be quantified by an acid-base titration
