# “There is no Anti-Ocquean Urabah”: racial markers, archive and armed conflict in the Urabah

This paper explains the multiple dimensions of racism that we know as structural. It makes an ethnography of archives and a lexical-semantic analysis of the terms Urabá antioqueño and Urabá chocoano as racial markers. The municipalities antioqueños center of analysis were Murindó and Vigía del Fuerte, mainly in facts related to the armed conflict. To do this analysis, press documents were used from a human rights archive related to the armed conflict; a tracing of institutional processes for the consolidation of a human rights database; search for information from primary and secondary sources about the Urabá; a tracing of information from periodic publications about the Urabá and the conversation with journalists who have knowledge of the region. The main findings are concentrated on cognitive traces that leave certain everyday uses, understood as racial markers, which affect the multiple dimension and deepening of racism . of the region.
