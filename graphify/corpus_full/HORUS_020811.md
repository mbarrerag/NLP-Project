# Food between divorced spouses in Colombia Sanction or solidarity?

In this thesis an analysis was made to understand why in Colombia women who during marriage perform unpaid domestic work when divorced must prove the guilt of their spouse to access the right of food without being able to resort to the direct application of the principle of solidarity. From the study carried out it was found that in the norms of family law there is a gap in the regulation of the maintenance between spouses when there is a divorce of mutual agreement or without guilt, since, as the norm is designed, they owe food “to the guilty spouse, to the divorced spouse or separated from the body without his fault” (C.C.,1873, art. 411). This void is being filled by the high courts who have made judgments in those who award food based on solidarity and necessity by leaving guilt. It was also found that, the legislator attributed to the food a legal function by discounting that the inequality of opportunities within the marriage affects in their majority the women who perform unpaid domestic work. rm is designated, alimony is owned “to charge of the quality spouse, to theuse divorced or separated from the body without their fault” (CC, 1873, art.411).
