# Assessment of the impact and importance of firefighting systems in the process of power generation in hydroelectric plants (case study: 660MW generation plant).

The lack of knowledge of the functional relationship of firefighting systems in the process of generating power in hydroelectric power plants is an obstacle in the way of implementing corporate policies that help prevent fire events. This deficiency begins from the idea that firefighting systems are systems independent of the generation process, which causes a low participation of the plant's personnel and a low sensitivity of these with regard to the importance of firefighting systems. This research focuses on studying the operation of firefighting systems within the process of producing energy in a power generation plant by characterization of the installed firefighting systems, the definition of an interaction between these systems and the energy production process and the evaluation of this interaction through strategic indicators such as reliability
