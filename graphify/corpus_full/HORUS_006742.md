# Relationship of environmental and landscape variables in the distribution of two Andean Magnolias and their floral visitors

Magnolia jardinensis and M. yarumallensis are species with distribution restricted to the Noroccidental of Colombia. These species face an imminent threat of extinction, mainly due to the degradation of their habitat.
