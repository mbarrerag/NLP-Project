# Strategies and Challenges of Circular Economy as Opportunities for Business Models in Construction Companies in Colombia

This research studies the strategies and challenges of the implementation of the Circular Economy (EC) in construction, as business model opportunities for construction sector companies in Colombia. To meet the research objectives, a qualitative methodology is carried out, which is divided into a systematic literature review (RSL), a survey aimed at business leaders part of the construction value chain, ending with the report and conclusions of the findings found in the first two phases. There is evidence of a growing interest on the part of academia and industry in the implementation of EC in the sector, as well as in the impressions obtained from respondents, the importance of recognizing the challenges of this economic model, implementation strategies for the sector that seek to improve resource efficiency, product design, systems and buildings, and the potential for the development of new circular business models (MNC) or the transformation of current businesses towards an EC and a sustainable development of the industry. .
