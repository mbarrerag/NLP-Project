# EXPLANATIONS IN PSYCHOLOGY: A DIALOGUE OF KNOWLEDGE

This book aims to bring the reader closer to the problems that constitute the object of study of psychology. From the Introduction, we will be able to know the explanation and understanding of psychological phenomena. This implies a certain rational articulation that requires considering the motivations, moments and lines of thought that researchers enter into to give meaning to the work of the psychologist. Therefore, educational, clinical, social, psychometric psychology and neuropsychology receive a close approach to their epistemological bases and to the logical explanation that they use to understand study objects and their problems. In each chapter, an example of research in each area is offered and how the phenomenon of study was explained to illustrate the strategies applied in the inquiry. In addition, a qualitative review of the literature around the topic of study is carried out covering the most notorious and relevant of the last years. This book was carried out by university professors, the majority of the Vice-Rectorate of research of the University Manuela Beltrán, and is aimed at undergraduate and postgraduate students of psychology, as well as anyone who is interested in psychology and its epistemological and explanatory bases
