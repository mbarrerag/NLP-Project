# Evaluation of external reinforcement alternatives for horizontal drilling block masonry.

The masonry construction covers more than 80% of the total of the houses in Colombia being the horizontal drilling block in self-construction homes one of the most used ma-terials given its affordable prices and good functioning before the service loads of these homes.A large part of these houses are in areas of high vulnerability to seismic events or mass removals, events for which the ma-postry, of fragile nature, does not present the desired behavior to safeguard the life of the people.The expensive price of the reinforcement steel, and the socio-economic conditions of the country, demand to investigate alternative materials that, analogous to the ace-ro, confess ductility to the system with a balance between the cost, its mechanical contribution and its low constructive complexity.The present investigation, proposes four (4) alternatives of external reinforcement embebi-do in the masonry for the horizontal drilling block, and each design.The experimental plan included three of the masonry. The Commission has also taken a number of steps to ensure that the system is not less efficient and that the system is more efficient. Finally, a multicriteria analysis was carried out considering the contribution of each technique to mechanical resistance, ductility of the system, cost and ease of construction, finding that the reinforcement of the padet with metal fibres is the most appropriate alternative at a dosage of 40 kg/m3, followed by the guadua mat and finally the hennet. The reinforcement with metallic fibers at a dosage of 60 kg/m3, is not recommended as reinforcement due to its high cost and does not represent greater mechanical contributions than the alternative with lower proportions of fibers
