# A CULTURE APPROXIMATION TO WOMEN'S VOICE ON THE CONSERVATION OF WATER IN COLOMBIA

"Woman and water: a natural relationship" is a work that places women in different roles in which their knowledge and abilities are exalted as managers, scientists and even protectors in defense of water, the environment and the communities present in the territory. The text is divided into three parts: 1) women and action for water, 2) women and science and 3) ecofeminism; in which the role of women as articulator of processes and bridge of union between science, academia and society is highlighted. It is a collective invitation to provide comprehensive alternatives to the resolution of environmental problems and conflicts, where water is often a focus of interest. The book extends an invitation to take challenges and to undertake adaptation actions, in times of change
