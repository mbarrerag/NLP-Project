# Influence of organizational culture on frugal innovation: A study of small manufacturing enterprises (pymes) in the metropolitan area of Caldas

This study aims to determine the influence of organizational culture on the innovation of the fruit in small manufacturing companies, using the model of the framework of values in competition (CVF) of Cameron & Quinn (1999).
