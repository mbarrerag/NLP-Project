# Violence in Buenaventura-Colombia: reflections from the perspectives of Aníbal Quijano and Achille Mbembe

Abstract The article aims to analyze the violent dynamics in Buenaventura-Colombia, as part of the necropolitics implemented by the State in which various illegal armed actors and their repertoires of action - terror and death - are established as means to control the population. This analysis is oriented through the theoretical perspective of Aníbal Quijano on > 8220;The coloniality of power> 8221; and Achile Mbembe's vision of > 8220;Necropolitica> 8221; Methodologically, the inquiry used a qualitative approach based on the analysis of the newspaper El País, the Open Truth portal and the reports for Development and Peace (INDEPAZ), during the years 2010-2019. It is concluded that the socio-political context of Buenaventura is a clear example of the relationship between modernity, terror and death, on account of a system of domination in which the ethnic-racial and social class classification of its inhabitants converges
