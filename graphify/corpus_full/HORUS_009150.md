# New business models in the Colombian energy market: Energy arbitration through storage systems

Currently the energy transition is a phenomenon that is changing the entire paradigm on which it rests the Colombian energy market, thanks to this, new actors have emerged that are necessary to achieve decarbonization and the organization of a new sustainable energy market, which in turn requires a regulatory component that facilitates the entry of new players and allows the creation of business models that are attractive to investors. In this sense, the energy transition enables the massification of distributed energy resources, promoting the creation of new markets and business opportunities. This work focuses on making a tour of the Colombian regulation, reviewing especially the new business opportunities that are generated from the incorporation of energy storage systems as distributed energy resources, to finally analyze the energy arbitration through this technology, for this five scenarios were proposed which were economically evaluated through the VPN and the IRR, finding according to the results of the indicators that it is not a sustainable model by itself considering the purchase and sale of energy in the energy exchange and that it requires a complement, such as self-consumption or incentives. regulatory
