# In vitro tests (Limulus Amebocyte Lysate test (LAL) and Monocyte Activation Test (MAT)) as pyrogen test substitutes in rabbits, for anti-ophidic serums: Scope review.

The analysis of pyrogens is one of the tests in quality control during the production and release of injectable products. The effect on human health is associated with the physiopathological consequences that generate bacterial endotoxins that can cause the death of patients. To describe and critically evaluate the current state of knowledge about the capacity of the in vitro tests Lisado de Amebocytes de Limulus (LAL) and the Monocyte Activation Test (MAT), to see the animal model to evaluate pyrogenicity in anti-ophideal serums. Review of scope in Wiley, Scopus, LILACS, Google Scholar, PubMed, and Cochrane Library of articles published until July 01, 2022, following the recommendations PRISMA. The articles included were classified according to inclusion and exclusion criteria. They were included articles published in scientific journals, review and observational studies whose outcomes and study object were related to the object of the research.
