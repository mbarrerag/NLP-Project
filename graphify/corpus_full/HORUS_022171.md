# A Data-driven Representation Learning for Tumor Tissue Differentiation from Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Histopathology Images.

The lung cancer is the second most common type and the main cause of death by cancer in the world. It is divided into different types according to the cellular and tissue characteristics, and in turn, these types are distinguished by the typical histological patterns that represent them. Each histological subtype of lung cancer is associated with the prognosis and treatment of patients, and is subjectively stratified by pathologists mainly by their morphological characteristics. However, by the very nature of the disease, this stratification varies since there is no specialized gradation system, and also by the difficulty of characterizing the cases that usually contain histological and unspecified tissue combinations, which can affect the accuracy of the diagnosis and prognosis of patients. This research work addresses a computational strategy based on data to characterize the histological patterns of lung cancer in a constructional space
