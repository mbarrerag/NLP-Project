# NARRATIVE REVIEW

The comprehensive management of the patient with mental disorder from nursing includes approaches that are carried out in home care, allowing the development of activities of evaluation, follow-up and care to the patient and his family. A strategic search was carried out in Medline, Epistemonikos, JBI Base, Virtual Library in Health, using the terms "disease", mental illness, home care, nursing?. The extraction and analysis of the data was carried out according to the approaches of the JBI, RESULTS: 25 articles were identified that met the inclusion criteria and were classified into 4 topics: 1. The experience of home care of the patient with mental disorder 2. Adherence to the medication of patients with mental disorder who receive treatment at home. 3. The elderly with mental disorder and 4. Technological strategies for giving home care to the patient with mental disorder.
