# DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTING OVA'S AS A PRE-SIMULATION LEARNING STRATEGY

The project to design and implement virtual learning objects (OVA) arises in the midst of the pandemic of the COVID-19 because of the restrictions imposed in health institutions for the use of the practical scenarios.According to the evaluations of the implementation of the program, it was achieved to determine that the design of pre-simulation activities as are the OVAs. Are they key to ensuring that the student meets the objectives set in the simulation scenario because they provide him with a means to strengthen the knowledge prior to the development of the simulation scenario. On the other hand, the use of the AODEDEI methodology allows the teacher to have a specific and rigorous guide for the elaboration of the OVAS that meets the expectations of the digital resource
