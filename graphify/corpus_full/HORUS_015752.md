# The health of the athlete in Colombia: a matter of inequality.

This study analyses the health of athletes in Colombia and highlights how social determinants generate inequalities in health. The use of the generic expression "sportists" includes men and women in this text, and it does not mention sportsmen and athletes in a way that is conducive to fluidity. Three sports were analyzed: tennis, boxing and cycling. The study was based on the qualitative and interpretative research approach of ethnosociological perspective. The analysis of information was carried out within the framework of the approach of social determinants of health. The contribution of this work to public health is to recognize or visualize how political and social logics in professional sport, which has been promoted as an occupation that contributes to improving health, produce inequalities in health. Work aims to understand how sport produces and reproduces social inequalities and invisibility in all these daily practices that affect the health of athletes and forces them to be at the level demanded by sports guilds and sponsors
