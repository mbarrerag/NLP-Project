# Job stability of internal medicine specialists in the city of Bogotá: comparative between public and private procurement.

This research paper makes a comparison between the forms of linking health personnel to the public and private sectors, showing the precariousness of the work of physicians who specialize in internal medicine in Bogotá, with the recruitment through figures that have been introduced by the easing of labour standards, figures that do not meet the prospects of decent and dignified work. To this end, it is necessary to start from a descriptive analysis of the General System of Social Security in Health in Colombia, its background, the double dimension of the concept of health as a service and right based on the 2008 judgment T-760, its financing in integration with economic globalization, in order to understand its concept as a market of which health personnel are an essential element, we highlight within this work the global crisis that health personnel faced by the declaration of a global pandemic by COVID 19 that sharpens the precarious conditions of health personnel
