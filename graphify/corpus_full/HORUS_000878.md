# Teaching and heritage: the Taminango Museum's approach to educational innovation through transmedia narratives

The Taminango Museum, located in Pasto, Nariño, Colombia, has implemented an innovative educational program since 2012, which has not only adapted, but has prospered under the challenges imposed by the COVID-19 pandemic. This program uses a combination of analog and digital didactic tools that serves as a replicable model for local and regional museum institutions with limited budgets, because it emphasizes community design and participatory methods. This article presents the way in which the museum has used transmedia narratives to enrich educational experience, positioning it as a proactive entity in the promotion and preservation of local cultural heritage. This model highlights the museum's fundamental role in promoting cultural accessibility, sustainable community commitment and promoting changes in the regional museum sector
