# Biopolis 2100: Architectures and landscapes for non-humans (and humans)

Biopolis 2100 is a research-creation project in the field of landscape architecture. We seek to reformulate the human settlement model based on the grey infrastructure, to propose other forms of intervention of the territory based on solutions based on nature (NBS), living infrastructures, and green infrastructures that contribute to the environmental and social resilience of a territory. For this case, the Seed works in the urban area Juanchaco and Ladrilleros, 2 human settlements in the second most biodiverse region in the Colombian Pacific coast. During the first semester of 2024 the members of the Seedman made a field exit to the territory to collect information about environmental, cultural and other determinants of the landscape
