# Methylene ash retention in low cost adsorbents; biocarbones Versus hydrocarbones obtained from biosolides of waste water treatment plants

Methylene blue (AM) is a highly used colourant in the textile, leather, cosmetics, paper and plastics industry, which results in water resource contamination when the management of these residues is not adequate. The use of low-cost biocarbons (BC) and hydrocarbons (HC) can allow adequate filtration of water resources giving an additional choice for water sanitation and avoiding pollution. In this work BC and HC were obtained from biosolids of urban wastewater treatment plant in the City of Bogotá. The BCs were obtained at 250aC, 430oC and 520oC and HC at 180, 200oC and 250oC in pilot plant. Both products were characterized (nearly analysis, IR) to determine some of their properties.
