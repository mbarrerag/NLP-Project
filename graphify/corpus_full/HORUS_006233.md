# multidimensional poverty index (MPI) estimates in Bogotá D.C. and some nearby cities using satellite images

This paper studies an alternative to obtain poverty estimates using satellite images from Bogotá, D.C. and some nearby municipalities. To achieve this, the two methodologies presented in this paper are compared: a model of convolutional neural networks (CNN) and a model of tensile regression (GTR), the two models applied for the classification of pixels. From these models, criteria or metrics are defined that allow us to select the model that best captures the distribution of the multidimensional poverty index (IPM) at the pixel level. Finally, an application of estimation of poverty using the images of Planet Scope (PS) and the information at the level of apples of the last National Population and Housing Census (CNPV2018) where it is found that the model of GTR presents better metrics of performance compared to the model of CNN .
