# DEPLOYABLE LOOP ANTENNA ARRAY BASED ON A SCISSORS MECHANISM

A novel series-fed loop array is presented in this contribution, which is deployable by means of a scissors-type stretchable mechanism. The antenna structure exploits the use of a single wire with proper size to produce in-phase vertical currents that creates a highly directive pattern. The design methodology and simple guidelines for obtaining desired input impedance, gain and front-to-backlobe-ratio (FBR) are presented
