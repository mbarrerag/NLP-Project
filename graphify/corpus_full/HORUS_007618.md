# Contribution to the evaluation of organic leachables in a nasal pharmaceutical product presented in a low density polyethylene container (PEBD - LDPE)

The importance of the study of extractables and leachables (E&L) lies in assessing the possible transfer of unwanted and unsafe substances from the materials of construction of primary packaging to pharmaceutical products and assessing their possible toxicological risks to the patient. The findings of these studies can provide a potential risk assessment, allow monitoring the presence of impurities in the finished product and provide information on the suitable packaging that can be selected at the product design and development stages, aligning with the design-based Quality Approach (QbD), to ensure that a given pharmaceutical formulation is contained in the packaging that is the highest degree of compatibility with the product to mitigate the risks of toxicity and contribute to ensuring the safety and stability of the finished pharmaceutical product. When the pharmaceutical industry and quality control laboratories in Colombia face to undertake the E&L studies, difficulties arise since a given pharmaceutical formulation is not always known of the extractable substances from the packaging, thus hindering the guidance of subsequent leachables studies, since the risk of interaction requires an extra- and adequate leaching, specific, packaging studies for each pharmaceutical formulation in contact with the case
