# What to do with Altos de Cazuca? Habitat Improvement Guidelines

Soacha, Cundinamarca, has transformed that agricultural and landscape vocation that characterizes it in its beginnings, by the image of a city with great problems in terms of misery, monetary poverty, mass migration, environmental problems and other aspects that move to the municipality of the whole country. One of these problems is the qualitative deficit of the autoconstructed settlements predominantly located in commune 4 and 6, which affect the quality of life of its inhabitants, which has led to review the idea of improving what was built. This was driven since the 1970s in Colombia, influenced by the advances in the discussions around the habitat, which consisted in recognizing the informal settlement as a form of access to housing that needs its intervention in terms of improvement. Considering the above, they have been carried out in Colombia and recently in Altos de Cazuca, Soacha, institutional programs of improvement in neighborhood in order to cover the basic needs in terms of infrastructure as a sewerage and paving of vineyards, however, despite the fact that the actions improve the living conditions of the city, so much more than the urban ones. In the physical improvement actions, called La Meseta, located between the Villa Mercedes neighborhood and the Luis Carlos Galan I neighborhood, the following question arises to answer in the development of the investigation: How to promote actions to improve the integral habitat in high places of Cazuca, from the case of study in the La Meseta neighborhood, if from 2010 to 2023, are processes of improvement in the neighborhood from a perspective focused on improvements in physical infrastructure? With this, it is proposed, on the one hand, to contribute to the theoretical discussion about the improvement of the quality of life from the systemic vision of the habitat, and also, to involve the community of the neighborhood that is studied and a group of inhabitants of the commune 4, in the collective construction of a definition of improvement of the habitat to build a plan to improve the habitat that complements the physical improvement actions built up to the moment
