# Methodological scheme for the initiation of Symphonic Band processes in Municipal Training Schools

This paper presents the main problems that a director can find in an initial training group, these are framed in the development of three exercises proposed for the group and with a research framed in the quantity of existing repertoire on which the technical elements involved can be put into practice within the exercises, elements can be found to change and make more dynamic the development of students and the group in general. .
