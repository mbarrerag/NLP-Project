# USE OF SATELITAL INFORMATION TO OBTAIN A ASSISTANCE TO STORAGE SUBTERRANEAN WATER IN THE FIVE MAIN COUNTRY FINDINGS

The project carries out a processing of satellite information oriented to the generation of an approach to the state of storage of groundwater in Colombia, for this purpose it downloads information from the mission GRACE and the project GLDAS, which in conjunction with a series of processings allow to obtain trends and behaviors of the water resource. By using the language of Python programming, applying a hydrological balance and making a cut of the information downloaded to the extens ion of the Colombian territory are estimated the anomalies presented in the storage of the groundwater. Segmenting the study in the five main basins of the country, Amazonas, Caribbean, Magdalena Cauca, Orinoquía and Pacific, is made use of different mapping representations in order to understand the changes in the storage of the water subterran ea under different approaches: geographic, temporal and analysis of extreme values, obtaining useful information on each of the regions, also highlighting the existing values, minimum and maximum; temporal analysis were made in the first and most recent measurements, managing to produce heat maps on those extreme values identified, thus observing the geographic regions in which have presented critical scenarios
