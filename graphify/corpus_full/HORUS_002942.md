# IMMOVILIZATION OF PB(II) IN SOLUTION BY UREOLITIC BATERIES AND THEIR POINTIAL APPLICATION IN AGRICULTURAL SOUND

Lead contamination (Pb) has become a problem due to its various sources of emission by anthropogenic activities, and represents a risk to health and the environment due to its toxicity to living beings, as well as its permanence in the environment. Recently, the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms (MICPII) has been explored using ureolytic bacteria as an alternative to immobilize the Pb as a low soluble carbonate, thus reducing its bioavailability. In this study, the potential of immobilization of Pb(II) in solution by the bacterial isolates Serratia 5b and Bacillus 92g2, from cocoa farms from Santander, Colombia, was studied. The tests of immobilization of the metal in solution showed removal values greater than 96% for the two isolated ones in 72 h, as well as an increase of pH corresponding to hydrolysis of urea by the enzyme urease. The characterization of the precipitates obtained with each bacterium by difraction of X-ray (DRX) and the electro-scopic acid (SEM) was performed
