# PROBATORY MEANS OF PATRONAL CULPA BY LABORAL DISEASES IN COLOMBIA. A LOOK FROM SAFETY AND HEALTH AT WORK

According to the report of the Colombian Security Council (CCS) in 2021, a cup of 394.9 labor diseases per 100,000 workers was registered [CCS (Colombian Security Council), 2021). According to the same report on the distribution of the labor disease by economic activities was found that `Social and health services' presented the highest rate of labour disease with 3,696 diseases rated per 100,000 workers. [ccs (colombian security council)], 2021, p. 1). The increase of these rates can cause in the medium and long term an increase in the judicial conflicts through which the affected workers will demand the employer's fault for labor disease, based on the legal pronouncements issued between 2019 and 2021. For this reason, the aim of this article is to explore the evidentiary means taken into account by Colombian court judges at the time of resolving legal conflicts in which the employer's fault for labor disease is discussed, based on judicial pronouncements issued between 2021. For this, a systematic and exploratory review of the sentences and acquittals issued between 2019 and 2021 was carried out
