# Conflicts of use and tenure in the areas of national natural parks: tension between constitutional rights to the environment and the rights of peasant communities.

The peasant population that lives and makes uses in the areas of Natural National Parks, after the historical processes of widening the agricultural border, faces a limited materialization of rights, given the restrictions provided for by the legal framework in environmental matters. This evidences a tension of constitutional rights between those of the peasant communities (subjects of special constitutional protection) and the collective right to a healthy environment that is intended to materialize, among other instruments, with the natural national parks. After a historical and legal analysis, this document proposes the search for a solution that allows the weighting of the rights in tension and their realization, to the greatest extent possible
