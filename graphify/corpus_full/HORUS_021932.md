# On a Ortega principle and the linear stability properties of periodic responses with symmetry in MEMS oscillators.

In this work we obtain a novel result of the existence of odd periodic responses with certain nodal properties in the context of a general family of nonlinear oscillators with symmetries, which in particular have applications in the area of oscillators type MEMS or micro electromechanical systems (Younis, 2011). Moreover, in the context of a model MEMS electrostatic type comb with a non linear cubic restorative force, we obtain a novel result on the existence of odd periodic responses with certain nodal properties, and stability in the linear sense of some solutions found when the amplitude of the AC component in the input voltage is sufficiently small. Some simulations and numerical continuation results are presented with the objective of numerically validating the analytical results for the MEMS type combe considered. The main results of this work are obtained by applying the variable principle of R. Ortega (Ortega, 2016), the technique of truncation, a priori quos of periodic solutions for nonlinear oscillators and the disturbing strategy introduced in (Cen et al., 2020) for stability in the linear sense
