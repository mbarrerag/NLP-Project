# In-silico search for volatile compounds of natural origin for the sustainable development of insecticides applicable in the control of banana and banana pests.

In recent years, the development of agriculture in Colombia has been prioritized in strengthening the competitiveness and productivity of strategic chains such as banana and banana crops. The first one is highlighted for being the most sown in the country, achieving the export of 117,524 tons (2020), which shows its economic importance. Unfortunately the country does not have the capacity to maintain an agro-industrial dynamic with sustained development. This due to the high production costs, technological barriers, health eligibility and especially the low productivity due to the presence of pests and/or diseases that deteriorate the quality of these crops. To try to mitigate this latter problem, farmers use indiscriminately insecticides of synthetic origin such as chlorpyrifos; that despite its effectiveness, has shown a high toxicity, which has led to being restricted by the European Union and the United States. In a way that the implementation of these restrictions will represent a decrease of the high output of Colombian fruits as well as banana, affecting producers and marketers. Therefore, it is essential for the agro-industrial development of new substances, accessible, and safe for the control of natural products. which can serve as an alternative for the management of banana and banana pests. For this purpose, a selection of promising CVs will be made, using in-silico tools and a virtual screening of PN databases against targets such as ACHE. And from the CVs selected in the first phase, the biological activity in vitro versus the target of study (AChE) will be evaluated, in order to select the best bioactive compound
