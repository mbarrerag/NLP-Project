# Manuel Zapata-Olivella Medical Dialectics: Renewed Clinical Thought and Social Medicine

Regarding the writer Manuel Zapata Olivella little comment is made that in the mid-20th century he received the title of Doctor of Medicine and Surgery at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. Although his thesis to opt for this title was supported in 1949, it remains unpublished, so this document could complete the author’s work from a little explored facet: as a scientific essayist in medicine. Moreover, while literary criticism recognizes in Zapata Olivella the traces of medical-social thought of his time, the accounts about thinkers of such current in Latin America do not mention it. Once the the the thesis was recovered in the historical archives of the Gabriel García Márquez Central Library of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, and analyzed its form and content, it was possible to establish that it was a scientific essay written with the intention of rewriting the clinical medical thought from the dialectics and thus rethinking the medical practice of its time, being this intention still in force at the present time.
