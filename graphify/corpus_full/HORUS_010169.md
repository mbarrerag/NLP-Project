# EFFECT OF THE INCLUSION OF SACHA INCHI FLOUR (BY-PRODUCT OF THE PROCESS) ON THE QUALITY AND STORAGE PROPERTIES OF BAKERY PRODUCTS

Modernization in the food industry has posed the challenge of developing healthier snack-type bakery products with better nutritional profiles to cope with changes in consumer habits. For this, new raw materials that allow these developments have been evaluated, among the innovative raw materials is Sacha Inchi (SI) (Plukenetia spp.), which is a nut native to the Amazon rich in oil (35-60%) , proteins (25-30%), essential amino acids, minerals and vitamin E. SI is mainly used to extract the oil, which is rich in polyunsaturated fatty acids (a-linolenic, ~50%; and linoleic, ~35%). The protein-rich press cake is generally used in animal feed or discarded. The objective of this work was to add value to a byproduct (press cake) from the processing of the Sacha Inchi seed with a view to improving the nutritional profile of a bakery product. For this reason, a bakery cake was elaborated with the incorporation of flour from the Sacha Inchi oilseed press cake. A control and three treatments were established with different percentages of inclusion by substitution of wheat flour (60%, 80% and 100%). The changes were evidenced in the quality properties of the cakes (Moisture, water activity, color and texture (TPA), amount of protein and fiber) and their behavior was evaluated during 14 days of storage. The results showed a relationship between the increase in inclusion with an increase between 10 - 12% protein and 5% fiber. Additionally, it was observed that the texture and color properties are affected by the degree of inclusion and the dry storage time of the product. In conclusion, the inclusion of the pressed cake in bakery products is possible. In addition, it allows adding value to the main byproduct of Sacha Inchi oil processing. Thus giving the possibility of promoting the Sacha Inchi industry towards the trends of sustainable industries and circular economy
