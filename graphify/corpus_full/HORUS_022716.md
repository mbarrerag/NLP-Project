# Metaheuristic optimization applied in asphalt flooring management

The floor engineering is a crossroads between geotechnical engineering and transport engineering with a solid base in construction materials. There are different applications of optimization algorithms in floor engineering, which emphasize the management of pavement due to its socioeconomic implications and the inverse calculation of layer properties due to its complexity. A detailed review of the literature shows that optimization has been a permanent concern in floor engineering; however, only in the last two decades, the increase in computational power allowed the implementation of meta-heuristic optimization techniques with promising results in research and practice. The management of the pavement requires powerful optimization tools for problems with multiple objectives, such as minimizing costs and maximizing the state of the pavement from the level of the network to the level of the project with limited budgets. A substantial amount of research focuses on genetic algorithms (AG), but new developments include particle intelligence (PSO, ACO and ABC). The study should go beyond small-sized networks to improve the management of existing road infrastructure (pavement, bridges) based on mechanicist and conflabllistic criteria
