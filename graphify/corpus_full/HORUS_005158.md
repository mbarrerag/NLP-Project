# What is the current evidence about family caregivers of children with cancer?: A Umbrella Review

Abstract Introduction: Childhood cancer has a great impact not only for children but for their family caregivers. To determine the current state of evidence about family caregivers of children with cancer, based on literature reviews published between 2010 and 2020. A review of the type >=8220;Umbrella>8221 was carried out; including literature reviews, systematics and meta-analysis on the family caregivers of children with cancer as the main topic. It was searched in the databases Academic Search complete, Scopus, Science Direct, Complementary index, Google Scholar and Scielo. Twenty-one articles were included in the review. Six prevalent research topics were identified: 1) Psychosocial symptoms: Impact of cancer on the family caregiver, 2) Needs of the family caregiver in the transition of the role of caregiver, 3) Parental communication in the context of childhood cancer, 4) Addressing the caregiver in the context of the child's disease, 5) Overloading of the family caregiver, 6) Del of the family caregiver at the end of the child's life with cancer.
