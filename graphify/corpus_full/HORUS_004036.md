# Evaluating a sigmoid dark energy model to explain the Hubble tension

In this study, we analyze Type Ia supernovae (SNe Ia) data sourced from the Pantheon+ compilation to investigate late-time physics effects influencing the expansion history, (Formula presented.), at redshifts (Formula presented.). The change in the equation of state is implemented as a transition in the DE density scale factor driven by a sigmoid function.
