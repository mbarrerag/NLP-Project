# Music tools, techniques and expressive tuba today

The present work is intended to be a tool for consulting composers and arrangers who intend to give in their creations a leading role to Tuba, an instrument that has so far had little soloist repertoire within the works written by Colombian authors. Here are deposited extracts of fundamental works in the repertoire of Tuba, sound effects and extended techniques that have greatly enriched the artistic advancement of the players and that today allow to interpret works that require a great level of skill. .
