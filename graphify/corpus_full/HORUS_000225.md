# Financial stability and credit in the Colombian economy

This article analyses the relationship between credit, financial stability and regulatory capital from a macroeconomic perspective with time series. To do this, we analyze the evolution of the solvency ratio and credit in the Colombian economy during the period 2010-2023. An econometric exercise is carried out to analyze the effects that regulatory capital brings on credit and its growth. The results show that greater regulatory capital decreases the rate of growth of loans in the Colombian economy
