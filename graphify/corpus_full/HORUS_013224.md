# Geometric, dynamic, and topological considerations of horseshoe-type functions

This thesis will address the study of a version of Smale's horseshoe function and the Baker function, as well as its dynamic properties, from a topological perspective. To this end, some necessary notions on hyperbolic geometry, dynamic systems and Riemann's surfaces will be presented. Subsequently, we will define the horsesadura and Baker functions from the composition of two geometric transformations defined on the unit square, denoted by Q. These transformations will induce a series of identifications at the border of Q and we will use the notions introduced on Riemann surfaces to study the geometric and topological properties of the quotient space generated by those identifications.
