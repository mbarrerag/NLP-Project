# EFFECT OF A TRANSVERSAL CANAL ON THE VORTICE-ANTIVORTIZE STATE IN A SUPER-CONDUCTORY FIELD

In this study, the resistive state of a mesoscopic superconductive sample was studied in the presence of a Ja transport current, and at a zero magnetic field. A non-centered channel of depreciated superconductivity (of width m and lower critical temperature Tc) was located at a distance d from the center of the sample. We varied the width a from the metallic contact by which the external current is applied. We studied the density of superconductive electrons, the vortex-antivortex velocity and the rates of annihilation of the supercurrent for various widths of the metallic contacts and for the position of the channel. Our investigation showed that the critical currents and the velocity of the v- Av pairs depend strongly on the contact size and the channel position in the sample
