# Compendium of visual methods for product design and development process

This academic contribution presents a compendium of visual methods, which are very useful in the design and development of products to understand the variables that play a role in such processes. It is the result of a research that included a systematic non-exhaustive review of methods, field observation and validation with experts. The methods are organized into different categories that facilitate their location, depending on the design activity being carried out
