# THE SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM AND INDIVIDUAL OVERVIEW MODES. CRITERIA FOR STUDYING CORRUPTION AND CRIMINALITY

Interpretation of violence, crime and corruption in society, based on the study of a multitude of sources of various kinds, and the critical analysis of the current interpretations. Therefore, the pernicious effects of the predominance of Platonism in the interpretation of these sources are discussed in some detail, and it is proposed, by Nietzsche and evolutionary thought, to approach the problem, which, supported by the perspective of inverted Platonism proposed by the philosopher, conceives society as an ecosystem, that is, a living system, where each individual must face the challenge of survival, managing to obtain resources that will ensure its existence. Thus, we have identified different modes of survival and classified them into three categories: 1) generators (creators-producers); 2) looters (parasite, predators and opportunists) and 3) symbiont (mutualists and leonists)
