# COSMOS HISTORY

Through this book, the reader will be able to embark on a captivating journey through stories where science is the great protagonist; stories that encompass a wide variety of characters and epochs, and that reflect the triumph of the human intellect in its desire to unravel the mysteries of the cosmos. The amazing lives of some of the most famous characters who fertilized the ground to astronomical knowledge; the answer to great questions about our amazing universe, the description of spectacular phenomena that occur on earth, in the sky, and beyond our planet; the arduous path that has led us into the exploration of space; and the science that is hidden around us, are central themes in the stories that the author writes with the rigor, but also with the passion necessary, to make this book a pleasant reading that does not leave indifferent to the fanatics of the cosmos, and to those who want to begin to discover its wonders
