# Risks in sales and deliveries of housing construction projects and management strategies.

This document seeks to analyse risks associated with the sale and delivery of housing construction projects in Colombia, in order to design management strategies in construction companies that serve as a guide to mitigate the impacts that are presented in the writing stage of completed housing. To carry out the objective, an in-depth analysis of the current situation of real estate projects in Colombia, focusing on a breakdown of supply and demand in the main cities of the country, is undertaken. Furthermore, the financing mechanisms of housing projects, the status of the bank portfolio for the construction credit product, as well as the subsidies offered by territorial and governmental entities that stimulate the new housing market, are analyzed. Finally, the main causes of failure of real estate projects in Colombia are defined and detailed, as well as the impact, magnitude and probability of each of them. With these causes defined, each of the proposed and recommended management strategies, which will serve as a guide to housing builders for the correct management of the risks present in the writing and delivery of finished buildings, are listed
