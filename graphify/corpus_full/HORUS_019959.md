# A preprojective Auslander-Reiten component for the socle projective modules of some right-peak algebras

Let Λ be a right-peak algebra in the sense of Simson (J Algebra 92:532–571, 1985). Denote by mod sp(Λ) the full subcategory of mod - Λ consisting of those right Λ-modules M such that the socle of M is a simple projective right Λ-module. Suppose that for any non-simple indecomposable right projective Λ-module P, rad P= Tl, for some indecomposable T and some positive integer l. Here we show that the Auslander–Reiten component of mod sp(Λ) containing the simple projective right Λ-module is a preprojective component. To do so, we describe an Auslander–Reiten component of a Krull–Schmidt category with four suitable properties. Then we relate mod sp(Λ) with a subcategory of morphisms between projective Λ-modules and with some category of prinjective modules. We prove that the right-peak algebras associated with representations and corepresentations of p-equipped posets satisfy the mentioned condition for all prime number p
