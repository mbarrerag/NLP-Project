# Aesthetic mediation, ephemeral communities and participatory art strategies from collaborative practices in the 2007 and 2015 MDEs

The International Art Meetings of Medellín or MDE, 2007 2011 and 2015 were artistic events that were created for the city of Medellín with the intention of renewing the languages of the art of the city and promoting artistic practices that manage relations in the public. Thus, changes are made in the institutional horizons of artistic practices to include, from a micropolitical and postautonomous conception, processes of work with communities. Thus our objective is to elucidate the aesthetic construction methods of the political and the being-together based on the artistic practices and the modes of participation citizens made possible in the Museum of Antioquia, in the framework of the MDE 2007, 2011 and 2015, and the consolidation of a participatory strategy as a mechanism of social and community management based on a hermeneutic of the different traditions of the aesthetic and contemporary politics relationship. We work from an applied aesthetic methodology (where we carried out information collection via interview and documentary archive) with a hermeneutic approach.
