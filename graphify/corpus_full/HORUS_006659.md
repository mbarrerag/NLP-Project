# Influence of personality traits of managers and owners on their financing decisions through alternative sources in cultural and creative SMEs of Bogotá

The recent financial literature proposes exploring the area of behavioral finance to explain the financing decisions in the SMEs since their approaches are better suited to decision-making in these companies. Consequently, the purpose of this research is to explain the influence of the personality traits of managers and owners on their financing decisions through alternative sources in cultural and creative SMEs in Bogotá. A quantitative, cross-sectional approach methodology is used, whereby two questionnaires are applied to a sample of 372 cultural and creative SMEs in the city of Bogotá and classification trees are constructed. Among the findings of this research it is emphasized that the five great personality traits influence the probability of deciding to use alternative and traditional finance, although in the case of the traditional opening to experience feature is the only one that has no influence on them. In addition, the impact of personality traits on such probability is less in the case of alternative financing compared to the traditional.
