# INCLUSION OF ARCOIRIS TRUCK FOOD (ONCORHYNCHUS MYKISS)

The American red crab [Procambarus clarkii] is an invasive species, whose presence is confirmed in the central zone of Colombia. In the south of the United States, its natural distribution area, is an important aquaculture resource; however, high environmental impacts have been documented in areas where it has been introduced, precisely because of its high capacity for colonization and the consequent impact on native species. The growing record of its presence in different systems, in the center of the country, suggests an accelerated expansion pattern and assumes that, as it has been presented in other countries, negative effects on the general structure of these ecosystems of the altiplano, of not applying mechanisms that allow some level of control. The possibility of controlling the expansion of the red crab by manual eradication is a viable alternative, provided that some type of use is validated; an economic stimulus to motivate fish meal, which is used for the extraction by fishing. The process to obtain flour that serves as a protein source for concentrates in aquaculture is an option to be assessed. The purpose of the work was to evaluate the inclusion of crab flour, as a partial replacement of fish meal, in a concentrate formulated for the terminal treatment stage of the Pile. ]. Between the treatments, and for the variables evaluated, there were no statistically significant differences between the treatments. It is concluded that, until the length reached by the fish, inclusion at a level of 6% crab flour is feasible as a partial replacement of other fish foods.
