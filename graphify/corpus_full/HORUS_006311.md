# Burnout in psychotherapists of a Psychological Care Service: an exploratory look oriented to the construction of alternatives for its management from the perspective of those who experience it

The Burnout is a problem experienced by psychotherapists who can be addressed from a psychosocial perspective, from which this arises from aspects of the work and institutional order, which interact with personal and psychological aspects of the psychotherapist, integrating the different roles in which it is developed. However, the research of the Burnout in psychotherapists is scarce and the studies carried out give information that does not transcend interventional actions. In this sense, the present research is oriented to offer a mixed exploratory sequential approach to the Burnout, oriented by a quantitative phase, that collects information from the use of the Inventario de Burnout de Psychologists, and a qualitative phase, in which two focal groups oriented by the methodology the Cores of Social Education (NES) are developed. As results, it is offered to a Psychological Care Service (SAP) and to the psychotherapists participants as well as to the psychological performance of the current state in the Burn, knowledge about the triggering factors, protective factors and impacts, as a compendium of alternatives for their management; all of this with the potential to establish clear interventions of the psychological care (SAP) and psychotherapy and psychotherapy participants. search on Burnout in psychotherapy is excluded and the studies carried out out information that does not have an interventional action.
