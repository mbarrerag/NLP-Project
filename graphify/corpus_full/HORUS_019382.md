# Optimization of the plant design of copper oxide recovery by foaming flotation.

In a profit plant, all processes that require a mineral for extraction are carried out. Starting from the primary phase of crushing and grinding until the final stage of concentration. One of the most relevant aspects in the profit process is the concentration method that is selected to extract a mineral, since this allows the process to achieve optimal results and the recovery of the mineral is desired in terms of extraction. In this specific case, the method in plant for the concentration of copper oxidized minerals is the foaming float. In the initial design that was applied to the plant, certain technical criteria of great relevance were not taken into account for the recovery process, which caused the flotation to have not the expected results and, therefore, the enriched mineral to go to the tails. This optimization allowed to increase the metallurgical indexes of plant. In recovery, passage from 4.9 to 67%, the reason of concentration of 2,79 to 6,4 and finally the reason of enrichment went from 0.4 to 5,4.
