# Antonio Gramsci and the Italian "Risorgimento". Towards a history of the groups and subaltern classes in the Prison Journals

The doctoral thesis presents a proposal for historical reading regarding the groups and the subaltern classes in the singular process of the Italian Risorgimento in the nineteenth century. We take into consideration the critical and thematic edition of the Cuadernos de la Jail written by Antonio Gramsci between 1929 and 1935. To develop the thesis we have followed the concern for common sense in the groups and subaltern classes. This is a comprehensive history that considers the individual and collective autonomy of these groups and classes in Italian history and, in particular, in the foundation of the Italian State. . The doctoral theses present a proposal for a historical reading of groups and subordinate classes in the singular processes of the Italian management in the 19th century.
