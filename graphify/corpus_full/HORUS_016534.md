# MEDIATICAL REPRESENTATIONS ON SEXUAL DIVERSITY IN THE PRESS WRITTEN. THE CASES OF THE DIARIOUS PATRIA AND Q. WAS IT IN MANIZALS- CALLS (1985-2015)

This article analyses the news publications issued by the written press of Manizales (Caldas, Colombia) (the daily homeland and the daily Q'hubo) during the years 1985-2015, with the purpose of identifying social representations presented and characterized by these media around the construction of sexual dissidences in the city. In light of the critical analysis of the discourse, we focus the discussion through Van Dijk's ideological theory and the approaches on the normalized attitudes and actions of construction of other negatives. The key words that were selected to carry out the press tracing were LGBT community/population, gays, lesbians, homosexuals, transvestites, transsexuals, prostitutes, sex workers, as well as any kind of colloquial enunciation that was used to name LGBT people, sexual dissidences and sex workers
