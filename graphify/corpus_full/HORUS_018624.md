# Because you already buried your dead man.

The following paper explores the question of (non) farewells in times of contagion from three cases of study in Ecuador, Canada and Colombia. It is with these examples, placed on the page, that the reflection on the testimony and gender of the Farewell begins. Here, it is proposed that the fact of narrating in a testimonial manner implies in itself a “presentation”, a selection, a memory and forget and, therefore, a use of various literary devices that blur the boundaries between reality and fiction, and also that dichotomy. .
