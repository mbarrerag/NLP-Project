# From information auditing to knowledge auditing: a fundamental reform to the curriculum plans of systems engineering and public accounting programs

The primary objective of this study is to propose a reform to the curricular plans of information systems auditing courses of both the systems engineering and the public accounting degree programs from Colombia. There is currently a need to evolve from information auditing to knowledge auditing that would allow senior management to evaluate the current and future state of their processes. Themes and methodologies currently applied are analyzed for higher education institutions in Colombia. A proposal for the curricular reform of information systems auditing courses is designed, which is oriented towards a knowledge audit that allows organizations to both improve its control capacities and to adapt to changes in the environment, in addition to training more suitable engineers and accountants. Based on the proposal designed, it is concluded that its content covers the topics and practices that current organizations require to achieve their strategic objectives in auditing
