# Impact on Empocaldas S.A. E.S.P.'s income from the implementation of micro-measurers replacement programs for the reduction of unaccounted water – case study

This paper assesses the financial impact on the public service company Empocaldas S.A. E.S.P. following the implementation of micro-measurers replacement programs with the objective of reducing the unaccounted water (ANC). Empocaldas, which operates in the department of Caldas, faces a high rate of ANC, which significantly affects its income and operational efficiency. The research analyzes the volumes of water recovered from the replacement of obsolete and deteriorated micro-measurers, and measures the effect of this measure on the income of the company. The study uses a quantitative methodology with water consumption data of subscribers that have been intervened between 2023 and 2024, comparing the volumes before and after the replacement of the measurement instrument. Additionally, the perception of users regarding the initiative is explored, given that the cost of micro-measurers is assumed by the subscribers. who were intervened between 2023 and 2024, comparing volumes before and after the replacement of the measuring instrument. Additionally, the perception of users regulating the initiative is explored, given that the cost of the micro-meters is Born by subscribers, which may generate resistance. The results show an increase in billed volumes after the intervention, demonstrating the program's effectiveness in collecting NRW and enhancing company avenue. It is included that while the program presents clear economic benefits, it is essential to enhance communication and management use perfections to maximize acceptance and effectiveness
