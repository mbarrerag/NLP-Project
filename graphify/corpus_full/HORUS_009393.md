# EPHIMERUS: A SYSTEM FOR GRAPHICAL GENERATION OF RESEARCH REPORTING: EPHIMERUS: A SYSTEM FOR GRAPHICAL GENERATION OF RESEARCH REPORTING

The universities are currently strengthening the field of research from the academy. However, the spectacularity of the results is opaque with the amount of information. A strategy, is to work to present in an easy and more understandable way the achievements of institutional research.Based on the information offered by Colciencias called science in figures [7], the institutions propose to generate a pilot, with some of the main reports, that transcend graphic presentations, and that are easily compatible with other institutional bodies. As a result, Ephimerus, a semiautomatic system, emerges to implement the consultations and generate the reports graphically, easily and amicably, so that they can be absorbed into the daily work of the administrative team that, within the institution, are in charge of the research
