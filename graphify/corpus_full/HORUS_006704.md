# Water quality for human consumption and possible association with kidney disease in the department of Tolima

Water pollution is associated with diseases that impact human health, due to the presence of microorganisms and chemical substances. This encourages the implementation of surveillance systems in public health to ensure the quality of water destined for human consumption and reduce the risks to health. In Colombia, both microbiological pollutants and chemical substances are monitored, including metals, to detect levels that exceed the limits established. This research focuses on the possible negative effects on health, especially kidney disease, associated with water consumption in the department of Tolima. To identify statistics and published research on water quality and its possible association with the occurrence of renal disease in the inhabitants of municipalities adjacent to the Magdalena river in the department of Tolima. The data of the Risk Index for the Quality of Water for human consumption (IRCA) were analyzed, reported by the SIVICAP between 2015 and 2022 for the department of Tolima, together with the CIE 10 diagnoses of the renal disease group (N17-N19).
