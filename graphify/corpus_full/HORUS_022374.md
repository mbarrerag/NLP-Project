# THE VIRTUAL HOTEL OPERATOR: A NEW BUSINESS MODALITY IN THE ACCOMMODATION INDUSTRY

The aim of this study is to explore how the business modality of Hotel Virtual Operator in Colombia is developing. With the review of press and specialized literature it was found that Asian countries such as India, Indonesia and the Philippines are pioneers in the creation of this type of companies and that in Colombia a group of young entrepreneurs has entered it, under the name of Ayenda. In addition to the written sources, interviews with its creators were analyzed and the certificate of existence and legal representation of the company and some of the affiliated hotels was obtained. To know the perception of the users about the service offered in Medellín by the Colombian company, the portals of Booking and Expedia were reviewed. It was found that the majority of users are Colombian nationals and that they consider a good cost-benefit relationship in the service received. In future research lines it is recommended to follow up the plans of improvement of hotels based on user feedback and also can establish a comparison of the perception of the clients regarding the service among Virtual Operators of Hotels and platforms of collaborative economy such as Airbnb
