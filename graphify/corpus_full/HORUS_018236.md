# MODEL FOR EVALUATING THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE THIRD UNIVERSITY MISSION: A APPROACH FROM ORGANIZATIONAL LEARNING.

The development of the third university mission is a trend in the management of universities. This mission is related to the transfer of knowledge and some currents the association with the support and development of entrepreneurship. Organizational learning is widely studied in literature as a dynamic capacity to generate competitive advantage in organizations, even some authors affirm that it is the most important source to achieve it. For the development of the third mission in universities, dynamic abilities such as organizational learning, poorly studied construct in literature, are required. The article proposes and validates, with structural equations, a model that measures the capacity of universities in the development of the third mission, in terms of entrepreneurship results, taking as a variable the mediator of organizational learning. An instrument for surveying 19 entrepreneurship centers in the Bogotan universities is designed and validated. The instrument presents the reliability and validity of the construct with statisticians that measure the quality of the relationships between factors and questions. Through structural equations, it is found that organizational learning in the IES is determined by individual learning and this in turn by the sources of learning and organizational culture, being organized together with this determinant
