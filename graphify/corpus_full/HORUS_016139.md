# `VICTIMS IN PEACE AGREEMENTS: OLD ACTORS OF THE COLOMBIAN CASE

Colombian history has shown a phenomenon of political violence since the beginning of the nineteenth century. The various disputes and wars have been justified by wealth, land tenure and their division, political-administrative ideology, religion, education, communism, politicization, among other reasons. The consequences are the creation of scenarios of destruction and death, material losses and an environment of cyclical and even omniscient conflict, where the harsh reality of violent deaths is reality, as well as forced displacement, expropriation of property and land, and the forgetfulness of the victims.The various actors and governments of the turn have sought solutions, with the aim of stopping the fratricidal onslaught, for which it has resorted to amnesties, humanitarian exchanges and peace agreements.However, how has it been assumed to the victims from the perspective of the peace agreements and the governmental normative framework?
