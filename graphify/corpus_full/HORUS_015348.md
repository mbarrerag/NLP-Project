# DEVELOPMENT OF A NUGET BASED ON PLUMUDA PAST (OPISTHONEMA SPP.) AND CARDUMA PAST (CETTENGRAULIS MYSTICETUS)

Based on the availability and nutritional characteristics of the species of the Colombian Pacific, the plumuda and the carduma, methods of product development were applied that allowed to obtain a nugget with the nutritional properties appropriate for the infant population
