# A stochastic model in resilient agri-food supply chains with export scope. Case study avocado Hass

The resilience capacity allows the productive chain to prepare, respond and recover before the occurrence of unexpected disruptive events, maintaining continuity in operations at desired levels and reducing the likelihood of interruption. Currently, it is recognized that resilience integrates a set of capacities such as: flexibility, agility, collaboration, adaptability, visibility, ingenuity and speed that make it dynamic. Study how resilient an agri-food supply chain can be becomes complex given the intrinsic characteristics of the same: climate, perishable character, seasonality, supply peaks and long delivery times.These characteristics generate the need to make a probabilistic analysis of occurrence of possible Interruptions, which has been replaced in this research with the elaboration of a model of stochastic linear optimization of two stages.The proposed model was validated in the productive chain of the avocado Hass, in the anti-oqueño area in the municipality of San Vicente de Ferrer: allowing to recreate interrupt scenarios.
