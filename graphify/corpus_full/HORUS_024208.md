# Manifestations in the mouth cavity and face associated with COVID-19

Abstract Objective: To describe and analyze the reports of cases and published studies, in which a possible relationship between the COVID-19 and mucocutaneous lesions in the oral cavity and the face is presented. review in the databases of pubmed, google academic, proquest and medline complete. 56 articles were selected that reported mucocutaneous manifestations in the oral cavity and the face associated with COVID-19. 91 cases were found with mucocutaneous manifestations in the oral cavity and face, associated with COVID-19. In 18 % of patients there were facial and oral lesions. The cheeks were the most affected areas (with 16 %), and oral lesions, the most affected site was the tongue (26 %). According to the findings found, the presence of mucocutaneous manifestations in the face and oral cavity were associated with infection by SARS-CoV-2. It is expected that tissues with greater expression of the ACE2 and TMPRSS2 are more susceptible to infection by the CNS-cocutaneous disease
