# More than the consultation.'' Contributions from family medicine to cure and heal.

Title of the paper: Beyond the practice. Contributions from the family medicine to cure and heal Work type: Other: Educational experience Axis: Education (Education for social transformation): seminareeducacion.hilando@ucaldas.edu.co.Authors: - Nora Badoui Rodríguez (nbadoui@unal.edu.co), specialist in family medicine - Catalina Ascanio Noreña (cascanion@unal.edu.co), specialist in family medicine Topics derived from the presentations: Education and social transformation Abstract The medicine has become a professional practice with technical emphasis ¿scientific detachment from the socio-humanistic character, causing a huge fracture between the disease and the person suffering; even so, it is expected that the medical conditions, contextually located and humanized, are allowed by the medical elements (McWhinney, 1995; Cruess, 2004; WONCA, 2010). It is recognized that the health needs of life in peace; the armed conflict directly impacts on the development of individuals and the regions (National Health Institute, National Health Observatory, 2017). It is a series of clinical cases around a family and its members that invite to explore and analyze the conditions in which people live, the expectations and social tasks in front of the individual's life moment, as these interact to determine the health process disease and possible proposals for intervention. The learnings are: a) understanding education as a possibility of transformation requires identifying the tools proper to knowledge that allow it to become an agent of change, b) The clinical case should allow the student to visualize himself participating in a situation, that although hypothetical, it bears similarity with the real professional practice, c) dialogue with other knowledge is fundamental for the integral approach to health.
