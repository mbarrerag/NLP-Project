# Versar para investigar: Body and presence in the classroom

This degree work seeks through: (1) the review and the account of my history within the educational-academic, community and artistic spaces I have inhabited, (2) the constitution of 3 axioms that go guiding this research (epistemological adolescence, verse to investigate and search for one's own voice) and (3) the writing of verses and improvisation of tenths spinels as a research device, putting in the center my body of artist - teacher - researcher as an anchor element for the construction of a classroom in which presence, body and community are fundamental axes of a pedagogical proposal. Finally, I establish a dialogue with other researchers who in turn put experience and bodyhood in a central place from where they propose an alternative to traditional educational methodologies. .
