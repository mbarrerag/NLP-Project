# COLOMBIA: A SCHOOL TEACHING ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION WITH FACTS, THE DAY OF ARCHITECTURE, TURIN, OCTUBRE 2023. ISSN 2284-1369. AVAILABLE EN HTTPS://ILGIORNALEDELLARCHITETTURA.COM/2023/10/12/COLOMBIA-UNA-SCUOLA-CHE-INSEGNA-LEDUCAZIONE-AMBIENTALE-COI-FATTI/

Thanks to the passionate collaboration of teachers, students, volunteers, organizations, the Town Hall and the local community of San Jerónimo, small town a few kilometers from Medellín, the public school ¿El Rincón¿ was transformed into the first sustainable school of Colombia. The construction developed through a sustainable building course. Tens of students from Mexico, Argentina, Ecuador, Peru and Colombia participated in lectures that united theory and practice, addressing various themes: systems of construction, reuse of materials, water treatment, biodiversity, production of green energy, food sovereignty. The course was accompanied by a series of free workshops, with open debates to the community, to involve citizens in the construction process, with the aim of learning some basic principles related to holistic sustainability. The project was commissioned by the TAGMA civil association, dedicated to the development of educational, participatory and sustainable projects in Latin America, which has already implemented a system of nine environmental classrooms in Argentina, Uruguay, Chile, Peru, and Colombia.
