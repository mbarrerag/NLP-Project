# DESIGN OF AN INTERVENTION OF NAVIGATION OF PATIENTS WITH CANCER IN TREATMENT

With the increase of cancer treatments in outpatient settings, patients experience symptoms in their homes. Through navigational approaches, self-efficacy of the patient is promoted, which allows them to influence their own behavior and activate a self-care system. In addition, nursing roles are explored in a holistic model of patient-centred care, promoting leadership and interprofessional work. Design and validate an intervention in navigation of patients with cancer in treatment to improve self-management behaviors and symptom load. Pilot study that welcomes the proposal of Sidane and Braden and includes the accuracy of the problem, definition and validation of the intervention. 10 experts who analyzed the main components of the intervention with the criteria clarity, precision, understanding, relevance and relevance of the content. The note attributed to each of the components and from this, the weighted average of the options indicated in the Likert scale with variation of 1 to 5 points.
