# Causes of water shortages for human consumption in Yopal

This final master's work analyzes the causes of the lack of water supply in the city of Yopal Casanare, with the aim of identifying: the direct causes, critical problems and the immersal consequences that intensify this problem. The study uses the methodologies of analysis of social networks and analysis of priority problems, to identify the actors with the greatest influence on the problem, through the use of semi-structured interviews, to collect information from each one on causal relations between different problems and aspects, with the purpose of recognizing the critical problems that require priority attention to focus efforts and increase efficiency in the allocation of resources. The results show that as key actors to the municipal mayor's office of Yopal, the Governorship of Casanare and the Veedure of Water for Yopal. In addition, it was identified that the critical problems are: inefficiency in the planning and execution of different solutions, lack of institutional coordination, and low governance and increased corruption. The development of this study contributes analytically to the decision-making in the search for solutions to the problem posed . rupture.
