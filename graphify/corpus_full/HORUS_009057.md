# D-OPTIMUM DESIGNS FOR COVARIANCE ANALYSIS MODELS

The optimal designs are useful in various areas of research because they provide the points of the experimental region where an experiment should be carried out in order to obtain good inferences regarding the parameters of the model under consideration. In this work, it is intended to develop and implement a methodology for the search for D-optimal designs in linear models involving both quantitative and qualitative variables. These designs are obtained by maximizing the determinant of Fisher's information matrix associated with the models of covariance analysis. For this purpose, two scenarios will be considered, one where the experimental region associated with the treatments is the same and the other where the experimental regions are different. By developing a program in R, the different D-Optim designs are found, their properties are analyzed and the D-efficiencies of the designs used in the literature are calculated for two examples with real data
