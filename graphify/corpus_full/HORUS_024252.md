# Digital educational resources accessible to students of secondary education with visual impairment and related to the teaching of chemistry. A documentary review.

This paper considers education as an indispensable practice for the construction of knowledge, scientific training and ethical training in the integral development of human beings, since it is part of an activity of transformation of men in search of the construction of more just, free and happy societies. Therefore, this work is responsible for making a systematic documentary review of some Digital Educational Resources (RED) for the population with visual disabilities, specifically in the area of natural and chemical sciences, in order to glimpse the possible gap at the level of accessibility and education in this population. Thus, initially the concepts related to the use of ICTs, Digital Educational Resources, web accessibility and others related to the concept of visual disability are reviewed. The pedagogical actions framed in inclusive education and the Natural Sciences Skills that seek the training of scientifically literate citizens are also discussed. The concepts related to the use of the ICTs, Digital Educational Resources, web accessibility and others related to the concept of visual disability are also performed.
