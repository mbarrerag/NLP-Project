# Relationship between child adverse experiences and stress coping strategies in health workers

The adverse experiences in childhood (EAI) negatively impact cognitive, psychological and emotional development. The coping strategies act as mediators between the IADs and different diseases. At present there is a theoretical and research gap in relation to the relationship of the adverse experiences of childhood and coping strategies in health workers. To determine the relationship between the IADs and the use of coping strategies in the stressful situations in the working environment of a group of health workers of different IPS. An analytical quantitative study of retrospective cut, with consecutive sampling. The instruments used are the inventory of traumatic experiences in childhood Colombian version and the scale of coping strategies – modified. A descriptive and bivariate statistical analysis with the software IBM® SPSS® Statistics. 50% of the women experiment more than 9 IADs and 50% of the men more than 12 IADs. The most frequent IADs are physical, emotional and general traumatic events. The most used coping strategies are the solution of problems, search for social support and positive reassessment. stressful situations in the work environment of a group of health workers from different Health Care Providers.
