# Evaluation of the effect of torrefaction and slow pyrolysis on the development of porosity of special activated carbons obtained from lignocellulosic biomass.

In this thesis we studied the effect of torrefaction and slow pyrolysis in the development of porosity and structural changes oriented to the obtaining of activated carbons from a lignocellulosic residue (palm stock). To this end, an analysis of the effect of torrefaction at different temperatures was carried out as a previous treatment stage, proposing a torrential route – slow pyrolysis – activation. From this, changes were found in the dynamics of devolatilization and recomposition of the material, finding that the previous torrefaction favors the decarbonilation reactions reflected in losses of compounds and functional groups via CO2, which are not evident from the direct pyrolysis of the material. Additionally, a detailed characterization of the biochar obtained at different pyrolysis temperatures (220, 280, 350, 550 and 700°C) was carried out, and the analysis of the functional structure of the pyrolysis was made as a result of the functional effect of the pyrolysis process.
