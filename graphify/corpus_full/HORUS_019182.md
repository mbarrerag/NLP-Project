# NAMÉU AND THE ABUELO OF THE BOCANA: AQUATIC WORLD/ECHOLOGY SCHEDULE/THE COUNCIL WORD OF THE PHÉMENEM NA PEOPLE

In the framework of the research project led by Professor Consuelo de Veneechea, oral memory and practices of the muinane language in the Colombian Amazon, were we in the malaca of two staltillos of the late José Daniel Suárez with his uncle, Aurelio Suárez, both men of the Feenem na people of the clan k m jo `Gente de Maguaré?. Our conversation mainly dealt with the importance of one’s own language and his learning in the family: how does he start the process of learning one’s own language? Do we ask him, asking him, did he answer emphatically Aurelio. Does not the child speak, does not invoke, but he already understands. You say, “He’s a nyg?”, aay [eye], n j mee, or [oreja,], until he is going to express it.
