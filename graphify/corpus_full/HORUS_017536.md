# Inhabiting and being inhabited: Activity in the domestic space

This research aims to analyze the activities that are developed in the housing in six cases of study, of closed set and single-family housing, type apartment and type house to understand the complexity in the habitat in the housing, through an ergonomic approach with ethnographic perspective in the city of Bogotá D.C. Through two visits to each case of study an architectural survey was carried out and the narrative of a daily day of each of the inhabitants of the home through social cartography in the framework of the pandemic by the COVID-19. The results show that the activity in the domestic space is framed in vital activities, care and control, work, relations, storage and modifications. This reflects the existence of three types of space in all cases: the physical space, the place and the existential space in which a series of spatial tissues are identified specific to each human and non human inhabitant. Thus, the theory of activity in the work is translated into the context of the domestic space and the being inhabited in this. It concludes that the activity is a consequence of the sociocultural context of the inhabitants, and that, also the inhabitation of the housing is an activity of the sing social cartography in the framework of the COVID-19 pandemic. The results show that the activity in the domestic space is framed by essential, work, relationships, storage, care and control, and modification activities.
