# COMPARISON BETWEEN NI-CE CATEGORIES IN POWDER AND MONOLITE FOR THE TRANSFORMATION OF GREEN EFFECTIVE GAS

This paper presents the results of the comparison of conversion, selectivity, stability and coke formation during the RSM (CH4/CO2 = 1, 600oC, 8h) with prior reduction, of Ni-Ce/Mg-Al catalysts in powder vs. immobilized in monoliths of cordierite, both systems with variable loads of Ce
