# CO-LABORATIVE-LAB - Experimental and Virtual Laboratory of BIOCLIMATICS

1. The project Architecture and physics of the Bioclimatic consisted of an encounter Art - Science between professors and students of the School of Architecture and professors and students of the Department of Physics and developed a methodology of measurement, analysis and simulation of the thermodynamic behavior of two interior spaces of the university campus obtaining knowledge and a methodology of measurement analysis of the data and simulation for the improvement of the thermal environmental quality of these spaces. 2. The Kotaiki project 2 A pilot for the active, collaborative and researchive learning of the Bioclimatic Architecture in the University Campus, in development, replicates the interdisciplinary encounter now in the classroom of Bioclimatic Architecture of the School of Architecture, replicating the conversations about the thermodynamics and the bioclimatics and the physical concepts that make clearer their understanding and the physical nature of the Architecture, the space, geometry, the atmosphere, etc. From the methodology designed in the first project is introduced the deep analysis of what means the results of the simulations that generates learning and immediately replicates about the students projects and interest in the research on the bioclimatic strategies and their functioning
