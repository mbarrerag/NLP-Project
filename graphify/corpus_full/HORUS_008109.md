# The Political and Electoral Projection of the FARC Party

The FARC political party arises as a result of the peace agreement between the Colombian government and the guerrilla organization Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia – People's Army. The creation of this party makes possible the political reintegration of former combatants, the renunciation of the armed struggle and the transition of this organization to legal political action. After the foundation of the party, it is necessary to undertake the search for the consolidation and survival of this party. The aim of this work is to analyze what is the political and electoral projection of the FARC party. To this end, a qualitative investigation was proposed, through the documentary review and the conduct of interviews. In this regard, it is argued that in the context of its political transition, different factors that form a hostile political environment, which, together with some difficulties arising from its configuration and political performance, affect the possibilities of this political party. difficulties timing from its political configuration and performance, affect its fate, and end up undermining the possibilities of this political party. Give this complex scenario, the political and electoral projects of the FARC party are not very promising, which could lead to its eventual atomization and extinction
