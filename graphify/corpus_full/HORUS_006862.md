# Structural analysis and assembly process of the work: the olympus of the gods, Oscar Navarro

This document presents a structural analysis of the work The Olimpo of the Gods of Oscar Navarro, as well as a strategic plan that allows an optimal assembly of the work. All this from the need to provide resources and tools that allow us to know the work in depth and have a good development when directing it. The research is carried out in 4 phases, historical context, context of the work, the analysis of each of its movements in three fundamental pillars, orchestration, form and outstanding musical elements and the assembly plan that is carried out with this work. The realization of this document was intended beyond the structural analysis, knowledge and understanding of each of the musical elements present in this work for a cleaner interpretation. The results lead to highlighting the importance of knowing in depth a work of this level from different strategies that allow for an adequate performance to direct this type of repertoire .
