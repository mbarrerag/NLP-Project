# EFFECT OF THE SOLVENT ON THE LOVED VIBRATIONAL BAND OF THE N-METHYLACETAMITIDE MOLECULA

1. Introduction and objectives Infrared spectroscopy is a type of vibrational spectroscopy that has been widely used for structural characterisation of molecules, since it provides information of the associated functional groups and their interactions.Each functional group has a particular vibration frequency that is affected by changes in the chemical environment, this change can be produced by gain or loss of interactions of hydrogen bond type, by resonance effects and in general, by any factor that alters interactions in the molecular system.[1,2] 2. Computational Methodology In this work we studied two different methodology, dynamics and static, for the determination of the amide band I of the N-Methylacetamide molecule, with different hamlitonians. The exploration of the conformational space was carried out using classical dynamics. 3. Results It was observed that the dynamic methodology is better in terms of the instantaneous modes; however, when using the static methodology with the xtb method, the results are comparable with the experimental methods; being able to reproduce the experimental trend using as a set of test 5000 micro-solved molecules for 7 different solvents. 4.
