# INSURANCE CARE IN ILLEGAL PSYCOACTIVE SUBSTANCES WITH PROBLEM CONSUMPTION TRAINECTORY

The latest global drug report of UNODC states that about 275 million people worldwide used psychoactive substances in the last year, plus more than 36 million people presented some disorder associated with substance use, so they claim that this is a problem that is increasing 1. For 2019, in Colombia, the prevalence of consumption of substances of illegal use in people between 12 and 65 years was 9.7%. 2.6% of this group reported the need for help to stop consuming some psychoactive substance 2. Bogotá reported 9054 cases of abuse in general population.3 Description of the problem: The use of substances is considered as a problem of mental health, of which it requires an interdisciplinary team to be able to intervene properly and in a comprehensive way. Within this inter-disciplinary team is nursing. For this reason, from the nursing practice in mental health, the experience of the practice of the nursing in mental health, with the prevention of psycho-social care and with the help of the participants during the periods of December 2021 and June 2022, in subjects with problematic consumption of psychoactive substances in the process of rehabilitation, was sought to system of life-related, with the prevention and with the help of the knowledge and psychological care. The educational and psychotherapeutic accompaniment should focus on the abuse of psychoactive substances as an externalizing behavior, as a consequence of social vulnerability and low psychological resilience, as well as the enhancement of personal, community and social resources focused on the individual. Limitations and/or successes: Within the limitations it was found that due to the physical sequelae of substance use especially at the cognitive level of some caregivers it was impossible to achieve a compilation of their experiences. As it is correct that the strategies implemented by the teacher and students favored communication and therapeutic relationship with the subjects of care, which allowed the collection of the information contained in this systematization.
