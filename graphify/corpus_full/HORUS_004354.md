# METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL FOR THE ASSESSMENT OF AGRO-PECUARY PRODUCTION SYSTEMS FOR PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION AND TERRITORIAL PEACE

The productive inclusion and territorial peace proposed since the construction of governance processes, around the practices of producers and their forms of agrotechnological management, as presented in the first chapters of the book, requires the characterization and understanding of the agricultural production systems that allow their assessment and account of the main restrictions that these systems of production face, as well as their potentialities, competitiveness, multifunctionality, sustainability and governance, contributing to the management of the high levels of risk to which they are exposed, to the decisions made by the farmer based on his knowledge of agrotechnological management, the social fabric constructed; and from other capacities derived from his productive experience, which are part of his cultural heritage.In this chapter, a methodological proposal is presented for this characterization, understanding and evaluation of the agricultural systems, through four indicators that account for the restrictions given by the biophysical conditions of the production system, this is the technological receptivity index (ITT), the productive lines and the management that makes of the same, this is the management index of the agricultural systems, given by the knowledge of the agricultural system and the applied technology that are necessary for the expression of the production culture, the productive system
