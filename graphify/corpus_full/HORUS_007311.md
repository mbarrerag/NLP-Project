# The Sun in the classroom: a didactic proposal for meaningful learning in science

This paper explores the teaching of science from an integral perspective, focused on the Sun and its influence on the natural environment. The difficulties faced by secondary school students in understanding scientific phenomena are analyzed and how the orientation of the teacher, combined with innovative didactic strategies, can improve their learning. Through practical activities and interactive resources, the aim is to connect theory with practice by promoting scientific curiosity and the development of critical thinking skills. The results show an increase in the understanding of key concepts and a greater environmental awareness among students. .
