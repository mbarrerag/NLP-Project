# The suspension and/or restriction of fundamental constitutional rights during the state of emergency “State of Economic, Social and Ecological Emergency” Pandemia by Covid19.

In the research project we carried out an analysis of the suspension and/or restriction of fundamental rights during the pandemic by Covid19, for this purpose, we will take academic texts and judgments, with the aim of defining, first, the ordinary and extraordinary powers of police, the state of emergency and its possible consequences. From here, we examine whether, in Colombia during 2020, there was a suspension or limitation of guarantees; whether the measures taken and which were heavily involved were subject to constitutional abstract control. We also study the role of the Constitutional Court, which has the function of exercising legal control over the acts declaring the state of emergency and those that carry it out. To this end, we make a case law line of abstract control judgments.
