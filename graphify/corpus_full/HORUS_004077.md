# Water security in the supply from the Morrosquillo aquifer: water management under a climate change scenario at 2040

Water safety appears in the international context as a concept that, despite having different approaches, emphasizes the need for water supply in quantity and quality sufficient for human populations and economic sectors. This research investigates how water safety will be at 2040 in the hydrogeological unit of the Morrosquillo aquifer?, based on an exercise of territorial perspective to 2040 considering a medium-term climate change scenario. Starting from a diagnostic exercise, it is realized the current state of the territory in the municipalities that contain the water unit within its jurisdiction, allowing to establish trends and propose a prospective scenario. The work was carried out from official secondary information and consultation to experts. This information was processed through SmartPLS 4.0 and ArcGis 10.4 software, and the platform The Prospective in the different stages of the research.
