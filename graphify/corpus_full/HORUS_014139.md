# Strengthening international cooperation and doctoral training of the project related to the Study of Dynamics in Differential Equations: Stability and Controllability of Solutions

This project aims to strengthen the doctoral training capacities of mathematics students, both from the National University of Colombia, Manizales Headquarters, and from the Federal University of Pernambuco in Brazil and the Federal University of Alagoas Brazil together with the generation of new knowledge. The main essence of the initiative is to continue the research and training work that emerges from the Hermes Nro project. 57774, as well as to advance the doctoral training of two students committed to this process and the publication of results of problems related to the study of controlability, stability and good approach of partial differential equations of evolution that model the propagation of waves of different amplitudes in dispersive media. It is of particular importance to highlight that publications will be subject to review in renowned journals with a notable impact factor in the field of pure mathematics. This approach is implemented with the purpose of avoiding and counteracting the current problem associated with publications ``predator's. It is essential to transmit a clear message to the academic community, especially those dedicated to the study and development of mathematics.
