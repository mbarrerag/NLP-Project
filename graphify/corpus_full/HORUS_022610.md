# Reverse migration in time for acoustic and visco acoustic media, by pseudospectral methods of the wave equation and the fractional wave equation in two dimensions.

The RTM migration or reverse migration over time is a method in which, the complete wave equation is used and not approximations. In this thesis, a unified mathematical approach is developed and numerical algorithms are implemented for the correct understanding of the pseudospectral method of the generalised Fourier, the simulation of sources and receivers for the acoustic wave and acoustic viscose, the correct implementation of the image condition or “imagining”, in addition to the application of the absorbing borders hybrid ABC, PML and NPML, where the former works with a paraxial approximation of the acoustic wave and the following two with a “treaching” of the coordinates. In the process of RTM migration two conditions of “imagin” are used, applying migration to several synthetic models, both for acoustic wave and acoustic viscose. This allows for a free use tool for RTM migration, since commercial tools are of high cost which hinders access to students of geosciences
