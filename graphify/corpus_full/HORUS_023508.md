# SYNTHESIS AND RESISTANCE TO THE CORROSION OF ZIRCONIUM-CERIUM OXINITURS

Sillow-skinning oxynitrus of cerium-zirconium (Ce-ZrOxNy) increase the corrosion resistance of stainless steel 316L in one or two orders of magnitude, depending on the nitrogen flow used as reactive gas in the synthesis of the film. From a concentric white of Ce-Zr were deposited on AISI 316L CeOxNy-ZrOxNy films by sputtering (RF). Its morphology is evaluated by electron microscopy of sweeping (SEM), the structure by diffraction of x-ray (DRX) and the resistance to corrosion by means of the potentiodynamic polarization curves. Given the difference of potential and the presence of oxygen in the chamber of sputtering, because it is a low-skinned metal pyrophoric, the white enters ignition and forms oxide of cerium in the perimeter of the same. The formation of oxide, brings as a consequence the low adhesion and subsequent de-skinning of the films of CeOxNy deposited on AISI-LB
