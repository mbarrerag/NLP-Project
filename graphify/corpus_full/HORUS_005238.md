# SPECIAL TRIBUTARY SYSTEM IN COLOMBIAN SOCIAL SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS: ANALYSIS FOR ARTICLES TO THE APPROACH OF CIRCULAR ECONOMY

This article describes the social sector organizations in Colombia, understood as non-profit organizations, which may present a qualification, update and readmission to the Special Tax Regime, mainly those located in the Coffee Axis region based on their typologies and development of meritorious activities. As regards the methodology, it is based on the mixed integration strategy that allows for a more complete and nuanced vision of the phenomenon. Based on the findings obtained, the impact of the non-profit sector entities, caused by the new control requirements required by the Colombian National Tax and Customs Directorate (DIAN) since the 2016 tax reform, which involved a progressive reduction of these organizations in the regime from 76 per cent to 2017 and from 90 per cent to 2022, and in the Coffee Region, which integrates the departments of Caldas, Risaralda and Quindío, generated that 59 per cent of their entities remain outside the rating by losing special tax rates.
