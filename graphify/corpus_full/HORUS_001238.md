# Evaluation of corrosion of TiO_2 nanotubes synthesized by electrochemical anodizing: an application in materials engineering

In this work, nanotubes of TiO_2 were manufactured and characterized by electrochemical anodisation. The corrosion resistance of these nanotubes was evaluated in NaCl saline solutions at 3.5% wt, using advanced characterization techniques such as SEM, AFM, XRD, EDX, FTIR and Tafel polarization curves. The results show that, after 2 hours of exposure, the nanotubes presented cavities and superficial cracks, while after 8 hours significant structural degradation was observed, including collapses and fractures in the walls of the nanotubes. The EDX polarization curves showed that prolonged immersion favors the formation of a denser protective layer, reducing the rate of corrosion. The FTIR spectra revealed that, despite the exposure to NaCl, the crystalline structure of the TiO_2 remained stable. The structural structure of the EDX was the most stable. The polarisation curves showed that prolonged immersion favors the formation of a more dense protective layer, reducing the rate of corrosion.
