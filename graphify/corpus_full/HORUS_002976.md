# Geographies of university memory: analysis of memory sites at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters (1977-2006)

This analytical research seeks to analyze the places of memory related to the violation of Human Rights in the framework of the armed conflict and its forms of appropriation within the campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá (1977-2006). For its development, the methodology used was of mixed approach and was based on the activation of memories, spaces and archives that give account of the practices of resistance to the political violence of the region.
