# JUSTICE AND HEALTH EQUITY IN BOGOTA CONCEPTIONS, TERRITORIES AND REFORMS

Bogotá considered one of the most populated cities in Latin America, it embraces the concept of urban-rural health, which requires management in everyday life scenarios - home, school, work and public spaces of coexistence. The deployment and concentration of the model of health care and provision in social territories of Bogotá shows the urgent need to advocate for a more inclusive social and political participation, with a full exercise of health rights of the entire population resident in vulnerable localities of the city. This work is intended to enable the reader to understand the complexities to address categories such as social justice and health equity in our Colombian health system based on recognizing social voices; this understanding will allow to construct and redefine public health policies by determining structural adjustments to the local health care model
