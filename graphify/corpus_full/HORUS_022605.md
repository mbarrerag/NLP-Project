# ACCESSIBILITY TO PUBLIC SPACE OF MANIZALS, MEDELLIN AND QUITO, BY ANALYSIS OF RADIES OF INFLUENCE

The theoretical accessibility to the public spaces (EP) of the neighborhood, neighborhood and communal level in three Andean cities is analyzed, characterized by valley and hillside reliefs, with important restrictions for their urban expansion. The EPs of the three cities were classified, according to their extent, from which the radius of influence of each of them was calculated, which varies according to the category of the EP and is taken as a buffer around each polygon. The analysis of the coverage of the influence radios is assumed in this exercise as an indicator of accessibility to the considered EPs, from which the portions of urban areas and neighborhoods that have access to said EP were evaluated. The results show that in Manizales 42 per cent has coverage of neighborhood EP, 34% has access to neighborhood EP and 40% to communal EP. In Medellín these coverages are 46%, 70% and 87 per cent respectively, and in Quito they are 12%, 41% and 78%. An analysis per neighborhood results similar results, according to which Manizales has less access to urban EP, although it has the highest coverage of neighborhood EP, although it has the highest levels of EP
