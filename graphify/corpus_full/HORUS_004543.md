# About the radius of blessing that must be given to concrete members main reinforcement at maximun tension stress points

Note: The contents of this article were conceived as an explanation of a serious accident that occurred in the design of a concrete structure, around the end of 1970. Its recipient was the American Concrete Institute, and this is the fundamental reason why it was written in English. The reaction of the A.C.I. was not conclusive, because it seems that they required experimental verification of the phenomenon. At the Faculty of Mines we are currently carrying out an investigation, financed by Colciencias, which although it does not attempt to corroborate the distribution of tensions given in the formula (9) of the article, including the phenomena of flux and microfissuring, is closely related to that, as we try to find a rational form of design of the curved pieces, elbows, etc., of reinforced concrete, with results to date very encouraging.
