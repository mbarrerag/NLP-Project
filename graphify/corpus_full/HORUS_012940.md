# Employment Missions in Colombia: Discovering Informality, 1949-1985

Informality is a crucial concept for understanding the job market in Colombia. It has proven to be a structural phenomenon rather than a transitory one. This chapter traces the discovery and conceptualization of informality throughout four major multilateral economic missions that played an important role in evaluating and designing development policies in Colombia during the second half of the 20th century. The Currie mission in 1949, the CEPAL mission in 1954 and 1959, the ILO mission in 1970, and the Chenery mission in 1985 attest to the process in which economic experts diagnosed the Colombian economy, according a more significant place to informality and its characteristics
