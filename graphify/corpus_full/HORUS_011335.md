# Application and sensory evaluation of a hydro-etanolic extract of capuchin (Tropaeolum majus L.) in a tropical fruit and vegetable drink

Capuchin is diverse in bioactive compounds such as carotenoids, anthocyanins and glucosinolates. Due to these attributes, it is important to apply them to novel foods such as beverages. The aim of the study was to make a sensory assessment of the feasibility of the addition of capuchin extract in a drink. Solvent extraction was performed with a solute-solvent ratio of 1:10 – 1:15 and ethanol purity of 45%, 70% or 96%; antioxidant capacity was analyzed by the FRAP method and the content of total phenolic compounds by the Folin-Ciocalteu method. Additionally, three samples of drink including capuchin extract (extract content 1000, 2500 or 5000 mg kg-1) were evaluated by means of a 5-point hedonic test and a preference test for the attributes of flavour, aroma, consistency and the product in general. Samples with higher soluto-solvente ratio presented higher antioxidant capacity, while samples with ethanol purity of 45% and 70% obtained the highest content of total phenol compounds.
