# COVERING PERSON IN CRITICAL HEALTH SITUATION IN ICU (EBOOK)

The major transformations tend to follow the great challenges that life presents us. We must learn and adapt to the changes; and the pandemic of 2020 was not the exception to this, since it became the engine to reflect on the nursing practice in the intensive care units (uci). Colombia has 65445 nursing professionals, according to figures of the Directorate of Human Talent Development in Health of the Ministry of Health and Social Protection, with a nurse/patient ratio in intensive care unit of 1:5 (Arango, Peña and Vega, 2015). To have an adequate number of nurses and nurses in the uci is fundamental, considering the complexity that the care, the condition of severity of the patients and the execution of various procedures carry. The adult unit uci must have an adequate nursing team to the scientific, technical and behavioral care, since patients in these units demand much more specific and differentiated knowledge. To this extent, nurses carry with responsibility for care and monitoring of the biophysiological, cognitive and emotional variables of patients and their family; and they perform the signs of the mental changes, and the patient's health care. Chapter 1 includes the conceptualization of the adult in a critical health situation and his/her care environment. The family is also presented as a fundamental and supportive part of the patient’s recovery. Chapter 2 also includes the knowledge of the competencies that a nurse should have in this area, together with the theoretical foundation to address the patient. Finally, the humanization strategies in the patient are contemplated. Chapter 2 elaborates on the assessment and monitoring of the person in a critical health situation from the main systems that are compromised in critical patients, and addresses the types of monitoring and some classification scales used in the patient’s treatment. Chapter 3 addresses aspects related to the patient’s care with mechanical ventilation, considering the assessment, monitoring and the different ventilatory modes. Chapter 4 shows the use of sedation, analgesia and vasoactive drugs as an integral part of the management of the critical patient in the uci alteration. Chapter 5 identifies the main nursing diagnoses and the specific interventions in the main care.
