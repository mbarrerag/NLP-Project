# Rational design, synthesis and characterization of chimeric hybrids of nitrogenated and sulphurated heterocycles as potential antifungal agents.

The high rates of morbidity and mortality caused by fungal infections are intrinsically related to the limited development of antifungals and the high toxicity of these compounds. Mycotic infections are a public health problem at this time, since the drugs currently used are not fully effective due to the development of resistance, toxicity and undesirable side effects. In addition to this, the search for new pharmacological targets is a great challenge, since there are many similarities between fungal and human cells. All this limits the use of antifungal drugs in current medicine, therefore it is crucial to investigate new antifungal agents and new pharmacological targets.
