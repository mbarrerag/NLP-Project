# IMPACT OF THE AUTONOMOUS STUDY GROUPS -GEA - AS A PERMANENCE STRATEGY IN THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA HEADQUARTERS OF MEDELLIN.

With the purpose of improving the approval rates of the most repetitive subjects at the National University of Colombia (UNAL) and, consequently, reducing the abandonment due to non-voluntary academic causes; the University has launched the Autonomous Study Group (GEA) program. The GEA promote the development of autonomy and academic success in students through the accompaniment and interaction between students, peers and tutors. In this work is presented the methodology of the assessment of impacts and results of those at the Medellin Headquarters of the National University of Colombia. The data correspond to the following academic periods: 2019-2S, in which the results of the study are implemented in a face-to-face, and the periods 2020-1S, 2020-2S and 2021-1S in virtual scenarios due to the global health emergency produced by the COVID-19. It is important to clarify that a period corresponds to a semester of study, therefore, its designation does not make use of the first or second semester of the year (1S or 2S).
