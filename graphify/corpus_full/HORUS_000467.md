# Insects in agri-food systems: from food to sustainable bio-economy

In a context where food security and sustainability are global priorities, the agri-food system faces increasingly complex challenges. The growing demand for animal protein, competition for natural resources and the need to reduce the environmental impact of agricultural production have spurred the search for more sustainable alternatives. In this scenario, insects have emerged not only as an innovative and efficient source of animal nutrition, but also as a key tool for waste management, optimisation of productive systems and the drive for bio-economy. Their integration into agro-food systems requires an approach based on science, regulation and adaptation to different contexts
