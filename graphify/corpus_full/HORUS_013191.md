# Obtaining roughness from images obtained from an Electronic Barry Microscope

This research focuses on determining surface roughness and its correlation with the fractal dimension from images obtained by scanning electronic microscopy (SEM). Free software was used to process images and calculate parameters of roughness and fractal dimension in 3D. The study focused on micrographs of an incision in an aluminium foil, using SIFT to detect characteristic points and the box counting method for the fractal dimension. The results showed coherence between the measured roughness profiles and those obtained by means of a profiler, in addition to establishing significant correlations between the averaged roughness and the fractal dimension with the fractal dimension from the narrowing scantling electron microscopy (SEM). This research methodology was used to determine the roughness and its correlation with the fractal dimension from images obtained through Scantality Electron Microscopy (SEM)
