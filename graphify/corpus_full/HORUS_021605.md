# Method for the temporal prediction of torrential avenues from open data using machine learning.

The torrential avenues are characteristic destructive phenomena of mountain regions. In the department of Antioquia (Colombia), these events occur frequently and the losses in economic terms and human lives reflect the importance of predicting them. The extreme climatic conditions, urban expansion and population growth tend to increase the risk in those areas where events have already been presented in the past. Currently, there is no database that collects the detail of torrential avenues that have occurred in Antioquia with their respective hydrometeorological variables, in addition, most of the research are oriented to identify the spatial susceptibility of these phenomena. With the boom of the techniques of machine learning, a binary classification method is proposed for the temporal prediction of torrential avenues from open data. Thus, the multiple sources of information to construct an inventory of events with their respective hydrometeorological variables are identified and filtered.
