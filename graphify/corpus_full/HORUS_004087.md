# Socio-economic study of the inhabitants of Medellin: using the basic models of the Basesian regresion

In Medellin, Colombia, one of the biggest concerns and problems is the po-bruza. By 2020, the monetary poverty rate in Medellin was 32.9%, making the number of people in this situation out of 1.225.526[1]. Therefore, it is crucial to address poverty and its close relationship with the lack of food in households, with the intention of responding to this great problem, which would help improve the quality of life of thousands of inhabitants. For this purpose, there are certain fundamental factors that can help explain hunger, such as poverty, job instability, the living infrastructure of the household[2]. Based on these factors and the quality of life survey carried out in the city of Medellin in 2020[3], a Bayesian logistic model will be proposed, resulting in accuracy and precision of prediction of 68.1% and 68.6% respectively. The results obtained suggest that it is worth exploring the application of Bayesian logistic regression models, to predictive modelling tasks in socio-economic studies, using non-informational prior distributions
