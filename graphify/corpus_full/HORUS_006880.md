# QUALITY OF LIVING IN URBAN AND CONBURDED INFORMAL SECTORS: THE CASE OF METROPOLITIAN, COLOMBIA AND MEXICO AREAS

The objective of the article is to construct housing indices for informal settlements in two Latin American cities with greater poverty and inequality located in border areas: the town of Bolívar and Soacha (Colombia), and the metropolitan area of Oaxaca (Mexico). The purpose is to analyze quality aspects, their size, services and the regime; for this purpose a quantitative study was carried out of the characteristics of the places of habitation and their environments. Likewise, the statistical technique of factor analysis was applied to construct the indices of the quality of housing, and the technique of regression of ordinary squares to know the correlation between the indices and their variables. The results show that the inhabitants are generators of strategies to access a dignified life within their possibilities. Consequently, accelerated demographic growth and migration from the field to the city is encouraged in the search for better opportunities for entry. This is confirmed by the indices obtained and by the regressions with the variables. It is concluded that the quality of housing in these settlements is determined by their internal conditions and by the conditions of the population, of localization (risk zones) and of access to services, equipment and infrastructure
