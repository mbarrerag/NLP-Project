# POLICE EXPERIMENT IN CASE. MALUS LAW AND PHOTO-PLAY

A polarization experiment is presented to verify Malus' law and explain the phenomenon of photoelasticity. The experiment is carried out with an experimental assembly at home, which includes the manufacture of cardboard supports, a flashlight of a white light LED, the camera of a mobile phone, an acrylic squad and a pair of linear polarizing acetate sheets. The camera records a sequence of images without the squadron to verify Malus's law and another sequence of images with the squadron to observe the color stripes typical of the photoelasticity phenomenon. The images are analyzed in its R-G-B color components and explains the formation of color stripes as an additive mixture of primary colors and as a process of interference
