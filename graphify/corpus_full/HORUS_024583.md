# LINGUAJE AND ACTION: CREATIONS, INSTITUTIONS AND POLICY.

This article seeks to consolidate the conceptual bases as analytical tools of a social research based on strong concepts of language and action. This is done by clarifying what can be understood as language and how this necessarily results in human action. Three fundamental components are developed for social research in this sense: beliefs, discourse and behavior. Each one of them develops in a particular way, with examples of how they can be carried out. The whole proposal is the practical notion of reason, which not only particularises the conception of budget language, but also clarifies the scope of research questions that are worth inquiring into in the field of social sciences
