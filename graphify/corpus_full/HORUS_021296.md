# --Effect of supplementation with flax seed in pre and post transcriptional regulation of novo lipogenesis in Colombian cryolla hen

The Western diets are considered proinflammatory due to the high content of fatty acids such as omega-6 (AG n-6), which in addition to generating biochemical imbalances are related to the premature ageing of the metabolism predisposing of chronic consumers to non-communicable diseases (ENT). In addition to its ant-inflammatory nature and the physiological demand of AG n-3, it is presumed that the consumption of sources rich in AG n-3 modulates the expression of central genes in the energy metabolism becoming a nutritional effect with potential nutraceutical factor against the NTP (ENT).
