# RESTAURATIVE PEDAGOLOGICAL PAUTS FOR THE PREVENTION AND ATTENTION OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCES AND SEXUAL VIOLENCES

From the Welfare Directorate, the Committee for Conflict Resolution and Disciplinary Aspects (corcad), the Gender Affairs Unit and the Pedagogical Reflections Group of the Faculty of Human Sciences (fch), we gave ourselves the task of working for the recognition of the pedagogical and institutional training mission for a sensitive education that promotes the integral training of students, that is guarantor of the right to a life free of violence and safeguard the university coexistence between students, teachers and administrative staff. This proposal for the resolution of situations of coexistence through the pedagogical and restorative process in attention to vgb and vs, is justified in the search for ethical-political and methodological tools that allow us to reflect on the historically constructed relationships between men, women and non-heteronormative identities with the purpose of working progressively for gender justice, care and reciprocity in the university context
