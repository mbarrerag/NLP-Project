# Setting up the exercise of food citizenship in southern Bogotá, a construction by two community-based agroecological organizations.

The imbalances in the agri-food systems are linked to conditions of inequality such as poverty, lack of access to natural, economic and social resources for food supply; lack of sufficient information on the food chain; and inefficiency of formal spaces of participation and political impact for civil society. Therefore, alternatives are required that seek to solve the unsustainability of the agri-food system in both rural and urban areas. According to the above, the purpose of this work is to identify the contributions to the exercise of food citizenship from the experience of two agro-ecological community-based organizations located in the south of Bogotá.
