# MODEL OF TECHNOLOGICAL ACCEPTANCE IN DIGITAL CANALS FOR THE ACQUISITION OF FRESH AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS

to determine the factors associated with the use of digital channels for the acquisition of fresh agricultural goods. Design/methodology: a model of structural equations was performed based on a confirmatory factor analysis that allowed to evaluate the seven constructs of the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology 2 - UTAUT2- (expectancy of performance, expectation of effort, social influence, facilitating conditions, hedonic motivation, price value and habit) in a sample of 175 consumers of agricultural products. all variables, except the facilitating conditions, were found to have a significant impact on the intention of consumers to use digital channels to purchase fresh agricultural products. In addition, habit and behavioral intent were identified as key background to the use of digital channels. the research concludes that experience, gender and age have a positive impact on the intention of using digital channels for the purchase of fresh agricultural goods. It is suggested that digital marketing strategies in the agricultural sector should consider these factors to optimize the adoption of digital channels.
