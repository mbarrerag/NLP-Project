# FACTORY ANALYSIS AND GOVERNMENT FOR THE INTERPRETATION OF INDICATORS AND DOMINIES OF ROOMS IN BOGOTA

The main objective of this study is to understand which indicators are related to the different dimensions that make up this construct. To achieve this, a multidisciplinary task force has been established with the aim of obtaining the greatest amount of open and available data from the city of Bogotá. This team of experts will be dedicated to establishing the domains and indicators of these domains. Subsequently, methods of modeling the reduction of dimensions and factor analysis will be used to analyze these data. This approach will allow a deeper understanding of the underlying structure of the data and how these relate to each other. As a result of this analysis, a total of 10 main components of the 32 indicators were obtained within the 5 proposed domains. This finding allowed establishing a relationship between these components, thus validating the proposed models. In addition, this process helped to deepen the understanding of the phenomenon of urban habitability by assigning names to the components that reflect their possible relationship with habitability in the city
