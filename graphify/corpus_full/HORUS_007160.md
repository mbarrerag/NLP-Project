# Petrophysical characterization of the Royal Group as aquifer potential, in an area of the central sector - north of the Magdalena Middle Valley, Colombia

The study of the underground water potential of the Real Group in a sector center north of the basin of the middle valley of the Magdalena, was carried out from the construction of a stratigraphic and litological 3D model, elaborated from information of 5 seismic lines, 32 hydrocarbon wells and three stratigraphic field columns, in which four operating units were identified in the Real Group, named from base to ceiling GR1, GR2, GR3 and GR4. By the processing and analysis of resistive, acoustic and nuclear records of hydrocarbon wells, the values of the petrophysical values of hydrogeological interest were estimated as the volume of clay, effective porosity and salinity. The volume of clay was calculated in 24 wells, identifying the units GR2, GR3 and GR4 with greater aquifer potential, with values in the median of 44.7%, 44.6% and 57.9% of the clay units respectively, while for the GR1 unit a median of 29.6% of sandstones was calculated, so this last unit was classified as a water related model Y. The poros effective volume was calculated in the same wells and the clay was calculated as a median of 2.9%. The GR3 unit generally presents slightly salty waters and the GR4 unit fresh waters, therefore according to resolution 90341 of the Ministry of Mines and Energy, groundwater of these last two units should have a potential use for human consumption and the GR2 unit towards the centre east of the study area, could have an industrial use such as drilling, injection and hydraulic fracture, among others, since water with salinities above the 4,000 ppm of SDT .
