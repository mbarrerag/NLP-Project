# Confrontation and adaptation in the adolescent with Diabetes Mellitus

Afrontamiento y adaptación en el adolescente con Diabetes Mellitus Introduction: La Diabetes Mellitus Type 1 is frequent in childhood and adolescence, its treatment includes controls of glycemia, injections, performance of sport and monitoring of carbohydrate intake. Its management becomes a complex challenge given the variety of physiological, social and emotional changes that occur at this stage, the pressure of the comrades, the desire to be 'normal' and the identity in formation, tests the ability to confront and adapt in adolescents. Describe the ability to face and adapt in the adolescent with Diabetes Mellitus. Study is quantitative, descriptive of transversal cut, the scale of facing and adaptation version in Spanish was applied to 100 adolescents with Diabetes Mellitus of two IPS of the city of Manizales. Adolescents with Diabetes Mellitus T1 of this study have a level of facing and adapting in general: half 55% and high 9%; biological sex and the diagnostic time does not impact in the level of coping and adapting. In the coping strategies focused in the problem, the adolescents can highlight their autonomy. The challenge is to be able to adapt to the needs of the young people. To describe the ability to cope and adapt in adolescents with Diabetes Mellitus. The study is quantitative, descriptive, cross-sectional, the coping scale and adaptation in Spanish version were applied to 100 adolescents with Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus from two IPS from the city of Manizales.
