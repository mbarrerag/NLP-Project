# CARE IN THE HEALTH OF CHILDREN

Early childhood care presents challenges for families, since children are exposed to factors that influence their growth and development, such as cultural and family practices, which have an impact on their health and well-being. The aim of the study was to understand the meaning of the family in early childhood health care of the Families in Action programme. A qualitative study, based on interpretative ethnography, which included ten families with children from the municipality of Girón, Santander. The results confirmed that child health care in families, from the cultural component, is provided according to the beliefs and customs acquired in a transgenerative way and with the predominance of the female care role, intrafamily agreements for the coexistence and social development of children
