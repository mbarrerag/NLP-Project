# DISPERSION OF LEADER WARK MATERIAL AND VIOLATION OF LEPTONIC KNOWLEDGE IN NON-UNIVERSAL FAMILY EXTENSIONS

In the framework of two extensions with symmetry U(1) additional to the group of the Standard Model of elementary particles, it is proposed to carry out studies related to the mystery of the nature of dark matter and processes related to the violation of leptonic taste.On the one hand, despite the gravitational evidences of the presence of dark matter charged from astronomic and cosmological observations, the numerous experiments of direct detection have not reported signs of dark matter particles dispersing atomic nuclei. From the theoretical point of view, it is usually assumed that these particles are relatively heavy (with masses greater than 1 GeV) and weakly interacting, capable of depositing energy into atomic nuclei. But the lack of evidence of this type of dispersions has led to reconsidering this supposition, and have begun to form studies with light dark matter load (in the order of 1 MeV), which do not achieve interaction with the nuclei but with the most external electronic layers of atoms. On this idea, a model with U(1) symmetry with less tautic moun character is proposed in this project. Adding new exotic particles whose couplings with neutral bosons generate changes in flavor, and in this way study possible processes of leptonic flavor violation. From the calculations of decay width, it is expected to be able to compare the predictions of the model with the reported data and thus to narrow down permitted regions that make the model viable
