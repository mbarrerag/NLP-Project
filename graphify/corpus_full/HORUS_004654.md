# IDENTIFICATION OF HETEROTIC GROUPS IN THE TROPICAL MAIZ LINES BY DIALELIC CRUSHING AND SNP TYPE MOLECULAR MARCARDERS

One of the objectives of the genetic breeding programs of maize is to develop lines of high genetic value, which, when crossed, can produce hybrids with superior agronomic characteristics. In Colombia, the acquisition of hybrids is of high interest since they can help increase yield and production of maize and close the gap that exists between the national production and the import of maize that currently is 74%. Valle S.A. Seeds is a Colombian company that has a program of breeding hybrid maize focused on the tropic and subtropic areas. Currently the program of genetic improvement of maize of Seeds Valle S.A. does not have a characterization of germplasm that allows to establish efficient strategies for the formation of high yield hybrids. In this study the classification of lines of endocerated maize in heterotic groups was carried out from 12 209 SNP markers and phenotypic variables in 66 lines of improvement program Valle S.A.
