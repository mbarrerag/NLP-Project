# Quantum and neuroaltherapeutic phenomena: an integrative vision.

It is noted with amazement that all aspects of life are permeated by quantum processes within and outside the living being, in its physiological processes and in physiopathological ones, therefore, to enter into these complex conceptual paths of Physics, Mechanics and Quantum Biology can represent lights in the therapeutic path to understand the intrinsic mechanisms that are given at the molecular level with the application of Neuraltherapeutic, as an inspecific stimulus in the recovery of the health of patients attending the consultation of Neurotherapeutic Medicine. Understanding more deeply this valuable therapeutic tool will help to broaden our field of action, to impact the environment more heavily, and why not, to transcend with public health policies that carry from the general, to the particular, with attention to the more holistic, integral, complementary and integrative patient considered in its mental, physical, spiritual, social and economic spheres to achieve its self-eco-organization, in this dynamic world, changing with new rationalities and technologies from the quantum
