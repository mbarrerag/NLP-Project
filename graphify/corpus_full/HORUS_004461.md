# Organizational Analyses: A Functionalist Management Management Tool

Abstract Objective: This research article builds an analogy between physical pain and its management with painkillers and the pain-malesting of workers and their treatment with organizational analgesics. This analogy reveals that workers experience pain and discomfort caused by working conditions associated with functionalistic management and that those who are in charge of these organizations do not seek to solve the causes of pain but to alleviate it. To achieve this, the research examines some practices that have been installed in the business environment and constitute themselves in authentic organizational analgesics. Analgesics because their purpose is to mitigate pain. The work on the ground involved three stages: exploration through 102 surveys, targeting through 16 interviews and deepening through a focus group with seven participants. Main results: The research showed four types of analgesics: the sense of self-responsibility, the production of expectations, the messages and deterrent ideas and the material world. The analysis allows to see how these organizational analgesics operate and the effects on the action and subjectivity of the workers.
