# PRISONER SPELEOLOGES OF THE COVAS: HOW TO TAKE SUBTERRANE SCIENCE TO THE SUPERFICIENCY?

The speleology in Colombia has been developing for about 200 years, presenting accelerated scientific production and proliferating over the last two decades. However, why are we still rare creatures between the academic community and the general public? Why do public policy treat underground ecosystems tangentically? Why is the word speleologist little understood among colleagues, friends and family? The answer is simple, we have been doing speleology for speleologists for decades, or for researchers specializing in underground environments. This makes us prisoners of the hypogeal world that we are so passionate about. In this paper I present the main historical constraints for conducting research that impact the general public, and I carry out a short review of the main suggestions and strategies made by multiple authors to carry out wide-ranging and accessible research to a general audience
