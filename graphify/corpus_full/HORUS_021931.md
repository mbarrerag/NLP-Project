# Design and implementation of a teaching unit for training in the generic competence of quantitative reasoning of students of the health programs of the Catholic University of Manizales.

In this work, a didactic unit for training in the generic competence of quantitative reasoning of students was designed and implemented some of the health programs of the Catholic University of Manizales, as pedagogical strategy of preparation for the SABER pro tests. For the development of this research was counted with the participation of 32 students of last semesters of the nursing program, in a course of free choice. Due to different situations outside the University, 12 of them could not continue and finally was accompanied to 20 students until the entire process was finished. 5 sessions were carried out with a duration of 2 hours in synchronous work. The work was developed through the university's Moodle platform, using the digital resources and implementing different activities for the scope of the proposed objectives. An entry test of 35 questions was applied that allowed to identify the difficulties in the learning process in each of mathematical thoughts (random, numerical, metric and spatial implementation). The exit test showed good results compared to the entrance test, highlighting the benefits of the use of didactic unit designed through semiotic representations and the use of each perssive message
