# Study of fatal cases of methyl alcohol poisoning that entered the headquarters of the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences, Bogotá, Colombia, November 2022 to January 2023

Methyl alcohol is a toxic to humans and the poisoning usually occurs after the ingestion of adulterated alcoholic beverages for our study population. In Colombia it is common to find fatal cases due to methyl poisoning and through history some outbreaks have been seen, in Bogotá the most recent one occurred between November 2022 and January 2023. Thus, a descriptive cross-sectional study is carried out with analysis of the information contained in 42 necropsy files, with the aim of describing the sociodemographic characteristics, the point findings regarding the liquor consumed, toxicological history, symptoms, medical care and postmortem findings. In the necropsy of these cases, the most representative was the acute tubular necrosis very severe and the bilateral necrosis of the putamen at macroscopic and microscopic level. To prevent new events, it is necessary to strengthen interinstitutional communication, increase public awareness through more effective campaigns and improve the packaging of liquors. .
