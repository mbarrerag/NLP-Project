# The territorial approach to criminal policy, crime prevention and the accompaniment of the post-penalty population

This project to strengthen the CDPP seeks to clarify in Colombian public criminal policy the competence of the administrative territories in relation to the prison system, the prevention of crime and the support of the post-penitentiary population; this is based on the analysis of the local institutions working on the issue of security and coexistence, the construction of a matrix of knowledge management in them and the prioritization of citizen training in processes of social destigmatization and popular public discussion
