# Aviation technology analysis for the energy transition

The global energy transition requires an analysis of liquid fuel consumption. This study focuses on some aircraft used in Colombia, evaluating fuel consumption and CO2 emissions per person on the Bogotá-Medellín and Bogotá-San Andrés routes for a single flight. Structural and thermal differences in the internal thermodynamics of turbofan and turboprop engines are analyzed through literature. Comparative figures and tables of fuel consumption and CO2 generation per person for ATR72, A320 and A321 aircraft are presented. Interviews are conducted with experts related to the topic of aeronautics. This explains why regional flights benefit from the use of turboprop, while longer routes prefer turbofans. This analysis is relevant for decision-making in the Colombian aviation industry and in the fight against global climate change
