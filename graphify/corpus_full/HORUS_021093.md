# Right of the ETTE ENNAKA people Right and justice proper to the Ette Ennaka people today: experiences of the exercise of justice of 3 communities in the departments of Magdalena and Cesar.

The indigenous people of Ette Ennaka are currently located around the population of the municipality of San Angel and in the plains of the departments of Magdalena and Cesar, a non-excessive territory for them. This change of territory has been due to various circumstances such as colonization, westernization, armed conflict, State negligence, land appropriation by people outside the town, among other situations. This problem is visible thanks to the ruling of 20 November 2018 of the Fourth Civil Decongestion Court of the Special Circuit in Restitution of Lands of Santa Marta, in which it is decided in favour of the restitution of the land of the people in question. An indispensable part of this process of cultural change and evolution is the recognition and diagnosis of the effectiveness of the existing and recorded right; for this reason, it is important to know the situation of the administration of justice and the rules of this people, since this will allow for an evolution of other aspects. For this reason, the research team seeks to describe the functioning of the right and justice of the Ette Ennaka people at present, observing it from its norms, actors and procedures in three indigenous communities located in the departments of the Magdalena and Cesar
