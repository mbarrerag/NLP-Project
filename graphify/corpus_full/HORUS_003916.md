# Territorial connectivity and accessibility strategies from the strengthening of local infrastructure from a multimodal and intermodal perspective

Colombia is a diverse country, with a large number of natural resources, different ecosystems besides its cultural and ethnic diversity, however it presents high indices of inequality, specifically in terms of accessibility to basic services in the different regions of the country, this is seen more frequently in the peripheral or adjacent areas of the urban centres, one of the reasons why this problem is presented is due to the low quality in connectivity between municipalities, causing the possibilities of mobility between rural inhabitants to be reduced.The concepts of accessibility, connectivity and discursive mobility have been aimed at the dynamics clearly urban, however, the question arises of how these concepts have been applied in terms of rurality?The present document seeks to analyze the conditions of connectivity between urban and rural areas of the region of low Putumayo based on the notion of satisfaction of human needs and access to basic services from the discussion of concepts, the study and the relationship of the physical-spatial conditions of the municipalities and finally to identify the interventions in terms of road infrastructure and transport with a focus in the rural areas. a different country, with a great majority of natural resources, different ecosystems, in addition to its cultural and ethnic diversity, however, it presents high inequality indexes, specifically in terms of accessibility to the different regions of the country, this is more frequently in the peripheral areas or areas close to urban centres, one of the reasons for this problem is due to the low quality of connectivity between municipalities, causing a decrease in the possibilities of mobility rural inhabitants. environment access and connectivity as rights in the quality of life of human beings, and in this way to think about tracing the concepts with an emphasis on rural dynamics. From the analysis of the region it was identified that the most disconnected areas are located in territories with specific characteristics such as: location in peripheral areas, territories that widely conserve their forests, have a high index of natural resources and luxury, on the other hand has a high profile of rural population majority peasants, indigenous, Afro-descendants all other vulnerable populations, taking into account the above identified a low quality of life due to its limited access to basic services for the vulnerability of population
