# Making destinations: a story of my family from recollections around El Águila, Valle del Cauca and other places

This work addresses the transition from the life of my family during the twentieth century in El Águila, Valle del Cauca, Colombia. The vital relationship between a relatively recent past and the present is marked by the remembrances, word that designates in the family one of the ways in which the plots that unite different people, places and moments are developed. In the first of the following courses, the difference and the liminal places are explored as routes to think about the ability of the kinship both to unite and to separate. The second of the courses follows two fundamental milestones, coffee, as a crop that concentrated the family work and marked the rhythms of life, and violence, as a phenomenon that determined the senses of the past and the present family and the possibilities for the future.
