# Index, compilation and contextualization of Oriol Rangel's piano work

The following paper presents the index and cataloguing of Oriol Rangel's piano work based on documentary research and the organization of his works according to Colombian genres and rhythms. In this research, the qualitative methodology was used with descriptive and propositive approaches of documentary type for contextualization and cataloguing as a digital resource for musicians. .
