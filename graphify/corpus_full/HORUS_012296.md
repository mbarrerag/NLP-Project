# Sustainability of the Bovine Livestock in the Orinoquía: Case of Paz de Ariporo municipality, Casanare.

The purpose of this research was to analyze the sustainability of livestock in the municipality of Paz de Ariporo, Casanare, through a tool developed for this purpose through the use of sustainability indicators in the environmental, social and economic dimensions. The study was carried out on 130 producers in the municipality Paz de Ariporo, using semi-structured surveys and visits to farms for the diagnosis of productive activity. The research addressed the qualitative and quantitative methodological combination. For the analysis of results, factorial analysis of mixed data and subsequent construction in main components were used where the variables that contribute most to the sustainability of the system were identified. Based on the results obtained, a hierarchical cluster analysis was carried out with 4 groups that determined the level of sustainability of the farms evaluated, categorizing in low sustainability (51 of the farms evaluated), medium sustainability (29), high sustainability (37) and very high sustainability (13).
