# Effectiveness of the Prostactive Nursing Intervention for Self-Management of Symptoms in Men with Prostate Cancer Treatment

Prostate cancer is the cancer of the highest prevalence in men. The disease and treatment generate urinary, intestinal, sexual, hormonal and emotional symptoms that have an impact on the functional performance of men who suffer them, self-management of symptoms has been considered from the World Health Organization (WHO) as a strategy to allow men to better know their disease and generate skills to manage it from a home environment. To determine the effectiveness of Prosctive Intervention for self-management of symptoms and its impact on functional performance, in people with prostate cancer in treatment that attend the Oncological Unit of the Clinic Nogales between 2022 and 2023. It is an experimental parallel design determined by the random conformation of two groups with a sample size of 100 participants, 50 for control (GC) that will receive the usual care and 50 for the experimental (GI) to which the Prosctive intervention was implemented in the auto-contact week consisting of 4 in-person sessions in which it will explain how to handle the symptoms that presents due to prostate cancer and treatment.
