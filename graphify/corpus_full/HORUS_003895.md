# PARIS IN MELLIFERA APIS, XYLOCOPA FRONTALIS AND SPECIES OF MELIPONIIN IN THE DEPARTMENT OF THE VALLEY OF THE CAUCA

The purpose of the research was to identify the parasites associated with the Apini: Apis mellifera, the Meliponini tribe and the carpinter bees in tropical areas of the Colombian Pacific regions (Valle del Cauca and Department of Cauca). This study was carried out as part of a surveillance strategy to prevent the transmission of diseases and reduce the risk of spread between wild pollinators and melliferal bees, as well as contributing to the strengthening of the entomological collection of Hymenoptera: Apidae of the National University of Colombia in Palmira (CEUNP-70).
