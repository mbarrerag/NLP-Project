# Stakeholder-Driven Insights Into the Colombian Palm Oil Sector: A Qualitative Study

This article characterizes the Colombian palm oil supply chain and its socio-technical context through qualitative analysis of in-depth interviews conducted with various stakeholders. The objective was to map the current supply chain and identify its limitations in achieving sustainable production from the stakeholders' perspective. To achieve this, 8 semi-structured interviews with key actors were conducted from February to May 2023 and were analyzed using inductive qualitative coding techniques. The interviews included representatives from producers, processors, associations, retailers and academics, providing a comprehensive view of the palm oil industry's operations from cultivation to consumption. According to them, the main limitations include environmental impact, complexity, lack of innovation, difficulty in adapting to changes, and lack of coordination of stakeholders' actions. This study highlights the need for innovative interventions and policy reforms to enhance the efficiency and sustainability of the palm oil value chain in Colombia. The analysis offers a context-specific view of the supply chain that can serve as a basis for proposing such interventions
