# Assessment of biodiversity assets for the sustainable development of the chagra in the N+maira Km11 indigenous community of the Colombian Amazon.

The cosmovision that indigenous communities have is a rich source of ethnobiological wisdom, since within this we form knowledge that can be useful and must be preserved. In our region this knowledge is transmitted through oral tradition of parents and grandparents to children and grandchildren; in general the new generations show little interest for this valuable resource, putting at risk its preservation, with which it is threatened to disappear. Through research, sustained for more than 16 years with indigenous communities and industrial actors of the area, our team has consolidated particular competences for access to traditional knowledge, its conservation and the generation, from it, of ideas useful for the sustainable use of biological diversity. Chagra is the sustainable agroforestal system adapted to the characteristics of the Amazon region. This work aims to characterize the growers, exploring the associated traditional knowledge and the perception of value, in the context of the Amazonian chagra of an indigenous community; also, some species will be prioritized, under the lens of commercial opportunity.
