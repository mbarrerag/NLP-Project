# Analysis and development of a portable lighting protection system.

Colombia has a very high ray activity as shown in different published ray maps. Any activity that develops in open field during a storm, even more in places recognized as high ray activity, at certain times and seasons, increases further the risk of suffering some type of injury for the exposed people. Remote and difficult places away from urban centers worsen this scenario. For the Colombian National Army it is reported in an average of fifteen years, up to 48 soldiers per year victims of lightning impact. To reduce the risk of lightning to health in the vulnerable population that cannot avoid its exposure, the most likely mechanisms of injury are analyzed considering some scenarios with existing human models. Considering that a portable shelter for lightning protection requires light materials, some types of electroconductive tissues subjected to impulsive lightning currents with standard wave shape were investigated in the laboratory. Some samples of conductor tissues were subjected to several subsequent lightning currents and analyzed, revealing some noticeable changes in their surface.
