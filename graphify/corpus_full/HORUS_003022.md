# Preface

The COP 16 of Biodiversity represents a great opportunity for Latin America, because this region is one of the richest in biological and cultural diversity. Human, animal and ecosystem health is intrinsically linked to biodiversity, since biodiversity supports ecosystems that provide food, clean water and clean air. In addition, many medicines come from plants and microorganisms, which highlights the need to conserve biodiversity to ensure access to effective treatments. The loss of biodiversity can increase the risk of zoonotic diseases and affect the resilience of ecosystems to climate change, endangering our health and that of the planet. Protecting biodiversity is crucial not only for the survival of many species, but also for the future of humanity
