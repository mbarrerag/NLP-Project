# Intermittance of daily pre-meditation in Colombia

Rain is a highly variable phenomenon in space and time. The amount of rain, as well as its distribution in these two dimensions, is key for planning water use and for preventing and mitigating problems caused by its excess or deficit. Intermitence is a feature of time series related to the changes in the state of the process. Schleiss and Smith proposed a way of quantifying the intermittance of daily rain using two metrics, Burstiness (B) and Memory (M) which respectively provide a standardized measure of dispersion and a temporary order of time intervals between the occurrence of precipitation events. In this work we apply the Schleisss and Smith method to daily rainfall in the Colombian territory between 1981 and 2020 coming from CHIRPS, which has a spatial resolution of 0.05°. The results show that the region of space (M,B) occupying the Colombian series is strongly removed from the Schleisss and Smith for 325 sites in the United States, Switzerland, Germany, the United Kingdom and the Netherlands.
