# Artificial vision system for monitoring and monitoring analgesic and anesthetic effects administered via epidural neuroaxial in obstetric population during labor to strengthen maternal health services at the University Hospital of Caldas - SES HUC.

Regional anesthesia is a modality of anesthesiology aimed at relieving pain and/or completely annuling sensitivity, whose use has increased in clinical practice because of its innovative and safe character. Particularly, analgesia via neuroaxial imaging has proven to be one of the safest measures for the relief of pain from labor facilitating the function of anesthesiologist in the accompaniment of the obstetric patient, allowing pain relief to complete the course of vaginal delivery and improving physiological conditions. Early detection of the effectiveness of analgesia allows for corrective measures and establishes with safety the possibility of making interventions to the patient for example, increasing number and quantity of doses or relocating the catheter. Thermoalgesic sensitivity tests tests, cutaneous punctures and superficial touch and modalities based on techniques of image processing have been used classically for this effect. However, these tests of determining the effectiveness of analgesia present important limitations: 1) high time investment by clinical personnel, 2) strict cooperation is required by the patient during the evaluations of psychophysical sensory type, which increases the rate and the high intensile. The aim is to develop a predictive model for the time of onset of the sensory block, sympathetic blockade and the perception of pain relief through the analogous visual pain scale (EVA) developed and validated for this purpose. Finally, the methodology for processing thermographic data in conjunction with a strategy for capturing and storing these data is integrated into a portable system that allows for monitoring of the anesthetic effectiveness in clinical settings through a low cost and intuitive mobile application. Consequently, the technological innovation system proposed will allow for improving the quality of maternal and perinatal health care processes at the Hospital Universitario de Caldas SES HUC in Manizales
