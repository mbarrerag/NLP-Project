# THE CIVIL SOCIETY BUILLING PEACE AMID WAR: A PROPOSAL CONCEPTUAL

Through the analysis of the Colombian case (2012-2021), which includes mapping of 211 Civil Society Organizations (CSOs) working for peace, we propose a mixed conceptual framework that allows conceptualizing CSOs based on fundamental and complementary attributes. As a result of the intersection of these attributes, we propose four ideal-typical forms: (1) CSOs for the culture of peace and democratic strengthening, (2) CSOs for the strengthening of democracy based on membership of a vulnerable group within the context of conflict, (3) CSOs for integral development as a means of pacifying society, (4) CSOs for the development of a group and/or territory affected by conflict, based on membership of such a group or zone. Although overlap is possible as ideal abstractions, the results show that 166 of the CSOs studied work around the democratic cause. Specifically, 97 of them related to the culture of peace, and 69 based on membership to vulnerable groups. 64 of the CSOs observed work for development.
