# Agroecology and energy. Relations and possibilities constituting a Peasant Reserve Zone

The outstanding contribution of agriculture and the agri-food system to climate change demands the analysis of specific transition strategies for this human activity. In this context, the energy transition becomes necessarily agroecological, given the strong dependence of fossil fuels characteristic of the eco-economic sector influenced by the green revolution. This research uses two methodological tools, energy balances (BE) (Funes-Monzote, 2009) and the Main Agroecological Structure (EAP) (León-Sicard, 2021), to analyze 10 farms in the newly constituted Region of Campesino Reserve Venice Part High. The purpose was to contribute with information relevant to the planning of the Sustainable Development Plan of the area, besides establishing connections between both methodologies. During 13 visits to the area, georeferencing data were collected, which were carried out 10 structured interviews and collected monetary data to study the BE and the EAP of each farm. In a workshop of socialization and collective construction, a proposal was prepared to improve the data obtained through a proposal of collective implementation of biogesters which would seek to strengthen the agro-energy transition.
