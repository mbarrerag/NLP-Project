# Decays of heavy months: a theoretical perspective using effective field theories

This document presents cases of study of the disintegration and production of hadrons with quark content $c$ from the point of view of effective field theories. It shows a theoretical review of the disintegrations $D_S\rightarrow KK^-K and $B\rightarrow H(c\Bar{c})\;X$. The radiation topology of $W$ is introduced and visualized in the disintegration $D_S\rightarrow 3K$, also known as the release topology of $W$ external, using graphics of Dalitz. The document continues with a review of the non-relatitive quantum chromodynamic factorization (NRQCD) for the production of charmonia and its complementary form tools, the expansion in the threshold and the covariant projectors, to end with an adjustment of the elements of long distance matrix (LDMEs) present in the inclusive disintegration rate of $$$c$c$$, using results of the literature to the next disruptive order (NLO) of QCD_ and the estimation of theoretical uncertainty of $c$. • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • value of their branching fractions.
