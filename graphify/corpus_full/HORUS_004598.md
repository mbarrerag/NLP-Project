# REVIEW OF FACTORS INCITING BETWEEN GREENWASHING AND GREEN BRAND EQUITY: AN RESEARCH AGENDA

to identify the elements or factors that influence the relationship between the greenwashing and the green brand equity.Design/methodology: to carry out this study was chosen by an exploratory type methodology with a qualitative approach, highlighting the conceptual search that outlined the key authors and the factors associated through the documentary analysis. the inquiry revealed that the factors linked in this relationship include the green trust, green brand image, green credit, green satisfaction, green brand association, green relationship, green confusion, green brand attitude, word of mouth, brand avoidance, green perceived risk, green brand perceived study, green loytyy green brand legitimization, which are studied in the literature and, depending on the context, have different roles in this relationship. it is concluded that this relationship constitutes a research area underexploited, green between green law and green brand legitimization, which is an opportunity, however, which is generated by the effects of the literature and, depending on the context, has different roles in this relationship
