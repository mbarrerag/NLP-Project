# APPLICATION OF DEEP LEARNING TECHNIQUES AMENDMENT AND OBSERVATION OF SOLAR PHOTOSFER

This work is framed in the applications of neural networks in the study and modelling of the phenomena present in the solar fotosphere. The proposed research is based on the model of generative re-anaversarias using the artificial intelligence modules of Pytorch. It seeks to train a neural network capable of generating groups of images of a high similarity with training images, these images correspond to physical magnitudes of the solar fotosphere such as density, fieldmagnetic, plasma velocity, temperature, among others, obtained from the MURaM simulation code, although the neural network can be trained to generate images of any physical magnitude. The work focuses on the generation of magnetic field images in the solar fotosphere.
