# Mathematical modelling of social dynamics leading to sustainable peace: demobilization, coexistence and citizenship formation.

Colombia is a country that has been hit by many war and civil conflicts throughout its history, many of them for very long periods, this has generated various problems and barriers to peace building such as mistrust between the parties and the search for benefits of each participant; therefore, demobilization processes, peace support and citizenship creation play a major role in Colombia in the transition to a healthy society. However, the dynamics behind the different specific social variables that interact in these processes are of great complexity, which does not allow efficient decision-making, and in many cases biased or driven by historical or emotional reasons. Mathematical models and simulation can offer a tool and new methodologies to assist with the formulation of effective strategies and policies and the decision-making based on data to eliminate bias. However, the study in this area is in a premature state, there are no approaches or models sufficiently rigorous to advise a process and much less adapted to the context and need of the Colombian people. This project focuses on identifying, through modelling and simulation techniques, the dynamic relationships between the social variables that sustains the creation of Colombian people
