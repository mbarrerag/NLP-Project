# The instrumental effectiveness of the implementation of electronic administrative procedures through digital governance in the Capital District

Currently, with the consolidation of the use of ICTs and the paradigm changes in society that emerged with the fourth industrial revolution, as well as the massification of the web 4.0, the one that makes public administrations has had to mutate to the digital scenario. However, this change is neither sporadic nor organic since it must correspond to guidelines set through public policies that understand the shortcomings presented by society such as the themes of connectivity and digital literacy. In this study it will be evidenced how Bogotá applying a Digital Governance has carried out actions aimed at digitizing the administrative procedures of its entities, through transparency, efficiency and citizen participation. .
