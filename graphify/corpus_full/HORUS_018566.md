# Transformations in the ways of living in the framework of the construction of Peace in Colombia: contributions to the configuration of a populated center. Case study: Territorial Space of Training and Reintegration Antonio Nariño, Icononzo, Tolima

The implementation of the Peace Agreements has been sinuosa by the failure of the National Government. This has made it difficult for the persons reincorporated in the Territorial Areas of Training and Re-incorporation (ETCR) to remain, causing several people to return to the guerrilla. From the commitments in territory and housing, the possibility was decreed that the places where the persons reincorporated live are legally and normatively integrated into the plans of Colombian territorial planning through the configuration of these spaces as populated centers, but this has not been carried out with any of the ETCR. For this reason, this research focuses on the definition of guidelines of intervention of the territory that support the consolidation of a populated center from the modes of inhabiting the inhabitants of the ETCR Antonio Nariño in Icononzo, Tolima from a participatory methodology. From this perspective, through the ethnographic process and the voices of the re-incorporation of the ETCR were identified the transformations of the modes of inhabiting in the guerrilla, until today in the return, through the complexity of the change and the change of the local life of the ETCR. this has not been donated with none of the TSCR. For this reason, this research focuses on the definition of territorial intervention guidelines that support the consolidation of a populated center based on the ways of living of the inhabitants of the Antonio Nariño TSCR, located in Icononzo, Tolima based on a participatory methodology. From this perspective this research research researched, through ethnographic processes and the voices of the reincorporated people from the TSCR the transformations of the ways of living in their change from the insurrection in the guerrilla to today’s reintegration, passing through the complexity of change from a non-resident life to a headquarters one.
