# Modeling and simulation of photovoltaic systems under partial shaping conditions

This thesis presents a methodology of modeling of commercial photovoltaic panels at cell level operating under conditions of partial shading. In the first part a review of the literature on the representation of photovoltaic cells is carried out, in which important characteristics are considered as the mathematical formulation, the circuital model, the appropriate representation of the behavior in direct and reverse mode and the estimation of parameters. In the second part, some of the most models used in the literature for the modeling of photovoltaic cells, the single diode model (SDM), the DRM model and the Bishop model are considered, paying special attention to the current-voltage relationship, the mathematical formulation, the circuital model and the parameters necessary for its evaluation. To model the panels at the medium level of the cell, the third part focuses on detailing a procedure to obtain the I-V curves in terminals of a panel, without needing any physical intervention. For this procedure a panel without diode of bypass, information obtained when sombrating the panel and some measuring equipments that allow to acquire current, voltage and irradiation.
