# Reconstruction of Inhabiting Modes in Armero Guayabal: Adaptation and Memory After the Tragedy of Armero

This research aims to understand the changes in the ways of living and the processes of reconstruction of collective memory in the communities that survived the tragedy of Armero in 1985, and that, after the disaster, they were displaced and subsequently established in Armero Guayabal. More than a historical review of the event, this work investigates the traces left by the volcano in the daily life, in the bodies, in the houses and in the words of those who lost not only their families, but also their place in the world.From a phenomenological approach, it is part of the recognition of the subjective experiences as a legitimate source of knowledge. It seeks to give voice to the memories embodied in those who remade their life on the rubble of the past, not as a form of nostalgia, but as a vital, active affirmation, of their ability to live again.The physical-spatial transformations, symbolic and socio-cultural ones that were given in this new territory, understood not only as a physical space, but as a place of deep meaning, emotions and links.The informal settlements that emerged in Armero Guayabal but not only should be read from a lack, or from a photographic agency
