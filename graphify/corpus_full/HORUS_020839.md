# STRENGTHENING THE SOCIAL TREATMENT OF THE VERGE OF THE MUNICIPLE OF VERGARA CUNDINAMARCA

In rural areas of the world, bamboo contributes to meeting the subsistence needs of more than one billion people.The traditional use of it as wood, for the construction of houses, manufacturing of useful and musical instruments, in the preservation of watersheds and conservation of the soil; it covers only a minimal part of its wide spectrum of application.Often, the policies and research _x0093_traditional _x0093_ have not taken into account the role of the local population in designing and implementing the measures, projects and programs.The community management of natural resources proposes an alternative approach focused on the management of these resources, with the peasant men and women of the community, recognizing the existing knowledge in the same way about the local resources, as well as its agency in the criteria on the use and management of the resources and the motivation to improve the productivity if the achievement of benefits is identified.In the framework of the National Convocation of Solidarity Extension 2022 a team was formed with the peasant community of the Vergel of the municipality of Vergara-Cundina, teachers, students and graduates of the National University of Colombia. To fulfill the general objective of social diagnosis, the execution of activities, the monitoring, evaluation and the systematization of the experience, being: (i) Diagnosis and characterization, with emphasis on the recognition of the geographical and population characteristics of the Vergel valley (ii) Training process in the management, use and use of the Kunth Guadua angustifolia; (iii) Design of the modular structure for community use; (iv) Socialization of the results and, (v) Systematization of the project. The main concepts of this process, are the management and use of the Kunth Guadua angustifolia, dialogue of knowledge and sustainability, recognizing that the present project initiative focuses on promoting social innovation through the use of the Guadua as a resource for sustainable constructions, posing the need to use both local materials in rural infrastructures. In a transversal way, it is intended to promote participation among the inhabitants of the community, carrying out a joint logistic planning to make workshops focused on theoretical-practical knowledge about the use of the Guadua angustifolia Kunth_93.
