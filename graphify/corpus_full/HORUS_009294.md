# COMADREO: BUCA HEALTH: DIALOGUE OF KNOWLEDGE OF ANCESTROL PRACTICES

The first step is to ensure that women are able to take advantage of the health care system. This is achieved by means of knowledge and practices that reproduce from generation to generation; and in the case of oral health care, the focus is on oral hygiene activities, care for dental pain and the reference to health services. However, this work is invisible by a health system that does not know the importance of care and the people who do it. The Network of Women Mariposes of New Alas building a future, form a community-based organization that for many years works for reconciliation with women, the rescue of ancestral practices and beliefs and the empowerment and recognition of their rights as black women. One of the main causes of the emergence of this network are gender-based violence and violence in the framework of the armed conflict, including the violation of the right to health, emphasizing the Bucadós and ancestral practices of women. dental surgeons are not considered preventive mechanisms because of the high cost of periodically going to the particular dentist for review and control; in addition, EPS dilates the care times, so they choose to go to the dentist when they can no longer handle the condition at home. Health care is a gender role, where women are responsible for all health care within families generating physical and emotional overloads in them, because they are the ones who develop health care strategies from the African diaspora and through the wedreo, allowing to exchange ancestral knowledge supremely useful to address the failures in the PSA in marginalized territories
