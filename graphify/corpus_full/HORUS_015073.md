# Anesthetic considerations for the approach of surgical patient with COVID-19

Abstract Introduction: The current situation caused by the COVID-19 demands the implementation of new techniques in the anesthetic management and pre-existing risks in the surgical services. To identify the anesthetic considerations for patients with COVID-19 in order to suggest interventions in the surgical area. Integrative review of descriptive scope in conjunction with the fulfillment of Whittemore-Knafl methodological steps and the PRISMA parameters. search was carried out in the databases: pubmed, bvs, coronavirus research database, scopus, elsevier and sage. Nine hundred and fifty-three articles were obtained that, together with a critical analysis by CASPe, met the established criteria for inclusion and exclusion. Twenty-seven articles classified in: criteria for the selection of anesthetic technique; general anesthesia and the use of specific drugs for the anesthetic management that reduce cough and pre-advert the release of aerosols
