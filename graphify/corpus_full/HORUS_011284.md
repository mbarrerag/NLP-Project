# DENSIBRATING AND INTERCEPTING LIGHT OF INTER-SPECTIFIC OXG HIBRITER CULTIVES

The photosynthesis is one of the most important processes for life on the planet. Through this metabolic process, the energy of the sun is converted into chimi-ca energy in the form of carbohydrates, which constitute the first entry for the trophic chains. In addition to light, the chemical reagents fundamental in photosynthesis are carbon dioxide and water. Of these reagents, light is essential for photosynthesis to occur optimally. Thus, the low light radiation on the leaves causes the photointhetic apparatus to be underutilized, what is ocasio- na low sugar production and that there are limitations in the accumulation of biomass. This, in turn, results in deficiencies in growth, development and pro-ducivity of the plants.
