# Acupuncture treatment for carpal tunnel syndrome

The purpose of this review is to know and to carry out an evaluation on the effectiveness of acupuncture in the management of carpal tunnel syndrome, performed by a physician specialized in acupuncture in the management of neuropathy by trapping the median nerve at the wrist level; for this purpose the review of available literature will be carried out, the studies that reported an improvement of symptoms and function with the management of acupuncture, in comparison with the conventional non-surgical treatment performed from Western medicine. The STC has a high incidence in the population whose labor activity executes repetitive movements, specifically in hands or wrists (1). In Western medicine, the electrodiagnosis tests (EMG) identify the axonal damage, to choose the conventional treatment or the discomprehension of the nerve (2).
