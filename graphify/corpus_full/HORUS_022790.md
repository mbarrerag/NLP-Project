# SPACE FROM OBJECTS, RELATIONS BETWEEN DOMESTIC SPACE AND PRACTICES OF VOICE VOICE.

This conference reflects on the relationship between the space and the daily habits of its inhabitants through furniture, the ways in which the furniture arranged in the domestic space shows a social or family structure, a period and a geo-climate. Using the concept of 'position structures', as elaborated by Jean Baudrillard in his book The System of Objects, a look is proposed here from which architecture is understood as incomplete without furniture and household items, since it is through the furniture that the practices of living in everyday life are evidenced. A comparison of domestic spaces is made, from the spatial structure in the nineteenth century house, passing through modern housing, to the proposals of contemporary housing. Contrasting the architecture of international reference at every time with Latin American architecture, especially in Colombia, where the tropical Andean climate poses phenomenic particularities in the space.
