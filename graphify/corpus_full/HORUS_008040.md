# The contribution of agricultural and food networks and short marketing cycles to the construction of sustainable agricultural and food systems

The situation surrounding access and availability of food in Colombia affects not only consumers, but also especially agricultural producers and stakeholders who are linked to the various economic activities related to food production. crisis has not only impacted the various activities related to food production, but also all consumers. Particularly, to families with lower incomes whose budgets are largely devoted to food purchases (even above 50% in many cases). Progressively, alternative food networks and short circuits of agro-food marketing have been forming in the country in resilient strategies adapted to the measures implemented, developing new tools and means to market or exchange their food, such as home distribution or electronic commerce, strategies that most maintained after the lifting of measures for the control of the pandemic, strengthening their capacities and their supply of services, and at the same time, strengthening the sovereignty and food and nutrition security.
