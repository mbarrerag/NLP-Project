# A model of sociodemographic segmentation for consumers of a portfolio of products and services focused on digital marketing.

The pandemic of the COVID-19, declared as such at the beginning of 2020, had serious impacts on daily life. The world population had to adapt to new conditions, among them, to the use of different physical and technological elements in order to contain the contagion. Thus, the capabocas became the most used accessory worldwide and, companies like advertising agencies, can know what people use it daily through the application of Machine Learning to their Digital Marketing strategies. To highlight this relationship, this thesis proposes a model of sociodemographic segmentation for consumers of a digital marketing campaign of the advertising agency “A” that offers the public a portfolio of disposable surgical tapabocas. The data used are those provided by the National Health Institute and the Ministry of Health on the positive cases of Covid-19 in Colombia from March 2, 2020 to December 21, 2022. Methodologically, a clusterization is carried out under the k-means method. As a result, 5 clusters were obtained from the unsupervised learning programming of Machine Learning.
