# ANTROPOLOGY OF THE (and their) CONNECTIONS: BETWEEN SCIENCES, TECHNICALS AND ARTICLES

In this dossier we seek to offer a space for dialogue between fields that, within anthropology, explore seemingly dissimilar areas. We wonder if there would be anything in that enormous variety that characterizes the doing and anthropological knowledge, that would allow us to bring together areas that are thought to be unrelated. Our interest was not in the epistemology of connections, but rather in what is at stake when we evoke that concept. What is it that connects?, How is it done?, In what ways the practices of knowledge production associated with scientific trades such as anthropology, the arts and others, converge to create something new? How do these connections hold on or are destroyed?
