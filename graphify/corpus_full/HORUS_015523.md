# Design of an auction multi-object: a method to maximize the social welfare in the allocation of the electromagnetic spectrum in Colombia 2010-2022

Since the end of the last century, the auction mechanism has been used for the allocation of spectrum in the world, in 2010 Colombia began to use this international dynamic. This research aims to familiarize the reader with the theory of auctions, as well as to detail the characteristics and regulations of our country regarding the management of the electromagnetic spectrum, to specifically evaluate the four auctions that have been carried out in Colombia. Finally, an auction model with signaling and a post-auction market is proposed, which both theoretically and computationally shows us that the auction is efficient as long as there are incentives to overestimate private valuation. Thus, it is recognized as a possibility to implement operation insurance for the winning bidder and reserve bands for the dominant operator as a solution to expand coverage and promote competition
