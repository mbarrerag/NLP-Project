# Pastoralizing of mestizo cows sweater at two altitudes of Valle del Cauca, Colombia

In Colombia, milk production systems, in the middle tropics, depend on grazing and supplementation with concentrate, in minimum quantity, by cost and not to make substitution of fodder per concentrate. Based on rotational grazing in star pastures Cynodont plectostachyus and commercial concentrate supplement, the animal behavior was monitored based on the ratio of milk production (liters-vaca.day-1) and the intake of dry matter per day (IMS.d-1) in cows Jersey-Holstein (Jer-Hol). The cows of 4 ± 2 births, 15 cows.finca-1, in Finca Canada (FC) (1,500 m s.) and Finca Chiquique (FCH) (980 m s. n. m.), in the valley of Cauca respectively were measured by organic efficiency and FC % were reported by 24 hours per 8 days, by 9 weeks.Finca
