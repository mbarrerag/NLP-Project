# Experimental houses at the Inter-American Housing Center-CINVA, 1952-1956

Abstract In this article, the partial result of an ongoing research, we describe how, during its first years of operation, the Inter-American Housing Center (CINVA) adopted the method of progressive development in order to design and build prototype low-cost housing and thus contribute to the solution of the existing housing deficit. The result was a set of experimental houses conceived under the principles of standardization, prefabrication and industrialization; however, a turn towards the production of housing by self-construction marked the end of this interesting technological experience in the Latin American context
