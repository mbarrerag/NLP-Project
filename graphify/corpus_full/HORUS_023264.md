# Design and implementation of HIL simulator (Hardware in the loop) of cardiovascular system for newborns.

This paper describes the development of a HIL simulator of the cardiovascular system for neonates, which allows monitoring 6 vital signals: ECG, heart pulse, ventilatory volume, blood pressure, oxygen saturation and CO2 level (Co2 expiratory pressure). Through an Arduino Due card, equations describing the signals to be monitored are implemented. The ECG includes the frequency spectrum of the RR interval (Interval between cycles R of the ECG signal, proportional to the period of a cardiac cycle-a beat), in which factors can be varied that basically affect the variability of the heart rate, the signals of ECG and heart pulse. To simulate arterial pressures, oxygen saturation and CO2 level, a hydraulic circular model of Goodwin modified model (Goodwin et al, 2004) is used, where the heart, systemic circulation, pulmonary circulation, alveolar gas exchange, and respiratory mechanics are represented. All these signals, in addition to configuration parameters are transmitted via WiFi, through the NodeMCUESP32 card, where the cardiac signal is represented, the mobile device (Cular or tablet), and how the patient is used to use it is used.
