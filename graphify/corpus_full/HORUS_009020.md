# OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE OF THE NUTRITIONAL CRITRICATE SCALE IN CANCER CHILD, SCAN-SP SPANISH VERSION

Malnutrition is a frequent problem in children with cancer and although its prevalence is very variable due to factors such as type of cancer, stage of the disease, type of treatment, among others, it has been shown that malnutrition affects the prognosis of patients and impact on morbidity and mortality rates. To evaluate the operational performance of the Spanish version of the SCAN-SP scale of nutritional screening in childhood cancer to identify risk of malnutrition in children with cancer in the Fundación Hospital Pediatrico la Misericordia HOMI, Colombia. Diagnostic scale validation. Sample size of 96 patients with cancer diagnosis and hospitalized in the Centro de Cáncer Infantil de la fundación HOMI in Bogotá. (p = 0.5, n = 352, level of significance of 95 % and sample error of 0.1). To each patient was applied the Spanish version of the SCAN-SP and also was performed a complete nutritional evaluation taken as a reference method to determine the nutritional status.
