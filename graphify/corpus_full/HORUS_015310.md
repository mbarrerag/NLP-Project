# Hydrogeological zone of Colombia from existing information, including crystalline rocks.

A methodology is proposed to hydrogeologically zonify the country considering experiences in other countries and including igneous and metamorphic rocks based on existing information. After reviewing the information available and evaluating the use of remote sensor products, the main inputs consist of the Geological Atlas of Colombia (AGC) 1:500,000 version 2020 which constitutes the most detailed geology approved and homogenized by the GSC with complete national coverage and the underground storage anomaly of shallow water (GWS-GLDAS) obtained from the assimilation of data of the mission GRACE in the GLDAS model version 2.2. The proposed zonification consists basically in the updating of the provinces defined in IDEAM (2010) considering conceptual elements taken from other countries and refining the limits according to the chronostrategic units and faults of the AGC, besides of divisibilities of areas and hydrographic zones. The crystalline rocks are included by naming the zones of “basamento” (in IDEAM, 2010) as eight new hydrogeological provinces, the geology used by the use of this information to dissect its hydrogeological potential
