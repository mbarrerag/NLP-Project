# WOMEN OF MEDELLIN AND THEIR INCIDENCY IN THE LOCAL POWER FROM THE 1991 CONSTITUTION

The balance of the 30 years of the Colombian Constitution of 1991 has been addressed in this chapter from the experience of women from active citizenships and their association to social organizations or collective actions of women in the city of Medellín. We begin from recognizing that, with the constitutional reform, we lived the hope of a promise of democratic opening thanks to the entry into the political scene of new political movements and parties different from the Liberal and Conservative ones, with unprecedented flags and interests of inclusion and institutional dialogue. The democratizing spirit of the new charter (and later laws that regulated it) also sought to incorporate into the political life of the population sectors traditionally excluded.
