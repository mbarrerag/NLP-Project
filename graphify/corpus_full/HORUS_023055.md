# RESEARCH EXPERIENCES IN COLOMBIA

In Colombia coffee is the largest export agricultural product, it is cultivated by some 563,000 coffee growers in approximately 903,000 hectares, producing approximately 14 million 60 kg bags each year, constituting a major source of foreign exchange for the country and a development engine for the agricultural sector. However, in recent years the coffee market has presented some difficulties and challenges, which have placed the selling price of coffee below production costs. Moreover, productivity in this sector in Colombia has decreased by obtaining fewer bags and presenting higher costs per 60 kg sack. Harvesting, for example, represents 40 to 45% of production costs. This paper shares some of the research and contributions made from the perspective of Agricultural Engineering and Agronics in specific harvest topics (vibration application) and post-harves (physical properties studies-colorimetry of fruits) aimed at technifying the production of the crop, seeking to increase the yields of the labor and the reduction of costs, but always having as a priority condition of the technologies investigated to maintain the high quality of the coffee
