# Sustainable production of under-cover vegetables using biodigestion residues

The water pollution, the increase of chemical fertilizers linked to its use and the inefficiency in organic waste management are problematic that affect the rural sector. This is why the main objective of this project is to implement and evaluate a sustainable fertigation system through the use of biodigestion residues and photovoltaic energy for the production of low-covered vegetables at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Bogotá. Through the chemical characterization of biodigestion residues, the design and implementation of a fertilisation system with alternative energies, the use of lyxinated in fertiriego, and the improvement of soil properties with biomass of biodigestion, the aim is to reduce the environmental impact and improve energy efficiency in agriculture. This approach aims to offer a sustainable solution to the problems of pollution and high costs associated with the use of chemical fertilizers and inefficient management of organic waste, thus contributing to responsible agricultural production and to the Sustainable Development Goals. The project will be carried out in the greenhouses of the Campus headquarters Bogotá
