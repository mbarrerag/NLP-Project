# Proposal of content for the teaching of environmental accounting in public accounting programmes: the case of the universities of Antioquia

This thesis develops a microcurricular methodological proposal to strengthen the teaching-learning of environmental accounting (CA) in the programmes of public accounting. To this end, the existing proposals and the coherence of the subjects in CA of the universities of Antioquia are characterized. As a research question, what elements should have a micro-curricular methodological proposal to strengthen the teaching-learning of environmental accounting? Thus, the the thesis assumes the problematization before the insertion of the CA into the accounting curricula, taking as units of analysis 23 programs of public accounting in the department of Antioquia, where only 7 offer the subject with a focus on environmental accounting properly. The information analyzed shows that the micro-curricula restrict a more critical content of the CA because it focuses on recognizing methodologies of the CA in the framework of the paradigm of utility, so it requires cultural and practical changes that promote new strategies that allow to incorporate the dimension of sustainability.
