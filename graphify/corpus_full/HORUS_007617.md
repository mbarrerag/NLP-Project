# SEARCH FOR NATURAL ORIGIN AND RATIONAL DESIGN OF DERIVED DERIVED AS POSSIBLE REPLACEMENT AGENTS TO CROPHYRIFOS

Taking into account the positive results found so far in the project Application of computational tools for the identification of potential replacement agents to chlorpyrifos (HERMES code 61102), and the urgency of finding a replacement to chlorpyrifos, mainly for use in banana crops (higher export fruit by Colombia), this new project is proposed entitled "SEARCH OF NATURAL ORIGIN VOLATILES AND RACIAL DISENO DE DERIVATES AS POSSIBLES OF REEMPLATION AGENTS TO THE CLORPIIFOS. In this new project we take the organic molecula with the highest efficiency (called AD14) in the in vitro tests on AChE found in the HERMES project 61102 (tests also carried out on Drosophila melanogaster ), and through this new project we will make changes in the chemical structure of AD14, looking to increase the biochemical interactions with AChE that increase the pesticide action. This last one takes the positive pattern of the AD14. With all this, it is expected that in a future project, the best molecules derived from AD14 will be tested in in vivo tests and in the banana zone of Uraba
