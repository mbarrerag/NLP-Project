# Quality of life in people with epilepsy: beyond seizures

People with epilepsy have multiple barriers to recovery: access to medicines, comorbidities and social problems. The objective of this study is to determine psychosocial factors associated with the perception of quality of life in people with epilepsy in the department of Bolívar, Colombia, in 2022. Subjects and methods. Descriptive study of cross-section, correlational, with a stratified sample with a margin of error of 5%, according to the calculation of the average of people treated by epilepsy in Colombia. 174 people with an average age of 39.55 years, 50% men and 50% women participated. An instrument was used that determined sociodemographic data, quality of life (Quality of Life in Epilepsy Inventory-10), adherence to treatment (Morisky test), self-care behaviors, perception of disability and provision of health services.
