# MONITORING OF MULTI-TERM LINE PERFILE IN PHASE II BY TALKING DATA ANALYSIS TECHNIQUES

This paper presents the fAO control letter to monitor, in phase II, multivariate nonlinear profiles. The proposed chart is based on the "functional adjusted outwardness" statistics and its performance is evaluated using Monte Carlo simulations. A real example related to sugar production illustrates the use of the chart
