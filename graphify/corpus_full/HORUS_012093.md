# SIMULATION OF SMART GRIDS: CONNECTIVITY FOR THEIR MODELS THROUGH ETHERNET TECHNOLOGY

This work presents a simulation strategy that offers a series of steps and algorithms for the integration of communication networks into smart grid simulations. As a result, it is possible to identify the delays that can be generated by including communication technologies during the execution of the electrical network operations. As examples of application, test networks have been implemented in Matlab/Simulink, for simulation environments and in the programming language C for embedded systems. In addition, communications networks based on the TCP/IP model and Ethernet technology have been used. These applications constitute a benchmark for those interested in hardware-in-the-loop simulation strategies (HIL), in the field of electrical engineering
