# Puzolanas in the vicinity of Irra

Pozzolans are addition materials, of great importance, in the cement industry because of their ability to trap the free lime left in the clinker after the process of sintering raw materials (calize, clay and correctors), reducing the heat of hydration and production costs. Volcanic materials in theory could have puzolanic properties, so it was planned to do a sampling and testing by Fratini's method for the volcanic-sedimentary sequence that is found in the vicinity of Irra, obtaining excellent results
