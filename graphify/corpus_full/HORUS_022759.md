# ANNO- COLOMBIA ENTRY MOBILITY

The current model of the electricity system is constantly changing. This is due to the integration of new electricity generation systems with renewable sources, progress of telecommunications systems, measurement and energy storage that aim to optimize the network and reduce greenhouse gases (GHG). One of these applications is the electrical mobility that aims to reduce the contribution of GHGs in the transport sector, through the replacement of vehicles that make use of fossil fuels. This document presents the initiatives of electric mobility nationwide in Colombia since 2015 with the Ideario proposed by UPME that became a first step towards the massification of technology because it identified the transport sector as one of the main energy consumers and consequently producer of GHG emissions. The electric vehicle becomes the alternative to reduce GHG emissions and to promote this initiative at the national level, the country has been underwriting a series of agreements such as the Paris agreement that encourages the reduction of GHGs through the use of the electric vehicle. In 2017, Colombia ratifies its presence in this agreement by means of Law 1788 which proposes strategies to reduce GHGs in the transport sector accompanied by a regulatory and regulatory framework of the Ministry of Finance and Ministry of Transport
