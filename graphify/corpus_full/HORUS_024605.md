# GEOGRAPHICAL ACCESS TO ORAL HEALTH SERVICES IN THE TOLIMA DEPARTMENT, COLOMBIA

Abstract - A descriptive cross-sectional study carried out for the 47 municipalities of the department of Tolima, Colombia, neighboring the department of Cundinamarca, where Bogotá, the capital of the country, is located. The objective was to analyze the geographical access barriers and the inventory of human talent and dental services in this department. A distance and time measurement system was applied to identify the duration of travel from each municipality to Ibagué (departmental capital) in public transport. The determinant of geographical access barrier endorsed by the World Health Organization (WHO) is a travel duration of more than four hours. It was found that the first level of public sector care is present in all municipalities, the second level is at six; the third level is only in Ibagué. Public transport represents a geographical access barrier in six municipalities; the number of dentists in the public sector does not satisfactorily cover the needs of the population. In the department of Tolima there are geographical access barriers specifically for oral health. Key words: Access to health, geographical access barriers, mechanisation, travel time, Colombia
