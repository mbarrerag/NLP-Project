# Orthotropic compressive behavior of the gyroid lattice structure of PLA produced via material extrusion additive manufacturing

The Gyroid Lattice Structure of PLA material was made with the open porosity of human bone by Material Extrusion Additive Manufacturing in order to examine its mechanical behavior. Five tensile test specimens, five compression test specimens, and five gyroid scaffolds were produced using identical PLA and printing parameters, employing a Creality Ender-3 V2 3D printer. The experimental measurements from the tensile and compression tests were used to determine the elastic orthotropic behavior of the PLA made by material extrusion additive manufacturing. Nine parameters that describe the elastic orthotropic behavior of printed PLA were determined from the tensile and compression tests. These nine parameters were used to simulate the compression tests of the five gyroid scaffolds. This study shows that the gyroid lattice structure exhibits more stiffness in the printing direction, and also shows whatever being the printing direction, the behavior of the gyroid lattice structure is orthotropic. The displacement in one of the perpendicular directions is nearly twice that of another perpendicular direction, whatever the printing direction. Therefore, the gyroid structure made by Material Extrusion Additive Manufacturing has an orthotropic component due to its geometry, and another orthotropic component due to printing direction. Simulating gyroid lattice structures as elastic orthotropic material reveals how the printing parameters affect its mechanical behavior
