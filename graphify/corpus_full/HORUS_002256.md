# Obtaining oil for biodiesel production from dry and wet microalgal material

The depletion of fossil fuels has generated a search for new renewable energy sources, such as biofuels. Biofuels such as biodiesel are produced from a transesterification process using, in this case, third-generation raw material, microalgae. The biodiesel production from microalgae depends on the number of accumulated lipids, which are influenced by the culture medium (nutrients), culture method, pH, photoperiod, aeration, temperature, and light intensity. Additionally, the lipid extraction methods and transesterification processes influence biodiesel quality. This chapter intends to compare these two methods regarding efficiency, obtaining oil to produce biodiesel from dry and wet microalgal material and considering the airlift bioreactor in the process
