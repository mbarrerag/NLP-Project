# My Nation's Marimonos: History(s) of a Songed Bonanza 1976 – 1985

In the 1970s and 1980s, a phenomenon known as La Bonanza Marimbera took place. It consisted of the trafficking of marijuana from the coasts of Guajira to the United States. In this period, the marimberos would emerge: men of peasant origins and urban peripheries that, by becoming the main intermediaries between the sowers of marijuana and consumers in the United States, would climb socially towards the high spheres of La Guajira and the Colombian Caribbean. They became known throughout the region for their unscathed way of spending the money, their eccentricities and the mecenargo that would offer the musicians of Vallenato. This thesis exposes the relationship between the traffic of marijuana and the consummation of the vallento as the Colombian musical genre by excellence between 1976 and 1985.
