# HISTORY OF INTER-SPECTIFIC OXG HIBRICULTIVES

The inter-specific hybrids (OxG) of oil palm result from the cross-over between the two species of economic importance for agro-industry: Elaeis oleifera, native of America, and Elaeis guineensis, coming from Africa. The histo-ria of the OxG hybrids part of the very history of their parent companies: in 1926, the palms of E. guineensis officially entered as commercial cultivation to Cen-troamérica, and for the same time the diversity of the centers of origin of E. oleifera and its main qualities was recognized. Subsequently, the E. oleifera was introduced into programs of inter-specification, in order to introduce its characteristics in E. guineensis and obtain in the medium term the cultivations that combined qualities of interest of both species. The development of the hybrid-two OxG organically developed in the 1960s due to scientific curiosity and then the need to have a lasting solution for the plant protection problems, especially the Ogolditis, however, their first evolution was not linear and productive
