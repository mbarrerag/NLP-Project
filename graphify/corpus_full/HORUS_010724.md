# INTERDISCIPLINARY TEAMS IN NEUROMUSCULAR DISEASES ¿PARADIGM OR UTOPIA?

The new landscape in the comprehensive management of neuromuscular diseases has required of conformation of multi and interdisciplinary teams in order to supply follow up and treatment alternatives due to multi systemic affectation derived from neuromuscular condition. The objective of this dissertation is to try to answer some questions such as: ¿What is the meaning of the term proactive care in neuromuscular disease? ¿Which advantages has the multidisciplinary follow up to a patient with neuromuscular disease? ¿Does a patient with neuromuscular disease has to be evaluated always by a neuromuscular team? If i don ́t belong to a multidisciplinary team in my everyday practice ¿what can I do with my patient with a neuromuscular condition? These and other questions will be addressed under scientific, technical and health care system perspectives
