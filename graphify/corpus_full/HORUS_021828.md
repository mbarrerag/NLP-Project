# Proposal of business scheme to promote the replacement or complementarity of diesel generation and reduce the costs of providing electricity in a case of study of the non-interconnected areas of Colombia.

The Non-Interconnected Zones of Colombia (ZNI) currently cater to 200,956 users in 1,769 locations, and to 36,000 users with individual solar solutions. The installed capacity is 303,409 kW in total, of which 265,666 kW correspond to Diesel plants, equivalent to 88%. The ZNI emissions are 855 gCO2Eq/kWh and the Unitary Cost (CU) is more than twice the National Interconnected System (SIN). Seeking alternatives for the reduction of costs is selected a study location, applying criteria based on size, quality, efficiency and cost, resulting in Miraflores (Guaviare) selected.
