# Bioprospecting of in vivo tissues and in vitro Alibertia patinoi cell cultures

Bioprospecting in Colombia represents an opportunity for growing development. Plants such as borodó have been used for their medicinal properties, attributed by popular knowledge. However, their growth characteristics require alternatives to obtain metabolites of interest. This work takes up the medicinal uses to evaluate some of them and study the chemical composition of the plant that can explain its pharmaceutical potential. In addition, it evaluates that same potential in its cultures of suspended cells and under the effect of elicitors studying present compounds. This study starts from a review of previous reports of the species Alibertia patinoi and other species of the same genus. Known for its characteristic aroma, a study of the composition of volatile compounds in the fruit was carried out taking into account different states of maturation using microextraction of solid phase in the head space.
