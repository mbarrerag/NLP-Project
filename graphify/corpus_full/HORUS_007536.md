# OBSERVATORY OF NATIONAL PUBLIC YOUTH POLICY

Our proposal for a National Observatory of Public Policy will be constituted by an interdisciplinary work network that combines the experience of professionals from different university headquarters in disciplines such as: philosophy, political science, business administration, sociology, environmental engineering, among others. The network that aims to closely link headquarters of our university such as Palmira, Bogotá, Manizales with very important territories where very significant youth experiences are experienced, such as Chocó, Huila, Neiva and Cali, so that we manage to follow-up the policy process with metropolitan, urban-rural coverage. It is also noteworthy that for the proposal there is also the support of researchers and researchers from different institutions of the capital, regions of the country and South American countries. The nature of the work of the network is also collaborative and aims to think and build critical and creative elements, besides practical, to contribute from our university contexts to the design of national youth policy. The main category of follow-up, product of creative, innovative and research work that OBJUN has done is the work of youth. This category involves a set of conceptual and observation and social literature is called the social research. BJUN. This experience has enabled us to build from the university practice of research the critical capacity of independence and autonomy to support and collaborate with state institutions at the national, district and regional levels and their bodies responsible for the topic of youth, developing our own approach, which we call youth approach, as well as with international organizations dealing with these same problems
