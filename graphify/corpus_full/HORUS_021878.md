# Design of a calculation tool based on the stimulation of circadian rhythms.

The most common way in which the circadian rhythms of the different functions of the human body are synchronized is with the day - night cycles of the natural light. When this synchronization breaks down it can lead to physical, mental and behavioural changes in human beings.The light sensors in the retina send two types of signals: one that reaches the visual cortex of the brain and provides the sense of vision.Another generated by the ganglional tissue of the retina that reaches the suprachiasmatic nucleus in the brain, which uses the pineal gland for such synchronization.The light that reaches the suprachiasmatic nucleus is called effective circadian light.For this reason the light perceived throughout the day (whether of solar or electrical origin), contributes to the drag (syncronization or desyncation) of the circadian cycles. One of the circadian cycles used in the human body is that of wake-dream, which is regulated to the secretion of the hormone melatonin; It is therefore very important to produce the correct type of lighting to which the users are subjected to the circadian cycles. ), it was found that from the spectrum change of a 4-channel LED light source, circadian CS stimulus values suitable for any of the schedules set in UL recommendation can be achieved. It was found that the CS value is very sensitive to the configuration of the CERC (spectral contribution of the circadian response) given by very small variations in the Duv of the lighting source. Similarly, it was evident that contrary to what would be expected, there are spectral configurations with warm CERC that would induce circadian stimulus to generate alert status, and spectra with cold CERC would be able to reduce it. In the case of a study of lighting design, it was possible to meet the UL24480 standard thanks to the adjustment of SPD with dynamic lighting. Finally, the current standards of circadian lighting were compared with the purpose of assessing the differences and similarities in favour of establishing an appropriate design criterion that contemplates such standards
