# Corrosion resistance of TiAIVCuN coatings deposited by co-sputting technique

In this work, TiAlVCuN coatings were deposited on stainless steel substrates 316 L using a reactive cosputting system with HIPIMS and magnetron sources. A titanium/aluminium target and a vanadium/copper white were used as a deposit material. The coatings obtained were characterized chemically, microstructurally and morphologically. The variation of the deposition voltage in the white of V/Cu (with 5 levels between 510 V and 580 V), generated a change in the chemical composition represented mainly in the variation of the quantity of V, changing between 3% and 12%. These coatings exhibited a columnar growth, without Cu precipitation on the surface. In addition, a FCC type cubic structure, corresponding to the formation of a solid solution of TiAlVN without the presence of Cu in the metallic state, so this could be dissolved in the grain edges of the matrix. On the other hand, the resistance to corrosion of the coatings and the substrate was evaluated in two types of electrolytes (Solution 3.5 wt. % of NaCl and Lacta).
