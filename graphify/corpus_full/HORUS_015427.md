# THE POSIBILITY OF AN IMPOSIBILITY

Why did Lacan make him laugh at the word freedom, why did he never speak of freedom? First of all to start answering such questions it would be necessary to say that freedom for psychoanalysis is a paradox, not something that is sought to do: a free subject. In that non-freedom from psychoanalysis opens up a whole possibility, unlike believing that there is a condemnation for not being free, for psychoanalysis the condemnation is trying to be free, the possibility is in that impossibility
