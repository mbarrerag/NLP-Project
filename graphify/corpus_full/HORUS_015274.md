# MODELING COMPLEXITY AND CHAOS IN THE HIGH ANDEAN MOUNTAIN: GIS, BIG DATA AND AI APPLIED TO CLIMATE VARIABILITY

Summary Due to the complexity and many variables that influence climate variability in high andean mountain landscapes, many unanswered questions and methodological proposals currently arise which attempt to offer new explanatory alternatives to this important modern important challenge: ¿How could factors such as the geography, geomorphology, climatology and astronomy, among others, be considered and integrated into this important scientific discussion?, ¿how different are climate cycles in different regions and latitudes worldwide?, ¿how many climate cycles were there and what was their intensity?, ¿were the traces left by the different glaciations or glacial events erased or superimposed?, ¿how long will the remaining glaciers last in the tropical andean countries?. These are some of the questions waiting to be answered. The study of the remnant andean glaciers has become very important in recent years since they preserve important traces useful for paleoclimatic and paleoecological climate change reconstruction and are considered important thermometers to estimate climatic variability. Precisely to advance in this need for study and modeling climate change in tropical glaciers in the northern Andean, high-quality spatial information must be obtained and analyzed, which is very heterogeneous, must have high temporal and spatial resolution, and must be analyzed with non-traditional technological tools, especially suitable or adapted. to display hard-to-detect patterns and provide information about the past processes and dynamics that are impossible to find or discover by conventional means. The integration of geographic information systems (GIS), modern Big Data and artificial intelligence (AI), are some of the latest generation tools which are being applied in the main research centers worldwide, precisely to advance in this objective. It is our objective to show some methodological proposals and the results obtained by applying these technologies to research on tropical glaciers and climate change in Colombia
