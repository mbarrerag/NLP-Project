# Characterization of the application for preoperative paraclinicals and economic impact of non-adherence to the recommendations of the NICE 2016 guide at the National University Hospital of Colombia.

The pre-anesthetic evaluation consultation based on clinical practice guidelines seeks to reduce preventable perioperative complications, and in some cases requires the application of paraclinical to supplement the information obtained from the patient; however, a non-individualized evaluation is related to higher economic costs and lack of opportunity in surgeries. Thus, the NICE 2016 guide becomes an excellent tool by having classification scales and studies that we are familiar with and easily available
