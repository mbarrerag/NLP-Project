# Digital transformation: perceptions and post-pandemic adaptation in the education sector – a case study

The digital transformation of the public sector seeks to create an effective and efficient government through technology. In the education sector, the pandemic accelerated the process of adaptation and the use of new technologies, demonstrating, at the global level, complex situations related to university work and its adaptation to virtuality. This paper presents a study prepared at the National University of Colombia, after the 2020 crisis, which sought to establish the perception of digital transformation in the entity. A study was developed with a descriptive methodology, in order to validate the advances and challenges that persist in relation to the digitization of the university in its activities. This, from a non-probabilistic sample of 519 people from the academic community. The main results indicate that there are aspects of context that facilitate or represent barriers to the use of digital tools, such as age, territory and university headquarters. The main problem with respect to information and communications technologies is access to the Internet, followed by physical access to related infrastructure and lack of skills.
