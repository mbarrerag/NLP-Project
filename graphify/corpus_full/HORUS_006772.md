# Integration of remote sensors, GIS techniques and isotopic analysis for the identification and analysis of potential recharge zones in a sector of the Ubaté-Chiquinquirá aquifer system, Colombia

The identification and analysis of the potential recharge zones of the Ubaté-Chiquinquirá aquifer system are key to maintain the reserves of the water resource of the subsoil, avoiding that the extraction rate exceeds the recharge rate, due to the increase of the demand of the resource as an alternative to the conventional surface water sources. To this end, satellite images obtained by remote sensors were processed and used Geographic Information Systems (GIS) as a tool to delimit the areas with the greatest recharge potential through the Hierarchical Analysis of the Pondered Water (AHP), overlay and assessment of the favorability of the recharge of (5) parameters defined by the IDEAM; geology, slope, density of structural guidelines, coverage, use of the soil and types of soils.
