# Residential Segregation: Macroproject of National Social Interest “Citadel New West” in the City of Medellín (2018-2022)

The work seeks to give a theoretical reading and empirical background, as the projects of mass production of social housing are being developed in the urban peripheries of the Colombian cities; as a result of the public housing policies imposed in the country in the last two decades of this century; the above having as basis of analysis the Macroproject of National Social Interest “Citadel New West” in the city of Medellín. In this sense, it is interesting to document how the location of this type of projects in the urban periphery of the city could have implications in the generation of residential segregation patterns. The main objective is to analyze the processes of socio-spatial organization in the Macroproject of National Social Interest (MISN) Ciudadela New West in the City of Medellín in terms of residential segregation, with the aim of identifying possible patterns of residential segregation both objective and subjective. The methodology uses some official data associated with the topic and analyzes them around a small survey of the population of the New West City. the results allow to show that there are some patterns of objective and subjective residential segregation in the study area. s of residential segregation, in order to identify possible patterns of residential segregation, both objective and subjective..
