# Practices of self-justice and scope of transitional justice in times of ‘total peace’: JEP case 05 (North of Cauca and South of Cauca Valley)

This research result presents some findings, analysis and testimonies of the scope and limits of transitional justice in the North of Cauca and the South of Valle del Cauca (Case 05 of the Special Jurisdiction for Peace) in the face of the justice proper to the peoples in the midst of the ‘Total Peace’ and the military dispute that persists in the territory. The field work—supported by interviews, listening, dialogues, ceremonies, walks, Mingas, focus groups, academic meetings, debates with authorities— confirms spirituality as the axis of the practices of self-justice and allows us to infer that, rather than an ethnocide or ecocide, what happened and continues to happen in the territory is a biocultural extermination of the indigenous peoples and the Black people, inhabitants of this part of the Colombian geography. Some of the epistemic barriers that persist in transitional justice and hinder the progress towards a material and cultural interculturality are also identified
