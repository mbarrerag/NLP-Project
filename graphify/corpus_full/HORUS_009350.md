# MECHANOUANTIC STUDY OF SPECTROSCOPY AND ANISOTROPIC EFFECTS IN ENDOHEDRIC FULLEREN HF@C60

The synthesis of endohedral fullerenes in which a molecule of HF is encapsulated within a fullerene C60 has provided a new case study showing the presence of anisotropic effects capable of breaking down the quantum states of the degenerated system when the molecule is in gas phase. In this study we suppose a rupture of the symmetry of the system caused by a deformation of the fullerene system that affects the energy levels of the molecule and causes the rupture of the degeneration of the lower excitated rotational state.[1] Using the MCTDH method and a potential energy surface obtained by calculations of electronic structure ab initio we reproduce the spectroscopy of the HF@C60 system.[2] We use different models to introduce asymmetries from uniaxial and flat deformations compatible with the effects of the first rotating and experimentally measured fully-blown network
