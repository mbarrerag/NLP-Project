# Cloud Technology Management

The Semillero de Investigación en Gestión de Tecnologías en la Nube has as main objective to contribute to the development of research and training of professionals in the area of Technology Management in the Cloud. To this, the seedbed will focus on the following topics: - The adoption of technologies in the cloud in organizations - The challenges of managing technologies in the cloud - The opportunities of managing technologies in the cloud The seedbed will develop in the city of Manizales, Caldas, Colombia, and will be aimed at undergraduate and postgraduate students of the areas of Information Systems Administration, Computing Sciences, and related careers. Information and communication technologies (ICT) have been showing different professional failures in booming topics, within which the Cloud Computing is located. Under this context, and focused on the advancement of knowledge and generation technologies that are not only implemented academically but also beneficial for companies in the IT sector, it aims to focus on contributing to the development of research and training of professionals in the area of Technology Management related to the cloud
