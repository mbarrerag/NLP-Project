# Importance of post-anesthetic adverse effects, which should we avoid? Perspective of patients in a University Hospital in Bogotá, Colombia.

The anesthetic and surgical act leads to the development of a series of adverse effects after anesthesia, commonly nausea and vomiting, pain, chills, etc. Both medical staff and patients can have a different perception based on own judgments and experiences of which, should be avoided,in search of the quality of medical care it is appropriate to personalized attention focused on the needs of each patient, in order to satisfy the interindividual needs. To recognize the less desirable adverse effects within a group of patients undergoing elective surgery in order to evaluate and make decisions based on the preferences of the patients. Primary, observational, descriptive, prospective study. According to the established protocol and corresponding emission, data were collected in preparation of surgical rooms, in a virtual collection format type survey, was divided into 3 parts, the first of this was socio-demographic data, in the second was questioned about the presence or not about previous surgeries, the presentation of undesirable symptoms, in the last section were described as the nine adverse symptoms postoperative (8%), and each of these were the patient was the patient was the patient's preference. In this study, patients had a willingness to pay higher for pain, followed in order to remember intraoperative, vomiting, nausea, headache, myalgia, chills, odynophagia and, finally, drowsiness, in the analysis by subgroups, no changes were evidenced in the ranking by age, sex or schooling. Knowing preferences and concerns regarding post-anesthetic symptoms of patients prior to a surgical procedure is a useful tool in the preoperative in order to define the unpleasant symptoms that generate more concern in each patient, making an individualized anesthetic plan addressing their preferences and obtaining greater satisfaction from the care received
