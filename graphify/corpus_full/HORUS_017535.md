# CLINICAL STANDARD BASED ON EVIDENCY: DIAGNOSTIC, TREATMENT AND FOLLOW-UP OF THE PATIENT WITH OBESITY AND MASSIVE WEIGHT LESS IN NATIONAL UNIVERSITARY HOSPITAL

This ECBE seeks to provide informed recommendations in the best evidence, using as a main tool a flow chart that represents the health care process, focused on the treatment and follow-up of adult patients with obesity and those in mass weight loss process, attended by external consultation, surgery rooms and hospitalization of the National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN). ECBE may be used by health professionals or in training (general practitioners, internists, endocrinologists, geriatricians, sports doctors, bariatric surgeons, plastic surgeons, nurses, nutritionists, psychologists, physiotherapists, pharmaceutical chemists, clinical laboratory and administrative staff), involved in the care of adult patients with obesity. This ECBE can also be used by teachers of the HUN and staff in formation of the Institution. Finally, this ECBE may be used by those responsible for decision-making in the Institution, in order to facilitate the implementation process
