# MODELING UV-C INACTIVATION OF FOODBORNE PATHOGEN BY CFD.

Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) was used to describe UV-C survival of Escherichia coli O157:H7, Salmonella Typhimurium and Listeria monocytogenes by the calculation of the average radiation intensity on the surface of bacterial suspension irradiated for 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15 min at 75 mm from the lamp. The inactivation of S. Typhimurium fitted better to the log-linear model, whilst survival curves of E. coli O157:H7 and L. monocytogenes were described with Weibull model because of their progressive adaptation to the UV-C stress. CFD is a numerical tool that allows to predict UV-C inactivation for the reduction of foodborne pathogen on plant food and to improve the design of disinfection equipment
