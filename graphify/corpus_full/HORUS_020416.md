# Determination of the involvement of YmcA protein in the regulation of hemolysis and biofilm formation in Staphylococcus aureus

Staphylococcus aureus is a positive gram-life bacteria, opportunistic pathogen with the ability to generate life-threatening infections in humans by the production of several virulence factors, in the same way many biological processes of this bacterium are not fully understood, such as the processing of its RNA. Recently, in our laboratory, the Qrp/YheA protein was identified which possesses the domain Com_YlbF, the deletion of the qrp gene produces an increase in erythrocyte hemolysis and decrease in the formation of biofilm, possibly associated with the deregulation of some factors of virulence of the bacterium. Another protein with the domain Com_YlbF is YmcA, whose absence in Bacillus subtilis inhibits the formation of biofilm, through the alteration of the activity of the YNasa, with whom it also interacts. In S. aureus the role of YmcA is not known.
