# Synthesis and characterization of Hass avocado seed starch nanoparticles for use in biofilm development

The seed represents between 11 and 16 % of the weight of the fruit and is considered a waste, however, since it is rich in starch it can be a potential source of this biopolymer. Furthermore, an alternative to reduce the carbon footprint generated by the plastic is starch, a biodegradable polymer with interesting physical and chemical properties. Although starch has been studied for many years, and one of its uses has been in the development of biofilms, it has not yet been sufficiently worked with the nanoparticles of starch, which could offer an improvement in the industry. Therefore, the objective of this work was to synthesize nanoparticles of starch from the seed of avocado Hass in order to develop a bioplyme composed in a partial way by the native starch of the nanoparticles and the xyloglucan as an additive. For the extraction and cleaning of the starch, physical and chemical methods were used.
