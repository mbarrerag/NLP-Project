# General concepts on the design of control systems

This paper shows a group of general concepts on control system design. All aspects of this task are mentioned and a brief explanation is presented for each one of them, giving particular importance to the possible general recommendations useful during process design, the design of the control system and an integrated task that develops at the same time the process design and the design of the control system. In the end, an example of automation and control is presented using a real application on a thread dyeing process
