# PAPAYITA SMELL - VASCONCELLEA CUNDINAMARCES

The Caricaceae family, the papaya of smell is the second most recognized species after the cosmopolitan Carica papaya L. Its long botanical name, Vasconcellea cundinamarcensis V.M. Badillo = V. pubescens (DC) SolmsLaub, gives testimony of its entangled biological history within the genus pasconcellea. Through the Andes, it is also known as papayuela, tapaculo, chilacuan, sapira and chamburo, arequipeña papaya and high papaya. Along with other native species, the papaya of smell fulfilled a singular food and medicinal function for the indigenous populations before the arrival of the Spaniards. Today, it is the most commercial of the 24 species of papayas of height. However, it is still appreciated in the home gardens of peasant and indigenous families, mainly in Colombia and Ecuador. Some cultures, such as Misak in Colombia, confer to the papaya tree of smell a mystical power of abundance and fertility
