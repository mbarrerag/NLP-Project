# Impact of COVID 19 on Colombian labour law.

This research paper analyzes whether the actions taken by the Colombian national government have been sufficient or not to eliminate the consequences on unemployment and consequently on labour relations that came about by the arrival of COVID 19 in our country. To develop the above, an analysis is made of other pandemics that the world has experienced and the consequences that these pandemics had in those times in labor law. Subsequently, an analysis is made of how COVID 19 has been experienced from the point of view of employment in other countries of the world with the aim of studying the possibility of finding other strategies that have not been applied in our country. After this, the measures adopted by the Colombian government and the unemployment figures that were before and after the pandemic concluded that the management carried out by our government was good and effective, however, in Colombia, it has not been possible to reach the unemployment figures that had been before the pandemic because in December 2019 the unemployment figure was 9.5% and in December 2021 the unemployment figure was 118%
