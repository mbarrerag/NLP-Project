# RADOMIOSARCOMA OF THE NASAL Wing IN A PATIENT PATIENT PATIENT PATIENT

Given the preferential nasal breathing that the pediatric population has in the first months of life, nasal obstruction has more repercussions at the clinical level than an adult, which makes it a priority to diagnose and manage. One of the main causes of nasal obstruction in children is nasal masses, which due to their multiple etiology cause quite a diagnostic uncertainty in the medical team. This article presents the case of a 10-month-old patient with a mass in the fast-growing nasal wing; a rapid diagnosis of rhabdomyosarcoma is made that requires proper management. Alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma occurs unusually at this location, which is why it is considered important to present this case to keep it in the diagnostic algorithm as a possibility and that, as in the present case, an early diagnosis may be made
