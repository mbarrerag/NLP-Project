# Model of a computer-assisted four-bar mechanism synthesis system

The model proposed for the Four Bar Mechanism Synthesis is based on the following characteristics: Simplicity, Efficiency and Accuracy. Highlighting in the model implemented is the versatility to solve various problems of Four Bar Mechanism Synthesis: Position Synthesis, Speed Synthesis, Acceleration Synthesis and Pathway Synthesis. All of which may have solutions for a varied number of precision points. In the validation of the prototype presented at the end of this article reference is made to 12 precision points but could be more the precision points required for the prototype. The design of the model was structured according to the methodology given below: a) Plan a hypothesis b) Transform the mechanism into an equivalent but simpler one c) Reduce the number of incognites d) Line up the resulting system for its solution and e) Dimension "the mechanism with the required precision. To finalize the summary we refer to the hypothesis in which the implemented model is maintained: "The fundamental for the design of four bars mechanisms are the relative positions"
