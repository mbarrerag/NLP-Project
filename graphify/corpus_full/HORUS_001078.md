# ASSESSMENT OF THE DELIRIUM'S DOLTURE AND MONITORING IN INTENSIVE CARE UNIT: SCOPE REVIEW

Abstract Objective: Mapping the scales in the evaluation of pain and monitoring of delirium in intensive care units. A scope review was developed using the MesH descriptor with terms such as Pain, Delirium and Critical Care Unit. The BOOLEAN AND was included in the databases BVS, SciELO, EBSCO Dissertations, PubMed, CINAHL and LILACS. A total of 33 articles were selected, some focused on scales used for pain assessment and monitoring of delirium. For the evaluation of pain and monitoring of delirium, the use of validated scales, which objectively measure pain such as CPOT, BPS, NRS and delirium with CAM ICU, ICDSCED in intensive care units, as well as its application in a routine way, during procedures and at different times, is recommended
