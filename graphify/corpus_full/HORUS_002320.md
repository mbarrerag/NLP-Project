# Diversity and patterns of occurrence of the genus Vanilla in naïves with different degrees of intervention in the basin of the river Valle (Bahía Solano, Chocó)

Vanilla is one of the most basal genera of orchids, with pantropical distribution and with about 100 species described. They are considered nomadic lianas, adapted to disperse and explore habitats with light and nutrients necessary to produce offspring. Colombia, as part of the center of diversification of the genus, reports 27 species. Despite their ecological and economic relevance, little is known about the distribution, adaptations and in general, the history of life, which makes it difficult to implement conservation programs. In Bahía Solano (Chocó), extensive flooded swamp forests dominated by Euterpe oleracea, present high abundance of wild vanilla and historically were used by the communities in the peaceful chocoano for rice cultivation. These ecosystems present today a mosaic of successive coverages where various species of the genus are distributed. The main objective of the research was to identify the species of Vanilla and analyze their patterns of occurrence in relation to structural, floristic, eductural and luminal variables in the river basin Valle, in Bahía Solano (Chocó).
