# OBTAINATION OF POROUS METALS BY TERMS OF SINTERIZATION-DISOLLUTION TECHNICAL.

The research aims to obtain MPs, through the technique in solid state, known as SD and the most used in the production of MPs starting from the solid state metal, with the aim of producing MPs with a greater integrity of the surface and a suitable network connection between each pores. In previous studies together with the UAM-Mexico we develop an improved technique that implies obtaining these materials in a faster, efficient and at a lower cost, which was already patented in Mexico and is intended to be implemented in our laboratory of the UN-Manizales. The MPs will be manufactured from chips of aluminium base alloys. In the process the shavings are then placed within a stainless steel mould, with a cavity in cylindrical form of 2 cm of diameter and 10 cm of length, to obtain samples of 2 cm of diameter and lengths that can be 1.6, 3 or 4, according to the ASTM standard. Subsequently, the shavings are compacted using a tap acting through a hydraulic press at different pressures of compaction, between the 5 PSI and 40 PSI are used with increments of 5 and 5 years of thermal. This device will be connected to a vacuum pump and an inert gas tank that allow the exchange of its internal atmosphere. For this research, it is intended to link four undergraduate students who are part of the research seedling who will be in charge of the manufacture of the samples necessary to determine their structural and mechanical properties. This study will be able to determine the optimal Ts and compaction pressure for the manufacture of MPs, from chips and using the SD technique. Knowing these values will help us in the design of this type of materials for specific applications, mainly in the absorption of energy under impact. The proposal allows the development of research skills in the students who make part of the research seedling. These skills will be acquired through the realization of practical work during the execution of the project. The proposed technique will be developed in LabMMA, which is the only laboratory for the development of practical activities with which the Department of Industrial Engineering obtained from the seedbed.
