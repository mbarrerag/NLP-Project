# Nursing Care Plan for Neurocritical Patient Care available in a Mobile Virtual Tool

The implementation of nursing care plans to achieve the best clinical results in patients, have positioned care as a study object that seeks to describe initiatives and strategies to nursing professionals to provide individualised services through innovation processes mediated by technological resources, which has provided rapid consultation tools for decision-making in the development of daily activities. To formulate a nursing care plan for the care of neurocritical patients, available for use in a mobile application. Quantitative study with descriptive design developed throughout three phases that were conformed by an integrative review for the mapping of nursing interventions; the formulation of a care plan based on evidence and postulates of the theory of technological competence as nursing care (TCNI) and finally the design of a functional prototype of a mobile application, for the transfer of the care plan.
