# THE SOCIAL AND PEOPLE'S REBELION OF 2021 IN COLOMBIA ELEMENTS FOR ITS COMPRENSION

The strike of April 28, 2021 and the social rebellion of the months that followed marked a milestone in the history of the rebellions in Colombia. The events that occurred in those months showed novel and relatively unpublished aspects expressed in different forms of mobilization, resistance and social protest. The rebellion of 2021 had a spark that ignited the meadow: the malhadado and regressive project of tax reform of the government of Iván Duque that evidenced the indolence of the government- such in front of the severe deterioration -through the effect of the pan-demony of the COVID- 19- of the work and the income already precarious. This landscape showed different con-figurations on the malaise of the population in general and the discontent of different social and popular sectors in particular, at various levels and scales, with precise locations -although they are not located -which could well be recorded as a heterogeneity convergent in the process of social breakdown and in the process of social breakdown. The new cultural and political moment unleashed by the social rebellion, although it is clear that this after-ciende the electoral event. The months of direct confrontation (May and June 2021), as well as the subsequent ones of reflux and configuration of spaces of articulation, expressed as a general feature an anti-systemic disobedience. Also an attempt to break the normalized orders of domination. Although with the coming of the rebellion they were revealing their limits, the process lived did not find the definitive closure. It opened, yes, a new social horizon in construction that could combine, in historical perspective, the strengthening of the democratic dynamics and the combination of other destitute dynamics of constituent scope, which could well have future stable configurations of counterhegemonic character. Taking as a reference this general framework, which essentially accounts for a new cultural and political situation, this book has been proposed to contribute to the characterization of the social and popular rebellion lived during the year 2021, as a historical milestone and at the same time as an unfinished process, which transcends the facts, the moments of intensity and direct confrontation recorded between April and July that year
