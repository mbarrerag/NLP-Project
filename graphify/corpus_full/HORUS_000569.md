# Maternity and paternity course for pregnant women, in clinical contexts in Valledupar, Colombia

Introduction The accompanying approach to motherhood is a strategy that allows timely education for pregnant couples, in order to facilitate the appropriation of skills to safely address the physical, emotional and social changes inherent in the gestational process. The hospital scenario may be an opportunity to encourage adherence to this type of courses. Objective To know the experiences of hospitalized pregnant women in relation to the implementation of a maternity and paternity course. Methodology A descriptive qualitative study was carried out in a population of 12 pregnant women hospitalized in an IPS in the city of Valledupar, using in-depth interviews. Internal and external validation was guaranteed through triangulation. Likewise, fundamental ethical aspects such as confidentiality of data and respect for the dignity of the participants were considered. Results The participants, mostly primordist, expressed that the information received allowed them to clarify doubts and strengthen knowledge in topics such as breastfeeding, humanized delivery and self-care. Conclusion The participants highlighted their positive impact on the emotional and practical preparation for maternity areas, correction of myths and construction of new knowledge
