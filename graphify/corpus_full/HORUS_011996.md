# LEGAL AND ECONOMIC ANALYSIS OF AMENDMENTS INTRODUCED TO THE PROGRAMME OF BENEFITS BY COLLABORATION BY DECREE 253 OF 23 FEBRUARY 2022

This work acquires a fundamental value in the context of the dialogue on the current state of the normative and institutional regime of the right of free competition in Colombia, a value that illustrates the challenges that public competition policy must face, challenges that arise from the necessary adjustments imposed by time on the becoming of norms and institutions, as well as from the challenges that arise from the reflections on the dynamics of markets and their role in society. It joins the importance of this work, a call for public discussion of reforms, unfortunately at this time as an ex post circumstance, due to the vacuum that leaves the absence of public discussion, prior and sufficient on which at one time they should have been reform projects that were advanced both by the Legislature and the Executive
