# M.I. SHAPIR¿S THEORY OF VERSE AND HIS WORKS ON THE HISTORY OF POETICS STUDIES

The article examines the significance of works on the history of philology in M.I. Shapir's research legacy, and their influence on shaping his academic personality and style. The authors explore the interconnections between theoretical and historical studies of one of the most original and interesting Russian scholars of versification and philologists of the second half of the 20th century." Key words: M.I. Shapir, linguistic poetics, history of philology, theory of verse, Russian formalism
