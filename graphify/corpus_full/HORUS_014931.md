# Influence of eye movements on the biomechanical of the optic nerve head

The eye, as an organ of vision, has very fragile nervous tissues that, when damaged, can result in serious disorders in vision. Given the impossibility of carrying out invasive tests on these tissues, it has been attended in recent years to computer modeling and simulation using numerical methods. Different authors have studied the influence of high intraocular pressure as the main mechanical determining factor that could damage these tissues. However, there are other factors that could affect these tissues, such as the eye movements, in which it deepens in this work. It compares the influence of these movements with that exerted by intraocular pressure (IOP), using a three-dimensional model adjusted to the anatomy, and finite elements. It was obtained as a result that, in the motion of abduction for pre-lamin nervous tissue and the peripapilar retina, the value of the maximum and minimum main deformation is doubled, if compared with the normal values (IOP 15 mmHg). software.
