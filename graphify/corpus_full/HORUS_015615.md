# Geodynamic environment of the Gneis de Naranjales and its relationship with the matching metamorphic rocks.

The Gneis de Naranjales emerges in the east of the Central Cordillera, with an approximate area of 106.9 km2, is an elongated body of direction NE-SW, which extends from the co-regulation of Samaná to the east of the municipality San Carlos - Antioquia to the Victoria-Caldas. It is formed by, granofels and gneiss of quartz-feldspato-biotita-granate, sillimanite and and andalucite and migmatitas. The rocks of the Gneis de Naranjales have contents of SiO2 between 58,9 - 81,9 wt%, the TiO2 in all samples is <1,28%, with values of Al2O3 of 8,2-20,2 wt%, the Fe2O3 contains 7,7 - 0,7 wt % and the fusion Na2O+K2O was obtained, probably a few. The ages of anatexia of 236,61±3,234,1 and 236,4±1,8±1,8p,8 and258,5 and258,5 and258,5 and5 and5 Natexia 236 Ma of the Gneis de Naranjales and probably do not form part of the Cajamarca Complex. The inheritances in igneous zircons of Mesoproterozoic and Ordovicic age of the Cajamarca Complex and the Gneis de Naranjales are correlated temporarily and in turn present a relationship with the remnants of the Grenvillina and Famatinian orogeny.
