# REPLY TO PEMBROLIZUMAB OF MULTIPLES CUTAN CARCINOMAS IN CHILDREN WITH XERODERMA PIGMENTOSO// MULTIPLE RESPONSIBLE FOR SKIN CARCINOMAS IN A CHILD WITH XERODERMA PIGMENTOSUM.

Xeroderma pigmentoso is a rare genodermatosis with a variable incidence worldwide, with a certain predisposition in Asian countries. This disease is characterized by photosensitivity and appearance of skin cancer at very early ages, so the management of these patients is a challenge due to the great condition in quality of life and the high risk of progression and mortality, so in some cases immunotherapy has been described and published as a therapeutic strategy
