# Methodology for data quality management using a data drive approach to implement data quality assessment and improvement processes in master data management initiatives.

In recent years, the increase in data-based decision-making has seen a dramatic increase. This has led to a number of problems related to data quality, making clear the importance of having strategies to improve and guarantee the quality of data sets when implementing master data initiatives. This research focuses on a methodology that allows to evaluate and solve data quality problems directly on data, giving first a review and evaluation of the efforts and methodology found in the literature, followed by the presentation of the proposed methodology and detailing the processes for its implementation immediately and finally, implementing the methodology in a data set and presenting the results obtained at each stage
