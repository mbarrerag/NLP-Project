# PSYCOMETRIC PROPERTIES OF CHILD BEHAVIOR CHECKLIST 4-18 (CBCL/4-18) IN A SAMPLE SEMIURBAN AND RURAL CALLS IN 2021

the high prevalence of internalizing problems, such as anxiety, depression, somatic symptoms, and externalizing, such as behavioural disorders, social problems and attention problems, in childhood and adolescence demands practical methods for timely detection. CBCL/4-18 has been used for the identification of emotional, behavioral and social problems in children and adolescents. to analyze the psychometric properties of CBCL/4-18 in a Colombian sample of semi-urban and rural population. quantitative, correlational study, carried out in educational institutions of four municipalities of Caldas during the second semester of 2021. The validity of content was performed by expert judgement, which was applied to a sample of 859 caregivers of children or adolescents aged between 6 and 18 years living in semi-urban area (47,62 %) and rural (52,38 %). Univative analysis was performed to determine central tendency measures, bivariate analysis to explore the validity of construct and multivariate analysis to analyze the degree of difficulty and discrimination, besides the internal consistency. an item 0.0bach: 0.94) was obtained, and a sample of the whole scale of the psychological strength was obtained
