# Evaluation of the effect of a hybrid laboratory on learning a Hardware Description Language (HDL)

The initiatives to improve the learning of students have been increasing thanks to advances in telecommunications and a greater presence of computer equipment in educational contexts. Among these initiatives are remote laboratories that allow experimentation at distance. In the same line are the hybrid laboratories, which can group both remote elements as virtual or face-to-face. Learning requires that these tools can be effective at a high level. This way, they can contribute to motivate students to learn and generate a positive flow in their qualifications and academic rewards.
