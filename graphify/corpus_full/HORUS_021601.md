# Antimicrobial susceptibility profile of microorganisms responsible for bacteraemia in patients with stage 2 cancer.

The complications associated with cancer are multiple, bacterial infections are highlighted, these prolong hospital stays, in addition to generating greater morbidity and mortality. Empirical antibiotic treatment is one of the pillars of sepsis management, this should be based on the local epidemiology and type of infection. The main objective is to determine the frequency, profiles of susceptibility to antibiotics and the antimicrobial resistance genes of the microorganisms involved in bacteriumemias in patients with cancer in a developing country. Epidemiological study, observational, prospective and multicenter in six institutions of Bogotá. The active search for adult patients with cancer and bacteremia confirmed by the laboratory in the different participating institutions was carried out. Variables of demographic, clinical and microbiological interest were obtained, including the patient with cancer and bacterial disease, with 207 isolated microorganisms
