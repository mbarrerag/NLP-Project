# Improvement of a prototype of a pilot fluidized bed plant for the thermal processing of biosolids

The objective of this project is to adapt a prototype of an experimental combustion plant in fluidized bed for the conduct of experiments of biosolid pyrolysis produced in the wastewater treatment plant (PTAR) The Salitre of the city of Bogotá. Currently, the experimental installation of fluidized bed can carry out experiments of combustion of residual biomass as is the oil palm cousk. However, to be able to carry out experiments of pyrolysis is required an additional system for the post-treatment of gases and tars generated. Specifically, the design and assembly of a heating system is required before the exchanger, causing a temperature of approximately 500°C from the outlet of the gases from the reactor to the entry to a heat exchanger where condensation of the generated tars occurs. The heating system aims to avoid condensation in the system before the technology is carried out, causing plugging and malfunctioning of the installation. The adaptation includes the redesign of the system of unions and packagings, in such a way that the condensing of the generated tars is carried out by the heating system and ensuring the condensation of the reactor.
