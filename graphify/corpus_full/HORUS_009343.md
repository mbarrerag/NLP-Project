# Social promotion of science, technology and innovation through the interaction between SOCIETY and ACADEMIA for the solution of social problems in the territorial areas of training and re-incorporation of ex-combatants of FARC-EP, within the framework of peace agreements

This work shows an experience of social appropriation of science, technology and innovation (CTeI), through a participatory strategy that integrates socialization, interactivity, knowledge, communication and skills, through activities that encourage participants to develop processes of scientific appropriation and promote the dissemination of the CTeI in communities where the ex-combatants of the Farc-EP are settled in Antioquia, after the Peace Accords. The methodological proposal revolves around the promotion of the skills, capacities and opportunities of the communities through interdisciplinary work, exchange of knowledge and collaborative work, in which contextualized knowledge is articulated, and ideas are generated to local and regional problems, for the development of the territory (Salinas, et al., 2010)
