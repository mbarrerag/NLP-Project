# SUB-DIARY CHARACTERIZATION OF EXTREME TORMENTS IN COLOMBIA

The understanding of the characteristics of extreme storms on a sub-day scale is fundamental in the analysis of rains that detonate landslides, in the estimation of laminar erosion, in the study of sudden increases, in hydroclimate modelling and in hydrological design, among others. However, overall, the properties of these extreme events are little known, and even more so in Latin American countries, which generally lack information and databases associated with this type of storms. In order to help characterize extreme storms in Colombia, the first database of this type has been developed in the country, which includes information of approximately 14,000 pluviographic charts per day, selected and previously digitized for 70 pluviographic stations located in the national territory. Using this information, the time of onset, duration, total precipitation, diurnal precipitation cycle and the dimensional curves of mass have been characterized for each season and by hydrographic region
