# Comparison of photovoltaic and hydroelectric technologies in Colombia.

Unconventional renewable energies such as photovoltaic and wind energy are currently leading the energy transition and year after year their installed capacity is growing around the world, which is mainly due to the fact that this type of technology has become much more competitive, reducing its installation costs to levels never recorded. For the case of photovoltaic solar energy this has reduced its installation costs by more than 80% in the last ten years, while the renewable energy most used worldwide, the hydroelectric generation, has been losing its power due to the environmental impacts that are presented with its construction and the reduction of costs of non-conventional renewables. This has been reflected mainly in Asian, European and North American markets, while other countries have hardly begun the large-scale implementation of non-conventional renewable energy sources as a result of the generation of incentives by local governments. Colombia is a sample of those countries with a nascent participation in the installation of non-conventional renewable energy sources, counting with about 68% of hydroelectric generation in its energy matrix.
