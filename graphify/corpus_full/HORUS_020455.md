# Modeling of an internal combustion engine conditioned with hydrogen for power generation.

Hydrogen as an option to minimize environmental problems caused by fossil fuels is presented as an energy alternative in internal combustion engines, in production and application within that engine. The behaviour of hydrogen generation and use as a fuel in a single-cylinder engine via water electrolysis was studied. Hydrogen is obtained by means of a cell powered by batteries charged with solar panels. The engine is coupled to a synchronous generator to obtain electrical energy; system control was implemented remotely by means of an application in a mobile device (IoT).
