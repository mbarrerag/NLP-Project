# Anatomical review and introduction of concepts in pterigopalatine fossa techniques in neurotherapeutic medicine, for the management of related pathologies.

Neural therapy, for the management of related pathologies Introduction and objective: Neural Therapy seeks to modify the health-disease status through the intervention with procaine, on nerve trunks, ganglia, plexia, nerves and their terminal ramifications. However, the classic techniques of neurotherapeutic medicine present some difficulties, therefore, the objective of this work is to carry out a descriptive study anatomical and demonstrative, for the review and updating of the techniques used in neurotherapeutic medicine, for the pterigopalatine fossa, and its advantages in related pathologies, with greater impact on the population. A bibliographic review of the most frequent pathologies, as well as of techniques in neurotherapeutic medicine, their risks and respective complications, in the pterigopalatine fossa. Subsequently, revisions were carried out with dissection and prosection material (predissecticized quadratic tissue) available in the amphitheater of the faculty of medicine of the National University of Colombia.
