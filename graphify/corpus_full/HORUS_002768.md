# PALINOLOGICAL APPLICATIONS IN COLOMBIA: THE POLAND AS A BOTAN ORIGIN IDENTIFICATION TOOL

The palinology is a tool that allows to study microscopic particles (5-500 micrones). Its main axis of study throughout the history has been pollen, thanks to its more important character - its wall- because it is composed of one of the most resistant biopolymers of the planet, the esporopolenin. It could be said that each plant species has a polynic signature, that is, a grain of a specific form. Pollen plays an important role in pollination, as well as in the maintenance of the ecological functions of the ecosystems; despite this, its value in taxonomy and ecology has not been used 100%, especially in tropical ecosystems. The study of pollen in the current context of climate crisis and loss of biodiversity is imperative, since it can help to better understand the dynamics of our environment, especially in this geographical region, recognized for being highly diverse and very affected by the anthropic influence.
