# Use of plants for the treatment of vulgar acne: systematic review of literature.

Common acne is a chronic inflammatory skin disease that can generate sequelae such as hyperpigmentation and scars. Antibiotics are the main treatment, however, the increase in antimicrobial resistance and side effects have aroused interest in seeking other alternatives for its management. Medicinal plants have been studied as a therapeutic alternative for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial and antioxidant effects, so, several researches have been carried out exploring their use. Despite this, a recent document is necessary that synthesizes the results obtained in the studies and evaluates their methodological quality, to guide future research, and preliminary, this therapeutic. Searches were carried out for studies that use medicinal plants for the treatment of common acne in various databases until August 2022. 38 articles were included with a total of 4,539 participants and 167 different species of medicinal herbs. Medicinal plants are effective in the treatment of common acne, compared to standard treatment or placebo, with some exceptions. Their safety profile is adequate, generating only mild adverse events.
