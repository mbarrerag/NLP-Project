# Estimate of demand for air passenger transport from a hub-type network approach: El Dorado Airport case Bogotá, Colombia

This paper analyses the behaviour of passenger demand at El Dorado airport (Bogotá, Colombia), to identify the variables used in the planning of air transport and to analyze other aspects that should be considered in the estimation of demand, in order to raise the discussion on the need to take into account territorial aspects and the urban-regional environment of airports, both to analyze the demand, as to incorporate variables of the aviation network, the physical functional relationship of the airport with the city or urban areas to which it serves, and the network of cities it contributes to the airport passengers. Depending on the characterization of the variables identified, this research proposes two approaches to estimate demand at El Dorado airport, the first, a traditional model of simple linear regression, the second, a combined model (linear and gravitational regression), incorporating macroeconomic variables of regional scale (GDP per capita departmental) of the city network that supports the airport operation. According to the characterization of the identified variables, this research proposals two approaches to estimate the demand for El Dorado, the first, a traditional model of simple linear regression, the second, a combined model (linear and gravitational regression), which incorporated macroeconomic data on a regional scale (GDP per departmental capita) of the network of cities that supported the operation of the airport. For the case study of the El Dorado airport, it is broken by means of two different methods, that the data best explains the demand for domestic passage are of a regional nature, a conclusion reached reached thanks to the analysis of the transport networks and the territorial dynamics. This shows the inseparable relationship between mobility and territory infrastructure, which should not be construed and operated by means of the transport networks and the territorial dynamics
