# Basic Radiology: Rx, CT, MRI, ultrasound

The third edition of the book Basic Radiology maintains the purpose of serving as a guide text in the field of diagnostic images to students of the careers of the health area and to general physicians, although it can also be used as a reference work by specialists. Basic Radiology in its third edition contains nine chapters and a table of indications of diagnostic imaging examinations in specific clinical contexts: Chapter 1: Physical principles of diagnostic images. Radiobiology and radiation protection; Chapter 2: Chest images; Chapter 3: Images of the abdomen; Chapter 4: Neuroradiology; Chapter 5: Images of the neck; Chapter 6: Images of the osteoarticular and musculosketic system; Chapter 7: Images of women; Chapter 8: Paediatric images; chapter 9: interventional radiology; annex: table of indications of diagnostic imaging studies. The book Basic Radiology has updated information and numerous images of each area. All chapters present an updated list of bibliographic references that will allow the reader to delve into the different topics. -- Contracadas
