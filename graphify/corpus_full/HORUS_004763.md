# Risk analysis for sliding in the Doña Juana sanitary filling in Bogotá

In this work, an analysis of the risks associated with possible slidings of solid waste in the sanitary filling Doña Juana, Bogotá was carried out. The emergencies that occurred from mass removal movements have been criticized for the operation of the sanitary filling, affecting the provision of the public toilet service and even compromising the water resource, as happened in the 1997 slide with the damping and pollution of the Río Tunjuelo. Given the occurrence of these adverse events, there is a need to carry out an assessment of the risks associated with a possible sliding by the operation of the sanitary filling. For the assessment of the risks, the guidelines of the GSC-UNAL Methodological Guide (Ávila et al. 2016) were used for studies of threat, vulnerability and risk by mass removal. The analysis of the threat is carried out using a quantitative methodology in which the security factors in different profiles of the filling are calculated and in the current area. In addition, a probability analysis of failure was carried out using the Montecarlo method, which allowed the zonification of the threat.
