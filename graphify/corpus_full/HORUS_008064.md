# INNOVATION INITIATIVES FOR INTELIGENT CITIZENS IMPLEMENTED BY THE SPECIAL DISTRIBUTION OF SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION OF MEDELLIN, WITH A APPROACH IN THEIR CONTRIBUTION TO SOCIOECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT AND IMPROVING THE LIFE QUALITY OF CITIZENS

Today, Colombia’s cities are in a process of digital transformation and implementation of artificial intelligence tools that allow them to be more efficient and intelligent, given the use of new technologies and tools that allow them to address different action fronts such as mobility, security, industry 4.0, digital appropriation models, adolescent pregnancy reduction and Smart Cities. Given this, the special district of science, technology and innovation in Medellín addresses the study and analysis of the improvement of all aspects that contribute to the social, economic and welfare impact of citizens, based on data models that have already been implemented in other cities worldwide, having them as benchmarks for their results in the topics of digital innovation, smart cities, digital transformation, data governance implementation and artificial intelligence
