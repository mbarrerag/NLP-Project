# Infinitely paradoxical. The history of infinity as a didactic resource for understanding and teaching the relationship between mathematics and philosophy

This paper shows the construction of a didactic unit focused on the recognition of the relationship between mathematics and philosophy based on a historical journey through the concept of infinity. It points out historical moments in which, both philosophy and mathematics, faced paradoxical reasoning and results that allowed the development of both disciplines in the search for their solutions. To conclude, it confines the work in a pedagogical framework based on problem solving, socratic dialogue, active pedagogy and critical thinking. .
