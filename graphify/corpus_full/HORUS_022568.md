# CITY AND ITS FORMS OF REPRESENTATION.

Inhabiting a city, whatever it is, is ultimately discovering that it is an unfinished and, moreover, weak construction; as if a destructive shadow was always about to fall upon it, as if disaster were inherent to its condition. Why is that link between city and end? By asking for the apocalyptic vision that follows it is sluggish, this book seeks to think about the representations that sustain that relationship. From fantastic stories like those of Lovecraft, through references to classic horror films and even works recognized for their literary value, those that have emerged from the pen of Alfred Döblin, Georges Perec, Italo Calvino or Paul Auster, The city and its forms of representation. Disaster strategies is an invitation to think how the city, as a second nature, can only be an expression of our condition. The city is the touchstone in which our dreams and nightmares become evident, is the place where we question our condition with all its contradictions
