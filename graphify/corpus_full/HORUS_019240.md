# PATRIARCAL MUSEUMS, WOMEN'S RESISTANCE: THE WARS OF THE CENTRE IN THE 2016 - 2019 MUSEUM

This article analyzes the possibilities of curatorial activism located and its ability to activate anticolonial and decolonial processes for social justice within the museum institution; starting with the case of Las Guerreras del centro, a community of women in prostitution who work in the vicinity of the Museum of Antioquia (Medellín, Colombia), who participated in a proactive and authoral way in five projects within the center, between 2016 and 2019
