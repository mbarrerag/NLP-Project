# Study of the operation of two commercial anaerobic treatment systems for domestic wastewater, used in rural areas of the municipality of Manizales. Case study: Sector Tablazo

The management of water resources in Colombia at a regulatory level has been developed approximately during the last 30 years, being the subject of public policies with high relevance in the last decade. This is due to the monitoring and control of processes by the environmental authorities, since a large part of the population, due to the instinct of survival, safety and health, lives near bodies of water, becoming collection points for the use and method of natural transport of wastewater. The problem of environmental impact, generated by the emission of poorly treated wastewater into water bodies, is one of the most important challenges facing the discipline of bioengineering today. The presence of polluting substances in wastewater directly depends on its use, such as toxic chemicals, heavy metals, organic matter, and pathogens can deteriorate water quality and affect the health of organisms that depend on it for restoration. The existence of contaminants in water directly affects its quality, causing the death of aquatic organisms and the alteration of ecosystems. To face this global problem, large cities have been adapting to the construction of sanitary facilities, sewage networks and increased coverage of wastewater transport and conduction systems, these being carried towards centralized systems. of treatment. At present, population censuses indicate a trend towards an increase in the number of inhabitants in rural areas, due to the search for greater well-being and tranquillity offered by these environments, a consequence of the almost annual reformulations of land use plans, which are more attractive due to the proximity of these sectors with the urban capital. The regulation of decentralized wastewater treatment systems has greater limitations in its control and management, being the greatest environmental responsibility, and the design of the treatment units that make up these systems; however, the commercialization of prefabricated systems has become the default choice. for these areas, associated with availability in the market, ease of installation and start-up. But it has been observed that there is a disparity between the recommendations of current regulations and reality, in terms of load removal efficiency. contaminants from these systems. From this, an evaluation of the commercial anaerobic treatment systems was carried out, in the start-up, start-up and stabilization for two selected populations, with similar socioeconomic characteristics, thermal floor, meteorological conditions and spatial location, located in the village of the Tablazo municipality of Manizales. In addition, it is desired to establish the efficiency of the wastewater treatment plant in the elimination of contaminants and to guarantee that the treated water meets the minimum standards required for its reuse or discharge to receiving water bodies. It mainly seeks to verify the hydraulic and constructive behaviour of the treatment systems about the design, based on the technical treatment guides
