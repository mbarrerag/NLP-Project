# Solutions for a problem involving a φ-Laplacian-like operator via energy analysis, phase plane and shooting method

In this paper, we establish the existence of a countably infinite family of radially symmetric solutions that exhibit sign variations. These solutions are obtained for a Dirichlet boundary value problem incorporating a φ-Laplacian-like operator. Our main tools are the shooting method, phase plane and energy analysis, which demand extensive use of a Pozohaev-type identity
