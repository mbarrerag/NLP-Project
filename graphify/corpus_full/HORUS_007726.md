# China's trade insertion in Colombia in the period 2010-2020

For approximately four decades China has been experiencing an unprecedented process of economic growth and development in the world economy; and in recent years it has become an important economy; especially for Latin American countries. Indeed, China has the second largest economy in the world, with GDP valued at 2022 in 18.1 trillion dollars, equivalent to 18 per cent of the world product. Its share in world exports of goods rose from 3.9 per cent in 2000, one year before its accession to the World Trade Organization (WTO), to 14.4 per cent in 2022, and in 2009 it became the world's leading exporter. Similarly, its share of world imports of goods rose from 3.4 per cent in 2000 to 10.6 per cent in 2022, thus becoming the second largest importer after the United States (ECLAC, 2023). Our research will focus on trade between Colombia and China because trade relations with this Asian country are increasing, and according to its economic potential, this nation, along with the other Asian countries, is expected to become the most important trading partner of our country in future years.
