# CHEMICAL PRECIGOTIC ISILATION OF FRUGIPERD SPODOPTER

As mentioned in the previous chapter, another type of pre-geotic isolation is chemical isolation. This type of isolation has evolved especially in moths and is related to the communication system between females and males that depends largely on the pheromones emitted by them (Jurenka 2004). Pheromones, are part of substances produced by animals as communication signals that are called semiochemicals (Reddy and Guerrero 2004). The function of these is to induce an attractive reaction among individuals of the same species (Reddy and Guerrero 2004, Jurenka 2004)
