# MANIZABLE CAMPUS: UNIVERSITY CITY + CONNECTED CITY

This book presents the technical studies and designs carried out during the conception of the project "Campus Manizales: university city + connected city"; developed through an inter-administrative agreement between the National University of Colombia and the Mayor of Manizales. In this context, the study of mobility conditions and its application to the urban and architectural design of the active and sustainable mobility network of the city was carried out; specifically, on a longitudinal axis that crosses it from the west to the east. What is sought is to connect the sector of the traditional center with university campuses, passing through some of the most representative institutions of primary and secondary basic education, as well as cultural, religious, sports, recreational, commercial and administrative centers. The authors propose an integrated system, composed of boulevards and cyclerrutas, in which the public space and the collective space of streets, squares, parks and urban interstitios are adjusted and redistributed fairly, to involve all strategic actors of mobility and generate comfortable and safe spaces for the displacement and enjoyment of the population in general. The theoretical and practical reflection between the procedures and results of the technical engineering is put into consideration
