# --Strengthening the Fauna Marina Colombiana group: Biodiversity and Uses and the postgraduate course in Biology Sciences, Marine Biology Line

The postgraduate course in Science, Biology, Marine Biology Line is linked to the activities of the research group, since its creation in January 2000. The group has been strengthened with the activities of the postgraduate and has contributed to the growing success of that. The project seeks to consolidate the close relationship existing between the Fauna Marine Research Group Colombia: Biodiversity and Uses and the postgraduate course in Biology Sciences, Marine Biology line of the Caribbean headquarters through the financing of field trips. Likewise, the group will continue its participation in the academic discussion scenarios presented within the university, especially at the IX Seminar _x0093_Las Ciencias del Mar at the National University of Colombia_x0094_, with the participation of students and professors, as exhibitors of the results or advances of their research
