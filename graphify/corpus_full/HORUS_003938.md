# Evaluation of photocatalytic activity of FexOy/TiO2 obtained from ilmenite from mining waste for degradation of cyanide dissolved in water

In this study we study the synthesis of catalysts based on structures of Fe(III)-TiO2 (synthesis of titanium doped iron), obtained from the natural mineral ilmenite extracted from black sands that constitute a type of residue of the mining industry (gold exploitation) in the region of El Bagre Antioquia-Colombia. The synthesis was developed by the controlled acid extraction of the titanium-iron species, allowing the obtaining of catalysts with different iron contents.
