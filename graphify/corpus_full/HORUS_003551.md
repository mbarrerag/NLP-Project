# AGRICAL TERRACES SYSTEM WITH A SUSTAINABLE APPROACH

A model terrace was built in the Paysandú (U.N.) agricultural station, Santa Elena, using the agricultural level. The objective was to know and standardize the design and construction techniques to replicate them in other areas. Subsequently, these practices were implemented in Nariño with the indigenous community Chiles, combining traditional and local knowledge. The standardization of these techniques seeks to promote sustainability and improve agricultural productivity
