# DISEASE BY VIRUS SINCITIA LRESPIRATORIO IN PEDIATRIA: MORE THAN THE RESPIRATORY COMMITMENT

respiratory syncitial virus (RSV) is usually associated with respiratory disease in the pediatric population, however multiple extrapulmonary manifestations have been documented, especially neurological ones. to present an overview of reports in the literature indexed on neurological involvement in infection with SRV. systematic formal exploratory review of literature in the Pubmed and SCOPUS databases, which included primary and secondary studies until 17 July 2022, published in English, Spanish and French. publications describing the experience in 1,681 patients with a median age of 17 months and greater male involvement (64.59%) in the framework of severe respiratory disease by SRV. Seizures and central apnea appeared as main manifestations, either by viral invasion and secondary inflammation of the central nervous system (CNS) or by compensation derived from neuroendocrine adjustments to severe respiratory damage. Long-term sequelae included developmental delay and cognitive disability. severe respiratory infection syndrome can occur with neurological involvement, possibly derived from the direct management of the SNC virus
