# Territorial processes in living beings

The human being has been considered a territorial species; however, there has been little dialogue between the zoologists and humanist scientists around the concept of territory. Although the existence of territoriality in microorganisms and plants is recognized, the relationship between the concept of territory in human sciences and that applied to other living beings is not clear. This study is intended to identify the territorial processes present in a variety of living organisms, in order to understand in greater depth the concept of territory. From a review of literature, the fundamental elements that make up this concept were identified in both the biological sciences and in the human sciences. Despite the great conceptual diversity existing, certain unifying principles that allow the use of this concept to be integrated into different disciplines such as microbiology, botany, zoology, and humanities. This analysis highlights the territorial processes in various living organisms that require to defend a space to ensure its survival
