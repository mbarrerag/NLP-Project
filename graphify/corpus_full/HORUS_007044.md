# Relationship between performance, distribution and added economic value indicators as a totalising financial indicator in RevloG and its business partners during the period 2015-2022

This document, in the research key, analyzes the relationship between the Performance, Distribution and Added Economic Value Indicators, for purposes of understanding them as a Totalizing Financial Indicator in the Revlog Company and its Business Partners in a time window between 2015 and 2022. The analysis aims to read and assume the Added Economic Value (EVA) as a possible integral tool to measure both financial performance and value creation in RevloG and its associated companies. Thus, the study examines the alternative of assuming the EVA as an effective consolidated of all financial management in a single metric, examining its relationship with various performance and distribution indicators. In methodological perspective, the research used descriptive, correlational and multivariate procedures to study the connection between the EVA and other financial indicators such as the Return on Investment (ROI), the Cost of Fiscally Fiscally Fiscally Fiscal (CCPP) and the Rotation of Operational Assets (RAO) among others. The results show that, although it was initially expected that the EVA could capture the financial performance in an integral way, its correlation was significant with some performance indicators, and it did not fully reflect the results.
