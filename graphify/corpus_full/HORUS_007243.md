# Methodology for obtaining travel distances from mass movements in the North-East Andean Region based on probabilistic methods

The Colombian geography presents great challenges due to the variability of its topography and the materials present, resulting in one of the countries with the highest density of mass removal processes in the region, each progress in understanding and analysis of these processes being valuable. This document seeks to take a step further in the fulfillment of this objective, proposing a methodology that starts from the obtaining and categorization of the information, going through a statistical validation analysis and ending in a model of Machine Learning that serves as an alternative to the regression curves currently used for the calculation of the travel distance. The result is composed of an algorithm that can be used depending on the level of processing of the information that the user has, a model of Random Forest developed in R to facilitate its use and calibration along with a probability curve of excess travel distance applied to a study case. These products present an improvement based on the models currently used, increasing their accuracy and accuracy by reducing the relative error by giving a solid alternative of calculation . and calibration along with a run out distance excess probability curve applied directly to a study case.
