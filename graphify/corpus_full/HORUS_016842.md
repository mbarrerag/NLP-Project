# RELIGIOUS CREATIONS OF PUBLIC COLEGE STUDENTS IN BOGOTA

The article presents the results of a quantitative research that characterized the young Bogotans who study in public schools in terms of: religious identity, religious practices and beliefs, subjective importance that they attach to religion, and conflicts that they perceive between science and religion. By descriptive statistics, data provided by a survey applied in 2019 to a random sample of students who attended the last grade of secondary school are presented and interpreted. Among the findings, the high percentage of young people who no longer identify with Catholicism stands out, and those who, while maintaining religious beliefs, do not belong to a church or community of believers. Furthermore, the data indicate that: religious identity, religious beliefs, participation in religious rites and frequency of conflicts between religious beliefs and scientific explanations, vary with respect to the gender and identity of young people
