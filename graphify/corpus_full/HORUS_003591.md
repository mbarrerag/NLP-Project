# SOLID TARIFIED

The sieve is a physical-mechanical method by which solid particles present in heterogeneous mixtures can be separated. The larger particles of the mixture are retained on the surface of the sieve by a simple procedure. Only particles whose diameter is smaller than the sieve holes will pass through this instrument.7 This method can be illustrated in the basic laboratory using soil, flour and husk samples, among others
