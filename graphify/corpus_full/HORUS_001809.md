# LoRaWAN technique to identify electrical failures and increase reliability in distribution systems in non-interconnected Colombian areas

This research carries out a review of the current problems of the national interconnected system (SIN) and how the implementation of new intelligent connection alternatives will offer solutions to improve the interconnectivity and quality of service in the different areas of the country; an analysis is also made based on the alternatives projected in the “Map of route for the implementation of intelligent networks in Colombia”. Special emphasis is placed on the interconnectivity of rural areas in Colombia, since significant challenges are still presented in these areas in terms of coverage, quality of service and tariffs, as reported by the research carried out by the University Distrital of Colombia (Hurtado-Beltran, 2020). For this reason, a detailed analysis of the latest advances in electrical fault detection techniques in distribution systems in rural areas, including the study of technologies such as sensors, advanced algorithms and neural networks; in order to study the possible application of these techniques in the Colombian context. search conducted by the Distrital University of Colombia (Hurtado-Beltrán, 2020). Therefore, a detailed analysis of the most recent advances in techniques for detecting electrical failures in distribution systems in rural areas is proposed, including the study of technologies such as sensors, advanced algorithms, and neural networks; to study the possible application of these technologies in the Colombian context. The implementation of early detection systems for electrical failures can significantly improve the quality of the electricity supply in these areas, reducing downtime and increasing service reliability.
