# Effect of pulsed GMAW (GMAW-P) on the integrity and microstructure of solders applied to martensitic stainless steel with EC410NiMo electrode

This work was carried out to evaluate the impact of some GMAW parameters pulsed in the geometry and microstructure of welding rechargings performed with electrode EC410NiMo and 98% mixtureAr-2%O2 as protective gas on stainless steel ASTM A743 degree CA6NM. The experimental development was divided into two stages where in the first one was sought to determine the transition current and the modes of transfer of metal for the electrode by applying solders and analyzing its electrical signals. The second stage of the experimental development was focused on determining the parameters of GMAW pressed under the UGPP condition and by the variation of the duration of the type time for wire feeding speeds between 150 and 300Pul/min and determining its effect on the geometry and microstructures final of the welding recharches.
