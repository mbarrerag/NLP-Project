# Teaching Colombian History: An Approach to Historical Memory and Collective Memory, within the Framework of Public Education Policies

The purpose of this research is to carry out a critical analysis of the public educational policies on the teaching of the history of Colombia, which promote memory and identity. Initially, the concept and function of a public policy by the State is developed, as a way of intervening the community and its territories. In the case of Colombian educational policies, the analysis of those that form students in cultural values and assets, promoting the national identity is taken. As a central axis is taken the Law 1874 of 2017, which determines the obligatory teaching of the history of Colombia in the school. The norm presents a focus from the development of historical thinking and historical consciousness, to promote the historical memory and a national and cultural diversity identity. The training of students as citizens and Colombians is regulated by laws and decrees that allow the execution of programs and projects in educational institutions. The critical analysis is made of the normivity, projects and plans at the national, territorial and local level.
