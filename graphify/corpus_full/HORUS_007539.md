# Influence of ecotourism activities on the economic, cultural and territorial sovereignty of an indigenous community and an Afro-descendant community in the municipality of Orito: a case study

In response to the limited participation of ethnic communities in the formulation, development and implementation of tourism development plans in their territories, this project focuses on the analysis of the influence of ecotourism activities on the economic, cultural and territorial sovereignty of an indigenous community and an Afro-descendant community in the municipality of Orito. As a general purpose, it is proposed to formulate recommendations and strategies that make it possible to implement sustainable ecotourism activities in ethnic territories, respecting the cultural and territorial identity of local communities. To achieve this, in the first instance, it addresses the identification of practices and challenges that involve the development of eco-tourism activities in these communities, their local perception and the analysis of their influence. Finally, the proposed methodology combines systematic review of literature, the development of semi-structured interviews and the analysis of case studies
