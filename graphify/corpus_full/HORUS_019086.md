# ASSESSMENT OF EXTERNAL REFORCEMENT WITH MALLA GALLINERA FOR HORIZONTAL PERFORATION BLOCK MAMPOSTERY

The research aims to evaluate the mechanical performance of the simple masonry reinforced externally in a non-conventional way using hexagonal gallinary mesh. The reinforcement consists of the coating, on one side, of mortar reinforced with the mentioned technique. In the study three reinforced specimens and three with only coating were tested to compression and diagonal traction, having as variable the presence of the reinforcement. The specimens were instrumented in order to know the results of load and maximum effort, the stress-deformation curves and their rigidities, in addition to the record of the mode of fault and cracking observed during the test. The project includes the physical and mechanical characterisation of the materials constituent of the masonry, the application of the technique of reinforcement, its verification and accompaniment in the construction and the tests according to the technical standards ASTM C1314 and ASTM E519. Based on the results of the improvement in the structural behavior and the difference of the cost per square meter of the technique studied.
