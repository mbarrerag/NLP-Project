# A measure to calculate the speed limit rate in a region.

This thesis proposes a measure that, from the trajectories of moving objects, determines the speed limit rate given a speed limit in each cell in which a region is segmented (the space where objects are moved). To do this, the concept of speed limit rate, based on speed segments, is formally defined. Time is also segmented in intervals. Therefore, the movement of objects in a cell can be analyzed within a given time interval. To do this, the corresponding algorithm was implemented and experiments were carried out with taxi paths in Porto (Portugal). The results showed that the speed limit rate measure can be useful for analyzing the behavior of mobile objects with respect to their speed. In addition, the measure could also serve as an approximate estimate of congestion in a (sub)region, being useful for traffic analysis, including prediction techniques
