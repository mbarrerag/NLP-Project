# TEACHING READING ARGUMENTATIVE TEXTS IN UNIVERSITY: AN EXPERIENCE OF RESEARCH

A didactic experience is presented to contribute to the development of the competence to read and write argumentative texts at the National University. A qualitative and quantitative analysis of the data collected with students is made. Quantitative analysis is done with the results of the pre and post test application
