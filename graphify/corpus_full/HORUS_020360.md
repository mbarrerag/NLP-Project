# Dynamics of participation and community resistance strategies in disputed territories: Remedios and Ituango 2002-2016

This work aims to understand how the dynamics of participation and community resistance are established and shared in territories that are subject to the study by various actors between 2002 and 2016, where the armed conflict and the extraction of natural resources constitute the main factors that affect the existence of communities in two specific territories: the municipalities of Ituango and Remedios, in the department of Antioquia. To get close to the understanding of these dynamics of community resistance, the movement Rios Vivos (Living Rivers Movement) and the Corporation Acción Detabilidad para la Convivencia y la Paz del Northeast Antioqueño (Humanitarian Action and Peace in the Northeast of Antioquia Corporation, CAHUCOPANA) are considered as a social economy and economy from the North of Antioquia. and learning in a context where the community is victimized by violence by illegal armed groups, energy mining companies and State security forces
