# The effect of four spiny-Zolinine molecules on the growth and development of B. Pylosa and T. OFFICINALIS seeds.

Weeds are plants that interfere with the development of the crop, being a factor of influence in the yield of the crops, in some of the current production systems, herbicides are a tool for the control of weeds. Currently, the majority of herbicide molecules used in the country do not come from own technology, so, producing herbicide molecules becomes an expectation of national technological growth. For this reason this research was proposed, which, aimed to evaluate, in controlled conditions, by dose response tests, the herbicide activity of 3 Ftalyl compounds - nomenclature RM1, RM2, RM3 and RM5- and a Cumaric compound - nomenclature MR4- synthesized at the National University of Colombia on the growth and development of species B. pylose and T. officinalis. Differences in the survival of plants exposed to molecules - 18 days after exposure - are observed mortality with statistical difference with the witness for B. pylosa in RM1 (D1 and D2), MR4 and RM5 are found.
