# Prognostic evaluation of the score PADIT for the prediction of infection associated with implantable cardiac devices in the Colombian population.

Currently, the implantable cardiovascular electronic devices became the fundamental pillar of the treatment of patients with arrhythmias, however, one of the complications associated with their use consists of infection, their presence is equivalent to greater morbidity and mortality. The main objective of this study is to evaluate the prognostic performance of the score PADIT, scale designed to identify patients with risk of infection. An observational study was carried out with analytical component of retrospective cohort in patients attended in the last 6 years in the electrophysiology service. In the Hospital Universitario Clínica San Rafael, 3745 patients with implantable cardiac electronic devices were attended in the described period. In the study, data were collected from 1780 patients, taking into account the inclusion and exclusion criteria.
