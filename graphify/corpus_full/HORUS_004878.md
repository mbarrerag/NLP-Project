# Prototype of a Transactive Energy System for the development of renewable energy resources distributed using a distributed registry technology

Transactive energy systems have become mechanisms that favor the use of renewable energy sources by allowing new suppliers to market surplus energy within their community. The distributed transactive energy systems offer benefits by enabling trade between peers. Some ongoing projects have implemented this concept through a particular application of the registered technologies distributed specifically Blockchain. However, their adoption, especially in small communities, involves high implementation and operating costs, long transaction approval times, commissions in each transaction and high energy consumption. Thus, this work proposes to explore a technological alternative of distributed registry that allows the implementation of a prototype of transactive energy system distributed more conveniently for use in local communities. To achieve this, the main features of the registered technologies are identified and the most relevant TRDs are listed. Then, the aspects of design, implementation and tests of the prototype of the distributed Transactive Energy System are described.
