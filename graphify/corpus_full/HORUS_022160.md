# Experience in the surgical management of brachial plexus lesions in a level IV hospital in Latin America.

The lesions of the brachial plexus plexus [LPB] cause severe functional deficits with substantial implications for mental health and deep socioeconomic impacts. Due to their common association with motorcycle accidents, the incidence in Latin America is expected to be high, but the data in our region are scarce. Describe the distribution of the BLP, the characteristics of the patients and evaluate the clinical outcomes of surgical management in adults treated in a reference hospital in Colombia. This study was mixed, descriptive and inferential, including patients evaluated between January 2015 and June 2021.
