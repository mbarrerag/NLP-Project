# SCIENCE IN DIPLOMACY: THE ROLE OF SCIENCE IN THE INTERNATIONAL CLIMATE CHANGE REGIME

This presentation focuses on the causes, impacts and challenges of climate change for Latin Ameirca. In particular, we discuss to what extent does scientific evidence influence (or not influence) the outcome of climate change negotiations in the context of the UNFCCC. Second, how do the IPCC working groups deal with contradictory scientific evidences,. In spite of the geographic diversity of the IPCC, there is some kind of unintentional bias in the outcome report. And what are the particular contributions of Latin America and Caribbean to the science of climate change, how it should be improved
