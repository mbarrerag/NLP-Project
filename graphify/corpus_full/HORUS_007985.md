# ♪ With the feet on the earth and the look on the stars ♪

From the earliest antiquity humanity has looked to the sky with wonder and admiration. Stars, planets and the immeasurable vastness of the universe have inspired and intrigued civilizations throughout history. But beyond the captivating celestial phenomena that served at first to feed countless mythological and cosmogonial adventures, and then to understand those that occur beyond the boundary of our planet, the observation of the sky has had a profound influence on our knowledge and understanding of the soil we tread, of the Earth itself. On the following lines we will make a brief journey in time, which will take us from the first inquiries into the form of the Earth to the present knowledge that is nourished by the sciences of space, exploring revealing aspects that helped to lay the foundations of scientific knowledge of the ground and the sky throughout history
