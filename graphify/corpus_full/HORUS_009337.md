# Control of electromechanical oscillations in electrical power systems by wind generating plants.

With the increasing incorporation of renewable resources into the energy matrix, one of the main challenges is to determine the impact this type of generation has, based mainly on converters, on the dynamic stability of the electrical power systems, being necessary to determine the additional control systems that must be incorporated in this technology to keep the main electrical variables within the established operating range. In this research, the design of a Gaussian Quadratic Control Linear, LQG, implemented in the reactivation power control loop of wind generators, to cushion inter-area oscillatory modes in electrical power systems is presented. For this purpose, the linear model of the electrical power system is first obtained, initially through linearization with small signal analysis and then with the Loewner identification method based on measurements. After tuning the control, its behavior and influence on the damping of oscillator modes, using the graphical tool of the geometrical mode of the roots, is demonstrated. Finally, the performance of the controller is tested through transient simulation on the nonlinear model of two test systems, which shows the oscillator mode of control, where the influence is demonstrated
