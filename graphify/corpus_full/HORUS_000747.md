# The Mathematical Chaos in Today’s Engineering

Some reflections are presented on the state of teaching and the application of mathematics in today's engineering in Colombia, pointing to notable deficiencies and their consequences. To contribute in a constructive sense, some ideas of deterministic chaos of low dimensionality and their quantification in nonlinear dynamic systems are presented and applied to the evolution of the Lorenz system (1963) and to the monthly series of rainfalls in Medellín
