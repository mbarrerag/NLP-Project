# EDUCATION IN VIRTUAL AULA OF INSURANCE CARE INTENSIVE CARE UNIT

Significant learning in the virtual environment is considered a space that allows establishing a dynamic movement of construction and reconstruction of knowledge by generating an interaction between teacher-student, in such a way that the exercise of autonomy in the exploration of the contents favors the resolution of the proposed situations.In the meaningful learning it is necessary to have two fundamental elements: one corresponds to the learning material, that the student can retake and relate in a logical way taking into account the previous knowledge; the other element is the student's willingness to learn, which implies that his interest is focused on linking what has been learned to his cognitive structure. Thus, these two elements must be present necessarily in every formative process. Describe the types of meaningful learning obtained through the realization of a virtual asynchronous course of nursing care to the critical patient. Descriptive, transversal study, with 109 participants who completed the course of critical care with a 48 hour intensity of asynchronous way, aimed at nurses with more than one year.
