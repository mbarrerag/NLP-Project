# GENETIC ANALYSIS FOR GROWTH AND REPRODUCTIVE CHARACTERISTICS IN BRAHMAN WINTER USE OF BICHARACTERISTIC MODELS

A high number of characteristics influence the profitability of a livestock herd, it is necessary to define which should be included in genetic improvement programs, taking into account the associations between them. To perform a bi-racter analysis between growth and reproductive characteristics in a Brahmin herd. Productive and genealogical data were used for 38,483 animals belonging to the San Juan de Bedouth farm. The included characteristics were: 1. Growth: early stage weight (P4), wean weight (PD), 12 (P12), 18 (P18) and 24 (P24) months, early maternal ability (Hm4) and weaning (HmD). 2. Reproductive characteristics: first birth age (PPP) and birth interval (PMI). The animal models included the maternal (Hm4 and HmD) and permanent environment (AP) effect, respectively, for P4, PD and IEP, using WOMBAT software.
