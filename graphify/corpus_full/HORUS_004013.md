# Land, violence and politics in the Urabá Antioqueño, 1990-2010: approaches to the construction of a characterization and understanding of the institutionalized dispossession

This research aims to contribute, through a documentary analysis of the news related to land dispossession in the Urabá antioqueño, as well as case law in which the cases are addressed (Justice and Peace, Land Restitution and JEP), to the understanding of the process of concentration of wealth in Urabá, which we have called Corporate Crime and which forms an institutionalised dispossession, since it involves an association between entrepreneurs, state officials and members of criminal organizations of various types, providing illegal actions of appearance of legality and producing an unusual number of victims, in terms of land dispossession, forced displacement, selective assassinations, intimidation and other forms of violence. The study will be carried out from a relational perspective, which manifests itself in multiple ways through means, strategies and instruments; in the case of analysis, land as a means to accumulate wealth, violence as a strategy for the achievement of land and politics as a legitimizing tool for certain practices under the label of “common good”, the discourse of development and progress. .
