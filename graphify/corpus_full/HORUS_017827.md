# OPTIME DESIGNS FOR NON-LINEAL MODELS WITH CORRELATION STRUCTURE: RUBUSTACE STUDIES

The optimal designs provide the best experimental conditions, where the investigator must observe or measure a variable of interest. To find these designs, generalmente te is required the assumption of incorrelation in the term of error of the model, this allows to obtain in a simple way the matrix of information of Fisher used in the optimization process for the search of the designs of interest. In this article we propose a method that allows to compare accurate D-optimal designs when the supús-to of incorrelation of the term of error in the model is not fulfilled and we have under consideration four structures of covariance to model it. In this article we deduce a simplified expression of the matrix of information of Fisher in the general case of correlated observations and is used in the four structures of covariance considered. With each structure of covariance is found the respective structure of each covariance, known as nominal design, and the evaluation of the robustness of the other optimal designs via find the efficiency of these with relation to the nominal design.
