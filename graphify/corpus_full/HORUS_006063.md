# Evaluation of a multi-enzymatic immobilized system for the production of galacto-oligosaccharides from whey

The whey is a byproduct of the dairy industry which, due to its high organic load, can be used as feedstock for obtaining high added value substances. In this work, its lactose content was used for the enzymatic synthesis of galacto-oligosaccharides (GOS), prebiotic compounds destined for the market of functional foods. The synthesis of the GOS was made with β-galactosidases isolated from Aspergillus oryzae and Aspergillus niger; this is reversible in nature and is inhibited by the accumulation of glucose in the medium.
