# STUDENT TRAINING FOR TECHNICAL TRANSFER TO RURAL PEOPLE VICTIMS OF ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA: CASE UNIVERSITY EXTENSION IN BOVINE ARTIFICIAL INSEMINATION

The aim of this chapter is to describe a successful experience in training students from academic programmes in the agricultural sector for the transfer of technology to rural inhabitants affected by the armed conflict in Colombia and their training in leadership, rural management and artificial insemination. First, it describes the methodology used in the training process of student leaders, in which students were trained in theoretical and practical aspects of skills for extension of farming, the management of cattle trade and the technical skills of artificial insemination. Then, the process of transfer of knowledge from students to rural dwellers is presented
