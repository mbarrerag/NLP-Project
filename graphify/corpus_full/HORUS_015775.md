# INSTITUTIONAL RELATIONSHIP OF THE CREATION AND PURSUANT TO THE HEADQUARTERS OF PEACE

The ninth headquarters of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia was created by the Agreement 250 of October 2017 of the Superior University Council (CSU) and began activities in August 2019. Its name: La Paz Headquarters - by the municipality that ceases where the campus is erected and in allusion to a country that enrbola as a flag the construction of peace-, carries important meanings for the thousands of young people in the Caribbean Region who now have as their life project to become part of the National University of Colombia, La Paz Headquarters. In this document the design and consolidation of the National University of La Paz, showing the complexity of carrying out a project of this magnitude, as well as the commitment, hope and management that allowed it to be consolidated. To know the narratives of the people who actively participated in the creation of the headquarters allows understanding of the different paths in the project, the difficulties, the risks and the consequences of the project
