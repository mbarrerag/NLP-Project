# Primary central nervous system sarchomas in paediatric patients: radiological and pathological characteristics.

Primary sarcomas of CNS are a rare pathology and there is little literature that addresses in detail their imageological characteristics. The present is a retrospective descriptive study that aims to perform a clinical, imageological and pathological characterization of these neoplasms. 31 patients were collected between 2008 and 2021 attended by the HOMI Foundation. Clinical histories were reviewed, and pathology samples and diagnostic images (CT, RM) were analyzed. There were 19 boys and 12 girls, between 1-17 years old (average 9 years old). The vast majority presented headache and vomiting as the first clinical manifestation. The tumors were predominantly supratentorial (96%) and frontal location (62%) and parietal (21%). In 33% of cases the tumor crossed the middle line. The greater proportion of the masses were unioctal, with well defined edges and large sized. The incidence of activity was measured with KI67, showing a high proliferation (> 30%) in 86% of cases and areas of marked pleomorphism.
