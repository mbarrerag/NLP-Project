# Imaging of the heated subsurface structures beneath the western flank of Nevado del Ruiz volcano, Colombia.

This thesis reviews new information, existing geological and geophysical data and open source data from the western flank of the Nevado del Ruiz volcanic system (NRV), specifically within the valleys of Molinos/Rio Claro. These legacy data sets, dating from more than fifty years ago, are reviewed in the context of new data from the last ten years. Current interpretations are discussed in the light of past interpretations. The intention is to examine the effectiveness of the use of existing "old" data and to determine whether such data conform to current understanding in the framework of commercial geothermal exploration. New low frequency magnetotheluric data collected in 2013 are included in the data studied. In addition, the locations of surface geothermal manifestations (such as fumaroles, thermal waters and other phenomena) are qualitatively correlated with data and interpretation. Finally, a 2D model is developed and presented
