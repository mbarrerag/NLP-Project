# POMPE DISEASE: HISTORIA, EPIDEMIOLOGY, PHYSIOPATOLOGY AND CLINICAL MANIFESTATIONS

Pompe disease or type II glucogenosis belongs to the metabolic myopathy group and is produced by partial or total deficiency of the acidic alpha glycosidase enzyme. The absence/deficit of this enzyme generates a storage of glycogen inside the lysosomes in various tissues, including skeletal muscle, myocardium and smooth muscle cells. It is a multisystem disease that may have an early or late onset of symptoms. Contents: This article will describe the historical aspects of the disease, its pathophysiology and its clinical manifestations, with the emphasis placed on its early or late onset
