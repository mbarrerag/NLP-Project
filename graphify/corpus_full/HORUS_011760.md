# Genesis and classification of mineral deposits: (First prize in the competition opened by DYNA)

The importance of the issue. One of the most interesting topics for the mine engineer is undoubtedly the one related to the way in which the deposits are formed, their different types and the way they are presented in the earth’s crust. Although most of these issues remain almost exclusively under the domain of speculative geology, their study offers, however, a primary interest for those who, because of their profession, are dedicated to the search, study and exploitation of mineral substances useful to man. The needs of the time demand from the mine engineer a solid preparation in the technique of execution: with greater reason these needs must require a strict knowledge, until scientific research allows it, of the constitution, conditions and possibilities of the field in which it is operated.
