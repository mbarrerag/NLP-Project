# ELECTROCHEMICAL SENSORS FOR THE DETECTION OF MERCURY BASED ON MODIFIED COAL MATTERS WITH GOLD NANOPARTICULATE

In Colombia, one of the main sources of mercury (Hg) pollution is due to artisanal gold mining due to its high accumulation capacity in aquatic ecosystems that end up transporting it in different chemical forms throughout the trophic chain, affecting the population adjacent to the areas of mining activity. Currently, the clinical diagnosis of poisoning is confirmed through analysis of samples of blood, urine or hair that are carried out with the technique of atomic absorption spectroscopy with cold steam and flow injection. The increasing demand to determine mercury in biological samples needs the development of simple, fast and low cost techniques that allow to perform these analyses especially in areas far from the main urban centers and in this sense, the electrochemical methods offer a great possibility to meet this need. In this work, the electrochemical sensors based on nanoparticles of gold (AuNP) immobilized on carbonous materials (CM) and the SNP were developed using the methodology of the SNP3.
