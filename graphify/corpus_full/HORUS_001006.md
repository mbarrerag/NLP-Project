# Social representations and perceptions of young people about urban public space in the municipality of Rionegro (Antioquia), 2020-2023

This research focuses on analysing the social representations and perceptions of young people about the urban public space in the municipality of Rionegro, Antioquia, during the period 2020-2023, and their influence on the appropriation and use of these spaces. Through a qualitative methodological approach, various tools were used as field journals, non-participating observation, social mapping and semi-structured interviews to collect data in 5 public spaces in the municipality. The results obtained show the complexity of the youth perceptions, highlighting recurring concerns about safety issues, accessibility and quality of public spaces. Likewise, specific patterns of behaviour and appropriation of these spaces were identified by the youth population, as well as a variety of opinions and perspectives on the current offer of public spaces in the city.
