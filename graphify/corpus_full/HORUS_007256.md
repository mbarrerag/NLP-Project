# Between laughter and crying: Life in the circus Continuities and ruptures of the Colombian circus in the 21st century

This thesis is the research result on the transformations that have been presented in the modalities of the traditional, social and contemporary circus in Colombia during the 21st century from the conception of the body, forms of learning, stage and organization. With these elements four acts were constructed from an ethnographic perspective; what allowed to identify the various forms of body during the historical development of the circus, addressing the grotesque body, Freak, heroic, comic and the new paradigms of the body in the 21st century. Likewise, the processes of teaching of the landscape in the three forms of doing circus, starting from the stories of the Uncle Memo, Steps of Payasos, and Sarita; addressing the theory of performativity, extra-cotidian techniques, art and agency, comic effect and ethnownography to account for the transformations in these processes. Likewise, the putting into the scene of the traditional circus, contemporary and street, placing in the center of discussion the viewer as a transformative element in the different places in question and with whom the dialogues from the theory of the semiology and the local economy are based on the nographic perspective, enabling the identification of various forms of embodiment throughout the historical evolution of the circuit. These include the grotesque body, the freak, the heroic, the trade, and the new paradigms of the body in the 21st century.
