# THE INVENTION OF SCIENCE IN AMERICA.

This work investigates the images of science in a particular form of historical discourse: the Colombian historical novel. Its theorists affirm that one of the main virtues of this genre is its ability to offer critical perspectives on the dominant narratives of the country’s social, political and cultural history. It is interesting to understand what happens to this critical character when the focus is science: does the genre test the dominant narratives of Colombian scientific historiography or reinforce them and amplify them? The answer of this book is that the Colombian historical novel does not usually assume a critical stance with such narratives when its theme is science, but serves as a platform to reaffirm and propagate them
