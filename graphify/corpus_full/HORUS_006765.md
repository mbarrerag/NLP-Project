# The obscene bird of the night: the dream of the imbunche produces monsters

This final work develops a proposal to read the novel El obscene bird of the night by José Donoso, published in 1970, taking as a starting point the crisis of the narrative in the modern novel. It focuses on how the work manages to narrate the experience despite the intrinsic difficulties of the act of narrating in a complex historical context. The author analyzes formal elements such as the configuration of the space, the characters, the points of view, and the repetition of motives. Through concepts such as the imbunche and the grotesque, the dialectic between isolation and openness is explored, as well as the construction of a new narrative order based on the accumulation of versions and points of view. The author argues that the work does not seek to represent chaos or madness, but rather it configures an alternative and ambiguous order that allows to narrate from a multiplicity of perspectives .
