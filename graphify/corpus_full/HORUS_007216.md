# Expression of Hand2 and Nphs2 in the renal morphogenesis of the chicken embryo

The kidney plays an essential role in the maintenance of the homeostasis of the individual thanks to its multiple regulatory and excretive functions. The various congenital pathologies that affect it represent an important cause of chronic kidney disease in children and young adults; and in animals a common cause of glomerulopathy mainly in dogs and young cats; so it is essential to expand knowledge about the genes that can trigger these conditions, as is the case Hand2 and Nphs2 in a regulatory and functional context, respectively. Although the importance of the Nphs2 gene for the proper functioning of the renal filtration barrier and the essential role of Hand2 in terms of morphological regulation of the kidney has been demonstrated previously in a temporary context during different stages of the embryo development of the chicken. Therefore, the objective of this study was to evaluate the expression of both genes in a temporary context of this research model. To meet this objective, it was evaluated the expression of both genes obtained through RT-PCR, which was subsequently confirmed with the expression of the embryonary development of the chicken.
