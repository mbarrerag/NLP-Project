# Parkland Scale as a prognostic factor associated with post-surgical outcomes in patients undergoing laparoscopic cholecystectomy at the National University Hospital of Colombia.

Colecystitis is the acute inflammatory disease of the gallbladder, which is attributed in most cases to the presence of gallstones. It is the most common cause of hospitalization due to gastrointestinal disease and the third cause of admission to surgical emergency services. Laparoscopic cholecystectomy is the most frequent surgical management. Parkland scale is a classification designed to estimate surgical difficulty depending on intraoperative findings of laparoscopic cholecystectomy and thus establish the level of surgical difficulty. The aim of this study is to establish the association between the Parkland scale and the presence of post-surgical outcomes related to the completion of laparoscopic cholecystectomy in patients with acute cholecystitis.
