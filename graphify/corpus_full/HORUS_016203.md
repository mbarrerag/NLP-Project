# CLINIC, ENVIRONMENTAL AND MULECULAR CHARACTERIZATION OF FIVE DOMINICAN FAMILY WORK AND/OR HOLD PALADAR

To characterize five Dominican families with non-syndromic LPH (LPH-NS) with autosomal dominant inheritance, in a clinical, environmental and molecular way. METHODOLOGI ́A: Five Dominican families were recruited using the database of the Fundación Nín~os que Rien, to whom genealogical trees were made with clinical and population data about three generations in each family. A clinical and dental review, complete genome analysis in trio was made by using Illumina technology by means of the HiSeqX platform with average coverage of 30X. A workflow was implemented that included the call of bases, multiplexation and subsequent alignment with human genome reference hg19. Insilicone prediction teams were applied to evaluate the conservation and possible effect of the detected variants. IRF6 was considered to be elements of the clinical history and criteria for classification of variants of the American College of Mechanical and Genomic Gene (ACMG) for the final definition.
