# Organizational structures of small producers for access to financial services in Colombia

Due to the barriers faced by small agricultural producers in accessing agricultural credit granted by formal financial institutions, there is a need to deepen self-managed instruments and mechanisms such as the savings and self-financing groups formed by the scattered rural population, so that it is the same rural population that can, in a self-managed manner, address the deficiencies in access to credit. This work of master's degree in deepening seeks through literature review and consultation with actors and promoters of self-managed mechanisms operating in some rural contexts of the country, describe the context, identify the objectives drawn up for these mechanisms and, based on their opinions, contrast the information to generate conclusions and recommendations for their implementation . Due to the barriers that small agricultural produce, face in accessing credit from formal financing institutions, it is necessary to strengthen and expand the use of self-managed instruments and mechanisms—suchaly savings and self-finance rural institutions
