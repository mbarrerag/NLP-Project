# ACTIVITIES FOR ACTIVE LEARNING IN BIOCHEMICAL CLASSES FOR BIOLOGY PREGRADING STUDENTS

The following is a description of the experience resulting from biochemistry classes with students of Biology, executed during the first semester of 2021, in the framework of the Laboratory of Pedagogical Innovation, at the headquarters of La Paz, of the National University of Colombia, whose aim is to promote a Community of Learning. The following articulating axes were contemplated: (i) Biomolecules; (ii) Metabolism; (iii) Balance and Energy; and (iv) Nucleic Acids. The introduction to each axis was carried out by the students; for this purpose, they used guiding questions suggested by the teacher in charge; as feedback, the teachers clarified concepts of rationale concerning. In conclusion, in relation to traditional academic practice, during this period, the students had a higher percentage of participation in the activities proposed, as well as a lower percentage of the academic loss of the subject
