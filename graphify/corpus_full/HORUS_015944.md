# INFLUENT VARIABLES IN VACUNATION AGAINST COVID-19: ANALYSIS FROM THE DATA MINERY

The objective of this research is to determine the most influential variables in the vaccination process against COVID-19 in the adult and active Colombian population in social networks, through data mining and the J48 algorithm applied through the Weka platform. To do this, a dependent variable (will it be vaccinated?) and 10 independent variables are analyzed. A survey was applied through social networks to 200 people in the Colombian population between 20 and 60 years. As a result, it is found that with an effectiveness of 97% the most influential causes in vaccine resistance are: difficulty in mobilizing, effectiveness of vaccines, side effects and fear of dying. In conclusion, the data mining technique developed in this research allows establishing the main reasons why people in the context of Colombia are not vaccinated against COVID-19
