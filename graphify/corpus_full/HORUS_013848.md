# Sovereign Habitat, An Opportunity for Rural Life

Colombia since the second half of the twentieth century has been characterized by an almost infinite wave of internal migration, today at the beginning of the third decade of the twenty-first century our country is once again the scene of migration with forced displacement, migrations from abroad, migration from the countryside to the city and from the city to the countryside. All of these migrations produced for endless reasons, which raises many questions about how to live in urban and rural areas. This research aims to link the issue of population mobility with other elements, such as rural habitat, rural development, productivity, memory and belonging, aspects that are indisputably part of the discussion on how to build rural scenarios for the population in Colombia, in such a way that territorial dynamics are generated that contribute to the construction of a rural productive social fabric
