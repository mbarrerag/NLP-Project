# Construction of rural housing in the village of Manizales

The rural area of the municipality of Manizales faces a quantitative housing deficit of 5,163 households, according to data from the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE, Population and Housing Census 2018). The poverty conditions of the inhabitants, combined with economic and social limitations, make it difficult to build decent housing, which aggravates the situation of vulnerability of the rural population. Many of these dwellings do not meet the minimum standards of habitability, since they present structural deficiencies and lack access to basic services. This work aims to assess the factors limiting the construction of rural housing with minimum housing conditions, which makes it more habitable in the areas of the municipality of Manizales. A number of variables were analyzed, such as income level, economic activity, educational level, number of people working in the home and number of children in charge. Through a study of 95 families in 35 villages, the main socio-economic obstacles that prevent the improvement of living conditions in these rural areas were identified, as well as the results obtained have allowed to propose solutions that address these limitations, in order to promote the construction of accessible and sustainable housing, which guarantee a better quality of life for the rural families (the fifth and rural families). zed, including income levels, economic activity, education level, the number of working individuals in the household, and the number of dependants children.
