# The night atmosphere in a tropical urban area of complex terrain. Case study: the Aburá Valley (Colombia).

This research characterized the thermal and dynamic structure of the night limit layer for the year 2017 in the Valle de Aburá-Colombia, a tropical urban area with complex topography. Records from a microwave radiometer, a wind profiler radar, a ceilometer, meteorological stations and the model WRF-ARW coupled to the urban parametrization model SLUCM were used. The latter was executed with six schemes of parametrization different from the atmospheric limit layer, for 33 nights distributed in the study period. An exploratory analysis was executed to identify spatial-temporal processes using state variables such as winds, Richardson number approximated, the potential temperature virtual and the intensity of retrodispersion. A sensitivity analysis of the records was found that the thickness of the night limit layer corresponds to a number of Richardson critical 0.5. In addition, the model for hours of the night was evaluated and the more acceptable performance of the pattern of parametrization MYNN was found. Also, through a sensitivity analysis of the records were found that the thickness of the night limit is at a critical level of Richardson number 0.5
