# FROM OVER TO MATERIAL FROM RUBBLE TO RECYCLED CONCRETE. FROM RESIDEL TO MATERIAL ISN'T THE COMPREHENSIONS TO THE RECYCLED CONCRET?

This work, which is a derivation of the PhD Thesis in Human and Social Sciences project, presents an interdisciplinary reflection that integrates the technical, environmental, economic and anthropological vision (socio-aesthetic), on the object of study there proposed: the transit of the residue (scre) towards a new material (recycling concrete).In a positivist world, of disciplinary specializations, with a predominant economic model based on linear flows, and in which the edilicia activity is for humanity something practically unavoidable, the balance is dramatic in terms of the extraction of non-renewable raw materials and the generation of waste.In this panorama, it is necessary to find new alternatives such as that explored here to materialize the habitats; for example, the valorization or resurrection of the detritus, the restructuring of its becoming new materials of optimal performance, affordable cost and aesthetic value
