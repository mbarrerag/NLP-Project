# Development of an aerobic granular sludge for water treatment in a sequential discontinuous reactor (SBR).

This work has as main goal to evaluate the formation of an aerobic granular sludge from an active sludge in a Sequential Discontinuous Reactor (SBR), proposes a procedure that describes the experimentation for the formation of aerobic granules and determines the removal of organic matter by means of the CODs of the tributary and effluent. The experimentation was developed in three phases. In the first, the assembly of the reactor was made with a working volume of 5 litres, a Volumetric-RIV Exchange Radio of 60% and the automation of the system, was inoculated with active sludge and synthetic residual water of 600 mg/l of DQO and the start of the process, started with a time of operation of cycle of 12 hours.
