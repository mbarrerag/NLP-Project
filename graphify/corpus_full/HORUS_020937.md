# -Prevalence of VIRUS SINCITIAL BOVINO RESPIRATORY IN TERNERS OF SIX MONTHS OF AGE IN FINAL MILK OF BOGOTA SABANA

-Respiratory disease in calves causes a significant morbi-mortality with significant economic losses and high costs of treatments and consequences in the medium and long term. It has been shown that stressful events, after a primary infection of viral etiology, are the beginning of tables with subsequent infection by opportunistic bacteria that affect the low respiratory tract. The bovine respiratory syncitial virus (BRSV) has been described as the primary agent and is the most commonly identified in bovine respiratory complex and in epizootic pneumonias in dairy herds, with morbidity rates of 60-80% and mortality rate of 20%. Previous seroepidemiology studies carried out in Colombia have demonstrated indirectly its presence, which makes its direct identification important and determination of its participation in respiratory syndromes in calves in the country.
