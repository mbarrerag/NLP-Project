# MUSEUMS, ETHNOEDUCATION AND ANTI-RACISMS. THE EXPOSURE CONSENT IS: THE NEGARIAN FAMILY

This essay problematizes an Afro-repair case in a joint initiative of a museum and communities from the exhibition The consensus is: The black family, collectively curated by artist Liliana Angulo and members of the organizations of the Afro-social movement of Medellín (Colombia) in the Museo de Antioquia (mda) during 2019. The text places both coloniality and structural racism as problems that extend to the present day in Colombia and raises the complexity of the museum's decolonizing claim, at a time when dissent is used as a good of exchange to cover up the colonial regime that it still represents
