# What to do with Altos de Cazuca? Habitat Improvement Guidelines

The city of informal origin for a long time was recognized as a problem that had to be eradicated, however, in recent years, with the paradigm shift around the habitat in Colombia, different programs of improvement have been carried out that include the legalization of the settlement and the intervention in terms of basic sanitation, which raises a new question: Are the physical spatial improvement actions sufficient to improve the quality of life of the population? The research carries out a discussion around this question, making a critique of the technocratic model that understands the quality of life, only as an attribute, ignoring the ways of living and the dynamics of coexistence of informality that are different from conventional ones, placing as object of study, Altos de Cazucá, Soacha. .
