# A look from nature: the poetics of the tale of Tomás González

This paper presents a reading of the stories of Tomás González, published in three volumes and gathered in La espinosa be the beauty of the world. More than collected stories (2019). Through an attentive analysis of his work, we seek to propose a poetic of the story that gives an account of the author’s evaluation proposal. The reading proposal that is presented here about the stories of Tomás González is that in them the consideration of nature is central as a value-value point, a place where the author positions himself to elaborate a critical look at reality. His story is a wonderful laboratory of this aesthetic evaluation. To defend this reading of the stories of González, the following order will be followed: the first will be to present briefly the socio-cultural context in which González’s work is developed; then the bulk of the interpretative proposal will be presented: it goes from a characterization of the type of experience that the writer takes advantage of his stories, the resources used to form an evaluation of that type of experience, up to the type of criticism that becomes a reality. . first, a brief presentation of the sociocultural context in which González's work develops will be made; then, a balance of the contributions of criticism to the study of Tomás González's work will be made; after that, the bulk of the interpretive proposed will be presented: which ranges from a characterization of the type of experience that the writer privileges within his stories, the resources used to form an evaluation of that type of experience, to the type of criticism made of reality
