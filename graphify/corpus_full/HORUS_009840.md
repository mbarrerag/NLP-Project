# Housing habitability of social interest. Spatial strategies for VIS, based on patterns of housing appropriation in Bosa

The concept of appropriation as a means of qualifying housing habitability and the applicable regulations are analyzed, establishing a methodology based on the language of patterns of C. Alexander, which seeks to resolve the antipatterns of Social Interest Housing by analysing three cases of housing development study in the town of Bosa: self-managed housing, housing by state management and housing by private managers (VIS), to propose spatial strategies that enable habitability in housing of social interest. . The concept of acceptance as a means to quality the habitability of the welfare, and the applicable current regulations are analyzed, establishing a methodology based on the pattern language of C. Alexander, which is to solve the anti-patterns of Social Interest Housing through the analysis of the case studies of housing development in the town of Bosa
