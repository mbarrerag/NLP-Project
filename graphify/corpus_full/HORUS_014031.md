# ASSESSMENT OF THE INTERACTION OF SUPERFISTIVE WATER - SUBTERRANEAN WATER IN URBAN ECOSYSTEMS

Wetlands are water extensions declared as strategic ecosystems (Ramsar Wetlands Convention), whose permanence depends on interaction with groundwater. Among its functions is to cushion floods and regulate river and ravine flows. To carry out the project, the aim is to design low-cost equipment for monitoring groundwater such as infiltrometers, as well as the carrying out of the isotopic characterization by sampling in surface and underground waters. Among the expected results, the seedbed will have laboratory analyses of each monitored sample. Based on the above, the interaction between surface water and groundwater in urban ecosystems such as the Torca wetland and campus of the Universidad Nacional-Sede Bogotá will be determined
