# Ecosystem services of urban wooded areas of the Aburra River Valley in Medellín

Urban ecosystems became a fundamental part of sustainable land management, with urban forest being one of the most representative components of these ecosystems. Urban woodland provides various ecosystem services such as microclimate regulation, carbon capture, water regulation, habitat supply and food for wildlife, pest and disease control, just to mention some, improving both the quality of life of people and the surrounding environment. This study evaluated, assessed and quantified some of the ecosystem services provided by urban woodland present in four parks of the Medellin city valley through the use of the I-Tree Eco software and the environmental meter 4 in 1 and, from this information, analyzed the importance of urban woodland in the city’s public spaces. The results obtained allow highlighting the importance of urban woodland in the reduction of temperature and increased climate comfort in public spaces, as well as in the capture of atmospheric pollutants and production of oxygen, although they were not determinants in the formation of noise barriers.
