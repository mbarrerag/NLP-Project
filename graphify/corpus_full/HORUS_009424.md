# CHEMICAL AND BIOLOGICAL ANALYSIS OF THE ESSENTIAL BURSERA SIMARUBA OIL.

B. simaruba is a plant species that could contribute to the re-establishment of Bs-T. The chemical composition and antioxidant and repellent activities of B. simaruba's AE were determined in this work. The chemical composition of the AE allowed establishing a sustenance that explains some traditional uses of this species
