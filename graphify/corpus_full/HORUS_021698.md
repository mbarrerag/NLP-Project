# On-line meal detection module coupled with a zone MPC for closed-loop blood glucose control

Meal announcement is a method used to improve the performance of control schemes for blood glucose regu-lation in type 1 diabetes treatments. Automatic carbohydrate intake estimation is a challenge to achieve a fully automated artificial pancreas. Here, a previously proposed on-line meal estimation based on a feedback structure is modified and coupled with and impulsive zone model predictive controller to achieve improvement of the blood glucose regulation. The proposed scheme is tested on 10 virtual patients. The time in hyperglycemia is reduced almost 40 % without major risk of inducing hypoglycemia, which suggests that the method can be effective
