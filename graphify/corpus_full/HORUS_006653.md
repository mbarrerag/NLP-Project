# Favourability and effects on the profitability of Mipymes benefiting from the simple taxation regime compared to the ordinary regime, in line with the tax reform of 2022

The aim of this investigation is to determine the favourability and effects on profitability in Mipymes which fall under the simple RST tax regime compared with those remaining under the ordinary ROT tax regime as doing so is a voluntary option By conducting simulations to find the actual tax rates (TET) in each of the groups and income ranges established in the RST, calculating the profitability indicators ROA and ROE taking into account the tax value generated in both regimes and establishing the pro-fesibility or disfasability of taking advantage of the RST versus the ROT. Additionally, tax scenarios to be paid in the RST should be simulated, modifying the tax benefit variables such as the number of employees, ICA rates and in the ROT by varying the tax costs obtaining a broader view of the results. As a mechanism of verification and control the calculation of the profitability indicators to be determined on the basis of the tax burden of companies that reported their financial statements 2022 to the SIIS system of the industry and trade of Colombia, what enabled the success of the results to be compared and determined by the profitability that would have been achieved by the MITSTIT and by the s, it was possible to establish the favourability or unfavourability of using the RST versus the ROT. Additionally, tax payable scenarios were simulated in the RST, modifying the tax benefit variables such as the number of employees, ICA rates and in the ROT by varying the fiscal costs, obtaining a broker view of the results. As a verification and control mechanism, profitability indicators were calculated on the basis of purified data from companies that reported their 2022 financial statements to the SIIS system of the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce of Colombia, which made it possible to compare and determine the benefit to those MSMES would have obtained if they had joined the RST.
