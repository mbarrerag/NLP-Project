# Paraaortic Lyndopenectomy in Cancer de Cérvix Locally Advanced: Experience of the National Institution of Cancerology, Bogotá, D.C., COLOMBIA

Paraaortic lymphadenectomy for surgical staging, in locally advanced cervical cancer, allows to detect microscopic ganglion disease that is not visible by pre-surgical diagnostic imaging. The aim of this study is to describe the surgical and oncological outcomes of patients with locally advanced cervical cancer, according to the classification FIGO 2009 (IB2-IVA), who presented pelvic ganglion involvement in images and were taken to paraaortic lymphadenectomy at the National Cancer Institute in Bogotá, Colombia. A retrospective cohort study that included women with locally advanced cervical cancer, attended at the institution between January 2016 and March 2019. The clinical, histopathological and surgical outcomes characteristics were described, including operative and postoperative morbidity. In addition, the overall survival (SG), and disease-free survival (SLE) were evaluated. A univariate analysis and description of the oncological results obtained in this population.
