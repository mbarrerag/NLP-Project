# TIME TRANSEMPOINTS

This book of research and creation is a journey, a book-work that invites us to contemplate life in its shoots of light and form. The spaces that name us and at the same time chisel us become image-word, a new sensation that condenses a bit of life, a vestige of love, a memory and a definitive blow. This book traces with the brush and ink two experiences that accompany each other
