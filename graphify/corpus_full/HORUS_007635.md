# Design and preparation of biocarbonized for selective amoxicillin adsorption. From computational chemistry to the production of materials with social impact

The continuous use of pharmaceutical products and personal care has generated concern due to their presence in waste water. Although these compounds have been present in water bodies for decades, concern for their consequences has recently increased, thus being called emerging pollutants. Within pharmaceutical products, antibiotics raise additional concern for their impact on water bodies, as well as for the resistance that different microorganisms can develop. In response to this situation, studies have been carried out in Colombia on antibiotics in hospital waters and Waste Water Treatment Plants (PTAR), finding levels that require measures for their removal [1]. Amoxicillin is one of the most consumed antibiotics and it is estimated that 78% of the medicinal product is excreted without modification [2]. Therefore, the possibility of using the circular economy model has been proposed as a solution.
