# CHAPTER 1: GENERAL ASSISTANCE OF TUBERCLESIS

This book has as its objective the inclusion of a text with the necessary bases to control tuberculosis in Colombia, as one of the research and intervention priorities in line with the national guidelines laid down in Colombia’s National Plan for Public Health (DP)2022-2031 and in the development of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), in target 3(3)(3) related to ending tuberculosis, HIV/AIDS and malaria epidemics.In this regard, it is essential that the academy contributes to a need identified as the training of qualified human talent in the management of tuberculosis, as many studies have identified shortcomings in the knowledge of health workers that have to do with the diagnosis, treatment and control of the disease.In the first chapter, the chapter will include the training of qualified human talent in the management of tuberculosis, as well as the topic of general tuberculosis. Chapter 5 deals with the general theme of infection control measures, general control measures, the characteristics of tuberculosis Mycobacterium and the control of infections and safe working practices. Chapter 6 describes measures for the administrative control of tuberculosis and its components, such as the active search for respiratory symptoms and the epidemiology surveillance of disease in health personnel. Chapter 7 lists environmental control measures, natural and mechanical ventilation, the use of particulate air filters, the high-efficiency side (hepa, by their acronyms ingles) and the rationalisation of the population with each of its components.
