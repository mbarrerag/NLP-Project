# Revitalization of an urban heritage environment: Barrio Polo Club de Bogotá

The “Polo Club” neighborhood of Bogotá, icon of the urban and architectural history of the Colombian capital, with its innovative urban approach, heir to the principles of the modern movement European and North American, has the presence of housing groups in series of marked modernist features. nowadays, it is classified as “sector of cultural interest”. In this unique urban piece, there is today a phenomenon of gradual change of use that transforms and changes its original residential character, giving rise to the establishment of new activities, especially commercial and services. The problem to solve was identified as the “loss of the heritage image and the spatial relations of the urban-architecnical urban-architecnical, resulting from the change of use”. This TFM proposes that through a proposal for a renewed and revitalized intervention, it will be possible to avoid the neighborhood losing its heritage and character completely as “Sector of Cultural Interest”. It proposes a project of general urban design with innovative-archi-archip interventions of the type of renewal located in the periphery of the sector studied and also detailed several road corridors that interlink throughout the sector. Redisindiising profiles of some streets and sections of some streets and sections of some streets and sections of the public space, and to the establishment of new activities, especially focused on the commercial and service business. The Problem to be solved, was identified as “the loss of the patrimonial image and loss of urban-architectonic spatial relationships” due to the land-use change. The present document states that it will be possible for the quarter to avoid the total loss of its hereditary conditions as well as its legal status (“Cultural Zone”), through a revitalizing intervention proposal.
