# Strategy for the transfer of technology from academia to industry: Case of the Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering of the National University of Colombia.

The relationship of the Academy with society, and the institutions that make up it, allows to strengthen the economic and social development of nations from the knowledge and appropriation of technologies. In order to contribute to the establishment of actions that promote this relationship, the present thesis has been aimed at establishing strategies to adopt in the processes of transfer of technology generated at the University to Industry from the identification of the technology to the stage prior to the negotiation, starting from a case study of the Department of Chemical and Environmental Engineering of the National University of Colombia. Strategies were mainly addressed around the identification, evaluation and valuation of technologies, which were applied to three case studies of the research line of bioprocesses, biofuels and biorefination in different degrees of development: i) pilot system of distillation-reaction for the production of esters, which can be transferred as Know-How; ii) reaction system for the production of the esters of fatty acids using a liquid film reactor operated in reverse, whose transfer can be carried out by means of licensing with analysis of future benefits; iii) process of drying of cannabis operating power
