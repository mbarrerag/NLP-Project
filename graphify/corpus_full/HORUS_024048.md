# THE CHALLENGES TO TELEVISIONS IN LATIN AMERICA POLITICAL CULTURE, PUBLIC COMMUNICATION AND INNOVATION.

This book studies audiovisual television production in terms of its cultural, historical and political importance in our societies, as well as its formal dimension and ways of communicating through the TV style. In this way, collaboration was born between the Group of Research Communication and Culture in Televisualities, Comcult/ufmg and the Group of Research Communication, Culture and Citizenship of the Institute of Political Studies and International Relations of the National University of Colombia
