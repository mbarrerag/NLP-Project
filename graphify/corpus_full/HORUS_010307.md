# COLOMBIA ACCOUNTS WITH NEW PAPA VARIETIES FOR THEIR PRODUCTIVE SYSTEM IN THE COUNTRY CENTRE

The main output of a genetic improvement program is the registration of new cultivars, which are expected to be used by farmers in productive systems. This article presents some general characteristics of the new varieties registered by the FCA-UNAL’s potato genetic improvement program. Its advantages for the productive system and consumers are highlighted, as well as the reviews of the names given to them by the research program. It is written in simple language with the expectation that it will be understood by farmers and the general public
