# The information security management system used in the ISO/IEC 27000 family of standards

The information is currently one of the most important organizational assets, not only because it is the fundamental input for decision-making, but because it is one of the critical resources for the operation of companies, and even becomes a business transformer. Hence the need to establish adequate levels of protection. The discipline that allows the protection of information and technological infrastructure in which it is supported is the security of information. This is an integrator of computer security and cybersecurity as conceptual structures that have a direct impact on the protection of technological devices that generate, transform, store, process, retrieve, disseminate and transmit data and information. According to the above, this text seeks to contribute with the concepts, elements and components required for the implementation of an information security management system from the perspective of the main standards of the iso/iec 27000 family, taking into account that its incorporation into the organizations usually starts as a project but ends as an organization process that must be managed permanently under the principle of the absence of absolute security
