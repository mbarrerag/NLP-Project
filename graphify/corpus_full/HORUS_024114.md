# DIVERSITY OF CICADELIDES AND DESILIDES ASSOCIATED TO QUERCUS HUMBOLDTII BONPL (FAGACEAE) IN BOGOTÁ, COLOMBIA

Quercus humboldtii plays an ecological role in several ecosystems of the Andes and is part of the urban wooding in Bogotá. This species is affected by the groups of phytoplasms 16SrI and 16SrVII, which are bacteria transmitted by insects.The objective of this work was to contribute to the study of the diversity of Hemiptera associated as vectors of phytoplasms in oak, for which they were determined taxonomically cicadelides and psilidos.In 2019 insects were collected in oaks located in three sites with contrasting landscape structure in Bogotá using direct and indirect samplings.Insects were determined morphologically and molecularly, sequencing the IOC gene to establish codes of DNAmt bars. Alpha, beta were calculated and the most abundant species of Kruskall-Wallis were obtained with the abundance of individuals captured in function of environmental variables, type of sampling, place or orientation of traps. 510 cicadélidos (13 species) and 129 psilidos (8 species), the most abundant species of Scaphytopius sp. (n=244) and Acizzia)
