# GEOCLIMATE CONDITIONS AND SOCIO-MOGRAPHICALS OF URBAN SPACE IN THE ANDINIAN TROPIC CLIMATE, NORTHERN MEDELLIN, COLOMBIA CASE STUDY

The urban dynamics of growth of Colombian cities located in topographic mountain reliefs can affect the conditions of the microclimate and in turn the daily life of the domestic spaces. In this research, the conditions of the geoclimate of the urban spaces and the sociodemographic conditions of housing in the slopes of Medellín, located in the Valle de Aburrá, average altitude 1450 msnm, latitude 6.25° North and longitude -75.4°. Through a deductive analytical methodology, the climatic variables, sociodemographic, human and spatial architectural typology were studied in three blocks of an urban strip with east - west perpendicular to the river, in the north of the city of Medellín. It was identified that the growth of the city was from the center of the valley to the slopes and that the sociodemographic and geoclimatic conditions are different: in the bottom of the valley the radiation is less in the morning and the tree section of the city, but it is where the urban climate is less populated.
