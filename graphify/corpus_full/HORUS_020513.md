# Modelling of the lithospheric thermal field by the method of finite conservative differences applied in the area of the Oriental Cordillera, Colombia

In this thesis the equation of conduction-advection was differentiated by the method of conservative finite differences and a code was written in Matlab that solves the field of temperature of the lithosphere in 2D. This code was validated comparing its results with analytical solutions of some simple tectonic scenarios in 1D and 2D reported in the literature. The numerical solutions obtained agree with the analytics showing that differences are reached less than 1°C provided that the discretization is dense (>5000 vertical nodes). The best results are obtained in cases where there are no sources or sinks of heat in the modeled space. Subsequently, the code was used to solve the lithospheric thermal field in the Eastern Cordillera of Colombia. To constrict the numerical solutions were used the database of geothermal gradient LogDB and some estimates of heat flow carried out in the Eastern Plains basin.
