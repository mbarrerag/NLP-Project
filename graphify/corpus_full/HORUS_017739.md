# ANALYSIS OF THE INTENSITY OF DISCONTINUITIES IN TALUDS THROUGH PUNCUAL PATRONS

In the development of road infrastructure and mining projects in rocky massifs, the stability of the slopes may be controlled by some characteristics of discontinuities, such as the number of families present and their orientation, openness, persistence, spacing and intensity. In particular, the intensity of fractures perceived on the surface of a rocky slope is a determining parameter in stability and their estimation may be complex. Conventional analysis methods assume a homogeneous distri- tion of the intensity of fractures, by means of a constant value determined from compass and tape measurements in some sampled sectors of the slope. Pro- we combine spatial statistics, taking data with short-range photogrammetry and three-dimensional image analysis techniques, to estimate and map the intensity of fracture on the surface of a slope. Each family of discontinuities, defined co-mo-intensity a set of lines, is represented by a spatial pattern of points and their marks as the direction and magnitude of discontinuity.
