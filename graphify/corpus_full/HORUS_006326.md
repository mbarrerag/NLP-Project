# The Problem of Theoretical Realism of Sets

This text is investigated whether Maddy's Theoretical Realism of Sets in Realism in Mathematicas represents a coherent intermediate path between Gödel's Platonism and Quine's naturalism. To do this, it is analysed in depth how theoretical realism of sets appears as an intermediate position, and likewise, it is evaluated whether the interpretation of the North American Philosopher of both positions is correct.
