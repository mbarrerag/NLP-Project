# Response of osteoporotic bone tissue to an implant.

Osteoporosis has become a public health problem due to its high prevalence and is defined as a skeletal disorder characterized by increased bone fragility and an increased predisposition to fracture. In most cases the treatment required is by surgical procedures where biocompatible materials are used. The aim of this study was to identify the response of osteoporotic tissue to an implant in an animal experimentation model. The methodology was developed according to specific objectives: 1) A systematic review of the literature of the histophysiology of bone tissue, its changes with age and its normal response to biomaterials was carried out; 2) An animal model was developed that allowed to evaluate the response of osteoporotic bone tissue to a biomaterial; 3) The histological changes that presented the bone tissue to a biomaterial were described.
