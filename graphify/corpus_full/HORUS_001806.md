# Between the fiscal rule and the guarantee of rights: critical analysis of the Colombian State's response to the pandemic crisis (2020)

This paper proposes a critical analysis of the relationship between fiscal policy and social policy implemented as a response to the crisis caused by the virus virus pandemic COVID-19 under the perspective of the rights approach for the analysis of public policies. The scope of monetary transfer programmes, the limitations established by the tax rule and the implications on full rights guarantee are contrasted. Barriers imposed by the dominant model of fiscal policy that turn their backs on full employment and make it impossible to take the countercyclical route in a crisis context. In the end, a job buffer proposal is proposed as an alternative to transfer programmes, exceeding its scope in terms of rights guarantee, with a view to full employment and merit goods as guiding elements of fiscal and social policies. .
