# Comparing machine learning algorithms and feature extraction techniques for the automatic detection of surface EMG activation timing

This paper introduces a methodology for denoising, feature extraction, and pattern classification of surface electromyography (sEMG) signals, for the sake of automatically detecting the presence of muscular activity. Features extraction of the proposed methodology is based on the Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT) and on the Willison's Amplitude Algorithm (WAMP). Five classification alternatives namely: Neural Networks, Classification Vector, XGBoost, LGBTM, and ExtraTree were tested using several figures of merit, such as F-Measure, Precision, and Recall. The use of XGBoost with the Eigen values feature achieved the best training performance using k-fold cross-validation with a F1-Score of 98.71%. While for the test group the LGBM classifier with WAMP, and NN and both features (WAMP and Eigenvalues) give the best performance on average with 96.52 ± 3.45% and 96.52 ± 3.07% of the F1-Score respectively. These results demonstrate that the proposed approach has the ability to detect EMG
