# GREEN BONDS FOR RENEWABLE ENERGY IN LATIN AMERICA AND THE CARIBBEAN.

The development of the green bond market for the renewable energy sector is growing in the Latin American region. This market is relevant not only for its volume but also for the impact it has on meeting the Sustainable Development Goals. Brazil, Chile and Mexico have the main emissions in terms of volume and projects. These financial instruments are expected to continue to grow and energy generation is renewable and financed through sustainable financial mechanisms
