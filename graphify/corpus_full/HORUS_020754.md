# Let the truth become a heritage! Recommendations for the construction of public policies for the management, administration and promotion of the collections of Human Rights in Colombia

This document contains the critical memories of two museum processes of which I took part while studying the Master’s Degree in Museumology and Heritage Management, as well as the reflective process that I propose as an approach to a public policy of managing human rights collections based on recommendations. These are: 1. The formulation of guidelines for the renovation of the hall the imaginary of the archaeological and historical Museum of Santa Maria de la Antigua del Darien in collaborative work. 2. The process of design, planning and structuring the Museum project of the Commission of Truth, which was approved from the professional experience carried out and 3. The systematization of some of the recommendations and demands that organizations and communities have expressed as necessary in the structuring of a public policy that saves the human rights collections in Colombia
