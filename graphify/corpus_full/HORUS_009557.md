# USE OF A GENERALIZED LINEAL MODEL TO SEARCH PARTICULAR LATENT CONTEES WHEN ONLY TOTAL CONTEES ARE AVAILABLE.

This work uses an econometric model that allows the estimation of latent counts by product types when only total counts of goods acquired online have been observed.The model's base is a generalized linear model with negative binary response with a hurdle component for the decision to buy or not.An approximation, through expansions of Taylor, of the covariance structure of latent counts allows its subsequent estimation. In addition, a proposal for validation of the me- todology is discussed and correction mechanisms are mentioned for the case where the latent estimate does not present the appropriate adjustment
