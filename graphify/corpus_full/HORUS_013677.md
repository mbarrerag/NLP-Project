# Public housing rental in Colombia: a possibility of access to housing for vulnerable populations

The aim of this final work is to establish a model of public rental housing program that positively impacts the universalization of the right of access to housing, taking as a specific beneficiary population the most economically vulnerable social classes in the country. In this purpose the model is based on the characteristics and needs of the population segments referred to and positive experiences in the matter. The goal of this final master's document is to establish a public rental housing program model that positive impacts universal access to affordable housing, focusing on the country's most vulnerable socioeconomic classes as the specific benefit and population. For this purpose, the model bases itself in the characteristics and needs of the questioned population segments and the positive experiences associated to the topic
