# Dynamics of thermal conductivity and the physical and chemical properties of a sodium saline soil with the application of low-range coal and its relationship to plant production

The use of low-range coal (CBR) in sodium saline soils significantly improved its physical and chemical properties. In this research, CBR was applied in pots at doses equivalent to 0, 2 and 4 tonnes per hectare; in field at doses equivalent to 0, 2 and 4 tonnes per hectare and in pots with 0, 12.5, 25 and 50% p/p. The results showed that CBR reduced thermal conductivity (CT), pH and electrical conductivity (EC), while increasing the organic matter content and the capacity to exchange cation (CIC), improving the conditions for plant growth. CBR also reduced the apparent density and increased the porosity of the soil. CBR favored plant growth, improving the survival and foliar development.
