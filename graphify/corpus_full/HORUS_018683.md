# Victoria - Life is a constant combat

This final work, written for the Master’s Degree in Creative Writings of the National University of Colombia, is a cinematographic script structured from all theoretical stages – practices for the elaboration of a document that gives start to the making of a film. ‘Victoria’ is categorized into the genre of black comedy and, although it was structured under the classical canon of the three aristotelic acts, the script poses dramatic risks in the temporalities, epiphanies that touch the grounds of the absurd, acid dialogues, inserts that enrich the story and the genre; dissolving characters and subtrames that do not dissociate themselves from the main plot and that contribute, from different conflicts and dramatic actions, to the transformation arc of the main character, thus fulfilling one of the basic principles of the script: all history is a promise. The work contains: title, theme, premise, storyline, overview, description of character, escaleta, note of intention and script dialogued. .
