# DESIGN, INSTITUTIONAL CAPACITIES AND STATE MANAGEMENT.

The structure of the public administration in Colombia has been in a permanent process of reform and, although in the discourse its horizon is based on the search for criteria such as effectiveness, efficiency, economy, equity, accountability and transparency, its achievements are still far from the objectives and goals set out. In the context of this situation are problems of design of the State apparatus, lack of institutional capacity and legitimacy and a public management approach that is not attuned to the safeguarding of universal rights and the satisfaction of citizens' needs. This chapter aims to identify the main failures in the state design, limited institutional capacities and problems in the models of public management as factors that explain the inability of the Colombian State to be guarantor of citizens' rights. It also proposes ways of analysis and action to overcome some of these problems, returning contributions from various authors and sources
