# DOES POLLUTION AFFECT THE PEAK LIGHTNING CURRENT? A STUDY ON THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN AEROSOLOPTICAL DEPTH AND PEAK LIGHTNING CURRENT

¿The following paper presents whether pollution affects the peak lightning current. We use data from the LINET lightning location system to obtain cloud-to-ground lightning data. We used the aerosol optical depth data from MODIS to get the pollution. This study was carried out in the city of BogotáColombia. An increase in lightning peak current is observed as a result of the pollution
