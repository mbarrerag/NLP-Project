# RESPIRATORIAL INTENSITY AND QUALITY ATTRIBUTS OF THE INTEROR BALANUM CV. `CAVENDISH ́ UNDER DIFFERENT CONSERVATION TREATMENTS.

Banana (Musa acuminata) is a fruit of economic importance that is characterized by climateric nature, so its natural maturation process is fast. This factor affects the quality of the fruit and the storage conditions for long periods of time. In order to help avoid economic losses related to the conservation of bananas, this study evaluated the behavior of quality attributes and respiratory activity of the fruit using different treatments under cooling conditions of 13±2 °C and 92±2% HR. The fruits were selected, washed and hygienized, and divided into four groups to which different treatments were applied: Cerabrix 50% edible coating (v/v), CaCl2 1% (p/v), Cerabrix 50% (v) + CaCl2 1% (p/v) and control. Attributes such as pH, titrable acidity, color, total soluble solids, weight loss, firmness and respiratory intensity were evaluated every 48 h.
