# Maliciosa Corcovea In the Midway of a Danced Language

This research project, created in order to approach a state of body in permanent metamorphosis, the movement and at the same time the containment of it, are the techniques used to deepen the notion of a bodily work, recognizing with these materialities the urgency of putting into tension the scriptural body as provocation of the movement, and the conversation as porous device between the bodies present in space. The invented anatomy is a layer that allows to deepen in the physical particularities of animal bodies that crack and allow to access the drowning of the appearance of the word, that being crossed in the throat is resisting to exit, in the empty space of objects where only foreign bodies are looked at, the voice appears inviting to the listening, and in turn the poiesis of the action, that leaves open the transit between possessa, rabies, indignation, humidity, cold, domestic, rural
