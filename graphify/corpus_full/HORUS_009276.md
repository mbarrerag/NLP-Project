# A STORY OF THE COLOMBIAN CAPITAL MARKET

This chapter offers a look at the Colombian capital market with its particularities from the prism of history, which is, by definition, dynamic. In the initial part, it focuses on a review of the aspects of domestic public debt that have ended up concretizing a stock market dominated by the TES (treasure titles) as its main instrument. In the second part, the focus is on the role of the capital market in private sector financing, reviewing the interesting transfer of the stock market and the more marginal role played by the corporate debt market, in order to end the historical relationship between the stock market and the exchange rate
