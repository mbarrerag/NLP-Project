# PRODUCTIVE ASSESSMENT OF ADAPTATED PASTS UNDER PHYSIOGRAPHICAL POSITION IN UNIFIABLE ARACIAN SABANES, COLOMBIA

The objective of the study was to evaluate some parameters of yield and protein value of three pastures adapted in a low physiographic position in araucana floodable savanna. Samples of each pasture were collected in the period of dry-rain and rain-dry transition. The results showed the pastures and the transition period showed statistical differences in the four variables analyzed. B. humidicola showed higher yield of VF and MS in the two transition periods, but lower PC content. Pastures A. zizanioid and H. amplexicaulis showed adequate nutritional value in floodable savanna conditions. In conditions of a floodable savanna, adapted pastures show better productive behavior in the drought-rain transition and higher protein value in the rain-dry transition
