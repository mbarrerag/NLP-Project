# Architectural projecting as an expression of living; case study: housing of social interest Plaza de la Hoja, Bogotá

The architectural project is generally seen as the development of the design of a building before being built that supposes a preconceived ideas regarding the living. However, from the paradigm of complexity, this work seeks to reflect on the importance of the modes of living in the architectural project process. Thus, the architectural project from the project, i.e., recognizes and incorporates the specificities of the modes of living of the future inhabitants. Through an analytical research, with a focus on complexity, the concepts of the architectural project process and the living are addressed and related. The case of study is taken of the social housing, specifically the housing of Priority Interest Plaza de La Hoja in Bogotá, to which, a matrix of analysis based on the conceptual system proposed, where categories of the project process are established, from the basic ideas, the approach, the management and the construction, to its habitation. Finally, it is concluded that the projectation is understood as an evolutionary process in which the habit of and this generates knowledge, which gives feedback to the project cycle. . specifically the Plaza of La Housing of Priority Interest in Bogotá, to which an analysis matrix was applied based on the proposed conceptual system, where categories of the moments of the process are established project, form the basic ideas, the approach, the management and the construction, to its habitability.
