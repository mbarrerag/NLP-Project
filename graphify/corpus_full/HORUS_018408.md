# GROWTH AND QUALITY OF THE WIN MUSCLE FOR CONSUMPTION

The quality of the meat represents a complex aspect both for the livestock farmer and for the industrial producer and retail market that is controlled not only by genetics but also by environmental effects.Some of the mechanisms that govern the quality of the meat have been clarified, but others are still subject to research.In this sense, the edition of this text was generated primarily with the purpose of contributing to the knowledge of the science and technology of the meat, initiating in chapter 1 with the growth and prenatal development until the postnatal growth of the animals destined for this purpose and how, by the interaction of the different metabolic components, the development and growth of an animal to generate an optimal quality product.In chapter 2 a review of the environmental factors before the death and postmortem that influence negatively on the quality of the meat and that affect the ability of an animal to produce meat of good quality and the quality of the meat is affected by the resultant body and the activity of the slaughterhouse is related to the main factors (ad librium and high quality). Chapter 6 addresses the theme of muscle color, one of the characteristics that is constituted in the first sensory attribute perceived by the consumer when making the decision for purchase, showing that an optimal color gives meat quality properties commercially desirable. This book will provide the community in general and research groups and students with the basic concepts and principles for a better understanding of the fundamentals of animal growth and the science of meat applied to animal production
