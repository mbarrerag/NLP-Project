# MINDFULNESS IN EDUCATION IN ENGINEERING: A REVIEW OF LITERATURA AND A PROPOSAL FOR THEIR CURRICULARY INTEGRATION

This paper presents a review of the literature of the effects of mindfulness practice on university students, especially in engineering areas, which has been motivated mainly by the current challenge faced by engineering educators to improve the academic performance of our students while considering the health of their mind and body. It includes tools used to measure physical bodily variables that demonstrate the effects of mindfulness practice as well as self-informed measurement tools. It concludes with a course proposal for the inclusion of this practice in engineering curricula
