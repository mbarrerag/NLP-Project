# Inclusive education in Bogotá. D.C.: An analysis of the global - sectoral benchmarks in the shaping of public policy for the care of persons with disabilities of school age in the official sector between 2000-2018

This research carried out the analysis of the global and sectoral benchmarks including the configuration of the public policy of inclusive education in the city of Bogotá, D.C. for the care of persons with disabilities in school age in the official sector, in a trajectory of time between the year 2000 and 2018. Methodologically, it is part of the integrationist or mixed approach of analysis of public policies, which highlights the cognitive analysis approach and the theory of the benchmarks proposed by Pierre Muller and Bruno Jobert. As a qualitative study, it is part of the constructivist paradigm and of a hermeneutic character. The configuration of the overall reference of the disability and the sectoral reference of inclusive education allowed to identify, describe and analyze the trajectory and progress in guaranteeing the rights of people with disabilities at social level. The access and participation in the educational sector of the general education showed changes and adjustments in the cognitive framework, given the significant increase in the enrolment system. Sible to identify, describes and analyzes the costume and progress in guaranteeing the rights of people with disabilities at a social level, access, and participation in the educational sector. Official evidenced changes and adjustments in the normative cognitive frameworks give the significant increase in the development system. Likewise, It managed to demonstrate both in the policy community and the decision system, the organization and hierarchy of the mediating sectors in the position of disability as a social category that transformed practices, policies, and culture
