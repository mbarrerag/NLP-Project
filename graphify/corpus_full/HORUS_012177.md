# PROSPECTIVE ANALYSIS AND STRATEGIC ACTION LINES FOR COLOMBIAN AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION IN A POS-COVID-19 ENVIRONMENT

The article develops an exploratory prospective analysis, oriented to the identification of elements of analysis, scenarios and strategic lines of action on Colombian agricultural production in a post-COVID-19 environment. The method employed developed literature review, structural analysis for the identification of scenarios and the enunciation of lines of action. The findings suggest that the demand crisis is the most influential element, and that possible transformations of the sector would have to undergo changes in the use of agricultural territories, institutional subordination and a novel food security perspective
