# Biology and ecology of lionfish (Pterois volitans) in the Corales de Deep National Park, Colombian Caribbean

ABSTRACT In the mesophotic environment, 237 specimens of Pterois were observed, mostly in the upper mesophotic zone. Males were larger than females with differentiated growth models. 16 items of prey were identified, with fish Acanthuridae and Monacanthidae being the most important. The average of +948;13C was -17.08 ± 0.36 +8240; and that of +948;15N was 8.68 ± 0.46 +8240; no gender differences were detected. The species occupies a less extensive isotopic niche than in the shallow sectors, has a transfer between sexes and specialist trophic habits (Levin = 0.22). In females the condition factor (FC), the gonadosomatic index (IGS) and the hepatosomatic index (IHS) increased with the gonadal development reaching peak in active spawning phase, and decreasing in regression
