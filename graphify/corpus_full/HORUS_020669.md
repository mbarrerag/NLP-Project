# Evaluation of inelastic behaviour in reinforced concrete elements with short steel fibres submitted to bending: application of fibre method

This study assesses the inelastic behaviour of reinforced concrete with short steel fibres under conditions of flexion, with the aim of understanding its behavior and ability to dissipate energy. A constituent model was developed to simulate the inelastic mechanical response of the material under axial loads, supplemented by a moment-curvature analysis using a layered discretization approach in cross-sections. The methodology included an experimental validation of theoretical models by testing of concrete elements subjected to pure flexion, allowing direct comparison between theoretical and experimental results. The findings indicate that the fibre method significantly improves the ability to model the non-linear behavior of reinforced concrete with fibers, offering more precise predictions compared to conventional methods. A notable increase in the ductility and the absorption of energy of the material was observed, attributed to the inclusion of steel fibres. These results highlight the importance of considering the mechanical and geometric properties of fibers in the design of reinforced concrete mixtures to optimize their structural performance . conventional methods.
