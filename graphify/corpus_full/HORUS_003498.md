# Social imaginations about public space: Case study of the territory Alto Fucha

This research aims to identify the social imaginarys built in the territory of the Alto Fucha and the way in which these are materialized on the public space and structure the ways of living. The neighborhoods of the study area were formed through an informal process of urbanization in a territory with privileged environmental and aesthetic characteristics, so the permanence of the inhabitants has been threatened through different legal processes associated with different dynamics of real estate speculation. These events have detonated in the community different community processes: political, cultural and social, many of them materialized in the public space. These actions are structured and become meaning from a network of meanings, which are socially constructed, defined as social imaginary. The methodological proposal of a phenomenological approach, the territory as a source of life. . The objective of this research is to identify the social practice: the territory for conservation, the territory as a source of life. are socially constructed, defined as social imaginations. The methodological proposal of a phenomenological approach proposals to identify the imaginations, based on the representations constructed by the inhabitants and that materialize in different interventions on the public space. As a result, 3 social imagery are exposed that give means to social practice. The territory for conservation, The Territory for the market, The territorial as a source of life
