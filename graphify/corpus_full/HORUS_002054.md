# Risk assessment in enterprises according to ISO 31000:2018. Case study: beneficiaries of the Fund Emprender program – SENA

The study aimed to analyze the impact of the application of the methodology of the standard ISO 31000:2018 in the risk assessment in a subset of enterprises beneficiaries of the program Fund Emprender – SENA. A methodological approach was adopted based on the multiple case study, which included eight enterprises as units of analysis and the implementation of the method of Expert judgment. The methodological development was structured in three phases, combining qualitative and quantitative techniques oriented to the fulfilment of specific objectives. In the first phase, 19 risks were identified, based on the semi-structured interviews and the analysis between cases. These risks were classified according to their impact in the indicators of the condoning of the program. Subsequently, through the opinion of experts and articulating the results of the first phase, the underlying causes of these risks, grouped into four categories: limitations in the capacities of the entrepreneurs, weaknesses in the support staff, structural failures of the program and barriers of the entrepreneurial ecosystem were identified.
