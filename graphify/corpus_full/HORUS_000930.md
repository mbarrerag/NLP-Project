# New records of Pucciniales (Fungi) and rust-like hosts on the Poaceae family in Colombia

To know the fungi belonging to this Order, it is estimated that only 18% of the rusts that are present in the national territory have been registered. The objective of this research was the study of new registers of rusts and parasitic hostes within the family Poaceae in Colombia. Samples deposited in the Mycological Museum of the National University of Colombia – Headquarters Medellín (MMUNM) were studied and the morphological characteristics were described by conventional optical microscopy. Four species of rusts belonging to the families Pucciniaceae and Pucciniastraceae (Puccinia dignifica, Pucciniastrum circaeaeaeae, Uredo chryphyllica and Uromyces sporobolica) were recorded for Colombia. Four species of rusts belonging to the families Onagraceae, Poaceae and Sapotaceae were collected in the departments of Chocó, Cundina, Nariño, Putumayo and Santander.
