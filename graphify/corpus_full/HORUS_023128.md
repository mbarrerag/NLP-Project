ABSTRACT Background: The presence of canines with different degrees of malposition is a common problem in orthodontics. The advance in the technology of low rigidity alloys has generated many clinicians to decide for the use of smooth arches from early stages of treatment. However, the three-dimensional and continuous loads are unknown and occasionally can lead to unwanted effects. To evaluate three-dimensionally and continuously the forces and moments consequent to the alignment and leveling of a maxillary canine with different degrees of angulation, rotation and inclination. Experimental study. The experimental model simulated 8 malocclusions of a canine, with different degrees of angulation, rotation and inclination.
