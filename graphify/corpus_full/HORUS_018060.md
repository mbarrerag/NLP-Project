# BUILDING MANAGEMENT FOR A DIGITAL ERA. TECHNOLOGY, TRANSFORMATION AND COOPERATION AS A CHALLENGE OF PEDAGOLOGICAL EXERCISE IN DESIGN MANAGEMENT AND BUILDING

Creativity, the capacity for development and innovation of the human being is unlimited and even if barriers can emerge, will always be able to establish mechanisms to respond to a constant need for transformation or adaptation to the challenges that the same global dynamic imposes as the years go by. We have entered an era of accelerated changes and constant new ones that, despite our conditions and geographical limitations of the region, the penetration of the technology and its multiple advances have impacted very quickly all those training and productive development scenarios in which our culture is supported to respond to the needs of particular professionalization. Although, our innovation and technological development indices local, both academic and union, have been incipient, the applications, management models and developments created in other countries are a constant of permanent adoption and recently have become a dynamic to gain ground in the whole of the value chain and, moreover, a determined aspect of the construction industry, where we have had a large backwardness of the construction industry
