# ASSOCIATION OF TWO SNPS WITH THE NATURAL PROLIFFICITY OF THE COLOMBIAN HAIR OIL IN THE BMPR-1B GENE

The Colombian hair sheep (OPC) is a breed with good adaptation to tropical conditions. The size of litter (TC) in the sheep is related to the rate of ovulation, the number of fertilized eggs and the embryo survival. The STC is determined by genetic and environmental effects. Among the genetic factors the 1B receptor of the bone morphogenic protein (BMPRR1B) is recognized as the 1B receptor of the bone morphogenic protein (BMPR1B) and within this the polymorphism called FecB (Boorola) and C864T. To associate the natural prolificity in the biotypes OPC Ethiopia and Sudan with the polymorphism FecB and C864T of the BMPR1B gene.
