# Teaching resilience in times of pandemic: Technological adaptations, didactic improvements and revolutionary methodologies

The teaching processes of the teachers in the faculty of sciences of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters Bogotá, during the period of isolation caused by the pandemic of the COVID-19, were affected by the disruption of the traditional methodologies in the classroom. This situation forced the transition towards digitalization of information and communication in the academic field. From the perspective of each teacher, the teaching processes had to be redirected in an optimistic and constructive way in this adaptive process. In the present work, the strategies used by the teachers, which sought to improve their teaching and learning processes in the classroom, were identified and analyzed, seeking to overcome the difficulties that became evident in this period. For this, a survey was designed and applied in which the teachers reported their experiences during and after the pandemic, highlighting the learning, the challenges and the cases of success in the teaching work, was carried out in the context of the university. This situation requires a transition towards the digitization of information and communication in the academic branch. From each teacher's perspective, teaching processes had to be redirected optimally and constructively in this adaptive process. This study identifies and analyses the strategy employed by teachers to enhance their teaching and learning processes in the classroom, importing to overcome the challenge of the challenger during this period.
