# SYNTHESIS OF A SCHIFF BASE TO FROM DOPAMINE AND CINAMALDEHYD

In this research work, evidence of the synthesis of a Schiff base is reported, from dopamine and cinnamaldehyde, obtained by wet condensation. The compound was analyzed by infrared spectroscopy and visible ultraviolet spectroscopy and the experimental results were compared with those obtained by the computational method of Functional Density Theory (FMD). The analyses confirmed the chemical species: 4-(2-{[(1E,2E)-3-phenylprop-2-en-1-iliden]amino}ethyl)benzeno-1,2-diol
