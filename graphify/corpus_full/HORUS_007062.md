# Feasibility of the use of renewable energies in San Francisco, Cundinamarca under the National Energy Transition Strategy

The final master's work focuses on an analysis of the viability of the use of renewable energies in the municipality of San Francisco, Cundinamarca. The problems of intermittance, the high costs of the local electricity network, and the use of wood or electric energy to cook as the main reasons for a high percentage of the rural population of the municipality (67%) lack of a renewable and clean technological option for cooking food. Two participatory workshops were held that revealed the energy priorities of some of the inhabitants. Solutions are proposed, including solar panels, biomass and micro hydropower, options that were evaluated. The inclusion of topics such as diversification of energy sources, citizen participation and inclusion of renewable projects in the Municipal Development Plan 2024- 2027 are indicators of the participation of the community that are reflected in the proposals given to the mayor from the development of this work.
