# Prevalence and clinical characteristics of postural obstructive apnea patients attended by the Colombian Vigilian Dream Foundation in Bogotá over a 6-month period between 2022 and 2023

To determine the prevalence and clinical and polygraphic characteristics of patients with postural apnea wlin treated at the Fundación Suedón Vigilia Colombiana, in Bogotá. Observational descriptive prospective study of patients over 18 years of age who, on suspicion of sleep apnea, were performed a respiratory polygraphy in a 6-month period between 2022 and 2023. For each patient, polygraphic variables were recorded in addition to demographic, clinical data and the scales of Epworth, STOP-BANG, FOSQ-10 and Friedman were applied. A total of 255 polygraphic studies were performed in different patients. 223 patients had sleep apnea, of which 43% met postural apnea criteria. The respiratory events of patients with postural apnea presented a lower IAH (p < 0.001) and a lower minimum recorded saturation (p=0.039). In addition, they had less arterial hypertension (p=0.049) and a lower body mass index (p=0.002) compared to patients without postural apnea. ER obtained for each patient. Epworth, STOP-BANG, FOSQ-10 and Friedman scales were also applied. A total of 255 polygraphic records were given in different patients who met criteria for the study. 223 patients had OSA, and 43% of these met criteria for positive apnea. Respiratory events in patients with positive patients had a lower AHI (p< 0.001), and a higher NADI (p=0.039). Additionally, they had less hypertension (p=0.049) and a lower BMI (p < 0.001) than patients with non-relative position apnea.
