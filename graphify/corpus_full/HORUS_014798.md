# WORK 8. 8 ARTISTS WORKS

The eighth edition of the OBRA collection is dedicated to the artistic creation of 8 teachers of the Faculty of Architecture of the National University of Colombia. In the preamble it is a question of answering the question "Why art?", making a historical tour of the concept to conclude in the idea of art as experience. The central chapter dedicates a section to each of the artists Edith Arbeláez, Natalia Echeverri, Véronique Mondéjar, Alejandro Castaño, Álvaro Correa, Federico Londoño, Juan Luis Mesa and Luis Fernando Escobar analyzing 8 works of each one in a critical way and within the universal, national and local context. Then, an interview is presented that the author does to the artists and then includes 8 concepts about art made by a diverse set of people who have some affinity with art
