# Characterization of respiratory system lesions, spatial distribution and factors associated with pneumonic patterns in three-week cavia porcellus in production systems in the municipality of Pasto

The study aimed to characterize macroscopic and microscopically corrigient lesions and pneumonic patterns of the respiratory system of the cuy in production systems in the Municipality of Pasto - Colombia, as well as to establish possible risk factors associated with the presentation of pneumonias in the cuy. For this, a study of transverse cut-off of 3-week-old gazeps from a representative sample of the cuycultural population was carried out. Necropsy and histopathological evaluation of the nasal cavity, upper respiratory tract and lung were performed using the technique of inclusion of paraffin and coloration of Hematoxyline and Eosina. Additionally, a tool for collecting information was used in each of the farms from which the animals came to determine demographic, productive and health factors. In the animals included in the study, lesions in the nasal cavity and respiratory pathways were established in a percentage less than 5%.
