# 50 YEARS, 15 RESEARCHS, 1 IDEA: PUBLIC SPACE IN URBAN STUDIES

With the opportunity to review in 5 decades the School of Urban Planning Regional, and in the culmination of at least 400 research, and a good percentage of them dedicated to investigating the derivatives of a concept: the PUBLIC SPACE in URBAN STUDIES. From the visibility of an important group of works, attempts are made to anticipate the fields of interest in this topic.
