# “Don’t name me so bad a man, who is more tyrant than president”: corrupt practices and behaviors during the visit to the president of the Audiencia de Santafé Don Antonio González, 1597

The main objective of this thesis is to analyze the practices of government during the administration of President Antonio González (1589-1597). To address this problem, several sources were consulted, mainly the visit made to that minister after the completion of his period of government. The analysis focuses on the "corrupt" actions that were woven throughout the period of President González. In this space it is possible to write a story that gives account of the way in which the formation of sides was made during the visit, the types of corrupt actions that were committed and the use of sexuality to discredit the work of President Antonio González.
