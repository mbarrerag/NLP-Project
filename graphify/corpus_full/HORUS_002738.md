# MICROBIOMA OF MALARIA VECTORS IN LATICIA, AMAZONS, REVELA HIGH FREQUENCY OF ENDOSIMBIONTS AND BACTERIES WITH ANTI-PARISITARY BIOTECHNOLOGY POOTENTIAL

The department of the Amazonas stands out as one of the main malaria centers in Colombia, with 5% to 10% of the total recorded cases.As strategies for its control have traditionally been employed physical and chemical methods. However, the microbiome of the anofelins constitutes a promising source for the biological control by containing bacteria with the potential to inhibit the development of the parasite, alter the immune and/or reproductive system of the vectors, thus interrupting the transmission cycle. In this work was carried out the exploration of the total bacterial community present in specimens of Anopheles darlingi and An. triannillatus, considering the breeding sites, immature and adult states from San Pedro de los Lagos (Leticia, Amazonas). The results of bacterial composition obtained by sequencing of new generation of amplicones of the ARNr 16S gene in the platform Illumina have revealed greater richness of bacterial genders in the sample from breeding water (Leticia, Amazonas).
