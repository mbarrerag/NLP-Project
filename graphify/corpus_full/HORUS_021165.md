# Evaluation of remote and environmental sensor products for monitoring runoff water resources in urban areas

In the last few years the problems associated with the management of the rainwater in the urban areas have been increased. Due to the climate change and the little planning problems arise like urban floods, the contamination of bodies of water and erosion. Some research indicates that if we do not change the way we design our cities, these problems degrade the quantity and quality of resources and natural environments in the cities (CIRIA, 2015). In order to be able to carry out an effective management of the rainwater and avoid the problems associated with it, it is necessary to have the necessary information of the quality and quantity of the runoff to make informed decisions. The obtaining of hydrologic, hydraulic, water quality information, topographical, among others, requires high investments of money making the solution of the problem difficult exposed to it. In addition, the countries in the way of development, like Colombia, prioritize the economic inversion in other type of needs other than the management of the rainwater. It is therefore the cities that normally allow the monitoring of the environmental waters in cities as Bogota is necessary to perform the information. The purpose of this methodological proposal is to identify and choose types of urban drainage systems (SUDS), based on the data collected in the first phase. The selection of areas for the implementation of SUDS is to support the management of water and flood flows produced by the urban runoff of medium intensity. For the development of this phase, a bibliographic review of SUDS types of water resources will be carried out, necessary conditions for its implementation and an analysis of suitability for the location and selection of these systems. Finally, the third phase is based on the generation of a methodological proposal for the location of urban runoff monitoring stations based on weighted overlay overlay using Geographic Information Systems (GIS) tools.
