# Global public debt and ensuring the applicability of Economic, Social and Cultural Rights in Colombia.

This research revolves around the research question: How could the obligations assumed by Colombia in terms of public debt and guarantee of economic, social and cultural rights be harmonized?So, it is intended to verify and evaluate the guarantee of the applicability of these rights to the world public debt, taking as a hypothesis that the States have acquired some of their global public debts in times of political, economic and/or social crises.Apart from these circumstances the balance of the commercial relationship between the different debtor states and the different states or financial institutions that have obtained them.In view of such particularities of crisis, it is necessary to consider that the cancellation and/or relaxation of the world public debt is the most effective tool to restore that negotiating balance and the harmonization of the obligations assumed by Colombia in terms of the world public debt and the guarantee of the DESC. Thus, a historical analysis will certainly allow to identify that debts must be cancelled and/or reduced, in reason of the abusive historical and economic circumstances in which they were acquired. In the light of the above, in sum, this research aims to formulate proposals that allow the harmonization of the obligations in the field of public debt and in the area of guarantee of the ESCR in a specific context: Colombia. To this end, an analysis will be carried out with a view to human rights, which will allow us to overcome the economic perspectives, promoted by ideas of a neoliberal nature, that are harmful to rights. Additionally, the historical circumstances in which Colombia's different debts were acquired today will be analyzed. Such an input will allow us to identify whether or not such obligations of a credit nature were acquired in historical circumstances that allow us to think about any possibility of forgiveness and/or reduction of these rights; it will also allow us to identify possible periods of abuse with high interest rates
