# Teaching proposal to help counteract academic frustrations of a group of adult English as a foreign language students

This qualitative study seeks to present a non-parametric didactic tool that can help Colombian adults between 18 and 65 years of age in their search to face academic frustrations at an A1 level (according to the European Common Framework) when learning English as a foreign language. In fact, these frustrations were identified and analyzed using the “Latent Approach”, revealing how students’ classes were carried out and what their experiences were. Underlying meanings were discovered that highlighted, first, the desire for an innovative experience in which information can be provided in a unique way in English and with which students are understood. Secondly, the experience of being understood is that in which students can adapt to predictable and unpredictable scenarios while responding with claims, denials and forms of question.
