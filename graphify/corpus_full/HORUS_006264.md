# Calcium dynamics in soils with high magnesium saturation dedicated to sugarcane production in the north of Valle del Cauca.

In the geographical valley of the Cauca river, about 117,000 hectares are affected by the excess magnesium in the soil, in this sense, its traditional management follows the principle similar to the management of sodium: increase the calcium levels. With the aim of studying the dynamics of calcium in soils with high magnesium saturation dedicated to the production of sugar cane located in the north of the Cauca Valley, three experiments were carried out in the Risaralda Ingenio: two in field (plants and socas) under an experimental design of Complete Blocks to the Azar (BCAA) and one under controlled conditions (containers) in a design Completely to the Azar (CAA). Seven treatments were applied in 5 repetitions for a total of 35 experimental units. The effect of two amendments (soil and calcium carbonate) and three doses corresponding to the application of soil, 3; and 4.5 tons per hectare were considered as growth variables, tons of cane per hectare (TCH), accumulation of sugar per hectare (TAH), dry matter (g), nutrient content in the soil and the foliar tissue, and physical parameters associated to the soil (soil) and to the soil. sugarcane production located in the northern part of Valle del Cauca, three experiences were developed at the Ingenio Risaralda S.A: two in the field (templates and socas) under a Randomized Complete Block experimental design (BCAA) and one under controlled conditions (containers) in a Complete Randomized design (CAA). Seven treatments were applied in 5 replications for a total of 35 experimental units.
