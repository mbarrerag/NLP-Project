# ETHICAL, CAUSES AND CONSEQUENCES OF BURNOUT SYNDROME IN HEALTH PERSONNEL IN COVID-19 TIMES

This is a bibliometric review on burnout syndrome that affected health professionals at Covid-19 times, the ethical conflicts that precede and happen, and the strategies to combat it. Methodology/focus: a bibliographic review was carried out in which review articles, cross-sectional studies, metaanalysis and qualitative studies were systematically identified from around the world. Once the selection filters were applied, the results were analyzed with the deliberative method of Diego Gracia. four main subtopics were identified, incidence of burnout during the Covid-19 pandemic, ethical stressors that favor burnout syndrome, consequences of this syndrome and strategies to prevent and combat it. Each subtopic is described from the individual, institutional and social areas. Discussion/conclusions: it is argued with Diego Gracia's theory and it presents a hierarchy of ethical principles involved in burnout syndrome, consequences of this syndrome and strategies to prevent it from being a deep bioetic, in which the individual areas of economic recognition, equity, job security and the need to take care, good health care
