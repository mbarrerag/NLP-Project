# DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR SMALL PRODUCERS

This document seeks to highlight the main difficulties that small producers present in achieving their economic and social objectives, recognizing the potential that these productions have. To identify the opportunities that can be developed from the intervention of the problems through different strategies. Checkland's soft systems methodology is used for the analysis of literature and trends and to find priorities for making proposals. The problems of small producers and their families are derived mainly from the small size of the properties and the difficulties that they have to achieve economies of scale or access to markets. Four key strategies are highlighted, in which cooperation and capacity integration can try to mitigate the impacts of these difficulties
