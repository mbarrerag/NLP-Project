# Optimization of the reactive power dispatch in solar autogenerators based on the 2018 CREG 015 resolution

The entry into force of the resolution CREG 015 of 2018 has led to the implementation of control actions on the consumption and export of reactive energy to the end users who have integrated solar autogenerators as a source of active power generation for their installations. This work seeks to provide a solution to these end users so that they can make use of the characteristics of solar investors that allow the generation of reactive power. Two control algorithms based on the mathematical modelling carried out in this work are developed, which are subsequently simulated to evaluate their behavior with data taken from a 1.8MWp plant, in which the algorithms are subsequently implemented over 30 days. With the results obtained, a 99.4% reduction in the excess of inductive reactive energy consumption and the 94.9% in the magnitude of reactive power generation to end uses what has integrated from the source, The entry into force of CREG reused 015 of 2018 has led to actions to control consumption and implementation export of reactive energy to end uses of the source, which has been integrated into the structure of the management.
