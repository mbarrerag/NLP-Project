# Semantic segmentation model of satellite images based on convolutional neural networks for classification of land cover in moors

The eco-friendly environment of the Union is a key to the water regulation and biodiversity, but it faces significant threats due to climate change and human activities. The lack of clear delimitation and continuous monitoring systems hinder its protection, highlighting the need for coverage classification techniques with high thematic accuracy and short processing times. In this research it evaluated two architectures of convolutional neural networks (CNN), U-Net++ and DeepLabV3 in its capacity to perform semantic segmentation of satellite images to classify coverages of the Tota-Bijagal-Mamapacha mooring complex (TBM), located in Boyacá and Cundinamarca.
