# Design of a classroom project that helps to enhance the interpretation and resolution of problems in agricultural contexts in the random component and data systems

This research is carried out from the analysis of experience that allows to confront the teaching practice and the rethinking of the strategies used in the classroom, obtaining a transformation in the shortcomings presented in the learning of the students, for this reason this work shows the design and implementation of a class project to enhance and improve the understanding of the measures of central tendency (MTC) in agricultural environments for students of the 10th degree in the Educational Institution Julio Mejía Vélez de Pie de Pató, Alto Baudó - Chocó. The theoretical reference is the significant critical learning of Marco Antonio Moreira and the methodology of pedagogical action "Reflexion of the teacher on his practice", with four phases: diagnosis, design, implementation and evaluation-reflexion. The use of non-traditional strategies to improve the teaching-learning processes and to transversalize mathematics in other disciplines is used.
