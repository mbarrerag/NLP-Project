# Subordination or autonomy: The experience of two Protestant missionaries between the Matse and Curipac in the Amazon, 1945-1995

to offer a reading of the subordinate conditions or autonomy of the work of two Protestant missionaries, Harriet Fields and Sophie Müller, belonging to two different religious organizations: the Summer Linguistic Institute (ILV) and the New Tribes Mission (MNT), between the Matsé and Curipaco in the Amazon. the work follows archival materials, printed texts of the Missionaries and literature on them to detail the process of their work. Originality: the article is a contribution to the historiography of Protestantism in the Amazonia since it specifically deals with the work of the Missionaries leaving aside the conventional androcentric approach. the cases described allow to conclude that the Protestant missionary woman plays an important role in evangelization and that her performance is accepted without major inconvenience as field workers; but at the managerial level her participation is minimal and still subordinate to the male decision-making control that prevails in religious entities
