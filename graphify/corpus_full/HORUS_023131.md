# EQUIPMENT HAND CONDITIONS IN A COLOMBIA BENEFIT PLANT

The conditions of handling of equidae subjected to slaughter can generate physical, physiological and behavioral changes, with consequences on the welfare and products of that industry. At the national level there are few studies related to the topic, preventing the visibility of the problem and establishing improvement plans. Describe the conditions of handling in equidae subjected to slaughter in a profit plant in Colombia. The individual and collective management conditions were analyzed in 160 individuals during the stay in pre-slaughter pens. An evaluation format was used to record management indicators related to the wellbeing of equidae.
