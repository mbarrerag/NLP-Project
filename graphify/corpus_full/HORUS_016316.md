# THE HIGHER INTEREST OF THE CHILD.

The best interests of the child are a principle that goes through all legal relationships between children and adolescents, but little has been done to address their conceptualization and application. In this paper, these aspects will be worked on in terms of the state of art about the best interests of the child, exploring its role in both history and law, making a conceptual approach to it and problematizing it through the main decisions of the Constitutional Court that incorporate it
