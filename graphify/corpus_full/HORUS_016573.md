# Computer implementation of spatial process models for social media analysis.

The statistical modeling of networks allows identifying their probability distribution, imputing missing data and making predictions about the formation of links. Latent models approach modeling from a marginal perspective, incorporate non-conditional dependencies through random effects. A particular case of latent models is the model based on completely Bayesian spatial processes that solves the problems of overadjustment of the latent space model of distance. In this document, the computational implementation of the model is carried out and a study of its adjustment benefits and prediction benefits is carried out through synthetic and real networks. The model has good qualities for replication of statistics observed in the network and estimation of the latent surface. However, predictive power, measured through the area under the curve (AUC by its acronyms) does not exceed the value of 0.7. An alternative way of adjusting the model is also presented using the case-control algorithm. The model based on the estimated log-verosimilitude has a good quality of goodness of adjustment
