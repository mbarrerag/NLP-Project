# TEMPORARY CHANGES (1995-2011) IN THE MACROALGAL FLORA IN TWO OCEAN AIR AIR CARIBBEAN

The marine macroalgae are organisms at the base of the trophic chain, which respond quickly to environmental changes. In reef environments, an increase in algal coverage has been associated with the deterioration of the ecosystem, in particular with the proliferation of grasses formed from small filamentary algae. The Serrana and Roncador cay islands are two oceanic atolls that are part of the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina. Their location is away from direct anthropic impacts such as industries, discharges of black water or of agricultural origin.The first study on the algal flora of these two atolls was developed in 1995.In 2011, an expedition was carried out to these cayos where macroalga were collected, and in 2016 an additional collection was made in Serrana.
