# FAMILY CAREER OF PERSON WITH PALIATIVE CARE: IMPACT ON LIFE QUALITY

Taking on the role of family caregiver of a person with cancer in palliative care (CP) is a work that affects the quality of life of the person exercising it. Factors such as age, patient functionality and care dedication in daily hours, influence the level of quality of life of the caregiver. to describe influential factors in the quality of life of caregivers of people with cancer resident in Colombia. Transversal study with 208 family caregivers of people with cancer resident in Medellin Colombia. Characteristics and correlations of the quality of life measured through the instrument quality of life in diseases threatening the life-career (QOLLTI-F). 79% of the caregivers were women, 36% with secondary education degree, 47% married, 53% of household occupation, 90% of the care taken from the time of diagnosis, the care status of the patient, the care status of the patient, the care was observed 16 to 18 hours daily care
