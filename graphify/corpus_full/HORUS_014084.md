# Knowledge network in political education and community participation

The project Knowledge Network in political education and community participation has as main objective to promote collaboration and networking between civil society agents and the National University of Colombia that strengthen the production of knowledge and research/creation, through the systematization of experiences of co-construction and community participation in political education in Medellín, Manizales and Bogotá, considered relevant by the research team as the most representative and relevant, given the work that has been advanced from its research groups in the field with several of them over the last years.In this objective, three main results have been proposed.In the first place, an analysis document will be socialized to relevant governmental bodies in each region, highlighting the implications for political participation and collaboration between actors in the construction of public policies.Second, the active participation of the research semi-workers will be promoted in this system.In this, the results will be analyzed and the research system will be shared by relevant government agencies in each region, highlighting the implications for political participation and collaboration between actors in public policy. In summary, the project will generate relevant knowledge based on the systematization of experiences, promote the participation of research seedbeds and strengthen collaboration between the UNAL headquarters. This will contribute to the strengthening of the National University's School of Thought and will lay the foundation for the creation of a National Network in Political Education and its articulation with government agencies. The activities proposed will allow to achieve the expected results and generate impact in the field of political education and community participation
