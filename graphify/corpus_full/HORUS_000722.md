# THE PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRY AND ITS DEVELOPMENT STRATEGY IN COLOMBIA

This book, which is the result of a rigorous and multidisciplinary effort, offers a detailed analysis of the evolution of the sector, the regulatory challenges, the market dynamics and the opportunities that are presented both locally and internationally.From a historical review to a diagnosis of the present and forward-looking proposals, this document is an invaluable tool to understand the Colombian pharmaceutical industry and to design strategies that strengthen its growth
