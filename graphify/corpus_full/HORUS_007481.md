# Sustainability of agrarian systems in peasant territories of Valle del Cauca: Case study in the Cerro Rico, Guadalajara de Buga

The agricultural model prevailing in the world is based on an intensive and extractivist production which contributes to the current global systemic crisis, which leads to degradation of the common goods (water bodies, soils and biodiversity), ignores the particularities of the rural territories, and ignores the peasant culture putting at risk its existence.The loss of peasant territories increases the crisis, since these spaces can pass to monoculture type agriculture and to large agribusiness, which increases the concentration of land tenure and inequality, with negative effects on the environment, the economy and the culture.This research proposal seeks to understand and mitigate this crisis of the peasant territories, as a contribution from agroecology and based on the concepts of sustainability, systemic vision and peasant territories.This research has as general objective the assessment of the sustainability of the agricultural systems in the Valle del Cauca as a contribution to agroecology, through a case study in the neighborhood Cerro Rico, Guadalajara de Buga.The research assumes that agricultural production is the main economic activity of the rural world and depends on the state of the environment, but can also affect it, according to its own life. The main agricultural systems of the peasant territory of ASTRACAVA were characterized by the participation of the peasant sector, i.e. coffee, citrus and vegetables, in the ecological, agrarian, economic and social dimensions through a rapid rural diagnosis; this showed that the common goods are preserved with some stability, supported by a strong social organization based on the agrarian committees of ASTRACAVA and the communal action board (JAC).The sustainability of the agrarian systems of the territory was then evaluated using the indicators selected with the agrarian committees in the dimensions mentioned, which gave an average result 3.0/5,0, using a scale parametrized with the Likert method, which shows a considerable extent to improve the sustainability of the territory. Subsequently, the perception of the concept of sustainability by the territorial actors with the construction of the historical memory, semi-structured interviews and surveys on the established indicators was identified in order to improve the community perception; while there were some differences in the economic dimension, the perception of the community is not far from the evaluation carried out by the researcher using the indicators. Finally, and in a participatory way, guidelines were identified to improve the economic and economic indicators.
