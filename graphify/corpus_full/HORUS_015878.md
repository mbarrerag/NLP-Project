# PROMOTION OF MENTAL HEALTH IN SCHOOLS DURING THE PANDEMIA COVID-19.

The WHO has declared in March 2020 the IVOD-19, as pandemic. This biological disaster forced the countries of the world to take measures to prevent and control their contagion. The disease has generated bio-psycho-social consequences given by the imposed measures of biosecurity and control. There is a significant negative impact on the mental health and well-being of the general population and of the school children. Description of the problem or need: During the pandemic by IVOD-19 people have had to make many adaptations to their daily life due to the confinement and social distance. The entrance to the schools has been suspended, the pedagogical relationship has been transformed in an abrupt way, it is passed from person to person, to use virtual technological tools. The school routines, personal contact with friends, cannot be shared in the classes, nor other leisure and leisure activities. The problems and mental disorders, which with higher prevalence the research has documented during this pandemic are: feeling fear, loneliness, boredom, frustration, worry, besides, presenting disorders of sleep and appetite, deterioration in social interactions, anxiety, stress, stress, stress, stress, stress, mental disorder and addiction to games in line
