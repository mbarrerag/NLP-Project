# Selection of Solanum tuberosum potato genotypes Phureja and Andigenum Group with water stress resistance

The potato is the fifth most important crop in the world and has an important role in food security, especially in developing countries such as Colombia. The morphological characterization is the first step in the description and classification of the germplasm (Smith & Smith, 1989). The understanding of the morphological characters facilitates identification, the selection of desirable attributes, the design of new populations, the transfer of their genes and the resistance to biotic and abiotic factors (Singh, Chaubey, Upadhyay, Jha, & Pandey, 2014).
