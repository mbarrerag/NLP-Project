# Chimera Gesto

The space is dark, you hear a palpitus that spreads between different materials, the sound changes, but the vibration seems to remain the same. It is a vitality that spreads among the bodies, indifferent of the matter that composes them. The forces pass through each skin and surface, makes them sound, move, levitate, fly, escape, escape, unadjusted, stop being fixed and be significantly alive, there resides the chimera. The Quimera is that capable of sustaining the contradiction, the paradox, the relations of alienation and make them live. A chimera is inappresible, inclasificable, the overflow of the binary, is by nature the space for the atopia, the without-lugar, a space for the co-existence of that radically different, its body enables the paradox and contradiction as a vital condition. The Gesto Quimera seeks to expand the relational file, it is an invented practice to expand repertoires of relational actions between bodies, objects and immaterials, it is an accumulation of supports and simultaneous supports, of skins, of porous, of the folds. The Chimera is that capable of sustaining the contradiction, the paradox, the relationship of stability and making them live. A chimera is elusive, unclassifiable, the overflow of the binary, it is by nature the space for atopy, the placeless, a space for the coexistence of that which is radically different, its body capable of being paradox and contradiction as a vital condition.
