# SUSTAINABLE PRACTICES AND FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE IN LATIN AMERICA: AN ANALYSIS OF EVENTING SCRESS

In this study, the relationship between environmental ratings and the financial performance of Latin American companies was investigated. Design/Methodology: Based on a data set that covered 1'708 observations from 372 companies between 2015 and 2020, this study used panel data to analyze the relationship between environmental ratings and asset profitability. According to empirical findings, the impact of current environmental performance in Latin America on the financial performance of companies in the region is limited. Despite this, positive results were observed in Brazil, Mexico and Chile, where the successful integration of environmental practices and financial results is highlighted. Thanks to the analysis of the impact of environmental, social and governance ratings, particularly environmental ratings, on the profitability of Latin American companies, this study contributes to a deeper understanding of the complex relationship between sustainability and financial performance in the region. In addition, it highlights the challenges and opportunities that Latin American companies face at the time of aligning their environmental performance with their profitability. Likewise, it highlights the need to develop strategies and mechanisms more effective to reduce the gap between environmental and financial performance in the Americas.
