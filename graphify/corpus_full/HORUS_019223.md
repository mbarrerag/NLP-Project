# FROM ANGOSTURA TO SEPARATION. 1819-1853: THE STATE-GLYNSE RELATIONS IN THE FIRST REPUBLICIAN YEARS.

Here we will observe how relations between the State and the Catholic Church were delineated, as an institution, from 1819 to 1853, that is, from the Fundamental Law proclaimed in Angostura, the military victories of the emancipating army against the realistic troops, near Bogotá (essentially that of the Pantano de Vargas, on July 25, and that of the Bridge of Boyacá, on August 7,), and the beginning of the religious-political campaign promoted by Francisco de Paula Santander to justify, from the pulpit, the break of the colonial order all this in 1819, until the legal separation of the State and the Church in 1853
