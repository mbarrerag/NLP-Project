# TRANSITIONS IN SET-UP SET-UP SINGLE-FRONT LABOUR REINTEGRO: INTEGRATIVE REVIEW

Breast cancer is one of the most common diseases among women worldwide. Breast cancer treatment often involves surgery, radiation therapy, chemotherapy and/or hormone therapy, treatments that in the long term can generate effects in women. These women who have been treated for breast cancer can face different situations when deciding to return to work, due to others that can be perceived as less productive. Describe the transitions experienced by breast cancer survivors against work reintegration through an integrative review. integrative review of descriptive scope, through the identification of the problem, literature search, data analysis and presentation of results.
