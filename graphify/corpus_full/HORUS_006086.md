# Evaluation strategies for CPR training for schoolchildren: a scoping review

The teaching of cardiopulmonary reanimation (CPR) has gained importance due to the high rates of morbimortality due to cardiorespiratory arrest and the lack of people able to act quickly. The literature suggests initiating CPR teaching in children aged 4 to 12 years in schools, a well received practice. Evaluating the effectiveness of these trainings is crucial to ensure the acquisition and retention of vital skills. This review seeks to strengthen and optimize the CPR teaching processes through an effective evaluation.
