# Risk factors analysis for type 2 diabetes and obesity in the Colombian population aged 40 to 70 years.

Obesity and type 2 diabetes mellitus (DM2) are representative diseases of metabolism alteration. This research compared risk factors to obesity and DM2 in Colombians aged 40-70 years. Preprandial glucose and lipid profile were evaluated to 535 volunteers; they were applied the FINDRISC questionnaire, which includes information on family history, lifestyle, composition and body mass index (BMI); in addition, the atherogenic indices were analyzed. Biochemical and anthropometric measures in the population showed situations of dyslipidemias (54%), hyperglycaemia (23%), alteration of the waist perimeter (73%) and of the BMI (70%). FINDRISC assessed that 57% of the population presented risk between moderate and high to DM2. The genetic risk factor was evaluated by high yield QPCR, 111 volunteers, and included the analysis of 16 PNPs related to DM2 and 58 PNPs with obesity; of these 27 presented OR≥1 (probability ratio) as risk factor to obesity.
