# CONSTRUCTION OF ENEMY AS A STAFF OF ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA

The work focuses on the question about the causes of violence in Colombia since the findings of the TRUTH COMMISSION transitional entity that emerges after the 2016 Peace Agreement as a way of claiming victims and recognizing a history of pain that was silenced for years. In particular, the book exposes the transversality of one of the factors of persistence of violence, this is the notion of enemifgo as justification for the elimination of the adversary
