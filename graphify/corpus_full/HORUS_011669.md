# CHARACTERIZATION IN VITRO OF VERTICILLIUM SPP ISILLINGS.

The early maturity of the potato crop (Solanum tuberosum L.) is caused by fungi of the genus Verticillium, with three reported species: V. dahliae, V. albo-atrum and V. tricorpus. In Colombia, the presence of V. albo-atrum and V. dahliae is reported. The objective of the work was to characterize in-vitro isolations of Verticillium from potato cultures with early maturity symptoms in Cundinamarca producing areas. The pathogen was isolated from affected plants from the municipalities of Guasca, Subachoque, Mosque, Zipaquira and Funza, obtaining isolation from each locality. From monosparous crops at temperatures of 10, 20 and 30°C and pH of 4, 5 and 6 was evaluated from the area under the micelial growth curve (AUMGC) in the middle PDA for 45 days and germination of conidias in the middle agar water for 24 hours. As results we found that the isolateds presented at 30°C were lower values of AGC and 6 and 6 was evaluated with respect to AUM (385). The study was characterized by increased mycelial growth and germination at 10 and 20°C and pH of 5 and 6 and melanized hyphae formation, and a smaller number grew at 30°C and formed microsclerotia
