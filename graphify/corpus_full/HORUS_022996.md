# EXPERIMENTAL CHARACTERIZATION OF JOINT FLOW CONDITIONS IN PLUVIAL SCOPE COLLECTORS

This research experimentally characterizes the mixed flow in the initial stretch of a rain drain for the case of loading entry from the upstream end, due to the increase in flow resulting from the runoff of a precipitation event that exceeds the design. A significant precipitation event may, in many cases, exceed the design measures of some elements of urban drainage as catchment elements and ducts, causing the drainage system to overflow. In the ducts, there is a progressive increase in the flow rate and therefore starts a loading entry from the upstream end that can result in flooding through the streets with associated consequences. Following the criteria of Colombian regulations, the theoretical design of the initial stretch of a rain drain that would be responsible for the evacuation of a flow that would result from the runoff of a hypothetical lot located in Manizales city. Subsequently, an experimental assembly was developed that represented this conduit. The experimental characterization carried out demonstrates that the main factors limiting the capacity, the slope and the relative depth of the water within the pipeline are affected.
