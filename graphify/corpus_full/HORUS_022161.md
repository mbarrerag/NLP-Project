# Saliva as an alternative for drug abuse detection: a systematic review of literature.

The increase in drug abuse is a global problem that negatively affects not only the person who decides to consume but also his/her environment, the great social difficulty that triggers such consumption stimulates the scientific community to investigate methodologies that help to detect these substances. The detection of drugs of saliva abuse is a subject that does not have detailed and consensual information, presenting the need to make known these methodologies in order to provide scientific evidence that can be used in different population areas.
