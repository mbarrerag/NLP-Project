# Impact of the fiscal sustainability strategy on economic activity: analysis for a small and open emerging economy

This thesis assesses the sustainability of public debt in Colombia and the macroeconomic effects of the different fiscal adjustment measures that seek to correct the high level of indebtedness.To carry out this work, econometric models and a model of general dynamic and stochastic equilibrium (DSGE) are used; also, the fiscal advantage is analyzed. First, the sustainability of the debt in Colombia for the period 1985 and 2023 is evaluated by means of empirical tests of sustainability based on the fiscal reaction function (FRF) and using econometric techniques such as a model of changes of the Markov regime (MSM) and a nonlinear self-regressive model of fiscal lags distributed (NARDL, by its acronym in English).These methodologies allowed to identify sustainable and unsustainable tax regimes and asymmetric responses of the Colombian government against the increases in the public debt. The results indicate that there are different tax regimes throughout the period of study with significant asymmetric responses, especially during the periods of crisis and external shocks. In addition, the results confirm the concerns of the fiscal adjustment measures needed in Colombia and the fiscal consolidation are analyzed. The study highlights the importance of a balanced and careful approach to fiscal and monetary policy implementation to ensure the sustainability of public debt, the country’s macroeconomic stability and long-term growth .
