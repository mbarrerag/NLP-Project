# Erigeron bonariensis L.: Characterization of glyphosate resistant accessions in Colombia

The effective management of weeds is essential in modern agriculture. Currently, glyphosate is the most used herbicide in the world, offering effective control of weeds, non-selective in post-emergency when inhibiting the enzyme EPSP syntase in chloroplasts. The recurrent use of the same herbicide mode of action can select biotypes resistant to herbicide and result in loss of efficacy. Erigeron bonariensis L. commonly called venadillo is a native plant of South America that has invaded many ecosystems in the world and has been reported as glyphosate resistant weed in Colombia. E. bonariensis is adapted to many ecological niches, including agroecosystems of essential crops. It has been suspected of resistance to glyphosate in this species since the 1990s and it has confirmed resistance since 2006.
