# Urbanization of rural land in the Municipality of Zipaquirá, 2000-2022

The degree work analyzes the urbanisation of rural land in the municipality of Zipaquirá in the period 2000-2022, giving an approach to the background of the suburbanization process, which reveals that the municipality maintained a compact development in several stages, which was modified to a model of extensive growth, where it has managed to consolidate the typology of bar and low density as the most usual, then reveals the characteristics of rural soil in the study area and a selection of 20 flagship cases is made within the area of study of rural soil eastern Zipaquira, of which a study is found that analyzes and calculates the 3 most important variables within this work, density, intensity and soils enabled for each of the projects. A transformation of the landscape has also been observed, due to the discharges of wastewater and other pollutants in the bodies of water, and degradation of the soils. In short, a balanced and harmonious territorial development has not been achieved with the environmental and ecological conditions of the municipality. .
