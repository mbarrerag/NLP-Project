# Iteration as a management and planning tool in the design of implementation in construction projects

The construction industry is an important actor in the global economy with $10 trillion spent on goods and services each year, employing approximately 7% of the world population of working age, but with an index of productivity below the average of the global economy, due to a recurring problem of delays in the delivery of projects and costs. The aim of this work was to identify and categorize the project management tools, used for the planning of the same, in order to establish parameters of development of a computer tool that allows to optimize the necessary iterations in the execution design. Iteration is an inherent aspect in any design project, which occurs in undefined cycles until the process is completed. If iterations are not anticipated and planned properly, the reprocessing and redesigning can occur at random, resulting in an overload of time and costs.
