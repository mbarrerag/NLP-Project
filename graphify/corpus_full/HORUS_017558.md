# Detection and classification of electrical faults in electrical power distribution systems by using continuous wavelet transform and infinite support mother functions.

The analysis of electrical failures in Power Electrical Systems has seen a significant advance in the implementation of methodologies based on Digital Signal Processing, such as the analysis in time and frequency. In this work, a methodology is proposed to use filters with Infinite Impulse Response as Mother Functions to compute the Transformed Wavelet Continuous by evaluating the concept of digital signal resampling. This methodology is evaluated by comparing it with the methodology most commonly reported in the literature (Transformed Wavelet Discreta with Daubechies 4 Mother Function) and research is undertaken on the effects of the most relevant parameters inherent to the method and signals. The implementation of the code is carried out in the software of MATLAB and the results are validated by comparing the spectral energy associated with the Wavelet coefficients obtained. On the other hand, it is proposed to evaluate the stages of detection and classification of electrical failures in electrical energy distribution systems using a series of simulated signals in the ATP/EMTP Software and artificial neural networks. The results obtained validate the proposed methodology in addition to present a notorious improvement in the detection rate and classification of electrical failures in electrical energy distribution systems
