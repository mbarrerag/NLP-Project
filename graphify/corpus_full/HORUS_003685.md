# INTERACTIVE APPLICATION FOR THE LEARNING AND ANALYSIS OF THE GRAPHIC OF EVENTS IN CONFIDIBILITY

The construction of event graphics in reliability is essential to visualize the distribution of fault times and censorship, allowing to identify patterns and evaluate the reliability of the system. This helps in making decisions about maintenance, resource allocation and planning, optimizing performance and industrial profitability. This work presents an interactive application in R Shiny designed to facilitate the understanding of these graphics, useful both for the training of students in statistics and for decision-making in various areas. The application uses databases and functions of the R SMRD package
