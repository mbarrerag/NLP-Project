# DEPARTMENTAL PLAN FOR PLASTIC, VISUAL AND ARTSANIAN ARTS 2023-2035

To think of the plastic arts for many people of the common, is to have a romantic posture of the artist creator in his workshop, illuminated and inspired by his own reflections, for the elaboration of the work of art. However, this is not entirely true, since the current art is elaborated with others, in places and territories never thought and through multiple artistic languages that, when articulated, produce paintings, murals, interventions in public space, modeled, among others
