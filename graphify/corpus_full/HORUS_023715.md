# FLOWING FUNDS MADE BY LIGHT AND POINTIALITIES OF PHOTOSELLECTIVE BADNES AND SPECTER SHOES IN FLORICULTURE

The floriculture is an important sector in Colombia, not only because of its capacity to generate employment and rural development, but because it is an agro-industry that has solved technological lags in terms of the use of innovative alternatives. Based on this, and understanding that for this sector one of the most important and most studied topics has been the light and processes that governs, the objective of this chapter was to address the theoretical foundations associated to the plant development following the innovative strategies of manipulation of the luminous environment and the use of emerging technologies in horticulture. It includes issues of flowering: photoperiod, hormonal regulation and theories of control of flowering, with the recent discovery of the proteins ft, which revives the concept of the floricultor, in the same way as the characteristics and functions of the photoreceptors. Likewise, such data on the use of the photoselective meshes in greenhouse crops are presented, and the higher the 30 % of the mesh, the higher the color is maintained as a theoretical and practical approximation of the potential use for the flower sector of an emerging technology: the spectral footprints
