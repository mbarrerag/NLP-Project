# Study on electron paramagnetic resonance and heat capacity of Zn0.95Cr0.05O calcined at three different temperatures

Results of the study using electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR) of Zn 0 . 95 Cr 0 . 05 O calcined at 773 K, 823 K, and 873 K are presented. The EPR results show that for samples calcined at 873 K, there is a substitution of zinc ions by chromium ions within the crystal structure, whereas for samples calcined at 773 K and 823 K, this substitution cannot be confirmed. The symmetry of the sites where Cr ions are located after substitution is axial with rhombic distortion. The interaction between chromium ions for samples calcined at 873 K is of an antiferromagnetic nature. The heat capacity study allowed the estimation of the contribution of Cr to the heat capacity of ZnO, finding Debye temperatures consistent with those reported in the literature
