# FRACTIONAL MULTICOMPLEX POLYNOMIALS[[C860 ]]PAOLA - DIPLOID POTATO VARIETY

In this paper we will investigate fractional analytic properties of multi-complex valued polynomials defined on BCn (the space of multicomplex numbers refers to the space generated over the reals by n commuting imaginary units) using a modification of the Cauchy'Riemann operator that substitutes the classical partial derivative for a fractional derivative
