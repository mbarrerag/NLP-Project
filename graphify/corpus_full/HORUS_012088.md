# Analysis of intestinal microbiota in the Colombian adult population associated with risk factors for type 2 diabetes mellitus and obesity.

The intestinal microbiota is the community of living microorganisms of the gastrointestinal tract, the loss of balance in this has been related to type 2 diabetes mellitus (DM2) and obesity; two diseases of high comorbidity in recent decades. The objective was to study phylogenetic profiles of the intestinal microbiota in Colombian adults between 40 and 70 years. Health days were carried out for the recruitment of volunteers; clinical evaluation was carried out to 535 participants, which included measurements of body composition and anthropometry. The risk scale FINDRISC was applied and blood sample was extracted for biochemical analysis; a sample of fecal matter was received, for the extraction of the intestinal microbiota DNA. 70% of the enrolled population presented alteration in the body mass index (BMI), 50% risk to develop DM2 and 66% presented metabolic risk. 85 volunteers were selected for the study of microbiota 22%, using the sequence of the 16S rRNA gene
