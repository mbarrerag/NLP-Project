# Illusions and Evasion. Guilt and Shame in Three Colombian Novels

The object of this article is the exploration of the logic of guilt and shame in three Colombian novels: In December, Marvel Moreno's (1987) breezes arrived, Delirio (2004) by Laura Restrepo and Las cuitas de Carlota (2003) by Helena Araújo. The implicit hypothesis of the essay is that narrative acts are neither innocent nor neutral, and that guilt and shame are the driving force behind a whole system of illusions and escapes that shape the epic world. Thus, guilt and shame are not assumed as literary themes, but as elements that are sedimentary in the narrative form. In the case of the three novels studied, in addition to the patriarchal system there is a crucial issue that allows to unravel the logic of guilt: the question of the social class, in front of which the three narrators employ diverse strategies
