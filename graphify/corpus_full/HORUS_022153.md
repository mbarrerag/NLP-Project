# Comparative evaluation of functional performance and satisfaction of patients with unilateral transtibial amputation with the use of hydrosockets in the Central Military Hospital.

The study compares the physical function and comfort level of patients with unilateral transtibial amputation, who previously came in use of a manual manufacturing socket system by technicians with high expertise and with ISPO II certification. These patients were manufactured two sockets, one of them would be a total contact socket of manual manufacturing by an expert technician and the other a pressure molded socket (PCAST); this latter technique helps to reduce the dependence of the technician's expertica that is currently required for the manufacture of sockets and the correct adjustment that these require when they are adapted to the patient. It is an observational longitudinal study of before and after that it was carried out in adult patients over 18 years with transtibial amputation unilateral, who had previously been protected and attended to the consultation of the prosthesis service for change of the Central Military Hospital in Bogotá. It was carried out the evaluation of modular prosupplants with total contact and suspension by pin and work with a longer use time than one year. Postgraduate to the manufacture of the sockets and components, the corresponding test was performed by the tion, compared with the PCAST socket. A direct inversely proportional correlation was found between the hydrostatic pressure exerted by the system and the difference in the distance travelled (Kendall Tau B of 0.857 and Spearman Rho of 0.941). Subsequent studies are required to establish whether the interface is an important variable at the time of the manufacture of the socket, in addition to determining the performance of the pressure in order to improve the performance of the PCAST system
