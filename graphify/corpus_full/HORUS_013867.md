# The stone is forever

Architecture can be defined as a transformative exercise, an autobiographical experience that leaves scars, ephemeral ruins or abnormal objects that for some reason remain attached to the landscape. This photographic project starts from an exercise of observation of those traces caused by the demolitions in the city of Bogotá, which function as a starting point for the construction of new images that allow to re-signify these objects properly, forming ruins that give rise to a new scenario of fictions. Ruins that refer to the ambiguous, to the unknown, in order to propose thus, another look where reality is questioned and temporality can be questioned. A fiction that through the image can be a representation of the antithesis of a false idea of modernity that is presented today as a vehicle of freedom and life. The re-enactment of a space that is transformed, to create a reality where it would be perishable
