# Virtual learning object for primary prevention of anxiety and depression in adolescents from a school context: intervention in mental health literacy. Design, application and pilot test

The exponential increase in mental health impacts worldwide is a critical aspect to cover, therefore, it is part of one of the Sustainable Development Goals proposed by the United Nations in terms of health and well-being. The early identification of this type of effects allows to improve the quality of life indices of the individual and his family, however, a sector of the population does not have sufficient knowledge to detect them; for this reason, mental health literacy becomes important to mitigate this problem. The literature mentions that educational environments are optimal scenarios for the individual to develop skills that allow to have criteria in decision making for care, prevention and promotion of health. The present paper develops the work plan of a proposal to design a Virtual Object of Learning for mental health literacy in adolescents, aligned with the pedagogical approach Problem-based Learning, aimed at the primary prevention of anxiety and depression disorders in a group of students of tenth degree in an educational institution in Bogotá.
