# Post-Cardiac Post-Cardiac Syndrome in Hospitalised Adult Patients

Summary Intrahospital cardiac arrest in adults (IHCA) is defined as cessation of circulation occurring within hospital facilities. After high quality cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), if spontaneous circulation return (ROSC) is achieved, then cardiac postpartum syndrome (PCSP) appears. This review aims to present the current state of diagnosis and treatment of the PCSP. To address this topic, a bibliographic search was carried out through the direct digital consultation of the scientific literature published between 2014 and 2020 in English and Spanish collected in the databases MedLine, SciELO, Embase and Cochrane.
