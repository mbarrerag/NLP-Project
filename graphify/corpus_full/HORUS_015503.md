# CHARACTERIZATION OF PROCESS INVOLVED IN THE PRESENTION OF ANTIBIOTICS IN SUPERFFICIAL WATERS OF THE HIGH AND MEDIUM-TERM FOOD

The reuse of waste water has been a trend in recent years, this is an effective tool that allows a region to reduce the pressure caused by the demand for water for economic activities. Among all productive sectors, agriculture is the one that reuses domestic waste water the most, due to the high nutrient content (Ababsa et al., 2020 ), which reduces the use of fertilizers and increases crop production (Choudri et al., 2020; Marangon et al., 2020). In Colombia this practice has been implemented in an extensive way, so much so that there is no real quantification of the reuse of diluted waste water in the country (FAO 2017 ). The participation of state entities in the reuse of water is fundamental.
