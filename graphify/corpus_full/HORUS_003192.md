# VALIDATION OF COMMUNITY INUNDATION UMBRATES FOR A EARLY WARNING SYSTEM

The community of Lusitania de Manizales (Colombia) lives with the threat of flooding, to mitigate this risk has implemented a Community Early Warning System (STC), which requires the definition of flood thresholds (levels). The estimation of these thresholds is initially based on the observations of residents, who, with the support of a Limnimetric View, identify levels in the river that they consider dangerous, these levels coincide with estimates made with the full banking approach. To validate these observations, a hydraulic model showing flood stains, which combined the community experience, confirm that the occurrence of these episodes coincides with the approach of full banking in the channel. The results of this validation guarantee an effective tool (limnimetric look) that allows communities to take preventive measures in flood situations, improving the response capacity and contributing to risk reduction
