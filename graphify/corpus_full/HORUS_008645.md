# The Paují guava (Eugenia cribrata), a fruit with possible antioxidant effect and great chemical richness

The Guayaba Paujía is a plant native to our country, it is found especially in the Orinoquía region, does not have research records to know its chemical composition and its potential biological and pharmacological properties, although for other species of the same genus it is possible to find different chemical and biological activity studies of the plants. As objective we have evaluated the biological and antioxidant activity and the majority chemical components, present in the fruit (seed and pulp) of the Paujia Guayaba.
