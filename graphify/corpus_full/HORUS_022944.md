# USE OF A PHYSICAL MODEL TO UNDERSTAND THE INFLUENCE OF FRESHNESS IN LICUATION FOR THE GUAMO-COLOMBIA ARENA

The phenomenon of liquefaction has been evident during several earthquakes around the world. However, the behavior of granular soil during this event has not been fully understood; this is demonstrated by the number of researches in physical models, constituent models and laboratory testing proposals on this topic. At this point, some questions arise: one of them is the exact moment when liquefaction occurs. To address this problem, it is proposed to create a physical model composed of an oscillator of a degree of freedom suitable to evaluate the phenomenon of liquefaction. This was done through a new device capable of transmitting cyclic loads to the soil; the measurement methodology was based on artificial intelligence. Also, a new alternative liquefaction criterion was presented based on the evolution of vertical displacement during the tests. With this new model, the number of cycles necessary to liquidate the sand saturated was studied according to the frequency and breadth of vertical displacement on the edge of a square shoe
