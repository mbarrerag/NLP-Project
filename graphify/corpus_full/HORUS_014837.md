# Effect of climate change on the spatial distribution of rainstorms for Bogotá

The growth of large cities worldwide entails significant changes in the surface and use of the soil seeking to satisfy the needs of the population that inhabits there. For the city of Bogotá, this process has had an important impact, since the pre-Columbian period, the arrival of Europeans (XIV century) and the acceleration of urban growth in the 20th century the city has been experiencing a constant metamorphosis. The large scale urban expansion brings with it alterations or modifications to the natural systems both in its structure and operation, thus generating changes in the energy exchange dynamics between the surface and atmosphere. This is seen clearly in the temperature of the soil and air, the local patterns of circulation and convection, as well as in the precipitation regime, giving signs of local climate change. In addition to the above mentioned that occurs in a local way, Bogotá can be influenced by the global trends directly related to global warming and climate change to large scale, where studies indicate the increase in intensity of extreme precipitation events.
