# Approximation theorem for the Kawahara operator and its application in the control theory

Control properties of the Kawahara equation are considered when the equation is posed on an unbounded domain. Precisely, the paper's main results are related to an approximation theorem that ensures the exact (internal) controllability in (Formula presented.). Following [23], the problem is reduced to prove an approximate theorem which is achieved thanks to a global Carleman estimate for the Kawahara operator
