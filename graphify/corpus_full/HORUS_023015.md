# Technical and financial evaluation of tomato lines type “chonto” Solanum lycopersicum L., of determined growth.

There is little information on the structure of production costs and profitability of new tomato cultivars type “chonto” of growth determined that the research program in “genetic improvement and vegetable seed production” of the National University of Colombia Sede-Palmira has been developing. The research was carried out under open field conditions in two localities of Valle del Cauca, Colombia with the objective of technically and financially evaluating five advanced lines of tomato type “chonto” (Solanum lycopersicum L.) of determined growth habitus based on a regional test, with a view to releasing a new line of tomato type “chonto”. An experimental design of complete blocks at random (BCA) with four repetitions and a commercial witness (UNAPAL-Maravilla). The lines were evaluated by phenology, morphology and by productivity. The financial analysis used the method of direct cost, first determined the structure of production costs and later line was found.
