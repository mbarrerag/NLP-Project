# Road safety analysis using simulation models in VISSIM and Surrogate Safety Assessment Model in the implementation of Transit Management Plans in the city of Bogotá

The main objective of this work was to propose a methodology for conducting road safety analysis through traffic conflicts generated by the SSAM program with the output files of a model calibrated in the VISSIM program. The analysis of 4 hours of information corresponding to the maximum demand hours in the am period and pm period for a base scenario and another scenario with a PMT implemented at a semi-phorized intersection in the city of Bogotá. The methodology presented satisfies the requirements established by the district secretariat of Mobility, which is the regulatory entity that reviews and approves these procedures. The conflicts generated in the base scenario were compared with those generated in the implementation of the PMT and an increase was obtained between 740% and 790% of conflicts being the most affected type of conflict by scope. Additionally, the SSAM program generates the substitute measures to be able to perform the analysis of the impact generated with the implementation of a PMT. It was evaluated for the impact to be achieved, post-invasion time (PET), maximum speed (MaxS), difference in the speeds of the vehicles (Delta thisS) and the initial deceleration rate (DR). base scenario were compared with those generated in the PMT implementation scenario, revisiting an increase between 740% and 790% in conflicts, with rear-end conflicts being the most affected type. Additionally, the SSAM program generates substitute measurements to analyse the impact of implementing a PMT. the following were evaluated: time to collision (ttc), post encroachment time (pet), maximum speed (maxs), difference in vehicle speeds (deltas), and initial deceleration rate (dr). Graphs presented the results obtained are provided, highlighting the impact of implementing a traffic management plan in the city of Bogotá
