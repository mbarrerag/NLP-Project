# Freeze drying and vacuum drying

This chapter reviews various aspects of vacuum drying (VD) and freeze drying (FD) of foods. Initially, the history of these technologies and an overview of the vacuum-dried food markets are concisely presented. The basic aspects and a summary of the mathematical modeling approaches that have been proposed for these operations are presented. It also describes some of the most widely used equipment and accessories in the production of food by FD and VD in a conventional way or by hybrid technologies. Finally, the effects of drying approaches on the relevant quality parameters of food products are briefly discussed
