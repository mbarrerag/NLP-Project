# Hybrid model application in ecommerce recommendation systems using artificial intelligence

Electronic commerce has experienced exponential growth in recent years, driven largely by the digital transformation accelerated after the COVID-19 pandemic. Factors such as confinements and increased trust in digital platforms have encouraged companies to optimize their technological strategies to remain competitive. In this context, the implementation of recommendation systems has become a key tool to improve the purchasing experience, allowing platforms to offer products and services customized according to user preferences. To achieve precise and relevant recommendations, it is essential to use advanced data analysis techniques and artificial intelligence algorithms. Hybrid recommendation models, combining content-based, collaborative filtering and deep learning, have proven to be highly effective by improving the accuracy of suggestions and user satisfaction. The integration of these models allows to take advantage of large volumes of data and behaviour patterns, optimizing customization and increasing customer loyalty. This study explores the application of a hybrid model in e-commerce recommendation systems, analyzing their impact on the accuracy of suggestions and user experience.
