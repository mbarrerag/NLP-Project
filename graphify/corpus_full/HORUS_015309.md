# Learning approaches in human morphology teachers.

Learning approaches in human morphology teachers Learning approaches have been extensively studied since their inception in the 1970s and have evolved to be consolidated as one of the main ways of addressing learning from a measurable and determining perspective. These approaches are subdivided into two broad categories: superficial and profound, each with two components: motivation and strategy, which are influenced by particular curriculum determinants in a particular context. to determine what learning approaches are used by the human morphology teachers of the National University of Colombia during their postgraduate training process. observational, descriptive and cross-sectional quantitative study, in which the universe of active master's degree students was taken as a sample for the first academic period of the year 2025. Participation was voluntary after dissemination and awareness of the objectives, methodology and scope of the study.
