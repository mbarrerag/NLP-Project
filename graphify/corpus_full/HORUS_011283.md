# QUALITY AND POOTENTIAL USES OF EXTRAID PALMA OIL OF OXG INTER-SPECTIVE HIBRINES

In the market, palm oil obtained from the interspecific hybrid OxG is still considered a new product of which its physical and chemical characteristics are unknown. Therefore, it is pertinent to characterize the parameters of quality of this oil in order to be qualified commercially, which is done based on certain quality criteria already established for the commercial marketing of palm oil from commercial cultivars E. guineensis. To this purpose, in this chapter some of the most relevant quality physical parameters are described and discussed in the main functional and chemical in the oil extracted from different cultivars of interspecific hybrids OxG palm and compared with the results of commercial cultivars Elaeis guinesis type DxP (dura x pisifera). In addition, the effect of the conversion of the oil from raw palm oil from the high cultured DxP and the hybrid OxG on the iodine index and the profile of the fatty acids resulting oils is presented by the relative quality of the lower compounds group of the palm oil produced by specific cultivation of the organic oil
