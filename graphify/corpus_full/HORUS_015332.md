# THE CALLEJERO ART SOCIOCULTURAL PROCESS CONTEMPORARY IN FIGHT FOR URBAN SPACE

This research is an attempt to claim worldly living, embodied in the public space of the city of Manizales provoked from among others, artistic expressions such as muralism and graffiti, named here: Street Art, which has suggested other places, stimulating the city to inhabit new and different realities. For this purpose, we understand the city not as an empty receptacle or an inert stage to occupy, but as a living, dynamic and performative body, that is, as a place, where from physical experience an existential experience is verified, putting in place so much lived and symbolic, the practices and social relations that happen there. The question: How could street art contribute to symbolize, re-signify and transform the social production of public space in the city through its practices in the scenario of everyday life?, has guided us to a trialogical relationship that has been established as necessary for the understanding of the phenomenon, which part of the questions by the one who: understood as a collective subject or citizen who is the driving, the how: understood as the practice that this subject has carried out in the three categories that has been established and what is necessary to us to live around
