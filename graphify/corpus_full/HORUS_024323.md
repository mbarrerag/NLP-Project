# AZOTOBACTER VINELANDII FAVORERES THE GROWTH AND PHYSIOLOGIC WATER BEHAVIOR HASS

The avocado (Persea Americana Mill.) is the fourth most important tropical fruit for human consumption worldwide. Chemical synthesis fertilizers generate soil and water impacts and so alternatives have been sought to achieve the nutrition of avocado. The aim of this study was to determine the effect of the biological agent Azoobacter vinelandii on the physiology and growth of avocado `Hass? in three stages of growth. Three experiments were proposed in different plant growth states: avocado seedling (experiment one; Fresno, Tolima), young trees (newly transplanted plants) (experiment two; La Sierra, Cundinamarca), and adult trees of avocado (10 years) (experiment three); Table, Tolima). A completely random design was performed for the experiment one and a complete block design at random (FC = 0 %, FR + block design at least (CF = 0), FR + block design at random for the experiments two and three.
