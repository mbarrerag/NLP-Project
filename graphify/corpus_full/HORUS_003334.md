# Geomechanical analysis of risks in underground storage of CO2, case: Bullerengue South well 1, Sinú-San Jacinto Basin, Colombia

This work analyzes the probable geomechanical risks due to the underground storage of CO2 in a field of study, for which information was used provided by the Colombian Geological Service. A methodology based on international experiences, bibliographic references, advices and the experience of the author in geology of hydrocarbon deposits was applied. Initially, information was used from a wide sector of the basin, then a process of screening was applied that selected the geological sector characterized by the exploratory well for hydrocarbons Bullerengue Sur 1, this due to differentiating characteristics in relation to other sectors, in aspects such as: reliability of the information, prospect formations for storage and seal, conditions of depth and geomechanical.
