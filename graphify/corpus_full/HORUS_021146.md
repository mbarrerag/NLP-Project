# Strengthening strategies to improve the management and optimize the operation of the laboratories of the National University of Colombia.

The laboratory of analysis of pesticide residues of the Faculty of Science, the chemistry department headquarters Bogotá and the laboratory of soils, waters and foliars of the headquarters of the Orinoquia, laboratories accredited under the standard ISO 17025, are ready to join efforts and create strategies to make available to the different actors in the university, within them the national management of laboratories, their knowledge, trajectory and experience with the objective of strengthening the quality management systems of the laboratories of the university through the harmonization and standardization of operating and management processes. The transfer of these capacities to the laboratories of services, research and teaching of the National University will facilitate their operation and the assurance of reliable and quality results
