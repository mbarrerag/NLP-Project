# CORPORATIVE Entrepreneurship TRANSPARENCY: NEW PARADIGMA OF Entrepreneurship GERENCE

Corporate transparency has evolved significantly over the last decades, reflecting changes in social expectations, regulatory environment and technology. In the postmodern era, corporate transparency strategies, -ETC- have emerged as the dominant paradigm of corporate management, overcoming other traditional pillars such as efficiency, quality, innovation and customer service. The conference presented here explores the main stages of this theoretical evolution and the critical variables that should be considered when designing transparency strategies. In addition, it exalts the reasons for this change, based on the demands of contemporary society, technological advances and new expectations of interest groups, supported by theoretical and empirical references. As main conclusions, it is argued that TNCs are redefining the paradigms of postmodern business management, in a world where trust and accountability are essential, surpassing efficiency, quality, innovation and customer service as the main competitive differential. Companies adopting this new paradigm should not only reap economic benefits, but also contribute to a more ethical and sustainable development. Corporate transparency has passed the strategic mechanism of technology, to strengthen customer service as the main innovation
