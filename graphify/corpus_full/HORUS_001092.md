# THE SIBIOTA CONCEPT AS INNOVATION IN THE AGRICULTURE OF THE LAND IN COLOMBIA

The comparisons between the TA versus the motorization with tractor need a multivariate analysis with the inclusion of agroecological and socioeconomic aspects.The objective of this work is to present the partial results of the TA axis of the research project called Center of Excellence (CE) for innovation in mechanization and energy options for family agriculture (code Hermes U.N.: 52937).For this purpose the reference framework of the Third Mission of the University is presented, the methodological aspects related to the concept SIBIOTA (Biomecanic System to Animal Traction), are presented the results obtained and finally the conclusions appear
