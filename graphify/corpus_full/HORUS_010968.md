# ARCHITECTURA IN BAMBU. ANALYSIS AND PROJECTS, IN ARQUITECTONICS MIND, LAND & SOCIETY, 35, WHAT IS HAPPENING TO MODERN ARCHITECTURE? UNIVERSITATE CATECHENIC POLITECYA. OFFICE OF THE CUP'S DIGITAL CURRENCY. MARCH 2024. (PP. 125- 142). __KEEP______KEEP____

The article describes part of the results of the research project "Architecture in Bambú" developed in Me-dellin, at the Faculty of Architecture of the National University of Colombia. The research was divided into two main phases: an analytical and another projectual one: the first one reflects on the work of Simón Vélez, Marcelo Villegas, and the Ekawa group with bamboo, through new methods of assembly, new connections and various structural so- tecnological systems. The second part relates a set of personal essays focused on the topic of the architectural project that propose a reinterpretation of the constructive methodologies analyzed in the preliminary phase, and an application in the professional field. A reinterpretation is forged to test new technical, structural and aesthetic possibilities and through projects founded on the use of this extraordinary natural material
