# SEQUENCES OF VIRUS IN NATURAL POPULATIONS OF DARLINGI ROOT ANNOPHELES 1926 (DIPTERA: CULICIDAE) OF THREE REGIONS OF COLOMBIA

Anopheles darlingi is one of the main vectors of malaria in Colombia. In addition to its competence to transmit parasites of the genus Plasmodium, it houses bacteria and fungi that make part of its microbiota, and that play an important role in different aspects of the biology of the vector. Currently, microbiota studies take advantage of the sequence of new generation to determine the composition of microbial communities in the host. The objective of this study was to characterize the bacterial and fungal microbiota of An. darlingi using only RNA-Sec data. The mosquitoes were collected in Bajo Cauca, Pacific and Amazonas, three endemic regions for malaria in Colombia. The specimens were grouped and the extraction of RNA was carried out. The sequencing of the RNA was performed in NovaSeq6000 Illumina. The quality of the sequencing was determined by FastQC and the most reads of the mosquito was confirmed in analysis, the contigs were assembled in MetaSPAdes.
