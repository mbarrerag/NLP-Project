# FUTURE TIME PERSPECTIVE AND ACTIVITY FOR LIFE IN SCHOOL YOUTH

The presence of a clear orientation towards the future, is a protective factor in adolescence because it allows to associate a wide range of positive results at the time of making decisions in certain situations of their lives. This construct, strongly associated with the satisfaction with life, represents a protective factor against suicidal behavior; an event whose prevalence in increase among the young people of Colombia represents a major public health problem. Based on these approaches, a study is carried out on the associations between Prospective of Future Time (PTF), Suicide Risk (ERS) and Satisfaction with Life (SWLS). For this, during the second quarter of 2020, with the participation of 982 schoolchildren between eighth and eleventh degree, three validated questionnaires are applied for the measurement of these variables in Colombian youth (SWLS scale and Temporary Orientation - AOT, test on the risk assessment of the Suicidal Risk, the PTF scale and the need for the participation of life - SWLS).
