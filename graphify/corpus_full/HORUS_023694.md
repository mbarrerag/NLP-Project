# COLOMBIAN CONSENSUS SARS-COV-2/COVID-19.

VI.1. clinical classification of cases and stratification of risk vi.1.1. What is the clinical classification of cases and stratification of risk? Clinical cases in pediatrics are classified according to the severi- ty in asymptomatic, mild, serious and critical. 1. Asymptomatic: they are those that have no signs or symptoms, and if they have chest X-ray, this is normal, with a positive nucleic acid detection test for SARS-Cov-2/COVID-191. 2. Mild: They include patients with non-complicated disease and mild pneumonia.1,2 Uncomplicated disease, is defined as those patients who have symptoms of acute upper respiratory tract infection, including fever, fatigue, myalgia, cough, throat pain, nasal secretion and sneezing. To physical exa-men, inespecific findings in pharynx and absence of changes to pulmonary auscultation. Some cases may not have fever or only have digestive symptoms such as nau-s, vomiting, abdominal pain and diarrhoea
