# Analysis of strategies for the creation of waste value in the context of circular business models in Colombian agro-industry: A multiple case study.

The current economic model of “take-do-discard”, consumption levels and population growth have influenced global warming and other important environmental problems. The agro-industrial sector is not foreign to this situation. In response, the Circular Economy (CE) has gained attention in the scientific, political and business field because of its model of “take-do-reuse”. However, it is not clear how companies can move to Circular Business Models (CSM) specifically in the agro-industrial sector. For this reason, the objective of this research is to analyze strategies for the valorization of waste and by-products in the context of circular business models in agro-industry for emerging countries such as Colombia. A multiple case study was applied in three companies that value coconut shell, whey of milk and agro-industrial waste, respectively. The results of qualitative data analysis suggest elements of the circular business model to value waste. As for key activities is i+D+I of products and processes, development of machinery and management of trade. As key partners are the accelerators of business, suppliers and agricultural producers.
