# Effectiveness of an intervention in the need for family information in intensive care

to establish the effectiveness of a nursing intervention to improve satisfaction with the need for information from patient families in intensive care unit (ICU). prospective experimental study with randomized intervention. The sample was recruited into a ICU in Bogotá, Colombia, and was composed of family members of patients admitted to the ICU. The evaluation of the outcome of the intervention was carried out using the questionnaires “Inventory of the needs of the patient family in critical care” and “Satisfaction survey of the patient’s family in critical care”. The sample size was 66 families and the random allocation was made by means of random numbers in permutated blocks, with 33 families in the control group and 33 families in the intervention group. a significant difference was found in terms of the communication and support axes. In the proximity dimension, a statistical significance was observed in relation to the possibility of talking with the same nursing professional every day (p = 0.009). The general satisfaction was favorable and it was identified that the intervention group experienced a change of higher magnitude (p & lt; 0.001***) to the breakdown of the patient’s ability to maintain a nursing group
