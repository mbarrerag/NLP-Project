# ESTIMAL FACTORS FOR EMISSION OF PARTICULAR MATERIALS BY AGRICULTURAL ACTIVITIES IN COLOMBIAN ORINOCIA

The Colombian Orinoquia has an area of 25 Mha (Fig. 1) of which, by 2017, 1 Mha were sown with different crops, including oil palm, rice, soybean, cocoa and fruit trees (Jimerez et al, 2022). The different operations that are carried out in the agricultural confined soils generate particulate material by resuspension, generating acute and chronic effects on the health of the working community and increasing in the concentrations of the background. Direct emissions of the crops are generated by unusual soil preparation activities (e.g., flattened and subsolated), by more frequent activities (e.g., lime) and by activities linked to the phenological cycle of the crop. These include plowing, dissed, subsolated, sowing and de-sherb, along with the harvesting activity itself. Some of these operations are usually more frequent for short cycle (SC) crops than, for permanent crops (LC), which have a longer useful life, [year].
