# LINE TITLES STUDY

Therefore, I submit to the public opinion the text Study of Titles, Online, in which the transformation that is undergoing one of the most classic institutions of law is explored, as a result of the impacts coming from the development of the Legal Tech industry, that in the field of property technology (Proptech) has begun to unfold through smart contracts (Smart Contracts) supplied by techniques of blockchain shared registration that allow the jump from the titling to the real estate to the tokenization, innovation that rises as a new challenge of the Digital Democratic State of the 21st century
