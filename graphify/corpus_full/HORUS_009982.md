# ARCHEOLOGICAL STUDY OF THE IMPACT OF PRE-COLOMBINE FISHING IN THE RAUDAL AREA OF THE MAGDALEN RIUM, DURING THE TERRITORY PERIOD (SIGHTS XI TO XII A.D.)

In this publication we present the results of the archaeological investigations carried out in the area of the valley of Magdalena, between the municipality of Honda (Tolima) and the co-ordination of Puerto Bogotá (Cundinamarca). The archaeological problem of the last period of pre-Hispanic occupation (Periodo Tardío) is addressed, and it is mainly the study of the Faunistic resources, with emphasis on the exploitation of migratory species, based on the results of excavations carried out in three archaeological sites that provided abundant vestiges of fauna, associated with plant remains, ceramic materials and lithic instruments.
