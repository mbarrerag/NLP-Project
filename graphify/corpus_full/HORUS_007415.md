# Relationship between leadership and strategic planning in a micro-enterprise in succession

This work focused on investigating the relationship between leadership and strategic planning in a micro-enterprise in succession, during the period July 2021 and March 2024, when a generational transition was made due to the death of the founder without having a succession plan. Although the heirs actively participate in the company, they do so essentially for economic purposes. Although they have a genuine interest in the development and success of the business, their work there did not generate them satisfaction or personal/professional growth. In addition, they have evidenced weaknesses in the processes of decision-making, initiative and creativity in the execution of the daily tasks that have a direct impact on the results of the company. However, this research has implemented a process of strategic planning that allowed to address priority actions to improve the financial and operational stability of the company. However, the lack of a visionary leader that assumes the direction of the business towards new opportunities and strategic alliances has limited the effectiveness of these planning efforts.
