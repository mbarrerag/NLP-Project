# ACE-BASE ENTITLING

Acid-based reactions are frequently present in nature and are important in different chemical and biological systems.This type of chemical reaction, commonly known as neutralization, is characterized by the reaction of an acid and a basis for the formation of a salt and water. Considering that acids and bases react in a particular way with colored indicators, these can be used for their differentiation.In this practice indicators will be used to determine the acid nature based on different solutions. In turn, a quantitative analysis of acid solutions and unknown concentration bases will be carried out using titrations with reference patterns. In this practice, the role of titration as a central tool in quantitative chemical analysis will be evidenced
