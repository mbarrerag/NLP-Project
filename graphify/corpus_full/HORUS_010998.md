# ENTRY IN ORGANIZATIONS: A BORDER FROM THE COLLABORATION BASED ON THE PRACTICES OF THE DESIGN

The entrepreneurial activity has been related in general with the individual ability to bring innovative value to society through the goal of creating a productive unit. On the other hand, the concept of intra-entrepreneurship is also posed as an entrepreneurial activity, but in a specific organizational context with some changes in the perspective of its results. The relationships of this perspective, between individual decisions and decisions determined by the relationship with a specific business context, conflict due to the fact that companies operate through the deployment of resources and actions of a group of people with common goals. This is where the concept of intra-entrepreneurship that is proposed in this article becomes relevant, where processes of exploration and innovative viability of ideas and business models are facilitated. This document is the result of the work with creative teams aimed at facilitating processes of facilitating ideas with innovative perspective. Thus, the deployment of creative collaborative teams and of innovative design and of innovative design and design, can be an open-ended approach to the context/specific approaches of the exploration processes in the organizations.
