# Diagnostic performance of the morphological study of bone marrow biopsy in the day 15 evaluation of patients with acute lymphoblastic leukemia B.

Determine the response of the treatment against acute lymphoblastic leukemia B (LLA-B) has been the pillar in the improvement of the survival figures of patients suffering from this disease.The protocol currently used by the Colombian Association of Hematology and Pediatric Oncology (ACHOP) is the ALL IC-BFM 2009 which indicates the evaluation of the response on days 8, 15 and 33 of treatment based on bone marrow aspirate (AMO) and flow cytometry (CMF); however, during the evaluation of these differences can be presented discrepancies that hinder the affirmation of a diagnosis and given the serious implications in the treatment scheme that these patients should receive based on this response is of vital importance to make the diagnosis as accurate as possible. The bone marrow biopsy is then a third parameter that can confirm or better approximate that diagnosis. This study was then conceived as a retrospective cohort in which the diagnosis of the following cases was taken: CF, AMO and report of bone marrow biopsy of patients with LLA-B between October 2012 and December 2014 and the patients were diagnosed as a disease of the disease and the case of the hospital. The cases that differed between the two morphological study modalities (AMO and BMO) represented 12.6%, being more frequent to find positive AMOs with negative BMOs leaving the opposite case of negative AMOs with suspicious BMOs with 8.3% of the discordant cases. The comparison of the discordant cases with the results of CMF as a gold standard method confirmed the positivity of positive AMOs with negative BMOs, but additionally confirmed the presence of blasts in BMOs of the suspected cases confirming the usefulness of BMOs as a complementary method, which is vitally important in staging and prognostic. Additional findings found that in AMOs the probability of having inadequate samples is twice as high as in BMOs. Then, the performance by quality of the sample is better in the BMO versus AMO study. In the cases of AMOs diluted the CMF studies were positive for EMRs in 55.5% but only in 22.2% of the cases their values were found in morphological study levels
