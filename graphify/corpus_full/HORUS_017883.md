# Evaluation of the soil-atmosphere-vegetation model MESH in a complex relief Colombian tropical basin with information limitations.

Previous implementations of soil-vegetation-atmosphere interaction schemes (SVAT), have shown the limitations of these models in the simulation of horizontal flows, in areas with orographic complexity, information scarcity and located in tropical areas. Particularly in the Colombian case these difficulties have been presented in the Upper Magdalena. Understand the reasons, for which these limitations have been presented is of particular interest for the importance of these models in the coupled analysis of climatic and hydrological variables. For this reason, in this work, the analysis initiated by Arboleda (2018), who implemented the model MESH - which contains a SVAT model - in the Upper Magdalena river basin (CRC) and later in the whole of the Magdalena-Cauca macroconcaña (MCC). By implementing the MESH model, the results were obtained an adequate estimation of the observed flows, in the middle and low areas of the MCSH, but with deficient results in the Upper Magdalena.
