# Research advances at the Faculty of Nursing

In the framework of the celebration of the centenary of its foundation, the Faculty of Nursing presents in this work the most recent research developments of nine of its groups. The chapters point to where research progresses in each of the areas and offer clues on how knowledge in nursing is consolidated in Colombia, through the voices of the public university with the greatest impact on the training of professionals in this field since 1920. As a discipline, we highlight the scientific knowledge that is born from this work and that requires to be accumulated and synthesized, so this is the starting point of the book. Therefore, various research designs are employed that with rigour, harmonization and dialogue with nursing care and collective health allow us to highlight the need to continue developing increasingly strengthened research lines. We all want to see the fruits of the centenary trajectory, so that empirical indicators are necessary when speaking of research. According to Jaqueline Fawcett, these allow us to reflect the philosophy, the theoretical approach and methodological of nursing thinking.
