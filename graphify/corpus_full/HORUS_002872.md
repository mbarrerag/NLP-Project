# THE FACHALITY OF THE ARCHITETHONICA EXPRESSION OF THE COTIDIANA LIFE NOT URBAN DOMESTIC SPACE OF MONTANHA EM MEDELLÍN, COLÔMBIA.

It is intended to understand from an architectural perspective how contemporary life affects the domestic space and how, in turn, the domestic space affects the way in which life occupies it in the unique mountainous context of the Valle de Aburrá in Colombia, with tropical Andean climate and equinoccial latitude to 6oN. The traditional research approaches of these topics, start from epistemologies of European origin based on the conception of the predominantly flat world -topographical and culturally- (KRÄMER, 2023), with seasonal climate and in northern latitudes.When these categories are addressed in a mountainous context with tropical Andean climate and in an equinoccial latitude, they are insufficient to describe and understand the complex relations between the domestic space and the life that inhabits it daily.
