# Computational model for the fusion of optical data obtained by means of satellite sensors and manned and unmanned airborne sensors.

This research aims to develop and implement a computational model for data fusion using the pansharpening method, i.e. by integrating the spatial detail of high spatial resolution images and the spectral richness of multi-spectral images. The model will be evaluated using optical data obtained by Sentinel satellite sensor - 2, large format aerial and orthophotomosaic photographs obtained from data acquired by the unmanned aerial system (UAS) Phantom 4 RTK. The computational model will be developed for the implementation of the component substitution and multi-resolution analysis approaches that currently constitute the state of art in the fusion of Earth observation data and will be defined later on the basis of an evaluation of the performance of the algorithms the most optimal for the fusion of the data described above. An experimental methodology of quantitative character will be used to develop in four stages: (i) delimitation of the area of study and capture and acquisition of data; (ii) geometric and radiometric correction; (iii) digital processing of satellite images and images UAS using the data fusion algorithms (GmidàS) transformation
