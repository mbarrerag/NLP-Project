# Osteopathic techniques for emotional release: Monography.

Osteopathy, originally from the United States in the nineteenth century, is one of the branches of alternative and complementary medicine that has had the most boom in recent years due to the increase of its demand by patients; it seeks to restore the structure, function and mobility in each altered system, with this result avoiding the pathology (1). Several authors in osteopathy have focused on the study of emotions and their release, mainly Dr. John E. Upledger who coined the name of “somatoemotional liberation”. It has been observed that the emotional or physical traumas generate an excess of disorganized energy that is especially located in the fascia, producing “energy cysts”, which the patient hides generating somatic discomfort. What makes the palpation the osteopate is to identify these cysts and through different described maneuvers, produce the release of emotions and even the re-experience of these and the improvement of symptoms
