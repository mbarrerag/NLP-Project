# EXPLORING THE AGRICULTURAL DIVERSITY CATALOGUE OF THE COLOMBIAN COLLECTION OF MORA GERMOPLASM (RUBUS SPPP.)

The cultivation of the mora (Rubus glaucus Benth.) and its related species is frequently located in mountainous areas and is of great importance in the Colombian agricultural context, especially among peasant and ethnic communities. Its importance lies in several dimensions that deserve an integrated reflection, which can cover not only economic and productive aspects, but also social, cultural and environmental implications.In the economic aspect, the cultivation of the mora has become a source of income crucial to Colombian producers, since it contributes to the diversification of its sources of income.In productive terms, the use of a combination of traditional and modern techniques to maximize the production of mora.In the economic aspect, the use of high quality cuttings and the implementation of efficient irrigation systems are common practices that have been improving the yield.In addition, research and development projects have been carried out with the objective of promoting alternatives that reduce the excessive use of agrochemicals. This includes the implementation of an integrated management plan of pests and diseases, using sustainable methods that include both biological and chemical solutions.In the social and cultural dimension, the production of mora has allowed to maintain and strengthen the social fabric. Challenges and opportunities are to be found in closing the technological gap. In particular, it is necessary to provide access to modern technologies and continuous trainings that enhance the productivity and quality of the crop, such as the implementation of efficient irrigation systems and the use of technological solutions to monitor crops, which are examples of how technology can transform peasant agriculture
