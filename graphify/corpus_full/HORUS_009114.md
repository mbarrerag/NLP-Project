# • Development of an income with cannabinoids for the beverage industry

The CBD is one of the most abundant compounds within the Cannabis plant. Considering its remarkable medicinal benefits, great interest has been generated for its application in various industries including food, which has encouraged the processes of regulation and legalization of the cannabis market in several countries of the world. In this work the best extraction conditions were investigated with ethanol at different temperatures, times and proportions of solvent and plant material, as well as the most suitable emulsion configuration under the maximum percentage recovery of CBD in the extract. To perform the extraction process, it was decarbonized to 140oC by 30 min to have the greatest conversion of CBDA to CBD, then the decarbonitated flower was placed in the extraction team followed by ethanol, the speed of agitation was adjusted to 400 rpm.
