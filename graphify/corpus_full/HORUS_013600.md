# MOISTURE CORRECTION IN MIXTURES USING INNOVATIVE THERMOGRAVIMETRIC TECHNIQUE TO DETERMINE WATER ASBSORPTION IN DIFFERENT COLOMBIAN FINE AGGREGATES

One aspect that affects the strength of Portland cement mortars is the moisture correction of the mixture. Standardized techniques are often used to obtain the absorption value in fine aggregates; one of them is the ASTM C128 standard. However, this standard presents a variability associated with the identification of saturated surface dry state in aggregates. This research compares the water absorption of four types of Colombian aggregates obtained by means of the ASTM C128 standard and an innovative instrumental and analytical technique with the thermogravimetric halogen light balance (TBHL), coupled to a software that reads the water data retained in real time. Also, the resistance of mortars designed from moisture correction is optimized. The absorption results show that TBHL presents results with less dispersion (not more than 10%) compared to the ASTM C128, which indicates greater precision and less uncertainty. In addition, the resistance of mortars designed by applying TBHL, requiring 25% less time, 86% less energy consumption and 98% less fine aggregates for a test.
