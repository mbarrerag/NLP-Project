# Review of ants of the genus Nesomyrmex Wheeler, 1910 (Hymenoptera: Formicidae: Myrmicinae) in Colombia

Nesomyrmex Wheeler, is a genus of ants diverse and little studied, with 86 species known globally, of which 23 species are known in the Neotropical region. Despite this richness of species, there are no recent taxonomic reviews for the Neotropical region, being the synopsis of Kempf in 1959 the only treatment for fauna of that region. Currently, in Colombia eight species of Nesomyrmex are known, although the material deposited in the collections and museums of natural history of the country, and recent collections in different places, suggest a greater richness. Specimens of several entomological collections were examined and samplings were carried out in four departments of the country. The specimens were identified using the original descriptions and the review of Kempf (1959); also comparisons of the material were made with specimens identified deposited in foreign collections and with the images of high resolution of some types of specimens from from the same.
