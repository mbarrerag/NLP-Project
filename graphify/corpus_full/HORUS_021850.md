# Computer model implementation for social engineering detection based on machine learning and natural language processing.

Computer security or cybersecurity is responsible for the protection of data and services against unauthorized individuals and protects the characteristics of information such as integrity, confidentiality and availability. There are multiple threats and attacks that put computer security at risk, such as ransomware, malware or malware, denial attacks, injection failures, social engineering, among others. In many cases the most vulnerable part of systems are users, for this reason cybercriminals use social engineering to illegally acquire information from users. Social engineering consists in the manipulation of individuals by deception to disseminate private or confidential information. This type of cyberattack is very difficult to detect as it can be executed by any individual at any time and exploits psychological aspects of humans to deceive them. In this work, the implementation of a computational model based on natural language processing techniques is presented to extract characteristics from texts and feed three algorithms of machine learning (neurnal networks, support vector machines and random forests) to detect possible attacks of social engineering results (the three algorithms of social engineering were trained in texts, which exceeded the accuracy of social engineering results)
