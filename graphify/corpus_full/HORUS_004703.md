# TOWARDS DEMOCRATIZATION OF THE UNIVERSITY GOVERNMENT (FOR A COMMON GOOD)

The general theme of democracy as a historical and paradigmatic form of government and the most specific one of university democracy have as their basic element in common, the principle of autonomy of the community to which reference is made. The importance of this factor is related to the fact that, in modernity, the government, understood as the sociopolitical activity related to the definition of collective ends and the orientation of human groups, is conditioned by the ability to be self-nomes or, in other words, by the ability to establish its own norm
