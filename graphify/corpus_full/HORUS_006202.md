# The “only mystery of Quantum Mechanics” explained by generalized interference phenomenology

About 60 years ago, Richard Feynman stated that single matter particle interference is “a phenomenon which is impossible to explain in any classical way and which has in it the heart of Quantum Mechanics. In reality, it contains the only mystery of Quantum Mechanics.” Despite of the accurate predictions of single particle interference outcomes provided by quantum mechanics, the phenomenological explanation of particle behavior in a basic interferometer is still unclear. A novel and exhaustive explanation of this mystery, based on the notion of confinement in geometric states of space, is proposed in this paper. It has new important physical implications regarding the role of ordinary space in interference. Generalization of this explanation for light and single photon interference is discussed, thus showing the feasibility of a unified ontology in ordinary space for any type of interference
