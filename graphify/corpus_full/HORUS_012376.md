# The utility of flexible bronchoscopy in patients with haematological neoplasia treated in the chest unit of the National Cancer Institute during the period from 1 January 2017 to 30 April 2022.

The patient with haematological neoplasia attended in the chest unit of the National Institute of Cancer during the period from 1 January 2017 to 30 April 2022. Patients with haematological neoplasia undergo a wide variety of immunosuppressive therapies. The lungs may be affected by infectious, toxic processes up to 60%. Materials and methods A descriptive, retrospective study of a cohort of patients over 18 years of age with haematological neoplasia was performed, attended in the chest service of the National Institute of Cancerology between 1 January 2017 and 30 April 2022. Patients with admission to the intensive care unit, coagulopathy, hemodynamic instability and severe involvement in oxygenation were excluded. The collection was performed by non-probabilistic sampling of consecutive cases of all subjects who met the inclusion criteria. Results A total of 162 subjects were taken into bronchoscopia.
