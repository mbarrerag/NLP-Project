# Development of a software method to assist in the thematic analysis of responses to open end questions in English-language surveys

Thematic analysis is fundamental in qualitative research, offering valuable ideas but often requiring a significant amount of time and experience. This work addresses some limitations of Computer-assisted Software for the Analysis of Existing Qualitative Data and presents a novel method specifically designed to assist in the thematic analysis of open questions with multiple labels for Spanish surveys. The proposed method combines domain experience with advanced language models to establish preliminary categories. Subsequently, human discernment is combined with similar measures to speed up the categorization of some responses using these preliminary categories. The process culminates in a robust and scalable automated categorization, using various models, language models and precision metrics. The proposed method is composed of three modular phases that can function independently or collaboratively, offering a comprehensive solution to researchers. It can reduce the long manual coding process by taking advantage of the Great Language Models (LLMs) and techniques of Natural Language Processing (PLN). The method is evaluated through its application in a set of data from the National University of Colombia, showing promising results through its various modules and options.
