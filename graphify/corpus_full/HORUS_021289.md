# Risk from mercury consumption and incidence of microplastics in commercial fish in coastal regions with high anthropic influence in the Colombian Pacific and Caribbean

A wide range of ecosystemic services of great economic and social value are provided by coastal ecosystems by hosting a high biodiversity and having a high productivity, but the supply of these services is being affected by mercury and microplastic pollution. This is of particular importance for the Bay of Buenaventura and the Gulf of Salamanca, since they are coastal ecosystems with multiple anthropic disturbances, but associated with the production of ecosystem services of which, to a large extent, have historically depended their inhabitants. The aim of this proposal is to determine the effects and the state of pollution by mercury and microplastics and the risk by consumption of fishery resources in coastal regions of the Pacific (Bahía de Buenaventura) and Colombian Caribbean (Golfo de Salamanca). For this purpose, two sampling campaigns will be carried out per site over the course of a year, to cover different hydroclimatic conditions (dry and rain), in three stations representing environmental variability in sampling sites.
