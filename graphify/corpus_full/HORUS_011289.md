# OXG INTER-SPECTIVE HIBRICULTIVES POLINIZATION

The inter-specific hybrid OxG (Elais oleifera x Elaeis guineensis) is characterized by its high productivity, oil quality and partial resistance to the rotting of the bud. However, its rate of extraction of oil under natural pollinated conditions is very low, mainly due to the low viability and low germination of its pollen. Consequently, to achieve the formation of commercially usable clusters, pollination is made artificially with me- todologologies such as assisted pollination using E. guineensis pollen or applying the 1-naphthalenecetic acid growth regulator (ANA) throughout the production cycle. As these costly tasks, due to the high requirement of labour and inputs, in order to achieve adequate implementation of these methodologies, it is necessary to know the reproductive biology of the OxG hybrid and technical parameters for its implementation.
