# EFFECT ON RESISTANCE TO COMPRESSION IN AN ALIGERED CONCRETE USED A HEBRID CEMENTING MATRIX.

The study and development of the AACs has been increased due to the possibility that they have to be a variant of the OPC since it has equal or better properties. In a hybrid cement product of the OPC mixture and an alkalineally activated cement (AAC) base fly ash, the main reaction products of each of them coexist, that is, hydrated calcium silicate (C-S-H) in the OPC and hydrated sodium aluminosilicate (N-A-S-H) in the AAC ash base. Several research studies have been directed towards the production of light concretes (LWAC) using AAC with different precursors. However, until now, the production of light concretes with hybrid cements has not been reported, which has great potential since, hybrid cements are the most expeditious way to achieve the substitution of the portland cement, in addition, it has already been demonstrated that concretes can be made lightened with each of the OPC and AAC cements separately that have shown adequate performance
