# HOW IT'S OUTSIDE IS INSIDE

In 2020, the National University of Colombia and the Museum of Modern Art of Bogotá created an inter-institutional alliance for the support of the research processes between art and science. On that occasion the MAMBO invited the visual artist and professor of the Faculty of Arts Rosario López to propose a curatorial exercise from his vision and artistic practice, putting into dialogue works that are part of the Museum's collection with his recent research around the landscape, nature and territory. In the following year the exhibition Traces, Offices and Territories showed that presented the results of the research of López and his team of co-investigators, formed by the Dr. dolors armenteras and fagua alvares teachers of the faculty of sciences and dra tania pérez-bustos of the school of gender studies; besides a select group of students in formation of the three disciplines.
