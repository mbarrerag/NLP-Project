# Characterisation of a construction material obtained by recycled plastic mixtures and agricultural and forestry waste.

The characterization of construction plates or plastic wood “WP” manufactured by thermoforming is carried out, analyzing the variables present in the process such as temperature, pressure, time, percentages of mixture and their influence on the mechanical and physical properties of the material obtained. The plates are made with different mixtures of recycled plastic and agro-industrial waste (PP, PVC, PET, bamboo chips and rice husk), MAPE is used as a coupled additive to increase the cohesion between the polymer matrix and plant reinforcement, tests of tension, hardness and percentage of water absorption are carried out. The material developed is implemented in constructive applications to supply the social and environmental problems presented in Colombia and Latin America
