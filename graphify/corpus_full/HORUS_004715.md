# Zoning of water demand for oil palm cultivation (Elaeis Guineaensis. Jacq) under the agroclimatic conditions of the department of Cesar

The present study addressed the low availability of water in the department of Cesar and the high consumption associated with the ignorance of the water requirement and low efficiency irrigation systems in the cultivation of the oil palm (Elaeis guineensis. Jacq).These factors have led to decreases in productivity and inefficient use of the water resource.The main objective was to determine the water demand of the oil palm cultivation in areas with homogeneous agroclimatic conditions in the department of Cesar.To achieve this, it was zoned with cluster methodologies, evaluating four different approaches.The methodology of machine learning K-Means distances euclideanas proved to be the most suitable for the climatic conditions of the department.This methodology identified 10 agroclimatic areas in the department, of which the available resource ratio (Kc) was for the cultivation of the oil palm (10 and 15 years) and the result of the analysis of the water use in these areas.
