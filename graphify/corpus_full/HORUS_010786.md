# THERMAL BEHAVIOR OF STARCH FROM DIFFERENT BOTANICAL SOURCES BY THERMOGRAVIMETRIC KINETIC ANALYSIS

Starch is nowadays one of the most promising biopolymers for use in the pharmaceuticals, plastics, construction, and the food industry due to its versatile characteristics for chemical and physical modifications. A thorough understanding of physicochemical properties, specifically thermal behavior, could facilitate decision-making in terms of product design and manufacturing process optimization involving starch as raw material. This study is focused on the determination of the kinetic parameters for the thermal disintegration of the starch granules and its correlation with the physicochemical features
