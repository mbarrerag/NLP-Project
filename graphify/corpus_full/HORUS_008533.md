# REDUCING THE LATITUDIAL SESIGUS IN MARINE PALINOLOGICALITY: THE ROLE OF THE DENOFLAGER KIDS IN THE BIOSTRATIGRAPHY AND NEOCEANOGRAPHY OF THE NEOCGENE IN THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

The dynoflagelates cysts represent a group of marine palinomorphs that have been used for more than half a century to date and correlate rock sequences deposited in marine environments, as well as to infer paleoceanographic conditions, mainly in the Northern Hemisphere. However, the limited number of marine palinology studies carried out in the tropical zones has resulted in a biased understanding of the palinological record. Studies of marine palinology in the Colombian Caribbean over the last decade have allowed both to evaluate the use of this group of palinomorphs as biostrategic markers.
