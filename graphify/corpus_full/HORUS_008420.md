# LITERATURA REVIEW ON THE INFLUENCE OF FERMENTATION CONDITIONS IN THE QUALITY OF THE GRANTEED CACCOAO (THEOBROMA CACAO L)

The fermentation of cocoa is essential for the quality of chocolate and can be affected by a variety of factors such as microorganisms, enzymes, temperature and environmental factors. This literature review aimed to explore how fermentation conditions, including materials, temperature and technology, affect the quality of fermented grain. Analyzing 11 cases of cocoa fermentation in different countries, several approaches and maturity levels were found prior to the fermentation process, as well as various designs, materials and technologies used. The importance of controlling temperature and the strategic introduction of specific microorganisms, including innovative technologies such as ochmic warming, was highlighted. The need to maintain high hygiene standards and comply with regulations was emphasized, highlighting the cutting test as a means to ensure the quality of fermented grain. These findings show the importance of constant innovation in cocoa fermentation practices, with the aim of adapting to changing demands of the market and consumers. In addition, the need to further improve the efficiency and sustainability of cocoa fermentation processes is highlighted, in order to promote responsible and environmentally friendly practices throughout the chocolate supply chain
