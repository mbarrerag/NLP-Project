# A BRIEF REMEMBER CONTEMPORARY BY THE STRATEGIES OF THE REGIONALIZATION IN COLOMBIA: FROM THE EXPECT OF DESCENTRALIZATION IN 1991 TO THE REGIONAL FRACKS

The regional organization, that political-administrative level that mediates between the nation-state and the local level -municipalities- is in Colombia, more product of the economic and political geography than of planning processes protected by theories about the region. It is worth recalling that the theories about the region, used as strategies of regionalization, are essentially those that pose the natural region as a framework of civilization development (French case at the beginning of the twentieth century), the nodal and functional region (cases of the United States, a good part of the European powers and even in the Latin American context, since the middle of the twentieth century) or to a lesser extent, the lived region that refers to the notion of the imagined and felt community. After passing through the federal regime with a very high autonomy organization (including fragmentation) regional between 1830 and 1870 with the Landing States, is essentially reflecting a progressive concentration and centralization of the economic and political power that derived from the unequal agro-exporting articulation to the world market
