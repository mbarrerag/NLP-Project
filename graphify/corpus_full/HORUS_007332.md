# The form of inactivity: spaces not programmed in learning environments in Colombia

This research analyzes how some contemporary architects in Colombia have integrated the notion of the non-programmed into the learning environments, challenging the functionalist and utilitarian approaches. Through a methodology that addresses three interrelated components—activity, space and user—the dynamics between the programmed and the non-programmed are explored. The findings reveal how the incorporation and manipulation of the ‘non-programmed’ in different instances of the learning environments fosters spontaneity, leisure and social interaction, transforming educational spaces into environments that prioritize the experience and freedom of the user. It is concluded that this approach challenges traditional coercive models, promoting designs that prioritize the freedom and interaction of users.
