# Diversity of Aquatic Macroinvertebrates Along Altitudinal Gradients in Colombia

Some case studies that evidence changes in the composition of aquatic macroinvertebrates along the altitudinal gradients of Colombian rivers are analyzed. Most of the works on ecology and distribution of this biological group have been developed in the Andean zone (center and west of Colombia) and the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta (north of Colombia), thus models of the distribution of macroinvertebrates along these two altitudinal gradients are presented. The studies include some orders widely investigated (e.g., Ephemeroptera, Coleoptera, Trichoptera, and Diptera). In this context, the relationships between some attributes of the trophic network structure of aquatic insects and the altitudinal gradient in a river of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, are shown. Finally, historical drivers linked to past climatic and geological events, along with the anthropogenic effects and their relationship with the invertebrate distribution, are examined
