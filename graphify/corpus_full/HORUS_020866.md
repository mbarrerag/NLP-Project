# --Hegemonic or subaltern? Interactions, cuts and ontological and epistemological mediations between institutional memory(s) and indigenous memory(s)

The following work aims to study and problematize the indigenous memory that has built the Colombian State (based on Law 1448 of 2011; Decree 4633 of 2011 and the auto 004 of 2009) in the reports of the CNMH and, specifically, in the report: Time of life and death: memories and struggles of the indigenous peoples in Colombia (CNMH - ONIC 2019).From the characterization of this documentary collection (defined in the project as institutional memory), it is sought to contrast these institutional artifacts of memory with the indigenous memory present in the academic literature and knowledge of the indigenous authorities of the country attached to their main organizations. The previous exercise is justified insofar as it is possible to identify four frameworks where the indigenous ontology and epistemologies are stressed by the institutional memory erected by the memory artifacts created under the mandate of Law 1448 of 2011, the Decree-Law 4633 of 2011 and the auto004 of 2009. First, the regulations recognize only human beings and, in this sense, it is not possible to think, from the time of memory, a second place of the human or the most thought that is defined in the indigenous- 2009. ) Finally, there is the construction of an imaginary memory anchored in a new beginning because memory, as a tool of political transition, is part of the civilizing crisis in the West (world wars, dictatorships and armed conflicts) (Terdiman, 1993; Schindel 2011); this is unknown to the existence of memories that are not anchored to a project of a new beginning and that has other means of transmission and conservation. To fulfill the objective described above, the work does a heuristic and hermeneutic exercise of two moments: on the one hand, making a brief characterization of the reports of the CNMH associated with the indigenous communities of Colombia and, in particular, of the report: Times of life and death: memories and struggles of the indigenous peoples in Colombia (CNMH - ONIC 2019); on the other hand, a state of art is constructed on the indigenous memories present in the academic literature and the knowledge of the indigenous authorities registered in the National Indigenous Organization of Colombia (ONIC), the Indigenous Regional Council of the Cauca (CRIC), among other organizations. * We ask the readers of the proposal to read the annexed document for their understanding
