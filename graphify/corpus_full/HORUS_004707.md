# VIOLENCE EVIDENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND CHILDREN IN A MIDDLE HORIZON CEMENTERY IN THE CENTRE COST OF PERU.

Although it is true, it has been documented from the archaeological point of view that several societies that inhabited the central coast of Peru women could have fulfilled important roles during the Middle Horizon, little is said about their subordination and that of children in hierarchical societies, which seems to derive in many cases in various forms of physical violence, typical in current cases of violence against women. To this is added the high infant mortality due to violent causes. We start from the idea that societies with varying degrees of hierarchicalization and therefore stratified, influences the way in which gender relations are established, which affects all levels of the population, but which is particularly detrimental to women and children.
