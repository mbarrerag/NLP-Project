# Validation of uncertainty estimation methodology from a metrology laboratory for calibration of high voltage dividers.

This paper selects two methodologies for the estimation of uncertainty that can be applied to the calibration of high voltage dividers in a calibration laboratory that demonstrates its technical competence with ISO/IEC 17025. The first is the methodology proposed by the Guide to the Expression of Measurement Uncertainty (GUM) which is based on an approximation of the Taylor series of the first order of the measurement model and the second methodology is the Monte Carlo Method proposed in Supplement 1 to the GUM in which iterations are performed taking into account the probability distribution functions that make up the measurement model. Historical calibration data and experimental data are used to perform the validation of methodologies
