# The San Antonio Macroproject and the territorial autonomy of Buenaventura in the period 2008-2020

The present final master's work seeks to present through a theoretical reading and presentation of empirical background, the way in which the instruments of planning and management of the territory have been employed by the different levels of government to solve the housing deficit in the country. In this group are the macroprojects of National Social Interest as one of the bets of mass production of social housing in the country. They have positioned on the national public debate their articulation as large urban operations with the territories. In this sense, the research deepened on the implications that had the MSN San Antonio in the management and planning of the territory of the district of Buenaventura, formulated and designed by the national government, which sought to reduce the housing deficit in the municipality. But also to attend to various strategic interests for private and public actors that saw in this possibility to generate an essential development for the port industry of the city, the POT and the other norms of adoption of the different ethnic communities settled in proximity to the macroproject, as well as the families benefiting from this. The methodology uses official data associated to the topic and analyzes them in relation to the national and municipal development plans, the POT and other norms of adoption. Into the implications of the National Social Interest Macroproject (MISN) in San Antonio for the management and planning of the Buenaventura district's territory. Formulated and designed by the national government, the MSN encouraged to alleviate the housing deficit in the municipality. However, it also encouraged to address various strategic interests for both private and public actors who saw the potential for essential development for the city's port industry. This, in turn, led to conflicts with different ethnic communities resolving in proximity to the Macroproject, as well as families benefiting from it.
