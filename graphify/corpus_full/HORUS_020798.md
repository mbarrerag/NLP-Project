# Monsterish desires of the aji: Burning pots to reveal themselves

This work addresses the search, transit and subjective journey to be able to inhabit desire. It begins with an exploration of the depths and unconscious roots of a monster that faces society and its gazes. The next stop presents the ají as a device that transports us along the routes of desire to mobilize the body and memories. The transit routes of desire lead to a re-signification to a subversive desire that emerges by allowing to name and inhabit that monstrous and wild woman. A monster that chooses its flavors to taste the encounter with others. Inhabit a desire of its own, a desire that has become a spicy monster that has been difficult to emerge, recognize and invoke . The present work is about a search, a transit and a subordinate journey to be able to live the desire. It begins with an exploration of the depths and unconscious roots of a monster society and its gaze
