# Quality of life at work: Concept, torramients, Axes and Challenges.

A literature review was carried out in six databases: Virtual Library in Health (bv), ebo, Embase, Redalyc, SciELO and Scopus. In the search, the descriptors of "Quality of life at work" were used. And "Quality of working life" with their respective translations in Spanish, English and Portuguese. The filters used for the search were: free access articles (full text), in the period 2012-2022, in the languages mentioned. Boolean operators were used and "and" and "or" to achieve a greater scope of search. The results obtained were exported to Mendeley, from where the duplicates were removed. From the remaining articles the following information was extracted in an Excel sheet: search equation, year, country, authors, title, objective, concept of vt, measurement tool and description, the profession that carried out the study, profession that was subject to study, methodology, models or theories used, results and challenges for the quality of life at work.
