# USE OF DIAMONIC PHOSPHATE FOR THE IMPROVING OF RECYCLED CONCRET ARIDES AND THEIR ENVIRONMENTAL EVALUATION IN THE MANUFACTURING OF MIXING MIXES IN THE CITY OF MEDELLIN

This study focuses on the use of Recycled Concrete (RCDs) Arides treated by using Diamonic Phosphate (DAP) solution to improve their physical performance. The technical and environmental aspect of the implementation of improved RCDs is evaluated in the city of Medellin, using different scenarios that include source, transport and transformation of raw materials. The Life Cycle Analysis (ACV) implemented compares; the use of conventional structural mortars made from natural quarry aggregates with non-conventional mortars from improved and non-improved RCDs. Environmental impacts are determined in terms of CO2 emissions (kg CO 2 eq./m 3 mortar mixture) and non-renewable energy (MJ/m3 mortar mixture). The results show that in most of the proposed scenarios, mortar mixtures that include RCDs treated with DAP show significant improvements in environmental impacts measured against mortar mixtures with natural aggregates
