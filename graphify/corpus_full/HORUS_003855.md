# DENAMIC PARTIDISTANTS AND ELECTORALS IN PUBLIC CORPORATES OF THE NATIONAL AND SUBNATIONAL LEVEL IN COLOMBIA: BETWEEN COINCIDENCES AND DIFFERENCES.

The presentation is framed within the currents of the discipline that highlight the importance of avoiding the so-called nationalist bias in the analysis of political phenomena of subnational order, posing as hypothesis the heterogeneity existing between the different levels of political representation in which the State is configured and, therefore, the existence of dynamics specific to the subnational level, that cannot be read with the same lenses with which the elections, the political parties and the democracy itself are studied at the central level. With a methodology of case study and supported by abundant documentary and electoral information, the presentation puts this hypothesis to test in the analysis of the public corporations of national and subnational order in the case of a unitary state such as Colombian. To this end, it starts from characterizing the different levels, identifying the circumscriptions and the positions at stake in the Congress of the Republic (senado and Chamber of Representatives) and comparing them with those of departmental assemblies and municipal councils.
