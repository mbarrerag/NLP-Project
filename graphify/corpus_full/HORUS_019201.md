# Estimation of the discontinuities of the Manto Transition Zone in the North American North from the analysis of the P Wave Receptor Function

The transition zone of the mantle is limited by two seismic discontinuities to 410 and 660 km, _these are visualized in the north-western part of South America by the use of the technique of receiving function and seismological information, which has been recorded for more than 20 years by the national seismic network of Colombia. Important and spatially systematic variations were observed in the depths of the discontinuities. The average depth for the boundaries of the transition zone of the mantle are 412±5.3 and 671±5.9 with an average thickness is 258±6.5 km, which is close to the overall average value. The low coefficient of correlation of the depths indicates that the variation of these depths is significant. The increase in the thickness of the transition zone of the mantle in some areas is caused by the subduction of the Nazca plate and the Caribbean plaque under the funmeric plate. On the other hand, the thinning of the transition zone of the mantle under the Nazca plate is possibly due to the presence of the bridge of Malpelopematic.
