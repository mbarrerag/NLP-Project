# Solution for drying clothes in reduced spaces

This document deals with the development of a product that seeks to satisfy the need for drying clothing, derived from the process of washing the garments, in dwellings whose areas are considered reduced, taking as an initial point the context offered by the apartments of the city of Bogotá. In the first part, the task of washing clothes in the homes of the city of Bogotá is contextualized, starting with a historical approach, and the changes that this task has undergone. In the second part, the potential market that has a solution to this problem is contextualized.
