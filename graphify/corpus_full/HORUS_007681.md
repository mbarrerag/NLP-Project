# Contribution to the Chemical Study of the Bioactive Compounds of Streptomyces 5.1 against Plant Pathogen Colletatrichum gloeosporioides: A Chemical Approach to the Search for Natural Antifungal Agents

The avocado has an important nutritional value in our diet, obtaining great economic relevance in countries such as Mexico, Peru and Colombia. However, the fungus Colletatrichum gloeosporioides damages this fruit and causes losses in its production, generating a disease called anthracnosis. This work seeks to find a solution to these diseases, so it will be made use of natural antifungal agents (biocontrol), such as bacteria of the genus Streptomyces, specifically of the strain Streptomyces 5.1. which has been shown to be effective in the control of phytopathogen. The bacteria will be cultivated at laboratory scale (tens of liters), extraction of this crop, fractionation, purification of active metabolites, analysis by LC-MS/MS and characterization of molecules by 1D and 2D RM, in order to identify the compounds responsible for antifungal activity
