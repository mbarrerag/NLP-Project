# DENTAL AGE IN NON SYNDROMC CLEF LIP AND PALATE PATIENTS

Dental Age in Non Syndromc Clef Lip and Palate Patients Objectives: The aim of this study was to assess the differences in the estimated dental age (ED) and chronological age (CD) between boys and girls with and without non-syndromic cleft lip and palate (LPHNS) using Willems and Demirjian methods Methods: A case control analitic retrospective study was carried out, which compared panoramic radiographs of 146 subjects with LPHNS (aged 6 to 12 years) with 171 controls paired by age and sex using the Demirjian and Willems methods Results: The Demirjian and Willems methods overestimate ED in healthy Colombian children, however, the Demirjian overestimation was 0.84 years (SD: 1.18) being greater than the Willems overestimation (0.29 years (SD: 0.84)). No significant differences were observed between ED of cases and controls. Statistically significant differences were found between ED and EC for all groups with the Demirjian method, and only for the control and LPHNS unilateral right groups with the Willems method. It was found a high positive correlation between ED and EC for the two Demirjian (r=0.856) and Willems methods (r=0.849). -Similar dental development was found in patients with LPHNS and healthy patients according to Willems and Demirjian methods. -The Willems method is more accurate than Demirjian for the ED calculation in the Colombian population, however, it overestimates the age in healthy individuals by approximately 0.29 years
