# Extraction and analysis of apoplastic phenolic metabolites in root and carnation stem (Dianthus caryophyllus L)

This study describes the conditioning of some parameters for the efficient obtaining of apoplastic extracts enriched in polar compounds, mainly phenolic. This described workflow may even be applied to different plant species to be used in the particular or global analysis of metabolites in this peripheral extracellular space. To this end, using roots and stems of carnation (Dianthus cariophyllus L), different infiltration solutions were evaluated for the extraction of the apoplastic metabolites. The best result was achieved with the damping dissolution NaH2PO4-Na2HPO4 0.1 M pH 6.5/NaCl 50 mM, because the greatest amount of phenolic apoplastic metabolites was obtained, with the lowest contamination of intracellular compounds. The metabolites were separated by HPLC-DAD-ESI-MS, obtaining chromatinal profiles with reasonable quality parameters based on resolution, selectivity and number of theoretical dishes.
