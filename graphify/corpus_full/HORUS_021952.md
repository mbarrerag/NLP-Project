# The commercialization of the independent book in the bookstores of Medellín: Analysis of opportunities and difficulties.

This study analyses the current picture of the marketing of independent publishing books in eleven bookshops in Medellín and proposes support strategies for this process. The methodology used is qualitative and developed from an analysis of the book value chain that focuses on its marketing phase. The techniques used to gather information were documentary research and interviewing, 11 interviews were conducted with booksellers and 4 with publishers in the city. Based on the categories of the methodological tool, the opportunities and difficulties of marketing these books were analyzed and a series of recommendations and strategies were put into practice to strengthen their dissemination and sale. These libraries support the marketing process through media such as exhibition, cultural programming and digital media. However, publishers still have difficulties in distributing books, face strong competition from large publishing groups, and need to strengthen their communication with bookshops.
