# 3.9.3. NUCLEAR RECEPTORS: THERAPEUTIC ALTERNATIVE POINT IN DISEASE OF ALZHEIMER

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is a histologically characterized dementia by the presence and accumulation of protein aggregates in the brain. Although the AE is a pathology without clear therapy, it has recently been found that the activation of a type of nuclear receptors called LXR may be a potential opportunity for the development of a therapeutic strategy against the AE. The NCD receptors are dependent ligand transcription factors that are linked to their response elements in DNA and once active are involved in the transcription of genes related to lipid metabolism. The last years the Cellular Death group, using the murine model of EA (3xTg-AD), has investigated the potential effect of activation of these receptors and we have found that treatments with agonist molecules of these receptors lead to better performance and performance of 3xTg-AD animals being involved in behavioral tests by providers that evaluate memory and spatial learning dependent on the hippocampus.
