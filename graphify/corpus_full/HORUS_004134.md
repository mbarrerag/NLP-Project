# Planning policies and strategies in the configuration of the Bogotá-Sabana regional urban system of the 1960s

The work starts from the concept of city region to analyze the way in which the Bogotá-Sabana relationship was addressed in the 1960s through two paths: the first, from the administrative history of Bogotá and the Sabana, from the political-administrative decisions of the late nineteenth century to the creation of the Special District of Bogotá and, the second, around the studies and plans that advanced in the period by the district government and, on the other hand, the Regional Autonomous Corporation of the Bogotá Sabana and the valleys of Ubaté and Chiquinquirá (CAR), sometimes in a coordinated way, others in an independent way. The analysis focuses on the plans about water and the road network, both structural components of the regional urban system in a metropolitan area that has existed de facto although with difficulties for their formalization. .
