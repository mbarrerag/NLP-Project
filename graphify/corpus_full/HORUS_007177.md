# Strategies for teaching and learning radiology in undergraduate medical students: Narrative review of literature

this grade work has three main objectives: the first is the description of the academic activities related to the accompaniment to the subjects of Introduction to Diagnostic Images and Diagnostic Images for undergraduate medical students of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia of VI and VII semester, respectively, the evaluation of the functional and functional aspects of the study. The second is to identify the main strategies used for teaching radiology in undergraduate medical students worldwide; and the third is the formulation of recommendations for the adaptation and adoption of those strategies most relevant to implement in the Faculty of Medicine of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. the first part is developed through a narrative of experiences from the resident perspective, who acted as a follow-up tutor in the subjects described. The second is carried out through a structured review of the literature following the strategy PRISMA in the database of Medline, Embase, LILACS Eric and Google Scholar.
