# Adaptation of evidence-based clinical practice guide on tuberculosis infection control measures for health service providers

Tuberculosis, is perhaps one of the oldest diseases of humanity, its first findings date from approximately year 2400 BC (1), The WHO estimated in its latest report of tuberculosis control, that for the year 2021, a total of 10.6 million people were diagnosed and 1.6 million people died of respiratory disease. (2) Tuberculosis, is not only a public health problem, but also an occupational health problem, that is to say it can be directly related to the work, since health workers during the use of their work enter into continuous contact with patients with presumptive or confirmed tuberculosis, both cases of sensitive tuberculosis and cases of resistant tuberculosis (3).
