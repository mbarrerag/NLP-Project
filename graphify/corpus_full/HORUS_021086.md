# Divergence in the exploration of new products: approaches from individual and collective behavior in decision-making

The construction of peace in Colombia is also based on the existence of organizations that can generate sustainable economic value and wealth. Economic income for many organizations is determined by the quality of their value proposals and that these proposals are differentiated. New and better proposals of value for consumers are the result of efforts in design (predevelopment) of products (goods, services, processes, experiences, ideas, business models). Organizations seeking higher levels of performance, can exploit their resources or can explore new resources, capacities and products that involve better performance results (March, 1991; Lavie, et al., 2010). The exploration has been the basis of the dynamics of innovation in companies.
