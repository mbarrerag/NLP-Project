# FLEXIBILITY RESTRICTIONS OF THE COLOMBIAN ELECTRIC SYSTEM BY 2030

Multiple factors lead to a transformation of conventional electricity systems worldwide to allow the participation of non-conventional renewable energy sources in generation schemes. Some of these factors include: increasing global electricity consumption (IEA, 2021b), the participation of the electricity and heat generation sector in global greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions (IEA, 2021a), the scarcity of fossil fuels (Huber, Dimkova, & Hamacher, 2014) and the reduction of generation costs from renewable sources (Babatunde, Munda, & Hamam, 2020)
