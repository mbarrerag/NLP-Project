# A SHORT REVIEW OF COSMOLOGICAL BUILDINGS IN THE FIRST ORDER WITH INCLUSION OF BARIONICAL MATTERS FROM A EULERIAN PERSPECTIVE

In modern cosmology, the problem of the formation of large scale structure has been studied through different analytical and computational methods, and has become a cornerstone in astrophysics. The complexity of the equations that describe the evolution of small fluctuations of the field of matter with respect to the universe of Friedmann - Lemaître - Robertson - Walker (FLRW), what is commonly known as linearized gravitational disturbance theory, is a good idea to describe the problem. Specifically, the approach of sub-horizontous scale has allowed opening scenarios where semi-analytic tools play an important role in achieving a better understanding of how the significant structures in our universe have evolved and how the structure of the cosmic network is formed. In this sense, this type of techniques have allowed to make comparisons with extensive simulations and have provided a basis to contrast with high precision observations in this context.
