# Analysis of crossover designs with repeated measurements using generally estimated equations.

The experimental crossover designs are used extensively in medicine, agriculture and other areas of the biological sciences. Due to the characteristics of crossover design, each experimental unit has longitudinal observations and presence of drag effects in the response variable. In addition, in many scenarios it is not possible to leave a washout period between applications of different treatments, which generates problems when estimating the effects of the treatment without an adequate specification of the model. As a solution to the above, this thesis deals with crossover designs without washout period and with repeated measures. In the first place, a methodology for analysis of crossover designs is developed when the response variable is a Poisson count. For the estimation, generalized estimation equations are used assuming that there is no washout period and that the experimental unit was observed once per period. In addition, this methodology is easily extensible to any variable response that belongs to the exponential family. In the second place, the previous methodology is extended to cross-measures with repeated measures within each period, that is to say, when an experimental unit is observed more than once in each period.
