# EFFECT OF INTERCOMPHASE IN THE EFFECTIVE TAX OF TEMPLATE IN MARKETING SUCURSALS: A CASE STUDY

This paper analyses the effective tax rate and tax strategies of a multinational trading company during the years 2015 to 2019, specifically in relation to the use of intercompany expenditure for technical services and assistance for the development of its social object. To this end, the relationship between tax planning and the effective tax rate for financial decision-making is summarised
