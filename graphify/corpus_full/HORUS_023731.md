# THE LAW FOR THE GOVERNMENT OF SLAVES IN THE SANTANDER SYSTEM

In 1822, Francisco de Paula Santander, in charge of the power of the Republic of Colombia, put into effect the Royal Cédula of 1789 for the government of slaves. This decree was accompanied by a set of articles that reinterpreted the colonial document to take advantage of the spirit of control and surveillance that inspired the Cédula, in favor of the interests of a republican order that sought to consolidate itself in a context of political and warlike instability. This article presents this decree and briefly analyses the context of its publication
