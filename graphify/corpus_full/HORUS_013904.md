# Violence is not tradition: an approach to the discursive analysis of the reports of gender-based violence - VBG- occurring in the indigenous reserve of the Fuga de San José del Guaviare.

This grade study is a research that proposes to address, from the accounts of Tukano Oriental women belonging to the indigenous reserve La Fuga, located in San José del Guaviare, the effects of armed conflict on gender-based violence. The methodology that the research will have will be done from a feminist perspective of the analysis of the discourse for the reflection of violence
