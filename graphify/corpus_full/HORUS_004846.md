# THE MARCH OF 21 APRIL 2024 OR WHAT WE HOPED IN 2026 IF THE GOVERNMENT OF THE HISTORICAL COVENANT DOESN'T RECOGNIDE THE RUMBUS.

The article analyzes the mobilization of the opposition parties to the government of the Historical Pact carried out on Sunday, April 21, 2024. The analysis is carried out on the basis of the photographic record of the slogans of the mobilization, carried out by the author himself, and based on the notes made on different aspects of the mobilization, as a field diary, by the same author. Special attention is paid to some of the criticisms of the opposition to the Petro government, which the author considers worthy of consideration to improve governance and “recompose the course” in some policies that appear not to march or to be marching in an inappropriate way
