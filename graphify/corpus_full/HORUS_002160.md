# . RENAL CRONE DISEASE ADVANCED: SUSPEND DIALYSIS

The aging of the population is a challenge that we face all over the world, and providing effective palliative care to our elderly in its final moments is a task of great importance. This manual, carefully designed, is aimed at students and professionals in the health area who want to offer quality care at the end of life. Organized in Four Key Sections: Generalities: Start with a solid foundation, exploring the fundamental principles of palliative care in the context of care for the elderly. Approaching by Pathologies: Adtract specific knowledge of the most common medical conditions in the elderly population and how to provide them with palliative care effectively. Approaching by Symptoms: Mastering the management of symptoms and pain control in the elderly patient at the end of life, improving his well-being and quality of life. Practical Aspects: Offering tools and concrete strategies to implement palliative care in an effective way, including practical resources. Within its pages, you will find a multidisciplinary approach to communicating sensitive, pain and symptoms, ethical aspects, caring needed by the person, learning to adapt the palliative care, maintaining the individual needs and caring
