# ASSESSMENT OF THE SCIENTIFIC-CLINICAL DIMENSION OF CARE IN THE INSURANCE PERSONNEL*

The evaluation of the scientific-clinical dimension of nursing care based on the HANC model in the Intensive Care Unit (VDCC-UCI) allowed measuring the quality of care in the nursing care processes in the Intensive Care Unit. To describe the scientific-clinical dimension of nursing care in the Intensive Care Unit, comparing nurses from public and private institutions. A descriptive cross-sectional study carried out between July and December 2019. 186 nurses working in the Intensive Care Unit of public and private institutions in the country participated. Data were collected with the instrument of the scientific-clinical dimension. Participants, mostly women (74%) and men (48%), had a specialization (75%) and with professional experience more than ten years; the most common type of recruitment was the indefinite term. Statistically significant differences were found in 18 of the 46 items between the responses of nurses from public institutions compared to those from private institutions. The evaluation of the scientific-clinical dimension showed that some stages of the nursing process were completed by the most rigorously implemented public nursing institutions
