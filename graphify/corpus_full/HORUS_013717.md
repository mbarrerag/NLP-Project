# The metamorphosis of governance: experiences and lessons learned from the response to the COVID-19 in Bogotá D.C.

"For 37 months the health emergency was in force in the country, because of the pandemic by the COVÍD- 19, The response to this event consisted of the execution of a series of actions and strategies that developed on a large scale and in a short time. Undoubtedly, this experience left learnings for the actors involved. Values such as solidarity, cooperation, integrity, speed, perseverance and responsibility, were the pillars of the response to the pandemic in the city of Bogotá, D. C.."-- Contravest
