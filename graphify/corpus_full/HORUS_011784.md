# OPTIMIZATION OF STERILIZATION OF FOOD IN AUTOCLAVE

The problem of sterilization of prepackaged foods is modeled, which results in numerical solution of a differential equation in particular derivatives of parabolic type. Usually the equations thermal conductivity do not have an analytical solution, for except for some simplified cases with a simple boundary. Therefore, we used the variational calculus and finite element method using splines for discretization. Next, the finite-dimensional optimization problem was solved using MATLAB® package. Numerical experiments were compared with other authors and methods
