# Implementation of a set of hydrological models to study the impact of variability and climate change on water resources in moor ecosystems in Colombia. Case study, Chuza River, Chingaza Paramo

Throughout time, the hydrological modeling has been used for different purposes, among them, the assessment of the impact of climate change scenarios on the water resources of a basin. However, the usual has been to employ a single hydrological model, which can result in errors and biases due to its structural uncertainty. Therefore, in this study was carried out the assessment of the possible impacts of climate change in the Chuza river basin in the Chingaza para, part of one of the drinking water supply systems of the city of Bogotá. This assessment was carried out through the construction of a hydrological assembly to Granger Ramanathan in its variant C. The scenarios of climate change used were obtained from projections of two GCMs: CMCC-ESM2 and ECearth3-CC, and were constructed with the methodology delta change for the trajectories SSP2 4.5 and SSP5-8.5.
