# Identification of factors limiting the yield of maize production from observational data

Following the specific agriculture approach of the Site, this study identified the limiting factors of yield of climate, soil and management in maize production. The information was obtained from the observations of the farmers on the cultivation events, between 2013 and 2016 in Tolima, one of the regions with the highest maize production in Colombia. Using Random Forest techniques, factor analysis and cluster, the factors of climate, soil and management, related to the variation of crop yield were determined. Through regression analysis Random Forest, the factors of climate and soil explained 23% and 32% of the variation of yield, respectively. Relative humidity, mean temperature and precipitation were the most important climatic factors associated with the variation of yield, while slope and motting were the most important edaphic factors. Factorial analysis in combination with cluster techniques, allowed to establish groups with similar climatic and edaphic conditions. Among these groups, the agricultural practices that favor yields, such as mechanization, fertilisation and management of grain moisture, characterized the results
