# The criteria of scientificity in the social and human sciences: a case to be referred from the work of Karl Marx

The present research project seeks to show, from the research activity of Carlos Marx in the Grundrisse and in El capital, in the particular case, that the social and human sciences define their own criteria of scientificity with which they validate their research activity as scientific. This purpose starts from the identification of a fundamental problem in the field of social and human disciplines: that their apparent lack of demonstration of the scientific rigor of their research activity puts them in disadvantage vis-à-vis the academic community and the governmental and private sectors to participate in the organization of social institutions and the decision-making of social, cultural, citizen and other projects. This research problem is formulated in terms of questions: can it be demonstrated from the criteria of scientificity defined by Carlos Marx in the Doctorate and in El Capital and given the fulfilment of the requirements of his own concept of science and the current research. with the fundamental problems and challenges of the country, fulfilling with this the missionary character of the academy
