# Description of a home musicotherapy process aimed at protective factors of children facing cancer disease

The recovery and improvement in health cannot depend only on traditional medical treatments; they must be a collective effort involving both internal (emotional, cognitive) and external factors (family, environment) of the individual. This research aims to describe the influence of a home music therapy process on the protective factors of children with cancer, from a descriptive qualitative approach. Two case studies were carried out with a 7-year-old girl and a 9-year-old child, with interventions of 7 to 9 sessions of an hour. The applied music therapy was adjusted to the specific needs of the participants, using the four main methods of the music therapy. The data collection tools included interviews, field journals and analysis of the physical, verbal and musical reactions of the children, as well as the impact on the protective factors, especially the creativity, resilience and family support from them. The results show that the muscle therapy facilitates adaptation to destabilizing events, promoting resilience and encouraging creativity, which contributes to the emotional and physical well-being of the participants..
