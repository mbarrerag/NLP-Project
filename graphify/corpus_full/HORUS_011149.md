# Study of the interaction of combustion gases on refrigerant film in a gas turbine.

Gas turbines operate above the permissible temperature of materials. For this reason, a portion of air is extracted from the compressor to cool the blades externally using a method known as film cooling. Air is expelled through a series of holes forming a protective layer that reduces the temperature of the surface. In this study, the performance of the cooling film is numerically evaluated for three Reynolds numbers of the main flow and four speed ratios (blowing ratio or BR). A computational model based on finite volume discretization was used to solve an incomprehensible and transient flow on a NACA 4412 cascade profile. The model included several passive scalars to evaluate the conditions of adiabatic temperature and constant temperature on the alabic wall. The analyses show a discrepancy between these two approaches. For the condition of adiabatic temperature, the film effectiveness depends mainly on the jet path and recirculation zones. For the condition of constant temperature, the net layer of heat flow taken (net heat flow reduction or NHFR) varies in the limit of the volume of heat (the volume of water flow)
