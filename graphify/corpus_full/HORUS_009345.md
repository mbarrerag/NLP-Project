# Towards a systematic understanding of the transformation of social and economic systems, case-law of the COLOMBIAN APOLOGY

The Colombian beekeeping industry can be considered a sociotechnical system that contributes to food security and ecosystem services. This system is linked to heterogeneous agents, of which small producers are the main agents. The current conditions of the beekeeping industry in Colombia and the desired conditions reflect the need for transformative actions in the different elements of the system. This process of change is characterized by a high complexity, given the presence of nonlinear relationships and rules that are executed in parallel by a set of heterogeneous agents that interact contingently with each other. This requires a transformative innovation agenda applied to the apiculture sociotechnical system and robust methodologies that allow to study the complexity of the system. The analysis of the literature shows that there is still no representation of the process of transformation of a sociotechnical system in a longitudinal way that describes the performance and co-evolution of the different agents that make up it, in follow-up to a transformative innovation agenda. To address this problem, the conceptual, theoretical and methodological framework of transformational innovation and sustainable transitions in Colombia is used.
