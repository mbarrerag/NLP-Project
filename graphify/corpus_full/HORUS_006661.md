# Model for the operational management of the international physical export distribution applied to the exporting gyme in Bogota

This model is developed in response to the lack of continuity and sub-use of the export potential of the Bogota SMEs, mainly due to the lack of knowledge in the operational management of the International Physical Distribution (IFD). The research focuses on answering the question of which elements should contain an operational management model of the IFI in order for the Bogota SMEs to efficiently develop their export activity. The methodological design combines quantitative and qualitative approaches, using mixed methods to obtain an integral understanding. The study population consists of 17 exporting Bogota SMEs, using advisability sampling. The analysis of the results reveals deficiencies in key areas such as load analysis, packaging preparation, selection of the mode of transport and documentation management and above all the lack of a clear model as a reference framework. The proposed model is structured in three phases: planning, implementation of the Export Process for SMEs and continuous improvement. The model highlights the importance of the assertive communication, effective feedback in the companies and adaptability to the reality of each SME. population consist of 17 exporting SMES in Bogotá, using agreement sampling.
