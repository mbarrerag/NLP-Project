# Financialization and deindustrialisation in Colombia (2005-2019): an analysis of causal relations using VAR models

The financing and deindustrialisation are two economic phenomena present in some economies, however, its differential impact in developed and developing countries is highlighted. The lack of a clear methodological approach to the determinants of financing does not allow for an understanding beyond the theoretical, hence the need to understand the interactions between these phenomena. It is suggested that there is a relationship between financing and deindustrialisation in Colombia and it is argued that the growth of the financial sector in GDP and the decline of the industrial manufacturing sector support this relationship. The use of VAR models reveals causal relations between economic variables, underlining the interconnection between them and the performance of the industrial and financial sector. It is concluded that industrial production significantly impacts on the financial sector with a certain temporary lag
