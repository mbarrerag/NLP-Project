# Innovation proposal through the Strengthening of Spin Off-Agatec SAS

AGATEC SAS is a Spin Off type initiative within the CDT Méntor program, which has been evolving since mid-2021. The main object of Agatec SAS is to build evidence and frameworks of understanding to accompany the decision making processes, by applying scientific data analysis methodologies, adjusted to the specific needs of the clients, that allow generating value to the recommendations and continuous actions. Some of its fields of action are: (i) quantitative and qualitative modelling (ii) formulation and implementation of data analytics strategies for business intelligence, (iii) evaluation and monitoring of customized solutions. The vision of Agatec to 2025 is to be a benchmark in the market of prescriptive analytics, as providers of model implementation services, application development, optimization of decision-making processes, and other business intelligence processes, highly qualified by the firm's robustness and robustness of scientific solutions, with demonstrable results of risk management, cost reduction, increase in sales.
