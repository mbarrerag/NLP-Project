# Video-based fraud prevention model. An application of neural networks and statistical models.

Given a new typology of fraud, in which ATMs are blocked with the purpose of creating a distraction of users; which, allows the change of a card associated with a financial product and the capture of its key. This work, proposes a generalized linear model as a tool for forecasting the probability of occurrence of fraud, by structuring a database, extracted from videos through convolutional neural networks. These models estimate the presence of people, finding the starting point to carry out the tracking of each individual through siamese neural networks. The methodology, allows the construction of covariates according to the spatial-temporal location of people in the place of events, I assume that allows the identification of the linear model
