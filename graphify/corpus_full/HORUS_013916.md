# Autobiography, Spiritual Diaries and Epistolary of Sister Gertrudis Rosa de San José (1696 - 1765), a nun of the Monastery of Santa Inés de Montepulciano de Santa Fe de Bogotá. Transcription, critical editing and historical introduction

In the year 2014, circumstances that went through the process of disciplinary formation in the history of the author of this project, allowed him to know the pictorial collection and the historical archive of the community of Dominican nuns of the Monastery of Santa Inés de Montepulciano. Cenobio conventual character founded in Santa Fe in 1645. His first approaches to the community as a lay researcher with concerns about the role and agency of women during the Cologne, and especially of the nuns, as responsible for female self-governments in a world structured by and for men, has demarcated the work that continues to be constructed from a historical perspective with a cultural focus on the female monastic in the New Kingdom of Granada. In 2006, in one of the days of consultation carried out in the monastery, the first references to the unpublished writings of a religious woman considered exemplary that would have been written during the eighteenth century, as an archival epiphany of the Gercoba was given the opportunity to encounter a series of manuscripts by Sister Gertrudis Rosa de San José (1696-1765), which in the present tradition of the nuns between them Sorélica de San José and her in the in the in the latter. as a product of Catholic practices linked to mysticism and which, adapted to local and personal realities, acquired their own form and meaning. According to the above, this project will seek, in the first instance, to bring to light the unpublished writings written by Sister Gertrudis Rosa of San José, by organizing a critical edition, whose basis is the production of a version elaborated from the original for a modern reader. And, in the second measure, the drafting of an introductory text that allows contextualizing the reader in the temporal and spatial framework in which the scriptural production and the experience of the religious is framed, as well as the daily life and spiritual practices developed in community.
