# Sacha Inchi gel emulsions as a functional ingredient in the making of sausages

In this work the inclusion of oil and pressed cake as a natural source of Omega 3 essential fatty acids and Sacha Inchi proteins (Plukenetia volubilis) were investigated in the preparation of frankfurt type sausages. For this, gel emulsions (O/W) were elaborated with Sacha Inchi oil and soy protein, which was replaced partially by Sacha Inchi pressed cake in proportions of 10, 20, 30 and 40%. The technical and physical chemical properties of these emulsions were evaluated during 14 days of refrigerated storage. The gel emulsion with 20% of Sacha Inchi pressed cake which presented the best properties was used as a functional ingredient in a meat product type sausage as a partial substitute for the dorsal fat of pork (15, 25, 35%) to reduce the saturated fats of animal origin, and in the framework of the circular economy to take advantage of Sacha Inchi pressed cake as an alternative to use in the Sacha Inchi value chain.
