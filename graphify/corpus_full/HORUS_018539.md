# IMPACT ON MORTALITY WITH THE APPLICATION OF A GUIDE FOR SHOCK'S HANDLING IN PATIENTS WITH SERIOUS DENUTRITION IN SMALL FIVE YEARS IN A PEDIATIC INTENSIVE THERAPY UNITY IN BOLIVIA.

Malnutrition is a pathological state generated by a deficit of energy, proteins and micronutrients due to an imbalance between the requirement and intake of nutrients. For its management, the Bolivian Ministry of Health in conjunction with the World Health Organization (WHO) developed the Manual of Integral Management of Severe Acute Malnutrition with the objective of reducing mortality, including patients with shock. This study describes patients admitted to the pediatric intensive therapy unit (UTIP) who received management with and without WHO recommendations. Material and Methods: A series of cases of severe acute malnourished paediatric patients who required medical care in the UTIP from 2016 to 2019. Two subgroups were defined: Group A (concluded protocol), Group B (not met protocol). Data were collected on mortality, electrolyte disorders and water overload were compared between the groups.
