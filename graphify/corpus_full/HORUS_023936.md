# METHODI QUANTITATIVAE: A LUDIC WAY FOR LEARNING QUANTITATIVE METHODOLOGY IN PSYCHOLOGY

CONTEXT Throughout the sponsorship of the Call for Research on Pedagogical Innovation of the National University of Colombia (Bogotá Campus) in 2016, a video game was developed for mobile platforms (smartphones) in the Psychology program, for the Quantitative Methods in Psychology course. The game allows strengthen the learning process within the classroom regarding the main topics of the course, which are: Understanding the research process and methods for conducting descriptive, applied or experimental research. PURPOSE OR GOAL The objective of the paper is to detail the way in which the topics of the class were placed in the environment of a video game for educational purposes, in its pilot or initial version. This was done to make explicit the routes and strategies chosen by the development team to elaborate the levels, articulate the narrative, select the general aesthetics and give continuity to the ludic and learning experience in coherence with the parallel progress in the topics of the class. APPROACH To support the development and planning process, we critically adopted game-based learning, which has the following implications as a learning system: 1. To define the information about the learner or user model, since it is possible to make the record of information about the knowledge or competence requirements of the students when starting the game; 2. To propose the pedagogy to guide the activities and problems to be solved that allow making decisions regarding the student's progress in the game, update his/her profile and make the adjustment according to the student's responses. ACTUAL OR ANTICIPATED OUTCOMES The results of this project detail the strategies used by the development team to transfer the class subjects to the video game reality. It also shows the way the subjects are articulated like a whole throughout the narrative and aesthetic, as well as the character's score system and levels. Furthermore, the limitations that the pilot version currently has the main learnings about the first attempt with the class and the challenges that it should be passed to end the final version are shown CONCLUSIONS/RECOMMENDATIONS/SUMMARY It concludes with the advantages of using the learning approach based on video games, for the construction of an educational video game. It also includes an overview of the main strengths of the specific strategies implemented in each of the levels, as well as recommendations for similar projects that seek to develop specific video games for teaching research methodology in areas of social and health sciences. That is done by recognizing the need for additional efforts to complete the development and implementation of the video game within a class oriented by active pedagogies
