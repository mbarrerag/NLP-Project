# Single-step and sequential pretreatments for sustainable lignocellulosic biorefineries

The pretreatments have been classified as the first link in the design of biorefineries, since it facilitates the fractionation of biomass for the production of platform products, affecting the reaction and downstream stages in the future. However, many studies report the use and improvement of simple stage pretreatments for the valorization of a subcurrent, preventing the integral valorization of biomass. Therefore, the sequential pretreatments are seen as an alternative for the selective fractionation of the components of the biomass for future integral valorisations. This work focuses on analyzing the performance of the sequential pretreatments in the sustainability of biorefineries through experimental work and simulation. Subsequent to a preliminary heuristic analysis of both simple and sequential stages, different proposals of sequential combinations were evaluated during the first stage and the higher fertilisation of the platform of cellulose, hemicel and ligin. It was determined that the combination of acid dilute-oxidated with wet air (DA-WAO) hydrolysed the 79% of hemielulose during the first stage and the higher 89 % of the ligin, with a second stage of the pre-lutive and ligin.
