# OXIGENATION THERAPY WITH HIGH FLOW SINGLE SINGLE STUDY IN PATIENTS WITH A CONTINUED RESPIRATORY INSUCIENCE BY COVID-19, A COHORT STUDIO

Bloodstream infections (STIs) in patients with COVID-19 infection who are admitted to the intensive care unit (ICU) are frequent. Determine the impact of STIs on hospital mortality of patients with SAR-CoV-2 infection admitted to ICU. A retrospective cohort study was conducted in adult patients with confirmed or suspected COVID-19 infection who required hospital admission and management in ICU between 29 March 2020 and 19 December 2020. Based on the identification of the ITS two mutually exclusive exposure groups were defined. The ITS was defined as bacterial or fungal microbiological isolation by haemocultivation.
