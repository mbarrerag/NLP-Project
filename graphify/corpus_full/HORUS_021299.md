# PHITO-CHEMIC STUDIES AND ASSESSMENT OF ANTI-PRESIVE ENTITLE TYPE ACTIVITY OF Methane EXTRACT AND FRACTIONS FROM Hypericum mexicanum L., IN MURINO MODEL.

Depressive disorders are frequent mental disorders in the population. Around 280 million people on the planet present depression. These disorders vary in temporality and causes, symptoms such as sad, empty or irritable mood are commonly presented, along with somatic or cognitive changes which affect the functional capacities of the individual, bearing in mind that most people from countries with low to medium income are not treated or not correctly diagnosed. Similarly, many of the medicines used in the therapy of depression are not accessible, have adverse effects or fail to solve the symptoms in the affected population. The study of medicinal plants arises as an alternative for the treatment of these disorders, species of the genus Hypericum, mainly H. perforatum, have been used as antidepressants and reducers of anxiety. Therefore, it is expected that the chemical and pharmacological study of species belonging to this genus will allow me to expand the knowledge of the plant species native to Colombia and may be useful for the treatment of this pathology.
