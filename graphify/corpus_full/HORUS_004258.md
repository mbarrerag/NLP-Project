# BIOETICS IN PATIENTS WITH VENTRICULATE ASSISTANCE DEVICES LEFT ABOARDED FROM PALIATIVE CAREERS: SYSTEMATIC LITERATURA REVIEW*

The left ventricular assistance devices have become major allies in the treatment of advanced heart failure, since they have as their main objective with-troll symptoms generated by the progression of the disease; depending on the therapeutic objective, these mechanical elements can serve as bridge therapy to transplantation, bridging therapy to recovery/decision or as definitive therapy. Patients with diagnosis of cardiac failure who are in therapy with these devices go through multiple situations, processes and bioethical issues, where the support of palliative care plays a fundamental role. With the objective of determining the approach of palliative care to the bioethical issues present in the process of care of patients with left ventricular assistance device, a systematic bibliographic search was carried out, in three databases, PubMed, Google Academic and Embase; 13 publications were included, of which emerged 3 categories: expectation versus therapy, symptoms and need for palliative care.
