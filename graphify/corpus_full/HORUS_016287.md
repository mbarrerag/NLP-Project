# INTRODUCTION. RELATING THE ANTROPOLOGY OF CHRISTIANIS.

This text introduces anthropological studies on the symbolic, practical and ideological forms involved in the cultural appropriation and recreation of Christianity as a dynamic field of studies. Specifically, from Latin America, the question of the indigenous adaptation of the Christian message and praxis was central to anthropological studies on religious syncretism in the regions of the Central Andes, and the South American Grand Chaco. Likewise, in Latin America, the reforms promoted within Catholicism after the Second Vatican Council (1962-1965) and the Conference of Bishops of Medellín (1968) were contemporary to the sustained growth of evangelical and Pentecostal churches in an inclusive where the question for Latin American modernization and transformation was central.
