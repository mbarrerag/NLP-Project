# MODELATION OF THE BIOACULATION OF TOXIN IN FOOD CHANGES

This paper addresses the problem of ciguatera poisoning, an unpredictable condition linked to the consumption of fish contaminated with ciguatoxins. The difficulty in detecting these poisonings lies in the absence of visible symptoms in fish carrying the toxin. In order to understand the bioaccumulation of ciguatoxins in the marine food chain, a mathematical model based on Lotka-Volterra equations and the laws of mass balance to describe the transfer of the toxin between species by the consumption of prey is proposed. The ecological and mathematical validity of the model is shown and stability analysis is carried out to examine the behavior of the system.
