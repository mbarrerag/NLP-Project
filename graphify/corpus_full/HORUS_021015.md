# A chair and the gif

A chair and the gif is a project of research-artistic creation result of the study process in the Mastery that I have been able to explore around the paper, the different materialities of the paper have allowed me to redo the chairs that surround me, especially that which my father inherited at the time of birth; by the process of rebuilding the chairs that surround me only with the material of the paper explored the various utilities of the chair by completely cancelling them.This project allows to think about the futility of the objects that are made in mass and only for utilitarian purposes, stripping them of the usefulness but preserving the form, at the moment of decalcar an object in paper is actually the object something useful or only visible?Also giving a new way to the visuality with which you can get to see an object that does not really work, since the chair is one of the most common elements of the artistic process, in this project I have to think that the visuality of the chair is now even by means of the rotoscopy that the method of animation picture by picture, calcating virtually videos or photographs already existing, I have explored the various parts of my artistic process, in this process, in this project I am
