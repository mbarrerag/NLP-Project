# INFLUENCE OF THE GENDER VARIABLE IN THE ACADEMIC DESIGN OF PREGRAPHED STUDENTS OF THE MECHANICAL ENGINEERING AND ELECTRIC ENGINERY PROGRAMMES OF ANTIOCHY UNIVERSITY

Despite advances in gender equality in higher education, a significant gender gap persists in the field of STEM areas, reflected in a small proportion of women entering and continuing careers in this field. This minority status in academic contexts has consequences throughout the academic and professional trajectories of students, and contributes to maintaining gender prejudices and stereotypes. To analyse this phenomenon, a study is presented on the academic performance of women and men students of Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering at the University of Antioquia. This study follows up on academic performance, in terms of averages of qualifications per period; this, in turn, relates to non-academic co-variables such as gender, socioeconomic strata, region and school of origin, for a period of analysis between 2014-2020. The results allow for the visibility of a difference between men and women from their enrolment and admission to their academic performance in each academic period, and this, in general, is better for women, especially during the first semesters after admission, since this difference in academic performance is higher than in the previous academic performance
