# Analysis of the strengthening of ecosystem services associated with the implementation of an ecological restoration in a dry tropical forest in Cali, Valle del Cauca

The anthropic intervention in sites of ecological interest as it is the Parcelación Chorro de Plata has generated a detriment of the ecosystem services (SE). This is why this research has the objective to determine the strengthening in the ecosystem services associated with the implementation of the ecological restoration process in the dry tropical forest, located in the Regional District of Integrated Management Pance (DRMI). For this purpose, the characterization of the study area during 2019, where monitoring of fauna was carried out through observation and capture in field, plots for characterization of flora (fustal, latizal and brincal), were finally taken 120 soil samples, which were sent to the laboratory for its chemical physical analysis, these characteristics influenced the selection of the sites to carry out ecological restoration (RE), Otoño, Tanque, Antena, Administración y Charco. The results showed that the area presented extensive coverage of Browning Helech, large number of ant nests and soils with acidity conditions, these factors were taken into account to choose the plant coverages that could give rise to the primary succession process.
