# PLANNING OF PHYSICAL INFRASTRUCTURE IN UNIVERSITARY CAMPUS

The book involves a analysis of the historical context related to the growth of the Amazonia headquarters: the diagnosis and valuation of the physical infrastructures of that headquarters is also elaborated, the perspective of future development and culminates with conclusions and recommendations aimed at achieving the Plan of the Plan, which can be replicated on other campuses
