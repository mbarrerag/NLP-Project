# VALIDATION OF THE FAMILY CAREER IN ADMINISTRATION OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS

To design an instrument to measure the adoption of the role of family caregivers in the administration of drugs in people with chronic disease and establish their validity both in terms of content and facial. Methodological study that included three phases: instrument design, determination of content validity with 9 experts and determination of facial validity through cognitive interviews with 14 caregivers and judgments of 30 caregivers of adults with chronic diseases. A questionnaire consisting of 32 items was obtained, distributed in 3 dimensions called tasks, organization and response to the role. The instrument presented an adequate content validity since all items exceeded the minimum CVI of 0.58, while for the total scale it was 0.97. For its part, facial validity showed that the clarity was 99.6%, the understanding was 98.4% and the accuracy was 96.9%. The instrument Adoption of the role in the administration of medicines measures the construct and the items that shape it properly represent its domains. Key words: Medications; Chronic disease; Family caregiver; Instrument; Validation study of the role in the administration of medicines measures of construction and the items that it contains
