# Methodological proposal using REMSI strategy in a cinematic theme in grade 10

This paper aims to create and evaluate a methodological proposal to teach a topic of the area of kinematics, for tenth grade based on the REMSI strategy (REality, Modeling and Simulation) and oriented towards active learning. To determine the topic to be addressed, the collaboration of experts in the area was counted, who participated in a survey that resulted in the selection of the semi-parabolic movement. Following, specific stages focused on the development of scientific and social competences were designed. Using the REMSI strategy, the conceptual gain of the students was assessed through the Hake method. This methodology allows students to interact with reality through experiments, modeling concepts using tools such as Excel to develop equations and graphs and finally employ simulations to deepen the topic and extract meaningful conclusions . The objective of this work is creating and evaluating a methodological proposal to teach in order to be active for tenmatics based on REMSI’s (Reality, Modeling and SImmulation) strategy and oriented to determine the initial design and strategy to be active in the area for tenthgrade based on the environment (RESI)
