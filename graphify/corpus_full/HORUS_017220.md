# Relationship of distractions, sociodemographic and contextual characteristics to safe practices of injections during clinical practice of nurses

To analyze the relationship between distractions, sociodemographic and contextual characteristics with the realization of safe practices of injections performed by the nurse during the preparation and administration of the drugs in the hospitalization and intensive care services of adults. Quantitative, cross-sectional and correlational study that used structured observation guided by checklists, with a non-probabilistic sampling regarding 446 injection practices.
