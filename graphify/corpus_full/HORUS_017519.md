# Analysis of the dynamics of innovation in a company of the agroindustrial cluster of sugar cane in the geographical valley of the Cauca River (Colombia), by implementing the Hyper 666 model.

An analysis of the dynamics of innovation was carried out in a company of the agro-industrial cluster of sugar cane in the geographical valley of the Cauca River (Colombia) through the implementation of the complex model of innovation Hiper 666 for which a technology base company of the cluster was selected, as well as an innovation of relevance to the organization. The processes of innovation of the company were characterized, the capacities of the organization were identified for the development of the innovation process and the potential of the company was determined for the relationship in knowledge networks. The methodology used is the case study with an applied research approach. A semistructure interview was applied to the key representatives of the organization in the innovation processes, which included questions associated with each of the three dimensions raised in the Hyper 666 model. The results of the study indicate that the company has a good capacity for the development of the innovative processes which is supported mainly in the knowledge, experience and creative and invention capacity of the working team. During the innovation process, informal networks of collaboration with customers, suppliers and some agents of the knowledge market and the local industrial mesh are suggested
