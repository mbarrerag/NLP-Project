# Evolution of the morphodynamics of the Magdalena River River system during the Holocene in the Magdalena Middle Valley basin between Barrancabermeja and Aguachica, Colombia

The Magdalena river, the main fluvial system of Colombia, exhibits a behavior of river braided from the Upper Valley of Magdalena (VSM), however, changes its morphodynamic model in the Middle Valley of Magdalena (VMM), so that between the populations of Barrancabermeja and Aguachica presents intercalate sections of the braided and anastomous system. Finally, to the north of Aguachica it is constituted as an an anastomous river. The possible explanations given to this phenomenon suggest a close association between the rate of sinking of the basin with the rate of sedimentation and fluctuations of the level of the river product of deglaciations in Holocene.
