# Deforestation, climate change and economic activity in Colombia: an analysis at the subnational level

In the last five years, the Colombian government has set itself the goal of boosting the economic growth of the country, while ensuring the sustainable use of natural capital and social inclusion. This goal, however, could be diminished by the economic structure of the country, determined in turn by economic policy. In this research we are interested in determining the long-term causality relationship that for Colombia, economic activity, foreign direct investment and greenhouse gas emissions (CO2). In addition, it is proposed to analyze the possible impact of the commercial activity associated with free trade agreements on deforestation in the departments of Colombia. On the other hand, taking into account the double-track relationship that may exist between economic activity and the environment, it is also proposed to study the impact of climate variability on the economic performance of departments in Colombia. All these analyses will be done through a quantitative approach, using statistical data available in different sources. These data will be analyzed through different statistical and econometric techniques
