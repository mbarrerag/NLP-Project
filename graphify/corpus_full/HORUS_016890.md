# Prevalence of SARS-CoV-2/COVID infection in health care staff of the National Cancer Institute of Colombia.

Introduction The SARS-CoV-2 is the new coronavirus that causes the COVID-19, declared pandemic by the World Health Organization (WHO). Its rapid spread created the need to develop molecular diagnostic tests (PCR, antigen), genomic and serological background that allowed to quickly identify the cases to mitigate the transmission and take the necessary measures.
