# Space priority for sustainable management of Moriche palm in northern and eastern Colombian Amazonian

The sustainable management of plant species seeks to make use of the natural vegetation coverage without affecting its composition, structure or function. Identifying potential areas for productive projects based on native species is essential for the conservation of ecosystems. Therefore, the study of the ecological distribution of species is a priority, especially in the Amazon region. The Species Distribution Models are useful tools that have served to identify areas with high species richness, evaluate impacts of climate change and analyze the distribution of native and invasive species, among other applications. In this context, the Moriche palm, is a species of high importance in the Amazon thanks to its various uses and the ecological role it fulfils. For this reason, the aim of this research is to identify priority areas for the sustainable use of palm Moriche in the jurisdiction of the Corporation for the Sustainable Development of the North and the Amazonian East using the model of potential distribution and spatial variables . model and spatial variables
