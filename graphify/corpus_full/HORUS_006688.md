# Molecular analysis of the GLA gene inactivation pattern and its contribution to phenotype in a sample of Colombian women with Fabry disease, using methylation-based trials

The aim of this study was to analyze the pattern of inactivation of the GLA gene, through methylation-based trials, and its contribution to the phenotype in a sample of Colombian women with Fabry EF disease. A heterogeneous severity of phenotype is reported in women from family cases, being able to present all symptoms and documented signs in men with EF; associated with a marked deterioration in subjective perception of quality of life. Likewise, a heterogeneous methylation of the CpG positions is reported in the region analyzed from the GLA gene. Based on the results of the HUMARA study, more than 60% of the samples with random inactivation were observed, and no statistical relationship was found between the pattern of ICX of the samples analyzed and the phenotypic severity. In conclusion, EF affects in all cases the physical, psychological and quality of life of women heterozygotes for pathogenic variant in the GLA gene. with random inactivation, and no statistical relation was found between the ICX pattern of the samples analyzed and phenotypic seriality. Conclusion, in all cases, FD effects the physical and psychological health and quality of life of women heterozygous for the pathogenic variant in the GLA gene; and nonstatistical association was found between the security of the systemic phenotype in FD and the ICX pattern of the samples analyzed
