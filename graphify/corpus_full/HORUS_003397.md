# CYCHO-INRICATED COCO MILK WITH EXTRACT OF CHONTADURO EPICARPY: ASSESSMENT OF SENSORIAL PARAMETERS AND COLOR ATTRIBUTS

The plant by-products can be a source of natural additives in the food, cosmetic and pharmaceutical industry, the agro-industrial valuation of these resources would allow to give fulfillment to the Sustainable Development Goals. Therefore, the objective of this research was to evaluate the sensory parameters and the attributes of color in coconut milk enriched with epicarp extract of chontaduro (Bactris gasipaes). From the lipid extract of chontaduro epicarpium, an ultrasound-assisted O/W emulsion was developed, using soy lecithin, xantana gum, and water.
