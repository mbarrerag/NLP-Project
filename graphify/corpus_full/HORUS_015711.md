# Strategies to encourage demand response in Colombia by demand aggregators based on analysis of international energy markets.

In Colombia there is currently a single demand response programme (RD), which is the voluntary disconnectible demand (VDD). This final master's degree work analyses RD programs in Colombia and in selected countries (France, USA, South Korea and Australia), identifies their main programs and the different participants in the RD mechanisms that take the role of aggregator, the programs were classified into three types: Complementary Services, Energy Market and Emergency or Capacity RD. This way, the status of the implementation of RD programs in the selected countries is identified and a comparison is made with Colombia, allowing to elaborate an overview of the limitations that are presented in the Colombian context to understand the incorporation of RD programs, and from there to provide strategies to encourage the creation of new RD programs from a general perspective and from the demand aggregator
