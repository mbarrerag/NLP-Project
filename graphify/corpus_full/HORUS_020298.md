# The incorporation of criptomonedas companies in the public interest: possibility of financial fraud

As of 2009, the so-called virtual currencies arise, which present a great boom in their use, often without considering its relationship with the possibility of financial fraud, taking into consideration that these are recent ways of carrying out operations that generate deception to the public, merchants and the market that is not familiar with the so-called virtual currencies. The objective of this document is to carry out a literature review in which the relationship between financial fraud that the administration of companies could face due to the use of cryptocurrencies is established, together with evaluating whether the forms of recognition of operations with crypto-assets and crypto-liabilities in accordance with international financial reporting standards, allows the identification of fraud. The exchange of goods or services for cryptocurrencies and purchase / sale operations for currencies that do not meet the characteristics of currency, such as the dollar or the euro, generate the possibility of using cryptocurrencies in fraudulent and illegal activities. This possibility becomes relevant with the absence of regulation in some countries, among which is Colombia, that allows identifying the activities classified as outside the legal frameworks or the transactions that require supervision of a state agency because they frame a higher level of complexity and consequently risk. As part of the reviewed documentation, some forms of fraud that have existed for many years were identified, such as Ponzi schemes, pump and dump, digital currency price manipulation, among others, which migrated to the digital world with some modifications that includes the use of social networks, but with similar foundations. The analysis additionally considers entities that have linked cryptocurrencies in their operation and the ability to react to adapt their internal procedures, as well as the Corporate Governance policies to accept or reject transactions with digital currencies, with the aim of proactively responding to this type of transactions that are currently booming and have uncontrolled growth that increases the risk of fraud. The analysis was carried out through a literature review of articles published in books and magazines that deal with the use of cryptocurrencies by organizations and their possible relationship with financial fraud, derived from the absence of regulation
