# CARE: TRAINING CITIZENSHIP CAPACITIES FOR THE EXERCISE OF CARE AS A RIGHT

The care is a new position of citizenship that claims and endorses the culture of care, that which women have practiced for millennia, is a citizenship that no longer favours self-sufficient beings, but that is based on the vulnerability existing in the reality of human being and care.The care according to Blanca Rodríguez (3) is based on care as an idea that includes the right to care for us and to be cared for, to take care of in dignified conditions and of course not to take care of.The right to put life in the center and to claim the intimate relationship of this with the environment. A virtual chair for the formation of citizen capacities for 250 students of the National University of Colombia with the aim of recognizing care as a citizen right linked to the daily actions of the life of the people, as well as in those that do professional care, the recognition of certain daily actions for the preservation of life as a public good and the integration of care as a citizen right in the problems and elements of professional work. This through an innovative pedagogical proposal from an active approach and professional that was based on the ideas and the construction of the first generation. education, environmental rights, economic rights, as well as the need for recognition of unpaid care work and the importance of the distribution of care within households to generate awareness first of the effective enjoyment of individuals and individuals as a right that implies interpersonal relations and then collective consciences that allow the guarantee of this right in society. Limitations: The time of pedagogical work sessions proved short for the exploration of affirmative actions and personal experiences around care
