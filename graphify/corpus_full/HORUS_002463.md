# STUDIES OF SAFETY AND HEALTH IN WORK THAT SURVEY SCALES: BIBLE ANALYSIS 2018-2022

This chapter presents a bibliometric analysis on the use of scales in research on safety and health in nursing work, based on articles published between 2018 and 2022 in Scopus. 792 studies are identified, 39 of them focused on nursing. The United States leads in publications, followed by China and Australia. The predominant topics include burnout, mental health, coping, resilience and biosecurity, with special emphasis on the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. The most influential institutions are the Harvard School of Medicine and Johns Hopkins University. The most used scales evaluate stress, anxiety, post-traumatic symptoms, fatigue and organizational well-being, highlighting Maslach Burnout Inventory, PHQ-9 and DASS-21. An increase in scientific production is observed in 2020 and 2021, reflecting the effect of the pandemic on research. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health is the most published on SST. The most cited articles come from China, United States and Canada. In conclusion, the study evidence of future research and scale trends and analysis is presented
