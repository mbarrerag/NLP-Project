# On the Study of Dynamics in Differential Equations: Stability and Controllability of Solutions

The area of Partial Differential Equations EDP's, in particular, gathers working forces for the study of theoretical and physical models governed by equations containing derivatives in various variables. The main objective is to address in a monographic manner the topics referring to good approach and properties of controlability and stability of differential equations, as a result of previously published research, ongoing research or interest proposed by researchers of wide trajectory in the field. The main objective is to generate a Master's thesis of the Master's Degree in Sciences program - Applied Mathematics and a final degree work of the undergraduate program in Mathematics, as well as to strengthen the formative processes of the members of the semillero Analysis and EDPs that has undergraduate, master's and doctoral students. Finally, and in parallel, progress will be made in some research projects with academic peers in France and Brazil
