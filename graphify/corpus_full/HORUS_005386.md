# NATIONAL PLAN FOR CULTURE 2024-2038

The diversity of life in Colombia is revealed in the biological richness that positions us as one of the most biodiverse nations in the world, and in the immense cultural wealth that we find throughout the national territory and that manifests itself in the plurality of communities and populations, native languages and creoles, living cultural heritages, landscapes and cultural practices linked to their environments.The National Culture Plan 2024-2038 proposes to expand the views of national cultural policies so that they are separated from anthropocentric conceptions and approach holistic, systemic and complex perspectives, in which human development and sustainable development are fundamental for the care of the diversity of life. It also seeks to become a guide and tool for the design, management, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of national, departmental, district and municipal policies and projects in the cultural sector and those actions with which competence is shared and which have an impact on the guarantee of the cultural rights of the inhabitants of our country
