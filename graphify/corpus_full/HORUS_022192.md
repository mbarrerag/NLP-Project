# The exercise of preventive and concomitant fiscal control in the face of the prohibition of co-administration in public administration.

This research paper seeks to address the main characteristics of the exercise of fiscal control from the 1991 Constitution, as a subsequent and selective control, currently in force; among them, the so-called warning function, provided for in the original wording of article 5 paragraph 7 of Decree Law 267 of 2000, which empowered the Office of the Comptroller General of the Republic to “Warn on operations or processes under way to foresee serious risks that compromise the public assets and exercise subsequent control over the facts so identified” but which, as a result of being declared unenforceable by the Constitutional Court. Subsequently, we will propose a critical study on the new categories of preventive and concomitant fiscal control exercised through the new warning function, but now provided for through Legislative Act 004 of 2019. The warning function and the new preventive and concomitant tax control system, require a review of those categories now of constitutional rank, and their compatibility with the subsequent and selective fiscal control enshrined in the same constitutional provision, as it allows us to review from a constitutional point of view, if the legislative act was necessary to improve the preventive management of the Comptroller General of the Republic, against the monitoring of the fiscal management of the State
