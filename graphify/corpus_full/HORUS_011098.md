# PUBLIC FOOD PROGRAMMES AS A DEVELOPMENT MECHANISM IN COLOMBIA: AN INTEGRATIVE REVIEW OF THEIR POTENTIALITIES IN CONNECTION WITH SDG CHALLENGES.

The objective of this chapter is to highlight the potential of public food programmes to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals, SDGs. The chapter is presented in four sections. The first section identifies the opportunities and challenges of public food procurement. The second section presents a brief historical account of the transformations in the regulatory frameworks of the programmes and how these affected the public food purchase. The third section identifies the potentialities of this mechanism and presents recommendations of public policy. Finally, the main conclusions of the research are presented
