# Analysis of Colombia's National Circular Economy Strategy from two models

Abstract The purpose of this article is to show the results of the review of the scope and limitations of the National Circular Economy Strategy (ENEC) of the Colombian government based on the analytical models of the policy mix and the twelve circular strategies. The main results of this study are, firstly, that ENEC mobilizes some typical instruments of a public policy to contribute to the transition from a linear economy to a circular economy. Secondly, ENEC focuses on industrial sectors in general and the last link in the production chain. Thirdly, while the circular economy has the potential to link policies related to the prevention of the use of resources and consumer behavior, these preventive dimensions are still incipient in the version of ENEC analyzed. The articulation of policies of the enterprise-state-academy sector with small producers and informals, as well as with rural and urban communities, is necessary to achieve a transition to a more inclusive and inclusive circular economy
