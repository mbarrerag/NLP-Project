# PROPOSED STRENGTHENING WATER MANAGEMENT WITH COMMUNITY ADUCTS

The project STRENGTHENING THE MANAGEMENT OF THE WATER WITH COMMUNITY ACUEDUCTOS will characterize experiences and practices of community management for the supply of drinking water and for multiple uses in the departments of Cundinamarca, Risaralda and Caldas, promoting dialogue of knowledge between communities and other local actors through spaces for the exchange of knowledge.The purpose is to contribute to a better access of the rural population to clean water by 2030, in harmony with the objective of Sustainable Development number six _x0096_ SDG 6. The project STRENGTHENING OF THE WATER GOSTICTION WITH COMMUNITY ACUDUCTS is to contribute to the collaboration between the research groups of the Department of Social Work of the Faculty of Human Sciences of the National University of Colombia Headquarters Bogotá, and the Center of Thought in Culture, Territory and Management _x0096_ CPCG of the National University of Colombia - Manizales, and has strategic allies in the territory (e.g., ASATECUNDI, AMAC, FACORIS), promoting the active participation of social organizations of the aqueducts associated with the community. (ii) Diagnosis and proposal to strengthen community management in three (3) social organizations that supply water for multiple uses in the department of Cundinamarca, with field visit identifying cultural aspects and environmental education, socio-economic analysis of quotas or tariffs, and possible social, management and investment projects. (iii) Preparation of a document with recommendations for municipal, departmental and national public policies that contribute to closing gaps in access to water for areas with a coffee vocation in Colombia, based on secondary information and the results of workshops and field visits. (iv) Preparation of a document with guidelines and learning guide for strengthening water management with community aqueducts
