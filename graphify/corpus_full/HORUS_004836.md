# DECODING MPOX: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND META-ANALYSIS OF THE TRANSMISSION AND SEVERITY PARAMETERS OF THE 2022 - 2023 GLOBAL OUTBREAK

Background In 2022-2023, the largest outbreak of mpox in history occurred. Epidemiologic parameters of mpox epidemiology and transmission can serve as input to mathematical models to better understand mpox outbreaks. In 2022-2023, the largest outbreak of mpox in history occurred. Epidemiologic parameters of mpox epidemiology and transmission can serve as input to mathematical models to better understand mpox outbreaks. Methods Systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies in MEDLINE, EMBASE and other sources up to September 2023. To assess risk of bias, we used the Joanna Briggs Institute Critical Appraisal for observational series and a custom-designed quality assessment questionnaire for mathematical models. Meta-analysis was performed using a random-effects model. Results We found an incubation period of 7.56 (95% CI 7.13-8.02) days, a serial interval of 8.25 (95% CI 6.45-10.55) days, a generation time of 10.83 (95% CI 8.11-14.46) days, a CFR of 0.03% (95% CI 0.00%-0.24%). The basic reproduction number varied between 0.19 and 3.02, and the effective reproduction number between 1.18 and 3.74, showing a peak between late August and early September, 2022 in all locations. Conclusions Our study provides a comprehensive review of mpox epidemiological parameters related to transmission and disease severity; crucially a larger serial interval compared to the incubation period suggests most of the mpox transmission occurs after symptom onset. These results may help the development of mathematical models to assess the impact of interventions for future outbreaks and improve epidemic responses
