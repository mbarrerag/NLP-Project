# _x0093_Selective hydrophobization of Apatito for phosphor foaming flotation and subsequent agricultural application_x0094_

The design of a methodology for recovering apatito from phosphate rock ore is proposed as an opportunity in engineering, innovation, with emphasis on principles of microflotation and foaming float as a more sustainable alternative in the processing of minerals, in order to characterize surface chemistry properties for phosphorus, which is vital for the agricultural industry for the production of fertilizers, assuming the multiple challenges in Colombia for the phosphate rock, such as the low concentration of the mineral in deposits and the presence of impurities such as silica and clay significantly increase the processing costs. Also, the current situation in the country where new disjunctures are required to supply the growing demand for food by the population and much of the raw material to produce agricultural inputs must be imported at high costs from countries such as Ukraine and Russia that are currently in conflict, which increases costs and generates delays in agricultural development
