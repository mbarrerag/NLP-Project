# TRADE RELATIONS AND INNOVATION IMPACT

The purpose of this article is to identify the fields of research on commercial relations and their impact on innovation in the business area. The methodology consisted of a systematic review of literature in the Web of Science and Scopus databases on articles published between 2014 and 2020. The documents were submitted to two types of analysis, the first at the bibliometric level with VOSviewer software, where the clusters of authors, citations and keywords were examined; the second a content analysis, in order to determine the methodological models used by the researchers. These procedures allowed identifying eight study variables, among which they stand out, trust, commitment, dependence and collaboration. The results of the research show how commercial relations are the main external source of innovation for the organizations. It is concluded that this study reveals different lines of research to be followed on innovation and its relationships
