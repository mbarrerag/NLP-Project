# Factors affecting the gender gap STEM. Case Study Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Bogotá Headquarters

There is a gender gap in STEM areas worldwide, exacerbating the shortage of professionals in these areas, which, in the current context of the fourth industrial revolution, is disadvantageous for the organizations.In the research that has been carried out to intervene this problem, it has been determined that the factors that affect the most are the social and individual ones and by their nature so specific requires its study in homogeneous populations.In order to propose actions that allow to intervene the gender gap in STEM careers in the UNAL, it was necessary to know the factors that affect the latter. To this, the present investigation was designed, from a qualitative approach, of type of single case study, holistic or integrated subtype, in three phases associated with the specific research objectives, namely: Phase one consisted in the identification of factors that affect the gender gap in the STEM, through a systematic analysis of the data obtained from literature - RSL. Phase two was contrasted with the factors that affect the gender gap in STEM identified in the interview with the factors identified in the local context of the UNAL, through the analysis of the systematic data obtained from the case., it has been determined that the factors that have the great impact are social and individual and due to their specific nature, their study must be in homogenous study population. To propose actions aim at reducing the gender gap in STEM degress at UNAL, it was necessary to know that that factor factors influence it.
