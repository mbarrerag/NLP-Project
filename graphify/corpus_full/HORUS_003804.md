# THE MOMABLE PLASTIC ARTISTICAL PARTIME OF THE NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA HEADQUARTERS MEDELLIN. 1

This article begins a series of deliveries with the analytical description of the works of art that make up the plastic movable heritage of the Medellín Headquarters of the National University of Colombia. The texts are part of the project that the academic cloister has undertaken to identify, register, inventory and evaluate the artistic artistic artistic furniture creations that it possesses. The author of the text coordinates the research in a way articulated with the Institutional Cultural Network
