# Optimization of a prototype of natural antioxidant functional raw material encapsulated in biopolymer coated nanoliposomes through analysis of artificial neural network systems

Colombia is an agricultural country with large areas of cultivation in all its thermal floors, which allows it to have a great diversity of crops and one of these corresponds to the Uchuva that is an exotic fruit provided with a protective calyx. This calyx is rich in various bioactive compounds, where the antioxidant Rutina has stood out as the majority component, describing, moreover, a high protective potential against diseases related to oxidative stress. However, to the present day, there is still no bioprospective use of this agro-industrial waste. Therefore, this proposal is initially framed in the obtaining of a natural extract enriched in the antioxidant Rutina from an efficient and unpollucent extraction method such as supercritical fluids. Subsequently, this extract will be incorporated into nanoliposomal systems with biopolymeric coating, which is expected to improve its properties of chemical stability and antioxidant performance. Finally, this proposal is framed in a context of optimization of formulation and process conditions based on desired quality attributes through the use of artificial intelligence focused on neural networks
