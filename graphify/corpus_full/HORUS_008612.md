# Stochastic cutting law optimization model for an gold mining company

One of the most studied decision variables in the technical mining literature is the law of cut, in which the most accepted objective function has been the maximization of the Present Value Net (VPN). However, a considerable number of mining projects determine their cutting laws through the use of deterministic models that do not allow to perform an analysis based on uncertainty. In this work a model of stochastic optimization of cutting laws is formulated for an auriferous deposit, considering the risks and uncertainties inherent to the mining activity, with the purpose of maximizing the VPN of the project with underground mining operations. The methodology selected for the model corresponds to the stochastic optimization implicit, which uses a hybrid approach which combines a metaheuristic algorithm (Genetic algorithm) and the simulation of Montecarlo. The validation of the model was performed using real data to verify its industrial applicability and provide an alternative to the traditional models commonly used to date.
