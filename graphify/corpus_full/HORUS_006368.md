# The biorefinery concept for the industrial valorization of banana peels

Banana peels are a potential feedstock to be upgraded in a portfolio of products and energy vectors by applying the biorefinery concept. Indeed, the chemical composition of banana peels are potential source of different biotechnological and chemical products since C5, and C6 sugars are the most promising platforms to obtain several products such as n-butanol, ethanol, levulinic acid, and biogas. On the other hand, small- and large-scale biorefineries can be proposed as banana peel upgrading options to favor different parts of the banana productive chain. In this way, this chapter provides an overview related to the biorefinery concept and how this can be applied to upgrade banana peels in a series of value-added products. Then, several study cases are presented to give an idea about the possible banana peels use, as raw material, in industrial processes. The study cases involve the techno-economic analysis of four biorefineries, including concepts related to multifeedstock processing, the influence of the socioeconomic context on economic performance, and scale. The above evaluations allowed to elucidate the high potential of banana peels as raw material in biorefineries since economically feasible processes can be proposed at different scales and contexts. Moreover, the effect of the banana peel valorization on the sustainability of the banana productive chain is done, considering Colombia as a base case
