# CHAPTER 5 RELATING TO THE EXPERIENCE OF DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION IN THE SCADA COURSE AND INDUSTRIAL CONTROLERS OF THE INGENIY FACULTY OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY - BOGOTÁ

For the National University of Colombia, education, science and technology are the pillars that enable us to face the changes and challenges that Colombia needs. To this extent, we have joined efforts and skills towards the intelligent articulation of capacities in the UNAL ecosystem. As a result of this work, we present the book Digital Transformation: Collective Experiences, a series of articles narrated by experts in Digital Transformation of Colombian public and private sector organizations. Here you will find theoretical approaches, reflections and real cases of institutions with a great trajectory facing the challenges and problems of their sectors to take on Digital Transformation as a path towards cultural change. These trajectories take as a basis the range of possibilities offered by the tools of the Fourth Industrial Revolution
