# Heterogeneous Cultural Landscape in the Center of Medellín

The concept of landscape in Colombia has not developed beyond the recognition of international actors of a common cultural framework in a given territory, and the legislation that indirectly supports its protection is limited to purely descriptive issues, understanding the landscape strictly as a geographical scenario and ignoring the symbolic, spatial, cultural, visual and economic conditions that encompass the term. That is, the landscape in our country has been limited conceptually by its indeterminacy and by the absence of theories that allow its semantic expansion, which has made impossible a construction of meaning at the local level and has relegated its appearance in the laws of the country to a mere formal question at best, or to a simple representation of polygonal traces of territorial planning maps. In view of this panorama of uncertainty of national legislation and the lack of public policies that consolidate a strategy of treatment, protection and valuation of the landscape in the country, it is proposed to identify the constituent elements of a cultural landscape from the heterogeneity rather than homogeneity (which is as it is currently addressed) in the Centre of Medellín, recognizing its diversity and the spatial conflicts, which represents social heritage
