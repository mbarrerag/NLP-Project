# Automatic generation of orthomosaic GIS vector layers using deep learning.

This thesis presents a methodology based on three methods for the extraction of points, lines, and polygons of vector objects present in orthomosaics using a generative model based on deep learning as an alternative to the semantic segmentation approach used by default. The first method consists of two lines of work, the training vector layers are acquired either by manual digitization of objects of interest or directly from Open Street Maps (OSM). The input raster layers are increased spectral and geometrically, thesised and paired in pairs image-mask that are checked before the imbalance. The balanced data set is then randomly broken to obtain the final set. The generative, conditional and non matched models are compared and the best chosen to perform the translations between image and mask. The results of the comparison and those obtained by the best model on different sets of data, and their configuration are reported using the metric mIoU. A batch of ten for a set of 1000 image-mascaras is called the most expensive.
