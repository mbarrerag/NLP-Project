# Water deficit mitigation by application of calcium in yellow potato diploid (Solanum tuberosum L. group Phureja)

Climate variability favours the occurrence of droughts that decrease yield in potato crops, so it is necessary to develop strategies to mitigate the effects of water deficit. The objective of this research was to evaluate the effect of the application of calcium (Ca) in physiological parameters and yield of Solanum tuberosum L. group Phureja cultivar criolla Colombia under water deficit. Tubers-seeds were sown in bags and maintained at field capacity until the beginning of tuberization. Between 44 and 54 days after sowing (dds), edaphic applications (E) of: CaCl2 of 3 and 4.5 g plant-1; Ca(NO3)2 of 4.5 and 7.0 g plant-1 and Ca(NO3)2 + B of 6.0 and 9.0 g plant-1 were performed. Also foliar application of: CaCl2 of 10 and 20 mM plant-1; Ca(NO3)2 of 10 and 20 m plant-1 were performed. At 55 dds plants were subjected to continuous irrigation (BR) and water deficit by water suspension of irrigation during 7 days (DH)
