# FIRST RECORD OF MYRCIANTHES PRODIGIOSA (MYRTACEAE) FOR COLOMBIA, AND NOTES ON ITS FLOWERS AND FRUITS

A new record of Myrcianthes (Myrtaceae) for the Andean forests of Serranía de Los Yariguíes, Santander department, Colombia, is reported. Notes on the species¿ habitats, expanded distribution range, and morphological observations on its flowers and fruits are provided
