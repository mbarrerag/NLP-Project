# Strategic environmental assessment of Colombia's tourism sector plans for the period 2014-2022

Tourism is a social phenomenon of considerable economic importance in Colombia, which has received special attention by the national governments who have sought to encourage it through various policies and particularly, through the sectoral plans of tourism, documents that seek to strengthen the competitiveness of the sector, considering as part of its pillars the environmental sustainability. Considering that the competitiveness is based on economic growth, there is a complex relationship between economic growth and environmental sustainability. In this sense and considering that parallel to the increase of the economic importance has been presented an increase of environmental conflicts, the need to deepen in the discussion on the relation growth and environmental sustainability and in the way that environmental sustainability is understood from the governmental perspective, so the present work seeks to address these aspects by means of a Strategic Environmental Assessment of the sectoral plans of tourism in force during the periods 2014 to 2022.
