# Structural characterisation of molybdenum co-ordination compounds by X-ray diffraction of polycrystalline samples

The X-ray diffraction of polycrystalline sample samples (DRXP) is a non-destructive analytical technique used for the identification and quantification of various compounds in solid state whether organic, inorganic, metallic or organometallic (DRXP). This technique can be used for the structural determination of compounds presenting a repetitive ordering of their atoms, without resorting to X-ray diffraction measurements of monocrystalline samples.
