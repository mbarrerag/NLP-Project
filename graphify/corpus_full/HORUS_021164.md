# DEVELOPMENT OF A COMSTABLISHING SNACK TO PARTY FROM A PROTEIC CONCENTRATE OF DULCE LACTOSORUER INCORPORATING PULPA AND MANGO CASCARA (Mangifera indica l.)

At present, agro-industry generates a considerable amount of waste that affects the environment, by-products containing nutrients that can be used in the food industry, giving the possibility of becoming a co-product and reducing this environmental impact. The application of dehydration processes to dairy products is very common in powdered milks and matured cheeses; however, the development of edible snacks based on protein concentrates of sweet whey and fruit matrices with nutraceutical characteristics is little studied and implemented. Pre-tests will be carried out for the choice of a mixture of mango, pulp and protein concentrate shell obtained by the ultrafiltration of sweet whey, will be performed a rheological analysis and then subjected to thermal treatment by forced convection, in order to obtain a sensoryly acceptable edible snack, with physical and chemical characteristics that provide functional properties in terms of the amount of antioxidants and protein
