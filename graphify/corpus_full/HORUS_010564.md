# ENGINEERING FOR LIFE: A DECLARATION FROM MINES FACULTY, AWAY TO SUSTAINABILITY

In this chapter we explore the Manifesto of Engineering for Life of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia, an invitation to design sustainable engineering from the territory. We present initially a brief contextualization of the Faculty of Mines to review some of the elements of its history and evolution that have allowed to generate the conditions for writing this manifesto. Next, we review some of the challenges that we currently face as a species and in which engineering has a leading role; by exploring a little bit the nature of these problems, it is highlighted how many of them come from a mechanistic interpretation of the world we live in. We are facing the urgent challenge of designing an engineering from a new paradigm, from a systemic approach that allows us to take into account, in every engineering action, the fundamental elements to maintain the sustainability of life in the territories. The academy plays a fundamental role in this change, and as one of the possible strategies to respond to these demands, we close the text proposing a group of competences that we want to commit ourselves to in the processes of learning from the Faculty and that seek to develop a profile in which engineering is built from scientific knowledge and from territories
