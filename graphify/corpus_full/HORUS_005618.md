# Oral health policy of Bogotá, D.C., with social participation for the decade 2011-2021: Evaluation of the axis of strengthening the management of human dental talent

This paper focused on analysing the evaluability of the first two Strategic Lines of the Third Axis of the Oral Health Policy of Bogotá, D.C. with Social Participation for the decade 2011-2021, which focuses on the Strengthening of the Management of the Odontological Human Talent; the analysis covered the phases of design and implementation, as well as on giving visibility to the perceptions and proposals of some dentists regarding these Strategic Lines. The study methodology consisted of a documentary review on the background of the Policy and the Strategic Lines to be analyzed, and the conduct of semi-structured interviews both to actors involved in the design of the Policy and to general dentists. The analysis of the evaluability was designed based on the compilation of the main steps described in the literature. The results of this analysis showed that the actions proposed in the first two Strategic Lines of the Third Axis were not implemented, and their design was not allowed to be evaluated.
