# Sovereign Habitat, an Opportunity for Rural Life

Colombia since the second half of the twentieth century has been characterized by a near infinite wave of human mobility, understood as migration, internal displacement or forced displacement. Today, at the beginning of the third decade of the twenty-first century, the country continues to be the scene of the exodus presenting human mobility from the outside, from the countryside to the city and from the city to the countryside. These migrations produced by an endless number of reasons, raise questions about how to inhabit the rural territory to encourage the permanence and return of the population. This research needs strategies for the transformation of the rural habitat of the municipality of Gachetá, which contribute to the development of one of its rural areas through territorial and economic dynamics. The study links the mobility of the population with elements such as rural habitat, rural development, productivity, memory and belonging, because they are part of the debate on how to build rural return scenarios for the Colombian population, which promote actions for the construction of a productive rural fabric. The research is based methodologically on the phenomenological approach, studying with this, rural facts at depth, identifying ways of inhabiting and proposes strategies for the return and permanence of the population in rural areas. questions about how to inhabit rural areas to foster the sustainability and return of the population.
