# TRAINING GUIDELINES FOR INTEGRAL PROTECTION OF SOCIAL DEFINERS AND LEADERS WITH SCIENTIFIC AND LEGAL APPROACH

In some Latin American countries, the need for guarantees and protection of the work of defending human rights and social leadership in general has become one of the main areas of work of organizations of different kinds or as the seeds of social mobilization and organization processes. From Central America to the Southern Cone, numerous moments have been recorded in which the integrity of those who commit themselves to the defence of life has been stigmatized, persecuted and put at risk from multiple dimensions. But we have also seen how from community, community, youth, women’s organizations, networks or platforms of very different kinds, self-protection strategies are being constructed, while denouncing and demanding that States fulfil their obligations in terms of guaranteeing rights. In the context of the social mobilization and protest that characterizes our region, this pedagogical initiative called Diploma in Management of Services to Support Prevention, of the Exigibility of Guarantees and Protection of Defenders and Social Leaders with Psychosocial and Legal Approach, developed in a virtual way between the months of March and June 2023 and with the participation of 15 leaders of the Social Guarantees, Peru and Colombia.
