# IMMUNOSENSCENCE IN WOMEN WITH SINGING OF MOTHER AND ANSWER TO QUIMIOTERAPANIA NEOADYUVANTE.

In Colombia, breast cancer is the main cause of cancer death in women. The incidence increases linearly with age after 45 years, having the highest rates after 60 years, suggesting a link between cancer and aging. The immune system is essential in the prevention and control of tumour formation, however, with aging the immune cells lose this ability to immunovigilance and the development of cancer is favored. In this context, in which different immunosuppressive phenotypes in immune cells associated with senescence can be related to the loss of immunovigilance and cancer development; this work evaluated different subpopulations of immune cells of patients with breast cancer and the ability to reverse any of the phenotypes with the immunomodulatory effect of neoadyuvant chemotherapy.
