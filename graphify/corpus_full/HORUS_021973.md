# Teaching the first law of thermodynamics by problem-based learning (ABP) with students of Cycle V.

Teaching of the first law of thermodynamics through problem-based learning (ABP) with students of Cycle V. In this work, the previous ideas or preconcepts of the students of Cycle V on the first law of thermodynamics, heat, temperature and internal energy were identified, to design and implement a didactic sequence, based on the pedagogical model of problem-based learning (ABP). This research was developed with students of the I.E.D. rodrigo lara bonilla of the town of ciudad bolívar, bogotá colombia. Four activities were proposed, which included laboratory activities, use of interactive platforms, construction of a steam engine using home material and the solution of different thermodynamic situations using the ABP methodology, to strengthen in students the development of scientific skills in group and individual work. A pretest and postest test without group control was applied that showed significant gains in the assimilation of concepts related to the first law of thermodynamics according to the Hake gain factor and the Pearson correlation coefficient. Finally, a qualitative analysis of the students' discourse was carried out without group control that showed significant gains in terms of scientific knowledge (Vo the development of concepts of the thermodynamics)
