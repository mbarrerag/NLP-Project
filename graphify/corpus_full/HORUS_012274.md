# Identification of the phenological status of rice cultivation from Sentinel-1 radar images.

The availability of timely and accurate information on rice phenology is essential for various crop management activities that is essential for agro-food safety. Although direct data collection in the field provides reliable information, that task requires a lot of time and work. As a technological alternative, satellite optical remote sensing collects reflectance data every 5 days. However, in tropical environments, cloud coverage obstructs the view from above. Due to its ability to see through clouds, images obtained by synthetic aperture radar sensors (SAR) have potential for monitoring the phenological stages of rice. This study implements a technical workflow to process and analyze polarimetric SAR data for the mapping of rice phenology. A multi-temporal set of images Sentinel-1 of band C acquired in two areas of rice in Colombia were used to validate the working flow. In this study, the phenological classification using polarimetric and interferometric indices as explanatory variables was performed.
