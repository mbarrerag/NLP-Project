# AN AGENT-BASED METHODOLOGY FOR SEAPORTS DECISION

This chapter presents considerations to develop an agent-based methodology invoking seaport authorities deciding on a collaborative environment. Seaport authorities as decision makers must base their standpoints on different scenarios (e.g. through simulations) suitable to show outputs against empirical decisions. The agent-based modelling allows for the generation of a simulated emergent behaviour, which could be observed in the dynamics of the seaports given its consideration as a complex sociotechnical system. Key properties to investigate are agents¿ active or passive behaviour as well as their autonomous capability and jurisdictional heterogeneity. We state that two possible frameworks to support agents and multi-agents¿ interaction and communication, i.e., the knowledge level to support the dynamic behaviour needed in the system are IEEE-FIPA and BSPL. Finally, we provide an example of how to represent agent's knowledge in a semantic graph, considering a declarative formalism as a set of objects that can be represented in a universe of discourse that semantically contribute to the identification of decision-making choices
