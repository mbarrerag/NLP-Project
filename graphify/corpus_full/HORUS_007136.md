# Influence of structure on dynamic behaviour of clay soils

The main objective of this study is to analyze the influence of structure on the dynamic behaviour of clay soils stabilized with cement, identifying the variations in their mechanical and dynamic properties. The research focused on the generation of structure induced in the soil by the mixture of a clay soil (caolin) with Portland cement, evaluating its response under static and cyclical loads. To this end, a series of tests were carried out of physical, chemical, mechanical and dynamic characterisation. The results showed that the induced cementation process generated a structure at the microscopic level in the soil, increasing significantly the structure of the cutting (G) and reducing the dampening ratio (D) as the cement content was greater.
