# A Honey-lined Squirrel: Intellectual Biography of the Anti-Oquean Caricaturist Ricardo Rendón Bravo (1894-1931)

Ricardo Rendón Bravo (1894-1931) was an anti-Oqueño political cartoonist who lived during the period of the Conservative Hegemony (1886-1930). His cartoons appeared on the pages of the country's main republican and liberal magazines and newspapers that flourished during the last two decades of the Conservative Hegemony period as a product of the new press freedoms granted by the constitutional reform of 1910. He worked in the cultural magazines Avanti, Panida, Cromos and El Gráfico and in the main newspapers opposing the conservative regime, such as El Espectador, La República and El Tiempo. Thus, his political cartoons became important weapons to oppose the conservative regime and relevant public opinion configors in electoral policy matters.
