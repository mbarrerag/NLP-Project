# Latin America: Early deindustrialization

The transformation of an agricultural society into an industrial society was the classic path of economic development to employ workers without any technical qualifications while increasing the productivity of the economy and wages. Therefore, the composition of employment and production over the last two centuries, translates into an increase in jobs and industrial output, driven by the growing demand of industrial workers and exports
