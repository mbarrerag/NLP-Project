# (ALTERNATIVE) TYPE-GRAPHICAL DIMENSIONS

We learn to design typography and typography very intuitively, perhaps too intuitively. However, in the decision-making of design algorithmic processes can be as important as heuristics, the latter being the ones we usually give more relevance to. This talk seeks to present a somewhat different approach to the teaching and learning process in the subject through exploring some of the typographical dimensions that could be most useful for students and professionals of Graphic Design
