# ALTERNATIVE PROTEIN SOURCES FOR HUMAN FOOD ON MARS

This article shows a part of the documentary review carried out in 2020 in the framework of the Mars City State 2020 Contest of The Mars Society, focused on the issue of ensuring food safety, posing the following question: Why are protein sources an alternative to human food on Mars?
