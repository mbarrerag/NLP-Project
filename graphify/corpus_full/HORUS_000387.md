# A rapid FTIR-ATR method for determining the protein content of Sacha Inchi oil press-cake and its derived protein concentrates

The feasibility of using Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy with attenuated total reflectance (FTIR-ATR) to determine the protein content of Sacha Inchi oil press-cake (SIPC) and its derived protein concentrates was investigated. The proposed method was developed by replacing an analytical starch powder with a Sacha Inchi protein concentrate (SPC) (83 % protein content), in different weight proportions, ranging from 10 % to 100 %. The results revealed that as the amount of protein added to the starch powder increased, the carbohydrate band within the range of 1200–880 cm−1 diminished, while the area under the curve related to proteins, increased within the range of 1710–1480 cm−1. Partial least squares regression demonstrated excellent performance in predicting the protein content, as indicated by the high coefficient of determination (R2 = 0.998). The relative calibration and validation errors were low (0.150–0.107), suggesting that the proposed method is appropriate for determining the protein content of SIPC and its derivates, by using the area under the curves of the predominant protein and carbohydrate bands. The advantages of this method are related to its quickness, straightforwardness, the hazardous chemicals freeness, the small sample amounts required, and the good concordance with the traditional Kjeldahl method
