# CHAPTER 11, MARINE ENERGY, NEW TECHNOLOGIES AND OFFSHORE INDUSTRY

Humanity is a complex social collective that has developed thanks to the energy available in the environment. We are captivated by energy because it has allowed great transformations in our quality of life since we are aware of its availability, use and control. With energy we make food, we move, we develop our creative capacity. We are linked to energy, there is nothing to do. But its dependence has created a relevant problem, because in the face of its scarcity or absence we are limited by the everyday nature created. The geographical distribution of energy consumption allows us to observe the demand of the US, Russia and China, mainly, and in contrast to a marginal consumption of Colombia. Energy in general has the virtue of allowing transformations, but in every transformation we generate some kind of loss, so we always seek efficiency elements in these transformational processes. Energy in general is found in the nature in various forms. Since the industrial revolution (nineteenth century), we have increased its consumption in the form of hydrocarbons, coal and biomass.
