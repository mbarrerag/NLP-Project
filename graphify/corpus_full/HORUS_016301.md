# LESSONS IN AULA: LINKS, TissueS AND TREATMENT

In this second volume we find a compendium of written works by teachers and teachers, where it is possible to see several contexts and learning and teaching processes. The reader will find a dialogic relationship between the reading and writing processes and the processes specific to other areas of knowledge, such as: the role of augmented reality in the construction of meaning; a proposal to build literary anthologies from readers; the finding of irony in texts as interpretative stimuli; the link between theatre and social conflict; the role of reading and writing in the construction of democracy; the relationship between science and reading; the ways of building peace from a philosophical reading; the strengthening of critical reading in basic and middle education; the strengthening of identity from reading; the learning of English from virtual tools; the school garden as a setting for learning reading and writing, listening and speaking; the possibilities that reading and writing grants for human development; narratives built from the body and territory; biology enriched from reading and living together that is consolidated from the air to reading and writing
