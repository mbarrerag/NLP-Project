# Sensory platform with energy collection without battery and Gateway for visualization

This thesis focuses on addressing problems related to food and energy efficiency in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT). In an increasingly connected world, where IoT plays an essential role, there is a need to overcome the limitations of conventional battery and explore innovative solutions to ensure sustainable operation in the medium and long term. The project focuses on the implementation of IoT devices “batteryless” that use solid state components for energy storage, using the collection of solar energy indoor and using efficient communication protocols. An outstanding aspect of this research is the incorporation of a Gateway that provides a visual interface for users, which facilitates the interpretation and effective use of data collected by IoT devices. This interface plays a crucial role in making information accessible and understandable, which significantly expands the potential of this technology for different applications and contexts.
