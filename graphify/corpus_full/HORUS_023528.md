# CARBON STORED IN CACAO TREES AND SOILS IN AGROFORESTRY SYSTEMS

The decrease of CO2 in the atmosphere is a challenge for the man of the XXI century to mitigate climate change. Plants play a relevant role in the solution to this problem because they take atmospheric CO2 and transform it for the construction of their structures, where carbon is stored in organic form. In this work the carbon accumulated in biomass, leaf and soil, of the cocoa trees in agroforestry systems (SAF), located in three living areas of the system of Holdridge, tropical dry forest (bs-T), pre-montane humid forest (bh-PM) and tropical humid forest (bh-T) were quantified. 48 cocoa trees were extracted from the SAF, the soil was collected and sampled, to quantify the carbon stored in these components of the SAF.
