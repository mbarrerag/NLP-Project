# BIORREMIDATION OF POLLUTING METALS BY BACTERIES INDUSTRING THE PRECIPTATION OF CARBONATES: KEY ASPECTS

There are toxic elements in the environment that represent a problem due to their negative impact on humans, animals and the ecosystem. Among the alternatives to solve this problem are the biotechnological approaches such as microbiologically induced carbonate precipitation (MICP), which is considered an innovative and eco-friendly strategy. This article seeks to show the importance of MICP biotechnology for the bioremediation of polluting metals, as well as key aspects that must be considered to understand the MICP process, and thus acquire the basic knowledge for its study and application
