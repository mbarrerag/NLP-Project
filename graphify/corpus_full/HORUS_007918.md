# THE ENGAGEMENT OF PARTICIPATION IN REAVING PEACE

From the beginning of the peace process that motivated the Agreement (AFP) there were suspicions that prevented that, once a final resolution of the negotiations and agreements reached in a document has been reached, the process would not be defined. The subsequent phase of the construction of the stable and lasting Peace in Colombia would be crossed not only by unprecedented opportunities but also by new obstacles, especially, disputes over the definitions of the elements and mechanisms that would give it concrete effect during the period of implementation. Just as, the real, broad and effective participation of the whole nation, up and down (above all, from below) suggested to be one of the elements of the peace that would enhance the expectations of reconciliation and hopes to reconduce the social conflict towards the spaces and institutional responses. Among others, it would be a way to suspend the armed confrontation as an alternative of political participation (non-conventional) going towards a phase of democratization of the powers of society. Unfortunately, the evolution of the Agreement that (we insist) overshadowed the systemic reactions and the emerging positions of sectors and social forces that were dominant, predicting the construction and walls. In this way, it is clear that the fundamental role of the approach of participation of the AFP in the possible structural transformations that the country needs by overcoming the social and armed conflict and, above all, to reach basic levels (at least) of equity, social justice and freedom proper to democratic societies that overflow its liberal-bourgeois expression. And, therefore, it is also evident that, as it continues to be the subject of disputes, the actions of the social and popular movement will tilt the balance in society as a whole and within the Statehood in favour of its full implementation and, with this, of a real political opening that allows deepening and making it a permanent component of society
