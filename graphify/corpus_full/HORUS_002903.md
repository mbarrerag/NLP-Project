# STRUCTURA-PROPERTIES. A ROUND TO SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT?

Structure-property relationship. A route to sustainable development This plenary session aims to study carbonous materials and raw materials from which it is possible to obtain them according to the results of own research and other authors. This involves appropriate knowledge on carbonous materials understood as traditional inputs for the energy industry and/or currently posed with a strong dedication to a strategic scenario with opportunities for the energy transition, while contributing to the environment towards sustainable development, is a long-term task that must be carried out with different approaches and disciplines
