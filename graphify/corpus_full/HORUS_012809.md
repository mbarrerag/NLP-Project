# Analysis of the sustainable production of hydrogen as an energy vector in Colombia

The first step towards the energy transition that promotes a society that is neutral in carbon is the implementation of renewable energy sources, accompanied by the development of new energy vectors with lower pollution rates and higher energy efficiency. Based on this, the energy transition requires numerous efforts in the processes of transformation, generation and consumption of energy. Thus, the discovery of new resources and alternative energy systems has become a priority and the role of hydrogen in this task has already been recognized by multiple governments and large industries, such as IEA. However, given the multiplicity of pathways for converting raw materials into energy products, it is impossible to formulate sound decisions without a preliminary conceptual design and multi-criteria assessment. To date, the transformation routes for the production of hydrogen have been focused on petrochemical and thermochemical schemes, and have been analyzed as unitary processes, ignoring the relevance of other production schemes such as electroly-based and electroly and biological processes. In addition, there are no rigorous studies that analyse all production routes as a whole.
