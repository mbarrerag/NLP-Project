# Development of REFERENCE MATERIALS IN HOUSE A FORENSE CHALLENGE.

Forensic laboratories need to use certified reference materials (CRM) to ensure reliability in the results obtained. Similarly, some analytical techniques require using reference materials (RM). However, there are limitations given the low availability of these. From the above, the need has arisen for analytical laboratories to develop RM for their own internal use, which are known as internal RM. or RM in house? (RMih). These materials are usually developed when the commercially available RM matrices, do not match the samples that are to be analyzed or when looking for a comprehensive supply that is available for a long time [1,2]. It is important to note that, in order to develop these materials, some aspects must be taken into account for the approval of the assigned value, storage and distribution [3].
