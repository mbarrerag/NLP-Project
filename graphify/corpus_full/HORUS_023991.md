# PRIORITY CHALLENGES IN THE INTERVENTION OF CHILD BALANCE IN COLOMBIA

The chapter addresses the most important challenges for the intervention of child abuse (MI) in Colombia. Initially, data are presented to enable the quality response to this problem from a public health perspective. Failure to implement the mandatory report significantly influences the invisibility of the MI. It is necessary to implement the trauma-focused approach as an epistemological precept for the approach of the MI. Improvements in the training and training of professionals linked to the National Child Protection System, as well as the intersectoral and inter-institutional articulation, are identified as strategic areas of work. The application of specific tools such as SPUTOVAMOMO-R in emergency services for the detection of physical abuse or some intervention tools such as EMDR, are some of the specific proposals for work proposed within the chapter
