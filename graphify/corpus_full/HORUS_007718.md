# The reflective functioning of parents and their effects on the prosocial behavior of adolescents, mediated by their emotional regulation

Parental reflexive functioning (FRP) is the ability of parents to understand their own experiences and the behaviors of their children, in terms of underlying thoughts, feelings and desires. This research, of a cross-sectional nature, seeks to establish the effects of the FRP and the prosocial behavior of adolescents, mediated by emotional regulation. The sample will be of an intentional nature and will be made up of 1000 parents and their adolescent children (between 13 and 15 years) who attend grades 8 and 9 of secondary basic education in Colombia (Bogotá, Tunja, Pereira and Zipaquirá) and Costa Rica (San José). The research problem will be investigated by the FRP's effects on prosocial behavior in adolescents, mediated by emotional regulation. The ethical interrelations of dependence (effects) between nonobservable constructs called latent variables will be taken into account. The research problem will be investigated by the effects of the FRP on prosocial behavior in adolescents, mediated by the emotional regulation. The ethical recommendations of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia and those established by law 1094_Defetological Law of 2006 will be taken into account
