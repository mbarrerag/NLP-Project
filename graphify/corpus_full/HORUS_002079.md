# Stratigraphic characterization of the basal cretaceous in the Yaguará area, Upper Magdalena Valley: implications for hydrocarbon deposits

The Upper Valley of Magdalena constitutes the third producing area of hydrocarbons of Colombia. To the south of this area, the Horse Formation forms the base of the Cretaceous sedimentary succession. It presents levels of generating rock and important reservoirs of hydrocarbons. In addition, it exhibits lateral variations in its stratigraphic characteristics that influence the quality of the deposits. The objective of the study consisted in performing the stratigraphic characterization of the succession of the Cretaceous basal near the field producer Yaguará – Los Mangos from an integral analysis of various sources of information. It addressed the available information of surface and subsoil through an initial analytical phase that included the petrographic characterization and analysis of the DRX and XRF samples collected, mainly arenites with impregnation of crude.
