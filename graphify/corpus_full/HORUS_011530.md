# SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNOLOGICAL BASIS FOR THE PRESENTATION OF A BOVINE SELL OF QUALITY IN THE REGION OF ANTI-IQUE URABA

The development of a Quality Seal for Bovine Meat Cuts, based on its value chain and on the standardization of the productive conditions of the animals, the biotype of livestock for slaughter, the adequate transport, the slaughter under sanitary standards, the technical handling and implementation of the system of classification of the channels, the clear and concise protocol of the processes of deposition for obtaining different cuts and the valuation of its quality that results in the best way of use, will enable the development and rationalization of the internal market and will enhance the possibilities of export of a product of quality guaranteed by the Sello, becoming the model to follow in the country. In search of this objective, the research group GICTA, of the National University of Colombia, headquarters Medellín, in association with the fourth highest utilization of the bovine channels classified by the Region, developed the project "Scientific and technological bases for the implementation of a quality seal of bovine meat produced in the region of the Urabá Antioqueño" (In the case of the fourth highest utilization of the bovine channels and the Grooms according to the NTC 4271 (1997), the first generation and third generation). Restrepo M., D., Alzate A. V., Cabrera T. K. (2021); Restrepo M. D et al., (2021); Restrepo M. D, et al (2022). The homogeneity in basic productive characteristics, the standardization of primary cuts of the front train, the compliance with the standards on heavy metal levels and veterinary residues provided for in Colombian and European legislation, as well as the results of the evaluation of the bromatory and nutritional quality; texture and sensory and industrial that exhibit many of the proposed cuts of the fourth front of the carcasses classified with three, four and five stars, credits the meat of the region, both for fresh consumption and for industrialisation processes as a product with excellent attributes to compete in the international market. The linkage of all actors of the meat production chain to the establishment and implementation of a Bovine Meat Quality Seal of the Anti-Oqueño Urabá Region, will yield benefits for each and every link, improving the positioning of the country in the international meat sector concert and a greater development of the region
