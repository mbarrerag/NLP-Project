# PHONOTIPIC PLASTICITY, IN RELATION TO MAXIMUM CRITICAL TEMPERATURE (CTmax), IN RENACUAJES OF THE ALTOANDIN RANA Dendropsophus molitor: ECOLOGICAL IMPLICATIONS WITHIN THE GLOBAL CLIMATE CHANGE SCENARIES.

Climate change has impacts on the ability to modify the adjustments in the thermal physiology of organisms that can lead to adaptive, ecological and evolutionary consequences for species. Variations in the historical environmental and climatic patterns have become a necessity to study how the environmental temperature can have an impact within the physiology of the species and how this can affect the assembly of communities in ecosystems. Most of the future scenarios at climate level, determine an increase of the environmental temperature, as well as a greater temporal and spatial variability in the thermal extremes both in terrestrial and aquatic environments. In ectotermics as the amphibians during the larva/juvenil stage present little capacity of selection in the habitat as thermal shelters, which limits their capacity of thermoregulation and are exposed to the thermal variances within the ecosystem.
