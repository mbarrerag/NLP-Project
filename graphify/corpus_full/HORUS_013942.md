# Characterization, risk assessment and educational strategies for reducing the harm caused by the use of psychoactive substances on the campus of the National University of Bogota, Bogota Headquarters.

One of the problems that the National University of Colombia has is the consumption of psychoactive substances (SPA) within the university campus in spaces shared by the whole community. Such consumption is problematic because in most occasions there is a lack of awareness by the consumer about the risks associated with the consumption of SPA generating mental health problems, low academic performance, irresponsible behaviors (in some cases of public knowledge) and learning of atypical behaviors by other students. Likewise, it is important to note that the spaces of the university cannot be taken as spaces where the free consumption of this type of substances is allowed since it generates a climate of insecurity in the university environment and a detriment in the image of our soul mater. It is also important to mention that both students and the university itself can generate problems of a legal nature that question the good academic environment that must exist inside the University. Based on the above, the present project will be able to identify the risk factors associated with the consumption of psychoactive substances (SPA) within the university campus through an epidemiological diagnosis.
