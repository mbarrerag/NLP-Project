# Preliminary characterization of agronomic, chemical and molecular variables of Colombian accesses of Azadirachta indica (neem) for the commercial production of bioinsecticides

The use of chemical pesticides, particularly in Colombia, has increased considerably in recent years, representing a high demand for substances that in many cases have harmful effects on the environment. For this reason it is of particular interest in agriculture, the search for alternatives that can substitute competitively for these agrochemicals. Neem tree (Azadirachta indica) originating in Asia is widely known for multiple applications, particularly to control pest insects. Currently there are different products in the market with neem derivatives, being the most common oil extracted from its seeds by its content of limonoids, bioactive metabolites against insects. In this work, different Colombian accessions to the neem tree will be studied, to determine if there are plantations that have significant differences in their agronomic characteristics related to its propagation and growth at the level of the live limonoids and its chemical characteristics, such as the amount of limonoids produced in leaves and seeds, to determine the yield of these compounds of agro-industrial interest.
