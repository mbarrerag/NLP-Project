# Comprehensive public policy guidelines for the preservation of traditional knowledge: Colombia in response to the achievement of goal 18 of the strategic plan of the biodiversity convention.

When we look for spaces for dialogue with indigenous peoples, we find in every step, in every word, the importance of accompanying a process that builds Guidelines for Integral Public Policy for the preservation and [protection] of the Traditional Knowledge of indigenous peoples insofar as Colombia must have references to meet goal 18 of the Strategic Plan of the Convention on Biological Diversity and that addresses the protection of indigenous knowledge. In dialogue with indigenous peoples, we realize that there is a huge wealth in their ancestral knowledge that from their thinking brings with them other perspectives, other logics that must be addressed as structural axes because they transform life and allow us to understand that we are one more in the territory in the diverse and complex interpretation of the natural and spiritual world where each being has its place and a meaning that contributes to the harmony of all those who remain and contribute with their original knowledge to the preservation and protection of the universe according to its uses and customs. Let refer to guidelines oriented under structural axes from the logics of indigenous peoples, seeks to understand ancestral knowledge as an associated whole, that is to look at the integrality of thought that conjures from the multiple disciplines of knowledge (Text of their own cosmogonies)
