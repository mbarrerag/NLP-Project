# Silenced Voices: Dynamics of Socio-Political Violence in the Nelson Mandela District of Cartagena de Indias, 1995-2007

Social-political violence in urban contexts represents one of the most valid research topics in Colombia. The violation of human rights against the civilian population and especially against social leaders, is one of the most visible results of the persistent internal armed conflict, which has led to the displacement of more than 5 million Colombians, from the fields to the cities. This research work focuses on the case of Nelson Mandela, a neighborhood formed in the mid-1990s in the outskirts of the city of Cartagena de Indias, mostly by people displaced by violence. The vicissitudes of the neighborhood for its conformation and community development conceived from within its own inhabitants, in the midst of a context of violence, control of the neighborhood by the paramilitaries, threats, murders, new displacements and revictimization, especially of the social leaders who resisted violence from the city to the city, are the main objective of this research: Analysing the dynamics of socio-political violence in the Nelson Mandela neighbourhood of Cartagena de Indias between 1995 and 2007. .
