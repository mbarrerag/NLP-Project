# Ukraine, war and new decolonizations

The Russian invasion of the Ukrainian territory, which began in February 2022, has marked a milestone in contemporary international relations and a substantial impact on the international system. The article focuses on proposing, from the comprehensive field of social and human sciences, the understanding of these events, otherwise unpublished in current world politics, based on three fundamental concepts: (i) the Russian justification of its actions, as part of a process of re-imperialization, based on a historical account, to reclaim former territories; (ii) the Ukrainian perspective, which is framed in a neo-nationalist position, as a strategy of armed defense of its territory and political project as a sovereign and independent state, and (iii) from the perspective of decolonizations, which aims to investigate the relationship and tension of both force fields (Russia and Ukraine), in the light of historical processes that are reactualizing and reconfiguring the world geopolitical map
