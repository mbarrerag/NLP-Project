# A COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS, THROUGH GEOGRAPHICAL ACCESSIBILITY, OF URBAN PUBLIC TRANSPORT SYSTEMS IN CITIES OF COLOMBIA'S COFFEE-GROWING REGION.

The valuation of urban public transport plays a vital role in a city's growth and development. Furthermore, an exemplary system connection allows the formation of functional relationships between the different users and the environment surrounding them. That is why, in this research, a comparative analysis of the levels of coverage offered by Public Transport Systems in cities of the Colombian coffee-growing region is proposed to visualize each city's current state of operation. The research methodology uses geographical accessibility as a visualization tool complemented by socio-demographic coverage analysis. The main result is that the assessment of accessibility and coverage shows that the population has easy access in a journey time of fewer than 35 minutes. It can also be concluded that the assessment process provides a broad view of the levels of transport supply in each city
