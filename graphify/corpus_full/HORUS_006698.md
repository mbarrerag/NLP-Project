# Physical activity level and quality of life in patients with waiting list and renal transplant recipients

Chronic Renal Disease (ERC) represents a major public health problem and renal transplantation (TR) is the ideal treatment to improve Health-Related Quality of Life (CVRS). However, during the waiting period for the procedure they enter a waiting list (LE) with renal support; period that can impact on various aspects of patients' life, including AF and HRQV levels. To determine the correlation between the level of AF and HRV of patients with CRS, which are in LE or have been renal transplant recipients (RTR) in a Hospital of High Complexity, in the period from June 1 to 2023 to August 15, 2023. Transverse, descriptive and correlational study. A sample was performed by non-probabilistic convenience, with a sample of 36 patients, the FAD was characterized with statistical calculations and a protocol of accelerometry with devices ActiGraph GT3X+ during seven days, the HRV was evaluated through the questionnaire KDQOL-36. The statistical software was used to perform statistical calculations of the SPSS and the descriptive data and the identify negative correlations between sedentary time with EKD (- 0.460), CER (-0.634) and a positive association between mild physical activity time (AFL) and physical component (CF) (0,453) and ERC-related symptomatology (0.508). By using an accelerometer, a precise method, this study demonstrated that patients in LE are sedentary and RTR are slightly active, highlighting the importance of considering AF as an integral component in the management of the ERC. Scopes and limitations: This is a pioneering research in the Cundinamarca Cardio vascular Hospital that links AF with HRQ offering an evaluation for the monitoring and introduction of AF programmes, and highlights the need for an accelerometer protocol in population with chronic diseases to standardize the results. It is recognized that the sample does not reach the levels of significance and that the normality test has not yielded positive results.
