# A Utilities Path to Implementing Disruptive Emerging Technologies: From Demonstration to the Full-Scale Implementation of MABR Technology

The water industry has recognized the need to accelerate the development and implementation of emerging technologies that can more effectively and efficiently address the unprecedented challenges we are facing. This paper presents a case study on how a water utility has successfully implemented membrane aerated biofilm reactors (MABR), a process intensification and low carbon footprint approach, starting with the demonstration testing of this potentially industry-disruptive technology and its eventual successful full-scale implementation, providing significant process intensification and reduced carbon footprint in the achievement of low nutrient effluent requirements in a water resource recovery facility
