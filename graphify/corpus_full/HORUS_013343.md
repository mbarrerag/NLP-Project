# Butterfly of the Ecoreserva La Tribuna (Vereda Tamarindo-Neiva, Huila).A comprehensive approach as a contribution to the knowledge and conservation of the biodiversity of the Tropical Dry Forest in Colombia

The Tropical Dry Forests (Bs-T) are listed in the highest level of global critical hazard. The relicts of the Ecoreserva La Tribuna, in the municipality of Neiva (Huila), are considered of importance to be affected by industrial activities such as oil extraction. This certainly justifies the study of the presence and diversity of these species as a baseline of information for mitigation, conservation and management activities. 2,370 individuals were collected, identified as belonging to 142 genera and 198 species, all previously registered for the country. The families Nymphalidae and Hesperiidae represented 66.5% of the total wealth. Within Riodinidae, Pieridae and Lycaenidae were recorded 24, 17 and 17 species, respectively.
