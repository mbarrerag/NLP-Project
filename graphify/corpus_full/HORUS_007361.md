# The category of person in non-human animals: philosophical analysis of the Colombian judicial context

The category that human beings recognize to non-human animals is the subject of debate, not only in the field of ethics, but also in a legal context. Both scenarios, although different, influence each other. In this sense the present work makes an analysis of the discussion in both fields. Developing a proposal of the concept of person, which for the ethical case, is defined as a subject of social relations based on the susceptibility of its moral interests. Defending that in this category it would be covered by non-human animals. Subsequently, the use of this category is transferred to a legal context, particularly an analysis of the argumentation of judicial cases is made in which the recognition, or not, of non-human animals as persons in Latin American countries is given. The emphasis of the research is on the analysis of the state of the discussion in Colombia, the arguments used in judicial cases, and above all the arguments proposed from the ethical context to answer some problems that arise. .
