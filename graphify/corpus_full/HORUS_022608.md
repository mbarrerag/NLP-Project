# LATIN AMERICA IN THE GLOBAL BUILDING OF WAR AGAINST DRUG

This chapter argues that hegemonic interpretations of drugs, such as placing the genesis of the war on drugs exclusively in the United States, should be complemented by the empirical identification of the meaning that Latin American actors give to their actions against illicit drug trafficking, consumption and production.With a sociological approach, and through the analysis of facts, political statements, diplomatic documents, and press and archival consultation, the chapter explores the participation of the Latin American region in the international construction of the war on drugs
