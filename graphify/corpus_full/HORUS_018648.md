# Relationship and design

This thesis covers the relationships between delay and the processes of ideation in design. To do this, concepts, plans of discussion, types of relationship and modes of circulation are established so that the delay within design processes, its effects and benefits are explained by knowing how to actively implement it as a resource of imagination. Procrastination and design is characterized as a skill to be managed in the learning processes and design work, and then understood as technology, resistance, slowing device and ecological instrument within creative behavior. .
