# Floating polymetallic sulphides

In this study, the incidence of the granulometry, collectors, activators, depressants and pH factors on the tenor and recovery of lead, iron and zinc sulphides in a selective flotation process was studied. The best tenors and recoveries for Pb, Fe, Zn concentrates were as follows: 54% and 81.22% for lead, 43% and 69.3% for Fe; 55% and 88.76% for Zn. The experimental results obtained allow the selective flotation process to be considered as the most appropriate technique for the concentration of these sulphides
