# COERCIVE BROKERAGE: THE RISE AND FALL OF THE COLOMBIAN PARAMILITARY HERNAN GIRALDO, 1976 - 2006

Hernán Giraldo was a paramilitary figurehead'and egregious sex offender'who operated in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta región, SNSM, between the late 1970s and 2006. He was a coercive broker. On the one hand, he established a complex system of intermediation between different territorial levels. On the other, he was a provider of prívate secu- rity to key economic and political elites which in the context of the Colombian State counterinsurgent strategy eventually allowed him to become a major paramilitary player
