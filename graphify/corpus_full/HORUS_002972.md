# THE EMPIRE CHANGES BY PARTS: WARDS AND WARM ADMINISTRATIONS IN SPANISH ATLANTIC (1700-1750)

The article analyzes the coexistence of the concessions of Correo Mayor and the postal administrations in the Spanish Atlantic. In Castile the postal concessions were extinguished and incorporated to the Crown after 1700. Within the Indies this process was prolonged for at least half a century. However, this was not an inconvenience to integrate overseas and terrestrial circuits. I intend to show that, the legal situation of the concessions of Correo Mayor in America, the costs of installation and the difficulty of offering a postal service comparable to that of the Habsburgs, caused the introduction of the administrations of mail (indian) by parts and at different rhythms.The empire changes parts: concessions and postal administrations in the Spanish Atlantic (1700-1750)
