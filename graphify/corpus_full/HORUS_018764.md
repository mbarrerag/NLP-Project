# CHANGES IN THE DECUSE PATRONS OF ALCOHOL AND TOBACO BEFORE AND DURING THE COVID PANDEMIA-19.ENSANUT 2018 AND 2020

Examine changes in the prevalence of alcohol and tobacco con-sumo before and during the Covid-19 pandemic in Mexico. Data from Ensanut 2018 and 2020 were used for adolescents and adults and prevalences of current and excessive consumption of alcohol and current and ex-smoker smokers were obtained
