# The Management of Construction and Demolition Waste (RCD) in Bogotá. An analysis based on the case of UPL No. 25 Usaquén

the management of construction and demolition wastes (rcd) in bogotá. an analysis from the case of UPL No 25 Usaquén. The management of Construction and Demolition Wastes (hereinafter RCD), during the last two decades, in Colombia has represented a challenge that has been reflected in the content of the normative framework that applies at national and district level. In this it is sought that the construction industry adopt management plans for these wastes during the execution of the Constructive Phase, so that control of the management and final disposition of these wastes is carried out, seeking the mitigation of the negative environmental, social and urban impacts associated with that activity. In order to identify the gaps of the national and district regulatory framework that apply for the city of Bogotá, D.C., an analysis of the legislation is made from thematic blocks, as well as the analysis of the implementation of RCD management plans in housing projects that are developed in the Local Planning Unit UPL Usaquén. Similarly, the review of the regulatory frameworks of international and local referents, in this way to identify concepts and actions that should be relevant to be developed in the development of the local planning. work applied at the national and district levels. The aim is for the construction industry to adopt management plans during the Construction Phase, enabling proper control and final dispositivo of these waste materials to mitigate associated environmental, social, and urban impacts. To identify gaps in the national and district regulation framework applied to the city of Bogotá, D.C., an analysis of legislation is conducted to thematic blocks. Additionally, an analysis of CDW management plans implemented in housing projects within the UPL Use is conducted, along with a review of international and local regulatory framework.
