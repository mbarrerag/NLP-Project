# Development of strategies for the efficient use of energy resources in predictive control based on model implemented in a embedded system for the treatment of type I diabetes mellitus.

Current technological advances have brought to reality the project of an artificial pancreas (PA) safe, portable and efficient calculation for people with type 1 diabetes. Among the control strategies developed for type 1 diabetes, model based predictive control (MPC) has been emphasized in the literature as a promising control for the regulation of glucose. However, these control strategies are commonly designed in a simulated environment, regardless of the limitations of a portable device. In this work, the performance of six embedded systems, three open code optimization packages and four MPC formulations with a hardware scheme in the cycle is evaluated (HIL by its acronym in English), to find the best combination taking as criteria the temperature of the processor, the execution time, the patient coefficient of variation, the percentage of time in normoglycemia, the energy consumed, the amount of events of hyperglycemia and the difference with the evolution obtained in MATLAB.
