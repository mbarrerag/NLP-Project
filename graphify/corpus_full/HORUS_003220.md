# RADIOGRAPHICAL CONCORDANCE OF INTERPROXIM LOSSES IN PATIENTS WITH STATISTICAL PERIODONTIS I AND II.

The purpose of the periodontic X-rays is to detect the level of alveolar bone, including the pattern and extent of alveolar loss. However, establishing the presence of bone loss in initial stages can become a challenge since it is only evident in advanced stages. A diagnostic concordance study was carried out at the Faculty of Dentistry of the National University in the adult I and II clinics (2021). The clinical histories of patients attended in these clinics were selected and they had periapical X-rays in physical and digital formats. A calibration test was performed for two evaluators, which ensured a calibration in the interpretation and measurement of the X-rays agreement. Once the calibration was completed, the radiographic measurements were performed in both radiographic versions. Subsequently, the agreement between the results was evaluated using different methods, such as the Kappa intra and interobserver index, the percentage of agreement and the intraclass correlation coefficient.
