# A PANORAMIC REVIEW

The knowledge available about suicidal behavior in pediatric populations exposed to violence is dispersed and a systematic and critical approach to the subject is required. A panoramic review was carried out with the aim of describing the scope and nature of the literature published in the period 2010-2020. JSTOR, SciELO, LILACS and PubMed databases were consulted, using as descriptors words from the area of health in English, Portuguese and Spanish. Research articles were included carried out in any geographical location and in populations between 6 and 19 years of age. 31 articles were recovered from a total of 1578 for full text review. Most of the reviewed papers used argumentation with referents of the biomedical field, which explains the academic readings of the phenomenon that privilege biological realities usually decontextualized from potentially determinant psychological and social issues
