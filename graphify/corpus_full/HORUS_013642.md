# Inter-organizational integration through the process of developing new products in the footwear and leather industry micro-enterprises in Bogotá

There are micro-enterprises in the footwear and leather industry that manage to differentiate themselves from the design and to involve the knowledge that circulates outside their borders.The research seeks to observe the deployment of their integration capacities throughout the development of new products (DNP) to describe the types and degrees of linkage that establish with other organizations. Fifteen micro-enterprises that execute DNP processes were selected classifying them into three groups: designer-commercializing (DC), designer-producers (DP) and designer-producers-commercializing (DPC), using the Design-based Research to collect and analyze the information. It was possible to establish that DC companies reach high levels of integration, especially with DP companies; that DP companies during the pre-development and development reach middle levels of integration with their customers and with DC and are the ones that establish the most links; that DPC companies are the ones that facilitate the integration of the design, achieving low levels of integration where the relationships with the clients predominate; and that companies led by designers can lead in the formulation of challenges in the DNP, encouraging the development of integration capacities.
