# Risk aversion and prenatal testosterone measurements

- Risk aversion can be measured in different ways, each with its pros and cons. This project seeks to evaluate risk aversion by different metrics for the same person and see if these metrics are consistent with each other regardless of context. Since there is debate about the relationship between risk aversion and other phenomena, such as proxies to prenatal testosterone, it will be studied whether the relationship with other variables depends on the measure used
