# INDICATIONS FOR USE OF PERTUZUMAB IN CANCER DE MAMA HER 2 NON-METASTASYCLE POSITIVE IN THE NEOADYUVANT AND ADYVANT SCENARIANS. REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVANCEMENT AT THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CANCEROLOGY - COLOMBIA

The addition of therapy for cytotoxic chemotherapy in patients with breast cancer has significantly improved oncological outcomes in patients with erb2-positive tumors. Pertuzumab use during neoadjuvant management significantly increases the complete pathological response and currently allows for similar anthracycline-free regimens and lower side effects. The benefit of adding Pertuzumab in the adjuvant scenario in patients without previous HER2-treatment is limited to positive ganglia. The implementation of HER2-controlled dual block regimens during initial HER2-positive breast cancer treatment will allow for significant improvement in prognosis in this group of patients
