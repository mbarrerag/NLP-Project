# IN VITRO ASSESSMENT OF THE PROBIOTIC PROPERTIES OF THE NATIVE MICROBIOTA ISOLATED FROM COLOMBIAN DOUBLE CREAM CHEESE

Countless health benefits are provided by the ingestion of foods containing probiotic cultures [1]. Double Cream Cheese is a type of artisanal Colombian cheese, manufactured in the regions of Ubaté and Chiquinquirá Valleys. This cheese contains a native microbiota that gives it its sensory organoleptic and physicochemical characteristics, mainly from the group of Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB) [2]. However, the probiotic capacity of this native microbiota is unknown. The aim of this study was to characterize the probiotic properties of some bacteria present in natural microbiota of Colombian Cheese
