# PEDAGOLOGICAL AS HEALTHY

After the health crisis and the consequent period of isolation, as a university professor of the Industrial Design program at the National University of Colombia, I have the experience of several academic periods that led to an important pedagogical transformation. The serious possibility that this period provided to re-evaluate knowledge and teaching performance was achieved thanks to the offer of courses that were promoted and to those that we accessed with institutional support.From this nutritious opportunity I have used digital concepts and resources to improve my work in the process of training of industrial designers (for example, to understand what virtual learning environments mean) and with this also to dimension roles and characteristics of the actors linked to the training processes.But beyond establishing common points or differentiators betweenpresentential education and that carried out in virtual environments it is a question of understanding that much of the variables that they characterize are part of any educational process independent of their modality (presential, virtual, hybrid) and what leads to it is to diversify the forms of interaction.This awareness has permeated the courses of which I am responsible, for the purpose of presenting the print-related process of the industrial development that has developed the dynamic that is applicable
