# Numerical modeling of the arc effect and sludge pressure in a directed horizontal drilling.

The construction of a horizontal guided drilling (PHD) has three (3) main stages: the preparation of the pilot tunnel, the widening of the drilling to the required diameter and the installation of the pipe. The above activities generate stresses and deformations in the soil in which the arc effect influences, the depth of the soil cover on the PHD and the pressure of the drilling sludges. In the work developed for the analysis of the construction of a PHD is used the program of 3D Plaxis computer, which has a module for the evaluation of tunnels, which facilitates the idealization and the modelling of the PHD. Through this module the user of Plaxis can analyze a sequence of construction steps that is repeated along the perforation in a number of selected sections. By means of these steps it can be analyzed the excavation and stabilization of a PHD, by means of the spatial removal of the soil volumes and the application of a stabilizing pressure on the walls of the excavation, which represents the progression of the PHD and the use of the drilling sludges respectively.
