# Comparative analysis of the profile of the vizomanual dominance and the characteristics of the ball-racquet impact on the youth tennis players of Bogotá.

The body of the human being, from the functional point of view is asymmetrical. In the organs of par configuration, usually the individual shows preference for the use of some particular side; this phenomenon has been called laterality. Manual laterality has had a predominant place. However, understanding the physiology beyond the representation of the function, it is necessary to assume an integrative posture; this involves the generation of certain motor responses as a result of the processing of information, coming from multiple sensory inputs in parallel, that support the efferious construction of complex actions. The present study proposal was motivated by the interest in the possible influence of possible combinations of laterality dominances (visual -sensory- and manual -motor-). This in response to the specific demands of field tennis as a thematic pretext. In this sense, the neuro diversity is recognized as a central element. Previous studies on performance and laterality have traditionally considered the manual or podal component as appropriate with the sport mode. In some approaches the cross profile (dominant eye and dominant hand) is analyzed as a possible factor of analysis with relation to the high achievement of sports
