# Introduction of aggregators to Colombian electricity distribution networks through a business vision

Abstract The main objective of this article was to establish the most significant parameters that could be used to promote the integration of Independent Information Management Agents (GIDI) based on an analysis of different international experiences in which these agents are known as +8220; adders +8221; to establish a business strategy around the functions and products that they can offer within a Colombian energy market. To this end, a study of the art focused on the different cases of success on a global scale was established; thus, with the help of dynamic systems and systemic thinking, the main parameters that govern the optimal functioning of these agents in the market were determined and, finally, through the Canvas model, a business strategy was established using the results of the systemic analysis. In this sense, the characteristics that enhance the entry of agents were obtained and the scenario and under which opinions the proposed system can be evaluated were established. Finally, a business option is established through the trade of demand response programs as a feasible scenario for analyzing the proposed model
