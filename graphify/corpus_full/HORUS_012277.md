# Evaluation of rapid pyrolysis in Auger type reactor (double screw) for the use of residual biomasses of agroindustrial origin in the department Norte de Santander.

This work consists of the design and implementation of an Auger type reactor, at a laboratory scale for processing 2 kg/h in order to carry out the rapid piolysis process of different types of biomass such as rice husk, coffee cisco and oil palm cousk. For this purpose, a methodology was carried out for designing sinphine screws with the steps for calculating diameters, filling areas, thermal expansion, rpm and power. In addition, an experimental design was carried out that allowed to evaluate rapid pyrolysis at the temperature of 550°C and 650°C in the three different biomasses with physicochemical characteristics of high potential for exploitation in thermochemical processes
