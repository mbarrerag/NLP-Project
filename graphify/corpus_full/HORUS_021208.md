# Feminist struggles in Colombia after the agreement. Claims of justice and organizational aspects

The role of women and Colombian feminist organizations in order to create the individual and their microsocial forms of organization for the construction of a new majority, seems decisive to judge by their spontaneous participation in the days unleashed in the mobilizations of 2019 and 2021, which adopted the name of Colombia wakes up, as well as by their massive intervention in the recent electoral process.This research aims to identify what type of understanding of their own capacities these sectors have, as well as to place themselves within the framework of the global and national system and to design the necessary organizational instruments to influence the construction of Colombia of the future.The Colombian social explosion is transferred with mobilizations of different social actors, each with its own organizational forms, as well as with capacities for the definition of particular interests and identities.To the mobilizations that began on April 28 to oppose the proposal of fiscal reform, called the Sustainable Solidarity Law, promoted by the Duque Government and its minister of finance Alberto Carrasquilla, were added the workers' centrals, social organizations, collectives of women and feminist organizations. This is where, in our perspective, the study of the organizational forms of different social sectors, but also of their capacities for defining interests and identities, is key. Thus, the research question on which we will advance is: What are the demands and forms of organizational articulation that guide the sociopolitical activity of Colombian feminist sectors in the context of the signing of the agreement for the termination of the 2016 conflict?
