# MMP-2, MMP-9 and TEMP-2 Salivales and tissue as biomarkers for the diagnosis and prognosis of oral squamous cell carcinoma

Oral squamocellular carcinoma (OSCC) is the most common malignant neoplasia in the oral cavity, with high morbidity and mortality. Various proteins, including matrix metaloproteinases (MMP), have been investigated as possible biomarkers for the diagnosis and prognosis of this disease. To evaluate the utility of MMP-2, MMP-9 and the tissue inhibitor of MMP-2 (TIMP-2), as biomarkers in the diagnosis and prognosis of OSCC.
