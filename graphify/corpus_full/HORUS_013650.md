# Effects of music therapy on the self-esteem of a group of people with visual impairment

This research aimed to carry out a quantitative and qualitative analysis of the effects of music therapy on the self-esteem of a group of people with visual disabilities, young students of different careers at the National University of Colombia. The methodology included measurements through music therapy registry instruments and Rosenberg's self-esteem scale at the beginning and end of the research, which provided the necessary information for the development of the intervention and its respective final evaluation. The results showed that students could be aware of the changes that promote their personal growth, improving their perception of well-being, taking into account the importance of maintaining a good self-concept, their autonomy, identifying their strengths and qualities and allowing them to express their emotions. This allowed building and maintaining adequate self-esteem at the end of the intervention music therapy is thus effective in strengthening the self-esteem of the group intervened mainly from the work with songs and methods of improvisation and receptiveness. .
