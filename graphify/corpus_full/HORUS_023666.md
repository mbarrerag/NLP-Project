# Types of carbapenemases expressed in Klebsiella spp., and Pseudomonas aeruginosa resistant to carbapenemics in six hospitals of high complexity of the City of Bogotá - Colombia

Abstract Introduction: In recent decades, the prevalence of infections by gram-negative bacillus resistant to carbapenemics has increased. To determine the types and frequency of the different carbapenemases in Klebsiella spp. and Pseudomonas aeruginosa isolates, in six hospitals of high complexity in Bogotá-Colombia. A descriptive observational study in six hospitals in the city of Bogotá, in the period January 2017 to August 2018. RPC was performed for genes of KPC, GES, VIM, NDM, IMP and OXA-48 in strains of Klebsiella spp and P aeruginosa resistant to carbapenemics. 52 isolates of P aeruginosa amplified for a carbapenemase, of which 39 (75%) were positive for KPC, 11 (21%) for VIM and 2 co-producciones of KPC and VIM. As for Klebsiella spp., 165 strains amplified for a carbapenemase, 98% expressed KPC and 4 years of isolated co- and PSC9 had co-related production
