# Give me a call and I'll co-produce projects, researchers and evaluators

In the academic and scientific field, the calls have various purposes, such as financing internships, research, monitoring or professional experiences, seeking that these activities respond to the spirit and nature of the institution that proposes it. For 2013, a private university in Bogotá, through the Vice-Rector of Research, formulates the Call No. 06 that seeks to finance interdisciplinary research projects. From this year until 2019, 286 research proposals were put forward and 162 were funded. This thesis shows the different accounts that the researchers, participants of the aforementioned call, elaborate around interdisciplinarity. To this end, the theoretical framework is guided by the concept of co-production, developed by Sheila Jasanoff (2004a), as well as by the categories that it proposes (identities, institutions, speeches and representations) with the aim of ordering the Call No. 06 and denote the different challenges, experiences, difficulties and wealth that have interdisciplinary and interdisciplinary research.
