# VIRUS OF AGRICULTURAL CULTURES OF COLOMBIA. ILLUSTRATE DIRECTORY.

Viruses are one of the groups of causal agents of diseases more limiting in humans, animals and plants; and although their importance as pathogens has no discussion, the difficulty for their diagnosis and identification is a restriction for the resolution of different practical challenges, including the availability of methods of control, the design of useful strategies to avoid their dispersion or their introduction to new regions; and for the plant case, the generation of resistant materials or seed certified by their viral health. For the elaboration of this directory a detailed search of the information available in the scientific literature, molecular databases, lists of national phytopathogens (especially the directory of Buriticá, 1999) and international repositories and other sources was carried out for the purpose of registering the viruses that have been reported in 20 of the most important agricultural crops in Colombia, which is accompanied by illustrations of the main symptoms that cause, their morphology, genomic organization, and their vectors or main means of transmission. Also, tables are presented for each virus with the main first, probes and commercial antibodies (when they are available) that are used for their molecular detection and/or serological. a list of the viruses reported in each host, as well as of the species that cause the greatest losses and that could represent a threat to national agriculture, in case they are introduced into the country. These lists present the complete names of the viruses and their acronyms recognized by the ICTV, in the list of species #37 (MSL #37, Master Species Lists, 2022), and for reasons of extension later in the document only refers to the acronyms. In the directory the nomenclature currently recognized by the ICTV, which has as its base English language and uses complex names for each species, is used. However, it is important to indicate that at present a process of definition of binomial names in virology is being carried out, which will surely in the future require updating of this aspect in the directory (Walker et al., 2021).
