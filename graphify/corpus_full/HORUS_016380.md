# EXPOCIENCE AS A DIDACTIC SCENE, TOWARDS A COLOMBIAN SOCIETY BASED ON SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE

For about 30 years, the Colombian Association for the Advancement of Science (Avanciencia), together with another series of activities, has been betting on an event as a strategy for achieving popularization, appropriation of science in Colombia. From the didactic perspective, the Science Fair, or Expociencia, as it is known, has been, from its beginnings, a bet from three approaches: on the one hand, what could be identified as an informal learning strategy1 around science, on the other, as a way to bring science closer to the school environment, but also, to relate the scientific community to society in general, to Colombians. The purpose of this paper is to explore how this relationship has been, the scope of this relationship, as well, the factors to be improved in the purpose of contributing to a society based on scientific knowledge, in the realization of the Expociencia - Expotecnologia
