# Planning factors affecting the implementation of projects in the territorial public sector.

Public investment projects in Colombia, continuously, report non-compliance with their objectives due to reasons analyzed under legal or contractual approaches, in which administrative concepts are not evaluated. Various entities, including control bodies, have indicated that poor planning is one of the causes of problems in the execution of public projects, but this concept is not detailed. This study seeks to understand the impact of planning on the final results of projects, considering the actual performance of project planning and the characterization of results in the dimensions of time, cost and scope, in order to explore relationships between them. The qualitative methodological approach of the study, part of an interpretative epistemological approach, complemented by an abductive working logic, leading to emerging categories for the analysis of the problem. Using content analysis, they reviewed contracting and planning documents, available in state information systems, for 30 projects of investment completed between 2015 and 2023 (first quadrimestre) by municipal public entities in the 30 Colombian cities of the largest population. The results show that the results are inefficient project structuring processes, which affect the quality or completeness of studies
