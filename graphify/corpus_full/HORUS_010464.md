# IN A HOSPITAL OF BOGOTA, 2022

the concern for the nursing work environment has become even greater since the pandemic by COVID-19, which affected the quantity and quality of nursing staff around the world. to describe the work environment of nurses who perform care functions in a hospital in Bogotá, Colombia. exploratory descriptive study. The questionnaire Enlasa-Infermeria was sent to all nursing care staff in the hospital studied through RedCap®. 50% of the nursing care staff (49 nurses) of the institution invited to participate in the study answered the complete questionnaire. 57.1% of respondents considered the unhealthy work environment.The structural elements dimension was seen as unhealthy (64.49/100), and the organizational processes as healthy (71.18/100).The sub-dimensions considered unhealthy in structure were the planning of the nursing resource (54.66/100) and human resources policies (58.91/100).With regard to processes were the interaction of nurse organization (63.10/100) and autonomy (66,07/100).The socio-motive outcomes were not found to be association with the single nursing environment (54.66/100) and the patient was not affected by the disease
