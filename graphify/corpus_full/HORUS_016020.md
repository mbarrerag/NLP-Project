# NETWORK OF UNIVERSITIES COMMITTED TO EDUCATION IN DDHH: A MODEL OF IBERO-AMERICAN UNIVERSITY COOPERATION WITH RESULTS

This chapter focuses on the analysis of the model of cooperation chosen for the Network in an informed way: the South-South model complemented by the Triangular model of partnerships with other organizations. The document is organized in five parts. The first presents the conceptual framework, when addressing the concepts of international cooperation, international cooperation for development, South-South cooperation and Triangular cooperation with its advantages and limitations and as organizational challenge for this Network and its positioning as signs of organizational identity. The second point addresses from the analysis of different international reports the concept and trends of university cooperation and its relationship with Education in Human Rights and Equality. Subsequently, the third and fourth points examine the relationship between Scientific Diplomacy and Scientific and Research Development of universities and the approach they propose for the organizational development of the Network. Finally, a series of partnerships with cooperation organizations for the further development of the Network are proposed
