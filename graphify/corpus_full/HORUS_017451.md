# BUILDING FORESTAL LIVING IN THE GUIDE TO GENERATING WIN-BASED MODELS IN ARBOLES

The change in the paradigm of extractive livestock requires the renewal and restructuring of the ways in which it has traditionally been managed and the choice of implementing sustainable, sustainable and environmentally friendly models. As an example of this, there are the silvopastoral systems in which the production of improved pastures, shrubs and trees is mixed that offer food and comfort sources to animals. For the implementation and maintenance of silvopastoral systems it is of great importance to have a nursery, understood as a breeding site and replication of plants for reforestation. In this sense, in the regeneration livestock, the nursery is the starting point for changing the traditional livestock production model; its use allows to attempt to reverse the environmental impact and improve the conditions of the animals. This document presents the necessary guidelines for the establishment and maintenance of forest nurseries and collects the experience derived from the implementation of silvopastoral systems from them. The book is the product of the project entitled "Resilient agricultural Systems in the humid for the rurality of the post-conflict country", attached to the call Colvopastoral Systems, 745 Innovation and 2016
