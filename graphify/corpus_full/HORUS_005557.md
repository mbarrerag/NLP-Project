# TRANSFOUNDATION OF THE ENGLISH WING IN A DIVURSIVE COMMUNITY

The aim of this article is to carry out a theoretical exploration of the very concept of discursive community and the pedagogical characteristics that an English class, regardless of its status as a foreign language or second language, must have to be treated as such. Likewise, this article is aimed at language teachers and researchers who are interested in enhancing their professional praxis and the significant learning of their students, since it makes sufficiently solid theoretical contributions for these education professionals to begin applying a new paradigm with their apprentices: the construction of a discursive community, which implies several specific properties such as critical thinking, meta-cognition, bidirectional interaction, exchange of ideas and opinions, horizontal learning, meaningful discursive practices, permanent participation, the construction of discourse relevant to real life contexts, among others. For a better understanding, this theoretical exploration is divided into three main sections; a) discursive community, b) discourse, and c) pedagogical characteristics of a discursive community
