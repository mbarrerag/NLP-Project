# Failure analysis of repetitive cracking experienced by the “Housing” of EH5000 trucks from the Hitachi manufacturer used in the open-pit coal mine of El Cerrejón (La Guajira - Colombia).

The maintenance division -Flota 320- guarantees the availability of Hitachi EH5000 trucks for the continuous transport of inert material in La Mina (Dept. La Guajira). This activity has become a real challenge due to the frequent cracking of the structural elements called “Housing”, which presented seven catastrophic failures in the period 2009–2011. After understanding the philosophy of joint tensor bars and by recreating by FEM analysis, the operation cycle of the mining trucks: Cargo -Transport - Volkting Cycle -Return -Position, it was possible to determine how the fracture of tension bars alters the level of effort in different areas of the “Housing”; and in turn, how it affects life to fatigue using the criteria of the standard BS 7608, two cargo spectras (mode and theoretical) and the theory of cumulative damage Palmgren-Miner. These results were compared to the failure analysis for three failed components (“Housing”, Tight bars and Screw); where it was able to determine that they experienced failure due to fatigue.
