# IDENTIFICATION AND FIDELIZATION OF AFFILITIES IN UNDERTAKINGS WITHOUT LUCRO: COMMUNICATION AND ACCOUNT RENEWAL

The Unanimous Entities of Lucro (ESAL) face a complex relationship with their stakeholders.For their performance to be successful, it is crucial to implement strategies that link the social value of their mission to their management, enhancing their legitimacy without affecting their operational efficiency.Objective:This work aims to identify the communication factors used by the ESAL in their accountability, which generate identification and retention of the members of the Colombian Association of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (ACOPI) Bogotá.Cundinamarca in the period 2016-2020. A qualitative, descriptive and interpretative approach was adopted, based on interviews with members of the Board of Directors and ACOPI officials.Results:The organization has developed an effective strategic communication that favors the retention of affiliates, the positioning of its brand and the fulfilment of its mission, using interactive channels as a traditional one.Members show a strong affective and behavioral attachment, which reinforces their identification and permanence in the aggregation. The analysis revealed that the fulfillment of the mission improves organizational performance and strengthens the link of trust with the affiliates
