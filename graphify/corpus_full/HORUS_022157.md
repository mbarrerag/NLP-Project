# Consensus for the treatment of high urinary tract infection during pregnancy.

To generate informed recommendations in the evidence, through a formal consensus, oriented to the treatment of the infection of the upper urinary tract during pregnancy. The group developed counted with the participation of thematic experts in Microbiology, Public Health, Internal Medicine, Infectology, Obstetrics, Fetal Maternal Medicine and Gyneco-obstetric and perinatal Infectology. They also participated in this group with training in clinical epidemiology and in systematic search of information. The group also had the participation of representatives of the health secretariat and the Bogotana Association of Obstetrics and Gynecology. The participants presented their conflicts of interest. From a clinical question the graduation of the outcomes was carried out and a systematic search was carried out that included the databases PubMed, Embase, Lilacs, Bireme. The pesquisa was extended to institutional repositories and reports of surveillance of antimicrobial resistance, without restriction of language or date, the search was updated on October 1, 2022. The methodology used was the initial and the initial evaluation. and as a third option the use of piperacillin/tazobactam. It is recommended to modify antibiotic therapy guided by the result of the antibiotic. Among the key points of consensus are the suggested therapeutic schemes as well as the duration of therapy
