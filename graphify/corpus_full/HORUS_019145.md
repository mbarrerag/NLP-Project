# TECNOCRACY, DECISION-MAKING AND ECONOMIC POLICY IN COLOMBIA

The analysis of public policies has strong theoretical and practical connections with economic analysis and, in this sense, the chapter on economic policies allows understanding the relationship. It analyzes how economic experts or those called economic technocrats have played a crucial role in building capacity for the analysis of public policies in four moments of shocks experienced by the Colombian economy between 1980 and 2016: the debt crisis, the institutional change of the 1991 constitution, the 1999 crisis and the oil boom that began near 2015. In general, the combination of institutional factors (capacity of institutions to adapt to changes in politics) is observed along with the formation of an expert technocratism that could assume short-term changes
