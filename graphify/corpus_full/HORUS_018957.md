# Model for the recommendation of collective investment funds for natural persons in the Colombian financial market.

The Collective Investment Funds (FIC) are a mechanism or vehicle for promoting savings and investment in the national capital market, through which several people deposit resources collectively in accordance with the regulations of the respective fund. A study carried out by ASOFIFUCIARIAS in 2018, showed that the FICs are a product on which there is knowledge and even generates quite confusion in the Colombian population. Currently, the diffusion of this mechanism is scarce; moreover, the advice provided by the administering companies is biased under their own products and do not allow to evaluate the total supply of the market. This work proposes a model of recommendation of FIC that allows autonomously through a standardized questionnaire of risk profiling to connect users with the universe of available vehicles. To develop this model, a process of purification of the available information and calculation of the profitability and volatility of the portfolios is carried out by means of the profile of the FICC and clustering.
