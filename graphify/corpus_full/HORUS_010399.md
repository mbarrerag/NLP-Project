# THE GLOBAL CHALLENGES OF THE LONG COVID-19 IN ADULTS AND CHILDREN

During the last three and a half years (2020'2023), the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), caused by the severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2), generated an international emergency, defined as a Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC) and was later declared a pandemic by the World Health Organization (WHO)
