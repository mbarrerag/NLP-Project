# Neutron flux modelling of the IAN-R1 nuclear reactor using data-driven technologies

The conventional models for predicting the power of nuclear reactors often face difficulties due to uncertainties in system parameters, loss of dynamic information by simplifications or linearizations and, in some cases, inadequate calibration of control bars that affects the accuracy in the modeling of inputs. This work presents a novel approach to modeling the power of the IAN-R1 nuclear reactor in Colombia, using the framework of the operator of Koopman by means of the Decomposition in Extended Dynamic Mode (EDMD). Various DMD algorithms were tested to identify the most suitable in the approximation of the Koopman matrix, ensuring a better representation of the system. Despite the challenges inherent in the acquisition of operational data of the reactor, as incomplete or inconsistent data sets, it was possible to construct a comprehensive and reliable database, capable of capturing the normal behavior of the reactor. The proposed model from the present in relation to the reactor, but also provides predictions about the rate of radiation., ensurring better system representation. Despite the inherent challenges associated with the reactor’s operational data acquisition, such as incomplete or inconsistent datasets, a comprehensive and reliable database was successfully constructed, capable of capturing the reactor’s normal operating better. The proposed model not only estimates the reactor’s power but also forecasts for the radiation dose rate. The validation of the Koopman model was conducted by comparing its policies with those of other models, and the results were further evaluated as an ongoing operating data from the reactor. The findings demonstrate that the proposed model shapes well, offering adaptability to changes in core configuration and system parameters
