# DEVELOPMENT AND ASSESSMENT OF A METHOD FOR QUANTIFICATION OF GRASS ACIDS (FAMS) IN FOODSTUFFS BY THE GAS CRIMATOGRAPHY TECHNICAL.

Fatty acids are the main component of lipids and play a crucial role in biological systems. Therefore, it is vital to have qualitative and quantitative analytical methods to analyze these compounds in different food matrices. Methods The method for determining fatty acid content by gas chromatography will be implemented and selected, through the evaluation of the resolution of chromatographic signals and asymmetry. The performance parameters of the measurement method to be evaluated will be selectivity, linearity, accuracy and bias. Results and Conclusions Validated analytical method for the quantification of fatty acids by gas chromatography GC-FID and GC-MSD in foodstuffs with its declaration of fitness validity for intended use
