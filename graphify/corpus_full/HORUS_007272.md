# Protocol for the nutritional care of hospitalized oncological patients

Cancer is one of the main causes of death in Colombia and the world. It is already recognized that timely and adequate nutritional care contributes to better clinical outcomes in cancer patients. In this sense, the proposal of this book is to gather a set of technical elements necessary for nutritional care, based on updated scientific literature and Colombian legislation, to generate a protocol oriented to the standardization of nutritional care provided by nutritionists, doctors and among other professionals, who participate in the comprehensive care of the oncological patient of hospital institutions. This text, concise and adapted to the national reality, is an excellent facilitator for the process of creation and adaptation of protocols for nutritional care of oncological patients, indispensable document for the quality of care.-- Contracadas - In this text a protocol of standardized nutritional care for the hospitalised oncological patient is presented. The authors developed a specific protocol and coupled with the needs of the National University Hospital of Colombia and, in order to share the experience with the different hospital institutions of the country, a protocol of consultation for nutrition professionals is generated, in order to manage the nutritional care of each hospital. The authors develop a specific protocol and the nutritional care, and the latter is provided with the relevant legislation
