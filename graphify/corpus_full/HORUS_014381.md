# Nephrectomy complications due to oncological pathology in a national pediatric hospital.

Complications in nephrectomy by oncological pathology in a pediatric hospital of national reference According to the world literature, renal tumors equal approximately 7% of all diagnoses in childhood cancer, in our hospital, because they are purely pediatric with emphasis on on oncological pathology, there is a high concentration of patients with renal oncological pathology, hence we proposed to evaluate the population that has been taken to nephrectomy by renal tumour pathology, evaluating its demographic characteristics and characterizing the postoperative complications by applying the Clavien / Dindo scale as a method to make them assessable and comparable with the world literature. A retrospective cohort study in a period of time that includes from June 2012 to June 2022, the data of patients operated by renal oncological pathology were collected and that had to be taken to nephrectomy either total or partial, including these sociodemographic variables and making a judging review of clinical history to evaluate the presence or not of postoperative complications and categorizing them according to the Clavin Dindo scale. It also shows that the milder complications are widely overlooked in the literature and that, in fact, they represent the highest percentage of deviations from the normal postoperative course. Within the variables evaluated, only a greater hospital stay was found in patients with Clavien Dindo complications greater than or equal to III, with an average stay of 28 days, compared to 14 days in patients who did not, with a statistically significant difference (p<0.05). However, we consider that this finding should be considered a consequence, but not a cause of Clavien Dindo complications greater or equal to III. This study should serve as a starting point to standardize the categorization of complications through the Clavien/Dindo scale with the intention of finding improvement points and standardizing behaviors regarding pediatric cancer care
