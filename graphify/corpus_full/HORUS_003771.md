# SUSTAINABLE FINANCES: BANKING SECTOR

This book offers a complete analysis of sustainable finance, especially in the Colombian banking context. Through a combination of theory, practice and prospective reflection, this book aims to be the road map for those financial institutions that seek to implement sustainability and participate in a fair, equitable and sustainable transformation. Through a detailed exploration of various topics, this work offers a comprehensive vision of how finance with environmental, social and governance criteria play a crucial role in sustainable development not only in Colombia, but also in the region. The chapters that make up this collective work reflect on the main elements of sustainable finance, sustainable investment portfolios, innovation in sustainable financing, thematic bonds, social finance, fair energy transition, financial innovation, financing of agro-food systems, smart cities, governance, taxonomys, trends in regulation and the role of the banking sector in climate action. Additionally, with a prospective approach, it examines how the banking sector can evolve towards the principles of responsible banking for 2030. This book seeks to be a benchmark of sustainable finance in the financial sector, as well as an essential reading for the banking sector committed to transformation
