# CHARACTERIZATION OF DIDACTICAL CHANGES IN THE UNIVERSITARY PRACTICE OF ORGANOMETAL CHEMICAL

This paper presents the first study of the characterization of didactic changes in the teaching practice of a university professor of organometallic chemistry, in relation to the inclusion of the nature of science in his educational work and his didactic knowledge of the content. To this end, a multiparadigmatic and plurimethodological approach was adopted and a process of reflection was carried out with the teacher throughout an academic year, through spaces of joint discussion. The implementation of other data collection instruments, such as interviews and class observations, complemented the information collected in the working sessions. Due to the emergency caused by the COVID-19, the intervention was carried out in a virtual way. The data obtained were analyzed in terms of the categories of work proposed. Among the results, the understanding of the reflection of the practice as an essential process in which to do teaching (attitudinal), the emergence of alternative forms of explanation (procedimental), and the recognition of the importance of addressing historical knowledge within the classroom (conceptual).
