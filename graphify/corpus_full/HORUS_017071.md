# TRANSLATION THROUGH THE RSI INDICATOR WITH THE APPLICATION OF GENETIC ALGORITMS FOR IMPLEMENTING IN THE Entrepreneurship SECTOR OF INVESTMENT

This article proposes for the literature on investment of variable income assets a methodology applied through Genetic Algorithms (AG) and its implementation in the business investment sector. The study was developed by simulation of B&H, AT and AG investment strategies on the stock index. The process of maximization of investment return for financial assets was carried out using Genetic Algorithms, which were based on equations defined in the chromosomal typification with operations immersed in genes. It was initiated with a random population of individuals with chromosomes representing a solution to achieve the highest possible yield. The application of algorithms with the RSI generated returns greater than 9 and 16% with respect to B&H and technical analysis, which means higher investment risk for B&H with volatility 17.6% but comparable to those exhibited by genetic algorithms and technical analysis. Better performance-risk and efficiency ratio in fundamental parameters of Portfolio Theory is possible through investment strategies based on genetic algorithms including the RSI oscillator.
