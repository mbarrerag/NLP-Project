# Preventive and corrective maintenance of refrigeration equipment and autoclave of the Industrial Microbiology laboratory (19A-312).

In the laboratory of industrial microbiology attached to the Faculty of Sciences, corrective maintenance of two freezers, a refrigerator and a horizontal autoclave is required to ensure the proper preservation and sterilization of reagents and laboratory materials. The maintenance of the horizontal autoclave is essential given the safety conditions for users and visitors of the laboratory and block 19A which represents the malfunction of this equipment. Additionally, preventive maintenance of five refrigeration equipment (fridges and freezers) is required to ensure its proper functioning during the normal execution of cold storage activities of laboratory materials
