# HYMANGIOSARCOMA AND LYMPOMA IN CANINE NETHERLANDS: CASE REPORT

in pets neoplasms are one of the main causes of death, thus, individual contributions on atypical cases are fundamental to deepen the understanding of tumor biology of these entities. Anamnesis: a surgical piece belonging to a canine Schnauzer, female, 12 years old, of Bogotá was received. Clinical findings: moderate splenomegaly. Macroscopic findings: soft nodules, dark grey 2 x 1.3 cm, and 3 x 3cm respectively were observed in the head and body of the spleen. Histological findings: In spleen two processes were observed: non-encapsulated neoplastic proliferation of round cells that expands the lymphoid follicles of the white pulp and generates coalescence between them, invading the splenic capsule, solid nodular and central nucloids, cytoplatic cells, size of 1.5 erythrocytes, anisocytosis and mild pleumorphism, round nuclei, chromatin depleas, and mild granular pleats, and a central nuclis, anis. to investigate new cases, biological behaviour and possible clinical implications that may be associated with the presence of both entities in the same organ
