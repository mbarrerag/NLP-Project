# PUBLIC, UNIVERSAL, AUTONOMOUS AND SOVEREIGN HEALTH SYSTEM

Undoubtedly, the unprecedented context of the pandemic by covid-19 and the social outbreak lived in Colombia for several weeks brought the issue of health, as never before had happened, to the center of social concerns and public opinion. In that turn, one of the triumphs of social and popular mobilization was the archive of the government bill 010 of 2020, which sought to deepen the process of privatization of the health sector initiated by Law 100 of 1993. A triumph that led to the assembly deliberations to ask themselves where to go with the demands of health in the country and in whose response, with evident emphasis, the alternative focuses on the need to establish a new health system in the country, that ends once for all the EPS and returns to the public in the driving and delivery of the health services. This view to where to go must take what has been configured for more than two decades, product of organizational processes and multiple collective discussions given in meetings, workshops, forums, assemblies, congresses, national and international health-care systems
