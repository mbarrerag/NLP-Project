# TEXTURE CLASSIFICATION OF COMPLEX VECTOR VORTEX BEAMS

Recently, design and generation of vector vortex beams changing its transverse spatial modulated features upon propagation demand the development of reliable characterization tools. In this contribution, for the first time to our knowledge, we proposed a simple texture classification algorithm to evaluate changes depending on topological charge in numerically generated Laguerre-Gauss vector beams. In order to take into account all of the information, our approach decouples the complex electromagnetic field in three grayscale images (channels), where each pixel provide the local light intensity, orientation angle of the polarization ellipse major semi-axis, and ellipticity angle. In order to classify the vector field and because of its transverse symmetry, an azimuthally-sensitive gray-level co-occurrence matrix is constructed and statistical Haralick features calculated. It is worth to mention that the analysis of these vector vortex beams is based in digital image processing and depends on the spatial sampling and bit-depth. However, there are methodologies in image texture analysis to compensate this limitations. In our opinion, these exciting results can be easily extended not only to the case of transverse vector structure changes upon propagation, but also to more complex vector vortex beams
