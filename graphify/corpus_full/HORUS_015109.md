# Primary socialization process in three women diagnosed with Turner syndrome linked to the Turner Colombia Heart Syndrome Foundation

The process of primary socialisation in women diagnosed with Turner syndrome linked to the Foundation Heart Turner Syndrome Colombia Turner syndrome is a genetic disorder that affects women exclusively and is characterized by the absence or incompletion of an X chromosome, which generates various problems in physical and psychological development. Primary socialization, for its part, is the process by which people learn the norms and values necessary to develop in society. In this sense, this research focuses on investigating the experiences of primary socialization in three women diagnosed with Turner syndrome. From the perspective of social construction, it is understood that socialization is not a natural process, but is built by social interactions in a certain cultural context. Therefore, qualitative techniques were used to reconstruct the life stories, in order to describe their experiences in the socialization process. Thus, it is necessary that among the circumstances that can affect primary socialization living with this condition, the limitation of interaction with men imposed by parents and the lack of social skills.
