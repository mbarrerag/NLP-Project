# Clinical Standard Based on Evidence: Diagnosis and Treatment of Outpatient with Chronic Heart Failure at the National University Hospital of Colombia.

Chronic heart failure (CFC) is a clinical syndrome characterized by congestion. Due to population aging, the overall incidence of CCF has increased. In Europe, it is presented with a prevalence of 1–2% in adults. The European registry of CCF has shown that adherence to the recommendations of clinical practice guides has a direct relationship with survival. In the National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN), CCF represents a major disease. During 2019, 604 patients with this condition were treated, representing a total of 761 outpatient consultations. The Clinical Standard Based on Evidence (ECBE) seeks to reduce variability in the diagnosis and treatment process of CCF in the UF, using an interdisciplinary approach and based on the best available evidence.
