# DEVELOPMENT OF PHARMACEUTICAL AND COSMETICAL PRODUCTS FROM THE FRUIT OF PERUVIAN PHYSALIS

Based on research carried out with the fruit of Physalis peruviana in the research group, and its application in the pharmaceutical and cosmetic sectors, the aim is to show the results and methodological path built over fifteen years that could serve as a model for the development of similar studies
