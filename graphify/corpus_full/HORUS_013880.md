# AGEING AND AGEING IN COLOMBIA AND THEIR DEPARTMENTS: DEMOGRAPHICAL Aging AND SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT. LIVING CONDITIONS OF THE OLD INCLUDED IN CHANGES BEFORE AND AFTER CIVID-19 PANDEMIA. ENTRY AND GUY TO STRENGTHEN PUBLIC AGEING AND AGEING POLICIES

The ageing of the population of Colombia is a decisive and irreversible process; in recent decades the growth of the largest population has high levels compared to the other vital stages and with the whole of the country; for the period 2015-2020 the growth of the largest population was 4.46%, for the period 2020-2025 this growth was 3.52%; while the growth of the total population is estimated at 1.7% and 1.04% for the same periods (annual average growth of the five-year period) (DANE 2023). Studies on the subject indicate that the social development of the departments (GDP, rurality, poverty) goes hand in hand with the progress in demographic transitions that are the context of the ageing of the populations (Medina M. et al., 2020). Under this perspective, the first specific objective of this research is to: to assess the demographic ageing in the context of the social development of the departments, considering: the index of ageing, the rate of population growth of the population with 60 and more years, the feminization of old age, rurality, GDP and the monetary poverty. It is proposed to identify the second degree of the groups of the departments, where each grouping of the interior presents a certain type of the population (Decent; Díaz Pinzón 2020). Considering the importance of considering the implications of the health, economic and social emergency among the elderly, the third specific objective of this research is: in the framework of the socio-economic conditions of life of the elderly, to assess the changes before and after the health, economic and social emergency generated by Covid-19, with an emphasis on indicators of economic activity, health conditions and use of ICT (information and communication technologies (ECV 2019, 2022, DANE). A final product of this research proposal is to harmonize the indicators referring to the living conditions of the elderly, with main structuring of certain public policies of old age and ageing, with the interest of identifying parameters and useful guides for social planning directed to this population group (MINSALUD 2020; SDIS 2015). Another final product of this project is the production of academic material that includes: academic content (themes and activities according to time intensity), presentations illustrating the professor's exhibitions, and workshops based on the data used in the project, to illustrate the teaching of descriptive and multivariate methods applied in the research
