# Design of lighting, control and beautification of the football court of Alfonso López Stadium.

The purpose of the present work is to show the design of the lighting of the flagship sports scene of the city of Bucaramanga (Colombia), the Alfonso López Stadium, as well as the results obtained, understanding that a football team transmits through a party the passion of a city with this sport, being fundamental for the enjoyment of the actors involved, such as players, spectators, viewers and arbitral college, the adequate lighting of the sports grounds for the development of the game. Condition that is currently not fulfilled in the best way, given the low qualification granted by FIFA to the stadium of the city. This work proposes the embellishment of the sports grounds through the modernization of lighting to LED technology luminaries, focusing on the court, the facades and the stadium stands, fulfilling the requirements issued by FIFA and providing the sports scene with a classification of Class V, which will allow the creation of international television and national non-televised parties, as well as other lighting configurations for other events through a control system.
