# Development of premixtures based on banana flour (Musa AAB Simmonds) and cocoa liqueur (Theobroma Cocoa L.) for the manufacture of beverages and baked products

The main purpose of this project is to determine the effect of the proportion of banana flour and cocoa liquor on the physical and chemical properties of pre-list mixtures for the production of beverages and baked products brownie type. The potential for the use of fermented and dry cocoa beans (Theobroma cacao L.) and green banana (Musa paradisiaca AAB) from the municipality of El Peñón, Cundinamarca, Colombia will be used. The conditions of the process for obtaining cocoa liquor and pre-cooked banana flour will be established. First, these raw materials will be characterized by the proximal analysis of moisture content, fat, ash, protein, raw fibre, carbohydrates following the official methodology of the A.O.A.C. Then, the formulations of the mixtures in powder for the beverage and for the mixture of the product will be established by a product not trained with a proportion of products. Thus, the increase in demand for local products would benefit the peasants of the region, which would expand business opportunities for this cocoa-producing area
