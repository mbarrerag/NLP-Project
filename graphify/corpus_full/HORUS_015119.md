# INTERSECTIONAL FEMINISM TO DENATURE VIOLENCES

This chapter examines the transformations that feminism has undergone by incorporating into its agenda demands that link it to the struggles against exploitation, extraction, oppression or domination experienced by racialized women, that is, those who have been subjected to a relationship of power that racializes them
