# OPTIMAL CONTROL OF AN ELLIPTIC PROBLEM

In this talk, the basic concepts of optimal control problems restricted by partial differential equations (DDS) will be introduced. Some practical examples will be presented where this theory plays an important role in the development and solution of technological, environmental and medical problems. With a concrete example, we will illustrate how using results of the analysis of PSD and the variable calculation, we can answer the three characteristic questions that we face when dealing with an optimal control problem. These are: Establishing the existence and/or uniqueness of optimal control and state, obtaining necessary and/or sufficient optimality conditions and finally constructing a numerical scheme to solve the problem
