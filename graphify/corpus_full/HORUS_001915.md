# EXISTENCE OF THREE POSITIVE SOLUTIONS FOR A p-SUBLINEAR PROBLEM INVOLVING A SCHRÖDINGER p-LAPLACIAN TYPE OPERATOR

We prove the existence of three positive solutions for the problem (Formula Presented) where λ > 0, ∆ p is the p-Laplacian operator, (Formula Presented) is a bounded domain in R N with connected and smooth boundary. In our study, V ∈ L ∞ (Ω) and f: [0, ∞) → R is a C 1 function. The reaction term, f, is increasing and p-sublinear at infinity.
