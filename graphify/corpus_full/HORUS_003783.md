# Shiny implementation for the law of major numbers

This project develops a web application for the visualization of the weak law of large numbers through the Shiny package of statistical software R. The main motivation of the project is to support the learning and teaching of convergence in probability, a subject covered in the inference subject, this application allows users to interact and explore three distributions: Normal, Exponential and Poisson, this generates a greater understanding for the different types of users, including those who are far from the statistic. The application is very intuitive and provides the results of convergence in probability of sample mean to the population average, using the data generated from these three distributions
