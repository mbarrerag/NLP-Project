# LOSS THE FIRE. NEW POETICS FROM THE LATIN AMERICAN ACCOUNT

The book entitled Prender el fuego. New poetics of the contemporary Latin American story (eds. Iván Padilla and Diana Diaconu) is an anthology of criticism about the current Latin American story carried out by the research group Debates Critics and Theoreticals on Contemporary Latin American Literature, whose direction I assume. This group was developed following an old Semillero of research and is composed by graduates of our career of Literary Studies and our Mastership. The book contains an introductory study followed by nine chapters on the following storytellers, authors of innovative poetics of the genre: Álvaro Enrigue, Ricardo Piglia, Rodrigo Fresán, Roberto Bolaño, Rodrigo Rey Rosa, Juan Villoro, Fernando Iwasaki and Albalucía Ángel. It focuses on the reformulation of the genre in the contemporary, more dynamic and comprehensive studies that we have in the new Latin American map. Our intention is to open a space of debate, of authentic dialogue, of confrontation of views to allow the Colombian reader to take up to a different, more dynamic and more extensive issue
