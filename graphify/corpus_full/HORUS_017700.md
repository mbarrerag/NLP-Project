# POLITICAL PROJECTS DISPUSTING PRESIDENCY IN COLOMBIA

The article analyzes the results of the first round of the presidential election in Colombia and examines - in the face of the second round - the political projects of both the League of Anti-Corruption Rulers of candidate Rodolfo Hernández, and of Gustavo Petro’s Historical Pact. Rather than taking a position in the face of a presidential candidacy, the text aims to provide elements of analysis, for an understanding of the political moment, accompanied by a preliminary assessment of the aforementioned political projects, including the options of stabilizing the tendencies to the crisis of the class domination regime that can be synthesized between disguised authoritarianism and moderate reformism
