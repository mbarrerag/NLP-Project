# Biomechanical study of technical gesture of the Tejo launch.

the roof is an ancestral sport, with pre-Columbian records original of the Muisca culture in the Cundiboyacense altiplano, declared national sport in Colombia since the year 2000. It has been objective of biomechanics studies focused on kinematics as parameter to achieve greater performance, however, there are no works with clinical focus that describe regular kinematic patterns. to identify and analyze the regular patterns of the body kinematics of the technical gesture of the Tejo launch. a transversal descriptive study was designed, 3 professional athletes of the Bogotá tejo league were included, with a total of 15 releases, of which the 3 best releases of each sportsman were processed. The movement capture was made with cameras of high speed 120Hz and the data was processed with video analysis software in the coronal and sagital plane was more than 89%. a total of 14 variables of joint mobility in the sagittal and coronal plane was identified as the articulation with maximum mobility of 96 ± 17°, maximum bending of 120 ± 16°
