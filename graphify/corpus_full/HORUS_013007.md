# Design and validation of the questionnaire to measure delirium knowledge (CCD)

to design a questionnaire to measure the knowledge levels of nursing staff regarding the care of patients with delirium and to determine their apparent validity, content, construct and reliability. methodological research that consisted in the development and verification of the validity of a questionnaire. the present study presented two phases. The first phase, in which 92 items and 4 categories were established by means of the literature review, as well as its evaluation by 7 experts in which the content validity and its application were established to 11 nurses for the apparent validity; and a second phase in which the construct and reliability validity was performed with its application to 300 nurses. after its application and analysis, 9 categories and 31 definitive items were established for the final questionnaire, which reached the psychometric properties necessary to determine the knowledge of the care of the patient with the delirium. the clarity of the facial validity using the Kappa de Fleiss with the criteria of clarity was 0.82 and 0.81 and of accuracy 0.78.
