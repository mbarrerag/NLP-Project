# Risk of gastric cancer in patients with atrophic gastritis and intestinal metaplasia in stages OLGA and OLGIM III and IV: systematic review and meta-analysis

Recent studies have found that patients with OLGA II, may also have long-term risk of gastric cancer (CG) and even more if they have first-degree family members with GC. The objective of this meta-analysis is to determine whether OLGA II-IV stages have risk of GC and what is their magnitude, as well as to estimate whether risk increases when there are first-degree family members with GC. Systematic review and meta-analysis. They will be looked at in several databases, including PubMed, EMBASE, Medline and the Cochrane library, articles published in the last 10 years until May 2024 on the association between the different stages OLGA/OLIM and the risk of GC. Statistical analysis with estimation of the effect in terms of Odds Ratio (OR), relative risk (RR) and confidence interval of 95%. Heterogenerity taken with I2 and Chi2.
