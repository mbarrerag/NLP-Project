# SEMI-INFINITE PROGRAMMING: THEORY, ALGORITHMS AND INDUSTRIAL APPLICATIONS

Semi-infinite programs (SIPs) are mathematical programs with finitely many variables and infinitely many constraints. A non-trivial problem is finding a constructive algorithm that provides such a solution. In this talk, the current fundamental ideas to solve SIPs are classified into two classes: discretization/exchange and local reduction approaches. The advantages and disadvantages of the different algorithms are discussed. Then, recent applications in robust parameter estimation in the food industry and robust dynamic optimization in chemical plants are also highlighted. Finally, a discussion on remaining challenges and future research directions are presented
