# Predictors of clinical activity in a Colombian cohort in a patient with early rheumatoid arthritis.

The project was carried out from a historical cohort of the Cónica de Arthritis Reumatoid de la fundación santa fe de Bogotá, selecting patients with onset of symptoms less than 12 months. We sought to find factors associated with sustained short-term remission defined as DAS28 VSG <2.6, DAS 28 PCR <2.6, SDAI <3.3 CDAI <2.8 in at least two consecutive occasions where the time between the first and last visit in which the disease was in remission was ≥ 6 months or ≥ 24 months sustained long-term remission. Univarial analysis and multivariate analysis of Logistic regression was performed for the selection of variables Results: 165 patients were included. Sustained short-term remission by DAS 28 VSG was achieved in 74% of patients, remission by DAS 28 PCR in 50% of patients, CI= 0.62 and CI- 5 years of age
