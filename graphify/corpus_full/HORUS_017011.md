# CHAPTER 3 IMPORTANCE AND RELEVANCE OF THE TEACHING AND USE OF GEOINFORMATIVE TOOLS IN ARCHITECTURE

The geoinformatics tools are part of a wide group of technological resources to refer and locate objects in the terri- thorium, which have been used for decades in some disci- plinas to improve the response to certain problems.In the faculties of architecture, the topography and cartography are spoken as tools to position architectural and urban projects without deepening their knowledge and management, and other tools are unknown that would allow better results and saving of resources.The tools of this type closer to the work of the architect and builders are the geopositioners or gps that, by the hand of the cartography, support the work of those dedicated to the urban projects, or understand the importance of the management of the environment for the location and orientation of their projects.The curricular meshes of the programs of the arquitec- ture and the constructional ones must contain, as foundation, knowledge related to the geography and its tools of location and reference of ob- jetos in the space and in the territory, and as a requirement to advance towards the stage of the disciplinary formation, in which the students and the natural resources are involved. in the areas of cartography, planning, topography, reading and interpretation of maps and aerophotography, landscape reading, ethnography and other topics that complement the work of the architect as a constructor of the human habitat.
