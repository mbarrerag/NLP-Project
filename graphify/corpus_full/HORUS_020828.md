# STATUS, NATION AND CONFLICT IN CONTEMPORARY UKRAINE.

The intention of the book Between East and West. State, nation and conflict in contemporary Ukraine is not much less to take stock of the EuroMaydan and its effects on Ukraine today but, on the contrary, to open up a panorama of the past that allows to place the dramatic events of 2014 in a broader historical perspective and thus contribute to a better understanding. This book is composed of texts of five authors that bring particular perspectives, but that, despite this particularity, revolve on a common thematic axis that is that of the controversial existence of Ukraine as a border land between East and West. Each of the individual contributions covers a specific stage of Ukrainian history leading thus, in progressive deepening, from the most remote times, through the nineteenth century and the long Soviet stage to the most recent political and social upheavals of independent Ukraine
