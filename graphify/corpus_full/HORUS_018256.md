# Bases for the structuring of a rural health model in Arauca

Objective This work aimed to identify some successful models of rural health care which facilitate the construction of a model of rural health care with a differential and intercultural approach for the department of Arauca, Colombia. Methods A systematic review was carried out based on databases such as BVS, PubMED, SciELO, and LILACS. Results The main finding highlights the need to build a health system based on a model of primary health care (PHC) with a community approach. In order to do this it is necessary to apply strategies related to human talent, management of care networks and others such as the use of technological tools; all focused on providing more efficient and affordable care, aligned with the Colombian health system and adapted to the department of Arauca
