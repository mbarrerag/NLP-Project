# Analysis of the process of building up innovation capacities in informal firms in developing countries: an approach from agent-based modelling

Innovation in the informal sector is a process that is very little known, not only because of the difficulty of obtaining information from this type of firms, but also because from the literature of the innovation studies little attention has been paid to these actors, among other reasons because having a strategy of imitation of innovations, they are not considered as innovative actors. However, for the developing countries in general and Latin American in particular, it is essential to understand this phenomenon given the great weight that the informal sector has in their economies, both in terms of Gross Domestic Product, and in terms of employment. Being outside the State radar, studying informal firms from a longitudinal and systemic perspective presents many difficulties that can be overcome from the computational model, so that a model based on agents representing the emergence of an innovation system that is the result of the interaction of heterogeneous agents, between those who are informal agents and those who traditionally have been included in the conventional approach is proposed, therefore it is possible to propose scenarios that allow to analyze the process of accumulation of capacities of these firms in the system. Through this analysis, it is possible to identify the best performance of the agents of informal agents that are involved and those who traditionally are included in the conventional approach. of Gross Domestic Product and in terms of employment. Since it is outside the State's radar, studying informal firms from a longitudinal and systematic perspective presents many difficulties that can be overcome through computational modelling, which is why an agent-based model is proposed that represent the emergence of an innovation system that is the result of the interaction of heterogeneous agents, including informal agents. With the proposed model, it is possible to propose scenarios that allow the analysis of the processes of capacity accumulation of these firms within the framework of the work of the system.
