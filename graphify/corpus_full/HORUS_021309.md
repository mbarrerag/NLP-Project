# Evaluation of the neuroprotective potential of myelinization, of an extract from the genus Zanthoxylum (Rutaceae) with LXR agonist activity

Multiple sclerosis (MS) is an autoimmune disease of the central nervous system, causing demyelination, axonal degeneration and astrogliosis, resulting in progressive neurological disability. In recent decades, modifier therapies of the disease of anti-inflammatory and/or immunomodulatory type have developed. However, often, they fail to control the more aggressive forms of the disease, indicating the need for new treatments beyond the immunological landscape.1 In this sense, the pharmacological activation of the LXR nuclear receptor is presented as a new therapeutic target2, is a transcription factor with functions in lipid homeostasis, associated with the myelinization process both in physiological and pathological conditions.3 For these reasons, the search for LXR agonists is currently the topic of research. Recently, the research groups in neurosciences, cell death and QuiProNaB of the National University of Colombia have found natural products with agonist activity LXR. For these reasons, the search for agonists is proposed to evaluate the therapeutic potential of a vegetal extract and will apply a velocileptic effect
