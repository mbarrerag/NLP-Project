# ANTI-GAN POLICIES IN THE PANDEMIC CONTEXT: COLOMBIA

This text is part of the second phase of the regional study on gender and politics in Latin America, a project that is under the leadership of the Observatory for Sexual Policy (SPW). It aims to make a general context on the crisis of the coronavirus in the case of Colombia and to explore some aspects related to actions of conservative groups and anti-gender agents, as well as relevant aspects on sexual policies in the framework of this pandemic. The document first shows an overview of the pandemic in its epidemiological and social aspects. Subsequently, it presents some elements of the national political context in the context of the pandemic, and then more specifically deals with the issues of gender and sexuality more expressive. Finally, it mentions some actions carried out by anti-gender groups in the context of the Covid-19 pandemic
