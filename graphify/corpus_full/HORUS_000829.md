# NOTES ON LINEAL CALGEBRA

Notes on linear algebra, is a book that encompasses different notions of linear algebra discussed in a thorough and rigorous manner, which was developed with the purpose of providing a guide to students with greater interest in this area of mathematics. This book is expected to enable the reader interested in linear algebra to strengthen the concepts and tools that usually appear in basic courses of this topic. In particular, readers with an interest in mathematics will find in this text a rigorous discussion of mathematical objects that appear in linear algebra but are also related to other areas of mathematics. The book is divided into six chapters. In the first chapter there are the necessary preliminaries for the development and proper understanding of the other chapters. The following four chapters are dedicated to the content related to linear algebra, and the last chapter presents some suggestions to exercises proposed throughout the book
