# Basic training guide for the management of military gangs in Colombia

This document presents a summary and analysis of the activity carried out by a band of musicians in the military field, both in its ceremonial aspect and in its artistic activity, offering a characterization of the dynamics of military bands in the Colombian context. In a second section, it offers a series of orientations to address in the most technical and expeditious manner the mentioned responsibilities, in accordance with the conditions of service in each of the Forces (Army, Navy and Air Force), seeking that the proposed lines of action provide criteria for successful decision-making processes, favouring the artistic quality of military bands in any case. .
