# IMPLEMENTATION OF MINI-QUECHERS METHOD VERSION (MINI-Q) AS A CONVINIENT QUALITY CONTROL FOR LABORATORIES PERFORMING PESTICIDES RESIDUES ANALYSIS

quality controls are a critical aspect in pesticide residue analysis laboratories. They ensure that the results obtained are accurate and reliable, help to identify any errors or issues that may occur during sample analysis, which allows for immediate corrective measures to be taken and prevents the spread of problems throughout the analysis. The analysis of recovery trials within each sample batch is a good quality control that ensures the reliability of the results of the analytes that are within the laboratory's scope. One of the major problems in conducting recovery trials including all the analytes within the scope is that commercial pesticide mixtures are very expensive and come in very small quantities. For this reason, a mini QuEChERS method was validated and implemented for recovery analysis, which is a modified version of the QuEChERS method that uses very small amounts of reagents, sample, and fortification solution, resulting in a reduction in analysis time and cost. Comparison of the QuEChERS with the mini QuEChERS method was carried out by performing recovery analyses with matrices of different water content such as fruits and vegetables, cereals and matrices with high fat content. Recovery percentages between 70 and 120% and relative standard deviation less than 20% at 10 ug/kg fortification level were obtained for all compounds by both methodologies, indicating their equivalence. A pair t test was performed to compare the two methodologies and the results showed that both are statistically equal, which allows us to use the mini QuEChERS method for the analysis of fortified samples as a convinient quality control including all the analytes within the scope of each laboratory in Pesticides Residue análisis laboratories, saving solution of fortification and therefore cost of análisis
