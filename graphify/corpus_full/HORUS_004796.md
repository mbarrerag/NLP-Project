# CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDE FOR THE NEUMONY HANDLING ACQUIRED IN THE COMMUNITY

Pneumonia remains one of the main causes of consultation and hospitalization, to which, in addition to its high impact in terms of morbidity and mortality, the current problem of antimicrobial resistance is added, so that establishing guidelines that allow its proper diagnosis and treatment is of great importance to obtain better clinical outcomes and promote the rational use of antibiotics in these patients. This clinical practice guide (GPC) contains evidence-based recommendations for the diagnosis and treatment of community-acquired pneumonia in adults, which were carried out through the process of adaptation of evidence-based PCG for the Colombian context
