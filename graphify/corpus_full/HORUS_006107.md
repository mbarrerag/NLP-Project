# Analysis of the relative efficiency of public expenditure on early childhood in Colombia using the Data Enveloping Analysis (DEA)

Public investment in early ages has been considered a key factor in economic development and it has been proven that social return is higher than in other life cycles. Therefore, this research will measure the relative efficiency of public expenditure in early childhood in Colombia for the period 2019 to 2021, applying the methodology of Data Enveloping Analysis, called DEA by its acronym (Data Envelopment Analysis). The model includes 2 input variables (inputs) and 9 output variables (outputs) obtained from official databases for 32 departments and Bogotá D. C., i.e., was evaluated in total 33 DMUs. The homogeneity of the selected data set, tested by the high and significant correlation between the output variables, allowed to estimate a basic DEA model CCR with constant returns to the scale (CRS) oriented to the exits. In 2019 and 2020, the unit of the departments was evaluated in total 33 DMUs. The homogeneity of the capital of the selected data, demonstrated by the high and significant correlation between the output variables, allowed to be estimated by the information of the target and the referenced., evaluating a total of 33 Decision-Making Units (DMUs). The homogeneity of the selected dataset, confirmed by the high and significant correlation between the output variables, allowed estimating a basic DEA model Constant Returns to Scale (CRS) oriented to outputs. In 2019 and 2020, 39% of departments were located above the efficiency border, increasing to 51% in 2021. The analysis of basic DEA model results was conducted based on information from targets and references estimated using the deaR package in the R programming environment. Ultimately, the obtained DEA scores were correlated with the GDP of the evaluated DMUs. The study demonstrates that the commonly accepted dependence between the potential of human capital and economic growth is not reflected by the evaluation of the DMUs
