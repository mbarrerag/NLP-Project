# EXTENSION AS A GREAT OPPORTUNITY

The National University of Colombia supports all its actions in its missionary axes: teaching, research, science, artistic creation and extension. It is precisely under the axis of extension that there is a greater appreciation, mainly because of the idea that, through it, we have the opportunity to relate to the communities, the company and the reality of the country
