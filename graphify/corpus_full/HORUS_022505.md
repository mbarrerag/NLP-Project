# Safety and effectiveness of reduction or withdrawal of antihypertensive therapy in pregnant women with chronic hypertensive vascular disease.

To estimate the safety and effectiveness of reducing or withdrawing antihypertensive medication in pregnant women with chronic hypertension. Randomized controlled trials were included that compared treatment with antihypertensive drugs versus any antihypertensive, drug suspension, placebo or other interventions. Searches were performed in CENTRAL, MEDLINE, LILACS and ClinicalTrials. Manual searches were also performed in the conference proceedings (ACOG, FIGO, RCOG, FECOLSOG). Two authors of the review evaluated independently the trials for inclusion, extracted the data and evaluated the risk of bias. Disagreements were consensually terminated. We used the GRADE approach to evaluate the quality of the evidence.
