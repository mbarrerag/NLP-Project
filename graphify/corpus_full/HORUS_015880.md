# POETICAL OF CARNAVALARY CORPORALITY

Carnival voices: festive rituality, cultural re-signification and mercantilism, arises from the interest of sharing experiences of festive rituality in various latitudes, especially Latin American, to generate new senses of cultural encounter. This plural space, motivated by the Colombian Festive Network together with the Institute of Studies in Communication and Culture of the National University of Colombia headquarters Bogotá, several universities of public, the Salesian Polytechnic University of Quito, through the career of Anthropology, the research group Philosophy of Education GIFE and the publisher Abya-Yala, opened the door to this polyphony of analytical and critical reflections that decant themselves in this book. The reader will find diverse thoughts that re-create the feast from the individual and collective bodies and their ways of understanding and feeling the inherited rituality, but at the same time mediated by the emerging needs of relating and participating in the enjoyment of freedom as the main disinhibitor of social collectives that demand their right to express what is limited, through the prohibited forms outside the ceremonial sphere
