# THE PRESENCY OF TRADITION.

The text refers to a series of drawings entitled “Urban Cartoons” that Professor Gustavo Adolfo Rendón Castaño realized from the central space of eleven cities of Antioquia, which won the Talent 2012 award of the Government of Antioquia and include anecdotes, traditions, historical and representative characters, made in different medium-sized formats. The analysis of the work brings to light the documentary value of the plastic set as a document that lists a look that recognizes the poetic condition of the urban space within a cultural and geographical context of enormous richness and shows the particular vision of the construction of its own landscape
