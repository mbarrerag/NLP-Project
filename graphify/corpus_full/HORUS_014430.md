# Socioeconomics of the peasantry in the villages Roble Hueco and Rincón Santo. Case studies in the municipalities Bojacá and Zipacón (Cundinamarca).

The peasantry is the cornerstone of society and the main actor in Colombian rurality. The logics of peasant production allow this population to remain at present and to be a response in the search for sustainability in food production. Despite this, there are multiple threats, highlighting urban growth and the adjacent situations such as rural migration and the ageing of the peasant population. In this context the present research aims to describe and analyze socio-economic aspects in two paths that have an urban influence such as Roble Hueco in Bojacá and Rincon Santo in Zipacón. The method applied is that of case studies, being the unit of observation the peasant family. In the research it was found that the main social factors that describe the peasant community of both paths are the aging of the population, the reduction of family members and the lack of solid relationships that allow to generate organizational processes. Regarding the peasant economy, it is found that a mini-fundist production with high diversity of species integrating agricultural crops as well as foliage and vegetables with total sale to markets of specialized.
