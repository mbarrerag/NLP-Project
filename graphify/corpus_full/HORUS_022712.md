# Monitoring of fine particulate material and black carbon in microenvironments of the Andean city of Manizales: pilot study

Summary Manizales currently has an air quality monitoring system that measures ambient air concentrations of particulate matter (PM). However, this system does not allow to know exposure concentrations in the vicinity of roads. The aim of this study was to make a first approximation to the levels of exposure to fine particulate matter (PM2.5) and black carbon (BC) in some roads of the city of Manizales, evaluating microenvironments related to pedestrians on roads and as bus passengers of public transport. Concentrations of fine particulate matter PM2.5 and BC were measured for two weeks per five hour period per day in morning hours. Two photometers DustTrack models 8533 and a Micro-Aetalometer model AE51. The data were evaluated based on each microenvironment and three study areas in the city. The value obtained from the median for PM2.5 and BC concentrations in the routes analyzed were 10 % and 30% lower on the city center, indicating the relevance of the B2 traffic as a source of emissions in the city.
