# The evolution of environmental permit and licensing regulations and their relationship to environmental conflicts in Colombia

This research analyzed the evolution of the environmental licensing regulations and their relationship with the environmental conflicts in the specific cases of Cerro Matoso due to the extraction of ferronicel and the Superintendencia de Mares by extraction of hydrocarbons, since both projects began their operations before the entry into force of the current environmental regulation in Colombia, bringing with it questions about the validity of the respective authorizations that were not harmonized with the environmental reality of the territory. For this research a bibliographical and documentary review was carried out on extractivism and environmental conflicts, investigating the specific categories that allowed to address various aspects of the conflicts generated by the annotated projects. On the other hand, field visits were also carried out in order to extract elements for the characterization of the environmental instrument that these projects have, as well as for the analysis of the transition regime of the two projects.
