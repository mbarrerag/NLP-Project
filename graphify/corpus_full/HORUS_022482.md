# TOWARDS POSTESTATE GOVERNANCE IN A BARROCO CULTURE CONTEXT FOR THE 21st CENTURY

The study analyses the evolution of State institutions, conceived as a moment of civilizational transition, called baroque or neo-baroque, which leads to new forms of governance. Three scenarios are established: Democratic, hybrid and hyperrational governance
