# MODEL STRUCTURAL ECUATIONS FOR THE EVALUATION OF TECHNOLOGICAL COMPETENCES IN UNIVERSITARY STUDENTS

The digital skills of university students are a determining factor in their training process, as they provide key tools for the management of information and the development of problems immersed in their academic, higher-level and learning activities, given the challenge of digitalising and the accelerated use of information and communication technologies in the process of learning and learning in times of pandemic. The aim of this research was to verify whether students have the skills necessary to adapt to the educational environments and, on the other hand, to design strategies that guarantee the image required by students to maintain a process of learning and successful learning
