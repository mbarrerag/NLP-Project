# Evangelization and materiality. Relationships and exchanges between missionaries and indigenous people in Vaupés and Guainía during the 20th century

to analyze the meanings and uses that missionaries and indigenous, both Catholic and evangelical, assigned to the exchange, possession and consumption of certain objects in their respective projects of evangelization in Vaupés and Guainía in two different historical moments.The first moment covers from the arrival of the Montfortian missionaries to Vaupés in 1914 until their departure in 1949 and the second begins with the arrival of evangelical missionaries affiliated to New Tribes Mission in 1944 and ends in 2009, when indigenous testimonies on the processes of evangelization were collected. this article uses archival work, periodic publications of the missionaries, testimonies and oral history to account for the representations that missionaries and indigenous people built on the material dimensions of the processes of evangelization. Originality: the existing historiography on missions in Colombia analyses separately the Catholic and evangelical missions; on the contrary, this article compares in one case of study the projects led by Catholic and evangelical missionaries from their material dimensions and the exchange of objects. This approach in the materiality of the missions allows us to both the differences and the material similarities that existed between the projects of evangelization and the Basque missionaries
