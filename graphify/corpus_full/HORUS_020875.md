# ANALYSIS OF THE COMPETITIVENESS OF MEDIANA AND GREAT INDUSTRY OF THE METAL-MEchanical MANUFACTURER SECTOR OF CALLS, FROM THEIR TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT LEVEL.

The metalworking sector is the basis of industrial development, not only regional, but national and international, given its capacity to generate externalities in different economic sectors, and it also has a high multiplier effect, since the processes involve greater technology and complexity, which contributes to generating investment, highly skilled employment and to developing high value-added products; in this sense, the companies of this sector require to organize new technological modernization and automation programs to ensure greater productivity and quality.This research project aims to identify the technological gaps that exist in the metalworking manufacturing industry of broths, through the analysis of the different levers of manufacture, determining different strategies that contribute in the development process and thus increase their competitiveness
