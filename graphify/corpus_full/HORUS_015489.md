# URBAN-Rural LINKS WITH INTEGRAL APPROACH FOR REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT TO A SUSTAINABLE URBAN BITTER IN COLOMBIAN AMOUNT

The population in the region of the Colombian Amazon has increased in a vertiginous way, with an increasingly urban distribution that reached a historical milestone in 2018, where more than the third part (i.e. 666 188 people) lives in urban areas or municipal headquarters, which represents 77.1% of the population (dane, 2020).This has triggered an urban expansion that was not planned and has serious urban problems and social, cultural and environmental conflicts, which have changed the traditional pattern of socioeconomic segregation of its cities, where a considerable sector lives in peripheral areas with high population density vulnerable, with deficiencies in social services (Gómez, 2020) and low welfare indices (Cepal and pn, 2013).
