# ANTIOXIDANT AND ANTI-RADICAL PROPERTIES OF EXTRACTS OF MOTILONE ARANDANS (HYERONIMA MACROCARP) OBTAINED BY CLEAR EXTRACTION

The mature fruits of Hyeronima macrocarpa have a high potential in the food industry due to their high content of antioxidant compounds such as anthocyanins and phenols. The use of these substances in food matrices is determined by adequate extraction, incorporation and conservation. Some extraction methods use solvents such as methanol or acetone, but use in food is restricted due to their toxicity, especially methanol. In this study the effect of four extraction methods was evaluated: acidified ethanol (EtOH), acid water (WOH), water at 70 °C (W70) and a biphasic ethanol/ammonium sulfate (ATPE), on the content of anthocyanins, polyphenols, flavonoids, and antioxidant capacity (ORAC, ABSS, FRAP, radical hydroxyl and superoxide), of mature fruits of H. macrocarpa.
