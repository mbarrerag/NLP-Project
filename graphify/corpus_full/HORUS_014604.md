# Manufacture of thin, transparent conductive films from copper nanowires

The main objective of this project was to manufacture thin films of copper nanowires (Cu) that presented good optical and electrical properties, i.e. conductive and transparent films. To this end, different procedures were tested for obtaining the Cu nanowires by hydrothermic synthesis, trying to establish the optimal concentration of reducing agents using ascorbid acid and glucose. In addition, the synthesis was made using copper chloride (II) dihydrate (CuCl2↔ 2H2O) as a precursor of copper and Octadecilamine (ODA) as a surfactant. First, it was determined that the use of glucose allowed obtaining better nanowires. Likewise, the hydrothermic method was used in synthesis and both nanowires and nanoparticles were obtained, so an electro purification method was used for the separation of nanowires. The thin films manufactured by Spin Coating were used as a result of the following techniques: electron microscopy of sweep (MEB), UV-VIS spectrophotometry, IR, measurement of the resistance and of the leaf resistance.
