# THE KNOWLEDGE AND PRACTICE OF ASTRONOMY IN THE NEW GRANADA KINGDOM

Aspects related to the history of astronomy in the colonial period are commented, in particular of the main exponents who practiced, taught and did astronomy research between the 17th and early 19th centuries
