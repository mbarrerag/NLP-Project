# FINANCES IN THE PUBLIC ACCOUNTING STUDIES PLAN

Through a qualitative methodology and a documentary collection with a bibliographical study of the most significant research work related to finance in Colombia, between 2015 and 2017, the syllabus of the finance areas of the Universidad Santo Tomás were analyzed, to identify the study styles and address the main characteristics of each research, finally it was considered if the academic spaces were updated with the literature available
