# Public debt and fiscal sustainability in Colombia: analysis through fiscal reaction functions

Abstract In this article, we evaluate fiscal sustainability in Colombia by using three different methodologies: steadyness tests; estimation of fiscal reaction functions - linear and non-linear - by square minimums in two stages and by the generalized method of moments; and calculation of the synthetic indicator of fiscal sustainability proposed by Mackiewicz-=321;yziak and >=321;yziak, (2019). In the calculation of this indicator, the effect of the debt interest rate is incorporated, which is considered as an endogenous variable, as well as the response of the interest rate against changes in the level of debt is evaluated. The results, although they show the fulfilment of some of the sustainability conditions for Colombia up to 2019, also highlight the risk represented by the high level of indebtedness that the country reached in 2020, resulting from the recent crisis of the Covid-19 pandemic.
