# Design and implementation of a micro-hydroelectric plant in the banana-growing area of Colombia

This paper describes the design, installation and operation of a micro-hydroelectric power plant (MCH) with a capacity of 10 kW, located in the banana-growing area of Colombia. The MCH uses an intermediate Kaplan axial turbine, a permanent magnet generator and a regulation system based on power electronics to guarantee a reliable power supply of 110 V at 60 Hz. The energy generated is distributed to local homes, powering a refrigerator, LED lighting and a fan, contributing to access to clean energy in rural areas
