# Development of statistical learning model for identifying the risk of money laundering and terrorist financing

This paper proposes the development of an effective statistical methodology to solve the problem of not having a model to evaluate the risk of Money laundering and Financing of Terrorism (LAFT) in the municipalities of Colombia based on indicators that provide information on the social, economic and demographic situation of the municipality. Initially, a review of the literature is made to identify and show an evolution of the techniques that have been applied to measure the risk LAFT in municipalities of Colombia; secondly, the database is consolidated with the indicators updated from different sources of information, consolidating 55 variables associated with the risk of LAFT organized in eight tables according to its main theme. Finally, through a Multiple Factorial Analysis (MFA) and hierarchical grouping by main components (HCPC), the municipalities are segmented in four groups depending on their level of risk LAFT, and the indicators that most affect this result are determined. The results show that the methodology is effective to identify critical areas and guide the implementation of prevention policies of LAFT.
