# Space organization for the care of elderly people with chronic conditions in the Colombian Wayuu people

The purpose of this study is to analyze the spatial organization of care of elderly people with chronic conditions in the Wayuu people resident in the Media Luna, Colombia. The study is based on a qualitative methodology with 11 elderly people with chronic diseases and their families, interviews and observation were carried out. The results show how the care space has two levels of organization, one at a macro level regarding how social and health resources are organized within the Media Luna which accounts for the material conditions that play in care; the second is regarding the organization of spaces for care at a micro level within the rancheries, which gives an account of how different ways of organizing the space in relation to the cultural position occupied by the elderly or elderly in the hierarchy of relatives in the Wayuu family. On the other hand, the data show the limited residential mobility of the elderly, i.e., they rarely change from residence from one ranch to another because of care. From the data it can be concluded that the spatial organisation of care is an important dimension to consider in the care analyses and how the physical and social space determines geographies (Vatic of care the differentities of the age and social). built in relation to the cultural position occupied by the older man or woman in the hierarchy of parents in the Wayuu family.
