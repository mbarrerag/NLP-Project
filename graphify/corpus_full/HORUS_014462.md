# Formulation of an enzyme chemical route for the production of 5 – hydroxymethylfurfural sustainably from maize fibre.

Global warming generated by the heavy dependence on fossil fuels requires different types of solutions, and among the most promising are the production of chemical products platform from residual lignocellulosic material. This work formulates a sustainable production route of the chemical compound platform 5 – hydroxymethylfurfural from the lignocellulosic biomass corn fiber. The route formulated consists first of a pretreatment with ion fluid in combination with microwave, followed by an enzyme hydrolysis to access structural glucose and finally a stage of reaction and separation in situ in a biphasic water-organic system known as reactive extraction. To conclude, the modeling and simulation of this last stage of the process developed in the literature is presented
