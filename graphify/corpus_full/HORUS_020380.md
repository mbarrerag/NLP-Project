# Alternative planning models and experiences in Medellín (2010-2020)

Alternative planning arises from a critical point of view to traditional planning, presenting a planning model from the bottom up. Promoting projects, plans or project ideas whose initiative comes from the inhabitants of a territory; using as a main tool for the formulation of their own experiences and specific knowledge about the territory. This research makes a tour of the local planning processes that could be considered alternative planning in Medellín after Law 388 of 1997 and the other regulations that followed it related to urban planning and citizen participation. Indications of an institutionalization of the participatory instruments and less support for local initiatives from below, based on the analysis of data of the management, formulation, methodology and actors of each experience in the communes 1, 2 and 8; highlighting the alternative planning model proposed by the Habitat School of the National University of Colombia, Medellín headquarters, the Social Construction of Habitat. .
