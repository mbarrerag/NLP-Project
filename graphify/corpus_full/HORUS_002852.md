# ECOGOVERNMENTALITY, CONSERVATION AREAS AND INDIGENOUS AUTONOMY. QUILLASING PERSPECTIVE IN THE LAGUNA OF THE COCHA, COLOMBIA.

The La Cocha lagoon, located in the south of Colombia, forms a geographical space of great environmental importance. It has three conservation areas: a Natural National Park, a National Protective Forest Reserve and an international category, a Ramsar wetland, which coincide with the presence of a Quillasinga Indigenous Guard. However, this territory faces threats that compromise its sustainability. This study focuses on discussing, based on the eco-government, the effectiveness of these conservation areas and the indigenous community’s perspective on this issue. The methodology included the analysis of land coverage maps for three periods (2005-2009, 2010-2012 and 2018) at a scale of 1:100,000, together with semi-structured interviews with key actors and observation participants with the indigenous community between October and November 2023. The results obtained reveal significant rates of deforestation in dense forest areas. Consequently, the need for more effective management by the government authorities in relation to these conservation areas is raised.
