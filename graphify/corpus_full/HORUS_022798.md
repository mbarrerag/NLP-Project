# LETTERS TO A GREAT OLD MAN

These letters are addressed to you, Mr X, that is, to myself, to that which I will be when the skin gets older, when I wait on my bed for the arrival of death and only wish to be able to exchange my place with someone else. I am writing to remind you of some things that you liked, but especially because in these days I have thought a lot about ignorance and I know that you, who have already become an ignorant old man, will still have to be excited about the subject. Well, you will have forgotten so many things and forget it is only a way to ignore. I am writing to you to feel the pleasure that it brought you to think about these matters when you were less years old. To get interested in ignorance, who produces it and how it is produced, how it is encouraged and how it is dismantled, who benefits and who harms it. So many things that you dedicated days and days of reflection and are already just a vague feeling in the body that is confused with your last sterors
