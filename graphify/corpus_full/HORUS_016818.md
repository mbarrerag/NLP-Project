# SUMMARY OF RESEARCH

The migration in Colombia in the top of the Darién. The violence that unleashes the global disputes on the population. The south-north migration that flows through the Latin American subcontinent has experienced important features in the last 10 years. The migratory caravans that began at the end of 2018 show a new way of migrating.These migratory movements are constituted in real human rivers, achieving great visibility worldwide.At the same time, the subcontinent lives the flow of extracontinental and Caribbean populations that are concentrated in the Tapón del Darién, north of Colombia.This border crossing has become for about 10 years, in the route of thousands of migrants who try to reach the United States.People who, in their passage through Central America, join the migratory caravans.The people who have gathered in this border, have very different origins on the problem of the 50 nationalities.The trajectories can last years, testimonies that speak of 3 and 5 years, from the moment of leaving their place of origin to the US. All the places of origin are territories of the south. Territories overwhelmed by the war, overexploitted in their natural resources. From this perspective, the militarization of borders is strengthened and restrictions on population mobility are deepened. Restrictions on population mobility towards the global north, boost the emergence of new migration routes, strengthen criminal organizations that control the territories of passage, complement the transitory and definitive borders and increase the cost and risk of migrating and intensify South-South migration
