# DYNAMIC MODELS FOR PROCESS CONTROL IN THE KALMAN RESEARCH RESEARCH SMALL.

The mathematical modeling of dynamic systems is an opportunity that continues to be in force due to the complexity of the real processes, the interaction between components, the uncertainties in measurements and parameters, among other aspects. This work explores the evolution that has had the development of dynamic models necessary to control a system. It addresses the definition of system from the point of view of automatic control and the tools that are available in modern engineering for the search of models as perfect and useful as possible. Some problems are presented that are found in modeling as: differences in the results obtained with different models of the same system, uncertainty in the measure and difficulty in the mathematical solution. It is concluded by presenting a classification of the types of models and a methodology posed within the Kalman research group for the generation of models.
