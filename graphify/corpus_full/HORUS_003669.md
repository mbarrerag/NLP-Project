# H2: FUTURE ENERGY

Hydrogen (H2) is considered an energy alternative that could reduce the environmental impact caused by the use of fossil fuels. However, its acquisition, storage, transport and, consequently, its application presents several difficulties. This document addresses the possible solutions to these challenges from different perspectives. It describes how energy can be obtained from this simple molecule, highlights the main difficulties associated with its integration as an energy system and clarifies some expressions of the language used in this area of science
