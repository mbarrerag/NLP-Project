# A Bayesian multi-faceted TRI model for the evaluation of classroom teaching performance

The teacher's performance evaluations are based on the assumption that students learn more from highly qualified teachers and the fact that students observe the teacher's performance in the classroom. However, many studies question the methodologies used for such measurements, in general, because the averages of categorical answers have little statistical sense. In this article, we propose Bayesian models of the Theory of Response to the Item of multiple facets to measure performance. The proposed model takes into account the effects associated with the severity of students responding to the survey and the courses that are evaluated. The model applies to a set of data obtained from a survey of the teacher's perception of performance conducted by the Faculty of Sciences of the National University of Colombia to its students. The teacher's scores obtained as model results are real numerical values that can be used to calculate common statistics in the teacher's evaluation. In this case, the statistics are mathematically consistent. Some of them illustrate the usefulness of the model
