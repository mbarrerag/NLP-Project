# ENGINEERING FOR LIFE: A MANIFEST FROM NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

In the search to build an Educational Project of Faculty relevant and relevant to the present society, the advisory committee of the Institute of Education in Engineering of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia prepared a working document called “Mani-fisto: Engineering for Life”. The purpose of this article is to present a set of competences and learning results that allow incorporating the essential elements of the Manifesto into the curriculum of the engineering programs. It describes several strategies designed to achieve the proposed objective and mentions some of the achievements achieved. Engineering for Life implies that in all academic programs and projects of an Engineering Faculty there are significant connections between engineering and the natural history of the territories, the collective wisdom of the communities that inhabit them, and the knowledge of the patterns, processes and flows necessary to maintain life in them
