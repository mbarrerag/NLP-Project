# Video: dialoging with Video

The aim of this article is to expose particularities of video, because it is constantly analyzed as cinema or audio. To ignore the intrinsic of video constitutes a risk because, like any technological medium, if one ignores how it operates, it is at its mercy and part of its creative potential is obvious. This article has three axes of presentation. First, one observes what happens when video devices capture and emit images; second, a dialogue between video and cinema as characters is developed through a fictional exercise to emphasize the difference between these and other media; and, third, it exposes photographic experiments that, in turn, feed the reflections of dialogue. Methodologically, observing merely the capture of images generated by the video and its broadcast, resorted to comparison and experimentation. The main results are that the video when capturing is a "see but not touching"; for its immediacy, it is "see to believe", and it extends-reflects the viewer and this is extended-reflected in the video; and when it is immediately broadcast to capture and depending on its spatial configuration, it establishes singular sum of spaces and times
