# HW/SW architecture for acceleration of mobile robot tasks by integrating FPGA and ROS.

The inherent complexity of mobile robots has required interdisciplinary work in various fields of engineering, such as information and communications technologies, artificial intelligence and software development. In turn, the community of engineers and developers using ROS has contributed to making robots better, more accessible and present almost in any type of application. Increasingly, robots integrate multiple sensors and heterogeneous actuators to perform more complex tasks that are often computer-intensive or require real-time processing that processors, no matter how many cores they have, cannot fulfill. To address this problem, it is proposed to develop, evaluate and demonstrate on a prototype robotic platform the integration of ROS with SoC FPGA devices that combine processing units and reconfigurable hardware resources on a chip. The target robotic platform was a mobile differential traction robot used in education, research and development called Turtlebot3 Burger. This robot compatible with ROS includes a LiDAR sensor and a Raspberry Pi as a processing unit. The Raspberry sensor was re-placed by the FPGA Ultra96v2 SoC map to implement a software routine.
