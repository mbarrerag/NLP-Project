# Impacts of collective gardens on the education and eating habits of the participants. Cases of the National University of Colombia and the Ecobarrio La Esmeralda, Bogotá.

The university and community gardens have taken on relevance as an instrument of sustainable development and social cooperation in various contexts, especially in disadvantaged communities. According to the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, FAO, in developing countries there are around 266 million urban households participating in crop production. Community use spaces such as urban and peri-urban gardens are fundamental scenarios for the creation of social fabric, cultural expression and capacity development (Coldind, 2011), which is why it is important to take into account the role of the gardens in the intergenerational transmission of traditional knowledge and not only their role for the supply of food. This project, entitled: _x0093_Impacts of the collective gardens in the education and eating habits of the participants. Cases of the National University of Colombia and the Ecobarrio La Esmeralda, Bogotá, is focused on understanding the transformative role of the gardens in the university campus and an area close to this. The aim is to explore how the orchards have influenced the food habits and knowledge of the people involved, especially focused on those who participate in the garden
