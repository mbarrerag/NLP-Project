# Farmers and Democratic Struggles in La Macarena, Meta

The municipality of La Macarena in the Colombian department of Meta is today the object of a social movement that, among other things, defends the right of the peasantry and other communities to inhabit the territory. In this sense, the organizational commitment is interesting for the academy as a democratic proposal for the country, consolidation laboratory for peace and scenario of the new conflicts of the XXI century. I propose to make a theoretical approach that allows me to expose the context, the tensions and the analytical possibilities of my field work, while looking to use these findings as the basis of a contribution to the consolidation of rural peace for Colombia of the post-conflict that has begun to arise with the signing in 2016 of the peace agreements between the state and the FARC-EP. In this order of ideas, I will make a journey through the state of art of the issue, I will show La Macarena within a social, political, historical and structural context, I will point out to the consistency of the conflicts around the colonization exercises in Natural National Parks and disaggregate the proposal that has structured the Macarenense community, whose sense encourages to revitalize the institutional aspect of the integration (the integration of the territory). which has been taken to emerge with the following of the peace agreements in 2016 between the state and the FARC-EP. In that order of ideas, I will review the state of the art on the issue, present La Macarena within a social, political, historical, and structural context, highlight the consistency of conflicts over territory that revolves around colonization exercises in National Natural Parks, and break down the coexistence proposal that the Macarena community has developed, which aims to revitalize democracy in light of the gaps left by the traditional approach of institutional liberalism
