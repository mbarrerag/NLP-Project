# H2O2 ELECTROGENERATION IN FELTROS OF CARBON: A LOOK AT SUSTAINABLE TREATMENT OF WASTE WATER

This paper presents a detailed comprehensive perspective to evaluate the performance of the Electro-Fenton process with H2O2 in-situ generation in the treatment of an industrial textile waste water, as a contribution to the fulfilment of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) number 12 Responsible Production and Consumption and 6 Clean Water and Sanitation. For this, the performance of two commercial carbon felts in the accumulation and decomposition of H2O2 is initially compared. Finally, a comparative life cycle analysis (ACV) of the electro-Fenton process for both felts showed that its application is promising, because the use of industrially produced H2O2 is not required, which presents a high carbon footprint (GWP-100 = 1.48 kgCO2-Eq/kg H2O2 produced)
