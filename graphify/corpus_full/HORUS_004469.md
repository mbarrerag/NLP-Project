# CONTENT ANALYSIS AND ITS METHODOLOGICAL APPLICATION AS A PURSULAR HEALTH EXERCISE

The chapter aims to illustrate the combination of content and statistic analysis, particularly the multidimensional scaling and multiple correspondence analysis applied to the study of human rights coalitions in the process of updating the Compulsory Health Plan (POS) between 1994 and 2010 in Colombia
