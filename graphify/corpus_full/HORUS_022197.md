# Modeling of thermomechanical deformation of the subduction zone of southern Colombia.

The form of the topography of the surface in the southern part of the Colombian territory is the result of the deformation produced by the subduction of the Nazca plate under the South American Plate. A thermo-mechanical numerical modeling is generated to solve several equations that describe the main physical phenomena associated with heat and effort. This subduction process is modeled under the framework of the mechanics of continuous media. It is presented the evolution in the chosen subduction modeling the scenarios in approximately 150 million years from the Jurassic geological period until now, parametrized by the control that exerts the form of the current topography. This modeling is done under the MatLab computational program and takes into account computational codes of several authors who are working on these solutions. A fundamental aspect is to dissect the space based on flat coordinates forming a grillated of 24.888 marks and representing an area of 300 km high and 3000 km long as the angle of 3° degrees. It determines effort, temperature, composition, geometry and properties of the ocean and continental crusts for a total of 10 scenarios.
