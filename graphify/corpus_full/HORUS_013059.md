# Publication and retraction in the SARS-CoV-2 era and COVID-19

ABSTRACT The pandemic caused by SARS-CoV-2 accelerates the time of review and acceptance of manuscripts, increases the free access to published information on the topic and popularizes the servers of manuscripts, preliminary versions or preprints. That is why the objective of the study is to focus on evaluating the trends in the din mica of the retractations and to determine patterns existing among the variables analyzed. A bibliom trico analysis of articles associated with the pandemic was carried out, in which the trends and din micas of 103 manuscripts retracted between December 2019 and June 2021 were evaluated. This revealed a positive association with a shorter review time, as well as with manuscripts published in magazines Q1 and Q2 by Scimago Journal Rank. Likewise, the number of retracted articles decreased nine months after the declaration of pandemic
