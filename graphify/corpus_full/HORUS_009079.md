# Public art in two streets in downtown Medellín: a bicycle museum (1995 – 2020)

This research, called Public Art in two streets of the center of Medellín: A museum by bicycle (1995 – 2020) addresses public art from the perspective of the user bike. It focuses on a specific space, with particular characteristics: the road axes through which the high section of the Medellin Metro passes, which bursts into the historical center of the city, generating the particular phenomenon of a roofed street.
