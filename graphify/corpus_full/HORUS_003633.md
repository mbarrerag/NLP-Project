# THE TEAR THAT THE MACHINES ARE GETTING TO DOMINATE US IS PHILOSOPICALLY FOUNDED?

In this paper we critically analyze some fictional scenarios that support the thesis that eventually machines will achieve total world domination, including human beings, and develop the arguments that combat this thesis. To this end, we divide the article into four parts. In the first, we advance an exercise of conceptual clarification of the terms decisive for discussion, among them, empirical possibility, logical possibility, machine, and domination? In the second, we discuss the most popular arguments in film and fiction novel, in particular, the argument of overwhelming evidence, which supposedly shows that machines already dominate us. In the third, and in the way of replicating these arguments, we present a synthesis of the main reasons that support the the thesis of empirical impossibility of machines coming to control us. Finally, we formulate the argument of logical and epistemic inconsistency or the scenario of machine governance in the Kepler 452b and we demonstrate that the fear that machines dominate the world is not well grounded philosophically
