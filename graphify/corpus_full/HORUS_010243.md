# EFFECT OF ROOTSTOCK ON VEGETATIVE AND PRODUCTIVE BEHAVIOR OF TAHITIAN ACID LIME (CITRUS LATIFOLIA TANAKA) IN COLOMBIAN TROPICAL DRY FOREST CONDITIONS

Abstract Objective. Evaluate six rootstocks Carrizo, Cleopatra, CPB 4475, Kryder 15-3, Sunky x English, and Volkameriana on the vegetative growth and yield of the Tahiti lime in a region of inter-Andean valleys in southwestern Colombia. Material and methods. Under the experimental design of randomized complete blocks, an experimental plot was established in the Colombian Agricultural Research Corporation AGROSAVIA Palmira facilities. The vegetative behavior was evaluated annually for ten years (2010-2018) through the evaluation of plant height (m), crown diameter (m), and stem diameter variables (mm). Crop production was measured from the fourth year after establishment, counting the number of fruits and the weight per tree. Our results demonstrate an influence of the rootstock on the evaluated vegetative growth and yield variables. Volkameriana and Kryder 15-3 induced greater development for the height and crown volume variables, the best compatibility index of the rootstock-variety union was presented with the Carrizo rootstock. Trees grafted onto CPB 4475 and Kryder 15 -3 produced the highest cumulative yields. Sunky x English stood out within the group of rootstocks evaluated, for generating short trees, with high production efficiency. The interaction between the Tahiti acid lime and the rootstock significantly affected the crop's vegetative development and productive behavior in this region of the country. Sunky x English stood out, within the group of rootstocks evaluated, for generating low-sized trees, with high productive efficiency, requirements in line with modern citrus farming. Keywords Citrus, pattern, development, compatibility
