# Colombian Altillanura: potential and strategies for sustainable agro-industrial development

The Colombian Altillanura is a region with a vast agro-industrial potential due to its extension, topography, agricultural and market conditions. However, it has a low level of productive development, characterized by the underutilation of the soil, infrastructure problems, legal insecurity and low investment in technology. Despite its agricultural and livestock vocation, the region faces structural barriers that limit its competitiveness and the socioeconomic well-being of its inhabitants. This work aims to analyze the factors that have restricted the development of the Altillanura and to propose a sustainable agro-industrialization model that strengthens its productivity and competitiveness. To this end, a mixed methodology was employed that combines the analysis of official statistical data, the review of the history of public policies, and the dialogue with key actors in the productive, academic and governmental sector. The results show that, although there are advances in strategic crops such as maize, soybean and oil palm, as well as in the agricultural and livestock sector, the region continues to be lagging behind in productivity and productive chains.
