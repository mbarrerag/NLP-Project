# STATEICAL MACHINERY FOR RECONCILIATION AND PEACE-BUILDING

The study of peace-building practices allows us to understand the forms and spaces in which peace is produced, as well as the manifestations and regularities that this process has involved, in the understanding that practices are links of materially mediated activity that allow us to grasp both the historical experience of the agents, as well as their horizon of expectations. The practices are records, traces and indications of knowledge and social experience and allow us to understand, in addition, the forms, mechanisms, operations, ways and senses with which the world is socially constructed and the spaces in which this process occurs: in the case of this research, the practices for the construction of peace. Thus, a common commitment is to understand the war to bring to peace. This implies that the profound reflection on the causes of extreme violence and its devastating consequences, both individually and collectively, is intended to enable bets for other social ties that are fairer, equitable, worthy and creative for the country.
