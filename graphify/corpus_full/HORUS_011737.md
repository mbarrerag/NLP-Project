# APPROXIMATION TO THE PROJECTION OF EMISSIONS BY TERRESTER TRANSPORT BY PORT IN COLOMBIA

A number of factors threaten the sustainability of societies, including air quality, environmental conservation, and the quality of life and public health of their inhabitants. Air quality is deteriorated, inter alia, by emissions generated in the development of various activities of society, especially energy production and transport, which is heavily dependent on fossil fuel burning. In particular, in Colombia by 2019, 81 per cent of cargo transport and 73 per cent of passenger transport were carried out through road transport (Ministry of Transport, 2019). Thus, it is necessary to analyse emissions of atmospheric pollutants produced by the transport system and possible scenarios for the growth of such emissions. There is now a global interest in reversing the climate crisis and identifying actions to be prioritized for its mitigation. Thus, emission projections are an essential tool for decision makers. This research focuses on the estimation of national emissions of black carbon, atmospheric pollutants and greenhouse gases (GHG) generated by road transport for 2020-2050
