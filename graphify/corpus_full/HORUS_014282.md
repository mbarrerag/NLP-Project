# Implementation of a phase display platform for the production of recombinant anti-NAD+Plasmodium falciparum kinase (PfNADK) antibodies.

The phase display technology allows the generation of monoclonal antibody libraries for use in research, diagnosis and therapy. In this study, the construction of an immune antibody library presented in bacteriophages, against the enzyme NAD kinase of Plasmodium falciparum. Recombinant NAD kinase protein was expressed in E. coli completely and truncated, and the latter was purified from the insoluble fraction for the construction of the library. The variable fragments of light and heavy chains of the antibodies, were amplified from mouse DNAc and were cloned in the pSEX81 phase. The presentation of the library was made on the bacteriophage aider M13KO7ΔpIII, with subsequent selection by affinity. For the identification of the bacteriophages, the test of the PNA was validated by PFISA indirect antibodies. The analysis by PCR indicated the correct cloning of the light and heavy chains in the fásmid, with a size of 1x107 cfu.
