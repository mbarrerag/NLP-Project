# Assessment of water availability in soil, on physiological response and performance of three Zapallo genotypes, Cucurbita moschata Duchesne.

The research involved the evaluation of the effect of the soil moisture regime, regarding the yield, quality and physiological response of three Zapallo cultivars (UNAPAL: Abano-75; Green and Golden Ball).Two tests were carried out, where 4 irrigation sheets were applied based on the Kc of the crop: 0.4; 0.6; 0.8; 1.0 in the semester 2019-B (Prueba I) and 0.6; 0.8; 1.0 and 1.2 in 2020-A (Prueba II).The treatments were based on 8 repetitions for a total of 96 plants per test.The statistical design was divided plots, where the main plot was the irrigation treatment and the sub-parcella, the cultivars.
