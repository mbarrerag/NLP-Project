# THE EXOTIC POSITRON BOND

Some of us recently reported on the energy stability of a fundamentally different type of positronic molecule [1], formed by two otherwise repelling hydride anions and one positron, e+[H2 ¿ 2 ]. This finding has raised great amount of interest. As a result, the energy stability of other positron bonded diatomic systems have been reported including 2e+[H2 ¿ 2 ] [2], 3e+[H2 ¿ 2 ] [3] and homo- and heteronuclear e+[X-Y -] compounds formed by two halide anions (X, Y¿¿F, Cl, Br) and one positron [4]. In this talk we analyze the energy stability of a fundamentally new matter-antimatter molec- ular species formed by two positrons and three hydride anions, 2e+ [H3 ¿ 3 ] [5]. Our study pro- vides compelling evidence that the otherwise repelling trihydride system [H3 ¿ 3 ], is stabilized by the formation of a three-center two-positron bond. In several aspects, the 2e+ [H3 ¿ 3 ] sys- tem is similar to the purely electronic analog, [Li+ 3 ] trication which is bound by two electrons.
