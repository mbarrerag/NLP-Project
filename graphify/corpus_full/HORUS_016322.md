# FORMALISING BASIC TOPOLOGY FOR COMPUTATIONAL LOGIC IN SIMPLE TYPE THEORY

We present a formalisation of basic topology in simple type theory encoded using the Isabelle/HOL proof assistant. In contrast to related formalisation work, which follows more `traditional¿ approaches, our work builds upon closure algebras, encoded as Boolean algebras of (characteristic functions of) sets featuring an axiomatised closure opera- tor (cf. seminal work by Kuratowski and McKinsey & Tarski). With this approach we primarily address students of computational logic, for whom we bring a di'erent focus, closer to lattice theory and logic than to set theory or analysis. This approach has allowed us to better leverage the automated tools integrated into Isabelle/HOL (model ¿nder Nitpick and Sledgehammer) to do most of the proof and refutation heavy-lifting, thus allowing for assumption-minimality and less-verbose interactive proofs
