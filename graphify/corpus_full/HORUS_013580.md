# Interdisciplinary studies of elites in Colombia

The inequalities that characterize Colombian society, which is the driving force of the violent conflicts present since its territorial configuration, have as one of the central actors to the economic and political elites of the country. Despite this, the considerable academic production that results from the effort of researchers and academics to understand Colombian society and its transformations has focused the attention on the role of the subaltern populations and the armed actors. It is for this reason that this book seeks to contribute to the analysis of the role of the elites from an interdisciplinary and long-term perspective that is carried out from the Social Sciences, and that it involves both classical studies carried out from the History, the Political Science and Sociology, as contributions of the Anthropology, the Economy and the Communication Sciences.
