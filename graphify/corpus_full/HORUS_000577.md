# Management of power congestion in areas with high renewable energy penetration in Colombia to mitigate operational restrictions

This document addresses the management of power congestion in areas with high penetration of non-conventional renewable energy sources (FNCER) in Colombia, with the aim of mitigating the operational restrictions that compromise the stability and efficiency of the electrical system. The work focuses on two critical subareas of the Colombian Caribbean: GCM (Guajira-Cesar-Magdalena) and Atlantic, where the increasing demand and limited infrastructure capacity have generated bottlenecks that hinder the effective integration of solar and wind projects. To address this challenge, metaheuristic optimization algorithms are used, specifically the Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) and the Genetic Algorithm (GA), in order to carry out an optimal dispatch of generation.
