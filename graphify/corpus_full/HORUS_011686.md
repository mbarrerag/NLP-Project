# BIOLOGICAL COLLECTIONS AND RESEARCH IN THE HEADQUARTERS OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The Orinoquia Headquarters of the National University is located 10 kilometers from the city of Arauca. Due to its rurality, the 50 ha of the campus are ideal for the development of research in savannah ecosystems. Inventories carried out since 2011 have revealed that the headquarters houses 33 species of bats, 19 of amphibians, 22 of reptiles, and 114 of birds, 60 day butterflies, 70 species of ants, 30 species of coprophages beetles and six species of medium mammals that transit sporadically the university campus. As for vegetation have registered 101 species of trees (5508 individuals), more than 50 shrubs and about 140 herbaceous species.In 2012 two biological collections were created, the Entomological Collection of Orinoquia (CEO) and the Orinocense Herbarium (HORI) that have been registered 101 species of trees (5508 individuals), have already been accredited by the IAVH and with some 7,000 records published in SiB Colombia and Gbif. The collections have allowed to generate new records of geographical distribution for the region or Colombia.
