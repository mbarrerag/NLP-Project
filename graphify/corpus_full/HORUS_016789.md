# Contribution to phytochemistry, to the evaluation of antioxidant activity and the possible neuroprotective effect of the species Polygala paniculata.

The Chinese herbalist occupies a very important place in the therapies that make up Traditional Chinese Medicine. In Colombia, due to the limitations in the achievement of herbal medicines of Asian origin, it is necessary to find similar medicinal species in our flora. Polygala paniculata was chosen due to its taxonomic proximity to the species Polygala tenuifolia of the herbalist in China, which has previous studies on its neuroprotective effect, being constituted in a promising species for the treatment of neurodegenerative diseases, which affect public health. The phytochemical presence of roots and aerial parts of the species Polygala paniculata was studied, where secondary metabolites such as flavonoids, terpenes/steroids and cumarins were detected, and its antioxidant capacity was evaluated and the presence of flavonoids was analyzed qualitatively and quantitatively. Comparison with the phytochemicals of Polygala tenuifolia showed significant differences.
