# Convergent and divergent elements of the organizational diagnostics applied to the DANE. A proposal for the improvement of the organizational climate

The purpose of this master's degree final work is to identify the convergent and divergent elements of the organizational diagnoses applied to the National Administrative Department of Statistics - DANE between 2013 and 2022 in order to design an organizational climate intervention program. The study was conducted taking into account the definition of work climate of the Administrative Department of the Public Function in Colombia (2018a), understanding that this refers to the characteristics perceived in the work environment and that have consequences on the work behavior patterns of the public servers. Within the framework of the analytical empirical paradigm, with a pragmatic approach, a descriptive and comparative analysis of the content of three diagnostic reports (Cardona Gallego, 2016; Corporvisionarios, 2013; We Team, 2018) and an analysis of the microdata obtained with the application of the DANE Institutional Environment and Performance Survey (2023a) during ten consecutive years were carried out, using the SPSS program to identify frequency distribution and contingency tables between questions. During the ten years analyzed, the results profile of the entity shows mostly convergences, with few variations in the percentages of perception of agreement or disagreement of the employees regarding the different variables of the work environment. The following elements stand out as converging: difficulties in the management style, perception of long working hours and imbalance between work and personal life, difficulties in performance feedback, limited development opportunities and the implementation of rewards. An intervention plan was formulated focusing on the distribution of workloads of the organizational orientation variable and the performance evaluation processes of the management style variable. It was concluded that the analysis of the perception of the work environment by workers over time in public entities is necessary and relevant to formulate a plan that can be implemented with well-defined policies and practices to be followed up and impact evaluated
