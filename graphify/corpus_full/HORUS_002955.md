# Predicting by neural networks design and process parameters for the manufacture by masked stereolithography (MSLA) of scaffolds subjected to compression loads

In this work, the aim is to predict by neural networks the design and process parameters for the manufacture by the masked stereolithography of scaffolds subjected to compression loads. To this end, we worked on a database constructed from an experimental design Taguchi L9. The parameters to be predicted correspond to the compensation of the average surface (O), the unit cell size (CS) and the layer thickness (LT). The input variables correspond to the flow stress at 0.1% (Sy), as well as the elastic module (E). We planted a regression model for the parameters of O and CS, and a classification model for LT. For the definition of the architectures of neural networks we used three algorithms of hyperparameters, which correspond to: GridSearch, Random and CoartoToFineat. The model selected for the regression is three (3) hidden layers, with 8, 18 and 14 neurons respectively, achieving an absolute error (MAE) of 0.011 for the error model O, and 0.144 for the model CS.
