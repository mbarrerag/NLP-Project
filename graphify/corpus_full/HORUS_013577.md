# Analysis of intimate femicide in Bogotá from 2015 to 2019

Violence against women in Colombia has presented fluctuant values according to the figures shown in the observatory against violence of the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences, from 2015 to 2019, a total of 658 deaths of women in the hands of their partner or ex-partner have been presented,(1) and 115 deaths have been presented by 2020. Deaths with characteristics within the context of gender violence, characteristics that are analytical and useful for the prevention of such events, so it is vital to know the role of risk assessments (Danger Assessment - semi-structured interview) (2) in the fatal outcomes related to intimate feminicides. This study aims to provide more basis to the point of departure of previous studies and, in turn, the continuation of future research establishing an art status for the problem of gender violence, since for the servers and justice operators working in Bogota, it is important to know more of the pre-valent characteristics in the victims of fatal outcomes, and it is possible to evaluate the effectiveness of the “ver assessment of the fatal risk in partner violence at present risk of partner violence”. This study aims to contribute further to the starting point of previous research and service as a continuation for future investigations, establishing a state of the art regulating gender-based violence. For justice services and operators working in Bogotá, understanding prevailing characteristics in victims of fatal outcoms is essential. Additionally, evaluating the effectiveness of ‘mortality risk assessment in partner violence’ conducted by psychologists in Bogotá can help determine the predominant risk factors in these tragic femicides
