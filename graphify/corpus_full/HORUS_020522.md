# Proposing a Non-Paraxial Mach-Zehnder Interferometer for Testing 3D Spatial Coherence

Three-dimensional spatial coherence should be considered by interference with nonplanar arrays of emitters. A Mach-Zehnder interferometer with mobile masks is proposed for testing it and described by the confinement in spatially structured Lorentzian wells formalism
