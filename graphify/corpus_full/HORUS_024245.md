# Data Mining/Text–Structural– Multicriteria as a strategic resource in the selection of personnel.

The use of Data Mining/Text (M-D/T) in the selection of personnel (SP) is a promising field. However, several challenges must be overcome, among them: (1) The few existing applications are not taking advantage of the value of psychological/administrative constructs. (2) The M-D/T has paid little attention to the development/use of procedures to confirm the psychometric quality of such constructs. (3) Nor does it usually develop, with criteria of reproducibility, the interpretation of patterns and decision-making (evaluation, sorting and choice of the candidate). This work develops a framework of M-D/T assisted by models of structural equations and multicriteria decision analysis for the SP, called M-D/T-Structural- Multicriteria. This framework consists of four processes (1. Data recognition, 2. Pattern Discovery, 3. Confirmation of patterns, 4. Evaluation of alternatives and final decision) with semi-automated operations. The framework is validated using multiple/joint representative data (primarily texts) from organizational/individual domains, formal/cases, online/off and strategic.
