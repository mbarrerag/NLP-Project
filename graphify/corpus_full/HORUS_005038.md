# State of methylation of regions that promote three inflammatory response genes in a sample of Colombians with alcohol consumption problems: an exploratory study

The dependence of alcohol exhibits a multifactorial inheritance pattern. The inflammatory response pathways have aroused a particular interest, specifically the epigenetic aspect is very influenced ethnically and by the personal evolution of the disease. To compare the methylation status of a promoting region with CpG islands of some genes candidate for pharmaceutical targets in the inflammatory response pathway, between controls and individuals with problematic alcohol consumption from a sample of a Colombian university population. Previously, studies were carried out on 29 genetic variants that affect 10 genes involved in the inflammatory response. Then analysis of the expression of RNAm and proteins of some of these genes was performed, profiling three genes for methylation studies, namely SNCA, MIF and IFNGR1. The state of methylation of modified DNA with bisulfite was analyzed by PCR with fluorescent marking, electrophoresis capillary and validation with Sanger sequencing.
