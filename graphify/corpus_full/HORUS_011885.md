# MODEL OF THE JOINT ENTIRE PROGRAMMING TO LOCATE LOGISTICAL PLATFORMS IN THE FERRECTOR OF ATLANTIC IN COLOMBIA

Introduction and ObjectiveThe research project aims to identify a proposal to solve the problem of location of logistic platforms through the iron corridor of the Atlantic using the mathematical modeling Facility Location Problem (FLP) identifying as a case of solution the installation of logistic platforms or hubs with the use of the Hub Location Problem method. Methodology and ResultsIn the development of the research, a mixed mathematical model of programming is formulated which is based on the Hub location problem methodology, which seeks to generate the location of logistic platforms evaluating strategic points in the iron corridor of the Atlantic and assigned these as hubs in such a way that with the multimodal articulation of the country the transport times are minimized and maximizing the profits generated by the transport of cargo, this is achieved following the methodology of the work, and articulating the different modes of transport of cargo supporting the multimodality to which the country is betting in the National Council of Economic and Social Policy (CONPES) 3547 for the year 2020.
