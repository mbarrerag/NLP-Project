# Exile accounts. Research-creation

The Exilio reports is a research project created by a collombo-Argentino interuniversity team on migration. It focuses on mapping experiences of Venezuelan migrants on the collombo-Ecuadorian border, Colombians and Venezuelans in Argentina and the United States. The stories have different narrative contexts and intensities; therefore, they were selected, analyzed and screened in the direction of a webdoc series. The team recognizes and makes visible, in ethnographic audiovisual stories, a productive reference of intercultural knowledge and migratory practices that shed light on types of knowledge that can strengthen – in some ways innovate – ways of learning theories and methodologies about interculturality, migratory and transmedial culture. It attempts to look at contrasts, nuances, complex life experiences. The Exilio reports are a strategic audiovisual communicative commitment to understand what happens or may be happening in countries where migrants move and aquietan. They are levels of intercultural and hypermedial knowledge transfer established not in the academy, journalistic knowledge and scientific research, but in the experience of people who write with their history
