# SEXUAL AGREESION ON ADOLESSENCE AND YOUTH: GUIDELINES FOR THE COMPREHENSIVE PROBLEM AND DISAMINATION OF INTERVENTION

This paper aims to highlight the diversity of profiles of sexual offenders identified in the criminal investigation case study and academic literature, in order to show the different types of intervention programmes that have been proposed from a psychological perspective for perpetrators. It highlights that several programmes aimed at juvenile sex offenders do not present scientific evidence of their effectiveness, from which several recommendations or guidelines are drawn to be taken into account in the psychological treatment and reduction of recidivism of this subgroup of adolescent offenders
