# Composional and techno-functional attributes, of seeds germinates of Zapallo (Maximum Cuburbit) stimulated by ultrasound

At present, there is a growing trend in the market for functional foods, i.e., they provide bioactive compounds when consuming them. This trend has led to the promotion in the agro-industrial sector of research aimed at obtaining new foods with functional, nutraceutical and general characteristics, natural raw materials or bioactive compounds that serve as inputs in food processing processes, a technique for obtaining and optimizing bioactive compounds and secondary metabolites is seed germination. Currently there is a great interest in evaluating seed germinations containing significant bioactive compounds, such as Zapallo seeds (maximum cubic) in turn, seed germination is related to technical, nutritional, physical, chemical and antioxidant benefits by direct or indirect consumption (Prime Material). It is important to emphasize that this technique can be combined with Ultrasound technology that acts as a seed elicitor mechanism at the pregerminative stage, and it would be expected that the combination of these techniques can potentially lead to the production of bioactive compounds and germinated seeds of Zapal seeds
