# languages such scientists as enhancers of interest in the kinematic: Case study: Educational Institution Jaime Duque Grisales

This work has its origin at the beginning of the regular physics course 2022 at the Jaime Duque Grisales School in Villamaría, Caldas, there was talk of how interesting and entertaining it would be, when a student warned: "Teacher, that is very difficult, only you understand it, and the truth is very tiring". This situation is repeated periodically, the students arrive with a predisposition and a negative image about the study of the phenomena that occur in nature. The need arises, then, to explore methodological alternatives inside and outside the classroom that awaken the interest of the group and motivate them in the process of learning movement in one dimension. An integrating vision of movement is proposed: verbal language, written language, mathematical language and graphic language, all based on a dynamic of experiential laboratory and field work that allows the mechanical physics students of the I.E Jaime Duque Grisales to approach the concepts with joy, curiosity, questions and the search for answers. Initially, a pretest instrument will be used to assess the interests, motivations, difficulties, obstacles and limitations that students have to approach the concepts of displacement, velocity and acceleration. With the application of the initial instrument and the implementation of a didactic route with theoretical and practical elements that allow learning by doing and playing, there is a frame of reference that allows evaluating and comparing the scope and achievements of the proposal. For this purpose, a final instrument is applied, a six-practice instrument to serve as a benchmark of the achievements attained
