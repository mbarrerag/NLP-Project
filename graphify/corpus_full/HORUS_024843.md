# Some comments concerning “Measurement and correlation of solubility data for atorvastatin calcium in pure and binary solvent systems from 293.15 K to 328.15 K”

The reported solubility data of atorvastatin calcium in binary aqueous mixtures of acetone at various temperatures from 293.15 K to 328.15 K was reanalyzed based on some well-established cosolvency models and a number of points regarding the correlations were discussed
