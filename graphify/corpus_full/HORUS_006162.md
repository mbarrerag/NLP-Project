# Pore-scale analysis of the effect of salinity, type of brine (NaCl/KCl), and pressure on underground hydrogen storage (UHS)

The main objective of this study is to explore the geo-storage of hydrogen in H2/salmurera systems, considering different salmour, salinities and influence of hydrogen flow. The research is structured in four stages: i) Estimation of dimensional numbers, especially the capillary number for different systems Hydrogen-Salmurera: this stage involves the evaluation of capillary number for drainage and imbibination stages by varying the type of brine, salinity and pressure. The study identifies a slight modification of capillary number with respect to salinity and type of brine, without variations in the pressure range considered.
