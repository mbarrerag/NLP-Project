# DATA FILTERING AND CLASSIFICATION FOR THE IDENTIFICATION OF TEXTS RELATED TO SECURITY IN BOGOTÁ COLOMBIA

Abstract'The perception of security (PoS) refers to the subjec- tive evaluation of risk and the magnitude of their consequences. PoS often is quantified using surveys, this information is very valuable but it requires a big time inversion to get it, and for their nature, it does not adapt to the changing dynamics of the PoS. This paper explores the use of expert knowledge combined with classification algorithms to identify security-related texts from social networks, particularly from Twitter. This model is made up of a set of compound keywords defined by Bogota's ¿Secretar ¿¿a de seguridad, convivencia y justicia¿ (SSCJ) and text classification strategies that also consider the language in which the text is written. Results suggest that the use of classification strategies based on expert knowledge and machine learning improves performance in this task compared to methods based simply on the appearance of keywords, however this improvement is not very great, perhaps because the use of words related to security are also used in many other contexts and that the appearance of these texts on social networks is notably less than other types of messages that are published on social networks
