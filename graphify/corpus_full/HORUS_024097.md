# The legal personality, structure and normative dispersion of the Pacific Alliance as obstacles to investment in the economic bloc.

This paper shows an analysis of the legal personality, structure and normative dispersion of the Pacific Alliance. To this end, a qualitative and dogmatic-legal, basic or fundamental research methodology was used, predominantly deductive and of course documentary, which exhibits descriptive data and avoids reductionisms when considering contexts as a whole, analyzing even their past. Consequently, the dissertation includes a preliminary study of the crisis of globalization, regionalism and multilateralism in Latin America, as scenarios prior to the emergence of the Pacific Alliance. Subsequently, the birth of the bloc during the crisis and its tension with other models of integration is analyzed. Then, the problem of legal personality in accordance with international law is studied; the absence of a clear and supranational structure, as well as its normative dispersion is criticized. Finally, some conclusions are presented about the treatment of investment and legal security within the Alliance. Key words: Legal personality, structure, normative dispersion, Pacific Alliance, investment, globalization, regionalism, multilateralism, integration
