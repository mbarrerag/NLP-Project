# Analysis of works and projects as a foundation for artistic creation. Critical review of the work of Borrero, Zamorano and Giovanelli, 1953-1963

This research project aims to establish a close relationship between the theory, which arises from the analysis of the works and projects of architecture and, the professional practice (artistic creation), of the current undergraduate and graduate students of the School of Architecture and Urbanism, which is attached to the Faculty of Engineering and Architecture of the Manizales headquarters UNAL. For this purpose, the critical review of an exceptional and little widespread series is being undertaken, the work of the firm of architects and engineers Borrero, Zamorano and Giovanelli, developed in Cali between 1953 and 1963. Thus, the proposed reflection contributes both to the recovery and dissemination of a work of the Colombian modern architecture, which took place in the periphery of the country, as well as to the training in research-creation of the students of the undergraduate and master's degree in Architecture, starting from the development of the minimum skills required for the approximation, analysis, criticism and conception of the architectural projects. The identification of the project problems, the definition of spatial strategies and formal structures, employed by the firm to respond, from the architecture, to the specific demands of the clients and the context, will provide to the future researchers, teachers and professional references
