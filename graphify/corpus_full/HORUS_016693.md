# THE LABOUR MARKET IN SAN ANDRÉS ISLA: A LOOK FROM TOURISM

The text “The labour market in San Andrés island: a look from tourism”, explores three important aspects to understand the labour market in a sector as important for the island of San Andrés as tourism. In the first chapter, the informality of the labour market is explored, which is predominantly in the territory and is motivated by aspects that go beyond the economic stimulus. It describes this labor condition, preferred by those linked to tourism, but with vulnerabilities for those who exercise it. In the second chapter, the inconsistencies between the business owner of tourism and the future workers of the sector are analyzed. The coincidences and disagreements between the two are identified and highlight the difficulties of the entrepreneurs in finding suitable personnel in the study of occupational gaps in chapter two. Finally, it is analyzed a world situation as extreme and as particular as that faced to stop the spread of the COVID 19, and that in San Andrés determined the closure of the island between March and September 2020. This offers important lessons and calls for attention on the consequences that the great dependence of tourism, recognizes its importance and the affect both formal workers and informal of the sector.
