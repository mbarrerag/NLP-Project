# Design of an antiseptic liquid soap and evaluation of its antiseptic activity, using essential oils and/or extracts from them as an active ingredient.

An antimicrobial soap product was designed using essential oils of cinnamon and orange, where a microemulsion foam soap was reached, the oils chosen were the result of an organoleptic evaluation carried out, in which it was found that the use of Callistemon essential oil is not the most suitable due to its strong odour; additionally taking into account the cost of essential oils it is decided to evaluate cinnamon and orange for use in the final soap. Several combinations of the two oils were prepared to which the antimicrobial activity in vitro was determined against the following microorganisms Enterococcus hirae, Staphylococcus aureus, Pseudomonas aeruginosa and candida albicans; that test showed that the essential oil of cinnamon has a higher antimicrobian activity than the essential oil of orange. However, the formula of best performance was chosen taking into account aspects such as foam formation, organoleptic characteristics, costs and in vitro results of the antimicrobian evaluation.
