# MODELATION OF ELECTRIC SOUND DRIVING WITH APPLICABLE AGRICULTURE

This study analyzes the effect of temperature on electrical conductivity in a KCL potassium chloride solution, guaranteeing the homogeneity of the solution by means of a mechanical agitator. In order to replicate the methodology in the processing of experimental data for the estimation of a mathematical model, which allows control or predict values of electrical conductivity. The identification and results were analyzed by means of the Matlab PID Tuner toolbox achieving an estimate of 33.5% and by the Econometric modeler toolbox obtaining a greater similarity to experimental data
