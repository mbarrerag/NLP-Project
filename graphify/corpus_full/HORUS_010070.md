# CITIZEN SCIENCE AND ITS ROLE IN A MEGADIVERSE COUNTRY: EXPERIENCE WITH HETEROPTERA (INSECT: HEMIPTERA)

The majority of animal species are insects. Colombia is considered one of the megadiverse countries on a global level, mainly based on vertebrate and vascular plant data, being the contribution of insects in this largely unknown sense. Taxonomic knowledge of entomological fauna in the Neotropics, including Colombia, is deficient. This lack of knowledge negatively impacts its proper conservation and use, at a time where different anthropic factors are apparently negatively affecting the abundance and diversity of insect species. The challenge on the part of the community of systematic entomologists today is how we can document this diversity in the face of rapid transformation and impact of ecosystems. Traditionally, we have based our research efforts as taxonomous on the collection of information contained in biological collections. For the proper functioning of biological collections it requires a significant investment of resources both in qualified personnel and in physical infrastructure.
