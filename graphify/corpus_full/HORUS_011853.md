# Automation, modelling and evaluation of an NFT aquaponic system for growing Carpa Roja (Cyprinus carpio) and Lechuga Crespa (Lactuca sativa).

This research project shows the development and evaluation of an automated system for the cultivation of different species on an aquaponic simple recirculation NFT scheme. Based on a systematic review for the identification of the current state of automated, technified and traditional aquaponic systems, an overview of the research orientation was obtained in addition to the critical variables within the system and its optimal ranges, fish-plant ratios and flow management for an adequate exchange of nutrients between the aquaculture and hydroponic blocks. Considering the above and the main concern of the CBA Mosquera Agricultural Biotechnology Center, the collection of system data for the standardization of production cycles and the cost of the equipment, was proposed the design and modelling of an automated system that included the measurement of pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, turbidity, level and flow in the aquaculture tank, and the activation of valves and pumps under manual and automatic control modes, applying industrial technologies of low and medium cost. Subsequently, the comparative evaluation of the biomass yield of Lechugena crespa (Lactucucative) was found as a result of the traditional biomass was applied
