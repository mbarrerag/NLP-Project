# SUPERFICIES IN SPACE FORMS NOT FILLED WITH A PROPOSED CHARACTERISTIC VECTOR

In this talk we will study the surfaces immersed in the three-dimensional non-flat spaces (Riemannians or Lorentzians) that satisfy the condition L(H) = cH for c real, where H is the mean curvature vector and does the Cheng-Yau operator denote the surface.
