# Strengthening and improving the Electronics and Control Laboratory

The acquisition of electrical and electronic equipment and inputs is fundamental for the Electronics and Control laboratory of the Department of Electrical and Automatic Energy of the Faculty of Mines of the National University for several reasons. First, these elements are essential for students of the electric engineering, control engineering and postgraduate courses such as: Master of Engineering - Electrical Engineering, Master of Engineering - Industrial Automation can work more comfortably and efficiently, which contributes to improving their learning experience. Furthermore, having updated and quality equipment allows to offer a better service in practical classes, since students can experiment with state-of-the-art technology and apply the theoretical knowledge in practice. This is key to comprehensive training in the field of electrical and control, since students can familiarize themselves with equipment and technologies that will find in their professional life. On the other hand, the availability of equipment and suitable inputs also benefits teachers, since it allows them to deliver their courses more effectively, using tools that facilitate the understanding of the concepts and the realization of experiments. This results in a different kind of equipment and in a better preparation of electronic equipment and equipment. Finally, we provide them with the most effective daily experience of the electrical equipment
