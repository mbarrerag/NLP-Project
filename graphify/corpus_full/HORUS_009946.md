# Tool for estimating net change in forest area and carbon dioxide emissions due to forest fires

The article describes a tool developed in the platform Google Earth Engine to analyze deforested and burned areas by using satellite images of radar Sentinel-1 SAR and MODIS respectively. The script provided allows users to perform a detailed analysis of CO2 emissions equivalents generated by deforestation and forest fires in a defined study area. The process includes filtering Sentinel-1 images according to various characteristics, calculating specific image quotients and applying filters to reduce the speckle effect. A threshold is established for the loss of vegetation, and deforestation equivalent emissions are quantified in hectares, making it defined into equivalent CO2 emissions by means of specific data for the Colombian Amazonian biome. In addition, the MODIS FIRMS tool is used to identify burned areas, delimit them with the deforested areas and calculate the equivalent emissions by them in a similar way. The resulting tool includes a user interface that displays the results and a legend area to interpret the colors on the map.
