# Cultural strengthening of indigenous students of UNAL - headquarters of La Paz based on the exchange of knowledge and recognition of the territory

This project aims to generate processes of cultural strengthening taking into account the agenda of the group of indigenous students of the National University of Colombia - headquarters of La Paz, as conceptual and organizational guide. The accompaniment to this group of students by University Welfare allowed generating a series of meetings between the teachers involved in this project and the students, in order to define their main interests, summarized by themselves in three main axes: 1-Kunsamu kumu uka'si. (Cultural strengthening - central axis); 2- Kunsamu unkunta'si, ga'kunamusin awiri unisi uzweykwasin. (Interchange of knowledge); and 3- Niwiumuke jwaunka'si (Territorial recognition). One of the main concerns of the students is the fear that the university training will become a way of distance from the traditional and community-based ways of living and knowing. Thus, this project hopes to strengthen the strategies proposed by themselves to re-enfer the experience in the university. One of the project will also succeed to deepen the construction of a more inclusive and diverse university, while it will contribute to the innovation. These activities will always be carried out through the guidance and advice of majors, leaders, mamos, cultural or spiritual leaders of the communities to which the students belong (arhuacos, wiwas, Kankuamos, Koguis, Wayuus, among others). This process will motivate the realization of artistic and cultural events, the development of intercultural dialogue methodologies, the construction of a literary piece, which is a contribution to the pedagogical innovation model of the Headquarters of La Paz
