# Origin and classification of mineral deposits (Continued)

Classification.—The mineral deposits that have been preserved without substantial modification in their structure or composition, being found as they were originally formed, are called primary or hypogenic. In contrast to these, those that have undergone modifications due to dynamic causes, are distinguished as deformed, and those that have been altered by surface agents as secondary or supergenic. In this sense the study of mineral deposits involves three consecutive stages, namely their formation, their deformation by mechanical causes and their superficial alteration and consequent enrichment, or impoverishment.
