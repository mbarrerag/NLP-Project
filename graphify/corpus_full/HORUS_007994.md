# USING MODERN FIELD MAPPING TECHNIQUES TO CHARACTERIZE MAGMATIC-HYDROTHERMAL IOCG DEPOSIT ALTERATION ZONES IN A LOWER CRETACEOUS PERUVIAN PLUTONIC COMPLEX

the cerro media luna plutonic complex (cmlpc) is part of the peruvian coastal batholith (pcb) near pisco. The CMLPC has been considered a Cu porphyry (CP) system; however, this model needs to be reassessed since the PCB hosts the 180-100 Ma Andean IOCG belt. To characterize the CMLPC metallogenic deposit and the magmatic-hydrothermal system, we used in-situ analyses, hand sample description and GIS methods for preliminary semi-quantitative results on alteration zones and mineralization types. Fieldwork employed portable iPad, Garmin GLO 2 GPS, magnetic susceptibility (MS) meter, and gamma spectrometer (GS). Strabospot 2 on an iPad recorded mineralogical, magnetic, and gamma data. The GS gave immediate K, U, and Th values. The IOCG and CP deposits have similar suites of secondary minerals; however the hematite and magnetite alteration intensity, and the Na-Ca alteration are distinctive for this deposit. Previous whole-rock geochemistry reveals Na2O and CaO rich rocks, with variations of 4-8 and 3-12 wt%, respectively. The GS, K (0-1.1%) and U (0.1 to 8.2 ppm) values are low to moderate, and U values are mostly constant across the pluton with a positive correlation with magnetite. Defined alteration zones based on outcrop observations and Infrared (IR) spectroscopy include: Mgt+Hem+Qz±Sd±Ank, Mgt+Hem+Ep, Mgt+Act+Ep+Chl±Kspar, Hem+Mgt+Qz, and Hem+Ep±Qz±Sd±Ank. The changes in MS from 0.05 to 43.60 ×10-3 SI corroborates the different zones. This coupling of modern field techniques provides a quick and easy way to define alteration zones, even though hand sample description provided more detailed information than the gamma ray spectrometry data, in this case. Proposed alteration zones are based on mineralogical observations made in the field, IR, and MS anomalous measurements. The MS anomalous values, low U concentration, and sodic-calcic alteration better fit the description for IOCG than CP
