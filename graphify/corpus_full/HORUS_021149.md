# Applications of Symbolic Computing and Combination in the Quantitative Study of Discreet Large Size Structures

In recent decades there has been an exponential development in the analysis of algorithms and its implications both at theoretical level and applied. Today many of the tasks that carry out different sectors of society are modelled by algorithms that improve the productivity of the sector and automate the mechanical activities that develop. Within the analysis of algorithms arises a line of study that allows predicting the quantitative behavior of discrete structures of large size. These structures come, in many cases, from scientific models of disciplines such as probability, statistical physics, computational biology, information theory, among others. The basic problem is summarized in estimating the number of elements of a family of discrete objects that satisfy a certain property or parameter. To analyze and model quantitatively the discrete structures of large size, the quantitative analysis is used for the objects of combinatorical and discrete mathematics, for example the functionalities, which is based on the exhaustive generation of the objects to study, among others. This has given the development of different strategies that allow to model these objects, even if they are of different nature.. The applications of this topic range from cryptography and entire programming, to theoretical topics in number theory and combinatory. This project will focus on the analysis of the distribution of reticular points on graphs associated with polyominos of text strings. The study will be developed in the two phases described above, that is, a computational phase and a second phase of combinatory analysis. In particular in the second part the symbolic method will be used to rigorously demonstrate the results conjectured in the first part. The research theme of the project is related to the works that have been developed in recent years within the DiscreMath research group: Discret Mathematics and Computer Science and the Combinatory Analysis Research Semillero. The techniques proposed in this research have been used successfully in the study of other discrete structures (see pdf of the project attached for more details). Added to publications in high impact journals, in the last five years have been linking undergraduate and postgraduate students in the study of these themes. Several of these students have participated actively in publications and in conferences
