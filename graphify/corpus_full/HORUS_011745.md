Furthering Grothendieck perspective, Galois theory on monoidal categories could be framed as an equivalence with the Eilenberg-Moore category of actions of a Hopf monad. In this talk, we expose how to recover Artin's perspective in this framing. We show that, in the case of an augmented monad, there are universal denitions of invariants and stabilizers which yield a naturally-arising Galois correspondence. Additionally, in the case of an augmented Hopf monad over a monoidal closed category, we found explicit procedures for the computation of invariants and stabilizers
