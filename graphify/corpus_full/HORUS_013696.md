# Adaptive governance as a strategy for the conservation of tropical dry forests

The main objective of this research was to establish the viability of an adaptive governance system for the conservation of the tropical dry forest in the municipality of Candelaria, Valle del Cauca, where the largest potential area of the tropical dry forest of the department is located, in which, various organizations see in its conservation a form of re-appropriation of the territory, the possibility of promoting new forms of production and consolidation of spaces for recreation; needs that have triggered an environmental conflict in territory called in this research as “conflict by the tropical dry forest”, which, represents an emergency for the government institutions, since complex strategies have not been managed to allow the processing of the same. Thus, in the research, interviews were developed with the officials of the state entities and a focal group with the environmental organizations of Candelaria, in addition, social networks were developed that allowed to know the dynamics of the conflict and conclude that in Candelaria if the development of strategies such as adaptive governance is feasible through the arrangements to three key spaces with which the municipality already counts: the co-management of the Step in Tip, the consolidation of a community monitoring network and the reactivation of the Municipal Environmental Committee (Text from the Int. the processing of the same.
