# ASSESSMENT OF THE EFFICIENCY OF DIATOMIES IN REMOVAL OF METALS PESED

This research analyzed the behavior of different Peruvian diatomites and their potential use for removal of heavy metals of contaminated effluents. Seven samples of different diatomites were characterized by X-ray diffraction, X-ray fluorescence, scanning electron microscopy, specific surface area and cation exchange capacity. The evaluation of the removal efficiency of copper, zinc and lead was carried out by shaking 1.5 g of each diatomite in solutions with concentrations of 10 ppm for Cu and Pb, and 3 ppm for Zn for one hour. The evaluation of each sample was filtered by gravity and analyzed by atomic absorption spectrometry. The results obtained for removal efficiency were correlated with microstructural, chemical and physical chemical characteristics of the diatomites. It was found that each Ptomite was adjusted by percentage and maximum percentage of Pn for removal of heavy metals of contaminated water, even with low silicon oxide content. Zn was the most efficient removal area, being this was studied up to 98 %
