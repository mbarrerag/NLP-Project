# History of the Colombian capital market and other trials

This work aims to carry out a thorough analysis of the past, present and future of the Colombian capital market, one of the main actors in providing financing and investment possibilities to virtually all economic actors. Throughout nine chapters, a history of about a century is reconstructed on how the capital market has been built in the country, what has spurred and prevented its greatest growth and how the relationship with banking has been created to build the Colombian financial ecosystem. In this re-examining, a review of the regulation on the capital market is also carried out and its relevance to its development is evaluated. This book presents the results of the Second Capital Market Mission, to understand the advances proposed to develop the sector, and offers an overview of the areas in which it is necessary to continue working. It also provides a detailed analysis of the dynamics and evolution of private capital funds in Colombia. The judicious study of the past and present of the capital market in Colombia allows to speak of its imagined future, where various participants present their perspectives in terms of innovation and deepening. Finally, three border investigations are presented where the financial crisis and construction of portfolios allow. This compilation seeks to be a reference in the history of Colombia
