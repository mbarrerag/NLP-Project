# BOUNDARY CONTROLLABILITY PERSPECTIVES OF COUPLED SYSTEM OF TWO KORTEWEG-DE VRIES EQUATIONS

We delve into the examination of exact boundary controllability for a nonlin- ear coupled system comprising two Korteweg-de Vries equations con ned within a bounded interval. This model delineates the interactions between two weakly nonlinear gravity waves within a strati ed uid. Given the system's nature, six boundary conditions are necessary. However, in investigating the controllability property, we explore alternative combinations of control inputs, utilizing a maximum of four. Initially, we derive results for the linearized system employing a classical duality approach, alongside uncovering some latent regularity properties of the boundary terms. This method simpli es the controllability issue into an- alyzing a spectral problem, which is solved using the PaleyWiener method introduced by Rosier in 1997. Subsequently, we address the task of determining when a speci c quotient of entire functions remains an entire function, posing it as a problem of factoring an entire function. Depending on the control con guration, this leads to the examination of a tran- scendental equation. Finally, by leveraging the contraction mapping theorem, we establish local controllability for the complete system
