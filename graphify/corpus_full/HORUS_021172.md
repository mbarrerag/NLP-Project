# Non-Linear Optical Phenomena Dynamics in Optic Fibres and Machine Learning Algorithms for Identification of Contaminant Gases in Air

In this project, the fotonic and machine learning technologies will be implemented for the detection under controlled conditions in the laboratory of air pollutants such as: particulate matter (PM), aerosols, NOx, ozone, CO and SO2, according to the territorial needs and productive bets of the Department of Antioquia. With the execution of this project, the alliance Universidad Nacional de Colombia _x0096_ Sede Medellín and the institute CREOL the College of Optics and Photonics, affiliated to the University of Central Florida, allow their support for the technical implementation of the project, to increase the scientific production, the generation of new knowledge and the consolidation of relevant human talent. The technological infrastructure left by this project together with the generated knowledge, would help to form the bases of a Femto-LIDAR system for the simultaneous detection of several polluting gases and the monitoring in real time of the environmental conditions of the entire Aburrá Valley, the fluidity and the analysis of the different components of the Ramto-LIDAR. - Increase the quality of research on air pollutants - International cooperation between research groups. - Generation of new knowledge in the area of optical metrology for environmental variables in the tropics - Formation of high-level human capital specialized in the area of air quality measurement As mentioned above, photonic and Machine Learning technologies will be implemented for the detection of air pollutants under controlled conditions in the laboratory using HC-PCF, according to the territorial needs and productive bets of the Department of Antioquia. The Alliance with the CREOL institute will allow to increase the scientific production, the generation of new knowledge and the consolidation of relevant human talent. The concepts, theory and phenomena of photonics will be used to take spectral traces of polluting gases and Machine Learning techniques will be implemented to identify the characteristics of these pollutants present in the air environment. In this project, a technological advance is proposed with the use of HC-PCF to confine both gas and light waves simultaneously within the fiber so that multi-species measurements are made, opening the way to be able to monitor in real time, for example, the production of pollutants within factories without changing the multiple-points production
