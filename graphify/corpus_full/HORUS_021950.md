# Social appropriation in the implementation of renewable energy projects: Case study, Isla Fuerte, Bolívar.

This research is carried out in response to the latent need to connect by means of a route, to project managers of such an important sector, as is the energy, to the communities and their territories, in an exercise of empowerment that takes place around 6 axes around the concept of Social Appropriation. Thus, actions related to the recognition of the territory, the citizen participation, communication, transfer, management and transformation of Knowledge, Science, Technology and Innovation are carried out, around the energy projects that are developed in the place. This academic exercise has as territory of application to Isla Fuerte, a co-organisation of Cartagena, Bolívar, which has gone through multiple projects that have failed in terms of energy. As a non-Interconnected Area (ZNI) to the national system, initiatives have been formulated that have not only failed by the technical problem, but also because there are collective failures, collaborative work, transparency and governance in which it is suggested to the project managers, strengthening the communities for the success of the project. According to this research, the sustainability of a project will depend on the citizens and the managers in the measure that both are linked to the networks and to the involvement of the project
