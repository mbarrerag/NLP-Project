# Fiscal rule and objective employment in Colombia: estimation of Okun Law coefficients for the measurement of the product gap

The structural balance sheet rule for Colombia defines targets around debt collection and fiscal deficit levels. As part of the fiscal deficit arrangement, the product gap is found, whose unobservable value is used to estimate the cyclical component of the fiscal rule. This work criticizes the current arrangement of the fiscal rule and defines a potential new GDP through the estimation of Okun Law coefficients with the use of the ARDL UECM model, calculating a new product gap starting from defining a full employment goal as an alternative proposal to the idea of existence of a NAIRU. . The structural balance rule for Colombia defines targets regulating debt stock and fiscal deficit. As part of the fiscal deficit setup, there is output gap, which is unobservable value is used to estimate the cycle component of the fiscal rule.
