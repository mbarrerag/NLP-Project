# Standardisation of plant extracts with biocidal activity with perspectives such as biopesticides

The use of agrochemical synthesis includes an industrial type tool focused on maintaining the biological yield of crops. This system is the most used in the face of the growing world population that consequently leads to the increase in the demand for food, the synthetic pesticides (fungicides, insecticides or herbicides) have been related to serious environmental and human health impacts as they are cumulative. As a consequence, in the face of the generation of resistance to pesticides and deforestation, there is an increase of herbivorous agents that have gained status of _x0093_plagas_x0094_, the increase of phytopathogenic infestation in monocultures and soil degradation. Moreover, taking into account the origin of synthetic pesticides, which depend on non-renewable sources for their production, it is necessary to seek sustainable alternatives that can supply the demand, be effective and without the environmental impact that is evidenced in the current. It is therefore that this proposal is proposed with the aim of continuing to explore sustainable alternatives from plant species native to Andean forests. Colombia is the most biodiverse country by square area, the potential of many of plants is considered to be in the present. The following are the results obtained: the following are intended to continue the work with the species from which the antifungal and allelopetical bioactivity and chemical characterization are obtained, with a view to the standardization of extracts as potential biocidal agents. Thus, collections of the selected species (Familias Fagaceae and Annonaceae) will be carried out, and the optimal form of extraction will be determined to establish the stability of the chemical profiles with respect to the bioactive compounds that are held from previous work or have been recognized after biodirigid fractionations in the course of this proposal. Once the metabolic profiles have been obtained, the samples derived from the extracts will be dosed, to verify the effectiveness and reproducibility of the potential generation of biopecidal agents
