# Biserial algebras, Brauer graph algebras and some of its applications.

The main objective of this work is to study the algebras of Brauer configuration. For this purpose, it begins by the exposition of basic aspects of the Theory of representation of carcasses, then the bierial algebras and special biseriales are described from examples and properties of them that relate them to the emergence of Brauer graph algebras, the latter are defined and exemplified to make the subsequent presentation of Brauer configuration algebras and some of their properties. From the above, this work offers as a result the definition of Brauer configuration algebras associated with points in the plane, establishing equations that allow to calculate the dimension of these algebras and their center. In addition, examples related to the construction of Brauer configuration algebras associated with points in the plane, regions of freezing and regions of mutation, determining properties for them. Finally, some conclusions and recommendations are presented that will serve as a basis for future research works
