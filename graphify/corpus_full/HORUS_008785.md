# Surfaces in Non-flat 3-Space Forms Satisfying □ H→ = λH→

In this paper, we locally classify the surfaces immersed into the non-flat (Riemannian or Lorentzian) 3-space forms satisfying the condition □ H→ = λH→ for a real number λ , where H→ is the mean curvature vector field and □ denotes the Cheng–Yau operator of the surface. We obtain the classification result by proving, at a first step, that the mean curvature function must be constant and, in a second step, we complete the classification
