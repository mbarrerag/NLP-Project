# Development of a surface modified zinc oxide nanomaterial with Hexadeciltrimethyl Ammonium Bromide (CTAB) to inhibit fine migration

The migration of finos in the porous medium is an inherent cause of the high flow rates and changes in pH (Russell, et al., 2017). These fine ones block the porous gorges thus reducing their permeability and therefore the productivity (Loi, et al., 2023). The industry has addressed this problem with different strategies of remediation, where the most employed has been the matrix acidification which attacks the problem in a direct way. However, this technique induces alterations in the matrix, increasing the deconsolidation (Sources, et al., 2022). The nanotechnology has been established in itself, as an emerging technology since the nanoparticles can alter the superficial loads of the finos and sands, increasing the force of attraction and thus inhibiting their mobilization through the porous medium (Franco, Zabala, & Cortés, 2017).
