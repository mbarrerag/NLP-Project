# NATURAL THERMAL INSULATION: MONTANOA QUADRANGULARIS SHULTZ BIP MARROW

The scientific name is Montanoa quadrangularis Schultz Bipontianus in K. Koch, known in Colombia such as arboloco, anime, koya, pauche, jiquimilla or escorzonera.The arboloco is an endemic species from the Colombian-Venezuelan Andes region (Álvarez Mejía, 2006) (Naranjo, 2013).An advantage of The advantage of the tree is that after each cut, it continues to generate stems from the same cut strain, which makes the cultivation of this species unnecessary for its exploitation, contributing to the sustainability of the material.Young trunks are composed of pith surrounded by a thin cortical wall.Its pith has an appearance similar to expanded polystyrene, which is why in some regions of Colombia it is known as natural thermal insulation (Álvarez Mejía, 2006). The density of this material is 0.034 g/cm3, a water absorption percentage of 425.17%, a thermal conductivity in anhydrous state of 0.044 W/mK and a high resistance to acidic and alkaline.In this research, the analysis of the microstructure, composition of the material was carried out using scanning electron microscopy, infrared spectroscopy by Fourier, confocal microscopy, differential scanning thermogravimetry and X-ray diffraction, in addition analysis of durability in acid and alkaline environments, along with other properties physical and thermal.Finally, it was compared with other materials of natural and artificial origin, to validate its potential as a thermal insulator in construction
