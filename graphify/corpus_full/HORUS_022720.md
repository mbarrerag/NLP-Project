# Textural characterisation of the front train muscles and proposed new cuts in three, four and five stars from the Anti-Oqueño Urabá (Colombia)

The main objective of this research was to characterize the muscles of the front train of cattle of the Anti-Oqueño Urabá in Colombia. 12 quarters of channel classified in three, four and five stars according to NTC 4271 (1996) were dissected and 28 of the most bulky muscles were separated. The hardness was determined by a Warner Bratzler probe coupled to a TAXT System Plus Texturometer. From these values and using the criteria of optimality (HG: max; HC: min; ASW: max) was sought to define a low number of primary cuts that presented similar ternity, offered continuity and, as far as possible, had identifiable skeletal molds, which allowed to establish a rigid procurement protocol. It is concluded that simultaneous compliance with these conditions is not possible and that the system of classification by stars does not allow establishing differences between the values of ternity by classification
