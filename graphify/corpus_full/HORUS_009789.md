# Proposal for the implementation of a digital repository of undergraduate studies in the Department of Social Sciences at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Palmira headquarters, as a strategy for preservation and consultation in open access.

This study aims to present a proposal for the implementation of a digital repository (RD) for the Department of Social Sciences (DCS) of the National University of Colombia, Palmira headquarters (UNAL-PAL), as a strategy for preservation and consultation in open access (AA) for undergraduate studies (GG) of undergraduate students. To this end, literature and web portals of universities in Colombia and other countries were reviewed, with the purpose of having theoretical criteria regarding: preservation of TG, guidelines, AA policies, trends in research data management (GDI) and guidelines for the implementation of RD. The methodological approach presents a qualitative research style based on the grounded theory. The purpose of proposing structured interview based on the technique with a nonprobabilistic sample 70 people in two groups: 1) undergraduate students and professors/researchers of the DCS; 2) library staff and entrepreneurs of the region, with the aim of proposing preservation strategies that fit the institutional landscape.
