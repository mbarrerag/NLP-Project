# TECHNOLOGIES USED IN THE HOSPITAL DISCHARGE PROCESS OF TECHNOLOGY-DEPENDENT CHILDREN

Identify health technologies used to guide hospital discharge of technology-dependent children and promote discussion in light of the Deficit Theory of Self-Care.Method: Literature review prepared in order to answer the following question: What health technologies help the hospital discharge process for children? dependent on technology?Therefore, we used a high-quality search equation sensitivity in the following databases/data library: MEDLINE, SCIELO, CINAHL, Pubmed, Scopus and Web of Science.The selection was carried out through managers of references, blindly, by independent reviewers.The information extracted was discussed in light of the Self-Care Deficit Theory.Results: Eight were selected publications that identified five process technologies (three assistance scripts with different methodologies and two teleconsultation processes), and three product (health education booklets).Conclusion: In short, when mapping technologies, the scarcity in quantity and the absence of theoretical reference in its development. health technology;hospitalized child;chronic illness;high hospital;self-care;nursing
