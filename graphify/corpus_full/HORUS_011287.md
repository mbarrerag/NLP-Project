# OXG WATER HANDLING AND USE IN INTER-SPECTIVE HIBRINES

It is estimated that, by the end of the 21st century, the temperature of the planet will be between 1.8 °C and 4 °C above that observed at the end of the 20th century. It is stated that, if Colombia does not take action to mitigate the impact of climate change, it will affect 80% of the crops and 60% of the cultivated area. Reductions in rainfall will result in difficulties for the establishment and maintenance of traditional crops, and oil palm will not be indifferent to this situation, due to the alterations in the rainfall patterns that suffer some areas of the country where it is sown or to the scal- scal- ed of water sources required to meet the needs of the crop. In this context, the management and efficient use of water in the OxG hybrids (Elais oleifera x Elais guinensis) will become relevant, considering that this species is sown in regions with at least three months dry.
