# COMPOSITIONAL AND MICROBIOLOGICAL QUALITY IN DUBLE DUBLE MILK PURPOSE IN ARAUQUITA, ARAUCA COLOMBIA

Milk is a product whose composition and microbiological state varies depending on the breed of animals, diet and the hygienic and processing conditions during its production. Inadequate handling reduces nutritional quality and increases the proliferation of microorganisms that can affect the health of consumers. to evaluate the physical and microbiological composition of milk in double purpose systems of the municipality of Arauquita, Oriente de Colombia. samples of the total milk generated per day were taken in 30 double purpose farms, during four weeks of the dry period of the study area. The physical and chemical composition of the milk was estimated with an ultrasonic equipment (LAC-SA-90) and the microbiological component by the technique of Spearman (TPC CL).
