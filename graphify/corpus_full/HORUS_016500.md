# Sixty years of the Zootecnics program at the University and in Colombia

We would have to go back to the pre-neolithic archaeological finds to find the first forms of interaction of man with animals, from which a millennial dependence would be confirmed that, following domestication, could rather be called “interdependence”, as ever since the Neolithic Revolution to the present day is a biological co-evolution of many animal species and of man himself
