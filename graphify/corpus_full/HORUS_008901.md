# MINERAL COMPOSITION OF NATIVE GRAMINIES AND CULTIVE INTRODUCIDES UNDER THE ECOSYSTEM OF BANKS IN THE UNIFTABLE SABANE OF COLOMBIAN ORINOCIA

The graminea respectively constitute the main source of minerals in the ruminant grazing diet, necessary to guarantee animal health and performance. In tropical forages, the available information on mineral composition and its influencing factors is limited. to compare the mineral composition in different ages and periods of native grasses and introduced into the ecosystem of floodable savannahs of the Colombian Orinoquia is limited. in floodable savanna conditions and in the bank fissiographic position were established by triplicated in plots of 9 m2 (3 x 3 m) native species Paspalum plicatulum was dried up by means of a Pánicum versicolor and the introduced Bracharia hybrid mulato during the transition periods rain-sek and drought-lluvia. In each period, the greasy forage samples at 30 € 40 and 50 days was used by means of the near infrared soil (NIRS). P < 0.05). P. plicatulum and P. versicolor grasses presented higher ash contentsNa and K compared to mulatto grass.
