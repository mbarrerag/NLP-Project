# A BIBLE REVIEW OF FINTECH AND ITS MAIN STUDIES

This article shows a review of the academic literature on financial technology (Fintech) allowing to identify three sub-areas. Traditional banking presents a great challenge with Financial Technology (Fintech) and the transformation of financial services. Which is perceived as a significant disruption in the economy with the arrival of these new business models. It is a relatively new topic that each day acquires greater importance and which still presents many research topics, so that a review of the sub-areas is constructed. Therefore, the objective of this article is to show the evolution of the sub-areas of Fintech through a review that presents the perspectives of this topic of study at the present time. For this purpose, a network analysis supported by scientometric techniques was carried out, the Web Tree of Science tool was used from a consultation in Scopus. The most important documents were classified and an analysis of the citations was carried out. The results showed three different sub-areas: Fintech new business model challenge in the digital economy; the future of banking in credits a competitive advantage and privacy in the data
