# AFFROCOLOMBIES, CONFLICT AND RECONCILIATION

The specific forms in which armed conflict has impacted Afro-Colombian populations, the re-existences and resolutions brought by these same populations in the face of the injustices of war, the particularity of the Afro-urban experience, the coloniality of traditional historiography and the plurality of the symbolic expressions of black peoples are addressed in this book with broad intellectual vision and rigor, two qualities that coexist. Afro-Colombias offers both the general public and the specialized public a rich perspective on the current anthropological, historical and aesthetic research on Afro-Colombianity, a panorama in which the study of trap, ancient ceramics or alabae is combined with economic history, anti-racist ethnography and the evaluation of state policies
