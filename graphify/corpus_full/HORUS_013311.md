# Numerical computation of the acoustic radiation force exercised by a standing wave on an object immersed in a fluid by using a Lattice Boltzmann method for waves

This work introduces a numerical procedure to calculate the acoustic radiation force produced by stationary waves on a compressible object immersed in a non-viscous fluid. Instead of directly simulating the equations of fluid mechanics, the proposal uses a Lattice Boltzmann model for waves to calculate the first-order disturbances of the pressure and velocity fields, and uses them to calculate the second-order acoustic radiation force on each element of the object surface, using an interpolation scheme with kernel to increase accuracy. The calculated force can be used later to integrate the movement of the object by molecular dynamics. The method is implemented in the simulation software Lattice Boltzmann LB3D and in a C++ code of own development, and is used to integrate the total force on a sphere and disk, respectively. The results reproduce with good precision the theoretical expressions of the functionality, Gor’kov and Wei for the sphere and disk respectively, even with a modest number of cells of Lattice-Botzmann. In addition, the force calculated in the case 2D, when combined with a sample of molecular dynamic integration, reproduces, the of the object, and it employment an interpolation scheme with kernel to increase the accessibility.
