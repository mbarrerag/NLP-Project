# Case study: Dynamics of three family systems in the context of family reunification of girls and boys who survived severe child abuse

This case study aimed to characterize three families responsible for the care and protection of children who had previously been institutionalized in an operator of the Colombian Family Welfare Institute (ICBF) due to the experience of severe child abuse. It was carried out in the city of Bogotá and a descriptive case study design was used. The sample consisted of three family systems in the process of family reunification. The variables were evaluated by means of a sociodemographic questionnaire, the inventory to evaluate the family environment in preschools (HOME), the Q-sort of maternal behavior for preschools and the Q-sort of family functioning "Georgine Family Q-sort". The results obtained show that the profiles of the families studied are favorable for the reintegration, which is associated with the selection process and psychosocial support carried out by the inter-disciplinary team responsible for the Administrative Process of Resting Rights (PARD) of each child, girls and adolescents.
