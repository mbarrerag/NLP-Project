# The application of generalised LINE MODELS (MLG) for the analysis of the national survey of quality of life: the case of study of Saint Andres Island.

This paper presents the results of the application of generalized linear models (MLG) in the data of the National Quality of Life Survey (ENCV) of the National Administrative Department of Statistics - DANE, and in particular in the data of the island of San Andrés. Naturally, ENCV has several dimensions of data that seek to categorize the living conditions in which housing, households and individuals of the population are found. These dimensions store data on housing, health, labour force, household living conditions, education, home services, among others. These data were taken to run the MLG model to know the estimated probability of being considered poor from the sample of the population of San Andrés in 2011 and 2021, and thus contrast the results. Preliminary findings have been that, among the highest academic degree reached by the individual and parents, have their own home or are paying it, are less likely to be considered poor.
