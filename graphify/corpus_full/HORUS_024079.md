# Habita-culo

The Habitá-culo project includes the artistic experience of a personal process that is consolidated in the mastery of Visual and Plastic Arts. The starting point is the experience of the sensitive body in leisure and sedentaryism that manifest themselves in writing and gesture. Besides the body, the space is equally important. It is noted as a habitacle, this is an impossible place to place in a tangible spatiality. The white cube shows the importance of pointing out the body inhabited by space and the interaction between the private and the public. From this habitacle two artistic proposals are developed. The first is the Device, which responds to the space of the habitacle whose origin is the photographic principle of the dark chamber. The second result is the materialization of the creature that tries to make public daily actions in the space of the intimate. This character is born from the experience of the body and is linked to the device. materialization of the creation that three to make daily actions public in the intimate space. This character is born from the body’s own experience and is linked to the device. Finally, the project collects the material, compiled from the forms and directions of the exterior that allow disdaine and build a new look against the dust
