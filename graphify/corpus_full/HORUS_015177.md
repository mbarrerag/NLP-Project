# Development of a calibration procedure for sound meters and acoustic calibrators in accordance with IEC 61672–3 and IEC 60942.

This document describes the development of an acoustic instrument calibration system in accordance with international standards IEC 60942 and IEC 61672–3. It begins with a brief review of the systems developed so far, analysing their advantages and opportunities for improvement. A study is then made of the normative framework and the nature of the equipment under test, and the instrumentation required for the calibration system is determined. The sonometer calibration system is controlled by an application that uses image recognition, so after the normative framework is explained the method designed for the recognition of numerical characters, and includes results of the processing and performance of the classifier. A section is then introduced describing the implementation details of software applications encoded in Python; in particular, the GRAFCET model is presented, which was the basis for the development of the application for acoustic calibrators.
