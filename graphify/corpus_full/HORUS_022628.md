# CUTHANEAN OSTEOSARCOMA METASTASIS PRODUCTIVE GREAT THREE IN CANINE.

osteosarcoma represents up to 85% of malignant bone tumors in canines, affects middle-aged dogs (seven years) and large breeds; Golden Retriever is one of the most predisposed breeds to develop this disease. Metastasis when it occurs usually affects lung in 90% of cases, ganglia in 4 % and skin varies between 4 and 11% of the times; additionally the presence of metastasis to ganglia and skin represents a worse prognosis of survival. Anamnesis: a surgical piece of the right chest member with multiple masses, belonging to a female canine, Golden Retriever, 8 years old. Diagnosed aids: X-ray and histopathology with staining of Hematoxilin and Eosin Clinical findings: in the radiographic examination was also evidentitis and megalophilous growth of bone region. osteosarcoma, which is rare
