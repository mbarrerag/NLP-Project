# FULL GRAPH PARTICLE SYSTEM WITH REMOVAL ON JUMP

We study a system of particles in the complete graph, in which each particle is removed after visiting a vertex and/or waking up a dormant particle if the vertex contains one.This is a variation of the model known as the frogs with non-geometric lifespans.We consider that the process starts with an active particle at a single vertex.We show that the proportion of vertices seen ited and that the absorption time of the process converges in probability to zero when the number of vertices in the complete graph tends to infinity
