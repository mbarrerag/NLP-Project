# Teaching of statistics in natural science contexts of sixth and seventh grades

The aim of this work is to design and develop a didactic proposal that integrates statistics with the natural sciences, to promote the development of statistical and scientific competences in students of sixth and seventh grades. The results show that the didactic proposal facilitates students to construct statistical concepts such as: variables, tables, graphs and central trend measures, through the collection, organization, analysis and interpretation of data from contexts of natural sciences. In addition, students develop observation skills, research, communication, argumentation and critical thinking, when formulating questions, hypotheses, analysis and conclusions on the natural phenomena investigated. It is concluded that the integration of statistics with natural sciences is a didactic strategy that enriches the learning of both subjects, by promoting the use of statistics as a tool to understand, analyse and interpret real situations, and by promoting the development of competences that contribute to the integral training of students . statistics as a tool to understand, analysis and interpret real situations, and by fostering the development of competitions that contribute to the integral formation of students
