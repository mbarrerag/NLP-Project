# Maurice Ravel's noble and sentimental values: Structural and interpretive analysis

This work intended mainly to familiarize the reader with the "Noble and Sentimental Waltzes" from various approaches. To this end, the composer's context is considered in the first instance, taking into account the current structure of the relationship between the artistic currents of the moment and the composer's musical and aesthetic style. The specific traits of the waltz as a genre are used as a background as well. Second, a structural analysis was developed, organizing the musical element independently.
