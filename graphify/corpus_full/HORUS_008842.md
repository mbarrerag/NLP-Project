# BODY TERMAL INCREASE BY POSTPRANDIAL THERMOGENESIS IN LACHESIS ACROCHORDA (VIPERIDAE)

Postprandial thermogenesis, Postprandial reflux is a term used to reference the energetic increase of an organism after feeding. We analyze the postprandial thermogenesisreflux using thermal response in three individuals of Lachesis acrochorda belonging toin the National Institute of Health of Colombia. We used a thermal camera to evaluate the thermal changes across the time from feeding to 40 hours later. We feed the snakes with mice that represent 10.4-16.1% of the snake body mass. The results showed two increases of temperature, the first one at the first 5 hours, and the second one at 25 hours post feeding. These temperature increases are probably due to the stomach digestion and the beginning of intestinal digestion respectively.
