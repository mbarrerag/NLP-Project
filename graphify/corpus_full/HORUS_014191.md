# Engineering contributions to human management in organizations

Problem situation: From the functional point of view, organizations are structured in areas or dependencies. One of those areas is called human management. In human management, professionals from different disciplines participate, namely social work, business administration, law, psychology and engineering. However, it could be assumed that when an organization seeks a professional for the area of human management and, among the different professions, chooses an industrial engineer or administrator, it is because the organization expects this engineer to provide something specific that it does not find in the professionals of the other disciplines. And that contribution, probably, is associated with the technical training, proper to engineering, which is focused on identifying problems and developing solutions for them
