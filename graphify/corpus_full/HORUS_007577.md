# Genetic architecture of the response to the watering in yuca, integration of transcriptionic approaches and mapping by association

The Yuca cultivation is the main energy source for about a tenth of the world population and one of the calorie sources of the highest importance in the developing countries of the tropics. Yuca is the main food in the diet of the Colombian Amazonian population. It is used in ritual traditions, trade exchanges and as a key element in the food supply of the region. The Amazonian communities for millennia have cultivated this plant in firm ground chagras and associated with floodplains. The latter have potential to tolerate the flooding and promote food sovereignty. In order to recognize the potential of tolerance to the amazon cultivars and the genetic traits that characterize them, this study aims to evaluate the physiological response of a panel of cultivars of cauca from Colombia enriched in specimens of Amazonian origin. It will be associated its performance in response to the angnization with allelic variants obtained from the genotypic characterization of the entire panel carried out by the research group Manihot biotec. The profile of global expression of the susceptible genes in relation to the potentials will be evaluated in terms of the differential and the differentials
