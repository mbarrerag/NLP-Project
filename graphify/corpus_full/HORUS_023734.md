# NANAENCAPSULATION AS A STRATEGY FOR IMPROVING THE DELIVERY OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS FOR THE TREATMENT OF INDIECTIVE DISEASES: A GENERAL VISION

Chemotherapy control is one of the main ways to combat infectious diseases, however, there are several limitations that put this strategy at risk, among which are: the low number of drugs for clinical use, high costs associated with the way some drugs are administered or their chemical nature, high toxicity, the presence of drug resistant pathogens and serious side effects. As a result, alternative therapies arise as a fundamental requirement to improve the treatment of these pathologies. The new knowledge associated with drug administration strategies has led to the proposed development of new formulations against infectious diseases, especially aimed at optimizing the current drugs for clinical use. In this process of optimization, nanotechnology has enabled different substances to be efficiently encapsulated through the use of nanoparticles, which has led to new drug administration systems that can be potentially proposed as alternative therapies for the treatment of infectious diseases
