# Analysis of the university education of the French Corn at the National University of Colombia and its articulation with the musical field of work in Colombia

The research focused on analysing the training in French corno in the instrumental music programme of the National University of Colombia and its articulation with the country’s musical field. The emphasis teacher was interviewed and the curriculum mesh of the programme was analyzed, determining the skills developed by a cornist during the career and how this position is in the field of work. The same protocol was made with 13 university programmes in the country, with lines of training comparable to that of the UNAL, establishing a broader picture of the musical training in French corn and obtaining a comparative. The most important thing about the process of investigation, was to show that the cornists who graduated from the UNAL instrumental music programme, obtained a solid training with respect to instrumental practice and some pedagogical principles, which are very few, making it clear that they need to seek a more functional integrality to the work context, from the country’s actuality and the nature of the instrument, the spaces in which the Cornista can play at the professional level, are limited to groups of symphonic character, which are very few, making it clear the need to seek a more functional program of labour, from the source and the nature of the career (The first sector). and some pedagogical principalles, which positions they very well in the labor field that covers these areas, however, due to the reality of the country and the nature of the instrument, The spaces in which the horn player can play at a professional level are limited to symphonic groups, which are very scary, making happens the need to ensure a great functional integrality to the work context, from the training in the career
