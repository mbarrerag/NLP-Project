# Strengthening the DLSB maintenance support area for the provision of operational qualification services for laboratory equipment.

This investment project seeks the implementation of a system of Operational Qualification of Equipment, COE, in the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, with a view to dynamizing in the University the consolidation of equipment and laboratories that allow the correct implementation of Good Laboratory Practices, BPL, according to the particular application. This system will be implemented in the existing workshop of maintenance of the Headquarters Laboratory Directorate, making use of the already installed physical and technical infrastructure, the staff that stops knowledge about maintenance of laboratory equipment, and in particular, with knowledge of the specificities of the University, the system of management of applications of already operational maintenance and the procedures of laboratories with which the University has, and in particular those in use in the Department of Laboratories Bogotá, DLSB. This project is proposed in a horizon of execution of eighteen months, and a total horizon of impact and self-sustainability of the tools will be carried out.During the execution period, a diagnosis of the teams of the University that are capable of being operationally qualified will be carried out, at least four types of equipment to be prioritized to be included in the pilot phase of execution of the project, and a horizon of the technical talent and self-s, and will be carried out. The project will be supported by the use of money financing resources, which consists of 75 million pesos, financed by the National Directorate of Research and Laboratories and 60 million pesos, financed as a counterpart to resources from headquarters Bogotá. As part of the project’s activities, sustainability will be based on the implementation of a sustainability plan, including the maintenance and operation of the equipment acquired, and expansion of the capacities installed for COE, and a portfolio of services, with their respective fee table. Completed in the first year and a half of the project, sustainability will be based on the application of a fee table that will allow the consumption to be replenished, maintenance and calibrations to be carried out, validations to be obtained, and the updating and substitution of instruments to be carried out
