# Experimental evaluation of the hydraulic behaviour of a SUDS-type stop tank, built with cellular elements.

The Urban Sustainable Drainage Systems (SUDS) are becoming increasingly necessary in Colombia, due to the increase in people living in cities and the drastic and rapid changes that are generated in nature by accelerated urbanization. Colombian regulations have advanced in the regulation and demand of the SUDS as an important feature for development projects, and these systems are increasingly used in our environment. In this research, the hydraulic evaluation of a type of SUDS known as a detention tank was carried out, which was constructed with cellular elements in the Laboratory of Hydraulic Tests of the National University of Colombia (LEH-UN), Bogotá. The existing methods of dimensioning of arrest tanks with cellular elements, assume that water has a horizontal surface, and this was experimentally evaluated by varying the flow of entry into the tank, to measure the variables of level and output flow. The data measured were compared with the results of a mathematical simulation under condition of leveled pool and by means of statistical analysis the hydraulic performance of the tank was evaluated. This document seeks to provide knowledge about the hydraulic behavior of the detention tanks with cellular elements
