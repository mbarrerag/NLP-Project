# 18 TRANSFORMATIVE ACTIONS TO DESIGN, LIVE IN AND ENJOY CITIES IN HARMONY WITH NATURE BY 2030

BiodiverCities by 2030: Transforming Cities with Biodiversity was developed under Contract No. 21- 017, signed by the administrative department of the presidency of the republic of colombia and the alexander von humboldt biological resources research institute. The latter carries out the conceptual and methodological development and global positioning of the BiodiverCities transformational initiative. BiodiverCities is part of the Public Sector Strategic Management Capacity Strengthening Program, through the line of urban development and Cities System for sustainability, productivity, and quality of life
