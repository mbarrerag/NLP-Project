# DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION AND CO-EXPRESSION OF RNA IN SPLENIC VALUES INFECTED WITH MAREK'S DISEASE VIRUS

Infection with Marek disease virus (MDV) in birds causes neoplastic transformation of lymphocytic cells that infiltrate different tissues and lead to the appearance of tumors and neurological signs. However, not all infected birds develop neoplasms. The objective of this research was to determine the differential expression of mRNA in birds infected with MDV that develop splenic neoplasms and model their coexpression with other RNAs (myRNA and lncRNA). Three transcryptomic profile databases obtained by high-performance sequencing were used available in the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) of the NCBI
