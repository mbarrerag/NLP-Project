# THE LAWSATION OF PUBLIC POLICE: BLURRED BOUNDARIES BETWEEN LAW, POLICS, AND GLOBALIZATION

In the past thirty years, Colombia has witnessed an accelerated process of change in the general conception of law and, in particular, of constitutional law, which has been irradiating various areas of the legal and political fields, such as public policies. In this country, where there is a lack of protection and realization of the social rights established in the Constitution of 1991, the Constitutional Court began to issue a series of judicial decisions that affect the process of formulating public policies by the State authorities
