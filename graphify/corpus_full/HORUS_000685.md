# Bioprocesses in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

Bioprocesses are a branch of biotechnology focused on the use of bioinsumes (cells or their derivatives) and technologies for the production and purification of bioproducts of commercial interest from raw materials. These bioproducts can be classified into commodities, such as probiotics or biofuels, and specialties, such as monoclonal antibodies or gene therapies. On the other hand, scientific and technological advances have boosted the development of artificial intelligence (AI), a disruptive tool that is part of Revolution 4.0 and is transforming multiple sectors, integrating even into daily life. In this context, AI also has the potential to be integrated into bioprocesses to improve key indicators related to bioproducer and raw materials, such as production, productivity, performance and conversion
