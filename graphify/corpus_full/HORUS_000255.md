# Particles of Cu-Co mixed oxide and Ag supported on multibranched α-Fe2O3 as heterogeneous Fenton-like catalysts

New solids with catalytically active surfaces are needed for different environmental processes such as water quality conservation. In this work, two catalysts, Ag/α-Fe 2 O 3 and Cu-Co/α-Fe 2 O 3 , were synthesized from precipitation-deposition methods of metal silver or copper-cobalt oxide particles on a multibranched particulate hematite with fern leaf morphology. The catalytic support was obtained with high fractality and roughness, improving the deposition-fixation of the silver and Cu-Co particles. The solids were characterized by powder-XRD, SEM, TEM, EDX and Auger spectroscopy. The characterizations confirmed both the morphology and structure of the materials. The potentiality of the solids Ag/α-Fe 2 O 3 and Cu-Co/α-Fe 2 O 3 to be used in Fenton-type heterogeneous reactions were evaluated through hydrogen peroxide decomposition in water, at a pH near the neutrality (5.9 – 6.2) and 25 °C, obtaining excellent performance (100 % of H 2 O 2 conversion, with 300 mg/L of catalyst, in 100 min). Additionally, these catalysts were tested in the heterogeneous-Fenton degradation of reactive yellow 145 (RY-145), under mild conditions (25 °C, atmospheric pressure, and pH 6.5). The catalytic results confirmed a higher catalytic performance for Cu-Co/α-Fe 2 O 3 , achieving high degradation levels (80 % of RY-145 conversion and 70.7 % of COD removal in 180 min) with very low values of metal ion leaching (0.60 mg/L of copper ion, and 4.15 mg/L of cobalt ion), when the load of catalyst was 300 mg/L, and 70 mM H 2 O 2 . Under similar conditions, the solid Ag/α-Fe 2 O 3 had lower performance than Cu-Co/α-Fe 2 O 3 . These results show the finding of new surfaces with relevant catalytic properties
