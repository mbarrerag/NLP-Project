# DOMINIUM 3. EXCHANGE ELIMINATION

In the evaluation of the domain of Elimination and exchange we find different systems present in the body that generate a secretion and excretion of waste or waste products; therefore, the detailed exploration of this includes the careful evaluation of the four systems that develop an excretory function such as the urinary, gastrointestinal, tegumentary and respiratory system
