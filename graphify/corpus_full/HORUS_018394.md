# AGROINDUSTRIAL PROCESS IN PIÑA MD2

This document includes the results of the products and processes selected according to the gaps identified for pineapple md2, both fresh and processed, and a comparative analysis of these results is carried out. Technological surveillance was carried out including 4 types of surveillance (competitive, commercial, scientific-technological and strategic). Based on these, the products and processes corresponding to the fruit that was the subject of this study were selected
