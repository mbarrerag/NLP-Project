Biopolymers such as xanthan gum (XG), diutan gum (DG), hydroxyethylcellulose (HEC), carboxymethylcellulose (CMC), schizophyllan (SPG) and scleroglucane (SG), arise as promising candidates for improved recovery (EOR) applications due to their molecular structures.These biopolymers exhibit good reological properties and high resistance to hydrolysis, pH, electrolytes, mechanical stresses and temperature, but are susceptible to oxidation and biological degradation.In this study, fluid-fluid experimental tests were performed to evaluate the effect of the ionic force, pH, temperature and cutting effort on the viscosity of SG solutions (EOR grade).It was observed that the SG preserved its reologic properties and stability in the evaluated conditions due to its semi-rigid structure of triple helix and non-ionic nature.In addition, SG solutions exhibited an excellent filtering.In the experimental tests of the nano-fluid rock and the conditions of the bio-gas, the SG increased the efficiency of the oil displacement and the The SG+Al2O3 nanofluids and SG+TiO2 are highly unstable, but the SG+SiO2 nanofluids are stable independent of the preparation method.
