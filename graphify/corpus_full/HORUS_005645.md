# Designing the design process of a Hybrid Energy Storage System composed of batteries and supercondensers, with application to residential electric microgrids

This paper by Thesis of Doctorate addresses in an integral way the different stages in matter design and implementation of a Hybrid Storage System of Energy Storage (SHAE), composed of batteries of the family of lithium ions and supercondensers, with a potential application for electrical microgrids of residential type.The way to interconnect the elements of storage and the operating characteristics of this type of electrical systems, generate specific conditions for the design, construction and operation of the energy storage systems.While the requirements for the energy storage systems in residential microgrids, regarding large densities of power or energy, are not very high, the percentage change so high in the current or power parameters, when they are connected or disconnected loads, or when there are pulses or intermittances in the local generation, they promote a high electrical stress in the batteries, which leads to a decrease in the life, the analysis of a storage element that handles a high number of cycles of charge and discharge, as well as high power densities, as is the case of the condeners of the top layer or supercondators and the, and operation of energy systems.
