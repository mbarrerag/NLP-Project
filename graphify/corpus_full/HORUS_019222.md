# INTRODUCTION: ON THE STORY OF RELIGION IN COLOMBIA

Until recently, religion was not a topic of conversation in an average Colombian home. It was, yes, an experience, a practice or, in the worst case, a nominal presence, that is, it belonged to religion because it had been baptized in it, but nothing more. It was not a topic of conversation to avoid controversies and discussions that could become heated. In that sense, it was assumed without further questioning, as previously given, a heritage, which should not be questioned. We are talking, essentially, about the Catholic, apostolic and Roman religion
