# PERSPECTIVES OF EDUCATIONAL COOPERATION: BETWEEN COLOMBIA AND BRAZIL BASED ON RESEARCH AND DISSEMINATION RECORDS ON AFRICAN, BLACK, ORIGINAL AND PALENQUEIR COMMUNITIES IN COLOMBIA

PORTUGUESE In this document, issues relating to Afro, black, indigenous and Palenqueira communities in Colombia are analyzed.This study exposes data obtained from a literature review, research records and civil society productions.Furthermore, this research also uses the analysis of records that are intertwined with fragments of approaches carried out by researchers involved in the field of studies of blackness and Afro-Colombianity.In this way, when filtering previously collected data, aiming to identify the work carried out by researchers with a long history in the aforementioned research field, an analytical exercise is carried out motivated by these data.In short, the text identifies the paths that these research and dissemination works have taken, as well as projects (or proposes) the paths that can be taken to increase the visibility of such works within the institutional framework of educational cooperation. SPANISH This document presents an analysis of data derived from a review of literature, records of research and the production of civil society on Afro, Black, Roots and Palestine themes, which combines data analysis intertwined with fragments of planting carried out by some researchers involved in the field of studies on blackness and Afro-colombianity.It is decided that, using a filter of previously collected data to identify work carried out by researchers with a large trajectory in the aforementioned field of investigation, in general;and the afro, black, rooted and palenqueras people, in particular, there is an analytical exercise of themselves, Ultimately, the text identifies the paths that have taken these investigative and dissemination works and projects or proposes the paths that remain to be resorted to its greater visibility in the institutional framework of educational cooperation referred to the populationAfro-descendant between Colombia and Brazil
