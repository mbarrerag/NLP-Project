# Optimum design of mass dampers tuned by differential evolution to reduce dynamic response of structures subject to seismic loads

ABSTRACT This article presents a methodology for the optimal design of Tuned Mass Shock Absorbers (AMS) for the control of dynamic response of buildings subjected to seismic loads. The selection process of optimal design parameters is carried out by a metaheuristic approach based on Differential Evolution (ED) which is a fast, efficient and precise technique that does not require great computational efforts. The algorithm aims to reduce the maximum horizontal peak displacement of the structure and also the quadratic average (effective value) of displacements. Additionally, four other objective functions derived from multiple weighted linear combinations of the two parameters mentioned above are studied to obtain the design configuration of the most efficient 32 floors. In parallel, a process based on a comprehensive search (ES) with precision to 2 decimal positions is used to validate the optimization methodology based on DE. Subsequently, the proposed methodology is applied to a case of study derived from a real building of 32 floors subjected to different seismic soil acceleration records.
