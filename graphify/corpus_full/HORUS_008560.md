# OPTIMASIZATION OF THE STANDARDIZATION OF HANDS AND LAVIES OF THE SURVEY RHIPICEPHALUS MICROPLUS IN THE LABORATORY.

In this work we show the progress in the laboratory of the adaptation of a model to test the potential acaricidal effects of plant extracts. 21 inurgital ticks were used Rhypocephalus microplus, weight 0.270g, (range 0.130g-0.424g) taken from cattle, which were cultivated in the laboratory of reproductive biotechnology and genetic improvement of the National University of Colombia-sede Orinoquia, Arauca, Colombia. The cultivation conditions were 32.9°C (range 25°C-36°C), humidity of 77% (range 71%-98 %), after 3 days the posture of eggs began, 23 days later the eggs began to hatch the larvae. In one case the larvae began to hatch at 7 days. To manipulate the larvae of the laboratory environment, we used the inhalation of aceton for 7 minutes.
