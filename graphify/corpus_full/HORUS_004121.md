# DEVELOPMENT AND TRANSFORMATION OF ELECTORAL SYSTEMS IN LATIN AMERICA, 1900-2024

This paper presents the results of a research project that analyses historically the evolution of electoral systems in 20 countries in Latin America and the United States. In order to overcome the limitations in this area of study, an updated database has been created that tracks the evolution of electoral systems in the region, focusing on the Lower Chambers of each of the countries, which allows comparison between the single- and bicameral systems. In turn, two novel indices are described: the Proportionality Potential and the Personal Voting Potential, which help to understand how the magnitude of the district impacts on proportionality, and on the personalization of the vote, respectively
