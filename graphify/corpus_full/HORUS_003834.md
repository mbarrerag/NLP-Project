# A methodology for identifying the dynamic factorial threshold model

The general objective of this document is to explore a methodology for the identification stage of the number of regimes and the number of factors in a dynamic factorial model of thresholds. To achieve this objective, the sample canonical correlations of the multivariate time series process were used, together with the ideas derived from a nonlinearity test. Thus, indications are shown to identify both the number of factors and the number of thresholds. Finally, the proposed methodology is applied to simulated and real data sets .
