# PHITO-CHEMIC STUDIO AND ACTIVITY ASSESSMENT ANTI-PRESIVE TYPE OF ETANOLIC EXTRACT AND HYPERICUM MEXICANUM L. FRACTIONS, IN MURINO MODEL

The most common mental disorders are depression and anxiety. In 2015, it was estimated that 4.4% and 3.6% of the world population suffered from depressive and/or anxiety disorders, respectively [1]. Pharmacological therapy, psychotherapy or a combination of both are currently the most used treatments for mood disorders. Also, medicinal plants have emerged as an alternative for the pharmacotherapy of various psychiatric disorders.
