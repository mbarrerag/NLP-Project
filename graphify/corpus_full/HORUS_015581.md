# A GENDER-PURSE PROCESS

The article offers a reflectional work that aims to contribute to the generation of knowledge that contribute to the construction of a stable peace, particularly in the gender approach, as an interpretation tool regarding the power relations developed from gender or sex, especially the inequalities that such allocations foster or create in society
