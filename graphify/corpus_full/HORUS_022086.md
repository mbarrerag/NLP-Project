# On Ladyzhenskaya’s Inequality and its Applications

The term Ladyzhenskaya’s inequality refers to any of a class of related functional inequalities that have played important roles in the theory of the Navier–Stokes equations. Here we present their detailed derivation and discuss potential applications beyond Navier–Stokes hydrodynamics
