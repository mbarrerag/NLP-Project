# EVALUATION OF A 3D-PRINTED INSERT WITH ANTIMICROBIAL CAPACITY IN ACTIVE PACKAGING OF FRESH CAPE GOOSEBERRY (PHYSALIS PERUVIANA L.) FRUITS

1. Colombia is one of the largest exporters of fresh fruit in the world. One of the fruits with the most significant potential is the cape gooseberry, given its sensory and organoleptic profile. However, current storage and packaging conditions limit the shelf life of the fruit. The use of conventional packaging increases the moisture inside the system and, therefore, the development of microorganisms such as Botrytis cinerea. This study evaluated the application of an active porous insert saturated with cinnamaldehyde as an antifungal compound for volatile liberation in the packaging headspace combined with modified atmospheres (MAP) to preserve fresh cape gooseberry fruits. 2. Initially, using 3D printing technology porous double-layer polylactic acid (PLA) inserts 2 cm high by 5 cm wide were obtained,which contained 0.3 mL of liquid cinnamaldehyde inside. In the second part of the experiment, 75.0±2.0 g of fresh cape gooseberry without calyx were stored in polylactic acid trays sealed with a PLA film. Two packaging treatments containing an active insert (AE) with cinnamaldehyde and without the active element (C) were evaluated. The packed fruits were stored at 6°C and 55% RH, determining several physicochemical properties during the storage such as color, firmness, total solids content, and titratable acidity; each test was performed in triplicate. Additionally, the weight loss, deterioration index, and general appearance of the packaged fruits were determined. 3. The use of an antimicrobial active packaging with an active insert with an antimicrobial compound and the modified atmosphere conserves the general appearance and reduces the deterioration index when compared with the control fruits (simple PLA sealed trays). Likewise, in the sealed PLA trays with the inserts, it was possible to the weight loss of the fruit, reaching a weight loss of 3.5±1.1% for the fruits with the AE treatment versus 5.3±2.0% for the control fruits. In addition, it was possible to obtain a shelf life of 39 days for the fruits packaged with the active element and 30 days for the control fruits. 4. The use of an active antimicrobial element including cinnamaldehyde as a volatile releasing antifungal agent and combined modified atmospheres allows to maintain the quality of bare cape gooseberry fruits without calyx and increase the shelf life of the product, which can contribute to the safety of the product and the commercialization of this fruit and other similar fruits
