# RESEARCH AND EARNING.

Regarding the 15 years of the Prize for Research and Educational Innovation, awarded by the city of Bogotá to teachers, teachers and directors of the public sector, with the management of the Ministry of Education of the District ¿SED¿ and the Institute for Educational Research and Educational Development ¿IDEP¿, this book presents passages and gives the voice from its writings to a good number of participants in the Prize. The assessment on some of the pedagogical trends and recurrent themes in innovation and pedagogical research of teachers and managers of the city constitutes in itself a recognition of the efforts not only in the scattered work of writing the academic article with which it is participated but above all in the transformative activities that teachers and the support of families develop each day. Beyond having received the awards this book is a tribute to the teachers, teachers and managers participating in the calls for the Prize from 2007 to 2021
