# KNOWLEDGE, ACTIVITY AND PRACTICES IN PATIENTS WITH TUBERCLESIS: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA

Tuberculosis is an infectious contagion disease of easy transmission. For its control, the knowledge, attitudes and good practices are indispensable, as well as the factors relevant to adherence to treatment. To characterize the definitions or concepts of the dimensions of knowledge, attitudes and practices in tuberculosis reported in the literature during the last ten years. A systematic review of the literature was carried out under the methodology Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses. Initially, 1,720 possible publications were found, of which, after the application of the exclusion criteria, 34 were left to be included in the analysis of this review. Most of the studies were published in 2018 and publications predominated in the African continent. The characterization of the dimensions is related thus: the knowledge is defined as characteristics of the disease, the attitudes are related to the social stigma towards the patient and the practices correspond to the patient's search for medical care.
