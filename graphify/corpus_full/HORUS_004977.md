# CHARACTERISTICS AND PARTICULARITIES FOR HEALTH CARE

This book aims to provide a general overview of old age and aging. Ageing is an inevitable process through which we go through most living beings. Knowing this stage will allow us to make decisions in the present that will impact on how we face old age and that of the other. The reader will find some basic concepts of gerontology in these pages; the morphophysiological changes of each system of the human body; the assessment of the elderly from the physical, biological and social, through different scales for the assessment, with the purpose of establishing a situational diagnosis and providing guidelines for care in each case; and the experience in teaching gerontology in nursing is shared; a panorama of the Colombian policy 2015-2024 on human aging is offered, as a response by the State to solve some problems affecting this population. Finally, the panorama of the COVID-19 pandemic is shown as a public health problem, where the elderly population is the most vulnerable group, which required priority and specific action to reduce the risks of infection and, in this way, does not compromise the health and life condition of this population
