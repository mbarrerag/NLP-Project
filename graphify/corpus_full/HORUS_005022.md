# VALUE CAPTURE OF SOUND DURING DEVELOPMENT ORIENTED TO TRANSPORT.

This article presents the results of an investigation on how to enhance and optimize the "capture of value derived from land rent" through urban interventions in the real estate market based on Transport-oriented Development (DOT), taking as a case study the Subterranean Project of the First Line of the Bogota Metro 2014 (PSPLMB), structured for many decades, but without having been able to complete its construction. Taking into account this, the document develops an ex ante analysis methodology based on the fact that it is possible to enhance the use of the urban soil gains generated when urban public transport systems are implemented and operate in the areas of influence of these systems. Likewise, the potential of the capital gains can be greater, when the urban development of these zones of influence is conceived ex ante and in harmonization with the development of the transport project, and when the urban proposal of the transformations of the surrounding areas is made from the DOT approach
