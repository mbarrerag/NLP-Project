# Determination of the form of administration and level of protection of a vaccine supplied via food for the prevention and control of Newcastle disease virus. (In vivo phase 2)

Newcastle disease is a viral pathology endemic in Colombia that causes great economic losses to the national poultry. Due to the high pathogenicity with great mortality that causes is a mandatory report to the world animal health organization (OIE) and the Colombian Agropecuario Institute (ICA). Historically the outbreaks occur mainly in productive systems of traspatio or small scale, which although they are abundant in the territory, account for a low proportion of birds in relation to technical systems. These birds typically remain in poor biosecurity conditions where they can act as reservoirs, amplifiers and spreaders of the virus being a risk to the national industry, so there is a need to expand the vaccine coverage in the non-technified systems and thus strengthen the national programme of control and eradication of the disease, favouring the development of the poultry sector in small-scale systems as a strategy of prevention and control of the country. The objective of the project is to continue with the viability of the infecting of a live vaccine of the Newcastle disease in food for the immunization of the poultry populations in the country. 14 and 21 days post-vaccination (dpv) and 14 days post-challenge (dpd). To determine excretion, RT-qPCR and quantification shall be performed following a previously standardized absolute quantification curve, samples of tracheal and cloacale hysopates taken prior to vaccination, 4 and 21 dpv, and 2 and 4 dpd. The determination of injury indices in immune organs by histopathology of samples taken at 21 dpv and 2 and 4 dpd shall be made. Non-parametric statistical tests shall be used for the viral load analysis of variance and for the histopathological injury indices parametric statistical methods, with the aim of establishing significant differences between groups. This phase seeks to corroborate the results obtained in previous projects; it is also intended to make a contribution to the plan for the control and eradication of Newcastle disease in the country, by strengthening vaccination strategies in traspatium birds
