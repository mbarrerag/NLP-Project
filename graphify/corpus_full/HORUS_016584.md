# The butterflies on the colombian Orinoquia

Colombia, with 3642 species of butterflies, is one of the countries that expresses the most richness of these organisms worldwide, however, in regions with a high rate of landscape transformation and habitat fragmentation such as Orinoquia, the study of these insects has focused on local inventories. This work provides a first list of butterfly species in the Colombian Orinoquia for the families: Hesperiidae, Papilionidae, Pieridae, Nymphalidae, Riodinidae and Lycaenidae. In order to visualize the Orinoquia as a region beyond the plains and to avoid overestimation caused by the inclusion of Andean taxa, the bioregion was divided into five subregions. From this, the material deposited in six collections, associated literature and field trips were reviewed. 7828 records corresponding to 1115 species were obtained from 1941 to 2018, hosting 30.61% of the estimated wealth for the country and close to that given in regions such as the Amazon with 1088 species and the Chocó-Darién with 970. Nymphalidae is the richest family with 389 species and the Piedmont is the subregion with the most species with 829. This work visualizes the Orinoquía as a diverse region in butterflies and is an important contribution to the knowledge of the richness of these insects in Colombia for the creation of conservation strategies and management of their knowledge in this region
