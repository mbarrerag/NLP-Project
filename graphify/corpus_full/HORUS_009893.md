# Evaluation of spectral data analysis tools for the identification and quantification of early maturity in potato.

The potato cultivation is affected by different diseases that decrease its yield, among them, the problems associated with early maturity (MT) caused by Verticillium spp. have gained importance in Colombia. The lack of management strategies and especially tools of diagnosis and early detection has generated the need to identify techniques of indirect detection with potential application at commercial level. Therefore, this work has the objective of evaluating tools of analysis of spectral data for the identification and quantification of MT associated with Verticillium spp. in potato crops. The work is divided into two phases in order to characterize at spectral level healthy and sick plants, besides making an approximation to the indirect quantification of different levels of severity of the disease.In the first place, we compared spectral signatures acquired by means of a fixed radiometer spectrum under controlled conditions in order to identify bands and indexes of the spectral and Aboos contrasting by their ability for detection and indirect quantification of this pathology.
