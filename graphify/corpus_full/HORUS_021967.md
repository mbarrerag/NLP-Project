# Study of the lectines present in the poison of the scorpion Tityus macrochirus.

Lectins are non-immun immune-origin, which recognize carbohydrates with different affinities, therefore, they have a wide spectrum of study and application, in different fields such as the physician, for example, in antiretroviral and antitumor therapies. At the same time, they are of great importance in the study of formation of lectin–carbohydrate complexes, as well as the interactions protein carbohydrate, which lead to different biological responses. They are found in all organisms from virus to humans, although they have been studied very well in animals and higher plants. Their high distribution in different kingdoms shows their importance in the cellular response, metamorphosis, differentiation, muta, among others. In the case of lectins in poisons, the physiological functions of the lectin are not yet established, however, there is evidence of their importance in the immune response, the metamorphosis, the differentiation, the plant–simion, among others.
