# IDENTIFICATION OF A BEST MATHEMATIC MODEL ADJUSTED TO THE LACTANCIA CULTURE OF LUCERNA VACAS FOR Several PART ORDERS*

The nursing curve (CL) is a trait that can be estimated through periodic records that allows to analyze the productive behavior of the milk system, establish appropriate management methodologies and know other characteristics of interest to the producers. Little information has been reported in the Colombian breeds Creoles for this milk trait. To identify the best nonlinear mathematical model that describes the CL of cows of the breed Lucerna in several birth orders. The information of milk production in the control day (PLDC) of two shed Lucerna de Colombia, for births (NP) 1 to 8. The PLDCs were eliminated that were outside the biological range for the breed, subtracting 142006 PLDC belonging to 4206 cows. The mathematical models used to describe the CL were: Brody, Cappio-Borlino, incomplete gamma function, Papajcsik - Bordero, Wiltmink. The model with best description of the CLs in the different NPs of agreement with lowers CLIC and BICs and higher percentages of convergence (PC) and R2.
