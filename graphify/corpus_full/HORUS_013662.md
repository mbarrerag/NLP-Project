# Urban quality, based on the counterproposal of commercial redistribution for the Madelena sector and the Dream in Bogotá D.C.

This document is a theoretical-normative reflection, which provides a conclusion on how new urban developments could be, specifically focused on large developments of a real estate nature; the above thanks to an analysis of the physical, spatial and social aspects. Conceptually, the strategies are formed around conditions that relate directly to human life and one of these is housing, the basis for the rest of its functions in the city.
