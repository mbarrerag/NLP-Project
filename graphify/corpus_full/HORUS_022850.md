# SPACE ANALYSIS OF THE PENOTIFIC VARIABILITY OF SEMILS AND MEXICAN TEJOCOT ENDOCARPIES (CRATAEGUS SP.) AND ASSOCIATION WITH THEIR ADAPTATIVE VARIABILITY

The study of the diversity of the tejocote in Mexico has been carried out since the end of the last century, based on characteristics of reproductive and vegetative structures. Its characteristics of endocarpium and seeds have been used in the characterization of germplasm with different purposes, such as taxonomy and diversity, since they show high variability and also have low phenotypic plasticity. On the other hand, the ecogeographic characterization of collection sites may be an alternative or complementary to genetic or phenotypic characterization, due to the relationships between genetic or morphological characteristics and environmental conditions of the sites where plants are developed. Diversity maps allow easy and rapid identification of areas or regions where high variability is concentrated, becoming a tool for decision-making in conservation matters. Space information combined with characterization and evaluation data has also been useful in defining core collections, identifying gaps in collections, designing collection strategies and determining representativeness in ex situ collections. Spatial analysis of diversity can significantly improve their understanding and monitoring, since the results obtained can provide important information of diversity present in certain geographical areas and use to evaluate conservation
