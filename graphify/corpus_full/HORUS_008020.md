# IDENTITY AND SUSTAINABILITY OF COMMUNITY CRIULL AND NATIVE SEMILL CASES

The community seed houses (CCS) represent a collective alternative of conservation, organization and territorial governance of the local plant genetic resources, especially of native and criolla seeds. The consolidation of a CCS is determined by technical and organizational elements that allow to establish necessary steps for its establishment and functioning.The purpose of this research was to identify the main steps in the process of establishing two CCS in two municipalities, Albán and Sasaima, of the department of Cundinamarca, Colombia.For this purpose, the methodology of Research and Participative Action (IAP), particularly applied in a diagnosis to evaluate the index of agrobiodiversity that is part of the index of diversity of the agroecosystem (IDA) was used.Then, the construction of a network of innovation for each CCS, identifying the actors, interactions, roles and functions of the members. Finally, the collective definition of the identity of each CCS and the establishment of the type of agriculture was realized
