# WITH INSURANCE PROCESS: JAQUE MATE AL COVID-19

ABSTRACT Patient safety is a pillar in health care. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), there is an estimated 1 to 300 chance of harm during hospital care, so it is considered as a public health problem at present[1], in addition to its far-reaching impact on morbidity and mortality of different diseases. The objectives are achieved through prevention, risk assessment, events and injuries, within a systemic approach to managing those risks[2]. The current pandemic by COVID-19 represents a challenge in this area, being a battle difficult to combat for the multiple interactions and repercussions on the individual and the community that it entails; for this reason, it is of utmost importance to identify the factors involved, their role and the interconnection of these. In summary, the proposed strategy to address this crisis must be based on the safety and joint actions of the different sectors of society.
