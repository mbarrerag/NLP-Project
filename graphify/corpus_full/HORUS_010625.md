# VARIATION OF THE CAPTURE BY EFFORCE UNIT (EUCP) OF THE ARTESANAL FISHING AND THEIR SUBSTANTIVENESS IN RELATION TO THE ENVIRONMENTAL VARIABLES IN THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC

Contextualization: artisanal fishing in the Colombian Pacific is positioned as one of the main sources of income of the communities settled on the coasts. There is a strong pressure on the fishing resources, with little selective fishing gears such as trammel and the line of hook of hand, capturing individuals that have not presented reproductive events and belonging to incidental and discarding catches. Research vacuum: in the Colombian Pacific, the spatial-temporal relations between the objective and discarding catches according to the environmental variables have not been explored. Purpose of the study: To evaluate the spatial-temporal dynamics of the capture by unit of effort and the sustainability of the artisanal fishing associated with the environmental variables in the bay of Tumaco, Colombian Pacific. four samplings were carried out throughout the year 2020 and 2021, two in the dry season and two in the rainy season with the trasmallo and line of the hand hook, in the internal and external areas, was also carried out
