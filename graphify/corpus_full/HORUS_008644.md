# ANTIFUNIC ACTIVITY ASSESSMENT OF MICROORGANISMS ASSISTANT FOR FUSARIUM OXYSPORUMF. SP. PASSIFLORAE

Fusariois, is a disease in the cultivation of commercial species of the genus Passiflora caused by the phytopathogenic fungus Fusarium oxysporum f.sp passiflorae, which causes a severe limitation in the production of fruits and a reduction in the longevity of the crop1,2. As a control method synthetic fungicides are used that are toxic and generate resistance in the fungus, therefore it is necessary to find new alternatives for its control 3. Taking into account this, the present research aims to find biological agents that allow control of F. oxysporum f.sp passiflorae from the confrontation between this pathogen with endophytes fungi, macromynetos and bacteria To this, dual confrontations were carried out in PDA (Agar of Papa Dextrose) with the strains of endòphytes (UN67, 86, 82, 314, 94, 123, 73 and 70) and the strains of macromicets fungi (QHM1-10) and bacteria of marine origin (PNM216, 102, 9, 200, 172, 208, 123b during the period, 201, 210 and 115 in the period, strengthening the production chain of passionflowers and avocados in Arauca phase I: biological control and chemical characterization of the National Research and Laboratory Directorate and the Faculty of Science and Engineering of the National University of Colombia in alliance with the company Aitia Biotech SAS
