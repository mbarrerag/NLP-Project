# Voices and feelings in community water management: Collaborative experiences in three areas of the San Antonio de Prado district, Medellín.

The research project, voices and feelings in community water management: Collaborative experiences in three areas of the San Antonio de Prado district, Medellín, proposes to review the relations that are presented in rural areas around the community water management and from this process to understand the socio-environmental conflicts that are presented in the territories. It proposes a documentary exploration that shows the situation of the management of the water bodies of the San Antonio de Prado district as well as in the municipality of Medellín, and also hopes through stories, narratives and local experiences to recognize the realities of three communities in the Vereda San José, Vereda La Florida and Barrio Palo Blanco that are present, in front of the care, conservation and techniques of management of the common good -water. It aims to collect the feelings of the inhabitants and from there, to trace possible routes to address the problem of the hydric sources in rural peasant environments, especially in contexts of climate vulnerability and postpandemia by COVID 19. Dialogic research and collaborative strategies as a theoretical approach will allow to take the process to other rural territories of the municipality of Medellín and strengthen the presence of the semi-drous research Chagra Territory in La Vida de la Alianza
