# Knowledge of the nursing team in the prevention of pressure injuries in a university hospital

Pressure lesions (LPP) are constituted as an adverse event related to nursing care, which is preventable in 95% of cases if effective strategies are established for its prevention. These strategies are well documented in scientific literature, however, the problem persists in hospital centers. Determine the level of knowledge of the nursing team on the prevention of pressure injuries in a university hospital. Descriptive study with a quantitative cross-sectional approach. The questionnaire on knowledge in prevention of pressure injuries CPUPP-31 was applied. The analysis was performed with the statistical package SPSS24. The sociodemographic characterization of the sample was performed to determine the level of knowledge and identification of care provided in prevention of pressure injuries in the nursing team. The overall level of knowledge was 80.6% with a statistically significant difference of p=0.001, being representative the level of knowledge of nursing professionals.
