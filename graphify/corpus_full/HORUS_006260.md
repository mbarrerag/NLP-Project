# Metric thinking and its significance in the development of processes associated with statistical thinking in students of secondary school

This degree work has its main objective to contribute to improve the metric thinking skills of seven grades of the Liceo Arquidiocesano de Nuestra Señora of the city of Manizales. The methodology used is qualitative research of descriptive scope, using the design methodology. The development of this involvement three phases: diagnosis, intervention, and evaluation. A didactic analysis is implemented based on the development of processes associated with metric thinking as the collection of measurable phenomena (classification of phenomena, identification and relation of magnitudes) construction and optimal use of measuring instruments (Recognition, selection and construction of the meassurable phenomena) the reflection of measurement and evaluation (status of measurement) processes associated with metric thinking such as recognition of measurable phenomena, construction and optimal use of measuring instruments, refinement of measurement processes and recognition of the role of measurement in social decisions. Within the expected results, a significant improvement in the processes of that thought and in the transcendence in strengthening other processes associated with statistical thinking, development of critical thinking and a positive impact on social decision-making is anticipated
