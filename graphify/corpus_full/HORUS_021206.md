# APPLICATION OF A CAROTENOID EXTRACT FROM THE CHONTADURO EPICARPY (BACTRIS GASIPAE) AS ANTIOXIDANT AGENT IN A MARGARINE

Different studies report the negative effects of synthetic antioxidants on the health of the consumer, a situation that requires to advance research that allows to explore the use of antioxidants of natural origin. An alternative of natural origin can be fruit residues, since this biological resource according to previous studies reports important values of bioactive compounds with antioxidant properties. Therefore, the aim of the proposal is to use a carotenoids extract from chontaduro epicarp as antioxidant in a margarine. In this research will be used a green extraction technique when combining extraction assisted by homogenization and a renewable solvent like sunflower oil to obtain carotenoids extracts from study samples, a central composite rotational design combined with response surface methodology will evaluate the extraction of carotenoids compounds in the study matrix.
