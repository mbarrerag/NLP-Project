# CONCENTRATION OF MONACITARY TO RARAS LAND ELEMENTS (REE) FROM THE VALUATION OF ALUVIAL RESULTS: REDUCTION OF ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS BY EVALUATION OF LIFE CYCLE UNDER CIRCULAR ECONOMY APPROACH.

The assessment of the life cycle (LCA) has been accepted by the European waste policy as a useful tool to measure the impact of products and services on the environment and reduce it by implementing the circular economy. Results on the implementation of the life cycle analysis in alluvial gold mining to assess how sustainable this extraction technique is. Alluvial mining is the extractive method that has the lowest environmental impacts compared to open and underground mining [18]. However, the alluvial technique is less conventional because it requires more specific geomorphological characteristics; deposits of the flood plain. The residual tails of the alluvial mining extraction, present a high potential of added economic value. Research carried out in the region of the Bagre-Nechi mining district in Colombia, indicate that this alluvial gold deposit contains mineral resources (monact) and supercandium, with potential to be used for obtaining REE in the rewind mining operations.
