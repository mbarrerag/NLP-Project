# Purpose of the Mine Action Development and Innovation Centre

The importance of innovation systems for the development of nations, regions, sectors, technologies, among others, has been recognized. In order for these systems to exist, it is necessary for the actors that make up them to relate to the purpose of generating, disseminating and using knowledge and technology; however, this relationship does not always occur in a natural or simple way, especially because the different actors called to shape them (universities, companies, government agencies, technological development centers, research centers, incubators of technology-based companies, among others) have generated remarkably different organizational routines, creating gaps of different dimensions (cognitive, communicative, organizational, functional, cultural, among others) that hinder links and generate high transaction costs due to the mistrust caused by this distance
