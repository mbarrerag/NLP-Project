# ASSISTANCE OF THE CEREBRO-INTESTINE INTERACTION (DIGESTIAL FUNCTIONAL TESTORS), RATIONALITY FOR THE USE OF NEUROMODULATION

In the broad spectrum of therapeutic options for the management of digestive functional disorders, which have been redefined by the Rome Foundation in the Rome IV criteria as disorders of brain-intestinal interaction (ITCI), some drugs with antidepressant, anxiolytic or antipsychotic effects are used more frequently. These drugs, which act in neurogastroenterological dysfunction of these disorders, have also been renamed by the Rome Foundation as neuromodulators, so that they can now take advantage of their therapeutic benefits in this clinical field, due to their use as psychiatric medicines. This narrative review aims to update and clarify the indications of neuromodulators in the main ITICs, and in this first section it addresses rationality and justification for their use
