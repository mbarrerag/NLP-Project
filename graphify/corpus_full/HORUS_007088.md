# Feasibility study of the implementation of trains composed by SUDS in residential VIS assemblies in the city of Bogotá

The present final master's project addresses the technical and economic feasibility of implementing Urban Sustainable Drenage Systems (SUDS) in social-interest housing in Bogotá, Colombia, with a specific focus on the residential complex VIZCAYA. It highlights the importance of the SUDS in the management of urban water resources, entering into a methodology that includes preliminary analysis, identification and design of SUDS, hydraulic modelling and economic evaluation. The hydrological analysis of the study area reveals that the annual average rainfall is 752 mm, a value that poses hydrological challenges for the use of rain water due to the low volumes generated from runoff. Despite this, it was possible to replace approximately 30% of the total consumption of drinking water in the sanitary areas, thanks to a SUDS train that includes a storm tank, a treatment system (anthracite filters and sand, together with a tablet chlorinator), and a storage tank. It highlights the need to modify the regulations to promote the recirculation of rain water and develop effective methodologies that ensure the technical and financial viability of the SUDS in horizontal property projects.
