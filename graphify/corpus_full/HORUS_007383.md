# Key factors for Business Intelligence & Analytics (BI&A) maturity in the public sector for strategic decision-making processes

This research examines the key factors that influence the maturity of Business Intelligence and Analytics (BI&A) in the public sector, especially in terms of strategic decision-making processes. Despite the recognized importance of the BI&A in improving organizational performance, there remains a significant gap in academic literature, particularly in the contexts of the public sector. This study employs a theoretical framework that highlights three stages of the development of the BI&A: Initial Foundations, Development of Key Concepts and Modern Approaches. Using an interpretative paradigm, research adopts a qualitative methodology, focused on exploratory case studies of two public hospitals in Canada and Colombia that have implemented initiatives of BI&A. Data collection involved in-depth interviews with administrators and users, providing a comprehensive understanding of the maturity factors of the BI&A through four-dimensionality: data, people, processes and technology. Key findings highlight the importance of effective dissemination of information and data integration, advocating for a culture of decision-making based on data, supported by the leadership to reach operational levels.
