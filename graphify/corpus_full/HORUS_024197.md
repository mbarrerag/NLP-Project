# PROPOSALS AND STRATEGIES FOR CAMPESIN FAMILY AGRICULTURE IN A TERRITORIAL PEACE SCENE.

institutional level, the most ambitious proposals and with operational strategies to respond to the enormous inequality and gap in the field are: i) the 1st item of the Havana Agreement signed by the State and the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (farc), and ii) the document entitled El Campo Colombiana: a path to well-being and peace, elaborated by the Rural Mission of the National Planning Department (dnp). Both documents recognize the crisis that the rural world is facing and that if it is really to achieve peace, the enormous gaps that exist must be closed. They propose the importance of including local actors in the plans and proposals, recognizing and giving centrality to the farmers, peasants, traditional ethnic, Afro and Raizal organizations, due to the fact that they have been invisible at the time of implementing rural public policies inclusive in socio-economic, productive and eco-economic aspects. However, despite the lack of technical support and infrastructure, this type of agriculture called peasant or family agriculture provides more than 43% of the food products of the basic basket of the main urban centers of the country, due to its enormous agricultural and rural development (the fact that it is considered to be a productive sector). The present chapter aims to: (i) analyze the rural problem in Colombia from the perspective of peasant family agriculture and (ii) propose guidelines and operational strategies that encourage the promotion and support of peasant family agriculture to reduce the gap and inequality for these peasants; (iii) show different styles of ownership of the territory by the peasantry; (iii) first, the text sets out the problematic situation in which the forms of family farming in the country are being carried out; and, second, different routes, strategies and proposals are being proposed by social and peasant organizations to dynamize from the peasant styles of agriculture
