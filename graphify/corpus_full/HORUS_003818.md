# HALOPERIDOL AS PROFILAXIS FOR NAUSES AND POSTORY VOMITE: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

The effectiveness of haloperidol in postoperative nausea and vomiting prophylaxis (NVPO) has been demonstrated in previous studies summarized in 2004 by Buttner. Since then, new evidence has emerged, which is why our objective is to update the present state of knowledge in this topic. A systematic review and metaanalysis was carried out in order to approximate the effectiveness and safety of haloperidol use in NVPO prophylaxis. The systematic search, the selection of relevant articles, the extraction of data, the critical analysis of primary studies, comparisons and analyses were carried out based on the recommendations of Cochrane Collaboration and through the software RevMan5. Ten controlled clinical experiments, published between 1962 and 2010, which include 2,711 patients, meet the selection criteria. Compared with the Dropridol when compared with the RR: 0.97; 95% CI: 0.52-1,79) and with the ondansetron (RR: 1.24; 95% CI: 0.66-2,35), no differences were found in the effectiveness of the 24 h.
