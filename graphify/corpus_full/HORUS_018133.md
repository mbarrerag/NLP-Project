# LEARNING AND TeachING IN ARCHITECTURE

This text aims to reflect on the way in which architecture is taught and learned, starting from considerations such as what is and what is the profession of the architect, what are the basic tools with which it should count, what is the specifics of the profession, and above all tries to make think about a way, among several possible ones, to learn architecture: to know it through ideation and implementation, to bring the intangible universe of ideas to the concrete world of its materialization through construction, always seeking to respond to social purposes
