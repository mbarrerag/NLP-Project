# Analytical solution for soil interaction structure of a non-uniform sectional pile under non-homogenous soil conditions.

Non-prismatic batteries are often used to withstand large lateral loads. In many of these applications such elements are implemented in partially or fully embedded stratified soils to improve the load transfer to the ground with a more efficient resistance distribution, due to the increase of the cross section at the top of the element. However, they require a more complex and comprehensive analysis and design than uniform elements. This research presents the derivation of the rigidity matrix and the load vector of an analytical solution for non uniform section piles totally and partially embedded in a non-homogenous soil. The methodology presented allows, i) static and stability analysis of circular non-prismatic batteries (i.e., conical batteries, staggered batteries, etc.), ii) consider the variation of the ground in depth with linear and trapezoidal distributions of the soil reaction module, iii) evaluate fully and partially embedded batteries in multi-layer soils, iv) consider partial and fully restricted connections, v) consider a Pasternak foundation model
