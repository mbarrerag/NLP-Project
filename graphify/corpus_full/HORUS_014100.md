# Creation Critical Thinking Center of Politics, Environment and Society.

The Center for Critical Thinking of Policy, Environment, and Society is created in order to contribute to real solutions of public problems, community challenges, and territorial challenges, that improve the quality of life and the capacities of transformation of various social actors, through the implementation of processes of theoretical, methodological, and technical innovation, that depart from the collaborative knowledge of interdisciplinary and community groups.For this, it proposes four lines of action: a) Public policies, participation, democracy and citizenship b) Ordination of the territory and environmental sustainability c) Historical memory and construction of territorial peace d) Social, solidarity and popular economies.In the framework of this call, and as a starting point for its creation, the center of critical thinking will address the limitations of governance and participation for climate action; problem that is directly related to the first two lines of action.The models of governance, especially those that emerge from the local to the local as well as the climate, face great difficulties in terms of their operation and their articulation at the local level and end generating problems such as the disarticulation of the multiple instruments of planning and public management, and the low or zero recognition of strategies and the grassroots models. Beautiful- The center of critical thinking is framed in understanding and analyzing the challenges around its four lines of action, as well as the nodes and meeting places promoting a collaborative approach that involves academia, authorities, civil society and other relevant actors, so that the interaction research, politics, and society is consolidated as the fundamental axis of its work. It also seeks to address elements of territorial geography to understand processes such as the risk to climate change; and elements of territorial policy to analyze conflicts regarding access and community use of resources, and the management around water, that allow understanding how local and institutional climate governance networks are configured. In relation specifically to climate governance in Medellín, it aims to develop and promote processes of incidence, participation, and evaluation, to overcome identified limitations, articulate local strategies with the administration, and have instruments for continuous monitoring.
