# STRATEGIES FOR THE PREVENTION OF ERRORS IN THE ADMINISTRATION OF INTRAVENOUS MEDICINAL PRODUCTS

The medicines that are mismanaged can produce adverse effects, which can cause patient affectations and additional costs in institutions. The four most common errors in intravenous drug administration are incorrect doses of the medicine, incorrect volume, incorrect mixtures and pharmacological incompatibility. The level of pharmacological knowledge in nursing professional is of particular importance. to analyze the literature available on strategies used to prevent the occurrence of these errors in intravenous drug administration by nursing staff from 2010 to 2019. an integrative review (RI) was carried out to answer the question: what is the evidence available on strategies for the prevention of errors in intravenous drug administration? Databases were used among those found OVID, ScienceDirect, SciELO, Medline Complete, Academic Search Complete, PubMed and Guidelines of Clinical Practice, with time limit between 2010 and 2019
