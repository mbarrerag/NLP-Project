# THE PRESENCE OF THE LABRILL IN THE COLONIAL ARCHITECTURE, CASE OF INDICIAN PEOPLE'S CHURCH OF THE SIGNATURE OF THE XVIIth CENTURY

The ceramic brick appears recorded in Colombian history from the moment of contact between the two cultures on the arrival of the Spaniards to the New Kingdom of Granada, forming an important part of the growth of the new society. Its introduction in architecture generated the development of new ways of doing expanding the constructive and structural possibilities, which resulted in various techniques that it is still possible to identify clearly in the colonial buildings of the Altiplano Cundiboyacense. The work presented addresses the results of research on the churches of the Indian peoples in the seventeenth century, in which there is a wide variety of architectural uses of the brick with full awareness of the advantages offered by this material in the colonial period over other previous materials or in combination with these. From the historical archives and enabling as documentary source the building itself, the different techniques used according to the requirements of the performance of each case were defined, as well as the evolution in the approach of the architecture that used the brick as structural material.
