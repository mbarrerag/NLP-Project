# MOLECULAR DETECTION OF CUCUMBER MOSAIC VIRUS AND PEPPER SEVERE MOTTLE VIRUS IN ARVENSES ASSOCIATED TO THE CULTIVE OF AJI (CAPSICUM SPP.).

In recent years, the ají (Capsicum chinense, Capsicum frutescens and Capsicum annuum var. Acuminatum), cultivated in the Valle del Cauca, has been affected by viral diseases caused by Cucumber mosaic virus (CMV-ají) and Pepper severe mottle virus (PepSMoV). Although these two viruses are limiting for production of the ají culture, at present, few studies have identified the alternate hosts of CMV-ají and PepSMoV. In this work, the presence of CMV-ají and PepSMoV was evaluated, through RT-PCR, in foliar tissue samples, of 121 arvens plants, associated with the cultivation of ají, in the Valle del Cauca, Colombia. The molecular analysis indicated the presence of CMV-ají, in 21.4 % of the plants collected and PepSMoV, in 20.6 %. aimed at preventing viral infections in the ají crops
