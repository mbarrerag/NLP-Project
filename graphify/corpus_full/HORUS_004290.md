# ■ The Commission will be able to provide information on the implementation of the measures to be taken to ensure that the measures taken by the Member States are in place.

This chapter aims to identify the challenges and synergies existing within the agri-food systems, analyzing some public policies related to food purchases and acquisitions, which promote food and nutrition sovereignty and security in Brazil, with the aim of identifying paths, challenges and potentialities for the moment of political conjuncture that Colombia is going through. The results point out that, first, although the trajectories of both countries are different, there are factors that affect food through conflicts underlying the areas of dispute that, in the Brazilian case, led to recent setbacks and in the Colombian case have made reforms impossible. Second, to position the issue of sustainable agro-food systems on the public agenda of both countries and to recognize the positive effects of synergies of inter-institutional actions is urgent. Third, to show the paths taken in the Brazilian case allows identifying the potentials, as well as to propose priorities that must be developed urgently to combat hunger, socio-economic inequality and contribute to social justice and total peace in Colombia
