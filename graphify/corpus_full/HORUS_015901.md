# CLIMATE VULNERABILITY INDEXS: A LITERATURA REVIEW

Climate change is a global phenomenon that has aroused growing concern among the scientific community and decision makers about its associated impacts. Therefore, climate vulnerability assessment becomes one of the main topics of study of global environmental change. Several authors have been responsible for generating indices for the assessment of climate vulnerability of different systems. Vulnerability can be understood as the propensity or predisposition of human beings to be harmed to any extent, as a result of some dangerous event, depending on their profile and environment. This study systematically reviewed the indicators, threats, components and concepts most used for the construction, development and application of climate vulnerability indices
