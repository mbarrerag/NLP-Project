# Rehabitation catalogue: Obsolescence of housing in Bogotá's seventh race

The present Final Work of Mastership (TFM) addresses the problem of functional obsolescence from a methodological instrument, dynamic and adaptable and called "Catalogue of Rehabitation". The proposed methodology is formed by three phases. First, there are the background that evidence and delimit the problemmatic, accompanied by theoretical and projectual support, which cause the approach of the TFM from the discussion of these. Second, two fundamental instruments are consolidated in the development of research and project implementation as they are: the Multiscale Diagnosis, with the aim of analyzing qualitative and quantitative values of the study cases and the Catalogue of Rehabitation, in which a multi-scale systematization of current categories, types and levels of intervention is carried out, allowing the refunctionalization of the cases of study in part or progressive way. And finally, the projectual implementation evidences one of the possible applications of the catalog to refunctionalize the three scales (urban, grouping and modalization).
