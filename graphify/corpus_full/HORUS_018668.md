# USE OF NATURAL EUTECTIC SOLVENTS AS ALTERNATIVE FOR EXTRACTION OF BIOACTIVE COMPOSITIONS IN AGRO-INDUSTRIAL WATER-HASS WASTES

Avocado is a fruit that has taken on a lot of relevance at national level in recent years, especially the Hass variety. Its use has been mainly linked to fresh consumption, although recently products with higher added value have been developed from its pulp. As a result, it is estimated that the generation of residues, mainly by epicarpium and seed, will increase in the coming years. Recently, these composite residues have been found with important biological properties, which has increased interest in them. However, most of the research that have analyzed them have used volatile organic solvents (VODs), known as being toxic, which significantly limits their use. In this sense, and being aware of the principles of green chemistry, research has begun to be carried out on the use of natural eutectic solvents (NADES) to obtain bioactive compounds of different matrices, including agro-industrial residues.
