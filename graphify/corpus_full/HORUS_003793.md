# Wellness Shine: A lighting design for a hospice center aimed at the elderly population

The ageing can affect the vision, making the visual clarity diminish. Although the eye-care examinations and the use of lenses are useful, it is crucial to have adequate lighting for daily activities, especially for the elderly who need more light due to visual deterioration and possible eye diseases. In addition to the amount of light, the quality is vital to compensate for changes in vision and maintain well-being, enjoyment and safety. Currently, many lighting systems have fixed emission and do not consider physiological and emotional needs, which can alter the circadian rhythms and affect health. Therefore, an adaptive lighting design that promotes a healthy and comfortable environment is essential, especially for those who spend a lot of time indoors. This document, proposes two lighting solutions directed to nursing centers for the elderly, comparing technical and economic results and integrating natural and electrical light to create a pleasant environment.
