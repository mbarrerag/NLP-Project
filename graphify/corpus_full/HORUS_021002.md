# From the classroom of answers to the classroom of the question-making. A didactic strategy that promotes autonomy and meaningful learning in students of the Digital Geomatics subject, of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia.

The educational exercise should focus on continuous improvement processes through the evaluation of methodological and didactic developments that are implemented in the classroom, as well as on the learnings that are acquired in the daily teaching exercise. It is like the teachers who build this research proposal, have been asked how to improve the teaching exercise? how to transform passive learning schemes into active learning? how to become a guide to the teaching-learning processes implemented as a student active agents? How to motivate critical and autonomous thinking in students? These and many other questions have led to the implementation of methodologies in the classroom, among which is highlighted the formulation of questions as a didactic strategy for meaningful learning implemented in the student's Digital Geomatics subject, and which has been developed in the framework of the research project Hermes code 53585, from which the present research proposal is fed.The increasing developments in terms of information and communication technologies and the increase in availability and access to them lead to believe that the proposals live in a world of answers in which all events are addressed. that feed people the ability to achieve comprehensive training over the acquisition of knowledge partialized and segregated by areas of knowledge
