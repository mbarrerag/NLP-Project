# TOTAL PROTOSIS AND QUALITY OF LIFE IN MAYOR ADVANTAGES OF A VEREDA IN MARIQUITA, TOLIMA

Dental prosthesis are essential in the rehabilitation of people with partial or total edentialism, being an adequate, affordable and economical option compared to other treatments.Older adults make part of the population that requires a prosthetic, due to a higher rate of dental loss due to factors such as barriers to access to the health system, poor oral hygiene or systemic problems.The dental prosthetics aim to return function and aesthetics to the stomatognatic apparatus, therefore, it is important to know the perceptions regarding its use, care and impact on the quality of life of those living with them.OBJECTIVE: Analyzing in older adults of a vereda, in Mariquita ¿Tolima, the perception against the use of total prosthesis in relation to their quality of life. An explanatory mixed study. The quantitative component had a cross-sectional analytical design, used the Geriatric Oral Health Assessment, performed a total Pássssement. The quality of life is related to: few or no knowledge about oral health and prosthetic care; the total prosthesis is considered an inevitable treatment that solves edentialism, and is it a necessity in old age?The quality of life is affected when the prosthetics used are maladjusted; there are economic barriers and lack of information to access timely and quality rehabilitation. The negative impact of quality of life is evident in older adults with barriers to access to timely and quality rehabilitation, limited economic resources, and poor knowledge about oral health care or total prosthesis. Pain, discomfort, inability to eat, inability to speak properly, absence of disadapted teeth and prosthesis, are determinant factors of poor quality of life and poor emotional and psychosocial well-being
