# New species and new records of Disteniidae (Coleoptera: Chrysomeloidea) from Mexico with a checklist of Mexican species

Two new species of Disteniidae are described from Oaxaca, Mexico: Distenia (Distenia) toledoisp. n. and Elytrimitatrix (Grossifemora) chimalapensissp. n. Distinguishing characters from closely related species are given and discussed. New state records are provided for Distenia (Basisvallis) rugiscapis Bates, 1885; Disteniazteca fimbriata (Lacordaire, 1869); and elytrimitatrix (grossifemora) sauria santos-silva & hovore, 2008.
