# MANUAL ARCGIS ARCHIS ARCHMAP DESKTOP ADVANCED

The following is a guide for students and those who use the ArcGIS Desktop tool for the development of daily or work activities. The exercises are detailed and explain, step by step, all the main features of ArcGIS Desktop software, such as image processing, spatial analysis, spatial data processing, as well as spatial database editing, satellite image processing, among others. Likewise, the exercises are supported by conceptual discussions at the beginning of each chapter and, as necessary, throughout the book. Otherwise, this guide is the continuation of the ArcGIS ArcMap Desktop Basic and Intermediate Manual
