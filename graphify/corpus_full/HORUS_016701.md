# For four little corners of nothing: Relationship experiences from artistic practices in population with cognitive differences

The connection between art and education in this research is an element that allowed to discover numerous ways of seeing and experiencing the world. It is a proposal that links from cognitive differences, processes of thought, subjectivity and relational experiences as significant methods for the development and construction of thought, autonomy, life skills and participation from an emancipating thought. It is an artistic-pedagogical experience created with young and adults with cognitive differences of the Corporation Un Ser Feliz based on some ideas and concepts addressed by the Brazilian artist Lygia Clark who, from three aesthetic experiences, allows reflection and dialogue on the recognition of their identity processes, the confrontation, reflection and dialogue of their imaginarys related to their daily life, the perception of the other and the standard patterns of the capitalist system in which we are immersed. . The connection between between and competitive education in this research allowed discering number of ways to see and experience the world. This is a proposal that connects and processes and subjects, between between and education in this research. ed
