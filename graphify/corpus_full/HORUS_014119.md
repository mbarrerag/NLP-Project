# Control and optimization system in small-scale aquaculture

In the framework of food insurance at a global level and sustainability, it is necessary to develop technologies of food production that have an impact on the environment. The FAO [1, 2, 3] has given indications on where to focus interest from food insurance, highlighting among this, aquaculture systems as a generator of protein of very high quality.The structuring of this work and guidelines for its implementation are equally addressed by the FAO, where it is indicated how to implement it and how they should be shaped.Despite the advantages of aquaculture production as compared to other types of terrestrial animals, the feeding and maintenance mechanisms of its environment (water) remain a challenge, since these influence the growth and cost of production.The present proposal seeks to propose an alternative solution to improve the process of aquaculture, optimizing the costs of environmental control and growth of fish.The aquaculture systems, either open or recirculation, are of aquaculture, are of constant quality and assurance of water.
