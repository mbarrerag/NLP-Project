# Generation of a recombinant protein of Parvovirus swine 2 (PPV2) to be used as a diagnostic system.

The porcine species has been affected by the porcine parvovirus porcine 1 (PPV1) which produces reproductive faults. New PPVs have been detected during the present century, whose participation as disease trigger agents is being studied. To date, six new PPVs have been found that have been called PPV2 to PPV7. Our research group has carried out several research studies where we have found, as results, the participation of PPV7 in reproductive failures and PPV2 mainly associated with respiratory faults, that is to date, six new PPVs have been found as a possible agent involved in the so-called porcine respiratory complex (CRP). These results have been obtained through research work carried out by master and doctoral students. Particularly with PPV2, we established their presence as a single agent or co-infection with other viruses (PCV2, PRRSV, SIV) mainly at the level of the respiratory system (pulmons) in pigs with clinical symptoms and in the productive stages of pre- and prior- and Cuba.
