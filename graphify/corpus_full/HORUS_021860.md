# Sociopolitical analysis of national mental health policies in Colombia 1998-2019.

This research analyzed the configuration of national mental health policies in Colombia between 1998 and 2019, from a sociopolitical and historical perspective, taking into account the dynamics in which these policies have been immersed and that respond to a regime of expansion of capital, which affects the materialization of mental health as an individual and collective right. Likewise, the way in which practices in the field of mental health, governed by the biomedical approach, are reflected in public policies and, both practical and political, are subsumed by a health system oriented by financial profitability in a context of consolidation of the industrial and financial medical complex. A documentary research was carried out that included policy documents, legal norms and institutional documents of the national executive health sector. Academic publications were included and 25 sociopolitical actors were interviewed, as well as reviewing other primary and secondary sources, which reported the process of structuring mental health policies in Colombia, the context, the conjunctural and sociopolitical dynamics.
