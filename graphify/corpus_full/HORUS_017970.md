# EFFECTS OF DEFAUNATION ON STRUCTURE AND ROLE IN FRUGIVORO-PLANTA INTERACTION NETWORKS

The defaunation is the process of sequential loss or decrease of the population densities of the animal species of greater body size, mediated by anthropic activities such as deforestation and hunting.This phenomenon has the ability to affect the stability of the communities, as well as its functional performance through the disruption of the trophic interactions.In this work we evaluate the effect of defaunation on the structure and function in networks of frugivorous-plant interaction.For this we construct networks of interaction in two localities highly intervened from northern Colombia, and we use a model of simulation of extinction of species under three different scenarios.The first scenario eliminates the species in order of their body size, measured through the body mass, according to the progressive effect of non-randomized loss of species (defaunction); the second scenario eliminates the effect of the species according to their importance in the structure of the network (CNS); and finally, in the third scenario, they eliminate the species according to their potential as seed dispersor (SDP).Based on these simulations, we evaluate the species and the functional network and the diversity of species
