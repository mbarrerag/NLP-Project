# Method for estimating the state in mineral milling processes with semi-autogenous mill.

The implementation of advanced controllers in mineral grinding processes that allow optimization, for example minimization in water consumption, requires the complete measurement of the state. However, this is in many cases impossible due to the non-availability of sensors for this. Therefore, in this work a Kalman filter-type state estimator was designed for the mineral grinding process known as semi-autogen mill (SAG). To achieve this, a phenomenological semiphysical model of the grinding process was obtained, the non-linear observability of the same was tested, the model was discretized, the Kalman extended filter-type state estimator was designed, its behavior was simulated in the event of disturbances and the correct functioning of the estimator was verified. Finally, the conditions necessary for the actual implementation of the estimator were explained and schemes were presented to improve performance in an industrial plant
