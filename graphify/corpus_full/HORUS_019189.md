# DESIGUATES IN COLOMBIA: REFLECTIONS ON AN URGENT ITEM IN THE AGENDA FOR DEVELOPMENT.

Inequality is a topic that has been present in economic theory since the very emergence of discipline. The distribution of income in benefits, wages and income was one of the concerns of the classical political economy. The aim of this paper is to raise some of the problems of inequality in the Colombian case, in light of Piketty's postulates on inequality, its tendency and the ideology that sustains it
