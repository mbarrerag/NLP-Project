# GEOGRAPHICAL ACCESSIBILITY AND ARMED CONFLICT. HOW TO CONSTRUCT PEACE BY VIAL INFRASTRUCTURE?

The emergence of the insurgent and counterinsurgency war, ending the decade of the fiftieth century, serves as an incubator for the Colombian armed conflict, which gradually went from residual armed conflicts in specific regions of the country to an internal armed conflict of a national character, that is, that the war had its own face by 1978 (Commission of Truth [cvc], 2022). The Truth Commission (cvc) lists the processes that emerged as an incubator: (1) absence of an electoral competition for the management of power in a centralist and presidentialist regime; (2) distribution of State resources in a clientelistic manner between the two parties, leaving minorities out; (3) new frustration with agrarian reform; (4) military autonomy in the management of public order that triggered repression; (5) direct interference by the United States in national conflicts; (6) consequences of the Cold War that were expressed regionally; (7) emergence of urban citizenship, which was not aligned with traditional parties and mobilized for unsatisfied fundamental rights; (8) fraud and corruption. (Tomo 3, pp. 76-77)
