# SIMULATION OF THE EMISSION, SECONDARY PRODUCTION, TRANSPORT AND DISPERSION OF MATERIALS PARTICULARLY USED IN THE GEOGRAPHICAL VALLEY OF THE RIUM CAUCA

The Valle geográfico del rio Cauca (VGRC) houses some of the most important populated and industrial centers of the country, and one of the most intensive agro-industrial and livestock activities of Colombia, which implies the emission of a wide variety of atmospheric pollutants with complex spatial and temporal distributions. Additionally, its atmospheric circulation is complex because it is an inter-Andean tropical valley influenced by the intense convective activity of the Colombian Pacific, which governs the transport and dispersion of air pollutants. The VGRC presents a complex topography, caused by its location in the middle of the western and central branches of the Andes mountain range. The Central Range apparently provides a natural barrier that limits the influence of synoptic phenomena, for example, associated with the Chorro del Orinoco, including the seasonal burning of savanas, of great influence in the eastern mountain range of Colombia; while, by the western branch, its lower height and areas with topographic depressions allow the passage of winds from the Pacific.
