# women in the path of social advancement of knowledge through the elemental

Indigenous women in Latin America face discrimination and violation of their rights. In the Muisca Indigenous community of Suba, the study seeks to strengthen their autonomy and collectivity in order to achieve a just and equal society. The study analyzes the role of women in the indigenous lobby, highlighting their empowerment, leadership, transmission of ancestral knowledge and their role as healers. Women play various roles in the community, from family care to leadership and transmission of knowledge
