# THEORY OF SOCIAL REPRESENTATIONS: THEIR DEVELOPMENT IN COLOMBIA IN THE LAST TWENTY YEARS

In the last twenty years, the study of social representations (RS) in Colombia has been characterized by its progressive openness to disciplines different to social psychology, field in which initially the Theory of Social Representations (TRS) was placed. This theory, understood as the approach to systems of values, ideas and practices that serve to establish the social order and facilitate communication (Moscovici, 1972), was assumed as a means for understanding social behavior and instrument of psychosocial intervention. Its foundation in the rescue of common sense, group interaction and the value of actions as a product of culture, provide researchers with new ways of understanding social phenomena. As in the rest of Latin America, the introduction of the TRS in the country is assumed as an alternative to the dominant model of social psychology of Anglo-Saxon court, which focuses attention on the individual and the study of behavior under controlled conditions.
