# EFFECTIVENESS OF THE DUAL THERAPY FOR THE ERADICATION OF H. PYLORI IN A COLOMBIAN POPULATION

Abstract Background: Helicobacter pylori (H. pylori) affects 50% of humanity. Usual treatments have decreased their efficacy by increased resistance to antibiotics, except: amoxicillin, tetracycline, furazolidone and bismuth. Recently there is a new interest in dual therapy with high doses of proton pump inhibitors (IBP) and amoxicillin as initial therapy and as rescue. In our environment there are no studies on this. to determine the efficacy of dual therapy with high doses of IBP and amoxicillin to eradicate H. pylori. Material and methods: quasi-experimental study performed between December 2019 and July 2020 in older than 18 years, with H. pylori identified histologically. All received esomeprazole 40 mg half hour before breakfast, lunch and dinner plus amoxicillin 1 mg oral gram every 8 hours per 14 days.
