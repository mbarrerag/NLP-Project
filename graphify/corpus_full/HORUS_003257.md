# IMPACT OF IA CHATBOTS ON PROGRAMMING TEACHING

In recent years, the artificial intelligence (IA) chatbots have become a tool with a revolutionary potential to transform the teaching and learning processes. Its versatility to offer instant responses, facilitate the search for information, adapt to the individual programming of the students, provide immediate feedback, and create personalized learning environments, among other skills, has generated a great interest and exploration within the educational community. This article aims to explore the impact of the IA chatbots, specifically ChatGPT, as tools for teaching subjects involving programming activities. In the article it describes the teaching experience in preparing and teaching the subjects Basic Technologies of Mechatronics of the fifth semester and Microcontrollers of the eighth semester of the Mechatronic Engineering program of the National University of Colombia.Does the University of Peace, La Paz, Cesar, Colombia? Our findings reveal the potential of IA chatbots in teaching programming. These tools provide students with guidance to address specific doubts about the syntax of programming languages as well as providing instant feedback about the written code and offer personalized support
