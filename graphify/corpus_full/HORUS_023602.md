# EQUITY OF GUNNERO IS KEY FOR GOOD LIVER: FAMILY AND INDIGENOUS COMMUNITIES OF POVO PASTOS, COLOMBIA

The article presents the construction of the gender strategy with indigenous families of the Pueblo de Los Pastos in Nariño, south of Colombia, bordering Ecuador. For three years (2015-2018) the participating research project was carried out: More nutritious popes carried out between the National University of Colombia, the University of McGill ¿Canada, and the Pueblo de Los Pastos, an open dialogue was established from the worldview to strengthen the component of gender equity and Good Living, being fundamental the recognition of the role and autonomy that women have in the territory
