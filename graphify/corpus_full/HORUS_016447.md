# SIMULTANE COMPARISON OF URBAN AREAS TERRMICAL CONDITIONS IN MEDELLIN, COLOMBIA

This study presents an analytical, comparative, deductive methodology between the environmental variables of dry bulb temperature and relative humidity of the city's meteorological stations and the data taken in situ from two sectors on slopes located on the eastern and western side of the Aburrá Valley, where Medellin, Colombia is located. It was identified that the installation of the sensors, the convective ascending wind by the hillsides, the vehicular and pedestrian circulation, the daily habits and microclimatic conditions of each place affected the procedure of measurement and validation of the data of the measuring stations in situ.
