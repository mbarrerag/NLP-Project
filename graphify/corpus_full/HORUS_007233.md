# Additional tests on rib-type connections with concrete-filled circular column of steel

The composite structural systems have gained popularity due to their high rigidity to control displacements and drifts, as well as their great energy dissipation capacity. This thesis extends a previous set of tests on connections with a configuration similar to those already pre-qualified, with the aim of deepening the behaviour of the connections between the two regions and extending the validity range for their application. In this study two specimens with the same connection configuration were validated, with the aim that they could be viable alternatives for moment-resistant composites. The process was carried out from an experimental approach, focused on the observation and interpretation of the results. The results obtained showed that both connecting specimens met the requirements necessary to be used in moment-resistant Pórticos, both with special capacity to dissip of special energy as well as moderate, according to the guidelines of the NSR-10(AIS, 2010) and the AISC(AISC341-202). .
