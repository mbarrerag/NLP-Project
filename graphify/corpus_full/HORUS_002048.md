# International e-commerce in consumer protection: the dynamism of contemporary private law

This paper analyses the intersection between international e-commerce and consumer protection, addressing legal and contractual challenges in a globalised digital environment. It examines the associated risks, such as contractual imbalance, lack of transparency and personal data protection, considering the effectiveness of the Colombian Consumer Statute (Law 1480 of 2011) against the dynamics of electronic commerce. Through a mixed methodological approach, combining dogmatic and empirical analysis, regulatory deficiencies are identified and solutions are proposed based on international experiences. It is concluded that it is essential to update the regulatory framework to ensure effective consumer protection in the digital age. .
