# AFRICA IN DIASPORA AND AFROCDENTIENT POPULATIONS: DIASPORICAL SPACE AND INTERCULTURE IN COLOMBIA

The relationship between the formation of space for the Diaspora and the manifestations of Africanism is analyzed; as are the contributions of the African diaspora in Colombia and its challenges to multiculturalism
