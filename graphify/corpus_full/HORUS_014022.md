# Chem-E-Car: A scale vehicle powered by chemical reactions.

The vehicles as a conventional means of transport mainly use fossil fuels for their mobility, and represent 60.6% of the annual greenhouse gas emissions only in the European Union. However, recently, a huge boost has been given to electrification of the vehicle fleet in the world, which implies the need to develop increasingly efficient electric batteries, and built on low cost and low environmental impact materials. In this project, it is sought to design, manufacture and launch an electric vehicle on a scale, whose batteries present an alternative to those commonly used by the electric vehicle industry. This development seeks to give an initial direct proposal to a different form of mobilization in which not only is given with zero emotions, but that additionally a low-cost energy source and high energy density can be developed. This development is framed within the annual competition Chem-E-Car carried out globally by the American Institute of Chemical Engineers (AIChE). This competition seeks to contribute to the achievement of sustainable development objectives and aims to encourage the participation of engineering students in this task. The work team will be formed by students from the departments of engineering and electronics engineering and engineering
