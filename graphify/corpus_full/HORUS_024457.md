# Influence of socioeconomic level on the learning of socio-emotional skills in children at the Maternal School of the National Pedagogical University of Bogotá

The education of socio-emotional skills in early childhood is necessary to improve the inequality of education at socio-economic level and in the socialization of children. However, when observing the results of tests worldwide, these skills in Colombian children are low. Therefore, the research was carried out with the questions, how are these skills taught? and does the learning of these skills vary depending on the socioeconomic level of your family or the parent-child relationship? Observations were made of pedagogical programs, interviews and surveys in a kindergarten under the jurisdiction of the National Pedagogical University. As a result, it was found that the education of socio-emotional abilities is carried out through the musical and cultural learning, and the interaction of children with teachers. In addition, on the difference in socio-economic skills, and the relationship parent-child, it was found that these skills in the children of low socio-economic mothers were low-level. . , and the interaction of children with teachers. In addition, on the difference in socio-emotional skills according to socioeconomic level and parents-children relationship, it was found that these skills in children of single mothers of low socioeconomic status were low.
