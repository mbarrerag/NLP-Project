# Analysis of Opportunities in the Massification of Advanced Measurement Infrastructure for Demand Participation in the Colombian Electricity Market

The energy transition that is currently experiencing the country imposes new challenges in the operation, monitoring and control of distribution networks.This paradigm change implies to start thinking of the end user as an active agent of the energy market, which has the potential to provide flexibility to the system and to solve in real time problems or reductions of reliability that are presented in the other links of the electricity supply chain.In this sense, the enablement of the demand for electricity as an active agent of the market, and the successful integration of units of variable generation near to the centers of consumption, is conditioned to the integration of systems of intelligent measurement that allow to have a bidirectional flow of information and energy between the operator of the network and users of the system.In this sense, the integration of the intelligent measurement systems in the country has the potential to bring with it benefits related to the reading and remote operation, which would allow the electricity demand of the system to increase flexibility and adaptability by the active participation of the dynamics of the system, the connection and the limitation of power of the remote way, and the identification of possible connections of the fraudulent ones in the country, allowing to reduce the related losses of electricity and to the users, which allows to the users, which allows to the active of the electricity generate tools for the training of users in the use of smart measurement systems, and the identification of strategies and criteria that would allow network operators to solve the challenges related to the implementation of advanced measurement infrastructure in their distribution networks
