# The use of autonomous learning as a complement to the pedagogy of research in the concept of atomic structure

This didactic research was carried out in the Educational Institution José María Córdoba de Florida, Valle del Cauca, in rural context with multigrade classroom, consisting of 25 students of eighth, ninth and tenth; ages predominantly between 15-16 years (56%). The objective was to implement innovative didactic strategies to improve understanding of the concept of atomic structure in the secondary and middle education level with learning difficulties. In addition, a study was implemented with a mixed approach and sequential exploratory design developed in four phases: diagnosis (pre-test), design of didactic strategy (BOA), application and evaluation (post-test). A didactic strategy was applied. The higher level of the action was increased from 8% to 24% in the first post-test and 20% in the second. The basic level increased from 12% to 16% in both post-test.
