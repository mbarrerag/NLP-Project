# The theme of “gender ideology” in RCN News and Facebook and its influence on the results of the plebiscite for peace in Colombia

With the plebiscite for peace, held on October 2, 2016, the aim was to endorse the agreements reached between the Colombian government and the FARC-EP, to put an end to more than half a century of armed conflict. However, the plebiscite campaign was marked by polarization and manipulation of information, in which the "gender ideology" became a strategy to delegitimize the Peace Agreement and of course the incorporation of the gender approach into it. This work seeks to analyze the themeization of RCN News and Facebook on the rhetorical discourse of the "gender ideology" in the framework of the plebiscite for peace in Colombia. The above is aimed at understanding the role of the media in the formation of a public opinion and to analyze the instrumentalization of the gender ideology as a strategy with political purposes in Colombia.
