# Beliefs of people with pulmonary tuberculosis during treatment

Tuberculosis is not only an infectious disease already contagion, it is a social disease that causes great suffering, that affects the biological, psychological, social and cultural sphere of the individual. The current treatment of pulmonary tuberculosis, consists in taking several antibiotics for a prolonged period of at least six months, which can be extended to 18 months or 24 months in cases of multiresistant tuberculosis. The stigma of the disease, the characteristics of the program, as well as the beliefs that originate, reinforce or change around tuberculosis and its treatment, are factors that can affect the successful treatment. To describe the beliefs of people with pulmonary tuberculosis during treatment. A qualitative-interpretative study. The data were obtained through semi-structured interviews to people with pulmonary tuberculosis, whose condition of admission to the tuberculosis control distrital program was new patient, that is to say with diagnosis of pulmonary tuberculosis for the first time and that was receiving anti-tuberculosis treatment, in total twenty people. The collection and analysis of the data occurred in alternating sequence. The qualitative analysis of the data was carried out according to the guidelines of Sally Thorne.
