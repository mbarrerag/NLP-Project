# GERPH BIOLOGICAL COLLECTION: STRATEGIC TOOL TO KNOW THE BIODIVERSITY OF HEMOPARASITS

Biological collections have played a fundamental role in the knowledge of biodiversity for centuries, they can be compared to a photograph of diversity in a given place and time, which constitutes a scientific basis with which to make comparisons in the future. Biological collections allow not only to catalogue species, but also to understand aspects of biology and evolution of organisms. However, there is a ubiquitous group of microorganisms that carry one of the most successful life strategies in the natu-raleza and make a fundamental part of ecosystems, impact- tando the biology and evolution of other organisms, and despite this, they are little represented in collections; parasites. Colombia is one of the most biodiverse countries in the world, highlighted by its number of vertebrate animal species, therefore it is logical to assume that the diversity of parasites of these vertebrates, must be equally high.
