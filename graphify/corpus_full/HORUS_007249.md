# Electronic system design for monitoring a small-scale aquaponic crop for urban spaces

This thesis presents the development and evaluation of a remote monitoring system for aqua-ponical culture, capable of measuring key parameters such as temperature, dissolved oxygen, pH and ammonia, nitrite and nitrate concentrations using IoT networks. The system, developed in two TempBox and AquaBox devices, proved to be effective in precise monitoring of environmental conditions, with robust sensor performance and stable data transmission using LoRa technology. Calibration techniques, including the use of multiple linear regression, improved measurement accuracy. The proposed low-cost monitoring system demonstrates a significant potential for commercial application, offering an accessible solution to manage water quality in aquaponic systems .
