# POTENCIOMETERIC ENTITLING OF AGROUNDUMS.

In everyday life we find a large number of substances that have acidic or basic characteristics. Among the substances of basic character we can find baking powder (sodium bicarbonate), substances that act as stomach antacids, cleaning products such as detergents, chlorine-based bleaches and ammonia cleaners, among others. For their part, many agroinsumos are substances of acid or basic character
