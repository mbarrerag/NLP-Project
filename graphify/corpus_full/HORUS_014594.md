# Evaluation of the epidemiological behaviour of clinical isolates of Klebsiella pneumoniae by complete genome sequencing (WGS)

The phenomenon of bacterial resistance has gained strength in recent years, it is estimated that by 2050 the number of deaths associated with it reaches more than 10 million individuals in the world. In Colombia one of the most isolated resistant microorganisms at hospital level in ICU services and hospitalization is called Klebsiella pneumoniae type KPC, a Gram negative bacillus capsule forming, producer of carbapenemases. The gene blahKPC located near the Tn4401 transposon in its genome, confers resistance to carbapenemic antibiotics.The aim of this study was to evaluate the epidemiological behavior and variation of the genomic profiles of resistance to antibiotics KPC strains of K. pneumoniae, coming from clinical isolations, by means of the equencing of the complete Genoma (WGS).In total 37 isolates collected between 2019 and 2020 were processed, of which all ST.35% presented a multidrogo resistant phenotype (MDR).The mechanism of resistance pump of antibiotics was the most frequent in the hospital (51.16%).
