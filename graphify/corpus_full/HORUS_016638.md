# CHANGE DETECTION IN THE ROLE OF ACUMULATED INTENSITY OF A TRP PROCESS

In repairable systems, there is a wide variety of stochastic models that can be used to model and explain the behavior of system failure times, which depend on the way in which the repair of the system is performed once it fails. When one has a single fault mode and the repair is imperfect, among the most used models is the TRP process, proposed by Lindqvist. In this work, a procedure for the detection of change in the cumulative intensity function of a TRP process is proposed, based on its recursive estimation and the definition of a CUSUM statistic on a succession of recursive residues. Some characteristics of the procedure are studied through Monte-Carlo simulation. Among the results are that the behavior of the proposed CUSUM statistician is similar to that of the CUSUM statistic for structural change tests in linear regression models under process stability, with some differences
