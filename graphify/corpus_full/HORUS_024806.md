# PROJECT FINANCING IN THE MINING SECTOR: PERSPECTIVES AND CHALLENGES

This paper analyses the main financing options for mining projects in Colombia. The main objective is to present the mechanisms for financing available debt and assets, especially those offered by the Colombian Stock Exchange. It highlights the importance of formalizing the mining sector as a strategy to improve access to bank credit, as well as the possibility of obtaining financial resources through the capital market. Finally, there are some challenges in the sector related to formalization and access to finance
