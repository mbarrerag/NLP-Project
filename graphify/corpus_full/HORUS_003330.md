# ACHIEVEMENT OF NUTRITIONAL GOALS IN DERLY PATENTS: EXPERIENCE IN A HIGH COMPLEXITY HOSPITAL IN COLOMBIA.

The study entitled "Achieving nutritional objectives in elderly patients in a hospital of high complexity in Colombia" focuses on characterizing nutritional status and meeting nutritional goals in elderly hospitalized adults in a high complexity environment. Made in the Hospital Universitario Mayor Méderi, in Bogotá, Colombia, this cross-sectional study included patients aged 65 years and older between 2019 and 2023. Malnutrition is a prevalent problem in elderly hospitalized adults, which highlights the need for comprehensive nutritional assessments to hospital admission. The study reveals that 85% of patients received a nutritional evaluation in the first two days of their hospitalization, identifying severe protein-calorie malnutrition in 44.6% and moderate in 21.8% of cases. In addition, 78.8% of patients were prioritized for nutritional intervention, receiving nutritional support mainly through oral (65.7%) and totality (25.7%).Achieving nutritional objectives, defined as achieving at least 75% of nutritional requirements, significantly increased from 35.9% to 63.2% of patients admitted to hospital discharge.
