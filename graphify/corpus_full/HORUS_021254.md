# The reality of the territories in Colombia: the effects of the implementation of the multipurpose cadastre

One of the main causes of the armed conflict in Colombia is the structural problem regarding access and ownership of the land. In this context, according to the same Final Agreement for the termination of the conflict (2016) the clarification of who has the land, in what conditions, its value and the other aspects regarding it are indispensable if we want to finally solve the problem of the land in Colombia and consequently the armed conflict. That is why the implementation of the Multipurpose Cadastre (CMP) in the country is fundamental, since it is through this tool that we seek to reach knowledge of the realities of the territory from the inventory of lands.However, the implementation of this mechanism advances at a slow pace and has had difficulties ranging from the fiscal and financial problems to the opposition of the civil society by the increase of the values of its lands.As we progress in this process, it is fundamental that the public entities that are developing this program as the IGAC and the DNP have information regarding the impact that this policy has had on the territories. It is therefore our interdisciplinary alliance will advance in the study of these problems and will seek to diagnose the physical implementation of the in the national implementation of the IGA. This is to be done by means of specific techniques such as interviews with local and national officials to identify the main areas of impact, surveys with representative samples, focus groups to advance discussions with the community and data collection that serve as indirect sources to assess the economic impacts, especially those related to the change in ICA and Rent taxes, by requesting anonymized declarations to use quantitative instruments on them, such as linear regressions and descriptive statistics. Thus, the aim is to position the National University as an interlocutor among the populations and public entities that develop public policies on them, as well as to provide possibilities for improving the implementation of these policies in order to advance the implementation of a stable and lasting peace, to guarantee the development of the territories as well as to generate a contribution to the generation of factors that will stimulate the practicality of the academy's contributions to the community and the social processes of change
