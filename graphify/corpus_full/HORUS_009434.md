# A LITTLE URBAN FOREST IN MEDELLÍN

The project, designed by Edgar Mazo from 2017 and inaugurated last year, is part of a large project to transform various green areas of the city, called "Pyectos urbanos estratégicos" and developed by the Municipality for the direction of Alejandro Restrepo. The project manages to link with elegance and wisdom a system of pedestrian paths, resting spaces and small architectural elements at the service of the public space, with a varied tropical vegetation. A careful, sensitive and experimental project, with a limited budget (2.15 million dollars), an important example of respectful and ecological transformation of our cities
