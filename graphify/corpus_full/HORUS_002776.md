# VALLE DE BURRA, ANTIOCIA

The Aburra Valley is located in the central area of Antioquia and presents an irregular topography with height between 1300 and 2800 meters above sea level. Within the metropolitan area different protected areas have been established that are embedded within a large urban matrix and that allow the care of the flora and fauna of the region and the enjoyment of this. Our objective was to characterize the political diversity of the flora of importance for the bees in six parks of the Aburra Valley. In a sample monthly carried out during 2021, in six protected areas, floral buttons were obtained from 73 taxons of plants visited by bees, of which 24 are at the level of gender and 49 at the level of species. The material was processed using standard methods of extraction of pollen, was observed and measured using a microscope of visible light. We took measurements for 25 grains of pollen and were followed by 17 species collected.
