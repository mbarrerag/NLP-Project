# A look at local museums through the critical museum for the patrimonial activation in Pamplona-North Santander

This work analyzed the museum spaces of the city of Pamplona in Norte de Santander, through the Critical Museum, one of the contemporary theoretical currents, which renews the functions of these spaces in a society, thinking them as articulated to the realities and needs of the population where they are inserted. As spaces, of enjoyment of the cultural rights of citizens, of debate and of social transformation. The look, in this work, focuses on the local museum spaces in Colombia, through the experience of them, in an intermediate city like Pamplona; realizing the administrative, financial, political and theoretical realities that cross this type of museums. Taking into account the context of the cultural policies, as well as the looks given to them from the public administration of the national and local order. The primary interest, is to have real supports that contribute to having an argumented panorama of the heritage that exists in these spaces, which are at risk, as well as the need to take sufficient measures at the national, departmental and local level to activate it and allow through this heritage, the San School's enjoyment of the cultural rights of the citizens. and social transformation.
