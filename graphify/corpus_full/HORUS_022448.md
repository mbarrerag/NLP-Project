# EVALUATION, DIAGNOSTIC AND INTERVENTION IN ORGANIZATIONAL PSYCHOLOGY: NIVEL GROUP.

This book is a contribution to the field of Organizational Psychology and related areas. A compilation of some variables of organizational behavior was made at the group level. Each chapter of this work addresses the explanation from different perspectives on organizational behavior in groups and teams, as well as the description of tools that allow evaluating, diagnosing, and some proposals for designing interventions, depending on the reality of the organization and its organizational culture
