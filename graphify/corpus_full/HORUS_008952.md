# VIAL SAFETY IN THE CITY OF MANIZALS.

This book is the result of the research on technological development based on the methodology proposed for road safety audits in human behavior and the education of road actors. The debate and reflections that have taken place in the research that supports this text were built on a transdisciplinary research that involved professionals in civil engineering, urban planning, anthropology and social work. This was developed by the Territorial Research Group, the Communication, Culture and Society research group of the University of Caldas and, the E-business Knowledge Management research group of the National University of Colombia between 2019 and 2021 in Manizales. Often studies on urban mobility contain knowledge that comes from civil engineering. Many of these studies are taken as a basis for the construction of public policies that allow the planning of networks of connection and communication between cities
