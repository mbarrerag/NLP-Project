# RADIOFRECUENCY OF BAXTER NERVUM IN PATIENT WITH FASCITIS PLANT REFRACTARIA TO CONVENTIONAL MANNEJO

Plantar fasciitis is recognized as the main cause of thalalgia worldwide, in the vast majority of cases it is managed by using adequate footwear, stretching exercises and changes in sports activity, a few cases require infiltrations or surgical interventions, the latter poses a greater risk for the patient being reserved for the most severe cases. It is proposed the use of pulsed radiofrequency of the nerve of Baxter as one of the options of management of the painful symptom in those patients in which no adequate response to conventional treatment is obtained. We expose the case of a patient with refractory plantar fasciitis, not candidate for surgery, who was taken to pulsed radiofrequency with satisfactory results in the short and medium term
