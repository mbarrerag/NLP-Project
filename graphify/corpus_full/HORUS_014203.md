# Analysis of the political opposition processes of the party during the period 2021 - 2023 in Colombia.

In this research proposal, the processes of political opposition carried out during the period between the years 2021 - 2023 are analyzed and compared, the process of political party participation carried out during the period of the period of the years of political opposition is completely implemented. Specifically, the research interest regarding the development of the political party opposition initiated since the year 2021 with the consolidation of a social outbreak against the policies proposed by the national government of Iván Duque, which used the opposition as a means to channel social and political unrest, and consolidated as a strong, alternative and transformative political force. In this sense, with the first year of the government of Gustavo Petro, it is possible to demonstrate the political and social changes in which these opposition groups are immersed, the relationship of government - opposition and how different processes are evidenced with respect to the implementation of guarantees and rights protected by the Statute of the Opposition (Law 1909 of 2018) during these presidential periods. The topic that will be studied in the research project is the political opposition party, developed during the period of time between the years 2021 and 2023. Specifically, the political opposition will be investigated from a set of normative frameworks (normes, laws, laws and legal devices), Law 1909 of 2018) and its legal and political sense has been distorted, leading to the violation of rights and guarantees for the opposition political forces.
