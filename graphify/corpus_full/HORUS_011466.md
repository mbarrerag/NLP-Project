BOOK Environmental Crisis, denial of rights and social outbreak. Volume 1 Gregorio Mesa Cuadros (ed.) 2023 It is specified that perhaps the greatest current conflicts that reflect the widely theorized and discussed in the last five decades on the environmental and civilizing crisis, have to do with the climate crisis, the anti-democratic crisis and the crisis of the recent pandemic, which surely will not be the last to the extent that the direction is not reversed. It is expected that the governments will act in line, in order to understand its causes, its consequences and the most appropriate mechanisms to resolve the environmental conflict. But the circumstances usually do not reflect what is desired and expected, in particular of ways to act from the States and the international community in the face of the planetary crisis. The Colombian case is paradoxical in many of these issues. Before traveling to Glasgow to COP26 and without a message of urgency, the national government has presented to Congress the draft law for the ratification of the Escazú agreement, with the intention of showing itself committed to the many of these issues.
