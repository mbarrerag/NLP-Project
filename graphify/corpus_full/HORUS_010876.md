# Recognizing knowledge and flavors. Experience of the same in the peasant women of Sesquilé

The relations that women establish with land through labour, the production of food for self-consumption and the care of the human and the non-human, are invisible by the centrality that has the argument of productivity that underlies the legal notion of property that historically has favored men. Consequently, this work seeks to explore the ways in which peasant women in the municipality of Sesquilé have experienced the same from their everyday knowledge-does in the materiality of the land, from those that emerge practices and spatialities linked to the care and support of life.
