# PROSPECTS FOR SUSTAINABLE USE OF THE TROPIC DOMESTIC GRYL (GRYLLODES SIGILLATUS) FOR HUMAN FOOD IN COLOMBIA

By 2050, an increase of 60 to 70 per cent in the consumption of animal products is expected. This increase in consumption will demand enormous resources, with traditional sources of protein being the most expensive, overexploited and environmentally harmful. Exploring new sources of animal protein becomes a necessity for the agricultural sector. This is why FAO (2009) included the use of insects in human and animal feed as an alternative source of nutrients since 2003 due to their nutritional characteristics and low environmental impact. One of the most promising species is the tropical domestic cricket (Gryllodes sigillatus), whose potential as a sustainable productive system has been demonstrated in several Asian countries as European. The purpose of this article is to present the aspects associated with the exploitation and production of the species G. sigillatus that can make it sustainable as a food in Colombia, taking into account the general and nutritional characteristics of the species and the socio-economic and environmental advantages of the breeding of these crickets and insects in general. It is established that, although there are enterprises in the country, it is important to continue research on this species in terms of large-scale production, as well as in terms of nutritional terms of the country to enhance the farming conditions of the sector
