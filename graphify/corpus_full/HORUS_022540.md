# Supervised learning in the construction of a Credit Scoring model for credit and savings cooperatives in Colombia.

The financial institutions of the solidarity sector provide credit to their associates, exposing themselves to a risk derived from this activity. This research work aims at the methodological proposal of a credit risk model, which assigns a credit score to debtors based on the information they provide to financial institutions. Analytical techniques of supervised learning are used for the construction of the model with the ASUM-DM methodology. Data are taken from a credit portfolio of a solidarity sector company in Colombia to illustrate the methodological development in the construction of the Scoring model for the behavior score of the associates. Biplot methods, correspondence analysis and main components are used for the reduction of dimensionality. Also, the techniques of decision trees, probabilistic regression models, K-Vecinos closest, vector support machines and neural networks are used. The above methods are used for the Credit Scoring model in the allocation of credit score and the selection of variables that are significant in the study.
