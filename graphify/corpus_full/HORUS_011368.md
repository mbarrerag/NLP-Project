# LOGICAL STRATEGY FOR INGENIERS: PLAYING THE WHITE PLAYING MINI CATAPULTS

The subject integrated mechanical design of the mechanical engineering program of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, is a subject of practical nature in which complex competences are handled that include the integration of knowledge and skills acquired in the different fields of engineering. The course starts with a playful activity, consisting of the design and creation of a game. The playful strategy consisted in carrying out the game: shooting at the target using mini catapults, which is developed as a competition in which groups of two members must design and build a mini catapult with specifications defined in its dimensions; and with the aim of to bring down a certain number of objects to a certain distance determined by a projectile that is thrown by the mini catapult, the mini catapult must also meet requirements of the type of spring to use and of the deposit where the projectile is found; and where the winning game team is the one that employs and manufactures the most points when to top the objects, which have different scores according to the mini catapulta. In the first phase the participants of the game must have very clear rules and consider the parameters to be used to design and to make the game. the goals of the game, where as engineers they discover the importance of reducing parts as it facilitates the manufacturing process, assembly, disassemble, manufacturing costs, labor time. In addition, the play activity allows participants to encourage creativity by generating alternatives of solution that meet the requirements to a defined problem, provides the game members an engineering exercise that leads them to a viable solution technically and economically, encouraging collaborative work and assertive communication
