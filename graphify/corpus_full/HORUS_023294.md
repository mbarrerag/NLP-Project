# COMPRENSION, LOVE AND RESILENCE AT PANDEMIA DUE TIMES

The coronavirus pandemic COVID-19, has represented a crisis of global health that defines this time and a challenge facing humanity. People live an extraordinary situation and different processes of mourning due to death of a loved one, fear to infect or to become ill, lose the home or work, etc. The purpose of this experience was to generate a space for the expression of emotions, doubts and imaginary around death and to identify mechanisms of coping for the processes of mourning. Description of the problem or need: Death by COVID 19 at Bogotá level has presented a large number of cases that at the moment has left 27,487 deaths that is equivalent to 1.9% of the Colombian population; this situation increased by 34% the need for care in mental health. In times of pandemic aspects such as fear, death and grief are more present and complex issues to understand and face, so there is a need to provide information about these processes and mechanisms of confrontation that potentiate resilience. Intervention, strategies or implemented practice: In the framework of the practice of Nursing Care in mental health and mental illness, Francisco, there are implemented workshops (instructural) The mental health needs in the pandemic and 98.2% state that the workshops provided mental, emotional and social support resources for the grieving process. Some expressions were: Did I find the dynamics very interesting, I would like them to do them more often? and do I like the workshop because it allows to accept the departure of a loved one, and that is very painful? Limitations, difficulties and/or successes: One was the relevance of the topic, so, the pandemic by COVID-19 brought losses of all kinds for the population subject of care and for nursing students, some in mourning process. On the other hand, the limited time of the practice did not allow to expand the coping mechanisms. References: 1. Bogotá, A. M. (07 September 2021). Health Secretary. (S.
