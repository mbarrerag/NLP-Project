# COLOMBIA LICENS, A ORIGINAL SOURCE OF BIOACTIVE SUBSTANCES

Lichens (LQ) are organisms resulting from symbiosis between a fungus and one or more photosynthetic organisms. They can thrive in various habitats including those subject to extreme conditions in temperature, humidity, UV radiation, among others, where other organisms would not survive. This has been associated with the biosyntheticization of secondary metabolites that protect them from such adverse conditions. Colombia, has a privileged position in the diversity and abundance of LQ, however, research to know its biological properties is incipient. At the National University of Colombia, efforts have been focused on determining the potential of LQs of Sumapaz param as an original source of bioactive substances. Considering that in the param the LQs are highly exposed to solar radiation and oxidative stress, compounds with antioxidant and photoprotective properties that could constitute the preventive agents of skin cancer have been sought.
