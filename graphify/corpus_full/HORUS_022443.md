# ANALYTICAL AND NUMERICAL SOLUTIONS OF MECHANICAL EFFORTS IN ISOTROPICAL RETANGULAR PLACAS

In this study, a model is developed and validated to simulate numerically the normal and cutting forces induced on the upper and lower sides of a rectangular and isotropic flat plate. The plate is supported on its four edges and the pressure is uniformly distributed over its entire area. Analytical solutions were found by means of the general equation of plates using the Fourier double series method. The numerical solutions were obtained by the finite element method (ANSYS 19.1 software). The validation of the model is performed by statistically comparing the analytical and numerical solutions of the induced forces in a classic experiment with three pressure levels. The results show that there is no significant statistical difference between the two types of solutions. It is concluded that the numerical simulation model is valid
