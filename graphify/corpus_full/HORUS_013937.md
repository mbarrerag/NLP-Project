# The Prison System in Medellín, the Valle de Aborra and Antioquia, 1914-1934

This proposal for a degree in research, to qualify for the title of historian of the Faculty of Human and Economic Sciences at the Medellín headquarters of the National University of Colombia, aims to address the functioning of the prison system in the city of Medellín and of some institutions of the Department of Antioquia during the first half of the 20th century, specifically between 1914 and 1934
