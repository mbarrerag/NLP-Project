# Back to the Forum: SIMBOLIC RETURNS IN THE ORALITERARI TEXTS OF HUGO JAMIOY

This essay proposes a reading of Hugo Jamioy's oraliterary texts as a symbolic solution to dilemmas related to uprooting and identity, since in his texts he refers to the return to territory, highlighting the symbolic connotations of the cultural practice of burying the placenta under the fire as a gesture that links the poetic subject with his territory of origin, connecting him with the space where memory is fed from orality
