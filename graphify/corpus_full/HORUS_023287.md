# LAND AS A CULTUREAL RELATIONSHIP AND HUMAN EXPERIENCE?

This article deals with the analysis of land as a multiform object in various experiences and cultural representations. This defines identification processes, according to various knowledge, disciplines and sciences. The representations, besides having a set of meanings and productions of meaning, are inscribed in a historicality of their own. The earth, although it appears as a visible reality, hides itself in multiple meanings. In order to clarify its representations, it will be presented: the Representations and constitutions, a plane of immanence, the angst of presence, the instrumental reason, and the relation of law? The first, as unique forms of etimology of words. The second, as a geological stratum that recognizes spatial phenomena. The third, as the tension between earth and nature in the nutritional function. The fourth, as the instrumental use of land for the sustenance of life. The fifth, in the representation of the earth as an object of space law, is concerned with the analysis of land as an object of land
