# Children orphaned by femicide and its impact on the family in Colombia

The article aims to analyze the effects of femicide on the families of victims, especially children who remain in an orphan situation. The research was carried out on the basis of the monitoring and registration of women victims of femicide, and of interviews with families of such women, officials of institutions and academic experts. Additionally, the analysis of laws of other countries was used to assess the progress of Colombia in the design of specific policies. As a result of the research, three fundamental aspects were established: (1) the lack of a register of children in an orphan situation due to femicide; (2) the lack of a protocol to repair and care for children of women's relatives; and (3) the lack of a public policy exclusively for the care of this population
