# THE PROBLEM OF CRONICAL DISEASE IN WORKING POPULATION: A REVISION REVIEW

The demographic and epidemiological transition has led to an increase in the prevalence of chronic diseases (EC) that not only affects the population above 65 years of age, but also the population of economically active age. The purpose of this study is to analyze how the approach of the EC has been in the working population from the models presented by Arredondo, in public health (SP). A systematic search was made for type documents revisions of the literature in indexed databases and search engines during the period 2006-2019 and excluded studies focused solely on the exposure-effect relationship or clinical management of the EC. To evaluate the quality of the studies included, the AMSTAR instrument was used. It was analyzed how the problem of EC in the working population has been addressed, from the models in SP, which transcends the classical biomedical approach. In particular, it is evident that the approach of this problem must go beyond the lifestyles, nutritional habits and the need to deepen its study in the Latin American context
