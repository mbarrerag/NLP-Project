# NEEDS OF THE PATIENT'S FAMILY IN THE URGENCY SERVICE

The family of the patient who enters the emergency service, sometimes, is excluded from the care process of his family, which generates different emotional responses in the family. Descriptional review of the type Scoping Review, a search was carried out with the terms Decs and Mesh ¿FamilyEmotions?Emotions?Emergency service?. The terms were used in the databases Pubmed, EBSCO, Embase, BVS, and Web of Science looking to describe the literature existing regarding the emotions of the patient's family in the emergency service. The publication period between 2013 - 2023 was limited. 17 articles were obtained in which they identified 3 thematic axes in the literature: Importance of the family in the Emergency Service, responses of the family members that transcend the emotions, needs of the family members. For the practice in nursing, it is essential to adopt a focused approach in the family, recognizing the family members as active collaborators in the care process. This implies overcoming the historical barriers that have seen the specific presence of family, promoting a collaborative approach
