# Regional pain syndrome secondary to SARS-CoV-2 infection. Case report

Complex regional pain syndrome (RCS) is a rare condition whose diagnosis is made based on a physical examination and symptoms reported by the patient as there is no definitive diagnostic test. Treatment of this condition, which is also limited and often does not produce complete relief of symptoms, focuses on increasing mobility and use of the affected member. The case of a patient who developed CRS SD as a result of an infection by SARS-CoV-2 has not been documented to date. Since a causal relationship between these two entities has not been documented, this is considered an atypical case. Presentation of the case. 28-year-old woman who consulted a fourth-level care institution in Bogotá (Colombia) for neuropathetic pain and limited mobility of the left upper limb. Three weeks earlier the patient had presented infection by SARS-CoV-2. Given the symptomatology, it was considered that she was in secondary SDRC with infection by SARS-CoV-2.
