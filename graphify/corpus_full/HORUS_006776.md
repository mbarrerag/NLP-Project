# Incidence of intramolecular hydrogen bridges on the anomeric effect on bis-benzilimidazolidin-type heterocyclic systems [BISBIAs]

In this paper, the general characteristics defining a hydrogen bridge, parameters, classification, incidence in the physicochemical properties and their relationship to the anomeric effect were first described through the inference that hydrogen bridges sometimes exert on the conformation of molecules, especially in the heterocyclic systems of the bis-benzilimidazolidine type through the analysis of their spectroscopic data.
