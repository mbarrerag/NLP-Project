# The records of La Etnnia in the 1990s

The present study focuses on the first three musical productions of the rap group La Etnnia, published between 1995 and 1997 in Bogotá, Colombia. It should be mentioned that, although there are later re-editions of the same albums in different formats, these will not be considered within the present work. Likewise, the background to the publication of the albums is involved, which opens a contextual panorama about the local hip-hop movement that led to the formation of the band La Etnnia. This allows an outline of the main features of appropriation of the hip-hop to the Bogota context found in the components of these three albums. As part of this study, different levels of analysis are involved that involve the themes of the lyrics of the songs, the musical elements present, the iconographical elements of each of the albums, and a characterization from the perspective of the musical poetics of the rap presented by Adam Krims; the latter with the intention to cement of the spatial development of the slowness and the., musical elements, iconography and ultimately; a characterization from the musical poetry of rap music proposed by Adam Krims.
