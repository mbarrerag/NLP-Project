# Virtual screening and evaluation of the tranquilizing activity of new thiazepine, thiazolidine and isoquinolin compounds.

The computational chemistry allows the use of multiple tools for the development of new drugs. In this work the approach of design of drugs associated with the structure was explored, using the GABA-A receptor as target for the study of molecular coupling of three series of organic compounds with the nuclei; spirothiazepinone, spirothizolidone and tetrahydropyrazoloquinoline.The molecular docking site of benzodiazepines was used between the interface, /, and the component of the GABA-A receptor. The coupling study was carried out with the programs Autodock 4.2.6, AutoDock Vina and Dock6, then a consensus of score was made with the scores of each docking, which allowed to make a selection of the most promissory compounds, in conjunction with the prediction of the common physical, anti-kinetic, anti-toxicological and anti-surgical properties
