# Method for customer segmentation incorporating the prediction of the customer's monetary value as a segmentation variable.

The aim of this research thesis is to propose a method for customer segmentation, incorporating the prediction of the customer's monetary value as a segmentation variable, for this purpose, a quantitative methodology, in which the data to be used correspond to the transactions of an online gift shop for every occasion in the United Kingdom, called “Online Retail II”, consisting of a total of 5,833 clients and 1,067,371 records; from which a process of characterization of the data is carried out, followed by the prediction of the monetary value of each client using statistical and machine learning techniques, which is subsequently included as a variable in the segmentation process. Finally, a comparison is made between the results of segmenting clients without incorporating the prediction of the monetary value and the segmentation of clients incorporating the prediction of the monetary value; thus it is concluded that the proposed method, using the closest neighbor algorithm for the prediction of the customer's monetary value, by incorporating it into the segmentation of clients, achieves an economic performance between 10% and 20% better than segmentation without incorporating this variable
