# Remote monitoring systems for the conservation of the Amazon rainforest: a systematic review

Amazonian rainforest plays a crucial role in stabilizing the global climate and is vital to preserving biodiversity, wildlife and indigenous cultures. Despite efforts to protect Amazonia, deforestation, wildlife trafficking, mining, oil exploitation and other extractive industries continue to threaten the region. Remote monitoring systems (RMS) are necessary to effectively detect and monitor these threats. This systematic review of technological initiatives to protect natural resources demonstrates the importance of technology in conservation and the need to continue research and development in such technologies. This article identifies the types of technologies used on different continents and highlights the challenges posed by the development of an MSY for Amazonia, including harsh environmental conditions and limited infrastructure. Early detection of threats could help authorities to take corrective action and avoid further environmental impact on the Amazonian forest
