# DISCIPLINING INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS IN LATIN AMERICA. CONTRIBUTIONS, LIMITS AND PARTICULARITIES.

This book explains the classical and emerging contributions that have emerged in Latin America for the field of International Relations (RI), and analyzes the limits of some of the approaches that have predominated in studies and academic production in the region. How has the discipline of RI developed in Latin America? What are its contributions to regional and global debate? What are the scope and limits of the approaches and concepts that have been applied in the region? These are some of the problems that authors explore in this work, pointing out the historical, sociopolitical and academic vicisitudes that have influenced the disciplinary construction
