# Effectiveness and safety of the use of probiotics as adjuvants in the eradication of Helicobacter pylori. Systematic review and meta-analysis

It has been proposed to add probiotics to triple and quadruple therapies to improve their effectiveness in the eradication of Helicobacter pylori, but there is controversy about their usefulness. Evaluating the effectiveness and safety of the adjuvant use of probiotics in triple or quadruple therapy for the eradication of H. pylori in adults. Systematic review and metaanalysis. A search was carried out in Embase, Ovid Medline, Cochrane Library and LILACS of randomized clinical trials (ECA) published in English or Spanish between January 2010 and May 2020 that evaluated the effectiveness and safety of using probiotics as adjuvant therapy in combination with triple or quadruple therapy in the eradication of H. pylori in adults.
