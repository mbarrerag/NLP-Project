# INFLUENCE OF TEMPERATURE AND HUMIDITY RELATING TO MECHANICAL RESISTANCE IN THE FIELD OF BAMBU PHYLLOSTACHYS AURE

This research evaluated the mechanical behavior of the species of bamboo Phyllostachys aurea, under two conditions of temperature and relative humidity (environment 1 and environment 2), with the aim of determining the influence of these conditions on the compressive resistance parallel to fibers. Following the guidelines present in the NTC 5525, a significant decrease in resistance in environment 1, characterized by a higher relative humidity and temperature compared to the environment 2, was observed. This reduction is approximately 22% with respect to the values recorded in the environment 2. In addition, it was identified that the presence of knots also decreases resistance. The results obtained for simulated environments were compared with the criteria established in the Colombian Construction Regulation Sismo Resistante - NSR10.
