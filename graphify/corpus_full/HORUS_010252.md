# RESILIENCE AND VULNERABILITY IN A PANDEMIC CONTEXT: A STATISTICAL ANALYSIS ON THE FOUR URBAN ARGENTINE URBAN ARGENTINE URBANS

The resilience, understood as the ability of a system to absorb impacts, is raised as a central concern in metropolitan areas: national and supranational institutions develop plans to treat it from the reduction of vulnerability. Therefore, this article focuses on the four Argentine urban agglomerates that exceed the million inhabitants: Gran Buenos Aires-Gran La Plata (AGBA-GLP), Gran Córdoba (AGC), Gran Rosario (AGR) and Gran Mendoza (AGM); the objective is to design an alternative of the Social Vulnerability Index to Disaster (IVSD) to calculate it from the first quarter of 2020 to the third of 2022 and thus to approximate its resilience. methodologically it was applied to the microdata of the permanent household survey (eph) of the national institute of statistics and censuses (indec). Given the three dimensions that make up the index (social, housing and economic), the housing is the one that presents the greatest heterogeneity among the four spaces being that access to network water and to cloacal service stand out in this regard.
