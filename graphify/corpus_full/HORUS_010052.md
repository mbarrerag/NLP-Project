# MODELIZATION, ANALYSIS AND ESTIMATION IN AN EIDEMIOLOGICAL MODEL WITH ALLEATORIAL PERTURBATIONS: A APPLICATION TO THE RESpitated INFECTIONS IN BOGOTÁ

The impact of air pollution on human health has become a research topic because of its effect on the respiratory system. Since air pollution levels in Bogotá, including particulate matter (PM10 and PM2.5), have increased significantly; it should be considered for the analysis of cases of Acute Respiratory Infection (ARI). This talk proposes a stochastic epidemiological model with random disturbances to study the dynamics of cases recorded by ARFs in Bogotá. Random disturbances in the model allow to include the impact of variations due to external factors, such as particulate matter: PM10 and PM2.5. The model parameters are estimated using a data update method
