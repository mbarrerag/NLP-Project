# LARGE TIME BEHAVIOUR OF A DISPERSIVE TIME-DELAY DYNAMICAL SYSTEM.

In this presentation, we examine the asymptotic behavior of the solution to higher order dis- persive systems with time delays, which are defined on the real line. Specifically, our goal is to prove the stabilization results of the solutions for a time-delayed higher-order dispersive system with a strong dissipative term. Thus, our main aim is to furnish sufficient conditions on the coefficients ¿,¿0 to have well- posedness of the model (1) and exponential decay estimates. Essentially, by imposing appro- priate assumptions on the time delay coefficients, we establish the exponential stability of the system in two distinct ways. Firstly, if the coefficient of the delay term is bounded from be- low by a positive constant, we employ the Lyapunov approach to demonstrate that the energy associated with the solution of the higher order dispersive system decays exponentially. Subse- quently, we extend this result to encompass cases where the coefficient of the undelayed feedback is also indefinite. Both scenarios are investigated for values of the exponent p within the interval [1, 2j), where 2j + 1 denotes the order of the dispersive system
