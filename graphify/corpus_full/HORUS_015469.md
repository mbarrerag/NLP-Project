# ANALYSIS ON SCIENTIFIC ACTIVITY RELATING TO PASSIVA CLIMATE STRATEGIES USED IN INVESTMENT: PART 1: BIBLE ANALYSIS.

To ensure food security, under the current and future scenario of population growth and resource scarcity for production, the agricultural sector has interesting challenges within which it is worth mentioning the development of innovative and sustainable techniques to increase food production.One of the most used practices is the production of food under greenhouses.However, in the tropical region this cultivation practice must be technified in order to provide plants with an optimal microclimate for growth and development.One of the main problems of greenhouses used in countries of the tropical region is their low technological level and low energy efficiency, which prevents developing heating and cooling work conventionally. However, in recent years in countries such as China, Turkey, United Kingdom, Spain, among others, strategies have been developed to conduct climatization activities without the use of fossil fuels. Therefore, this work aims to carry out through bibliometric techniques the analysis of scientific information published between 2005 and 2021.
