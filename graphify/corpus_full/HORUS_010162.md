# GENERATING THE FUTURE, ACTING THIS: ACTUAL DILEMS AND CHALLENGES OF LATIN AMERICAN FEMINISM

This article examines the dilemmas and challenges faced by Latin American feminisms in the paradoxical current situation, in which, on the one hand, feminism, in all its diversity, occupies a prominent place as one of the most important and dynamic social and political forces of the subcontinent; and on the other hand, it faces strong external and internal tensions due to a strong onslaught by conservative, anti-right and fundamentalist groups and internal polarizations around the very objectives of the feminist project. To this end, I intend to describe the characteristics of these contemporary feminisms that are actively intervening in the political conjunctures of each country of the region, demanding an end to violence against women in all its manifestations, the legalization of abortion, and respect for their labour, territorial and economic rights. Secondly, I will refer to the challenges faced by these feminisms in a context in which a strong reactionary counter-offensive has been generated against some achievements in terms of equality and the sexual and reproductive rights of women and the LGBTQI+ population
