# Institutional framework of Private Public Association for university housing projects in Colombia. Case National University of Colombia.

The university housing contributes to the reduction of student drop-out, well-being and quality of life of university students; however, the current coverage is not enough, the present research has a theoretical proposal of Institutional Framework for the development of university housing projects at the National University of Colombia (UNAL) through Public Private Associations (APP) to contribute to the solution of this problem. The proposal is developed by reviewing literature, regulations and supporting documents in force
