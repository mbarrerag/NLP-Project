# Case study for optimisation of load-distract scheme in power systems with generation penetration with FNCER

This document presents the case study for the optimization of the energy dispatch contemplating contingencies of type n − 1 that may involve load-distracting schemes for the electrical system of normalized power in the 118 node model of the IEEE, integrating the behavior of non-conventional renewable energy sources (FNCER) and involving diversified energy demands (different actors and types of load). The proposed solution is aimed at minimizing the costs of generating traditional and non-traditional renewable sources, and at assessing the risk in the form of penalties to establish a burden-distracting scheme that includes the different operational constraints. A methodology is proposed that establishes the input variables of the system, which will enter into the mathematical model as operational restrictions, modelling criteria and evaluation criteria. Additionally, it is proposed through a meta-healthy algorithm (PSO) the optimization of the adjusted model, and provides criteria for establishing a de-straction scheme that can be implemented in real electrical systems.
