# SCHOOL DESERVATION RISK SENSOR: A CASE OF DETECTION STUDY IN THE LEVELS OF SECENTARY BASIC, MEDIA AND CLEI IN IEBS

Has education always been the most powerful tool to promote social mobility and build equity? (MEN, 2019) According to DANE, Colombia has the following dropout rates: 3.6% - 2016; 3.5% - 2017; 3.2% - 2018; 3.2% - 2019 and 3.3% - 2020. To this we ask, why is it important to detect behaviors or deserting behaviors in students? The purpose of this paper is to show how to detect the risk of school dropouts in IEBS by identifying the most influential factors and implementing data mining techniques
