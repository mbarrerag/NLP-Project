# --Political-economic pathways of progressive governments of the second wave in Latin America (2018-2022). Cases from Mexico, Argentina and Bolivia

-In the beginning of the third decade of the new millennium, the changing political landscape in Our America suggests the need to make a renewed and updated balance regarding the relations of state and governmental power and social forces, analyzing the political perspectives, the political terms and their respective execution and trajectories registered during the last years in the regional political economy. Among the explanatory elements of the open nature of the political field in Our America are positioned both the impossibility of stabilizing the social order crossed by different crises and the permanent search for scenarios for its resolution, either with favorable results for the dominant or popular sectors.
