# Molecular characterisation of pathogens associated with cases of acute undifferentiated febrile disease in Leticia and Villavicencio

The undifferentiated acute febrile syndrome (EFAI) refers to a set of diseases that, in addition to fever, manifest with symptoms such as headache, general malaise, lack of appetite, among others, which makes diagnosis difficult. These diseases may be associated with viral, bacterial and parasitic infections, so their differentiation requires the use of specialized techniques that are not accessible in the most vulnerable regions. In this work, multiplex PCR molecular detection strategies for viruses such as dengue, zika, chikunkuña and oropouche, bacteria such as Leptospira spp, Rickettsia spp, Anaplasma spp, Brucella spp and Bartonella spp parasites were designed and validated. Samples of febrile patients of Leticia and Villavicencio were sampled between January 2021 and May 2022, using a strategy of pools.
