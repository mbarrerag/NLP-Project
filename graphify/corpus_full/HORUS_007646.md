# Study of the potential of macromycet fungi as control agents of avocado and maracuyá phytopathogenic fungi grown in Arauca.

The department of Arauca, located in the nororeiente of Colombia, is characterized by its rich biodiversity and its agricultural potential.However, the crops of avocado and maracuyá in this region face several challenges, among which the fungal diseases occupy a predominant position.These pathologies not only reduce the productivity of the crops, but also affect the quality of the fruits, generating significant economic losses for local farmers.The problems related to the control of phytopathogenic fungi, such as Colletotrichum gloeosporioides and Fusarium oxysporum, affect these crops and negatively impact on their production and quality.The traditional use of chemical fungicides presents disadvantages, such as environmental pollution and the development of resistance in pathogens, which underlines the need to explore sustainable alternatives for disease control. In this context, the study of the potential of macromicates as agents of biological control offers a promising perspective. These fungi produce bioactive metabolites that can inhibit the growth of phytopathogens, which is presented as a viable solution., preparing a new generation of scientists with essential skills to face challenges in agriculture and in the development of sustainable solutions
