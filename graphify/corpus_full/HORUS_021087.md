# Why do organizations tend to address the creation of new products without sufficient exploration, and what can be done to motivate divergence and exploration?

The exercises of divergence are carried out at the beginning of any design process, these exercises include the realization of interviews, experiments, tests, test with prototypes, investigation in databases and different dynamics for the ideation and alignment of equipment, among others. To carry out these activities allows, in essence, to obtain information and evidence to take informed decisions, this in order to reduce the uncertainty and risk associated with the phase of production, development and subsequent insertion in the market. However, it happens frequently, that this phase -the divergent phase-, is eliminated from the process or does not invest in it the time and resources necessary. This happens in multiple organizations, whether private, public or not for profit and, in the long run becomes one of the main causes for the failure of the enterprises, who intra-enterprises and initiatives for new products and services.
