# Inflexibility of public spending in Colombia: an approach from budget praxis

The studies on the budgetary inflexibility for the Colombian case usually adopt a normative and legal approach product of the nature of this phenomenon. However, Colombian legislation has shown that the inclusion of budgetary allocations within the Budget Law corresponds ultimately to the discretion of the National Government, despite the existence of laws that establish the system of compulsory expenditure compliance. Therefore, conclusions of previous studies on inflexibility have made explicit the need to carry out an analysis from the budget praxis. Thus, the aim of this document is to make a statistical estimate of the inflexibility of public expenditure that determines the variability of the temporary expenditure series during the period 1997-2020. To this end, the legal, sectoral and functional classifications of public expenditure are taken as the main points of study, in accordance with the budgetary classification manuals predefined by the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit. The results found state that, from the statistical point of view, it is possible to corroborate the conclusions found from the normative and legal approach that establish the existence of inflexibility of the operating expenses. The objective of this study is to estimate the inflexibility of public planning using statistical techniques to determine the variability of the time series of waiting during the period 1997-2020. The legal, sectoral, and functional classes of public waiting are taking as study techniques, in agreement with the budget classification manuals predefined by the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit. The results established that, from the statistical point of view, it is possible to corroborate the conclusion of the objective classification manuals predefined by the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit.
