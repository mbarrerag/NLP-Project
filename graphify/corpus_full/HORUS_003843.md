# Illustrated Guide to the Islamic and Historical Science of Jose Quatrecasa

This is a tribute to the memory of the illustrious Spanish botanist José Cuatrecasas Arumí, one of the most prolific botanists in the world during the second half of the twentieth century and who made great contributions to the knowledge of the neotropical flora and, of course, to the Colombian flora. The permanent motivation to elaborate this guide has always been the low value towards the Xiloteca collection and its scant dissemination as a scientific work that, carried out between 1943 and 1946, has rested about 70 years in oblivion. The aim, therefore, is to inspire pride and sense of belonging in all the estates of the university community and in the people interested in wood plants. This impressive collection is composed of 1078 samples and this book is part of the efforts to update their state of art, with sufficient information to allow to dimension the diversity of trees and shrubs producing wood, and to encourage the production of new works and publications. All this in order to deepen the knowledge of native species, generate strategies for their conservation, preservation and sustainable use, and contribute with the construction of environmental thinking focused on the valuation and respect for our country
