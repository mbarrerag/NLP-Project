# AUDENT INOXICATION BY ACETAMINOFEN IN PRIMARY ATTENTION

Acetaminophen is a pain medication and antipyretic considered safe and effective. Because of this, in Colombia it is over-the-counter and widely used in both children and adults. Due to its easy access, acetaminophen poisoning is a frequent event, so primary care physicians should have knowledge about its diagnosis and management, in addition to risk factors for the development of liver failure and the indications to refer patients to specialized services. The purpose of this article is to guide primary health care staff about the pathophysiology, diagnosis and treatment of this poisoning
