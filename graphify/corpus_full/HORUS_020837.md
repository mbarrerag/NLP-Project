# Linking a vulnerable community in Santander department to the technological development cycle of a passive solar dehydration system of plant-based food matrices

In different territories of Colombia it is frequent to find problems associated with the lack of technicalization of processes of adaptation and transformation of food, which, together with the lack of appropriate communication routes to facilitate the mobility of food, causes a high volume of losses of products and reduced useful life times, affecting a vast amount of rural population that sees limited its possibility to obtain income and, consequently, lives permanently under conditions of social vulnerability and paradoxically, under a constant threat of food insecurity.This problem leads to the need to develop alternatives for the generation of strategies of accompaniment and exchange of experiences with this vulnerable population.The complex social environment, together with the lack of infrastructure and a near total state abandonment, has led to that despite the fact that in the department of Santander there are productive areas of high agricultural value, it does not necessarily result in an agro-industrial progress.Thanks to the association of the University, through social innovation and participation of the university, with a participation of the university, with a participation of the university, with a participation of the university, with a participation of the university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with a university, with LOTO 2016-2018 SECOND COHORTE. For this purpose, interaction processes will be carried out between the company Apiarios Los Cerezos, the Association Apisy, the Mayor of Galán and the members of the University with a view to dimensioning, designing, constructing and starting up the passive solar dehydrator in the sidewalk of the Cavellinas of the municipality of Galán, with a view to strengthening the productive capacity of the association, based on the results of previous experiences in the department of Boyacá, which allow to evaluate the aptitude for drying different plant matrices and develop possibilities of generating value for their commercialization. Thus, the proposal combines efforts between the academy, the community and the private sector in the search for an innovative solution to the problem described above, through a strategy that can be applied later to different associations of the agro-food sector and in different territories of the country
