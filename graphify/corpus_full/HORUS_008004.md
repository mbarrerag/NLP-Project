# RESOURCE AND REDUCTION OF FOOD DESPERCES AND DISPERDICIES.

The losses and waste of food (PDA) have been gaining visibility in the Latin American academic and political agenda as a major problem, with serious environmental, ethical, social and economic repercussions, but in turn policies, strategies and actions are being generated to contribute to its reduction and incidence. The Master's Degree in Sovereignty and Food and Nutrition Security of the National University of Colombia has not been foreign to this situation and since its creation in 2015 has been studying the topic since the research carried out by students and professors of various disciplines, analyzing its causes, repercussions and alternatives for the reduction of PDA in different spaces, including the losses generated in the production and distribution processes of papa criolla in Nariño and Cundinamarca and vegetables in the Central Zone of Chile, as well as the waste that occurs in the largest public educational institution in Bogotá and in two educational institutions in the capital of Arauca. The learning obtained through participatory research processes reflects the experiences and perceptions of those who relate to the PDAs from the production, distribution and consumption of food, and contribute new looks and strategies to achieve their reduction and management in a sustainable way
