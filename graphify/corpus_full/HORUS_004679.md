# Sociomoral Reason for Failure to comply with the Law in a Context of Illegality, Violence and Distinguished Presence of the State

The failure to comply with the law is a phenomenon that has moral implications due to the way in which it can affect the well-being of people. This study is framed in the Theory of the Specific Domains of Social Knowledge and sought to examine the psychological judgments and the sociomoral judgments, as well as the judgments of attribution of causes and gravity and punishment on four situations of transgression to the law: drug trafficking, environmental damage by illegal mining, use of firearms and lynching to a thief, by 96 people (M = 29.8 years) originating from a municipality marked by illegality and differentiated presence of the State. The population was divided into three age groups (14 to 17 years M = 16,03 years, 21 to 30 years M = 25,4 years of and 40 to 55 years M = 47,2 years). It was counted with a quantitative design and a semi-structured interview. The analysis of data was given through ANOVAS and MANOVAS as well as through simple linear regression models.
