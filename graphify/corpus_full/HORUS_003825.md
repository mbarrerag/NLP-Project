# DOCUMENTATION BY OPPOSES OF STRENGTHENING DISAPPEARANCE AND THE VIDEO CRIMINAL PERUAN INSTALLATION (2019) OF MAYA WATANABE

The aim of this presentation is to analyze how the video installation of the Peruvian Liminal (2019), by Maya Watanabe, discovers that the documentation of enforced disappearance is carried out, fundamentally, by the encounter of opposites. As this crime is usually visible with strategies that, being normalized, do not incite reaction in the spectator, this analysis is relevant because it points to other proposals to provoke reactions in those who see. With an analytical process in this presentation, initially, a historical contextualization of enforced disappearance is realized in Peru. Subsequently, it explains what is an artistic documentary that deals with violence and is presented Liminal. Next, we analyze how this work is constructed from the encounter of opposites, of which three are considered: the absence of the disappeared in front of the presence of his relative (who speaks for his absence); a body without a name (N.N.) against a name without a body (who inhabits the memory of his family); and the space of exhumation confronted with the expository space. It is concluded that the resistance against forced disappearance must continue from different fronts, such as art or theory, to try to eradicate it
