# Evaluation of the efficacy of non-invasive mechanical ventilation vs medical devices with PEEP valves in patients with acute lung oedema of cardiogenic origin: systematic review of the literature.

To evaluate the efficacy and safety of medical devices with PEEP valves compared to non-invasive mechanical ventilation in adult patients with acute lung edema of cardiogenic origin. We carried out a systematic review of the literature in the main databases as of January 2022, identifying 172 references. After eliminating the duplicates and reviewing the references lists of the articles we obtained full text articles for 6 references. Finally, we included 3 studies with a total of 419 participants treated with non-invasive mechanical ventilation V medical devices with PEEP valves for cardiogenic pulmonary edema. The included studies reported results that included the need for endotracheal intubation and mortality, suggesting that, in the management of acute cardiogenic pulmonary edema, therapy with medical devices with PEEP valves presented a similar result compared to noninvasive mechanical ventilation
