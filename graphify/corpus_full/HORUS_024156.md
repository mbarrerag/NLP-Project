# Relationship between happiness at work and job performance: a bibliometric analysis, evolution and trends

The purpose of this article was to perform a bibliometric analysis of the thesis of the happy worker and productive (Happy-Productive Worker -HPW-), and identify its evolution and trends, with in order to recognize the progress of the literature on the relationship between happiness at work and work performance, the authors, countries and representative documents, in addition to the clusters relevant, which has been identified as a need for scholars of the organizations. The analyses were performed with the use of sci2 software, gephi, vosviewer and bibliometrix. It was found that, to date, 404 papers have been published in Web of Science and 532 in Scopus. Among the most representative results are: countries (States United States and Spain), universities (Valencia and California), journals (Journal Happiness Studies and Frontiers in Psychology), authors at Scopus (Sonja Lyubomirsky) and at Wos (Andrés Salas). As the main contribution, it was identified that in the literature four clusters stand out in the study from the HPW: international happiness and the economy; happiness in life and performance; happiness, engagement and job performance; and emerging approaches in the relationship between happiness and performance, coinciding in these, as the most studied construct of happiness, the satisfaction in life and work
