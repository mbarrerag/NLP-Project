# ASSOCIATION BETWEEN THE FOOD SECURITY LEVEL AND THE NUTRITIONAL STATE OF THE BINOMIO MADRE-SON TO ASSIST TO A MASTER-KANGUR PROGRAMME

The nutritional status of an individual reflects the state of health and the level of food security. Currently, there is no evidence on the relationship between food security and the nutritional status of the mother-child binomial in Mother Kangaroo programs in Colombia, being a need of great importance for the optimization of care for this population. Determine the association between the level of food security according to the ELCSA scale and the nutritional status of the mother-child binomial in the Mother-of-Native Program of a hospital in Bogotá, Colombia, during the period of time between August and November 2019. Observational descriptive research of cross-sectional cut. binomials attended in the mother-nature program of the university hospital méderi between september and november 2019. We can evaluate sociodemographic variables collected through surveys; the food safety program was evaluated using the ELCSA scale and nutritional status was determined from the assessment of the weight for age, size and weight for the children; in mothers we have evaluated the multiple percentage of the number of births (IMC) and we have performed the nutritional status
