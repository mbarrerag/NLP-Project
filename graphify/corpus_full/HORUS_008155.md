# On SL(2 , C) -representations of torus knot groups

The aim of this paper is to study the algebraic structure of the space R(Γ n,m) of representations of the torus knot groups, Γ n,m= 〈 x, y: xn= ym〉 , into the linear special group SL(2 , C)
