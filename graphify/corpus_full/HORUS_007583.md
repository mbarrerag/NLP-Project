# Identification of physical and chemical quality characteristics of sheep meat and aspects of its consumption in the department of Boyacá

At present, there is exponential growth in the population at the world level, which translates into a significant demand for food, obtained from effective and sustainable productive systems, due to the reality of climate change, and which at the same time are products with optimal nutritional and quality characteristics to meet the respective basic needs (FAO, 2009; UN, 2022). In turn the OECD (Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development), publishes figures related to the consumption of different types of meat in the world, mentioning a global average of 34.1 kg/person/year, including consumptions of pork, beef, poultry and lamb. Taking into account these data, sheep meat is positioned with the lowest consumption with 1.8 kg/person/year, representing 5.3% compared to the other types of meat (OECD, 2021). On the other hand, it is important to recognize the changes in the trends of the consumer versus the quality of the food, associated with different social, cultural and economic patterns, focused mainly on the search for healthy and sustainable food that represents positive contributions in their consumption (Cubas, 2023). sheep in a potential resource for income and food security for small producers, and even in a significant source of nutrients for the human diet (Lorenzo et al., 2019; Moreno & Grajales, 2017). At the national level, per capita consumption of sheep meat is at 500 g, according to estimates by the Organisation of the Ovino-Caprina National Production Chain, occupying the last place of consumption compared to other sources of protein of animal origin such as chicken, beef, pork and fish (Fedegan, 2023; ministry of agriculture and rural development, 2020). However, it is worth noting that this figure is overvalued taking into account the data reported by the ACI related to sheep inventory in the country (ICA, 2023)
