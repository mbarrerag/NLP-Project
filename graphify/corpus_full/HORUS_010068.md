# Factors associated with mortality in patients over 60 years of age hospitalized with SARS CoV2 infection in the Central Military Hospital between March 2020 and March 2021.

The elderly have been one of the demographic groups most affected by the arrival of the pandemic of COVID-19. Therefore, they represent a challenge in clinical care by the different health teams. The objective of this study is to determine the factors associated with intrahospital mortality in patients over 60 years of age, assessed by the Geriatric Service, who had infection with SARS-CoV-2 and were hospitalized in the Central Military Hospital between March 2020 and March 2021. Cross-sectional study with analytical component that involved patients over 60 years of age with diagnosis of infection SARS-CoV-2 by specific test. A descriptive analysis of all the variables involved in addition was performed a bivariate analysis whose dependent variable was mortality. For all of the above, the use of a non-conditional regression model for dependent variable (OR 4.44 p< 0.001) and the association with the presence of intrahospital mortality was performed
