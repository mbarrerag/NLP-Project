# EXISTENCES OF ORGANIC CARBON IN KIKUYO PAST CULTIATED SOUNDS IN THE NORTH OF ANTIOCIA

The bovine livestock systems are considered to be emitters of greenhouse gases. However, some of its components such as soil, allow carbon capture. The aim of this work was to compare carbon stocks in soils grown with kikuyo pasture for several years and soils with forest or helechal coverings under different geoforms, in farms of northern Antioquia. A design was used in randomized blocks, in which the blocks corresponded to the type of coverage of plants. The treatments in factorial arrangement 3x5, comprised the geoforms and the samplings. In the model the effects of the geoforms (concave, convex and plane) were considered, type of coverage of plants (kikuyo, forest and helechal) and depth of sampling (0-20, 20-40, 40-60, 60-80 and 80-100 cm. The analysis of variation for variables: amount of carbon in soil and fine and thick roots, stems, leaves, stoles and dead biomass; soil and pH of the soil were.
