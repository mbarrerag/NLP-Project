# IMPACT PROTOCOL TO INTERVENTION: WHERE PATIENT FAMILY CAREERS ARE IMPACTED IN IMPROVING THE QUALITY OF LIFE, ADOPTION OF THE ROLE AND SOCIAL SUPPORT

With the increase of the overall burden of cancer due to the support of the number of cases and deaths, the need for palliative care has increased over time, taking as a consequence that countries like Colombia are faced with a challenge due to the low integration of these interventions into the health system. Under this scenario, the family caregivers of these patients have been affected by the few interventions focused on them, reflecting changes in their quality of life due to the abandonment they receive in the exercise of their roles as caregivers. Currently there is a randomized controlled trial in phase ll, in which the viability, acceptability of the intervention called Caregivers PalliActives is verified in the caregivers of patients with cancer. According to this background this study is intended to estimate the effect of nursing intervention. Caregivers PaliActives¿ in the improvement of the quality of life, adoption of the role and increase of social support of family caregivers of patients with palliative care in a health institution in Medellín-Colombia. This is a controlled and randomized clinical trial in a hospital of high complexity in the city of Medellín-Colombia, during the year 2021 and three patients. Currently, the evidence has not documented studies of this type in the national context, there is a record of a preliminary evaluation of the intervention Caregivers PaliActives in a phase II clinical trial. A recent study of becque et al. (2019) suggests that further nursing interventions are required for family caregivers of patients with palliative care cancer, as those currently available still require further development
