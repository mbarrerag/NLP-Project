# Assessment of the potential of bioprospecting of natural resources for the discovery of new therapeutic agents in public health

The growing threats to public health, such as chronic diseases, resistant infections and the scarcity of new treatments, highlight the urgency of innovative solutions. In this context, Colombia, as one of the most biodiverse countries in the world, offers a huge potential for Bioprospecting, defined as the systematic exploration of biodiversity in search of bioactive compounds with therapeutic applications. This field encompasses economic, social, ethical and political dimensions, regulated by regulations such as the Convention on Biological Diversity and the Nagoya Protocol. Bioprospecting in Colombia can boost the development of innovative therapies and economic growth, provided that principles of equity in the distribution of benefits are respected and the conservation of biodiversity is prioritized. This study assessed the potential of the Bioprospecting of natural resources as a strategy to discover new therapeutic agents in public health. By means of a bibliographic review and qualitative analysis with codification tools, relevant studies were examined, comparing terrestrial and marine bioprospecting, and reviewing global and local regulatory frameworks. The results identified promising anti-microbial, anti-inflammatory and anti-cancer compounds with successful cases in the Amazon and marine ecosystems.
