# COMPASION PRACTICES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE DISEASES IN COLOMBIA

to recognize the practices of compassion and their obstacles by administrative nurses in Colombia. a mixed study of a cross-sectional, descriptive, exploratory and international type with an online survey applied in 17 countries. Data from Colombia were extracted with a sample of 69 administrative nurses. Quantitative data were analyzed with descriptive statistics and qualitative data were processed with the Nvivo program and with a thematic analysis. 90% women, mainly from the hospital area (40%). The practice of compassion in administrative nurses involves four categories: listening; defending and recognizing the individuality of the staff; defining the practice of compassion, needs and advantages; receiving compassion and complementary opinions of compassion and administration in which the influence of cultural and organizational patterns is presented as obstacles to compassion. compassion for nursing demonstrates the intentional ability to recognize the uniqueness of the suffering of the other, to understand their needs, to provide support and to find solutions from the understanding of the sense of the human. These aspects require significant changes at the organizational level and of health legislation to mitigate the obstacles in order to offer compassion
