# EQUUS ASINUS

The reproductive biotechs constitute a series of tools for the conservation and use of biological material of species whose population is at risk. The objective of this research was to evaluate the use of inductors of the spermatic training in asus cryopreserved semen with asss. Five Colombian cryollolo-gas asses were collected, located in Antioquia (Colombia), with ages between 4 and 8 years. Three ejax per animal by artificial vagina were obtained. The semen was diluted in Equiplus® with 5% of dimethylformamide and 5% of egg yome, and was conventionally frozen. Two straws of each asne were defro and ejacated in water at 37 °C for 30 seconds, the volume obtained was divided into eight aliquots of 125 μL of semen that were separately supplemented with pentoxifyfil (PTX) to 2.5, 5.0 and 7.5 mM, ethyldiamino-tetraacetic acid (EDTA) to 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 mM, a positive control with heparin (HEP) to be replaced (HEP) to be replaced and control separately, and control (PTP) to 1.1 mBBP) frozen and thawed
