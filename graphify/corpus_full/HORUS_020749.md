# Study of permissible compression and cutting efforts parallel to the fibers of the Phyllostachys aurea bamboo from the municipality of Pacho Cundinamarca

Taking into account the large number of bamboo species in the world and the research that has been developed in terms of their behaviour and their different uses in the pharmaceutical, cosmetic, food and construction industries (The Faculty of Pharmaceutical Chemistry et al., n.d.), we can see that the bamboo industry is in growth and this could mean high income for countries that know how to direct and exploit their crops. Since their costs are low bamboo is an accessible material for all social classes unlike steel, which is currently the most used material at structural level in the country. In this research we determine the permissible efforts of compression parallel to the fiber and cut parallel to the fiber of the Bambú Phyllostachys aurea coming from the Municipality of Pacho, Cundinamarca, Colombia.
