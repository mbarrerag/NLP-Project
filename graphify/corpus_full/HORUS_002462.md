# The influence of ICT projects on Bogotá’s urban development

This research analyses the influence of housing projects promoted by the Institute of Territorial Credit (ICT) on the urban configuration of Bogotá during the 1960s. Through a bibliographic review, documentary analysis and reconstruction of historical cartography, eight housing projects of the ICT were characterized and their impact on the urban structure of the city. The urban plans of the period were geo-referenced to assess their contribution to urban growth by road accessibility and the provision of public space. The results indicate that between 1960 and 1970 the Institute built 31,216 dwellings, covering 31.5 of the urban deficit of the period. Larger projects were located in peripheral areas attracting other developments and contributing to the growth of road infrastructure. It is concluded that the projects of the Institute were fundamental in the urban expansion of Bogotá. The development dynamics during this period continue to affect the current urban structure, showing the importance of a more balanced planning in future housing policies .
