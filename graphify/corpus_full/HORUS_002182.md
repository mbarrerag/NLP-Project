# Solubility determination and thermodynamic analysis of amlodipine besylate in non-aqueous mixtures of polyethylene glycol 400 and 1-propanol from 293.2 K to 313.2 K

The quantification and analysis of amlodipine besylate (ADB) solubility, a compound with limited solubility utilised for hypertension management, is of paramount significance in pharmaceutical development. The present investigation ascertained the solubility profile of ADB within a non-aqueous binary solvent of polyethylene glycol 400 (PEG-400) and 1-propanol across a temperature range of 293.2 to 313.2 K, employing the shake-flask technique. The data delineated a non-linear augmentation in ADB solubility concomitant with the PEG-400 mass fraction increment. Solubility data points were proficiently interpolated using the van’t Hoff, Yalkowsky, Jouyban-Acree and Jouyban-Acree-van’t models. Apparent thermodynamic parameters pertinent to dissolution and solvation phenomena, inclusive of enthalpy, Gibbs free energy and entropy, were elucidated via Gibbs and van’t Hoff equations to explicate the solvation dynamics of ADB in the PEG-400 and 1-propanol systems
