# PREOPERATIVE BRACHYTHERAPY FOR EARLY-STAGE CERVICAL CANCER: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND META-ANALYSIS

To assess the rate of pathological response rate, and the oncological outcomes of preoperative brachytherapy (PBT) in early-stage cervical cancer. A systematic literature review was conducted according to the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and meta-analyses (PRISMA) statement. MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials, and Scopus databases were searched from inception until April 2022. Only English and French articles were included
