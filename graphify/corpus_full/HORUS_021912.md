# Market risk in foreign exchange options bank portfolios.

The Basel regulation of FRTB for risk management in the banking industry comes into effect in 2023. It poses new challenges in terms of implementation, risk quantification, and reserve capital impacts. This paper proposes a methodology to quantify the risk metrics specified shortfall, ES, and risk value, VaR, of a portfolio of currency options within the framework of the internal models of FRTB, choosing as a portfolio valuation strategy a traditional model, a model with stochastic rate, or a hybrid model with stochastic rate and volatility. The methodology defines adaptations of risk metrics, the generation of stress scenarios, and specifies the mathematical mechanism to integrate portfolio valuation with such metrics. The methodology is implemented through three applications for a portfolio of options on GBP/USD, impacts on capital are investigated, and the performance of the VaR metric is evaluated by back-testing tests. In each development, it is shown that the methodology presented is appropriate, contributes to scientific literature, and can be scaled up as a technological tool
