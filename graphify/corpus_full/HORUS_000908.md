# INTERDISCIPLINARY, TRANSDISCIPLINARY, MULTIDISCIPLINARY AND ANTIDISCIPLINARY.

The disciplines, as plots of knowledge, possess their logics and processes based on modern rationality that favours analytical thinking and fragments reality for their understanding. We have now realized that this attitude has put the planet on the verge of collapse and the human species in danger of self-destruction. Faced with a present in permanent and accelerated change, which imposes unprecedented challenges, it is necessary to project the formation of our professionals and postgraduates towards an attitude of cultural, epistemological and ethical openness that allows to confront problems and to propose solutions overcoming the positivist and exclusionary vision of the field of knowledge on which the traditional formation of each profession or occupation has been based and goes beyond an interdisciplinary dialogue or transdisciplinary work to adopt an anti-disciplinary posture that allows to approach and interact with reality from multiple angles. In this chapter, the hypothesis of a professional formation that attends to that way of seeing education to contribute to the construction of a respectful, friendly and sustainable world, breaking limits and conventional positions to offer to the wise middle of great heart
