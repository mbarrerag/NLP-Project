# THE IMPACT OF EVANGELICAL MOVEMENTS ON RECOGNITION OF LGBTI RIGHTS IN LATIN AMERICA

In theory, Latin America is one of the most advanced regions of the world in terms of the recognition of LGBTI rights (Corrales 2019; López Sánchez 2021). According to data from the Observatory of Political Reforms in Latin America (1978-2022), more than 65 initiatives related to the legal recognition of civil, social, political and sexual rights of persons of sexual diversity have been implemented in the last 20 years; however, these normative changes have not been exempt from controversy. While feminist movements, LGBTI groups and other progressive social sectors have been conquering rights and mainstreaming in public gender policies, evangelical, Catholic and other conservative movements have reacted against laws, judicial decisions and public policies that recognize egalitarian marriage, homoparental adoption and comprehensive sexual education, among others (Biroli and Caminotti 2020: 1; Corrales 2019). Thus, the present presentation raises the following question: what has been the impact of evangelical movements on the recognition of LGBTI rights in Latin America?
