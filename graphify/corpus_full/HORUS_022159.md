# Thyroid cancer: clinical pathological characteristics in a series of cases at the National University Hospital 2017-2020.

To describe in a population with diagnosis of thyroid cancer, pathological characteristics related to biological behavior and risk of recurrence. The cases with histopathological diagnosis of thyroid carcinoma were included between 2017 and 2020, whose pathology reports were reviewed to gather information regarding sociodemographic and pathological variables associated with recurrence. Data were recorded and frequency measures were quantified for the sample in general and with subsamples: microcarcinomas and tumors greater than 1 centimeter.
