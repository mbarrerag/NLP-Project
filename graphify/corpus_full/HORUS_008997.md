# Synopsis of the genus Parablechnum (Blechnaceae) for Colombia

An overview of the species of the genus Parablechnum growing in Colombia is presented. A review of the material deposited in several herbariums and the images available online is made. The genus is represented by 18 species, representing 45% of the American species. These species are distributed mainly in the Andean region from 800 to 3800 m of altitude. Two new species, P. givalum and P. imbricatum, are described and lectotypes are designated for four species. A key to determine Colombian species is presented and their diagnostic characteristics are described for each species
