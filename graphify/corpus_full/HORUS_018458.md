# CHARACTERISATION OF THE PRODUCTIVE SYSTEM OF MEAT WINNERING IN THE SUBREGION OF ANTI-QUEÑO URABA

The Anti-Oqueño Uraba represents a territorial extension of 11,664 km2, 18.6% of the total of the department and thanks to its geographical location is positioned as a fundamental territory in the processes of economic integration between the Caribbean, the Andean region, and the west of the country. Indeed, the case of the Anti-Oqueño Uraba is one of the most successful in the area of business development in the country, due to the consolidation of a strong agro-industrial sector with the capacity to produce and market its products. The information used for the presentation of the following results originated from a survey carried out between August 2019 and July 2020 with visits to 55 farms located in the subregion of Urabá of the department of Antioquia
