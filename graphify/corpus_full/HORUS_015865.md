# NETWORKS OF GOVERNANCE AND CONFLICT FOR SUSTAINABILITY OF SOCIO-ECOSYSTEMS WITH TOURISTIC APPROACHMENT IN A COLOMBIAN NATURAL PARK

The paper is part of the preliminary results of the doctoral research on the challenges of the actors of governance in the sustainability of the socio-ecosystems with tourist use (PNNN). This presentation was limited to this case study, where field data were collected. The survey, interview and focus group were used as data collection tools; the data were analyzed with the tools Nvivo and Ucinet. The NNN is a reserve of 61,388 ha, between the 2,600 and 5,321 MSN, where various activities can be enjoyed. The communities are directly or indirectly involved in the development of tourism that operates in the Park, which receives approximately 55,000 visitors per year.
