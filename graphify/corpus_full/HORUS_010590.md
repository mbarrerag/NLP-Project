# THE CONTRIBUTION OF PSYCHOANALYSIS AND PHENOMENOLOGY TO PSYCHIATRIC SEMIOLOGY

This chapter describes important contributions of psychoanalysis, phenomenology and semiology to psychiatry in the 20th century, continuing a story that began in the article "The emergence of psychiatric semiology during the era of revolution" (2016).The first part examines the influence of psychoanalysis, while the second part examines the key contributions of phenomenology to descriptive psychopathology, where new subjective categories make up the semiological field
