# Preliminary study of subsea slide susceptibility in the Colombian Caribbean

In Colombia, multiple studies of stability of slopes and analysis of susceptibility, threat and risk due to mass removal processes in continental areas have been carried out, but few studies of stability in underwater reliefs are carried out. In order to better understand the general conditions of stability in these environments, this research carried out a preliminary study of susceptibility to underwater landslides in a sector called the Sinu Offshore Basin, opposite the coasts of the Colombian Caribbean, where basic information is available, since the exploration of hydrocarbons is projected in an area of 15,530 km2. The study is carried out at a scale of 1:50,000 and has as its basis the model of digital elevation, geology, geomorphology and the density of faults in the region. In view of the lack of detailed information, a geological mapping based on stratographic and structural data, supplemented by a single analysis of constructual weight, five units and the analysis is integrated to establish statistical relationships with conditioning factors, allowing to identify areas with greater susceptibility to instability.
