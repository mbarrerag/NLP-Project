# LUMI-FI: Informative Light, Transforming Light

The LUMI-FI project focuses on the development of an innovative luminaire that uses Communication by Visible Light (VLC) technology to transmit data efficiently, safely and without needing internet connection. This luminaire not only improves lighting in environments such as airports and public transport stations, but also provides crucial information in real time to users, improving user mobility and experience. In addition, the luminaire adapts to the Circadian cycle, promoting a healthier and more comfortable environment. The main objective of this project is to raise the level of technological maturity (TRL) of our prototype from TRL 3 to TRL 5, ensuring that it is ready for validation testing in relevant environments. To achieve this, we will focus on optimising design, integrating advanced technologies and conducting comprehensive testing. At the end of the project, we hope to have a ready-to-implementation luminaire in real environments, offering a revolutionary solution for lighting and data transmission in public spaces
