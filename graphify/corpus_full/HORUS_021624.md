# Graphic language as teaching strategy and learning the concept mixes.

Chemistry today continues to assume a complexity which is part of one of the difficulties with regard to teaching and learning in classrooms (Torres, 1994), however, this event opens up spaces for teachers to experiment and play with the classic and modern, generating new strategies that achieve significant advances in the educational theme (Mendoza et al., 2009). Without ignoring the challenges presented by natural sciences and sociocultural aspects that shelter Colombia, a didactic strategy was designed in which the graphic language is implemented as a main tool to improve and transform the previous knowledge regarding the concept mixes. The research was carried out in the educational institution José María Falla, located in the municipality of El Dovio – Valle del Cauca, with 22 students of grade 5 of primary school, at the María Inmaculada headquarters. It was developed by the use of 5 guides built from the initial analysis of the pretest, with the aim of working concepts related intimately to the mixtures, that allow understanding and transformation of some notions that were previously had in a better way.
