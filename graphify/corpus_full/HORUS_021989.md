# Towards the phylogenetic resolution of the ferns Dennstaedthioids: Contribution of the Neotropical species of the genus Dennstaedtia (Dennstaedtiaceae).

A molecular phylogenetic review of the Dennstaedtioideae subfamily (Dennstaedtiaceae) presents itself as a distinction between four markers of chloroplastus for approximately 40% of the subfamily species. Additionally, 18 morphological characters were encoded that were used to find diagnostic clades morphologically. Dennstaedtia s.l. was found to be polyphylactic and its type is nested within Microlepia. The genera Leptolepia and Oenotrichia were transferred to Dennstaedtia, and Coptipteris was transferred to Sitobolium. Other species traditionally recognized in Dennstaedtia were transferred to Sitobolium, Mucura (gen. nov.), and two species to Microlepia, among these species recognized by a new type. D. flaccida, the type of Dennstaedtia s.l. This result motivated the presentation of a proposal to the International Code of Nomenclature for algae, fungi and plants to conserve Dennstaedtia, among them, with a new species. Microlepia and with a genus of age group. adaxial, and spores triletes with a unique ornamentation of the perisporium. To complement this classification, a morphological and geographical overview is presented, a list of the constituent species and the new combinations needed. A key is provided for the eleven genera of Dennstaedtiaceae, and a taxonomic review is presented for the new genus Mucura
