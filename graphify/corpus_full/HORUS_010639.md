# Sweet Controversies: Case Study: Educar Consumers- Red PaPaz y Postobón - RCN

This paper analyses the public controversy arising from the issue of a television message from an organisation of civil society, which questioned the large quantities of sugar consumed daily with soft drinks and sugars, and urged to replace them with the consumption of water and milk. This commercial provokes a discussion that will involve various sectors of society, such as the big companies of the food industry, the large media, some state institutions, social organizations, non-governmental organizations, etc. It analyzes here the actors and arguments presented by them, around aspects such as, the scientific evidence to which it argues from both sides of the controversy, how science is mobilized in favor of the actors in the struggle, the public participation in the debate, the construction of new publics around the controversy, the role of experts, and in general the relationship of the triad science – society and economy. All this framed in the context of the development and transformation of the economic and social relations of capitalism in the midst of the society of risk. For this we make a retoritive and discursive analysis of the controversy through the objects from sugar and commercial. the controversy, how science is mobilized in favor of the actors in conflict, public participation in the debate, the construction of new publics around the controversy, the role of experts, and in general the relationship of the science-society-economy trial. All this framework in the context of the development and transformation of the economic and social relations of capitalism in the middle of the risk society. For this, a rhetorical and discursive analysis of the controversy is made through the objects: suggest and commercial, as choices that run from beginning to end of the controversy
