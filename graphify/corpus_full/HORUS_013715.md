# Education and Youth Violence in the Amazon Triple Border Case study at IFAM Campus Tabatinga, Brazil and SENA Regional Amazonas, Colombia

This master's thesis presents the current situation between Formal State (institutional) Education and Youth Violence, with the case study of two public schools, at the federal (national) level, aimed at vocational technical education, located on the Amazon triple border, specifically, in the sister cities, the city of Tabatinga, in the Municipality of Tabatinga, State of Amazonas, Brazil (BR), and the city of Leticia, capital of the Department of Amazonas, Colombia (CO). In these cities we have respectively the public educational institutions, IFAM campus Tabatinga (CTB), Brazil, and SENA Regional Amazonas, Colombia. In our first chapter, we will discuss the etymologies and concepts related to Education and Violence, for then to present the investigated institutions. In the second chapter we will address the Legislation and Educational Policies related to our coordinators of research, in the academic, legislative and territorial fields, locating these educational institutions and delimiting the research space. In the third chapter, we will review studies, data and analyses conducted at the American Latin America, Brazil and Colombia, issued by international and national organizations, on the issues of Education and Youth Violence, also specifically in the cities of Tabatinga and Leticia, in their achievements and difficulties in education institutional and local legislation. In chapter four we will present our Case study in the two aforementioned institutions, with the analysis of the questionnaires and opinions delivered, elaborating a comparative of the research with the staff of the two educational institutions. Finally, based on the bibliographic research, data and analysis of the Case Study, in relation to Education and Violence, specifically institutional and juvenile, we will demonstrate the advances and setbacks, whether or not there is a relationship between the current institutional education model and Youth Violence on the triple border amazonian. There is a very close relationship between the social history lived by people in the tri-border region and the emergence and maintenance of violence in that space.
