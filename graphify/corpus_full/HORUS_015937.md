# INTERFERENCE OF INTELLIGENT TELEPHONES IN THE CODIFICATION OF MEMORIA: THE ROLE OF VIBRATIONS AND NOTIFICATIONS

The contemporary society, immersed in technology, connectivity and constant access to the internet, welcomes without suspicion its utilities and supplencies, as well as its possible negative impacts. The constant use of the cell phone, for example, is related to harmful effects on health, sleep, behavior and learning, in educational contexts. The aim of this study is to determine if the vibrations of surrounding cellulars generate negative effects on long-term memory in an academic context. Research on the subject suggests the automatic grabbing of cognitive resources in response to the demands of the cell. Thus, it is interesting to investigate if there is a homologous effect with vibrations, and if this fact has consequences on memory. To test the hypothesis, 50 university students saw an educational video of four minutes. During the video, the mobile of the experimenter, located in the view of the participant, lived on three occasions. Other contents were identified in those that did not present vibration. After eight minutes a memory test was applied with key in which was asked for video contents, presented with vibration or without vibration.
