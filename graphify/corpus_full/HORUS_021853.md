# Estimated burden of gastric cancer disease in Boyacá, 2010-2018.

in Boyacá, during 2009-2018, neoplasms were the third cause of death, with 17.40% of total deaths. Stomach cancer was the main sub-cause of death, with a rate of 16.71 deaths per 100,000 inhabitants in 2009. to estimate the burden of disease due to gastric cancer in Boyacá, during 2010-2018. ecological descriptive study of disease load in the framework of the Global Burden of Disease. A literature review estimated the number of cases of gastric cancer incidents from the Statistical Yearbooks of the National Institute of Cancerology and the deaths reported to the National Administrative Department of Statistics. Mortality rates for gastric cancer, prevalence, incidences and cause of death/case were estimated. Healthy years of life (AVISA) lost were estimated, combining measures of years of life lost due to premature death (AVPP) and years of disability (AVD) were estimated to be greater than 65 %, broken down by year, sex and quinquennial groups in 15 years.
