# MULTICRITERIAL RECOMMENDATION OF LOCATION OF BUILDING PARKS, BIOSALUDABLE GIMNESIES AND MANIZABLE SPIRIT AREAS

The elaboration of a weighted overlap is described in a Multicriteria analysis [SPAM], helping with various tools available in different geographic information systems and in turn, guided by the Analytic Hierarchy Process [AHP] method to give an intrinsic percentage to each criterion which functions as an objective mechanism for decision-making. This project is proposed for the city of Manizales, Colombia proposing the installation or adaptation of new areas of effective public spaces, parks and biohealthy gyms under the assumption of the direct correlation between diseases and accessibility to spaces of recreation. It is proposed by comparing the benefits between an initial scenario and a future scenario which obey four criteria (Accessibility to Biohealthable Parks and Gymnasiums, Effective Public Space Index, Age Rate and Index of Tendency of Mental Diseases and Cardiovascular Diseases)
