# --Real Time Thermocycling Equipment Maintenance Required Light Cycler 96

The maintenance of the real-time thermocycling equipment Light Cycler 96 is required, which is important for the development of the following research projects: THE EFFECT OF THE SUPPLEMENTATION WITH LINE SEMILLATION ON THE EXPRESSION OF THE GEN FADS2 AND THE PROFILE OF GRASOS ACIDS IN JABOLA HOURS' JOB. This is currently being carried out at the Clinical and Genetics Unit of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and Zootechnics with the participation of the student of the Master Carlos Andrés Zabala. And of the research project EFFECTO DE LA LINE SEMILLATION IN THE PRE-PRODUCT PREPARATION AND POST TRANSCRIPTIONAL REGULATION OF THE LIPOGENESS OF NOVO IN GALLINA CRILOLA COLOMBIANA (Code Hermes 57711), which is already approved and close to beginning with the participation of the students of Zoonics Daniela Blanco Daza, Erika Valentina Musilera Research and the student of the Autonomous Region of the Autonomous Region of Catalonia
