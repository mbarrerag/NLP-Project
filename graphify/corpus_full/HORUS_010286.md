# Ways to inhabit public space by women. Case study: neighborhood Babylon, Usaquén locality. Bogotá

The following research work arises from the interest of considering how women inhabit and appropriate public space, through the case of the Babylonian neighbourhood in Bogotá, based on the categorization and characterization of their modes and ways of living and appropriating public space. In this sense, an analysis is presented that allows to consider how the city and public space are conceived, by women, how they live this public space and how a series of factors related to the diversity of violence and the vulnerability that they imply, prevents them from appropriation in the use of public spaces, showing that the city remains a historically hostile and exclusive place for women. The methodology of collecting information was applied through a survey, as the first way of ordering and classifying everyday aspects; later, through maps made by women and, finally, through exploratory tours that condensate the location of socio-spatial problems of women to understand the place where they live. This work is intended to contribute to the recognition of the inhabitation of women and to emphasize the importance of appropriating public space as a social, practical and essential dimension of social construction of women
