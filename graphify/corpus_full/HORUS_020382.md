# Mitigation of tsunami risk in the municipality of San Andrés de Tumaco from natural-based solutions. Reflections for planning and spatial planning

The topic of the Nature-Based Solutions (SbN) begins at the beginning of this millennium by recognizing the role of ecosystems in human well-being and the benefits of their implementation from the ecosystem-based disaster risk reduction approach (Eco-RRD) to propose comprehensive solutions. The country in the last decade has advanced strategies for incorporating disaster risk management into municipal planning plans, generating the opportunity to introduce this approach that provides multiple benefits to the community. Tumaco is a municipality located south of the Nariño department in the subduction area between the Nazca oceanic plate and the South American continental plate, presents possibilities for occurrence of natural events such as earthquakes, liquefaction and tsunamis of seismogenic origin, with simultaneous occurrence.
