It corresponds to a study of the use of medication of type indication-prescription. It was carried out by means of the review of clinical histories of 103 patients of the psychiatric program during the year 2021. analysis of data in Excel by means of dynamic tables tool, it was identified that in 81.2% there is adherence to the GPC from the pharmacological point of view. The groups most affected are the age group adolescent and youth. The most prescribed psychopharmacists, were quetiapine (22.3%) and sertraline (19.3%). there is a high percentage of adherence to the GPC of depression, in those patients who did not comply, the adherence could not be identified clear factors, therefore, they appear to be influenced by the clinical experience of the psychiatrist (Text)
