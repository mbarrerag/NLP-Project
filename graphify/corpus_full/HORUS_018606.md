# DEVELOPMENT AND FETAL PHYSIOLOGY

The understanding of the fetal economy has gone in parallel with the dizzying progress of fetal care. Biophysical fetal surveillance, the approach to circulatory hemodynamic redistribution and the understanding of the fetal endocrine environment determining organic maturation at the end of pregnancy are becoming more urgent. Human development has become a fair of lights where genes are lit and others are shut down, it is a concert of molecular biology
