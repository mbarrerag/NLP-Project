# Impact of the closure of the border crossing on the productive, reproductive, identity and political-organizational cross-border social relations of the border area (EF) San Miguel International Bridge, Putumayo, between 2020 and 2022.

The seedbed of Borders, Society and Political Participation, linked to the research group International Relations and Global Affairs (RIAG), in its work plan has defined the characterization of the border areas of the country _x0097_especially the zone of Amazonian border collombo-ecuatoriana_x0097_ to promote reflections, debates and approaches between the academy, institutions, social organizations and the citizenry on processes of political participation that are articulated with the social and economic reality of the border areas. Our goal is to analyze the incidence of the closure of the border crossing point due to the contingency of the covid-19 on the productive, reproductive, identity and political-organizing social relations of the border area (EF) San Miguel International Bridge (Putumayo) between the years 2020 and 2025 since this is characterized by the fact that it is a zone with great traffic of people, high commercial flow and a point of exchange essential for the region.
