# GAIT RECOVERY AFTER NUSINERSEN TREATMENT IN SMA TYPE 3

Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) is a neurodegenerative disease characterized by degeneration of motor neurons leading to weakness and loss of gross motor abilities in time. Is well known that a percentage of patients with SMA Type 3 tend to lose walking performance and become wheelchair bound. Actually, there are pharmacological alternatives which serve as disease modifiers changing the landscape to the patients. Clinical case Male 6 years 11 months old. Molecular diagnosis of SMA with homozygous deletion of SMN1 gene and 4 copies of SMN2 gene. Lost ability to walk at 6.5 years. hammersmith functional motor scale expanded (hfmse) 37|66, revised hammersmith scale (rhs) 27|69, 6 minute walking test (6mwt) 0 meters, pediatric disability inventory (pedi) mobility score 45.2%. intrathecal injections of Nusinersen 12mg started at 7 years old with loading dose (days 0, 14, 28 and 63) and maintenance doses every 4 months. After a year of pharmacological treatment (7 doses) and standard care including Physical therapy, Hydrotherapy among others patient regain gait ability with direct repercussion on motor function scores: HFMSE 44|66, RHS 36|69, 6MWT 54 Meters PEDI mobility score 57.31%. Although SMA is an incurable disease nowadays there are pharmacological alternatives which used in combination with standard care can reduce the progression of motor loss and in certain cases help to recover lost skills. Specialists in physical medicine and rehabilitation must be aware of these novel technologies to offer best possible alternatives to our children with neuromuscular diseases
