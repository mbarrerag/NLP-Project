# Loyalty and perceived value in mobile telecommunications services in Colombia

The mobile telecommunications market is characterized by being highly competitive in countries such as Colombia (Sidak, 2016). Given this, regulators decreased the barriers of change which helped customers maintain their services with a fixed supplier for a longer time (Gerpott, Rams, and Schindler, 2001). It is therefore important that operators seek strategies that allow to generate loyalty from their customers and thus improve their financial performance (S. Gupta and Zeithaml, 2006).
