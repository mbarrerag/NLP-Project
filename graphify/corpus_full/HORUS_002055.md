# Calculation of the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions from new technologies in the electricity sector

This study focuses on estimating greenhouse gas (GHC) emission reduction in the electricity sectors of Colombia and Canada through the implementation of innovative energy technologies. To achieve this objective, various scenarios of technological adoption were developed and simulated using the HOMER Grid software. These scenarios include the integration of photovoltaic systems, wind generation, energy storage systems and electric vehicles, considering variables such as incentives and adoption policies, capacity projections installed by technology, evolution of electricity demand and electricity tariffs in each country. The evaluation was carried out under a conservative approach, estimating GHG reductions for the years 2030 and 2037, using as a baseline the year 2022. The results show that, regardless of the degree of implementation of new technologies, in some cases there is no significant reduction in GHG emissions. This is due mainly to the increase in demand for users and electric vehicles in the medium term. However, it is identified that the incorporation of wind generation has the greatest impact on the reduction of emissions for the years 2030 and 2037 . 22 as the baseline.
