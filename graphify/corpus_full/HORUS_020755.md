# Water Landscapes as a territorial planning unit

The urbanization and evolution of the urban footprint Bogotá - Region has generated tensions on the different territorialities of the border Bogotá - La Calera, where, in spite of the administrative division, the coexistence of neighborhoods of illegal type of strata 1 and 2, rural housings of dispersed order of strata 5 and 6 and areas of environmental protection of national order is overlaid. This has led to territorial imbalances in the absence of a unit of regional urban planning, so this final work of master has the objective to propose an alternative of urban planning - regional that prioritizes the identitary actions in the territory. To this end, the Cartesian representations of the space are examined by means of territorial planning instruments.
