# INSURANCE AND COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH IN PROCESSING REHABILITATION OF YOUNG PEOPLE ACIDING TO PSYCOACTIVE SUBSTANCES

This work is the result of an internship whose objective was to identify the nursing role from the experience of nurses in training, applying the strategy of community mental health, in social rehabilitation processes of young people with addiction to psychoactive substances.1 Description of the problem or need: The consumption problem has become a need for intervention, not only because of its increase, but because of its repercussions on the physical and mental health of people, as well as its impact on society. According to the National Survey of Consumption of Psychoactive Substances carried out by the DANE, it was estimated that, in Colombia, the psychoactive substances that consume the population between 12 and 65 years are alcohol with 84%, tobacco 33.3%, marijuana 8.3% and cocaine 2.1%, among other substances.2 Additionally, the nursing role in the care of the community mental health care in the area of rehabilitation of psychoactive substances. As the nursing model of Hildegard Peplau and the Validant Emotional Linking Model put it. Communication skills incorporate respectful, humanized, empathetic and assertive treatment to promote the development of the bond and with this the interventions have an impact on the community. Finally, the experience of the internship showed that the nursing role in mental health co-munitarian with the subject population, consists in the formulation of the care plan in conjunction with the care subjects, leading the therapeutic process and integrating the interdisciplinary interventions under the premises of scientific knowledge of nursing and professional ethics. 5 Limitations, difficulties and/or successes: The scientific literature that required payment was not consulted
