# MUSICTHERAPY FOR EMPATIC COMMUNICATION BETWEEN THE MAIN CAREER AND THE PEDIATRIC ONCOLOLOGIC PATIENT AT THE END OF LIFE.

This research and intervention seeks to promote an empathetic communication between the pediatric oncology patient and his primary caregiver in a context of palliative care, through the four methods of music therapy in favor of improving his quality of life. It will be carried out at the HOMI Foundation in Bogotá, with a total of 10 participants in a work of 5 days, each one made up of a child between the ages of 7 and 11, who has been referred to palliative care and its primary caregiver. The intervention will be of short duration with an approximate of 5 to 8 sessions and from a qualitative-phenomenological case study design. In order to understand and analyze the communication between the participants a semi-structured interview is designed (based on the Scale of Evaluation of Communication in the COMPA Crianza); an analysis protocol that integrates the analogous visual scale, a report of discomfort; description of manifestations (verbal, gestual/corporal, socioemotional and sonoro musical profiles) and two tools of evaluation are applied that integrates the individualized quality of evaluation in Musicotherapy and the profiles of psycholean expression are reflected in the expression of the perception of the expression of the patient
