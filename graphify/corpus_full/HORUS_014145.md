# Natural regeneration timing of the mangrove in Cispata: integrated tools for sustainable conservation

The mangroves are recognized for their biodiversity and the role they play in conservation, mitigation to climate change and livelihoods of various human communities. Despite their recognized role, mangroves face threats of deforestation, intensive aquaculture, pollution and climate change, which compromise their resilience and affect communities. This project proposes an interdisciplinary approach, using geospatial tools, vegetation analysis and arthropod monitoring to complement the initiative "Mangrove ecosystem services under pressure: the history and future of carbon sequestration hotspots", which focuses on carbon sequestration in mangroves and its impact on sea level rise and human interference. The proposal aims to strengthen this initiative, expanding the study areas on the Caribbean coast of Colombia and integrating the monitoring of arthropods, geospatial tools, vegetation analysis along a gradient and economic assessment of its services. The resulting information will contribute to the conservation programmes of these ecosystems, allow for a better understanding of the succession dynamics after events of disruption and, consequently, to contribute to global emissions, and to Colombia's compliance with reductions in terms of success
