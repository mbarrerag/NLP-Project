# • Taking care of workers in health at times of covid-19

The world changed more than a year ago when the COVID-19 arrived untimely in our lives, especially to the lives of health workers who have been on the front line since the first moment that was spoken of a highly contagious respiratory disease in Wuhan province, China, and that has so far claimed more than five million lives according to official reports of the World Health Organization (1) hereafter WHO. Even this same organization, in its report of May 2021, pointed out that this number may be two (2) or three (3) times higher than reported, given the interruptions presented in essential health services, household spending and the estimated health care budget
