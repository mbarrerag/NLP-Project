# SFRP1, Possible biomarker in progression or regression of cervical lesions associated with Human Papillomavirus

Summary Objective: To compare the expression of mRNA and SFRP1 proteins, PTPRN, CDO1, EDNRB, CDX2, EPB41L3 and HAND1 in patients with low and high grade cervical intra-epithelial lesion, with subsequent progression or regression. Material and Method: Gene expression analysis was performed using RT-PCR and protein expression analysis by immunohistochemistry. Statistical analysis was performed with tests: Wilcoxon, Spearman correlation coefficient and concordance index. Samples were paired at time 1 and time 2. SFRP1 showed a tendency of higher mRNA expression in low grade intra-epithelial lesion (moment 2) Vs. high degree (moment 1). The expression of proteins by immunohistochemistry of SFRP1 in cases of progression (83.3 %) showed a decrease in its graduation (p = 0.00313*); the other genes in study did not have statistically significant changes. SFRP1 hyper showed adjusted behavior to previous studies where it was found in terms of bioexmethyl in intra-epthelic lesions lesions
