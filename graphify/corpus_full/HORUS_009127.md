# N-HETHEROCIC CARBIONS: FROM REACTIVITY TO NANOPARTICULES STABLERS FOR CATALITICAL APPLICATIONS IN WATER

The Carben N-Heterocyclic ligands (NHC) have proven to be of great interest to the scientific community due to their high giving capacity s, which generates stronger M-NHC bonds, thus stabilizing organometallic species. Consequently, the metallic complexes with this class of ligands have proven to be good catalysts for various chemical transformations in organic and aqueous phases. In fact, thanks to these unique properties, one of the most recent applications of NHCs is in the stabilization of nanoparticles. However, their high basecity and nucleophilia make these molecules unstable in the presence of air. This makes it necessary to look for chemical strategies to overcome this limitation.On the other hand, the activation of C(sp3)-H bonds is of great interest to the scientific community, since it is a powerful tool to functionalize organic molecules.The formation of C-D bonds using this strategy allows access to molecules marked with deuterium, which are widely used in the pharmaceutical industry and in enchemical and organo-metallic metal studies
