# The financial controller public designs of road infrastructure not granted in Colombia: a consultant who follows up and controls the contract of another consultant

This document, which has been developed in a general way as an investigation, deals with the contractual equality of the design controller and the designer of public road infrastructure projects in Colombia as a source of deficiencies in the products between-the designer, which serve as a basis for the structuring of public investment projects. Thus, the legal framework of the design intervention was documented through a regulatory review updated to the year 2022, then a comparison of the consulting-designing and consulting-intervening roles was made and finally a procedure for reconciling differences, a design process and a maturity matrix of the PDRI-type project was developed as tools to improve the quality of the final products delivered by the design-the designer. For this task, a background study was carried out, the applicable and updated legal framework was consolidated graphically, and the structure of the consultation was developed in the consultancy contract, and the contracting processes in public entities for studies and designs were reviewed, as well as for its intervention, the proposed database was used, and the structure of the analysis was applied to a group of experts to identify the structure and the structure of the project. a design process and a PDRI type project matrix were proposed as tools to improve the quality of the final products. For this purpose, a background study was carried out out, the applied and updated legal framework was graphically consolidated, the consulting contract was studied in depth, contracted processes in public entities for studies and designs as well as for their supervision were reviewed, the Scope database was used, the Delphi method was applied to a group of experts in order to know their positions on the issues discussed and to validate the structure of the status or maturity matrix and the proposed reconciliation process
