# Composition of three melodic technical studies for Corno Frances based on traditional Colombian rhythms, applied to the horn cath in Colombia.

This work contemplates the creation of three technical-mellonic studios for French horn based on traditional Colombian rhythms. These studies focus on two main aspects of the musical training of the French horn: the musical interpretation of our traditional Colombian rhythms and the strengthening of the technical depth of the French language and the learning of technical skills that can be applied in the universal repertoire. Three musical rhythms were selected: Currulao, Cumbia and Bambuco. Each of these studies is a technical and interpretative contribution of the French language and the interpretation of the French language
