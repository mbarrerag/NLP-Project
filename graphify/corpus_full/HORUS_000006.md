# Study of Tm3+ incorporation in TiO2 coatings formed by plasma electrolytic oxidation at various process durations: characterization, simulation, and photocatalytic application

In this work, the synthesis and characterization of TiO 2 coatings incorporating Tm 3+ and PO 4 3− ions via Plasma Electrolytic Oxidation (PEO) were investigated, along with an evaluation of their photocatalytic activity for methyl orange (MO) degradation. The coatings were synthesized at different PEO durations (1–10 min) in an electrolyte containing Na 3 PO 4 ⋅10H 2 O and Tm 2 O 3 particles. Characterization techniques included SEM/EDS, AFM, XRD, XPS, DRS, and PL, complemented by Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations to analyze stability and electronic structure. Results demonstrated a progressive incorporation of Tm 3+ (from 0.22 to 1.57 at. %) and phosphorus into the TiO 2 matrix, confirmed by EDS and XRD analyses, with the identification of crystalline phases such as TmPO 4 and Tm 2 O 3 . Surface morphology exhibited heterogeneity with increasing roughness (311 nm to 648 nm) and coating thickness (3.89 μm to 16.03 μm), dependent on the PEO treatment duration. Potential chemical interactions such as Ti–O–PO 4 and Tm–O–Ti bonds were detected via XPS analysis, revealing the presence of Ti 4 +, Ti 3+ , and polarization of Ti–O bonds induced by Tm and P. Photocatalytic activity reached its maximum at 2 min of PEO (k app = 3.33⋅10 −3 min −1 ), attributed to the synergistic effect between Tm 3+ and TiO 2 in enhancing charge carrier separation. Finally, DFT calculations revealed that the substitution of Ti by Tm in TmPO 4 is endothermic (+2.00 eV/atom), but effectively modulates the electronic structure by narrowing the band gap, thereby promoting potential photocatalytic applications
