# Review of basic electronics teaching and learning strategies for engineering neo-university students

The main objective of this article of type bibliographical review is to identify and analyze the most effective teaching and learning strategies for teaching basic electronics to neo-university students. In the search, databases such as Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar, among others. The main results of this bibliographic review indicate that the use of active methodology, such as project-based learning, problem solving, experimentation and the use of ICT, are effective for the teaching of basic electronics. It was also found that the use of educational technologies, such as games and simulation software, can be a useful tool for improving the learning of this subject. In conclusion, the teaching of basic electronics can benefit from active teaching methodologies that involve students in their own learning
