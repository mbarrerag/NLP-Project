# Tree growth response to nutrient addition in post-mining forests of gold and platinum depends on size and species identity

The open-pit gold and platinum mining is one of the main economic activities in the tropical region of the planet. However, it destroys the ecosystem, including soil, which has negative consequences on the growth and recovery of vegetation; therefore, soil fertilization is expected to have significant effects on tree growth. To assess these effects in post-mining forests of the Biogeographic Chocó Biogeographic Chocó, we conducted an experiment to add fertilising to soil with five treatments: N (125 kg N ha-1 year-1), P (50 kg P ha-1 year-1), K (50 kg P ha-1 year-1), NPK and control.
