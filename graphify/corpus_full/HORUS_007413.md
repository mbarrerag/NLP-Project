# Proposal of criteria for measuring innovation in the electricity sector in Colombia

The research focuses on the analysis of innovation in the energy sector, considering its importance for productivity and competitiveness of any country, especially in the current geopolitical context. It establishes the need to explore new resources and develop clean energies to respond to market demands and promote sustainability. The methodology used is based on the bibliographic review to define concepts and dimensions of innovation in the electricity sector. The energy sector in Colombia is characterized in terms of innovation and the relevant information is analyzed to establish criteria for evaluating innovation, validated by experts in the field. The results show that the criteria selected to measure innovation in the electrical sector are valued as relevant by experts, although the difficulty of accessing specific information on the decrease of pollution and carbon footprint is identified. The importance of managing multiple sources of organizational information and performing data triangulation to achieve objective analysis and the identification of critical indicators. It is concluded that innovation in the energy sector can boost economic growth and sustainability, but requires efficient management of human and technological capacities, as well as public policies that encourage investment and technological development.
