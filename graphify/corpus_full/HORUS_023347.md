# BODY, EMOTIONS AND LIFE SENTITION. RESEARCH FOR THE COMPRESSION OF THE EMOTIONAL SUFFERENCE PROFUND AND THE SUICIDED IDEA IN STUDENTS OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY - BOGOTÁ HEAD.

The book is the result of research carried out during 2019-2020 with students from the National University of Colombia. They have as a reference the conception that mental health is not an individual result and responsibility exclusively, but an area in which the primary (partner-family) and secondary links, specific to the socioeconomic and cultural contexts where relations are inscribed, converge. Mental health affects the life and integrity of all people, therefore, is an institutional responsibility. This perspective seeks to expand the comprehensive framework of mental health as an exclusive territory of biomedical logic, to the social, articulating the contexts that signal the life of people, in this case, the suffering subject and suicidal ideation, trying to understand and specify a growing problem in the university environments in general and the National University of Colombia in particular. It is an input for the construction of an institutional policy in Mental Health
