# DESIGNER OF EXPERIENCE OVER SERIGRAPHS' LEARNING

The learning of screen printing is a technique that has been shared ancestrally. Over the years it has evolved technologically into a complex art of various elements for its practice; which generates frustrations and bad practices of apprentices in the beginning of the experience. Causing the abandonment of their initial motivations, on costs of investment in inputs. Obtaining mental and physical wear due to lack of previous knowledge. The purpose of the project is the design of an initiation kit for screen printing under the emotional design approach that allows the immersion in the practice and self-learning of the screen printing. The project is carried out in four phases: 1) Anthropological research. 2) Construction of the narrative and experience. 3) Conceptualization and design of the kit. 4) Validations and checks under emotional design protocols. Obtaining the design of an initiation kit; which allows the practice of the technique, supported in the basic workshops of the company Stampados de Colombia. In the verification is identified the interaction of apprentices with the technical information of the workshop through the elements of the kit.
