# Prototyping for simultaneous biofiltration of volatile organic and inorganic compounds through an organic bed.

This work evaluated a prototype of biofiltration at laboratory scale using an organic compost bed for simultaneous removal of volatile inorganic compounds (IVC: hydrogen sulfide and ammonium) and volatile organic compounds (VOC: toluene) producing offensive odours, simulating concentrations of a wastewater treatment plant (PTAR). Taking the case of PTAR El Salitre, Bogotá, Colombia, these concentrations varied between 5 and 86 ppm for H2S, 1 to 5 ppm for NH3 and 1 to 10 ppm for VOCs, and were higher in dry days than in the rainy days.
