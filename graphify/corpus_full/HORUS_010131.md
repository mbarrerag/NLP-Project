# Development of a flexible film with intelligent characteristics based on purple yam flour (Dioscorea alata) with addition of nanoparticles obtained by aqueous collision

The main axis of the research is the development of a bioplastic biourtic based on purple yam flour (Dioscorea alata), using its anthocyanins content as pH indicator. An antistatic material, insulation, shock absorbing, resistant and completely biodegradable and/or compostable source is projected. To carry out the process, it starts with the extraction of the pigment (Antocyanins) present in the flour, and chemical characterization of the anthocyanins by means of the extracts assisted by ultrasound, in which the best extraction conditions varied temperature (30, 50 and 70 °C), amplitude (20, 40 and 60%) and the Ethanol ratio: Water (80:20 and 50:50). Additionally, the extraction of nanoparticles of flour through a mechanical process called (Contra Collision Acuosa weight) were evaluated. For this, the analysis of the material was carried out using 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5% in relation to water: flour under a pressure of 200 MPa. Finally, the production of the films was carried out; the nanopicle content was evaluated (Contractor Colision Acua and 25 %).
