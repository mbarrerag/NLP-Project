# Corporate transparency: identification of business implications from the point of view of its consumers

Based on the hypothesis “The dimensions of corporate transparency (identity, economic, relational, social and environmental) have a significant and positive relationship with the decision to purchase consumers in Medellín”, this document presents the results of a research project whose main objective was to analyze how transparency influences the purchasing decisions of this demographic group. To do this, a quantitative research was designed under a positivist rationalist approach, using a survey type form applied to 126 consumers in Medellín. The findings reveal that the most valued corporate transparency dimensions include environmental transparency, which covers aspects such as the sustainable use of natural resources, waste management and carbon footprint. Also highlighted is the social transparency, related to the company's contributions to the community and its social responsibility. Although to a lesser extent, it was identified that economic transparency, which involves the disclosure of information on utilities and financial practices, also plays a relevant role. Among the results, it highlights that, when deciding on a purchase, people tend to prioritize information that reflects a genuine commitment to the environment and society.
