# CHAPTER VII - CULTURAL PRACTICES: POWER AND HAND OF ARVENSE PLANTS

PODA: The interest in the pruning of rubber trees has increased considerably in recent years, due to the need to seek alternatives that reduce the unproductive period of cultivation. Among the main factors of the aerial system of the tree, which have importance in the production, is the growth of the perimeter of the stem and the structure of its branching, which has a direct relation to the resistance offered by the cup to the break by the wind. The pruning is defined as the set of practices that lead to the intervention of the canopy to alter the natural vegetative development, control the production, the vigor and the health of the tree (Casagrande Pereira et al., 2019). The prun seeks to balance the vegetative growth with the production, in the search for a physiological balance, which results in a greater and prolonged yield.
