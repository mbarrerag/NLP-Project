# WATER PULSES: AN INTEGRATED WATER LETITATION

Leticia to the south of Colombia, grows and develops on the edge of the Amazon River. There the continuous rainfall regime is concentrated at the end of the year and during the first semester, which generate pronounced changes in the levels and flows of the Amazon River. This dynamic generates the pulses of water which are defined as the stages of the annual cycle in which the river rises level when they increase its flows (growing) and low when they decrease (estiage). Leticia has about eight types of wetlands some of which house diversity of species of fauna and flora, while other sectors of the city have been built on old wetlands that were desicated, channeled and/or eliminated by what in periods of rain flood roads and low areas. The ecosystems and coastal peoples live in amphibious conditions adapted to the palpitations of the river that are expressed in their annual and multi-year pulses. In these sectors the annual flood is a natural phenomenon that unites the river with their flood lakes. This happens in the complex of wetlands of Yahuarca with about 22 lakes, the quebrada Yahuanca and seven communities. The aim of this proposal is to develop a methodological approach based on the paradigm of complexity, since it integrates a dialogue of local and academic knowledge from various areas of knowledge, from diversity and plurality, with the intention of solving a common problem and expressing its proposal for a solution based on exercises of experimentation, creation, documentation and sound and visual recording of the territory. This is achieved by means of a participatory, ethnographic and systemic approach, which will contribute to the contribution of public policy recommendations in the different scales of approach, in the artistic, cultural, habitat and territorial aspects, aimed at achieving an adequate interaction with the various populations located in this area and the nature and consolidate an interdisciplinary group that studies in an integral and collaborative way the characteristics of the national territory as well as the basic results of the national sanitation.
