# Presence of markers of epithelium-mesenchyma transition by immunohistochemistry in pediatric osteosarcoma and association with clinical outcomes.

The epithelium-mesenchymal transition (ETM) is the cellular ability to change its original phenotype transiently and has been identified as a tumor mechanism associated with aggressive clinical characteristics such as distant and disseminated disease. In sarcomas, these mechanisms are not well diluted. This study evaluates the expression of two transcription factors involved in TEM regulation by immunohistochemistry: Snail and Twist-1 in osteosarcoma in paediatric patients and their association with clinical variables and outcomes. retrospective cut-off study in patients between 0-18 years with osteosarcoma. Immunohistochemistry study was performed for Twist-1 and Snail and clinical variables related to the disease were collected. The most frequent immunomarking analysis was performed by means of the clinical associations and the positivity of the markers (28,1%) and the correlation between the overall survival and each immunomarker was evaluated by means of log rank test. 53 patients were included.
