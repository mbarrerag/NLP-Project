# TRANSCULTURE AND VALIDITY OF CONTENT OF AN INSTRUMENT TO DETERMINE PALIATIVE NEEDS IN COLOMBIA

Methodological study of transcultural adaptation and content validity. The data collection was carried out from June to November 2020. The sample was of 12 specialists. The NECPAL instrument was evaluated by determining the significance and the items, identifying the clarity, relevance and relevance. To evaluate each item, a 3 level Likert scale was used. The content validity was evaluated by the content validity index (IVC) of Lawshe. The NECPAL instrument was determined to be suitable for use in the Colombian context. Adjustments were made to improve its interpretation. The IVC of the NECPAL instrument was 0.96. The NECPAL instrument in its Colombian Spanish version is appropriate to determine the palliative needs of patients hospitalized in the ICU
