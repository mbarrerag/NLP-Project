# Development of a methodology to design the business model of a spin off whose mother organization is the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá Faculty of Engineering Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering

This paper presents the development of a proposal for the design of the business model of a Spin Off type organisation that has as parent organization a research group associated with the department of electrical and electronic engineering of the national university of Colombia Sede Bogotá. For the development of the methodology, first, it gives clarity on the concept of business model and as this has been studied from different approaches. Likewise, the concept of academic Spin Off is presented identifying its main characteristics. Additionally, it is asked about how the National University of Colombia has addressed the topic. Subsequently, the methodological design used which consists of three sources of information is presented. A review of the literature that aims to identify the existing methodologies for the design of the business model, a questionnaire addressed to the directors of the research groups of the electrical and electronic engineering department and an interview that was carried out to the managers of the organizations type Spin Off. From the information collected, the proposal of the methodology for the design of the business model is made presenting each of its phases and the processes that integrate each phase. A review of the literature that aims to identify the existing methodology for the design of the business model, a questionnaire addressed to the directors of the research groups of the electrical and electronic engineering department and an interview that was carried out with the managers of the Spin Off organizations. Based on the information collected, a methodology proposed is proposed for the design of the business model, presenting each phase and the process that makes up each phase. Once the methodology proposed is presented, it is put to the test by formulating the business model for the knowledge of a research group of the department.
