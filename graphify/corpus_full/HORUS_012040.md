# SPATIAL ACCESSIBILITY TO HEALTH CARE SERVICES USING THE E2SFCA METHOD: CASE STUDY FROM THE CENTRO OCCIDENTE METROPOLITAN AREA IN COLOMBIA

The present research study aims to assess spatial accessibility conditions in relation to health care services, based on indicators of the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD). Geospatial location of health care services available is obtained for the Central West Metropolitan Area (AMCO, in Spanish) in Colombia, which includes the city of Pereira, capital of the State of Risaralda, and its neighboring municipalities of Dosquebradas and La Virginia. The assessment is performed by applying the enhanced two-step floating catchment area (E2SFCA) method. The results show that most health services are concentrated in Pereira, which has 86.90% of all beds available in the State of Risaralda-only three healthcare service providers are not located in this city. It is concluded that the number of average beds and medics per one thousand inhabitants should be calculated by considering both population distribution and the location of health care services
