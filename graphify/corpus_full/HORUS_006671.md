# Factors associated with the banana ripening stain (Musa AAA)

In banana (Musa AAA), a physiological disorder called a maturity spot is presented, manifests itself as a tanning in the nuts, altering quality and generating the rejection of the affected fruit, which results in detriments in productivity. In addition, the symptoms are only evidenced in harvest, which prevents preventive actions to reduce losses. The research is scarce compared with other problems affecting the crop. Therefore, morphometric and nutritional responses were evaluated that were presumed related to the physiopathy. For this, first a model was generated for monitoring the development of the plant, starting from the calculation of the foliar area and thus estimating the approximate moment of the meristematic differentiation, as a follow-up element to the growth of the plant. Subsequently, an observational study was carried out, in two farms of banana for exportation in Colombia (Antio, Magdalena).
