# Psychometric properties of the TWEAK questionnaire in Colombian pregnant women: Comparison of application modalities in the evaluation of the risk of alcohol consumption

The Disorders of the Fetal Alcoholic Spectro Alcoholic Spectro Disorders (TEAF) affect the somatic, neurocognitive and behavioral development of individuals exposed to alcohol during pregnancy. Early detection of maternal consumption is key to its prevention, and screening questionnaires offer a viable alternative in hospital contexts. This research preliminaryly adapts the TWEAK questionnaire to the Colombian population and evaluates the sensitivity of its data according to the method of application. A quantitative, instrumental and cross-comprosup methodology is used, with translation, review and psychometric analysis of the instrument. Through the Kruskal-Wallis test, differences in the results according to the method of application are identified, with the most sensitive self-application. These findings highlight the importance of the application modality in the detection of alcohol consumption, as a greater sensitivity in the evaluation could reduce the bias in pregnant women when responding, favouring risk identification and the use of appropriate treatments to prevent disorders of the fetal alcoholic spectrum. .
