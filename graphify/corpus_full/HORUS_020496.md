# Preliminary characterization of the electric field radiated by lightning in mountainous tropical zone. Manizales case (Caldas)

The present thesis work contains the preliminary characterization of atmospheric electric discharges in mountainous area for the city of Manizales. To develop this characterization, a low cost measuring system consisting of a parallel plate sensor, an electronic circuit, an oscilloscope and a personal computer that is responsible for controlling the system through an acquisition software developed in this work. An additional software was also developed, which is responsible for processing the acquired lightning signals previously obtaining amplitude characteristics (Botto values and their relationships, peak value normalized to 100 km), temporal (times of zero to peak, crossings by zero). Comparisons were made with other studies carried out in tropical areas, showing important variations mainly in the times of slow and fast fronts, as well as the times of 0-100% and 10-90%. . This software works with regard to the preliminary structure and characteristic of lighting discharges in mountain region as the city of Manizales.
