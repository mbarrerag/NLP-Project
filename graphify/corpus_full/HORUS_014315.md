# Synthesis and structural analysis of an asymmetric azacyclophane via Mannich cross macrocyclisation of L-tyrosine derivatives

This article describes the synthesis of an asymmetric azacyclophane via a three-component (tyramine, L-tyrosine ethyl ester and formaldehyde) intermolecular hydrogen bond-assisted Mannich reaction
