# MATHEMATICAL MODELS IN AGROINDUSTRIAL PROCESSS,

The drying of food is a necessary unitary operation in the food industry, since products such as vegetables, fruits and meats can be preserved or preserved with the aim of reducing the activity of water and moisture within them. We present a bibliographic review of the specialized literature in which mathematical models in the food industry are studied, with the aim of knowing this operation and understanding the phenomena that occur there
