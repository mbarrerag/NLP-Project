# UNIVERSITY AS A MOTOR OF INNOVATION: UNIVERSITARY MISSIONS AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP WITH INNOVATION CAPACITIES

The university has been transformed, adapting and evolving throughout history to respond to its fundamental objective: social welfare. These transformations have been carried out through the evolution of the university mission, beginning from training and teaching, then focusing on research as a fundamental axis to generate knowledge and be able to give way to university extension and responsibility and its sustainable articulation with the society in which it is immersed. Within this context of transformation, the characteristic role of universities becomes much more important as a result of the incorporation of innovation activities. Currently the challenge of any university system is differentiation, therefore the development of innovation capacities becomes a priority because with them it seeks to respond to that need for strategic differentiation in the short, medium and long term. The aim of this study is to investigate, from a documentary review, the role played by current universities from the adopted missions, which highlight the need for teaching, research, extension and sustainability activities and the relationship between the main university missions and innovation capacities as inseparable elements.
