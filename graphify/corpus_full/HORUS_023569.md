# Geological framework and structural architecture of the Mining District

Characterizing the structural domains, deformation environments and tectonic processes that are recorded in the rocks that host the auriferous philones in the Auriferous Mining District Segovia – Remedios (DMSR), is essential to understand the structural architecture, the tectonic and evolution of the mineralized structures. Such characterization of the structural style(s) of the study area was made based on the analysis of bibliographic sources, maps of linear structures (based on satellite images), geological analysis and structural styles identified in field. The integration of these tools allowed the formulation of hypotheses on the genesis of the structures and their possible relationship with mineralization in the DMSR. The study framework of the research area can be divided into two sectors: East and West, where it is separated by the metagenis of the Otúnicos fault zone (ZFOP), the mineralization relationship with the structural regime and spatial relation to the plutonic bodies of the area. s (Providing System) and, finally, the main structures or areas of cicillas N70°W to E - W (Cogote System)
