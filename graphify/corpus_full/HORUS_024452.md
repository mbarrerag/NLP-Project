# THE HUGE OF DIGITY. THE 1971 AGOST PETROLIER PARO IN BARRANCABERMeja

Half a century after the events, this book studies the 1971 oil strike in Barrancabermeja, from an analytical perspective that favours the workers' experience in the course of the labour conflict. To this end, the social context of the time is analyzed, and, subsequently, the taking of the refinery, the daily life during the conflict and the verbal war councils that were developed against the main trade union leaders are reconstructed. The research was based on the specialized bibliography on the subject, official sources, press of national and regional circulation, and Ecopetrol documents; but it also examines exhaustively sources generated by the workers themselves, such as the Frente Obrero bulletin, the unpublished texts of Gilberto Chinome and some testimonies of strikers, civic leaders and family members of the workers
