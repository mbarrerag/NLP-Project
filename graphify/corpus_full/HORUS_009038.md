# OPTIMIZATION OF CATALYTIC ACTIVITY OF A USY-MEDIATE ZEOLY ATTACK

The mesopores formed during the process of ultrastabilization of the zeolites with steaming (heat treatment with water vapour) are internal cavities with few channels that facilitate the transport of molecules through the structure of the zeolite [1]. To obtain a catalyst based on USY zeolites with good performance (high catalytic activity, stability and selectivity), it is necessary to eliminate or reduce the content of amorphous material formed during the steaming. In this work the method of simplex optimization [2] was used to optimize the variables of the process of extraction of amorphous material of a USY zeolite by acid attack. The variables studied were [NH4Cl], [HCl] and liquid to solid ratio, at 30 oC and 120 min of treatment. The function of optimization was taken as the number of molecules transformed by zeolite gram (TON, molecules g-1) in the reaction of cracking of 1.3,5-triisopropylbenzene (TIP).
