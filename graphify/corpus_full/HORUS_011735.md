# MODELATION OF GEOMETRY AND WATER OF Auxiliaries IN THE FOURTH WAY OF THE BOGOTÁ RIO WITH Emphasis IN THE FORMATIONS OF THE GUAdalUPE GROUP TO INTEND THE INTERPRETATION OF INTEGRATED GEOPHISICAL ANOMALYS AND PERFORATION DATA

The objective of this research project is to model the geometry and thickness of the sedimentary sequence of the cretaceous superior to the recent one of the middle basin of the Bogotá river to estimate the limits and bases of the stratigraphic sequence, its structural and hydrogeological aspects with emphasis on the critical zone for the exploitation of groundwater delimited by the CAR (Regional Autonomous Corporation) from the interpretation of data of electrical resistivity, potential fields, seismic reflection and drilling data. Based on the results will be estimated the potentiality of deep aquifers and with this to propose the sites to make better exploratory wells that allow to satisfy the need of water for the agro-industrial and population activities of the study area, which have not been able to be solved by exploring aquifers more shallow. Additionally, the potential of geothermal energy of low intalpity and with special needs of regional engineering and engineering will be assessed to determine the underground water of deep aquifers.
