# Consolidation of productive systems for rural communities in cultivation in hillside areas using farming tools and field designs on terraces, under a sustainable approach.

The cultural, technological and economic transformation of the Colombian countryside is indispensable if you are to comply with the SDGs and support the development of the Peace Agreement. According to FAO, Colombia is one of the few countries with great potential to expand its agricultural area without affecting the area of natural forests (SAC and PROEXPORT, 2012). The Ministry of Agriculture and Sustainable Development (MADR) estimated that you can still expand the area with agricultural and silvo-agricultural vocations to 16.6 million hectares (DNP, 2010). However, the largest contribution of food to the Colombian family basket comes from small and medium producers. These producers do not need to expand their work area, what they need is to increase their productivity. The approach of this project is to provide good agroecological practices for small and medium agricultural producers located in areas of the hillside. The project will be developed on four productive units with area that do not exceed the hectare, as model Open Surges for rural communities in conflict areas or vulnerable agricultural areas to strengthen the Nariño Department, or areas of academic character, under a sustainable system for small agricultural producers or academic sectors (Agros) in the area of the area of the terraces
