# JUST-IN-TIME SEMI-INFINITE OPTIMIZATION

Mathematical model considered production task "just in time" (just-in-time), which presented as a semi-infinite optimization problem (SIP). For it solution, a variant of the discretization algorithm was used. After that, for numerical solution of the finite optimization problem was used method of quadratic programming SQP and carried out numerical experiments comparing the results obtained with other authors. The above algorithms were used to solve the problem of just-in-time delivery construction company products. The results showed performance of the constructed circuit
