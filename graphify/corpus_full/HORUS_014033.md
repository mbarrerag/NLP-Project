# Heliodon as a tool for bioclimatic analysis in the design stage to increase energy efficiency in rural constructions

To improve the design of buildings taking into account the energy efficiency, the heliodón, which simulates the path of the solar rays to determine the impact of these on the different geometries of the structures in order to guarantee the optimization of energy resources.The benefits of using the heliodón are: (a) Predict and analyze how the solar light is distributed on the different buildings and thus decide when to take advantage of the impact of the solar radiations; (b) Allows to strategically locate the operating equipment required by the building, which guarantees the decrease of the energy consumption and of high emissions of polluting gases; (c) Allows to adapt the design of the building to the climatic conditions of the place and improve its location taking into account the most energy efficient way; (d) Search for new materials for construction and (e) Suitable for the design of the roofs for constructions. The heliodón is a tool composed by a flat surface (generally a table) that makes the times of the building land, an arc through which a lighting system is displaced by the sun. With the information obtained in the simulations, the heliodón is able to improve with the construction. high impact solutions that contribute to the climate improvement in rural constructions, leading to the expansion of the research field in this area and the possibility of working together with other related areas of knowledge
