# Minecraft Education Edition: A didactic way to enhance the agility of solving mathematical problems.

Minecraft Education Edition: a didactic way to enhance the agility of solving mathematical problems This work was developed with the objective of constructing, applying and analyzing a learning tool designed from the platform of Minecraft Education Edition free version, which would enhance the agility of solving mathematical problems with rational numbers. This didactic unit was designed with three key names; the numerical world, geometric world and random world. Each world contained three mathematical problems related to the basic concepts of order, fraction, area, volume, reason and percentages, where the student had the ease to manipulate the video game overcoming different levels problem to problem. Under these characteristics of the video game, the student had the possibility to use previous concepts in its development to interact with the world to solve and in this sense, try to do it in the shortest possible time. It is important to clarify that, to end each world, they had to solve the three problems, given that each answer led him to find the following problem within the same world.
