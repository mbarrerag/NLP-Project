# MULTI-ULTURALITY AND INTERCULTURE: THE ROLE OF HIGHER EDUCATION FOR GENERATION OF INTERCULTURAL COMPETENCES FOR THE ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT

to identify intercultural education and career plans as a need for academic and organizational interest in a globalized society where cultures that try to differentiate and survive converge, which demand the design of strategies that integrate different social entities. a critical documentary review was used based on hermeneutic processes of documentary interpretation. the importance of intercultural-focused career plans in organizations is highlighted to benefit from differences in the fulfilment of organizational objectives and personal growth of workers. In this same sense, intercultural higher education is consolidated as a generator of knowledge and values of inclusion and respect for different cultures and their diversities. for the creation of career and intercultural training plans in universities and organizations, questions are raised aimed at thinking about what aspects should be focused on strategies involving intercultural education processes? How to innovate, seeking to maintain the necessary balances to strengthen their identity, social cohesion, culture, and be both development partners? It is proposed to address interculturality in a multidimensional way, mainly from the knowledge of each cultural group, in order to achieve greater effectiveness in intercultural processes
