# NEW RECORDS OF WHITEFLIES (HEMIPTERA, ALEYRODIDAE) AND THEIR HOST PLANTS FROM COLOMBIA

More than half of all species of whiteflies (Aleyrodidae) are distributed in the tropics. Very little is known of the whitefly fauna of Colombia. This situation causes limitations in the study of basic and applied entomology of these sap-sucking insects, which are of considerable importance in agriculture as they affect many cultivated and wild plants. In this work, we provide new distribution records for six species of whitefly in Colombia. We also provide maps depicting the distribution of these species in the country and data on their host plants
