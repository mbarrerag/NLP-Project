# NiTi Lyma Fatigue Failure for Endodontics: Exploratory Review of Thematic Coverage

Abstract Introduction: the Níquel-Titanio (NiTi) limes used in endodontics are made of a hyperelastic material with shape memory. However, these limes suffer sudden fracture during treatment, which is considered an unfavourable prognosis. Various studies have been carried out to establish resistance to limas fatigue, and determine which brand is best. Although the most common fault mechanisms have been established for motorized endodontic limes, the information is dispersed, making it difficult to define clear research trends. a review of thematic coverage was carried out using the databases Scopus, Dimensions.ai, Web of Science and Science Direct, to answer guiding questions related to the predominant fracture mechanism in NiTi limes, types of tests and equipment used for experimentation and identify the most active authors in the area. using general search terms, 435 research works were found.
