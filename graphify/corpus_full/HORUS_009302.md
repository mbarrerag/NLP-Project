# VULGARIS) AS

In recent years, a significant increase in demand for food products that are healthier and more natural has been observed. Avoiding the presence of synthetic additives such as artificial dyes, which have shown negative effects on consumer health. This has prompted research into natural alternatives to replace them. Therefore, in this study, the agro-industrial evaluation of betacyanins extracted from beet as a natural dye in gelatin, using natural euthectic solvents (NaDES); novel, safe and environmentally friendly solvents; which have been shown to be an alternative to conventional organic solvents. NaDES was used based on citric acid and three sugars as solvents, and beet flour (without epicarpium). The selection of the best NaDES was made and an extract was obtained, by means of ultrasound assisted extraction (UAE), with an absence of betacyanins of 338,92 mg/g MS with the NaDES formed by citric acid and sucrose (this compound was quantified by spectrophotometric techniques). Finally, the formulation of a gelatin± was made and the presence of the total of the extract was included in it
