# EXPRESSION OF CITOQUEratINE 14 (CK-14) IN THIRD-MOLAR FOLICULLAR SACOS, INCLUDING

In teeth included the histopathological diagnosis of dentiger cyst is made difficult in early stages because the lining epithelium in these cases is very thin and focused, therefore the diagnostic aids such as immunohistochemical cells are required to allow the identification of these lesions in any stage of development. Within the cytokeratins (CK) odontogenic marking the odontogenic epithelium are found CK14, CK18 and CK19 OBJECTIVE: To determine the expression of the CK 14 protein in biopsies processed with diagnosis of dentiger cyst or follicular sac METHODS: Research approved by the ethics committee of the institution, so the registration of blocks with a previous diagnosis of the denticular cyst (CIE-10: K090) or of the sac of the Oral Pathology service was obtained, a total of 20 blocks of paraffin, 17 with a diagnosis of dentigenous cyst and 3 with a diagnosis of the sac of pulicular sac (CIE-10: K090) or of the sac of the sac of the sac of sac of sac of. In the 3 cases with diagnosis reported as follicular sac, expression of CK 14 was negative. The results show an association between the intensity of positive cells to the CK 14 marker in the odontogenic epithelium and diagnosis. CK 14 is constituted as a useful CK in the diagnosis of the dentiger cyst in early stages. Studies with a greater number of samples are suggested to allow analysis of the expression of CK in the mucosa, as well as comparison and use with other odontogenic markers
