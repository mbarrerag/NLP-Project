# Elucidation of the primary structure of Salvia bogotensis lectin (LSBo-I) specific for the detection of T and Tn antigens.

The study of plant lectins has been of great importance due to its ability to recognize carbohydrate residues, which have been called glycotopes; some of them are masked on the cell surface and are associated with a large variety of carcinomas. Some of the lectins that have been most studied are those of plant origin of the Leguminosae family, however, they have also been isolated from other botanical families, such as Lamiaceae. The lectins of Lamiaceae are characterized by recognition of the Glycotopes T and/or Tn.
