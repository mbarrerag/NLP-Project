# Postponed Population: Sociolegal Recomprehensions of Criminal Theory in Colombia

This doctoral thesis proposes a recomprehension of criminal theory based on the establishment of the post-pennated population as a concentric axis of system analysis, based on the considerations made by popular constitutionalism and the reference frameworks of public policy. This objective is based on the problematization of the adequacy of the structure of the penal system vis-acondicative to the constitutional end of resocialisation, the difficulty of materialising constitutional guarantees in prison treatment and the limited State intervention in the post-terminance processes.This research will establish as a methodological basis the ethnography, working collectively with the research group Ipcua Bquyscua and the population after three years of resocialisation, and assessing in the field the theoretical considerations and hypotheses. From this, politicization is established as a determining element for the problem to be understood by the person processed by the penal system, the complexity and paradox as reference frameworks of analysis and a reorganization of the penal system where the human face of the post-pened population allows feedback and reorganization .
