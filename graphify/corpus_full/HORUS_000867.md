# MULTIPLICATION OPERATOR ON WEIGHTED LEBESGUE SEQUENCE SPACES

In this paper, we study the multiplication operator acting on the Lebesgue sequence space lp,ω, for 1 ≤ p ≤ ∞, which generalizes the classical lp spaces by incorporating a weight sequence ωn. We focus on properties such as continuity, inverse continuity, finite range, compactness and essential norm of the operator
