# DYNAMIC MODELS FOR CONTROL OF PROCESS IN THE KALMAN RESEARCH RESEARCH SMALL

The Research Group in Dynamic Processes Kalman, which is currently in the A1 category of Minsciences, has as fundamental objective to promote and develop research in modeling, design, optimization and control of Dynamic Processes at all levels of academic formation, as well as cooperation and transfer of knowledge to the industrial environment and society in general. Therefore, given the lack of research training in undergraduate, the Research Seed has been operating since 2010, in order to promote a space of formation and permanent discussion on research in the topics of interest of the group. To this end, there is also a weekly space of one hour, in which training activities are carried out in the being, doing and thinking, with students ranging from the undergraduate to the doctoral. The activities consist of presenting advances of the pregraduate, master's and doctoral thesis; advances of research and extension projects; talks and training debates, and talks with graduates.
