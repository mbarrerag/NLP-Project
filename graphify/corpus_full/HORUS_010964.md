# SENTING SOCIAL RESEARCH IN PUBLIC HEALTH

This book is the result of a collective work of professors and students of the University of Antioquia that systematized the experience of the Seminar. Was it strengthened the research practice in public health? which was carried out between 2017-2018 and was aimed at members of research seedshops and young researchers. The seminar initiative was born and was financed by the Research Center of the National Faculty of Public Health, was accompanied by four professors and also had semilleros and young researchers from other faculties of the University.It is a proposal that can contribute to the training in public health research and other fields, bearing in mind that here a political intention is played that places students and teachers in a horizontal relationship and represents at the same time the closing of a process and an open invitation to academic dialogue on foundations, contents, methodology and pedagogical strategies, here inscribed in another paradigm of research, open to being enriched or interpelled with new contents and proposals, anchored in the intention of doing with others and others, is a political act
