# Peasant innovation in Silvipastorile Systems as a rural development strategy in Montes de María in the Colombian Caribbean

In the ecoregion of Montes de María historically a conventional model of extensive livestock has been developed that has generated a transformation of the landscape, accompanied by the erosion of the soil and loss of biodiversity associated with the tropical dry forest (Bs-T) (Rozo and Rivas, 2023). However, from the local communities, alternatives to the use of the soil have been configured, based on their traditional knowledge, as are the Silvipastorile Systems (SSP), which promote a different form of livestock production, where the arboreal, herbaceous and animal components are prone to the conservation of the forest, as well as the systems of local knowledge of the communities. From the characterization of the SSPs implemented in Montes de María (San Juan Nepomuceno, Bolívar), it has been analyzed how a strategy of rural development in the territory has been configured, systematizing the silvipastoril experience that has developed in the territory, quantifying its contributions to eco-semic and productive levels, and re-signifying the socio-technical innovations adopted and adapted by the local actors.
