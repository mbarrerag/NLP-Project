# Incidence of family structure single parent on the performance of young people in higher education - Colombia 2007 - 2018

This document presents an analysis based on the statistical study and a multilevel econometric model HLM for Colombia 2007 - 2018, in order to quantify the influence of single-parent households on the higher education of their children. This is done through the results of the generic language test of the Saber Pro exam, taking into account family, social, economic factors and characteristics of higher education institutions that allow identifying the weight of single mothers and fathers on the children's academic performance. The results show that the impact of single-parent homes varies with respect to the level of education of the parents, however in families headed by men this influence is greater and contributes positively to the academic performance of students
