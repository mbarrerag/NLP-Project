# A TRAVEL FOR THE LITERARY CAMINARY IN LATIN AMERICA: THE PURSUANT TO OUR CULTUREL PATRIMONY

The intrinsic relationship of literature and travel has been transformed since ancient times according to the symbols and emotional geography that human communities have had throughout the times, in the Greek, Roman and medieval times, it was connected mainly with the knowledge of the unknown world, the conquest of new universes, the search for sources of eternal youth and golden treasures, and the search for God through paths of illumination or iniciatic; after the Renaissance there is a transfiguration of this literature of the journey that culminates with Romanticism, the journey becomes in a circular path coming out of the desire of knowledge, freedom and encounter with nature, to get to yes and yes, and to find a diffuse being in the abyss of becoming space-time.The modern literature of the journey in its multiple facets continues to dig into this infinite vein that Romanticism has taught us, and under the influence of Europe, many writers of Latin America have undertaken this circular journey, a kind of eternal return, to its landscapes, its multicolor culture, its traditions, its magical-religious universe, trying to find with themselves through complex symbolic systems and sensitive spatialities that it would be about the soul and its cities
