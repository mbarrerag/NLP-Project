# Implementation of the ergetic method for the analysis of eco-efficiency in the roasting process of a brewing malt production plant.

Ecoefficiency is defined as a relationship between the economic benefit perceived by a productive system and the environmental impact generated. Among the methodologies available for the calculation of the latter, is the ERGETIC analysis which quantifies the amount of direct and indirect energy used to create a good or a service under the same unit, showing the investment made by the ecosystem in that product or service, which allows to integrate with the analysis of ecoefficiency. Thus, the purpose of this study is to implement the ERGETIC analysis for the calculation of ecoefficiency in the roasting process of a brewing malt producing plant, where the energy consumption is considerable. For the development an analysis of inputs and outputs of the system was carried out, transformations were applied at each entry and the ERGEGETIC indicators were calculated that characterize the system. With these data, the total emergy required by the system was quantified and the ecoefficiency with reference to the economic income was found, obtaining a value of 2,90x10-13 USD/sej, and 6,43x10-16 ton/sej regarding the amount of malt produced.
