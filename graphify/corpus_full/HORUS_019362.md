# THEORY OF IMPREVISION IN PANDEMIA: A BIND BETWEEN LEX MERCATORY AND HUMAN RIGHTS

The theory of unpredictability has been recognized in international contract law as a component of so-called lex mercatoria, since it was included in different normative texts. However, despite its wide doctrinal recognition, the theory of unpredictability had found few practical scenarios in national and international courts prior to the pandemic. The broad effects and challenges posed by the covid-19 in contractual matters create a favourable scenario for the theory of unpredictability to become one of the main tools of private law and lex mercatoria to reach solutions of justice, solidarity and guarantee of human rights to those affected, in their commercial relations, with the pandemic. This article addresses this problem by analyzing the potential that the theory of unpredictment has in post-pandemia to become a bridge of dialogue between lex mercatoria and human rights, in order to advance in overcoming the fractionation that has existed between the two regimes
