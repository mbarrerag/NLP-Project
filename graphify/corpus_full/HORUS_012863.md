# Exploratory predictive analysis to determine the relevance of higher education programs based on the national academic offer

A relevant higher education favors the employability of professionals, solves environmental problems and reduces inequalities. This paper presents a descriptive analysis of approximately 27,840 higher education academic programs and an exploratory predictive analysis to determine their relevance based on the national academic offer. The data is obtained from the National Higher Education Information System, which contains the records of the academic programs offered in the country. The results allow us to identify some preferences of those registered and enrolled in higher education programs in terms of the sector of the institution that offers the program and the area of knowledge of the academic program. Such results constitute an input to support the decision-making of Higher Education Institutions on issues related to the curricular design of new academic programs and the renewal of curricular designs of existing programs
