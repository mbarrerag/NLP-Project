# BEGGARS AND PATRONS. FOREIGNERS IN CARTAGENA DE INDIAS IN THE 1630‘S

In 1630, the Visitor General of the New Kingdom of Granada, Antonio Rodríguez de San Isidro Manrique, made a judicial visit to the city of Cartagena de Indias to identify foreigners living there without a royal license. If they met specific conditions, had adequate capital, and were not suspected of being Judaizers or Lutherans, they could receive a letter of nature to reside in Cartagena in exchange for a monetary payment. On the contrary, if considered dangerous, they could be fined and expelled from the port. The article examines general aspects of the visit and outlines the life trajectories of some of the Portuguese and Italians who were processed by the visitor
