# Comparative analysis of the energy efficiency of container terminals in Colombia.

The Colombian ports and container terminals, as well as their regulators, have been addressing sustainability in terms of greenhouse gas emissions and energy efficiency. This study is part of this effort, as it analyses the relationship between energy consumption, yield and overall costs in container terminals. This is the first research that considers results for all container terminals in the same country for four years. The data analyzed in this study are from a joint effort between the Ministry of Transport of Colombia and the University of Los Andes. These institutions designed and applied a survey to all port terminals in Colombia, offering an overview of the activities of the port sector from 2010 to 2020. This study focuses on a specific subset of the data collected: the container terminals from 2017 to 2020. In addition, it uses these data to compare the state of these terminals in Colombia with those of Chile. The results of the analysis showed that energy consumption was not defined as well during the period 2017-2020. This study also found some promising changes in energy sources and a reduction in energy consumption in different terminals.
