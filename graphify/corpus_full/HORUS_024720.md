# Political ethical debates in the family conception, a look from some feelings of the court.

The article recovers the challenges of the role of professionals involved in the family institution, whose practice is immersed in a dichotomy between the legal order and the duty to be of social work.For this, the author together with some students, in an academic exercise of reflection in practice reviews three judgments of the Constitutional Court and one of the Supreme Court of Justice, which highlight some of the contemporary debates on the family and which require the judicial branch to accept in this regard.The first ruling of the Constitutional Court analyzed is the C-577 of 2011, in which the definition of the family as set out in article 42 of the Political Constitution, referred to the free decision of a man and a woman to marry, which seeks to be transformed by that judgment for the recognition of families formed by couples of the same sex, in which a debate is raised about the possibilities of adoption and procreation that show other ways of forming the family. The social context is transformed by complex family scenarios, which have not been adequately assimilated by State institutions whose intervention in case law, academic and professional terms has been short in cases of imminent violation of rights. In conclusion of its review, the author studies SP 8666-2017, which redefines the scope of domestic violence and its forms of care, guided by a situation in which the spouses, upon termination of their relationship, continue to manifest violent forms of interaction, which surpasses family scenarios and leads to the crime of intentional personal injury, which is alien to domestic dynamics as soon as they are not generated within a space of cohabitation
