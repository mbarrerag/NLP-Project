# THE CASE OF LUCIANA TOLEDO IN THE LOCAL ASYLUM OF BOGOTÁ

From Luciana's history, I seek to reconstruct significant folds in the history of asylums, treatments and their relationship with the body of women and the unproductiveness or harm granted to women suffering from mental illness. The methodological perspective that prevails throughout the text is the reconstruction of the history from primary sources, related to the clinical histories, the report of the medical mission or mission Humphreys, sent to Colombia by the Unitarian Service Committee and carried out between October and November 1948, the reports to the General Board of Beneficiency and other secondary sources. I place Luciana's mediated voice, brought from her clinical history, to reveal her reactions to the therapeutics used. Key subject to reveal particular constructions of what was going on in her body and her experience in the asylum
