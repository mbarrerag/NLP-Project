# APPLICATIONS OF DATA SCIENCE FOR zonING OF THERMAL CONFORT AS A FUNCTIONAL RESPONSE FOR THERMAL COMFORT ZONING FOR THERMAL PACKING

The purpose of this study is to provide thematic spatial modeling elements for the zoning of thermal comfort as a functional response variable in cattle Bos taurus, Bos indexes, Cruces F1 (Bos taurus x Bos indexus) and Criollos in agroecosystems. Analysis that seeks to provide an integral evaluation of the bioclimatic factors on the functionality of the system in terms of animal welfare, which is crucial for cattle raising in a context of climate change
