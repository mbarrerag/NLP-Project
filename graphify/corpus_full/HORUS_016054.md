# CLINICAL FOLLOW-UP TO THE POSSIBLE TUMORAL RETURN OF CANCÉR TRANSMISIBLE CANIN WITHOUT MEDICAL TREATMENT

Canine transmissible venereal tumor (TCTV) has become a neoplasm of wide scientific interest because it has complex mechanisms of tumor adaptation, speciation, biological behavior and providing a unique opportunity to investigate the evolution of cancer, as well, because even with high criteria of cytological and histopathological malignancy, its rate of metastasis is low (in some cases only reaches 7%), so to investigate what mechanisms are involved in this process can give comparative answers for other tumors that are highly aggressive
