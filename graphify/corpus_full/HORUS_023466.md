# CYMBOPOGON NARDUS (POACEAE) ESSENTIAL OIL OBTAINATION AND FRANCTIONING BY AIR DESTILATION WITH VAPOR

The condensate was collected every 5 and 10 min during the distillation time. The yield was calculated as the total mass of AE obtained on the mass of treated plant material. The yields obtained in this work (0.37-0.55%) were similar to those reported by Chong et al. (2). The highest yield was obtained with the following conditions: 240 kg/m3(load density) and 200 mL/min (steam flow). The AE was characterized by GC/MS and the majority components in the fractions were quantified by GC/FID. The composition of the AE was similar to that reported by Rastuti et al. (3). The majority compounds were citronel (12-72%), citronol (9-36%), geraniol (0.2-15%) and emel (1-17%).
