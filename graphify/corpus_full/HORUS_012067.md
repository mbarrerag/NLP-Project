# GENERAL METHODS FOR THE CONTROL OF PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL QUALITY IN THE MEDICINAL PRODUCTS, FOOD AND RELATED PRODUCTS INDUSTRY: FUNDAMENTS AND APPLICATIONS

The trials proposed in this document focus on those common in an analysis laboratory to assess the quality of products and products, whether in process or completed; however, it should be borne in mind that there is constant technological progress that allows advanced methods for quality control in the pharmaceutical and food fields to be available day by day. The evaluation of general quality involves the use of tests for the identification, characterisation, determination of impurities and quantification of the substance of interest, tests that also apply in the standardization of production processes and in the establishment of regulatory parameters for the marketing, surveillance and control of medicinal products, food and related products. In the field of food, some of the tests treated allow, in addition, the nutritional evaluation of the product and the ve-rification of compliance with requirements for its marketing. Many of the tests here treated, in the same way, are applicable to cosmetic products and medical devices
