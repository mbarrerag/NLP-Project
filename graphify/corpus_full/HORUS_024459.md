# Development of a substance classification system based on an electronic language sensor arrangement.

The analysis of liquid substances is a current research area with applications in food, agricultural, chemical sciences, among others. These analyses usually take time and are carried out with expensive equipment. Additionally, the way to analyze liquids is with a panel of previously trained experts who can evaluate some kind of flavor and although this method has shown to be effective is subject to the disturbances produced by the human being that can affect the classification process. The arrangements of electronic language sensors have emerged as an alternative to the traditional methods of analysis of liquids since they have demonstrated their effectiveness as classification of liquid substances and have allowed automating this process. This doctoral thesis presents the development of an electronic system of classification of substances based on an electronic language-type sensor arrangement. The system consists of a sensor arrangement, an electronic system of data acquisition and a computerized system of pattern recognition and multivariate analysis of the obtained experimental data. These are described different stages in the development of the system of classification of liquid substances such as the selection of sensors and electronic equipment of data acquisition, system configuration, assembly and experimental design.
