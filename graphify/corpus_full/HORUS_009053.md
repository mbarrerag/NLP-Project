# DESIGN AND ASSEMBLY OF A PROTOTYPE EQUIPMENT FOR MEASURING THE REFRACTIVE POWER OF PROGRESSIVE OPHTHALMIC LENSES

The design and assembly of a prototype device to measure the refractive power of progressive ophthalmic lenses is presented. We highlight its capabilities and expected applications in ophthalmology and the optical industry
