# The role of the territory in the Colombian prison system

This project to strengthen the IPCUA BQUAYSCUA research group: AFTER PAYING A PENA seeks to clarify in Colombian public criminal policy the competence of the administrative territories in relation to the Prison and Prison System; this is based on the analysis of the local institutions working on the issue of security and coexistence
