# Spaces deployed for conscious bodies: alternatives of thought in formative processes

I feel the body as a powerful device prepared to fill and deploy knowledge. But in traditional academic processes seems to be programmed to be flat and archived. This research seeks to pass through the body the “routines of thought” with the aim of resignifying the concept of “rutin” and open the possibility of alternatives of thought that emerge from the consideration of the essentials of the body in the formative processes. The text is presented in two large parts: a first state that is deployed and that manifests the body at the beginning of a process, where it is transited by readings, experiences and reflections; and a second state, which aims to be folded, where I enter into a dialogue with myself about the redirections that I have had throughout the mastery. The reader will see unfold a journey of my past research, starting from the comfort of the traditional routines, going through personal discomforts, both family and school. It is to reach those points of break in time and in the role that transformed and liberated a pedagogical action, which explores a routine of self-thought that is questioned about the inconclusiveness of the bodies and the result of unconscious in the classroom..
