# EEG, ECG and SpO2 integration system for newborn follow-up in intensive care unit of the University Hospital of Caldas - SES HUC

The permanent monitoring of the vital signs of newborn in Neonatal Intensive Care Units (UCIN), favors the early identification of alterations and potentially catastrophic events, facilitates a timely and adequate intervention, while allowing the evaluation of the response to these interventions in order to reduce the adverse effects in patients.The main objectives of neonatal monitoring are summarized in: (i) the identification of possible organic alterations of the high, medium and low risk neonate through the control of physiological parameters; (ii) the establishment of a time-control of each of the vital signs; (iii) the monitoring of the limits of the alarms according to the needs and baseline parameters of each newborn; and (iv) the correction of the factors that are altering the frequency or characteristics of the vital signs in the neonate.
