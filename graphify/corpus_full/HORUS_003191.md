# IMPACT OF CLIMATE CHANGE AND TEST ON THE HYDRICAL BALANCE OF THE MAGDALENA, COLOMBIA RIVER WAY

Climate change and El Niño and La Niña (ENSO) phenomena are significantly affecting water resources in tropical regions. The Magdalena River's middle basin in Colombia is a critical area due to its biodiversity and intense economic activity, especially agricultural and energy production, which depends on water availability. This study seeks to assess the impact of climate change and ENSO on the water balance of this basin, using an advanced hydrological model (SWAT) to simulate the effects under different climate scenarios, including socioeconomic trajectories SSP3-7.0 and SSP5-8.5, as well as the impact of the operation of the Hydrosogamous hydroelectric power plant
