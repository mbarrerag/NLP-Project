# Role of teacher motivation in inclusive education practices, the case of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá headquarters

Inclusive education in the university context is positioned as a transformative approach aimed at overcoming challenges and capitalising opportunities, in order to ensure equal opportunities and equitable access to higher education. In this process, teachers play a crucial role, facing obstacles ranging from adapting pedagogical strategies to overcoming barriers such as resource scarcity and specific training. Their attitude and motivation are raised as fundamental pillars. This study focuses on identifying the factors involved in educational practices and how motivation leads teachers to adopt inclusive practices, facing challenges and proposing solutions to achieve equitable and inclusive education.
