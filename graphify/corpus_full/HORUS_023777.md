# Invasion of the Western Atlantic by El Pez León (Pterois Volitans and P. Miles): Input from the Adn Mitocondrial

The species complex of the lionfish (Pterois volitans/P. thousands), native to the Pacific Indo, was introduced into waters of the Western Atlantic, becoming invasive species that cause the deterioration of ecological processes, decrease of resources in the different marine ecosystems and their derived goods and services. Mitochondria molecular markers, widely used in the analysis of the invasive process, have allowed to generate information on the genetic structure of the invader in the affected geographic areas. This study collects information available in databases of the different mitochondria regions of the lionfish, where it was possible to analyze the cytob and IOC gene, allowing to show the evolutionary process and present the phylogenetic relationships of the invasive species complex (P. volitans/P. thousands) with information available from species of the Pteroinae subfamily collected in their native habitat. In this sense it was possible to analyze two mitogenomas for the complex of species that constitute the invasion.
