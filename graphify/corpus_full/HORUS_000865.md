# Biomimiesis as a model for the design of resilient architecture

The resilience should be a fundamental axis in the architectural design. This research work proposes the development of a model of resilient architecture based on biomimimes, considering the ability of some microorganisms to adapt to phenomena of mass movements. Through a transdisciplinary approach and a detailed analysis in the laboratory of general and cellular biology, a design model is presented that integrates the principles of life in the architectural management. This seeks not only to promote solutions based on nature, but also; to promote resilient structures, capable of dealing with extreme conditions effectively. The study analyzes an area of Medellín with relative threat by mass movements, where the recurring slides compromise the stability of the housing and the safety of the habitat.
