# Teaching training strategy to strengthen educational practice in the area of Natural Sciences.

This paper shows the design and implementation of a teaching training strategy, which sought to strengthen educational practice and promote more significant teaching processes in the teachers of the Natural Sciences area of the San José de Calasanz College. The methodology used in the development of research was critical action-research, which provided the possibility of opening reflective and transformative scenarios in teachers against the teaching methodology used. The strategy was developed in the following six moments: identification, recognition of the problem, construction, implementation, how was it done? and evaluation. The results show significant changes in the planning of activities and the development of classes, making them now focused on the student and with an important modification in the role of the teacher as a companion and guide
