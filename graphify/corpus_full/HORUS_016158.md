# URAL BUILDINGS FOR AGRICULTURAL ENGINEERING: GUIDELINES FOR BUILDING AND MANEJO

The agricultural and livestock sectors in Colombia have shown a growing dynamic and its importance acquires greater value because of the conditions of pandemic that began to live since the end of 2019. Generating greater quantities of food, with a high quality standard for a demanding consumer, taking into account that the energy consumption is high and that the conventional energy sources are increasingly expensive, it becomes a priority for farmers and ranchers in the country. In addition, it is necessary to keep in mind that the mere number of producers in the country is decreasing due to the migration phenomena, and that these producers are becoming more and more important because their children, in a high percentage, are prepared in other disciplines that do not have to do with the rural sectors. In this context, it is the function of agricultural engineers to seek a technological development of the rural sector, taking into account that the solutions require large investments and are of long duration of the study. On the other hand, it focuses the chapter of the structural education and the other part of this document. The fifth chapter deals with the variants found in open and closed systems of pig production, through the different growth rates and taking into account the requirements of both space and climatic conditions to optimize production. All these topics are part of the Rural Constructions course in the Agricultural Engineering curriculum. For all the above, this document can serve as a consultation for professionals and technicians involved in agricultural and livestock production systems, which shows variables whose appropriate management can contribute to improving efficiency in production processes and yields. However, it is emphasized that solutions to the problems presented in the production infrastructure generation require the participation of multiple disciplines in collaborative work for their co-creation
