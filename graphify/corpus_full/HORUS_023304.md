# The interrelations in ecosystems, their teaching by inquiry and impact on scientific thinking skills in cycle two.

The interrelations in ecosystems, their teaching by inquiry and impact on scientific learning skills in cycle two. In the teaching of science it is important to carry out activities that contribute to the development of scientific thinking skills and allow a reflection regarding the interrelations present in ecosystems to raise students' awareness of their relationship with the natural environment. Taking into account this, a classroom work is developed, with second cycle students from the school Gabriel Betancourt Mejía IED, which seeks to promote the understanding in students about interrelations between organisms and the abiotic component in ecosystems, through the teaching of research-based sciences (ECBI). The process begins with an exploration of essential previous concepts related to the ecosystem and the identification of the state of development of observation skills, description, classification, approach of questions and interpretation of information. Subsequently, it is designed and applied a proposal based on research (ECBI) that addresses some interactions in ecosystems and seeks to strengthen the scientific thinking skills.
