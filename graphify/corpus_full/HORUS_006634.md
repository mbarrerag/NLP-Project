# Guiding with Light - Design of Lighting of Bolívar Park in Girardot Municipality

This document presents the lighting design of the Bolívar park that seeks to recognize its tourist role within the municipality, highlight its architecture and contribute to the decrease of light pollution. To this, a detailed characterization of the environment was carried out, including furniture, textures, heights, fauna and flora, both resident and tourist users, as well as the analysis of the public and private buildings surrounding the park. With this information, a technical and creative design process was generated, taking into account the regulatory guidelines of mandatory compliance for the Colombian territory under resolution 40150 of May 03, 2024 RETILAP, as well as the technical recommendations of the CIE in its guides CIE 115, CIE 140 and CIE 150. Since the Bolívar Park is an area with considerable size, a concept of design was formulated based on interconnection through the hierarchicalization of the light, with the aim of guiding the transit through the different areas of the park. This concept of interconnection promotes the creation of a master plan of urban lighting in Girardot, with the Bolivar Park as an epicentre, linking different sites of tourist, cultural and commercial interest of the municipality . guideines CIE 115, CIE 140, and CIE 150.
