# FRAERNITY OF MULTS

The works of Paul Cézanne and Francis Ponge constitute forms of resistance and desertion with respect to the dynamics of painting and poetry of their times. Cézanne, harraño and marginal to the art of the gallery and the museum, breaks with the artistic movements of the moment.
