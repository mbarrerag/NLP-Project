# Does the movement of conspicues reinforce the attention of depredators?: BIOROBOTICS AND THE DESIGN OF EXPERIMENTAL PROTOTYPIES OF MOVEMENTAL LASSES

The colorful tails of some lizards seem to divert the attention of predators to vital parts (head and trunk) and redirect it towards non-vital ones (autotomous tails).Consequently, the conspicuous colouring of these tails is considered as a lure for predators visually oriented as birds.
