# PHENOMEOLOGICAL STUDIO: I AM A FATHER AND I ENTRY TO THE PART, IT WAS A DREAM REALITY!

The implementation processes in the accompaniment of women in labor and childbirth in Colombian institutions are still deficient and lack solid support in nursing theories, however, as the world progresses the new parents seek to get involved in the birth of their child. To understand the experience of the parents who experienced the birth of their child in a health institution in Bogotá, Colombia between July 2018 and March 2019. phenomenological interpretation qualitative study that integrated the nursing theory of human development proposed by Rosemarie Rizzo Parse. We took into account the ethical aspects informed consent and ethical endorsement of the institution. The information was collected through interviews with 8 parents with non-participatory observation and participant, field journals and telephone calls. The experience of the parents was consolidated in 3 topics: did the birth of the parents?, did we have the three guides and would be sickened by an excellent support? A structure is permeated by feelings, feelings, attitudes and values of the parents essential to experience the significance of the form described, did they have.
