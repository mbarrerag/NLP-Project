# Market information management by operators of the VALUE CHANNEL OF THE SUREZ RIVER HOURING PANEL (COLOMBIA)

An analytical framework was designed to examine the management of market information by the actors linked to the value chain of the Hoya del Río Suárez panel (Colombia), and the information was obtained from semi-structured interviews applied to agents linked to various links in the chain, inquiring about the identification, access and type of information source, and how it is used in decision-making. Following analysis of categorical data and their frequency measures, cultural conflicts and patterns of mistrust are identified that contract the bargaining power mainly of the agents linked to agricultural production and rural agro-industry in the value chain analysed. Uncertainties and power asymmetries are generated that move to price formation in local markets, hinder the interest in adding value and exacerbate unfair competition in favour of chain intermediaries. Own experience and oral tradition are used as main sources of information, in view of the intuitive recognition of social environments of proximity, privilege for individualistic actions and low capacities for consultation and use of information in formal media
