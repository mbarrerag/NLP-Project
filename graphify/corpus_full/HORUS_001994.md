# Optimizing Small Hydropower Key Material Considerations

Small hydroelectric power plants in Colombia have an unexploited potential considering the 2024 installed capacity could be surpassed, diversifying the country's energy mix and taking advantage of a renewable source with low environmental impact. However, as of 2024, only 8.45% of the 25,000 MW potential is being exploited. This shows the opportunity to exploit this power generation in Colombia. To found optimal solutions, its required to achieve optimal selection of materials. To solve this problem, this article presents an optimization methodology of material selection for hydraulic power generation. This methodology will allow the development of new designs of desanders and other technologies for small hydroelectric power plants that are adapted to local conditions and increase the efficiency and profitability of this type of projects
