# Freedom and the Construction of Political Unity in the United States of Colombia 1867-1886

This research addresses the concept of freedom in the period of 1867-86. Discussions on the senses of freedom were given within the framework of the construction of political unity. This is constituted as the articulation and establishment of a single and dominant sense in the political body that gives reason for the community's relationship. In the period of 1867-1886 a way to achieve political unity was through the construction and consolidation of a common bond based on the senses of freedom that mobilized two visions about the duty to be of society and the citizen, both in public opinion and in school. On the one hand, from the sense of freedom as a moral emancipation of the individual, promoted by the radical liberals, it was attempted to shape a society in which the guarantee of the civil right of freedom and its regulation was given from the autonomy of the citizen and his respect for the law. Thus, the bond of unity of the republic consisted in the preservation of freedom, and was rendered as one of the community's assets.
