# Productive diversification and economic growth: the relationship between the economic dynamics of Colombia's departments and the complexity of its export basket between 2005 and 2020

This research aims to analyze the relationship between the economic complexity of the departments of Colombia and their generation of income, both total and per capita. Bipartite networks were used that link the departments and their products exported competitively, which allowed to calculate indicators that summarize the state of their productive structures, such as the Economic Complexity Index (ICE) and the Exports Sophistication Index (EXPY). The results indicate that, on average, the departments that have more diverse productive baskets not only generate higher income derived from international trade, but also export products that are less common in other departmental economies. Likewise, it was found that the departments less diversified and backward tend to be distanced from others, since they can be located mainly in peripheral areas of the country. On the other hand, the analysis of the relationship of interest was made through three data panel models considering information from 33 departments of the country between 2005 and 2020.
