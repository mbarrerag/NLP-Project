# GEOCONSERVATION IN COLOMBIA: THEORICAL-Practical APPROXIMATIONS

In the framework of the Corographic Commission, the recognition of milestones of the Colombian landscape, such as the Nevado del Ruíz Volcano, the Peñol de Guatapé or the Furatena Strait in the River Minero, allows us to show that the geological features are also a constitutive part of the collective construction of memory and identity in the Colombian territory and, therefore, are a common good of the whole of what we call heritage.This recognition has not been foreign to the Colombian State, which by Law 63 of 1959 and Law 45 of 1983 has recognized the need to preserve features of geological character in the country because of its relevance to the construction of knowledge and national identity.These laws, together with the Constitution of 1991 and other norms, laid the basis for the issuance of Decree 1353 of 2018, by means of which the integral management system of the Geological and Paleontological Heritage of the Nation is established, in terms of its identification, protection, conservation, rehabilitation and transmission, under the leadership of the Colombian Geological Service SGC, articing institutions, academia and local communities, around the protection and conservation of this heritage, the national, the geological heritage, the Colombian heritage and the geographical heritage.
