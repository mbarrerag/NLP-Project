# Recovery, digital transcription and interpretive analysis of three choral works by master Arnulfo Briceño Contreras

This project, besides being a tribute, has the purpose of recovering the valuable work of one of the composers and directors of the most important choral Colombian music, that over the course of time has rarely been interpreted, the master Arnulfo Briceño Contreras. For the fulfillment of this objective, the digital transcription of the manuscripts of the choral works “Who do you cheat grandfather”, “Colombia my people” and “I need to love you”. Secondly, an interpretative analysis of the morphological elements immersed in the melodic, harmonic and rhythmic construction of each musical piece is carried out, taking as its axis the interpretation of the author, the poem and the different anecdotes of the coristas who were under the direction of the master. Finally, as a result of this exercise, this project is a part of the composer and is considered to be of use to future interpreters and directors of the bets of the moment of addressing other works of the master Arnulfo Briceño Contreras. s of singers that have been conducted by him. Ultimately, as a result of this exercise, the main musical paths shared by the three songs are identified in order to know the composer’s style, which can be useful for future performers and choral conductors when approaching other musical works composed by Arnulfo Briceño Contreras
