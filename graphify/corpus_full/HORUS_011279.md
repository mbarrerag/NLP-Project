# STATUS OF IMPLEMENTING EMERGENT TECHNOLOGIES APPLICABLE TO HEALTH AND INTELIGENT HOSPITALS: A REVIEW OF LITERATURA

The growth of smart technologies in various areas represented in hardware, software and communication systems has accelerated the administrative and operational processes of companies that rely on them for their decision-making. Health is not alien to this incorporation of technology in their processes, this is why this work focuses on identifying the status of the implementations of emerging technologies applied to the health fields and intelligent hospitals through a systematic review of literature with the purpose of establishing the areas of application, types and management of technologies. The literature review protocol describing three moments: planning, execution and results. The results allow establishing trends and predominant needs by identifying important niches that require research and deepening that contribute to the improvement in the adoption and expansion of technological infrastructure in health-related companies as smart hospitals
