# LOVEMARKS AND ACADEMIC RESEARCH IN MARKET: PROMOTING A SYSTEMATIC LITERATURA REVIEW PROCESS

This document presents the results of a Systematic Literature Review (RSL) process, which aims to analyze the scope or academic research level on lovemarks or loved brands in the marketing context. The search equation that was designed to find related academic products is operated in the Scopus scientific database as follows: (TITLE-ABS-KEY (lovemarks OR (brand AND love). Likewise, the VOS viewer program is used for the visualization of information. As a result, the search gives a total of 422 documents. After the process of debugging, the conceptual, theoretical and methodological aspects of 151 summaries are analyzed in them. Preliminary results expose the geographical distribution of the results, representative authors, the type of documents, the journals in which it has been published on the topic and the variables used for conceptual perspectives, i.e., dolovemarks, love, and brand?
