# The Bible has value: analysis of the civic perception

This book includes the application of the Thematic Content Analysis to estimate the value that citizens give to the Medellin Public Library System, giving an account from a social dimension of the values, meanings and benefits recognized by the inhabitants of the city about public libraries, about their lives and those of the people around them. This, considering the different perspectives among the different groups of participating actors: general citizenship (users and non-users); strategic actors of the cultural, librarian, community and planning sectors; and internal staff of the Medellin Public Library System. The results allow us to understand public libraries as important agents in the social transformation and quality of life of the territories where they are located
