# Political culture of young students of the National University of Colombia, Medellín headquarters in 2023

Previous research has suggested that the political culture of the youth has been characterized by disenchantment and political disaffection. Moreover, that this sector of the population considers political institutions as traditional, conservative and authoritarian. In particular, university environments are spaces that allow the creation of networks of social and cultural interaction that can contribute to a construction of values and political attitudes of the young students there. In addition, in universities the youth can form their citizenship and propose political actions on the social becoming. Thus, the present monograph in political science aims to answer the following research question: what are the knowledge, beliefs and values that make up the political culture of the National University of Colombia, Medellín headquarters in 2023? In theoretical terms, this study is based on the category of political culture that allows to analyze the individual, cultural and social orientations towards the political system. Specifically, the concepts of knowledge, beliefs and values will be integrated closely with the notion of political culture and allow to approach the way people are informed and value the political system that allows for the previous visions of the students.
