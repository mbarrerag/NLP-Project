# DEBT AS A COLOMBIAN CASE DEPOSITION AND INDIVIDUAL ACAPPEARANCE DEBT

This article aims to provide a brief historical history of the behavior of debt in Colombia, especially the public, and from these elements to place the urgency of progress in a process of international auditing of debt. We are convinced that auditing allows us, on the one hand, to pave paths that contain the brutality of this funded capitalism, and, on the other, to provide inputs in a horizon of economic and tax justice
