# Fungemia in patients with solid neoplasm between 2012 and 2019 in institutions in Bogotá.

In Colombia there are no studies that relate solid cancer (CS), mortality and invasive fungal infection (IFI) despite the elevated morbidity and mortality of this pathology. The objective of this research is the characterization of patients with solid and fungigemous neoplasias, through a retrospective, observational, descriptive and multicentric study in different health institutions in Bogotá. The most affected population is that with cancer progression, the most frequent CS was gastrointestinal. Among the factors the use of central venous catheter (CVC) was associated with infection by C. albicans and C. not albicans. As for the search for complications associated with IFI, the limited use of diagnostic tools was observed. The focus of the fungimy was identified in 80.5% of the patients, 59.2% of the CVC users, which was associated with the risk of developing fungal change p 0.114. The factors associated with mortality at 30 days were ECOG>1 p 0.076, abdominal surgery (CA) p<0.01, parenting, naïvetylolitis (NP) P0.01)
