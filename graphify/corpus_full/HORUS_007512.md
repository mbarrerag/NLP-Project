# Co-laboratory of knowledge and collective experiences in agroecology: permaculture and intercultural environmental popular education

Co-laboratory of knowledge and collective experiences in agroecology constitutes an exercise of co-design that groups together part of the experience in urban agroecology developed in the city of Manizales. This booklet serves as a tool of collective memory and as a guide for replicable actions, promoting the development of new multispecies meetings. The importance of this booklet lies in its ability to promote the implementation of agricultural practices respectful of the environment, while stimulating imagination, creativity and collective collaboration through practical and innovative actions. Likewise, it strengthens agroecological organizations by offering relevant reflections on urban agroecology. In addition, the booklet is enriched with full-color images that illustrate examples of good agricultural practices and actions harmonious with the rhythms of life. Written from different perspectives and shared experiences, in a clear and accessible language, this booklet becomes a valuable resource for consultation on planting practices, caring for the land and other possible forms of life.-- Contracadas
