# EXTRACTION OF NUCLEAR ACIDS

Molecular biology is an area in constant development and with the application of its techniques fundamental biological processes have been explained in different organisms. This book chapter presents the principles related to the extraction of nucleic acids, both DNA and RNA. It addresses from theoretical bases the phases of the main commercial and non-commercial methods. Updated data derived from the updated review in extraction methods are presented, as well as illustrations that aim to offer the reader additional information for his understanding
