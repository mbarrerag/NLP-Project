# Comparison and analysis of instruments to evaluate drug administration in nursing students: proposed standardization in Colombia

Comparison and analysis of instruments to evaluate the administration of drugs by nursing students: proposal for standardization in Colombia. The process of administration of medicines is carried out through observation and administration under supervision in highly complex clinical learning environments, which build the career of nursing students and can influence their performance, either positively or negatively. Although it is assumed that the newly graduated have the necessary competencies for this process, scientific literature and teaching experience show that they face fears and deficiencies in knowledge and skills. In Colombia, no studies have been conducted in the last 15 years that compare or analyze instruments validated psychometrically to evaluate that learning process. For this reason, the aim of this thesis is to compare and analyze the instruments to propose a standardization. The study was developed in two different stages. The first consisted of defining the concept and identifying the instruments used to evaluate the administration of medicines in nursing students. In the second stage, the results are compared and analyzed the existing pedagogical methods and tools, as well as the weaknesses present in their implementation in the real clinical practice scenarios. years comparing or analyzing psychometrically validated instruments to evaluate this learning process.
