# Photo-oxidation reactions catalyzed by covalently linked Cu complexes on TiO2 nanotubes

In this work, the complexes bis-[(4,4’-dicarboxy)-2,2’-biquinoline]copper(I) (1) and [(4,4’-dicarboxy)-2,2’-bipyridine]dichloride-copper(II) (2), which were covalently linked to the surface of the nanotubes of TiO2. The respective free complexes and the nanotubes were synthesized using hydrothermic conditions. The process of anchoring the respective complexes on the support was carried out under inert atmosphere (N2), using a transesterification reaction between the carboxylic groups of the substitutes present in the ligant and the superficial hydroxy groups of the nanotubes of TiO2 previously modified by a silicon reaction. The nanotubes of TiO2 were characterized by the photocopies and the employment of the nanotubos (SEM), the photo-Omphide spectrotype.
