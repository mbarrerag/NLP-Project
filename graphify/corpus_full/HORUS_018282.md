# APPLICATION OF THE JERARCHY OF CONTROL IN THE ATTENTION OF PANDEMIA BY SARS-COV-2

In relation to the development of the pandemic resulting from exposure to SARS-CoV-2, better known as COVID-19, many global entities and various natural or legal persons in the area of health, engineering and economics have spoken out, among others. In this regard, and in order to seek truthful information, this document consulted bibliographic sources of recognized international organizations or institutions and articles of highly impact indexed scientific journals in order to make the information provided below reliable and free of conflicts of interest. Following the hierarchy of controls (Stempliuk, 2020) proposed by the Pan American Health Organization (OPs) and the World Health Organization (OMs), which consists of an inverted pyramidal relationship where the first resource is the elimination of risk and the last one the endowment of personal protection elements to the worker, this document presents some measures for the elimination of the virus in the workplace, then proposes engineering and administrative controls and concludes with individual protection measures
