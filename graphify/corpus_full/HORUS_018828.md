# ELEMENTS FOR THE COMPLIANCE OF RESEARCH IN COLOMBIA

My aim is to draw attention to the need to expand the reference points to understand and evaluate the situation of accounting research in the country, emphasizing the importance of generating knowledge that responds to the central problems of the accounting field in our social and organizational context. I believe that an understanding of accounting research in Colombia implies complementing the diagnosis of the current situation, based on indicators and metrics, starting from emphasizing some aspects of the duality between the structure and the agency that are impacting university accounting research in the country. Thus, I want to highlight aspects of the institutional structure of research (the socio-institutional change in the context of the society of knowledge and the pressures on the actors of the ses and scyt) and of the individual agency or subjectivity of researchers (the struggle for a place in the field and the transformation of academic habitus)
