# ANALYTICAL CHEMICAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY

In an increasingly aware world of environmental challenges, sustainability has become a global concern that encompasses multiple aspects of our society.From the conservation of natural resources to climate change and loss of biodiversity, environmental sustainability has become fundamental in adopting strategies that not only mitigate the environmental impact of waste, but also promote its reuse and recycling, transforming them into valuable resources in a continuous cycle. However, addressing environmental sustainability goes beyond simply adopting ecofriendly practices or implementing conservation policies. It requires a deep understanding of the complex interactions between the environment, the economy, society and politics. It is at this point that the interdisciplinary perspective emerges as a fundamental approach to understanding and addressing sustainability in a holistic way. The importance of this webinar lies in its ability to bring together experts and professionals with different interdisciplinary perspectives of environmental sustainability, highlighting the importance of collaboration between various sustainability disciplines that allow us to develop more effective and sustainable solutions to address environmental challenges. Webinar will also provide the opportunity to discuss the key role of the circular economy in the transition to a sustainable development model, reducing the re-use of sustainable development and employment.
