# Methodology for designing an optimal decision-making system for the operator of a fleet of shared electric vehicles providing electrical auxiliary services.

The use of electric vehicles for public transport and shared car services has been widespread in recent years, in response to the needs to reduce global emissions of gaseous pollutants and reduce the number of private vehicles. However, the implementation of the practice of shared vehicles still has some challenges to overcome, such as the limitations in regulations and the low profit margins that can be achieved by the logistic agents of the fleet. In this thesis proposes to address the challenge of the low profit margins for the case of a single logistic agent in the fleet, giving electric vehicles the possibility to provide power to the electrical grid; through the development of a decision making tool for the optimal operation of a fleet of electric vehicles that are used in a shared way, managed by a single operator or logistic agent (aggregator). This agent additionally counts with the possibility to provide ancillary services to the electrical grid model, taking into account the ageing of batteries and the risk in the profits due to the variation of the demand of the shared vehicle service and the energy consumed in the journeys.
