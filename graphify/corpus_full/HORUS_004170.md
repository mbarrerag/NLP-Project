# Our Economy: a new utility distribution proposal to advance towards sustainable agri-food systems

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the United Nations Organization do not have actions at the scale and speed required for their fulfillment in 2030 (UN, 2022b) due to multiple barriers among which is that the market of neoliberal economies generates political subordination (Roderick, 2020). For this reason, the present master's thesis explores what the new utility distribution proposal that Our Economy poses consists of; what are its basic concepts; identifies the type of organizations that, according to their object, could use Our Economy; and the tools that could be used to diagnose their sustainability. """"""Our economy provides the agri-food systems with a new proposal for utility distribution that has 4 axes: "backwards", improving the conditions of suppliers, workers; "forwards", improving the conditions of customers; "outward", improving the environmental conditions; and "inward", increasing long-term utility. It uses proportionality as the basis for the distribution of three of these axes. It has a mixed brand proposal, a certification one and three-dimensional multiples. It requires the organization to have financial statements, as well as the quantification of its externalities. It is legally viable in Colombia in Societies of Benefit and Collective Interest, which are hybrid organizations that may belong to agri-food systems. And the sustainability of organizations that use it can be evaluated through management reports that use international standards such as the B Company certification, the GRI Standards, the AA1000 AccountAbility Standards, the SDG Compass or the ISO 26000 Standards. .
