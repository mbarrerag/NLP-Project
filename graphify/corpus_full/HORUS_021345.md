# Memories and best fictions: women and other field and peace stocks

This project proposes the participatory construction of memories and fictions with women or people (some of them with sexual and gender non-regulatory experiences) in process of re-incorporation of the AECTR La Fila, Icononzo, Tolima and other women of the municipality, through the collaborative and experimental construction of products of the audiovisual genre docu-fiction or short documentary, mini podcasts and short written or graphic accounts, by using the ethnographic methodologies and of _x0093_sociology of the image_x0094_ (Rivera Cusicanqui 2018) and of ephemeral diffusion through networks (Instagram) and the preservation of audiovisual, sonorous and graphic living archives.
