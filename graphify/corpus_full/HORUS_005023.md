# INFLUENCE OF THE CONSTRUCTED ENVIRONMENT AND SOCIOECONOMIC PATRONES IN THE MODAL ELECTION BY RETURN ANALYSIS ON SPACE DATA

The planning of transport policies is fundamental to study how people use the various modes of transport available and, in the same sense, it is important to know what factors have the greatest influence on the choice of a particular mode of transport. In this work, the modeling of population patterns is used to characterize the modal choice in the study area for 4 modes of transport: public transport, walking, automobile and motorcycle. To this end, three methodological stages are developed: (1) geographic accessibility modelling, (2) database configuration, and (3) statistical regression models. The results suggest important statistical relationships of variables such as vehicle tenure, travel times, level of schooling, income and age groups in the use of the modes of transport analyzed. The methodology used provides a decision-making tool for transport policy planning, adding information inputs on the impact of variables on the modal choice, with the focus on promoting modes of transport with greater spatial, environmental and economic efficiency
