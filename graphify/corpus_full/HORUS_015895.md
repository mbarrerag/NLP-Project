# Distribution of gamma radiation doses in radiosensitive gel through nuclear magnetic resonance imaging.

In this project, the dose given by ionizing radiation in the dosimetric gel with MAGIC formulation is estimated. This has been commonly used for radiation dose measurement above 1 cGy by means of regression techniques between relaxation rate and dose values. The gel is characterized by being composed of methacrylic acid, used in fantoms equivalent to soft tissue. The 99mTc radioisotope is used to radiate and thus calibrate gel samples with different dose values in the low dose range (μSv-mSv). It is proposed to perform linear regressions between measurements taking into account the standard deviation in measurement and the value of adjustments with the (χ2/DOF), such that a better adjustment is obtained. A method is presented that selects the areas of gel samples that offer greater reliability in the data and improves the correlation between nominal dose and relaxation rate.
