# Guania: triple border and cross-border dynamics

The work plan for the period 2022-20232019-2020 of the Research Group on International Relations and Global Affairs RIAG, aims to strengthen the lines of research in foreign policy and regional integration. To achieve this, it is proposed to develop a project that aims to continue the project planted in 2019. The first of these establishes the border as a crucial theme within integration dynamics, the objective being to deepen knowledge in cross-border spaces through a diagnosis of Inírida, Guainía, and the triple border with Brazil and Venezuela. Likewise, it seeks to strengthen the lines of foreign policy and regional integration with the articulation of research groups and research institutes located both at Bogotá headquarters and at the border offices of the National University of Colombia, a question that has been promoted since the previous project, with the consolidation of a working group for the publication of an Art State on the borders of Colombia, which involved the consolidation with the Cross-Border Studies Group of a Thought Center in Borders.
