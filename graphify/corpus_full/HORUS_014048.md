# Preventive maintenance for laboratory equipment prospecting and biomolecule design

This project seeks to carry out the preventive maintenance of equipment of the laboratory of prospecting and design of biomolecules, like the microplate reader Multiskan Go of the brand Thermo Scientific, Microscope Nikon model eclipse, Memmert Incubator, Incubator Shaker Orbital Lab Companion, pH meter Hanna instructmrnts Model, Micropipette Multichannel Thermo scientfic (30-300 ul) and micropipettes from 1000 to 100 ul, from 200 to 20 ul and from 20 to 2 ul. In the laboratory the main applications in which these equipment are used are tests ELISA, Bradford method, hemolysis, cellular cytotoxicity tests mainly for cell cancer lines, micro-dilution tests to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration of microorganisms and make up the final equipment kinetics of bacterial equipment (01$ sacrophone and bio-technical) (supplementation) and use of equipment (supplementation)
