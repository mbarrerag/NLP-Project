# PERMANENCES AND CHANGES IN THE ARCHEOLOGY AND BIOARCHEOLOGY OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY, THE LAST SEPTENIO FRONT TO HIS FIVE YEARS OF HISTORIA

Recently, a book was published that celebrated half a century of contributions from the Department of Anthropology of the National University of Colombia. UNAL- to the knowledge of the biocultural diversity of our country. However, the balance ends in 2016 which leaves already a whole septenium to analyze. This period is also very particular, since in it there are presented at least two key events for the formation and research in archaeology, on the one hand, the Colombian Institute of Anthropology-ICANH- established in 2017 the National Registry of Archaeologists, and on the other, came the pandemic of COVID 19 and with it, the confinement. In addition, the program of Anthropology of the UNAL is discussing its curriculum with the aim of making changes that respond to the challenges that its graduates and graduates live in the professional field. This presentation seeks to present the contributions to the research in archaeology and bioarchology that have been carried out in recent years since the National University of Colombia, as well as the changes that have already produced in the previous aspects of the formation of their students
