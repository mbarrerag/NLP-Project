# Development of photocatalytic systems type Ba1-xMxTiO3 (M = Fe, Al) supported by activated biocarbons for removal of emerging pollutants present in aqueous matrices

The presence of emerging pollutants in water represents serious risks for the environment and human health, so it is necessary to investigate new and minor methodologies that allow the efficient elimination of these pollutants. In this context, this doctoral thesis focused on the development of new materials for removal by adsorption/photocatalysis of emerging pollutants in aqueous matrix. The studies were carried out in three stages, in the first stage activated biocarbons were prepared, and their performance in the adsorption removal of acetaminophen and amoxicillin was evaluated, in order to select the appropriate material to be used as carbonous support. In the second stage, the synthesis of BaTiO3 materials replaced with iron and aluminium, and the effect of the substitution on the photocatalytic activity of the obtained materials was determined.
