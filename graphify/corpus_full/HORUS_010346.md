# Effectiveness of a context-based learning proposal to improve the learning of concentration calculation in medical students

The students of medicine need to learn and acquire skill in the calculation of the concentration of chemical solutions in order to be able to use them properly during their professional life. Evaluate the effectiveness of a didactic resource designed with the context-based learning approach (CBL) to improve the learning of the calculation of the units of concentration of chemical solutions in students of first semester of medicine of a public university. Research based on the design carried out in three phases: design, intervention and evaluation. In the first phase the didactic resource was elaborated. The intervention was carried out during the second semester of 2020 with the students of the Medicine program of the National University of Colombia enrolled in the basic chemistry subject for health sciences. An experimental study was carried out in which 66 students answered a test of pretest knowledge voluntarily and were randomly distributed in two groups. The experimental group (n=30) used the resource and then observed the differences of post-use knowledge of the national university of Colombia. The test was performed by the control group (n=36) did the opposite. Subsequently, 120 students presented the regular examination of the subject. Bivariate analysis was carried out to evaluate differences in the performance in the two groups and then the academic review of the subject
