# The “send of injustice” and its “back stairs”. About the place of tradition in Hannah Arendt’s political thinking

The aim of this research is to offer an analysis of the Arendtian interpretation of the tradition of Western political thought. The work assumes as a starting point that an adequate understanding of this concept allows to understand in more detail the different elements that make up the political theory of Hannah Arendt and, moreover, that the examination of the Arendtian conception of tradition houses an exemplary instance to understand the figures and movements that comprise its philosophical methodology. This research, then, rests on a triple characterization of the problem. On the one hand, there is an analysis of the concept of tradition in which it examines how the rupture of the tradition constitutes a challenge of methodological character in the thinking of Arendt that, in turn, is driven by the need to reinterpret the sense of politics and our relationship with time. On the other hand, there is the analysis of the Arendtian phenomenon of totalitarian government, as well as of the different elements that, according to its reading, allow to conclude that the tradition of western political thought is to exhibit a negligent attitude towards totalitarian domination. representative an exemplary instance to understand the figures and movements that encompassing her philosophy methodology.
