# MATERIAL TEST

At the beginning of this space, a talk was held that emphasized some pests that are frequent in materials used in preservation and/or storage (e.g. cotton, cardboard, yarn, etc.). Some materials susceptible to invasion by pests were identified and some materials were listed that are not suitable and suitable for use in biological collections and why of each one. Subsequently, through a demonstrative session accompanied, with the use of a guide, was taught to identify which are good and bad materials in collections from easy and economical tests, such as: - evidence of acidity (paper, cotton, ink, etc.) from a pen that detects acidity or from a glycerine test with pH-indicator paper. - Test to detect the quality of the ink. - Test to detect chlorides in plastics. - Test to detect evidence of deterioration in bottles, as well as closing of the lids. - Tests to show moisture and acidity conditions in storage rooms.
