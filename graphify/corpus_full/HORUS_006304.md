# Reflections on motivation as an operation in the phenomenology of the Husserlian

The main objective of this paper is to describe how the concept of motivation can be understood from an operational point of view in the phenomenology of Husserl. For this purpose, methodological clarifications will be made in relation to the core concepts in the phenomenological work as are those of “phenomenon” and “causality”. In turn, the treatment of the horizontal structure of the experience and the use of the meaning as “possibility of interaction” will allow to work the way for the descriptive clarification of the motivational operation.
