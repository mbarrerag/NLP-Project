# Comparative life cycle analysis as a basis for the production of a cobblestone made with concretes with recycled RCD aggregates in Bogotá

This investigation proposed the manufacture of a concrete paving block manufactured with Recycled Attachments (AR) and one manufactured with Natural Attachments (AN) to assess the relevance of the manufacture of Concrets with Recycled Attachments (CAR) as an alternative to its conventional manufacturing mode in the city of Bogotá. The above was justified by the performance of mechanical tests and a Life Cycle Analysis (LCA) comparative, which would allow validating the compliance with applicable technical requirements and the reduction of environmental impacts in its manufacturing process. The mechanical tests performed were those of abrasion, absorption and resistance to flexotration. The four concrete mixtures for manufactured cobbles obtained a result below the 23 mm of wear footprint for the abrasion test, above the 7% of mass absorption in the absorption test; and below the 2 MPa unit required for the flexotration test. Therefore, the mixtures had to be adjusted to meet the functional unit in the development stage of the LCA. For the development of the LCA, the CML-IA methodology was used, and the result of a concrete unit of the CF (fifth) The four manufactured concrete mix and obtained results under 23 mm on the abrasion test, over 7% on the absorption test and under the required 4.2 MPa for the blessing test. Becuse of this, the concrete mix and the concrete concrete mixes obtained results with the functional unit, over 7% on the utilization test and under the required 4.2 MPa for the blessing test. Our studies in this line of research and it’s estimated that the proposed concrete mixtures for the LCA stage of this investigation will be compatible with the requirements of the NTC 2017 (2018) standard for concrete investments in Colombia
