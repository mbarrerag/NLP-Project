# Method for estimation of weeds in lettuce cultures using deep learning and multispectral images.

Weed estimation is one of the most important tasks during the weed control process, since it depends on the estimation of costs to be used to protect the crop, therefore this work presents the development of a method that uses multispectral images captured by an unmanned aerial vehicle and convolutional neural networks to make the percentage estimation of weeds in lettuce crops. The method presented has an accuracy of 89% and a value-F of 94% for the detection of the crop, with an average run time of 0.4 seconds without GPU and a correlation of 0.57 in the evaluation of weed coverage in relation to a Ph.D in malherbology. These results indicate that the task of weed estimation using CNNs is more accurate and rapid than that performed by experts, but without departing from the tacit knowledge which is important in the estimation of costs and resources for the control of weedness
