# REDOX ENTITLING: REASON EXERCISE

Volumetric analyses based on titrations with reducing or oxidizing agents are very useful for many determinations. They can be performed using visual indicators or measuring the potential with an indicator electrode suitable for constructing a potentiometric titration curve. A redox titration is based on an oxide-reduction reaction between the analyte and the valuer. That is, these are those assessments in which, in the reaction that takes place between the analyte and the valuer, there is electron transfer: one of the substances gains electrons and simultaneously the other loses them
