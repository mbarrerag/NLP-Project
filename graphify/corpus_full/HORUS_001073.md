# Evaluation of the effect on the distribution charge of network operators' investments in Colombia

The growing concern for high prices of electricity in Colombia has generated a national debate on the need to regulate and stabilize these costs. The significant variation in the price indices to the producer (IPP) post-COVID-19, according to studies, indicates that its use is not the optimal option for the end users. In response to this scenario, strategies were implemented, such as the change in indexation, with the aim of mitigating energy costs. In this context, there is the need to evaluate the impact of the investments made by network operators on the distribution charge in Colombia. The evaluation of the effects of the investments made by the Network Operators (OR) is presented as a crucial aspect to determine whether these investments are translated into tangible benefits for the end users. In order to address this issue, in this final work a model is developed that simplifies the understanding of the investments affect the position, following the guidelines established in Resolution CREG 015 of 2018.
