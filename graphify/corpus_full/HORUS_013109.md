# Technical evaluation of a boom reactor for the rapid biomass pyrolysis process

It was demonstrated through modelling and simulation that a booming reactor is a viable technological alternative to obtain biofuels with yields of up to 65% by means of the fast biomass pyrolysis process. It was initially compared with different reactors currently used in the industry, identifying their advantages and disadvantages. Subsequently, the chemical reaction in the reactor is modeled assuming the flow of piston, stationary state and with the use of steel pellets as a heat transport element. The model is numerically solved and data taken from literature were used to perform a parametric analysis, allowing to determine that the reaction temperature and diameter of biomass particle have high incidence in the performance of products achieved. The model is tested using experimental data from the literature, finding good adjustment for certain operating conditions, with a relative deviation of up to 65% model
