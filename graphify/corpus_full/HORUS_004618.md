# Analysis of the hydraulic performance of the distribution networks of the aqueducts of the San Isidro, Catalamonte and Aguasimal located in areas with strong topographical slope changes in the municipality of Tena-Cundinamarca

This report describes the hydraulic analysis of the distribution networks of three rural aqueducts located in the municipality of Tena-Cundinamarca, on areas with pronounced differences of the terrain, with difference of position in each aqueduct of the order of 250, 300 and 560 meters, which causes damage to the accessories of the networks, due to the lack of an effective system to dissipate the energy. There, the problem lies in the fact that the aqueducts lack the appropriate technologies for the technical management of the water resource. In addition to this, taking into account that the administrative management of the aqueducts is carried out only by the community of the sector, there is a marked lack of economic resources; in turn, the systems have one or two plumbers to solve any inconvenience related to the operation and maintenance of the systems.
