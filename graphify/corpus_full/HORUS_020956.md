# Sustainability of agricultural systems in peasant territories of Valle del Cauca: Case study in the Morales River Basin

The prevailing agricultural model in the world is based on intensive and extractivist production that contributes to the current global systemic crisis, and leads to the degradation of the common goods, moreover, it ignores the particularities of the rural territories, and ignores the peasant culture putting at risk its existence. The loss of peasant territories increases the crisis, since these spaces can pass to monoculture type agriculture and to large agro-industriality, thus increasing the concentration of land tenure and inequality, with negative effects on the environment, the economy and the culture. This project has as general objective to evaluate the sustainability of the agricultural systems in peasant territories of the Valle del Cauca, as a contribution to agroecology, through a case study in the Río Morales basin. This territory lives in constant struggle to maintain its identity and its way of life, with an agricultural link focused on coffee and fruits, in transition to agroecology; it is part of a peasant reserve area in fact, and as a social organization part of the Association of Peasant Workers of the Valle del Cauca (ASTRACAVA) that promotes aspects of territorial planning and local governance. The project has a participatory approach and agricultural methodology
