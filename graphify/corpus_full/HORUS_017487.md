# Cancer response to treatment by chemotherapy and electrochemical therapy using biocomputation modelling and simulation.

The electroporation and electrochemotherapy models are theoretical schemes that describe electrophysiological processes based on mathematical formulations that help to understand and predict the behavior of phenomena of transport and dosage of medications by modeling and computational simulation. In this work the mass transport in electroporated tissues was simulated using techniques without mesh using a parametric study of the influence of voltage and spacing between pulses in efficiency, aggressiveness and uniformity of the electroporation process analyzing the change of concentration of medications between extracellular and intracellular spaces. The method of Radial Base Functions (RBF) was used to solve the system of differential equations of the phenomenon and the efficiency and aggressiveness of the electroporation protocol.The analyses focus on comparing the response of two chemotherapy drugs to the change of the electroporation parameters and the influence of the electrical field is obtained by the indirect effect of the drug and the indirect effect of the drug on the indirect effect of the electroporation protocol
