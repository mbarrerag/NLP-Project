# Evaluation of the sexual reproduction of wild Pasifloras in the Andean region of Colombia, a contribution to its conservation.

The Andean region in Colombia is recognized as a center of diversity for the genus Passiflora in Colombia and at a global level (Ocampo, 2010). Andean forests have undergone a process of recovery - transformation, where historically high rates of deforestation have been reported in the region resulting in high levels of fragmentation, a fundamental factor for the current distribution of Passiflora in Colombia (Caleño Ruiz and Morales- Lizcano 2019).Although at present, the higher rates of deforestation are found in other areas of the country, the anthropic pressure on the Andean forests remains a factor that puts at risk the biodiversity and the 17% of the species of Passiflora in Colombia is in some threat category.The decrease of the richness of plant species directly affects both the plant communities and their ecological relations, including the relationships between many plant species, the human communities and their culture, (García et al, 2010). Passiflora is a genus with great ecological importance.Pasifloras have multiple interactions with other organisms: bees, bees, colibries, bats and other mammals (Yocks and other mammals). sexual reproduction in species of Passiflora in the wild in the Andean forests of Supatá, Cundinamarca as a tool for the development of conservation and management strategies both at local level and exitu.
