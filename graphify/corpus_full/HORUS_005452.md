# PROCESS ASSOCIATED TO THE METRIC THOUGHT IN RECONSTRUCTION OF ADVANCED RESULTS OF THE MATHEMATICS FROM THE RESOLUTION OF PROBLEMS, IN THE SIMPOSARY MEM 2023

This work aims to contribute to the development of processes associated with metric thinking in the context of integral calculation and its connections with advanced notions of mathematical laa. It corresponds to the qualitative approach of explanatory scope. The design is based on the grounded theory (Corbin and Strauss, 2002) and the theory based on design studies (McKenney and Reeves, 2012), which is energized during the intervention process by means of 4 activities in the framework of 2 types of problems: problem of estimation of geometric quantities (of integral calculation) and problem of estimation in the context of variation (of differential equations). It is developed with undergraduate students of the National University of Colombia Manizales headquarters, of the courses of integral calculation and differential equations. The written productions of students and asymmetrical communications between these and the professor are used as sources of information.
