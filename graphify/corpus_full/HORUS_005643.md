# DEVELOPMENT OF A MODEL OF INTERACTION FOR VINCULAR DOMINIUM OF DESIGN IN TEACHING-APPRODUCT IN AECCO PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The creation of links and interaction between teams of projects of Architecture, Engineering, Construction and Operation (AECO) is a complex process, given the transitory nature and the interdisciplinary nature of the actors involved. At present, the project teams need to adapt interactively to the factors of change that are revolutionizing the practices of production and management of digital information. This represents a challenge for the training and training of students. In this sense, a model is adopted to design a model in which connectivism and social learning serve as guiding axes of the didactic structure of the subject Integrated Project Management (IMG 1) at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín headquarters. The structure is evaluated by 44 students during two academic periods, which reveals a positive acceptance and identifies ideas of improvement for their development. The study highlights a model of interaction for teaching and learning, in which the dominance and individual behaviors in the interaction processes are analyzed as variables for the pedagogical innovation of the subject and its curricular alignment in the career of Construction of the Faculty of Architecture
