# A CRITICAL REVIEW OF THE ADMISSION MODEL FOR ACADEMIC STRATEGIES IN COLOMBIA NATIONAL UNIVERSITY

In the last decade, the process of admission to undergraduate programs at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, a public higher education entity, has presented significant changes motivated by the need to optimize the use of offered quotas, understood as scarce public goods. Based on a statistical analysis of some indicators of the admission process to undergraduate curricula at the Facultad de Ciencias, Bogotá headquarters, there is little or no optimization of the use of quotas, in contrast to the justification for introducing the academic strata model into the admission process. The most significant effect of this model is academic homogenization, with high scores, of the admitted applicants; and that it goes against social policies that allow the entry of low-level applicants
