# Comparison of performance of reinforced concrete buildings designed with two different seismic demand specifications

The assessment of the seismic threat is a fundamental element for the seismic-resistant regulation, since it establishes the minimum level of seismic demand required to guarantee an appropriate level of security in the design and construction of the buildings. Currently, there are two proposals of seismic coefficients for the design of structures, which present important differences with respect to the design spectra, site coefficients and seismic threat zoning. In this work we analyze the performance of buildings with reinforced concrete porticoes designed following the seismic demand specifications given by these two proposals for the Colombian territory: (i) the one proposed by the Colombian Association of Seismic Engineering (AIS) and (ii) the one presented by the Colombian Geological Service.
