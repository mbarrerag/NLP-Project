# Joanne Rappaport. The coward does not make history. Orlando Fals Borda and the beginnings of participatory research-action.

This new book by Joanne Rappaport continues to approach the agrarian struggles—especially indigenous ones—in Colombia and the forms of knowledge that occur around them. It defines it as an ethnographic history of the beginnings of the Participatory Action Research (IAP) promoted by Orlando Fals Borda in the south of the Atlantic coast in the early 1970s. In our view, this work is an IAP exercise on the origins of the IAP, although not literally, since many of the interlocutors, starting with Fals Borda, are no longer among us
