# CLIMATE ADAPTING STRATEGIES IN THE MUNICIPIA OF THE CAUCA - COLOMBIA VALLE

AC (CC) and climate variability (CV) are phenomena that usually generate negative changes in economic, social and environmental systems; they produce different levels of risks, threats and vulnerability, in each of these systems. The VC affects the existing infrastructure, compromises the subsistence, environmental governance, ecosystem services, increase poverty and reduce the quality of life in populations. Colombia, like other Latin American countries, is not a great generator of Greenhouse Gas (GEI) effects, but if it has negative impacts due to consequences of the CC and VC, such as floods, heat waves, marginalization and poverty, droughts, sea level rise, forest fires and loss of human lives, ecosystems and biodiversity. What this implies, that these countries should implement climate adaptation strategies (AC) as a priority, in order to reduce the levels of vulnerability and climate risk, according to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), the AC in developing countries is vital and has been highlighted as a high or urgent priority.
