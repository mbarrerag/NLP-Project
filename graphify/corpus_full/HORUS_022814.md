# A method for the identification and early prevention of cybersecurity incidents on Internet of Things devices.

The increase in the implementation of cyberphysical devices connected to the Internet in recent years has made them a focus of interest for potential attackers who see an opportunity to violate systems that are composed of these devices such as IoT systems. Due to the particular conditions of such devices such as their processing capacity, the duration of their battery, among others, is added an additional challenge to be able to protect the information associated with IoT systems in terms of availability, confidentiality and integrity. Thus, in this research work, a method based on the union of convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks for the detection and classification of denial attacks of services in the IoT context is proposed. For this method, data flows are transformed into a three-channel image format, a method that has proved effective for the detection of attacks that compromise cybersecurity. The generated model has promising results with a correct rating rate of more than 99 per cent in the detection of attacks and a rate higher than 96 per cent for the classification of attacks addressed
