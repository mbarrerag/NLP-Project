# Nine class summaries for the Inter-American Housing Center and their effect on the construction of the concept of “Communal Action” in Bogotá, 1952-1960

The significant influence that Bogotá experienced in its socio-urban transformations, marked by important actors, ideals and collective action, manifested itself in various products, including the Class Reports of the CINVA published in the 1960s. Such documents influenced the formation and knowledge of the Community Action (AC) in Bogotá and its subsequent introduction into the academic field, positioning education as a catalyst for the study of community dynamics. Through their reading and analysis, it is expected that this research shed light on a part of the urban evolution of Bogotá during the 1960s and provide lessons from the past with contemporary applications, thus contributing to the decision-making process in the urban arena. Ultimately, it seeks to understand more fully the interactions between formation, social change and community development in Bogotá . of the role played by the documents of interest on CA.
