# Laboratory of co-creation and social innovation for the strengthening of food communities in the municipality of Tumaco, Nariño.

The laboratory of co-creation and social innovation for the strengthening of communities of food in the municipality of Tumaco, Nariño, has as objective, to design and develop a strategy of co-creation and social innovation in the right to food, under the focus of the research participatory action; that allows to contribute to the organizational strengthening, the construction of communities of food and to the improvement of the situation of food and nutrition insecurity in the municipality of Tumaco. In the framework of this initiative, the participation of undergraduate students, of the program PEAMA resident in Tumaco, of postgraduate and researchers and graduates of the National University, some that are part of the Observatory of Sovereignty and Food and Nutritional Security, on the other hand, will count with the participation of the communities, in the lead of the Association of Women Canasteando del Bajo Mira and Frontera of rural base and the initiative of consolidation of the urban council of the municipality 5, Raizales and Ancestrales of the South Pacific, therefore it will count with the participation of both urban and rural area and a high percentage of students. This process will be implemented in 2 (two) phases, the first of the institution of the implementation of the Leader and of the South National Tumaco, the National Chair of Problematic Knowledge and Challenges of the Colombian Pacific and the linking and participation of PEAMA students. Finally, it is promoted by the production of ethno-educational tools and other contents, which from the alternative communication for DHA, contribute to the visibility of the situation and generate transformational bets led by the communities
