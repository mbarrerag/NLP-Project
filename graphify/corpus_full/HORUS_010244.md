# Design lighting a hospital floor by applying the WELL standard.

The Circadian cycle has been taken as a very important topic for the human being especially in the area of health and work. The correct lighting of a given space helps to regulate the circadian cycle of people and also regulate their melatonin levels, the hormone produced by the body responsible for regulating day and night cycles / cycles of vigil and sleep. For this case of study, the lighting design of the hospital floor focuses mainly on two spaces: the hospital rooms, where the patients in recovery require specific lighting levels and the nurses' work area to prevent the circadian cycle of health workers from being altered or affected due to their long working hours. A comparison of 2 lighting designs for the hospital floor will be made: the first basic design meeting only the requirements of minimum lighting levels according to an international or national standard endorsed (as currently carried out in Colombia) and a second design not only taking into account the requirements of visual lighting levels, but also visual comfort, circadian cycle and lighting requirements of the Well standard in 2 areas (Hospitalization and Nurse Area) (Text)
