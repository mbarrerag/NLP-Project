# IDENTIFICATION OF RELEVANT FACTORS FOR TRANSFER OF TECHNOLOGY IN THE UNIVERSITY CONTEXT

The transfer of technology generated by universities to the market through formal mechanisms such as licensing or the creation of spin-off generally finds several difficulties. This transfer process involves different actors with diverse and even contrary interests and encompasses several stages that must be drawn to achieve it successfully. For this reason many universities have very few success cases to show, even when they have a vocation to applied research and carry out many projects in company with industry, as in the case presented in this work. Faced with this problem, it was sought to contribute towards the understanding of the transfer process, specifically identifying the main factors that affect the achievement of a successful technology transfer. For this purpose, a review of the literature on factors that affect the success of the transfer of technologies generated at the university, from which an exhaustive list of factors was constructed, which were grouped according to their similarities, which resulted in a list of 40 factors that were classified into 6 categories: technology, inventor, market and financial, socio-economic development, intellectual property and institution.
