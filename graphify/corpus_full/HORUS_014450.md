# Image sorting method, using artificial intelligence techniques, integrated into an intelligent agriculture IoT platform.

The majority of the variables that are measured in an agricultural crop can only be detected visually, for example, the inventory of plants and fruits, the development and the phenological stages of a crop or the presence of pests and diseases.The arrival of the technologies of Industry 4.0 to agriculture, has given this domain the possibility to solve many of its problems.One of the tools that are currently being implemented are the platforms based on the Internet of Things (IoT for its acronyms), better known as platforms of Intelligent Agriculture.However, many times the agricultural farms cover very large and/or remote areas, in which it is not easy to access energy resources or connectivity. Therefore, implementing technologies as those offered by Industry 4.0 sometimes becomes a challenge. This research work has as main objective to contribute in the solution of this type of problems, when proposing a method of classification of images integrated into an intelligent agricultural platform, which seeks to reduce the computational cost of the digital image classification process, applied in a rural context with devices that have limitations in its proposed computing capacity. The first part of this research is focused on the strategy. The method selected was MobileNet, with the following performance results: 96.31 % accuracy (Accuracy), 95.55 % accuracy (Precision), 95.55 % sensitivity (Recall), 95.93% sensitivity, F1–95.72 % score, with 3,762.056 parameters and a size of 28,7 MB. Finally, the method selected was evaluated in three scenarios of reduction of its architecture, to know its robustness by having to adapt to conditions with limited computing capabilities. For the evaluation, an intelligent agriculture platform was implemented in real working conditions, in two production units of tomato under greenhouse cultivation, obtaining metrics above 90 % in all cases
