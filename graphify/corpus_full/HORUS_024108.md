# ELABORATION OF DIDACTIC RESOURCES FOR THE TEACHING AND LEARNING OF THE ANALYTORY CRIMINAL CRIMINAL CRIMINAL CRIMINAL CRIMINAL CRIMINAL CRIMINAL FOR SCHOOL GROSS

The teaching of random thinking has revealed shortcomings at the primary level, from the lack of clarity in content and level of complexity by degree to the lack of concrete material for it. This project aims to generate strategies that facilitate teaching and learning of this competence by degree, taking into account the learning abilities of students in each one, and giving adequate and assertive management to random thinking. Texts will be designed for each grade of primary school, with the aim of contextualizing teachers in the specific topics to be addressed in accordance with the basic learning standards and basic learning rights (DBA). The texts will be worked out considering the formats and standards used by the Ministry of National Education of Colombia (MEN) with the aim of making them possible in the future to be used in all schools of the country. Work is being done to address the gaps in content and connectivity in the present situation of Covid and to serve to work remotely with and without technology. This material aims to facilitate inclusive learning for children of Manizales, with a view to extending it to all teachers in the department of Caldas and later to develop to all of Colombia
