# Study of the influence of temperature on pyrolysis in biosolid fluidized bed reactor generated in the PTAR El Salitre

The production of biosolids in the treatment plants generates problems of environmental pollution and public health. The city of Bogotá D.C. seeks to develop a strategy of valorization of biosolids in response to the constant increase in the production of them. In this work a study is carried out of the pyrolysis of biosolids produced in the PTAR El Salitre using the fluidized bed technology in order to establish the influence of the temperature in the process. Initially, the setting up of the experimental equipment that included the coupling of heating and thermal insulation systems for different components of the installation, the installation of a system of condensation of tars and the adaptation of a point for the sampling of gases was carried out.
