# Effectiveness of an on-line group intervention based on Contextual Therapy for the Regulation of Wrath in young adults

The majority of treatments for the regulation of anger are in a face-to-face format, emphasis on the reduction of symptoms and minimize the functional and positive markers of emotion. Except the Contextual Therapy for the Regulation of Ira (CART) based on a transdiagnostic approach (transdiagnostic approach). The general objective of the study was to evaluate the effectiveness of a group intervention in on-line format based on CART, applied to a sample of a single group, consisting of 12 young university adults (age: M = 25.83 years). The intervention consisted of six main sessions and one follow-up three weeks later. The measuring instruments that were applied were the Anger and Violence Impairment Scale, the Questionnaire of Acceptance Action II, the Questionnaire of Cognitive Fusion, the Questionnaire of values and the Questionnaire of the “values” and the Minority Attention of the influence. The instruments that were applied were applied in four moments of the intervention (“preatt”, “intermedia”, “post” and “seguiment”).
