# TO REFERENCY

With the article I propose to show that the teaching of history can be carried out on the basis of literary works. For this, I contribute some perspectives on literature to base the pedagogical act of teaching/learning of history. With this, I will result in the exposition of the experience that is carried out in the subject of Humanities at the National University of Colombia, Manizales headquarters. In the article I show that, based on the pedagogical experience referenced, attending the academic study of history with literary works provides a methodological practice with more favorable emotional disposition in students, while it is possible to pass the informative borders and methodological technicalities of teaching; thus, it reaches areas of critical and propositional understanding on the contents framed in the field of study of history. One of the conclusions is that the literature concretes its work as a social fact by giving account of the subjectivity of its creator and the social environment in which this emerges
