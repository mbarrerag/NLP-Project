# TECHNOLOGICAL INNOVATION IN THE PLANNING OF CIVIL CONSTRUCTIONS USING A 4D-PS TOOL

This paper presents an innovative method for planning construction projects which face great challenges to meet the budget and the scheduled dates, due to the direct incidence of uncontrollable external factors such as weather, dollar volatility and inflation. The innovation lies in the application of a methodology based on 4D-PS model in a real construction project in the city of Medellín (Colombia), with the aim of obtaining a better estimation of the costs and time required in the project.
