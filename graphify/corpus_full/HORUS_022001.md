# Barriers to access to health care perceived by populations vulnerable to HIV and/or HCV in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic in Colombia.

Since the COVID-19 was declared a pandemic by the World Health Organization (WHO) in March 2020, a series of prevention measures have been necessary to curb the contagion caused by it. These measures have led to a disruption of services unprecedented in society, with consequences at the economic, social and health levels, exacerbating the existing disparities in the matter and deepening the inequalities between marginalized groups, such disparities and inequalities can have severe consequences in the medium and long term, especially in communicable diseases and chronic pathologies such as HIV and HCV. To analyze the barriers of access to health care perceived by populations vulnerable to HIV and/or HCV in the framework of the COVID-19 pandemic in Colombia.
