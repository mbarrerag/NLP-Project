# DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION IN THE PUBLIC UNIVERSITIES OF COLOMBIA.

Digital transformation is the profound transformation of business and organizational activities, processes, competencies and models to fully and strategically leverage and prioritize the changes and opportunities of a combination of digital technologies? (Gobble, 2018). Digital transformation is a complex problem because it covers the full scope of the organization and requires a complete rethinking of skill sets and investment strategy. As a result of the rapid pace of technological change and pervasive technologies that emerge almost daily, there is no "one-size-fits-all solution" that supports effective digital transformation in organizations. (Carcary et al., 2002). Higher education institutions have responded to the impact of digitization through multiple, separate and uncoordinated initiatives. As a result, they run the risk of not considering important elements of digital transformation or ignoring solutions that are more favourable to specific situations, which could have unintended adverse consequences. (T Hess et al., 2016)
