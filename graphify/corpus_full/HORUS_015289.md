# MULTI-PARKABLE POLINOMIAL RETURN METHOD FOR CALIBRATING CAMERAS TO MEASURE LIGHT

This document describes a practical method for calibrating in luminance, three cameras based on CMOS technology and evaluating their performance as luminancmeters. To begin, the XYZ tristimulus values were calculated by processing data files in 14-bit RAW format of some 150 images of a luminance reference, taken to fill the device sensor. After that, a three-dimensional surface of weighting factors for luminance was determined, using a multivariable polynomial adjustment. The implemented regression method gives a luminance error of less than 6%, when compared with the measurement of a calibrated standard luminancimeter. It is noticeable at the end of this performance evaluation, that the three cameras with different brands and technologies are suitable devices for measuring luminance levels in road lighting
