# Meanings and practices of youth motherhood in the City of Bolívar de Bogotá, D.C.

The objective of the research is to understand the meanings and practices of motherhood from the experiences and experiences of a group of young mothers of the City of Bolívar de Bogotá D.C. A qualitative methodological approach of phenomenological cut that allowed to collect the experiences and experiences located of the young women on the youth motherhood. Semistructured interviews and observation were carried out participant to 16 young women with antecedent of motherhood between the 15-19 years. The results are described in three units of meaning: 1. Metamorphosis, which contemplates the relationship loving, pregnancy, childbirth and the first months with the child. 2. Juvenile maternity - refers to the meaning that the phenomenon of motherhood has in young women; and finally 3. The young mothers - are explored the limitations and/or ruptures that deepen in the youth with the act of maternalness. Within the main findings is found that motherhood is not seen as a problem or defect, and from this place women reconstruct a new way of being and inhabiting the world. Their practices are aimed at the care, protection and provision of the means of life of the children. analytical units: 1.
