# MODELING OF HYSTERETIC SYSTEMS USING PHYSICS-GUIDED UNIVERSAL ORDINARY DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

This work introduces a framework for modeling and identifying hysteretic structural systems using multilayer perceptrons and physical principles. The framework models hysteresis based on universalordinary differential equations (UODEs) [1] and physics-guided neural networks (PGNNs) [2], two machine learning models that combine data with scientific knowledge. The proposed models are UODEs that approximate equations of motion with system state dynamics, using multilayer perceptrons to represent the unknown components. The training of the models follows the theory of PGNNs and incorporates data and physics to avoid the physical violations. The framework is validated with experimental data from ferrocement and recycled plastic lumber walls, showing good accuracy, generalization, and physical consistency. These results may suggest that the proposed hysteretic model could be an alternative to the Bouc-Wen model because they meet its accuracy and versatility while avoiding some of its physical inconsistencies, such as the lack of an elastic range and theviolations of the plasticity postulates [3]
