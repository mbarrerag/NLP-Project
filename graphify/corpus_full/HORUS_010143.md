# Methodological proposal for quality assurance in the avocado hass supply chain, through the logistics of departure for the port of Cartagena.

The objective of this project was to develop a methodological proposal for quality assurance in the avocado hass supply chain, through the logistics of departure in the port of Cartagena. To this end, a descriptive methodology was used to analyze the technical conditions of the supply chain, process management and the logistics of exit from the macro to the micro. For the collection of data, the three companies with the greatest quantity of tons exported of avocados were selected by the port of Cartagena in the last two years. As information collection instruments, surveys are used to identify the needs of the logistic process of departure vs the normative requirements that apply to the fruit; also, interviews are carried out to identify needs of the logistics of departure and field observations to recognize the internal processes in the terminal carried from the port
