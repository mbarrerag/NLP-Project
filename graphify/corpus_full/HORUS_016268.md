# ANALYSIS OF THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN CHANGES OF LAND COBERTS, TERRESTERAL SUPERFITIAL TEMPERATURE AND THE FRAGMENTATION OF THE LANDWHERE IN THE DRMI THE CALLS IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE COLOMBIAN POSCONFLICT

The postconflict configuration of a new stage in the history of Colombia, and together with the global climate change, involve multiple challenges to face. A region strongly impacted by the armed conflict is the Anti-Oqueño East, where have been implemented Regional Districts of Integrated Management for the conservation of biodiversity and the sustainable management of natural resources. The behavior of the surface temperature is related to the evaporation of water in the hydrological cycle, affecting the spatial and temporal distribution of rains. Thus, the alteration of the surface temperature and the precipitation can accommodate processes of desertification, decrease of the bodies of water and affectation in the health of the ecosystems with emphasis on vegetation. in 2015, the regional autonomous corporation cornare, declared the regional district of integrated management - drmi las camelias, which is in the jurisdiction of the municipalities of guatapé, granada, san carlos and san rafael, department of antioquia, colombia.
