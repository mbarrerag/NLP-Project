# ANALYSIS OF SUSTAINABILITY IN THE MILK PRODUCTION OF THE CUNDIBOUS HIGHLIGHT BECOME A TOOL FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF PUBLIC POLICY FOR THE PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION AND BUILDING OF PEACE IN COLOMBIA.

This chapter is a case of research on productive inclusion and territorial peace for rural development, whose approaches are detailed in the first two chapters of this book. Milk production occupies more than 380,000 producers, producing about 7,393 million liters per year in 1104 municipalities, a large part produced by small and medium producers (less than 25 cows). It is estimated that this sector generates more than 900,000 direct jobs, its contribution to the gross domestic product (GDP) is estimated at 12 % of the (GDP) agricultural and 25 % of the livestock. In this context, an analysis of the sustainability in dairy production of six (6) farms in the Cundiboyacense highland in search of identifying threats, opportunities strengths and weaknesses of these systems was selected (3) commercial farms, with at least twenty cows in the milk sector, and three (3) peasant farms, with ten or fewer cows in the milk sector.
