# Methodological proposal for teaching the Sustainable Development Goals from the issue of dissolutions.

This work focused on the design and development of a hydroponic culture, following some elements of the methodology of Project-based Learning. For its development, a cross-sectional work was done that involved social and chemistry subjects whose objective was that the participants (44 students of the eleventh grade of the Liceo Navarra) could clarify the concepts of dissolution, dilution and concentration units, and then be able to associate them with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in a real context. The project was designed following a mixed research model, that is, with a quantitative and qualitative approach to the collection, processing and analysis of the data. The project was carried out during the Covid-19 pandemic, so that it was developed in two moments: virtual, theoretical and face-to-face part the practical part. The project had a greater impact than expected, since not only the students clarified the proposed disciplinary concepts, but they managed to establish relationships between their learnings and the SDGs, allowing also integrating knowledge from different areas.
