# LESSONS LEARNED IN SOCIAL APPROPRIATION OF KNOWLEDGE IN FAMILY CAREERS OF PERSONS WITH CRONICAL DISEASE

Special number INTRODUCTION: Family caregivers of people with chronic disease are faced with individual, family and social changes, which affect their quality of life and are exposed to healthy and unhealthy practices. Transfer the knowledge generated in the line of adoption of role of the caregiver of the person with chronic disease. Social Knowledge Appropriation Strategy (SAC) with a community-based participatory research approach. The following phases were contemplated: review and update of scientific literature, identification of actors, situational diagnosis; planning and development of strategy, and follow-up. In Bogotá and Cajicá (Colombia), with family caregivers of people with chronic conditions. The need to dynamize networks through ASC strategies was confirmed. Focal groups were carried out in which 21 themes were prioritized and addressed jointly between caregivers and the Young Talent. Pre and post-est application of the Rol instrument in which an increase in the adoption of the role was evidenced.
