# EXPERIMENTAL CASES IN THE INTER-AMERICAN CENTRE OF LIVING-CINVA, 1952-1956

This article, the partial result of an ongoing investigation, describes how, during its first years of operation, the Inter-American Housing Center (Cinva) adopted the method of progressive development in order to design and build prototype low-cost housing and thus contribute to the solution of the existing housing deficit. The result was a set of experimental houses conceived under the principles of standardization, prefabrication and industrialization; however, a turn towards the production of housing by self-construction marked the end of this interesting technological experience in the Latin American context
