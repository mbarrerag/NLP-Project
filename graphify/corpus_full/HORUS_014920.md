# RINGING THE STAR SHIRP

Sounding is the use of voiceless audio to transmit information and can be used to support inclusive astronomy, particularly in the development of initiatives to approach the wonders of the universe and create accessible content for audiences with visual impairment. In this work we present the design and implementation of a device to soundly reproduce the brightness of stars, turning it into audible sounds. The device consists of a light sensor, a 16-bit digital analog converter and arduino. The sensor can be installed in the eyepiece of a telescope to acquire light data, thus obtaining a sound as output, with different frequencies depending on the apparent magnitude of the star observed by the instrument
