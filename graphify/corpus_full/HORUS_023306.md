# ENTRY OF THE NEW GRANADA KINGDOM AND THE REAL AUDIT OF QUITO,

The learning of a trade has been poorly studied in comparative Indian territories. In the few cases this process was carried out by the offices of the scribes to be formalized, but in the learning writings found in notary volumes, there is information about the life of artisans, apprentices and the obligations they assumed to ensure the dissemination of knowledge and the increase of labour in the workshops. The presentation will analyze in a preliminary way, the agreements between artisans of different trades and young people in the New Kingdom of Granada and the Royal Audience of Quito during the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries, in order to understand the fabric of these negotiations
