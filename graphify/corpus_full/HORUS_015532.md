# BAD SCPRESS TEACHING IN SCHOOL AGE

Cardiovascular diseases are the biggest cause of death in the world (1-2), ischemic heart disease predominates (3), with its most lethal complication being cardiorespiratory unemployment (PCR) (4). 70% of PCR is presented extrahospitally, including the workplace, housing and public way. The initial care falls on the people around (5). For this reason, it is fundamental, that the community from early ages, is trained in basic life support (SVB), since the timely start of reanimation and early defibrillation increase by 28% the survival (6). to socialize the experience of the project UN- Salvavidas Kids in their process of teaching SVB to children of school age in the city of Bogotá. Project of solidarity extension led by teachers and nursing students.
