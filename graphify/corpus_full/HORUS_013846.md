# Development of an easy-to-implement alternative for hydrophobic modification of Portland cement concrete and mortars.

This project will investigate the effect of three hydrophobic agents used as additives to develop novel air lime mortars and concrete Portland cement that provide effective protection against water infiltration by prolonging the life of these materials. The research work is divided into four stages and includes in the first stage the preparation and characterization of all constituent materials of the mixtures, including the evaluation of the properties and the morphology of hydrophobic agents, following the national and international standards established for this purpose. The stage II includes the preparation and characterization of live lime mortars to which the hydrophobic agents will be added in three different doses and their properties will be determined in fresh and hardened state as they are fluidity, consistency, decoupling times, compressive resistance and durability, following national and international standards. The influence of hydrophobic agents will be investigated and the concrete proportion of the mortars will be determined using the techniques of SEM, TG-DSC, FT-IR, XRD and heat-thermic. In addition the contact angle of the concrete agents will be measured with the water, 0 %. Finally, in stage IV, the analysis of results and the presentation of reports and publications will be carried out
