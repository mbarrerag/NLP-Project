# Catalogue of Latin American composers for solo percussion

In Latin America, the repertoire for percussion has been quite explored and addressed. Already since the beginning of the twentieth century, composers like Amadeo Roldán (Cuba) and Carlos Chávez (Mexico) wrote for formats of percussion ensemble. The Rhythmics V and VI of Amadeo Roldán are the first works written for percussion ensemble.
