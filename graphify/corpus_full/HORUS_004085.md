# MODELS AND REFERENCE MARKS FOR RISK MANAGEMENT IN DIGITAL ENVIRONMENTS

The role of information and communication technologies (ICT) in the day-to-day strategy and operation of organizations results in the consolidation of dependencies that may become critical to their survival, and even lead to levels of uncertainty that should be adequately managed through the incorporation of good corporate and technological governance practices and, in particular, appropriate models and methodologies for managing digital risk. In this framework, this book aims to raise awareness of the broad spectrum of information technology risk management. The reader will find here an overview of digital risk in contemporary society, a description of the risk management of information technologies as a discipline, some existing ICT risk methodologies and cybersecurity, as well as some elements proposed by the authors from a multiprospective methodology that contributes to the reduction of technological uncertainty in organizations. The topics addressed in this publication aim to provide a holistic look at digital risk management and contribute some elements that contribute to incorporating this variable as part of good technological governance practices
