# DRYING KINETICS OF ORGANIC PARCHMENT COFFEE BEANS (LARBIAN COFFEE L.) USING A FLUIDISED BED: A SEMI THEORETICAL MICROWAVE MODELING

The aim of this research was to model semi-mitesically the kinetics of drying with microwave and fluidized coffee beans bed (CoffeaarabicaL.). A completely random design was implemented with six treatments: three with microwave (MD) and the remaining with fluidized bed (MFBD). Drying curves were established to reach a final moisture content of between 10 and 12% b.h. The results indicated that drying time decreased with the increase of the power level. When the MFBD was used, drying times increased by approximately 4 times with respect to MD treatments. The effective diffusivity values for drying the grains with MD and MFBD varied between 0.913 -1,717×109 m2.s-1 and 0.228 -0,417×10 m2.s-1, respectively. Although all models presented good adjustments, the two-term model presented the highest R2 (0,98) and lowest RMSE (0.0128-0.0285).
