# HANDS TO THE CUENCA. Social Laboratory for Integrated Water and Territory Management in Suesca - Phase 2

The project Manos a la Cuenca (MAC): Social laboratory for the integrated management of water and territory in Suesca was born in 2016 as a collective initiative for the strengthening of the relations between the local community of the municipality of Suesca and the National University of Colombia (through the Facultades de Ciencias Humanas, Ingeniería y Biología) in the development of community-based scientific and technological projects. In the first phase, developed with resources from the call for extension of Solidarity - Social Innovation for Peace 2016 were integrated knowledge of geography, engineering and biology with local knowledge in the study of integrated water and territory management. Among the most important results taking into account the sustainability of the process, it is necessary to highlight: The adoption by the Educational Institution Departamental Pablo VI of the initiative Manos a la Cuenca as an institutional project that constitutes its School Environmental Project (PRAE), the formation of a local group of the MAC that has given continuity to the process since the completion of Phase 1 and the generation of technical inputs for water management and biodiversity by the territorial authorities and communities.
