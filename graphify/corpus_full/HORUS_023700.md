# Psychological and social factors relevant to education, ecotourism and environmental conservation in the Chingaza National Natural Park (2020)

This research arises from the need to find routes to optimize and improve the articulation of the processes of environmental education, ecotourism and environmental conservation, in the National Natural Park (PNN) Chingaza. An exploratory-descriptive study was carried out to identify the main dynamics and problems associated with the territory of Chingaza, that is, the PNN Chingaza and its area of influence. The method included qualitative and quantitative assessments that involved the actors of the territory, starting from an exercise of action-participation research and knowledge management. The analysis of the content of the responses of the actors in the different stages, allowed to identify the main psychological and social factors present in the dynamics that occur in environmental education, ecotourism and conservation in the PNN Chingaza. The results of the different stages of the study, showed to be compatible and complementary, keeping similarity with various previous research carried out in protected areas, as well as coherence and support from the theoretical framework. Finally, some proposals for optimization and integration of strategic processes, aimed at achieving sustainable tourism in the territory. .
