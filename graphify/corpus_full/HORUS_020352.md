# Doctoral Journals: applying the conceptual-theoretical-empirical structure (CTE) to nursing research

As the disciplinary knowledge of Nursing evolves, it is strengthened, and its use in practice or research may seem diffuse or complex. Applying the empirical theoretical conceptual system (S-CTE) clarifies, simplifies, articulates, delimits and guides practice and research. Simplifies the complex!-- Contraved
