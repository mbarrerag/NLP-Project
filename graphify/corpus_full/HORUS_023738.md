# ADVANCEMENT TO RISK FACTORS RELATING TO ALZEIMER’S DISEASE

The aim of this article is to develop an application that integrates technologies of artificial intelligence, machine learning and cloud computing to reduce the user’s chances of suffering from Alzheimer’s disease in the future. Expectations regarding the application include that it can collect data on the most common risk factors in Alzheimer’s patients such as educational level, hormonal and nutritional levels and lifestyles (smoking, sedentaryism, alcohol, etc.) to prevent Alzheimer’s. It is also expected that it can determine the level of danger and the main risk factors that the user should know. This software will continue to be run in cloud computing to ensure that it is always synchronized with the databases containing the factors described above, such as MEDLINE, PubMed, AlzForum (ALZRISK) and DNAI. The latter compiles different data sources such as AIBL and DoD-ADNI
