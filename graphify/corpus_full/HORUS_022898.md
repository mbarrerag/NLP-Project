# Femicide: an analysis from the general preventive purpose of criminal law.

Act No. 1761 of 2015 establishes the autonomous crime of femicide, which makes it possible to make the extreme violence against women visible because of their gender status; a phenomenon that has occurred throughout history. This research aims to publicize the relationship between femicide and negative general prevention, in order to reveal whether the increase in penalties contributes to the reduction of these gender-based violence; to this end, the conceptual bases of femicide will be reconstructed and the theories of punishment, as well as empirical data will be provided to respond to this problem. Finally, a criminal policy will be proposed with a gender perspective and a preventive character to address these behaviours from education
