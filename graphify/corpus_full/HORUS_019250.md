# PRESENTISM AND STORY OF THE PRESENT TIME: ELEMENTS FOR A CURRENT DISCUSSION OF THE HISTORY OF THE RAPHY

The article explores the reflections around the present tenses and history as a backdrop to the current work of historians. It addresses the main thesis of François Hartog and Hans Ulrich Gumbrecht on the presentistic experience, characterizes this historiographical perspective in connection with the return of the event and deepens the contributions of three authors who advocate the distanced study of the present. The text closes with a call to think of the current historiographic practice in function of the dominant order of time as an exercise of critical reception of some debates of the theory of history
