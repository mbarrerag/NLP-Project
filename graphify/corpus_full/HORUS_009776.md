# ITUTUJI. THE MOUNTE CHACK.

Collaborative process with the community Carijona de Puerto Nare (Guaviare) of contextualization of the world of plants, origin, stories, types and varieties. They dialogue local knowledge with academic knowledge, to present one by one the plants known by the community. They present stories, characters and associated songs, a guide for taxonomic identification with names in Spanish, Carijona and Latin. The map of a tour proposes an exercise of plant identification that is articulated with an ecological path created by the community in its shelter
