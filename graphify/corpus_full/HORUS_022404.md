# GLOBAL QUALITY OF MIGRANT RESIDENT FOOD POPULATION IN CHILE

the migration process involves change, modification or acquisition of new patterns and forms of food, which do not always have a positive impact on the health of migrants, being frequently associated with a higher risk of metabolic diseases and malnutrition due to excess when the quality of the food is not adequate. to analyze the global food quality index (ICGA) in the Colombian migrant population in Chile, comparing it with the Chilean and non-migrant Colombian populations. Material and method: the total sample was 834 individuals over 18 years old: 206 Colombian migrants residing in Chile, 266 Colombians residing in Colombia and 362 Chileans. A survey of the overall food quality index was applied to them to categorize how healthy the food pattern of the participants is. there are significant differences in all the ICGA scores as regards healthy, unhealthy and food times. It was observed that Colombians living in Colombia have the highest healthy classification score while migrant Colombians present the highest figures within the unhealthy condition.
