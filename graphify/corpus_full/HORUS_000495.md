# Optimization and efficiency in the ARSINT LTDA dining room: Comprehensive proposal to reduce and manage food waste

This work develops a replicable and capable of improvement tool, oriented to companies in the food services sector, whose practical implementation seeks to contribute effectively to the reduction of food waste. This proposal is aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals set out in the 2030 Agenda, while promoting the optimization of operational processes and the reduction of costs in the organizations that adopt it. In particular, the implementation of the Food Management and Prevention Programme, corresponding to the specific objective 3 of this research, offers a comprehensive approach that allows not only the obtaining of favourable economic results, but also the promotion of territorial development initiatives through the promotion of sustainable markets and short marketing circuits. In addition, the tool represents a significant contribution in terms of food security and environmental sustainability
