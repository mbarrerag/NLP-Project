# BRIOPHITICS OF TRONES IN DISPOSAL OF THE BIOLOGICAL STATION THE ZAFIRE, AMAZONS-COLOMBIA

ABSTRACT In this work, the briofytic flora was characterized in decaying trunks in the meteorological station El Zafire, Amazonas-Colombia. To this end, a sampling of 100 sample units was carried out in 20 trunks in different decomposition state and in two types of forest (firm land and floodable rod). 120 species, 81 liver species and 39 mosses, belonging to 55 genera (33 liver and 22 mosses) and 21 families (nine liver and 12 mosses) were reported. Four new species were recorded for the country, 25 for the Colombian Amazon and 41 for the Amazon department. In terms of total wealth, the varillal forest presented a higher value than that of the firm earth forest.
