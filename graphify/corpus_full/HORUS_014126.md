# Photodegradation of emerging pollutants with zinc oxide-based materials

Emerging pollutants are chemical compounds or anthropogenic material suspected to be present in different ecosystems and whose toxicity or persistence is likely to adversely affect flora and fauna in a significant way and represent a growing environmental and public health concern. One class of emerging pollutants is pharmaceutical products, among which are the antibiotics that are frequently detected in various environmental matrices, particularly in water samples, reflecting a high consumption of these medicines globally. Therefore, it is necessary to investigate processes or methodologies that allow to decrease the concentration of emerging pollutants in water. In addition, the generation of agro-industrial waste in a country such as Colombia represents an environmental challenge. This makes it necessary to implement chemical transformations of agro-industrial waste to obtain value added products under the circular economy approach. In this project, it is proposed to synthesize materials based on zinc oxide (ZnO) supported by silica and modified with Bi2O3 for the degradation of emerging pollutants. The silica will be obtained from rice husks, the use of waste from the rice industry is striking, and is one of the main crops in Colombia and this allows to give added value to this eco-friendly economy
