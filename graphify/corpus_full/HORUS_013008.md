# Interventions by the nursing professional in the management of anxiety in women diagnosed with breast cancer in chemotherapy treatment: a scoping review

According to WHO, breast cancer represents one of the most prevalent pathologies in the female population, for it the choice of treatment due to the specific pathology to be treated may represent the difference between living and dying. Among the multiple therapeutic options for Cancer, chemotherapy (QT) represents the most widely used, it is in itself generating a series of side effects, which if not controlled can generate changes in physical and psycho-emotional health. Being at the psycho-emotional level the anxiety and depression disorders that require the most attention. Various studies have proposed intervention options to manage the anxiety of women with diagnosis of breast cancer, why it was proposed through this scope review, to achieve a comprehensive view of these types of approaches, their characteristics, and resources necessary for their implementation.
