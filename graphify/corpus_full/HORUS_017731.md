# Natural and signal allelic diversity responses associated with the tolerance to the water deficit in Solanum tuberosum Phureja Group.

The genetic improvement of the diploid potato (Solanum tuberosum Group Phureja) for tolerance to the water deficit and yield of tubers is a challenge due to the complex nature of these traits. In order to develop an investigation that considers the complexity of the trait, it is associated with the fact that, at the same time, we make the experiments more treatable, we carried out two studies. The first was carried out to investigate the genetic architecture of water deficit tolerance using the Working Collection of the Papa Improvement Program of the National University of Colombia. In the second study, we investigated the physiological, biochemical and performance-related variables of four diploid potato genotypes with water deficit tolerance that contrast with water deficit, with emphasis on temporal trend responses. The comparative analysis successfully identified that physiological changes were more rapid and pronounced in water deficit sensitive genotypes.
