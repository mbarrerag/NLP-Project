# OPEN AND SIMULTANEOUS PRIMARY ELECTIONS: LATIN AMERICA EXPERIENCES / OPEN AND SIMULTANIAN PRIMARY ELECTIONS. EXPERIENCES IN LATIN AMERICA

An initial approach to the study of open and simultaneous presidential primary elections (PAS) conducted in various Latin American countries over the past 25 years is presented. Brief articles are included that offer different approaches to presidential primary elections in several Latin American countries, with an analysis of their particularities, adaptations, exceptions and regulations, within the different contexts in which this mechanism has been incorporated and implemented throughout multiple electoral processes
