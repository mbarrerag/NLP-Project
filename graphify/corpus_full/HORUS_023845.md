# G

The body is the organic and universal structure of the human species. Beyond its biological, evolutionaryly constructed substrate, this is culturally sculpted. Its concreteness in meanings, aesthetics and techniques is manifested in different forms depending on society, the historical moment or the place of the planet. This research seeks to understand how this process of cultural concretion occurs among the Ikğ (Arhuaco) indigenous people of Yo’sagaka, a large mountain located in the southeastern slope of the Sierra Nevada of Santa Marta (Colombia). In this sense, the proposed anthropological analysis concentrates on the ethnographic understanding of the Gch, the concept ik that defines the human organism, considering the geographical, historical, cognitive and daily conditions that have enabled it, reproduced and transformed in time. .
