# Xiuaguasinza, water-ground prayers for Fúquene

The project includes the realization of poetic and ritual actions that, on the one hand, allow the recognition of the lagoon of Fúquene as a sacred entity in danger of extinction, and on the other, allow the fabric of an affective bond, committed to it. Among the actions to be carried out are conversations with fishermen, artisans, campesinxs, CAR workers, researcherxs experts of the territory and, in general, dialogues with inhabitants of the surroundings of the lagoon. Likewise, a workshop will be held one day with a weaver of reed and enea. On the other hand, tours will be held in boat and on foot, within and on the periphery of the same, where its geographical and biodiversity characteristics can be recognized, collecting physical records, sonorous, photographic, videographic and textual, and where it can be carried out offerings, performances, concerts or other rituals of communication with this body of sacred water. The actions, sound pieces, videos, images and texts that emerge from these journeys, as instances of an exercise of thought-creation and collective events will be the protagonists
