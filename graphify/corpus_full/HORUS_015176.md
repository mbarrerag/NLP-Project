# Exploration of multidimensional needs in dementia: an approach from recognition to social justice in Colombia.

To explore and identify the multidimensional needs of people with dementia, their caregivers and the network of health professionals. Design of mixed methods of sequential exploratory type. Colombian population, of various territories, insurance regime, literate, with availability and access to the Internet and a connection device. Two phases were carried out, a quantitative, consisting of people with dementia (N:28), and caregivers (N:208), and a qualitative one with 88 people (N:49 caregivers and N:39 professionals). The quantitative information was analyzed with R software® and also employed the technique of analytical hierarchicalization; the qualitative information, was analyzed with the software Maxqda® and Coheris Analytics SPAD®. From the six needs explored, the needs related to the health, the needs of the participants are emerged (training, care and access to health, mobility, and environmental risks, legal-occupational-laboral, patrimonial, psychological and behavioral, socio-cultural, respite and values)
