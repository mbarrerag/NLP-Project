# A methodological approach for the evaluation of options for deferment of photovoltaic solar power generation projects

This doctoral thesis proposes a methodological approach to assess the real option of deferment in projects of generation of solar photovoltaic energy in Colombia, considering the high uncertainty associated with the national electrical environment. Unlike the traditional approach based on the Present Value Neto, the flexibility of deciding the optimal moment of investment is explicitly incorporated by stochastic simulation and advanced valuation techniques. The methodological development is structured in four stages. First, the most relevant variables for the profitability of the project are identified from a analysis of deterministic sensitivity. Then, a hierarchical simulation system is constructed that represents the long term dynamics of key variables such as the price of electricity, irradiance, water contributions and demand. Third, a valuation methodology based on the Least Squares Monte Carlo method (LSMC) adapted to the conditions of the Colombian context. Finally, this methodology is applied to a real case of a solar park of 100 MW in La Guajira.
