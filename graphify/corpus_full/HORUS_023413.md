# ADDITIVE MANUFACTURE APPLICABLE IN A PARALEL ARCHITECTURE MACHINE

The work presents an evaluation model that allows to identify the strategies of Competitiveness and Productivity of the production sector of handicrafts of Cúcuta and its metropolitan area. The research is descriptive in type in which a working instrument was used directly applied to the actors involved in the marketing of handicrafts, analyzing their managerial, competitive, technical and productive capacity, and of available human resource, and also valuable information was collected on the research and development activities that are being carried out in this sector. Based on the results obtained, strategies are designed that will allow it to analyze and plan the activities that they must carry out internally in the organizations of the sector and improve the decision-making and implementation of measures necessary to project their articles in the region competitively
