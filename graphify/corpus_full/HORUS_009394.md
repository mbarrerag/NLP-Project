# Blood of your blood

The "Blood from your blood: Grandmother Spider" research exploration explorations connections with female universities through material experimentation. The process involved three stages, starting with two-dimensional experimentation and culminating in an installation proposal. In the first stage, I propose the figure of the spider and the textile craft of western as a metaphor to apply family networks in a female key, connecting with an incomplete genealogy that arises from interview women from my mother's family. The second stage is an alternative to the bodies of these women—and to my own Body—through blood as a connecting matter: the practices that affect the entire landscape.
