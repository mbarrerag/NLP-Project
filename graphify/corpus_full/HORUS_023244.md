# NATURE CONTRIBUTIONS FOR PEOPLE

In this chapter, different contributions of nature are identified and valued for Colombians from various points of view, including economic, ecological, social and cultural. The first part of the chapter evaluates each of the specific categories of contributions of nature proposed in the report of the fifth IPBES plenary. The second part presents an evaluation of the studies of valuation of Colombian biodiversity using an integral and plural scheme, highlighting its importance as an ideal alternative for decision-making in the Colombian territory.
