# The Engaged Engineering as a Transformative Factor of Engineering Education for the Transition to Good Living in Latin America

Engineering, in all its specialties, involves making influential decisions in the way in which the world is conceived. Throughout its history, it has had a strong relationship with war and business interests, sharpened because the education in engineering favors the development of skills and the solution of technical problems, lacking critical reflection on its actions and the way it transforms the world. However, the existence of aspects, currently marginal, that are striving in committed interventions, aimed at social change, encouraging spaces of active education, transcend the paradigm of conventional engineering. These sides have multiple approaches and methodologies, articulated with the umbrella of Comprometida Engineerings. Society has not been alien to the construction of alternatives to face the civilizing crisis, as the plural concept: Good Living. This research focuses on critical reflection, from engineering, with the intention of transforming its practice, through the understanding of the Comprometida Engineerings and its relationship with the Good Living.
