# From regional to universal: the story of José Félix Fuenmayor and the Colombian Caribbean

The following work makes an aesthetic evaluation of the story of José Félix Fuenmayor in the panorama of Colombian letters for the middle of the 20th century. It is possible to show as a starting point the elements used by the barranquillero author of the modern form of the genre in Spanish, without falling with the above in the repetition of a formula, but in the exploration of the possibilities of expression that the story allowed. It highlights the preference of José Félix to build characters with a particular way of understanding the world, with a vision that does not perceive reason as the only tool to interpret reality, the Caribbean axiology as an identity trait. At the end of his life, there appears a series of posthumous texts that depart from the classical model worked in his stage of the 1950s, these texts are concerned to continue exploring the possibilities of the story as a genero renewer of the country literature.
