# INTERCULTURE ELEMENTS FOR PRIMARY ATTENTION IN BUCAL HEALTH IN THE Pijao POPULATION OF ORTEGA

PRECAEDENTS: Primary Health Care is a health care strategy where the way of life of a community is fundamental to guarantee the right to health and improve health conditions. Among the indigenous communities, specific aspects of their culture should be considered in activities of promotion, prevention, care and rehabilitation, activities to be implemented in institutional and community areas. Specifically, in the Pijao de Ortega population, Tolima, there are evidence of barriers of social and cultural access to dental care, generating lags in the oral health of this indigenous population. Identifying intercultural elements for the construction of a Primary Care Program in Oral Health for the Pijao population of Ortega, Tolima. A qualitative study with an ethnographic approach. The research instruments were used as a semi-structured interview to the Pijao population in the Vergel-Calarma community, Ortega municipality, Tolima, and health personnel of the indigenous IPS, The Wala. The participant observation was also carried out in these two scenarios (community and institutional), and the daily life of the field of the analysis to write the reflections. The following are the main topics of the programme: pain, bleeding and the prevention of caries, and the use of medicinal plants in the face of various conditions. There is also a strategy for organized care with the community and for the whole community without discriminating the type of affiliation to the health system and including mouth health promoters in the family and community, and a relevant allocation of dental appointments for this population. The type of food, geographical barriers and socio-economic status influence the health status of the Pijao population. The intercultural programs of PHC are necessary to achieve effective access to health by the Pijao indigenous people
