# Bogotá as a built heritage, 1960s: the permanence of residential neighborhoods of state management

La Fragua, La Esmeralda and San Felipe are three residential neighborhoods of housing series built by state management during the decade of 1960, whose comparative analysis, quantitative and qualitative, between conditions of origin and current conditions, allows to show the transformations of those that have been objected and the urban and architectural permanences of each neighborhood, starting from the elements that make them up. In the first category, of urban permanences, there are urban layout, public space, green areas and equipments, occupied space, heights and uses. In the second category, of architectural permanences, the predial structure, occupation of the properties and volumetry of the houses are linked. The comparative analysis between the three neighborhoods, in each of the revised themes, is fundamental to validate differences and similarities in the transformation processes, since they are located and constructed in the extensions of the central city, from 1960, to the south, to the west and to the north.
