# Application of the accumulation flow technique; making use of geographic information systems; in slopes adjacent to the Bogotá – Villavicencio route that have failed and also those already recovered, in order to determine new points where new landslides can occur

The purpose of this study is to identify unstable sites in slopes adjacent to the Bogotá-Villavicencio route, by identifying sites where there may be a risk of material slippage and by analyzing through the accumulation flow technique, using Geographic Information Systems and digital information processing; with the aim of conducting additional studies in these areas and implementing mitigation works that do not allow the route to be affected, reducing costs caused by damage to existing infrastructure and the cost generated by road interventions. .
