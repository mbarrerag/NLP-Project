# THE REPRESENTATION OF THE SPACE FOR WAR BETWEEN THE XVI AND XVIII SECRETARY MARKS: USE AND DISCUSSION OF THE PERSPECTIVE OVER FORTHCOMING TREATIES

This article explains how the treaties of military fortification or architecture, printed between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries, contributed to the use and diffusion of the military or parallel perspective, favouring it above the conical perspective that was then very well valued between artists and architects. The reason for this can be explained both in the geometric ease of its construction through parallel lines, and in the fact that through its use the dimensions of the spaces did not experience variations, constituting an advantage from the military and strategic point of view
