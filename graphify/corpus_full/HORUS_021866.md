# Structural optimization of interfloor systems made up of prefabricated elements, for use in housing of social interest VIS.

This research contributes to the solution of a particular challenge in the Colombian construction sector, that of optimizing the construction of concrete mezzanines for VIS applications. As it is incorporated within the framework of the research project “Automating Concrete Construction for Social Housing in Colombia”, this research addresses the latest innovations on automated construction in particular and proposes a technical, economic, architectural and socially viable solution for the construction of systems of prefabricated mezzanines in VIS in Colombia. Initially, the problems present in the use of mezzanines prefabricated in VIS are addressed, according to the architectural, social, economic, administrative and constructive conditions given in our country. Subsequently, some viable solutions are proposed that consider the problems addressed, and then an analysis of alternatives is carried out that allow to choose the most viable one within the Colombian environment. After having chosen the optimal section, it was designed in detail for its subsequent production, considering the two stages of production.
