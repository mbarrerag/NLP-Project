# Analysis of the certification process in education of the municipalities of the department of Cundinamarca and its impact on the implementation of the principle of decentralization: comparative study of two territorial entities

The aim of this project is to investigate the effects of the certification system of the municipalities in education. For this reason, it is sought to establish whether the current regulation and functioning of the said mechanism contribute to strengthening administrative decentralization, or if, on the contrary, they inhibit the concreteness of the purpose of the Constituent of 1991 in this matter and thus generate greater inequality between the municipalities of the department of Cundinamarca, for which two comparisons will be carried out: 1) A macro, aimed at comparing the results of the 15 provinces of the department of Cundinamarca in educational matters, using as comparison patterns the quality, the average net coverage [not including higher education] (summation of all the coverage rates of each of the municipalities that make up the province/number of municipalities that make up it) and the average results in mathematics and critical reading in the Tests Know 11 (freeness of the average score in each area of each area of the municipalities that make up the province/number of municipalities that make up it). Likewise, it is sought to identify which municipalities are certified in each province and which do not, as well as the distance between the latter and the capital, as between the closest and the municipalities and the most of the municipalities that are independent The project will have a duration of 12 months, during which meetings will be held for periodic review of progress at least every 15 days, in which specific tasks will be assigned to each member (and therefore in section _x0093_work plan_x0094_ they are not a priori) and the implementation of those assigned at the next meeting will be reviewed. Similarly, collective work will be carried out at those meetings on the organization and processing of bibliographic, case law and quantitative information. As the Research Group to implement the project is DTGI, the main areas to be covered are Diversity, the Territory, the People and the Institutions, seeking to gather pluralistic and heterodox perspectives in order to enrich debate and empower the inhabitants of both the Andean Region and the poor national territories, and thus to promote the management of communities of their own interests and to strengthen participation in the allocation and control of public expenditure
