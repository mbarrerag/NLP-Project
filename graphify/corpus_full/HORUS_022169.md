# Diagnostic reference levels in computed tomography in a highly complex pediatric hospital in the city of Bogotá.

The objective of this study was to determine the most common local diagnostic reference levels (NRDL) and achievable doses (DA) for paediatric patients during the computed tomography (CT) procedures in a third-level pediatric hospital in the city of Bogotá, Colombia. A retrospective descriptive cross-sectional observational study that included studies in a period of time between January 2019 and April 2022, of a phase, of skull, simple thorax, contrasted thorax and abdomen-pelvis contrasted. The diagnostic reference level (NRD) was obtained based on the volume CTDI dose index (vol) and the product of dose length (DLP) for five age groups in paediatric population (up to 18 years). Patients' data (577) as regards CTDI vol and DLP were reported from five age groups, so that the results could be compared with existing literature publications.
