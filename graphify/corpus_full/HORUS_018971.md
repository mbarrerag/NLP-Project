# Prognostic factors of in-hospital mortality in elderly patients with COVID infection 19 admitted to a third-level hospital in Bogotá, Colombia.

The present study aims to establish the prognostic factors associated with the development of intrahospital death in adult patients over 65 years of age with SARS- CoV2/COVID-19 infection confirmed in the National University Hospital of Colombia. This is an analytical observational study. Adult patients over 65 years of age were evaluated with a diagnosis of SARS-CoV2/COVID-19 infection confirmed. Patients were stratified by state of fragility and functionality. Mortality was determined at 28 days and an unconditional logistic regression model was created, adjusted by confusion factors. Statistical significance was established at p ≤ 0.05. 170 patients were stratified in survivors and non-survivors. The percentage of men in survivors and non-survivors was similar, without significant differences in the average age between both groups, nor in relation to the presence of symptoms. In the multivariate analysis, the following variables predicted the occurrence of the outcome mortality at 28 days: fragility, history of cancer, ARDS, acute renal injury and AFSS.
