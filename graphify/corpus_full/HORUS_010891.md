# INEQUITY IN HEALTH OF PROFESSIONAL DEPORTERS IN COLOMBIA: CASES OF CYCLISM, BOXEO AND TENIS

This article analyzes the inequalities in the health of athletes in Co-lombia by comparing three sports. Describe the main inequalities in health of professional athletes in cycling, boxing and tennis in Colombia based on the analysis of life stories. Ma-terials and methods. The qualitative study used the ethnosociological approach to analyze the life paths of 9 athletes. The analysis of in-formation was carried out using the approach of the social determinants of health. Regardless of the sport analyzed, inequalities related to the socio-economic and political context (sports policies, culture and social values) and power relations (social class, gender and race/ethnicity) are evident. As well as in the intermediate determinants related to material resources (economic and emotional support, relationship with the trainer) and working conditions (work contract, working day and autonomy). The study describes how sport produces and reproduces social inequalities and invisibility of the daily practices that affect the development of the public, the professional practices that have been promoted
