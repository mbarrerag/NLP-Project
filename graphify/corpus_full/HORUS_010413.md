# MUSIC: REFLECTIONS ON THEIR SOCIOECONOMIC TRANSFORMATION POWER

Music is one of the most important cultural expressions of human beings, as an intrinsic manifestation of their creativity, emotion, sensitivity and beauty. Furthermore, music is not only a form of human manifestation, but it allows the construction of identity insofar as individuals are recognized as part of a specific context and community; in this way, music can have implications for social and cultural configuration, as well as economic effects in the territories. The objective of this article of reflection is to raise music as a human need based on four dimensions that shape it and contribute to the socio-economic transformation of the territories, these are: Cognitive Development, Social and Civic Value, Aesthetic of Being and Self-Recognition. As a complement to these reflections, some data from the Cultural Consumption Survey in Colombia, which demonstrate the behavior of the population vis-à-vis musical activities, are valuable instruments of intervention that contribute to improving the well-being of communities
