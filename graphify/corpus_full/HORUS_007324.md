# The museum through the senses. Participatory museum in the Archaeological and Historical Park of Santa María la Antigua del Darién

This document reflects, through the integration of disciplines such as industrial design, museum and museum, on the importance of participatory museography in heritage management. The aim of the paper is to explore how the participatory museographic approach can transform the visitor's experience and the development of exhibitions, taking as a case of interest the Archaeological and Historical Park of Santa Maria la Antigua del Darien. This document recognizes participation as a key approach to integrating communities into contemporary museum development, as it not only enriches the museum narrative and the development of exhibitions, but also strengthens the relationship between museums and their publics .
