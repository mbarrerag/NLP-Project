# USES A LOGIT MODEL TO PREDECIDE THE PLURALITY OF OFFERENTS IN PUBLIC LICITATIONS

Abstract State procurement is a relevant topic for the participants of society, since through this mechanism resources are invested by the State, seeking to achieve the objectives of the development plan, which in turn are synonymous with collective progress. In this sense, a brief theoretical explanation of the enabling requirements of financial capacity required in the specifications is provided, with the aim of being clear about the expected behavior in the proposed statistical model. Below, the description of the methodology used in the research and the theoretical approach that supports the development of the proposed regression models is presented, the validation tests carried out are presented and the model is shown by which the probability of a plurality of suppliers is predicted according to the enabling requirements of financial capacity
