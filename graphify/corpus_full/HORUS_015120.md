# CATHOLIC CHURCH AND ITS REALITIES IN TIMES OF HORSE AND GONGORA ANTONY, ARZOBISPO-VIRREY OF THE NEW GRANADA (1778-1788)

In 1778 Antonio Caballero and Góngora, who belonged to the Council of the King, was appointed Archbishop of Santafé of Bogotá, to begin his duties in March of the following year. He came from acting as bishop in Mérida, Yucatán. In July 1782 King Charles III appointed him viceroy of the Viceroy of the New Granada, captain general and president of the Royal Audiencia. Thus, in one person all power was accumulated in the viceroyalty both in political and in the ecclesiastical, military and administrative. In September 1788 he was appointed archbishop of Cordoba, in Spain, which forces him to return to the old continent. Caballero and Góngora is a clear example of the regalist policy undertaken by the Borbons.
