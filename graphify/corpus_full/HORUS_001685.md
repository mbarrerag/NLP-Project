# Urban integration: new public spaces, San Miguel neighborhoods, Modelo Norte and Simón Bolívar Park, Bogotá

This master's thesis addresses the issue of urban disintegration manifested in the relationship of the districts San Miguel and Modelo Norte with the Metropolitan Park Simon Bolivar (Sector Novios II) in Bogotá. The research critically analyzes the planning and urban design of these spaces, highlighting how the lack of an integral vision and the consolidation of residual areas have created physical and social barriers that hinder integration between neighbourhoods and equitable access to the park. The thesis proposes an urban intervention focused on the creation and recovery of public spaces, as well as the generation of vital spaces that promote urban integration at the barrial level. The project aims to break down existing barriers, revitalize the urban fabric and promote community ownership of the public space, thus contributing to the construction of a better city. The proposed intervention is based on an analysis of successful projects at both national and international levels, identifying strategies and best practices that applied to the specific context of the districts studied. The the thesis concludes by emphasizing the importance of community participation and consideration of the historical and cultural memory of the neighborhoods in the design and implementation of projects of urban integration . contributing to the construction of a better city.
