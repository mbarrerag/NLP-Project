# Barriers and drivers of sustainable construction in Colombia. An approach in Life Cycle Analysis (LCA) and from Organization Strategies (OE)

This research explored the phenomenon of sustainable construction in Colombia from a qualitative research design, with a focus on the concepts Life Cycle Analysis (ACV) and Organizational Strategies (EO). With the aim of recognizing how sustainable construction can be promoted in Colombia, through the identification of barriers, drivers, tools, strategies and trends of sustainable construction, based on the theory and experience of the stakeholders in the sector. Its methodological development was divided into three phases, the first one was carried out through a Systematic Review of Literature (RSL), the second phase was developed from a survey of perception of the issues associated with sustainable construction to the interested parties of the value chain in Colombia and the third one is a final report that integrates the results of the first two phases.
