# In vitro determination of the biocompatibility of a marker peptide coupled to USPIO nanoparticles in an induced neuroinflammation model

Neurological disorders are degenerative diseases of the central nervous system (CNS) and constitute a frequent cause of morbidity and mortality in the world, and have increased in recent years. The nanoparticles USPIO (Ultrasmall Superparamagnetic Iron Oxide) are a novel category of contrast agents in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) that can be coupled to various molecules and specifically target molecular markers that reflect specific alterations. Thus, the aim of this study was to characterize the effect of the nanoparticles of USPIO conjugated with the P88 marker peptide in an in vitro-induced neuroinflation model with potential applicability in MMI. To achieve this objective, the cellular lines HCMEC/D3 (endotelian cells of microvasculature of the brain) and T98G (astrocyteal) were incubated. USPIO conjugated with the marker peptide and its effect on cellular viability was determined by the LDH method.
