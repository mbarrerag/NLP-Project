# Adolescent participation in the territorial planning of public spaces in Sumapaz (2011-2021)

The adolescent participation in the territorial planning of public spaces in the town of Sumapaz has been promoted with the public policies of the urban government of Bogotá. especially with the public policy on childhood and adolescents of bogotá (2011-2021). However, these public spaces, still, do not reflect the contributions of the adolescent population. To understand this situation and contribute to building a city with diverse and inclusive public spaces for citizens in general and adolescents in particular, it is key to analyse the adolescent participation in the territorial planning of public spaces deployed in the town of Sumapaz considering the cycle of the Public Policy on Children and Adolescents of Bogotá (2011-2021). Therefore, this work addresses five points: (i) approximation of the concept of adolescent participation in the territorial planning of public spaces; (ii) identification of the proposal for adolescent participation formulated in the Public Policy on Children and Adolescents of Bogotá (2011-2021); (iii) qualitative and quantitative description of the adolescent participation implemented in the town of Sumapaz of 2011 to 2021; (iv) contrast of the adolescent participation formulated with the implemented in that town; (v) the phenomenon of the territorial policy of the public policy and adolescents and adolescents; (v) the territorial policy issue; (v s: (i) approach to the concept of adolescent participation in the territorial planning of public spaces; (ii) identification of the proposed adolescent participation formulated in the Public Policy on Children and Adolescents of Bogota (2011-2021); (iii) qualitative and quantitative description of adolescent participation implemented in Sumapaz from 2011 to 2021; (iv) contrast of the adolescent participation formulated with that implemented in Sumapaz; (v) conclusions and recommendations on the phenomenon in question
