# Formalization of good game design practices following a model for unified definition of practice.

The design of games is the process by which a designer creates a context that discovers a player and from which a significant game emerges. The good practices are actions of standardization of processes, repetitive and observable with acceptance in different contexts of the industry and the academic community. The approaches of various authors to the denomination of practice in the area of game design are not explicitly realized; on the contrary, the approaches to definitions of good practices are inaccurate and lack standardization. This is partly due to the lack of an adequate definition of practice in the design of games, besides the diversity of methods, models and frameworks that are found in the literature and in which different ways of carrying out the process of design of games are proposed. In this Master's Thesis, a set of good practices of game design, characterized from different models, methods and frameworks, defined following a model for the unified definition of practice and represented under the Quintessence kernel is proposed to meet the mentioned problems, this set of good practices allows to integrate the vision of different proposals into each area of practice
