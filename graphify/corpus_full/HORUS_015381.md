# RESEARCH OF ALTERNATIVE TRAINING IN THE FIELD OF EDUCATION IN LANGUAGES: IN FORCE OF SOCIAL JUSTICE HORIZONTS.

The learning of foreign languages has a trajectory that has been based on the teaching models that have occurred in mainly English-speaking contexts of the great powers in the world that have been strengthened subsidized by the new trends to the internationalization of curricula and the call to globalization. These models have been used to develop homogenizing practices at macro (educational policies) levels at meso (of popular social practices) and micro levels (in classroom practices of educational actors) that have been indifferent to the socio-cultural contexts, to the specific needs of them and to the diversity of the population. The semillean teaching and learning of foreign languages, culture and social justice has based its reason on being to establish a critical positioning to these ways of addressing knowledge and, seeks through alternative and inclusive proposals of research, that this phenomenon can be understood as a complex of power relations that oppress social justice and consequently, seeks to identify pedagogical and didactic possibilities with attention to diversity, to the multiculturality and the equity of our nation, and the needs of the current world, what the educational city considers the scientific impact and the educational world, what the educational country, what we consider the educational city and what we are doing in order to answer to the educational research, what is, what is the educational city
