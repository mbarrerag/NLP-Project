# Potential economic and social impacts of installing a floating plant to recover Moscow mica in the Department of Valle Fertil, San Juan, Argentina.

This research work aims to analyze the economic and social impact of the future installation of a floating plant to recover mica muscovita from pegmatíticas scrappers in Valle Fertil, San Juan, Argentina. The economic impact was analyzed through the methods Present Value Economic Net, Internal Rate of Return, and Period of Recovery. The results allow to recognize the viability in economic terms of this project since the profit that would leave the investor is 3’970.648 USD, the investment is recovered in 4 years and 11 months, and the result of the TIRE is 34.8%, value that is higher than the discount rate of the opportunity rejected. With regard to the social impact, a baseline was established by the search for documentary information of the social and economic conditions of the communities of the Department of Valle Fertil. The exploitation of the social impact has been established by the analysis of the most structural impact on institutions and community of the influence area.
