# Illustrated Ancestral Receitary.

This recipe is the result of a joint work between the National University of Colombia and the Arhuaca community of the Jimaín reserve, in the framework of the solidarity extension project, related to agro-acuculture production. In order to ensure the continuity of the initiatives that were created there and that the community could integrate in a harmonious way the consumption of fish in its diet, we made a preliminary evaluation of the real possibilities of appropriation of these practices, both in their technical dimension, linked to the breeding and raising of fish, and in the incorporation of this protein in their diets. Therefore, we decided to invest in a deeper knowledge of the Arhuaca food practices through interviews and workshops, where we identified recent and ancient food and preparations that are important for the community. Although we are working with a focus on the appropriation and dissemination of fish knowledge, the recipe book is not limited to recipes with fish, but rather it extends to other daily and significant foods, highlighting those preparations that make part of local gastronomy and regional and ethnic identity, seeking to strengthen the own agricultural traditions
