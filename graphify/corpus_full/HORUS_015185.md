# Study, limitations and critical analysis of precautionary measures within the executive process before the Administrative Litigation Jurisdiction.

When a person is sued for damages, the application may be filed for precautionary measures that seek to ensure the enforcement of the decision in advance. When it comes to an executive process advanced within the jurisdiction of the administrative dispute, a disadvantage is presented for the plaintiff because the state property enjoys special constitutional protection, which is why its assets cannot be seized under the principle of fiscal and budgetary sustainability. This has resulted in a multiplicity of precautionary measures decreed and judgements that cannot be executed because the state property is unembargable. The precautionary measures in this type of proceedings lose their full effectiveness and the judgements become illusory, since the law does not establish mechanisms to ensure their compliance, a situation that causes a failure of the administration to deal with its administrators
