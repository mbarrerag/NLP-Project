# THE PEACE CITIZENSHIP PROJECT: A HABITAT BUILDING ALTERNATIVE FROM THE COOPERATIVE MODEL IN THE RECILIATION PROCESS OF ARCHITECTONIC AND TECHNOLOGICAL DIVERSITY IN THE CHURCH OF THE INDISM PEOPLES OF THE UNDIBOUS HIGHLIGHT AND THE COLOMBIAN BUILDINGS CONSTRUCTED BETWEEN 1579-1616

The article focuses on the possibilities offered by the cooperative construction of habitat as a model for the reintegration of peace signatories into the post-conflict Colombia, taking as a case study the Old Territorial Space of Training and Reconciliation- AETCR of TierraGrata in San José de Oriente- Cesar. Through a academic work that has involved different research groups around the production of habitat through participatory design, the challenges, possibilities,instruments and advantages of building common spaces, complementary to housing, for the Citadel of Peace in the Vereda Tierra Grata in the Cesar, where the community finds re-incorporation scenarios through the cooperative exercise of architectural production, giving response to the need to establish itself in the civil life, after the signing of the peace agreement in 2016. Through the proposal of technical support from the academy to the cooperative dynamics of the community, the emphasis was placed on their knowledge and their associative capacity as a starting point for the design of the House of Materials, as part of the productive units of the ETCR, seeking to provide elements that nullify the decisions and their association as a starting point of departure from the design of the institutional design of the material, as part of the model of the country, and the sector
