# Analysis of the production chain of the achiote (Bixa orellana L.) in the municipality of Medio Baudó in the department of Chocó under the focus of value chain

The production chains are made up of actions developed by different actors that interrelate to produce a common good, from the production of raw material to the marketing of the final product to the consumer, which produce food that contributes to reducing hunger and poverty. There are companies and producers of achiote in rural areas that carry out productive activities, but their situation of poverty and ability to generate greater income does not improve with the passage of years; probably, it is due to the fact that there is no comprehensive analysis of the chain oriented to improve competitiveness. The Chocó because of its environmental conditions has facilitated the adaptation, growth and development of the achiote; which has traditionally been used in the culinary and ancient magical-religious knowledge, being prioritized in departmental development plans. Nowadays, the natural products are the trend of the market and the achiote because of its ability to pigment is a colorant with great demand that counts with the characteristics of not being toxic or carcinogenic, used in the industries of food, cosmetics, textile and pharmaceutical. The reason for which, it is necessary to investigate on the analysis of the productive chain of the achiote, will be a tool to improve the great value chain and the activities in the activity
