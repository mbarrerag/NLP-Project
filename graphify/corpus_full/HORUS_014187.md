# Micelio 2.0: The Fintech in the construction of impact capabilities for research at the National University

The project proposes a comprehensive research on the implementation of financial technologies (Fintech) to improve the funding of research at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Bogotá and Medellín. The main objective is to analyze the feasibility and potential impact of Fintech's adoption in the academic field, in order to strengthen the collaboration between the university and the private sector, diversify the sources of financing and promote innovation in research. The research is divided into six stages. In the first stage, a comprehensive review of the literature will be carried out to familiarize itself with the effective systems of capital capture, the financing of university research and the use of financial technologies. The second stage will consist of an institutional diagnosis to understand the structure, dynamics and financing of research in both headquarters of the National University. The third stage will focus on evaluating the technical and legal feasibility of implementing Fintech systems in the university. Subsequently, the design of a detailed implementation proposal, which will include specific objectives, implementation strategies and necessary resources. The fifth stage will involve the validation of the proposal through consultations with necessary contents and adjustments
