# Working conditions in a mining company, Segovia, Antioquia (Colombia 2022)

According to the International Labour Organization (ILO), 2015, “Despite the efforts made in many countries, the rate of deaths, injuries and diseases among the workers of the mines of the world confirms that, in most countries, mining remains the most dangerous occupation if we take into account the number of people exposed to risk.” An economic activity where male labour has historically been employed, after 2015, Colombian legislation enabled women to work in operational positions in the mining of socavón, after almost 30 years of restriction. . Agreeing to the International Labour Organization (ILO) in 2015, “Despite effforts made in many countries, the rate of deaths, injuries, and illnesses among mine workers worldwide confirmations that mining remains the most dangerous occupation in terms of the number of people exposed to risks, injuries, and illnesses among mines worldwide constructions or employment dominated by the most dangerous diseases in terms of the number of people exposed to risk in countries.”
