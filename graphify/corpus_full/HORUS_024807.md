# THE RUBRISHES IN THE AUTHORREGULATION OF THE LEARNING IN THE PRACTICE COMPONENT OF INSURANCE STUDENTS

AbstractThe pedagogy has now evolved, to such an extent that the teacher's figure as the centre of the learning process has been transferred by a greater role of the student. For this reason, this article aims to raise the importance of the implementation of rubrics in the development of nursing practices as a way of strengthening self-regulating learning in students. To achieve this, a theoretical approach is made to the concepts of self-regulating learning and the importance of the rubrics is determined for the monitoring of training actions in the educational context. Finally, a proposal is established to implement the rubrics during the component of the internships in students.Palabras Clave: Heading, Self-regulated Learning, Pedagogy, Nursing, Autonomous Learning
