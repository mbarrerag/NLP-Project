# INTEGRATED OVERSET MESH AND VOLUME OF FLUID METHOD FOR MODELING FREE-SURFACE DYNAMICS IN VERTICAL AXIS HYDROKINETIC TURBINES

This article presents a comprehensive Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) study that centers on assessing the impact of freesurface effects on the efficiency of a vertical axis hydrokinetic turbine (VAHT). The research is driven by the goal of creating an advanced model utilizing the innovative overset mesh method, enhancing accuracy in estimating the power coefficient of the rotor. The integration of the overset mesh method with the Volume of Fluid (VOF) multiphase approach ensures a precise representation of free-surface effects on the turbine's performance. This powerful combination not only facilitates the identification of a suitable immersion level, safeguarding the turbine's performance from any surface interface impact but also allows for a meticulous evaluation of performance variations under diverse operating conditions. This is particularly valuable in scenarios where achieving the required operational depths may pose challenges
