# Survey of dermatologists in Colombia: diagnosis and treatment of preference in patients with atopic dermatitis.

Atopic dermatitis is an immunomediated disease that is increasingly studied. The aim of this study is to identify the diagnostic criteria, clinimetry scales, preferred treatments. In addition, the causes of choice and change of therapy and the waiting time to see the therapeutic response. It is an observational study, descriptive of cross-sectional. It was made by means of a virtual survey distributed during academic activities, after conducting pilot tests. 68 dermatologists were surveyed, 29.3% based the diagnosis according to the clinic, followed by the criteria of Hanifin and Rajka (23.08 per cent). The most used clinimetric scales are DLQI, EASI, and clinical evaluation (29.85 per cent). The first therapeutic line for mild disease are corticosteroids of medium potency (52.31%). In the second line they prefer to change the potency of the corticosteroid (38.46%). The main causes of initiating systemic therapy are: 1. the severe clinic and 2. severe alteration of the quality of life.
