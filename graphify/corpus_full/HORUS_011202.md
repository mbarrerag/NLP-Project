# INVESTMENT GAS EMISSIONS IN THE AUTONOMOUS CITY OF GOOD AIRES: A ANALYSIS OF SCOPES IN FIXED INSTALLATIONS WHOSE AUTHORITIES ARE

This article is based on a statistical analysis focused on fixed installations (trades, institutions and residences) of the Autonomous City of Buenos Aires (CABA) based on their greenhouse gas emissions between 2000 and 2019, and discriminates against them according to three "scopes" (scopes). The objective involves analyzing these emissions in relation to the demand for electrical energy and appliances; to achieve this goal, methodologically, it was chosen to articulate data provided from BA Data and the INDEC (National Institute of Statistics and Censuses). It is concluded that the absolute increase of household appliances and the production of electric energy are not associated with a higher volume of emissions, at least since 2016, being that one possible explanation is the efficiency of those devices when consuming energy
