# Inverted classroom and implementation of virtual tools in higher education

This work was developed during academic periods I and II - 2020 and I - 2021. It was the result of the subjects: teaching practice and internship, which are part of the Master's programme in higher education, offered by the National University of Colombia, Bogotá. This was aimed at seeking the closing of the gap that was presented at the time of the initiation of the Pandemic and that, as teachers, we were led to find solution and virtualize an academic exercise that was initially proposed, clearly present. For this reason, it was necessary to make use of much of the resources offered by the platforms and applications offered by the network at the world level, in some cases valuable information that I acquired in subjects offered by the master's degree, and in others, result of an autonomous research exercise, proper to the teacher. In this work, there is a theoretical presentation to the tools and platforms used, followed by a selection of works that account the implementation of those tools in some of the classroom exercises; proposals and tools that became effective at the time of practice. lications available on the worldwide web, in some cases valuable information obtained from the courses offered by the master’s program while in others was the result of an autonomous exercise of research, typical of teaching.
