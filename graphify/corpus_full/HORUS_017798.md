# Master's Degree in Microbiology: 30 years adding value to Microbiology in Colombia

The emergence of the Inter-Faculty Master's Degree in Microbiology at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia should be traced back to the very origins of the Institute of Biotechnology, where a group of visionaries headed by Dr. Dolly Montoya Castaño, current rector of the University, evidenced by 1987 that the country should generate a development pole in the field of Biotechnology. Thus, it could contribute to adding value to the knowledge that, in other parts of the University and the country, were growing around the study of our biodiversity. It is worth mentioning, moreover, that these studies were increasingly gaining relevance thanks to the findings and claims of the country as protagonist in the different rankings, where the number of species of birds, amphibians, reptiles, trees, orchids, and butterflies, among other wonderful organisms of our geography, were reported. However, despite the above mentioned advances, there was a group of organisms that, due to their small size, usually went unnoticed, unless they were due to their large negative effects usually around public health
