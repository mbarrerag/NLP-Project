# Bayesian Co-integrated Panels: An Application in Digital Marketing

This document develops the methodof the Bayesian integrated panels of Koop, Leon-Gonzalez and R. Strachan (2008) in the field of digital marketing, particularly in the modeling of a system where they model metrics of investment performance how clicks and impressions of a given digital campaign, the planned investment and the sessions obtained. As, we want to evaluate how the system reacts to disturbances in investment due to changes in the strategy of flight or periods and seasons of investment, and its impact on the visibility of the given campaigns. Thus, the model is used with a set of real marketing data with different investment campaigns on the same geographical territory. By means of the breakdown of the variance of the error of prediction, the results indicate that the clicks and impressions have a significant impact on the generation of sessions. The estimability of the proposed estimate algorithm is also evaluated through a comprehensive simulation study that considers different processes. The results indicate that the algorithm has significant capabilities in terms of estimability and accuracy
