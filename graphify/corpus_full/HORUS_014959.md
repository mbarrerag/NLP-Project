# PROCESS OF SUPPLY OF HOSPITAL FOOD FOR MOTHER IN MANAGEMENT

The chapter consists of the description of the process of supply of food and nutrition to the pregnant mother in the hospital institutions, through the activities that make up this process, taking as reference the cycle PHVA. The chapter groups the description of the activities of the supply of food in activities of planning, doing, verifying and acting. In the planning, the nutritional state of the pregnant mother is identified when entering the hospital institution, in order to understand and interpret its gestation process and to infer its energetic and nutritional needs according to its age, physical activity, physiological state and to be able to determine the diet necessary for its nutrition and nutrition. The doing consists in creating the diet ordered by the clinical staff in the service of supply of food in the areas of preparation and distribution, where the responsible must elaborate and dispose of the different components of the diet depending on the request. The responsible of the preparation will also rely on the standard menu of the institution understanding pattern menu as the list of components, weights and frequencies of food of a diet.
