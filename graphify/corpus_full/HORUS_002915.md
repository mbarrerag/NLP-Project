# Classroom project based on pollinating insects as a didactic strategy for teaching biodiversity

Biodiversity has become a paradigm to create awareness about its biological value and the consequences that would result from the loss of ecosystem resources. The research work was based on the application of a classroom project that aimed to address the concept of biodiversity through the observation of pollinative insects, with a qualitative methodology focused on Problem Based Learning, where through the learning sequence that includes phases with exploration activities, introduction of new concepts, structuring of knowledge and finally implementation activities, in order to promote a significant critical learning with the fifth grade primary students of two institutions, one private and the other public, located in the municipality of Retiro and Medellín respectively. The results obtained enabled a construction on the concept of biodiversity through the process of argumentation and evidence of learning of students, showing sensitivity, understanding and respect for the services and resources of the ecosystem. student learning, demonstrating sensitivity, understanding, and respect for the ecosystem services and resources provided by pollinating insects
