# Metabolic profile and bioprospecting of sulfur Tripogandra serrulata (Commelinaceae) and antibiotic Justice cf. colorifera (Acanthaceae), two plants of traditional use in Orinoquía

The World Health Organization (WHO) recognizes and accepts the fundamental role of plants with medicinal properties to meet the health needs of many populations in the world, since they represent an economic alternative and sometimes very effective to treat various diseases in populations, especially in rural and economically vulnerable areas. The region of the Colombian Orinoquia is characterized by its plant richness with these properties, although it could be considered that there are few or no studies and records on the biological and medicinal properties of many of these species, as well as on the identification of bioactive compounds present in them, although many of these species have wide use of the region's inhabitants. Given this, it is proposed that the study on some pharmacological properties and the chemical composition of the T. serrulata and J. cf. colorifera species, medicinal plants of the region, of which are not found records of research carried out in the Orinoquia, would provide accurate information for a more safe use of these medicinal plants by communities, which also allows a greater use of all the pharmacological properties that can have these same plant species, and which are not related to the organic plant. With the realization of this project, it is expected to offer a bibliographical reference of these plants of the Orinoquia region on their chemical composition and medicinal properties, to give added value to these species and to highlight the potential uses of these natural resources in a future elaboration of products of pharmaceutical interest
