# Genotyping and mutations associated with antimicrobial resistance of Mycoplasma gallisepticum and M. synoviae in commercial birds in Colombia

Mycoplasma gallisepticum (Mg) and M. synoviae (Ms) are pathogens of importance in poultry farming considering that their presentation is frequent and characterized by a chronic disease, which results negatively affecting the productive parameters of poultry farms, generating besides the impact on the health of birds an important economic impact. Although the gold test for diagnosis is the cultivation of the agent in vitro, by its characteristics and demands for growth it makes it an unefficient, costly and long-term method. In this sense molecular tests take great relevance as an alternative and complementary method that between itself as part of an integral strategy. However, although there is control of the disease by means of vaccination and administration of antibiotics, problems of reversion of some strains vaccine and the genes associated with Mg and Ms's DNA are identified in this country
