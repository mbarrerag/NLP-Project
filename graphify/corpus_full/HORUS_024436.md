# DISTRIBUTION OF RADIOACTIVE NATURAL Ocurrence ELEMENTS (NORMS) IN THE TRAINING OF THE MOON, VALLE MAGDALINEA ENVIRONMENT: CONSIDERATIONS OF THEIR DEPOSIT ENVIRONMENT

The Formation The Moon is not only considered as the unit generating petroleum of conventional extraction in the Middle Valley of the Magdalena, but also the unit of greater prospection for the production of non-conventional hydrocarbons of this basin. Unlike other rocks that correspond in the world, this unit is predominantly carbonatic of biogenic origin rich in hydrocarbons where detritic content becomes marginal and excel the diagenetic processes that have transformed the original material (silicification).The anomalies of radiological measurements in sedimentary rocks within the Middle Valley of the Magdalena (VMM) in the framework of the project MEGIA What is the high-valued contract? No. FP44842-157-2018, suggest that the intervals with potential to possess anomalies of radioactive elements are associated with the Member Galembo. Discontinuously arising along the western and eastern edges of the VMM, in the presence of the Serranía de San Lucas and the Cordillera Orientales respectively, and found at the width of the valley reaching some kilometers of depth. The Galembo Member consists of calcareous rocks (of high carbon content, non- and stergenous and dissimilar material) and dissimilar material.. On the other hand, thorium remains constant and in a smaller amount throughout the section (between 0.8 and 6.8 ppm). The study not only contributes to elucidating the geological history of the upper cretaceous in the VMM basin but also environmental considerations that involve access to the resource of the La Luna Formation with respect to radioactive material of natural occurrence
