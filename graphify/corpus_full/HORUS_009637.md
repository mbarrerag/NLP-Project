# CHAPTER 3. AXIS 1: AGROECOLOGY AS AN ANSWER TO THE NEED FOR HEALTHFUL ECOSYSTEMS

An assessment of agrodiversity was carried out in eight farms under three different production systems (agroecological, transition and conventional) located in the sidewalks of Arenillo and Mesón in the rural area of Palmira Valle, Colombia. Eleven components of the sociocultural, 2 economic, and 59 environmental dimension were evaluated. The first two components were based on data collected through semi-structured surveys and using the methodology of evaluating multiple functions of agroecosystems. For the environmental dimension, data from accompanying or arvenses plants were evaluated, physical chemical analysis of soil and the presence of bacteria determined by metabarcoding. It was found that agrodiversity is related to the production system and that this depends on the knowledge, traditions and technologies employed by peasants in their cultivars. The diversity of bacteria in the soils was high and is related to the presence of arvens and the chemical and physical characteristics of soils. Key words: Characterization; floristic diversity; metabarcoding; interactions; multiple factorial analysis
