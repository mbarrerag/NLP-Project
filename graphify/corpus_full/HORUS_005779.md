# THE MODEL OF BUSINESS OF CAMERA, SYNONIC AND LIRIC SONG MUSIC IN BOGOTA

The business model is a conceptual tool that shows the simplified structure of an organization, allowing them to guide decision-making and actions for the implementation of the strategy. At present, changes in the environment are causing changes in the business model of musical organizations working with classical music, diluting the barriers between those that are for profit and those that are not for profit, making their identification important for its constant restructuring. This article addresses the business model of six organizations that perform classical music in the city of Bogotá through semi-structured interviews with their respective leaders. The results allow characterizing the business model taking into account how the mission, vision and artistic mission or artistic approach affect the value proposal, operational value and value capture
