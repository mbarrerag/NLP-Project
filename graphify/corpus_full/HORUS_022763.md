# The role of technical training in the labour market performance of new managerial programmes

This is a reflective text that analyzes the role that undergraduate university education can play, fundamentally that offered in the faculties of administration, to form a generation of young people who have in entrepreneurship the most real option to have employment. Theoretical references: the reflection is based on analyses carried out by various authors that evaluate the relationship between university education and employment and the new trends in the labour market in the twenty-first century, especially from the sociology of work and observatories of higher education in Colombia. Discussions: Some of the questions that different sectors make to contemporary university training are addressed from the after-trainings that are taking place in the current world of work and the role that entrepreneurship has as a labour strategy for young professionals to ensure an economic income in the twenty-first century; the importance of non-administrative technical training in the training of future administrators is analyzed. The empirical experience of celebrated entrepreneurs indicates that the relationship between prior technical knowledge and entrepreneurial success is positive, which must lead to the faculties of administration reflecting the type of training they provide, usually focused on administrative management processes, as well as the processes of skills promoted by the skills and by the various graduates
