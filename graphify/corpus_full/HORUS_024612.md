# COMPARISON ANALYSIS OF THE REGULATION OF HUERPHANOUS MEDICINAL PRODUCTS AND VITE MEDICINAL PRODUCTS NOT AVAILABLE IN COLOMBIA

The auotres carry out a comparative analysis of the regulation of orphan drugs and vital medicines not available in Colomiba. It takes up and deepens the research carried out by the master Luz Andrea Olivares in his thesis entitled Vital medicines not available: analysis of national and international regulation and characterization of import applications, according to decree 481 of 2004, based at the National Institute of Surveillance of Medicines and Food (Invima), in 2016 and 2017
