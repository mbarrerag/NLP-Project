It has remained throughout the years in the global and national increasing scope, which worsened by the pandemic of the COVID-19. In Colombia, in 2020 12,582 cases of TB have been reported in all its forms and the city of Bogotá contributes 8.8% to the total of cases in the country. According to the WHO, more than 9,299 cases of tuberculosis are diagnosed annually among health workers, which were reported in only 60 countries, with the rate of transmission of Mycobacterium tuberculosis, which increases the risk of exposure to biological pollutants, including the Mycobacterium tuberculosis.
