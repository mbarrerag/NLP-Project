# Methodological proposal for the assembly and direction of the bambuco “Guabina Chiquinquirá” in a symphonic band of category – children’s level

This document presents a series of methodological proposals from the systematization of the experience experienced by the author in the process of assembling a work of the Colombian repertoire in a symphonic band of children's category whose result was successful. It presents a contextualization on the symphonic bands in Colombia, the symphonic Band of La Calera (Cundinamarca) and on bamboo. It shows an analysis of the form, a harmonic analysis and a analysis by textural role of the work “Guabina Chiquinquireña” as well as the different phases of the pedagogical process carried out. . In this document, a series of methodological proposals are proposed from the systematization of the experience lived by the author in the process of assembbling a work of the Colombian handicap in a symphonic band of children's categorided from the system outcome
