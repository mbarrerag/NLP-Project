# Analysis of the physicochemical and rheological properties affecting the sedimentation of mineral suspensions in thickeners in the cement industry

This research study focuses on evaluating the effect that the change in the physical and chemical properties and rheological results in the performance of mineral sedimentation. To this end, the impact of particle size and distribution was examined, as well as the percentage of solids in the suspension, varying between 15%, 25% and 53% of solids. Also considered was the z potential of the minerals present (calcite, "bauxite" and "caolin") together with the coagulation-flocculation process using doses of 7 and 12 g/t respectively. The experimental processes, performed by the Kynch method for the sedimentation batch, showed a marked influence of these parameters, achieving significant increases in the sedimentation rate, of up to 335 %. In addition, the impact of a wide distribution of particle size was observed, but also significantly reduced the phenomenon of paddedness (teat of water). meters, obtaining significant increases in sedimentation speed of up to 335%. Additionally, the consequence of having a wide particle size distribution was observed, where fine particles exhibit the phenomena of sewing, affecting the coarse particles. Furthermore, the importance of the coagulation-floculation process in gravity separation methods is highlighted, not only achieving an increase in sedimentation speed but also significantly reducing the turbidity of the overflow water
