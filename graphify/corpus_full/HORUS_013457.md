# Adaptation of the instrument “The Georgia Family Q-Sort” to the Colombian context

The dynamic families are understood as those patterns of interaction between all family members, which have implications for the family functioning and mental health of their members. The instrument “The Georgia Family Q-Sort” is identified as an observation measure that uses the methodology Q, whose theoretical framework is consistent with the systemic epistemology, so it is considered a measure sensitive to the relational characteristics of this construct. Currently there is no measure of observation of the family dynamics sensitive to the particularities of the Colombian context, so in this study the first version adapted culturally to the Colombian context of the instrument The Georgia Family Q-Sort is developed, evaluating its semantic, idiomatic, cultural, operational and measurement equivalence. The study was developed in four phases, taking into account the need to adapt the items, the manual of codification and the training of codifiers. The application of the instrument was carried out to 19 Colombian families, which had children between 21 months and 13 years of age, as well as various family compositions (nuclear, large, single-parent, homoparent and anuclears). t.
