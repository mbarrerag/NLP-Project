# THE PLEBISHING OF THE PEACE A PROCESS OF CITIZEN PARTICIPATION IN WHICH ALL COLOMBIA LOST

The article presents a reflection on the process of citizen participation that took place around the plebiscite for peace held in October 2016 as a mechanism for endorsing the negotiation process signed between the government of Juan Manuel Santos and the FARC guerrillas. The article presents the contribution made in this regard in the framework of the book "Winning No, Lost Colombia: The Refrendation of Peace Four Years Later", edited by IEPRI
