# IMPLEMENTING AN INSILIUM PLATFORM FOR IDENTIFYING UNTIL ANTIGENS FOR DESIGN OF VACUNES AGAINST SARS-COV-2 AND OTHER PATOGENS

On March 12, 2020, WHO declared the pandemic alert by SARS-CoV-2 with devastating cost to the lives of the most vulnerable: adults over 65 years of age and people with comorbidities and the design of vaccines against SARS-CoV-2 is an urgent need. The response of T-CD8 lymphocytes (LT-CD8+) that recognize viral antigens is a fundamental part of the immunity against viruses. For LT-CD8+ to respond to an infection, sequences of 9-10 amino acids derived from viral proteins must be presented to LT-CD8+ in class I major histocompatibility complex molecules. By its simple and low cost chemical structure synthetic peptides should be more used as a vaccine.
