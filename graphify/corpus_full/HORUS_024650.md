# PHYSICS APPLIED TO ENGINEERING: TO PROPOSAL METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL

After several years with difficulties in teaching electromagnetism, in which the teacher is aware that only a small part of the students of the class are understanding and that the vast majority only meets the minimum of the course requirement to pass, I saw the need for the appropriate knowledge to be really useful, applied to the needs of their lives and that it was not possible to separate students from it in what remains of their training and then in their professional career. The alternative was to create a subject, that students will take when they have passed 70% of the career in which they, with the realization of short projects, integrate the knowledge of the career already seen and approved, in physics, thermodynamics and heat transmission, electromagnetism, circuit theory, mechanics and resistance of materials, photometry, complementing with work in statistics, economy and numerical methods. At this conference I will present the experience and achievements with a view to inviting to evaluate it from the scientific and pedagogical point of view
