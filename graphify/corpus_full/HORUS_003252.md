# PHOTOCATICAL DEGRADATION OF CIPROFLOXACIN BY BI2O3/ZNO SEMICONDUCTORS

Pharmaceutical products are therapeutic resources used in modern society for human and animal welfare, however, they are classified as rapidly growing emerging pollutants (EC) as residues of these compounds have been found in various aquatic ecosystems worldwide [1]. Pharmaceutical products are a category of EC that covers a wide range of compounds, ranging from analgesics, antibiotics, hormones, personal care products among others [2] In particular antibiotics have become a growing concern worldwide, as these compounds, designed to combat bacterial infections, are now found in various sources of water and their presence has been associated with antimicrobial resistance [3]. The presence of these antibiotics in aquatic environments is mainly related to processes of excretion of the drug and its metabolites by renal or biliary route [4], as well as by hospital waste or by direct disposal in domestic waste water due to an inadequate final disposition [5].
