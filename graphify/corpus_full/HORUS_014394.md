# Characterization of cases of central nervous system glial tumors in a reference hospital of high complexity in the city of Tunja, department of Boyacá - Colombia.

To describe the characteristics of the glial tumors of the population of the Hospital of San Rafael de Tunja between 2017 and 2021. Observational, unicentric and retrospective study of 51 cases of the period 2017 - 2021, demographic, clinical, imageological, functional and survival variables were analyzed. RStudio v2022.07.2 was used for statistical analysis. There is a lower survival in patients with grade IV glioma (average survival 505.18 days SD +/-169.3) compared to lower grade gliomas (average survival grade III 1862.3 SD +/- 668.7) (Log Rank   0.05) The oncological treatment is associated with higher survival independently of the histological subtype (Kaplan Meier Log Rank 0.023) and higher duration of the mRankin score less than or equal to 2 (Log Rank 0.0023).
