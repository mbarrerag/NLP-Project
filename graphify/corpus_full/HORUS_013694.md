# Deforestation in the Colombian Amazon following the signing of the Peace Agreement between the Government of Colombia and FARC-EP

This paper examines the environmental problems of deforestation in the Colombian Amazon region following the Peace Agreement signed between the Government of Colombia and the FARC-EP in 2016. It analyses in particular the situation in the 42 territorial entities of the Amazon, which were previously occupied by the FARCEP and were free to access various legal and illegal structures. To address this problem, the factors driving deforestation, such as illegal and licent mining of minerals, illicit crops, hydrocarbon exploitation, land grabbing and extensive livestock were considered. These elements are fundamental to approaching the deforestation process and its effects in the Amazon region, by providing a detailed vision that allows contributing to the comprehensive understanding of the challenges and opportunities faced by the inhabitants of the Colombian Amazonia in their search for peace and the conservation of this important ecosystem. .
