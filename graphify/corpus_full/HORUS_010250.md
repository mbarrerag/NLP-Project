# On the moral significance of the testimony of the damage in the report Enough! Colombia: Memories of War and Dignity

This paper presents a study on the moral meaning of the testimony of the damage in ¡Enough! Colombia: memories of war and dignity. As is well known, the accounts of the surviving victims have been the main source of the documentation of the Colombian armed conflict that has been carried out by the Historical Memory Group (GMO). This raises the concern about the type of witness that has been the GMH in Enough! Indeed, such testimonies do not only offer information about the events, but, even more, they seek to challenge those who are willing to listen to them about the seriousness of the moral damage suffered by their owners.
