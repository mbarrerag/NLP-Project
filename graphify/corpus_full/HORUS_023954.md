# MAGDALENA

The Curves of Duration of Caudals (CDC) are a tool used in the Engineering of Hydraulic Resources and its regionalization allows to obtain data of flows in areas in which there are no records. In this study the regionalization of CDC is addressed from the similarity between different parameters of the basins of study. The method was implemented in a basin of 31140 km2 in the Middle Valley of Magdalena (VMM), a central area with important economic activities, such as exploration and production of hydrocarbons, agriculture and livestock. The regionalization was obtained by means of a methodology consisting of the grouping of sub-basins as a function of the drainage area, carrying out the adjustment of the CDCs by means of five mathematical models of regression: potential, exponential, logarithmic, quadratic and cubic, to later generate the regionalization model from a multiple regression group in function of the following characteristics of the basins: drainage area, perimeter of the basin, value of the multi-year average precipitation, length of the main channel and mean elevation of the basin Error, to obtain the logistic adjustment by the Cush Cushient for the CD
