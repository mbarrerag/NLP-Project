# FACTORS INFLUING IN THE DESIGN OF THE BOVI OWN DOUBLE SYSTEM IN ARAUCANO PIEDEMONTE (COLOMBIA)

The present study aimed to evaluate the factors that influence the performance of the double purpose system in Piedemonte Araucano. The pro-ductive records of 973 cows belonging to the project were analyzed. Did development of a technological management program for the social and productive innovation of meat and milk in bovine production systems of the Llanos region of Colombia (PBA)?, led by the National University of Colombia, between 2016 and 2017. In this research, we evaluated the genetic potential of the bovine animal resource in the double bovine production system, for this purpose we found that the characteristic production of milk by lactation (PLL) here is greater or less according to the number of births, as well as varies significantly according to the municipality, the racial group and the duration of lactation; as to the variable weight system (PD), we found that the characteristic of milk by lactation (PLL) is greater or less in accordance with the number of births (Arauquita, Fortuena and Tame)
