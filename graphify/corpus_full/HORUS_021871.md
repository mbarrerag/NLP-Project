# Assessment of the impact on motivation in learning of computer programming students by using an automatic evaluation tool.

The teaching and learning in the educational context has required through the time of different efforts by the students and the teachers to be able to give a satisfactory process in which the objective of guaranteeing a complete learning is met. In the learning of computer programming the students have been able to face different challenges, so the role of the teacher, in its constant search and update, chooses to include innovative strategies through the support of computer technologies in order to facilitate the appropriation of knowledge by the student. There are increasing educational strategies that integrate technological tools of automatic evaluation of type judge online in the methodologies of programming class since they allow: to motivate the student, promote interest in the topics from practice and competence and improve academic performance. These integrations in the methodologies of programming class have recorded through the literature that their effects are positive in terms of improvement in performance, skills, improvement in logic and participation. However, it is evident that there is a lack of studies in order to determine whether the motivation, as a learning factor, is affected by the intervention of automatic evaluation tools in the class sessions. It is for this reason that the article focuses on the impact of the students. The results show that students have increased self-efficacy levels for their performance. These results allow them to better understand the effects of integrating automatic evaluation tools into the motivation to learn from computer programming students
