# Building consensus in practice: the case of Wikipedia.: Actions and mechanisms of the platform to consolidate reliability. Regarding the validity, verifiability and veracity of information on the Internet

This monograph addresses the study of reliability in Wikipedia in Spanish, focusing on four key aspects: the validity, verifiability and veracity of the information, as well as the editing system performed by its users. The reliability is configured not only on the Internet, but also on social interactions, social networks and the organizational strategies of communities, covering areas such as government, trade and knowledge production. The study of reliability of Wikipedia has been addressed from different perspectives, such as statistics, comparisons with other publications and historical analyses, as well as the review of the process of editing and collaboration.
