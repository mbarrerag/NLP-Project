# Location of power quality stationary disruptions sources in distribution power grids

The origin of the seasonal disturbances is a problem that has been addressed from multiple perspectives. Several methods have been developed to determine sources of disturbance, devices that inject harmonics, the contribution of the elements to the development of a disturbance, assignment of responsibilities and a long list of terms used to refer to the same thing, the problem of Causality. This problem in the context of quality of power part of the own definition of cause and, with it, the clarification of other terms such as Source and Responsibility. Once clear, the identification and development of mathematical techniques that allow its analysis is a task that involves multiple efforts aimed not only at mathematical development, but also at the identification and construction of scenarios that allow to contrast each theoretical proposal. This work addresses the problem of the causality in power quality from different mathematical disciplines. In addition, each proposal is evaluated through case studies, some implemented from the point of departure of istation hasted into real systems.
