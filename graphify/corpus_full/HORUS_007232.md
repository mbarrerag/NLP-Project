# The legal challenges of the principle of the most beneficial condition in survivors' pensions: Analysis of their legal impact

This investigation is a dogmatic study of the interpretation and argumentation criteria used by the Constitutional Court and the Supreme Court of Justice regarding the principle of the most beneficial condition in the survivor's pension. By identifying dissimilar criteria between the two courts, the Constitutional Court with a more guaranteeist and extensive criterion, while the Supreme Court with a more restrictive and cautious view, proceeded to perform a hermeneutic-analytic analysis, on the most relevant judgements to identify the philosophical and theoretical positions that support each position and can be generating the existing legal uncertainty vis-à-vis the legal institution under consideration . This research work is a dogmatic study on the criterion of interpretation and argument used by the Constitutional Court and the Supreme Court of Justice ruling the principle of the most favourable condition in the survival pension pension
