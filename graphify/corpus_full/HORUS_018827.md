# Electoral Reforms in Latin America: Challenges in Institutional Change Processes and Challenges for Democratic Construction.

Latin America is one of the regions of the world that has carried out the most electoral reforms in the last forty years. Since 1978, at least 265 reforms have been identified to different key dimensions of electoral systems, such as the representation formula, legal thresholds, the introduction of gender or parity quotas in the registration of candidates, presidential reelection, the size of legislative houses, voting from abroad, among others. This chapter gives an account of the challenges and challenges that electoral reforms continue to face in the area of democratic construction. These relate mainly to (1) the emergence of endogenous and exogenous sources of electoral reform, (2) the role of strategic negotiation in the approval of reforms, (3) the impact of reforms on political parties, and (4) gender equality in political representation positions
