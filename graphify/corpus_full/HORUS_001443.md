# SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF PROTOCOLS BASED ON HIGH SENSIBILITY TROPONIUM TO CONFIRM AND DISCLOSURE THE AUDENT INFART OF MIOCARDIUM: A COMPARISON OF TRADITIONAL APPROACHES AND ASSISTED BY PRE-ENTRED GENERATIVE TRANSFORMERS (GPT)

Risk in patients with chest pain is classified using a focused clinical history and a risk assessment, the clinical decision pathways are protocols to define risk. Natural language processing (PLN) allows computers to understand, interpret and generate human language. ChatGPT is a new network architecture based on care mechanisms. To investigate ChatGPT's potential for natural language processing by selecting articles within a systematic review and using ChatGPT to assist in writing an article. On a systematic review of the previous literature to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of protocols based on troponin for early discharge of patients with suspected infarction, ChatGPT was entrusted with the task of selecting articles by reviewing its titles, summaries and full texts. The results were compared to those of the original search. The papers were entered into the chatpdf.com platform under strict supervision. From 3,509 identified studies, three articles were selected for inclusion in the systematic review by ChatGTP: the HEART Pathway, a selection of the ADAPT of 2 hours vs. the review of the article.
