# Impact of rewards on the work performance of civil servants at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Manizales: The role of mediator of commitment

This study aimed to evaluate the impact of rewards, both material and non material, on the labor performance of the administrative public employees of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Manizales. The research was based on the theory of the Vision of the Firm Based on Resources (RBV) and was structured into five hypotheses. A self-managed survey based on the selected variables was carried out, which was sent online to the 200 employees, where 105 of them responded freely and without pressure, which could increase the sincerity of the answers. The methodology used was the modeling of structural equations of partial squares (PLS-SEM), which was selected for its advantages in the analysis of small samples and its ability to manage complex structures. The validity and reliability of the constructs were evaluated through indicators such as Cronbach's Alfa and the average variance of the employed (AVE). The results of the analysis showed that the results of the analysis, which positively influenced the hypotheses 1 and 2, which suggested that the material rewards positively influence the work performance, were not confirmed.
