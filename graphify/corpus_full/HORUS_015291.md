# Technical and economic feasibility for scaling up of production technology of cocoa seedlings (Theobroma cacao L.) at the level of Biofactory.

The cocoa (Theobroma cacao L.) is a commercial crop in many developing countries and provides the raw material for chocolate production. As the demand for food increases, it is necessary to establish optimal crops and in this sense, the production of plants at scale is necessary to satisfy the growing demand, becoming the propagation systems of seedlings into a challenge for agro-industrial strengthening. To address this obstacle, the method of somatic embryogenesis (ES) is proposed and to make the transition from a research scale to a commercial scale. The estimated time for production is usually underestimated, which turns out to be unviable to face markets. In the first chapter the production of the genotype CCN51 at a laboratory scale with a view to reducing the time in the process. The different stages of production of seedlings were defined by SE: initiation, multiplication, ripening and acclimatisation. The minimum time obtained for the production of CCN51 was 30, 70, 50, 70 and 30 days respectively. In order to continue technically enabling the system of propagation of seedlings was necessary. The main indicators of the process in materials, labor, quality and performance are detailed. Finally, considering the ES as a technology that is usually expensive compared to other propagation techniques, chapter 4 identified key factors in the financial viability for the production process. The costs of the process were estimated, identifying the factors that influence the process with a Monte Carlo Simulation (MCS). The components of the cost identified were medium of cultivation (CM), indirect manufacturing costs (IMC), labor (direct and indirect) and operating costs (GO). The labor force had the greatest cost participation, with 53%, followed by GOs, with 30%, CM, with 12% and BMI, with 5%. The MCS helped define that the variables with the greatest impact on the unit price were the response of embryos at the germination stage and the proliferation coefficient. This projection showed a figure of US $ 0.73 per seedling
