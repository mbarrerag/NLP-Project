# A 25-RlD REVIEW. INTEGRATED CALDS MONITORY SYSTEM.

The rivers have been very important for the development of humanity, since since the beginning of civilization human beings have settled near them for water consumption, ease of transport, fertility of the lands and agricultural productivity, among others. Without them, it would have had a very limited development, since they supply much of our needs, hence, for example, many of the cities are located next to a water source. In contrast, while enjoying their beneficent ones are made inappropriate use of rivers, are exploited inadequately and cause considerable imbalances in them, as decrease and change in the regulation of the flows that carry, pollution, increments in the frequencies of floods, among others. Thus, the populations that live near the rivers very often become victims of devastating floods, very prolonged droughts or diseases of water origin.
