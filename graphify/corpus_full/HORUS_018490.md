# THE FAMILY IN THE PREVENTION OF DELIUM IN THE UNITY OF INTENSIVE CARE: SCOPING REVIEW

Introduction Delirium is a cognitive alteration related to negative results in the internal patient in the intensive care unit (ICU), family interventions have proven to be effective in reducing the incidence of this condition. Objective To identify the strategies that include the family in the prevention of delirium in the adult ICU that can be integrated into the ABCDEF. Inclusion criteria: Studies that describe actions and interventions that include caregivers and family members in the ICU for the prevention of delirium in adults, carried out in the last 5 years, available in full text, in Spanish, Portuguese and English. Methods A review of scope was carried out using the keywords "Critical Care, Delirium, Family, Primary Prevention" in 11 databases (PubMed, Virtual Health Library, Cochrane Library, TRIP Data base, EBSCO, Ovid Nursing, Springer, Scopus, Dialnet, Scielo, Lilacs) and other sources (Open Gray, Google Scholar), between the months of August-October 2019; 8 studies were considered as relevant and were analyzed in the outcomes
