# Induction of defense in Solanum betaceum towards attack by Phytophthora infestans sensu lato by β-aminobutyric acid

The drop or the tick caused by Phytophthora infestans (Mont.) De Bary, is a disease of great importance in the production of potato and table tomato, because it is devastating and of great economic impact. In addition to these two species, its presence in other crops of the family Solanaceae as Solanum betaceum (Tomate de arbol o Tamarillo) and Solanum quitoense (Lulo) was reported. The agroecological characteristics of the areas where tomato tree is grown are highly favorable for almost the whole year, to the development of P. infestans, identifying strong epidemics in recent decades, which has caused significant losses to farmers. The fruit of tree tomato is characterized by its high nutritional and agro-industrial value, therefore, has an export potential to several countries. The disease management, in the absence of resistant varieties, is a difficult task and focuses on the use of conventional fungicides as its method of control. Consequently, a negative impact on the health and the environment is generated, in addition to an increase of pheno-BAtica-resistant diseases. The late blight caused by Phytophthora infestans (Mont.) De Bary, is the most devastating disease disease in potato and tomate crop worldwide. Besides these, late blight has been reported in other Andean crops of the Solanaceae family such as Solanum betaceum (Tree tomato or Tamarillo) and Solanum quitoense (Lulo). The agroecological characteristics of the areas where tree tomatoes are favorable to the development of P. infestans, with strong epidemics being identified in recent decades, which is a serious factor of advantage to farmers.
