# Development of strategies for the initiation of tuba learning in the Symphony for Peru programme

Development of Strategies for the Initiation of Tuba Learning in the Symphony Programme for Peru This work by means of research, locates problems, skills and knowledge, which a student who initiates the tuba specialty of the Symphony programme for Peru needs to solve and develop, so that at the end of his participation of the program has the basic knowledge for a correct interpretation of the tuba. To achieve this objective the reader will find throughout this work very concrete teaching strategies, which the Tuba teacher of any Symphony core for Peru can develop in the training process of his students. Not to mention that many of these students could not choose to continue with professional music studies. .
