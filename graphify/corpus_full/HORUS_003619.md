# DISTRIBUTION OF LLUVIOUS WATER CONCESSIONS IN THE FLORICULTOR SECTOR IN THE EAST 2014-2023

The flower sector in Colombia stands out as a key player in the country's economic development and a relevant player in the global market because it is not only the agricultural activity that generates the most employment per unit of area [1], but is also characterized by its efficiency in the use of a relatively small but highly technified space, with vast knowledge and great entrepreneurial capacity [2]. This work analyzed the spatial distribution of water concessions for the flower sector in the East of Antioquia for the period 2014 to 2015 with the aim of identifying the different strategies used for the reuse of the water resource and the realization of the activity in a sustainable way
