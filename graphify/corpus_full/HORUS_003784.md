# BIVARIATE DISTRIBUTION BIRBAUM-SAUNDERS UNITARIA

The Unitary Distribution Birnbaum Saunders (UBS), \cite{Mazucheli(2018)}, has support in the interval (0,1), which is why it is used successfully in the modelling of rates and indicators. Two new bivariate distributions, the Bivariate Distribution Birnbaum Saunders Unitaria (BVUBS) and the Bivariate Distribution Sinh-Normal Birnbaum Saunders Unitaria (BVUSHN), are presented as a natural effect, the regression model for the case of covariables in the model, using the concept of conditionally specified distributions, these distributions are able to model rates and proportions in the unit plane, and present a better adjustment to data compared to other distributions. Likewise, some general properties of the models, expected values and inference by maximum likelihood and application to real data.
