# Evidence-based clinical standard: diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of patients with SARS-CoV-2 infection with lung involvement at the National University Hospital of Colombia

This book represents the Clinical Standard based on evidence: diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of the patient with SARS-CoV-2 infection with lung involvement in the National University Hospital of Colombia. This ECBE is framed in a process of standardization of health care, taking into account the best evidence, available resources and interdisciplinarity, with the purpose of generating a comprehensive approach that improves the outcomes of patients and optimizes the use of resources at hospital level. It recognizes the importance of standardization of care of patients with this condition, due to its importance for the institution and for the comprehensive care of the patient with this condition.-- Contracovered - Based on the best available evidence, this ECBE provides recommendations on the diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation of adult patients with suspicion or confirmation of infection by SARS-CoV-2 with lung involvement attended in the University (general hospitalisation room or ICU). The recommendations contained in this ECBE are also addressed to healthcare professionals involved in the care of patients with SARS-CoV-2 infection with lung health (specialists in internal medicine, infectology, negology, intensive care, radiotherapy and infirm)
