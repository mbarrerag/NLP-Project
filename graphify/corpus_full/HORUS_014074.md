# Maintenance of the equipment of the Genesis Soil Laboratory, Sluggish Sections and Impregnations

The laboratory of soil Genesis, Slices Delgadas and Impregnations has not developed activities in recent years. The new coordination of the laboratory is very interested in quickly re-establishing research and teaching activities and therefore needs funding to adapt the space and maintain it to the instruments used. For the practices currently being developed in the courses of Geomorphology, Soil Chemistry and Geochemistry, it is essential to recover the stereoscopic microscopes and magnifying glasses that will allow to develop practices to analyze rock composition, mineral identification and soil characterization. Repair and maintenance of laboratory equipment will contribute to the improvement of methodologies of teaching earth sciences
