# IMPLEMENTATION OF SPACE PREACHING FOR IMPROVING THE COBERTURE OF THE COMPARTED BICICLETES SYSTEM IN MANIZALS, COLOMBIA

Abstract The objective of this research is to evaluate the influence of increasing the number of stations in the public bicycle system in Manizales (Colombia) by applying models of transport supply and identifying the sectors benefited in terms of average travel time. From the pedestrian network, charged with the operational speed of walking, geostatistical models are applied for the calculation of the isochronic curves in three scenarios, the current one and two proposals for new stations; then a geospatial coverage analysis is carried out by relating the curves obtained for each scenario to the sociodemographic and socio-economic layer information layer. Both proposals resulted in an increase in coverage of the lower-income population, contributing to greater equity. It is concluded that planning the implementation and expansion of public bicycle systems is crucial to offer to a greater percentage of the population other options for efficient mobility. Key words: supply models; coverage; sustainability; transport system; urban planning
