# Death among Panches: Bioarcheological Analysis of Funeral Urns of the Late Period in Guaduas, Cundinamarca

The archaeological site La Bodeguita, excavated in 2016 and located in Guaduas (Cundinamarca), corresponds to a tomb of well and chamber belonging to the Panches, a group Karib that inhabited the valley of the Middle Magdalene between the eighth and sixteenth centuries, until its extermination with the arrival of the Spaniards to America. The aim of this research is to contribute to the knowledge of the funeral practices of the Panches and to identify the relationship between these and the biological profile of the individuals, from a bioarchaeological analysis, mixed (qualitative and quantitative) and integral of the funerary context. The sample is composed of 6 funeral urns and 1158 mixed bone structures, most with a significant tafonomic damage. The integral analysis allowed the following to be evidenced: a differential sexual and etary distribution of the bone structures in relation to the ceramic characteristics of the funeral urns, and the cult of the shamanism and the gypst.
