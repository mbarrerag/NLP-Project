# In vitro equivalence study of some acyclovir medicinal products, presented as immediate-release tablets, marketed in Colombia.

The present study was carried out in order to determine the possible in vitro equivalence of two medicinal products containing acyclovir in pharmaceutical form of immediate release tablets against the leading product in Colombia, in accordance with the guidelines set out in Resolution 1124 of 2016 of the Ministry of Health and Social Protection. To this end, the validation of the analytical methodology for the quantification of the active principle aciclovir was carried out, at each of the pH levels evaluated, as well as the quality control of the different batches of the three products that make part of the study, verifying compliance with the specifications set out in the monograph of aciclovir tablets USP 41. The profiles of dissolution at the three pH levels (1.2; 4.5 and 6.8) were statistically analyzed, comparing efficiency and mean time of dissolution. The results showed that both the comparator product and the two test products were dissolved very quickly, so these two products were considered equivalent to the market leader and the comparison of profiles was not necessary by calculating f1 and f2 factors.
