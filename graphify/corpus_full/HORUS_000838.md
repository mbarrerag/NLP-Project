# ABY WARBURG. THE IMAGE, FOSIL IN MOVEMENT

Aby Warburg was one of the greatest thinkers and critics of the most outstanding culture of the 20th century. He is the patient apprentice of images, the philologist with many eyes, and the psychohistory who perceives Western schizophrenia in the figurative world. This book offers a tour of the key notions of Warburg's work, especially Nachleben and Pathosformel, in which a fruitful proximity of this thinker's work is noticed with the work of Sigmund Freud
