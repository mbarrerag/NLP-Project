# Characterization of the ancestry profile in a sample of the three main ethnic groups (Amerindians, Caucasian-Mestizos and Afro-descendants) of the Colombian population, using information markers of ancestorry – INDEL

Colombia is one of the most diverse countries at cultural and ethnic level, the different historical migratory processes and sociopolitical dynamics of the country have allowed that at the present time there is a population with different mixing patterns derived from three main ethnic groups: European, Native American and African.The majority of previous studies have focused on characterizing the Colombian mestizo Caucasian population using different types of informational markers of ancestrality (IMA), however, few studies have analyzed the Amerindian and Afro-Colombian populations.In this study, a total of 153 Amerindian individuals (AMR) of 28 indigenous communities of Colombia, 164 Afro-Colombians (AFC) of different regions of the department of Chocó and 150 individuals of mixed ancestrality (MIX) of the central Andean region were used.In the analysis of 46 markers of type AIM-INDEL amplified in a single multiplex PCR (AMR) and were analyzed by means of the software Genepper ID.The Allelic frequencies, analysis of the balance of Hardy-Weinberg and the analysis of genetic distance (Fstquin) were calculated using the software. The work presented here contributes to the continuous effort to document the human genetic variability in Colombia, by focusing on exploring populations that have not been studied in previous analyses of Colombian genetic structure. .
