# Radar based monitoring system to protect the Colombian Amazon Rainforest

The Colombian Amazon rainforest faces multiple pressures and threats, including wildlife exploitation, illegal mining, deforestation for agriculture and cattle ranching, and oil exploration. Different technologies can be used to alert and consequently prevent these illegal activities. In this context, this paper proposes the design, development and implementation of a prototype monitoring system for the Colombian Amazon rainforest. This system uses a compact radar system, PTZ cameras, satellite communications and artificial intelligence algorithms to detect, track and identify moving targets. It also provides real-time data on possible intrusions or disturbances in the area and sends early alerts by email with detailed information on detected targets
