# Feminist Confluences: 30 Years of Gender Studies at the National University and its Impact on Cultural and Social Transformation

The School of Studies of Genero emerged from synergies that sought to create a space of study and teaching of the problems that generate the inequalities lived by women, their emancipatory struggles and the questions of the androcentric canon of knowledge.Compromised with the political, social and ethical transformation, the EEG has promoted critical approaches in its research and university curricula.Since its inception, it has worked with grassroots communities and local and national institutions to promote equality through public and gender-based policies and plans, reflecting in numerous research and extension projects. This 2024, with the occasion of the 30 years of the academic program that gave origin to the EEG, have organized the events "Feminist Confluences", designed to reflect on the EEG as an academic and polycentric project. The objective has been to review its precepts, bets and methodologies, raising key questions about its origin, evolution, shortcomings and future. These events have generated a valuable set of reflections that, although they have a significant dimension of academic, also integrates the communities. highlighting the fundamental role played by humanities and social sciences with a feminist perspective in the commitment to a more just and egalitarian society
