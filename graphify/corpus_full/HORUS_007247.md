# Influence of organic matter content on resistance to residual cutting of Bogota clay

An experimental study of the resistance to residual cutting of the clays of Bogotá with high organic matter content (CMO) was carried out. For the above, a annular cutting model was developed, designed to evaluate the resistance to cutting of the clays to large displacements. The samples analyzed were extracted from the campus of the National University of Colombia and characterized physically, chemically and mineralogically with the aim of evaluating its mechanical behavior. The results obtained showed that the high MOC of the samples analyzed increased the natural water content and the limits of consistency while the specific gravity decreased due to its porous structure and its low density. The MOC did not directly influence the angle of peak resistance (-), remaining approximately constant; while the apparent cohesion (c′) tends to decrease as the MOC increases. Regarding the residual resistance, a significant decrease was identified with the increase of the clay particles, decreasing the coefficient of residual resistance (-r/) to the high point of contact, and the applicable resistance angle (-) decreased 20% with an increase of 38% of the CMO.
