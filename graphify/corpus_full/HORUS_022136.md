# TUNABILITY OF MULTIPLE TRANSMISSION CHANNELS IN QUASIPERIODIC ONE-DIMENSIONAL PHOTONIC CRYSTALS

This work proposes a multiple transmission channel model in peri-odic and quasiperiodic one-dimensional photonic crystals. The constituent layers of the photonic structure are a plasma layer and a dielectric (quartz). Herein, the magnetic field is the tuning mechanism for propagating modes in the transmittance spectrum.
