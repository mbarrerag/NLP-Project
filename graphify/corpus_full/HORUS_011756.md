# BUILDING PEDAGOLOGICAL KNOWLEDGE: RESEARCH IN HIGHER STUDIES AND OTHER EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS

At present, one of the central purposes of the Colombian State is the initial and ongoing training of teachers capable of guiding the children and young people that the country requires in the context of post-conflict education. In this order of ideas, the fourth strategic challenge of the Ten-Year Education Plan 2016-2020, which focuses on the construction of a public policy for the training of educators, has as one of its objectives to promote research in education and pedagogy and research training in educational institutions and in graduate programmes through strategies such as seed-miners, groups and research networks, among others. (men, 2017, p. 25). In line with educational policies, it is essential to support and accompany teachers in their research processes, especially at the most critical stage and on which they have less experience, which is the publication of results of their research and pedagogical reflections.
