# Lead 210 and polonium 210 measures in human consumption water and residual water

In Colombia, there has been both a lack of constant studies on the presence of natural radioactive materials (NORM) and a normative framework, which has contributed to the lack of knowledge about the magnitude of this problem at the national level. Human beings need water sources and in particular water to be able to live, because it depends a great part of the functioning of the human body and its environment. The presence of radioactive materials in water sources and in water for human consumption sometimes comes from mining or lying in wells in some regions where these waters are still extracted, this generates some harmful effects for human beings and animals. This project aims to determine the presence of lead210 and polonium210 in waters not only of human consumption but also waste water, since in some cases residual water passes through treatment plants, but still there are regions in Colombia where these waters go to places near homes where it is expected to evaporate and return to the environment generating an exposure for which there are not many studies.
