# Hydrospheres Biomimetic pedagogic park-pabellon for water management in the former industrial area of Toupnot, Lourdes-France

Hydrospheres is a proposal for a park - pavilion developed in order to manage rainwater based on biomimetic technologies extracted from the ecosystem and local agencies of Lourdes-France. In this space, both pedagogical, cultural and recreational programmatic activities and spaces that manage rainwater are articulated to provide a closed cycle of rainwater
