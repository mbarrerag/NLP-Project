# Use of cocoa shell as a primary source for obtaining materials applied to engineering by studying mechanical properties.

The by-products generated by cocoa can be used by using these in the field of materials engineering. The mechanical properties of cocoa shelling accompanied by a type of binder can be determined by means of tests carried out in a laboratory as they are: tension, bending, compression and impact. The results obtained are presented in the VS deformation stress diagram where it is possible to deduce with what type of material is being treated and what properties it presents. The objective of this final mastery work was to analyze the by-products of cocoa generated during the production processes as a substitute for materials used in engineering. In some research, the use of agro-industrial products is made more evident, since they are the raw material to produce new materials or products thus avoiding both waste and environmental damage. The methodology was based on the manufacture of 9 test pieces for each of the tests for a total of 36. Groups of three probetas were made with volumetric percentages of 50%, 70% and 90% of crushed cocoa shell.
