# MORCOLOGIC AND MOLECULLAR CHARACTERIZATION OF SERATOCYSTIS SP. CACCOA CULTIVES PROVIDENTS IN COLOMBIA

This is the case of the plant origin of cocoa in Colombia. The plant protection problems of cocoa in Colombia have been the main cause of the reduction of production of the crop, with losses that can reach 100% due to inadequate management. The machete disease is one of the diseases that affects this crop in the country and has been reported to Ceratocystis fimbriata as the cause agent of the disease. However, there are still incompatibilitys between authors that contradict the causal relationship of this pathogen with the disease and in Colombia, the information on the importance of this pathology, the identity and taxonomic aspects of the fungus is reduced. This study had as a morphological and molecularly isolated characteristics of Ceratocystis sp., obtained from symptomatic wood material and soil samples, from cocoa producing zones in Colombia.
