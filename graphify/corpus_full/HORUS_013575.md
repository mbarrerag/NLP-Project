# Morphisms between semi-graded rings and polynomial applications

In this thesis, we study various classes of morphisms between families of semi-migrant rings with their corresponding polynomial applications. First, we present some notions of the theory of rings of these objects that are necessary throughout the the thesis. In order to show the generality of these rings in areas such as the theory of rings and non-conmutative geometry, we include a non-exhaustive list of non-conmutative algebras that are particular examples of these rings. Second, we develop a theory of cv-polinomies for extensions of Ore iterated over arbitrary rings extending some results in the literature. We characterize these polynomies using internal drifts, and we also consider the problem of isomorphisms between these extensions. We illustrate our treatment with various non-conmutative algebras.
