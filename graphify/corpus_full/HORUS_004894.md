# IN SUPERVIVIENT TO BOJAYA'S MASACR: NARRATIVE STUDIO OF TOPICS

the Bojayá massacre constitutes one of the most visible events of the armed conflict in Colombia. The death of approximately 119 people and the effects on survivors remain important topics of analysis and study. to characterize the mental and psychosocial health of the victims of the Bojayá massacre from the narratives of the survivors. qualitative narrative study of topics. Records of 66 survivors located in the municipality of Bojayá and in the city of Quibdó were analyzed. the mental health of the survivors to the massacre is mainly affected by persistent symptoms of sadness, fear and hopelessness. Regarding psychosocial health, the family is considered the main support network and a fundamental resource for well-being, in addition to other community networks. Family and community networks were modified, linking family fragmentation with forced migration and displacement. both the profile of signs and symptoms encountered, as well as coping strategies and forms of resilience used by survivors coincide with what was reported in other studies carried out with survivors of health.
