# CHAPTER 10 IMPORTANCE OF HUMAN MATERN LACTANCY IN BREAKEN AND UNDER WEIGHT

Comprehensive care in the health of the enteral food therapy of newborns is essential: it is a goal of singular importance in the context of timely, safe and quality care. This comprehensive care should include the realization of a nutritional medical therapy that provides the tools and supports required to care for premature newborns. Thus, this text aims to respond to challenges that arise in clinical practice and are analyzed in the context of clinical care and academia in order to meet the nutritional needs of premature newborns. In 10 chapters, this book elaborates on key aspects of clinical practice and responds to the academic and research needs that arise in the Intensive Care Units Neonatal. In sum, this work constitutes a rapid consultation document for health professionals, teachers and students who want to complement the lessons learned in the book Parental Nutritional Therapeutics. The objective of this chapter is to describe the strategies during the stay in the hospital and at home, to ensure the initiation and maintenance of human breastfeeding in the premature newborn.
