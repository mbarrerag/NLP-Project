# U'wa=Pilo, platform of didactic games for teaching and learning random thinking in first grade.

The challenges of the Covid-19 pandemic include more independent and inclusive teaching. The lack of materials that would allow teachers, parents and guardians to help in the appropriation of knowledge in children was evidenced. After the analysis and study carried out by our research group in another project, where the need for teaching materials for the teaching of random thinking by grades of schooling was demonstrated. In this project, books were made to correct the lack of material. However, there is a need to extend the work to digital material that complements the previous one, ensuring that it reaches all equally. This project aims to create a platform of serious or didactic games for the teaching of random thinking in first grade. This platform will meet certain requirements so that it can be used on any computer and that it does not need internet connection, which would allow any child in Colombia to use it. Students who will participate are associated with the Semillero de Game Serious and Video Games
