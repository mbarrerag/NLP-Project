# CONFENTRATION, DEPHOSFORATION, LIXIVATION AND EXTRACTION PROCESSING WITH SOLVENTS OF LAND LIGEROUS RARRARY ELEMENTS TO PUT OUT OF WATER ALUVIAL MINERITY RESIDUELS IN THE REGION OF THE BAGRE-NECHI MINERAL DISTRICT

The rare earth elements (REE) are a group of chemical elements that include all the lanthanides, ytrium and scandium, often divided into two categories: light rare earth elements (LREE), which comprise La, Ce, Nd and Pr, and rare earth heavy elements (HREE) covering Sm to Lu, Sc and Y [1]. Due to their particular spectroscopic and magnetic properties, the ER plays a significant role in the global economy because of its importance in many fields of advanced material science; therefore, the industrial demand for them has increased significantly [2]. In nature, the ERs do not occur in the elemental state or as individual compounds of rare earths.
