# Processes of recovery of spent pick-up acids by precipitation of heavy metals with oxalic acid and tartaric acid.

With the rise of policies more restrictive environments the industry of galvanized by hot immersion has identified a potential environmental risk in the dumpings of spent baths of picketing, which, they can mean economic losses in the long term. This motivates the research on processes able to minimize the dumpings and recover the spent acid. In this thesis is proposed the study of the recovery of these acids for its later reuse with the reaction of chelation-precipitation with oxalic acid and tartaric acid, for this, a solution of simulated the spent baths of picketing was prepared. That served as a reference for simulations of molecular dynamics in systems that emulated the conditions of the precipitation-quelation reactions. These simulations managed to replicate density measurements with a maximum error of 5%, moreover, by means of the radial distribution functions the effect of the most interaction between the metallic ions and the organic acids is determined to be the oxygens belonging to the carboxylic groups, implying a possible reaction between the ions and the carboxylic acids
