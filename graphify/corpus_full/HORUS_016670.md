# TRANSLATION IN/ FROM SEMIPERIFERIA (RUSSIA, ITALY, LATIN AMERICA)

In the last decades, the idea of center and periphery is employed insistently to describe the power relations between different languages, cultures and literatures.In 2001 Abram de Swaan described what he called the global language system, that is, the hierarchical relations between different groups of spoken languages that allow a communication at a world level. Johan Heilbron, in turn, described the hierarchy of languages in the world translation system (which includes a hypercentral language, English, central, semiperipheral and peripheral languages).From this perspective, Spanish, Russian and Italian are part of the same group: that of the semiperiphery.
