# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION BY GC/MS OF THE SENTAL OIL OF LANTANA CANESCES CULTIVATED IN COLOMBIA

The yield of essential oil extraction was 0.1% (p/p). 52 compounds (relative GC area > 0.1%) were identified. Of these trans ß-caryophyllene (15.8%), terpinene (7.7%), lemonene (7.6%), germacrene D (7.4%), p cimene (7.3%) and a-humulene (5.1%) represented ca. 51% of the total chromatographic area. trans-ß-Cariofilene and germacrene D, the most abundant sesquiterpenes, are considered as common constituents of floral and woody fragrances found in many plant genera (3). These two sesquiterpenes could alone explain several properties attributed to L. canescens; the first has been shown to exert significant anti-inflammatory effects in mice, and the second acts as pheromone of pollinizing insects. These results encourage further study of the plant with research leading to develop applications of agro-industrial utilization
