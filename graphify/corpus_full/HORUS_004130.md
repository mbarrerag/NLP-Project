# Evaluation of conductive nanofillers addition in scaffolds for myocardial tissue engineering application

This thesis explores the design, manufacture and analysis of scaffolds of nanofibres polymers with electroconductive nanorellen for the engineering of cardiac tissues, focusing on the repair of myocardial tissue by incorporating different graphene and polypyrrol (PPy) into polycaprolactone matrices (PCL) through electro-wired. The objective of the work is to establish manufacturing parameters and evaluate the morphological, mechanical, chemical, electrical and biocompatibility properties of scaffolds, in order to select those that offer an optimal electromechanical coupling with myocardial tissue. Two strategies are distinguished: one focused on incapacitated scaffolding with graphene, which show improvements in structural uniformity and mechanical properties, and another on the harmonised scaffolding of nanoparticles, known for their significant electrical conductivity, both strategies present specific advantages, such as the improvement of hydrophilicity and the promotion of cellular adhesion and proliferation, crucial for tissue regeneration. mechanical coupling with myocardial tissue.
