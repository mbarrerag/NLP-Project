# ELEMENTAL AND ELECTROCHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF BIOCERAMIC RECONCRUTIONS WITH NANOPARTICULS OF GRAPHEN, FOR THEIR POTENTIAL USE IN REPARATION AND REGENERATION

This paper presents the characterization performed by spectroscopy of electrochemical impedance and Raman mapping, for the evaluation of the composition of the bioceramic coatings of graphene-CaO-P2O5-SiO2-TiO2-TiO2 on Ti6AL4V, obtained by electrolytic plasma oxidation technique (EPO). The impedance spectroscopy (EIS), is a technique of study and analysis of materials, which allows to evaluate the current and impedance of transfer of a system of interest. In this study, the system of interest, corresponds to the interaction of the bioceramic coating on the substrate of Ti6Al4V treated, with the electrolytic solution used SBF (simulated physiological serum). The analysis of this experimental technique is carried out under the analogue configuration of a material system (show of interest)- studied medium (SBF) and an electrical circuit. The selected equivalent circuit is evaluated taking into account not only the porous nature of the coating, but also the chemical/elemental composition of this one.
