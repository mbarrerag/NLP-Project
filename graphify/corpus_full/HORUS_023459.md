# FUNCTIONAL SUMMIT ON CITY BASE, OBTAINED BY VACIUM EMPREGATION IN AN OSMODESHYDRATED MATRIX

A functional food was developed by vacuum impregnation of calcium and vitamin D in osmoded acid, hydrated using an inulin and citric acid. Impregnation parameters were determined and a calcium and vitamin D solution was formulated for 20% of the daily intake per serving. Microstructure was evaluated under the treatments and sensory tests were performed.
