# Origins and continuities of the popular neighborhood in Medellín.

In and after the development of this project it is intended to infer some of the social bases that enabled the genesis of the popular neighborhood in Medellín, to its concrete support and its discursive continuity, from the middle of the twentieth century until today. This inquiry part of the premise of the existence of social forces and economic dynamics that are attached to the industrial capitalism -in boom since the late nineteenth century and in its greater regional expression in Antioquia in the first half of the twentieth century- that they joined other phenomena of national scale and in themselves, they unleashed social processes with their respective spatial reflections in the expansion of the city, in the incorporation of lands to urban development and in the densification of their places, among many others, having as one of its consequences the emergence of the neighborhoods understood as popular, given the characteristics of their inhabitants, their architecture, their material deficiencies and their role of habitation, and containing the social conglomerate owner of the working force. árárez Betancur and directed by Professor Oscar Iván Calvo Isaza. As a contribution to the above, the researchers will investigate the sources arranged in the Documentation Unit of the School of Habitat of the National University _x0096_ Headquarters Medellín, will conduct interviews with people associated with the exercise of governmental and community power and will carry out a comprehensive territorial recognition to contrast the historical and current conditions of these neighborhoods. It is hoped to generate an important database, revised and commented on relevant studies related to the popular neighborhood, obtain some narratives of direct experiences of constitution and territorial conception, in relation to the topic of research and also, build a current popular cartography of the city.
