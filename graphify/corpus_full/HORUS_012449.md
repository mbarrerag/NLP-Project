# THE MARKET VALUE IN THE GOVERNMENT ENTITIES IN COLOMBIA. RESULTS OF TRANSITION

The research examines the application of market value in government entities in Colombia and some associated problems; for this, a case study was carried out. This document shows the results of a review of literature related to criticism of measurements, using fair value and market value in government entities. Subsequently, the main financial impacts of the application of market value as an alternative measure in the entity under study are examined, complemented by a semi-structured interview with officials involved in the process to establish the perception of the measurement criterion. As a result, it is evident that market value measurement, in the study entity, may be appropriate for obtaining up-to-date and useful information, especially for the accountability process; however, it could be risky to try to link this valuation criterion in non-market contexts, affecting transparency and accounting of accounting information
