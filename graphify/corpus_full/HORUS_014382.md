# In-hospital mortality in a patient diagnosed with RASD associated with SARS-CoV-2/Covid-19 infection requiring intensive care in a fourth-level hospital in Bogotá.

Intrahospital mortality in patients with diagnosis of RASD associated with infection SARS-CoV-2/Covid-19 with requirement for Intensive Care Unit in a fourth level hospital in Bogotá Introduction: Pandemia by COVID 19 has caused an increase in the diagnosis of acute respiratory distress syndrome (SPRS), with an unacceptably high mortality, event with high enhancement and scientific medical interest. A comprehensive characterization of patients with RASD associated with infection by Sars-CoV-2 has been described, with an analysis of mortality and of the main clinical and sociodemographic characteristics that contribute to the process. Main objective: To establish mortality in adult patients with diagnosis of RASD associated with infection SARS- CoV-2/Covid-19 in the intensive care unit Material and Methods: Type of study: Analytic observation, Design: Retrospective weight, Place: UCI Hospital Universitario Nacional de Colombia, Population: Patients who entered into RASDS during the period of pandemic Results: 576 patients were analyzed, the majority of men 411 (71.35%) and 185 women (45%). CoV-2/Covid-19 is high. Age, creatinine value at admission to ICU and the requirement for renal replacement therapy and mechanical ventilation are independent factors associated with mortality.
