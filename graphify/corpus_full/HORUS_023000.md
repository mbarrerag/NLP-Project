The development of a methodology for estimating curves is presented. Intensity Does it last for a non-stationary period for the central area of the department of Antioquia, starting from historical series of precipitate events with a temporary resolution of 15 minutes from 34 rainy seasons. A homogeneity analysis was carried out in order to assess the non-stationary nature of the series, prior to a non-stationary frequency analysis. The usefulness of implementing the non-stationary approach was demonstrated, mainly in high return periods and short durations. The non-stationary nature of the IDF curves of the precipitate in future scenarios was assessed, using the Global Climate Models of the CMIP5 and CMIP6 projects with resolutions of 1 and 3 hours, showing that the non-stationary nature of the most recent events in all cases was underestimated
