# Analysis and prediction of professor performance through student surveys. Search of relationships using data mining

The primary objective of this research study is to analyze and predict professor performance by using student surveys. Ninety-seven percent of the students of a university located in the central region of Colombia are surveyed. The survey consists of 19 questions: the first 18 represent the independent variables while question 19 represents the dependent variable (professor performance). Using the J48 algorithm (Weka platform), the main independent variables and their hidden interrelations capable of predicting the behavior of the dependent variable are selected. The results show that the most effective variables are: would enroll another subject taught by this professor, the professor cares about student learning, changed student way of thinking, learning through exams, learning in depth, encouragement in topics covered, adequate mentorship, and class preparation. In conclusion, the data-mining methodology designed here allows predicting the behavior of the dependent variable, professor performance
