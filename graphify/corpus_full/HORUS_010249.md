# PROPOSAL FOR THE USE OF DOMESTIC WATER RESIDUAL WATER UNDER THE CONCEPT OF CIRCULAR ECONOMY

Colombia has recently adopted within its regulations the concept of circular economy (EC) by making sure that a solution to the current problem in the country is made available to the lack of alternatives for the disposal and treatment of effluents that guarantee access to non-conventional technologies in the rural context, adapted to the established norms in force. This project aims to establish an alternative in the context of water flow defined in ENEC in order to take advantage of domestic wastewater (ARD) in dispersed housing. As partial results, the rural context is classified in conceptual and technical guidelines regarding the current policies and standards of the country. In addition, a database has been developed that compiles alternatives to use of ARD of potential interest for the study
