# Educational Digital Content for the Learning of Parents, a Care Act

Although access to health is a human right promoted in international policies, it is not evident the creation of virtual educational tools in health for teaching deaf parents about skin care of the newborn. The objectives of the following article were to design and evaluate digital content for teaching deaf parents, in relation to skin care, during the hydration of the newborn. An evaluational research was carried out, with descriptive scope, developed in two stages: 1) digital content design and 2) evaluation of the content by experts using the Stake model, estimated with two data matrices that allow the description and judgment of seven experts in maternal-perinatal specialty and neonatology. The thematic axes of the digital content are organized in three categories: a. basic skin care of the newborn: the first bath, air currents, dry skin, use of hypoallergenic creams; b. massages based on reflex stimulation; c. signs of alarm: persistent crying and/or acute that does not yield with food, interpretation of the high temperature by other causes. The digital content was adjusted, according to the evaluation of experts, who approved the digital care for the elderly children
