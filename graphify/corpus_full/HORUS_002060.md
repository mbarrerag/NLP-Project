# Community social networks: an interpretation from Social Work to Community action, in the context of an experience of musical training

This research carries out an exercise of interpretation through the systematization of a process of action, linked to the social networks community within the framework of the artistic and musical training program of the Philharmonic Orchestra of Bogotá, specifically in the Philharmonic Center of the City of Bolivar and in a field of contemporary action as Social Worker, specifically in the culture sector. Through the analysis of an experience of community action led by families, registered to the Philharmonic Center, it identifies how the community initiatives favor the emergence of processes of participation, defense of social programs, social cohesion and community relationation. The qualitative methodology of systematization of experiences was developed through research workshops, promoting an active role in the participating population, in order to visualize its interactions, the organization, participation and value of the action, within the framework of the social networks, the community mechanisms and the musical formation. s favor the emergence of participatory processes, the defense of social programs, social cohesion and community relationships.
