# THE OBSERVATORY OF GENDER MATTERS OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY: HISTORY, CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES

The National University of Colombia’s gender policy was established through agreement 035 of 2012. This is thanks to a history that begins in the late 1980s, when the foundations of the School of Gender Studies that would be formalized towards the first decade of the 21st century begin to be consolidated. The OAG emerges as a joint effort of various actors and today it brings together the highest authorities of the Institution and the representations of the various stages. Recent advances in the projects being implemented at the moment will be presented
