# THE STATE AND ITS TERRITORY: TERRITORIAL CONTROL AND ORDER

The State, as an expression of a territorialized political project, includes not only ideological differences and interests of political, economic, cultural, religious and military domination and control, but also the natural material basis upon which the population takes the goods that enable it to live and reproduce in it. This study refers to situations and heritages of the colonial past from a geohistorical approach, and focuses on Colombia that emerges as an independent national State, in use of the model of the national States built in Europe and North America between the end of the eighteenth and the first half of the nineteenth century. The analysis is situated from the fall of colonial empires, one of which, Spain, saw the partition of all its vast colonial territory in America become dozens of independent territories, each one committed to becoming a modern national State, with a political-administrative structure and a sovereign power over a delimited territory, which would henceforth be a territorial unity. Despite the border conflicts that would appear, the territory of each nation State.
