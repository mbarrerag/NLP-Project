# THE MIRROR: A PANGENER FILM

The mirror: a pan-genre film Summary: The Mirror (1974), by Andrei Tarkovski, is a film with a heterodox editing, as unusual and extraordinary as the very conception of the script and filming.What is the genre?Dramatic, lyrical, epic?Is it a documentary or a plot?Is it a docufiction, a fake documentary, a film essay?Is it experimental, underground?The questions could go on, since this is one of the artistic characteristics that makes this cinematographic work one of the most disturbing of the second half of the 20th century, and maintains relevance and devotion.
