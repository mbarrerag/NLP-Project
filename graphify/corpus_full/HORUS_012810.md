# Spatial transformations of the ramp-house. Projects of João Vilanova Artigas between 1949 and 1975

This research recognizes and analyzes the process of spatial transformation of a ramp houses set designed by Vilanova Artigas between 1949 and 1975. The comparative review of the projects allowed, on one hand, to determine a sequentiality between the houses and, on the other, to establish four transformations linked to the spatiality that accompanies the ramp: garden, patio, atrium and lounge. In addition, the interest of the investigation to establish the origin of the houses set led to the revision of another group of houses designed by Vilanova Artigas in 1949. The projects had many comparative variables, which were helpful for establishing the groupings and understanding the order mechanisms that prevailed in this ramp houses set. From the spatial point of view, the properties, the opening or closing to the outside, the ramp position and its relationship with other spaces, the chronological location of the houses, among others, were compared. Finally, the progressive and gradual concatenation of the works and projects of the houses set led to the conclusion, not of the investigation itself, but of what Artigas found and searched through this group of houses. About this, the idea of a typological sequence found in the investigation is appreciated in a solid and consistent way, as a structure of spatial order and formation of a type of house-ramp
