# TEM IN-SITU DEFORMATION STUDY OF MAGNESIUM REINFORCED WITH CARBON NANOTUBES BY BENDING TEST

Both grain boundary (GB) migration and grain rotation are widely recognized as important mechanisms for the release of energy in polycrystals [1,2]; this is associated with both GB (capillarity) and elastic energy contributions. Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations demonstrate that GB migration and grain rotation occur simultaneously [3]. GB migration and grain rotation are linked at specific GBs, through shear-coupled GB migration [4]. On the other hand, there is little experimental evidence on natural, polycrystalline materials that show these effects. Here, we focus on a grain-by-grain analysis of these effects in polycrystals
