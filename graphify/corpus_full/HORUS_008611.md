# POPULATION

The statistical models that relate mortality rates to the variables age, period and birth cohort (known as APC models) are frequently used in epidemiological and social studies. This study aims to evaluate from the mortality data in Colombia, from a specific disease, the effect of these three predictor variables on these mortality rates. The models of Poisson and Binomial Negative regression with which mortality rates are obtained are proposed, then the adjustment will be made to each model using the Gamma Inversa and SBeta2 distributions as a priori distributions for the parameters of variance of temporal effects age, period and cohort. Comparisons are made between the models through the Deviance Information Criterion (DIC), Finally they are evaluated by calculating the Middle Absolute Percentual Error (MAPE) between the real and predictive values for the last period, which will not be taken into account in the model adjustments
