# ANALYSIS PARAMETERS STANDARDIZATION TO EVALUATE THE AROMATIC PROFILE OF COLOMBIAN COFFEE OF ADISTANT AREAS AND EPOSES OF POSSESSES, USING ELECTRONIC NARIZ AND MULTI-TI-TERM STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

The aromatic profile using electronic nose, physicochemical and sensory analysis were used to classify three Colombian coffees with origin in different areas of Colombia (Huila, Caldas and Santa Marta (Sierra Nevada)) roasted in grain and ground roast, of two harvests (main harvest and mitaca), this last classification for Huila samples and at two moments of collection in samples of Santa Marta. For the standardization of parameters of operation in electronic nose, a 32 factorial design was used with two factors, three levels and management techniques, obtaining gas flow: 150 mL/min and 10 min incubation time for commercial electronic nose of 10 sensors. With these parameters 10 coffee samples were analyzed, which were able to be classified by their aromatic profile through PCA and classification methods. The results show that the electronic nose can be a useful tool for differentiating samples by regions, type and coffee harvest. The results obtained were compared with the physiochemical and sensory results
