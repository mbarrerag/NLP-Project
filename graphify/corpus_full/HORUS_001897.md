# Birds of the Pacific in the Century of Colombian Biological Collections, 1913–1999

This is the story of how the bodies of thousands of birds from the Colombian Pacific retained the trace of the relationship of Colombian societies of the 20th century with nature. This work explores five collections of birds that emerged throughout that century in Bogotá, Popayán and Cali. Due to the variety of sources, an methodology is proposed to interpret biological collections as historical archives within the field of environmental history, in order to construct historical narratives about the socio-cultural and political impacts of birds from the Colombian Pacific.
