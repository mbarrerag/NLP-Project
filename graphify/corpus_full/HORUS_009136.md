# DESIGN OF SAMPLING AND PHYSICAL CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION FOR THE DETERMINATION OF XENOBIOTIC COMPOUNDS IN PIÑA (ANANÁS COMOSUS (L.) MERR) COMMERCIALIZED IN THE CITY OF SANTIAGO DE CALI.

For more than a decade, pineapple has been counted among agricultural products with export potential. Starting in 2017, in the department of Valle del Cauca has been implemented the Integral Plan of Fruit Farming, whose implementation has focused on working on strengthening 9 productive chains, among them that of pineapple. This Project gives to know the physical and chemical results obtained from the sampling of pineapples marketed in the city of Santiago de Cali, collected at different points of sale, for a subsequent study that will evaluate the presence of residual pesticides in the pulp of pineapple. Ineffective control of this parameter limits the incursion of small and medium producers to the international market due to its negative effects on health and the environment. The determination of the physical and chemical parameters of the fruit was carried out according to the norm NTC 729-1 for fresh pineapple fruits, as well as the guidelines stipulated in the Codex Alimentarius values, while the results of CXS 182-1993 for pineapples.
