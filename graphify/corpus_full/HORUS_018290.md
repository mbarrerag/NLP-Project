# DIVERSITY OF EPYPHITE HEPATICS (MARCHANTIOPHYTA) FROM THE SUSASIE SECTOR IN THE NATURAL CHINGAZA NATIONAL PARK

The briophytes form a conspicuous group of the flora of the planet. Their ecological contribution in terms of species richness and coverage in many habitats is fundamental for the balance of ecosystem services. In Colombia there is a record of approximately 715 species of Marchantiophyta (hepatic), which present their greatest richness in the Andean region. The study was carried out in the Chingaza National Natural Park - Suasie sector given its ecological and hydric importance for the surrounding municipalities. The main objective of this study was to determine the diversity of the liver community in the Suasie sector of the PNN Chingaza. To this, three elevations were made in transects of 2x50 m in an altitudinal gradient, in each transect the livers present in four forophytes selected at random. The samples collected were identified for the analysis of composition and diversity of the community.
