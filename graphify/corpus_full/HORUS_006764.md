# Humor, laughter and language in the classroom: study of epipragmatic and metapragmatic skills in preschool children of IED Republic of Panama College

The research aims to describe and analyze the study of the development of epipragmatic and metapragmatic skills of preschool children of IED Colegio República de Panamá, through the pedagogical strategy mediated by humor. The research methodology has an exploratory scope and is situated from the constructivist paradigm, with a mixed non-experimental approach. The theoretical review of the study takes into account the humor and metapragmatic development at the linguistic, psychological, social and pedagogical level, recognizing the phenomenon of presupposition as metapragmatic and communicative skill with respect to manifestations from the communicative role of speech, listening and context. The results indicate that the development of epipragmatic and metapragmatic skills mediated by humor, are evidenced in the preschool stage and are significantly increased according to the age from the epigmatic and metaplastic approach and the strategy of the children. videnced in the preschool stage and incredible significantly agreeing to age, children's training, significant experiences, and context
