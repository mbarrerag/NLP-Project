# Valorization of used cooking oil in biorefineries - Epoxidation using exchange resins as catalyst

During the fritting, vegetable oils and fats degrade it becoming used cooking oils (ACUs) non-edible, toxic and problematic. Only in the city of Bogotá, are generated about 45000 t/year of ACUs that are poured inadequately into sifons or solid waste.In both cases there is a series of social, environmental, economic and public health problems. Currently the ACUs are collected and used in the production of biofuels (i.e. biodiesel, HVO) under highly subsidized schemes, which represents a risk to the sustainability of the collection chain. In addition, the use of biodiesel is in decline due to the prohibition of diesel engines in some cities and the increasing electro-reflection of the vehicle fleet. As an alternative, the use of ACUs as a raw material in biorefinerías is proposed to be used to produce higher added value products, in particular biopolymers. It has been demonstrated that ACUs can be used for the production of epoxidesides oleochemicals. This model will be implemented in a process simulation software to determine the best operating conditions and to evaluate the technical and economic feasibility of the process in a preliminary way. The results of the project could favor the sustainability of the cities by making the use of waste through the creation of the process, reducing their environmental impact.
