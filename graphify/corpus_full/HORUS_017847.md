# VACUNATION PROCESS AGAINST CORONAVIRUS DISEASE (COVID-19) FROM A HISTORICAL-TERRITORIAL CRITICAL PERSPECTIVE

The text analyses, in a comparative way and using the critical and historical-territorial approach, what happened at a second moment of the Covid-19 pandemic in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, when vaccination processes begin in those countries. The problem of the existing social inequality in health is highlighted. To give a broad view of the issue, the text analyzes, initially, the development of capitalism and the biotechnology industry, and then stops in the study of vaccination processes, in its historical and territorial articulation
