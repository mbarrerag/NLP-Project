# COST OF INSURANCE CARE

Advanced Health Care is a collection of monographs aimed at health professionals and undergraduate, master and doctoral students within the field of health sciences. The first edition of monographs was published in 2014 and seven years later, the result of the exion reflection and the interest of readers, we launched a second edition with some new features. In this second edition we reduce the number of monographs regarding the previous edition to try to take on the changes that, in recent years, have taken place both in health systems and in the educational ones. The collection of 2021 is focused on the key elements for research development in health, as are the tools for research collected in the evidence-based monographs and Statistics applied to the health sciences, in which the authors provide updates of interest regarding the previous edition. Likewise, they include in the collection updated knowledge on clinical management with a cross-cutting approach, which seek to cover training requirements at all levels of higher education.
