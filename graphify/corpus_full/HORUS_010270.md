# BOREOUT AND BURRITANCE IN WORK: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW EXPLORATORY.

With the 4th industrial revolution, a trend is emerging linked to the development and use of new information and communication technologies, as well as the immersion of artificial intelligence (AI) within the work environment. This reality brings with it a series of opportunities and the reduction of some problems in the work contexts. However, there is also an opening up to discussion on topics such as the closing of some jobs, new forms of hiring, the appearance of personal jobs and the separation of the work-family areas (Foladori & Ortiz-Espinoza, 2022). H0: Boreout syndrome and boredom at work stem from monotonous and repetitive work environments lacking cognitive stimulation, low levels of demand, and low growth at the personal and work levels
