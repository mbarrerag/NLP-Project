# Evaluation of the use of InSAR techniques for the monitoring and detection of mass movements in an early warning system in the Colombian Andes

In this work we evaluate the use of techniques of Interferimetric Colombia of Synthetic Open Radar, (InSAR, for its initials in English) for the detection of mass movements in tropical mountain environments, specifically in the Colombian Andes. Furthermore, we propose a methodology for the integration of these techniques into a system of early warnings in urban-suburban areas taking as study area the Valley of Aburrá, Colombia. The document is structured in four scientific articles independent of each other, which will be potentially subject to publication. Article 1 presents the theoretical framework for the application of InSAR techniques in tropical mountain environments. This first article seeks to contribute to the knowledge of InSAR to the Spanish literature. Article 2 addresses the successful application of InSAR at regional level and the detection of multiple zones of deformation of the terrain, associated to the displacements in the study area. Article 3 presents the conceptual limitations of the field and instrumentation in a case of study in the Valley of Aburrá, where InSAR is applied due to a mass movement that has caused serious effects since 2018, finding that the zone of formation exceeds more than ten times the area initially defined with field and instrumentation.
