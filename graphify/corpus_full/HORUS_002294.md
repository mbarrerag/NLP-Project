# Hypercurpx

HIPERCUERPX is a video-performance experience that presents in an immersive way the exploration of migration from dissidence, redefining the perception of the body as territory. This project exposes how identity is deconstructed and reconfigured in front of binary dynamics and imposition. Through the synchronization, distortion and connection between body and sound, HIPERCUERPX builds a space of enunciation for the experiences of migrants marikas, southerners, Andean and irreverent. The installation experiments with the programming language by nodes, projecting and fragmenting the photographic image to visually distort the bodies. These visual bodies actively react to the voices and sonorities of dissident artists who transit migration from the south of the country, seeking new forms of gender and identity enunciation. The fragmentation of the photographic image allows disarticular pre-established narratives, presenting marginalized bodies that are reconfigured in an act of mass emancipation. HIPERCUERPX turns the body into a challenging territorial, questioning the internal body and exposing the sexual aspect through the encoded projection of the encoded process of the phenomenon
