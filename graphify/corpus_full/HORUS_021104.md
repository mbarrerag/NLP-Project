# Democratic crisis in the department of Arauca: a study on the infiltration of armed actors in the governorate of Arauca, between 2001 and 2021.

The department of Arauca is a territory that historically has been marginalised by the Colombian State what, thanks to its border status and its wealth in terms of fossil resources, has become during the last decades, a scenario of irregular political and social events that has triggered in a democratic instability due to the proliferation of different illegal groups of varied nature, such as the National Liberation Army (ELN), the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia - People's Army (FARC-EP) and the Concessional Block of Arauca (BVA). Thus, due to the state abandonment that presents the region, the armed groups installed in this area of the country have taken over much of the legal and illegal businesses present in the interior of the department and, also, have been introduced into the democratic mechanisms and electoral processes of the region, deriving the influence of these groups in the electoral and governance processes, generating a political crisis that brings more to the electoral process and that has become more noticeable during the first decade of the twenty-first century and that is maintained until the present. This is evidenced, since the election and dismissal of the governor Federico Gallardo in 2001, where, for the first time, the electoral process, was the electoral. For this purpose, the central axis of the investigation will be developed from the study of three specific periods in the history of the department of Arauca: the first one, dates from 2001 to 2003, where research will be carried out on the first infiltration of the FARC-EP and ELN guerrilla groups in the governorate; the second one, the period from 2004 to 2011, which aims to study the influence that the BVA maintained on the political decisions that were taken in the region; and the third, the period between 2012 and 2021, where it was discussed a _x0093_restablished democracy_x0094_ in the department, a term that is undermined when the two governors who were present during this period were captured and dismissed in 2021 due to alleged links with the ELN.
