# The scrap war and its impact on the supply chain in the Colombian steel sector

This research analyzes the supply chain/supply of the Colombian steel sector, starting with the processes of obtaining steel, as is the basic oxygen process which uses as raw material iron mineral and the process of electric arc furnace that uses scrap as input, are reported the steelworks with presence in the Country, Gerdau Diaco, Grupo Siderúrgico Reyna, SIDOC S.A.S, Ternium and Acerías Paz del Río. The latter is the only steel that obtains steel through the two processes mentioned, while the others only use scrap as main raw material. Likewise, three main links are identified within the supply network of the sector: raw materials suppliers, steel sector and final customer and through its study is recognized the first link as a critical factor, already this generates the so-called “spinware utility” that makes reference to the steel industry to ensure a scrap inventory, besides that the scrap metal represents a valuable strategy in terms of circular economy and promotes sustainability, efficiency and environmental responsibility in the industrial sector. while the others rely soly on scrap as their main raw material. Additionally, three main links within the supply chain are identified: raw material suppliers, the steel sector, and end customers. The study collects the first link as a critical factor, as it generates the so-called “scrap war,” referring to the intense competition among steel mills to secure a scrap inventory. Futuremore, scrap represents a valuable strategy in terms of circular economy, as it promotes sustainability, efficiency, and environmental responsibility within the industrial sector.
