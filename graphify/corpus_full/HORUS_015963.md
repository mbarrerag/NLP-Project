# Hybrid concrete beam drawer bridges with lattice walls.

The concrete bridges of the segmentals of the drawer beam are one of the most used solutions in the engineering of bridges due to their multiple advantages over other typologies, among which are its constructive ease, economy and aesthetic appeal. In this paper a bibliographic review was carried out and the design of 16 segmental bridges of the drawer made in Colombia was analyzed, in order to compile aids for the pedrimensionamento of its elements and obtain reference tenets that allow to estimate preliminary the quantities required by them. Next, the possible reduction of mass and presuppression steel obtained by replacing a conventional section with a hybrid ( concrete slabs and walls formed by tubular profiles of steel), which is one of the solutions used throughout the world to optimize the bridges of the drawer beam. As a result, reductions between 10% and 22% of the flector moment are obtained in the face of the support for the isostatic phase, between 13% and 25% of the total mass of the bridge and between 20% and 30% of the steel of the presuppression required in the isostatic phase
