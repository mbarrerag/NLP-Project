# Development of scientific skills based on the teaching strategy of problem-based learning

This paper was aimed at strengthening the scientific competencies of natural sciences that correspond to the comprehensive use of scientific knowledge, research and the explanation of phenomena. The study was carried out with students of the 6th grade of the Redemptorist Seminar College. Initially, a diagnostic test was applied which allowed to evaluate the level of competences. Subsequently, a didactic unit was designed under the approach of problem-based learning, with the themes of ecosystems, ecosystem dynamics and environmental pollution factors, considered by the Ministry of National Education in Basic Standards of Competences and Basic Learning Rights. As a result of the work, a change in the state of the competencies evaluated can be evidenced, reflecting a positive impact on the students . The present work was induced to strengthen the scientific competence, in the Basque School
