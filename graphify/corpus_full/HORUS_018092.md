# OPTISMATION OF THE PATOLOGY DIAGNOSTIC METHODOLOGY OF DISARMAMENTED HUMEDIES IN THE STUDY OF UNIVERSITY BUILDINGS IN BOGOTA.

The pathology of construction operates with universal methodologies with three distinct phases towards the diagnosis that leads to an intervention. By defining the deteriorations that are presented in the building and collecting the corresponding information, the route of diagnosis, from field work to the formulation of the intervention, depends on the criteria and procedures used, which often does not allow to carry out an integral diagnosis that is actually used in the process. This makes it difficult to integrate projects and to remove them from structured and systematic environments such as the BIM (building information modelling). Based on the development and optimization of a diagnostic methodology developed in the pathological studies of buildings of the National University of Colombia, the process was structured through the minimum unit, which are the mechanisms of damage, collecting and managing from the beginning the information and inputs that will allow to carry the process to the most advanced project phases. The method presented, applied to the construction moistures, whose complexity is known, allows to guide the diagnosis in its execution or its teaching, and also to address the need to integrate in a parametric way to a BIM environment
