# SHINY APPLICATION - PROFILES OF VERISMILITUD

This project proposes a fundamental tool for statistical inference, the profiles of verisimilitude, which allow to calculate an unknown parameter. An interactive application in R Shiny will be developed, based on techniques used in the literature of statistical inference, with the aim of facilitating the understanding and importance of the profiles of verisimilitude for students of statistics and research. The tool focuses on helping students to acquire practical skills in the construction and interpretation of the profiles of verisimilitude, as well as in its use to select statistical models and understand the results
