# Emissions of air pollutants by road transport in Colombia.

The purpose of this research is to develop a conceptual and applied approach to quantify and project emissions of atmospheric pollutants generated by land road transport in Colombia. A methodological route is constructed that allows the estimation of emissions from the kilometers traveled annually by vehicles (web portals of vehicular sales and technical-mechanical reviews), the emission factors of Computer Programme to calculate Emissions from Road Transport (COPERT) and the number of active vehicles per department. The methodological approach for the projection of emissions is supported by the growth of the fleet according to the demographic and economic dynamics of the country; the activity and emission factors are considered constant (base year values). The methodological approach was applied for the construction of the inventory of national emissions by road transport by base year 2019 and for its projection in the period 2020-2050.
