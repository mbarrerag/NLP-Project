# Development of a model of digital transformation of geographical data through Information and Communications Technologies (ICTs): Case of use: Group of Geological and Geomorphological Cartography and Research of the Colombian Geological Service (SGC)

Currently, the Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs) are immersed in a broad technological, social and economic context that has enhanced their use in research processes and in the development of digital transformation projects. In this sense, it is crucial that organizations optimize their methods, workflows, data management and process automation through solutions based on digital technology. At the same time, there is an increase in the importance of geographic data in the operations of the organizations, but lacks orientation for their digital transformation. This research responds to this need by presenting the Geo-TD model, a specific digital transformation approach for geographic data using ICTs. It is applied in one case only: the Geological and Geomorphological Cartography and Research Group of the Colombian Geological Service (SGC). Geo-TD is based on measuring the level of digital maturity in four key dimensions: digital competence, data management, digital processes and technology. The approach includes an in-depth architecture to select technological components in the construction of a validation platform.
