# Study of mass movements detonated by the earthquake of Quetame (Cund.) May 2008

This document develops the study of mass movements detonated by the earthquake of Quetame, Cundinamarca, which occurred in May 2008. In the study carried out, a methodology was developed to map and analyze the geomorphological traits and the morphometric properties of the movements detonated by the earthquake, and to differentiate the existing movements prior to the earthquake. Both the regional context where the earthquake occurred, how the geological materials involved, the precipitation, the geological structures and the previous seismicity of the study area were analyzed. In the study area it was found that the vast majority of the mass movements detoned were of the type of detritus flows, and in the lesser number of mud flows (“debris flows” and “mudflows” according to the classification of mass movements of Varnes, 30° and 40° with respect to the study area, 1996), and the study area did not show a preferential direction of movements relative to the epicenter of the earthquake. Most of the hills in which occurred the triggered landslides had a preferred gap ranking between 30° and 40° to the horizontal.
