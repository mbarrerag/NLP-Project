# The study of precious stones as a didactic strategy for teaching the chemical bond and molecular structure in families of inorganic oxides

This research is focused on the teaching processes of Natural Sciences; especially in the area of Chemistry, in the final high school grade; created from the Pedagogical Theory of Conceptual Fields by Gérard Vergnaud and providing teaching strategies for the understanding of the concept of chemical bonding in the process of oxidereduction in basic inorganic oxides through gemstones (mineralogy). This process was developed through a qualitative methodology based on meaningful experiences allowing learning in chemistry
