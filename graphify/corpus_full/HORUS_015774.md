# INSURANCE USE OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS IN CARE: RELIABILITY AROL-M INSTRUMENT

The role of the caregiver of people with chronic diseases, polymedicated and in a situation of dependence, is largely assumed by a family member, who sees himself in the need to develop various skills that allow him to respond to the tasks for which he is not prepared, such as the use of medications. In view of this reality, caregivers turn to organizational strategies that impact the meaning of care in their lives, so that each care action taken with them becomes determining (1). In adopting the role of caregiver, a great instrumental responsibility is generated, which demands a series of knowledge and skills seeking the greatest benefit to the person cared for. Using medicines is increasingly frequent, however all the requirements and factors necessary to carry out increasingly safe actions is little described in the Colombian context(2), and since there is not found in the literature an instrument that allows to know the important role of this style focused only on caregivers and their relationship with the burden of care, an investigation was generated to design and validate a tool that evaluates the level of work, organization and responses in the use of medicines in the home by caregivers. Next, the third phase of the research is presented, with the objective of the help of the evaluation of the intervention, with the aim of the evaluation of the intervention of internal medicine
