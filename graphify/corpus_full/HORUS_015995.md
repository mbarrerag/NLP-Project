# Thermodynamic and metabolic analysis of a biological system insect cells Spodoptera frugiperda (sf9) and its infection with a recombinant autographa californica nucleopolyhedrovirus (BvGCN4-NA1).

This paper addresses the analysis of the production of recombinant proteins with bio-pharmaceutical interest through the technology of expression in insect cells through infection with recombinant baculovirus. This is a widely used strategy that still has challenges in design, modelling and understanding (Monteiro, 2015). First, the phase of cell growth is analyzed under a thermodynamic perspective of black box, finding that its modeling is possible through a methodology, mainly aimed at microorganisms, which can be adapted to eukaryotes with special consideration in the definition and differentiation of the source of energy, carbon and nitrogen. In particular, when considering in the analysis a source of organic nitrogen, which induces an overflow of electrons that can end in biomass or in carbonated sub-pods as lactate. Although it was not possible to reproduce the scenarios posed in a metabolic model, by means of metabolic analysis the importance of considering the routes of assimilation of nitrogen (organic and inorganic) and the use of statistical methods as a tool for the improvement of these processes was identified as the event of the disease, it was found to be based on the analysis of characteristic times derived from the disease and the diseased virus
