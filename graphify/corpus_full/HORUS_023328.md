# Perception of the student in using a digital educational resource for teaching peripheric venereal catheterism

Health sciences are frequently incorporating Information and Communication Technologies, to teach concepts related to care, safety and quality care, through the construction of Digital Educational Resources (RED), characterized by their educational intent that aim at specific objectives and their design responds to appropriate didactic characteristics for learning content, acquiring procedural skills and promoting attitudes and/or values (1). The research group developed a Digital Educational Resource (RED) for a procedure commonly used in health institutions - peripheral venous catheterization (VPC)-, based on the model of instructional design ADDIE, (2.3) (context analysis, resource design, development, implementation and evaluation). To know and interpret the meanings assigned to the student when using the RED for learning of the CVP and to identify what types of learnings were achieved with this resource. The study was carried out in the central half of the population with an interpretative qualitative approach, which analyzed the interaction of the student of III semester of nursing with the RED, for teaching and learning of the CVP 2019. At the disposal of students at any time for their consultation, this facilitates a focused review and deepening of concepts and issues that have been programmed by the teacher and that are relevant to the participant(1.4). The RED had as its center of the teaching-learning process the student, evidenced in the responsibility, autonomy and self-learning acquired. Some authors point out that the development of content using ICTs is generating new educational paradigms, focused on the student, the management of knowledge and self-learning (1,4,5); this allows to move from a unidirectional model of training focused on the teacher or the content, to open and flexible models, where information located in large databases, tends to be shared, raising in the student, their level of motivation and their ability to search for solutions to the proposed problems(6). Limitations: The creation of RED requires the support of the respective directions of pedagogical innovation, and the support of ICT professionals for the design and production of audiovisual material
