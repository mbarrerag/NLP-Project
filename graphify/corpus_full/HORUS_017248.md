# Estimate of the number of motor units of the short thumb abductor by means of the Composite Muscle Action Potential (MScanFit) scan method in healthy adults: reference values.

The exploration of the MScanFit PAMC is a novel MUNE method that quantifies the number of motor units, is reproducible, sensitive to change, precise and takes a short time. to estimate the number of motor units in the short abductor muscle of the thumb, using the MScanFit method in healthy adult people of Colombia. records were made in the short abductor of the thumb in healthy individuals, the normality of the distribution of the data was determined with the technique D'Agostino & Pearson. For the presentation of the descriptive statistic, minimum values, maximum, median, interquartile range, 3 and 97 percentiles were calculated. In total 109 individuals were evaluated, the median age was 29 years (minimum=18, maximum=49); the MUNE value was (median: 103 UM) in the ABS, with an error (median: 7.4%) and a percentage of maximum step: 17% (median: 3.3%). Correlations of the age were found against the number of UM and step % without statistical significance. The first unit of the Colombian population is the population. Conclusion
