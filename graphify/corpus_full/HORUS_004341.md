# Monetary poverty and female informal labour in Colombia, a problem of endogeneity? Study 2020 - 2021

What are the determinants for a woman in Colombia to be informal and have informal employment? Why are some women who have certain common characteristics, such as being head of household and belonging to the lower strata the most affected by the informality of employment? This paper sought to answer these questions by means of an analysis of the database of what the people employed in the Great Household Survey (GEIH) respond to. To this end, the probability of being informal and being poor was calculated, when women are in Colombia and a model was built to estimate whether the relationship between these variables is strong. The results indicate that the probability of being informal and being poor when women is increased, but also when other variables are considered as level of education, age, head of household and level of income. Even taking into account these explanatory factors in the model, poverty and informality are influenced by generating a problem of dual to bring about and possible endogeneity. .
