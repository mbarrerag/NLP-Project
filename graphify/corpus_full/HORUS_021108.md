# From Medellín that you walk to that you want to walk: An analysis of the scenario that motivates citizens to walk along three routes to the Medellín Metro.

Today’s cities have a very big challenge at the front, with a planning and re-construction that involves a resilience to the climate crisis that can put at risk the well-being of citizens. Likewise, mobility is a factor that can be limiting in the search for sustainability, since the fleet contributes to environmental problems related to air, noise and temperature, so it is necessary to evaluate alternatives how the walkability, consisting of the conditions of the environment how comfort, landscape or safety and that encourage the action of walking. However, the construction of a more walkable city remains a task of learning, since the present physical space and the success of its use implies knowing the context and perspectives that generate novelty and taste in the users that make the city more accessible. That is why in this work it is wanted to analyze the effective use of the space in three access routes that lead to three stations of the metro of Medellín, observing variables related to the behavior of the people, the elements with which interact and then confront them with the wishes of the citizens inscribed from the surveys.
