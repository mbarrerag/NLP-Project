# Characterization of banana-producing agrosystems (Musa AAB) in the cantons Santo Domingo and El Carmen, Ecuador

ABSTRACT The research was carried out in Ecuador in the cantons Santo Domingo de los Colorados and El Carmen, belonging to the provinces of Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas and Manabí respectively. The aim was to identify the agricultural systems of banana production (Musa AAB) and characterize them according to their components. A survey was applied to the producers where qualitative data were obtained and evaluated with a multivariate analysis of multiple correspondence (MCA), as well as a study of conglomerates that allowed grouping the production systems by intragroup affinity and intergroup differences. 5 indicators representative of the agrosystems and two types of production were obtained as a result: one dedicated exclusively to banana monoculture and the other in association with crops, with the greatest frequency combined with fruit and forest perennial species
