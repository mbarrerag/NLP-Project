# Effect of therapeutic exercise on the variability of heart rate in patients with coronary disease.

To evaluate the effect of therapeutic exercise used in a cardiac rehabilitation program on the short-term heart rate variability (HRV) of patients with coronary disease (EC). Analytical observational study. 24 patients were included and 10 completed discharge data (60.1 ± 7.1 years). The RR intervals were recorded at rest with a Polar H7 monitor over a period of 15 minutes. Short-term HRV indices in time domain (DT), frequency (DF), non-linear (NL) and autonomic indices were calculated in the software Kubios HRV. Bayesian inference was performed to verify the effect of therapeutic exercise and correlation between HRV indices. The initial analysis, at the end of the rehabilitation program, showed decrease of the HRV associated with a sympathetic - vagal positive balance, with increased sympathetic activity. No difference of the HRV according to sex, myocardial infarction or smoking was observed. The subsequent analysis, at the end of the rehabilitation program, showed decrease of the patient activity parasympathetic, decrease of the sympathetic exercise and recovery of the AVV.
