# Determination of damage caused by phytophagous insects and their taxonomic identification in Passiflora foetida (L).

Passiflora foetida is a plant very little studied in Colombia because it does not cause any economic impact in this country. In contrast it occurs in regions where it is registered as an invasive plant as is the case of Australia, where the environmental and governmental authorities have the intention to carry out a classic biological control with natural enemies of this species, among which they consider the phytophages insects. Due to this it is important to carry out research on insect-plant interaction in countries where the plant is native as in Colombia, where it is widely distributed and where it is important to specify the effect of the phytophages insects that can be considered as potential for biological control, under natural conditions. This thesis was developed by chapters. Chapter 1, includes taxonomic identification of insects that interact with the plant and recording the damage.
