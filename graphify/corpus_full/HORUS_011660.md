# Street, Corps = Vogue, teacher in transgression

summary calle cuerpo and vogue. A PROFYMESOR IN TRASSGRESS It aims to make a critical reflection in relation to the experience lived through dialogue and pedagogical knowledge as an important topic within the educational and personal field; seeking to make catharsis from the vision of a teacher and his own perspective permeating the reader with the events counted from his own perspective, where the street is described as context and place creator of knowledge, the body as temple and instrument accountant of experiences and the Vogue as a transgressor element of movements and thoughts. The concept street is the main axis of this work since it is the place where these knowledges are collected, from this same one, the perspective of a subject is worked being part of experiences and as the being and the mind solidify this ideology to become something personal by means of the body as an instrument counter of events. This narrative is focused on an analysis of monographic reflection of compilation where the objective is to generate a critique of different points of view through the dance and the body as a transforming element of minds.
