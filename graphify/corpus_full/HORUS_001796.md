# Evidence-based clinical standard: evaluation, intervention, risk stratification and follow-up of patients who enter the cardiac rehabilitation program of the Hospital Universidad Nacional de Colombia

To establish recommendations based on evidence for evaluation, intervention, risk stratification and follow-up of patients entering the National cardiac rehabilitation program of the National University Hospital of Colombia. The method for establishing the clinical standard based on evidence (ECBE) in the cardiac rehabilitation program of the National University Hospital of Colombia will be based on updated scientific information of the international literature. The process consists of seven components: Conformation of the developer team-Definition of the scope and objectives of the ECBE. - Systematic review of clinical practice guides (GPC). -Elaboration of the preliminary algorithm. -Development of an interdisciplinary agreement. -Conclusion and edition of the final algorithm. -Review of the ECBE. The recommendations for care in phase I are constructed with the Clinical Standard Based on the evidence: Evaluation, intervention, risk stratification and follow-up of patients entering the cardiac rehabilitation programme of the National University Hospital of Colombia. - Systematic review of clinical practice guidelines (GPC). - Development of the preliminary and algorithm. - Development of an interdisciplinary and agreement. - Development of the final algorithm. - Review and editing of the ECBE. Recommendations for phase I care are built using the Evidence-Based Clinical Standard: evaluation, intervention, risk strategy, and follow-up for patients entering the cardiac rehabilitation program at the National University Hospital of Colombia. ConclusionsThis work established clear, evidence-based guidelines for evaluating, intervention, risk-strategy, and monitoring patients in Phase I of the cardiac rehabilitation programme at the National University Hospital of Colombia.
