1. Introduction and objectives The isotopic exchange H/D presents significant importance in different fields of science at present.1 Recent results show that the metallic nanoparticles (MNPs) are efficient nanocatalizers for this reaction through the activation of C-H bonds, especially those of rutenium and iridium, since the first deutera efficiently links type C(sp3)-H but with reduced aromatic rings while the second is able to deuterate different substrates without presenting effects.2,3 2.
