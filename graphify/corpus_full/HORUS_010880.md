# ELECTRIC MOBILITY

The transport sector has historically mediated the economic development of nations and the exchange of goods and services. However, it is the world’s largest consumer of petroleum products (IEA, 2019), responsible for 24 % of direct greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions worldwide, resulting from hydrocarbon combustion (IEA, 2020) and for 10 % of total anthropogenic emissions of PM10 and PM2.5 (IEA, 2018)
