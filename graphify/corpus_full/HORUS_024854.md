# Search for LXR agonists in Colombian plants with therapeutic potential for Alzheimer's disease.

Alzheimer’s disease is the most common dementia in the world population, has an unknown etiology and does not have an effective treatment. It is characterized by the aggregation of the amyloid peptide β and Tau hyperphosphorylated, increase of free radicals, lipid deregulation and exitotoxicity. Considering this, hepatic X receptors, are postulated as a therapeutic target, since they are transcription factors that regulate genes responsible for cholesterol homeostasis and are associated with mechanisms of elimination of the amyloid β peptide, a lower phosphorylation of Tau and a lower inflammatory response, etc. In this work, 82 extracts of Colombian basal Angiospermas were counted, to which their agonist activity was evaluated on the nuclear receptors LXR β, the inhibitor activity of acetil collinesterase.
