# Assessment of the technical/economic feasibility of using nano-fluid to mitigate damage by condensate bank, in the Colombian clay mill.

The condensate bank (BC) is one of the most common sources of formation damage in gas-condensed deposits. One of the most used strategies to remedy this damage is the mobilization of condensate using solvents or chemicals. However, the greatest disadvantage of these treatments is the limited durability they offer. Recently, they have been designed and tested at nanofluid laboratory scale to increase bank mobility and enhance the effect of durability of treatment. Despite the good laboratory results, the implementation of these treatments has been limited by the uncertainty that has to be taken to estimate the performance at field scale. In this document, a methodology to simulate the potential benefits that could be generated by implementing a nanofluid base treatment to mobilize and inhibit the BC. As a case study, a gas-condensed well of the Colombian flatbed was taken to evaluate the Colombian soil and nanofluid used by the high-flow process in the French et al. (2018). To simulate the flow of fluids and the profile of the BC, a numerical model was used.
