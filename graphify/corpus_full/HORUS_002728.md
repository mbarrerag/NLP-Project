# MOLECULARY TOOLS FOR THE DETECTION OF MYCOBACTERIUM BOVIS, GENES OF ANTIBIOTICAL RESISTANCE AND MICROBIOTA OF MILK AND CHEESE OF BOVINOS OF THE REGION OF SUMAPAZ, COLOMBIA

The bovine tuberculosis (TBB), produced by Mycobacterium bovis, is a zoonosis of high prevalence, which produces significant risks to animals, livestock and consumers, as well as significant economic losses [1].This type of disease can generate high losses at the level of the livestock sector, both at the level of meat and milk production.The dairy industry is one of the main economic activities in Colombia; however, bad hygienic practices cause contamination of products, together with the indiscriminate use of antibiotics for economic purposes [2, 3].In this work, the microbiota associated with raw milk or derivatives samples, obtained from small livestock production centers with suspected cases of TBB and other potential pathogens, in small production centers of the Sumapaz Region (Fusagasuga, Colombia) associated genes showed the total amplification of M. bovisitàre, was initially standardized for the total DNA of milk and cheese based on extraction with phenol-chloroform and silica columns. The results obtained suggest problems in hygiene practices affecting the safety of milk and its derivatives in small production centers in the region of Sumapaz, Colombia
