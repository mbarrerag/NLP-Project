# History of the Department of Sociology of the National University of Colombia

Long before the terms such as epistemologies of the south and decolonial perspectives were in vogue, before that in the nineties thinkers like Aníbal Quijano spoke about the coloniality of knowledge and the westernizing character of sociology, already in the 1950s and sixties of the last century had thought about and thinkers in different parts of Latin America seeking a sociological thought according to the conditions of the Latin American society. Between 1955 and 1960, the first departments of sociology were created in Buenos Aires, Bogotá, Caracas, Ciudad de México, Lima and Santiago de Chile. Among these pioneers, Orlando Fals Borda and Camilo Torres are nowadays heritage of Latin American sociology, but little is known about what happened with the Department of Sociology that founded in the National University of Colombia in 1959. This volume is the result of a tracing in physical archives — both in the University and those who kept in their homes and offices professors and students that have transited by that department — and incorporates various testimonies that allow a close to the foundation projects, the relations and tensions in the first decade of the Department
