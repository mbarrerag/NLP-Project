# Evaluation of a plant extract of Zanthoxylum sp with LXR agonist activity as a potential myelin protective agent in the cuprizone demyelination model

Demyelinating diseases constitute a heterogeneous set of disorders affecting the nervous system. They are disabling diseases that affect millions of people around the world, highlighting multiple sclerosis as the one with the highest prevalence and socioeconomic impact. The main characteristic of this group of pathologies is the loss and dysfunction of myelin, consequence of cell death of myelinating cells, generating the need to find therapeutic alternatives for the protection of this cell lineage. In this context, the pharmacological activation of the hepatic X nuclear receptor (LXR) is presented as a promising alternative, these proteins are transcription factors activated by small ligands, with functions in the homeostasis of lipids and associated with the process of myelinization in physiological and pathological conditions. Its pharmacological regulation, has shown to reduce the demyelinization and promote remyelinization in live models. In this project, the therapeutic potential of a plant extract and a mixture of fractions enriched in LXR molecules derived from a plant species of the genus Zanthoxylum.
