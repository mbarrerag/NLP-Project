# Analysis of the delimitation of moors in Colombia: The case of the Pisba Paros Complex

The general objective of this final work is to analyze the delimitation of the moors in the context of the strategies of economic development of Colombia, for the case of the complex of Pisba moors. To this, we analyze two main elements: 1) the assessment of impacts generated by the economic activities that are currently carried out in the complex of Pisba moors, applying a methodology based on the theory of complex networks; 2) the main conflicts of use and tenure, with their respective incidences in the changes of natural coverage, through the bibliographic review directed to identify the current uses (activities developed by the human being for the use of the soil resource) and the uses allowed according to the environmental determinants. The above, allows to propose at the end of the document some guidelines for the joint environmental management between the State and the communities in the areas of moors. It is concluded that the delimitation of moors, in itself, will not achieve the protection and conservation of these strategic ecosystems, since the past and present realities of can not guarantee the effectiveness of the conservation strategies if it does not involve in all the stages of the process to which inhabit the ecosystem, allowing the legal functions and their most urgent needs, and the most urgent, since the situation, since the ir respective incidents in the natural coverages changes, through bibliographic review oriented to identify the current and legal uses laid in the environmental determinants. This information allows to focus, at the final section of this document some guidelines to environmental management joint state and communities in paramos areas.
