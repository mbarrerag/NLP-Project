# Comparative study of Ni-Co catalysts formed (pellets, ceramic monolith and metallic monolith) for the production of hydrogen from the OSRE process

The urgent need for humanity to address climate change is the great challenge of the energy transition. Indeed, the world faces one of its greatest challenges for the 21st century, to supply the energy basket by implementing renewable energy sources that are able to contribute to the transition that seeks to leave dependence on fossil energy sources. An energy vector like hydrogen stands out as a promising alternative to contribute significantly to the energy demand. To have processes of obtaining hydrogen viable environmental and economically manages to incentivize the scientific and technological field of the country facilitating the path towards sustainable and autonomous development.
