# Malaria case modelling in the Ashanti-Ghana region using logistic regression, Machine Learning and Fisher linear discriminant

Globally, significant advances have been made in reducing malaria cases; however, the disease remains a challenging problem in Ghana’s public health. Approximately 40 to 60% of people are hospitalized due to this disease. Despite this, the examination by using the microscope remains the best method worldwide to determine whether a patient has the malaria parasite. This study sought to determine whether haematological parameters, age and gender of patients could be used to predict malaria by using logistic regression models, naive Bayes, linear discriminant analysis of Fisher and K closest neighbours.
