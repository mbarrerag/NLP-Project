# DIVERSAL FAMILIES: COMPLEXITIES OF EROTIC-AFFECTIVE RELATIONS

On November 25, 2020, I presented the main findings of my research since 2017 in the educational field with high school students who claimed to be LGBT diversity and were linked to public schools in the city of Bogotá. The research was called: Different families, complexities in relationships Visible or invisible?. I set out from the narratives that the people interviewed indicated and made an approach to their families, mothers and fathers respectively. The situations that the young women and the 17 years of age lived in their interpersonal relationships, especially with their families, allowed me to make a parallel between what is narrated and visible to the interviewed student and to the family is invisible, denied, or hidden to avoid scandal or assume gender diversity within families
