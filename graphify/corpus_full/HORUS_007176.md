# Integral sustainability in vehicle bridges in Colombia: challenges and opportunities. An approach from complex thinking

The work addresses the urgent need to implement a holistic approach in the planning, construction and operation of vehicle bridges in Colombia. This approach overcomes the mere structural efficiency, encompassing economic, social, environmental and cultural dimensions, essential to ensure the long-term sustainability of these structures. It underlines how the construction sector, being a significant emitter of greenhouse gases, must evolve towards more sustainable practices. An exhaustive analysis of the historical evolution of bridge engineering in Colombia is carried out, highlighting both technological advances and persistent challenges. It identifies a notable lack in the integration of sustainability considerations, which is crucial to facing contemporary challenges. This analysis includes a critical review of collapse incidents and other structural problems that reveal deficiencies in the design and construction, underlining the need for a reassessment of current approaches. The document proposes a series of recommendations to understand the concept of integral sustainability in the construction of bridges. Finally, it emphasizes the importance of adopting a transdisciplinary approach to planning and execution of road infrastructure projects.
