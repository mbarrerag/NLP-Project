# Phenotypic and genotypic characterization of antimicrobial resistance of Salmonella spp. isolates from poultry farms

Salmonellosis is one of the most important diseases for the poultry industry and one of the causes of food poisoning in humans due to the zoonotic potential of some serovars. The inappropriate use of antimicrobials as prophylactics and growth promoters in the livestock industry has contributed to the development of resistance in bacteria of this genus. For this reason, it is important to identify the profiles of antimicrobial resistance (RAM) as part of the control measures.
