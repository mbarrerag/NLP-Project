# Molecular detection of RNA viruses in foliar tissue and tomato seeds in Antioquia by RT-qPCR and LAMP

This project aims to evaluate the usefulness of the innovative isothermal technique of LAMP for the detection of RNA virus in foliar tissue and tomato seeds from Antioquia, in order to close the gap of knowledge that is currently held on the main phytopathological limiting agents that affect the agronomic production of this vegetable in the country. Although initially for the detection and identification of the viruses will be used a battery of conventional methodologies of plant virology that include extraction of dsRNA, conventional PCR, RT-PCR in real time and/or sequencing, with the execution of the research we hope to implement assessments of the presence of some of these viruses by LAMP from raw extracts, which currently constitutes one of the main fields of research in the etological studies in different pathosystems, given the possibility of its use in laboratories not specialized in molecular biology and in the decrease of the costs associated with the detection of pathogens.
