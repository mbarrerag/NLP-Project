# Composting as a transformative tool in soil protection and agronomic regeneration in terms of sustainability

This research study addresses solid waste management and its environmental impact in the educational institution Diego Maya Salazar. It focuses on the proper management of organic waste produced at school, home, neighborhood and city level through composting as a sustainable alternative. The study focuses on the implementation of actions to promote environmental awareness and the application of sustainable practices. The aim is to generate significant and applicable learning related to environmental protection and also highlights the importance of environmental education from an early age to create responsible habits in students. The main understanding of this strategy was the realization of a composting process. In parallel, activities were carried out that allowed a continuous diagnostic evaluation of Diego Maya Salazar Educational Institution. It focuses on the progress and understandings achieved by students . The research project address solution management and its environment
