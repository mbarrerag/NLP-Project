# Navigating the Discursive Transformations and Practices of the Organization Girls Without Fear

This research revolves around the transformation process of the foundation Niñas Sin Miedo between January 2019 and March 2022 as a non-profit-making private social organization working on the prevention of gender-based violence and unplanned pregnancies in girls and adolescents through experiential and self-directed learning. The central objective is to analyze the scope of the discursive and practical transformations of the organization within a context of oenegization based on the resources offered by the field of Feminist and Gender Studies. The argument takes theoretical resources from the debates around the neoliberalization of feminist discourses and practices, the phenomenon of oenegization and the relationship between development and pedagogical proposals oriented to historically marginalized communities through the so-called ‘social innovation’. To analyze the case qualitatively, I propose a transversal category: Feminism-Development-Learning and self-directing.
