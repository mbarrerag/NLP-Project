# TRAINING ASSESSMENT A LOOK FROM INSURANCE STUDENTS

The training evaluation allows nursing students to evaluate their learning goals and improvements in the subjects Objective: To know the perception of university students on the training evaluation using the traffic light strategy. A qualitative and exploratory study carried out on forty-seven university students who study a theoretical subject from a public university. The collection of information was carried out through an online format with an open question. It was carried out during the month of December 2020 and analyzed with the Iramuteq software. Three categories were identified: the training evaluation as learning, the training evaluation and the self-assessment, the traffic light as a tool. The training evaluation constitutes a re-signification of the evaluation process seen from the teaching-learning dynamics. In this sense, the traffic light strategy allows to give meaning to the training process to recognize the shortcomings and enhance the learnings. Key words: Training evaluation; Self-evaluation; Student evaluation; Evaluation, Learning activities
