# Effects of sectoral economic cycles and unemployment rates on different types of credit in Colombia

This work, based on the monthlyisation of gross domestic product (GDP) time series, total and sectoral, and on employment and unemployment rates, shows the effects of economic cycles on the different types of credit in Colombia, both in terms of the dynamics of their variations and their non-compliance or default. To achieve this, a treatment is followed with three different perspectives: initially it is part of a visual analysis, where general features are highlighted; then it is implemented late correlations, which allow to find temporary periods of maximum correlation; finally, by applying the methodology of self-regressive vectors (VAR), the temporary effects of different macroeconomic shocks are found.
