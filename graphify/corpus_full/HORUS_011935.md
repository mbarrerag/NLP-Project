# TO BITE, WORK, CULTIVELY COVER AND SPIRIT, CIRCULAR. FROM THE FABRICA TO THE INDUSTRIAL COMPANY OF TOBACO S.A MEDELLÍN, COLOMBIA

To research Inhabit, WORK, cultivate body and spirit, circular. From the factory to the industrial group of the Colombian Company of Tabaco S. A., Medellín, Colombia is derived from the Master's Degree in Architecture of the National University of Colombia (Sede Medellín), where it was presented as a grade work of its author in 2015, obtaining laureate distinction. This document also obtained the third place in the category of Research, Theory and Critical in the XXVI Colombian Biennial of Architecture and Urbanism in 2018. In addition to analyzing the Coltabaco industrial group with historical perspective within the context of modernity in Medellin, this text seeks to document urgently the vestiges of modern architecture in this city, since they are extinct and, worse, after its disappearance, there is no memory or testimony of this architectural and urban period. This work makes a contribution from the discipline to the construction of a state of art, still embryonic, from the industrial vocation that the city has had. Likewise, the research process has added for some buildings of the project in mention today it is in order to be listed to become assets of cultural interest of the city
