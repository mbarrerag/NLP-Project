# ̈Greenium ̈ in Colombia: case study of the green bond market based on a two-factor structural model.

Colombia is not alien to the dramatic environmental situation that is currently going through the planet. Despite the efforts made from the public and private sectors there are still theoretical and methodological gaps that do not allow to elucidate effective measures to counteract the problem. In this context green bonds play a fundamental role in the allocation of resources required for a transition to a global economy friendly with the environment, where the modelling of this type of instruments becomes a challenge of quantitative finance.This thesis addresses fundamental aspects for the development of the green bond market in Colombia through a structural model of two factors with stochastic risk variables that incorporate climate and economic effects.The research had four main purposes: first, to determine the value of environmental and market variables not observable of the first green sovereign bond issued by the Colombian government at the end of September 2021; second, to theoretically determine the value of the so-called green and monetary model for Colombia, which constitutes a fundamental concept for establishing favourable financing conditions through green bonds; third, to analyze the sensitivity of the Greenium in Colombia against different environmental parameters, which is also employed to identify the environmental and economic mechanisms necessary for the financing of green economy, which it has been used, which it has been used to facilitate the financing of green financial
