# SEXUAL AND REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH PROMOTION IN THE MUNICIPLE OF TUMACO, NARBIUM, BY THE USE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGIES AND COMMUNICATION

In Colombia, women between 10 and 14 years old who have children is 6,292 per year. The highest global fertility rate is presented in departments with high poverty indicators and according to the National Policy on Sexuality, Sexual and Reproductive Rights (2010) there is a direct relationship between the increase in this and low level of social development indicators (2).The specific rate of fertility for children (10 to 14 years) by 2020 was 2.2 girls mothers per thousand, a figure lower than the figure reported in 2015 (3). At the departmental level, it is observed that the rate of fertility for children decreased between 2015 and 2020, except for Chocó, which in both years presented a rate of 3.7 and Nariño, which in 2020 was 2.8 (4).
