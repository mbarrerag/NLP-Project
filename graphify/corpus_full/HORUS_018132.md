# PREVALENCE AND MORPHOMETRIC CHARACTERISTICS OF THE ANTEROLATERAL LIGAMENT OF THE KNEE IN A SAMPLE OF COLOMBIAN HUMAN CADAVERS

The anatomy of the knee and its ligaments is clearly described in the literature; however, several studies have found new structures related to this joint, such as the anterolateral ligament (LAL) of the knee. The prevalence of this ligament is very variable worldwide and studies on the subject in the Colombian population are scarce. The prevalence of LAL was 24.13%, as this was identified in 7 of the 29 dissected knees (4 left knees and 3 right knees). The average length and width of LAL found were 37.5mm (range: 24-52.4mm) and 3.9mm (range: 2.6-6mm), respectively. It is concluded in the present study that confirmed the presence of LAL in the Colombian population, although with a lower prevalence than that described in countries such as France, India, China, USA and Brazil. Its morphometric characteristics were similar to those reported worldwide, since the length and width averages found here coincide with what was described in the literature
