# SENSORIAL FOOD ANALYSIS, TOOL FOR CHARACTERIZATION AND QUALITY CONTROL OF ANTIOQUEÑO CARNE BOVINA CUTTINGS

The sensory analysis of food is a tool that allows to characterize and establish criteria for the quality control of a food; it is as valid as the physicalochemical and microbiological analysis, allowing to establish and maintain the food quality from the production to the final consumption of a product. The different established protocols, both international and national give it validity, suggesting it useful to approve the final acceptability of a food. With the passage of the years it has become a valuable strategy to evaluate the products that generates day after day the food industry, and although it is considered subjective, well employed, and following all the normative parameters that are established for its application could generate extrapolable results. It has been determined by methodology of quantitative descriptive profile the sensory differences in cuts of bovine meat coming from the region of the Urabá Antioqueño (three stars, four stars and five stars) subjected to ripening and different methods of cooking, allowing to define the changes of sensory attributes of the cuts influenced by external factors.
