# Glycerol Valorization Using N-Heterocyclic Carbens Based Catalysts for Glycerol Carbonate and Dihydroxyacetone Obtaining

The production of biodiesel in Colombia has been constantly growing over the last years as a result of the search for alternative fuels different from those coming from fossil materials. Currently, in the 11 plants that counts the country, there is reached a production of approximately 55000 tons monthly. One of the major by-products of the process of biorefinery for obtaining biodiesel is 1,2,3-propanotriol, better known as glycerol. This is an organic compound with hydrophobic and hydrophilic properties that represents 10% of the production of biodiesel, which implies that for every 10 kg of fat matter 9 kg of biodiesel and 1 kg of glycerol are obtained. The great accumulation of this subproduct, which is considered as industrial waste, has caused that the scientific and industrial community are interested in its chemical transformation either in the aggregate, the hetero-hetero-specific, the hetero-hetero-specific, the hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-hetero-he synthesis of imidazolium salts, precursor of NHC ligands, as well as obtaining the respective Ru(II) complexes as possible organocatalizers and organometallic catalysts for obtaining glycerol carbonate and dihydroxyacetone, respectively, using glycerol as starting material. The project will allow the training of a master's degree student in chemical sciences, as well as linking two undergraduate students in chemistry to carry out their undergraduate work within the framework of this proposal. Additionally, the activities will be linked with the training activities carried out in the Inter-Semillary Research Semillero of Organic and Nanocatalysis Catalysis that includes students of different levels of training in chemistry
