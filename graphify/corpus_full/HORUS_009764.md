# POSSIBLE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN SPACE VARIATIONS IN PARAMETER B (B-VALUE) AND THE PRESENCE OF SERPENTINITS IN THE NORTH OF SURAMERICA

In this work, we seek to establish possible causal-effect relations between the zones of placement of the main serpentinite belts and seismicity patterns in the north of South America. Considering that the serpentinite belts, depending on their location, can present different ages and different water contents, the possibility arises that these differences influence the seismic activity present in the study region. Thus, we analyze the approximate distribution of the serpentinite belts placed under the current tectonic configuration, as well as the impact that this distribution of serpentinite belts can have on the current seismic activity
