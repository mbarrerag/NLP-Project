# DIDACTIC SECUE FOR THE TEACHING OF THE AREA CONCEPT USING ABI IN DOCENT TRAINING

This article presents an educational experience of continuous training of science teachers, based on the application of a didactic sequence called "A la semado", based on the Learning Based on Research (ABI), as teaching methodology, which was proposed from the need to strengthen the didactic component in the teaching of natural sciences with teachers working in areas far from the major urban centers. With this in mind, a form of design and implementation of experimental activities was explored to strengthen the didactic skills in natural science teachers, in topics of flat geometry and measurement. Based on a research - qualitative approach, the results revealed that these training processes based on the (ABI) favor possible transformations of the pedagogical practices of the participants, where also the teachers expressed a good acceptance of the process, taking it in some cases until its implementation
