# Determination of critical success factors (CEF) for the adoption of building information modeling (BIM) in the management of construction projects in Colombia by micro and small enterprises

This research seeks to determine the Critical Factors of Success (FCE) for the adoption of Building Information Modeling (BIM) in the management of construction projects in Colombia by the micro and small enterprise, using qualitative and quantitative methodologies developed in three phases: i. in phase 1 a literature-depth review is carried out to define the CEFs, which authors have found, related to the research object; ii. in phase 2, through consultation of experts on the CIM in Colombia, the relevance and relevance in Colombia of the critical factors of phase 1 is validated; and iii. in phase 3, the CEFs validated by experts in phase 2 by BIM users joined in the BIM Forum Colombia. From an initial total of 44 CEFs found in the review of the literature, BIM is determined to use the CEFs of the BIM, which can be grouped into the following categories: availability of resources, management and leadership, BIM software, general processes of the company, internal processes BIM, external processes and technology (BIM) is used by BIM. The CSFs validated by the experts in phase 2 are rated by BIM users affiliated with the BIM Forum Colombia. From an initial total of 44 CSFs found in the literature review, it is reduced to 21 resulting CSFs from the assessment by BIM users, which can be grouped into the following categories: availability of resources, management and leadership, BIM software, general company processes, internal BIM processes, external BIM processes, and use of technology (non-BIM) in the company. In the end, a protocol is proposed to be followed by companies wish to implement BIM
