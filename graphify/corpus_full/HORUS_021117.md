# Dissemination of the proposal of the Unit for Indigenous Health Care UASI for the community of Charco Gallo del resguardo Ríos Uva y Pogue _x0096_ Quebrada Taparal, of the municipality of Bellavista, Chocó.

The project of solidarity extension: The geographic information systems at the service of the Architecture as a support tool for the solution of public health problems in vulnerable communities. Case study: Creation of a Unit of Care in Indigenous Health UASI in the municipality of Bellavista, Chocó (Antiguo Bojayá), was responsible for characterizing the types of buildings existing in the indigenous shelter and based on them, proposed the model of Unit of Care in Indigenous Health, responding to the language of its own, the conditions of location and accessibility and the requirements of the Ministry of National Health, for the construction of infrastructures of care to the population. The socialization of the result will be done by means of a poster with the basic information and the construction of a three-dimensional digital model to define and validate the existing technical specifications
