# Design and development of a prototype of an automated vertical aeroponic system, powered by water obtained by air condensation

One of the challenges to improve food security in the face of a constantly increasing population, requires promoting sustainable and adaptable agriculture. For this, it is necessary to implement emerging technologies, aimed at improving the efficiency and productivity of resources such as water, soil, energy and agricultural inputs. The objective of this experimental work is to develop a prototype of automated aeroponic culture system, using software and open source hardware. An innovative aspect of this prototype is the recycling of water obtained from the active condensation of air moisture, which is used as a means for the cultivation of leaf vegetables. The system of automation is based on an integrated network of smart sensors that allow to measure and control essential parameters for the efficiency of the crop, such as relative humidity, ambient temperature, lighting, water level, pH, electrical conductivity and temperature of the nutritive solution. Likewise, it involved the use of actuators and repleteers that activate and deactivate devices such as water pump, electro-valve, nutrient and dehumidifier.
