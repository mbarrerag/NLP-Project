# Eco-living system: Participatory and eco-productive housing in the former industrial zone Toupnot, Lourdes, France.

E(co)-living System is a project that develops a participatory housing typology, capable of producing ecosystem services, applying the theme of regenerative architecture to fulfill the natural cycles of 4 main elements, energy, biodiversity, water and food. This project is located in an old industrial area called Toupnot, in the city of Lourdes, France, and is framed in a partial development plan by the city's mayor
