# ACTIVE LEARNING METHODOLOGIES DEVELOPED IN THE ASIGNATURE OF PROBILITY AND FUNDAMENTAL STATISTICS IN THE NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF PEACE-KEEPING COLOMBIA

The innovative Academic Project of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia La Paz Headquarters suggests moving from a conventional model, focused on teaching, to one focused on learning (heutagogical). The objective of this study was to describe the classroom experiences of three active learning methodologies (MAAs; project-based learning - ABP, team-based learning - APE and game-based learning ¿ABJ) implemented in the subject of Probability and Fundamental Statistics of three groups constituted by students from the six programs offered at La Paz Headquarters (Biology, Statistics, Geography, Cultural and Communication Management, Biological Engineering and Mechatronic Engineering) during the first module of the semester 2023-1. This was a study of qualitative nature of descriptive scope and with a non-probabilistic sample to convenience, collecting the data from the observation of researchers and presenting a descriptive and reflective statistical analysis of the academic project that transits from the conventional classroom and the laboratory of learning, creation and transfer of knowledge and full living, harmonizing the 20th century, research line of teaching, and extension of teaching (Alcádigo, research and extension)
