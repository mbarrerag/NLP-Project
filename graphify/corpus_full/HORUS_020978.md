# Predictive model of relevance of higher education programmes based on national academic offer

In recent years, enrolment in higher education in Colombia has been growing significantly, largely due to the country's public policies and the increase in the number of scholarships to access higher education.This increase brings a great deal of challenges to the IES, which, together with the quality policies of the Ministry of National Education, affect the academic supply and demand of institutions.This research project seeks to generate explanatory and predictive models that enable the different IES in the region and the country, to offer new programs according to the relevance and behavior of the national academic offer, as well as the preference of students in the search for a programme in higher education.The study will be based mainly on data provided by the national higher education information system (SNIES)
