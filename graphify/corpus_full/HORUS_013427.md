# Re-Thinking psychoanalysis in the 21st century: Towards a materialistic clinic around the void

What is materialism and how does it operate in psychoanalytic praxis? This is the question this article attempts to answer. It offers a brief historical survey of theoretical-practical concepts from Freud, via Lacan, to contemporary thinkers. It looks more closely at certain ideas of Slavoj Žižek, specifically his materialistic thinking, or what he himself calls "a refoundation of dialectical materialism", which offers an unconscious not-All combination. Taking this theoretical perspective, the paper questions some clinical debates in philosophy and psychoanalysis
