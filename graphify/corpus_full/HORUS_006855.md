# Between notes and tradition: adaptation of Zipaquirá's anthem from the study and analysis of the choral work of master Guillermo Quevedo Zornoza

This degree work focuses on the preservation of the cultural heritage of Zipaquireño as a means to strengthen the social fabric and contribute to a sustainable future. In a sociocultural context that seems to threaten the indigenous and local identity, due to globalization, an initiative is proposed based on the compositional works of the master Guillermo Quevedo Zornoza, creator of the Hymn of Zipaquirá. Through a detailed musical analysis, the necessary information will be collected to carry out the coral adaptation that allows its proper integration with the existing symphonic arrangement. Additionally, this work is aimed at promoting the interpretation of the anthem in educational institutions, so that the knowledge and appropriation of this cultural heritage is encouraged and thus contributes to the preservation of the sustainable future.
