# The (de)humanization of the street: Ways to inhabit the street as a space for intermediation between the domestic and the city.

The streets and their role as a space for intermediation are threatened by planning practices guided by the idea of order, in addition to the interests of the real estate capital that took part in the urban development of the city. From a purely spatial connotation, cultural practices lose their stage, the relation street and domestic space began to dilute many years ago. The street as a space for intermediation tends to disappear.
