# Fetal death by gastrosquisis associated with intrauterine growth restriction. Case report

Gastrosquisis is a congenital defect of the abdominal wall that has a low prevalence and whose etiology is still little known. In most cases, the prognosis is favorable if it is detected early and the associated mortality is usually low if adequate follow-up is done; however, when the defect is associated with restriction of intrauterine growth (IRU) the risk of adverse perinatal outcome increases. Presentation of the case. 16-year-old with a 34.6 week pregnancy, who entered the epulsive stage of labor in the emergency department of the Simón Bolívar E.S.E. hospital in bogotá (colombia). The patient, reported echographic finding of gastrosquisis and IURC, conditions that caused fetal death. The identification and care of gastrosquisi is necessary, not only to ensure a outcome compatible with the life of the fetus, but to achieve the well-being and dignified conditions for the newborn, since the complexity of this condition makes it difficult to obtain satisfactory perinatal results. The present case highlights the importance of, on the one hand, initiating early prenatal control given to identify and identify the appropriate conditions for the newborn
