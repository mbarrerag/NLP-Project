# Conservation of the aesthetic value of architectural facades with the use of nanomaterials

The negation of architectural skins due to pollution and environmental dirt is a constant challenge that affects both aesthetics and the functionality of buildings, as well as the urban environment in general. The passage of time reveals the effects of aging in buildings, exacerbated by the lack of maintenance of facades. Therefore, it is necessary to explore new materials that can prevent and counteract these problems. The collaboration between construction, science and technology is essential. The advances in nanomaterials, such as nanoparticles, offer optimal characteristics to optimize processes and materials in innovative and sustainable projects. This study focuses on the architectural facades of the Balmoral work of Armenia, as a case relevant to address these challenges. The results demonstrated its effectiveness in improving the resistance to pollution and dirt, offering a promising solution to mitigate the aging in buildings. In conclusion, this study highlights the importance of innovation in building materials to address the challenges of ageing and degradation of the buildings.
