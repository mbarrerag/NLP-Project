# INTEGRAL LITORY LITORY FISHERIES AND AGROINDUSTRIAL PRODUCTS.

This proposal includes two components as follows: The first one focused on the integral use (pulp and seed) of the fruit of the species Euterpe (E. oleraceae and/or E. precatorio) known in the region as Naidí. The second component aims to the implementation of a technological tool of low cost for informed decision making (scientifically based) by the fishermen's households in the Pacific coast of Nariñense for intelligent fishing in changing climatic conditions. As the first component is proposed the implementation of a methodology for the agro-industrial transformation of the fruit of the species Euterpe (E. oleraceae and/or E. precatorio) that allows the obtaining of a standardized ingredient pulp type that can be used in the formulation of products (food and/or cosmetic life) of added value and destined to the final consumer. From the standardized ingredient of the product will be designed and developed at least three final products that include in its formulation of the fruit of the naidí.
