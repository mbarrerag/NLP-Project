# A didactic study on the approach to variable thinking in mathematics texts of the 10th grade in Colombia.

This work aims to carry out a didactic analysis around the coherence between the approach of variable thinking in the tenth degree textbooks of mathematics (LTM) most used in Colombia regarding the curricular guidelines issued by the Ministry of National Education (MEN). The work is framed within the mixed approach with descriptive scope and the technique of analysis of contents is used as an instrument for the collection and treatment of information in 5 phases. The analysis is carried out on 3 variables: i) coherence between the implicit topics of Basic Standards of Competences (EBCM) of degree of variational thinking (PV) and the topics addressed in the texts, ii) Coherence between the scope of the PV (10o) and the proposed activities in the texts, and iii) Coherence between the processes associated with the development of PV and the development of the contents, in texts of the editorial measures, Santillana, Norma, Educar and the Editorial Books and Books.
