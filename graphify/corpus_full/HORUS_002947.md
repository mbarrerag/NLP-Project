# ENVIRONMENTAL PLURIVERSE: IMAGINARY IN DISPOSAL AND ONTOLOGIC CO-EXISTENCE OF NATURE IN THE QUINCHAS SERRANIA

The Serranía de Las Quinchas (SDLQ) in Colombia has been configured as a complex space where multiple environmental imaginarys converge, collide and coexist, between state control, local practices and hybrid proposals that seek to reconcile conservation and development. The objective of the research was to identify and understand the imaginarys that operate in the region analyzing the tactics and strategies employed by communities and the State in the management of the territory. The state imaginary is manifested through official environmental discourse, as a tool of spatialization, through representation regimes based on control, surveillance and scientific conservation.The traditional local imaginary, due to its expression to historical cultural practices of the communities that have inhabited the SDLQ, which in our research we will call tactical.In this intersection, a third hybrid imaginary, of strategies, which seeks to reconcile conservation demands with local development needs, is considered from the point of view of theoretical-methodological, cultural practices were analyzed, classified as strategies and tactics (De Certeau, 1996).The results show the ontologically plural nature of the territory, which allows the coexistence of multiple ways of understanding, assessment and relationship with the new practices
