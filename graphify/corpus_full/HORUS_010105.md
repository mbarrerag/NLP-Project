# MITIGATION OF ABIOTIC AND BIOTIC STRESSES WITH SILITATION IN PIMENTON (CAPSICUM ANNUUM L.)

The paprika is one of the vegetables of greatest importance for human consumption worldwide. The use of biostimulants like silicon (Si) has improved growth, yield and response to abiotic and biotic stress conditions in different crops. In this context, three experiments were developed to evaluate the application dose, method of application and response to water deficit conditions. The three experiments were developed separately in the greenhouse of the National University of Colombia Palmira headquarters between August and September 2021 and February and March 2022, this in order to study the use of three sources of Si (acidulated and basic), form of application (foliar, edaphic or combined) on the physiology, growth and dynamics of arthropods in paprika plants under conditions of water stress.
