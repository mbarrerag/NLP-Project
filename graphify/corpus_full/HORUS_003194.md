# ORGANIC CARBON AND EDAPHIC CHARACTERSISTICS OF A CATENA FROM THE COLOMBIAN ANDES

The formation of soil or pedogenesis is a process that is influenced by various factors, where geomorphology takes an important role; through the changes of the relief it can be evidenced variations in the composition of the soil regarding the physical and chemical characteristics. This study proposes to know the changes edaphic and pedogenic in the basin The Poleo, through a catena that covers geoforms and plant coverings, where the type of soil is determined, the physicochemical properties are evaluated and the values of carbon stock of each profile are estimated. The low areas of the slope presented higher organic content that is derived in COS regarding the highest areas with greater slope, which resulted in the most eroded properties of the soil (COS), and the specific physical and chemical properties were analyzed for each horizon. The differences in the relief organic properties generated by the soil, the higher concentrations of organic matter generated in COS. The higher areas with higher slope, were estimated by pedogenesis, and the specific properties of each site were presented.
