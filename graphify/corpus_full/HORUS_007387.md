# Coordination strategy to mitigate the impact of drug shortages on a highly complex IPS

The final work to opt for the title of Magister in Administration proposes a coordination strategy to mitigate the impact of the shortage of medicines in a health provider institution (IPS) of high complexity. The literature and practice indicate that the IPS face difficulties in the provision of health services due to the scarcity and interruption of the supply chain of medicines, a public health problem recognised at a global level. The causes of this problem are diverse, including external factors such as difficulties in obtaining raw material, manufacturing problems, and delays in the import processes, as well as internal factors such as increasing the demand for treatments, low precision in the planning of needs, delays in the acquisition of medicines, inadequate inventory policies and financial difficulties. For the development of the final work, a case study is proposed as an empirical investigation within a real context.
