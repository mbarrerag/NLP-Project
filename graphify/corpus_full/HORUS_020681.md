# ANALYSIS OF THE TEMPERATURE CAMP IN THE CAVITIES OF A POLICIES INJECTION MOLD

A common situation in the design of injection moulds lies in achieving efficient performance in terms of thermal transfer, this will allow a higher production rate with better finished parts [1]. One of the most important factors in the design is cooling time: about 80% of the processing time is determined by it [2]. Seeking to contribute to the increase in productivity, this work presents results of simulations using the finite volume method (MVF) of the injection molding process; these results are compared to an analysis of experimental design (DOE) with different injection conditions, revealing study variables that are fundamental to improve the process. Thus, a statistical analysis and analysis using computational simulation, are presented to identify the variables inherent in the process and recommend its values
