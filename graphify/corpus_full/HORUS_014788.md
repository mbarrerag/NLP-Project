# THE AUTONOMY LITERARY FROM LATIN AMERICA: SCISION OF THE SIGNATURE, THE OPULATION OF THE POLITICAL

The notion of autonomy is one of the pillars of Pierre Bourdieu’s theory of fields. Other scholars have reported some of the implications of such a notion, whether with or without references to Bourdieu, in different studies, or have included it in their proposals. This is the case of, for example, Ángel Rama, Pascale Casanova and even Michael Foucault, among others. In this article I pre-tend, without intention of completeness, to show different ways of analyzing or using the notion in Latin American authors working in the literature of the region and how, in general, it is associated with ways of concealing the political conditions of cultural production. Likewise, I find and trace in Latin American authors the excision of the sign in significance and meaning as con- sequence of the colonization processes in Latin America
