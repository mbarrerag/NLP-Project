# Stratigraphy of veins and fluid evolution oriented to the study of metalogenesis in the northeast sector of the Mining District of Segovia -Remedios, Antioquia - Colombia

The Mining District of Segovia Remedios (DMSR) is the largest producer of gold in Colombia. It is located on the eastern flank of the Central Cordillera, north-east of the Department of Antioquia. Mineralizations east of the district consist of a series of vetiform structures hosted in the Batolith of Segovia and the Sedimentitas of Segovia. The electric gold extracted in this area is enriched in silver towards the southeast of the district. With the objective of knowing the metallic variables that are controlling the enrichments of silver, were analyzed within a transecta NW-SE the crystallized veins, Techo, Chumeca, Argentifera, Vera and Guide Antigua within the study area, from conventional and digital petrography, mineral microchemistry, microthermometry, ablation of the iron and the septipia Raman in fluid inclusions and isotopia Pb-Pb. Three hydrothermal events separated from each other were characterized by two events of fracture. The hydrothermal events are related to the occurrence of gold and silver. The average fineness of the calculated electric is between 6.6 – 15,1 wt. %NaCl eq. Additionally, the average fineness of the calculated electric is 495 (Crytal Veta Cristals), 350 (Cryt Roof Vet), 553 (Cumeca Veta), 538 (Vera), 514 (Old Guide). For the two events, the homogenization temperatures decrease and the fineness of the electric increase towards the southeast of the DMSR.
