# Collective mental health occupations and production: experiences of artistic community groups in Bogotá during the national strike of 2021.

This work sought to understand how the subjective experiences of occupations are related to the production of collective mental health, based on the accounts of community artistic groups in Bogotá that participated in the context of the national strike of 2021. It was carried out from a critical, political and situated view of mental health and occupation, with emphasis on subjectivity and community responses, including the notion of social suffering that distanced itself from the psychopathological paradigm. It was a qualitative study with design multiple instrumental case study, combined narrative and ethnographic techniques for the construction of information and had interpretative-hermeneutic perspective for analysis. The findings suggest that the suffering and resistances of social explosion were shaped in the interaction of different social conditions, repressive, symbolic and media violences; hurting emotions, daily life and community life. As they arise responses of health and life as the need to collectively engage in search for expression, care and community healing.
