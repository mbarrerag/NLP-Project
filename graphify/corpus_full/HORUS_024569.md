# BUCODENTAL HEALTH IN ALL POLICIES: ADULT POPULATION

The high prevalence of oral diseases in Chile and the world places them as one of the most important public health problems. The impact of these diseases is evident at multiple levels, from the impoverishment of the quality of life to the general deterioration of health. This document presents an analysis of some of the adult health policies in Chile, and seeks to identify those programmes and strategies in which it is necessary to focus efforts to develop proposals for oral health policies. The prevalence and public health studies show that there is a close relationship between oral diseases and some systemic diseases, such as association with cardiovascular diseases and diabetes. It is also important to note the increased risk of oral diseases when patients and the population have acquired habits such as smoking and alcohol, while not forgetting the changes that have occurred with the modernization associated with diet
