# THE ARCHITECTURE TURNED INTO THE BERNARD FOREST TREATY OF BELIDER: THE SCIENCE DE ENGENIEURS... (1729)

This article describes and analyses the contents of which has been considered the first modern engineering treatise: La Science des Ingenieurs, by B.F. Belidor (1729), of great importance in the academies of training of engineers in Europe and America. The main objective is to demonstrate the way in which in it a simplification of architecture is made in the form of a purely decorative art, giving supremacy to the value of mathematics and geometry
