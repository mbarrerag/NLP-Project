# Constitution and democracy: current debates and challenges in the digital age

With this proposal the Research Group _x0093_Constitutional Law and Human Rights_x0094_ will focus on developing, in its different investigative lines, i) a balance of the constitutional regime, following the 30 years of validity of the Constitution of 1991, regarding its conceptual foundations, fundamental rights and the system of brakes and counter-prisons, and ii) an analysis of the state of law and democracy within the framework of digital transformation. Thus, in this item the conceptual framework, the problematization of which the project starts, the objectives marked in function of the latter and its realization, will be briefly explained. In the form of an initial conceptual framework, and in accordance with the current perspective of the Constitutional Court, on the one hand, fundamental rights can be defined as those that relate to human dignity; (ii) they are translated into subjective rights; and (iii) they have an independence between the fundamentality and justiciability of rights (T-428/12, 2012). On the other hand, to analyze the system of pesos and counterweights, initially they should take into account the division of the State enshrined in Article 113 of the Constitution, and to the three organs of the Constitution.. In this understanding, the manipulation of new technologies in society over the last decades has generated changes in the model of democracy and it is pertinent to identify what their impact has been. For this reason, the research group problematizes the way in which digital transformations have affected the configuration and exercise of fundamental rights.In this order of ideas, the changes that have been derived from the widespread use of information technologies, leading to a crisis of representation of political and democratic institutions.In virtue of the above, the main objective of this project is to analyze, on the one hand, the main developments that have taken place in the 1991 Constitution from a conceptual, dogmatic and organic point of view; and, on the other, the ways in which digital transformations have affected the configuration of democracy and the exercise of fundamental rights enshrined in the Charter, through a qualitative methodology that allows for the advance of a theoretical, descriptive and propositive analysis of the phenomena concerning digital democracy, the system of checks and counterweights, and the exercise and configuration of fundamental rights after the issuance of the Constitution, the project is presented as a basis for the formation of the results of society
