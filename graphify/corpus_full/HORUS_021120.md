# Presence and comparative effects of new epistemologies in the field of Colombian administrative doctrine in gender perspective within the National Planning Department and the Administrative Department for Social Prosperity (2014-2022)

This paper seeks to compare the effect of administrative doctrines in the last Colombian governments in key of the new administrative epistemologies and the gender approach. The analysis of administrative doctrines in these will have a comparative framework through public procurement and the profiles of managerial positions in government entities. For the case in particular, the effects will be analyzed and compared based on changes in the posts of free appointment in two administrative departments, the National Department of Administration (DNP) and the Administrative Department for Social Prosperity (DAPS). The significance between the recruitment of public appointments positions, through the political agenda driven from each direction, indicates the administrative paradigm that is dominant in the periods of government, which, for the purpose of the comparative objective, will be realized the contrast in a synchronous and diachronic way, between the temporalities of governments and institutions.
