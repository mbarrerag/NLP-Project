# Analysis of correlations of the price of electrical energy exchange with variables of the electrical and economic sector

This study aims to develop a predictive model based on the ARIMA model to estimate the cost of electrical energy in Colombia, focusing on the national stock price and its relationship with key variables such as the supply price of hydropower generators and the economic indicators IPC and IPP. To this end, historical data from the Colombian electricity market were used from January 2010 to April 2024. The proposed model combines advanced techniques of time series analysis to capture temporal patterns, trends, seasonalities, and model uncertainties and nonlinear relationships. The methodology used follows the CRISP-DM process, which ensures consistency and rigorousness in each phase of the analysis. The results of the study show that the ARIMA model is effective in predicting the cost of electrical energy in normal and atypical conditions, as extreme climatic phenomena or economic crisis. However, limitations are recognized, such as the dependence on quality and availability of data and the complexity of modelling dynamic and multivariate systems. In conclusion, this study contributes to the progress in the prediction of energy prices by proposing an integrated model that can have a significant impact on the planning and operation of the system.
