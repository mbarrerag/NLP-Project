# Operator approach to epidemic systems in networks.

Epidemic models are a very valuable tool for public health and epidemiology, since they are able to simulate the outcome of an outbreak based on assumptions and data. In particular, stochastic epidemic models are an interesting tool that is based on the theory of leap processes, these models can be simulated with a description similar to the field theory called “Doi-Peliti Formatism.” The aim of this thesis is to describe epidemic models in such formalism using the properties of formalism and models. Here I present a variety of results based on a representation of operators of the Markoviana master equation that are useful for simulating small systems, also I present a result that allows to calculate the probability that there will not be an outbreak even when the basic reproductive number is greater than one.
