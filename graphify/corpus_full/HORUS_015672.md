# INVOLVEMENT OF A VERTIONAL EDUCATIONAL RESOURCE FOR CARDIOPULMON REAMBURANCE TEACHING

During the 21st century, the world has presented a great challenge in terms of care and prevention of diseases such as obesity, hypertension, heart attacks and strokes. The World Health Organization (WHO) and the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) have joined efforts with different countries to propose and implement tools and solutions that may reduce the occurrence of diseases or mitigate the damage they may generate. According to WHO statistics, in 2012, ischemic heart disease and stroke were the first and third causes of death, respectively, worldwide. The present work aims to present the partial results of the implementation of a virtual educational resource for the teaching of basic cardiopulmonary resuscitation and the perception of the participants on this resource. Quantitative study of descriptive scope. The data were obtained from the results of the pre-test, post-test and the survey of perception of the virtual educational resource.
