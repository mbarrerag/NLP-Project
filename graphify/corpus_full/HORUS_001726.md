# Caregiver role adoption of people with chronic illness in Pasto: new contributions to Situation-Specific Theory

To determine the relationship between depression, anxiety, and loneliness with the adoption of the caregiver role in a group of low-income caregivers from Pasto, Nariño (Colombia). Quantitative study with a cross-sectional analytical design with 295 caregivers of people with different chronic diseases. A characterization form and three instruments were applied: UCLA Loneliness Scale, Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS), and ROL Scale-Caregiver Role Adoption-. This study reports relationships (albeit weak) between the caregiver's age and the adoption of the caregiver role (response dimension), as well as between the patient's diagnosis duration and the caregiver's anxiety, depression, and loneliness. There is a strong and significant direct correlation between depression and the variables of loneliness and anxiety. The age of caregivers in the municipality of Pasto impacts the positive or negative recognition of their role, while the patient's condition, such as the duration of the illness, influences the deterioration of the caregiver's mental health. This confirms the need for transitional care to support the longterm experience, rather than just short-term interventions
