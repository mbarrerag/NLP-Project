# THE STORY AND RESEARCH OF THE STORY OF INDUSTRIAL PRODUCT DESIGN IN COLOMBIA. APPROXIMATION TO THEIR POSSIBLENESS FROM THE CASES OF THE HISTORY OF THE FIRST LOZA FACILITY OF BOGOTÁ (1832), SALMAN INDUSTRY (1952) AND PALMIRA METALIQUE INDUSTRY (1980)

In Colombia, the link between history and design could be characterized by its rejection, its association-confusion with the history of art and architecture and by the difficulty of establishing links between these two disciplinary fields.These difficulties are marked both from the edge of design and from the history itself, which in our country has emphasized in political and economic history.However, with the strengthening of social and cultural history, it is possible to anticipate in Colombia a more promising future for the already known in the world, history of design. For this reason, the question about how to develop this history becomes as relevant as the acceptance of its field of study. That is why in this opportunity it presented the history itself as a resource for research in design and for the construction of this history in the country. To do this I will also go to the comparison of three stories of national industries, of different periods, like the first factory of Bogota (1832), Salman (1952) and Metal Industries of Palmira (1980) and I will try to show how this historical comparison allows us to recognize the historical aspects of the design and local industry
