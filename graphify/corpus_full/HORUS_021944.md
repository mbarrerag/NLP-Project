# Design of a measuring instrument for logistics management in companies in the metal-mechanical sector in Colombia.

The logistic management, in the companies of the metal mechanics sector in Colombia, is characterized by its many variables and forms of implementation. In this research, the generation of an instrument is promoted, based on previous research related to the logistic sector in the metal mechanics companies worldwide, that allows to measure the logistic capacity, its impact on the company and characterize the model used within them. The structure of this document is deductive in nature so that the macro variables: logistics at national and international level of the Mipyme and, the micro variables that characterize the companies directly related to the metal mechanics sector in Colombia are taken into account.
