# Street art as a transforming tool for a new citizenship. Muralism in Manizales, Colombia

Summary Today, the street art -specifically, the mural and graffiti - has contributed imagined different from those implied in the planned cities with a heritage positivist and rationalist, and although the investigations in Colombia are few, there is a nascent field of study with a wide range of possibilities for your understanding in relation to the appropriation of public space and of other ways of thinking about, understanding and inhabiting the city. This article attempts to glimpse the role that contemporary artistic expressions of muralism play in the inhabitation of the urban space of Manizales. This work focuses on the inventory of murals made between 2012 and 2020 and the subsequent reflection on the socializing dynamics to rethink the public in the city. The research methodology was qualitative, based on a hermeneutic and phenomenological analysis. A geographic information system (GIS) was used to group the murals into four categories: artists, themes, places and festivals or urban art narratives. The results denote preponderances and common aspects in relation to the defined categories, which allowed a reflective exercise based on the observation of the phenomenon itself. This work allowed to expose a critical view of muralism in the city identifying it as a participant in the appearance of differentiated places and as an expression that contributes to create imaginaries different from those established suggesting the transformation of public space
