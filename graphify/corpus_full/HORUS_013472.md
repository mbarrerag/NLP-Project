# Systematization of the Experience of Related Intervention Based on the Apego (IRBA) with children between 6 and 12 years of age in the city of Bogotá, Colombia

The relational intervention based on attachment (IRBA), is a novel intervention, implemented for the first time in Colombia in order to improve the sensitivity of caregivers to prevent child abuse. A systematization of the experience (Barnechea & Morgan, 2010) was carried out in order to describe and analyze the process of implementation of this intervention in the context of the protection services, in an operator of the ICBF in the city of Bogotá. A documentary review was carried out and eight interviews were carried out with participating families, coordinators and intervention teams. From an approximation of the methodology Framework (Ritchie & Spencer, 2002) the information collected was analyzed. A system of analytical categories was constructed from which information was ordered obtaining three main categories of the experience that organized the experience. It was found that the intervention was constructed as a response to the problem of re-entry of children to the protection system, so that the intervention was adapted to the context and added the role of community weaver. ams.
