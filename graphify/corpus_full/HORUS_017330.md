# Glosses to the Chapter of the Final Report of the Commission on the Truth on the relationship between culture and internal armed conflict in Colombia and to the recommendations for achieving a culture for living in peace.

The article critically analyzes the chapter of the Report of the Truth Commission on the relations between Culture and the Internal Armed Conflict in Colombia, highlighting the significant and valuable elements on which the Commission draws attention and highlighting some issues that could have been addressed with greater breadth and precision
