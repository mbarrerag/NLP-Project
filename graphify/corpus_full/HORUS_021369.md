# Battery Savors: Lithium Cell Recycling

Our initiative consists of the lithium battery recycling project in Bogotá, with the aim of reducing the environmental impact and costs of batteries used in electrical devices. To achieve this, we collect discarded or disused lithium batteries from different electronic devices and, through a separation and measurement process, we identify cells that meet the minimum requirements of capacity and useful life. With selected cells, we assemble new recycled batteries for electric bicycles and other devices, such as monopatins, power banks or power walls. To carry it out, an automated machine will be developed for the selection and classification of cells. With this project, we seek to promote the circular economy and reduce the amount of waste that ends in landfills, as well as to make recycled batteries more accessible and cost-effective for the general public. In short, our project aims to be a sustainable and cost-effective solution for the recycling of lithium batteries, and thus contribute to the preservation of the environment and the promotion of the use of environmentally friendly means of transport
