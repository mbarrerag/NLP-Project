# NUMERICAL OPTIMIZATION OF THE SOLUTION OF THE GINZBURG- LANDAU EQUATIONS: CRANK-NICHOLSON AND MATRICIALS METHODS

In this contribution, we analyze several numerical proposals to solve the Ginzburg-Landau system of equations. In the first proposal, we consider the quasi-linear coupled system in matrix form, as a second option, we consider a computational molecule with its respective restriction of the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the matrix, and finally, applying the Crank-Nicholson method we solve the system of equations; we clearly and concisely obtained the eigenvalues that lead us to an optimal spatio-temporal convergence solution of said system of equations. We compare the computational convergence times obtained by these three methods with the known time found by applying the link variable method
