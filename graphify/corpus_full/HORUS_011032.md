# Temporary spatial modelling of fishing landings in the Colombian Pacific using functional data

The document below aims to estimate stochastic models of ARIMA time series for the tons of fish species most important at social and economic level in the Colombian Pacific, caught by means of fishing. It is intended to use as covariables of the production models per species, the salinity and temperature curves measured at -0.5, -41 and -86 meters under the surface of the ocean, which were calculated using spatial analysis tools and functional data analysis, taking as reference for each species, the area formed by the pixels with the highest probability values of the species presence cluster and the Pacific temperature and salinity NetCDF files for different depths; in order to explain the behaviour of the production of landings by species in function of the changes of the oceanographic variables. .
