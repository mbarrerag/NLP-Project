# Between 'Invasions' and housing policies. Analysis of the role of social imaginaries in informality, habitat planning and management in Yopal, Colombia in the period 1990-2022

This document describes the production dynamics of urban informality and housing in the city of Yopal based on the analysis of social imaginarys, from where it is explained why there is a historical continuity of this phenomenon even with different political and economic systems. The planning and management of the habitat of Yopal, has been given from the social imaginary of the formal and planned city, with which a rational order of the space has been sought, so that the self-managed settlements challenge the system of hegemonic sociocultural significance of the formal planning. This research is addressed from the phenomenological paradigm, where from the narrative analysis the social imaginarys that support the urban reality are identified. It is concluded that the disputes about the public policies of housing and the city are in essence disputed by the social meanings of the city, to whom it belongs and the way in which it is lived, moreover it is evidenced that despite the dimension of the informality of housing in Yopal is minimized by urban rulers and planners, have been the self-constructed settlements and their formalizations responsible for generating the urban land in the municipality, turning the social institution and informality of housing into a social informality. nic management of formal planning.
