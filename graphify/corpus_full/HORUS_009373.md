# MODELATION OF THE EFFECT OF BIOMASS ANISOTROPY IN ITS CONVERSION AND PRODUCT PERFORMANCE DURING THE RAPID PYROLYSIS

In this work, a particle scale model is developed for rapid biomass pyrolysis. This model considers a two-dimensional particle with anisotropic behavior in intra-particle mass transport, incorporating a detailed reaction mechanism that includes the lignocellulosic composition of the particle and the formation of the intermediate liquid phase that appears due to the depolymerization reactions of the pseudo-omponents of biomass. The model allows to observe the effect of properties the thermal and transport properties of the particle and other characteristics such as its size, initial humidity on biomass conversion and the yield of the products obtained. The model also allows to observe the effect of process parameters such as the rate of heating, the final temperature and the pressure of the reactor on the product yields.
