# Model of simulation of the behavior of chickenpox infections in the city of Bogotá based on dynamic systems and intelligent control

The mathematical model SVEIR (Susceptible, Vaccinate, Exposed, Infected, Recovered) proposed to represent the contagion of varicella in the context of the city of Bogotá, includes the use of a periodic function to represent seasonal behavior per epidemiological week, which was identified in the historical cases of individual chickenpox reported by the INS of Colombia between 2007 and 2020. As a result of sensitivity analyses and simulations performed on the mathematical model, it was identified that the vaccination rate parameter has a negative impact on the basic reproduction number R0. Implementation in the computational environment, of a controller based on diffuse logic that allows adapting the value of each parameter in relation to the deviation of the behavior of the model with respect to a desired behavior in terms of the number of infected individuals, allowed to identify an adaptive rate of vaccination close to 94%, Vacted by the peak of use of infected (semanas 16 and 38), achieves a behavior inferior to the reference value defined. .
