# Modeling of hysteretic structural systems using multilayer perceptrons and physics-guided technologies

This research develops a methodology for the modelling and identification of hysteretic structural systems, which employs multilayer perceptors and physical principles of structures. This methodology consists of three hysteretic models and their training algorithm, and is based on two models of scientific machine learning called universal ordinary differential equations (UODEs) and physics-guided neural networks (PGNNs). The proposed hysteretic models are UODEs and correspond to motion equations with system state dynamics, where multilayer perceptrons approximate unknown components of dynamics. Training uses the theory of PGNNs and considers the physical data and principles of structures to identify system dynamics and induce those principles in models. The proposed methodology is validated with experimental data from ferrocement walls and recycled plastic wood
