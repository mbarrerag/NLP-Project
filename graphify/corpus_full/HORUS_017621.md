# INSURANCE INJECTION PRACTICES AND DISTRACTIONS DURING THE CLINICAL INSURANCE PRACTICE: THEORICAL ARTICLES

The safe injections (pis) and distractions (Ds) practices during the clinical practice of nurses are considered as one of the six priorities to be investigated to improve safety in the use of medicines in the world. This chapter aims to analyze the concepts of safe injections (pis) and distractions (Ds) practices, and to identify the theoretical, conceptual and empirical links between them, as a contribution to the study of the phenomenon and the understanding of the results. The conceptual-theoretical-empiric system approach (cte) is carried out in two moments: the first corresponds to the organization of knowledge pre-existing to the development of research; the second, to the knowledge generated from the results of the study taking into account what Fawcett proposed.
