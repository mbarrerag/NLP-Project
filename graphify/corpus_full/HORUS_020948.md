# Networks of interaction between plants and bees without sting as a risk factor for virus infection in food niches shared with Apis mellifera in two meliponicultoras farms Cundinamarca.

The production of fruit, vegetables or seeds of 87 of the main world food crops depends on animal pollination, natural pollination services help maintain the diversity and production of crops (Klein et al., 2007). My master thesis project proposes to determine the networks of interaction between the bees Tetragonisca angustula _Apidae: Melitonini), Nannotrigona sp. (Apidae: Meliponini), Apis mellifera (Apidae: Apini) and its floral resources in two farms meliponicultoras of Cundinamarca.
