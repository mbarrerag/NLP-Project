# AIRPORT VARIABLES FOR PRIORIZATION OF CRITICAL POINTS EVALUATED FROM AUDITORS IN VIAL SAFETY

This research aims to compare the order of prioritization of high accidentality critical points by involving pedestrian behavior variables based on information collected through road safety audits processes, complemented by direct observations made in an urban area. The methodology is based on the use of the web application PrioriASV, through which the result is compared taking into account only infrastructural variables (Scenario 1) or including pedestrian behavior variables (Scenario 2). The results show that the methodology and procedure proposed to complement the road safety audit process and the prioritization of critical points can be easily replicated in other cities, as well as it is possible to adapt and adjust the base variables given the existence of special features. It is concluded that pedestrian behavior variables directly influence the order of prioritization, so it is recommended to be considered in the methodological processes and analysis of road safety audits. Key words: road safety audits; road safety sinisterality; pedestrian behaviour; prioritization analysis
