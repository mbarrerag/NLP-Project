# BACKGROUND FOR AN ITINERATING SCHOOL IN MEMORY AND PATRIMONY. RELATIONS TO THE ALIMON

The Latin Speech Al Alimón refers to an act in which two or more people take action together. This chapter is constructed from that logic. It was written as a conversation between its different authors and the public present at the First International Meeting of Culture, Territory and Management in Urban and Rural Local Development; presenting some reflections, constructed in a dialogic way, on the impact of the University's visits to the places of memory located in Granada and San Carlos, municipalities of the East of Antioquia. It represents, as well, a pilot of the dialogic research processes -based on the proposal of Epistemologies of the South - that the Itinerant School intends to implement with the help of the Colombian Network of Places of Memory¿ in different venues of the National University of Colombia
