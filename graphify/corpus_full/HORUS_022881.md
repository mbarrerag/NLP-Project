# Social Being and Domination in Marx's Capital

In this thesis, a reading is made of Marx's first book of Capital, seeking to develop two philosophical problems: the first, of an ontological nature, referring to the status of the social for Marx; the second problem referring to social domination in two dimensions: the domination of the production process over individuals and the domination of the mode of production over society. In this dissertation a reading of Marx's Capital volume one is made in order to develop two philosophical problems: first, an ontological problem related to the status of the social on Marx; secondly, a problem linked to the social domination in two dimensions: the domestication of processes of production over the individual and the domination of mode of production over the society
