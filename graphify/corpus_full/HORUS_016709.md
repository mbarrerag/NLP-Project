# ARRUBLA AND STUDIES ON COLOMBIAN SUB-SOPED

It is a critical evaluation of the writings Mario Arrubla, entitled Studies on Underdevelopment in Colombia, starting from his position as an intellectual of the left in Colombia. His writings are located in the trajectory of the economic history that had been initiated by Luis Eduardo Nieto Arteta, Antonio García and Alejandro López. He has the virtue of having used the model of sectors of accumulation of capital of Marx to the Colombian economy. From that attempt Arrubla concluded that Colombia was a neocolonial and dependent country. The article finally explores compares his work with the book Dependence and Development of Latin America by Cardoso and Faletto, finding its argumentative similarity. Finally, he unleashes some elements of Arrubla’s discourse, such as the dependent character of the Colombian economy, which, seen today, continues to be valid. He also highlights the shortcomings and criticisms made to the work of Arrubla
