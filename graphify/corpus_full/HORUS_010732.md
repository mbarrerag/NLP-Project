# ASSESSMENT OF THE POTENTIAL FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF AN ELECTRO-FENTON PROCESS WITH IN-SITU GENERATION OF H2O2 BY COMMERCIAL ACTIVATE CARBON FIELD

In this work, the capacity of application of an electro-Fenton process (EF) with H2O2 in situ generation was evaluated for the treatment of an industrial textile effluent contaminated with the black acid colourant 194 (NA194) from a primary coagulation-flocculation process (ARnD-CF).
