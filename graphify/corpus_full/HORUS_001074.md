# CIRCULAR ECONOMY IN ARTESANAL PRODUCTION CHANNAS: AN APPROXIMATION OF COLOMBIA CASE

The aim of this study was to analyze the opportunities for the implementation of the circular economy in the Colombian craft sector, first, the main conceptual contributions are described and the importance of this new alternative is recognized in the economies that support ecosystems, maintaining an innovative thinking, which creates new opportunities for reducing the environmental impact. Subsequently, Colombian legislation is analyzed to understand how the implementation has been being encouraged in various industries, and then applied in the craft sector. As a methodology, this study proposes a qualitative approach based on the documentary review of various sources regarding the literature generated on the subject, mainly from 2010 to 2024. It emphasizes the importance of the implementation of the principles of circular economy, mainly through the use of recycled materials and the reuse of waste from agro-industry and other sectors. It is concluded that these initiatives can create high quality products with innovation, with all the cultural and ancestral content and with greater acceptance in the markets while caring for raw materials for the future of new artisans in the communities
