# INCIDENCE OF THE MATERIAL AND MORPHOLOGICAL CONDITIONS OF THE URBAN STRUCTURE AND THE VIAL SPACE OF THE VALLEY OF BURRANTS IN HUMAN COMPORTMENT.

A typological analysis of two blocks on the hillside located in the Aburrá Valley, with descriptive analytical methodology of human behavior patterns in relation to the urban structure and morphology, relevant for the future development of urbanism in conditions of the hillside. The morphology of apples corresponds to a rectangular form, aligned to the street and contiguous one of the other with three floors, each of an average height of 2,50 m., oriented in a southeast direction is northwest, with typical housing constructed in brick, mud tile besides concrete, with slopes of 5.18% to 27% and sections of road from 4.5 m. to 6,45 m. The predominant pedestrian movements are in the north-south and west direction, in a radius of 120 m that connect with services and equipments neighborhood. The estimated duration of stay of a pedestrian structure oscillates between 3 and 5 minutes depending on the type of user in relation to direct solar radiation in the morning and afternoon, besides the absence of public space and furniture for rest during the journeys.
