# DESIGN FOR BEHAVIOR CHANGE APPLIED TO STRATEGIES FOR ADDRESSING OBESITY

The chapter summarizes the experience developed with researchers in design and health, applying behavioral change theories in proposals related to the prevention and reduction of obesity. Two strategies focused on improving decisions in food choice and physical activities were designed, which evidenced the potential of the design applied to health
