# COMPETITIVENESS OF THE COLOMBIAN WINNER SECTOR IN RELATION TO THE FTA WITH THE UNITED STATES, UNDER THE PERSPECTIVE OF THE PORTER FORCES

The research seeks to determine the competitiveness of the livestock sector, taking as a focus the Theory of the Five Forces of Porter, within the framework of the Free Trade Agreement between Colombia and the United States. The impact with the entry into the market of new competitors and products, the bargaining power of buyers, customers, suppliers, in addition to the state of the competitors existing in the Colombian livestock sector, was analyzed, which allows us to know the current situation of the sector and make some recommendations. The results of the research indicate that the threat of entry of new competitors is high, as well as due to substitute products; on the other hand, the bargaining power of the customers is low while, in contrast, that of the suppliers turns out to be high, finally, the rivalry among the existing competitors is high..
