# RECOGNITION AND IMPROVING LOCAL TECHNICALS, CONTRIBUTION TO THE RE-ARRAY AND EMPORATION OF COLOMBIAN COMMUNITIES IN POSCONFLICTO.

The role of memory as an instrument in the reconstruction of communities affected by the armed conflict in Colombia is fundamental, since it contributes to the restoration of their sense of belonging, autonomy and sustainability; contributing to the fabric of a country that seeks to be increasingly supportive and egalitarian.Among many possible strategies, this research program assumed the preservation of the material and intangible heritage of vernacular constructions, as a mechanism to promote the rooting in post-agreement Colombia.In a broad concept of technology, the materiality of the local buildings in relation to their communities is studied and the basic aspects of economic, social and environmental sustainability are identified, as well as social mobility, as an alternative to pauperization, migration or displacement that generates the uprooting in these communities.The research focuses on the characterization and documentation of the traditional construction systems of the local architecture, as well as the knowledge and skills that have been lost in these complex socio-economic and political processes, even when the communities must inhabit another territory that is not their own.The communities affected ones are involved seeking to recognize the value of their own knowledge in new housing or their own improvement, to reconnect their own territory
