# THE WALL OF THE STREET AND THE CENTRIC PERIFERENARY

The mural of the street and the central periphery is the name that comes from the identification of different interactions, forms and structures that are articulated within the daily sphere of transgender sex workers who, from the social organization Open Wardrobe, agency different exercises of identity enunciation and activism in the Calle de las Guapas of the city of Manizales, Caldas. It is worth clarifying that the concept of which we refer here becomes of recognizing that the ideological construct that allows the cohesion of human beings in a socio-cultural network configures through education, the media and politics the construction of forms that crystallize in habitations, places, practices and norms of center, and in turn for a reason of effect, establishes in the disiencia, some habitations, places, practices and bodies of periphery
