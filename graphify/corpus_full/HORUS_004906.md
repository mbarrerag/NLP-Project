# CLIMATE GREE IN THE URBAN PLANNING OF BOGOTÁ

Currently, cities are at the centre of the debate on climate change. Urban governments around the world are implementing ambitious plans to minimize or respond to the risks generated by climate change. This article examines the process and ways of integrating climate change as a field of urban planning in Bogotá. Based on a documentary review and textual analysis, the article argues, on the one hand, that the city is experiencing a climate shift resulting from a relational process where both exogenous and endogenous forces converge. On the other hand, it is argued that the proposed mitigation and adaptation measures move between an established international repertoire and a dose of local experimentation
