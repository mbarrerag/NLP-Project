# Methodological approach to improving the commissioning of new plants by integrating ISO 55000:2014 and ISO 14224:2016, CASE OF STUDY: ELECTRIC GENERATION PLANTS SOLAR PHOTOVOLTICA AND EOLIC

The commissioning of new plants is a fundamental part of the energy production process, the objective of this article is to present a methodology that allows to improve the commissioning of wind and photovoltaic solar plants, for this purpose the standards ISO 55000:2014 and ISO 14224:2016 were taken and with this result a methodology of four phases: Phase 1: ISO Standard 14224 Taxonomy. phase 2: iso standard 31000 criticity. phase 3: methodology rcm (reliability centred maintenance). Phase 4: Contributions of the phases to the requirements of ISO Standard 55001. It is concluded that the application of this methodology improves the commissioning of wind and solar photovoltaic plants
