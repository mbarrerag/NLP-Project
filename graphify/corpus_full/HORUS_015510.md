# EXPERIENCES OF INSURANCE PROFESSIONALS DURING CIVID-IN-COLOMBIA PANDEMIA

Since the emergence of the SARS-COV virus 2/COVID-19, in December 2019, nursing staff together with the rest of the healthcare staff, has carried out its work in the midst of circumstances that are associated with fear, uncertainty, ignorance and physical effort. I weighed this, the health care has continued, demonstrated results and the healthcare professionals have been classified as heroes. It has been an unparalleled opportunity to make the nursing role visible in society. To account for the experiences related to the professional nursing exercise during the pandemic by SARS-COV 2/COVID-19 in Colombia METHODOLOGY: qualitative study, with a phenomenological approach. The population considered were nursing professionals who attended patients COVID Positive in ICU or emergency mechanisms between 2020 and 20221. We performed semi-structured interviews via Google meet. The information was analyzed based on the constant comparison method, identifying, substantive codes, nominals and categories. This study preserves the ethical criteria for health research in Colombia, classified as minimal risk, we guaranteed the confidentiality of the families
