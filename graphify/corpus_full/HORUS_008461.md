# 17.ECHOLOGY RESPONSIBILITY IN ARCHITECTONIC PEDAGOGAGE NATIONAL BULLAR INVESTMENT, MEDELLIN, ATIOCIA

The paper describes several projects developed in the basic cycle project courses at the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters of Medellín, in which attempts were made to incorporate different topics linked to systemic sustainability, through the dialogical chaining between the natural world and that artificial. Projects have been addressed on different scales: from the 1:1 scale of small modules and urban furniture systems, to basic projects of self-sufficient neighborhoods. Experiments were developed that incorporated reflections linked to the issues related to the increase of population and the inhuman growth of cities, territorial destruction, resource scarcity and climate change
