# _x0093_Museomics: an alliance to connect the entomological collections of the National University of Colombia, the Alexander von Humboldt institute and the Pedagogical and Technological University of Colombia, promoting the formation of human capital and the scientific and social projection of the same_x0094_.

Insects guarded in museums or collections of the country are a valuable source of heritage, historical, scientific and cultural information. This information has been relatively unexplored by the lack of recognition and valuation of collections, in addition to the limited availability of economic resources for their management. Such management is necessary to maximize the collection and use of data that derive from the genetic and systematic information that rests on them. This alliance seeks to unite academic and scientific strengths of the National University of Colombia and two external allies, where biological heritage collections are guarded and that also have recognition and trajectory in biological, genetic and entomological studies in the country. It is considered an approximation from the museum and the integrative taxonomy, in three groups of agronomic importance, medical and in conservation of biological diversity, from three orders of insects and four entomological collections. The project seeks to respond based on the study of specimens of all collections and their genomes, questions about evolution, genetic diversity, limits between species and molecular markers for the monitoring of populations of economic importance and in bioprospection.
