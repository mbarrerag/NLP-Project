# MONITORING OF ADVERSE EVENTS IN A CLOSED POPULATION: CONTRIBUTION FROM COLOMBIA TO COVID-19 VACCINES SAFETY

Active pharmacovigilance is an important strategy to reinforce the safety surveillance programs worldwide and to overcome the sub-notification of adverse events. Information on the eectiveness and safety of vaccines for COVID-19 is limited as clinical trials did not provide enough evidence about long-term effects and all possible adverse events. Little is known about the safety profile of the vaccines used in Latinamerica and Colombia. This research has the potential to create high-impact knowledge in public health by allowing comparisons between the safety findings of COVID-19 vaccines and national data, and to generate information for populations not included within the clinical trials
