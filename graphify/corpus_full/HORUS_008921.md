# Recommendation system for planting fruits and vegetables in an agricultural production project under the diversification strategy

Title in English: Recommendation system for planting fruits and vegetables in an agricultural production project under the diversification strategy. Agriculture in Colombia is one of the most economically important sectors, there arises the need for tools that allow the small farmer to face the reality that he inhabits in an optimal way, under the diversification strategy the aim is for the farmer to mitigate the risks that arise. of traditional sowing under monocultures, which has to do with greater economic security when marketing the crops, health in the crops that emerges from the multiple symbiotic relationships that can occur between the spices explored in this work. Once the need to link the diversification strategy to this work is understood, a recommendation system is designed that allows the farmer to determine which crops are suitable for planting. This process is carried out according to a relationship that was made with The different crops are linked to this recommendation system, in this process the planting distances, symbiotic relationships and cycle duration of each crop are taken into account
