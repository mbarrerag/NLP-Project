# Evaluation of the performance of four experimental stages and the respective asphaltic test mixtures, after five years of operation in the road mesh of the city of Bogotá.

In this work, the evaluation of functional and structural performance was carried out, in four road sections in flexible pavements and in particular, of the rolling layers made in modified asphaltic concretes, object of experimentation, elaborated in 2015 by reconstruction of the existing pavements, initially in poor condition and located in different places of the road mesh of the city of Bogotá. The road sections were designed and constructed in 2015, for a period of 10 years, and operation under real transit and natural environmental conditions, including in each one improvement of drainage, subrasing improved with rajón, identical granular materials, asphalt base penetration 60/70 (Refinería de Barrancabermeja, Colombia), rolling layers with different modified asphalts, and with similar production and construction processes, as a result of the financial and technical cooperation, of the first working equipment (the unit of the road of Bogotá and the National University of Colombia), the evaluation of the final working and fluxant oil (as of the last working phase of the first, the first, the second, the second and second, the second, the second, second, second, second, second, second, second, second, second, second, second, third, and third, second, The results of the experimental section located in Barrio Villa Luz presented the lowest performance.
