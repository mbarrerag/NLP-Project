# Detection and localization of tectonic tremors in the NW region of South America and their relationship to regional fault systems

The discovery of low frequency earthquakes, in particular tectonic tremores, reveals the complexity and diversity of seismic sources associated with active deformations in various tectonic environments. Through a multitemporal analysis of continuous records coming from the CARMA network (CARibbean Merida Andes Broadband Experiment), an arrangement of 65 broadband stations extended from the Caribbean Sea in northern Colombia and Venezuela to the interior of the continent that operated between 2016 and 2018, was achieved a preliminary detection of one of the first reports of low frequency earthquakes in the Caribbean region (Colombia). This was achieved by the implementation of the Chao and Yu methodology (2018) for the search for tremors detonated by the passage of surface waves of telesisms. Once identified 5 tectonic tremors generated by the Spirit-Santo, and the failure of the Tremors de Tremors by means of Back-Projection Imáng (BPI).
