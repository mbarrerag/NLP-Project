# Technical evaluation of the exposure of polyethylene terephthalate (PET) to the action of microbial strains and/or enzyme extracts at the laboratory level for degradation purposes

The polyethylene terephthalate (PET) is a plastic commonly used in the textile and packaging industry. It is currently one of the most polluting post-consumption residues in the world due to the fact that it takes centuries to degrade in the environment. As a consequence of the above, the assessment of the degradation of PET following exposure to the action of microbial strains and/or enzyme extracts was carried out in this research.
