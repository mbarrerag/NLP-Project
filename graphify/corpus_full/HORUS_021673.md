# Performance evaluation of a battery-inverter-load system in a laboratory environment

Battery-inverter-load systems are one of the energy solutions for communities in rural areas. Therefore, a performance assessment of those systems can help communities in their selection and operation. The experimental performance evaluation of a battery-inverter-load system in a laboratory is presented in this paper. The system was composed with industrial and commercial equipment in order to observe a close behavior of systems used by rural communities. The laboratory environment allows to use several measurement instruments that are not available in most rural houses.
