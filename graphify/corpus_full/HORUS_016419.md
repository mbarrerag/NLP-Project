# Perception of the development and evaluation system of clinical practices of optometry students of a Bogotá Higher Education Institution Colombia

To describe the perception of development and evaluation system of clinical practices of optometry students of Bogotá, Colombia Methods: A descriptive observational study of cross-sectional non-comparative by means of the application of a survey was carried out. Validation of the survey was carried out and later the application was carried out through the Google Forms tool. Data was tabulated in a previously validated database in Microsoft Excel. A univariate analysis was carried out and its results were reported by means of proportions, means and standard deviations. The answers of 51 students of seventh, eighth and ninth semester of optometry were included, where the majority were women and the average age was 22 years. 9.8% considered that the way in which they were evaluated was not in accordance with the development of the practices, they were not satisfied with the practice sites and did not believe that the form of evaluation was able to increase their capacity. More than 10% were not satisfied with the infrastructure, with the dialogue space when they were evaluated, nor with the feedback provided.
