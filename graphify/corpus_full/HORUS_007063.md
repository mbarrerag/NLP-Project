# Incidence of billable hours in the articulation work - life, an approximation to the legal sector Bogota

The constant search for balance between labor and personal demands has gained significant relevance in contemporary society. In this context, the legal sector emerges as a particularly challenging scenario due to the high professional demands and the intensive nature in time of its operations. This document goes into the intersection of these dynamics, exploring the incidence of billable hours in the working-life interaction within the legal sector. By applying the scales of Work-Family Balance and Interaction Work-Family as a measurement tool and the theoretical perspective of the Roles Theory, this study aims to extract the connections between the time spent in the work reflected by the billable hours and the perception of job-life articulation in professionals belonging to the legal sector using descriptive statistical analysis, difference of means and structured equations for a sample of 185 legal professionals and 75 lawyers. The results aim to visualize not only the challenges faced by these professionals, but also suggest new discussions from the business field towards an appropriate and sustainable enrichment between their work and personal responsibilities .
