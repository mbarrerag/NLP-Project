# Development of creativity in the initiation of guitar from Latin American music for adolescents aged 12-17 years belonging to the Orquest@rte Guitarras Group, of the Marcato Foundation

The present research work entitled "Developing creativity in the initiation to the guitar from Latin American music for adolescents between 12 and 17 years old belonging to the Orquest@rte Guitarras Group, of the Marcato Foundation" aims to explore and promote musical creativity in guitar students in initiation stage. These students, who are part of a specialized educational program of the Marcato Foundation, are at a crucial stage of their musical and personal development. The research is based on the integration of a Latin American music repertoire and the composition of new works for guitar, seeking to provide an educational framework that promotes the technical and creative development of the students. This approach not only focuses on the acquisition of technical skills, but also on the ability of young musicians to express creatively through their instrument, thus enriching their experience and musical training. The research developed under a research-creation model, which combines theory and artistic practice.
