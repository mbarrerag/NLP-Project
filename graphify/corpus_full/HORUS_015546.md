# CONFESSION AND PENITENCE IN THE NEW GRANADA KINGDOM APPROXIMATION TO AN INEDITOUS ITEM WITH SUPPORT FROM THE CASE OF NEW SPAIN.

The article shows how confession and penance were presented in the New Kingdom of Granada in the second half of the sixteenth century, that is, in the beginnings of Spanish colonial consolidation. The chapter is guided by the experience of Mexican historiography in the same subject. The article presents the sacrament of penance in the first years of evangelization, as well as the difficulties it had to carry out, in practice, what was indicated from the doctrine should be done so that individuals would fulfill confession and penance
