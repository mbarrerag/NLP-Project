# Motivations, professions and perspectives on the future: analysis of educational trajectories of young people

Abstract The article presents the analysis of the educational trajectories of young people in relation to their social position and disposition, defined by the perspective of future time of each student, in relation to their realization in university life. The article takes a sample of 968 young people, resulting from the selection of students who presented the State tests Saber 11 (year 2006) and Saber Pro (years 2012 to 2018). The study is longitudinal panel type. A regression analysis of categorical variables is carried out, obtaining as a result that, although there is a direct influence on personal motivations in the selection of professional careers, there is a low probability that students will succeed in studying in the desired careers
