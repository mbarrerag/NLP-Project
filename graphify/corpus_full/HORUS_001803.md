# The Tales of Roberto Bolaño: the redefinition of an emblematic genre in Latin America

This thesis outlines a poetic on Roberto Bolaño’s storywork by means of the analysis of his three story books: Telephone calls (1997), Murderable Putas (2001) and Insufferable Gaucho (2003). The research proposes the redefinition of this literary genre from the break with the aesthetic norm consecrated by the contemporary authors to the Latin American boom. In the first instance, the axiological profile of the author is analyzed in order to place it within the literary field and to support the interpretation of his work. Secondly, the renewal of the artistic form proposed by Bolaño in his short stories is studied. Following this, the axiological assessment of the author about the identity of the Latin American subject who has experienced terror within the framework of dictatorships and violence in Latin America during the second half of the twentieth century.
