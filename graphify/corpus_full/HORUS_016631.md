# Suspect of delirium and possible related factors in the hospitalized elderly

The objective: to describe the suspicion of delirium in elderly adults hospitalized in internal medicine and its related factors. cross-sectional and convenience-sampled correlational study, with 49 adults over 70 years of hospitalized in internal medicine who accepted to participate voluntarily. The Delirium Detection Scale by Nursing (Nu-DESC), which measures the suspicion of delirium, was applied, and descriptive and bivariate analysis was used. the prevalence of suspicion of delirium in the study patients was 20.4 %.
