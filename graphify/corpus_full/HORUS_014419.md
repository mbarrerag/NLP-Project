# Characterization of a Colombian asphalt modified with polymers and evaluation of the effect on its rheological properties.

The use of materials that modify the properties of asphalts is a recognized practice thanks to which it represents a solution for the adaptation of the material to different project conditions. The limited offer of asphalts in Colombia is a condition for the need for the implementation of modified asphalts. A recognized material used in the construction of pavements are the SBS polymers, they have presented great results worldwide when modifying the asphalt and respectively to asphaltic mixtures. The research aims to evaluate the effect on the rheological properties of the incorporation of a SBS polymer in a Colombian asphalt 60/70.
