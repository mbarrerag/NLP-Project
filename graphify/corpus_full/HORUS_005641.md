# Bring the stick and sing! Daily life, experiences and representations of the family's affective musical practices Prieto Monroy

This study seeks to understand the configuration of musical practices in the daily life of the Prieto Monroy family. The research involved observing the nodes and networks between actors, places and spaces, objects and actions; identifying patterns and routines of listening, speech and interpretation; and analyzing in a complex way the musical practices of the family. The case study around the music that happens with and in the Prieto Monroy family is presented as a micro social research that addresses the private space, written in the first person. Thus, it aims to interpelle and be a point of perspective and reflection on how the musical practice has been understood from the cultural studies and the social sciences.
