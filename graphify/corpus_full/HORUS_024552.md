# POLICIES OF THE mirARATION IN PROCESSING MUSEALIZATION OF THE MEMORY OF THE ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA.

This research project created investigates the modes of production of the memory of the Colombian armed conflict from the visuality in the specific case of three exhibitions: The witness, memories of the armed conflict in the lens and voice of Jesus Abbot Colorado, Fragments of Doris Salcedo, and the permanent room Memory and Nation of the National Museum of Colombia. These are echoes of the so-called boom of memory (Hyussen 2002, Gush, 2014, Wechsler, 2015, Larralde Armas; Salvatori; & Diz, 2018, Arboleda; Morales Herrera, 2016). These exhibitions involve visual and verbal discourses, representations, images and imaginarys, looks, acts of seeing, spatiality, subjectivity, bodyliness and power. Through them I am interested in exploring and producing sensitive knowledge around institutions, visual regimes, the place of the audience, among others, contributing to the understanding of how certain constructions of the social through visuality are normalized. I intervene from critical bets of artistic studies, in inter-epist dialogues between visual studies and feminist approaches
