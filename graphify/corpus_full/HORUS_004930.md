# PRINCIPLES OF AUDIT REHABILITATION

For hearing persons, the meaning of the ear is a fundamental part of the sensory experience, since it allows to interact with others and understand the world, enjoy music, art and sounds of nature. However, the loss of hearing can become a communication barrier that affects social participation and negatively impacts the quality of the listener's life. The impact of hearing loss on the everyday life of a listener manifests itself in a limitation to carry out activities related to communication, which, in turn, results in a restriction on participation in different social scenarios. For this reason, speaking of hearing loss also implies understanding what communication means, altered states of communication (desordens) and alternatives of intervention. Hearing rehabilitation has been a field of study in constant evolution, driven by the growing understanding of the importance of hearing in the daily life of the people and the technological advances in the diagnosis and treatment of hearing loss. Beyond making an approach from a structural-biological component, it involves analyzing and understanding how an auditory alteration affects the communication of an individual. From the phonoaudiological perspective, the human beings are related to the social beings and their relationship with the hearing. the collection of results of different research carried out between 2012 and 2022 in the Colombian School of Rehabilitation, research that developed students and professors researchers of the programs of Phonoaudiology of the specialization in Audiology and that constitute a line of interest of the group Human Capacities, Health and Inclusion, of the Audiology group and related areas of the National University of Colombia, to highlight the understandings around the hearing rehabilitation, the approach of scientific evidence and the experiences of patients and to show how the hearing rehabilitation can transform the life of people with hearing loss, and thus allow them to re-enjoy the sounds that were familiar to them and give them hope in their journey towards a quality hearing
