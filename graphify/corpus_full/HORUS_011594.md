# Diseases of Work in School Teachers: A Systematic Review

Teaching is one of the main professions linked to the development of a society; like any work, it is exposed to different risk factors that can generate a disease of labor origin. Identify and characterize the current literature that presents the main diseases of school teachers as a result of their work and/or working conditions. systematic search with search in the databases academic search complete (ebscohost), apa - psycnet, eric (education resources information center), medline (pubmed), scopus and open grey. Independent peer selection is carried out under inclusion criteria and analysis of narrative form, including generalities, disease information and outcomes in teachers. 47 studies were included, of which 25.5 % were published in 2015; Brazil was the country with the highest degree of contribution to the work. Most of the studies grouped school teachers without distinction of their cycle (primary, high school and middle school). The main diseases identified were disorders of the report 51,1 %, musculoskeletals 23.4% and psychosocial conditions were identified by 21,3 %.
