# PRODUCTION AND IMPLEMENTATION OF A SYSTEM OF PSEUDOVIRAL ENTITIES FOR THE STUDY OF THE INFECTION OF SARS-COV-2 IN A TEST IN VITRO OF INVASION TO HUMAN CELLS

SARS-CoV-2 is a virus that in its detection and quantification represents a health risk, due to its biosecurity levels 3. As a solution, the use of pseudoviruses with a lower biosecurity level requirement is proposed, allowing studies to be carried out as if they were counted on the virus. Construction of SARS-CoV-2 pseudovirus by plasmid packaging system. Cell transfection in line HEK 293T. Identification of recombinant protein on cell surface by flow cytometry. The construction of the SARS-CoV-2 pseudovirus was performed using three plasmids: the one of wrap that expresses the S protein, the packaging plasmid that encapsulates the RNA and the transfer plasmid that expresses the proteins involved in the pseudovirus. The expression of the recombinant protein on the surface of 293T cells by flow cytometry is detected and a trial is implemented for the study of the SA-CoV-2 infection process to the cell
