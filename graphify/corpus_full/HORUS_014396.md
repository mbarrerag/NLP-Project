# Adherence monitoring and clinical outcomes related to the clinical standard based on evidence for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the obese patient in the National University Hospital: cross-sectional cohort study, prior to dissemination and implementation of the standard.

Objective To determine the baseline adherence and the economic outcomes related to the “Clinical Standard Based on Evidence (ECBE) for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the obese patient in the National University Hospital of Colombia” of patients treated with this condition in 2021. Methods A retrospective descriptive observational study of cross-section was performed based on a sample of patients from administrative databases of the National University Hospital (HUN) in 2021. Socioeconomic and clinical data were obtained for patients with obesity and a baseline adherence to the clinical standard based on evidence for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of the obese patient in the National University Hospital was determined by calculating a global compliance index (IGC) based on 7 indicators that allow to evaluate the control points defined in this study. In addition, a cost analysis of the services used by these patients was performed Results A sample size of 118 patients with a diagnosis of obesity was calculated CIE-10 in the third hospital database of 2021 attended in external consultation. However, its diagnosis was confirmed in 98 patients only when the review of the clinical histories of the same patients was performed. o remission by bariatric surgery of all patients with BMI greater than or equal to 40 was achieved in 14.29% (95% CI: 0% - 44.44%). The seventh and last indicator related to pharmacological treatment in patients with BMI greater than or equal to 30, was met in 36.73% (95% CI: 25.51% - 44.9%). Finally, a global compliance rate of 40.35% BEC (95% CI: 34.91% - 45.47%) was found. On average, the care of a patient with a diagnosis of obesity during the year 2021 was $173,318,5 Colombian pesos. Conclusion The objective of the study was to evaluate the baseline status of obese patient care in the HUN in order to monitor the implementation of the recommendations of the obesity BEC. A GCI of 40.35% (95% CI: 34.91% - 45.47%) was found during the period evaluated prior to the implementation of the BEC. Therefore, strategies are required by the NU to ensure greater adherence to the recommendations of this BECE and to offer comprehensive care to obese patients.
