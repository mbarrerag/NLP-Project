# CHARACTERISTICS OF THE INTENSIVE POST CARE SYNDROME: SCOPE REVIEW

to identify the available evidence of the characteristics of the post-intensive care syndrome in the patient who leaves the intensive care unit. scope review with the methodology of Joanna Briggs Institut, in five databases with the search strategy ([PostIntensive AND Care AND Syndrome]). Primary studies published between 2010 and 2020, available in full text, were included. after analysis of the 48 primary studies, six thematic categories were extracted as follows: cognitive aspect in the post-ICU syndrome, osteomuscular, psychoemotional, prevention strategies with models of ICU survivors, post-unit intensive care syndrome in the family and evaluation tools. to improve the quality of care of patients presenting this syndrome, prevent the appearance and increase the quality of life provided to ICU survivors and their families. Evidence reports that among caregivers different disorders can be triggered that decrease their quality of life. Nursing plays a crucial role in preventing the onset of the syndrome through strategies and interdisciplinary outcomes can be avoided
