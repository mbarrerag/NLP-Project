# Tools for the construction of the dream territorial sustainability in the Campesino Reserve Zone of Venice Part Alta

This research presents a diagnostic-participative tool to promote territorial sustainability in the ZRC (ZRC) of Venice Upper Part, Cundinamarca. The territorial sustainability was understood as the configuration of social and ecological systems adaptive that fulfill the aspirations of the people who inhabit each territory through processes of collective action and dispute. Initially, through the dialogue the dreams of the ZRC were constructed collectively, in order to generate a veredal tool to evaluate the level of territorial sustainability of the ZRC and propose strategies of action. As a result, 34 indicators organized in 5 attributes were obtained: Welfare, Education and Arraigo, Economic Autonomy, Organizational Autonomy and Environmental Care. Subsequently, the pilot was applied in a road of the ZRC, where it was found that this is characterized by strong leaderships that seek to survive as a peasant economy in the midst of precarious conditions of well-being, migration of the youth, dynamics of economic dependence and a positive relationship with ecosystems. s the level of territorial sustainability of the ZRC and to propose strategies for action.
