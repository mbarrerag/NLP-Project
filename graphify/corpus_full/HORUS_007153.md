# Critical approach to the labour law and social security system in the treatment of endometriosis in working women in Colombia. 1993-2024

This qualitative study focuses on three key concepts to understand the problem posed in relation to endometriosis as a diagnosed disease. First, it examines the Social Security System in Health in Colombia, a crucial component of the context of medical care. Second, it addresses endometriosis as a central concept to be able to identify this exclusively female and high prevalence disease. Third, it analyzes the gender gap in the health sector, a significant problem related to the late diagnosis of the disease and its negative consequences specifically for the female population of the country. From the quantitative point of view, it is carried out the analysis of data obtained through three surveys that seek to establish the knowledge of men and women in the endometriosis, diagnosis and treatment .
