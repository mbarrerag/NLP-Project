# Design and manufacture of a wind turbine rotor using fique fibre reinforced composite materials.

The micro generation of wind energy is one of the possible solutions to bring energy to the Non-Interconnected Zones (ZNI) to the Colombian Caribbean energy network. To reduce manufacturing costs and encourage the use of wind energy of these ZNI, this thesis aims to design and manufacture the rotor of a low scale turbine using cheap composite materials reinforced with fique fibers. The wind turbine designed by WindAid, an NGO that installs wind energy in rural communities of Peru, was used as a starting point to meet the objectives of the the thesis. The geometric design of the rotor was optimized using an analytical model capable of increasing the aerodynamic power generated. On the other hand, the critical conditions of load of the rotor were established in La Guajira, following the recommendations of the British Standard 61400-2. The first approximation of the structural design of the rotor was made using the method of the finite elements MEF and using traditional composite materials with fiber of glass and carbon. Once manufactured, the structural design was validated experimentally using a standardized material flexion instrument in the alabes, obtaining differences of the 7% with the numerical model
