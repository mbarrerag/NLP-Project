# PHENOTYPIC INTRAPULATION VARIATION IN QUINOA FROM THE DEPARTMENT OF BOYACA, COLOMBIA. INTRAPOBLATIONAL PHONOTIPACION IN FIVE IN THE DEPARTMENT OF BOYACA, COLOMBIA

The quinoa (Chenopodium quinoa Wild.) is an Andean crop, native to the South American Andes, with great agronomic, industrial and pharmaceutical potential and also with a great capacity to tolerate adverse environmental factors. In Colombia, currently, it is cultivated in the departments of Nariño, Cauca, Cundinamarca and Boyacá. It presents a great genetic variation, both at the molecular and morphological level, which has been little documented. In Boyacá there are few studies of morphological characterization of cultivated materials and there is no certified seed material, so farmers sow a mixture of materials. Qualitative and quantitative descriptors and an analysis of main components and grouping were used to characterize the structure of intrapopular phenotypic variation of the materials of quinoa Blanca Jericho, which are cultivated in the department of Boyacá. The analysis of main components explained more than 70% of the observed variation, being the most variable characteristics AP, LP, DP, LHS and AHS.
