# Analysis of possible alternativeas productive in tone to the conservation of the páramo de Pisba in the framework of the law on moors – case Socha Boyaca

The consumption and production needs of the industry have accelerated the deterioration of the natural environment, leading to global uncertainty about the medium and long-term sustainability of the economic and social system. In this sense, the ecosystem of the Páramo, which is unique in South America, is considered strategically important, among other things because of its ability to produce water and feed large animal populations, as well as its connection with other ecosystems. Although these areas are considered strategically important, they have been inhabited in the past by people who exercised productive occupations such as mining or intensive agriculture, which reduced the capacity to extract the sought-after water resources against the background of global warming, climate change and the growing global precipitation deficit, which represents a conflict between the conservation of ecosystems and the economic alternatives for the inhabitants of these ecosystems.
