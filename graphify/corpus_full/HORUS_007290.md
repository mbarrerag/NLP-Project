# Description of the perception and knowledge of patients diagnosed with dementia and medical specialists about the anticipated will in artificial nutrition in advanced stages of dementia in a hospital using grounded theory, Bogotá 2024

The decision-making on artificial nutrition in patients with dementia, specifically in advanced stages of the disease, always raises ethical and medical problems in the care of these patients, in this work attempts to obtain the opinion and perception of patients with dementia at an early stage on aspects such as problems perceived in the course of the disease, artificial nutrition and anticipated wills. Likewise, the opinions and perceptions of physicians specialists in clinical neurology and geriatrics on approach to dysphagia, artificial nutrition and anticipated wills in patients with dementia were explored. Through semistructured interviews, data were collected that were codified and analyzed following the principles of the theory founded. This allowed to develop general categories and subcategories that offer a view on the care of people with dementia
