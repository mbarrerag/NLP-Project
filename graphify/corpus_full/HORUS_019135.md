# VALIDATION AND ADOPTION OF A LIST OF CASH TO EVALUATE INSURANCE PRACTICES

Safe injection practices are a set of actions aimed at preserving the sterility of liquids and medicines, reducing the risk of puncture of healthcare personnel and preventing the elimination of injection devices inappropriately. Adapting and validating the instrument Revised injection safety assessment tool (Tool C¿ Revised) to be used in the Colombian population. Methodological study that included cultural adaptation, validation of appearance and content (mandated Lawshe model) of an instrument that measures the attributes of safe injection practice, carried out between June 2018 and January 2019. This adaptation resulted in the reduction of 73 to 42 items distributed in three sections: Observations of the institutions, composed of 13 items; Practical observations of injection; and Observations of Phlebotomys increasing consisting of 29 items. Of all items, the drafting of 20 was improved, 28 were merged and 11 were eliminated because they did not apply to the Colombian health system
