# The creation of constitutional courts in Colombia, a reform necessary to guarantee a constitutionalist due process in the process of guardianship actions.

The present thesis was proposed as a general objective to demonstrate the importance and need for institutionalization of constitutional courts for the review of guardianship actions in Colombia; the above is understood that one of the problems that mainly affects justice is the judicial congestion, with the prolific presentation of amparo actions being one of the causes that has led to the aggravation of this historical problem. To this end, invoking a descriptive socio-legal methodology and a mixed approach based on the documentary review, the present thesis will demonstrate the role of the amparo action in the logistic oversaturation of the courts, proposing as a strategy to mitigate this anomaly the creation of specialized courts in the care of protection actions in the first and second instance
