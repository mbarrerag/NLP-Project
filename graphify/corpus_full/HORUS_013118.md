# Adding biochar to anaerobic digestion for adequate recovery of residual biomass

The anaerobic digestion is a natural process by which the microorganisms degrade the organic matter, this occurs in the absence of oxygen and its products are biogas (CH4, CO2 and traces of other gases) and a watery phase of suspended solids.The operation of these systems is strongly linked to the stability of the environment in which the microorganisms coexist, for this purpose the addition of carbonous materials such as biochar has been investigated.The biochar is the product of the thermochemical conversion of the biomass in the absence of oxygen, its addition to the anaerobic digestion yield allows to increase the buffer capacity of the medium, besides that it decreases the concentration of inhibitors and functions as support for the formation of biopelicles.In the development of this work the possible improvements in the performance, the quality and stability of the biogas obtained by the use of the microgas in the use of the Paerobic digestion, the organicity of the organic system was obtained by means of abometric digestion.The biochar was obtained by means of slow pyrolysis from the palm and waste of prun, then it was used in laboratory scale tests and in the tests of the organic digestion and in the test of Finally, the positive effects of the addition of palm cusco biochar in the anaerobic digestion of cellulose and restaurant residues are identified by the following: the positive effects of the addition of palm cuesco biochar biochar in the anaerobic digestion of cellulose and restaurant residues on both laboratory-scale PBM and pilot-scale UASB systems. .
