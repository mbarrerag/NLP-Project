# BIOTECHNOLOGIC PRODUCTION OF MICROBIAN DEXTRANO AND ITS IMPLEMENTATION IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF FUNCTIONAL FOODS

The product of several researches in the Institute of Biotechnology (IBUN) and in collaboration with the Institute of Science and Food Technology ICTA of the National University of Colombia, was able to develop the biotechnological acquisition of a biopolymer type dextrane, using the native microorganism: Leuconostoc mesenteroides IBUN 51.2.58 at a laboratory, pilot and industrial scale. It was achieved to increase the efficiency and production of the biopolymer by two independent bioprocesses: production of the enzyme dextransacarosa (Dsa) by biotransformation and enzyme reaction respectively, decreasing fermentation times and increasing enzymatic activity, establishing this biotechnological production as one of the fastest reported industries (4.5 h).Dextrane was characterized by its molecular structure and functional activity as prebiotic fiber, parallel research was developed in terms of inclusion and application of biopolymer in various chemical, pharmaceutical and food lines.Due to its reologic and functional behavior, dextrane was possible to include in various food matrices such as: dairy, products based on flour, fruit and vegetables
