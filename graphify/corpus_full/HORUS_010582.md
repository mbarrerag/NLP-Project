# Urban quality in the street from the potentialities of the plot: Case of 45th Street Bogotá, Colombia

The purpose of this final master's work is to identify those key variables that allow to grant urban quality to the street, by positively influencing the perception of the pedestrian to generate a pleasant sense of independence and autonomy, having as main base the dynamics that each road hierarchy presents within the urban plot. As cities grow and new projects are constructed, the roads that structure the urban fabric tend to change their dynamics and hierarchy within the city, so the problem of loss of urban quality within the existing streets arises from the inadequate response of the morphological elements of the street composed by the apple, the properties and the buildings as they are not modified according to the conditions of the plot.
