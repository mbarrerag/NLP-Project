# HONDA'S BALANCE: A PLACE-ACTANT IN COLOMBIA'S STORY

Abstract This article deals with the case of the `Salto de Honda¿, also known as Honda rapids, a crucial place in the history of Colombia through the Magdalena River. The objective is to demonstrate the agency of this actant-place, then contributing to the construction of the environmental history of this important river. To achieve this, the narratives that have spoken or represented the ¿Salto de Honda¿ were analyzed, based on primary, historical and institutional sources, as well as emerging from the memory of the historical ones, physiogn and inhabitants of the Salto. The narrative construction of the agency of Honda is present as a methodological proposal to represent the historical of the history of the river, physiognities and inhabitants of the Salto
