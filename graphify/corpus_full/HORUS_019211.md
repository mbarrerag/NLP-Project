# Reinforcement of brick walls in republican constructions using external confinement with metallic elements (CELAM).

Preservation of cultural goods is fundamental to keep history alive and is considered a global need. Among the cultural goods of interest is a significant number of buildings of the republican period (1880-1930), whose predominant structural system are the walls of simple brick masonry and lime mortar (Molina and Hinojosa, 2011).Many of these buildings, have high seismic vulnerability and some of them have been reinforced to meet the current structural requirements; however, it is necessary to develop methods of reinforcement more efficient and aesthetically less invasive.In response to this need, this research focuses on determining the improvement in the behavior before lateral loads in the plane of masonry walls of the republican period by strengthening them by means of the External Confinement with Metal Elements (CELAM).The investigation is mostly experimental and is complemented by numerical simulations in the elastic range with finite elements.In the experimental part a type of mortar based on calcareous elements has been developed as well as a short term has mechanical properties similar to those of the wall used in the walls of republicans
