# INCIDENCE OF CIVID-19 AMONG CHILDREN IN BOGOTA DURING THE PERIOD JULY AND NOVEMBER, 2020

To estimate the incidence of acute respiratory symptoms and Covid-19 in children in public schools in Bogotá during the second half of 2020. telephone survey conducted in more than 5,000 schoolchildren. Information was collected from a medical background and use of health services. Descriptive statistics were used. A total of 151,470 people-days were counted for an incidence rate of 157,8 x 100,000 people, almost three times what was reported by the official surveillance system of the city. We found deficiencies in the diagnosis and consultation of children when comparing them with the general population. More research is needed to clarify the true burden of disease in the child population
