# A story without words: reading the world of disability from the area of Physical Education

This research project aims to enrich the educational inclusion processes of the population with disabilities, by designing a pedagogical strategy in the area of physical education, which provides support and strengthens the articulation with social skills, by promoting the interweaving of these competences and by providing greater solidity to the life projects of students with cognitive disabilities. One of the most interesting findings of this research was the strengthening from the area of physical education, not only of the social skills, but also of the skills for life and functional communication, which are reflected in the same way in the results of each moment of the pedagogical strategy. It is important to emphasize that in students with disabilities there is a greater need for the development of thought, interpretation of actions, management of elements of the environment and understanding of language in context. Hence, the need for curricular flexibility, a pedagogical articulation of social competences, which allows for transforming the academic experience of students with cognitive disabilities of the ninth grade, in the College of Bolivia. .
