# Inhabiting conservation: tensions, resistances and challenges in Protected Natural Areas: case study of the Integrated Management District of the Thames Garden Knife

The Protected Natural Area (ANP) Integrated Management District (DMI) Cutilla Garden Támesis located in the southwest of the department of Antioquia, was declared in order to conserve the ecosystem under a predominantly biophysical vision, since in relation to social studies, it only integrates archaeological, ethnic and socioeconomic factors, leaving aside the territorial and cultural interests of local actors, making the processes of participation that it promotes insufficient. This case that not only occurs in Colombia but in different places of the world, has triggered multiple socio-ecological conflicts that put at risk the environmental conservation. For this reason, this research has the objective to contribute to the analysis of these conflicts from the case study of this National Environmental Protection Agency (NPA), which allows us to show that the integration of biophysical, social and broad understanding of the human habitat in areas of conservation can transform this situation.
