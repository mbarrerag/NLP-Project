# Construction, boom and crisis of local organizational joints in the city of Medellín and national of the Afro-descendant ethnic movement in Colombia, 1990 – 2016

The purpose of this doctoral work is to reveal the processes of construction, boom and crisis of local organizational joints in the city of Medellin and national of the Afro-descendant ethnic movement in Colombia between 1990 and 2016. From the understanding of ethnic subjectivities of African descent, understood as the processes of shaping practices of thinking, feeling and doing with the ability to challenge ethnic identities in the management of dissent, difference, desire, impulses, instincts, interest, language, egos, affectivity, and other subjective forms in the organizational universe of the Afro-descending political field. . The purpose of this doctoral work is to unveil the processes of construction, boom and crisis of the local organizational articulations, influence, nationality of the Afro-descending organization, nationality of the ethnic movement in Colombia between 1990 - 2016
