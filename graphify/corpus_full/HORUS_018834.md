# Two versions (1911 and 1947) of the ballet Petrushka by Igor Stravinsky. Comparative analysis of orchestration, compositional structure and the effects of these changes on the performance of the conductor

This paper presents a comparative analysis of Petrushka from various sources, a first approach in a general way by studying the compositional structure between the original version of 1911 and the edited version of 1947; then a detailed observation of the most relevant changes in orchestration, identifying in this way the different textures, colors or sensations that Igor Stravinsky sought at the time of the new revision; concluding finally the effects that these changes produce in the performance of the conductor. . This project aim aim to expose a comparative analysis of Petrushka from different points of view.
