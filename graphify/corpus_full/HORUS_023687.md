# GUIDE FOR CARE OF CRITICAL ADVENTURE PATIENTS IN AMERICAS. SUMMARY, VERSION 3

This third summary version of the Guidelines for the Care of Critical Adult Patients with COVID-19 in the Americas was prepared with the aim of facilitating the consultation of recommendations for the management of critical adult patients with COVID-19 treated in intensive care units. It offers evidence-based recommendations for the identification of markers and risk factors for mortality of critical patients, control of infection, sampling, assistance to patients (ventilatory and hemodynamic), pharmacological treatment, early rehabilitation, the use of diagnostic images, prevention of complications and discharge criteria. The recommendations are addressed to all health care personnel who care for patients in emergency and emergency services and intensive care units. They are also aimed at providing support to decision makers and members of government entities related to the management of patients with COVID-19 in intensive care units in the Region of the Americas. The guide is in continuous process of updating the evidence in order to provide the most up-to-date recommendations for the management of critical patients with COVID-19, in particular on the use of immunological antibiotics and in the intensive care units of the Americas
