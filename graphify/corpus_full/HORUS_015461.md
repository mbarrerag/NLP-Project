# TOWARDS A COMMUNITY PERCEPTION OF CLIMATE CHANGE AND VARIABILITY IN SAN ANDRÉS DE TUMACO, COLOMBIA

The perception information on climate change concepts and climate adaptation strategies was collected from a sample of the urban population of San Andrés de Tumaco, in order to identify the knowledge base in relation to the topics associated with climate change and territorial public policies. It was evidenced that perception varies according to each age range and that the community recognizes many of the negative impacts of climate change as well as the desire to take part in actions to adapt to this phenomenon. A territory with environmental and social characteristics such as Tumaco requires further studies of this type and the evaluation of national, departmental and local efforts to advance in terms of climate adaptation
