# Search for active substances with neuroprotective potential for the treatment of Alzheimer's disease from a species of the genus Zanthoxylum caribaeum (Rutaceae).

Alzheimer's disease (EA) is the most common dementia, a neurodegenerative disorder of a multifactorial character characterized by the presence of amyloids plaques, neurofibrillary ovyls, reduction of cholinesterase activity, oxidative stress, among other mechanisms. Despite the investment in research during the last decades, it is considered that the research should take new approaches, search for new biological targets and develop new drugs, that is why this work is carried out the search and characterization of compounds with multi-diana activity from natural products, taking into account that previous studies of the research groups demonstrated the biological activity and the potential neuroprotective protection of species of the Rutaceae family and particularly those belonging to the genus Zanthoxylum of the Colombian flora, which have antioxidant effects, the agonist model of LXR and cholinesterase inhibitors. The objective of this work was to perform a search of neuroprotector agents from the species Z. caribaeum. Initially, an extract was obtained by means of etanolic maceration in the root, then the type of metabolites present using the techniques of the ZX and the caretherase, the protection against lipid peroxidation and inhibitory activity of butyryl cholinesterase; from these fractions and by chemical isolation directed, the compound 10H-furan [3,2-a] carbazole, the compound 5,7 -dimetoxy-4H-chromen-4-one, and a mixture of sterols containing stigmasterol and β-sitosterol were obtained. The compound 10H-furan [3,2-a] carbazole presented agonist activity of LXR, neuroprotective effect and antiplatelet activity of Aβ was observed; the compound 5.7 -dimetoxy-4H-cromen-4-one was first reported in this species, presented neuroprotective effect and antiplatelet activity of Aβ; on the other hand, the mixture of stigmasterol and β-sitosterol sterol sterol sterol sterol presented agonistic activity of LXR, neuroprotective effect and antiplatelet activity of Aβ. Our results allow us to conclude that both fractions and compounds isolated from Z. caribaueum present a potential for the therapeutic use
