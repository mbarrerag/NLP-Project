# Influence of the implementation of the West Regiotram on the consolidation of the urbanization and metropolization process of the municipalities of Funza, Mosquera, Madrid and Facatativá

The provision of regional-scale transport solutions to improve the mobility conditions of the population - which carries out activities beyond the limits of the city - favours the metropolization processes. This research seeks to analyze how urban growth has occurred in the municipalities that are part of the layout of the West Regiotram project, for which satellite images and processing based on Geographic Information Systems -SIG were used, allowing to describe how the urban occupation and expansion of this territory has occurred and, likewise, to make an approach on the possible effects of the implementation of this regional-scale project. .
