# Adherence to medical treatment in chronic patients: use of positive airway pressure therapy

Lack of adherence to medical treatment is a chronic and complex problem affecting patients of all ages and with different types of diseases. Adherence to medical treatment, which refers to a patient's ability to follow the recommendations prescribed by a doctor or health care provider,1 is essential to achieve complete recovery and improve the quality of life of patients. However, this is not effective in many cases and represents a major problem worldwide, as it is estimated that only 50% of patients follow treatment recommendations consistently.2,3 This may have serious consequences for health, such as increased likelihood of complications, higher hospitalization rate and higher cost of medical care.3,4
