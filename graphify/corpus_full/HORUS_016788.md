# Identification of infections and immune changes in patients with adverse reactions to modeling substances in the city of Cali Colombia.

In the search for modeling substances (biopolymers) capable of providing volume and generating aesthetic changes without surgical intervention, several materials have been used, such as petroleum-derived chemicals, of plant and synthetic origin which can produce adverse reactions such as infectious, inflammatory and autoimmune processes. To identify infections and immunological changes in patients with adverse reactions to the use of modeling substances in the city of Cali. It is a cross-sectional and descriptive study in which tissue samples were processed for microbiological analysis and total blood and serum samples for immunological analysis of 113 patients with adverse events to the use of biopolymers in the city of Cali during 2019-2021. Microbic isolations were identified in 22 patients (19.4%), 15 (13.2%) patients presented mono-microbian infections and 7 (6.9%) patients presented antimicrobian infections in the sample, the frequency of the isolates identified was Bacillus cereus (n=8, 36.3%), Mycobacterium fortuituum type 2 (n=5, 22.7%) and Pseudomones of the sample)
