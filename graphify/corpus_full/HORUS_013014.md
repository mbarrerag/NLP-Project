# Care for the person with cancer and neutropenia

This work was carried out in response to a problem of nursing practice by having been carried out in the framework of a product required from the post-gradual formation of nursing master's degree with depth in oncology, seeking to contribute and transfer knowledge to society. The work is oriented to the education required by adult patients with acute lymphoblastic leukaemia of B cells CD 19 positive with negative Philadelphia chromosome in relapse or refractariness to treatment or with minimal residual positive disease and requiring treatment with Blinatumomab. The aim was to develop a practical guide of care aimed at the caregivers of these patients with the intention of providing tools from the knowledge that allow to know about the disease, treatment, identification of signs and symptoms associated with adverse events by the administration of the Blinatumomab described in the same guide, to prevent or reduce the occurrence of fatal outcomes in this population of patients. It was counted with the participation of professional nurses and caregivers of patients who have administered and received therapy with Blinatumomab respectively to identify the needs in education from the professional practice and the development of the care.
