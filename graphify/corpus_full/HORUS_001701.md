# Valorization of rice husks through a biorefinery strategy and environmental impact determination through process life cycle analysis

Due to the wide availability of the rice husk, its low cost as a byproduct of rice processing, the current inadequate disposition and its underutilization, arises the need for its recovery. Therefore, in this study it is proposed to apply a biorefining strategy to transform the rice husk into metabolites of added value such as xylitol and glucose, in addition to the realization of a life cycle analysis (CVA) to determine the environmental impact of the proposed biorefining process. To this end, the effect of the particle size and acid hydrolysis time for the production of xylose was evaluated, and subsequently the effect of the enzyme load FoodPro® CBL and the substrate in the enzymatic hydrolysis for the production of glucose was established. Among the possible applications of glucose, its use in the production of ethanol was explained and finally an ACV was performed.
