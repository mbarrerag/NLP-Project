# COMMON (CON) AND (SIN) COMMUNITY.

This work reconstructs the reemergence of the debate about the Commons by taking into account the theoretical discourses recently produced in the literature from the social sciences, especially during the 21st century. We trace the key productions that are capable of being typical- each, classified and organized around two main perspectives: pro-capitalist interpretations, the common without comu- nity, and anti-post-capitalist understandings, the common with community, reflecting their analytical senses and meto-dological criteria, as well as their socio-political, economic, ecological implications, etc. From the analytical description it is possible to locate similar key litudes around the subject, but above all, crucial differences even in the hermeneutic and analytical levels (including, not specific) of the theoretical production as a whole today in force.
