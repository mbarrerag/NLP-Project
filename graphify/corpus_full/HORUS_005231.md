# PEDAGOLOGICAL TERRITORIES EXPLORING THE FIELD OF ARCHITECTONIC PROJECTATION

From the teaching experience of more than three decades of the author, this book addresses the workshops of architectural design, the backbone of academic formation, in an unconventional way from its components and protagonists. It makes a historical tour of the workshop from the Middle Ages to the present day to highlight its dynamics and propose some guidelines, routes, strategies, games, laboratories and exercises within an educational multimodel that enhances the creative capacities of students and fosters a change in the horizons of discipline, in accordance with the complex contemporary global situation. With a wide theoretical review, it is possible to link various epistemological positions that support the didactic proposal from history, anthropology, anthropology, aesthetics, philosophy, quantum mechanics, pysology, psycholinguistics, neuroscience, dualism, biomechanics, antidisciplinarity, transhumanism and pedagogy
