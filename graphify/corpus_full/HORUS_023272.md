# THEMATIC LITERATURA REVIEW ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF EDUCATIONAL GAMES TO IMPROVE STUDENT DESERTION INDEX

The reduction of student drop-out rates is one of the main concerns of higher education institutions. As a multifactorial phenomenon, a wide variety of solutions have been proposed. Among them, and making use of new technological trends and the taste for digital games by young people, one that has gained relevance in recent years is the use of educational games in their different variants as a didactic within the courses to increase the motivation and commitment of students. Considering this panorama, this research aims to carry out a systematic review of literature of those studies that have contemplated the use of educational games in reducing dropout. The purpose of this review is to determine the most relevant research, particularly in basic science courses in virtual or hybrid modality and, finally, to generate some conclusions in relation to this topic
