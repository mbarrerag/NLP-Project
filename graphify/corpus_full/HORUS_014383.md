Fragmentation of healthcare care and its association with the overall 5-year survival in patients with colorectal cancer belonging to the contributory regime in Colombia Introduction Fragmentation of health care care care and its association with the overall 5-year survival in patients with colorectal cancer belonging to the contributory regime in Colombia The fragmentation of health care is a phenomenon in which patients experience a discontininuity in healthcare care. Patients with colorectal cancer are more likely to experience fragmented care (in the majority of colorectal cancer patients) (in the majority of patients with colorectal cancer in Colombia). The patients with colorectal cancer are more likely to experience a fragmented care because they require specialized treatment by multidisciplinary teams. The objective of this study was to identify the association between the fragmentation of health care and the overall 5-year survival of patients with colorectal cancer in Colombia. Methods A retrospective cohort study using administrative databases using an electronic algorithm to identify patients with colorectal cancer based on codes was estimated 5 years and the patient density rate of patients in patients (ITRTI) for the patients with the patients with the upper quartile of fragmentation.
