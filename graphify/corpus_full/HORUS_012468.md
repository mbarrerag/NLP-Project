# INTRODUCTION TO THE FUNDAMENTS OF THE ANALYSIS

An introduction to the fundamentals of analysis brings together some basic topics of mathematical analysis and topology of the real straight. The intention of these concepts in the context of real numbers is to help the student to increase intuition to face part of the subject exposed in higher courses, in which they are treated with greater depth. For example, the notions of continuity, convergence and successions that are also presented in metric spaces or, generally, in topological spaces. It is a book suitable for students of mathematics and is also a solid support for students of related disciplines who must face courses of mathematics in their professional formation. It also constitutes a good reference, reinforcement or guide for students of advanced calculus.
