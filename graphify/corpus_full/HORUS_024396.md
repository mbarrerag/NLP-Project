# THE VISIT OF HANS JENNY TO COLOMBIA, 19461947

The prestigious pedologist Hans Jenny visited Colombia for six months in 19461947. In our opinion, the circumstances of this visit and its effects on science at a global level, and on the development of technology and education in Colombia, have not been sufficiently documented and valued. By the date of his stay in Colombia, the knowledge of tropical soils constituted a hiatus within the framework of his Treatise Factors of Soil Formation. Until then, Jenny had data almost exclusively of high latitudes. The studies in the tropics had a strong motivation for it, contrasting experimentally the premise that the nitrogen and carbon content in the soils has an inverse relationship with the annual average precipitation. Colombia represented a unique opportunity for the exploration of the relationship between the nitrogen, carbon and the humidity content in warm climates, since in its low lands there is a great diversity of the pluviometric soils. The experimentation in Colombia brought surprises to Jenny by contrasting some of its preconceived ideas and prompted him to depurate the until then paradigm of the factors of formation.
