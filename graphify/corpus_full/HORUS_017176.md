# CHEMICAL, MORPHOLOGICAL AND STRUCTURAL CHARACTERIZATION OF MATERIALS

In the fields of science and material technology, the research work has achieved advances of significant importance, which have been embodied in the production of materials with dimensions in the order of the nanometers, with a single dimension (nanotubes), with two dimensions (grafene) and with three dimensions (nanoclusters).The size and dimension of these materials confers unique and different physical and chemical properties to the materials in the order of the microns.In this sense, the high electrical conductivity, high tenacity and hardness, and the high thermal conductivity of the graphene, are highlighted.These characteristics have allowed the development of devices used in microelectronics, telecommunications and computing. Therefore, the dimension and wide spectrum of applications of the new materials require the rigorous and systematic knowledge of their properties, through the techniques of the chemical composition of the surface (up to the last 10th and chemical), with the ability to resolve in the scale of nanometers.
