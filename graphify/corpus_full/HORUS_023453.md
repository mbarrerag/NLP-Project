# THE IMPORTANCE OF ECONOMIC ANALYSIS FOR THE REALIZATION OF THE LAW OF COMPETITION

The authors present a review of the economic concepts and methodologies fundamental to the practice of competition law, with the aim of providing a permanent consultation guide for competition law professionals and policy makers. The understanding of these concepts provides clarity on how markets operate and the incentives that companies find to behave more or less in accordance with competition rules. Among the concepts covered in the chapter are consumer welfare, the definition of the relevant market, market power, dominant position and abuse of that position, practices of exploitation abuses such as excessive prices or abuse of exclusion such as predatory prices, collusion and cartel formation, its effects, valuation and quantification of damages, digital markets and challenges of competition policy in these markets. Thus, this chapter elaborates the important relationship that is woven in law and economy when competition problems are addressed, an element of necessary understanding for practice in this field
