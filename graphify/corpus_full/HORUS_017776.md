# The reproduction of symbolic effectiveness in agrarian reforms in Colombia. The implementation of the Final Agreement in Decree Law 902 of 2017.

The Decree Law 902 of 2017 does not present a definitive solution for the lack of use and equitable redistribution of land by virtue of the provisions of paragraph 1.1 of the Final Peace Agreement. The current legal mechanisms for the rural sector prolong the concentration of land and are contrary to the objective proposed in the Final Agreement (A.F.) These are the areas of interest for rural, economic and social development, the formalization of lands superior to a family agricultural unit and access to rural property of subjects who do not comply with the condition of vulnerable peasant population. Indeed, the DL 902 of 2017 has been established as a measure to implement the first point of the A.F., along with the massive titling of land, which limits the action of the peasant on its use. This reproduces elements of the previous failed agrarian reforms, which historically have not allowed the distribution of land and have led to inequality in the rural sector. In fact, it is identified that the implementation of the Integral Rural Reform (RRI) is given “symbolic efficiency”.
