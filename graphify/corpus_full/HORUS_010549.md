# COMPREHENSIVE REGIONAL AND NATIONAL CONTEXT AS A DETERMINANT OF THE PROFESSIONAL EXERCISE OF INDUSTRIAL INGENIUM IN COLOMBIA

The professional exercise of the industrial engineer focuses on the organizations, the design and execution of objectives and strategies for the improvement of organizational performance, however, it is evident that it is necessary to understand the regional and national context surrounding the company for the successful development of that exercise. Knowing and understanding the interaction of the organization with its context is fundamental to achieving the business competitiveness, objective of every organization, and basis of the competitiveness of the country. The aim of this work is to share a classroom experience on a course at the undergraduate level in the last semesters of the training of the industrial engineer called "Competitivity" which proposes a work applied at the business level, where it is promoted by the improvement of the professional exercise from the understanding of the context of the organization
