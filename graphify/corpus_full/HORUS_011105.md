# Procedure for the implementation of maintenance management software for shopping centres in the city of Bogotá.

Today Bogotá has become an epicenter of the economic activity of the country in which the 20 main commercial centers of the capital have recorded revenues close to 304 billion pesos. The dizzying advance of the construction from the year 2000 generated to the date more than 256 commercial platforms between the 5,000 and 50,000 m2. Seeking alternatives to improve the management of maintenance and decrease times of operation and consumption of electricity was selected a commercial center of the capital (Cafam Floresta) to apply a procedure of implementation of a software of management through the analysis of data supplied by the existing BMS (Building Management System) and the history of maintenance failures. Through the identification of the profiles of tension and the integration of the architecture of monitoring of the software, it has been possible to generate programmed maintenance activities and to reduce by 18.6% the consumption of electricity from two common areas of the commercial center. Finally, the maintenance plans were adjusted and the operations were identified with the support of the software of management implemented
