# IMMUNUTRITION, CIRCRUGY AND ONCOLOGY: A MODEL OF NUTRICIONAL ATTENTION BASED ON EVIDENCE / IMMUNUNUTRION, SURGERY AND ONCOLOGY: AN EVIDENCE-BASED MODEL OF NUTRITIONAL CARE

This literature review allows the proposal of a model of action to identify patients who require major surgery in a timely manner and who may beneficiate from nutritional interventions such as immunonu-trition based on scientific evidence. The purpose of this review and synthesis is to propose a nutritional intervention model in the nutritional management of patients in the perioperative period.Material and methods: A rapid review of the literature was carried out, based on the consultation in the following sources of data,EMBASE, MEDLINE (Pubmed), Cochrane Database ofSystematic Reviews (Wiley), LILACS (BVS, iAHx interface) and Google Academic search engine. 40 articles were identified, which met the parameters established for the systematic review and the quality criteria, which allowed establishing four fa-ses for the proposal of nutritional intervention in perioperative nutrition management, nutritional screening of routine consultation, preoperative supplementation with the therapeutic dose of immunonutrition (treaup-rapéutic) and post-hospital follow-up
