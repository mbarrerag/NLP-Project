# EDUCATION FOR PEACE IN COMMUNICATION AND DESIGN PROFESSIONS

Communication and design are areas with a high impact on today's societies, derived from their potential for information, persuasion and interactive. However, much of their production continues to spread stereotypes, stigma and discriminatory forms. In view of this panorama, the need to strengthen education for peace of communicators and designers in training, especially in countries that, like Colombia, are going through post-conflict times, is reiterated
