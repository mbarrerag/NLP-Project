# EFFICIENCY OF THE VIDRIC FIBRE POSTS CAD/CAM VS. PREFABRICATED AND COLATED POST SYSTEMS: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW.

Provide a general description of the scientific evidence available with respect to the comparative efficiency of the fiberglass poles of computer-assisted design (CAD) and computer-assisted manufacturing (CAM) with precast and collated metal poles, for the restoration of endodontically treated teeth. This systematic review was carried out following the PRISMA guidelines. Electronic and manual searches were carried out using the databases PubMed, SciELO, Cochrane, ScienceDirect, Web of Science and EBSCO. The lists of references of the selected articles were reviewed to identify relevant articles. There were no year restrictions, and the most recent search included studies published until 9 February 2021 inclusive. The eligible studies to complete glass were those in English publications and describing the in vitro studies that evaluated intraradicular reteners with respect to: fracture resistance, union resistance, adaptation and thickness of the cement layer. The studies were excluded from the literature, systematic revisions, the metaanalysis was necessary for the studies of cases, the in vitro studies with nfage. filtration (p = 0.001), and better adaptation to the root canal (80%). Also, they performed better in oval-shaped roots compared to precast glass fibre posts. Despite the heterogeneity of methodologies and the reported results, the results of these studies indicated that the metallic and CAD/CAM glass fibre posts showed greater efficiency in terms of fracture resistance, retention and adaptation, compared to prefabricated glass fibre poles
