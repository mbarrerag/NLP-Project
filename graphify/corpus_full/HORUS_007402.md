# Analysis of the relationship of tendering capacities and the success of the MiPymes recruitment processes that sell services to the Colombian State. Analysis of cases in the environment and basic sanitation sector

From a qualitative and interpretative approach, and with the participation of six MiPymes who sell services to the environment and basic sanitation, this work began identifying conceptual approaches to the tendering capacities. Subsequently, through the technique of Focus Group, the competitive capacities of the Colombian entrepreneur were described. Through semi-structured interviews and content analysis, the links with the success of the procurement of contracts transposed on the SECOP II platform were documented. It is concluded that, on the one hand, the procedural capacity of the Colombian entrepreneur is consistent with literature, while the relational capacity is not. On the other hand, the frequency of the search for contracts, the number of persons involved in the tendering, the strategic alliances and the strengthening of the commercial team are associated with success. .
