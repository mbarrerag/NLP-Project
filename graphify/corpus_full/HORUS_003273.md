# TUBERCLES PERICARDITIS IN A PATIENT EXPOSED TO SILCIUM OXIDE.

The case of a patient with tuberculose pericarditis, an uncommon form of tuberculosis with a high morbidity and mortality. This report is a contribution to the medical literature since it represents a complete review of the approach by images of this pathology. Presentation of the case. 52-year-old man with exposure to silicon oxide, who was referred to the Intensive Care Unit of a fourth level of complexity hospital in the city of Bogotá (Colombia) with hyperdensus pericardial effusion, initially considered of malignant etiology. An imaging follow-up with suggestive results of tuberculosa pericarditis, was performed, so that a pericardiocentesis was performed whose microbiological and molecular detection studies in pericardial fluid were negative for Mycobacterium tuberculosis and fungi; only the adenosine deaminosa test (ADA) was positive, which raised the suspicion of tuberculosis pericarditis. The definitive diagnosis was obtained by pericardiitis biopsy and was instituted daily antituberculosis treatment by oral route for 56 days, with which the result of a significant improvement of the patient'scopic pericardiitis
