# Determination of Critical Success Factors (CEF) for the integration of sustainability in the management of construction projects in Colombia

The Sustainable Construction (CS) is an urgent necessity worldwide to achieve the decarbonization of the planet in the years ahead, so the organizations that manage construction projects must integrate the perspectives of sustainability to their processes as mechanism to ensure durable and resilient buildings to climate change. Under this premise and taking into account that most organizations of the construction sector in Colombia lack robust methodologies for the Management of Sustainable Construction Projects (GPSC) and that there are disparities in the direction and management of the constructive projects, besides particular political, social and economic situations in the country that hinder the transition to a complete CS in Colombia, the present research has as main objective the determination of the Critical Factors of Éxito (FCE) to incorporate the sustainability as an innovation in the management of the construction projects in Colombia (2000); to achieve the proposed objective ECE is carried out a more sequential exploratory design of type qual → QAN (from qualitative to quantitative) composed by 3 stages.
