# Obtaining peptides derived from biologically active animal toxins through a recombinant system and chemical synthesis

The toxin BmK AGAP, from the scorpion Buthus martensii Karsch, modulates the function of various cellular ionic channels, exhibiting antitumor activity in different cell lines derived from tumours. It has been identified that the functional TRPC6 channels are overexpressed in various types of cancer, which makes them possible markers of malignancy. In this work, by molecular docking using AutoDock Vina, the interaction of the toxin BmK AGAP with the TRPC6 channel was studied. From these results, eight peptides were designed, with the best interaction (higher binding energy) with the channel. Three of these peptides (MR-B1, MR-B2 and MR-B3) were obtained by chemical synthesis in solid phase, while the remaining two peptides (Bmk17 and Bmk20) were selected for production by recombinant expression in E. coli.
