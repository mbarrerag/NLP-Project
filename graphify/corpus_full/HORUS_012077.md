# Description of outcomes of different intervals between procedures in endoscopic management with dilation in paediatric patients between 0-15 years with subglotically acquired post-intrusion stenosis in the Hospital of Mercy HOMI.

to describe the characteristics and outcomes in the treatment of a paediatric population with subglototic stenosis managed with delayed endoscopic dilation (DD) and another with Common Serial dilations (SD) between 2009 - 2022. A descriptive study of analytical cross-sectional cut was performed with the review of the database of clinical histories of patients between 0-15 years with diagnosis of subglotically stenosis taken with (DD) between 2009 - 2018 and a group with (DS) between 2019 and 2022. The demographic characteristics and percentage of therapeutic failure defined as requiring more than 5 dilatations, tracheostomy or Laring trastracheal reconstruction (RLT) in each of the groups. 140 patients were reported during the study (DD) with a median of 38.5 (CRP 2852) days between dilatations and 50 patients in the group (DS) with a median of 27.5 days (RIC: 440) between dilatations.
