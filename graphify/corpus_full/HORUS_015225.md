# PEDAGOGICAL EXPLORATIONS IN CONFORT AND ENERGY EFFICIENCY BY THE EMAT RESEARCH GROUP IN COLOMBIA

We educate our students to understand more than we, their teachers, understand today. To achieve this, it will be necessary for them to learn on their own what we do not know today. Academic autonomy necessary to achieve that goal will be the most important thing that can be acquired in our classes and it is often read exhortations around this intellectual deliberation, although less common are warnings about that it is not something automatic, but it takes time and, above all, demands favorable situations to acquire an appropriate intellectual position. The ability to learn without having someone take charge of the teaching is prerequisite for any research, is an essential tool in the postgraduate courses, but it is applied little in the undergraduate courses, we think that wrongly. Perhaps for this reason many applicants to a master's degree face their little academic autonomy with the typical crisis that generates them the realization of their own thesis. In the EMAT research group we have tried to close that gap and educate people oriented to the production of new knowledge, trying to later to pregrade the research projects and the teaching ones to be one thing.
