# Synthesis, chemical properties and synthetic application of three aminal box

The aminals are organic compounds containing the function gem-diamine N-C-N in their structures and can be presented in open or cyclical chain. Heterocyclics, especially the tricyclic tetra amins that make up the family of the box aminals, are very little studied compounds, something misunderstood and very interesting, due to their chemical properties and their promising use in various fields of organic synthesis. Despite their somewhat atypical reaction as mentioned above, they are poorly studied substances and therefore there is not much information about them, and the existing one is not centralized. However, it has been a few decades since the study of physical and chemical properties, as well as the use of these substances mainly in organic synthesis, it has been taking importance. The present work constitutes a scientific review, which compiles, interprets and makes available the scientific knowledge about the box aminal aminaire, specifically 1.3,5,5-tetrazatricyclo[3.3,13,07decan (HMTA), 1.3,6-tetratrazatricyclo[4.3,3.3,3.3,13,13,08-acyclo-ncyclo-organ[43,13,8]-, and therefore, there is little information available on them, and what exists is not centralized. However, in the past few decades, the study of their physical and chemical properties, as well as their use in organic synthesis, has been gaining importance. The present work is a scientific review that compiles, interprets, and makes accessible scientific knowledge about cage aminals, specifically 1.3,5,7-tetrazatricyclo[3.3.1.3.13,7]decane (HMTA), 1.3,6.8-tetrazatricyclo[4.3.13)undecane (TATU), and 1.3,6.8-tetrazatricyclo[4.4.1.1,8]dodecane (TATD) for those interested in understanding or learning about this specific area of knowledge
