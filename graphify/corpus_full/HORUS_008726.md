# ASSESSMENT OF CATALYTICAL ACTIVITY OF A MIXING OF NATURAL MINERAL MIXINGS PROVIDING FROM THE INDUSTRY OF ACERIAL WASTES IN COMPLETING AS CATALIZERS IN DEGRADATION OF AMOOXICILINE BY A PHOTO PROCESS

In the present investigation, a solid material based on a mixture of natural minerals (hemathite, goethite and siderite) from steel mill industry residues was evaluated as a catalyst in a photo process.Fenton for amoxicillin degradation. A concentration of amoxicillin of 20 mg/L was used and parameters such as H2O2 concentration, pH and quantity of catalyst were evaluated to find suitable conditions for complete degradation. It was found that the catalyst was highly active, reaching a complete degradation of the antibiotic in 40 minutes of reaction with 50 mg of catalyst, [H2O2 ] = 50 mM and pH 3.0. After analyzing the reaction mixtures by atomic absorption spectrometry, it was found that the maximum amount of iron leachate in the reactions performed was 0.538 mg/L, a 10 times lower amount than that commonly used in literature to perform the Fenton reaction in a homogeneous phase, which is considered to be completely heterogeneous.
