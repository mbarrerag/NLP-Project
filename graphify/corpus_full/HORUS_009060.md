# ESTIMATION OF A MULTI-VALUATED NORMAL PROCESS UNDER MULTI-MODALITY

In modern industrial processes multimodality is a common feature and process control tools should be able to detect the occurrence of anomalies in the presence of changes in process mode. This presentation aims to make known some of the multivariate control techniques used in these cases, including the Bayesian approach. A critical step to establish any statistical control scheme over a process, is its estimation under normal operating conditions, which, in multimodal processes implies first a correct classification of observations in the different operating modes that are part of the stable state of the process. Under this latter, the partial results of a simulation study on normal multimodal samples with different operating modes (clusters), three sample sizes and nine levels of "closeness" of the operating modes will be shown. The ability to classify the hope-maximization algorithms (EMs), the classification EM (CEMs), the stochastic EM (SEMs) and the Bayesian approach is assessed
