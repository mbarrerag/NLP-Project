# Knowledges on the Margins. Strategies of Production and New Medialities in Latin America from the Perspective of Conviviality-Inequality. Introduction

This dossier brings together works by authors from different disciplines (sociology, geography, anthropology, cultural studies and philosophy) that analyze strategies for the production and circulation of knowledge in Latin America from the analytical perspective of conviviality-inequality. The objective is to address from different examples, how in the framework of structural asymmetries, voices that have been relegated to the margins both ontologically and epistemologically, struggle to position their knowledge and life proposals with intermediate strategies
