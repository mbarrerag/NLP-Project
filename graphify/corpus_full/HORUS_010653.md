# FORESTS: QUANTITATIVE SELVICULTURE AND FORESTS SUSTAINABILITY: QUANTITATIVE FORESTRY AND SUSTAINABILITY

The challenges currently facing postgraduate training and quality forest management are very relevant due to the occurrence and magnitude of global environmental problems (climate change, invasive species and loss of biodiversity), as well as the growing demand for ecosystem goods and services by society, to live in sustainable communities. The United Nations Organization approved the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, which is set up through 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), several of which are related to high-level training and forest management. among these, two are: sdg-4: quality education and sdg-15: life of earth ecosystems. This project has strengthened the teaching use of tools developed by our group earlier (  and   has strengthened the international collaboration network on educational innovation with high-level universities in Argentina, Colombia, Italy, Portugal and Vietnam and has enabled us to draw on the experience of the SMART Global Ecosystems UVa-SNGULAR Company Chair (http://smartglobalecosystems.uva.es/).) This project has generated learning objects and strengthened further the degrees of the SMART Global Ecosystems UVa-SNGULAR Company
