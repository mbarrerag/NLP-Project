# STUDY OF FIELDS DELIGATED ON A TIO2 PHASE IN ANTI-ADVANTAGE FOR APPLICATIONS IN ANTI-REFLECTION.

In this investigation, using the chemical vapour spray-assisted (AACVD) deposit technique, TiO2 titanium dioxide-based films (Titanium Oxiacetilacetone (IV) were manufactured with 90% purity on glass substrates. Before the manufacturing process began, the precursor concentration was established to obtain TiO2 in a stable phase (0.05 M) and the technological parameters of the fundamental process in order to create an ideal environment for the manufacture of films: Air flow (4 L/min), temperature variation from 250 °C to 450 °C on a scale of 50 °C, with the aim of observing at which temperature a stable phase is formed and a better crystallinity. By using the x-ray diffraction technique (DRX), it is observed that from 350 °C to 450 °C the anti-reflecting phase of the anatase was formed without the presence of any other phase and the best crystalline was obtained at 450 °C.
