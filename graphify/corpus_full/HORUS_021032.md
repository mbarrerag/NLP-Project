# Rural Development and Food Sovereignty. Voices and Proposals of the Peasant Movement in Colombia

Historically, rural societies have experienced a set of transformations at the social, political, economic, cultural and environmental level, result of the implementation of hegemonic models that have defined and oriented the strategic guidelines of the rural development agenda in Colombia. According to Kay (2005) and Pérez & Farah (2002), rural development has passed different paradigms, approaches and hegemonic models in certain historical periods, since the post-war period in the 1950s with the Modernizing Model focused on a productive and technical vision; the structuralist/institutionalist model of industrialization via import substitution (isi) of the 1960s _x0096_70; the Neoliberal Model, the structural adjustment programmes of economic globalization since 1980; and the Neo-Institutional Model of early 1990s.
