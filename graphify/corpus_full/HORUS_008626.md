# Isolation of MULTIFUNTIONAL UREOLITICS FOR THE BIORREIDIATION OF CDS IN AGRICULTURAL SOUNDS

In Colombia, it has been shown that the cocoa soils contain high levels of Cd, a potentially toxic metal, which is absorbed by the plant and transloced to the cocoa almond (primary chocolate matter). It is therefore important to develop treatments that decrease the availability of the Cd, a potentially toxic metal, which is absorbed by the plant and transloced to the cocoa almond (pri primary chocolate matter). Therefore, it is important to develop treatments that decrease the availability of the Cd, and thus mitigate its presence in edible fruit. The use of ureolitic bacteria that induce the precipitation of carbonates (U-MICP), is a biotechnological strategy with promitive results related to the decrease in the availability of metals. However, little attention has been given to the multifunctionality of these bacteria, which can also promote plant growth.
