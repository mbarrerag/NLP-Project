# TEACHINGS OF ANTONIUM CARO CREATIVITY STARS

This article explores the educational contribution of the Workshops of Visual Creativity that Antonio Caro (1950-2021) taught in the country and abroad for more than thirty years. Through a novel pedagogical program that gathers ideal of John Dewey to integrate in a natural way art and life, the artist manages to democratize art and enhance creative capacities by consolidating new possibilities for aesthetic-educative experience, inclusion and the development of social skills through dialogue and active participation. The objective is to show that this contemporary artistic practice incardina ethical and aesthetic issues that allow to bring educational theory closer to the experience of art in function of its effects and pragmatic potential. The methodology was qualitative in nature and is limited to an exploratory documentary review of printed, electronic and audiovisual sources. The results allow to conclude that the pedagogical content of the workshops, when remade and reviewed permanently, builds its own method of research, opens alternate routes to the educational models of institutional art, enhances the communiability and deploys tactics that give meaning and direction to life, allowing art and education to enable the dimension of the experience that modern reason could not encompass
