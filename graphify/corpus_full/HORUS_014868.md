# THEORY OF CHANGE AND DESIGN OF POLICIES TO THE TIME OF DIGITAL REVOLUTION AND OPEN GOVERNMENT

The current health crisis caused by the COVID-19 pandemic is a powerful accelerator of social transformations. In the field of administration it facilitates the expansion of a new model of management of organizations and collective action, which has already been developed. The main challenge for the leaders of any organization is in their capacity to dominate, which is defined as the probability of obtaining obedience from subjects to their mandates. Obedience means obtaining from subordinate agents a behavior as expected to achieve (hypothetically) the objectives pursued by the leaders of the organization. Likewise, for a government it involves designing public policies that guide the behaviour of citizens as effectively and efficiently as possible towards the achievement of the desired ends. In other words, the design of administration or public policies is based on a theory of behaviour change or domination of the members of the organization (administration) or of citizenship (public policies) in order to achieve certain objectives effectively
