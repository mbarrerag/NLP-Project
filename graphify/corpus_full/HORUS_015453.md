# SINTESS OF THE ILLURE OF AZOMETINE 5-(CARBOXIMETHYLAMINE) ILUR PREcursor FOR THE FUNCTIONALIZATION OF FULLERENUM C60

The scope of the C60 fullerene derivatives is wide due to the possibility of synthesizing a variety of molecules that can exhibit unique properties. To contribute to the research of the synthesis of a new C60 monoaduct, a fulleropirrolidine, from 5-aminoisoftalic acid (1). The synthesis of: 5-aminoisoftalate dimethyl (2), 5-(cyanomethylamino)isoftalate dimethyl (3), and 5-(carboxymethylamino)isoftalic acid (4), was carried out; where, 4 and 3 molecules are previously not reported in the literature.
