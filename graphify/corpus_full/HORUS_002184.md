# Rack representations and connections with groups representations

In this paper, we study some algebraic properties of the rack structure as well as the representation theory of it, following the ideas given by Elhamdadi and Moutuou [Finitely stable racks and rack representations, Comm. Algebra 46(11) (2018) 4787-4802]. We establish a correspondence between the irreducible strong representations of a finite and connected rack with the irreducible representation of its finite enveloping group which allows to use techniques of the latter topic in the other setting. We also prove that strong representations of a finite rack X are in one to one correspondence with the representations of the inner automorphism group Inn(X) of X
