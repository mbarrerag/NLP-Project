# Digital Segregation in Bogotá: Territorial Impacts of the Implementation of Public Policies Related to Access to ICTs since 2009 to the present

The digital territorial segregation is a field of study widely addressed in the international literature on the diffusion of information technologies and emerges as a dimension of socio-spatial segregation. However, with some exceptions, little has been explored its manifestation in the city of Bogotá. This document aims to present the conditions of territorial digital segregation in the Colombian capital and to determine whether this phenomenon has been strengthened through national and district public policies for the strengthening of the “smart city”. This study focuses on the theoretical and methodological construction of the concept “Smart City” and the influence of technology companies and multilateral agencies that determine the route of action for the implementation of information and communications technologies (ICT) in contemporary cities. In the second place, the study identifies the constituent elements of the ICT infrastructure and its territorial deployment, which has been given in an unequal and sectoralized way. Finally, it presents a territorialization of the distrital and national policies and projects from 2009 to the present, to recognize to what extent they have been effective to close the digital gaps or, if on the contrary, they have strengthened the digital segregation in the city.
