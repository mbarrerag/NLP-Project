# A comparison between statistical models and Machine Learning for the prediction of multivariate time series.

The prediction of time series is a classic area of study in statistics and a growing field of Automatic Learning (or Machine Learning in English). This paper aims to compare performance to predict a step forward in multivariate time series between the classic statistical models — Self-regressive Vector (VAR) and Vectorial Error Correction Model (VECM) — and Machine Learning models — Multilayer Perceptron (MLP), Long Short Term Memory (LSTM) and Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU). An alternative to both types of models was considered the combination of forecasts of various statistical models and Machine Learning to improve predictions. The performance of predictions was examined by means of the observed error metrics: Middle Quadratic Error (ECM), Middle Absolute Error (MDE) and Middle Quadratic Error Root (MDR). In addition, the statistical difference between predictions was contrasted by means of a Diebold-Marian test and of coverage (or in English-mode) taken violence (multi-economic, seven-series, epilogized) were used
