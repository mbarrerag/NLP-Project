# Data-driven modelling of micro and ultra - filtering processes

The processes of microfiltration (MF) and ultrafiltration (UF) are widely used in various industrial and research fields, and different companies have emerged to develop improvements and new designs of these technologies. However, some drawbacks re-lapidated with the operation of the process, namely the polarization of concentration and soiling, prevent the use of membranes from spreading in all sectors of industry. The polarization of concentration and soiling are the main problems in MF and UF to be managed for designing a process of separation. The dynamic operation strategies are used to mitigate the adverse effects of polarization and soiling and improve the performance of separation. However, there is a balance between the operating conditions to achieve the desired effects. The effect of concentration polarization is analyzed and explained by the polarization module. This module is defined as the relationship between concentration on the surface of the membrane and concentration on the breast of the fluid. Values as high as 160 for this module have a negative impact on selectivity, while values close to or below 34 improve separation.
