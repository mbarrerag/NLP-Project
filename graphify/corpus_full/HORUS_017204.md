# Parameters for the visual and instrumental evaluation of dental colour in in-vitro studies. Review of literature

to establish the parameters for the visual and instrumental evaluation of dental colour in in-vitro studies from the scientific literature published between 2015 and 2021. the search was carried out in the databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Science Direct, Scopus, Scielo and Lilacs; also in the search engine Google Academic and libraries of the publishing houses Wiley and Springer. The key words used were tooth, color, in-vitro, color perception, shade matching, thresholds, application, surrounding, “CIELAB” and “CIEDE2000”.
