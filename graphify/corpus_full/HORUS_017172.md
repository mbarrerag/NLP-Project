# HOW TO MAKE A PORTFOLIO

The portfolio is a powerful tool for documenting, collecting and structuring information about students’ progress, allowing them to look back, in the present and towards the future. For students, teachers or professionals the portfolio is very useful for DIBUJAR tuLearning in motion. The Guide presents the steps for the design of the portfolio
