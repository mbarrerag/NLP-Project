# The solo trumpet in the music of the Nariñense composers

This project aims to study the trumpet soloist in the music of the composers of Nariños Thiiffermusic and compositions through a musical project exposed in a recital where the repertoire interpreted revolves around the trumpet, including new works, where some aspects of the rhythmic rhythm will be studied common and typical of the region, also stylistic aspects of each composer exposed in his musical language, is proposed music composed in different instrumental formats. This work begins with the contextualization of the son-southian rhythm, the “Pote Mideros” as a musical representative in Nariño and the influence of the work Sandoná in the culture of the department, when it aims to highlight different aspects that characterize the Nariñense culture in its interpretive style and how it manages to influence the composer and the interpreter, in order to understand the musical language of the region from its origin to the present. Interviews were made to several composers, musicians and researchers of Nariño and considered the works that were created for the entire programme of the recital, showing a new contribution to the repertoire of the trumpet, with different musical and interpretatid proposals.
