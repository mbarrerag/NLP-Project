# Cordycipitaceae) and two plant extracts in Gaeolaelaps aculeifer (Mesostigmata: Laelapidae).

Frankliniella de partidoris is an important population pest in multiple crops where cut flowers are included; this has forced to use different management tools such as Biological Control Agents and plant extracts. Mushrooms such as Metarhizium anisopliae and Beauveria bassiana are used in the control of F. occidentalis, however, lack information on the feasibility of use in conjunction with some species of predatory pests of the pest. Gaeolaps aculeifer is a predatory mite that consumes F. occidentalis pupas. This work was performed with the objective of evaluating the effect of Metarhizium anisopliae, Beauveria basiana and two plant extracts on G. aculeifer. We performed laboratory tests for direct contact and treated surface on G. aculifer and pupal states of F. occidentis. Similarly, we performed mortality tests by ingestion and tests to evaluate the change in the rate of consumption of treated dams.
