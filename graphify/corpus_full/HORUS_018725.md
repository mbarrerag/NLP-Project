# Evaluation of remuneration schemes for large-scale battery-powered energy storage systems connected to transmission networks in Colombia.

The storage systems with batteries at transmission and distribution scale represent a technology of incipient development in the context of energy transformation in Colombia. These systems are of great interest for the technical and economic benefits associated with their operation at all levels of the energy supply chain. One of the challenges at the global level associated with this technology is the definition of products and remuneration models to capitalize the benefits mentioned, which are attractive to investors while promoting efficiency in network costs. The aim of this work is to propose and evaluate remuneration models for a practical case of a battery storage system connected in the distribution network of the city of Barranquilla, Colombia. Initially, the services applicable to the Colombian market under current regulatory conditions are identified and mechanisms are put in place for the capture of value associated with these services, by the investor, based on reference to international markets. The mechanisms proposed consider revenues from regulated cost recovery, through participation in energy markets and complementary services.
