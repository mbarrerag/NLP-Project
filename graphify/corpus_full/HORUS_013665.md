# Characterization of the physical-mechanical properties of the Guadua angustifolia kunth municipality of La Victoria, Boyacá

The aim of this investigation was to determine the physical and mechanical properties of the Guadua angustifolia kunth of the municipality of La Victoria, Boyacá, with the intention of characterizing it and analysing its performance in comparison with the results of other research carried out in Colombia. To achieve this objective, tests and procedures established in the NTC 5525 ‘Test methods to determine the physical and mechanical properties of the Guadua angustifolia kunth’, the NSR-10 ‘Colombian regulation of seismic resistance’ and the ISO 22156:2021 standard were carried out. The tests were carried out with 14 culmos of 9 metres in length, which were cut in three portions which had the following measures: 1.5 m for the lower portion, 4.5 m for the medium portion and 3 m for the upper portion. From these segments, the results of the study were selected the probetas necessary for the conduct of the flexion tests, tension parallel to the fibres, cut with and without the knot, compression parallel to the fibres, compression perpendicular to the fibres, the density and moisture content correspond to the last.
