# FACTORS CERTAIN THE ELECTION OF THE PROFESSIONAL CAREER: ANALYSIS APPLICED TO ASPIRING AND FIRST-SUMMER STUDY STUDENTS OF HIGHER EDUCATION PROGRAMMES

The choice of responsible professional career favours the adaptation and retention of higher education, as well as the integration into the labour market; elements that together with others account for the relevance of a higher education programme. The objective of this article is to determine the main motivations for the choice of professional career by candidates and students of higher education programmes. These motivations are identified from surveys conducted by applicants of undergraduate academic programmes offered by the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín and the University of Antioquia. Additionally, the data collected by a questionnaire conducted by the students of the first semester of the University Institution of Envigado are analyzed. Finally, the discussion and conclusions section identifies that, in relation to the data analyzed, there are practical implications for the generation of predictive models as an expanded structure of the information systems of the Ministry of National Education that allow support to be given to the analysis of the relevance of higher education programmes
