# Gender impact on leadership and economic performance: an analysis in small and medium-sized Colombian companies

It presents as a proposal for research to analyze the impact of gender on the leadership and economic performance of small and medium-sized Colombian companies. This proposal is framed within the objectives of sustainable development (SDGs), specifically in achieving gender equality (Objective 5) and promoting full productive employment and decent work for all (Objective 8). It is recognized that gender plays a fundamental role in the transformations proposed in the National Development Plan 2022-2026, which highlights the importance of adopting a gender rights approach to achieve total peace and eliminate stereotypes that limit the advancement of women in the business environment. Women's participation remains numerically low in the organizational context (Glass & Cook, 2017).In Colombia, women face difficulties in accessing leadership roles and have limited participation in the social capital of companies (Confecillas, 2024).
