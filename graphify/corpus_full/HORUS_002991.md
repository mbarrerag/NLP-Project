# Application of an age-period-cohort model (APC) for cases of mortality due to a specific disease in the Colombian population

The statistical models that relate mortality rates to the variables age, period and birth cohort (known as APC models) are frequently used in epidemiological and social studies. This study aims to assess from the data of mortality due to diabetes in Colombia, the effect of these three predictor variables on these mortality rates. The models of regression Poisson and Binomial Negative with which mortality rates are obtained, then the adjustment will be made to each model using the distribution Gamma Inversa and SBeta2 as a priori distributions for the parameters of variance of the temporal effects age, period and cohort. Comparisons are made between the models through the Deviance Information Criterion (DIC), finally being evaluated by calculating the root of the Middle Quad Error (RMSE) between the real and predictive values for the last period, which will not be taken into account in the adjustments of the model. .
