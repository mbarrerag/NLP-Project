# ACA, Hashimoto thyroiditis and thyroid cancer: a diagnostic performance study in a university hospital.

Objective To evaluate the diagnostic performance of thyroid AFCA when comparing with Gold Standard, the pathology of thyroidectomy and describe in it the frequency of Hashimoto thyroiditis in the pathology specimens. Methods A retrospective descriptive study was performed on a sample of thyroidectomy performed by the head and neck surgery team of the National University Hospital of Colombia from 2016 to 2021. A AFCA of presurgical thyroid nodule and the last presurgical ultrasound of patients taken to these procedures was performed. The results of 465 thyroidectomy specimens were found to be malignancy, to perform a demographic description and search for prevalence of thyroid cancer and Hashimoto thyroiditis. Finally, a diagnostic performance study of presurgical AFCA was performed when comparing it with surgical specimen. Results Of 465 thyroidectomy specimens were found in 32 cases (ca) papillary, 5 follicular, 3 medullar, 2 onctitis and 4 malignities in thyroid cancer (paraganglioma, in association with escalutal association). The interpretation of pre-surgical cytology can explain the high number of surgical specimens that resulted in malignancy
