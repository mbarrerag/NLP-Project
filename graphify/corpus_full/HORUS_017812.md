# Fertility analysis using mining techniques data

The objective of this research is to predict the most important variables that affect fertility in a person. Weka machine learning and data mining platform is used. The algorithms of Clusters EM, SimpleKMeans and J48, which are of behavior similar to a Bayesian algorithm. Initially, an existing database is modeled, to adjust 105 records and 9 variables, 8 of them independent (age, diseases, accidents, surgery, fever, alcohol, smoker and sedentaryism) and one dependent (fertility). As a result, the 5 most influential variables in this process are identified: 1) age, 2) accident, 3) fever, 4) surgery and 5 alcohol. A success rate of more than 90% is achieved when cross-validation is used 80% - 20%. It is concluded that the techniques of decision trees and cluster used in this study allow to establish clearly the most important variables in a person's fertility processes
