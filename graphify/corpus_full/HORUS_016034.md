# CRIME POLICY IN THE CRIMINAL PROCESS: THE DELATOR, THE COLLABORATORY TEST or THE COIMPUTTED TEST AS A SPECIAL TECHNICAL RESEARCH REFERENCE TO THE ORGANISED CRIME

Under Colombian criminal procedure law, the legislator, by transnational mandate, has introduced special investigative techniques (TEI) as mechanisms for the efficient fight against organized crime, including the infiltration of criminal organizations, the undercover agent, the undercover computer agent, the award-winning delation or collaborating witness and the interception of communications, with the aim of strengthening the investigation and judicialization of criminal organizations in our environment and as a consequence of the transnational policy of efficient struggle against this type of crime. At the outset, this chapter aims to show the reader the social, cultural and political-criminal context in which these IETs arise; then, on the basis of the guaranteeist approaches of Professor Arenas Salazar, proposes the evaluation of the testimonial evidence derived from the special technique called the award-winning or co-defiant witness
