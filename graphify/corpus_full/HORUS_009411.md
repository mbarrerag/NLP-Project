# SIX PATAS ROOMS: ILLUSTRATED GUIDE TO THE UNIVERSITY CITY ENTOMOFFAUN

Colombia is one of the countries with the greatest diversity of insects in the world. Recent figures estimate that about 12,000 known species of insects, although the largest percentage of species, which is still to be discovered, live in the natural ecosystems of Colombian forests and forests. However, the urbanization of the big cities has reduced forest cover, which has displaced and extinguished most of these populations, while reducing human interaction with nature, so there is a widespread lack of knowledge of the biology and importance of insects, as well as its close relationship with human activities.The campus of the University City offers a wide range of green areas that makes it a major space for the stationary and permanent residence of a large number of animal species, including insects, which are a heritage that must be known and preserved.The illustrated guide of the entomofauna of the University City is the result of the active work of a group of students from different academic programs of the National University of Colombia, motivated by the scientific illustration, which has been elaborated with the spirit of knowledge and the most interesting of the insects
