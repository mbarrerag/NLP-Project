# VOCALS OF THE SPANISH AND DEEP LEARNING: CLASSIFICATION OF SPECTROGRAMS USING CONVOLUTIONAL NEURONAL NETWORKS

This research sought to classify different acoustic representations of Vocal segments of Spanish through techniques of Deep Learning, specifically, Convolutional Neuronal Networks that used as input fragments of spectrograms generated from acoustic information. For this, a subset of data was extracted from the open source multilanguage Common Voice, to which were performed manually 1,207 phonetic transcriptions (206 of the /i/, 269 of the vowel /e/, 319 of the vowel /a/, 224 of the vowel /o/ and 189 of the vowel /u/).
