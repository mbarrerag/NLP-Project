# Anti-Gender Speeches in Ibero-America: Moral Panic in the Face of Self-Determination of Bodies

The success of the feminist and LGBTI social movements in the politicization of sexuality has led to the possibility of historically criminalized, pathologized and stigmatized corporal practices becoming rights and public policies in Ibero-America.In view of this situation, various conservative sectors have put into effect a discursive strategy called “gender ideology”, which had a special boom in the region between 2015 and 2019.With this expression, anti-national activists of different nationalities have tried to hide their own ideological position based on biological determinism, conservative familism and Christian dogmatism, while promoting moral panic in society. Unlike movements that propitify for self-determination of bodies, the anti-ethnic counter-movement seeks to maintain social hierarchies, domination and the order of social and paternal gender with a biopolitical objective: control the identity, sexuality and human reproduction. In this context, there arises the need to better understand how they have tried to persuade their publics, the anti-energist activists through the argument presented in their speeches, to present them in countries of the region such as Peru, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia and Spain., while encouraging moral panic in society. Unlike movements that advocate the self-determination of bodies, the anti-gender globalization seek to mainstream social hierarchies, domination and the cisheteropatriarchal gender order with a biopolitical objective: to control identity, sexuality and human reproduction.
