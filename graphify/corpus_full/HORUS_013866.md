# Solar fauna: heliotropes for the continuity of life

Solar Fauna is a creation project framed in the postgraduate program of the Interdisciplinary Master's Degree in Theatre and Living Arts of the Faculty of Arts of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá. It consists of an interdisciplinary project that integrates reforestation practices and spatial writing in a space located and specific that is the forest site of the Faculty of Agronomy of the UNAL. Through relational practices that emphasize the relations between an artist and his environment, it seeks to deploy an artistic project from a relational ethics and a sense of reciprocity with the territory and the beings that inhabit it. Solar Fauna consists of a performative act and a written text, as well as audiovisual records that contain the practices developed throughout the last two semesters of the master's degree.
