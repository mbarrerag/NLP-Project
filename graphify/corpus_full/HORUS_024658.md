# INVESTIGATE MENTAL HEALTH AND NEOLIBERALISM FROM PSYCOANALYSIS

This work aims to illustrate to the reader the need to perceive the importance of reparation as a right of victims and as an obligation of States. For this purpose, the author builds a document of easy understanding, which part of the classical conceptions of the person, institutions and mechanisms and their contribution to the formation of robust protection structures that led to the positive realization of rights enforceable to States. Likewise, the work allows to clearly determine the sources of law that constitute the obligation to redress and the jurisprudential development that has originated in the different systems of protection of human rights. Finally, the author proposes actions to increase the effectiveness of the fulfilment of the obligation to redress
