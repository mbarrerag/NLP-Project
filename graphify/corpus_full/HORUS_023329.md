# TALTIL ESTIMULATION PLANT LOCATED FOR IMPROVING BALANCE CONTROL

The second largest cause of accidental death is falls, according to the WHO. Balance control is fundamental to the prevention of falls. It receives information from the vestibular, visual and somatosensory system including proprioception and touch planting in the somatosensory system. Devices that seek to reduce falls support these three systems. Of which the templates are the most economical, practical and easily accessible devices in developing countries
