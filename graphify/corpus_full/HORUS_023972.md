# PUSHKIN AND DENTE: CROSS HISTORIA OF A LITERARY RECEPTION.

The presentation presents Aleksandr Pushkin’s relationship with Dante Alighieri’s work from the perspective of histoire croisee, a methodology that allows exploring previously unknown aspects of cultural and literary transfers. It is shown that the Russian poet’s relationship with the Italian poet was influenced by the reception of the English and French poets of the time
