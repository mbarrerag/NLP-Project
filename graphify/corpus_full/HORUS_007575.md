# The Right to Education in Figures: An Index of the Right to Education in the Key to Statutory Law

The right to education is an idea recognized as fundamental within modern societies that has mobilized different actors of society. In Colombia, currently it is the draft statutory law of education that seeks to expand the scope of the right to education, not only in its coverage but in its understanding. Education as a human right has already been recognized since the declaration of human rights of 1948, now transcends and is installed as a fundamental right that requires greater understanding. In Colombia, progress has already been made in measuring the realization of the right to education. For example, in 2014 the index of the right to education (IDE) was launched, which employs the four dimensions of the right to education proposed by Tomasevski (2004): Availability (Acquibility), Accessibility, Adaptability and Acceptability (IDE, 2014). In addition, a process of validation of the indicators of the IDE using the methodology Delphi, which allows through repeated rounds, to reach consensus of experts. In two rounds, 158 experts from all the country that have great experience in the education sector was carried out by the 12 indicators (85 subindicators) analyzed in one of the four dimensions of the accessibility. The aim of the updated construction of an Education Right Index (EDI) is to become a robust tool that informs the entire education system and contributes to decision-making by public policy makers. An appropriate IDE can enable decision makers to focus on education policy more precisely. Additionally, having an IDE will identify the factors associated with the realization of the right to education. Therefore, this research seeks to resolve the questions: How to measure the realization of the right to education in an IDE that responds to the re-textualization of the right to education in Colombia? and what are the factors associated with the gaps in the implementation of the right to education? It is essential to understand what are the factors that limit the full enjoyment of the right to education in order to provide inputs to policy makers for the design of a course of actions that allow the realization of the right to education. In addition, and more importantly, it is expected that the IDE will be used as a tool for monitoring and political control
