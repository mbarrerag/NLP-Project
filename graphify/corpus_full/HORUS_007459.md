# Influence of coastal geomorphological configuration and sedimentary environment on the storage of carbon in mangroves in the Colombian Caribbean

The mangroves provide ecosystemic services such as coastal protection, the provision of shelter for multiple species, and among others, the operation as a carbon deposit. At the global level different values of carbon storage have been reported in mangroves, showing great variability in this service depending on the environmental conditions, age and geomorphological type of forests. This study aims to understand the relationship between the variation of carbon storage in mangrove ecosystems with different geomorphological configuration, in the Island of San Andrés (SAI), an open coast environment of karstic origin without influence of rivers, and in the Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta (CGSM), a deltaic environment with abundant contribution of terrigen sediments from the Magdalena river and rivers that originate in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta.
