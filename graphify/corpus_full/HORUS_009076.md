# PRODUCTION OF ANION REFERENCE MATERIAL AND CATIONS AVAILABLE IN SWEELTH

The determination of elements available in soils presents several analytical challenges associated with the dependence of the methods used; generating a limitation in the assurance of validity and comparability of laboratory results. Thus, metrological tools are needed to help mitigate this limitation, so in this work two reference materials (MR) were developed to measure iron, copper, sulphates, chlorides (MR1) and phosphorus available in soils (MR2). The MR were prepared from two commercially acquired soil samples, which were subjected to stabilization of the species available by means of soil washings, subsequently they were dried to 50 °C for 48 hours. Once dry, the soil was ground and sifted, collecting less than 200 ¿m. The assessment of homogeneity of MR1 was performed by ICP-MS for Fe and Cu using the extraction method with DTPA-CaCl2 based on the TNC 5526:2007 was performed by means of ionic chromatography using the method of water extraction based on the TNC 5402:2006
