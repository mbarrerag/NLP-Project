# - Overoxygenate in trance

-Overoxygenar en trance is a research-creation project that sprouts from the body as a living and porous territory, where the breath is rooted, the tremor is heard and the memory is made gesture. This proposal moves from the territory to the city of Bogotá, forming an incarnate transit that interweaves sensitive, physical and artistic practices that blur the boundaries between the human and the animal, opens the way to the trance, and enables other modes of perception, existence and relation. > 65533; In this process, the body is configured as a living laboratory: a sensitive threshold crossed by elementary movements, voices without language, ancient vibrations. It is not a question of representing, but of letting oneself be crossed. It is a question of becoming rhythm, murmur, tremor. Thus, the boundaries of the modern self are removed to listen to the plant, the invisible and the molecular thing. > 65533; This work is part of the cracks of the reexistence, the gesture as a sensitive archive and the listening way of knowing. Thus, the boundaries of the modern self are removed from the conceptual, the invisible and the molecular thing, the one, the one that is located, the one is the one
