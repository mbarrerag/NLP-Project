# Solvent production by intensified processes with fermentation and coupled separation

The production of butanol by fermenting means is a process with more than 100 years of history that, for several decades, was the main route for obtaining this commodity, but this process lost relevance to the development of the petrochemical industry (Moon et al., 2016). Currently, the need to develop environmentally friendly processes, within the biorefinerías scheme and under the concept of circular economy has aroused interest in the fermentation ABE taking advantage of the natural capacity of the Clostridia family and the improvements that by genetic engineering can be implemented.The present project seeks to contribute in the development of a sustainable process for the production of butanol, applying the strategy of fermentation integrated with recovery of products by flash distillation, which has shown to be a valid option to give competitiveness to this process (Mariano et al. 2011).The project takes advantage of the development and capacities of the Institute of Biotechnology for the study of fermenting processes, and the department of Chemical Engineering of the Faculty of Engineering will be analyzed the process of separation by liquid-vapor. Initially will be studied the strains available in the bank of the same IBUN during the most promising forms, later will be studied and the process of the ABEA In addition, the developments obtained in this project will be synergistically promoted with others obtained in the engineering faculty groups, mainly in the areas of biorefinerías, alcohol chemistry and fermentation of second and third generation, and in the groups of the Institute of Biotechnology, focused on bioprospecting, solvent-genic microorganisms and improvement of fermenting processes. In the short and medium term, it is sought to consolidate an interfacultades and intersed alliance that allows maximizing the utilization of the capacities of the UN in the development of sustainable processes of biotechnology base
