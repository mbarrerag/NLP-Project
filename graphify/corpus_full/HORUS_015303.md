# DEVELOPMENT OF AN INSTRUMENT FOR QUANTITATIVE EVALUATION OF THE EFFECTIVE PUBLIC AREA OF VECINAL LEVEL

The purpose of this work is to understand that, in order to enhance its benefits to users, ensure its sustainability, enjoyment and access, it is necessary to manage it on the basis of permanent activities that require monitoring. The design of a public space (EP) is as important as its management, however, the planning and design of new spaces are research fields that predominate. The evaluation of the quality of the EP is part of the management. Thus, the approach of an evaluation instrument based on a quantitative method can be a contribution to the management. Key words: public space; quality of life; urban area This presentation is part of the products proposed in the framework of the Research Project 55014. The proposed instrument serves for the elaboration of a technical diagnosis of the condition of the EP.
