# THE SISEMIC PLACE OF THE PRISIDENCIAL ELECTION.

The text proposes an analysis of the electoral process from the perspective of the political regime, on the understanding that a change of government does not mechanically lead to regime change; it aims to provide elements of judgment for a critical and more complex understanding of the political moment.In developing these purposes, it has been structured in the following paragraphs: The regime of exceptional democracy as a negation of democracy; The peace agreement as a democratizing purpose of the political regime; The blockade of the peace agreement and the purported re-conduction towards war; The authoritarian strengthening of democracy of exception; The rebel transgression of democracy of exception; The electoral moment and social-liberal progressism
