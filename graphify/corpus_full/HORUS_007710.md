# Countercurrent Dance: Interdisciplinary challenge for the first experience of the national circulation of the UNAL Triádico Ballet

The Ballet Triádico UNAL (BTU) is currently an adaptation to the context and Colombian culture of the work Das Triadisches Ballett de Oskar Schelemmer (1922) - known as the work of total art due to the perfect integration of various artistic disciplines in its conception. The project began as a commemoration of the centenary of the German Bauhaus school in 2019, promoted by an interdisciplinary group of students and professors of the Faculty of Arts, which today form a seedbed of research and creation on pedagogy in arts, seeking to revive the Bauhausian spirit of union, cooperation and experimentation among diverse disciplines. In 2023, as a project of research and creation that counted with support from the Vice-decanature of research, the second version of the Ballet Triádico UNAL, was produced, to which was given the name of _x0093_ Contracorriente_x00_. This put on stage, inspired in Leticia, the waters of our Colombian landscape and its cycles, was already presented in various academic and cultural scenarios of the city, inside and outside of the university, such as the University of the Andes and the Museum of Arte Miguel. The main challenges are: the systematization of the operating processes, the programming or automation of lighting; the construction of a suitable packaging for clothing; the recording of music; among others that help facilitate logistics and that reduce the number of people involved in production, reducing costs. Considering that a real case is being addressed, the methodologies of interdisciplinary workshops of creation and work for challenges or projects, as common in the seedbed, will be applied to attend one of the invitations and achieve the first experience of national circulation of the Triple-Couple Ballet. This artistic and pedagogical exercise will contribute to the training of students linked through a real experience of cultural management and scenic production, in a platform open to experimentation and body-manual practice from different disciplines. In addition, making it part of the network, the partners of the network, will contribute to the formation of students linked to a real experience of cultural management and scenic production
