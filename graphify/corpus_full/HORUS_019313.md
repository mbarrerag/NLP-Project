# Soft tissue necrotizing infections in children, experience in a pediatric hospital.

In recent years, the incidence of necrosizing infections of soft tissues is rare in the paediatric population, with inconclusive morbidity and mortality. In recent years, we have experienced an increase in cases in the pediatric surgery service of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia and the Hospital la Misericordia Foundation. The objective of this study is to characterize the population with this type of infections that was taken into surgical procedures by our service to understand the behavior in our environment. It is a descriptive study in which a total of 52 patients were collected between January 2017 and December 2021 and demographic variables were evaluated, clinical conditions, surgical interventions and mortality. The results show an average age of 8 years and predominance of female sex with 65.4%. The predominant risk factors were neutropenia (71.1%) and acute lymphoblastic leukemia (74.1%).
