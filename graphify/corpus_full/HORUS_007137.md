# Deposition and characterization of DLC films doped with silver nanoparticles

This research employed nanotechnology as a pillar for the development of Diamond Like Carbon (DLC) hard coatings, using the PECVD technique, and thus achieving the study of microstructural, mechanical, tribological, anticorrosive and bactericidal properties of these coatings doped with silver nanoparticles. Silver nanoparticles were used with sizes between 10 and 50 nm in a hexane suspension. This suspension was obtained by means of the agitation process under different conditions in a high-power ultrasound equipment. This process allowed the integration of the nanoparticles of silver into the DLC deposited on titane alloy substrates Ti6Al4V. The coatings were characterized by various techniques, to determine the changes in the microstructural, mechanical, tribological, bacterial and antichorrhic properties
