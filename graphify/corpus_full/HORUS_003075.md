# SIMULATION OF CAUDALS IN PARAMO USING A NEURONAL NETWORK IN OTHER TEMPORARY RESOLUTIONS

The hydrological modelling in the basins of moorland is configured as a tool for the management and evaluation of the water resource, especially in basins that provide water for human consumption. Traditionally, these simulations have been carried out with conceptual hydrological models or physically based. In recent years, it has been shown that the use of neural networks for this purpose generates better results than the traditional approach. However, this approach has not been implemented or studied in moors. This research focuses on hydrological modelling of the flow in the Chuza river basin, located in the Chingaza parame, using the Long-Short Term Memory neural network model (LSTM). This model was implemented in different temporal resolutions (daily, weekly, monthly, and other additions) to evaluate its performance and the representation of the seasonality model, peak and base flows.
