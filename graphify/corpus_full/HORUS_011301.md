# α-Fe2O3-based solids as a Fenton process catalyst.

In response to the need to design water treatment technologies, Fenton-type reactions have emerged as Advanced Oxidation Processes that have demonstrated high efficiency and potential application in pollutant degradation. Moreover, iron oxides have been widely used as catalysts for Fenton systems. The following document details the synthesis, characterization and evaluation of α-Fe2O3-based solids as catalysts for the Fenton-type reaction for the degradation of the textile colour AR 145. Solids of α-Fe2O3 were synthesized and characterized using techniques such as: SEM, TEM, DRX, Espectroscopes IR, Raman and Vis-NIR, sortometry, TGA/DSC, among others. Subsequently, their catalytic activity in RA 145 degradation was evaluated. The results obtained showed the successful synthesis of solids with multi-ramified morphology and haematite-type structure. In addition, the Cu-Co/α-Fe2O3 solid exhibited the best catalytic performance, reaching a percentage of AR 145 oreization higher than 70% in soft conditions and 180 minutes of reaction
