# OXIDATIVE DEGRADATION OF A RESIDUAL INDUSTRIAL WATER TEXTILE WITH HIGH CONTENT OF BLACK COLORANT ACE 194 MEASURED BY THE ELECTRO-FENTON PROCESS. VALUATION OF OPERATIONAL PARAMETERS

The EF is an advanced electrochemical process of oxidation based on the continuous electro-generation of hydrogen peroxide peroxide (H2O2) in-situ and the regeneration of Fe2+ (fe3+ reduction) in the cathode. The production of (OH), using Fenton reagent, generates a series of oxidation reactions which degrade the organic matter present in an aqueous matrix. This process has shown high effectiveness in the treatment of effluents contaminated with pesticides, dyes and drugs. The EF efficiency was achieved by means of colour and DQO of 63% and 95% respectively. However, the values of DQO (1300 mg O2/L) and DBO5 (460 mg O2/L) were performed in previous studies, developed by the GI-PRISMA, from a design of experiments of two factors and two factors. The EF was achieved by means of colour and DQO of 63% and 95%, respectively respectively ) to a concentration of Fe2+ constant (1mM) in the removal efficiency of COD, color, and operational costs. For the effect of the Fe2+ concentration, removals of COD were obtained between 84% and 92%, of color from 54% to 91% and of OCTO from 89% to 93%. For the effect of the current, removals of COD were obtained between 88% and 92%, of color from 65% to 96% and of OCTO from 51% to 94%. With these results it is possible to comply with the Colombian environmental regulations (DQO<400 mg O2/L) except for the test at 100mA. The minimum operating cost of EF, to comply with the legislation, was 5.44 USD/m3 of ARND treated, for a current of 200mA
