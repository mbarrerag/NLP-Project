# The musical ecfrasis in the River of Time, work of Colombian composer William Leonardo Sáenz García

The existence of several original works for guitar that use as a material compositional elements extra musical, has spread within the compositional field to enrich the catalogue of works for the instrument, the literature has been a source of inspiration in the creation of new repertoires.The original work for guitar presented here, takes elements of the auto fictional saga “The River of Time” of the Colombian writer Fernando Vallejo; what through its five books, gathered in a single work, narrates its life through the years, mixing both real and fictional events as an anecdote, where it develops its literary style recognized within Latin American letters, not only because of its difficult stylistic enclosing, but also because of its strong diatribes against the church, the state and the family.The process of transmediation from one artistic medium to another, in this case of the literature to the music, is known as écphrational has its own musical structure, through which the composer takes elements of the work of Vallejo and the recreates of musical form in the solo guitar. .
