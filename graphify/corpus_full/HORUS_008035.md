# Education and multilingualism in school: study on the use of languages in the classrooms of San Andrés Isla

The islands of San Andrés and Providencia are in a complex sociolinguistic situation; in the territory converge Spanish (the official national language), the Creole sanandresano (native language of the islands) and English (language inherited by settlers). In this situation, the population has expressed the need to propose an educational model that includes teaching and learning processes in the three languages. That is why this research sought to describe the relations between these languages in the classrooms of educational institutions in San Andrés Isla, to propose a material with pedagogical and didactic guidelines aimed at teachers who seek to incorporate the Creole variety in the communication of their classes. To carry out this, a qualitative analysis was carried out that made use of ethnography in the classroom to observe, describe and analyze what was happening in the classes. Three educational institutions were visited: an institution of San Luis me, traditional area of the island, an institution in ‘La Loma’, where many root and island families were located, and another one in the Center of the island, where there is a large population from the mainland Colombia.
