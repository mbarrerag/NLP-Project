# STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY FOR THE ESTIMATION OF THE INDIVIDUAL CARBON HAIR IN THREE HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS

The estimation of carbon footprint in educational institutions is essential because of its great environmental impact, derived from its size, infrastructure and daily activities, ranging from energy consumption to mobility of students, teachers and staff. A preliminary pilot study is conducted to address the estimation of individual carbon footprint in three institutions of Higher Education: Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Sede Medellín, Nucleo el Volador, the Envigado University Institution and the Universidad Lasallista Corporation. Through a descriptive analysis of the information collected and using a stratified random sampling for each institution, the sample size necessary to estimate the contribution of the individual carbon footprint to the total calculation of the carbon footprint for each of the institutions involved in the study is determined
