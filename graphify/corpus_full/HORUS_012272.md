# Monograph: self-care guide in type 2 diabetes mellitus.

Chronic pathologies and particularly type 2 diabetes mellitus (DM2) represent a high economic and care burden for health systems. Although there has been considerable progress in therapeutic strategies, preventive measures should not be overlooked: there is strong evidence of the impact of self-care education strategies in diabetes (DSME) on empowering patients and their families in addition to improving control of their disease. However, the structure of the current medical exercise in its different scenarios rarely tends for this reason; the high care burden and limited time hinder the process of educating: a reasonable and cost-effective alternative to alleviate this deficit is the use of a web consultation platform for the patient and his family, within the framework of digital education tools. This monograph collects scientific evidence and recommendations in: -DSME -Lifestyle interventions with emphasis on nutrition and physical activity -Glucemic monitoring and metabolic goals that each patient should know and pursue for the control of DM2 and the most frequent comorbidities -Identification of microvascular and macrovascular complications of the disease, as well as the prevention of their disease
