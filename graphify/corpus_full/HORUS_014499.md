# Colombia bioblue: two seas, one country; territories to explore

CECIMAR aims to develop academic research and extension activities in accordance with the principles of the National University of Colombia. It proposed the creation of the National Chair “Colombia bioblue: two seas, one country; territories to be explored”, with the objective of “Presenting and discussing concepts, contexts and key tools, necessary for the development of theoretical and practical competencies, that will allow to strengthen the knowledge of the ocean earth system, the biological, ecological, environmental, sociocultural and economic importance of the ocean, particularly of the marine-coster systems of Colombia.” It was held in 14 sessions, 53 lectures were given and 42 national and four international exhibitors (National University 45.2 per cent and 54.8 per cent of other institutions). Given the great assistance and political and health difficulties of that time, it was proposed to edit a book that would gather, if not all, most of the presentations that cover the main branches of the sea sciences.-- Contracadas
