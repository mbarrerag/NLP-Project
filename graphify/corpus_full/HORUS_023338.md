# ARCHITECTONIC AND ARCHITECTONIC FORMS OF THE URBAN VOICE, CASE OF BOGOTÁ MORPHOLOGICAL ARCHITECTONIC BUBLIC SPACE

ABSTRACT This presentation focuses on the public space of Bogotá from the point of view of its architectural quality and its consequences in urban life. A series of spaces from different periods have been selected within a previous framework represented in a series of plans of Nolli (1538 - 1992) of the entire extension of the city. However, this selection does not aim to encompass the entire public space, but is a sample of what will be an integral typological study that allows to infer other urban phenomena from the collective habitability (Gelh, 2014). The point of view of formal analysis is reversed by considering the public space as an architectural object with properties of composition, proportion and formal structure. To do this, it is necessary to represent the space both in its reverse Nolli version, and in physical models to establish what the architecture of the public space would be
