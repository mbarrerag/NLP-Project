# Territorial planning and environmental management of the municipalities that make up the Administrative and Planning Province (PAP) of Sumapaz

This work identifies the difficulties and challenges faced by the municipalities belonging to the Administrative and Planning Province (PAP) of the Sumapaz, in relation to the planning and environmental protection of the territory, considering that this territorial associative scheme promotes cooperation between municipalities to manage issues of common interest. The municipalities of the PAP of the Sumapaz defined as an issue of associative interest the one regarding the extension of the agricultural border that threatens the conservation of the Cruz Verde Sumapaz paramo, which has coverage in five municipalities: Arbelez, Cabrera, Pasca, San Bernardo and Venice. The analysis carried out allows to establish several problems in the territorial and environmental management order for the conservation of the paramo: existence of different planning and management instruments, the lack of control and surveillance on the uses of the soil in the area of paramo by the public entities, the need to regulate the areas adjacent to the Paramo with a buffer function, the action disarticulated of the institutions (environmental, the agricultural and territorial sector) and the complexity for environmental governance linked to the multiplicity of the actors involved. Finally, it concludes and the participation and the Bernardo and Venice. The analysis allows several establishment problems in the territorial and environmental planning for the conservation of the moor: the existence of different planning and management instruments, lake of control and surveillance of land use in the moor zone by public entities, the need to regulate the areas adjacent to the paramo for planning buffer function, the disjointed action of institutions (environmental, agricultural and territorial) and the complexity of environmental governance linked to the multiple application of actors involved. Ultimately, it is concluded that the scenario for participation and coordination between the State and social actors must be strengthened to enhance the articulation of instruments and public entities, with the objective of ensuring the environmental protection of the territory and the rights of the community
