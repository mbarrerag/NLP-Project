# Energy analysis, exergetics and proposals for improvements of a 90 MW cogeneration plant operating under the STIG cycle.

Proelectrica, was the first private generation power plant in Colombia. It is located in the city of Cartagena and has been operational since 1993. Currently, Proelectrica operates under the model of charge for reliability, with an electrical power generation capacity of 90 MW to 66 000 kV. The power plant consists of 2 turbines General Electric LM5000 operating under the STIG cycle. To determine the current state of the plant was carried out an energetic and exergetic study of the generation cycle. It was determined that the energy efficiency is 42,12 % and the exergetic one of 35,62 %. Likewise, it was evidenced that the equipment with the greatest loss of exergía is the combustion chamber with 68,78 %, The efficiency of the turbines and the recovery boilers have fallen by 2,08 % and 12,71 %, compared to the efficiency of design.
