# SYNCOPE AND ARTERIAL HYPOTENSION AS AN ADVERSE EFFECT OF LATANOPROST

The objective of the study was to present the case of a patient with episodes of syncope and hypotension related to the use of latanoprost. Case report: A 32-year-old man with a bilateral enlarged cup/disk ratio, with normal visual fields, an abnormal optic nerve optical coherence tomography, and a 582/563 μm pachymetry. Eye examination: best-corrected visual acuity of 20/20 in both eyes, IOP: 13/13 mmHg OU, and a cup-to-disk ratio of 0.7/0.7. A water-drinking test was performed, with a peak IOP of 22 mmHg OD and 21 mmHg OS. Latanoprost was prescribed to both eyes. Later, he presented three episodes of syncope, with arterial hypotension that required hospital care. No related structural or functional cardiovascular lesions were found. At this point, the possibility of a secondary effect from the use of latanoprost was considered. When episodes of hypotension or syncop e are documented in a patient taking latanoprost and no other possible triggers are found, it is prudent to opt for another pharmacological or non-pharmacological hypotensive treatment for glaucoma control
