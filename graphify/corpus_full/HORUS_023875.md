# TOLIMA, COLOMBIA.

Abstract - A descriptive cross-sectional study carried out for the 47 municipalities of the department of Tolima, Colombia, neighboring the department of Cundinamarca, where Bogotá, the capital of the country, is located. The objective was to analyze the barriers of geographical access and the inventory of human talent and dental services in this department. A system of distance and time measurement was applied to identify the duration of the displacement from each municipality to Ibagué (departmental capital) in public transport. The determinant of geographical access barrier endorsed by the World Health Organization (WHO) is a duration of travel longer than four hours. It was found that the first level of care in the public sector is present in all municipalities, the second level is at six; the third level is at Ibagué only. In the department of Tolima there are geographical accessibility barriers to health and, specifically, to oral health.
