# Implementation of a tool for the design of lighting systems.

The application of electrical engineering must comply with a series of regulatory policies aimed at standardizing components and installations, thus reducing electrical risks and improving the reliability and efficiency of the installations. In Colombia, the "Technical Regulation of Electrical Installations - RETIE" has been published and connoted of mandatory compliance, as well as the "Technical Regulation of Lighting and Public Illumination - RETILAP" establishing the requirements and measures of mandatory compliance in lighting systems, whose legitimate objects are: • National security in terms of guaranteeing energy supply through the use of systems and products that apply the Rational Use of Energy. • The protection of human life and health. • The protection of animal and plant life. • The prevention of practices that may mislead the user. • The protection of the environment. Since RETILAP is a technical-legal instrument throughout the Colombian territory, it is of vital importance to know, interpret and know how to properly implement each and every one of the concepts established therein, especially the DETAILED DESIGN of interior type lighting. Due to the technical absence of a professional and educational tool to interact with each of the requirements necessary to perform the necessary manual calculations in an interior lighting design, the understanding of the basic requirements demanded in Section 210.2.3 are often misunderstood. Therefore, this degree work has been carried out with the aim of making up for its absence in Colombia
