# Design and validation of an instrument for identifying isoaparence in the primary packaging of injectable drugs.

The isoaparency is the similarity in the packaging or labelling of medicines that increases the probability of making mistakes during healthcare. Globally, each country is free to manage these risks by implementing safety barriers so that no medication errors are triggered in a patient. The aim of this research was to design and validate an instrument by characterizing the minimum attributes in the primary packaging of injectable drugs that allows to identify their isoaparency. The materials and methods for developing this master's thesis consisted of six stages. In the first one a comprehensive bibliographical review and exploration of the Open Data portal was carried out. For the second one was formed a Delphi group with experts at national and international level. In the third one was designed an instrument for the collection and analysis of information. The fourth consisted in piloting the instrument in a low complexity institution. In the fifth was validated the instrument. The sixth was oriented to construct a digital tool.
