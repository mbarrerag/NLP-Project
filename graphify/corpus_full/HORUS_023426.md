# Optimization of pre-forced drawer beams for continuous bridges.

The usual process of designing bridges requires large amounts of time and expertise from the designer. In order to obtain an economic, safe and functional design, in this study a computational tool was developed in the programming language MATLAB R2019b that allows the optimization of continuous beams type drawer using genetic algorithms with generational gap, metaheuristic technique inspired by the natural selection process. The verification of the section was carried out using as a reference the Colombina Standard of Seismic Design of Bridges, considering totally and partially pre-forced elements, which are stressed in a single stage. As selection mechanisms were used: selection by roulette (RWS), random selection (RAND), selection by stochastic sampling (SUS) and tournament selection (TOUR), for the crossing operator were evaluated: crossing at 2 points, flat crossing and uniform crossing.
