# Self-esteem, self-efficacy, resilience and risky sexual behaviour in adolescents and young people in Bogotá

to establish the relationship between self-esteem, self-effectiveness and resilience with sexual risk behaviors in adolescents and young people enrolled in public educational institutions in the town of Tunjuelito in the south of Bogotá. quantitative research with a cross-sectional correlational design. The instruments used were the scales of self-esteem, general self-efficacy, resilience and the F-M scale of intention for risk sexual behaviors. For the analysis, the descriptive statistics and the coefficients of Spearman, d de Somers, Tau-b de Kendall, Tau-c de Kendall and Gamma were used. 458 students from 15 to 20 years of 3 public schools in the town of Tunjuelito, 53.9% of the participants were women and 46.1% of the men. The mean of self-esteem of the participants was 62.82, of self-efficacy 67.78, of resilience 66.34 and of sexual risk behaviors 36.30. The values of self-efficacy and resilience were higher in men and of the sexual risk behaviors in women.
