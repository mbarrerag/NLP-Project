# Machine Learning Operations applied to the development and provisioning of models

In the current era of software engineering, where Machine Learning (ML) plays a crucial role in technological innovation, the effective application of development and operation practices is essential. DevSecOps's approach to development security operations has been popularized by its ability to integrate safety and quality in all stages of the life cycle of secure software development. However, in the specific context of Machine Learning, there is a need for a specialized approach that considers the characteristics of models and algorithms used. Machine Learning Operations (MLops), despite its relative novelty, seeks to establish a framework to characterize the life cycle of ML development, uncouple it from software development and ensure quality attributes such as scalability, sustainability and safety. It also faces the challenge of managing training data, security in the field of analysis and development models, and the need for a quality oriented culture. This work focuses on how the implementation of MLOps can positively impact the management of the ML development cycle, with the aim of contributing to the knowledge, innovation and development of the best practices. establishment a framework for characterizing the ML development lifecycle, decoupling it from software development, and acquiring quality attributes such as stability, maintainability, and security. It also staples with challenges related to managing training data, security throughout the model analysis, development and deployment process, and the need for a quality-oriented culture. This these approaches on researching how the implementation of MLOps can positively impact the management of the ML development lifecycle, with the goal of contributing to knowledge in this emerging field and promoting the adoption of best practices in ML-based solutions
