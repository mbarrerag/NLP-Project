# Center for Sustainable Livelihoods of Marine-Coast Communities

The proposal for the creation of the Center for Thought in Sustainable Living of Marine-Costereal Communities in Colombia is presented as a comprehensive response to the complex challenges faced by the marine-coast communities of the country. These communities, intrinsically linked to marine and coastal ecosystems for their subsistence, are under increasing pressure due to overexploitation of resources, climate change, coastal erosion, pollution, and lack of effective environmental management policies. This proposal seeks to promote a holistic approach that integrates environmental, economic and social considerations. The aim of the center is to generate interdisciplinary knowledge, foster collaboration between the private sector, public entities and civil society, and develop sustainable strategies that are not only environmentally responsible but also economically viable and socially fair for these communities. The project is justified in the urgent need to address in an integrated way the environmental, economic and social challenges, promoting sustainable solutions that improve the quality of life of communities and protect natural resources. With clear objectives such as the articulation of knowledge within the National University, the development of communication strategies to increase the visibility of the center, and the generation of spaces of interaction between key actors, the project hopes to achieve tangible results, regional development and practices, with the development, with the national development and with clear objectives
