# Evaluation of the model of compensation and monitoring for logging in the Capital District

There is a difficulty to objectively evaluate public policies and the activity of the State. It is for this reason that we must seek new guidelines to consolidate evaluation processes in accordance with the collected evidence. To elaborate this methodology, it is necessary to consider that Colombia is a social state of law, which implies that both its officials and its entities are subject to the law, they fulfill a previously established purpose and evolves according to the aspirations and desires of each society that is consolidated in the processes of democratic election In this framework we chose the constitutional and legal function of the Colombian State to protect its ecosystems and, in this sense, to regulate everything regarding the management of the urban woodland, preventing the loss of plant material in the city. Following this same reasoning, the democratic process is fully realized with the citizen participation, which has one of its expressions the oversight function that citizens can exercise over the institutions. Thus, in relation to the number of individuals and their corresponding compensation, according to the fact that every individual arboreo must be compensated to prevent the environmental deterioration, the State, in fulfilment of its purpose, must exercise a function of surveillance and control and make effective its sanctionable data, demanding to the damage that they produce. The Commission has been able to assess the impact of the measures taken by the State to ensure that the measures taken by the State are consistent with the objectives of the internal market.
