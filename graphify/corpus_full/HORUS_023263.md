# The role of the State in forest management in Colombia, historical perspective and case study

Deforestation is probably the most serious environmental problem facing Colombia today. The effects of forest logging will be devastating for the Colombian population and the world. Although deforestation is a complex phenomenon in which a diversity of actors has been involved, this thesis is deliberately focused on analyzing the role of the State in forest management in Colombia. Two methodological approaches are developed, an analysis of the transformations of the environmental state since the 19th century and the analysis of a case study in the Department of Guaviare between the years 2014-2018. Thanks to these approaches two trends in the mechanisms of state management were identified. Research found that the main mechanism of the State for forest management in Colombia since the 19th century has been the granting of land rights. Likewise, despite the fact that the Colombian environmental state has greatly increased its size, technical capacity and specialization in the last 50 years, it has not been able to find the right mechanisms for better forest management in the country.
