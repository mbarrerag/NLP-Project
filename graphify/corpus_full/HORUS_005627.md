# Proposal for the integration of active demand into the distribution system: related variables, integration scenarios and case study

This article proposes the integration of active demand into the distribution system by using meta-heuristic techniques. It uses the system dynamics methodology, which allows identifying variables relevant to demand participation. The systemic approach used helps characterize both endogenous and exogenous variables, which is crucial to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of the process of integration of active demand. In addition, the transition time in the evolution of the system is considered. The work proposes a development by stages, defining different scenarios and integrating them with the data science to create roadmaps adapted to different distribution systems and specific conditions. A classic case study is presented that evaluates the impact of this transition and determines the regulatory policy necessary to achieve an effective integration of active demand
