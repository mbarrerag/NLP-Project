# Mental health of human talent in health during the course of the 19th century in Colombia

Understanding and addressing mental health in this population has been constituted as a challenge of the current phase of the pandemic. to describe the risks in mental health, consumption of psychoactive substances and violence in health workers during the isolation period due to the pandemic by COVID-19. descriptive-correlational cross-sectional study. The sample was composed of 3163 health professionals, through non-probabilistic incidental sampling, with use of instruments such as Self-Reported Questionnaire (SRQ), Reduced Scale of Resilience of Connor-Davidson Resilience, ASSIST, in addition to sociodemographic data that investigated for violence and for seeking help during the first phase of the pandemic. 27.4% presented symptoms of anxiety or depression, 4.9% presented risk of suicide, 14.1% presented moderate or high consumption of some substance and 7.2% were exposed to some type of violence.
