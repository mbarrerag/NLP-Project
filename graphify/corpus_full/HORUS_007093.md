# The table-based role-playing game as a tool for the development of communicative competence in English: a look from non-parametric didactics

This research aims to design and determine the degree of contribution of a didactic strategy that allows the strengthening of the communicative competence in English of undergraduate students of the National University of Colombia. For this, different learning objectives were implemented over the course of sixteen weeks, the corresponding ones were based on the ability of the participants to expand their world of meaning through language and their particular interests. Likewise, it was sought to strengthen the learning process of the players through the increase of the quality of their dialogical interactions, in order to improve their performance in the skills of listening and speaking. Consequently, thanks to the various conceptual references and the corresponding data analysis, it was possible to show that the process was effective for both students and the institution .
