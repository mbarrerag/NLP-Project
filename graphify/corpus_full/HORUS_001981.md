# Young and Creole aesthetics: Cultural identities in the music industry of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina

This research aims to understand how young artists of the Department Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina in the Colombian island Caribbean configure their cultural identities in the various practices linked to the music industry, taking into account the wide and complex context of conflicts and structural violence of their societies. From decolonial theoretical approaches of the Caribbean studies, in particular from the aesthetic-political project of Eduard Glissant and the identity joints of Stuart Hall, the research analyzes according to the semiotic multimodal practices, of bodily, linguistic and (audio)visual type, of a reduced sample of artists of the islands, collected through the ethnographic and narrative method from 2017 to 2023. the chosen artists are joe taylor the fire band, elkin robinson, hety and zambo, luis osorio and oliva. The research, in particular, is structured in the description and understanding of three categories of identities in the Caribbean, which are the multidimensional concept of the territory and languages.
