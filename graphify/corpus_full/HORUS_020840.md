# PRESENTATION OF ORGANIC AND BIOMASS WASTE FOR THE PRODUCTION OF METAN AND BIOABONOUS GAS AND FOR THE EVALUATION OF THE ELECTRIC ENERGY COGENERATION POTENTIAL WITH GAS MICROTURBINE IN THE NAZARETH INDIGENOUS COMMUNITY OF THE MUNICIPY OF LATICIA, AMAZONS

The project will be developed in the Community of Nazareth, of the municipality of Leticia, with 1053 inhabitants, where it is intended to provide a comprehensive solution to a problem generated by the waste produced and to be able to meet a needs of generating alternative and local energies. The energy provided by fossil fuels and modern technology has had both positive and harmful effects on the environment, term that defines the effect that produces a certain anthropic action on the environment in which human settlements are developed. The consumption of energy on a global scale grows every year, the development of certain alternative sources becomes increasingly transparent, especially in terms of the elimination of residues and the use of energy, with the eventuality of reducing the dependence on limited fossil fuels. The project seeks to use organic residues, harvest residues and residual biomass of the forest available in its locality, which are not being used, therefore will be used for the generation of methane gas through two tubular chambers operating as biodigestor of anaerobia in situ and integrate it into a system of microturbine of gas to measure the potential in the generation of bioproduct and clor, in real time. The project seeks to develop technologies based on the integration of biogas generation elements, power systems, from signal measurement and adequacy, data acquisition, implementation of technologies associated with information and visualization management, based on a biocapacity of the territory without exceeding its limits of use, for which it will have maps, ecogeographical characterization and multiactivity of the territory (ecological maps, social cartography and maps of housing linking areas) of the indigenous community as a potential scenario for biogas production. The participation of the community throughout the project development will allow the development of strategies for the social appropriation of knowledge, which will allow the exchange of knowledge, its monitoring and the sustainability of the project over time
