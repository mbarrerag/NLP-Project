# Proposal of a simulated case with a CTSYV approach as a scientific controversy to teach about climate change theories in middle school students.

The great lack of knowledge at the social level and more specifically at school of what climate change really is, either due to excess information, inaccuracy in sources or lack of scientific basis, has been a constant, which gives rise to various versions of this topic. For this reason, the main reason for the following research is to create a didactic sequence that allows teaching two media groups around different theories existing in both the anthropogenic and natural fields about the generation of climate changes on land and from there generate an environment of creation of scientific ideas from a controversy or debate from the two groups of students focusing on different theories of anthropogenic character around the generation of current climate change and contrasting them with those related to solar activity as a historical vector of climate influence. Through a didactic sequence focused on learning by simulation of structured cases in which the teacher acts as a facilitator of information for the development of activities and with the help of guided questions implemented from ICT platforms and using the prior bibliographic review, it will lead to the two groups of students of media during the research so that they themselves identify the solar variability and causes (the resulting from the fact that they take to identify the solar variability)
