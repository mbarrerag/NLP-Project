# Miasis in cancer patients at the National Cancer Institute between 2008 and 2018: Case Series

Abstract Introduction: Myasis can be considered a neglected disease; it corresponds to the infestation of dipterian larvae in skin, wounds or natural cavities. The literature for oncological patients with infestation is limited for Colombia. A series of cases of miasis were performed in patients attended at the National Cancer Institute from 2008 to 2018; data were obtained from the clinical histories in order to characterize sociodemographic, oncological variables and received treatment. 32 records were found: 27 met the inclusion criteria, median age 69 years, 74% were men, 48% were from rural areas, 33.3% from areas with heights above 2000 msnm, 70% had head and neck cancer, followed by breast cancer 14%; 40% ECOG 2-3; 77% viewed larvae before consulting; no patients presented sepsis at the time of consultation, 29% presented secretion in infected areas, 85% received ivermectin and cures by nursing; 70% patients received systemic antibiotics, and 60% other treatments in addition to mechanical removal and iverconceptions
