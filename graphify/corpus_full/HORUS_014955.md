# METHODOLOGY FOR QUANTITATIVE STRATEGIC ANALYSIS IN PROJECTS TO PARTICULAR FROM RISK ANALYSIS

This article proposes a methodology for the construction of a matrix of analysis of weaknesses, opportunities, strengths and threats in the formulation and evaluation of projects, based on quantitative risk studies. It is based on a literature review to obtain a description of the analysis of weaknesses, opportunities, strengths and threats and their uses, as well as tools in the risk studies of projects. It then shows a methodological framework for the construction of analysis of weaknesses, opportunities, strengths and quantitative threats and the necessary sections to carry out the analysis, which is validated with an application case. It is concluded that, by quantitative analysis of risks in projects, the subjectivity in the construction of the strategic matrix of weaknesses, opportunities, strengths and threats can be reduced
