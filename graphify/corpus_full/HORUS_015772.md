# SIMULACRO: A STRATEGY FROM THE DISEASE PAT THE MANAGEMENT OF THE RISK OF EMERGENCY AND DISASTER IN CUNDINAMARCA TAUSA

Self-compassion is understood as a state of personal well-being in which the suffering of one's own is recognized and managed, previous studies report that positively impacts the mental health of nursing professionals and contributes to their ability to provide compassionate care, which ultimately improves the perception of people with respect to the quality of care.1 The model of cultural and compassionate competence of Papadopoulos mentions self-compassion as part of cultural and compassionate consciousness, the basis for the development of this competence.2 The self-compassion is a phenomenon of relatively recent approach, and in Colombia there are few studies in this regard. Thus, the aim of the study is to describe the level of self-compassion in nursing professionals in Bogotá. Cross-sectional descriptive study. The sample corresponds to 93 nursing professionals from three university hospitals in Bogotá, who participated voluntarily, after explaining the study and signing of informed consent. With the support of the platform RedCap, the results socio-demographic, professional, as well as the Self-Compasion Scale (SCS) in its Spanish version (a: 0.77 and coefficient of women variable-retest of 0.92).3.
