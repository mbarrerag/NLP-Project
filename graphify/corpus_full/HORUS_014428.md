# Behaviour of guadua panels with ground-revoicing before horizontal force.

It can be said that vernacular architecture is the way of adaptation of the human being to the ecosystem where it inhabits looking always for a degree of comfort, wellbeing and stability. The baharek is a technique widely used in the peasant constructions of the Andean and coffee-growing regions of Colombia, in this last region was evidenced its good structural behavior during a seismic event in Quindío in 1999. The houses in the coffee-growing axis adapted to their seismic condition and the ancestors formed a culture of construction resistant earthquake in this region where they replaced the walled by panels of guadua with ground-revoque, this as a sample of the adaptation of the human being to the conditions of its location. This research was aimed at evaluating the experimental behavior of panels of guadua with revoque in land to analyze its use in a housing of social interest (VIS) from a structural system based on panels of land. As a numerical analysis was carried out for the proposed structural configuration, where the maximum efforts according to the capacities of structural elements of bamboo were verified according to the characterization of the material obtained in the municipality of Bituma Cuná
