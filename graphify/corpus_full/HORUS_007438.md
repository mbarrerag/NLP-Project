# Strategies to contribute to the dignified stay in the territory of the young people of the association Red de Mercados Agroecológicos Campesinos del Valle del Cauca (REDMAC)

In the Colombian context of rural ageing, driven largely by the migration of young farmers to urban centres, this research aimed to identify strategies that contribute to the dignified stay in the territory of the young people of the Association Red de Mercados Agroecológicos Campesinos del Valle del Cauca (REDMAC). Through a qualitative approach, with a social critical perspective and the methodology of Research Participative Action, a deep understanding of the reality of these young people and the characteristics that shape their collective identity was achieved. These features include the affectivity and the symbolic in the habitation of the territory; the seed as a symbol of the custody of life; and the migration as a practice that re-signifies the work of the peasant. Furthermore, key strategies that contribute to their dignified stay in the territory can be promoted through local strategies, as demonstrated by the process of young people of the REDMAC. This local process has contributed to the disaggregation of the decision-making and access to resources. on the territory; the seed as a symbol of life’s guardianship; and the minda as a practice that redefines the work of pedestrians. Additionally, key strategies that contribute to their differentiated permanence in the territory were identified: the recognition of their tasks, feelings, and thoughts; local and territorial education; organizational strengthening; and participation in decision-making spaces and access to resources. Although the permanence of young people faces complex structural challenges, it can be promoted through local multi-actor strategies, as demonstrated by the processes of the young people from REDMAC.
