# Method for the construction of spatially focused agro-economic reference frameworks.

The lack of knowledge about the clients and their production conditions by the financial intermediaries in the agricultural sector generates difficulties for the access of the farmers and ranchers to products and financing services for the anchoring of their economic activities.This lack of knowledge in part is due to the lack of standardized, clean and updated information on production costs incurred for the development of productive activities, which are the key input for the construction of cash flows by the areas of risk of banks, financial institutions and savings and credit cooperatives. Colombia is a megadiverse country in terms of biophysical characteristics and agricultural production systems, therefore, the budget and the time involved in carrying out field studies for the raising of agroeconomic information (costs, yields and sales prices), have been factors that hinder the sustainability of various sectoral, public and private processes that have been carried out in order to contribute to the solution of this market failure. To professionals of different entities linked to the subsector of the cattle-farming specialized in the production of milk, through workshops and interviews that were complemented with a review of literature at national and global level for the definition and characterization of a reference agricultural system (SAR) to work, also they have selected, defined and prioritized the main biophysical variables that influence the structure of production costs, to later use the process of hierarchical analysis and spatial analysis in order to delimit spatially a reference agricultural system (SAR), as a geographic framework of the study, facilitating the determination of locations and types of farms to be sampled in the construction of the agroeconomic reference framework.
