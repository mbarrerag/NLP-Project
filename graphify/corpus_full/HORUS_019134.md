# Poverty and Black Development

Poverty is a scourge that affects millions of people in the world. The impact of this condition on the developing brain is a subject that has been under study for more than 7 decades and is now a major aspect of research from neurosciences and public health. This book presents a review of literature that presents the current evidence of the implications of poverty in the development of the brain and deepens the determinants of this relationship. In this text, it is deepened in critical periods and epigenetics, attachment, chronic stress, nutrition, neurological risk factors, neurological diseases in childhood related to poverty and possibilities of resilience. This written work aims to serve as reference material and historical memory
