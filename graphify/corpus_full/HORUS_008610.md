# ANALYSIS OF THE RESULTS OF THE TESTS KNOW 11' USING STATUS STATISTICS

Students who wish to continue their studies towards higher education in Colombia have as a mandatory requirement the performance of tests called Tests Know 11. One of the purposes of the Saber 11 test is to measure the quality of education in Colombia. In this investigation, a study was carried out from the descriptive point of view of the results of the tests Know 11, in which the behavior of those tests was analyzed before, during and after the pandemic. In addition, some multivariate statistical techniques such as ACP and cluster were applied, which allowed us to study the behavior of those results during the period of time analyzed. From the results, a tentative index was proposed that allows us to classify certain individuals according to the characteristics of the studied evaluated studies
