# Identification of the gene expression in mango (Mangifera indica L. cultivar sugar) that favors the pathogenicity of the tropical endophyte Colletatrichum in fruits

The sugar mango, the fourth crop of economic importance of the department of Magdalena, is limited by the disease called anthracnosis, caused by the phytopathogenic fungus Colletatrichum that behaves as an endophyte and can become pathogen, that is, able to move from a mutualistic to parasitic relationship. Endophitism in fungi is a changing state, if favorable conditions are given for these in the host, such as biochemical and physiological changes, and environmental. The aim of this work is to identify the gene expression of mango (Mangifera indicates L. cv. Sugar) that favors the pathogenicity of the tropical endophite Colletatrichum in two states of ripening of fruits. For this purpose, an evaluation of the differences in the gene expression of fruits in two states of ripening of mango cv. Sugar, infected with the tropical endophyte C., will be carried out by the use of RNA-sec and a design of a hypothetical model of interaction
