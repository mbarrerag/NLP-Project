# Arithmetic properties of biregular overpartitions

In this paper, we investigate the arithmetic properties of B ̄l1,l2(n), the number of overpartitions where no part is divisible by l1 or l2, with l1 and l2 being relatively prime. Specifically, we establish congruences modulo 3 and powers of 2 for the pairs (l1,l2)∈{(4,3),(4,9),(8,3),(8,9)}. We use generating functions, dissection formulas, and Smoot’s implementation of Radu’s Ramanujan–Kolberg algorithm to prove the main results
