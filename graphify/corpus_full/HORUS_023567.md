# IMPROVISATION AS A TRANSVERSAL TOOL FOR MUSIC CREATION. METHOD AND PRACTICE

In the field of artistic creation, improvisation constitutes a tool that allows to unite theoretical approaches with musical practice, exploration and personal expression, being understood as an opportunity to address musical work, pedagogy and learning, from places different to the literacy writing, allowing in this way to deepen in various aspects as they can be the development of intuition and aurality. In the scenario from which this study is proposed, musicians from exclusively classical schools do not receive a structured formation in improvisation. It is expected, from the development of the project, to generate spaces of comprehensive approach towards improvisation that contribute to blur the myth of separation between different musical styles, specifically between the classical tradition of Central European origin, jazz and traditional music. To distween this wall that separates them can help the artistic practice of the musicians expand their versatility, enriching their training, as well as their results in creative terms.
