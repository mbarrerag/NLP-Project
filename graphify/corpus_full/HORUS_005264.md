# Challenges for the reintegration of ex-combatants and ex-combatants of FARC-EP. An analysis based on the results of the district census

The Peace Accords between the former guerrilla fighters of the Farc-ep and the Colombian Government promoted an innovative approach to reintegration that challenged reintegration as a traditional perspective of the disarmament, demobilization and reintegration (DRR) process. However, six years after the implementation of these agreements, several challenges have hampered an adequate reintegration process. Since the results of the district census of signatories of the Peace Agreement resident in Bogotá, interviews and four focus groups, the article analyses the challenges faced by the signatories and signatories to the agreement to carry out a process that guarantees their proper reintegration
