# PHOTOVOLVAL ARRANGEMENTS WITH CROSS STRUCTURE WITH IRREGULATE CONFIGURATION: A MODEL

Photovoltaic systems (PVS) are widely used in urban environments to produce electricity. These FV installations are designed to take advantage of unused spaces such as roofs, where other installed devices (antennas, chimneys, etc.) are available, so the available space is not regular. Therefore, a mathematical model should be designed that takes into account these irregularities in the FV installation. This article introduces a new model for such irregular PV installations, which uses an implicit formulation of the current-voltage feature of modules, thus allowing the use of complex calculations such as the Lambert-W function, which reduces the calculation time. The model is based on a system of equations formulated from the circuital relationships of the cross structure, which is an ideal candidate for irregular PV installations. Finally, the model is validated using a realistic PV installation, which is based on commercial PV modules
