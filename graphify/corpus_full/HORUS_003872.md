# Interstitial processes: the violation of the limit in programmed activities

Interstitial has been an occasional research topic since the 1950s, however this type of spatialities have been more common in contemporary architecture. However, the need for interstitial spaces to introduce another type of relations seen from ambiguity and multiplicity is limited to buildings with uses directed to the non-programmed, therefore, the aim of this work of Mastery was to transgress the associative function of interstitial in projects with programmed activities. The implemented methodology consisted in determining four prescriptions that analyzed the formal, structural, activity and scale of interstitial in the selected cases of study, subsequently these results of the analysis shaped an operational matrix that established the guidelines from formal, programmatic and projectual procedures.
