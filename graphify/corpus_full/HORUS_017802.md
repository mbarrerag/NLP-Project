# I meet art from the classroom in the I.E. Cristóbal Toro.

This research project is a cartographic invention that designs four navigation routes to travel on the pedagogy of art, the experience in the classroom, the image of the city and the position of a teacher knowledgeable of the cultural problem of a school located in the Aranjuez district of Medellín. The cartography displays images that become in four navigation cards named as follows: Sentina, Standarde, Ports-Faro and Archipelago Aranjuez. In these navigational charts approaches to the educational experience in the area of Art Education are designed, which rethink knowledge of the pedagogy of art by proposing incentives to the educational act of the artist through declarations, tours, relationships, routes, voices, experiences and events that start from the stories of life of the young people. Percibir the way of feeling of them implies recognizing the stories with which inhabit worlds, circumstances, hatreds, loves, conflicts and dreams. The expression of these affections puts in dialogue the knowledge of doing pictórico, the realization of self-ports, collages, photographs and videos with school settings. images that become four navigation charts name as follows: the Sentina, Standarde, Ports-Faro and Archipelago Aranjuez. In each of these navigational charts I design an closing to the educational experience in the area of Educational Arts, that rethink knowledge of the educational of art by proposing incentives to the educational act of the artist through declarations, tours, relationships, routes, voices, experiences and events that start from the life stories of young people.
