# Comparison of results between percutaneous and open osteotomy of the calcanum.

The osteotomies of the calcanium are procedures used to correct retrofoot deformities. Complications are usually low, occasionally in relation to wound complications in 5 - 28%, non-binding or irritation of the soft tissues, especially with approaches requiring extensive dissection. In an attempt to reduce complications of the open osteotomy of the calcanium, minimally invasive techniques have been developed to perform the cut of the percutaneous heel. A retrospective comparative study of patients treated with osteotomy of calcanium during the period 2018 to 2022 was developed. A sample of 12 patients was obtained, of which 6 received percutaneous osteotomy and 6 patients were taken to open osteotomy. The average follow-up time was higher (p 0.01), the average time for starting support was lower (p 0.04) and the mean score of the AOFAS scale was higher (p 0.03) in the open osteotomy group, however, the rate of complications was more favourable in this group (p 0.01), the mean time for starting support was less than 0,045).
