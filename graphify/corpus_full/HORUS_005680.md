# Analysis of spatial behavior of murders of leaders and social leaders in Colombia from 2000 to 2022

In Colombia, human rights violations have been reported in the national territory since the colonization period, but few researches have been carried out analyzing the spatial behavior of the human rights impacts of leaders and leaders in relation to environmental impacts, so the research presented below carries out an exploratory analysis of spatial data focused on the figures of murders of social leaders and leaders in Colombia, at a municipal scale from 2000 to 2022. It discriminates between eight social and political roles exercised by leaders and leaders among those who are Environmental Leader, Campesino Leader, Civic Leader, Community Leader, Student Leader, Indigenous Leader, Trade Union Leader and Representative or Leader of Victims. The murders are contrasted with some variables of environmental impacts, where it is sought to determine how high the correlation exists between oil extraction projects, projects with mining licenses, density of land abandonment, coca cultivation density and the area of forested by municipality. . In Colombia, human rights violations have persisted with the national occupation, projects without the third. possession or abandonment, density of coca crops, and the deforested area by municipality
