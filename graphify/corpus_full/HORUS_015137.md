# PREVALENCE OF RNA VIRUS IN TUBERS-SEMITED SOLANUM TUBEROSUM CERTIFIED SABANA CAPITAL

The potato (Solanum tuberosum) Diacol Capiro is one of the cultivars with the highest production and domestic consumption in Colombia, being the departments of Cundinamarca and Boyacá, the main producers. This crop, is affected by a complex of viruses, which decreases the quality of tubers and yields. In this work, the prevalence of the viruses of RNA was evaluated: PLRV, PVY, PVX, PVS, PVV, PVVV, PMTV and PVB, in outbreaks of tubers-seeds certified, from the Cundiboyacense savanna, through RT-qPCR. The results reveal the occurrence of seven of the eight viruses in the samples, with levels of infection of 100 % (PVS, PVX and PYVV), 75% of the virus need being a sample, 50% (PLRV), 50% (PVY), 37,5 % (PMTV) and 12.5 % (PVB).
