# EVA DESIGN TO IDENTIFY THE OBSTRUCTIVE ASSISTANCE RISK OF THE DUE.

To implement a virtual learning object to identify the risk of obstructive apnea of sleep aimed at dentists in training. This work presents the design of an object of Virtual Learning (OVA) to identify the risk of obstructive sleep apnea for dentists in training, with the aim of creating a tool of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) that optimizes resources and generates a significant learning, in a way that impacts on the early detection of AOS in order to improve the quality of life of patients. This training experience uses resources for virtual learning by highlighting methods of gamification, interactivity through transmedia resources, for which the creation of the CIELO (Centre for Innovation E-Learning Odontological) for the faculty of Odontology of the National University of Colombia is contemplated. We use the ADDIE model and we implement the Mayer Principles in the construction of the OVA. The project follows the pedagogical model of Learning Based on Problems (ABP) as a component of the OVA.
