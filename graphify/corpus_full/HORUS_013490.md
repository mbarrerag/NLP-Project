# : Textual consistency of Met. Z 17 depending on the levels of speech of dicto and re

Met. Z has been named by several scholars as “the Everest mountain of classical philosophy”. This denomination is due to the numerous challenges presented in the reading of the text, at the time of rebuilding its development and argumental approach. However, such exegetical difficulties are not the only ones present in this work: the production, transmission and configuration of this work involve its own challenges. In light of the above, this research is proposed as an approach that starts from the textual linguistics to a particular debate that deals with this text. This is the thematic progression of Met. Z.
