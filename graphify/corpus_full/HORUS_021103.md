# Domestic workers during the pandemic in Bogotá: analysis of normative effectiveness from a feminist perspective (2019-2022)

The research seedbed _x0093_Both and the Economy of Care_x0094_ seeks, among other objectives, to analyze the effectiveness of the normative that covers the domestic work paid in Colombia. According to the book Stories after the curtains: domestic work in Colombia, the relationships of servitude continue to be part of the socio-cultural patterns that reinforce the logics of discrimination based on class, race and gender, categories associated with jobs of lower social value and greater precariousness.This contributes to the fact that some trades are naturalized as women, such as domestic work (Osorio, 2019).The precariousness of the working conditions of domestic workers is evident.For example, by 2016, 90% of domestic workers did not have a written contract, only 16% were affiliated to an insurance company of occupational risks, only 17.6% were affiliated to the pension system and 70% of the realized work conditions were not completed by their work (Osorio, 2019). and the alternative use of the right to have a critical approach to this problem, which, as described above, has repercussions for women in vulnerable situations. We will combine different methods of research, including normative analysis, descriptive statistics based on administrative records and interviews. This initiative seeks to contribute relevant information from the population of domestic workers in Bogotá, due to the precariousness of data regarding the work situation of these women. Similarly, the proposed methodology containing interviews will allow educational spaces on two tracks, since the information provided by the interviewed women will serve as input for the project and, in turn, the relevant information found in the first stage regarding the recognition of their rights will be shared with them. It is also expected that the results of the project will serve as relevant contributions to think of tools that will address the situation of domestic employment critically
