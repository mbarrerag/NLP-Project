# CLIMATE EXTREME INDICATORS IN THE PROVINCE OF GOOD AIRES: TORRENTALID AD SIMULATIONS AND STREET WOODS FOR THE PERIOD 2015 2039

This work dates from a statistical analysis of the torrentiality and heat waves in the Province of Buenos Aires based on simulations covered between 2015 and 2039. The torrentiality is operationalized from R95pT (amount of days with precipitation above 95 percentile) and heat waves of the Warm Spell Duration Index (WSDI number of days, with at least six, where temperatures exceed 90 percentile) 1. In this sense, the aim of the writing is to group the values obtained using the Bonaerian parties as a spatial unit. In terms of the method, the same intervals were chosen in order to obtain three qualitative categories (low, medium, and high). The results of the torrentiality indicator show a low graduation from northeast to southwest as those of heat waves greater dispersion
