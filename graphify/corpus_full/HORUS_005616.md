# Design of a system that determines regions of cylindrical object gripping in a semi-structured vision-based environment

This master's thesis focuses on the development of a system designed to determine the regions of grip of cylindrical objects, specifically plastic bottles, in a semi-structured environment using vision by computer. Despite the diversity of shapes, sizes and colors that present the bottles, an average size of 500 ml is assumed for the investigation. The project aims to address challenges in robotic handling and automation, especially in industrial applications. It starts with the creation of an image bank that serves as a basis for a system of image processing, which, together with tools of artificial intelligence, allows to train a specific neural network for the task of gripping. This research deepens in the methods and technologies used in the planning of grip and robotic manipulation, highlighting the use of techniques of deep learning. The document is organized into chapters covering the context of the research, the motivation, the related work, the specific objectives and the development of the system for automatic generation of grip regions based on computer vision and automatic learning. In the framework of the development of this research, it is found in the physical system of individual objects arranged in the scene and the functional characteristics of a specific job.
