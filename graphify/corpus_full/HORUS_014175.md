# Engineering applied to the development, training and sustainability of vulnerable communities in Caldas.

The project addresses a current and relevant problem, such as the lack of access to quality education and the difficulties of sustainability in vulnerable communities in Caldas municipalities. This approach responds to an urgent need at both the local and global levels, aligning itself with the UN Sustainable Development Goals, specifically the #4 objective of ensuring inclusive education and promoting sustainability. This is the aim of the project to implement a course of autonomous irrigation systems to promote the development of technified orchards in communities with a vulnerability situation in Caldas municipalities, which allow the development of technical skills that promote the inclusion and empowerment of children, young people and adults in the management of irrigation systems, using the taxonomy of bloom as a pedagogical model and learning based on projects that promote co-creation with the community and the exchange of experiences and knowledge among researchers, farmers and community members, it is expected that the project will promote adaptation to climate change and the construction of sustainable communities that are empowered through education and technology applied to agriculture.
