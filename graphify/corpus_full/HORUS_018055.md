# : INFLUENCE OF THE RACIM BREAKBALL IN THE PRODUCTION AND QUALITY OF BABANUM FRUIT (MUSA AAA) SUBGRUPO CAVENDISH IN MAGDALENA (COLOMBIA)

The practice of bagging the banana clusters increases the speed of growth of the fruits by keeping around it a higher and constant temperature, and improves its overall quality by reducing the attack of some pests and the physical damage during the development of the fruits and the harvest. To determine the color of bag that favors the growth and development of the fruits in the area of production of Magdalena, an experiment was established in commercial farms with factorial design 4 x 4 x 5, where factor 1 farms, factor 2 times of bag (transition from rainy to dry season, dry season, transition from dry to rainy season, rainy season), factor 3 treatments (green plastic bag, white plastic bag, bag of white cloth, bag of plastic blue Santa Lucia and bag plastic blue piggy) and four repetitions. In each bag the firmness of the firmness and solids of the fruit were measured by the total weight of the pulp, the number of exporting hands, ratio and content of chlorophylls in the fruit shell.
