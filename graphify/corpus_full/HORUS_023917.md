# Methodological approach for spatial identification of land sharing and land spreading models in the Colombian Andes region.

Given the spatial conflict that has existed between the establishment of productive areas and the creation of areas for conservation, the debate has been generated land sharing land spreading, which proposes two strategies of landscape management in which these areas are combined or separated. This debate has been widely discussed at a theoretical level, but at a practical level and in terms of territorial planning of the landscape, has not been sufficiently applied. In view of this panorama, this project has as its main objective to construct a spatial methodological approach that allows identifying the suitable areas for the establishment of models of landscape management land sharing and land spreading in the Andean region of Colombia. The Colombian Andes are an area suitable for evaluating the applicability of landscape management strategies framed in the models land sharing and land spreading, since it is considered a biodiversity hotspot due to their high levels of endemicness, besides being constituted as the great food pantry of the country by their agricultural production.
