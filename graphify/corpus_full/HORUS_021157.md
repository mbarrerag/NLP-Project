# Strengthening of the laboratory operation through improved storage of equipment, instruments and reagents at Caribbean Headquarters

The National University of Colombia - Caribbean Headquarters (UNAL-Caribe) with presence in the cities of San Andrés island and Santa Marta, in fulfilment of its mission of professional and research training in the Caribbean region, has five (5) laboratories registered in the National System of Laboratories, which offer both to the university community and to the community in general in the Department Archipelago, the services of support to teaching, research and extension. Given the limitations of the Caribbean Headquarters, in terms of financial and physical spaces, which makes it impossible to acquire goods and services that strengthen the management of the laboratories, it is necessary to take advantage of the resources available to the National Directorate of Research and Laboratories (DNIL) to strengthen this important dependence on the University. The coordination of laboratories of the Headquarters, in the framework of the implementation of the integrated management system of the University, has identified some weaknesses in the functioning of the laboratories, which must be attended by resources such as those that has been provided by the DNIL in the _x0093_Invitation for the strengthening of the management and of the areas of Transversal Support of the National System of Laboratories of the United Nations
