# Protocol for the care of patients over 18 years of age who receive Electroconvulsive Therapy with Anesthesia and Relaxation (TECAR) at the University of La Sabana Clinic.

The electroconvulsive therapy consists in the administration of a small electrical stimulus at the cephalic level to induce a neurological convulsion controlled under general anesthesia and muscle relaxation to treat some mental disorders that have been observed to respond to this therapy. The international clinical practice guides propose similar indications for when a patient can benefit from TECAR, which is indicated in unipolar or bipolar depressive disorders, with psychotic symptoms, acute mania, schizophrenia or catatonia. The reasons for considering the treatment with TECAR for mental disorders are varied, however the great majority of patients who receive it is due to resistance to pharmacological treatment, also due to clinical characteristics such as severity of the disease, the presence of medical comorbidities, the need for a rapid response, high risk of suicide, previous good response in a previous episode or the preference of the patient. There are large variations between continents, countries and regions in the use and practice of TECAR. Despite the existence of clinical practice guides for this purpose, therefore it is necessary to standardize the provision of the service from guidelines of these guides.
