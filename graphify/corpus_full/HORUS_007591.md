# Evaluation of the technological potential of sweet potato flour (Ipomoea Batatas Lam) in the inclusion in baked foodstuffs.

The species Ipomoea sweet potatoes (L.) Lamb., popularly identified as sweet potato, is a tuber with high nutritional content, besides being a versatile crop with low requirements for its production. To generate the use of the food, it is intended to evaluate the technological potential of sweet potato flour in the inclusion in a baked food product, by means of the construction of a protocol for the production of sweet potato flour that allows its obtaining in a standardized way, determined by variables evaluated physicochemical, microbiological and antioxidant capacity. Likewise, to allocate the use of flour in the development of baked food products to diversify the application of sweet potato. By having a clear methodological route for the diversification of a food grown in the regions of the country, it will allow the cultivation of sweet potato to be encouraged and also it can be developed a supply of healthy products generating income to the community and favoring local development
