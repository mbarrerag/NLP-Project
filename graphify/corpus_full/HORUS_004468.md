# CARTESIANISM, THE FILE OF THE Philosophical REFLECTION OF MICHEL PHOUCAULT WITH PSYCOANALYSIS

This is a text that brings to the fore various criticisms formulated by Michel Foucault against psychoanalysis, as an argumentative strategy to support the thesis that this French philosopher considered that the way of analysis that defined his philosophical reflection for the human condition was divergent to which he guided the psychoanalytic doctrine. In the text it is going to be affirmed that Foucault believed throughout his professional career that his thought was oriented by the lines of exploration that fixed Kantian philosophical reasoning, which led him to develop a perspective of analysis very different from the same problematics of discussion that also addressed psychoanalysis, whose epistemic and philosophical foundation Foucault had been in Cartesianism
