# MELLITUS DIABETS IN RURAL AND URBAN CONTEXT CONTEXT: AN ANTHROPOLOGICAL APPROACH IN CUNDINAMARCAMARK-COLOMBIA

The pharmaceutical activity in Colombia has been predominantly industrial (1), and only recently has been oriented towards the care areas. Nevertheless, Colombian society still does not know the role of the pharmacist in the health care process. Among others, this situation could be explained by the health care process in the country and by the training of the pharmaceutical professional, which focuses on the domain of the drug as a product (2), away from the socio-cultural processes of the patient. Therefore, the Health Anthropology could be fundamental in the practice of pharmaceutical care. In a public health care institution in the region of Cundinamarca (Colombia), there is a wide panorama of research, mainly because it presents a high concentration of population in urban areas, in contrast to the depopulation of rural areas (3). This factor implies the existence of profound social inequalities, which, combined with the cultural particularities of each context, results in the differentiation and distancing of population characteristics.
