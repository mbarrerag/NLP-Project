# Indicators of violence in a sample of the Muisca population of the Cercado Grande de los Santuarios, an approach from bioarchaeology.

The project is aimed at carrying out an analysis from bioarchaeology, based on bone evidence from a sample of 40 individuals from early Muisca to late Muisca, from the Great Shrines Encircling in Tunja, in order to establish whether indicators of violence for this society are found in that site. It is noteworthy that most researchers the absence of war practices or signs of conflict, which, however, is not consistent with the type of Muisca political organization, which is mediated by forms of social hierarchy, which is usually linked to different forms of violence
