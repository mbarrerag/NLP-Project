# Method for polarity detection based on a film review database based on a combined selection and classification technique.

The film reviews both positive and negative have a direct impact on the box office collection of cinema productions. To detect whether a criticism is positive or negative, represents a matter of importance for the production companies, since the more positive reviews they receive, the better the chances of the film being a success. The analysis of feelings is presented as a useful tool to understand the reaction of the public and the criticism towards a film, especially when nowadays the social networks are a source of data of the opinions that users make about them. By using the techniques of natural language processing, different researches have explored the best way to detect the polarity of a critique; these have been focused on two aspects mainly, the selection of characteristics and the method of classification, however, none of the studied researches proposes a combined technique of a method of selection and classification, as are the methods of assembly.
