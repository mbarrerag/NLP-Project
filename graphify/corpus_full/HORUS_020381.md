# Bello, social reality of the territory: artistic practices of counter-mapping

This work explores the social reality of the territory of Bello as opposed to the look with which the politicians of turn promote the idea of modernity, progress and wellbeing. It reveals how contemporary artistic practices establish a real connection with a territory by putting under the critical magnify the urban milestones that are projected through render and visual pieces that differ substantially from the factual reality of its inhabitants. The contramapeo appears here as a creative tactic that allows generating a critical dialogue on the representations of the landscape and a territorial conflict in which the privatization of the public becomes evident.
