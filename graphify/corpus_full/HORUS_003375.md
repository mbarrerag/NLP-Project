# AGRICULTURAL ENGINEERING AND THE CHALLENGES FOR SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE

In the medium term, the agricultural sector will face enormous challenges, as according to the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO), total agricultural production will have to increase by 60% to meet the increased demand for food resources and all the nutritional needs of the world population in continuous growth, since it is expected to reach 9 billion people by 2050.Food producers will compete for land, water and energy resources, experiencing the need to restrict the adverse effects of food production on the environment.The growing gap in demand and food supply presents therefore a set of new challenges that cross-cutting by adding pressure on the agricultural supply chain.The role and capacity of intervention of agricultural engineering in the rural sector has been recognized.This curriculum programme plans, takes advantage of, manages and introduces processes of adaptation of land to the productive environment (natural resources), for obtaining raw materials (vegetal-animal) in the perspective of its agro-industrial processing, through the following fields of action of its professionals: engineering of water and soil; engineering of agro-industrial processes and construction; Mechancialization and industrial engineering
