# METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL FOR Similar an INCENDIUM ON A SUPERFFICIAL SOLE IN WORKER

Forest fires represent a growing threat to natural ecosystems worldwide and in Colombia, with anthropic activity being the main cause of ignition in most cases. This study addresses the need to develop a methodology to simulate the burning of a soil under controlled laboratory conditions, reproducing a low-severity fire. Measurable parameters, such as temperature and time, similar to those reported in the literature, were established, considering the limitations of replicating it at laboratory scale. Through the proposed methodology, a laboratory fire was successfully simulated through a controlled burn. This research provides valuable information and a methodological basis for future research related to the representativeness of forest fires
