# Classification of agricultural areas in Colombia through satellite images with deep neural networks

Satellite images are a valuable source of information about the land, which allow us to analyze its surface and structures created by the human being, such as the covering of the soil, vegetation, topography and urban areas.In recent decades, significant advances have been made to improve the quality of these images, including the use of multispectral images of high resolution that provide a more precise description of the objects and their environment.In addition, deep learning models have been developed using these images for classification and segmentation of objects; mainly in urban and climatic areas, with few models focused on agriculture and crops. However, given that Colombia is a country with a vast extension of land dedicated to agriculture, it is important to develop deep learning models to classify and predict the distribution of these areas, which provides valuable information both to the government and to farmers.
