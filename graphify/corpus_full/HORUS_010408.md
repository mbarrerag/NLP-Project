# Salt industry heritage of the Cundiboyacense highlands: A heritage analysis of the salt region to structure an industrial landscape and formulate the bases of its management

Analysis of the territory, society and the saline culture of the Cundiboyacense plateau, to identify the cultural relations and manifestations associated with the saline industry, which allow to structure the bases of an industrial saline landscape. It is divided into four chapters and a section for the conclusions. First, we locate and delimit the object of study and its units of analysis, taking into account its historical, social and administrative development. Then, we present the cultural elements of the industrial landscape, its current state and the factors that affect it. In the third chapter we delvelop the evolution of the concept of cultural heritage and examine the international and national panorama of industrial ensembles and landscapes. Finally, based on the assessment and conservation criteria, we present a basic proposal for management adapted to the singularities of the saline landscape and the cultural manifestation of the basin
