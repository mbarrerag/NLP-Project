# Gröbner-Shirshov bases for Sklyanin algebras

In this thesis we study the basic theory of Gröbner-Shirshov for the algebras of Sklyanin three and four-dimensional. First, we present a brief construction of the free algebras, then describe the basic theory of Gröbner-Shirshov for these algebras. In addition, we present examples on the calculation of these bases, and in particular, we connect these bases with the algebras PBW. Then, we approach the origin and review some of the properties on the algebras of Sklyanin three-dimensionals, especially the property PBW. Thanks to this, we classify in at least eight families the algebras of Sklyanin three-dimensionals that are or are not algebras of PBW, and we calculate their bases for the Gröbner-Shirshov, obtaining in some cases finite bases and in others, apparently infinite. In the same way, we study the algebras of Sklyannthalds four-dimensional, and we analyze their bases, and we calculate their bases for Gj-Syr-Shir-Shir-Shir-Shir-Shir-Shir-Shir bases of these algebras. In addition, we present examples on the computation of the bases, and in particular, we consider some relationships with PBW algebras. Next, we address the origin and review some of the properties of three-dimensional Sklyanin algebras, especially the PBW property. With this, we classify the three-dimensional Sklyanin algebras that are or not PBW algebras into at least eight families, and we compete their Gröbner-Shirshov bases, obtaining in some cases finite bases and in others, alternatively infinite ones.
