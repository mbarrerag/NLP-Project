# Formal P-gevrey series solutions of first-order holomorphic PDEs

We provide a complete and self-contained proof of the Gevrey character, in an analytic function P, of formal power series solutions of some families of first-order holomorphic PDEs. Our approach is based on a majorant series technique by applying Nagumo norms joint with a division algorithm
