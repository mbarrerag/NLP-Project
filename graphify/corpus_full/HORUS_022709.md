# Agricultural sustainability indicators associated with land properties, processes and management

Summary Sustainability assessments are the most appropriate mechanism to determine whether a crop production method, alternative or trend is environmentally, economically and socially viable. These assessments are carried out through indicator-based tools, some associated with soil properties, composition, processes and management. This review provides an overview of the impact of soil management activities on the sustainability of agricultural production systems and provides an overview of the soil indicators that have been used in agricultural sustainability assessments. 28 indicators, grouped into soil-inherent indicators (16) and process indicators related to soil-water (3), soil-atmosphere (5) and soil-plant (4) systems have been used. It is suggested that at least one indicator be measured by each group of indicators associated with soil properties and processes, the inclusion of indicators that take into account future climate change scenarios, as well as the adaptation of current tools to assess the sustainability of various production alternatives
