# ALIJANDRO DE HUMBOLDT: FROM CATALOGUE TO LANDSAGE. NATURALIST EXPEDITION AND LANDSAGE INVIDTION.

The systematic exploration of America marked the thought of Alejando von Humboldt. The vision of animals and plants in new scenarios, the geological observations of American soils and the comparison of these observations with everything he knew about Europe, led him to develop a new discipline: the geography of plants. His work on the geological deposits of Europe and America allowed him to move away from the universal concepts about the conformation of the planet, leading him to seek a more "regional" way. He then studied plants according to the soil where they are found and the climatic conditions in which they grow, in order to understand their way of developing in a particular medium and of associating with other plants to produce landscapes
