# DESIGN AND ASSESSMENT OF A FORM FOR EXTRACTION AND PRESERVATION OF RNA

Many methodologies provide for the purification of the RNA obtained by cellular lysis before being used in gene detection by PCR1. We report the formulation of a lysis/preservation buffer that maintains the integrity of the RNA and is compatible with the PCR, eliminating the need for purification of nucleic acids. The design of the lysis/preservation buffer was based on information about cell lysis methods, RNA stability in aqueous solution and enzyme requirements. The evaluation of the efficacy of the formulation was carried out by qPCR tests and verification of the integrity of the RNA in agarose gel. The RNA integrity tests show a protection to the degradation of the RNA in samples extracted from cultured cells treated with the formulation presented here. The value of Ct of 18s and ß-actin is displaced by five units, in samples treated with the formulation designed by the effect of dilution of the sample.
