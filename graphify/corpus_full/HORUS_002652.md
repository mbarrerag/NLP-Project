# ANTROSOLES: A BIOMIMETIC UNIVERSE UNDER OUR BEING.

We diverge in the exciting link between Biomimesis and Antrosoles. This paper focused on the relationship between the biosphere and the soils, the latter considered as the "brain of the biosphere". Due to the multiple connections in the lithosphere, which interlinks functionally and systemicly the atmosphere, the hydrosphere, the geosphere, the biosphere and even the anthropospheric. This terrestrial substratum, is a leading organ that interlinks and synchronizes life on Earth. It is illustrated, a portion of the Colombian territory, to mention, the value of the connectivity that exerts the soil as a sustenance of the biodiversity and other mosaics of ecosystems that the human being has formed, being as well as the Antrosoles influence in the ecosystems, generating a tissue of interdependence. The Biomimesis emerged as a guide, inviting us to learn from Nature to design sustainable solutions.
