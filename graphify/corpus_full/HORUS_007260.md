# The battles of freestyle in Bogotá as a cultural, social and youth phenomenon: A reflective characterization of its implications in rap language

“The battles of freestyle in Bogotá as cultural, social and youth phenomenon: A reflexive characterization of their implications in rap language” is a research that focuses on the analysis of the Battles of Freestyle Rap in the city, addressing its cultural, social and youth implications. The main objective is to develop a reflective characterization of the phenomenon, based on the experiences and experiences of the community, which is evidenced in three musical products of rap. These products will return to the community the results obtained, reflecting the key dimensions of the phenomenon. This thesis, seeks to explore how the battles of rap contribute to the construction of social tissue, youth development and identity in the city, contributing theoretically to the field of Hip Hop and cultural management. Likewise, it examines how these battles create cultural cohesion, although they face tensions between the authenticity of artistic expression and marketing. From the methodological point of view and the result of the general research, it adopted a qualitative approach with the objective of integrating different strategies that address 3 key moments of research: the exploration of the rap battles in Bogotá as object of study, the implementation of a participatory space with members of its experiential community and the objective method.
