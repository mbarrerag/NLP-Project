# ENTRY IN ROMANTIC RELATIONS: A PERSPECTIVE OF MENTAL HEALTH FROM THEORY OF SOCIAL REPRESENTATIONS

Through the study of the social representations of attachment, the sociocognitive meaning of this concept can be analyzed in depth. This is why the general objective of this research was to identify the social representations of attachment in young people who are in a romantic relationship to analyze and interpret their beliefs about this psychological behavior so essential for affective links. The specific objectives include the need to conceptualize the attachment linked to romantic relationships from the point of view of young adults, to explore this vision from the theory of social representations and to analyze the content and organization of social representations. This study, as it is of an interpretative qualitative nature, is to say finally by epistemological issues, did not include the formulation of hypotheses. In this study there is a clear objective of approach to the construction of purely subjective knowledge expressed by both oral and written language which makes it possible to interpret the thought of the subjects in question. This study is a qualitative study of phenomenological-hermeneutic court and focused on trying to understand the phenomenon investigated from the subjective vision of the participants (Samperior, Fernández, and Baptist, 2014).
