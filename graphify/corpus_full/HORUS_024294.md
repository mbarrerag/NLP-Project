# ERGONOMY, WORK AND HEALTH SYSTEM: REFLECTIONS FROM COLOMBIA

This article introduces the line of research on ergonomics, work and health proposed in the project to create the doctorate in ergonomics that the Universidad del Valle and the Universidad Nacional de Colombia are carrying out as part of the construction of knowledge on the path travelled by the community that has worked on ergonomics in Colombia, especially since the implementation of Law 100 of 1993, which creates the comprehensive social security system in this country. This system establishes three major subsystems: pensions, health and occupational risks, the latter being the main scenario of application of ergonomics in Colombia. The article reflects on the research challenges that are visible in, for and from the ergonomics of the health system, especially in the current pandemic situation. The ergonomics of the health system is understood from the commitment to the institutions and policies that shape this system. The ergonomics for the health system seeks to address the real institutions and procedures in the functioning of the health system, understood as frameworks that regulate human health.
