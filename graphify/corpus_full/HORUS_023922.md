# TERM INCAPACITIES AS A INFECIFICIENCY AND DISPERDITATION FACTOR IN THE COLOMBIAN HEALTH SYSTEM (PHASE 1)

With the objective of analyzing the behavior of temporary disability due to general illness in Colombia in the period 2016-2018, an observational study of retrospective cross-sectional was carried out.The records of temporary disabilities reported by the Health Promotor Entities (EPS) to the Ministry of Health and Social Protection for the periods 2016 to 2018 were analyzed.The results of the records of formal workers from 18 to 70 years of age affiliated to the Health System and with at least one episode of temporary disability in the period of study.A descriptive analysis was carried out, one and two-way, which allowed the estimation of the percentages for the variables of the study: sociodemographic (sex, age, age groups of age), related to the work (type of contributor, base income of contribution) and related to the disability (diagnosis, duration).The majority of the disabilities corresponded to the female sex and the average of the elderly (workers, 6%)
