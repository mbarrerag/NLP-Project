# FUSARIUM OXYSPORUM F. SP. DIANTHI POSSIBLE VARIULENCE FACTORS RESPONSIBLE FOR VASCULAR KEY MARCHING

The carnation is one of the most important plant species for the floricultor sector, since it constitutes the second most exported flower species after the rose. However, the production of carnation is affected to a large extent by the vascular wilting, caused by the fungus Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. dianthi (Fod). Within the plant interaction pathogen different biochemical responses related to the expression of genes involved in the defense response in the plant or linked to the production of virulence factors by the pathogen were determined. In this study, some of the virulence factors that Fod secrete in the apoplast of the carnation during its infection, in order to establish an interaction compatible with its host.
