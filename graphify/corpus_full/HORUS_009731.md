# PROPOSAL FOR A INDEX TO EVALUATE GOVERNANCE OF WATER IN FIVE ANDIN COLOMBIA HYDROGRAPHICS.

Water is a vital resource for human life, ecosystems, biodiversity, health, economic development and social well-being. Water governance recognizes water priority as an essential element for life in coordination and cooperation processes of various social, sectoral and institutional actors involved in integrated management; and assumes the watershed as a basic unit for its analysis [1]. Measuring water governance is a challenge for socio-economic variables around this concept. There are some indices and tools used to measure water governance: Water Governance Index developed by the World Resources Institute (WRI), which evaluates water governance at the country level; water management index (water management index), transparency index in water management (water transparency index) and water resilience index (water resilience index) in water resilientness in water (water resilience index), among others [2]. None of these indices are designed to measure water governance at the water basin level.
