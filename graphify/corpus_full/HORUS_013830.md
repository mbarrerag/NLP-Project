# Social and eco-hydrological sustainability of the moors through the empowerment and participatory monitoring of rural communities

The moors are Andean ecosystems located above 3000 msnm and up to the perpetual snows or up to the top of the mountains. Its importance has been widely recognized, especially because rural populations and large cities depend on the water resource emanating from these ecosystems. Despite its importance and its wide water supply, historically they have been occupied and used by indigenous communities and currently migrant peasants from the lowlands. Changes in the use of land in the moors have transformed the landscape, impacted the soils, so that they have affected the ecosystem services, including the water supply, so that nowadays there is a growing concern for the effects that have been caused by the transformation of these biomes.
