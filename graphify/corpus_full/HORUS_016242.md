# Representation and analysis of the processes of disintegration of subatomic particles by applying the formalism of Network Analysis.

Understanding the final state of collisions of high-energy particles is an extremely difficult theoretical and experimental problem, for this reason, the high energy physics community (HEP) has been resorting to computer tools, including databases describing the processes of subatomic particle disintegration. Thanks to this information, particle relationships can be explored under network analysis. By graphical representation as well as the method of structural analysis to explain the relationships between communities and individuals with the relationships between subatomic particles discovered in the context of decay processes
