Knowledge of the forces of interaction between solutes and solvents is of great interest in fields such as Pharmacy or Physical Chemistry, among others. The Hildebrand-Scatchard Regular Solution Theory was applied to estimate the solubility of nonpolar drugs in nonpolar solvents [1], however, it is the polar system of irregular solutions that are commonly found in Pharmacy.
