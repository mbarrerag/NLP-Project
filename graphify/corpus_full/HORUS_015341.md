# Proposal for supply chain management through the Demand Driven Adaptive Enterprise. Case balanced food producer.

This document presents a proposal methodology for the management of the supply chain for a balanced food producing company. The proposal is supported by the Demand Driven Adaptative Enterprise -DDAE- and SCOR models, contributing to the reduction of the invested working capital and in the maximization of the level of service of the different SKUs. This research is descriptive and deductive in nature, its composition is given by general considerations in which the problem is presented to solve, subsequently the constructs of the models of the researched supply and supply chain of the company to which the study was carried out are developed. After analyzing the different constructs, a proposal of model was developed by merging the DDAE and SCOR models and applying it to the supply chain of the company, finally the methodology used and with historical data of the company is validated the proposed model. As a final result, a methodological proposal is elaborated that aims to facilitate the management of the supply chain for a food producing company balancing achieving with this reduction of the capital of inverted work while maintaining or improving the levels of service of the portfolio.
