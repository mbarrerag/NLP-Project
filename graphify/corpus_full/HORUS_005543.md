# ARTICLES

This physical joint examination guide provides a standardized semiological exposure to evaluate patients, which allows describing a more accurate and reliable method for examining the joints of patients with rheumatoid arthritis. It addresses in a simple and graphic manner topics such as the background and reasons why joint examination is an important part of the evolution of patient follow-up. It also outlines the reasons that led to the physical examination techniques of the joints presented, as well as the general principles for proper and complete examination. It also describes the review of each joint affected by rheumatoid arthritis. It also documents these techniques along with an exhibition of images that serve as a graphic explanation. This guide meets the objectives of being short, clear, concise and didactic, but complete in the standardized techniques that must be applied to examine the patient. It is aimed at all health personnel who examine rheumatoid arthritis patients. It is therefore useful for a wide and varied range of health providers
