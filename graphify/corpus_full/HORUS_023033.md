# Evaluation of the growth, development and production of the cultivation of maracuyá (Passiflora edulis f. flavicarpa Degener), grafted into five species of Passiflora L.

Maracuyá (Passiflora edulis f. flavicarpa Degener) is one of the main fruit species of the Neotropical region due to its high nutritional value as a source of minerals and vitamins. Colombia with about 10,000 hectares and a production of 175,000 tons without a diameter after Brazil. Despite this potential, the crop is affected by multiple plant protection problems, such as the dryness caused by Fusarium solani, which imposes chemical treatments for its control. The objectives of this research were to estimate the effect of the passivercio spp. forks resistant to the dryness in the vegetative growth of the cup and to evaluate agronomically the production and quality of the fruit in the maracuyá grafted by analysis of variance (ANOVA). The study was carried out in the experimental station of Agrosavia in Palmira, Valle del Cauca (Colombia) at 1032 m. n. m. under a system of blocks evaluated showed that the five portinjertos presented 100% compatibility, and the greatest effect on the diameter, height, number of leaves and between them, and between them were 90 m. n. The most important species of the species were P. maliformis (87.5%), P. quadrangularis (87.5%) and P. alata (81.25 per cent) at 15 months after planting. Cholupa has a great potential as a porttainjerto for the maracuyá because of its tolerance to the dry land and its agronomic behavior. These results are the basis for a programme to improve the maracuyá in Colombia that involves the technique of grafting so that producers can mitigate the effect of soil diseases.
