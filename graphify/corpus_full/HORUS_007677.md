# Hydraulic modelling of landfills of excess thermoelectric power plants

The proposal addresses the need to optimize efficiency and minimize the environmental impact of thermoelectric power plants in Colombia, focusing on the precise measurement of waste water discharges. It identifies that water quality in the Magdalena basin is compromised by the discharges of these plants, affecting dissolved oxygen levels and aquatic ecosystems. The central hypothesis is that flow measurement is incorrectly performed without considering the flow type. The objective is to hydraulically evaluate the discharge measurement system to optimize its accuracy, efficiency and sustainability, ensuring compliance with environmental regulations. The methodology includes dimensional analysis, numerical models and physical tests. Technical reports, a representative physical model, experimental data, a validated numerical model and scientific publications are expected. Efficient and accurate water resources management in the plants is crucial to improve sustainability and minimize environmental impact
