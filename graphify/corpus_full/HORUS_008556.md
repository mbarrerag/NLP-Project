# ANDES TROPICALES

The restoration of ecosystems in the Andean montane forests is a necessity in the region due to the ecosystem services that they provide and the biotic importance in the region. The project ¿Más Bosques para Medellín¿ was formulated as a proposal to restore the forests located in the rural areas of Medellín, which supply a large amount of the hydric resources of the city. In order to evaluate the quality of the established silvicultural arrangements and their performance in carbon sequestration 10 years after their establishment, we fitted allometric equations using data obtained by destructive methods for 144 individuals, that is, a general equation for the project and six specific equations for each one of the six most frequent species. The carbon sequestration was estimated with the information of the last inventory (May 2023) for the 147.3 hectares corresponding to the study area in 9055.37 tons (t) of CO2 equivalent. The aerial biomass per hectare was estimated at 44.06 +/- 29.96 t · ha-1, which corresponds to 80.84 +/- 54.98 t · ha-1. A great variability in carbon accumulation in the form of aerial biomass was found between the study areas, which can be related to the environmental characteristics of each site. We conclude that the forests within some properties are in successional stages close to secondary forests typical of this area (low montane wet forest), while others require management to optimize their restoration process. In addition, it is projected that these plantations will be able to generate ecosystems similar to the primary forests of the region, storing for 80% more carbon than they currently store
