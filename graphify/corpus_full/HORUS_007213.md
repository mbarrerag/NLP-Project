# Between density and shape: urban dynamics on the eastern edge of Tunja [2005-2018]

The research work entitled “Between density and shape: urban dynamics on the eastern edge of Tunja [2005-2018]” aims to characterize urban growth and transformation resulting from residential densification on the eastern edge of the city of Tunja. Through a mixed approach combining quantitative and qualitative analysis, census data from 2005 and 2018 were analyzed, using tools such as QGIS and 3D models to visualize and understand spatial transformations in this area. The study covers a detailed description of densification patterns over time, highlighting how certain sectors close to the main urban roads have experienced a more accelerated growth, with an increase in the height of the buildings and the compaction of the soil. In addition, the predominant construction typologies and their interaction with the residential densification are explored, revealing a diverse urban consolidation that includes from self-construction to residential blocks. The results indicate that densification in Tunja has been driven not only by the restructuring policies, but also by the dynamics of the market and the processes of self-construction.
