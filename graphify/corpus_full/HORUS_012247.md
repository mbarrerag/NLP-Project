# Atmospheric dispersion of particulate matter in mountain and tropical cities.

The mountain cities are urban areas located in complex terrain, where the processes of dispersion and transport of pollutants are influenced by topographic and meteorological factors.The Aburrá Valley is an urban area located in the tropics in a narrow and deep valley, where the air quality reaches critical values in the transition periods of dry and humid season, being the particulate material less than 2.5 microns (PM2.5) one of the contaminants criterion for air quality analysis due to the health risks generated by the exposure to it. The objective of this work was to propose a numerical procedure capable of simulating the presence and transport of particulate material, which allows identifying the processes and patterns of atmospheric dispersion in tropical and mountain cities.
