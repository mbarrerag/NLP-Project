# Porno-Colombia: imagining the nation from the magazine Corps

This research aims to analyze the type of nation imagined in the pornographic journal Bodies: erotic magazine monthly for men and women, exclusively adults, throughout the issues published between 1980 and 1990. Specifically, I examine the politics and the syntax editorial of the journal to elucidate the aspirations of the project; concentrating on the political arena that is configured from the statements raised about Colombian sex. By this way, I dedicate myself to mapping the boundaries of the erotic regime produced by Bodies, closely following the type of citizenships created in the articulation between pleasure and capital within the pharmacopopornographic spectrum of capitalism. To round off the argument, I conclude with the identification of some of the nationalist fantasies that inhabit the journal and that give account of the elusive nature of Colombia produced in the publication: a modern nation, illustrated, secular and simultaneously, Catholic, bruxic and incest. .
