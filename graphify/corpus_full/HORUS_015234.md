# Inside and outside: the sociability as a transformer of the urban landscape. Bogotá 1880 - 1919

This research paper explores the conflicts that arose through the transformation of public space in Bogotá at the end of the 19th century and beginning of the 20th century. It is based on the hypothesis in how it is the social practice of the inhabitants that is really responsible for articulating and creating the public space, which was covered and examined the space of different scenes of the city where not only the physical urban fabric was analyzed, but also the interests dictated from above and below, as well as the flexibility of use in the space that is adapted or rejected by users' decisions. In this way this work demonstrates how some public spaces were constituted by tensions between the traditional and the new. But, it also explains how those decisions on the public space were made many times from above, relegated a part of the population to develop their social practice with greater freedom in the eccentric areas of the city. . to the outskirts
