# Model recommendation for investment in Colombian shares belonging to the colcap index based on technical analysis and local market sentiment.

In 2018, the Second Capital Market Mission was created in Colombia, with the aim of studying the current state of local financial markets and suggesting solutions to the problems encountered. Among the findings was found that the stock market has had a notable decrease in recent years, so that actions should be taken to encourage participation in this market. As a contribution to the solution, this work develops a model of recommendation of actions belonging to the COLCAP index, whose objective is to be included in a tool that, besides showing the results of the model, allows the investor to see risk figures and inputs used by the model to reach the result: indicators based on historical prices of the shares, news and financial indicators of each issuer. To this, two models of recurrent neural networks that predict the prices of the shares are proposed, and then these predictions are used to classify the actions from better to worse according to the calculated profitability with those forecasts.
