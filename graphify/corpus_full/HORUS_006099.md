# Hydromechanical interaction in a rocky massif around a tunnel

The link between the mechanical and flow properties of a rocky mass, in relation to the construction of a circular tunnel, is evaluated by different numerical models hypothetical. To this end, the program of finite elements Plaxis 2D® was used mainly. Based on these simulations a comparative analysis was carried out that gives the influence of the following aspects on the stability of the tunnel and on the changes of flow due to its construction: structural model, lateral pressure coefficient, Biot coefficient, separation and opening of the discontinuities, condition of saturation, coverage and diameter of the tunnel. The parameters of evaluation were: deformation maximum around the tunnel, subsidence, deviant effort, flow of infiltration and safety factor. It was found that the lateral pressure parameter significantly modifies the distribution and concentration of forces around the tunnel; the coefficient of Biot directly affects the value of the effective forces, so it should not be underestimated and the deformations around the tunnel, the subsidence and the deflector efforts are less in relation to the anisotropic models. and flow changes due to its construction: structural model, lateral pressure deficit, Biot coefficient, discontinuity separation and opening, saturation condition, overburden and tunnel diameter.
