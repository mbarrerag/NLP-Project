# NEW APPROACH TO THE OPTI-TIME LOCALIZATION PROBLEM FOR JUSTICE EQUIPMENT

This article shows the development of an optimal location model, designed to respond to the deficit in the supply of justice equipment in the city of Bogotá D. C. Colombia. This optimal location model takes into account spatial efficiency and territorial justice criteria, as well as normative, geographical and social aspects that limit the selection of optimal sites, and uses multicriteria analysis techniques based on diffuse logic. This model aims to be a contribution to the planning and planning processes of urban territory and constitutes a tool of spatial analysis focused on the attention of security and coexistence needs in the city
