# Life cycle, minimum temperature threshold and thermal constant of Gonipterus platensis in Medellín, Colombia.

To study the life cycle of Gonipterus platensis, at three temperatures (21, 23 and 24.5oC), in order to determine the minimum temperature threshold and the thermal constant for the biological states of the pest. Scope: The life cycle from egg to the adult emergency was determined, the duration of each biological state (egg, larvae, incentivising larval - L1, L2, L3, L4- and pupa) at 21, 23 and 24.5oC. This information allowed to find the minimum temperature threshold also known as base temperature (Tb in oC), indicating the minimum temperature that allows the development of the insect, and the thermal constant (K in degrees day + GD), which refers to the quantity of heat units required to reach a biological state. The life cycles obtained with a puperal foot (gggggggg) and in a linear rate (gpapapate) of Medellín, Colombia, between February 2019 and July 2020. Life cycles at 21 and 23oC were made incubarator; and at 24,5oC (gg) were performed in the ambient state. country
