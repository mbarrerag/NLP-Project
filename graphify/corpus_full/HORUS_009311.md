# Report of characteristics of voice and fundamental frequency of trans women in a third-level hospital in Bogotá, D.C.

The fundamental frequency is defined as the number of glóticos vibrating cycles per second, consistent with the lowest frequency produced by larynx during phonation. This parameter is related to characteristics of masculinity and femininity of the voice, according to the acute or severe values of its production. The fundamental frequency is the parameter susceptible to be intervened by voice therapy and speech surgery, which aims to bring the fundamental frequency (F0) to an adequate value for the patient's gender. The evaluation of the degree of female voice (very male, male, neutral, female, very female) perceived by the participants (trans women) and the values of fundamental frequency obtained by stroboscopy of 7 participants. The fundamental frequency values had an average of 195.8 Hz, (DE +/- 22.8), with minimum values of 160 Hz and maximum values of 240 Hz. Regarding the qualitative assessment of the degree of female voice, 57.1% of the participants considered their male-grade voice, while the 28.5 % (2 participants) considered their male voice and only one participant constituted a neutral voice
