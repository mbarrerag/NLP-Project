# New technologies in the management of Human Work.

From the changes in the production and delivery of services, provided especially by the technologies that emerge permanently, the direction and application of the workforce and the dynamics in the relationship between worker and employer have important impacts, with the consequent need for transformation and adaptation to the new means and demands in the work scenario, and moreover, in view of the respective social and economic incidences. Thus, the incursion of the Information and Communication Technologies (ICT), preceded by the processes of automation, the incorporation of electricity and the specialized machine in the Fordista model in the Work, and in the incorporation of methods and tools computerized from the decade of the seventies and eighties, and the most recent advances like the Internet of things, the computing in the cloud, the artificial intelligence, the big data, among other technologies, have contributed to the emergence of tensions and opportunities for improvement in the labor market, since there is an improvement of the undisputable time in the manufacturing processes, other effects are identified regarding the replacement of personnel, or the need to reconvert the job, without the clear opening of new opportunities, therefore, in the social areas. The use of new technologies is compatible with fundamental notions about the right to work in dignity? How to respond responsibly to the age of information and technology in the workplace? How to mitigate the impact of new technologies on the control society, specifically on the work environment? How automation tools, algorithms and artificial intelligence affect workers' labour and fundamental rights?
