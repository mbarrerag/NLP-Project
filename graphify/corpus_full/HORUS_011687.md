# EFFECT OF TEMPERATURE IN THE GERMINATION OF SEEDS

Weeds represent an important limitation to agricultural systems due to their great ability to interfere in the development of the crop and their adaptability to climatic conditions variants, which generates interest in studying the behavior of these populations under conditions of climatic variability, so it was proposed the execution of an investigation which will evaluate the effect of different temperatures on germination-emergency in 3 species of weeds; Cirsium vulgare, Senecio vulgaris and Epilobium montanom. for the achievement of this objective an experimental design was proposed with 2 treatments, 3 species and 5 repetitions per treatment, for a total of 30 experimental units (EU), each EU was conformed by a vial of 20 mL, in which 6 mL of water and 10 seeds of the species were arranged to evaluate; to allow the flotation of the seeds a previously weaving fiber is placed. Subsequently the EU was placed in chamber of incubation at 2 different temperatures (a 18 and 25 °C).
