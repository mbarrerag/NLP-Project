# Effect of the Bicirum programme as an active transport strategy and lifestyle modification

At present, the promotion of lifestyles that promote integral health has emerged as a prevailing mission. Among these aspects, the implementation of strategies inherent to active transport, established as fundamental foundations in the prevention against the growing health dilemmas related to inactivity and sedentaryism, problems that affects at a global level with unusual magnitude and that can cause the appearance of chronic non-communicable diseases (NCNT) are a problem of public health at a global level, according to the WHO the increase of these increases the mortality in all nations. 53% of the mortality and disability is caused by ECNT in developing countries but with an allocation for its treatment with only 2% in the investment in health. In this context, the use of active means of transport, like the bicycle, acquires a significant relevance. Given its accessibility to a large part of the population, the option to adopt it as a means of mobility instead of vehicles and motorcycles is highlighted as a sustainable environmental strategy, able to address the ECNT derived from the physical activity and the campus mentioned if the initiative encourages healthy habits, but also the possibility of the university to facilitate it. The development of this research proposal will contribute both at the local and national levels to understanding the environmental changes necessary to combat this problem. It will also enable the administrative and governmental authorities to make more precise and effective decisions to reduce the levels of sedentaryism and ECNT in the population
