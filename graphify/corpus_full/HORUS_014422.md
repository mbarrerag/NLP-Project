# Urban geotechnical map of Duitama from an existing database using Q-GIS.

The main objective of this research is to create a compilation of Geotechnical Maps for the urban area of the city of Duitama (Colombia), based on a database supported by existing soil studies that were collected during the research. Interpolations are used for the process by the method of Kriging and Thiessen polygons using Q-GIS software; a statistical analysis was carried out in advance to the input data, in order to obtain the best results that fit the reality of the field. The delivery methods obtained have as their scope data up to a depth of 9 m, where maps are presented each metre, with the expected values of N for the SPT test, soil classification, stratigraphy and a general map of the water levels; in turn, validations are carried out by means of complementary thematic maps and quantification of errors in measurements for their correct analysis and implementation in order to become a support tool for the development of the municipality
