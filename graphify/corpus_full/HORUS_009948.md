# Physicochemical, textureal and sensory properties in cookies made with wheat, oats and quinoa

Summary The objective of this study was to evaluate the addition of quinoa flour (HQ) in wheat flour cookies (HT) with oatmeal, its effect on the physicochemical, textureal and sensory properties. The properties as water absorption (CAA) and oil (CAAc) of flour mixtures for the preparation of cookies were also analyzed. Three treatments with different levels of Avena and HQ leaflets, T0: 15% and 0%, T1: 10% and 5% and T2: 5% and 10% were evaluated. It was found that the HQ and Avena also affected the flours AAC, as it decreased T1 (186.62% ± 3.28) and T2 (184.71% ± 3.62) compared to the control sample (194.23% ±3.57), while the ACA was affected, T2 presented the maximum value (169.05% ±7.84) with respect to the control sample (147.79 % ± 4.68), and the moisture of the mixtures presented significant differences (5.36% ± 0.38.42 % ± 0.04 and 6.13% ± 0.07% ± 0.07)
