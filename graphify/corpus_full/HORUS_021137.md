# Impact Assessment of the Covid 19 Pandemia on the Performance of Secondary and University Students in the Knowledge Tests

After the emergence of the epidemic caused by the SARSCov-2 virus, later called the disease COVID-19, in late 2019 in Wuhan, China, a great effort was made to contain the contagion to the region where it first appeared. That effort was unsuccessful and the infections spread around the world. The World Health Organization (WHO) declared the pandemic on March 11, 2020. From that moment, in Colombia, measures were taken to reduce the effects of the epidemic, especially in public health. Several universities in Colombia suspended the face-to-face classes from March 15, 2020 and therefore, from that moment all the classes were provided remotely. All the tasks that required the presence of people in one form or another, including all the courses and laboratory practices, were put back in the presence of the presence, at the national level a general consolidation, which allows for the increased public capacity, especially for the students of lower resources. For more than a year, the classroom was suspended at all levels. Gradually they were reinstated the activities of the public, starting from schools and universities. The results and percentages of each student, in the overall score and in each of the tests. It is sought to establish, from the performance of the tests Know (in its modalities 11, TyT and Pro), how the state's response to the pandemic has affected students from different strata, sexes, localities, and types of institution, using only the measurements of their performance in the tests
