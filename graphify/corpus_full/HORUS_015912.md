# Why do some countries change their rules more than others? A multi-stakeholder analysis of the causes of political-electoral reforms in Latin America.

This paper explores longitudinally various explanations regarding the level of reformist activism and seeks to show through a multivariate analysis to what extent a series of hypotheses (institutional, attitudinal, political-competitive and economic) allow to explain the differences of this reformist rhythm among the countries of the region.
