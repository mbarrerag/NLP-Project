# CORROSIVE RESISTANCE OF NANOSTRUCTURED CRTIALNNI COATINGS DEPOSITED BY HIPIMS

To Analyze influence of Ni amount on crystalline structure, morphology and corrosion resistance of nanostructured CrTiAlN-Ni coating deposited by HiPIMS on stainless-steel 316L, hard metal K20 (Tungsten carbide) and silicon wafer (100), through characterization techniques X-Ray diffraction (XRD), Scanning electronic microscopy (SEM), Energy-dispersive X-Ray spectroscopy (EDS) and electrochemical test EIS and PP (Tafel)
