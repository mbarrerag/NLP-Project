# Approach to perceived changes in Self and their impact on the lives of people who were part of a psychodynamic-relacional approach psychotherapy process

The objective of the research consisted in exploring the changes perceived in the same-life, their impacts on the daily life and the contributions of psychotherapy, from the perspective of 5 people participating in a psychodynamic-relational therapy process with closure by fulfillment of objectives between 3 and 6 months before the investigation. The participants were selected to convenience. A qualitative research design was used with descriptive scope. A semistructured interview was carried out. The content analysis technique was implemented. In the face of perceived changes in the self, it was identified: redefinition of itself in positive terms, emergence of feelings of personal worth and decrease of feelings of little value, development of the skills to establish limits and internal-external dialogue, better understanding about themselves, identification of behavioral patterns, ability to evaluate experiences and to define themselves (self-care) as priorities; these changes were based on the strengthening of self-esteem/self-conceptive. As for the impacts in relational aspect, it identified: recognition and reaffirmation of other, reactions to change, innovative relational experiences and distance from personal relationships; these changes were based on the impact of self-esteem/self-conceptuality..
