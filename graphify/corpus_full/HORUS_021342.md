# 100 years of Mariquita Air Cable - Manizales: Material Heritage

In 1922, a system of transport of goods by Air Cable was inaugurated between the towns of Mariquita and Manizales, which contributed to the development of the region for more than forty years. Today, when this transport system is imposed again by its environmental conditions, the look at the old air cable to understand its mechanism, the form of its construction, the vicissitudes of its operation, which allows understanding other customs of engineering, other leaderships and other forms of life that contributed with its dailyity to the modernization of Manizales is reviewed. Despite the regional importance of the Mariquita-Manizales air cable, and considering that to date there is no precise information about this old system of transport of goods, it is proposed: the elaboration of a precise map that accounts for the layout of such infrastructure; a meticulous architectural survey of all the stations; to identify with precision the location of the stations and structures surrounding it; to help to counteract the risks of the stations that are in imminent danger of being destroyed. The present project, which is strategic for the region in terms of tourism and economic development, aims to solve the previous questions of the historical interest
