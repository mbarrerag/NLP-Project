# CHAPTER 10: CHEMICAL RISK 1

This chapter mentions the generalities of chemical risk in the occupational context, the epidemiological aspects related to the impact of chemicals on health, describes some of the main economic activities with exposure to chemical risk, presents general aspects related to the toxicity of chemicals, presents generalities of chemical risk management at the labour and environmental levels, and emphasizes strategies for epidemiological surveillance, and use of biomarkers for biological monitoring
