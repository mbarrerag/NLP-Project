# The effects of the criminalisation process, the persecution of persons prosecuted in the framework of the National Stop of 2021 expressed through the cartographies textiles and corporeals

This research seeks, from a framework of interpretation of destructive plasticity, to create tools for expression and memory of the experiences of criminalization, persecution, judicialization of persons deprived of their liberty in the context of the National Paro of 2021. Thus, understanding from Malabou the existence of accidents and traumatic events that go through the body and alter, in this way, their identity, we seek to glimpse, together with the young women and the judicialized, the effects that these processes, which are part of the criminalization, had on their body and their identity, through encounters of creation of body textile cartography
