# THE PHONE OF THE TALK: GUIDE TO PRACTICAL EXERCISES

The book Fundamentals of Speech: guide of practical exercises was elaborated with the purpose of offering students and professionals of phonoaudiology a series of activities related to the basic concepts of the process of human speech. The text does not aim, in any way, to deepen the concepts presented, but rather it is proposed to reinforce with different exercises, the theoretical notions that are already presented in a wide and very well elaborated way in other specialized books. Therefore, at the end of each section, the reader has a list of documents that can help him to better understand each of the phenomena exposed. Being a practical tool, the apprentices can develop each of the exercises proposed autonomously as part of the preparation of the theoretical sessions or as a subsequent exercise of reinforcement; however, the accompaniment of the teacher for the resolution of the doubts that are generated. Intentionally, each section has been written with a simple lexicon, with- servanando the technical signs that are expected to be used as part of his professional jargon.
