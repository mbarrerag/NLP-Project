# Fluorescence optical spectroscopy using LED light in ex-living tissues of the cervix

Abstract To carry out studies of biological tissues such as the cervical with alternative techniques allows to go to a support to the medical diagnosis of cervical cancer (CCU), the fourth most common worldwide and the first cause of death in women of 30-59 years of age in Colombia. In the cervical tissue are present different fluorophores such as: NADH, flavina, elastin and collagen; molecules that generate fluorescence thanks to their ability to absorb electromagnetic energy, which allows them to emit photons of less energy to a given wavelength. In the initial stages of the dysplasia, the cells change their metabolic speeds, physical and structural characteristics; this alters the spectral response of the molecules present in the cervical tissue.
