# Influence of surface roughness and macrogeometry on the quality of adjustment between fixation screw and implant in implant-supported prosthesis

Large international companies such as MIS are engaged in the design and manufacture of prosthetic components with standardized processes and quality controls. In Colombia there are turning centers that manufacture fixing screws that ensure adequate adjustment to the original implants, however, there are no evaluations or standards to be certified as countertypes. The aim of this study is to evaluate the quality of adjustment of fixing screws obtained by CAD/CAM of two national turning centers on original dental implants and analogue. For this purpose, the characterization of 5 macrogeometric parameters and 12 roughness parameters is done with Alicona InfiniteFocus G5 for 9 fixing screws, 2 implants and one analogue, analysis of dimensional tolerances under ISO 965-1. Statistical analysis under T test for two samples assuming unequal variables (a.0.05); and finally, an assembly of screw-implant to be used more to perceive and generate the proposal of manufacturing characteristics for CNC turning centres. Tolerance analysis and T test indicate that CT2 screws have less compatibility with the MIS screws, excluding the angle and length of Thread. The objective of this study is to evaluate the adjustment quality of fixation credits obtained by CAD/CAM from two national tourism centres on original dental implants and analyses.
