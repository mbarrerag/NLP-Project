# -None teaches baby baby baby care with oxygen in the home

--In 2017 NUNA was created an animated character representing a nurse from the National University of Colombia. (Designed by Diego Alejandro Pedroza Beltrán and Nubia Castiblanco López) with NUNA is sought to recreate various animated comics that teach mothers and families of premature newborns and low weight at birth about their care. It is considered important comics given in a clear and reliable language, this implies a content based not only on real facts but also on images, sound that generate satisfaction so that finally the caregiver can believe and make the decision.
