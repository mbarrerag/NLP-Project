# Quasi-recurrent behavior in a model applied to structural engineering by means of geometric oscillator variation and excitation frequency

The text addresses the practical theoretical analysis of an inverted pendulum type oscillator, which will have its application in an example of a elevated tank with a pump that produces sinusoidal movements at the top of the latter. To do this, the behavior for 1 and 2 degrees of freedom was studied; where 1 degree of freedom is x-shift and 2 degrees of freedom are displacements in x and in y. An analysis was used in the dynamics of oscillatory systems. To this purpose, the Poincaré methodology was used, which provides metaphorically an x-ray of the oscillatory system. By means of the analysis of Poincaré maps, Poincaré rotation numbers and phase states of the system, the frequency of these were analyzed and a direct relationship was found between the geometry of oscillators and the change of behaviour in the periodicity of the system. Thus, by means of the change in the oscillator geometry, the Poincarré rotation numbers and the phases of phases of phases of the system was passed from a periodic system of period 1, to a system with period 2,3... to a system with quasi-perdic behaviors .
