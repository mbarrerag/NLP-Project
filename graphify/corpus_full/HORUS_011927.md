# NEUROLOGY TRAUMA BY DESECILERATION AND ACCELERATION FORCES

The acceleration movement in a force exerted on human body has the ability to generate multiple internal traumas that sometimes, although the absence of external trauma is forgotten and not valued in the clinic, most of these traumas are associated with sacred tearing of different structures primarily vascular and some particularities about solid organs. In this chapter, real specimens of brain lesions are photographically documented more visible and common in the context of acceleration and deceleration, and a brief explanation of their biodynamics is also made
