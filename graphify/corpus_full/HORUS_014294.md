# Short distance constraints from HLbL contribution to the muon anomalous magnetic moment.

The HLbL dispersion is not the largest hadronic contribution to the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon, but this has the largest relative uncertainty of all contributions to that observable. With the tension between the value predicted by the Standard Model and the measurements currently at 4.2 μ, the theoretical physicists have focused on reducing the uncertainty of the HLbL contribution to reduce the tension or take it beyond the threshold of discovery. In such scenario, the high energy contribution of the HLbL dispersion to the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon plays an important role. The objective of the research developed in this the theory is to study the HLbL contribution of first order in the region of maximum symmetric energy far above the limit of the hadronic threshold. This is achieved by performing a product expansion of the HLbL tensile tensile tensileity, which we perform systematically with the method of background fields. We consider our approach to the very efficient problem, among other reasons, because this allows the direct renormalization of the results of field theory.
