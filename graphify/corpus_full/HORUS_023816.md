# MICROPROYECT PUBLIC SPACE. COMMUNITY RESILENCE STRATEGIES IN THE URBAN RENEWAL PROCESS IN MANIZALS, COLOMBIA

This text is an inquiry into the urban, cultural and disciplinary implications of the disappearance of traditional domestic architecture, sometimes considered informal, which has contributed to the local identity sense in the urban development of the Western Centre of Colombia. In the city of Manizales, this daily, proseic architecture, clinging to the rugged topography of the Andean Central Cordillera and proliferating in the vicinity of the traditional historical centre, has not been spared from the interests of the land real estate market, which has led to gentrifying urban renewal processes, marginalizing a historically neglected population, displaced by singular socio-cultural violence, and has led to a precarious socio-economic situation and territorial inequality. This situation is faced by ordinary people with community actions and organized collectives that promote new ways of being and making urban life in the context of public space, a political place through which citizenship is shaped, resists peacefully
