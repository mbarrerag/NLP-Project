# Methodology for the location of faults in distribution systems.

One of the events that requires greater attention by the maintenance operator in a distribution network is the occurrence of a failure at some point in this network. Defining the location of the failure is one of the activities that require immediate attention to restore the service and avoid the decrease of service quality indices. The work of this thesis proposes a methodology for the location of failures by focusing the analysis on the stages corresponding to the process associated with the management of the maintenance operator during a failure: segmentation and detection, localization, characterization of the cause. Each of these stages is presented individually and proposed methods to solve the problem. Initially, the problem of the identification/segmentation process in which the transient and stable state states of the failure are determined from the instantaneous values of tension and current validating the application of this proposal with events with rectangular wave form and events of rapid and slow transition.
