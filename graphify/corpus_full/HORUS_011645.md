# SPACE ANALYSIS OF THE QUALITY OF THE AIR IN COLOMBIA BY SATELLITAL DATA

cams is the copernicus atmospheric monitoring service, implemented by the european center for medium-range weather forecasting (ecmwf). CAMS has at its disposal daily information on the overall composition of the atmosphere. This service generates data on all air pollutants on a time-scale. CAMS has complete spatial coverage and includes territories where there are a low number of surface monitoring stations (Inness et al. 2019). Therefore, this information can be used to complement national networks, and have a regional idea of how air pollutants behave, not only in the main cities but throughout the Colombian territory. The data of reanalysis of CAMS are mostly public access, contain information of atmospheric composition with a spatial resolution of 0.25°X0.125° and temporal resolution of 3 hours (Inness et al., 2019). For this report was downloaded information from all Colombian territory, between the coordinates Lat: 14 to -6 and Lon: -82 to -64.
