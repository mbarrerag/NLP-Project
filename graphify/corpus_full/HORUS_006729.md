# The judge of land restitution as guarantor in the follow-up of sentences and the realization of rights Analysis of cases in the communities of El Dorado, Meta and Salamita, Magdalena

At the national and international levels, it has been demonstrated that it is necessary to implement mechanisms for monitoring and monitoring the decisions of the Tribunals to address the problems presented during the execution of the sentences. The aim of this work is to highlight the role of the land restitution judge in following up the decisions that protect the rights of the displaced population. Based on the theory of dialogical judicial activism, it is argued that the judicial authority should issue broad orders, actively monitor the sentences, include all actors in the process in this work and ensure the participation of the victims. Thus, based on the systematization and analysis of two cases: Salamita, Magdalena and El Dorado, Meta, from 2016 (date on which the judgements were issued) until October 2023, it is achieved: i) to state how the follow-up has been carried out, ii) to show that some verification mechanisms have the greatest impact on the execution of the orders and iii) to highlight that social mobilization occupies an important place in the post-fall stage of the land restitution process . s were issue) until October 2023, it is possible to: i) expose how monitoring has been carried out, ii) evidence that some verification mechanisms have a greater impact on the execution of orders and iii) compact that social mobility occupations an important place in the post-judgment stage of the land restitution process
