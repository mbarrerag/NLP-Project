# LOCAL SYSTEMS OF JUSTICE IN COLOMBIA: THE WAY TO A COLLABORATIVE JUSTICE PRIORIZING PERSONS

Justice with a focus on people places people and their needs at the centre of policies, programmes and justice services. Justice with a focus on people begins by understanding their most common problems and their experiences in trying to solve them, and adopts a data-based and evidence-based approach to preventing and resolving people's problems. By focusing on fair outcomes for people, justice with a focus on people is different from current approaches to justice, which tend to focus on processes and institutions and appear to serve a few rather than all of the outcomes. Local Justice Systems (SLJ) in Colombia are a good example of a people-centred justice approach. Its success depends on effective coordination and collaboration among various actors of justice, which encompass the national and local levels. The strategy did not emerge from a policy established by a particular entity, but from the convergence of various initiatives in an organic way to the impact of the sustainable development goal, which points to the outcomes of more than two decades in different regions of the country. This case study seeks to highlight transitional justice systems in Colombia and its contribution to the 2030 Agenda, focusing especially on the target of the effective structures and the outcomes of access to the transition
