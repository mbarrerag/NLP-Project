# Propagation of compaction induced by the mechanical harvest of Saccharum spp. in two soils of Valle del Cauca, Colombia.

The objective of the research was to analyze spatially in terms of mechanical strength to penetration and apparent density in wet the compaction induced by the intensity of traffic during the mechanical harvest with self-voltage wagons of Saccharum spp. (sugar sugar cane) in a soil of clay texture and a soil of free texture of the municipality of Palmira in the region of Valle del Cauca. The fundamental concepts of soil compaction in agricultural production were reviewed: compactability, compressibility and causes of compaction, such as the transit of machinery, as well as the effects of intense traffic on the compaction of the soil. A portable geographic information system was developed that, when tested, showed valid theoretical results with 99% of confidence in the method.
