# CARTOGRAPHICAL COMMUNICATION, CARTOLOGY AND MODEL OF RATAJSKI

This editorial reviews Ratajski's model (1977) because, despite all the technological changes involved in cartographic production techniques, his vision of the cartographic communication process, of the map as a central element of this process and of the interactions between reality, the emitter and the receiver, remains in force 45 years after its publication. Lech Ratajski's cartographic model and the observed reality (i) - cartographer - map and (ii) map - user - interpreted reality are concepts that every geographer and all professional related to cartographic composition should always have in mind in the development of his daily work
