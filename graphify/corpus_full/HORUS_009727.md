# FINANCE INSTRUMENTS FOR URBAN DEVELOPMENT IN COLOMBIAALKEEPING ITS IMPLEMENTATION BY THE MUNICIPAL GOVERNMENTS

In the context of urban transformations and the competencies of municipal governments resulting from the decentralization process, recently the planning and territorial management have formally incorporated innovative instruments for financing urban development, not only based on fiscal resources, but also on the capture of value. This work analyses the scope of these instruments in Colombia, through a mixed research in a sample of 444 municipalities, based on an analytical categorization of sources of financing for municipal public infrastructure and the consolidation of the results of the collection and its destination. It is evident that, although these instruments have a high potential for financing urban goods and services, they are still little recognized by municipal authorities, are not representative in public budgets and are only recently incorporated as sources for urban development
