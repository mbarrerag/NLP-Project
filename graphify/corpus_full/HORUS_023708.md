# THE PEACE AGREEMENT WITH FARC-EP. SOME OF THEIR CONTRIBUTIONS TO AN ALTERNATIVE AGENDA

This text has as its main purpose to propose elements for the analysis on the contribution of the peace agreement concluded between the national government and the guerrillas Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia-Ejercito del Pueblo (farc-ep) to the construction of an alternative agenda.In developing this proposal, the work has been divided into five sections.In the first, based on the relevance and urgency of an alternative agenda for Colombia, some general considerations are formulated about the current conditions of the Peace Agreement and its implementation, and its meaning for an alternative agenda.In the second, it is shown that the possibilities of such an agenda have as condition the definitive overcoming of the non-closed cycle of war and violence, for which the proposal of the complete peace is constituted in an imperative; from an understanding of peace that exceeds the idea of the peace agenda.In the third section of this chapter, the present content of the Peace Agreement is explored within the alternative agenda, on the assumption of its reformist and transformative potential
