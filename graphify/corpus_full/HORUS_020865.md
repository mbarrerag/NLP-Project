# Memory of the victims in Colombia. A philosophy of praxis in the context of democratic security

This proposal analyzes the emergence of the processes of historical and collective memory in Colombia during the period of the policy of democratic security of the first decade of the xxi century; it is a question of understanding the mobilization and social organization widely motivated by the demands of memory and its consequent claims of rights by groups and subaltern classes generated in that period.The historical victimization of these groups and classes emerges in a plural and antagonistic way in relation to the power exercised in the frameworks of that policy of security, which is characterized globally by the so-called fight against terrorism, and translated nationally as a war against armed and unarmed insurgentness, then resorts to questioning at least the constitutional order of 1991 in its reference of social state of law and a diverse framework of human rights.In this context, the present proposal proposes that the memory of the victims enters into a history of the form of social relations, their discourses and practices with which to re-signify the common sense
