# Analysis of soil microbiomes of a commercial rice crop in the department of Meta, under a differential nitrogen biological fertilization scheme

Rice is the third most important crop in Colombia and contributes to improving food security. However, there are challenges such as decreasing yields, reducing land, emerging diseases and inflation of production inputs, which have generated challenges to maintain high levels of production and productivity.
