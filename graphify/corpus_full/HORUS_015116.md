# CONTEMPORARY URBAN SCENE AND MIGRANT CIRCUITS

In this chapter I seek to account for the contributions that, from the Department of Anthropology of the National University and the recent creation of the Urban Life Studies Group of the Center for Social Studies, I have tried to open up in the last 15 years through research and extension projects that have addressed the crossings of legality, conflict and violence in street settings; the practices of searching and resettlement by displaced inhabitants and their families in urban peripheries; the defense of the environment and ancestral culture in urban-rural borders; and the maintenance of status and privileges in the enclosuresing high-class neighborhoods
