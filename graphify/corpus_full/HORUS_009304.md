# LABORATORY MANUAL FOR MOLECULAR TECHNICALS APPLICABLE TO AGRICULTURAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL MICROBIOLOGY

In recent years, the study of applied microbiology has incorporated as a fundamental aspect the use of molecular techniques that support the processes of identification, characterization, bioprospecting and management or control of microorganisms. In this manual 49 experiments are presented, distributed in 10 laboratories that support these processes in the agricultural and environmental fields, and that according to their objectives have been divided into three sections: 1) Insulation of microorganisms and viruses 2) Identification and molecular characterization of microorganisms and viruses 3) Phenotypic characterisation of microorganisms and viruses Each section begins with a general introduction of the topic treated and offers a list of bibliographic references and internet tools that allow the reader to deepen these aspects. Subsequently, the laboratory practices are presented, each of which includes the objectives, followed by the introduction and the principles that support the methodologies treated, the required materials, and finally, the images commented on the expected results, and the use of micro-plants and the results of the manual were performed. Some experiments are based on the use of kits for frequent use in our laboratories; however, their manufacturers are mentioned only for information purposes and do not correspond to a commercial recommendation. These procedures can be performed with kits from different companies, depending on the availability of these materials for each laboratory. In the manual different angligisms are used because of their frequent use in molecular biology laboratories.
