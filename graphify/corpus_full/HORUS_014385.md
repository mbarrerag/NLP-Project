# Is the mortality attributable to Candida auris greater than the mortality attributable to other species of Candida spp. in patients with candidemia in Colombia?.

Objectives To determine mortality by Candida auris compared to other species of Candida spp. in adult patients with candidemia. Methods A retrospective cohort of adults with candidemia was performed in 7 institutions of Colombia between 2016 and 2021. The primary outcome was mortality at 30 days and the secondary hospital stay among survivors. The adjustment of the confusion variables was made using weights of the inverse probability of the exposure propensity score (C. auris candidemia), survival models (Weibul) and negative binomial count. Results Of 512 patients with candidemia, 244 (47.6%) died in the first 30 days. Crude mortality in C. auris was 38.1% vs. 51.1% in the other species (CNA). In the Weibull model, mortality in the group with C. auris was less (HR adjusted – aHR0.69, 95% CI: 0.53 -0.90).
