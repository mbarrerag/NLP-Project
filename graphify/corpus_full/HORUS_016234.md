# COMMUNITY ORGANIZATIONS SUPPLYING WATER, LAW OF GOOD LIVING IN CONSTEAD WITH COLOMBIAN NORMATIVITY

Community organizations provide water to about 20,000 communities living in rural areas and periur-banas in Colombia, as a result of a common history of collective effort. The form of community water management developed by these organizations is in contradiction with a normative and institutional framework oriented under the premises of sustainable development and corporate management. The researchers present a documentary review of the historical evolution of the regulations applicable to these community organizations, and then carry out an analysis of the dilemmas posed by the regulatory framework for those who supply community-managed water systems. To this end, they analyzed the testimonies presented by users, users, leaders and leaders of community organizations affiliated to the Confederation of Community Water and Sanitation Services Organizations of Colombia (COCSASCOL) during interviews, workshops and forums held between the years 2021 and 2022 in the departments of Valle del Cauca, Cauca, Risaralda and Cundinamamarca
