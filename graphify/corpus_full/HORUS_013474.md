# Immersing bodies: an ethnography with street people, puppets and objects in Bogotá

This is a research-creation project that gives prominence to the street bodies in the city of Bogotá, following their daily flows and movements in an ethnography street, imaginative and multispecies. Through the narrative with puppets and objects, it becomes aware of the experiences and practices of mobility in the street, the brands are traced, both in the bodies and in other places, and the relationships and interactions between discourses, objects and bodies. Here the street is understood as a multi-situated space, and not exclusively as the public space; in which the links of trust and affection are the starting point, decentralizing from the institutions as the main focus of attention and academic analysis.
