# EXPLORATORY REVIEW OF THEMATIC EYES ON FINANCING IN RELATION TO FINTECH AND THE BANK SECTOR

In the fourth industrial revolution, financing processes have been digitized thanks to emerging technologies in financial services, known as Fintech. This study aims to analyze the thematic axes of research on financing in relation to Fintech and the banking sector. The methodology used is descriptive and exploratory, based on an exhaustive review of the literature published in Scopus. The results show an increasing adoption of innovative technologies in financial processes, which contributes to making them more inclusive, safe and efficient. Three central thematic axes are identified: the use of technology in financial services, Fintech financing mechanisms for SMEs and entrepreneurs through peer-to-peer and crowdfunding loans, and the influence of theFintech traditional banking. This articulation of findings provides a clearer understanding of the dynamics between Fintech and the banking sector
