# HYDROLOGIC MODELATION SEMIDISTRIBUTED IN THE FIELD OF THE KINGDOM, COLOMBIA, FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF THE HYDRICAL RESOURCE

The heterogeneity in the different characteristics within urban-rural watersheds makes it necessary that the approach to the modelling of water availability take into account this variability. A case study is presented in the Borra river basin, a highly populated basin, with diversity of uses, soil cover and physiographic characteristics, for which the calibration and validation of a semi-distributed hydrological model at sub-basin scale is carried out to estimate the average monthly flow along the Borra river basin, using the WEAP tool. The results presented satisfactory efficiency indicators for calibration and an adequate representation of hydrology characteristics in the Borra river basin, so that this type of approach can be considered as a useful tool for adequate water estimation and management within basins with high hydrological variability and soil use
