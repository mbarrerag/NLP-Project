# Theory of contemporary Latin American architecture

The construction of a panorama of the theory of contemporary Latin American architecture is necessary, first, because of the absence of global visions since the 1990s, and, second, because of the expansion of the spectrum of architectural practices in recent decades. Faced with the difficulty imposed by the great amount of theoretical production in different formats (texts, events, projects, among others), two methodological lines are proposed: a traditional theoretical interpretation and an innovative one based on tools of digital humanities to review large amounts of information
