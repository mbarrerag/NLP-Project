# Strengthening a spin off project through the implementation of a real scale laboratory for the use of waste with black welded fly

The use of organic waste through the black welded fly larvae is a productive alternative of great interest that provides a high added value to waste by-products, re-valuing them within the context of the circular economy, allowing them to be used as high-value raw materials as sources of protein and energy for animal feed and organic fertilizer and soil amendments. The development of productive systems in the field with the function of real-scale laboratories allows understanding the functioning of the system within uncontrolled environments and developing optimal strategies that allow enriching the transfer and improvement of production processes. Additionally, the spin-off EntoPro project hopes to be able to support on a larger scale the research projects of the CINAT laboratory
