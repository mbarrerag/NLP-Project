# Biocarbonising applications in agriculture: assessment of the capacity to retain and release nutrients and herbicides

The high dependence on agrochemical inputs for agricultural production and low efficiency when applied to the soil have led to deterioration of the quality of surface and groundwater waters. Faced with the challenges of increasing crop fertility, ensuring crop protection and minimizing environmental impacts, in this study biocarbonized with potential properties as a means of support for the retention and release of fertilizers and herbicides were evaluated. The physical and chemical properties of three biocarbonized plants produced from invasive plants of spiny retame (ReB), carbonous (CaB) and black acacia (AcB) tests were characterized. The adsorption-dissorption capacity of nutrients N, P, K, Ca and Mg and of herbicides produced from the ametrine and atrazine treatment from the method of “batch equilibrium” were evaluated. Finally, the formulations of the biocarbonized and Mcacia (Ac) were prepared and tested in the release and leach side tests in soil columns (in laboratory conditions).
