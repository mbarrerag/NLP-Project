# PANIFICATION WITH HARINAS COMPOSIES OF WRITING AND AHUYAMA (CUCURBITA MOSCHATA DUCHESNE)

There is a range of processed foods that satisfy hunger, but do not nourish, causing a progressive deterioration of human health, however, the bread enriched with micronutrients has a beneficial effect for health contributing to the functioning of the organism. The objective of this research was to develop a functional breadmaking product based on flours composed of wheat and ahuyama from two cultivars; an experimental work was proposed with six (6) treatments T0: 100% wheat flour (HT) is 0% ahuyama flour (HA); T1: 80% HT 20% HA; T2: 85% HT 15% HA; T3: 90% HT 10% HA; T4: 95% HT 5% HA; T5: 97% HT is 3% HA and three repetitions per treatment, under a completely random design, was evaluated in each treatment for cultivating chemical, physical and organeptic properties; for the sensory test the Friedman color test was performed.
