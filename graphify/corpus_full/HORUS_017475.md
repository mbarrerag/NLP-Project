# Does the Union make the force? Pre-electoral coalitions and Partidistic competition in local and national elections in Colombia (2018-2019)

This chapter elaborates an analysis of the formation of pre-electoral coalitions in Colombia, their frequency, composition and electoral performance during the period 2018-2019. To this end, the following questions are raised: How was the party composition of pre-electoral coalitions during the regional, legislative and presidential elections in Colombia, in the period 2018-2019? What was their electoral performance? And why do some parties decide to form pre-electoral coalitions, while others prefer to compete independently in the same election?
