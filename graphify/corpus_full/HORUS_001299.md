# Implementation of the Public Quality Policy in Health Care in relation to Decent Work: Analysis from the Voice of Users, Workers and Administratives of the Integrated Subnet of Health Services South E.S.E. of Bogotá

This research work explores the implementation of the quality of health policies in Colombia, using as theoretical frameworks the model of street level bureaucracys in Lipsky and the approach of rights to the fulfilment of the right to health, enshrined in the Law of Statutory 1751 of 2015. The study is based on semi-structured interviews with three key groups: health workers, users of the system and institutional managers. Through a qualitative analysis, the challenges faced by workers in implementing these policies, working conditions, equity in care and tensions between regulatory frameworks and operational reality. The results reveal that the care workers, in their role as street level bureaucrats, exercise discretion due to the labour overload and scarcity of resources. These decisions impact the equity in the access and quality of service received by the patients.
