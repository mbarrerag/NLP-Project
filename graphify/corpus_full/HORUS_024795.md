# DEVELOPMENT AND STUDY OF THE TIO2 NANOSTRUCTURES AS DILUTED MATERIALS FOR NONVOLATILE MEMORIES: A SHORT REVIEW

In this work we are realized a discussion of TiO 2 nanostructures fabrication via Chemical Bath Deposition ( electrochemical anodization, and sputtering Likewise, the dependence of fabrication technique and growth mechanics was presented with the different nanostructure types formation like nanoflowers, nanotubes, and thin films The presence of TiO 2 polymorphous (anatase and rutile) has been identified and characterized via XRD, Raman, and IR, as well as the phases changes associated with thermal process during and after the synthesis procedure On the other hand, the morphological and topographic properties have been studied from HR SEM, stereoscopic micrograph, AFM, and TEM measurements Nanoflowers arrangement obtained with formation by nanorods or dendrites of approximately 130 22±12 5 nm size, whilst the nanotubes presented a 6 5858±0 74 μm of length Considering these properties, TiO 2 doped with Co nanostructures present a potential application in the advances of the electronics for obtaining diluted materials or spintronic devices I V curves present a SET and RESET processes and a switching of TiO 2 doped nanostructures
