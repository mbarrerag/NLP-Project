# Conceptual and methodological approach to identify the ecological structure in environmental management, reference Río Sucio Alto

The Ecological Structure -EE- has had different definitions throughout the Colombian environmental system one of them given by van der Hammen and Andrade (2003): “The set of natural and semi-natural ecosystems that have a location, extension, connections and health status such that together they guarantee the maintenance of the integrity of the biodiversity, the provision of environmental services (water, soils, biological resources and climate), as a means to guarantee the satisfaction of the basic needs of the inhabitants and the perpetuation of life.” With the aim of understanding the plot that is part of the configuration of the Ecological Structure, a conceptual approximation was made from the paradigm of the systemic approach, called systemic because its observation is given from the basic components proposed in the systems theories.
