# CHEMICAL PROFILE AND BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY OF THE JUSTICIA LEAFS CF. COLORIFERA (ACANTHACEAE)

The Orinoquía region is located in the southeast of the country and contains 17.5% of the flora species registered in Colombia, including 2.6% of the endemic species of the country according to the Amazonian Institute of Scientific Research SINCHI. A great variety of these species are used in the popular medicine of the region for the treatment of wounds by using extracts from these plants. However, it has been found that some of these species do not have prior documentation or information about their biological activities and even their metabolic profiles; among them is a plant commonly called antibiotic ? whose scientific name is Justice cf. colorifera. Therefore, this project constitutes an evaluation of some biological properties of this species, as well as the analysis of its chemical profile. Etanolic extract, obtained from the leaves of the plant, will be subjected to various tests to know the type of secondary metabolites present, with the help of colorimetric reactions and analysis by Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (MNM) (mono- and bidimensional), will be evaluated its antimicrobian, cardio-anti-oxidant and anticancerous.
