# INFILTAR, JUDICIALIZING AND CHARGE. JUDICIAL montajes IN COLOMBIAN PUBLIC UNIVERSITIES. THE HOST LEBRIXES.

This book analyzes judicial montages as a systematic practice used by the Colombian State to silence those who think differently. The investigation begins by making a historical look at the construction of the internal enemy in Colombia as a general context to understand the nature and configuration of the so-called "judicial" elements against the popular movement and specifically against the university community. At a second moment the book engages in collective conversations with the protagonists of the so-called "Case Lebrija", including relatives, lawyers, and members of the police institution, to seek to unravel through these voices how terror and stigmatization has been used in Colombia to pursue critical thinking and invisibilize poverty
