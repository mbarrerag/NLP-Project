# Protocol of care in the health of the transgender patient.

This protocol is designed to be implemented in health institutions for the proper care of transgender patients, who have been discriminated against both in the social and health spheres, compromising the integrity of the person, as well as the violation of his fundamental right to health, which is understood by the World Health Organization as the physical well-being, but also social and mental well-being of any person: therefore, timely and clear information on the approach to health of transgender patients is imperative in order to provide the best care to this marginalized population from knowledge and empathy
