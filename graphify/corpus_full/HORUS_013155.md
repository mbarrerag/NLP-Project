# Determination of heterotic groups in tropical maize endogamic lines by diallelic crosses and SNP markers

One of the main objectives of the genetic improvement programs of maize is to develop endocrid parental lines of high genetic value that when crossed can produce hybrids with superior agronomic characteristics. In Colombia, the obtaining of this type of hybrids is of high interest since they can help increase yield and production of corn and close the gap that exists between the national production and the import of maize that currently is 74%. Currently the program of genetic improvement of maize of Seeds Valley does not have a characterization of the germplasm that allows it to establish efficient strategies for obtaining hybrids of high yield. In the present study the classification of lines of endocridated in heterotic groups was carried out from genomic data using molecular markers type SNP and phenotypic variables in 66 lines of the improvement program of Seeds Valley S.A., and from the results of field of field of a dialletic type where 30 parental participated. From a cluster analysis of the data SNPs three heterotic groups or subpopulations were identified., the Seeds Valley maize brewing program does not have with the characterization of the germplasm to be used in the establishment efficient strategies for the high yielding hybrids. In this study, we are going to evaluate the classification of broken lines into heterotic groups using molecular SNP markers, and field data of 66 lines from the Seeds Valle S.A. maize breaking program, and from field data of a dialoging crossing essay where 30 parental lines involved. From a cluster analysis of the SNPS data, we identify three heterogeneous groups or subpopulations. In the other hand, dialect results allow us to infer three different groups.
