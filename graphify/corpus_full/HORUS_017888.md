# Control of electromagnetic waves using metamaterials and metasurfaces based on Huygens' sources.

In this paper, we study a source of Huygens and its application in two designs in which low reflection is required: a left-handed metamaterial adapted to the impedance of the vacuum and a metasurface of Huygens that presents abnormal refraction. In this work, we begin with the analysis of the polarization tensor of wave sub-longness dispersors, and, for some dispersors, we study that components of co-pollarization and cross polarization can be related. Subsequently, we obtain the dipolar moments and the polarization tensor requirements to have a source of Huygens anisotropic and isotropic. We demonstrate that a pair of split ring resonators (SRR) can satisfy the conditions. Then, we use these results for the design of the metasurface for the anosotropic and left-handed refractive method.
