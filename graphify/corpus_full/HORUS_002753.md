# THE COLLECTION OF VOLCANIC ROCKS AND EXTRATERRESTRIAL MATERIALS AT UNIVERSIDAD NACIONAL DE COLOMBIA-MEDELLÍN

Well-preserved hand samples and thin sections of rocks are crucial not only for teaching courses such as Physical Geology, Igneous Petrology, and Volcanology, but also for their use in research and geoconservation and geoheritage initiatives. A collection of volcanic rocks and meteorites has been put together with the primary objective of use in classes and laboratory activities, but with the potential to become both a tool to support research and a means to preserve geological heritage from around the world. The collection resides at Universidad Nacional de Colombia-Sede Medellín and is a part of the Geosciences Museum of the College of Mines
