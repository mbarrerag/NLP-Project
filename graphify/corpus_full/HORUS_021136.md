# Virtual Archaeology in Parque Berrío

The foundational square of Medellín has the particular condition of having been subject to transformations throughout its history that are only documented in photographs and oral testimonies, where it realizes the changes of style depending on its temporal context, and demonstrating from the architecture and the urban changes what Adolf Loos called the _x0093_spirit of the epoch_x0094_. From the urban component, the changes of Parque Berrio as a square are denoted, to be transformed into a park confined by consolidated paraments and finally in the current public park, whose edges are diffused by the establishment of the elevated station of the Medellin Metro. These general changes from the urban morphology are complemented by the changes in the loteo and replacement of buildings, that formerly represented a traditional configuration of dwellings and milestones of a commercial and cultural character, and that now have not a defined character of a permanent way (as the change of use of the building of Bancolombia to a headquarters of the Remington University Corporation).
