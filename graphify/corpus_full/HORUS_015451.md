# SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION OF QUANTICAL CARBON POINTS OBTAINED FROM CHARTER CASSARS AND NITROGEN DOPED: CISTEIN FUNCTIONALIZATION FOR THEIR POINTING APPLICATION IN HG2+ DETECTION

N-CQDs were prepared from chontaduro shells. Subsequently, they were operated with cysteine (CYS) to form NCQDs/CYS. These materials were characterized, obtaining for N-CQDs a semicrystalline material with a grafhetic nucleus and a surface rich in amino and carboxylate groups that stabilize the nanoparticle in water, these materials had a high absorption and fluorescence in the visible, obtaining a 29% o.k. NCQDs/CYS retained the grafhic nucleus, in addition to a functionalized surface with cystein; it presented an increase in absorption and fluorescence in the visible, increasing the ¿ to 34%. The characteristics mentioned make N-CQDs/CYS excellent candidates for further use in fluorescent detection of mercury
