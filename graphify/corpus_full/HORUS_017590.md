# POETICS GERMINATING BETWEEN VOICE AND LESSON. ITINARY OF THE WORD TO PARTICIPATE THE WORKS OF HUGO JAMIOY JUAGIBIOY AND ANASTSIA CANDRE YAMACURI

In this book, a hegemonic notion of culture associated with the illustrated literary tradition is controverted. Taking as a theoretical reference some postulates of the decolonial studies, as well as categories that Latin American literary and cultural criticism contributes, a tour is proposed by the two contemporary indigenous authors of the Colombian Amazon from an hermeneutic-literary exercise, complemented by an ethnographic and documentary approach to the cultural contexts of the authors. This reading is displayed from a conceptual, geographical, epistemological and ontological itinerary in search of references to destabilize a hegemonic interpretative framework and thus to delve into the poetics of these authors.The creative exploration of these authors alludes to certain cultural practices of the original peoples with which they identify themselves, with which they visualize their cultural niches in face to the majority society by revealing, at the same time, their individualities. Thus, the study of these works involves the recognition of a tissue in which complex symbolic systems linked to the native languages and their verbal art are linked to the abstract and literary commitment in which the social and social-based context of these works are exposed
