# Capacity development for the recovery and use of plastic waste in marine-coast communities in the Pacific, based on strengthening cooperation and knowledge exchange between the University-Company-State-Society

The project of alliance is located in the municipality of San Andrés de Tumaco, located in the Pacific coast of Colombia, at the southern end of the coast, as part of the region of the Biogeographic Chocó. Tumaco is located 300 kilometers to the south of the city of San Juan de Pasto. Its urban helmet and expansion zone are located both in continental and insular area, the latter formed by three islands: Tumaco, La Viciosa and El Morro. The proposal arises as a result of the environmental impacts observed due to the pollution by plastics, being the mangrove ecosystems one of the most threatened, together with the dynamics of the territory where the low access to formal education, the few job opportunities and the crisis of governance and governance limit and hinder the initiatives of solution to the local needs in the municipality of Tumaco. The development of capacities for the recovery, exploitation and transformation of plastic waste in marine-cost communities, with an impact on the strengthening of cooperation and exchange of knowledge between the University-Empresa-State-Sociedad, promotes by the stimulus of the potentials for the human development and the integral communities.
