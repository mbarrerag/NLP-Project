# RESISTANCE ASSESSMENT TO THE COMPRESSION OF AN ALCALLY ACTIVATED CONCRETHOOD BASED ON CARBON VOLUNTARY CENIZA.

The use of industrial waste for the manufacture of new agglomerants contributes to the reduction of CO2 emissions since, they can replace the Ordinary Portland Cement (CPO). Alkaline activated cements arise as a proposal of cementing material. The manufacture of concretes with this type of cements presents good properties of durability, however, there are still questions about the functionality of this material. In this study the mechanical response of an alkaline activated micro-concrete (AAMH) with a fly ash (CV) base, activated with a sodium hydroxide and sodium silicate solution, was investigated. A single factor and 3 replicas was designed, analyzing the mechanical response of the effort to compression. The silica/alumin ratio was varied, maintaining constant aggregate-cement and liquid-solid ratios. The prepared samples were cured at 80°C, for 24 hours. The resistance to compression of AAMHs compared to the control sample, are promising for use in structural elements
