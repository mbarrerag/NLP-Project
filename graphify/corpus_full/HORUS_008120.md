# SUSTAINABILITY IN THE DAIRY INDUSTRY THROUGH QUALITY MANAGEMENT PRACTICES

The sustainability of the dairy industry is a current demand of society and an opportunity for the industry since it generates benefits through the optimal use of resources, waste, and non-conforming product. The objective of this research was to analyse how sustainability indicators can be strengthened in the dairy industry through quality management practices. To address the objective of this work, the multiple case study was used as a methodology and involved two companies in the Colombian dairy industry. The study variables were 32 Quality Management practices and 7 sustainability indicators. Data collection instrument were in-depth interviews. Findings revealed that all productivity indicators exhibit changes after Quality Management implementation. In addition, cases approaching ISO 9001 have the highest number of practices implemented, as well as evidence of control of all productivity indicators, which allows them to perceive greater benefits. The interviewees perceive that the relevant Quality management practices for the seven sustainability indicators of the Colombian dairy industry are: all the practices of the "Top Management Support", "Human Resources Management", "Process management" and "Process control" constructs. As well as the QMp "Innovation" and "Feedback and auditing". In addition, the indicators with the highest number of Quality management practices considered relevant were those related to the amount of whey and defective product. This research generates novel findings that contribute to the consolidation of theory by analysing how sustainability indicators of the dairy industry can be strengthened through Quality Management Practices. At the same time, it provides valuable and useful information for managers, practitioners, and policy makers
