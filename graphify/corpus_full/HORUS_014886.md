# FLOWING AND VEGETATION OF THE BOYACA DEPARTMENT

The plants with flowers in Colombia have close records of 24,000 species, distributed in the five natural regions that make up the Colombian territory. In the Andes, we have information representative of the different floristic groups recognized for the country and therefore we have inputs to perform analysis in territorial units such as department that support the environmental management of them. The department of Boyacá, located in the northeastern portion of the eastern mountain range, presents a wide knowledge of the paramuna flora, however, its territory is heterogeneous and presents environmental and altitudinal gradients with differential floristic arrangements and that have not been systematically evaluated. Therefore, the aim of this book is to present the compilation of the floristic catalogue of the department of Boyacá, as well as its formations and plant associations. For the compilation of the information was carried out field work from the low lands to the high mountain, by the western and eastern slopes of the department and was complemented with information from the databases of the research group Biodiversity and Conservation (Instituto de Ciencias Nacional de Colombiaaceae) and other repositories of 5240 species, mplants, 1480 m2 m2 m2 m2 and more. families surpass the values known for the natural regions of the Chocó biogeographical, Orinoquia and Caribbean.
