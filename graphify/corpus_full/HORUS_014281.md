# Experimental work with hens to teach the concepts associated with animal nutrition, in a school of agricultural modality.

This work is a proposal for teaching the concepts associated with animal nutrition, applied and valued with students of sixth grade of high school of an agricultural school. The strategy consists of three guides with conceptual elements and activities of experimental type; the activities are proposed to be applied in the feeding of two groups of twelve hens, this in order to improve the learning of the concepts of animal nutrition, in a meaningful way in the students, allowing to strengthen conceptual relations between animal nutrition and digestive, circulatory, respiratory and excretor systems. It was possible to show that the group of students who developed the farm activities, improved their conceptual performance with respect to the group with traditional classroom activities by 8.1%. In relation to the previous knowledge, this same group presented an improvement of 19.7%, while the traditional group only increased its conceptual performance in 8.0% respect to their previous knowledge. The gain of learnings was low for the two groups, measured by the Hake method (1998).
