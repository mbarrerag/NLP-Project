# The child's brother with cancer deserves to be visible: Scoping Review

The healthy brother in childhood cancer situations is often an invisible subject for his family, health professionals and institutions. The aim of this work was to map and integrate scientific literature about the experience of the brother of the child diagnosed with cancer. A Scoping Review was developed of 28 articles published between 2016 and 2020, in the Spanish, English and Portuguese languages. Twelve qualitative studies, 15 quantitative studies and a mixed study were included. In the face of the findings, it was identified that there are changes in the relationships between brothers, parents and social, as well as personal changes and a limited offer of support to siblings of the child with cancer. It is concluded that childhood cancer directly affects family members, including the healthy brother, who faces drastic changes in his life that involve new needs, feelings and risky behaviors. Further research and approach is required about the impact of childhood cancer on healthy siblings, as well as the design of interventions that highlight the need to make visible to this subject who suffers from the collateral damage of childhood cancer and who has been neglected by his family and health professionals
