# GENOMIC ANALYSIS OF THE MEDICINE PLANTS PETIVERA ALLIACEA AND LIPPIA ALBA OF A number of REGIONS OF COLOMBIA.

Abstract This study performed a genomic analysis of Lippia alba and Petrigia allicaea, species with pharmacological potentials, for this, was extracted DNA from foliar samples and were sequenced using Illumina Novoseq6000 under the RADseq methodology. The results offer information to identify genes related to the pharmacological potential of these plants. Key words: RADseq, Population structure, Genetic diversity, PstI. Introduction Knowledge about the pharmacological potential of medicinal plants has been transferred ancestrally and culturally between different generations, however, being considered these plants as non-model, i.e. they are not of agronomic interest, the scientific knowledge that can support this potential is little. Therefore, knowing the genetic diversity and population structure of these plants will allow the presence of different compounds of each plant to be compared with the pharmacological potential of each plant, antitumor, analgesic and anti-stugal.
