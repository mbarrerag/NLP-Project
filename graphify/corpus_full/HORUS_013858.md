# The Vertiente.

The Vertiente is a research project-creation that aims to find modes of the body and alternate mental and affective dispositions that allow us to reestablish the affective and perceptive bond with the surrounding – obstructed by conditioning or imperatives coming from different sectors of our experience- through an intense interaction and an urge to establish a communication and a close relationship with a wetland at the National University of Colombia. The project includes the creation of a gesture, which seeks to stimulate resonances in the participant that comes to the experience surrounding the idea and perception of a _x0091_great body_x0092_ of which everything is part as an infinite network of connections and flows, as well as the record, _x0091_the footprint_x0092_ of the process in which they are addressed, in other languages -diary, poems, prose-, tensions, reflections, delights and fantasy that breeds this attempt of reintegration that involves interaction with the wetland
