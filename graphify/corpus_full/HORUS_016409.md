# TERRITORIAL SUSTAINABILITY OR SEARCH FOR A PLANNING OF THE ECOLOGY-BASE TERRITORY

The city is the territorial expression where the main achievements of human civilization materialize, but also where the greatest problems of society manifest themselves (Agudelo, 2002). Lezama and Domínguez (2006) add that one of the evils of the city is derived from an excess of the rationality and artificiality that it embodies; to which should be added that, the traditional approach of planning the territory in Colombia, has focused on the urban areas and restricted to the municipal limit, so that many of the interactions that occur between society and the ecosystems that supply them with goods and services are not taken into account, or they begin to be considered only when ecosystems fail to adequately fulfill their functions, when their natural limits are exceeded or when the resources begin to be scarce. That is why sustainability, as a global concept that poses alternative scenarios to the estimated trend scenario for cities, has taken force in the last decades.
