# Detection of credit card frauds, issued by a particular company, using binary rating methods in a highly unbalanced scenario

In recent years many companies have incorporated the credit card as a strategy of sale, which has allowed this means of payment to become increasingly frequent, becoming a focus of attention for the cases of crimes. The company under study has not been alien to this modality of fraud that in recent years has increased considerably generating losses in the organization. For this reason, this work seeks to implement a model that identifies frauds by means of the application of methods of classification reducing financial losses in the organization. In this work is presented a model for the detection of frauds in credit cards for a particular company, applied to a highly unbalanced database of data, that is, that the observations of one of its classes is greater with respect to the others.
