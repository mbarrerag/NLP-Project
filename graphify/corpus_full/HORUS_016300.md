# CHARACTERISTICS AND USE OF WRITTEN MATERIAL IN ENGINEERING COURSES

This paper discusses the characteristics and uses of the material written in engineering courses. A brief discussion is presented about the type of readers that exist in a classroom, seeking to characterize the type of written material to be used. It speaks of textbooks and class notes, listing their characteristics and capabilities to be supported in teaching a topic. Everything is linked to the narrative of the course, a brief account of the concepts and theories that are discussed during the course. Final comments are given, left as open subjects for the reader to discuss from his experience in a classroom
