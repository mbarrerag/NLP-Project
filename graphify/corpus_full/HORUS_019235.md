# PUBLIC POLICIES FOR THE RECONSTRUCTION OF SOCIAL TEJIDE BY EDUCATION IN TECHNOLOGIES

Social construction implies factors that could not be done or said separately and that, however, together form the masgamum that unites the living fabric, the organism in which the community must be transformed. The importance of implementing a comprehensive and inclusive public policy is ready and impacting the great majority of the population as it transcends over time, education in this sense plays a key role, therefore, in the face of technological dynamics, new educational perspectives must also incorporate technological developments, in the sense of public policy must be directed towards there
