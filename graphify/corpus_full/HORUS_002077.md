# Implementation of an alternative method for estimation of the combined dose in external beam radiation therapy and brachytherapy for the treatment of cervical cancer

The quality of a radiation therapy treatment for cervical cancer, which combines external beam radiation therapy (EBRT) and high dose rate brachytherapy (HDR-BT), depends on multiple radiological factors. The biologically effective dose (BED) and the dose equivalent to 2 Gy per fraction (EQD2) are crucial metrics for assessing the overall clinical impact, especially when deviations occur in the total duration of treatment. To address this need, a script was developed and validated that automates the extraction of dosimetric data from the planning system (TPS) and the total EQD2 calculation, comparing the results with traditional manual records. The study was based on a retrospective analysis of 160 records corresponding to 20 clinical cases. The initial validation demonstrated a perfect agreement between the physical dose data (D90 and D2cm3) extracted by the script and the overall assessment of TPS (Dose Statistics).
