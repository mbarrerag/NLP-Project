# Rating of a PRM connection from beam I armed to tubular column composite armed with fillet solders

A critical aspect in the metal structures has been the design and manufacture of the connections since they have a large participation in the response of a structure to static and dynamic loads. The type of connection that requires a deeper study, are rigid connections, that is, connections that can provide lateral stability to the structures against seismic forces. Therefore, the present research has a focus on this type of connections. In the same way as it is researched in connections that are resistant and functional and that ensure satisfactory behavior to seismic loads, it is necessary to evaluate manufacturing methodologies that are not complex and costly.
