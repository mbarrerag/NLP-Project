# THE ROW PUCCINIA KNOWS A NEW REGISTRATION FOR COLOMBIA ON PIPTOCOMA DISCOLOR (ASTERACEAE)

Colombia is a biodiverse country, where its climate and location allows not only the adaptation of different cultivars and plants (native, forest, ornamental, among others), but also of microorganisms that parasitize them; among them we find the fungi of the order Pucciniales (royas) that constitute one of the most numerous and most economically important groups of phytopathogenic fungi.The study of the rusts present in Colombia is necessary not only to know their diversity, but also their habits, hosts, geographical distribution and their life cycles.The Asteraceae family has the largest number of records of Pucciniales reported in the country, for this reason it is of great importance to advance in the studies on the present species and their hosts, among which is the native species Piptocoma discolor considers an alternative plant for the forage production in silvopastoral systems.The studies of collections from the department of Santander deposited in the Museo Micologico-MMUNM of the National University of Colombia, Medellín. The objective of this study is to present a new host of the family Asteraceae parasitized by the Spanish. found corresponds with the species Puccinia conoclinii, registered parasitizing species of the genus Ageratum in Colombia in the departments of Antioquia, Caldas, Cundinamarca and Huila. The genus Piptocoma and its species P. discolor is constituted in new records for the country. This study allows to obtain information from the hosts parasitized by rusts, its geographical distribution and the present esporical states
