# Proposal for financing between the suppliers and claimants for credit to promote the implementation of the CIRCULAR ECONOMY.

This research proposal raises the need to understand the phenomenon of financing directed at companies interested in adapting to models of Circular Economy, specifically in the tourism sector of the Department of Caldas, seen as a systemic and complex phenomenon, that responds to the challenges of climate change, the Sustainable Development Goals and the 2030 agenda, under an approach that allows to relate the impact of financing directed to the circular economy on the applicants of this type of financing, and the fulfilment of expectations for the consolidation and success in the use of financial resources in processes involving adaptation to models of circular economy in the companies that demand these resources. Specifically, the tourism sector in Colombia and Caldas presents a potential for their intervention in the transition to a circular economy, offers conditions for the development of tourism under the circular economy model according to the guidelines of the World Tourism Organization. Given these conditions, the objective of designing a proposal of financing from the systemic perspective that generates an agreement between offerors and applicants of credit to strengthen the implementation of the circular economy in the companies of the qualitative sector of the department of Caldas is posed to the development of the research, a rational method of collecting data, with a description of the reality
