# Atmospheric basins and their importance in the environmental management of the territory.

The legal rules that protect and regulate the use of environmental goods affect, in general, society and the economy, in an attempt to achieve conditions that favor sustainability without undermining the constitutional regime. In this framework, by mandate of Law 99 of 1993, the environmental authorities play a main role in the National Environmental System. It is thus in the use of the competences declared by law, the Metropolitan Area of the Aburrá Valley (AMVA), entity with environmental jurisdiction in the urban area of that territory, introduced for the first time, in 2011 the concept of “atmospheric basin” linked to the area of source of particulate material PM10; and it has endorsed in 2017 the same figure, without modifying or redefining it, but associating it with the declaratory area of source of fine particulate matter PM2.5. Although this entity is the only entity in Colombia that has applied the concept to the metropolitan environmental regulations, it still has several problems related to its definition, interpretation and technical application.
