# "The effects of interventions in the Magdalena River on artisanal fishing and food security. Colombia 21st century_x0094_

The present project focuses on addressing one of the problems identified during the research exercises already carried out on the great river of Magdalena and has to do with the impacts that the deterritorialization of the population of fishermen and fishers has had on the production and consumption of food and with the destruction of an ancestral activity that constitutes an element of both cultural and social articulatory and protection of water and food. The research aims to investigate the impact that has been had by the construction of mega-mining-energy projects and other infrastructures and interventions on the life and artisanal fishing activity in the basins of the selected municipalities. Field work will be carried out in different places of the basin to open spaces to the voices of fishermen and fishers, and that through other visibility regimes we can approach the processes of deterritorialization and the possibilities that can arise in order to revalue artisanal fishing as an activity of high value that contributes to food security, that protects the bodies of water, that keeps alive a culture of care and that guarantees the permanence in the territory
