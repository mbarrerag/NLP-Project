# SUSTAINABLE CITY, URBAN AND ARCHITECTONIC DESIGN CRITERIA

The objective of this article is to disseminate the results of the application of the methodology of sustainability in the design of urban and architectural projects carried out in the workshop of projects 6 of the undergraduate degree in architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín, to solve the problem of adapting the city to the phenomenon of global warming and make it more sustainable. The results indicate that the methodology of sustainability is an effective method of learning based on problems (ABP), because it has allowed students at all levels to meet the objectives of learning the subject projects 6. The results also allow to conclude that it is possible, in urban renewal neighborhoods, to re-naturalize the city as a sustainable ecosystem in harmony with the anthropic space, as a general strategy of urban and architectural design to adapt the city to the phenomenon of global warming and make it more sustainable
