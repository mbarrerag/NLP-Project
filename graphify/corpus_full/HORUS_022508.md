# Safety and effectiveness of different screening strategies for the detection of preneoplastic cervical lesions.

To carry out a systematic positive review of the effectiveness and safety of the different screening strategies for the detection of preneoplastic cervical lesions in women over 18 years of age who are at risk of presenting a precursor lesion of cervical cancer. Controlled clinical trials and systematic reviews of the literature were included with meta-analysis that evaluated the different strategies for screening preneoplastic cervical lesions. Searches were carried out in MEDLINE, Embase, Cochrane Library, CENTRAL and LILACS. Two reviewers independently evaluated the tests for inclusion, extracted the data and evaluated the risk of bias. Disagreements were resolved by consensus. The GRADE approach was used to evaluate the quality of the evidence. The search for the literature available until March 8, 2020 was performed.
