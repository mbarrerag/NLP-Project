# EcoCambión Rural: Intelligent Recycling and Training for Sustainable Development in Rural Communities

The project "EcoCambio Rural" aims to address the environmental and social challenges of the inhabitants of the El Higuerón village in the municipality of Palestine, Caldas. Through a comprehensive approach, where the integrated management of solid waste will be carried out, implementing an innovative prototype that will allow the exploitation and transformation of waste. The initiative will include; community training to encourage active participation, providing guidance on separation of waste from the source, environmental culture and, how these practices can generate resources to be reinvested in local improvements, contributing to the reduction of waste and the promotion of environmentally sustainable practices, as well as social and economic development. Additionally, a data analysis system will be implemented and that will provide valuable information on the quantity and periodicity of waste collection, the rate of waste taken advantage of versus the volume of waste not exploitable and other relevant aspects. These data will be presented in an accessible way through an interactive board, facilitating informed decision-making and allowing continuous monitoring of the impact of the project.
