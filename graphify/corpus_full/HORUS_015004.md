# I ask to be feminist!: Design, didactic and content parameters for the teaching of feminism in early childhood, materialized through a publication

Project that aims to understand, appropriate and integrate feminism as a movement that promotes equality, empowerment and non-discrimination to the new generations, starting from identifying problems such as: Violence and discrimination that is exercised from early environments such as school, produced by gender and class stereotypes; the glass ceiling existing by the sexist division of labour, marriage and early motherhood; androcentric education that does not seem evident to our eyes; self-love and self-esteem dictated by a sexist education exercised within families and constantly reinforced by the media. I intend to identify parameters of design, didactic and content for the teaching of feminism in early childhood and to materialize them through a publication, so I begin with a theoretical conversation with authors such as bell hooks and Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie that have similar purposes to mine. Simultaneously I will analyze didactic material used for the dissemination of feminism in the first childhood in the commercial and educational field, then identify didactic parameters, in design, content and pedagogy and from this process I propose a prototype of the type of publication and industry. aching feminism in early childhood, and materialize them through a publication, that’s the reason why I begin with a theoretical conversation with authors like bell hooks and Chimamamanda Ngozi Adichie, who have purposes similar to mine.
