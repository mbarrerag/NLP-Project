# MICROPLASTICS IN THE LANDSCAPES OF MARINE-COASTAL ENVIRONMENTS. CASE STUDY CIENAGA GRANDE DE SANTA MARTA (CGSM), COLOMBIA

Microplastics are plastic sediments that can be released into the environment from various sources, their abundance and distribution increasing as their size decreases. Mass production and consumer habits cause these to accumulate and increasingly contaminate the different environments, as happens, in the marine-coastal environment of the Cienaga Grande de Santa Marta, located in the north of the Colombian Caribbean, it behaves like a zone of convergence and accumulation of these polluting sediments, in the Cienaga, where there is a natural exchange between the Caribbean Sea and the interior of the country
