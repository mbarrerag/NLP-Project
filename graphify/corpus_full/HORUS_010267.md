# Choose Tumaco and Nariño Andino: A case of complementarity between sovereignty and food and nutritional security.

The aim of this research is to analyze how the Schools of Leaders in Sovereignty and Food and Nutrition Security – ELIGESSAN implemented in municipalities of Nariño Andino (Guachucual, Tuquerres, Cumbal and Carlosama) and in Tumaco, manage to address the complementarity between SoA and SAN in these territories. The main findings show that the schools of leaders are spaces where the approaches of SAN and SoA are addressed in a complementary way. This is seen in the perception of the leaders managing the food and their practices of vindication in their territories. In addition, the materialization of these elements of complementarity is conditioned by 1- the context 2- the relationship with the territory 3-the relationship with the food (culture). It is then concluded that the complementarity offers a possibility to address the complexity of the very reality of the territories around the food process, understanding that this complex look incorporates the relationships between opposite elements in a complementary way and not in an exclusive way.
