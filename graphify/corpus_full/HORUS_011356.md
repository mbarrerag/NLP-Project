# LIGHT CITY. PROGRESS SIDE TECHNOLOGY IN MANIZALS, 1905-1949

This urban history was constructed from the analysis of the processes that reflected the technicalization and modernization of the city during the first half of the 20th century, which were shaped from the transformation of the thinking of the elites, the introduction of foreign technical advances and the construction of new infrastructures that expressed the ideal that the contemporaries associated with progress. Urban modernization is exemplified in the case of Manizales since the implementation of hygienic, urbanistic, stylistic and technical measures that sought to make the city a stage of representation of the advances of the time. It also responds to the establishment of a new order and a new power that, during this period, can be differentiated from three aspects: the city seen as a prototype that establishes a system of administration and financing in which, the moralizing ideology of the elite determined the correct way to occupy and live the space; the city understood as a network of connections and disconnections in which, during this period, public works acted as tools of ordering, segregation and social control; and, the city as an artifact in which the century articulated the scientific knowledge of foreign professionals and the construction of infrastructure of the avant-garde, it was the city, to implement new scenarios and to the public works of the universal
