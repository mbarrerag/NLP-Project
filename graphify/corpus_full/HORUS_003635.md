# INTRODUCTION TO THE LABORATOR - LAPLACIAN

The purpose of this talk is to introduce the f-Laplacian operator which is a more general p-Laplacian operator. We will then present an application. Specifically we will work with a Dirichlet problem in the unitary ball whose differential equation involves the f-Laplacian operator
