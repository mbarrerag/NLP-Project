# TETRAGONISCA ANGUSTULA IN CUNDINAMARCA: BIOLOGY, ECOLOGY AND POTENTIAL MARKET OF HIS MEL.

Book with results of research, of studies of reflection and of experiences in science in the framework of bees without sting, with emphasis on the angelite bee Tetragonisca angustula of Colombia. Aimed at the agricultural sector, the academic-research sector and all those interested in the bees without sting and meliponiculture, the book includes two sections, one related to the bionomy of the species and another related to meliponiculture. In addition to the contribution to knowledge about the species in Colombia, it is intended to awaken the interest and passion for research, transdisciplinary work and the recognition of our knowledge as fundamental values of scientific, social and territorial development for the country. It emphasizes the ethical development, respectful and responsible of meliponiculture, as passion or economic, social and cultural alternative of the human communities
