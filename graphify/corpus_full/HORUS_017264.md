# HYDRATATION RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CHILDREN AND ADULTS

Water is the main constituent of the human body, therefore adequate hydration is necessary to maintain all functions of the body. Water is found at approximately 75% in newborns and at 50-60% of total body weight in adults (European Food Safety Authority, 2010). Adequate fluid intake and hydration are important, in addition to physiological reasons and control of thermoregulation, by adopting healthy and sustainable consumption habits from early ages. It is relevant to encourage the promotion of water intake through multicomponent interventions that combine educational, environmental and behavioral aspects to maintain adequate hydration levels (Bottin et al., 2019). In the early childhood from birth to adolescence, marked changes in the distribution in body fluid compartments and replacement rates per unit of body weight are presented, and therefore in this chapter the hydration recommendations for different age groups are discussed
