# The Project Education and the challenges for education of the island of Providencia: Routes for professional strengthening, reconstruction and pedagogical reorientation, in the context of the health emergency of the COVID 19 and the effects of Iota.

The teachers of the educational institutions of the island of Providencia, of the pre-school level, basic and medium, are now constituted as a vulnerable group due to the effects of the devastation of the hurricane Iota in November 2020 since their territory was highly destroyed and their daily work is fractured. Currently, they are working in temporary educational facilities, responding to the requirements of presence of the MEN in conditions that require the development of pedagogical and didactic strategies of the whole educational community that allow to reconstruct the social fabric and the sense of life in the midst of the still existing ones, restrictive conditions of the health emergency of the Covid 19. The educational system of the island of Providencia, formed by 47 teachers, 874 students and 3 public educational institutions, has a population of more than 95% isleña, providenita, root teachers that participate in the group of approximately 20 teachers/s of the island of providence. This social condition demands specific projects and programs that respond to their needs and training horizons. The Research Group State and Society (GES) affiliated to the Caribbean Headquarters proposes to carry out a job of accompaniment to a group of teachers in the five members of the island of the province of the province of the province of e.g. school broadcasting, podcasting, inter-school correspondence, publishing project with publications of teachers' and students' life stories and articles of scientific dissemination, adaptation of literary works on the islands to radiotheater, theatre and cinema scripts, production of audiobooks in Spanish, English and Kriol, among others. Phases of the proposal Phase 1 (July and August 2022) Emotional and intellectual support to teachers - individual mentaries - through life stories about the experience of devastation of the hurricane and of work in the context of the health emergency, compiled orally or through letters, with active listening. And, characterization of the teachers of Providence in terms of their sociocultural skills, their knowledge and ancestral knowledge to build a pedagogical project in the context of the catastrophic environmental situation and the post-pademic, in the perspective of the gradual recovery of educational dynamics. Phase 2 (September 2022 to March 2023) Planning and development of pedagogical projects as an authentic means of curriculum integration of disciplines and knowledge, languages and follow-up to stage 3
