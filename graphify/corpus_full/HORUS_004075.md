# Salt value chain in La Guajira: a look at your social progress

The purpose of this project was to analyze the perception of social progress of the main actors linked to the value chain of salt in La Guajira, for this purpose, a descriptive approach research was carried out with a cross-sectional cut. Theoretically it is supported in the concept of value chains, in the existing literature on social progress and in the methodologies that are responsible for its measurement. For its development, a review of the literature on the value chain of salt in the region was carried out with a view to identifying the direct or main actors, subsequently, a semi-structured interview instrument applied to selected informants through intentional sampling taking into account two criteria: to be legal representative, manager, or directors of associations, organizations, or companies that are studied; or to have participated in this activity for at least 5 years.
