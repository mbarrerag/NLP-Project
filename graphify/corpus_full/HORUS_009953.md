# A UNUSUAL CASE OF SINTOMATICAL RESPIRATORY IN HIV INFECTION

A 68-year-old man was evaluated in the outpatient infectious disease clinic for a recent diagnosis of HIV infection. He consulted 15 months earlier for unintentional weight loss (30 kg) associated with asthenia and adynamia. In addition to the picture, he had been presenting cough with expectoration, without dyspnoea, without any other related symptoms.
