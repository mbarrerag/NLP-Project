# Evaluation of an active collection of sugarcane germplasm (Saccharum spp.) for sugar content in the first third of the harvest in Ecuador.

The objective of this work was to determine the components of variance, and the heredability in wide sense for sugar content. The seeding and evaluation of the genotypes were carried out in three localities, Ingenio COAZÚCAR, sector Vanillo A014-150, Ingenio San Carlos, sector 020604 and the third locality in the Research Center of the Sugarcane of Ecuador (CINCAE), the treatments were formed by 90 genotypes, each genotype, were sown in a plot of two grooves of five meters of length, distance 1.5 m between grooves (CINCAE) or 1.9 in double groove (COAZÚCAR and San Carlos). A design of complete randomized blocks with two repetitions, under a mixed model, at 12 months in plant cane and 12 months in sugar cane was used, here a sample of seven primary stems was taken and the analysis of quality of the cane was performed, with the data obtained from Pol, Brix, purity and fiber in cane, was calculated the yield of sugar in POLY (total solids).
