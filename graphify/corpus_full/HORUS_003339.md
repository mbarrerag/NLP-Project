# PHITO-CHEMIC ANALYSIS AND VALUE OF BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY OF SERRULATA TRIPOGANDRA SHEET (COMMELINACEAE) RECOLECTED IN COLOMBIAN ORINOQUIA

The species Tripogandra serrulata is a plant that grows in the Colombian Orinoquia, is commonly known as sugar, and is popularly used to assist in anti-inflammatory, analgesic and healing processes. Despite its importance in the region, this plant does not have scientific research records that describe its chemical composition or its biological activity in the Orinoquia region. This study aims to evaluate the main chemical components, the antimicrobial, anticancer and antioxidant activity of the leaves of T. serrulata. The methodology of this project includes the collection and drying of leaves, followed by the obtaining of the etanolic extract by maceration, a subsequent fractionation of the extract with the method of Kupchan, to obtain several fractions (FO, WB and WW). The preliminary chemical analysis carried out to the extract allowed the presence of various prokaloids, polyphenols, tannins and its flaomous analysis. The study of the leaves of T. serrulata carried out so far shows the presence of various compounds, and a good antioxidant potential against the radical DPPH. It is expected that this research will contribute to the knowledge of this species, enriching its biological and pharmacological value, offering a bibliographical reference for this plant and its use in the Orinoquia region
