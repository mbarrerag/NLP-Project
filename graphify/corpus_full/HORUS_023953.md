# A CHARACTERIZATION OF EXTREME LLUVIAS IN COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN DURING THE BUILDING OF CURVAS IDF

The intensity-duration-frequency curves (IDF) are intended to be an input for the design of hydraulic structures, in accordance with local precipitation conditions. Currently IDEAM has approximately 130 stations with IDF curves in the country, of which only 12.3% correspond to stations in the Caribbean region. In this sense, this study concentrates on the construction of IDF curves for 10 additional stations located in this region, using the IDEAM records between 1964 and 2012; this in order to ensure statistical significance in the analysis of extreme short-duration rainfall. In this sense, the procedure began with the identification of digitally available rainfall charts and the selection of the 15 most intense storms of each year; subsequently, through the HIDFUN software, the data were processed digitally, to then construct the IDF curves from the annual series of precipitation intensions for durations of 15, 30, 60, 120 and 360 minutes, all of this with the help of the IDFtool library.
