# Experimental simulation of an environment with solar UV radiation on Mars and its effects on germination and photosynthetic pigment content in Chenopodium quinoa

This research has two important phases, the first analyzing the behavior of irradiance in the atmosphere of Mars and the second evaluating the effects of simulated solar UV radiation on Mars in real Quinoa and Marangani Yellow.
