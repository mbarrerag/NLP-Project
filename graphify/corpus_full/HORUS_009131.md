# BOGOTA NODE OF SLAM NETWORK

Result in printed book of the results of the RED Galería Santa Fe 2021 Plastic Arts Programming Scholarships. In this award, Diego Aguilar, professor of the National University of Colombia, as director of the Tecnopoetics Group, made a series of main exhibitions and curatories with professors from different universities in Bogotá, working in the area of Mediatic Art. The professor together with his working group, held three high-level exhibitions, held in La Casa Jorge Eliecer Gaitán, the Exploratory Building, La cinemateca Distrital and a Digital exhibition that brings together the problems of art and technology in Colombia
