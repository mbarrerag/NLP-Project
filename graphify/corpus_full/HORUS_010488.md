# The future promise of bio-generation and use in Colombia.

Biomass is a renewable source of energy. One of its uses is the generation of biogas from the anaerobic digestion of organic waste from different suppliers. Can biogas be used in crude to generate heat and/or electricity, and through processes can be obtained biomethane, which can be transformed into natural gas for vehicles and liquefied natural gas. Colombia has the different conditions for biogas generation, which contrasts with the low number of biogas installed and biogas utilization. Despite this, the literature shows the elaboration of studies that evaluate the potential for the generation and use of biogas, together with the possibility of using the existing infrastructure of natural gas, which leads to a promising future for the scale use of this renewable energy resource
