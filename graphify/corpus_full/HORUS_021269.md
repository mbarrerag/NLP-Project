# Design and formulation of a strategic action plan based on the energy efficiency assessment of the La Paz headquarters of the National University of Colombia.

The aim of this project is to identify the determining factors that generate high energy consumption and could be reduced by the proposed study. The development of a strategic action plan arising from the headquarters energy efficiency assessment will allow systematic measures to be taken based on information collected on the day-to-day basis of the university work. The study proposed by measuring the electrical, thermal and CO2 quantity variables in both closed and open spaces will lead to the precise precise measures to reduce consumption. It is also important to mention that the importance of the proposed study is not only within the national government’s guidelines for mitigating consumption and thus CO2 emissions produced by such high energy expenditure but also that the participation of students for their training in this context becomes paramount and necessary for the Caribbean region, where very high energy consumption occurs due to its geographical position and the need for thermal comfort
