# DIFFICULTATION OF SLEEPING AND ANTROPOMETRIC INDULTS OF THE HIGHEST COLOMBIAN ADULTS

The book deals with research results of members of the Food and Human Nutrition Research Group of the Faculty of Medicine. This chapter is the result of a research that analyzes the association between the difficulty of sleeping with anthropometric indicators in a representative sample of Colombian elderly people
