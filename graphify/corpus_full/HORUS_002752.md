# INFLUENCE OF PROMOTORS IN THE EFFICIENCY OF CATALIZERS OF OR IN THE REFORMED METANO SECO

In the search for renewable energy sources, biomass has resurfaced as a promising alternative. One of the most interesting transformation mechanisms, is anaerobic digestion, where the decomposition of organic matter by bacterial action generates a mixture of methane (CH4) and carbon dioxide (CO2), known as biogas. This mixture, coupled with the process of dry methane reform (DRM), can generate valuable intermediaries such as synthesis gas (H2(g)+CO(g)). Designing an economically viable, stable catalyst, and with a high catalytic activity is the main objective to implement the DRM as a strategy for the valorization of biogas. Ni/Al2O3 catalysts have been widely used due to their excellent activity and accessibility, however, their long-term stability is low due to problems of synterization and deposition of coke.
