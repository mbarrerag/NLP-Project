# Sociodemographic, labour and knowledge factors on tuberculosis among health workers

Health workers (TS) present vulnerability to tuberculosis due to individual, occupational and institutional factors. Therefore, approaching these characteristics allows to orient interventions. to describe the sociodemographic, labor factors and knowledge about tuberculosis in TS of a health institution in Bogotá. quantitative cross-sectional study, which applied a knowledge, attitudes and practices instrument on tuberculosis for TS. The sample was calculated with a sensitivity of 5 % and a reliability of 95%. The inclusion criteria were: TS with contract for service provision, fixed or indefinite term, with access to the internet; exclusion criteria: students in the health area and TS who had suffered the disease.
