# Strategy of articulation and cooperation in order to contribute to the STI from the Laboratories of Palmira Headquarters to the Pacific region in compliance with the missionary processes of the National University of Colombia

This project is proposed by the need to articulate the Laboratories of the National University of Colombia (Sede Palmira and Sede Tumaco) in order to enhance the research, teaching and extension services provided to the Pacific region of Colombia. To this end, it is necessary to identify the technological tools available in each laboratory, provide spaces for exchange of experiences, find and agree common lines of work that benefit the laboratories and the headquarters of the Colombian Pacific region, strengthen the laboratories with new technologies to generate tools of articulation with the missionary purposes of each laboratory, hoping to influence within a thematic axis that allows the laboratories to grow and contribute to the development of the region, planning the formulation and execution of joint research lines and that benefit the university community. With the strengthening of the research areas of the headquarters, it is intended to generate the possibility of generating projects that allow to address problems of the region, related to the sanitary diseases that affect the agricultural sector. Specifically, it aims to directly address the productive lines of the agricultural sectors of cocoa and cocoa crops and currently diagnostics and from the livestock sector, to provide the agricultural farms and the generation of agricultural products, through the agriculture, which are installed in the agricultural sector
