# Seed of Territorial Fiscal Decentralization: Cannabis Regulation

This research project seeks to develop a proposal for comprehensive and comprehensive fiscal policy for the collection and regulation of the cannabis market in Colombia, taking into account the latest attempts at the congress to legalize marijuana, starting from the scenario where these attempts are approved and consequently, creating the need for the Colombian State to issue a norm to regulate the production and distribution of cannabis. This research project will discuss how and where fiscal regulations on the subject should be focused, in order to avoid that the State will end up creating legislation that is inconsistent with the reality and needs of the communities immersed in this market. To address this, two fundamental axes are addressed. First, to understand gender perspectives, and other differential approaches that appreciate the multiple legal and constitutional contexts, with a view to the economic and social development of these communities, starting from the co-extension of the future legalization of cannabis for adult use in the country. Second, in a proactive way, it aims to project a fiscal framework that considers the multiple legal and constitutional principles, such as the social state of law, fiscal sustainability and human dignity; and it focuses on the economic and social needs of the communities.. In order to conclude with the result of presenting to the academic world the proposed comprehensive proposal that is suitable in considering the social and economic problems of the communities, and realistic in being formulated according to the current possibilities of the Colombian State
