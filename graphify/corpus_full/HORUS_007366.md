# Destination: “The vine of the soul” The ritual of the yagé as part of the tourist wellness offer in Sibundoy

This research project seeks to explore the relationships, impacts and perceptions between tourism and the ritual practice of the yagé, recently configured as part of the offers of the tourist service of well-being directed by some members of the village kamëntsa in the municipality of Sibundoy, Putumayo. This population has worked hand in hand with governmental institutions such as the Chamber of Commerce and the Government of Putumayo in the construction of a project that aims to consolidate the Sibundoy Valley as a tourist district but at the same time generates awareness to the tourist so that during its visit there is a respect and an assessment for the territory and the people who live it, based on the multiculturalism of Colombia. The study uses methodological strategies such as multi-situed field work, ethnography, the participant and non-participative observation or visual analysis to identify the changes and permanences that occur in the interaction of this ceremony with a tourism approach that aims to establish codes of conduct in tourists and promote at the same time the cultural significances that the people give to this ritual.
