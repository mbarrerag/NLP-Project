# Development and Experimental Analysis of a Metal-filled Side-hole Photonic Crystal Fiber-based WGM Temperature Sensor

The development and experimental analysis of a WGM temperature sensor using a Bismuth-filled side-hole PCF microresonator is reported. The sensor exhibits a sensitivity of 17.14 pm/°C, maintaining a nearly constant Q-factor from 25°C to 61°C
