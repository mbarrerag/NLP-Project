# VOT AND VOTE RATIO IN HHISPANICABLANTS WITH PARKINSON DISEASE

The Voice Onset Time (VOT) is an acoustic measure that refers to the difference between the time of release of the occlusion and the beginning of the vibration of the vocal cords in occlusive sounds; the VOT ratio (VOTr), on the other hand, refers to the relationship between the VOT and the duration of the syllable that seeks to model the effect of the speed of speech on the VOT. Both measures have been studied in speakers with Parkinson's disease (EP) in experimental speech tasks, but there are few jobs in people with EP speaking Spanish and in the context of speech connected. In this study the behavior of the VOT and VOTr in speech connected in Colombian Spanish speakers with EP. Through the software Praat were calculated the VOT and VOTr of the sounds /p, b, t, d, k, g/ in the most VOT were related to the body PC-GITA that corresponded to 50 people with EP and 50 without EP paring by age and sex.
