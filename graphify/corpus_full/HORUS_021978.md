# Forecasts in nonlinear time series: application of the TSARX model and comparison with models for seasonal data.

The introduction of the TAR models in economic analysis has allowed to capture nonlinear behavior, usually observed in this type of time series. In addition to nonlinearity, economic time series may present seasonal behaviors whose analysis could lead to conclusions more in line with reality. On the other hand, making accurate forecasts about the values that a series of time will take, is an important topic in economics, so a sector of literature has been responsible for comparing the accuracy of forecasts generated from different types of models. In this work, the accuracy of forecasts obtained by adjusting the Multiplicative Seasonal Threshold Autoregressive model with exogenous input - TSARX, with respect to other models usually used in the literature.
