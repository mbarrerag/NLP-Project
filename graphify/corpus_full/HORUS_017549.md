# Evaluation of public policy based on the management of acute child malnutrition in Bogotá 2015-2020: Comprehensive analysis of the guidelines for the management of acute malnutrition in children under five years of age, with emphasis on the Integrated Subnet of Health Services North.

Child malnutrition is a problem of global interest, on which Colombia has made adjustments to management during history, based on policies and regulations. These regulations are usually subordinated to the international guidelines; in this case, the design of resolution 5406 of 2015 is based theoretically, on WHO guidelines on how to manage acute malnutrition. The resolution is established in order to give optimal, rapid and effective management to acute malnutrition. This guideline is interested mainly in reducing the figures of morbimortality, but also in guiding a protocolized management by all the actors that converge in the health sector to solve this problem. In the development of this work a retrospective analysis of resolution 5406 of 2015 in the period 2015-2020, through models of public policy evaluation, in order to identify the consequences of its implementation in the exercise of the multiple actors that converge in this problem and how the actions derived from its implementation in Bogotá was performed. For the above, the document of the resolution was contextualized and characterized, in order to later to deploy the changes that go beyond the execution of the linement and later made in the reorganization. The second contribution, recognizing how in clinical and healthcare imagery, priority is given to a single cause of malnutrition, which is the one that gives the guideline, not meaning that there are no public programmes and strategies that address the other forms of malnutrition, but that health professionals, especially in medicine, focus on what has to do with nutrition, in which anthropometric measures of weight for the size of a minor are appropriate
