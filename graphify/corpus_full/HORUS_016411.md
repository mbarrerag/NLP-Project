# SOLUTION OF THE ONE-DIMENSIONAL PROBLEM OF STERILIZATION OF PRODUCTS IN THE AUTOCLAVE

The task of sterilizing prepackaged food, which is of great importance in the food industry, corresponds to the problem of optimal control, the dynamics of which is described by a nonlinear heat equation. Using the Crank Nicholson technique, this problem can be reduced to a discrete optimal control, which in this work was solved by interior point methods based on quadratic SQP programming methods
