# Genotyping of NNVs from the promoting region of the genes IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-a associated with inflammatory response in the obstructive apnea phenotypes of sleep and periodontal disease.

The objective of this study was to determine single nucleotid variants (SNVs) in the promotating region of the genes IL-1β c.-511C>T, c.-31T>C; TNF-α c.-308 G>A; IL-C C.- 572G>C, c.-174C>G, and the protein levels of these proinflammatory cytokinins in saliva of individuals with obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and chronic periodontitis (CPC). A study of cases and controls was performed, including a total of 129 individuals from Bogotá, Colombia. The group of cases was subdivided into PC, AOS, PC plus AOS. For genotipification, DNA extraction and amplification by PCR was performed to carry out Sanger sequencing. The data were analyzed by means of case and control, including a total of 129 individuals from Bogot, Colombia.
