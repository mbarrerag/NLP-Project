# REFLECTIONS AND EXPERIENCES IN HIGHER EDUCATION AND SUSTENTABILITY: COMING TO HIGHER EDUCATION FOR SUstenTIABILITY.

This book shows us, from multiple perspectives, how the challenge of incorporating sustainability in higher education in Colombia, understood this one, perhaps, as the main challenge of education in the face of the civilizing crisis facing humanity today, is posed through aspects such as the passage of simple thought towards complex thinking; from anthropocentric positioning towards a new geocentric vision of the world; from a technological development based on mechanical and linear logics to new logics of technological and engineering development based on system paradigms; and all this, in turn, including a rethinking of university culture and identity. It also presents real experiences of transformation towards sustainability in areas such as the promotion of transversal competencies, the use of new technologies, accreditation and management of campuses and extension through alliances with multinational companies and entities in the territories, among others
