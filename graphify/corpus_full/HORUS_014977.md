# THE ADVANCED EQUILIBRIUM POINT (PEA): TOOL TO INSURANCE THE SOSTENI

The financial sustainability of companies depends on their ability to generate value, i.e. profits, yield above the rate of return and positive free cash flow, so their sales must exceed the units of equilibrium point, this base to establish sales goals, so it must be correctly calculated. To this end, the advanced equilibrium point model (Pea) is presented, which corrects the defects of the traditional model. It was built by investigating the entrepreneurial capacity in Colombia, using the logit method, observing the success or failure of projects financed during 2006-2017 by the Emprender Fund. The Pea is oriented to correct and replace the traditional model by ensuring sustainability and business development
