# LOCAL PUBLIC CULTURAL POLICIES IN FIVE CASES - CHARACTERIZATION OF MANIZED TERRITORIES, VILLAMARIA, ARAUCA, SABANET, TEUSAQUILLO -BOGOTÁ -

This book is the product of initial research work of the teams formed in each node of the CPCWG integrated by the governmental, private and community cultural agents in the municipalities of Manizales and Villamaría (Caldas), Villa de Santa Bárbara (Arauca), Sabaneta (Antioquia) and Locale de Teusaquillo (Bogotá). It relates a series of statistical and documentary variables consulted to establish a first approximation to the base characterization of the territories as a fundamental input to make diagnoses and formulate recommendations of local cultural public policy - reason of being of the CPCGG. In this exercise a methodological design of descriptive and interpretative type has been carried out, in which it has been used sources 1 HERMES is the official research platform of the National University of Colombia. 14 secondary documental documents (2016 is included in the population 2021) and primary (inspections and conversational of the public resources, economy and infrastructure). The main objective of the cultural agents was to be used. The management of public policies of culture for an integral, equitable and sustainable development.With these research processes for cultural management in the territories, the effort to recognize, understand and promote the culture and diversity of our regions and their knowledge was sought to materialize, to take advantage of it as a vital source of our creative spirit.
