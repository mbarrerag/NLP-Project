# INTERSECTIONS — POLICY AND PEACE PERSPECTIVES

Intersections: political and aesthetic perspectives for peace is the name of this novel commitment. In it, we wanted to bring together the reflections from academic research with those that emerged from the same phenomenon in the field of art. Thus, we start from a transdisciplinary work in which artists and researchers exchange ideas, reflections and experiences. Thus, this book seeks to make visible these intersections between academic reflection and art, for which we are buried in the strangeness, the unfinished, the openness of that signifier that we do not know as a nation called peace
