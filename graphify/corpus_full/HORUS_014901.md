# CORPORATE GOVERNMENT: EXPLORATION OF TRENDS IN RESEARCH FROM BIBLE ANALYSIS

The conceptions of good corporate governance have changed over time, even definitions vary considerably and it is not possible to state that there is a single definition that specifically describes the concept of corporate governance. Theme relevant at the global level as it becomes a tool to address the loss of public confidence generated by fraudulent practices of managers and managers of recognized organizations. In this sense, this document aims to analyze the evolution of scientific production on corporate governance; from this consideration the main publishing trends are identified and future research is suggested for stakeholders in the topic. For this purpose a bibliometric analysis of performance and scientific mapping was carried out, as well as a factor analysis and cluster analysis. 501 articles were identified in the period 1973-2022 and 958 authors. The results show that changes in corporate governance research are important, in the initial period from 1973 to 2000, financial reports and decision making show greater relevance in corporate governance research, while accountability and governance of managers present a higher degree of corporate development.
