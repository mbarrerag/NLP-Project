# EXPERT CAREER: CO-CREATION WITH YOUNG UNIVERSITARY

Care is a human condition that allows first of all the survival and from the ethics of care, implies the relationship with others to take care of them and them. Caring for a person with some degree of dependence, is usually a role that is invisible, requires preparation and is assumed mainly by women. On the other hand, the research has shown that a phase of importance of the caregivers leads them to transmit to other new or potential caregivers their experience lived and through dialogue of knowledge strategies to pass on to this role in a healthy way. There are initiatives developed and validated by the proposing group, which at this time has considered the approach of expert caregiver, as the strategy of co-creation viable to develop strategies of social appropriation of knowledge for the care systems. Co-create an expert care strategy that contributes to improve the adoption of the role, the quality of life, the agency of self-care and the decrease of the overload, anxiety, depression, loneliness in university caregivers.
