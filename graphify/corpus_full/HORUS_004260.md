# SCIENTIFIC AND TECHNOLOGICAL MONITORING PROCESS IN PHYSICAL-ENZYMATIC MODIFICATION PROCESS IN SCIENTIFIC ALMIDON AND TECHNOLOGICAL WATCH ON PHYSICAL-ENZYMATIC AMENDIFICATION PROCESSES IN STARCH GRANULES

The current demands of industries make it necessary to use new tools for the generation of technological development and innovation, so that the need to implement technologies in the search for trends in the field of the processes of modification of the starch materials has been identified. This study was aimed at determining the dynamics of scientific production, through innovation tools, such as scientific surveillance in the modification of starches by enzyme hydrolysis, assisted by emerging technologies. To this end, a quantitative analysis of the results was carried out, based on data collections derived from scientific bases, reported in clusters and trend maps, with information on the main journals, authors, timelines, entities and areas of knowledge in the physical-enzymatic modification of starch.
