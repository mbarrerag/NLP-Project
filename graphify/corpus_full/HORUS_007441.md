# Health risk assessment for exposure to chemicals in laboratories of an arts faculty at a higher education institution in Bogotá 2023

The health risk assessment applied in work contexts is an important tool to identify conditions that may be generating potential situations that represent a risk to health, in this case due to exposure to chemical substances, of those who develop activities specific to a work and academic environment such as an institution of higher education, and contributing to provide the necessary guidelines for timely intervention and implementation of measures that guarantee good practices and risk mitigation in the scenarios in which chemicals are handled. Justification: This research starts from the need to verify the conditions in which chemicals are handled within the academic spaces of the Faculty of Arts of the National University of Colombia, taking into account that the use and inappropriate handling of such substances could generate alterations in medium and long term health in the different members of the academic community. To evaluate the risk to health derived from exposure to chemical substances in the laboratories of the Faculty of Arts, which is required for the individualization and characterization of the different substances (I.E.S). For the development of this research, the simplified methodology for the evaluation of chemical risk of the French National Institute of Research and Safety (INS) was applied to the chemical substances (in the following is the use of the individualization and the individualization and the use of the individuality The use of substantive majority has been carried out in the context of the internal market. The laboratory identified as having the high risk was the one dedicated to sculptural processes, due to its frequent and extensive use of solvents, which predominated together the prioritized substances. The graphic and geography laboratory also presented risks, particularly from substances such as Dektol, a developer for paper and film. Combined with solvents, these substances pose risks of carcinogenicity and significant organizational-specific effects. Although no health changes were reported during the investigation, it is essential to understand these to implement risks of preventive measures.
