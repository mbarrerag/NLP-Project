# U-Pb petrology and geochronology of the Norean and associated ryolite formation located on the eastern and western flanks of the Magdalena Middle Valley, Colombia.

The Norean Formation located in the eastern and western flanks of the Middle Valley of the Magdalene (VMM) is composed of a succession of lytic, feldspatic, lodolite and toba to the base, and predominant packages of crystalline, crystal-vitreous and collapsing tobas and masquillies to the middle and upper part of the formation, and intrusive bodies (outcomes in the west of the VMM).This unit is related geochemical and geochronologically to the cartographic bodies as riolites in the sector of El Playon to the north of Bucaramanga, but in this study they were classified as tuffas (crystalline and lytic) and lavas of trachite quartz composition.The geochemical analyses of tobas, lavas and intrusive bodies of the North-West formation of both sides of the MMW indicate that they belong to the magmatic series calcoline, and present negative anomalies of the NB, EE, Sr and EuLR Playon, and continued north and west, towards the Aguachica and Arenal sector, while producing effusive material from the dacitic lava and sedimentation processes. The possible units of contribution for the Tobas of the Norean Formation and those of the El Playón sector are the Amazon Craton or the Gneis of Bucaramanga. Using petrographic, geochemical and geochronological information, the extended formation environment of the intra-arch basin was proposed, in which an extensive volcanic archoeic, with main pulses of vulcanism and subsequent placement of plutonic bodies was developed
