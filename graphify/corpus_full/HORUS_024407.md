# Strengthening of skills in meaningful learning of Genetics.

In the present work the design of a didactic booklet is evidenced as a methodological strategy for the strengthening of competences in the critical meaningful learning of genetics. This intervention proposal in the classroom is based on the qualitative interpretive approach and arises from the need to promote scientific education in the San Luis Gonzaga Educational Institution, located in the municipality of Santa Fe de Antioquia, due to the performance obtained in the Saber tests 11 in recent years, showing that progress has not been made in skills-based education. With the implementation of the didactic booklet, it is intended to strengthen the competences: Comprehensive use of scientific knowledge, explanation of phenomena and inquiry. The proposal is expected to be applied and evaluated with eleventh grade students when the current public health problem caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus is overcome or when the attendance of students under the alternation model stabilizes
