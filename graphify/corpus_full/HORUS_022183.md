# Influence of silicon in the development of foliar diseases and physiological and agronomic responses of pea (Pisum sativum).

Within the main limiting factors of the cultivation of arveja (Pisum sativum L.) are the foliar pathogens that can cause losses between 20 and 100%. The application of biostimulants such as silicon (Si) has been reported as an alternative for the management of diseases, however, the information on its use in arveja is still limited. In this research the effect of basic and acidulated silicon on the development of Ascochyta spp. in vitro and low greenhouse conditions in plants of arveja variety Vizcaya. Likewise, in conditions of field two experiments were carried out, which evaluated the effect of the edaphic or foliar application of the Si (acidulated or basic) on the development of diseases caused by Ascochyta spp., Peronospora viciae and Erysphe pisi. In addition, the physiological and agronomic responses of the crop were determined compared to the single application of fungicides. As variables, in vitro conditions, the micro-like growth of the colony was evaluated; the greenhouse and field content of the disease were evaluated
