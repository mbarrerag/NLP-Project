# SECURITY AS A CATEGORY OF SOCIO-JUDIC ANALYSIS

This text seeks to explain the concept of security as a category of socio-legal research, based on the use given by the political authority, its relationship with the social, the legal and its effects on human rights. For this, a documentary research was carried out that, first, proposes to reflect on the category of security as the object of research of legal sociology and, subsequently, analyzes four moments that relate to the development of this concept, through a systematic review of the bibliography
