# Dissociations in the management of trapeciometacarpian osteoarthrosis: experience in hospital II level in Latin America.

The objective of this study is to record the outcomes of the different surgical interventions, performed in the same center of care, and to evaluate if there are significant differences between the intervention groups in terms of pain or functionality of thumb Methods: A descriptive, mixed-sectional study was conducted in a single follow-up center, of all patients with diagnosis of trapeziometcarpian arthrosis taken to surgery, in a period of 10 years (2011-2021).The results of the different surgical management (joint replacement, trapecyectomy, arthroplasty of interposition), evaluating the functionality during the follow-up Late POP (over 6 months), pre- and post-operative pain with the analogous visual pain scale (VAS), as well as the complications and re-intervention requirement of each group of patients. 28 interventions were included in 26 patients who met the inclusion criteria with the following distribution: 11 interventions in the joint replacement group, 12 in the trapecectomy group, 5 in the artroplasty group, where the differences in the preoperative pain status and surgical mobility were found
