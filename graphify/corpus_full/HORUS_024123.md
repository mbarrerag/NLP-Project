# Determination of sex by mastoid apophysis in a sample of Colombian population

ABSTRACT Objectives: To determine sex through mastoid apophysis by means of a descriptive study, in order to establish the main anatomical differences of mastoids in men and women. A review of the literature found in the different medical databases at national and international level was carried out and then 19 human skulls worked under the standard of quality of bone pieces of the anatomical amphitheater of the University of Applied and Environmental Sciences (U.D.C.A) previously dissected and with unknown sex and age of provenance of the indicated sample were evaluated. The measurements were taken in millimetres by a digital calibrator and were taken photographic record of the selected sample. Six (6) of the parameters established by Mazza 27 were evaluated in seventeen (17) human skulls, of which five were men and 12 women. The results obtained in the present study with that of Mazza et. al. have a high agreement, since with an interval of confidence of 95% it was established that the intervals of the mean are significant and applicable in the two populations.
