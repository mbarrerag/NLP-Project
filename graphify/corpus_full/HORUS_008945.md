# GREEN BONDS FOR DEVELOPING SUSTAINABLE INFRASTRUCTURES.

Short communication: In our contemporary world, where sustainability is gaining increasing attention, financial mechanisms designed to support the development of eco-friendly infrastructure systems play a pivotal role. One such mechanism is the green bond, in which proceeds are used only for financing or refinancing (total or partial) projects with positive environmental impacts. Given that, they are becoming increasingly important, particularly in regions committed to combating climate change. Thus, they are essential for accelerating sustainable infrastructure development and achieving the Sustainable Development Goals. While this market has witnessed significant growth in recent years, leading to an increase in both public and private issuers, there is a pressing need for more incentives and regulations. These should aim to broaden not only the base of investors but also that of infrastructure developers who view it as an ally in shaping financing strategies and assessing their environmental and societal impacts. Hence, capital markets should re-evaluate the costs associated with green bond issuance. This step will motivate issuers and foster the development of financial mechanisms derived from green bonds, such as hybrid debt, subordinated debt, and green mortgages. Such measures will allow for expanding the supply of green financing products. In the coming years, the investment world will no longer talk about sustainable finance or its strands, such as social or green finance, it will simply be finance because environmental and social components will be included by default.
