# FREE COLOMBIA. TRIBUNE OF ANTI-CONSERVER OPPOSITION FROM EXILE

The paper analyzes a specific case of opposition to the governments of Mariano Ospina Pérez and Laureano Gómez articulated around the newspaper Colombia Libre, published in Paris during 1950 and 1951 under the direction of Gerardo Molina. The work is structured around two complementary axes: 1. Characterizing the newspaper and rebuilding its networks of writers and collaborators inside and outside the country. 2. Analyzing the strategies and mechanisms to constitute a platform of anti-conservative opposition from French exile. It is argued that the publication is part of the European tradition of the anti-fascist press and under this prism reads and presents the Colombian reality in the international arena
