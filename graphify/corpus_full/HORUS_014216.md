# Evaluation of roof alternatives for the reduction of energy consumption in refrigeration in Housing in Santiago de Tolu.

The present study was carried out in order to develop strategies for the improvement of housing in Santiago de Tolú, taking as a baseline the thermal comfort standards of the homes of cultural interest with palm covers and Bahareque walls, which have been recognized by the population, and the comparison of 3 alternatives proposed in the literature focusing on the roofs. This in order to present to the population three alternatives to reduce the consumption of energy in cooling, contributing to the improvement of qualitative deficiencies of the buildings (Charid, 2020) with different economic scopes and avoiding the problems associated with the palm covers. This is expected to develop an alternative for the reduction of the consumption of energy in cooling through specific interventions in the housings of cultural interest that involve a consumption of energy in thermal comfort less than the current one
