# Impact of mobility on social capital, case study Green corridor of the seventh race

This work develops in an integral way the bidirectional interrelation between social capital in urban environments and the modal choices of transport of individuals, taking the case of study the area that will intervene the transformation project of the seventh race in Bogotá. Based on theoretical reflections on the notion of social capital expressed in the public transport journeys, the research proposes a methodology of application of a model of discreet choice of maximum verisimilitude with data from the Mobility Survey of Bogotá 2019. The results reveal not only the influence of the pre-existing social capital on the use of public transport, but also the potential change of social capital induced by new urban projects that affect the bidirectional interaction use between social capital in urban settings and individual's mobility choices, as an opportunity and a challenge to integrate them into future urban projects. .
