# TRAINING PROCESS OF RESEARCHERS IN THEIR DIFFERENT STAGES

Formation, process of shaping oneself. One is formed with interaction with others. To form is to interpret the world from its own uniqueness and interpret it. It requires the desire to be a singular creator, to sublimate and make academic contributions in various disciplinary and transdisciplinary fields, as well as in contemporary developments. Training researchers require developing competencies, skills and group knowledge that allow them to expand their trajectories in the field of specific knowledge. The management of training of researchers should encourage the development of disciplinary, transdisciplinary and interdisciplinary academic culture. It is necessary to develop a scientific and professional culture that agrees to identify with symbols specific to the area to which it belongs. One of the most complicated points of training is to become an author, to write experiences, ideas or representations specific to a scientific discipline. This work is developed mainly in four chapters: Chapter 1. Training models Chapter 2. Motivation of researchers. Chapter 3. Professional culture and training of teachers/researchers. Chapter 4. Considerations on academic writing, (to provide the reader with an overall vision of processes of training of researchers)
