# PLANNING AGROECHOLOGIC TRANSITION FOR FOOD AND NUTRITIONAL SECURITY IN CHIA MUSIC GUARANTEE

The current society faces strong challenges to guarantee Food and Nutritional Security (SAN) especially in rural and indigenous communities. This study builds the bases for an agroecological transition process in order to contribute to the guarantee of the SAN in families farmers of the Muisca indigenous reserve of Fonceta and Near Piedra? in chia-cundinamarca (colombia). The limitations and potentialities of the territorial context and the farming families were analyzed, to undertake a process towards the agroecological transition, which is conceived as a complex process of change from a conventional way of producing food to an alternative form of sustainable agriculture of agroecological base. A participatory methodology was proposed with mixed approach that identified categories of analysis from a framework of analysis for the SAN with agroecological approach. The work was carried out with 13 families of the indigenous reserve dedicated to family agriculture with a total of 45 people. 23% of the family predoms are planted with food crops, aromatic, medicinal and/or ritual plants and are located in general in the initial transition, although they present strengths in advanced stages
