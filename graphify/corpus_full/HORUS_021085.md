# Asbestos as carcinogenic material present in construction systems: a methodological approach to the eradication of asbestos in Colombia and its contextualization in the region with the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Medellin headquarters as a case study pilot

The national government has a period of five years (counted from the entry into force of the law, 2021) to form the so-called _x0093_National Commission for the Replacement of the Asbesto_x0094_, but in the absence of this, it is worrying that there is not yet a public policy of substitution of the asbestos installed without inspection regulation or compliance with the law and the development plan, thus leaving in evidence the little political will to give particular response to the problem of management and withdrawal of the asbestos.It is vital to understand that there are unsustainable materials, one of those materials is asbestos, therefore, recognizing, withdrawing and managing this material, presents in itself a work of deconstruction, which contributes to the sustainable construction of the habitat.In this sense, the withdrawal and management of this material at the global level is one of the major challenges in the modernity, and in this way, we consider to be involved in presenting the local and international advances that have been carried out for the removal and management of this material. to establish standards that are integrated into Colombian legislation, and thus contribute to the sustainable construction of the territory
