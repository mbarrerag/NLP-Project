# EXPLORATION OF THE Mesophotic ECOSYSTEMS (30-120 M) BY TECHNICAL CIRCUIT SEARCH (CCR-TRIMIX)

The mesophotic coral ecosystems (ECM) are little explored, which has aroused great interest in studying them. This has been given by an obvious impediment since they are below the limits of recreational SCUBA diving, but on the other hand they are too shallow for exploration with remote or underwater vehicles. As was expected, corals and other organisms in this area have recently been found to present symbiotic associations and unique adaptations. In this chapter we present the techniques and scopes of CCR closed circuit technical diving (for its acronym: Closed Circuit Rebreather) and air enriched with Helio (trimix) for research in the NDEs of various regions of the Colombian Caribbean: the island of San Andrés (SeaFlower Biosphere Reserve, archipelago of San Andrés Providencia and Santa Catalina), the National Park Corals of Depth and the coral banks adjacent to Barú Island, Bolívar
