# Overview of migrations in the heart of Africa: DYNAMICS AND MIGRATORY MANAGEMENT

This paper seeks to present a panoramic view of intra-African migrations with an emphasis on sub-Saharan migration, in order to help break the general view that African migrations are directed more outwards than inwards on the continent. Thus, a diagnosis of the migratory reality and the challenges for managing African migratory flows is presented. It is hoped to promote a more comprehensive understanding of African migratory mobility, with the possibility of fuelling debates on African migrations connected with the formation of black diasporas in the Americas in general and in the Latin American region in particular
