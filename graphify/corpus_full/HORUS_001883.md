# DEVELOPMENT OF A SYSTEM OF INFORMATION MANAGEMENT OF RESEARCH BASED ON CASE KNOWLEDGE REPLIES: RESEARCH AND EXTENSION OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY - MANIZED HEADQUARTERS

Knowledge has become one of the intangibles most appreciated by organizations because of its value for decision-making and the achievement of competitive advantage. In universities, it is necessary to have technologies that allow the storage, recovery and use of knowledge from research projects. This study presents the development of a system of information management of research processes implemented as a knowledge repository. The unified process was used as a methodology for software development. The developed system was applied in a case study at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Manizales Headquarters. The results show that the system allowed the management and monitoring of products derived from the execution of projects carried out by research groups. In addition, the system was tested for a MinCiencias call aimed at measuring and recognizing research groups
