# Association between muscular core activation, kinematic variables and career economy, in bottom athletes.

The race economy is a complex and multifactorial concept that reflects the integrated combination of a variety of metabolic, cardiorespiratory, biomechanical and neuromuscular characteristics specific to each athlete. In addition, the career economy is considered an important physiological measure for endurance athletes, especially in the bottom corridors. The objective of this research was to determine associations between the muscular activation of the core, the kinematics of the movement and the career economy expressed as the consumption of oxygen and energy expenditure necessary to maintain the race to a sub-threshold intensity. This work was carried out with background athletes in the city of Bogotá D.C. The study methodology was developed in two phases: a first phase corresponding to the characterization of the athletes by means of the body composition and ventilatory parameters calculated during an incremental effort test. In the second phase, biomechanical evaluation of the career and the measurement of the level of muscular activation of the core during a test of 20 minutes at constant load below the ventilatory threshold 2 (VT2).
