# TRAINING IN PERSONAL FINANCES BY A SERIES GAME: AHORRO VS. INVESTMENT IN THE FACULTY OF MINES- NATIONAL DECOLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The financial concept is associated with monetary resources, with money and availability of it, Finance is defined as the Art and the science of managing money, and the economically active people are the ones who collect money and spend it or invest it, (Gitman and Zutter, 2007) cited by López, Juan; (2016). Personal finances are the ability of an individual or family group that is available to maneuver in the financial sector and the real sector. Bodie & Merton (2003) express how finance studies the way monetary resources are allocated through time and consumer, because the family economy maintains close links with individuals who make up the family unit. (Velecela et al., 2017) Personal financial planning is a set of activities required to define objectives, establish action plans and meet financial needs of individuals or family groups, Rojas, Maya and Valencia, (2017)
