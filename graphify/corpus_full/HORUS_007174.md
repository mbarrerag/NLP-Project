# Technical and regulatory proposal for the diffusion of large-scale energy storage with batteries in Colombia

This thesis carries out a comprehensive analysis of the state of art at the international level related to the implementation or diffusion of energy storage systems using large-scale batteries, as well as a diagnosis of the regulatory framework in Colombia, assessing the economic viability of different scenarios. The results indicate that, although these systems may improve the stability and efficiency of the network, their adoption is limited by the lack of an adequate regulatory framework and by the absence of financial incentives that reduce costs and improve incomes. The most promising scenario considers energy arbitration together with the implementation of incentives, which can transform the economic viability of these projects. However, it is concluded that a flexible regulatory framework is essential to enable these systems to participate in multiple markets with adequate remuneration for their services. Finally, criteria are proposed to guide the adoption of this technology in Colombia, including the creation of financial incentives, the development of a favourable regulatory environment, and the diversification of business models associated with energy storage .
