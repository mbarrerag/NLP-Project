# Parallel trials of court proceedings by the media

This document aims to determine the effects and tensions of Mass Media in relation to the use of parallel or compulsory judgments in practice. Interferences, difficulties and theoretical aspects were analyzed that allow to discern in which cases judicial information can create changes in public opinion and create conviction to the judge. The determination of fundamental rights violated in the judicial process due to Mass Media generating parallel trials was relevant. It was analyzed how there is a set agenda by the media, what differences exist in their denominations and their applications in history. It was explained how in other latitudes the concept is handled, and elements such as: the real truth, procedural truth, the publicity of judicial proceedings through social networks and the focus on victims and aggressors make a presence in a framework where there is an epidemiology of crime. . The purpose is to determine the effects and tensions of the mass media in the practice or oblique judgments
