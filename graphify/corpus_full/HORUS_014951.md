# Optimization of a transport network applied to the bulk cement sector in Colombia.

This thesis aims to design a model that optimizes the transportation network of cement in bulk in Colombia. The study, evaluation, modeling and constant optimization of transport networks allow companies to have significant savings in identifying optimal origins-destinations guaranteeing high levels of service in quantity and delivery time. For the development of this thesis, the data of operation of a Colombian freight transport company that provides its services to a recognized cement producing company at the national level is used as a source of information. The results start from the descriptive analysis of demand, the construction of the kilometers and cost matrices, the mathematical modelling of the problem and the estimation of the parameter load transport cost index (ICTC) to make use of academic software in the optimization exercise. Subsequently, the validation of the model with respect to the reality of the last 10 months is carried out, reaching an adjustment of 95%. It is modeled and optimized the following 3 years of operation achieving a potential saving of close to 5% allowing leverage of key business decisions regarding the entry and exit of vehicles taking into account the dimensions necessary to meet the demand
