# ECOSYSTEM SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE OCEAN

Today it is very common to talk about ecosystem services (ES), a concept in which, from the properties of ecosystems, the relationship between human beings and nature is expressed. SEs are defined as the benefits that human beings obtain from ecosystems, that is, it is a positive perception between nature and culture. Perception can also be negative, and then it is spoken of as services, that is, harmful relationships perceived from ecosystems. An example of service is found in coastal wetlands, where there are abundant insect populations, some of which can be vectors of diseases, such as malaria, which can eventually cause death. Regardless of whether perception is positive or negative, tacit or explicit, such perception has justified for many centuries the making of many decisions and human intervention in different ecosystems.
