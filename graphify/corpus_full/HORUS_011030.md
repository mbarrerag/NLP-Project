# Management of lumbar spondylolisthesis from osteopathy. A bibliometric review.

Although academic literature has demonstrated the effectiveness of osteopathic management of spondylolisthesis, the predominant paradigm in medical practice continues to minimize its effectiveness in favor of surgery. This research aims to analyze the development of the scientific field that studies the conservative treatment of spondylolisthesis, identifying the structure of the area and establishing the research streams. To accomplish the purpose, a bibliometric analysis was applied to 441 documents indexed in the Scopus database that relate directly to the object of study. The findings allow to conclude that, in the past, the research focused on highlighting the benefits of surgery with respect to the conservative treatment of spondylolisthesis, however, since the 2010s, the researches that demonstrate the benefits of non-surgical treatment of spondylolisthesis have grown significantly, in addition, the scientific production has been concentrated in Anglo-speaking countries such as the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia, presenting a low production in Latin America.
