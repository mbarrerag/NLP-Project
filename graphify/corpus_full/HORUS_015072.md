# Process of settlement and production of space around mining activities in the urban areas of Segovia and Remedios, Antioquia.

The form of a city is the physical reflection of what happens in it, its economy, its culture, its relations as society and the other relations that shape the environment in which they are inhabited. Interpreting the urban form involves understanding the process that generated the city product and the functional conditions that shaped the space in which they inhabit. The aim of this research is to identify the spatial production process that occurs through the urban function and the morphological product that results from these processes around the mining activity in the municipalities of Remedios and Segovia. The functional processes were identified through the review of historical and scientific literature, of the press and statistical data, which allowed to give historical traceability to the most important events that occurred in these two municipalities since their foundation, which were based on the statistical quantitative data generation.
