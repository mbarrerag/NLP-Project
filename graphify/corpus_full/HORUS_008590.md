# Genesis and evolution of pegmatites in the Colombian East through mineral chemistry

The study of the magma generator, its degree of magmatic fractionation and its relationship with the presence of minerals carrying Nb-Ta (strategic minerals) was carried out in the department of Guainía. For this purpose, 31 rock samples were studied and analyses were carried out with electronic microsounds of EPM in apatito, biotita, garnet, microclin, muscovita and turmaline. The results obtained from biotita, granate and turmaline suggest that these rocks were generated from a peraluminine magma rich in Fe. In addition, the variation of the Ba content in microcline, Na in muscovita, Fe-Mg in biotita, and Mn-Sr in the apatito allowed the proposal of a magnetic evolutionary line for these rocks. Nb-Ta carrier samples were present in the most fractionated rocks, where turmaline and granate were found, and therefore proposed as possible indicators of strategic minerals
