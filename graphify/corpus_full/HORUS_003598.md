# CONCENTRATION OF DESIGNATIONS: COLORIMETRY

The color intensity is a property of the solutions proportional to the quantity of solute. This fact is the principle of the colorimetric methods in which it is possible to know the amount of solute in a solution by comparison of its color with that of a series of solutions containing the same solute in varying and known quantities. The colored solutions absorb light in the visible region of the electromagnetic spectrum and, quantitatively, the intensity of light is measured in spectrophotometers. This property allows to determine the concentration of a solute colored in dissolution, since the absorbed light is directly proportional to the intensity of color in a certain range of concentrations that must be determined experimentally. This methodology is used at an industrial level, in research and also daily in the clinical laboratory
