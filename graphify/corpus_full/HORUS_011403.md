# THE CLIMATE OF STOPS IN COLOMBIA

The climatic conditions of continental ecosystems are determined by their specific location on the planet (latitude, altitude, distance to the oceans, seasons and atmospheric and ocean circulations), as well as their exposure to wind masses. In the neotropics, the mooring ecosystems are subject to complex weather conditions, due mainly to their altitude position (towards the peaks of the Andean mountains), which determines their condition of cold and humid climate and frequent presence of fog, and their exposure to wind masses, which generates changes in precipitation, temperature and relative humidity of the air, determining in part the water supply of these ecosystems. To make known the weather conditions in the paramo ecosystems in Colombia, in this chapter the results of automatic and detailed measurements of the weather variables made in each paramo and their statistical analysis, thus determining the general weather trends in the seven (7) páreas in Colombia included, by characterization of their spatial-temporal behavior.
