# Synthesis and characterization of pyridin coumarin derivatives and their application in the detection of a metallic ion

Three new hybrids of coumarin pyridines with a donor system • acceptor were synthesized in a series of 5 steps, without metal catalysts, nor special experimental conditions. These compounds were characterized by melting point, IR-TF, RMN 1H, APT and 13C, UV-Vis spectroscopy and fluorescence. In addition, mass spectrometry. Photophysical characteristics of the pyridin coumarin derivatives were studied showing that they were strong emitters of blue light when excited to a 300 nm μm reaching quantum yields between 10 and 12 %, these were evaluated against various metallic ions, showing a high selectivity by Al3+
