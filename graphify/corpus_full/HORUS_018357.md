# PHARMACOLOGICAL MANNEE OF Odontogenic INFECTION

Odontogenic infection is one of the main reasons for dental consultation. In this regard, it is important to understand the microbiological elements that are part of the different infections that may occur in the oral cavity, with the aim of using the ideal antibiotic agent. Not all dental infections require antibiotic therapy and dental professionals should be able to determine clinically and with knowledge in the area of pharmacology, which cases demand the prescription of these drugs. Thus, the understanding of the antibiotic groups available, the pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic characteristics, doses, adverse effects, interactions that may occur, among others, constitute a central axis for the dentist and the dentist specialist. Finally, it is essential to know some systemic conditions that require special care when prescribing antimicrobial agents such as patients with some cardiac pathologies, in whom antibiotic prophylaxis is necessary before undergoing an invasive dental procedure, polymedicated patients, patients with diabetes, among others
