# THE ERA OF THE OMICO IN ENDOCRINOLOGICAL: A GREAT OPPORTUNITY FOR THE ADVANCE OF KNOWLEDGE IN THE PATOLOGICAL RUTS OF ENDOCRIMINAL DISEASES

Omic sciences are scientific disciplines that allow the large-scale study of biological molecules such as genes, proteins and metabolites (1), and these disciplines include genomics, proteomics and metabolomics, among others (1). Analyses of data obtained through omic sciences can lead to a better understanding of biological mechanisms underlying different diseases, better classification of diseases and the identification of new therapeutic objectives
