# SCIENCE SIGNATURE: THE WAY OF TWO SCIENTIFIC STAFF

The book is a transmedia commitment to scientific dissemination from the profiles of two great scientists of Caldas. It is sought through a journalistic exercise, with visual and multimedia synthesis to involve the reader with the stories that support two outstanding scientists of the department in the activities that from their field of action promoted the social transformation and social appropriation of knowledge. The stories narrated, have been documented from a chronological, reflective and sensitive perspective that allows to approximate the life and work that from science has managed to transform lives and regions
