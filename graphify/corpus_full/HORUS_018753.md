# Operational planning of smart micronets considering intraday markets.

Sustainable development promotes the adoption of smart micro-grids. However, economic interests stimulate the increase of income, which can result in non-optimal situations, such as non-supply of demand, the formation of monopolies and the formation of essential agents to supply demand. In this context, research analyzes an intelligent micro-network that negotiates energy commitments with intraday markets and binding dispatch. Likewise, in this model, penalties are quantified for the uncertainty of renewables with intraday markets. In addition, the profits associated with the management of the distributed generation, loading and unloading of energy storage systems, an intraday exchange station of batteries and residential electric vehicles are estimated. This model introduces the uncertainty in the planning problem in an intelligent micro-network related to (1) renewable generation, (2) demand forecast, (3) market price variations, (4) time planning of electric vehicle travel and (5) demand forecast of batteries in an electrical vehicle station.
