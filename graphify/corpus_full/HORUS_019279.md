# TEACHING OF OPERATION IN BIOREACTORS AND THE SCALE OF BIOPROCESS: MULTIDISCIPLINARY TRAINING IN A THEORICAL-Practical COURSE

Biotechnology is an emerging area and priority for developing countries. Bioreactors are a fundamental team and become a key element for the development and innovation of bioprocesses. Knowledge of these teams is fundamental to achieving the growth and development of microorganisms, animal and plant cells, and including micropropagation of plants. In a joint initiative by the authors of this article and with the support of the respective academic institutions, since 2012 the International Course on Bioprocesses and Training in Bioreactors Operations has been carried out seven times. The aim of the course is to offer the scientific community of Ibero-America theoretical - practical training in bioprocesses. This course has been developed for over 1400 people through conferences and 216 people with practical skills in the laboratory. The course has allowed to foster a network of contacts that operate in consulting and in the mobility of people. In addition, one of the outstanding products is the integration of a book, which contributes as a reference work on the subject
