# THE BOGOTAN OFFICER A LOOK AT HIS HEALTH AND WORK CONDITIONS.

The likelihood of hazards to the ex officio recycler is greater in developing countries, as this activity involves the direct manual handling of recyclable materials, exposing them to different risk agents (physical, chemical, biological, physical, mental, public load) (Cointreau, 2006, p. 11) without sufficient control or individual protection. Those who survive from this work are in poverty condition, so they decide to engage in the activity by not having another accessible employment opportunity (ILO Programme on Safety and Health at Work and the Environment., 2010, p. 7), which guarantees them a better quality of life
