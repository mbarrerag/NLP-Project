# ACTUAL PSYCOMETRY CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE MEASUREMENT OF MENT HEALTH AND WELFARE PROCESS

This conference presents concisely the current picture of advances in psychological measurement of mental health. On the one hand, it analyses the methodological contribution from the main developments of psychometric theories, such as the Theory of Response to Item (TRI), factorial analysis and measuring instruments. The contributions are noted, emphasizing the need to perform: a. Factorial analysis appropriate to the type of variables studied; b. Factorial invariance studies; c. reports of differential functioning of items (dif); d. Analysis of information with models such as intensive longitudinal ones. In relation to the instruments, the considerations on transcultural adaptation are highlighted, some of the current constructs being addressed and the standards for the design, use and selection of instruments in the field of mental health, similar to those presented in the Cosmi project
