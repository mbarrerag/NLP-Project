# Trends in the scientific evidence of economic assessments and non-transmissible diseases: a biblymonetary analysis

The aim of this study is to analyze the scientific production of economic assessments of NCDs and to identify less explored research areas. A search of literature was carried out on Web of Science (WoS), PubMed and Scopus. R/Bibliometrix and VosViewer were used for data analysis. The results revealed an increase in scientific production over time. The journal Value in Health was highlighted as the one that has been produced most and the authors Mark Lamotte and Lieven Annemans were the ones that published the most. The main information nodes were related to diabetes in NCDs and with disease and cost-effectiveness burden in economic assessments. In conclusion, scientific production related to NCD economic assessments is focused predominantly on the analysis of direct costs associated with these diseases and subanalysis of the Global Disease Burden Studies
