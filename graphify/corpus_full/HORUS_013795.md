# Sevillana

This is a research-creation project that documents and investigates through photography the contamination of the water of the Tunjuelo River, in its low basin, south of Bogotá, in the Tunjuelito Location, between the Barrio la Sevillana and Isla del Sol, where, the degradation of the quality of the river water, has been affected, mainly, by the industrialization, the management of waste water, plastic and textile waste, as well as the residues of the automotive industry of this sector
