# Strengthening mango post-harvest processes in vulnerable production communities in the department of Cesar

The diseases by fungi in post-harvest cause damage in fruits and high control cost, raising the costs of production and limited export of fruits (Weir et al., 2012). The approach to this problem is an opportunity to generate new knowledge articulating the academy and the productive sector (Vulnerable peasant communities of Cesar) surrounding the challenge of bioprospecting and bioproducts of the La Paz Headquarters of UNAL, which is articulated with the focal points prioritized by the department, in the strengthening of the human capital with high level of education for the development of Cité, the prioritization of the lines of natural sciences, agro-pequaria and green biotechnology, with topics such as food security, sustainable production, systems of crops more friendly with the environment, innovative, competitive for the management of diseases. In the project, an alternative solution to the problem of post-harvest management of the Keitt variety to farmers and communities of African descent of the municipalities of El Copey and Curumane in the Cesar is proposed to be used as a solution for the post-harvest of the production of the products, based on the post-harves. essential oils, in the elaboration of bioinput formulations from plant species that grow on their farms, in the application and management of the bioinsumption prototype, and also participate actively in the research with all their practical experience in the postharvest management of mango, as well as their cultural and traditional knowledge that contributes to the integral solution of the problem and that generates a positive impact for the communities
