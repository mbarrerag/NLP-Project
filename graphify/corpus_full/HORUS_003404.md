# Decreased effect of cadmium (Cd) on cocoa seedlings (Theobroma cacao L.) with the application of Bacillus subtilis DMS18595

The study aims to analyze the effect of cadmium (Cd) on cocoa seedlings (Theobroma cacao L.) by using the strain of Bacillus subtilis DMS 18695. The results highlight the resistance of Bacillus subtilis to Cd in concentrations of 2, 5 and 10 ppm, with growth comparable to the control group. At 2 ppm Cd, the bacteria undergo an adaptation phase with an initial decrease in growth. Bacillus subtilis accumulates cadmium around their cell wall, especially 5 and 10 ppm, evidenced by dense clusters of cadmium at intracellular level (TEM), suggesting a strategy of defense against the Cd. In conclusion, Bacillus subtilis shows resistance to Cd in several concentrations, with capacity of accumulation around its cell wall and maintenance of non-toxic internal levels. The presence of B. subtilis positively impacts on the growth and development of cocoa seedling in presence of Cd, stimulating plant growth and promoting greater height, diameter and concentration of chlorophyll (the emphasis of non-toxic factors). evidenced by dense clusters of cadmium at the intracellular level (TEM), suggesting a defense strategy against Cd.
