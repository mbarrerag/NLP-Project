# The measurement to be carried out in fixed assets.

This paper analyses the choices and implications of accounting policy for the measurement of fixed assets of home public service companies (PSBs) listed on the Colombian Stock Exchange in their financial statements, for the first time adoption of International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), as well as in subsequent measurement. A conceptual perspective is adopted on the dual role of accounting in decision-making and control. An exploratory, analytical and descriptive methodology analyses the financial information and accounting policies of the 18 DPS quoted in Colombia for the years 2013 to 2015. The results show that at the time of adoption for the first time, DSUs used the Reasonable Value for several classes of fixed assets, with important implications for their financial situation; however, in subsequent measurement, cost measurement prevails. The study of accounting policies implemented by these companies is relevant to investors, regulators, supervisors, managers, academics and practitioners
