# HYDRATIATION IN PREVENTION OF SPORTLES

The practice of physical activity and sport forms part of healthy living habits which contribute to improving the quality of life of people, promote health and prevent disease through strengthening the body, to strengthen the cardiovascular system, and thus favour the oxygenation of cells, weight control and, with this, avoid diseases such as arterial hypertension, diabetes and coronary disease
