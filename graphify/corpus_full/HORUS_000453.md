# Optimization of sales forecasts in Disilores: evaluation and selection of time series models, machine learning and advanced techniques

Dislicores is a Colombian company leader in the distribution and commercialization of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, being its main portfolio the wines (tintos, whites, rosé and foams), liqueurs (whisky, gin, rums and tequilas), beers and drinks without alcohol. In addition, it complements its supply with foods such as passive, sweet and matured food, strengthening and complementing its proposal for mass consumption. The aim of this study is to improve the accuracy of demand forecasts, by evaluating and comparing advanced models of prediction. To this end, six models were implemented: Average Mobile Simple, Lineal Regression, Holt-Winters, MSTL with Polynomic Regression, XGBoost and LightGBM, using historical data from the period 2020-2024.
