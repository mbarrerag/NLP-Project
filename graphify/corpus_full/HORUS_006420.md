# A correspondence between racks and g-digroups

The aim of this paper is to establish a correspondence between the categories given by the structures of racks and g-digroups, by defining three functors, denoted similarly to those in the case of rack-groups, Conj:g-dig→Rack, As:Rack→g-dig, and Inn:Rack→g-dig. Additionally, we establish certain properties of these functors and prove, as in the case of rack-groups, that the functor As is left adjoint to the functor Conj
