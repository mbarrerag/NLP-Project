# OVERVIEW OF FOLLOW-UP TO ACQUIRED COMPETENCES

The process of developing and learning medical skills and knowledge involves a complex cog. From the first days in medical school, as well as during the daily exercise of general medicine, the training of a specialist and even the daily activity of a subspecialist, that cog must include the imperative element of continuous education, which will allow not only to meet the certification requirements necessary for all health professionals, but also to provide safe and quality care to patients
