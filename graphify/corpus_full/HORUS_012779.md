# Evidence-based clinical standard: diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of adult patient with enterocutaneous or enteroatmospheric intestinal fistula at the National University Hospital of Colombia

This book represents the Clinical Standard Based on Evidence (ECBE) related to the diagnosis and treatment of the patient with intestinal fistulas. ECBE is part of a process of standardization of health care, taking into account the best evidence, available resources and interdisciplinarity, with the aim of generating a comprehensive approach that improves the outcomes of patients and optimizes the use of resources at the hospital level. It recognizes the importance of the standardization of care of patients with this condition, due to its importance for the institution and for the comprehensive care of the patient with this condition.-- Contravest
