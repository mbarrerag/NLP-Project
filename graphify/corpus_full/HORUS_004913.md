# BIT A BIT: DIGITAL DEVELOPMENTS IN COLOMBIA NATIONAL UNIVERSITY

In an increasingly interconnected and digital world, the educational institutions face the constant challenge of adapting and evolving to continue being relevant and effective in their mission to form future generations. Conscious of this reality, the National University of Colombia has undertaken a path of digital transformation that not only seeks to integrate technology into its academic and administrative processes, but also to foster a culture of innovation and collaboration that transcends the territories of the country.This book is a testimony to that collective effort, the stories that are expressed are the reflection of a shared commitment by teachers, students, administrative and management who show us a chronic of the digital evolution of our university told through the voices of those who have been protagonists and witnesses of this change.Throughout this book, we explore how the cultural and digital transformation of the National University of Colombia, from the implementation of information systems and academic management to the creation of virtual and hybrid learning spaces, as well as the strengthening of the management of research and technological innovation. others to join this adventure of transformation and growth
