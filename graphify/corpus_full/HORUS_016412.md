# Evaluation of tripanocid activity of plant extracts of the genus Piper in Trypanosoma cruzi cultures.

The Chagas disease is one of the most impacting in Latin America, caused by the para parasite Trypanosoma cruzi. Currently there are few medicines available for the treatment of this disease, which are limited in use due to the difficulties that arise in its administration and the genotoxic effects they cause. This also shows the need to seek new therapeutic alternatives for the treatment of Chagas disease, for which plants continue to be an affordable first-line option in endemic areas. In this sense, this study contributes to the search for substances with antiparasititis potential for T. crui from Colombian species belonging to the genus Piper. The methodology included the selection of extracts from the genus Piper with antiparasite effect on T. crossi epimastitis, which present low levels of toxicity in Vero cells.
