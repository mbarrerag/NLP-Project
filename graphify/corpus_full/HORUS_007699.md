# DensiPowder - Innovative device for determining particle density of granular material

The particle density or apparent density is a property of granular solids arranged in bulk loads that takes into account the volume occupied by particles excluding the volume between them. This property is of particular importance in the chemical industry, the food and pharmaceutical industry and in general industry that works with granular solids as it defines the properties of flow, compactability and reaction of particles, among others. The traditional and most accepted technology makes use of mercury as a picnometric liquid. Mercury is highly restricted impact in Colombia [1] and in most countries of the world due to its high potential for pollution and affectation to living beings. Its use requires specialized assembly, causing the availability for the performance of this test to be highly limited. In some cases water or paraffin to replace mercury dust is used as a low-voluntile substance, but its use is restricted to low-porous materials and which do not absorb these substances. Currently there is no adequate measurement alternative to multiple industrial and academic sectors. [2] In response to this need is posed the implementation of an innovative method of particle density using fine powder as a substance.
