# Being a Mother and Father during a Pandemic: Imaginaries of School Education

The family and the school historically have demonstrated a series of tensions and research interests in various disciplines, however, the scenario of the years 2020 and 2021 reinforced all the questions and problems contemplated up to that moment, giving way to new logics of relationship between the two institutions. Hence, this research responds to the general objective of understanding the senses, meanings and motivations for action present in the mothers and fathers of the Préspero College IED Pinzón amid the effects derived from the pandemic generated by the SARS-CoV virus 2, Covid-19. To reach this understanding it is adopted from the methodological paradigm hermeneutic interpretive with qualitative approach, starting from the approach to the social imaginarys of five mothers and two parents, making use of the in-depth interview from the initial recognition of their own school experiences to the transformations, practices and effects lived between them regarding the school education of their children and daughters under the compulsory social isolation. . wo fathers, making use of the in-depth interview from the initial recognition of their own school experiences until they are able to read the transformations, practices and effects live concerning the school education of their sons and daughters under the framework of compulsory social isolation
