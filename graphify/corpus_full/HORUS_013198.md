# A Panel Model Application - VAR in Continuous Time in Economics

This technique allows the estimation and interpretation of models regardless of the interval in which the observations are taken and allows analysis to be made in any period of time. It is illustrated by a VAR Panel in continuous time of the Monetary Mass (M1) and the International Reserves (IR) of two Pacific Alliance countries: Colombia and Chile, using as an exogenous variable common to the international price of oil (WTI). . The methodology for estimating continuous time Panel VAR (PVAR) models is presented.
