# Hybrid model of network architecture and security highly available for data channel, engineering faculty (Bta).

The hybrid network architectures allow to play a better role in the distribution of resources and in a very important way improve efficiency and robustness of the data networks helped to minimize both connectivity failures and isolate security incidents either of type DDoS, TCP/IP, remote access, etc. For the case of study, the object was to identify critical failures in the services that the university community uses within the LAN network through servers responsible for the distribution and storage of those services. Another point evaluated was the verification of the networking and computational infrastructure, taking the security as another element that under certain peculiarities as traffic policies, users and directionation within the engineering faculty were added to the identification of the incidences on certain assets of the information. In the first place, the current protocols in the distribution of traffic and availability of the equipments to non-expected failures, was identified the service V’s performance and was better. In the second place, we identified a solution that minimized the number of failures in the active equipment, the current state of the engineering network and as a plus the improvement of the connectivity between the different network devices. The communication method was used to determine that between nodes of different segments at the ping connectivity level, the operation and response obtained was between 2 and 5msec between communication devices such as Switches and routers, in the communication channel of the engineering faculty (headquarters of Bogotá), reflecting that the high availability protocol HSRP contributed to the best results expected for the model designed
