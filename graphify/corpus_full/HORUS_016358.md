# STUDY OF SOLLING AND RADIATION IN DIFFERENT GEOGRAPHICAL AREAS OF THE VALLE DE ABURRA

The study is presented to approximate the climatic phenomena of sunning and radiation in urban area of four municipalities of the Valle de Aburrá, Colombia, the relevance of the study is that the conditions of the topographic relief of the Valle de Aburrá constitute a determining variable when it comes to understanding the differentiated climate behavior in the different thermal floors of the place. Based on the analysis of data from the network of sensors of the early warning system of the Valle de Abura - SIATA- in 9 blocks located in the hillside and plain, the modelling of the apples was carried out and a series of computational simulations was generated in the application Climate StudioTM, whose data released, by means of an inductive analytical method, allow to know the microclimate found in the Valley. It was found that the solar radiation in the roads is reduced between 23% and 55%, according to its orientation and arborization. The municipalities south of the Valley of Aburrá have a better performance in the attenuation of the radiation and the facades to the west presents differences of solar radiation between different places of study.
