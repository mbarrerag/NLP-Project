# Geographical adaptations of the modern house in Colombia Four case studies on the coast, the valley, the mountains and the highlands

Abstract An analysis is presented on the relationships between the domestic architecture built during the decades of I950 and I960 and the geographical characteristics of Colombia. The article exposes, through the works, the organic link between the territory -e/ topos- and the different architectural types that emerged in accordance with it. In particular, the cases of the firms Obregón-Valenzuela and Borrero-Zamorano-Giovanelli are studied, and of the architects Gutiérrez-Arango-Sanint, who conceived houses on the coast, the valley, the mountainand the altiplano. The reflection revolves around the variations and invariants of the building types; that is, the structure they share and what they differ in. For that, first a description of the geography in which they settle is presented. Then the way in which each of the houses establishes a relationship with the site is characterized. It is configured, then, a contribution to the reflection on the Moderna architecture of the XX century in Colombia, with a look at the topological dimension of the house
