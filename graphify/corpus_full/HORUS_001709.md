# Comprehensive strategies for Fintech type Lending in the Coffee Shaft: Addressing Scalability Challenges

This research aims to propose comprehensive strategic approaches that allow Fintech type Lending Feveragellary Colombian Coffee Shade (Caldas, Quindío and Risaralda) to scale in a sustainable way while maintaining controlled levels of credit risk. To achieve this, a mixed methodology was adopted that integrated surveys aimed at commercial establishments and interviews with founders of the Fintech. Both data sets were codified through a unified system of categories, which allowed the comparative analysis of perceptions between both parties through cross tables. The results revealed significant differences between Fintech and commercial establishments in the perception of credit value and risk. For commercial establishments, the main incentives to adopt digital credits are speed and ease in approval, while Fintech highlight the diversification of financial products and the improvement of the user’s experience as key elements. Regarding the credit risk, the businesses also identify the over-indebtation and the defaults as their major concerns, while Fintech companies focus on the monitoring of changes in the user’s information.
