# GUARANTEE AS A MOTOR FOR THE FINANCIAL INCLUSION OF THE PEOPLE'S AND COMMUNITY ECONOMICS

According to the Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC), financial inclusion consists in providing access to financial services to households and MiPymes that do not have them, as well as in optimizing the use of the financial system by those who already participate in it. It is conceived in three dimensions: (i) access, which is related to the supply of financial services that enable it; (ii) use, which relates to the use of a certain financial service; and (iii) quality, which is linked to the efficiency of the service and its adequacy to the needs of the beneficiary of the financial product.2 Under these parameters, as will be seen below, as we advance in the financial inclusion of largely excluded sectors of the economy, such as the popular and community economy, we will effectively achieve the closing of historical gaps in our country
