# DESIGN AND MANUFACTURE OF A LIGHT-SHIFT EQUINE OF THREE RODICAL BAND

In multiple materials finishing activities, the sanding process is usually used, such as to match reliefs, prepare a material before a treatment, or polish a piece. To not perform this task manually, different types of sanding machines are used. Among them the sander of band is highlighted, this type of machines is static so it depends on the user to make the movements of the part or material to polish where it is required; moreover, this type of machines may have different shapes, quantity of rollers and sizes, depending on the use that it is intended to give. With the need to implement this process in the Laboratory of Welding of the National University of Colombia Headquarters Medellín, is proposed the project of designing and manufacturing an electric strip sander, that meets the needs of the laboratory, a design methodology was used that allowed to carry out the different phases of conceptual design, achieving the specifications of the equipment, generating design alternatives and selecting the best alternative solution, that led to the manufacture of the machine; the whole manufacturing and assembly process was carried out in the University of Soldadura and Processes de Manuilla is finally used
