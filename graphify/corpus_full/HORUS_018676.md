# PARTNERSHIP OF WORKERS IN FACILITIES OF COLMOTORS - ASSOCRECOL: A CASE OF FIGHT FOR THE RIGHT TO HEALTH AT WORK IN COLOMBIA.

At the beginning of the 1990s Colombia initiates a series of transformations, in the context of economic reforms and the limitation of social action of the States, promoted by the international financial organizations, that touched on various areas between labor and that of the social security, reforms that have hitherto prevailed in the country. Through Law 50 of 1990 in Colombia has advanced a labor reform that led to transform the relations capital-work from generating a process of flexibility and labor deregulation. And through Law 100 of 1993 a State policy on health has been established that has the basis of the individual insurance to a market of services of care to the disease of common and labor.In Colombia these labor and social security reforms, which included the component of labor risks, established a framework of collective social action of struggle and exigibility for the right to health and the right to health in the workplace, has guaranteed them the case of the SSP. The methodology for the case study included a review of secondary sources and grey literature, obtained mainly from web pages and press articles and obtained information from primary sources through interviews with former ASOTRECOL workers and the medical-legal documentation of their cases, provided by them. The document presents a description of the business context of Colmotores and the working conditions it generated and its effects on the health of workers, which shaped the framework of action for the generation of the organizational process of ASOTRECOL that group sick workers from that company, the way ASOTRECOL emerged and the dynamics of collective action it undertook, and then presents an analysis seeking to account for several of the questions and topics proposed in the analysis of the MSP project
