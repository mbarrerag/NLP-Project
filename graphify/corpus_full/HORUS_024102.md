# Central American Countries and Colombia: History, Relations and Disagreements with the Greater Caribbean

This book is an interdisciplinary and international initiative that seeks to contribute to the complex process of understanding the past, present and future of a region that we could call the Central American Caribbean and, from there, seek to explore the relationship of that space with Colombia and the rest of the Caribbean. This effort is part of the academic work that in recent decades has focused on the Caribbean as a region, as subject and object of study. In this sense, different continental countries with coasts in the Caribbean Sea have shown their interest in studying and dimensioning their connections with the rest of this geographical and historical space, while academic and political actors recognize these continental and Caribbean spaces from island territories. This situation has benefited the consolidation of the Caribbean as a large region that shares a geography, a similar culture and a common past that provide it with a multiple and diverse identity. And the Caribbean itself has constituted a place that brings together research and encourages researchers that explore, build and destroy it every day. That is why this book is about the Caribbean, but it also speaks to us of the multiple caribs that are created in everyday life
