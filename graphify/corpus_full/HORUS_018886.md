# Optimum control of glucose in blood by means of continuous insulinin infuse

This study presents the problem of the insulin supply regimen in a diabetic patient as an optimal control problem, so that no overdose or failure of the hormone medicinal product is generated under different feeding styles.The interaction between glucose and insulin is modelled as a nonlinear system of ordinary differential equations.The existence and overall uniqueness of the solution of that system is demonstrated, as is the optimal control.The optimal control problem is solved directly using the sequential quadratic programming method.The numerical results suggest establishing, according to the patient’s feeding style, the level of glucose concentrationprescribed to be maintained during the day.The analytical and numerical study of this proposal is expected to be of assistance in future developments in insulin pumps
