# REAVOID PEACE AGREEMENT WITH FARC-EP. PROPOSALS FOR PLANNING AND PUBLIC POLICY

The book presents concrete proposals for planning and public policy to contribute to the debt with the implementation of the peace agreement signed between the Colombian State and the FARC-EP guerrillas being settled and certain conditions are established for the deployment of the transformative power contained in the Agreement. The book is composed of four six chapters. The first chapter, Guidelines for the update of the Framework Plan for Implementation of the PMI, is composed of a proposal of guidelines, parameters and structural aspects that should be taken into account for a process of updating the PMI Implementation Framework Plan, both at the strategic level of public policies and in its financing plan. The second chapter, Guidelines for the formulation and inclusion of a specific chapter of peace in the National Development Plan, presents a technical, financial and monitoring proposal to advance the construction of a specific chapter of the implementation of the Agreement (specific chapter of peace) in the National Development Plan for the period 2022-2026, which serves as a reference for the plans of the subsequent governments. Chapters 5 and 6 present proposals on cross-cutting ethnic and gender approaches. The six issues studied throughout the book have a major importance and can be considered strategic - albeit not exhaustive - in order to revive the Peace Agreement in order to bring it back to its full implementation and thus to advance the process of building a stable and lasting peace. It is a question of constituting, on the basis of concrete proposals, the political, planning and budgeting bases required to implement the AFP in a comprehensive manner. This is in a context in which the restorative and transformational horizons defended by the participatory, territorial, gender and ethnic approaches of the Agreement are revindication
