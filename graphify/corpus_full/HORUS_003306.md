# AGRO-PECUARY SYSTEMS FOR THE PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION OF RURAL POPULATORS AND THE CONSTRUCTION OF TERRITORIAL PEACE IN COLOMBIA. CASE STUDY: PORT VENUS CORREGATION

This chapter is one of the cases of research on productive inclusion and territorial peace for rural development, carried out in Puerto Venus, co-regulation of the municipality of Nariño, located between the mountains of the east of Antioquia, 31 kilometers from the municipal head, whose time to travel is three hours; the distance to Medellín, capital of the Department of Antioquia, are 171 kilometers, and the time of travel is seven to eight hours; it was scenarios of the armed conflict in the period 1997-2005, so the Government of Colombia and the United Nations Organization (UN) recognized the necessary productive elements for the implementation of the productive elements with lemon Tahiti (Citrus x latifolia), avocado (Persea Americana), cocoa (Theobroma cocoa), cedar (Cedrela odorata) and walnut (Cordia alliadora).This plan, implemented in partnership with the association for the agricultural development of Puerto Venus (ADAVE) and the economic model. The results show, for all the properties, high vulnerability with a technological receptivity of maximum restriction due to the slopes greater than 50% and soils of heavy textures. Most of the properties have agrotechnological management systems that degrade their productive conditions, except for the PvLP site whose management consults these conditions of high vulnerability and makes a sustainable management. As for the intensity of the employment of the labor force, it varies between very intensive (four properties) and intensive (two properties), however, the lack of generational replacement constitutes a threat in the short square (one to two generations). It is worth noting that, in 50% of the evaluated properties, non-agricultural activities are carried out that generate economic income that are usually invested, as a rule, in the productive activities developed in the field obtained. As regards the diversity and multifunctionality of the results, it is important to highlight that, in the 50% of the evaluated properties, non-agricultural activities are carried out that generate economic income that are usually inverted, in the productive activities developed in the predial area
