# STANDARDS FOR THE REPORT OF QUALITATIVE RESEARCH

This working paper describes the main standards for qualitative research in order to provide a basis for future discussions on their relevance and need in Economic Sciences, where quantitative methods are often preferred. The coexistence of three paradigms in research in Social Sciences is analytical, symbolic and socio-critical hermeneutic. Does this paper highlight the importance of theoretical and conceptual frameworks for addressing research problems through qualitative methods. By discussing standards such as SRQR, COREQ, RATS and CASP, this paper highlights the importance of establishing standards to improve the quality, transparency and replicability of qualitative studies in the field of administration and beyond. Key words: reporting standards; qualitative research; administration; research integrity; research integrity
