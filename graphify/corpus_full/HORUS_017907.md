# RIGHTS TO WATER AND WATER¿S RIGHTS: PLURAL WATER GOVERNANCES IN MINING CONTEXTS OF COLOMBIA AND PERU

This text is based on research carried out in Colombia (La Guajira-Cerrejo ́n) and Peru (Apuri ́mac-Las Bambas) during 2018'19. It is backed by ethnographic research that studied and monitored local access to water, narratives of conflicts stemming from water access, data of mining companies and local communities¿ access to water (concessions), and water institutions diagrams in each country. In Colombia, we carried out participatory research that considered local proposals and political agendas surrounding water use and access in mining contexts. The chapter is organized into three sections. The first section consists of a conceptual discussion of water governance, its multiple conceptions, and its materialization through infrastructure. It also addresses the implications of other notions of rights, common goods, and water justice. The second section details the cases of Colombia and Peru. The final section offers conclusions that aim to spark debate on water rights and governances and relationship with this resource in large-scale mining contexts
