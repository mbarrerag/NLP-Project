# (SARS-COV-2) IMMIMENT THREAT FOR WORKERS IN THE HEALTH SECTOR

According to the International Labour Organization (ILO) and the World Health Organization (WHO), health workers are the cornerstone and the most valuable resource of health systems, consisting of 128 Health and safety at work in times of COVID-19, in terms of their role and social value for the activities they carry out since the promotion of health, prevention of disease, treatment and rehabilitation.According to these international bodies, countries must ensure the rights and protect the health of these professionals in the sector, first line in the process of care in the global health emergency caused by COVID-19. This will only be possible if the continuous improvement of their working conditions is optimized, which means adjusting the processes of labor linkage and implementing policies, guidelines and technical measures on the intervention of hazards in the workplaces (oms and oit, 2010), without which it will not be possible to provide access and quality in health services
