# 16. STAFFING BETWEEN MONTHS. ARCHITECTONIC EXPRESIONS BETWEEN DOMESTIC SPACE AND COTID LIFE IN THE GEOCLIMA URBAN OF THE VALLE DE ABURRÁ

It is intended to understand from an architectural perspective how contemporary life affects domestic space and how, in turn, domestic space affects the way life occupies it. Describe and characterize some transformations of daily life to understand the way in which they relate to domestic space, in the geographical and climatic conditions in the Aburrá Valley, are the objectives of this thesis, so that from the architecture one can contribute to an open debate on the relevance and currentity of housing that is designed, constructed, consumed and inhabited today, not only in the Aburrá Valley, but throughout the country. Methodologically, research is developed with a qualitative approach, with methodological strategies associated with categories related to space, geoclimate and daily life, and with techniques for collecting quantitative and qualitative data from two blocks in the hillside located in the Aburrá Valley. The results and conclusions relate both microclimatic data with sociodemographic and direct observational ones, which allow to infer correlations that allow the understanding of reciprocal effects between domestic space and daily life in the mountainous context of Valle de Aburrá and tropicala Colombia
