# METHOD OF FINAL ELEMENTS FOR DEGENED PARABOLIC ECUATIONS. THEORY, CONVERGENCE AND APPLICATIONS

The study of Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) has a great importance in Mathematics, Sciences and Engineering because many situations that come from practical problems are modeled through these equations. This makes today the numerical approximation of PEDs one of the areas with the greatest development in both Applied Mathematics and Engineering. Among the different numerical methods that can be found in specialized literature, the Finite Elements Method (FEM) is one of the most studied today, mainly for Second Order Elliptical Partial Differential Equations. For the case of Second Order Parabolic Differential Equations, FEM is frequently used in combination with some approach scheme for the temporal derivative. In this text we study theorems of existence and uniqueity of evolutionary problems, called evolutionary problems in mixed form. Subsequently, a numerical approximation of these parabolic equations is proposed, using the Euler method implicit in the temporal derivative and finite element method for space
