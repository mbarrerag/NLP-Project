# RCM AND ARC APPLICATION FOR THE REDUCTION OF FAILURES IN ENGINES OUTSIDE BORDA: CASE OF STUDIES IN THE PEÑOL-GUATAPÉ EMBALSE, COLOMBIA

The aim of this work is to analyze the causes of faults in outboard engines (MFB). Problems such as the accumulation of sediments in fuel, damage to the trimming unit and lack of revision of the transmission oil are identified. It is recommended to clean the fuel tank and regular revision of the transmission oil for effective maintenance. The lack of adequate revision of the transmission oil and damage from blows in the lower unit highlight the importance of the habits of use and maintenance. These findings emphasize the need for greater awareness by boat owners to prolong the life of the engines. Training in preventive maintenance by the distribution companies could address the lack of adequate technical service in the area of the Peñol-Guatapé reservoir
