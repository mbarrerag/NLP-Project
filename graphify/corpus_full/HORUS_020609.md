# Creative writing at school: The Explorers Project, an experience of literary aesthetic training at the Consolata Missionary Sisters College

In the practices on creative writing of the institution Colegio Hermanas Missioneras de la Consolata, in Bogotá, in which this thesis is developed, one perceives a distance from the Curricular Standards of the Spanish language of the Ministry of National Education, which propose the learning of creative writing as a basic concept in the language training in the school. The previous situation will find place in the subsequent documents developed by the Ministry. In this research, a phenomenological exploration, of a qualitative nature, has been carried out, examining the practices of the students in the development of the literary aesthetics as a creative, critical and propositional process in the classrooms. It also analyzes the results of the creative writing, through the design, planning and execution of the Explorers Project, created to put into practice the creative learning.
