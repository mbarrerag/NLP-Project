# COMMUNICATION OF SCIENCE AFTER BASID: CASE STUDY OF AN ART AND SCIENCE COLLECTIVE

This work investigates the construction of public communication products of science and technology (CPCT), as a counterpart to the prevailing approach, focused on the relationship of these products with the public. The study focuses on the dynamics of an art and science collective in Medellín (Colombia). As a main result, it was found that the collective was guided by a pragmatic attitude, without a significant role of the budgets and theoretical debates of the PCCT. Methodologically, it is a descriptive case study that relies on the ethno-gráco approach, and that documents the practices according to its analysis in laboratory studies. It has a clearly empirical scope, without analytical pretensions or generalizations based on a single case, in line with the medical and natural science studies that provide an initial report on a phenomenon, as a contribution to subsequent meta-studies that generalize similar cases
