# Research support for the project Challenges and advances in non-financial information assurance in Latin America, 2020-2023

This project aims to enable an assistant student of the Faculty of Economic Sciences to provide support in research work related to the collection of information, the construction of databases and the use of software for content analysis. The project Challenges and advances in the assurance of non-financial information in Latin America, period 2020-2023 (Research of Teaching Day to which this project would be enrolled) aims to describe the situation during the study period in six Latin American countries, using mixed methods of research
