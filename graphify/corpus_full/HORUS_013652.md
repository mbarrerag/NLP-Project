# Methodological guide for the correct implementation of the polishing of cylindrical concrete specimens as a method of non-adherent restraint

The concrete is one of the most used materials in construction around the world, for this reason the properties that define its quality and durability are verified through standardized laboratory tests. Because the concrete works very well to compression it can be said that this property determines its quality (Mantalana, R, 2019). In Colombia the norms NTC 550 (2020) and NTC 673 (2021) govern the procedures of elaboration of cylindrical specimens and test resistance to compression respectively. In practice different variables are found that condition the final result of the test, among other factors, the conditions of the sides of the cylinders and the witnesses influence considerably on the results of the tests (Kosmatka, S. H. et al., 2004). The reface, capinado or head of the cylinders is performed conventionally with methods adhered to like the plaster of high resistance or sulfur; and not adhered to the use of neoprene pads.
