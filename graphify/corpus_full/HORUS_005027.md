# ORIGIN, SCOPE AND CONSTRAINTS OF THE GOVERNMENT OF GUSTAVO PETRO

The present text aims to contribute to providing elements of analysis for a better understanding of the contents, possibilities and limitations of the progressive government in Colombia. In developing this purpose, the work has been divided into four sections: in the first, the basic conditions that led to the emergence of the progressive government are presented, based on an analysis of the tendencies of the accumulation regime, of the configurations of the political regime, of the impacts of the peace agreement with the FARC-EP, of the latest trends of resistance and struggles, and of the effects produced by the government of Iván Duque Márquez; in the second, a preliminary study of the progressive government in its more than year and middle of mandate is presented; in the third, the more general features of the progressive political project are presented, taking as a reference the assessment of the government programme of the electoral coalition of the Historical Pact and the discourse of Gustavo Petro; in the fourth, a preliminary study of the progressive government in its more than year and middle of mandate, taking into account the governmental action and the limits of institutional designs, some structural conditioning, the opposition political action, the limits of the progresism and its government, the situation of the real movement and the most important feature of the
