# THE IMPORTANCE OF ECO-INNOVATION AND CIRCULAR ECONOMY FOR THE SUSTAINABILITY OF SOCIAL SECTOR ORGANIZATIONS

In the search for innovative pathways to sustainability, two key approaches have been consolidated in the international programmes and policies of the European Union, on the one hand, the EcoInnovation (EI), which is framed in terms of eco-efficiency to increase the productivity of pre-existing technologies; and, on the other hand, the thought of Circular Economy (CE) as a solution so that organizations can reduce to the maximum the use of resources through a system that is restorative or regenerative. However, these concepts do not relate to civil society, there being a notable exclusion of the social sector actors from the discourse on eco-innovation and circular models. Therefore, this research focuses on designing a conceptual model that relates the variables of EI and EC, to contribute to the transition of the organizations of the social sector of the coffee sector to sustainability. This allows to direct the relationship between the IE and the EC, to the extent that complements and facilitates the generation of synergies in the implementation of a sustainable economy that leads to the opportunity to generate economic, social and environmental value from the exploration of new business models to solutions that allow, both a positive impact of the social organisations and the demographic change, to be able to analyze, through a systematic change
