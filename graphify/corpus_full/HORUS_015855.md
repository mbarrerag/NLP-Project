# Porosity: procedures and principles for dematerializing the boundary between the full and void in collective housing

This final master's work investigates the projectual procedures that build a permeable relationship between the interior and the exterior, starting from the formal manipulation of the full-empty relationship in the collective housing projects. Taking as hypothesis that the operations that produce a porous architecture can represent advantages both in the environmental conditions of the housing units and in the relation of the building with the rest of the city. The porosity is understood as a physical characteristic that measures the proportion between the full volume of the mass and the quantity of hollows or empty space contained in it, taken to the field of the architecture, the porous buildings are those in which the form captures the void (which usually is segregated or contained in a single point), in order to integrate these spaces opened in a more constant and diverse way with the closed and useful areas of the project. The project procedure results from the qualitative and quantitative analysis of four cases of study: Walden 7, Ricardo Bofill (1952); utec, grafon architects (2015); odhams walk, donald ball and the apartements okuramás de sanaa (2009). rest of the city.
