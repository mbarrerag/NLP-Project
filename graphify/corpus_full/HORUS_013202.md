# Systematic review: analysis of osteopathic therapy in rotator cuff tendonitis

The present systematic review of the literature focuses on the analysis of the efficacy of osteopathic therapy in the treatment of the tendonitis of the rotator cuff. A thorough search of studies was carried out in recognized databases, such as Pubmed, Cochrane Library, Lilacs and the International Journal of Osteopathic Medicine, which led to the identification of 10 studies that met the established inclusion criteria. To evaluate the risk of bias in the selected studies, various tools were used as, Cochrane Risk of Bias (ROB), Risk of Bias Assessment Tool (ROBIS) and JBI tools (Joanna Briggs Institute). The quality of the evidence obtained was evaluated using the GRADE Pro tool and the results are presented through a narrative synthesis, the findings of this review revealed evidence of quality that varies between low and moderate.
