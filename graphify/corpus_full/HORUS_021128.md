# Research in housing from TdG proposal of undergraduate students in Architecture (Emphasis Subject in Architecture of Housing I)

Present the various research questions and general conclusions on housing and habitat problems, which the undergraduate students of the Faculty of Architecture have developed in 2022, from the subject Énstress in Architecture of Housing I, taking into account the learning results and problematic nuclei developed in the processes of harmonization of the programs of the Faculty of Architecture. The subject has been managed by a team of professors with extensive experience in processes of design and construction of housing, housing plans and policies, urban and spatial planning: Teachers: Marco Aurelio Montes, Luis Fernando Dapena Rivera, Pedro Pablo Peláez, Luis Fernando González, Mónica Mejía Escalante, together with invited teachers from various areas of knowledge and with experience in reflecting on housing as a problem. The objective of the Énfásis in Architecture of Housing I is to support in the theoretical construction, both of the student, and of the course itself, within the framework of understanding the problem of housing and its relationship with architecture, urbanism, urban planning of the cities and social processes, from the construction of a research proposal to the pre-emotion and research of the research of TdG level, to solve the problem of the problem of the problem of students
