# Poetics of living on the informal edge of Bogotá: occupy, build, cultivate 'la Loma'

This thesis is approaching the informal edge built in the city of Bogotá by considering the poetics of the habit as an interpretative approach. To this end, the area formed by the Potosí and Caracolí neighborhoods was chosen as a sector of study. The view is situated from the inhabitants, who daily practice in living that allows to interpret the informal edge as a result of other ways of building the urban area. The practices of occupying, constructing and cultivating were understood as categories oriented to the interpretation of reality phenomena, from whose material results were studied about the logics of meaning within the framework of which they operate. Within the framework of these logics, poetics of the habitation occur in the context of the habit that respond to specific temporal space joints: from urgency, from recognition and from the dream. In these logics, this sector is an essential feature of the habitation that can be interpreted as a poetic to scale of the living in urban: the poetics of the resistance, which encourages the continuation of the edge making life possible where it was denied. .
