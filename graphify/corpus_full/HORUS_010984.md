# TECHNOLOGY MANAGEMENT IN COLOMBIA

The technology is fundamental in the development of electoral processes. This factor had an impact on the elections of 2014 in Colombia, especially on the transparency of electoral management (GE). The objective of this article was to propose guidelines for technological management (GT) focused on electoral counting, where the results of popular elections are defined. The methodology followed was qualitative in nature and with an interpretative approach, oriented to the understanding of the unit of study from administration, in a complementary way to its legal and political bases. It was developed in four phases, using in the first two of documentary review and analysis of contents of sources of current information on the poll in Colombia, to develop an analysis DOFA of this process, as a basis for generating, in the third phase, a proposal for technological management for the scrutiny, validated by means of expert judgment in the fourth phase. The diagnosis showed weaknesses such as lack of control and third-party vision, which impact on the integrity, transparency and legitimacy of the elections, but also strengths in terms of availability of computer tools and quality certifications. GT proposal is formed by six stages: the identification of technological needs for the transparency of the scrutiny, selection of technology, the protection, but also concludes the strength of the electoral path
