# Design of outdoor lighting for a national airport.

This work consists of a design of lighting of the external areas for a national airport, which seeks to provide a solution that better meets the needs and budget, for an airport that has characteristics similar to most non-international airports in the Colombian cities, due to the problems identified with respect to the identification and application of lighting on runways, technological change of luminaires and electrical failures in the provision of lighting service, which has generated insecurity, delays in operation and cancellations of flights. This design is aimed at ensuring the airport security, that of its passengers, crew and personnel of assistance in ground of the aircraft. Providing an adequate lighting solution for its use, and considerations of levels of illuminance, luminance, reliability, energy efficiency, durability, capacity and maintenance of the lighting system of the airport. To this end, an alternative lighting for the external areas of: runways, road of shooting, lighting of approach of the runways, platform, among other external areas.
