# Hosting capacity and optimal sizing: a Colombian case of study

The energy transition has facilitated the integration of renewable generation resources into global distribution networks. However, this transition has presented new operational challenges for these networks. One of these challenges involves determining the optimal size of these resources and the hosting capacity of the distribution networks, with the aim of maximizing their potential to improve the technical parameters of the system. To address this issue, this article presents an integral procedure to determine the accommodation capacity and the optimal size of the penetration of photovoltaic solar generation in a Colombian case study. This analysis is carried out using specialized software, which allows accurate and efficient evaluations
