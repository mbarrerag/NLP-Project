# ELEMENTS FOR AN ALTERNATIVE REFORM TO THE PENSION SYSTEM

Elements for structuring a proposal for an alternative reform to the pension system such as the principles, policies and objectives to be promoted, the nature of the system and the approach to the reorganization measures of the current system are proposed
