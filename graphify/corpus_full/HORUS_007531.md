# Verdad & Memoria Observatory. For the right to information, truth and peace.

Since the government in 2018 declared as purpose "to tear apart the agreement" referring to the Peace Agreement signed by the previous government with the FARC (2016), effectively with the monitoring of news from JEP, CEV, UBPD and the SIVJRNR (today SIP) we concluded in the book "Observing Truths and Memories" when critically analyzing the discourse of the media, during the period 2019-2022, that they have not assumed the social responsibility of truthfulness and impartiality that constitutionally is imposed on them, reinforced by a culture of peace. We propose to continue with the Observatory Verdad & Memoria (hereinafter V&M Observatory), qualifying and enriching the analysis of the discourse, starting from the records of the years 2023, 2024 and the projected first semester of 2025; making visible and positioning UNAL before the various publics and counter-publics; influencing the practices of citizen discourse with the different actors in key of peace; making recommendations of public policy of the rights to information, to truth and to peace, all with medium-term work, alliances and guaranteeing its permanence
