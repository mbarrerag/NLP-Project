# OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE

Specifying parameters in the conceptual design phase is crucial to the operational performance of manufacturing systems (SM), and it is important to understand their joint effect when specifying the desired capacity.This article aims to characterize the operational performance deterministic SM with a single server (SMus), the simplest case.We conduct a review with practical and theoretical purposes for decision making, whether in the conceptual design stage or in routine management.Our results describe the quantities, times and measures involved, allowing us to discuss the implications for managing capacity, entity flow and variability in SMus, as well as understanding the most complex cases
