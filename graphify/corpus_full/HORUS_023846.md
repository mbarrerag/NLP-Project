# STATISTICS OF THE VACIUM AND EXPERIENCE OF THE NON-WORD FROM THE LEADER TO THE TRACE

Shitao (s. XVII-XVIII) makes the experience of the Medium-Vicium - as stated in the Tao te King - the dynamic principle of the unique brush stroke. Shitao tells of this in his speech about painting by the bitter pumpkin monk. The notions of Medium-Vacium and unique brush stroke, decisive contributions by Shitao to Taoist aesthetics, will be of great interest to Jacques Lacan and François Cheng, who find in the discourse about painting the pretext for a rather fruitful dialogue: for Lacan brings more elements to review the notions of letter and emptiness in psychoanalysis; while Cheng will devote much of his book Emptyness and fullness. The language of Chinese painting to show the West the prominent place that Shitao occupies in Taoist aesthetics
