# Spectrum - a body that resists disappearing

Spectrum - A body that resists disappearing, is the project of creation - research, with which the artist and the bots David Caballero Puentes culminates his process in the Interdisciplinary Mastership of Theatre and Living Arts, of the National University of Colombia. This gesture of the stage-poetic bodies is asked for the place of the body that disappears, in the current conditions of virtuality, in the violent situations or in unknown conditions. In a totally dark space, inhabited by art reduced presences, by vultures, by unreadable bodies, will reconstruct the facts of the death of the artist's father and will appreciate the emergence of his son in the time of Pandemia. Through a sound installation, testimonies, memories, sounds, images and voices of various times, we are invited to resist to disappear and not forget our own dead ones.
