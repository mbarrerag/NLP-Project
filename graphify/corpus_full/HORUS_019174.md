# ART, CULTURE AND ECONOMY ALLOWING THE CONCEPT OF THE VOLCANS OF NARBIUM, COLOMBIA.

The study of the uses of volcanic products and the analysis of the relations of mutualism between communities and volcanoes is important since, for generations, products of volcanism, such as sulfur, lava and pyroclasts have been a source of materials used in the areas of health, construction and decoration. The extraction and use of these resources has driven the economy mainly of the private sector of the Department of Nariño (Colombia). Likewise, volcanoes have been seen as symbols, far from being considered as threats, have been venerated as protective and benefactor entities to which they owe respect. 267 observations of different forms of volcanic representations, collected during field tours in the period 2017-2020, of which audiovisual evidence was preserved. Such representations are associated with different contexts, within which the most relevant are: art, culture and music, areas that together include 53.94% of representations. These representations reflect the sense of belonging of the natives and highlight the relationship of the volcano-individual as a mutuality that includes affection
