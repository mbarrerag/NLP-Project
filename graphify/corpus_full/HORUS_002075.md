# Study of lead bioprecipitation mediated by ureolytic bacteria and its potential application in agricultural soil

Lead has become an environmental problem as a result of the increase in emissions by anthropogenic activities, and represents a risk to health and the environment due to its potential toxicity to living beings.To mitigate the negative impact that this metal may have, this project has recently explored the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms (MICP) using ureolitic bacteria to immobilize Pb(II) precipitating it as a low soluble carbonate, thus reducing its bioavailability for organisms such as plants in the soil. This project had as objective to evaluate the precipitation capacity of carbonates of ureolitic bacteria to be tested to Pb(II) and their performance in immobilization of metal in agricultural soil.
