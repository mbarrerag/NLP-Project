# OBTAINATION OF BIOACTIVE COMPOSSES OF THE CASCORA OF THE COFFEE OVER VERDE EXTRACTION METHODS

Colombia is the third world coffee producer with 696 billion tons/year. At the industrial level, only the seed is used, which accounts for only 54% of the fruit, which causes a large amount of solid organic waste generating wastes equivalent to 593 million tons. The shell has been reported as an interesting source of antioxidant bioactive metabolites such as anthocyanins and other polyphenols, but the production of these substances is mainly done using organic solvents that are harmful to health and the environment. The objective of this proposal is to obtain antioxidant bioactive compounds from by-products of coffee peel through sustainable and efficient extraction methods and to use these substances as natural antioxidants in easily oxidable food systems, such as lipid matrices. Within the expected results, innovative and environmentally friendly alternatives to the use of by-products of coffee-growing agro-industry are designed, as well as to propose a novel use that adds value to this national production chain
