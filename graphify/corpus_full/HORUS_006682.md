# Differences between sleep patterns resulting from the night use of a passive-activity screen and a 3D video game, Maduro +17, interactive, in the first person, in university students of Bogotá

The use of the cell phone before sleeping, is consolidated as an increasingly frequent habit among society, however, the type of activity that is carried out with this is not widely recorded, among these activities is recognized the use of video games as an attractive and frequent activity among the young population, but, the effect of video games before sleeping on the sleep pattern has not been described repeatedly. to identify the differences regarding the baseline sleep pattern resulting from the night use of a screen with passive activity and a video game 3D, Maduro +17, interactive, in the first person, in university students of Bogotá. 11 people type evening with average age = 22.1 ± 1.1) participated in a design counterbalanced within the subjects with three nights of registration on the sleep pattern: control, night of film (passive activity) and night of videogame. The experiment was developed in the home of each participant. for regular players it was found that, in relation to the night of film and video game, the latency of sleep increased (p=0.029) and reduced the subjective drowsiness (p=0.021).
