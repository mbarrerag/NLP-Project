# FUNDAMENTS AND DEFINITION OF DIVERSIFICATION OF ATTENTION TO INDIVIDUALS.

The way to make effective the educational inclusion with the learning of all, without didactic segregationist exceptions, is at the center of this study. It aims to define the concept of diversification of attention to individual differences. With the use of methods of theoretical level, an analytical review of the literature corresponding to the topic was carried out. Theories were outlined that base this conception, such as: Multiple Intelligences Theory, Neurolinguistic Programming, Universal Design for Learning, and Neurosciences findings. Finally, the conceptual definition was proposed, being a theoretical contribution to research, debate and exchange on a teaching process - efficient inclusive learning
