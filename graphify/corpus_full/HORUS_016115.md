# Politics, Football and Bars: An Analysis Based on the Public Policies of the City of Medellín in the Period 2008 - 2021

This paper investigates how public policies oriented towards barrism have been developed in the city of Medellín, understanding that they have a birth in 2007 and currently has a local public policy called “Culture of football” that is regulated in agreement 075 of 2017. This public policy aims to construct a model of coexistence oriented to live football as a collective spectacle with bases for the construction of positive social tissues, above what can be coercive or limiting actions of freedom. In the national context there is also the Ten-Year Plan of Security, Comfort and Coexistence in football 2014 – 2025, which also presents a series of guidelines and objectives similar to local politics. What differentiates this work from any other developed in the framework of football and barrism, is that this focuses on meeting the needs that founded the construction of a public policy, and how from the actions of the bars themselves and the local administration, it manages to address these difficulties and reduce those violences oriented around football. .
