# HUMANIZATION IN ODONTOLOGY: PERSPECTIVE OF A PATIENTS GROUP OF IPS IN BOGOTA

During dental consultation, patients place their trust in the professional who attends them, this requires the dentist to have the ability to establish links with them and provide attention with bioethical and humanization parameters. The humanization of dentistry transcends clinical practice, includes aspects related to empathy, assertive communication and respect for individuality, among others. To understand the perspectives and horizon of meaning given to humanization by a group of patients attended by a public IPS in Bogotá. A qualitative study with a phenomenological approach, carried out in a Health Services Provider Institution (IPS) in the city of Bogotá. It used as information collection tools the semistructured interview and the patient's day-to-view patient, the field work was carried out in the facilities of the IPS. It was attended by 16 people: 8 women and 8 men, patients of a public IPS. Based on the research objectives, a matrix was designed for analysis. It was carried out encryptation, patient, disposition and grouping of data (ductative analysis); it was possible to identify the three persons and to identify them. and with integrity where they juxtapose, while complementing, technical-scientific aspects of clinical work, assistance with elements related to communicative interaction in an assertive manner, as well as mutual recognition and empathy between dentist and patient that is evidenced in a dignified treatment mediated by the praxis of ethical virtues leading to professional excellence and interest in the health process
