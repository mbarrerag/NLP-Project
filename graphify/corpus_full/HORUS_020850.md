# Co-construction of community pilots for adaptation to climate change and sustainable habitat in the high mountain of Valle del Cauca.

From the extension of solidarity, the proposal seeks to provide a space for encounter between the UN and the rural communities of high mountain, taking advantage of the advances of the research project realized, _x0093_ESCUELA DE PENSÓN AMBIENTAL STANDARD UNIVERSITARIO. The post-agreement as an opportunity for a new relationship between society and its natural and cultural heritage. palmira, valle del cauca_x0094_ (epapu 2019-2020). Through this proposal, it seeks to continue the results of the research, through the transfer of knowledge, with the co-construction of pilots that contribute to the reduction of the negative impact of climate change and improve the quality of the habitat of the rural population of mountain. Initiatives that contribute to environmental sustainability, so theoretical (international and local agreements) and so little applied, seek to materialize through family pilots in topics such as: use of rain and waste water; reforestation of the paramo with frailejons; restoration, recovery and conservation of ecosystems as a tool of the education of the environmental sciences; and with green seals as a strategy of competitiveness for small farmers of the Colombian agriculture. The project aims to protect biodiversity and ecosystem services in order to improve water quality, promote climate, reduce natural hazards and conserve the landscape. It also seeks to work with the community to provide conservation education mechanisms for the appropriation of the moor. With the restoration, recovery and conservation of ecosystems, as a tool for the teaching of environmental sciences, environmental culture is strengthened, with positive effects that will minimize the negative impact of people. And with green stamps, as a competitiveness strategy for small producers, incentives will be obtained for agricultural production that can generate added value to their production and increase their economic benefits. It is hoped that the results of the project will achieve the transfer of knowledge and encourage replication, with greater benefits for the peasant habitat, for climate change and for the environmental sustainability of the territory, for the low-scale use of rhetoric
