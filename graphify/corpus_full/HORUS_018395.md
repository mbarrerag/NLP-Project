# Methodological proposal for the processing of signals and videos applied to the detection and characterization of the multiplicity of atmospheric electric discharges.

Currently several researches have focused on analyzing from high speed videos, characteristics of atmospheric electric discharges in order to acquire better understanding of the phenomenon, which involves among other aspects the development of robust protection systems. Most of the investigations have required an observer that, in the event of the event, provides a manual shot to the camera allowing to store the visual information of the phenomenon. Therefore, this work was oriented to propose a methodology for the detection of the discharges using two implementations based on signal processing and computational vision, with the purpose that the system autonomously is the one that provides the shot, removing the observer from the realization of this task. The detection system based on imaging techniques required the adequacy of methods of segmentation, representation, description and classification. The recognition algorithm with computational vision was implemented by means of the neuronal network convolutional EfficientNetB4. Out of line the source, the techniques based on image processing techniques provided an accuracy of 81.81%, while making use of computational vision the accuracy was 71.63%.
