# Did he kill me, but he didn't say anything, preventing gender and sex violence in the operation of mines?

In 2012, the National University of Colombia defined a policy that promotes equality between men and women through strategies of redistribution, cultural recognition and prevention of detection and support against gender violence. In the framework of this policy, measures were proposed in the Plan of Action 2018-2020 of the Faculty of Mines to promote gender equality. One of the aspects addressed in this plan is gender and sexual violence. Although it is known that it occurred, until 2019 no diagnosis had been made of the prevalence of manifestations of gender and sexual violence in the Faculty. Then, in 2019, a survey was conducted to learn the type and incidence of sexual harassment manifestations and propose prevention actions based on this diagnosis. It was found that 12.8% of students, mostly women, had received unwanted sexual behaviour within the university or by persons belonging to the Faculty, mostly male students. Based on the data, intervention projects were defined to re-evaluate in a year and determine if there was any change. Key words: sexual and gender violence; prevention; manifestations sexual harassment
