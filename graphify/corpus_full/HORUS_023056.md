# DIFFERENTED HEALTH POLICIES FOR PEOPLE WITH AFRICAN CONTINENTAL ASSOCIATION IN THE WORLD.

In Colombia, people with similar continental African descent are defined as those who recognize themselves as black, Afro-Colombian, root and palenqueros (NARP), who possess a culture of their own, share a history and have their own traditions and customs and retain identity awareness that distinguish them from other ethnic groups; they are granted special protection by the Constitution. In the world, their status, general and health status is diverse. The difficult situation in health they face, is reflected in Colombia, with indicators such as maternal mortality rate, which is equivalent to three times the total national, or with 68.9% of households of African descent presenting food and nutritional insecurity. To establish a balance of knowledge published on policies or systems of differentiated health care for people with African continental ancestry in the world; from the determination of the scope and nature of the existing literature on the subject. systematic exploratory review in databases such as PubMed, Jstore, Bireme, in Google Scholar and literature of expert recommendation; from the demographic and health issues identified; in English, Spanish and Portuguese languages; using qualitative synthesis and quality evaluation using Toolt
