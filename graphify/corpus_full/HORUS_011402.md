# ECO-HYDROLOGIC VULNERABILITY OF PARARAMS IN THE CHANGE IN THE USE OF SOUND AND CLIMATE CHANGE

The ecohydrological vulnerability to climate change was evaluated for three eco-paramo ecosystems in Colombia, using the ecohydrological modeling using the spatially distributed model WaSiM that after being calibrated and validated, was used to simulate the hydrological responses of the basins against six different scenarios of climate change that were projected as likely occurrence in the medium term (2045-2065), focused on three rcp (Representative Concentration Pathways): 2.6, 4.5 and 8.5 and two precipitation realizations for an average dry year and an extreme dry year. The results show that the simulated flows in the Belmira and Chingaza moors varied less than 8% with respect to the base year (2015) indicating a low vulnerability to the variations expected by the climate change in the medium term, while the projections of the flows for the Valumales-Berlin model showed variations of up to 67 % with respect to that same year, which place it at a level of high vulnerability to the climate change.
