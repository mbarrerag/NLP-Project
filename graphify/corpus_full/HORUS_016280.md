# Economic criminal law in Colombia: A study on the agiotía and sanctions against legal persons when they affect the protected legal property of the estate and the economic order.

The commercial and consumer activity in Colombia has been affected by the problem of the cartelization of prices in products of first necessity by companies in their respective sectors; this causes, besides distrust in the economic system, direct damage to the economy of consumers. This so-called cartelization constitutes a conduct proper to the economy and consists in that several companies, taking advantage of their dominant position in the market, decide to fix prices for their products, violating the rules of the supply and demand market, also affecting protected legal goods. Therefore, this document considers this situation and proposes solutions
