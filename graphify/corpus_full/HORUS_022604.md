# MONITORING PROTOCOL AND WATER FOLLOW-UP

This technical document updates previous protocols that have been produced to meet needs of guidance in the realization of hydrological practices of monitoring, which will be mentioned in paragraph 5.2 Protocols and monitoring guides.In this opportunity new procedures are presented, preserving an integral vision of the hydrological cycle in the continental and marine field.The application of standardized practices and supported in scientific precepts to carry out the monitoring of water, allows to strengthen the knowledge of the water systems and improve the understanding of the functions, pressures and affects, with purposes of management and planning from national, regional and local fields of action.It is important to emphasize that, beyond the technologies and practices of field is relevant, moreover, to ensure the strengthening of the human talent of the institutions responsible for monitoring, the base of accredited laboratories, the affordability of the actions and strategies of monitoring and the management of the information.This last component facilitates the disposition of information of high quality, timely, accurate, relevant, accessible and, above all, the useful resources of the different users.From the institutional point of view, the protocol is aligned with the principles, objectives and components, both operating and strategic of the National Policy for the Intituts management (Hd) and the National Health Department
