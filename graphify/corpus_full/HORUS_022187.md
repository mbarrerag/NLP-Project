# Good faith free of blame in land restitution processes regulated by Law 1448 of 2011. Tensions between victims' rights and the rights of current owners and owners.

The requirement of good faith free of blame to the opponents as prerequisite for obtaining compensation has been the centre of criticism of the processes of restitution of land regulated by Law 1448 of 2011 causing legal, political and social tensions in the country. The purpose of this standard is to prevent those who benefited from the armed conflict from continuing to dominate the property and therefore it is necessary that the opponent demonstrates that he was not aware of the reasons that led the victim to carry out the legal business nor that there was conflict in the region, in addition he must demonstrate the performance of additional actions to those that are carried out in the normal tract of the business. The courts have analyzed a variety of evidence provided by the opponents to demonstrate their good faith exempt from guilt among those that they are: study of titles, advice of legal professionals, award by auction, authorizations of sale by the State, payment of fair prices, expert opinions of graphological and dactyloscopic, acquisition after the first business, sucessions, among others, but the argument given in valuing the evidence is not consistent and there are contradictory decisions.
