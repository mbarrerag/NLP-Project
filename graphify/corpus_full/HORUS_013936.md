# Exploration of visual methods within the EQUID model (Ergonomics Quality in Design) for understanding contexts and process optimization in product design

The design processes are complex, because they require the consideration of multiple variables at different scales, multiple forms of communication and explicit and hidden interests, among which there are many relationships. In addition, the understanding of such variables should be given in a limited time and with normally scarce resources. In this scenario, visual methods are an alternative to explore, discover and define such variables, since they make use of metaphorical thinking that facilitates access to implicit knowledge, as well as facilitating logical and spatial partnerships, among others. This research project addresses the exploration of such methods in the framework of the Ergonomics Quality in Design EquID 4.0 model. It is a sociotechnological model for the design and development of products towards sustainability, which emphasizes both the quality of ergonomics and human factors (social sustainability) and environmental sustainability. The purpose is to contribute to the improvement of understanding of the variables in design projects, as well as the optimization of design processes itself.
