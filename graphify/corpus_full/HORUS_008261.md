# 15. CASES OF STUDY AND MODELS OF LIVING FOR RURALITY IN COLOMBIA 2017-2022 AN APPROXIMATION FROM THE NOTION OF FORMAL STRUCTURES.

The proposal “Cases of study and housing models for rurality in Colombia 2017-2022” aims to point towards the pedagogical horizon of problem-based learning, in which a subject of order proper to architecture (the notion of formal structure) can be linked with the recognition of the problems of culture and housing that are not specific to the discipline and transversal with respect to the society in which the National University of Colombia is enrolled based on the experience of life and diversity of origins of students in the national territory. This research project from the classroom is aimed at students of final degree in Architecture from the National University of Colombia, headquarters Medellín, as citizens of a diverse and constantly transforming country. It corresponds to an elective emphasis on the project in architecture.
