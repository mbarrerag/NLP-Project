# Resocialization or guarantee fallacy in the perspective of prison law in Colombia: its relationship with work and study (1934-2015)

Resocialisation or guarantee fallacy in the perspective of prison law in Colombia: its relationship with work and study (1934-2015) Description: This research analyzes the study and work activities that Colombian legislation provides for to redeem punishment, making a historical journey until reaching the present time, based on a qualitative methodology of collecting information, on the legal and jurisprudential gaps that exist in this regard. To carry out the research, interviews were conducted with persons deprived of liberty, a judge of execution of sentences and the guardians of the Inpec implicit in the activities of redemption, in order to give the reader a panorama of reality that was possible to contrast with the guarantee theory of Luigi Ferrajoli, which will allow to determine that despite the fact that law 65 of 1993 stipulates activities of study and work, the same do not resocialize the inmates, subsisting a fallacy guarantee of the Colombian State. . thus subsisting a gurantee failure of the Colombian State
