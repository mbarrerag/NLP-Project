# Colonization and settlement in Antioquia (Colombia), 1850-1890. The cases of Titiribí, Concordia and Jericó

This article aims to analyze the process of colonization, settlement and production in the localities of Titiribí, Concordia and Jericó between 1850 and 1890. The study considers the settlement dynamics and examines the modalities of population and economic configurations of those places. The selected cases reveal both spontaneous and entrepreneurial forms of settlement, particularly supported by mining and agricultural exploitation. This historical approach seeks to offer a procedural analysis, proposing a case study perspective taking into consideration both the regional historiography and the availability of local archives
