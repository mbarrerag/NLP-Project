# Diversity of the species of Tacsonia Supersection (Passiflora L.) in the Andes of Peru

The Tacsonia supersection is distributed in the Tropical Andes from 1,500 to 4,100 msnm and are characterized by having flowers with elongated hipantio. Peruvian Andes are one of the main zones of distribution and richness of Tacsonia species, and despite this the species have been little studied. The objective of this research was to study the diversity, distribution and conservation of Tacsonia species in Peru from information of herbarium, literature and field, following the criteria and categories of IUCN.
