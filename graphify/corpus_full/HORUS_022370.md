# THE ACCOUNTING OF INSTASTANIBLE DEVELOPMENT: A CONTRACTING ANALYSIS ON THE LESS OF BIODIVERSITY IN THE JBS BRAZIL CASE

Sustainable development is the iconic concept of current capitalism, covering both economic and social and environmental issues (World Commission on Environment and Development, 1988).
