# Synthesis and characterization of the new material with triple perovskit structure R2AMn3-xCoxO9 (R = Gd, Dy; A = Ca, Sr; x = 0.0, 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5).

This paper shows a detailed study of materials based on manganese of rare earths RMnO3 (R = ion of rare earths).In the literature there are several reports that the structural, magnetic, electrical, and optical properties of these materials depend on the nature of the ion of rare earths, as well as their size. Taking as a starting point the manganeses of rare earths with gadolinium (Gd) and dysprosium (Dy), substitutions were made in the positions of the ions R and Mn to give rise to the family of materials given by the generic formula R2AMn3-xCoxO9, where A ion alkalineterreo (Ca or Sr) and x determines the degree of substitution of cobalt ions (Co) in the positions of the ions of manganese (Mn).These substitutions were mainly intended to induce mixed valence states in the ions Mn, to generate ferromagnetic in the materials, equally, was sought to strengthen this behavior with the introduction of medium ions Con. The magnetic behaviour of the materials was studied by means of magnetization measures depending on the temperature in zero field enfied (ZFC) and field cooling cycles (FC), observing a paramagnetic character at ambient temperature, with phase transitions below 100 K to competing ferromagnetic and antiferromagnetic states, with antiferromagnetism below 50 K and ferromagnetism between 50 K and 100 K being favored. Finally, the electrical response of the materials was studied by complex impedance curves as a function of temperature in a range of 70 K to 300 K with applied frequencies of 100 Hz, 1kHz, 10 kHz and 100 kHz, observing a semiconductive behavior throughout the temperature range and phase transitions dependent on the frequency associated with the transport and dielectric relaxation mechanisms of the materials
