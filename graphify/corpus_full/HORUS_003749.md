# LP-BOUNDEDNESS OF THE LAPLACE TRANSFORM

In this paper, we discuss about the boundedness of the Laplace transform L : Lp([0,8)) ¿ Lp(A) (p = 1) for the cases A = [0,8), A = [1,8) and A = [0, 1]. We also provide examples for the cases where L is unbounded
