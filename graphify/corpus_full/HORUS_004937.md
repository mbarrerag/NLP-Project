# Overexpression of CTPF in MURINE LATENTING TB AND USE OF INHIBITOR C490

Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb) expresses ATPases type P which are a cellular membrane transporter protein that maintain intracellular ion balance, essential activity for survival within the intraphagosomal environment (1). The Mtb genome encodes 11 ATPases type P which are classified according to the ion they transport, these are transporters of heavy metals (CtpA, CtpB, CtpC, CtpD, CtpG, CtpJ, CtpV), alkaline metals and alkaline-terreous (CtpE, CtpF, CtpH, CtpI) (2).CtpF is an ATPase type P2 coupled to Ca2+, is the only one regulated by the Dos-R system which is the master genetic system that regulates the state of latente tuberculosis (TB) and is activated in the presence of Inactive Species, Nitrogen and Hypoxia. CtpF, which maintains the bacterial hometasis at this time in the intraphagosomatic environment (3) and the active at the end of the end of the end of the patient. The CtpF gene is expressed 11 to 18 times more in mice' lungs with TB than in progressive TB. These results suggest that c490 can be useful for the elimination of bacteria in latent state
