# Evaluation of alpha-adrenergic-type mechanisms of active metabolites (Flavonoids) of Achyrocline bogotensis (“Vira Vira”).

Given the increasing impact on public health of benign prostatic hypertrophy (BPH), in view of the increase in the male adult population and having demonstrated in previous work the relaxing effects on the smooth muscle of the Achyrocline bogotensis extract, associated with alpha-adrenergic antagonist-type mechanisms (Lara et al, 2017)(1), this research evaluated the relaxing activity of two of the main flavonoid metabolites of the species: quercetin and 5-hydroxy-7,8-dimethoxyflavone (mossoflavona), in order to determine its participation in the overall activity of A.bogotensis, in the preparation of spontaneously hypertensive rat aorta isolated rings (HR). The results showed a remarkable effect of quercetin, comparable to the alpha-antrol pattern used: prazosin and similar to the total extract, so much as the effect of the mosolosoflavone was mild
