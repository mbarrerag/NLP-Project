# Latifundio, from Yoknapatawpha to Macondo

There are two ways to explain why nations can be poor: one, because they have a shortage of natural resources, human resources or capital equipment. And two, the difference between wealth and poverty can be explained by “the quality of public policies and institutions” (Olson, 1996, p. 19). Countries with the best institutions and policies get the most out of their potential, while others, with the worst institutions and policies, are inefficient and poor
