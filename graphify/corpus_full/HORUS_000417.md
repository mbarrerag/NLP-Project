# Morphoagronomic characterisation and performance performance of quinoa genotypes (Chenopodium quinoa Willd.)

The quinoa (Chenopodium quinoa Willd.) stands out for its nutritional value and agroecological adaptability and position as a strategic crop in specialized markets. In Colombia, its consolidation in high Andean areas requires the identification of the improved genotypes with high yield and grain quality. The objective of this research was to characterize agronomically 14 families improved of sweet quinoa, including the San Jorge family and three commercial witnesses (Aurora, Blanca de Jericho and Tunkahuan) under conditions of the municipality of Silvia (Cauca, Colombia), by means of a completely randomized block design with four repetitions. 11 morphoagronomic variables were evaluated and statistical analyses of ANOVA, Duncan test (P&;0.05), analysis of main components (ACP), analysis of hierarchical conglomerates and selection index.
