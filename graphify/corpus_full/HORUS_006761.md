# Hegemony of Catastrophist Fields: Tensions of Emerging Collapse with the Societal Collapse Studies

Adopting a revitalization of the Marxist tradition in the Social Studies of Science and Technology (STS) and in contemporary philosophy, my work deals with the emergence of the collapse in France and Belgium, Europe and the world, its constitutive tensions with the hegemonic field of the societal collapse studies. The thesis carries out a thorough follow-up of the collapse and its founding prophets, the agronomist engineer and Doctor in Biology, Pablo Servigne, and the environmental manager of companies and Master in Holistic Sciences, Raphaël Stevens, in his rhetorical attempt to constitute a new field. In the first chapter, I perform a critical dialogue and assembly of dynamics and functionings of the fields, studies of rhetoric and ideologies of the sciences, specialized literatures on hegemonys of risks and catastrophes, reconsidering the catastrophystic and the terminalities of capitalism.
