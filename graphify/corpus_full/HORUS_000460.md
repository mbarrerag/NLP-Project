# Modeling of the generation and primary expulsion of hydrocarbons in an area of the Attrato Basin

In order to evaluate the windows of generation and expulsion of hydrocarbons in an area of energy and economic interest within the sub-basin Atrato (perfect to the large sedimentary basin of the Arc Panama-Choco), located in the Colombian north-crowd, a two-dimensional model of a representative section was established.This model assumes a structure corresponding to that of the last evolutionary state of the petroleum system where the tectonic efforts of distant field have little influence on the geometry and consequently the effects of structural reconfigurations during the simulation are dispensed.The evaluation of the generation window for the southern zone was made through the computation of the Temporal Index of Temperature (IT), by its acronym in English) under the assumption of four scenarios for the type of cherogene considering the geochemical data and history of burial. Subsequently, the evaluation of the transport in porous medium linked to these results was made. This modeling was carried out in close proximity to two wells in the northern area by means of finite elements, solving the advection of the fluid mobility schemes. The physical-chemical properties of the different geological formations and the results of the different groups were determined.
