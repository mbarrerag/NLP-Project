# Model for evaluating the performance of a health system from network analysis: an approach based on morbidity patterns and their relationship to health services.

The general objective of a health system is to promote, restore and maintain the health of the population, as well as to influence the determinants of health. To assess the degree of fulfilment of this objective, it is necessary to know the health status of the populations and to do this one of the approaches is the study of population morbidity, which links information from one or more diagnoses independently and whose indicators in general are fragmented. Considering that it is possible to use relational approaches such as the Network Analysis to study morbidity patterns, this thesis proposes relational representations to characterize morbidity patterns and their relationship with the service delivery component of the system, considering the determinants of health of age, sex and socioeconomic condition. Based on the application of these representations in this thesis, a set of network measures is proposed that provide information on the structure and dynamics of health systems that, together, shape the conceptual model for evaluating the performance of the systems studied
