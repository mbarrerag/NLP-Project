# A question from the art.

Sometimes sound presupposes an action, in other cases an action precedes a sound. This sound world represents emotion. The lights that sound emits become strategies and mechanisms for the practice of creation and its pedagogies of research and creation. The state of listening underscores the obstacles to listening as ethical, poetic and political action that contemplates among its possibilities the creation of communities and citizenships. Denial and openness to the forces of this ghostly matter are put into tension in this paper. This text is accompanied by a video made to complement the presentation
