# From biomimises to hygromorphic materials: Characterization of the hygromorphic behaviour of a bilayer material composed of wood and flax fibre, in front of changes in relative humidity in the city of Bogotá

From the biomimimes understood as “nature based solutions”, we seek to contribute to the specific knowledge by means of the characterization of a bilayer material composed of wood and textile fibre of linen of natural origin, under the conditions of humidity of Bogotá. Process realized from the emulation of the movement presented in conifer pineapples as a response to the changes of humidity. Therefore, it is necessary to understand the hygromorphic processes that generate deformations before humidity, as an opportunity of passive constructive solutions in front of the control of environmental factors, that take advantage of the properties of the material.
