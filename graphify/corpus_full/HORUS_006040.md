# Co-creation of value in the university-business interrelations of the Coffee Axis

The objective of this doctoral thesis is framed in the analysis of the structure of the variables co-creative of value in the university-company interrelations of the Coffee Axis, taking into account, also, the implementation of the disciplines of value for a better performance in both types of organization. A methodology with positivistic epistemological position and systemic approach was used, subject to empirical verification the Model DART, derived from literature, as a scale of measurement to co-create value, and which was adapted to the needs of the study by designing a questionnaire structured with levels of measurement and techniques of comparative and non-comparative scaling. For the processing of the data, the adaptation of the tool dashboard power BI, and statistical software such as SPSS, Año y R.
