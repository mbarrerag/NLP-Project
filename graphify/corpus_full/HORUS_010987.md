# LIGHT MONDAY

About the book: The book brings together a set of articles that think and expose considerations of the scale OF THE HUMAN at a time of post-pabundice, this document makes an orchestration of counter-fectations or new stories that mobilized our consciousness and our relations between the human, reality and the city. On the article proposed is entitled LUZ MONDAY and is constituted in a literary territory in which grammars and theoretical concepts are capitalized towards a creation of literary in which language is constituted as a character or a fundamental component of its plot. It proposes a sort of state of post-human language, from a certain marginality or underground of the city, a semiotic indignity as a waste of which would resurrect singular and particular semiotics, escaped from what Guattari would call the dictatorship of the signifier. The article links notions of: memory as fiction, fiction of fact and not of false, that is to say: memory is made; with the city as dominant language v/s its marginality as a line of escape and language as a living entity
