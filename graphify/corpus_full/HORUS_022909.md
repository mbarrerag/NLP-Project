# Obtaining and characterizing flours composed of Cucurbita moschata D. and Cajanus cajon L. as alternative sources of protein and vitamin A

The use of guandul (Cajanus cajon L.) and ahuyama (Cucurbita moschata D.) as nutritional and technologically viable alternatives for the production of composite flours for the preparation of food products rich in protein and vitamin A of traditional consumption by the Colombian population was studied. For this purpose the main stages of processing plant matrices during the conversion into flours were evaluated. The nutritional composition was determined including the total carotenoids content of ahuyama flour and the amino acid profile of the protein of guandul flour. The flours were characterized by the distribution of the particle size and its functional properties. It was found that from both matrices it is possible to obtain flour-type products, with properties suitable for their use at commercial scale as concentrated sources of nutrients. To confirm the results, the flours were used in mixtures provided (beverage, are present and bread) in formulations that comply with the descriptors: ‘high’ in vitamin A and ‘high’ or ‘good’ in protein, besides the characteristics of organic and composition
