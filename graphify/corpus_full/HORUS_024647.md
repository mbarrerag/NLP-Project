# COLUMNING DISFUNCTION STRUCTURES IN LAVAS ASSOCIATED TO THE NEW VOLCANIC COMPLETE OF RUIZ (COLOMBIA): FACISES, DIMENSIONS AND GEOMETRY.

Associated with the Volcanic Nevado del Ruiz (CVNR) are several lava flows with columnar disjunction structures formed by the physical cooling regime. These are of interest both for the field of volcanology and geotourism, especially for the Geopark Volcán del Ruiz aspiring to UNESCO recognition, since, not only do they provide information about the mechanisms of flow and cooling of the lavas, which in turn have implications in the permeability usable by hydrothermal fluids, but they also form part of the main geoturistic features at world level. In this work the disjunctional features present in four accoutations located in the northeastern flank of the CVNR on the Manizales - Murillo route. Of each accretion, an average description of the morphology of the site, based on the photo interpretation and the observations of field, as well as the polygonal patterns, are determined the dimensions of the columns and their minor identifiable features.
