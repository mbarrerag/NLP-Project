# SENSORIAL ACCEPTANCE OF PLUMUDA NUGET (OPISTHONEMA SPP) AND CARDUMA (CETENGRAULIS MYSTICETUS) IN CHILD POPULATION IN THE SCHOLAR AGE IN TUMACO, COLOMBIA

With the aim of determining the yield in cooking, reducing the size in cooking and performing the sensory evaluation with children of school age in Tumaco, Colombia, four treatments were performed with mixtures of the useful part of the Plumuda and the useful part of the Carduma (100% Carduma, 100% Plumuda, 75% Plumuda - 25% Carduma and 50% Plumuda - 50% Carduma) in the elaboration of a product type croquette (fried bread) called nugget. The maximum reduction in size for the croquette was with the mixture constituted by 100% useful part of Plumuda and those of minor reductions were that of 100% useful part of Carduma and that of the mixture 50% Carduma - 50% Carduma. The highest yield value in cooking (13.75%) was presented by the croquette (Nugget) of the mixture 50% plumuda -50% carduma. With respect to the sensory evaluation, all the Nuggets elaborated from Carduma useful part paste, of paste useful part of Plumuda or mixtures of these pastes, obtained good values of general acceptance without evidence of characteristics in these cases
