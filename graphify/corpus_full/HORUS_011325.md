# Responses of native maracuyá and their commercial cultivars to the incidence of Fusarium oxysporum

Maracuyá (Passiflora edulis f. flavicarpa) is a fruit with high economic potential in Colombia, but the prevalence of some diseases often causes farmers to desist from growing it. Likewise, there have been no releases of varieties tolerant to some pathogens that have high prevalence in Colombia. The aim of this study was to contribute to the characterization of 63 populations of maracuyá, including 46 cultivars and 17 natives. Plants were evaluated using morphoagronomic descriptors (leaves, flowers and fruits) as well as ecophysiological parameters (stomatic conductance, chlorophyll content and degree of chlorosis) evaluated against strain 21-02129 of Fusarium oxysporum isolated from maracuyá morado (gulupa). The results showed low levels of germination (55% native and 50% cultivarable).
