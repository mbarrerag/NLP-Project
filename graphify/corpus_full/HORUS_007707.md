# ARS301: expanded publication

ARS301: expanded publication is a defining step in the search for tools, platforms and strategies of production and distribution of the publication. To do this and articulating with the STEAM classroom we will develop a virtual space taking as a point of reference the 3D model of Building 301 where users/readers can find 5 rooms with editions of the publication, archive material and complementary material. This first stage will concentrate, then, on the visual, immersive and content development of the first 5 rooms for the ARS301. ARS301 Magazine. ARS301: expanded publication is a project that links the analogous graphic processes with the wide possibilities of digital technology, virtuality and immersive experiences. ARS301 is a hybrid publication of the Faculty of Arts that at its origin (2016) had a printed destination; the pandemic and the multiple institutional forms that have affected the Faculty of Arts forced us to reconfigure the future and destination of the publication. Since 2022 ARS301 began to explore the digital and analogous tools as a possibility of development and distribution of the publication.
