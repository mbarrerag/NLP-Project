# The Fever of _x0091_Gold Green_x0092_. Sociotechnical Networks in the Multi-Environmental Conflict, around the Colombian Hass Avocado (American Persian). A Comparative Study between Cajamarca, Tolima and Genoa, Quindío.

This project of the thesis presents the multiple ontology of the socio-environmental conflict that agency and joins the Colombian Hass avocado, in the municipalities of Cajamarca, Tolima and Genoa, Quindío. The Hass avocado is a leisure object that has been able to build networks, on the one hand, between a corporate agrifood system of a global character, aguacateros guilds and state entities that regulate the agricultural sector; and on the other, around the Hass, the activity of environmental groups, non-governmental organizations and other social actors is deployed. This work is then proposed to follow that powerful non-human actant that is the Hass avocado, to determine the scenarios in which its presence agency processes, does do things to other actants, human and institutional, that participate in its productive chain, of its consumption or that oppose its intensive production. My methodological commitment And is supported by tools such as ethnography, observation, semi-structured interview and content analysis, within the framework of an open conception of the method (in continuous construction), in the research in social sciences.
