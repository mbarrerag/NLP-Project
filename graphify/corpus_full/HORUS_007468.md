# Position control for a quadripenter based on a virtual reality system

The positioning systems for UAVs in indoor applications, usually present problems with conventional sensors, e.g. sensors based on absolute reference frames, frequently introduce errors in measurements when there are minimal changes in position, or positioning systems based on high speed cameras and visual servo systems, computerly have a high load and limitations in image quality, and are generally quite expensive. To counteract those problems, due to their characteristics, an interest has arisen in the use of virtual reality sensors for position control in UAVs. These sensors are affordable, have high accuracy and work well in low light conditions without relying on communication networks. In addition, they are resistant to interference from other sensors and can track multiple reference points simultaneously. The use of virtual reality sensors as a positioning system significantly improves the performance in the tracking of UAVs in internal environments, increases security and facilitates research, due to their low cost. This work describes the procedure implemented to integrate virtual reality technology into the control of a free software drone.
