# Some results associated with the ECUATION OF RICCATI and its applications

The Riccati equation is a study of the Riccati equation described in its most general form by the equation \begin{equation{1} y'(t)alpha(t)y^2(t)beta(t)y(t){gamma(t). \end{equation} It is a first order nonlinear ordinary differential equation, for which a solution can be determined only in some cases. In general, in practical applications where it is not possible to find its solution, a numerical treatment is required. From results associated with the study of the equation \ref{1}, Riccati equations with general structures that has solution will be determined. Some Riccati equations that appear in the literature will be associated with these results in order to determine new paths of solution. Riccati equation, has a strong relationship with a second order equation that also has the same problem of Riccati Equation, however, the latter has additional studies, with some results that may be associated with the equation \ref{1}.
