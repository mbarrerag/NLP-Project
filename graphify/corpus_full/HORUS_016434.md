# Communication strategies for the transfer of knowledge in the Biological Sciences to rural communities. Case study: Heliconia-producing flower growers at the Marco Fidel Suárez College, Valle del cauca.

The transfer of knowledge is understood as a process that fosters access to knowledge learning, which are highly influenced by culture, political hierarchical orders and socio-economic aspects. In this process, the presence of an issuer, a message, a medium and a receiver is indispensable, who will be permeated by different perspectives, forms of communication and interpretations (Palao, 2015; martínez, valdés & vera, 2015). In the case of this research, it is decided to work with the educational community Marco Fidel Suárez headquarters Bolívar who have in their premises a productive system of cut flowers (Heliconias), this is inhabited by peasants, indigenous and Afro-descendant, where some present complex socio-economic conditions that limit multiple aspects of their lives (Duque, Quintero & Duque, 2017; Vega, 2014). It is aimed to develop a proposal of strategic communication of transfer of knowledge of productive systems, with the aim of making knowledge from universities complementary to traditional local knowledge and divulge in the target population, in the search to provide the rural communities with tools in order to enhance their knowledge.
