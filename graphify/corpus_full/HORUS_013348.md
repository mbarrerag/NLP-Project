# About two editions of

The two translations, almost simultaneous, are born of different academic and institutional projects and without any relationship between them. The translation into Portuguese (Whiffen 2019 [1915]) was an initiative of Professor Hélio Rodrígues da Rocha, of the Department of Letters of the Federal University of Rondônia. The Spanish translation of the book of Whiffen (2022 [1915]), instead, was made under the editorial care of Roberto Pineda Camacho, a Colombian anthropologist, who has published extensively on the history and ethnology of the groups of the region of the Interfluvia Caquetá-Putumayo, on which the work of Whiffen is concerned. The translation was carried out by Felipe Cárdenas Arroyo, an archaeologist who has worked in the Department of Anthropology of the University of the Andes (Colombia) and who leads the group of archaeology of the Colombian Institute of Anthropology and History (ICANH). a work very well conceived and built, that is presented as the work of an amateur, but that makes all the effort to position itself as a work of the highest scientific level. But all this achieves it from a series of impostures and deceptions, that the authors of the introduction to Spanish have not wanted to point out, but that are more than obvious.Perhaps the lesson that leaves us a work like that of Thomas Whiffen, appreciated more than a century after its publication, is that these ethnographs of great style are constructed on impostures, concealments and deceptions, supported by a structure of colonial domination, that it is necessary to reveal in order to appreciate them in their just value
