# Palas Athena: model of the Guardians of Plato's Republic

Palas Athena as a guardian goddess of classical Athens was a prominent figure in the daily political and liturgical life of democracy and empire. He was a literary and complex cult character, with male characterization as that of being a warrior. On the other hand, Socrates’ proposal, in Plato’s Republic, of a guard or military ruler of a just city describes women who, by their political action, are masculinized. Thus, we will show how the guards rework the archetype of Palas with respect to reproduction, sexuality, desire and nudity
