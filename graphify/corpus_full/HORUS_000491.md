# SYSTEMATIC CRICTIS IN ORTHODONCIA

The exercise of university education in dentistry has undergone great transformations in recent decades, including processes of information globalization, adoption of new technologies, interaction of multidisciplinary activities and support in platforms of artificial intelligence and engineering of multiple formats of data in health. Thanks to these developments, the student-teacher relationship is framed horizontally, in a dynamic of permanent accompaniment that not only involves the transmission of knowledge, but also, the facilitation of competences of analysis and construction of a criterion with deductive and inferential qualities, which allows the decision-making in the framework of the Odontology based on evidence. This concept that navigates the teaching in the daily life of the pre-graduate and post-graduate classrooms constitutes structurally the backbone of the programs in areas of health. Partly in orthodontics, it must solve the argumentive floor on which, the academic discussions that are robust and give north to the context of the disciplinary training of our students. In this way, it is necessary to include the four pillars that derive from the medicine based on evidence, in the professional teaching of the orthodontic theme, in which the professional perspective is intastic. and international; teaching, research and extension are the bases on which we seek to shape professionals and researchers who are suitable from the ethical and human point of view; giving them a critical conscience that allows them to act responsibly in the face of the requirements and trends of the contemporary world, without leaving aside that teachers should be protagonists in the formation of individuals, leaders in the management of true processes of change. Orthodontics should not be left behind in this task, since, with the recent advent of techniques supported by experts without specific studies in health, educational routes of an extramural nature that do not place themselves in the formal university context and oversized efforts of the industry to enter into processes not very well structured of educational indoctrination; the strict nature, which implies the responsibility of guiding those who have made the decision to work in disciplines related to the care of a patient's health condition, is put at risk.
