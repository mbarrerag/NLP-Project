# Identification and classification of microplastics in water bodies using electronic languages and artificial intelligence

Microplastics in water represent one of the great environmental challenges facing humanity, this due to the possible impacts on the health of people and ecosystems. These materials have been detected in bodies of water used for human consumption and in some foods of frequent consumption such as salt. Their origin is due to the fragmentation caused by interaction with their surrounding environment, such as solar radiation and friction with water, rocks and organic matter, as well as their implementation in the cosmetic and textile industry. Currently there is no technology that allows rapid detection of these micromaterials, although there are different technologies that have proven to be able to detect microplastics such as Raman spectroscopy, fluorescence microscopy or the technique by polarized light dispersion usually require equipment and specialized personnel in laboratory conditions. Additionally, the most commonly used technologies require more time for their analysis due to the high level of human capital required for the collection of samples. In this sense, this project proposal is oriented to propose the development of a portable system that allows the use of electronic languages for the detection and classification of microplastics in the given bodies of water
