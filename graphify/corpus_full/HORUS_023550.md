# Comparative analysis of the implementation of public policy on cardiovascular diseases, in the Departments of Cundinamarca and Atlantic, 2007 – 2017.

Cardiovascular Disease (EC) is the main cause of morbidity and mortality in the country and within its natural history, certain modifiable risk factors are recognized that are part of the group of prevention interventions that the State must guarantee. In Colombia, according to information from SISPRO, changes in the behavior of the EC in the periods of change of national health plans in the country are found, therefore it is necessary to analyze the processes of implementation of cardiovascular health programs in this period of change. This policy is embodied in the period 2007 – 2010 in the PNSP and in the 2012 – 2021 in the PDSP, which are implemented in the territories through the PTS under an established methodology. In the country the EC continues to be one of the main causes of morbidity and mortality, so it is sought through the comparative analysis of public policy, under an interpretative approach, to identify the changes and differences in the implementation processes of cardiovascular health programs in the departments of Atlantic and Cundinamarca, in the period of change of national public policy.
