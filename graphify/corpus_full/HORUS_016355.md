# PREVENTION FOR DEVELOPING FUNDAMENTAL STATISTICAL COMPETENCES IN HEALTH SCIENCE STUDENTS

A curriculum for undergraduate students, under conditions of high academic diversity, sociodemographic and professional interests, is proposed. The curriculum design has been based on international recommendations and a consultation with programme coordinators, from a Colombian public university, on statistical training needs. This curriculum aims to develop a selection of key statistical competencies required for future professional practices in Health Sciences, recognizing the importance in the training process of both the role of the apprentice and the role of the teacher
