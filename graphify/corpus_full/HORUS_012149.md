# HORUS: SCIENTIFIC VISITABILITY SYSTEM FOR THE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

This chapter presents the HORUS system of visibility and surveillance of technological capabilities of the National University of Colombia, as a result of a process of Digital Transformation that optimizes access and availability of data of scientific capabilities of the university. It is an intelligent and secure system that uses technologies such as business intelligence, natural language processing and data mining, which is scalable and adaptable. HORUS is considered a CRIS system (Current Research Information System) for including information of research that is carried out at the National University of Colombia
