# Ecocide, international crime?

The current academy, especially in the global north, has analyzed the possibility of constituting an environmental crime of international type, which has been called ecocide, especially in terms of the legal and political justification of this intention. This document is governed by a problem question: Is it possible to construct an international crime that protects the environment?
