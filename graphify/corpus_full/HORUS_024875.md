# MORPHOPHYSIOLOGICAL MANUAL AND CLINICAL HEART CORRELATION

The Heart Morphology Manual contains all the updated aspects related to anatomy, development biology, comparative anatomy and steps to make a proper dissection of the heart; it also contains the basic techniques of dissection, physiology and pathology. The Manual is intended to serve as a guide text in learning, highlighting the most important structures of this organ; it also contains didactic photos, in which relations, vascular structures and all the procedure carried out in dissection can be observed. Through this technique of dissection, the most relevant structures can be shown, in order to guide, in a practical and applicationive way, the study of the anatomy of the heart, with the advantage of having the respective conventions, to make the learning process easier
