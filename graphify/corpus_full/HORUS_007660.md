# TERTIUS ORBIS RESEARCH SEMILLER - COMPUTER SYSTEMS DISTRIBUTED

•9642; The need to strengthen the knowledge, functioning and structure of distributed systems used by all people today, such as the Internet, allows to develop skills for the design and creation of new strategies in the management of computer resources distributed and interconnected by different means such as air, fibers, copper. Likewise, it provides the skills for the design of data-based applications and to meet the challenges of distribution, replication and collection, as well as the inclusion of heterogeneous devices. •9642; Formalization and understanding of the operation and algorithmy of this type of systems, increasingly common in work, academic and research activities, is essential. This is crucial for the development of computational solutions under these emerging environments, such as the Internet of Things, Cloud Computing, Computerization in the Fog, and Borde Computing
