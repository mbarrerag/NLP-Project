# Evaluate pedagogical intervention identifying difficulties in learning concepts: current, tension and energy

In the educational community of electrical engineering and electronics of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá, research was carried out on the common problems in the interpretation of the concepts of electrical energy, current and voltage. This by evaluating a pedagogical intervention implemented in students of the subject of electrical circuits I during the academic periods of 2022 (semi-versus I and III). The intervention designed to address problems in conceptual learning, focused on microscopic and macroscopic approaches of the key concepts of the subject. The evaluation of the intervention was carried out by means of an inventory of concepts applied to two groups, the experimental group (which received the intervention) and the control group (which did not receive it). Statistically, the results of both groups were compared and quantitative advantages of the experimental group were found.
