# Letter to the reader

Today, with great satisfaction, the Faculty of Mines dedicates the entire DYNA Magazine to the career of Administrative Engineering, as a tribute to the celebration of its 30 years. It is fair to note that the edition of this Journal is due largely to the orderly and dedicated work of a group of students that has already made history under the name of Grupo Imagen. Likewise, the participation and the commitment put by the Engineer Administrator Alfredo Hernando Osuna for the success of this small company, which is to edit the Journal of the 30 years of Administrative Engineering
