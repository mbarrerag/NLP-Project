# Musicotherapeutic intervention in patients with chronic renal failure undergoing haemodialysis: reduced anxiety

This research aimed to perform a musicotherapeutic intervention, seeking to reduce the levels of anxiety presented generally in people suffering chronic renal disease and who are undergoing hemodialysis treatment, contributing to the improvement of the quality of life. This research process was developed under a mixed research approach, with case study design, where three participants in ages between 64 and 76 years, who receive haemodialysis 3 times a week in the hospital of San Cristobal South of the city of Bogotá, D.C, the interventions were performed individually in a context of home care, in face mode. During the therapeutic process 12 sessions of music therapy were performed with each one, with a duration of 30min to 1 hour approximately, being in total 36 sessions throughout the process. These interventions were based on the four methods specific to the musicotherapy, contributing through these, experiences with the music that were of help to face different situations that manifested in the wake of his disease and the emotional states present, such as anxiety.
