# Optimization of the operation of the photovoltaic generation system and demand of the San Andrés Islas departmental hospital

The hospital of San Andrés is a public health institution that attends the population of the island. The energy demand of the hospital is 171.520 kWh in addition to year, which represents an important consumption of energy. To reduce the dependence of fossil fuels and optimize the supply of energy of the hospital, it is proposed to implement a photovoltaic system. This system would generate electrical energy from solar radiation, which would allow to cover the energy demand of the hospital and save natural resources. The general objective of the project is to optimize the operation of the pilot project of photovoltaic generation and the energy demand of the departmental hospital of San Andrés Islands by means of the use of submediation systems for the analysis of consumption and the characterization of the demand. The project was carried out, starting with the diagnosis which was carried out an energy audit of the hospital to identify the main energy consumption and the opportunities of improvement of the hospital. Then in the characterization of the demand, intelligent measurement systems were used to measure the energy consumption at different points of the hospital. On the other hand, the prediction of the demand was used a model of prediction based on the hospital to assess the correlation of the energy of the hospital and the energy of the hospital in the future.
