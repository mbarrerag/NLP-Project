# PROSSOCIAL/PERNICIOUS AFFECTION AND COMPORTMENT

Various research finds that it is generous with those who are socially close in the dictator's game. Prosocial behavior consists of a variety of voluntary behaviors intended to benefit others. On the contrary, pernicious behavior involves behaviors with the intention of harming or harming someone. Affection could be a variable that explains the prosocial behavior and harmful behavior part of the social distance. The aim of this experimental study is to identify if you tend to be generous with whom you want, and selfish and even pernicious with whom you hate. In this research you use a variation of the questionnaire of social discount of Jones and Rachlin that is based on the Dictator's Game to measure the influence of affection on the prosocial and pernicious behavior. 114 undergraduate students from different universities of Bogotá respond a questionnaire of Google Forms. Of the one hundred fourteen, seventy-eight participants remained after filtering out incongruent responses. In the positive affection, participants tended to donate more money with a higher degree of positive affect. In the negative affect, participants tended to distribute more losses to the other with an accelerated increase in the negative affection and there are differences.
