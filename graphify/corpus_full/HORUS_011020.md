# Method for calculating the percentage of water in water-crude emulsions using machine learning.

A study was carried out on the emulsions of water in oil (W/O) in heavy crudes from the images acquired from the laboratory of surface Michael Polanyi, using optical microscopy tools. Interest in the study of these images is created in order to determine the percentage of water contained in an emulsion called by the acronym %BSW (Basic Sediment and Water) without making use of chemical techniques that require investment of resources. To address the solution, a characteristic analysis is used, determining the main variables that can be extracted from the images obtained from the laboratory, such as the amount of particles, size, mass, signal, eccentricity, epsilon, location of the particles, all performed by means of a computer vision method that allows identifying these attributes in the emulsions with an algorithm based on the particle tracking. Once the variables are extracted, the statistical techniques are used to identify trends, correlations and selection of variables for a subsequent use of techniques of supervised machine learning as the mentioned model, Regression of the Neuromalistics and the results of the variable are used
