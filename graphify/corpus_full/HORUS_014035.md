# IMPLEMENTING THE POLICE RESIDUE EXPENDITURE FOR IMPROVING ASHALIC MIXES IN TRANSIT LOW-WIDE WAYS

In Colombia's day-to-day life, the deterioration of road infrastructure and the low habit of recycling have been identified to solve these situations. This product is the type (P) expanded polystyrene, which is easily found in the protection of household appliances. The purpose of the project is to provide an opportunity to extend useful life to this material; in addition, to propose the innovative design and modification of a Colombian asphalt that supports the process of renewal of road infrastructure in the country. Taking advantage of this research opportunity, the behavior of the modified mixture will be analyzed, subjecting it to physical and mechanical tests that can prove the improvement of its properties compared to an unimproved asphalt mixture, and its feasibility in low-transit roads will be verified when the study is focused on a section of the road ring of UN headquarters Bogotá. It should be emphasized that expanded polystyrene has become a critical problem in waste management, so not only structural solution is given but also environmental medium
