# REFERENCE TO THE CEREMONY OF INCICIATION OF INDIVIDUALS: A PEDAGOLOGICAL APPROXIMATION

The Nursing Care Initiation Ceremony is a symbolic moment to inaugurate a beginning, to celebrate a beginning. Hannah Arendt said that every human action has a beginning or beginning and a end or end. Acting in the world, making experience of it, involves initiating new things, taking an initiative, which never starts from the void, but from a legacy or inheritance. For this author is education the point at which we decide whether we love the world enough to take responsibility for it and thus save it from the ruin that, if it were not for renewal, if it were not for the arrival of new ones, would be inevitable. Also through education we decide whether we love our children enough to not throw them out of our world and free them from their own resources, or take away from their hands the opportunity to undertake something new, something that we do not imagine, enough to prepare them with time for the task of renewing a common world. (1, p.208)
