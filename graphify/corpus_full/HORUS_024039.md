# Assessment of a surfactant of natural origin as a dispersant in oil spills in seas

Among the different agents of oil spill control in the seas, natural dispersing formulations are presented as a sustainable alternative to conventional chemical dispersants. This study analyzes the efficiency of a surfactant mixture of plant origin with D-limonene solvent, to disperse petroleum hydrocarbons present in saltwater bodies. This was evaluated with two types of commercial hydrocarbons in Colombia: crude Castilla Blend of 19.1° API and crude South Blend 29,8° API. The data showed an average dispersion effectiveness of 22.83% for Castile Blend and 50% for South Blend crude respectively. These results reveal the potential of natural surfactant mixtures to be an ecological and economic solution in the control of hydrocarbon spills
