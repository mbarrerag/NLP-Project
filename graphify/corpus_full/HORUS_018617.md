# INSILIOUS IDENTIFICATION AND EVALUATION OF PEPTIDES DERIVED FROM FUNCTIONAL COMSTABLISHMENTAL HONGS WITH ANTIVIRAL PONTIAL AGAINST SARS-COV-2

The SARS-CoV-2, the etiological agent of the COVID-19, has generated about 508 million people infected and caused 6.2 million deaths to date according to the World Health Organization. Its pathogenicity is associated mainly with the human respiratory system and other organs. Although there are currently vaccines that have decreased the severity of the disease, the mutations, the appearance of new variants such as Omicron and its virulence, keep it as a priority study. Existing antiviral drugs are limited, so the use of antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) with antiviral potential has been explored as an alternative. MPAs are polycationic sequences of between 8 and 40 amino acids, which allows them to establish a greater area of contact with the white protein of the virus, compared to the small molecules as used in the common antiviral drugs. In the functional mushrooms, in addition to their nutritional properties, their immunomodulatory potential, antimicrobial and anticancer has been confirmed. Therefore, the objective of this work was to analyze the protomes, the functional ones of the antiviral drugs. SARS-CoV-2 (PDB: 7TF8), obtaining favorable coupling scores (-189 and 206) with HDOCKPEP tool and an average cluster RMSD of 0.99 Å and 5.19 Å with CABS-dock, indicating that in most peptides predictions are with high precision and favorable distances for pharmaceutical interaction. 6 peptides with high antiviral potential against SARSCoV-2 S protein were identified from a silic analysis, which allowed the establishment of a methodology for identifying peptides with high antiviral potential and subsequently expected to be able to carry out the in vitro validation
