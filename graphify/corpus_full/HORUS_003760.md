# USE AND MANAGE GROUPS OF THE SOILS OF THE MARENGO AGRO-PECUARY CENTRE

The study area is the Marengo Agricultural Center (cam), of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, located in the municipality of Mosquera-Cundinamarca, which covers 94.55 hectares. Based on the study of Agricultural Survey of the Centro Agropecuario Marengo (cam) of Ordoñez and Bolívar (2014), the physical and chemical properties of the different Soil Cartographic Units, represented by 15 modal profiles, were analyzed in order to establish management groups, taking into account the problems of drainage, compaction, salinization, sodicity and fluctuating water levels. The study area lacks an adequate drainage system that allows the rapid and efficient evacuation of excess rain water, giving rise to stagnations, combined with the deterioration of the surface layer of the soil caused by the trampling of cattle, goat and sheep, in addition to the passage of agricultural machinery. Four management groups were defined, allowing the proper evaluation and characterisation of drainage, compaction and the influence of salts including sodium, which limit the development and productivity characteristics
