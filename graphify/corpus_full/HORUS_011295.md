# Participatory experiences in the Itinerant Museum of Memory and Identity of the Montes de María -El Mochuelo-

This document presents the three written components that make up the final grade work to opt for the title of Magister in Museumology and Heritage Management. The first part contains a conceptual work called ‘Participative experiences in the Museum Itinerant of Memory and Identity of the Montes de María — El Mochuelo —’ which describes the participatory practices of the museum based on its educational model called ‘Mochuelos Cantores’. The second part consists of the critical memory of the stay in the Museum Museum Tequendama, where a diagnosis was made for the formulation of a museum plan of the institution. Finally, the critical memory of the academic practice in the educational area of the Museum of Modern Art of Bogotá (MAMBO) in which I broke down mediation activities and realized a public study in the framework of the exhibition ‘Conversation to the South’. Through these three experiences, some reflections on the culture of participation in museums are proposed. .
