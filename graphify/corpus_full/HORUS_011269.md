# Neoliberalism and Speech: Analysis of Conceptual Metaphors in Two Speeches by Former President Ivan Duque from Cognitive Linguistics

This paper analyzed the political discourse of the former president, Iván Duque Márquez, in two moments of his administration: the first of them, speech of choice of June 17, 2018, date on which he was elected president of the republic; and the second, on August 7, 2021, during the balance of 3 years of government. This from cognitive linguistics, particularly from the theory of conceptual metaphor. This analysis contributes to revealing the neoliberal hegemonic posture promoted in these two discourses through this linguistic strategy, object of analysis, conceptual metaphors. Although the study of conceptual metaphors, is relatively recent, it has been possible to observe, through different research, that the metaphors have been used as a tool, not only of conceptual understanding, but as a means of persuading in the (re)construction of the reality. The metaphors, as Bustos, E. (2000), "displiation of their systematicness in the linguistic plane". That is to say, the metaphorical expressions are not “ling acts” but the manifestation of the conceptual system Bustos, E. . This research aim to analyse the political issue of the former President, Iváv Duque Márquez, at two moments of his administration: the first of those, the choice issue of June 17, 2018, the date on which he was appointed president of the Republic; and the second, the assessment issue issue of 3 years of government proclaimed on August 7, 2021. This analysis contributes to reverse the neoliberal hegemonic position promoted in these two issues through this linguistic strategy, objective of analysis, the conceptual metaphors. This qualitative research was developed from the approaches of cognitive linguistics, specifically through the theory of conceptual metaphor proposed by Lakoff & Johnson (1991). Also, it has been considered the proposal of Rudolph Schmitt (2005) to collect, classify and analyse the metaphorical expressions, through the Nvivo 12 software, that shows the existence of this neoliberal position in the subjects of President Iván Duque Márquez
