# Methodologies Employed in the Study of Protein Interactions and Applications in the Case of Plant-bacteria Relationship

Proteins are the molecular machinery that allows the correct functioning of the cells. To achieve these functions, proteins establish interaction between them. There are several molecular techniques to understand the interactions, such as yeast-two-hybrid, co-immunoprecipitation, GST pull-down, cell localization, BiFC, and FRET. This review presents a general description of these technologies and a brief explanation of how their application expanded the knowledge of the mechanisms activated during the interactions between plants and their bacterial pathogens
