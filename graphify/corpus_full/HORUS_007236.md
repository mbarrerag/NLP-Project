# Use of concrete material for the approach to the infinite concept in primary cycle 2

The research addresses the pedagogical challenge of teaching the concept of infinity in primary education, cycle 2 of the Fontán College in Envigado, Antioquia. To this end, a didactic sequence was designed and applied; where in each of the three structured phases the use of concrete materials was integrated for an effective approach to the concept. After the completion of the application, significant progress was observed in the understanding of the concept of infinity. At the beginning, 57% of the students showed an intuitive understanding of the concept, while the rest was confused or insecure. Subsequently, 100% of the population managed not only to define the infinity, but also to extrapolate it to various situations raised. Of the total of students, 20% reached a conception of the potential infinity, while 80% came to glimpse the real infinity, considered as a more complex and abstract concept. Of this last group, 30% further elaborated, recognizing that there are different infinite, each with its own characteristics that can be compared and studied. .
