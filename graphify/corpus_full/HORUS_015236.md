# Co-construction of body cartography in women survivors of the armed conflict since the emotional turn.

Within the framework of the national historical process of the post-agreement understanding as the moment given to the collective construction of peace, transformation and change, recognition of the victims of the armed conflict, their experiences and truths, identifying women as great survivors of this. This work makes a commitment from the female body as the main axis that is influenced by the brands and paths walked and lived that leads to sharing an individual and collective construction based on the affective turn considering the feelings as elements of knowledge and power of them, varying their form of relationship in the reflection around the lived. A work was carried out with a group of women the Altos de la Florida Soacha, under a methodology of corporal cartography and an analysis from the emotionality from 6 workshops of work of coconut construction, focused on achieving reflection around the categories of body-territory-emotions
