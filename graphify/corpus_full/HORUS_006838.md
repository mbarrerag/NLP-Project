# Product Design/Service for Mothers of Single-Marental Homes in Bogota

This report details the design, development and launch of Nona, a digital ecosystem aimed at supporting mothers of monomarine households in Bogotá. For the purposes of this project, the term "mothers of monomarine households" is used to refer to single mothers, highlighting the family structure they lead in solitary. This platform promotes the personal, emotional and professional well-being of mothers through a mobile application that offers personalized content, guidance in parenting and interactive activities that favor the development of skills in their children. Nona is presented as a warm brand, inspired by the figure of the "nona" or grandmother, symbolizing confidence and guidance, and is based on four fundamental values: Love, Community, Wellness and Harmony. The application includes key functionalities such as professional psychological advice, personalized app, learning paths with access to articles and videos on nutrition and psychology, guided meditations, daily affirmations for inspiration, and a gallery of experiences with activities to realize at home with children, strengthening the family link.
