# Significance of pedagogical practice by students and teachers in isolation

This research seeks to understand the significance of pedagogical practice developed in isolation time during 2020 and the first semester of 2021, since the relationship established between students and teachers. Twelve students of eighth grade and four teachers belonging to the Technical College Tomás Rueda Vargas IED participated in its development. The research has a qualitative approach with an exploratory scope taking into account the recent nature of the phenomenon addressed. For its development, the hermeneutic phenomenological method was used which allows to know the experience of the participants through the reflexive and interpretative analysis of their narratives, for this purpose two instruments of collection of information were applied: a survey and a semistructured interview. During the analysis of the information, the proposed phases in hermeneutic phenomenology were applied which led to know the experience experienced, to describe the pedagogical practices experienced and to describe the significance of the participants to these.
