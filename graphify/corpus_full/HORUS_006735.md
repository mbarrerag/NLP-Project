# Metric thinking as a enhancer of mathematical physical thinking in middle students

The main objective of this research is to explore and analyze the development of mathematical physical thinking and scientific thinking through metric thinking in students of tenth and eleventh grade of the Liceo Arquidiocesan institution of Our Lady (LANS), using contextualized problematic situations in classical mechanics, with an interdisciplinary approach that integrates concepts of physics and mathematics. The research has a qualitative approach with descriptive scope. The methodological design is based on the theory of educational design composed of four phases: a preliminary bibliographic review, the design and application of the activities of diagnosis, strengthening and evaluation of the didactic sequence and teaching intervention, the observation of the educational environment and the analysis of results. The results describe the level of performance shown by the students in the different processes of mathematical physical thinking and the progress shown in the identification of variables involved a physical phenomenon, the formulation and validation of hypotheses, the representation of phenomena in different systems of semiotic representation, and the explanation of physical phenomena according to the behavior of their variables and the analysis of graphic and algebra models. The results describe the level of performance shown by the students in the different processes of physical-mathematical thinking and the progress presented in skills related to the identification of variables involved in a physical phenomenon, the formulation and validation of hypotheses, the representation of phenomena in different semiotic representation systems, and the explanation of physical phenomena according to the behaviour of their variables and the analysis of geographic and algebraic models. In general, there is evidence of a significant development of physical-mathematical and scientific thinking in the participating students, as well as an improvement in their attitude
