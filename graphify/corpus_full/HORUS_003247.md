# RESOURCE OF ENERGY IN-SITU IN-TUBS WITH AL-SI12 CELLULAR FOR APPLICATIONS AS PASIVE ENERGY AMBASSORS.

Using a modified removable fill infiltration technique (IRRM), 304 stainless steel tubes were in-situ fillers with a cellular metal and their energy absorption capacity was evaluated by subjecting them to quasi-static compression and flexion tests in three points. For the manufacture of the cellular metal an aluminium alloy with 12% silicon was used, as base metal and particles of average size 2.8 mm of high purity marine salt, such as space holder or removable fill. The mechanical tests were carried out in a universal machine universal machine MTS® System Corporation, with a capacity of 1000 kN load.
