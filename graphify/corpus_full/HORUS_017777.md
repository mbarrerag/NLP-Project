# Evaluation of the National Development Plan 2018-2022: pact for Colombia, pact for equity, for the sports sector according to the human development approach

The actual document does an analysis of the “Sport and Recreation Sector for the integral development of individuals, for the social cohesion and coexistence”, of National Development Plan (NDP) 2018-2022 commitment for Colombia, Commitment for equality, to do the study the focus on Human Development is used, this focus is one of the pillars of public policy of the sector as it is the Decanal Plan of Sport 2009 – 2019. In first instance a presentation of Human Development Index (HDI) is done, its components and the different measures that exist for Colombia at national and regional level, following, mentioned components are related with the ambits of society where sport generate a positive impact. Following the examination, the congruence that is between the diagnostic done by the NDP and other public policies with the objectives that were followed during the four years, is revised; as well as the strategies to implement, the targets that are looking to achieve and the vision for 2030 planted by the government. The document takes that study of congruence of the objectives and the policy proposals that propose and review the coherence with the focus and impact on human development through an analysis of correlation between the levels of the HDI and its components with the results of the implementation of strategies that were designed on the Planation document. Having into account the results of the contrast of coherence of the document and the correlations between the HDI and the application of the programs, recommendations of public policy are done for the sector, the national Sport System and the Sport Ministry, for close with conclusions of the investigation.
