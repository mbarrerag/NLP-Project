# The effect of internal control on financial reporting: a systematic review of literature

This paper summarizes the direct relationship of internal control over financial information, through the systematic review of literature through qualitative methodology and the method of deductive approach. Thus, the purpose of this document is to analyze the theoretical contributions of the last 10 years of the IFAC member jurisdictions that have adopted IFRS, understanding that having IFRS are already considered representative. The results obtained show a positive association of internal control over financial information, since it guarantees the principles that have taken force since the implementation of IFRS. However, as regards the results by adoption of those standards, there are still gaps arising from differences between developed and emerging countries, between voluntary or mandatory processes, regulators, institutional reforms and local frameworks of each jurisdiction. Simultaneously, a bibliometric work is presented with the evidence supporting the rise of research in these topics. .
