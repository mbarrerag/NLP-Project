# Correlation between food addiction and levels of arachidonic acid in obese adults after nutritional interventions

Summary Introduction: Endocannabinoids are a target in the treatment of obesity and are produced from essential fatty acids, the derivatives of linoleic acid act as agonists of the cannabinoid receptors type 1 (CB1), also, the derivatives of linolenic acid exert effects of antagonists of such receptors, therefore it is proposed that modifying the dietary consumption of omega 3 and 6 fatty acids could modulate the activation of the endocannabinoid system, which could be favorable for people with addiction to food, considering how this system promotes the activity of dopaminergic pathways that alter the addiction to psychoactive substances.
