# INTRODUCTION: LINGUISTIC DIVERSITY AND THE RESEARCH OF DANGEROUS LANGUAGES

This introductory article formulates points of discussion on the research of indigenous languages, Creole languages and marginalized dialectal varieties in the world. It promotes the thesis that the threat to linguistic and cultural diversity requires profound transformations of research activity that lead to a position of commitment to the defense of endangered languages. An overview of linguistic diversity under threat is initially presented in the world and in Colombia. Some advances in documentation and linguistic description and in ethnoeducation in Colombia are detailed. Subsequently, the challenges of linguistic documentation and the opportunity to strengthen the links of cooperation between sociolinguistic research and linguistic documentation and description are discussed. Finally, some celebrations of linguistic diversity have become relevant in recent years and articles of this special issue are introduced
