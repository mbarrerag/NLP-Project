# SIX DECADES OF THE BIOLOGY DEPARTMENT OF THE UNIVERSIDAD NACIONAL DE COLOMBIA: A LEGACY OF EXCELLENCE AND ACADEMIC COMMITMENT

On December 16, 1965, the Universidad Nacional (UNAL) created the Department of Biology as one of the most important educational projects in the biological sciences in the country. The Department marked the beginning of ab new academic structure at the university and established the foundations of the discipline that it maintains as a legacy and a national reference to date. To recall the history, since the creation of the Department of Biology, is part of the Faculty of Sciences according to the agreement 61 of the University Superior Council. Later, in 1971, the University Superior Council approved Agreement 16 (of February 17) granted autonomy to the Institute of Natural Sciences of the Department of Biology
