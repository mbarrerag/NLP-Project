# Application of Artificial Intelligence to Investment in the Securities Market, through Variable Income Assets

2. Summary The use of Artificial Intelligence (IA) techniques in finance is currently of great importance in the efficient processing of large volumes of financial information through increasingly accessible computational resources. Additionally, AI has proven to be highly efficient in improving the speed and veracity of predictability in the financial field leading to maximizing the profitability of investment strategies. Artificial neural networks (Artificial Neural Network) RNA represent a more recent evolution of AI and are a powerful computational tool with great advantages to be exploited in the investment sector. RNA have the ability to perform learning routines comparable to the processes of rationing and human thinking through its algorithmic architecture. RNA have been successfully applied in several fields of finance, as the prediction of evolution of foreign exchange prices of the Forex market and investment in mutual funds. However, the study of the practical utility of RNAs in more dynamic and unstable sectors such as investments of variable income has been little or non-existent according to the current literature. This literary vacuum shows an opportunity for generating practical knowledge strategies for RNA
