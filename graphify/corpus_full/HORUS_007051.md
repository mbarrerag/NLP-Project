# Lighting design for Av. Cra. 60 between Cl. 57 and Av. José Celestino Mutis with focus on the safety of public lighting inspections

This project focuses on the lighting design for a 1 kilometer stretch of Avenida Carrera 60 in Bogotá, which includes a roundabout. The main objective is to improve the visibility and safety of the track during night hours, complying with the normative standards established by the RETILAP. A detailed analysis of the characteristics of the track, including the dimensions of sidewalks, cycling and separator, has been carried out to determine the appropriate levels of lighting. For this design LED luminaires were selected, such as the model Flut 280 G2 14000 8 NW 97 W From the supplier LAMP and LED STREET ZD 229 150W NW 7P from the supplier Sylvania, due to its energy efficiency and ability to provide uniform coverage. The configuration was chosen to minimize the number of necessary luminaries and reduce the energy consumption. In addition, a risk assessment has been carried out for the outdoor lighting inspections, with the aim of implementing appropriate safety measures to protect the staff and equipment.
