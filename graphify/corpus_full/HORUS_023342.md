# MANAGEMENT OF THE OBSOLESCENCE OF DIGITAL ASSETS IN THE ELECTRIC SECTOR

Given the differences between physical assets and IT assets (also called digital assets), a methodology is proposed to manage the obsolescence of digital assets, which seeks to support decision-making based on the risk of obsolescence. The objective is to identify the probability and impact of obsolescence on each asset to determine the actions to be executed. Some steps of the methodology were implemented in four digital assets that are used in the Colombian electricity sector. The methodology can be used and adapted by any company to manage IT assets, whether hardware or software
