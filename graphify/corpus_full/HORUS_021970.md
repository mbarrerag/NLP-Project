# Counting on polyminoes associated with Catalan Words.

Catalan words are words about natural numbers whose growth is restricted. Catalan's set of words is listed by Catalan numbers. This paper presents the generatric functions for polyminoes associated with Catalan words according to the area, the semiperimeter, the interior points and the degree of vertices. In addition, the results obtained for recurrence relationships and exact formulae for the total number of these parameters are shown
