# UREOLITIC BACTERIES AND THEIR POTENTIAL ENGINEMENT MECHANISMS

Lead contamination (Pb) has become a growing concern given its multiple sources of emission, and the risk it represents for health and the environment due to its toxicity and permanence in the environment. Recently, the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms, MICP has been explored by its acronym, using ureolytic bacteria (capable of hydrolyzing urea) to immobilize the metal precipitating it as a low soluble carbonate and thus reducing its bioavailability.
