# Enhancing logical thinking processes in elementary school students from gamification. A contribution from the socio-epistemic approach

In this paper, it seeks to strengthen some processes and sub-processes related to logical thinking in primary school students in an official educational institution in the municipality of Pereira, Risaralda. This is framed in a qualitative paradigm of descriptive scope, since it is aimed at contributing to the development of logical thinking based on the implementation of a sequence of activities based on the socio-episthemological theory of mathematics and mediated by learning gamification processes. Among the results obtained, progress is highlighted around i) the identification, analysis and implementation of efficient solutions to problems; ii) the acquisition of capabilities to automate solutions through the correct use of algorithms; iii) the ability to reason and solve problems in numerical and literary contexts; among others . This paper looks to enhance some processes and sub-processes related to logic thinking in elementary students in an official educational institution in the municipality of Pereira, Risaralda
