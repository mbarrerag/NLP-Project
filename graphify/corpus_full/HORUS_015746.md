# Design and implementation of a personal navigation system oriented to the movement of users with visual impairment in closed environments.

The navigation in closed environments can become complex, especially for people with visual impairment. To assist navigation in closed environments have proposed indoor navigation systems (SNIs) that involve technologies such as WiFi, Bluetooth, RFID among others, which are different from the usual Global Satellite Navigation Systems (GNSS) because these are inefficient because of the loss of the signal that causes the structure of the buildings. On the other hand, to assist the navigation of people with disabilities, solutions have been proposed that involve the recognition of obstacles and spaces through cameras and sensors, which is costly to implement. Therefore, the exploration of methodologies for the development of SNIs that achieve a balance between implementation cost, performance, accuracy in the location and above all that provides useful information to people with disabilities. The purpose of this research was to propose an internal navigation system (SNI) oriented to users with visual disabilities, integrating the standards of the OSC IndoorGLL and CityGML, for the construction of semantic models and 3D representation.
