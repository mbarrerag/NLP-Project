# Proposal to analyse the current measuring infrastructure components of electricity networks as an enabling axis of energy management in the Non-Interconnected Areas of the country

The provision of electricity service in the Non-Interconnected Areas (ZNI) of Colombia presents challenges that compromise the continuity and security of electricity supply to these areas. The high dependence on fossil fuels results in high costs immersed in the acquisition and transport of fuel, while it implies the emission of large quantities of greenhouse gases to the atmosphere, which is aggravated by the fact that many non-interconnected areas are close to or within natural national reserve areas and parks. In addition, the lack of access to a reliable and affordable source of electricity affects the quality of life of the inhabitants of these areas, limiting their capacity to carry out economic, educational and social activities. In this context, the implementation of measuring infrastructure becomes a tool that can help to overcome some of these challenges, by allowing greater control over resources, and allows to improve the decision making as to the management and use of electrical energy. The overall objective of this research is to evaluate the potential of implementing measuring infrastructure as a tool to enhance the management of energy in the municipal headlands of the non-interconnected areas of the country.
