# EVA ASSESSMENT FOR ELECTRONIC TEAM RENEWAL EQUIPMENT

The oscilloscope is a measuring equipment widely used to visual signals, analyze electrical phenomena that collaborate in the verification, purification and solution of multiple field problems. This instrument allows the visualization of electrical signals over time and also the measurement of the respective characteristics of these signals (e.g.: ampl, peak to peak value, average value, period, frequency).In its first approaches to the oscilloscope, the s-students tend to have fear in their handling, because its front panel offers a wide number of controls, to which it is an instrument to which there is no wide access to it, as it can be a digital multimeter, and which, due to its cost, prefer to limit its handling to avoid damage. Given that it is of great importance to promote in the students of engineering cart-carre-ras such as electronic engineering, electrical engineering, electrical engineering, mecatronic engineering, networks and telecommunications and other other uses, as well as skills to obtain the correct visualization and also perform the measurements with the oscilloscope and interpret the results of those measurements, a virtual learning object (OVA) was developed that allows students to manipulate, electrical object to use other object (OVA) that allows students other alternatives available, continues with the results of validations with engineering students, the comparison between results of each university, continues with improvements and projects in the future to continue with collaboration between uni-versities and is concluded with the conclusions of the experience realized
