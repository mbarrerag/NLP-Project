# Case study of non-conventional CORPORAL AMENDMENTS: A CALL FOR NEW WAYS OF UNDERSTANDING PSYCOSOMATIC MALESTAR

A case study of men with extreme and non-conventional body modifications. In-depth interviews were conducted, qualitative categorical analysis, with interpretation of data from psychoanalysis. A category was identified around which modifications and the body transformation project revolved. They included: 1. Pain in psychosomatic experience; 2. The image of itself in disidentification, and; 3. Self-denomination and vital project. In the context of the subject of research, it is identified that when it came from a small village, there were no tattoos or perforations in the community or in the family, since it was not the right thing for the time or the place, it is then that the desire for transgression of culture, transgression of the other, is evidenced in the subject, for it begins with perforations in the face and scarification in the back, communicating with his body, positioning himself as a subject, so that the desires and demands of those who surround him impact him, acting in a contrary way, performing an interior reflection after a time on his image and tastes, thus creating his project of extreme body modifications
