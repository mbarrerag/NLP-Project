# Embroidering experiences: Independent bookshop management pathways in Bogotá

This research focuses the focus on the study of the current publishing industry Bogota, in particular in the books and independent bookstores of Bogotá; for the nobleness of its labor objective and the conjunctural that becomes its mission in the life cycle of the book: uniting, object with industry and consumer. Waiting to answer What does it mean to be independent bookseller today in Bogotá? and how have the changes in the publishing industry in the last 16 years been experienced from independent libraries? Contextualizing the birth and integration of the publishing industry from the appearance of writing, going through industrialization to the technological innovation and paradigm change that has experienced in the twenty-first century. Putting in dialogue the works that in recent years have presented a dialogue between the main actors of Latin America and Colombia. The methodology employed is of qualitative type and developed from a social and historical analysis of the context of independent libraries, the methodology is constituted in the elaboration of narratives from the technique of conducting interviews.
