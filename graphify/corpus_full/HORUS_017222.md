# CHAPTER 10 RELATIONSHIP INTERVENTION BASED ON THE ADVANCEMENT FOR CHILDREN 6-12 YEARS

The objective of this chapter is to guide and provide tools for the development of a relational intervention based on attachment with children between 6 and 12 years of age. During the chapter, it will deepen the skills and knowledge necessary for the proper application of relational intervention by relational facilitators. In addition, it will emphasize the main differences between intervention for children between 0 and 5 years of age and 6 to 12 years of age, exploring some strategies adapted for this last group
