# EFFECTS OF COVID-19 ON PHYSICAL AND SPORTFUL ACTIVITY: A BIBLE-ETRIC STUDIO

The aim of this study was to carry out a bibliometric examination in Scopus® on the research carried out regarding the consequences of the COVID-19 pandemic on physical and sports activity, finding the vulnerabilities that people have had as a result of the spread of the virus. The review identified the trends in the analysis of the impact of the COVID-19 on the physical activity and health of people. A collateral impact was found on the confinement that affects the health of people in the physical and emotional. The difficulties resulting from sedentaryism, weight gain, changes in eating and sleeping patterns and diseases associated with unhealthy lifestyles. In opposition, efforts by the medical and sports sector were also analyzed when using telemedicine and social networks to support physical practice, the paulatine return to exercise and professional sports activities. Key words: Physical exercise. COVID-19. Physical and sports activity
