# Work in care and pedagogical practices in an alternative school of education. An analysis from the pedagogy of care

This research project was written with the aim of characterizing the pedagogical practices that we teachers and professors perform and with which we accompany the academic processes of students from an alternative education college. These practices can be analyzed in the light of the theory of the pedagogy of care. Throughout this research methodology I did an autoethnography of non-academic spaces, such as recesses, corridor conversations or talks in the rectory between teachers and students. I also made focus groups with teachers and teachers, and with some students. And, I had a semi-structured interview with the academic director, Lisa, and with the rector, Jeff. The results obtained led me to conclude that a semi-structured interview with the secretary was also necessary, due to the way in which Lisa, Jeff and the students spoke about her and her work. her of. The data obtained allowed me a critical reflection of the care work behind the education of care implemented at the school. This allows me to show how this care work is feminized
