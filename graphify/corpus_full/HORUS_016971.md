# Energy Management Systems in Latin American Industry: Case Study Colombia

Summary The increase in energy demand, due mainly to the dependence of industry and the transport of fossil fuels, has led to its extensive use and an increase in emissions to the atmosphere, generating a high level of environmental concern in the global sphere. It is then evident the importance of a proportionate consumption of energy resources and the decarbonization of the economy. Therefore, the implementation of energy management systems (SGEn) within industries becomes meaningful, seeking to promote the rational and efficient use of energy. Therefore, as a goal of this work is presented a bibliographic review on the integration of SGEn into industry, highlighting the main barriers and motivations for its implementation, emphasizing the protagonic role of management within companies to convert these programmes into sustainable initiatives over time. In addition, an analysis of the Latin American panorama, especially the Colombian one, on the integration of mechanisms to improve energy efficiency.
