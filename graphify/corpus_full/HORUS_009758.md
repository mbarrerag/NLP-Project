# THE INTERSEED MODEL OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY APPLICABLE TO THE TRANSFORMATION OF LARCHITECTURE IN INVESTMENT PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The commitment to the Intersedes Model at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia has promoted a transformation in the Subsystem of management projects and institutional support to operate accordingly and contribute to introducing its philosophy and routines as a network organization, while increasing the level of effectiveness and contribution of the projects to the fulfilment of goals of its Global Development Plan and providing an environment of greater collaboration between headquarters and areas, as well as a greater deconcentration of operations. This has required intervention of several dimensions progressive way and using techniques of continuous improvement, which have considered mainly: the conceptualization of the new architecture, deployment, leadership, transparency and timeliness of information, monitoring and acceleration of processes. As a result, a new way of managing investment projects has been obtained, with better informed decisions, greater transparency and institutional trust
