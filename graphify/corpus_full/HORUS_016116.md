# Yellow letters. Encounters with identity

Amarillas Letters. Encounters with identity, is a reflection on the recognition of identity that is woven through a series of artistic actions motivated by the encounter with a family archive inscribed in a historical era. The archive is initially composed of letters and these become the basis of a personal archive that has been structured during the research through the relationships that have been established between artistic practice and sports practice. .
