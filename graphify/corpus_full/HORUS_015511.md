# INFLUENCE OF THE ADDITION OF HUMIC ACIDS IN THE RETENTION AND MOBILITY OF METALS FISHED BY THE SOUND

The increase in heavy metals in soil can have harmful effects on human health and ecosystems, as they can accumulate in living organisms and generate carcinogenic, teratogenic, genotoxic and/or mutagenic effects [1]. As remediation measures chemicals such as lime, EDTA, DTPA have been used, among others, which have negatively affected the physicochemical and biological properties of soil. Countries such as Spain, Italy, Holland, Poland, Brazil and the United States have been focusing on the use of substances of natural origin, as organic substances (humic substances), which minimize those effects, obtaining promisitory results [2, 3]. Humic substances (SH) are heterogenous mixtures of macromolecules as regards their structures and chemical composition, which depend on parent biomaterials and the environmental conditions where they are developed [4]. They are composed of humic acids (AH), pulvic acids (AF) and humin.
