# 20th Century Music in Colombia from Gabriel Uribe's Perspective as an Interpreter

This work is a study of the artistic life of Gabriel Uribe García (1910-89), based first on the review of archive materials, press publications, programs and personal documents. Secondly, it examines the repertoire that was dedicated to him, which he performed in radio programs and that he recorded on albums that contain mainly Colombian music. It is intended to situate and understand his artistic trajectory, in the environments in which he worked together with other musicians of his time. Also studied are the groupings that integrated and the repertoires of the moment that oscillated between academic music and popular music and led to the introduction of repertoires that initially generated resistance. In addition, the appropriation of new technological resources such as radio and albums with the intention of contributing to the history of the interpreters in the Colombian field. .
