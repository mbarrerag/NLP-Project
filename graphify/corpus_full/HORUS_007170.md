# Data generation strategies for the application of adaptive learning analytics

Quality education is essential to reduce inequalities and improve society. The analytics of learning, for its part, is a discipline that through techniques of data analysis seeks to understand and improve learning and teaching. Within this discipline, the analytics of adaptive learning personalizes the teaching according to the actions of the students. This thesis focuses on designing data generation strategies for the application of analytics of adaptive learning in hybrid courses. After a systematic review of the literature, knowledge gaps were identified and the elements necessary for the applications were characterized, defining considerations and strategies CTP (Context, Theory, Practice), structured and complemented by own contributions and practical examples. Subsequently, specific strategies of data generation were developed with technological, pedagogical and communicative elements, which were integrated into a prototype that provides a guide for teachers on how to implement these strategies in the design of courses. Finally, validation was carried out through a short course, showing a correct flow of data and being complemented by an illustrative application in two sets of data. and complemented with original contributions and practical examples.
