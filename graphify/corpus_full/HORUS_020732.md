# Documentation and study of the opera production "The Last Day of Francisco Pizarro" by composer Moisés Bertran

This research proposes an analytical description of the opera montage ‘The last day of Francisco Pizarro’ (EDFP) by the Colombian nationalised Catalan composer Moisès Bertran. From interviews with the main actors (composer, conductor and stage director) a documentary survey is made on the development of EDFP montage, carried out in the León Auditorium of Greiff of the National University of Colombia and the Julio Mario Santo Domingo theatre in Bogotá, Colombia.
