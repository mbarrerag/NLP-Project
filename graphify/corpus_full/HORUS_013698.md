# Methodological proposal to facilitate intra-entrepreneurship processes in mipymes through the theory of realization

There are conditions in companies that represent a barrier to the implementation of intra-enterprise activities. These activities are of the utmost importance because they pursue innovation, which facilitates organizations to be more competitive in the current market. These barriers could be overcome thanks to the use of the theory of realization, theory part of the understanding of the characteristics of entrepreneurs and the dynamics of entrepreneurship to explain the process of decision making of successful people in the entrepreneurship. However, it does not contemplate scenarios of intra-enterprise and it becomes necessary to make adjustments or complements from this theoretical basis to make it more usable in entrepreneurship scenarios within companies. The aim of this thesis is to propose a methodological tool that complements the theory of realization to guide it to the overcoming of barriers faced by the mipymes in implementation processes of activities of intra-enterprise. For this purpose, a Positive Strategy is proposed that is composed of three phases: Indagation, Ideation and Implementation.
