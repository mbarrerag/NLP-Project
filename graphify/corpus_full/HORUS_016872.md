# FIRST INTERNATIONAL MEDICINE AND EQUINE REPRODUCTION CONGRESS: BUILDING AND MANAGE OF EMERGENCY

From 9 to 13 August the project was carried out First International Congress of Medicine and Equine Reproduction: Address and Management of Emergencies, organized by the professor Claudia Cecilia Valderrama Martínez, teacher attached to the Department of Animal Health. The workshops precongress in the Clinic of Great Animals of the National University, the precongress day in charge of AO, in the auditorium of the National Hemeroteca and the conferences of the congress in the Virgina Gutierrez Auditorium of the Faculty of Human Sciences. it was supported by the companies gabrica, mare, biotronitech, magistral vet, laboratorial erma, laboratorios lt s.a.s, natural veterinary, cidagro, over, vitaequinos, laboratorio i-vet, proconvet, medicina détiva, colegio royal plus e importadora jl.
