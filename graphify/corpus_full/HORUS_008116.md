# MIGRATIONS, PRE-HISPANICAL AND COLONIAL LIVING IN THE VALLE DE ABURRÁ

This work presents in a succinct way the relationship between migrations and pre-Hispanic and colonial housing in the Aburra Valley. From the archaeological records, the chronicles and literature, it is shown that both the type of the pre-Hispanic cabin and the colonial house of courtyards have an invisible symbolic vertical structure that alludes to the dimensional interconnection between the underworld, the superworld and the mortal world, to give the home a spiritual and numinous sense that initially gains magical meaning in the indigenous environment, and in the colony acquires transcendental aesthetic features
