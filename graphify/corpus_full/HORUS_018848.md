# Real Group petrophysical analysis to generate a conceptual hydrogeological model (sector between San Martín, Aguachica and Gamarra).

The Royal Group is a geological unit that has been objective of hydrogeological studies by its classification as a multilayer aquifer of regional extent. It presents thicknesses between 1000 to 1200 meters and important facial changes. In this study a petrophysical analysis of the Real Group was carried out, addressing the following properties: Volume of Arcilla (Vshale), Total Pyosity (PHYA) and Effective Pyosity (PHIE), discriminated by intervals to select those with best hydrogeological characteristics, in such a way that they allow to improve the current conceptual hydrogeological model of this unit. The study area is located in the Middle Valley of Magdalena (VMM) Sedipular Basin and includes the municipal headlands of San Martín, Aguachica and Gamarra; and it was carried out in the framework and with support of the MEGIA Project, which has a database of hydrocarbon wells, Pneumal and Photness, which includes information of 53 wells for analysis, which included: (1) classification of wells suitable for the use of petrophysics, (2) general correlations using the Gamma Ray (GR), generating data and data for the control by the Real Group, and Pneums, water storage, and (2) most observed intervals have regular rating. Additionally, it was found that the best method of estimating Vshale is linear GR, this due to its similarity to the description of gravels, and that the porosities (PHIA and PHIE) of the intervals rated as good are in appropriate ranges compared to theoretical values
