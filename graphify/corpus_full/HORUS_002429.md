# THE HISTORYERS AND THEIR DISCIPLINATION: THE CASE OF NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA, HEADQUARTERS

The National University of Colombia (unal) was seen from its beginnings as a space for the teaching of method, or the school of method, within the framework of a positivist conception. Also, since 1868, it had in its own being the humanistic formation and teaching of the history of the homeland and the universal history. It was then that the idea of strengthening scientific education was predominant, starting from the plans of studies of its six schools, among which was the School of Literature and Philosophy. It was there that the teaching of history and other subjects specific to the field of humanities was implemented, which were conceived as an important foundation for other courses, Law, Medicine, Natural Sciences, Engineering and Arts and Crafts, but also as part of a curriculum aimed at forming bachelors who would contribute to strengthening the identity and historical consciousness of Colombians (Ramírez and Londoño 2017, 56-57)
