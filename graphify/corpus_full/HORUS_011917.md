# The surfaces in the Postgraduate Building of Human Sciences of Rogelio Salmona. Structural principle of spatial relations

The thesis approaches the surface as an element that structures spatial relations in the building of the Postgraduates of Human Sciences of Rogelio Salmona. As a result: continuity. The study investigates the operationality of the surfaces and the relationships they produce, in order to explain how a building that can be understood by parts, at the same time it can be perceived as continuous. In the analysis, the study shows the continuity from opposites that become similar in a spatial sequence where the clear is combined with the interior, the interior with the exterior, the top with the bottom, the part with any part, to such an extent that the notion of part ceases to exist. Finally, the study closes with the spatial definition of a building that is understood under three conditions: the non-limit, product of the floor and the wall that operates and extends from space to space; the simultaneity that is explained from the capture of an instant, involving space and time; and the ambiguity as a result of the identity of one or several spaces that merges with the building. .
