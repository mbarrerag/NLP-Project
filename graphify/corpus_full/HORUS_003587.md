# PREPARATION OF A FERTILIZER DISOLUTION NPK 15:15:15

The composition of NPK fertilizers, also called ternaries, is expressed by means of three numbers indicating the proportions of the three nutrients: the first number refers to nitrogen, the second to phosphorus and the third to potassium. However, these figures do not correspond directly to the percentages of each element, since nitrogen is expressed as N2, phosphorus as pentoxide (P2O5) and potassium as oxide (K2O).9 The numbers indicate the percentages in which elemental nitrogen and phosphorus and potassium salts are held. This practice seeks to clarify the calculations and concepts associated with the preparation of this type of fertilizer, since their importance remains valid and they still have extensive use as part of our agricultural practices
