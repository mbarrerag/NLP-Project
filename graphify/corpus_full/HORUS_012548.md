# Ultrasound-assisted extraction of phenolic compounds from uchuva chalice (Physalis peruviana L.) and formulation of liposomal systems as a method of encapsulation.

The by-products generated from food agro-industry represent a source of phytonutrients such as phenolic compounds, which can be explored as bioactive in the natural product industry, generate added value to the residues and contribute to the measures for the mitigation of environmental impact. However, when the compounds are extracted from the source matrix, they are highly susceptible to degradation by physiochemical phenomena. In this sense, this research studies the ultrasound-assisted extraction of phenolic compounds obtained from the uchuva calyz (Physalis peruvviana L.) and the formulation of liposomal systems as a mechanism for protecting the antioxidant capacity. In this regard, this research studies the study of the ultrasound-assisted extraction of phenolic compounds obtained from the uchuva caly of chuva (Physalisalis peruvvivial L.) and the formulation of the flavonol Rutin by HPLC.
