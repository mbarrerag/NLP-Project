# Assessment for learning: a model of training for experimental chemistry in secondary education

This paper documents a process of formative evaluation whose aim was to generate a change in the perception of students of the mean of the IEM Integrated Liceu of Zipaquirá, versus evaluation. The methodology has a qualitative approach and research participatory action, which developed in three stages: the first diagnosis, the second process and the third analysis. Thus, the diagnosis was performed and analyzed through structured interviews, then a model of evaluation for the learning of experimental chemistry (MEPAQ), a structured evaluation system of formative participation for the implementation in the classroom, which was applied with the theme of “The Matter” and finally the efficiency of the model was analyzed by contrasting the diagnosis. In this study, 35 students of the tenth grade participated, the treatment and analysis of data was carried out with the help of the ATLAS.ti.23 program and the analysis of the discourse.
