# Digital marketing model design to position independent coffee brands in Caldas department

This document aims to present the design of a digital marketing model for the purposes of positioning independent coffee brands in the department of Caldas. To achieve this, the research built a series of phases that addressed, first, the identification of the characteristics of the independent coffee market in Caldas, the analysis of the digital marketing strategies employed by these brands, and finally the identification of key factors that impact on the planning of brand positioning strategies in digital environments. This work seeks to provide a solid and effective framework for independent coffee brands to stand out and compete in the current market .
