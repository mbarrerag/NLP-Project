# DISULFURATION

Colombia is among the countries with the highest production of coffee, generating 14.8 million bags of 60 kilos and 7.2 billion pesos of income in 2019, constituting one of the main lines of the Colombian economy. The process of production of coffee begins with the collection of the cherry, which is depulpated in order to obtain an almond free of shell, thus generating 2.25 tons/ha of pulp in the country. Per 100 kg of fruit of mature coffee, you can obtain 39 kg of pulp, 22 kg of mucilage and 39 kg of parchment coffee. The pulp constitutes approximately 28% of dry weight of the cherry and contains 0.51 to 1.13% of caffeine. Most of the residues of the coffee do not have an adequate treatment, often the medium gas, the presence of the liquid gas and an imidazole ring. In particular, the imzoloid ring can be used for the production of bioactive compounds such as caffeine. The structure of the caffeine contains two substructures, a pyrimidine ring and an imidazole ring. In particular, the imzolid ring. According to the literature, the LIs base imidazole present a selectivity in the removal of sulfur and nitrogenous compounds present in fuels.
