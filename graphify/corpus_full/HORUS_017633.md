# Virtual music therapy program for the treatment of work stress in a sample of district school teachers

The impact of a virtual music therapy program on the coping with work stress was evaluated in a sample of teachers of a district school in Bogotá. Participants voluntarily agreed to participate (n=5) and received virtual interventions of music therapy, based essentially on the methods receptive, improvisational and compositional, over two months. An exploratory, pre-experimental study of a single group was carried out, with measurements PRE and POS of quantitative type and qualitative elements. A qualitative analysis was applied throughout all sessions, evaluating aspects such as the sound-musical production, emotional expression and verbal discourse. This is a standardized psychometric test that is validated for the Colombian population. In addition, a qualitative analysis was carried out throughout the sessions, evaluating aspects such as the inopportional production, emotional expression and speech. The results indicate that there is a possible relationship between the virtual music therapy program and the increase in the use of active coping strategies, such as the solution of the inopération and the inopération.
