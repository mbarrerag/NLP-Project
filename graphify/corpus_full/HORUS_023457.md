# 3¿-4¿-5-TRIACETHYL-3-7-DI-O-METHYL QUERCETIN, FLAVONOID OBTAINED BY SEMI-SYNTHESIS WITH VASODILATOR PROPERTIES

Given that natural flavonoids are subjected to extensive first step metabolism effect, their potential as pharmacological agents are an issue despite their beneficial effects in health [1]. Therefore, new flavonoid compounds obtained by synthesis could be a useful strategy for therapeutic purposes. This work shows that 3¿-4¿-5-triacethyl-3-7-di-O-methyl quercetin (TADMQ), obtained by semi-synthesis [2], displays vasodilator properties in isolated aortic rings from Wistar rats
