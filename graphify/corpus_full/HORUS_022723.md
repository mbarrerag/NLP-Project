# Effect of inoculation with Rhizophagus irregularis and phosphatized fertilization on the local community of mycorrhizae mycorrhizae formators

ABSTRACT The Mushrooms Forming Arbuscular Micorizas (HFMA) are crucial microorganisms in agricultural soils because of the positive effect they have on the mobilization of one of the fundamental nutrients for plants such as phosphorus (P). The available P contents in soils of Colombian Orinophagus are low, so fertilisation with phosphate is indispensable in commercial crops such as cassava. The aim of this work was to study the effect of phosphatized fertilization and inoculation with Rhizophagus irregularis (HFMA model fungi), on the resident communities of this group of fungi. The diversity of the HFMAs was described from the spores present in the rizosphere of yuca plants maintained under traditional agronomic conditions. The richness and diversity of the HFMA communities also affected by the interaction of the factors analyzed. In terms of structure of the HFMA community in the rizosphere of the yuca plants, this result in the abundance of the additional Glomus and Acaulosporousspora genera are detected
