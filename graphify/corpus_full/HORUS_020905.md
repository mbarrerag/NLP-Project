# Legal protection of unpaid care activities for persons in dependent situations

This proposal for a research project focuses on those and caregivers of persons in a situation of dependence, since their work is not paid and neither do they have measures of labour protection, social security and care in accordance with their care activity. The purpose is to reveal the roots of the lack of protection, the incidence of these deficiencies in the protection of the needs of the caregivers, the measures proposed by the Colombian State to address the situation, and the challenges and challenges that exist. Currently the care work is not taken in part, nor as a priority by the Colombian State. On the contrary, in the households, usually women, they take them without receiving any remuneration or access to social security. Thus, the phenomenon of ageing of the Colombian population will bring with it the progressive increase of the dependent persons up to the year 2100. Likewise, when the caregivers assume jobs different and additional to those of care, they also have opportunities to reconcile between the working and family life. The health system and its possibility of accessing a pension or other social protection measure. Finally, it is important to analyze the actions of the health system and, in general, of the Colombian State in the care and labour protection of dependent caregivers in order to consolidate criticisms, proposals and reflections in the light of national and international legislation, comparative cases and reports from international entities
