# Understanding citizen road behaviors from open data and artificial intelligence.

Road accidents remain the second leading cause of death in adults worldwide, with pedestrians and motorcyclists being the people with the highest mortality rate and injuries in traffic incidents. This is why this work aims to generate a methodology based on artificial intelligence for the identification and automatic quantification of road behaviour, both for pedestrians and for motorcyclists. For this, open data was used in order to obtain relevant information that allowed training of an object detection model with which sufficient characteristics can be extracted to measure whether the actions of people on the road are adequate or not. Once the detection model was trained, whose sensitivity and specificity was more than 90%, it was found that the behavior of pedestrians in the study area tends to be inadequate, since more than 70% of these fail to meet the traffic rules when they are located by where they should transit the vehicles.
