# A GENERALIZATION OF GROUPS: THE GENERALIZED DIGROUPS.

The digroup structure has been proposed as a generalization of the group structure whose tangent space is governed by a Loday or Lebniz algebra. We study a further gener- alization of such structure, the g-digroup, in which we do not demand that the inverses with respect to any given bar unit are necessarily bilateral. In this talk we will define g-digroups, describe some of its properties and some group related results. work joint with luis alberto wills toro, josé gregorio rodríguez nieto (universidad nacional) raúl velásquez (universidad de antioquia)
