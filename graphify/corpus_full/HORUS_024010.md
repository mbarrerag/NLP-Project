# TO DEVELOPMENT WITH A PROGRESSIVE AND EFFECTIVE TRIBUTARY SYSTEM

Despite the many modifications that have focused on the short term, adjustments and even reforms to the Colombian tax system, over the last decades, tax collection in relation to GDP has not been the most favorable has been one of the countries, worldwide, that has the lowest productivity and efficiency in tax collection.The low productivity can be explained by the incorrect administration of taxes, an inadequate planning, the constant evasion and circumvention of taxes, the abundance of preferential treatments, excessive deductions and exemptions on the income of the capital, as well as by the growing informal economy.The regressivity of the tax system in Colombia has generated a great inequality, the coefficient of Gini is, in itself, very high before and after taxes, practically remains unchanged, as happens in very few parts of the world.In general, in the OECD countries, the coefficient of Gini is not the product of a great tax payment, that is to say that these countries do fulfil their function of redistribution of income and wealth. As in the Colombian case, this means a low tax burden, reflected in a low tax burden, insufficient to meet the State's obligations in education, health, housing, roads and other social programs. In the Colombian case for 2017 with figures from the World Bank, the tax burden was 15.2 %, almost half of European countries. This document analyses alternatives to increase the tax collection, fulfil the redistributive function of taxes by making the progressive system, for this purpose alternatives are proposed in them taxes on the income of natural persons, legal persons, taxes on property, inheritance, cadastral update and other elements that make the system more efficient and just
