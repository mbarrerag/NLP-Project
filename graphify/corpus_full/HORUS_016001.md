# Walking food, sharing knowledge, discussing its defense: interpretative analysis of the formulation of intercultural public policy on food and nutrition security for the Jiw and Nükak peoples of San José del Guaviare (2016-2020)

This document presents an interpretative analysis of the formulation of the intercultural plan of food and nutrition security for the Jiw and Nükak peoples of the municipality of San José del Guaviare carried out in 2015, which is a reference in the practical and theoretical discussion of the differential approach in Colombia. To this end, it begins by constructing a genealogy of such a concept and its contributions in the public policies of the country, for example, by proposing the inclusion of intercultural knowledge dialogue with indigenous peoples in its formulation. Then, and following the methodological proposal of Dvora Yanow (2000), it addresses the narratives and local knowledge that allow to establish the contextual frameworks, artifacts and interpretative communities that give meaning to the political-food reality analyzed.
