# telework: Your administrative model

Telecommuting has experienced significant growth in Colombia, especially in response to the Covid-19 pandemic. However, it is acknowledged that its successful implementation depends on how telecommuting models are oriented, the planning processes, and the constant attention to the needs of telecommuters in their workplaces. The main objective of this research is to propose a management model for telecommuting in Colombian companies, based on successful experiences from other countries and private sector companies. To achieve this, a qualitative methodology is used to understand the telecommuting phenomenon through document analysis, texts, reports, and the experiences of individuals and groups related to this work modality. The research process comprises four fundamental stages. Firstly, successful telecommuting experiences in other countries are explored, identifying criteria for success in developing models that have yielded positive results for companies. Next, the Colombian regulatory framework for telecommuting is compared with countries that have a higher penetration of telecommuting workers. This comparison allows considering key elements to guide the proposed model. In the third stage, a management and performance monitoring model for telecommuting in Colombian companies is proposed. This model addresses fundamental aspects, such as worker training in ICT handling and organizational adaptation processes to a new work culture. Additionally, it aims to improve planning, implementation, and evaluation of this work modality to ensure worker well-being and the fulfillment of corporate objectives. Finally, to validate the proposed management model, a survey is conducted among experts in the telecommuting field. The feedback obtained from these professionals will allow for adjustments and improvements to the model, ensuring its effectiveness and suitability for the Colombian work context. Initial research results highlight the advantages of telecommuting, such as worker autonomy and cost savings for companies. However, challenges are also identified, such as handling technological tools, cultural adaptation, and work-life balance. It is crucial to address these challenges to ensure the success and sustainability of telecommuting in Colombia
