# Validation of deformable image registration based on photos for dose accumulation in external beam radiotherapy.

The purpose of this project is to study and characterize a protocol for evaluation of deformable image registry (DIR) in the context of dose accumulation to quantify intrafractional anatomical changes in patients with external beam radiation therapy. The patient cohort included 10 studies of non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), each composed of 10 images of computed tomography (CT) taken on the breathing cycle. The full exhalation image was selected as a reference for each patient and the IRD was performed with the ANACONDA algorithm, included in the treatment planning system (TPS) Raystation. Four configurations were used for each registry and the resulting deformation vector field (DVF) was evaluated using the manually drawn contours as a reference standard. Metrics available in the body cycle and CTV consisted of the common DICE coefficient of similarity (DSC), the mean concordance distance (MDA), the Hausdorff distance (HD) and the patient correlation coefficient were defined on the Pearson contours and CTV.
