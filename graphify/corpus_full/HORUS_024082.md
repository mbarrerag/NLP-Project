# State and youth, a look at rural youth in the department of Arauca and state actions to solve labour problems in response to migratory phenomena

Rural youth are actors with high vulnerability, this reflected among many variables to the emergence and progressive thickening of migratory phenomena in search of improving their living conditions, either by attraction to cities or by expulsion from their territories. In Colombia, these migratory processes are framed and represented mainly by the lack of job opportunities, representing 42.3%. Very marked variable at the territorial level, where, the youth unemployment of the department of Arauca reaches 29.7%. As a State of law, the work is conceived in a central place of triple quality (as principle, duty and law) and the entrepreneurship as a political strategy to amend the demands that the society demands, as well as the science of the State, the analysis of public policy implemented here according to the cyclical model proposed by Knoepfel et al. (2006) allows to evaluate the conditions of the youth, the actions from the State to the labor problems in the period 1991-2019 and the Ordinance 019E of 2015 in the stages of definition of the public problem and programming. Thus, the main problem of rural youth of the department of Arauca is framed in the little, null or insufficient participation of youth in political, social and economic spaces
