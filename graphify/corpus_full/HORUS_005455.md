# FIFTY-FIFIETH ANNIVERSARY OF THE DEATH OF TWO MITS: PICASO AND NERUDA, SOME COMMON POINTS

This article, accompanied by reproductions by the author, of the originals of Picasso and Neruda that illustrate some texts, pays tribute to these two artistic personalities of the 20th century when they turn 50 years of their death. The text deals in a succinct and analytical way with the confluence of graphic and poetic works to discover the aesthetic closeness and the genius power of their creative expressions that, as universal masterpieces, become fully valid in the midst of the conflictive contemporary world panorama
