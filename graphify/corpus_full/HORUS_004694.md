# “A SITE THAT DIXED CALLED” — A METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL FOR THE ANALYSIS OF THE TOPOPONIMIA AND RECONSTRUCTION OF THE MEMORY OF THE TERRITORY OF THE BOGOTA SABANA IN THE XVIth CENTURY.

In this paper, the advances of the research will be presented: "The territory of the savannah of Bogotá from the word: an indigenous construction and Spanish reconstruction from the toponymy of the sixteenth century", specifically in what has to do with the methodological proposal elaborated for the interpretative study of the toponyms. This proposal is focused on a contextual analysis, which implies the location of the toponym in the current territory, characterizes it geographically, translates it from the linguistic-missionary sources and relates it to the archaeological data of the place. To this end, it was used to the colonial documentation of the second half of the sixteenth century and of the first decade of the seventeenth century, specifically to the processes associated with land disputes between the owners, visits of the authorities of the Royal Audiencia to the peoples of Indians, and processes of demarcation of the indigenous reservations. In these documents not only are found a large number of muisca names, today completely forgotten, but also indicate their geographical location according to the spatial references of the period.
