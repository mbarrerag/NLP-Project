# Analysis of the application of the new regulatory accounting framework of the public sector regarding historical and cultural assets in Colombia.Case study

Historical and cultural assets differ from other public sector assets, they are unseizable, inalienable and difficult to replace.However, the recognition of these assets in the financial information of public sector entities has been considered.Several countries have specific standards for the accounting recognition of these assets, but they are heterogeneous in terms of their recognition, identification and measurement.Thus, problems arise regarding historical and cultural assets such as their definition and characterization, identification and recognition in the accounting regulatory framework, and the economic-accounting problem of their measurement and valuation.In Colombia, Contaduría General de la Nación, the regulatory entity for public sector accounting, issued the latest framework for the Public Accounting Régimen, which presents substantial changes in relation to the recognition, measurement and disclosure of historical and cultural assets;Therefore, it is necessary to identify the implications that this regulation had on entities.In view of the above, this master's thesis aims to analyze the implications and effects of the recognition, measurement-valuation and dissemination of historical and cultural assets, with regard to the treatment prescribed in the new framework of the Public Accounting Régimen established by the Contaduría General de la Nación.Office of the Nation, through the case study of Banco de la República. The qualitative research analyzed the Bank's financial and non-financial information and applied semi-structured interviews to members of Contaduría General de la Nación and Banco de la República.As a result, it became clear that the bank recognizes historical and cultural assets as fixed assets and historical and cultural heritage.The entity applied evaluation methods such as market values ​​for some assets such as precious metals, art market reference prices, acquisition costs, technical evaluations and symbolic valuation.According to the above, it is clear that it is possible to recognize them, that there are measurement and valuation mechanisms that entities can use.However, accounting challenges still exist.Intangible historical and cultural assets are not yet considered for recognition in accounting, additionally, academia has shown that accounting is also required to account for the safeguarding and protection of cultural heritage.Heritage assets are different from
