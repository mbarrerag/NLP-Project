# Development of processes associated with metric thinking in the context of metalworking in students of medium technique.

This work aims to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with metric thinking based on the approach and solution of problem situations in the context of metalworking, with students of grade 10 and 11 of the Francisco José de Caldas Technical Institute of the city of Manizales – Caldas. The work is framed in the qualitative approach with descriptive scope, whose methodological instruments are dynamized supported by Design Based Theory. The instruments are of 3 types, diagnosis, advance or entrenchment and final or challenge. Among the results achieved, advances in relation to skills in the comprehensive use of the measurement (object, magnitude, size, measurement of size), refinement of the measurement processes, optimization of the measuring instruments, critical perspective on the social background of the measure (internal and external), the ability to pose and solve problems, among others
