# STUDY OF THE QUALITY OF ESSENTIAL OILS ISLATE FROM GENDER CYMBOPOGON PLANTS IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

In the AE of C. martinii, geraniol (84.1%), geranyl acetate (8.1%) and linalool (2.3%) were identified as major components. In the AE of C. Nardus, citronelal (41.1%), geraniol (16.8%) and citronelol (15.7%) were identified. The extraction yields obtained for C. Nardus and C. martinii were 0.75% and 0.33% respectively. The physicochemical properties of the AE of palmarrosa and citronel obtained were found within the acceptance ranges established by international quality standards. The variation in chemical composition was due to differences in geoclimatic conditions and cultivation of experimental plots, but all AEs met international standards ISO 4727 for C. martinii and ISO 3848 for C. nardus AE
