# Minimality of the D-groupoid of symmetry of a projective structure

In this article we study the Kummer D-groupoid, which is the grouping of symmetries of a meromorphous projective structure. We give necessary and sufficient conditions for its simplicity, in the sense of not having non-finite sub-D-groupoids. The condition we find turns out to be equivalent to the strong minimality of the nonlinear schwarzian equation and non-integrability by means of liouvillian functions of the linear schwarzian equation
