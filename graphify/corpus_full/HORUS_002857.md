# INPAINTING IN ARTWORK: RESTORATION OF THECORONATION OF THE VIRGIN USING MATHEMATICAL METHODS,

This approach involves taking a digital version of the original painting and applying mathematical methods to fill in the damaged regions of the image. Three mathematical methods were employed for the restoration: The method of Criminisi et al., which is based entirely on exemplar-based inpainting; another method, proposed by Bornamann and M ̈arz, is focused on efficiency, and relies on diffusion; and the third method, proposed by Le Meur et al., combines diffusion with the exemplar-based inpainting. These methods were chosen for ease of implementation and their respective strengths in handling texture synthesis, smooth region propagation, and maintaining the structural coherence of the artwork. The goal was to assess the effectiveness of these methods in restoring the integrity of the artwork while preserving its visual and artistic qualities. Preliminary results indicate that while each method has its strengths and limitations, the combination of diffusion and exemplar techniques shows better results in detail preservation
