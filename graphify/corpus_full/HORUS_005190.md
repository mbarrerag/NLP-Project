# PORTABLE DEVICES FOR EXPERIMENTAL LEARNING OF BASIC FLOW CONCEPTS IN TUBERS WITH EFFASIS IN PRESURIZED RISK SYSTEMS

Currently, pressurized irrigation systems are promoted as as a spray and microirrigation, more efficient compared to the irrigation area that consumes large volumes of water. These new technologies are part of the measures of adaptation to climate change, and require complement of innovative education strategies. An experimental team was developed for teaching flow in pipes articulated with the SDG 4 in order to contribute to an inclusive education. The device designed and assembled corresponds to a light structure, manual operation and easily transportable. It consists of a cylindrical tank of 50 cm of height in whose base are connected pipes of interchangeable PVC, either horizontal or with changes of level, that in turn have the best way of operating and managing systems of pressurized irrigation, and simple mechanisms of capacity and resilient to the climate change.With the device have implemented both hydrostatic and hydrodynamic practices to promote the teaching, learning and reinforcement of basic concepts in pipes, with focus on promoting the best operation and management of systems of pressurized irrigation, for a more sustainable, equitable and resilient agriculture to the system
