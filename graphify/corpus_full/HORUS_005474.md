# CHAPTER 5. ASSISTANCE-INDUSTRY DISEASES WITH SKIN MANIFESTATIONS.

Inflammatory myopathy is a heterogeneous group of diseases affecting multiple organs and systems, including muscles, skin, lungs and joints. The five most recognized types of inflammatory myopathy are dermatomyositis, overposition myositis (including antisynthetase syndrome), immunomediated necrotizing myopathy, sporadic myositis of inclusion bodies, and polymyositis.
