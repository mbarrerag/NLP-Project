# COLEOPTERA CATALOGUE OF THE NATIONAL TAXONOMIC COLLECTION OF INSPECTIONS LUIS MARÍA MURILO (CTNI), CUNDINAMARCA, COLOMBIA

The taxonomic collections of arthropods are, per se, reference systems for the understanding of natural and agricultural entomodiversity. The Colombian Corporation of Agricultural Research (agrosavia) shares with the scientific community and the producers the information associated with their collections. With the aim of generating a informative catalog on specimens and taxa of the order Coleoptera of the National Taxonomic Collection of Insects (ctni) Luis María Murillo¿ of agrosavia, the specimens were organized based on international curatorial standards, and included their cataloguing and systematization. They were cured 22,883 specimens, which are preserved in Ward model boxes, Cornell drawers and compactor cabinets. For the order Coleoptera, the collection represents 61 families, 805 genera and 1,609 species. Likewise, the identifications found correspond to the levels of family (100%), gender (35%) and species (65%) of the genera.
