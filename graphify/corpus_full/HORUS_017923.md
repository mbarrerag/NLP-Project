# TERRITORY TECHNOLOGY-DIFFUSION CARTILLA ON NEW TECHNOLOGIES AND INNOVATIONS IN THE BPIN 2020 project000100698

Extrusion of food: It is a process where ingredients such as flours, cereals, vegetables are taken and mixed with water to make a dough. This dough is introduced into a machine called extruder (illustration 5), which has one or two screws that presses the dough and pushes it through small holes in the end part of the machine, applying during the high temperature process in short time (Riaz, 2000). Products obtained from the equipment can be snacks, breakfast cereals, biscuits and many other foods
