# Family meanings around art as a reference for socialization in young people in the sector of Tiguaque, Usme, Bogotá

The present is a research commitment that seeks to understand the way in which socialization is developed in young people who are part of artistic and community spaces, specifically in the sector of Tiguaque, in Bogotá. In order to understand the meanings that families attribute to community art as a potential reference of socialization in young people from popular and peripheral sectors, a methodological strategy was chosen, which allowed a deeper understanding of the problem, which was based on semi-structured interviews with families of young people who participate or have participated actively in artistic and community processes. Likewise, the methodological framework was complemented by the experience of more than 9 years of the author in the territory accompanying those processes. In general it was found that the participation of young people in artistic and community spaces has influenced both the process of socialization of these/s and the perceptions that their families have on the community and on the art.
