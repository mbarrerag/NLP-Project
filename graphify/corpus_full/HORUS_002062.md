# Rebel, imagination and morals. The identity of the Barranqueño oil worker (1923-1946)

The aim of this thesis is to analyze how the oil workers in Barranwecabermeja built their identity between 1923 and 1946, through a series of cultural and political devices, nuanced by language and the construction of meaning. To this, we will relate the living and working conditions of the oil workers in the enclave economy established by the Tropical Oil Company; we will identify the strategies of moral control used by the workers to produce union and consensus; we will demonstrate the way in which the workers constructed their image based on a process of imagining the other; and we will analyze the political and class behavior of the oil workers, as well as the elements that constituted it. In light of these elements, it is possible to avigorate the formation of identity in the Barranqueña working class, from a theoretical-methodological period different from the predominant ones.
