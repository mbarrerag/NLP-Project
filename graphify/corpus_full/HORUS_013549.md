# Assessment of the relationship between nutritional status and spectral response of potato culture (Solanum tuberosum L.) for the estimation of nitrogen and potassium content

The rational use of fertilizers is a measure that seeks the sustainability of the productive system of potato in Colombia, since this can reduce the environmental effect and the costs associated with the fertilisation of the crop.The objective of this work was to evaluate the use of spectral information to estimate the nutritional status of Solanum tuberosum L, Bacata variety under different levels of fertilization, for this purpose was carried out a test in a commercial batch of potato in Soacha, Cundinamarca, Colombia. A design was established in repeated measures for an arrangement in generalized blocks and at random using the times as an intra-subject factor, with eight treatments with variation in nitrogen (N) and potassium (K). The nutritional variables were measured with selective ion sensors Laqua ® and chlorophilic meter SPAD.
