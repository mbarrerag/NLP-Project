# POTENTIAL ECOTOXICOLOGICAL IMPACTS OF COAL BOTTOM ASH: PRELIMINARY STUDY

Some industries in the world generate impacts to the environment due to the use of coal as an energy source. Coal combustion increases the presence of fine and ultrafine coarse particles suspended in the air, and there is a relationship between morbidity and mortality of people due to exposure to the contamination of these substances [1]. Health risks to people exposed to this type of activity have been evidenced [2-4]. In addition, ash residues are disposed of on opencast becoming a public health and environmental hazard [5]. Coal bottom ash (CBA), is a waste produced during firing coal; in this sense the aim of this study was analized the potencial ecotoxicological impacts of this ash comes from a Colombian industry. CBA was characterized through chemical analysis and scanning electron microscopy (SEM), transmission electron microscopy (TEM) and optical microscopy, where particulate matter of environmental interest PM10 and PM2.5 was observed. Exposure to this type of particulate matter can cause adverse effects on human health, especially respiratory diseases. In addition, a bioassay was applied with Vigna radiata, which indicated a more noticeable reduction of radicle at concentrations of 50% to 100% of CBA. Therefore this sample (CBA) could be compatible for agricultural use.
