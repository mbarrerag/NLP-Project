# DORIS SALCEDO

Since the 1990s, Colombian sculptor Doris Salcedo has used unique objects and materials of memory in her sculptures, with the purpose of intervening and re-textualizing them, relying on listening schemes of victims of violence as a methodological practice of life. This practice of Salcedo is central in her work, because thanks to it she not only manages to document and visualize testimonies that allow to polemize the truths expressed by various political actors, although empathically felt by the victims themselves and their relatives (in the case of murdered or disappeared), but also connect those objects and materials with life. In the last, Doris Salcedo masterfully achieves, through his methodological practice of life, that the spectator of his work connects with the life of the victims through his unique objects and materials of memory
