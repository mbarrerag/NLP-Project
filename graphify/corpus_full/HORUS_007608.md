# Development of a collagen and isotretinoin complex of possible application in topical treatment of acne vulgaris

Acne vulgaris is the most common chronic skin disease and has an incidence of 85% in the juvenile population, is generally manifested between 12 and 25 years, although in special cases it may initiate or persist at older age (Tuchayi et al., 2015).The mechanisms of the disease are not fully known, although they have been associated with genetic factors, hormonal changes, sensitivity to foods with high glycemic index and stress (Ogé et al., 2019).The pharmacological treatment for acne can be performed topical or systemic via antibiotics or with retinoids.However, antibiotics such as tetracyclines have generated resistance by C. acnes. For this reason the use of retinoids is preferred, with the most effective isotretinoin (Kmiec et al., 2013). However, for this drug the use has been reported teratogenicity, mucocutaneous disorders, photosensitables and alterations in the lipid profile of patients when administered systemic route (Palencia et al., 2013). The contribution of the topical, but also the adverse, This type of complex has been developed, for example, from ferritin transporting doxorubicin (Bellini et al., 2014) or daunosamine (Ma-Ham et al., 2011) or from thermal shock proteins with doxorubicin (Toita et al., 2013). However, information about complexes with isotretinoin is not known, which gives some degree of novelty to this research proposal. Initially, the complex has been modeled in silica using a free access software to identify the junction poses between type I collagen and isotretinoin and on this basis to determine the binding affinity. The favorable results obtained, which suggest the possible formation of the complex, opened the door to take advantage of the experience in the preparation and characterisation of complexes of the active type -amylose, achieved in the pharmaceutical group of Gideca research, and in this way continue to meet the two specific objectives in which the financing achieved in this call would be reversed; essentially to obtain and characterize the complex and (ii) to formulate the complex obtained in a useful way of application pharmaceutical to Colombia
