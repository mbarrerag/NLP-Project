# INFLUENCE OF ARCHILLED MINERALS IN STABILITY OF LOW-PLASSED SOUNDS WITH ALCALINALY ACTIVATED CEMENTS

The diversification of construction technologies to achieve sustainability has highlighted the potential of soil stabilization in different types of engineering uses, so that materials from earth movements or excavations can be functionally used. This stabilization process can be physical, mechanical, and/or chemical, where in the latter, by adding cementing agents, soil properties are improved, optimizing their mechanical and durability behavior to external agents. This is why different types of cementing agents are currently incorporated, usually normal Portland cement and/or lime. Seeking to implement sustainable technologies that exploit local resources and residues, while providing eco-efficient solutions to the needs of the most remote regions, this article gives to the importance of clay minerals in the stabilization of soils with alkalineally activated cements (CAA) as a sustainable technology, so that the main effects on clay soils of low plasticity can be identified, which are the results of the research project FABRICATION OF CEMENTOS DESILABLES OF THE FUEL VALUTION OF THE PLACE AND THE FACILITY OF THE FACILITATION OF THE FACILITY
