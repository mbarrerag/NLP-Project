# PARTICULATES OBTAINED FROM THEOBROMA CASCARA CACONE AND EVALUATION OF INDEMBERED USE IN COSMETIC PRODUCTS

The shell of the cocoa macorca (CMC) is approximately 90% weight of the fresh fruit, when presenting poor management of residues are generated foci of propagation of microorganism generating diseases in the cultivation of cocoa, so it becomes a necessity to find applications for this byproduct. One possible application of the CMC is the obtaining of particulate material for use as a raw material for cosmetic exfoliating products, for this purpose it is necessary that the material obtained from the CMC have certain characteristics that allow it to be performed as cosmetic exfoliant.
