# Current state of training in pediatric anesthesiology in Colombia

Resumen Surgery is an indispensable part of medical care, the surgical component and the anesthetic fundamental within the health system in countries of all levels of development. Annually, approximately 230 million surgical procedures are carried out worldwide, with pediatric surgery being a percentage, since around 85% of children require a surgical procedure; however, the provision of surgical and anesthetic services safe from the training and training of actors involved in child surgical care in middle- and low-income countries has been absent from the overall health discourse. The development of the field of anesthesia in pediatrics faces multiple challenges: it is a relatively new specialty, presents clinical challenges related to anatomical, physiological, psychological and procedural differences, as well as there are few training opportunities with the consequent limited number of specialists in trained and qualified pediatric anesthesiology. For Latin America, the possibilities of applying to a specialization in pediatric anesthesiology are limited and specifically for Colombia it has not been possible to create the subspeciality of pediatric anesthesiology, so that it should encourage the formal and informal training in this discipline, with the aim of providing safe care
