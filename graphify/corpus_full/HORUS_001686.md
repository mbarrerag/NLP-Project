# Urban connection: link of vitality at the intersection of 72nd Street with 96th race, Bogotá D.C.

The present Final Work of Mastership (TFM) proposes an urban intersection for the intersection of Avenida Calle 72 with Carrera 96, in Bogotá, starting from an idea of Norman Foster that synthesizes the concepts of cohesion, vitality and urban connectivity. This idea in turn is synthesized in the concept of Conectoma Urbano. The intersections, as fundamental elements of urban road structure, are characterized by prioritizing the traffic, ignoring the importance of pedestrian space and urban coexistence. The current intersection of the 72 with 96 reproduces perpetual this idea, limiting the function of a road to the connectivity vehicle, contributing to the discontinuity of urban activities. It is crucial to transform these places where people can walk, interact, and prioritize the space for pedestrians, thus promoting urban coexistence, social interaction, pedestrian security, and environmental sustainability. The new proposal transforms the urban site into an inclusive and multifunctional place. From the concept of “Conectoma Urbano” and an initial idea of the importance of Calle 72, emerged the site of experimentation for the idea.
