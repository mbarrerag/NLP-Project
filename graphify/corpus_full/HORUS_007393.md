# Corporate tax burden in Colombia for the period 2020-2021: measurement from average and marginal actual tax rates

The tax mix that characterizes the tax structure in Colombia shows a marked inclination towards corporate taxation. This work quantifies the tax burden assumed by companies in the country for the years 2020 and 2021 from estimates for the marginal effective tax rate (TEMT) and the average effective tax rate (TEPT) in 18 subsectors of the economy. The results, incorporating some methodological refinements, show in both indicators a fall in the levels of corporate tax during 2021. Likewise, the persistence of high levels of dispersion, which is due to the disproportionate use of tax benefits by some sectors. Although the management of the tax subsectors differs between the TMT and the TEP, the results agree that the tax sectors and electricity present reduced levels of tax pressure, a situation that did not vary during the years studied. In addition, the sensitivity analyses for the TMT allowed to conclude that the tax policy design should consult the final effect of the changes introduced on the tax rates, since the interactions between the parameters are not unequivocal and often involve tensions between efficiency and equity criteria. .
