# Outdoor lighting design for sports complex

The present grade work consists of the design of the outdoor lighting of the sports complex in the municipality of the Patios, department of Norte de Santander. The sports complex brings together multiple sports diciplinas in the same place, which includes sports scenarios such as skating rink, synthetic football court, tennis court, multifunctional courts, cycling, parking spaces for vehicles, bicycle parking lots and pedestrian trails, which allow generating an adequate space for the integral sports training of the athletes of the region and in turn generate an adequate space for recreation and recreation for the municipality. The document presents the lighting design for the outdoor areas that comprise the sports complex, validating the lighting levels required for the sports scenarios in the training, recreational modality and the external areas as they are pedestrian paths, cycling, parking taking as reference the Technical Regulation of Lighting and Public Lighting (RETILAP) resolution 40150 of 03 May 2024 and the standard UNE-EN-12193 of April 2020 . x, validating the lighting levels required for the sports scenarios in the training mode, recreational mode and external areas such as pedestrian paths, cycle paths, parking lots taking as reference the Technical Regulations for lighting and Public Lighting (RETILAP) resolution 40150 of May 3, 2024 and the UNE-EN-12193 standard of April 2020
