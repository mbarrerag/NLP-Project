# Obtaining anthocyanin microcapsules extracted from Corozo (Bactris guineensis) in a chemically modified polymeric starch matrix.

The microencapsulation was chosen as a strategy to protect the anthocyanins extracted from Corozo (Bactris guinnensis) against environmental factors, due to the low stability of these compounds. The wall material used was starches from natural sources such as achira (Canna edulis) and yuca (Manihot sculenta), which were chemically modified, by wet way, with acetic anhydride (acetylation reaction). A fraction enriched of anthocyanins (F) was obtained from the accousus extract of B. guineensis, whose composition was analyzed by CDC and HPLC.
