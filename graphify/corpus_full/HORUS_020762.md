# Analysis of concrete behaviour with tyre crushing added to non-structural elements

The excessive growth of the population and the lack of awareness of production and consumption have triggered a problem of generation and management of solid waste. A clear example of these solid waste in Colombia are used tyres, which do not easily degrade and thus generate sources of pollution in the main cities. It is necessary to seek solutions to these problems from different sectors, in this case, from the construction sector, the purpose of this work is to analyze the use of these tyres used as a partial replacement to the aggregate for hydraulic concrete in order to reduce the great environmental damage caused by the poor management and disposal of these waste. .
