# Collective Equipment. Impact on Social Welfare in Housing Megaprojects VIS/VIP 2000-2020. Case Study: Mega-Project Hogares Soacha

The collective equipments, are spaces where social needs are satisfied; their location, proximity, use and coverage, produce social well-being and allow people to establish relationships of appropriation with their habitat. Due to the historical housing deficit in Colombia, since the 1990s, normative reforms have been promoted for the production of social housing that have resulted in its massification, with an opposite effect in providing the necessary equipment to meet social needs.
