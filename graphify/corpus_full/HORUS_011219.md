# LUIS BARRAGAN. THE SEARCH OF THE EMOTIONAL ARCHITECTURE

The great masters of architecture have understood in this a greater art. They have intended, through it to excite and elevate the spirit of the one who inhabits it. The Mexican architect Luis Barragán is part of a select group that in the first half of the 20th century undertook that search. His architectural work has been associated, through various analyses, with the use of the resources of Mexican popular architecture, such as the color and treatment of surfaces. In general, he is made to propose an austere and severe architecture inspired by tradition. Also the treatment of light has been a reason for recurrent analysis. To a lesser extent, the gardens have also been a cause of study of his work. Elements that, combined, make an evocation to silence, mystery and solitude. This research has been used of these characteristics and deepened in them, to then associate them with a superior purpose intended by Barragán: architecture as art that excites, that elevates the spirit, that gives to the human being moments of transcendence. It is understood here that the search and process throughout his life, aims to elevate the architecture to a higher condition of art. Sublima
