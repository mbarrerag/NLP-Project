# ♪ Children of renoir ♪ ♪ Children and art in Mariana Robles ♪

This essay shows, in general, what the presence of the child or childhood means in art and literature. Secondly, it presents the writer-artist Mariana Robles and her book The Children of Renoir, subscribing her work in what we might call ecfrastic literature. Finally, she comments on some poems and presents some reflections on them from an ecfrastic and hermeneutic perspective
