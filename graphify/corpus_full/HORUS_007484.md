# Classification and determination of lithofacies using Artificial Intelligence methodologies in the Royal Group of the Magdalena Middle Valley Basin from Electrical Records

The MEGIA Group has been carrying out during the last years different types of studies that help to study and understand the behavior of aquifers present in the Basin of the Middle Valley of the Magdalena (VMM).One of these studies was the petrophysical, where different formations and faces were classified litologically within the unit of interest; this work is an extension of such petrophysical studies, using methodologies of Artificial Intelligence (IA) among them different algorithms of Machine Learning (ML) that help to classify and predict different properties and characteristics of the basin. In this research, these techniques were used to replicate classical methodologies in petrophysics, to predict and model missing curves in the wells, and to classify and predict lithofacies, which is the main objective of this project. For the petrophysical calculations, the conventional equations were used, calculating then the porosity, volume of clay and water saturation in the wells, besides the soil of the Gamma Ray (GR) and the Photoelectric Factor (PEF), besides the necessary for the calculation of the structural mechanisms (DT), calculating each of the poresity, density (RHOB) and the soil).
