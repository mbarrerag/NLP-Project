# URBAN MODERNIZATION IN THE INICIES OF THE XXth

At the beginning of the 20th century, the Colombian city was characterized by being the scenario where the assembly of technical advances arrived from abroad took place, with a transformation of the thought of the elites that sought to achieve an urban and cultural modernization simultaneously.For the urban transformation, innovations from other cultures were used that strengthened the role of the engineers in the implementation of systematized models for the construction and in the introduction of industrialized materials that responded to new parameters of efficiency and development, showing the technicality as the way to introduce progress in the city. As for the cultural modernization, this was reflected in the new ways of inhabiting that were propitious from the new urban spatialities. These processes that identify each urban moment can be understood in a diacronic way as the historical traces that become cultural symbols and give meaning to each place; and in a synchronistic way the application of the general work of the first years of the 20th century, the styles and trends that represent the idea of modernization for each generation.The variations in the social expectations on the use of space allow to understand the parameters with which, the elites addressed the technical development of the first years of the twentieth century
