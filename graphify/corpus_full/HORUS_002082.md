# Dosimetric analysis of the effect of stretcher structure configuration on dose calculation absorbed in radiation therapy

This research evaluates the dosimetric impact of different representations of the treatment stretcher in the Monaco planning system, using an integral approach that combines experimental measurements and clinical simulations. Four modelling methodologies were compared: air (HUaire), water (HUagua), theoretical value for carbon fibre (HUteoric) and experimental value obtained by CT scanning of a stretcher extender (HUexp).The first phase included point measurements with ionization chamber, used to show the variation in the absorbed dose at different depths when modifying the representation of the stretcher. Subsequently, dose-to-depth profiles were obtained by means of EBT3 radiochrome films, analyzing clinical metrics (Dmax, D2, V95) in the target site and the dose differences in the experimental model. Finally, 16 clinical plans were performed (IMRT and VMAT; 6 MV and 15 MV) on a lower than normal, anthropometric fantoma, analyzing clinical metrics (Dmax, Dmedia, D2, V95) in the target site and at risk systems and validating the delivery of doses were performed by means of ArcCheck.
