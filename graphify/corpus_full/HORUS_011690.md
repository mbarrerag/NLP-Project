# METHODOLOGICAL EVALUATION IN THE HIPOPOTAM MANNEY (HIPPOPOTAMUS AMPHIBIUS) FOR THE MAGDALENA COLOMBIAN ENVIRONMENT

The illegal introduction of hippos in Colombia in 1981 has generated social and environmental impacts that although not addressed exhaustively, do the few researches suggest that they will continue to affect ecosystems? native species? local communities and their livelihoods. According to the latest demographic analysis, is it estimated that there are at least 181 individuals who are occupying vulnerable ecosystems such as the swamps found in the Magdalena River basin? For this reason, was hippopotamus declared as an exotic invasive species of Colombia in 2022 and in order to give it appropriate management? is it suggested that actions be implemented that control and eradicate the species. During the implementation of these actions, however, is it important to evaluate how these impact the species and their relations with biodiversity and local communities. Based on this, the work presents the design of a tool that allows to evaluate the impacts of actions to manage hippos from the One Welfare framework?
