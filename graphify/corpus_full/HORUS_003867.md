# Critical Analysis of Student Narrative Discourse on Impoverishment in Priority Class Bogota School

This research aims to analyze the social representations around the impoverishment of students and former students of a privileged class college in Bogotá, compiled in ten (10) narratives of students of grade 10 and six (6) interviews to ex-students. The methodology followed in this work is qualitative in nature, through processes of inductive, abductive and deductive type, and part of the theoretical and analytical postulates of the Critical Studies of Speech (ECD). Among the findings found, it is identified that the narratives reflect the macrostrategy of legitimation articulated through various discursive strategies, which show social representations about the impoverishment ontologized from aspects such as identity configuration, anomia, assistanceism and representation of the physical space from the latter.
