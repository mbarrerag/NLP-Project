# REVITALIZATION OF THE URBAN AXY THE MOMPOX BALANCE

The interest in the conservation of heritage has evolved from the valuation of the isolated monument or the singular piece towards the understanding of the significance of the urban complex and its dimensions, economic and social. In the particular case of Mompox it is necessary to expand the spectrum of valuation, due to its close relationship with the Magdalena River and the uniqueness of its covetous environment; beyond the historical center the associated environment, such as the synthesis between a lush natural environment and a beautiful city, must be protected
