# From Hacienda in Fontibon to workers' neighborhood in Bogotá: social processes in the urbanization of the Atahualpa neighborhood 1950-1980

This work gives an account of the process of urbanization and consolidation of the Atahtualpa neighbourhood of the town of Fontibón, in the city of Bogotá. This process was preceded by the change of administrative situation of the then municipality of Fontibón, when it was annexed to Bogotá in 1954 under rules issued during a state of siege, and there was a lack of clarity about the urban standard applicable to the areas incorporated into the city. In this context, around 1967 the urbanization of the Atahualpa neighbourhood began to be built up to the applicable rules, but did not complete the process before the competent authorities and the urban developer, without being authorised to do so, it left several lots to be removed, and the buyers were not in compliance with the conditions of sale. It is exposed to the fact that the inhabitants of the neighbourhood have managed the problem to the Council of Bogotá and to obtain the expropriation of the land to the urban development, the installation of public services, the improvement of the conditions of habitability of the neighbourhood and the title of the buildings of the neighbours, thanks to the struggles and collective claims, as well as well as to the accompaniment of political and social actors during the process. . rights. A paramount objective of this work is to show how the newbourhood residents put that problem on a city council political agenda and achieved land development agent, installation of infrastructure for public services, improvement of the living conditions and land titling and registration, as the result of collective action of the community and involvement of social and political actors
