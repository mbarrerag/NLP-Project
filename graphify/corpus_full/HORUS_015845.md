# Morphology and taxonomy of immature forms of the genus Anastreph Schiner (Diptera: Tephritidae) for Colombia.

With 305 species described, the genus Anastrepha is the most diverse within the native Tephritidae of America and its species are considered pests of great economic importance in the neotropical region. These dipterans are known as true fruit flies; which cause serious damage to commercial fruit trees. Likewise, their presence in the fruit exporting countries generates strong quarantine restrictions.The taxonomic descriptions of the immature forms of the Anastrepha species are known only for 10% of the species described with adults.By working with optical microscopy, electron microscopy of sweep (MEB) and structural analysis, in this study the description of the external anatomy of the egg for 22 species of the genus Anastrepha, belonging to the curvicidae, fraterculus, grandis, leptozone, mugira, pseudoparallela, serpentine, spatulata and striata.
