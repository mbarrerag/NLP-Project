# PROGRAMMING APPLICABLE TO ELECTRIC ENERGY DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS

This work presents an evaluation of the main numerical methods for the solution of linear and nonlinear systems, applicable to electrical energy distribution systems. The techniques emphasize the development of methods for calculating short circuit currents in electrical energy distribution systems. Likewise, the determination of start currents in AC machines. Examples are developed in Fortran, Matlab and Python, and constitute a reference for those interested in this field of electrical engineering
