# Experience of women in voluntary termination of pregnancy, Hospital de Aguazul Juan Hernando Urrego E.S.E. Colombia, 2022

This research describes the experience of women in first care in the provision of health services during the Voluntary Interruption of Pregnancy in the year 2022 of the Hospital de Aguazul Juan Hernando Urrego E.S.E. and that in its reports of the experience of women, the majority of participants studied showed the compliance with the care route of the IVE established by the Hospital of low level of complexity determining findings in family planning advice and follow-up for the IVE, although it is worth mentioning that this aspect is not representative or conclusive at the national level, since, in the majority of the participants studied, the active participation of mental health personnel within the processes of guidance and accompaniment and above all the implementation of follow-up sessions by the area of psychology or social work of the institution in order to ensure an adequate emotional elaboration and integration in the life trajectory of women. accompanying and above all the implementation of follow-up sessions by the area of psychology or social work of the institution in order to guarantee an appropriate emotional elaboration and integration into the life costume of women.
