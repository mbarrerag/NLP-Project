# NEUROECONOMY AND FINANCIAL DECISION-MAKING: APPROXIMATION FROM A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA

The study of financial decision making is an emerging field of basic and applied research. In the face of this panorama, advances in cognitive functioning knowledge allow us to generate the question of how, from the neuroeconomy, financial decision making is analyzed. In this sense, this study had as main objective to analyze related studies on neuroeconomics, emphasizing those focused on understanding financial decision making. For this purpose, a systematic review of literature supported in the Web of Science database was carried out to identify the main references on the topic, taking into account its type of publication, authors, area of knowledge, keywords, focus and instruments used. Based on the proposed methodological strategy, different studies were identified that have analyzed financial decision making from other perspectives: financial risk, personal finance, investment decisions, among others, being the design of experiments, supported by diagnostic images, studies of greater impact. Finally, the systematic review finds that high impact studies are located in the United States and Europe with an extension throughout the time of empirical and experimental techniques to understand the financial decision making process; additionally, this review aims to be related to Latin America
