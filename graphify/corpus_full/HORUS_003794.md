# Experiences of women with the permanent surgical method of family planning

The permanent surgical method of family planning or female sterilization, is a permanent contraceptive that can generate changes in health; despite being the preferred by Colombian women, little have been addressed the experiences surrounding this. Describe the experiences of women in Bogotá-Colombia, around the implementation of the permanent surgical method of family planning (before, during and after). Descriptive qualitative research; the information was collected through semistructured interviews, recorded, transcribed and subjected to content analysis. Twelve women who had different experiences from their approach to the surgical contraceptive until several years after their implementation. These experiences were grouped according to the moments explored: in the “before”, women came closer to the method by the need to adopt a good contraceptive; for the decision-making they influenced both personal and contextual aspects. In the “during”, the experiences differ according to the moment of completion of the procedure (postpartum or interval).
