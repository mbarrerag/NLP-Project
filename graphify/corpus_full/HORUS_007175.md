# Determination of solar energy potential in La Dorada Caldas

The solar energy is a source of renewable and clean energy that is increasingly used to meet the global energy demand. However, the quantity of solar energy available in a specific place depends largely on the meteorological variables, such as the amount of sunlight, cloudiness and temperature. Knowledge of the meteorological variables is fundamental to assess the potential of solar energy and predict the evolution of irradiation in a time series. To achieve this, a more precise quantitative and qualitative assessment is required using various techniques, including models based on solar brightness, models based on temperature, cloudiness and other meteorological parameters. In order to adequately adapt to climate change, it is important to note that the records provided by the IDEAM for the meteorological variables often present errors, atypical and missing data. As a result, it is necessary to carry out a rigorous process of quality control to ensure the reliability of the data obtained from the meteorological station. In this sense, a process of imputation was carried out for the filling of the missing data in the time series, using appropriate statistics and interpolation techniques. This process allowed to obtain a more complete and precise time series, which the average data was used in the average of the 58% and the year was used. The results indicated that all empirical methods had a similar adjustment according to R2, but the methods of Chen et al., Hunt et al. and Mahmood and Hubbard were the best to fill the missing data in terms of MABE. However, the average percentage error (MBE) showed a negative value, indicating an overestimation of the results of solar insulation for the year of calibration and choosing the empirical methods of Chen et al., Hunt et al., for the calculation of solar insulation in the full time series.
