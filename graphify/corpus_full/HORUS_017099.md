# IN-LIVING ACTIVITY OF THE ARRIVE ACID ON BIOSYNTHESIS AND/OR ACTION OF PENDOGENS

Prostate cancer and benign prostatic hyperplasia are public health problems that depend on androgens, therefore, finding compounds that allow interference with the biosynthesis and/or action of these hormones, is a strategy for their pharmacological treatment. In this study the in vivo effect of atraric acid was determined, which presented in vitro activity on targets involved in the biosynthesis and/or action of androgens. This compound was isolated from Stereocaulon stritum (collected under contracts of access to genetic resources and its derivatives No. 248 article 252, Law 1753 of 2015 amnesty) and was characterized by spectroscopic methods. Its in vivo effect was determined in the Hershberger trial on male rats (dose of 2 mg/kg body weight, subcutaneous route) quantifying its ability to decrease the weight of the prostate, comparing against the finasteridol. In addition, the prostate treated animals were subjected to histopathological analysis. Compared with finasteride, the atraric acid did not significantly decrease the active weight of the prostate
