# ASSESSMENT OF EXTREME PRECIPTATIONS OF MERG USED SUBDIARY REGISTERS OF COLOMBIA STATUS

The understanding of the characteristics at the sub-day level of extreme precipitation events is fundamental for various purposes, including the occurrence of landslides, soil erosion, sudden floods and hydrological design, among others. However, there is a great lack of knowledge of extreme rains at the sub-day level in most parts of the world, including Colombia, where there is no database with durations between 0.5 and 6 hours that allows characterizing and investigating these extreme storm events. In order to help overcome this limitation, digitalised storm charts of extreme storms available in 83 stations in Colombia have been used to construct a database for the whole country including approximately 8,000 extreme events that occurred between 2001 and 2010, to validate in the sub-day scale the performance of the satellite precipitation product IMERG FINAL V06B using a cell-point analysis. To do this, the magnitude, duration and intensities have been investigated.
