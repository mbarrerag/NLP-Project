# Mechanisms for the enforcement of the measures of satisfaction of the Inter-American Court of Human Rights.

Despite the obligation to comply with the reparation measures ordered by the Inter-American Court of Human Rights, in accordance with the international commitments undertaken by the Colombian State, there remains a deficit in implementation with respect to the 26 sentences adopted against Colombia between 1995 and 2022. This deficit is compounded by the lack of provision of institutional mechanisms with the capacity to establish the entity or entities responsible for the implementation of measures in a timely manner, to overcome the interpretative differences in the implementation and to enforce compliance in time by the responsible entities. Furthermore, while there are some regular remedies to require compliance that may be advanced by the victims and their representatives, these are not adequate for the enforceability of all reparation measures, especially those other than compensation measures.
