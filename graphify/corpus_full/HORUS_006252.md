# Contribution of rural entrepreneurs cores in a project for scaling up innovations

The small agricultural producers and their organisations face the difficulties of the environment, to adopt proven technical innovations and to use them as an opportunity to generate new businesses that take advantage of the innovations. Strengthening the capacities of small agricultural producers organisations is fundamental to the success of the collective actions they undertake. Within the framework of the project “more nutritious lands” as a strategy for the scaling up of three new varieties of more nutritious, resistant and productive yellow potatoes; it was proposed to design quality seed production systems to develop inclusive businesses with small agricultural producers organisations, in which the parties (organisations, universities and ally of commerce) pooled capacities and resources to implement new inclusive enterprises. The methodology was designed to strengthen the technical, organisational and market capacities, with four small producers organisations from the departments of Nariño, Cundinamarca and the rural structure of Bogotá; with each one carried out a self-assessment of strengths and weaknesses, from the performance of action plans were designed to strengthen the technical, organisational and market capacities, with the implementation of the organizational structure and the support of the new enterprises and rural development., resistant and productive yellow potatoes; It was proposed to design quality seed production systems to develop inclusive business enterprises including organizations with organizations of small agricultural producers, in which the parties (organizations, universities) pooled capacities and resources to launch new inclusive ventures. Rural entrepreneurship’s nuclear methodology was used with four organizations of small producers from the departments of Nariño, Cundinamarca and the rural areas of Bogotá.
