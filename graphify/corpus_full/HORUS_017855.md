# CONCEPTUAL APPROXIMATION OF THE CONSTRUCTION OF DYNAMIC CAPACITIES: CLASSIFICATION AND RELATIONS

This article aimed to establish the types of capabilities that are categorized as dynamic (CD) and analyze the links that connect them conceptually.For this purpose, a systematic literature review was developed and a concept analysis was carried out to determine the types of dynamic capacities and their relationship. Seven types of dynamic capacities of importance to the organizations were unified and defined: 1) Detection Capacity (CDE); 2) integration and coordination capacity (cic); 3) Reconfiguration Capacity (CR); 4) Learning Capacity (CAP); 5) Innovation Capacity (CI); 6) Alliance Capacity (CA); and 7) Management and Leadership Capacity (CGL).The conceptual relationships between the constructs identified were developed, which allowed to show that these capacities complement and enhance each other.The results are a fundamental input for the business sector, as they facilitate the identification and application of dynamic capacities by the organizations and provide a basis for their scientific study
