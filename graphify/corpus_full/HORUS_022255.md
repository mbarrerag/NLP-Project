# Euglosin bees from Nariño and Putumayo, Colombia

In recent decades bees have received increasing interest from the academic community because of their crucial role as pollinators of wild and cultivated plants. This has generated the development of new knowledge about particular groups of bees that, such as euglossines (also known as orchid bees), do not go unnoticed by nature observers in tropical America. This book summarizes data on the natural history of orchid bees that inhabit southern Colombia in the departments of Nariño and Putumayo and describes their diversity in a field guide that will serve to identify species. In addition, knowledge about their interaction with native plants, their social organization and the methods used for their scientific study are summarized. -- Contraportada
