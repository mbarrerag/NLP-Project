# The continum of the violences based on gender - VBG: youth between the public and the private

The results of the research "From the private to the public: multidimensional study of sexual and gender-based violence at the National University of Colombia" is presented. chapter related to the continuum of gender-based violence in students of the national university of colombia. An eminently qualitative research that addresses individual and collective aspects of the student body. It began in 2021 and ended in 2022, being a principle of researchers to establish dialogues (hard post-pandemy times) in which we also recognized with complex situations, some violence that have affected us, not only because of the context of the Colombian armed conflict, but because violence has become present in many ways, including in the workplace
