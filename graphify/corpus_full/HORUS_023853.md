# METAHEURISTIC OPTIMIZATION OF A MODIFIED TUNED INERTER DAMPER FOR SEISMICALLY LOADED STRUCTURES

The control of structural response under seismic loads is a rapidly growing field in most of the countries seismically active, as an alternative to the conventional design methodologies that are based on a ductile response. The design of buildings that include structural control may result in smaller elements dimensions and larger column spans, which, in turn, may reduce construction costs and increase space usage. This paper presents a numerical investigation for the optimization procedure of the design parameters for a Tuned Inerter Damper (TID) device. The classical location of the TID is changed from the base of the building to the last two levels to control the vibrations induced by seismic loads. A metaheuristic technique based on Differential Evolution (DE) was used for the device design parameter optimization. Through this procedure, the frequency and damping ratios to minimize the structure response are obtained through an objective function that aims to minimize the horizontal peak displacements and the root mean square (RMS) of the displacement. To verify the methodology, a case-study building equipped with an optimized TID device is modeled. A sensitivity analysis was conducted for the selection of the seismic records which are expected to have greater impact on the structure response. The results show that the proposed methodology is effective in finding the optimal configuration for the TID design parameters that reduce the structural response
