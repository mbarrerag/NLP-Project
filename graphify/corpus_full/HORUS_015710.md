# Decision model for the conceptual design of a sustainable energy supply system for the Leticia Headquarters of the National University of Colombia.

The decentralized energy generation model emerged as a solution for energy supply in isolated areas, ensuring energy security and increasing coverage. However, this model often leads to a dependence on a single energy source, so it is necessary to change the paradigm of energy generation by adding other more sustainable sources. Unfortunately, there is no defined route to establish which energy sources should be added and in what way, turning this restructuring into a very complex problem involving decision-making. Generally, these decisions are made considering all the economic or technical aspects, leaving aside other dimensions such as environmental, social and political, which could provide a more contextualized perspective. The aim of this study is to develop and test a methodology that allows finding an optimal energy source arrangement in a decentralized electricity production model taking into account all the dimensions of sustainability. The methodology proposed in this work can help the main stakeholders during the planning phases of energy supply systems.
