# Evaluation of predictive ability of vector support regression in multivariate time series

The study on the application of vector support regression (SVR) in multivariate time series has been scarce, despite the advantages offered by this algorithm in the analysis of univaried time series, since it facilitates precise modeling and forecasting, especially in situations where the series are non-linear or do not comply with the assumptions of traditional models. Due to these advantages, the interest to investigate whether, in a multivaried context, the performance of this algorithm is more precise for prediction compared to a classic model. To this end, a routine has been developed that allows implementing multivaried vector support regression (MSVR), based on the article “Multi-step-step-ahead time series prediction using multiple-output support support vector regression” (Bao, 2014), with the aim of assessing the effectiveness of the prediction in multivaried series. This algorithm has been adapted considering the structure of auto-corralisation in the multivaried time series.
