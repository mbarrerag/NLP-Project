# Wawaë wiyae: the Magütá people

The research project aims to generate knowledge about the Magütá people's problems through the production and socialization of the Arrullos in the Digital Archive of Indigenous Languages the Amazon (ARDILIA) and the publication of an album plus a quick distribution booklet for their listening to the Magütá communities involved. To achieve these objectives, a field work is proposed during the month of August 2023 to collect more ruffles than already available. Finally, it is expected that an academic article on Magütá children's traditional ruckus and games, a more booklet album and the presentation of the advances in the corresponding month of research will be published
