# ON THE METAPHORS OF THE FOUNDERS OF ANNALS. A REFLECTION ON THEIR IMPACT ON THE THEORY OF HISTORY.

This article examines the metaphors on history used by the founders of the school of Annales, Marc Bloch (1886-1944) and Lucien Febvre (1878-1956). The purpose is to show that both historians used meaningful metaphors that have shaped the work of historians in the early twentieth century. To do this, the method of critical reading of texts is followed and as a focus the theory of conceptual metaphor by George Lakoff and Mark Johnson. It will be demonstrated by this that Annales founders used metaphors in which knowledge of history is represented as a building? in constant construction, and that the historian, besides the casador?, is a constructor? that creates theories and interpretations. The importance of this analysis lies in the fact that metaphors structure the forms of representation and the sense of historical discipline and are fundamental elements of analysis for the theories of history
