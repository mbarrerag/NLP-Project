# Strengthening the Public Policy Analysis and Public Management Research Group (APPGP)

The project Strengthening APPGP seeks to consolidate its activities in the lines of work of the research group in two interrelated fields: the study of public policies and public management. In the field of public policies, 4 fronts were established to strengthen: conceptual and theoretical development from and for Latin America, development of research in the implementation phase (better understand the _x0093_brech_x0094_), strengthen cooperation with the group of inv. _x0093_Equality and Food and Nutritional Security_x0094_ (ESAN) of the Faculty of Medicine of the UNAL and Flacso-Quito, and finally, to consider the possibility of conducting a deliberative analysis of public policy through a citizen council. In the topic of public management, the strengthening of the group will be considered through the constitution of the Observatory of the Public Administration (OBSAP), also from the experience acquired in the development of the project Observatory of Administrative Culture (OCA), with the collaboration of the Administrative Department of the Civil Service Distrital (DASGP) and an alliance with the University of Valle.
