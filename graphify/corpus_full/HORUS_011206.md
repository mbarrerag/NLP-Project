# We're going to be amorrhaging- cooking, sciención and family.

Food as an element that integrates knowledge, family, science and fun has been the vehicle with which I have developed strategies for students to take ownership of the heritage concept, in addition to integrating what they have learned in the classroom into experiences of repoding the knowledge of hand with their families in their kitchens in their homes. With the result that students and their families are the nucleus where traditional cultural knowledge (intangible cultural heritage) is disseminated and transmitted by hand with the academic knowledge learned in the classroom
