# 3D simulation model for cleft palate surgery.

The cleft lip and palate (LPH) constitute the most frequent craniofacial malformation. Integral part of the management includes the participation of surgeons trained in different surgical correction techniques. However, the training during the residence is limited by restrictions for interaction with patients, greater administrative responsibilities and reduced opportunities for supervised clinical practice Objective: Design and develop a 3D simulation model for learning a technique of palatorrafia, low cost, portable, easy handling, reproducible and reliable regarding the representation of anatomical structures involved in this pathology Materials and methods: A review of the literature 3D simulation models for the teaching of the cleft palate was carried out. A prototype was designed based on the surgical and anatomical knowledge of the palate in patients with LPH, as well as the models documented in the bibliographic search. A model in dry form was made with materials such as plaster, acrylic and silicone, recreating the main anatomical structures. Three surgeons expert in cleft palate surgery made qualitative evaluation necessary for the characteristics of the biliary as far as the hanna experience and aesthetic appearance is used
