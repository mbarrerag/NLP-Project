# Effect of microwave treatment on the enzyme peroxidase, bioactive compounds and colour in tree tomato (Solanum betaceum Cav.) red variety.

Establishing an appropriate relationship between the enzymatic inactivation achieved by the scaling process and the nutrient impacts and changes in the color of the food is essential to preserve the quality of the processed products. In the first chapter, the objective was to study the effects of the scaling at different levels of microwave power (300, 600, 900 and 1200 W) by times (15, 30, 45 and 60 seconds) on the inactivation of peroxidase present in this fruit. In comparison with the control treatment, the microwave-scalded managed to decrease the residual activity of peroxidase below 10% in a time of 45 seconds to a power of 900 W. With this information, the second chapter evaluated the effect of the microwave-scald conditions on the bioactive compounds, antioxidant activity and colour finding that the content of vitamin C, total phenols, carotenoids, antioxidant activity, luminosity, color and discolouration index did not show significant difference (p> 0.05) compared to the conventional and fresh method.
