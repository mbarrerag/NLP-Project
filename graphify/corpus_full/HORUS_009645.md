# THE OLD WE STOPPED.

The urban history of Medellín has been written from many silences and forgetfulnesss. more volunteers than involuntary. A narrative thought more of and from the frame of the square than from the outskirts of it. Many inhabitants have been expelled from history, their names have been erased and their territories renamed. A much earlier practice than thought. Likewise, many of those who were architects of urban and architectural works representative of the city have been denied an important place. Their names are not exalted or rescued despite the important presence of any of these works. In other cases they were buried in oblivion along with the demolished buildings and also forgotten in urban history. This book of essays, some unpublished and others published, is an attempt to rescue both some forgotten places and neighborhoods, as well as the history of architectural works and their architects, whose importance in being and doing the moment was of great value, but that then the account condemned them to forget and even more serious, built another story interested between oblivion and myth.
