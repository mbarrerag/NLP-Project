# PARAMETERS FOR VISUAL AND INSTRUMENT ASSESSMENT OF THE DENTAL COLOR IN INVITRO STUDY.

to establish the parameters for the visual and instrumental evaluation of dental colour in in-vitro studies from the scientific literature published between 2015 and 2021. the search was carried out in the databases: PubMed, Web of Science, Science Direct, Scopus, Scielo and Lilacs; also in the search engine Google Aca-demic and libraries of the publishing houses Wiley and Springer. The keywords used were tooth, color, in-vitro, color perception, shade matching, thresholds, application, su-rounding, CIELAB¿ and CIEDE2000¿. Taking into account the eligibility criteria, the studies according to the title, summary and full text were selected. the search showed a total of 37 publications that were grouped into three topics: visual color take-up: environmental conditions, observers and leveling; 2. instrumental colour take-up: instruments; and 3. data processing: calculation of the color difference and perceptibility thresholds (PT) and acceptability (AT)
