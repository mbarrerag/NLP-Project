# EXILIATION AND DISPLACATION IN CONTEMPORARY CIVIL WARS: SPAIN, MEXICO AND COLOMBIA

Mexico has hosted twenty thousand Republican exiles; we will show those who survived sixty years later and whose dispersion across different parts of the country made their location an odyssey. In Colombia, the insurgency has prevailed for years: political violence, eviction and struggle for land, realities that do not allow for territorial peace. It is living a civil war between self-defense, paramilitaries, state and guerrillas. The victims of the conflict take refuge in cities depopulating towns and villages
