# 2+1 roads as a prior alternative to double roads.

In this study, the operational analysis of the road 2+1 is carried out in order to study its viability as an optimal intermediate alternative to be implemented in a design by stages towards the double road, which departs from a conventional road to a 2+1, and finally to double road.For this, a case study was carried out, where operational data of the road CV13 located in the province of Valencia (Spain), on which 109 scenarios are simulated combining volume variables between 700 veh/h to 1600 veh/h by direction and percentage of trucks between 10% and 50%, these scenarios are simulated in TWOPAS (calibrated program for transit conditions of vehicles transiting on Colombian road), and allow to compare the vehicle operation in three stages: two lanes, 2+1 and double roadway variables where the design of the road 2+1 criteria for each direction of traffic were verified and the two authors and were identified findings that allowed to propose criteria with better technical support when generating a road stretch “2+1”. Results and analysis of the average speed variables and percentage of time in follow-up for each direction of traffic and combining both directions, obtaining to 82% and PTSF) between 11 and 19% and increases of up to 14 km/h. Finally, economic evaluation is carried out at the different thresholds found and for the different stages, considering only the savings in travel time and operational costs, resulting in this alternative being operationally and economically viable
