# Analysis and contextualization of "Vier Gesänge für Frauenchor" Op. 17 by Johannes Brahms

This work seeks to familiarize the reader with the work “Vier Gesänge für Frauenchor”, Op. 17 by Johannes Brahms by means of the exhibition of the elements considered essential for this purpose. Such characteristics include the context of the composer’s life; the process of founding, boom and decline of the Hamburg Women’s Choir and the specific history of the object work of this work. As for the composer’s life, its fascination with choral music and the musical production that derives from it, especially in its early stage. Likewise, a musical analysis of the four pieces that make up the “Four songs” is performed, in which elements of melody, rhythm, harmony, texture and form are addressed. All this with the aim of providing tools to the choral directors of women’s groups who wish to address this repertoire, taking into consideration some strategies from learning, assembly and interpretation of the “Four songs for Femininian Choir”. . choral conductors of women's groups who wish to approach this repertoire, considering some strategies for learning, assembling, and interpreting the "Four Songs for Women's Choir"
