# FLORA FARALLONENSIS III: THE MELASTOMATACEAS OF THE PREMONTAN FOREST OF PIC DE AGUILA (CALI, VALLE).

This article is the third product of the series of manuscripts on the flora of the Farallones de Cali, a botanical project that aims to make known the inventory of the flora of this region. This paper presents the species of melastomataceae (Melastomataceae), native to the premontane forest of the village Pico de Águila, department of the Valley, Colombia, located at 1550 m altitude on the eastern slope of the Farallones. It describes each species and reports its biogeographical data in Colombia, as well as some illustrations to facilitate its identification
