# DEVELOPMENT OF A PYTHON APPLICATION AS A TEACHING STRATEGY IN THE ESTIMALATION OF COSTS OF OPERATIONS IN AGRICULTURAL MECHANISM

To develop an educational application in Python that allows to calculate the costs of using agricultural machinery in the context of agricultural engineering. An application was created in Python to calculate the operating costs of agricultural machinery, following the method proposed by the American Society of Agricultural and Biological Engineers (ASAE). Its results were compared with an application developed by the Observatory of Proven Technologies of the Government of Spain. To determine if the tool helped to achieve the learning objectives was implemented a participatory validation method in the classroom. the total costs thrown out by the application are an acceptable prediction, with a maximum difference of 2.6% from those considered real. Students showed a greater motivation and commitment to learning when using the digital tool. Students achieved a better understanding of the topic through the use of the digital tool. The application Agricultural Mechanization - Operational Costs v2021.6 proved to be useful and accessible to students of machinery and agricultural mechanization. The results of the survey revealed that the tool significantly improved understanding of the costs of agricultural operation, facilitated collaborative work and was easy to use for the purposes of the estimation
