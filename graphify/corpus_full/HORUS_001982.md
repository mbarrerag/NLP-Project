# Ways of life and differential forms of use-occupation of the landscape in the Late Formation: Los Montes de María in the archaeology of Lower Magdalena and the Colombian Caribbean

The research carried out in the Montes de María sucreños, more specifically in the middle-low basin of the Mancomoján stream, has provided data from which it is possible to maintain that the formation groups that occupied the domestic units studied used strategies of subsistence-settlement linked to a seasonal residential alternation and a restricted mobility around two mutually contrasting landscapes. It is arrogated by the idea that the human groups, who inhabited the three domestic units under scrutiny, made a differential use of the space in function of satisfying their basic subsistence needs as a response to the particular environmental conditions of the area of study. The results obtained, integrated to the approach of the Socio-Ecological Systems (SES) and the Adaptive Complex Systems, have made possible to propose a theoretical model of the community social resilience in the Middle Basin of the Arroyo Mancomoján, which allows to describe and interpret the occupation dynamics to the local employees for the Tardío formative .
