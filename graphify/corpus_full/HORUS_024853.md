# Analysis of the economic commitment and the capture of co2 in a gasification plant operated in continuous coupled to a photobioreactor for energy and biomass generation from a static and dynamic point of view.

This grade work shows the steady-state and transient analysis of a gasification plant with photobioreactor coupling, this with the aim of verifying whether the revenues generated by the marketing of microalgae can favor both the reduction of carbon dioxide emissions and the economic benefit in a process involving conversion of biomass. In relation to the dynamic part, a new multi-objective control strategy based on game theory is proposed, in order to operate the system between economic operating regions and emission reduction with a simple tuning. In the first chapter, a compilation of the results obtained by various authors is shown in the counterproductive relationship between the reduction of carbon dioxide emissions and the economic benefit of a process, in addition to a short introduction of the basic concepts. In the second chapter, the mathematical models of the photobioreactor, the gasifier and the configuration of the complete plant with the objective of evaluating the dynamics of both processes, in Chapters 3 and 4 is found the relationship between process economy and reduction of carbon dioxide emissions for the selected plant from the design and control of processes, and the result of the proposed methods for the construction of the above
