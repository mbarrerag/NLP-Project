# A Bioeconomy Model Based on Sustainable Biorefineries to Ensure the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Colombia

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) have made it possible to establish goals that countries must achieve through adaptable strategies to social, environmental, and economic patterns required today. Besides, bioenergy, biorefineries, and bioeconomy are considered as the main pillar concepts on which technology developments must be based. Hence, creating bioeconomy models applicable to the country allows a real and evident sustainable development, providing benefits for current and future generations. Colombia is a tropical country with high biodiversity. The great biomass production capacity offers potential for producing and transforming products and represents a strategic stage to promote agricultural and agro-industrial productive diversification. The action points for the bioeconomy in Colombia are linked to government policies from a territorial level, private sector industries, improvements in agricultural practices, technology transfer, support in economic resources, and, finally, the environmental and social sustainability that summarizes the impact generated by the implementation of the bioeconomy in Colombia. Through biorefinery designs, it is possible to deeply understand each of the impacts on the bioeconomy and the SDGs. Additionally, these analyses allow the models to be standardized to be comparative in certain aspects with the bioeconomy of other regions, contexts, or processes. This chapter describes how the bioeconomy in the Colombian context can be built from the sustainable analysis of biorefineries through a logical path that allows the fulfillment of the SDGs. How the bioeconomy has been growing as a concept and as a tool is discussed. Several examples of sustainable biorefineries that can be considered are analyzed; besides, these aspects are constantly related to the SDGs and the circular economy and compared to the bioeconomy standards of other countries
