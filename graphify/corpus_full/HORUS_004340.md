# Synthesis and chemical structure elucidation of [(E)-2-(2,6-dichlorobenzylidene)-N-ethylhydrazinecarbothioamide] and cytotoxicity activity against human cancer cells

The E-isomer derived from thiosemicarbazone was obtained by reacting 4-ethylthiosemicarbazide with 2,6-dichlorobenzaldehyde. This compound was characterized by UV, IR and NMR spectrometry and its crystal structure was determined by single-crystal XRD. The cytotoxic activity of the compound was determined against PC3 (prostate cancer), A549 (human lung carcinoma) and U87MG (human glioblastoma) cells. The compound [(E)-2-(2,6-dichlorobenzylidene)-N-ethylhydrazinecarbothiamide] inhibited the growth of PC3 (IC50 = 2.51 ± 0.575 μM) and A549 (IC50 = 4.75 ± 1.68 μM) cells.
