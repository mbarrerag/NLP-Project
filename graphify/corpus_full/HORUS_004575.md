# Carbon determination in the productive system of avocado cv. Hass in the department of Antioquia - Colombia

The avocado carbon cv. Hass (Persea americana Mill) in Colombia is economically important, among the fruit trees is the second line of export. Of the global emissions of Greenhouse Gases Effect - GHG - (including carbon dioxide - CO2 -) estimated at 57.4 GtCO2e the country contributes 0.57%. By 2030 the goal is to decrease 51% of the GHGs where the agricultural sector, forestry and other land uses (AFOLU) will emit 174.50 MtCO2e (50.46% of the total sectors and 24% of the agricultural sector). In the productive chain of the crop, it is important to investigate the carbon storage and the ability to mitigate, compensate or reduce the CO2 emissions to contribute to the national targets against climate change. With the research, the carbon stored in two phenological stages (harvesting and flowering) of the avocado cv. Hass in the department of Antioquia - Colombia. The evaluated compartments were air biomass, underground, necromass and soil; in the latter, the micro-based activity and its relationship with the carbon is complemented by the analysis of the use (AFOLU) sector issuing 174.50 MtCO2e (50.46% of all sectors and 24% for agriculture and livingstock).
