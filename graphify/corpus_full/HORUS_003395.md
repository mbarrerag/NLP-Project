# SIBACORE: Knowledge-Based System for the Recognition of Mechanical Elements

The objective of the article is to present initially the foundations of the Advanced Vision Systems used in image processing and recognition. Then, it proposes the architecture of the model of the SIBACORE knowledge-based vision system for the recognition of mechanical elements. The algorithms used in each of the stages of recognition and diagnosis, specifically of the mechanical elements: washers and screws, are analyzed in detail. Finally, the aspects of the computerized implementation of the prototype of the SIBACORE system (Knowledge-Based System for the Recognition of Mechanical Elements) which works on contours of parts obtained by preprocessing are discussed
