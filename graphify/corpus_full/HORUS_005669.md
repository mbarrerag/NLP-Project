# Role-playing mechanics to review ethical consequences of design decisions in digital projects

This research project through design seeks to explore the ethical design, finding concepts of systemic design and speculative design together with role-playing mechanics, for the creation and validation of a tool that encourages ethical discussions in digital projects. Role games serve as a framework for exploring hypothetical scenarios with decision-making situations; systemic design serves as an anchor for examining these processes, their implications with the actors involved and the systems interconnected to the project; and speculative design allows a critical look, which serves as a connection with the ethical consequences of the decisions taken. To test the tool, three experiments were made: (1) with a fictional project, (2) with four academic projects and (3) with a commercial project, these experiments served to collect information analyzed using affinity maps to reach findings that discuss the tensions between reality and fiction, business and ethics, vulnerability and trust; and narratives and prototypes in digital projects. . that was analyzed using affinity mapping, to find insights that may trigger discussions about the tensions between reality and fiction, business and ethics, vulnerability and trust; and narratives and prototypes for digital projects
