# XR TECHNOLOGIES: EXPLORING DIGITAL IMMERSATION AND CREATIVE ACADEMIC RATION FROM THE UNAL INDUSTRIAL DESIGN PROGRAMME

The Industrial Design has been impacted by 3D digital environments and the integration with the physical world through the technologies of Extended Realities (XR), indicating a change to more immersive environments, expanding the understanding of the industrial, which has traditionally been related to the creation of physical artifacts; the literature shows that these technologies can enhance collaboration and creativity in the early stages of product design; as well as facilitating verification and sustainable simulation. This initiative was based on an academic collaboration; the students of a class called Multimedia and Interactive Media (MMI), accompanied by the professor and an external director of video games art, perform an iterative process of design of avatares and its wearables (functional digital objects and cosmetics), ranging from the 2D ideation, the 3D modeling and ending with an experience of augmented realities (AR) of their digital creations inserted in physical sites of the National University of Colombia (UNAL).
