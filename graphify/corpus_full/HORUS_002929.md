# IDENTIFICATION OF FOOD USE VOCATIONS FOR THE VEGANO SECTOR, AS VALUE STRATEGIES FOR GUARANTEMENT (CICER ARIETINUM L.), IN THE ROLE OF THEIR NUTRICIONAL AND TECHNOLOGICAL PROPERTIES.

Changes in the preferences and food needs of the population, under principles of sustainability, have led to a high dynamization in the development of new products. Thus, emerging market niches demand the diversification of high quality proteins, with the possibility of incorporating more and better nutrients. The chickpea (Cicer arietinum L.) is a prominent leguminous for the elaboration of food suitable for vegan and vegetarian publics, since it stands out for its contribution of proteins of high biological value and excellent digestibility, profile of unsaturated fatty acids, resistant starch, dietary fiber, vitamins and minerals. However, its implementation at food and gastronomic level is still limited, concentrating on a domestic or as a complementary input in the formulation of some products, most of them artisanal or ancestral.
