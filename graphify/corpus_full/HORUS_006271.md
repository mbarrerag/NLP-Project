# Hourly electricity consumption foreign for Antioquia-Colombia using statistical-machine learning models

The energy sector plays a fundamental role in promoting economic growth and social progress in a country due to its functionality as input of productive processes and as a public service asset that provides greater welfare to the population. The forecast of the consumption of electricity is a valuable instrument for policy makers to guide the decisions of tariffs, taxes and investment, as well as planning of energy and operational security, contributing to guarantee a continuous supply of electricity and reducing the costs associated with the provision of energy distribution services. The aim of this research is to forecast the consumption of electricity hours in Antioquia-Colombia using models of Statistical-Machine Learning with exogenous variables such as the day rate and the maximum temperature. The results show that the Neuronal Network LSTM can be an efficient model for the operational deployment of electricity distribution due to its average error of electricity supply to a business sector and economy to be considered in ca. 493 MWh, while the reference model of the Market Operator XM obtained an error of 3420 MWh during the evaluated week.
