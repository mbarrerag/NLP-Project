# THE BUCAL HEALTH OF VENEZOLAN MIGRANTS IN CHAPINERO, BOGOTÁ.

PRECAFECTIONS: In Latin America, the last decade, Venezuelan migration has been observed, requesting refuge, asylum or seeking quality of life. Hence, Colombia is one of the countries most affected by its proximity to Venezuela having a greater number of migrants, whether permanently or temporarily, and within the full development of human health oral health is paramount, since it is part of the health of an individual, which must be guaranteed by the state, is a conclusive element in the quality of life, because when conditions appear that damage the oral cavity lead to painful consequences, therefore, it is necessary to promote daily oral hygiene to maintain a healthy mouth free of diseases that allow to preserve health. As regards, Venezuelan migration has encountered challenges in Colombia, being one of the most affected in the topic of xenophobia and the economic crisis that they had to face taking into account the labor shortage to subsist, suffering an impact in covering the basic needs among them; housing, food, education, hospital care and migratory status, in addition to these causes, the migrants have a lack of knowledge of the processes of care in the health centers in Colombia.: Venezuelan migrants experienced extensive and complicated migration processes to be established in Bogotá, Colombia, which meant that when they arrived they would not be found regularly in the territory and therefore had no documentation required to be accepted within the Colombian health system, the legal status in families is demanded by the government, which delayed dental care, and this triggered existing oral health problems and due to the social working and economic conditions they had difficulty accessing dentists due to the costs of the treatments
