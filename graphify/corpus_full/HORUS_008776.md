# Contact surfaces, the transformation of the base plane as a strategy of mediation between the interior and the exterior.

This final master's work addresses the forms of relationship between the ground and the base plane, architecture and city. The research is focused on the transformation of the horizontal surface, questioning the procedures that arise from the perspectives exposed through 4 study cases. The importance of the base plane and the ground in the conception and achievement of the architectural space leads to a transformation in function of internal and external factors. Being a contribution to the integration between the city and architecture, linking the two areas to the same time that its potential is integrated. This TFM aims to show that the transformations of the ground and the base plane go beyond the resolution of a topographic problem, the present work is focused on exposing how these transformations fulfil a role of mediator between the interior and the exterior, linked the two areas to increase the possibilities of relationship that dissolve the boundaries. The place does not remain on the side, on the contrary, the importance lies in that the architecture seeks to move away from the individuality by directly linking with what happens in its context.
