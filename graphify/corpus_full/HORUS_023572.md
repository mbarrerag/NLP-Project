# DIDACTIC RESOURCES WITH REALITY INCREASED FOR THE LEARNING OF PARASITOLOGICAL

The use of didactic tools for the learning process has become a challenge for many teachers especially in this time of pandemic because of COVID-19 where these processes had to be accelerated to generate educational environments that encourage the autonomy, critical capacity and social awareness of students. In this sense, this work shows how the collaborative work of an interdisciplinary group of teachers focused on a specific theme the biological cycle of parasites generates a didactic tool using the augmented reality that allows students to strengthen the autonomous study and motivate them to search for answers and alternatives both in the control, as well as in possible medical interventions of selected parasitosis
