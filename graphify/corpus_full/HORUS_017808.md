# The Campaign to End Malaria in Colombia, 1959-1979

Summary Introduction. In the mid 1950s, the country adopted and implemented the Malaria Eradication Campaign (CEM), without having been evaluated until now. Evaluating the results achieved in the attack and consolidation phases of the malaria eradication campaign in Colombia, between 1959 and 1979. A descriptive and retrospective study was made of the results +8220;malariometrics +8221; and CEM operational in Colombia between 1959 and 1979 based on data collected from the archives of the Ministry of Public Health. The criteria established by the World Health Organization (WHO) related to the phases of a malaria eradication programme were used. The data were stored, tabulated and analysed, and the malarymetric indicators were developed and applied.
