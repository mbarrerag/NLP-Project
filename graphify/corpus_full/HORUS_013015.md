# Educational strategy of newborn care in the Ucin, based on the model of the universe of care for development

ABSTRACT The high complexity of nursing care in the newborn, in the intensive care units, makes necessary the presence of nursing professionals with quality training processes, solid knowledge and humanization. Therefore, giving compliance to the approach of generating knowledge of nursing discipline, the present internship was developed for the implementation of an educational strategy seeking to strengthen the application of comprehensive care by nursing staff, based on the model of the Universe of Care for the Development of the newborn, which contributed to the well-being of the newborns hospitalized in the Neonatal Unit (UCIN) of the CAFAM Santa Barbara clinic. The process of implementation began with the initial evaluation of the application of the care of the newborn hospitalized in the UCIN; afterwards, an educational strategy was built for nursing staff based on the model of the Universe of Care for Development, strengthening the practice of comprehensive care. The process of socialization of the educational strategy directed to nursing staff to strengthen the comprehensive care in the newborn. Finally, the application of the knowledge acquired by nursing personnel involved in the intrahospital care was verified. The high complexity of nursing care care requires the presence of professional professionals, this interaction between quality training and the implementation of the educational approach, the solid foundations for care at home and to guarantee the development of neonate in the best conditions. The areas of care applied from the perspective of the Universe of Care for the Development of the Newborn in the UNIC are: monitoring, oxygenation, nutrition, comfort, positioning, skin care, thermoregulation, infection control and safety; achieving a harmonious interaction that could relate theoretical concepts to modern nursing practices in care contexts. .
