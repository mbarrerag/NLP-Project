# Contributions for an Anthropology of Love: Study on the Love Relations of a Group of Feminist People

This research paper analyses the loving experiences of a group of people who have participated in feminist spaces. Based on the dialogue between the anthropological theory produced on emotions, the couple, kinship and friendship, and the reflections of feminist studies on the hegemonic model of love as a device of the sex/gender system and the reproductive of violence, the experiences of the people who participated in the study are described by emphasizing the way in which they built and signified love from what they learned in their family, and how these notions have been re-meaned, and re-configured in their subjective processes, motivating a series of changes in their loving practices. Finally, love is proposed as a political resource that has participated in women's spaces
