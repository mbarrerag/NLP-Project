# Validation of an instrument to measure the clinical scientific dimension of nursing care in ICU

Abstract Objective: To describe the validation of an instrument that measures the scientific-clinical dimension of nursing care in the intensive care unit (ICU). Psychometric study that determined the apparent validity and content of the instrument through expert panel, the construct validity by means of exploratory factorial analysis and the internal consistency by Cronbach alpha. The research included a sample of 186 nurses working in ICU in Colombia. The apparent validity and content showed a correlation index between 0.778 as a lower value and 1 as a maximum value. The internal consistency by Cronbach alpha was 0.887 for the total instrument. In the construct validity, the exploratory factorial analysis evaluated the 3 factors contained in the original version of the instrument, identifying a greater correlation of items with the nursing care process and, to a lesser extent, with the administrative process and the research process in ICU. From the analysis of psychometric properties, it is evident that the instrument to measure the scientific-clinical dimension of nursing care in ICU allows a greater correlation of the nursing care process and, to apply the scientific elements that are valid from the nursing process
