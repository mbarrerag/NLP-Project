# EVALUATION OF THE PHYSICAL STATUS IN SENT VOLVEIBOL PRACTICES

Sitting volleyball is one of the sports disciplines that encourages the participation of people with physical disabilities; practiced worldwide at the paralympic level. Colombia has made some international participations demonstrating the great potential that this modality has; however, there is no protocol of evaluation and diagnosis that allows to know the physical condition, muscular behavior and biomechanical aspects for the performance of the technique, that allows the best conditions for the practice of such discipline, and that can be applied to every practitioner or possible practitioner of seated volleyball.
