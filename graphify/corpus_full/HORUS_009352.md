# INFLUENCE OF THE PRESSURE DIRECTION AND THE ENDOR OF A PAYMENT IN CHEMICAL, STRUCTURAL AND SUPERFFICIAL PROPERTIES IN NYLON PA 12 POLIAMIDE PROBETS MANUFACTURED BY SELECTIVE LASER

Currently, we seek to better understand the influence of design variables on the physical, chemical and surface properties of pieces printed by Sinterizado Laser Selectivo (SLS). In particular, we want to understand the effect of some design variables such as the direction of printing and wall thickness on the properties of parts manufactured in PA 12 in order to apply it in research related to low weight. In this work we characterized the chemistry, structural and surface roughness of specimens manufactured by SLS. It was found that wall thickness and the direction of printing do not influence functional groups, crystalline planes and phases of specimens. It was found that surface roughness presents significant differences in function of thickness, where the thickness of smaller magnitude (2.0 mm) presented the lowest mean value and the greatest variability
