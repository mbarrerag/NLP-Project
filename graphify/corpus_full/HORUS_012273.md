# Clinical characterization of pulmonary and extrapulmonary tuberculosis in patients with HIV co-infection in an IPS specialized in Bogotá.

Tuberculosis co-infection is the main cause of death in patients with HIV infection. There are no recent data regarding the sociodemographic and clinical characteristics of patients with tuberculosis and HIV co-infection in Bogotá. To establish the prevalence of HIV infection in patients with pulmonary and extrapulmonary tuberculosis infection and describe its characteristics. A retrospective cross-sectional study in patients with tuberculosis infection diagnosis and HIV co-infection, attended at the Simón Bolívar Hospital in Bogotá between 2019 and 2021. 54 patients were included, representing 52.3% of patients with tuberculosis attended in the hospital during the period evaluated. The median age was 38 years (RIQ: 13 years). 79.6% (43) were men. The median CD4 count was 59 cells/μL (RIQ: 88 cells/μL); 72.2% (39) had a contetage less than 200 cells/μL. The diagnosis of HIV infection of novo was based on the suspicion of tuberculosis infection was found in 35.2% (19). Our population presents TB of meningeal location more frequently, with important clinical implications and association with in-hospital mortality
