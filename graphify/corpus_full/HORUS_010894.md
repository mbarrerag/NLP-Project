The article - the results of the research PAISAJES ANTROPICOS - analyses the project of the new Centre for the Development of Children located in the municipality of Rionegro, in Antioquia. The centre, which is part of the public institution ¿El Porvenir, can accommodate up to four hundred children, mainly inhabitants of the neighboring district, from which the work derives its name.
