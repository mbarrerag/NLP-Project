# Music therapy intervention in young people aged 20-22 with depressive symptomatology, Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá headquarters

This research arises as a response to the growing concern generated by the alarming suicide rates registered in the country. Similarly, it is disturbing to observe statistics and close cases of individuals who currently face depressive symptoms. For this reason the author focuses on providing interventions that positively impact and contribute to the sense of life of these people. The purpose of this intervention is to examine the effects of music therapy on the sense of life of young people, with ages between 20 and 22 years, belonging to the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, and that present depressive symptoms. To this end, the identification of the depressive manifestations of the participants before and after the intervention is addressed. In addition, it seeks to analyze the evolution of the self-concept and self-esteem from the beginning of the process, as they progress in the music therapy, with the purpose of strengthening their sense of life. This research is based on a descriptive qualitative approach and develops through three case studies, carried out in a total of seven individual sessions per participant and a group session. The approach is supported in the four methods of the musicotherapy, being the composition and the central method. the cases of individuals close to us who are currently experiencing pressure This method aims to guide the sense of life by analyzing the sound-musical creations of young participants in order to address their self-concept, self-estem, and sense of life, with the aim of reducing depressed manifestations. The results of the process show an reinforcing outlook, demonstrating a transition from severe depression to a middle depressed state in all three participants. It is recommended to carry out music therapy processes that involve longer intervention periods focused on the young population
