# Interethnic relations between Afro-Colombians and the Emberá Dóbida in Bajo Atrato, 1970-2016

This thesis has as main objective to analyze the evolution of the inter-ethnic relations between Afro-Colombians and the echmberá Dóbida in the Bajo Atrato during the period from 1970 to 2016. This research is designed in four chapters that seek to explain the different forms of relation between indigenous and Afro-Colombians. In the first chapter, it addresses in detail the configuration of the native nations in the Chocó, its contact with the Spanish colonizers and the policies implemented by the Spanish monarchy for the reduction and domination of the Indians. It also examines the connection between the African slave process and the development of the mining production in that region. In the second chapter, it analyzes the population dynamics of the emberá Dóbida and the Afro-Colombians in the Bajo Atrato, highlighting the singular coexistence in the same territory and the emergence of the indigenous and black movements during the second half of the twentieth century. It also deepens in the emergence of the indigenous reserves and the allocation of collective territories to the Afro-descendents in the Pacific. The third chapter, describes the main economic activities carried out by the Afro-Colombias and the ethnic groups, the emberá Dóbida.
