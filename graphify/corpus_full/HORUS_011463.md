# ARCHITECTURE

The Solar Decathlon is an international university competition related to the construction of sustainable housing based on the use of solar energy. The contest, which originated just over a decade ago at the initiative of the Department of Energy of the United States, with the participation of North American universities, subsequently extended to Europe (Spain, France) and more recently to Asia (China).For its short career the contest has gained recognition and reputation worldwide as the most important contest on sustainable housing. At the end of 2015, in the city of Cali was held the first edition of the Solar Decathlon for Latin America and the Caribbean (SDLAC 2015), this time promoted by the organizers of the contest, the Alcaldía de Cali and the Ministries of Mines and Housing of Colombia. As a particularity, and unlike previous editions where prototypes for isolated housing were built, for this edition was formulated the construction of a prototype belonging to a group of housing, with the intention to directly attend the housing sector of social interest in the Tropica. Undoubtedly, this was an excellent opportunity for universities of Colombia and the foreign country to formulate proposals for housing aimed at addressing energy issues in the last- and big-place. The results of the competition were based on the following: the research on the reduction and efficiency of energy consumption, the implementation of new materials and constructive techniques, as well as the offer of decent social housing for the country and the region; the strengthening of the State-Company-University triad, which is essential for the country's development, was another benefit sought; the book contains the memories of what were some of the most important parameters and disciplines of the proposal presented by the National University of Colombia, including urban planning, architecture, construction, civil and electrical engineering; and finally, the aspects that were most taken into account in the area of sustainability; the texts are produced by some of the professors and advisers who participated during the competition; the experience reflected here; the satisfaction of having fulfilled the project's expectations, since the proposal and the prototype built offer concrete and feasible solutions to real problems of the community and the environment; and the impetus to continue to advance on this topic of sustainable social housing that is so much needed in the local and regional contexts
