# INDIGENOUS HISTORY, DOCUMENTARY SOURCES AND ETHNOHISTORICAL NARRATIVES: DIALOGUES WITH JEAN LANGDON

Born in Colorado, the state with the largest mountains in the United States and a strong influence of Hispanic culture, Jean graduated in anthropology from Carleton College (Minnesota) and completed her master's and doctorate in this area at the University of Washington (Washington) and Tulane University (Luisiana), respectively.In 1970, he received a scholarship from the International Center for Medical Research at Universidad del Valle/Tulane University to travel to Colombia, where he began a long journey of research among indigenous peoples, mainly with the Siona, on the Putumayo River.During this period, in 1973, she was also a professor at the Universidad del Cauca.The experience of almost four years in Colombia was decisive for her later choices: she abandoned her career as a university professor in the United States, motivated to come to Brazil
