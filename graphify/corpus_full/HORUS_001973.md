# Virtual didactic tool for learning MesoRex referral in pediatric population for graduate students of the Radiology and Diagnostic Imaging Specialist through a narrative review of literature

The obstruction of the extrahepatic portal is a particularly pediatric pathology, whose treatment is essentially surgical, being the MesoRex referral the best option for these patients. Graduate students of Radiology and Diagnostic Images and radiologists should be familiar with the use and interpretation of the different imaging modalities in the diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of these patients.
