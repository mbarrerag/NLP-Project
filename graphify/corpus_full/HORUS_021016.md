# The wild nature of space shuttles.

From the work of archive corresponding to the alunizaje of the Apollo 11 in 1969, take as a starting point the socio-cultural relations that were structured by the press and audiovisual media in the 60s in Colombia regarding this event. In the framework of the spatial race to reach the moon, the NASA will realize the decision to convene companies or international entities that work a textile material non-flammable, able to cover the inside of the module of command of the Apollo 11. It is like this call, Raquel Vivas Rincon, and its textile company Huatay are selected for the realization of a fabric made from wool of buoyan sheep. From this historical event in Colombia, I begin to build a fiction of conspirative character, interweaving elements of the popular culture that lead me to think about the artisanal development of subjects such as: the powder (for mechanisms of ignition), the glass blown (which account the lightness and impossibility of the material to leave the atmosphere). tive to interact in an exploratory and critical way, the metaphorical and fruitless aerospace processes that give rise to a new exploration of the forms and materials that may or may not fly. empathetic with a conspiracy established in the fiction of the project
