# VKHUTEMAS SCHOOL OF ARTS AND THE SOVIET ARTISTIC VANGUARDS

This work is an interview with Prof.Dr. luiz carlos cólon llamas held in february 2020. The professor explained what motivated him to develop the research on the Vkhutemas school and clarified some doubts about the structure of this institution.Furthermore, he commented on the debates that took place in artistic collectives after the 1917 Revolution, especially the experimentation and objectification of artistic practice.Finally, he was able to explain about the textile ateliers, where the artist Varvara Stepanova, object of research by one of the interviewers, was present.Thus, the interview aimed to show some of the main debates, impasses and artistic groups that were involved in the creation of an arts school that wanted to be revolutionary
