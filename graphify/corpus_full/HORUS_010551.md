# Articulation of an urban void with a water edge and metropolitan edges: Counterproposed to the Procables Plan, Bogotá

This work arises from a personal concern as urban resident on the relationship so indifferent that they have to cities regarding water bodies from the point of view of urban design. This situation is seen in the context of Bogotá by the large areas that exist in disuse between the canal rivers and the different structures that are constructed in their borders, places that generate problems of continuity in the urban routes and pedestrian continuity, among others. As a consequence to this idea the proposal of design motive of this work has the objective to respond in a different way to this problem of voids and urban borders, understanding them from its origin and reason of being in the cities to reach a position that is more problematic to the city according to its existing urban structure and with order in structure itself. The area of interest was selected due to a personal experience of travel in my childhood, is a great area that possesses different characteristics for this it has categorized and evaluated the different gaps that make it, thus choosing the most problematic gap for the design proposal. This particular gap is included between the edges of the Avenue Boyacá and the Avenue 13th, together with the water edge of the canal of the Fuchawater. To make an idea and to make an idea of the design. the design proposal that is the subject of this work aims to respond in a different way to this problem of urban voids and borders, understanding them from their origin and their reason to exist in the cities, to design a proposal that can join them according to the same to their existing urban structure and with an ordered structure in itself.
