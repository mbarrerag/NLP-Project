# THE RIGHT TO WATER AND FOOD AND NUTRICIONAL SECURITY IN AFROCED PEOPLES

Among the objectives of the research are to describe the general situation of Afro-Colombian communities and to identify the characteristics of access to water in these communities. documentary review, systematic search in academic databases JSTOR, Science Direct, Oxford Academic Journal, SCOPUS, PudMed. Identification of categories related to access and coverage of water and INSAN in national statistics and instruments. low income, low educational level, poverty, among others and being Afro-descendant are associated with higher levels of INSA in households (Berning et al., 2022; Drewnowski, 2022; Kruger et al., 2005).
