# CHARACTERISTICS OF COVID-19 HOSPITALIZED PATIENTS IN ATLANTA GEORGIA

Studies show that the underlying characteristics of a patient have a strong influence on their condition as being infected by COVID-19. It is an epidemiological phenomenon that brought with it high mortality rates and hospital increase. This study evaluates the medical characteristics of an adult patient with SARS-CoV-2 infection admitted to hospitalization in Georgia Atlanta, to determine their influence on mortality, given a clinical profile. From four possible Bayesian logistic models, the most appropriate one is selected. Uninformational a priori distributions and MCMC methods are used to approximate the subsequent distribution of the parameters of the model. In the final model, the conditions of type: pulmonary (2), cardiovascular(3), hematological (2), inflammatory (2) and other (4) were indicated with significative influence
