# Analysis of gravel breakage using the DPIV imaging technique taking into account the shape effects

A granular composition was developed to use the technique of polyparticle velocimetry with digital images DPIV, according to its acronym, in the facilities of the Laboratory of Geotechnics of the National University of Colombia, with the objective of analyzing the different mechanisms of breakage present in a process of edometric load to different test witnesses manufactured using adhesive for ceramics, commonly known as ceramic adhesive, arranged in different sets of granular ensambles, of a size classified as gravel, according to USCS, taking into account the variation of their location with respect to the arrangement, in terms of the coordination number, the form of the test subject and the type of contact present. For each test, the compressive efforts to which the ensambles were subjected and the prevaluating break modes were established. The breakage modes correspond to the division or split, and the process of motion of the fragments is established in the process of breakage, the type of break is conditioned by the form of the particle and by the type of contact with the other members of the granular assembly.
