# MOLECULAR DETECTION OF BEGOMOVIRUS ISLATE FROM ARVENSES ASSOCIATED TO THE CULTIVE OF AJI (CAPSICUM SPPP.) IN THE VALLE OF THE CAUCA, COLOMBIA MOLECULAR DETECTION OF OF BEGOMOVIRUS ISLATED FROM WEEDS ASSOCIATED WITH CHILI PEPPER CROPS (CAPSICUM SPPP.) IN VALLE DEL CAUCA, COLOMBIA

The Potato yellow virus will be used to implement a plan for the PYmoMV/Co, Passionfruit leaf distortion virus (PLDV), Pepper rugose mosaic virus (PRMV) and Rhynchosia golden massaic Colombia virus (RhGMCV) are considered to be a form of agricultural activity, isolated and molecularly characterized in the Valle del Cauca. However, at present there is not enough information about its alternate hosts. Since the arvenses play an important role in ecology and epidemiology, this study aimed to detect the presence of these bervones in the Valle del Cauca, Colombia. The presence of viruses in the Valley of Cauca was evaluated using 121 plants that are used for the genus Desviruses, palloviruses, and specific for PYMV
