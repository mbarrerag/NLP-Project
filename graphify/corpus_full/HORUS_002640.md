# Event detection in electrical variables of electrical power systems

In electrical power systems, disturbances caused by failures can compromise the stability and efficiency of the power supply. These disturbances generate abrupt variations in the electrical variables, which must be detected in order to determine the cause and magnitude of the impact on the system, allowing to identify areas where expansion proposals are required to ensure stability in relation to this type of events. In this context, the detection of structural changes in time series of these variables is an essential tool to identify events that could put at risk the integrity of the electrical system. Currently there are different methodologies that manage to detect these events, but it is necessary to have a high rate of sampling of the variables and data of each phase of the system elements. The approach of this study is the application of methodologies to data collected with a periodicity of, at least every 4 seconds according to the current Colombian regulation and, at least one of the phases of each system element. rate of the variables and data of each of the phases of the system elements is required. The focus of this study is the application of methodologies to data collected at a frequency of at least every 4 seconds in agreement with current Colombian regulations and at least one of the phases of each element of the system. This study explores and adapts different changes detection technologies, such as Changepoint, Strucchange and FastCPD methodology, for their application to time series of electrical variables
