# Corporate Governance and Organizational Social Responsibility: Discussion on the Case of the Multilatinas

Abstract This article explores the relationship between Corporate Governance and Corporate Social Responsibility (or Organizational Social Responsibility). To do this, the document is based on a case study on a Colombian firm of long tradition, known as Carvajal Organisation, which has extended its activity throughout Latin America. The case study covers the period 2008-2015, from the arrival of a non-family CEO until the last year that contains sufficient information on the subject at the time of the investigation. The relationship between Corporate Government and Organizational Social Responsibility is studied from a model that considers the interest of the Family Block of Wholesale Owners and the CEO on the disbursements of the firm for Organizational Social Responsibility. The presentation is constructed in the following way: first, the central themes of the disciplinary context are shown. These place the relationship Corporate Government-Organizational Social Responsibility in the Multi-corporation Societies within the organizational discussion. Second, the Review of Literature explains the conceptual framework to visualize both the above mentioned relationship and the field work. These parts establish the theoretical problem used as reference. Finally, the case is described, the findings and the main structure of the majority is incorporated
