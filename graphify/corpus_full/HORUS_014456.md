# Conceptual model of copper (II) involvement present in synthetic industrial waste water in contact with biomass of household waste water in a sewer.

This thesis, by means of a conceptual model seeks to know the effect of copper ion (II) coming from a water prepared with CuSO4 whose concentration characteristics reflect the concentration from a dumping generated by a cobridated process, when coming into contact with the biomass of a synthetic domestic residual water (ARDS), whose composition recreates the conditions of the residual water flowing through the public sewer. For this purpose, the sewer was considered as a flow-piston reactor according to the operating conditions, carrying out a laboratory scale of the composition of the waters with content of copper and synthetic domestic. The inhibition of the respiratory activity caused by the CuSO4 in the biomass of the wastewater using respirometric technique and measured copper concentration by atomic absorption, counting of heterotrophics per unit of formation of colonies (UFC) and the total suspended solids with the method 2540D of the Standar Method was evaluated in the tests carried out in the Shaker team. The results show the bactericidal effect of copper on some of the microorganisms present in the residual water, as well as the concentration of the solid solid solid solids (II) of the solution
