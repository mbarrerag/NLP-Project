# CLINICAL RESEARCH HANDS

According to Colombian legislation, is clinical research considered, any research carried out in human beings with the intention of discovering or verifying the clinical, pharmacological and/or any other pharmacodynamic effect of product(s) in research and/or identifying any adverse reaction to research product(s) and/or to study the absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion of product(s) in research, with the aim of verifying its safety and/or efficacy? (1).This previous definition could be excluding studies, which, while not being directed to testing research products, make an important part of clinical research. Thus, it is worth extending the definition to the term clinical trials.These are prospective studies of biomedical or behavioral research in human participants dissected to answer structured questions about biomedical intervention or behavioral studies, including new treatments (such as vaccines, medicines, dietary choices, new medical devices) and known interventions in the diagnosis and comparison.The research with human subjects to develop or evaluate clinical laboratory tests (such as the study or evaluation of clinical laboratory tests) could be used to test. The study is based on the need to continuously advance by science and technology, and the development of excellent quality clinical research based on international principles and standards should be one of the main objectives of any health science teaching programme, as this contributes value to the teaching and care mission (3). And, despite the recognized importance of clinical research training for students in health science programmes, there are a small number of scientists dedicated to clinical and translational research, which in turn is reflected in a low number of research products and publications. When the relevance of clinical research for students in health sciences (medicine) is assessed in terms of evidence-based medicine, clinical trial execution, and ethics in research, among others, knowledge about the execution of clinical trials has very low scores
