# POISSON AND METRIC COMPATIBILITIES AND ITS FOLIATION

It is known that special cases of symplectic structures are the ones associated to Kähler manifolds, where we get three different (but compatible) structures: metric, complex and the 2-form. In contrast, Poisson structures do not have such relations due to different issues coming from the dimension, the (possibly singular) foliation and the proper geometry of the leaves. In this talk, I will motivate some possible compatibility conditions and show how they relate to the properties of the symplectic foliation
