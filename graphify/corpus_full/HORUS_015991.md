The pandemic of the COVID-19 has made it clear that sectors like agriculture must be more sustainable social, economic and environmentally in the use of resources (water, soil, biodiversity, energy, agrochemicals), because they are scarce, they fulfill ecosystem functions or present high prices in the market. Part of this sustainability implies an adequate management of agricultural by-products, materials with potential for use in the elaboration of bioproducts. The shell of the macorca of cocoa is a subproduct that can represent between 75% and 90% of the total weight of the fruit and is a source of important compounds in the pharmaceutical and cosmetic industry.
