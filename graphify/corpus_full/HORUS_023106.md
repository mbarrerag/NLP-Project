# POTAMOLOGY - RIO STUDIES - ORDER TO RECOVERY

nymphs worshipped for giving life and blessings to all beings... from there comes potamology, union of the Greek term potamon, which means river and dialogues, study, being thus the science that deals with the scientific study of the watercourses, integrating different disciplines related to the knowledge of their behavior, requiring a multidisciplinary work of engineers specialized in: hydrology, hydraulics, geology, geomorphology, geotechnology, sedimentology, biology, water quality, risk management, etc. among many other aspects that intervene in the operation and management of the channels
