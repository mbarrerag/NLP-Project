# EFFECTS OF EXPLOSIVE ARTEFFACTS IMPROVED ON HUMAN HEALTH.

an improvised explosive device (IED) is an unconventional weapon used in irregular armed confrontations, generating complex injury patterns. Its use in Colombia configures a public health problem neglected. to investigate the effects on health in AEI survivors, determining the scope and nature of published literature. Exploratory Systematic Review in databases and repositories of thesis, for grey literature and published in the last 20 years; without population restriction; in english, french, spanish and portuguese languages; evaluating possible biases and quality of the documents to include, by MMAT scale (Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool Version 2018). out of 123 documents initially identified, 97 were included for qualitative synthesis, according to established inclusion and exclusion criteria. The military population was more studied than the civil. Their findings were organized according to: injuries or resulting diseases reported; resilience and positive health indicators identified in survivors; protective and destructive health processes described for these persons. the most common injury reported in the AEI explosion was the indirect amputation and related disorders
