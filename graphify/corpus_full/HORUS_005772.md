# MODELIZATION OF THE IMPACT OF CHANGE ON LAND USE IN THE HYDROLOGY OF THE NETHERLANDS, PALERMO-HUILA, COLOMBIA

In this article, the effects of changes in land use/coverage on direct runoff and maximum flows in the Rio Bache basin were investigated. The analysis was carried out for a spatial scale, the Digital Elevation Model (DEM) of the basin, the soil map and land use/cover maps for three scenarios (1995, 2005 and 2015), using GIS and remote sensing techniques. 3 satellite images representative of these scenarios were processed. The model was calibrated for each of the scenarios. Three precipitation-running events observed in the basin, of similar magnitude, each occurred during one of the three scenarios. An increase in the agricultural surface and loss of forests and herbaceous area were found. The results obtained indicate that most of the changes occurred on Andisol and Inceptol soils. These soils are characterized by a high content of clays as they are derived from ash, and are developed in areas adjacent to the drainage network of the basin
