# CRITICAL TO CONTEMPORARY COMMUNICATION OF THE ARCHITECTURE

This work seeks to reflect on the current communication of architecture from the academic and professional field. The methodology consists in asking questions, which are resolved by means of the contrasting concepts that are sometimes confused, such as project and architecture, image and imagination, artificial intelligence and conscience, pornography and eroticism. In the contrast, fundamental notions of architecture are intertwined as spatiality, the journey and its temporal experience with philosophical reflections about technology, image, or artificial intelligence. It is evident that contemporary communication of architecture is dominated by visuality, to the detriment of the holistic perception of the senses, of time, imagination and the mystery that involves architectural experience. The excessive interest in image, mass communication through social networks and unconsciousness about technological advances have spurred the fading of the physical world, to the point that we have lost contact with others, with matter and reality. It is concluded that we must not lose ourselves in the smoke of communication, to present resistance to the flood of data and information, to recover a consciousness that allows us to return to the real, to the vivid experience of architecture
