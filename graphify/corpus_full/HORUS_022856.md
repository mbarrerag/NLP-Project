# DETERMINATION OF THE PHENOTIICAL DIVERSITY IN RUBUS SP., IN COLLECTION OF THE VEGETAL BANKS OF THE COLOMBIAN NATION

The genus Rubus L., is of the largest number of species within the family Rosaceae. The subgenus Idaeobatus and Rubus have been domesticated and include raspberries and blackberries, highly heterozygous. The first with distribution in Asia, South Africa, Europe and North America. while the second in south america, europe and north america. Reproductive biology, is determined by a two-way biennial cycle of reproduction from vegetative stem (turion) and rooting of the apex of the floriferous stem. Despite these reproductive characteristics, hybridization and apomixis are presented generating genetic inter and intraspecific variability. In Colombia, high variability has been detected in the wild and cultivated states, however, in the ex situ collections it is necessary to expand knowledge, with the purpose of recognizing the variability of the germplasma. Despite these characteristics, the additional collection needs are presented as a strategy of conservation and expand the genetic base. The analysis of the main component (ACP) showed 8 components that together explained 73.48% of the variability in the morphological characteristics of the accesses studied.
