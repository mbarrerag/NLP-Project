# Design of three superstructures of bridges in reinforced concrete and post-stressed concrete drawer beam

According to the requirements of the Colombian standard of design of bridges LRFD-CCP14, in the design and evaluation of structures for bridges, these must comply with certain basic requirements, as they are: safety, utility, deformations, constructibility and economy.Under these parameters the structural design of bridges must be conceived under a global study of all variables. Based on the above, this document proposes a technical comparative analysis and the evaluation of the structural performance of three superstructures of vehicle bridges, for this purpose, the study is divided into two stages: the first, the analysis and structural design, taking into account the requirements of the Colombian Standard of Design of Bridges LRFD-CCP14 and the second, includes the technical comparison of three vehicle bridges. The structure under study of this document corresponds to a vehicle bridge of length 80 m with intermediate supports.
