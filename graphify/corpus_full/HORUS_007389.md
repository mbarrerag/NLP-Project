# Citizen Security and Criminal Economy: An Analysis of Microtrafficking and its Effects in the Cities of Ciudad Bolívar, Bosa and Kennedy, Focusing on Kennedy's Case

The research entitled "Citizen security and criminal economy: an analysis of micro-trafficking and its effects in the localities of Ciudad Bolívar, Bosa and Kennedy, with a focus on the Kennedy case" addresses the intersection between citizen security and the criminal economy, specifically focusing on the phenomenon of security versus micro-trafficking in the aforementioned localities of Bogotá. Development that infers analysis, on how micro-trafficking, a form of criminal economy that involves the trade in small quantities of drugs, affects security, coexistence and daily life critically in the inhabitants of the town Kennedy. Micro-trafficking is a significant problem in Bogotá, due to its impact on citizen security, public health and the local economy. This phenomenon has both positive and negative effects on the communities, depending on how it is handled.
