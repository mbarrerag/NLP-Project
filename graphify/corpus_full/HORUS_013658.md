# Musical reading on the guitar through Colombian music

This work proposes ten pieces with the aim of improving the musical reading in the guitar, using as a basis various genres of Colombian music; some of these works have been adapted and arranged, while others are original compositions for the instrument. The purpose is to help students of basic and intermediate level to develop reading habits in the instrument through these genres, which have been a fundamental axis of musical formation in our country and are considered part of our artistic tradition and cultural heritage of Colombia .
