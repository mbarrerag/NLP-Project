# FICTIONS OF POSSIBLE FUTURES: NARRATIVES OF WOMEN AND LBTIQ+ SIGNATORIES OF THE PEACE AGREEMENTS IN COLOMBIA.

As part of an ongoing ethnographic project, funded by the Fals Borda grant, the chapter put into consideration and discussed at the workshop explores the experiences of people with gender identities and non-normative sexuality, as well as heterosexual and cisgenerous women, who signed the 2016 peace agreement between the Colombian government and the Revolutionary Armed Forces.
