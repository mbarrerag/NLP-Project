# EVALUATION OF FACTORS FOR EXTRACTION OF ANTOCIANINS FROM MILPESILL FRUITS (OENOCARPUS MAPORA H. KARST.) USED NATURAL EUTECTIC SOLVENTS

In order to evaluate the influence of some factors on the eco-friendly extraction of anthocyanins from milpesillo fruits (Oenocarpus mapora), trials were carried out to obtain extracts under different conditions using micro-maceration-assisted extraction and using choline-lactic acid chloride as a natural euthectic solvent (NaDES). The results showed that with a NaDES formed by choline-lactic acid chloride in relation to molar (1:2) containing 60% water combined with a 20 minute extraction time and a solvent sample ratio of 90 mg/mL, it would be an appropriate solvent to maximize yield in terms of extraction of anthocyanins
