# Chemeric peptides derived from Lactoferricin Bovine and Buforin II: antifungal activity against clinical isolates and reference strains of Candida spp.

The reduced number of antifungal molecules and the appearance of resistant clinical isolates such as yeasts of the genus Candida spp. has limited the therapeutic efficacy of antifungals in recent years. In this study we examined the effects of chimeric APMs containing the minimum cause of LfcinB and BFII in C. albicans (CAAL), C. glabrata (CAGL) and C. auris (CAAU), with the evaluation of the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) and minimal fungicide concentration (MFC), kinetics of growth and the combination of QC2 chimera with FLU. Our results indicated that the QC1 and QC2 chimeras exhibited fungal and fungicide activity dependent on concentration against Candida spp resistant, greater activity compared to the minimum cause of LfcinB, the anti-malwood of BFII and the other five chimeras studied; and an effect of additive with fluconazole to be anti-al256 (resistant of FLU), Curitta 2001 and C. glamera C
