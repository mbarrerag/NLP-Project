# Advances in peirce's mathematics: A short survey (1960-2020)

This chapter provides a short survey on advances done on Peirce's work in the period 1960-2020, which use modern mathematical tools in their outlook. A back and forth between global general (philosophical) concepts and local particular (mathematical) techniques is one of the main strengths of Peirce's approach. It enabled him to introduce very precise ideas to support his main conjectures in the following four key areas: (1) the pragmaticist maxim, (2) topology and combinatorics, (3) the continuum, and (4) the existential graphs. The chapter explains how using more contemporary technical perspectives helps us to gain a better understanding of Peirce's work
