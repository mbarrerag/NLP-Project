# On decay and regularity of solutions to the modified 2D Zakharov-Kuznetsov equation

This work is devoted to study the relation between regularity and decay for solutions of the two-dimensional modified Zakharov-Kuznetsov equation in the anisotropic weighted Sobolev spaces Zs,(r1,r2):=Hs(R2)∩L2((1+|x|2r1+|y|2r2)dxdy)
