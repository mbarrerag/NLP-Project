# Development and characterization of reference material for mercury species in fish

Mercury is a widely studied toxic element and the determination of its species has been prioritized, however, there are currently no measurement references available in species characteristic of the Latin American and Amazon region. In this sense the objective of this research was to produce a reference material of total mercury and methylmercury in bagre final. To this end, measurement methods were developed and validated that allowed to determine homogeneity, material stability and measurement of analites of interest. The methods developed had a range between 0.25 mg/kg and 20 mg/kg and for all variations and acceptable biases were obtained. Finally, to solve this problem, a certified reference material of mercury species in catgre striped; the IMM-017-1, with a lot size of 119 units, of 15 g of content with a certified value of HgTotal of 3.94 ± 0.2 mg/kg (k= 1.97) and of MeHped of 1.97 . striped catfish was obtained; the IMM-017-1, with a batch size of 119 units, 15 g content with a certified value of HgTotal of 3.93 ± 0.27 mg/kg (k=1.97) and MeHg 3.79 ± 0.32 mg/kg (k= 1.97)
