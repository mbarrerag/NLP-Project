# Evolution of sedimentary materials from the Quaternary of the Alluvial Plain of Araucana, Colombia.

The araucana alluvial plain (LAA) presents a complex stratigraphy in the various landscape elements that make it up. Although there are different studies in the sector, its recent geological evolution has not been defined. In this work, the abrupt changes of direction in the water tributaries of the Araucano territory have been evaluated, its current distribution, physical characterization, profiles, water parameters (Rivers Casanare, Cravo Norte, Arauca and Cinnaruco), the link with structures in depth and the potential contribution of sediments from the Eastern Cordillera to the LAA. On the other hand, the supervised methodology identifies geoforms of denudal, fluvial and eolic environments as elements that support the current geological evolution model in the LAA, highlighting the dunes in various successive states and paleochannels exhumados (Fpea) model that are associated with the extreme tendency and complexes of the Cranario deposits.
