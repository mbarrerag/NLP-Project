# Study of the homogeneity of ASFACTS USING PETROGRAPH AND IMAGE ANALYSIS

The performance of the asphalt concretes used in road works depends to a large extent on the internal arrangement achieved by the aggregates, asphalt and voids; however, the distribution and interaction between these components are not properly evaluated in the routine quality controls, since homogeneity is represented mainly by characteristics of the aggregates, such as the area they occupy, the relative position and the macro texture, giving less importance to the influence of the other components. In this work, it is deepened in the study of homogeneity achieved by the components of concrete asphalt compacted under controlled conditions in laboratory, with three different contents of asphalt and three compacting energies. For these purposes, microphotographs taken under the microscope of the petrographic samples of concrete asphalts, with the help of specialized software, are analyzed.
