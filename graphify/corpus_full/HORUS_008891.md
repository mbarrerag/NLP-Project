# INCIDENCE OF THE BLOCKS IN THE QUANTITATIVE ESTIMAL ESTIMALITY OF PRECIPTATION BY USING METEOROLOGICAL RADARS IN COLOMBIAN ANDES

The spatial variability of precipitation is difficult to measure due to the lack of rainfall and rainfall stations on surface. As a result, meteorological radars have become crucial sources of information to estimate precipitation fields. However, the locks, which refer to external factors of nature that affect the quality of radar data, pose a significant challenge and contribute to errors and uncertainties in the quantitative precipitation estimation. This work has as main focus to analyze the impact of the blockades on the C band weather radar of Barrancabermeja, located between the Eastern and Central Cordillera of the Colombian Andes, specifically for the quantitative precipitation estimation (QPE). The analysis was carried out using radar information collected during events without rain between 2019 and 2020. To identify the blocking areas, a frequency analysis of the reflectivity in dry conditions was performed, which had a high correlation with the topographic interferences caused by the surrounding ranges of the radar.
