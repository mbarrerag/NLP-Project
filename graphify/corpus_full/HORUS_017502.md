# CONCENTRATION OF SUGARS, ALMIDON AND CAROTENES IN ZAPALLO (CUCURRBITA MOSCHATA) IN TWO AMBIENTES-POST POSECHA

The zapallo Cucurbita moschata, is a vegetable appetizing for the nutritional value of its fruits, the pulp is rich in carotenes, minerals and protein, which makes it attractive for agro-industrial processes. The objective of this research was to analyze in the fruit of Zapallo the behavior of the content of total carotenes, starch and sugars in two environments controlled in post-harvest. The fruits were classified and selected according to the morphological parameters of the fan-75 cultivar, for 12 weeks in 2 environments (amb.): amb. 1: temperature controlled (16°C) and amb. 2: to the environment (28-32°C), three fruits were evaluated per week and environment, for a total of 36 fruits per environment and a variance analysis was performed.
