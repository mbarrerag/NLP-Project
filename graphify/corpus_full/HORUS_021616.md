# Construction of a predictive instrument of psychomotor agitation in adults in the general emergency department.

This work is part of a three-phase project (1. construction of the preliminary instrument, 2. derivation of the predictive instrument, 3. validation of the predictive instrument), which aims to construct an instrument to predict the risk of psychomotor agitation (AP) in patients attending the emergency department of a general hospital. This work will address the first phase of the project “Paste of construction of the preliminary instrument”, in which, through the development of an expanded review of literature and multiple interviews with experts in the field and patients, about psychomotor agitation processes and violence in emergency services, the characteristics of these events and approaches currently available for their prediction are identified; the elements identified by these means will be taken to an informal consensus process by the research group, where items for the construction of the preliminary instrument are selected to be applied in the second portion of the project
