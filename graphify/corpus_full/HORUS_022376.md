# OS REVESES DA AOUTENCE: AS RACIAL CHESTOES NO ACADEMIC PRODUCATE DO SERVICO SOCIAL NO BRAZIL (1936-2013)

Reivindicado por segmentes professionales militantes del trabajo Social as important field of studies for the specialization and recognition of users and users of social services the study of the racial questions does not gain notoriety as in other areas of the Social and Human Sciences in Brazil. The book analyzes the course and expression of this topic in academic literature, from the historical and contemporary point of view in Social Work in the period 1936-2013. In this course I analyze as the academic environment of the discipline inhibits and/or promotes, motivates or questions the initiatives of production of research and studies on the racial issues. The book also examines how this topic is studied from the Social Work, which are the issues that stand out and which are the most used theoretical references. They were analyzed monographic works of the first cohorts of égresadas de la campo Social founded in Brazil, articles of scientific journals specialized in the area, works presented in scientific events, theses and disertations of post-graduate programs in Social Work, with reference to the multidimensional method in terms of qualitative, quantitative approach, studies of science and documentary analysis.
