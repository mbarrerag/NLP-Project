# Knowledge and perceptions of primary school teachers about difficulties in learning in reading in Colombian educational contexts

The specific difficulties of learning in reading “DEAL” are associated with low performance and academic failure, generating negative impacts at social or emotional level. The evidence recommends early intervention as the most effective course of action. Thus, the first grades of school are an ideal space for the identification of students at risk and to provide timely attention, so it is essential that the teachers have sufficient and updated knowledge. Having this in mind, it is interest of this study to analyze the knowledge of teachers of primary basic on the DEALs and to propose a series of recommendations for their approach. A questionnaire was designed that consists of three main sections: sociodemographic information, knowledge about the DEALs and perceptions of the teachers. The instrument was carried out by teachers of public and private schools of different academic performance in the city of Bogotá and a frequency analysis by categories and variables was carried out through nonparametric tests. The participants showed greater skills to identify signs and manifestations, compared with their understanding of the approach and the nature of difficulties in the learning. The knowledge and perceptions were positively related to the years of experience, previous experience and the specialized training of the participating teachers.
