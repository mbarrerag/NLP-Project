# Production costs of Tithonia diversifolia and Zea corn mays gold button silage

At present, a deep-rooted, native, pantropical expansion herb Tithonia diversifolia (Hemsl.) A. Gray (TD), a perennial shrubland, is researched in Colombia, with proven potential as a fodder plant, which has been evaluated in silvopastoral systems and as silage for small ruminants.The objective was to develop an economic analysis of the silage process of Tithonia diversifolia (STD) compared to the silage of Maize (SMA) complete plant.To estimate the costs of production and silage of STD and SMA, a matrix was developed based on technical manuals of Agrosavia and data of prices of Dane.The analysis was divided into three phases: establishment of cultivation, preparation of the silage and evaluation of productive indicators. Finally, a total of SMA indicators were applied to the cost of production and silage of STD, TIR and ROI to assess profitability and, reached a differential cost of 85.461 and 157.687 COP$.ton-1 for STD and SMA respectively.
