# INTEGRATED AGROACCUULTURE BETWEEN INDIGENOUS COMMUNITIES: AN EXPERIENCE OF SOCIAL APPROPRIATION OF KNOWLEDGE IN COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

This text describes and analyses the experience of implementing an integrated agro-acuculture system (AAI) in an Arhuaca community in the Colombian Caribbean, with an emphasis on social appropriation of knowledge. Here we detail the accompaniment to the community and the way in which new technologies were integrated into traditional practices. The results show an important commitment to the initiative and show how the community solves crisis situations arising from historical processes of violence and dispossession, as well as the serious environmental deterioration in the region and climate change
