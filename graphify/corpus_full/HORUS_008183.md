# TRANSIMPERIAL COMMERCE, MARITIME LOOTING AND NETWORKS OF COMPLICITY IN THE MID-EIGHTEENTH CENTURY IN THE CARIBBEAN SEA

This article analyzes the role played by companies corsair in the Caribbean sea in the EIGHTEENTH century. Through the procedure of these companies we can appreciate the articulation of Caribbean regional trade in a scenario of imperial struggles and conflicts for the control of American trade. In particular, we analyze the Spanish privateering actions after the loss of the mercantile monopoly in the Caribbean after the Treaty of Utrecht
