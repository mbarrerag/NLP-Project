# EFFECTIVENESS OF TOPICAL SEVOLPLURAN IN THE CICATRIZATION PROCESS IN VENOUS VASCULATING DUCERS

The chronic vascular ulcers of venous etiology is a disease of difficult management due to its high degree of complexity and the impact on the economic, social level that generates, affecting the quality of life of the people who suffer it. The vascular venous ulcers have an incidence between 2 and 5 cases per thousand people per year and is frequent in women who in men 1. Consequently, these lesions are usually very painful at the time of cleaning and changes of the dressings, coming to interfere in the therapeutic management and therefore in the healing process.2 Among the innovative practices in wound management, the application of sevoflurane topical in wounds has been considered, demonstrated solid evidence on the analgesic and healing efficacy. Description of the problem or need: Despite the conservative treatment with compressive therapy, it remains a challenge for nurses and the health system for the treatment of wounds especially of venous vascular origin, due to the high rates of recurrence and the healing time. 75%). With active ulcer 32 years ago polymedicated and multitreated with different therapies, it is treated for 4 months with topical applications with sevofloran plus elastocompressive therapy, observing a 50% closure of the lesion, decrease of the exudate, with a healing and analgesic effect. In addition, a review of literature is carried out in search of scientific evidence and the efficacy of sevoflurane in wounds is corroborated
