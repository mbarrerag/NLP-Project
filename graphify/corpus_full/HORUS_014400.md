# Major cardiovascular events in patients admitted to a university hospital in Bogotá in 2020, a prevalence study.

It is estimated that perioperative cardiovascular events after non-cardiac surgery occur in 3% of hospital admissions by surgery (1), these events represent a significant health burden. This study seeks to describe the frequency of these events in a determined period of time in a hospital of iv level in Colombia, a descriptive, retrospective, cross-sectional study was carried out; adult patients admitted were included, electively for non-cardiac surgery during 2020, a total of 132 patients were included, with an average age of 50.8 years (SD 14.6), 66 patients (50%) female, prevalence was 3%, with a total of 3 events, of which acute myocardial infarction and acute lung edema were documented in 1 patient (0.76%) and acute lung edema in 3 patients (2.3%), respectively, cardiovascular events were exclusively presented in male patients (3 events, 100%), none of whom presented intrahospital deaths and no stroke; all patients with cardiovascular events were hypertensive 3 patients (100%), the similar geographic origin was distributed as follows: Bogotá 124 patients (94%), other municipalities of Cunna, 100%, none of whom presented as a possible association
