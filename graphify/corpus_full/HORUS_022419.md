# CONFLICT GEOGRAPHY OF THE PEACE-AGREED COLOMBIA. GEOGRAPHICAL INFORMATION

The article gives a general overview of Colombia's main territorial transformations since the 1990s and deepens the changes to the process in recent years, following the signing of the Peace Agreement with the FARC guerrillas. Demographic and social developments allow us to understand the expression of new demands, especially of young people, who are confronted with a model of economic development producing inequalities and physical, material and symbolic violence. During the post-Peace Agreement period, new expressions of social, environmental and cultural conflicts have emerged and redefined territorial relations
