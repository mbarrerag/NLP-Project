# STATUS OF FOOD SECURITY DIAGNOSTIC IN ARGENTINA, BRAZIL, COLOMBIA AND ENGLAND POST COVID-19

The article analyzes initiatives to promote food security in Argentina, Brazil, Colombia and England based on the design of a situational diagnosis of the countries mentioned, in the post-Covid-19 period. It is a recent secondary data of local and multilateral research agencies that question the range of indicators of wealth and poverty in the face of food insecurity among the most vulnerable population. It also advances on the way to produce large-scale food, especially the economy based on commodities, questioning the sovereignty in food production and the contradiction with the premises for sustainable development. It seeks to show evidence about the implementation of some social programs and policies. In those countries, to care for families in situations of social vulnerability.
