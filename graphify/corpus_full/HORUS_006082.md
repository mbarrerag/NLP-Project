# Challenges for governance actors in the sustainability of tourism-friendly socio-ecosystems in Colombia

The aim of this study was to analyze the main challenges for the actors in the configuration of governance structures that allow the sustainability of socio-ecosystems with tourism use in Colombia, a qualitative design with quantitative moments was applied, in which the direct observation was used, was surveyed to a total of PNNN (94), Saleno-Cocora (113) and San Cipriano (77) tourism actors, were interviewed in total PNNN (24), Saleno-Cocora (27) and San Cipriano (18) and three focal groups were carried out in the PNNN and Saleno-Cocora and 2 in San Cipriano. The instruments allowed to collect data to identify the actors, describe their relations, characterize the socio-ecological conflicts, describe the governance structures and propose the scenarios that are prospective for sustainable tourism use in each case of study. Different analysis techniques were used, such as the Analysis of Social Networks (ARS), the characterization of the socio-ecological conflicts and the methods Mic-Mactor for the alternative scenarios. The results show that the main challenges for the actors of governance and the with tourism in Colombia. The study used a quantitative design with quantitative elements.
