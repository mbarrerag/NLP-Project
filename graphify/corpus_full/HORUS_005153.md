# The transformation of the courtyard a critical look at the formal and structural aspects that re-signify it from the void in contemporary architecture

The present master's thesis entitled “The transformation of the courtyard. A critical look at the formal and structural aspects that re-signify it from the vacuum in contemporary architecture.” focuses on the evolution of the concept and the functionality of the courtyard in the context of contemporary architecture. The research analyzes the morphology of the void and its impact both on the formal aspect and on the programmatic use of contemporary architectural spaces, using a qualitative and quantitative methodological approach through the study of selected cases. The work ends with the design of a specific architectural project, the Aguaviva Library, which incorporates the design strategies identified in the research, such as the manipulation of the void to create spatial and programmatic innovative dynamics. The results demonstrate how the reinterpretation of the courtyard, understood as a vacuum space, significantly contributes to the flexibility, social interaction and sustainability in contemporary architecture. .
