# CONDITIONS OF ACCESSIBILITY OF THE PRIVATE AUTHORITY OF THE PROPOSAL FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A CYCLIST INFRASTRUCTURE

Abstract This research aims to determine the impact on the operation of the mode of transport in private vehicles, given the proposal to implement the new network of cycling routes in the city of Manizales (Colombia). Interventions in road infrastructure, associated with the implementation of sustainable autonomous transport systems, have an impact on the operability of motor transport modes, due to the structural requirements for the safety and operation of both. The methodology contemplates the application of models of supply of transport infrastructure as a basis for assessment, supported by analysis of geographical accessibility, coverage and geospatial impact. As a main result, it was obtained that the proposal for implementation generates an impact of up to 3% of the average travel times in private vehicles, which concludes that the accessibility conditions of users of such mode of transport do not change significantly. Key words: geographical accessibility; road infrastructure; private mobility; model; coverage;
