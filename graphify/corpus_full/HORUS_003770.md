# NARRATIVE LITERATURA REVIEW: INNOVATION CAPACITIES IN SME Entrepreneurship SUSTAINABILITY

Despite the increase in the trend of research on innovation capacity in SMEs, no reviews of previous literature focused on business sustainability were found. Therefore, this study aimed to carry out a narrative analysis of literature on the facilitators of innovation capacities in SMEs and their mediators in business sustainability, through the narrative review of literature of 81 documents through the Scopus and WoS databases and a brief descriptive analysis of scientific production through Scopus. The revised literature leads to the conclusion that SMEs that are receptive to external facilitators and that encourage internal facilitators generate innovation capabilities and these through mediators contribute to business sustainability in the economic field; with export performance, marketing performance and corporate orientation, in the environmental field; with green innovation, technological innovation and corporate orientation and in the social field; with open innovation and corporate orientation, in order to create competitive advantages for the survival and growth of SMEs in the long term
