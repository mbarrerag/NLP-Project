# Implementation and validation of additive manufacturing processes of ceramic and metal components in medical manufacturing and in components for industrial applications

A project is proposed that takes advantage of the work developed in additive manufacturing of metal components to strengthen the research training of several of the students of the seedling. The work is part of a program aimed at recycling high-value metal and ceramic materials, in an effort aimed at environmental sustainability based on circular economy. To this end, it is proposed to produce components of hard metal and alumina-based ceramic, developing the filament to create pieces by deposition of cast filament and subsequent sintering. To this end, the values of critical processing factors such as premixing temperatures of filaments, depolymerization methods, extrusion temperatures and parameters for sintered processes should be discovered. For the control of geometry and dimensions of the components, the use of advanced metrology using optical measuring equipment with focal variation is proposed
