# From higher education to professional environments: emerging learning from the study of internships and internships

The book contains research results from thirteen universities (12 Colombians and 1 Mexican) that, through a case study methodology, contributed to a multi-case study whose objective was to “characterize the previous learning and competencies required by different work environments in which students from different university programs perform professional practices.” From the thirteen contributions, and with an interdisciplinary approach, it is contributed to discussions on: “soft” and “hard” competencies; the adjustment of the practices to this “new reality” from the virtual; the relationship of professional practice to the discharge profiles; the review of pedagogical practices, within the framework of the educational quality and national policies to improve it; institutional responsibility for the exercise of insertion of students in non-academic contexts; the need for analysis of contextual problems in the various curricular components; the possibilities of new contexts of professional exercise; opportunities and restrictions of professional practice; the forms of accompaniment to the practitioner and intern; the relationship and tension between professional and ethical dimensions; the variations in acts of orality, reading and writing in the communicative environments of academic, professional or organizational, against the forms of support and practice; the relationship between the professional and the practice;
