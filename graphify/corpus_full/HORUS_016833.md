# Presentation

This issue 33 of the magazine Ciencia Política appears with some delay, but with conjunctural compensation. It is dedicated to remember with plural analytical lens the new student and university movements that are triggered by the protests lived from February 25 and 26, 1971, with epicenter at the University of the Valley ad-portas to celebrate in Cali the Pan American Games
