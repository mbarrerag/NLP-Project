# GENETIC ASSESSMENT FOR MILK PRODUCTION AT 305 DAYS IN VACAS OF THE LUCER COLOMBIAN REASON CONSIDERING PARTS DIFFERENT

The production of milk accumulated up to 305 days (P305), has been the main criterion of selection in milk breeds. Two approaches can be used in genetic evaluation (EG) for P305: a repeatability model (REP) or a multi-character model (MC). In the Lucerne breed, there are no works comparing both approaches in genetic improvement for P305. to perform genetic evaluation for milk production accumulated up to 305 days by means of two approaches: repeatability and multi-character, to identify alternatives in genetic improvement of this characteristic in the Lucerne breed. P305 information of two sheaths Lucerne from Colombia was used.
