# Model for the analysis of the relationship architecture structure. Case: The architecture of Simón Vélez - Caldas, Colombia 1979-1999

It provides a model for the analysis of the relation architecture structure, through the study of the work of architect Simón Vélez built in the department of Caldas, Colombia between 1979 and 1999, in order to provide a tool that is applicable to the study of the work of other architects. By means of the recognition of the evolution of this relationship and of the concepts implicit in it, two terms are defined under which the methodological model is developed: form and tectonicity. In addition to this, we study works with a similar approach in order to retake aspects that may be applicable to the own model, which is proposed as a case of comparative study of units that are selected works. The retrospective and a synthesis of the life and work of Vélez are presented from its beginnings to the period studied, to investigate the influences and to try to understand which are the most relevant aspects of its architecture and the relationship of this with the structure.
