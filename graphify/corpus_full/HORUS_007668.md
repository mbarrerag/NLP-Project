# Optoelectronic sensors embedded in 3D printed structures for monitoring the wrist mobility range

This project proposes the use of 3D manufacturing technologies to build sensor structures that can adapt to the human body and monitor position variables that allow to evaluate the range of movement of the wrist. The sensor device that is proposed to be incorporated within the 3D structures consists of fiber optic sensors that can measure the deformation of a structure (i.e. the compression or elongation of this structure). Fiber optic sensors are suitable for this type of devices because of their small size (<125 um), their immunity to electromagnetic interference and measurement accuracy
