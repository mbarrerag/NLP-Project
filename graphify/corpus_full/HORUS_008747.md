# ANAGULAR DIFFERENCE IDENTIFICATION IN THE PHOTOVOLTICAL ENERGY DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM

The angular behavior of the electrical system has an interest in study by the phase difference between the angles of the voltages in two nodes, if they are interconnected in the electrical grid allows to establish an indicator of the stability of the system. In this article is presented the implementation of a WAMS network application for the study of the angular difference in the electrical system of the University Campus, this by applying a categorization algorithm that allowed to group events related to the angular difference between two points of the network related to a solar generation system, this application allowed to find that the phase angle of the tension between the two nodes where the PMUs are located has oscillations associated with the photovoltaic generation, characterizing the operating region of the system related to its angular difference in a node of the system
