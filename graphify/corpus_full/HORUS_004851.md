# An analytical proposal for the conceptualization and empirical study of economic vulnerability: a pilot for the Medellín case

This research aims to make a critical approach to the concept of economic vulnerability and to theoretically analyze the set of its definition criteria, main systems of understanding, as well as its attributes and difficulties of application. From this inquiry, a proposal will be made to redesign the analytical content of the vulnerability, as well as the parameters of operationalization and empirical observation applicable to this concept within the economic field. For the execution of this objective, a theoretical inquiry will be made on the analytical problems of the concept of vulnerability and the way in which they impact their disciplinary application in economic matters. Once exposed and verified the difficulties present in the theoretical approaches around the concept of vulnerability, this research provides a concept of vulnerability theoretically debuged that links its conceptual content to a set of observation criteria and empirical characterization applicable to the economic field, this without the limitations evidenced from the other concepts available around the concept of vulnerability. al, a theoretical investigation will be conducted on the analytical challenge surrounding the concept of vulnerability and how these issues affect its disciplinary application in economics. After identifying and contrasting the limitations inherent in existing theoretical formulations, this research proposals a refined concept of vulnerability. This revised notion link the conceptual content to a set of empirical observation and characterisation criteria that are applicable within the economic domain, overcoming the strategies identified in previous conceptualizations. Building on this theoretical and operational reform, the study will implement a pilot application of the analytical proposal, focusing on the characteristic vulnerability in the context of Medellín.
