# CSR review from John Searle's social ontology.

This project proposes a conceptual reflection on Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) from the framework of the ontology of social reality by John R. Searle. It is intended at first to show the background of CSR, the criticisms that have arisen with respect to it and to show that it needs a more solid foundation at the theoretical level. At a second moment it is sought to review and reconstruct the proposal of the ontology of social reality, to show that companies are institutional social facts (HSI). At a third moment, and from the two previous ones, it will be asked about the logical possibilities and limits of CSR. The proposed work is of a theoretical type, in the sense that it seeks to review and reconstruct the main elements and relations that, from Searle, can explain the role that organizations and CSR perform in an institutional fact system. The proceeding is of a deductive nature, since after the reconstruction carried out it is sought to infer the possible implications of this knowledge on CSR. The final document will be the result of a work of a bibliographic, analytical, critical, interpretative and argumentative nature.
