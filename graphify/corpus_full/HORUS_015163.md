# Effects of chronic altitudinal hypoxia on the Redox balance of preteens and adolescents

Abstract Introduction: living in height is a factor that is associated with oxidative stress. Growth and maturation can be an additional stressor. It is insufficient evidence on redox profile alterations in peripubers resident at moderate altitudes. The purpose was to establish the effect of living at a moderate altitude on the redox and inflammatory profile in preadolescent and healthy adolescents. cross-sectional study in preadolescent and healthy adolescents (9-18 years) living at low altitude (n = 26) or moderate altitude (n = 26). The plasmatic oxidative state and thiols were evaluated by means of spectrophotometry through oxidation markers (malondialdeside and hydroxynonennal and carbonyl groups) and antioxidants (total antioxidant status, glutathione, catalase, dismutous superoxide, uric acid and higher and moderate altitude).
