# THE FIRST ASTRONOMIC OBSERVATORY OF AMERICA

the director of the national astronomical observatory of colombia, mario a. Higuera, reconstructs the history of the first astronomical observatory in the Americas, founded in Santa Fé de Bogotá by Spain in the former Viceroyalty of New Granada (present-day Colombia, Venezuela, Panama and Ecuador and parts of Peru, Brazil and Guyana) in 1803.
