# Spectral behaviour of bananas with Foc R1 infection: analysis of Williams and Gros Michel clones

The Marchitez by Fusarium is the greatest threat to the world production of musaceae, for its detection are proposed techniques of remote detection based on spectroscopic reflectance. The spectral response of leaves of healthy plants and infected with Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense Raza1 (Foc R1) in two banana cultivars, during the period of incubation of the disease was characterized. The spectra of 400-1000 nm were measured in plants healthy and infected with Foc R1 of banana Gros Michel (GM: susceptible) and Williams (W: resistant) with a portable spectrometer Ocean Optics HR2000+. General patterns were obtained in the similar spectras for both cultivars in the Vis, about 25% in the green region, but as the foliar development progress decreased the reflectance throughout the spectra-range, about 12.5% (Green region of the Vis range) in the leaf of both.
