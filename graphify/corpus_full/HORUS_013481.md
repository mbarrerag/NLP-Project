# Contributing to the reconstruction of the living conditions and context of pre-Hispanic mummified individuals in Colombia, a bioarcheological approach

The collection of prehispanic individuals and momified data of the Laboratory of Physical Anthropology of the National University had application of new techniques of research allowing to establish data to deepen the knowledge of life in the past and to point out contextual information of prehispanic mummies. For the 36 individuals, between mummies and masked skulls, new codes, photographs and scanners were established. The analysis is carried out by taking samples of bone tissue in 24 individuals for dating, 25 for stable isotops N15, C13 and 018 in collagen and bioapatite finally with 17 for Sr86 and 8 contextualized samples of control. The dating establishes a temporality of prehispanic mummification being remounted to dates of the Late Formative. The possible places of origin by proximity to the contrast samples discarded regions as Water of God and the Serranía del Perijá, having affinity in Andean areas such as Santander and Boyacá. The areas of life by 018 were divided in High, Low and Middle Lands. A dietary reconstruction supports a diet based on the C4 with differences in the Late Protein.
