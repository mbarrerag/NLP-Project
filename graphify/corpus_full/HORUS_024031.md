# DYNAMIC OLTRAFILTRATION - MODELATION OF SELECTIVITY INTENSIFICATION

The polarization of concentration and soiling are two important problems in the ultrafiltration (UF) and microfiltration (MF) processes. Due to its extensive field of application and hybridization potential with new technologies, it is important to develop a mathematical model that can predict the behavior of these phenomena that affect membrane performance and thus validate the intensifying effect of dynamic operation. The aim of this work is to develop and adjust two mathematical models of hybrid nature that allow predicting the behavior of concentrations of the polarisation layer in UF and MF with dynamic operation, using experimental data of UF with dextrane T500. The results indicate that the dynamic operation intensifies the performance of the UF process in terms of rejection with respect to conventional operation.
