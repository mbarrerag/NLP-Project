# Bovine leukosis virus in the dairy sector of Antioquia: molecular prevalence, circulating genotypes and factors associated with its circulation

The bovine leukosis virus (BLV) is a retrovirus that affects specialized dairy production systems worldwide, related to lower milk production and reduction in the life of infected animals. Eleven genotypes of the virus circulating have been identified and there is currently no treatment or vaccine for the disease so control strategies are essential. In Colombia, dairy contributes to the economy and food support of the country, Antioquia is the largest producer department. To establish the current state of the BLV in the production systems of Antioquia, 575 cattle belonging to 53 hectares of North, East and Aburra Valley regions, of different races and ages were selected. The characterization of the productive systems was made, molecular prevalence was studied by means of a nested PCR of the BLV gene, resulting in 17.04 per cent in animals and 75.5 per cent in hatos and by a phylogenetic study of the tax gene establishing the presence only of the genotype.
