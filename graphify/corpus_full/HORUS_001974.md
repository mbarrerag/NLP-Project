# Spatial distribution analysis of a particulate aquaculture waste collector for floating cages using computational modelling tools

The proper management of sedimentable waste is a challenge that confronts daily aquaculture in floating cages, particularly in shallow waters with limited flow, product of the constant discharges of organic waste associated with the feeding processes, which travel through the water column and accumulate in nearby sediments. This study establishes a multiphase model in steady state, which allows to analyze the influence of the liquid phase on the dispersion of different solid particles released from a floating cage in a aquatic environment of stable behavior, where no significant fluid changes are presented in the time. It consists of a set of dynamic methods of computational fluids (CFD) and discrete phase models (DPM), coupled with the model of actualable turbulence and the method of jumping porous, with the purpose of assessing numerically the performance of a watery waste collector while varying its spatial distribution, under standard flow conditions (0.006m.s-1 and 0.138m.s-1 ).
