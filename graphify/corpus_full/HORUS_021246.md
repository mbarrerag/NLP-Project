# Study of the potential for antiobesity of species of the genus Piper via inhibition of Pancreatic Lipase

The digestive enzyme pancreatic lipase, plays an important role in the metabolism of lipids, constituting a therapeutic target attractive for the treatment of obesity and other related complications.The treatment of these diseases includes a program of dietary control and methods like the physical exercise, the use of supplements and medications.The drugs that are used present some dangerous side effects for health, besides that many are no longer effective.So it is necessary to search for effective and safe substances that help the treatment of these pathologies, finding in the plants a potential source to undertake research in this field and that in turn can promote the development of the bioeconomy.The species of the genus Piper are popularly known as cordoncils or anises and are important worldwide by their wide use by the native populations with culinary or medicinal purposes.In Colombia, the species of this genus are widely distributed throughout the national territory and many of these species lack studies at scientific level.Although it is known in a preliminary way that some species of Piper contains substances capable of inhibiting digestive enzymes related to obesity, it is necessary to develop the genus of this genus
