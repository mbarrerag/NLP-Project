# Geotechnical classification system for road projects based on quality index (HSQI), application and validation of susceptibility to sliding in the Pacific route 2.

The calibration and validation of the methodology of geotechnical classification of slopes for road projects based on the quality index of HSQI slopes is proposed, which allows to establish in a simple but systematic way the sectorization of the road corridor according to its susceptibility to sliding, taking into account the projected geometry, geomorphology, hydrogeology and soil mechanics. This is intended to obtain accurate information on sectors that may present problems of instability during the different phases of the road project, giving elements to improve the forecast of works and costs associated with the stabilization of the corridor. The initial calibration and validation of the HSQI methodology was carried out in the Las Palmas road corridor, in the municipality of Medellín, which has a bimodal rainfall distribution and geology composed mainly of metamorphic rocks. Additionally, a calibration was carried out with another track located in a tropical mountainous region in the northern part of Costa Rica, with climatic and geology conditions different from those foreseen in the initial validation, with a regime of intense rains throughout the year and a geology characterized mainly by volcanic ash and pyroplastic flows.
