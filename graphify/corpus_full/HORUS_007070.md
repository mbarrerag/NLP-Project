# Self-managed settlements in Bogotá. A process conceived from two logics 1972-2020

This work seeks to contribute to the understanding of the phenomenon of self-managed settlements in Bogotá during the period 1972-2020: its determinants, three of the main models as it manifests itself (pirate urbanisation, invasion and public programs of lots with services), and the logics of the communities to develop them and of the State to exercise a reactive administration. The studies are examined that explain it as problem of economic backwardness, social phenomenon and result of the community development. UPZ Alfonso López (Usme), the New Chile neighborhood (Bosa) and the program of lots with services of Sierra Morena (Ciudad Bolívar), constitute the cases of study where they are analyzed, based on documentary and quantitative information from indirect sources (institutional mainly), three contradictions derived from the logics that the State and the communities have adopted for its consolidation. As conclusions are identified the challenges of capitalizing the potential of the settlements to expand the supply, types and forms of housing tenure; considering that the settlements generate a good part of the resources for the progressive development of housing, reflect on a greater allocation of public expenditure that complements the economic effort of the household; implementing the “legal plan”, to the social measures, recognizing the social development of housing. UPZ (Usme), the New Chile needborhood (Bosa) and the program of serviced lots in Sierra Morena (Ciudad Bolívar), constitute the case studies where, based on documentation and quantitative information from indirect sources, three offences derived from the logistics that the State and the communities have adopted for their consolidation are analyzed.
