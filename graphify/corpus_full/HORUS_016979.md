# Model for quantifying the expected effects of electrical demand management strategies

Abstract The main objective of this article was to conceptualize, implement and validate a dynamic quantitative model that would allow to find the response of a group of users to different strategies of energy demand management that are offered to them. As a result of a bibliographic review, a model was conceptualized that includes classic techniques of demand response, proposes new actions of the client and other novel aspects such as culture and energy pedagogy. The general structure that interrelations the actions of the client, proposals to demand, the cost-benefit analysis and the customer response were established. Mathematical models were established and implemented in Modelic language. Simulations were carried out for intra-day and inter-day displacement strategies, using real values of the Colombian electricity sector and different price factors. Finally, the graphs obtained for changes in the daily consumption profiles, cost of energy, variation of the peak of the system, changes in the load duration curve. The main conclusions obtained are: (i) Culture and energy pedagogy are essential elements to accelerate the customer response time, (ii) The value of the invoiced by the client is more quickly reduced
