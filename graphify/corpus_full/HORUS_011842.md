# NEW SPACE: A NEW SOCIETY CONNECTED FROM SPACE TO LAND LIGHT

After more than half a century since the first steps in space exploration, achievements were achieved that previously seemed restricted to science fiction, space sciences still have new interests, growing challenges, and new actors. For several decades space agencies around the world have been making efforts to launch satellites and create platforms that allow us to study and monitor our planet. Anyone in the world can access this information virtually free of charge, it is enough to have some minimal knowledge. On the other hand, the cost of launching satellites is increasingly reduced, allowing private companies to launch entire constellations of satellites for internet access and Earth observation, among others
