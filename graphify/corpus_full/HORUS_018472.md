# FOOD LERS AND DISPERDIES IN CHILE AND COLOMBIA: A LOOK FROM THE CORCITICAL AGRO-FOOD MARKETING CIRCULATIONS

This chapter analyses the picture of the ADPs by deepening in the cases of Chile and Colombia, as middle-income nations that correspond according to their gross domestic product to the fourth and fifth economies of the region, and the third and fifth countries by population, respectively (about 65 million inhabitants corresponding to 10% of the population of the region). The ADPs are also analyzed in relation to agro-food marketing circuits, since food distribution logistics is one of the most important factors to explain the volumes of food wasted, which has greater relevance in Latin America. It is emphasized in the case of short marketing circuits given their potential to reduce ADPs against long circuits or chains
