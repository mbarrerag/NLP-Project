# Spontaneous motility patterns as an indicator of muscle tone progression in infants attended at Hospital San Rafael, Bogotá.

From a physiological perspective, this research presents a close approach to the evaluation of spontaneous motility based on observation, characterizing the patterns of movement of 9 infants with the objective of establishing whether there is a relationship with the progression of muscle tone in this population. Based on a multilevel review, the conceptual evolution of what have been the scales of evaluation of the newborn was documented, finding the lack of an integrative element that accounts for the function beyond the topographic and/or anatomical in the motor development. Hence, the active muscle tone was defined as a component of the traditional neurological evaluation, revealing a condition of processing much more complex than what is known as a reflected response; for the evaluation of spontaneous movement, a model of analysis of the results was established in three categories proposed by the researchers: quality, harmony, and symmetry of the movement.
