# COLOMBIAN SNAKEBITE GEOVISOR / GEOVISOR COLOMBIAN OF OFIDISM

Among the South American countries, Colombia ranks third in the number of snake bites per year (~4.469) and sixth in snake bite incidents per 100,000 inhabitants (~9.27). 66.9% of clinically important poisoning cases are caused by viperids and elaptors. These data highlight the great human-serpent conflict in Colombia, which has been historically neglected. Although Colombia has a robust epidemiological surveillance system, it does not have mechanisms that facilitate territorial compression of the oestism and the human-serpent conflict. Likewise, it lacks digital tools that compile in an articulated way the records of geographical distributions of the snakes of medical importance, the registers of bites, as well as their associated social and environmental determinants.
