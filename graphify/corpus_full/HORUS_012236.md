# WATER MICROGOTS MEASUREMENT AND CONTEE METHODOLOGY USING DIGITAL IMAGE PROCESSING

This article synthesizes a procedure performed for the counting and measurement of the area and diameter of individual drops from a piezoelectric fog generator. An optical set based on a camera was constructed to capture images of water drops against light. Using ImageJ software, the auto-localized thresholding technique Sauvola was applied, simultaneously binarizing the focused drops and discarding the blurs. Subsequently, the area and diameter of the drops were calculated; data that were processed using MATLAB. The results show that the method behaves adequately both in the binarization of the focused drops and in the discarding of the defocused drops in a single step, resulting in a reliable count of drops with a measurement of 5 precision micrometers
