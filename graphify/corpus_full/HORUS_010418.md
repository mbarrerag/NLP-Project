# Study of the applicability of standard electromagnetic propagation models in the millimetric wave band for 5G systems in Bogotá.

Electromagnetic wave propagation models are essential tools for the design and implementation of wireless communications technologies, with the millimetre wave band being a potential candidate for the implementation of fifth generation (5G) communications. This requires a propagation model that provides a true prediction of the reality of wave propagation behavior in this frequency. Different studies show that these waves are strongly influenced by environmental factors, so their behavior may differ from place to place, with great importance being given to perform a relevant validation of the predictions given by the model for an environment where technology is expected to be implemented using this band. This work aims to develop a test bench and carry out a campaign of measures that validates the model of loss per trajectory in this frequency band
