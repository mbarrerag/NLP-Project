# NUTRITIONAL CONSEQUEMENT IN GERIATRIC SYNDROMES IN THE MAJOR ADULT: A LITERATURA REVIEW WITH WORDS TO PROMOTE HEALTHY AXYING

Fundamentals: Nutritional counseling in the promotion of healthy aging and prevention of geriatric syndromes, in a context of comprehensive care in a prevention model have been related to an impact on the quality of life in elderly patients. In this review it was intended to gather scientific evidence on factors to be taken into account in nutritional counseling in older adults to promote healthy eating habits, according to the Declaration Preferred Reporting Items in Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses PRISMA. The search was carried out in the databases PubMed, ScienceDirect and Embase. Thirty articles were included. Nutrition counseling in older adults addresses recommendations on healthy eating and hydration, including diet changes in appetite loss, mental health nutrition and nutrition to prevent weight loss and malnutrition.
