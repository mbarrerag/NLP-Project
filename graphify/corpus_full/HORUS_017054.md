# Inquiring and playing in learning the concept of living.

The teaching of the sciences in the current educational paradigms has an evident emphasis on the development of competences and skills that allow the understanding of the environment and the development of social and emotional competences. Thus, and within the didactic proposals that, as alternatives to traditional teaching, supplement the requirements of the 21st century, are recognized and used the methodology of teaching of the game and the inquiry. Therefore, it is essential to implement an integrative approach of playful and research experiences as central axes of teaching and learning concepts such as that of living; concept on which organistic and ecosystem notions are generated and intermixed that allow the understanding of the environment around us. In this sense, and from the mixed approach and the sequential exploratory design with descriptive and interpretative scope, the present study determines the influence of integrating the characteristics of the ludic and inquiring approach in the elaboration and implementation of a didactic unit that, it is worth noting a novel focus when considering that the articles associated with the teaching of living beings has been mainly addressed in the experimental emphasis, so determined the research that correlates the research approach with the research and the activity
