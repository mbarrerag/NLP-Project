# Urban reconfiguration in the sector of the Verbenal 1, Canaima canal, Bogotá

This Final Project of Mastery (TFM) presents a formal and spatial solution to the sociospatial deterioration of the Canaima Canal and its environment (the San Carlos College and Verbenal neighborhood 1) in the north of Bogotá, Colombia. The solution is achieved by the spatial reconfiguration of the Canaima Canal and the polygonal barrier created by the San Carlos College. This approach is structured by an urban operation based on a methodological framework that combines the analysis of the site with the exploration of urban and theoretical concepts to develop an urban project. The project incorporates four main concepts: physical and visual permeability, spatial fragmentation, nodality as a dynamic element and connectivity, which links the public spaces of meeting or contemplation with the equipment and residential architecture. These concepts have been materialized in three main components: a north front with urban equipment of an educational and cultural character, a south front with housing and commerce, and a central platform that transforms the body of water into a landscape and spatial axis for both edges. s a dynamic element, and connectivity, which links public gathering or contemplation spaces with facilities and residential architecture.
