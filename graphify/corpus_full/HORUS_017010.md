# NUEVA LANGUAGE: HISTORY AND UPDATE STATE OF THEIR REVITALIZATION PROCESS

The nonuya is one of the three surviving languages of the Witoto linguistic family, which also includes Murui (uitoto) and Ocaina. The Nonuya were a large people, almost exterminated during the cautery period at the beginning of the 20th century. Their descendants (about 100 people) lost their father tongue and adopted the language of other groups. Since 1992, this nonuya group by lineage, but not by language, decided to reaffirm their identity and formed a new community. They began a search for surviving relatives in the Peruvian Amazon, and managed to find three speakers with whom a process of re-learning and documentation of the language was initiated.
