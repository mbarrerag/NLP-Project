# Societies and nations at war; local and regional geopolitical conflicts (XIX and XX centuries)

The present issue of HiSTOReLO (vol. 16, no. 37) is a topic of vital historical importance: geopolitical conflicts and their profound impacts on societies and nations during the 19th and 20th centuries. Our dossier explores how wars and regional clashes have shaped not only maps, but also identities, cultures and power relations in Latin America, specifically, in Argentina, Chile, Colombia and Peru
