# A WASSILY PALMIRENSE: NOTES ON THE B3 HISTORY FOR THE PURPOSE OF IMP

This chapter deals with the experience of production, marketing and sale of the Wassily chair in Metal Industries of Palmira (imp), Co-lombia, fifty-five years after its creation. From the interest fundamentally productive and commercialization of the company around this chair, the text suggests a projectual history of the B3, of stationary character, in which a stage of conception and another of production is identified, which take place in moments and territories distinct. The writing alludes to some factors that favored the reproduction of this furniture in imp, such as the technological possibility and the interest for the international design by this company.
