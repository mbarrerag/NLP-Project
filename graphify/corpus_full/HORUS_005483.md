# THE VIGENCY OF THE MASTER WORKS. WAR IN THE WAY OF THE DEATH OF HIS AUTHOR

In the commemoration of the fiftieth anniversary of the death of the painter Pablo Picasso, this essay takes up the Guernica oil that the artist produced for the Spanish National Pavilion at the 1937 Paris International Exhibition based on the aerial bombardment of the city that gives name to the painting. A reading of the work is made from a philosophical perspective to demonstrate the validity of the hermeneutic message today, reviewing in a synthetic way the history and genesis of violence and war in the human species. Through the text, the extensive interpretative capacity of this painting is emphasized, which allows corroborating its status as a masterpiece. In the final part of the writing, we reflect on the possibility of re-conducing the belligerent attitude of man in order to humanize the war conflicts
