# EFFECT OF COBRE NANOPARTICLES ON THE BIOMECHANICAL COMPORTMENT OF RESIGN-DENTINE INTERFAZ

Evaluating the effect of the application of a etching-rinsing adhesive modified with copper nanoparticles on the microtensil bonding strength (μTBS) and the elastic module on the resin-dentin interface. This study was approved by the institutional ethics committee [B. CLEFU-112-21]. Nanoparticles of copper (Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, USA) were added to powder (25 nm) to 1 mL of adhesive Adper Single Bond 2 (3M ESPE, St. Paul. Min, USA) To obtain concentrations of 0.1% and 0.5% copper nanoparticles. Microbars with a cross section area of 1 mm2 were obtained from human molars and obtuted to evaluate the μTBS and the elastic module. Samples were divided into test groups: control (-), 0.1% copper nanoparticles and 0.5% copper nanoparticles, for the etching-in protocol (n=30). revealed a stable hybrid layer in samples treated with copper nanoparticles after thermal aging. These findings showed that the addition of copper nanoparticles to the adhesive preserved the bonding resistance and improved the elastic module. This strategy has beneficial effects on the biological and mechanical behavior of the resin-dentine interface and could be used to effectively stabilize the hybrid layer
