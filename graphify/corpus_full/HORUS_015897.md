# CLIMATE VULNERABILITY AND ITS APPLICATION IN THE FIELD OF THE RYO BOLO IN COLOMBIA.

The change and climate variability are phenomena that are strongly impacting humanity, ecosystems and biodiversity at the global level. Developing countries are not generators of large volumes of greenhouse gases (GHG), however, they suffer dramatically the effects and impacts of extreme climate changes. The impacts include heat waves, floods, droughts and landslides, which generate material losses, in human lives and ecosystems. The climate vulnerability (VC) defined as the propensity or predisposition of human beings, ecosystems and biodiversity to be harmed in any extent, as a result of some dangerous event. Measuring the HV is not a simple process, as it is an integrated, complex and multiple concept. Likewise, the overall scale of measurement is at the country level. This study applied and analyzed an index to assess climate vulnerability (ICC) at the level of watersheds of the Andean region. The index was developed through the regular, state-response (PER) and diffuse logic indicators of artificial intelligence.
