# CALLADITA SEES MORE BONITE: SIMBOLIC VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN AND FORMS OF COTIDIAN RESISTANCE

This chapter of book will present the partial results of the research entitled "Creation of a playful tool to visualize forms of violence against women and of daily resistance." 59 A qualitative methodology was used to collect information to identify forms of invisible violence against women in Bogotá, Cali and Jamundí and the forms of daily resistance. The narratives obtained through workshops and interviews conducted with the 150 participants coincide in locating the family as the main promoter of gender stereotypes and in the reproduction of violence against women. With regard to the forms of reaction, it was found that it is very scarcely presented, were classified as transgressive actions, or individual resistance; the forms of collective resistance, in some groups of community leaders, are not linked to gender violence. It is important to note that this research not only collected information on this subject, but also allowed the reencounter with other women who through the social bond grants them a place and their word
