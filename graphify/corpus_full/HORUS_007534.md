# Study for optimization of the pumping system of the hydraulic laboratory

The hydraulics laboratory has an obsolete and deteriorated pumping system that works by consuming a lot of energy from the electric grid. It is proposed to carry out a study to optimize energy consumption, renewing equipment and pipes, changing the configuration of the pumping and introducing speed changers for the pumps engines. In the study the cost of updating and the return period of the investment will be estimated
