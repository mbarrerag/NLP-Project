# Characterisation of modal choice by applying statistical regression considering spatial heterogeneity

In the decision making of public decisions in the field of transport and urban planning, it is key to know the use of the population of the means of transport available in a geographical area and, consequently, to characterize the determinants both personal and collective in the decision of the mode of travel of the people. In this research, techniques of regression analysis on spatial data of socioeconomic, transport and constructed environment patterns are developed to characterize the modal choice of public transport, walking, automobile and motorcycle in Manizales (Colombia). With this objective, four phases are used: (i) models of geographical accessibility, (ii) collection and adjustment of socioeconomic data, of transport and of the constructed environment, (iii) descriptive and spatial statistical analysis, and (iv) statistical regression models. The application of this methodology and its results exhibit interesting statistical associations of indicators such as accessibility to proximity services, the possession of vehicles, the temporary cost of displacement, the educational levels and income in the trend to use public transport, walking, automobile and motorcycle.
