# PALINOLOGICAL COLOMBIA: REVIEW OF THE STUDIES PUBLISHED IN THE DEPOSITS FROM PALEOZO TO THE NEOGEN

The book "Colombian Palainology: Methods, Applications and State of Knowledge" aims to disseminate information about the basic principles, and the current state of palinological research that have been published until 2020 in Colombia. This information is presented in chronological order and associated with the sedimentary basins of the country where the research was carried out. The oldest organic matter fossils reported in Colombia date from the Precambrian, and correspond to an association of Acritarcos of the Ediacarian, found in the northern sector of the Eastern Plains. The Paleozoic research is mainly found in the Eastern Plains and covers from the Cambrico to the Carboniferous. However, there are studies of the deposits of the Lower Ordovic in the Amazon, the Siluric of the Eastern Cordillera and the Devónico de the Upper Magdalene Valley. The Palinology of the Mesozoic has been studied in continental deposits of the Jurassic.
