# WAR IN UKRAINE

At the beginning of 2002, the war against Ukraine took the world by surprise. However, more than an isolated fact, it is part of a strategy by Russia that is coming back in time, in an attempt to regain the geopolitical position it held until a few decades ago. War in Ukraine is a book that will allow us to understand the reasons why a war and an invasion against the country of Eastern Europe were unleashed. Readers will have the information necessary to know the historical context of these events, supported by maps and graphics. Wars and Russian military actions will be presented that have been carried out since 1992, the situation of Ukraine, Crimea and Dombass in 2014, as well as the positions of the United States, NATO and the European Union, and the possible international consequences of this war on the world order
