# -Do cellular notifications affect memory and metamemory? An approach from cognitive neuroscience

In this project it is proposed to study the effect of cellular notifications on the memory of educational contents. There are not many researches on the distracting effects of notifications, and in general they point to a negative effect with worse performance in both work and academic tasks. This is worrying, considering that cellulars are omnipresent devices in modern life and that even the existence of a cellular addiction by an important part of the younger population is discussed. Here it is proposed to study how notifications can affect the coding and retrieval of information in young students, who could be vulnerable to the possible negative effects of notifications. To this end, several experiments are proposed in which educational content (e.g., a video) will be presented and notifications of a mobile device will be presented at times when the video transmits specific information. Other information will not receive notifications and will serve as controls. Afterwards, it will measure and compare the memory for both types of content. In the planned experiments, a multilevel strategy will be used for the information in which the effect of a behavioral measures (e.g., results in memory tests) and electrophysiological measures (e.g., potentials will allow the study of the initial or integral events)
