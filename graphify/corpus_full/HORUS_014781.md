# Effectiveness and safety of two Clinical Risk Management Systems (Fault and Error-proof Mode - AMFE and Continuous Quality Improvement Method - MMCC) for the prevention of reportable events in hospitalized patients: a systematic review.

To determine the effectiveness and safety of two Clinical Risk Management System (AMFE & MMCC) for the prevention of reportable events in hospitalized patients only. Systematic review of qualitative synthesis following the methodology established by the group Cochrane Effective Practice and Organisation of Care (EPOC) for complex interventions, primary studies that have implemented AMFE or MMCC for the prevention of reported events with unwanted effects. A systematic search was carried out in the databases MEDLINE (through the OVID platform), EMBASE (Elsevier platform), Cochrane-CENTRAL and LILACS (Virtual Library in Health – BVS), to identify studies published between September 2010 and September 2022. A proposal for evaluation of the validity of studies was made from before and after the group, the analysis of the most control, all the design strength (causing effect) and critical approach.
