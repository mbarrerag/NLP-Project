# Redesign of the weather monitoring network for the Department of Caldas

The present study proposes an optimization of the meteorological monitoring network of the department of Caldas through the application of the Analysis of Main Components (PCA) and the analysis of Entropy (ENT), combined with heuristic criteria.The current network has 120 stations with pluviometric records between 2000 and 2024; however, after an evaluation of the quality of the data, 65 stations with adequate information were selected, reducing the analysis period to the interval 2017–2024. The study was developed in three temporary scales: 5 minutes (considering four extreme events), daily and monthly.The analysis PCA, combined with the technique of grouping the K-means, allowed to identify homogeneous spatial patterns in each scale, highlighting especially the influence of the param ecosystem on short-duration precipitations in the department center, as well as the increase of variability in the western area from the present, the Conditutional and the Transformation.In the case of the analysis of Entropia, a procedure was established based on the criterion of maximising the information and minimizing the redundancy, by means of the calculation of the Entropy Marginal, joint, the Conditional, and the analysis obtained. d at three temporary scales: 5-minute (based on four extreme events), daily, and monthly.
