# Experimental and theoretical study of the influence of solvent on asphaltene-aggregates thermo-oxidation through high-pressure thermogravimetric analysis

This study is focused on the understanding of n-C7 asphaltene-aggregates oxidative behavior under high-pressure conditions. For this aim, different asphaltene model solutions were prepared using three asphaltene concentrations (0.1, 1.0, and 10 wt%) on three xylene isomers. The experimental results revealed that the asphaltene aggregate size increase in the order o-xylene 1.0 wt%, the mass gained by oxygen chemisorption increased in the order: p-xylene < m-xylene < o-xylene.
