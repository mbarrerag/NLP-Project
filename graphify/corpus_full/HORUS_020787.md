# Environmental analysis of urban fungiculture in three localities of Bogotá

The cultivation of mushrooms also called fungiculture, is an activity that belongs to the agricultural sector in Colombia and in recent years has emerged the urban fungiculture on a small scale in Bogotá, due to the increase in the demand of organic products, with proteins of non animal origin and the interest of the producers to find an alternative of subsistence. The research carried out seeks to investigate the characteristics and environmental impacts of this activity, as well as its relation with agroecology. By means of a case study of the productive chain of orellanas (Pleurotus ostreatus) in three productive units of three different locations, the application of the Product Life Cycle Analysis (ACV) and the participatory design, the advantages and disadvantages of this activity were determined and possible improvements to promote the fungictor sector in the city. The adjusted conceptual LCA allowed to know the environmental impacts throughout the non-constant and functional production, from the obtaining of raw materials to the final disposition, it was found that urban fungiculture in Bogotá is a recent activity with presence in almost all localities, with productive units that are usually registered and with a non-constant production and of small scale, it is recognized. an alternative livinglihood.
