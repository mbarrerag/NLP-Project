# Analysis of NORM concentration in water samples from the Magdalena Medio Valley.

The radioactive isotopes 232-Th, 238-U and 40-K are known as NORM (Normally Occurring Radioactive Materials). 25 water samples were taken in the study area, the Middle Magdalena Valley, from which concentrations of these radionucleus were determined using a high resolution gamma spectroscopy system. Determining the threshold of decision and detection limit it was found that 7 samples have presence of 238-U, 2 samples contain 40-K but these levels of uranium and potassium are below the limit of detection. Additionally, it is reported that none of the samples have concentration of thorium activity above the decision limit
