# CLUSTER CHARACTERIZATION OF SARS-COV-2 IN MILITARY PERSONNEL DEPLOYED TO EGYPT AND SUBSEQUENT INTRODUCTION OF B.1.1.7 AND C.36 LINEAGES TO COLOMBIA

Severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) lineage B.1.1.7, also known as the Alpha variant, and catalogued a variant of concern (VOC), was first identified in the UK in September 2020, and is now rapidly spreading with 812'731 deposited genomes from 135 different countries, including Colombia as of 9 June 2021. Sequence data have shown a rapid expansion of this lineage, suggesting a likely selective advantage, most probably mediated by accumulation of critical mutations associated with increased virulence and transmission. Evidence suggests that B.1.1.7 transmits more efficiently than other variants, displaying increased infectivity and risk of hospitalization
