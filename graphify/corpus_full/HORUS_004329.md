# OBTENTION OF CARBONIZED FOR MOOD AND COFFEE WASTE FOR ADVISORATION OF ANTIBIOTICS IN WASTE WATERS

Wastes obtained from the anti-Oqueña wood industry and from coffee preparation are being used as raw material for the production of carbonized with adsorbing properties. These materials can be used for the adsorption of a wide range of pollutants. This study focuses on the adsorption of amoxicillin. An antibiotic present in significant quantities in wastewater given its wide use. This antibiotic is part of the recalcitrant pollutants that are difficult to transform and treat in water treatment plants; therefore, it is proposed to make a selective adsorption on materials obtained from residues, posing a possible environmental solution making use of the circular economy model. The results obtained so far by means of simulation of electronic structure, for the carbonized-amoxycilline model, indicate that the main interactions are presented by hydrogen bridges, allowing preselect parameters of preparation of the carbonized,2p.
