# Diversity and distribution of phytoplanktonic assemblies in the Ecological Park of the Wetland (PEDH) Torca-Guaymaral and its relationship with environmental factors.

Urban wetlands, such as the Distrital Ecological Park (PEDH) Torca-Guaysomaral, located in Bogotá, are important from the environmental perspectives as they fulfill functions of water damping, preservation and conservation of biodiversity and maintenance of ecological connectivity. Despite their importance, these ecosystems are reduced and deteriorated and for carrying out restoration actions it is priority to know the diversity of the organisms present. An important limnological component are the phytoplanktonic assemblages, since they present fast physiological responses to environmental changes. In view of the lack of information, it is necessary to investigate the biodiversity and spatial-temporal distribution of the algae of the Torca-Guaymaral PEDH and to characterize the dependence that they have with the climatic and physical chemical environmental factors of the water, as well as with the type of habitat. In this study a quantitative and morphofunctional analysis of the phytoplankton and the physical and chemical characteristics of the water in two contrasting climatic periods and in three types of habitats of the wetland was carried out: Canal, Léntico and Quebradas.
