# Cosmic dynamo equation under cosmetic disturbance theory at first order.

This paper aims to give an introduction to cosmological disturbances and applications from the point of view of numerical Relativity, in particular how these disturbances can be applied to formalism 3+1. Cosmological disturbances are given at first order on the spatially flat solution of Friedman-Lemaitre-Robertson-Walker (FLRW), this with a view to obtaining the cosmological dynamo equation, under the kinematic dynamo approach, in order to study the evolution of the primordial magnetic fields and their amplification. The computational study of cosmological disturbances from the Numerical Relativity using Einstein Toolkit software will also be shown, emphasis is placed on FLRWSolver for the numerical solution of Einstein field equations from the cosmological point of view
