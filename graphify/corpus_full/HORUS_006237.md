# Alternative classification diagrams and algorithms to distinguish the ionization mechanism present in AGNs and Starburst galaxies

The active galaxies are classified into two groups: AGN (active clusters of galaxies) and Infrarrojas - Starburst, characterized by the ionization of the accretion disc and the stellar formation respectively. Within these categories are the subclasses of the Seyfert and LINER galaxies, belonging to the AGNs. Baldwin, Phillips and Terlevich (BPT) developed a method based on spectral lines to distinguish them. The analysis focuses on the diagram BPT and lines of identification in the infrared spectrum. The objective is to evaluate the performance of machine learning models to categorize galaxies and complement visual observations with infrared. Machine Learning, or machine learning, is presented as an efficient tool to automate the identification of types of galaxies, which motivates the evaluation of the BPT diagram and the implementation of automatic learning programs to identify galaxies according to the infrared categories, Seyfert and LINER, in addition to executing and testing various dimensionalities in the implementation of the automatic learning models. This research work seeks to respond to answer the evaluation of the BPT diagram, and the feasibility, and the implementation of automatic learning programs to be based by the functional learning programs to be able,, emerge as an efficient tool to automate the identification of galaxy types, prompting the evaluation of the BPT diagram and the implementation of machine learning programs to identify galaxies based on infrared categories, Seyfert and LINER. Additionally, it involves running and testing various dimensions in the implementation of machine learning models. This research aims to address various questions related to the effectiveness and reliability of using machine learning in galaxy identification
