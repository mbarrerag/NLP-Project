# Characterization of the determinants that have led to the failure of the Mipymes of the textile industry in the city of Bogotá: analysis for the period 2020–2022

The capital textile industry has experienced great challenges due to factors in its environment, which has affected its production and sales levels, having an impact on the survival of its mipymes. Therefore, it is vital to have information that avoids this business failure. Thus, in this master's work the determinants that have led to the failure of the textile industry mipymes in the city of Bogotá during the period 2020 – 2022 were characterized. To achieve this objective, a methodological design composed of three stages carried out in an orderly manner. In the first stage, a conceptualization of the business failure was carried out, obtaining a text in which the types of failure and the situations that trigger it were described. In the second stage, a characterization of the mipymes of the textile industry in the city of Bogotá was carried out, obtaining a description of its particularities and the environment in which they are located. In the third and last stage, the determinants that have led to the failure of the mipymes of the textile industry during the period of study.
