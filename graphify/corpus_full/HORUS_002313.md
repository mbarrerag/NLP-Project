# Quantification of Amino acids by Liquid Chromatography

The main objective of the project is to develop and implement analytical methods for the quantification of amino acids using liquid chromatography without derivatives procedures. This approach seeks to overcome the limitations of traditional methods that require chemical derivation, such as the instability of derivatives, lack of reproducibility and interference of reagents (Kennedy & Bivens, 2024; Thibert et al., 2018). The project includes three main activities: exhaustive bibliographic review; implementation of the analytical method, where challenges are sought in sample preparation (Mitchell et al., 2014; González-Fernández et al., 2015); and optimization of the analysis conditions; and validation of the method according to international standards
