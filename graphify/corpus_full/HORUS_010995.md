# WRITING, IMAGES AND PLACES FROM VITAL LINKS

In this paper we want to make known the second phase of a project that proposes an exercise of correspondences as a practice of expanded creation in which visual and textual assemblies are made possible; intimations connected in distant spaces, diverse bodies and images. We are a multidisciplinary group of students and teachers from different fields of the Arts and Human Sciences, such as anthropology, cultural studies, cinema, sound art and the plastic arts, which seeks to create dialogues between knowledge and neighboring practices. Thinking of the epistolary manager in an expanded sense, the group’s inquiries have allowed us to understand the possibilities that correspondence opens up as an exercise of common practice, both as a critical and expressive and sensitive elaboration of the present convulsion of which we are part
