# ARTEFACTO READ AS ADAPTATIVE SYSTEM IN THE APPROACH OF AECO PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The current changes in AECO (Architecture, Engineering, Construction and Operations) require pedagogical innovations that not only respond to technological needs, but also promote critical and socially systemic adaptive learning. In Colombia, the Skills Catalogue Sector Construction points to the disarticulation of criteria between the education system and talent needs in the sector, a situation that invites the exploration of teaching-learning strategies that respond to their needs. This study introduces a SIPOC device (Supplier, Input, Process, Output, Customer), under the hypothesis that its use, from a connectivist perspective, allows an adaptive system for the development of management skills in AECO projects. Students are grouped into Teams-Rol (EqR) creating knowledge networks through PMBOK® processes that are consolidated into a WBS load system (Work Breakdown Structure).
