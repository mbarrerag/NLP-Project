# Development of a prototype digital holographic microscope without a lens certifiable open-source

The digital holographic microscopy without lenses (DLHM) is perhaps the simplest technology in terms of hardware for the observation of samples without staining; a source of coherent illumination of spherical waves, a digital registration system and a mechanical body to integrate them, constitute the hardware necessary for its implementation, which profile DLHM as a technology with a high potential to be developed in an open, portable and accessible at a low cost. Despite this potential the commercial solutions of DLHM are far from being a technology accessible to various sectors of education, research and production due to high cost and low portability. In this thesis of mastery in Physical Engineering are condensed, refine and articulate the designs of DLHM produced by the group of Optic and Processing Digital DL system (GODP) results related to the constituent elements of the technology, to develop a prototype of DLHM microscope certified as hardware open source. To achieve this, an elevation of the necessary systems for a prototype of DLHM is constituted as hardware or source, integration, are the necessary hardware components for its implementation. This simplification of hardware sets DLHM as a technology with high potential for open, portable, and cost-effective development. Despite this potential, commercial DLHM solutions are from being accessible to many sectors in education, research, and production, main due to its high costs and low portability. In this Master's thesis in Engineering in Physics, the designs of DLHM developed by the Optics and Opto-Digital Processing DLsource Dlyptic, technical development of the technology incorporated, refined, and articulated in the development of a certified open-source hardware prototype of DLHM. work of the 10th International Symposium "Optics & its applications," sponsored by the ICCP
