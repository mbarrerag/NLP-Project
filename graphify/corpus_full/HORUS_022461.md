# AGENCY FROM THE SATBALL: THE CASE OF THREE ENVIRONMENTAL MOVEMENTS IN COLOMBIA

ABSTRACT This study reports the agency of three environmental movements in Colombia: the Environmental Committee in Defense of Life (CADV), the Sembrando Ando Collective (MSA) and Environmental Agenda (AAM), through its assembly process and its main repertoires. In methodological aspects, this qualitative study, of an exploratory-descriptive nature, interrelates categories as assemblies and agency from the study of three movements that operate on different scales (national, regional, local). The main techniques of data collection were unstructured interviews and the revision of written and audiovisual documents. Thus, it was observed that the agency that is produced from assembly involves elements of heterogeneity, having effects on the action of movements. Environmental movements. Assembly.
