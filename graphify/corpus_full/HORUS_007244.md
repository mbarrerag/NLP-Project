# Vulnerability of reinforced concrete structures in response to accidental explosion events in the context of Colombian construction

Colombia has been marked by accidental explosion events of anthropic origin, these have caused loss of human lives, multiple injured and even disappeared, with the corresponding economic damage associated to the infrastructure. Bearing in mind this situation that occurs with some frequency, given a policy of managing the risk of limited disasters in the country, this research aims to establish a reference framework that allows to evaluate the vulnerability of buildings constructed in compliance with the Sismo Resistant Construction Standard for Colombia (NSR-10, AIS 2010) with structural systems in reinforced concrete porticos to accidental explosion events by means of fragility curves. In this document, we can initially find a robust methodology that allows to evaluate explosive loads against buildings based on the UFC-3-340-02 document of the Department of Defense of the United States, combined with the ALOHA software of the Environmental Protection Agency of the United States. Subsequently to evaluate the structural response of buildings under analysis to the explosive load, this research uses the free access software developed by the University of Berkeley OpenSes, with processing of data by means of disasters associated with the assessment of more than 27,000 explosive events by typical building with them.
