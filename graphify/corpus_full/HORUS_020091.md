# Building peace and caring for life: recommendations for the National Development Plan 2023-2026

How to turn the National Development Plan into an instrument of building a new nation pact, beyond the technicalities and formalities it involves? The package of recommendations that the reader has in his hands intends to join the participatory and deliberative spirit that, we believe, should inform the construction of the National Development Plan 2022-2026. It was elaborated in the shadow of the Project Conflict Violent and Peace, in principle launched by the National University of Colombia's Center of Thought Region Nation, but to the present day constituted by researchers and researchers from five National University Headquarters (Amazonia, Orinoquía, La Paz, Manizales and Bogotá), besides the Universities of the Valley, Nariño, the Military New Granada, Technological of Chocó, of the Andes and City of New York.
