# Innovative strategies for the appropriation of communicative competence of mathematical thought in grade 3 of a rural institution of Guaviare

This work is a qualitative process of research action carried out during a pedagogical intervention in the Educational Institution El Vidal, a rural establishment of official character located in the Municipality of San José del Guaviare in the department of Guaviare, with the aim of formulating innovative didactic strategies in mathematics under the resolution of problems to optimize the communicative processes in the classroom and promote the learning of the learners. The research was developed in three stages; first, the practices of mathematics classroom that were carried out by the teachers, as a means to strengthen communication with the learners and to dynamise their learning. Subsequently, with the strengths and weaknesses detected, training plans were proposed for teachers in mathematics didactics with the use of problem situations, which were developed in encounters with the learning community and with application in the classroom. Finally, to know the impact of these strategies in the classroom, the results obtained by the educators through a new test of mathematical knowledge. With the development of the research it is shown that communication fulfills a relevant role in the learning processes that develop in the classes of mathematics and becomes a fundamental element of the education.
