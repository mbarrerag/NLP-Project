# Strengthening of students at the Amazonia headquarters as researchers through the understanding and use of territory as a living laboratory

The Amazonian Headquarters is interested in strengthening the processes of training research of its students, reason why it considers that its own campus as a living laboratory, is an ideal place to carry out transdisciplinary and multicultural research activities. In particular, it has the interest to conserve and enrich the campus through a living collection of trees and palms, to give it as a gift to the future human generations that inhabit or visit the urban environment of Leticia. It is noteworthy that the campus as the result of a natural regeneration process (passive restoration) during 46 years, it will support a great diversity of flora, fauna and funga. However, the entry of an invasive palm (Elaeis guineensis) threatens with this biodiversity. Considering all the above mentioned purpose, the general objective of this project is to increase the competences and skills for research in the undergraduate and graduate students of the Amazonian headquarters, through the creation of semilleros integrated to the agents and local populations with projects focused on the conservation, management and sustainability of the territory. For what will be realized three central activities and the management of the internal and post-graduate training (i) of the Amazonian headquarters, through the creation of the interpretation, through the creation of semillores and local research, of this project, and (iii) dissemination and dissemination of results using various written and audiovisual media
