# Observatory of power, role and activity of police in the framework of respect for human rights, the guarantee of security and coexistence.

In the framework of a Social State of Law, the police institution is conceived as a transversal element for the realization of the constitutional aims of which article 2 of our Constitution is addressed. It is necessary that the police institution must ensure the materialization of these, especially those of serving the community, guaranteeing the effectiveness of the rights and duties enshrined in the Constitution and ensuring peaceful coexistence and the validity of a just order. It is part of the fact that the police institution within the contemporary theory of the structure of a Social State of Law must aim at a civil structure responsible for dealing with problems, in principle civil, and in a very exceptional way of an institution of armed and militarized persons. However, due to the Colombian historical reality framed in the armed conflict, the police has had a militarized structure and conception, which has led to it being an institution of a military type conformed, with military actions that must also ensure peaceful coexistence, social order and security for the communities and the citizens.
