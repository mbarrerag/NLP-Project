# The Simón Bolívar Park project, a paradigm of leisure architecture for Bogotá

The architectural project that was developed for the Simón Bolívar Park within the framework of the formulation of the Master Plan of 1981 proposals a change in the paradigm of entertainment architecture for metropolitan parks in Bogotá, since it modified the type of existing urban park in the city from different perspectives, the landscape, the programmatic, the artificial and the conservation of the natural landscape. The architectural project was developed on disaggregated parts to configure a new unit. It takes as starting point a series of pre-existing conditions with an alternative relationship and another objective to articulate the structure and the management of the management to propose the unification of all in a single show of metropolitan park and approximately 350 hectares. It takes as its starting point a series of pre-existences without an apparent relationship and through a strategy of articulation it manages to propose the unification of all in a single large metropolitan park of approximately 350 hectares. The objective of this research is to find out what was this strategy of unification and articulation of each area and part of the park, and as this proposal ends up consolidating a new paradigm of leisure architecture for Bogotá. After a systematic and historical analysis on a series of local and international references it is intended to show the evolution of the urban park and as this shaped the structuring ideas of the proposal of the park Simon Bolivar of 1981 and that is inscribed in the Master Plan of the same year
