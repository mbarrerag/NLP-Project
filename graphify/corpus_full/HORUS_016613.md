# REIMMINING THE HUMAN AND CONVERTING SOCIAL AMBIENTAL DISAGUALITIES: OTHER VISIONS OF POSSIBLE WORLDS IN LATIN AMERICA

In Latin America, the proposals of indigenous peoples and women converge with the re-thinking of the social and human sciences, which seek to re-imagine the human and confront socio-environmental inequalities, and position different forms of interaction between human and non-human. Processes that allow to decenter the predominant anthropocentric approach and demand the recognition of the agency of non-humans and its multiple forms of reciprocal affectation with humans. However, there are complexities and paradoxes that we must address to see the possibilities and scope of proposals and processes around possible worlds
