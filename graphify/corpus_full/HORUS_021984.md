# Microdosimetry of proton radiation therapy using gold nanoparticles as sensitising agents.

Proton radiation therapy is one of the most promising approaches to cancer treatment. Compared to modern techniques of photon radiation therapy, such as modulated intensity radiation therapy (IMRT) and volumetric arcotherapy (VMAT), this technique achieves a better dose conformation in the tumor tissue at the same time as the dose is decreased in nearby risk structures and surrounding healthy tissue. Similarly, since the early 2000s, experiments of irradiation of cell lines (in-vitro) and tumors in small animals (in-vivo), have demonstrated the potential of the gold nano-particles (GNP) to be used as sensitizers in photon radiation therapy. In this regard, during recent years the scientific community has directed its interest towards a third potential option for the improvement of radiation therapy, where the two previous alternatives converge, now studying the increase in the biological effect of the use of the GNP in proton irradiation., so that the maximum dose region (Bragg's Peak) is reached on a shorter path of protons. Increases in the LET, at the end of the route, are estimated to be between 7 and 38 % for gold concentrations of 5 and 25 mg/ml respectively, as a result increases in the absorbed dose up to 10 % are shown
