# Design and construction of a Heliospheric Camera for Spectral Images

The active regions in the Sun are zones of great interest in modern physics, given the great amount of energy that can be stored and released suddenly, this has motivated us to develop an instrument with which spectral information can be obtained from various zones in the disk and solar crown, all of this trying to cover the greatest possible area and try to obtain information of the evolution of the active regions. For this reason a spectrograph was designed, constructed and tested to multimode optical fiber, which we call an IFU, for observations between 400 and 650 nm, with a spectral resolution of the order of 0.3 nm, making use of 12 optical fibers OM4 with a diameter of 50 um, which can be placed in any arrangement within the focal image of the telescope used. As a goal we intend to create this instrument to obtain information of a scientific nature, preserving a low budget and high reliability, for this reason, according to the data obtained, to make the respective improvements to the instrument. s a goal we set out to create this instrument to obtain scientific information, keeping a low budget and high reliability, so that, in a future, agreeing to the data obtained, we can make the respectful improvements to the instrument.
