# Music therapy in the face of stage anxiety in theater students of the University of Bogotá El Bosque

The purpose of this undergraduate project was to carry out a musicotherapeutic process in order to promote the tools of coping with escenic anxiety in students of dramatic art at the University of El Bosque. 16 sessions were held with a group of 8 to 10 students from different semesters. Some students were in the academic test of several subjects such as acting, dance, body expression and verbal expression. The intervention was made through the music therapy and psychodrama, to enable students to use the tools acquired from their same profession in order to face the escenic anxiety. In these 16 sessions the escenic anxiety was addressed from the re-creative, receptive, improvisation and composition methods, strengthening coping strategies through the use of breathing techniques, psychodrama and other dynamics in group, which also enabled an improvement in the self-efficacy, the present study had a qualitative character, collecting bitactors from the students, who expressed themselves at the end of each session.
