# FUERARY DIVERSITY IN THE HIGH MAGDALENA (FLANDS, GIRARDOT).

In the framework of a project of Preventive Archaeology, it was identified in Flanders Tolima, a site categorized as ritual and funerary space with an area of 20450.6 m2. Located on the Ancient Espinal Alluvial Fan Coello, at 305 msl, its geological formation records the scope and dimensions of the lahars of the Cerro Machín Volcano. This space has an excellent panoramic northwards on the Magdalena River and the terraces of the other bank. To the east and west it is surrounded by two deep cavals that isolate the place and serve as areas of absorption of rain water or flood by river growth, providing stability for the human settlement.The archaeological work carried out in this area allowed to define an important deposit: tombs, burials of offerings, traces of vertical structures, vasssijas, lithic and many fragments of ceramics are an interesting collection of data on specific practices of a community of the late period, between the 8th and 12th centuries.According to the research carried out, the characteristics of the deposit and the registered materials, the site was located in the phase of the first and last phase of the complex and later
