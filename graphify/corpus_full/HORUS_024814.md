# METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL FOR THE ESTIMATION AND VALUATION OF EXTERNALITIES IN METROPOLITIAN ENVIRONMENTS: CASE METROPOLITIAN REGION OF BOGOTA

This research seeks to explore and propose a methodology that would allow generating a model to estimate and evaluate the externalities of cargo transport in metropolitan environments. In this sense, first, the scope of the conceptual and methodological model will be presented, and later the methodology will be proposed, from which externalities to evaluate were identified. Secondly, the methods of estimation and valuation of externalities recommended to include within the model are presented. This methodological model is supported by the concepts developed in the research, among which are highlighted: positive and negative externalities in an external economy perspective, the approach of cargo transport as an externality generator and as territorial externality, the multiscale transport, the distinction of expressions of externalities in different territorial scales and the transfer of externalities to the interior of the metropolitan territorial system understood as a city subsystem.
