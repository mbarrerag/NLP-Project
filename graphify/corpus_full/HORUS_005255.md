# Lighting of educational center based on studies of control of lighting and impact in the circadian cycle for saving energy and visual comfort

This project focuses on lighting design for a higher education center located in the center of Bogotá, with the aim of improving visual comfort in its specific spaces. The design was carried out using the software DIALux to make precise calculations that met the requirements established by RETILAP and international standards, without sacrificing the aesthetics of the lighting system. The project presents significant opportunities for research and standardization of the minimum values of the photometric parameters, applying both technical and artistic concepts. In addition, systems of lighting control, both natural and artificial light, were incorporated, allowing to optimize the energy consumption and to make the most of the day.
