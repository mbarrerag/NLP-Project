# INVENTIFICATION OF POLIN PRODUCER TO PARTICIPATE DIGITAL IMAGES

The production of pollen is an agricultural activity of great importance. Obtaining a label of designation of origin for this product can give it added value and improve the economic conditions of producers. However, for the verification of the designation of origin it is important to have mechanisms that allow corroboration if a sample of the product comes from a particular region. The current strategies of verification of the location are expensive and difficult to apply in marketing environments. In this work, a new model based on color segmentation and supervised machine learning is proposed for the automatic identification of the geographical origin of a pollen sample from digital images of the same acquired under controlled conditions. The proposed strategy achieved a performance 0.89 in f1-score, suggesting that the method can determine with a high level of certainty the producer, and consequently the geographical origin
