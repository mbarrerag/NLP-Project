# Flora and similarities of a rocky outcropping in Caatinga, eastern South America

The rocky environments show a great heterogeneity of habitats and very specific microclimatic characteristics. These conditions select very well adapted species to compose these floristic sets, compared to other areas of their environment. In this context, a floristic survey was carried out in a rocky outcropping in the Environmental Protection Area (APA) of Cariri - Paraíba (07°20’17”–07°12’10.3” South, 36°25’40”–36° 10’02.2” West), a region located in the driest area of the Caatinga, in the northeast of Brazil, with the aim of expanding knowledge about the local flora and analyzing its similarity with other Caatinga floras. 128 species distributed in 103 genera and 52 families were recorded.
