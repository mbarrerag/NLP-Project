# Green energy markets in Latin America: Experiences and trends.

In recent years we are seeing essential changes in electrical systems, where international agreements and environmental measures are pushing towards an energy transition with a tendency to incorporate cleaner energy technologies, energy efficiency and new ways of doing energy business. With regard to the concern for security of energy supply, reducing environmental impacts, improving conditions of adaptability to climate change and incorporating elements of competition in the provision of energy services. In this document, we analyze the emerging market for green energy in Latin America and review the green electricity policy of the countries identified with this market. We examine the effectiveness of policy in the evolution of renewable installed capacity as part of the electricity matrix. It surprises the growth of renewables throughout the region, but also the overall growth of installed thermal capacity. We also analyze the key factors that influence the penetration of green energy products into the market, such as market design, pricing, incentives, policies and interaction between them. In Latin America, we identify a greater market penetration in Chile and Panama; however, until now it is not a very dynamic market
