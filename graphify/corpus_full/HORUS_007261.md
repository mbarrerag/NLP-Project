# Comparison of the characterization between optimized polycrystalline silicon panel with geometrically modified surface plate Vs. The Solar Roofing CIGS

This thesis addresses the challenge of optimizing efficiency in solar energy uptake and conversion into photovoltaic systems, a topic of great relevance due to its profound economic and environmental implications.In a world that advances towards sustainable energy sources, the implementation of innovative technologies becomes central.In this context, the Optic Tolva with Specific Refraction Index is presented as a key solution to improve the competitiveness and performance of the solar sector. The central axis of this research is the development and analysis of the Solar Radiation Capture Optimizer: Tolva Optica, a device designed to efficiently concentrate solar radiation through precise manipulation of light, taking advantage of a specific refractive index. This approach not only allows to mitigate energy losses by reflection in solar panels, but also substantially improves the efficiency of the photovoltaic cells to redirect the radiation to the active areas of uptake.The optical tolva, proposed in reverse pyramidal geometry, stands out as an innovative alternative due to its superior optical concentration capacity, its ease of installation and its simplified maintenance.
