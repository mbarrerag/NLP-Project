# Dynamics of the cold solar chromesphere observed with ALMA

In this thesis, a dynamic study of the cold regions of the solar chromosphere observed by the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) in the band 3 is presented.The analysis integrates data of the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) and the Interface Region Imaging Spectrograph (IRIS), focusing specifically on the region of the calm sun D06 of the Solar ALMA Science Archive (SALSA).In the processing of data before the study carried out in the the thesis, a method of alignment based on the helioproyective coordinates of the SDO, using the ALFicient of Pearson Correlation and the Structural Similitude Index to estimate the correlation of the images.The analysis revealed that the cold structures detected in the band of ALMA are mainly located in the areas of Internetworks, which are characterized by a weak vertical magnetic field.ALT. A total of 44 cold events were identified in ALMA. The distribution of the duration of events indicated that shock waves pass through the chromosphere approximately every 3 minutes. On the other hand, when measuring the displacement of the centre of mass between consecutive tables, we estimate the speed of the dark characteristics. Our analysis revealed that the distribution of the speed reached a peak of 8.5 km/s, with an average speed of 21 km/s. These speed values were observed in simulations of the propagation of shock waves in chromospheric heights, reinforcing the hypothesis that, in fact, the dark regions are post-shock regions. This study not only contributes to the understanding of the thermal dynamics of the solar chromosphere, but also provides valuable information for future research and the refinement of numerical models in astrophysics solar.
