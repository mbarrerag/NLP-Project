# LACTOSA AS ERGOGNIC AID IN SPORT

Carbohydrates play a major role in the maintenance and restoration of muscle and liver glycogen, being fundamental in the energy contribution in moderate to high intensity activities and in the recovery between training sessions or competitive events; being considered one of the main nutritional ergonomic aids. The consumption of carbohydrates immediately before and during exercise becomes an effective strategy to provide the muscle and central nervous system with an exogenous source of carbohydrates seeking to maintain glycemia levels and delay the emptying of glycogen deposits, which becomes a stressage to improve performance. Lactose is the main carbohydrate of milk, structurally defined as a disaccharide composed of glucose and galactose. The intake of lactose can be re-committed as a pre-training fuel due to its low glycemic load, as mentioned by scientific evidence a low glycemic load before exercise can prevent post-prandial hyperglycemia and maintain lipolysis at levels suitable for the energy contribution in moderate intensity activities, in addition, it could promote the availability of carbohydrates carried out throughout the sport, improving performance during the event and the long-term
