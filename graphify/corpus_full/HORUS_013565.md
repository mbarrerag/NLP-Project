# Cooking Books in Medellín City, 1908-1960

This thesis addresses the study on the printed cook books published in the city of Medellín between 1908 and 1960. Based on the concept that these works contain more than cooking recipes, and that through them one can learn aspects of the society that produces them, in this work a history was developed that interwoven for this locality the books, women and, finally, food. To this end, systematic reviews of the period's publications such as the prescription books, the newspaper El Colombiano, the magazines Letras and Encajes and Cromos. With the analysis of these primary sources and research of various authors who have worked on the subject, these stories were constructed. The developed work shows that the cook books were constituted in one of the media used by a sector of the city to try to change the habits of food of the population. Likewise, they also took part in the efforts that were carried out to define what the functions of the women of Medellín should be made. .
