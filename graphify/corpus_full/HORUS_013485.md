# The literacy of Venezuelan migrant children in two areas of Bogotá: Engativá Pueblo and the Zone of Tolerance

According to Migration Colombia (ABC Estatuto Temporal de Protección - Migrantes Venezianos, 2021), the migration of Venezuelans has been booming during the last years and almost one third of the population that migrates corresponds to minors of age. This implies a challenge for the Colombian class classrooms as long as the curricula and practices are different in each country. This is evidenced in the literacy that children have built and that manifest through reading and writing practices. Through a qualitative approach, this research aims to answer the following questions: What differences do the literacy of Venezuelan migrant children and Colombian children of fifth degree are in the Zone of Tolerance and in Engativá Pueblo? and What are the characteristics of the writing of Venezuelan migrant children and Colombian children of fifth degree located in the Zone of Tolerance and in Engativá Pueblo?
