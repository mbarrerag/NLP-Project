# ADVUVING HORMONETERApia IN CANCER DE MAMA. REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVORDAGE AT THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CANCEROLOGY, BOGOTA, COLOMBIA

Breast cancer is the most frequently diagnosed cancer in women around the planet. According to GLOBOCAN 2020, its incidence and mortality worldwide are estimated at 47.8 and 13.6 per 100,000 women, respectively; for Colombia, the estimated incidence is 48.3 per 100,000 women and the mortality rate of 13.1 per 100,000 women. About 70% of breast cancer expresses hormone receptors (RH+) and is susceptible to hormone therapy management, which improves the clinical outcomes of patients in the adjuvant and metastatic scenarios. Specifically in adjuvant, studies have shown that hormonal therapy decreases by almost 50% the risk of relapse and by 30% the risk of death after 10 years of follow-up. For this update, a search was made for the most relevant systematic reviews and meta-analysis on the subject.
