# Methodology for multidimensional assessment of accessibility through public transport as an expression of social vulnerability in Bogotá.

The purpose of this thesis is to develop a methodology that, from the notion of accessibility, such as the potential for access to opportunities, and the exercise of the right to the city, allows for a measurement and evaluation of accessibility to the basic functions of the city through the offer of public transport, to demonstrate that a measurement of this from the conditions of mobility, can be used to understand the degree of accessibility as an expression of the social vulnerability. For this, the information collected by EMB2019 is used where socioeconomic conditions and variables of mobility are reviewed. With the latter, a synthetic measure is generated that allows establishing an index associated with each function of the city, which is used to zonate the territory according to the level of accessibility and to carry out a series of analyses comparative with other conditions of vulnerability. The methodology and index developed are an opportunity to show the conditions of deep vulnerability of the communities, understanding that improving the quality of mobility especially in public transport helps those who are most vulnerable not entering into processes of social exclusion
