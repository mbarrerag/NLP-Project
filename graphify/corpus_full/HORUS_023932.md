# Development and implementation of a methodology for the analysis of hydro-dynamic economic services

This article describes the proposed methodology for the characterization of hydrological ecosystem services, specifically those for regulation and sediment retention, for environmental economic valuation purposes. The methodological proposal is based on the analysis of scenarios for change in plant coverage and soil use (CCV), supported by a rigorous mathematical modeling. The proposed methodology begins with the selection and hydroclimate characterization of the area of interest, includes various modelling methods, depending on the availability of existing information and expertise in user hydrological issues, is supported by the analysis of CCV scenarios, and consolidates the results in ecosystem properties and indices, simple to evaluate, related to services and benefits of the same, including, among others, components of the water balance (ETr, Q), the water regulation index (IRH), the water use index (IUA), for the water quantity theme; and the production and concentration of sediments for the water quality theme. It also illustrates the implementation of the methodological proposal in the Susaguá river basin in Cundinamarca
