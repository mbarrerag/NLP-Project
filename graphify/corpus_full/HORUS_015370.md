# VARIATIONS OF COLOUR TRIBUTES IN THE MAGO IV GAMA DURING THE STORAGE REFRIGERED, DEBATED TO THE APPLICATION OF UV-C LIGHT, ULTRASONIDE AND CHLORE AS METHODS OF INFECTION.

The effect of individual and combination treatments of alternative disinfectants such as shortwave ultraviolet light (UV), edible coating based on aloe vera (CR) and ultrasound (US) and a traditional disinfectant (sodium hypochlorite, CL) on post-harvest quality of the handle `Tommy Atkins' was evaluated.Minimumly processed in PET containers under refrigeration (5 °C and 70 % HR) for 15 days. Physicochemical attributes (pH, titrable acidity, soluble solids, maturity index, firmness and weight loss), surface colour and overall appearance were analyzed every 3 days. While the counts of mesophilic aerobics, total coliforms and molds and yeasts were performed on days 0, 9 and 15 of storage. The results showed that combined UV and RC treatment allowed maintaining quality attributes and ensuring minimum processed mango quality for less than 9 days. Key words: Mangifera indicates; minimal processing; microbial chemical attributes; microbial counts, useful life
