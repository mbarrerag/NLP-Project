# Incidence of the diversity of passive subjects of harm (cisgenerous woman, transsexual man or woman with different capacities) in the way in which the elements of the judgement of State patrimonial responsibility for the gynecobstetric medical activity in Colombia should be understood and proven.

The present project aims to address the topic of the judgments of patrimonial responsibility of the State derived from the exercise of the medical activity gynecobstetric, problematizing them from the understanding and probancy of its elements in function of differential approaches such as gender, gender-gender-dividences and different capacities. Hence, it is asked on this issue under three specific aspects: the typologies of the damage (how they should be understood and applied in those judgments), the mechanisms of easing the burden borne by the plaintiff (and in particular the theory of the predominant probability) and the failure in the autonomous service derived from the lack of attention -in both legal and legal matters - to obstetric violence on sex-gender-dissident persons. Such study is justified in the problems faced by the acting party in the processes of medical responsibility of the State, since the latter is the deficiency of the contentious object (both in medical and legal matters), as well as the probatory difficulty that falls on the legally relevant facts. The type of damage that exists with a view to its particular application in the State's judgements of patrimonial responsibility arising from the exercise of the medical activity gynecobstetric. Taking into account the above elements, it is possible to point out that one of the variables addressed in this study relates to the quality of the passive subject of the damage and the elements of the State's responsibility for the gynecological and obstetric activity.
