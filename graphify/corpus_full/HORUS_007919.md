# PARTICIPATION AS A DEMOCRATIC PRACTICE IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF A STABLE AND LURABLE PEACE

Throughout all these decades, various social organizations, especially those rooted in the rural field, indigenous, Afro-Colombian, root, precisely the main direct or indirect victims of the war, seem to have demanded a consensual path and a political solution to the internal social conflict, in particular its lethal (armed) expressions.For this reason, the search for peace always transcends any understanding that intends to exhaust it in the "silence of the rifles", or a pact between, and for, the armed actors (including the State).The peace as a vital support for a dignified con-vivence for all and all involves the whole of the social thing.I would then demand that all the voices be heard, specifically those of its victims; the proposals, especially from the working class and the disabling of the efficient factors generating the war that they feel the firm bases for their deconstruction and therefore, for the construction of peace.This understanding of the peace was adopted by the Havana Round of Conversations (MCH), from there would be recognition given to the social and community participation in the design, execution and the concrete policies. The purpose of the chapter is to present the general guidelines for the theoretical and methodological frameworks that guided the research and, using these, to show some elements of analysis and description that would allow us to situate the understandings that emerged from the discussion table in Havana regarding the role (potential) of citizen and community participation in the construction of peace. The aim of the study was to identify the main aspects of the process of participation in the implementation and territorialization of the Final Peace Agreement in Colombia 2016-2021. The aim of this study was to develop a methodology of follow-up that was adapted to the exceptional impact of the Colombian agreement on the mainstreaming of the participation approach and, on the other hand, to draw up a balance of the process of participation in the implementation of the Final Peace Agreement during the first five years of its signature
