# Sedimentology, stratigraphy and geochemistry of Grupo Guadalupe in the area of San Luis de Gacene (Boyacá, Colombia), as a potential reservoir of tar sandstones

In the foothills of the river, the sedimentary rocks converge with the sedimentary ones that share similarities between the basins of the Eastern Plains and the Eastern Cordillera. This Thesis of Maestría studies a stratigraphic section of the Grupo Guadalupe in San Luis de Gacene (Boyacá, Colombia), by means of a detailed sedimentological and stratigraphic description, complemented by petrographic and lithogeochemical analysis (diffraction and fluorescence of X rays), to evaluate the potential of these rocks in the storage of bituminous sands and other hydrocarbons. Within the detailed analysis of the faces, 7 sedimentary faces and 9 petrofaces were identified in 13 segments described, corresponding to the Arenitas formations of San Antonio, Lodolitas de Aguacaliente, and Arenitas de San Luis de Gacene. These studies explain the evolution of the sedimentary environments, revealing two regressive stages and a transgressive during the Tempranian to the Maastrichtian Campan, remaining as the main area of supply of sediments the Sanctano and mineral (the presence of Sanctano), Colombia), through a sedimentological and detailed strategic description, completed with petrography and XRD-XRF analysis, to assess the potential of tar sands host and other hydrocarbons. In the facts analysis conducted, was identified 7 sedimentary facts and 9 petrofaces in 13 described segments, corresponding to San Antonio Sandstones, Aguacaliente Mudstones and San Luis de Gazeno Sandstones formations.
