# Pilot process of community music therapy for strengthening the community social fabric of members of the association of affected by the El Quimbo hydroelectric project (Asoquimbo)

This research was carried out with the objective of analyzing, if a process of community music therapy contributed to the strengthening of the Community Social Tissue of the Affected by the El Quimbo Hydroelectric Project (Asoquimbo), through the use of tools of qualitative research, to contribute to the development of this practice and the research line. It was worked with two groups, one in the municipality of Hobo and one in the municipality of Garzón in the department of Huila, Colombia, where the population remains in socio-environmental conflict with the El Quimbo dam. The methodological process of the research was developed in four stages (reflection and analysis of the information, community approach, implementation-closure and results), based on qualitative research and community music therapy, using research-action designs and grounded theory, and using different tools for the collection, analysis and monitoring of the information. The integration process began in November 2022 and ended in June 2023 and was carried out in two stages: the approach to the community and implementation-cloirre, through the four main experiences of the Community Social Therapy. party of Huila, Colombia, where the population remains in socio-environmental conflict with the Quimbo dam.
