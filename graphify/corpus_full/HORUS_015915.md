# Construction of an educational module for teaching large geriatric syndromes through the use of problem-based learning and information and communication technologies.

Population ageing is a reality, however, the main challenges posed by this phenomenon are yet to come. Health systems require medical experts in the care of the elderly patient, however, the large amount of knowledge and skills contained in undergraduate medical programs greatly limit the availability of time for resources dedicated to geriatric learning. The use of problem-based learning (ABP) and information and communication technologies (ICT) have been used as learning tools with positive results. The aim of this work is to create an educational module that allows teaching of the five main geriatric syndromes through ABP and tics, seeking that students take ownership of knowledge in a self-directed way by enabling them to develop new skills that empower them to solve various problems affecting the geriatric population
