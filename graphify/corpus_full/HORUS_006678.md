# Mechanisms for social participation and their articulation with social programmes with Food and Nutritional Component, Child Development Center (CDI) in the municipality of El Contadero - Nariño

When social participation is understood as a complex process of debate and consultation, through which decisions are taken in favour of a common objective; this participation is perceived as an essential element in the development of policies and programmes that seek to materialize the policy objectives at the territorial level. From this, it has been observed that the way to achieve the common objectives for each territory in the area of food and nutrition security is through the social programmes that deal, especially, with the care of the vulnerable population. Thus, in Colombia, programmes that incorporate food and nutrition components have had a great history, generating attention under a care approach towards the recipients of support; although with difficulties to incorporate an effective participation.
