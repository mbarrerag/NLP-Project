# GEOMETRY SPECTERIAL OF THE L1 OBSERVER

In this talk we will study the L1-2-type surfaces immersed in the Sitter S^3_1 space and in the Sitter H^3_1 Anti space. These surfaces are characterized by the following spectral decomposition of its position vector, is it = 0 + 1 + ?2, L1Is 1 = 11, L1Is 2 = 2?2, is 1 = 2 where is 0 a constant vector in R^4_1, and is 1, is 2 non-constant functions differentiable in M2, R^4_1-valued. Additionally, we will show that a L1-2-type surface is either an open surface of a pseudo-riemannian standart product, or a B-scroll on a null curve, or is of non-constant mean curvature, non-constant Gaussian curvature, and non-constant main curvatures (see [1]).
