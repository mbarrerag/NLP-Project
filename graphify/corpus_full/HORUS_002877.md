# ASSESSMENT OF THE ENZIMATIC STABILITY OF THE LACE PRODUCTED BY PLEUROTUS OSTREATUS

The use of ligninolytic enzymes has gained great importance in the field of biotechnology in recent years because of its remarkable ability to oxidize different organic molecules, a characteristic that has undoubtedly enhanced their implementation in various industrial applications, such as pulp delignification and waste water treatment. The house, from the well-known white rot fungi is produced in large quantity and in an extracellular way. For its better functioning, these enzymes must operate under optimal conditions of temperature and half acid. In this sense, this work focused on the evaluation of the stability of the house enzyme and this way of preserving its enzyme activity for the longest possible time
