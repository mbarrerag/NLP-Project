# LINE BASED ON ECONOMIC ACTION AND FOOD SECURITY OF THE STARAMEMEQUE MUNICIPUM IN SCIENAG OF THE ZAPATOSA

The project is followed by development, transfer of technology and knowledge and innovation for the economic revival in security and food sovereignty derived from the economic, social and ecological emergency caused by the COVID-19 in the vicinity of the swamp of the Shoesa-Cesar¿, is financed with resources of the General System of Regalías of Colombia, and executed by the National University of Colombia, headquarters La Paz, Bogotá and Medellín, in alliance with the Universidad Popular del Cesar and the private company.In the Ecoregion Ciénaga de Shoesa (ECZ) (Department of Cesar ¿Colombia), inhabits a human population with low levels of schooling, low offer of formal employment, in extreme poverty, that derives its livelihood from the extractive activities of subsistence that degrade the ecosystem and compromise its biodiversity.The objective of this work was to establish a diagnosis participatory line of the municipality of Tamalameque, from sources of secondary and primary information.
