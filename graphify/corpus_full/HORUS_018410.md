# DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES FOR STRENGTHENING MILK HATE MANAGEMENT AND IMPROVING THE QUALITY AND INOCUITY OF MILK

This book contributes to the management of bovine herds and to the improvement of the quality and safety of milk through the description and analysis of the adoption of digital technologies in the dairy industry. Is presented the mobile application (app) UdeA LabLeche, a tool provided by the laboratory of quality and safety of milk of the University of Antioquia, which allows the dairy producer to access the information associated with the payment of milk in real time, which allows the timely decision making that contributes to the improvement of productivity and competitiveness of its production unit
