# IGF-1 SUPPLEMENTATION DURING THE VITRO CULTIVE OF BOVINE EMBRIONS ENHANCES THE EMPRO-BROONARY OBTENTION TAX IN ROMOSYNUA AND Brahman BLANCO BIOTYTS

It has been suggested that the growth factor similar to insulin-1 (IGF-1) can assist the bovine embryos produced in vitro affected by caloric stress in tropical regions, improving the survival rate of the embryo after transfer to the receptors. However, the effect of the addition of IGF-1 on the rate of obtaining in vitro embryos produced from breeds of Creoles such as the Romosinuano considered tolerant at high environmental temperatures has not been diluted. Therefore, the objective of this study was to evaluate the effect of the administration of different concentrations of IGF-1 during the in vitro cultivation of bovine embryos of the Romosinuano and Brahman white breed maintained in conditions of lower Colombian tropics. A total of 26 adult females of the Romosinuano breed (RS) and 20 adult females of the Brahman Blanco breed (BB) were used during the in vitro production and recovered from the Córdoba department, Colombia.
