# Estimation of cardiac work as an index of cardiovascular function in echocardiographic videos

The fraction of ventricular ejection is the echocardiographic parameter used to evaluate cardiac function (Moya, et al. 2023), even when its sensitivity is considered low and its estimation depends on the specific cycle moments to calculate the load of the blood volume. Recently, the analysis of the myocardial deformation has shown a superior sensitivity to evaluate the regional function of the myocardial and quantify the rate of contraction, while the early deterioration of the cardiac function has been reported estimating the overall deformation of the heart in the longitudinal direction. Although these methods estimate the contractile capacity of the heart in a global and regional way, they still do not capture the temporal pattern of cardiac work, whose importance has been growing since the current indicators of cardiac function, such as the ejection fraction, are inconsistent in many clinical scenarios in which these indicators are reported as normal, in the most patient-cycle errors, or are altered in conditions without pathology, probably a consequence of the number of mechanisms of adaptation of this organ to multiple load conditions.
