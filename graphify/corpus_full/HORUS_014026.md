# Sustainable optimization of vegetable production through efficient water management and water stress detection from performance simulation models and precision agriculture

Through the implementation of innovative technologies such as performance simulation models (Aquacrop) and precision farming tools that allow the detection of water stress (thermographic camera and sensors), it is possible to support decision-making to optimize vegetable crop production in a sustainable way. The use of sustainable agriculture is essential to guarantee the production of long-term food without exhausting natural resources and promoting the improvement in water resource management. The use of simulation programs allows for the prediction of crop behavior, which facilitates the making of more informed decisions in terms of irrigation, fertilisation and management. This project aims to promote research and development in the field of precision agriculture, by combining simulation technologies and monitoring systems in real time. The project will be developed in greenhouses located on the campus of the National University headquarters Bogotá
