# FINANCIALIZATION OF THE LIVING FOR RENEWAL AND PRE-FAMILIZATION OF LOW-INCOME FAMILIES IN MEDELLIN (COLOMBIA)

We use a qualitative methodology based on the analysis of secondary information on rents, incomes and poverty, together with its spatialization in the city; we also made a comparison with Cali (Colombia). We concluded that while in developed countries this funding is provided through the Real Estate Investment Funds, in Colombia, particularly in Medellín, the financing of housing through rent is manifested when families with surplus capital buy Social Interest Housing, which is promoted by the State, and rent it to lower income families: the original recipients of these policies
