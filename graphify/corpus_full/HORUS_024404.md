# Methodology of mass balance and prediction of metallurgical indicators in coal washing plants.

In a washing plant, the separation of coal and sterile products from backrests (floors, roofs and intercalations) from the exploitation mantle is of vital importance in the refining of coal, because intrinsically the mantles have a high degree of contamination (minerals) which affects the calorific power of the coals. Currently, the Cerrejón Colombia washing plant uses gravity separation by dense means, which is based on the use of pseudofluid compounds composed of suspensions of water with magnetite. The company has since previously identified that the production adjustments of the inventories of the coal cell from the washing plant have been considerably high when compared with the measurements made between the different agents involved in the estimation of the mass flows. To make an adjustment that allows the control of the data in the washing plant, a methodology of mass balance and prediction of metallurgical indices is proposed from the unification and structuring of data from the coal washing plant, using the current programmatic environment of the statistical software “RStudio”.
