# Evaluation to measure the financial viability of installing renewable energy by solar panels in textile companies in Manizales, Caldas, Nicole case study in the city of Manizales

This research evaluates the financial viability of implementing photovoltaic solar energy systems in the textile sector of Manizales, Caldas, taking the company Nicole as a case study. The work examines the global and local energy landscape, analyzing the various sources of energy generation and their impact on the industrial sector. The research starts from a comprehensive analysis of the global energy context, focusing on the transition to renewable sources and its implications for industry. It specifically examines the textile sector in Manizales, assessing its energy consumption patterns and opportunities for improvement in terms of efficiency and sustainability. The methodology used focuses on a detailed financial analysis, using key indicators such as the Internal Return Rate (IRR) and the Current Net Value (VAN) to assess the economic viability of the project. This analysis considers variables as installation costs, maintenance, useful life of the equipment and savings projected in the energy consumption study. The results of the study demonstrate the financial viability of the implementation of solar panels in the company Nicole, supported by positive economic indicators and a period of recovery of the attractive investment. The conclusions highlight the strategic opportunity that represents the adoption of the photovoltaic energy framework for the textile sector.
