# SYNTHESIS PARALEL AMINOPIRAZOLIL-HYDROXIINDOL-2-ONES COMBINATORY BY A NON-CONVENTIONAL HETERAROMATICAL ELECTROPHIL SUBSTITUTION

A simple, clean and fast methodology was implemented, with which 34 derivatives of 3-(aminopyrazol-4-yl)-3-hydroxyindol-2-one were obtained simultaneously. The methodology used consisted of a parallel combined synthesis assisted by ultrasound of the above-mentioned derivatives, from 1-aryl-3-methyl-5-aminopyrazoles in reaction with isatine derivatives. The same study evaluated the optimal reaction conditions with respect to solvent, catalyst, time and reaction temperature, with which yields were obtained between 52 and 92% of the final products
