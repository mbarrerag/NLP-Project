# Manufacture of clinker with gold mining waste

In Colombia, more than 15 departments and about 100 municipalities develop gold mining at different scales and these operations generate mining waste composed of processing fluids and crushed rock with metals and high silica content. Clinker, plaster and other corrective additives are required for cement production. Clinker results from the partial fusion of some minerals, including silica. In this study, it is proposed to evaluate the effect of the use of sands from mineral tailings from gold mining on the manufacture of clinker. The proposed methodology will allow to characterize sands to manufacture a cement proveta and measure their physical properties. .
