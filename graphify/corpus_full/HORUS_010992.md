# ﻿A new species of (Piperaceae) with peltate leaves from Serranía de las Quinchas, Colombia.

is described and illustrated as a new species occurring in the understory of wet montane forest of the middle Magdalena Valley in Colombia, the easternmost portion of the Chocó Region. Its relationships are discussed with related taxa from the clade. An identification key for 35 Neotropical species with peltate leaves is provided
