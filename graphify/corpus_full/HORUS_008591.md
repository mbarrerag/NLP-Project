# Municipal associativeism as a strategy for overcoming territorial imbalances in Antioquia: the case of Cartama province, South West Antioquia

The subregions of Antioquia suffer deep territorial imbalances, which are evidenced when contrasted with the living conditions in the capital of the department and in the municipalities of the Aburrá Valley. In view of this scenario, the present research seeks to understand if, the figure of Province as model of municipal associativeness, can help to overcome these territorial imbalances, and to improve the quality of life in the network of the South West of Antioquia; above all, in the matter of higher education, health and public services house. Thus, it is a zoom in the province Cartama and how it has contributed to the improvement of these variables in the 11 municipalities that make it. For this, a combination of quantitative methods was carried out as the comparative analysis of data of the province Cartama, from 2016 to 2025, the assessment of the Provincial Strategic Plan and the realization of surveys to the community; as well as qualitative methods by means of field visits, interviews to leaders/as of the subregion. The intention is to confront the theory and normivity of the province, with the reality in which it is inserted in the state of the subregional network of services and the location of the territorial imbalances, in the three regional imbalances. and public uses in the older municipalities that purchase it. Quantitative methods were used, including a comparative analysis of data from 2016 to 2022 and evaluation of the Provincial Strategic Plan, along with community surveys. Qualitative methods, such as field visits, interviews with experts and subregion leaders, were also employed. The intention is to face the theory and regulations of the province with the current reality, understanding the state of the subregional network of services and the location of territorial implications.
