# Chemical modelling of methanol as an extragalactic shock tracer

The behaviour of methanol (CH3OH) in the compound galaxy NGC 1068 was analyzed to investigate its effectiveness as a tracer of extragalactic shocks and to study the effect of the shock speed and the formation routes on the expected interstellar abundance of CH3OH. With a physical model parametrized shock rate type C, the chemistry of the shock with UCLCHEM was investigated to study the chemical abundance of CH3OH according to the time in different locations of the Circumnuclear Disc (CND) near the Active Nucleus of Galaxia (AGN) and the Starburst ring (SB) of NGC 1068. It is concluded that the gaseous phase methanol can trace C-type shocks, since its abundance increases significantly due to the spraying or sublimation of the frozen grains during the propagation of the shock.
