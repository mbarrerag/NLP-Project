# Marginal quantile modelling in the presence of missing data using the regression model class with normal/independent multivariate distribution

In this research work, the main objective is to achieve marginal quantile modeling under the presence of missing data, taking into account the association between the variables of the response vector. A Bayesian approach is used, taking advantage of the tools offered by it, as well as algorithms (which will be described later) to carry out the process of imputation and approximation of subsequent distributions. The validity of the model is evaluated by simulation studies, which confirm the satisfactory performance in the process of estimating the parameters. In addition, a practical application of the model to a set of real data is presented, thus providing an additional validation of its usefulness and applicability in empirical contexts. . In this research modeling, we propose the development of a multiple linear regression modeling with the class of normal/independent multiple distributions
