# VIRTUAL SENSORS IN INGENIería: A CONTRIBUTION FROM THE MATHEMATICS IN THE DESIGN OF STATE ESTIMATORS.

Virtual sensors are devices used to obtain important information about the variables of a process, when such variables are difficult to measure directly. The design of virtual sensors is based on a state estimator, which is a dynamic system, normally represented by differential equations, capable of predicting the internal variables of the process with some precision. This estimator has several mathematical properties, which ensure that the design of the virtual sensor meets the specifications of the actual problem
