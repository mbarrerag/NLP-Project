# Increased production in agroecosystems of Theobroma cocoa L. with the use of organic residues stabilized in the form of biocarbon for agroecological alternatives

The project is entitled _x0093_Increased production in agroecosystems of Theobroma cacao L. with the use of organic waste stabilized in the form of biocarbon for the purpose of agroecological alternatives_x0094_. It aims to study, evaluate and analyze the addition of organic waste stabilized in the form of biocarbon in an agroecosystem of Theobroma cacao L. for the purpose of improving its production. The exercise consists of taking advantage of the residues of the agroecosystem itself to create a soil conditioner such as biocarbon that in its addition to the soil, and that, as experience shows, can improve soils in terms of the physical, the biological and the chemical. It is expected that through an edaphological analysis, the physiology of the plant and a study of the soil-plant relationship, it can be determined how well-used these practices are to increase production. Likewise, the study leaves room for analyzing the positive externalities that could contribute to the use of such waste, particularly in this agroecosystem, from the environmental and social perspectives
