# Quantum Trajectories in non-Markovian system via the reaction coordinate mapping.

The dynamics of systems coupled to an environment are often difficult to obtain; their solutions are based on approximations, which can be unreliable in many cases. Here we use the reaction co-ordinate mapping and quantum trajectories to study the dynamics of systems that interact ́uan strongly. A master Lindblad derivation equation is tested for the Spin-Boson system at the phase limit, where the map of reaction coordinates was applied. The master equation is disentangled with quantistic trajectories, where a numerical improvement was found. Lindblad’s master equation was used for a pair of qubits that do not interact submerged in a bosonic bath. The map of reaction coordinates shows the presence of interlacing between the qubits and rebirth of interlacing.
