# Pedagogical devices for memory. A reflection from the grammars of listening

This research is carried out in a private institution in the city of Bogotá. Its main objective is to implement a pedagogical device that allows teachers to teach about the Colombian armed conflict with the intention of making the voices of the victims of violence visible. Therefore, the stories of the victims of the armed conflict were used for teaching violence and in this way to bring students closer to the study of the history of Colombia. The research that was developed was participatory in that it allowed more information to be obtained and the subjectivity of the responses of the students was not overlooked. Finally, the implementation of the pedagogical device turns out to be favorable, since the students enjoyed the way in which they learned about the history of the conflict and recommended to implement the workshop in different places, since it is visible through listening to the voice of those who have suffered the horrors of the war and allows generating a consciousness and empathy vistabilty towards the topic of the armed conflict. .
