# ROBUST CONTROL H8 OVER AN ACTIVE DAMPING SYSTEM OF A VEHICLE ROOM

This paper aims to show and analyze the different types of suspensions: passive, semi-active, and active in a vehicle room. It presents a robust H8 controller for an active suspension system of the quarter vehicle. Implementing this controller and estimating the weight functions are to minimize vibrations in the vertical axis. This paper compares three control systems corresponding to high comfort, balanced, and highly maneuverable systems. This comparison from the active suspension will allow us to observe that these systems reduce the deflection of the suspension and the acceleration of the sprung mass below the rattle space frequency. A time-domain simulation used a path disturbance signal representing ground irregularities to evaluate the three designs as an essential contribution to this paper. This paper shows that it is possible to develop a control strategy H8 with robust performance to control a complex analysis system through state feedback
