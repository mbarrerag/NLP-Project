# Support for strengthening the Thesis project: Reading and writing pathways in indigenous leaders of the Amazonian northeastern region

This research proposal aims to analyze the strategies associated with the use and management of reading and writing in the exercise of education administration, within the framework of the political organizational processes of the Association of Traditional Captains and Authorities of the Pirá Paraná River, -ACAIPI-, department of Vaupés, Colombian Amazonia. The analysis is posed from the sociocultural perspective of reading and writing studies, which allows recognizing and addressing historical, social and cultural aspects to understand contextual forms of access, appropriation and participation of actors mediated by written culture. The approach will be focused on knowing the trajectories of different actors, configured in hegemonic scenarios on reading and writing, and the strategies developed in relation to institutionality. This research is qualitative, descriptive and analytical in nature with an ethnographic approach and gives priority to the temporal perspective. The field work will be guided by a set of techniques and tools such as life histories, interviews, participant observation, and will have an important documentary and archival review. This research is expected to contribute to the understanding of the relations of indigenous organizations with the written culture and organizational role
