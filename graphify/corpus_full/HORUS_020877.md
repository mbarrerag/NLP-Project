# Sustainability proposal based on the analysis of the socio-ecological conflicts of tourism use in the Condor Route (Villamaría, Caldas)

The problems associated with the sustainability of tourist destinations, such as the Condor Route, have been extensively analyzed in the literature available in the last decade. In this study, it is common for tourism to be attributed the destruction or deterioration of socio-cultural, ecological and economic contexts in which the business associated with tourism is developed. The project seeks to analyze the sustainability of the Condor Route from conflicts in the socio-ecosystems with tourism use in the villages and communities involved in the municipality of Villamaria (Caldas). To achieve this, it is necessary to describe the main conflicts in socio-ecosystems with tourism use as a productive strategy in the aforementioned Route. Likewise, to identify the effects of conflicts in the socio-ecosystem with tourism use; and, finally, to propose a socio-ecological sustainability structure for the tourism use in the Condor Route
