# Virtual course implementation as a pedagogical tool for learning neonatal ultrasound, in graduate students of the specialty of Radiology and Diagnostic Images

In Colombia, there is a progressive increase in the percentage of preterm births. The prematurity and low weight, recognized risk factors for illness and hospital stay, most of these children need diagnostic medical images. The ultrasound is the diagnostic modality of choice in newborns and neonates, due to the absence of ionizing radiation, its wide availability, portability to the patient's bed-tub and the improvement in image resolution. In view of this panorama, the Department of Radiology and Diagnostic Images, of the National University of Colombia develops strategies and didactic material so that the resident of the specialty receives adequate training for the optimal realization and report of ultrasound studies in newborns and encourages in doctors, pediatricians and neonatologists to count on his support. A virtual course is carried out as a pedagogical tool for learning neonatal ultrasound, aimed at residents of the specialty in Radiology and Diagnostic Images. Diagnostic Imaging of the National University of Colombia developing strategies and teaching materials so that the resident of the speciality received adapted training for the optimal performance and reporting of ultrasound studies in newborns and promotion in doctors, paediatricians and neonatologists have your support.
