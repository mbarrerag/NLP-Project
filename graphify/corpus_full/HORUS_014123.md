# Contributions to a sustainable habitat through the use of plywood panels of reforested wood and bamboo guadua

In Colombia, the housing deficit amounts to 31% of households, showing a latent need for affordable, safe, and environmentally sustainable housing. Likewise, this demand should be framed in the sustainable development of the region, considering the environmental impact of the construction sector. One of the proposals to address this need is the use of biomaterials in the construction of housing, such as wood and bambu. Biomaterials possess the property of capturing CO2, and the elements destined for its structural use have mechanical properties of great interest. These elements, called Wood Ingenieria Products (PIM) involve products manufactured from sawn wood pieces, sheets, particles or wood fibers, uniting the mechanical and geometric properties between similar elements. Among the PIMs are the incorporation of Contrachapada wood and the press (Cros-Laminated Timber, CLT), which consists of a solid panel of wooden layers, varying between three, five or seven, stacked Europeans, alternating form of opposite the description of fibers, a panel. In three main phases: In the first phase, it is proposed to carry out experimental tests of the constituent materials and of the behavior of the composite panel under horizontal loads, in the laboratories of structures of UNAL and UDFJC. In the second phase, it is sought to simulate the panels tested through the method of finite elements in order to evaluate states of effort and analyze in greater depth the performance of the panel, in a collaborative work between UNAL and UDFJC. Finally, in the third phase, it is proposed the development of an an analogue-digital communication strategy of this system with the potentially beneficial community, favoring the social appropriation of knowledge, using didactic resources articulated with the concept of Design Thinking, led by Unicolmayor. With this proposal, it is sought to present a contribution to sustainable habitats through an alternative of safe, affordable housing, and built with local manpower and resources, aligned with the commitment of sustainable construction that is being promoted globally
