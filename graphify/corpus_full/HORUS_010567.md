# CASE STUDY: SUSTAINABILITY IN THE CAMPUS, UNIVERSITARY OF THE UNAL - HEADQUARTERS

The National University of Colombia in Manizales is divided into three campuses: Palogrande, El Cable and La Nubia. Teaching at the university is taught by 260 professors to about 5340 students in three main areas: Administration, Exact and Natural Sciences and Engineering and Architecture. This chapter presents a reflection and a diagnosis to understand the importance of sustainable development for the case of the three campuses of Manizales Headquarters as case studies. It focuses on: 1) assessing the current situation of sustainability on campuses; 2) reviewing the sustainable solutions being carried out at the University; and 3) providing an overview of alternative sustainability solutions. Research included the conduct of semi-structured face-to-face interviews with the use of primary and secondary data. Emphasis will be placed on research carried out on campus, the measures applied and those that could be implemented. How can campus resources be better managed? What is the University’s policy and what avenues could be explored for sustainable management? The results are used to recommend options for sustainable management of the university environment
