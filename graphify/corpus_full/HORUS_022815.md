# Assessment of the detection capability of the AWS D1.5 ultrasound procedure for examining vehicle bridges using artificial reflectors.

The present study focused on quantitatively determining the detection and evaluation capacity of the ultrasound method (with an echo amplitude approach) of the American Society of Soldering for the examination of bridges and welded buildings. Before addressing the experimental part, analysis of the state of art of the origin of this procedure and the criticisms that have been made during almost sixty years of use in the United States was carried out. Three experiments (0, 1 and 2) were carried out consisting of the examination of steel blocks ASTM A709 Gr. 50W, used in bridges, to which two types of artificial flat reflectors (NTH and FBH) were introduced and one type of volumetric reflector (SDH) located in two thickness positions, of four sizes (0.5 mm, 1.0 mm, 3.0 mm and 5 mm). The blocks were examined with a pulse-eco ultrasound equipment, of manual form, following the prescriptive technique of the AWS procedure (Expo 0) and altering two characteristics that could influence the detection capacity of the procedure: the size of the crystal (experiment 2) and the sample 5 mm).
