# Development of environmental and cultural strategies with local communities for the reduction, management and final disposal of plastic waste through the formation of a Territorial Development Network for Circular Economy, Environmental Education and Social Innovation of San Andrés de Tumaco

The project of joint and several extension is located in the municipality of San Andrés de Tumaco, located in the Pacific coast of Colombia, at the southern end of the coast, as part of the region of the Biogeographic Chocó. Tumaco is located 300 kilometers to the south of the city of San Juan de Pasto. The proposal arises as a result of the social and environmental impacts observed due to the geographical and institutional limitations for the management and proper management of plastic waste, which result in pollution problems, mainly in coastal areas and mangrove ecosystems, as well as problems of public health and environmental sanitation. This, together with the regional dynamics that have placed Tumaco as a zone of high vulnerability with low access to formal education, few job opportunities and crisis of governance and governance, makes necessary the formulation of initiatives that articulate various actors of society for the strengthening of the areas of territorial development, will seek to develop the strategies of the integrated, environmental and cultural communities for the reduction, management and final disposition of plastic waste through the formation of a Network of Territorial Development for the Circular Economy, the Environmental Education and the Social Innovation of San Andrés de Tumaco, will seek to analyze the strategies of the sustainable. the municipal administration and other local, public and private actors will contribute to the transfer of knowledge that leads to critical reflection and pedagogical innovation of teachers and students of the university community with institutional actors, the various social sectors and local communities
