# Morpho-agronomic and molecular comparison of fourteen varieties of rice (Oryza sativa L.) with the lines that gave their origin

With the aim of determining the morpho-agronomic and quality differences, and the genetic diversity between 14 rice varieties in Latin America with their respective lines of origin, a study was established (random complete blocks, with 28 genotypes, three repetitions and two sowings over time), in which 25 morpho-agronomic and grain quality variables were measured. The molecular analysis was made by an arrangement of 96 SNP type markers with high capacity of discrimination for rice Indica. The statistical analysis was done by combining the data of the two sowings because there were no statistical differences between them. Furthermore, molecular data were analyzed together with the morpho-agronomic and quality, using the Gower index to generate a similarity matrix. Through the program SAS agronomic and molecular data were analyzed both independently and together. The results showed that, of the 14 varieties, eight were grouped with their line of origin and there was a variety that was grouped with a sister line of its ancestro. The results were consistent when the data analysis was made independent and given to make the distinctive varieties within them, eight were grouped with their origin
