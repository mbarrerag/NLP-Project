# Model for the planning of routes and services for the collection and transport of waste in the Valle de Aburrá.

The present project examines from an analytical point of view the variables existing in the problem of solid waste in the Aburrá valley, from the external and internal components of the organizations providing the public toilet service. External factors such as citizen incultura, the problem of migrants in cities, people in street situations, as well as internal factors associated with the mechanical problems of the collection vehicles, the times and movements of the operation, and their logistical problems within the fulfilment of the routes are described. A model is proposed that generates control points for the companies providing the public toilet service, taking into account population, commercial, tons historically collected data, in order to generate an improvement in the points indicated by the model, that allows to optimize the processes with efficiency and quality based on the current normivity. For the validation of the model, two scenarios were considered where the services to be provided were modified by day, and based on these data, control situations were posed to be verified by each provider of the service in case of improvement have application in their organization
