# DETERMINATION OF ANTIBIOTIC RESISTANCE OF BACTERIA REPRESENTATIVE OF THE MICROBIOTA OF DOUBLECREAM CHEESE

Antibiotic resistance is a global public health problem. Lactic acid bacteria (LAB) are introduced into the food chain as starter cultures and due to their health benefits on the host also as probiotics. Concerns have been raised in the use of some probiotics strains that carry antibiotic resistance genes themselves. Then it is important to select probiotics that do not contribute to the spread of antibiotic resistance [1]
