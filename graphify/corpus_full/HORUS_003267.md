# STRATEGIES FOR INCREASING THE NUMBER OF ENGINEERING SIGNIERS: REVIEW AND RECOMMENDATIONS RELATING TO LITERATURA

The decrease in the number of applicants in certain engineering careers is a trend that has impacted several programs in Colombia, including IE, Electronic Engineering and Control Engineering. This phenomenon is not unique to Colombia, as it has been observed in other countries, which has led to the implementation of various strategies to encourage senior students from middle school to choose these careers. Such strategies range from conducting studies and surveys to understand students' motivation, to implementing collaborative projects between colleges and universities. This article presents a review on this topic, accompanied by recommendations to address the problem in the Colombian context
