# Cadaveric description of the technique of reconstruction of the interosseous ligament talus calcaneous subtalar by arthroscopy.

The instability of the subtalar joint (IAS) is an entity that in many cases is subdiagnosticized, however, is a common condition that occurs in approximately a quarter of the cases of lateral instability of the ankle.It is a non-recognized cause of failure during the surgical management of the lateral instability, as well as the persistence of functional instability in the ankle. Clinically it can manifest with pain at the level of the tarso, stiffness and sprains to repeat OBJETIVES: Describe by means of the models cadaveric the technique, all in, of reconstruction of the talo-calcan ligament assisted by subtalar artroscopic, determining its safety and viability MEDOTODOLOGY: Anatomic study of the ligament remnant, in 10 specimens made in the Latin American center of minimally invasive surgery CLEMI. The structures of the lateral and medial graft were determined by the previous perforation using the perforation of two tunnels one at the calcanion level with reference of the ligament footprint and another in the astragalus, using a guide of the C, step of the bypass and the structures. Arthroscopic reconstruction of the LITC is a minimally invasive option for the treatment of subtalar instability management
