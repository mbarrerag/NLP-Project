# PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN INTESTINAL FUNCTIONAL TRAITS (FBD) AND THE PATRON OF FRUIT, VERDULES AND WATER CONSUMPTION IN A BOGOTA GROUP

the functional intestinal disorders-FBBDs- arise from disordered interactions of the intestinal-brabrain axis (1). Factors to develop FBD include insufficient consumption of fiber and water, due to the increased risk of intestinal dysbiosis and modulation of the colonic transit (2). On the consumption pattern of Colombians there are no recent indicators, but according to (ENSIN-2015) there are frequency/day of 0.5 for fruits and vegetables, drinking water (77.9%) (3). General objective: to determine the association between the presence of intestinal functional disorders and the consumption pattern of fruit, vegetables and water consumption in a group of adults aged 18-60 years old in Bogotá, (2023). case-control study, 121 adults. The cases were diagnosed with FBD by ROMA-IV criteria, according to R4DQ. A virtual questionnaire was used with questions: number of water/day/day glasses (volume:200 to 250 to 250,240ml), frequency of consumption of fruits and vegetables. FiberScreen about daily portions of FBD by FOMA-IV and vegetables (number-in-to-type). as a graduate work of a student (Nutrition and Dietetics-UN)
