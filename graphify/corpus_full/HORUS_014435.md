# Carbon Nanoespheres: A look from the synthesis process.

Carbon nanospheres are a potential exploration field, since their formation and properties allow them to find solutions or alternatives to environmental problems. Carbon nanospheres and their great versatility in shape, texture and size are a material that has characteristics of potential use in the industrial and, can be synthesized from different methods however, the hydrothermal carbonization method (HTC) is promising in economic and environmental terms, because of its advantage in the use of reagents, conditions such as temperature, pressure, residence time and pH, generate noticeable changes in materials that, from its study, can be used in the removal of pollutants, as catalysts and other applications. Finally, biomass has shown good performance in the process of synthesis of carbon nanospheres, since its water content is used in the HTC process, thus forming a productive and viable relationship between residual biomass and HTC method.
