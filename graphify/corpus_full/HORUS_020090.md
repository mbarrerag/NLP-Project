# Agro-industrial processes in avocado cv Hass

The project is funded by the Science, Technology and Innovation Fund of the General System of Regalías of the National Planning Department and aims to benefit 15,000 people in the Cauca Valley. It is aimed at increasing sustainable competitiveness in agriculture on the slopes of Valle del Cauca, through research and development processes in the different links of the productive chain, ranging from the initial stage of cultivation to the agro-industrial stage of the three selected fruit trees: pineapple md-2, avocado Hass and mora de Castilla. -- Contraved - This document corresponds to the deliverable of the specific activities 1, 2 and 3. Here are the results of the products and processes selected according to the gaps identified for the Hass avocado chain, both fresh and processed, and a comparative analysis of the results is carried out. Technological surveillance was carried out including 4 types of surveillance (competitive, commercial, scientific-technological and strategic). Based on these, the products and processes corresponding to the fruits taken as the object of study were selected. -- Page 11
