# Child-psychism from the perspective of Esther Bick

The objective of this documentary review is to show the importance for mental health professionals of the observation of babies in their own environment (Esther Bick method) since this contributes directly to the generation of theoretical postulates about child development and helps to detect possible alterations in mother-child dyad. The theoretical review is the product of the search for information through scientific articles published in journals indexed in the databases: Redalyc, Dialnet, Scielo and Medline. Esther Bick’s method of observation of babies facilitates the diagnosis of disorders in infants by favoring the perception of early psychic development processes. It also helps the discovery of problems related to the early mother-child bond
