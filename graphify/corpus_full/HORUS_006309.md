# Guerrilla Wisdoms: about the FARC-EP training process (1982-2016)

Cultural, educational and epistemic are dimensions from which to investigate the formation process of the FARC-EP. In this task, this work focuses on the development and incorporation of wisdoms and practices that this formative process has given rise to; generating a look, an epistemic point of view to interpret reality and to practically affect it. In this context, there is a wealth of guerrilla knowledge located, memories, experiences, which are interwoven, discovering the complexity of an insurgent formative process and its organizational structures. In the field of Cultural Studies, especially Latin American studies, it is necessary to broaden the horizons of critical reflection about the training experiences in in the insurgence, and the epistemic point of view that underlies them.
