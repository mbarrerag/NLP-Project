# METHODOLOGIES FOR THE CONSIDERATION OF PROTEIC INTERACTIONS AND APPLICATIONS IN THE CASE OF PLANT-BACTERY RELATIONSHIP

The machine that allows the correct cell function involves mainly proteins. To fulfill their activities, proteins establish interactions between them. For their study, the techniques of double hybrid yeasts, co-immunoprecipitation, GST pull-down, cell location, BiFC and FRET have been used. In this review, a description of these methodologies is presented. In addition, a brief description of how the application of these methodologies has allowed to deepen the knowledge of the mechanisms that are activated during the relationship established by plants with phytopathogenic bacteria is presented, as a case study
