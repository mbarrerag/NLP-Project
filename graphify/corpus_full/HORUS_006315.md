# Who are they? characterization of the bodies of the amphitheater of the medical faculty of the National University from forensic anthropology

The aim of this research is to characterize, from a forensic anthropological approach, the Unidentified and Unclaimed Bodies, which have been arranged for teaching and research purposes in the medical amphitheatre of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá. This is in order to understand the form in which the amphitheater and its practices were formed, the disposition of corpses for academic purposes, their characteristics, as well as to support their identification processes. This by means of a methodology, both quantitative and qualitative, consisting of three phases: documentary phase, laboratory phase and contrasting phase. In this study, we sought to collect in the largest amount of context information about the amphitheatre and regulations of academic practices, as well as of the information before the mortem, peritem and postmortem of the bodies that were delivered to the faculty of medicine. As a result, we analyzed a total of 885 historical records of corpses in the amphitheatre from 1971 to 2012, we analyzed in laboratory a total of 85 individuals and 106 groups, and we performed a contrast of information from 152 historical records and 34 individuals for the Amphitheatres.
