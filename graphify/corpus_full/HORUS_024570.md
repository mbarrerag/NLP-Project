# Contributions and challenges of the Collection of Mammals “Alberto Cadena García” of the Institute of Natural Sciences to the knowledge of this group in Colombia

The Collection of Mammals “Alberto Cadena García” of the Institute of Natural Sciences has specimens of great heritage and biological value for the knowledge of mammals in Colombia, which is concrete through the availability of the information associated with these specimens and their relevance for teaching, research in different areas and for access to information by the general public. In order to demonstrate the impact that the Collection has had, the taxonomic, geographical and temporal representativeness of the specimens was examined by analyzing the information associated with these specimens and reviewing the literature related to their use. The Collection has specimens of all orders of Colombian mammals, 80.5 per cent of the species reported for Colombia and specimens from all departments of the country. On the other hand, the Collection has also contributed to the training in mastozology of many students of the undergraduate biology program, to the development of 167 undergraduate and these works in various subjects, to more than 100 publications reported in recent years and has been visited by more than 190 national and foreign researchers in the last ten years. Given the impact of the Collection it is important to strengthen the processes of dissemination of information and the these studies in various subjects, to contribute to the diversity of the population to the continuing to the knowledge of the population collection
