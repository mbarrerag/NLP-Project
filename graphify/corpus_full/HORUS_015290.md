# Effect of an imaging training on biomechanical aspects of a sports gesture in gymnasts of the club Met Chia.

Mental training is a fundamental part of the training of any sportsman, including children (Rivera et.al, 2015). Imagination and observational action are techniques that allow to simulate mental movements and therefore train cognitive aspects of learning, memory and motor control (Guilot & Collet, 2010) that are necessary for the practice of sports. However, its effect on children athletes is unknown. Therefore, this research aimed to propose an imaging training (EIM) designed for children athletes, which allows to evaluate its effect on psychological and biomechanical aspects in the execution of a sports gesture in artistic gymnastics. To this end, a pre-post study was developed where both the ability for imagery and the execution of the behind arch (ArcA), in 5 gymnasts, between 7 and 12 years old, both before and after a training of 14 imaging sessions, varying its difficulty and specificity. The results suggest that imagery was effective in improving aspects of the execution of movement as the signal coordination and propulsion.
