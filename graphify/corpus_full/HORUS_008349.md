# Reference levels for the most common diagnostic imaging procedures performed at the National University Hospital

To establish the typical values or institutional diagnostic reference levels of the most frequent fluoroscopscop and tomographic procedures in the National University Hospital, it is necessary to understand the functioning of the ionizing radiation generating equipment; to determine the dosimetric quantities that are used pre-dilecturally for this purpose; to collect sufficient information and to carry out statistical analysis for each procedure; but if fluoroscopic equipment does not have the technology to measure the dosimetric parameters directly, a methodology is proposed to estimate the Kerma in air at the input surface and the product kerma area. To characterize the radiation at the exit of the x-ray tube, (performance function) and to evaluate this function with the technical voltage and current values used during each recorded procedure
