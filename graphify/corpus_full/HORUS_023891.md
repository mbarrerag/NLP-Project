# ADAPTATION OF RENEWED CONTROL PRACTICES USED BY LABVIEW AND TEAMVIEWER

This article presents the remote adaptation of the laboratory of the course Control Input-Output, which has as main objective that the student applies the concepts of the theory of control, while, acquiring logic skills of programming through the LabVIEW software to generate own solutions to a real problem of control. For the adaptation to remote modality it was taken into account that: access to a computer with LabVIEW is required to communicate with plants and one person is needed to light plants. In addition, since it is only counted with two plants HVAC (Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning) and two plants VTOL (Vertical Take-Off and Landing) is necessary to be able to take the students to their plants for their homes. Initially, all the laboratory sessions that were to be implemented during the work were analyzed, to identify the hardware and/or software needs in each of them. Thus, the designs and implementations necessary to adapt existing practices according to the requirements were carried out.
