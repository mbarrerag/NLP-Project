# In search of the national soul. Capital cities and commemorative times: Bogotá, Quito and Lima, 1909 – 1924

Since the beginning of republican life, capital cities have played a relevant role in the construction of the political, cultural and economic life of the nations they were part of. Despite this importance, there are still few researches that account for the process through which these urban spaces managed to build and maintain a relevance to other national territories that tried to claim for themselves the place of political center the nation. This research seeks to understand the way in which the primacy of the capital city was built that is still a relevant fact especially in Latin America. To this effect, two major scenarios are addressed; the first of them sought to understand how through culture and, especially, scientists and intellectuals a discourse was designed that justified the role of that urban territory specifically as a sumun of the nation. On the other hand, it explains how together with intellectuals and politicians, urbanists, engineers and architects designed spaces, they formulated narratives and constructions that would account for that claim to be the axis of the national soul.
