# The civil society counter-accounts: Case analysis in the sugarcane agro-industrial sector and indigenous and Afro-descendant communities in Cauca, Colombia

This paper discusses the academic literature on accountability, problematizing the shortcomings of the socio-environmental information disclosed by the organizations to account for the socio-environmental conflicts that cause in their areas of influence. Thus, from the critical perspective of accountability, the counter-accounts elaborated by civil society actors emerge as an alternative way of giving and demanding accounts to dispute the sense of truth to the organizations. To address this topic, the socio-environmental conflict caused by the agro-industry of the cane in the north of the department of Cauca is taken as a case study.
