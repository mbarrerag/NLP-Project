# Study of the retrievability and quality of rare earth minerals as a by-product of alluvial gold mining.

This work presents a possible route of benefit for minerals of rare earth (REE) and other minerals such as magnetite, ilmenita, rutil and zircons contained in the black sands of an industrial process of alluvial mining of gold. This route of benefit includes separations by size, magnetic, gravimetric and electrostatic sources of a current that is part of the current tails of the exploitation of a deposit of alluvial pleasure of gold located in the Lower Antioqueño Cauca (Colombia). This current has a concentration of heavy minerals greater than 50%, distributed in different ranges of size and mineral associations. It was found that the monacita, mineral of rare earths of greater interest, is concentrated in the thick fraction (+20 meshes, size greater than 0.85 millimeters), with a 1.4% of participation in this fraction, this fraction of thick ones at its time equals to 7% of the total current study, and in the light of the technical fraction (-70 meshes, sizes less than 0.212 millimeters), with a 0.23% of participation in this fraction that at the same time equals to 27% of the current of this study
