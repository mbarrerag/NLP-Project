# CHAPTER 5: THE TRAYECTORIES AND THE SELF-ADSDCRIPTION PROCESS OF VICTIMS IN SOCIAL NETWORKS

The recent history of the recognition of the victims of the armed conflict in Colombia has two distinct moments: the first is the period before the year 2005, whose greatest difficulty was the lack of public knowledge, by the political elite in power, of the existence of an armed conflict, which meant that there was no legal framework guaranteeing the protection of the rights of the victims; the second starts with the Law of Justice and Peace (2005) and is consolidated with Law 1448 of 2011, also known as the Law of Victims and Restitution of Lands. With the legal formalization of the victims in Colombia, a series of social and political transformations has been promoted: one of these is the greater presence and public exposure of the victims from their testimonies to what happened. Another of these turns has to do with the creation of contents and dissemination by those who have been recipients of some violence, some of whom have been exemplified in the previous chapters and will be presented also in this chapter, learning from the current ones for the visibilization of their cases. the possibility of interaction and immediacy of publication, allowing victims to reconstruct the facts and reflections while giving a sense and a shared identity to experience through this type of media
