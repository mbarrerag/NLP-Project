# Walk the word “museum.” Between the rite, artifact and the community

Walk the word “museum”. A museology that mambea and takes yagé The present grade work is composed of three written components to opt for the title of Master in Museumology and Heritage Management. The first part contains a conceptual work called Walking the museum. A museology that mambea and takes yagé. In this section I present a reflection on the relationship of ancestral practices with plants of knowledge and the concept of museum, through a historical review and a reflection on the concepts of the new museology. The second part consists of memory and reflections on practice to accompany the project Sweetening the Word in which I participated in the elaboration of a didactic booklet as a tool of mediation for the exhibition. Finally, I present the memory of collaborative practice in the ETCR Las Colinas where a popular school of museological formation is proposed for strengthening the peace process in the community. as a result of the virtual activities and the exit from the field. Finally, the memory of the collaborative practice in the ETCR-The hills where a popular school of museological training is proposed, for the strengthening of the peace process in the community, as a result of virtual activities and the field trip. Through these three experiences some reflectionare proposed to think about a museumlogy in relation and practice from the wisdom of peoplesand plants of knowledge
