# INTERSECTIONALITY.

This new book from the Masa Crítica collection, supported by TNI, asks about what underpins the privileges of the elites in Latin America; and the answer leaves no room for doubt: racism, classism and sexism. This book argues that it is not possible to speak of class inequalities without addressing racial inequalities and sexism as a fundamental structure in Latin American societies. Intersectional and decolonial debate is part of reflections on inequalities, as well as the fruitful tradition of feminisms as social movements and critical theories
