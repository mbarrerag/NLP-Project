# PRACTICE VISIBILITY, ALTERNATIVE IN THE TRAINING OF YOUTH FOR IMAGE REFLECTION AND PRODUCTION.

From a search for the generation of visual content, in which the level of intention and awareness about the message to be transmitted is mediated by an experiential and practical process of image writing, this research-creation is based on the premise: the social changes bring with them new challenges for the formation of young people, their dynamics, collective practices and expectations that are transformed permanently turning them into technological nomadic.In recent years, photography has been pervaded by such changes: the popularity of social networks, the wide coverage of the communications networks and the technical improvements of mobile devices have accelerated the flow of digital information, on exposing users to avalanche of visual messages to the reach of a click. As a society, we require a visual literacy that allows reading and writing with images, in full awareness of the messages circulating. By rethinking the teaching process - learning of photography, an alternative proposal emerges that proditates by the awareness of the present, sharpening the look from the experience of visuality without image capture.
