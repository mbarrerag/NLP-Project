# TIPOLOGIES AND APPROACHES OF URBAN PLANNING AND MANAGEMENT IN MEDELLIN BETWEEN 1947 AND 2020: FROM THE STATE AND FUNCTIONAL PROBLEM TO THE MERCANTILIZATION OF URBAN SPACE

The objective of the chapter is to review the approaches and trajectories of urban planning in Medellín, including this from professional practice to its development in the academy, its current state and its future perspectives in the face of the main challenges of contemporary urbanization at the local and regional level. It is part of a faculty research project, entitled "Urban and territorial planning: themes and reflections for the future"; the main objective of this is to present and propose new reflections regarding the question on urban and territorial planning from the different disciplinary perspectives that compete and interrelation in its study, production and impact from the National University of Colombia. This research has been justified from the need, relevance and urgency to update the debates, theoretical, pedagogical and methodological proposals of what related to the studies and the approximation of the urban and territorial planning problems in and for society.From the institutionalization of urban planning, to the trajectorial and the academic approach that have occurred in accordance with the political-economic, social and technical contexts that preceded them, from the diversity of urban planning instruments produced by the local and metropolitan government
