# DRIS I: development of DRIS indices in oil palm cultivation

ABSTRACT The nutritional diagnosis based on foliar tissue analysis is an efficient instrument to detect nutritional imbalances and assist in the process of fertilizer recommendation. Thus, the purpose was to use this information for the implementation of the Integrated Diagnostic and Recommendation System (DRIS), a method that uses the relationships between nutrients and their respective coefficients of variation, in a population of high productivity, constituting the standards. In this work, the standards and indices DRIS were developed, in the palm oil crop, hybrid IRHO in the central palm region of Colombia. 113 samples were taken, of which 28 were considered as representative of the population with high yields in the production of fresh palm fruit with yields greater than 32.9 t ha-1.
