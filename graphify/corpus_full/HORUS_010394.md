# ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY OR ECONOMIC INDICATORS? EXPLORATORY REVIEW OF THEIR RELATIONS

The purpose of this article is to carry out an exploratory review of the literature to analyze the relationship between environmental sustainability and the economic indicators of an organization. To do this, a systematic search methodology is used in three international databases of high impact, of which it was possible to identify and select 64 scientific works with which the state of the issue is constructed. The analysis of these documents confirms, in a significant way, a tendency for companies that develop environmental management policies and practices to obtain better reputation and greater profits and financial results. The findings of this work are useful for both the productive sector and academia as soon as they reveal; for the first, the positive effects of the implementation of environmental strategies and, for the second, the progress in theoretical development and the consolidation of agendas for future research
