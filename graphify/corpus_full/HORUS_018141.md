# CHAPTER 5 NUTRITIONAL ASSESSMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT

Gestation is a physiological state in which profound morphophysiological adaptations occur and in which nutrition plays a fundamental role. In order to provide updated information, this book has been organized in a chronological sequence that begins with the pre-conceptional stage and preparation for gestation with a preventive approach. Clinical nutrition and gestation articulates the experience of authors in health service institutions and the review of literature. This paper highlights how maternal nutrition and nutrition have a direct relationship with the course of normal pregnancy and with the development of pathologies that affect pregnant women, the fetus, the newborn and subsequently children, adolescents and adults, hence the importance of knowing these topics in the usual clinical practice
