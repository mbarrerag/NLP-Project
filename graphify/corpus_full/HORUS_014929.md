# Profile on the central nervous system of vitexin-O-glucoside and a fraction rich in flavonoids and saponins obtained from the extract of Passiflora quadrangularis L.

The objective of this study was to contribute to the knowledge of activity on the central nervous system of the species Passiflora quadrangularis Passiflora quadrangularis Linn, evaluating the effects of the metabolite vitexin-2”O-glucoside and a fraction rich in the flavonoids and saponins of that species, in view of the interest generated by them after detecting their cardiovascular effects in the murine model. For this purpose, the following treatment groups (n=6) were used as experimental models for mice albinous CD-1 with weight between 25 - 35 g, which were assigned to the following treatment groups (n=6)): (1) vehicle: distilled water 78%, glycerine 10%, propylene glycol 10%, polyprogligligligligly, polysorbate 80 2%/ 0.1 plyrolrol, 0.1 vehicle: distiled water, gligril 78%, gligril, gligril 10%, polyprogligligrol 10%, polyproproproproproproproproproproproproprosup, poly-inlet, propyl insoluble in dichloro in 75 mg/kg, 100 mg/kg, 100 mg/kg This study may therefore suggest that Passiflora quadrangularis L. would have an anxiolytic-like effects possibly due to saponin- and flavonoid-type metabolites, on the other hand, Vitexin-2”O-glucoside in proportional quantities to those found in the extract, does not seem to play a crucial role. Further studies are needed to clearly establish the anxiolytic-like effect of Passiflora quadrangularis Linn and the role of its flavonoid and saponin metabolites in this study
