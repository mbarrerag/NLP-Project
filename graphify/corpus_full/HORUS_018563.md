# EFFECT OF PRONUTRIENTS (BOTANIC MOLECULES) ON INTESTINAL INTEGRITY AND PERFORMANCE IN BROILERS THAT RECEIVE A DIET WITH RAW SOYBEAN

Plant extracts had been investigated as natural alternatives to growth promoters antibiotics (GPA) in order to improve intestinal integrity, optimize feed utilization and consequently, increase productive performance. The purpose of this study was to evaluate the effect of a mixture of plant extracts on hematological parameters, seric cytokins regulation, intestinal morphology, organs weight and productive parameters. The trial was conducted with 480 one-day-old male broilers of the Ross AP 308 divided into 3 groups: Group CON - Basal diet only, Group AB supplemented with bacitracin methylene disalicylate and Halquinol continuously, and Group EP with a mixture powder of plant extracts, respectively. The experiment lasted up to the 35th day of age. 3 % of crude soybean was added to all basal diets. Hematology values as well as the cytokines concentration in seric samples were determinated. Small intestinal morphology parameters, goblet cells count and organs weight, performance parameters and efficiency indexes were analyzed too. A decrease of all seric cytokines in supplemented broilers with a mix of Allium sativum, Origanum vulgare y Thymus vulgaris essential oils was observed. The intestinal morphology parameters increase in performance parameters throughout the growth period were increased in supplemented broilers with plant extracts. Dietary supplementation with natural additives demonstrated to be an alternative to GPA to improve intestinal morphology, organs weight and regulating hematological and immune values in challenged diets with raw soybean
