# Facilitating agency for transformation: A practice-based approach to design research, for change processes

This qualitative research paper explores the emerging field of facilitation in design and its relationship with the ethnographic tools and the participant agency. This paper explores the integration between research and practice. It discusses the desire for an integral transformation and collaborative journey towards a more solid and adapted system to meet the needs of the community. The document incorporates perspectives from different sources and emphasizes the importance of participatory design and facilitation to achieve these objectives. Through the analysis of a case study, where the theory is put into practice, it develops how ethnographic tools, the participant agency and the facilitator's experience are fundamental variables to study the participatory design approach. In addition, the relationship between the results of three workshops held during the Eighth Encounter of Creation and Research (2023), and the theoretical concepts for future planning exercises.
