# Socio-environmental tensions in Black communities. Case study: San Rafael de Negua Community Council and San Francisco de Ichó Community Council

The Commission is addressing, on the basis of a research binding with the territory and the communities settled and organised in the Community Councils of San Rafael de Neguá and San Francisco de Ichó Community Council, belonging to the Community Council of the Rural Association Integral del Atrato – COCOMACIA, an analysis around the socio-environmental tensions, which materialize not only by the access and use of land, but also by the duality of nature rights and biocultural rights. From this research it is possible to identify that these tensions are exacerbated by various variables such as the disconnection marked between state policy and local realities, the presence of illegal actors, the absence of a differential approach in the formulation of public policies, by the ownership and ownership of land, inconsistencies in the application of the regulations governing the activities of the Community Councils, among others. the tenure and ownership of the land, inconsistencies in the application of the regulations that regulates the activities of the Community Councils, together others. This research starts from the voice of the communities, giving total primacy to the traditions and forms/projects of persistence that have found out into tension with the vision of public policy and regulation that historically the governances of the day have granted during their mandates
