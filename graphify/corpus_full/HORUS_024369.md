# Voices in continuum to epistolary rhythm

This degree project aims to investigate yes the image -considered colloquially as a work of art - resulting from the process of artistic creation that took place during the two years of the Master's degree effectively derives from the conceptualization, materialization and presentation of an image of symbolic character according to the historical and aesthetic postulates of the theory of the German philosopher and historian Aby Warburg. According to this theory the work of art is understood as an image of symbolic character and follows certain paradigms adjusted to different aesthetic and historical fields that are repeated as a pattern throughout the centuries. In the specific case of my project, the scope of analysis of the corresponding image was the Pathosformel. This symbolic image must be verified in front of the only verifier of an art work that is the common sensibility, that is the spectator public. For this, the Master's design and mount from a collective exhibition -following the paradigms of museology- to present the projects in the Exploratory Building of the Jorge Eliecer Gaitán Museum in the city of Bogotán. There, there is a multimedia installation composed by a cluster of painted chamizones, a white, and a book.
