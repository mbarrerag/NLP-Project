# School Road Safety in Bogotá: Geoportal and Practical Recommendations for the Educational Community and Local Authorities

According to the report of the World Health Organization (WHO), on youth and road accidents in 2021, young people aged 14 to 28 continue to be the main victims of traffic collisions worldwide, with alarming figures of 1871 deaths in one year. In this scenario, the National Road Safety Plan (PNSV 2022-2031) reflects the recognition of the value of life and the importance of its protection, in line with the 2030 Agenda and the SDG 3.6.1, which seeks to reduce deaths and injuries caused by road accidents by at least 50% by 2030 (National Road Safety Plan 2022-2031, 2022). The implementation of awareness and awareness programs, such as the portal of the National Road Safety Agency, is essential to address all areas of action of the National Health Insurance Agency, ensuring that the standards are met and that the infrastructure and vehicles are used in a safe way, contributing to the reduction of accidents and to general well-being.
