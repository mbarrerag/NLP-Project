# Methodological approach to validate geomechanical and hydraulic parameters in a fractured medium (Línea tunnel case).

The models of fractured media present the limitation that they commonly do not take into account the analysis of fragile deformation or fractures, using homogenization techniques where hydraulic and geomechanical parameters are usually assigned as an equivalent porous and continuous medium. This work presents a methodological approach based on the analysis of aerial photographs, digital elevation models, field data and thin sections, for the identification of directions and geometry of guidelines and discontinuities, in order to produce a 2D model of fractures based on the methodology of discrete fracture networks (DFN). This statistical procedure, is presented by means of a code of its own in Python. Thus, values of hydraulic conductivity (K) were obtained, geomechanical parameters from the measurement of roughness (JRC) and laboratory tests. Consequently, the enclosures of resistance associated to the synthetic prototype were constructed by means of a modeling of fractures in Phase2D.
