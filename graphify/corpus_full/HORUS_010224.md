# FERREOUS AND FLUVIAL COLOMBIAN TRANSPORT: A PROSPECT FOR ELECTROMOVILITY

Transport is one of the key activities for the competitive development of countries. This requires alternative modes for the mobility of passengers and goods with particular characteristics that in their combination lead to more efficient transport processes in costs, time, security conditions among other ways of evaluating the performance of this activity. In Colombia, this potential for using different modes of transport is limited; since in the country the road mode has been the most developed due to the neglect of other alternatives that in the former were relevant to the country, such as transport via national waterways and railways. This is why the mode of road transport is the protagonist for the movement of goods inland, as well as to and from ports for external trade purposes. However, the weaknesses in this way lead the country to losing competitiveness at the international level and costly trade processes affecting the economies of companies and the country; also, the almost logistic dependence in this way forces to cease operations when the roads must be closed by social, meteorological or operational incidents.
