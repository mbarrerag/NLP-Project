# Cooperation network for research on urban and territorial problems

This proposal seeks to consolidate a research network in urban-regional problems based on the experience accumulated in the work of several research groups. These background refer to various publications on networks of cities, middle cities, evolution of networks of cities in historical periods, regional historiography, regional spatial economy, processes of metropolitanization, urban growth, armed conflict, urbanization and territory. Thus, the Network that elaborates this proposal includes a rich mix of senior researchers with associate researchers who have important research experience and are consolidating a scientific career in the four linked headquarters, and also junior researchers who begin to explore the scientific and publishing activity.Thematically the proposal focuses on the urban-regional problems that although they are broad and diverse, by the experience of the team will focus on the analysis of urban systems in four scales: the global scale, which we want to emphasize in the work of the network and the regional networks that are not included in the regional studies.The national scale, which has also been widely studied but mainly at the level of large metropolis, leaving behind the study of the medium and small cities on which we want to emphasize in the regional studies and the regional studies. enrich these analyses with geostatistical indices and thematic cartography (Salas S. and Montoya G. 2023)
