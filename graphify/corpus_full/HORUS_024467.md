# Popayán Religious Music Festival 1964 - 2021

This work investigates the background, origin, realization and evolution of the Festival of Religious Music, which has been carried out since 1964 in the city of Popayán, department of Cauca, Colombia. The city has been the historical scene of very profound political and social changes, from the colony to the present. The various musical manifestations of now, have served to postpone as long as the political, economic and geographical importance of the colonial city in the past. However, through its history musical manifestations have always been present. During 58 years of life of the Festival, it has been periodic the visit to Popayán of professional and amateur musicians, taking advantage of this space having direct impact on the musical culture at local and national levels, carrying out at least twelve concerts each year, coinciding with the celebration of the Holy Week. The aim of this work is to review the history and analyze its influence and music importance, as an event of religious music that has become the cultural heritage of the Nation.
