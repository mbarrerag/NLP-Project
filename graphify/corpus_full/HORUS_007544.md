# Implementation of theoretical teaching of nanomaterial synthesis by combustion, co-precipitation and hydrothermal, Raman-electrochemical spectroscopy characterization and microscopy of atomic-electrochemical force, for courses in Materials Science, Material Characterization, Engineering Materials and other applicants.

Since 2016, the Biomaterials laboratory has been an instrument for carrying out practices related to the synthesis and characterization of materials, applied to the biomedical and engineering fields. This support in strengthening the theoretical concepts offered in the courses of materials science, biomaterials, engineering materials and material characterization that are taught to approximately 120 students every year between undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, has allowed to be updated in the scientific and technological advances that the state of knowledge worldwide and national has demanded. However, the constant search for new topics that can be incorporated in the areas of objective knowledge, make new advanced technologies that have proved to be efficient in the development of new materials applied to the energy, health, infrastructure, environmental and other areas of interest, are designed to be integrated into the offer that the Biomaterials laboratory can offer in the coming years, directly benefiting the professional profile of engineers formed at the National University of Colombia and the the the the testers, both undergraduate, master's and doctoral fields. These new topics of synthesis and characterization of new materials are considered linked to the challenges of the energy transformation of the country, the closing of social gaps and the improvement of infrastructure
