# Analysis of the implementation process of the social competence and school health program in public schools in Bogota

In the 1990s, the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) promoted the initiative of Health Promoting Schools in the Americas with the aim of training young people with the skills and abilities to promote and care for their health and that of the environments in which they operate, from an integral and multidisciplinary perspective. Schools took a leading role in the intersectoral work of promoting social competence and it was quickly necessary to identify the approaches required to incorporate these approaches into the Colombian school environment. In this context, the "Social Competence and School Health" (CSSE, for its initials in Spanish) program emerged in 2000 as a healthy school program, whose spiral development process of design-implementation-evaluationimprovement-adaptation is documented through a qualitative observational method. The first years of implementation (2001-2002) led to presenting CSSE as a primary prevention program for substance use, although that was not the primary objective intended. Between 2003 and 2005, the program became increasingly configured as a health promotion program focused on promoting psychosocial factors of protection and promoting the quality of life of schoolchildren. Towards the end of 2005, three studies were developed to analyze school health activities and associated organizational conditions in district schools in Bogota, establishing the essential elements that facilitated or hindered the development of a healthy school strategy, including the CSSE program. Based on the experience of the first years of implementation and evaluation of the CSSE program, the identification of organizational factors associated with the acceptance of this type of strategy in the city of Bogota, the recognition of the complex nature of health needs of the school population, and the ongoing development of analysis of emerging conceptual and research advances, the CSSE program is consolidated as a motivational strategy that integrates goals of preventing diseases and behavioural problems in children and young people, with goals of human development, achievement of quality of life, and promotion of mental health throughout the entire school cycle
