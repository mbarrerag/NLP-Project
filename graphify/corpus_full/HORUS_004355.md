# AGROECOSYSTEMS OF SPECIAL IBAGUÉ, TOLIMA COFFEE TO PROMOTE PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION AND TERRITORIAL PEACE

The chapter addresses productive inclusion in a comprehensive and comprehensive manner aimed at improving the quality of life of rural inhabitants, which aims to promote, in a participatory manner, policies, plans, programmes and projects with rural communities, while at the same time strengthening public institutions aimed at this end. The chapter presents the main results of an investigation, in which special agroecosystems of coffees in Ibagué (Tolima) were characterized and evaluated in order to promote productive inclusion and territorial peace. This research was carried out as part of the projects on Sustainable soil management in the coffee area of Ibagué (Tolima) to strengthen territorial systems of innovation, implemented by Amucet, the University of Tolima and the National University of Colombia and financed by the Colombian Association of Faculties with Agronomic Engineering and Agronomy Programs (Acofia); and agricultural systems for the productive inclusion of rural dwellers and the construction of territorial peace in Colombia.
