# ANALYSIS OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE DECREE 1782 2014 ON THE REGISTRATION OF BIOLOGICAL MEDICINAL PRODUCTS 2014 - 2021

In a recent study carried out by IFARMA, the consequences of protection to the test data were explored, as established in Decree 2085 of 2002, during the period 2012-2019. The results revealed significant concerns, especially in relation to biological medicines. The analysis highlighted the lack of competition and the marginal participation of Colombian pharmaceutical laboratories in this segment. In addition, an increase in the importance of biological drugs in the total expenditure of the health system was observed, particularly in categories such as monoclonal antibodies. To address these concerns, IFARMA, in collaboration with the Association of Pharmaceutical Industries in Colombia (ASINFAR), signed the Other No. 001-2021. This agreement focuses on studying the impact of biological medicines and their relationship with the application of Decree 1782 of 2014 between 2014 and 2021. The justification lies in the weight of these products in the health system budget. The objective is to generate information that explains the possibilities for the national industry to have a more relevant role in the market of biological medicines. With these purposes, the report includes the analysis of the IFARMA working group's characteristics in the implementation of the National Decree, the five-based health system.
