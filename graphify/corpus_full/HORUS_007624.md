# Synthesis of iron nanomaterials carrying methotrexate (MTX): Photostability and phototoxicity study by generating reactive oxygen species

The exposure of organisms to electromagnetic radiation triggers physical and chemical processes, which can be beneficial or harmful to life. These processes are varied and include simple uptake of radiation as a signal (photosensors), photosynthesis, phototoxic processes in which chemical modifications occur that cause damage to an organism, photodynamic therapy as an application for the treatment of tumors, among others. Photoensibilized reactions are mechanisms where a molecule is oxidized by absorbing radiation through an sensitizer. These processes can generate reactive oxygen species (ROS) highly oxidative, inducing cell damage, but some may also be useful in photodynamic therapy (TFD) against cancer.
