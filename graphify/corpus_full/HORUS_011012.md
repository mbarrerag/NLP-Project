# Evaluation of the modified cassava starch in the quality and sensory acceptance properties of a fermented soy beverage

The soy drink despite having a good protein quality and bioactive components such as isoflavones, has the disadvantage of presenting antinutrient factors. Of all the anti-nutrient factors the trypsin inhibitors have the disadvantage of being thermoresistant proteins, so that thermal treatments are required more intensive than the pasteurisation HTST or UHT to reduce their levels. However, the thermal treatments reduce the protein solubility, which directly affects the physical and reologic characteristics as an increase of synéresis, low viscosity and reduction of texture. To improve these properties, modified yuca starch was used. On the other hand, as it passed secondary processing the soy drink was fermented to improve the bioavailability of nutritional factors, and the reduction of antinutrient levels.
