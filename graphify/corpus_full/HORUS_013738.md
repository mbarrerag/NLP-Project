# RIOS HYDRAULICA

In Colombia, the extraction of materials in rivers is governed by mining and environmental regulations. The difficulty of ensuring the monitoring of this type of exploitation in order to mitigate environmental impacts requires the development of control and warning strategies. In the case of an alluvial channel, solid transport is in balance with the dominant flow rate and geometric variables that characterize the same: width, depth and slope. When one of the parameters is altered, the others are also modified. The present study had as scope to analyze and develop monitoring tools of a natural channel in the context of material extraction considering its geometric variables. The research work was carried out based on the concepts of a balancing channel and a dominant flow that determine its geometric characteristics. It is proposed to use a long-term monitoring tool to establish acceptable thresholds, control and mitigate the environmental impacts of material extraction in a basin considering the dynamic transport processes that happen in the medium. The convergence analysis of the geometric variables in the form of series to the limit presents an interesting monitoring tool to determine acceptable thresholds, control and mitigate the environmental impacts of material extraction in a basin considering the dynamic transport processes that occur in the medium
