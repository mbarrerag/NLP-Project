# CODE OF FAMILY SPECIES OF SOME MUSCIDAE (INSECT: DIPTERA) OF INTEREST FORENSE

Muscidae is a family of dipters with approximately 5,200 species reported in all biogeographical regions of the world; by their necrophagous habits, some species such as: Domestic Musca (Linnaeus,1758) Hydrotaea aenescens (Wiedemann, 1830), Hydrotaea chalcocaster (Wiedemann, 1824) and Synthesiomyia nudiseta (Van Der Wulp, 1883) are of legal medical importance.One fundamental aspect in forensic entomology is the precise identification of species, however, some challenges related to the low availability of taxonomic keys, lack of specialists, additionally in some cases only fragments of the specimen in the crime scene are found, which makes it necessary to use DNA sequences of regions of genes such as Citocromo Oxidasa 1 (COI) have been found as an alternative or complementary tool for taxonomic identification.The aim of this work was to evaluate the usefulness of a short region of the IOC for the identification of some species of forensic interest of the Muscidae, CITC. Specifics ranged from 0.0 to 1.2% in all species, and interspecifics ranged from 6.8 to 13.8%. 23 identifications consistent with the specimen label were detected. An intraspecific 0.1% and interspecific 0.9% Gap Barcode was determined. Genetic distances are within the ranges reported in other studies with these same species. This region of the IOC gene was validated as a tool for the identification of these species
