# ILLEGAL ECONOMY AND REACOORDATION OF ARMED GROUPS IN THE POST FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT DURING THE ADMINISTRATION OF IVAAN DUCHE (2018-2022)

This project of the seedbed on Illegal Economies and the re-accommodation of the armed actors in the framework of the post-agreement agreement during the government of Iván Duque Márquez is developed as part of the work of forming the research seedbed part of the progress made in the framework of the research processes of the Group of Investigation in Security and Defense (Gisde) of the National University of Colombia that advances in the topics of mafia, drug trafficking, organized crime, criminal gangs and State capture. In this it makes a general presentation of the perception of the problem and the solutions proposed to the phenomenon of illicit drugs of the different organized expressions of civil society, following the experience obtained in the National and Regional Forums, ordered by the Round Table of Conversations of Havana on this topic, focusing on the analysis in the period between 2018 and 22. In essence, the research is concerned to make a conceptualization of the phenomena of mafia, drug trafficking, drug cartels and organized crime, since the signing of the Final Peace Agreement and the challenges that have had its implementation in the illicit crops and drugs in a phase of the re-active and dynamic of the armed sectors. These phenomena are linked to the growing rooting and degradation of conflicts, in particular the armed and social conflict in which the interests of the local political and economic elites are mixed with the competing interests and purposes of drug trafficking mafias, paramilitaries, insurgent groups and organized crime gangs in what constitutes the social and armed conflict in post-agreement. The particularity of these transformations is that they no longer focus exclusively on economic aspects, but are part of a higher interest that is political control of the State and territory, its resources, areas of decision-making, administrative bodies in the local, regional and national order, and the moral subjugation of significant population groups, in what could be called dynamics of _x0093_strategic criminality_x0094_, insofar as they constitute a model that is based on a plan of control and permanent territorial domination.
