# Role of the Testimony and Place of Truth: The Case of “Faint Positives” in Colombia

This thesis is inquiring about the relationship between testimony and truth based on the particular case of the extrajudicial executions of the youth of Soacha, known to the public under the euphemism of “fals positive”. In the first instance, a contextualization is made on the establishment of the counter-insurgency war system which operated as a trigger for extrajudicial executions in Colombia, subsequently, from a psychoanalytic approach, elements are developed for the understanding of war and the phenomenon of “fals positive”, finally, to give way to the approach to the function of testimony and the place of truth that is structured by word, thus locating a saying of the subject, which is, properly for psychoanalysis, the saying of the subject of the unconscious. .
