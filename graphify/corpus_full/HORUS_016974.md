# From effective public space to equitable public space: Medellín, strategies to reduce quantitative deficit with territorial equity

Since 1999, the public space in Medellin has been gaining place in the discussions on the development of the city, 21 years later you can see what are the challenges that have been overcome and which persist; the quantitative and qualitative deficit of square meters of public space effective per inhabitant is perhaps one of the most mentioned. However, in this process the question is how equitable have they been?
