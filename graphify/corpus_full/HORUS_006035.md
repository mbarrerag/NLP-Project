# Multipillar Pension Reform in Colombia: An Analysis Based on the Pensional Principles and Latin American Experiences

This work carries out an analysis of the Colombian pension system from its beginnings to the proposal of reform of the National Government to determine what has been the adjustment process and the main challenges presented for the fulfillment of the so-called pension principles: coverage, equity, solidarity, sufficiency and sustainability. The current state of the system based on dual model proposed in Law 100 of 1993 is analyzed to demonstrate that it does not fully comply with the pension principles. The levels of coverage in the country are low, only 30% of the economically active population is listed as pension, and about 20% of the elderly population has a contributory pension. It is inequitable and regressive due to the concentration of the subsidies in the population with high income and the low amounts given to the population in condition of vulnerability by the social assistance programs.
