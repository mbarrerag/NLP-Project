# Identification of the relationships between apparent electrical conductivity and physical properties of the soil.

The characterization and identification of the physical, chemical and biological properties of the soil represent a high demand in time and resources that limit the number of samples that can be representative for this purpose. Measures of the apparent electrical conductivity of the soil (CEa) carried out with electromagnetic induction equipment have allowed to establish relationships that can replace some conventional methods for the determination of some properties of the soil. The spatial variation of the CEa depends on soil factors such as texture, moisture content, porosity and the capacity of cation exchange among others. At the Experimental Center of the National University Sede Palmira (CEUNP) an experiment was established in three soil associations, in order to identify relationships between the moisture content (CH), texture and penetration resistance (RP) with the CEa of the soil. The soils varied in moisture content CH from 17% to 42%, sand content from 26,58% to 84,58%, clay content from 9.42% to 49.02% and limos content from 0% to 34%, while the CEa soil fluctuated between 1 to 115 mS/m.
