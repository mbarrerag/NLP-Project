# CORPORATE RESEARCH AND ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH ORGANIZATIONAL INNOVATION

In modern organizations, corporate entrepreneurship, which is addressed from the perspective of strategic entrepreneurship, emerges as a source of renewal of competitive advantage, through the transformation of management processes that allow for the introduction of organizational innovations, by deploying, through a balance-sheet strategy, the exploration and exploitation skills. This book exposes the relationship between corporate entrepreneurship and organizational innovation, and analyses human management practices oriented towards generating contribution opportunities and the role played by the exploitation and exploration competencies in transforming organizations that seek sustainable competitive advantages in the current turbulent business environment. Transforming the understanding of the human being within the institutions that advance this type of practice is fundamental in addressing this type of entrepreneurship, since it implies the re-signification of the collaborators of the firms, transforming them from simple, easily disposable resources to the central axis of the entrepreneurship processes that are being developed
