# Effect of pelvis bone on the state of residual limb stress in interaction with the socket of a transfemoral amputee.

Three types of numerical models of 15 people with transfemoral amputation were developed that allow to analyze the state of efforts in the residual limb due to interaction with the socket during posture and running cycle in a maximum request condition. A first model that includes only femur bone and a portion of the residual limb that does not include the gluteal zone, a second that only includes femur bone and a portion of the residual limb that includes the gluteal zone and a third model that includes femur bone, pelvis bone and a portion of the residual limb that includes the gluteal zone. Also, the possibility of developing a numerical model of people with multilayer transfemoral amputation was determined. Finally, it was found that the pelvis bone has no impact on the state of effort in the interaction of the socket with the residual limb, but that including a portion of the residual limb in this type of models has an impact on the distribution of the stress in the interaction of the residual limb with the socket, and a multilayer numerical model was developed for people with a realistic transfemoral amputation
