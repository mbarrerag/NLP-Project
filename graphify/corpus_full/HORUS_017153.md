# CORRELATION BETWEEN THE PARASITAR CHARGE WITH HAEMONCUS SP. AND THE FLOOR OF AGLOMED CELLS IN THE ANTIOCH DEPARTMENT

Gastrointestinal parasitism is a negative factor of great impact on small ruminant holdings in the department of Antioquia, whose control is far from being reached due to the resistance of these helmints to antiparasitic drugs. To establish the minimum parasitic load (HGH) with Haemoncus sp. which generates decrease of the package of agglomerated cells (HTO) in small ruminants. This work was carried out in two aprisks located in the municipalities of Guarne and Medellín of the Department of Antioquia. 56 animals, 25 sheep and 31 goats of different races, ages and sex were sampled. Fecal matter was taken directly from the rectum for coprological study according to the technique of McMaster, and blood from the venipuncture vein was included, to perform micro hematocrit (0.5%), Trichostrongylus spimet (7.50%) and the minimum HGHGST.
