# SCHEMES OF FRANCTIONING IN ADVANTAGE RADIOTHERAPY FOR SINGLE CANCER: REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVANCEMENT AT THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CANCEROLOGY, COLOMBIA

Radiation therapy is a fundamental component in the management of breast cancer, particularly in the adjuvant scenario, as its benefits in treatment and in terms of local recurrence, disease-free survival and overall survival are widely demonstrated. Thus, the hypofractionation schemes with radiation therapy are the current standard of adjuvant treatment in patients with early stage breast cancer. For this reason, the Radiotherapeutic Oncology Area Group and the Functional Breast and Soft Tissue Unit of the National Cancer Institute decided to review the evidence on the subject and establish indications of the various hypofracturing schemes at the Institute, both for patients with early stage cancers and for those with locally advanced tumors
