# Expert Consensus on Evidence-Based Recommendations for the Diagnosis, Treatment, and Follow-Up of Fabry Disease in Pediatric Patients

Fabry disease (FD) is a rare X-linked disease characterized by the accumulation of glyco-sphingolipids in lysosomes due to the deficiency in the production of alpha-galactosidase A (α-Gal A) enzyme. Despite its low frequency, this disease has a serious impact on the life expectancy and quality. To make evidence-based recommendations for the diagnosis and treatment of FD in pediatric FD in Colombia and Latin America and clinical decision-making for disease management. Notably, making an early diagnosis ensures a reduction in the impact of this disease on the quality of life of patients and their families
