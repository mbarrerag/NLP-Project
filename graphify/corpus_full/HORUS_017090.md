# DISPUTATION. DIALOGUES BETWEEN SUBSTANTIVE AND PROCESSAL LAW

This paper presents the most outstanding research produced by the students of the Specialization in Procedural Law program in 2020. These researches are valuable not only because of the rigorous analyses, reflections and results that each of them presents, but because they fully reflect the commitment, sacrifice and dedication of each of the authors, who managed to achieve excellent research results during a very difficult year in which we all had to take on enormous challenges at the personal, family and social levels
