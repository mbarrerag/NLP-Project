# Faire ensemble, faire responsable: la recherche et l_x0092_innovation responsable (RRI) aux tiers lieux du Grand Est français / Do together, make responsible: Responsible Research and Innovation (RRI) in the Tiers-lieux of the Great East French.

This project seeks to formulate a process of evaluation of the practices within a great-eastern French innovation team (LF2L belonging to the Université de Lorraine) according to the table proposed by the Responsible Research and Innovation (RRI). For this we propose a participatory research where, through observations on the ground, group workshops and interviews, current research and innovation practices are characterized, reflection on them and alternatives are proposed to address the notion of ethics proposed by the RRI. As a result, we hope to obtain a qualitative rating of the practices that are being developed today within the LF2L and recommendations to advance in consolidating more responsible practices
