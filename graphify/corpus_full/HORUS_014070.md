# Seed: intellectual property-PI, and open science Reindustrialisation and IP: access to technology and freedom of operation-FTO

The National Development Plan 2022-2026 proposes: "A balance will be sought between the needs and interests of the holders and users of the IP." To achieve this implies expanding and complementing the traditional or orthodox looks to intellectual property focused only on protection, this is important. But it is necessary to consider additionally the access to knowledge and technology. --The country has proposed to reindustrialize, and has identified five key sectors. For this purpose intellectual property can have a multiple role. On one hand as incentive to protect results or outputs, on the other hand, as a barrier or obstacle to access inputs, or use technologies for applied and commercial purposes only in sectors such as health, agriculture, defence, ICTs and others.
