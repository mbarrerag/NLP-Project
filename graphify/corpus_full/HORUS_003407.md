# CHAPTER 9 PROPOSALS FOR HEALTH PROMOTION: ACADE-MICA BETTER OF STUDENTS AND DOCUMENTS OF THE POSGRADE

The specialisation in food and nutrition in health promotion fulfils 15 years of continuous work and, as a celebration, presents its publication entitled Health promotion: university reflections. Fifteen years of experience of the Food and Nutrition specialisation in health promotion. This work reflects the legacy built along the years, renews the theoretical discussion on health promotion (PS), stimulates teacher practice by making visible what is taught in the specialization and projects the academic programme towards the future, given that it addresses with great depth several of the issues that today are enormous challenges for the PS. The nine chapters of the book update discussions of importance in the strategic aspects of labour, such as the ageing, the development of the population, the alternatives to the original development of the population
