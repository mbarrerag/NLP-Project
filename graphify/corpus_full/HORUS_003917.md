# ANALYSIS OF EFFECTS GOVERNING KNOWLEDGE ON COMPETITION FOR INNOVATION: A EARLY STUDIES IN SOFTWARE ENTERPRISES

Knowledge is an important resource that can drive innovation in organizations. This study seeks to characterize the relationships that exist between knowledge management and the competences to inno-var. To this end, an explanatory model is developed to evaluate what is the global effect that brings a proper management of the acquisition, exchange, storage, application and creation of knowledge about the development of innovations. A sample of data was constructed from a survey applied to 150 software development companies in the city of Medellín (Colombia) and a model of structural equations was carried out. The findings allow us to affirm that knowledge management influences positively on the development of innovations. Furthermore, knowledge management is explained more strongly by the creation, acquisition and application of knowledge
