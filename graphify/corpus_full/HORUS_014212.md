# Research Group School of Community Justice of the National University of Colombia EJCUN

The School of Community Justice of the National University of Colombia (EJCUN) as a research group carries out research, teaching and extension aimed at strengthening community from the processes of social transformation and of self-management and peaceful conflict. It is composed of professors, graduates, interns and students of Law, Political Sciences and Human Sciences, as well as leaders and social actors. It is located as an academic entity of theoretical, practical and pedagogical production that seeks to generate knowledge from the exchange of diverse knowledge, empowerment, peaceful management and the right to access to justice, this as a response to the need for democratization of justice administration practices and law in Colombia. The EJCUN has carried out a wide-ranging work in various territories and communities at the national level, based on social pedagogy in issues such as reconciliation in equity, citizenship, management of community and state justice operators, social mediation, among others. This exercise has continuously dialogued with many and varied voices of Colombian society, without the latter being simple to study, but are relevant and legitimate actors in and for community justice
