# Characterization of microorganisms associated with the rotting of roots of Avocado Persea americana Mill in nurseries of Valle del Cauca, Colombia.

Colombia is the third country with the largest area harvested from avocado, participating with 6% of the world area and a production of 596.814 tons. This participation in the international market is due mainly to the growth in the area cultivated with Hass avocado, of which more than 20,000 hectares have been established in the last five years. Despite this increase, the production chain is affected by the low availability of seed material with genetic, physiological and phytosanitary quality required for the production conditions of the country. Where one of the main limiting phytosanitary is root rot, which contribute to a significant commercial loss during the phase of establishment of productive gardens. The purpose of this work was to characterize morphological, molecular and pathogenic Nectriaceos fungi associated with the rotting of avocado roots in the nursery region of the Valle del Cauca.
