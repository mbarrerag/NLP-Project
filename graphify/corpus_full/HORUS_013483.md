# Midwives, Births and Contacts: A Polyphonic and Acuerporated Ethnography

Departoras, parturitions and contacts: A polyfonic and acuerporated ethnography, is an investigative work that was generated around stories, plants, tissues, songs, arrullos, travels and friendships. It is an attempt of many, to try to understand the relationship of explicit power that exists currently between academic medicine and traditional midwifery in a country like Colombia, where the “plurietnico” and “multicultural”, despite having an inclusive rhetoric, is a mechanism that conceals new forms of colonization within state policies (Rivera, 2010).
