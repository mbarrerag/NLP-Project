# Prediction of collisions effects in the system Water + NaCl through Machine Learning

The use of traditional models such as the modified model of Debye-Hückel, the model of Pitzer, MSE (Mixed-Solvent Electrolyte) or e-NRTL (Non-Random Two Liquid - Electrolyte) for prediction of the collegial effects of the system Water + NaC is difficult because although they have had good results in terms of predictions, their implementation in statistical and computational form has required different efforts. On the other hand, the application of Machine Learning algorithms for prediction of phase equilibriums in systems with dissolved electrolytes. In this work the implementation of 3 Machine Learning algorithms (Neural Networks, Machine Learning Vector Support Machines and Regression Decision Trees) has been studied for prediction of the decrease in fusion temperature and saturation pressure of the system was assessed with NaCl. The results were compared with the empirical variant of the model of Debye-Hückel.
