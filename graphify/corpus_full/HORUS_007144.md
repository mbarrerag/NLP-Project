# A deep learning approach for 3D reconstruction of indoor scenes

This master's thesis exposes the foundation, experimentation, results and analysis of training and evaluation of methods for the implicit 3D reconstruction, specifically Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), through different approaches for the data set, which refer to the original images and to techniques of augmentation, with the purpose of establishing the impact of the augmentation of images on the performance of NeRF methods and selecting the most viable approaches. The original data sets were manually collected for six scenes categorized in two variants, three for specific objects and three for wide views, where an augmentation process with color and geometric transformations, resulted in 18 final sets generated with the COLMAP software, which calculated the poses of the cameras and key points of the scenes.
