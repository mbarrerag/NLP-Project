# Teaching linearization as closing the gap between the concept of polynomial functions and their contextualization with the real world

The aim of the study is to reduce the gap between theoretical concepts and student reality by teaching the linearization of polynomic functions through the modelling of contextualized situations. A bibliographic review was carried out analyzing research studies on variable thinking and significant learning in mathematical education. Activities are suggested that use real water consumption data to understand billing, while Tracker software is used to analyze parabolic movement and free fall. Students are sought to apply their knowledge of physics and mathematics to model real world phenomena. The work focuses on national and international regulatory frameworks for mathematical education and educational innovation. The goal is to promote the progress of instruction and knowledge of mathematics in Colombia. . The objective of the work is to reduce the gap between theoretical theoretical analysis and the reality of students on education and innovation. The goal is to promote the linear utilization of functional functions through modelling of contextual situations.
