# Taxonomic and molecular identification of Thysanoptera in avocado in Valle del Cauca

The present research was carried out in 15 Municipalities of the department of Valle del Cauca and a municipality of the department of Cauca, the sampling consisted of locating 10 avocado trees per farm and collecting the trips of the four cardinal points of each tree, the total obtained was 210 trips, to which were made morphological and molecular identification with the markers COI and ITS2. For the morphological identification 206 trips were mounted in the balsam of Canada after passing by process of clearance and drying and by means of the dichotomical keys of Mound and Marullo (1996) were identified. In parallel the extraction of DNA with the Kit of Quiagen for tissues and 174 sequences were amplified, of which 142 amplified with the marker ITS2, 135 with the marker COI and 104 amplified the same sequence with the two markers. The size of the PCR products obtained for ITS2 was 406 pairs of bases (ptb) and for COI of 575 (pb). Finally, the species of Frankliniella gardena Mourilton, 1948, Sciella and Scipus were determined. The total of 210 threes were collected, which was less than organically and molecularly identified using COI and ITS2 markers. For morphological identification, 206 threes were moved in Canada balsam after under cleaning and drinking processes, and were identified using Mound and Marullo's (1996) said intratomous keys. DNA was extracted using the Quiagen tissue employed kit, and 174 sequences were amplified, with 142 amplifying with the ITS2 marker, 135 with the COI marker, and 104 amplifying the same segment with both marks.
