# BERSE INTERACTION NETWORK WITH DIFFERENT GROSS SOCIALITY AND USE OF FLORAL RESOURCES IN A FAGMENT OF TROPIC SECO FOREST IN HUILA, COLOMBIA

The pioneering study of the political resource used by bees in the Tropical Dry Forest (bs-T) in Colombia is crucial to generate new knowledge about their ecological interactions and to identify the plants used by bees in that ecosystem. Therefore, it was sought to identify the botanical origin of the polynic resource used by some bees and establish plant interaction networks bee in a fragment of BsT in the Huila department, by collecting with entomological network of specimens from October 2021 to September 2022 in transects of 50m x 2m. 115 individuals of the genera Augochlora, Augochloropsis, Lassioglosum and Friesiomelita with their respective polynic loads were collected; thirteen polynic types distributed in twelve botanical families were identified, being the most abundant Cyperaceae, Euphorbiaceae and Sapindaceae. The genera Rynchospora and Croton, belonging to herbaceous vegetation, were the most abundant polynic types, indicating the availability of herbaceous vegetation in this area.
