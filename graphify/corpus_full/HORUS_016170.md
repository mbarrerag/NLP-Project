# VSMM (Value Stream Macro Mapping) design, extended as a methodology to improve delivery times for a closed manufacturing company with discrete production in the medium-sized distribution transformer manufacturing industry.

Application of Value Stream Macro Mapping (VSMM), in a closed manufacturing company with discrete production. Case applied in a company manufacturing electrical distribution transformers. In this thesis it is intended to apply the Macro Mapping of the Value Chain (VSMM), in a closed manufacturing company with discrete production as a valid tool to improve the processes inside a company manufacturing electrical distribution transformers. The VSMM has its bases in the LEAN methodology, hence the importance of highlighting in the first chapters of this thesis the main tools of that methodology, since they are fundamental basis in the development of this. Relevant information about Value Stream Mapping (VSM), Just Time (J.I.T.), Kaizen, Andon, SMED, the 5’s, Poka-Yoke, Kanban, Hoshin Kanri among others will be supplied. It continues to give important definitions about VSM and highlight the great importance of VSMM as a vital tool that allows identifying waste, possibilities of improvement and in general obtain an X-ray of the productive process inside of a manufacturing plant. The theoretical framework of VMM will be concluded with the applied (VMS) MED’s, Poka Yoke’s, JIT’s, development of suppliers, among others. The reason for the realization of this thesis is because the company case study requires custom-made Lean manufacturing models and value maps (VSM) (Taylor made if you like), since the production system, market behavior, the special components used in its manufacture, the high times for the delivery of raw materials, the high cost of them together with the great impact they have on inventories and the great variability of products offered by the company; they make it difficult to replicate standard/commercial methodologies that show possibilities for optimising production, processes and production costs among others
