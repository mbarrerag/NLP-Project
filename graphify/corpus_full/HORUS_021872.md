# Removal of dyes present in real water from a microbiology laboratory using the CWAO process with an Mn, Cu, and/or Fe catalyst supported by activated charcoal from rim rubber.

The development of new technologies, allows the control of hazardous chemicals present in effluents in various industries or educational premises, whose removal is not feasible by conventional methods. Also, the increase in the number of vehicles each year is greater and this is generating residues such as tire rubber. In this research rim rubber is used as a raw material to produce activated carbon that is used as a support for Fe, Cu, Mn metallic oxides and a mixture of the two best analyzed metals (CuMn), these catalysts are prepared by means of the method of wet impregnation and characterized by XRD, SEM-EDX, TPR-H2, FTIR and texture properties. Solids were evaluated by means of catalytic wet oxidation with air of an effluent contaminated with Gram staining dyes to environmental conditions of reaction: 25 °C, atmospheric pressure. and air flow of 2 mL/min. The experimental results indicated percentages of removal of 98%, elimination of DQO of 33% in just 3 hours of reaction and conversion of total organic carbon (COT) The total reaction of 80% to the reaction (COT) was demonstrated by the highest source of reaction
