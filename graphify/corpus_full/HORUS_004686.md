# The Richeza of Nations in Castilian

On June 5, 2023, it was the three-hundredth anniversary of Adam Smith's birth, an occasion that served to make newspapers and magazines a valid character for Smith. The Richeness of Nations, his best-known book, was published in 1776, 248 years ago. This book is the foundational of political economy, and still influences contemporary economic affairs, and Orthodox economists use their authority to push forward and enforce free trade policies on the basis that all countries benefit from open, duty-free and various impediments
