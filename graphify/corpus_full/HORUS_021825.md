# Nonlinear didactic program of analysis of transversal sections in structural elements to bending and axial force.

Through this master's thesis, an open source didactic program is developed and documented, which analyses the cross section of a structural element under the action of axial force and biaxial flexion, considering the nonlinear behavior of concrete and steel. The MKAPPA developed program was written in a structured way in the high level programming language interpreted by MATLAB and GNU Octave, preserving visible and modifiable each of its routines. Likewise, the program includes routines that allow integrating the pre and post-graphic process performed in Gmsh. The formulation implemented in the program includes the method of fibers applied to cross sections of structural elements (Kaba & Mahin, 1984) and the uniaxial constitutive models of steel and concrete proposed by King et al. (1986), Liang (2009), Mander et al. (1988) and Susantha et al. (2001). In the examples of application ́on seven cross sections were analyzed for different load conditions.
