# Heuristic evaluation process of user experience for the design of cosmetic virtual goods

The trade and use of cosmetic virtual goods (BVC) has increased in video games and virtual worlds thanks to its positive reception by players. However, no greater effort has been made in the academy to translate the attributes present in its interaction into categories of analysis to take into account in a design process. Similarly, neither has it been studied in depth on the methods focused on evaluating this type of goods from the field of the User Experience (UX). From a qualitative approach, this research was proposed to develop a systematic process for the creation of a tool of heuristic evaluation that evaluates the design of the BVCs, from the perspective of the UX, based on the attributes present in its interaction with the players. For the recognition of those attributes, an analytical-descriptive and propositive research was carried out that used the method of systematic review of the literature and the netnographic techniques of the interview, the groups of discussion and the non-participatory observation. As a result 22 attributes present in the BVC, of which 18 were adapted to heuristic and allowed to be evaluated in an existing design process. systematic process for the creation of a heuristic evaluation tool that would evaluate the design of CVG, from the perspective of UX, based on the attribute that are present in their interaction with players.
