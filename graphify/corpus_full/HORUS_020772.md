# MÚ S I C A, M I G R A C I N Y E D U C A C I N BETWEEN EUROPE AND AMERICA: THE MUSIC RACE OF GUTIERRE FER- NANDEZ HIDALGO (C.1547-1622/23) IN SANTAPHÉ, 1570-1630

The musical career of the Spanish composer Gutierre Fernández Hidalgo in Santafé between 1584-86 is examined based on original documents in archives of Bogotá, Sucre and Spain. From the reconstruction of his musical formation, his professional, educational and establishment activity of European polyphonic music is analyzed, from the perspective of his relationship of patronage with royal and ecclesiastical officials, as well as in his interaction with society and musical environments in America
