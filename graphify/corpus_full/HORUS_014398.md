# Prevalence of sesamoid bones and accessories in women and men over 18 years of age in a diagnostic imaging service in Bogotá, D.C.

Sesamoid bones and accessory huesecilles are incidental and asymptomatic findings. However, these can generate painful syndromes and, in many cases, structural complications or degenerative changes. Likewise, they can be fracture points or even simulate them. This study sought to determine the prevalence of sesamoid bones and foot accessories in women and men over 18 years of age in a diagnostic imaging service in Bogotá. A descriptive observational study, of cross-sectional cut, was carried out in a population of women and men equal to or over 18 years of age, which consulted the service of Radiology and Diagnostic Images of the Colombian University Clinic between January and December 2021. The information was obtained directly from the clinical history and diagnostic images of the patients with selection criteria. A statistical analysis was performed using the tools R and RStudio.
