# MEMORIA MEASUREMENT AND ATTENTION WITH THE COGNOSTIC DIAGNOSTIC MODEL

The cognitive diagnostic evaluation allows us to know more depth and specificity the strengths and weaknesses of individuals with respect to their attributes. In addition, it allows to identify mechanisms and skills that underlie the performance in a task that account for the processes carried out during the exercise rather than only focusing on the results obtained. This becomes evidence of content validity which, in turn, allows the interpretations that are made of its results to be more robust. The Research and Evaluation Seed in Psychology and Education, from the line of research of Evaluation and Improvement of Educational Processes, offers workshops to public schools in Bogotá to stimulate psychological processes associated with the learning process. To measure the impact of these workshops have been used tests such as WISC IV, the ENI II or the Beatrices of Raven, tests that have a history of development and a widely researched standardization processes. However, the results released from our children, when compared with the Mexican or Spanish barbars of these tests, consistently place them in a low performance.
