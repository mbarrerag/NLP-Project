# Analysis of technical criteria to consider in the planning of projects involving an investment plan for voltage level 2, in function of the indices of quality of service

An adequate planning of the local distribution system - SDL tends to significantly impact the improvement in the behavior of the continuity and / or quality indices of the conventional electricity service. In Colombia, the Energy and Gas Regulation Commission – CREG through resolution 015 of 2018 whose purpose is to adopt the methodology, tariff formulas and other provisions for the remuneration of the electricity distribution activity in the country, establishes the characteristics that network operators – OR's must meet with respect to the quality of the electricity distribution service in the SDL through which perform the provision of the service; Likewise, this resolution establishes the general standard to be taken into account in the presentation of investment plans for a certain tariff period in which investment projects motivated by the attention of demand that can represent both the installation of new assets and the replacement of existing assets to obtain a greater capacity of the system are highlighted. Taking into account the above, this document is focused on studying the causes of affectation that directly affect the SDL of voltage level 2 and that causes the service quality indices of the distribution systems to fall, with the purpose of analyzing certain technical criteria and transversal aspects to these, that allow an adequate planning of the system, oriented to the optimization and prioritization of the resources that are related during the presentation of an investment plan for a certain tariff period, thus ensuring a reliable service and the profitability of the investments. In this order of ideas, it is intended to carry out a detailed review of the current state of the art in the planning of distribution systems and the Colombian regulatory framework that allows identifying the problems that arise and their relationship with the behavior of the service quality indices, the advantages of implementing automation and a factor analysis, methods and risks that allow the establishment of certain criteria to be considered in the preparation of the investment plan aimed at an adequate planning of the system; then a simulation will be presented based on a case study, evaluating whether the criteria selected for the correction of network problems were adequate and how these directly in the behaviour of the system average interruption duration index (SAIDI) and System Average Interruption Frequency Index (SAIFI); Finally, it will be concluded according to the results obtained about whether the selected criteria directly affect the behavior of the quality of service indicators and whether these should be part of an adequate planning of the system and be taken into account in the elaboration and proposal of the investment plans
