# Configure space from strategies of limiting and making continuous

The topic on which the research is carried out, is born from the inquiry of the spatial configuration from the continuity that can be present in a project. Necessarily when studying how continuity is generated, it is appropriate to understand that there must be a delimitation of the space that reflects how continuity can be prolonged, and that border is the limit of that extension. Adrian Forty in his chapter space points out “The edges become liquid; the space is conceived as fluid. The openings and limits, perforations and surfaces, move the periphery to the center and open the center to the exterior. (...) The space is a simple cosmic continuum, visible from the buildings, besides the idea that the space is the product of the movement.” To finally test the strategies in a formalization of the space, where the elements like the constructed result, reflect the continuous idea and their delimitations determine the architectural space. . s articulate them as well as developed a building. Borders does not fracture the fluid space, so limits create various continuums. Finally, the project tests those strategies in a build space, in which architectural elements and the building states the continuum and its borders, building the space configuration
