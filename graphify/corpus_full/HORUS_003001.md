# The formative evaluation in the area of mathematics, multigrade classroom, primary basic: rural headquarters mouths of beautiful water, San José del Guaviare

The research is a reflection of the Evaluation System of the Educational Institution Agua Bonita, the rural headquarters Boca de Agua Bonita, of San José del Guaviare; it is a question of the design of the training evaluation in the area of mathematics, in multi-grade classrooms with students from the second to fifth grades of Primary Basic. The research was based on the documents that underpin the Constitution of Colombia (1991 Articles 64, 65 and 67), which refer to the importance and duty of offering rural education in the regions of Colombia, considering the Curricular Guidelines, of the Ministry of National Education (MEN, 1998). likewise, it addresses article 3, decree 1290 of 2009, on the institutional system of apprenticeship assessment. The research was based on the qualitative approach, with data collection taken from interviews, from a focused group and ethnographic records of class sessions, to determine the progress of learning. .
