# Support system for active and passive design of sustainable constructions.

This work presents a tool developed with the objective of providing support to the active and passive design of a construction. This tool uses the methodology of thermal resistance modeling and capacitance to estimate the thermal and energy dynamics of a construction. The support consists of providing suggestions to the professional designer mainly on the passive design of the construction. These suggestions are produced by solving a problem of multi-objective optimization based on simulation in which the initial investment cost of the construction materials, comfort and energy consumption is optimized. The optimization method is developed by integrating the algorithm of differential evolution programmed in Python with OpenModelica as the simulation software of the construction. This tool uses the libraries Aixlib and Buildings developed in the framework of the project IEA EBC Annex 60 in the Modelic language. This methodological tool is applied to the case of study established in Section 5.2.1 of the ANSI/ASHRAE 140-2001 standard, the case of test 600. In the optimization method, the orientation, inclusion or exclusion of awnings, the thermal properties of the thermal envelope and the thermal properties of the windows are optimized for the same purpose.19
