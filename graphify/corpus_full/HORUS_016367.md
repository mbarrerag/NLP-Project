# EFFECT OF DIFFERENT DIFFERENT TECHNOLOGIES OF DRYING CAPACITY AND CONTENT OF OLEEP MORINGE LEAF ANTIOXID METABOLITES

The drying of Moringa oleifera leaves is used as a method of conservation; however, during this process changes occur that degrade several of the bioactive components responsible for nutraceutical activity; for this reason, the evaluation of different technologies and drying conditions are of great importance to minimize the loss of these components and the deterioration of quality during this stage
