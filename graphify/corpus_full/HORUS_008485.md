# Updating of the map of CENTRAL OLEO CORREDOR MOVEMENTS (OCENSE)

The analysis of susceptibility by mass movements is an indispensable input for the assessment of the threat by external forces for the management of integrity in hydrocarbon transport systems.The susceptibility by mass movements at regional scale exhibits causality relations with multiple variables related to the origin, state and condition of the superficial geological materials, with the geomorphology and morphometry of the terrain, as well as the anthropic actions.The main problems encountered in preparing the variables related to the causal factors of instability are the lack of homogeneity and continuity of the information due to diversity of criteria and methodologies of acquisition in the case of geology and geomorphology, as well as the updating of the information in the case of the uses and coverings of the soils.In addition, there are limitations with the spatial resolution of the morphometric information.On the other hand, there is a great difficulty to model the mass pipeline of Ocensa as well as the functionality of both the geology, geomorphology, the uses and the soil coverings, together with continuous variables, as those derived from the digital elevation model.The displacement of the mass of the unit, Ocensa is considered to be a factor of the functional
