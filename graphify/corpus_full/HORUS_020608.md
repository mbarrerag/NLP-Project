# The language of life: the young Marx in defense of the experience of the common human being

This monograph presents a reading in which it is evident that the young Marx seeks to express in his theoretical thought that which the workers already recognize as certain from the way in which they inhabit the world. For this it will be shown how this is the result of a relationship that Marx establishes between human beings and nature, in a way in which it will be determined that the way in which they live in the world will be a crucial element in order to truly apprehend reality. Likewise, the texts of the young Marx will be interpreted as foundational for his scientific theory of history, in a way in which it will be possible to recognize how they establish the necessity of apprehending what the common human being knows from their real existence. Which will be shown as part of a general project by which the young Marx intends to make a critique of the intellectuals and technicians who embody a way of thinking from which it follows that their work is to educate the workers about what they should think.
