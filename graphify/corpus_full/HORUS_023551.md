# Multi-criteria evaluation for the selection of decentralized alternatives for the treatment of water and basic sanitation in rural Colombian areas.

With the intention of facilitating decision-making in the implementation of techniques and technologies for the treatment of water and basic sanitation in a rural area in Colombia, a joint work was carried out with the UNDP-UNAL team to execute 4 multicriteria algorithms (TOPSIS, ELECTRE, PROMETHEE and AHP) based on the criteria of 15 experts from the basic sanitation sector, concluding with a methodological tool that was applied in the rural area of the Uribe municipality, department of Meta in Colombia. Finding that using four different methodologies allows post-processing the results and offers to reduce uncertainty in the selection of techniques and differential alternatives that for the case of the study were selected a conventional domestic waste treatment plant, multifamily solid waste management systems and multifamily artificial wetlands. Finally, the perception of the importance of the criteria evaluated is highlighted because the experts consider that, in economic terms, cultural socio, environmental and technical projects should be equitable in their feasibility analysis and planning phases
