# Gene variants in the Human Leukocyte Antigen (HLA) and alpha TNF system in chronic and aggressive periodontitis phenotypes: a systematic review.

Systematic review of the literature where the results of the search for gene variants of the Human Leukocyte Antigen System and TNF-α associated with chronic and aggressive periodontitis phenotypes and their possible ligation imbalance are reported. Periodontitis is an infectious disease that affects the supporting tissues of the tooth (bone and gum), within its most common presentations are chronic periodontitis and aggressive periodontitis. TNF-α is a cytokine involved in the development and progression of both chronic and aggressive periodontal disease; the TNF-α gene is found within the HLA III genes on chromosome 6, these genes are inherited in block along with HLA I and HLA II (unbalance of ligation). Due to its role in the development of periodontal disease, variants of TNF-α, HLA I and II may become indicative of susceptibility to suffer periodontal disease
