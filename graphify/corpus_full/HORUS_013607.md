# PERCEPTIONS AND NARRATIATIVES OF CAMPESIGNAL AND AFRODESCENDENT WOMEN IN A TERRITORIAL PLANNING CONTEXT TO THE CONSERVATION.

This study reflects on the differences and convergences between the perceptions and narratives of women leaders, peasant organizations and community councils of black, mad-lying communities in the northern highlands of the department of Antioquia, around the processes of nature conservation in their territory. In this context we develop the case study on perceptions about the conservation planning processes, of women belonging to peasant organizations and community councils of black communities located in the North Highlands of Antioquia. And we ask ourselves what visions do the leaders express in the face of the dominant discourses of planning-planning-conservation or production-deforestation-economic growth? These opposing narratives develop in our multiscale context: Colombia, Antioquia and the North Highlands?
