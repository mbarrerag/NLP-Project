# Impact of gold mining on social accountability: Analysis from behavioral economics

Although the extraction of mineral resources is necessary for the economy and the progress of the societies, positive and negative effects have been observed around this activity, both at national and local level. These effects lead to conflicts that can be harmful to human well-being. Based on the literature of the curse of the natural resources at local level, it is also possible to analyze the social capacity and social capital, and with the tools of the economics of the behavior and the impact assessment, in this thesis is carried out an investigation that aims to determine the impacts of the gold mining on the local communities.
