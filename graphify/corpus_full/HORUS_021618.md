# Model of sustainability analysis in specialized dairy.

This research, proposes a model of comprehensive indicator, to measure the sustainability of production systems in specialized dairy, and contrasts with the application of multicriteria decision methods, compare and combine multicriteria decision methods to evaluate the robustness of the measurement. The study was carried out with information from 18 herbs, dedicated to the breeding, raising and production of milk; the sheds were selected for convenience and are located in the municipalities of San Pedro de los Milagros and La Unión; Antioch. The information related to financial, technical, social and environmental dimensions, was obtained through primary information collected in a semistructured interview. The information was determined by reviewing the reproductive record program that handles each herd; and through direct field observation, there are the results of the integral indicator model and the multicriteria decision methods. The results were used by the analysis of the magnitude and subcriteria (7 technical, 6 social and 10 environmental).
