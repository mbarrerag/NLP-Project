# Criteria, resources and strategies for the elaboration of the interpretation of the symphonic repertoire. Ninth Symphony – Dimitri Shostakovich

This research focuses on the analysis of the first movement of the Ninth Symphony of Dimitri Shostakovich, starting from the need to offer criteria, resources and strategies to interpret the symphonic repertoire rightly and propose a tool for the study and direction of this. The research developed in three phases, these were the description of the historical and socio-political context, the formal analysis of the work and the approach of suggestions related to the gesticular resources from the management technique, until achieving a rigorous study of the repertoire, the analysis of contexts and the specific training to contribute significantly to the criteria and performance of the conductors. .
