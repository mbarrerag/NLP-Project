# Formal verification of the high availability quality attribute for software systems based on microservices architectures.

The decisions made by software architects can have serious repercussions if they are made incorrectly. Therefore, it is essential to base these decisions on concrete evidence rather than relying solely on heuristic experiences to determine whether the architectural design decisions were correct. This approach can help avoid refactoring or reengineering costs in the future. This work proposes a general formal verification model for software architectures and presents a case study of a microservice architecture to ensure the quality attribute of availability. Throughout this thesis will be exposed the different steps necessary to carry out formal verification and will explain how they should be applied to guide software architects in making decisions based on formal evidence, using automatic verification tools
