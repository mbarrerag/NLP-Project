# Modeling and simulation of mass, momentum and energy transport in a single spindle plastic extruder

With the aim of studying and improving the prediction of models applied to the process of extrusion single spindle, the project began with a study of the state of the art about models used to represent the process of extrusion in extruders single spindle, highlighting two main approaches: the phenomenological base models and models based on soft computing techniques, such as artificial neural networks (ANN), diffuse logic, genetic algorithms and expert systems. In this project two complementary methodologies were developed and validated: a phenomenological base Semiphysical model, and another one based on artificial neural networks (ANN). A phenomenological model was developed that contemplates the unidimensional dynamic balances of mass, momentum and energy. The model showed high precision in the prediction of the temperature of the cast and the prediction of the casting profile, as well as some slight deviations in the intermediate profiles without losing precision at the output. Following this, a model based on ANN was developed to capture the highly non-linear nature of the extrusion process, focusing on the dynamic prediction of the pressure profile in different locations of the construction. -based models and models based on soft competitiveness technology techniques, such as artificial neural networks (ANNs), fuzzy logic, generic algorithms, and expert systems.
