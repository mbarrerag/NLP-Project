# Chains, procedures of relationship between programmed parts in the whole, from the route as sequential instrument of the collective in the architectural project. (project case: block A, building 303)

The analysis of the route and the way in which it is experienced, constitute fundamental aspects in the research of the city. From the avant-gardes of the early twentieth century —dadaist, futuristic, surrealist, and subsequently situationist, —the behavior of the subject within a specific context has been examined, establishing the wandering as an act of absolute freedom in contrast with the established order. Architecture, has not remained alien to the positions of these plastic currents. Concepts like the promenade architectale of Le Corbusier, the flâneur of Walter Benjamin, the errar of Rogelio Salmona, and the architectural performance of Rem Koolhaas, stand out as particularly suggestive approaches.
