# ♪ I'm not gonna be a fool ♪

Disorders neuromotores of the speech: disartria flácida en miasthenia gravis is an academic material, result of the work developed in recent years from the research group Voice, Speak and Deglución, which is part of the National University of Colombia. The data with which the different chapters were written were taken from the speech of different people diagnosed with myasthenia gravis (mg); most of them are members of the Colombian Association of Miasthenia Gravis, to whom we dedicate this work. The contents are directed to phonoaudiologists graduates and in training, as well as to other professionals with interest to deepen the disorders of speech secondary to mg. The book is organized in six chapters. The first is an introductory chapter of Colombian Spanish, as the general recommendations presented here, provides readers with a genuine overview, since the majority of the data existing in the scientific literature comes from speakers of other languages, for example, the English. life of people with mg and their families
