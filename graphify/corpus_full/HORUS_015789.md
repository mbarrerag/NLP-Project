# COMPARISON OF CONTROLLED LIBERATION OF IBUPROPHENE IN SCAFFOLD POLYMERICS BECTROSPINNING AND SOLUTION BLOW SPINNING.

Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs such as ibuprofen (IBU), are usually administered orally to treat signs and symptoms of joint diseases. However, despite their therapeutic efficacy, their side effects are known, especially in long-term treatments such as those required in patients with osteoarthritis: increased risk of myocardial infarction, stroke, renal failure and gastrointestinal bleeding, among others. The research carried out has been directed towards devices loaded with drugs that coadyugate the treatment of these diseases and that can be implanted in the area to be treated to increase the effects at local level. In this study, to evaluate the influence of manufacturing technique on the release of delpharmaceutical, they were loaded with IBU dos scafold polycaprolactone (PCL) produced by electrospinning and solution blow spinning. The scaffold obtained were characterized and determined the kinetics of the IBU in a saline solution buffer at 37°C.
