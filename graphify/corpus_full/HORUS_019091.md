# LAMP as an alternative diagnostic method for SARS-CoV-2 virus detection

the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus, which causes the COVID-19 disease, has become a global public health problem that requires the implementation of rapid and sensitive diagnostic tests. to evaluate and compare the limit of detection of the LAMP method with respect to the standard method and to apply the RT-LAMP method for the diagnosis of SARS-CoV-2 in clinical samples of Colombian patients. a descriptive and cross-sectional study was carried out, analyzing a total of 25 samples of nasopharyngeal hysopate including negative and positive samples for SARS-CoV-2, through the RT-LAMP method compared to the standard method RT-qPCR. the LAMP method detected ~18 copies of the N gene, in 30 min, demonstrating a limit of detection similar to that obtained with the standard method, in a shorter time and a 100% agreement in RT-LAMP with the results. RT-LAMP is a sensitive, specific and fast method that can be used for the diagnosis of the COVID-19 disease
