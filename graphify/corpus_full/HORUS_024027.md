# Effect of corporate governance on the corporate performance of listed companies

This paper develops a qualitative analysis of the role of corporate governance in firms and presents the results of a brief quantitative analysis of the possible effects of the implementation of corporate governance practices on corporate performance. The analysis is carried out for a group of firms listed as issuers of securities from the Colombian Stock Exchange between 2015 and 2018. A series of reflections aimed at challenges in this area is left to consideration, which can promote research from the field of the economy with respect to the effects of the implementation of corporate governance practices in companies, the market and business reality
