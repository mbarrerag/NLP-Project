# Optimization options for ruminal development and growth in Gyrolando calves

The replacement veals are the fundamental pillar within dairy livestock, since of them the future productivity of the herd will depend. The inadequate nutritional management in the breeding phase, results in poor ruminal development, high morbidity and mortality and increase of production costs, therefore, the feeding of the calves is key to optimal physiological development, for which supplements with high nutritional content are required; however, the high cost of raw materials for its elaboration increases the costs of breeding, therefore it is necessary to evaluate alternatives in the feeding in this growth phase. The objective of this study was to evaluate the productive and economic efficiency of different feeding options to optimize the ruminal development and the daily weight gain in calves Gyrolando F1 treatments. The study was carried out in the correlation of Santa Rosa de Tapias, municipality of Guacarí, Valle del Cauca. The study was more than 19 Gyrolando F1 calves with ages between 14 and 22 days, distributed in three treatments: treatment 1 (T1) (control group), consisted of the supply of 4L of milk and divided at the day.
