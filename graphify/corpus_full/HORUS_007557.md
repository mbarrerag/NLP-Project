# Proteomic characterization of in vitro communication between umbilical cord and choriocarcinoma mesenchymal stem cells

The extracellular vesicles (VEs) derived from mesenchymal stem cells of the umbilical cord (CMMCU) have been tested for cancer treatment, however, antitumor activity is variable and changes produced by the interaction of these vesicles in choriocarcinoma (CC) are unknown. In this study, it is proposed to evaluate the in vitro effects of CC stimulation with VE-CMMCU from a proteomic perspective. To this end, SC spheroids will be formed in a bovine serum free environment (SFB) and stimulated with Exosomes (EXOs), microvesicles (MVs) and apoptotic bodies (CAs) derived from CMMCU grown in 3D. Subsequently, the effects produced on the migration capacity, invasion, proliferation and angiogenesis of the CC in combination with the protein response emitted through the extracellular ves derived from CC (VEs-CC) will be evaluated. The proteomic profiles data of entry, sensitivity to VS-CMMCUCU and proteomic response of the CMM will be statistically revealed
