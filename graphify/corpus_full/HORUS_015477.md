# MONITORY OF GEOMETRIC HYDRAULIC VARIABLES OF A CAUCE AS A MATERIAL EXTRACTION CONTROL TOOL

The present study had as scope to analyze and develop tools for monitoring a natural channel in the context of material extraction considering its geometric variables. The research work was carried out based on the concepts of channel in equilibrium and a dominant flow that determine its geometric characteristics. It is proposed to use a method of analysis of series to limit to establish acceptable thresholds, control and mitigate the environmental impacts of material extraction in a basin considering the dynamic transport processes that happen in the environment
