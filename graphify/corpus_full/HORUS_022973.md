# Implementation and evaluation of an interactive eBOOK for the teaching-learning of data compression

In the study of abstract subjects, as is the case of data compression, students find difficulty in learning because of the absence of interactive digital educational resources (Echeveste and Martínez, 2016). Interactive eBooks, through the integration of multimedia elements, allow new learning practices of greater interaction. This work is part of a research whose objective is the analysis, design, development, implementation and evaluation of an interactive eBook for the teaching-learning of data compression. In this chapter, the implementation of the interactive eBook on the target audience and its subsequent evaluation is presented, for which the descriptive quantitative research methodology applied to students of the course of Information theory and communication systems of the National University of Colombia is carried out.
