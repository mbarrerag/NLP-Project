# Effect of ultrasound on hydration and nutritional quality in cooking of common frijole (Phaseolus vulgaris).

The common frigol (Phaseolus vulgaris) is one of the most important food crops worldwide and its production extends in different regions of Latin America, Africa, Europe and East Asia, due to its ease of planting in various environments. In addition, the frigol is an essential component in the human diet due to its high nutritional content and its easy access to the rural and urban population of low resources. However, the processes of preparation of this food can be extensive, achieving affect the nutritional quality of this. The implementation of the ultrasound as an optimization tool in the process of hydration of two varieties of frigol (BIO101 and NimaCalima), its effect on the cooking process and nutritional quality. To this, the behaviors of conventional hydration (HC) and conventional hydration with pretreatment with ultrasound (HCPU) were analyzed by the most of the response surface methodology (MSR).
