# Characterization of cloned foal diseases in Colombia, and the impact of placement, their pathologies on the success and survival of cloned foals from two different cell lines.

The clonological foals that have been born in the different cloning programs in the United States, Argentina and recently in Colombia have had problems in the peripartum within which they are found: early embryonal deaths, abortions, stillbirths and birth of weak founts; showing better results with cloned founts product of bone marrow cells compared to those of more differentiated tissues such as fibroblasts.
