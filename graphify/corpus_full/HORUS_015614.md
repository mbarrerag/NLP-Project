# Evaluation of a treatment system, at the laboratory level, to reduce the concentration of organic load, bacteria and/or parasites using synthetic residual water, with a view to its applicability in crop irrigation in the western savannah of Bogotá - Colombia.

This work contains information related to the hydrological and water quality characterization of the Hydraulic Environmental Management and Flood Control System La Ramada, together with the experimental evaluation of a biological reactor, on a pilot scale, for the treatment of wastewater oriented to the removal of DQO, parasites and bacteria, for concentrations similar to those found in the residual water of the area under study. To achieve the proposed objectives, the study has been developed in three stages. In the first, a hydrological and quality characterization of the water of the area under study was advanced; in the second stage, the selection and definition of the type of biological treatment of the highest application and adaptability to the area under study was carried out and, finally, in stage 3, the design, construction, start-up, monitoring and evaluation of the reactor at laboratory scale monitoring the removal of DQO, parasites and coliform bacteria.
