# OPTIC FUNDAMENTS.

This book presents the classical optics subjects of the course Fundamentals of Optics that I have taught for 10 years as a professor of the Department of Physics of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá. I initially gave the course as an elective subject1 and from the second semester of 2009, until the second semester of 2017, I gave it in uninterrupted form as an optional course of the Physics career2. The course program I proposed taking into account my experience as an optical researcher applied from my PhD in Optics Science (1998-2001, Center for Research in Optics, Mexico). The program is formed by 4 chapters: geometric optics, polarization, interference and diffraction, distributed in 30 units (and 6 appendices), which are given in 28 2-hour classes. The course is given in masterly form with demonstrative practices. The aim of this book is to serve as a guide for students of the course Fundamentals of Optations and Opttoria (Edi, Optics Opttoria) of Opttoria.
