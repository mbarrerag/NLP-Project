# GENETIC DIVERSITY IN THE BOVINA CRILOLA COLOMBIANA CHINO SANTANDEREAN GERMOPLASME BANK USING GENOMIC INFORMATION

Domestic livestock was brought to America by Christopher Columbus in 14.2.93, which were parked for 32 years on the island of Spain, where they were multiplied on a large scale until their departure was authorized with the expeditions of the conquerors. In their arrival to the Colombian territory, the number of the different agroclimatic regions was estimated. A large variety of locally adapted animal species developed were developed. Among them, the CCS breed of the Colombian Creole of China Santandereano (CHS) presents a process of adaptation of more than 500 years to the Colombian mountain trotrop; however, despite its benefits and adaptation, it has been replaced by specialized alien races (SGNA), it is estimated that there are less than 400 pure CSHS specimens in the country, and also it is presumed that individuals present high kinship and consanguinity. The objective of this work was to estimate the genomic diversity and population structure of the Chinese race conservation group Santandereano belonging to the SBGNAAA* in the custody of AGROSAAVIA.
