# Evaluation of robustness to atypical observations of generalized linear models scale-image via total variation.

The application of any statistical model based on a set of study data not only implies the approach and subsequent estimation and interpretation of the parameters of the study, but also requires a thorough process of validation of assumptions and analysis of influence. The latter is important for the identification of sample units that can significantly affect the estimation of parameters or that may manifest some particular characteristic within the population under study. The identification of high-leverage observations, outlet observations and influential observations has been studied in depth within the classical linear models. However, the literature in this subject is scarce within the models with functional data. Thus, the aim of this work is to evaluate the level of robustness to atypical observations of generalized linear models with scalar response and whose covariables can be scalar and images, in which the parameters are estimated via total variation, through a simulation study. Additionally, methods are proposed for the identification of possible influential observations
