# IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE FOR DIAGNOSTIC IN ORTHODONCIA

The aim of this study is to introduce to the audience a methodology of IA as artificial neural learning networks (Artificial neural network) and machine learning (computational learning) in useful areas in Orthodontic diagnosis as cephalometric data and model analysis. Show results of different multidisciplinary research work carried out, related to the use of Artificial Intelligence and computational learning in Orthodontic. The methods of data mining and computational learning allow to extract information from patterns, predictive and descriptive models, automatically induced from large amounts of data (big data). The identification of morphological relationships of the components of the craniofacial complex and the identification of patterns of morphological development of craneofacial, are recurrent research questions in orthodontics, which have been traditionally addressed with statistical methods one or multivariate through linear regressions. However, taking into account the complex nature of the data and the possibility of establishing morphological patterns from a large number of cases; the methods of data and the analysis are used as a basis of the analysis and the analysis of the based on segmentation, characterisation and assisted measurement techniques in digital dentistry.
