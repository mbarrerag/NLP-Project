# RESISTANCE AND MOBILE GENETIC ELEMENTS IN SILMONELLA TYPHIMURIUM ISLLINGS OF COLOMBIAN CLINICAL ORIGIN.

In the world, Salmonella is the cause of ETA and EDA1. The increase in its resistance represents a problem of world interest.2 The emergence and dispersion of S. Typhimurium resistant3, can be explained by the acquisition and mobilization within or between DNA molecules of mobile genetic elements. 30 clinical isolates of S. Typhimurium, 22 of hemoculture and 8 of non-invasive sources were analyzed. The region surrounding 9 types of class 1 (IntI1) integrations previously identified was analyzed by GGS. The phylogenetic relationship was established with analysis of MLST and SNP. The isolates analyzed correspond to the ST19 sequence. Several types of class 1 integrons were obtained. Four of them are associated mainly with hemocultive and correspond to variants of SGI1 of S. Typhimurium DT104, which suggest a mechanism of clon dispersion.
