# Development of a drink based on tropical fruits and vegetables and inclusion of a capuchin extract (Tropaeolum majus)

In recent years, we have seen an increasing trend in the study of bioactive compounds from a food matrix that have the functionality to treat and/or prevent certain diseases when consumed by people (Corbo et al., 2014).. Thanks to the above, we seek, from the technology in food, to add these compounds to certain foods in order not only to provide the nutritional value of the food itself, but also to provide additional well-being, such as preventing non-communicable chronic diseases (Pathak, 2014; Zeng et al., 2015)..
