# Effect of Fusarium oxysporum fungal mycelary extract on the production of phenolic compounds, cardiotonic glycosides and terpenoids in plant cell suspension cultures of Thevetia peruviana

T. peruviana is a perennial leaf tree belonging to the family Apocynaceae, traditionally recognized for its capacity to produce compounds such as flavonoids and cardiotonic glycosides with very valuable biological activities (anticancers, antivirals, antimicrobials among others), however, the low concentration in which these are synthesized generates great difficulty for its use, this is due to the need of certain stress conditions that activate the secondary metabolism responsible for its biosynthesis. Strategies like the cultivation of plant cells in vitro in association with the implementation of substances simulating a state of generalized stress, such as elicitors and phytohormones, allow to have continuously a natural quality resource for the production of the metabolites of interest at industrial and commercial scale Although there are various types of abiotic constituents for the production of metabolites in plants, the currently biotic elicitors based on microorganisms, they have aroused interest due to their efficient ability to imitate a response of disease in plant cells that activates the expression of various genes involved in the biosynthesis of in the in vitro metabolites
