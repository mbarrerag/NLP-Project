# A MODEL FOR THE ESTIMATION OF THE FRUIT OF COFFEE (L ARAB COFFEE) BY ARTIFICIAL VISSION TECHNICALS

The estimation of coffee production in a lot, which was earlier simpler, today, due to factors consistent with climate change, is presented as a considerable challenge. This is because such production is the sum of the harvest of all the trees of the lot, which implies the need for a large amount of data due to the inherent variability of the individuals within a lot, reflected in the presence of trees in different phenological states. Therefore, the precise estimation of the production of a tree requires the estimation of the individual weights of its fruits. In this study, an automated system of extraction of characteristics is presented that only requires an image of 36 fruits selected from a tree and their respective weights to define a regression model able to calculate the weight of the coffee fruit. An image of extraction of characteristics in Python language that obtains the projected area and the characteristic color (chrome a) of the coffee fruits, and uses the actual weights to construct an error model that estimates its weight.
