# Glass ceiling in the academy and accounting profession in Colombia: an analysis of perceptions

One of the most interesting topics in investigating gender-sensitive inequalities is the differences in access to opportunities for advancement in professions for women, due to the existence of obstacles that seem invisible, and therefore traditionally known as _x0093_crystal ceiling_x0094_. This occurs in many disciplinary fields, but this research takes as context the work of women in the academy and accounting profession to answer the question: What are the perceived situations as obstacles (glass ceiling) or as facilitators of women's progress towards managerial positions in academia and accounting profession in Colombia? To answer this question, it is proposed to apply a qualitative interpretative methodology based on interviews with women accountants working in academia or in the profession, with which their experiences and perceptions about their paths, obstacles and possibilities of progress in their organizations can be learned. Research will be carried out in organizations operating in Bogotá. The results of the analysis are expected to contribute to the design of organizational strategies that promote diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) explicitly, beyond the appearances or "gender-wawak"
