# Design of a hybrid laboratory for the study of measurement technologies present in industry 4.0 applicable in Colombia

The industry 4.0 has allowed a global transformation of the different production chains and impacted various social sectors. For its part the electrical sector is not alien to the implementation of the industry 4.0, since much of the current production processes make use of electric energy for its operation. For this reason the use of tools of industry 4.0 would allow to make measurements in real time to carry out control and monitoring of the electrical system in a decentralised way, obtaining indicators of production and facilitating the implementation of strategies that seek to improve the way in which energy is consumed within companies, having a direct impact on the production. However, one of the great challenges generated for the adoption of the industry 4.0, is the lack of skilled personnel that have the necessary skills for the implementation of the various technologies that bring about its implementation.
