# Complementary services in the energy transition

The supply of electricity has been characterized by the use of large-scale generation, distribution networks with one-way flows and passive demand. However, the expansion of the industrial sector, population growth of populations and technological progress of recent decades has strengthened the energy transition, which can be defined as the shift from a fossil-based economy, large-scale generation and centralized operation, to an energy economy based on low carbon energy sources, distributed generation and decentralized operation. In this context, this book emerges as a tool for decision-making aimed at implementing sustainable energy solutions that ensure a continuous, reliable and secure supply of electricity in environments of massive energy resource inclusion distributed. To this end, solutions are proposed that include three basic complementary services for the control of the main technical variables of the power system, which would allow for increased operational flexibility in the context of the energy transition. -- Contraclusive
