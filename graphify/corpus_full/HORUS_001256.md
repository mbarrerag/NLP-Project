# Plastic vacuum modeling. Duality mass - vacuum in public buildings by Germán Samper (1958 – 1968)

The research here described decrypts a fragment of the intrinsic conditions in the constitutive duality of the notion of mass and void present in the architecture, by contrasting the theoretical postulates and the operations revealed on the work of Germán Samper. The analysis categorizes the processes addressed in public equipments and establishes an emphasis on the spatial constitution of the Concert Hall inside the Luis Ángel Arango Library, located in Bogotá and developed during the period from 1960 to 1965.
