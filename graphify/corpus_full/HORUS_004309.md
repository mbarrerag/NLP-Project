# Factors affecting the adoption of teleworking by intermediate levels of a public university in its administrative areas

This study analyzed the factors that influence the adoption of teleworking by middle management at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, using a quantitative approach and a descriptive correlational design, the attitudes and perceptions of 110 participants were evaluated through an adapted questionnaire by Silva et al. (2019).The results showed a strong intention to adopt teleworking, influenced significantly by the Attitude towards teleworking (= 0.722, p < 0.01) and the Perceived Use Facility (= 0.632, p < 0.01).The only negative correlation was the work experience with the Perceived Use Facility (= -0.194, p < 0.05), in the same way, differences were found in the perceptions between genders, with men showing greater variability in their responses and women presenting a more uniform distribution. Likewise, employees who see teleworking as advantageous for productivity and the employment, accessible for all work and personal life. 0.01). The only negative correlation was work experience with perceived age of use (= -0.194, p < 0.05). Futuremore, differences were found in perceptions between genders, with men showing greater variation in their responses and women showing a more uniform distribution. Additionally, employees who see telework as advantagen for productivity and the balance between work and personal life tend to adopt it with greater ease. This suggestion is that motivations may differ depending on personal characteristics and specific needs.
