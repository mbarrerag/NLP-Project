# COMPARISON OF THE MORPHOLOGICAL SELECTION CRITERIA OF OOCTIC CUMULOUS COMPLETS AND THEIR EFFECT IN VITRO PRODUCTION OF BOVINOUS EMPIRES

The selection of the Oocyte Cumulus Complexes (OCCs) is crucial in the process of in vitro embryo production and is based on non-invasive methods such as morphological evaluation. However, some studies have suggested that CCOs with slight signs of atresia have the capacity to generate embryos. To evaluate the effect of the morphology of the CCOs with signs of early and no signs of atresia on the rates of embryo development in vitro. The CCOs were obtained from females worked, divided into two groups: Type I (TI): Homogenous Ooplasm and = 4 layers of compact CCO and Type II (TII): Granular Ooplasm and = 4 layers of slightly expanded CCO. The CCOs were matured by 24 h in medium TCM199 supplemented, and the results of the embryos were performed in vitro for a period of 18 h.
