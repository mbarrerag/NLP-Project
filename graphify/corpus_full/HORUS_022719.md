# Valuation of occupational performance: integrative factor in the rating of loss of work and occupational capacity

Abstract Introduction: Occupational performance (DO) as an integrative concept allows the holistic assessment of the person and identifies the potential for return to productive activity through the occupational concept, as an input for the assessment of loss of work and occupational capacity (PCLO), this allows an objective qualification of the aspects that constitute the Single Manual for the qualification of loss of work and occupational capacity (MUCPCLO). An analytical cross-sectional study with application of a scale of evaluation of the DO to a sample of workers selected at convenience, with alterations in functions or body structures, participation or restriction at occupational level. Analytical comparisons were made with the MUCPCLO qualification standard and differential and integrative factors were identified. The sample was composed of six subjects, four presented a work accident and two, occupational diseases. As regards the evaluation of performance, it was evidenced that 66 % presented mild dependence on specific activities; as a result, a differential analysis with emphasis on the qualification of MUCPCLO was made. Specific factors are recognized in the process as the objectivity of the DO, the multiple contributions of the concept of manual MULO and the possibilities of articulation of the PCLO
