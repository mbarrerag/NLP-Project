# Avatars of the Sensitive and small resistance itineraries: a study of the yoga trajectories in Medellín from an ethnographic exercise and life stories of yogis (1980-2017)

This research examines the appropriation of yoga in Medellín between 1980 and 2017, through an ethnographic exercise and life stories of pioneer and pioneer yogis in the city. It asks if yoga can be considered an "alternative body practice", in terms of putting into tension the relations of power reproduced in religiosity, education and biomedicine. The study is organized into four chapters. The first raises the question to the western values of the narratives of yoga. In the second and third, I deepened in the consciousness of "corporatized self-construction", the senses of "work on itself" and the configuration of "processed industries", with emphasis on the manifestations and scope of the appropriations of yoga as "emancipation practices", from the emergence of esoteric and devotional tendencies of yoga to the emergence of a new sensitivity around the configuration of a yóguico itinerary. The last chapter examines the role of yoga in the social and activist spheres, recognizing the subjetivity of non-violence and the deployment of new symbolic capitals with other authorized speakers. awareness of "embodied self-construction," the means of "self-work," and the sharing of " roaming of transformation," focusing on the manifestations and scope of yoga approaches as "emancipation practices", from the emergence of aesthetic and devotional yoga trends to the rise of a new sensitivity regulation yagic roaming configuration. The final chapter examines the role of yoga in social and active areas, collecting the subjectivities of non-violence and the deployment of new symbolic capital with other authorized speakers.
