# EXTRACTION, OPTIMIZATION AND ANTIOXIDING CAPACITY OF EXTRACTS OF PALMS SILVES (E. OLERACEAE, O. BATAUA, AND O. MAPORA) FROM THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC

The nutritional value and chemical characteristics of the species Euterpe oleracea are well known; however, in the Colombian Pacific region there are other species not known in the family Arecaceae with potential value in terms of the content of bioactive compounds. The main objective of this study is to determine the content of phenolic compounds and the antioxidant capacity of three wild species of the family Arecaceae (palms). For the fulfilment of this objective, the edible parts of the ripe fruits of Euterpe oleracea, Oenocarpus bataua and Oenocarpus mapora were subjected to ultrasound-assisted extraction, using hydroalcoholic mixtures with different concentrations of acetic acid as solvent for extraction. The optimisation of the extraction process by a composite central design (DC) showed that the best extraction conditions were obtained with a concentration of ethanol of 65 % (v/v) and an a concentration of acetic acid of 0.5 % (v/v).
