# Atmospheric circulation patterns in the geographical valley of the Cauca River and their impact on regional air quality

The quality of regional air is directly related to the flow of emissions of atmospheric pollutants and phenomena such as atmospheric dispersion, however, the annual, interannual and daily variability of meteorological phenomena and their interaction with the topography make the atmospheric dispersion a complex phenomenon, especially in regions considered to have complex topographies such as the north-west of South America.
