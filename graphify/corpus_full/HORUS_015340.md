# Geological-geotechnical characterization of a weathered transported material in the north of the Aburá Valley.

The Valle de Aburtúr is located in an inter-cordilleran area of the northern Andes of Colombia and is characterized because a large part of the middle and low areas of the slopes are covered by alluvial deposits, alluvotorrenal and mud flows and/or debris, many of them weathered by chemical processes.The chemical alteration has a direct effect on the geotechnical behavior, therefore, the concepts of the mechanics of classical soils (transported or sedimentary soils not metorized origin), do not allow to diagnose the problems in residual soils.This research work helped to dilute the geological and geotechnical framework of a deposited and altered soil located north of the Valley of Aburrá, the analysis of its geological origin and the incidence of the advanced processes of meterization in the geomechanical behavior.The detailed study of these soils helped to dilute the geological framework of the deposit, the research object of study and the interpretation of its origin and environment with relation to the geological context of the Valley of Aburrá
