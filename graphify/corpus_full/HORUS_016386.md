# A NEW ALLIGNATURE IN ENGINEERING POSGRATS: Educative RESEARCH IN THE ENGINEERING WALK

Engineering education is a recent research branch that arises from the need to study specific aspects of the learning processes of engineering students. In general terms, this engineering research area seeks to answer questions how to learn engineering? and how to carry out teaching-learning processes of engineering? In this context, the classroom is constituted as a primary source of information that allows to obtain data to answer research questions in this area of knowledge based on the design of learning environments that allow to study the processes and interactions that are generated between students and teachers. In this context, it is necessary to create academic spaces that have as goals that engineers know the basics of engineering educational research, learn what it is and how to carry out educational research in engineering classrooms, propose research proposals in this knowledge-based branch, know how to disseminate the results of their research and know different ex-periences of engineering educational research. This article presents an initiative in which a post-graduate subject called Educational Research in Engineering classrooms is presented in the Engineering Class of the Engineering Faculty of the University of Bogotánica - will present the details of engineering in the Engineering Faculty of the University of Colombia
