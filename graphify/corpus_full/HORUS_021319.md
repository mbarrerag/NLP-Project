# Behaviour of steel porticoes with cutting connection in composite section with concrete walls with edge elements and bolt type saw connectors welded

In systems of lateral resistance composite steel concrete, steel and concrete components work together to provide the strength and lateral rigidity required in the design. During the last decades in Colombia research has been carried out to develop systems of lateral resistance for seismic zones that adapt to the needs. Following this same line, this research is proposed that focuses on the experimental study of the behavior of a system composed of lateral resistance formed by sectional steel porticoes _x0093_I_x0094_ with connection to cut-off section composite concrete reinforced with edge elements and wall connectors welded perno type (PCMCC). In the system composed of lateral resistance of steel porticos with connection to moment (PRM) in section composed of reinforced concrete walls, the columns and steel beams serve as edge elements to resist the gravity loads and the greater part of the moment due to the seismic load. In the system composed of concrete filling reinforced (CR) it provides resistance to the cutting. The CR wall connected to the moment of concrete (PRM) by means of the cutting connectors, it has the advantage of increasing in a more than a few steps (the structural load). cyclical load parameters of maximum load, maximum deformation and failure load?, At what level of load initiates low cycle fatigue of the cutter connectors?, What is the difference between the behavior of the system with columns acting on the weak axis versus the experimental behavior of the system with columns acting on the strong axis? How is the performance of the PCMCC system versus the portico with connection to the cutter? The expected maximum load value obtained with existing design methodology is close to that obtained in the experimental trials? In this research project, it is proposed to develop, experimentally, the questions posed. For this purpose, eight specimens will be tested on a real scale. The project will be carried out with the research seedbed in metal structures, with a undergraduate student, a specialization student and a PhD student who acts as a leading student.
