# Thematic review of syndromes due to external pathogens for educational dissemination.

The syndromes by external pathogens according to the Six Layers, the Four Levels and the Three Jiaos, is a classic medical topic that every student of Traditional Chinese Medicine and Acupuncture should know, therefore the main objective of this grade work is their knowledge and make a proposal of educational dissemination according to the current communication technologies, such as the YouTube platform. I performed a thematic review of the external pathogen syndromes, based on the study of the two great classic and fundamental works of the Chinese medical history on the subject, such as the Shang Han Lun “Treatment of Febrile Diseases caused by Frio” by Zhang Zhongjing and the Wen Bing Xue “Theory of Warm Diseases” of the School of Calientes Diseases, approaching them through the works of qualified Western translators and interpreters. Likewise, the unit of YouTube called “Shanghan Colombia” is created, where through short explanatory videos the fundamental concepts on the subject are exposed, as a tool of educational dissemination.
