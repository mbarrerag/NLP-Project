# Self-government experience of the Association of Indigenous Cabildos of Villagarzón Putumayo ACIMVIP

Dialogue of knowledge, of the Association of Indigenous Cabildos of Villagarzón Putumayo, around the identification of cultural, territorial and environmental effects in relation to the Exploratory Drilling Area APE-la Cabaña of the Canadian multinational Gran Tierra Energy, as a strategy of enforceability of the fundamental rights of life, territory, autonomy, participation and consultation and prior free and informed consent
