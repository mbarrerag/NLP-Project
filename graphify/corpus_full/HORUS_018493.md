# EFFECT OF FRY PREPARATION OF COFFEE (LARBIC COFFEE L. VAR. CASTILLO) ON ANTIOXIDING CAPACITY AND SENSORIA QUALITY

The main objective of this study is to determine the retention of antioxidant metabolites, antioxidant activity and the cup profile of cold coffee drinks prepared using five methods: Toddy, Cold Tower, Mizudashi, Cold Brew and Fretta. UV-Visible spectrophotometry and high-efficiency liquid chromatography are used to quantify antioxidants and antioxidant activity. The results show that the method that recorded the highest antioxidant retention was Cold Tower, followed by Toddy and Cold Brew preparation. The antioxidant activity was proportional to the retention of antioxidant compounds. The methods with the highest overall printing were Toddy and Mizudashi. The most predominant hydroxycinamic acid was chlorogenic acid. The highest content of hydroxycinamic acids was obtained with Mizudashi, Cold Brew and Toddy. The consumption of coffee with the methods Cold Tower, Toddy and Cold Brew is recommended because of its good acceptance, retention and expression of antioxidants to find good balance in a refreshing beverage, with sensory quality and functionality. Key words: drink; coffee; cold preparations; tannins; antioxidant activity; profile; cup;
