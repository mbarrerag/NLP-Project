# Technical update of screening methods in the process of modern manUFACTURE as an integral training strategy in the quarricula of mechanical engineering and refining

The characterization of surface integrity and surface quality of machine elements obtained through different manufacturing processes directly influence the performance and reliability of machines during service. Because the Design of mechanical elements in Engineering is a common practice of design and manufacturing engineers, it requires the use of different techniques of precision control and measurement in each of the manufacturing processes that quantify and qualify the properties of surface integrity and quality. For this assessment, different manufacturing processes are used, including CNC and conventional machining, welding, 3D printing and machining of non-metallic materials, among them some wood. The experimental characterization of the parts and components produced will have a significant effect on the future performance of the components, for which it is necessary to perform some destructive and non-destructive tests, in laboratory before bringing them to their final destination
