# Ventriculitis associated with intracranial device. Case report

Ventriculitis is defined as inflammation of the ependymal lining of the cerebral ventricles, usually of infectious origin and frequently associated with the use of intracranial devices such as external ventricular drainage (VSD). It is characterized by high morbiditymortality, high hospital costs and the risk of severe neurological sequelae. Presentation of the case. 20-year-old woman who in 2024 entered the emergency department of a third-level healthcare institution located in Bogotá, Colombia, by headache, phosphenes, tinnitus, vomiting and a convulsive episode. During her hospital stay she was diagnosed with agenesis of the callous body, hypoplasia of right lateral ventricle and ventriculoma by non-communicational hydrocephalus secondary to stenosis of the aqueduct of Silvio, so she required the placement of a ventriculoperitoneal bypass (VSD).
