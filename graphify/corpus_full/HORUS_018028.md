# Electrical safety in a power system considering intermittent sources of electrical energy.

This grade work addresses the main challenges of the operation and planning of the electrical system under the regulation CREG 060 of 2019. It addresses the topic of solar plant power forecasts using recurrent neural networks. A methodology is proposed to operate the system safely taking into account the short-term variation associated with this type of plants and finally a modification is proposed to the calculation of the safe region of operation that considers the variation of renewables
