# Medicinal products and loam, Chapter 36

The use of drugs during pregnancy is a controversial issue, partly due to the lack of studies that evaluate their safety at this stage of life. In this chapter, a review of the different groups of medicines is made, how they are categorized according to FDA criteria. The pharmacodynamic physiological changes specific to pregnancy are described and the use of the main drugs that can be used in pregnancy divided into the different pharmacological groups is presented; the main vaccines and their safety during pregnancy are also presented. Finally, some recommendations are generated for readers when making prescriptions for medicines during pregnancy
