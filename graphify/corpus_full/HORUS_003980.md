# Entropy Bekenstein-Hawking: a connection between information theory and thermodynamics

Bekenstein-Hawking entropy is modeled based on the microstate count of Hawking radiation in Schwarzschild and Reissner-Nordström black holes. The conservation of information and entropy is shown after a process of evaporation of a black hole by Hawking radiation via quantum tunneling. (Text taken from source.)
