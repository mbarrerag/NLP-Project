# APPLICATIONS OF REPRODUCTION ASSISTED TO BUBALIN PRODUCTION TRANSFER OF EMPRlION AND LANDING

Introduction We consider very important contextualize the readers on the use of reproductive biotechnologies in the production with buffaloes.The ones that we will address in this chapter were designed for humans and other species of animals, in the bufalino case the most used are the biotechnologies designed for cattle, but it has happened that the researchers have had a lot of unexpected results that have limited their application due to the characteristics of the species and the needs of production with buffalo.The application of biotechnologies is a concept in construction.In this chapter will describe the own and other experiences of the researcher, will be presented results that are considered very illustrative (without being the only ones), without going into the explanations of why of the events.We should remind the readers that the transfer of embryos, in the context of the animal production, is associated with the possibility to take advantage of the reproductive potential of a female by allowing to obtain more than one calf per year, as would happen naturally in spite of the 305 days of gestation of the buffalo. The production system is fed and managed by the embryo transfer.
