# Evaluation of unsupervised clustering strategies in the determination of patterns associated with thermal system failures in bulk truck tractors.

This thesis aims to evaluate unsupervised grouping strategies for data associated with bulk truck tractors, in the detection of fault patterns in thermal systems. The study of these techniques is important in the field of predictive maintenance based on data with the implementation of machine learning algorithms that allow to adequately plan maintenance schedules in cargo transport companies. For the development of the the thesis, the telemetry devices of bulk truck tractors of a Colombian freight transport company are used as a source of information that report real-time data of the measurement of variables such as speed, temperatures, state of operation of the vehicle, among others for 2020. The results are also used the history of income to workshop of the 116 tractor truck fleet where the revenues to workshop for the intervention of thermal systems are analyzed. These data are the input for the evaluation of the proposed grouping strategies of operations in this work.
