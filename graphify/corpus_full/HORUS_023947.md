# Diversity of epiphytic livers (Marchantiophylla) of the Suasie sector in the Chingaza National Natural Park.

The birophytes form a conspicuous group of the flora of the planet. Their ecological contribution in terms of species richness and coverage in many habitats is fundamental for the balance of ecosystem services. In Colombia there is an approximate record of 715 species of Marchantiophyta (hepatic), which present their greatest richness in the Andean region. The study was carried out in the Chingaza National Natural Park - Suasie sector given its ecological and hydric importance for the surrounding municipalities. The main objective of this study was to determine the diversity of the community of livers in the Suasie sector of the PNN Chingaza. To this, three uprisings were made in transects of 2x50 m in an altitudinal gradient, in each transect the liver present present in four forophytes selected at random. The samples collected were identified for the analysis of composition and diversity of the community.
