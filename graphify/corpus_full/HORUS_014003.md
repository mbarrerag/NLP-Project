# Effect of a statin on glucose consumption in 3T3-L1 adipocytes and amplification of cDNA by encoding the extracellular portion of a hybrid receptor protomer IR/IGF1R

This project focuses on one of the research lines of the UNIMOL group (molecular pharmacology line), particularly as regards the action of insulin and the dysfunction of its action in pathological situations, or in response to treatment with certain drugs. On the one hand, it seeks to evaluate whether a drug from the statins group (Rosuvastatin or another) affects glucose consumption (and/or induces insulin resistance) in a model of fat cells. On the other hand, and in order to carry out structural studies that complement previous insylic results of our group, it seeks to advance work that leads us to have recombinant plasmids so that in the near future we can express the hybrid IR/IGF1R receptor. Finally, the project seeks, through the literature review, to create a new master's thesis project that allows us to address the topic of microRNAs and their possible role in insulin resistance and/or in response to the use of drugs
