# Immediate effect of two exercises with semioccluded vocal tract on the glotic contact of occupational voice users

Abstract Introduction: Teachers are occupational users of voice with a high risk of developing vocal pathologies because of their work. To address this problem, exercises with semi-occluded vocal tract (TVSO) are usually used, education/rehabilitation strategy used to generate a change in the vibration pattern of the vocal folds, minimizing the risk of vocal injury by reducing the stress of collision to which tissues are subjected. Various reports have indicated that this treatment has effects on the closing ratio (CQ), indirect measurement of collision stress. To examine the physiological effect of two exercises with TVSO on the laryngeal activity of teachers with constant vocal effort but without vocal pathology. Samples of 43 teachers were recorded before, during and after the performance of two exercises with TVSO (language vibration and phonation in tubes).Etroglotography samples were analyzed to obtain the CQ. A significant difference was observed when comparing the values of the CQ before and during the performance of both exercises. No changes were found in this parameter after execution of the activity.
