# Reactive energy compensation for photovoltaic solar generation systems with penalty by power factor

The implementation of renewable energy projects using solar energy is one of the solutions used in Colombia with the aim of diversifying the national energy matrix and charting the course towards a more responsible energy transition with the environment, as well as using the renewable resources available in the territory. However, this type of project produces consequences and imbalances in the triangle of power of the installations in which they are developed. The aim of this work is to evaluate the technical and economic feasibility of two methods used for the compensation of the reactive energy and therefore the restoration or balancing of the power triangle. Therefore, an analysis of the existing scientific, legislative and technical literature was carried out, which has been presented in the theoretical framework of this work, followed by the methodology for carrying out the analysis in the behavior and effects of the associated electrical and economic variables in the implementation of this type of projects is considered to be the most effective tool for the purpose of choosing the successful decision-making methods of the use of the photovoltaic systems within the range of 100 kWp and 3000 kWp. cing of the power triangle. Therefore, an analysis of the existing scientific, legislative and technical literature was made, which has been exposed in the theoretical framework of this work, then the methodology is present to perform the analysis on the earlier and effect of electrical and economic variables associated with the implementation of this type of projects at different times. Taking as a sample a group of nine nominal powers for photovoltaic systems that are within the range of 100 kWp and 3000 kWp.
