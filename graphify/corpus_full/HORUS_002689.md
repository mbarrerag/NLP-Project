# ECONOMIC TECHNICAL STUDY OF POLIN FERMENTED BY THE LOTS OF BOLIVAR MARIA, COLOMBIA

The apiculture pollen is considered a super food, due to its high content of nutrients and bioactive compounds, however its bioavailability is limited, which requires that during its processing biotechnological techniques be applied that protect its functional properties.The use of bacteria or enzymes in these processes can attribute overcosts in the process.This technical-economic study evaluates the feasibility of producing fermented apiculture pollen with functional properties using 100 g of pollen in a laboratory.Key aspects such as equipment and inputs necessary for production and operating budget are analyzed.For fermentation, probiotic crops such as Lactobacillus plantarum (LP), Choozit TM 82 LYO 50 DCU (CZT) and Yomix 885 LYO 50 DCU (YM) are used, with different proportions of water: pollen, with the aim to improve the bioavailability and nutritional properties of pollen.The analysis of direct costs and profit margins for bags of 100 g of fermented pollen showed that the project is profitable.With a profit margin of 20%, the Y1.0 results Y$ 0,05 and the results for bags of 100 g$
