# After the ball's memories with Peama Sumapaz students. 2024-I

During the first semester of the year 2024, as a grade option in the academic program of Social Work of the Faculty of Human Sciences, the student John Sebastian Montaño Ladino, has proposed to advance the project called After the balloon memories with students of Peama Pemapaz. 2024-I, until achieving the general objective of analyzing the relationship between football and the construction of collective memories in Sumapaz, with students of the Special Programme of Admission and Academic Mobility (Peama) Sumapaz of the National University of Colombia. To achieve this objective proposes four specific objectives and a methodological process that includes a documentary review of the unit of analysis of the fish; as well as a series of thematic workshops that, in turn, combines with the methodology of _x0093_four times_x0094_ of the popular football and allow it to merge times in which it performs: (i) preliminary approaches to texts on football and collective memories, (ii) talks with students of the Peama Sumapaz related to these categories, (iii) workshops with students around sports trainings and parties by memories, reading, listening and listening to and listening to the four-a-a-a-a-a-a-a-a- The Peama Sumapaz project on football and the construction of collective memories of Sumapaz, considering the conceptual and methodological findings obtained in the documentary review, to represent in an audiovisual and narrative way the influence of football and the construction of collective memories in this context. In short, the project aims to ensure that the research carried out by the University in the framework of the professional training of one of its members is put at the service of the society located in a strategic ecosystem for the country and the world, as is the Sumapaz paramo. In particular, it aims to advance a collaborative work that co-creates alternative methodologies with students of the Peama Sumapaz to help overcome the educational gaps that affect them, strengthen their capacities, competences and talents in a local way, as well as to visualize their collective experiences and memories in such a way that they contribute to the construction of a dignified human life in harmony with nature
