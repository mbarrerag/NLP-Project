# From the invisible to the printed: The search for the presence/absence of women in the graphic work in Colombia

This research examines the graphic work and its ability to intertwin subjectivities and networks of affection, revealing both the struggles and resistances of artists and the injustices they have faced. Through a network of gestures, materials, body and memories, the graphic not only leaves an impression, but resonates in affections, complicity and disputes. At the intersection of surfaces and resistances, traces of silenced stories emerge, presences that intertwine in the opacity of exclusion. Exploring this field implies recognizing the technical gesture as a political act and matter as a space where permanence and fragility converge, especially for those relegated to the margins of history. Likewise, it analyzes the interaction between materials, body and tools, together with the hierarchicalization of disciplines in Colombian history, showing the double invisibility of women in this field. Together, this research seeks to open new avenues for representation and social criticism in graphic art, examining how women have been represented and excluded from their history . the interaction among materials, the body, and tools is analyzed, alongside the hierarchy of disciplines in Colombian history, highlighting the double invisibility of women in this domain.
