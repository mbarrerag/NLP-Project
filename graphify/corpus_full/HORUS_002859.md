# Analysis of price performance of electricity contracts for the regulated market in Colombia

The Colombian energy market has evolved significantly since its creation, striving to achieve transparency, efficiency and fair prices for all users.This market is divided into regulated demand, which includes residential users, and unregulated demand, which encompasses the industry, users of high consumption and public lighting.To meet this demand, various mechanisms have been implemented in the Wholesale Energy Market (MEM).In this work, it is proposed to analyze the variables that influence the formation of prices of contracts for the regulated market and to examine the behaviors of the market agents, using various statistical techniques that allow to identify and understand patterns in the data, such as metrics of dispersion, centrality and central tendency, as well as methods of grouping and analysis of the variability, distribution and relation between variables.The analysis reveals that until 2020, the average prices of contracts for agents without control situation were higher, but this trend is reversed from 2021 with the implementation of the Public Convocational Information System (SICEP), suggesting that the regulation has influenced the prices.The anticipation of contracts significantly reduces the weighted average prices, highlighting the importance of the situation of the contracts.
