# Proposal for a design methodology based on ISO/ASTM 52910:2018 standard for gears manufactured additively by extrusion using an anti-wear polymer.

The gears are mechanical elements used to transmit mechanical power between arrows. Traditionally, their manufacture is made by means of subtractive processes using standardized strawberries under module or diametrical step systems that carve the teeth on a cylinder or cone; however, sometimes it is observed that there are gears with tooth sizes that do not correspond to either of the two standards, or that the company does not have the correct mills and machines to make a particular gear, making it difficult to manufacture spare parts. Additive manufacturing then becomes an alternative to be able to manufacture this type of components, but the designer must take into account the design considerations proper to the additive process and its materials to take advantage of its unique capabilities and improve the performance of the gear. This project proposes a design methodology for additive manufacture by anti-wear polymer extrusion that the designer can apply to generate topologically optimized gears in its core.
