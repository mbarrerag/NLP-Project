# AUTHORITY CAPACITY AND CARD-VASCULAR HEALTH IN PARENTS OF SCHOLARIZED ADOLESCENTS OF SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The main objective is to determine the relationship between cardiovascular health and the self-care agency in parents of adolescents attending school. Correlational study with parents of adolescents of a medium school in Santander - Colombia, selected by stratified probabilistic sample. Main results: of the 325 accudents, 61.8% were women with average age of 40.6 (SD±7.1) years, 42.3% of medium socioeconomic level; only 39.3% with normal body mass index, 23.8% and 10.5% showed systolic and diastolic blood pressure values in the range of hypertension stage I or II; the mean self-care score (94 SD±8.4) indicated good management. The sex distribution revealed differences in the family history of diabetes (p=0.030), dyslipidemia (p=0.008); smoking (p=0.009), alcohol consumption (p=0.000) and physical activity (p=0.001). Main conclusion: Parents reported cardiovascular history and a capacity of middle self-care agency, affecting modifiable factors such as sedentarism, alcohol consumption and tobacco
