# Youth struggles in Colombia after the agreement (2016-2022).

The social outbreak that occurred on April 28, 2021 had as one of its central protagonists to urban youth, which, from different spatial and social connections (barrial, school, university, etc.), has manifested its discontent with a situation of exclusion, invisibility and absence of perspectives of future to which it has been subjected in contemporary Colombia. Unlike what believe those who interpret the social dynamics as the result of the actions of leading minorities, sociopolitical explosions like that mentioned emerge from below and in a decentralized way. It is the boiling of multiple forms of microsocial organization that are put in motion from relatively contingent events (as in the case of the tax reform of the former minister Carrasquilla). Of course, plurality and decentralization are expressed in terms of objective circumstances shared as those already described, but to consummate their unity must, first, articulate themselves in network and second, design suitable mechanisms to intervene effectively in the established scenarios of decision-making. The articulation in network is given in good measure, subproduct of which is the formation of informal ties and, that is the formation of informal ties. In this matter, it is worth noting that one of the by-products of the pandemic is what seems to be increasingly emerging as the exhaustion of the globalization model established since the early 1970s. Such a model rested on what some have characterized as a separation of management scales: the economic _x00961_x0096__ developed globally and shaped in the light of the autonomy of the market mechanisms_x00961_x0096_ on the one hand, and that of political and social management, which remained in the hands of the states and their hierarchical instruments of government, on the other. The role of Colombian urban youth in facing this process seems decisive to judge by their spontaneous leadership in the days unleashed at the end of April 28, 2021, as well as by their massive intervention in the recent electoral process. This research aims to identify what kind of understanding of their own capacities these sectors have, as well as to locate themselves within the framework of the global and national system and to design the organizational instruments necessary to influence the construction of Colombia of the future.
