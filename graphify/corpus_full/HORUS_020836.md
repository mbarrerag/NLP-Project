# Technological school garden from the vision of the Kankuamo Indigenous People

The project _x0093_Teaching plant from the vision of the Kankuamo_x0094_ Indigenous People arises as a response to the serious situation of food vulnerability that Kankuama population living in the Kankuamo indigenous reserve located in the northwestern area of the municipality of Valledupar (Cesar).In recent years the access and availability of food has been, conditioned to the vejamen of the armed conflict, this has caused to a great extent a cultural uprooting, the loss of the agricultural vocation and decrease of the environmental consciousness.This proposal has aimed to promote the use of the new technologies for the monitoring of the state of health of the mother nature (soil health and water health) within the scenario of the fourth industrial revolution. It seeks to recover the agricultural vocation in the young people of the Kankuamo village and promote in them the care of the mother nature initially by means of a proof of concept that consists in the implementation of technological tools in a school garden that will take place in the Agricultural Institution The Mina headquarters Rioseco located in the indigenous model Kankuamo.
