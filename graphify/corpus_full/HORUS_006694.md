# Histological findings in cases of acute rejection of kidney transplantation, analysis in a transplant clinic in Bogotá-Colombia

The acute rejection of renal transplantation (RTA) is a pathology that generates risk of renal graft loss, its diagnosis is histological and is based on the biopsy study using the Banff classification. An analytical study was carried out with descriptive component, quantitative type with a retrospective observational analysis of the caseuistic of patients with diagnosis of RATE in the Shaio Clinical Foundation in the years 2021 and 2022 doing non-probabilistic sampling for convenience. A description and photographic record of the main histological findings of the biopsies using the criteria of Banff 2019 was made, association between the types of RATE and the donor of the renal graft (Cadaveric/Vivo) was sought by the Chi-square calculation. A series of 44 cases of rejection of the acute graft were found that were divided into two groups, those presenting RATE N=35 and another group that presented RATE recurrent (RTA) N=8 (a subject had 2 episodes of repetition) was found. The type of rejection of the highest presentation was the ACE type of cellular (62.9%), followed by the MAT type of the ORT type of cellular group, compared with the <2%) in the ERT type of -probabilistic sampling by agreement. The description and graphic record of the main historical finding of the biopsy was made using the Banff 2019 criteria, association between the types of ATR and the graft donor (Cadaveric/Living) was sought by calculating Chi-square. We found a series of N=44 cases that were divided into two groups, those who present ATR N=35 and another group that present Recurrent ATR (RATR) N=8 (one subject had 2 episodes of recurrence).
