# Role of dynamic capacities in companies in the food and food sector of Colombia

The dynamic capacities are defined as the aptitude of an organization that allows it to integrate, build and reconfigure internal and external competences to address environments that change quickly.Since the agri-food sector is dynamic due to the globalization and the characteristics of the sector, such as volatile prices and dependence on the climate, among others.The objective of this proposal of the thesis is to analyze, through a systematic review of literature, the characteristics of the environment in which the companies of production and commercialization of agricultural products are immersed in Colombia and countries referent, and to identify the dynamic capacities of greater impact for the organizations of the agri-food sector, in order to allow the companies belonging to this sector to have a point of reference about the dynamic capacities that must be developed to obtain a sustainable competitive advantage according to their environment.It is also intended to adapt a methodology of measurement of the dynamic capacities for the agri-food sector, that allows to evaluate the current state of the deployment and promote the processes of creation of these capacities., it has been decided to use an exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis method.
