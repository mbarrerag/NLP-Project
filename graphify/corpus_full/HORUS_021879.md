# Learning analytics in the subject Computer Programming: a research based on mixed methods.

The recent growth of new forms of educational data, has made the analytics of learning emerge as a solution to identify relevant information in the decision making of education.A group of researches in learning analytics focuses on identifying variables of the learning process related to the academic performance of students.However, few researches consider the use of mixed or qualitative methodologies, which limits the understanding about the behaviors of students.The general objective of this work is to determine the relationships between the measures and metrics derived from the learning process and the academic performance of students in the subject Programación de Computadores de la Facultad de Ingeniería en la Universidad Nacional de Colombia en 2019 y 2020. This work proposes a methodological design with mixed, non-experimental approach, where the qualitative phase of the methodology is focused towards the analysis of content.The results show that there is a positive correlation between the attempts of solution made by the student and his academic performance, which can possibly be associated to the perceptions of the educational platform used as an environment that promotes constant practice due to their availability in line.The errors/veridcs of the solutions sent (correct answers and their limits and their academic performance). are corroborated, supplemented or refuted by the observations of qualitative data
