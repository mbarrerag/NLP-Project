# High performance phenotyping by analyzing digital images in maize roots (Zea mays L.)

Two methodologies were used for the analysis of the root system phenotype: I) manual phenotyping and II) phenotyping by digital imaging analysis. The variables associated with the aerial and root part were related using Pearson correlations. Main components were used to evaluate patterns in the variation of the root architecture. The manually measured root diameter was correlated with the root diameter derived from the image (r = 0.97) and the right and left opening angles with r = 0.96 and 0.94 values, respectively. The results presented in this study show that an automated root phenotyping protocol can be adopted under the REST software that allows a level of phenotypic research suitable for the evaluation of genotypes and physiological studies
