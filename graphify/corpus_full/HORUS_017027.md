# STRATEGIC PLAN AND PACKAGE LEAFLET FOR FRESH FRUIT IN THE VALLE OF THE CAUCA 2033

This document is carried out within the framework of the Project "Strengthening the C&CTI System of the Cauca Valley: towards a Knowledge Economy", which seeks to strengthen the articulation between researchers and research groups with the company, the State and civil society belonging to the C&CTI ecosystem of the region. Likewise, it promotes investment and measurement in the processes of research, innovation and transfer, in order to contribute to competitiveness and regional development and transform the Cauca Valley into a sustainable territory in the knowledge economy. Within the project is the component 2 "Measurement of C&CTI and its relationship with the development of the Region" and specifically the product 2.1 "Pilot proposal for technological and industrial perspective of the fresh fruit sector, which can be replicated in other sectors", which aims to design a pilot system of measurement of the C&CTI and the development of the Cauca Valley, which allows the adoption of intelligent decisions that transform the region into a knowledge economy. Within this product, three specific objectives are to be achieved, the first of which is a pilot system of measurement of the fresh fruit sector and the development model.
