# THEORICAL APPROXIMATION TO SPACE DIMENSION: FROM COLLECTIVE ACTION

This book is part of a series of publications through which the Faculty of Architecture of the National University of Colombia - Headquarters Medellín, wants to make public what is discussed inside it every day. Products derived from the project called "Topics and reflections of the Faculty of Architecture. Where are we going?, here are contents approaches of great interest, not only for those who seek to form under the shelter of this academic institution, but also for everyone who searches looking for new reasons of cavilation that allow him to a greater understanding of the environment that inhabits socially and of his own condition as citizen.The structure of this volume revolves around the analysis of various conditions and factors that affect and determine the regional urban planning in its character as a fundamental tool of understanding and action in the framework of the realities of the cities and territories.The chapters that make up this book bring us, in this sense, reflections and analysis derived from rigorous research processes that seek to understand the fragile relationship between urban and rural, as well as establishing points of departure for the configuration of public policies that account these problematics.The need to concentivize the citizenry on these needs that do not also arise from the spatial, are also looking at the theoretical action, as part of the urban,
