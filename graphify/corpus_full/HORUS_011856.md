# CHARACTERIZATION AND COMMISSIONING OF TO TRIPLE-GEM DETECTOR

The characterization and operation of a triple-multiplier detector of gaseous electrons (triple-GEM) is presented in detail. The response to several sources of radiation was studied, and their efficiency, gain, energy resolution and temporal resolution were calculated. An energy resolution of 19.5% was found, a time resolution of 40 ns. and a maximum gain of 5×106. We conclude that the detector can be used with confidence in future studies on medical dosimetry, since its functioning falls within the proportional region
