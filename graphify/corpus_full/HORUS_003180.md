# TOWARDS SUSTAINABLE ENERGY MATRIX IN COLOMBIA.

This study analyzes the current composition of the energy matrix in Colombia, encompassing its legal framework, configuration, impacts and future projections. The research, based on a systematic review of literature, bibliometric analysis and text mining, reveals that hydroelectric power plants are the main source of clean energy. Fossil fuels, while essential for energy availability in the territory, generate the greatest carbon footprint, which represents a significant environmental challenge. Despite legal efforts and projections towards clean energy, limitations remain in infrastructure, technology and social acceptance towards energy production projects. The transition to a sustainable energy system requires overcoming these technical, regulatory and social barriers, integrating legal, environmental, social and technological aspects in planning to meet the objectives of the energy transition of the current matrix, which presents a complex landscape that requires comprehensive strategies for a sustainable energy future
