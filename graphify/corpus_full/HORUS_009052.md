# D-OPTIMUM EXPERIMENTAL DESIGNS INCREASED: A COMPARIENT STUDIES

The experimental D-optimal designs provide the experimental conditions, together with the respective replicas, that the researcher must take into account for the realization of the experimental runs, guaranteeing an estimate of the parameters of the model that minimize the volume of the trust ellipsoid associated with the parameters of the model. It is known that the number of support points of the D-optimal design coincides with the number of parameters of the model that is to be adjusted. This does not allow to use the D-optimal designs for the performance of adjustment goodness tests. To solve this problem, the literature has proposed methodologies that allow to increase the number of experimental points of the D-optimal designs from different approaches. In this work a comparative study is made between these methodologies and, with the help of an illustrative example, it is determined which of the methodologies proposed the obtained design achieves an efficiency close to the efficiency of the original D-optimal design
