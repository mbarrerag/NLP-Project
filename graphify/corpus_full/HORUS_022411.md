# APTENTS FOR A CINE HISTORY IN THE HIGH BLACK RIGHT VALUE. COLOMBUM-BRAZILEÑA FRONTERA IN AmazONIA, 1921-2006

This article offers the results of a preliminary exploration of the filmography carried out in the border region of the Alto Río Negro Vaupés between Colombia and Brazil. The area is a remote area of both countries, occupied by more than thirty indigenous peoples of three linguistic families who share cultural elements. Films have been made about the region and the indigenous people since the early decades of the 20th century. The study offers an inventory and attempts to make a preliminary characterization of these filmic records
