# Technology surveillance study on TV White Spaces, focused on connectivity in rural areas in Colombia.

The elaboration of this research is aimed at knowing the most important aspects of the technology TV White Spaces (TVWS), operation, architecture, cases of use, regulation and implementations in Colombia and in different parts of the world, as a starting point to the evolution of wireless communications in the rural sector.The first step to know the current state of the technology, is adopted the process of technological surveillance (VT) of which the planning, search and acquisition phases and analysis and organization are applied.In the first phase of planning is found that there is no existing study of technological surveillance of the TVWS technology and that we open up to factors that we can reach with this study such as early anticipation of technological changes, minimizing technological risks associated with innovation and exposing the early detection of opportunities and technological cooperation.Next, the needs of the existing in Colombia and the critical factors of surveillance of the TVWS technology as the technology that reduces that gap of non-connectivity
