# Relationship Intervention based on attachment (IRBA) in virtual format and care sensitivity. An exploratory study with early childhood

This exploratory study aimed to analyze the effects of the Related Intervention Based on the Virtual Approach (virtual IRBA) on the sensitivity of care, attitudes towards sensitive care and problematic behaviors in children. A pre-experimental design was used, specifically a pre-test/post test design with a single group composed of 12 days of primary caregivers and their children from 0 to 5 years. The variables were evaluated by the Q-Sort of maternal behavior (MBQS), a self-reporting questionnaire on attitudes towards the sensitive care and the Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL 1 1/2 - 5). For the analysis of data, the non-parametric test of Wilcoxon was used for matched samples, with a level of significance of 0.05. The results showed that no statistically significant differences were found in the sensitivity scores of the caregiver and the attitudes towards the sensitive care before and after the intervention. However, an increase in the frequency of descriptors of sensitive behaviors in the sample group were observed.
