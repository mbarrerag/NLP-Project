# Comprehensive evaluation of the interventions carried out in the La López ravine (Barbosa, Antioquia), within the framework of the Quebradas Plan of the Abura Valley: contributions to water governance

The objective of this research was to evaluate integrally the interventions carried out in the Quebrada La López (Barbosa, Antioquia), within the framework of the Quebradas Plan of the Aburrá Valley.
