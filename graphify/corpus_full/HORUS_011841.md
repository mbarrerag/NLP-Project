# More space, more dimensions, towards a broader object of study of the General Theory of Process.

The present final master's work proposes some criteria to redefine the General Theory of Process, its object of study and methodologies, so that, on the one hand, the procedural theory of account of spatialities different from that of the nation-state; and, on the other, for the procedural theory to focus on analytical, normative and empirical dimensions
