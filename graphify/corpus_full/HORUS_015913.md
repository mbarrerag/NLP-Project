# Synthesis and characterization of thin films of CH3NH3PbBrXI3-x deposited by sequential evaporation of precursors.

Currently, new hybrid, organic/inorganic semiconductor materials with a Perovskit type structure are being intensively investigated, because these materials have exceptional characteristics to be used in the manufacture of solar cells, such as optimal prohibited energy gap, high photon absorption capacity and excellent electrical transport properties; in addition, their constituent elements are cheap and abundant. In this work special emphasis will be placed on the development of a new thin film preparation route of the organic/inorganic hybrid compound CH3NH3PbBrxI3-x (MAPbBrxI3-x) which were deposited in a process that includes the sequential deposition by evaporation of the inorganic precursors lead iodide (PbI2) and lead bromide (PbBr2) followed by the deposition of the organic precursor and metilamonium sodium (MAI) using the CSS method (close spaced sublimation).
