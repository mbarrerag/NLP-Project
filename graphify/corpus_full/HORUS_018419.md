# The search for meaning as a tool in musical interpretation

In the musical field it is necessary to have certain skills to obtain recognition, agógica, interaction, listening, technical and stage domain, performance among others. But without any doubt the ability to control nerves in public is one of the most important, no matter how much it has been studied if you cannot control the mind during the concert. Scenic anxiety is a condition that can be given in musicians, sometimes it can alter the interpretive performance and musical performance. This autoethnographic work collects own experiences that from the self-analysis pose an alternative in the process of recognition, control and overcoming the escenic anxiety. .
