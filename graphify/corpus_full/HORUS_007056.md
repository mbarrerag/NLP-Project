# Design of a didactic unit based on DIY methodology to generate meaningful learning in imaging for eleventh grade students

For decades, the teaching of physics in educational institutions has separated the theory from practice, creating a culture in which one learns about nature without having contact with it. This has generated learning difficulties, making students see science as an inaccessible and static knowledge, dominated by the teacher. In order to challenge traditional trends, a didactic unit based on the Do It Yourself (DIY) methodology was implemented to promote meaningful learning in physics, specifically on the formation of images with lenses, with students from the VID College of Medellín. This work allowed to diagnose the previous conceptions of the DI methodology to the eleventh degree and to evaluate their significant learnings through a mixed data analysis. At the end, an increase in motivation, a significant improvement in the understanding of the topics dealt with and a high degree of satisfaction among students. This experience opens new possibilities for further researching from the impact of the DI methodology to the modern structure, as well as the transformation of the laboratory into a makerspace, a space dedicated to experimentation and practical creation. .
