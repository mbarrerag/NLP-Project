# Effect of music therapy on a group of elderly people, with manifestations of anxiety, institutionalized in a gerontological house of Tenjo, Cundinamarca

This research, of mixed type, describes the results of a musical therapy process focused on the impact that the musicotherapy has on the manifestations of anxiety of a group of seven (7) senior institutionalized people. It developed with residents of the Gerontological House Re-Creando Vida en Tenjo -Cundinamarca (Colombia) during the peak moment of isolation due to the pandemic of COVID 19, through an intervention of twenty (20) sessions, implemented between the months of February and June 2020; the one that sought to offer tools of emotional coping with the manifestations of anxiety, facilitating the emotional expression and promoting spaces of well-being for the senior institutionalized people. The musicotherapeutic work was carried out from the four methods of muscle therapy.
