# The map of Colombia

To replace the ancient map of Colombia that served us throughout the last century so to teach in schools as a guide and standard of our statesmen in the discussions of limiles, the Government created the Commission of Lengths, an entity that began its task since 1909 and published in 1919 a large map in scale of one in a million, that is, one millimeter per kilometer of the land. “It also published in the space between 1919 and 1932 the partial maps of each Department and finally, another general map of the Republic made in scale of two kilometers in the field for every millimeter of map. The procedure adopted by the Commission of Longitudes was to fix by geographical coordinates almost all of the Colombian populations, of some important reference points and very especially, of border samples. Thus, that important Commission managed to give us in a short time a map of quite correctness for now in very short time, although the greater part of the topographic details were drawn approximately in relation to the fixed points whose position was determined exactly. In short, it gave us a map that responded quite well to the conditions and requirements of the Republic during the first third of this century and given the plan of the indefinite peace
