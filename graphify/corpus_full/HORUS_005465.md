# LINEAL ALGEBRA TEACHING IN THE ENGINEERING WAYS

This paper presents a selection of works that are considered relevant for the teaching of linear algebra through plausible reasoning both in engineering careers and in other research areas. We considered work in which a comprehensive study is carried out on the teaching of algebra through the North American school, others from the European school and another selection of schools from the rest of the world, then we made a small selection of works that relate to plausible reasoning in the teaching of mathematics, only a work is found where the teaching of linear algebra is related to plausible reasoning. Studies carried out since the 90s of the last century show that learning of linear algebra presents great difficulties in students and therefore research has been carried out to detect its causes and propose solutions to the problem; of them we can summarize the following aspects.a. Lack of an active participation of the student, since the teaching of the teacher focuses on the acquisition of manipulation and computational skills, which reduces the motivation of them. b.
