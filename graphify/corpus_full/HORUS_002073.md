# Synthesis and evaluation of photocatalytic activity of Zn1-xMxO semiconductors (M = Cu and Ni, x = 0.01 - 0.20) in H2 generation

The ZnO is one of the semiconductors most used in the photocatalytic generation of H2; however, it presents recombination of pairs e-/h+ and absorption in UV. Therefore, this research proposed the synthesis and photocatalytic evaluation of Zn1-xMxO (M = Ni o Cu and x = 0.01-020) coated with Pt, mixed with garnet Y3Al4.95Er0.05O12 (Er3+:YAG) and using methanol as a slaughter agent, in order to improve the deficiencies of the ZnO. The materials were synthesized by the sol-gel method and characterized by DRX, SEM-EDX, Raman, UV-Vis-DRS, adsorption-disorbition of N2, EPR, magnetisation and spectroscopy of photoluminescence. Initially, 700 °C and 2 h were determined to improve the activity of the ZnO, so they were used to prepare samples with formula CuO/Z. The findings contribute significantly to solving the shortcomings of the ZnO, as activity in the visible region increased and the recombination of the e-/h+ pairs decreased. .
