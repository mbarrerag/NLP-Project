# Adaptation of an intervention based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy aimed at couples with indicators of dissatisfaction

The intervention in pairs is a relevant topic for the research and clinical practice of psychology, since its results generate impact on the well-being of individuals, families and society. The project aimed to validate the content of the adaptation of an intervention protocol from Acceptance and Commitment Therapy ACT by expert judges and subsequently implement it in a single AB case design with follow-up. The proposal was evaluated by three clinical psychologists, with experience in ACT, giving excellent results in the IVC Content Validation Index for most of its components. The implementation was carried out with three heterosexual couples, to evaluate the effect of the intervention was carried out the analysis of non-overover all the NAP pairs and the results for the satisfaction indicator exhibit a medium-large effect with percentages between 76.7% and 100% for the three couples after the intervention, on the other hand, in the results of the tests of psychological flexibility are progress in some participants, which accounts for the need to continue to investigate the impact of the protocol on psychological flexibility and the possible adjustments that are required for future implementations.
