# PROBLEMS IN SMEs IN THE APPLICATION OF IFRS FOR SMEs AND THEIR POSSIBLE SOLUTIONS IN THE CONTEXT OF COLOMBIA*

The aim of this paper is to establish the problems of the application of International Financial Reporting Standards for SMEs (NIIF for SMEs) in Colombia and to propose alternative solutions; the above, in the framework of compliance with the regulation. The methodology to be used will be qualitative, through the documentary review in national, regional and international sources that allow identifying problems of application of niif for SMEs. Likewise, an analysis of niif for SMEs is carried out based on experience with these entities and an approach is made to companies through semi-structured interviews to achieve a broader view of the problem posed. Preliminary results show a diversity of difficulties in specific topics such as deterioration and amortized cost and, in general, the accountants of the entities do not identify the benefits of the model, so that the efforts of the responsible agencies should focus on awareness raising and training in order to understand, analyse, apply and know the constraints of the international model. As a future research line, it is proposed to investigate with the objective of simplifying specific measurement processes based on specific conditions of the context
