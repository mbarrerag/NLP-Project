# VALUE OF THE TREATY ACCIDENT RISK IN PATIENTS IN PATIENTS IN NON-ONCOLOGIC CRONE PATIENTS FOR CHRONIC CRONIUM DOOR

Objective The management of chronic non-oncological pain with opioid analgesics has been of importance for the control of symptoms and the restoration of activity, however, the risk of addiction associated with these drugs is widely known and evaluated. This study evaluates the risk of addiction presented by patients with tramadol management describing the most frequent factors in the sample studied compared to what was reported in the literature. Methods A sample of 76 patients from a pain clinic who are in tramadol management and are given a questionnaire with demographic characteristics and with the Opioid Risk Tool scale for the risk of addiction. Results 57.89 % of the subjects were women, 55.20% were between 29 and 59 years of age. The risk of moderate addiction was found in 9.09 % of women and 37.05 % of men. The inclusion of other diseases such as anxiety and post-traumatic stress disorder increases the risk of addiction to severe in 6.06% of men. Conclusions The risk of opioid addiction should be considered in the factors found in the Colombian population
