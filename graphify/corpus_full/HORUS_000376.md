# The development of language as a mediator between child abuse and internalizing and externalizing behaviours in Colombian children and adolescents

It is largely unknown how child abuse (CM) affects child development, especially in low- and middle-income countries. To analyze whether the development of the average language is the relationship between the CM and internalizing and externalizing behaviours. Participants and context: 84 Colombian children and adolescents between 5 and 14 years old (M = 9.67, SD = 2.18); 50% with a history of CM and 50% without it, recruited in a protection institution and in the community, respectively. A quasi-experimental, cross-sectional study, with two groups. Two assessment instruments were applied: Child Neuropsychological Assessment Battery (ENI-2) and Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL). A mediation analysis was performed with regression of Ordinary Square Minimum (OLS) and logistics. The CM was associated with internalizing behaviors through language development (effect = 2.1243; CI [0.3184, 4.5415]; effect size = 0.473), but not directly related to language
