# Creation and piloting of a clinical protocol based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy for a mixed team of Ultimate Frisbee in Bogotá

The athletes have particular psychological needs that affect their performance within the training and competition scenario, but also on their general psychological well-being. The Contextual therapies propose interventional models for the behavioral aspects involved in performance, decision making, focus of attention and motivation. This project proposes the creation of a clinical protocol based on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and the mindfulness technique. It was implemented with a pre-experimental design without control group, in a sample men and women players of Ultimate Frisbee in the city of Bogotá. The protocol consists of 5 intervention sessions plus one of follow-up, applying pre-test, intermediate, post-test and follow-up measures, using the instruments AAQ-II, CFQ, VQ, MAAS and PTQ-1 to demonstrate the differences resulting from the application of the protocol.
