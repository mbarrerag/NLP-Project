# Model-based real-time monitoring of large-scale urban traffic networks for decision making.

In this thesis, a real-time urban traffic monitoring system is proposed and implemented for timely decision-making, which positively impacts the mobility and air quality of the city. This system emerges as an alternative to sensor-based solutions installed on the roads, as it takes advantage of data generated by Waze, a mobile application used for navigation within cities, through the smart phones of people who use road infrastructure. As part of the traffic monitoring system, FLEXI was developed (by its acronym of flow estimation based on collaborative data), which transforms the average speed and delay reported by Waze, into flows and tails of vehicles through a mathematical model. In addition, the traffic monitoring system merges data online, offline and collaborative in order to improve the accuracy of the estimates. The monitoring system, was implemented in real time for a major area of the city of Medellín - Colombia, which includes 38 semaphorized intersections. Such implementation required the taking of data in the field for the validation of the monitoring system, with which the result was obtained a mean error of 15 % and a sample of the vehicle size (the average of 1 % taken for the average vehicle)
