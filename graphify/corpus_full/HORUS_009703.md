# FROM DEVELOPING URBANITY TO NEOLIBERAL: PRODUCTION OF URBAN SPACE IN MEDELLIN (1947-2018)

From developmentalist to neoliberal urban planning: production of urban space in Medellín (1947-2018) is a search to understand the forms of urban planning that have modelled the city of Medellín since the mid-century XX, because in the context of urban planning in Colombia it has shown that it has a tradition to care for you in the formation, consolidation and implementation of regulatory and urban management frameworks that, although, derived from national urban public policies, have been adapted to the local scale of urban action depending on the interests at stake behind urban development. In this work the author is interested not only in the description of the spatial forms derived from the different types of urban planning in Medellín, but also in the understanding of the processes and strategies through which the different geohistoric periods of urban planning in the city have been restructured, structured and restructured, both developmental urban planning and neoliberal urbanism, studying each period through the mechanisms of regulation, management and financing and the strategies behind them that have shaped them
