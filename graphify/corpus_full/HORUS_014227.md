# STRATEGIES FOR DEVELOPING PRODUCTS VALUE TO PRODUCTION OF SMALL HABANERO AJI PRODUCERS (Capsicum chinense) IN THE VIANI MUNICIPE (CUNDINAMARK)

The project of Extension of Solidarity aims to co-create technical knowledge between the National University of Colombia and Asoforuvi - an association of agricultural and livestock farmers and marketers of Vianí. This non-profit organization was formed on January 29, 2014 and is formed by vulnerable communities of small farmers with low levels of education and income. Its social objective is to produce, transform and market agricultural and horticultural products. This community is interested in exploring alternatives for the development of products that add value to the production of ají habanero (Capsicum chinense). The community wants to learn and implement good practices of post-harvest management, dehydration of the product and design of sauces to expand their business opportunities and improve the quality of life of the beneficiary community. Through this project, we aim to foster a collaborative environment in which both the university and the association can share knowledge, skills and resources to achieve a common objective. By strengthening the capacities of these small farmers with technical knowledge and promoting sustainable practices, we hope to contribute to their economic and social development in the long term
