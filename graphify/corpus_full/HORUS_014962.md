# MULTIVARIATE ANALYSIS TO STUDY THE PERFORMANCE OF MASTER ́S AND DOCTORAL STUDENTS

A multivariate analysis is presented to identify significant relationships between the different factors involved in the admission and training process, and their impact on the academic performance of students who receive content and methodologies in relation to digital transformation. This study aims to identify the factors that affect the performance of graduate students. The institutional factor, the personal factor and the training factor will be considered. The theoretical lenses with which the research hypotheses are raised correspond to the theory of student development and the theory of social learning. This last theory is important, since learning is a cognitive process that takes place in a social context and can occur through observation or direct instruction (training factor)
