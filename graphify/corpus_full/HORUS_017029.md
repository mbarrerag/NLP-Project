# GENETIC RESOURCES FOR AMERICAS AND CARIBBEAN (SIRGEAC): AGROBIODIVERSITY, A WORLD OF OPPORTUNITIES IN TIMES OF CRISIS

The International Symposium on Genetic Resources for the Americas and the Caribbean: Is SIRGEC, in its thirteenth edition, brought together important researchers, scientists and technicians whose main focus is agrobiodiversity, as well as researchers in training, students and citizens interested in the preservation and sustainable use of this genetic heritage? With the slogan “Agrobiodiversity, a world of opportunities in times of crisis”, the first SIRGEC organized in Colombia was launched, and given the contingencies of the global pandemic caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, developed in a virtual way. The entities that led the organization of the event were the Colombian Corporation of Agricultural Research Agrosavia, the National University of Colombia and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development. The Bioversity International Alliance CIAT also joined the organization and design of the event. Funding came from Agrosavia, through the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development and the National University of Colombia. All these conditions allowed for the first time to offer a totally free event to all people who signed as lecturers, speakers or attendees
