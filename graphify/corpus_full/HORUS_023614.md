# Symmetries and reductions of Dirac-Jacobi structures.

In this text, the reduction of structures Dirac-Jacobi is studied on a differentiable variety under the presence of symmetries given by the action of a related Lie group. The main tool used is the so-called "homogenization trick" and the already known Dirac reductions. Two particular cases of Dirac-Jacobi reduction are shown; when there is presence of an application moment and the case when the base variety is provided with a 1-form of contact
