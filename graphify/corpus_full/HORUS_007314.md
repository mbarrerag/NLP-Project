# Search for marine microorganisms for the biocontrol of rice pyricularia oryzae

Rice is an essential cereal in the Colombian diet, but its production faces threats of phytopathogens, within which the fungus Pyricularia oryzae stands out, which can reduce the productivity of the crop up to 50%. For the management of rice diseases, resistant varieties, irrigation management, fertilisation and fungicides are used to combat these diseases. However, given the adaptability of piricularia, biological alternatives are sought for the management of pyriculariasis. This study focused on selecting microorganisms capable of controlling P. oryzae. It preselected 15 microorganisms, mainly from marine environments, and evaluated their antifungal activity through tests of direct confrontation, diffusion in agar plaque and tests in greenhouse. Eight isolates showed a control comparable to the fungicida Kazugamicin in direct confrontation.
