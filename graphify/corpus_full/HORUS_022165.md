# Complications and mortality in the management of urinary lithiasis in Colombia: Analysis of a retrospective national cohort.

Complications and mortality in the management of urinary lithiasis in Colombia: Analysis of a retrospective national cohort. Urolithiasis is a disease of high prevalence with an important association with complications and mortality, both in its presentation and in relation to the active management of the patient. The epidemiology surrounding this pathology, the complications and mortality of the different approaches to intervention is generally based on data from international series, not knowing the national data. The objective of this work is to determine mortality (30 and 90 days) and postoperative complications (30 days) of patients from the contributive regimen in Colombia who were treated actively for urinary lithiasis during the years 2016 and 2017.
