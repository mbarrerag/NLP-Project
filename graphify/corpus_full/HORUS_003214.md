# PEDAGOLOGICAL PAUTS RESTORATIVES FOR PREVENTION AND ATTENTION OF GENDER-BASED VIOLENCES AND SEXUAL VIOLENCES.

It is a pedagogical material that emphasizes processes of prevention of gender-based violence and sexual violence. It contributes to the reflection of coexistence on the university campus and the responsibility to implement educational actions to overcome crises and interpersonal conflicts and to establish pedagogical measures in cases that deserve to be restorative
