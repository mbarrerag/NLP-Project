# INNOVATION IN PROCESS AND PRODUCTS BASED ON POLICOLS FOR FOOD OBTAINATION WITH FUNCTIONAL CHARACTERISTICS

The special conditions of the Cundiboyacense highlands in terms of the productivity of bee-keeping pollen and the specialisation of beekeeping in this region, based on this natural resource, have motivated the realization of different research projects supported by the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development and the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, with the participation of scientific staff from the National University of Colombia, beekeepers and marketers, whose results are synthesized in the following work. This book shows the establishment of parameters of the process of collecting, adapting and drying apiculture pollen from the Cundiboyacense altiplano, as well as the design of a prototype dryer applicable to the conditions of the region. Finally, alternatives of development of food and functional beverages with added value are proposed, whether using the dehydrated pollen or by applying bioprocesses with lactic bacteria.
