# WHAT ARE THE COUNTRY¿S RESOURCES TO FACE THE POSSIBLE LOCAL PRODUCTION OF BIOSIMILARS?

The international event of Biosimilars LatAm 2023 provided us with several approaches towards the ultimate goal of producing biosimilars locally. Colombia, with 27 approved biosimilars, is already a key player in terms of access (close to Brazil), but productivity-wise, Colombia is still a work in progress
