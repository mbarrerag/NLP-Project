# COACHING FOR THE CHANGE OF ORGANIZATIONAL CULTURE

The Pontifical University Javeriana Cali and the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Palmira, present this work whose purpose is to advance in understanding the foundations of coaching beyond what traditionally circulates in this regard. This is especially true if one remembers that coaching as part of organizational dynamics is an input of the conservation of the order of the whole society. To that end, the topic is interwoven with two objects of study of long academic tradition, organizational culture and leadership.On this basis, coaching is linked to the identity of the organization and to the group relations.This means a step from the individual scope of coaching that then passes through the group scope of leadership and above to the collective reach proper of organizational culture.The particularities of each concept are considered in such a way that they pose a logical relationship between the three. Finally, it is argued that the organizational change takes shape in the organizational culture and expects a useful tool in the individual transformations that a leader can manage through coaching.
