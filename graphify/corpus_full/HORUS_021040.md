# Maintenance Heat Pump- PARR-marked calorimeter with plate 2266991 is an Isoperibolic oxygen pump calorimeter controlled by a microprocessor that is widely used to determine the energy content of food in both animal and human nutrition. The animal nutrition laboratory provides this service to both faculty professors and students, as well as professors from other faculties (Agrarian Sciences, Medicine, Science, Engineering). In the case of the faculty we estimate that at least 10 teachers and their students make regular use of this equipment. It is important to note that at Bogotá headquarters, this is the only equipment available for this purpose.

-The heat meter PARR with plate 2266991 is a calorimeter of oxygen pump Isoperibolica controlled by a microprocessor that is widely used to determine the energy content of the food in both animal and human nutrition. The laboratory of animal nutrition provides this service to both professors and students of the faculty area, as well as to professors of other faculties (Agrarian Sciences, Medicine, Science, Engineering). In the case of the faculty we estimate that at least 10 teachers and their students make regular use of this equipment. It is important to highlight that in the Bogotá headquarters, this is the only equipment available for this purpose. The calorimeter is located in the building 561-B, Animal Nutrition Laboratory, which has just completed a process of locative adequacy. The laboratory requires a maintenance and calibration of the equipment since its pumps have fulfilled the expected hours of use and the current measurements of the pumps and the energetic value of the same sample. Therefore, the team cannot be used despite a constant demand of this test. It is therefore essential to guarantee its maintenance and calibration that it guarantees its functional and the results are accurate and reliable
