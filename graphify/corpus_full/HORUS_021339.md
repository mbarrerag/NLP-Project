# BIORREFINERIES FROM DOCUMENTATION, EXTENSION AND RESEARCH (BioR-DEI)--

The cooperation network _x0093_BIORREFINERIES FROM THE DOCENCE, THE EXTENSION AND THE INVESTIGATION (BioR-DEI)_x0094_ of the National University of Colombia seeks to generate collaborative research and dissemination work around biorefinerías, under the perspective of the Sustainable Development Goals-ODS and green chemistry, attenuating the border between teaching and extension-research that occurs in our institution; the above with a view to: (i) impacting regional and national different actors and agro-food productive chains, and (ii) training and strengthening, in the field of the SDGs, young researchers and professionals. The BioR-DEI Network will be formed by researchers from the headquarters of Bogotá, Medellín, Orinoquía, Palmira and Tumaco. The link that the BioR-DEI Network will generate among the researchers of the UN aims: (i) Beneficiate research groups, researchers and the different actors that integrate the National R-DEI Network, facilitating access to the other institutions and the national R-DEI Network. sdgs, green chemistry, and the strategic institutional plan (plei 2034) and the global development plan (pgd) 2022-2024 of the national university of colombia. --
