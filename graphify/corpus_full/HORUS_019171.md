# RESEARCH TRENDS IN DISEASE IN CANCER PERSONS

Cancer is a prevalent disease in Latin America. Nursing professionals should recognize the priorities and developments related to research on the care of the person with cancer. To describe the research trends in nursing in people with cancer from studies published in the top 10 journals of nursing research in Latin America. An exploratory literature review was carried out. Information was sought in the top 10 journals classified in Scimago Journal & Country rank for 2020, organized in Q1 to Q4 quartiles, during the period 2016-2020 using the terms cancer patients, cancer patient, cancer patients, cancer. 67 researches were selected after the process of critical evidence. From the review emerged the following topics: meaning of the experience of the disease and treatment process, quality of life of the person with cancer in Latin America, impact of cancer on the physical and psychosocial dimension, coping with the disease and perception of nursing care care. The studies analyzed showed two main trends in nursing research in Latin America related to the type of cancer and the research phenomenon
