# Rural ETNOGRAPHICAL EXPERIENCES ON COVID-19 IN COLOMBIA

The presence of Covid 19 among the peasants of Colombia showed various strategies in which this population understands and attends to their respiratory infections. It is important to recognize these strategies and their socio-sanitary implications. Understand the socio-sanitary strategies implemented by the peasant population of Colombia against Covid 19. ethnographic research carried out with peasant communities and organizations in eight regions of the country (Amazonia, Orinoquía, Cafetera, Magdalena Medio, Caribbean, Chocó Biogeographical, Altiplano Cundiboyacense, and Surocidente Andino), process where several research tools were used (field diary, participant observation and interviews). The health system is scarcely consistent with the health needs of the rural population. The government strategy against Covid 19 was designed on the social control in urban areas and rejected the modes of life of the peasant areas. The social organizations have oriented mechanisms of solidarity and social control during the pandemic, and also have generated socio-political debates on the strategies faced by this disease, including the vaccination. The rural population of the country has been affected by the armed conflict, the disease and the disease itself
