# Frontality and twisting: duality in the Giraldo Building of Fernando Martínez Sanabria (1958)

This master's thesis confronts the concepts of frontality and torsion as a duality that affects the conception and perception of the architectural form. In this analysis, the frontality-torsion interaction —where the first manifests a direction and an image and the second a change of direction and counterpoint— acts as a dual condition that structures the character and complexity in architecture from a relationship of tension or equilibrium.
