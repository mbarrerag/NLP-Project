# Synthesis of bioMOFs from phenolic acids present in agro-industrial cocoa residues as antioxidant therapeutic agents

In this doctoral thesis, initially describes the synthesis of a novel bioMOF and the optimization of the synthesis a bioMOF already reported. Synthesis that parallelly led to the obtaining of an additional material, a structure of coordination of zirconium, with attractive structural characteristics for the area of catalysis. In all these cases, we looked for conditions of scalable synthesis and environmentally friendly using phenolic acids (commercial reactives) present in agro-industrial cocoa residues. Considering this last, the process of purification of phenolic acids, especially galic acid and protocatéquic acid, was optimized from the extracts obtained from these residues. Additionally, the potential of the bioMOFs synthesized as systems of controlled release of ligants and molecules with therapeutic interest such as curcumin and rollmin, and opposite to cellular panels, one of them of high resistance, where cytotoxicity was determined against each synthesized material.
