# DEMOCRATIC OPENING OR MAJOR SELF-GOVERNMENT CLOSURE. DISJUNCTIVE AND PERSPECTIVE AFTER THE COLOMBIA PEACE AGREEMENT

The text explores the basic historical conditions of structuring the "democratic order"; it sets out the contents of the peace agreement concerning the question of democracy, approaching a general assessment of the state of implementation, and it deals with a presentation of the recent configurations of the political process based on a characterization of the government of Iván Duque, the identification of an ongoing fascistization process and the recent trends of resistances and struggles
