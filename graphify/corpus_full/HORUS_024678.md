# ERGONOMY IN HEALTH SYSTEMS: THE ERGONOMUS AS PATIENT AND ACUDENT

The safety of the patient, the use of medical devices, processes that are carried out in health institutions, as well as the health systems themselves, are topical and have been addressed to a greater or lesser extent by ergonomics, being for the most part studies carried out by ergonomos in which they act as observers of the condition, without necessarily having lived such a situation. This article shows another facet of these events, based on the experiences of two ergonomes that, being in the situations of patients or accudiente, reflect on what they experienced in several processes of disease that they had to face and overcome. The analysis material is the anecdotes recorded by the ergonomom, as a phenomenological - hermeneutic method, from which reflections arise on the implications of the disease care processes for the restoration of the health of people, from an ethical approach. The recognition of anecdotes as research instruments in ergonomics, overcomes the hegemonic approach of that discipline, outlining the usefulness that this approach can have to contribute to the identification of categories relevant to the improvement of health systems from an ethical perspective
