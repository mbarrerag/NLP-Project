# Economic evaluation model for balanced general anesthesia versus total intravenous anesthesia (IVA).

General anesthesia is an anesthetic technique essential for the care of most patients who are taken to surgery. Although the cost of anesthesia represents a lower percentage compared to the total cost of surgical patient care, the large number of procedures performed represents a significant total cost for any health system. This research aims to quantify and compare the direct costs of general anesthesia techniques balanced with isoflurane, sevoflurane and desflurane, and general intravenous anesthesia in Colombia. An economic analysis was made, type minimization of costs, based on an economic model constructed with the variables determining the differential costs for general anesthesia techniques, using values that fit the Colombian context. 10,000 possible scenarios were simulated using the Montecarlo statistical method and it was found that on average the general anesthesia balanced with desflurane was the most costly of all techniques, the total intravenous anesthesia was only cheaper than the general anesthesia balanced with desflurane and the anesthetic technique was cheaper was the is the isoflurane, followed by the sevoflurane and the TIV. The main difference between the techniques used for the administration of general anesthesia, the above, should be taken for the most efficient anesthesia
