# NUTRITIONAL QUALITY AS A CERTAIN FACTOR OF THE INEGRAL QUALITY OF MILK PRODUCTS

The sensory and microbiological quality have prevailed as indicators of the integral quality of dairy products. However, the situation of the pandemic revealed that one of the challenges for the food industry is the supply of products that really contribute to the adequate nutrition and maintenance of people’s health. In light of the above, it is necessary to include nutritional quality as a critical indicator for production and in the choice of food by consumers, therefore, the aim of this research was to analyze the current state of literature on the nutritional quality of dairy products. The objective was addressed through the methodology of systematic literature review. The findings also suggested that fat is one of the most important components of milk by contributing to the neuronal development and protection against bacterial infections of newborns, because it has bioactivity in the brain function and immune system and because it is a source of energy for different organs and systems. On the other hand, it is also identified that, with relation to proteins, beta-casein A1 is only present in cattle, while variant A2 is present in the milk of many mammals that allow the most human products to be identified
