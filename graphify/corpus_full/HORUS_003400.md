# HYPEROSMOTIC STIMULATIONS INDUSTRY MODIFICATIONS IN THE FOSFILIATION AND ACTION OF TRPV1 IN SYLLAR CELLS TO HUMAN ODONTOBLASTS

The hyperosmolar sweet foods in injured teeth cause intense dental pain. Odontoblasts are considered signal transducers through some channels, including TRPV1 and the channel phosphorylations are related to sensitization processes. The objective was to evaluate if the mannitol hyperosmotic stimuli over OLCs, can activate TRPV1 and if those acute stimuli modify the receptor phosphorylation. OLCs were sown in plates using supplemental DMEM and were stimulated with a hyperosmotic solution of mannitol (370 mOsm/L), for a time of 5, 15, 30 or 60 min. Subsequently they were smoothed and with the protein extract Western blot for TRPV1 and phosphorylized TRPV1 was performed.
