# OPTIMAL LABOR FORCE IN A AGROFORESTRY SYSTEM

In 1971, Clark Colin proposed a model which it is concluded that extinction is due to the low growth rates of resources and the high price-cost relation of harvesting. In some cases, growth rates cannot be controlled, so it is search to maneuver the relationship in order to control extinction. That is, the probability of extinction can be reduced through policies that cause a decrease in the price/cost ratio (Swanson,1994). In this article the existence of a solution to a problem of optimal control of renewable resources is studied and some numerical results of the same are studied which say that it would be good to invest in agriculture
