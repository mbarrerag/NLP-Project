# The Intelligibility of Pre-Garden Children's Speech: Program of Pedagogical Intervention with Families at Carlos Albán Holguín School

The research that this document gives is based on the teacher's observation, in which she notes a real development of the language of children with some delay in relation to the typical development of language according to age (3 – 4 years). To evaluate the result of a pedagogical intervention program with families aimed at favoring the development of the intelligibility of the speech of children who attend the pre-garden degree in the Carlos Albán Holguín School IED, Morning. The research approach is mixed concurrent with exploratory scope. It is possible to identify an improvement of approximately 21 % in the base group and in the control group. Analysis and discussion: The increase was influenced by two uncontrolled variables that are related to this result: the intervention of the teacher in the classroom and an institutional project of parent school that reached both the participating students and the non-participants in the analysis of strengthening strategies for the integral development of children. The intervention achieved both in the family routines opening the informed communication spaces, allowing the participation of children and improving their participation in the sector.
