# BENEFICIATION AND SOURCING OF RARE EARTH MINERALS FROM ALLIVIAL BLACK SANDS

The supply of rare earth elements and the location of stable sources of these are one of the major geopolitical, economic and technological challenges of our time, taking into account their strategic importance for the manufacture of components for energy systems and advanced technology mechanisms. Due to this search, minerals associated with alluvial deposits of gold, known as pleasures, have received considerable attention since they can contain significant quantities of these elements in minerals such as monacita and xenotima. The benefit of these minerals, however, represents a challenge taking into account the diversity of minerals present in these deposits, and which are grouped together into a material generally known as black sands. In this work, advances are presented in the study of recovery and characterization of the currents of a gold alluvial operation located in the northwest of the Department of Antioquia, with a high potential as sources of rare earth elements and with a view to the possible installation of a complementary process that allows its concentration within the parameters of the current operation. The study included the determination of the current with greater potential of rare earth, and the application of different techniques of separation and characterization.
