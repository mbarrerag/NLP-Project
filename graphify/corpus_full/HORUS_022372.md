# CONTEXT FOR A PHILOSOPHY OF EDUCATION AND PHILOSOPHY EDUCATION IN LATIN AMERICA

The text presents the philosophical foundations with which to shape a Latin American philosophy that supports a philosophy of education from popular education. This is based on the examination of the philosophical reflection of Leopoldo Zea and Amado Osorio. To this end, it develops: 1) around the alleged existence of a Latin American philosophy, 2) Latin American philosophical thought, 3) an alleged agreement on philosophy, 4) a philosophy of education in Latin America, 5) a philosophy of education as a bastion of a philosophical education in Latin America. The text concludes, moreover, that a Latin American philosophy does not have to have as a condition its rupture with the thinking of generic man; it is only a question of recognizing its vital problems to provide reflective clarity and act on them with answers, even inspiring for other peoples
