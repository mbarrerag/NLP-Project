# HEGEMONY OF THE GREAT CORPORATIONS AND THE TRANSNALATIONALIZE OF CAPITAL

The article establishes first a conceptual characterization of the decision-making process of big corporations and international companies. Hymer’s theoretical work (1979) has been used to do this, who has argued that this form of transnational organization is the dominant structure of contemporary capitalism or of capitalism really existing. This analysis is complemented by the idea that what the company seeks to maximize is its hegemony, thus building a historical bloc between foreign and national capital based on strategic alliances. This allows the company to subvert its power through corruption, investment policies and coercion as a means of appropriating profits, while limiting market competition and coercing national democracy
