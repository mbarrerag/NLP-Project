# I'd have to say something about words - talk about contemporary poetry

One should say something about words is highlighted by its rhythm in poetry, contemporary sulliorism and its irony in the face of reality. It is a nove- dosa proposal for the author’s interest in taking poetry out of the margins of the poem, by its obsession with the reader and by the way in which it involves him to put it at his service, through a game of me-taparas. In this sense, it is a wink between author and reader, with which he intends to cleanse the world with words, to make a prayer on the mountain for life. Finally, it is worth saying that it is a book in which there is a transmutation between life and death, the longing, of the past through the path of spiritual hunger, where the useless trophy, solitude falls upon houses stripped of eternity
