# Technological diagnosis as a tool of strategic planning. Case study: technology-based company.

The aim of this study is to make a diagnosis and evaluation of the technological capabilities of a technology-based company, identifying its strengths and weaknesses, in order to contribute to the formulation of strategic plans that allow it to achieve and maintain a competitive advantage. To this end, the hierarchical analysis process is used to evaluate technological capabilities, as well as each of its key indicators individually. Subsequently, through structured interviews, the performance of the organization in the outstanding elements is reviewed, allowing them to weigh them according to the relative weights discovered through hierarchical analysis, thus determining the level of technological capabilities of the company
