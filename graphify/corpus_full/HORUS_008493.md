# ASSESSMENT OF THE PAPA HAIR AS A MEAN FOR THE PRODUCTION OF LACASE BY FERMENTATION IN SOLID STATE

Summary Countering the pollution of water bodies and colored fauna represents one of the great environmental challenges facing the current governments. Textile and food companies are the main responsible for the discharge of coloured effluents to rivers and lagoons. These colours, given their recalcitrant nature, cause a series of damages that affect the dynamic balance of flora and fauna. Biological procedures have become increasingly strong for the treatment of these colored effluents. In particular, biotechnology, which makes use of micro-organisms, proposes the use of liginolytic enzymes for the degradation of these synthetic colours, where the fungi of white rot has shown satisfactory results.
