# The Caqueteño livestock landscape. Deforestation and privatization of the Amazon rainforests: 1933-2018

This research analyzes the process of configuration of an “extra-farming landscape of cattle” between 1933-2018, in the current department of Caquetá, Colombia, which is created and superimposed on a strip of the pristine Amazon, when the Colombian south-eastern forests are incorporated into the national and international economic dynamics (capitalism); that is, the destruction of the ecosystem biodiversity by means of the implementation of an extractivist capitalism, by means of the expansion of the national agricultural frontier towards this part of the Colombian territory. This has been a process of appropriation and colonization that, on the one hand, generates the privatization of millions of hectares of waste forests by large landowners, farmers and, on the other hand, the consolidation of a livestock landscape as a dominant element of the rurality of the Caquetano 2018, which also involves the establishment of a primary population that will be established – throughout the studied period – in areas that are incorporated through the formation of rozas and minifunds in which there were previously jungles, canangús and other types of rural areas (the Amazonian, the latter). ben a process of acceptance and colonization that, on the one hand, generates the privatization of millions of hectares of uncultured forests by large landowners, ranchers, and smallholders and, on the other, the consolidation of a hunting-raising landscape as the dominant element of Caquetá rurality. This also details the establishment of a primary settlement, which will gradually establish—over the period studied—in the areas that are being incorporated through the formation of cleanings and smallholdings where forests, canangchales, and other types of Amazonian werelands previously existing.
