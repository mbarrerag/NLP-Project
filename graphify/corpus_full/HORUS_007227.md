# Determinants of the growth of air passenger transport in Colombia 2005 - 2022

Over the last years the Colombian passenger air transport market has presented an extraordinary growth, among others, due to a stable and growing economic environment, and the liberalization of the commercial air transport policy. These conditions have generated an adequate environment for the entry of new competitors with innovative and disruptive business models, which allowed to increase the supply and increasingly affordable the services of the aviation sector, allowing the users of the air transport to access better conditions in a country characterized by its extension and geographical difficulties. Taking into account the hypotheses developed throughout the document was estimated a model of vector of correction of errors VECM to determine the short and long term relations between the number of passengers mobilized and the gross domestic product.
