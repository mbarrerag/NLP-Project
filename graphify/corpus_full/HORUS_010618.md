# Design and construction of a multi-element phased array radio interferometer.

This paper presents the implementation and optimization of a novel design of a three-element interferometer radius with a temporal correlation system to observe solar emissions in the radio band, in a bandwidth around the neutral hydrogen line or HI line to 1.42GHz. For the implementation and testing part, it is proposed to perform astronomical observations in conjunction with series of measurements to determine the sectors of the sky with less interference as well as to verify the correct functioning of the radio interferometer. In addition, it is a novel instrument design, which opens the door to perform new radio emission studies in the National Astronomic Observatory, with data collected locally, using it as a scientific research tool whose use can extend beyond Solar Astrophysics to other cosmic radio sources, contributing to the development of a discipline that is emerging in Colombia. It is also intended that the instrument be a form of pedagogical support and to foster interdisciplinary collaborations in the country
