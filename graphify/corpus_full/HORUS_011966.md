# MARINE ARTESANAL FISHERIES IN COLOMBIAN PACIFIC - PERCEPTIONS AND CHALLENGE TO CLIMATE CHANGE

To think about the perceptions and challenges that arise from climate change in the national territory, it raises the need to link directly with the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the 2030 Agenda for Colombian people, in which fisheries and aquaculture contribute in one way or another in the scope of these goals; having a special importance in SDG 14: Underwater life - to preserve and sustainably use oceans, seas and marine resources for sustainable development - (UN, 2015).Since 1961, the annual global growth of fish consumption has doubled population growth, demonstrating that the fishing sector is fundamental to achieving the goal of the fao: A world without hunger and malnutrition (fao, 2018). In total world production, fish reached a historical maximum of 179 million tons in 2018, considering the capture and cultivation of fish in marine and continental waters (UN 2020; fao, 2020)
