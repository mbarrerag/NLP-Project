# Power asymmetries and emancipation of rights: Colombian constitutional structure and limits to corporate power as a dialogue between democracy, economy and environment.

This research focused on the reconstruction of the role of human rights in the face of power asymmetries between the State, Companies and communities emphasizing its potential as limits to corporate power. To this end, a reflection on rights was developed from different approaches such as historical memory, cost-benefit analysis and the characterization of corporate power. It also deepened in the Colombian constitutional model and developed a theory called constitutionalization of barriers to rights. Finally, several disputed spaces were addressed where power asymmetries are expressed in conflicts such as those generated between the right to development, citizen participation, the right to territory, extractivism and corporate accountability in transitional justice scenarios
