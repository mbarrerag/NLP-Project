# EFFICIENCY OF STAFF UNDERTAKINGS IN COLOMBIA DURING THE PERIOD 2017-2020

The footwear sector is strategic for Colombia because of its potential to compete internationally contributing to regional economic development, however, the coronavirus pandemic (COVID-19) has generated an unprecedented economic crisis seriously affecting these types of companies that were confronted with restrictions, logistical problems, decrease in exports, sales falls and consumer distrust. The aim of this work was to evaluate the efficiency of footwear companies in Colombia during the period 2017-2020 through a Data Enveloping Analysis (DEA) and bootstrap. The results show that companies, despite the pandemic, achieved outstanding technical efficiency levels with an average of 93.5%. However, in 2020 a statistically significant drop in technical efficiency was found. Likewise, the opportunity for improvement in inputs was detected for reducing efficiency gaps
