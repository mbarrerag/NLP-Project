# CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES FOR HUMAN TALENT MANAGEMENT IN ORGANIZATIONS: PSYCHOSOCIAL RISKS.

This book is a contribution to the field of organizational psychology and related areas. It sought to give a broad look at the challenges and opportunities that human talent management has in the face of psychosocial risks. Each of the chapters addressed in this work shows from different perspectives and variables that are immersed in psychosocial risk, tools that allow to evaluate it, diagnose it and some proposals to carry out an intervention.
