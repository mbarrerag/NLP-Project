# THE RISK OF OTHERS (NO) EXISTING: BUILDING A CAMPO, CREATING POSSIBLE BRONTERS

This chapter is an ethnographic reflection, made from political, research and as formators of public policy throughout about twenty years of history of the authors, on the existence of people outside the norms of sexuality and gender, which is commonly known as LGBTI collectivities and, at the same time, an attempt to narrate in ethnographic and analytical record part of the memory of the construction of political and knowledge fields at the state level and in the academy in Colombia, which are now part of the terrain in contingency in which the material and symbolic existence of these persons and collectivities is played in part
