# Tuning confined states in Si1−xGex photonic cavities

In this work, using the transfer matrix method, we calculate the transmittance spectrum in a defective one-dimensional photonic crystal with coupled cavities. The proposed heterostructure has been designed using intercalated Si and SiO 2 materials with a coupling cavity of Si 1−x Ge x doped material. The results show the existence of edge states that tune to longer wavelengths in the transmittance spectrum as both the germanium concentration and the angle of incidence of light increase. We observe that the energy stored in the cavity is higher for transverse electric polarization, with a maximum quality factor Q of 8.5×10 3 . Finally, we find that by inserting a mirror heterostructure of the topological one-dimensional photonic crystal, we can investigate the coupling of the topological states. We observe that the degeneracy breakup occurs for angles less than 85° when the incident angle is increased
