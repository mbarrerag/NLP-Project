# WORK HEALTH FIGHTS AT WORK IN COLOMBIA

This book presents the results of a doctoral research that studies the collective action undertaken by sectors of workers in Colombia, to demand their rights linked to health at work at the beginning of the 21st century. It asks why, how and what for this action, from the structure and the collective subject, is a reflection. It locates the framework of action of this subject; the identities, demands and repercussions configured; the closures and openings of political opportunities; the organizational form adopted; the repertoire of actions undertaken; the resources for action; the configuration of allies and networks, as well as the definition of adversaries, which established a political contest for the recognition of the labor origin of the health damage, and the labor, health and social security rights that derive from this recognition. The research shows three realities. On the one hand, the changes in the world of work in recent decades, the result of the labor and management in the field of work and the forms of labor protection, which have intensified and prolonged the work, forming a toxic work scenario in which the confrontation between capital and work leads to the failure of work, as well as the removal of the health from work and the health that is the protection of the health in the work and control of the work. The public is also aware that work, besides being a determinant of health, is the substantial element that organizes the life of society, establishing the dynamics of production and social reproduction of human groups, so that there is a close relationship of the forms of work with the processes of health-disease of society, not only with accidents and occupational diseases. Likewise, it asks that it understands that collective organization and action is a powerful determinant in the protection and production of health, so it must be taken into account in its practice
