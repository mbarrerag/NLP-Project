# Wide-ranging systematic review: Safety and effectiveness of hormonal contraception versus non-hormonal contraception methods or non-use of contraception methods for women of reproductive age with desire for future fertility and high cardiovascular risk

In recent years, the age of women at the time of childbirth has increased. The advanced age in turn is a risk factor for the appearance of chronic diseases. It is necessary to evaluate the safety and effectiveness of contraceptive methods in women with chronic diseases and high cardiovascular risk. To evaluate the safety and effectiveness of hormonal contraceptive methods versus non-hormonal methods or non-use of contraception in women of reproductive age with desire for future fertility and arterial hypertension.
