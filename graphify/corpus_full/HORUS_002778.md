# POLICE RESOURCES USED BY MEGACHILIDAE BABIES IN SIX PARKS AND CERTAIN TUTELARIES OF THE VALLE DE BOURRA

Urban expansion acts as an environmental filter that can limit the development of species and biological diversity. Lonely beebees like those of the Megachilidae family, have been particularly affected by the unstoppable growth of cities that affects their forage strategies, type of food, sociability and type of nesting. This, together with the present climate crisis, makes it necessary to advance in the knowledge of fauna and flora, to provide inputs that allow to generate effective strategies for the conservation and preservation of the ecosystem services provided by pollinators. This study seeks to characterize the floral resources used by some individuals of the Megachilidae family from pollen collected by them. To this, monthly sampling with jama during a year, in six protected areas of the Valley of Aburrá, in the department of Antioquia. The 44 collected specimens were preserved in alcohol and were taken samples of pollen from 20 individuals. These samples were washed with KOH at 10%, were mounted in plaques and the habit were counted 500 pollen grains per individual.
