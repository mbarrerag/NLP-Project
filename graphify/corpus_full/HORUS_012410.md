# Experiences of women and men with visual impairments who practice goalball in Boyacá: contributions from co-visuality.

This research has its basis in critical studies on disability, which have welcomed the influence on the studies of colonial, gender, queer, crypt, ethnic rational, epistemologies Other, epistemologies of the south, besides the areas of human development, the functional and institutional differences, the social sciences, the economic sciences and the arts, therefore it finds a diversity of debates, around the embodied experiences, the experiences wasted, the dissidential bodies, the sensoryities, the Corazonar, the otherness, the alterity, the other voices and the Co-visuality.Considering these theoretical and conceptual bases, it calls to interweave together from the experiences located to co-construe new emancipatory meanings and shorten the “elements epistemics” (Mumenvar, 2013a).The objective of the research is to be directed in Corazonar the practices of Goalball with women and men from the visual diversity and the experiences located in Boyacá.As regards the methodology, this research was bowed to the qualitative approach and the functional classifications, regulations, the tendency of the medicated and institutionalized sport, para-sport as a tool of biopolitical control of life itself in the bodies of the Otredad, diversity in sport, etc., all of them are framed in 4 categories: Corazonar, visualities, markers of difference and goalball. The main reflection has shown that, people with visual diversity in Colombia suffer oppressions within and outside of sports practice, from social, cultural and political imaginary, mainly from the system of values perpetuated around capacitism, androcentrism and productivity. That is to say, those bodies that do not comply to a large extent with the obligatory body integrity, are devalued, excluded and led to institutionalization to “try” to recover those capacities and normative standards among other important reflections
