# CRITICAL ACCOUNTING IN LATIN AMERICA: TRAYECTORIES, INTERACTIONS AND CONVERSATIONS BETWEEN NORTHERN AND SOUTH

To introduce the special issue, this document aims to contextualize readers who are unfamiliar with critical accounting and its development, local and international, and to provide some bibliographic sources that may be valuable for initiation and research in this field.
