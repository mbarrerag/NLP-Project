# GENETIC IMPROVEMENT OF OXG INTER-SPECTIVE HIBRICULTIVE CULTURES

The competitive and sustainable development of oil palm in Colombia needs the generation of new cultivars, which must integrate characteristics of high production and high quality oil, adaptation to different environments and re-sistence to diseases. The sustainability of palm cultivation in Colombia is strongly threatened by the disease called rotting of the bud (PC), which reaches epidemic incidences and causes the loss of thousands of hectares of the crop. Currently, the only proven sources of resistance come from the interspecific hybrids between Elaeis oleifera and Elaeis guinensis, known as OxG hybrids. A program of improvement of interspecific hybrids OxG must count with genetic resources that represent the genetic variability of the two species, with the aim of evaluating, selecting and, subsequently, helping to increase traits of interest, as are the production of clusters and oil, the quality of the oil in terms of vitamin E and carotenos, the slow growth of the estipite and the resistance to the PC, among other characteristics of agronomic value, the present ones are discussed
