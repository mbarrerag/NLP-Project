# THE STUDY OF NATIONAL UNIVERSITY FACRES GENDER VIOLENCES AND TAKES RESISTANCE MEASURES

The article is the result of a research that was done with students from the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, and the continuum of violence is evident in the situations that the student relates, especially in the family, public settings and in the same institutional dynamics. Among the main results are recognized the actions of resistance and the various proposals to face conflicts and multiple violence
