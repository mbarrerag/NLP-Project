# PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE AS A LABOUR LIFE immersive tool

The immersion in working life through professional practice is considered by mechanical engineering students as one of the most important phases in the closing of the undergraduate training cycle. At the National University of Colombia, professional practice is considered a modality of the subject grade work, by which the student strengthens, applies and develops his or her research capacity, creativity and work discipline for the treatment of a specific problem, applying the knowledge and methods acquired in the curriculum. This study was carried out in a population of 195 mechanical engineering students who developed their professional practice between 2017-2020, an average of 49 students/year. It was found that 10% of the practices were performed by women and that 84% were carried out in private sector companies. It is emphasized the participation of practitioners in the specific economic activities of food (11%), public services (10 per cent), automotive (9%), plastic manufacturing (8%), metal mechanics (8%), engineering services (8%) and construction (8%). 40% of the trainees were performed in the maintenance departments; 68% of the trainees were performed in the secondary economic sector and 27% in services.
