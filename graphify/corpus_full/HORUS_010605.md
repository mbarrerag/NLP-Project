# SUBJECTS AND OBJECTS ON TIKUNA

In this article, we present a close approach to grammatical relations in tikuna, particularly around the marking of subjects and objects. First, we outline a proposal related to the existence of a media voice in tikuna based on the differential marking of subjects of certain verbs and, on this basis, we discuss the possible existence of features of intransitivity scinded in this language. Secondly, some proposals are collected and discussed around the codification of the object through different case marks (Facó-Soares, 2000; Montes, 2004; Skilton, 2016) and are proposed, based on field data, precisions and details necessary to address the direct object marking in a typological framework. The hierarchy of animacity as well as the semantics of a limited number of verbs affect the selection of casual marks assigned (or not) to the object
