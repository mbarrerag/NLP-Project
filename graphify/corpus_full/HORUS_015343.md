# Teaching Scenarios Facing the Digital Transformation of Higher Education Institutions

Higher Education institutions have been influenced by the technological advance brought by the Industrial Revolution 4.0, which forces them to face a digital transformation in all its dimensions. The objective of this research is to identify which are the different teaching scenarios facing the digital transformation in Higher Education Institutions. After a systematic literature review on the subject, and after analyzing the 19 articles classified for total reading, it is evident that the digital transformation in Higher Education Institutions has been developed from different dimensions, especially from teaching, responding to individual, multiple, separate and / or uncoordinated initiatives through institutional digital transformation policies. As a consequence, there is a latent risk of not considering comprehensive solutions that give an assertive response to the current and future requirements of institutions that allow them to appropriate, and take advantage of, the new challenges framed in the fourth industrial revolution
