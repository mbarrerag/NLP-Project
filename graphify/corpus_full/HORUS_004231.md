# Recognition of phytoparasitic nematodes associated with chontaduro (Bactris gasipae Kunth) cultivation of the Bajo Calima Buenaventura - Valle del Cauca

The chontaduro (Bactris gasipae Kunth) is a perennial crop of the Colombian Pacific, it is considered a crucial source for the economy and food. However, it faces different plant protection problems caused by microbial agents and pest insects, whose dynamics and distribution are mostly unknown. With regard to phytoparous nematodes, there is little information and research that needs the ecological, parasitic, population, morphological and morphometric parameters of the genera of the most important in the cultivation of chontaduro, with the aim of providing management strategies for farmers of the district of Buenaventura.
