# OPTIMMATION OF THE ELECTROCHEMICAL PEROXIDATION PROCESS FOR THE TREATMENT OF AN EFLUENT OF THE TEXTILE INDUSTRY POLLUTED WITH BLACK COLORANT ACID 194 PRETRATATED BY COAGULATION/FLOCULATION

This work evaluated the capacity of the sequential process Coagulation/Floculation-E electrochemical peroxidation (C/F-ECP) for the degradation of organic matter contained in an effluent of the textile industry contaminated with black acid colouring 194. The effluent studied presented physical and chemical characteristics that exceeded Colombian environmental standards for different parameters, such as the Chemical Oxygen Demand (DQO=3270 mg/L), Biological Oxygen Demand (DBO5=648.2 mg/L), among others, being a non-biodegradable and highly coloured effluent (DBO5/DQO=0.2 and 45850 U. Pt-Co, respectively).
