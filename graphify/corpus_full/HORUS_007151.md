# Product design for the valuation of sugarcane syrup. Study case: Formulation of cereal bars

The consumption of cereal bars has been increasing over the years due to a higher demand for natural, integral, functional and nutritious foods. Generally, the cereal bars are composed mainly of dried and dehydrated fruits, cereals, vegetable oils and sugars or sweeteners. During the conformation of the bars, the solids mixture is agglomerated using a highly viscous liquid, mainly glucose syrup, which also acts as a sweetener agent. However, the glucose syrup is a synthetic product that can cause various health problems, such as obesity or cardiovascular diseases. To solve this problem, glucose syrup could be replaced by healthier and naturally-originated substitutes, such as honey bee, syrups extracted from different plants (e.g. agave, maple, birch) and concentrated syrups obtained from the processing of sugar cane juices (e.g. melaza or panela syrup) or from other sugar-rich plants (e. beet, sorghum, dátil, coconut and palm Palmyrah). These potential substitutes are considered as suitable for this function, already functional foods.
