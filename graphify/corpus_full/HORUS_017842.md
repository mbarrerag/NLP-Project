# CONFLICT OF LIMITS AND USE OF HISTORICAL CARTOGRAPHS: THE CASE OF BELEN FROM ANTIOQUEÑA

Between February and June 2017, there was intense controversy in traditional media, virtual media, social networks and political spheres, not only regional, but also national, due to the announcement made by the Agustín Codazzi Geographical Institute (igac) of the publication of a map of the department of Chocó, which would include the small town called Belén de Bajirá, which also claims as part of its territory the neighbouring department of Antioquia. This announcement was intended to conclude a border dispute of several years between these two departments, located in the north-western corner of Colombia, which had even been brought before the Territorial Ordering Commissions of the Chamber and the Senate of the Republic, competent law agencies in Colombia to settle this type of litigation. The text follows up the maps and narratives used in this controversy by the authorities and people who argued in favour of the department of Antioquia
