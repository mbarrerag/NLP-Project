# CLINICAL PRACTICES OF VOCATIONAL TRAINING.

Professional training practices guide and introduce students into the logic of the professional field of work, they approximate them to the stage of the chosen profession. Studies show the adverse effects of anxiety during professional training practices on the academic results of students in the health area (high levels of anxiety generate lower academic performance). Dentistry is classified as one of the most stressful careers, in which students can experience high levels of anxiety during their training practices. Therefore, it takes importance to investigate anxiety, its manifestations and effects in students. To understand, from the experiences experienced, the phenomenon of anxiety faced by students of dentistry of a Colombian university during the development of clinical practices of professional training. Study with a qualitative approach of a phenomenological type, its main focus was the experience of participants in the field of anxiety during clinical training practices. The study showed that the teacher-student relationship is guided mainly by professionalism and/or vocation by teachers, and is perceived as a relevant strength/limitant in the optimal development of practices. The established model of clinics, the implications that this entails and the difficult adaptation to it, is directly related to the presence of anxiety and this with negative emotions, under academic performance and limitations in the full development of capacities for clinical practices of professional training
