# EFFECT OF THE CONDITIONS OF THE BIORAACTOR CULTIVE ON BIOMASS PRODUCTION AND SHIITAKE ESTEROL CONTENT

Lentinula edodes is a basidiomycete fungus commonly known as shiitake, its biomass has an excellent nutritional value and contains metabolites that generate benefits to human health. Such is the case of sterols, which among other biological activities contribute to the reduction of cholesterol levels. This fungal biomass can be produced by the use of culture submerged in bioreactors, which allows the maintenance of controlled conditions, which in turn favours the efficiency of the process, and provides information necessary to perform the scaling. However, the concentration of biomass and metabolites produced in the crop is influenced by the composition of the medium and the levels of aeration used, which makes it necessary to identify the conditions that improve the yields of the process. Based on the above, the objective of the work was to evaluate the influence of the concentration of the substrate and the level of aeration produced by the lower biomass, the output of the biomass of arousal strain L. edes, as well as in the production of sterols.
