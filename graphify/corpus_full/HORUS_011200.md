# SOCIOECONOMIC CHARACTERIZATION

In Colombia, microenterprises are fundamental to the economy, but many fail due to different reasons, such as lack of economic planning, lack of business knowledge, lack of business experience, lack of entrepreneurial skills and difficulty in facing competition. In order to better understand microentrepreneurs and their socioeconomic characteristics, this research was carried out with quantitative approach of longitudinal design and descriptive scope, using data from the portal "Open Data Colombia". The research characterized 10,136 microentrepreneurs who obtained credits between 2016 and 2019, both in rural and urban areas of Medellín (Colombia).For the research different statistical tools, such as graphs, diagrams of boxes, histograms and frequency tables, as well as indicators of central tendency and dispersion.The results showed a tendency to grant credits to women, the ages of microentrepreneurs beneficiaries were around 40 and 42 years, but with a few different variability.
