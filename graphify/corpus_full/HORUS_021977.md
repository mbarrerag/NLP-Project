# Adsorption of organic solvents from gas phase and organic liquid phase on modified activated carbons. Energy characterization.

The adsorption from gas phase and liquid phase of benzene, toluene, cyclohexane and hexane was evaluated on five thermally and chemically modified activated carbon samples, characterizing the solids by means of different techniques. For the gas phase, the isotherms of adsorption of hydrocarbons were evaluated on porous solids, while for the liquid phase solutions of organic compounds were used as adsorbates; subsequently the isotherms were adjusted to the models of Langmuir and Freundlich. The kinetics of adsortion and were also determined to the kinetics of the solids interaction and were adjusted to the models of pseudo-first and pseudo-second order, as well as to the model of intraparticular diffusion.
