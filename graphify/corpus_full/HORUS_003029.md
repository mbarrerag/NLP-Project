# A remedial strategy for strengthening the teaching of differential calculus as a strategy for pedagogical intervention, which contributes to the reduction of academic dropout in the course calculation differential of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín.

This paper presents a pedagogical intervention based on a levelling course to strengthen the teaching of differential cal calculculculculcul teaching students at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters Medellín. The proposal identifies weaknesses in previous knowledge, such as the empowerment, fraction management and analytical geometry. Through initial diagnosis and innovative didactic strategies, the understanding of key concepts was improved through technological tools such as GeoGebra and Mathematica. With a research-action approach, the interaction of students with technological materials and platforms was analyzed, generating spaces for reflection and conceptualization. The results show a significant improvement in the academic performance and motivation of students. . This work presents a pedagogical intervention based on a milding course to strengthen the training of differential calculus for students at the National University of Colombia, Medellín campus.
