# PHOTOVOLTAICS VERSUS FOSSIL FUEL: FINANCIAL FEASIBILITY MODEL FOR EXPANSION OF ACCESS TO ELECTRICITY IN OFF-GRID REGIONS

Global electricity access was 87% in 2016, an increase of 15% since 1993 [1]. While progress has undoubtedly been made, such improvement as there is should not obscure the fact that 1.1 billion people, mostly who live in rural areas of developing countries, still lack energy access [2]. Energy is fundamental to improve living standards [3'6] and enable basic services, such as clean water, energy for cooking and heat, personal safety at night and for health and education services, which all contribute to poverty alleviation [7]. The landmark Millennium Development Goals (by the United Nations, 2000), however, did not include energy needs as one of their eight stated objectives for eliminating world poverty. Nonetheless, in 2015, this oversight was remedied with the publication of Sustainable Development Goals [8], which lists universal energy access among its 17 objectives
