# A Comparative Assessment of Point Sources for Digital Lensless Holographic Microscopy

Point sources for Digital Lensless Holographic Microscopy are comparatively analyzed. Sources based on pinholes, cone-shaped optical fibers, optical pick-up units, and aspheric lenses are assessed on their robustness, compactness, reliability, cost-effectiveness, and imaging performance
