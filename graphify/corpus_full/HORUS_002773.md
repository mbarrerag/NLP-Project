# INFLUENCE OF PYROLYSIS TEMPERATURE ON CARBON CAPTURE IN CACCO-CACARA BIOCARBONS AND PALMA COAL CUSTOM.

In pyrolysis, the biomass type is fundamental in the properties of biocarbon (BC), due to its influence on the functionalization of the material and the physicochemical properties. In this study the elemental analysis of the cocoa shell residues (CCA) and palm cousk (CUP), the thermal effect on the structure of these biomasses and the influence of the pyrolysis temperature (Tpir) on the chemical parameters of the biocarbons (BCs) obtained were studied. The residues of CCA and CUP were characterized by elemental analysis (AE), near micro TGA and high spectroscopy (IR ATR). The pyrolysis was carried out at 350, 550 and 750°C, by 1h and 10 °C/min.
