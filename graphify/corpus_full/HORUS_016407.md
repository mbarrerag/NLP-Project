# CONTEMPORARY ARTIST PRACTICES AND URBAN SPACE: APORTS FOR AN INCONCLUDING DISCUSSION.

The interest of this chapter of book is to present different aspects relevant to the professional training of visual artists, in the framework of a discussion that has taken place over the last two decades on the productions, processes and artistic projects that have reached some relevance because of their ability to influence places of social life different from the places of the artistic.The incidence of these artistic practices, which is as they are known, has been translated into the recognition of population groups, cultural circuits, community-based social organizations and groups of activists different from artists and artistic collectives.In addition to the above precision, the set of issues, especially theoretic and conceptual ones involved in this text, suggests the effectiveness of ideas and social representations of the artistic that have encouraged various collective actions on neighborhood, local and global scales
