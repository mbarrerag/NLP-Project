# SMART CAMPUS FOR ALL: GEOGRAPHICAL INFORMATION TECHNOLOGIES TO THE SERVICE OF SOCIAL INNOVATION - NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

Smart Campus for All is a project that seeks to strengthen the National University of Colombia, La Paz Headquarters - Cesar, as an inclusive and accessible institution. To this end, it is essential to have geographic information of the campus and a model of spatial databases that allows to optimize the conditions of management, use, travel and more accessible enjoyment of the physical space of the campus. It also seeks to contribute to the management of the territory through the use of geographical data, from the inclusion and accessibility reflects a participatory approach, which is an important basis for the construction of peace in educational and social contexts. In methodological terms, spatial and non-space information of the campus generated from internal dependencies and projects of various kinds was collected. Once the data were collected, it was then refined, georeferencing additional data, editing and updating of the existing information. Additionally, the relational model of the database was designed to guarantee the spatial relations between them. Within the results a GDB was constructed with 58 entities grouped in 7 dataset (Biodiversity, Infrastructure, Accessibility, Emergency Services, Basic Services, Campus Networks and Services Management Services)
