# Armed conflict in Colombia: A study of the notion of victim from the perspective of the State (1997-2021)

The master's thesis aims to analyze how the notion of victim in Colombia has been configured from the perspective of the State in the period 1997-2021. It presents a review of the national and international legal framework, as well as of the case law development that has occurred in relation to the object of study of the investigation carried out by both the Constitutional Court and the Inter-American Court of Human Rights. Likewise, the the thesis carries out an analysis of some temporary space stages identified in the period 1997-2021, which allowed to study the changes in the State's discourse in relation to the different political conjunctures and their correspondence with the construction of the notion of victim in Colombia, thus presenting a political and legal analysis of the national and international framework as well how the notion of victim has been established in Colombia from the institutional perspective to the period 1997-2021.
