# 4.71. INSILIOUS EVALUATION OF THE EFFECT OF SATIVE CANNABY METABOLITES AND PIPER NIGRUM ON THE CELLULAR SIGNALIZATION MEASURED BY GPR55 AND PINK1 IN GLIOMAS

The glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) is an astrocytic malignant tumor characterized by rapid growth, high invasiveness and high vascularization. Despite all the advances in this field, the prognosis of the patients suffering from this neoplasia is still very precarious. The protein PINK1 controls critical mitochondrial and metabolic functions that regulate cell survival in cancer, stress resistance and cell cycle; the GPR55 receptor and its ligand endogenous L-a-lyslipphosphatidilinositol (LPI) has been shown to play an important role in several types of cancers.
