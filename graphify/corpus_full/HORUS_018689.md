# Impact of measures to prevent the transmission of COVID on children.

The aim of this study was to describe the impact of the three mitigation measures of the transmission of COVID-19 on the child population (mascalilla, physical distance and hand washing) during the period March 2020 to June 2021. This research is of a qualitative type, using techniques of narrative description of the literature, for which the use of mask, frequent hand washing and physical distance were selected as categories of analysis, seeking to identify possible deleterious effects on the physical, mental and social health of the child population. A search and documentary analysis was carried out from an interpretative perspective with support of the Mendeley bibliographic manager. For the analysis of the basic operations of asking and making comparisons, as well as the definition of terms, codification and identification of emerging categories. The mention of risks and effects associated with the changes of routines, interruption and disruption of daily life, interruption of life path and continuity of learning, as well as effects on mental health, increased violence, exploitation and sexual abuse of children in the Internet was found.
