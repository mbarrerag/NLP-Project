# ALTERNATIVES FOR THE TREATMENT OF MERCOSUR-CONTAMINATED GOLD SANDS: CHEMICAL AND THERMODYNAMIC ASPECTS

Mercury is a highly harmful metal that may be present in the air-ground-water components, presenting interactions mediated by local environmental conditions. Soil-water interaction is not fully known, and therefore the impacts generated by the use of mercury in mining work are not only a problem in terms of affecting the water resource but also the soils and organisms that coexist there. So far, perhaps the most difficult component to address has been the water environment due to its great variability, the mobility that presents mercury and the multiple and complex chemical relations that result from it. In the case of soils and solid residues contaminated with this metal, treatment techniques mostly point to the chemical stabilization and immobilization of mercury. In this work, a review of the main techniques developed or proposed for the treatment and control of solid residues contaminated with mercury is made, with special emphasis on mining residues, from a chemical and thermodynamic perspective. An evaluation of the thermodynamic stability of different mercury compounds of the evaluated alternative is carried out, by means of the elaboration and analysis of the Pourba diagrams, an analysis is carried out.
