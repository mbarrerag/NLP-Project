# Geogebra as a tool for the development of variable thinking.

This proposal is framed in identifying the advantages and disadvantages of teaching activities applied in pencil and paper in comparison to those applied using a technological mediator, for this case GeoGebra; basing the analyses from the considerations that have professors of university level. To this end, a theoretical framework is presented that addresses three constructs that support the proposal. The first construct, is the disciplinary framework that describes the historical development that gave rise to the sums of Riemann and to the defined integral. The second, is the didactic framework that details elements specific to variable thinking and, the last, refers to the use of ICTs as a mediating resource in the mathematics classroom. Based on a proposal of teaching on approaches to the area of regions built for its application in pencil and paper format, this is adapted in a digital format in the GeoGebra tool. These two formats are presented to teachers in the area of mathematics of the Manuela Beltrán University for comparison and from their experience and knowledge to reflect on the benefits and limitations that both resources have since the approach of the development of variable thinking.
