# A representation based on the Fifth Essence on good practices for improving business processes.

Business leaders implement various management philosophies to ensure the fulfillment of organizational objectives. Consequently, in the industry, project management is recognized as the key to their business operations. The project management process involves the reengineering of the business and its processes, including a combination of methods used to analyze, model, optimize, automate and measure business processes. The classic project management includes business process management (in English, business process management, abbreviated BPM) that combines knowledge of information technologies and management sciences. Therefore, the organizations that execute a BPM model increasingly depend on the implementation of business process improvement (in English, business process improvement, abbreviated BPI) to increase organizational capacities. In the industry and academia they try to define the best practices of BPI by means of creating methods, working frameworks, techniques and models. However, the previous approaches explicitly depend on the management area and lack formal representations of the elements identified as best practices of BPI.
