# Alternative evaluation strategies for learning in accounting courses. Paradigmatic case study.

Since before the pandemic generated by COVID-19, wide discussions have been developed that involve the implementation of active learning methodologies and evaluation strategies that transcend traditional practices that leave students in a passive role of receiving information and rating based on memorization and repetition of contents. Faced with the scenario of pandemic, remote work and classes mediated by the technology that was deepened since 2020, this type of proposals has become more relevant although there is a perception that they continue to dominate historically rooted methods.. Accounting education does not escape from these debates and it is considered necessary to deepen the research on the type of procedures applied in the training of professionals of the public accounting system, experts who every day need to improve their skills to fulfill their work beyond the technical and standardized dimension. It is expected that the professionals in this discipline will be competent in critical thinking, decision making, in process innovation, among other dimensions, and for this it is not enough to have educational processes based on the traditional school. One of the most important processes in university training is the evaluation of those students as proposed several studies in the country and international context, without having sufficient information. Alternative evaluation and active learning methodologies. To achieve this goal, a paradigmatic case study methodology with qualitative approach will be applied, through the revision of documents (programmes or syllabus of the subjects) and the application of focal groups and in-depth interviews with teachers and students. The selected case is the Public Accounts program of the National University of Colombia (Sede Bogotá), considered as paradigmatic for its leadership in the formation of public accountants in the country and for being prominent not only in teaching but also in research
