# HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS. A LOOK FROM OPERATIONS

This article aims to identify the current context of higher education institutions, as well as to characterize them as service providers organisations and identify theirsystem of operations. To achieve this objective, it was based on a bibliographic review and a verification was made of the characteristics that make universities service companies and the particularities of their system of operations. It is possible to identify that the literature that studies the is as limited organisations Conclusions: Based on the characterization carried out, it can be concluded that these are service providers organisations that are part of the knowledge economy and that they have an operating system in which inputs (inputs and resources) are converted into outputs (trained students, research carried out and articulation with the external sector)
