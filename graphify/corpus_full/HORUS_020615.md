# Organization and resistance in the town of Suba in Bogotá: conflicts and socio-territorial struggles against neoliberal policies (1989-2021)

This research is a sociological study on the processes of popular organization and resistances developed in the town of Suba, in the city of Bogotá, between 1989 and 2021. This work begins with the review of the strategies to which the counterinsurgency model deployed by the Colombian State against social protest, shows that the global dynamics of neoliberalism were incorporated into the internal social struggles in Colombia and intervened in the urban planning and territorial planning of the Colombian cities. Suba, former municipality of Cundinamarca, was incorporated into Bogotá and suffered a process of forced urbanization, where a large number of urbanisations were erected, among them, at the end of the 1980s, the settlement of the neighborhoods Lisbon, Villa Hermosa and Bilbao, object of this research.
