# Evaluation of the efficiency of an ecofriendly adsorbant based on montmorillonite, hydroxyapatite and gelatin on steroid hormones as emerging pollutants in wastewater

A focal point that attracts the attention of researchers is the search for alternatives that remove part of the pollution caused by domestic, industrial and hospital waste in the waste water that reaches waste water treatment plants (PTAR).The waste water is not only contained in a high level of organic elements but all kinds of chemical pollutants that affect the surrounding ecosystems.Contaminants that are in low concentrations but represent a risk to both human health and the environment are the emerging pollutants.Estabolic hormones are among the emerging pollutants (EC) are both classified as endocrine disruptors (EDC) as they affect the endocrine system of the exposed organisms.In this research it is proposed to employ a novel phase eco-friendly adsorbent composed of montmorillonite (MMT), hydroxyapatite (HAP) and gelatine (GEL) as a second bed in the case of the constituent type (MMT/HAP/GEL) and as a component of the functionally-agulated, a few of the functionally-agulated, which is an interesting alternative for the removal of the emerging pollutants. and its corroboration by the LC-MS system. Finally, the evaluation of the ecofriendly adsorbent in real environments with both domestic and hospital wastewater supplied by allied institutions will be carried out
