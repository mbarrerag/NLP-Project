# BIOECONOMY: MULTIPACLES, REFLECTIONS AND CHALLENGES FOR A COUNTRY COMPLETE A BOOK ON DIVERSAL ECONOMICS, AND ECONOMICS WILL YOU BE FOR LIFE

This book seeks to re-signify the concept of bioeconomy in such a way that it transcends a modern vision, centered on markets and technology, and does it evolve into a more complex idea, in keeping with the diversity of a country like Colombia, which starts from the multiple ways of relating to the territory and therefore different logics of well-being and rationalities, many of them oriented to understanding the economy as a means and not as an end in itself, a means for the maintenance of life in the systemic sense explained by Maturana, Varela, Capra and many worldviews of the south, the understanding of the great network of human being-nature, interdependence, something that also has to do with resilience, adaptation, solidarity economy, regenerative economy. The diversity understood beyond the biological, to include the multiple relationships between ecosystems and human being, worldviews that transcend competitiveness and market logics, and are more associated with diverse economies, economies, other under a logic of planetary well-being and care of the other
