# A PROFESSIONAL OF SCIENCES OF LAND IN THE REGIONS: HOW ARE WE GOING?

The project, a professional of Earth Sciences in the regions, initially proposed for Colombia, aims to guarantee the presence of at least one professional in the territory. This initiative, also known as "Is a geologist per municipality", seeks to generate the conditions for a better performance of the municipalities in topics related to geo-threats, prospecting of groundwater, identification and prospecting of mining-energy resources present in the territory, plans of territorial planning and social appropriation of knowledge in land sciences generating more and better knowledge of the issues related to geology and its importance for society since the lack of geological culture prevents a correct and optimal management of the territory.The fundamental inputs for the development of the proposal come from Law 9 of 1974 which regulates the exercise of the profession of Geologist and the territorial entities and the poor delineation of the reserve zones, the frame and areas of use, it exacerbates this aspect
