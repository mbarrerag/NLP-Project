# Influence of chlorides and trace metals present in MSWI FA on the development of cementing phases during CSA clinkerization

The incineration of urban solid waste (MSW) is a common practice and increasingly adopted at the time of managing the disposition of MSW has worldwide. Derivative of the incineration process is generated by certain by-products such as fly ash (MSWI FA) and bottom ash (MSWI BA). Contributing to the circular economy, sustainability and development of new materials, MSWI FA has gained a great interest in the construction sector by being able to incorporate as fill material in mortar and concrete mixtures, and even as a raw material in the production of different types of cements. The possibility of using MSWI as a base for sulphur to produce CSA cement has been demonstrated, however, the studies carried out so far on the subject present a great opportunity to deepen topics that have not yet been addressed, such as the role of chlorine and metals is present.
