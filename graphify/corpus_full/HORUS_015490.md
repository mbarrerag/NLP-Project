# GUIDE FOR THE IMPLEMENTING OF GOOD PRACTICES FOR THE PRODUCTION OF ACUICULTURE

Worldwide, it is recognized that the transfer of knowledge is vital to improve the conditions of production and, therefore, the life of those involved. Technical assistance remains demanded but now with elements of social appropriation of knowledge (ASC). The ASC, is an intentional process of understanding and intervention of the relations between science, technology and society, built from the active participation of the various social groups that generate knowledge. (Colsciences, 2010) The ASC has as purpose, among other things, the empowerment of society from knowledge. In this sense, the solidarity extension previews that, for the formulation of the projects, vulnerable communities are linked. For the purposes of the apprehension of knowledge, a substantial element, is that the identification of the problems and possible solutions, are generated in a process of co-creation between the academics and the community. In this Guide, it is one of the elements of ASC, which has been built to facilitate the implementation and evaluation of the good water practices of autonomous way by the fishers associated with POWZ.
