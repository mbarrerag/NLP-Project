# Development of a self-contained vehicle at scale using chemical reactions as a source of energy

The project focuses on designing and manufacturing a prototype of electric vehicle on a scale that explores alternatives to improve its energy and braking systems. The project seeks to develop batteries with low-cost and high energy density characteristics, offering a potentially cleaner and more efficient solution compared to existing technologies. Since its beginning in 2016, the research seedling of "Chemical Reactions Study for Electromechanical Devices" has worked on the search and analysis of new energy alternatives through an interdisciplinary methodology. This work has allowed significant advances in research into chemical reactions capable of generating energy for electromechanical devices, uniting knowledge of chemical, mechanical, electronic and related engineering. The current project, framed within the annual competence Chem-E-Car organized by AIChE (American Institute of Chemical Engineers), not only seeks to address a worldwide problem, but also to offer students from the departments of mechanical and mechatronic engineering, electrical and electronic engineering, chemical and environmental engineering, and chemical engineering of the National University of Colombia, an opportunity to develop skills in research and work in multidisciplinary equipment.
