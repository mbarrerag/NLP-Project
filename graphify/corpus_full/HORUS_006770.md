# Children and adolescents of Venezuelan origin in official schools in Bogotá: Guarantees for quality education and discrimination situations at INEM Francisco de Paula de Santander

The economic and political crisis in Venezuela opened a migratory flow that grew significantly since 2017 and many of its citizens settled in Colombia. This meant an institutional and social challenge for their attention due to its unexpected, voluminous, diverse and family character. Since then, over 92% of children and adolescents [NNA] from Venezuela have been enrolled in schools of the official Colombian sector, with Bogotá, Antioquia and Norte de Santander being the territorial entities that received them the most. The Official Education System of Bogotá has not been able to guarantee a quality education understood from the criteria of affordability, accessibility, acceptability and adaptability to migrant children. With the enrolment of Venezuelan students that has been increasing annually, the official institutional efforts of Bogotá for affordability and accessibility have not been sufficient.
