# ELECTRIC SYSTEMS IN NON-SINUSUAL SYSTEM

This work presents a critical look at the analysis, modeling and simulation of electrical systems in non-sinuoidal regime. Initially, the reader will find the necessary foundations to introduce himself into the concepts of quality of electrical power in terms of voltage and current signals. Secondly, he will be immersed in the modeling and simulation of electrical systems in non-sinuoidal regime, through various mathematical representations, modelling techniques and algorithmic implementation methodologies. Finally, he will face a different formulation, proposed by the authors, of the concept of power in non-sinuoidal regime. All this, based on a novel mathematical formalism, called tensory theory of electrical power, and its applications to the study of the quality of energy. With the tensory formulation of electrical phenomena in terms of the concepts of power it is possible to analyze, measure, evaluate and compensate in an integral way the deterioration of the quality of the electrical power
