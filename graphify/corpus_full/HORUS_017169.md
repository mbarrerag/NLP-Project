# LOW-COAST SENSORS FOR MONITORING IN REAL TIME THE TEMPERATURE OF GOVERNMENT POPULATIONS

One of the challenges presented by poultry agro-industry in tropical countries is to control the temperature of production facilities. The internal temperature of the birds is closely related to its thermal comfort and well-being. This has been successfully associated with the surface temperature of the birds, therefore, can be used for the decision making of microclimatic and managerial control. However, the temperature taking is usually manual and the continuous measurement systems by means of industrial chambers are expensive. To implement a system of monitoring in real time the temperature of the birds of fattening and microclimatic variables that condition their thermal comfort (CT), using technologies of low cost, that allow in turn interpret biological phenomena that impact their well-being. A system of monitoring of CT of birds of fattening using the sensors of low cost and open code platforms. The variables ambient temperature, relative humidity, lighting, speed of the wind and sound were monitored by the hospital of 2x. In addition, the temperature of the birds was measured continuously using a low cost thermographic chamber.
