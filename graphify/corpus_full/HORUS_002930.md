# MONITORY OF CORPORAL TEMPERATURE AND THE EQUINE BUILDING FORCE DURING AN ARATED WORK IN ANIMAL TRACTION TESTS

This work presents the instrumentation of sensors applied to horse-drawn tests during a plowing day. A body temperature measurement module (TC) was used in two horses pulling from a plowing cart, in turn equipped with a set of monitoring equipment of traction force and depth of chisel, to establish the influence of the operating variables on the physiology of the animals as an indicator of effort. The project has the approval of the committee for animal experimentation of the National University (CICUA). A control team based on the Arduino Mega technology was used as the control center of the system, in turn, the depth of the plowed tool (cincel) was measured by means of an ultrasonic sensor HC-SR04, with the ability to measure distances from 2 to 450 cm; for the thrust force in the shooting work was implemented a load cell type S with load capacity up to 1000 kg. The CT of the horses was individually recorded in each animal using two sensors DS18B20, with the ability to measure distances of the data associated with an ESP32.
