# Technical and social accompaniment to producers of Buchadó (Vigía del Fuerte) in their production, processing and marketing processes of boronjo.

The situation of vulnerability and multidimensional poverty of the communities of African descent in the basin of the river Atrato, results from a multifactorial dynamic integrated by causes and effects of years of conflict, presence of armed groups outside the law in dispute for the territory, illicit crops, forced displacement, precarious housing and health conditions, informality of labor, food insecurity, systems of agricultural production of low productivity and poor agro-industrial development, among others. Buchadó is a co-regiment of the municipality Vigía del Fuerte, located in the subregion of Urabá, on the banks of the river Atrato, has a population of 759 inhabitants that derive their subsistence mainly from fishing, agriculture and forestry. Their main crops are banana, cauca, rice, sugar cane, pineapple and boronjo. Their production especially for the autoconsumption, in the case of the productive surplus that are commercialized in fresh, through intermediaries that transport it to Quibdó, Turbo and Medellín. In response to a request submitted by the legal representative of the Community Council of the Buchadó community, directed to the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences of the Universidad de Medellín, Colombia and some of Colombia. This project seeks to characterize and improve the production systems of borojó in the Buchadó district, the best packaging of fruit for fresh marketing, its transformation into jam and packaged pulp, which contributes to the improvement of its food security and the permanence of the community in its territory. The project seeks to bring undergraduate students of the National University of Colombia closer to real conditions of Afro-descendant communities, agro-industrial processes that solve felt needs, methods of research-extension participatory action and the territorial approach to rural development
