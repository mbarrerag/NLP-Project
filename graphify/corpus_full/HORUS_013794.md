# Empowerment of the Environmental Classroom La Hive (Sibaté-Cundinamarca) by integrating empirical and technical knowledge into agroecological practices for the production of aromatic plants.

The present project seeks to achieve an exchange of knowledge between the empirical knowledge of the community Aula Ambiental La Colmena, located in Sibaté-Cundinamarca and the scientific knowledge of students and teachers of the Department of Chemistry of the National University of Colombia. The community is made up of peasants inhabitants of the rural area of Sibaté, students of schools, mothers head of family, children and people of the third age, who cooperate for the care and production of the cultivation of aromatic plants for its subsequent use for medicinal purposes and elaboration of ointments. The objective of this project is to empower the community of the Environmental Class La Colmena by means of education and technical evaluation of their agroecological practices for the production of aromatic plants. The above by means of the 1. Integration of knowledge of farmers, students and teachers around ecological farming practices for the production of aromatic plants in the area, 2. The determination of the effect of these practices on soil quality, expression of genes involved in the biosynthesis of volatiles and the composition of volatile plants obtained and 3. Promote the empowerment of the ecological plants in the environment, environmental class and the practice of the practice of the soil
