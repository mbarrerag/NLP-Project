# A network of EC-OLOGIC CITY-BUILDING CONNECTIONS: REFLECTIONS ON THE FUTURE OF MEDELLIN IN PANDEMIA TIMES

The text, the result of the research "City and architecture: analysis, projects, transformations", is a reflection on the mutations, sometimes drastic, in the way of living and interacting, which occurred in Medellín in the last year. It is proposed the possibility of seeing some positive aspects, almost hidden in the current difficult social situation. Some recent characteristics of the new urban life are described, in order to be able to reinterpret them and apply them to the transformation processes in the years to come. Projectual reflections, carried out in the scope of the workshops of projects of the basic cycle of the Faculty of Architecture of the National University, Medellín Headquarters, concentrated in three areas of the city: the civic center, the area of the airport and the old center. In the conclusions, some conceptual points derived from the project experiments, useful to promote a system of ecological transformations in the cities, are summarized
