# Value stream mapping applied to the process of demand management of requirements in the company Segundadora Solidaria de Colombia.

In this work, the Value Stream Mapping (VSM) methodology is implemented, which is part of the tools of Lean Manufacturing, in a company in the insurance sector; with the aim of identifying and eliminating activities that do not add value to the process “demand management of requirements” and in turn, contributing to improve the operation of the management of processes and quality of the company, the perception of the service towards the client and achieving a balance in the allocation of services for the consultants of the area. At the end of this work it is concluded, that the application of the VSM tool, allowed to identify the pain points of the process where there are waste, and based on this, propose and implement improvement actions that contributed to the ideal functioning of the process. The actions that were proposed in this work, are being implemented and during the two months that the process has been monitored, has been managed to reduce waiting times, cycle time, workload and improve the indicators of management of the area, the perception of the service by the client and the working environment at the internal level of the management
