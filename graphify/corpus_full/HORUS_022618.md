# Assessment of the threat by landslide by climatic events in the Quebrada El Rosario - Manizales basin, using the ALICE ® application.

There are currently many ways to make threat assessment maps by mass movements at basin level. However, mechanical methods are popular since they can capture physical and mechanical characteristics of the mass that slips that other methods cannot. However, sometimes it is thought that these methods are only applied at scale of detail and this is not enough for decision making regarding risk of sliding to larger scales. This is why it is important to select a mechanical method, PBM, which can be applied to many types of mass movement in parallel.
