# ASSISTANCE OF INSURANCE CARE IN AN INTENSIVE CARE UNIT IN A VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENT

Abstract Introduction: The creation of a virtual course in the Moodle platform allowed to expand and strengthen specific knowledge for the care of the person in critical health situation during the pandemic period by COVID-2019. To describe the types of significant learning obtained through the realization of an asynchronous virtual course of nursing care to the critical patient. A descriptive, cross-sectional study, with 109 participants who completed the critical care course with an intensity of 48 hours in an assynchronous manner, aimed at nurses with professional experience greater than one year. The study was carried out in May 2021. The significant learning was present with positive results, the participants were motivated given the need to have the theoretical elements of critical patient care. A virtual learning environment allowed the processes of conceptual learning, propositions and representations mediated by personal motivation and control in nurses. Key words: Learning analysis. Learning motivation. Online courses. Virtual classroom. Professionals
