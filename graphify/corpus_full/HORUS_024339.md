# TRANSIT ENGINEERING MODELS

This document brings together the basic commands for the modeling of isolated intersections and road networks in the main software used in the market and currently being developed in the Advanced Transit Engineering subject. As a practical example, the modeling of an isolated intersection with HCS2000 and Cider 5.1 softwares will be carried out, and the modeling of a Road Network of the city of Bogotá with the TSIS software version 6, Syncro 8 and Vissim version 6 will be carried out. This manual will support students of the subjects of Transit Engineering and Advanced Transit Modeling Tools, so that the management of the tools is known and in the subjects is deepened in the interpretation of the data that the software releases
