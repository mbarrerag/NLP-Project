# VIOLENCE AND WAR IN THE POST PEACE AGREEMENT, THE END OF POLITICAL WARS: Insurgencies, dissidence, resurgence, neo-military and criminal gangs

In the process of political settlement of the armed conflict between the government of Juan Manuel Santos and the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC-EP), different dissidences were generated, not always, by a political discussion about the scope of the process, its uncertainties and achievements, but, in most cases, by an attachment of commanders to the dynamics and privileges of war and to the powerful war economy built around a portfolio of criminality, in which without a doubt, the most important resources come from illegal activities and drug trafficking that serve the support of illegal armed groups and, mainly, towards the construction of processes of criminal accumulation in complex relationships of growing individual privatization and insertion into traditional economies
