# HAMBRENT HOUSING SYNDROME, IS IT POSSIBLE TO PREVENCE IT?

Hungry bone syndrome is an entity characterized by severe and prolonged fall in serum calcium concentrations, which appears in the postoperative period of parathyroidectomy of patients with hyperparathyroidism. to review the updated evidence on available strategies to prevent the appearance of hungry bone syndrome.Methodology: exhaustive search in specialized databases, including Medline/Pubmed, Embase and Lilacs, while for the grey literature Google was adopted as search engine. The terms MeSH used were: Hungry Bone Syndrome, Hypocalcemia, Parathyroidectomy, Hyperparathyroidism, Bisphosphonates, Vitamin D. These key words were combined using Boolean operators with the aim of optimizing the search for relevant studies. In addition, manual searches were performed in the reference lists of the selected articles to identify additional studies of interest. there is still little evidence available on the strategies that could be instituted to reduce the risk of the disease of the hungry bone, however, the biphosphonate need to be considered as a result of the possible outcome
