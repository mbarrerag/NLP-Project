# Simulation of the energy, thermodynamic and spectroscopic properties of nanoconfined molecules

The development of models and methodologies aimed at understanding the peculiarities of certain confining molecular structures, such as clatrates, endofullerenes and nanogotes, has direct consequences in the field of molecular sciences. These systems constitute 'nanolaboratory' extraordinary characteristics in which encapsulated molecules behave as quantum rotors, and where the translational and rotational levels are mixed by the containment, generating a rich structure of energy levels.
