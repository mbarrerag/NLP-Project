# OBESITY-BUILDING VARIABLES: A ANALYSIS FROM THE DATA MINERITY

The main objective of this research is to identify the most influential variables in the degree of obesity through data mining techniques and the WEKA platform. An existing database with 2,111 records, 16 independent variables and a dependent variable (obesity grade) is restructured. The variable body mass index is added and the J48 algorithm is used to perform a statistical selection process. The results show that the most influential independent variables are: gender, height, weight and body mass index. The success rate exceeds 97% by means of the J48 algorithm and other intelligent techniques executed with the same platform by cross-validation. By suppressing the most influential variables, age and family history have a moderate influence. It is concluded that it is the responsibility of each person to be aware of and control each of these variables if he or she wishes to have effective control of his or her body weight
