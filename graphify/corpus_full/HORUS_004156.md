# TOGETHER AND ALSO REVOLT? PRE-electoral COALITIONS FOR THE ELECTION OF GOVERNMENTS IN COLOMBIA (2019-2023)

The results of the 2019 and 2023 subnational elections show that pre-electoral coalitions are an effective strategy for achieving the office of governor. The present study aims to characterize the types of coalitions and their competitiveness for subnational elections to government. For this purpose, data from the National Civil Status Registry are used, the statements in favour of the Opposition Statute before the National Electoral Council, and as a classification criterion, the type of coalitions proposed by Uribe, et al. (2022). This study is a contribution to the emerging literature on coalitions, subnational elections and governorate elections in Colombia. The findings reaffirm the separation between national and subnational political logics, given the effectiveness of moderate inconsistent coalitions and the dominant role of coalitions in the candidates to governorship, both as a way of competing as a winning strategy
