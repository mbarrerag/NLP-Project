# Research networks as a strategy to address the challenges of women in the Colombian accounting academy: case study

As an international research line, gender studies have been identifying the obstacles that women face in order to achieve their objectives in their respective careers, in the academic field or in the professional. Such research highlights those that have focused on the accounting profession, addressing both the participation in quantitative terms of women in different dimensions of this work (feminization) and the qualitative aspects of their forms of work (feminization). These approaches agree to affirm that different obstacles persist to overcome the gaps between men and women in the accounting profession, findings that promote new studies that identify how they are organized to overcome such challenges in highly masculinized environments. Simultaneously, in the Colombian accounting academy it is frequent to organize experts and experts in research groups (research networks, wage gap, stereotypes, but among others).
