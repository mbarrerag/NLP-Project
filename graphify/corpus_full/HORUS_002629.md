# Perspectives on mental health needs from the experience of health professionals: a mixed study in the region of Catatumbo, 2024

Mental health is an essential component of individual and collective well-being, influenced by personal factors and the social, economic and political context. This research aimed to describe the mental health needs of the Catatumbo population from the perspective of professionals related to more than six months of experience in the region. A mixed study was carried out with a pragmatic epistemological approach, structured in three methodological phases. In the first phase, the mental health needs of the population were characterized through the application of a survey that yielded quantitative results. The second phase addressed the analysis of mental health needs from the perspective and experience of professionals through qualitative interviews. Finally, the third phase resulted in a methodological integration of qualitative and quantitative findings. The study showed the impact of armed conflict on the mental health of the population and its possible association with the prevalence of mental health disorders from the perspective and experience from the perspective of anxiety. Participants emphasized the need to promote mental health education for its proper recognition.
