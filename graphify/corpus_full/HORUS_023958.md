# MORFODINAMIC ANALYSIS OF THE RIUM WITHOUT BEFORE AND AFTER URRA I OPERATION

ABSTRACT The river systems of tropical plain rivers, such as the river Sinú, have geomorphological and morphodynamic characteristics particularly due to their high natural mobility. The anthropic intervention of these channels by different urbanistic, agricultural, or hydroelectric processes, affect positively or negatively their natural system. The operation of reservoirs and dams located in the limits of the high and medium basins cause different effects and impacts of the natural conditions of sedimentation, hydrology, hydraulic that manifest in the mobility of the sinuous or meandering river systems. The river Sinú, located in the plains of the Colombian Caribbean, was intervened since 1999 by the operation of Urrá I. Of the results of the morphodynamic behavior of the natural mobility of the river Sinú in its natural scenario (1968-1999) and controlled by the Urrá system of mobility (1999-2019), it can establish remarkable patterns of differential behavior.
