# THE THEORIC-EMPIRIC CONCEPTUAL SYSTEM (CTE): WHAT IT IS AND HOW WE USE IT

This chapter presents the elements that allowed to guide the construction of the conceptual-theoretical-empirical system (Cte) that will be addressed in the following chapters, as they are applied in the consolidation of the research carried out by the doctoral and master's students of the National University of Colombia during their training process. Thus, this section develops in two moments: firstly, the description of the system, the definition of what and what for the Cte, as well as a orientation for the construction and generation of this one, is presented; secondly, the way in which this system has been used both in the academy and in the construction of research projects according to the design selected
