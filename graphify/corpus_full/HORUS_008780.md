# Prevalence of auditory-vestibular disorders in the elderly institutionalized in Bogotá D.C.

Objectives To establish the prevalence of auditory-vestibular disorders (hypoacusia, vertigo and tinnitus) and associated factors in adults over 65 years of age institutionalized in the city of Bogotá D. C. Methodology A cross-sectional study was conducted whose target population was the elderly resident in specialized institutions for their care. In this study, 217 elderly adults (100 men and 117 women) participated (aged between 65 and 99 years), who were subjected to auditory screening and application of the Inventarios de Impabilidad Auditiva para el Adulto Mayor (HHIE-S), the Inventario de Impabilidad para Vertigo (DHI-S) and the Inventario de Impadicia para Tinnitus (THI). From the information obtained, a bivariate analysis was made, according to the factors associated most relevant in relation to auditory-vestibular disorders in the target population. To this end, the reasons of prevalence for their respective confidence intervals were calculated
