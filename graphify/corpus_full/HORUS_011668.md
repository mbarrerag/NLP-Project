# BACHUÉ, JACKY AND VILLA, NEW PAPA VARIETIES FOR THE COLOMBIA PRODUCTIVE SYSTEM

The genetic improvement program of the potato of the Faculty of Agrarian Sciences of the National University of Colombia (UNAL) has developed varieties for the country from a collection of work that has been formed since 1988. In this process, it has been important to interact with improvement programs of South America and support of the CIP. From selected parents of several years, segregating populations (about 5000 individuals) were built, which began selection process in the San Jorge farm of the ICA, then the initial clones selected were taken to a low precipitation environment (Carupa Carmen) (1100 clones) and, those selected there, from 2017 onwards continued their selection process in the municipality of Villapinzón (Cundinamarca). The work presents the new varieties registered in Colombia by UNAL and its main characteristics
