# Hybrid models for prediction of flux in pervaporation: case study separation of alcohols and acetates

The proposal requires general models to predict the flow transport through the membranes. In this proposal, a hybrid model is made that will allow to discover the effect of including thermodynamic information generated by activity models in the prediction of flux in multicomponent systems. Two mixtures will be studied for this purpose, separation of alcohols generated in fermentation systems and separation of mixtures from the generation of acetates from the reaction of alcohols and acetic acid. The data will be collected in the literature. It is expected to generate results for two presentations at international events
