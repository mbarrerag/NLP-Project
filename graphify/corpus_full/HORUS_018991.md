# PROPOSAL FOR DESIGN OF A TERRITORIAL VULNERABILITY INDEX FOR VALUE OF THE IMPACT OF EXTRACTIVE ACTIVITIES IN COLOMBIA

Over the last four decades, the extractive model of Colombian development has been based mainly on the exploitation and export of minerals. This has explained and intensified ecological destruction, having negative impacts not only at environmental level, but also social and economic, impacts that have not been fully quantified or evaluated so far, neither at national level nor much less territorial, considering different levels of exposure and local vulnerability. Currently, it is recognized that the processes of control and monitoring in Colombia are lax and whose legal flexibility contributes to the increasing opening of institutions that promote the collation of the extractive sector in the Colombian territory. Generally, the measurement of vulnerability has been used as a tool for managing the risk of projects by governments and private companies as they propose it. (Climent-Gil et al., 2018 Mandes et al., 2019). However, other authors like Esteves et al. (2017) propose a change of approach in which risk management is oriented to the communities and territories in which the impact takes place.
