# Women, flowers and struggles. A generational look at the resistance of flower workers in the Bogotá Sabana

The arrival of the flower industry in Colombia in the 1960s changed the spatial structure of the territory, and impacted the dynamics of social and labour interaction. In particular, it offered an opportunity for the integration of women into the labour market. The aim is to examine the changes in the perception of women workers around the impact of flower production in their lives. This in three generation cohorts: the first generation, composed of women who entered in 1960 and 1982; the second generation, which was incorporated in 1998; and, finally, the third generation, in 2015. It describes the arrival of industry in the country and its configuration in terms of production, as well as a reflection on the phenomenon of feminization and the union struggles undertaken by women.
