# Voices of women with disabilities: discussions on care as collective occupations.

This research experience is the network of different experiences embodied from the daily life of eight women with disabilities. It arises from a concern, deep interpellation and critical position that seeks to recognize the experiences of women subject to or objects of care throughout their life course, in order to allow for a greater understanding and visibility of care from themselves. The general objective of this research is to recognize the understanding of care, interdependence and possible collective occupations emerging in the daily life of women with disabilities who have been or are the object or subject of care.This research, of an exploratory type with a qualitative approach, seeks to reveal the other voices of care.For this reason, the study has a critical-social approach from the south, which reveals the experiences lived and embodied throughout the life course of women participants.For this research process, the participation and the participation of the population, and the participation of the disabled, through cordial conversations around the life histories and experiences in the daily life. The narratives, lives and skills of women with disabilities in a system that excludes them from being women and from having disabilities. Finally, this research generated two types of final reflections related to decolonization and the accompaniment of processes and moments that are relevant from personal and professional work. On the other hand, the pending generation of conversations among women around care as collective occupations, which is expected to be developed in future research
