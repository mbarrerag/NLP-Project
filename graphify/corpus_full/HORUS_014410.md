# Evaluation of the modification of cassava starch (Manihot sculenta) via enzymatic deramification and intercrossing and the use of modified starch for the production of biodegradable films.

In this research, the morphology, crystallineness and the gelatinisation process were studied by monitoring viscosity and differential heat-scanning for yucca starch. The enzymatic hydrolysis process with pululanase was evaluated to obtain enzymatically de-ramified starch (ADRE), and it was established that the best conditions for the process are reaction temperature 60 °C and pH 4, and by chromatographic monitoring it was determined that a mixture of high, medium and low molecular weight with filmogenic capacity is produced. Subsequently, the sterification of native starch and de-ramified starch (ADRE) with citric acid, malic and adipic, was initially performed in organic medium with DMSO for obtaining adipate of ADRE at 90 °C with a degree of substitution of 0.009, in the second part the evaluation was performed using the method of dry heat in the oven, malate and adipate of starch and ADRE with a reaction temperature of 100 °C and the frequency of 2.5 °C and acid/alvidon ratio (inf)
