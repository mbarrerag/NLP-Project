# SOCIOLOGY OR MARXISM

The paper proposes to make a critique of postmodern pose in the field of sociology, which in turn allows us to corroborate that sociology does not have to transform the material and cultural conditions of society. This will be developed from three basic ideas: I- the theoretical nature with which sociology was shaped from its beginnings in the nineteenth century; II- the tendency of what to do sociological today, with emphasis on studies in Colombia; and, III- sociology cannot be Marxist, as Marxism cannot be sociological. With these ideas we seek to draw attention to the theoretical and practical coherence of sociologists in particular, the importance of political and ethical praxis rather than as an intellectual as a human being committed to the dignity of life, and a brief exaltation, in the face of the magnificence of the contribution of the political, ethical and all-humanistic work of Carlos Marx
