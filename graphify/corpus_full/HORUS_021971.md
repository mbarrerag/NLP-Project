# Heat transfer in architecture: computer modelling and simulation of the indoor globe temperature in Bogotá, Colombia.

The thermal sensation inside a building is the result of the interaction between the architectural space and its environment through heat transfer. In Colombia, although computer simulations of the interior temperature are usually performed, its predictions are rarely compared with measurements and therefore the relevant aspects to be considered in simulations are not always identified. This work aims to clarify which aspects should be included in the simulation to obtain reliable predictions of the interior temperature for buildings in Bogotá, using LadyBugTools software and numerical models in Python derived from early principles. The results are compared with balloon temperature measurements inside two spaces of the National University of Colombia in Bogotá. Our study identifies the relevant ingredients to be considered in simulations and clarifies which heat transfer mechanisms are the most relevant. The study is a significant contribution to the use of simulation tools to evaluate indoor thermal comfort in Colombia
