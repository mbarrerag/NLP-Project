# Assessment of the energy dissipation capacity coefficient R in steel portico buildings, fitted with hysteretic panning restricted steel dissipators (BRB), located in high seismic threat zone

This paper assesses the coefficient of capacity of dissipation of energy R in buildings of steel porticos provided with hysteretic dissipators of steel restricted to the pandeo (BRB) located in zone of seismic threat. Four buildings of 4, 8, 12 and 16 levels were numerically modeled, in which the BRBs were represented by a methodology that employs elements of frame, allowing to identify the states of plasticization of these devices. For the structural analysis of the buildings a non linear static procedure of gradual plasticization (Pushover) was carried out, with the result that the capacity curves were obtained and subsequently the performance points for the design earthquake and for the maximum considered way (MCE). The dimensioning of the BRBs was based on the methodology of Oviedo A. et al. (2010), and their final verification was carried out according to the values of the buildings of 4 and 8 levels, maintaining the concentration of the ASCE 7‐22 for structures with dissipation systems.
