# Standardized protocol for the observation of the tomographic image in endodontics

to establish a standardized observation protocol for the CT scan of conical beam (CBCT) in endodontics, to determine systematically the dimensions of chronic apical lesions. analysis and application of the tools of the tomographic conical beam software used for the exploration of dental and periapical tissues, with prior bibliographic review of the topic. Subsequently, the systematic standardized observation and measurement steps of the dental tomographic image in endodontics were designed, with which the protocol was established. Finally, two endodontists were trained in the application of the protocol to systematically measure the dimensions of chronic apical lesions (height, depth and width) Results: A document was generated with graphic material on the steps to follow from the standardized protocol and a statistical test of concordance was performed, which showed a high degree of agreement between the measurements made by the trained endocants (observers, depth and width) compared to those of an expert radiologist (observer 1).
