# THE LATIN AMERICAN ACCOUNT OF OUR DAYS: READING, WRITING, CRITICAL (TO WAY OF INTRODUCTION)

The book entitled Prender el fuego. New poetics of the contemporary Latin American story (eds. Iván Padilla and Diana Diaconu) is an anthology of criticism about the current Latin American story carried out by the research group Debates Critics and Theoreticals on Contemporary Latin American Literature, whose direction I assume. This group was developed following an old Semillero of research and is composed by graduates of our career of Literary Studies and our Mastery. The book contains an introductory study followed by nine chapters on the following storytellers, authors of innovative poetics of the genre: Álvaro Enrigue, Ricardo Piglia, Rodrigo Fresán, Roberto Bolaño, Rodrigo Rey Rosa, Juan Villoro, Fernando Iwasaki and Albalucía Ángel. It focuses on the reformulation of the genre in the contemporary, more dynamic and comprehensive context
