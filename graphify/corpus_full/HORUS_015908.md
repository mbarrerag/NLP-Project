# ARCHITECTURE ARE NOT BUILDINGS

This chapter reflects on what architecture really is, understanding that buildings are only a manifestation of it, but that what is behind the material, as content involving the body and spirit of those who inhabit them, predetermines the behavior, relationation, thought and feeling. It is an invitation to discover architecture as an expression of an artistic character in which the poetic capacity of the human being is manifested
