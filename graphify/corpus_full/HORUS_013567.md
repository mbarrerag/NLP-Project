# Patient-centered care and the humanized care dimension in professional nursing practice (integrative review)

This research is a critical analysis of the characteristics that nursing professionals must assume to provide humanized care, taking into account the patient-centred care model, through an integrative review of the literature, whose main objective was to describe the characteristics that nursing professionals must assume to develop humanized care from the perspective of dignity in the patient-centred care model. The need to carry out this research arises from the negative perception of quality and the dissatisfaction of users about the care they receive from nursing professionals. Therefore, the search for evidence in the scientific literature on the professional care must be used to treat the user with dignity in the patient-centred care model .
