# IDENTIFICATION OF FLEBOTOMINES (DIPTERA: PSYCHODIDAE) PRESENTED IN TWO DEPARTMENTS OF THE COLOMBIAN AMAZON REGION, MEASUREMENT OF INTEGRATIVE TAXONOMY

The pathogen Leishmania is transmitted in Colombia by 14 species of phlbotominous mainly of the genera Lutzomyia, Nysomyia and Psychodopygus. The collection of taxonomic and molecular inventories allows to know the fauna of phlebotominets in a given area, to focus those with presence of vectors and to act in time before outbreaks of the disease. In this work the inventory of buccal aspirators and Prockopak® was updated. The taxonomic identification was made following the keys of Galati 2023 and the molecular identification was performed by means of partial amplification of the Citochrome Oxidase I (COI) gene from the total DNA, using the first LCO1490 and HCO2198. The products were sequenced and then the sequences analyzed. In total, the species were confirmed 28.6%. supported by integrative taxonomy, they provide valuable information for local health institutions in the design of vector control strategies and disease prevention
