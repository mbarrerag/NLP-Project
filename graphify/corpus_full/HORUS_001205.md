# TERRITORIES: ISSUES AND DISCUSSION IN THE PUBLIC SPACE

One of the contemporary challenges is the generation of just territories, thought on a human scale (Gehl, 2014), that claim the right to the city with comfortable, safe spaces, that provide opportunities and an adequate quality of life.From the Group of Urban-Regional Studies (Geur) and the Faculty of Architecture, we believe that some of these purposes can be achieved from the public space (ep), as they are proposed by Carmona et al. (2010). Thus, we address issues around the role of the ep as an urban-social and environmental structural structure, investigating the ecosystem services (se) that provide and by the ways to connect them to improve the benefits generated. This chapter presents some concepts, also invites the scientific-academic debate and raises more questions that guide the research and construction of the knowledge. The urban green infrastructure, of which also part the ep, generates to the inhabitants of the city (Wolch et al., 2014). The benefits that we obtain from the functioning of the ecosystems (Daily et al., 1997), are increasingly scarce in spite of the important demand of the urban goods (a, and the urban services)., overcoming the physical-spatial issues (Rabotnikof, 1993). With this in mind this chapter was developed, in which some concepts are presented from the collective positions that are being worked out from the Geur; the interrelation between elements of the territory, the landscape, the se and the territorial justice is analyzed; all around an element that seems to integrate them, not only conceptually but physically, in the construction of the city: the ep
