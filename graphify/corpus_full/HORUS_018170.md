# NATURAL PRODUCTS AS POTENTIAL ALTERNATIVES IN THE TREATMENT OF DIABETES MELITUS: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF PATENTS (2016- 2022)

Diabetes mellitus (DM) is a multifactorial metabolic syndrome that represents an important and growing health problem for all countries, regardless of the level of development.It is characterized by the exacerbation of blood glucose levels, associated with the absence or inability of insulin to capture glucose into cells.Currently, both in clinical and scientific practice, the search for more effective and safe treatments for diabetes has been ongoing.In this scenario, preparations of natural origin have gained increasing relevance in the area as they have great pharmaceutical potential.In view of the above, the objective of this review is to present an overview of products of natural origin as potential alternatives in the treatment of DM, through compilations of patents published between the years 2016 and 2022, in the Espacenet database of the European Patent Office (EPO).19 patents, their components and associated active metabolites are discussed in this chapter.The selected patents describe the use of plants, algae, fungi and some minerals and associate the presence of metabolites such as tannins, organic acids, polyphenols, saponins, terpenes, flavonoids with the observed activities.Furthermore, some patents are included with pharmacotechnical strategies for the transport of active ingredients, such as solid forms with modified release profiles and adhesives. 32 transmucosal, which provide improvements in relation to the safety and effectiveness of products.The above confirms the high potential of natural products as a source for the development of new medicines for the treatment of DM
