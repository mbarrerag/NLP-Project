# The City and the River: Relations with Urban Sustainability. Medellín Case (1998 – 2020)

The purpose of this research is to study the relationship between cities and their respective rivers and the possible association between the urban sustainability of river-owning cities and the relationship established by the city with the river, through the design of an indicator that allows characterizing the city-river relationship applied in the city of Medellín. To this end, urban sustainability was calculated from the ecological footprint indicator, and an indicator called the city-river relationship was designed to calculate the relationship established by the city with the river; finally, a statistical correlation was used based on the outcome of both indicators for the period of time of 1998 and 2020 to identify its degree of association. The result was to prove that there is no evidence that the city of Medellín is reconciling relations with the river, and that there was no correlation between the sustainability of Medellín and the relationship between the city and the Medellin River for the period of time studied. .
