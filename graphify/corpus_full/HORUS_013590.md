# KNOWLEDGE, IDENTITY AND RESISTANCE: RELATIONS OF KANKUAMA WOMEN OVER THE EARTH OF THEIR MOCHILS AND CHINCHORRES

This article presents the results of an investigation that proposed to understand, promote and relate knowledge, practices and techniques that constitute processes of resistance of colonized populations in the Colombian Caribbean, in this case, that of indigenous Kankuama women around the fabric of their backpacks and chinchorros. The methodology used was the systematization of experiences based on interviews and conversations carried out in Chemesquemena in 2018, where the approach of dialogue of knowledge in the production of knowledge was put into practice by adopting the perspectives of the protagonists as the guiding thread. Among the conclusions we find that the fabric of backpacks and chinchorros de fique is one of the main foundations of the re-etnizing process of the Kankuamo people, that women are protagonists in the recovery of their ancestral culture and that the fabric is intimately linked to the other areas of community life
