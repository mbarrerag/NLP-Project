# Three-field brand manager (GM3): Proposal to facilitate brand management in the incubated microentrepreneurs of Bogotá in postpandemia

In Colombia 40% of the enterprises do not exceed the first year of existence, and more than the remaining 80% does not manage to remain in operation beyond the three years (Henao, 2020).This problem, among several other factors, is due to the deficiencies in the practices of business management among entrepreneurs (Quintero, 2013).The business management includes several assets, but in this research it was decided to focus exclusively on the management of the brand assets.These assets, of intangible nature, play a crucial role in generating value for the company, facilitating its positioning in the market.The management of the brand includes a wide and complex spectrum of activities, which can also be challenging for those who have no experience in the topic.For this reason, in the search to make it more accessible, this research reduces the management to three types of decisions: producing, preserving and transforming the available assets.This division of the management in three possibilities is the one that gives rise to the three fields, which function as a work space for the entrepreneurs in which they can freely organize their brand assets.The design of this proposal has been significantly influenced by the postpandememia.
