# VERTICILLIUM SPP. OF TEN VARIETIES OF PAPA (SOLANUM TUBEROSUM L.)

Among the soil phytopathogens that affect potato cultivation is Verticillium spp., which causes the disease known as early maturity. This pathogen invades xylema and interferes with water transport in the early stages of growth, however, symptoms develop late in the filling stage of the tuber or after flowering. The aim of the work was to evaluate the response of nine varieties registered in Colombia from Solanum tuberosum subsp. andigena (bacata, betina, diacol capiro, esmeralda, punto azul, parda pastusa, pastusa suprema, roja nariño and rubí), and a clone of the international center of the pope (cip) 395112.6 to verticillium spp
