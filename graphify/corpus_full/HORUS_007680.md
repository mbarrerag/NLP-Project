# Evaluation of a rapid method for the determination of transfecting promastigants of the genus Leishmania

The parasites of the genus Leishmania are the causal agent of a group of diseases called leishmaniasis (or leishmaniosis), with different types of clinical manifestations. Millions of people around the world are exposed to this type of diseases including in Colombia, in its different versions being the Leishmaniasis Cutanea (LC) the most frequent, followed by Leishmaniasis Muco-cutaneous, Post-kala-azar Syndrome and Visceral Leishmaniasis. There is no effective vaccine for this disease and therapeutic treatments are characterized by being highly toxic. Additionally, there are reports in the literature indicating the possibility of the emergence of parasites with less sensitivity to the drugs of greater use. Skin leishmaniasis is the most frequent clinical form of tegumentary leishmaniasis and it is a major problem of public health in Colombia.
