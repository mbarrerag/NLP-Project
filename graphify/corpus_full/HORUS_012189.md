# FOLLOW-UP TO THE PATIENT WITH THE MAMMARY CANCER DIAGNOSTIC. REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVANCE AT THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA CANCEROLOGY INSTITUTE

Cancer survivors are defined as individuals who have completed their initial treatment and have no evidence of the disease. For breast cancer survivors, follow-up involves not only monitoring locoregional and remote relapse, as well as screening of breast primary second, but also evaluating the effects related to the therapies received. Today, there is controversy about what the method, frequency, duration and type of health personnel should be to follow up these patients. The guidelines of the different scientific societies show significant variability in the recommendations in this regard. This document aims to review the best evidence available on procedures for the detection of locoregional relapse, of distant metastases, of a second contralateral breast cancer and of adverse events related to treatments for breast cancer. Additionally, the percentages and sites of tumour recurrence related to clinical stage and tumour biology are examined
