# TWO-PHASE FLOW SIMULATIONS OF GAS-SOLID FLOW IN A CONICAL DIFFUSER

A numerical study of dilute turbulent gas-solid flow in a vertically oriented conical diffuser is compared with experimental data from the literature. The solids-phase is modeled by an Eulerian approach based upon the kinetic theory of granular flow. The k-omega SST and Reynolds stress model (RSM) with turbulence modulation terms are used to estimate the gas-phase turbulence. Profiles of gas-phase axial velocity downstream the diffuser show good agreement with experimental data. The RSM correctly predicts turbulence modulation at the analyzed downstream positions. The effect of particle collisions and wall-boundary condition on the pressure recovery is analyzed in detail. This work demonstrates that interparticle and particle-turbulence interactions need to be considered within the Eulerian framework to correctly simulate the dilute gas-solid flow in a diffuser
