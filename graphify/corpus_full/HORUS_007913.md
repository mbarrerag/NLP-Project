# Educational program for the prevention of fires and explosions through emerging technologies

Four real cases of fire and explosions in Colombia were used as a basis for an educational programme that aimed to train undergraduate and graduate students in fire and explosion safety engineering. A systematic review of available emerging technologies (ETs) indicated the most important for fire and explosion safety. The four real cases of fire and explosions in Colombia were selected from a set of incidents collected from local industry as the most suitable for educational processes. The educational programme was based on four premises: (i) Bloom taxonomy application; (ii) empowering and promoting student autonomy; (iii) using real-life problems; (iv) application of modules in compulsory courses. The programme was implemented in courses of different undergraduate and postgraduate engineering careers. The evaluation of the programme was carried out by comparing the perspective of experts and students on the required skills in fire and explosion safety, before and after students were exposed to educational modules. Overall, students ' perception of competencies considered important for fire and explosion safety increased after exposure to the educational programme
