# Obtaining organic carbonates from chemical fixation of CO2 with cyclohexene oxide catalyzed by functionalized metal-organic networks with Zr and Ce

The growing economic development along with the excessive use of fossil fuels has caused the atmospheric concentration of carbon dioxide (CO2) on the planet to increase, year after year, generating disastrous consequences in the climate of the planet, in public health, in water systems, biodiversity, among others. Thus, it is necessary to develop scientific-technological strategies that contribute to the processes of CO2 capture and that allow their valorization in products of high added value. According to the above, this research project proposes the study of a sustainable catalytic transformation that valorises CO2 turning into organic carbonates, which have interesting industrial and commercial applications. In addition, this proposal is articulated within the framework of the commitments made by Colombia in the United Nations Framework Convention, to reduce by 20% its emissions by 2030. It is thus the proposal that establishes the catalytic performance of catalysts type metal-organic (MOFs) in the process of the petroleum networks (MOFs), as well as the functionalized with amino acids in the reaction of CO2 cycleaddition with cycloexeno oxide (OCH), as a low-cost substrate of the metal-based. (ii) Catalytic tests in the reaction of CO2 cycleaddition with OCH. In which the effect of CO2 pressure and temperature on the conversion and selectivity of the reaction will be studied through a factorial design of experiments and subsequent statistical analysis and, (iii) Structural characterisation of the products of the catalytic reaction using spectroscopic and/or chromatographic techniques. The scope of the proposal is to establish the correlation between the physicochemical properties of the catalysts synthesized with the catalytic performance of the reaction based on the hypothesis that the materials display an exceptional acid-base cooperative effect of Lewis to catalyze efficiently the production of organic carbonates in the process. Finally, in the context of the development of the proposal, the linkage and training of students at the undergraduate (Gradework) and postgraduate level (Maestry Thesis advance) level will be included in the dissemination and appropriation of knowledge provided in the research semillero "Co2 validation and catalytic processes"
