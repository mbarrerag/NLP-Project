# Methodology for monitoring, spatial and morphological control in construction, supported by tools of the BIM methodology, for concrete structures in construction projects in Colombia.

In Colombia, we find in the execution of the National Strategy BIM 2020-2026 which has as objective the implementation of this methodology in the public estates of the country, and with this we promote the modernization in the construction sector, also, we highlight other initiatives like the BIM Forum Colombia, promoted by CAMACOL, and the increasing number of companies that have found in the BIM, the tools that they needed to give a technical solution to their projects.However, and despite being adopting with enthusiasm the fundamental principles of the methodology, already in practice, this, in most cases, manages to adapt partially to the processes of design and coordination, and in few occasions it comes to have some real impact in the construction, where, after having developed three-dimensional models able to simulate the reality, quantify, detect errors and monitor processes, these are translated to traditional languages of bidimensional planimetrics where they lose most of their real capacities.
