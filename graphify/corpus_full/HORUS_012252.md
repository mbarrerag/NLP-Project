# Methodology for the reduction of the non-detection zone in island condition of distribution systems with high penetration of distributed generation based on inverters

A change is taking place in the electrical system, where the distribution networks begin to have a different behavior and new electrical phenomena are observed that are objects of study, mainly, these changes are given by the high penetration of distributed generation, being an alternative method where there are generation sources near the points of consumption in the network, causing the circulation of bidirectional power flows, both for normal operating conditions and for failure. One of the problems is islanding, which occurs when the main feeder of the network is out of service and the loads near the distributed generation connection point are fed. When this microgrid is formed, the islanding condition must be detected quickly to stop energizing the load in service, however, current detection methods have weaknesses when it comes to detecting islanding when there is a balance between generation distributed based on inverters and the load demanded from the network. In this work, a method for the reduction of the non-detection zone (NDZ) is proposed, based on the measurements of a relay in a simulation software, which detects the variation of the frequency and the injection of power disturbances, when a distribution network is in an island condition
