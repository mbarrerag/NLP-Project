# A METHODOLOGICAL PROPOSAL FOR THE ASSESSMENT OF THE STATUS IN TERMS FOR THE STORAGE OF ENERGY WITH BATTERY (BESS) USED BY KPIS

Lithium-ion batteries have been used in various applications of consumer electronics and their high efficiency has allowed their use in the energy sector, especially in electrical mobility projects and power system storage applications. For this reason, obtaining an estimate of their operating condition is relevant to ensure reliability in the electrical grid. This work presents a methodology for evaluating the condition in battery storage systems (BESS battery storage systems) through key performance indicators (KPIs Key Performance Indicators). This approach serves as a guide for energy companies and owners to make decisions during the system life cycle. This methodology is implemented in a computational tool called RENOBAT, which facilitates monitoring of the BESS condition by considering design, construction and operation information
