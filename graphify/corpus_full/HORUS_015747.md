# THE INTEGROUS MANAGER

This document is constructed with the purpose of providing tools to people who aim to be managers, or who already have that role, regardless of gender, productive sector, manufacturing, services, among others; the nature of the organization, public, private or solidarity, or management level, from the first line to the top management level. In the first chapter, management issues focused on the person, including emotional salary, are explained as a contribution to new organizational trends. In the second chapter, traditional managerial levels and a particular approach are analyzed where the different responsibilities and skills associated with functions in each type of direction, abilities, competencies and main features of those who intend to reach managerial positions are related. In the third chapter, the tools necessary for good organizational performance based on knowledge and skills, expertise and leadership characteristics associated with the position within the organization are related. The fourth chapter is focused on ethics, which was taken from the doctoral thesis of Laura Marcela Londoño Vásquez, and explains each of the moral values she describes in the doctoral thesis, which in particulars of the current society have been undervalued
