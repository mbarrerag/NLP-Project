# Strategies for local cultural management: approach from Villamaría, Caldas

This article aims to show the design of local cultural management strategies articulated in municipal cultural policy with local development processes in the municipality of Villamaría, Caldas. The research was qualitative in nature and developed from four phases, namely, revision of the bibliographical collection and theoretical references; design of information collection instruments - field work; validation and analysis of information; and formulation of strategies. Finding: The administration provides important references and instruments for cultural action in the territories, such as strategic planning, and from it the local cultural management is nourished by inputs and operational tools, which, by systematically incorporating them, contribute to institutional strengthening and in a more comprehensive way to the consolidation of municipal cultural development. The exercise of local cultural management is directly inserted in the planning, execution and general promotion of the cultural sector. It is an exercise that must be accompanied by municipal agents and actors for the achievement of short, medium and long term goals.
