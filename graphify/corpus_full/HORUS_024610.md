# OWN VOICE, AJENA WORD: POSTURE AND AUTHONOMY OF THE BODY IN CREATION PROCESS

In this chapter I propose categories of subjective dialogues to understand the creation processes that occur within the framework of an academic institution. Based on the need to rethink the place, posture and voice as necessary elements for an independence of thought and therefore the place of the word autonomy (which is so much mentioned in academic and educational processes at present), I pose three relational moments from which the continuous creation of three dialogue bodies emerges. I sit in the notion of corporealized education to understand these bodies as bodies that respond to creation from resistance, pointing or leading to excision, and validation from contact with the skin: the formed/formed body, the forming/forming body and the classroom/cage body.
