# TECHNOLOGICAL ADVANCES IN THE CULTIVATION OF UCHUVA (PHYSALIS PERUVIANA L.) IN COLOMBIA..

ABSTRACT The uchuva is a yellow-orange fruit that grows within a closed calyx, being probably from Peru. It is a shrub plant that grows to a height of 1.0-1,5 m. Round fruits, usually between 4 and 10 g, contain high concentrations of ascorbic acid, beta-carotene, phosphorus and iron. In Colombia they are mainly produced in the departments of Boyacá, Cundinamarca and Nariño at altitudes of 1.800 to 2.700 msnm, with annual temperatures of 13-16°C and precipitations between 1.000 and 1.800 mm year -1. The uchuva can be classified moderately tolerant to salinity. The crops are established in distances between 2-3×2 m (between rows and plants) using the tutoring system formed by two galvanized wires, 1 m distant in height of 2 m above plants, to which they are tied with the reproductive branches. According to agroecological conditions the harvests begin after 5-7 months and can follow up to 1.5 to 2 years of cultivation.
