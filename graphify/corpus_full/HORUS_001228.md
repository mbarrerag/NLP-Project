# GEOMETRY UNDERLYING OF THE GAUGE PRINCIPLE

In this study, we investigate the underlying geometry of the gauge principle as an alternative way for understanding particle interactions. We employ the linear sigma model, based on the global'local symmetry group O (N+ 1), reducing to O (N) after a procedure of spontaneous symmetry breaking.
