# Analysis of internal, external, brand, employer and brand value related marketing strategies

Although the literature has studied the welfare of employees and the value of a brand with relative depth, both from objective (observable data) and subjective points of view (non-observable data), there is not enough evidence of the types and strength of the relationship between the two variables., this project will analyze in principle the literature on variables for later, to propose an empirical validation on the topic
