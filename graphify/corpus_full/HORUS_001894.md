# Design of a risk-based continuous audit instrument for the implementation of extension projects within the Faculty of Engineering and Architecture - Manizales headquarters

The research proposes a risk-based audit tool, focused on improving risk management of extension projects at the Faculty of Engineering and Architecture of the National University of Colombia. Since higher education institutions face increasing demands of efficiency, transparency and quality, this tool would allow continuous and effective monitoring of projects, detecting problems in time to optimize decision-making. The proposal fills a gap in knowledge identified through bibliometric analysis and literature review, as there is little research on audit continues to be applied to extension projects in the academic field. This study not only seeks to improve management in the university, but will also offer a reference framework for other educational institutions with similar needs. By implementing this tool, a culture of continuous improvement and a proactive response to contingities will be promoted, contributing to sustainable development .
