# BUILDING, STRUCTURES AND TRANSMISSION OF KNOWLEDGE IN THE ARCHITECTURE OF THE 20th CENTURY. ARMED HORMIGONE LASMINS OF THE FELIX ARCHITECT CANDELA IN COLOMBIA.

This book describes and analyzes, making use of correspondence between Mexican Hispanic architect Félix Candela and Colombian engineers and architects, the way in which the constructive technical knowledge related to reinforced concrete was transmitted and discussed at the beginning of the second half of the 20th century
