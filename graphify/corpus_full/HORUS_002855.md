# ENGINEERING OF SOFTWARE PROCESS

This publication is a practical guide that explores principles, techniques, standards, applications, examples and contributions made in the area of software processes. Software process engineering approaches software development from a process-oriented approach as fundamental elements for successfully building software products. Software process can be considered as a series of activities, procedures, roles and artifacts related to the delivery of high quality products on time and within the established budget. Practitioners, teachers, researchers and stakeholders who wish to know and deepen in this type of processes will find a source of consultation and initial reference point to the fascinating world of software development from a standard-based and international benchmark perspective of industry and academia. This book aims to: (a) serve as a text that guides teachers, students, practitioners and software engineering stakeholders in knowing aspects related to the software process; (b) enable readers to understand the definition, modelling, measurement, execution, control and improvement of software processes; (c) understand the current state of research in the area of software processes in order to establish new routes and research questions that, as they are resolved, strengthen the knowledge of the area, and (d) provide a successful knowledge-based approach to software
