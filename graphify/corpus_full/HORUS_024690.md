# Preliminary study of the interaction of geminivirus using tomato and tobacco plants as model.

Numerous diseases caused by begomovirus have been the result of pseudorecombination events, which involves transcomplementation and transreplication processes. The yellow potato mosaic virus (PYMV) is a begomovirus that affects tomato cultivation in Colombia, the golden croton mosaic virus (CohGMV), the golden rhynchosia mosaic virus in Colombia (RhGCV), and the heterologous plant virus (VeMV) were isolated from the arvenses Croton hirtus, Rhynchosia minima and Verbena sp. respectively, all of them associated with tomato cultivation in Valle del Cauca. The aim of this work was to evaluate the possibility of presenting the heterologous transcomplementation events between the genomic components A and B of the begomoviruses, PYMV-B, CoGMviruses isolated from these arvens and PYMV.
