# MODEL FOR THE ANALYSIS OF THE STRATEGY OF OPERATIONS OF EXPORTING COMPANIES IN THE METAL-MEchanical SECTOR OF THE COLOMBIA COFFEE TRIANGUL

The objective of this study is to statistically validate a model for the analysis of the strategy of operations of exporting companies in the metalmechanical sector of the coffee triangle of Colombia. A theoretical construct is developed based on existing analysis models and based on the judgement of experts to determine the categories and variables required. The use of correlation statistical technique allowed validating with highly significant results that the model was constructed with the appropriate competitive priorities for the sector and type of selected companies. A model of integral operations strategy was obtained that evaluates seven categories: cost, quality, process flexibility, product flexibility, delivery, service and environment. It is concluded that this model is adapted to the needs of the companies studied and can be used in similar ones. Key words: modeling; processes; strategy; operations; metal mechanic sector; export
