# Disca, diverse and dissident observatory. A critical space to transform the policies of difference management in Colombia

The Observatory disca, diverse and dissident proposes as a scenario for critical deliberation around the oppressions that affect people and communities living disabilities, bodily diversity and dissidencies embodied in Colombia. Our central intention is to monitor, understand and contribute to the transformation of those structural problems that go through the lives of such populations through policies that seek to build conditions of future so that they enjoy lives increasingly dignified, felt, realized and free. This space of jointness summons agents of change of multiple disciplines and origins to conjure possible solutions aimed at solving the violences and systematic violations that have marked the discaly subjects, diverse and dissidents of Colombia. Not only from efforts that aim to understand how they have operated matrices of power such as patriarchy, capacitism and modernity/coloniality in various Colombian regions, but also with the constant search for alternatives of action that allow to overthrow each of the oppressions and communities in the country.
