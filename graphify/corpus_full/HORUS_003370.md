# INTEGRATED MONITORING SYSTEM OF OCEANOGRAPHYC, CLIMATE AND HYDROGEOLOGICAL VARIABLES IN SAN ANDRES ISLA

Currently, the sustainable management of marine resources is fundamental, especially considering the risks arising from climate variability and overexploitation of these and that impact the marine environment.This study focuses on the implementation of an integrated monitoring system in San Andrés, seeking to characterize and evaluate the environmental and oceanographic conditions of the region. Between August 2023 and March 2024, three field campaigns were carried out in collaboration with CORALINA in the framework of the project promoted by MinCiencia and the General System of Regalias SGR called "How to take advantage of the potential of sea water, through the development of a prototype for alternative energy production coupled with the system of desalination of sea water in San Andrés Island", in which they were installed in different strategic points of the island and significantly enrich the database of oceanographic variables, weather and hydrogeological, also making it an invaluable tool to understand and predict changes in the local marine environment
