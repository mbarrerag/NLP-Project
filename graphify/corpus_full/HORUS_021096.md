# Adaptation to climate change from a regional perspective in territorial planning

The need to adapt safely and effectively to the consequences of climate change, focusing on the contribution that can and should be made in terms of territorial planning, is the focus of the research project. Ultimately, what is sought is to properly relate environmental management, with the country's territorial planning, at the regional level. The project covers the period 2011-2022 and is based on secondary sources of information from state entities, non-governmental organizations, universities and/or private entities. On the other hand, they will use qualitative analysis tools such as interviews and fieldwork, as well as spatial analysis tools specific to geographic information systems. It is then intended to apply an analysis from the regional scale to define an area of study in which development adaptation to climate change can be explored in detail and propose more precise actions if necessary.
