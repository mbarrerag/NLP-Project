# The Context of the Symphonic Direction of the Municipal Band of Fómeque Cundinamarca. An experience from basic musical processes and multilevel

This paper addresses the context of the Synfonic Direction in the Municipal Band of Fómeque Cundinamarca and how from the management, pedagogy and methodology aspects a successful result is achieved in the musical context of a municipal synfonic band formed by non-professional musicians with a multilevel musical approach, heterogeneous in ages and abilities. This monographic work will first account for how management makes it possible for the technical aspects of management to develop in an appropriate environment for their development, second it will describe how the musical methodology for band is addressed and designed, based on the correlation between the composer as an issue subject, his aesthetic concept and the director as a propagator that is who expresses and communicates to the grouping the discourse and subsequently reproduces the interpretative proposal turning all this into the sound effect that allows us to have a look at the depth of the director's work from all structural aspects to its work, first phase and in the specific sector that music is. .
