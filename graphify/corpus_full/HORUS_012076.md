# Characterization of civil air transport in Colombia for 2020 and 2021.

currently the aeromedical transport is a well established and reliable operation, which arises as an option to mobilize patients from a remote or unexceivable place via land to a medical care site that supplies all the needs that the individual requires. Colombia has a good health coverage, but the distribution of services of high complexity is centralized, so the aeromedical transport service provides support in the guarantee of timely health care for inhabitants of remote territories that do not have institutions of high complexity. Given the above it is of interest to characterize the civil operation of the air ambulances along the national territory and to identify the main pathologies of the patients transported; to this end, the statistical approach to the current situation of the aeromedical transport services was established. an observational, descriptive, cross-sectional type study was proposed, which sought to characterize the various factors involved in the civil aeromedical transport in Colombia in the years 2020 and 2021. a caseuistics so large in Latin America; the most frequent diagnoses reported differ from those found in the literature, which indicates that the pathologies with the highest incidence are cardiovascular, neurological and trauma; in our investigation the context of the pandemic by SARS-CoV-2 favored a greater representation of respiratory pathologies transported. Based on the information reported, the importance of aeromedical transport in Colombia can be evidenced and as this provides a complementary service to guarantee high complexity care in these territories
