# The systematization of the housing in height: formal and spatial logics in the work of Jiménez & Cortés Boshell

This research shows the urban, formal and spatial logics in the construction work of the building of multi-family housing in height of the firm Jiménez & Cortés Boshell. By means of the analysis of three systems: urban, formal and spatial, five projects representative of the firm J&CB: building Residences Calle 17, building Calle 85 Carrera 10, building Residences Jorge Cortés Boshell, building Avenida 82 and building Virrey. The architectural work of Ernesto Jiménez and Álvaro Boshell with emphasis on multi-family housing buildings in height in the city of Bogotá, specifically developed in five decades from 1960 to 2005, corresponded to the lifestyle of the traditional family usually belonging to a high socioeconomic stratum. The analyses are presented showing the way to implement, compose and distribute the multi-family housing building in three scales: the unit, the grouping and the whole. Thus, this research proposes a systematic procedure presented from three premises: the insertion of the building into a traditional block that constitutes the vertical scale of the city, the logic structure of the architectural object that determines the formal composition, and the functional distribution of the housing unit, group and the whole.
