# RISK MANAGEMENT FOR PRIMARY PREVENTION OF TUBERCLESIS IN AN ADMINISTRATORY ENTITY OF BENEFITS PLANS IN HEALTH IN COLOMBIA

Objective To design and validate a model for the management of risk in health, aimed at reducing the incidence of tuberculosis in the population affiliated to the Colombian benefit plan (EAPB) management companies from the perspective of primary prevention of the disease. Methods Based on an inductive reflection, a model of tuberculosis care was designed aimed at coordinating actions of comprehensive risk management in health in the context of a model of insurance based on primary health care (APS). A facial validation and content of the model was carried out with thematic experts in the tuberculosis control program of some APBs and other sectors. Results The main aspects, strengths and opportunities for improvement were identified that were used as central elements for the model, which is aimed at preventing the development of the disease, while continuing to promote follow-up of treatments and rehabilitation processes. The expert review allowed validating and enriching the proposed design. Discussion The health risk management is a responsibility assigned within the Colombian health system to the EAPB. The design of this model contributes to the implementation of risk management in an organized, defined and standardized manner, in order to obtain better results in the prevention of tuberculosis
