# Factors associated with 5-year survival and costs in the care of patients with renal transplantation in Colombia.

Renal transplantation is the ideal renal replacement therapy for advanced chronic renal disease, given its impact on mortality reduction and costs compared with dialysis. In Colombia, it is necessary to establish own records on patient survival, costs and prognostic factors in the long term in post-renal transplantation patients. To determine the patient's survival rate, health care costs and their associated factors for 5 years in post-transplant renal patients belonging to the contributory regimen in Colombia. Retrospective cohort study using administrative health records, included 1437 patients [IC= IC/C, post-contributive, from 1 January 2013 to 31 December 2014. Analyses were performed to determine 5 years: 1) patient survival, 2) direct costs from the third payer perspective and 3) associated factors; by Poisson regressions, linear regressions and multivariate regression models, respectively.
