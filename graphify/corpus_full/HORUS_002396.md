# TRAINING IN ETHICAL ASPECTS FROM GAMES-BASED TOOLS

The need to inculcate and train students and society in ethics in general is becoming more evident every day, given the increasing cases of corruption and lack of ethics that are presented at all levels of society around the world. On the other hand, the use of games as tools within the teaching-learning processes at academic and organizational levels, takes on greater relevance and acceptance. The aim of this research is to reflect on the training in ethics and moral values and the tools used in the teaching-learning processes of such themes, especially those related to games, which allow for more meaningful and experiential learning. A review of game-based tools literature was carried out, whose objective in training in some specific aspect of ethics. Subsequently, the game Valoret is shown as a proposal for training in moral values, which was developed from a methodology for designing serious games that proposes three fundamental phases, which allow iterating in the design process and achieving the final version of the game Valoret in table games and video game format
