# Factors associated with survival in adult patients with pancreatectomy-induced pancreatectomy in Colombia.

Pancreatic cancer has a high mortality, associated with its usually late diagnosis, in stages III and IV, which limits its management. Seeing that only 20-30% of patients are diagnosed in early stages and are candidates for surgical management, pancreatectomy being the only option for curative management, with variable impact on the short- and long-term survival of patients taken to pancreatectomy. Thus, early mortality rates of up to 5% have been described in various international series. In Colombia there are few studies describing the survival results of patients taken to pancreatectomy, specifically by pancreatectomy, nor studies of this class at national level.
