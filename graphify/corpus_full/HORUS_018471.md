# CHAPTER 18- PRE-MEMBRAN RUPTURA

Chapter 18- Introduction- Premature rupture of membranes (RPM) occurs in 2% to 3.5% of pregnancies and is responsible for 30% to 40% of preterm births. (1)(2) The interval between membrane rupture and delivery is usually short and still with expectant management 50 to 60% of women with PMR away from the term have their delivery within the week following membrane rupture. The etiology of PMR is multifactorial, the infection being the most frequent, is often subclinical and is believed to ascend from the lower genital tract the following topics are exposed: Definition Physiopathology Diagnostic etiology Management Medicines Complications of the PMR References
