# AMENDMENTS IN THE NORMATIVE REGULATING THE LEGAL CAPACITY OF PERSONS IN SITUATION/CONDITION OF MENTAL DISABLEDNESS IN COLOMBIA

Taking into account the transformation of the legal relations of persons in situations/conditions of mental disability in Colombia, and especially the important reform to the aspect of the exercise of legal capacity of persons with mental disabilities, through qualitative documentary research, this chapter presents the main changes introduced by the Law 1996 of 2019 to the autonomy and legal capacity of persons in situations/conditions of disability in Colombia. Thus, the main aspects of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities and the interpretation of the exercise of legal capacity carried out by the Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, the advances in jurisprudence and the reasons that led to changes in the regime for the exercise of legal capacity of persons with disabilities, the main characteristics and scope of the new regulation recognizing that all persons with disabilities are active subjects of rights and legal capacity
