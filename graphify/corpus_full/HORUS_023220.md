# Machine learning and fractal attributes applied to automatic detection and classification of geologic plans in resistance image logs.

The purpose of this research is to develop a methodology for the automatic detection and classification of geological planes in resistive images acquired with cable. The methodology involves application of bioinspired filters, machine learning and elements of fractal theory. In the upstream activities of hydrocarbon deposits, the analysis and interpretation of resistive images is a repetitive task, which takes a lot of time and usually performed manually by specialized geologists. The delay in this task can result in operational problems, causing economic losses during field operations. To solve this problem, this research proposes the automatic extraction of geological characteristics in the images of resistivity of the well shortly after the acquisition of the image record. The characteristics are initially extracted from the analysis of pixels of normalized dynamic images, using a set of computer vision techniques to detect edges and sinusoids. Subsequently, these sinusoids are classified using statistical measures and automatic learning. During the stages of training and classification, the machines use the fractal dimension, laclarity and their derived statistical parameters (fractal attributes); the fractal information is complemented by the images of resistivity, gamma rays and photoelectric factors to improve the accuracy of the environment
