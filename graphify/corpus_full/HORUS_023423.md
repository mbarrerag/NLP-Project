# Evaluation of crystallization of palm oil fats as binders in pressed powder seasonings

The process of crystallisation of two fats derived from palm oil with different melting points was evaluated, during the maturation process, at -15°C, 4°C and 20°C for 12 hours, of a seasoning formulation in pressed powder, as well as its effect on the physical and chemical properties of moisture, flow factor and texture, and ease of use by consumers. The crystallization process was studied by means of the techniques of differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) and X-ray diffraction (XRD). The humidity was measured by means of an infrared balance and the flow factor by means of the Powder Flow Tester (PFT). The texture properties were evaluated using a texture analyzer and through a demenuzability panel with 30 consumers. It was possible to show that the maturation temperature affects the flow factor (ffc) of the seasoners formed without being broken down by the body and body. significant different between samples highlighting the importance of validating this process at real production conditions. These results are of interest for the bouquet industry while it states appropriate conditions for the maturing stage, highly important for obtaining superior quality products and allows for advancement in the investigation of real foods and their components
