# Working conditions and accidentality of transit in taxi drivers of the Municipality of Soacha

The accidentality of transit has become a public health problem, for several years all countries are obliged to contribute to the reduction and control of this phenomenon. The objective of this research was to analyze the working conditions and accident of transit in taxi drivers of the municipality of Soacha, Colombia. The design of the study was of cross-sectional quantitative cut with a descriptive correlational scope. It was used as population N:200 and a sample n:54 drivers. The instrument that was applied was the II National Survey of Working Conditions designed by the Ministry of Labour of Colombia. It is evident that the working conditions of taxi drivers are consistent with other studies, as regards the excess of working hours and lack of guarantee in minimum working conditions. After the correlation analysis of the variables, some significant correlations were evidenced between the working conditions with the accidentality of transit, such as the time of transfer, order, rhythm and distribution of tasks.
