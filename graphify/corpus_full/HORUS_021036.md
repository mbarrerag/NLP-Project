# The houses of the architect. Study of the conception of living in six houses of Bruno Violi for Bruno Violi

This master's thesis characterizes the contrast relationship between enclosure and lodge as a means of building intimacy linked to the landscape, starting from three considerations; the first because the action of re-sealing is associated with the concave and interiorized, with the construction of a limit where man is contained, seeking to supply the primordial need for refuge; the second because the formal nature of the lodge is associated with the concave, with glimpse of the horizon and the connection of man with the context; the third because from the relationship of the proportions of space with man, between the enclosures and the lodges, allows the individual to appropriate the landscape. In this sense, three (3) houses designed by Bruno Violi for himself and his family between 1949 and 1957 in Colombia are selected, because the three houses have isolation strategies related to the geographical context in which they are located; because there are formal strategies for the observation of different landscapes, one close to home and one distant hostile; because there is an manipulation of the dimensions in relation to the human proportion in the journey, which starts from the exterior to the concavos spaces, and between the concaves spaces
