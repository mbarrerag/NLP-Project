# COLLABORATIVE ASSISTANCE STRATEGY FOR THE HEALTH SECTOR DERIVED FROM CLINICAL CIVIDUAL STUDY-19

Abstract Hospital logistics comprises a set of activities associated with the supply, storage and distribution process of drugs and other inputs necessary for the clinical management of patients. This work presents the design of a collaborative drug supply strategy that allows the conduct of a clinical study of Covid-19, minimizing interruptions, costs and supply times. This work was carried out in three phases: in the first one, a context analysis and a review of the standards of each of the participating health-providing institutions (IPS) were carried out; in the second, protocols and manuals were designed for the supply and inventory management processes; and in the third, processes previously designed during the development of clinical research were implemented. As a result of this work, a strategy based on a collaborative model of purchasing and inventory management, composed of three pharmaceutical laboratories, a blending centre and ten IPS. In addition, processes were designed for the management and traceability of inventories in a collaborative way. As a result, these results can be used to evaluate the coordination of the entire hospital supply chain (CSH) in a pandemic scenario. Key words: supply, Covid-19, clinical study, logistic study
