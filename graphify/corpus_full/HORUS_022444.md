# EARLY RICARD AND THE CONGRESES OF IMPROVES (1917-1946). BASIC FOR A URBAN PLANNING IN COLOMBIA

The text presents the dissemination of architecture, urban planning and city themes through the so-called congresses of national improvements conferred between 1917 and 1946, a period in which Ricardo Olano Estrada, an important character of the anti-Oqueña elite, urbanist without training and main promoter of these events, maintained an active participation. The work is supported by a documentary research, whose objective contributes to the architectural and urbanistic interpretations of the modern city in Medellín between 1926 and 1947, since the discourses of Ricardo Olano Estrada. Methodologically, two stages are carried out: the first arises from a bibliographic review of the problem, and the second, from the search for archive material and specialized serial magazines of the time, having as main source the Revista Progreso, organ of dissemination of the Society of Public Improvements of Medellín. The results presented in the text have a national involvement; that is to say, that they are only of potential interest for the history of urban planning in Colombia
