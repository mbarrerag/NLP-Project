# IDENTIFICATION OF NATIVE PLANTS OF UNUNIFABLE ARAUCA SABANS, WITH POTENTIAL USE IN WINTERANCE

The study of different species of the water-influenced savanna of Arauca, in the botanical, phenological and nutritional fields, are relevant to be able to promote projects that seek to strengthen livestock from the implementation of productive systems different to monocultures. To identify native plants of floodable savannas of Arauca with foraging potential, as a nutritional alternative for farming systems of the region. Seven tours were carried out by the plant communities of mountain bushes (vegetal community developed in the middle of an open sheet), gallery forests (riparous forests, around hydric sources) and open savanna (majorly interventioned landscape, with little or no development of tree vegetation) between the months of February and April of 2021. Species were collected, which had a potential of production of fodder biomass. 500 g of leaves of selected species were taken, under a population sampling for each species, within 100 m of georeferential point diameter, the identification, biological characterization and codification for the data management were dried at 60 °C by 48 h
