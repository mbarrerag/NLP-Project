# USE 100 YEARS OF FIGHT AND DIGNITY

This book goes between the memory and history of oil workers, combining a rigorous photographic and documentary selection with analytical texts on the social and political history of the union organization in its 100 years of existence. Under this logic, it aims to become a heritage of documentary and photographic type, in which an essential part of the history of the US is reflected, transcending the political and organizational aspects, to include, among others, sport, daily life, the functioning of the enclaves, the persecution and cornering of the indigenous communities by the oil companies and violence against the workers
