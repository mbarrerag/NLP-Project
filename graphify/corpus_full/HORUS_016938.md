# VERDE TAXES AND WINNERSHIP: A DEBATE WHERE THE GIVE PENAL IS VALUE

A few weeks ago, a debate was revived on Twitter about a supposed possibility of imposing taxes on meat and milk in Colombia. Ideas in this direction are not new. Months ago, the Mayor of Bogotá presented a decree to promote the practice of a meatless day in the city. The Mayor of Medellín in 2017 tried to process an agreement of the Council (on December 076, 2017), to implement a green food in the municipality of Medellín and its decentralized entities. “Opportunally, that initiative was denounced (Caracol Radio, 2017) because it violates fundamental rights, especially of children and adolescents, as set forth in the country’s Constitution. These initiatives may have a commendable intention, however, as they say, “the road to hell is paved with good intentions.”
