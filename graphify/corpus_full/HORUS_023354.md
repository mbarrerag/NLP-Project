# Editorial: Critical Accounting in Latin America: trajectories, interactions and conversations between North and South.

The special issue seeks to gather, make visible and disseminate intellectual efforts that the Latin American critical accounting community is making, to problematize, understand and intervene our organizational and social contextual reality.But at the same time, it aims to contribute to dialogue, debate and reflection on the field of critical accounting between academics from the South and the North
