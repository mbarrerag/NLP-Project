# THE SOILS OF COLOMBIA PARARES AND THEIR HYDROPHISICAL PROPERTIES

The moors are ecosystems located towards the high part of the Andean mountains, between the upper limit of the forests and the lower limit of the nival zones, and are subject to considerable daily variations in temperature, a high relative humidity of air, high solar radiation, and frequent presence of fog, which confers specific properties, mainly to their soils, rich in organic matter and acid pH. These ecosystems have been considered as environments of high yield and water regulation; however the variables behind this operation are not clear, so it is necessary to carry out research related, not only with water working ducho, but also with the role of the soils in these processes. For this purpose, an investigation of the physical and hydraulic properties of the soils in seven páramos of Colombia (Belmira, Guerrero, Chingaza, Parque Nevados, Romerales-Berlin, Frontino and Chili-Barragán) was carried out at different depths in each site and in different places of each mare, which are analyzed by the materials in laboratory. The results indicate that in general terms of the soil included in this study, can be used as indicative of these properties in the moors of Colombia
