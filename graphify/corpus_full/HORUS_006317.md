# A descriptive-correlational study of moral judgments on euthanasia based on the preference for life values and autonomy

The morality of euthanasia has generated an important public debate in recent years. However, the moral judgments on this practice have not yet received an adequate description. For this reason, the main objective of this research is to describe the moral judgments on euthanasia based on the preference for the values of life and autonomy. Based on a theoretical review of some philosophical and psychological perspectives on morality, as well as an empirical review of the studies on euthanasia, two main hypotheses of research were formulated. The first hypothesis states that the preference for the value of life is associated with the moral condemnation of euthanasia, while the second hypothesis states that the preference for the value of autonomy is associated with the moral approval of euthanasia.
