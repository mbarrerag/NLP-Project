# Walking the City: Urban Walking and Accessibility in Medellín

In the midst of a condition of climate crisis and social inequality, the issues of sustainability and quality of life take on importance.The relationship between active mobility and urban accessibility, contribute directly to promote full participation and enjoyment for all people of services, public spaces and equipments. Promoting conditions in which any citizen can move and participate safely and efficiently in urban life, regardless of their age, gender, physical or cognitive capacity, is fundamental to create more inclusive and environmentally sustainable cities. The term "caminobility" refers to the ability of an environment to be walked comfortably and safely. In a context of active mobility, it is sought to promote the walkability and use of bicycles as everyday modes of transport. This not only has benefits for health and the environment, but also contributes to greater social interaction and to the construction of stronger communities. Measuring the walkability implies considering a wide range of physical and cultural factors that affect the experience of walking in a city.
