# Morphology and bone morphometry of the hip in the adult population of the city of Bogotá-Colombia.

The anatomic characterization of the hip has been shown to be significantly variable between population groups (1,2,3); these morphological differences are associated with intrinsic factors such as age, sex and ethnicity, or extrinsic factors such as the environment and lifestyle (4.5). There are multiple descriptive studies of the geometry of the proximal femur and the acetylbulus in different countries and groups of individuals belonging to an ethnic group, however, the knowledge of the morphology and bone morphometry of the hip in the city of Bogotá comes mainly from these studies centered in foreign population as there is no anatomic description of the hip in the present based on our population. Based on these differences and variability, this study aims to describe the morphology and bone morphometry of the hip in adults without articular pathology that allows to be applied to detect early structural anomalies and improve treatment approaches. A descriptive study was carried out by means of simple hip AP x-rays, obtained in the last 2 years of the database of two private institutions of the city of Bogotán. The first description of the morphological and morphometric characteristics of the hip is made in a sample of the population of the city of Bogotá-Colombia.
