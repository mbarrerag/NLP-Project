# A critical look at the National Music Plan for Coexistence PNMC - Publications aimed at children's choirs between 2003 and 2022

This paper aims to review the publications related to the National Plan of Music for Coexistence (PNMC), Area Coros, to know the impact and relevance from its contextualization, chronology and process through time; by looking at publications made between 2003 and 2022 to understand the importance of these publications and to consider their contribution to the construction of social capital, the process of forming the choirs and the rescue of the national repertoire. This work also reviews important pedagogical general aspects when considering this type of project. Within the methodology used, interviews are conducted with four teachers who were part of the development of the NPCM over the years, investigating their appreciation in terms of impact and publications.
