# Analysis and characterization of the atmospheric environment during radiative frosts in Colombia.

Radiative frosts in Colombia are a meteorological phenomenon of temperature decrease to 0 °C or less, by night cooling, which are harmful mainly for agricultural production in the Andean region of the country. For the above and by the relevance of the meteorological variations in the modulation of night cooling, the atmospheric conditions were analyzed at different spatial and temporal scale that can modify the occurrence of radiative frosts for Colombia. It was found that, depending on the altitude above sea level, the proportion of frosts has a bimodal cycle (areas above 4000 meters) with maximums in the first and middle quarters of the year, or unimodal (areas below 4000 meters) with maximum in the initial quarter of the year, in both cases due to being times of lower cloudiness, precipitation, humidity, temperature and magnitude in the winds.
