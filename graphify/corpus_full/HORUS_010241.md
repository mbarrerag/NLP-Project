# AGENCY AND AGENCY ` AS IF IN AmazONY: IMPLICATIONS FOR A SEMIOTICS OF ARTEFFACTS

This article offers elements for the understanding of the agency of the artifacts and of various types of agency. From examples taken from the field work carried out in the Amazon and ethnográco studies, is it argued that the human agency is de-ned in relation to the artifacts it produces. That relationship forms an augmented agency that emerges when designing and incorporating artifacts to expand the ability to act. It is proposed that the human agency is fundamentally augmented and that the ability to design is a primordial feature of our species. Additionally, agents can imagine or assume that an artifact behaves like a living being when they attribute agencies as if they were to the artifacts. The agency as if it did and its relationship with the augmented agency offers an alternative to the agency’s explanations of things and a new approach to the semiotics of the artifacts
