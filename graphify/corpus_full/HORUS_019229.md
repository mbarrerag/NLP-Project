# METHODOLOGICAL GUIDE FOR THE REVIEW OF AUTHORIZATION AND DETERMINATION OF CONTRAPRESTS BY PERMITS OF USE OF THE SPECTER

Title VII of the Colombian Constitution refers to the executive branch and chapter V provides for the public service. Article 209 of the Constitution stipulates that administrative authorities must coordinate their actions for the proper fulfilment of the State's purposes by establishing internal control. Article 269 of the Constitution, in turn, includes part of the regulations of the control bodies, requiring the design and application of internal control methods and procedures. In this context, Act No. 87 of 1993 establishes rules for the exercise of internal control in State entities and agencies and other provisions; and Decree No. 1537 of 2001 partially regulates the aforementioned law as regards technical and administrative elements that strengthen the internal control system of State bodies and bodies.
