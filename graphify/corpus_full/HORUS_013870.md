# --Characterization of Verticillus spp.: Causal agent of premature ripening of potato (Solanum tuberosum L.) in Cundinamarca

The potato in Colombia is within the basic species for food safety. However, in recent years an increase in the incidence of premature maturity of the crop has been reported. Among the diseases reported in potato, so far this disease has not been considered as limiting in the department of Cundinamarca. Premature maturity is caused by fungi of the genus Verticillion that can generate losses of up to 50% in the crop. Despite its importance, little information about this disease is available in the country and the species that predominate in the production areas of this department are not known. The aim of this research is to characterize isolations of Verticilium from batches of potato of Cundinamarca affected by premature maturity. The causal agent of the disease will be isolated from affected material in crops of the main producing municipalities. Subsequently, the identification of the species of Verticilium will be carried out by means of molecular tools and will be made morphological and biological characterization at in vitro level of the isolates obtained. Under greenhouse conditions, the pathogenicity of these isolates will be evaluated in three genotypes of potato will be determined
