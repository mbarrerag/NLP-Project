# Women in public policies associated with housing. Bogotá D.C. 2010 – 2022

This Final Master's Work (TFM) starts from understanding housing as a fundamental human right and recognizes the problems that women experience in a differentiated manner in the domestic space and concentrates on investigating their relationship with public policies and housing. To this end, a theoretical review is carried out in which concepts constructed by architects and feminist organizations that have documented women's relations with housing and its environment are identified. It also questions how much public policies have been permeated by feminist agendas in different scales of housing, in particular in Bogotá, based on a critical review of the regulations on women and housing. On the other hand, attention is being paid to how the pandemic intensified the crises for women in the domestic space in the midst of confinement, which from before the architects already evidenced, this work generates recommendations to clarify the incorporation of the gender approach into public policies associated with housing. . public policies associated with housing
