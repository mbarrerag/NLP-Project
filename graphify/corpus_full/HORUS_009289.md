# Design and implementation of an optical camera communication system that uses the rolling shutter method and color-shift modulation.

The world today is surrounded by wireless communications whose radio frequency spectrum is congested, leading to the search for other alternatives such as visible light communication that allows for sending data by modulating the light of an LED luminaire, fulfilling the function of lighting and data transmission with the same infrastructure, besides giving the possibility to deploy them in critical environments very sensitive to radio waves, such as aircraft and hospitals. With the arrival and diffusion of smartphones, most people today have a device with a camera, capable of performing a data processing, which makes the optical camera communications (such as VLC systems) have become an attractive area to investigate. Thus, this work presents the design and implementation of an optical camera communication system using the method of rolling shutter (RS) or roller shutter (s) and the CSK modulation, as well as the development of an Android application for the reception and processing of data transmitted by an RGB luminaire to a smartphone without eyeline, using a sermantic segmentation of a VG16–2x model
