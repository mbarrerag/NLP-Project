# CARBON POWDER A FRIEND ENEMY

The coal dust in mines has been ignored for a long time is such things as hygiene, cleanliness and safety. Often, we have become accustomed to living and working in an environment full of this dust without recognizing the risks involved. It is as if at home we get used to living with garbage, with waste, with fat, in any case. Surely our house is clean, tidy, pleasant. However, our workplace, where we really live, where we are 30-50 hours a week, is not so clean. For generations we get used to having the mine on all sides full of coal dust, and it is already the usual thing; we have not realized that that this dust can cause lung diseases, it damages our electrical equipment, mechanics, control boxes, and above all, many people still believe that it is harmless from the point of view of industrial safety; even that coal dust can cause explosions that are catastrophic in terms of human lives and damage to infrastructure
