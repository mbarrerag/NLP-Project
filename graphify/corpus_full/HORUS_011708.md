# VEHICLE ACTIVITY ESTIMATION FOR OVERVIEW OF ODOMETRO REGISTERS IN DIAGNOSTIC AUTOMATOR CENTERS

Passenger and cargo transport is indispensable for the economic development of municipalities and regions, however, most vehicles operate on fossil fuels, which produces a significant amount of polluting emissions into the atmosphere. Vehicle activity is considered a mobile source of pollutant emissions, therefore reliable quantification of these emissions is essential to develop accurate inventories, valuable for environmental governance and pollutant dispersion models. These models facilitate assertive decision-making to mitigate pollution and develop mobility plans
