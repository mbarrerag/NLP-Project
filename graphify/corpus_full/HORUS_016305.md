# BIOTECHNOLOGY APPLICABLE TO REPAIRING BASE-CETMENT MATERIALS BY USE OF BACCTERIES INDUSTRING THE PRECIPTATION OF CALCIUM CARBONATE

The base-cement materials are the materials most used in the construction industry due to their mechanical properties. However, with the use and passage of time these materials tend to crack what leads to a deterioration of the structure generating additional costs for their repair or replacement. Due to this, at industrial level different alternatives have been evaluated in search of a repair strategy, within which is found the use of bacteria that induce the precipitation of calcium carbonate (MICP) by means of hydrolysis of the urea. The search of new strains that perform this activity in these materials constitutes a line of promising research that allows to recover microorganisms from environmental matrices with suitable characteristics for the process, such as the mortars and concretes that have an alkaline pH. In this sense, the isolation, selection and characterization of materials that are made to date and use of 49 ureolite bacteria of which they have identified 3 genera that have not been previously reported. From this collection of microorganisms, 11 were selected that precipitate 99.7% of the calcium supplied in the medium of cultivation to perform tests of ureolitic activity.
