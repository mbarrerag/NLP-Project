# Evaluation of the co-creation ratio, customer engagement and e-hearing mouth recommendation in users of B2C e-commerce portals

This doctoral thesis evaluates the relationship between co-creation of value, client commitment and the oral recommendation to electronic mouth in users of e-commerce portals Business to Customer (B2C). To achieve this, it was necessary to investigate the origin, definitions, approaches, evolution, background, models and metrics of each of these variables by means of literature review. Subsequently, the theoretical model and hypotheses were generated, and the measurement instrument was designed. 2700 surveys of users of e-commerce platforms were collected in Colombia and Ecuador, which allowed validation of the model using the exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis. Using the technique of modeling structural equations, the hypotheses were contrasted. The results reveal a positive relationship between the three variables, and that the Mouth recommendation acts as a mediator between the client's commitment and the co-creation of value.
