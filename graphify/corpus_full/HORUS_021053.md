# Name: UMBRAL. Interstitial space as event generator in the Postgraduate Building of Human Sciences of Rogelio Salmona

The decade of the sixties represents in Colombia the incorporation into the local context of different conceptual components that resonated at international level in architecture and that were controversial at its time: as the question towards the architecture of the social thing or about how it should be the form of the housing.With a different look to the positions and statements of the modern movement led by Le Corbusier and manifested in the Charter of Athens, these questions, -new and critical- were developed through Team X. In the context of Bogota there are different projects of residential groups that explore these questions around the public and that debate different ways of answering the same concern: How to gather? In this order of ideas, the present research develops a study around some emblematic projects, both for the history of the modern architecture in Colombia and for the history of the housing in Bogotá, and more specific, the construction of the idea of residential group. These different projects share the same essence: the construction of the public as an agent in the form of the architectural project, what was defined as core by Bakema.
