# BIODIVERSITY COLOMBIA COVERNICOLA: KNOWLEDGE, USE AND CONSERVATION

Recognizing the importance of ecological, agroecological and ecosystem services provided by underground ecosystems, it is necessary to propose a national strategy for their use and conservation, defining the baseline for the establishment of a System of Protected Areas of Carstic Parks in Colombia, based on conservation and tourism as an alternative to local economic development
