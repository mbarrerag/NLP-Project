# LiFi technology in practice: Design and implementation of performance tests on LiFi devices

The wireless communication technology by visible light, also known as Li-Fi, emerges as one of the most promising communication technologies due to its ability to transmit data in an unconventional manner. Although there are studies suggesting that Li-Fi can be a valuable complement to existing wireless technologies, these research does not fully address the constant connectivity and cybersecurity necessary for the use of real-time communication protocols in industrial automation systems. In this work, Li-Fi is assessed for the performance of wireless connection of commercial Li-Fi devices. For this, a structure was designed and implemented to assess the constant connectivity, transmission speed and reception using network protocols such as ICMP, as well as compatibility with industrial protocols such as PTP, GOOSE, SV and MMS. Li-Fi was found to have good compatibility with these protocols, but presents challenges in terms of availability and performance, including loss of long transfer packages and times in some particular cases. In short, although Li-Fi may be a valuable complement to existing wireless technologies, there are still important challenges to be resolved before its widespread adoption in industrial automation systems
