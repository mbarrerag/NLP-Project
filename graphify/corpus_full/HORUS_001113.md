# ENHANCING WIND POWER PROSPECTIVE ANALYSIS WITH PROBABILISTIC PREDICTIONS OF WIND DIRECTION AND SPEED

The urgent need to decarbonize the energy system to combat climate change has driven a focus on renewable energy, with wind power playing a crucial role in the supply portfolios of nations like Canada, Denmark, Sweden, the US, and India due to its low costs and wide availability. However, the inherent variability and stochastic nature of wind pose challenges to its reliability. This research aims to address these challenges by emphasizing the importance of predicting both wind speed and direction as critical inputs of wind power potential. A spatio-temporal probabilistic model for wind data is developed, incorporating circular features of wind direction and its relationships with wind speed and power dynamics. The model's validity is assessed through established tools and comparisons with existing benchmarks, showcasing the added value of incorporating wind direction predictions in a probabilistic fashion
