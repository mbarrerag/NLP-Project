# ZOOMETRIC AND ETNOLOGIC CHARACTERIZATION OF THE COLOMBIAN CRILO IN THE SUCRE DEPARTMENT

The Colombian asno criollo (ACC) may be in danger of extinction. The alarming decrease in its inventory may be reducing its genetic diversity. Thus, the morphological characterization is a first step in future plans for conservation of the genetic resource. Therefore, the objective of this research was to characterize zoometrically and ethnologically the ACC of the five natural subregions of the department of Sucre - Colombia. In 20 males of each of the natural subregions of the department of Sucre (Golfo de Morrosquillo: GM, Montes de María: MM, Mojana: MO, San Jorge: SJ and Sabanas: SA) with average age of 4.7±1.0 years and estimated average weight of 126,0±19,4kg, 28 morphometric measures were recorded. For the analysis of data, the descriptive index and five productive indices were also considered. The majority of the asymmetric and morphological homogeneity can be evaluated based on the coefficient of variation and the relative correlations, respectively
