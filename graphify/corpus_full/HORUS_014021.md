# Study of the performance and degradation of commercial low-power PM fuel cells.

The present proposal seeks to strengthen the work being done from the Seed in the field of fuel cells type PEM. In particular in terms of the evaluation of the performance of low-power commercial cells, using the test bench currently available in the Pilot Plant of the Chemical Engineering Laboratory. This is how it is possible to improve the expertise of the group and train students in the domain of this technology that is expected to be popularized in the short or medium term
