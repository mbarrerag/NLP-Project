# EXPECTATIONS AND REALITIES OF THE LIVING FOR THE MEDIUM CLASS: THE CASE OF THE BARRIO THE LANDS OF BUCARAMANGA, 1961-1970

In this article, of an exploratory nature, the relationship between the national political groups aimed at guaranteeing access to housing for middle classes, urban growth and economic change was investigated, with local conditions that influenced the participation of private companies and inhabitants, in the mid-20th century in Bucaramanga (Colom-bia). This experience was framed by a Latin American trend, since the intense urban growth in the region and the framework of political polarization, resulting from the Cold War, led to the issue of housing being central to the public policies of the time. The work was based on the review of press information, official reports and cartography of the period. The results of the inquiry carried out confirm the difficulties and contradictions that the case of the Las Terras neighborhood represented as a model of the middle class neighborhood
