# Improving the recovery of gold from refractory ores with high copper content

The process of cyanuration for the recovery of gold is not efficient when there is interference of some minerals that react with the cyanide ion. The difficulty to treat an auriferous mineral with conventional methods where low recoveries are obtained is known as refractariness. Copper, for example, present in different sulphides and oxides, is a highly refractory element due to the form of compounds of Cu - CN, which leads to the use of excessive amounts of cyanide in the process and low recovery of gold. In this research a pretreatment in the presence of sulfuric acid and hydrogen peroxide was proposed to decrease the interference of copper in a subsequent process of cyanuration. An experimental design is proposed in order to validate the behavior of the selected variables.
