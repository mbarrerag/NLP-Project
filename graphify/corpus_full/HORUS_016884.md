# INTEREST PLANTS OF THE ZIORA-AMENA AMAZONIA COLOMBIAN INDIGENOUS COMMUNITY CHAGRA

Chagra is the traditional agroforestry system of the Amazonian indigenous communities, a method suitable to the characteristics of the Amazonian environment in which the availability of biomass depends on the constant replacement of nutrients, derived from the decay of dead material (tumba and burning); it is an area of transient polycultures (2 or 3 first years) along with the ¿rastrojos (which remain for a few years for the production of fruit trees), finally the natural vegetation is regenerated
