# Relationship between BLV and health problems in an area of specialized ANTIOCH LESHERY

The bovine leukosis virus (BLV) is a retrovirus whose white cell is the B lymphocytes, which has a negative impact on the immune system of infected animals, as it makes them more susceptible to other diseases of infectious origin. The aim of this study was to carry out a health study of infectious diseases in a specialized dairy herd of the department of Antioquia, and to analyze its possible association with BLV infection. For this, 87 blood samples were taken from holstein Friesian cows, to which were carried out an indirect ELISA for the Gp51 protein of the virus. In addition, the presence/absence data for three diseases (anaplasmosis, mastitis and metritis) and the fever sign during the period from 2006 to 2014. The data were analyzed through conting tables 2x2 and Odds Ratio (OR).
