# CHARACTERIZATION OF MANUAL FILTERS FOR RIEGUM: EFFICIENCY OF REPLYING PARTICULATES IN SUSPENSION AND EFFECTIVE FILMING MALE

Filtration is fundamental in pressure irrigation systems in order to eliminate various pollutants present in water, which can cause obstructions in emitters affecting the uniformity of irrigation damaging the growth and development of the plant. In order to determine the efficiency of removal of suspended solids in ring and mesh filters, tests were carried out in a closed hydraulic system environment under a constant pressure of 400 kPa at the filter entry. 4 test tests (different particle ranges) were performed using speeds at the filter input of 0.4; 0.6 and 0.9 m s-1 corresponding to flows of 20, 30 and 40 m3h-1, respectively, according to the effective area of the filters. A particle removal efficiency above 85% was accepted (ISO/TC 23/SC 18/WG 10 Filters). It was determined that the recommended speed to obtain optimum particle removal efficiency in both filter elements was up to 0.6 m s-1. However, it was determined that the 120 mesh ring filter has a higher range of operation both in the filter input speed and particle size
