# QUANTITATIVE METHODS FOR THE ASSESSMENT OF PUBLIC SPACE: ASSISTING THE COMPLIANCE OF SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES

The evaluation of the quality of public space through quantitative methods contributes to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals, because it uses concrete and measurable goals; the use of quantitative data facilitates the realization of a technical diagnosis of the condition of its elements. The variables were grouped in dimensions; accessibility and comfort were those that were most related to the Sustainable Development Goals. The methods consulted are expensive and complex, need an analysis of the activities and behaviors of users. Without practical methods it is difficult to visualize the needs of infrastructure and to meet the Sustainable Development Goals in terms of good quality public space
