# IMPORTANCE OF THE VARIABILITY OF GEOTHENIC PARAMETERS IN TESTS OF SUPERFFICIAL SCIMENTATION USED IN A ELASTOPLASTIC MODEL

The analysis of the variability of geotechnical parameters was performed and how they influence load capacity in load tests for surface foundations. This used finite element models (FEM) of a continuous shoe with an elastic constitutive model that takes into account soil compression. Random parameters were introduced in two ways in the FEM models, the first form was generated by random numbers of the constitutive model parameters (homogenous soils) and the second form was generated by random fields of parameters (heterogenous soil). Another analysis was performed by dividing the parameters into two types, cutting and compression because these parameters obtained from different tests. The previous analyses were performed using Monte-Carlo simulations. The results show the influence of geotechnical parameters on the load capacity of a surface foundation and which tests should be performed with more care because they have a higher load capacity weight of a surface foundation
