# BAUCH HEALTH CARE FOR PERSONS WITH SENSORIA DISABILITY

Persons belonging to ethnic or racial minorities, economically disadvantaged, with sensory disabilities, at different ages than the average, with different sexual orientation or gender, are violated in their rights to oral health. This paper aims to provide tools to future dentists and human talent in oral health to promote prevention in oral health of persons with sensory disabilities from birth to adulthood, to avoid invasive dental treatment and risk of preventable oral diseases
