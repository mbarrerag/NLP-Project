# Failure analysis, ground rotor in self-excited synchronous generators, using diagnostic techniques and relation to physical parameters of winding

It analyses the phenomenon of ground escape in rotor winding for synchronous generators, in order to characterize the condition of failure from its physical parameters, mainly resistance of isolation from the ground winding. It starts from a physical understanding of the fault, to select the descriptive mathematical model of the fault, recognizing in its initial approaches the need to describe circuits of ground rotor, that is, considering schemes of protection from ground leakage, until including the own feedback from the tension regulator system, later implementing diagnostic techniques, such as Fourier analysis and Hilbert-Huang transform, among others, taking as a basis the models of generator and its inclusion of the failed component, the results of the analysis are contrasted with simulation results on the condition with and without leakage to ground, finally a diagnostic methodology is presented that integrates from the techniques and models described in this research. .
