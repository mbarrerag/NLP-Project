# Path-loss measurement campaign for millimetre waves in electrical substations.

The main objective of the project is to analyze and characterize the loss of propagation (pathloss) of signals in the millimetric wave regimen in the specific environment of electrical substations. Signals in millimetre waves are fundamental for future communication networks, highlighting 5G in frequencies of tens of GHz, due to their high data capacity and low latency. However, given their nature, their propagation may be significantly affected phenomena such as: loss by attenuation, reflections by obstacles and materials and electomagnetic interference present in electrical substations
