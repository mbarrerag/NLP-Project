# COSTING GEOMOPHOLOGY IN THE ESTIMATION OF THE THREAT OF INUNDATION BY SAME TSUNAMI IN TUMACO

ABSTRACT The subduction of the Nazca plate below the South American plate, in the Colombian Pacific Coast generates a seismic activity tectonic, with the occurrence of earthquakes of high magnitude and intensity and low depth. Some of these events such as the 1979 one have generated tsunamis affecting populated areas such as Tumaco, Nariño. In this research new variables of analysis such as geomorphology are incorporated in a model of estimation of the threat of flooding by tsunami to determine if the variations in the coastal geoforms amplify or decrease the effects of flooding on the continental platform. Methodologically, the cartographic methods by remote sensors, geographic information systems, tidal analysis, analysis of seismic probabilities and numerical models of tsunami simulation were considered.
