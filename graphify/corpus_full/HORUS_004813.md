# Basic Radiology. 3rd edition.

The 3rd edition of the book of basic radiology is intended to serve as a guide text in the field the diagnostic images of students of the careers of the field of health and general physicians although it could also be used as a reference time by specialists. In relation to the previous revisions its content has been increased and deepened taking into account the advances and evolution of medical knowledge and radiology. The concepts schemas pictures have been included the different chapters.In the organization of the book the chapters of physical principles of diagnostic images, images of the chest, images of the abdomen, neuroimages, images of osteoarticular and musculoskeletal system, pediatric images, images of the woman and interventional radiology are maintained.In addition, one chapter is included images of the neck and a table of indicators of the studies of diagnostic images in specific clinical contexts.The text book basic radiology born and has been developed in the academic field of the faculty of medicine of the National University of Colombia and its University hospitals
