# Methodology to identify and characterize areas sown in potatoes, integrating optical and radar images in Cundinamarca.

In the potato production system, which is important for four departments of Colombia and more than 100,000 producing families, monitoring of the sown areas is vital, since this depends on the market. Recent advances in satellite imaging analysis allow monitoring, both the planted area, as well as the stress and health of the plant, soil moisture, among others. Cloud presence is the main limiting of the optical sensors and is characteristic of the study area. Fusion with radar images complements the information that hides the clouds. This project seeks to develop a methodology for the identification and characterization of areas sown in potato in Cundinamarca, integrating optical images (Sentinel-2) and radar (Sentinel-1). They are obtained as reference data sets of georeferential papax, of which will have date of sowing and phenological status. The images will be processed and will be applied to the random forest (RF) and the accuracy of the support vector machines (SVM), which have been widely used for the real crop classification. It will be used 60% of reference data for training and 40% for validation. It will be used to determine the accuracy of the total of the indicators
