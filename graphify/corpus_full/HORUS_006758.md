# Transitating the waters, adapting the land: the irrigation district of the Coello River, Tolima, Colombia, 1916-2000

The district of irrigation of the Coello river, one of the most extensive in Colombia, was built in 1953, however, its first mention dates back to 1916. The thesis reconstructs the history of this civil work in the plains of the department of the Tolima. Based on an attentive review of the Colombian and Latin American environmental and agrarian historiography, the research studied primary sources of the state order (Memoirs of the Ministries of Agriculture and Trade, Industries and National Economy), local (reports and documents of the public agricultural promotion entities involved with the irrigation district), legislative (laws and national and departmental decrees), cartography of the period, technical reports, among others.
