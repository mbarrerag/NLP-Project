# CONFORMATE AND COCTION OF TRADITIONAL CERAMIC OF ARCHILLA, USING TO MODE OF PRIMARY MATERIAL LODES OF GALVANIC PROCESS AS A MID OF MITIGATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL POLLUTION

The sludges of galvanic processes due to their high content of heavy metals classified as hazardous waste have become an environmental passive worldwide. Currently there is no type of profitable industrial exploitation, so they accumulate in safety cells where their leachate are source of contamination of soils and water effluents. The ceramic industry and mainly the production of materials based on clay offers a great potential to solve this problem. The cooked clay can be converted into a barrier that encapsulates the heavy metals Cr, Cu and Ni contained in the galvanic sludges. In the present research work, the effect on the mechanical, technical and environmental characteristics is studied; that the galvanic mud added in different concentrations (2%, 5% and 8% in weight) it brings to the clay that traditionally is used for the production of ceramic tablets, with a time of stay at the maximum temperature of 4 minutes. The ceramic pieces are obtained by means of conformation: dry pressing and extrusion. Two cycles of cooking at a maximum temperature of 1150°C are studied: Short of 34 minutes in single-stage roller oven, with a maximum temperature of 4 minutes
