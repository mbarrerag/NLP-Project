# Political violence in the post-conflict situation in Guatemala 1997-2007

After more than thirty years of armed conflict, Guatemala achieved the signing of a peace agreement as a means of closing political violence and the beginning of the democratic transition in this country. However, the persistence of this type of violence in the post-conflict period leads to analysis and differentiation between the political violence that is connected with the armed conflict and political violence under other more structural motivations.
