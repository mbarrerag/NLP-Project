# Revealing the urban enclosure, corporate image and formal relationships in the platform, block and tower typology through six emblematic buildings of German Samper, in Bogotá, Medellín and Cali

This final work of mastery focuses on exploring the development that the architect Germán Samper carried out in the buildings that are classified typologically as platform and tower with corporate use. When analyzing in depth these projects, a variety of architectural responses are discovered that challenge the canonical image normally associated with this typology. . The study begins with the separate understanding of the fundamental parts of the formal typological structure; the platform in relation to the city, the block and the tower with the corporate image and the formal categories between them. Through this analytical approach, it seeks to understand the individual role played by each component. From that understanding, the variations proposed by Samper in its designs are explored. The main objective is to examine how and why these variations are posed and how they affect the function and shape of the buildings in the cities of Bogotá, Medellín and Cali. By studying the different architectural responses proposed by Samper, the latter is sought to unscráng the principles and intentions behind its design decisions.
