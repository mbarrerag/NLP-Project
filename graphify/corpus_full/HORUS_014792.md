# Application of the navigable virtual sheet tool with annotations, in teaching in pathology of basal cell carcinoma and its variants.

With the advent of techniques for digitization of pathology sheets, new paths have been opened in the pathologist's workflow: Variables that are currently valued qualitatively, now have the possibility of being quantified, it has allowed very easily the interconsultation of cases with the possibility of making annotations about the sheet to facilitate communication at the time when another person visualizes the foil, as well as the possibility of remote work using virtual microscopes with access from anywhere with Internet access. These new tools already make part of the reality of many of the laboratories of pathology of the world, and even of some laboratories in Colombia. All these advantages make of virtual microscopes and histological plates digitized a great tool for teaching and learning the pathology, offering the student not only the static photo of a region of interest as occurs in an atlas, but the possibility of having the most frequent tumor in human beings, and to address the foil as if it is found in a real microscope at any time and from any place with access to the internet, as well as the possibility of leaving annotations to review later with the teacher.
