# Evaluation of corrosion resistance at high temperatures of the TiAlVCuN coating

This research aims to evaluate the resistance to oxidation of TiAlVCuN kinary coatings produced by a reactive co-sputating system TiPIMS and magnetron sources, deposited on stainless steel substrates AISI 316L. The coatings were deposited using a Ti/Al target and a V/C target. The deposition parameters were varied in the V/C white obtaining two coatings with different discharge voltages 535V and 550V. The morphological, structural and chemical properties were analyzed in the coatings produced using the scanning electron microscopy (SEM) techniques, X-ray diffraction (XRD) and X-ray dispersion spectrometry (EDS). After characterizing the coatings were performed the isothermic oxidation tests at 800 °C, supporting this temperature for 10 hours. The cyclic oxidation test was also performed at a temperature of 600 °C during one hundred cycles, each cycle conformed in a warm hour and a similar cooling hour.
