# Optimization and validation of potassium hydrogen phthalate coulometric titration: A potentially primary method.

The implementation of primary methods of measurement in the National Institute of Metrology of Colombia (INM) will allow the certification of reference materials (MRCs) which are required in the industrial and scientific sector of the country.One of the most important MRCs is the potassium biphthalate (KHP).The coulombimetry is a method highly known to be potentially primary, since it allows the quantification of the quantity of substance in magnitudes of the international system of measures (SI).In this work, the coulombimetric titration of KHP at metrological level was implemented.The implementation included the adjustment of two models to experimental data by nonlinear regression: the exponential function W (eWf) that has been used several years ago, and our new proposal the theoretical curve of titration (TC) obtained.With the CT2 effect on the results of the titration, the CO2 was impossible to do with the TC
