# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION BY GC/MS OF HETEROSPERMA PINNATUM AND HYPERICUM SP. ESSENTIAL OILS RECOLECTED IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The tentative identification of the components present in the ESAs was based on the coincidence of their mass spectra (EI, 70 eV, coincidence > 90%), using spectral databases (ADAMS, NIST, Wiley), and linear retention rates, obtained in DB-WAX and DB-5MS columns and reported in scientific literature. The majority components were lemon (68.8%), felandren (4.4%) and sabinene (3.9%), for the AE of Heterosperma pinnatum; a-pinene (28.1%), triciclene (16.4%), lemon (10.4%), ß-pinene (9.5%) and trans-ß-caryofilene (6.2%) for the AE of Hypericum sp. The extraction yields were 0.1% and 0.3% (p) for Heterosperma pinnatum and Hypericum sp, respectively.
