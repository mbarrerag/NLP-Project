# Colombia for the World or the World for Colombia: A Comparative Study of the Traditional Model and the Fintech Model for the Production and Financing of the Coffee Industry.

This research project aims to explore and analyze in depth two financing and production models in the Colombian coffee industry: the traditional model represented by the National Coffee Growers Federation and the Fintech model represented by EthnicHub. Colombia’s coffee industry, although successful in many aspects, faces important challenges, such as the need to innovate its production and financing processes to remain competitive in the global market. Small producers, in particular, often have difficulties accessing finance and improving their agricultural practices. This project consists of a thorough review of the literature related to traditional models and Fintech in the coffee industry, as well as in previous research on the current situation of coffee growers in Colombia. At a second moment, it involves a field study in the north of the Valley and the coffee industry axis, where a direct observation of the production conditions, testimonies of producers and experience with both models, complemented by the perspectives of relevant actors in the field. Finally, the results of the theoretical study are compared, with the observations and testimonies collected in the field work, the results of each model and the possible advantages of coffee are identified, and the advantages of the relevant actors in the field.
