# ASSOCIATED TO THE

The ultramáfico zonado complex of Bolivar (CUZB), is located in the eastern flank of the Colombian western mountain range, limited by the faults of Roldanillo and Cauca Amalguer, is formed by a series of ultramaphic rocks such as dunitites, pyroxenites and bane and isotropic gabronites. The discussion about its environment and structure has been widely discussed, however, the most accepted geotectonic model corresponds to ofiolites in areas of suprasubduction, the latter it causes the formation of a pegmatitic phase, diques and metasomatism of the suprayacent block composed of rich dungites in Mg allowing the development of an economic mineralization of magnesita cryptocrystalline type stockwork, characterized as a deposit type Kraubatch (Pohl, 1991). This research also has as objective the geochemical and micro-chemical characteristics of the pegmatites lodged within the rocks (CUZB), with the aim of identifying the thermodynamic conditions, and composition of the liquids of the mineralization. authors such as Pohl (1991), Johannes (1969), Greenwood (1967), who also propose a equilibrium temperature for talco+ magnesite+ silica paragenesis <500°C. Although paragenesis can be used as temperature and CO2 indicators alongside the equilibrium curves of experimental serpentinite dissolution models, it is necessary to specify the thermodynamic and compositional conditions of mineralizing fluids from the analysis of fluid inclusions in quartz, in which two FIAS (fluid inclusion assembly ) were identified that are found in H2O-KCL-CaCl2 and H2O-KCL systems with low salinities between 14.7%-17.2%, and homogenization temperatures of 146°C and 355°C respectively, suggesting at least two supergen and hypogene hydrothermal events
