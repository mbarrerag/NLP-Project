# SEX DIFFERENCES IN LIFE CONDITIONS IN THE POPULATION OF CERCADO GRANDE DE LOS SANTUARIOS (TUNJA, COLOMBIA. 2350-450 AP): PHYSICAL ACTIVITY AND PHYSIOLOGICAL STRESS

Human groups living in the plateau of Cundinamarca and Boyacá in the central part of the Colombian territory at the arrival of the Europeans, have been proposed as matrilineal societies regarding ethnic and political affiliation, but they have also been characterized as patrilocal. This study aims to explore if this could have had an impact on differential life conditions between men and women in the Cercado Grande de Los Santuarios-CGS. This site has been considered as paramount to the population dynamics, because it was a center for ritual, political and interchange activities. This project studies indicators of physiological stress (dental hypoplasia, porotic hyperostosis, cribra orbitalia and periostosis) and physical activity (entheseal changes and degenerative joint disease) in order to evaluate differential life conditions between men (n=66) and women (n=49) in the series from CGS. Frequencies of physiological stress indicators are higher for the male group while those of physical activity are lower. Discussion will turn around the role of geographic mobility of women as a new explanatory factor of physical stress and, especially, as a protective factor to infection. This possibility has not been considered before in the area
