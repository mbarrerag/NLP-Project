# Perception of the learning of anatomy of neurocranian with virtual reality, in undergraduate students of first semester, of the faculty of health sciences of the Colombian School of Rehabilitation University Institution.

Human anatomy teaching is a fundamental basis for students of health sciences, and one of the most complex anatomical regions to teach and appropriate is the skull. It was proposed as a goal for this study: to determine the learning perception in the teaching of neurocranean anatomy with virtual reality in undergraduate students of the first semester of the Faculty of Health Sciences of the Colombian School of Rehabilitation. A descriptive study was conducted, where a sample of 9 students was randomly selected, with whom four face-to-face meetings were held where they were given classes of neurocranean anatomy using virtual reality as teaching strategy, after this the perception of the use of this technology was measured through the questionnaire “mode of modified technological acceptance” (TAM) proposed by Fred Davis. it was identified that the total of the surveyed population, refers to be extremely in agreement, in that the use of virtual reality improves their learning and performance in the subject of morphophysiology. Likewise, most respondents indicated that the use of virtual reality during the classes facilitated the academic learning, referred to as the academic innovation
