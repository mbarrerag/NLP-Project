# REFERENCE TO REMOTE CONTROL: DESIGN OF TIME REDUCTION STRATEGIES IN GLOBOUS CIRCULATIONS

Engineering design consists in applying scientific and engineering knowledge to solve technical problems and then optimizing these solutions with requirements and restrictions that can be given by the materials employed, technology, economy, law, environment and human considerations [1]. Through the playful activity presented, the aim is to test the ingenuity of engineering students, in order to overcome a circuit with remote control carts, where with pins located in these, they must break the largest number of balloons that will be located in specific positions on the ground, in the shortest possible time. Thus, it is intended to encourage the ability to ingeniate solutions to problems with restrictions given in order to win the competition. Such activity would be carried out at the 10th National Meeting of the Iddeal Network, which will take place on 26, 27 and 28 April 2023 at the University of Antioquia
