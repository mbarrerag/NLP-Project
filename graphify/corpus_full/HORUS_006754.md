# Perceptions of teachers in the teaching-learning process of implicit and explicit grammar in future ELF teachers with a view to using the neurolinguistic approach (ENL)

This research seeks to know and reflect on the perceptions of the teachers of the plant and occasional program of philology and French languages of the National University of Colombia in front of the teaching-learning process of implicit and explicit grammar with a view to the use of the neurolinguistic approach in classes of Oral Communication and Communication. This research is carried out from a qualitative, ethnographic design and with an explanatory scope, in order to make an approximation to the opinions, beliefs and experiences experienced by the teachers in the processes of teaching-learning of grammar, in addition to exploring the notions that they possess about the neurolinguistic approach. The instrument that was applied for the collection of information was the semi-structured interview, allowing some freedom in the discourse of the teachers who participate in the research.
