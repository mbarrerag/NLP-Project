# BUILLING + ART: FARNSWORTH HOUSE ANatomy

Enter the intricate layers of universal architecture with this analytical volume dedicated to Edith Farnsworth’s House. This text systematically deciphers how the authentic architectural meaning emerges at the intersection of two fundamental areas of human effort: the practical mechanics of construction and the complex nuances of artistic thought. Approach the methodologies and concepts developed by the German architect Mies Van der Rohe, amidst the dynamic changes of the mid-20th century. Readers will navigate through a rigorous examination that outlines the architectural progression, from the initial conceptual sketches to the finished structure. Throughout this journey, the volume skillfully interprets the deep architectural philosophies and translates them into concrete and aesthetically oriented construction methodologies. Dive into this symbiotic relationship between functional pragmatism and aesthetic considerations, an essential reference for both architectural connoisseurs and academic researchers
