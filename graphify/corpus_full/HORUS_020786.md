# Analysis of the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture in the Colombian Framework

This paper analyzed the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture (TIRFAA) in the Colombian context. Part one contains the supporting documents for its implementation, the obstacles to ratification and general recommendations that support the process of approval, ratification and implementation. For this, the Method of Content Analysis in Atlas IT was used, in whose exercise the codification of 123 units of analysis was carried out, obtaining 1,367 units of meaning (US), which were grouped into 144 codes. The codes with greater units of meaning were: recommendations of participation, networks and coordination (71), reasons for implementing the treaty (68) and conservation recommendations (66). Among the reasons for implementing the treaty, it is found that the country could access foreign plant genetic material that supports the enrichment of agroecosystems, receive funding for conservation and participation projects, clarify and implement the right of farmers and improve the institutional, research and technological capacity for the protection and sustainable use of plant genetic resources for food and agriculture. Among the obstacles that have prevented ratification, is the lack of political will, the incidences of external policies and the institutional recommendations. to 147 coding units. The codes with most measuring units were: Recommendations for participation, networks, coordination and public-private links (71), reasons for implementing the treaty (68) and recommendations for conservation (66).
