# Implementation of a center of living experiences for training in agro-environmental processes, use and management of solid waste led by the corporation Mujeres y familias el Vergel in San Antonio de Prado.

The Women and Families Corporation El Vergel has been developing collective activities around agro-food and environmental processes that could be consolidated and optimized through an intervention from a social innovation approach. To achieve this, it is required that the Corporation strengthen administrative capacities and tools; as well as refine techniques and methodologies for the transformation of solid waste and develop knowledge management processes that allow them to perform the traceability of productive processes, implement mechanisms of systematization of data and control of information of the activities developed by the Corporation (development of community gardens, agro-environmental training, waste management, handicraft products, etc.). This project seeks to strengthen the associative, organizational and technical capacities through the implementation of a center of living experiences for the agro-environmental training and for the sustainable management of solid waste generated by the members of the Corporation in the gardens and in their homes. By developing administrative capacities, improving knowledge and tools of waste transformation, the Corporation is also intended to raise awareness of its community and transfer knowledge that allows them, in addition to managing waste efficiently, strengthen the agro-ecological process that they are developing and increase the income for its operation.
