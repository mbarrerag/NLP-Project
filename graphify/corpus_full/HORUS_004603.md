# AGROINDUSTRIA OF MILACE MATERIALS: METHODS AND CHARACTERIZATION PROTOCOLS

This scientific manuscript compiles methods derived from research activities of different professors and researchers of the University of Sucre who pursue in common, the promotion of the transformation of agricultural raw materials, the exploitation of by-products and the generation of added value that enhance the agro-industry of tuberculal crops in the Department of Sucre
