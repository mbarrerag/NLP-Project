# Innovation and Resilience: the SME in the face of the crisis provoked by Covid-19. An Narrative Review of Literature

Summary In order to curb the pandemic caused by Covid-19, both individuals and companies were forced to pause most daily activities, creating a crisis in trade and the global economy. SMEs were particularly affected, partly because of their economic vulnerability and their survival economy. However, for some, small size and flexible structure were drivers of change and adaptation. The purpose of this article is to provide a narrative about adaptation in SMEs in the face of the Covid-19 pandemic, exploring processes and strategies used to ensure their survival. From a methodological point of view, a narrative review of the literature of scientific production was carried out at WOS and Scopus bases, and the documentary basis for analysis was constructed as a semi-systematic review of literature. The main findings identified two adaptation processes in SMEs to address the pandemic: innovation and resilience, mediated by strategies such as the use of technologies and external collaborations, in understanding the new needs of clients. This review aims to increase the theoretical framework for future research and that the proposed strategies serve as possible guides for companies in future scenarios
