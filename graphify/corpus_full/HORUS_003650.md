# Effect of the type of packaging and organic acids on the quality of the pumpkin (Cucurbita moschata Duch. Ex Poir) minimally processed during storage

The marketing of the Zapallo (Cucurbita moschata Duch. Ex Poir.) in the retail markets is limited due to its large size. Inadequate handling practices and high moisture content accelerate its deterioration. The minimum processing is an alternative that allows to obtain a food harmless with characteristics similar to the fresh product. The aim of this study was to evaluate the influence of three types of packaging and the application of an organic acid solution on the quality of minimally processed zapallo stored at 6.0 ± 2 °C. From the results obtained the stability of the product was estimated using the overall stability index (IEG). The results indicated that the minimally processed zapallo obtained is suitable for consumption at nine days of storage. The application of organic acids decreased the growth of aerobic mesophils, but favored the growth of molds and yeasts. The packaging in the polystyrene expanded tray (PSE) and polychloride film of vinyl (PVC) showed the greatest weight loss (8 %), but the second order of organic mesophils, but it retained the minimum values of lumo and chroma.
