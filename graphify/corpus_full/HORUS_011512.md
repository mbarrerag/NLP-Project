# DECISIONS FOR THE ADEQUATION OF THERAPEUTIC EFFORTS TO THE END OF LIFE IN ONCOLOLOGIC PATIENTS OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA CANCEROLOGY INSTITUTE

the adequacy of therapeutic effort is a clinical decision based on the evidence that aims to avoid medical futility. Different factors have been pointed out that can influence this decision making that relate to the patient, the medical professional that makes decisions, system barriers, culture and economics, among others. The present study aims to identify those factors that influence the specialist physicians working in the institution referring to cancer in Colombia, in order to plan actions that improve the approach of decision making regarding the adequacy of therapeutic effort in patients with cancer. Qualitative design based on 13 in-depth interviews with medical specialists of the National Institute of Cancerology of Colombia. 3 women and 10 men participated, with an average age of 36 years, 7 belonging to the specialty of adult oncology, 2 to pediatric oncohematology, 1 to intensive care of adults and 3 to intensive care pediatric.
