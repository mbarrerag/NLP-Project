# Characterization of the condition of train rails using the frequency response function and deep networks

Due to the critical importance of the rail in the maintenance of the rail system, it is essential to develop a process that allows to evaluate the state of the rail, quantify the severity of its possible defects and changes in structural configurations. This analysis is essential to take appropriate measures, ensuring the reliability and maintenance of the system in its entirety. In this context, this research process focuses on the implementation of a methodology that characterizes the train rails through their dynamic behavior through the modal analysis. This, addresses the frequency analysis through three approaches: theoretical, numerical and experimental. The experimental process of modal analysis is carried out using the technique of hammer testing. To this, a study of the determination of the frequency behavior of the rail with respect to changes in the distance between fixations and the variation of conditions as generation of defects.
