# CYCLE OF MEETINGS SOCIAL INNOVATION IN HEALTHY AND SUSTAINABLE TERRITORIES IN THE PANDEMIC AND POST-PANDEMIC CONTEXT

This document is divided into thirteen chapters.The first corresponds to the introduction and methodology.The second contextualizes the actions of the InovaSocial Program.The third chapter brings the technical and scientific vision closer to the daily experience of families in the countryside and their knowledge.The following five chapters systematize the topics covered by the speakers in dialogue with the results of the surveys applied.The ninth chapter presents the reflections, sharing and learning of the work subgroups.The tenth chapter presents some social innovations shared during the Cycle.The eleventh chapter corresponds to the end of the year message.Finally, the chapter final considerations and future perspectives systematizes the conclusions of this document, highlighting lessons learned and recommendations for continued actions in the pandemic and post-pandemic context.The 2020 End of Year Message Annex is one of the results of the dialogue and a collective construction of the reflections of the Meeting Cycle
