# Alzheimer’s. An approach from the life paths of informal caregivers

The disease of Alzheimer, besides having a high incidence and prevalence rate both worldwide and at national level, has a high degree of complexity in its diagnosis because it is not yet precise on its etiology, resulting in a difficulty to understand the symptoms and signs of the disease. This leads to a late diagnosis, making it even more difficult to care for people who suffer from this disease. Given these characteristics, Alzheimer has started to be a public health problem, but is not yet treated as such from the Colombian health system. This research work seeks to deepen in the context of the experiences of three women who have dedicated part of their adult life to the accompaniment and care of their mothers diagnosed with Alzheimer's. Identify how it is understood and interacted with the disease from the family environment, as well as how its family dynamics affect being informal caregivers; it allows to reveal the difficulties of the Colombian health system to deal with this disease, where care almost falls to the family environment and the absence of programmes of educational and economic type. research work seeks to delve into the experiences of three women who have dedicated part of their adult lives to support and caring for their mothers diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease.
