# COLOMBIAN STUDY OF GARRAPATES AND PATHOGENS TRANSMITTED BY GARRAPATES FROM THE APPROACH OF A HEALTH?

Abstract Ticks are arthropods recognized as the main vector of importance in animal health and the second in human health, due to the transmission of various pathogens that are etiological of zoonotic diseases, many of which are considered to be emerging and reemerging in Colombia. The cycle of these pathogens transmitted by ticks includes the participation of these as vector, the favorable environmental conditions and a vertebrate animal as host. Recently, the approach of zoonotic diseases under the concept of One Health has become important, defined as an integral and unifying approach to human, animal and ecosystem health, recognizing their close bonding and interdependence.
