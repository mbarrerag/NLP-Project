# Obtaining improved populations of Zapallo (Cucurbita moschata).

The zapallo (Cucurbita moschata) is a very important vegetable in the world for the contribution in the food and medicine. In Colombia there are not many varieties improved in this species. Therefore, the objective of this study was to obtain new populations of elite zapallo in terms of the number of fruits per plant (NFP) and the weight of fruit (PF). The experiment was developed in the experimental center of the National University of Colombia Sede Palmira (CEUNP) between the period July 2021 to June 2022. The plant material was obtained from the crossing of the cultivars UNAPAL-Bolo Verde x UNAPAL-Llano Grande in three cycles of recurrent selection by self-fertilization. The selection carried out highlighted the behavior of the populations 12, 32, 71 and 115 that presented a range 4~6 fruits per plant with an average weight 1.32 2.52 Kg/fruto in F3 populations.
