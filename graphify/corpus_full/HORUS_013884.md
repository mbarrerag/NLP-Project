# Promotion of mental and cardiovascular health of young adults and adults based on inner strength: Feasibility study

Young adults are exposed to negative social and individual stressors with repercussions for their physical and psychosocial well-being, which generates increased risk factors for their mental and cardiovascular health. Reducing stress and increasing resilience through interventions based on the promotion of inner strength, could bring benefits in emotional and behavioral regulation. Determine the feasibility and feasibility of an intervention to promote inner strength in the mental and cardiovascular health of young adults. Experimental study type randomized controlled trial phase II
