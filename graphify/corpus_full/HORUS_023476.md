# ANALYSIS OF COMFORT AND INDOOR AIR QUALITY, IN SOCIAL HOUSING BUILT DURING DIFFERENT PERIODS (1918-2018) IN BOGOTÁ (COLOMBIA)

In the framework of the research project "EVALUATION AND SUSTAINABLE PROPOSAL FOR THE IMPROVEMENT OF THE INTEGRAL HABITABILITY OF SOCIAL INTEREST HOUSING IN BOGOTÁ (COLOMBIA) - VIS-BOGOTÁ, I-COOP B20431-CSIC¿, 7 social housing developments built in Bogotá in different periods within the years 1918 and 2018 are analysed. They represent different housing patterns regarding formal and constructive aspects, responding to the evolution of public housing development policies during one century. This work was conducted to assess current habitability conditions of these dwellings in order to suggest measures to improve indoor environmental quality. For this purpose, indoor environmental conditions of each dwelling have been recorded through the monitoring of hygrothermal comfort parameters ¿indoor temperature (oC) and humidity (%)- and indoor air quality (CO2), during more than 6 months, from November 2020 to May 2021. The present paper analyses indoor comfort in these homes to better understand their energy balance and propose the most fitting improvement measures. Part of the work consisted in the characterization of the building systems of the envelope for each selected development. Using this information, IEQ parameters monitored and building characteristics, an energy model will be adjusted and used for propose improvement measures and analyse the predicted behaviour. The project focuses on passive strategies for the buildings retrofit, since they are considered the most effective to achieve the objectives of thermal comfort while reducing energy demand
