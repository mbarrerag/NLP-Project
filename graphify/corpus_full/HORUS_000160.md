# Electrochemical characterization of Ni-Mn2O3 and Ni-Mn2O3-graphene coatings

This investigation involved the electrochemical characterization of nickel‐manganese oxide and nickel‐manganese oxide-graphene coatings deposited on common glass. Ni was deposited via DC sputtering, Mn 2 O 3 coatings were deposited through RF magnetron sputtering, and graphene was synthesized via the electrochemical exfoliation technique. SEM of high resolution evidenced that the coatings are formed by grains of micro- and nanometer dimensions. XRD analysis established the presence of Ni and Mn 2 O 3 . The Raman results showed Ni, Mn 2 O 3 peaks, and D and G graphene bands located at 1354 and 1597 cm −1 , respectively. Electrical behavior indicated that the Ni-Mn 2 O 3 film is isolated, while the three-layer film has ohmic behavior. EIS analysis determined that the graphene layer decreased the real impedance value of the Ni-Mn 2 O 3 coating, and Bode analysis indicated that both the bilayer and three-layer coatings exhibited capacitive behavior.
