# Alternative to single-use plastic

Alternative to the plastic of a single use MITIGA is a consulting firm of design and product development, created for the academic exercise. During the process of design and product development of Mitiga, it is proposed to think of the packaging as an integral element to the final product. This means that the consultancy conceives the packaging at the beginning of the design chain and not at the end as it is done in the usual design processes. (ICONTEC, 2022).
