# Comprehensive repair and conditionality regime: towards transformative integration

This research develops the relationship between the conditionality regime and comprehensive reparation as structural elements in the current transitional justice model created under the Final Peace Agreement concluded by the Colombian government with the FARC-EP armed group in 2016. To clarify this relationship, its characteristics and its relevance at present, the role of transitional justice in the situations of armed conflict and the strategies it incorporates for overcoming it is explained; later, the content and scope of the right to comprehensive reparation is specified as one of the main demands of the victims of the armed conflict.
