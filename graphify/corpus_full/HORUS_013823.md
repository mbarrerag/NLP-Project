# Place and meaning of spirituality in community-based agroecology in Colombia

At the present time, life on the planet faces an unprecedented crisis, and there is a dispute for the food system between conventional agriculture and community-based agroecology. The crisis on the planet is closely related to the production of greenhouse gases derived from the conventional agri-food system. This system also increases social differences and puts economic benefit before life.In this scenario agroecology appears as an alternative solution. However, although there has been mention of spirituality in agroecology, there is no clear understanding about the place and meaning of this human dimension, so it is necessary to reveal the spiritual substratum of agroecological thought of community basis.The result that is expected to be achieved is to determine the place and sense of spirituality in community-based agroecology.The impact that the results will have in the scientific field consists in the contribution to the epistemological status of agroecology through the clarification of the place and sense of spirituality in community-based agroecology.The scientific product will impact the scientific field in so much visible the contribution of the communities that make agroecology, the grassroots and grassroots-based agroecology
