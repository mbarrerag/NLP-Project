# Resilience of the peasant families of La Maporita village of Arauquita in the guarantee of food and nutritional security

This research was proposed with the purpose of carrying out an analysis of the relationships that may exist between the resilience of the peasant families of the La Maporita village of the municipality of Arauquita- Arauca and their guarantee in food and nutrition security. This research is based on quantitative and qualitative methods, making it a mixed investigation, through which it was possible to gather complete information regarding the dynamics of the area, its inhabitants, spatial location and aspects related to the level of food insecurity of the participants. It was attended by 31 families belonging to the village, who were represented by each of the heads or heads of household or in whom the responsibility for the purchase of the household food predominated and the participation of the same was voluntary. .
