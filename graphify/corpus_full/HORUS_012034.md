# WELL/WALK IN ORGANISATION IS THE HUMANIST ORGANIZATION A UTOPIA?

The well-being, as seen today in a large part of the organizations, is problematic and seems not to have the expected impacts. Based on the contrast between well-being and discomfort in the organization, the book analyzes the relationship of the subject with the work as a source of reflection for the construction of humanist organizations. Such reflection does not neglect the institutional purposes. A starting point of the book is to understand that in contemporary times, the work linked to an organization can be a source of realization for the subject, but also of alienation. And to examine the realization of the subject in the work, as well as his alienation by it, will allow to raise elements for the construction of humanist organizations
