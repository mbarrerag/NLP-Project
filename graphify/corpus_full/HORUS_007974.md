# SANTOS, CREENCES AND REVISED IN THE PUGNAS FOR LEGITIMITY: THE TWO HERES OF MANUEL OLAVA OLIVELA

In this article, will be presented two forms of heresy? in the work of Manuel Zapata Olivella as a novelist and as a cultural critic during the 1960s. First, we will observe how, in his novel In Chimá a saint is born, the heretical actions of the inhabitants of Chimá pose a direct affront to the legitimacy of the ecclesiastical authority that derives, at the same time, in the exercise of repressive violence by the state authorities and, in some way, in the political consciousness by the chimaleros. Secondly, we will observe how the exercise of cultural and literary criticism that Zapata Olivella carried out from the magazine Letras Nacionales and other publications during the 1960s can also be considered as heretical? in the context of the pugnas by cultural legitimacy in the nascent Colombian literary field
