# Detection and isolation of faults in a Thermocouple Network using Data-Based Techniques.

This document explains the process of developing a model for fault detection and isolation (FDI ) in a thermocouple network using data-based techniques. The document begins by describing generalities of plant operation, basic concepts on thermocouples, definition of IDF, its relevance in the plant and the way it is currently developed, which opens the way to the identification of the problem and the approach of the objectives. The project development is divided into 4 stages, starting with the recognition of the available data set, followed by a study of metrics obtained from the data set and its relationship with fault or normal states in the thermocouples, establishing a methodology for the training of data-based models and the results obtained from their application. The document concludes with the determination of parameters for the construction a data-based model showing a accuracy of more than 76%, according to applied validation tests, among other conclusions obtained from the development of this project
