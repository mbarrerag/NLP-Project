# Special relations of subjection in the Colombian National Police

The police system in Colombia is structured around social and legal components, police perform a constitutional role in representing the State, which guarantees, preserves and promotes a safe and peaceful environment throughout the territory, have a special relationship of subjection with society through the State, which although legitimate and with the vocation to guarantee the proper use of force and respect for human rights, does not prevent the existence of a gap plagued by mistrust, corruption, violations of human rights and exclusion of the community that lead to doubt about the effectiveness of that special relationship that causes the lack of awareness that as authority the Police has in Colombia.
