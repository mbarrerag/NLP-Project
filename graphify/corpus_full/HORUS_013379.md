# Advanced fenton oxidation process integrated with coagulation-flocculation or electrocoagulation for the treatment of industrial textile waste water

Colombia is considered the sixth country with the best water supply (2145 m3/s) and the tenth third in per capita water availability (42740 m3) in the world. In addition, it has projected environmental standards for a responsible use of water resource, penalizes the dilution of the waste water and limits concentrations of different substances in the dumping. Colombian textile industry represents 1.2% of national GDP and contributes to 9% of exports. However, this involves high environmental impacts due to the diversity of raw and reactive materials involved in its processes and the high consumption of water that in turn generates a large volume of waste water. These effluents are characterized by the non-biodegradable, toxic, persistent and recalcitrant matter, high DQO and high color content.
