# MANAGEMENT OF FINANCIAL, NON-FINANCIAL AND EMERGENT RISKS

In a world that is constantly changing and at great speed, risk management is indispensable to ensure the stability of companies, organizations and countries. Therefore, it is essential to strengthen mechanisms to identify, monitor, manage and prevent both traditional and emerging risks, since as those that derive from economic, environmental and social changes can have a serious long-term impact on financial institutions, which could be incremental in the future given the current situation.
