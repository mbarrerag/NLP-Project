# ANALYZING FREIGHT TRIP DYNAMICS LINKED TO GROCERY SECTOR ACTIVITY IN MEDELLIN, COLOMBIA: A QUANTITATIVE APPROACH

This research focuses on identifying variables that significantly impact the freight trip attraction of supermarkets and nano-stores in the developing economy of Medellin, Colombia. Supermarkets and nano-stores are commercial establishments that sell groceries. However, in developing countries, the grocery retail market is dominated by nano-stores rather than supermarkets, as in developed countries. Nano-stores are family-owned stores typically located in neighborhoods. To conduct this analysis, data from the Medellin Metropolitan Area in Colombia obtained from the two freight surveys conducted in 2012 and 2018 were used to estimate models using the ordinary least squares technique and logarithmic transformations to get a better fit. The total area of the establishment and the equivalent number of full-time employees were found to be the most influential variables. Practitioners, such as city planners, can benefit from these models, as they can use freight trip attraction in residential and commercial areas to improve the transportation planning process in large cities of developing countries
