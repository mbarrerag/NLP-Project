# Differential mobility of women in Bogotá. A study from the mobility survey 2019

This article addresses the impact of the socioeconomic stratum on urban mobility of women in Bogotá. Urban mobility has been configured as an important focus of analysis that allows to deepen the identification and understanding of urban problems associated with spatial distribution of equipments, access to the city and the socioeconomic conditions that allow or hinder population displacement. Based on the data of the Mobility Survey 2019 and with a gender approach, bivariate and multivariate analysis is carried out, use of standard deviation ellipse and tests of chi square dependence are applied. The findings reveal differential mobility expressed in destinations, modes and reasons for displacement, which raises socio-spatial segregation and widens the social gap between women .
