# Prospective assessment of the effects of decarbonization on socio-cultural and environmental dynamics of the coal-fired territories in the department of Boyacá

The decarbonisation, a fundamental tool in the fight against climate change, finds a particular challenge in Boyacá. Coal mining, an economic pillar of the region, has generated significant environmental and socio-economic impacts. A fair transition to a low carbon economy is necessary to mitigate these impacts and foster sustainable development. This Final Master's Work prospectively evaluates the effects of decarbonization on Boyacá's carboniferous territories mainly in its socio-cultural and environmental dynamics.In this sense, an analysis of the literature on, coal mining, regional urban planning, decarbonization and fair transition, using an interdisciplinary approach. The four chapters of the research address in the first instance a context of decarbonization in Colombia, highlighting the country's commitments and strategies at the level; secondly, an analysis of the economic and social importance of coal mining in Boyacá is made in the context of a low-costing and socio-economic impacting economy; as a third chapter an analysis of how mining has shaped coal-based territories and how the transition policies can affect them; and for a last-incorporate-based formulation of a low-economic and socio-economic approach. here, mainly focusing on their socio-cultural and environmental dynamics. In this survey, a literature review was conducted on coal mining, regional urban planning, decarbonization, and just transition, using an interdisciplinary approach.
