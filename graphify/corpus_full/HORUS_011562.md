# Predictive factors for perforated appendicitis in a pediatric population treated at a referral hospital in Bogotá D.C., Colombia

Acute appendicitis is the leading cause of abdominal surgical emergencies in pediatrics. Between 30% and 75% of cases progress to perforated appendicitis; however, timely diagnosis before perforation occurs remains a challenge.
