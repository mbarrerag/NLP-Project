# Alcohol abuse: a major invisible pandemic

Among In 2011 and 2021, the number of drug users increased by 23%, from 240 million to 296 million.1 Alcohol consumption is a risk factor for more than 200 health disorders. In America, according to the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), this practice is approximately 40% higher than the world average, which means that this population consumes alcohol in a pattern dangerous to health and therefore, among the various psychoactive substances consumed, this is the main risk factor for the burden of disease. 2
