# Proposal and context of chamber music for trombone in unconventional formats in the formation of the trombone player at undergraduate level. Case study: Capriccio da camera, op.35 for trombone and seven instruments by Bernhard Krol

The main purpose is to identify the qualities of Capriccio da camera, op.35 for trombone and seven instruments of Bernhard Krol as a work of chamber music not conventional in the formation of the thrombonist, for which various techniques of data collection were used, according to the research approach as they are: documentary tracing, musical hearing, contextual, harmonic and musical analysis of the work in mention and its composer. Among other aspects, the research revealed that the predominant qualities in chamber music not conventional in Trombon - taking into account the case study - are multiple, and you are leading the instrumentist to develop not only technical – musical skills such as: basic pulse establishment, descending and ascending chromatisms, rhythmic augmentation, melódic transposition, counterpoint, but also use teamwork due to the overall interpretation. .
