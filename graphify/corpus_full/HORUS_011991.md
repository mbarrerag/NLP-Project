# Morphogenesis and impacts of contemporary geographic patterns of residential segregation in the main Colombian cities: Bogotá, Cali, Medellín

The main objective of this research is to understand how the morphogenetic processes associated with the forms of conformation of the urban space, the production of housing and the production of goods and services have generated geographical patterns of residential segregation in Bogotá, Cali and Medellín.From a conceptual and methodological reflection on the importance of making studies of residential segregation from a historical and comparative perspective, an approach is made to measure residential segregation in the three main urban agglomerations of Colombia, the process of conformation of its geographical patterns and the main impacts that are presented.
