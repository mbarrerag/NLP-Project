# Geographical analysis as a theoretical-methodological bridge in the resolution of legal-environmental problems in Colombian territory.

This research, which is aimed at establishing a dialogue between specific lines of research that are born from Geography and Law, and which analyze a reality with legal guidelines and social implications: the territorial overlay -mostly not compatible- of areas belonging to the National System of Environmentally Protected Areas -SINAP- Sierra de la Macarena because of its large and important component of biodiversity, and the historical initiatives and practices of the community in the municipality of Macarena-Meta that contribute to its cultural identity. Throughout the document, it is put at the service of this reality of Colombian territory and history, the construction of an instrument of legal-spatial analysis, where it seeks to make geography through the use and appropriation of participatory tools linked to new technologies of geographical information that contribute to the State legal construction, where the figure and practices of local communities -especially peasant communities- prevail in synergy with the environmental entities that surround them.
