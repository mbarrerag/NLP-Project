# Andrés Ricardo Otálora Cascante. Under the claws of the lion and the wings of the condor. Health in the Independence of New Granada 1815-1820. Bogotá: Universidad Nacional de Colombia, 2023. 296 pages.

This book is the result of an investigation whose objective has been to analyze the health, bodies and material living conditions in the daily life of the Expeditionary Army of Costafirme and the Liberation Army of New Granada and Venezuela during the period of Independence. Based on a detailed knowledge of the medical theories in force at that time and the consequent medical, social and military treatment of the health-disease binomial, the author examines the sanitary practices, the concrete treatments of diseases and the control of epidemics, in relation to the formulation and execution of military strategies and the daily life of both armed forces
