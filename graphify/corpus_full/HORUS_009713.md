# PRODUCTION OF PUBLIC SPACE IN POPULARY BARRIES OF MANIZALS: APPROVAL, APPROPRIATIONS, APPROPRIATIONS AND PERCEPTIONS

The Comuna Nuevo Horizon de Manizales (Colombia), located on the northern slope, is made up of 7 neighborhoods with different forms of population, some were built by popular housing programs and others, by possession of land, self-constructed from popular knowledge, many of them already recognized from institutionality. In this consolidation of the territory, the public space (EP) is also produced and managed in various ways. To understand the social production of these public spaces, their appropriations, meanings and perceptions were carried out an update of the inventory of the community's EP in a Geographic Information System, being this the initial input for tours, direct observations (participants and non-participants) and semi-structured interviews. Two forms of production of recreation - sports EP were established: 13 planned by institutionality and 3 managed from popular knowledge (some recognized from institutionality). Three specific cases were selected to analyze the daily practices that allow recognition and interaction between neighbors, finding that perceptions and meanings are conditioned by the adaptations given from their community modes of production, where the community assumes different roles in the transformation and conservation of the EP
