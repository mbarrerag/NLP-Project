# Motivation for the approach of goals that transcend academia towards a life project

The expectations of a student in the classroom in each new semester can determine what much benefit they take from an academic space. While sixteen weeks are not a short time to create experiences, it is the mission to change how a student sees or feels a specific set of knowledges —a discipliner field. Even if one has in mind a specific goal that can be compared with the generality that is a university career, to allow each subject in its individuality to seem expendable is a loss of the impulse with which the apprentice starts his academic process, and of the time and effort that is expected to be devoted to him. The present project is mainly interested in generating in the student a perception that all learning is potentially significant in the extent that he allows it, and to open a space that connects the student with the professional field of experience design, from the subject Production in Digital Media —introduction to the design centered on the people within the plan of Graphic Design in the National University of Colombia. create a perception in every student that any application is potentially worthwhile, as much as they allow it to be. It also attempts to establish a space in which the students are opened up to the professional field of user experience design from having Digital Media Production —the introductory course to human centered design on the Graphic Design program at Universidad Nacional de Colombia— while seeking to inspire them to set significant goals for themselves, resulting in the drive to enable them in an academic setting, towards transparency in their life projects
