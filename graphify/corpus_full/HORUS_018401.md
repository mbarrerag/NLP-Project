# TRANSCULTURAL ADOPTION OF THE PRE-SCHOOL CONFUSSION ASSESSMENT METHOD FOR THE INTENSIVE CARE UNIT (PSCAM-ICU) IN ITS SPANISH VERSION

Objective To perform the transcultural adaptation of the PCCAM-ICU instrument for the diagnosis of the preschool delirium from the Spanish language (of Puerto Rico) to the Colombian Spanish speaking. Methodology The transcultural adaptation of the version of the PCCAM-ICU instrument in Spanish language of Puerto Rico to the Spanish speaking of Colombian was carried out in successive phases: preparation, translation, training, reconciliation and analysis session. Preparation For this phase, the doctors Ely Wesley and Heidi Smith (University of Vanderbilt, Nashville, TN, USA) were contacted by the authors of the validation process of the PCCAM-ICU in their original version in English. They, in turn, contacted Milagros Figueroa and Carmen Arroyo (University of Puerto Rico), who performed the translation of the instrument into Spanish language and requested, through electronic mail, the authorization to carry out the cultural adaptation to the Colombian context. Translation An appropriate professional in Puerto Rico, carried out the translation of the original version of the PCCAM-ICU from the Spanish language.
