# Impact of access to credit on growth and formality of micro-businesses in Colombia

This paper analyzes the impact of access to credit on the formalization and growth of micro-businesses in Colombia. Using data from the DANE Microbusiness Survey (EMICRON) for the period 2019-2022, the methodology of Propensity Score Matching and the models of binary choice compares Probit and Logit to examine this relationship was used. The results highlight that access to credit plays a fundamental role in stimulating the growth and formalization of these businesses, in the four dimensions defined in CONPES 3956: entry, input, production and tax. It was deepened in the analysis by differentiating between credits granted by formal and informal entities, revealing that micro-businesses with access to formal credit showed a greater propensity to formalisation and experienced a more pronounced growth compared to those who obtained credit through informal entities
