# Analysis, design and construction of a Kibble scale.

A Kibble scale was designed and constructed using Lego® pieces to form the structure, and non-ferrous magnetic elements for the moving parts. Using a calibrated 51-digit multimeter, a gyroscope and an ultrasound sensor, the mass of test loads of 1 g, 2 g, 5 g, 10 g and 20 g belonging to an IOML E2 class mass game was determined, thus allowing a mass difference of less than 1.5% of the nominal value to be found. The expanded uncertainty obtained was up to four (4) orders of magnitude higher than that of the IOML E2 class, thus identifying the speed as the dominant magnitude of influence on the uncertainty budget
