# Methodology for the formulation of the local solid mass transfer coefficient in the drying process. Tomato drying case.

The drying operation is a unitary operation critical in terms of energy consumption and product quality. The drying operation consists of removing a liquid phase, such as moisture, from a solid or liquid phase, by means of mechanisms of energy transfer and mass. Through the drying, in particular of food, the functional life of the product is prolonged by reducing its moisture content, which causes changes in physical and sensory properties of the product. In general, the description of the drying of solids found in the literature, is limited to particular models of the drying solid. In these models the kinetics of drying from a weight change curve with the time to determine the operating points are calculated. However, the drying operation can be described through models of phenomenological base that use coefficients of heat transfer and mass to represent the process.
