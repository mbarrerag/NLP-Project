# METALS PESED, POLLUTION AND EFFECTS

The pollution by heavy metals in the marine environment is a problem that cannot be analyzed if we take into account the human being, because finally it is the last of all those involved that are being involved by this pollution. Of the 118 elements known to man, 84 are metals, which since the iron age have played an important role in the development of civilization. The problem of metals occurs when they exceed limit concentrations. All metals have a threshold of toxicity, from which they produce adverse effects. Numerous problems have been described due to deficits and overdoses of these. Globally, there have been cases of poisoning that have led to massive deaths of people, such as the case of Minamata disease caused by methylmercury contamination or the disease of the "Itai Itai", by cadmium in the prefecture of Toyama, both in Japan.
