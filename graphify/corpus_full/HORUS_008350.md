# Amazonic Petroglyphs.

Petroglyphs are defined as figures carved on stone and together with cave paintings (or pictograms) make up what is known as rock art. It is considered that the number of engravings is less than that of paintings. Within anthropological literature, the integration of stone into the passing of humanity is the main event. For example, the rubbing of two rocks makes possible the generation of fire; the oldest tools were also made in stone; houses and cities were constructed with this raw material. In a ritual setting, we find the use of rocks together with plants and animals. In all parts of the world and from very remote times, the human being recorded on stone surfaces multiple anthropomorphic figures, figures of animals, parts of plants, figures called generically geometric, composite figures, series of incisions of points, semi-spheric incisions among others. The set of representations are associated with each other and some make compositions that trace anthropological contexts allusive to the past cosmologies of the populations that would have engraved these petroglyphs, the petroglyphs also reflect the concepts, and in addition they constitute the compositions of the compositions
