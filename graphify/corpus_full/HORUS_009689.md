# VIRTUAL PHYSIOLOGY TALK: DIRECT STRATEGY FOR EDUCATION, LEARNING AND ASSESSMENT OF THE ANIMAL TERMOREGULATION CONCEPT ON THE UNIVERSITY LEVEL

The chapter presents a didactic methodology for teaching, learning and evaluating physiology at the university level, in this particular case through the approach to the concept of animal thermoregulation, of vital importance in animal sciences. It is based on conceptual change and generates significant learning, strengthens analysis, argumentation and problem solving
