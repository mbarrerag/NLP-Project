# Impact of municipal regulations on the development of closed enclaves. The case of the Chia-Cajicá conurbation between 2000 and 2020

This research is questioned by the impact of normative instruments on the configuration of the territory of Chía and Cajicá, which is understood to be a response to metropolitan growth in Bogotá. The time frame responds to the implementation of Law 388 of 1997, which led to the regulation of the territorial system in Colombia, which meant that the formulation of the first Territorial Planning Plans for the municipalities under study was carried out in 2000. The analysis of the normative framework allowed the understanding that, the integration of public and private actors, led to a territory made up of closed enclaves, where the private sector takes precedence over the public and the urban over the rural, paradoxically, since they are municipalities that conceptually define themselves as rural. .
