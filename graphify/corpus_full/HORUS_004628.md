# MULTI-PARKABLE ACICLYTIC POLYNOMY

In this paper we study the acyclic polynomial of any simple graph G, which is closely related to the Hosoya index that appears in applications to physics and chemistry. In the different applications, acyclic polynomial has been defined in several ways. In one of them, it can be interpreted as a function of the number of pairings of graph G. In this work we show some of its properties. In particular, we demonstrate that multivariable acyclic polynomial M(G; w) divides the multivariable acyclic polynomial M(T; w), from the T expansion tree, from graph G. We also express the acyclic polynomial of G as a finite sum of determinants of G subgraph matrix obtained by eliminating the vertices and edges of some cycles in G
