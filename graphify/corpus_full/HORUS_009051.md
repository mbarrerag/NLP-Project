# COMPARISON OF THE PREDICTIVE CAPACITY OF THREE MODELS FOR LONGITUDINEL DATA,

In this work, three approaches to predict a continuous response of longitudinal interest rate are compared, via simulation, given specific variables to a hypothetical group of people. These approaches are Bayesian in nature and Statistical Learning (SA). The strengths and limitations of both approaches are discussed
