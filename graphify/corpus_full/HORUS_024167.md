# HUMANIZED CARE IN PATIENTS WITH ADVANCED CANCER: A PERSPECTIVE FROM PATIENT-FAMILY DIE

To explore the meaning of humanized care perceived by the family caregiver and the person with advanced cancer. To carry out this qualitative study, with an interpretative phenomenological approach, information was collected through in-depth interviews and field journals. For the development of hermeneutic interpretation, Heidegger's perspective was assumed, and the stages of intentionality, reduction and constitution were followed. As for the analysis of the discourse, Bardin's constructs were used: preanalysis, coding, categorization and interpretation. 17 days were considered (patient-family). The perceptions of humanized nursing care focused on the transpersonal relationship and the discourses collected reported an absence of closeness between nursing and the patient, whose main expressions were the lack of emotional understanding and dissatisfaction with needs. From these findings, topics based on the generation of a transpersonal relationship were derived of priority, going through the satisfaction of needs and empatic companionship fundamental to emotional well-being.
