# TECHNOLOGICAL DEVELOPMENT BASED ON A SOLAR DEHYDRATIATION SYSTEM FOR VALUE GENERATION IN FOODS PRODUCED BY A VULNERABLE SANTANDER COMMUNITY

Fresh fruit and vegetables have high water content, therefore, deteriorate in a short period of time if adequate handling is not given. Food conservation technologies consisting of storage at low temperatures or with controlled and modified atmospheres can be very expensive and, moreover, require a constant supply of energy to make the system work properly throughout the supply chain. The use of technologies for the dehydration of food has increased due to the reduction of post-harvest losses and provides a reduction in the weight and volume of products, so that it is facilitated its storage, transport and handling, and thus, it obtains a greater useful life. In the municipality of Galán, Santander, due to limitations in the communication routes and high transport costs, a considerable amount of post-harvest losses is presented, representing an important issue to solve in the short term.
