# Methodology for calibration of the Transyt-7f program for optimisation of traffic lights in urban areas.

The search for strategies to achieve efficient transit operation in urban areas, while preserving the supply of road infrastructure, has become one of the main engineering challenges. This research work establishes a methodology for calibration of the Genetic Algorithm of TRANSYT-7F for the optimization of semaphoric networks; the analysis is carried out by contrasting the optimization processes of the program with Genetic Algorithm (AG) and the conventional Hill Climb (HC) method, for the search for an optimal overall solution of the programming of semaphoric plans. A case study in the city of Medellín-Colombia is analyzed, where the current state of a coordinated network was evaluated and signal times are optimized using optimization using AG and HC. The optimal signal times resulting in each scenario are validated in a microsimulator to determine their effectiveness. With microsimulation in Aimsun Next it was possible to validate that the optimization process with AG, presents a superiority to the HC method and the conditions of the network, since a reduction of medium tail and delays in the network was observed.
