# Estimate of scenarios, productivity rates and probability analysis for optimization of construction project schedules using hybrid simulations

Traditionally, the construction industry has been characterized by delays and cost overruns in project implementation, as well as particularly a lag in the adoption of new technologies that will allow for optimisation and productivity enhancement. Good pre-construction planning efforts are a vital part of effective management and implementation of construction projects. To prepare more accurate schedules and cost calculations, realistic productivity scenarios and indices are needed to improve accuracy. Using simulation to model construction process scenarios can help with this aspiration by capturing complicated behaviors, uncertainties and dependencies. Consequently, this proposal raises the use of hybrid simulation approaches by combining discrete event simulation (DES), system dynamics (SD), and Montecarlo simulation to determine productivity rates and probability analysis in construction processes, which allow optimising construction project timelines
