# Mechanical and thermal CHARACTERIZATION OF AN EPOXIC RESIN WITH A RECYCLED MATERIAL EASTUMTER

The residual polymeric materials are used more frequently in different applications and waste wheels have become an example of the global problem, and there is a continuous effort to use recycled rubber. Epoxy polymers are systems with a large number of applications and recycled tyres can have a significant contribution. The aim of this work is to obtain and mechanically evaluate an epoxy/caucus compound with surfactants. The composite material was obtained with recycled pneumatics, of a particle size of 177 μm. The particle size of 177 μm was washed with solvents to remove oils present in the product (RW). The oligomers used to obtain the thermostable material (TS) are DER331 and TMAB amina. As non-ionic surfactant, ricin oil (CO) was used with HLB 14. The TS was evaluated with CO to establish the changes and evaluate the behavior of the composite material with this additive (RW).
