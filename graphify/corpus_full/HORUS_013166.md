# New seismic design approach for reinforced concrete wall buildings using base insulation

In Latin America, the use of walls of reinforced concrete as a structural system for the construction of housing in height has been popularized, due to its economy, construction speed and maximum use of areas; however, the system is characterized by low ductility due to the great rigidity of the walls, reason why its performance is compromised in response to the different earthquakes that occurred in recent years. To improve the performance of this structural system, the current regulations increase the demands of design and ductility with the use of elements of edge, increase of minimum sections and increase of the value of the increased cutting forces, which at the end of the exercise is concluded in an increase of construction cost of the building, and without the guarantee that the maximum expected performance is obtained with the application of these actions. Thus, the same method of optimizing the performance of this type of structures should be sought through other ways that do not require the increase of sections and of steels amounts; and it can be controlled by the seismic demand that the structure has before the presence of certain earthquakes. The results show that the performance of the isolated structure is improved in the face of the occurrence of earthquakes moving from a level of performance of protection of life to an immediate occupation. Additionally, it is observed that the super structure of buildings equipped with seismic insulators tend to show a rigid body behavior with floor drifts equivalent to only 10% of the drifts of the fixed base buildings.
