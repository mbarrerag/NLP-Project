# Socio-economic aspects associated with water management in the cacaoculture of Valle del Cauca, Colombia

The cocoa farming emerges as an expanding productive option, driven by the growing demand for quality cocoa at local and international level. To capitalize these opportunities, technologies supported by investments and technological transfers must be implemented, in addition to adapting to climate scenarios that affect production. The lack of knowledge about the management of water resources in cocoa cultivation is a common problem among cocoa growers, due to the scarcity of research, training and technical assistance, which affects productivity, competitiveness and development of the sector. The aim of this study was to evaluate, in a preliminary way, the water management in cocoa cultivation in the Cauca Valley, Colombia, considering biophysical, socio-economic and cultural variables in cocoa producing farms. 53 cocoa producers from 14 municipalities were interviewed; 60% of the interviews were carried out in production pre-production areas. The data were analyzed by means of descriptive, inferential and multivariate statistics techniques. The cocoa growers exhibit generation diversity and a high educational level, with 73.6 % of producers over 50 years of age and 49.1 % of the interviews were carried out in higher education studies.
