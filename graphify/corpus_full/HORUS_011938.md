# Determination of the Diagnostic Reference Levels in Procedures for Cardiac Catheterism in Pediatrics.

In this study, the Typical Values were determined in a Colombian pediatric hospital in Bogotá for the procedure of cardiac catheterization, arteriography and aortogram. For its achievement, the recommendations of the publication 135 of the ICRP of 2017 were followed, which showed its determination from the values of the product kerma area (PKA), kerma in air at the reference point (Kar), fluoroscopy time and number of images. These values were organized by age range of the values of 50 to < 1 year, 1 to < 5 years, 5 to < 10 years and 10 to < 16 years; weights < 5 kg, 5 to < 15 kg, 15 to < 30 kg and image quality for the thickness of 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20 cm of PMMA, values of maximum functioning of the procedure, and variosity of the procedure. The image quality in the various modes of operation and thicknesses was found to vary from 0.61 to 2.52 and the number of lines per millimetre between 0.9 and 1.2. It is observed that the typical values determined are higher for the greater range of ages, sizes and weights. Furthermore, when comparing the typical values of Kar values associated with cardiac catheterization procedures by age range, with those reported by Carlos Úbeda and collaborators, in the article: “Setting up regional diagnostic reference levels for pediatric interventional cardiology in Latin America and the Caribbean accounts: preliminary and results and identified challenges”, these are superior to those reported in the years 2022, with the exception of the range of 10 to 16 years. For the images it is concluded that the values of noise signal and pairs of lines per millimetre obtained for different thicknesses, show a low quality of the images, but useful for the clinical purpose
