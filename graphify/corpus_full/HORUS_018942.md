# OTHER WORLDS IN THIS: RITS, PRACTICES AND ESTATE CREATIONS IN COLOMBIA

The book analyzes the deep cultural and historical roots of esoteric practices present in the country. Based on historical documentation, hemerographical and a wide repertoire of interviews with people who practice these trades, the motivations, rituals, responses and orientations offered by experts in astrology, reading of tarot, etc. The book does not condemn or trivialize these practices and traditions, it is proposed to offer plausible analyses and explanations about these phenomena which are part of our popular culture
