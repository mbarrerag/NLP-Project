# REHABILITATION IN POSCOVID-19: A PERSPECTIVE FROM NUTRITIONAL INTERVENTION AND CORPORAL MOVEMENT

The effects associated with SARS-CoV-2 entail long-term implications that affect the health condition of those who have overcome the acute stage of the disease. The experiences and research instill the need to promote the comprehensive recovery of the Covid-19 survivors, awakening the need to establish rehabilitation approaches that tend to improve the functioning and quality of life of the people. A review was carried out under the PRISMA protocol of scientific publications in relation to the rehabilitation of the body movement and the nutritional intervention in post-covid-19 state. They were identified in to-tal 114 titles; after the reading and application of eligibility criteria were selected as final sample 32 articles. The results are presented in terms of: 1. Rehabilitation of the pulmonary function and cardiopulmonary resistance, 2. Rehabilitation of neurological and neuromuscular conditions, 3. Nutritional rehabilitation and changes in the body composition, 4. Nutritional recommendation for the post-covid-19 nutritional recovery and rehabilitation. The post-covid-19 physiotherapeutic intervention should include paradigms that involve the patient's socioeconomic conditions, ensuring that the patient's full-life is satisfied, and the patient's health is in order.
