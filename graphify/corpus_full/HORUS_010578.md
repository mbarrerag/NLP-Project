# TRAYECTORIES AND FORMS OF POLITICAL CONFIGURATION OF WOMEN VICTIMS OF ARMED CONFLICT IN THE REGION OF URABA, ANTIOCIA DEPARTMENT, COLOMBIA

This article presents the case of women victims of the armed conflict in the Urabá antioqueño, Colombia, and the life paths they have traced between the 1990s and the 2000s, making visible the humanitarian crisis, the absence of the State against this and the impacts on women and their lives. The guiding question is: how do women who have experienced situations of violence go from a victimistic trajectory to a therapeutic path of recognition, accompaniment and leadership, and position themselves socially and politically in their territories as incidents? Women's experiences, voices and practices are the methodological starting point for understanding their political trajectory and their contributions to the construction of democracy. Focal groups and interviews were conducted with activists of the women's movement, members of social, academic, women leaders and protagonists of the region. One of the results is the political configuration of women in Urabá and their impact on the construction of welfare conditions in their communities and the contributions of alternatives to armed conflict
