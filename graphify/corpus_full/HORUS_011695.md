# MULECULAR IDENTIFICATION OF LACRODECTUS GEOMETRICUS POPULATIONS POPULATIONS PRESENTED IN FORESTRY IN THE CESAR AND BIOTECHNOLOGIC APPLICATIONS OF THEIR VENEAN.

Latrodectus geometricus is a cosmopolitan species whose venom has neurotoxic effects in mammals and also has a biotechnological potential as insecticides? antimicrobials among others. In Colombia, this species has a wide distribution in the tropical dry forest ecosystem and has been reported to be present in several departments. However, has not been formally registered in the department of Cesar. In this work, were eggs and adult specimens of Latrodectus geometricus collected in the tropical dry forest ecosystem associated with the campus of the National University of La Paz Headquarters and surroundings? With the aim of establishing a useful colony to study the biologia and biotechnological potential of the poison of this species? were followed by crias that hatched the eggs, describing aspects associated with their behaviors? survival and maintenance. In addition, is it proposed to perform molecular characterization of local specimens with the IOC marker and the search for other markers to refine this characterization
