# Theoretical and experimental aspects of La2FeCoO6 lanthanide ferrocobaltite

In this work, the synthesis of the material called ferrocobaltite lanthanide La2FeCoO6 is reported by means of the modified method of Pechini. The experimental study of the structural, morphological and magnetic properties was carried out and the functional theory of density was implemented by means of the VASP code to analyze the energy bands, density of states and the thermophysical properties that characterize this material. Rietveld refinement of the experimental patterns of X-ray diffraction revealed the crystallization of this material in a monoclinic structure similar to the perovskita, spatial group P21 /n (#14). On the other hand, the images of electron microscopy of scanning and microscopy of atomic force revealed that the surface morphology is essentially polycrystalline, with medium grain sizes of this material, which is considered to be the result of an Im. The disperssive spectroscopy of X-rays was applied by means of the material. The material is a dis-interchange mechanism between states of high espin of Fe 2+ with low CO 4+ states of low CO 4+ expressed by the orbitals O 2−. The dependence of specific heat with respect to temperature and pressure, the thermal expansion coefficient, Debye temperature and the parameter of Grüneisen were calculated from the state equation using the quasi-harmonic model of Debye.
