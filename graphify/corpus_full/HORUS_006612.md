# Modeling CFD of a continuous cooking oven of Maize Arepas

For the Arepas industry in Colombia the technological development is given by empirical knowledge, families for decades performing the same process with the same tools, very natural process in the country, is why the need arises to get started the characterization of the processes, this by understanding each device separately. The present work was emphasized in the furnaces, whose function in the process of manufacturing arepas is the removal of microbial load, indispensable function in this sector. By means of a CFD model in OpenFoam open source software, the characterization of the baking process was carried out, defining first the conditions of cooking and general geometries, then a geometric model was generated and through the sofware solvers the inefficient heat accumulation areas were solved. The results obtained allowed to show a strong prescient of vortices in the areas between the burners and a rat of temperature growth in few steps of high time.
