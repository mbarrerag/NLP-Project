# Cane 39

Ricardo Luna is a prestigious neurosurgeon who disconnects from reality, when a car bomb explodes behind the ambulance in which he carries his wife in labor. Unable to endure loss, he takes refuge in the depth of a pipe where he lives with uprooted beings and a snake that constantly stalks him. Ushula teaches him to survive in the subworld until a fortuitous fact promises him to be the one of before, but adverse circumstances confirm his condemnation. .
