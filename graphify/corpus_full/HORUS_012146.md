# SANTIAGO DÍAZ PIEDRAHITA: ITS CONTRIBUTION TO BOTANICA IN COLOMBIA AND THE REAL EXPEDITION PROGRAMME OF THE NEW KINGDOM OF GRANADA

In the history of science in Colombia, Santiago Díaz Piedrahita (1944-2014) is without doubt one of the most outstanding botanists and intellectuals of his generation. Not only did he excel at his extensive knowledge of the taxonomy and systematic of the family Asteraceae in Colombia (family of the friar, the grove, the sunflower, among others), but he documented and analyzed, with the rigor of a professional historian, the origin and development of botany in our country. He was one of the greatest experts of the Royal Botany Expedition of the New Kingdom of Granada (Fernández-Alonso 2015), Díaz-Piedrahita who clarified the methodology with which the members of the Expedition worked when finding the correspondence between the herbarium, the iconography, the diagnostic works and the manuscripts that were elaborated by this scientific commission (Díaz-Piedrahita 1986).
