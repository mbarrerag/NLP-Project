# Intensification of energy efficiency for a multi-domain energy system by direct intervention in its dynamics

The efficient use of energy is currently a global objective to improve the quality of life and promote economic and social progress. Dynamic systems of multiple energy domains integrate various forms of energy (mechanical, electrical, thermal, pneumatic and chemical) to meet varied production and consumption needs. These complex systems, present in equipment and machinery of all kinds, are characterized by their multiple highly interrelated components. Traditionally, the analysis of these systems under a reductionist approach motivated by simplification tended to the omission of their internal dynamics, limiting the development of new operating strategies based on their dynamic and complex nature. This work proposes a strategy to increase the energy efficiency of these systems, considering their real physical manifestation. Through an intelligent control structure based exclusively on measurable behaviour, new trajectories of behavior available under conditions of operation subject to influences of the environment, the results demonstrate the effectiveness of the method by achieving precisely the desired operational objectives, using less energy from the system's power injection source. their real physical manifestation.
