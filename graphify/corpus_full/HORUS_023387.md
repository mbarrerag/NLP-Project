# Technical and economic approach of electricity generation using the Medellin (AMVA) aqueduct network.

Global goals for future scenarios are largely focused on creating sustainable renewable cities, energy affordable and not pollutant. Recently, a new form of energy production taking advantage of urban water supply infrastructure has been studied and implemented. It is about generating energy by using the implicit energy potential of water supply networks. The use of this potential can provide an extra benefit in the distribution networks since energy can be produced from the overpressure in the pipes. Electric power generation from the water system results in economic benefits, renewable energy production, a more resilient system, reduced pollutant emissions and reduced greenhouse gas emissions. The adoption of these technologies allows for the generation of energy within the city itself, achieving smarter, more sustainable cities and contributing to the country's energy transition
