# From the body to musical apprehension

I feel the need to make a journey from my current place to the “La Cumbre” neighborhood located in Ciudad Bolívar in Bogotá, neighborhood where I was born and place where my father family came displaced product of violence in the countryside. I want to reconstruct my experiences and focus on recognizing the artist-teacher who lives with me from the record, capture and audiovisual processing, remembering part of my experience by witnessing injustice, abandonment and poverty. I propose to share the process of building my being, as well as to deepen the idea about the hopeful critical look, giving an account of how it is part of the methodology that accompanies me in the processes of musical apprehension with others. I propose that from group musical compositions, the sounds produced with the body and with non-conventional objects, are recorded, mixed and produced by generating spaces of collective creation. I invite the participation of the different bodies tosonating children, young, teachers and companions of the master to make part of the “Sinphonia for Soñar”, a sound-vinculant proposal founded in the concept from the bodies intability and from the sound (the insup) part of the methodology that companies me in the processes of musical application with others. My purpose is to enable spaces for collective creation using the body and convention objects to produce sounds. I extended an invitation to the different resonant bodies of boys, girls, young people, teachers, and colleagues from the master's to make part of "A Symphony to Dream", a sonorous-binding proposal that is based on the reification of the bodies, both through sound and parting from the concept of sound, headed towards the perspective of social transformation
