# Evidence-based clinical standard: Diagnosis and treatment of acute myocardial infarction patients at the National University Hospital of Colombia.

The National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN), together with the National University of Colombia, have positioned themselves as a center of reference for healthcare and academic, with a projection to form clinics or centers of excellence; in the search for this objective, the development of clinical standards based on evidence (ECBE), which are documents made with the best available medical evidence and which allow adequate and standardized care of patients. Cardiovascular disease represents the main cause of death worldwide, and one of the main ones in Colombia; early detection of the disease increases opportunities for timely treatment, reducing morbidity and mortality and the economic and human costs associated with it. In 2019, 281 people with AMI were treated at the National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN), for a total of 1742 days of hospital stay, which is why AMI is considered a major disease in the hospital.
