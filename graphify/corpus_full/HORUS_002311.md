# Lambda fago for sequencing

This practice is aimed at the development of activities in molecular biology applied to the obtaining and characterization of reference material based on lambda fago, for sequencing purposes, in the framework of the agreement between the National Institute of Metrology of Colombia (INM) and the National University of Colombia (UNAL), whose general objective is to support the realization of practices in scientific and industrial metrology by master students of UNAL. The work includes support in the amplification and verification of fragments by PCR, the purification of amplification products, the production of reference material and participation in laboratory techniques associated with wet work, activities that focus on the standardization of procedures and methods with metrological traceability that contribute to the practical training of students in molecular biology techniques and in the generation of reference material for sequencing of Lambda Fago, aligned with the principles of research, technological development and innovation
