# Distances in Astronomy: a didactic approach to studying trigonometric functions in the ninth grade.

The inclusion of trigonometry as a branch of mathematics is usually established in the tenth grade within the Colombian school curriculum. However, the various difficulties were taken into account when introducing and developing trigonometry in school for both the teacher and the student. This allowed me to propose a methodology in which some basic notions of flat geometry were presented, leading students to understand the concept of proportionality in triangles and hence to define the reasons and trigonometric functions. Six activities were applied for this purpose. Although one of them covered two contents, the two finals were related to the topics of heights and distances in terrestrial situations and astronomical parallaxes. The student population on which this work was carried out has stated that their incursion into pre-trigonometry last year in ninth grade, has facilitated their performance and understanding in their current course of mathematics in tenth grade
