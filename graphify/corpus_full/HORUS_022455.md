# OBLIGATORY LICENCES: LEGISLATIVE RECOVERY

Historical vision of the regulation of compulsory licences from the 1971 Trade Code to the current regulations, Decision 486 of 2000 and WTO Adpic Agreements. Origin of compulsory licences to facilitate access to medicines and to alleviate abuses that may be committed with patents on medicines, Of particular importance in the current pandemic scenario, both for vaccines and for medicines against Covid 19
