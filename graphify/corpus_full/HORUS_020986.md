# Construction of experimental modules for the improvement of practical learning of control theory in state variables

In this project, we propose a didactic strategy for the meaningful learning of control in the framework of the variables of state that is imparted in the course: Analysis in State Variables, belonging to the Ing. of Control of the Faculty of Mines, through individual, portable and low cost modules.In the industry, the Engineering of control has taken a leading role, and therefore, the need to understand and apply control techniques more advanced than the traditional ones to the industrial processes.The theory of control in the space of states has traditionally been taught through mathematical examples, and, after the incursion of the computers, through computer simulations.In the Faculty of Mines, despite the existence of a control laboratory with some experimental plants, they do not adapt to its use in the framework of the state variables, so that the experimentation of the theory of control in the space of states is null, and therefore its training offered in automatic control is incomplete., therefore, these modules will have a strong impact on the training process of students. Hence, the validation of two modules already constructed previously, one with a linear dynamic, noise in measurement, disturbances, where the theory seen in the course (which is for linear systems) can be confronted exactly, that is, that simulation must give almost identical results in real plants. This will have a very positive impact, because students when confronting this reality that the theory works as predicted by simulation, will make them fully trust it by eliminating the normal doubts left by mere simulation. The second module is designed to be a nonlinear system, in such a way that it challenges students to control a more complicated process, which will also be multivariate, with noises and disturbances.
