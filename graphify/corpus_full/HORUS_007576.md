# Use of gamification and immersive technologies in programming teaching

Programming is a fundamental skill in the 21st century, but traditional teaching can be ineffective. Immersive technologies offer an attractive alternative to improve learning experience. These technologies seek to motivate and involve students in programming, as well as to improve learning and understanding of complex concepts and develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Based on the above, the need arises to review how gamification techniques and emerging technologies are impacting university students in programming learning and to develop a platform that integrates these tools to support these learning-learning processes, initially a process that will be validated in students of the computer systems management career at the National University of Colombia _x0096_ Manizales Headquarters
