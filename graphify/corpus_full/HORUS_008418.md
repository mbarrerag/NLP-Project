# LACTURE GRASE, A NATURAL SOURCE OF BUTIRIC AND CLA AND ITS IMPACT ON HUMAN HEALTH

The purpose of this review is to obtain a technical evaluation that can support the recommendation of the consumption of dairy products in the diet, despite being a source of fatty acids and cholesterol. Some fatty acids, such as omega-3, can contribute to nutrition and we can obtain them from dairy products. The Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) stresses the importance of milk as a source of nutrients, but its high content of saturated fatty acids and cholesterol suggests that its consumption can negatively affect human health. The review of the scientific literature has shown that milk fat contains a variety of bioactive components that can have beneficial effects for the human being. These components include omega-3 fatty acids, phospholipids, conjugated linoleic acids and sterols. For example, the reduction of the risk of cardiovascular diseases is related to the intake of omega-3 fatty acids, while the increase in calcium absorption is related to the intake of phospholipids.
