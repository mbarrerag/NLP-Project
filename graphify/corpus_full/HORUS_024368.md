# Image perspectives: categories and visual criteria in the academic context based on the concept “Water City”

This research addresses the absences, limits and difficulties of image analysis in the academic context and other activities linked to the construction of new knowledge. In particular, the relationship between text and record within scientific documents will be addressed and it will be recorded how such interaction has left the second in a subordinate position. With the demonstration of such imbalance, attention will also be drawn to the cognitive opportunity that is lost when the explanatory potential of the images is ignored and its enormous capacity to show different aspects of human societies; which are relevant to different disciplines. This situation is explained, among other things, by the pervivence of visual prejudices, the need to deepen the interdisciplinary character of the images. Especially, emphasis will be placed on the construction of the visual theoretical corpus from the definition of sets of images, which should allow for the synthesis of perspectives and interpretations of the various disciplines of knowledge. society is unknown; which are relevant to different disciplines. This situation is explored, together with other things, by the persistence of visual prejudices, the need to understand on the methods, elements, and concepts in the image as theoretical and informative collections, and the deficiency in methodological instruments available to analysis them. To solve these problems, this document proposals some specific methodological strategies for visual research and with which it is sound to answer the interdisciplinary nature of images.
