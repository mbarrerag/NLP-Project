# ADDITIVE MANUFACTURE APPLICABLE IN A PARALLEL ARCHITECTURE MAKING

This research is focused on technologically transforming a CNC machine with more than 10 years of use and modify it to add additive manufacturing capabilities with ceramic material. The purpose and result is to demonstrate that with a machine, not necessarily of latest technology, the small and medium enterprise can continue to be competitive making an investment in the recovery or modification of its technological resource as an alternative to avoid an investment considerably superior; that besides requiring training very specific to all the operating staff; also this model of acquisition of specialized technology limits the flexibility of experimentation in function of creating new products and processes by the restrictions of origin established by the manufacturer. For this experimental project the manual orthoglide CNC 3 degrees of freedom with parallel architecture constructed by thesis of the DIMA-UN group in 2006 with a hardware to which the manufacturer does not provide support for these 8 years and a software with limited capacities due to the flexible programming languages. For these restrictions it is decided to carry out a technological conversion, which implied redesign, adapt and consolidate 4 modules that were identified as fundamental for a process of additive manufacturing (subsystem of extrusor, structure and material)
