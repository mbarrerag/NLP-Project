# Local Zeta functions of Igusa and Bernstein polynomials.

The purpose of this paper, in which the final work of the teacher is developed, is to study the conjecture raised in 1988 by the mathematician Jun-ichi Igusa in [10]; which ensures a relationship between the poles of the local zeta function of Igusa Z(s, f) and the zeros of the Bernstein-Sato bf polynomial. In addition, basic concepts are addressed in the area of p-adic analysis and the behavior of particular families of polynomials f
