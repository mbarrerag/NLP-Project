# PROFILAXIS FOR GASTROINTESTINAL BLOOD IN PATIENTS OF THE INTERNAL MEDICINE SERVICE OF A UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL

Antiulcerous drugs are frequently used in hospitalized patients, however, this use is often not indicated. To describe the frequency of prescription and indication of medications to prevent gastrointestinal bleeding in hospitalized patients. Transversal, descriptive, prospective study of the internal medicine service of the Bogotá-San José Hospital of Bogotá, Colombia. Patients with diagnosis of gastrointestinal bleeding or history of allergy to antiulcerous drugs were excluded. Demographic data were collected, as well as prescribed drugs. It was determined whether the indication of the drug was adequate and the type of prescription error was identified. 179 patients were included, 102 (57% women. Average age of 61.3 years (±20.2). The main admission diagnosis was infectious disease 76 (42.4%). Of the total patients, 165 (92.17%) received medication for the prevention of gastrointestinal bleeding. The indication was adequate in 75 patients (41.89%). The most frequent error was the use in patients at risk of bleeding was 101 (97.1%). A high percentage of the gastrointestinal patients received medication for prevention was not indicated
