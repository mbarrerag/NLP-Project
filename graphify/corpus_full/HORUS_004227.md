# DISPLACING THE HUMAN IN ARTISTICAL PRODUCTION.

The panel on human relationship earth, I explained the way in which beings and forces of nature have a poetic agency in the production of two projects, following the question of how to shift the limits of the human in the artistic production: The paper exposes the metantropic principle as a way to place us topologically and ontologically, regarding the relationship with the beings and forces of our planet. It proposes a starting point for the sensitive production, understood as a possible epistemology, which can make ethical contributions to the intrusion of Gaia. The metantropic principle is explained pragmatically in two artistic projects: 1. In the living sculpture Native/Foraneas, where participation as an artist moves to be manager of the project and to be dispersor of seeds. The formalization of the sculpture depends on the vital and natural strategies of the plants: entangled natives of the high Andean forest that have the paradoxical situation of being foreign in the territory occupied today by the city of Bogotá. 2.
