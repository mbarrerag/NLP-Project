# Thou will not write. Reading inscriptions, touching files and the sacrificed in the Constitutional Court of Colombia

This thesis deals with a sacred object in a modern institution. For this, I take seriously a metaphor naturally occurring during my ethnographic step by the Constitutional Court between August and December 2019, according to which a file is sacred to be a public document. I wonder how a technology of legal knowledge - an inscription - as the file becomes a sacred object in the hands of those who manage it and, for this, I focus on the practices of touching the guardianship files in their review. I analyze how the sacred speaks of two different ways of touching and relating to this technology: as a working document and as the history of a case. In one of these, writing and the touch of files are part of the usual functioning of the Court and, in the other, writing and touch of the file are restricted and forbidden in its circulation.
