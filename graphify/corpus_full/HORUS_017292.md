# SPACE SAMPLING OF INSPECTS IN CULTIVES AS A SUPPORT FOR EVIDENCY-BASED CONTROL

The sampling provides information on the density, structure and population fluctuation of thrips (Thysanoptera:Thripidate) in the crops and constitutes an essential input to feed early warning systems and to define and evaluate control strategies.The sampling of insects in crops is usually carried out using models based on design (such as random, systematic, stratified, conglomerate and polyetapic simple sampling), for which it is usual to define the nature of the population, the parameter to estimate, the type and technique of sampling, using some probabilistic model (usually the normal) for the calculation of sample size, however, these techniques usually do not take into account the geographical coordinates of the origin of the entomological samples, as well as information inherent to the cultivation and to the area where it is developed.The methods of sampling of insects based on spatial statistics allow to include auxiliary variables related to the parameters to be estimated, involving the time and space of the entomological sample.Thrips are key pests of crops because they can reduce production, transmit viruses, generate cosmetic damages to the products that are marketable and restrict exports for many species. The algorithm is used to calculate the algorithm using conventional methods with space, which shows a decrease in the number of samples to achieve a lower bias in estimation using the spatial methodology. This algorithm can be used for sampling not only of insects, but of soils, pathogens or other fields of study in agronomy
