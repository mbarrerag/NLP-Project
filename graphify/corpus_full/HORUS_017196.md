# Design and development of an interactive soFTWARE for teaching and learning of the FRACTAL COMPRESSION OF IMAGES

In the study of courses with complex themes, as is the case with data compression, students experience difficulty in learning because of the abstract of these theories and the absence of interactive digital resources. In the fractal compression of images, one seeks to deduce a set of functions, called iterated function system (IFS), which by iterations reconstructs the image that is desired to compress; thus, compression levels are reached that are not achieved using another technique, because the image is considered compressed in the description of the functions that generate it, that is what is stored, and to recover the image simply iterate functions. This article describes the design and development of an interactive software that supports the teaching-learning of the fundamental concepts of fractal compression of images: contractual related transformations, iterated function systems (IFS), IFS coding (compression) and decoding (decompression) IFS
