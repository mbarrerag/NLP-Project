# REQUIREMENTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS IN THE DOMICILARY PARENTERAL NUTRITION IN ADULT PATIENTS: AN EXPLORATORY REVIEW

the population of patients receiving home parenteral nutrition (NPD) in the world is on the rise and providing it presents important challenges for both patients and health providers. The objective of the review was to identify and report the evidence available on the use of home parenteral nutrition, exploring the logistical needs for health teams, strategies for the fulfilment of clinical objectives, prevention and management of complications, impact on the quality of life of patients and associated costs.Method: exploratory review of systemic and thematic reviews, metaanalysis and clinical practice guidelines focused on the management of adult patients with indication of parenteral nutrition (mixed or exclusive), excluding those with required delivery by by or in pregnancy. The publications identified in the bases Cochrane, MEDLINE, LILACS and EMBASE were included.In 2018 to 2023 and without restricting the language.Resulted: 15 articles were analyzed, 12 of patients focused on health outcomes, 2 of the needs of logistics were identified
