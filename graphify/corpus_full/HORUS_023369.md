# Analytic methodologies for the study and quantification of flavonoids on the extracts of passiflora leaf ligularis justs.

The research interest in medicinal plants and the demand for the practice of traditional-complementary medicine have increased at a global level1-2, as well as the incidents reported by treatments based on natural sources.
