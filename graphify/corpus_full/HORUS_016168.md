# Ex ante evaluation between light train, tram and electric BRT in developing countries.

This research developed a methodology to be implemented in the initial stages of a project or in the planning of the transport of cities of developing countries, since, this type of economies demands more number of passengers to make a public transport system economically viable. The methodology was developed from a process of analytical hierarchy taking into account variables that influence the decision of a transport system such as: the demand of passengers, the form of the city, the type of public transport route and the uses of the land, to implement it, must follow two steps: 1. determine the maximum system to which a population group of a city can access and 2. the evaluation of the 3 medium capacity transport alternatives, such as the electric rapid bus, light train and tram. The methodology has proximity in a corridor of three main cities such as Medellin, Bogotá - Colombia and Guadalajara – Mexico, the first two belong to projects in development process and the last one in operation.
