# Vulnerability of the Colombian Pacific coast to coastal erosion: mangrove forests as mitigating ecosystems

The effect of spatial modification that mangrove forests produce on the coastal front by attenuating waves, capturing sediments and forming soil, reduces the intensity of marine threats on the coast, decreases the exposure and vulnerability of coastal communities, and these communities increase their resilience after the impacts of the threats.These benefits vary depending on the conditions of each region or country, so it is essential to understand both local mangroves, as well as the environmental environments where they are located.The objective of this research was to estimate the trend of position change of the front of the mangrove forest at the local level in the Colombian Pacific The discharge of sediments of the Miragrande river, municipality of Tumaco in the department of Nariño, constituted by a deltaic mangrove with influence of terrigenous sediments, protected by a dynamic coastal arrow produced by the action of the marine currents, the discharge of sediments of the Meastral River, and a mesomareal regime.It is compared with a mangrove of a contrasting region in the Old Point Regional Park in the oceanic island of San Andrés, located in the northwestern part of the Colombian Caribbean Sea, Colombia.
