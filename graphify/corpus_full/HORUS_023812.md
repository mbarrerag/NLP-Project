# ASSISTANCE AND CHALLENGES OF THE OCCUPATION THERAPY IN PALIAN CARE: A PERSPECTIVE FROM STUDENT PRACTICAL EXPERIENCE

The palliative care aims to impact the well-being of people who are in a chronic-degenerative health condition or other conditions that shorten their life expectancy. Considering the shortage of occupational therapists trained in this specialty in Colombia and other countries, this article presents reflections on the meanings and challenges it posed to two undergraduate students in Occupational Therapy and their teachers, their experience in a practical training in palliative care with hospitalized adults and older adults. Linking the subjectivity of each student with this context emerged five axes around which the text is developed: therapeutic relationship; significant and spiritual occupation; fatigue through compassion; ethical reflections, and professional training. It is concluded that in the training of occupational therapists it is necessary to implement pedagogical strategies that enrich the humanized, ethical, critical and person-centered approach
