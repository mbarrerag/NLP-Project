# COMMUNICATION BETWEEN DISEASE AND FAMILY: A RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN HONEY, DIRECT AND REAL HUMAN BEES

the stay in the intensive care unit (ICU) generates feelings of lack of control, uncertainty and anxiety in the patient and their families that are related to lack of information and communication both on the health-disease process lived and on the treatment. to describe the characteristics of communication in a determined ethnos between the nurse and the family member of the hospitalized patient. qualitative study with a micro-ethnic approach that used Bardin's proposal for analysis. Participants were 12 relatives of patients hospitalized in the adult ICU of the cities of Bucaramanga and Floridablanca (Colombia) during 2016. Semi-structured interview was conducted with five questions about communication with the nursing professional. the majority of participants were women, university students and daughters from cities different from the place of the interview. A main topic emerged: communication: an honest, direct and real relationship between human beings, formed by four categories: information as central axis of communication in ICU; communication that is not expressed with words, even more important in the language; communication does not create a positive relationship and affect us
