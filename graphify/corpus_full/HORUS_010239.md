# RESULTS_ EDUCATION SENSIBILITY.

The paper has as a starting point the striking thesis of the French philosopher Baptiste Morizot in his book Ways to be alive (2021), according to which the serious ecological crisis that we face today has its main cause in a crisis of our sensibility.It is, for this author, a crisis caused by the impoverishment of the ways that we have to relate to the natural world, from which human beings have progressively distanced ourselves by considering that our place, as human beings, is not in the midst of other living ones but in the area of reality defined as its opposite: that of the culture itself.In fact, this ontological separation between the natural and the human, which has been established since modernity, has brought as a consequence a certain indifference of our part to the destiny in vilo what face the other species called economic model and its practices of economic exploitation of the resources, and so on the basis of the above mentioned diagnosis, we will say that the possibility of giving an effective and lasting response to the ecological problem requires not only the launching of profound transformations of the economic model and its juridical order, and the most urgent forms of in the way of inhabitability of resources. Or is it a planetary shift from philosophy (i. e., Vinciane Despret, Baptiste Morizot and Emanuele Coccia), who, through their daring reflections on the living world, but above all, thanks to direct experiences on the ground, manage to destabilize our usual ways of observing, treating and responding to other non-humans. Indeed, we will seek to extract from these proposals, at the same time theoretical-practical, some productive keys to how philosophy can help remove thought schemes that hinder the possibility of experiencing the encounter with the alterity of the living world and contribute to the emergence of that new sensibility that the present critical moment demands of us
