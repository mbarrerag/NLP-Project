# CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDE FOR SCREANING OF PATIES AND THE MANNEG OF INFECTIONS CAUTED BY THESE BACTERIES// CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDELINE FOR SCREANING OF PATIES AT RISK OF COLONIZATION BY CARBAPENEEMase-PRODUCING IN

Infections by Enterobacterals producing carbapenemases (EPC) have increased in recent years. Colombia has become an endemic country for this group of microorganisms and infections that cause them have an important impact in terms of morbidity and mortality. Early identification of CCT carriers who enter health institutions as patients is necessary to implement isolation and control measures of appropriate infections that limit the spread of this type of microorganisms in hospitals. Furthermore, treatment of these infections is difficult due to limited therapeutic alternatives available and the scarcity of studies that demonstrate their effectiveness in this scenario. Thus, the aim of this work is to develop a clinical practice guide (GPC) for the screening of patients at risk of colonization by CPT and for the management of patients with infections, whether suspected or confirmed, caused by this type of bacteria, through a process of adaptation of CPT based on ADAPTE methodology.
