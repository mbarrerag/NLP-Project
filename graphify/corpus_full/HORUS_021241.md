# Magnetic hydrogels functionalized with Gemcitabine/NAB-paclitaxel as release vehicles for the potential treatment of pancreatic cancer in an in vitro model.

Pancreatic cancer represents one of the most important clinical challenges in modern oncology. Although this cancer has a low incidence, its mortality rate is one of the highest among the different types of cancer, which makes it a disease of significant importance in public health. The main limitation for the effective treatment of this cancer is represented in the desmoplastic stromal, a group of cells that grow in parallel with the tumor, forming a physical barrier that protects cancer cells from chemotherapeutic agents and other treatments. With the focus on demoplastic stromal in this type of cancer, this project aims to investigate how to reduce the difficulty of chemotherapy of pancreatic cancer by addressing the reactive stromal and pancreatic tumoral cells, by using in vitro models. To achieve this, it is proposed the use of biocompatible hydrogels, functionalized with magnetic nanoparticles, as vehicles for the release of agents such as gemcitabine/NAB-paclitaxel in pancreatic tumor cells and in the stard fibroblasts of pancreas through the use of the pre-aemic drugs
