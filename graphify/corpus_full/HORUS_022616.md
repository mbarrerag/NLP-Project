# TIN TIN - PASSIFLORA PINNATISTIPULA

The tin tin tin (Passiflora pinnatistimula Cav.) belongs to the family Passifloraceae and is one of the Andean high fruits. In the Andean region it is also known as round curuba, gulupa, chirubin, churilla and puru puru. It is a fruit that is basically restricted to direct use in rural food with little presence in the markets. The tin tin plant is a tomentose liana. The pulp is scarce with pleasant taste, slightly acidic, pale yellow color and numerous seeds
