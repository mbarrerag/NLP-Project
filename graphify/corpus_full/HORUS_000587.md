# SAULO - Generating artificial intelligence model to optimize Hass avocado production, using LLMs and real local data from the production unit

Colombia is the third world producer of aguacate Hass, with growth projection of 36% in the coming decades. However, the agricultural model of the country presents a low technical level in the fertilization of soils, which has led to an excessive use of fertilizers. Between 2002 and 2016, the consumption of nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium (N-P-K) in Colombia was 4.3 times higher than the global average, generating imbalances in production, environmental impacts and high cost. This research proposes SAULO – Agronome digital impact leveraged by Artificial Generative Intelligence to optimize the production of Avocado Hass, a model based on Generative Language Models (LLMS) to optimize the fertilization of the crop using the local data of productive units. The study is developed in a productive unit pilot in Colombia, where a platform with a Neo4j data base, optimized to handle graphs and semantic relationships is implemented. SAULO employs intelligent agents that interpret data in natural language (NL) and generate recommendations on fertilization, irrigation and pest control.
