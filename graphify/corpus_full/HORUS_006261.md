# Teaching mathematics through learning based on problems and playful strategies with chess game at the José Antonio Galán Educational Institution

This research focuses on describing the effective integration of chess and problem-based learning (ABP) as an innovative didactic strategy to improve arithmetic skills and other specific areas of mathematics of fourth and fifth grade students in the José Antonio Galán Educational Institution, located in Manzanares, Caldas. A mixed approach of descriptive scope is adopted, where the quantitative methodology collects numerical data on academic performance and resolution of activities proposed by the teacher. The qualitative methodology includes observation of classes, interviews and analysis of educational materials content linked to chess. Some results indicate significant improvements in academic performance, supporting the effectiveness of implemented pedagogical strategies. Significant advances in the critical thinking and mathematical reasoning of students are highlighted, underlining the importance of educational strategies that promote active and meaningful learning. This study contributes to knowledge about the integration of chess and the ABP into the teaching of mathematics, offering valuable perspectives to improve the educational quality at primary levels, both in arithmetic and in construction of figures, transformations in the plane, calculation strategies, accuracy of results, numerical regularities, three-dimensional classification, identification of angles, coordinates and proportionality of basics, and proportionality of measures, and of primary grades, and of of educational materials related to chess.
