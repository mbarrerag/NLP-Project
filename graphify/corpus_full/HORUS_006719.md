# Design of a methodology for the improvement of dental processes based on segmentation techniques in digital dentistry

This thesis proposes a methodology to support orthodontic treatments through techniques of image processing in digital dentistry. Directed by Juan David Tamayo Quintero at the National University of Colombia, the research aims to improve orthodontic processes using artificial vision and image processing. The study focuses on developing tools for the classification and analysis of the form of the dental arch, using methods of image processing and machine learning techniques. The methodology integrates data structures for dental models, including various file formats such as PLY, STL, OBJ and PCD. It explores the representation of data of cloud points, emphasizing techniques of neighborhood estimation and curvature. The document presents the creation of a database that includes digitized dental models and describes methodologies of arch-form classification, including ovoid, conical and square forms, together with the morphometric tools, and software development methods of fundamental truth determination and statistical analysis to evaluate the accuracy of the model.
