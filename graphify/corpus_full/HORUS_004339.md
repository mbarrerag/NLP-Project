# ♪ Think and build with the hands ♪ ♪ The art of reutilizing ♪ ♪ In the architectural house ♪

The presentation is a reflection on the results of the research. Are new approaches carried out by the research group Transepto?, of the School of Architecture of the National University of Colombia. The new generations, already from four to five years, are in constant contact with electronic screens, and develop an intense connection with the digital, unthinkable a few years ago. In years of new digital revolutions, the Universities have the responsibility to incorporate into the pedagogy contemporary technologies, but also to chain the digital world with that analog, and manual. Thinking with hands - and working with hands - develops new neural connections that foster the creativity of future architects. In our project workshops, of the basic cycle of teaching, various typical aspects of architecture, such as resistance, economy, the relationship with the environment, are studied in a practical way, thanks to the genesis of small architectural projects that students develop, and build, in small groups
