# Community Energy Solutions Cooperation Network

Within the country's 2030 goals for the electricity sector, it is found to have 100% access to electricity, increase to 73.3% renewable energy and double energy efficiency (energy intensity to 2.9 TJ/CO$).To achieve these objectives, plans and programs must be generated that allow progress in the generation of energy distributed near to the end uses, increase the levels of automation and supervision of the energy use processes that allow decisions to be made, with the objective of being energy efficient and ensuring the reliability of the use of energy. However, in several communities of the country it is evident under scientific and technological knowledge in the implementation of energy solutions, that allow to satisfy the needs of coverage, quality and efficiency of the electricity available, which leads to a decrease of the productivity and efficiency in the use of the available resources of the region. This presents a lack of research and innovation capacities to identify, interpret and contribute to the local challenges of the communities, around the development of sustainable energy generation projects. Therefore, it is necessary to form a Cooperation Network with the objective of implementing processes to contribute to increase the associated capacities and knowledge in the communities.
