# DYNAMIC MODELATION OF CHEMICAL AND BIOLOGICAL PROCESS

This book is not a compendium of models, nor is it a modeling manual, and less a summary of traditional unitary operations. The aim of the text is to show a simple and didactic way to construct dynamic models for chemical and biological processes, emphasizing the usefulness of such models in the analysis, optimization and control of those processes. In this sense, the characterization, representation (modelling) and control of industrial processes are shown as complementary themes, totally indispensable to have a global vision that highlights the utility of the model. The modeling methodology that is proposed and used provides semiphysical models of phenomenological basis (msbf), a family of models with very useful properties in the engineering of processes in general. In this methodology, the great novelty is that allows to introduce in a natural way, from the deduction itself of the model, the dynamic part in teaching of all the unitary operations, teaching that has been done until now only from the perspective of processes in steady state.
