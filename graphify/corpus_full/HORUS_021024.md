# Meditation on Death

_x0093_Meditation on death_x0094_ is a project of research and creation in which I inquire on death as a process of transformation, both of organic matter, and of that in some undetermined way that could be called soul or spirit. The act of meditation, leads me to think of the contemplation of that matter in that path of mutation, of a transfer of the vitality of a living being to another that is used as a host in that transformation process _x0096_ putrefaction _x0096_ possible regeneration. The center my plastic research in the figure of the tree. The appearance of that matter is static and sometimes almost in a state of suspension, but in constant transformation both in his life and in his death. In the seasons it may be more evident that they enter into a state of excessive inclination, but in one that they have reborn and so on one that they have cut off one of his branches, and then suddenly, in places without seasons like Colombia, a naked tree appears to be suspended or dying, or perhaps an impulse to her, just like one in a gesture of excessive inclination, but in one of one of one that they have been, and so, again, and again, the same I start with explorations and experiments with mostly natural or representative materials and that refer to those processes of death; the materialities proper to death, whether in human beings, animals or plants: earth in different compositions, mold, liquids in process of decomposition, coal, natural fabrics close to those of the shrouds, among others. Understanding the properness of such materialities, their possibilities, provenance and ends, I operate with procedures in which, under the poetic, metaphorical, the symbolic and reflexive, I agree to such meditation on death, just as the end, but as part of a cycle of transformations, changes and senses.
