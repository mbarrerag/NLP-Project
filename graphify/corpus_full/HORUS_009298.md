# PRELIMINARY EFFECTS OF A DISEASE INTERVENTION FOR THE ADOPTION OF THE ROL IN FAMILY CAREERS OF MAJOR ADULTS

Main objective: To examine the effects of a psycho-educational nursing intervention on the adoption of the role of caregiver. Design of a quasi-experimental type with repeated measurements (pretest and postest) and a single group. The study population are Family Caregivers of Older Persons with chronic disease. Sampling was non-probabilistic by convenience, with a sample of 17 participants. A psycho-educational intervention, group, was carried out, consisting of 8 sessions, of 2 hours each. It is two main: a statistically significant increase with a large effect on the overall score of role adoption (=85.1; DE=9.1 to =92.1; DE=7.3; t=2.644; p=0.018, d=0.661); and in the dimension of work (=27.9; DE=4.6 to =31.4; DE=2.3; t=2.644; p=0.018, d=0.685).
