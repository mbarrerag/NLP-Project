# The work of crisis-time care_x0094_ The impact of the Covid-19 pandemic on _x0093_the habitat_x0094_ and _x0093_the inhabiting_x0094_the family of caregivers of persons with disabilities

This research thesis focuses on examining the interconnection between two crucial concepts in people's lives: "The Inhabiting" and "The Care", specifically in the Colombian context.Although the pandemic of COVID-19 has been described as equal in its effects, the reality is that its consequences have not been equitable.Some sectors, families and communities have borne heavier burdens and more significant risks.A particularly affected group, although often overlooked, are the caregivers of people with disabilities or chronic diseases.These caregivers perform an exhausting work, without receiving economic recognition, and in many cases, they do not even consider their work.The majority of these caregivers are women, mostly belonging to vulnerable social sectors, who lack resources to acquire private care services.In addition, economically, families that have a person with a chronic illness or disability tend to descend on the scale of poverty.The the thesis also seeks to explore the inclusion of the concept of care and recognition of caregivers in the public agenda. This is crucial in a context of ageing of the population, changes in the labor dynamics and increasing challenges in mental health.
