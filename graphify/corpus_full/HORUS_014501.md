# A SEMILINEAR WAVE EQUATION WITH NON-MONOTONE NONLINEARITY

We prove the existence of weak solutions to a semilinear wave with non-monotone asymptotically linear nonlinearity when the forcing is dominated by a trigonometric polynomial
