# Effect of the use of methodology supported by ICT in the teaching of statistics

Effect of the use of ICT-supported methodology on statistical education This paper presents a deepening thesis on the effect of the use of an ICT-supported methodology in the teaching of statistics. Based on different authors, it is argued that the use of technology can significantly improve the teaching of this subject using tools such as statistical software, simulations and educational games. Based on a preliminary diagnosis, the learning standards established in the curricular guidelines associated with random thought and data systems management at the Manzanares Educational Institution were prioritized. This diagnosis allowed the design of a pertinent and suitable learning unit for the socio-cultural context in which the research was developed, supporting its implementation in the use of non-traditional methodologies mediated by the use of ICT. The research found, in the context in which it was conducted, that the use of technology can significantly improve the learning of statistics. This conclusion was obtained thanks to the results achieved by the students who used the methodology showing a broader apprehension of statistical concepts. On the other hand, this paper comparing the ICT-supported methodology with traditional statistics teaching in terms of effectiveness and efficiency, concluding that the ICT-supported methodology is more effective and efficient in terms of time and resources. Overall, this paper is a valuable contribution to the field of statistics education and can be used as a guide to improve the teaching of this subject in the future. Key words: (ICT, VLO methodology, statistics, random thought, data systems management)
