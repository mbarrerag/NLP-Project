# Management alternatives for sustainable electricity supply in Non-Interconnected Areas.

In Colombia, around 2 million people still live without electricity, you are located in geographically isolated places where interconnection to the national electricity network becomes unviable – known as Non-Interconnected Zones (ZNI); which is a challenge for the country as soon as it reaches universalization of electricity. On the other hand, ZNI communities with access to electricity service demonstrate other challenges, since the supply usually limits to less than six hours daily, usually generated from diesel plants, which raises questions about the adequacy of the supply. In this sense, this research is developed with the objective of “proposing a scheme for the management and sustainability of the electrification of communities outside the grid in Colombia” -ZNI communities. To do this, a qualitative analysis is made – focused on understanding the problem, characterizing the current conditions of service provision and the development of hypotheses.
