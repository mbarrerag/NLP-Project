# ENHANCED CONTROL CHART FOR THE MONITORING OF SKEWED-NORMAL SAMPLES

Morales & Panza proposed an exponentially weighted moving averages chart with dynamic probability limits for the on-line detection of changes in the mean level of skewnormal samples that are due to changes in the parameter ruling the asymmetry of the basic model. The scheme was called the EWMA-SN chart. Further simulations suggested that the proposed control procedure exhibits the undesirable property of not being ARL-unbiased, when the monitoring is carried out with the help of the two-sided version of the EWMASN. This is, the chart takes longer to detect a small change in the asymmetry parameter than it takes to show a false alarm. The drawback of the proposed EWMA-SN method can be presumably overcome when the algorithm by Guo & Wang is introduced in the design of the chart in order to improve its out-of-control performance
