# Study of the influence of the regulation of the distribution system of the reciprocating piston steam engine belonging to the laboratory of renewable energies and thermal plants of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá headquarters with integrated simulation of the kinematic and thermal models.

Software manufacturing is a process that has taken on great importance in industry for some years now, since the vast majority of information systems have been migrating their operational process to technological mechanisms. However, the accelerated growth of these structures has caused drawbacks throughout their development phase. For this reason, it is essential to find strategies for decomposition of software, particularly in Front-End components, that help mitigate these problems and improve the inherent characteristics of applications. Consequently, the final work aims to apply a decoupling strategy based on the architectural pattern of microservices in order to make an assessment of the cohesion between its resulting components. For this, a general context is presented on architectural decomposition and software components, as well as a definition of concepts that allow understanding the idea, then details some mechanisms that have been used to break down software components, presents the implementation of reference that was built based on one of the industries that most uses software at present, as is the financial area, and finally, applies the decomposition method together with their respective analysis
