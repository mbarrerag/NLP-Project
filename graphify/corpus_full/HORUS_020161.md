# Analysis and design of routes in the public transport system urban group of the city of Quibdó

The public transport system of the city of Quibdó has been showing deficiencies in service coverage over the past 10 years, due to, among other things, a low supply of public transport, a lack of restructuring of public transport routes and an increase in motorcycle taxi rides. As a result, most users of the system have decided to use other transport mode to meet the need to move around the city. regardless of the legality of the service, the high costs, the insecurity and inconvenience faced by passengers. In this context and according to the main objective of this work, the urban collective public transport routes for the city of Quibdó were defined by analyzing the operation and coverage of the existing routes and by studying the demand taking into account the urban expansion, which will allow an improvement in the transport service
