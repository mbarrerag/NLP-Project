# Introduction to the STATISTICAL RISK THEORY

The measurement, management, treatment and supervision of risk has been an essential element in the business and industrial spheres. Many types of tools useful for carrying out risk control have proliferated in recent years. Despite the various statistical-mathematic procedures used in risk processes, many texts address them superficially and in a complex way, some procedures are presented in this text in a clear and simple way; each of the concepts and methods are addressed by examples and several exercises are proposed to ensure that there is a proper appropriation of the topics and thus allow the users of the material to implement them in practice in an easy and smooth manner. The text is written sequentially; that is, the previous contents are important to have a better understanding of the development of the next section, which will allow the reader to manage to implement and incorporate these procedures autonomously in his working life
