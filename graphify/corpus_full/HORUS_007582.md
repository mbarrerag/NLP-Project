# Development of a candidate for certified reference material of toxic elements in Cannabis sativa foliar tissue.

The presence of cannabis products on the market has become increasingly frequent due to their therapeutic properties. The growth of the medical cannabis industry has led to the implementation of new regulations focused on licensees for the cultivation, sowing and sale of these products, and also to the quality of these products, with the primary objective of ensuring consumer safety. One of the most relevant aspects in terms of quality is the content of toxic elements, since it has been demonstrated that the cannabis plant has the capacity to accumulate these elements. In compliance with the current regulations, it is essential to establish reliable and comparable measurements to strengthen the cannabis production chain. To do this, adequate metrological tools, such as reference materials, validated measurement methods, collaborative studies and aptitude tests, must be used. It is important to highlight that there is currently a shortage of these available tools, which highlights the need for further metrological development in this production chain. Therefore, the objective of this research is to produce a reference material to measure toxic elements in the foliar material of the cannabis plant. To achieve this, it is proposed to develop precise methods and with a low reference to characterize such material
