# ANALYSIS OF GENERATION OF SUPERFFICIAL GRIETS BY MONITORY OF INFRARED EMISSIONS IN CARBON AIRS

Fatigue failures are classified as one of the most common faults by which mechanisms are put into repair. In fact, such failures are taken into account from the very design of components, when it is known that they will be subjected to cyclical loads. Hence the importance of maintenance, whether preventive, predictive or even corrective. The amalgamation of a good integral maintenance plan and its execution is the key to preserving the life of a team; that is why the design of fault prediction systems has become very important in recent industry. Therefore, a system of prediction of superficial fatigue failures in soul-demand components is proposed. Recent studies conclude that it is possible to take record of temperatures emissions of metallic materials in the infrared spectrum, without needing to intervene in the material. This is achieved by means of the device called bolometer. By capturing the infrared emissions emitted by a carbon steel, it is possible to relate the temperature to the effort, and therefore to the concentration factor, which once obtained can yield indicators of the material surface condition.
