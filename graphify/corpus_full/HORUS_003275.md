# THE TRANSCENTALITY OF PI

A very important problem for mathematicians since the time of ancient Greece, consisted in answering whether it was possible or not, to enthrone a rational polynomial not null that had the number pi as root. In the year 1882, the German mathematician Carl Louis Ferdinand Lindemann managed to give a proof of the transcendence of the number pi, that is, he demonstrated that the number pi cannot be root of any rational polynomial. In this article we will discuss the tools necessary to understand the proof about the significance of the number pi given by Lindemann
