# THE IMPACT OF NEOLIBERAL MULTICULTURE ON THE PROFESSIONAL TRAYECTORY OF COLOMBIAN BLACK MEDICULTURE CLASSES

Based on a research study on the social advancement of the Afro-Colombian population, this chapter briefly explores the following aspects: the transformations in the work paths of Afro-Colombian middle-class women and men; the work spaces that new multicultural and neoliberal policies have opened or closed to Black populations; the values in relation to work and consumption assumed by these social groups in these new scenarios; and, finally, the gender differences in these trajectories, spaces and values
