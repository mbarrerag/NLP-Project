# Quality assessment and dissolution profile of hydrochlorothiazide tablets available in Salvador-BA, Brazil

hydrochlorothiazide is a diuretic drug indicated for the continued treatment of hypertension. The drug is presented as a drug containing a single active substance or combined in formulations with concentrations of 12.5, 25 and 50 mg. The dose of 25 mg is the most widely used in medical therapy. Therefore, considering the incidence of high blood pressure and the prolonged use of the medicine, it is necessary to determine the quality of hydrochlorothiazide tablets available in public and private pharmacies in Salvador,Bahya. Complaints about the compromised quality of products stored in pharmacies are reported very frequently. Material and Methods: following the drug monograph and, consequently, methods of pharmacopoeia, this study was aimed at carrying out quality controls, physicochemical and microbiological tests of medicinal products containing hydrochlorothiazide, including the commercial name (referencing - R), generic (G) and similar medicine (S), which was disproportionate by the National Health Service (also known as SUS - SUS - Sistónico de Sasayude)
