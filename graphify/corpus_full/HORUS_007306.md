# Electrochemical determination of glyphosate with vitreous carbon electrodes modified with PEDOT/Cu

The glyphosate is the herbicide most used worldwide, but its use generates concerns due to its adverse effects on the environment and human health. This study presents a detection method based on electrochemical techniques, valued for its speed and profitability compared to other analytical techniques. The method is based on the ability of glyphosate to form complexes with copper, detectable by electrochemical techniques. A vitreous carbon electrode modified with poly(3,4-ethylenedioxythiophene) (PEDOT) was used. On this surface copper was reduced electrochemically in a phosphates regulatory solution with pH 7.4 and 1.4 M of chlorides.
