# Strategic partnership to increase the technological maturity level of cellulose nanofibre reinforced hydrogels for agricultural use

The continuous growth of the population reflects an increase in the demand for food and water, which, combined with the global climate emergency, has generated concern about the volume and quality of the food produced. The large production of food, and the inadequate management of the soil and water can cause the exhaustion of the soil, the erosion or the hydraulic scarcity.The water is one of the essential factors to improve the production of crops; however, several areas are considered unproductive due to their aridity and scarcity of precipitation, which limit the capacities of cultivation.The present research proposal seeks to contribute in the mitigation of these problems, through the design of superabsorbent hydrogels, which stand out as a low cost option for the management of the water in agriculture due to their rapid capacity to absorb and retain large quantities of water within their structure without dissolution.More than 95% of the absorbed water in dry conditions is depresorbed, which helps to moisturize the soil.A research group of the National University of Colombia Palmira has been working in the development of these structures and has achieved the development of the labor structure and has been strengthened with nanofibras and plants (the above mentioned). The study aims to contribute to the dissemination of knowledge through participation in international academic and scientific events and the publication of research articles
