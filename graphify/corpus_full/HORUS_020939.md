# Desarraigo: an ethnography of the children Emberá in the streets of Bogotá

The indigenous communities Emberá, originating in Andágueda, Chocó, and Pueblo Rico, Risaralda have been displaced by the armed conflict in Colombia since 2012. Most men have remained protecting the territory, while the women and children have had to migrate to the cities in search of work and governmental or district aid. In this context, on 29 September a group of more than 300 indigenous Emberá carried out a takeover of the Enrique Olaya Herrera National Park in Bogotá, the day when the housing and food subsidies that the District had granted them a year ago were completed. On 5 and 6 May 2022, a negotiation was held between authorities and delegates of the District and the National Government, which resulted in an agreement that allowed the mobilization of the more than 1900 indigenous people present in the park to some comprehensive protection units and transit homes in the peripheral localities of Bogotá.
