# Wigner-Weyl Quantum Entanglement

In this thesis, we describe the dynamics of interlinking a two-particle system interacting under an All-type potential in the representation of Wigner-Weyl of quantum mechanics. To begin, the main elements of the representation of Wigner-Weyl of quantum mechanics are presented, followed by the identification and quantity of the interlinking of the two particles in the whole system in the undulating representation. Subsequently, the main approaches to the classical description of the interlinking dynamics are considered, from where we are inspired to describe the chaotic and regular dynamics of the whole system in the phase space from the quantum Poincaré surfaces using stationary Wigner functions, obtaining comparable results with the classic equivalents. Finally, the analysis and interpretation of the dynamic of measured interlinking using two measures of interlinking in the phase space is presented.
