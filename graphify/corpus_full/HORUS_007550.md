# EMERGENCY VOICES ON INCLUSIVE EDUCATION: A PROPOSAL FOR CONSOLIDATION OF KNOWERS IN HIGHER EDUCATION IN COLOMBIA

This project arises from the process of drafting the policy of Higher Inclusive Education of the National University of Colombia that was in charge of one of the authors of this proposal and that had under its responsibility the mother project 603-03 of national level aimed at investigating and creating a new approach to inclusive education for the National University of Colombia between 2022-2024. The project developed actions such as documentary review, diagnosis and approach of the policy itself. However, it did not contemplate academic type deliverables, that were the result of a wide process of discussions with experts on the topic. In the year 2023 the draft of inclusion policy was registered in HERMES as a proposal for research without funding by the National University of Colombia, and that it was aimed at collecting academic discussions about the university's Higher Inclusive Education in the framework of the challenges present in the university and in the country.For the above, and with the conceptual background acquired on the subject, it is sought through this call, consolidating the process and having concrete tools for managing the diversity in university contexts that it merits the amplification of the discussion about inclusive education and the previous education strategy. participants as invited to the two national chairs to display on the topic, an article that gathers the experience of the diagnostic model for the elaboration of the inclusive education policy of the University identifying barriers and opportunities, the realization of a Colloquium on inclusive education in higher education and finally the delivery to the advisory committee of the cultural and communicative management career of a proposal to create a subject on research for inclusive education, which will be offered at Tumaco Headquarters, once the Cultural and Communication Management Program of the National University of Colombia opens at this Headquarters
