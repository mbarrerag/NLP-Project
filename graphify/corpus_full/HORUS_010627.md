# Rebirth of a tour: Reconfiguration and spatial connection of the Fourth Race, between Belén and Las Cruces, Bogotá

This Final Work of Maestría aims to present a proposal of urban project of the Fourth Career in the Historic Center of the city of Bogotá, that reconfigure and reconnect the neighborhoods Santa Barbara (Iglesia Nuestra Señora de Belén) and Las Cruces (Plaza de Mercado). It seeks to counteract problems as disconnection between neighborhoods by recent road reconfigurations; disconnection between heritage goods; physical spatial deterioration of the preexisting architecture with high state of ruin, apple fronts with unbuilt buildings, urban voids in unconsolidated properties and detriment of the Cultural Interest Goods, identified and declared in the area afferent of the same heritage axis. The construction of the Comuneros avenue (stree 6a) in 2007, was a trigger for the rupture and loss of continuity between neighborhoods, not only in terms of road and pedestrian connections, but also in morphology, in use of the pre-existing public spaces and in community relations.
