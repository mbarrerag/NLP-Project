# Impact of the implementation of Pillar I of Solvency II for Colombian companies in the insurance sector

The regulatory frameworks play a determining role in the insurance market, however, their potential impacts are the subject of debate from different fields and types of research. This study analyzes the possible impacts of the international regulatory framework Solvency II, especially its Pillar I, at the time of applying to the Colombian insurance market as planned. The research is qualitatively focused, employs methods such as analytic-s, content analysis and triangulation of data. The study contributes to the understanding of the insurance market in Colombia; to the depth of the local regulatory framework and the capital requirement based on risks of Solvency II. The results suggest that according to the rigour with which Solvency II is implemented in Colombia, there are degrees of impact, with the full implementation of impacts being a challenge for some companies and an obstacle for others as they would be out of operation by insolvency
