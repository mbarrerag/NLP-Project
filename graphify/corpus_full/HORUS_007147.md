# Development of decision-making tool for water resource management in the Magdalena Medio geological basin

The purpose of this document is to present the development of a decision-making tool containing information relevant to the water resource in the Magdalena Medio geological basin. For this purpose, information was taken about the water demand of different productive sectors of the nation, focusing mainly on the hydrocarbon sector, which is why data were taken about the operational management of the Ecopetrol water, the largest company in the sector in the country. Additionally, the most representative indicators related to water quality in the Magdalena Middle Valley were presented. These indicators were calculated with samples taken at different points in the basin. In addition, to complement the tool, information was taken from two exercises of perspective regarding the water demand of the agriculture sector and the impact generated from different scenarios of climate change on the water balance in a humid forest area. The tool was developed in ArcGIS and was taken as input data of the above mentioned fields.
