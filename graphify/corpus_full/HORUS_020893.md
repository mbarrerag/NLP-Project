# TURISINN-LAB - Open Innovation Laboratory for Sustainable Tourism in the Department of Caldas

TURISINN-LAB is a collaborative work strategy that aims to develop a process of mapping tourism opportunities (linked to potential sustainable social innovation); generate experiential training in the prototyping of social innovations on sustainable tourism; design of hybrid business models of social innovation linked to sustainable tourism; and development of a relational evaluation framework linking social innovation skills with transition processes towards sustainable tourism in the Department of Caldas
