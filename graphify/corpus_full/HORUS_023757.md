# THE THING AGENCY: A SEMIOS OF THE TRANSPORT NETWORKS IN BOGOTA

The urban transport networks constitute a social space in which the agents are related to multiple devices, signs, speed reducers, bridges, other vehicles, etc. As Latour warns, the study of this social environment needs to be materialized, in this case, considering the infrastructure, which allows, or does not the fluid use of the system, as a fundamental part of the analysis of the phenomenon of transport. With a focus based on the semiotic agent, I explore to what extent the infrastructure of the transport functions as a regulator of human actions, with an agency equivalent to that of the actors. In my proposal, the notion of agency is not assumed, by default, as an intrinsic characteristic of the objects widely assumed position, but is based on the concept of dislocation (Latour, 1996), which introduces a delegation of tasks human actions to the artifacts.
