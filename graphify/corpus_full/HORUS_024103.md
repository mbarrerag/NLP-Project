# Estimating specified returns with forecast combinations

This thesis proposes applying the forecasts generated by the aggregation of experts as a novel predictor of expected yields to 2 different portfolio strategies: 1) Mean-Variance as proposed (Markowitz, 1952) and 2) contraction of the covariance matrix S as in (Ledoit, 2004). Experts were built generating forecasts with Quantile Regression by Generalized Random Forests and automated versions of Exponential Smoothing and ARIMA. This study evaluated the accuracy of forecasts of two expert aggregation algorithms (1) ML-Prod and (2) ML-Poly by means of a simulation study, before applying the superior method to a diversified portfolio. After evaluating the accuracy of the forecasts, the higher ML-Poly algorithm was chosen to forecast the expected yields and showed promising results outside the sample for the portfolios considered, returning higher values for the selected performance parameters and marginal inferior results in terms of rotation ratio. Through the simulation study, the results of the portfolios were also validated
