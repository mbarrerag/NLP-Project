# PHOTOOXIDATION OF ESSENTIAL OILS CATEMINATED BY A METALORGANIC STRUCTURE (MOF) POST-FUNCTIONALIZED WITH DIOXOMOLIBDENO (VI) ACTIVE CENTRES

The oxyfunctionalisation of essential oils for obtaining epoxides is currently one of the important pathways for the valuation of renewable natural materials justified in the possibility of obtaining opening reactions of the ring to obtain compounds of great interest for the pharmaceutical industry, among others. For the obtaining of these oxygenated products it is necessary the development of environmentally friendly catalytic systems. The design of solid catalysts formed by active heterogenized metals photoactive is a route of great interest for the oxidation processes. The transition metals with two units of metal-ox (M=O) have reported an important activity for the transfer of oxygen atoms (TAO) being molybdenum one of the most studied structures. In this research was evaluated the catalyst MoO2Br2@COMOC-4 in the photoeoxidation of the main structure of the essential oil Illium verum and Pimpinella anisum.
