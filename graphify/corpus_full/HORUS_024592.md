# The sexuality of old women: narratives and discourses from experience

The sexuality of old women is an invisible topic, even by the same women. This thesis aimed to analyze discourses on sexuality in “old women” from the experience of the women themselves. For this purpose, narratives and discourses transversalized by gender, body, socialization and violences were examined. The research employed a qualitative-interpretative approach from a processual-discursive perspective, since it was based on the analysis of the discourses of the participants; therefore, it allowed reflection on the meanings that women give to their own experiences and ways of living sexuality. The participants were ten women resident in a traditional neighborhood of Bogotá, whose ages ranged from 60 to 79 years. Participation was completely voluntary. The results obtained showed that gender, body, interpersonal relations and violence are categories organizing discourses and experiences on sexuality.
