# IMPLEMENTATION OF A PEDAGOLOGICAL STRATEGY IN SCHOLARIZED CHILDREN OF THE ZENU INDIGENOUS RESGUARD WITH WATCHES ON THE CONTROL AND ELIMINATION OF THE TENIASIS/ CISTICERCOSIS COMPLETE: PILLOT TEST

The social appropriation of knowledge is an educational tool that is part of the strategies included in ¿A single health? and is considered useful in the integral approach with a view to the control and elimination of the Teniasis / Cysticercosis Complex. Previous studies inform the presence of swine cysticercosis, deficient personal hygiene measures and lack of adequate basic sanitation conditions in the Zenú indigenous reserve, Córdoba? Sucre, Colombia. This work had as objective to prototyp a pedagogical strategy using different didactic tools in a workshop on the Teniasis / Cysticercosis Complex aimed at a group of primary and high school students from an educational institution belonging to the Zenú Indigenous Safeguard. The workshop was carried out in a day with two groups of students (28 of grades 3 to 5 of primary and 56 of grades 6 to 11 of secondary) accompanied by three teachers from the academic area of biology.
