# ESTIMATION OF EDUCATIONAL ESTABLISHMENTS PERFORMANCE IN SABER 5-TESTS IN COLOMBIA.

Both identification of factors associated with the performance of students in standarized tests and the generation of high quality and low cost statistics are a necessity for decision makers today, in education these data are necessary to decide about the creation of policies, the continuity of programs and the allocation of resources from year to year. An application of the proposed methodology is considered to estimate the average performance of educational establishments in Colombia at departmental level for the Saber tests applied by ICFES on 2015. This methodology integrates the construction of a Fay-Herriot model based in small area estimation with multiple estimation of the performance of each student via plausible values and auxiliary information
