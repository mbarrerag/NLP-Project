# Maritime signs during navigation: declaration of the marine accident of breakdown in relation to the exclusive responsibility of the shipowner.

This qualitative investigation is framed in the analytical legal field, which seeks to establish elements specific to the declaration of a marine accident of breakdown by decision of the captain of the ship or by the failure of the shipowner's duties. . The study begins from the cases investigated and failed in Colombia both by the maritime jurisdiction, and by the ordinary jurisdiction during the period 2010 – 2020. For this purpose, it focused on the realization of an analysis of the jurisprudential reality, legal and doctrinal, national and international; what allowed to understand the global perspectives and propose new ways to address the cases of marine casualties of breakdown. As the main result, we find that, the establishment of responsible in relation to the declaration of a maritime act of breakdown, depends on whether or not the compliance with the obligations in charge of the ship-carrier, including those that do not relate directly to the operation of transport; and the determination of the compliance of the legal requirements to declare the bulk or common breakdown, under a reasonable decision.
