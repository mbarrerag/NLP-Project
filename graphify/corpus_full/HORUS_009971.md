# Influence of urban-rural expansion on the habitability of the municipality of San Agustín - Huila: Analysis of ordination from habitat

This work determined the influences of rural urban expansion on the habitability of the municipality of San Agustín - Huila; based theoretically on the systemic approach, the territorial approach and the approach of complexity, which puts into question the traditional theories of progress and development, and gives importance to the place, analyzing the interdependence and the interrelation of the variables of the territory; where it is established that the urban and the rural, as well as the global and the local, do not imply a contradiction, but a confluence, a complex synthesis around the concept of place. In parallel, the concept of habitat and habitability is posed from a perspective that includes the problems of complexity as a collective and not necessarily harmonious construction. From there, an observation of the dynamics as presented within the territory was made, studying the phenomenon of rural urban expansion, which, together with the technique of analysis of chorematic, correlated the conditions of habitability from the phenomenon studied, to finally construct a series of recommendations of territorial planning of the municipality of San Agustín – Huila, based on a systemic vision conceived in the territory, from the point of view of complexity in orientation to a better habitability.
