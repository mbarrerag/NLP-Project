# Method for predicting the intention of buying online users using machine learning techniques

The challenge of predicting the intention to buy online users is a critical aspect in the dynamic world of e-commerce. In this context, understanding and anticipating consumer behavior has become a priority for companies with digital presence. This research addresses this challenge by developing a predictive method for the intention to purchase digital users, using machine learning techniques. Through a representative data set, which includes detailed information about users' activities on an e-commerce site, machine learning techniques are adjusted, using a cluster approach of Random Forests and XGBoost. The results obtained demonstrate that the proposed method reaches a general accuracy of 89.69%, demonstrating its ability to correctly identify when users intend to purchase online and when not. The research provides the e-commerce field with a predictive approach that focuses on accuracy and generalisation, adapting to variations in the purchasing behaviour of digital users. This generalisation approach implies that the model is not restricted to a specific data set or to particular market conditions, but can adjust and maintain its accuracy in the face of the dynamic changes presented by the electronic commerce.
