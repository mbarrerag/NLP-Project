# YOU HAVE, THE LOST CITY, DIFFERENT AND LEVANTATION AS YOU GRAFFICLY ASSIST TO YOUR STUDIES.

"The graphic expression is a primary characteristic of architectural activity and its most common mode of representation. Architecture and urban planning have in graphic representation their basic tool not only to become reality, but also for their reflection and research. Practically daily, architects, urban planners and designers make use of any graphic tool to think, face problems and propose solutions, in different phases and at different scales. If to these facts we add the great quantity of methods and techniques that can be used, it will be understood that the Graphic Architectural Expression (EGA) is a field of knowledge rather wide." In the case of the article: "The Teyuna study aims to present a state of graphic art, a classification and a relationship between them, that allows to identify how the contributions and technologies used in each case, product of its evolution, are reconstructing in an increasingly detailed and less invasive way the cultures that inhabited it. In this sense the representation of the place, the identification of types of terraces, roads, and constructions as habitat and its relation to the natural environment, were discovered a place of important archaeological heritage as a precursor of urbanism and a constructive legacy of these civilizations."
