# VEGETATION AND FLOWERING PLANTS ON THE GORGONA ISLAND (GUAPÍ, CAUCA), CHOCÓ BIOGEOGRAPHICAL OF COLOMBIA

The vegetation of Gorgona Island (Colombian biogeographical Chocó) was characterized based on the floristic composition, aspects of the structure and architecture of the plant formation as well. In sampling areas (plots) of a 500 m2, all the individuals were count-ed, their coverage (%) were estimated according to the differentiated strata and the leaf biotypology fearures were analized. The alliance Myrcio splendentis-Symphonion globuliferae with the associations Dendropanaco arborei-Acalyphetum diversifoliae, Mico-nio sessilifoliae-Anaxagoretum phaeocarpae, Henrietteo fissantherae-Myrcietum splendentis and Miconio rubiginosae-Eschweileretum reversae are described. The Henrietteo fissantherae-Myrcietum splendentis association was the most vigorous vege-tation and in the best state of conservation (according to the differentiation of strata and the area covered by each one). This is located in the middle part of the island between 80 and 170 m of altitude; this community also presented a greater number of species in the arboreal (14), small trees (17) and shrub (22) strata. In the lower and middle parts of the island between 45 and 300 m the sparse forests of Dendropanaco arborei-Acalyphetum diversifoliae and those of the Henrietteo fissantherae-Myrcietum splendentis and Miconio orariae-Anaxagoretum phaeocarpae associations are established. The forests of the Miconio rubiginosae-Eschweilereetum reversae are ubicated in the upper part (200-300 m). A leaf level predominates the unprotected leaves of simple composition, entire margin, and acute base; the most frequent apex was the acuminate. The families with the highest number of species were: Rubiaceae, Fabaceae, Melastomataceae, Piperaceae, Araceae, Cyperaceae and Asteraceae. Relatively low richness species values were found in Orchidaceae, Annonaceae, Malvaceae, Bromeliaceae and Lamiaceae. The genera with the largest number of species on the island are Piper, Miconia, Psychotria, Clusia and Peperomia
