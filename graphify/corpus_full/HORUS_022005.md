# Implementation of a learning teaching unit for the development of scientific competence to investigate students in the fifth grade.

This paper aims to show project-based methodologies to consolidate the competence to investigate students of primary basic education; specifically, fifth grade. Additionally, it is also expected to illustrate how, indirectly, it is possible, through these same projects, to consolidate and strengthen theoretical knowledge in Natural Sciences seen in class or previous courses. The research was applied in the distrital educational institution OAS College, in the locality of Kennedy, Bogotá. The projects were carried out as part of the Natural Sciences course for fifth grade. The observation and application instruments were seven: a diagnostic test applied before and after performing the intervention and six activities. The diagnostic test aimed to measure the level of students in the competition to investigate. Its results were the presence of three basic skills that underpin the competence to investigate: to identify conjectures, understand and describe processes, and to abstract data to extract conclusions. The six activities were carried out in the classroom during the third and fourth period of the year 2021.
