# DIAGNOSTIC TRAINING NEEDS IN RESEARCH, BIOETICS AND SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY IN ENVIRONMENTAL SCIENCES

This chapter describes the results obtained in the analysis of the reports made regarding the training needs in three main areas: research ethics, bioethics and scientific integrity in the field of environmental sciences. The objective was to determine the training needs in ethics and bioethics of research and scientific integrity among academic researchers (type group 1), managers and non-academic researchers (type group 2) and research seed groups (type group 3), associated with this area. As results, in the three groups consulted, the need for training in ethical and regulatory guides associated with good research practices was identified, and three emerging categories were identified: prevention of environmental impact, knowledge and management of information. Likewise, a coincidence was observed with respect to the trend of data between manual analysis and that carried out through software, finding two categories in common: environmental awareness and author rights.
