# VALIDATION TO THE COLOMBIAN SPANISH OF A SCALE TO DETECT THE POSCIOUS SYNDROME

The intensive post-care syndrome (PCI) is a phenomenon of great impact and social relevance in critical patients (1); its detection is complex due to the great variety of affected components (cognitive, functional and psychological), in addition to the use of various scales of measurement and the absence of a specific diagnostic test (2.3). Currently, the Health Aging Brain-Care Monitor scale (HABC-M) represents a clinical tool with high potential to detect the PCI in survivors to critical diseases, allowing patients to indicate the frequency of symptoms experienced during their stay in the Intensive Care Unit (UCI) (4). The objective of this study was to perform a transcultural adaptation of the HABC-M scale by means of the retrotraduction technique and to evaluate the main psychometric properties of the Colombian version (5, 6). Observational study, psychometric type of validity (6). The sample was integrated by survivors to a critical disease of two health institutions in Colombia during the period 2021-2022. The validity of the content was evaluated by calculating the Fleg value of content (RVC) and the index of facial validity (CIV) The impact of this study on the surviving population of the ICU, as well as nursing discipline, is the benefit of having a clinical tool adapted and validated to Spanish that allows the rapid and simple detection of the SPCI (4). The application of this scale in clinical or outpatient settings seeks to guarantee a comprehensive approach that allows establishing care routes, focused on the restoration of cognitive, functional and psychological health; besides transcending from multidisciplinary and collaborative work in understanding the ICP in the Colombian context and in general Spanish speaking. In conclusion, the Spanish version of the habc-M scale is a tool with adequate psychometric properties, valid and reliable for the detection of the intensive post-care syndrome
