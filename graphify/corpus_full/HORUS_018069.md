# CRITICAL PANDEO OF BELLY OLD VIGS WITH RISKS DESIGNED WITH AASHTO UNDER CONCENTRATED LOADS

At present there are different options to increase the ultimate resistance of slender beams subject to concentrated loads. Some of these alternatives include the use of narrowly spaced cross-rigidizers and the use of longitudinal rigidizers. However, recent studies have shown that resistance also improves by using delta-type rigidizers, which consist of two welded sheets between beam-soul and latched fin. This work focuses on the study of the behaviour of elastic buckling of slender beams designed with AASHTO standards with delta-type rigidizers subjected to concentrated load. The study is carried out through the analysis of autovalues using the finite element method. A parametric analysis is carried out to determine the influence of different geometric variables on the beam's buckling coefficient. Finally, from the results obtained, some design recommendations are suggested
