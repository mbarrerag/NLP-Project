# SENSIBILIZATION ON PROTECTION

The high number of animals abandoned in the streets of the big Colombian cities can be explained by different cultural, economic and social causes, although it also reflects the ineffectiveness of the institutionality willing to address this problem. This research seeks to understand and contrast the notions that society and the right has to face animals and their relationship with human beings, which is crossed by paradigms of anthropocentric and specist background. In this regard, a doctrinal, legal and jurisprudential reconstruction will be carried out on the care and rights of animals, understanding consecutively the scopes and limitations that cause the problem of their abandonment in an urban area like Bogotá. Likewise, the project seeks to identify those causes that influence the behaviors that undermine the rights of animals, but also to recognize the achievements and challenges of the public policy established for their protection. However, given that the review from the theory and the dogmatics is not enough; it seeks to influence such behaviors from environmental justice, community and social action, as well as popular environmentalism, seeking a collective construction of knowledge and environmental practices
