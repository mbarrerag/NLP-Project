# Technological analysis of a technological superstructure for the monoincineration of biosolids in Bogotá.

In this work, the technical analysis of a technological superstructure for the monoincineration of biosolids in fluidized bed reactors is carried out. To meet this objective, the characteristics of biosolids in Bogotá are specified, and a review of the state of technological art is carried out. From this review, a database with 100 plants and a technological superstructure consisting of 29 biosolid incineration systems, composed of combinations of 29 processing technologies (subsystems). Subsequently, a methodology is established for the development of a technoeconomic analysis, which is validated by estimating the costs of a base plant that works in Moerdijk, Netherlands. As a result, the actual cost of the plant is between -15% to 30% of the estimated value by developing the respective technoeconomic analysis. Next, a comparative analysis is carried out between systems taking into account: profitability, energy recovery and pollutant emissions. As a result, it is recommended that the actual cost of the plant is between -15% and 30% of the estimated value by means of the respective technoeconomic analysis.
