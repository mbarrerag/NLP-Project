# EXPERIENCE IN VIRTUAL CONTINUOUS EDUCATION TO INDIVIOUS PROFESSIONALS IN CONTEXT OF PANDEMIA BY COVID-19

The global report on nursing 2020 proposes that governments, with the support of other interested actors, should catalyze and lead efforts to develop leadership capacity, management and nursing management, with relevant educational, health, employment, gender, and investment in the training and work of nursing professionals in Latin America 1, particularly in neurological diseases, such as Multiple Sclerosis (MS), where continuous education is necessary and relevant through network platforms, which entail important changes and educational transformations. Problem: The pandemic by COVID-19 has exposed the vulnerability of health systems, deficit of nursing professionals, lack of investment to strengthen the professional and specialized work 2, difficulties and fears related to the risk of exposure to the virus, overload of work and greater demand for care of patients and family members 3. In addition, the transition of continuous education to virtual mode requires introduction of information and communication technologies that favor learning, consolidating collaborative and innovative methodologies that facilitate transmission and assimilation of knowledge, independently, collectively and flexibly 2. intervention implemented: the virtual team of continuous education in neurological diseases, voluntary care, and voluntary care. • Communication and empathy for individual and family situations of professionals. Teamwork: interprofessional follow-up: participants. Motivation to specialized training in MS. Elimination of barriers: economic, transport and face-to-face. Interrelation: public education with private entities. 4. Indicators: Adherence 57%, fulfilment of objectives 91%, methodological relevance 85%, teacher contact 88%, satisfaction of participants 100%, interest participation in another program 100%, 10 inspiring projects and compliance with institutional commitments 100%. Limitations, difficulties and/or successes: Difficulties that influenced adherence: time available, work overload, staff and family, health problems, technological resources and public services, selection of heterogeneous participants and lack of institutional support. Main achievements: achievement of objectives, methodological relevance, teacher contact, satisfaction and 10 feasible projects in areas of work performance
