# REVISION OF THE CARGO FACTOR FOR COMMERCIAL VEHICLES C2 AND C3 IN BOGOTÁ, D.C.

Bogota is a city whose road mesh has some degree of deterioration of more than 50% of the total of the roads in its localities, constituted mainly in flexible pavements. This is due to different factors such as the real conditions of the design traffic, the aging in the rolling materials, the effect of the climate and the lack of maintenance, among others. One of the main elements involved in the proper dimensioning of a pavement structure of the local road mesh takes into account the load factor that defines the design transit. This can be found by different methodologies that may not be representative of the conditions of the city. This work presents a comparative analysis of the load factor found by different methods and the best approximation based on statistical analysis of the results of the weighing of trucks C2 and C3 carried out by the scales of the Road Maintenance Unit (UMV) in the last lustro, considering its distribution in different routes and localities of the city.
