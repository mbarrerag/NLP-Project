# Study of the insecticide action of chemical constituents present in essential oils and its effect on detoxifying enzymes and motor function for Sitophilus zeamais.

Sitophilus zeamais is a cosmopolitan pest that causes losses of 15 to 50% in stored grains, essential products for food safety.The most used method for its control are synthetic insecticides, which have disadvantages such as high cost, high toxicity and the generation of resistance. Therefore, it is necessary to develop plant agents that allow the efficient and safe control of this pest.In this aspect, plants are a promising source of bioactive secondary metabolites, with a wide structural diversity, which can be used as bioinsecticides. One way to obtain this type of substances is by means of the extraction of essential oils (AEs), which are characterized by lipophilic mixtures of volatile compounds, which allow them to affect in different ways the metabolic, physiological functions and the behavior of insects.
