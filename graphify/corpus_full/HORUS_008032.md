# Alouatta Palliata and Alouatta Seniculus: a review of the stral cycle

The Colombian red howler monkey (A. seniculus) and mantle howler monkey (A. palliata) are representatives of the genus Alouatta in Colombia and, according to analysis by the International Union for the Conservation of Nature (IUCN), their populations tend to decrease by multiple factors. This suggests the need to guide actions for its conservation, for this purpose it requires knowledge of their reproductive dynamics such as the identification and characterization of the estral cycle. Documents were examined under the criteria Alouatta seniculus, Alouatta paliatta, Alouatta, stral cycle and reproduction in Scielo, Google Scholar, PubMed, Web of Science, Wiley, Science Direct and SpringerLink. Fifteen articles, four books, four master's thesis and a monography were found; in these studies aspects of sexual behavior, changes in genitals and hormone measurement were addressed.
