# Mobilizing curatorial biological data from UNAB for global free use

The management of biological information contained in digital repositories allows to advance research and studies on diversity not only from Colombia but from all over the world.The use of these digital tools comes from a program of SiB Colombia for the collections of Colombia and the National University is an academic partner that encourages the publication process in each of its dependencies dedicated to research; one of these dependencies is the Entomological Museum UNAB, which contains one of the most active collections in terms of systematization activities as well as at the level of student training in topics precisely of management of biological databases in conjunction with curatorial processes.The group of Investigation SIA has qualified experience in the elaboration and mobilization of biological data and records, through the participation in two research projects achieved in Mincienciasos, one related to the registration and dissemination of data of three collections of agricultural importance of Colombia and another with the registry of insects cucarones of the Amazonia and the altiplano cundiboyacense.
