# The place of snow. Contemplation, waiting and silence in Abbas Kiarostami's visual poetics

This journey explores the powers and poetic materialities of the cinematographic, poetic and photographic work of Iranian artist Abbas Kiarostami, with the aim of showing how the singularity of his visual poetics establishes universal dialogues around three fundamental axes: contemplation as a way of seeing the world, waiting as a way of inhabiting the time of images, and silence as a profound and essential response to the vicissitudes of time. Through the readings of poets, mystics, writers, painters and filmmakers, an open dialogue is formed between these dimensions and contemporary aesthetic thought. Este recorrido explored the potencias y materialidades poeticas de la obra fílmica, poética y fotográfica del artista iraní Abbas Kiarostami.
