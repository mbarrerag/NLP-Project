# SILLA AND PHOTOGRAPHS: HISTORIES FOR DESIGN, OF AND FROM THE SILLAS IN COLOMBIAN PHOTOGRAPHS OF THE XIXth AND XXTH SIGNATURES

In a country with lack of museums of decorative arts and design, the image becomes a source for the construction of knowledge around the history of the material culture, of furniture, of the chair and of photography. For example, through this research and the review of about 6000 images of the period in study, we have constituted a visual corpus of approximately 800 images with chairs, within which we have counted, to the moment, at least 125 different types of chairs. Some of them like the friar chairs, possible rococo chairs, crownelian chairs (or possible Louis XIV and Louis XV), chairs Thonet and tubular chairs. Within this visual corpus we observe people of different races, ages, gender and socio economic conditions around the chair.
