# MODIFICATION OF PEDOT/CU VINE CARBON ELECTROCHEMICAL DETERMINATION FOR GLYPHOSATE

The glyphosate is a broad spectrum herbicide (1) and easy to use, reasons why it is the most used herbicide in the world (1-4), which has increased the exposure of people to it. Considering that it has been observed that it causes damage to liver and kidneys at chronic level (2.3), it is necessary to be able to determine the presence of the herbicide in a quick and simple way. The method selected for making the detection is the Voltamperometry of Differential Pulse (VPD) (5-7) in a solution of 0.1 M of PBS at pH 7.4, with electrode working of vitreous carbon modified in two steps starting with an electropolymerization galvanostatic of 3,4-ethyleneoxythiophene (EDOT) and a subsequent chronopotenciometry to deposit copper on the polymer PEDOT that promotes the nucleation of the particles of copper without passivating the electrode (8-11). This modification is made because the glyphosate is not electroactive (7,12), so to be detected by electrochemical methods it takes advantage of the polyworking with copper (II) (13)
