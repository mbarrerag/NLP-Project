# IDENTIFICATION OF NEW DEFINANCE INDUCTORS FOR THE CONTROL OF THE NEGRAD SIGATOKA

One of the main plant protection problems facing banana cultivation is the Sigatoka Negra disease, generated by the fungus Pseudocercospora fijiensis (Agrios, 2004). Control of this fungus is mainly chemical and generates more than 25-30 % of the cost of production, with a tendency to increase due to the rapid resistance that it acquires to the main molecules that exist today.
