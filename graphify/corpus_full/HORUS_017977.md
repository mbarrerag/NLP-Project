# LUXURY RISK FACTORS PRESENTED IN NEONETS OF AN INfanTIL HOSPITAL.

Introduction The Dysplasia of the Cadera in Development (DCD) has important repercussions on health if it is not diagnosed and treated in a timely manner. The objective of this research is to describe the risk and prevalence factors for DCD in births between 01/01/2016 and 31/12/2019 in a Children's Hospital in Bogotá, Colombia. Materials and methods A descriptive cross-sectional study. The luxation of the developing cadera (LCD) was clinically evaluated through the signs of Ortolani, Barlow and Galeazzi exploring the possible association with family history in first and second degree of consanguinity. The results of contingency tables 2x2 and application of the X2 test or Fisher's exact test, considering a statistical significance of (p<0.05). The information was taken from the database of the screening program for LCD. The descriptive analysis was performed with Stata15 and SPSS20.
