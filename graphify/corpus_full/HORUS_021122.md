# Seed Right and Land

Historically, the importance of having an updated cadastre in the countries to have an understanding of their territory, the use and other functions such as the establishment of taxes on the land has been recognized. However, despite this distinction, in countries like Colombia this task does not appear to have been fulfilled in full, generating mainly problems in the rural area, as established in a study carried out by the World Bank, where it was ensured that by 2019 60% of the rural lands would not be formalized in Colombia (Rural Week, 2019). Given the above and as a solution to a problem already known, the government through the National Development Plan covered by Law 1955 of 2019 and in CONPES 3958, through the proposal of the multiproposal cadastre, established the cadastral management as a public service that should be subject to new regulations and updates that would seek to increase the cadastral update, which would exceed 58% of the national territory before the implementation of the law according to the general director of the IGAC. Now, the recognition of the problem and the issuing of laws to reduce it at first sight seems to be sufficient, but its factual application must be analyzed before the life. analysis of the above is considered pertinent to study the impacts generated in a peasant population. In the competent part of this research, it is the one located in the district of Santa Elena in the District of Medellín. In this way, this research study seeks to determine the impacts of the proposal of the multipurpose cadastre on the quality of life of the peasantry of the corregimiento of Santa Elena. For these purposes, an analysis will be carried out taking as reference the objectives proposed in the legal productions, the mechanics proper to the actual implementation and the perception of the individuals who have been tried to favor them from the beginning
