# “La Suite Colombiana No.1 para guitara de Gentil Montaña (1942-2011): an analysis of its editions and contextualization of the work of a Colombian composer”

The guitar is probably the instrument of the most popular western music in Latin America. In the field of classical guitar, its development and evolution has generated a technique, a repertoire and a field of action that has produced great performers and composers comparable to others around the world. Julio Gentil Albarracín Montaña (1942-2011) is a reference for classical guitar in Colombia and the continent, as an interpreter and composer. The Colombian Suite No. 1, was the first of five that Montaña wrote for guitar and shows an evolutionary path evidenced in several editions. This work proposes contextualizing the work and trajectory of Montaña in Colombia of the 1970s, the time of the composition of this work and placing it in the context of the guitar and the composition for this instrument in Latin America exemplified in the show of Heitor Villa-Lobos, Manuel María Ponce and Antonio Lauro, authors of guitar suites, which adapted this incomposition to the Latin American musical genres. Finally, a comparison is made of the printed editions of this work, also taking into account the recording made by Montaña Play Mountain CD. it in the context of the guitar and the composition for this instrument in Latin America exemplified in the work of Heitor Villa-Lobos, Manuel María Ponce and Antonio Lauro, authors of suites for guitar, who adapted this European compositional scheme to Latin American musical genes. Ultimately, a comparison is made of the printed editions of this work, also considering the recording made by Montaña on the Mountain Plays Montaña CD. In this analysis, the changes made by the composer, addition of sections, bars and renaming of the movements of Said work are pointed out out
