# Qualitative study of interactions between victims of armed conflict and care professionals, for psychosocial rehabilitation under the Papsiv Programme.

A qualitative study of the interactions between victims of armed conflict and care professionals, for psychosocial rehabilitation within the framework of the Papsivi Program Political violence in Colombia has been long and degraded, generating effects in various areas of the personal and collective life of the citizens. The social dynamics contributed to the creation of Law 1448 of 2011, which established the public policy of psychosocial care and integral health for the victims of the armed conflict: in response to it, the Ministry of Health and Social Protection implements the Program of Psychosocial Care and Integral Health to Victims of the Armed Conflict - Papsivi. The program began in 2013 and, despite its short existence, it is possible to identify research that account for the achievements, difficulties and senses of care, but have focused on only one of the actors of the process: the victims or interdisciplinary teams. From the perspective of social psychology and understanding violence as a human interaction, this study explores the way that the process of psychosocial care occurs as a measure of reparation, from the voices of the professionals and the victims.
