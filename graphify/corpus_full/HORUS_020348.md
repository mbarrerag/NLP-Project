# Field massage for weight gain in extreme prematures. Feasibility and acceptability study

One of the main problems of extreme premature is the limitation in its weight gain due to factors typical of its immaturity; the ignorance about the type, time and amount of stimulation that it should receive, rescues the need to investigate new interventions from nursing care. To determine the viability and acceptability of Field massage therapy, in extreme prematures in a Neonat Unit of an IV level institution, and the effects in relation to weight gain. It is a quantitative study, quasi experimental of before and after, carried out in 15 extremes, where the pre- and post-intervention weight was compared, and an analysis for the estimation of the effect; the feasibility of the study was determined by means of selection, recruitment and follow-up rates and the acceptability of the intervention in terms of parental satisfaction.
