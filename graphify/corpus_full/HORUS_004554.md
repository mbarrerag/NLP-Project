# ALTERNATIVE Optimalization OF A POLLUTED REGION

Introduction- In this work, we consider a problem of industrial ecology under the semi-infinite optimization approach. Objective- The objective is to resolve the conflict between polluting emissions and environmental standards for the areas of a given region. Methodology- A version of the SIP2 algorithm is proposed that, simultaneously with the decrease in pollution, also allows an increase in emissions from sources, in such a way that it allows a certain freedom in the management of pollution and the elements that cause it in the industry, but ensures compliance with environmental normivity. Results- The results of two algorithms show different roles of sources in obtaining total pollution and as a consequence the need for changes in their emissions. The proposed algorithm offers more cost-effective solutions in infrastructure design assessments for areas with opposing interests, such as maintenance or increase of production (directly measured by the generation of emissions due to the productive system), while ensuring compliance with restrictive environmental standards
