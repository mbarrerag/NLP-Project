# THE FRONTERIZATION OF COLOMBIA IN CARIBBEAN. THE INCORPORATION OF THE ARCHIPELAGO OF SAN ANDRES AND PROVIDENCE. 1886-1936

This paper analyses the process of incorporating the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina into the effort to build a homogeneous Colombian nation from 1886, when the centralist state model, inaugurated with the political constitution of that year, was created, which left behind the model of federal State promoted by the liberals in the constitution of 1863. Starting from this new model of State directed in the context of a political project known as the Regeneration, its ideologues focused on three aspects to strengthen the unity of the nation, the first was the construction of the centralized State, which will guide the political direction of the country; the second was aimed at consolidating culturally and symbolically the references of a shared identity through an exercise shared between the Catholic Church and the educational apparatus. And the third sought a greater presence of the State in the peripheral territories of the country through the organization of new territorialities
