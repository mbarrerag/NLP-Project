# Analysis of the mechanical performance of industrial mineral in community mining areas of the municipality of Valledupar.

In this research work, the analysis of the mechanical performance of industrial mineral in mining community areas of the municipality of Valledupar was carried out by means of the physicochemical, thermal and mechanical characterization of the materials present on the active exploitation fronts of the area under study. The physicochemical, thermal and mechanical characterizations were performed using the following techniques: Distribution of particle size from mechanical sifting, limits of consistency and plasticity from the atterberg limits, elemental chemical analysis using X-ray fluorescence (FRX), mineralological analysis by X-ray diffraction (DRX), thermal analysis using thermogravimetric techniques (TGA) and thermodifferenceal (DSC) and mechanical analysis performed using hydraulic press to measure resistance to simple compression of the samples.
