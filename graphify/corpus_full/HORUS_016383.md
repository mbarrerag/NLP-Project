# COMPACTED UNCALCINED WASTE FROM GRAVEL AND SAND WASH MUDS OF AN AGGREGATES PLANT AS WASTES VALORIZATION STRATEGY

The management of the fine quarry waste has been commonly the final disposal in mining tailings and as fill material for landscape recovery in areas that have been exploited. However, different uses of these residues have been studied, highlighting the constructions of land due to the generation of a new economic benefit and reducing the environmental impact. In addition, compared with the constructions of cement, the constructions of land are environmentally friendly and represent an energy saving, due to their low emission of CO2 due to the low or zero content of cement in their manufacture compared to the good physical and mechanical properties obtained. Thus, in this work the use of the sludges of waste from an aggregate plant (resulting from the process of crushing and washing) as a strategy of circular economy. The raw materials were characterized chemical, mineralogical and physically and were employed in the production of the bulky products of non-calcinated material was evaluated, which were hydraulically compacted in mixtures of waste sludges, commercial grey cement and fine sand washed.
