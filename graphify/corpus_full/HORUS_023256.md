# A didactic sequence for teaching the concept of flat surface area in grade eight.

This work focused on the design and implementation of a didactic sequence for the conceptualization of the area as a magnitude in students of the eighth grade; which was based on a review of the literature, which allowed to prioritize units of analysis that guided the design of the tasks; for example, the conservation of the area, the iteration of square and triangular units, the comparison of the quantity of area, decomposition and reconfiguration of figures, conventional and non-conventional units of measurement, and estimation. In addition, they were incorporated into the variable tasks such as the use of grids of grids and points, non-conventional patterns of measurement and fair distribution. The methodological design of this work is characterized by the re-establishment of elements of the design research and case study. The implementation was carried out in a public educational institution of the city of Palmira (Valle del Cauca); for this purpose a strategy of remote work was designed with the group of students participating in the study.
