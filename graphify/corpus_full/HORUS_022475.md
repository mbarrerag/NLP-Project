# HEMATOPOYESS CLONAL OF INDETERMINATED PONTIAL (HCPI): MORE THAN A MODEL OF CANCERIZATION CAMPO

Clonal Hematopoiesis of Indeterminate Potential (HCPI), better known as CHIP, is defined as the clonal expansion of Hematopoietic Stem Cells (CMHs) that harbor one or more somatic mutations (in most cases a single mutation) without an underlying haematological cancer or definitive morphological evidence of dysplasia, with an allelic frequency greater than 2%. Individuals with HPPI progress to malignity at a rate of about 0.5% to 1% per year, thus becoming a cancerization field model. However, its implications go further because of the association with chronic inflammatory diseases, such as atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, diabetes and autoimmune diseases. In addition, it is considered a predictive factor in patients with hematol and non-haematological cancer receiving chemotherapy and radiation therapy
