# Adaptations of the Colombian Gentil Montaña suite for the orchestra of guitars “Orquestarte”

The Fundació Marcato has consolidated itself as a pioneering institution in favour of education in musical art and human development in Colombia and Central America, promoting, encouraging and initiating a guitar orchestra project entitled: “Orquestarte”, a project that promotes the formation of young Colombian guitarists. Within the guitar orchestra there is a wide variety of original practical-pedagogical repertoire. Likewise, it is indispensable to continue with the construction of an original repertoire that takes as its foundation our Colombian musical wealth and continues to strengthen the technical-interpretative capacities of young guitar performers. This research-educational action work proposes to be a contribution to the repertoire of the project “Orquestarte”, through the adaptation of three Colombian instrumental pieces: Far Paradise, Daniela and La Guabina Viajera, these works were composed between 1975 and 2005 by the teacher Julio Gentil Albastructricin Mountain (24 November 1942 – 27 August 2011) during his career as a guitarist and composer .
