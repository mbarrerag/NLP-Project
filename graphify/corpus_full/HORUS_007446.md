# Variability of precipitation and air temperature during 1979-2020 in Cartagena, Lloró and Mocoa (Colombia) related to El Niño Costero, Modoki and Canonic

The variability of the air temperature (Ta) and precipitation (Pr) is analyzed in the Colombian cities Cartagena, Lloró and Mocoa evaluating the influence of the modes El Niño Oscillation del Sur (ENOS), known as Canon, Modoki and Costero, presented during 1979-2020. The search for the relationship between the variability of the Ta and Pr and the occurrence of events ENOS was carried out taking into account the Oceanic Niño Index (ONI), Trans-Niño Index (TNI) and the Cost Index El Niño (ICEN). The monthly information of Ta and Pr was obtained from the reanalysis ERA5. The spectral density of the interest series was calculated by identifying the signals of processes that make up them. The components associated with the ENOS cycle and processes of scale were extracted, especially the response time of the signals found.
