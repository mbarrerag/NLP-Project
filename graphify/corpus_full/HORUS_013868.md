# Establishment of environmental and cultural strategies, for good handling, final disposition, decrease of the use of plastics in Tumaco.

The peaceful area, including Tumaco, has a great public health problem since the inhabitants coexist between pests, rats, mites and other species, which are attracted by the wastes, being a large part of these, plastic waste. For this reason, we must seek solutions to this problem, so, the objective of this project is to: generate a proposal of viable and achievable strategies, that allow to promote environmental and cultural conditions, for the good management and use of plastics and associated waste. To achieve this objective, they are proposed as specific objectives, to carry out a study of the baseline and the current state of the problem, and the methodologies used for the final disposal of these residues; moreover, it is proposed to identify possible strategies and methodologies to replace the plastic, separate it and realize a final disposition of this residue. Finally, we will propose methodologies for its use and reuse of the generated plastics. This work will be carried out in conjunction with the community, with the aim of selecting the main strategies and methodologies to replace the implemented strategies, once the project is completed
