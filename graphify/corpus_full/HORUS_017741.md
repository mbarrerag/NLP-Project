# STUDY OF FIRST AND SECOND PROPERTIES ORDER FOR PATRONS OF SPACE LINES.

A method for evaluating the first and second order properties of spatial line patterns is presented. This methodology is applied as a proposal for stochastic modeling of fractures in rocky slopes. The object studied corresponds to a slope characterized by short-range photometry, to which it is possible to adjust a network of discrete fractures, which determines a pattern of lines of interest. Traditional methods were applied to study the properties of first and second order of the pattern, analyzing the behavior of the associated point pattern in the representation space. Alternatives were proposed and applied to identify the properties of first and second order, which are based on the distance of Hausdorff and a pseudo-distance based on angles. Both the traditional method and the proposed method detected a pattern of grouping in the data, although the alternative presented allows to characterize the line patterns in an appropriate way by means of their position in space and inclination, providing more information than the usual statistics in the representation space
