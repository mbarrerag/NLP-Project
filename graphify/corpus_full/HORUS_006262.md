# The social background of metric thinking in problem solving with high school students

This work aims to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with metric thinking, by designing learning activities, based on the approach of solving and formulating problems, in students of 8° and 9° degree of the Rural Educational Institution La Arenosa. The work follows the guidelines of the qualitative approach, is descriptive in scope and methodological design based on the theory of educational design. The methodological instruments designed for carrying out intervention activities are of three types: diagnostic workshop, intervention workshop, and a final challenger workshop. Among the results obtained, progress is highlighted in the estimation of measurement (systematization of data and analysis of patterns, selection and refinement of units and measurement patterns, optimization of measurement processes), comprehensive approach on magnitude (construction of magnitude, understanding of the conservation processes of magnitudes, selection of units of standards and instruments), appropriate use of the critical perspective of measurement (critical interpretation of the numerical allocation and of the range of magnitude, perceived usefulness of the measure), among others .
