# A IMPLEMENTATION OF THE SYSTEMATIC PLANNING OF DISTRIBUTION AND TOPSIS FOR THE DESIGN OF OF THE POWER GENERATION PIZES OF TURBINE PARTS REPANIUM AND TOPSIS APPLICATION FOR THE DESIGN OF POWER GENERATION TURBINE PARTS REPAIR WORKSHOP

This document presents joint application of the Systematic Distribution Planning (SLP) and TOPSIS methods for the design of distribution of installations of a repair shop for electrical generation turbine parts. The SLP method has been known as an easy-to-use tool that can consider both qualitative and quantitative criteria. In this case study, the SLP method is adapted for a future plant project that is not currently under way. First, instead of a material flow analysis, the definition of process flow as well as the departmental requirements for the future plant is carried out. Then, the proximity relations between the process flow activities and the alternative plant distribution are determined. Finally, the TOPSIS method is applied to evaluate and select the best alternative of plant distribution, according to the compliance of proximity relations, the location of the hazardous departments, the flow of operations and the location of the departments around a current electrical substation. The design of the resulting installation meets the defined criteria and its construction and distribution designs in plant PFS are presented using the joint construction of the TOP software
