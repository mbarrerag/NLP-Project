# ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONING PAUTS AND METHODOLOGICAL TOOLS FOR THEIR ARCHITECTONIC FORMALIZATION

Any architectural object configures a double network of relationships: of environmental character criteria as the architectural object and the place of location and of sensitive character, between the architectural object and the people who occupy it. An architecture with environmental interest and that assumes commitments for the well-being and the health will have to reconcile this double network of relationships and prevent the inappropriate combinations: environmentally suitable buildings but uninhabitable, or buildings highly comfortable but with an excessive environmental impact. Designing the relations between the sensory systems of the people and the field of stimuli that all inevitably generates does not necessarily require design tools different from those conventional. What is also essential is to change the plans of interest so that the first plane is occupied by the environmental relations between the project, the place and the people. This way of dealing with the design process brings with it at least a couple of prerequisites. The first of them is that the design variables can be clearly formulated before starting to work, but this does not mean that the system of relations, which the variables make it, is free of implicit relationships not obvious. The following guidelines are used to help us to improve the quality of our work and to improve the quality of our work. The list of guidelines is far from complete. In fact, the lists and taxonomys are always doomed to perpetual improvement and it is desirable that this is not the exception. These guidelines can be expanded and enriched with other ideas, other positions, other professional experiences. Precisely, their implementation will provide elements to achieve this
