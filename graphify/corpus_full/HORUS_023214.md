# Impact of the constitutional principle of progressiveness on the implementation of periodic economic benefits (BEPS).

The present degree study aims to study the impact of the principle of progressiveness on the application of the Periodic Economic Benefits – BEPS, in accordance with this, was aimed at analyzing whether the BEPS were effectively created within the framework of the principles governing the Social Security System in Colombia, especially the principle of progressiveness, and whether, from its creation, the development and application of this program in Colombia fully fulfils the purpose for which it was created
