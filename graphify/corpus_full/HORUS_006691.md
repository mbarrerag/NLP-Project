# Experience of co-creation of an ELIGESSAN in the State of Sergipe, Brazil: traditional food through agroecology.

This research aims to establish a process of co-creation and initial implementation of a School of Leaders Managers in Sovereignty, Food and Nutrition Security and Human Right to Food and analyze the contributions of the process in the communities Campamento Resistencia de Cabrita y Quilombola Sítio Alto de los municipios Simão Dias y São Cristóvão en el estado de Sergipe, Brazil. As a result, it is obtained that the communities are located in the periurban and rural area, the level of schooling achieved by the respondents is basic primary and secondary, the level of household income is located at less than a minimum wage and all respondents reported having crops at home for self-consumption, sale or exchange of.
