# A study of materials and additive manufacturing techniques for a novel 2D Luneburg Lens

In this paper, characterization and manufacturing of a non-homogenous stackable 2D Luneburg lens, using additive manufacturing techniques is presented. The paper discusses the material characterization process, followed by the design, construction, and finally the prototype construction. It was found that ABS printed using SLA technology, produces the most promising results for the desired application
