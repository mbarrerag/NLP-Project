# --The impact on the human dignity of women deprived of their liberty, in 2023, in the city of Bogotá - Colombia

Women in societies have historically been subordinated in their social roles, which has reduced their role in society to a secondary (Casas, 2010), in the same line this has been reduced to an instrumental role that allows to carry out the purposes of others _x0096_how responsible for tasks of the home and related_x0096_ (Nussbaum, 2012).These inequalities manifest themselves in the social and economic fields, permeating even the state institutions that have unknown the special needs that they have, which has generated directly or indirectly the public policies of the states are excluded in relation to the care of them (Nussbaum, 2012).Based on the above, the present research project seeks to understand how these conditions of vulnerability are deepened by the deprivation of freedom in women, and particularly in how this impact of the women's role will not be realized in the quality of life that will once be in freedom, this, taking into account that pre-existing conditions of vulnerability seem to be combined with the consequences of the experience in prison, as the psychological, economic, at the individual level, so that the prison does not fulfil the structural, and managerial purpose. The tools will be part of the process of understanding the research phenomenon, as will the approach of the Restorative Justice, which will allow to evaluate in terms of reintegration, as the prison system is not only insufficient, but also worsens the social and economic conditions of women by replicating cycles of structural violence. In order to investigate in a sensitive social context, in order to minimize the possible tensions that may arise during the development of qualitative research, the methodology of Participative Action Research will be used, considering that the analysis of this phenomenon is intended to be carried out based on the active participation in the research of women who have experienced deprivation of liberty and who are currently deprived of their liberty
