# Production of clean energies in processes of transformation of recyclable material and others. An encounter between academia and communities for pedagogical praxis in science and technology.

In coordination between the Joel Sierra Human Rights Foundation, the Association for the Transformation of Recycled Products El Morichal-ASOMORICHAL- and the Orinoquia Headquarters of the National University of Colombia, we will build a process of training and appropriation of technologies for the production of clean energy in processes of transformation of recyclable material and others, which will allow us to know different strategies of science and technology that can be adapted by community groups through an extension course with workshop methodologies, that allow to develop pilots for this appropriation. The project will develop a call to all the research seeders of the National University of Colombia to present proposals for the production of clean energies that can be adapted to the conditions of small community associations in the Sarare region
