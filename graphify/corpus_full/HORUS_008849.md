# Cataloging and indexing the theme of Oscar Buenaventura's piano work

The following work presents the index and cataloguing of Oscar Buenaventura's piano works through qualitative methodology, with a descriptive approach of a documentary type for cataloguing works. These have been compiled from previous researches and works found to date from the documentary research of primary sources; all of them were organized according to the year of publication or collection. In addition, a biographical note of the composer is included from his work as composer and pianist, creating an open line of research on his piano work. Works such as Intermezzo 1, Ghost Dance and Interludio, Music Box, were collected and some others were added such as La reve de la Nimphe, Preludio and Giga de la Suite cated, as well as the Concert for two pianos. .
