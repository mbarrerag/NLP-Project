# On the banks of the Vichada: The construction of territorial sustainability in the Altillanura

This research analyzes the process of de-re configuration of territorial sustainability in the Altillanura, considering the confluence of global, national and local actors. This de-re configuration of sustainability is the result of a long historical process of confluence of multiple political, economic and institutional actors, to which the dynamics of global capital are added, which has had a central role in the last two decades. This implies the reterritorialization of the Altillanura and the conditions of the ancestral territory of the indigenous communities, in particular the sikuani. For this purpose, a systemic approach is used, using as an interpretative axis the methodological proposal of the ecosystem-territory matrix, composed of subsystems, categories and variables, which integrates quantitative and qualitative elements. The field work was carried out with the indigenous communities sikuani, in different resguards, through semi-structured interviews with the territorial integrity and the commercial groups.
