# Digital transformation and alignment with competitive strategy: Multi-case study in large food sector companies in Manizales

This study examines the alignment between digital transformation and competitive strategy, which responds to the scarcity of studies on the relationship between these variables, although a primary objective of digital transformation is to improve the strategic performance of the organizations. To achieve the defined objective, the opposite archetypes of strategies of Miles and Snow have been used -prospectors and advocates - to establish the alignment of these with the priorities of digital transformation of the companies. In this study, large companies of the food sector of the Department of Caldas, in Colombia, previously characterized as prospectors and advocates, were selected, in which their approach to digital transformation was analyzed through semi-structured interviews carried out to key managers of each company. The results of the interviews showed that the digital transformation in prospectors is oriented towards the development of new products and markets, while the digital transformation in the defenders is oriented towards the improvement of their operational efficiency. This indicates the existence of the alignment between the digital transformation and the competitive strategy of the companies analyzed. However, the prospectors present a more structured approach to the implementation of digital transformation within their strategic activities, in reference to the defenders. s prospectors and defenders.
