# IMPLEMENTING THE VIRTUAL EDUCATION STRATEGY ON VALUE AND FARMACOLOLOGICAL HANDLING OF THE DOOR IN DISEASE STUDENTS.

Pain as the fifth vital sign, declared by the Americas Association for the study of pain and by the UN as the fundamental right. The Nursing program UNAB has the commitment of the formation of its students, the holistic care of the person, the assessment and the management of the pain basing its practice in Nursing Based on the Scientific Evidence and use of clinical practices taking as international reference, the Guide of Good Clinical Practices of the Registered Nurses ́ Association of Ontario (RNAO). To implement the strategy of teaching virtual learning on evaluation and pharmacological management of pain proposed in the guide of good clinical practices of the RNAO for nursing students of the VII semester Methodology: Research project with educational design, to evaluate impact of the ICT strategy on virtual platform compared to the conventional strategy (traditional class) in students of the VII semester of nursing. The initial and final measurement of the study is performed. The study has 3 phases. 1. Design of the virtual educational proposal, 2) Validation of the educational material. 3) Intervention with the ICT strategy and evaluation of the impact. Population VII semester of the nursing program enrolled in the first and second half of the sample. The course design introduced aspects for care such as: the patient-centered care model, the cultural and compassionate competence in pain management, differentiating aspects with other virtual courses. Experts identified strengths such as the type of interactive educational material, educational strategies (education to develop evaluative challenges, Google forms for testing, to improve interactivity were included forums in Moodle). The course version was created in Spanish and English. Limitations: in 2020 the Covid-19 pandemic began, a situation that caused students high load of remote work, the invitation to develop the course generated greater demand for time to comply with what was established in the design of the proposal
