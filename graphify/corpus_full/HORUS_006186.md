# impacto agua Transformations of the Urban-Rural Space in Colombia and Their Impact on Water Supply

to carry out an analysis of the intellectual structure of knowledge about greenwashing identifying the main research trends in the subject and a literary narrative that includes a description about the concept, the main effects and strategies against greenwashing. a bibliometric analysis of the scientific production in the Web of Science (WoS) and Scopus databases was carried out, as well as a narrative review of the literature. The bibliometric results were processed through the application of platforms and software such as RStudio and VOSviewer. Main results: 282 documents were analyzed that allowed to map the scientific production (authors, documents, sources and affiliations), present a description of the intellectual structure and offer a literary narrative about the concept, classification, forms and drivers, effects and strategies to fight against greenwashing. 3 thematic clusters on the current state of research that shed light on trends in knowledge production were identified: sustainability, environmental social responsibility and green marketing. The results of the study will be useful for researchers, as well as for entrepreneurs and business managers, who will have the potential to expand the frontiers of knowledge on the subject and promote organizational strategies to combat this practice
