# CAMPINE TRADITIONAL COOK AND FOOD SOVEREIGNITY IN MONGUI MUNICIPLE, BOYACÁ.

traditional cuisine comprises a system of knowledge and practices that include techniques of acquisition, transformation and consumption of food; it includes knowledge, food and culinary practices forging the inheritance and cultural identity of peasant communities. Food sovereignty is the right of peoples to produce nutritious and culturally appropriate food, accessible, produced in a sustainable and ecological way. to analyze the knowledge and practices of traditional cuisine in function of strengthening the dynamics of food sovereignty, in the peasant population of the municipality of Monguí, Boyacá. qualitative with an ethnographic approach. Use of participatory observation techniques, discussion groups, participatory workshops of traditional cuisine, semi-structured interview. Analysis of results in 3 phases: discovery, codification/analysis, socialization. traditional cuisine and food sovereignty are found in a strong relationship with the production of food and the ecological practices associated with it, conservation of indigenous seeds, the preparation and consumption of traditional recipes, beliefs, cohesion and meanings associated with food, as well as traditional knowledge associated with land and care.
