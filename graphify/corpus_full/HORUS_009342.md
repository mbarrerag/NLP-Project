# FAMILY FUNCTIONALITY AND OVERCOME OF CARE AS A DERIVED DEPENDENCE OF THE MAJOR ADULT

Main objective: To identify the family functionality and overload of the caregiver according to the degree of dependence of the elderly with chronic non-transmissible disease in caregivers of Ibagué. A descriptive cross-sectional observational study included 76 caregivers of elderly people attending the adult care program. instruments: family apgar, barthel and zarith scale. Main results: Among the characteristics of the family bond of caregivers were women by 75%, of whom they were daughters by 54.8% and the couple by 15.1%. Carers had a chronic disease presence by 40.4%. The degree of dependence of the elderly caring for them was classified from severe to moderate according to Barthel's score (44.4%). The care load score perceived by caregivers was reported as intense (51.2%), the family functionality in caregivers was moderate between men and women (p=0.00 and p=0.41) without significant statistical differences. Main conclusion: It was found as a common factor that the caregivers of older adults were usually women. In addition, it was found that the level of dependence of older patients was proportional to the overload of their caregivers
