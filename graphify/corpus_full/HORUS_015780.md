# INTENT CARBON-BASE DRIVING PLAYING FOR THE MANUFACTURING OF ELECTRONIC TEXTILE: AN APPROXIMATION FROM THE WALK

The conductive inks are a viscous compound that allows the passage of the electric current after they are dried. Typically these are made from precious metals or nanocomposed so their price is high. The interest in these inks lies in their versatility to be used in multiple applications such as the manufacture of intelligent reversible articles, electronic textiles and printed circuits. We report in this research a first prototype of conductive inks developed within the classroom. These have been obtained from coal extracted from alkaline batteries, since their use contributes to the circular economy and, moreover, gives a solution to the problem of pollution caused by used batteries. The synthesis of these inks was made from activated mineral coal from Cesar department, within the logic of the energy transition and strategic metals.
