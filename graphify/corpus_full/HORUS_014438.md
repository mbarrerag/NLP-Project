# Self-condensation of Aldehydes α, β-unsaturated Organocatalyzed by N-Heterocyclic Carbenes.

The N-heterocyclic carbens (NHCs) are molecules that are usually used alongside transition metals as organometallic catalysts. Despite their great virtues as catalysts, the organometallic activity complexes are usually rejected in many industrial processes due to the presence of traces of heavy metals in the final products and the difficulty of their elimination. It is for this reason that for some years the NHCs have been the subject of studies that seek their implementation as organocatalizers.These processes have proved to be less selective than their competitors or organometallic.However, the easy access to these catalysts (many are extracted from natural sources or are synthesized in a simple way) makes the processes less expensive and of course, less toxic because the presence of metals is avoided.
