# Temperature influence on the accuracy of demand forecasts: Case study in a company that consumes soluble lyophilisate coffee

Influence of temperature in the accuracy of demand forecasts: Case study in a company of mass consumption of soluble coffee lyophilized. The main objective of this final master's work was to determine the influence of temperature on the accuracy of the forecasts of demand for lyophilized soluble coffee (for a Colombian supplier in the Mexican market. Historical data of monthly sales of soluble coffee and average temperatures (2004-2019) were collected in Mexico, with the purpose of analyzing the correlation between the average temperature and the sales of soluble coffee in each state by means of the Pearson coefficient, finding a strong negative relationship (-0.9 average). The results show that sales tend to increase in colder seasons (winter and autumn) and to decrease in warm periods (spring and summer). On the other hand, the accuracy of the forecasts of the company studied was improved by means of the company's owning and integrating for future studies of seasonality as a determinant. It is concluded that temperature is a key factor in the behavior of soluble coffee sales in Mexico, impacting the supply from its Colombian supplier. of -0.9.
