# Study of the behaviour of a steel reticular structure requested by high temperature effects.

This document shows how a high temperature request is incorporated into the mathematical analysis of steel structures, using two mathematical modelling software, OpenSees and SAP2000. Three reticular structures were taken: one floor and one hollow; one floor and two vanes; two floors and two vanes. Likewise, the analysis presents three approaches to the effect of fire: affectation of the elasticity module; affectation of the area exposed to fire; and implementation of a time-temperature heat curve. As a result, a comparative picture of the results found was made
