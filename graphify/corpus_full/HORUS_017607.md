# UNIVERSIDAD NACIONAL DE COLOMBIA - AGRICULTURAL SCIENCES FACULTY-INGENIERÍA AGRÍCOLA RESEARCH GRUOP

ABISURE is an international network sponsored and led by the National University of Colombia, which is made up of 26 national and 21 international institutions with 94 active researchers from more than 17 countries around the world. Currently, the lines of work of the network are the transformation of biomass, mathematical modeling, the optimization of physicochemical processes, the obtention of high-added value products (biomaterials), the development of academic courses, and the sustainability focused on both the life cycle analysis and circular economy. Worldwide, scientists from all disciplines make significant efforts to develop technologies, processes, and products that lead to mitigating the environmental impact of human beings and dependence on fossil fuels. In this sense, the use of biomass through thermochemical, physical, and biological processes is projected as a medium-term solution for the generation of new products, renewable energy sources, fertilizers, and the partial replacement of traditional fuels. However, these efforts must be carried out jointly to build a roadmap toward the so-called energy transition. Based on the above, to propose solutions and discuss the technological and scientific challenges regarding the use of biomass, the first international meeting of the ABISURE network was held in the city of Medellín on June 21-23, 2022. This event was attended by 83 national and international researchers from some of the most prestigious universities and research centers in the world. There, topics related to the development of technologies for the use of biomass in Colombian industry, the sustainability of processes, the production of new biofuels and fertilizers, and the challenges that humanity faces in terms of energy use were addressed. With these proceedings and on behalf of the National University of Colombia and the ABISURE Network team, we want to thank the authors, participants, speakers, attendees, and all the people who in one way or another generously offered their time and effort to make this meeting a great success.
