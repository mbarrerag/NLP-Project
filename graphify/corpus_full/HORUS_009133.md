# RESEARCH STANDARDS FROM THE ARTS: THE EPIA RESEARCH RESEARCH SEMILLIER

The book contains the results of a series of Bachelor’s degree studies in the plastic arts, which starts from the current need to involve writing as a resource for the public inscription of artistic thought. It also brings together the translation of different conversations with national latistas-researchers, close to the search for the works of the studyets. For this purpose, each essay presents important theoretical elaborations critically related to the formal proposals of their own artistic works.
