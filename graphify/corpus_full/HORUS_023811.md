# THE MEDELLIN 'MODEL' IN QUESTION: SPACE JUSTICE OR CAPITAL URBANIZATION?

The urban research carried out in and about Medellín has revolved over the last two decades around the analysis of a set of public policies implemented mainly between 2004 and 2011; they are framed in two lines of reflection. One of apology to these policies that seek to highlight their success, their innovative character, or even their role in achieving greater social justice. Another of questioning their contents, to their effects in various aspects of urban life, is habitat, mobility, education, participation, etc. The aim of this presentation, framed in this second perspective, is to seek an explanation of the conditions of possibility that favored the construction of the Medellin Model to identify how the argument of social justice has legitimized a less visible process: an intense wave of what Harvey (2018) calls as urbanisation of the capital
