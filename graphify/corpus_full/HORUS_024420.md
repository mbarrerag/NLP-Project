# Assessment of soil pressures using Land Accounts methodology in Caldas department, Colombia

The change in land use in an unplanned manner is causing problems of erosion, loss of productivity and conflicts over land use. Therefore, the loss of productivity due to land degradation has become a major global problem. In Caldas, the problem is serious due to the high susceptibility of soil to improper uses. Therefore, the aim of this work is to determine the changes that have occurred in coverages in the period 2000-2014. For this, it is proposed to use the methodology of Land Accounts developed by the European Environment Agency. This methodology allows to assess pressures exerted on the soil, defines changes over time, measures the speed of these changes and, in this way, predicts what changes have caused or could cause land degradation and loss of productivity. In Caldas, the net formation was -4.74 and -5.5 km2 in the periods 2000-2010 and 2010-2014 respectively, the agricultural areas were reduced by the same degree of land use
