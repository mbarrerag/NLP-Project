# OVER AFRAID BETTER ASSIGNMENT OF DISEASE

Tuberculosis is an infectious diseasecontagious and a social disease that causes great suffering, affects the biological, psychological, social and cultural sphere of the individual.When the person believes that his health is threatened can feel insecure to the future and seeks to organize the complex, strange or threatening information in a controllable and understandable way to find the psychological and social support that are necessary, returning old beliefs or incursion in them, by imitation or suggestion almost always of others, with which he identifies (González, 2004; mbuthia, olungah, & ondiche, 2018). Therefore the aim is to describe the fears of people with pulmonary tuberculosis after knowing their diagnosis. Qualitative type study. The 20 participants were selected by convenience, through the tuberculosis control program at local level in the locality of Suba. Given the presence of a new disease, they know or know little about the participants, they feel afraid, they question about their disease, whether there is or not cure and how to protect them related issues. they want to know more about the disease and about the new symptoms
