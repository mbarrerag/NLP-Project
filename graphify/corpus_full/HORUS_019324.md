# Apert syndrome: alternatives of orthodontic - surgical treatment and execution times. A review of the literature

to carry out a review of the literature about the orthodontic and surgical treatments of the Apert syndrome during the different stages of growth and development. a search was carried out in the databases MedLine (PubMed), Science Direct, Scopus and Wiley Online Library with the combination of the following terms: Synchroc criniosynostosis, Dental treatment, orthodontic treatment, Apert Syndrome, surgical treatment, dental care. Systematic and literature reviews, retrospective, longitudinal and cohort studies, series and case reviews published between 1990 and 2020 in Spanish or English were included; articles related to other syndromes were excluded, as well as studies in animals. Articles were selected according to their relevance and availability of complete text; repeated management findings were eliminated; additionally, the snowball system was used in selected articles; the quality of evidence was evaluated through the GRADE system. 34 negative articles were included (high quality: 2, moderate impact: 1, low and very low required: 12).
