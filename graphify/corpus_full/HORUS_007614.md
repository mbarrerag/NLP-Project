# Study of the inclusion of protease enzymes of fungal origin for the production of self-degradable polymeric PLA films

The fungi play an important role, since they are heterotroph organisms that produce enzymes with a wide field of action, involved in the transformation and mineralization of a large amount of organic substances. That is why the inclusion of fungal enzymes in polymers, has the potential to solve the contamination by this type of substances; it is evident that for this to be done the development and implementation of new technologies that convert these biotechnological proposals into viable alternatives at industrial level. These strategies, would lead to an increase in the demand for polymeric materials with a kinetic of predictable and controlled degradation.The study on the improvement of the properties of enzymes, their thermal stability, catalytic activity, the specificity of substrate, the types of reaction, as well as the energy and economic cost, are crucial aspects to take into account to overcome the difficulties in their application at domestic, industrial and open field level.For all the above, this work is proposed in order to contribute in the development of new materials based on allipathic polyesters such as the PLA, with increased degradation speeds by means of the inclusion of fungal enzymes obtained from macromices.
