# EUGLOSSINI BISH CAPTURE METHODS FOR ECOLOGICAL STUDIES

This book compiles some of the results obtained in the framework of the project "Echonomic and evolutionary relationships between orchids (Orchidaceae: Catasetinae and Stanhopeinae) and their pollinators, the euglossines bees (Apidae: Euglossini), in the Amazon region of Putumayo and peaceful Nariño, Colombia, with code 1120-659-45149, CT FP 44842-562-2014. The study was carried out with the participation of the National University of Colombia - Sede Palmira, through the research group in Orchideas, Ecology and Vegetal Systematic, and the Pontifical Javeriana University of Cali. The authors were involved in the capacity of researchers, master students and young researchers Colsciences under the coordination of Dr. joel tupac otero. It is expected that this book will be of great utility to understand some of the most important characteristics of the Euglisian bees, their role as pollinizers and their close interactions with orchids. In addition, we present ourselves are key taxonomic by gender that allow to identify the particular traitss for the identification of future species
