# Effects of muscle therapy interventions on the prevention of delirium in patients with risk factors to present it in the non-Covid intensive care unit of the Hernando Moncaleano Perdomo University Hospital (HUN) of the city of Neiva

The delirium constitutes one of the transitory neuropsychiatric disorders that occur most frequently within intensive care units, associated with a considerable infradiagnosis and linked to fatal and non-fatal outcomes that alter, in large magnitude, the quality of life of the sufferer, both intrahospital and extrahospital; therefore, it is important to strengthen the non-pharmacologic strategies that already exist to prevent delirium, putting at the service of the integrity of cognitive functions the musicotherapeutic resources from the biomedical and multimodal approaches proper to the musicotherapy; this study is therefore concerned with detailing the muscular therapy interventions that were applied to 7 participants in the ICU NO-COVID (in some cases, with subsequent transfer to hospitalization) of the FUN and its impact on the prevention of the institution of this disorder (delirium), the modulation of vital constants and the influence on the well-being of the participant, his family and the treating staff and in other clinical outcomes, from a mixed methodology. s at the service of the integration of cognitive functions from the biomedical and multimodal approaches of music therapy.
