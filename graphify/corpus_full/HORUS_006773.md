# A comparison for speech pattern recognition using Hidden Markov Models and Neuronal Networks in the Spanish language

With the progress of technology, especially in the field of computing, it is increasingly imperative that the interaction between humans and machines be dynamic and efficient. This evolution entails the need to develop systems that facilitate such interaction through human natural language, that is, speech. In the creation of these systems, two main approaches are highlighted: the theory of the Model of Markov Hidden and the Neuronal Networks, being the latter the most researched and those that have achieved performance improvements in recent years.
