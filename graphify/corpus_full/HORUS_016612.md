# Social representations of old age in young and old people in the countryside and the city, Colombia

The rapid population ageing of the country, as well as the different psychological, social and economic conditions with which the elderly live, have generated the need to ask themselves what is the social construction of this moment of life.The purpose of this work is to understand what are the social representations of old age, in relation to belonging to different contexts and generations.For this purpose the images, attitudes contained in such representations and their way of organizing, as well as the functions that these representations have for each group.The research problem is addressed from the theoretical perspective of the life course, from where it is understood that old age is part of a vital continuous in which historical, social and cultural conditions influence its construction.The research methodology is based on the analysis of thematic content of autobiographical narrative interviews and open questionnaires on the meanings of old age.The participants were persons from a rural area of the municipality of Duitama, Boyacá or from the urban area of Bogotá D.C.
