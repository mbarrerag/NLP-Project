# Structural complexity in descriptive and exhibition school texts: corpus study

Abstract This research paper aims to study the structural linguistic complexity, specifically that related to descriptive-exhibitive school texts. Thus, the text asked about linguistic factors (dimensions) that allow measuring complexity and classifying the texts of a sample. Eighty school texts were analyzed from a series of traits (grouped in two levels: morphosyntactic and semantic) and through a factor analysis. The results show that the quantity, prototypicity and informationity factors were significant and the partially productive variety factor. In the face of the features, the hypothesis that it is necessary to include in the analysis not only syntactic but also semantic and even pragmatic features is valid; however, these results are provisional because it is necessary to rethink the criteria and explanatory hypotheses given
