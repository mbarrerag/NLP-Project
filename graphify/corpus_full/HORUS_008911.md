# ESTENOSIS LARINGOTRAQUEAL POSINTUBATION BY COVID-19 IN TWO HOSPITALS

the infection by COVID-19 has left more than 400 million people infected to date, and between 10%-15 % requires orotracheal intubation. This has triggered a wave of sequelae in the airway in the long term, which have increased positive morbidity and mortality. The aim of this study was to describe the demographic, clinical characteristics and possible risk factors of patients with laryngotracheal stenosis (LTS) postintubation by COVID-19 in the Hospital Universitario Clínica San Rafael and the Hospital Militar Central in Bogotá, Colombia. observational descriptive study of retrospective case series that collected sociodemographic data, comorbidities, intubation times, stenosis characteristics, endoscopic study results, managements offered and results. 25 patients, 15 men (60%) and 10 women (40 %), with average intubation times of 15.7 days.
