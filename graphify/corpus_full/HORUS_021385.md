# Development of intralaboratory validation of qPCR - SYBR Green to quantify Escherichia coli O157:H7

Escherichia coli of the serotype O157:H7 is one of the diarrheal agents, with high incidence in hemorrhagic colitis and development of hemolytic uremic syndrome (HUS), mainly in infants. A strategy to prevent infections with E. coli O157:H7, is its early detection in contaminated food and water, for which reliable and rapid tests are required. In this research four protocols of quantitative polymerase chain reactions were developed and validated (qPCR-SYBR Green) to determine the presence of the bacteria, based on its rfbE, stx1 and stx2 genes. The results of the efficiencies were between 77 % and 97 %, with a high linearity (R2 0.99).
