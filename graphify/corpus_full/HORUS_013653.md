# Music therapy in chronic illness: Effects on the quality of life of pediatric patients participating in hospital classrooms in an institution in Bogotá

This research is of a qualitative type phenomenological hermeneutic and highlights the importance of the musicotherapeutic intervention in patients with chronic disease, especially those diagnosed from childhood. The study presents the results of a musicotherapeutic process focused on describing the effect that music therapy generates on the quality of life of two pediatric patients with chronic disease diagnosis. The study was carried out with participants of the Hospitaller Class Programme of an institution in Bogotá, Colombia, during 15 sessions that were held between April 2022 and March 2023. During the musicotherapeutic process, relevant aspects related to autonomy, self-confidence and creativity were worked out. For the collection and analysis of data, semi-structured interviews were used, a protocol of sessions and a follow-up of the sessions was carried out. As a result, the self-confidence and creativity of the museum process were favored, as well as the recognition of the ability to make decisions. significant aspects related to autonomy, self-confidence, and creativity were addressed. Data collection and analysis used semi-structured interviews, session protocols, and session tracking. As a result, it was concluded that the music therapy process enhanced the capacity to identify and express personal skills, as well as collecting the ability to make decisions on the go. This, in turn, strengthened the skills associated with autonomy, self-confidence, and creativity, which are interconnected with quality of life as part of the psychological and self-determination dimensions
