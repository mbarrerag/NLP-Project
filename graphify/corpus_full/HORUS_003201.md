# Perspectives and evolution of the concept of citizenship from the citizen competencies

When it comes to Citizenship, different challenges and problems can be presented because, it is a complex concept that keeps within itself, multiple interpretations that range from legal, political, social and cultural aspects, which can often be permeated by a given historical period. However, the Citizens Competences are the set of knowledge and skills that -in theory- function as guarantors for the practice of being a good citizen.
