# Alternatives for improving water quality and the hydraulic infrastructure used in food production in the greenhouses of the Bogotá campus

The shortage of food is increasing in different places in the world and Bogotá is not the exception. The exponential growth of the population forces to look for sources of food production, which are clean and of high nutritional value. The National University of Colombia through the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences, has more than 15,000 m2 of greenhouses in which large quantities of healthy and rich food can be produced nutritionally. The water used as a supply within greenhouses, despite being a collection of rains, is charged by physical, chemical and biological particles that can affect the correct functioning of the irrigation systems used, as well as harm the product that is being cultivated. Also due to the different uses that have been given to greenhouses, as well as the multidisciplinary nature of projects executed there have triggered the trait of multiple hydraulic lines within and outside the greenhouses creating inefficiencies of the operation of the infrastructure.
