# ANTI-PROSTITUTIONAL TYPE ACTIVITY OF THE FRANTION OF DICLOROMETAN OBTAINED FROM THE ETANOLIC EXTRACT OF HYPERICUM MEXICANUM L

According to the WHO, 3.8% of the world population suffers from depression. Therapy of this disorder can be psychological, and depending on its severity, be combined with pharmacotherapy; it is not exempt from problems [1]. As alternative treatment, it is used to use natural products, since they are considered safer than conventional medicines and have been shown to be effective in moderate depression or dysthymia [2]. This study describes the phytochemical profile and the evaluation of antidepressant activity of the dichloromethane fraction obtained from the etherlic extract of Hypericum mexicanum L
