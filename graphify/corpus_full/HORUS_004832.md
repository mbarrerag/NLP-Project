# INTERVENTIONS FOR ADULTS WITH FUNCTIONAL DEPENDENCE AND THEIR CAREERS DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIA

Summary The COVID-19 pandemic has affected the population, harming especially the members of those social groups in situations of greatest vulnerability. These specific populations, such as those with some functional dependence, could be more affected by the effects of the pandemic of the COVID-19. Therefore, the objective of this article was to describe the interventions to preserve general health, maintain function and independence and prevent infection by COVID-19 for adults with functional dependence (ADF). A systematic search was carried out in databases. The titles and summaries of each publication were reviewed to determine its relevance. Two independent reviewers agreed to the full text articles to determine their eligibility after initial selection. The searches were carried out in September 2021 and updated in January and July 2022. The information found was classified into 3 categories: 1) ADF during the pandemic of COVID-19; 2) ADF during the pandemic of COVID-19 according to a specific condition (neurological conditions, sensory disabilities/disability and cognitive impairment), and 3) the elderly with functional dependence each
