# Design and assembly of experiments for the teaching of Rock Engineering.

The Rock Engineering Laboratory (LIR) seeks to offer students from various undergraduate programmes practical training in areas of this discipline through experiments that allow them to observe, manipulate variables and understand concepts of two characteristics of the rocks: porosity and permeability, fundamental in numerous applications, such as deposits of hydrocarbons, groundwater, flow of gases, design of excavations, among others. The project consists of the stages: (a) bibliographic research of experiments. (b) Selection of 3 to 5 experiments to build. (c) Designs of experiments and assemblies. (d) Acquisition and manufacture of assembly elements. (e) Conducting of the assembly in the laboratory facilities. (f) Development of the didactic guides of experiments
