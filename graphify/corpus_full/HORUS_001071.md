# ESTABLISHMENT AND STRENGTHENING OF ARTSANAL PRODUCTIVE SYSTEMS FOR THE GREEN MARKET AND BIOCOMMERCE, IN THE COrdOB DEPARTMENT

The fruit of Totumo (crescentia cujete) grows in a few days, it has been recorded fruits that from the appearance of the button until being at the point of collection in green they pass 2 weeks. It is of constant flowering and production of fruits throughout the year, with a great opportunity to take advantage as raw material for sustainable crafts
