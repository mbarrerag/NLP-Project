# HEALTH AND LIFE IN THE FUNCTIONING OF THE MARKET ECONOMY

The third year of Iván Duque’s government was nuanced by the health issue, both the global phenomenon of the pandemic by Covid-19, which in March 2020 was declared in the country, did not manage to be mitigated, much less suppressed at the end of this third period of government. This health-care phenomenon as social, has had very severe implications for the life of the Colombian population, mainly in the social sectors of low economic levels, which besides being those that have suffered the most Covid-19 and where the greatest number of deaths are concentrated for this cause, have markedly worsened their living conditions, given the social and economic circumstances imposed by the pandemic. This situation is related to the type of responses given by the national government, both in the public health component and in the social and economic components, which must undoubtedly accompany the health measures in order to be able to adequately face an epidemic-pandemnity.
