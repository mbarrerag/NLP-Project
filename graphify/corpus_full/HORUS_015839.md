# ANALYSIS OF THE DISTRIBUTION OF SOURCE ASSOCIATED SOURCE-SACKED STRADDLE IN THE FOURTH OF THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC IN RECENT PHASES OF THE SUR OSCILATION CHILD

The influence of El Niño South Oscillation (ENOS) phases was analyzed in the process of surgence of the Colombian Pacific Basin (CPC). Differences in the distribution of the surgency zones were identified through the Superficial Sea Temperature (TSM)Surficial Sea Salinity (SSM) and Chlorophylla (Chl-a) during 2011 (La Niña)2013 (neutral) and 2015 (El Niño). Monthly horizontal maps of the variables were made to visualize their behavior and identify hot spots and their intensity and vertical temperature transects were constructed to demonstrate variations of the mixture layer and the seasonal thermoclination associated with the rise of water.This is the first work focused on recent phases of ENOS finding increase in TSM values?SSM and Chl-a in 2015 and decrease in the end of 2011 as reported theoretically.Thermocline presented somerization in transects of latitudes 2 and 4°N during the three years of the NSOs, especially finding the variable behavior in 2015 where a general deepening of the south is observed.The negative phase of the magnitude of the NSC is observed
