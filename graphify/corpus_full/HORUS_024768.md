# Visospace processing in the perception and understanding of action verbs in older people who report moderate to severe vertigo.

The research purpose was to explore the relationship between the perception and understanding of action verbs and spatial terms in people with and without vertigo between 50 and 80 years. The sample was 12 participants; they were evaluated with Dizziness Handicap Inventory (DHI), Videoelectronicsgmography, video-assisted cephalic impulse test (Vhit), Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) and neuropsychological tests of Neuronorma Colombia. They performed four tasks with verbs and terms of ligature in the EyeTracker Tobii Tx300 visual tracking team. The results were analyzed in the SPSS 21 program, using mean comparisons, correlations between variables related to gender, age, schooling, deviations, U Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon range testing and linear regression of successive steps.
