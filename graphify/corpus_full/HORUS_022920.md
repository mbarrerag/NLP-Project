# Analysis of management skills in the management of construction projects: Applied to residents and/or construction directors

Within the execution of the construction project, there are overruns and delays in the works due to the attention to unforeseen projects presented in the works; lack of coordination in the items that make up the project, which generates a lack of order in the execution of the project; inability to communicate with poorly trained staff, who are interested in the project to be implemented and several informalities presented in the Construction Sector in Colombia. This work proposes to establish criteria of good practice for the management and professional exercise of the resident and/or director of construction works, bearing in mind its knowledge and applicability. Initially, research is carried out on the different methods and methodologies applied to the Project Management, in order to know the good practices applied to the sector. Subsequently, a survey is carried out on Residents, Directors and Building Managers with the aim of validating their knowledge and applicability for their professional exercise. The results obtained help establish guidelines for the generation of good practice standards; in order to establish scopes for the professional exercise of the Resident and/ Director of works. . practices applied to the sector. Subsequently, a survey applied to Residents, Directors and Masters of work is carried out in order to validate their knowledge and application for their professional practice.
