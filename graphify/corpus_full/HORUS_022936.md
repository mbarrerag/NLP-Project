# Narratives on mental health and strategies for the management of park rangers of the Amazonia Territorial Directorate of Natural National Parks exposed to situations of public risk.

This study is an opportunity to expand reflections on the work of the park rangers in Colombia, to know the impacts of violence on mental health and to propose strategies for strengthening from psychology. Based on the qualitative methodology, a narrative interview was conducted to learn the history of the park ranger, was transcribed, and under the model of narrative analysis in health psychology of Wong & Breheny (2018) a matrix was constructed in which the information was organized and codified by levels: personal, interpersonal and/or social narratives. Subsequently, and based on the theoretical reference of Lazarus and Folkman were triangular and identified the effects and strategies of coping.
