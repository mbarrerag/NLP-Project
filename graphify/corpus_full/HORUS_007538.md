# The COPdi. Strengthening our Thought Center to co-build country policies with and on diversities

Proof of this is that our team has led the construction of numerous contributions to the field of human development and has established itself as a academic unit of first order with capacity to respond in a agile and pertinent way to the strategic problems of the Colombian society regarding the plurality of the citizens that make it.
