# Prototype of music software with ML-based feedback system for initial keyboard learning as an alternative method aimed at children.

According to the literature, music has accompanied the human being for millennia, in different situations, emotions and activities. Furthermore, it not only allows expressions of the internal personal states and feelings, but it can also produce many positive effects in those who practice it. . Various authors have explored these benefits that brings with it the musical activity, mainly in children. They highlight positive aspects of learning music in different areas of knowledge, in school performance and even, improvements in the intellectual coefficient of the infants. However, despite the large number of studies that this work offers to the benefits of music in children/girls and the different alternatives of nacent education, in Colombia the situation remains dramatic in terms of incorporating musical activity in the school curriculum. The above added to political factors, teaching spaces and teacher training.With this, this work offers a new alternative of musical learning, aimed at children/girls from 7 to 11 years old, by means of musical software focused on initial teaching of the instrumental keyboard.
