# CHARACTERIZATION OF IMMUNODOMINE EPITOPS AND N-GLYCHOSIS PATRONS IN THE HEMAGLUTININE OF PORCINE H1 VIRUS IN COLOMBIA

the swine Influenza is caused by the Influenza A Virus (VIA) which permanently varies as a result of mutations (drift) and genetic rearrangements (shift).When variations affect epitopes of Hemagglutinin (HA) or its N-glycosylation (NGli) patterns, the antigenic characteristics of the virus can be altered. In Colombia the presentation of drift as well as changes in the NGli are unknown. to determine the existence of epitopic variations and NGli in porcine VIA in Colombia. the EF genes of 37 VIA H1 detected in pigs of technified productions in eight departments of the country were sequenced and translated. From the amino acid sequences, the variation in the five epitops was analyzed - the difference in the virus - the EF is not divergent by comparison of hazing and pangenetic methods
