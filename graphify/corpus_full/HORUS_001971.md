# Safety and health practices at work as selection criteria for suppliers of the supply chain: case study company importing and marketing lighting products

This study has as main objective to provide safety and health practices at work (SST) as essential criteria in the selection of suppliers for an importing and marketing company of lighting products. Based on the need to guarantee safe and healthy work environments, a case study was developed in which the suppliers' responses were evaluated through structured interviews. This analysis is part of the fulfilment of the sustainable development objectives of the 2030 Agenda, especially the third one, which seeks to ensure a healthy life and promote well-being for all, and the eighth one, which promotes decent work and sustainable economic growth (International Labour Organisation, 2016). Through a qualitative approach, a case study was carried out to define OSH practices of the suppliers of the company under study. This analysis was carried out through structured interviews, followed by the criteria of OSH, the training plan, the integration and the audit of the most dangerous ones in the workplace. practice as essential criteria in the selection of suppliers and a company that imports and markets lighting products.
