# The Lima frigol (Phaseolus lunatus L.): a legume species cultivated with the potential to adapt to climate change in Colombia?

As a consequence of climate change, a global temperature increase and a decrease in precipitation in different regions of the world, including Colombia, are predicted. It is necessary to identify Colombian crops that have tolerance to stress due to water deficit. In this context, some legumes, such as the greenish Lima (Phaeolus latus L.), are an excellent alternative for their good performance in dry climates, their wide range of edaphoclimatic adaptations and their high nutritional value. The present research project aims to identify accesses of frijol Lima with potential to adapt to conditions of stress due to water deficit and identify genomic regions associated with this trait. To achieve this, the study of 400 wild and domesticated accesses of frijol Lima that represent the variation in precipitation that occurs along the wide geographical range that occupies this species will be analyzed for these accesses, the high-voltage and the high-voltage characteristics will be performed
