# RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN MANNERELLA FORSYTHIA AND DISEASE OF ALZHEIMER.

The research on the relationship between systemic and bucodental diseases, in particular periodontal disease (EP), is on the increase.This inflammatory pathology of multifactorial origin is influenced by the action of subgingival bacteria that generate a chronic inflammatory response in tissues, thus formulating theories that link it to the development of diseases such as Alzheimer's; although a causal relationship between them has not yet been established, it is possible to presume that they are associated by means of bacterial action.This document seeks to deepen on the action of Tannerella. forsythia (T. forsythia) and its role in the development of Alzheimer's disease (EA), through the information reported in the literature to know possible mechanisms of action of microorganism in patients with this type of dementia. OBJECHOW: Determine the relationship of EP (by means of T. forsythia) with EA as reported in the literature. A search of the literature was carried out in the databases of electronic data PubMed and Dish, using key words, terms and EMsythia T. forsythia virulence, are able to trigger a systemic inflammatory process, transmitting macrophages signals to the microglya, generating a pro-inflammatory state, resulting in neuroinflammation, which contributes to the onset and progression of the AE. However, research is needed to allow the proper identification of T. forsythia with a specific approach and thus be able to determine whether its role in the AE is at the central or peripheral level. T. forsythia could play a role in the pathogenesis of the AE through its ability to interact with proteins and host cells, causing an inflammatory response that would contribute to neuroinflammation and neurodegeneration. However, the mechanism by which they relate is not yet fully understood and more research is needed to determine its role in the progression of Alzheimer's disease
