# HISTORIA AND COMPARISONAL CONSTITUTIONAL THEORY, VOLUME III, ANTI-CONSTITUCIONALIS, NON-CONSTITUCIONALIS AND POSSITUCIONALIS

The basic idea of modern constitutionalism is the effective prevention of the dark side of the State. It is intended to avoid, with legal methods, the abuses of power, of which the world history is unfortunately so plagued. The present third and last volume of the series History and constitutional theory compared in three volumes is devoted to such dark sides of the modern State. Part of the idea that he who wants to study constitutionalism needs to understand tyranny. In the form of three chapters will be analyzed systems in which prevailed, in the twentieth and twenty-first centuries, the anti-constitutionalism, non-constitutionalism or post-constitutionalism.
