# Characteristics of the domain wd40-1 and its association with parasites of the order Haemosporidia

The proteins WD40 are not studied in Plasmodium spp. Our groups add interesting knowledge and open possibilities in advance with the approach of a protein that is proposed could be unique of the genus Plasmodium. We do not know if it is in other organisms of order Haemospyridia different from Plasmodium and Hepatocystis. We propose the following objectives: i. Study the presence of coding genes for the protein of PlasWD40-1, in the blood of wild fauna infected with parasites of the order Haemosporidia, and ii. Determine the presence of PlasWD40-1 in the blood infected with Haemoproteus collombae and in the sexual forms of the parasite. Our research focus is on the search for DNA sequences that allow to find encoding genes for PlasWD40-1 in other parasites of the order of Haesomoria of wild fauna, using the blood infected with Haemoporia Haemoporidia of the order of Haemoporidia of birds and reptiles. For this purpose, we will use the genetic information bank present in the biological collection of the group of the Haemocorical Since its characteristics In silico is proposed as a possible, interesting and promising therapeutic target. The impact and achievements of work points to sustainable development and aims to improve the quality of life in the face of the pathogen of malaria and points to PlasWD40-1, as a possible promising therapeutic target that proposes a bioprospective study in the short, medium and long term
