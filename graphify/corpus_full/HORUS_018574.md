# DYNAMIC EVOLUTIVE DYNAMICS OF SARS-COV-2 IN VALLE DE CAUCA, COLOMBIA

The study of the evolutionary dynamics of the coronamic virus SARS-CoV-2 responsible for the current pandemic COVID-19 and its possibility of continuous transmission in certain human populations, has allowed to monitor different viral variants that in some cases have been related to an increase in the number of positive cases and indirectly in the increase of the mortality due to the lack of effective response of the health services due to the high demand that has generated the disease. For Colombia, variants of concern have been identified (VOC, by their acronym in English) Alfa, Gamma, Delta and Omicron. Likewise, variants of interest like Lambda and Mu, (VOI, by their acronym in Spanish) predominant in South America. The objective of this study was to analyze the evolutionary dynamics of the bloodlines of SARS-CoV-2 identified in the population of the department of the Cauca Valley, Colombia, in a temporary window of the frequencies of the polymorphous, were collected and aligned complete sequences with the availability of metadata in the databases of GISAID and NCBI
