# Implementation of an integrated control system for the operation of the maneuver equipment of a ship type PSV (Platform Supply Vessel), for the Good Tide Agency company at the base of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

This document describes the work carried out during the internship at the Good Tide Agency, specifically in the dynamic positioning ship class 2 Barú Andes in the port of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. During the course of the document there is a general explanation of the systems contained in the ship, what is its importance in the operation and how they are controlled or supervised by the focused monitoring system. The process of adapting new systems to the centralized monitoring software is described, such as the fuel transfer pump and the calibration of pressure transmitters that, due to logistics issues, had to be changed by models with greater rotation in the market. For this, processes such as the increase in pressure by means of a hydraulic manual pump and digital control links and analogues of 4 to 20mA were generated to diagnose signals towards the monitoring system. .
