# Centrifugal pump design using the topological optimization and parallel cloud computing method.

This work is specifically focused on radial flow centrifugal pumps, whose axial velocity component is not considered in comparison with tangential and radial speeds, resulting in the design of two dimensions of blades. Consequently, as the design of alabe is a complex task, due to the quantity of free geometric parameters involved (curved radius, wing angle, etc.), the Topological Optimization Method (MOT) is used. The selection of the MOT in comparison with another optimization method (parametric, material or form) is justified by the simple fact that this method combines all the previous methods (more robust optimization). For the topological design of the alabes, the equations of state of Navier-Stokes are solved by means of the finite element method (MEF), to generate the fields of speed and pressure, which are the most dynamic distribution fields within the impeller, to later minimize two physical phenomena (objective functions) that are the problem of viscose dissification and the void. These functions are combined by means of the objective method of incombining in the impeller. Watts]. Additionally, the performance obtained when considering the minimization of dissipation and vorticity energy (wd=0.8 and wr=0.2) is 5.94 [Watts]. These topologies are extended in a complete domain design in a 3D model using ANSYS FLUENT, with the aim of validating the minimization of the objective functions obtained by the Topological Optimization Method (MOT)
