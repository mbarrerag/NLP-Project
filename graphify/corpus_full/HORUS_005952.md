# The National, the Revolution, Autonomy: Cuba Dances by Julio García Espinosa

In the very first years of the Cuban Revolution, the discussions concerning the directions of the new government took place, to a large extent, in the cultural and artistic production areas. In this article, one of the first films produced by ICAIC: Cuba Baila, by Julio García Espinosa. Through it and the debates that frame it, we can observe the very first discussions about the new revolutionary national narratives. This will be done from the perspective of the sociology of Pierre Bourdieu’s culture
