# ANTIGONA/PENTILEA/MEDEA. OTHER REWRITTENS OF THE SCENE TODAY

Reflections on the notion of identity as well as its representations in ancient theatre and its rewritings according to the times. The authors examine in particular the importance of the genre, the evolution of the main female figures of Greek literature as Antigona or Mesalina, but also the image of mythical heroines in modern and contemporary societies
