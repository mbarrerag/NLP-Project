# Lead 210 and polonium 210 measures in human consumption water and waste water

In Colombia, the lack of regular research and specific regulations on the existence of radioactive materials of natural origin (NORM) has resulted in widespread ignorance of the extent of this problem in the country. 14 water measurements (12 human consumption water and 2 waste water) were carried out in four departments (Antioquia, Chocó, Caldas and Tolima) in the country, with the aim of determining the presence of 210Pb and 210Po in each of these samples after applying a radiochemical process to be able to measure them in a LSC (Liquid Centelle Contador); only the M10 sample, taken in the municipality of Samana-Caldas, presents a content of 210Pb that exceeds the international limit set by the World Health Organization which is 0.1 Bq/L. To consume water that exceeds the limit for a long time can lead to health problems such as cancer. This study establishes a methodology for NORM material measurements ( 210Pb and 210Po) in water of human consumption and residual water. .
