# Factors affecting the controls implemented in construction works, to avoid accidents at heights

This paper addresses the factors that affect the effectiveness of accident prevention and control measures in activities with the height component for construction of buildings in Bogotá. The relevance of the study lies in the high incidence of accidents in this sector, which poses serious challenges for occupational safety and the welfare of workers. The main objective is to identify the elements that influence the effectiveness of prevention and control strategies in jobs in heights by determining the most implemented prevention measures, evaluating the knowledge and compliance of safety regulations by workers, and identifying habitual unsafe practices. .
