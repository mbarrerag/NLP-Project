# STUDY OF THE ESSENTIAL OIL OBTAINED FROM elaphondra QUINQUENERVIS RECOLECTED IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The distillation yield of the AE was 0.2% (p/p). 32 components were identified in relative quantities greater than 0.1%. The majority compounds identified were germacrene D (21.2%), ß-pinene (21.1%), sabinene (9.8%), a-pinene (6.9%), trans-ß-caryophyllene (5.2%), lemonene (4.6%), ß-cubenene (3.6%), a-humulene (2.6%), valencene (2.6%) and d-amorfen (2.6%). This work constitutes the first report of the chemical composition of the AE of the species E. quinquenervis
