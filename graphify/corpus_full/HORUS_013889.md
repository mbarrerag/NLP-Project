# OBSERVATORY "INQUIRY AND REPAIR OF THE DAMAGE WITH RESTAURATIVE APPROACH IN THE CONTEXT OF MACROVIICTIMIZATION"

The Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP) is the judicial component of the Integral System for Peace, whose objective is to investigate, judge and punish the serious violations of human rights and breaches of the IHL committed due to the internal armed conflict in Colombia, both by state agents, and by members of the FARC-EP. The duty to investigate, judge and punish those who are responsible for such massiveity of facts, as to ensure the rights of the victims of these events requires the adoption of a methodology of investigation proper to the macrocriminality contexts in which the international crimes to investigate took place. Therefore, the application of the traditional model of investigation and judicialization used by the ordinary criminal jurisdiction, based on a retributive and punitive conception of justice, excluding the victims. The closest experience and that has addressed the same problems addressed by the JEP, was the jurisdiction of Justice and Peace, in a systematic way and in a need of the Prosecutors of the Unit of Justice and Peace of the Attorney General Office adopted a methodology of grouping of cases with the aim of investigating the crimes being no longer attributable to individuals in specific, but to structures and to the macro-organized, in a systematic way and in the case. research and judgment, based on the premise that given the massivity of facts and actors involved it is impossible to study each of the events that took place in the context of the armed conflict (JEP, 2018), which makes it imperative to select events of outstanding gravity and with a representative character, in which the victims of cases that were not selected can be represented. (Rojas & Merchán, 2023) In view of the above, it is of vital importance that from civil society and academia, follow up on the decisions taken by JEP at its different levels, with the aim of making an evaluation regarding the methodology of investigation and instruction of the macro-cases opened up to this moment, in order to determine that in truth their judicial work is oriented to the satisfaction of the rights of the victims in restorative justice key, and not only to a justice based on punitive results.
