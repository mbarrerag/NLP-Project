# A CONJUGED MODEL APPLICATION

In Bayesian Statistics, conjugated models allow updating the a priori belief in the occurrence of an event of interest and infer about future parameters and observations through a subsequent distribution belonging to a family of known distributions. In this context, a Shiny application is presented that seeks to reduce the gap between theory and practice in teaching and learning normal conjugated models and discrete conjugated models, in the Poisson and binomial cases. Through this tool, users can simulate a priori distributions by entering their respective parameters and simulating data distributions by entering parameters or loading real data
