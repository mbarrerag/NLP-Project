# MODELATION OF POLLUTION NIVELS IN A SONG RIVER TREATMENT, BY ASSOLLING LATICE-BOLTZMANN METHODS AND FINAL DIFFERENCES

The modeling of flows and contaminants faces great challenges ranging from the definition of complex geometries to the convergence of the algorithm and the determination of the parameters of the model. The method presented here addresses this problem through a joint application of Lattice-Boltzmann methods and finite differences. The numerical solution of the equations of Navier-Stokes and advection-diffusion allows simulating the levels of dispersion of the spatial temporal pollution in a hydric source. The method solved the equations of Navier-Stokes through the proposal of Lattice Boltzmann, which provides the vectorial field of fluid speeds. The solution of the diffusion-advection equation by finite difference requires this known field of speed. The solution encoded in C++, first digitizes the map, label each map pixel as fluid or solid. The code uses the digital image to configure the geometry of the flow zone, then simulating the dynamics of the fluid and concentration of the pollutant.
