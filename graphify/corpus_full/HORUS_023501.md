# PHITOCHEMICAL ASSESSMENT AND ANTIOXIDING CAPACITY OF WATER HASS (MERICANA MILL.) RESIDUE RESIDUE CULTURED IN COLOMBIA

From the exploitation of avocado (Persea americana Mill., var Hass), a series of residues are generated mainly by epicarpium and seed. It is known that these residues have secondary metabolites type phenols that are highlighted by their antioxidant capacity. The objective of this research was to make an approximation to the phytochemical characterization of the agroindustrial residues of the Hass avocado that allow to infer a potential exploitation of them. For the fulfillment of this objective, the phytochemical profile was determined by presumptive chemical tests, the total presence of phenols (CTF) and the antioxidant potential by measuring the capacity of elimination of radical DPPH (CERDPPH), in epicarpium extracts, seed coating and seed of Hascates grown under conditions of the Cauca Valley, Colombia. The CTF and CERDPPH of the extracts obtained, were determined colorimetrically in a microplate reader (Biotek ELx800).
