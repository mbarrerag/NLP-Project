# "Factors associated with neurological outcome, after endovascular management, in patients with cerebral aneurysms, retrospective cohort"

The cerebral aneurysms are a complex pathology that affects cerebral vasculature, these can be asymptomatic or can cause cerebral bleeding with extensive clinical involvement or even death. Currently, the endovascular management is the first line of treatment for this type of lesions. Different factors related to the neurological outcome have been described in patients undergoing endovascular embolization of aneurysms, within which sociodemographic, clinical, morphological factors of the aneurysm and procedure are found. The importance of these analyses lies in the fact that they are relevant factors for determining the appropriate treatment for a given patient. Therefore, according to the above mentioned, the study is proposed, which aims to identify factors associated with the neurological outcome after the endovascular management in patients with cerebral aneurysms, by the group of neurointerventionism, in the National University Hospital of Colombia between January 1, 2017 and December 31, 2023. An analytical study of the retrospective type of the observational type will be carried out, based on the institutional clinical histories, where the initial, the clinical classification will be performed and the clinical procedure will be performed. embolization, including sociodemographic, clinical, morphological aneurysm, and procedure factors.
