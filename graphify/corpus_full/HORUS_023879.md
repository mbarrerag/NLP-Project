# MULECULAR CHARACTERIZATION WITH ISSR MARCHERS OF THE COLLECTION OF CITRICS OF THE UNIVERSITY OF CLANES.

Contextualization: citrus fruits are one of the most cultivated tree species in the world. They have a great economic importance because of their production is greater than any other fruit. Empty of knowledge: despite the high demand for citrus fruits in Colombia, very little is known about origin and genetic diversity. Likewise, no studies have been carried out on the molecular characterization of citrus varieties from the collection of the University of the Llanos, which are important to implement strategies for conservation and potential use of genetic resources. Purpose of the study: to evaluate the genetic diversity of four citrus varieties (Naranja Tangelo, Naranja Valencia, Mandarina Arrayana and Limón Castilla), established at the University of the Llanos with seven ISSR primers
