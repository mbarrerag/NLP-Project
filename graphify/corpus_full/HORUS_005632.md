# Diagnosis and proposal of modernization of energy in Non-Interconnected Zones: Case of Isla Fuerte, Bolívar

This research paper addresses the diagnosis of the provision of energy service in the Non-Interconnected Areas of Colombia, with a specific approach in the case of Isla Fuerte, Bolívar. Through a comprehensive analysis, the current conditions of energy supply in the region are examined, highlighting the limitations and challenges it faces. The proposal identifies the significant dependence on non-renewable sources and assesses the feasibility of integrating sustainable and clean energy sources. Based on the diagnosis, a comprehensive modernization strategy is proposed, designed to improve the efficiency and quality of energy service on the island. The proposal is based on the implementation of renewable technologies, such as solar and wind energy, and the optimization of existing infrastructure. It seeks to provide reliable and affordable access to energy for the local community, while reducing the environmental impact associated with energy generation and consumption. This study aims to contribute to the sustainable development of Isla Fuerte and provide valuable perspectives for the improvement of the provision of energy service in other Non-Interconnected Areas of Colombia
