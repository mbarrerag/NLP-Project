# Less Crime, Better Justice

The construction of criminal law, as a branch and discipline of law that seeks to judge and correct the conducts that are relevantly reprehensible by society, gives the possibility to analyze if the way in which it is being judged is the right one. But in order to reach this conclusion it is necessary to know how this process is carried out and therefore it is important to know each component of the criminal process, which are: the penal system that is applied (accusatory, inquisitive or mixed), the forms of evidence and the crimes typified and to determine if the latter are effective in the face of the behaviors that are questioned. Moreover, the purpose of the group is not to be able to study and investigate processes within the margin of action of criminal law, but also to be able to think about other ways to solve the conflicts such as the Restorative Justice, being this kind of judgment our central axis of work. Also with the group of Less Crimes More Justice seeks with this process to give to the students the possibility to study and investigate processes within the criminal law.
