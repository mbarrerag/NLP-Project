# Proposal for an internal audit model for cervical cancer program: Case study IPS Unisalud, located in the city of Bogotá during 2022.”

The general objective of this final work was to design an internal audit model, to evaluate the programme for the early detection of cervical cancer in the IPS Unisalud, located in the city of Bogotá. The type of study was descriptive with a quantitative approach. The design of the final work was non-experimental. The research procedure consisted of four phases: bibliographic review, data collection, data analysis and design of the audit model. The final work concluded that the IPS Unisalud lacked an internal audit model that would allow it to evaluate the functioning of the cervical cancer programme. Therefore, a model was proposed that included the organizational philosophy, the scope, methodology and management of the key processes for the programme. The proposed model was based on the national and international guidelines for the prevention and control of cervical cancer, as well as on the principles of process management and internal audit.
