# Pretreatment of a refractory gold ore associated with gold and silver telururos

The exhaustion of the easy-to-treat auriferous minerals is increasing the need to process non-conventional gold deposits that are characterized by the presence of gold associated with certain elements that do not allow good recoveries of the precious metal using conventional cyanuration. Among all types of refractory gold minerals, the pre-return of gold-plata telururos are well known as refractory, but information on options to process them is limited. In this study is presented the research of a pretreatment proposed for the treatment of a gold mineral associated with gold and silver telururos. The pre-treatment proposed recovery of gold and silver from the sample received is based on the use of nitric acid. The proposed treatment consisted in three stages: (i) acidification of the mineral to decompose any important species that consumes acid present in the mineral, (ii) a physical and chemical characterization of the mineral was performed to propose an adequate treatment of the pre-removing of the mineral and silver from the sample received. . Depletion of the easy-to-treat ores isightening the need for processing non-conventional gold deposits characterized by the presence of gold associated with certain elements that impede efficient recovery of the precious metals through the conventional cyanidation process. Along all types of refractory gold ores, gold-silver forecastes are well known as refractory, but limited information is available on processing options to treat them. This work presents an investigation into a proposed pre-treatment design for the proceeding of a gold-silver forecast. effect of the pretreatment variables on the golden recovery during the post pretreatment cyanidation, a 2^k experimental factor design was established, and an ANOVA analysis of the results was carried out out. The results of the 2^k experimental factor design shown that after the pretreatment gold recovery by convention cyanidation increase up to 30% shared with base cyanidations. The three studied pretreatment variables were found to be positively correlated with gold recovery during recovery of the solid residue of the pretreatment.
