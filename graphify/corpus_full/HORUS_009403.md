# AN ONTOLOGY IN CIVIL ENGINEERING TO MODELLING THE EXPERIMENTAL KNOWLEDGE

A key aspect of Civil Engineering is experimental knowledge as protocols. These Protocols are developed in laboratories to establish the characteristics of different materials; however, its documentation is limited to natural language descriptions. This paper is one of the first investigations that propose a way to model the ex-perimental protocols in Civil Engineering as semantic representations. In this way, it can be interpreted not only by humans but also by software agents. In or-der to achieve this goal, this work develops its own ontology domain, in conjunc-tion with other existing ontologies to offer an unambiguous representation, which allows reusing the experimental knowledge
