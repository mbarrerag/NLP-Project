# DYNAMIC CAPACITIES IN RESEARCH GROUPS AND ITS IMPACT ON INTELLIGENT CITY

This publication aims to evaluate the relationship between the dynamic capacities of Manizales research groups and the dimensions of Manizales Smart City, based on five research questions: what variables allow to measure the level of dynamic capacities in research groups ?, what is the level of dynamic capacities in research groups in the city of Manizales ?, what variables allow to determine Manizales as an intelligent city ?, what is the current state of intelligent city dimensions in Manizales from the perspective of research groups ? and what is the relationship between dynamic capacity variables and the dimensions of Manizales Smart City ? This research has both academic and practical implications. From the academic point of view, it seeks to fill a knowledge gap found in the literature review regarding the scarcity of theoretical and empirical work related to the application of dynamic capacities in groups or research organizations from a strategic approach and its impact R+D+i in intelligent city environments. From the social sciences, surveys related to dynamic capacities and smart city were applied to specific research groups, both from the academic community and research centers in general, to the research actors involved in the research network and to help with intelligent research
