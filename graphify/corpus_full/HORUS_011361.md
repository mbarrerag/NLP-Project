# WAYS FOR THE ACREDITATION OF THE UNIVERSITY PROFESORATE: EMPATIC LIST, CONFIDENCE AND DIVERSITY

This study evaluates the influence of empathic listening of teachers and the diversity in the classroom with the role of mediator of trust. The purpose responds to the scarcity of studies on these relationships in the academic context of university education, especially in what has to do with the teaching accreditation and the use of these variables in Latin American classrooms. Thus, hypothesis on the direct influence of empathic listening on diversity and on the mediation of trust in this relationship were evaluated through a Model of Structural Equations using the technique of the Partially Squared Minimums, in a sample of 107 Colombian teachers of higher education. The results of this study indicate that empathic listening positively and significantly influences diversity and, moreover, point out that trust is a mechanism that mediates in a partial but significant way, the relationship between the two variables. These results imply that diversity in the classroom is encouraged when teachers improve their ability to listen to students emphatically.
