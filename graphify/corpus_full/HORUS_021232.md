# Design of a Mach Zehnder integrated optical device for optoelectronic oscillators

This project proposes the design of a Mach Zehnder device in integrated technology as a basic element for possible implementation in optoelectronic oscillators and their application in communications. This design will be carried out using the Photonic Solutions software from Synopsis. This type of development is of great importance in our country as they provide the opportunity to contribute to the strengthening of communication networks in the remote areas of Colombia and also understand the importance of the study of modern telecommunications technologies
