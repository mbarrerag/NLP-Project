# Power and electricity generation from methane

Compared to other nonrenewable energy sources, methane (CH4) is an abundant, affordable, cost-competitive, and low carbon footprint feedstock for electricity production. Energy contained in gas and biogas is converted to power and resources in a wide number of devices. Complying with the Paris Agreement implies increasing efficiencies and introducing a real behavioral change in consumers to diminish energy waste, ensuring the stability and flexibility of the electricity supply chain, and taking advantage of renewable energy sources to decarbonize the world economy. By 2050, an annual abatement of 36.9Gton CO2 should be achievable; therefore electricity generation should be expanded threefold with renewables. Market evolution and forecast performed by different organizations suggest that fossil fuels are far from being eliminated from the energy basket in emerging economies. Hence, environmental and technical advantages of the available CH4 sources should be used to accelerate the transition of our society to greener energy sources
