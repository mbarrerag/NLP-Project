# Conflict and peace in the border and cross-border regions of Colombia 2016-2022

The strategic interdisciplinary alliance composed by the research groups _x0091_Border Studies_x0092_ (Amazon Headquarters), _x0091_Borders in movement: studies of the sociocultural diversity of the Greater Caribbean_x0092_ (La Paz Headquarters), _x0091_International Relations and Global Affairs- RIAG_x0092_ (Bogotá Headquarters) and _x0091_Nation Region and international relations of the Caribbean_x0092_ (Caribbean Headquarters), as well as by the Border Thinking Center, the Laboratory of Territorial Peace and the Border Research Seeder where researchers from the Bogotá, Medellín, Caribbean, Amazonia and La Paz Headquarters converge, seeks to evaluate what happened in Colombia and especially in its border regions and cross-border contact areas in the process of seeking peace and the end of the armed conflict, in particular with the implementation of the Agreements for the Termination of the Conflict with the FARC-EP from 2016. The project aims to analyze the conflict and violence in Colombia from its borders, trying to go beyond the conventional analyses and the national framework
