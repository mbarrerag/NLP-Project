# From evidence of madness, to a tool for historical research. Archivist intervention of the Clinical Stories of the Mental Hospital of Antioquia, 20th century.

The Medical Records are the Bureaucratic archives of Antioquia Mental Hospital that can be used, on the one hand, to illustrate the medical practices and social responses to illnesses rated as mental and to monitor the effectiveness thereof and the patient ́s clinical evolution, and on the other, to understand the life history of each individual supply from the disease, as well as the implementation of technologies and processes that marked a millennia in mental health on a global scale. Taking into account the record comparison a Documentary Collection that is addressed as a breakdown of the geographical importance in the study of medical practices and social and political life in the Antioquia, it was necessary to implement and standardise the archival process defined in Colombia, a focus on the medical practice and regulatory in the region, The Clinical Stories are the bureaucratic register of the Mental Hospital of Antioquia that allows, on the one hand, to show the medical practices and social responses to the diseases considered mental and to follow up on their effectiveness and clinical evolution of the patient, and on the other hand, to understand the history of life of each individual transversalized by the disease, as well as by the implementation of techniques and procedures that marked a milestone in mental health worldwide. Considering that they constitute a Documentary Fund that is considered of great importance for the study of medical practices and the social and political life of the Antioqueña region, it was necessary to apply and normalize the archival intervention processes proposed here, focused on contributing to the preservation of the documentary heritage and improving accessibility for the public consultation, through archival processes and instruments elaborated according to the current regulations in Colombia. As well as the creation of a proposal for dissemination, based on the models of the narrative of the construction of the subject and the way to interpret the documentary unit from a different perspective, which allows the proposal of a non-traditional model, oriented to a scientific disclosure of documents, which it is possible, achieving a field of the difference of the different forms of the knowledge of the subject
