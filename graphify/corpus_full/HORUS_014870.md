# EFFICIENCY OR EQUALITY?

Government action in view of achieving objectives defined by the political sphere is carried out through public policies. Activities related to the formulation, design, implementation and evaluation of public policies are also framed in a paradigm or a specific conception of public management in general. During the twentieth century, two major concepts of public management can be considered: bureaucratic management and management. The choice of one or another of these conceptions is not without implications in terms of values transmitted to society or principles inherent in its deployment. Thus, equality or efficiency can be promoted, which has implications for human rights
