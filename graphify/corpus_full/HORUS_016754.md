# CHARACTERIZATION OF ALTERNATIVE EXPLORATION IN THE CHILES VOLCAN COLOMBO-ECUATORIAN FRONTER.

The Volcán Chiles (VC), located in the collombo-ecuatoriana border, 24km from the cities of Ipiales and Tulcán, is included in the Chiles-Cerro Negro volcanic complex (CVCCN). Associated with the VC there is an active geothermal system of volcanic field type with superficial manifestations that include thermal springs, hydrothermal alterations, fumaroles and, apparently, minor explosive eruptions. The volcano is built in rocks of the Dagua and Diabásico complexes, and is composed by at least four episodes massive lava flows, whose compositions vary between basaltic, andesitas and dacitas. Currently, the geothermal project in the area is in prefeasibility stage, and the conceptual model of the thermal analysis includes the mapping of the lava flows, the study of the thermal sources, the structural characterization, the record of historical activity, and studies on the superficial geology and the geometric characteristics of the volcanic.. The preliminary results of the research will be the basis on which the samples will be correlated with the different cartographic lava flows, the secondary mineral phases will be quantified and the nature of the magmatic and hydrothermal processes and their relationship with the geothermal system will be analyzed
