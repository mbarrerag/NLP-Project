# SPATIAL COVERAGE ANALYSIS OF PUBLIC TRANSPORT BUS-STOPS THROUGH A CITIZEN PERCEPTION STUDY. CASE STUDY: MANIZALES ¿ COLOMBIA

Abstract The development of urban public transport systems, at present, has turned its interest in increasing operation features, trying to minimize travel times and mobilize the largest number of users, by increasing the operational speed, renewal of the vehicular fleet, increasing frequencies, and others issues. However, in addition to restructuring the operating distance and location of the stops, this process of increased operability has paid little attention to the perception of citizens about the location, coverage and possible services of these stop systems. Creating to a large extent, users disinterest to use of system, promotion of the use of other means of transport, or in the least of the cases, enforcement to make stops in unauthorized sites. Taking into account the above, this research proposes an analysis of the population and area coverage, based on the citizens' perception of the physical and operational variables of interest when implementing a set of stops in the transport system. With the aim of generating an environment of inclusion, with a broader vision of the ideal conditions for the inclusion of the set of stops. Within the research process, the structuring of a statistical sampling is necessary, which allows us to characterize the population of public transport systems. Manizales city in Colombia is taken as the study area through its 12 districts and 378,000 inhabitants. In addition, a geographic accessibility analysis is carried out, to assess the access condition in the average travel time of users who must travel somewhere to make use of the system; This evaluation model uses digital tools, type GIS, through geostatistical models that guarantee an adequate development of the calculations. The hypothesis of the study is that the coverage of the stops of the public transport system depends, to some degree, on the perception that users have of the system as a whole. That is why this research presents a methodology for diagnosing the public transport shutdown system in Manizales, in order to propose concrete improvements that increase the geospatial coverage of the system. Geostatistical models would be used to understand the impacts that would be made in implementing the proposed improvements of the system, taking into account the current perception of the performance of the transport system. Coverage, Public transport, bus-stop, accessibility, users perception
