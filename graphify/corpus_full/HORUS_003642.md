# COMPARISON BETWEEN THE PONK USED BY SOLITARY BISHES COLLETES AND MEGACHILE, AND EUSOCIALS TRIGONE (HYMENOPTERA, COLLETIDAE, MEGACHILIDAE AND APPIDIES WITH REGARD) IN VERDE URBAN AREAS

The cities are heterogeneous spaces characterized by fragmented green areas and asphalted surfaces that can house high diversity of bees, essential pollinators of native and introduced plants. As well as various species of eusocial bees of the families Apidae and Halictidae, species of solitary bees of Colletidae and Megachilidae are affected by urban growth. Especially these latter may be more susceptible to urbanization processes that affect their diet. To compare the floral resources of two genera of solitary bees with those of a eusocial genus, monthly samplings with jama in green areas of six urban parks of the Aburra Valley Metropolitan Area (Antioquia, Colombia), during a year. In the laboratory, the captured bees were prepared for palinological analysis with standard techniques, and were mounted on plates for the counting of 500 grains by XIV Colloquio of the Andean and Caribbean section of the International Union for the Study of Social Insects IUSI 95 by microscopia of light.
