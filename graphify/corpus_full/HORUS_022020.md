# The Hardy-Littlewood Inequalities in Sequence Spaces

The investigation of the relation between the sums of coefficients of bilinear forms on c0 × c0 and their supremum norms was initiated in 1930 by J.E. Littlewood. In 1934, in a joint paper with G.H. Hardy, Littlewood extended the previous results to lp spaces. The main goal of these notes is to present modern proofs of m-linear versions of the results of Hardy and Littlewood and the state-of-the-art of the subject. We also illustrate an application in a combinatorial game called Gale–Berlekamp switching game
