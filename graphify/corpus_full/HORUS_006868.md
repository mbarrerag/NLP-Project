# Teaching proposal for the musical initiation of a Symphonic band: rhythmic matrices of the Pasillo, Bambuco and Cumbia as elements of approximation

This research explores the approach of developing a musical didactic guide, aimed at the initiation of the musical reading of children of early age who start their musical process from the age of 5 in the processes of the symphonic bands of initiation. The guide entitled: didactic proposal for the musical initiation of a symphonic band: rhythmic matrices of the corridor, bambuco and cumbia as elements of approximation, has as objective and result of the research process, providing an accessible and effective tool to introduce children in the musical literacy and also, to address the basic musical concepts; similarly, to address the reading in some of the characteristic rhythms of Colombian music such as the corridor, bambuco and cumbia. The guide integrates from the pedagogical principles and educational methodologies in music of proposals by Orff, Willems and Kodaly, interactive activities, musical games and exercises to promote active participation and play learning.
