# VEGETAL PIGMENTS AS PH INDICATORS

In plants, mixtures of pigments with different colors are found, for example, chlorophyll-a (intense green), chlorophyll-b (green), carotenes (light yellow) and xanthophils (orange yellow) in different proportions. Extraction and identification of these pigments is interesting for the study and knowledge of their properties [1]. Some of them are potent pH indicator agents. Therefore, in this practice it is encouraged that groups of students investigate and choose a plant source that they consider appropriate to obtain pigments (which will be worked in crude) as pH indicators
