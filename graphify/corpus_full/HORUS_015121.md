# PEACE AGREEMENT, REINCORPORATION AND PEACE BUILDING FROM A TOURISM INITIATIVE: LAND GRATA ECOTOURS

This text provides a thoughtful analysis of the relevance and progress in the implementation of the Peace Agreement signed in 2016 between the Colombian government and the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia. Is the People's Army (FARC-EP) and its link with the social mobilizations of 2019 and 2021. Therefore, some ideas and strategies are offered that seek to contribute to overcoming or deescalating the social and political crisis facing Colombia today (2021-2022). This analysis also draws on the testimony of a former FARC-EP combatant and signatory of the peace agreement, Mr. Germán Gómez, who has been leading an ecological tourism project in the department of La Guajira since 2017 and up to the present day, called Tierra Grata Ecotours. This testimony illustrates a case study and an experience by a protagonist actor of the ups and the process of re-entry into civilian life, as part of the implementation of the Peace Agreement. Indeed, his testimony allows extrapolating a series of learnings and recommendations that could inspire strategies useful for more or less similar cases, or in general, for a country that is mobilized among other causes
