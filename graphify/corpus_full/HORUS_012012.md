# NUmeric TEST OF EXISTENCE AND UNICITY FOR SOLUTIONS OF A DEPREDING-PRESS SPACE-TEMPORARY SYSTEM

We propose a numerical method that ensures both the existence and local uniqueness of a solution of a parabolic system of partial differential equations that describes the temporal space iteration between a predatory population and another prey population. The method consists in the construction of a recursive succession of approximate solutions of a linear modification of the original system. The convergence of such succession is demonstrated by considering the regularity results of solutions of linear partial differential equations and Sobolev’s boxing. The numerical tests verify that the proposed succession is convergent to the model solution and illustrate the behavior of the two species in time and space
