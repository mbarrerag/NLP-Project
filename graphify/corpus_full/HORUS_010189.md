# PROPOSAL FOR USE OF DISCUSSION IN SUSTAINABLE ENERGY RESEARCH PROJECTS

This document presents the experience report of the impact of the methodology of debate within the process of learning university students for the acquisition of new knowledge in innovation and sustainable energy for students of master's degree in electrical engineering in San Andrés Isla. For this purpose, groups are carried out to which they are assigned a topic related to current energy issues, then they conduct discussion sessions following the methodology proposed by the University of Oxford and finally the students qualify the performance of each group on the four main axes: style, content, strategy and critical thinking. At the end of the course the students respond a questionnaire evaluating their competencies in the above mentioned axes and feedback conditions of improvement
