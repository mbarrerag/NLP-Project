# THE ENTIRE PERDURABILITY OF SOCIAL RESEARCH: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND RESEARCH PERSPECTIVES

The systematic review of literature presented in this article has as its purpose, to demonstrate evolution, relevance, importance and research perspectives of the business sustainability of social enterprises. This bibliographical study, which followed the PRISMA declaration, used the Publish or Perish software for the tracing of literature in WoS, Scopus and Google Scholar, as well as inclusion and exclusion criteria that allowed the elimination of duplicates and the selection of documents by relevance and subareas; while, the use of tools such as Rstudio, ToS, Gephi and Bibliometrix, made it possible to analyze the evolution and importance of bibliographic material, as well as the identification of collaborative networks and research perspectives. The results show a growth of academic productivity and the existence of three great perspectives that revolve around the problems and opportunities of the business sustainability of these enterprises. Likewise, research gaps that open up future works were identified
