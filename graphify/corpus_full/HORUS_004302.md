# BAYESIAN APPLICATION OF A MODEL AGE, PERIOD AND COHORT (APC) FOR DIABET MORTALITY IN COLOMBIAN MEN

This study aims to evaluate from the data of mortality due to diabetes in Colombian men, the effect of the three temporal variables age, period and cohort on mortality rates. The models of regression Poisson and Binomial Negative with which mortality rates are obtained are proposed, then the adjustment will be made to each model using the Gamma Inversa and SBeta2 distributions as a priori distributions for the parameters of variance of temporal effects age, period and cohort. Adjustment comparisons are made between models through the Deviance Information Criterion (DIC), and finally the prediction results of these are evaluated by calculating the root of the Middle Quadratic Error (MSE) between the real and predictive values for the last period, which will not be taken into account in the model adjustments
