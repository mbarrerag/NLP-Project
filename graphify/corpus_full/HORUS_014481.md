# Sustainability strategies for the material cultural heritage of the historic center of Manizales in the context of its dynamics of social appropriation.

The aim of this research is to propose a strategic framework for the sustainability of the material cultural heritage of the Historic Centre of Manizales based on its appropriation dynamics; understanding this as a cultural system in which actors, heritages and interrelations converge between them that determine a territorial identity. Analyzing the different territorial political configurations, cultural products and services, the challenges of sustainability of cultural goods, the different policies that allow or exclude the social appropriation of the heritage, the transmission and patrimonialization phenomena of practices and knowledge to have a contemporary understanding of that territory that allows to develop an exercise of prospective order and with a participatory character for the sustainability of the historical center from its material cultural heritage and the communities that form the cultural system of this one. The development of this one had four (4) moments from which the information is collected, (1) bibliographic review that allowed to identify the different conceptualizations around the cultural systems, the heritage and its dynamics of appropriation, (2) construction of the socio-space network of the Historical Centre of Manizales that allowed to identify the cultural system of this through the development of focal groups (3) development of interviews to actors of the public, private and civil society, thus achieving the integral diversity of the objectives of the structural diversity
