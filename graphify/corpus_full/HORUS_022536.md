# STUDY AND GROWTH OF SWEET ENGINE FIELDS DEPOSITATED WITH MAGNETRON SPUTTERING RF FOR APPLICATIONS IN SOLARES CELDS

In this work, solar multilayer cells of GaN/InxGa1-xN(N)/InxGa1-xN(P) were deposited on single-crystalline substrates of silicon (Si) (100) by means of the technique of cathodic spraying with RF magnetron in an atmosphere of argon and nitrogen, using an Indian (In) and Galium (GaN) target of high purity (99.99%). Variations in power power from 25 to 50 watts were made, with which the content of (x) in the InxGa1-xN ternal alloy was changed. Experimental conditions were correlated with the optical, structural and surface chemical properties of thin films of IngaN. By means of the measurements of optical absorption, the change of the prohibited band energy (Eg.) was determined, the analysis of X-ray diffraction (XRD) of the solar cells InGaN revealed a polycry structure with peaks belonging to the cubic and hexagonal phases. The morphology of the surface of the thin films was analyzed
