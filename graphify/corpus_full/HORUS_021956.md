# Pharmacovigilance in Latin America: Perspectives from its protagonists.

The pharmacovigilance at the global level has been focused on the detection, evaluation and monitoring of adverse effects of medicines, but has expanded to cover many more problems related to them. In Latin America, this discipline has not had a syncronic development in the countries of the region. This qualitative research seeks to know from a semantic and epistemological perspective the scope and achievements of pharmacovigilance, from the individual point of view of representatives of various Latin American programs. Its main objective is to categorize the perceptions of the protagonists of pharmacovigilance programmes in Latin America on some aspects related to the scope and achievements applicable in the discipline. For this purpose, semi-structured interviews were conducted with 16 people who are protagonists of some pharmacovigilance programmes in Latin American countries addressing different problems. It was evidenced that there is a desire to harmonize and standardize the concepts used in this discipline of the hands of international regulatory entities.
