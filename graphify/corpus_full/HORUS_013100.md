# The technological rationality as the analytical matrix of the present

The presence and massive use of technological devices is an inescapable element of the contemporary world. This phenomenon has been the object of multiple reflections that coincide in that the technodigital and the algorithmic occupies a central place in the understanding of contemporary societies. Likewise, a technological imperative has been extended that, in a silent way, has been lodged in the daily life of the subjects producing a series of generalized transformations of the existence that lead us to reflect on our present as a form of historical decipherment. From this form of problematization we try to identify the discontinuities produced by the technodigital and the algorithmic ones constructed by the reading of Foucault, configured by five axes (power, truth, knowledge, time and subjective). We propose that we attend to the emergence and materialization of a new rationality, that is, a set of daily practices in which the technogital and the algorithmic ones has a privileged place as a form of orientation of existence and subjective configuration. .
