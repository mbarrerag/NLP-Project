# SYNTHESIS, FUNCTIONALIZATION AND CHARACTERIZATION OF SILICA NANOPARTICLES AND FULLERENOL: POTENTIAL PLATFORMS FOR CELL PENETRATION VEHICLES

Several biological barriers are generally responsible for the limited delivery of cargoes at the cellular level. Previously, we developed a new family of nanobioconjugates capable of cell penetration and endosomal escape by immobilizing the Buforin II (BUF-II) peptide and the OmpA protein on magnetite NPs. Here, we propose to extend this approach to silica NPs (SNPs) and fullerenol as nanostructured supports for the conjugation of these potent cell-penetrating agents, to evaluate whether the new nanostructured supports improve the translocation capacity and endosomal escape
