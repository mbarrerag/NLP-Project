# Selection of minimum set of indicators for agricultural sustainability assessments at plot level

Some authors raise concerns regarding validity, trust and transparency when selecting indicators in agricultural sustainability analyses. In this regard, a series of selection criteria have been proposed for sustainability assessments at the farm, region, country or planet scale. However, evaluations at the scale of plot or experimental unit require, in addition to adapting these criteria or generating new ones, the construction of a selection methodology. The aim of this study was therefore to construct a selection framework for the minimum set of indicators that will be part of agricultural sustainability analyses at the scale of plot or experimental unit. A hierarchical order of indicators, composed of crude, base and central indicators, were established; the latter form the minimum set of indicators (MIC). Subsequently, the selection procedures and criteria were established, consisting of: mandatory, non-compulsory, non-compulsory, alternative and correlation criteria. To evaluate the proposed selection framework, the results of a study of fertilization in tomato under greenhouse were used
