# Conceptual study with geostatistic simulation to evaluate rare earth exploration targets (REE) in the department of Antioquia, Colombia.

Conceptual studies are generally the first economic valuation of a mining project. In this context, the main objective of this work is to formulate a technical and economic study of the potential viability of mineral resources of rare earth elements (Rare Earth Elements, REE) in the department of Antioquia by means of geostatistic simulation by rotating bands of the tenor of total rare earth oxides (Total Rare Earth Oxides, TREO) and analysis of modifier factors, to evaluate a detailed prospecting program in the whites or exploration targets. The data of the tenor of REE in Antioquia were constructed during 2004-2007 in the project “geochemical map of the department of Antioquia”. The TreO tenor was processed by the computational tools Isatis, ArcMap, QGIS and geoportals of the National Mining Agency (ANM) and the Colombian Geological Service (SGC).
