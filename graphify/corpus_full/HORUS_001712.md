# Progress and challenges in green, blue and turquoise hydrogen production through the use of carbonous materials

This paper provides a general analysis of the latest scientific and technological advances in the production of blue, green and turquoise hydrogen, describing the origin of its classification, differentiation, advantages and obstacles found in each methodology. It examines the added value of carbonous materials used for the obtaining of hydrogen as an energy vector, highlighting the contribution of their physical-chemical properties in improving the efficiency and yield of production based on the study of the difficulties reported in the studies that make up the state of art, especially for the scaling from laboratory level projects to bank and industrial.
