# Determination of the erodibility of a surface soil of the East Anti-Aqueno by Inderbitzen test.

In recent years the erosive processes have increased in the country as response to the environmental variations and the antropic actions, such as the excessive increase of the large urban centers and their consumption needs. This situation generates consequences that not only affect the stability of the land, but also affect negatively areas such as agriculture. Under this context it is necessary to establish mitigation and remediation measures for the soils susceptible to erosion. However, currently the methodologies used for the direct measurement of the erodibility of the soils of the region are scarce or its implementation is expensive. For this reason it is necessary to implement tests of direct measurement of the erodability that have functioned effectively in other countries, as is the case of the Inderbitzen test. The study presented below was developed on a soil with characteristics of volcanic ash, located in a slope without plant coverage when the Virgin High, of the Municipality of Guarne, Department of Antioquia. The recovered samples were subjected to a soil campaign of laboratory, which included tests of physical, chemical, mineralogy, structural and hydro-me-executable. The scale effect influences the erodibility (K) obtained in the field and in the laboratory, the magnitude of K_laboratorio being the multiplication of a scaler and K_campo. Finally, this research aims to highlight the use of a team that is used in Latin American countries such as Brazil, with the aim of encouraging its application in Colombia, since its simplicity and low cost represents the possibility of being employed in several areas of the country that suffer great erosion processes
