# ANALYSIS OF BIFURCATIONS IN A SYSTEM OF DIFFERENTIAL ECUATIONS MODELS THE PLAGUE OF THE COFFEE BROCHA

Coffee harvest is one of the most important agricultural activities in the world, and is severely affected by the Coffee Berry Borer (CBB) Hypothenemus hampei. This small insect, considered the main coffee pest, reduces the quantity and quality of coffee by drilling in the endosperm to feed and lay its eggs, continuing its life cycle. A strategy to combat this pest is to use biological control agents, such as certain species of ants (Hymenoptera Formicidae). In this talk we develop a system of ordinary nonlinear differential equations to describe the interaction between CBB and an undetermined species of ants. Then, the points of equilibrium of the system are determined and its biological sense is examined as well as stability when the parameter is varied a. This parameter represents the maximum consumption rate of the predator and the parameter of bifurcation is considered in the research of transcritical bifurcations and chair-node. The Sotomayorem is applied to demonstrate the existence of these bifurcations. Finally, portraits of phase of the system for different values of control are finally reduced to conclude the result of the result of a Cto-node
