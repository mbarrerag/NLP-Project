# CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF THE ESSENTIAL OIL OF BACCHARIS TRINERVIS, DE SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The characterization of the AE was carried out in a gas chromatograph coupled to a mass spectrometer. A column with a polar stationary phase (DB-5MS) was used. Individual components were tentatively identified, linking the mass spectra obtained (EI, 70 eV) to those of spectral databases (ADAMS, NIST, Wiley), and comparing their linear retention times with those reported in the scientific literature (2). 57 compounds in the AE were identified in relative quantity > 0.1%. The majority components found in B. trinervis AE were germacren D (13.4%) and karyofilene oxide (9.9%). The AE extraction yield was relatively low (0.07% p/p). The components found in the oil differ from those reported in plants collected in Brazil, in which they were identified as majority components of the AE ß-felandrenus (27.8%) and sabininen (13.3%) (3)
