# DESIGN OF A CHOCOLATE AND PANEL MIXING FOR INSTANT PREPARATION

This work shows a procedure carried out to design a mixture of chocolate and instant preparation panel that can be competitive in the market. For the formulation of a new food, the physical and chemical characteristics and sensory of the raw materials must always be taken into account, since their properties will be reflected in the quality of the finished product.The spray panel has different physical and chemical properties to those of refined sugar (Casas, 2019; Esparza, 2015), since, for the most part, it is obtained through the processes of the raw materials; this causes variability in physical and chemical parameters such as pH, color, humidity, aw, composition of sugars and organic acids (Carrera, 2014; Guerra, 2010; Cobeña, 2016).During the production process and in the storage, the reactions like those of Maillard are presented and this can affect the sensory quality of the final product (Asikin, 2014).The moisture is another essential factor, since the spray panel is naturally hygroscopic. necessary to obtain a homogeneous product, without formation of agglomerations, and which meets sensoryly (Chau, 2013)
