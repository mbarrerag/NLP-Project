# -MORTALITY IN THE OLD. COLOMBIA AND ITS DEPARTMENTS. 2015-2023. - Level and change of mortality, first causes of death. Survival of the older cohorts and lost life expectancy years (APVP). Excess mortality by Covid-19. Mortality of old age in the context of demographic ageing and social development in the territories. Elements for the territorial targeting of health care of old age.

Taking into account that in the country in recent decades demographic ageing is accentuated progressive and irreversible, and that this creates increases in the risks of illness and dying of the cohorts more long-term, and generates an increasing contribution of the elderly to the overall mortality, the study also analyzes the dynamics of four dimensions of mortality recorded in the people aged 60 and over in the country and the departments in two recent quinquenties: i) the trends and differentials of the specific mortality in old age with emphasis on the main causes of death, which achieves a basic view of the problem that serves as a reference for the next analyses of the proposed problem; ii) the dynamics of survival from 60 years old, and the years of life expectancy lost (AEVP) in this population group, indicator that allows to detect the causes of death that change quickly from the level of mortality; iv) the discussion of the social indicators for the Covid-19 in old age, valued from the series of time, thus measuring the strong influence of the pandemic in the decrease of the larger cohorts; iv) the discussion of the difference between the departments of the country and the social indicators in the old age. the dynamics of old age mortality, which achieves innovative contributions that favor the development of research and teaching on the subject
