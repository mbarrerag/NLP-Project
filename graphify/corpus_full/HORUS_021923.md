# Development of a piezoelectric nanogenerator for applications in biomedical sensors.

The use of the PENG (Petzoelectric nanogenerators) as sensors has caused a lot of interest in the last years in various areas of engineering and medicine. To develop these devices it is necessary to use piezoelectric materials. Within the piezoelectric materials that can be synthesized, the ciclonate lead (PZT) is found, one of the most used materials; however, the presence of lead and its impacts both environmental and human health, have increased the interest for materials that do not contain this element in its composition. The zinc oxide (ZnO) is considered an alternative, since it is not toxic and may be subjected to large mechanical deformations during prolonged periods, being both beneficial characteristics for the development of these devices.
