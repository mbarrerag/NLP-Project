# «He had bears and lions and other fierce animals that great lords usually have»: Some traces of animal collecting in the Spanish Romances of Chivalry

This article aims to study how certain cultural practices of royalty and nobility in Spain related to animals are registered in the romances of chivalry, particularly animal collecting, a phenomenon of great importance in European courts. In imitation of the Roman emperors, the possession of wild beasts an exotic animals became a sign of magnificence and social distinction. From the review of the historical testimonies, it was suggested that the Castilian books of chivalry poeticized some ways in which human beings related to animal during the Spanish Modern Age
