# Cooperation and exchange of technology in climate change mitigation. An analysis of opportunities and barriers from the theory of knowledge management through a case study from the Center for Transnational TRAJECTS

The dissertation analyses the opportunities and barriers of cooperation and technology exchange, in the climate change mitigation field, from the organizational knowledge creation process and considering the TRAJECTS case study. The research is interdisciplinary, developing from the integration of the hermeneutic triangulation of the Centre’s documentary and academic literature review together with the information collected through in-depth interviews and focus groups with different TRAJECTS stakeholders, as well as the respective analysis within the framework of knowledge management. As the results, the cooperation and exchange of technology in the case study are characterized, the main opportunities and barriers related to the research constructs are identified, and they are compared between TRAJECTS regional centres located in the so-called Global South and North. The study allows us to conclude that the main opportunities for TRAJECTS lie in the socialization of the Transnational Centre’s network actors, especially from the Global South, and in their articulation to generate contributions around climate change mitigation and just transitions. Besides, the main barriers arise in the hierarchies that can be internalized and transmitted in the operationalization of TRAJECTS measures and in the limitations of South – South cooperation
