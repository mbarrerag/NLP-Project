1.Amblyoma ovale is a tick of the family Ixodidae which parasites mainly carnivorous (domestic and wild) in its adult stages. It is widely distributed in tropical and subtropical regions of America, from Mexico to Argentina, and parasites commonly to domestic canines and human beings entering wooded areas. The cases of human parasitism by this species are frequent, which implies a potential risk given its incrimination as vector of the field Rickettsia parkeri strain of Atlantic Rainforest in Brazil and the finding of this pathogen in ticks (A. oval) collected in Argentina, Belize, Mexico, Nicaragua and Colombia.
