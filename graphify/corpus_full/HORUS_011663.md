# Levels of arsenic in hair and health disorders related to arsenic exposure in the population of Santa Rosa de Cabal, (Risaralda) 2021.

The arsenic (As) is a ubiquitous element and its compounds are known as toxic agents that generate negative effects to human health. Its presence in the environment is associated with natural and/or anthropic sources. In 2017, the presence of As was reported in the Campoalegrito river, the main water source of the municipality of Santa Rosa de Cabal (Risaralda - Colombia), whose origin is natural due to the contribution received by the river from the thermal springs present in its basin. This finding suggested the possible exposure of the population of the municipality to the As through the intake of water consumption. For this reason a cross-sectional study was developed, in 153 people of the region to establish the possible relationship between the presence of As in the environment and its presence in the population. The study included the application of a sociodemographic survey, a clinical evaluation focused on symptoms and characteristic signs of exposure to As and the taking of hair, as biomarker of exposure to As and taking of samples of water of consumption.
