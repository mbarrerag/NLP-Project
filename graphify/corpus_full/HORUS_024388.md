# AUTOMATIC CAPS GENERATION MODEL SIGNATURES FOR AIR IMAGES AND LEARNING

The production of maps is usually carried out manually by experts who restore the information by screen from aerial images, mainly satellite, this constitutes an expensive process in time, human resource and susceptible to errors. After this, the maps so produced go through a review or curing that increases the time necessary to obtain the final result. The creation of maps is a task ubic in engineering, the social sciences, natural and of course in the earth sciences, in which today the mechanism is also used the high availability of the aerial images of satellite and drone quality. The arrival of the techniques of Artificial Intelligence, and specifically, the Deep Learning, has facilitated the creation of maps automatically, whose entry are the aerial images and training masks created in a GIS. However, this procedure is still loaded with the programming languages and computer concepts that can be less used, the use of this technology, the open-ended model, the use of Ply-based model, the development of a script, in three stages, from satellite images or drone to the production of a GIS. These stages are: each form of the training data, the generation model and the open-ended concepts, and the use of the use of this technology. This research aims to shorten the time and facilitate the generation of maps from increasingly available aerial images
