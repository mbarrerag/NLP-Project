# IMPACT OF PROTECTION OF EXCLUSIVE TEST DATA (Decree 2085, 2002) 2012-2019.

The National Institute for Monitoring Drugs and Food (INVIMA) and the System of Drug Prices (Sismed) should improve the quality, relevance, availability and timeliness of the information they produce on medicines. The publication summarizes the findings of the study and complements the research work that IFARMA published in 2012 entitled Impact of 10 years of data protection in medicines in Colombia. The document provides an analysis of the implementation of Decree 2085, with emphasis on the requirements and definitions required for its implementation, as well as on the requests that the INVIMA Review Commission denied during the period covered by the study (2012 ¿2019).
