# INTRODUCTION TO REAL ANALYSIS. CLASSICIZED UPDATE GUIDE

This book arises from the development of the courses of Mathematical Analysis on the real straight taught by the authors during the years 2018 to 2022. Its main objective is to offer the reader an updated exhibition of classic themes in a self-contained way, assuming basic requirements in mathematical foundations and calculation in a variable. The text has been inspired and guided by the exhibition of the already classic book Principles of Mathematical Analysis by Walter Rudin, in addition to its Latin American counterpart Course of Mathematical Analysis vol. 1 by the brazilian author elon lages lima. These texts have been used by generations of mathematicians, and in particular by the authors, to impart this subject. However, the present text proposes to delve into basic ideas through solving problems in subjects related to the subject. These include series sums, particular values of special functions, integrals and notable inequalities, besides the forgotten theorems in the current literature. In this way, we focus on details and applications within the same Classical Real Analysis, but not in the search for generality. This example is reflected in the integral of Riemann plus integrals of Riemann and not integrals of Riegues. The text has numerous results that have been taken from these articles and proposed as guided exercises. The invitation is always to try to solve the problems on its own, and then to consult the articles cited. In this way one step is taken in the formation by entering into the scientific literature and contrasting the reasonings of the same with those explained in each reference. The book is divided into two parts. The first one exposes the basic theory from the axioms of the real numbers to successions of series and functions, going through the basic topology of the metric spaces and the processes of analysis as limits, derived and integral. Some sections within this part are posed as optional material -multiple series, Tannery's Theorem, applications of the integral-, but they continue to make part of the body of the text. The second part (called Projects) delves into parallel topics that can be developed with the tools of the first part and in applications, but which do not constitute a void in the formation if they are not explained in class.
