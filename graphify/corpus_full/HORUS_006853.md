# Evaluation of material produced from the excavations of the ground (MPET) in Bogotá for reintegration into buildings

To address the environmental problem arising from the production of waste from the construction industry in Bogotá, the present research is proposed around the evaluation of the material derived from land excavations (MPET) for its use, with emphasis on the exploration of the properties and potential applications. To this end, eight soil samples from the southern part of the city were characterized, collected at the Final Disposal Centre (CDF) of CEMEX, headquarters of Tunjuelo, to define their physical behavior and their usefulness in relation to the techniques of architecture with land. In addition, an exercise was proposed to show the viability of their reintegration, since their application as construction material in a housing. Laboratory results showed significant variations in the properties of the soils, although these were close to the recommended values in the literature for the construction with land. Two soil samples were highlighted by their granulometric composition, plasticity, compaction properties and volume stability optimal. However, the other samples are not discarded, since it is possible to adjust and stabilize the land according to the specific requirements of the buildings.
