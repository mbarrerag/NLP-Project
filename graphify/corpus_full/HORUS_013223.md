# Effect of the fruit load on growth, physiological performance, plant production and biochemical composition of the almond of Coffee arabica L

The increase in the load of fruits in coffee plants, affects the vegetative growth, the concentrations of nutrients, chlorophylls and soluble sugars at foliar level, and the biochemical composition of almonds. The aim of this research was to evaluate the effect of the burden of fruit on the growth and development of C. arabica plants, at production stage, in a crop located in the Colombian coffee central area.
