# The intervention of Social Work in Covid-19 times. A commitment of university social projection from the syndemic approach

This article presents the partial results of the systematization of the Social Care and Accompaniment Line: Unicolmayor Te Listen, of the Social Work Program. It highlights the teamwork, the collective creation of knowledge, the commitment to the intervention of Social Work from the university social projection, epistemologically founded to understand and overcome the horizons of traditional meaning.
