# Phytochemical study of the root ethanolic extract of Zanthoxylum caribaeum (Rutaceae) and obtaining secondary metabolites with possible neuroprotective activity applicable in the treatment of Alzheimer's disease.

In this document, the results of the phytochemical study and biological activity of the etanolytic extract of the Zanthoxylum caribaeum root (Rutaceae) are presented, and the obtaining of secondary metabolites with possible neuroprotective activity applicable in the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease. For the phytochemical study, the bioavailability of the product was used classical extraction and purification methods such as liquid extraction – liquid (using solvents of increasing polarity: hexane, ethyl acetate, butanol and water), column chromatography and purity monitoring by thin layer chromatography, while the methods of characterization and identification of metabolites were applied instrumental techniques such as high efficiency liquid chromatography, mass spectroscopic spectrometry and cases such as the magnetic resonance of hydrogen and carbon, in one and two dimensions. The study of biological activity was proposed in two stages; the first by bioatugraphy of the direct way of monitoring the process of extraction and purification of the crude extract, fractions and pure compounds using DPPH and ↔-Carotene to evaluate the potential of the free-in. -quinolin-2-one, 3-((1-methylcyclopentil) amino) quinolin-2(1H)-one, 10-ozaxol[3,2-a]quinoline and anthranylic acid and the evaluation of their possible neuroprotective activity, based on the results of antioxidant and anticholinergic activity, demonstrated that compounds individually exhibit medium to low antioxidant activities and that anticholinergic activity is lost as they progress in putification, with fractions being more active than pure compounds
