# Strategies and scenarios for the study of picudos as a chontaduro plague in Lloró, Chocó

The picuds (Coleoptera: Curculionidae) are pests in chontaduro crops that have led to a drastic reduction in their production on the Colombian Pacific Coast. In Chocó, the crop represents an essential agricultural economic activity and is a cultural part of their communities because of the cultivation derived handicrafts, food, drinks and events. The Agriculture Secretariat reports to the species Rhynchophorus palmarum (L. 1758) and Dynamis Borassi (Fabricius, 1801) as key pests and scientific literature has found alterations in the balance of these species due to anthropic factors, mainly. The present project aims to make a transdisciplinary approach to the problem from the visit, selection, recognition and sampling of insects in the fields of chontaduro crops in Lloró, Chocó and the dialogue with actors (farmers, municipal authorities, scientists) to determine the best strategies that allow the development of the project _x0093_Coleoptera as pest of chontaduro crops (Bactris gasipaes) in the municipality of Lloró, Chocó, Colombia_ Esa Moreno agó, ́sa
