# SAMPLING OF INECTS IN CULTURES: ANALYSIS OF TIME SERIES FOR DECISION-MAKING

Insect sampling, as an essential component of entomological pest monitoring, provides information on counts and other statistics used to guide control strategies, as well as supporting the detection of patterns that facilitate the prediction of indicators of population dynamics, useful in feeding early warning systems in the framework of integrated pest management. Descriptive and inferential analysis of indicators associated with population dynamics in insects is based on time series models. In this context, a time series is a set of observations of sampling of insects that has been recorded in a specific time period. Thrips (Thysanoptera: Thripidae) are key pests in horticultural and ornamental crops worldwide. The analysis of time series of samplings of thrips in crops allows developing an objective and quantitative view of the behavior of their populations in terms of time, and of particular variables of interest specific to the crop. However, the series and its structure will depend on the sampling scheme performed, so that both aspects must be considered simultaneously.
