# THE DRAFT SMALL PUBLIC PEDAGOGICAL PLACES?

The paper analyzes several designs developed by the students of the first year project workshops of the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín headquarters, in which themes linked to sustainability in architecture, recycling and reusing materials have been incorporated, through the dialogic chaining between the natural and artificial world
