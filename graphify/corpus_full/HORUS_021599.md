# Development of an asset management technique for distribution transformers based on monitoring system.

This document developed a system for diagnosing the current state of a fleet of four power transformers of the University Campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá, taking into account the real-time record of voltage, current and age of these devices stored in the National Laboratory of Intelligent Networks (LAB+i). To this end, an asset management system was implemented, consisting of a prediction module based on statistical methods and a diffuse logic module, with the aim of classifying the transformers considering the conditions of energy consumption and comparing performance with current management methods. It was identified that this system can be used both for monitoring the state of the fleet in real time and for its projection in the future, although challenges were presented associated with ensuring a continuous operation in time
