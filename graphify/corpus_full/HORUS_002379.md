# VENANOUS ANIMALS IN THE CARIBBEAN REGION OF COLOMBIA

This folding is a pedagogical tool designed to help prevent poisoning caused by snakes, spiders and scorpions. It illustrates the main features that allow to recognize animals of medical importance in the Colombian Caribbean region. It also provides recommendations to minimize encounters with these animals and indicates how to act in case of an accident, contraindicating the use of nonmedical practices that could aggravate the health of the affected person
