# Evaluation of the potential for the recovery of potato residues as a substrate for the production of biobutanol: a critical review

This thesis project aimed to evaluate the potential of utilization of potato residues as raw material for the production of biobutanol, specifically of tubers that cannot be marketed because they do not comply with quality parameters. For this, a description of the material was carried out to determine its composition, including moisture content, lignin, hemicellulose and cellulose. Subsequently, starch was extracted from the residues, obtaining a yield of 14.96 % p/p. An enzyme hydrolysis using amylase and amyloglucosidae on starch was performed and was maximized by kinetic tests, evaluating the production of reducing sugars with the DNS method, thus determining the optimal conditions of temperature and pH. Glucose achieved a performance of 47.78% p/p, and was quantified by means of a post-marketing, to which source of action ABE was determined, monitoring the growth and pH of the medium.
