# ESTIMALITY OF CURIE PROFUNDITY TO TURN OUT A VERSION OF MAGNETIC AMOLLIES DATA AND ESTIMATION OF THE WHERE THE WORLD-GOVERNED AREA ISSUED

The presence of pre-quaternary sedimentary sequences located in various geotectonic contexts where sedimentary intervals prevail with temperature ranges between 60-120°C, have allowed to identify patterns of reservoirs of hydrocarbons of economic importance.This pattern has been called Golden Zone et al. (2005) and Nadeau (2011) and refers to a hypothesis of coexistence of an oil system with hydrocarbon traps that guarantee minimum degradation or transformation of the organic matter.As the results increase the punctual observations reported, the distribution of belts of the Golden Zone in the world is specified and allows to configure maps that help to identify areas of greater potential and effectiveness of the exploratory exercise.The Profundidad de Curie represents the depth to which the rocks lose their magnetic properties, this occurs at temperatures between the 550oC and 580oC model of average power (Turcotte and Schubert, 2002).In this work, a global model of depth of Curie was estimated from an investment of magnetic anomalies with data of the EMAG2v3 grid, using the technique of radial spectrum of averaged power.
