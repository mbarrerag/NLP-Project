# Sticky floors, broken stairs and glass ceilings human rights and dignification of women's work in Colombia

This document seeks to analyse the outstanding challenges in the field of labour to guarantee human rights in relation to non-discrimination and the material equality of opportunities for working women in Colombia. To this end, the concepts of sticky floors, broken stairs and glass ceilings will be taken as criticism of the way in which these phenomena have been addressed, where, while progress has been made in guaranteeing human rights, discrimination and improvement scenarios still persist. To this end, it is part of the historical context of women's rights and labour integration, as well as the remaining labour gaps in Colombia, and then an analysis of each of the challenges and ways in which efforts have been made to overcome them in Colombia is being carried out. Finally, some of the protection trends in countries that have better protected these rights are being studied and a reflection is made to propose options for improvement
