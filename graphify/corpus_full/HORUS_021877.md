# Automatic data-taking system in the calibration of Digital Multimeters through Digital Image Processing.

The project aimed to develop an automatic system of acquisition and recognition of measurements by digital multimeter using images; this was proposed in order to improve its calibration process in the magnitudes of DC, AC, DC voltage, AC and electrical resistance. Thus, a system was developed that automatically captures, processes and recognizes the values associated with the multimeter readings in its calibration process, which was implemented in three stages: The first stage consisted in the creation of a database with information from the multimeter models to calibrate, the standard instrument (evaluating the feasibility of using it in the process) and the calibration points. In a second stage, an algorithm was developed for the identification and classification of the values displayed in the multimeter under test. This algorithm reached a level of accuracy of global recognition (made on 600 samples) of 99,33 %. In a third stage, the process of uncertainty estimation, the report and evaluation of the validity of the results was developed
