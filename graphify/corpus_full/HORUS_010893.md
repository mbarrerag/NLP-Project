# QUALITY OF PRIMARY MATERIALS BY MINERY OF DATA: APPLICATION IN THE FOOD INDUSTRY

In this research, the J48 algorithm (Weka platform) is used to determine the most influential independent variables in the quality process of the following fruit products: blackberry, orange, maracuyá, lemon, strawberry, lulo, mango, feijoa and peach. In this analysis, it is based on a database of 211 records, a dependent variable (quality) and a set of 10 independent variables: raw material type, oBrix, pH, acidity, color, smell, taste, maturity, washed pH and bleach washed. As a result of this process, it is found with an effectiveness of 93% that the most effective variables are: taste, color and smell. However, if these variables are suppressed, it is found that variables pH, °Brix and product can predict the dependent variable quality with a higher effectiveness than 93%.
