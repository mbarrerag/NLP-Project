# Determination of lateral displacement on tendon walls, in front of lateral, monotonic and cyclic loads

This research aims to deepen on a traditional construction technique Colombia, called tendinous walls. The research studies the behavior of these walls, before the external imposition of lateral loads, either monotonic loads or loading and unloading cycles, to obtain a scientific look to a net ancestral load, which, if seen in a holistic way, can occupy a place in the way of construction in the current of Colombia and Latin America. The system tendinous walls, consists of porticoes, on which semiflexible panels are placed. The porticos can be constructed in wood, guadua, metal or even concrete reinforced structures. On the other hand, the panel is armed with tendon of barbed wire, tensioned symmetrically in the perimeter of the portico, and on which they extend the costals of fique, vene meshes, matilla of internals and entrances. After the tests were completed, it was possible to determine the behavior of the three types of walls, obtain the maximum lateral displacement, analyze advantages, disadvantages and aspects to improve the construction system.
