# Plant Growth Promotional Bacteria as a Tool to Mitigate Salt Stress in Cultures: A Review

Salinity is a factor that negatively affects the physiology of most plants, even putting food safety at risk when it affects cultivated plants to produce food. This review provides a general description of the use of plant growth promoting bacteria (BPCV) as a strategy to mitigate saline stress in plants, with a look to provide a sustainable solution to this environmental problem. Salinity causes morphophysiological and biochemical alterations in plants due to osmotic and ion stress. Plants have different response mechanisms that allow them to survive and in some cases tolerate salinity. Different mitigation strategies have been evaluated, such as the use of plant hormones, fertilizers, nanofertilisers, silicon, antioxidants, tolerant genotypes and inoculation with microorganisms, among others. Among the microorganisms used for inoculation, are of particular interest the BPCV.
