# ODIR 4.0: Leading Accessibility to Real Estate Law

The research group Law and Economy: Commerce-Silent of the National University of Colombia is dedicated to the multidimensional research of the Law, addressing aspects such as the Economic Constitution, the history of the commercial law and the relations between law and technology. The group seeks to contribute to the solution of social and academic problems through the interaction of different sectors and their possibilities of action. Between 2018 and 2019, with the support of the research group DyE:CS (Law and Economy: Silent Trade), the project Observatory of Law and Business (ODIR) was developed, a proposal that created a research space for the development of academic activities such as the collection, analysis, study and publication of statistics in matters of property ownership and its registration. This led to the consolidation of the annual publication of the Observatory in which they provide recommendations, strategies and proposals to address the identified problems, applying methodologically the acquired knowledge. The observatory has developed projects, focusing on the academic and the research of the main problems of real estate law in Colombia. This culminated in an event that provided a forum of information, exchange and dialogue on the emerging technologies such as Big Data, artificial intelligence and catastro was multipropositivo in the law and the XXI century. The Observatory's continuity as a proposal in these terms is based on the greatest public utility in view of the real estate foundation of Colombian society, and the need to frequently debate the qualities of the real estate and registration law subsystem, whose effectiveness, efficiency, transparency and updating entails not only the interests of the State, but also the security of market owners and investors. Thus, the continuity of the Observatory of Real Estate Law (ODIR) is justified. In this context, we propose the continuity of the Observatory of Real Estate Law (ODIR) and the creation of an interactive book. We believe that the continuity of the Observatory is of great public utility, given the relevance of the real estate foundation of Colombian society and the need to regularly debate the qualities of the real estate and registration law subsystem
