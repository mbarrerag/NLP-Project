# The construction of the discursive legitimation of the Peace Agreement in opinion. The Colombian (2018-2020)

This paper seeks to understand how the Peace Agreement and the armed conflict in Colombia are represented in the discourses of opinion of the newspaper El Colombiano during the first and half year of the government of Iván Duque in order to clarify how strategies are implemented in the construction of legitimization in this type of discourse. The theoretical framework of this research is linked to the Critical Studies of the Speech, in which concepts such as social representations are expanded in the opinion press and the discursive strategies of legitimization raised by Theo Van Leeuwen (2015).The methodology used is predominantly subscribed to the qualitative research approach, and is developed from two phases: a descriptive phase in which a categorization of the discourses is provided by using techniques of analysis of textual data (ADT) through Nvivo software; the agents and their attributes, the thematic nodes and the disponsive strategies of legitimation of the 85 articles that shaped the corpus are identified. In the second phase, analytical-interpretative, from the emerging categories of agreement and anti-agreements are investigated the thematic interactions between the two agents, the disclaims and the distributive strategies.
