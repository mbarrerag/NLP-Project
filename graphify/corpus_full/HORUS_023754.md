# Musitar Equisetum

Musitar for her, for me, for all. The women of my family, the female bodies; productive, fertile; the mares. My body flees through time, inhabiting the movement; simultaneously from the experience of a bone fracture and the pressure to produce. The materiality of time stops, reproduces, stops, accelerates; impacts. Running in circles through the video, fleeing from family memories; accelerating the image, pulling the body itself through time. Breathing, pause, freeze the movement; looking at them in the eyes. To them, looking at my own gaze in the mirror. My gaze determines the movement of my body, the cadence and intensity of my voice. The audiovisual image arises as a space of relationship with the body. One body that allows me to mutate my times in simultaneous: the time of productivity made body and the time of contemplarized through the movement. A closeness whose only aim is the same contact with other bodies; I have the documentary image as a space of relationship with the body. Through time. Breathing, pausing, freezing movement; look them in the eye. To them, to look at me, analyzing my own look in the mirror. My gaze determines the movement of my body, the chain and intensity of my voice. The audiovisual image arises in the present, as the possibility of inhabiting two times simultaneously: the time of productivity made body and the contemplative-ritualized time through movement. An approach only objective is the contact itself with other bodies; I inhabit the image as a place of relationship with the other.
