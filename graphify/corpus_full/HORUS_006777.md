# Induction of systemic resistance in tomato plants against Pseudomonas syringae pv. tomato by Pseudomonas spp.

The tomato culture is highlighted worldwide by its economic importance and nutritional value, being essential in Colombian agriculture with a significant production. However, bacterial sine, caused by Pseudomonas syringae pv. tomato (Pst), threatens yields with damage in leaves and fruits. Despite the common use of agrochemicals, with its negative environmental consequences, strategies such as biological control with fungi and rizospheric bacteria, especially of the genus Pseudomonas, emerge as sustainable alternatives to induce systemic resistance to biotic stress and prevent diseases. Plants, with constitutive defenses and mechanisms of response, deploy key responses to threats of herbivores, pathogens and environmental stress.
