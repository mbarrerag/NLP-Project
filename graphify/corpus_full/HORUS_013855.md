# Identification of technical and economic criteria for the establishment of sustainable electrification methodologies in the Colombian Non-Interconnected Zone

The limited or non-existent access to electricity is a problem that affects millions of people around the world, especially in rural and isolated areas of developing countries. In Colombia, this problem is especially evident in the non-interconnected area of the country, where it is estimated that about one million people do not have access to electricity. The implementation of rural and isolated electrification solutions can be an effective alternative to solve this problem. However, it is important to take into account that these solutions must be sustainable from the technical, economic, environmental, social and institutional point of view. That is, they must be able to provide a reliable and safe energy supply, at affordable prices for users, and minimizing their environmental impact. Therefore, the present research project has as main objective to determine the criteria and indicators that should be considered in the establishment of methodologies for the implementation of sustainable electrification solutions in the non-interconnected area of the country. The above, with special emphasis on the exploration of energy potentials present in rural and isolated areas of Colombia, in order to promote their use in the generation of electricity from non-conventional sources and seeking to reduce the dependence of the diesel service in the previous areas
