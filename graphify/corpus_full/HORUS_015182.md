# Space-temporal model for deaths from CIVID-19 in Cundinamarca and Bogotá.

In this work, a spatial-temporal model for the number of deaths due to COVID-19 in Cundinamarca and Bogotá is adjusted. In the municipalities further away from Cundinamarca, the normal thing is that there will not be deaths in a long time, for this reason it can be considered a problem with excess zeros of space-time. In addition, an exploratory analysis of the database was carried out which allowed to detect the presence of temporal and spatial relation. Therefore, in the first part, the methodologies and concepts that can help to manage the excess zeros are exposed and detailed and subsequently, emphasis is made on the spatial-temporal model with excess zeros. In the search for literature it was found that a good alternative to adjust a model of this style is to do so by means of a Bayesian hierarchical model using the method of the approximation of the integrated nested Laplace (INLA). A descriptive analysis of the vaccination was performed leaving some details that allowed to complement the analysis of the adjustment of the models. Finally, it was obtained that the model that was best adjusted to the mean of the absolute error of prediction (MAPE), the information criterion of the zero--19 model of the vaccination was used in Colombia
