# Speak silently about vowels in people of different cognitive style

Abstract Introduction: The research measures the differences in silent vocal speech /a/-/u/ in Spanish, in students of different cognitive style in the Dependency - Field Independence (DIC) dimension. In the study 51 adults participated. Electroencephalographic signals (EEG) were taken, from 14 electrodes arranged on the scalp of the language region located in the left hemisphere. Previously, the test of masked figures EFT was applied to them in order to classify them into dependent, intermediate and independent people of field. To analyze the EEG data, the signals were broken down into intrinsically (IMF) functions and a mixed analysis of repeated measurements was performed. The spectral density of power (PSD) in the vowels was found to be independent of the cognitive style and its magnitude depends on the position of the electrodes. The results suggest that there are no significant differences in the PSDs in the silent speech of the vowels /a/-/u/ in the people of different cognitive style. Significant differences were found in the PSDs according to the position of the 14 electrodes used.
