# OF THE SUMMIT SAMPLES TO CAPITALINS EXHUMANED IN CENTRAL CEMENTERIES B S. XIX AND XX: CORPORAL PROPOSALS, DIET AND MESTIZE OF THE POPULATIONS OF THE BOGOTA SABA

Historical archaeology has the capacity to provide information that allows us to verify, expand and refute the historical premises with archaeological data, much of the findings in these contexts correspond to bone remains, so it is necessary a bioarcheological study, which seeks to reconstruct the cultural and environmental variables of a specific population, in order to answer questions of social, biological, political and economic type. In Colombia several researches have been carried out in the field of historical archaeology, however, there are few works that include the analysis of bone remains. These are fundamental to reconstruct the living conditions of the populations that lived during historical times, to verify or complement the information that the written documents offer and to approximate the changes that societies have had in time. In this work, we have wanted to explore the opportunity to observe the changes that the populations settled in the Sabana de Bogotá, have had through the time of the populations that lived during the historical periods, to verify or to analyze the information that the bodily and dietary proportions offered by the authors will then compare the data obtained on the physical and phytolit proportions in dental calculation, as from the Muisca collection of Soacha, thus, corresponding to the 10th century.
