# BOJAYA, CHICO: CONTRIBUTIONS FOR INTEGRAL ATTENTION IN HEALTH TO SURVIVENTS OF THE INTERNAL COLOMBIAN ARMED CONFLICT

This document contains the results of the Laboratory of Rural and Intercultural Health of Boiayá. Chocó, developed between 2018 and 2022 by the Research Group on Violence and Health of the National University of Colombia, in coordination with the community of Bojayá (Chocó), represented in its Committee for the Rights of the Victims of Bojayá. It presents in a synthetic way the findings obtained through the research projects concatenated in the framework of the mentioned health laboratory, in order to provide the participating community and decision makers in health, a set of guiding elements for the construction of public policies. By 2018, 16 years after the explosion of the improvised explosive device (IED) causing the Bojayá Massacre, at least 81.8 % of the survivors presented some diagnosis in mental health, 65 % in auditory health and 33 % in internalized squirles. Research results are presented in relation to this topic, along with a series of recommendations derived for the care of the main health diagnoses in this community, as a contribution to progress towards comprehensive health repair and guarantees of non-recurrecurrence
