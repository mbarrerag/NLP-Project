# Use of the habitat of the giant anteater Myrmecophaga tridactyla in fire-affected landscapes in the Department of Vichada, Orinoquia Colombiana

This thesis investigates the use of the habitat of the Gastronomic Hut (Myrmecophaga tridactyla) in landscapes affected by fires in the Department of Vichada, Colombia. Recognizing the ecological importance of this species and the impacts that habitat alteration causes on fauna due to fires, the objective of this study was to understand how these environmental changes influence the preferences and the use of the habitat of the anthill. Using a mixed method approach that includes chamber traps, spatial analysis and field observations, the research was carried out in two areas affected by fires within the department of Vichada. The results indicate that there are no significant changes in the availability of resources between both sites. In addition, our findings suggest that the use of the habitat is mediated mainly by its thermoregulation behavior, similar to what is reported in other studies.
