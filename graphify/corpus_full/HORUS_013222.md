# Development of an anomaly detection algorithm based on kernel density estimation, density matrices and quantum measurements

This thesis presents an innovative algorithm designed to detect anomalies in various data sets. This method, called Anomaly Detection through Density Matrices and Fourier Features (AD-DMKDE), integrates kernel-based density estimation (Kernel Density Estimation or KDE) and machine learning (known as Machine Learning in English) with density matrices and quantum measurement, two promising concepts from the field of quantum computing. The theoretical and methodological bases that underpin this method are established; the details of its development and implementation are presented. A systematic comparison of the proposed algorithm against twelve varied methods of anomaly detection and quantum measurement is made against twelve different methods of anomaly detection; AD-DMKDE shows a competitive performance when applied to a selection of twenty-four data sets.
