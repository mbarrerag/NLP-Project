# Intellectual structure of greenwashing research: bibliometric and narrative analysis

Abstract Objective: To carry out an analysis of the intellectual structure of knowledge about greenwashing by identifying the main research trends in the subject matter and a literary narrative that includes a description of the concept, the main effects and the strategies against greenwashing. A bibliometric analysis of scientific production was carried out in the Web of Science (WoS) and Scopus bases, as well as a narrative review of literature. The bibliographic results were processed through the application of platforms and software such as RStudio and VOSviewer. Key results: 282 documents were analyzed that allowed mapping of scientific production (authors, documents, sources and affiliations), presenting a description of the intellectual structure and offering a literary narrative on the concept, classification, forms and drivers, effects and strategies to fight against greenwashing. Three thematic clusters were identified on the current state of research that provide light on trends in knowledge production: sustainability, corporate social responsibility and green marketing.
