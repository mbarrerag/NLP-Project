# “We were even with rifles and songs”: uses and disputes of the Farc-Ep’s musical production practices from the life story of Alejandra Téllez (1999-2016)

This thesis seeks to understand the uses and disputes of music produced by the Farc-Ep within the framework of the cultural confrontation that took place between the guerrilla and the Colombian State. To give an account of this, the research takes as its axis the life history of Alejandra Téllez, who was part of the organization between 1998 and 2016, and who, in turn, was part of the musical groups Rebeldes del Sur, Rebeldía Oriental and Horizonte Fariano. The appearance of Fariana music as part of the cultural project of the Farc-Ep, within the framework of the political strategy of the guerrilla played a central role in the symbolic war against the State.
