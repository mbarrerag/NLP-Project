# Transfer of technology to the association of rural women _x0093_ASER_x0094_ of the municipality of Tame (Arauca), from agricultural practices of the experimental farm in Cairo (University National, Orinoquía headquarters)

The concept of food security refers to the _x0093_existence of sufficient and diverse food at national, regional or local level, and to the possibility of the people involved to have access to these foods_x0094_ (Bello et al., 2008). If less species are grown in agroecosystems, the food will be called, on the one hand, to a decrease in the quality of human nutrition and likewise to decrease the capacity of ecosystem resilience. Thus, the need arises to originate local food systems, dynamic and resilient, that guarantee a production of abundant, diverse and healthy food affordable for a growing human population. One of these alternatives to enhance such security is framed in the conversion to agroecological models that can transform and convert the production of the family farm from monoculture made a management based on the diversity of agroecosystems that foster, as well as agrobiodiversity and finally increase food security. In the relationship between land and food security the current project is based
