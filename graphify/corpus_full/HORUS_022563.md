# 20 CRITICAL EDUCATIONAL PROCESS ABOUT SEXUALITY: SUSTAINED CONSTRUCTION IN PAULO FREIRE’S ITINERARY

This text is centered on the account of the construction of an education experience for sexuality with adolescents from rural schools, based on emancipatory and dialogical aspects of Paulo Freire's Pedagogy.The Freirian proposal was adopted which, in addition to being a method, is a life stance embodied in teaching processes.It is understood that she means an extension of the "I" as a human being in the role of educator and the commitment assumed with society
