# Methodology of learning programming from music

This work addresses the teaching of computational thinking as a cross-cutting skill of the 21st century. Although, the computational thinking is taught through programming activities, it is important to address the need to maintain the motivation of students for their learning. Traditionally, programming courses are taught through problem solving, however, it is possible to teach computational thinking using motivational axes, such as robotics, video games development or music. In this work, a methodology is proposed to teach programming through music. To this end, the concepts of general musical education, the social appropriation of science and technology and the themes that are the basis of programming learning. In this process, a concrete didactic material is developed that includes a material kit based on an electronic embedded system, a programming environment called Catalejo Editor and a documentation material for the development of self-learning activities and the Internet, allowing for a final approach to students to "think with hands".
