# Branching Process to Model the SARS-COV-2 in the City of Bogotá

The objective of the work is to implement a methodology for the description of the evolution of the SARS-CoV2 virus in the city of Bogotá. For this, a Galton-Watson process is considered with two types of individuals: reported infected and unreported infected (asymptomatic). Use is made only of the daily statistics of Covid-19 cases reported by the Ministry of Health of Bogotá to give a prediction of the average value of the infected individuals population. Finally, those individuals who arrive in Bogotá from other cities and even from other countries and who contribute to the spread of the virus are included in the model
