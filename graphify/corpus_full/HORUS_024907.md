# TOPOLOGY OPTIMIZATION BASED ON THERMAL COMPENSATION APPLIED TO MOLD INJECTION DESIGN

One of the main issues in the injection moulding manufacturing processes lies in attention to the efficient material production and obtaining products at reasonable prices, which show a solid economy for large series production [1]. Mold design, and the simulation of this process, is an essential aspect in the product life cycle [1], the quality determination, viability and productivity in an injection-manufactured piece. Some works have defined and simulated the injection process to explore the distribution and filling process of a thermoplastic material in an injection die, for producing animal ear tags, which are used in the cattle visual control [1]. Through a design of experiments (DOE) with analysis of variance ANOVA [2], the main variables inherent to the process has been stablished and the ¿recommended¿ values for the best injection-mold performance has been determined, in order to achieve reduced cycle times production, quality and thermal performance; however, same result shown that a optimization process must be developed for achieving better injection manufacture parameters. Accordingly, a topology optimization (OT) mold design, considering thermal compensation, is developed. Thermal compensation means that the mold-deformations, which is caused for gradient temperature and cycling injection forces in the mold, are minimized in the mold cavity zones (contact zone between the mold and the material injected in the filling process), in order to improve piece tolerances [3]. The TO design is based on traditional SIMP material model and SLP implementation, considering linear thermo structural analysis. The domain design is considered as a 3D domain and the numerical implementation is developed in *.m code (Matlab implementation). The results allow the concept design for the mold injection process for plastics materials based on the topology optimization approach
