# Procurement models for the purchase of electricity to the regulated market

In the Colombian electricity sector, the commercialization of energy includes the acquisition and management of large volumes of electricity to sell it to end users and market agents (CREG, 2014). Currently, the marketers have multiple mechanisms for the purchase of energy to the regulated market, among these are the energy exchange, bilateral contracts, long term energy auctions, coverages through DERIVEX and purchases of surplus autogenerators. In addition, the Colombian regulation allows the possibility to create new mechanisms of marketing energy under Resolution CREG 114 of 2018. However, the participation in these mechanisms is heterogeneous, with the aim of improving the efficiency and resilience of the regulated market in Colombia. Through a methodology based on the identification of best international practices, the regulatory analysis and the evaluation of the performance of the existing mechanisms, the present work proposes a set of alternative measures to diversify the marketing mechanisms to improve the efficiency and resilience of the regulated market.
