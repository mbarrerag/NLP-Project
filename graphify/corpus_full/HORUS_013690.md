# Cocaine smoking in Latin America: analysis of the criminalization of street dwellers who consume bazooka - coca base paste-, after the Bronx intervention in Bogotá

The intervention of La L o El Bronx executed on May 28, 2016 was projected before the public opinion as a success in relation to the dismantling of the organized crime networks that controlled this place and, moreover, as a way of impacting the offer of illegal psychoactive substances in Bogotá. Despite the recovery of the territorial control of this place, the operation caused the illicit market to spread in more than 400 ‘ollas’ located mainly in the localities of Los Mártires, La Candelaria and Santa Fe. The investigation presents a case of study, attached to the field of the smoked cocaines, in order to deepen the effects of the prohibitionism in Latin America on one of the weakest links of the drug chain: the consumers of bazouca. This measure led to a transformation of the conditions of criminalization that characterize the modes of intervention on this population, not only from the public force through repression, but also from the public opinion and the public service in general. This development takes place from the presentation of accounts, experiences and trajectures of life of consumers. ritories such as Los Mártires, La Candelaria and Santa Fe. The research trends a case study, posted to smokable cocaines field of study, which applications to study in more detail on the effects of prohibitionism in Latin America, specifically against weakest actors in illicit substance chain: bazocco consumers who inhabit or frequent the streets. The intervention of ‘La L’ caused the transformation of criminalization conditions characterized by repression from the National Police, public opinion and society
