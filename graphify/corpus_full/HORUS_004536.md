# Wounds, roots, preservations and confrontations: family ties and their meanings between daughters and sons with sexual dissent

The family bond is understood as an intimate and affective relationship in the private space and from which the members of that group interact; it is a relationship connected with the external world and the internal world in the family dynamics. The research sought to understand the meanings that daughters and sons with sexual dissidence attribute to their family ties. This research proposal seeks to reflect and respond to the understanding of those meanings from the Symbolic Interactionism and the theory of the affective bond. The research is based on four narrative productions constructed from semi-structured discussion groups and interviews to daughters and sons with sexual dissidence considering some aspects of the Queer methodology. It was found that the family presents a tension-care relationship with the existence of the sexual dissidence of daughters and sons that, idealizes love as a point of escape to the painful and violent link. Likewise, it was identified that the family bond through the interaction, forms the personality of daughters and sons and allows them to explore their own identity and sexual orientation in relation to their family and other contexts.
