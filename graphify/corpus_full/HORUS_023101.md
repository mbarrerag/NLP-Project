# ANALYSIS OF THE CAPTURE BY EFFERCY UNIT IN FISHING FISHING FOUR MUNICIPIES OF THE CESAR DEPARTMENT (COLOMBIA)

The Catch per Unit of Effort (CPUE), is the starting point for the characterization of artisanal fishing as productive activity, allows to understand the effort made by fishermen and the kilograms of fish they obtain. The CPUE was estimated by the fishing haul of 4 municipalities of the department of Cesar, according to the type of fishing art that use passive, active or mixed (use of both gears in the same fishing). It was found that the municipality of Gamarra on average has more kilograms of fish per haul with 8.22, is characterized by the use of mixed fishing gears by 54% and by carrying out activity in rivers and swamps, for its part the municipality of Chimichagua has an average of 1.14 kilograms per haul with the characteristic that only fish in cienagas and 69 % uses passive fishing gears (APP). In the four municipalities prioritized of the department of Cesar, the CPUE estimated for APP was greater, because this group is composed of gears like trammel that are invagers with eyes of mesh smaller and with which the number of fish can be captured by different species and greater quantity of kilograms
