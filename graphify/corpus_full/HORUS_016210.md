# EXPERIENCES IN THE CLASS WALK ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PEACE AGREEMENTS AND RECONCILIATION.

Experiences in the classroom in the implementation of peace agreements and reconciliation at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters La Paz, Valledupar The Universidad Nacional de Colombia, headquarters La Paz in Valledupar, plays a key role in consolidating peace through education.Since the implementation of the Peace Accords in 2016, the academy has become a fundamental stage for reconciliation and the construction of historical memory in the country. In this context, the classrooms have been spaces for meeting students from various realities, including victims of conflict, ex-combatants and communities affected by violence. The pedagogical experiences at the La Paz headquarters have been oriented towards dialogue, critical analysis and the promotion of a culture of peace. Through participatory methodologies, such as project-based learning, the use of testimonies and research-action, students have been able to understand the complexities of the Colombian conflict and propose strategies for social transformation. In addition, the interdisciplinary approach has allowed for articulation of knowledge from anthropology, law, history and education, favouring a comprehensive vision of reconciliation.
