# Effective rates of personal income tax in Colombia

The purpose of this document is to estimate the effective tax rate of natural persons residing in Colombia with respect to Income Tax, for the taxable years 2017, 2018 and 2019. In general, it is assumed that the effective tax rate -ETR- is the ratio between the total tax payable and net income. Therefore, the following are presented as results of this work: the ETR of the income tax according to the origin of the income, likewise, the ETR of income by economic sectors and, in turn, the comparison and the variation between the estimated ETR and the Marginal Tax Rates -MTR- contemplated in the legislation. Finally, and by way of conclusion, it should be noted that the ETR of income of natural persons is significantly different from the MTR established in the Reforma Tributaria Estructural (taxable years 2017 and 2018) and the Ley de Financiamiento (taxable year 2019), for all types of income except for dividends and participations of the 1 sub-certificate in 2017 and 2018 and, that of dividends and participations of the 2 sub-cédula in 2019.
