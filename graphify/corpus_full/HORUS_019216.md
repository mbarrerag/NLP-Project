# A multi-registered sequence of learning, for understanding the Rationals in their part-all meaning, involving mixed numerical and one-dimensional figurative records.

This research aims to promote the understanding of students of sixth grade of a rural educational institution of the municipality of Tuluá, about the Rational numbers based on their part-all meaning, with the development of tasks focused on the articulation of different numerical and one-dimensional figurative representations. This, by designing and implementing a multi-registered sequence of learning based on the disciplinary and theoretical elements, specific to Duval's cognitive semiotic perspective (1999, 2004, 2006), as well as the master's and doctoral studies of the researcher Pontón (2008, 2012). The methodology used, integrated some elements of the design research, in the elaboration of a particular design for learning. Like, elements of didactic engineering such as: phases for the development of research and the contrast between the a priori and a posteriori analyses. This work offers an important contribution to the construction of the Rationals, given that, by the recognition of the design variables, the articulation of the different fractional, decimal, mixed and figurative representations was made, the multidimensional, highlighting the potential of the undimensional and numerical results
