# THE SOLUTION OF ILLICIT DRUG PROBLEM IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF PEACE. CHALLENGES IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE INEGRAL NATIONAL PROGRAMME OF ILLICIT CULTURES (PNIS) IN COLOMBIA

This article presents an analysis of the formulation and implementation of the programs and policies of the post-agreement in Colombia regarding point 4 of the Peace Agreement between the Government of Colombia and the FARC-EP on the solution to the problem of illicit drugs based on the critical approach of peace-building, liberal peace versus hybrid peace. The analysis is developed on the basis of a qualitative approach methodology, a balance of public policies and a systematization of verified data based on theoretical approaches. It is maintained that, despite the hybrid and contextualized notion that the Agreement demonstrates to the challenge of illicit crops, in practice the formulation and implementation of the National Development Plan responds to the monoontological and technocratic notion of (neo)liberal peace. This has been expressed in the short term (2016-2020) through two variables: (a) the targeting of expenditure as a solution to the problem of crops and (b) community participation and legitimizing the liberal vision in the implementation of collective and territorial agreements
