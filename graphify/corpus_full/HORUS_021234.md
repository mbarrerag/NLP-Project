# Modulation of expression and activity of transient potential receptors using capsazepine-laden nanogels: new strategies for the management of dental pain

Dental pain is considered the main symptom by which patients attend dental consultation and although there are some therapeutic alternatives, sometimes the analgesic effect is not adequate so it is necessary the development of new assets and new formulations, which can be used locally and which allow generating analgesia. One of the new therapeutic targets studied for the management of dental pain are the receptors of transient potential (TRP), which besides participating in the pain pathway (1) have been related to other alterations in oral cavity such as cancer (2), so that its modulation is considered as a possible strategy for the management of multiple pathologies. Moreover, in recent years, nanocarriers of organic and inorganic origin such as for example nanoparticles, micelas, fullerenes, graffenes, nanogels, liposomes, dendrimers, quantum dots, among others, have constituted an important field of research as active release systems (3). Several advantages have been related to the controlled release systems among which we can mention reduction in the frequency of administration, which can be associated with the increased adherence to the patient.
