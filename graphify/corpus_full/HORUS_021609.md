# Algorithm of grouping and classification for the detection of customers suspected of contributing to non-technical losses of energy in an electrical marketing company in Colombia.

The companies that market electrical energy contemplate within their strategic planning process the purpose of maximizing their yields and providing services with high quality standards. Therefore, they are continuously in search of a more efficient and profitable operation. The fundamental challenge for this objective is to minimize the losses of energy that correspond to the difference between the generated electrical energy and that that is billed finally to users. These losses are of two types: i) techniques, which manifest themselves as part of the physical phenomena associated with the transmission, transformation and distribution of the energy; and ii) non-technical features, which are associated with the interventions of the human being that affect the normal functioning of the measuring equipment, or other actions that do not allow the correct invoicing of the energy consumption.The nature of the non-technical losses makes their tracking a difficult and inefficient process, the solutions proposed by various authors have been grouped into three categories: the first focused to the implementation of intelligent networks and constant monitoring systems; the second based on analytics of data and application of the computational learning techniques on the users, networks and energy consumptions; the third, a mixed approach that takes elements of both for the construction of networks, the data, analyzed data, was addressed from the data. The results of the cross-validation process by K-Folds significantly contribute to the best configuration of the classifiers and minimize training errors by moving from initial accuracys from 0.6 to 0.8 of macro average precision (Macro-aggregation Precision). For unsupervised techniques, the nature of the data does not allow a clear differentiation between the groups obtained, so this approach is not considered appropriate for the solution of the problem, in this case, quite heterogeneous groups were obtained whose results were maintained below 0.06 of homogeneity score
