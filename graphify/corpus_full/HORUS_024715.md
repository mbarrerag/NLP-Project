# Partial glycerol oxidation on Cu-Co catalysts supported in luminised metacaolinite.

This work focuses on catalytic valorization of glycerol by transforming it into products of higher commercial value. To achieve this objective, the reaction of glycerol oxidation in aqueous medium was studied, since the oxidative pathway is one of more promising regarding other type of reactions. Supported catalysts based on mixed oxides of Cu and Co (CuCo2O4 spinel) were synthesized by means of the wet impregnation method, a dealumined metacaolite was used as a support, a solid obtained from thermal treatment and acid to the Colombian natural kaolinite, in order to generate high micro and mesoporosity. By applying different techniques of characterisation the successful synthesis of the spinel CuCo2O4 and the obtaining of solids with high surface area and dispersion of the metallic phase thanks to the treatment performed to the support. The experimental strategy was focused on parameters such as the optimization of temperature, concentration of hydrogen peroxide and reaction time.
