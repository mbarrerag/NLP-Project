# MONITORING OF GEOPHISICAL VARIABLES (SYSICAL, ELECTRIC, MAGNETIC AND GAS REGISTERS) IN THE ANDINAN AND NATIONAL PRESENCE OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY (UNAL) BY THE GEOPHISICAL NETWORK OF THE UNAL

The results of the project to expand the Geophysical Network of the National University of Colombia (RGUNAL) are described. By the end of 2023, the capacity of the multiparametric stations (seismic, electrical, magnetic and gas registers: CO2, CH4, H2) and six (6) seismic stations will be 15, and these stations will be located in all the headquarters of the National University of Colombia (UNAL), headquarters of the National Open and Distance University (UNAD), headquarters of the Antonio Nariño University (UAN) and repeat stations of Colombia Mobile (TIGO). In addition, two multiparametric stations located in Antarctica (Marambio and La Esperanza), in collaboration with the Argentine Antarctic Institute, are located in Bogotá. This instrumental network transmits unique data, in real time, to a data reception and processing centre located in the Department of Geosciences of UNAL, with the possibility of generating digital mirrors. All information is available 24/7 for the community in general and for regional institutionality as an input for geological, geophysical and ingenitional studies
