# LINEAL ASSCILLATIONS AND THE POINCARE ROTATION NUMBER

Linear oscillators have been extensively studied in different areas of knowledge, both from the theoretical point of view in differential equations and in applications to physics, engineering, etc. In this talk we will talk about the Rotaci'on Number defined by Poincaré and use it as a tool to study the dynamic behavior of linear oscillators
