# Flood threat study and torrential avenue to identify mitigation measures in the urban area of the city of Mocoa

Floods are overflows of water outside the main or low water channel, with low concentration of fine sediments, where an area is temporarily denied thanks to the occurrence of high extreme flows of medium duration. These events are frequent in Colombia especially in winter season or before the presence of the wet phase of the ENSO. The main objective of this final work is to recommend the location, and dimensions of river control measures to mitigate the threats by flooding and of possible torrential avenues in a stretch of 3.7 km of the Mocoa river in the vicinity of the city of the same name. The purpose of this final work is to recommend the location, and dimensions of river control measures to mitigate the threats by flood and of possible torrential floods in a section of the Mocoa river in the vicinity of the same name city, in order to support the administrative decision making in the framework of disaster risk management, integral water resource management (GIRH) and the territorial and environmental management of Mocoa.
