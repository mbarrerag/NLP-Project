# CHEMICAL EQUILIBRIUM - IONIC EQUILIBRIUM

The state of chemical equilibrium occurs when opposite reactions occur at equal speeds: the speed at which the products are formed from the reagents is equal to the speed at which the reagents are formed from the products. As a result, the concentrations cease to change, which makes it appear that the reaction stopped. In balance, then, the direct and reverse processes are carried out at the same speed and the system is in a balanced state. Altering conditions can disturb the state of equilibrium, before which the sense of reaction is modified, displacing itself in the appropriate sense to re-establish the condition of equilibrium (Le Châtelier principle). Knowing the constant of equilibrium is one of the most important issues when addressing the study of reversible systems
