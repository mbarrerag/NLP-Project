# Correspondences: everyday practices of living in the city

The proposal of research is part of the strategic areas established in the call for research and creative work between academic units and the inclusion of students of PEAMA, favouring the inter- and transdisciplinary work for the strengthening of disciplinary developments at the same time as the connection between regions. The proposal seeks, in a central way, to strengthen and to make visible the academic production of research and creation of the seedbed of Cinema and Gender of the Faculty of Arts and of the Monada research group; for which the project proposes / Correspondences: daily practices of living in the city from a gender perspective. Within the framework of this project, we aim to visualize, problematize and continue the work that the seedbed has been advancing on the practice of correspondences, besides maintaining the fundamental character of the seedbed as a space for the training of students/investigators of undergraduate and post-graduate forms. We are a multidisciplinary group of teachers and students of different fields of the Arts and Human Sciences such as anthropology, the cultural studies, cinema, the art sonoro and the plastic arts, which seeks to create dialogues between knowledge and the need of the future. a very expanded sense) translate into structures and procedures of investigative action.The initial inquiries in exercise within the seedling have allowed us to understand the potentialities that the practice of correspondence opens us as an ethnographic exercise as well as of critical elaboration and at the same time expressive and sensitive on the present convulsed of which we are part
