# REPLIES FROM THE PROFESSOR TO THE STUDENT'S CONTRIBUTIONS: A MICROETNOGRAPHIC STUDIES IN THE CLASS AULA

The results of this microethnographic study are included in the topic of Higher Education, specifically in Nursing Education. They are derived from a multicentric research effort led by the University of Barcelona in the context of the macroproject entitled "Disciplining knowledge, pedagogical knowledge and learning located: genesis and mutual influences in university teaching."The study describes how the teacher generates and builds a response to the student's contribution to the communicative interaction that occurs in the classroom. This was established from questions such as: what aspects did he base himself to offer a response to the student? What did he intend to achieve with his response? What criteria or strategies did he use to elaborate the answer? Describe how the teacher organizes and elaborates the answers he gives to the questions and contributions of the students in the academic field. Based on the approaches of the ethnographic research, the study was focused on identifying the explanatory categories obtained by comparison and theoretical interpretation of the data obtained from the observation and data in the field. The results were analyzed the classes given by two professors of nursing of the National University of Colombia.. It is noted that the main intention in answering a question is to resolve doubts that contribute to establishing a clear and understandable answer.
