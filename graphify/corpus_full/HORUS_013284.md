# Application of Deep Learning techniques in solar photospheric modelling and observation

This work is framed in the applications of deep neural networks for the modelling of phenomena present in the solar fotosphere. The proposed research is based on the construction of a convolutal 3D neural network of a generational type, DCGAN by its acronym in English, making use of the modules of artificial intelligence of Python as Pytorch for architecture of the neural network. It is intended to train a neural network capable of generating groups of cubes of a high similarity with training cubes, these cubes correspond to physical magnitudes of the solar fotosphere such as density, magnetic field, plasma velocity, temperature, among others, obtained from the simulation code MURAM. The present work seeks to take its results and take them as training data for the neural network developed between 2001-2005 with the aim of generating realistic simulations of magneto-convection processes and magnetic activities (later to perform the analysis of the solar zone and the results of the solar., temperature, together others, obtained from the simulation code MURaM. Simulation code developed by the Solar-MHD group of the Max Planck Institute developed between 2001-2005 with the purpose of generating realistic simulations of magneto-convection processes and magnetic activities, which have an effect on the convective zone of the sun, The present work seek to take its results and take them as training data for the neural network generating new data with a similarity in a visual way and in the physical sections, then make a comparison between the results and the training data, the challenges proposed to use these tools in the study of the solar photosphere, flux tubes and pores
