# Model of an innovation space that allows the connection between the capacities of students of the Faculty of Mines of the National University and social problems

The project is designed to provide a strategic model for the redesign of the Aula STEAM SonnyTF as a space for educational innovation aimed at strengthening the connection between the student capacities of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia and the problems of its environment. The model is based on the intersection of semantic and prospective analysis applied to key institutional documents: the Engineering Manifesto for Life, the Profile of Egress and the Profesional Profile. Through the TF-IDF technique, 30 strategic needs were identified, which were contrasted with the characteristics of the existing classroom. The analysis of gaps allowed to identify a smaller relative presence – compared to other dimensions – of aspects such as territory, ethics, sustainability, teacher integration and systemic articulation. Through the MICAC method, the strategic variables were classified according to their degree of influence and dependence, prioritizing six fundamental dimensions for the redesign: training, institutional capacity, pedagogical actions, teacher professionalism, student participation and individuals. Based on these variables, four prospective scenarios were constructed, selecting as a transformation horizon the “incorporating ecosystem”, oriented to position the classroom as a theoretical, and technical processes. The gap analysis revealed significant doubts and expectations in dimensions such as territory, ethics, sustainability, faculty integration, and systemic articulation. Through the MICMAC methodology, variables were classified based on their influence and dependence, leading to the priorization of six key dimensions for the redeployment: pedagogical training, institutional capacity, educational actions, faculty professionalism, student participation, and human development. Based on these variables, four prospective scenarios were constructed, conforming which the “Transformative Ecosystem” scenario was selected as the target vision.
