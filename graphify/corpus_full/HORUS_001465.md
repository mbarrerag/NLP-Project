# Epidemiology and results in a group of patients with clinical suspicion of pleural tuberculosis in the city of Bogotá, Colombia.

Objective Characterize patients and evaluate the diagnostic usefulness of molecular and phenotypic tests in the diagnosis of pleural tuberculosis (TBP) in the city of Bogotá. Methods 80 samples of pleural fluid were collected. Each sample was performed: real-time PCR (qPCR-IS6110); Genotype MTBDRplus®2.0; multiple PCR IS6110-hsp65; PCR-IS6110; bacyloscopy; fluid medium (MGIT) and solid media culture (Lowenstein Jensen and Stonebrink modified by Giraldo). Paraclinic data were included such as: cytochemical, ADA, HIV and cancer. Results 17/80 (21.2%) patients were positive for TBP, using molecular methods; of these, 10/17 were treated. TBP was more diagnosed in men (82.4 per cent), and in patients with HIV (58.8%). Culture and bacyloscopy were positive in 2/17 patients, the ADA was positive in 8/17 patients. Conclusions The diagnosis of the diagnosis was related to the diagnosis of the patient
