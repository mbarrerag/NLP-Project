# GEOMETRIC DESIGN OF VIAL INTERSECTIONS WITH 3D CIVIL

This document is the continuation of the text: "Ways with Civil 3D geometric design", since it describes the advanced design of special road infrastructures, such as intersections at T level, X level and roundabouts; intersections at level in trumpet and clover and special infrastructures such as Tunnels, bridges and local roads connections. This document is the result of the teaching and research work carried out by the authors in the development of the curricular projects of Topography Engineering and Specialization in Urban Road Design, Transit and Transport
