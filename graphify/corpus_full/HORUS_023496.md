# CHEMICAL EDUCATION CHEMICAL CHEMICAL CHEMICAL CHEMICALS CENTERED ON LEARNING.

This paper presents some challenges that the teaching of science in general, and chemistry in particular, must face in a global scenario that seeks to transition from traditional models based on the "transmission" of concepts to one in which the student is the centre. It is a document that, from a critical perspective, is part of a comparative documentary review of contemporary references in education, subject programs of chemistry courses, content of textbooks, national science standards and standardized test questions, to show the challenges for the teaching of chemistry today. The teaching of science in general and chemistry in particular must be transformed. Students and teachers must take on a constant role of autonomous apprentices that allows them to understand chemistry as teaching in a changing scenario in which learning is sought to be the center. Such learning must also be situated, meaningful, relevant to the life and context of students and of teachers in particular. Current curricula, classroom tools, standardized assessments that condition different classroom practices are far from a chemical teaching focused on the learning of students and teachers, so citizens must be transformed from the world they truly live to form
