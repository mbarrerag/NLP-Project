# Systematization of the intervention and family support methodology Care for me- Taking care of the Centers Protect Bogotá

Within the framework of the pandemic in 2020, the Centros Protective employers start the process of construction of the Methodology of care and family intervention Care me- Caring for us, which has as one of its main theories the Intervention Relationship Based on Trust (TBRI), an intervention that is born in the Christian University of Texas, attached to the program of Psychology. This methodology was constructed from the collection of the experience of working with the family of professionals of the centers Protect. In 2023, the Resolution No 0218 of February 8, places the Centers Protect as a service that has a Strategy of Risk Prevention of Institutional Location, which implies that the Methodology is applied with the families whose children are institutionalized or in the process of follow-up with an Administrative Process of Restoration of Rights (PARD) and with the families that participate in the Strategy. This research aims to systematize the experience of the development and implementation of the methodology based on the accounts of the Technical Team of the Centres Protect, the professionals in Be-workers applying the methodology and the families.
