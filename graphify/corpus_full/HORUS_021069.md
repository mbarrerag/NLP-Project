# White City Times: Research Seed Creation _x0093_Time, city and photography_x0094_

We propose this project as a visual and artistic reflection around the particular forms of the temporal experience in the context of the White City, the campus of the National University. This visual reflection starts from the premise that in our historical moment photography has become a sort of common language, very appropriate for the generation of that type of strangeness and break in the perception of time. Therefore the proposal focuses on experimentation around the photographic device and its possibilities as machine of time. It is the photography that has developed the idea of the instant during its history, but also that which has contributed to the visual fragmentation of time and that originates a malleable vision of the same that derives in the cinema and the video. Therefore, the photographic one constitutes the axis of this plastic experimentation. We detect that in the context of a contemporary mega urb like Bogotá, certain daily phenomena in the campus can represent particular forms of the time, that they are different from those that occur in the rest of the urban landscape. We are interested in questioning the way that the temporal experience assumes in the urban everyday life in these first years of the 21st century, since we consider that the time of the urban planning, that the current, and the consummaturated, that the participants of the Semillero de investigación creación _x0093_Time, city and photography_x0094_ (which was created in the faculty of arts of the National University of Colombia since 2018 and has already generated several projects continuously, code hermes 78) seek through their creative work to make visible other temporalities that inhabit us, magnify them, understand them and/or enhance them. It is in this sense that the use of the photographic device offers us a form of plastic experimentation appropriate in the context of this proposal. To this end we will explore methodologies of photographic creation that involve especially temporary problems such as long exhibitions, time and hybrid formats of photography and moving image such as time-lapse, animated GIF, cinemagraph and others.
