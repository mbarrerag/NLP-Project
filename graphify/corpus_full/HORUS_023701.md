# OBTAINATION OF NATURAL COLORANTS OF VEGETAL ORIGIN

The colour in the food industry is one of the most important quality attributes that directly influences consumer preference and final selection (Demirhan and Özbek, 2011; martins, roriz, morales, barros and ferreira, 2016). Synthetic colourants such as erythrosin (E127), ponceau 4R (E124) and tartraz (E102) are used in the processing of food. In order to intensify, compensate or add color to a manufactured product, thus maintaining a natural appearance, close to the fresh product (Santos et al., 2014). However, different studies have reported that the consumption of artificial colourants develops allergies and increases the risk of contracting cancer (McCann et al., 2007; Mpountoukas et al., 2010).
