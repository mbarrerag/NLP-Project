# Properties of composite materials reinforced with guadua fibers: a comparative study

Abstract A numerical correlation model was developed to predict the effect of the modification of the surface of a fibre on the mechanical properties of biocomposite panels made with bamboo fibres and vegetable resin. For the study, the three surface treatment methods were mercerization, plasma and ozone treatment. To analyze the influence of each treatment on the surface of the fibres, a study was carried out of its morphology, chemical composition and crystallinity by means of electron scanning microscopy, spectroscopy of energy dispersion of X-ray and diffraction of X-ray. A characterization of the physical properties of the fibres was carried out by determining the density and absorption capacity. The influence of the treatment on the mechanical properties of the fibres was analyzed by determining its resistance to traction. These results were used to determine the elastic properties of the layers that make up the biocomposite, applying the modified percentage rule for anisotropic materials. The numerical models were elaborated using a commercial program of finite elements, considering a linear analysis.
