# Alternatives for increasing the liquidity of the wholesale energy market – MEM – in Colombia.

According to the Administrator of the Commercial Exchange System – XM, at the last cutoff date (July 2021) the monthly liquidity index of the regulated and unregulated market was 101.2% and 185.5% respectively; that is, regulated demand is transpired only once, while unregulated demand reaches energy movements or transactions that reach 1.8 times that demand. By contrasting this figure with that of markets such as Germany or Nordpool that reach values that oscillate between 960% and 760% with respect to their individual demands (Office of Gas and Electricity Market, 2010), it becomes clear that in Colombia there is still a significant development gap in terms of liquidity. In this regard, the recognition of electricity as underlying the value of financial derivative contracts, and the consolidation of Derivex as administrator of these, has pointed to the generation of liquidity options that make possible the dinamization of energy transactions, timely in the negotiations carried out in the wholesale energy market –MEM. In this Final Work, we aim to propose alternatives for the second place of liquidity of the MEM, in Colombia, in terms of the first-to-consolidating energy development and the main-based market
