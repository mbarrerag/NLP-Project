# FREIGHT TOUR SYNTHESIS BASED ON ENTROPY MAXIMIZATION AND FUZZY LOGIC

This paper presents an improved entropy-based freight tour synthesis using fuzzy logic (FL). One approach used in formulating freight tour models is entropy maximization, which aims to obtain the most probable freight (trucks) tour flow distribution in a network based on traffic counts. These models consider fixed parameters and constraints. However, the costs, traffic counts, and truck demands vary depending on human behavior, and they are not captured in detail in such models. Thus, as FL can include such variabilities in its modeling, it allows for obtaining solutions where some or all the constraints do not entirely satisfy (but are close to) their expected values because of the flexibility provided by this method to the model. Moreover, the modeling approach used based on FL theory is the membership function, specifically the triangular membership function, which is defined by three points corresponding to the vertices. This optimization problem was transformed into a bi-objective problem when the optimization variables are the membership and the entropy. The performance of the proposed formulation was assessed in the Sioux Falls network.
