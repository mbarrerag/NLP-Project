# USING A MOOC IN NATURAL AND SOCIAL SCIENCES AS A MEAN TO PREPARE PRE-COLLEGE STUDENTS

Considering that many Colombian pre-university students exhibit poor performance in social and natural sciences, and that this condition becomes a limitation when accessing higher education, this article presents an initiative for the development of two courses in the MOOC format to fulfill such a need. A brief description of the creation process of these courses is presented, as well as a report of the experience in the execution of an edition that was carried out in 2020 with about 500 students.
