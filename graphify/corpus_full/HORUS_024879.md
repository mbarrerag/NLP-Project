# Parallel lives: contact, democracy and violence against WAIMIRI-ATROARI and NUKAK. A CHESTORY TEST COMPARIED ON TWO INDIGENOUS PEOPLES IN AmazONIA

This text provides a comparative exercise of contact between two Amazonian peoples. The sections of the population isolated after contact see dramatically affected their lives; this reality seems to be largely unknown to national society or little interest awakens it, despite the fact that what is committed is the survival of human groups. With a detailed follow-up of sources the text reconstructs under similar categories of analysis the two cases, which allow to affirm the right to life of these peoples and the responsibility that we have with them
