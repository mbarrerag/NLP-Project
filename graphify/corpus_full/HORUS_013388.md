# Identification of molecular markers associated with resistance to orange rust caused by Puccinia kuehnii (W. Krüger) E.J. Butler in sugar cane varieties

The orange rust is a disease of economic importance in the cultivation of sugar cane in almost all producing regions of the world. The variability in the susceptibility of commercial varieties to this disease has led to genetic resistance being the main control measure.
