# EFFECTS OF CRIMMINAL VICTIMMIIZATION IN THE PERCEPTION OF THE WORLD OF A SAMPLE OF COLOMBIAN STUDY STUDY OF HIGHER RACE

The objective was to know the effects of criminal victimization on the perception of the world in a sample of Colombian students from higher careers. A measure of the perception of the world was applied to a non-probabilistic sample of technical, technological and university students (N = 3,436) from 33 Colombian cities. The results showed an excellent internal consistency (Cronbach alpha =.94, Omega =.94) of the measure of the perception of the world, as well as an acceptable construct validity (CFI =.93, TLI =.92, RMSEA =.07, SMSR =.04). Victims of crime (sexual aggression, road injuries, homicide or suicide of a family member, harassment) showed a more negative world view than non-victims. In addition, men and low-class people reported a perception of the world more negative than women and those of middle and high class.
