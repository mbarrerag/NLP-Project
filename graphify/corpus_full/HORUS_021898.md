# The judge's interpretative challenge to the reasonable length of the trial, based on the paradigm shift of the 1991 Constitution. Study of article 121 of the General Code of the Process.

The change in the constitutional paradigm resulting from the entry into force of the 1991 Constitution generated a transformation in the traditional framework of understanding of legal interpretation in Colombia; this constitutes a challenge to the judicial work from the perspective of the Social State of Law and the right to Due Process as a multidimensional system of guarantees oriented towards material justice and the defence of fundamental human rights. This work lands the meeting points and the various tensions generated by the dynamic referenced by the case study of article 121 of the General Code of the Process; for this purpose, it is argued that the reasonable duration of the process and effective jurisdictional protection are constituent parts of the Due Process and fundamental axes of transformation that, from the perspective of judicial activism, must account for a jurisdictional agency that is consistent with modern constitutional developments
