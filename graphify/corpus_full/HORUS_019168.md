# HYPERTENSION AND CARDIOVOSCULAR RISK

arterial hypertension is one of the main diseases worldwide and constitutes an important cause of morbidity and mortality for low- and middle-income countries. to determine the epidemiological importance of arterial hypertension as a factor of cardiovascular risk in different studies carried out worldwide, in Latin America and Colombia. more than 90% of hypertensive patients suffer from the primary form of the disease, which is associated with an increase in peripheral vascular resistance. The socioeconomic characteristics of countries and the individual educational level are related to the prevalence and proper management of this pathology. The increase in the prevalence of chronic diseases, together with important historical events, were determinants for the development of global epidemiological studies such as Framingham Heart Study
