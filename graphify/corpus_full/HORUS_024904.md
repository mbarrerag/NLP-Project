# PARTIAL PURIFICATION OF PETITIONS OF THE VENAN OF SCIRPION HADRUIDS CHARCASUS (KARSCH, 1879) WITH PARTITAL ANTI-CROBIAN ACTIVITY PURIFICATION OF PEPTIDS FROM THE STORPION VENOM HADRUROIDS CHARCASUS (KARSCH, 1879) WITH ANTI-CROBIAL ACTIVITY

The poisons of many species of scorpions are sources rich in biologically active components, such as the antimicrobial peptides, biomolecules that have not yet been studied from the poison of Hadruides charcasus.The aim of this article is to evaluate the antimicrobial activity of the partially purified peptides of the poison of the Hadruides scorpion.Asus. From 15.46 mg of total protein of the poison of the H. charcasus scorpion were partially purified its peptides by means of chromatography of filtration in gel using G-75 sephandex, consecutive to an ion exchange chromatography in CM-Sephadex C-25. The estimated molecular weight of the peptides was determined by electrophoresis PAGE-SDS-Tricin at 15% and the evaluation of antibacterial and antifungal activity was used in CM-Sephadex C.
