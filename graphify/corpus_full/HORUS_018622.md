# SUSTAINABILITY, SHORT SUPPLY CIRCUITS AND PUBLIC FOOD PURCHASES

This book seeks to link themes such as sustainability, supply and the role of the state in directing the food system to guarantee food and nutritional sovereignty and security based on theoretical contributions and empirical research.Given the complexity of the themes, it was necessary to use interdisciplinarity;In this way, the book was organized and written by several hands, with researchers from different areas of knowledge.We also sought to address these themes based on the diversity of experiences of Latin American and European countries to illustrate them in a broader and more comprehensive way
