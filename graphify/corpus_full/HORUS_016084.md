# DEFINITION OF A META-Model FOR THE DESIGN OF EDUCATIVE SOCFTWARE APPLICATIONS BASED ON USEABILITY AND PEDAGOLOGICAL KNOWLEDGE

The aim of this study is to define a meta-model for designing educational software applications that includes usability and pedagogical knowledge criteria, in order to model easy-to-use teaching tools. Each relevant element in the design of an educational software application is conceptually described. Pre-conceptual schemas are selected as syntax to represent the identified elements and relationships, since the schemas can be easily exemplified by the use of instances, are computer-readable (they are executable) and are easy to understand. The meta-model is designed and validated. The results show that the meta-model improves the design of educational software applications. It is concluded that the meta-model contributes to the formalization of the design of educational software applications by ensuring the inclusion of criteria related to usability and pedagogical knowledge
