# Human Space Footprint (IHEH) in local landscapes, as a contribution to territorial environmental management. Case Study: Túa River Basin, Casanare - Colombia

Natural and human systems are integrated coupled systems, the product of complex interactions and feedback, which change from one state to another, over time and in space. These interactions have not been entirely harmonious, since human activities and their impact have become the main driving force of the degradation of the global, regional and local ecosystem, resulting in the alteration of the functioning of ecosystems and ecosystem services provided. To understand the human impact on natural systems, adequate representation of modified landscapes is required and an explanation of the relationships between socioeconomic and biophysical factors. This study, quantifyes the human footprint in local landscapes, with case of study in the Rio Túa basin (Casanare – Colombia), where not only includes 14 geographical indicators of human influence operating at a local level, but evaluates the incorporation of the biophysical vulnerability index into the human footprint equation. In addition, the correlation between the results of human footprint and the management of land use defined in the planning instrument (POCA).
