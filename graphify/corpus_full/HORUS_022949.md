# Impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on the classroom and the work of the natural science teacher: case study.

The present research addressed from the focus of a case study, complemented by a descriptive analysis, typical of a mixed methodology, shows how the disruption caused by the pandemic of the COVID-19 impacted in the classroom and in the work of the teachers of Natural Sciences and Exacts of the Abraham Lincoln College (CAL), forcing them to migrate from face-to-face dynamics; direct contact of teachers – students, to already existing virtual environments, but undervalued in the pedagogical work, showing the need to restructure and plan hybrid teaching environments, framed in the dimensions of adaptation, training, challenges and opportunities, established according to the phases that the educational establishment determined during the contingency, without stopping the training process
