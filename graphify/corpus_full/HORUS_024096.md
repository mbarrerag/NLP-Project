# The Intangible Cultural Heritage Communicated: the cases of the Caribbean Museum and the Carnival Museum of Barranquilla

This document contains three components of the final master's work, derived as a product of my inclinations during the formation in museums: the intangible cultural heritage; the communication in and from the museums, and the patrimonial/territorial marketing. The first component, a stay carried out in the installations of the Museum of Bogotá, which aimed to make an acknowledgement and historical comparison of the internal areas in key moments of the institution, to see in depth the relations of administrative dependence and management of resources associated with the safeguarding of the heritage in Bogotá. The second component, the practice, carried out in the District Institute of Cultural Heritage (IDPC), in support of the sub-direction in divulgation, to strengthen the programs focused on the Intangible Urban Cultural Heritage, with the aim of continuing a process of bibliographic research, consolidation of databases of the PCI and participation in the joint workshops with the carrying communities, which served for the promotion and dissemination of the manifestations in all Bogotá and non-carganese areas, with the aim of the future of traditions incumblish. The third conceptual component brings together a process of deepening and subsequent critical analysis of the actions carried out by: The mystitu and the Museum of the partnership with heritage's safeguarding in Bogota. The second component, the practical, carried out out in the "Instituto Distrital de Patrimonio Cultural" (IDPC), in assistance to the dissemination's sub directorate, to strengthenthen the programs focused on Urban Intangible Cultural Heritage, to continue a process of bibliographic research, consolidation of ICH databases and participation in joint workshops with the bearer communities, which served for the promotion and diffusion of the manifestations throughout Bogotá with bearers and non-bearers, with the future alternative alternative that the traditions do not result success to time and disinterest.
