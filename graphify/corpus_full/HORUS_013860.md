# Obstinated hardness, I call the door of a stone.

The research work - creation seeks to problematize the notions established in the body, objects and space from a political, erotic and poetic relationship of affection with the subjects. It aims to cultivate an affective and material relationship between objects and bodies, detailing the corporalities that are put into tension and the gestures with the matter that make this dialogue possible. Taking into account the practice of collecting, preserving and transforming matter, the study investigates in the temporal and spatial scale that a body can have being mineral, stone, rock, sediment. Likewise, it asks for the weights and burdens that contain the bodies and materialities, and how this is related to the histories, memories and affections among itself. It is a question for the possibility of eroding the body, of hollowing it, opening it, emptying it, so that it can renew its affective materiality and its creative power. All this happens under the living encounter with the subjects, the attention placed in the presence and listening with the plastic and sound materials
