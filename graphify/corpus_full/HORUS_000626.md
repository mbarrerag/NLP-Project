# Study of the possibilities of incorporating low-cost intelligent transport systems (ITS) to meet the functionalities required by traditional ITS on concessioned roads

The SITs aim to mitigate common transport problems such as congestion, road safety, reduction of accidents, among others. These attributes are sufficient to consider the SITs as a complement to concessions. Despite this, the implementation, maintenance and use of the traditional SITs is complex and costly. Therefore, in a concession contract on road the SITs are the last to be implemented, which generates delays in the works and reduces the comfort for the travelers. In order to reduce this complexity, in recent years the concept of low cost SIT appears, a technology that takes advantage of the low cost resources and the infrastructure already existing with which it is intended to replace, supplement or support the traditional SITs. In this sense, this research addresses the functionality offered by the low cost and contractual SITs established in the concessioned roads of fourth and fifth generation to study the viability of their operation and know whether they can be taken into account for future contracts of road concessions of next generations.
