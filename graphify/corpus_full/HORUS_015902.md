# CLIMATE RELILIANCE INDICATORS AND CONTENTS: A DISCRIPTIVE REVIEW OF LITERATURE

The increase in resilience to extreme weather events has become a major issue in recent years; this strategy reduces the likelihood of a community suffering significant losses during such events. Climate resilience is the ability of a system to cope with a climate-related phenomenon, trend, or disturbance. This concept can be seen as an integrated function of adaptive, absorbing, and transformative capabilities of a system
