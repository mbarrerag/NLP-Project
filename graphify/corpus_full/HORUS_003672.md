# Fintech and its relationship to SME funding: a systematic literature review and a future research agenda

This paper presents a systematic review of literature, the state of art and a future research agenda on the relationship between Fintech and the financing of Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). Digitization and technological evolution have transformed access to financial resources, challenging SMEs to obtain adequate funding. Fintech’s emergence offers new perspectives and opportunities, potentially revolutionizing the way SMEs access and use financial services. In this context, 26 articles were thoroughly examined to analyze the relationship between Fintech industry and its relationship with SME financing, possibilities, difficulties and expectations in the current market in different parts of the world. Exploratory research was found to be the most commonly applied perspective to analyze the relationship between Fintech and SMEs
