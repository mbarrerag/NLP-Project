# Information update strategy for the appropriate, safe and efficient control of the storage areas of substances, reagents, chemicals, reference materials instruments and other devices in the laboratories of the National University of Colombia

In laboratories for the repeated use of chemicals, reagents, substances, reference materials, instruments and other devices, it is essential that they be collected in ordered and protected spaces, in order to prevent accidents to laboratory users. The objective of this project proposal is to develop a comprehensive management plan for the storage areas and substances, reagents, chemicals, reference materials, instruments and other devices in laboratories at the UN. The expected results will be the updated inventory of storage areas of UN laboratories, updated inventory of substances, reagents, chemicals at the UN, updated inventory of reference materials instruments and other devices at the UN, updated inventory of hazardous waste generated by laboratories at the National University of Colombia, information on labels and safety data sheets of identified and required chemicals, laboratory census stickers, database in Access or Oracle with all information identified in laboratories, improved, updated and compatible with the Hermes system, update of the hazard matrix in laboratories according to the USG and the corresponding prioritization for their intervention, a strategy of communication and dissemination of results
