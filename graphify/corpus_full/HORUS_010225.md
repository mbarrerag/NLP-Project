# RELATIONS FROM PENUMBRA: DESINITION STRENGTHENED IN COLOMBIA AND THE WORK ON NEBULOSING

In 2019 I directed the documentary instalative-performatic Nebulous Relatos (RN), starring relatives of forced disappeared in Colombia. The aim of this article is to use this work to, first, analyze the artistic visibility of these victims, who are resiliding and demanding justice, and second, to feed the critique of art that studies their struggle. For this I establish three axes. The first contextualizes when mentioning accounts of the protagonists of RN, by providing information related to the regions to which they refer and by providing general ideas of what disappearance means for these relatives, who live in uncertainty by not knowing the fate of their loved ones. The second indicates an ethical position that allowed me to testify that the protagonists, daily, develop a documentary assembly that requires their bodies to be and that have feelings found with documents of this assembly (affected by photographs and rejection towards bureaucratic documents).The third one points out the importance of art proposing new ways of giving voice to the victims, exposes how I sought to create these forms of daily assembly of the protagonists for RN and that it enunciates metaphors between the materiality of this work and forced disappearance
