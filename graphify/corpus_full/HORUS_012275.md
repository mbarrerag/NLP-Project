# Parametric phenotypic stability analysis of apomictic hybrids of Brachiaria (Brachiaria humidicola) in multiple environments.

Brachiaria (Brachiaria humidicola) is a species of wide adoption as fodder for cattle feed adapted to large tropical and subtropical areas of South America, Africa and Asia. The program of genetic improvement of brachiaria, which advances the International Center of Tropical Agriculture (CIAT) in Colombia, seeks to select stable hybrids of high yield for acid soils. However, the interaction genotype by environment (IGA) hinders the selection and phenotypic stability, however, there is no common consensus on the definition of stability nor how to estimate it. For the analysis of stability have been used from univariate methods to multivariate models, where there is a current trend for the use of multivariate linear models (BLUP). The aim of this research was to compare the accuracy in the estimation of IGA by univariate models, the model of main effects additives and multi-al interaction of fixed effects (AMMI) and the model best linear unbiad prediction (BLUP).
