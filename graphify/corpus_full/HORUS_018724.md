# Latin America: GEOPOLITICAL DISPUTE SCENEY BETWEEN GLOBAL SIGNATURE POINTS

At the beginning of the third decade of the twenty-first century, Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC) faces challenges of considerable magnitude in its international relationship. Some of them directly concern the interactions between states, others involve non-state actors who call on the governments of the region and are able to deploy own strategies affecting the global issues; some more, as for example the international organizations, establish their links the subcontinent from their classification as developing countries.While the region is characterized by a great heterogeneity in the configuration and implementation of the foreign policy agendas of the Latin American countries, it is possible to identify some cross-cutting issues that establish a common basis and allow to make a joint reference to the trends that mark the international policy of LAC. These are challenges such as reducing the vulnerabilities faced by international markets, the redefinition of the traditional US hegemony in the region and the diversification of diplomatic ties, the responses to the ecological crisis and climate change, migration, drug trafficking and the fight against organized crime, and, more recently, the impact and management of the pandemic of the Covid-19.
