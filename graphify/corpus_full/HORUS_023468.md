# INTERTEITING PIXELS AT TIME. A MOCHE GUERRER, HIS KNOWLEDGE AND DIGITAL RECONFIGURATION BY THE ART OF THE PIXEL IN MINECRAFT AND IN A TELAR

This text addresses a pixel story related to its identification in a textile mache with a figure of a warrior and its subsequent reconfiguration in minecraft and in a digital loom. It will be about the possibility of acquiring knowledge of the art of an ancient Latin American culture by a community of digital natives through the identification that was made of a family element, the pixel; as well as the possibility that this provided to bring the work to the present with resources of the digital world; and because, as suggested by the poetics of things, it was possible through creation, a connection with the ancient artists. In that sense, in the text some particularity of the design of the Moche warrior will be presented, as that related to geometric design and the use of the early pixel, and immediately aspects of the experience of reconfiguration and brought back to the present through the digital resources already mentioned
