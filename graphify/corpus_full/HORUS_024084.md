# The Integrated Report in Colombia: an evaluation of the situation and the factors that drive its implementation in the period 2013-2018

This thesis evaluates the institutionalization of integrated reports in the Colombian context.Inside, it examines the implementation process of this type of corporate report, the key actors in the process and the dissemination of the report by Colombian companies that declare it in the GRI database in the period 2013-2018.The research question is: what is the situation and factors driving the implementation of the Integrated Report in Colombia?Thus, this thesis takes the case of the institutionalization of IR in Colombia, taking as units of analysis the Colombian companies that released this report in the period from 2013 to 2018. From a critical-interpretive perspective, socio-institutional references are used to account for this process of institutionalization, as well as the interests declared by companies in its disclosure.Likewise, a content analysis is carried out to characterize the IR disclosure process in the selected Colombian companies, identifying its evolution (companies, number of reports and years) and the degree of implementation.The information analyzed consisted of 34 reports from 10 companies that declare to publish integrated reports, as well as the results of semi-structured interviews with report preparers in companies, regulatory entities, auditing and consultancy companies and some academic experts.The analysis shows that integrated reports are not fully institutionalized in our context and their implementation is still in its infancy.Therefore, compliance with the concepts, guidelines and fundamental contents of the IR structure is insufficient and what is prioritized is compliance with the GRI.Therefore, the reports released by companies are sustainability reports with mention of the IR structure as references for their production.Finally, the approach and interests declared in the reports reflect the need for value maximization and legitimation pursued by companies to attract investors, present themselves as innovators and avoid reputational risks that
