# Characterization of the hailstorms on the city of Bogotá

Using historical records of local newspapers and secondary information from various sources, including IDIGER, SIRE, DPAE, EAAB, UNGRD and IDEAM, it is proposed to carry out the general characterization of hailstorms occurring on the city of Bogotá in the period 2005-2022. Previous studies, carried out by researchers of the group GIREH (within which is the Semillero de Investigación en Hidroología Urbana (SIHU), which presents this proposal), developed a methodology for this purpose, consolidating a geo-referenced database of hailstorms on the city of Bogotá, which includes events up to the year 2004. The proposal here proposed aims to use the same methodology to extend this database up to the year 2022. The usefulness of updated information of hailstorms on the city of Bogotá is evident in aspects such as the prioritization of drainage infrastructure replacement existing in areas impacted by the phenomenon of hails, in the design of new urban drainage infrastructure in the city, in the analysis of the city of infrastructure of all types and in the monitoring and forecast of the phenomenon, among other events, it will be possible to be considered to be an event
