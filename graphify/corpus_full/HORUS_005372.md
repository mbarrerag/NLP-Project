# Stage I non-seminomatous germ cell testicular carcinoma, overall survival in patients after primary treatment

Objective ABSTRACT. Characterize the overall survival (OS) and recurrence-free survival (RSR) of patients with non-seminomatous germ cell testicular carcinoma (NSGCT) stage I derived to different adjuvant options. Search for patients with stage I NSGCT who were subjected to radical orchiectomy from 2010-2022. The description was made with central trend measures. In the regression model there were no differences. No differences were found in OS or SLR. Prospective studies are required to corroborate findings
