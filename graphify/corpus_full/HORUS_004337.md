# The relationship between the enso and the transport of silk in the MAGDALANA-CAUCA area between 1981 and 2023

The transport of sediments has a significant impact on the operation of reservoir projects, as well as on the maintenance of canals for river navigation. In order to analyze the variability of suspended sediment transport and the ENSO, the joint variability between the ONI and MEI climate indices is examined based on information from hydrometric stations located on the Cauca and Magdalena rivers at points that characterize the sediment transport regime in the high, medium and low zones of each basin. It is observed for the two climate indices analyzed, an increase between the events Children and times of low sediment transport, as well as between the events Girls and times of high sediment transport. Through robust linear models between the climatic indices and the transport of standardized sediments, it is found that the MEI explains 43% of the variability of sediment transport in the Magdalena river basin
