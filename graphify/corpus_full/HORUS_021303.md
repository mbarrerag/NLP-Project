# Technological innovation for the exploitation of saline gradients in Colombia

The reverse electrodialysis technology (RED) is a technology of increasing interest for the production of energy from the saline gradient (EGS), which appears as a result of putting in indirect contact two solutions of different salinity, through ion exchange membranes. Currently, the Universidad Nacional de Colombia - Sede Medellín is pioneer in the development of a prototype of functional reverse electrodialysis, at laboratory scale. Previously, this prototype has been operated under laboratory conditions with artificial waters, which has allowed characterizing its electrochemical behavior and efficiency. The operation of this device on a larger scale and with natural waters such as sea water, river water or brine, would allow its implementation for the supply of clean energy in non-interconnected areas such as the Caribbean or the Pacific region. In addition, this latter poses challenges that still have to be addressed from the operational and phenomenological point of view, as well as improvements in the current energy conversion mechanisms.
