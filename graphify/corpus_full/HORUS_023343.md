# ANALYSIS FROM AGRO-ECHOLOGIC AGROFORESTRY OF ANCESTROL PRACTICES IN THE REGUARD OF YAQUIVÁ (INZA, CAUCA, COLOMBIA)

Within Colombian ethnographic literature, there are no records that systematize traditional practices and less their analysis from Agroforestry Agroecological (AA). Therefore, it was proposed as a objective to analyze from AA the ancestral practices in the Yaquiva Reserve. The methodology of research used was based on Participatory Action Research (IAP)
