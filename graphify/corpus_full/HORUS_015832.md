# MODIFICATION OF THE TEXTURE AND MORPHOLOGY OF DIATOMACE LAND BY TERMS TREATMENT

Among the natural ceramic materials, diatomaceous soil has a special interest due to its low toxicity, because it is a basically amorphous silica, with high porosity and permeability, with good mechanical and chemical stability, and relative low cost and abundance. Being a biogenic sedimentary rock, its deposits not only consist of fossilized remains of diatoms, but contain impurities that limit its use. In this context, in this research, the effect of the calcining temperature on the texture and morphology of diatomaceous soil from the municipality of Chivatá (Boyacá-Colombia) was evaluated. The methodology consisted of calcining different samples at temperatures between 200 and 1000°C, by 6h in air atmosphere, in a mufla brand Thermo ScientificF6018. Adsorption isotherms with N2 to 77 K correspond to Type IV according to IUPAC classification, with histéresis rings H3 type associated with the presence of mesoporos by capillary condensation
