# Technical proposal for the implementation of advanced measurement infrastructure in Colombia based on energy efficiency schemes in concentrated demand

In response to the challenges of the energy transition posed by Colombia in the 2030 period, the advanced measurement infrastructure aims to reconfigure the functioning of the sector, fundamental for operational improvement in energy efficiency and bidirectional models, where, typically, the electrical networks operated in a unidirectional manner. The entry of enablers such as intelligent measurement through MAI is planned to acquire, transmit and concentrate electrical information through bidirectional communication in order to improve the reliability, resilience and efficiency of the electrical service. Information and communication technologies stand out as fundamental tools for the control and monitoring in the distribution of energy, allowing flows of information between producers and consumers. This change has transformed users into active part of the system, participating in a dynamic model of active demand and facilitating the articulation of new services shared among those involved. Despite the technical challenges in the standardization and interoperability in the implementation of MAI, the electricity sector in Colombia has established important objectives to boost key processes of digitisation, decentralization and decarbonisation jointly.
