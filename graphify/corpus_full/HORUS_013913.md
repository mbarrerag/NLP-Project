# --Agrarian conflicts, forced abandonment and land grabbing in southern Tolima 1968-2016: From agrarian conflicts to land fever?

The present project has as objective to present and base the approach of the problem of research of agrarian conflicts, the forced abandonment and the dispossession of lands in the south of the Tolima between 1968 and 2016, as a pertinent topic for historical research, whose formulation is born in the framework of the seminars of research of the Master's in History of the National University of Colombia and in the sessions of the Research Group of Agrarian History and Rural Society, of the same department.The historiographical balance allows to glimpse the historical variations of the conflicts for land in the Tolima, which makes this geographical region a case of intrinsic interest that shows different dimensions of the problem of forced abandonment and the dispossession of lands, already as an element of territorial control, as military tactic proper to the armed conflict, or as strategy for land grabbing in a context of economic opening, extractive projects and _x0093_fiebre by land_x0094_.
