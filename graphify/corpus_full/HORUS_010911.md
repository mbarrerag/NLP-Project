# A ANALYSIS OF THE DIVERSITY OF THE STREET-BODY IN FIVE COLOMBIAN CITY

ABSTRACT This article deepens the knowledge of the women inhabitants of the street of the five most dense cities of the country, taking as a backdrop the patriarchal relations of inequality that permeate the different moments of the street habitation starting with the decision to go out and live in this one. With the information available from the censuses of the inhabitants of the street of 2017 and 2019 carried out by the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE) in Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Barranquilla and Bucaramanga, it investigates the various female forms of inhabiting the street and the conditions in which it is carried out. It aims basically to eliminate the masculinization of the street habitation, visualizing the presence of diverse women in cycles of life and with expulsorous experiences before and of habits of subsistence and maintenance during their stay in the street
