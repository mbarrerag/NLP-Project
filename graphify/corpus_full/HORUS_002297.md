# Residual Biomass Development of the Banana Industry for the Development of New Materials.

Within the framework of the specialization in _x0093_Product Design and Development_x0094_, the project entitled: _x0093_Accommodation of Residual Biomass of the Banana Industry for the Development of New Materials_x0094_ is being carried out as final work. This project is being promoted by Banana Paper, a creative and research laboratory formed as an academic group with professional projection, integrated by students of the specialization. Its objective is to research and formulate different types of fibrous foils from the use of the banana rod _x0097_abundant organic residue in the Colombian banana industry_x0097_for the development of sustainable packagings. The work team is formed by an industrial designer, a chemical engineer and an architect, who share the interest to actively contribute to environmental sustainability. The proposal focuses on expanding the base of materials of biological origin that can be integrated into models of circular economy, revalued that traditionally has been considered as agricultural waste.
