# ASSESSMENT OF GROWTH AND DEGRADATION OF THE LAMBDA-CIALOTRIN INSECTICIDES AND INTESTINAL MECADE OF FRUGIPERD SPODOPTER

Spodoptera frugiperda is a polyphagous insect that affects crops of high importance in the food safety world. Its chemical control with insecticides causes the distribution of resistant populations and the environmental accumulation of xenobiotics, representing a problem of interest in public health. Currently, some studies have demonstrated the role of the intestinal microbiota on resistance to insecticides in S. frugiperda. From intestinal bacterial strains of resistant populations of S. frugiperda, the need to evaluate the ability to degradation of commercial insecticides Lambda cyhalotrin (L) and Metomil (M) using spectrophotometric methods.
