# Cytonic of constellations and ferments

The book is expressed as a trace of the artist Gyna Cruz's performative gesture called Ctónica De-constellations and ferments. An investigation into pineapple and banana in Colombian territory that the artist puts in relation to her origins to create literary fragments of mythological and poetic characteristics, including stories of her travels, prescriptions, reflections on constellations and stars; and her encounters with the countryside, with the land, with the crops of the plants already mentioned .
