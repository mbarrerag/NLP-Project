# The gesture in the choral direction and its impact on musical interpretation on a sample of choral processes in the city of Bogotá

The following work weeks to perform an analysis on the incident that the use of choral conducting management can have on the interpretive musical work and development of expression. This process of investigation takes into account different types of choral processes found in the city of Bogotá; the analysis search for basic elements of the technique of choral conducting such as as patterns, marking events and interpretation. After a classification of the groups agreed to the nature of their activity as well to the type of population and voices that conform them, four representative provincial processes to the extent,, the management influences the development and interpretative and expressive result of the choir, since it reflects the driver's version and musical idea of the repertoire. For this reason, however the management is not decisive, it can limit for the progress of the artistic process of the group. This paper makes an analysis of the possible impact of the use of the gesture of choral direction in the musical work interpretive and development of the expressiveness. This analysis is carried out thanks to the observation of different types of coral processes found in the city of Bogotá, and part of the basic elements of the technique of choral direction such as: patterns, events of marking and interpretation. After making a characterization of the groups according to the nature of their activity and according to the type of population and the voices that make them, four representative coral processes of different origin and nature were chosen, with the aim of making a trial observation and an interview to their director.
