# Women victims of the armed conflict in Bogotá: between income generation and life care (2012-2019)

The Colombian sociopolitical conflict has condemned the victims to move to the big cities like Bogotá, where they must face an exclusive productive market, which condemns them to live in conditions of poverty and misery. In the public policy of victims, actions have been established in terms of income generation and socio-economic stabilization that seek to increase the productive potential of the victim population. However, these actions have not had the desired impacts. This qualitative research, carried out an analysis of the implementation of the public policy of income generation for the women victims in Bogotá, from 2012 to 2019. For this, it takes up the narrative analysis approach proposed by Emery Roe, rebuilding the narrative and the counter-arrative.
