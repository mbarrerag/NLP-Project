# New species, new combination, and a revised key to species of Eburella Monné & Martins (Coleoptera: Cerambycidae: Eburiini)

Eburella crucenan. sp. is described from Bolivia. Beraba cauera Galileo & Martins, 1999 is transferred to Eburella Monné & Martins, 1973, with a new state record for Pará (Brazil). An updated key to species of Eburella is provided
