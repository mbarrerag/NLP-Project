# NUTRITIONAL TAMISATION IN THE HOSPITALIZED MANAGEMENT

Nutritional screening is the gateway to a nutritional care process to guide the need for implementation of strategies to prevent malnutrition. This is why identifying the nutritional risk to hospital admission and during pregnancy allows for information on the risks associated with the relationship between weight, size and inadequate food intake, among others, and on the prescription of management plans focused on the prevention of complications during pregnancy that may have long-term implications for maternal and child health (Hoover and Louis, 2019). Over the last decade, research shows that early nu-trition and lifestyle have long-term effects on health and subsequent diseases, it being relevant to establish strategies by the health team focused on optimal programming of metabolic development or programming (Koletzko et al., 2019), a topic studied in the second chapter of this work
