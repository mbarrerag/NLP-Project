# Is accidents killing the Compulsory Traffic Accident Insurance (SOAT)?: a prognostic analysis of accidents in Colombia

This work seeksto determine the impact offuture accident on the sufficiency ofthe SOATtariff, considering both the accident rate and the insurance operating expenses. For the 2021 accident forecast, was used a time series where the variable ofinterest wasthe growth rate of accident vehiclesfromJanuary 2010 to December 2019; the real data for 2020 was not considered because it was an atypical year, so it was assumed that the growth has the same trend of the last 10 years. Based on the growth rate forecast, the number of vehicles involved in accidents was calculated to find the lossratio considering the values of the SOAT coverage. The resultsshowthattheSOATpremiumissufficientto coverthe claims derived fromtraffic accidents; however, when considering the operating expenses, the sufficiency may be affected due to the high percentage of expenseswithin the premiumsissued
