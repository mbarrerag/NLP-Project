# Student Support Search for Primary and Secondary Sources Project 57715 of Teaching Day.

This research aims to develop a historical and conceptual perspective of the influential book An Introduction to Corporate Accounting Standards (Paton and Littleton, 1940). The focus of this paper is to understand initially how certain circumstances were determining the appearance on the scene of this book. In this sense, for example, the creation of federal control agencies, the emergence of the accountants' guild and the institutionalization of accounting university education in the United States will be considered. A second aspect to consider the academic discussion that received monograph No. 3 and the identification of the theoretical roots of the central arguments of this book
