# Model for introduction of vehicle technology for mass public transport by simulation in dynamic systems

The data generated in two decades of operation of the BRT of Bogotá, show its complexity and uncertainty with a high impact on environmental, social and economic aspects. The BRT of Bogotá is one of the most important among the 187 already implemented worldwide, being attractive the application of the Dynamics of Systems to model it and understand its functioning. While most of the models available for the BRT are oriented to the optimization and efficiency of the operation in the day to day, this work seeks to contribute to the creation of a model of high aggregate and long term level. The identification of the main stakeholders with their roles and interests allowed to create a causal diagram of the system, where the normativeness and the investment in new fleet are key elements for reinforcement and stabilisers loops. The consequent diagram of flows and levels, allows quantifying scenarios with the visualization of the size of the fleet and its technological composition, as well as some of its impacts. Among the developed equations highlight those that relate the level of quality perceived by users with the demand of the system. The consequent diagram of flows and levels, allows to quantify the number of users according to the population and the extension of the fleet size of the implementation and the base of the systems. high-level Aggregate and long-term model.
