# Analysis from the normative framework for the use of the Non-Conventional Sources of Renewable Energy – FNCER. Progress and challenges in the implementation of the energy transition in Colombia

The article presents the findings of the search, selection and processing of standards related to the use of the Non-Conventional Sources of Renewable Energy (FNCER) and the Rational and Efficient Use of Energy (URE) in Colombia, for the period 1974-2023. It sought to establish an approach to the direction given to the Energy Transition in the country, which reflects an approach aligned with the conventional view of sustainable development set out in the Brundtland Report. This approach allows identifying niches of opportunity and significant challenges, both in terms of the global climate change scenario and in terms of environmental conflict in the territories in which the agenda is implemented
