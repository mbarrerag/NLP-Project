# RESISTANT UREOLITICS TO METAL FINKS ISILLED BY FINKS OF SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

Cadmium(II) is a toxic cation that produces serious health problems and bioaccumulates in cocoa seeds. This represents a problem for cocoa exporters in Santander (Colombia), since some soils in the area have high concentrations of metal, and must meet the limits of cadmium(II) in cocoa products imposed by the European Union in 2019. Recently, the use of ureolytic bacteria by the method of precipitation of carbonates induced by microorganisms (MICP, by its acronym Microbiologically Induced Carbonate Precipitation) as a measure to mitigate the availability of cadmium(II) in contaminated soils. Thus, the aim of this work was to determine the removal capacity of cadmium(II) in the aqueous medium by three ureolitic bacteria isolated from the cacootic farms of Santander, and to study their resistance to the metal.
