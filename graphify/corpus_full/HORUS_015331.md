# EFFECT OF THE HOLD, SIZE AND TYPE OF UNITS CELDE TPMS IN POROSITY AND PORE SIZE OF THE EXP MODEL OF LATICE TYPE STRUCTURES APPLICABLE TO ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING BY SCAFFOLDS POPHOPOLIMERIZATION

This paper analyzes the effect of the size, thickness and type of unit cells TPMS on the pore size and porosity of the CAD model of lattice-type structures applicable for the additive manufacture of scaffolds by means of photopolymerization processes (MSLA); parallelly, the incidence of intrinsic factors of the manufacturing process for the geometric reproduction of these structures is analyzed. Among the main findings are: calibration of layer exposure time, determination of minimum wall thicknesses and characteristic sizes that can be reproduced, geometric variation of the regions exposed against the unexposed ones, as well as obtaining porosity percentage maps based on wall thickness and cell size for each TPMS. The feasibility of obtaining TPMS structures within the range of permissible dimensional and geometric requirements for scaffolds was determined
