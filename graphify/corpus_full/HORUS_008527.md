# HYGHLIGHT IN THE NUTRITIONAL ONCOLOGY IN PEDIATRIC ONOCHOLOGY

Nutritional intervention in patients in the paediatric population with oncological pathology is fundamental in the comprehensive management of the well-being, growth and development of infants, children and adolescents, since it plays a fundamental role in multimodal management and cancer control, from prevention to palliation. An adequate nutritional care process allows us to understand that there are possible strategies for primary prevention, intervention and nutritional rehabilitation, taking into account that nutrition and nutrition are a potentially modifiable prognostic factor. The nutritional evaluation of patients from diagnosis to treatment and long-term follow-up so that interventions can be implemented and evaluated to improve the outcomes of cancer. This is why this conference illustrates the key aspects to be taken into account in this nutritional care process, a topic that although its clinical and scientific importance is recognized is underestimated in the treatment of pediatric cancer, from a systematic approach perspective and times of opportunity for care
