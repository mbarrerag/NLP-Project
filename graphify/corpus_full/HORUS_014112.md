# Center of thought in Nature-Based Solutions and Territorial Governance

The current society is concerned about the impacts of global climate change, caused by the constant release of greenhouse gases to the atmosphere. It has been projected that its effects will significantly affect the ecosystems and communities that inhabit them (Masson-Delmotte et al., 2021). That is why today we talk about the global climate crisis, positioning the human elements as central element and from there manage the attention of the crisis, especially from the national policies and from the inter-institutional action. In this scenario, increasing the global consensus around the interest in the identification, construction and/or implementation of actions for the conservation of biodiversity, sustainable production and mitigation of the climate change. In particular in Colombia, this interest comes in increasing and is expressed daily in many social actors of the Colombian territory through new perspectives such as the natural-based solutions (SbN). Our country has sufficient normative support to anticipate the territorial processes that favor the implementation of SbN that contribute to sustainable production and reduce the impacts and increase the resilience and adaptation to the climate crisis and loss of biodiversity. These different actions will allow each of the most significant contribution to the implementation of the objectives and the economic and the economic development. For example, resources from environmental obligations and compensations that private companies must transfer to State bodies, whether for compliance with environmental licensing processes and/or taxes related to the use of water resources, the exploitation of biodiversity, greenhouse gas emissions, among others. Thus, a proposal is presented by professors from the headquarters of UNAL Medellín, La Paz and Leticia and has the purpose of creating a think tank for the study and application of the concept of Nature-based Solutions and territorial environmental governance in UNAL, as a space for reflection on public policies, governance mechanisms and management of ecosystem services, encouraging the participation of multiple actors and decision makers from different regions of Colombia
