# ASSESMENT OF H2S, NH3 AND ORGANIC VOLATILE COMPOUNDS EMISSION IN THE PRE-TREATMENT ZONE OF A WWTP (EVALUATION OF THE EMISSION OF H2S, NH3 AND ORGANIC COMPOUNDS VOLATILE IN THE PRETRATTING AREA OF A PTAR)

The purpose of this study was to measure the concentrations of H2S, NH3 and VOCs (generators of offensive odours) and to identify the most abundant VOCs, emitted in the pre-treatment area of the PTAR El Salitre. To quantify, a portable gas detector was used and for the identification of the VOCs, air sampling with SPME - CAR/PDMS 75 fiber was used. This was disorbed in gas chromatograph coupled to mass detectors. Consequently, it can be concluded that the concentrations of H2S, NH3 and VOCs are higher at lower precipitation. The concentration ranges emitted in the PTAR-El Salitre are 5 to 86 ppm for H2S, 1.5 to 10 ppm for NH3, and 2 to 20 ppm COVs. On the other hand, according to the chromatographic analysis, it was identified that the most abundant volatile organic compounds are xylene, toluene, dean and lemon as other PTARs
