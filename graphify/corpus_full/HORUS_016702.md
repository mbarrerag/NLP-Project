# The school in sumapace a political and social history (1960 - 2000)

Rural education in Colombia has been crossed by a series of variations typical of our national history, such as the project of nation that was sought to consolidate through it, whose aim was to integrate the so-called subaltern groups into the economic and social project drawn up by the political elites. For the case of Sumapaz we find an additional element and is the reference to the agrarian conflict, which is the backdrop not only to interpret a good part of the historical configuration of this territory but of our nation. In this sense, it is essential to analyze how the process of gestation of the school in Sumapaz was during the twentieth century, period in which the peasantry was organized not only in the fragor of the struggle for land but in better living conditions as the construction of the rural school, since it was a cohesive element of the peasant population in the country. .
