# Factorial methods of analysis of multiple tables: for the study of some indicators of Colombian higher education.

As a mechanism for monitoring higher education institutions, the Ministry of National Education has information platforms, including SNIES, SPADIES, Minciencias and OLE, each concerned by particular topics of higher education. The data taken from these platforms are structured into tables that satisfy the constraints of the factorial methodologies STATIS and Multifactorial Analysis (MFA) that were used for analysis. The versatility of these factorial methodologies allowed, among other things: to characterize Colombian universities with the variables available in the tables, to show characteristics specific to each sector (Officeal-Private), to understand the dynamics specific to Colombian universities and to identify the shortcomings of information systems for higher education
