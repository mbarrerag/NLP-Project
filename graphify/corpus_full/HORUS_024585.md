# Technological and social innovation in coffee production: case study in the municipality of Pitalito-Huila (Colombia).

Since 2012, Huila is the department that has the largest area sown with coffee, reaching in 2019 17% of the national production. At the municipal level, Pitalito is the main producer in the country. It participates with 1.9% of the national production and 12.7% of the departmental (2018). Pitalito has been winner on 2 occasions (2005, 2006) of the Cup of Excellence contest that selects annually the best coffee in Colombia, promoting in 2015 the opening of the first National School of the Quality of Coffee that, led by SENA, seeks to improve the quality, production and competitiveness of the regional coffee industry. This work analyzes the innovation in the production of coffee in the municipality of Pitalito-Huila. It focuses on technological and social innovation and is based on a mixed research of explanatory-sequencial type (DEXPLIS). Methodologically the study is divided into 3 stages: 1) precampo (diagnóstico and search secondary information); 2) field (application of structured surveys and consultation to experts); 3) quantitative and qualitative information.
