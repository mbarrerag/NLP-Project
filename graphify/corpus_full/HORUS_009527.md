# Atlas corporeal: by an unfinished mapping of the body in key to a surface policy

The understanding of the body is not only fundamental to the establishment of a knowledge about life particularly human, but also to understand the world and the conditions in which bodies take place and can relate to each other. However, the body as an object of study poses challenges, obstacles and demands, since it is not possible to grasp it fully or to call it completely. This would mean separating it from the constant movement and exchange within societies. Therefore, it is necessary to approach the study of the body from a multiple approach that facilitates the location to such category. This work presents different frameworks for reflection that investigate the body in a political perspective: history, materialism, biopolitics and making the world are the locations from which the objective will be to establish the place of the body as a political category. .
