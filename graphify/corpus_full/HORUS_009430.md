# DEFINING MORALITY FOR PSYCHOLOGY: THE RISK OF INTEGRATING PARADIGMS.

The present essay is organized around four themes. First, the essay will address the importance for psychologists investigating morality not only to have a definition of morality, but also to make it explicit. Second, we will discuss how the definition of morality proposed by Dahl is not impartial, although in the effort to be so, it presents possible inconsistencies with the theory from which it is based. Third, we will address the relationship established between the definition offered and the goal pursued with it.
