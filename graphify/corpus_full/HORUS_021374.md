# Evaluation of a methodology for the analysis of the energy quality adaptable to the Tumaco-Nariño electrical system.

This project seeks to establish a methodology for the analysis of the quality of power in electrical systems, which can be implemented in the systems of the special district of San Andrés de Tumaco. Based on the study of the existing methodologies, a preliminary implementation will be carried out based on measurements made in the city of Medellín. From there, its advantages and events will be evaluated, as well as the relevance of the experimental results obtained. From there, a pilot test will be carried out in the special district, in order to evaluate its usefulness and validity. It is hoped that this project can have the support of the CEDENAR company, present in the region
