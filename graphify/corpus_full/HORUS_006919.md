# Portable System for Monitoring Water Quality Parameters

In this work, an electronic probe prototype for a water quality monitoring system is presented. This prototype can measure different water parameters such as electrical conductivity, temperature, potential of hydrogen, dissolved oxygen, and total dissolved solids. The measurements carried out with the homemade multiparametric probe were compared with those made by commercial and professional probes, showing similar precision in data acquisition due to the calibration algorithms implemented for the p H, total dissolved solids, and dissolved oxygen sensors. The prototype was built with low-cost sensors for in-situ measurements and is integrated with a mobile application that allows data to be stored locally or in the cloud, thanks to its Internet of Things technology. Also, the interface is quite user-friendly, with the intention that communities can manipulate the portable systems to carry out citizen water quality monitoring
