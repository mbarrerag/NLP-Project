# Truth and Memory Policy: Observatory of aesthetic expressions of peace

Professor Diana Hincapie Cetina, as co-director of the research group REPENSARelDERECHO and member of the Impulse Committee of the RedPaz UNAL suggested making use of the Anamnesis section of the Verdad & Memoria Observatory of the aforementioned group, opening space for students who benefited from PAES, PEAMA programmes and victims of CANI from the different headquarters, recording images about forgiveness, memory and social reconciliation in their territories or communities; after the unanimous approval of the Impulse Committee in May 2023, she began the formulation of the seedbed RedPaz UNAL and the linking of students. Considering that the UNAL Peace Network ends its activities in June 2024, it intends to continue this important work of the students and to advance research from the anamnetic justice and its relationship with the deliberative democracies that are missing to explore, according to the course of Political Philosophy offered by the teachers Óscar Mejía and Diana Hincapié: Demáticataria de Petit; Democracia wild and insurgent Abensour; Democracia conflicta Paz de Rosanvalónçancia - Sánciónción
