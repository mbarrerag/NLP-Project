# Variability of Heart Frequency (HRV) in healthy, adult canines, residents of hypobaric hypoxia (Bogotá, 2600 m.s.n.m.).

The variability of heart rate (HRV) refers to fluctuations in heart rate beat to heartbeat and represents autonomic and endocrine control over the heart. This parameter has been widely used in human medicine for the diagnosis, monitoring and prognosis of diseases such as Acute Myocardial Infarction. However, in veterinary medicine, specifically in the small animal clinic, it has not been widely implemented due to lack of information about its usefulness, data collection protocols and reference values. This paper seeks to describe the main rates of HRV in the domain of time and frequency in healthy canine residents in the natural hypobaric environment of the city of Bogotá (2600 m.s.n.m.)
