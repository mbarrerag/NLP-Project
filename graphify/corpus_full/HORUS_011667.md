# 1) ARCHOLOGIC HALLS RESTAURATION AND CONSERVATION TECHNICALS 2) DOES DIAGNOSIC TECHNICALS IN ARCHITECTONIC RESTAURATION?

Archaeological objects constitute a material and cultural heritage that must be transmitted to future generations, and therefore their preservation is a crucial issue. Conservation is a subspecialty of archaeology with its own methodology and procedures, therefore, must be considered an integral part of the archaeological process. The preservation of archaeological objects has been revealed as one of the fundamental themes of cultural heritage management. Conservation and restoration treatments are the set of operations and techniques that aim to prolong the life of material culture. A frequent phenomenon during excavations is the observation of deterioration resulting from the impossibility of applying the necessary conservation procedures to the artifacts due to the lack of knowledge about conservation processes. In this sense, the popularization of archaeological conservation, in the understanding that it is an indispensable element of excavations, increases the importance of this discipline
