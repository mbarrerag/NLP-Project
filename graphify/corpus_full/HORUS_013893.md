# Judicial parameters for the eradication of gender stereotypes in the Colombian criminal process: Progress and challenges based on the gender approach

At the international level there is a notable interest in implementing effective measures to eradicate violence and discrimination against women, such as the outcome of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW) and the Inter-American Convention on the Prevention, Punishment and Eradication of Violence against Women (Convention of Belém do Pará).This interest has also been extended to the national stage, as stated in Law 1257 of 2008 _x0093_. This law dictates norms of awareness, prevention and punishment of forms of violence and discrimination against women_x0094_. One of the most complex forms of violence to eradicate corresponds to gender stereotypes, since, since, being _x0093_preconception[es] of attributes or characteristics possessed or roles that are or should be implemented by men and women_x0094_ (Inter-American Court of Human Rights, 2009, para. 401), its diagnosis is complex because they are unconscious or simply not perceived as prejudices (Niño, 2019 and Clerico, 2018).These stereotypes are transversal to all social scenarios, including the judicial context (West, 2010). However, it should be noted that criminal judges in Colombia based on the adverse principle governing the accusatory process established by Law 906 of 2004, have limits on their informal capacities which can only be articulated to guarantee fundamental rights. Therefore, it is timely, current, relevant and novel, through an observatory of research, to propose actions, measures or guidelines to be applied during the development of judicial processes to contribute to the eradication of gender stereotypes taking into account the aforementioned limitations. However, to generate these proposals, the content analysis will be used at a first stage (López-Noguero, 2002), combined with the collection of information through categorical variables, which allows the application of statistical techniques to describe their behavior and identify possible associations among them (Mendenhall & Beaver, 2010). And, at a second point, a mixed approach will be taken through the conduct of interviews with criminal judges of knowledge of the circuit of Bogotá that address processes related to gender violence.
