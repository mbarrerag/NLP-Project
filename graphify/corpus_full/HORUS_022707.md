# DOING (US)FIRE: A CURATORIAL PROPOSAL IN COURSE

This text is based on various artistic/curatory practices that, since the late 1980s, have proposed possible responses to the ankylosing of institutions and their inability to dialogue -from the art system- with sociopolitical co-junctural processes in Abya Yala territories. From the notions of virtuality of G. Pollock, encounter and fabulation of D. Haraway, and the ch'xi of S. Rivera Cusicanqui, we approach these practices located. In them we highlight different poetic/political operations that function as a background to propose an artistic/curatory practice in the present, which we have called Making(us) Fire. This seeks to stress, among others, the following questions: What kind of artistic/curatory operations can we think today, in the context of social and pandemic outbreaks? In what space-time and with what methodologies can artistic events occur that give rise to other epistemologies?
