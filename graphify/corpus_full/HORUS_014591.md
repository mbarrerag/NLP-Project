# Creating environments for learning of chemistry through a primer based on food and cuisine

A booklet based on food and cooking was built to create a chemistry learning environment, including activities which allow women participation, addressed to senior students of the Sede Sabanilla of the Instituto Técnico de Oriente IED, in the rural area of the municipality of Ubaque, Cundinamarca. The instructional material was the result of a qualitative participatory action research, through which the contributions of academic peers as well as classroom teachers, a housewife, and 16 students of 11th grade from the educative community were recovered. Based on the evidence, the instructional material can be considered innovative, motivative, structured, representative, socializer, trainer, and curriculum-oriented. At the same time, it satisfies the factors of didactic adequacy, relevance, engagement, interaction capacity and technological quality. In that sense, the booklet is a contribution to the educational quality of the Colombian rural areas because it is relevant and adapted to the educational context.
