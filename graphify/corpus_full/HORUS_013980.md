# Strengthening spin off Regencol SAS

The direct invitation of the DIEB to strengthen the base-technology spin offs of the headquarters, offers the opportunity to consolidate the initial operation of Regencol SAS because it makes it possible to continue the process of isolation and production of collagen I, the elaboration of a website and the obtaining of the redesigned packagings of the product Regencol Plus A
