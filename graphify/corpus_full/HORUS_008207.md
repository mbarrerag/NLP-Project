# Influence of nutrient concentration in sediments on the structure of mangrove forests exposed to different degrees of impacts in the Colombian Pacific

The mangroves are ecosystems of the tropical intertidal areas adapted to fluctuating conditions that are valued by the multiple ecosystem services they provide. In the soils of the mangroves a large quantity of nutrients from natural and anthropic sources are stored, and which can lead to eutrophic conditions that deteriorate the environmental quality of the ecosystem and affect the function and structure of the biological community.
