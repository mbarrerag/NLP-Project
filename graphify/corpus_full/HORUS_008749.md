# Design of a teaching unit for astrobiology for children in cycle I in schools in the municipality of Ramiriquí (Boyacá-Colombia)

Astrobiology is presented as an opportunity for a multidisciplinary and transdisciplinary contextualized licentious education, with topical issues that arouse a great interest in society in general. The fascination with Astrobiology is due to the very object of study of this field of knowledge, which consists in answering the great questions about our origin and the possibilities of existence of life outside the Earth. Taking advantage of the interest that awakens, it is suggested to incorporate its study in the first educational levels in various municipalities of Colombia. This considering that, in cities like Bogotá, for several years now this multidisciplinary science has been incorporated in schools; but this topic has not yet been explored in many other places. This is the case of Ramiriquí Boyacá, where the schools of the municipality do not have as a axis of work astronomy, and less astrobiology. That is why the elaboration of a didactic unit for the youngest, in this case students of the I cycle. And that through routines of thought and from the natural knowledge of the children, awakens curiosity, and a critical and scientific thought taken by the astomy, and serve as an innovative strategy of teaching in the I. Ramiriquí Boyacá, remain unexplored in terms of incorporating astronomy and astrobiology into their educational framework. Particularly in Ramiriquí Boyacá, schools lake astronomy, let alone astrobiology, as a focal point of study. Therefore, there is a pressing need to develop a didactic unit tailored for young students, specifically those in cycle I. This initiative aims to spark curiosity, Foster critical thinking, and instill a scientific mindset managing astrobiology. By letting children's natural knowledge through thought approaches, this endevor seeks to serve as an innovative teaching strategy supporting educators in the process of providing natural knowledge throughout learning these challenges
