# POLICIES OF THE mirada, MEMORIALIZATION AND MUSEALIZATION.

This review article aims to map out a current state of knowledge about the musealization of memory, its relationship with image and affections, through a comprehensive background and referents study that will account for its main approaches and possible gaps, some of which I intend to respond with my own research project created here. To this end I have located the space opened by the debates of five problemic axes, these are: 1.) Memory giro, 2.) Musealization, 3.) Visual and colonial regime, 4.) Image and memory, 5.) Subjectivation and affections. I begin with recognition that more than definitive definitions, I present operational, provisional and insecure definitions (García Canclini, 2001) that allow us to continue to investigate. Many of the works presented here go through more than one axis, letting us see their porous character.
