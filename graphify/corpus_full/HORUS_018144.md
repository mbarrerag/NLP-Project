# SOIL CATENA IN THE PERUVIAN AMAZON BASIN

The studied soil sequence is in the southeast of Peru included into the departments of Puno and Madre de Dios. This transect was carried out covering a linear extension of 310 km. The toposecuence started in the Peruvian plateau in the city of Macusani, start in the Carabaya mountain range southeast of Peru in the Peruvian Oriental Andes and belonging the Amazon basin to the Puerto Maldonado City. On its upper part is formed by steeply mountain system on a strongly incised V shaped valleys towards the municipalities of Ollachea. The transect cross to a hill piedmont system that precedes materials of alluvial origin in the zone corresponding to the Peruvian Amazon jungle between the municipality of San Gaban and Puerto Maldonado
