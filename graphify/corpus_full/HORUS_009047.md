# INTERACTIVE PERIODISM. RESEARCH STORY IN THE CHASTETER AXE

This initiative aims to build, through 3 phases, a hypermedia journalistic product that recognizes the work of Caldas scientists. For this purpose, a filter will be made to identify these profiles. The tools of scientific journalism will be applied and finally the elements of interactivity (multimedia, augmented reality, among others) will be used to develop a book and an application that connects the analogous product (book) with other products that range from audios to images and dynamic videos. The Social Appropriation of Knowledge is a necessary element for the development of better informed societies and that can make better decisions, for this reason, this project is in line of the ASC Route of Colciencias in its search to communicate and highlight scientific elements to large publics. This project seeks to bring the communities of Caldas closer to the national and international activities and reaches in science and technology, developed or starred by scientists and researchers of the region, through a hypermedial product. With the journalistic profile it is intended to offer a perspective that transcends the conventional academic indicators in which the professional achievements of the people, and instead, will deepen in the path that will lead them to their reach
