# URBAN GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF PUBLIC SPACE IN THE CITY OF MANIZALS, COLOMBIA

Some cities have both urban and population evolution and expansion over time, which leads to thinking about new dynamics of urban growth from the known, without forgetting the importance of public space in the image and development of a city. The objective of this research is to contrast the urban growth of the city of Manizales, Colombia by periods from its foundation to the present from the relationship between public space area, number of inhabitants and urbanized area. From a geographic information system (GIS) with the free QGIS software, cartography was collected with the aim of processing it in conjunction with photographs and layers of data for each period, to analyze the urban form, public spaces, urban density and population increase. Nine periods of time have been identified from 1848 to 2021, where urban and public space growth was demonstrated in an effective way until the period 1993-2008, along with a gradual decrease in population density recorded since the founding of the city.
