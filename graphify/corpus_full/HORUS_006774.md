# Proposal for the study of soils in the school stage using technology

This final work proposes an innovative approach to the study of soils in the school stage, using Arduino technology as a pedagogical tool. The work was carried out with sixth-grade students at the Rochester school, addressing the teaching of soils by measuring temperature and humidity. The practical application of these concepts was implemented in various school areas, such as composter and gardens. In addition to its educational benefits, the project is strategically aligned with the school’s sustainability policies, providing a holistic perspective on the environmental importance of soils. This work contributes to the understanding of the Earth, space and physics sciences, promoting a practical and applied approach to learning .
