# Urban quality densification: counterproposal to Bonavista project on the southern outskirts of Bogotá

This work is a counter-proposal in which it seeks to determine which urban quality concepts can be applied in the development with high densities, especially in the periphery of the city according to the current context. In it, the problems related to the urban space caused by residential groups with high densities at present, specifically in the city of Bogotá. For this purpose, a theoretical analysis of the general context is made, an analysis of the thematic in the city of Bogotá, is then made of the theoretical concepts that improve these urban space problems, then an analysis of some projects considered successful in this topic is made throughout the history. Thus, the theoretical and projectual repertoire of the document is generated, which will give the principles of the final project. Subsequently, a site is chosen that exemplifies the problematic in the city of Bogotá, an analysis of the sector is made with the aim of diagnosing specific problems of the same, and thus a solution is made by a counterproposal of synthesis as a result of previous scenarios, in which the theoretical and projectual repertoire is applied. Subsequently, the feasibility of the counterproposal is evaluated, and finally, and the result of the conclusion of the exercise, is also made, leaving with some consider successful in urban space is made. this theme throughout history.
