# Critical analysis of the multimodal discourse of the video Abortion in Colombia: Death Penalty for the Innocent: Representation of abortion proposed by the Colombian Episcopal Conference

After the judgment C-355 of 2006, which decriminalized abortion in three specific causes, the controversy on abortion in Colombia intensified in multiple sectors of society. Various social actors have been identified as active in the debate, including the Catholic Church, which has been recognized as the main opposition to the practice being carried out, even in one of the three cases endorsed by the Constitutional Court. In the context of a new controversial dispute, at the beginning of 2020, the Episcopal Conference of Colombia, an institution that groups the bishops of the Catholic Church of the country, launched a video on its YouTube channel, according to its representatives, in order to inform and motivate reflection with regard to abortion.
