# • the identification of human immuno-deficence in two re-acceptors of the three-year-old family.

Routine examination of organ donors to detect human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infection has made transmission of the virus through organ transplantation rare. However, despite routine screening tests, HIV transmission remains a risk of organ transplantation as, unlike tissues, solid organs cannot be processed, disinfected, or modified to inactivate infectious pathogens.
