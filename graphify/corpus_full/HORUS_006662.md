# Integrated analysis based on the multi-criteria MCDM method and geographic information systems for the selection of optimal sites for the installation of photovoltaic generation parks in Colombia

Given the need to reduce fossil fuel consumption due to global warming, clean energy, such as solar energy, is presented as a key option for a sustainable energy future. Solar energy, inexhaustible and available to mankind for generations, has gained relevance in Colombia and the world. The implementation of projects that take advantage of this source requires selecting optimal sites, a critical process that must consider climatic, environmental, physical and social factors. In particular, social aspects are often marked by diverse interests that, in some cases, can be conflictive. In this sense, research proposes a combination of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and multicriteria decision-making tools (MCDM) to integrate these interests objectively, applying the hierarchical analytical process (AHP) to ponder the criteria. The model seeks to identify the ideal sites for solar farms in Colombia, balancing the maximum energy production, the minimisation of costs and the reduction of environmental impacts, while ensuring an equitable representation of the different actors involved . an objective manner, using the Analytical Hierarchical Process (AHP) to weight the criteria.
