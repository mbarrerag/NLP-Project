# Search of the B c + → PN c μ + μ Decay with LHCb data.

Using collisions pp corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 4fb −1, recorded by the LHCb experiment in 13TeV mass centre energies, the ratio B(B c + → PN c μ + μ )/B(B c + → J/μ + μ μ ) = (3.49 ± 0.86 stat. ± 0.20 syst. ± 0.34 PDG ) × 10 −1, is determined, where the first uncertainty is statistical, the second systematic and the third is derived from the experimental measurement of the decay rates NR c → pp̄ and J/ → pp̄. Evidence of the existence of the disintegration B c + → NR c μ + μ μ to 3.7μ, including statistical and systematic uncertainties has been found
