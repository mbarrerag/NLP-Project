# ORTOPEDIC CONSIDERATIONS OF THE PEDIATRIC PATIENT POLITRAUMATIZED

The polytrauma is a global public health problem. It continues to be the main cause of death in people between 1 and 45 years of age (1). This is still true in countries with high industrial development and with very advanced health care systems. However, it is worth noting that in the last five decades important advances have been made that have helped to reduce morbidity and mortality due to this cause. This responds to different statistics that suggest that up to 95% of deaths under these conditions are happening in developed countries (2). In these countries up to one million deaths are reported for this cause. It is considered that they are preventable injuries and deaths, even compared with immunopreventable diseases, since their decrease in incidence and prevalence are influenced only by the implementation of serious public health policies
