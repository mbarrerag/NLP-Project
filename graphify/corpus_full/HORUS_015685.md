# INTEGRAL OVERVIEW AND CHILD AND ADOLESS OBESITY: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA

Abstract Fundamentals: The nutritional status of the child population is related to the development of a nation, and it is necessary to implement strategies that will improve the health and nutrition conditions of the population. Therefore, the objective was to carry out a documentary review and propose strategies to guide interventions to address nutritional alterations due to excess at the time of life, early childhood, childhood and adolescence. A systematic review of structured literature under the PRISMA declaration, from January 2016 to November 2021. The databases established for the search were: SCOPUS, PUBMED, LILACS and SCIENCE DIRECT. 74 articles were included, which allowed categorizing strategies into four axes, physical activity, dietotherapy, food behavior and health interventions; of which the various recommendations for the current management of overweight and childhood obesity were reviewed. The importance of generating strategies and recommendations for the approach of nutritional alterations due to excess in children and adolescents, related to timely diagnosis and inter- and transdisciplinary treatment plans was demonstrated
