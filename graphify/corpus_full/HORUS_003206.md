# SHARKS, ANGELS OR OTHERS LIKE US: HOW MIGRANTS ACCQUIRE THE DOUBLE-FACE OF THE SACRED

This article aims to offer a global view of the migration problem from mimetic theory, starting with the study of the situation in Colombia. We will discuss the most relevant data available on migration as read by various social scientists, together with the ethical and political reflections of Anzaldúa3 and Girard. Our aim is that this interaction of data, theoretical analysis and mimetic theory opens a horizon from which we can address issues such as the fate of minors, women and the LGBTQ population, the pauperization of the work of migrants and the inadequacy of public policies. This will help us to escape the extreme position of considering the other as an enemy and as a threat
