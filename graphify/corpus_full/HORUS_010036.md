# CHARACTERIZATION OF STOCKETICAL MATERIAL RAICES

To model processes that evolve over time, discrete Markov chains are regularly used. Examples of such processes may be credit risk variations in the financial industry [3], or the progress of a chronic disease [1]. Depending on the availability of real data, the transition matrix estimate for these chains is limited to specified periods. For example, credit risk variations are estimated annually with information from some risk rating agency (e.g. Standard & PoorIs). However, in valuation activities they are performed in periods less than one year. The conditions required for a given stochastic matrix to have stochastic r-root and possible algorithms for obtaining them in 2×2 and 3×3 matrices (optionally 4x4) are analyzed in terms of its own values, traces and determinants. In addition, stochastic matrix analysis with absorbing and doubly stochastic states is analyzed
