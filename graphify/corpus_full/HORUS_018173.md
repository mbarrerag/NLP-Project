# PRELIMINARY ASSESSMENT OF WATER RESOURCES FOR WATER SOWING IN THE PALOMININE RIVER BASIN, COLOMBIA

The Seembra and Harvest of Water is part of the nature-based methods that take up ancestral practices for efficient water use, favouring the recharge of aquifers in humid periods for use in dry periods. Proper estimation of the amount of water resource available in a region or basin is fundamental for water planting, for which different hydrological models are used, depending on the quantity and quality of information. The Palomino River Basin study, located in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, Colombia, is presented, where a multi-model methodology is proposed for assessing water resources. Twelve aggregate models are applied, especially used in basins with little hydrological information. Calibration and validation is carried out for each model, and then long-term simulation is proposed to determine the amount of water available and calculate hydrological regulation indices. The multi-model approach allows estimating the uncertainty of hydrological balance, which is a typical problem for hydrological basins with little data availability. The proposed method is simple and can be replicated in other regions given its versatility and ease of use
