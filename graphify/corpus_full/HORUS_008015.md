# TIRFAA RATIFICATION AND INITIATION PROCESS IN COLOMBIA

The International Treaty of Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture (TIRFAA) is ad portas to fill its entire process of ratification in Colombia. This is achieved in the third attempt to ratify the TIRFAA in the Congress of Colombia, after 22 years of having been signed by the representatives of the Colombian government. By the time this summary is written, the Constitutional Court has announced by press release, the exequibility of Law 2285 of 2023, which is the legal instrument by which the TIRFAA is adopted in Colombia, subtracting only some short processes of procedure and the signature of the President of the Republic. The exercise carried out by the individuals and institutions that accompanied the process of ratification of the TIRFAA, the lessons learned and the historical moment that live the Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture in Colombia are presented here. Colombia is at a key moment where the three branches of power, the legislative, executive and judicial benefits are promoting the strengthening of the territories, the autonomy of the small grassroots organizations, as well as the recognition of the rights of the peasant sector are established. Colombia is in an initial strategy of socialization and the IT. implementation of TIRFAA in countries of the region and learning from what has been learned, to lay the foundations for the effective implementation of TIRFAA in Colombia
