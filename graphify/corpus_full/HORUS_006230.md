# Design of the by-products treatment system generated at a rural potabilization plant in the municipality of Palestine (Caldas)

Water is a vital resource for all living beings, but current human activities require potabilization processes to ensure safe use, conventional methods of potabilization include capture, conduction, flocculation, sedimentation, filtration and disinfection. These processes generate as a byproduct a sludge/water mixture, result of precipitation of solids present in raw water. Previously, it was common for water treatment plants to dispose of sludges in water bodies, which affected their quality. However, environmental regulation prohibits this practice and establishes the need to properly manage the sludge generated during the process of potabilization. Due to the current socio-environmental conditions, it is necessary to take measures to improve the drinking water treatment systems and reduce their impact on the environment. Therefore, this project proposes a methodology that offers options for the design of a basic treatment system, which includes the analysis of the water by-products, its quantity, the preliminary implementation costs and the possibilities of use or final disposal of the sludges generated during the water purification activities. ) Water is a vital resource for all living beings, but currently human activities require purification processes to gurantee safe use.
