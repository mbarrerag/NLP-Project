# CAMPUS-GW Project: Analysis of groundwater hydrodynamics in the north-western area of the Bogotá Headquarters Main Campus

The area between the western margin of the University City and the Uriel Gutiérrez building of the university campus presents particularities associated with water: during the rainy periods there are problems of watering in the football courts close to the acoustic shell and in the basement of the Uriel Gutiérrez building and its surrounding areas water is presented continuously throughout the year. The Vice-Rectorate of Headquarters has recently planned interventions in this field of the campus to enhance the natural character that has historically characterized it, understanding that the water that manifests in these sectors is not only superficial but also includes groundwater. This project seeks to make a diagnosis of this problem through the hydrodynamic characterization of water in the fordosa area in order to propose alternatives of solution or mitigation that are useful for decision making by the Headquarters. In order to achieve the objective set, it is expected to carry out the characterization of the area through geotechnical exploration and quantification of the water parameters of the soil, additional to install piezometers for the recording of levels periodically in order to establish the behavior of the flow of water in the area and to make the analysis of the information
