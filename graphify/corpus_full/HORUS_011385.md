# HISTORIA AND THEORY COMPARISONAL CONSTITUTIONAL TOOM II MODERN CONSTITUTIONALISM: TOWARDS DEMOCRATIC, SOCIAL AND ENVIRONMENTAL CONSTITUTIONAL STATE

The present second volume of the series History and constitutional theory compared in three volumes, will be devoted in a world perspective to Modern constitutionalism and its development from 1776 towards the constitutional democratic, social and environmental state ECDSA which is understood as the ideal type after two and a half centuries of state transformations. It is the central volume of the series that conceptualizes in detail the object of research in all its dimensions.The book consists of three chapters.The first will analyze the spatial-temporal macrodynamics of the world diffusion of the modern constitutional state, based on a scheme that starts from eight models of state modernity and six waves of transformation.The second chapter will structure modern constitutionalism according to the model of the twenty qualitative indicators of transformation, thirteen coming from the long nineteenth century (1776-1916) and seven additional from the later epochs.They are also oriented in the decisive institutions and values of the modern constitutional state and open the possibility of evaluating the place of a specific country at a certain time of transformation, since it is fulfilled partially or not fulfilled.The third chapter will contain a special study for the constitutional group, more than the constitutional state
