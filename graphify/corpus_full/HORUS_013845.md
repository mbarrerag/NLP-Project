# A DIG AND ADEQUATE LIVING FOR THE INFORMAL RESEARCH POPULATION IN THE CITY OF BOGOTA. CASE OF STUDY: ASSOCIATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL RESEARCHERS A BETTER LIVING (ARAUMV)

Access to social housing, in any Latin American country, is given on the basis of different plans, programmes and projects that are developed in order to guarantee a fundamental right, the right to decent and adequate housing. The former are created precisely as a response to a social, economic and cultural problem that prevails throughout the region and is the housing deficit. However, the magnitude is such that no government is able to have a total coverage of decent and adequate housing for each of its inhabitants. The precarious conditions in which millions of people live in the region, are given to some extent, as a result of globalization and an economic model thought of the accumulation of capital and the lack of controls that the free market places. Among all the people who are in a state of vulnerability, are the waste pickers of their own occupation. The recycling population of office, in its great majority, cannot access a housing solution within the offer of state entities. In Bogotá, only 11.4% of the waste pickers of office live in a housing, which most of the cases, is in a precarious condition.
