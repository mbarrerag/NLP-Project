# Co-production of knowledge for the governance of the Coello River Basin in Tolima, Colombia

From the framework of the co-production of knowledge for the Governance of Socio-ecological Systems [SSE], the objective of the article is to analyze the process by which the co-production of knowledge has mobilized new governance scenarios in the Coello River basin. In this case, a collective action mechanism called Citizens Action Talks [CAC] is taken as a reference. Through the application of a methodological route based on Participatory Research-Action [IAP], the origin of this mechanism is documented and a reading is provided on the links between social actors and organizational practices linked to instruments of municipal and departmental public power. This collaborative network, oriented towards goals, based on the socio-ecological, interactive and plural context, has generated capacities for regulation, adaptation and integration for governance at the basin level
