# Postgraduate medical education in Colombia. Ideas to improve it

Summary After 70 years of the formalization of medical specialties in Colombia, there has been very little progress in the educational models for the acquisition of clinical competencies in these postgraduate programs. In addition, despite the fact that there is already a law on human talent in health, in the Colombian medical education system there are no specific regulations on the training of health professionals (doctors) in the different medical specialization programs offered in the country. Likewise, at present, factors such as the financial crisis of public hospitals, the low number of accredited hospitals and the increasing number of medical faculties and specialization programs affect the integral and equal acquisition of medical competencies of professionals who are trained as specialists in the different medical postgraduate programs offered in Colombia. Taking into account the above, the purpose of this article is to present a proposal for postgraduate medical education that prioritizes the adequate acquisition of competencies beyond the completion of the time set to complete the curricula of different medical specialties.
