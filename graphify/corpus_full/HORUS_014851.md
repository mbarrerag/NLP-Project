# INDUSTRY 4.0 IN YOUR CASE: EMPRESSION OF PROTOTYPIES FOR PUNZONATE TOOLS FOR WORK IN HOT WITH MODEL EMPRESORATIONS FOR BASED COAST DEPOSITION

The additive manufacturing within Industry 4.0 has become a manufacturing trend in multiple areas this in view of its great ease and speed in the manufacture of prototypes or functional parts. Additive manufacturing of metals at low production cost has become one of the major challenges of the last five years at industrial and academic level, therefore, this research work shows the manufacturing route in obtaining metal components by means of additive manufacturing based on extrusion developed by the consortium SENA-UNAL-IMAL for H13 tool steel for hot working application. This research work shows the production of polymer-metal composite materials in the form of filament to be used in conventional FDM machines, as well as technical feasibility in terms of printing. Obtained a polymer-metal filament were generated prototypes of hot drilling punches. Printed prototypes are shown and explained the process by which 100% metal consolidation was achieved through the processes of depolymerization and sintering in solid phase and liquid phase and as these result of the current microstructure and mechanical processes 4.0
