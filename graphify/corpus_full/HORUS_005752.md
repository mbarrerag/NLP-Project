# EXTERNAL RELATIONS IN THE UNAL. NEXT FROM A LOCAL, REGIONAL, NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL PERSPECTIVE

As a university of the nation, the National University of Colombia is committed to reaching the most remote territories and communities, leading training opportunities and processes of transformation and multicultural recognition. The University, as a reference to higher education in the country, is widely recognized at the national and international levels. Therefore, since 2018, it has proposed to advance in consolidating and strengthening its relations with other national and foreign institutions, companies, governmental organizations, foreign ministries, consulates and other representatives of civil society. This, in the light of a conception of external relations as a process that is addressed from various scenarios, that is, from a local, regional, national and international perspective and in tune with the challenges currently involved in the internationalization processes of higher education institutions at the global level. This document sets out the strategies and academic management programmes put in place at the University during the period 2018-2024 to respond to the challenges of internationalization and external relations, with special emphasis on the Global Plan for the Development of Cultural, Scientific and Collective Development of the Nation (2022-2024) and elements that are expected to be consolidated in the goals set out in Plei 2034
