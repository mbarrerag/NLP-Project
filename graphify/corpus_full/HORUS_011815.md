# THE PRESIDENCY OF TRUMP AND THE RUPTURE OF THE INTERNATIONAL FREEDOM

Donald Trump's foreign policy questioned several of the foundations of the liberal international order in which the superpower had backed his leadership for decades. This paper investigates the factors that explain this phenomenon from the analysis of the political and economic transformations that have taken place in American society, and the way they were reflected in the main decisions of that administration
