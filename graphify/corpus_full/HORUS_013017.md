# Educational needs in photo protection for teachers, parents of primary school students of IPARM in Bogotá

This research, of descriptive qualitative type, seeks to describe educational needs in photoprotection for teachers, parents and mothers of primary students of the school Instituto Pedagógico Arturo Ramírez Montúfar (IPARM) of Bogotá, captured in the headquarters of the educational institution in the second semester of 2022. The collection of information is carried out through in-depth interviews, whose analysis is carried out with the method of analysis of content. From the analysis of the in-depth interviews, three relevant topics originate. Theme 1 focuses on knowledge in photoprotection, from which three categories emerge: knowledge related to health, knowledge related to radiation and knowledge related to sunscreens. Theme 2 refers to the practices of care in photoprotection, composed by the category of care, which, in turn, integrates the following subcategories: use of elements of photoprotection and time of exposure to the sun; and the practical category in health. The topic 3 addresses the factors that influence the use of photoprotection, composed by the categories of technology, family, professional health, school environment and experiences of others.
