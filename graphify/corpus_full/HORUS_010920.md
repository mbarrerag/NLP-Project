# Study of the interaction between the tracers from bio-oil heavy fraction and a catalyst in hydrotreatment process.

The bio-oil of rapid pyrolysis of biomass is a promising alternative to be used as a source of fuels and chemicals. It contains a wide variety of compounds and comes from renewable sources. The pyrolysis oil is formed by water, light organic compounds (compounds detectable by GC/MS), oligomeric fractions derived from lignin and holocellulose. This thesis focuses on the identification of oligomeric molecules in the pyrolytic fraction of lignin and its catalytic hydrodeoxygenation. This fraction can be used potentially for the production of fuels and chemical products through hydrodeoxygenation. The chemical structure of the ligomeric molecules of pyrolytic lignin is little known. In this study, quantum mechanical simulation was used to propose the structures based on calculations of the electronic structure. The DFT calculations were used to identify the thermodynamic structures most likely of the lignine pyrolytic molecules by pyrolysis followed by demethylation.
