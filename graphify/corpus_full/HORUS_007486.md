# Network analysis in the co-creation process of territorialized food guides in the south of the department Nariño

In Colombia, cross-sectoral efforts are being made to transform food systems to promote healthy food based on the Human Rights Approach, which allows working respectfully with different populations, recognizing their diversity, customs and traditions through nutritional education processes such as the Food Guidelines. In the guarantee of the Human Right to Food, the food systems and the actors that make up them are recognized through the dynamic relationships that arise as dynamic relationships that allow structuring forms of participation in the design of strategies to guarantee Food Security.
