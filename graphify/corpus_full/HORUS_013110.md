# Characterization of particle size distribution of particulate matter present in ambient air at two points in Bogotá

This study sought to carry out a pioneering exercise in the country in the measurement in ambient air of the concentration of particles (CNP) and the characterization of the size distribution (PNSD) with the ELPI+ particle metering team in two air quality monitoring stations in Bogotá, corresponding to San Cristobal (urban bottom station) and Las Ferias (vehicle traffic station), in order to identify the concentrations and sizes of the particles to which citizens are exposed. These measurements were carried out in three campaigns on different dates between June and October 2017. The results obtained corresponded to averages of concentration of particles between mean diameters from 0.016 μm to 5.3 μm.
