# The evolutionary relationship between innovation, categories of knowledge and the institutional context. Case study: A musical organization

Innovation is an economic and social phenomenon (Schumpeter, 1935) that occurs within the organisations. It is not a linear process, but rather a multidirectional process (Pinch & Bijker, 1984) based on knowledge (Tidd & Bessant, 2021), which can be articulated (codified) or non-articulated (Cowan et al., 2000), and is developed in an environment of uncertainty, so society has established rules of play or institutions (North, 1990) that facilitate interaction between individuals and organizations. This thesis seeks to understand how different categories of knowledge evolve towards innovation within the organizations in an institutional context. To this, a evolutionary cycle between knowledge and institutions that is marking a path defined by the economic activity of recorded music. A musical organization that creates and produces music and the unit of analysis is a recorded song, understood this as an innovation. It is concluded that there is an evolutionary cycle between knowledge and institutions that is marked by the decision-making of individuals with different levels of agency until the innovation is produced, i. how different categories of knowledge evolve towards innovation without organizations in an institutional context. To do so, the methodology of a case study of a single case within the economic activity of remembered music was used. A musical organization that creates and produces music was selected and the unit of analysis is a recorded song, under as an innovation. It is included that there is an evolutionary cycle between knowledge and institutions that marks a path defined by the decision-making of individuals with different levels of agency until innovation is produced, that is, the final product is released on the market. In this sense, it is set out that the understanding of codification of knowledge given speed to the innovation process by means of the innovative approach
