# EPOXIDATION OF CYCHLOHEXENE BY THE USE OF MACHINE LEARNING

The advance in the sciences is inherent in the development of the industry, immersed in this advance is the catalysis that represents a fundamental pillar in the processes involving chemistry [1]. This has forced catalysis to seek support in technological and computer developments in order to strengthen and accelerate different processes related to the parameterization of its experimental methodologies, extraction of patterns for the prediction of new catalysts and the creation of efficient catalytic processes using a unified foundation or database [2]. Artificial Intelligence (IA), in particular Machine Learning (ML), has gained relevance in the field of catalysis due to the need to adjust scientific development to industrial demands and to take advantage of the large amount of data generated in the laboratory [3]. The use of AI in catalysis is reflected in the increase of publications in the field during the XXI century [4], and in particular, the ML is widely applied in scientific research due to its versatility and efficiency in data processing [5, 6].
