# CURRICULLAR CONTENTS ON MAJOR ADULTS IN COLOMBIAN NUTRITION AND DIETETICAL PREGRAPHS

The book deals with the results of research carried out by researchers of the research group Food and Human Nutrition of the Faculty of Medicine of the National University of Colombia. In this chapter, research was carried out on the curriculum content of older persons in Colombian undergraduates of Nutrition and Dietetics
