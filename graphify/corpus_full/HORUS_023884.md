# Pedagogy of memory in political and simbolic violence: analysis from destitution, negation and crucibility.

Questions such as the following arise: What ethical and psychosocial impacts have we experienced as a society based on the prolonged situation of political violence? How is it possible from a pedagogical commitment whose focus is memory to deal with transgenerational impacts, based on prejudices and justifications for the denial and elimination of others? How to deconstruct the legacy of the politics of the internal enemy when it is inscribed in our bodies and in our social relations? Are we prepared as teachers to pedagogically contain the door we open through memory? Consistent with these questionings, this paper recognizes the fact that several generations have witnessed the emergence of impacts of political violence over time, not only on the direct victims of the various heinous events, but also on society as a whole and therefore intends to deepen the challenges of memory work, from a pedagogical commitment, in a context marked by logics of denial, contempt and cruelty
