# Political and Historical Ethnogram of the Victims of Armed Conflict in Colombia

The purpose is to investigate the social, political and historical configuration of the victims, through observation and follow-up to their expressions, testimonies, publications on social networks and documentary videos from 2016, when the peace agreements between the Colombian government and the guerrillas of the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) were signed; complemented by the historiographic analysis of what had been experienced in Colombia in the twentieth century with respect to the experiences of the victims during other peace processes. One of the results is that the different ways of communicating and narrating the pain on the part of the victims have allowed them to transform their victimistic trajectory, from the occurrence of the victimizing events to the moment when they are called victims and the process of constructing individual and collective subjects of their destiny begins, with a novelty, the direct voice of the victims and co-victims advocating and reflecting on themselves
