# LIFE AND DEATH IN IKU INDIGENOUS THINKING (ARHUACO)

the question for the understanding of the senses of life and death between the indigenous iku (arhuacos) of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, located in the north of Colombia, gives origin to this article, whose contents gather the results of a research of sustained presence in the indigenous territory between 2018 and 2020, which allowed the approximation to conceptions, material and spiritual practices that the Arhuacos mobilize daily around these two concepts. From the elaboration of ethnographic work and the review of the bibliographic production of the antro-pology on the Sierra Nevada, the article has as objective to develop what I propose to understand as iku vitality, a notion that expresses the intrinsic relationship between materiality and spirituality in the Arhuaco world, which maintains a special continuity with the idea of death and that in the exercise of the experience in the territory acquires different expressions, suscepti-ble to be read by the systematicity of ethnographic coexistence, thanks to different cultural practices that the Arhuaco people deploys for the ritual and social forms of the death, this analysis and the epitological principles of being considered as being the epitological
