# THE CHANGE OF QUANTITY IN RECONVENTION DEMAND: IMPACT ON THE INTEGRATION OF THE DOMESTIC ARBITRAL TRIBUNAL

In the context of domestic arbitration in Colombia, several doubts have arisen as a result of the application of the arbitral norm and its interrelation with the arbitration regulations and the general rules of the process contained in the General Code of the Process, among others. One of these scenarios of debate corresponds to the effect of the change of amount on the counterclaim, and its possible effects on the integration of the arbitral tribunal. In this paper, the underlying concepts surrounding the debate are briefly outlined, the different alternatives and positions adopted in the matter are presented and it is concluded that such reintegration is not mandatory, given the absence of a rule ordering it
