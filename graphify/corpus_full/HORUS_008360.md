# CONTRIBUTION TO THE HYDROLOGY OF THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN FROM THE ELABORATION OF INTENSITY-DURATION-FRECUENCE CURVES

The intensity-duration-frequency curves (IDF) are tools widely used in hydraulic design and water resource management, as they provide information on the maximum precipitation intensity associated with different durations and return periods. In 2016, IDEAM built 110 IDF curves for the Colombian territory, however, in the Caribbean region these are limited. In order to complement the existing IDF curves and study the temporal variability of extreme storms of short duration, 24 curves were constructed for the Caribbean region. To achieve this, the regional stations with flood charts suitable for the construction of IDF curves were identified, then the 15 largest storms of each year were selected. Then, they were digitized by means of the HDFUN software (Sources et al., 2018) and the design intensions were calculated using the IDFTool package (Zamora et al., 2016). From the 15 existing curves (IDEAM, 2016) and the 24 built, spatialisation of intensions by IDDF was performed, and results were evaluated by means of cross-validation.
