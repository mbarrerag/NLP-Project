# Different methods to synthesize biodiesel

When we talk about energy, it is associated with the development of technologies and different future perspectives that make science face great challenges. Nowadays, alternative biodiesel production studies have been made, due to political and socioeconomic impact of the applications of this fuel. In the last decades, as industrialization and population have increased, so has the need for energy. The predecessor of biodiesel, petroleum diesel, has been an important source of fuel throughout the world, and it has many disadvantages, including declining fossil fuel reserves and increasing air pollution. These pollutants have significant negative impacts on the global environment and human health. Also, it has a negative impact on the global environment and human health.
