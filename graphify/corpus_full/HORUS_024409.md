# MODELATION: MATHEMATIC OF THE DEPOSITION AND INILTRACTION PROCESS OF LIPIDIC SUBSTANCES IN THE PAREDES OF THE ARTY.

The world health organization (WHO) estimates that in 2015 this cause caused 17.7 million deaths worldwide. This has motivated researchers from different areas of science and medicine to join efforts to study and prevent this problem. This deterioration of the heart and blood vessels is due especially to the accumulation of fat and cholesterol in the walls of the blood vessels (arteries), which is called the atherosclero-tatic plaque. This talk, based on the interaction between the different cellular populations involved in the process of oxidisation and accumulation of bad cholesterol (LDL), presents a model of partial nonlinear differential equations that describe this pheno-meno. This analysis presents a stability analysis to determine the relationship between the different cellular populations that give rise to the formation of the plaque.
