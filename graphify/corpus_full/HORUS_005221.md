# Genomic and transcriptomic analysis of the interaction muse - pseudocercospora fijiensis as a tool in the identification and characterization of fungal genes of virulence and pathogenicity

The Sigatoka negra caused by the fungus Mycosphaerella fijiensis, synonym of Pseudocercóspora fijiensis is the most important disease in the cultivation of bananas in the world. The disease affects the photosynthetic capacity of plants and subsequently the size and quality of the fruit, generating great economic losses. There are few reported genetic studies that allow to understand how the fungus sickens the plant and how it evades the defenses of the plant; for this it is necessary to identify in the fungus candidate genes associated with pathogenicity and virulence. In this work a bioinformatic analysis and microargnosis was used to identify in the fungus the presence of secreted proteins rich in cystine (SSPs) with differential expression during the incompatible interaction plant - P. fijiensis.
