# CURVE OF FIRST LACTANCY IN BOVINO WINNER WITH DIFFERENT LUCERAL PROPOSAL AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP WITH ENVIRONMENTAL FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO MILK PRODUCTION

The Lucerne breed is a valuable genetic resource for Colombia due to its milk production and adaptability; however, few studies have evaluated its productive performance, specifically its breastfeeding curve. The objective of this work was to estimate the parameters of the first nursing curve of cows with different Lucerne ratio and establish its association with some environmental factors. 56615 daily milk production records of 4590 cows were used in two herds. An initial cleansing of the information was carried out and the parameters of the breastfeeding curve were estimated by Wilmink, Wood, Brody and, Ali and Schaeffer models. The criteria AIC, BIC, R2, convergence percentage and sum of squares of the residue were used to select the best model. A variance analysis was performed to establish the effect of the racial component, hato, time and year of delivery on the estimated parameters of the best model. In addition, a linear correlation analysis between the parameters of the lactation curve with the first interval between births, and the production of milk at the time of 180 and 305 days.
