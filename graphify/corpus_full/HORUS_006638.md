# Trends and state of the art of research in tourist routes. Bibliometric and narrative analysis of literature

Tourism represents a fundamental component in the field of travel, offering a wide range of experiences ranging from historical and cultural tours to adventures in nature and ecotourism. Each tourist route exhibits a series of attractions that allow travelers to discover new destinations and enjoy exciting activities. As travel patterns and consumer demands evolve, the tourist routes have been adapted to meet these changing needs. In particular, there has been a growing interest in sustainable and responsible tourism in recent years, which has spurred the creation of routes focused on environmental conservation and local development. The proliferation of information and communication technologies has led to the emergence of new forms of tourism, such as virtual and experiential tourism, revolutionizing the way tourists interact with tourist destinations. As a fundamental initiative for research in the communities with a view to the development of tourist destinations, it is crucial to evaluate the use of ecological and cultural resources, as well as the participation and responsibilities of each of the actors involved. It is also essential to analyze the interactions between human beings and their environmental environment, the results derived from the exploitation of these resources and the equitable and responsible distribution of the benefits, using tourism as a productive strategy. This comprehensive assessment will allow not only a sustainable tourism development, but also the strengthening of the social and economic fabric of the receiving communities.
