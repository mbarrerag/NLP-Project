# Evaluation of simulated laparoscopic skills in general surgery residents, using the OSATS scale.

Medical education in Colombia has been more than 2 centuries of history, has been evolving and adapting to the changes proper to modernization. Worldwide, the adequate education of the surgical resident has required on average 5 years. The education of surgical residents based on competences promotes the acquisition of attitudes and aptitudes in controlled learning environments, being surgical simulation a fundamental tool. The objective evaluation of surgical ability, with scales like OSATS, has demonstrated significant improvements in the participants' skills. The aim of this study is to evaluate the simulated laparoscopic skills of general surgery residents using the OSATS scale in table simulators. Transverse study, were included residents of first to fourth year general surgery and teachers and instructors of general surgery of UNAL in the years 2021-2022. The average score of the first year was calculated, the results of 5 exercises were applied in a table simulator and two blinded evaluators qualified each exercise with 5 items of the OSATS scale.
