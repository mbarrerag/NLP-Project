# The death as a teacher. Methodological proposal for the reassociation of body structures with soft tissue: the case of the amphitheater of the National University

This research presents a forensic anthropological analysis of the individuals who rest in the amphytheater of the Faculty of Medicine of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, who are in Condition of Unidentified (CNI) or Unclaimed Identified Bones (CINR). Due to the different managements that have been given, the bodies and body segments have been mixed with the passage of time. Thus, by applying anthropological methodologies for the approach of contexts of mixed bone remains and dismembered corpses, a process of individualization and reassociation of bone structures and body segments that make up each body is carried out. As a result, 85 individuals, 105 groups and 21 groups of miscellaneous structures are obtained. A methodological proposal is also provided for the approach of this specific context of bodies that preserve soft tissue. Likewise, it is invited to reflect on the care that is carried out with the bodies, especially in the academic field, and recommendations are made for the proper application of the protocols of care and management of the aphiteatro. Finally, this work contributes to the development of new tools, which have taken into account the different contexts of the laneous structures are established. A methodological proposal is also provided to address this specific context of bodies that preserve soft tissue. Futuremore, this research approaches to reflect on the way correlations, especially in the academy, are handle. So, some recommendations are set for the application of the care and management protocols in the amphitheater. Ultimately, it intents to develop new strategies, taking into account the different contexts where disappearances have occurring, to optimize the search, identification and remains return processes
