# HYDRATIATION IN SPORT AND PHYSICAL ACTIVITY: PRINCIPLES AND METHODS APPLICED

The practice of physical activity and sport involves a series of effects in the body, which depending on the level of training of the person who performs this practice and the guidance received by qualified personnel for its realization, can result in adaptation processes for the proper conditioning and performance or a risk for health with predisposition to the generation of injuries.One of the aspects to be paid special attention is hydration, which means ensuring an optimal hydroelectrolytic balance in the body, in order to favor an adequate physiological response to the demands that the practice of physical activity and sport pose in this regard, considering not only the characteristics of the practice that is carried out, but also the course of life in which the person who practices it is located, the type of discipline or activity that it performs, among other aspects. In the face of the above, the book Hydration in Sport and Physical Activity: Principles and methods applied, provides an approximation to the knowledge of the aspects involved in the process of hydration in children and adults and presents some of the bets that from the research inside of the National University have been made to facilitate the process of evaluation of the levels of hydration, that it is already employed or that it is in the field of activity
