# CO-VOLUTION BETWEEN INDUSTRIAL REVOLUTIONS AND ECONOMIC AND ADMINISTRATIVE SCIENCES

The emerging fourth industrial revolution, perhaps driven by the consequences of the disease COVID-19, appears today as a major leap in which technological developments of applications in information and communications converge (Rozo-García, 2020), but also new complexities in the combination of the use of resources, to generate collaborative processes oriented towards the generation of capacities, learnings and adaptabilitys of organizations towards the volatility of the environment and markets. Convergences assisted by artificial intelligence of physical, biological and digital domains, digitally related by new and more economic forms of interaction (Rozo-García, 2020), lead us to think about the present of the data markets and intelligent networks, as well as about the transformations in production, employment, the distribution of wealth on the basis of possible monopolies and the emergence of new conditions for access to markets through possible barriers to entry
