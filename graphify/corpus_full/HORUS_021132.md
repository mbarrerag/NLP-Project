# Revista archivos d'election

More than reference material, Reading Archives responds to a compilation of ideas without end point. Yarn readings of conjunctural concepts due to the need for participation and the open nature of exploratory expression of observation, measurement, formulation and analysis of optics. Its publication allows to spread the concerns and the work developed in the Research Seed in a direct exposition of the processes of reflection carried out by its members that builds a narrative of shared interests presented as a repository that welcomes, rather than containing, material that can be of inspiration or support for future ideas raised in the concern of its possible readers
