# OVERVIEW MODEL USING OVERVIEW METHODOLOGY OF REPLY SUPERFISTS WITH BLOCKS

This paper proposes a methodology to adjust a survival model to a response surface in the presence of blocks, with the aim of allowing the methodology to improve the estimation of parameters and prediction in models where the variable of interest is observed in the time to the occurrence of an event. The theoretical development for the construction, estimation and validation of assumptions was carried out, using as a basis the model of proportional risks of Cox and the methodology of response surfaces. To evaluate the performance of the methodology in comparison with other methodologies, real and simulated data were used. The results show the fact that when combining the model of proportional risks with the methodology of response surfaces, it is possible to identify the levels of treatments that optimize the response variable. The study of life time in grafts performed in Inchi plants carried out in Venezuela in a factorial arrangement 32 of a completely randomized experimental block design (sex) with twenty replicates can be concluded. Finally, it is concluded that this methodology has the advantage of being able to include a local control (block) that allows reducing the experimental error, improving efficiency when detecting minor differences between treatments, with which comparisons of the most reliable treatments can be made
