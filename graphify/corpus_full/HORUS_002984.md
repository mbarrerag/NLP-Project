# Study in the city of Bogotá from a vision of urban habitability, measured with respect to mobility from indicators constructed with free access information

This study focuses on the relationship between urban mobility and habitability in Bogotá, Colombia, in a context of increasing urbanization. The research seeks to analyze the dynamics of mobility through indicators constructed with free access information, with the aim of understanding how these relate to the notions of habitability. The methodology included a review of literature and of free access bases with which mobility indicators were estimated, analysis of main components and multiple linear regression models was used to examine the relationships between mobility and habitability. The results indicate changes between 2019 and 2023. The dimensions of mobility identified reveal complex interactions between indicators and underline the importance of considering socio-economic conditions. The linear regression models were carried out taking into account the socioeconomic stratum and allowed to identify not only which aspects of mobility are most relevant for each socio-economic group from the perspective of habitability but how mobility manages to explain in good proportion the levels of poverty and quality of life in terms of the mobility and satisfaction (the text of the objective). and high the importance of considering socioeconomic conditions. The linear regression models were conducted taking socioeconomic strategic into account, allowing for the identification of which mobility aspects are most relevant for each socioeconomic group from a livability perspective. They also demonstrate how mobility has a significant impact on levels of poverty and quality of life, embedsizing that travel is not an end itself. Thus, the need for mobility strategies that are not only conducive infrastructure but also promote inclusive and quality access for all citizens is underscored, acquiring a full and more satisfying urban life
