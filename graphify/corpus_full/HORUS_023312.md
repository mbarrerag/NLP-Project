# EVERYTHING YOU WANTED TO KNOW ABOUT CANNABIS AND NOT WAIT TO WONDER

Cannabis is a plant that has been used thousands of years ago by different cultures, such as India and China, for curative purposes. The recent approval of the medicinal use of cannabis in many countries, including Colombia, has allowed researchers to search for new pharmacological tools for the treatment of many diseases that so far do not have adequate treatments. Additionally, there is a great opportunity for the development of the cannabis industry in Colombia, not only because of its medicinal use but also because of the industrial use for the production of new materials such as textiles, fibers or paper. However, many people have questions about the medicinal use of cannabis related to its effectiveness (What diseases is it useful for?) and its safety (What adverse effects do they present? Can they cause dependence?); even its forms of consumption (smoked?). Develop a deep analysis on this wonderful plant, whose use is millennial, exceeds the scope of this material that we put into consideration. We have tried to compile some aspects that we consider relevant to its history, its biology, the way they act, its authors, adverse effects, precautions, among others.
