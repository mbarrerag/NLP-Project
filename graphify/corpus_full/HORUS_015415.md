# Model strain of bamboo-guadua laminates confined with soft wood.

The proposed model allows us to infer the behavior of Laminados de Bambú Guadua Confinados (LBGC) by soft wood. The containment is an effective resource to improve the rigidity and resistance of a material, in this case of laminated bamboo. The materials used for the characterization were Bamboo Guadua angustifolia Kunth and Pino Radiata. 576 laboratory tests were carried out divided into 4 sets of 144 specimens of cutting, parallel compression, perpendicular compression and bending. The materials tested were wood, bamboo laminated and material composed of bamboo confined to 20%, 40%, 60% and 80%. The model was validated with additional specimens composed of pine radiata and pine caribe. It was experimentally demonstrated that there is an increase in the rigidity of laminated bamboo confined by soft wood in the parallel direction to the fiber.
