# Characterization of the cases of use required in the development of an information system for a pathology laboratory

The pathology is a medical specialty dedicated to the precise diagnosis of different diseases from samples obtained from patients, such as body fluids, tissue biopsies and/or organ resection. These samples are subjected to hist engineering or cytotechnic processes that allow to obtain histological or cytology sheets that are to be read by the pathologist or cytologist using the optical microscope. Subsequently, in conjunction with other techniques such as immunohistochemistry, molecular pathology, immunofluorescence, among others, a precise diagnosis is established with which other medical specialties guide the management of patients. Paulatinally the pathology has been integrating in its development and performance of the day-to-day information technologies where the personnel of pathology laboratories, such as: auxiliary, histotecnologists, bacterologists and pathologists interact and maintain an effective communication between themselves through information systems or softwares that, in many cases, are short to meet all the needs of the current world. This project forms part of the macroproject entitled “Program of temporary detection of pre-malignant and gastric cancer”. This experience of interdisciplinary work has been rewarding because it has allowed those who took part in the project to know other ways of interpreting the world, sharing experiences and knowledge and finally to achieve a single objective for the good of the community. Although, it is a project that requires additional modifications, it has allowed to bring together two areas of knowledge that apparently do not relate but work very well at hand. It has been recognized that the new advances of science must be taken not as enemies, but as a help that will allow the doctor to make more accurate decisions and with a smaller number of errors. This generates that medicine is a more precise profession and, in turn, that the main beneficiary, the patient, obtains the best results of this joint work .
