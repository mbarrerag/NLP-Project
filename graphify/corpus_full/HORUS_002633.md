# Ethnobotany as an environmental analysis perspective for the islands of Providencia and Santa Catalina

This research was based on an idea of environment, built from the theory of Latin American environmental thought, in which this is conceived as the constant interaction and transformation between ecosystems and cultures, which give together form to everything that surrounds us. Under this conception and with the support of tools and concepts of ethnobotany, a novel approach was proposed for the interpretation of the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina in its ecosystem and cultural dimensions. To this, through participant observation, interviews with the community, botanical collections and georeferencing of routes and distribution of plants, the relationship of the root community of the islands with the plant “chinese root” was established as a case study, which, from the academy and together with the community, could generate different proposals for the conservation of ecosystems and cultural traditions linked to them. The research sought not only to understand the theory, but to transcend to the action, towards the design and implementation of new participatory approaches, which from the academy and together with the community, could generate different ideas for the conservation of ecosystems and the associated cultural traditions. dimensions. For this purpose, through participation observation, interviews with the community, botanical collections and georeducing of routes and distribution of the plant, a case study was established on the relationship of the root community of the islands with the “chinese root” plant, belonging to the Smilax genus and with an exclusive presence in their Tropical Dry Forest (BsT). The research society not only to understand the theory, but to translate it towards action, towards the design and implementation of new participatory approaches that could be used by the community and the academy to develop variant proposals for the preservation of ecosystems and the cultural traditions that are associated with them
