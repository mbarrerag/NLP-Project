# RESEARCH ACCOUNTED IN COLOMBIA DURING THE SECOND DUTY OF THE 21st CENTURY: TRANSITION TO EXTRAREGIONAL DISCUSIONS

Colombia has been highlighted in Latin America for the existence of a national accounting community, which has been strengthening since the 1980s. This community is in annual events, develops formal projects, has been gradually adapting to the demands of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (Minsciences) and has achieved increasingly qualitative research results. In the first decade of the 21st century, this national dynamic was integrated with other Latin American dynamics and, apparently, in the second decade, there was a transition to integration with extraregional research. The aim of this article is to present evidence that the second decade of the 21st century was a decade of transition to a more advanced level of interaction. For this, a literature review was carried out, the dynamics of eight national journals during the decade was analyzed and the first publications in recognized journals extraregionally were analyzed. In the results, participation in new international congresses and doctoral training, which have opened the doors to more active participation in extraregional discussions, is highlighted
