# Bioeconomy and socio-environmental sustainability in the production processes of the Association of Cocoa Growers and Marketers and Agricultural Products of Pueblo Bello _x0096_ Cesar (ACAPAPB)

The Association of Cultivators and Marketers of Cacao and Agricultural Products of Pueblo Bello - Cesar (ACAPAPB) is a multicultural project organized around the cultivation of cocoa, composed of about 82 producing families, where approximately 70% are Victims of the Armed Conflict, with a significant percentage of indigenous (Kankuamos and Arhuacos). In addition to presenting the local context the problems of economic inequality, informality, social vulnerability, insecurity, difficulty of infrastructure of public services, among others, the main problems raised from Acapapb are related to the need to increase the economic productivity of social base, also mention the need to improve the fertilization with the support of soil studies, since the majority of the producers fertilize without a scientific knowledge or technical advice. Likewise, there is a low knowledge of the capacities and conditions of the associates and their socioeconomic environment, in addition to a low social tissue that allows solidarity and support in the construction of formal processes of social and environmental sustainability. positive economic activity, articulated in a general way to municipal and departmental plans and proposals, where it is propensed for the sustainability of the productive relations of the associated people, improving the community welfare in the region
