# Editorial No. 95

Innovating has been consolidated, throughout its more than three decades of existence, as a reference publication in the fields of administration, accounting and social sciences in Latin America. Faithful to its vocation to be the channel of dissemination of academic research with quality character and consequently with our conception of being an editorial project in constant updating, this number 95 marks the beginning of a renewal in the graphic pattern of the journal, a step that reinforces our commitment to editorial quality and impact on our readers. Innovar’s path has always been of continuous transformation and improvement. With this graphic renewal and consolidation of monographic numbers, we reaffirm our commitment to academic excellence, editorial rigour and the dissemination of knowledge relevant to the development of our disciplines. We thank our community of authors, reviewers and readers for accompanying us in this process of evolution and invite them to continue participating actively in the construction of a high quality, impact and visibility editorial space
