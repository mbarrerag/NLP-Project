# Systematization of the ecofeminist experience of a community operation for the Delirio Nature Reserve in Bogotá

In 2019, the Commission in Defense of the Territory Alto Fucha, a collective subject representing the community of the Ecoterritorio Alto Fucha, formed by seven neighborhoods: San Cristobal Alto or the Pilar, Montecarlo, Manila, Gran Colombia, Laureles Sur Oriental, La Cecilia and Aguas Claras; it contributed definitively to the suspension of the environmental license that would allow the realization of the “Sendero de las Mariposas”, an ecotourism project that sought the hardening and environmental damage of the eastern hills of Bogotá, under a logic of extractive real estate exploitation.
