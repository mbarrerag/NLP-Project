# Determination of the deposit environment and geochemical, mineralogical and petrographic characterization of the Deer Formation in the Quebrada La Cascabel, northeast of Huila

The existing information on the Venado Formation (belonging to the Aguablanca Group) is restricted to general descriptions of the stratigraphy in the type section and to different paleontological reports, which involve discussions regarding the polarity, thickness and temporality of this unit. By means of a detailed stratigraphic column of the section of the Quebrada The Cascabel was performed a sedimentological and stratigraphic characterization to determine which rocks of the Venado Formation were deposited in environments dominated by tidal processes. By means of the petrographic process the rocks and their diagenetic processes were classified texturally and compositally. With the diffraction of X-ray the mineral phases were characterized and the index of crystallineness of illite was found. By integrating the results of both methods it is possible to establish the post-sedimentary processes that experienced the rocks. These analytical techniques have no precedents for the Venado Formation rocks.
