# Shiny implementation for the support of learning and teaching of the specific provisions

This project develops a web application for the calculation of probabilities and quantiles associated with some discrete distributions through the Shiny package of the statistical software R. The main motivation of the project is to support the learning and teaching of discrete distributions, a subject that is mainly covered in subjects such as probability and basic statistics, this application allows users to interact and explore the different distributions through their function of probability mass, cumulative distribution function and survival function, giving the opportunity to understand their behavior, manipulating and changing each of their parameters, this generates a greater understanding for the different types of users, including those who are far from statistics. The application is very intuitive and provides the results of the probabilities and quantiles both textually and graphically to achieve a better understanding of the different discrete distributions
