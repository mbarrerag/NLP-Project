# Delirium detection and measurement barriers in Intensive Care Unit: A scope review

to identify nursing strategies for the detection of delirium and barriers for its measurement in the intensive care unit (ICU). review of scope based on the methodology of the Joanna Briggs Institute and the PRISMA-ScR protocol, by searching six databases with the terms “delirium”, “diagnosis”, “nursing”, “critical care” and “critical care nursing” and extracting SUMARI software. 43 studies were selected for analysis and categorization in two emerging topics: “Frequently used tools for the detection of delirium” and “Barreras reported by nursing staff for the evaluation of delirium”. there is a wide variety of validated and standardized tools for the detection of delirium in intensive care. However, their use is limited on many occasions due to the different barriers of the patient, the evaluator and the multidisciplinary team. Therefore, it is important to train health personnel to be familiar with the delirium, its detection and the use of instruments
