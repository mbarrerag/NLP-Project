# Knowledge management in the programme of rebuilding the social fabric in post-conflict areas in Colombia (Chocó, Sucre and Caldas). Part 3: organizational culture and process management

The main objective of this study is to present the findings of the relationship between process management, organizational culture and the management of knowledge of the social fabric reconstruction program in post-conflict areas in Colombia. This study is based on the application of a knowledge management evaluation model, valued by 74 research professors working with communities in the social, education, entrepreneurship and ecosystem dimensions. The methodology is qualitative, with descriptive and correlateal approaches. The results show that there is a significant correlation between process management and organizational culture. It is concluded that the organizational culture that is being developed in the implementation of the program makes it possible to properly manage knowledge and that the findings presented here are useful for the definition of strategies for improving applied research processes, related to the work theme with the post-conflict community in Colombia
