# Application of diffuse reflectance spectroscopy (NIR) in the study of soil electrical conductivity.

Salinity is one of the biggest problems of soil degradation, so it is necessary to identify techniques that allow to evaluate and constantly monitor the soil. The aim of this research was to determine prediction models to estimate the electrical conductivity (EC) of the soil by means of diffuse reflectance spectroscopy – NIR for soils from three zones with different characteristics: an inceptisol of C.A.M (Mosquera - Cundinamarca), an alfisol of C.A.G (Espinal – Tolima) and an oxysol of C.I.C (Puerto Gaitán – Meta). Four soil samples were used in total to determine electrical conductivity (EC) by means of a ratio 1:2. Spectral signatures were obtained in the NIR region and from this and with the use of the partial least squares (PLSR) methodology and analysis of main components (PCA) were developed in total, one for each soil and one overall.
