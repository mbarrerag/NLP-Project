# The individual cosmos. The intimacy in three houses of Bruno Violi for Bruno Violi

This master's thesis explores the configuration of intimacy in architecture, through a deliberate contrast between retreat and mediation spaces, between the interior and the exterior. A “retirement space” is defined as a zone of difficult access, characterized by formal conditions associated with concavity, which fosters an atmosphere of confinement. On the contrary, a “mediation space” facilitates a connection between individuals and the surrounding landscape, achieved through architectural means. The formal characteristics of these spaces are aligned with convexity, giving rise to an atmosphere of openness. . This master theses explains the share of intimacy in architecture through a deliberate contrast between spaces of retreat and mediation, between inside and outside.
