# Valuation of iron waste material from the metallurgical industry as a catalyst in the phototransformation of pollutants present in waste water

Industrialization, urban growth and economic development have led to the fact that the effluents of water are contaminated with toxic materials such as heavy metals, colourants, emerging pollutants, among others. The processes of decontamination of water through the removal and/or degradation of pollutants are a subject of high interest worldwide.One of the main problems of the textile industry, is the high amount of dyes deposited in the bodies of water, which cause eutrophication and disturbances in the aquatic life.For its part the presence of drugs in water systems and particularly antibiotics, is a subject of great importance since they are considered a public health problem, due to the generation of antimicrobial resistance.These drugs after oral administration are partially metabolized in the body and much of them are excreted in their pharmacologically active form. Due to the above mentioned problems Fesals are investigated various methods for the removal and/or degradation of these compounds present in efluentes water. These methods include physical, chemical and biological systems. These catalysts will be evaluated by a Fenton reaction in the presence of an oxidizing agent such as H2O2. The effect of the concentration of contaminant, catalyst load and H2O2 concentration will be analyzed to achieve the best conditions
