# Dynamics of the medical and financial complex in physical rehabilitation in the General System of Social Security in Health (1993-2015): The case of hearing prosthesis.

This research brings an approximation to the understanding of the dynamics of the Medical Industrial and Financial Complex (CMIF) in the case of rehabilitation with auditory devices in the Colombian health system, from the approach of the political critical economy. It is a matter that has been configured since the middle of the 20th century with the logics of the Military Industrial Complex in the United States and that have been gradually shifting to the health sector, when the markets of hearing aids of transistors and the take-off of the cochlear implant took an important role in the audiology towards 1950. At the end of the 1960s and beginning of the 1970s, the exacerbation of the financial sector, the power of the banking system, the impulse of the technological developments and the Technologies of Information and Communications (ICT), has been evidenced by the historical rupture that has opened the transition from the industrial capitalism to the cognitive capitalism, where the devices and the life value of the companies were located in the capital, the technological and the technological development and communications (ICT) countries. hearing health in the health system
