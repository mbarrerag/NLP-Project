# PHOTOGRAPHS OF PIEDRAHITA FLORUS: URBAN LANDSAGE IN THE CITY OF THE PETROLEUM

Starting from the 169 photographs of the archive rescued by Floro Piedrahita, a reflection is made on the territory of Magdalena Medio and in particular of the city of Barrancabermeja in the 1920s. The previous process and the visualisation of its architecture in the photos of this forgotten artist from Antioquia
