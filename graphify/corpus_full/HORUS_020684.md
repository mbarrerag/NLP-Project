# THE CRITICITY OF THE FAMILY DISCUSSION ON PAREJA VIOLENCE

This paper assesses the validity of anti-feminist criticism in three key aspects of intimate partner violence: are the aggressors violent generalists who attack other people or specialists who attack their partners alone? Is violence in a unilateral couple, from man to woman, or bilateral? Is it an imported phenomenon from outside with migratory flows or simply concentrates on vulnerable populations? Using prison records data on perpetrators and victims corresponding to a cohort of convicted persons for a crime of intimate partner violence and a control sample condemned for any crime in Catalonia, it is concluded that anti-feminist criticism has little basis, at least in terms of violence prosecuted by the penal system. This violence is mainly a matter of men who specialize in assaulting vulnerable couples
