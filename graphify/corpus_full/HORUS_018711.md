# NUTRITIONAL ASSESSMENT OF THE MAJOR ADULT

The assessment of nutritional status is of great importance for timely nutrition intervention and consequently improvement in the prognosis of patients. In older adults, this is made even more important due to the nutritional vulnerability of this age group and the presence of comorbidities. There are tools specific nutritional screening for older adults, such as the Mini Nutritional Assessment (MNA) and the Geriátric Nutritional Risk Index (IRNG), and others such as Malnutrition Screening Tool (MST) that are validated for use in this age group. However, the anthropometric evaluation of this group presents significant challenges due to body composition changes, reduced height due to changes in the aging of bones, muscles and joints, altered skin turgency and hydration status. It is a key aspect to take into account the inherent biopsychosocial changes in the aging process that may affect the nutritional status of older adults, who in the hospital environment often present high prevalence of malnutrition
