# Time, culture, identity: Peirce and Cassirer

The aim of this research is to present a re-conceptualization of identity as it is expressed in terms of the relative logic developed by Charles Sanders Peirce, including the extension to the relationship of teridentity, his theory of sign and his system of cenopitagoric categories. The development of this work offers, for the first time, a notion of identity for anthropology based on the analysis of the nature of triadical relationships. It is shown that the terdentity is the most natural and simple form for the representation and understanding of identity . The aim of this research is to present a reconceptualization of identity based on the logic of relationships, the extension to the relationship of territory, the theory of signs, and the system of cenopythagorean categories developed by Charles Peirce.
