# IMPACT ON THE IMMOBILIARY MARKET ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF PLANNING INFORMAL SETTLEMENTS. CASE OF URBAN INTEGRAL PROJECT (PUI), NORORORIENTAL AREA IN MEDELLIN, COLOMBIA

This book is part of a series of publications through which the Faculty of Architecture of the National University of Colombia - Headquarters Medellín, wants to make public what is discussed inside it every day. Products derived from the project called "Topics and reflections of the Faculty of Architecture. Where are we going?, here are contents approaches of great interest, not only for those looking to form under the shelter of this academic institution, but also for everyone who searches looking for new reasons of cavilation that allow him to a greater understanding of the environment that inhabits socially and of his own condition as citizen. The structure of the present volume revolves around the analysis of various conditions and factors that affect and determine the regional urban planning in its character as a fundamental tool of understanding and acting within the framework of the realities of the cities and territories.
