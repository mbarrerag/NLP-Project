# Study of the state of conservation and proposed guidelines for the intervention of the San Antonio Bridge, Biene de Interés Cultural en la Locidad de Fontibón

This study focuses on the San Antonio Bridge in Fontibon, Bogotá, recognized as a Cultural Interest Asset. It evaluates its conservation status and proposes guidelines for interventions respectful of its historical, aesthetic and symbolic values. It explores the state of art and the roots of the deterioration of the bridge over the centuries, using documentary sources from the fourteenth century to the present. The lack of appreciation in Fontibon has led to the bridge to a state of deterioration. This study not only aspires to the conservation of the heritage, but also to raise awareness of the local community, often unrelated to the importance of the bridge. It proposes urban, physical and cultural criteria for future actions, supported by measurements that include citizen participation through workshops to collect oral accounts, providing a valuable qualitative perspective. The results reveal the transformation of the bridge from its origin into wood to its current configuration in qualifying. The conclusions highlight the urgent need for respectful interventions, aligned with the urban development planned in Bogotá.
