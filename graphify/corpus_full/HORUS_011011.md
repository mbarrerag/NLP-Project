# An educated bourgeois and the agrarian problem in Colombia

The anti-Ocoquen businessman Hernán Echavarría Olózaga (1911-2006) explained in his book The Common Sense of the Colombian Economy (2006) what he thought about the agrarian problem in Colombia and how it could be solved to turn the land into a productive asset and not a piggy bank that is only used to appropriate social surplus value through valorization
