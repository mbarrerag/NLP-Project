# A PENDING CRIMINAL: REPRESENTATIONS OF GENDER IDENTITIES IN DOCUMENTS OF PUBLIC EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS OF CALLAS (COLOMBIA)

The study is based on the tools of hermeneutic philosophy, on the assumption that human experience is mediated by interpretation in personal interaction through language, as an element that facilitates human cohesion. Data collection was carried out thanks to the application of projective and comprehensive techniques, addressed from symbolic interaction. The results given show how stigma and invisibility have been the constant towards non-normative gender identities-INNG-. The social representations revealed in teachers reflect the reality that prevails, are taken for granted as normal, do not know how to approach them in the educational context, in a way that continues to be reproduced within the system
