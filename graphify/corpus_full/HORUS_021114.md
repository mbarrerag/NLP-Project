# Environmental impacts of cryptocurrencies: Elements for comprehensive regulation in Colombia.

The following research project seeks to investigate the environmental impacts of cryptocurrency mining, based on the experience of the countries in which this activity is currently being developed, and bearing in mind that its use continues to spread throughout the world and, importantly, in Latin American countries. Under this assumption, the objective of the project is to provide relevant elements that can guide new initiatives for the integral regulation of cryptocurrency mining in Colombia. However, since the environment can be defined as the relationship between ecosystems and human societies, both the impacts generated by this activity in terms of energy consumption, generation and disposition of technological waste generated, as well as the dynamics of the distribution of benefits obtained in favor of the people and companies involved in this, in the face of the burdens assumed by the State and society as a whole by the adoption of new markets of digital assets among them the cryptocurrency. With a view to this objective, different sources of primary and secondary information will be analyzed to identify the environmental impacts that the cryptocurrency mining has left in the main countries in which Colombia, then will be carried out a review of the special initiatives of regulation of the cryptocurrencys and to this objective, initially will be analyzed to be analyzed by the environmental sectors
