# MATRICITY OF SOCIAL ACCOUNTING FOR AGRICULTURAL SECTOR CONSIDERING SIZE OF THE PRODUCTION UNITS AND ARMED CONFLICT

The social accounting matrix captures the economic and social structure of a country, allowing both sectoral and general balance analyses. The objective of this article is to describe the procedure followed for the construction of a social accounting matrix with emphasis on the Colombian agricultural sector, for 2014, according to the most recent data available. Among other sources of information for this procedure are the utilization and supply matrices, integrated economic accounts, national agricultural census data and the incidence index of the armed conflict. The matrix built emphasizes the following aspects: (i) main agricultural crops: coffee, rice, banana, maize, potato and yuca; (ii) size of agricultural production units: large, medium and small; (iii) armed conflict; and (iv) location of households: rural and urban. The social accounting matrix obtained in this work contains information on the interrelation between the different agents of the Colombian economy, highlighting the structure of the Colombian agricultural sector according to the main crops, the size of production units and the incidence of armed conflict at the regional level
