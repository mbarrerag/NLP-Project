# THE ROLE OF ADVANCE AIRLINE TICKET BOOKING ON AIRLINE CHOICE PREFERENCE BY DISCRETE CHOICE ANALYSIS

This research is based on a discrete choice theory where it explores how passengers choose airlines for domestic flights. The found model provides crucial information for both airline strategic plans and transportation engineering research. However, beyond considering prices and onboard amenities, the model shows that advance fly ticket purchase in days is a fundamental factor in airline choice process. This variable has a significant impact on the traveler's choice behavior
