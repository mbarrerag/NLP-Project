# POSTMORTEM EXPEDITION OF THE CHOROGRAPHIC COMMISSION OF COLOMBIA: THE DOCUMENTARY COLLECTION OF AGUSTIN CODAZZI IN ITALY IN A DIGITAL HISTORY PROJECT

The National University Library of Turin, Italy, has housed since 1898 the private archive of the geographer Agustín Codazzi, who directed the Corographic Commission in the territory of present-day Colombia between 1850 and 1859. The history of the journey of this collection made up of cartographic sketches, handwritten correspondence, annotations and maps is relevant. Although the existence of this fund is a known fact, this article provides new evidence about its arrival in Italy, as well as contextualization of the procedural history of the documentary collection. This reconstruction hopes to contribute to the knowledge of nineteenth-century historical cartography to which the Chorographic Commission contributed significantly. At the same time, the documentary study constitutes the preliminary step to the digitization of this collection, the first phase of the Codazzi Digital project. Illustrating this digital history project, the article discusses the challenges and opportunities of critical and decolonial Digital Humanities in relation to the documentary heritage of European travelers and explorers in American lands
