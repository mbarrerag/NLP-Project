# ESTIMATION ANALYSIS FOR THE SEIR MODEL WITH STOCHASTIC PERTURBATION FOR THE COVID-19 OUTBREAK IN BOGOTÁ

Stochastic modeling in epidemiology has become an important tool in analyzing the spread and control of infectious diseases. One of the most frequently used models is the SEIR model, which is divided into four compartments of individuals, the susceptible(S), the exposed(E), the infective(I), and the removed(R) individuals in the population. In this talk, we construct an SEIR model with stochastic perturbations on the disease transmission rates and present an estimation method of Maximum likelihood Estimation (MLE) and Mean-Squared Error (MSE) for the stochastic perturbation parameter (s), also called a volatility parameter. Finally, we apply our model to study and illustrate the basic measures of the infectiousness of the COVID-19 dynamic in Bogotá
