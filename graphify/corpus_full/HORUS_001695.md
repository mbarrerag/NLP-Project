# The dynamics of polarization in Colombia: An analysis of the type of polarization that operated in the country in the period 2016-2021

The polarization that intensified after the announcement of the peace process between the government of Juan that Manuel Santos and the FARC, generated a social fracture that exacerbated the radicalization of positions and created an atmosphere of anxiety that was evidenced in three events of the recent history of the country: the peace plebiscite of 2016, the presidential campaign of 2018 and the ‘social flare’ of 2021. The divisory dynamics and confrontations during these three events reflected a pattern of conflictivity based on an emotional frame of reference that consolidated the friend-enemy dichotomy, fed by the messages and signals that the political elites sent to the people on the issue of peace, degrading the discussion and weakening the public debate. This research focused on identifying and understanding several aspects of polarization, including the main theme of polarization, polarized groups, the main leaders of each sector and the strategies that they used, as well as the degree and type of polarization that operated in the country during the three analytical events. months and signs sent by political elites to the people ruling the peace issue, degrading the discussion and weekning public debate.
