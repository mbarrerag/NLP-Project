# IMPLEMENTING TECHNOLOGICAL INTERVENTIONS IN MENTAL HEALTH

The development and implementation of technological interventions involves important tasks and challenges at multiple levels. Despite the wide scientific recognition of technological resources, especially in terms of changes in the way we relate and how services in mental health are offered, there are still great challenges related to the process of implementing this type of interventions in different contexts (Aguilera, 2015). Within these challenges, many are similar to those of face-to-face treatments, such as the stigma in mental health (Clement et al., 2013). In addition to this, it is essential to consider that in the process of implementing any technological intervention, it is necessary to take into account elements that go beyond the scientific and technical development of this intervention. In general, the aspects to be taken into account in the implementation of this type of interventions can be divided into four main areas: contextual elements, related to the type of technology used, related to institutions and professionals in mental health, and related to users. At the contextual level, the elements of the deep and superficial structure of the culture, the political and regulatory bodies, the characteristics of the health systems, the access to the technology, the subjective representations and the practical aspects related to the mental health and the health and the users. Given all this, the implementation of technological interventions is complex, multidimensional and is influenced by a wide variety of factors. Therefore, it is essential to recognize that the conditions of possibility for the use of technologies in mental health care spaces are not only given with access to a technological tool, but that this process must take into account the importance of reading and modifying contexts, the types of scientific development required, the transformation of professional environments and practices, and to respond to the specific needs and conditions of users
