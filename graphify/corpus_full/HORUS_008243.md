# Linguistics through comics

The development of comics puts this discursive genre as one of the most important mass phenomena of the current in Colombia and the world. With presence in advertising, in magazines, in the publishing world and without a doubt where it is recognized most, in social networks; the comic has been shown as a means with communicative possibilities as wide as other discursive genres. This is due in large part to the semiotic nature that accompanies a specific hybrid language as it is the convergence of a textual and visual component. It is in this sense that an analysis of this hybrid language is shown as an opportunity to understand not only what makes the comic what is — its definition — but also to find the optimal way to integrate the comic book within the scientific and academic field. In this research, the main elements that make up the comic, proposing a definition and a classification of these; besides proposing a comic that integrates all these elements that make up the comic in a proposal of a disculative dye with a set of linguistic and semiotic concepts. . This research will analyze, from a semiotic and linguistic perspective, the main elements that make up comics, proposing a definition and classification of these elements.
