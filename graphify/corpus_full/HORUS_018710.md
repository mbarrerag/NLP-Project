# SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION NOVEL THIOSEMICARBAZONE DERIVATIVES AND THEIR PD(II) COMPLEXES. HTTPS://SILQCOM08.NAVUS.IO/POSTERS

In this work the synthesis, characterization and in vitro evaluation of the biological activity of four TSC-derived ligands (1-3) and two new Pd(II) complexes (4) are reported. The ligands were prepared by condensation reaction of nitrobenzaldehyde with thiosemicarbazide in M/L ratio 1:2. The compounds were characterized by means of microanalyses, molar conductivities, magnetic susceptibilities, IR, Uv-Vis, 1H and 12C NMRspectroscopies. In the complexes of Pd(II) TSC, were observed the deprotonation of the groups NH-C=S, which suggests that the coordination of the ligand to the metal ion ocurred through the nitrogen and the sulfur atoms
