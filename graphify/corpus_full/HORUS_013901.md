# NEW MATERIALS

Currently, intensive research is being carried out on photovoltaic materials based on organic/inorganic hybrid compounds that grow with perovskit-type crystalline structure, because in very short time they have allowed to manufacture highly efficient solar cells; as these cells are degraded when environmental conditions of humidity, illumination and temperature are exposed, recently began a research program focused on the development of fully inorganic perovskit-type materials with which solar cells of high efficiency and stability can be manufactured.With this proposal to be executed in cooperation with the State University of Miracle-Ecuador (UNEMI) and the National University of Colombia is planned, on the one hand implementing an infrastructure of synthesis of materials with perovskit structure and initiating a program aimed at developing in the medium term solar cells based on perovskta, and on the other hand making contributions to the development of a solar cell prototype based on inorganic perovskits, which exhibrates better stability than those manufactured using hybrid perovskits as an active layer; this particular activity would be developed in the National University of Colombia by the Group of Semiconductors (GMS). x by reaction of the PbI2/PbBr2 bilayer with the precursors CsI and CsBr dissolved in methanol, through a process of _x0093_dip coating; additionally, a study will be carried out to optimize the properties of the perovskita layer by correlation of synthesis parameters with the characterization results performed through X-ray diffraction measures (XRD), electron scanning microscopy (SEM) and spectrophotometry. A study will also be done to try to improve performance and stability of the device by substituting the Pb2+ cation by Zn2+ and Sn2+ ions
