# STANDARDIZATION AND RELIABILITY OF THE BATTERY FOR THE EVALUATION OF THE LINGUISTIC PROCESSING IN ADULT (PEPLA).

The battery for the Evaluation of Linguistic Processing in Adult (PEPLA) promises to be a valuable tool for Colombia, in order to obtain the linguistic profile of adults with acquired language disorders, in valuing the basic processes of understanding and production of spoken messages, as well as the understanding and production of written messages. This reliability study aimed to contribute to thenormalization of this battery in a region of the Colombian Southern. Its methodological design was quantitative, descriptive, non-experimental, and 145 adults participated, between 18 and 80 years. The responses were those expected according to the content of the test and were found above the 95 percentile of assertivity.The statisticalfadeCronbach at = 0.903 was indicative of reliability, classified as excellent; on the other hand, the statistic Kruskal-Wallis established a significant relationship of the responses according to the age range and academic level (p < 0.05 and p < 0.00).The battery is concluded that it presents stable normative data and has a high reliability of internal consistency; therefore, it is possible to meet the need for the Language, validation process
