# esterification between lactic acid and butyl alcohol for the production of N-Butyl lactate by reactive distillation.

Reactive distillation is a unitary operation that combines reaction and separation into a single team, which makes it an economically and energetically viable proposal. This work studies the process of production of n-butyl lactate by means of reactive distillation, from the foundations of the operation, such as phase equilibrium and reaction kinetics, to a complete design from a conceptual approach. Experimental information of phase equilibrium was evaluated and the interactions of the quaternary mixture were properly described using a model of activity coefficients for the liquid phase (NRTL, +0.3), while for the steam phase it was assumed ideal. A kinetic expression based on pseudo-homogeneous equations was used to describe the process of sterification with a heterogeneous catalyst. Subsequently, the conceptual design of the reactive distillation process was developed, using simultaneously the phase equilibrium and kinetics previously selected using the Aspen Plus simulator. Finally, with a case based on rigorous simulation of the operation, the optimization and control of the reactiva distillation process was studied, to obtain the most appropriate conditions of the industrial operation
