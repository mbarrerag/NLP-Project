# Evaluation of a semi-pilot scale electrocoagulation system for the treatment of synthetic waste water in the oil industry.

In the present study, a semi-pilot scale electrocoagulation system for the treatment of synthetic residual water of the oil industry was implemented and evaluated. To achieve this objective, the research was divided into two phases. In the first phase the experiments were carried out at laboratory scale, the methodology used consisted of varying only one parameter at a time while the others remained constant. In this, the parameters were analyzed: electrocoagulation time or residence time in the reactor, current density, initial pH and conductivity. In the second phase, the conceptual design, assembly and implementation of the semi-pilot scale electrocoagulation unit, operating in continuous mode and Batch. Based on the results obtained in the first phase and in some preliminary tests, a statistical design of experiments was performed, using the response surface methodology (called RSM by its acronym in English) with the aim of finding optimal operating conditions.
