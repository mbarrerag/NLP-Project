# Study of the inhibitory potential of chemical constituents present in Hypericum mexicanum (Hypericaceae) on pancreatic lipase and α-glucosidase enzymes

Obesity and diabetes, two of the main causes of mortality, also contribute to the development of other chronic diseases such as cardiovascular and renal diseases. A promising approach to the treatment of these pathologies is the inhibition of pancreatic lipase enzymes (LP) and α-glucosidase (AG), which is key in the metabolism of lipids and carbohydrates. Although synthetic drugs such as orlistat and acarbose are effective, their side effects have led to the search for safer alternatives, such as plant compounds (flavonoids, terpenoids and phenolic acids), which have shown inhibitory properties. This research contributes to the search for molecules with inhibitory potential against LP and AG from the chemical and enzyme inhibition study of secondary metabolites present in the air parts of Hyperum mexican.
