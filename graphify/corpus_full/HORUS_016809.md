# THE EFFECTS OF ZR ADDITIONS TO MAGNETRON SPUTTERED TISI NITRIDES: A STRUCTURE ¿ PROPERTIES ANALYSIS BY XRD, XPS AND NANOHARDNESS CHARACTERIZATION

Face centered cubic (FCC) transition metal nitrides were obtained by magnetron co-sputtering in a dual condition, in a reactive atmosphere. TiSi nitrides modified with Zr additions were co-sputtered a TiSi target and an elemental Zr target. The concentration of Zr was varied by tunning the power applied to the Zr target (0W, 20W, 40W 60W 90W and 120W), while the power applied to the TiSi target was fixed at 180 W. Thus, coatings with Zr concentration ranging zero up to 29 at. % were obtained. A small addition of Zr (1,3 Zr at.%) triggered a 40 % increasing of hardness regarding Zrfree coatings (33.7 GPa vs 24 GPa, respectively). Further Zr additions showed a marginal hardening effect (36.6 GPa for samples with 29 Zr at.%). XRD analysis showed formation of monophasic FCC nitrides with not evidences about formation of amorphous phase nor hexagonal phase. Zr bearing coatings showed increased lattice constant as the Zr content was increased and negligible variation of crystallite size (around (81± 4 Å)). X-Ray photoelectron spectroscopy analyses showed formation of both oxynitrides rich in Ti and Zr and Si nitrides, SixNy, which was interpreted as formation of a nanocomposite structure. Formation of such SixNy phase was sensitive to variations of the Zr concentration
