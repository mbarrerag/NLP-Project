# MEXICO MULTI-FAMILIARS AS EXAMPLE OF SUSTAINABLE SOCIAL LIVING

The priority theme of architecture and urban planning of the twentieth century was social housing. Modern architects took up the mass production of housing. Social housing in Mexico is the main issue of Mexican economic miracle. In the 1940s and 1950s the country had sufficient resources to invest in social issues. One of the avant-garde experiments is the German President Urban Centre (CUPA) (1946-1949). cupa was succeeded by the president juárez urban center (cupj) (1952), the multifamily for teachers in unam (1952), the santa fe unit (1957), the monoalco-tlatelolco single housing set (1964), as well as the independence unit of alejandro prieto and josé maría gutiérrez (1960).
