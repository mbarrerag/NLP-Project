# Perceptions and experiences of older adults on sexuality and sexual health services in Colombia

Abstract Introduction: Although worldwide it has been shown that the elderly population maintains an active sexual life, it is limited by stereotypes, physiological changes results of the aging process and the absence of comprehensive health strategies for sexuality. To document the sexual experience of older adults in the country and understand the reasons that promote or limit the search for sexual health care. A qualitative study in which 39 interviews and 4 virtual workshops were conducted with men and women aged 50 and over in Barranquilla, Bogotá, Cali, Medellín, without excluding people from other cities. Among the main findings it is evident that the elderly population has positive evaluations in relation to sexuality and sex, but their development is limited, especially for women, by gender roles. Sexuality in older persons is traversed by initial education, the transformation of ideas about sexuality throughout their life paths, the transformations of the body, the state of health, recent sexual practices and experiences, and the way in which they approach the sexual health dimension with health professionals.
