# Parenting, autonomy and socioemotional skills in preteens

The present study aims to evaluate the relationship between the maternal upbringing practices associated with communication and expression of affection with the socio-emotional abilities of the pre-adolescent children, mediated by the autonomy of the latter. The design of the research was transversal, the structural modeling was used with path analysis. The sample was composed of 280 days of mothers with their respective children, who had ages between 10 and 12 years, students from public and private schools in the city of Bogotá, D.C. Self-care questionnaires were used. As part of the results, the data showed a direct and significant relationship between the variables maternal communication and social ability. In turn, it was found that the covariation between the maternal variables communication and expression of affection have a significant relationship with the functional autonomy of the pre-adolescents. For its part, the autonomy directly and significantly influences the emotional, social and autoefficacy ability. These findings allow to conclude that the maternal upbringing mediated by the autonomy of the children does have a direct and indirect effect on the manifestation of the socio-emotional abilities of the children during the pre-adolescence. data shown a direct and significant relationship between the maternal variables communication and social skills.
