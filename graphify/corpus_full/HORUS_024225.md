# Words don't reach me

Dance in front of the camera, for the camera, for me. Can I change?, Can I reconfigure the way she sees me (the camera)?, can I see myself?. Dance as a daily exercise of training and re-configuration of the look within a family space, the room. Between sounds, textures, objects and dresses that inhabit space and body. .
