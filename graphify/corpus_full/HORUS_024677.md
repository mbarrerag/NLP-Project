# Of my shadows and his constant appearances

This project presents the process of creation-research developed around the question of the absences that inhabit the personal archive starting especially from childhood and the houses inhabited during this stage. This review provides an approach to collection, registration and organization processes as a plastic tool, to search for ways of visualizing and granting a “place” to those absences that appear as shadows intermittently posing themselves above the present. From these processes arises the Institute of absences saved with affection, an entity of fictional character that allows to cobibe the heterogeneity of the actions, searches and objects kept during the journey by mastery and that takes advantage of some characteristics of the museum spaces to create the connecting thread between the pieces that it keeps. . This project creates the research and creation processes developed around the analysis and process of the absent my personal archive, especially starting from the chilihood and the houses inhabit during this stage
