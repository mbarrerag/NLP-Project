# Knowledge and territories: a look at the Nodes of Cultural Development from the Young creators of Chocó and the Cultural Foundation Ojo de Agua de Barichara.

The present final master's degree is a research that uses the methodology of Participative Action Research (IAP) to solve the question posed by the student who opts for the title of Master in Cultural Management, what factors explain the achievements achieved by these two organizations in consolidating their own knowledge and in their relations with the community and what challenges they face to constitute themselves as nodes of cultural development? This research is located in the municipalities of Quibdó (Chocó) and Barichara (Santander), specifically in the organizational and cultural processes of the groups Young creators of Chocó JCH and Cultural Foundation Ojo de Agua; who have a wide artistic and pedagogical journey. Therefore, these groups have clear that their work is guided by the idea of building a social fabric for the transformation of the reality of their communities and territories from artistic education. This analysis is carried out from epistemological constructs such as Participative Action, dialogue of pedagogical-artistic knowledge, among others.
