# Contemporary three-dimensional thinking and creation. An exercise in relation between traditional techniques and processes with contemporary techniques and methods used in the creation of sculptural and utilitarian objects

The project seeks to encourage inter- and transdisciplinary work around a series of questions that derive from topics treated in undergraduate subjects in which the creation of sculpturals with symbolic value and objects of design with utilitarian character is addressed, establishing a dialogue between ancestral technologies as manual modeling and relating them to contemporary processes of digital manufacturing. Linking students, in principle, of the programs of Plastic Arts, Industrial Design, Architecture and with possibility of the participation of students of other programs of the Faculty of Arts It will be worked under the premise of creation as the beginning of the obtaining of the form and its transformation using different technologies and materials such as micro-smelling of precision, manual modelling and 3D modelling, digitization and editing of digital files, 3D printing in casteable resins
