# The children of the Millennium

This grade project, developed within the framework of the line of Narrative (Cuento and Novel) as a basis for the opening of the line of Visual Narratives within the Mastery in Creative Scriptures, presents a one shot of presentation and or sale for a graphic novel constructed from fragmented chapters. The work proposes a critical look at a dystopian city where the implementation of a mass transport system, far from representing an advance, has triggered a series of social, emotional and psychic transformations. In this context, the characters face a constant conflict between their inner world and the reality that surrounds them, marked by the exacerbation of negative emotions and a profound collective disease. The work is composed of two parts: a poetic one that defines the aesthetic and conceptual foundations of the project, and a first narrative result shaped by the script and the initial chapter, which lay the basis for the final graphic novel model .
