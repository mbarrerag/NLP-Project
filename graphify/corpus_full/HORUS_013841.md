# Strengthening of short marketing circuits of the Peasant Association of Antioquia (ACA) and the Social Urban Seed Corporation (CSSU)

The project seeks to strengthen the Short Marketing Circuits of the Asociación Campesina de Antioquia-ACA and the Corporación Social Semilla Urbana-CSSU, organizations of the subregions of Oriente and Valle de Aburra in the department of Antioquia. This proposal is articulated in the local context, within the framework of the demand for peasant rights, defense of water and environment, which has been created by the social movement of the East Antioqueño and which allows to place the dispute for the planning of the territory and sustainable development models in the use of land and water, which finally have an impact on the nutritional and health care system, based on the use of agroecology, conscious consumption, clean production, knowledge and peasant rooting. The Short Marketing Circuits (CCC) are a planning model that seeks the identification of farmers, consumers and distributors (amounted to the structure of an efficient distribution network that connects these actors, around sustainable practices that add value to the production-distribution-consumption relationship. For the development of this project, a methodological strategy is proposed that is part of a framework and an understanding of the ACA and CSSU, in addition, implement a social strategy of social and community appropriation of knowledge about it. Therefore, we seek to propose models of CCC that can last over time and that adapt to the needs and contexts in which they will be implemented
