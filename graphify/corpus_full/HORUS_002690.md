# A history of conjugal violence in the COLONIAL PANAMA, chagrin and portobelo.

This speech revolves around the conyugicide committed by Cristobalina González in 1795, in Chagre, Panama. Apparently, it was a weak husband married to Félix Borrallo, sergeant of the Royal Body of Artillery. It was prosecuted for killing her husband after a discussion in which he threatened to assault her with his sword. The woman took a knife that was under the bed and put it near her neck, which caused the man to die cut off. The marriage of Cristobalina and Felix had been made a witness because of the continuous infidelities of the military, who not only went to prostitutes, but when his wife went to Panama, ate and lived with a neighbor named Maria Dolores de la Puente.
