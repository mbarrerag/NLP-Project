# ESTIMATES OF PARAMETERS IN EPIDEMIOLOGICAL MODELS WITH ALLEATORY PERTURBATIONS AND APPLICATION FOR COVID-19 DATA IN BOGOTÁ D.C.

Recently, different stochastic modelling approaches have been introduced into epidemiological models for epidemic dynamics. In particular, models with random disturbances, in addition to predicting the number of infected per unit of time, also consider climatic fluctuations and any other external factor that can modify the infection rate of an epidemic. Recent articles have shown that factors such as particulate matter and contamination affect the spread of COVID-19, which is why it is important to consider such factors in the modelling of this epidemic. Thus, it is recommended to use a model with random disturbances. In this work, it is proposed to model the dynamics of COVID-19 for the city of Bogotá using a model with random disturbances and to evaluate corresponding parameters
