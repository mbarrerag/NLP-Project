# ASSISTANCE PRACTICE AND LEVEL OF DEPENDENCE OF THE MAJOR ADULT WITH CRÓNIC DISEASE

ABSTRACT Introduction: Self-management as a healthy behavior and the physical capacity proper to age are little explored, but can lead to changes in the lifestyle of older adults who suffer from chronic diseases. Characterize the relationship that exists between the practice of self-management and the level of dependence of older adults with chronic disease. Correlating and longitudinal descriptive design developed in the months of July to November 2020. The population of 105 older adults with chronic disease that are part of associations of older adults in Ibagué, Colombia. The measurement was carried out with the Partners in Health Scale scale and physical capacity with Barthel index. For the analysis descriptive analysis, Pearson correlation test and a linear regression model. Women represented 60% of participants and men 40%.
