# Design, formulation and application of a model for the assessment of pre-feasibility of housing projects in Bogotá- Planihovienda, validation in three housing projects

From a mixed research, a model for the evaluation of ‘Predictability of Housing Projects in Bogotá-PlaniVive: Case study’ is developed, proposing a methodology for the evaluation of housing projects in Bogotá that, in addition to traditional economic and financial analyses, includes normative variables from the urban, technical and environmental aspects. All this aims to offer a standardized tool that aims to minimize uncertainty in decision-making and, in turn, allows greater efficiency in the profits of investors and greater satisfaction to the end user. Thus, this research provides a tool that allows to demonstrate the primary parameters to determine the pre-feasibility of housing projects by allowing generating an evaluation scheme with real data from 3 housing projects and from the percentages of impact of each chapter evaluated from conception to completion of the project, which is possible through a methodology of economic analysis from projects in Bogota-planning, vulnerability of housing. from the percentages of incident of each chapter evaluated from the conception to the completion of the project, which is possible through a methodology of economic analysis that can be applied in the field of construction companies in Bogota D.C
