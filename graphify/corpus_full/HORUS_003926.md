# INTER-SPECTIFIC ANALYSIS OF THE ALLAR FORM OF MACHOS OF GENDER FANNIA (DIPTERA: FANNIIDAE)

The genus Fannia Robineau-Desvoidy, 1830 is the most diverse of the family Fanniidae (Diptera: Calyptratae). Despite the importance of some of its species in the medical and forensic field, due to its attraction to decomposition and excretion matter, knowledge about its biology and ecology remains limited. This limitation is, at least in part, linked to the difficulties in its identification, which depends mainly on the observation of morphological characters in the male genitals, which requires a technical expertitium. In this study the variation in the elongation of males of ten species of the genus Fannia of Colombia was analyzed. The comparison of the phenetic distances showed 12 reference points in the images of the wings, from which overlap of Procrustes was made and the variables of the form were obtained to include the most elongation with the multivariate analysis.
