# Assessment of the environmental impact associated with the transport of aggregates for construction in the Aburá Valley

The research project investigates practices and processes of extraction in the quarries regarding the consumption of equipment and its associated emissions, contrasting this with literature references, and in particular on the consumption and routes of transporters linked to these quarries, where characteristics and behaviors are identified that affect CO2 emissions in the region. This seeks to evaluate the environmental impact associated with the transport of aggregates for construction in the Aburra Valley from the design of concrete mixtures, where the properties of aggregates of the quarries studied are identified and, in function of this, establish alternatives for the use of these resources through more efficient dosages that meet design requirements and in turn estimate their impact by transport to the site. For the cases analyzed, very high values are found to the load by type of transport equipment and their operating time, where 76% of vehicles have at least 26 years of age and the most current one is model 2013. Of these, 97% use Diesel. According to (Parga and Romero, 2013) the main cause of climate change is due to CO2 emissions and these fuels
