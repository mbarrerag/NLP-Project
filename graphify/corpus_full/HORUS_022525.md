# What was lost with the closing of the Higher Normal School?

The Higher Normal School (1936-1951) was a pioneering institution in the field of scientific training of teachers of the country, conceived in the period of the liberal reforms of the 1930s. It was highlighted by its high academic standards, for incorporating under the lay and mixed format a specialized training programme on the bases of development psychology and the main advances in other fields of knowledge; it hosted in its payroll a significant group of European academics who fled from the totalitarian regimes that were established in Europe. It was closed in the transition period of conservative governments of the forties and early fifties.
