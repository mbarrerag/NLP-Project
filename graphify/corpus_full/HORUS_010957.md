# TOURISM: A sector that contributes to sustainable development objectives to the implementation of its actors.

The objective of this chapter is to analyze how citizen participation and the empowerment of the community socio-stakeholders contribute to the achievement of the goals of the sustainable development goals from the tourism sector in the municipality Usiacurí Department Atlántico, Colombia. To this end, the participatory practices of the community socio-stakeholders for the sustainability of the municipality from the tourism sector are described; representative empowerment of the actors towards the conscious transformation of its environment is characterized and finally the contribution that the participation and empowerment of the community socio-stakeholders represent to the fulfilment of the sustainable development objectives of the 2030 Agenda is determined. Based on the premise that the empowerment of the actors favors a tourism aligned with the objectives of sustainable development and with the implementation of a public policy of legitimate sustainability, a descriptive study is developed with an interpretative explanatory approach, to analyze the experience of the municipality Usiacurí as a tourist destination.
