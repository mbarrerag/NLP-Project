# Dialgebra Structure of F[x]⊗F[x], Derivations and Diderivations

Let F be a field of zero characteristic. We define a dialgebra structure over the polynomial algebra F[x]⊗F[x], and we give a complete classification of the Lie algebra of derivations and the space of diderivations. We also show that the results of this work are closely related to those obtained in Lin and Zhang (Commun Algebra 38(9):3417–3447, 2010) and Restrepo-Sánchez (Inmersión de dialgebras en dialgebras separables, adición de unidades barra y biderivaciones, Tesis de Maestría, Universidad de Antioquia, Medellin, 2010)
