# Proposal for multiple store designs based on partnership rules.

The success of a store depends on its ability to understand the behaviour of customers and its ability to react to changes in consumer habits. The analysis of data on purchases made by customers is key to seeking new sales opportunities. In this work, the state of art is presented based on the guidelines for the systematic review and classification of literature, and an algorithm is proposed for the generation of multiple designs of sections of a store in which it is considered: (i) the mining of partnership rules, (ii) the total number of units purchased from products in transactions in the generation of rules (aspect that is often ignored in the classic association rules), (iii) a hierarchical structure for the classification of products developed by the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs, (iv) the selection of interesting partnership rules (which meet certain thresholds) and (v) a set of restrictions that certain products or categories of products should not be close in a section of the store. Finally, an experiment is presented with the generation of designs for which the proposed algorithm on a set of public sales data from a supermarket is applied in order to see the feasibility of the proposal
