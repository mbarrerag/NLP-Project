# Barbarie and subjectivation: critique of categorical reason

The categorization in the knowledge processes carries the need to understand the world from its classification, and offers the possibility to organize thoughts and reflections in a field of understanding. However, categorization may have a dimension of dehumanization as long as it is used to make impossible the transcendence of human beings. In the events of the Second World War, a people suffered the violence of categorization by being defined by an ideology as mere objects for destruction. The present project aims to analyze the various ways in which the categorical reason was expressed in those times and that currently has certain vestiges in the persistent ambition to materialize attitudes considered to themselves as superior and that seek the classification and sectorization of human life, assuming for itself the correct way of seeing and preserving the world. Understanding the machinery of the categorical reason expressed in these times gives us clues of why the dehumanizing events liberated in the first half of the twentieth century, justify the need for their non-repetition.
