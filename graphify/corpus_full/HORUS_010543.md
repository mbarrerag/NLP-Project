# CLASSIFICATION OF COSMETICAL INGREDIENTS AND RESTRICTIONS

The United Nations Industrial Development Organization (onudi), through the Safe+ programme, coordinated several international cooperation strategies to support the growth of the Colombian cosmetics industry, including training in cosmetic product safety assessment. In this context, in 2018, one of the actions undertaken by the National University of Colombia, was to offer the Course of Training of Consultants in Cosmetic Products Safety Assessment, through the Department of Pharmacy of the Faculty of Sciences, with the advice and design of the international consultant Dr. Philippe Masson. In 2022, this document was expanded in number of hours taught, which allowed the Diplomacy in Cosmetic Products Safety Assessment (Safety Assessor Training), in the telepresential modality, which counted with the participation of more than 30 experts. This paper presents the summaries of 53 conferences, which made part of six modules: 1) regulatory context of the cosmetic materials and cosmetics; 2) safety aspects related to ingredients and cosmetic products; 3) foundations of physiology, immunology and risk management in Colombia; 4) tools of toxicological safety; 6) we offer the functional and technical assessment of the pharmaceutical nature of the product
