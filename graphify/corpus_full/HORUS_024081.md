# Cicadellidae) insect vector of Mollicutes in corn, in the department of Huila.

Dalbulus maidis is an insect of agricultural importance because it is considered one of the vectors of the pathogens Spiroplasma kunkelii and Candidatus phytoplasma asteris, pathogens that make part of the group of the Mollicutes, which cause the disease known as the flattening of corn. In recent years, an increase in the incidence of the disease has been observed in different areas of maize production, associated with the increase of the populations of Dalbulus sp. The department of Huila has presented affectation since 2015. Based on the problem posed, this work had as objectives, corroborating the species of the genus Dalbulus associated with the sanitary problem of maize in municipalities of the department of Huila, determining its abundance and evaluating the presence of possible morphotypes.
