# MOtivation for voluntary participation in a country without voluntary citizenship: the case of the programme in Colombia

The theory of dominant status (Smith,1994) and the theory of resources (Wilson & Musick, 1997), have been hegemonic in the explanation of who decides to be voluntary. For Hustinx (2022) these two theories indicate that it is the domination groups that are most likely to be voluntary because they possess the economic, social and cultural resources for this, resources that in turn are associated with a status of domination. A large number of empirical studies in developed countries suggest the validity of these theories.
