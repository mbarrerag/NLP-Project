# ENTRAMados. Appropriation, conservation and fair trade of the hat of Aguadeño

The National University of Colombia Sede Manizales in 2021 realized the project TRAMA. Weavers, appropriation and quality of life in Aguadas, a strategy that sought to identify the intangible cultural heritage of the municipality of Aguadas especially in order to give value to the work carried out by the weavers of water hats. This process generated the identification and validation of different problems of this population of women (approximately 500) and it was found that for the majority of weavers their work is not valued both economically and culturally and heritage, besides they are denigrated and mistreated by the marketers who make the final process of hat with machinery and where the great part of money of the sale of hats remains, these do not have accessibility to carry out the finishing of their products and if they do not know how to market them. All these factors, besides the health problems that present, have generated the disinterest of the new generations to continue with the tradition and see an economic possibility for their lives. Today there are very few young people who are engaged in the process of weavage. tradition, generate in young people appropriation of their culture and the productive processes that the hat entails and generate digital marketing strategies that allow to reach directly the consumer with fair trade characteristics
