# Humanize health through the design of communications services

The health institutions are developed in changing contexts and, at the same time, must respond to ensure a quality care in the provision of humanized services. Therefore, this research work has the general objective to propose a model of humanization in the National University Hospital (HUN), based on the design of services in communications, which is developed from the following specific objectives: to establish the approaches of humanization and the design of services applied in communications for the creation of the model; to construct a model of humanization supported in the co-creation with users, and to propose improvements in the humanization program of the HUN with its application in the external consultation. The research corresponds to a qualitative study with a phenomenological and propositive approach through the methodological application of the design of services and tools such as travel maps, prototyping and co-creation, starting from the identification of users; the context of the HUN to the construction of services and the theoretical foundations of the theory of the complexity; concepts of the design of services in communication, and humanization in health. Three phases of development were generated, where the theoretical information was triangulated and complementary to the practice model of the humanization of the humanization of the humanization of the human humanization model attributed by co-creation with users; to propose improvements in HUN’s humanization program applied on the outward department.
