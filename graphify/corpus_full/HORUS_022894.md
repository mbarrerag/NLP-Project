# Characterization by Colorimetry of Banana Accesions of the System of Germoplasm Banks in Colombia

The color is one of the sensory attributes that best defines the quality of a food, despite the investigations on the surface color of pulp of musacean fruits in Colombia are scarce. This information is of great utility to identify promising materials destined for fresh consumption and agro-industrial processing. In this study the superficial color of the pulp of 25 accessions of banana Musa sp. (musaceae) of the bank of germoplasma of musacea of the system of germoplasma banks of the nation for food and agriculture (sbgnaa) existing in the colombian agricultural research corporation (agrosavia) was characterized.
