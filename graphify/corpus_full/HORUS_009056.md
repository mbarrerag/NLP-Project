# SPACE ANALYSIS OF HOMICIDIES IN THE CITY OF MEDELLIN DURING THE FINALIZATION OF HEALTH EMERGENCY AND RETURN TO NORMALITY

Statistically analyzing the homicides of a city is essential to understand violence, support the decision-making of assertive, identify risk factors and protection, evaluate policies and programs for the reduction of the ́indexes of this phenomenon. This type of studies are fundamental tools to effectively address the problem of murder violence and work towards the construction of safer communities. The collection of information related to homicides brings with it a geospatial label intrinsically and its occurrence occurs randomly, therefore, we can analyze its spatial distribution and obtain conclusions supported by the theory of the punctual processes. In this study the interest is to analyze the spatial behavior of homicides in the city of Medellín between November 2021 and February 2023, period that corresponds to the completion of the health emergency and the return to normality, with the aim of determining if there are variations between the risk zones during this temporary window given the social context. In this study is carried out a statistical analysis supported from the characteristics of first and second order, which allow to identify the zones of greater probability of occurrence and to determine the dynamic of the phenomenon as a series of near spatial events.
