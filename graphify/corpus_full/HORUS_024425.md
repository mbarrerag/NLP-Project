# MORPHOLOGICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF LABOUR FLOWS WITH COLUMNING DISFUND IN FRONT TO SAINT ROSA DE CAbal, RISARALDA.

In this work, the characterization of a site where there is a occurrence of columnar disjunction in lava flows associated with the Paramillo Volcano Complex of Santa Rosa (CVPSR) was carried out in the Manzanillo sidewalk belonging to the municipality of Santa Rosa de Cabal, Risaralda. Observations of morphological, lithological and geomechanical type were made, finding themselves in the appreciable facietal flow of the opening and colonnade, quadrilateral as predominant geometry, lithology corresponding to Andesitic rocks and a geomechanical categorization of the rock of the mass as through the RMR (Rock Mass Rating) method. Based on these observations, it is also evident that the impurity, poor and irregular development of the columnar disjunction is also evident, which allows us to infer that the process of location involved a possible interaction of extra agents to the air, such as water or ice, which also represents a high and inefficient energy expense. Likewise, the features of the site have characteristics similar to some studied by other authors in various parts of Colombia and the world
