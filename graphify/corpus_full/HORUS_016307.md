# ASSESSMENT OF THE MICROBIAN COMMUNITY IN THE BIOFILTRATION OF H2S AND NH3

Biofiltration is mainly a biotechnologies of high efficiency and low cost for the removal of H2S and NH3 emitted in the treatment of waste water. However, the continuous changes in the load of gases or in the properties of the bed affect the removal of gases. Since the removal is carried out by microorganisms, this work sought to evaluate the relationship between the microbial community present in a pollinatha compost and bagasse of cane biofilter with the removal of H2S and NH3 under transitional conditions that simulate the industrial operation in a Wastewater Treatment Plant. During the process the residence time (60, 45, 33, 25 and 18s) was changed to two concentration levels (52 mg H2S/m3 and 2 mg NH3/m3; 260 mg H2S/m3 and 10 mg NH3/m3 removal) during the process the removal of the bed. As a result of changes in the removal efficiency of gases, soil samples were taken to analyze the microbian community by means of the sequencing of the H2S/m3 and the elimination of the H2S and the products. rmicutes, which is associated with resistance to extreme conditions
