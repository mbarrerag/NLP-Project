# Evaluation of the energy dissipation coefficient R, in steel portico buildings, provided with triangular metallic hysteretic dissipators type TADAS, located in high seismic threat zone.

The search for new technologies that allow to improve the behavior and performance of structures in the face of seismic events has allowed the development of a variety of alternatives, among which are passive dissipation devices of energy. The passive devices type TADAS (Triangular Added Damping and Stiffness) are within the category known as metallic hysteretic dissipators. A limitation in the design of structures with TED type dissipators corresponds to the ignorance of the device's modelling process in the commercial structural analysis programs. This work presents a methodology for the dimensioning and modelling of steel buildings with TED type dissipators, which allows to define the capacity curve of this type of structures, considering the behavior of the dissipation system and the main structure, both in the elastic range, and in the inelastic range.
