# CHALLENGES FOR THE TRAINING OF DISEASES IN PANDEMIA TIMES: EXPERIENCE OF THE FACULTY OF DISEASE IN NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

The pandemic of covid-19 has shown the scarcity of human talent in health, to meet the needs of the population in general. According to the data of the World Health Organization, the number of nurses per thousand inhabitants, is directly related to the income of a country. This situation is worrying, because in countries of lower income the health indicators are unfavourable. Although nursing professionals are urgently required in the first line against covid-19, the programs experienced great challenges and difficulties for the formation of human talent. Describe these challenges and difficulties, as well as the alternatives of solution implemented. The analysis of the experience of the Faculty of Nursing of the National University of Colombia, through the methodology of the logical framework, was carried out a critical analysis of the situation problem and its consequences.
