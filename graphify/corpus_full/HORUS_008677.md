# DEVELOPMENTS OF GENES ASSOCIATED TO THE EARLY EMBROGENSI OF THE PILOSTYLES BOYACENSIS (APODANTHACEAE)

Evolution of genes associated with early embryogenesis of the holoparassitic Pilostyles buoyacensis (Apodanthaceae) Angie D. gonzález1, natalia pabón-mora2, juan f. alzate3,4 & favio gonzález1 1faculty of sciences, universidad nacional de colombia, bogotá, colombia; 2institute of biology, universidad de antioquia, medellín, colombia; 3centro nacional de sequención genomica (cnsg), sede de investigación universitaria (siu), universidad de antioquia, medellín, colombia; 4grupo de parasitology, facultad de medicina, universidad de antioquia, medellín, colombia. The genus Pilostyles is formed by 11 species of holoparásita plants with vegetative growth limited to rows of cells that grow within the stem of their host plants. From these rows of cells, flowers emerge breaking the bark of the host plant. While the development of flowers and fruits occurs in a normal way, the embryos develop up to a state of six to ten cells without further differentiation., PiboRPK2 and PiboPIN11, while late modules of basal apical polarity would be associated with the activity of PiboPTL3, PiboREV, PiboAGO1, PiboAGO10 and PiboRPK2. Genes associated with the establishment of protoderma and apical stem and root meristemas such as ML1/PDF2 of HD-ZIP IV, WOX5 and WUS were not found in the fruit tissue with seeds, in accordance with the incipient differentiation of the embryos of Pilostyles
