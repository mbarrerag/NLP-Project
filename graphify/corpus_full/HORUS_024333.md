# Carbonous materials prepared from palm cousk in CO2 adsorption. Elementary, proximate and morphological characterisation

In this study, porous solids of activated carbon type (AC) were prepared from the chemical activation of the African palm cousk from crops in the Guajira region, Colombia, with Fe(NO3)3 and Cu(NO3)2 solutions with a change in concentration at two different temperatures: 973 and 1073 K. The adsorbents solids prepared were characterized to determine their physicochemical properties and CO2 adsorption capabilities. The obtained ACs present micro-mesoporosity with surface areas between 5 and 1300 m2g-1. The values with respect to the fixed carbon content are between 47.1 and 78.4 %; the results show that the activation process has effects on the textureal parameters, elemental and proximal composition of the obtained solids.
