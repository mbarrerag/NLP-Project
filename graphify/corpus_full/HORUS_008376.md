# BETA AND GAMMA INTERFERON, BUT NOT LAMBDA, 203 PULMONA CELLS INFECTED WITH RESPIRATORIAL SINCITIAL VIRUS

The acute respiratory infection (IRA) is one of the main causes of hospitalization of paediatric patients and most of them are caused by viruses, including respiratory syncitial virus (RSV). These infections generate high mortality and morbidity rates, so that studies of cell-lar, molecular, epidemiological, etc., which are directed to decrease their impact are required. It was proposed to evaluate the expression of Interferons (IFNs) in tumor cells infected with SRV. As a methodology, mues- were taken after nasal hysopates to patients with RAI, were inoculated in Vero cells for the extraction of RNA-total. Subsequently, A549 cells were infected with SRV to a response of 1, at different times p.i. the supernatant and the cellular monolayer was used by TRIzolTM as a result of the removal of RNA-total. By means of absolute quantification, the number of viral particles in the supernatants was determined, as well as the presence of transcribs for IFN I, II and III
