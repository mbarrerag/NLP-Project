# Analysis of financial indicators in the bidding process in Colombia

Abstract State contracting is the mechanism by which public resources are invested in order to meet government objectives focused on collective progress. The primary objective of the bidding processes is to guarantee the participation of stakeholders in the public procurement processes, allowing competition between bidders, provided that it guarantees plurality. In Colombia, the State contracting process is regulated by Law 80-1993, providing rules and principles governing contracts of state entities. One of the conditions for participating in competitive processes is the fulfilment of enabling requirements of financial capacity that should show strength in the organizations succeeding in continuing the selection process. Since each contracting entity has autonomy to draft the requirements presented in the statement, including the value requested for the compliance with financial indicators, an alert is presented on the possibilities of corruption that infringe on the State resources. In effect, the majority of the specifications are complex or clearly directed, posing the research problem and need to analyze the possibility of fulfilling the enabling requirements of financial capacity in tendering processes in 2017 in the segment of land, buildings, structures and roads. This article is developed to show research results from bibliometric review, i.e., analyzing the existing academic publications and the current ones
