# Dates and rights of children and adolescents

The purpose of this chapter is to present and critically assess the normative sources that, within the Colombian legal system, refer to the management and use of personal data of children and adolescents. For this purpose, the first section will present the rules governing the treatment of personal data of children and adolescents in Colombia; the second will be devoted to the jurisprudence that has touched on this subject; the third will deal with the habeas data of children and adolescents in the field of public administration; and the fourth will address the subject matter in universal and regional human rights protection systems. Finally, it is concluded that the handling of personal data of children and adolescents in the different areas where this is appropriate should take into account not only the general rules on their protection and proper management, but also: (1) respecting their best interests, which principle in this point constitutes a limit to the actions of the entities that host and/or administer personal data of that population group; and (2) recognizing that in certain cases it will be possible that authorization for the handling of personal data may be granted to the adolescent itself, in the development of their progressive autonomy and the role of the minors
