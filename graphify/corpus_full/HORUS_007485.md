# Mathematical Fundamentals of Quantum Field Theories

This thesis addresses the mathematical structure of quantum field theories (QFTs), starting from a solid basis in classical field theories. It begins with a review of the fundamental concepts of classical theories, including the equations of EulerLagrange, the differential geometry of field space, and symmetry in classical field theories, including Noether's important theorem and its consequences. From here, it explains how QFTs can be built from classical field theories. It explores the integral of Feynman's paths including its heuristic derivation. Finally, a QFT in zero dimension, a simplified case in which it is possible to define everything formally and which allows to explore general aspects of QFTs: the theory s is introduced to exploit the combinatorial properties of the theory
