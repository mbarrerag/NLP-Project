# NEW DEVELOPMENTS IN THE CONSIDERATION OF THE OSTEOLOGICAL COLLECTION OF THE B-GLOBE OF THE CENTRAL CEMENTERY OF BOGOTA

Since 2015, work has been carried out in the context of historical bioarchaeology on the bone and cultural remains left behind by archaeological excavations carried out in the framework of the construction of the Memory, Peace and Reconciliation Centre in Bogotá, a place that was part of the Central Cemetery and last dwelling of many Bogota residents buried there between 1850 and 1950. Academic events and some publications have presented the research results, carried out on the basis of some indicators of living conditions and on specific sectors of the population. On this occasion, we will present the advances made in the last biennium, which include the application of new methodologies to broaden knowledge of the osteological collection.
