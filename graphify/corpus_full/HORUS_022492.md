# AWARD FOR RESEARCH AND EducaTIVE INNOVATION. 15 YEARS

The 15th anniversary of the Prize for Educational Research and Innovation, awarded by Bogotá to teachers, teachers and directors of the public sector, with the management of the Ministry of Education of the District ¿SED¿ and the Institute for Educational Research and Educational Development ¿IDEP¿, this book presents passages and gives the voice from its writings to a good number of participants in the Prize. The assessment of some of the pedagogical trends and recurrent themes in innovation and pedagogical research of teachers and managers of the city constitutes in itself a recognition of the efforts not only in the scattered work of writing the academic article with which it is involved but above all in the transformative activities that teachers develop together with students and the support of families every day. Beyond having received the awards this book is a tribute to the teachers, teachers and managers participating in the calls for the Prize from 2007 to 2021
