# Stecast matrix roots

The Markov chains of discrete time have been widely used to model processes that evolve with time in various fields, such as credit risk assessment in the financial industry (Higham & Lin [2011]), progress of chronic diseases (Charitos et al. [2008]), labour planning available by periods (Guerry [2014]) and prediction of weather status in airports (Jacquilat [2012]), among others. In these models, estimation of the transition matrix is usually restricted to a certain period of time. However, in many applications it is necessary to know the evolution of the system in temporary scales less than those of the original estimation.
