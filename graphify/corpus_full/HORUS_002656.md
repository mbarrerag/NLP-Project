# Evaluation of the Cost-Equity Impact of the Health System in Colombia 1998-2005

Objective To carry out a study of economic analysis of cost equity, from the perspective of society, to evaluate the impact of Law 100/93 in Colombia, between 1998 and 2005.Methodology Economic analysis study, comparing costs and equity in health in Colombia, between 1998 and 2005. Data were taken from the National Administrative Department of Statistics and from the Demographic and Health Surveys,2000 and 2005. Cost information was taken from the National System of Health Accounts. Health inequality was estimated according to the Health Inequity Indicator (IHI).A trend was made to decrease average eincremental equity cost for three subperiods, as well; 1998-1999, in which there was a decrease in the per capita Gross Domestic Product in Colombia; 2000-2001, in which total health expenditure was decreased, and the period after the two-year period.Resulted A tendency to decrease the IHI was presented, although it was not stable throughout the period. There was an inverse relationship between health inequality and total public health expenditure, and a direct relationship between the expenditure of the two-year period and the in which the second-year-year-influcidence ratio was observed
