# Dialogues with Kaï Komuiya Uai: the solidarity extension of the Digital Archive of Indigenous Languages of the Amazon

The research project proposes a dialogue with the intercultural philosophy-practice of Murui through a study of the successes and failures in forming appropriate intercultural relations between the dance group Kaï Komuiya Uai (word of life) and the National University, Amazonia headquarters through reflections on the extension project Ruakïdo komekï jiyode iemo iyazikï uiñoga (The songs relieve the heart and take care of the territory of life) of 2019 and the recent opening of the Digital Archive of Indigenous Languages of the Amazonia (ARDILIA) with a view to the future formation of intercultural networks. To achieve this objective, a four-month fieldwork is proposed to carry out an ethnographic study of observation-participant, focus groups, and individual interviews. Finally, an academic article on the formation of the intercultural territory of Leticia is expected to be published, and a proposal for a joint extension project is being developed
