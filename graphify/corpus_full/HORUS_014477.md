# Existence of positive solutions for a Semipositone fractional p-Laplacian problem.

In this thesis we will do a brief study of the Fracionary spaces of Sobolev. We will give two equivalent finite spaces of these spaces using interpolation spaces and Fourier transform in the case p = 2. Finally, we will prove the existence of at least one positive solution for the non-local semipositon problem
