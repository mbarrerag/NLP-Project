# Design of Community environmental monitoreous level-level sensors in Community Templar Alert systems before flooding in mountain regions.

In the mountainous regions of Colombia, the design and implementation of low-cost water level sensors in community early warning systems for increasing is a significant advance in disaster risk reduction. Due to the steep topography, unpredictable climate and the increased risk of sudden flooding, these regions are difficult to monitor and forecast using conventional methods.For communities located in these areas, the creation of economic and user-friendly sensors that allow real-time monitoring of river and stream water levels presents a viable proposal for improving the effectiveness of early flood alerts. The design method described in this article focuses on the construction of economic sensors for measuring levels that can withstand adverse climatic conditions without sacrificing accuracy or reliability. Important features include the use of easy-to-achievement materials in the market, low-energy and modular electronic components to facilitate the installation and use of sound-proofing equipment in each case. Sensors are designed to send data wirelessly to central storage and processing points
