# ANDRÉS, COLOMBIA: TRAVEL

This document presents some of the preliminary results of the research on associations and characterization of the variables that are fundamental to explain and understand the conditions of quality of life of the Island of San Andrés with respect to housing, households and individuals, using the National Survey of Quality of Life (ENCV) of the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE). These associations and characterizations were obtained through the multivariate statistical method Multi-Color Analysis (MCA) applied to the ENCV of 2011. The main result was the Factor 1 of the ACM behaves as a type of synthetic indicator of quality of life that separates the sample of the population geometrically in two groups along the horizontal axis of the factorial plane with respect to the centroid, the point (0.0): on the left side is the population with better quality of life conditions, this is those that generally possess the services of internet, sewerage, aqueduct, electrical flow, higher academic level reached by the parents, higher academic level reached by the individual, social security system contributive and special, reached, among others; while on the right side are located the proportion of the population that do not count, to the academic network, under the low level of the academic level of the low level
