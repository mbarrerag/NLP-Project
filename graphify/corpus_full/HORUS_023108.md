# The_paper_currency_in_the_epo_of_your_cryptographic invoice.

The present writing is about a plastic project that links the instalative language as well as that of the body through actions that involve reflection on money/art and its productive character both physical and virtual. It investigates the duality fiction-reality of paper money in its social relationship in terms of its validation. It uses money as its main support in different dimensions (plastic quality of the economic ecosystem): currency and paper coin; faithful and low quality copies of didactic profile money; invoices and receipts; among others. The installation is given from the construction of machines and use of various technologies, as well as from the use of algorithms and digital platforms (machinical ecosystem). The project intervenes, modifies and alters in real time, through machines and actions the plastic support (mounting of invoices and receipts), in this sense, it is addressed to a proposal that is presented in real time or in a process form (the works of art have passed from a materiality of the aural (pictograms) to a secular and deacryralised virtuality (NFT), as well as the economic system in its genalogy took from the trueque to the latter. use of algorithms and digital platforms.
