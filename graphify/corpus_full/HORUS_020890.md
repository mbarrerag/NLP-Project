# Evaluation of physical, structural and functional changes in starch during the application of technological treatments in the process of obtaining a probiotic functional food based on banana flour

The present research project proposes the study and evaluation of changes in the structural and functional physical level of starch, during the formulation and development of a functional food based on banana flour with encapsulated probiotics. The effect of the refractance window drying technology for the encapsulation of probiotic microorganisms in a suspension of banana flour and prebiotics will be analyzed. The following research phases will be proposed for the development of the work: 1) development of a colloidal suspension based on banana, probiotics and prebiotics stable physicochemicalally, 2) experimental optimization of the process of refractance of colloidal lapensiòn window 3) Study by FTIR, SEM, XRD, DSC and TGA of physical and structural changes in starch during applied technological treatment 4) evaluation of the functionality of modified starches in the final product. It is therefore expected to contribute to the knowledge of changes in the structural matrix of amilaceous foods, allowing predicting their behavior during the application of technological treatments, functionality and life of anaquel and to transfer viable knowledge of the sector
