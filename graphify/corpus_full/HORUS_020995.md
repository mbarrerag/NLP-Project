# CHARACTERIZATION OF TUMACO - NARIÑO - COLOMBIA

The objective of this work is to characterize two representative production systems in cocoa production in the municipality of Tumaco. A conventional and other technicalized production system. The methodology to be used consists in analyzing the productive contexts of each system, comparing them to each other to determine differences in management, how these affect the quality of the crops and compare them with the practices they perform in a productive system of another region to know the weaknesses and strengths of the crops in Tumaco, so to be able and generate recommendations to both systems in terms of agronomic management
