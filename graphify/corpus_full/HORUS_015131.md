# ESTIMALITY OF THE VARIABILITY OF THE CONDITIONS OF ACCESSIBILITY LONG THE DAY FOLLOWING THE HORRARY FRANK: THE CASE OF THE CITY OF IBAGUE (COLOMBIA)

This research seeks to compare the conditions of geographical accessibility in motorized modes of transport between two slots (peak hour and valley hour) identifying the relationship between them. According to the moment when the information of speeds is collected on the track it is possible that the conditions of accessibility represent an instant of time or time slot and not the general scenario of the city, that is why in this research a comparison was made that allows to identify the change in these conditions according to the infrastructural offer and the studied time range. The zones of the city were identified in which the accessibility conditions were more favorable during the peak hour than during the valley hour, concluding that at the time of carrying out field measurements the definition of the schedules of these becomes an important parameter that must be taken into account in order not to obtain a biased result. Key words: accessibility; geostatistics; mobility; auto-driven transport; hourly variability; GIS
