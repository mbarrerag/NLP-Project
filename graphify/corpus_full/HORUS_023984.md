# Recognizing other capacities in school: contributions from physical education.

This research arises from the interest and questioning that occurs from the first moment in which the teacher of the area of Physical Education interacts, questions and raises the pedagogical attention for children with disabilities in the classroom of the public and mixed schools of Bogotá D.C. Taking into account that it mainly starts by recognizing, investigating and analyzing the historically marginalized, otreized, violent and oppressed alterities in the school context based on the significant experiences in the teaching profession. This work is posed as a participatory action research with a qualitative approach, because it addresses the experiences and the leisure-pedagogical processes developed in urban and rural public institutions of the different localities of the city of Bogotá D.C. Thus, it proposes strategies of empirical approach based on interviews, field journals, participant observation, interviews and analysis of documents.
