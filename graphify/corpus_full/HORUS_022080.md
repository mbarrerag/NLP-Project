# Natural Factor Based Solvers

We consider parametric families of partial differential equations (PDEs) where the parameter k modifies only the (1,1) block of a saddle point matrix product of a discretization. The main goal is to develop an algorithm that removes some of the dependence of iterative solvers on the parameter k
