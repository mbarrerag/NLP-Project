# Global Vectors and territorial transformations. Proposal of the Territorial Transformation Index (TTI)

Currently territories are interrelated with each other and with global phenomena that transform them in their spatiality, their temporalities or their social relations. Therefore, the article aims to propose a methodology to determine and compare the degree of territorial transformation that a space undergoes with respect to others that belong to a certain territory. The Territorial Transformation Index is proposed and applied to the case of Santa Fe de Antioquia transformed by tourism. This index provides valuable information for the knowledge of the territory, the location of the main effects of external processes and decision-making
