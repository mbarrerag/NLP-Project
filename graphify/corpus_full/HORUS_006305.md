# Effects of social isolation on exploratory behaviour in adolescent rats exposed to a novel environment

Stress and anxiety are currently common risk factors. A population that is particularly susceptible to the effects of stress on emotional response is adolescence. Within this stage of development it has been found that the type of stress that has the greatest conditions is social isolation.
