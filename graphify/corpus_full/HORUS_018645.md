# REGENERATION OF THE CUTAN BODY BY INTENDING NETHERLANDS

Tissue engineering is a science that seeks to develop tissue and organ substitutes to maintain, restore or increase the functions of its injured or sick counterparts in vivo. The skin organ is the tissue that constitutes the largest extension in the human body, and at the same time is the most external organ, which, consequently, makes it the organ most likely to be injured. Due to this problem, research and efforts to solve this problem are constant. The review approach is to present recent methods through which tissue engineering, using biocompatible compounds, has achieved a satisfactory regeneration of the skin organ, this will include a review of the use of tissue regeneration methods, such as 3D printing and the use of polymeric scaffolds, biodesignal dressings and stem cells
