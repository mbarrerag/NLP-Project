# Artificial intelligence and self-learning for the improvement of predictive models: case study with S&P500 index

The realization of precise forecasts is crucial in society and science, because they allow to anticipate trends and understand the impact of variables considered in the future behavior of a phenomenon of interest. This work addresses the challenge of improving the performance of predictive models. To this, a quantitative investigation was carried out with the objective of evaluating the prediction of the price of closing the stock index S&P500 by applying a Transformer model, integrating techniques of self-learning. In accordance with the above, implementation and optimization of the model was carried out, achieving an average percentage error of 1.52% and a maximum of 4.04% in the prediction of the next 60 values of the S&P500. To improve the performance, self-learning based on the distance of Mahalanobis was incorporated. Thus, the performance was improved by reducing the error to 1.22%, which corresponds to a decrease of 19.67% with respect to the value before incorporating the above mentioned method. Compared with traditional methods, the Transformer demonstrated superior accuracy, capturing complex patterns and it was possible to adjust its results automatically through the distance of model of Mahalanobis.
