# STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS OF PERSONAL PROTECTION ELEMENTS FOR THE COVID-19 USING THE MEF

In the light of the contingency presented due to the SARS-CoV-2 virus and the effort to return to the classroom without having high risks of contagion, this project designed a personal protection element (PPE) for children of primary basic education in the municipality of Medellín in order to reduce this risk of contagion. However, many challenges are to be faced, such as comfort and biosecurity, which usually go in contrast. On the other hand, it is very important to ensure structural resistance when the PPE is designed with rigid elements as shown in Figure 1, since children usually develop activities involving sudden and fall movements. In addition, the assembly requires strength to couple the different parts. All this makes it necessary to verify, before mass manufacturing, that the components of the PPE manage to resist the loads to which they may be subjected and thus increase their useful life
