# CHEMICAL FESTIVAL ACS 2023 OF THE BOGOTA CHAPTER

Abstract The Chemistry Festivals have been promoted by the ACS (American Chemical Society) as a way of disseminating chemistry and creating informal teaching spaces outside the classroom. The first version was presented in 2005 in Puerto Rico and from then on they have been disseminated internationally. Thus, the Festivals have been held in the Bogotá Chapter of the ACS since 2017, and for the XIX Colombian Chemistry Congress 2023 a simple version of the 7th Chemistry Festival is presented. This consists of the realization of a series of chemical demonstrations that use very low-risk materials and reagents that impact the public to generate in this significant learning of scientific concepts and promotion of positive attitudes towards science. The presentation of the demonstrations follows a safety protocol to make them safe, threatening and teach chemical concepts. With this, the participants of the Festival increase their motivation towards the learning of chemistry, achieve an adequate understanding of science and an improvement in attitudes towards it.
