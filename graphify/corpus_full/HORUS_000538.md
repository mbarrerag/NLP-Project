# PREDICTIONS OF MALARIA CASES IN THE REGION OF ASHANTI GHANA USED THE NAVIVE BAY MODELS AND NEAREST NEIGHBOURS

Malaria is a disease caused by parasites of the genus Plasmodium, transmitted by the bite of female mosquitoes of the genus Anopheles. In Ghana, this disease represents a significant public health problem, being the main cause of morbidity and responsible for between 40% and 60% of hospitalizations in the country.The aim of this study was to compare the performance of the nearest models of statistical learning K neighbors and Naive Bayes in the prediction of the number of probable cases of malaria.Hamatological variables, age and gender of patients were used, whose data were extracted from the laboratory of St Patrick's Hospital in Ashanti, from January 2018, to June of the same year, from the book of haematological records. For this study data were used 2076 patients.In the evaluation of the models, Naive Bayes obtained an AUC of 80.6% and a precision of 77.9%, highlighting haematological variables such as Hemoglobine (Hb), Plaquets (Plt) and Lymphocytes (Lymph), in addition to the age (Age), as the most relevant within the group analyzed
