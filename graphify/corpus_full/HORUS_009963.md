# Use of the AQUACROP MODEL TO SEARCH PERFORMANCES FOR MAIZ CULTIVE IN COrdOBA, META, TOLIMA AND CAUCA VALLEMENTS

The FAO Water and Soil Division (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations) has developed a model that simulates the yield of arable crops as a response to water, called AquaCrop. This model is the result of multiple research worldwide and experiences of successful adaptation and calibration are available in places with different environmental conditions and crop characteristics
