# Zilber’s notion of logically perfect structure: universal covers

We sketch the mathematical background and the main ideas in the proofs of categoricity of theories of several examples of universal covers — reducing an analytic to a model theoretic (discrete) description. We hope this discussion will be useful to a wide spectrum of mathematicians, ranging from those working in geometry to those working in logic; specifically, model theory
