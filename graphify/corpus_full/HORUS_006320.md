# Care relationships built by mothers and fathers with their children deprived of their liberty. A case study with two families. Bogotá, Colombia 2020-2022

The prisons in Colombia present a devastating panorama for the lives that live there; yes, for the lives, because in the history of each person deprived of freedom there is a network of relations that break, break and change because of the enclosure. Just as there are breaks arise new ways of relating in this hostile world; therefore this research was proposed to know the care relationships of mothers and fathers with their children deprived of their liberty. This required to know these families, the neighborhood of residence, their economic activities, who integrate the family and for their place the places of confinement of their children. In the accounts of the families, two great scenarios were identified in which the care work is found, these are the economic and the emotional ones. This research was carried out as a qualitative investigation through the study of the case, to understand all the elements that converge in these care relationships are the privás and prison. It is to address this institutional and institutional structure, which is based on the essence of individualism and does not contemplate the impact of punishment.
