# INVESTIGATE IN CULTURE MANAGEMENT: TOWARDS A KNOWLEDGE STATE

The article proposes a radical demand of conjugation life-knowledge as an object and research matrix of cultural management. For this purpose, the authors focus their attention on the pre-reflection dimension of experience and defend the formation and permanent transformation of the meanings of existence as a dynamic and fluid result of the full interconnection between feeling, thinking and acting. A dynamic that they propose to address methodologically through the aesthetic dimension of knowledge; that is, by removing the role of threshold that fulfills intuition, imagination and creativity in that poetic listening to the other? that pursues and constitutes cultural management
