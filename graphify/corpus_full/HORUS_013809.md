# Strengthening and modernizing the infrastructure of laboratories with a greater vocation in teaching and articulated with plans to improve curricula

Despite the many efforts made by the University at its different levels (National, Headquarters, Faculty, Department/School), there is still a significant lag in the availability of equipment and laboratories that are able to adequately and timely meet the current needs of teachers and students for the development of academic practices. Currently, a significant number of laboratories still operate in inappropriate facilities, with obsolete equipment and some in poor condition, due to lack of resources for the acquisition/renewal of equipment, and for maintenance and calibration, which puts at risk the quality of training for our students and compliance with the applicable standardity in terms of good laboratory practices, safety and health standards in the workplace and environmental management. For this reason, the present proposal initially seeks to identify and prioritize those laboratories with a broad vocation for teaching that require investment to overcome technical and technological limitations aimed at providing more and better services framed within good laboratory practices, OSH standards and environmental management.
