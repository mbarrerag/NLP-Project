# WAYS IN THE TERRITORY OF THE GREAT CALLS (COLOMBIA): HISTORICITY AND REVISION

The article offers a historical review of the routes and roads in the Gran Caldas. The roads are seen as a reference heritage, cultural and identity, so that historical processes such as the colonization of the Andean Central Range are evidenced. It includes migrations and population, besides the expansion and consolidation of Colombian coffee-growing, among others. Thus, the authors analyze the beginnings and transformations of these routes to consider, in the framework of a process of modernization of the infrastructure, the construction of roads, railways, tunnels and air cables. Likewise, they develop a regional historical approach, with sources, which are subject to bibliographic review, and include official documents, chronicles, cartographies and travel accounts, among others
