# 200 YEARS OF REPUBLIC THE CONSTITUTIONS OF 1821 AND 1991

When the Constitution of Villa del Rosario de Cúcuta was turned 200 years old and the Constitution of 1991, Villegas Editores, on behalf of the presidency of the Republic, published this valuable work. The first magna letter (1821) gave birth to the Great Colombia, the Bolivarian dream of uniting Venezuela, New Granada and Ecuador. Fabio Zambrano narrates from his background to his sanction. Unpublished documents and graphic material illustrate the process. The second, according to Manuel José Cepeda, is the only one that was not made against anyone, and has protected us all, and speaks here of the Seventh Papeleta, the National Constituent Assembly, the debates and agreements for its promulgation, while revealing its different facets, reach and strengths
