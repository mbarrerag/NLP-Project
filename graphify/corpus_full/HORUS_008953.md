# EFFECT OF TOPICAL SEVOLPLURAN IN CHRONIC INSTRUMENTS: METHODOLOGICAL APPROXIMATION

The leg wounds of venous origin has a great impact on the quality of life of the people due to their main effects generated like pain, infection, chronification. In this situation, various strategies have been presented for the therapeutic approach, such as the application of sevoflurane topical in chronic wounds1. Liquid, volatile and colourless anesthesia used as an inhalational anesthetic.
