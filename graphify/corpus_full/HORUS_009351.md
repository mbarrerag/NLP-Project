# APPROXIMATION TO A LABORAL PRE-CHARITY INDEX IN COLOMBIA

The present study was to propose an index to measure the precariousness of jobs in Colombia. Design/methodology: The methodology used was quantitative, given that its purpose was to measure precariousness, taken as a variable. And for each of the eight dimensions of precariousness, a descriptive analysis was made. The research analyzes and weighs those dimensions, as well as the indicators of each of them. For this purpose, a validation was carried out with experts and, subsequently, a survey applied to 365 people, working in sixteen economic sectors. The research showed that there is greater insecurity in the positions where the level of education required to perform it is lower; that persons with indefinite contracts do not receive an equitable wage when compared with the workload; and that, despite the preconceived idea, in many large and medium-sized enterprises there are no symbolic recognition schemes for good performance. The precariousness of work goes beyond material aspects such as salary. The precariousness of work is also present in medium-sized and large enterprises. The precariousness of work hits jobs.
