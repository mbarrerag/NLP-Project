# ASSISTANCE WAY TO THE UNIVERSITY COMMUNITY: INTERACTIONS WITH STUDENTS IN DISABILITY SITUATION

The document is part of the Observatory's plan of action for educational inclusion for persons with disabilities, and presents a route that guides actions for the accompaniment of students with disabilities who are part of the university community
