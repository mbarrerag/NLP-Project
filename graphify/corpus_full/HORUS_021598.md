# Non-static solutions of the Einstein equation in the vacuum

We obtain a numerical solution and a generalized analytical approximation. In the same way, there is a set of families of solutions that are of great interest to researchers. From the solutions, we observe that time in the general theory of gravitation plays an important role in the curvature of space-time
