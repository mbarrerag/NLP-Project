# Study of the degradation of mechanical properties in the concrete of hydroelectric generation structures by exposure to sewage of the Bogotá river

A study focused on the evaluation of the degradation of mechanical properties and durability of concrete in two hydroelectric plants located in the Bogotá river basin: the quieting tank of the El Paraíso plant and the Descargar Canal of the El Charquito plant, exposed continuously to the polluted waters of the river. The main objective was to analyze the impact of the pollutants present in the river water on the resistance and durability of the concrete. To achieve this, tests were carried out, compression, measurement of the modulus of elasticity and microscopic analysis to evaluate the integrity of the concrete at the microstructural level and determine the extent of the damage. The results revealed that the degradation is mainly superficial, with penetration of approximately 3 to 5 mm, caused by a combination of mechanical abrasion, generated by the constant flow of the water and a chemical attack due to the presence of sulfate, chlorides and organic matter in the waters of the Bogotá river. However, the concrete presented a good performance in terms of resistance to compression and modules close to those of design.
