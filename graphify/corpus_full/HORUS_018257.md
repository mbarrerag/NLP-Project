# Cellular theory in the botanical courses of Francisco Bayon in the Universidad Nacional De Colombia (1868-1875)

The founding of the National University of the United States of Colombia in 1867 occurred almost a decade after the publication of Darwin's Origin of Species (1859) and the publication of Virchow's Die Cellular Pathologie (1859), representative texts of the two basic theories that underpin modern biology: the theory of evolution by natural selection and cell theory.Analyzing primary and secondary historical sources we determined that cell theory was taught at the School of Natural Sciences between 1868 and 1875.Francisco Bayón's botany course (1868) explained this theory, including the proposals of Schleiden, Mirbel, Möhl and Unger;as well as the pollination and fertilization processes of plants.The outdatedness in some aspects is explained by the fact that Bayón's main sources are texts published in Spain and France and by the absence of translations of the pioneering works that emerged in Germany.The School of Natural Sciences took a step from natural history to modern biology by including cell theory in its courses
