# D-index as a risk factor for invasive fungal infection in patients with acute lymphoblastic leukemia from a reference hematological center in Bogotá.

Patients with haematological neoplasms are susceptible hosts for the development of invasive fungal infection. Currently, antifungal prophylaxis strategies are available, antifungal treatment schemes and, it is recognized as one of the main risk factors, deep and prolonged neutropenia. Cumulative D-index is a quantitative parameter designed in 2009, which determines the severity of neutropenia, evaluating the cumulative neutrophil deficit   0.001. The cumulative D-index is associated with the development of IFI in patients with acute lymphoblastic leukemia, with an increase in OR as D-index increases, being greater when the D-index value exceeds the value of 4,208
