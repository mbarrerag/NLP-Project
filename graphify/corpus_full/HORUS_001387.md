# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN EDUCATION: REFLECTIONS ON THEIR USE AND EMERGENCING ETHICAL CHALLENGES

This article presents some ethical considerations, as well as some challenges about the impact of the use of artificial intelligence in the education sector. The reflections presented are based on an interpretative ethnographic study. The firm use of this emerging technology allows the empowerment and modernization of teaching and learning processes by personalizing content and academic activities, offering them to students in a flexible and autonomous way and innovating in educational environments. Many advantages of the implementation and use of artificial intelligence in education are, but first a task of awareness-raising aimed at agents participating in educational communities must be done. It is imperative to indicate that there is currently a social schizophrenia product of different publications, mainly in social networks, about advances in artificial intelligence and its impact on the different dimensions of human. These disseminations have generated a series of myths, noises, disinformation and errors that are preventing a more assertive use in classrooms
