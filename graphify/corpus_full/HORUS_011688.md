# EXPLORES OF THE UNIVERSE

Astronomy is one of the most exciting sciences that leads us to ask existential questions such as: Where do we come from and where are we going? Is there life in other planets? How did stars and galaxies form? The challenge is how to communicate these concepts to children and young people in order for them to learn in an entertaining way in a context of digital transformation that has led to multiplatform teaching and tele-education in the pandemic
