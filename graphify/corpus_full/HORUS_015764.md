# GEOSPACE ANALYSIS OF COVID-19 IN COLOMBIA: DEPRIPTIVE STUDY MEASUREMENTING THE USE OF CARTOGRAMS

cartograms, epidemiology, covid-19. The SARS-CoV-2 virus causing the disease by COVID-19 was discovered in December 2019 and in March 2020 was classified as a pandemic by the World Health Organization (WHO) [1]. This research aims to analyze the frequency and geographical distribution of the spread of COVID-19 in Colombia in the corresponding period from March 2020 to October 2021 implementing the use of cartograms as a tool of geospatial analysis for epidemiological surveillance. The present research has a quantitative approach, with design of descriptive cross-sectional study. The data of the number of daily cases of COVID-19 and the data of the population estimation of the years 2020 and 2021, for each department of Colombia. The data were obtained from the National Institute of Health (INS) [2] and the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE) [2] were subsequently incorporated.
