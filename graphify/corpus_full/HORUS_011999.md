# The urban environmental conflict in the municipal capital of Vijes, department of Valle del Cauca.

A large part of the municipalities of Colombia, present deficiencies in environmental management instruments, which allow them to identify urban environmental conflicts in a short time and at low costs. In this document, by means of an analysis between the diagnosis of the municipality, together with the environmental indicators (a little known topic in our country) divided into components, and the management instruments, the urban environmental conflicts of the municipal head of Vijes were determined. In the case of the municipal head of Vijes, it was initially found that the history together with the culture of Vijes, has a radical influence on environmental pollution mainly in the air, and that its geographical location along with the development process that the municipality has been presenting, does not favor the economic development of Vijes excepting the process of limestone.
