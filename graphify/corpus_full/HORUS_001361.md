# “Let’s do a festival!”: Sociocultural analysis of the Festival of “peasant music” of the National Radio of Colombia

This research seeks to understand the uses and tensions of music from the place of cultural policies that is concrete from the phenomenon of the festivalization of culture and music. To account for this, as axis of the research were analyzed the two editions of the Festival of Peasant Music carried out by Radio Nacional de Colombia, during the years 2020 and 2021, in the territories PDET, through the Emitoras for Covivence and Reconciliation. The appearance of the festival shows how, in practice, the policies promoted by the State to administer culture in the territories violent before, is realized through the scenarios that, like the festival, promote the sample and rescue of the local artists. Therefore, this work is concentrated on the work of peasant musicians participating in the festival, to build from their accounts an approximation to the social function of music in the regions PDET. Thus, the research also seeks to deepen the debate on the (re)configuration of the "peaspheral identities", from the musical practices and experiences of the peasant artists and musicians participating in the festival. This discussion puts in the center of the use of music and the peace to the how the policies promoted by the state to manage culture in the formerly violent territories are materialized in practice through scenarios that, like the festival, promote the exhibition and rescue of local artists. Therefore, this work focuses on the music of the pedestrian musicians who participated in the festival, in order to build from their stories an approach to the social function of music in the PDET regions. Thus, the research also sees to discuss the debate on the (re)configuration of “peasant identities”, based on the musical practices and experiences of Palestinian artists and musicians participating in the festival.
