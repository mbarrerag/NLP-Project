# The interpretation of the growth mentality and its impact on pedagogical practice: perspectives of humanities teachers in the Dominican Republic School of Bogotá

The need to develop the capacities for students to meet personal aspirations and social expectations have generated analysis exercises that have identified gaps in skills necessary to develop in the different areas of daily life. Recent research in the growth mentality have demonstrated positive impacts by the look of challenges and obstacles as learning opportunities and presents persistence and effort as the way to achieve the goals. (Dweck, 2006). However, there is still a need to explore the topic with the education professionals. Therefore, this research-action focuses on identifying from the teachers' voices, the interpretation of the growth mentality in their pedagogical practice. This analysis also results in the characterization of the participants in this mentality and explores the effects on their interactions from intervention. The qualitative proposal incorporated spaces of theoretical and practical review, to deepen the principles of the growth mentality, as well as the material of exercise, reflection and contrast. The guiding questions in the manner of insect, Likert type and focal group led to socialization and contributions. The participatory approach showed a process of internalization of the theory in the teachers and the teachers.
