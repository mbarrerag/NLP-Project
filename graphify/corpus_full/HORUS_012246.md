# Diagnosis of incipient failures in rolling element beings under nonstationary operation conditions by vibration analysis.

Bearings are a fundamental part of most rotary machines, consequently, fault detection and early diagnosis of these elements are of great interest to increase reliability and maintenance levels of rotary equipment. Vibration analysis is the most used tool for bearing diagnosis. Despite advances in algorithms and digital signal processing for diagnosis, it is common to find great advances for cases in which operating conditions are maintained stationary. This is not the case of many rotary equipment, where angular speed can be variable over time. In general, operation under variable speed conditions generates vibrational responses in which angle-periodic phenomena are combined with time-periodic phenomena. This work presents a method for diagnosing faults in bearings, using algorithms of “Computer Order Tracking”, analysis of Cycle-No-Staticity and analysis of Spectral Kurtosis. The method was evaluated using a series of signals captured under variable speed conditions and a series of simulated signals, under different load and speed conditions. Additionally, a strategy is presented for the combination of signals, and faults between them
