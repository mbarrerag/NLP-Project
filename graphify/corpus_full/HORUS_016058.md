# ANALYSIS FROM THE AGROECHOLOGY OF ANCESTROL PRACTICES IN THE REGUARD OF YAQUIVA (INZA, CAUCA)

Within Colombian ethnographic literature, there are no records that systematize ancestral practices; therefore, the objective was to analyze from agroecology the ancestral practices, with emphasis on those threatened to disappear, as a contribution to the socio-agricultural re-signification in the Yaquiveña community. The research methodology used was based on Participatory Action Research (IAP); the reason for the loss of traditional practices and some alternatives for recovery is systematized; the practices that are being lost and those that are still being carried out. The results obtained in this research indicate that the Yaquiveña educational community is aware of the threats and risks that hang over its culture and that the emphasis of its responses should be on trying to reverse this type of conventional agriculture
