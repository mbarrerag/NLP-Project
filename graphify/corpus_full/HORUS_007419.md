# 13 VERIFICATION REPORT ON THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE FINAL PEACE AGREEMENT

In this report, the Technical Secretariat sought to identify the progress and obstacles to implementation during the period January to December 2023 and presents opportunities for improvement to promote faster compliance with the provisions that present delays or face such obstacles. On this occasion, the ST emphasized the analysis of the design and implementation of public policies of the administration of the National Government during the presidency of Gustavo Petro (2022-2026) considering that, since the beginning of his term of office, the President of the Republic has expressed the commitment of his administration to the fulfilment and the integral implementation of the Final Agreement for the Construction of a Stable and Lasting Peace
