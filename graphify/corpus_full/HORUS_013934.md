# -Cocao chocolate (Theobroma cacao) with partial replacement of cocoa butter with Copoazú fat (Theobroma grandi?64258;orum)

-This study seeks to adapt substitutes for cocoa butter, to achieve a chocolate without modifying the structure, for this purpose it must evaluate a fatty substitute from the copoazú, adaptable to chocolate and with a similarity to those of cocoa butter, which is the cause of the final structure of the chocolate; the different physical and chemical characteristics obtained must be taken into account for obtaining chocolate coverages, I know to undergo rigorous testing processes, the different trials of cocoa butter blend with Copoazú fats (Theobroma grandiflorum) that adapt to the necessity of obtaining chocolate, also, of acceptability to consumers. Technological surveillance provided quality information to establish the state of the art, which allows to select the extraction methods, methodological tools of physical and chemical tests and sensory, to evaluate the characteristics of the raw material and of the prototypes to develop, from the different mixtures, established in the experimental design.
