# Determination of optimal bandwidth and thickness of perovskita materials (PVK) for solar tandem cells of 2 terminals of the PVK/Si and PVK/CuInSe2 type

The research on solar cells tandem that include perovskita materials has undergone a remarkable increase due to its high efficiency potential, as the prohibited bandwidth of the perovskita materials can be adjusted in a wide range. In this work, we focus on modeling tandem cells using a theoretical model that can be applied to tandem cells with multiple joints.
