# Habitat production in San Pedro de la Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, a space of permanent conflict: understanding from collective memory

As a member of the community, this research was born with the intention of revealing the characteristics of the occupation and settlement process in San Pedro de la Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, through the collective memory of its inhabitants. A model of population with specific characteristics, where self-management and community organization continue to play a transversal role in the forms of relation with the territory. This study of the micro-scale habitat, sets out details of the ways of inhabiting in the countryside, remote places of the country, where various actors of the Colombian armed conflict have had an impact; telling from the voice of the family and community memory how the construction of the territory, the bets and fears as to the permanence in the space have been; recognizing as an object of study places in invisible appearance, in a bid against the concealment of its historical dynamics, and as a contribution from the personal experience that adds to the projections and representations of the young people and inhabitants of the place. .
