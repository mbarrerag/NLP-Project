# ACADEMIA AND PUBLIC POLICY: EXPERIENCES OF INCIDENCE FROM NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

Since the beginning of the formation of the field of public policy studies, the production of relevant knowledge to improve the effectiveness of government action has been prominent. Thus, the Lasswell (1951) call to academia and social sciences to develop a Policy Sciences that produces knowledge, both on problems and on policies, that would improve public decisions, was well received
