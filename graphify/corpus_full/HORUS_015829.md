# OBTAINATION OF BIOCERAMIC RECOVERY WITH GRAPHEN NANOPARTICULALS FOR POTENTIAL USE IN REPARATION AND REGENERATION

This work exposes the realization of bioceramic coatings with graphene, graphene- CaO-P2O5-SiO2-TiO2, on Ti6AL4V, using electrolytic plasma oxidation technique, with thermal treatment and intense discharge plasma of ionizing gas. The coatings play a very important role in the level of biocompatibility and bioactivity that will have the biomaterials when they are in contact with biological tissues, such as bone tissue. Electrolytic plasma oxidation (PEO) or micro-arch oxidation (MAO), is a method of coating synthesis, which uses continuous current electrolysis, with high voltages, which then passes to alternating current.
