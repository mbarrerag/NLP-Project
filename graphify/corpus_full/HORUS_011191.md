# DIDACTICAL CHANGES IN A UNIVERSIT PROFESSOR OF ORGANOMETALIC CHEMICAL: CHARACTERIZATION AND PERSPECTIVES

The following article is the result of the work carried out within the Master's Degree in Education of the Francisco José de Caldas District University. It presents the characterisation of the didactic changes in the practice of a university professor of organometallic chemistry, mediated by the Didactic Knowledge of the Content and Nature of Sciences. To this end, a multiparadigmatic (Interpretative-Sociocritical) and plurimethodological approach was used, so that it was possible to understand the practice of the teacher in depth. This was in order to promote educational changes, voluntary and gradual, at different levels (conceptual, attitudinal, procedural). The intervention carried out allowed the teacher, among other things, to transform his conceptions on the educational fact and to propose new strategies of explanation for the content of the subject. However, there are still resistances to the relation of the Nature of Sciences with his teaching, an issue that is in agreement with previous studies
