# From the Pacific we come. Migration of Tumache and barbecue families to Mocoa - Putumayo

"De Pacífico we come" is a research that has as objective, to analyze the migratory processes and territorial configuration of the black families coming from the municipalities of Tumaco and Barbacoas (Nariño) in the inspection of Puerto Limón – Putumayo. To achieve this purpose the epistemological position that guides the investigation was the constructionism from this, this document was structured in three chapters: The first presents conceptual aspects and. and a state of art about the black family in Latin America from which I show a position on the concept of black family that will be transversal in the development of the research.The second chapter shows the results of the field; migratory accounts of the black population of the South Pacific nation towards the Putumayo (Puerto Limón), the importance of the networks in the migratory processes and in the regionality of life: marriage, education and work.
