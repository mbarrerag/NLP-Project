# DETERMINATION OF THE CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF THE ESSENTIAL OIL OF LEONURUS JAPONICUS RECOLECTED IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The AE extraction yield was 0.03 % by weight. The AE characterization was performed by means of gas chromatography coupled to mass spectrometry. Two columns with different polar (DB-WAX) and non-polar (DB-5MS) stationary phases were used. The components were identified by comparison of their mass spectra (EI, 70 eV) with those reported in spectral databases (ADAMS, NIST, Willey), and by comparison of their linear retention rates with those reported in the literature (2). The main components found in the AE of Leonurus japonicus were trans-ß-cariofilene (14.2%), cariofilene oxide (13.4%), a-humulene (11.8%) and a-pinene (10.7%). The chemical composition found in the AE of Santander plants, Colombia, differed partially, with that obtained by Xiong and collaborators (3), where they found as main phytone compounds (19%), phytol (13.8%), karyfilene oxide (11.5%) and trans-ß-caryfilene (9.9%)
