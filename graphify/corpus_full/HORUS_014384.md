# Prevalence and clinical characteristics of respiratory infection in patients with cancer and febrile neutropenia. Pilot study.

Title: Prevalence and clinical characteristics of respiratory infection in pediatric patients with cancer and febrile neutropenia. Febril neutropenia (NF) is the most frequent and serious complication related to treatment in children with cancer, the introduction of molecular diagnostic tests for the identification of respiratory viral infection guides the early approach of these patients, guiding the evaluation, extension studies and the duration of antibiotics. The aim of this study is to characterize respiratory viral infection in patients with febrile neutropenia. An analytical study of prospective cohort in patients between 0-18 years with febrile neutropenia diagnosis. A molecular study with multiple respiratory PCR was performed in patients with febrile neutropenia. Clinical and paraclinic characteristics of each patient were determined. Later analysis of correlation by the Chi square or exact patient's test. The majority of the stay variables, neutrophil counts and result of the viral panel were evaluated by means of the Wilcoxon test for two samples. The linear regression model was performed for the hospital stay time variable lNF. 61 covering, extension studies, and hospital stay, These findings need to be confirmed in a longer time of observation to have more data on the variables evaluated in the study
