# NEURO-FARMACOLOGIC PROFILE OF NEW TIAZEPINIC AND TIAZOLIDIC COMPOUNDS DESIGNED BY VIRTUAL CRITICALLY DESIGNED

This research sought to evaluate the pharmacological profile on central nervous system of the thiazepinic and thiazolidine compounds cpTP-0, cpTP-1, TAP-2 and cpTAP-2, selected from virtual screening against the benzodiazepine binding pocket of the GABAA receptor, in murine models of anxiety, depression, hypnosis-sedation and convulsion; additionally, the possible acute neurotoxicity of the compounds on the central nervous system was evaluated by means of the Irwin test and the rolling axis test.In the initial phase of screening the compounds were evaluated at 100 mg/kg dose in the cross-nadated, forced suspension by the tail, boosted by barbituric sleep and induction of seizures with pentilentetrazole, using male and female CD-1-balanced mice (n=4/sexposed), where antidepressant activity of the cpTAP-2 compound was detected in male mice.
