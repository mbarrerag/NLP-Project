# Method for the development of applications in industry-based 4.0 robotic services and cloud computing.

In this work, the development of a methodology for the development of applications in robotics of services based on industry 4.0 and cloud computing was carried out. The first part of the work consists in characterizing the industry 4.0 elements applicable to robotics of services for interiors. Once defined, the models of cloud computing applicable to robotics of services were identified, and then artificial intelligence techniques were incorporated for the control of the navigation of mobile robots in interior environments. Then the elements of industry 4.0 and cloud computing were integrated, for autonomous navigation of a robotic system. With this, a methodology was constructed, adding elements such as sensoric, locomotion, manipulation, web development, data sources, cloud development environments, simulation, control architectures, and visual fiducial markers. Finally, the proposed method was validated through the realization of a set of tests in a logistic scenario, generating results and conclusions
