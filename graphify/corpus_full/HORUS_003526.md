# Allelopethic potential of Artemisia absinthium L. on seed germination and seedling growth of several plant species

In plant-plant interactions, the emission of secondary metabolites may have significant effects, which are potential tools for the management of weeds, so the study of these substances of plant origin offers an environmental alternative to traditional production processes. The research aimed to evaluate the allelopetic potential of the aqueous extract of Artemisia absinthium L. on seed germination of five species: Calendula officinalis L., Taraxacum officinale L., Bidens pilosa L., Senecio vulgaris L. and Brassica juncea L. The experiment had a factorial design with five species, five extract concentrations and five repetitions, for a total of 125 experimental units (EU). The aqueous extract of A. absinthium was obtained from dry foliage or aggregated concentration of 1/50 (p/v), and was applied in varying proportions (0, 25, 50, 75 and 100%) according to the respective treatments.
