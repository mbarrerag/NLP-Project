# Design of a didactic tool with video game format for historical teaching of the emergence of modern science

In Colombia, particularly in the department of Guaviare, the educational system is affected by the standardized approach of national and global education. The state rejects in its educational guidelines the context of the most remote regions, and as has been tradition, a centralistic vision of development prevails. This leads to the students of Guaviare taking classes in practice on contents completely disconnected from their interests and their experiences, which is reflected in the low school performance they present, specifically for this case, in the area of sciences. This work aims to design a didactic tool in the video game format, which shows us the role that science had in history, whose aim is to improve the interest of students, correct distorted visions of the history of science and improve learning in a meaningful way; as well as to address the inclusion of technology in science education. This work seeks to enrich the possibilities existing in terms of educational software that enable them to use technologies frequently and appropriately within schools, as well as to analyze how the same contributes to the learning process of the sciences. .
