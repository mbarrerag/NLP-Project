# School Religious Education in Bogotá: Case Study on the experiences of teachers in the town of Los Mártires

Within the framework of several reflections developed from the study of school religious education (ERE), this qualitative exploratory research was aimed at knowing the relationship between the professional and personal trajectories of school teachers and the criteria and methodologies used for the teaching of religion in public schools of the town of Los Mártires in Bogotá. By means of the conduct of semistructured interviews, aimed at five teachers in charge of the chair of religion in official schools of this locality, under a focus of life history and selection of curricular contents and the legislation of school religious education (ERE) in Colombia, it was concluded that being a teacher of religion is an opportunity not for the indoctrination of religion, but for the training in ethics and values and, at the same time, a challenge to the judgments and normative requirements to be able to exercise this role.
