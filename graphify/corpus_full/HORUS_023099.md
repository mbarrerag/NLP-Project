# ATMOSFERICAL GNSS APPLICATIONS IN COLOMBIA

GNSS technology provides a very specific atmospheric application: the estimation, with very high temporal resolution, of the water vapour column in the atmosphere on a receiving station. The basic principles on which this technology is based are presented here in a very compact way, which has great advantages compared to other methods of measuring water vapour. In particular its high temporal resolution, the fact that it works under any atmospheric condition, and its low cost. In Colombia there is already a relatively dense network of GNSS stations (~100 stations), but it is not yet used for atmospheric applications operationally. Through a case of extreme rain occurring in the Sabana de Bogotá, this article illustrates the great potential that this technology has for research into tropical atmospheric convection
