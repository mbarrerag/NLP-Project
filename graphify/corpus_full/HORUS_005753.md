# RESPENSE THE ROLE OF PARENTS IN SOCIOMORAL DEVELOPMENT: CHALLENGES AND OPPORTUNITIES

This article presents a review of the role of parents in the sociomoral development of children from different theoretical perspectives. It emphasizes the role of parents as attachment figures and in the opportunities for parental scaffolding that arise from conflicts that are handled in the family context, opportunities that relate not only to the development of moral concepts, but also to the construction of the sense of moral agency. It concludes by presenting challenges for research in our contexts and for strengthening processes and policies that support parentality
