# Analysis of the coexistence model between the miners of La María and the company Gramalote Colombia, its critical factors and sustainability

The agreements of coexistence are proposed as a possible solution to the conflict between the Mining and Small Scale (MAPE) and the Mining to Large Scale (MGE) due to the exploitation of mineral resources, where the first defends its tradition and means of life and the second, its right to exploit the mining title. The coexistence also proposes as an alternative to address the problems around the MAPE. However, doubts remain about the effectiveness, temporality and sustainability of the measures. In recent years, the technical literature has begun to point out the existence of social and technical factors that influence the success or failure of these processes. In this context, this research studies the model of coexistence between the Association of miners La María and the company Gramalote Colombia, in order to evaluate the factors that govern it and document this experience with an academic approach. For its analysis, the factors used using three criteria: literary review, interviews to key actors and criteria of professionals in MAPE. In addition, two factors were identified as emerging.
