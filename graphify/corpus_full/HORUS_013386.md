# Theoretical and computational study of the formation of thin nitride and metal carbide films

Through simulations implemented under the Montecarlo Cinético (KMC) method, a multiscale model is generated to describe the growth of thin films of CrN, ZrN, Cr and W. To determine the role of the processes of adsorption, diffusion, bonding energies, in bulk, cluster and surface of the aforementioned elements, on the evolution of the texture of thin films under experimental parameters. The growth simulation is based on rigid structures type FCC monocrystalline. Variations in the temperature and availability of the elements within the process of adsorption presented graphically. In turn, numerical calculations based on the Functional Theory of Electronic Density (DFT) will be used to obtain the energy and structural parameters required by the KMC model. The growth of the present morphology dependent on time, is analyzed with the parameters of roughness (RMS) and particle density per layer. In this way, a detailed understanding of the atomistic processes is sought to control the evolution of the texture of the above mentioned materials is achieved. required by the KMC model.
