# PHYSICOCHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF PD(II) COMPLEX WITH 1,3-DIPHENYLPYRAZOLE-DERIVED THIOSEMICARBAZONES. HTTPS://SILQCOM08.NAVUS.IO/PRESENTATION/86059

In this work, Pd(II) complex was obteined with 1,3-diphenylpyrazole-4-carboxaldehyde derived thiosemicarbazones. 1,3-diphenylpyrazole-4-carboxaldehyde ligand (L) was obtained by reacting 1,3-diphenylpyrazole-4-carboxaldehyde with 4-ethylthiosemicarbazone in a 1:1 M/L ratio, according to the procedure described in the literature (1). The Pd(II) complex was synthesized by reaction in ethanoic solution of L with PdCl2 in a 2:1 M/L ratio
