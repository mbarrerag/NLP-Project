# ASSISTANCE OF THE PRE-VOLULAR FOLICLE AND ITS RELATIONSHIP TO THE SIZE OF THE LONE BODY AND PROgestERONE PRODUCTION IN ROMOSINUA (RS) AND Brahman (BR)

In order to compare in the pre-ovulatory follicles of bovine females of the Brahman (Br) and Romosinuane (rs) breeds, the development of the different follicular components, such as the size of the follicular antrum, the thickness and volume occupied by the granular cell layer (cg) and internal teak (ti), and their possible relationship with the development of the luteal body (cl) and progesterone production P4, 5 females of the rs and 5 females of the breed Br were used. These were monitored for 2 ostral cycles and were sampled for the blood serum to determine P4 levels by Elisa. In the third estrum, the ovular morphometry containing the pre-ovulatory follicle was removed and a diametrical cut was performed for the females of the breed Br, hcyl, hc, and in 2 equal parts. In one of the parts, the histological cuts were made and the follicular morphometry was determined. rs and Br. The findings of this study can undoubtedly serve as a basis for future analyses in cattle resident in the tropics
