# DOCUMENT: A PIONER DEPARTMENT IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE HISTORY DISCIPLIN IN COLOMBIA

This research proposal aims to arouse the public interest with a visual, chronological and didactic narrative, where in addition to the historical contextualization, the intellectual and research work of the institution was exhibited as it has been concerned to perpetuate historiographically the value of the events that have contributed to the development of the country. This research will have the audiovisual collection in audio and video format of the interviews developed by the project Authors of Stories, a university initiative led by Professor César Augusto Ayala Diago with the collaboration of the undergraduate students in History and the Master's in History. The project has documented the academic, bibliographical, social, intellectual and each of the fields in which this discipline intervenes, told from the experiences of the professors of the Department of History. Likewise, it seeks to support the narrative with an archive of images and videos relevant for each chronological period addressed. This in order to locate the viewer in the historical contexts handled throughout this discipline, it has been carried out by the Spanish Department of the Spanish University of Colombia. The purpose is to make them aware of the intellectual, human, academic and bibliographical richness of the Department of History. This also seeks to ensure that the country recognizes the relevance of historical and historiographic knowledge to take it into account when it comes to expressing its opinion and charting both the present and the future of the country
