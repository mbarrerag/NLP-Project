# HYDRICAL EXTRACTIVISM BY CERREJON IN THE GUAYIRA A COLONIAL CONTINUITY

This research analyzes how the water extractivism of El Cerrejón in La Guajira corresponds to a colonial continuity. Methodologically, an investigation was carried out based on bibliographic review. In this work three forms of water extractivism were identified. In addition, seven relevant global hierarchies were identified. For the affected communities, this rooting in the colonial context means that they are affected not only by the effects of the mine, but also by global colonial continuities that are still perpetuated
