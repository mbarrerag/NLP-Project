# Casa Hija: Research, documentation, and knowledge management of Amazon languages and cultures

This proposal is articulated around the strategic areas of knowledge management that contribute to solving problems identified in the research seedbed _x0093_Casa Hija: Circles of the word_x0094_ of the Amazonian Headquarters on the languages and cultures of the people of the center, a cultural group that covers eight languages. First support the doctoral training, starting from a project on the history of the period of the cauterusian and the social reconstruction of the peoples of the center. The project, to the second degree, seeks to recover the oral memory of these peoples, starting from a project of documentation that we have called _x0093_Archivo Digital of the Indigenous Languages of the Amazon_x0094_. And in the area of extension and innovation, we promote the creation of a municipal university of Amazonian and the realization of an international event in the Amazonian headquarters of the National University. The project seeks to articulate the strategic areas of knowledge management of the languages and cultures of the center people, through three specific objectives: 1) Study the consequences of the contact between the people of Central and the western context, which took place in the Amazonian University and the definitive. Collections of audiovisual recordings of the Murui and Miranha peoples. For the third objective, a postgraduate course will be created, administered by the first indigenous PhD of our program, which will be offered at the Casa Hija (Maloca) of the headquarters and which will start an Amazonian Chair. In addition, an event will be convened on self-managed digital archives within the framework of the XIV Conference of the Society for the Anthropology of the Lowlands of South America in July 2023. The group of students and teachers who make up this proposal is closely linked based on their experience of constant dialogue in the circles of the word of the Casa Hija, whose activities come from years ago, and Professor Juan Alvaro Echeverri, director of the proposal, is the director of the the the the thesis of the three students involved. The expected results of the project are a research article submitted to an indexed magazine, an advance of the doctoral thesis of a graduate student in Amazonian studies of the National University of Colombia, the creation of a digital archive of languages, the creation of a postgraduate course, and the realization of an international academic event
