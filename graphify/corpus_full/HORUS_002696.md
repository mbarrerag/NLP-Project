# POROUS GEOPOLIMERS TO PUT ON CARBON VOLUNTING CENIZES: VALUATION OF WASTES.

The use of coal fly ash (CVC) as a precursor for the synthesis of geopolymers is framed in the incorporation of industrial solid waste in reuse processes and generation of materials with added value. CVCs, rich in aluminosilicates, are activated alkalinely to obtain geopolymers, which in turn are subjected to a cation exchange to achieve a high surface area. The synthesis of geopolymers followed the methodology of Hlavacek [3], using CVC as the sole source of aluminosilicates. Formulations were evaluated replacing partially CVC with silica and ¿alumimin in proportions of 10, 30, 50 and 75% by weight. The materials were characterized by physiosorting of N2 to -196°C, FTIR and DRX [1]. The CVC-separative material, 1:3, showed the highest BBET, reaching 19.80 m2/g. After a thermal treatment between 350°C and 500°C, it was observed that, at 400°C, the SBET increased to 138 m2/g.
