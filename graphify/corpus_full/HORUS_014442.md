# Lagrangian submanifolds under special conditions of degeneracy of symplectic structures.

The aim of this project is to study a version of the Lagrangian subvariities in the folded-simplexic and b-simplexic structures. We will begin by studying how we can give a definition of isotropic, coisotropic and Lagrangian subvariities in these structures that is consistent with the definition of the simplistic case, after that, from a variety, we will construct canonical examples of a Lagrangian subvariety in the folded-simplexic and b-simplextic case. Finally, we will make a version of the Lagrangian neighborhood theorem in these structures
