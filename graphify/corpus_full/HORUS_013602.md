# FISSIOLOGICAL INDEXS TO SIETE FORRAJER SPECIES IN DIFFERENT TROPICAL AMBIENT

The aim was to evaluate the physiological behavior of seven tropical fodder species in different environmental conditions during 2019 in Palmira, Colombia. A random complete block design was implemented in three locations with different altitudes, with four repetitions per species for a total of 28 experimental units per locality. A portable photosynthetic measurement analyzer Lcpro+ was used for the collection of data on photosynthesis rate, stomatic behavior, perspiration rate and internal CO2. The data collected were analyzed by means of a comparison of means with Duncan test (p= 0.05).
