# Research analytical and experimental connections mixed in columns of steel rods threaded pre-installed and concrete beams of normal strength to areas of seismic hazard high -

This research proposes a new mixed or hybrid connection to join H-shaped structural steel columns and reinforced concrete beams with two different grades of steel as extreme tension reinforcement in a seismic resistance system of moment-resisting frames. To assess the adequacy of the connection, three test specimens were constructed and subjected to a history of cyclic displacement. Two of these specimens were adjusted with continuity plates anchored to concrete beams and the contribution of these plates to the integrity of their joints was evidenced. The hysteretic curves obtained were characterized and undergone to the validation criteria for beam-column connections of title F of the NSR-10. The specimens passed the validation criteria, however, the specimen without continuity plates showed damage around the joint, which is unacceptable in moment-resisting frames. Finally, it was concluded that the proposed hybrid connection configured with continuity plates anchored to concrete beams, which provide integrity to the joint, is suitable for use in seismic resistance systems of frames
