# Syncope and hypotension as an adverse effect of latanoprost

Abstract Present the case of a patient with episodes of syncope and hypotension secondary to the use of latanoprost. 32-year-old male with a dismeasured cup/disk ratio, normal visual fields, an anomaly in the optic nerve according to optical coherence and pachymetry tomography of 582/563 <956;m. A better corrected visual acuity eye examination of 20/20 in both eyes, intraocular pressure (IOP) 13/13 mmHg in both eyes and cup/disc ratio of 0.7/0.7. A hydro-overloading test was performed, with a maximum IOP of 22 mmHg in the right eye and 21 mmHg in the left eye. Latanoprost was prescribed in both eyes. Subsequently, he had three episodes of syncope with arterial hypotension requiring hospital care. No structural or functional cardiovascular lesions were observed, with a related PIO of 22 mmHg in the right eye and 21 mmHg in the left eye.
