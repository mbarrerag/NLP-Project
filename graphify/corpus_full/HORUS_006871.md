# Analysis of programmatic elements in the piano works of Liszt and Scriabin as a basis for the development of one's internal narrative in interpretation

The elaboration of an internal narrative as a guide to interpretation is a resource used by some musicians to enrich their version of a work. In this research work, beyond merely exposing the internal narrative developed by the author for the interpretation of specific works (La Sonata en Si Menor S. 178 by franz liszt and sonata n°5 op. 53 by Alexander Scriabin), it is deepened in the process of construction of this one. It starts from a contextual and thematic analysis of the works, and is built on a program founded on a literary referent. The narrative elaborated is, moreover, supported by a theoretical framework, in which the nature of the programmatic music is investigated in detail, the methods of analysis used for the elaboration of the narrative, the possibilities of connection between the literary language and the musical language, and how some categories of the semantic language can be applied to the musical analysis (by the music for the first instance). categories of language semantics can be applied to musical analysis to facilitate the construction of one's internal narrative
