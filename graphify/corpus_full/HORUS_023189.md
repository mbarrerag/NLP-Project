# BULLATURE L. CONCATENATURE BETWEEN STRUCTURE AND MORPHOLOGY IN FOUR NON-MAILIZED NIEMEYER PROJECTS,

In order to illustrate some of the substantial values of the unimplemented projects, several designs of the Carioca master have been reviewed and analyzed and some have been selected to illustrate the quality of these experiments in relation to the development of the idea of harmonious concatenation between spatial morphology and structural typology. These projects have a strong originality component, in which support elements are minimized and non-conventional structural systems such as arches, shells and tensioners are applied
