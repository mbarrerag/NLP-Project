# Political parties and party systems. An analysis of their behavior in the cities of Medellín and Bogotá (1991-2019)

This thesis focuses on understanding the behavior of political parties, party systems and politicians of the cities of Medellín and Bogotá in the last three decades. To this end, it is divided into five chapters, each of which deals with a different but complementary aspect of the research problem.The first chapter puts to the finishing point the theoretical tools (concepts, categories and approaches), and specifies because it chooses priority for one of the approaches, the institutionalist, as the most appropriate look to read the political changes that the two cities mentioned have experienced in the period that the research comprises.The two and three chapters account for the electoral behavior of the political parties both in the dispute for the municipal councils of each city, and in the electoral competition for occupying the executive power.The four chapter deals with the transformations of the local party systems and the interference that on these changes have the electoral rules.And the fifth and last chapter focuses its analysis on the impact of the discursive strategies of four politicians who, at different times and partly with different resources and formats, entered successfully into the dispute for the political changes of Medellín and Bogotá. each of which addresses a different but complementary perspective of the research issue. The first chapter sets out the theoretical tools, and specifics why one of the approaches, the institutionalist approach, was chosen to read the political changes experienced by the two cities in the period covered by the research. Chapters two and three describes the electoral priority of the political parties in the alternative for the municipal partners of each city, as well as the political majority, for the executive branch, four challenges with the transformations of the local party systems and the influence of electoral norms on these changes. discussion on local politics to the extent that it proposals an analysis of the political parties (their electoral performance in common and major 6 elections), the party systems and the new policies that had electoral success, and it does so for the two cities studied, establishing points of convergence and differences in the processes of one and the other
