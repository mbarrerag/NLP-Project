# THEORY AND PRACTICE OF TRANSLATION: METHODOLOGICAL APPROXIMATION. ENGLISH-GERMAN-FRANCES-SPANISH

The aim of this work is to present the reader interested in the subject a methodological approach to teaching and learning translation, illustrated by examples and exercises mainly in English, French, German and Spanish. The use of these languages allows the book to be directed to a wider reader audience, although the basic language for theoretical discussion is Spanish. The text makes a quick journey through the milestones that marked the development of the translation thinking in the West, the constitution of translation as a scientific discipline and our methodological proposal for the transition from theory to pedagogical practice. There, emphasis is placed on learning centered on the student, bilingualism and biculturality of the translator, and the translation as a process of problem solving, according to the Dynamic Translation Model (DTM), in a specific text, within a context and within the framework of a communicative act. Then, the stages of the translation process from reading and understanding of the original are broken down and illustrated, through the elaboration of the translation until the revision and analysis of the translated text. Each chapter contains a theoretical reflection on the subject, practical exercises of analysis and discussion
