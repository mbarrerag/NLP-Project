# The Transition Generation: The Role of Provincial Lawyers in Greater Colombia (1780-1830)

This research focuses on the generation of transition in Greater Colombia, as it addresses the emergence of a group of provincial lawyers who adopted positions commonly understood as "liberal" within the period in question. The study seeks to offer a deeper and nuanced vision of the process of identifying this generation with liberalism and progressive reforms within Greater Colombia. To this end, the various influences and experiences that shaped these actors and how they contributed to the construction of the republican society, between 1780 and 1830. Through the analysis of various social and historical factors, such as the irruption of the military careers and the experiences that surrounded the consolidation of a constitutional government in 1821, we seek to understand why this group gravitated towards the defense of liberal reforms within the government. With a generational and sociological perspective, we have sought to distance ourselves from the personalistic vision and to highlight the relevance of the political networks and personal loyalities in the conformation of this first republican government, especially in relation to the individuals that formed the closest cabinet of the vice-president of Santander. In addition, we have used the primary ones as correspondences, memories and testimonies, with the objective. The study offers a collective vision of these provincial individuals and their role in building the republican society, especially in relation to the individual who was formed the close cabinet of Santander's vice president.
