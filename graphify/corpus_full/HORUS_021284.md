# Polymorphisms and differential expression of genes associated with follicular development in buffalo (Bubalus bubalis) and bovine (Bos indexus)

The project aims to identify polymorphisms in genes associated with follicular development and differences in gene expression in two highly phylogenetically related types of cattle, for this purpose blood and granular cells of follicles less than 7 mm will be obtained, in cows and reproductively normal buffaloes. DNA will be extracted from blood and RNA from granulars for: identification of polymorphic fragments of genes associated with follicular development and the amount of expression of the same genes in granulose cells of buffalo animals and cows will be determined to take advantage of the comparative approach. Polymorphisms will be identified by sequencing, gene expression by qPCR, with the help of bioinformatics techniques will determine the differences in sequence and quantity that will allow to propose theories of ovarian function based on comparison, in the studied species
