# Comparison of functional variables between football, basketball and rugby athletes with and without prior history of previous reconstruction of the anterior cross ligament between healthy and injured knee athletes, after the readaptation phase

In sports the lesion of Ligamento Cruzado Anterior (LCA) poses challenges to guarantee a return to the game (RTP) sure. The lack of guides in rehabilitation and functional rehabilitation, underline the importance of comparing functional variables between athletes with and without history of injury of LCA. The aim of this study is to compare the functional variables of strength-power and flexibility of lower members, speed and agility between sports of football, basketball and rugby, with and without previous variables of previous reconstruction of the LCA. Cross-sectional study, participated 124 sportsmen of football, basketball and rugby with (n=83) and without antecedence (n=41) of reconstruction of LCA. Sociodemographic, anthropometric and sports data were collected. Flexibility was assessed by the test Back Saver Sit and Reach, power with the Drop Jump, speed and agility with the V Cut and Illinois, respectively.
