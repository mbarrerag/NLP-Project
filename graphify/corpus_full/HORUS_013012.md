# Perceptions and practices of caregivers regarding child nutrition and physical activity

The feeding and physical activity of children are behaviors defined by the caregivers of children; those who are conditioned by their particular context. to describe the perceptions and practices of caregivers of children from 5 to 9 years old in Tunja, in terms of nutrition and physical activity of children. qualitative, descriptive, analysis of categorical content according to Bardin. Semistructured interview was applied through video call to 10 caregivers. emerged the central category: act according to what should be done, what is created and/or what is possible. Where the practices performed by caregivers regarding food and physical activity of children are conditioned to the perceptions they have, which in turn manifest themselves in them as beliefs, barriers or facilitators for action. the main determinants for the practices of caregivers were the preferences of children, the time and money available. These, according to their absence or presence, can manifest in them as barrier or facilitator for action. However, they are not always appropriate practices, depending on the balance between the barriers, facilitators and beliefs of children. “Act according to what should be done, what is lost and/or what can be donated.” Where the practices carried out by caregivers regulation child nutrition and physical activity are conditioned by the perceptions they have, and which are manifested in them as believers, barriers or facilitators for action. the main determinant factors for the caregivers' practices were the preferences of the children, the time and money available.
