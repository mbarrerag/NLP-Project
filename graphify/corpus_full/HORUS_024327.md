# MAPPING EDITORIAL TRACES INTRODUCTION

Mapping Traces is the title of a special volume of the Swedish journal Theoria that we co-edit in the company of Juliette Kennedy and Andrés Villaveces. This chapter introduces the thematic axis of the volume that starts from establishing an interdisciplinary dialogue between art, philosophy and mathematics; the main problem is based on the question about our possibilities of capturing and representing the world from our different disciplines: are we able to grasp the world our attempts to represent it? If so, how do you capture it? The chapter presents the key concepts that intersect all the essays: definiability, which appears from traces, unclear marks, traces, remnants of processes; the inexicity that becomes a common theme insofar as several of the narrative processes take their form in in indexitive languages (resets of memory, losses, existences, past dynamics, etc.), and the categorization that refers to the possibilities of completely covering the world from language without leaving anything remaining.
