# Asbestos as carcinogenic material present in constructive systems: development of a methodology for the removal and management of asbestos in Colombia and its contextualization in the region with the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín as a case study pilot

The asbestos or asbestos is a mineral that is presented in white and flexible fibers, of silky aspect and is constituted by silicates of Aluminium, Iron, Magnesium and Sodio (Kazan-Allen, 2002). This has been used largely in the construction sector for its good mechanical properties, fireproof and chemical stability. However, since 1977 the World Health Organization has classified asbestos as carcinogenic type 1, being the most dangerous for human beings, causing diseases such as asbestosis, mesothelioma and lung cancer. Due to this, a material that for its time was a response with different qualities and of low cost, nowadays is considered as a great problem worldwide that it is created to be removed. Colombia has about 300 million square meters of tiled processes of asbesto-cement in the territory and this is the case of the construction and the construction of the buildings of the buildings. This research aims to develop a methodology for the dismantling, management and replacement of non-friable asbestos present in construction systems in Colombia from the experience of good practices in institutions in Spain and its contextualization in the region of Antioquia. In addition, a diagnosis will be made of the current regulatory-legislative framework in the area of asbestos in Colombia that allows a comparison with Spanish legislation in such a way that information can be provided for the creation of a public policy for the management of asbestos in the country and the environmental impacts of eventual removal of asbestos in the cases of study and the different alternatives for the management and final disposal of waste resulting from these actions will be identified
