# C1 biorefineries: The CO2 case

The carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions have caused a significant impact on climate change and global warming, with concentrations reaching 400 ppm in recent years.There are several technologies for capture and use (CCU) of CO2 in industrial installations known as biorefineries C1, where CO2 is also considered as a raw material in the production of various products achieving a reduction in greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. Consequently, considering the current interest in carbon use technologies, this thesis focuses on analyzing the different alternatives of CCU under the concept of biorefinery C1. Initially, a heuristic analysis was carried out to select the best CO2 capture technology (CFC) and to study the main products that can be obtained from CO2 in each level of technological maturity.
