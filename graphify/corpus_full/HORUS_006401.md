# Experimental Setup for Path Loss Measurements at mmWave Frequencies

In this contribution we present an experimental setup for the characterization of channel path loss at millimeter wave frequencies and selected results from our measurement campaign. Our channel sounder integrates several hardware and software components, enabling measurements at 26 GHz, a band singled out for the future deployment of 5G systems in the millimeter wave band in Colombia. Some of these experimental results fit very well the 3GPP TR 38.901 standard propagation model
