# Harmonization between conceptualization and experimentation through the implementation of a teaching unit for the teaching of chemical balance for undergraduate students at the National University of Colombia

The chemical balance, is one of the great conceptual pillars in which chemistry is supported, is part of the didactic content of the subjects of General Chemistry and General Chemistry Laboratory, basic courses of the component of foundation in sciences, of the curricula of the curriculum of the Faculty of Sciences of the National University of Colombia. The courses of first semester present a high percentage of dropout by students, where one of the most significant causes are the failures in previous disciplinary knowledge. This implies a challenge for the professor of chemistry, which must adequately face the challenge of motivating and encouraging knowledge of the sciences. Therefore, through the modality of teaching practice in the Faculty of Sciences, it is sought to contribute to improving the teaching-learning process of general chemistry, in this work, a critical review of the experiments that are made in the Laboratory of General Chemistry for chemical equilibrium, while proposing new experiments, under the guideline of conceptual harmonization between the master class and the class of Laboratory, strengthening the conceptualization of knowledge and making the concepts of the disciplinary knowledge visible in the laboratory is is is. teaching practice in the Faculty of Sciences, we seek to contribute to improving the teaching-learning process of general chemistry.
