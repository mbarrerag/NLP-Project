# INDICATE AZADIRABIT IMPACT ON THE POPULATION OF INSECTS IN AN EXPERIMENTAL CULTIVE OF ARROZ

Rice (Oryza sativa) is one of the most consumed foods in the world, and is of great economic importance for Colombians. However, rice cultivation is commonly affected by pests, which has required the use of agrochemicals, which pollute the environment and harm human health. The aim of this study was to evaluate the impact of aqueous extracts of Azadirachta leaves indicates, a plant known for its repellent function, on insect populations in an experimental rice crop, in vegetative phase. The treatments of the rice plots were performed with three aqueous solutions prepared from A. indica leaves (T1 = 5 %, T2 = 10 %, T3 = 20 %), in addition a negative control (T4 = 0 %) was used. Weekly sampling of the insect population was performed to verify the efficacy of the extracts. 7418 individuals belonging to 12 orders were processed: Diptera (2720) was the most representative order, followed by Hemiptera (1854) and Coleoptera was observed by the family (1045).
