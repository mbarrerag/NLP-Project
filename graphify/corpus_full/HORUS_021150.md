# Circovirus detection in snakes native to Colombia

Circoviruses (CV) are ssDNA viruses belonging to the Circoviridae family and are present in different animal species. These viruses have generally been associated with an affectation on the immune system allowing other pathogens (bacteria and/or viruses) to affect susceptible species more severely. In particular, cirociviruses in reptiles were recently discovered and little is known about their tropism (organs and cells that affect them) and their pathogenesis. In these studies they have been found at the level of liver and lungs and their participation as a causal agent of pathology has not yet been demonstrated. Considering the diversity of snake species in Colombia, research is being proposed to detect circoviruses in these species from samples obtained from the national serpentine of Colombia. With the results of waiting to make the first report of these viruses in Colombia and determine their possible participation as disease-causing agents in these species
