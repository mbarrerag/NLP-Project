# Harnessing cannabis crop residues for sorbent materials.

The adsorption operations exploit the ability of certain solids to make specific substances of a solution concentrate on the surface of these. This includes contact of solid, liquid and gases and transfer of mass from fluid to solid. In the process of adsorption, molecules, atoms or ions, in a gas or liquid utility, are transported by means of diffusion to the surface of the solid where they form links to the solid surface or are retained by weak intermolecular forces. Most of the solids absorb gas and liquid species, but they are required of specific capacities since few have the selectivity and ability to qualify as serious candidates for commercial adsorbents. It is important a large specific surface (area per unit of volume), which is achieved by means of manufacturing techniques that give as a solid result with porous structures.
