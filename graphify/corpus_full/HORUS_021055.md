# Transculturality as a process in the construction of a transcendent habitat, seen from the Nasa community. Toribío Cauca case study. Addressed from 2016 (revitalization of the life plan 2016-2050) until 2021

This research is part of the interest in valuing the indigenous communities of the country, especially the Nasa community, located in the northern part of the department of Cauca in the municipality of Toribío. The main objective is to understand and analyze their ways of living based on their world view, describing the way in which their inhabitants reside, act and interpret their beliefs in the space and territory. To achieve the development of this work, it is necessary to work together with the Nasa community through the methodology of interactive research, using the techniques in which they review site studies, Nasa bibliography, visits, interviews, Nasa field diary, among others, in order to protect their indigenous identity and making it sustainable in time, creating awareness of what Nasa means, not only in the community, but also in the territory as an environmental, cultural and productive heritage; as a source of improvement and protection.
