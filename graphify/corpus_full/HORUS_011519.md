# BOGOTÁ WORKERS: OBSERVATION OF ENTHESEAL CHANGES IN A SAMPLE FROM THE CENTRAL CEMETERY OF BOGOTÁ ASSOCIATED WITH THE REPUBLICAN PERIOD

Entheseal changes (EC) have been studied in precontact and forensic contexts in Colombia, but historical osteological collections have not been evaluated. The objective of this research is to contribute to the knowledge of EC in a republican collection (1850-1950) from Bogotá. This period had dramatic political, economic, and social changes, therefore, this study approached to lifestyle changes linked with physical activities. Differences by sex and age were evaluated in a sample of 100 individuals over 20 years old from the bone collection of globe B of the Central Cemetery of Bogotá, an area where impoverished population were buried. In the observation of EC, two different methodologies recording 14 and 23 observation points for each methodology, respectively. Burial records were also studied to approach occupations there registered. EC would be associated to domestic activities as ¿sirvientas¿ and ¿lavanderas¿ in women and to day labors as ¿jornaleros¿ in men. Based on the results, no statistically significant differences were found between male and female individuals; however, the high presence of EC in the individuals under study suggests is similar in physical activity for both sexes
