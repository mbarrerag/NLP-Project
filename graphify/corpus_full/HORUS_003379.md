# Design of the internal structure of a scaffold (scaffold) for bone tissue

The engineering of bone tissues seeks the regeneration of damaged or lost bone tissue. The scaffolds are three-dimensional structures that provide scaffolding for cell and tissue growth. The advances in additive manufacturing technologies (MA) have facilitated the manufacture of complex and personalized scaffolds. This study evaluated the design and manufacture of scaffolds for bone tissue engineering based on the internal bone architecture. Polyhedron and dimensional diagrams Voronoi were used with cell sizes of 1,500 μm to 1.250 μm and trabeculas of 200 μm to 250 μm. The scaffolds were manufactured by stereolithography (SLA). The geometric and dimensional precision of the scaffolds was evaluated by comparing the CAD, STL and MA model, as well as their spatial and mechanical roughness as the upper body.
