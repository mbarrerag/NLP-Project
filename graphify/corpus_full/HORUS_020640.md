# CHAPTER 1: THE CARBON BUDGET OF AMOUNT

The main objective of this cross-sectional chapter is to summarize the state of the Amazon as a source or sink of carbon (C). The processes and studies involved are detailed in other chapters of the SPA. The main challenge of determining the state of the Amazon as a source or net sink of C on a continental scale is that many complex processes contribute to C flows. Unlike other regions, fossil fuel burning emissions are lower contributors to Amazonian flows. Instead, the main sinks and sources of C to the atmosphere are associated with the accumulation or net loss of biomass, with losses including deforestation, biomass burning and tree mortality followed by decomposition. Biomass accumulates in areas where tree growth exceeds losses. Amazonia includes not only intact forests, but also degraded and exploited forests, natural non-forest forests, agricultural and urban areas, and aquatic systems that include wetlands that contribute to the regional carbon cycle
