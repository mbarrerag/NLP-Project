# CHROMATOGRAPHY SEPARATION

Chromatography can be defined as a technique that allows the separation of a mixture of solutes based on the differential displacement speed of the solutes; this difference is established when the solutes are carried out by a mobile phase (liquid or gaseous) through a stationary phase (chromatographic milk), which can be liquid or solid. The properties of the components of a mixture determine their mobility with respect to the mobile phase. The practice presented below can be done in the laboratory or at home, and despite being very well known, has slight variations that can make it more interesting
