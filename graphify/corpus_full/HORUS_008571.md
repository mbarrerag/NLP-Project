# OBTAINATION OF TIO2 ANTI-REFLECTING VALUE TESTS BY AEROSOL ASSISTED CHEMICAL VALUE TECHNIQUE

TiO2 titanium dioxide-based films (Titanium Oxycetylacetone (IV) were manufactured with a purity of 90%) on glass substrates using the aerosol-assisted chemical vapour deposit technique (AACVD). Prior to the manufacturing process, the concentration of Titanium Oxiacetilacetone (IV) was established, the technological parameters of the process were fixed as the deposit temperature (°C) and air flow (L/min), the only parameter that varied was the temperature, following a scale (250°C, 300°C, 350°C, 380°C, 400°C and 450°C). Using the x-ray diffraction technique (DRX), it was observed that the crystallineness of the TiO2 films improved from 350°C and the best crystallineity was obtained at 450°C. Likewise, from 350°C to 450°C, the potential phase of crystalline anatase was located without variation in the difractancy of x-ray or presence of any other phase.
