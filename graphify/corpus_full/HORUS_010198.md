# A mentoring perspective on caring for caregivers: A contribution from nursing in Colombia

This chapter describes a successful mentoring experience in Colombia centered on the Social Appropriation of Health Knowledge in Caregivers of People with Chronic Illness. This experience allows for a contribution to the reduction of the participants' burden of care, as well as the establishment of a strong connection and relationship between the mentor and the mentee, resulting in growth, learning, and mutual recognition of abilities and skills. Mentoring entailed making a significant contribution to the reciprocal teaching and learning process through peer co-creation and collaboration. In this way, the project is strengthening the leadership of young talent in nursing, allowing it to grow and make contributions to the discipline and family caregivers. All of this is framed by the central concept of nursing, care, between mentor and mentee, researcher, and caregivers
