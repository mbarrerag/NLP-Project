# RESEARCH APPLIED ON LIVING

The idea of the house-major proposed by members of the Modern Movement at the beginning of the 20th century has been the subject of numerous criticisms and reviews, including that of one of its proponents, Le Corbusier. As a continuation of this review, this research emerges that rethinks the way in which we understand and design housing, with the aim of offering varied, efficient and affordable solutions, returning this modern and up-to-date sound. The work had its origins twenty years ago and, since then, had a decided vocation for the author. For this reason, it is also a contribution to the conceptual framework and several cases of theoretical and architectural studies, the research has made continuous use of models, prototypes, experiments and products applied as a fundamental part of its development. The final result is a proposal of Mat-building applied to the acquired social housing, in which the research takes place in a different way of identifying the different kinds of work and the research
