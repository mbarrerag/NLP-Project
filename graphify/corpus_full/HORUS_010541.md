# Multisensory stimulation education strategy (TVV) for mothers of newborns linked to a kangaroo plan program in the city of Bogotá

The newborns premature experience a large amount of integrated multimodal stimulation, such as the sounds of mother speech or heart beats and tactile or vestibular stimulation of mother movements (Symington & Pinelli, 2006). The development of the brain is conditioned by the effects of multiple factors, such as genetic endowment and environmental effects; therefore, sensory deprivation can affect the outcome of neurological development (Burns et al., 1994). The multisensory stimulation strategy has shown multiple benefits, which is why this initiative arises that aims to the training of nurses participating in the Mother Kangaro Program in the institution Fundación Kangaro located in the headquarters of the Hospital San José Infantil de Bogotá, in this way, they can include the practice in the educational interventions that are used daily with mothers and their children. The main objective will be to train nurses in the strategy of stimulation multitension (ATVV) by means of audio and video as an educational tool, allowing these knowledge to be introduced into the usual training provided to mothers of newborns of canguro.
