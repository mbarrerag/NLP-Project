# Hydrodynamic assessment of high-grade reliefs in supercritical regime by CFD simulation.

In this thesis, the analysis of the flow over reliefs of high cañuela in supercritical regime is presented. The research was carried out in two stages, where the commercial software ANSYS – Fluent was implemented, experimental data and information collected from this type of hydraulic structures located in the Valle de Aburá (Antioquia, Colombia). In the first stage the calibration and numerical validation of a high cañuela relief, of prismatic circular section, was carried out, with available experimental data. The effects of turbulence and the variations of the free surface were simulated using the model of turbulence k- the Realizable and Fluid Volume (VOF), respectively. The solutionor Coupled with the Pseudo-Transient method, which solve flows in stationary state and accelerate the convergence of the solution. The simulations show that the numerical model predicts the experimental flow with good precision, with relative errors of 3.65 and 3.12% for the flow and minor ones to the flow profile. The results obtained were compared with the circular design methodologies proposed in the current local norm.
