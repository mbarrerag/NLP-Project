# AGENCY AND THE EVOLVING ORIGIN OF THE CAPACITY OF DESIGN

Abstract The conference introduces the notion of augmented agency and offers elements to resolve a fundamental concern: How can one explain the origin of the ability to design in humans and how this ability has evolved? The augmented agency is the incorporation of artifacts into the naked body to expand the creative capacities of the original agency. Although there is a biological continuity in the way non-human animals manufacture objects and the way in which human beings design artifacts, it is argued that the ability to design is unique to human beings and is characterized by the ability to visualize future scenarios, the accelerated invention of new artifacts and the continuous improvement of them from the development of cognitive resources specific to the species. A stage-based model will be presented that proposes an explanation of the origins and evolution of the ability to design. To deepen in the evolutionary origins of the ability to design helps to understand the underlying cognitive processes and their relationship with other semiotic resources specific to the human species such as mimesis, pictorial representations and polysemiotic communication
