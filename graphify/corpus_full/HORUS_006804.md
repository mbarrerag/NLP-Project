# Thought and geographic research in Colombia: ideas, characters and institutionalization

Geography is one of the few millennial sciences. Born, or at least known, from the heroic tales of travelers and traders (from the Ulysses of Homer to Ibn-Battuta), it became an area of vital knowledge for the administration and government of the multiple forms of state that has experienced humanity. Thought and geographic research in Colombia is a book that opens with a reflection around the first representation of America in the mapmundi of Juan de la Cosa of 1500, and closes with an exercise on the spatiality of Covid-19 during 2020. It also includes several texts on the history of geographical science in Colombia and its role in the construction of the State and society during the twentieth century. This is a book of wide interest not only for geographic science, but also for the economy, politics, sociology, demography, urbanism, health sciences, environmental sciences, history... All of them are reflected in different sections of the book to conclude that our way of understanding the world has always been through our conception of space and our interaction with the physical environment.--Concluded--Contracted in the book, geography is one of geography is one of the world
