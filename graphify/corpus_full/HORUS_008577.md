# COLOMBIA - PACIFIC ASSOCIATION

This study describes the biodiversity of diatoms associated with parasitic forms in fifteen species of marine fish of commercial importance in Buenaventura Valle del Cauca-Colombia, one of the most important ports: Bagre panamensis, Bagre pinnimaculatus, Caranx sexfasciatus, Caranx vinctus, Centropomus unionesis, Cynoscion albus, Cynoscion praedatorius; Cynoscion squamipinnis, Eugerres periche, Lutjanus colorado, Lutjanus guttatus; Menticirhus panamensis, Mughl curema, Oreochromis niloticus niloticus, Parapsettus panamensis. A number of 26 samples of gastrointestinal content collected between March and June 2023 were evaluated; this first study allowed to make a first approach to the marine ecosystem.
