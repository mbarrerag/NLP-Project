# Antigenic mapping of potential epitopes of the Spike (S) virus SARS-CoV-2 virus causative agent of the COVID-19 pandemic. Implications for the development of multicomponent site-directed vaccines

The vaccines against the COVID-19 approved for use in Colombia are mainly aimed at stimulating a humoral immune response mediated by antibodies against a structural protein of the virus SARS-CoV-2 called Spike (S) encoded in the ORF2 vaccine of the genetic structure of the virus. This protein acts as the main binding of the virus whose main function is to bind to a receptor protein for this expressed constitutively on the surface of a wide repertoire of human cells called Enzyme angiotensin-1 converter of type 2 or ACE-2. The molecular interaction of these two proteins is the main mechanism of infection of the virus to the cells of the host in this case a human being susceptible to be infected. Once the virus penetrates to its white cells of infection are activated effective mechanisms of replication of new generations of the virus thanks to its ease of retro-transcir their genetic information in the infected cell using the molecular machinery of the latter to assemble new viral particles, with the consequent activation of the acute symptoms in the infected person which in some cases can cause to aggravate their clinical condition and eventually death
