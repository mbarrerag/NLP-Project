# Emotional work memory: evaluation of brain activity and performance of a standard n-back task in university students

The current research was carried out at a historical moment of pandemic because of the SARS-CoV-2, during this context public health problems were generated worldwide being the mental disorders associated with mood alterations one of the most common and with the greatest need to face. Recent studies (Veloso and Ty, 2021; Xiu et al., 2018) have shown that the training of the working memory with emotional content improves emotional regulation and anxiety, making this interesting relationship to study, mainly the effects on the emotion and the working memory. The present study aimed to evaluate the effects on the performance and on the electrical activity of the emotional content of images used in a work memory task. 32 university students completed a 2-back task divided into blocks of positive, neutral and negative valence produced by the analysis of evoked potentials, specifically the P3. The results showed that the performance between the different valences was not different, however, differences were found in the P3, when the stimuli were congruent, the P3 amplitude of the parietal generated by the negative valance images were higher. electrical activity of the emotional content of images used in a working memory task. Thirdtwo undergraduate students completed a 2-back task divided into positive, neutral, and negative value blocks designed to perform evoked potential analysis, specifically the P3. The results showed that the performance between the different values were not different, however, differences were found in the P3, when the stimuli were consistent, the amplitude of the P3 in the parietal region generated by the negative value images was higher.
