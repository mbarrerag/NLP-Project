# Protection coordination: A solution from the quality of the power

The theory presents, initially, a critical review of the state of the art of the protection of electrical systems, including functions, schemes and protection systems. From this review, the current limitations in the protection systems are presented, as well as the trends for their correction. To overcome these limitations, this thesis proposes a new protection system based on the analysis of the quality of the electrical power; using the mathematical format of the instant power tensor, a new indicator of the overall deviation of the ideal conditions of a synusoid and balanced system is defined and classified by the new indicator defined in this thesis. The proposed protection system manages to avoid the process of coordination of protections, improves the selectivity of the high impedance failures of linear and nonlinear characteristic, and achieves the mitigation of the risk of electric arc .
