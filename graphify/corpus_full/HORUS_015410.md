# The modulation of the shadow: Decoding in the deep facades of Fernando Martínez' Giraldo Complex

This research addresses the problem of the role of shadow in the definition of the limits of the architectural form by the treatment of the surface and the space. The study is focused on the decomposition of the facades of the Giraldo Set (Edificio Elvira Ogliastri, Giraldo and Blanca Ponce de León) by Fernando Martínez, who addresses the shadow, based on the conscious and modulated variation of the depth of the facades. The analysis adopts two parallel forms of looking at the project, one of the outer surface and another of the intermediate thresholds; which correspond to the categorization of projected shadow and shadow inhabited respectively.
