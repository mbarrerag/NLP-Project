# NEW REGISTRATION OF MYRMECOPHAGA TRIDACTYLA (PYLOSS: MYRMECOPHAGIDAE) IN GUAYIRA: ENLARGEING KNOWLEDGE ON ITS COLOMBIA DISTRIBUTION

The giant anteater, Myrmecophaga tridactyla (Mammalia: Pilosa: Myrmecopphagidae), is the only living member of the genus and is restricted to Central and South America. It is currently classified as a Vulnerable species (VU); however, in several countries it has already been declared as an extinct species. For Colombia its distribution pattern is fragmented and increasingly reduced throughout the country. Due to its importance and state of vulnerability it is important to generate reliable information about its populations, as well as its changing patterns of distribution. In this work, it is reported from phototrampeo sampling, the presence of M. tridactyla for the department of La Guajira, being the most recent northern registry of the species for Colombia. Additionally, an update on the distribution of the species at the national level is presented, and the need to implement monitoring and conservation actions of this species in the Colombian Caribbean is highlighted
