# Cognitive and Insomnia Rumiations Care Strategies in Chronic Disease Patients

The present study aimed to construct an intervention protocol for the care of cognitive rumiations and sleep disorders in patients with chronic diseases. Cognitive ruminations are repetitive and persistent thoughts, which generally focus on negative aspects or concerns, making it difficult to concentrate in the present (Watkins, 2008). Sleep disorders will create difficulties for people to have a rest, which turns out to be fundamental in a hospital context. Sleep is a fundamental biological function which is related to the general state of physical and psychological health of a person (Miró et al., 2005). 42 articles were selected which were previously reviewed and then the applicability for the study was evaluated by means of the software for systematic reviews Rayyan. As for the results it was found that the Cognitive-Conductual Therapy (TCC), the Basic Therapy in Mindfulness, and the Conceptive Prevention and Commitment Therapy (ACT) are effective interventions in the management of cognitive rumiations and insomnia . showed that Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Mindfulness-Based Therapy (MBCT) and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) are effective interventions in the management of cognitive ruminations and insomnia
