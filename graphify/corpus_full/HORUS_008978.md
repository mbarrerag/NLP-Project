# TECHNIQUES FOR THE MARKING AND MONITORY OF OCTOCORALS, FOR ANIMAL, CARIBBEAN AND PACIFIC COLOMBIAN

This chapter describes the techniques of marking, as well as photo-monitoring of arborescent octocorals. Methods are presented to permanently mark gorgonoce octocorals in their basal trunks, without harming the colony, so that they can monitor in situ their growth and health in time, as well as evaluate and monitor the population structure
