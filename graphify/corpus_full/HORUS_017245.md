# Sociocultural aspects that influence the food behavior of people at cardiovascular risk in the city of Ibagué

The aim of this study was to describe how the sociocultural aspects influence the food behaviour of people with cardiovascular risk hospitalized in a health institution in the city of Ibagué. A qualitative, descriptive study; in which 12 adults with cardiovascular risk hospitalized in the Health Institution of Ibagué Hospital Federico Lleras Acosta were included. The participants were subjected to the semi-structured interview and the collection of the data was obtained when an information saturation was reached. The following categories emerged for the construction of the analysis of the information obtained: the socio-cultural influence on the food behavior, the healthy lifestyles, the health risks, the cardiovascular risk, the consequences of the inadequate food behavior. The socio-cultural influence of people with cardiovascular risk is given by the customs and cultural traditions that modify their food behavior with a high social value; in the time they have adopted the food habits similar to those of other groups. chnique of content analysis in agreement with the postulates of krippendorff.
