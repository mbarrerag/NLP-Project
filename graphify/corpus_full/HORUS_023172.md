# Organogenesis, in vitro regeneration and expression of the genes gus, gfp, pcht28 and PpGlu in common tree tomato (Solanum betaceum Cav.)

The cultivation of tomato tree (Solanum betaceum Cav.) is a crop that, although well known and its fruits very consumed in Colombia, is not technified, as many other crops in the country. This fruit is susceptible to pests such as white fly and aphids, and to diseases such as those caused by virosis or fungi, for example, anthracnosis, disease caused by the fungi Colletochicium, which causes damage mainly in the fruit, either before or after its harvest, and also affects young parts such as flowers and primordial foliar. With a view to the stable transformation of tomato from common tree to eventually generate some resistance to diseases such as anthracnosis, in this work the in vitro regeneration of this species via organogenesis obtaining an average of 18 outbreaks per explanant, in the case of leaves.
