# Space and Society. Notes for the understanding of the San Ignacio sector in the context of modernization processes. 1900-1930

The following study is developed on the spatiality of the former square José Félix de Restrepo ‐ today of San Ignacio ‐, specifically on the two educational institutions that worked there during the first half of the twentieth century in Medellín. For its analysis, this spatiality was framed in the period corresponding to the first three decades of the same century, as this is a period of crucial importance to understand the modernization processes. According to the sources consulted, the possibilities of analysis on the University of Antioquia and the College of San Ignacio had as guiding axis, on the one hand, the hygienistic dynamics that began to be visible towards the end of the nineteenth century and, on the other hand, the characterization of an educational culture in the social space of the former square in the head of its institutions. The first aspect, then, reveals a state of education in general and opens the doors for the emergence of a new educational and confesional centrality in the direction of the city. their guiding axis, on the one hand, the hygienist dynamics that began to be visible towards the end of the 19th century and, on the other, the characterization of an educational culture in the social space of the old square based on the work of its institutions.
