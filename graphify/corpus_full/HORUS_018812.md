# TERRITORIAL DISAPPOSAL AS A CONCEPTUAL PROPOSAL FOR EXPLANATION OF VOLUNTARY TRANSNATIONAL MIGRATION

The following text focuses on circumscribing the explanatory model of a doctoral research project on the causes of voluntary transnational migration. Through the concept of territorial detachment, does the text question the rationalist and economic vision offered by the theory of factors of expulsion-attraction to explain the migration of citizens from integrated territories under the rubric of “third world” to other territories conceived as the first world? and in return emphasizes in the symbolic, political and existential significance that many of these citizens grant to the simple fact of living in countries that represent geosymbols of “power” and “civilization”, regardless of the concrete living conditions they may have in the receiving countries
