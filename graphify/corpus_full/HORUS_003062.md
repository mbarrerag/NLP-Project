# ♪ ♪

The article analyses the project to reorganize the PARQUE GRAN COLOMBIANO, built a few kilometers from the city of Cucuta, Colombia. The project sets up a joint patrimony consisting of four buildings of historical and cultural interest, among which stands out an ancient church: ¿el Temple histórico¿. It is located two kilometers from the border between Colombia and Venezuela, a problematic territorial fringe, full of social, political and environmental tensions that - as Jaime Cabal says - requires urgent actions that foment reconciliation
