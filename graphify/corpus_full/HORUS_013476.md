# Types of thought, Types of content and emotion

The aim of this research is to demonstrate that emotions are mental states with representative content. The above is done through three steps. (1) Generate an approach to the notion of content through an interpretation of Sinn (2) Give a definition about representational content (3) Argument the representative character of emotion. Through this process, three conclusions were reached, (I) emotions are mental states with representational content as they give and are based on information, (II) the way emotions present such information is not through a conceptual structure, that is, they do not have as content a thought and (3) emotions are mental states with nonconceptual content. .
