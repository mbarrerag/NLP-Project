# Implementation of the wireless communication process for the MEDICI Humanitarian Demining Test Device.

This research project aims to make a wireless link of a GPR-UWB radar with a processing device, in order to facilitate its use in the field, this device will be used in humanitarian demining. Wireless communication is achieved by designing an embedded device that communicates the GPR-UWB with a laptop that processes the data in Matlab. Then, measurements of the interference of the link in the A-scan generated by the ground penetration radar are made and the viability of the radio links near the ground penetration radars is analyzed. This research was funded by the MEDICI project of the EMC-UN research group and within the facilities of the National University
