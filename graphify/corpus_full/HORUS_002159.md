# Effect of hydrothermal process on native yucca starches (Manihot sculenta) and yam (Dioscorea alata)

At present, there is a need to develop starches with improved properties to increase their applicability in food matrices. The effect of hydrothermal treatment (HMT) on the physicochemical, morphological and structural properties of the native starches of yuca and yam was evaluated. The native starches of yuca and yam were subjected to low humidity (20 - 25%), high temperature (90 °C) and a processing time of 4 hours. The results showed that HMT significantly decreased solubility in cold water (CWS), while increasing the capacity of water absorption (WAC) and the degree of crystallineity (DC) of yuca starches. In contrast, in the yam the opposite effect occurred. In addition, the modification increased the content of amilose and the stability of pastes. In addition, microphotography revealed significant changes in granular morphology. In conclusion, hydrothermic treatment in tubercul starches is a promising technology to improve the hydrophilic properties and characteristics of the empamples
