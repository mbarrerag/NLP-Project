# TECHTIC, REVOLUTION AND BANDIJING. HISTORICAL TRAINING OF THE GUERRILLA CONCEPT IN COLOMBIA

This article presents an analysis of the process of transformation of the term guerrilla into a sociopolitical concept in the Colombian context. To this end, a study is offered of the semantic displacements of the term and the debates generated in this regard, in three moments: First, during the formation of the Colombian State when guerrillas were used, both descriptively (as much as military tactics), and in a valuable way (as a quality of the combatants). Second, regarding the partisan confrontations of the mid-20th century. And third, with the emergence of left guerrillas in the mid-1960s
