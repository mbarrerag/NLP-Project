# COMMUNITY DISEASE, 2022; V18:

Main objective: To describe the development of the mobile application, CareT-Well UN, to strengthen the knowledge, attitudes and practices of people with sensitive pulmonary tuberculosis. The methodology of Gasca, Camargo and Delgado was adopted in five stages: analysis, design, development, operating tests and delivery. The theoretical reference was the Information Model - Motivation and Behavioral Skills. Main results: The first mobile application was developed for people with sensitive pulmonary tuberculosis in Colombia. Its functions include: registration and session start; educational modules; calendar; frequently asked questions; communication with health professionals; motivational messages and reminders of medication intake. Main conclusion: CareT-Well UN is a mobile application that tends to favor therapeutic adherence and strengthen the knowledge of people with sensitive pulmonary tuberculosis. Its development process was validated with experts
