# Ramón Manrique Sánchez: journalistic and literary action in the first half of the 20th century

This work explores the journalistic and literary action of Ramón Manrique libre Sánchez from three works: Under the sign of the hoz: the conjuring of communism in Colombia (1937), The Presidencies (1944) and La Venturosa (1947). The aim is to place Manrique within the national tradition of journalism, essay and literature. To this end, I serve mainly the dialogue with the theory of the cultural production fields of Pierre Bourdieu, the notion of historical consciousness of Gadamer and the explanation of the institutionalization of the literature of Jacques Dubois. The demand that I propose of the intellectual work of Manrique is based on the sensitivity of the writer to the cultural, historical, political and social problems of the first half of the Colombian twentieth century, which he assessed in his artistic, essayist and novelist facets. Thus, with the intention of explaining his position to problems such as the experience of modernity in Colombia, the professionalization of the Colombian writer, the boom of communism in the country and the inability to overcome some historical contradictions, in the first part he reviews his intellectual profile and his social projection (19th) and in the second analysis and in the second analysis of the Colombian writer. nsitivity to the country's cultural, historical, political and social problems. Throughout his stages as a journalist, essayist and writer, the development of an aristocratic and modern world view is emerging. In this way, with the intention of explaining his position on the cultural problems of the time, the arrival of modernity in Colombia and the inability to overcome contradictions from history, the first part part reviews his intellectual profile and his Social projection. In the second and third part his aesthetic project is evaluated based on the analysis of the problems that he poses in his work
