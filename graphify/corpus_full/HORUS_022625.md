# MANAGEMENT OF LESSONS LEARNED IN EDUCATIONAL PROJECTS INVOLVING INFORMATION TECHNOLOGIES

To describe the current situation of knowledge management in educational projects incorporating new technologies. Different educational projects using ICT were analyzed using the LATI methodology to deepen knowledge and experiences learned as a valid supply for knowledge management. A model is proposed that describes the processes of management and characterization of lessons learned based on criteria that encourage their reuse and that contribute to the continuous improvement of educational processes. Knowledge management involves processes and knowledge assets that are recommended to articulate to promote the reuse of good practices in different contexts. Projects for the development of educational processes that create instances of new information technologies require effective knowledge management, lessons learned are the main active to promote this purpose
