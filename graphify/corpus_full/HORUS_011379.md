# PHRAMOCHOTERAPEROUS FOLLOW-UP BY DISEASE: AN INTEGRATIVE REVIEW

the recent increase in non-communicable chronic diseases has modified the global and national epidemiological profile. Thus, an increase in the complexity of pharmacological treatments and consequently polypharmacy has been presented. This situation is potentially problematic since it generates problems related to medicines, such as pharmacological interactions or therapeutic failure, derived from multiple factors such as lack of knowledge of drugs, therapeutic non-compliance, failures in the integration of the health team and even barriers to the safe transition of care in health. Therefore, nursing, from its guiding, educational and integrative role, has a great field of action and relevance in this scenario by performing an integral pharmacotherapy follow-up by nursing (SFTE) that allows to influence from several fronts the mitigation of the occurrence of problems related to medicines.Objective: analyze the recent scientific literature which are the components of a pharmacologic monitoring model by nursing (SFTE) in the last 10 years. integrative review of literature of articles published between 2010 and 2020 in the databases of ScienceDirect, Scopus, Wiley Online Libralete
