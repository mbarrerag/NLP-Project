# PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION AND TERRITORIAL PEACE IN THE OVERVIEW OF CAMPISIN AGRICULTURAL SYSTEMS IN THE VALLEY OF THE CAUCA

A study was presented and analyzed in which the agrarian systems of the Asociación de Trabajadores Campesinos del Valle del Cauca (Astracava) were addressed, with a focus on productive inclusion and territorial peace. The study was carried out in two peasant territories of Astracava. The first, located in the village of Cerro Rico (Buga), with agrarian settlements of people who were displaced from the upper part of the municipality, addressed a characterization, seen as a space for deepening and participatory work, to know their agrarian productive systems, their management and directionality, from the peasant territory and the different roles of peasants in this process. In the second, in the corregimiento of La Moralia (Tuluá), a peasant territory that is the flagship of mobilizations in the Valle del Cauca, the value chain, seen as nonlinear, was addressed with an important role, and that contributes information to the agrarian systems, as a binding axis to productive inclusion and food sovereignty in the peasant territory
