# Lighting design of an educational center applying integrative lighting

In recent years, greater attention has been paid to the effects of light on human beings, both visible and non-visible effects. Several researches have shown that aspects such as the spectrum of the source of lighting, the level of light and the exposure time have a strong impact on our circadian biological clock. In this context, the concept of integrative lighting is of great importance, being still relatively new, especially in the educational sector. In fact, the projects applying this approach in this sector are scarce. Those that adhere to the WELL standard and the design guide UL-DG 24480 are the closest, implementing the circadian lighting through the calculation of the circadian stimulus. In this work performs the integrative lighting design for the first plant of a new educational center in Manizales, Colombia, applying the criteria set by the standards WELL and the guide of design UL-DG-24480. This allows to make a comparison of results obtained in the design using both standards. In general, it was found that for both standards it is necessary to adjust the spect of power of distribution and implementation (the UL-D) and UL-DG-DG-Be). DG 24480 design guide are the most similar, implementing circus lighting through the calculation of circus stimulus. The objective is to design integrated lighting for the first floor of an educational center in the city of Manizales, Colombia, applying the criteria established by the WELL standards and UL-DG 24480 design guide. This will allow for a comparison of the results obtained in the design using both standards. Overall, it was found that for both standards, adjusting the power distribution spectrum and implementing different dimming levels are necessary to meet the established criteria
