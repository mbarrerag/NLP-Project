# FLAP (Freestyle microperforating glue).

The following atlas consists of a hundred procedures distributed throughout the human anatomy. Thirty-four in the head and neck, six in the trunk, fourteen in the upper limb, twelve in the genitals and periné, and thirty-four in the lower limb for the resolution of defects of coverage secondary to malignant pathologies, trauma, burns sequelae, infectious processes and ulcers due to pressure that compromise the skin and soft tissues. This atlas is intended to create a guide for the coverage of multiple defects that can occur in the practice of the plastic surgeon, as well as to promote the development and extension of the Plastic Surgery Unit of the National University of Colombia
