# CIUdadania, a possibility to rebuild care as a political act

This research aims to deepen and broaden the discussions around care in contemporary society, based on the contributions of feminist theoreticals such as Nancy Fraser, Carole Pateman, Natalia Magnone, Blanca Rodríguez Ruiz, Tomeu Sales, Denis Najmanovich, among others. For this purpose, the category of CUIDadania is developed, as an academic and theoretical bet that aims to make the power of care visible in all social spheres, from a broad, comprehensive and different view of care, so that care is not to continue to be seen as the “main obstacle to women’s participation”, but that it can be recognized as emancipatory political practice. This last, as far as the category of citizenship, historically patriarchal, is reformulated, and in its conception, care is included as a political act involving participatory parity, with the aim of making societies more cared, autonomous and democratic. At the end of this research, a methodological proposal is found to teach and share the concept of CUUDadania in social movements and social and community organizations. .
