# Productive efficiency model: management of productive rural land in Caquetá

This work develops a model of land management of productive rural land in the department of Caquetá, in view of the problem of inadequate use of rural land. The model contemplates the potential of productivity in new agricultural practices, the possibility of developing environmentally sustainable productive dynamics in relation to the Amazon forest, the processes of social sustainability through the characterization of the Amazonian rural populations and their needs, and access to land within the framework of the paradigm of the Integral Rural Reform. .
