# Iron-copper pillared benthite for water-based amoxicillin degradation.

A Colombian bentonite was successfully pillared with polyhydroxocated polymer precursors from Al, Al-Fe, Al-Cu and Al-Fe-Cu in solid state whose synthesis is novel. The effect of the content of Fe and Cu was studied on the physicochemical properties of the benthite pillared based on characterization techniques such as X-ray fluorescence (FRX), X-ray diffraction (DRX), electron scanning microscopy (SEM), photoelectron spectroscopy of X-ray (XPS), thermal analysis and texture analysis (nitrogen fisssion).The design of statistical experiments of Box-Behnken was used to determine the optimal parameters of the independent variables: hydrogen peroxide (0.120-0.144 M), catalyst load (0.5 1.5 g/L) and catalyst load (PILC FeCu1, FeCu5, FeCu10) in the catalytic oxidation of wet peroxide (CWPO) of amoxicillin.
