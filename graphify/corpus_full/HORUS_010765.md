# IS Possibilty for the consideration of primary health care as a national public policy within the current COLOMBIAN IMAGINARY?

The current Colombian health scenario leads us to think of primary care (PHC) as a conceptual model of thought that guides the health system and allows it to be addressed from a holistic and integral perspective. A paradigm shift based on community participation, health governance and dialogues between social systems away from positivism and the logic of the added value inherent in extreme neoliberalism is required. The Colombian health system needs to adopt the PHC model as a structured and thought out public policy, away from external influences and oriented towards the real social needs of the country and its population
