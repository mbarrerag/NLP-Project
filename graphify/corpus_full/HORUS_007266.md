# Generative models: Audio generation in bioaccustics

This study addresses the challenge of limited availability and low quality of audio data in bioacoustic, focusing specifically on the generation of realistic croaris of the Boana faber frog. We propose a novel approach using probabilistic diffusion models, a powerful technique of deep learning for the synthesis of audio. Due to the computational demands of these models, we implement a systematic selection process based on Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) and the K-medias grouping to identify a subset of samples generated of high quality of a larger group. We evaluate the audio samples generated through an experiment of human perception in A/B test format. The results show that our trained model generates convincing croaris of Boana faber, even with a truncated training, highlighting the potential of the model to generate bioacoustic data, low-cost audio. This approach offers promising possibilities to improve existing data sets and improve the performance of automated biodiversity monitoring systems .
