# CHARACTERIZATION OF GAS AND CHEMICAL RESIDUE OF FRUIT: CASSAGES AND SEEDS

The waste generated in the agro-industry of fruit processing and processing accounts for 50% of these, indicating that half of the processed fruit is wasted. One way to reduce the amount of these residues is to implement harvesting systems. However, before establishing which procedure should be performed on the residue for its use, it is necessary to determine its physical and chemical characteristics. This work provides a guide to the techniques of analysis of physical and chemical properties, and presents the experimental results of these characteristics for 50 fruit residues, between shells and seeds. Through the physical characteristics it was determined: the rate of generation of residues, size, thickness, form, hardness, density, total humidity and color. While, through chemical analyses, it was established: the next analysis, elemental, energy content, structural composition, bromatological, bioactive compounds and antioxidant capacity. The results of these analyses allowed identification - car possible alternatives of the use of fruit residues. These alternatives include: the production of activated charcoal, generation of energy by direct combustion, production of fuels by pyrolysis, production of food for animals, extraction of antioxidant compounds, among others
