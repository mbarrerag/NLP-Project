# Systematization and analysis of territorial experiences for the evaluation and transformation of food sustainability in the municipalities of Susa (Cundinamarca) and Vélez (Santander).

Due to the growing unsustainability of food systems, the project “Towards food sustainability in South America and Africa” developed a framework for analysis for the evaluation and transformation of local food systems under a transdisciplinary methodology called Marco FoodSAF - Sustainability Analysis Framework- applied in some countries of Latin America and Africa. This work systematizes the experience of application in the municipalities of Susa, Cundinamarca and Vélez, Santander for the case of Colombia. It describes the methodology for its development, makes a comparative analysis of the results obtained and presents the main learnings for the evaluation and transformation of food sustainability in the local peasant food systems
