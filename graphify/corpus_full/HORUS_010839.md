# Biosynthesis of silver nanoparticles from rhizospheric fungi and their immobilization in a natural fiber for in vitro control of phytopathogenic bacteria (Pectobacterium carotovorum)

In this study initially a bioprospecting study was carried out to select rhizospheric fungi of potato crops, promising fungi for the synthesis of AgNp’s with antibacterial action against Pectobacterium carotvorum, phytopathogenic agent of potato difficult to manage by conventional techniques. Five fungi of the species Penicillium simplicisimum, Aspergillus niger and Fusarium oxysporum were isolated and produced by AgNp ́s sphericals of sizes between 15 and 45 nm, which presented antibacterial action against the phytopathogen.
