# SPACE DETECTION AND CLASSIFICATION OF FATAL TRANSIT ACCIDENTS RAGS ON THE VIAL NETWORK OF COLOMBIAN CITY.

This project is motivated by the study of phenomena that occur on complex metric spaces, such as traffic accidents on the road network of a city. We will consider the problem of detecting spatial characteristics in the presence of point patterns noise in a linear network. In particular, the occurrence of any traffic accident can cause distortions in the analysis of a feature of interest. These events are considered independent random events or noises. In this research, the feature of interest are traffic accidents that end in lessons or death. In previous studies, the topic is addressed by eliminating noise through the distribution of distance to the nearest K-th neighbor and the spatial classification of characteristics are obtained using the non-homogeneous correlation functions of local pairs. We will extend these methodologies to a more complex geometric context, where the classical properties of a point-process change and the visualization of data is not intuitive. The performance and precision of the proposed methodology will be analyzed through the rate of false positives in a linear network. Finally, the methodology developed in the study of transit accident databases in the main cities of Colombia will be applied to provide a set of future casualty decisions
