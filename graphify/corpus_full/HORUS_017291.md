# ANALYSIS OF THE SOCIAL CAPITAL IN A TECHNOLOGICAL SYSTEM OF A SMART CITY USING A PLS -SEM MODEL

A methodological proposal is presented whose objective is to analyze social capital in a Smart City -SC through a Partial Least Square's Structural Equations Model -PLS SEM. The model conceives that the social capital is a capability of the SC, and its resources incise on the technological learning. The methodology is a mixed sequential exploratory type. Through the deductive reasoning method, the variables of the model are configured, which lead to the construction of an instrument that is tested with the users of the public urban bicycles system of Medellín 1 , EnCicla and subsequently, is refined as well as the model. The incidence of the technological learning is interpreted by relating the social capital resources (social interaction, trust and shared vision) the social capital sources (technological system) and the holders of the social capital (technological users). An exercise of scientiometrics is presented which shows the relation and gaps between the constructs and the process for the construction of the model. As a result, it is presented the construction of a theoretical model that represents the resources of the social capital capability and their relationship with the technological learning in a technological system at a SC
