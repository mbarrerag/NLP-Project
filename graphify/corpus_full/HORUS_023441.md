# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION BY GC/MS OF THE ESSENTIAL OIL OF HIRSUTA L. F.

The AE of L. hirsuta had a yield of 0.034% (p/p). 34 compounds in the AE were tentatively identified in relative quantity >0.1%. The Germanic seschyterpenic hydrocarbons D (26.3%), (E)-ß-cariofilene (24.9%), a-humulene (10.4%), ß-elemene (5.3%) and ß-bourbonene (3.4%) were the majority compounds of the AE. These compounds are used in the pharmaceutical industry, e.g., as nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, antimicrobials, antitumours, and in the flavour and fragrance industry
