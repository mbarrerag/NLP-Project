# ON A FRACTIONAL P-LAPLACIAN PROBLEM. EXISTENCE, A PRIORY ESTIMATES AND REGULARITY OF SOLUTIONS.

The aim of this talk is to present a brief introduction to the Fractional Sobolev Spaces which constitute the natural framework to deal with s-fractional p-Laplacian problems. In a certain way, the theory of Sobolev Fractional Spaces has been developed as a parallel theory to that of Sobolev Spaces: Fractional Sobolev Inequalities, Compact Embeddings, Holder Regularity, etc. As one of the most recent results on these fractional spaces and the fractional operator p-Laplacian, we mention the Hopf's lemma ([1]). In fact, we recently used this Lemma as an application to analyze the behavior of solutions to a non-local problem.
