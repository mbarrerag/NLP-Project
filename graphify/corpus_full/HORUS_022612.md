# CAMPESIGN WOMEN AND AGRO-FOOD SYSTEMS IN THE CONTEXT OF GLOBALIZATION

Around food there is a complex network (production, availability, distribution and access), which allows us to see the centrality of the agrifood sector in our lives. Feminist literature maintains that society has assigned to women the responsibility of caring for the family, where food is central, which implies a great amount of physical and emotional work. This is done despite its limited access to material and symbolic goods. It has also been pointed out that women actively participate in projects alternative to conventional models of production, distribution and consumption of food, linked to agroecology, as a female ethic of care. Therefore it is important to reflect on the link between gender and agrifood systems and contribute to understanding how the socio-environmental crisis we face today, affects not only the forms of production and consumption, but also deepens the unequal gender relations; we also want to understand how the processes and global policies affect the daily spaces and practices in plots, gardens, courtyards and kitchens.
