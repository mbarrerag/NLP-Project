# NUNOSTRUCTURAL TOPOLOGICAL MASSORANA MACHINERY MACHINERY IN LOW DIMENSIONALITY SYSTEMS

Topology is a mathematical concept that has opened new borders in the investigation of the behavior of electronic states in the field of condensed matter. The emergence of new systems with topological properties and the possibility of synthesizing them in complex heterostructures has aroused great interest in its possible application in spintronic and quantum computing. The wave function of these materials hides properties that allow generating states located in the border with high electronic mobility and robust before disturbances of their parameters. Of particular interest are the topological superconductors where these states are related to the so-called fermonies of Majorana, whose non-abelian statistics are useful for the construction of qbits as a basic element of quantum computing. The topological protection of these states is presented as an alternative to the parameters in quantum optics where the phenomena of decoherence can be more difficult to avoid. In this project we want to study joints and heterostructures based on nanoaldoms with induced superconduct by proximity effect. The topological protection of these states is presented as an alternative to the configuration of parameters that lead to topological superconductivity and thus favor the emergence of the modess of Majorana.
