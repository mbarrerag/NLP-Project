# ON THE MOVEMENT AND PHYSICAL PROPERTIES OF BRILLING PUNTOSMAGNETIC PUTOSGRAPHS IN SOLAR PHOTOSPHERE

The high resolution observations of the solar photosphere have revealed the existence of composite structures of tiny Bright Magnetic Points or MBPs. Such smallscale structures are associated with regions of strong magnetic field of the order of kilogauss (kG). Various researches have studied some of the physical properties of MBPs thanks to high resolution observations that allow at present to define details and evolution of such structures. This study makes an analysis of some of these dynamic parameters through the use of time series of images of the solar photosphere acquired with the high resolution solar telescope Earth, HiFI/GREGOR in the G (4308 Å) labanda and the Hinode space telescope. The following of MBPs is carried out to determine their own movements. Additionally, light curves are obtained that show their variation in intensity and analyze how the behavior of size and trajectory is in their lifetime.The results state that the measurements of dynamic parameters of MBPs are influenced by the instrument used, as the spatial change results are obtained
