# Teaching unit for teaching and learning mendelian genetics, based on Drosophila melanogaster, for the flexible thinking model.

Students often find it difficult to understand and learn genetics due to their complexity and abstract nature, which makes it one of the most challenging natural science themes for them. Because of this reason, the objective of this research was to develop a unit for teaching mendelian genetics using D. melanogaster as an educational tool for students of the Educational Institution Pedacito de Cielo in La Tebaida, Quindío, Colombia. For the development of this work, the previous knowledge of students on Mendelian genetics was identified, the unit was designed and implemented and the effect of the unit was assessed in the teaching-learning process in students. The unit included different types of educational aids, such as the use of technology, traditional and virtual laboratories developed in the STEAM classroom of the institution. In order to evaluate the didactic unit, an initial and final evaluation was used to quantify the transformations in the knowledge acquired by students.
