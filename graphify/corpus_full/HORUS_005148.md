# INDUSTRY 4.0: INTELIGENT PROCESS AND OPERATIONS IN CHEMICAL ENGINEERING

Colombian chemical engineering is constantly developing, the implementation of new technologies and strategies for the better use of resources such as energy and raw materials have generated a new way of carrying out procedures, and with this the innovation of different processes and bioprocesses. Industry 4.0 and chemical engineering intersects in an innovation horizon, integrating process improvements, generating intelligent operations and thus posing technological advances as a radical transformation in the way chemical processes are conceived, designed and executed, the transformation of raw materials, manufacturing, monitoring, supply chain, among others
