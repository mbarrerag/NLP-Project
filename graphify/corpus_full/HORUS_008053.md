# Vernacular architecture in the transition of habitat, towards the new paradigm of the construction of the 21st century: the case of the Bahareque houses in the municipality of Sincelejo, Sucre

The study of the vernacular architecture of the municipality of Sincelejo (Sucre) in the context of the evolution of the paradigm of the construction is an investigative bet that has the main objective to contribute to the revaluing of the technique of the baharejo within the emerging foundations of the technologies of the construction, the symbolic aspects and the valuations of heritage with the aim of achieving the increase of its spectrum for the materialization of future architectures in the municipality and extrapolable to the other zones of common use, which allows to find points of development for a technique that is commonly stigmatized but with strong sustainable nuances in the field of the environmental and the cultural. To this end, the perception of the population about the paradigm of the construction and its evolutionary processes in materials and techniques, the constructive weaknesses of the houses of Bahareque existing in the municipality, with the aim of indicating, finally, a series of guidelines for the optimization of the technique. These objectives are carried out by means of a descriptive type research with qualitative approach, using the participatory observation, semistructured interviews and groups of the discussion. The agreement between the two parties is based on the principle of the right to be informed by the parties. of a contextual, sustainable and economically viable architecture
