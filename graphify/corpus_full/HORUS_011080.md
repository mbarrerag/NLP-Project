# STAPHYLOCOCCUS AUREUS IN PEDIATRIA

From the identification and original characterization of Staphylococcus aureus, to the development of new therapies for its treatment and the possibility of developing a vaccine, the path that has been travelled in the knowledge of this microorganism and its characteristics has been long and full of ups and downs. Known as a positive grampositive, catalase and coagulase coconut, which can be from a colonizing microorganism of asinto-matic course to the potential cause of sequelae and mortality through multiple clinical entities such as bacteremia, osteomyelitis, necrotizing pneumonia and endocarditis, S. aureus is a bacterium of special interest to the general physician and pediatrician
