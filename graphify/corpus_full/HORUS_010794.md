# PUBLIC POLICY IN PALIANIC CARE AND INVOLVEMENTS ON SERVICES, OPIOIDS AND EDUCATION IN PUBLIC POLICY IN PALIAN CARE AND ITS IMPLICATIONS ON SERVICES, OPIOIDS AND EDUCATION IN COLOMBIA

The development of palliative care requires the intervention of multiple dimensions of public health, including the availability of health services, essential medicines and educational programs. In Colombia, various changes have been made in public policies to promote the care of people with palliative needs. Empirically evaluate the public policies, existing in palliative care and their implications on the availability of services, opioids and educational programs in the years 2010 2019 in Colombia. A sequential exploratory mixed study was designed in three phases: identification of empirical indicators of national policies, situational diagnosis of palliative care and qualitative evaluation of the results of the implementation of policies in seven territorial nodes in Colombia. Seven standards were reviewed, obtaining 12 empirical indicators for evaluation, six of which did not have sources of information.
