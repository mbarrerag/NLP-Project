# EVALUATION OF METHODS FOR SCREW-VERTEBRA FIXATION USING FINITE ELEMENT MODELING

Lumbar spine diseases treatments selection still a process without consensus that requires understanding the biomechanical behavior of vertebrae in interaction with fixation techniques [1]. Employing Finite Element Method (FEM) as modeling and analysis source is a way for this purpose of development of knowledge to make decisions. Nevertheless, FEM is well known by its dependence on mesh and geometry, and suppose defining the approach for optimal results (Avoid overestimated values) considering available resources, whether simplifying the geometry or constraint method [2]. The aim of this study is to evaluate two constraint approaches for screw fixation in lumbar spine by FEM
