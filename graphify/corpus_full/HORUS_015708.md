# Diagnosis of effort estimation in agile methods for software development in Colombia.

The estimation of the software development effort (EEDS) is fundamental to planning and controlling software and critical projects for its success. Practice has shown that the establishment of realistic objectives, precise estimates and good planning and control are essential activities for the success of the project. In addition, Agile Software Development (DAS) is today the most used model to organize all the activities required for software construction. This research work focuses on knowing how Colombian professionals make effort estimates within agile software development projects. For this purpose, a study of the state of academic art in topics related to estimating software for agile developments within Colombia and other countries was carried out, with the aim of being able to compare our practices with those of other regions. Subsequently, an exploratory survey was carried out to Colombian professionals with experience in estimation of effort for agile development. The design of the survey was based on similar studies identified worldwide in order to obtain comparable results. This survey was the instrument used to gather information, while a combination of qualitative and quantitative analysis was used to interpret the results; it is intended to know the state of practice in Colombia with regard to the results of the study, which are based on the techniques used to estimate of the accuracy of the results
