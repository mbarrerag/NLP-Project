# Technical and economic benefits of battery-based energy storage systems for the provision of complementary services in the Colombian electricity system.

This document presents a state of the art of energy storage systems (SAS), where the different types, characteristics and considerations to take into account of this type of elements are presented, together with the study of the different applications and benefits that SAE can offer. It proposes and evaluates the formulation for energy arbitration in the Colombian electrical system through battery-based energy storage systems (SAEB), which includes elements such as battery degradation and the cost generated by such degradation. Furthermore, given the benefits of SAEBs to provide multiple services, a co-optimization model is proposed that allows to evaluate the simultaneous participation of energy storage systems (SAEB) in arbitration applications, energy reserve and frequency regulation. The models are evaluated using historical data from the Colombian wholesale energy market. Two scenarios, related to renewable penetration, are analyzed. In addition, for each model, a financial evaluation is made, in which one case is analyzed, from the system point of view of the system and from the point of view of the reverse.
