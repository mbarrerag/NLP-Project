# PORCOVID-19 BURNOUT. The foundation of the National University of the United States of Colombia in 1867 occurs almost a decade after the publication of Darwin's Origin of Species (1859) and the publication of Virchow's Die Cellular pathologie (1859), texts representative of the two basic theories that underpin Moderna biology: the theory of evolution by natural selection and the cellular theory. Analyzing the primary and secondary historical sources, we determined that the School of Natural Sciences taught cell theory between 1868 and 1875. Francisco Bayón's botany course (1868) explained this theory, including the proposals of Schleiden, Mirbel, Möhl and Unger; as well as the processes of pollination and fertilization of plants. The out-of-date in some respects is explained by the fact that Bayón's main sources are texts published in Spain and France and by the absence of translations of the pioneering works that emerged in Germany. The School of Natural Sciences took a step from natural history to Moderna biology by including in its courses the cell theory.

Objective: The objective of this study was to make visible the mental health impacts of university youth in Colombia, in the framework of the confinement policies taken by the impact of Coronavirus, from March 2020 to October 2020, who had education mediated by technology and limitations in their studies. a self-administered application and sampling of snowball type of the one-dimensional scale of student burnout and stress indicators were carried out to 528 university students in the Colombian territory, to verify the level of syndrome and stress in the study population.Conclusions: high perceived levels of stress and anxiety resulting from social distancing and the inability to attend educational centres, as well as academic difficulties originated. Students who filled the survey demonstrated that they felt limited and that the situation of both confinement and anxiety and fear had affected their studies.The three dimensions of burnout (exhaustion, cynicism and inefficiency) were found in a positive and obvious way, the students who experienced high levels of anxiety that affected the quality of life of young people and their mental and physical health
