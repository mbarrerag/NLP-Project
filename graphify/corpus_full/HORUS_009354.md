# HOLE DOPING OF THE BI2PB0.6SR1.4(CA2-XYXCU3O10+D.

We performed structural, electrical transport and magnetic properties of pellets of thesystem Bi2 Pb0.6Sr1.4(Ca2-xYx)Cu3O10+d with hole doping concentration x = 0.3, 0.7 and 0.9. X-raydiffractograms show peaks corresponding to the Bi-2212 and Bi-2223 superconducting phases andreminiscent of the powder precursors. We find a superconducting transition for samples with x=0.3 and 0.7 at 70 and 75 K respectively, while for x= 0.9, an insulating phase is found. Magneticmeasurements are consistent with the observed resistance properties and suggest that Y substitutionincrease the overall diamagnetic response. In addition, moment vs. field curves show signatures ofthe nucleation of superconducting vortices. From all these results we infer there is an optimum valueof hole doping that stabilize a superconducting phase in the BSPCYCO system
