# Free-of-interference lobbying in the middle of the flood to follow the BIODIESEL challenge by the transfer of cold-off deposits

In order to trace the production of biodiesel in a transesterification catalyzed by the basis of used oils of frying and methanol, the spectra in the medium infrared were acquired by means of total reflectance attenuated with Fourier transform (ATR-FTIR) from the organic phases isolated from the reaction medium at 0, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes from the beginning of the process and were qualitatively analyzed in comparison with the IR spectrum of the oil used in its initial condition. Among all the signals or regions of the infrared spectrum proposed by other authors as suitable to monitor the content of biodiesel in mixtures, which were actually observed in these ATR-FTIR spectra, the signal at 1195 cm−1, attributable to the stretching of the link between carbon sp2 and oxygen in the methyl carboxylate, -(CO)-OCH3, resulted the most appropriate signal to follow the biodiesel intensity in the reaction medium through the corresponding organic isolated mode of the IR spectrum.
