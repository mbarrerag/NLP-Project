# Factors that influence the time that passes until a patient enters due to respiratory problems to the emergency room in San Vicente de Chucurí, Santander

The methods of analysis of survival are used to examine changes over time in a specific event (Dudley et al., 2016). To study the time that elapses until an event occurs, it has become relevant in scientific studies, especially for researchers in the area of health. The adequate analysis of this time helps to prevent diseases and analyzes progress or effectiveness of treatments of diseases, strokes or health problems and can therefore have a great impact on society. In this work, it is proposed, through statistical methods such as Kaplan-Meier (Kaplan Meier, 1958) and the model of proportional risks of Cox (1972), to identify the influential factors for a person from Saint Vincent de Chucurí, Santander to access the emergency service due to respiratory problems. .
