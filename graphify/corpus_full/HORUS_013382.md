# A critical approach to the concept of human capital based on the design and implementation of the National Qualifications Framework in Colombia (2010-2022)

The preponderance of the theory of human capital since the middle of the last century to explain the nature and dynamics of the educational process Thuemployment has led to multiple transformations in the educational systems of the states around the world. Colombia has not been alien to this process and in the last two decades has established an education policy that in its postsecondary level is characterized by the binomial between social equity and economic competitiveness. Thus, the rate of gross coverage of postsecondary education has increased significantly while the links between the education sector and the requirements of human capital formation and the needs of the labour market and economic competitiveness have been strengthened. In this context, the National System of Qualifications (SNC) and the National Qualifications Framework (NQF) have emerged as one of its components in order to close the gaps of human capital and promote the learning as a whole of life.
