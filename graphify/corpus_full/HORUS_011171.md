# CHAPTER 1 GENERAL ASPECTS OF THE FRESH FRUIT SECTOR IN THE VALLEY OF THE CAUCA

The main objective of this first chapter is to contextualize the reader on the importance of the fresh fruit sector in the Valle del Cauca, to this end present the historical series between 2017 and 2020 of the production levels in tons and hectares harvested from fresh fruits in Cali, Buenaventura and Tuluá, in addition to presenting the departmental total and its relative weight with respect to national production. The idea of this study is to address the entire production chain of the fresh fruit sector in the Valle del Cauca, starting from the suppliers and nurseries, continuing with the producers and ending with agro-industrialists and marketers, addressing in parallel actors that directly or indirectly influence the production chain such as academic, governmental, private and association organizations. That is why the approach is not limited exclusively to the production of fresh fruits in the department, but if it is a good starting point to identify the productive chains with a greater weight and impacting the largest amount of Vallecaucana agricultural population
