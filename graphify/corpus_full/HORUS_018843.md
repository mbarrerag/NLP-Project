# ADVANCES TO THE NATIONAL DIALOGUE

PRESENTATION: On May 5, 2021 the rectors of seven Colombian universities, including the University of Antioquia1, spoke publicly recognizing the social movement in progress "as an expression of the most felt needs of society"; an inclusive, plural and diverse movement that, with the national strike of April 28, 2021, resumed the path of the demonstrations initiated in 2019 with demands that included, among others, the protection of social leaders, the fulfillment of agreements reached in 2018 by students and university professors with the national government, the withdrawal of the labor, pension and tax reforms in the making and the implementation of the peace agreement of 2016. In its pronouncement, known as a university charter to the Colombian nation, the rectors made a call "to strengthen the broad, inclusive and honest dialogue" on some "structural and urgent issues"2 and invited the academic community to seek convergences, propose solutions to the crisis "and build a Colombia in peace, democratic, equitable, inclusive and committed to sustainable development.
