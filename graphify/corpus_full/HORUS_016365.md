# EXTERNAL STRENGTHENING WITH BAMBU STERILE, SIMPLE MAMPOSTRY OF CROSS LAND ADOBE UNITS OR COOKED ARCHITLE HUECCO LOCKS, FOR IMPROVING THE BEHAVIOR OF LIVING BEFORE SISMS

The self-construction of housing from walls made with units of raw earth adobes or blocks of clay cooked from horizontal drilling, is common in rural or peripheral areas of urban areas, because of its low cost. However, this simple masonry, as is known by not having steel reinforcement, is highly vulnerable to seismic events by having low ductility and presenting fragile faults. The problem is greater, considering that a high number of houses of this type, are in areas of high or intermediate seismic risk. Being the sterilla of bamboo, easy to obtain and low cost, could be used as external reinforcement of walls. To study the differences in mechanical behavior, were performed tests of compression and diagonal traction in muretes of units of uncooked land, without and with external reinforcement of both sides of bamboo and complete reinforcement of the block of clay was observed
