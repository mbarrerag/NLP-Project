# Interpretation of Sanguinean Arterial Gases: Review of the subject

ABSTRACT Arterial gases are fundamental tools for objectively evaluating patients with acute and chronic pathologies, since they provide information on the rates of oxygenation, ventilation, acid-base balance and oxygen supply at the cellular level. Interpreting them properly has great clinical importance, since it influences decision-making allows the evaluation of the response to therapeutic interventions. This article aims to present the reader with the basic concepts of the physiology of the gaseous exchange, the most common acid-base disorders and a practical interpretation of arterial gases. A combination of graphic illustrations, tables and algorithms is used to facilitate the understanding and clinical approach of patients with respiratory and metabolic disorders
