# Soil degradation: an active learning didactic sequence for agricultural technical secondary education

This paper describes the design of a didactic sequence of active learning on the processes of land degradation and its implementation with students of eleventh grade belonging to the agricultural technical secondary education programme in the IED the victory located in the municipality of El Colegio. The methodology used is based on action research, applied to the exercise of learning of the participants, in order to reflect and transform the educational practice through an action plan consisting of four phases: problem detection, planning, implementation and evaluation and feedback. During the execution of the project, it was possible to demonstrate the development of argumentative skills through the implementation of active technical education strategies, which in turn allowed to account for the structuring of information in the construction of knowledge related to the processes of soil degradation. The result of the process was the letter of the didactic sequence entitled “degradation of soils, a didactic proposal of active learning for the medium to education, agriculture, with the aim of generating educational strategy that allows to address the problem and reflect around it. . knowledge related to the processes of degradation of the floor.
