# RENACUAJES HILID RENACUAJES OF HISTORY

This guide allows the identification of the larva species of the Hylidae family on the island of Hispaniola in the field. It also helps to differentiate them from other invasive species such as Aquarana catesbeiana and Rhinella marina. The document contains texts, detailed diagrams of the diagnostic morphological characters of the tadpoles, an identification key and descriptive sheets with schematics. It also includes images and technical descriptions of eggs, larvae and adults
