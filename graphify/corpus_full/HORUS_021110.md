# Fintech against borders: A study on the role of digital financial tools in regional and border contexts.

This project aims to understand the adoption of financial technologies on the Amazon border, from the characterization and study of the factors that determine their proliferation or limitation, in view of the identification of the particular needs of the inhabitants of this region, the observation of the dynamics specific to the Amazon border and the recognition of opportunities in favour of the communities and the financial sector. In this process it will be necessary to take into account that this topic is regulated from the law, but its study is particularly relevant to economic disciplines, in addition to the fact that it is foreseen the need to adopt methods specific to sociology and anthropology with which to understand the type of population interacting in this territory and its trends in field study. This multidisciplinary perspective will be especially valuable in the understanding on the impact of Fintech, a relatively recent technology, but that has shown to offer a wide range of possibilities and solutions to the needs of the sector and communities, as demonstrated by the rapid development that has been experienced by industry, with annual growth rates of 24.8 per cent since 2018 (Maestre, 2022)
