# TOWARDS ENVIRONMENTAL PUBLIC MANAGEMENT AND POLICY

The present chapter aims to reflect and propose some theoretical elements for the construction of an environmental epistemology based on a new ethical, scientific, technological and productive paradigm of knowledge and experience, which allows the formation and development of true and legitimate institutional frameworks for public management and policies (States), which guarantee multidimensional sustainability (institutional, economic, social and environmental) and lead the way to sustainable development of each nation
