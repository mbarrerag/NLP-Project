# A GENERALIZED POHOZAEV IDENTITY: SOME APPLICATIONS

The Pohozaev Identity stated in [2] and many others of this kind have been used, since their first apparience in 1940 [3], to prove nonexistence results as well as to investigate the properties of the solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations. The aim of this talk is to show an adaptation of the generalized Pohozaev Identity established in [1] to a semipositone problem and, how can we use this to prove an existence result. Indeed we turned our attention to the radial problem $\Delta_{\phi} u=\lambda f(u)$ in a ball with homogeneous Dirichlet condition.} References [1] N. Kawano, W. M. Ni and S. Yotsutani. A generalized Pohozaev identity and its applications. journal of the mathematical society of japan, 42(3), 541-564, 1990. [2] S. I. Pohozaev, Eigenfunctions of the equations $\Delta u+\lambda f(u)=0$, Soviet Math. Dokl., 5 (1965), 1408-1411. [3] H. Rellich, Darstellung der eigenwerte von $\Delta u+\lambda u=0$ darch ein randintegral, Math. Z., 46 (1940), 635-636
