# Excited state processes of N-salicylidene anthracene Schiff bases in acetonitrile solution. A transient absorption spectroscopy study

White-light femtosecond pump−probe has been used to study the photodynamics of 1- and 2-((anthracen-2-ylimino)-methyl)phenol in acetonitrile solution. The early evolution of the transient absorption spectra shows sub-500 fs components, which are assigned to the excited state intramolecular proton transfer (ESIPT) and the twisted-enol isomerization reactions. In this study, both processes are characterized within ultrashort time scale and the general photoinduced mechanism could be devised. We show that, for both Schiff bases, the ESIPT reaction occurs in the sub-200 fs domain, while the rotation to the twisted trans-enol occurs within 500 fs. Subsequently to the ESIPT reaction, cooling from the hot cis-keto* to the relaxed S 1 cis-keto form takes place with a time constant of ≤ 608 fs, with following relaxation decay in the tens of picoseconds to both the cis-keto ground state and the final trans-keto photochromic product
