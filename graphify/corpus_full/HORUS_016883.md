# THEORICAL ISSUES OF MAQUINATE

The distinguishing and complementary features of this second edition are varied with respect to the first edition, and these merit being recorded in the introduction of this publication. First, it is remarkable the insertion of contemporary themes of experimental micro-scale machining research and in the multi-axis milling scenario of difficult-machineability materials such as Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy. This panorama is treated with the experimental modelling and validation of generation of the cross-section of STC cut as a certain footprint of the CCZ contact zone and with this approach is presented the treatment of the integrated technological parameter of machinability that is weighted with various metrics referring to the superficial quality integrity of the phylo-tool and the piece, as well as allowing to assess the energy and technological efficiency of the machining
