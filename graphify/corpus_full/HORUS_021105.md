# PROTOTYPIES AND METHODOLOGIES FOR DESIGN FOR SOLUTIONS OF RURAL VIC (CULTURE INTEREST VID) AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP WITH THE CULTUREL PAISAGE AND TRADITIONAL LIVING: CASES OF STUDY LIVING CAMPINE STAFF IN GIRON, SANTANDER, WAYU ANCESTRAL LIVING OF THE HIGH GUAYIRA AND THE PANELER PRODUCTIVE LIVING OF THE EAST ANTI-QUEÑO.

In line with the seventh macro problem of the new proposal for curricular harmonization of the Faculty of Architecture of the Medellín Headquarters, the field/new rurality, this project proposes the study of traditional rural housing and its relationship with the cultural context, recognizing the domestic place as the sacred space that argues and justifies the existence of a cultural landscape. The domestic ritual that is repeated from generation to generation in traditional families, determines its culture, and its vision of the environment, conditions that in turn gives added value to different spaces according to the function given to it by its inhabitants. In addition, it contemplates the critical study of the new regulations of (VIC) housing of cultural interest and how through it new ruralities are affected, and the same architectures of these contexts, which have an inherent relationship with the productive chains in the home. In relation to the above, it is proposed to project three prototypes of housing that express cultural and landscape readings in the different contexts of each case of study to give justification to the importance of the preservation and dissemination of traditional rural housing in the context of cultural landscape, argued under the concept of housing of cultural interest
