# Non-Alcoholic Fatty Hepatic Disease (non-Alcoholic Fatty Hygn)

Epidemiological, pathophysiological, diagnostic and therapeutic description of a frequent condition called Non-alcoholic fatty liver and known as NAFLD (Non Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease). The disease includes the passive infiltration of triglyceride and VLDL fractions into the cytoplasm of liver cells, usually related to obesity, metabolic syndrome and insulin resistance. The disease may evolve into steathepatitis, in which inflammation of the hepatic parenchyma is evident and accompanied by elevation of transaminases and; finally, progression to irreversible fibrosis or cirrhosis, which is the genesis of hepatocellular carcinoma, may occur. The chapter includes a systematic review of the literature and provides clinical experience of the authors, as physicians treating patients with this clinical condition
