# Adult care with chronic illness: principles for nursing practice

The Latin American Network of Care for the Chronic Patient and the Family (Red CroniFam) created in 2000 was derived from the Master's Program in Nursing. This book arises from the meetings around the Network at international events, with an academic interest and as a way to materialize the professional experience of its members for the new generations in the care of adult with chronic illness and their caregivers. Talking about principles is an inspiration in the need for nursing to return to the basics, to the essence of what we do. The authors of the twenty chapters thought thus, at the starting point from what is known in the subject and what will be brought forth, some reflective questions and basic concepts that give a theoretical context and closes with a case or situation of nursing that invite to the reader's reflection, along with some critical thinking activities and recommended readings.-- Contracáda
