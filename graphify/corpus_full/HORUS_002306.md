# Gentrification, popular economies and ways of inhabiting Black and Afro-descendant people in the Cartagena Historic Center of Indias

The project seeks to analyze the migration and adaptation processes of Afro-descendant populations that have arrived in Cartagena since the 1990s, emphasizing the voices of the actors who have experienced the process of insertion into the city. Cartagena, as an economic and cultural center, has attracted waves of migrants from the surrounding regions due to their concentration of job opportunities, economic, educational and forced displacement by armed conflict. Afro-descendant populations have been among the most affected in this process and have faced challenges such as racial exclusion and marginalization, despite this they have sought to put in place strategies to reinvent their ways of living adapting them to the territory and seeking to insert themselves in it while maintaining links with their territories of origin.
