# Algebraic properties of weak quantum symmetries.

This thesis investigates the properties of weak bialgebras (weak bialgebras) and weak Hopf algebras (weak Hopf algebras), their (co) representations and applications in groupoids, algebras of roads and algebras of Lie. Research uses algebraic and categorical techniques to explore the fundamental properties of these structures, establishing connections between algebraic and categorical frameworks, and addressing open problems related to their actions in non-conmutative graduate algebras. Combining theoretical findings and practical examples, this work improves our understanding of weak Hopf algebras as generators of symmetry and their wider implications in various mathematical contexts. Our results contribute to the field of non-conmutative algebra and Hopf algebras, paving the way for future research in these areas
