# Measurement Campaign for 5G Communications in Greenhouse Scenarios: Tropicario Bogotá Botanical Garden José Celestino Mutis

A measurement campaign was developed to determine how path loss models proposed for 5G by Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) are applicable in Greenhouse scenarios. This campaign was focused on mm waves regime, so 28 GHz frequency was used. In order to accomplish that, a wireless transmission system was developed, measurements were taken in the Tropicario greenhouses at Jose Celestino Mutis Botanical Garden, setting different distances and orientations
