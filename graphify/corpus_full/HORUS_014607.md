# Internal and external aspects of continuous logic and categorical logic for sheaves over quantals

In this text we explore and propose notions of beams on quantal commutative and integral, based on extensions of results of the theory of beams on locals: the interaction of beams as valued sets and the analogy of beams as enriched categories. On these proposals, we define logics that find their semantics in these beam type objects; on the one hand, a categorical logic that characterizes the notion of beams associated with whole valued sets as a model of a certain internal construction, and in contrast, an externally defined logic whose nature is based on continuous logic for metric spaces, which finds in the proposal of beams as enriched categories a structure to interpret their semantics
