# Nursing care at discharge from the emergency department: scope review

To describe nursing interventions in the plan for adult discharge in the emergency service. Review of scope developed according to the criteria proposed by the Joanna Briggs Institute (JBI) for the preparation of a survey. 49 publications were compiled that allowed to determine nursing interventions in the plan for adult discharge in the Emergency Service. The characterization showed that the topic has been constantly investigated in the last ten years with a predominance of English language publication. From the analysis of content three categories emerged: Nursing prepares the user in the institution; Nursing accompanies the user outside the institution and Nursing projects and proposes the plan for discharge.
