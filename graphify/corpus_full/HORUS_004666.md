# Relationship between the expression of ID1 and ID3 and the immune tumor microenvironment of the bone marrow in adults with acute lymphoblastic leukemia of precursor cells B

The diagnosis of acute lymphoblastic leukaemia of B cell precursors (BCP-ALL) is a condition that generally presents an unfavourable prognosis. Previous research identified the ID1 and ID3 genes as predictors of poor response in Colombian adult patients with BCP-ALL, genes that play a crucial role in various processes related to cancer development. In particular, in other cancer models, ID1 and ID3 were associated with the presence of immune populations that are regulatory in the tumour immune microenvironment (TIME).
