# CERVICAL VERTEBRAL MATURATION STAGE DETERMINATION WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, PILOT STUDY

The aim of this research was to evaluate the precision in the determination of the cervical vertebral maturation stage (CVS), in lateral skull radiographs, through the use of artificial intelligence (AI) methods. A sample of 489 lateral skull radiographs of growing children between 5 and 19 years old were used. The sample was taken from the dental radiology service of National University of Colombia (n=271) and Craniofacial Growth Legacy Collection Project of the American Association of Orthodontists Foundation (AAOF)(n=218). The radiographs were labeled and classified on the Labelbox platform, to design the contours of three cervical vertebrae (CV2,CV3 and CV4), one of the six stages of the cervical vertebral maturation stage (CVS), were assigned according to the method described by Baccetti et al. in 2005, by consensus between three evaluators. A MobilNet algorithm were used to train a model based on Deep Learning through a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN), to determine the stage of cervical development. The 70% of the sample were used for training CNN and a 15% for testing the precision of algorithm. The training parameters were 50 epoch, 16 batch size and learning rate of 0.001. For the first three stages (CVS1, CVS2, CVS3), the precision values were: 0.88, 0.50 and 0.50 respectively; for CVS4 and CVS6 the precision value decreased to 0.40, with CVS5 having the lowest precision to 0.38. The confusion matrix of the deep learning model based on CNN shows low dispersion of the estimates with values of the CVS very close to the true value (Figure 1). The developed model showed the ability to determine CVS, however, the current model still needs improvements to allow its use in clinical settings. Using more radiographs and decrease the region of interest (ROI) in the images could improve the performance of the model
