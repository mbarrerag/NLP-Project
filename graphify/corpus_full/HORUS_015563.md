# REFERRED TO IN PERINATAL LOSSES FOR THE GOVERNING PAIR

The perinatal loss is a public health problem that affects infant mortality, and affects the state of mental health of parents, aggravated by the cultural invisibility that gives rise to an unauthorised problem.The importance responds to few researches that allow to show the problem at regional level from the perspective of the meaning of perinatal loss for the pregnant couple, as a contribution to the humanized care of this problem. Describe the meaning of perinatal loss in a group of mothers and fathers with a history of this. corresponds to a qualitative descriptive study of ethnographic type, which adopted the methodological approach of the nursing ethno proposed by Leininger and Macfarland Results: the invalidation of the pain derived from the loss, both by the health team and by the relatives, gave rise to complicated processes of grief and the other members of the family, which remained in the time and interfered with their disposition for post-loss pregnancies, as well as with the socio-work performance.The conclusions show the high risk of the perinatal loss for the mental health of the parents and the family and the other members of the family group
