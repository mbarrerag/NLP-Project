# ANALYTICAL TECHNICAL EQUALITIES FOR THE MEASUREMENT OF ANIONES AND QUATIONS AVAILABLE IN SUELOS

This study aims to evaluate the equivalence and influence of soil properties in the measurement of iron, copper, sulphates and phosphates using different analytical techniques. Measurements of these nutrients were carried out in 40 soil samples with different physicochemical characteristics, using techniques such as inductively coupled plasma, ion chromatography, UV-Vis spectrophotometry and atomic absorption. In addition, eight chemical parameters of the soil were evaluated, additional in each sample. The error was determined between the results obtained by the different techniques for the mentioned nutrients, finding values of up to 50%. Correlation analysis and main components (PCA) were performed to identify relationships and variability patterns in the data set.
