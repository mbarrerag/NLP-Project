# Systematic review of literature and meta-analysis for research priorities in marketing

This project will carry out Systematic Reviews of Literature (RSL) and Meta-analysis in cases where it is possible to apply this technique to the research priorities raised for 2022. 2024 by the Marketing Science Institute (MSI), the Syrema protocol will be validated for the case of the RSL, besides seeking to obtain a policy recommendation document, for at least one of the 7 lines of research drawn by the MSI, as well as, will seek to update the lines of research of the postgraduates of the Faculty of Economic Sciences in the field of marketing and will suggest lines of work for the programs of subject matter of undergraduate and postgraduate
