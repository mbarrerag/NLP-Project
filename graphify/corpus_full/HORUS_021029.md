# - A palace of memory. How is everyday sacred?

_xvacuum we know_A palace of memory How is the everyday sacredness?" is a project of the thesis that happens in the framework of the Master's degree in Visual and Plastic Arts and that has the aim to do an investigation on memory as central theme, starting from the daily acts as object of analysis for a reflection on the family genealogy. Throughout the master's degree I have developed a taste for the definition of memorabilia. Images or memorable acts within our daily life. The idea starts from seeing the house as a contingency space of memory, of people and as we are going to be able to contain that impossibility of collecting all our history. Today, society has lost the faith and its gaze upwards, which is the sacred, which we adore. Now we look at the base, what we are going to do and we sacred it. We understand the importance of the absence and we are amassing our memories, appropriate and past. We are the mixture of all that remains and that is fragile. A fragility because we can break and we need to look for that only thing that will be able to do the world, stop, we are doing it. They were the center and the three symbols of this work. My grandmother was the archivist, the one who unconsciously made a museum of all of us, of her and of what was around her, I collect in her machine of sewing unimaginable treasures creating the largest archive of my family. My aunt was the forgeter. A strong Alzheimer’s, hard and disarticulating. The disease took away her language, language, words, speech. I speak from the personal and from what I end up being the framework of this project. Three key words of my work: The absence, the archive and the forgetfulness. I leave here to talk about the care of memory, or a memory of the acts of care, those who mark what touch and give symbolism to the objects. Those who fill the house with useless but totally significant things. I start from there and work useless but totally memorable ones that are in each of our homes. How to preserve the heat of a house, how I predict their story and I speak about it and I want to make it a stop
