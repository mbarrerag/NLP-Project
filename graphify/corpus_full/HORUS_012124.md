# COLOMBIAN DISEASES PERCEPTION ON A HEALTHFUL LABOUR ENVIRONMENT FOR ASSISTANCE PRACTICE IN THE HOSPITAL FIELD.

to explore the perception of Colombian professional nurses on the definition and main characteristics of a healthy working environment (ESL) for nursing care practice in the hospital setting. qualitative descriptive study. Semi-structured interviews were conducted with nine professional nurses recruited through non-probabilistic sampling. Interviews were recorded, transcribed and literally codified. Through the organization and structuring of these codes, categories and subcategories were established. from the analysis of the data it was possible to establish a definition of healthy working environment and two dimensions (categories) and ten main characteristics (subcategories) of the concept were obtained. the findings of this study allow a better understanding of the perception of Colombian nurses about the definition and essential characteristics of an SLA for nursing practice in the hospital setting. These findings contribute to the development of measuring tools of the SLA that conform to the conditions and reality of professional exercise in the Colombian hospital setting
