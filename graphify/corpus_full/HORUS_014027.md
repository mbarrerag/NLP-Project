# Integration of language- and electronic nose-based sensors with applications in the food industry

The integration of languages and electronic noses into a single device can have several justifications, including: Greater precision in the detection of substances: by combining the detection capabilities of both devices, greater precision can be obtained in the identification of chemicals and organic compounds present in the environment. Greater versatility: by having a single device integrating both technologies, a more versatile and adaptable tool can be available to different situations and environments. Cost and space reduction: by integrating both technologies into a single device, the cost and space required to have two separate devices can be reduced. Greater efficiency: by having a single device integrating both technologies, greater efficiency can be achieved in sampling and in the analysis of chemicals and organic compounds present in the environment. In any case, the integration of sensors supporting the monitoring or quality control process is a necessity in the food industry as the development of robust systems that merge sensors and data analysis can give added value to the guaranteed quality of the final product process. In this sense, this project is oriented to the development of a device that allows the integration of these two types of systems into one process with the aim of providing quality data for the process
