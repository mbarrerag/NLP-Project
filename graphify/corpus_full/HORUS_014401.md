# Characterization of the sentinel lymph node to determine the micro-metases presence.

The sentinel lymph node is a predictor of aggressiveness of breast cancer. The reported instantaneous risk index was 2.14, 95% confidence interval, showing that patients with micrometastases (MM) have a higher probability of a worse disease-free survival (SSE) and overall survival (SG) compared to those with negative ganglia, early detection of micrometastases analysis seems to be the most advantageous approach for patients. This work proposes an automatic detection of micrometastases by quantifying local cell changes. The proposed strategy characterizes the morphometry, color and texture of the nuclei to establish differences between MM and normal tissue.
