# VINE MALINO POLICY: THE CALENDAR OF TRUMP TO LATIN AMERICA

In the chapter “Is the politics of evil neighbor: Trump’s agenda for Latin America?”, Professor Diana Marcela Rojas addresses the main issues of U.S. policy towards LAC under the presidency of the Republican president. Hers was a diplomacy characterized by a confrontational and erratic style, in which old-fashioned prejudices, partisan interests and domestic electoral calculations prevailed, over traditional alliances and growing interdependence. The work wonders whether the Trump administration was just an unfortunate interregno, or even if it can be considered as part of a longer-term trend that marks the decline of American influence in the region
