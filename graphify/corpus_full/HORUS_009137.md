# IDENTIFICATION OF PLAGUICIDES IN THE FRUIT OF THE PIÑAS RECOLECTED IN THE CITY OF CALI SANTIAGO, BY GASEOUS CRIMATOGRAPHY AND HIGH RESOLUTION LIQUID CRIMATOGRAPHY POPULATED TO MASS SPECTROMETRY

The purpose of the project is to investigate the presence of agrotoxic compounds in pineapple samples marketed in the city of Santiago de Cali, collected at mass gathering points. The pineapple is one of the most consumed fruits in the region and the country, but for its optimal production the application of agrotoxics is required to prevent and repell pests and diseases. The extraction of the analytes of interest and the cleaning of the matrix is carried out by means of the QuEChERS method in dispersed phase. For the determination of pesticides, the techniques of liquid chromatography coupled with mass spectrometry and chromatography of gases coupled with mass spectrometry will be used. Results are expected to demonstrate the existence or not of pesticide residues in the analyzed pineapple samples. Various studies demonstrate residuality of pesticides in food grown in different parts of the world, as well as the risks of this type of agrotoxic compounds have been demonstrated for animal and human health, causing poisoning and damage to the nervous system.
