# AGROECOLOGY AS AN EFFECTIVE MECHANISM TO POW THE MULTIFUNCTIONALITY OF SIAL: THE CASE OF SIALS IN COLOMBIA

The panela in Colombia is an important traditional food produced in Agrofood Systems Localized from concentrations of rural agro-industrial industries that, under the family farming scheme, develop activities of cultivation and transformation of sugar cane. The SIALs of panela perform functions in the social, ecological and economic dimensions, which are not recognized in the market price, nor are they explicitly recognized by the different social actors; however, through a study of three of the main SIALs of panela in the country, under an agro-ecological approach, several functions linked to the SIALs were identified, with different emphasis and specificities
