# PREVALENCE OF OBSTRUCTIVE OBSTRUCTIVE DARK ANNEE IN AIR HYPERTENSION PATIENTS

To estimate the prevalence of obstructive sleep apnea in patients diagnosed with arterial hypertension. Obstacle study, in 449 patients with diagnosis of arterial hypertension from the Chronic Care and Follow-up Program of the State Social Company Hospital of El Tambo, Cauca. The Epworth somnolence scale and the previously validated STOP-bang test were administered. The study population had an average age of 67 years, with 67.3% women. The average body mass index was 26.8, the mean neck circumference was 36 cm, the mean waist circumference was 96 cm and 23.6% moderate daytime sleep, while in 5.3% it was severe. The mean of antihypertensive drugs used was 2, and 7.1 % had uncontrolled hypertension. A score of 3 or higher in the STOP-bang test was found in 32.8 % of participants. STOP-bang was positive for sleep obstructive medications in subjects with uncontrolled arterial pressure (p= 0,005), the upper body mass index was greater than 35 %
