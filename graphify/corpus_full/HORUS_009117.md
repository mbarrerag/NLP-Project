# POLIMORFISMS OF CASEINS AND ß-LACTOGLOBULIN IN BLANK BOVINES (BON) OF COLOMBIA

The white cattle have been aged 12 years (BON) is a genetic resource of Colombia, with characteristics of adaptation to the mountain tropics. It is one of the locally adapted cattle most used in Colombia, with double purpose characteristics. Although it is one of the most researched breeds of Creoles, there is little clarity about the frequency of alleleic variables in caseins (CSN) and ß-lactoglobulin (ß-LG). Considering that some of these variants are of economic importance for their relation to production, yield or classification of A2A2 milks, it is important to advance in the genotypic characterization of these major genes. The aim of this work was to determine the frequency of some genetic variations in the genes of the caseins and the associated ß-LG in the BON breed of Colombia and its evolution in time. For this, the information of 16 cattle of different regions of Colombia was used. The aim was to determine the frequency of some genetic variations in the genes of the caseins and the ß-Bovine-150 K and its evolution was found in the GGP-Bovéron and information. The population was more frequent (51%), while the haplotypic variant B of CSN3 associated with higher cheese yield presented a lower frequency (29.26%). No significant changes were found in the frequencies of the A2 haplotypes of CSN2 and B of CSN3 over the years, but they did occur among the herds. Also, differences in the frequency of the A2 haplotype between males and females were found. Finally, the BON population presented a frequency of favorable haplotypes such as those related to A2A2 milks, which shows an important potential for the valorization of milk in the breed
