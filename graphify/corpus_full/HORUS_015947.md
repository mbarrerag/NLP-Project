# MODEL BUSINESS PROCESS FOR A FOOD TRACKABILITY SYSTEM AS AN INEQUITY RISK MANAGEMENT RESEARCH IN AN ARTESANAL CHOCOLATE PRODUCING COOPERATIVE MICRO

Third sector companies, as is often called social economy and solidarity organizations, seek continuous improvement in each of their processes, in order to compete in different increasingly globalized markets, also need to adapt to the requirements and expectations of customers. The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic shows that customers are increasingly informed about the origin of the products and services they consume, so they investigate the background of the product that helps them make an informed purchase decision, so that food producing organizations demand tools that can help meet customers' expectations, with performance indicators to achieve the proposed objectives and in turn with the legal requirements requested by the health authorities, for this purpose, it is necessary to create strategies according to their needs and budget. The aim of this work is to model business processes in a cooperative artisan chocolate producer to identify each of the stages related to food safety, their association to food traceability and to develop the documentation necessary for the control of information, in the event of a withdrawal of the product from the market to be carried out in a timely and efficient manner
