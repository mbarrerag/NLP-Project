# Youth and gender-based social meta-bolims:

In this text, the detailed analysis of the survey designed by the Laboratory of Youth and Student Movements at the National University of Colombia is carried out. It is a collective design survey between researchers and students to perceive the diverse impacts of Covid's Pandemic 19 on the youth experience of the young students of Higher Education, particularly those of the National University, applied in the final months of the first semester of 2020. Reconfining, emotional wear, generational re-encounter, suspension of youth mobilization, virtual educational experience, new domestic obligations, are the elements that constitute this analysis. Key words: Pandemia, redefined youth, virtual way of life, emotional wear, care
