# DIMENSION PROCEDURE FOR A PHOTOVOLTIAN ISSALLED POTENCE GENERATOR UNDER PARTIAL SMOKING CONDITIONS

In the procedures of dimensioning of a low power isolated PVSYST photovoltaic generator, the effect of partial shading is usually considered in a simplified way and using simplified generator models. Software tools for dimensioning such as PVSYST, SAM, PVSOL and SOLARIUS are available, but do not use detailed models of the generator. This work presents a sizing procedure for an isolated low power GFV, which uses a detailed model of the GFV operating under partial shade conditions; a shading station of electric bicycles is considered as a case of study. The procedure starts with a presizing using a simplified method; then the estimate of the energy produced by the GFV hour by hour for a year is made, considering the effect of partial sombreting; then an iterative process including the losses by shading to validate the sizing, managing to supplement the load within a permitted range; finally, the battery bank is estimated and a charge regulator is selected; the procedure is validated by simulation for a case of a single-spanted GFVestimation and the software is used
