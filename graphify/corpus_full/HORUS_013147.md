# Geostatistics in circular data

A new methodology is proposed in the context of non-stationary geostatistics that allows for the prediction of circular data using residual circular kriging when the spatial trend is modeled through neural networks. Using simulated and real data (taken from the NASA power project) is compared with the proposed technique with medium-sized polishing. Results indicate that the strategy considered improves predictions. (Text taken from source.)
