# Comparative analysis of stochastic bridges: simulation, estimation and goodness of adjustment

This paper presents a comparative analysis between the classic Brownian bridge and fractional Brownian bridges with different Hurst parameters. It also discusses a approach to classical inference on the parameters of these models. It further explains how to simulate these processes when the parameters are already known or estimated. It also describes a methodology for conducting goodness-of-adjustment tests using wrapping techniques. Finally, three applications are presented in functional data, spatial statistics and natural language processing. .
