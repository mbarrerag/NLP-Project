# Homomorphism spaces of Artin groups at right angle

This research project is framed in the study of the groups of Artin at right angle and the groups of Lie. Recently, the groups of Artin at right angle have attracted great interest given their multiple applications in the geometric theory of groups, in particular in hyperbolic geometry. On the other hand, the groups of Lie play a fundamental role in geometry and physics since they can be interpreted as the group of symmetries of a physical system. This project is concentrated in the study of homomorphism spaces Hom(A,G), where A is a group of Artin at right angle and G is a group of compact Lie. In particular, it is proposed to study the homomorphism spaces of Hom(A,G) from the point of view of homotopia theory, calculating algebraic invariants associated with these spaces as homotopia groups and co-approval groups. Additionally, it is intended to obtain a stable decomposition of the spaces Hom(A,G) after a suspension
