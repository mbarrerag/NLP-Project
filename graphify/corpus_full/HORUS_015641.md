# Trends in acute respiratory infection and COVID-19 in students and teachers in Bogotá

Objective To describe the epidemiological trend of covid-19 cases in the educational community of public educational institutions during the application of quarantine (March-November 2020) in Bogotá, Colombia. Methods A descriptive study of epidemiological surveillance data on acute respiratory infection (ARI) and covid-19 in students and teachers in the official sector of Bogotá. Data were collected between 21 March and 20 November 2020, using a questionnaire that included sociodemographic, clinical and epidemiological questions. 5 252 cases were reported (incidence: 660.64 x 100 000 students), and 834 cases (incidence: 2 354.93 x 100 000 teachers) of IRA in students and teachers, respectively. The age group with the most cases among students was 6 to 11 years (45.1%). In teachers it was 30 to 39 years (35.9%). From 21 July to 20 August, a peak of IRA cases was observed in the entire educational community. Conclusion The educational community in Bogotá presented fewer cumulative incidences than the general population during the quarantine
