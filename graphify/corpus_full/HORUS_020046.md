# Analytical and Numerical Solutions to the Kapitza Pendulum Equation

We solve the Kapitza pendulum equation analytically and numerically. In the latter solution, we use the fourth-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) numerical method. The proposed methodology is beneficial in the study of nonlinear phenomena and control theory
