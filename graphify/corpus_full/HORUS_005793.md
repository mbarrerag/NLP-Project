# INTEGRAL FORM? RESEMANTIZATION AND LINKING WITH THE CRITICAL THINKING

In recent decades, it has been common to hear the term "integral training" in school and institutional settings. However, confusion, ambiguities and the indiscriminate use of this concept have generated a trivialization of this concept. Therefore, in this article of reflection, an exhibition is made about how comprehensive training has been understood and how it should be assumed according to its etymology and theories of the sciences of education. Subsequently, it is proposed to relate the concept of "integral training" to some models of critical thinking, as well as to expose its curricular articulation and missionary functions of university formation
