# TOWARDS THE STRUCTURAL TERRITORIAL CHANGE OF THE COLOMBIAN ECONOMIC AND SOCIAL

The purpose of this document is to show alternatives to democratize opportunities for territorial economic development, for which it is essential to recognize and support each municipality, province, department and region in their own trajectory of territorial structural change, in such a way that the diversification of productive activities and the increase of the added value of them are recognized and encouraged, starting from the improvements of the production and marketing processes. It is not a question of expanding in each subnational territory the reproduction of the model of centralized accumulation, that is, the primacy of the financing, the predation of the nature, the oligopoly property, the rentism of the capital, the persistence of the informal work, the deep social inequity and the lack of the democratic representative.
