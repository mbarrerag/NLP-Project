# Effect of diet, breeding density and surface area on productive parameters of the species Gryllodes sigillatus under laboratory conditions

This research aims to assess the impact of breeding density and surface area on the production of tropical domestic cricket, Gryllades sigillatus, in laboratory environments. This objective is addressed through detailed research divided into three distinct chapters. The first chapter focuses on the perspectives of the use of this species for human feeding in Colombia, exploring its potential social, environmental and nutritional impact. In the second chapter, it examines how the breeding density and the available space affect aspects such as survival and average weight of this species, demonstrating that to a low density of individuals (<0.023 grillos/cm2) were obtained heavier individuals and a low mortality rate. The third chapter, analyzes the impact of the inclusion of Manihot sculenta flour (yuca) and Alocasia macrorhyza (bore) in the diet, evaluating its influence on the survival, average weight per individual, food conversion and consumption of food, indicating that the yucca and bore flours are an opportunity as an ingredient in the diet of the grridges by improving some productive parameters as well as the benefits of a diet with a relationship of carbohydrate and food. the examination of how far greater density and available space affect aspects such as survival and average weight of this species demonstrate that at a low density of individuals (<0.023 crickets/cm2), heavy individuals and a low mortality rate were obtained. The third chapter analysis the impact of including Manihot esculenta (cassava) and Allocasia macrorhyza (bore) flower in the diet, evaluating their influence on survival, average weight per individual, feed conversion, and food consumption. It indicates that cashava and bore flowers present opportunity to improve the quality of the cricket diet, improving some productive parameters as well as the benefits of a diet system with regard to the quality assurance and safety assurance
