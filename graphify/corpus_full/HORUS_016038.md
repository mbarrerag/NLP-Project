# CRIMMINAL POLICY IN COLOMBIA: A NATIONAL VEGENCE!

the chapter is written as a tribute to maestro jorge arenas salazar, professor emeritus of the faculty of law, political and social sciences of the national university of colombia. The document resumes the article entitled “Is the Annex [Psychiatric] of La Picota: A National Shame!,” written by Professor Arenas in 1985 for the Revista Nuevo Foro Penal of the School of Law of the EAFIT University. This time a similar look is made, but of the current Criminal Policy of Colombia and, as it did at the time the one paid tribute to the Psychiatric Annex of La Picota of the city of Bogotá, this time and in the opinion of the subscribed author, such public policy does not notwithstanding the marked theoretical cloak of the current social and constitutional state of law that covers it, represents in the legal sphere a true national shame
