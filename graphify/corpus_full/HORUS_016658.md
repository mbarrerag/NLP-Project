# PERINATAL LESSAGE, ANGUSTIA PROCESS AND PLEASE EMOTIONS

Perinatal loss is defined as the death of a fetus or newborn that occurs from week 28 of gestation or 1000 g of weight, until the first seven days of life. According to the report of the World Health Organization (WHO) on reduction of mortality in childhood in the world, 2.6 million stillbirths are recorded. Almost half of the deaths occur in the first 24 hours of life and 75% during the first week. The Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), estimates that each year there are 2.7 million perinatal deaths in the world. In the report of Health Statistics World Health 2016 it is reported that in 2015 the stillbirth in Latin America was approximately 8.2 per 1000 live births, corresponding to 91 000 perinatal deaths for that year. These figures show a slight increase in relation to previous years in which the death rate was estimated at 7% in 2009 and 8% in 2011. In Colombia, the figures of perinatal deaths indicated 6% in 2009 and 11% in 2011. According to the National Demographic and Health Survey (ENDS), the perinatal mortality rate for each year of birth was between 14% and 19% in 2011
