# Educational strategy to promote family farming in a rural Afro-descendant community.

In the Guacoche district of the municipality of Valledupar(Cesar) lives an Afro-descendant community that was affected years ago by the armed conflict and is in the process of repair; in this work it was found that this community develops productive activities in the courtyards of the houses under a family farming model, but the crops present limitations due to the conditions of a semi-arid environment, however, they have not incorporated strategies for agroecological production or to mitigate the effect of environmental soil and climate tensors. To help in this situation, a didactic strategy called “laboratory to the courtyard” was developed, with the participation of 11 families led by older adults, to facilitate the understanding of scientific concepts that support the adoption of the use of humic biostimulants and allow to recognize the effects that the bioactivity of the humic acids causes on the growth, development and adaptation of plants under stress conditions.
