# BAUCH HEALTH ATTENTION FOR COLOMBIA CAMPESIN POPULATION DURING CIVID-19 PANDEMIA

Prioritatives: According to the ENSAB IV, the peasant population of Colombia has greater prevalence of oral diseases, and also considerable barriers to access to oral health services. Objetive: Characterizing the interaction of the peasant population of Colombia with the oral health services during the pandemic by COVID 19. Qualitative research with an ethnographic approach. Tools were used as participant observation, field diary, ethnographic interview and documentary review.A field work was carried out between February and December 2021, in eight rural regions of Colombia. (Amazonia, Orinoquía, Chocó Biogeographica, Magdalena Medio, Zona Cafetera, Caribe, Suroccidente Andino and Sabana Cundiboyacense).The information was organized and analyzed in a matrix in Excel from previously established categories of the researchers. The care in oral health in rural areas of Colombia is mainly provided by the public network. This network until the 1990s built a health infrastructure focused on the first level; however, these services have been generated by the political and financial services. Primary in Health and Tele-orientation, transcendental health mechanisms in the face of this type of socio-sanitary situations. Finally, it is emphasized that in the face of oral discomforts, the peasant population energized its knowledge and practices of self-medication in health, where there is a complementary use of herbal and pharmaceutical elements; these 81 Colombian Association of Dental Facultys?Herbal elements are distinguished according to the ecological characteristics of each region studied. Dental care remains precarious in the rural areas of the country, a situation that increased markedly during the pandemic. In addition to social, economic, geographical and cultural barriers, a political barrier emerged related to the organizational processes of the peasant population that relate to the worst management of the pandemic by national, regional and local governments. The peasant population strengthened its own knowledge and practices on bucal health care
