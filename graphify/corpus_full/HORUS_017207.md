# Non-genetic effects on pre-steet growth characteristics in COLOMBIAN HAIR OVINS

To determine the effect of some variation factors on the characteristics weight at birth (PN), weight at the adjusted weaning (PDA) at 90 days and weight gain pre-weaning (PGP) in Colombian hair sheep (OPC). 525 data were used for PCO offspring, managed in an extensive production system, under conditions of the lower Colombian tropics. A linear model of fixed effects was used, which included, the sex of the breeding, the type of childbirth (simple or multiple), the number of the lamb (primiper or multi-parter), the climatic period of birth (dry or rainy) and the year of birth (2017 to 2020) and its interactions.
