# FINAL PRODUCTIVES AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP WITH NATURAL FORESTAL SPECIES AND INTRODUCIDES IN SIX MUNICIPIES OF BOYACA-COLOMBIA

The identification of vulnerability in the ecological diversity of any agricultural area is established through the representativeness of forest species within the community. Understanding their distribution and quantity, Dominance, Equity and wealth allows generating specific recommendations, as well as decision-making regarding their conservation. The objective of the study was to characterize forest and shrub species at the lowest taxonomic level, identifying the richness of predominant species in the municipalities of Aquitaine, Bethlehem, Busbanzá, Cambita, Panqueba and Fishing in the department of Boyacá, Colombia. Twelve farms were evaluated in 6 municipalities for a total of 72, in an altitude range of between 2200 and 4000 msnm recognizing trees to species. The biodiversity indicators used in the farms were Relative Abundance (%), Specific wealth index (S) and Index of Diversity of Margalef (DMg). In addition, the methods of the Simpson Diversity Index (D) and the Diversity Index of Shannon-Wiener (H') species were applied to the municipality and to determine the overall richness of the species.
