# Development of aminated-hydroxyetyl cellulose coatings modified with cerium oxide for magnesium alloys for biomedical applications

The magnesium alloys have become promising candidates for use in orthopedic implants due to their biodegradability, the lack of need for secondary excision surgery and the potential to reduce the cost of hospital care. Magnesium alloys are susceptible to biodegradation in living conditions without completing the time required for the stabilization of the bone implant. In this work two magnesium alloys have been coated with the purpose of improving their resistance to corrosion and their cytocompatibility. For this purpose, the surfaces of the magnesium alloys ZK60 and AZ31 were evaluated by the deposition of the metallic samples in a PLA and dichloromethane solution.
