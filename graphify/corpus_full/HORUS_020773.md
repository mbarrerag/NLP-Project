# THE ROLE OF THE CARBON TAX IN ENERGY TRANSITION: A REVIEW OF ITS COLOMBIA APPLICATION

The carbon tax is promoted as a useful environmental policy instrument for mitigating carbon equivalent emissions. It is considered that, with its proper application, it can contribute to progress towards the energy transition, as it is an instrument that encourages investment in clean infrastructure and technologies, such as wind, solar and demand for hybrid and electric vehicles. After the pandemic, economic recovery has significantly increased global energy demand, resulting in a rise in carbon emissions to a historical peak. It is expected that the application of the carbon tax will mitigate emissions and encourage migration to clean energies. Based on a review of international literature, this article analyses the economic and environmental impacts of the application of the carbon tax, especially for developed countries, where they have been widely documented. Specifically, the characteristics and preliminary results of the application of this tax in Colombia are described
