# CHAPTER I. ANATOMIC FUNDAMENTS OF THE MACCULIN REPRODUCTOR APPARATO, A MACROSCOPIC WORLD.

If you imagine the male reproductive apparatus as a universe, without a doubt the testicle would be the planet earth, a planet where microscopic structures abound in the form of highways or seminiferous tubules, where in a unique and combined way sperm cells are formed capable of generating a new individual once it merges with an oocito. The testicle is not only responsible for forming spermatozoa, it also synthesizes the hormone of greatest interest for the sex more - culino: testosterone, a molecule that enhances the secondary sexual characteristics in the male. Surrounded by an important number of planets, the testicle is made up of hormones of cerebral origin such as the stimulating follicle and the luteinizing layer that surprisingly travel through the blood stream to enable the planet to fulfill its cytogenetic and hormonal functions. This magical universe, called male reproductive tract, is made up of organs among which we find the testicle and the layers that envelop it, the epididymus, the spermatic cord, the planet's duct of the species, the accessory genital glands, muscles and the penis.
