# Weaving colors: conversations with sexual diversity in disability.

This research is based on critical studies on disability, addressing as a main focus the experiences of structural oppression and the training expressions embodied by women who are enunciated as both physical and sexual dissidents from a gender perspective. In this sense, its aim is to explain the experiences of discrimination that have gone through them, identify social and/or political demands and, reveal processes of inclusion/exclusion within the groups or groups in which they are associated
