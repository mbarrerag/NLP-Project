# Determination of nutritional requirements of the gulupa plants (Passiflora edulis Sims.) cultivated under cover conditions in the Bogotá Sabana

The cultivation of gulupa is of great importance to the country, both for food security and for its relevance as an export crop to the United States and the European Union. Despite this, little information is available about the specific nutritional requirements of this species, so that the management of fertilization is carried out based on the information available for other pasifloras and the corrective deficiencies are made by observation of symptoms mainly; this practice generates inefficient management of fertilizers, thus affecting the quality of the fruits, profitability and production of the crop. The objective of this research is to determine the nutritional requirements of the cultivation of gulupa (P. edulis) in its different phenological states until the first reproductive phase and the effect of the deficiencies of N, P, K, Ca, Mg, B and Zn in the different phenological states.
