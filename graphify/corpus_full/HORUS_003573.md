# GEOLOGICAL-GEOTECNIC CHARACTERIZATION OF A TERRITORICAL RESIDUAL SOIL OF THE EQUANA NEIS

Due to the particularities that tropical soil exhibits when exposed to high temperature and humidity that favor the occurrence of chemical meteorization processes, more detailed studies are necessary for its application. Therefore, the objective of this research is to carry out a geological-geotechnical characterization of a tropical residual soil located in the city of Medellín (Antioquía, Colombia), on the geological formation known as the "Neis Micacean of Iguaná". The methodology used includes the evaluation of physical, chemical, mineralogical, microstructural and hydromechanical properties of a material derived from a metamorphic rock, by means of laboratory tests of classical soil mechanics and specifics such as the fast Tropical Compact Miniatura classification (MCT), determination of the Zero Load Point (CPO), pH, microscopy electronic dislocation of scanning, diffraction of x-ray, characteristic curve of water retention, duploedometric consolidation, pinhole test and disaggregation. The results obtained reveal the behavior of a non-plastic mineral soil, with a low proportion of aluminium and micro-organ structure
