# THE REGULATION OF THE COLOMBIAN CAPITAL MARKET

A deep and efficient capital market is able to intermediate and focus the surplus resources in the economy, facilitating the distribution of risk and supporting economic growth and financial stability.However, that the capital market manages to fulfil this function depends on several factors within which they are considered: macroeconomic stability; the existence of legal frameworks and solid market infrastructure; the participation of various investors; the existence of effective market supervision and effective regulation; among others.The proper design of a regulatory framework in the capital market has been recognized as fundamental to its development; in particular, for its importance as an instrument for managing inefficiencies associated with problems of iliquidity, high volatility, high transaction costs, concentration of short-term investments, etc. These problems become policy objectives that depend to a large extent on the existing regulatory framework and that are decisive for the correct determination of prices and efficient risk diversification.In this sense, and given the relevance of regulation to the proper functioning of the capital market, in this chapter a review of the regulation over the last three decades, with the objective of analyzing its relationship with the development of the market and efficient risk. The existence of a statistically significant correlation between the classic indicators of capital market development and the issuance of laws, decrees and, in general, regulation applicable to the market, gives evidence of the role that the regulatory framework has played in its dynamics and its contribution to the strengthening and consolidation of a local market. Section 4 identifies the regulatory challenges to be addressed in the coming years. For this purpose, the Policy Document for the Development of the Financial System 2020-2025 is taken as a reference, which considers the road map to promote the development of the capital market and lists the strategic objectives set out by the Financial Regulation Unit for 2023. Additionally, a discussion is presented around the challenges and development opportunities for the Colombian market. In particular, the need to improve education and financial inclusion, and the deepening of a stock exchange culture in the country and the further promotion of competition in the financial market are identified as major challenges
