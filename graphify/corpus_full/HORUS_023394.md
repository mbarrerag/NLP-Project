# INHIBITORY POTENTIAL OF EXTRACTS OF PLANTS USED IN COLOMBIAN TRADITIONAL MEDICINE ON LOGY RELATING TO OBESITY AND TYPE II DIABETS.

The Latin American Congress of Medicinal Plants (COLAPLAMED) is an academic event held annually, having as headquarters Latin American Universities. All the COLAPLAMED are characterized by offering a space for exchange of ideas, generation of synergies and networks, strengthening the South South cooperation in research of medicinal plants in the region. The congress is held from 2013 to the present. In all versions of the Latin American Congress of Medicinal Plants have been received scientists of recognized trajectory in research of Medicinal Plants as magistral lecturers, and have presented works in modalities of oral presentation and poster. The best scientific works receive recognition by the Organising Commission of the event. Another characteristic activity of the COLAPLAMED is the development of pre-congress courses, which allow the scientific update in topics related to the topic of the congress. All these activities motivate the young scientists of undergraduate and postgraduate, to continue contributing in the wonderful field of research in medicinal plants.
