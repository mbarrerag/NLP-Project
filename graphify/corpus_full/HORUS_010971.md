# URBAN LAW AND ORDER OF THE TERRITORY IN COLOMBIA

The book presents a reflection of the urban law and the regulation of the planning of the territory in Colombia. It begins with a reference to the different historical milestones in the legal matter of the evolution of the Colombian urban system and then presents a general reference to the concepts of city and urban planning. Once this initial part is finished, the different components of the Colombian urban planning system are analyzed, in which the planning, execution of the planning, the management of the soil and the urban discipline are highlighted
