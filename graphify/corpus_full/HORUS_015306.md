# PROTOPIA. NETWORK PROTOPTION FOR THE ACADEMIC COMMUNITY OF INDUSTRIAL DESIGN (UNAL-BOGOTÁ) WITH THE SMALL SMALL SMALL PRODUCTION ENVIRONMENT IN BOGOTÁ-REGION

Industrial design students require to interact with the productive field from the beginning of their training. While it is expected that each student will develop basic skills and skills that allow them to represent their design proposals at the level of physical models and models, on many occasions the formulation of projects requires support from external services. This is due to different factors. Sometimes it is a question of technologies that are not available in the University laboratories, other times the project requires support in technical or traditional knowledge that students do not master, for example textile techniques such as clothing or fabrics, the work with glass, leatherwork, etc. In this context the need to connect the student community, with the ecosystem of entrepreneurs and entrepreneurs of Bogotá; in processes of modelling and prototyping product of academic projects of industrial design, through the development of a web application that facilitates the interaction between members of what is perceived as a tacit community of practice until the moment.
