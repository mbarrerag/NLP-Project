# Evaluation of cardiovascular pharmacological activity and on the central nervous system of extracts and metabolites obtained from Colombian medicinal plants and structural analogues obtained by synthesis

This research is aimed at carrying out the cardiovascular pharmacological assessment and on the central nervous system of extracts and metabolites of Colombian medicinal plants, as well as structural analogues obtained by synthesis or semi-synthesis. Part of previous studies, to be initiated or in progress of the species: Cannabis sp (N.V. marijuana), Croton schiedeanus (N.V. starch), Hygrophila thyttha (N.V. amansamachos), Passiflora sp (N.V. curuba), Persea americana (N.V. avocado) and Onoseris purpurea (N.V. Santamaria leaf). To this end, pharmacological tests of ex vivo pharmacological screening in the bath of asylated organ and in vivo, with mice and laboratory rats or with human platelets. This is intended to contribute in the search for sources of Colombian biodiversity potentially useful in the therapy or prevention of disorders such as arterial hypertension, coronary disease, epilepsy, depression or pathological anxiety, providing in the formation of human resource trained in experimental and central pharmacological research
