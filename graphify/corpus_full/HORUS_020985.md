# Design and manufacture of a prototype equipment to measure the refractive power of progressive ophthalmic lenses.

The project proposes to design and build a prototype laboratory equipment with which to measure the maps of refractive power of progressive ophthalmic lenses. From the refraction of light rays in the progressive lens, a pattern of luminous points is generated in the form of distorted grid. This distribution of points is the starting point for the analysis of refraction. The luminous rays are generated by lighting with collimated light a screen with holes distributed in a regular grid. The optical equipment must include a lighting system (led or laser), collimator lens, hole screen, support for the eye lenses, registry screen and video camera. In addition, an electronic displacement system for the registry screen and camera that can be controlled from a PC. For obtaining the maps of refractive power it is proposed to use the analysis of Fourier.
