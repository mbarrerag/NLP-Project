# SYNTHESIS AND REOLOGICAL STUDY OF EUTECTIC SOLVENTS PROFOUND UNDER THE COMMERCIAL CAFFEIN WITH POOTENTIAL APPLICATION IN PROCESS OF DISOLUTION OF COMBUSTIBILES.

The deep eutectical solvents (DES) are a mixture of two or more chemicals known as acceptors and donors of hydrogen bonds (HBA and HBD, by their acronym in English) that, when combined in a particular molar proportion, present a decrease of the melting point inferior to that of the pure components, become liquids at temperatures less than 100 °C, low volatility and are not toxic, among other properties [1].The use of DES has emerged as a sustainable alternative to replace traditional solvents which, generally, are harmful to both the environment and human health, are expensive and difficult to reuse in most applications [2]. Among the most studied DES for the processes of desulphurization of fuels are those based on imidazole and benzimidazole whose prices are around USD 355 and USD 361, respectively [3]. One more economical alternative to these compounds is to use caffeine, whose value in the market is USD 132 dollars, besides being biodegradable and low toxicity.
