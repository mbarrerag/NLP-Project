# • BIOPROSPECTION AND EXTRACTIVISM

This article 3 analyses the policy and legislation adopted by Colombia on bioprospecting, through the consultation of the national development plans of various governments, and the corresponding normative nature from the entry into force of the Convention on Biological Diversity in 1994, until 2018. The objective is to determine the existence of an extractive logic of biodiversity in the country by the State. It is demonstrated that in the discourse there is such logic, because instead of applying protectionist and conservationist legislation of the biodiversity of the country, it is exposed to extractivist practices. However, in practice, it was found that scientific research carried out in Colombia by universities, mostly not for extractive purposes, since they are purely academic research or are in initial stages, which is not an option for some of these research to present extractive activities in the future
