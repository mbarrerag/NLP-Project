# Dialogue for teacher effectiveness in higher education: a meta-inthetic review

The strategies of dialogue are frequent forms of professional development teachers in higher education. This research investigated the impact of these strategies on the educational effectiveness, observed in the learning results of their students, for this purpose the methodology of metasynthesis, which integrates the findings of qualitative research, through a systematic and replicable procedure. The search, screening and selection was carried out, finding 11 relevant primary works out of a total of 4,410 possible. The rigor of each study was evaluated and analyzed through the postulates of the grounded theory. The results are explanatory concepts in the form of main categories that refer to sharing teaching strategies with peers, support for the instructional change, and support in documents elaborated by teachers, all of which are consistent with the change in pedagogical practices after the teaching participation in the spaces of dialogue, as well as the change of perception of students about teachers and the topics worked out, the commitment to their learning and the interest of participating in discussions with peers and teachers, which seems to affect the results of learning.
