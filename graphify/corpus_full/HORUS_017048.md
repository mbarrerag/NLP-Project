# Assessment of the deterioration of insulation in 15 kV XLPE cables used in underground distribution lines by running ageing test.

The current increase in demand for electricity and the obligation to comply with regulations, together with reliability and continuity in supply, have encouraged manufacturers of electric conductors to focus on carefully designing their products, paying special attention to the electrical properties of materials. The use of XLPE isolated cables in underground transmission lines in medium and high voltage systems has increased in cities due to urban, environmental and reliability needs. This is also related to the fact that there are aerial networks that, being exposed to external factors such as rays, comets, vegetation, animals, etc., may lead to service interruptions or impact the environment. The fact that transmission technology is implemented with isolated cables makes it necessary to assess the performance of its components in operation in the long term, in particular its isolation.
