Our first purpose is to compare the predictive capabilities of some models built to forecast daily demand for electricity in Colombia. Initially, we use generalized linear models, followed by Machine Learning models such as ensemble learning models, support vector machines (SVM), and finally deep learning models. The objective is to determine which model demonstrates higher predictive accuracy in the forecast of daily demand for electricity in Colombia.
