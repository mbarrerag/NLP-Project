# Food loss and waste recovery through sustainable biorefineries

The food waste has generated several problems at economic, environmental and social level. The Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) has established the concept of loss and waste of food according to the link of the value chain that generates it. The first is generated in the stages of agricultural production, transport and production, while the second is generated in the stage of sale and consumption. The food losses have been investigated from different perspectives in order to determine potential applications through the implementation of biotechnological processes. The main advantage of this type of raw material is related to its homogeneous composition. On the other hand, the valorization of food waste has been studied to generate energy vectors (mainly biogas). However, problems related to the standardization of the raw material have been identified as it varies depending on the socio-economic and cultural context. Therefore, there is a series of methodological and research gaps that must be studied before proposing transformation routes for both food losses and food waste. In addition, the study of potential applications is key to determine the product portfolio that can be generated from the losses and residues of food., other aspects were included to propose different schemes of biorefineries. Some factors considered were the link and the actor of the value chain that generates the waste, the integration of raw materials, the stages of generation of the waste and the heterogeneity of the waste of food. In this sense, two models of composition were proposed for the organic residues of kitchen and organic waste of supply centers considering the government statistics of consumption of food in Colombia. The four raw materials were characterized in terms of chemical composition, proximal analysis and content of total and volatile solids. In this sense, the strategy of design of biorefinerías was applied for the four raw materials in order to define the configurations of biorefinerías with greater viability to be analyzed. Biorefinerías were evaluated experimentally up to the stage of production of fermentable sugars. According to the results obtained in the design strategy, the obtaining of levulinic acid, poly-lactic acid, ethanol, butanol and lactic acid for the use of the fermentable sugars.
