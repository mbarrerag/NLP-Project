# Urban re-nature through the compactness applied on the edges of the neighborhood El Remembrane and the Bogotá River

Commonly, urban edges are understood as city boundaries, responding to their political characteristics in the face of the division of the soil and/or the development areas that are in their delimitations. However, they are important territories for the transition of relations between urban and rural areas, framed in the natural context and represented in the protection of environmental areas that allow contributing to the functioning of cities, contributing not only to the well-being of the diversity of flora and fauna, but also to the existence of the human species through a context rich in urban ecosystem services that regulate the inherent needs of the citizen and the place that inhabits.
