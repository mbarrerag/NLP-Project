# Study of strains of Colombian Mycobacterium leprosy that cause multiple symptomatic episodes of post-polychemotherapy leprosy.

Lepra is a chronic dermatous-neurological disease, caused by Mycobacterium leprosy, which in Colombia causes about 400 new cases each year. Lepra is treated with a polychemotherapy (PQT) and its efficacy is established by the follow-up of recurrent cases of the disease. Because recurrence cannot be differentiated into relapse or re-infection clinically, in this study, we use techniques of total sequencing of DNA (genome) and RNA (transcriboma), in order to know some genomic and transcriptic characteristics of M. leprosy strains associated with multiple episodes of post-PQT leprosy in Colombia.
