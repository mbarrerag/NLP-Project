# THE USE OF PUBLIC SPACE IN THE POPAYAN CALDES PARK, CAUCA.

The summary presented below is the result of the research carried out by the author during the last year and is constituted in a continuation of the doctoral thesis defended by the author in 2010, on the topic of the use of the space in major squares and case of study the Plaza de Bolívar of Bogotá. Some ethnographic techniques such as photographs and videos for time lapses, some interviews and surveys were used within the methodology. Also taken into account, within the conceptual framework, some reflections of four contemporary authors internationally recognized, and that have worked on the concept of urban life; they are richard sennett, jan gehl, jane jacobs and manuel delgado. In trying to solve the research question that is posed: how does Caldas Park define itself as a place of power and socio-cultural manifestations?, and applying the methodology of analysis; it can be inferred that the park Caldas is the result of interaction between the objects built and human behaviors in the space. It is shown that today Caldas Park is constituted in an urban event of the city of Popayán, that it has consolidated by means of the actions in the buildings
