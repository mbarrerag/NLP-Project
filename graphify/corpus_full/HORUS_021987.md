# Systematic review of economic evaluations of the disease course modifier treatment for multiple sclerosis.

Systematic review of economic evaluations of the disease course modifier treatment for multiple sclerosis Objective. Conducting a systematic review of economic evaluations of the modified treatment of the disease course approved in Colombia for multiple sender-recurrent type sclerosis. The search for economic evaluations was carried out for the condition of interest in specialized databases such as Medline, Embase, NHS EED among others. The relevant information was extracted through a standardized matrix and the quality of the studies was evaluated through the QHES tool. Subsequently, through the instrument proposed by Welte et al, the transferibility of studies considered of high quality was evaluated. With the search 1046 titles were recovered, after the purification 45 articles were included in the review for full text analysis which in turn fed the matrix of data extraction. After the evaluation of methodological quality 23 studies obtained 75 points or more according to the QHES scale and these were evaluated with the Welte instrument in relation to the methodological characteristics, of the health system and population finding that the best results were not affected by the outcome
