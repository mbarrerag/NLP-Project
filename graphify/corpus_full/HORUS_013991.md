# Design and construction of guides for the conduction of lightning currents applied to personal portable shelters for protection against lightning.

Several studies show Colombia as one of the regions of the world with the highest atmospheric electrical activity[1][2][3][4]. Values for Colombia range from 20 to 30 rays/km2/year. However, there are areas such as the lower part of the Magdalena Valley and Catatumbo where these values exceed 50 rays/km2/year[5]. According to data collected for 10 years by the Directorate of Preservation of Integrity and Security of the Colombian National Army, 282 accidents were reported during this period due to lightning, in which 72 soldiers died. This means that on average, there are 2 accidents per month due to lightning and one fatality due to this cause every two months.[6] For the rest of the population there are no official reports. In this project, it is intended to protect people exposed to rays because of their activity in the open air. To this end, light shelters against rays manufactured in conductive webs will be used, which will be reinforced by guides to drive the streams of rays to the ground without affecting users
