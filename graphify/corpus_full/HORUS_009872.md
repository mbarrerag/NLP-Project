# DIFFERENTIATED PRESENCE OF THE STATE AND MANAGEMENT OF SOIL USES IN CONFLICT IN SOUTH BOGOTÁ, D.C.

In this article we propose to analyze, first, the conflicts that occur between the different uses of the soil that generate social and economic conflicts in the periurban zone of Ciudad Bolívar. Second, we analyze the differentiated presence of the State for the management of this overlapping of uses in a social and economically peripheral zone in the megacity of Bogotá as a case that shows gaps between the management of urban and rural areas of the poor peripheries and a fragmented and duplicated state action that does not transform the reproduction of an unequal and unjust social order
