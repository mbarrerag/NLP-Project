# COMPUTATIONAL STUDY OF FLOW IN LABYRINTH SEALS USED IN GAS TURBINES

This paper describes the results obtained from the study by Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) met hod of the behavior and performance of two configurations of rotary labyrinth seals used in gas turbines (namely: straight labyrinth seals and staged labyrinth seals), with the purpose of establishing which one has better performance in terms of seal leakage. A brief theoretical introduction to labyrinth seals and their use in gas turbines is given, to then describe the study methodology with respect to boundary conditions and the evaluation and selection of the discretization mesh of the studied domain. The paper ends with the results obtained for parameters such as velocity and pressure inside the seal. It was found that the staggered seal has an 82% reduction of leakage with respect to the straight-through seal, and that the velocities reached by the flow in the staggered seal are lower than those of the straight-through seal.
