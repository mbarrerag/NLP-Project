# CHARTERS IN TIMES OF AUSTRIA OF THE HORSE FICTION TO NOBILIARY REALITY

This book offers a tour of the historic gardens that the Spanish nobles built for their delight and where they arranged botanical and antiquarian collections of enormous value. In parallel, it reviews the splendid gardens of the books of cavalry; there its characters cured their melancholy, had loving encounters or held banquets, parties and dances. With the help of numerous testimonies (chronic, letters, accounts of expenses, travel accounts) and the view always put on the adventures that enchand Don Quixote, a fruitful dialogue is established between the cabaleresca fiction and the reality of the time that allows to rebuild the panorama of the noble gardens in Spain of the sixteenth century, the mixture and hybridization of its medieval and Renaissance forms, the Andalusian heritage and the French, Italian and Flemish influences.
