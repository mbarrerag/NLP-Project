# Are companies liable to be held accountable in a manner that is morally?

This exhibition will present a conceptual framework of philosophical and sociological reference based on which the analysis of situations is addressed where individuals, in the name of a productive organization, commit to or lack of ethical action. This will lead to the examination of a concept of recent use such as that of moral responsibility of organizations, which poses a series of challenges and questions as an organization, not being a natural person, may, however, be subject to moral and immoral behaviors
