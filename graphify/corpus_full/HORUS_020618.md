# Topaga, the transition from a doctrinal set: from a Jesuit historiography to a secular history of the missionary campaign 1633-1644

Since the sixteenth century the Jesuit chronicles were the compendium of a multitude of letters written with the purpose of informing their superiors the vicissitudes through which the members of the Company passed in the different places.
