# VOCATIONAL TRIANGLE IN PERSONS WITH PARKINSON’S DISEASE

This study was framed in clinical phonetics and aimed to compare the vocal triangle of speakers of Colombian Spanish who present Parkinson’s disease (EP) with that of a group without disease control group (GC). For the analyses, recordings of 10 people with EP (6 men and 4 women) and 9 recordings of people without EP, both groups paired by age and gender were used. The vowels were extracted from recordings of a phonetically balanced reading and production of 5 vocals sustained for 10 seconds. In total 380 recordings were analyzed. Acoustic analyses to determine the values of formants 1 and 2 were performed with the Praat program version 6.2.07 (Boersma & Weenink, 2021). The data were analyzed with descriptive statistics and the Mann-Whitney test; and, with the F-plot program (Casali, 2021), the vocal triangle of the two groups was graphed.
