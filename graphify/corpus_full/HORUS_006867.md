# Current relations between design and traditional trades in Colombia. In search of new coherence

Since the origins of what is considered design, there has been a relationship between this and the traditional/artesian trades. This relationship has had convergences and divergences throughout history. This research is a normative, historical and phenomenological review that has semistructured interviews, contrasted with literary review that makes qualitative analysis in order to update the relations between design and traditional trades in the last 20 years in Colombia, with specific cases such as the Fundación Escuela Taller de Bogotá and Tambó ri Palenge. Relations that are traversed by decolonial looks and reviews in different dimensions, in search of a new coherence for future proposals around these relationships. .
