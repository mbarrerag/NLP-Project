# Creation of virtual reality experience for the supervision and operation of the equipment of the machine automation laboratory

This document compiles what has been done during the development of a virtual reality experience which allows to operate and visualize the state of a motor and speed driver from Machine Automation laboratory of Universidad Nacional de Colombia. To do this, firstly it was necessary to build a virtual environment that could reproduce the laboratory space, then, it was done the programming of some elements belonging to the experience in order to look for implementing dynamics that could become easier the interaction, and give more realism to the devices which allows the operation of digital twin. In other matters, a program was done for physical devices operation by means of an industrial driver, and next of carry out an investigation about comunication tools, Node-RED and comunications protocol WebSocket were used to create a conection between the application developed in Unity and the PLC. Finally, the virtual reality experience was tested in the lab and the devices under study could be manipulated successfully
