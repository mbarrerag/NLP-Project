# Adsorption of Cimoxanil and Mancozeb in soil through the use of grapeshell biocarbon: structural characterization and analysis of parameters of interaction between biocarbon and pesticides

The growth of the world population has led the agricultural sector to implement measures to increase food production, including the use of pesticides to control pests. However, these practices have led to environmental degradation, depletion of natural resources, the emission of pollutants and have contributed to climate change. Given this problem, biocarbon emerges as a sustainable alternative. This carbon material, obtained by the pyrolysis of biomass, is considered as a viable solution for soil remediation. This study focuses on reviewing and collecting information on the capacity of biocarbon based on grapeshell, as an adsorbing agent of pesticides, specifically mancozeb and carboxanil, in order to contribute to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) related to health and well-being, responsible production and sustainable consumption.
