# KEY QUESTION OF THE DATA FUSION PROCESS AND ITS VARIABLES: A REVIEW OF LITERATURA

The volume of information that reaches the industries is very high, this is due to the decentralization of the centers of operation, each one acquires, processes and provides reports that come in different formats and its data can contain alphanumeric characters, tables, images among others, that are not structured. Under this answer in the area of data fusion, there are opened some questions made by 3 experts regarding the atypical values, statistical tools, the process of data fusion and the importance of this in science. An investigative process is carried out, based on the results of various authors, the answers support the questions in such a way that it is deepened in the paradigms and expands the concepts of the fusion of data from different sources with heterogeneous data. It can be concluded that the importance of data fusion for the scientific and academic community, lies in the treatment and optimization of the data to obtain more complete and reliable information. The results of the research are analyzed and the conclusions focused on the benefits of the tools of artificial intelligence used for the optimization of the fusion of data are realized
