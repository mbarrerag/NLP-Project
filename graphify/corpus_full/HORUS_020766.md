# Music on the Edge. An artistic/political/pedagogical experience in construction

Musics on the edge is a research/creation proposal that starts with the reflection generated from the tension that is inhabited by developing processes of formation in peasant music formally in the rural territories of Bogotá and the empirical development of musical practices that occur naturally in those places. Through the look of a artist teacher – artist formador, we seek to share with those who approach this work the experience lived in the middle of the transition between the formal and academic study of traditional and peasant music and music from the pedagogical practice. In this dwelling and trying to put into dialogue both worlds or conceptions of music and its teaching, we have been finding different spaces for the creation and management of processes, which can contribute to the visibility of peasant culture, its recognition and development in and with the peasant communities that inhabit the rurality of Bogotá.
