# COLOMBIA STOPS. BIOPHISICAL CHARACTERISTICS, ECO-HYDROLOGY AND CLIMATE CHANGE

Between 2006 and 2020, a research programme related to the ecohydrology of the moors was carried out, in which all the ecological and hydrological variables that determine the ecohydrological functioning of the moors in Colombia were characterized. For this, 7 moors of the country were selected, under the criterion that between them the whole range of precipitation is covered in these ecosystems and to some extent that were distributed in the different mountain ranges. therefore, the moors of guerrero and chingaza (eastern cordillera), belmira, parque nevados, romerales-berlin, frontino and chili-barragán (central cordillera) were selected. In each moor were measured automatically and with equipments duly calibrated meteorological variables, ecohydrological, vegetation, soils and were evaluated the effects of climate change. The book of the moors of Colombia, the most important information used in the country, biophysical characteristics, ecohydrology and climate change, contains in 650 pages, all the information related to the new knowledge generated during these years of research in the country of Colombia.
