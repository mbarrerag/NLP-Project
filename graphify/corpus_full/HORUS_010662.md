# DETERMINATION OF PLAGICIDE WASTE BY GC-MS

Event consisting of a scientific update day as well as topics related to the determination of pharmaceutical compounds by chromatographic techniques. The event was held on June 15 at the University of Nariño, Pasto Colombia, and will be aimed at students, researchers, professors and interested people. Apart from the conference there was a training in liquid chromatography topics coupled with mass spectrometry, and evaluation of data with chemical analytical quality
