# LEADERSHIP: DEVELOPMENT OF THE STRUCTURE.

The environments in which change is stimulated develop skills in those who make up an organization and promote the development of skills and capacities to obtain sufficient versatility when managing work teams. This way, the concept of leadership can be understood, which has been attempted to define through explanatory models that, according to the author who develops it, have been adapted from different interests and from the perspective in which it is being evaluated (Van et al., 2017). As will be seen later on, throughout this chapter different aspects related to the leadership construct are addressed, such as its characteristics, models, aspects that allow evaluation, and some tools that enhance it
