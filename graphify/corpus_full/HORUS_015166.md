# Assessment of Machine Learning algorithms not monitored with climate data

ABSTRACT When using climate data, researchers have difficulty determining the clustering algorithm and the best performance parameters for processing a specific data set. The evaluation of unsupervised machine learning algorithms K-means, K-medoids and Linkage-complete, applied to three data sets with climatological variables (temperature, rain, relative humidity and solar radiation), is performed for three weather stations located in the department of Caldas, Colombia, at different altitudes above sea level. Five scenarios are defined for 2, 3 and 5 clusters for each of the two partitioned algorithms and 5 scenarios for the hierarchical algorithm, for each of the weather stations, and applying a different quantity and grouping of variables for different scenarios and using Euclidean distance, Davis-Bouldin as a method of evaluation of quality of clusters, standardization with techniques such as transformation of range and Z transformation, various algorithm iterations and reduction of dimensionality with PCA. In addition, the computational cost is evaluated. This research can guide the researcher on certain decisions in the analysis of clusters, as well as identifying the best conditions used in the meteorological data, and transformations
