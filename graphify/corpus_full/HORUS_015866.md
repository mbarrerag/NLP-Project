# Le Corbusier and Gottfied Semper: in the style of a machinist era

The present is a study of the thought and work of Le Corbusier (1887-1965) in the light of the theories of Gottfried Semper (1803-1869); but it can be read, also, as a study of the theories of Semper in the light of the work of Le Corbusier. The aim is to investigate beyond the easily observable differences between them to know what aspects they agreed in the field of architectural creation. We are motivated by the possibility to find indications of the genuine character of architecture in the relatively little explored terrain that unites these two characters. In Chapter 1 we examine the encounters of Le Corbusier, during its years of formation, with the remnants of the Semperian theoretical legacy. In Chapter 2 we explore the significant coincidences between the postulates of both architects around the concept of style. Finally, in Chapter 3, we examine some specific aspects of the work of Le Corbusier in the light of the theories of Semper period.
