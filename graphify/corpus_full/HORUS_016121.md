# Adaptation of web tools for the implementation of a massive collaborative software development course

This study presents the design of a massive collaborative course of software development through the adaptation of web tools. It integrates the teaching of software development and agile methodologies based on project-based learning, which allows students to relate theory and practice. A case study is carried out with the participation of 475 students in a Node.JS course and agile methodologies. The results of the course and the use of the different tools are satisfactory both in the final products of the students and in the perception of learning and the effective completion rate of the mass course. It is concluded that the massive course designed here allows the teaching of web development in collaborative work teams, emulating a real environment through project management platforms and repositories with mediation of a learning management system
