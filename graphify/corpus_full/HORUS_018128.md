# Teaching Astronomy to promote peace and reconciliation in vulnerable communities.

With a view to the new post-conflict era in Colombia and the need to implement alternatives that promote peace and reconciliation, it is proposed that a little explored context is education for peace addressed from the teaching of natural sciences. This research began with the conduct of a semi-structured individual interview with 3 teachers and the application of the Scale for Perception of the Relationship between Science and Peace to 251 teachers, with which it was concluded that the teaching community does conceive a relationship between Science and Peace and, likewise, it is necessary to develop educational resources around education for peace. As a result, a didactic sequence is designed regarding Astronomy and Space Sciences, and education for Peace. This consists of 4 units to develop in formal and non-formal educational environments, in school and/or community groups, for population with or without prior knowledge of astronomy. Each unit includes expected learnings from the area of Natural Sciences and citizenship of peace
