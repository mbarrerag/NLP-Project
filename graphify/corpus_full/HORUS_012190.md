# ASSISTANCE TREATMENT FOR THE CANCER OF MAMA HER2 POSITIVE ADVANCED. REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVANCEMENT AT THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF COLOMBIA CANCEROLOGY

Breast cancer is the most frequent and most fatal neoplasm in women worldwide. Human epidermal growth factor (HER2) receptor 2 is overexpressed in approximately 20% of breast cancer patients and is associated with increased risk of tumour recurrence and mortality. Prior to the development of monoclonal antibodies targeted against HER2, HER2 positive breast cancer was associated with an unfavorable prognosis. The use of HER2-targeted therapies have significantly improved overall survival rates both in the adjuvant scenario and in metastatic disease. New drugs have emerged in recent years that block this pathway, which has allowed the establishment of several lines of treatment with HER2 therapy in patients with metastatic disease.
