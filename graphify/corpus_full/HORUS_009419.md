# THEORY OF ELASTICITY USING MATLAB AND MAXIMA.

This book brings the theory of elasticity to the undergraduate student of Civil or Mechanical Engineering, through the physical-mathematic introspection and step-by-step deduction of each of the fundamental equations of elasticity. In fact, what the classic book of Timoshenko and Goodier deals with in sixty pages here is broken down into about three hundred. All this with a view to giving the student a solid foundation for subsequent courses on the method of finite elements, computational mechanics or plasticity. This work gathers the teaching experience of more than twelve years of the author; his writing was carried out with the permanent feedback of the students. The text makes use of the free program of symbolic algebra Maxima, to deduce the majority of complex equations, and presents Matlab codes that serve to illustrate numerically the examples. The translation of these codes to Python is found on the author's website, in Jupyter Notebooks format. The content is also complemented by videos of his authorship, available on YouTube
