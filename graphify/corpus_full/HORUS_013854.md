# Plantation Inside

_x0093_Plantación Adentro_x0094_ is a creative research project that materializes in a transmedia performance that, through experimentation with photography, video, body and certain kinds of organic materials, seeks to bring viewers to the heart of an image. In a digital operation _x0093_in situ_x0094_, a photograph appears to be unearthed, apparently everyday, to reveal different layers of historical, social, racial and environmental problems. In addition to the staging, the scriptural project (the footprint) is presented in a _x0093_Photo-Book_x0094_ that accounts for the childhood memories that passed into a sugar mill in the Cauca Valley. In it, images of family archives, documentary photographs and self-portraits that reflect the past and the present are juxtaposed. In this narrative, a question is opened for the future of a territory and its community
