# NEW CASE: MEDICAMENTALIZING THE INCERTISMBER: THE CASE OF HYDROXICLOROCHINE DURING THE PANDEMIA BY COVID-19?

The pandemics are the perfect scenario for irrational behaviors to flourish, due to the uncertainty that characterizes these unknown situations. During the pandemic by COVID-19, little or nothing was known about the new virus and about what would be the most appropriate approach to deal with it, so one of the first evidence of irrationality was the increase in drug use, whose utility against the virus had not been rigorously evaluated. The case that best describes this situation was that of hydroxychloroquin in the first months of the pandemic (Informed Decisions on Medicines [DIME], 2020)
