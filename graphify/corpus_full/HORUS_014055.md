# Continuous improvement plan of the Organic Chemistry laboratory

The Laboratory of Organic Chemistry is responsible for the teaching laboratories in organic chemistry and the introduction to biochemistry for different engineering and science courses at Medellín headquarters. It is currently in a continuous improvement plan, following the findings made by the Coordination of both areas and the Section of Safety and Health in the Trajo. Among the improvement activities are the preventive and corrective maintenance of the laboratory teams, giving priority to those of routine use in the different practices that are dictated in this space and that to date are a total of 25 in the two subjects. In the near future, the users and the number of practices will increase, due to the opening of the undergraduate program in chemistry, at Medellín Headquarters, whose procedure is in progress
