# Teaching sequence for teaching and learning the Periodic Table in secondary basic.

The process of teaching the exact and natural sciences requires new teaching strategies that will provide students with interest and motivation towards learning chemistry, taking into account the above, a didactic sequence was designed and implemented with the aim of strengthening through the game the concept of the periodic table in the students of the ninth grade of the Golden Educational Institution of the municipality of La Dorada. To carry out this purpose, a pretest of previous ideas was applied that allowed to diagnose the strengths and obstacles presented by the students on the concept, then with the results obtained a didactic sequence based on the game was designed and implemented as a methodological strategy to favor the teaching process learning the periodic table concept, then the students answered the initial test as a post-test test with the aim of comparing the results found before and after the application of the didactic sequence seeking to determine whether there was a conceptual change in their learning. The analysis of results allowed to conclude that the didactic proposal was appropriate for teaching and learning the periodic table because students overcome their initial obstacles and increased their strengths by demonstrating a greater motivation for the new strategies implemented
