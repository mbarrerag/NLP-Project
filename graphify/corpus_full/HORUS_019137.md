# RISK STUDY IN NATURAL COLOMBIAN SCENARIES WITH POOTENTIAL USE FOR ANALOGUES SPACE MISSIONS

Similar space missions are an economic alternative for conducting research and training for space missions in the face of space itself. This method is used by the different agencies and organizations of the space sector around the world and has allowed for more than 50 years to develop training and research programs for the different missions. This project aims to determine the types of scenarios and similar missions that can be carried out operationally in Colombia and Antarctica. Four phases were carried out: observational descriptive study based on bibliographic review and webgraphy, field trips, DOFA analysis of each scenario and risk panorama. It is concluded with the risks identified for the four areas of study visited, 1) Antarctic King George Island, 2) Tatacoa Desert, 3) Villa de Leyva Desert, 4) Ruiz snowfall and its mitigation measures
