# VISUAL TRANSMISSIONS TO THE MULTIPACIES OF THE CONTAGIOUS PATHOLOGIES OF IMAGES

The Visual Transmissions project. In view of the multiple pathologies of images, it explores the technical images protected in public or private institutions at a transnational level, including specific attention to the implications of Covid-19. With a wide scope that integrates advances in digital information and recognizes the influence of local and global relationships in the production of images and technologies, this project is involved in the analysis of isolated images, collections and archives of scientific institutions. Its approach is not only limited to examining the images themselves, but also to the patterns and pathologies that they reveal. Within the framework of this project, three cases of study are addressed: models of exhibition of sick bodies, showing non-human animals during the pandemic and colonial models in botanical gardens. These cases provide a complete view of the complexity of the technical images, highlighting the importance of recognizing the limitations and subjectivities in the interpretation of these scientific images. In addition, the Visual Transmissions project is placed in the broader context of the project. Do you act in the emergency?, thus reinforcing your commitment to the response and critical understanding of the current situation, in particular in relation to the Covid-19 crisis
