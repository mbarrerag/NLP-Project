# The Non-Commutative Brillouin Torus a Non-Commutative Geometry perspective

The non-conmutative geometry has found a fertile field of examples in quantum mechanics and has provided rigorous mathematical tools to extract topological and geometric information from those systems. This thesis is an incursion into some of the tools used by Jean Bellissard and collaborators for the analysis of homogeneous materials. With this in mind, our first step is to examine the objects used in that analysis, which are a pair of topological algebras designed to extend the scope of Fourier analysis over Td to the study of strong bond models for homogeneous materials. These algebras are presented as a generalization of C(Td) and C ↔(Td) and are considered as a soft non-conmutative variety in the field of Non-Conmutative Geometry, to which we refer as the Top-Brillouin No Conmutative Bull. We explore as various techniques of the analysis of this mild ind variety can be translated into the Frillouin Non-Conmutative Bull, for example, the coefficients of Fourier and the sum of Fejer, and how these techniques allow us to capture the top-toological structure and soft-soft Fourier.
