# PROPOSAL FOR METHODOLOGICAL RUTA FOR THE CONSIDERATION OF ORGANISED CRIME AND ITS RELATIONSHIP WITH THE TERRITORY.

In the fourth, the authors make an analytical effort to operationalize the conceptual categories of analysis and understanding of organized crime, with a methodological proposal of a mixed approach, which balances the quantitative and qualitative information of organized crime, supported also by spatial analysis tools, to achieve a very localized and defined mapping of organized crime activity and the actors of armed conflict. This proposal defines the theoretical assumptions and the operationalization represented by its analysis. Finally, the authors present for consideration by the readers a model of interview to be applied to people of civil society, to local and national authorities involved in the security of the regions under study. These interviews seek to recognize the activity of illegal economies and their relationship with the presence and action of organized crime structures and armed groups present in the territory
