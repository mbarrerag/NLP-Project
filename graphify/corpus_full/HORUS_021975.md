# Learning model to structure data from yellow machine life sheets.

The lack of relevant information for decision-making is one of the major problems faced by maintenance departments in companies. This thesis explores an automatic method to contribute to the solution of this problem by extracting relevant information from historical records of maintenance activities carried out in the teams. Given the nature of the data, unstructured text with technical language, it is proposed the implementation of different representations (bag of words, term frequency-inverse document frequency, Fasttext and Doc2vec) to feed the models of machine learning that perform the structuring of important information contained in the documents. In the search of the model with better performance models of support vector machine, random forest, Gaussian naive bayes and gradient boosting trees. These models were applied to data from a sales and rental business of yellow machinery. 12 models of 3 different models and 4 independent variables were considered to extract information: typology, found, tregradous, state and system.
