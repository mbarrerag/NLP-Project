# 5G Network Deployment in Colombia: Simulation and Analysis of Performance Metrics

This final master's work focused on the study and simulation of scenarios of end-to-end deployment of 5G networks, both current and planned, in an urban residential area of Colombia. 5G networks implementation scenarios were analyzed in independent (SA) and non-independent (NSA) configurations, comparing results with 4G networks were analyzed. For simulation, the NS-3 free software simulator, based on C++, selected after a literature review due to its modularity, versatility and online support. This simulator generates performance metrics such as throughput, jitter, latency and power, represented in the main REM (Radio Environment Map) maps, which, according to the Colombian regulations (Resolution 3947 of the MinTIC), allows to evaluate the quality of the service and coverage. Analysis and tests of two modules NS3 5G channel will be performed: 5G-LENA which was developed in the CTTC (Centre Tecnologicógic of Telecomunications of Catalonia) and mmWave that is developed by NYU WIRELESS and Sharp Laboratities of America. These two modules are based in the first generation of the first generation of the second generation of the first. The metrics obtained in the simulation, an analysis was carried out and recommendations were made for the deployment of 5G networks in Colombia, identifying challenges and possible future lines of work that could contribute to an efficient and agile deployment .
