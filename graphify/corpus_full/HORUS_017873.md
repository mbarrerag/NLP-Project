# The zaguán as a space connector its instrumentalization between the interior and the exterior

In the present T.F.M., an autonomous and continuous structure was designed that has as axis articulating the development and organization of the spaces in the architecture as proposed for the connection between buildings and the connection between the exterior and interior of the same ones, under the concept of transitional change and continuity of the Zaguán, applied in the eastern edge of the Barrio Las Nieves, located in Bogotá. The Zaguán that is between two limits, develops in the space by means of the conjunction of formal and functional elements, within which circumstances determine a duple nature are combined. Is an outside or an inside nature? where its parts form an ambivalent relationship of limit between the exterior and interior. It is the Zaguán, whose function is to correspond its different parts and activities, which make the building configured. The work is elaborated with an approach from the point of view in the characterization and study of the zaguans of the Spanish world and analogous spaces in contemporary architecture. The structure is designed within a building of mixed use associated with the above concepts. tionship of limit between the outside and inside It is the Zaguán, what function is to match its different parts and activities, which make the building take shape. The work is elaborated with a focus from the point of view on the characterization and study of the entry halls of the Hispanic-American world and analogue spaces in contemporary architectures. The structure is designed within a mixed-use building partnered with the enabled concepts. With the autonomous and continuous structure, it is intended to aim a structuring instrument for the organization of spaces and redefined the hallway as a space of interest
