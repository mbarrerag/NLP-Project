# WATER MATHEMATIC MODEL WE'RE THROUGH FOR A MARKET WITHOUT STOCK

In this paper, we propose and analyze a system of ordinary differential equations that describes the dynamics of a stockless market. We take as variables of state supply, demand and production capacity in development, defined in terms of an investment decision function, which in turn depends on the expected return on the investment or return obtained. This investment function takes three different values in three specific regions contained in the first octant, which are separated by two switching surfaces. The proposed system presents non-linearity and non-softness by the nature of the functions that determine it, which makes the system either Piecewise Smooth (PWS) or soft to stages. Then, we study the behavior of the system flows in the switching planes by means of filippov analysis, determining in these planes the points where sliding or crossing occurs, as well as the calculation and analysis of the pseudo-balances. Finally, we study the change of the investment decisions in the points of the switching surfaces, including randomness by means of risk aversion matrices.
