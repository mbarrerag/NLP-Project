# BOREOUT SYNDROME: A RISK EMERGING IN THE CONTEXT OF WORK

With the 4th industrial revolution, a trend is emerging linked to the development and use of new information and communication technologies, as well as the immersion of artificial intelligence (AI) within work environments. This reality brings with it a series of opportunities and the reduction of some problems in work contexts. However, there is also an opening up to discussion issues such as the closing of some jobs, new forms of recruitment, the emergence of more repetitive jobs, the protection of personal privacy and the separation of work-family areas (Foladori & Ortiz-Espinoza, 2022)
