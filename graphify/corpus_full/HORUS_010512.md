# Narrative templates and attitudes to mobilization processes

Understanding support for social mobilization processes requires identifying the narrative templates that are implemented for their explanation, associated with opposing political ideologies. The above since it would be possible to understand the underlying reasons in the mobilization processes and the support provided to them. The hypothesis of this study was that the narrative templates, used by the participants, and associated with a political ideology will predict the attitudes towards mobilization. A non-experimental – cross-predictive research was carried out, with 138 participants (83 women and 55 men) with an average age of 28.33 years (SD = 11.09). Three scales were designed to measure the narratives and attitudes towards mobilization. The political ideology was measured with the Norton and Tan Scale (2019), and according to the report of the participants. The analysis of the data was carried out through multiple linear regression models and structural equations. In general, the strategy was revealed to the predictive character of the political ideology for the preferred use of a specific template, which in turn predicts a particular attitude to the social mobilizations in Colombia.
