# Determination and characterization of the regions of PfRON4 binding to human erythrocytes and hepatocytes.

Plasmodium falciparum during its life cycle, expresses a wide range of proteins between which the neck protein of the roptria 4 (PfRON4). This is a candidate for vaccine promise, as it is expressed both in merzoites and sporozoitous cells (this is the case with the formation of strong bond with the host cell through the complex RONs/AMA1 and is refractory to genetic deletion.However, the key regions of this antigen interacting with host cells are not yet known, being this information of great use to combat the disease caused by this parasite. For this reason, in this research work 32 peptides derived from the preserved PfRON4 region were synthesized and the receiving-ligating interaction tests were carried out to determine the binding capacity of each peptide to host cells, and the heptidile heptiforms were identified
