# DESCRIPTION OF PEDIGRY, ENDOGAMIA AND FREQUENCY OF SOME GENETIC DESIGN MARKERS IN BLANK WIN (BON) OF COLOMBIA

The White orejinegro (BON) cattle is a genetic resource of Colombia, with characteristics of adaptation to the mountainous tropics. However, it has been replaced by specialized cattle, which decreased its population, with the consequent increase of endogamy Objective: estimating the coefficient of inbreeding (by pedigree and genomic) of the BON cattle, describing the pedigree and genealogical connections between populations, and estimating the frequencies of markers for genetic disorders in the population. The information collected in 14 BON productions from different regions of Colombia was used. The information included animals born between 1981 and 2018. The package Optisel of R was used for the analysis of pedigree and the Cytoscape program to make graphs, using parents or mothers as nodes to observe the connections between populations. The endogamy were proportionally 420 animals for the extraction of DNA and the genotype was estimated by the GGP Bovine50K chip. The analysis and editing of genomic data was performed with R and Plink software.
