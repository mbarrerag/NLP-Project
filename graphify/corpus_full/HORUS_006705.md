# Effectiveness of the plan to interrupt the vector transmission of Trypanosoma cruzi by Rhodnius prolixus in reducing the incidence of Chagas disease in certified municipalities of the department of Boyacá 2012-2022

The Chagas disease (EC) attributable to the vector transmission of Trypanosoma cruzi is a public health problem at Latin America level, In Colombia strategies have been implemented as.the interruption of the vector transmission of Chagas disease in endemic municipalities. However, this strategy is focused only on one vector: Rhodnius prolixus forgetting the presence of the parasite in secondary vectors such as the Triatoma dimidiata, which represents a risk of parasitic transmission for the population of municipalities where it is believed that the vector transmission of the disease is controlled, which significantly hinders the sustainability of the vector control programs implemented in departments such as Boyacá. To determine the effectiveness of the plan of interruption of the intradomicillary vector transmission of Trypanosoma cruzi by Rhodnius prolixus in terms of the incidence of Chagas disease in the municipality of Boyacá in 2012-2022. Complete ecological study of the intradomicillaria type (multiple groups and temporal tendencies), developed with data of secondary sources: the results of Chagas disease disease in the municipality of the municipality. The Commission has been able to assess the impact of the measures in question. Directed at secondary vectors. While it is true that a significant decrease in R. prolixus is observed after the certification of the municipalities, positive secondary vectors such as Triatoma dimidiata pose a risk for the presence of new cases of Chagas
