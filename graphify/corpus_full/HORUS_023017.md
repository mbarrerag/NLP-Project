# FROM THE PHILOSOPHY OF MATEMATICS TO THE MATHEMATIC CRITICS: A STEP FOR THE WORK OF FERNANDO ZALAMEA

The chapter shows the evolution of Zalamea's thought regarding the philosophy of mathematics from his first essay on the theme "Synthetic philosophy of mathematics" to his recent work on a proposal for a mathematical critique
