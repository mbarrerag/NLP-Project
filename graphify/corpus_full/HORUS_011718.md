# Low Dose Thymoglobulin Versus BASILIXIMAB IN CYTOMEGALOVIRUS POSITIVE KIDNEY TRANSPLANT RECIPIENTS: EFFECTIVENESS OF PREEMPTIVIE CYTOMEGALOVIRUS MODIFIED STRATEGY

We conducted a retrospective study to determine reactivation and disease by CMV in HIV-positive CMV renal transplant receptors under different induction schemes. A modified preventive strategy under induction with basiliximab and thymoglobulin at low doses was evaluated. We carried out a follow-up of viral load-chain reaction of polymerase-CMV; the values greater than 4000 copies/l received valdenciclovir adjusted to renal function. A total of 132 renal transplant receptors were included; 84 and 48 received induction with basiliximab and thymoglobulin respectively. Follow-up up to month 12 post-transplantation. Asymptomatic reactivation of CMV was significantly greater for thymoglobulin (77.1% vs. 16.7%, p < 0.001). The rate of disease by CMV was similar in both treatment groups (3.6% vs. 2.1%, p = 0.538).
