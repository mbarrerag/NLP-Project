# The search as central action and the spectrum as figure of the action: figuration devices in a contemporary theatre that expresses the relationship between theater, history and memory in Colombia

The reading of the works A memory in obl forgotten, If the river speaks... and Labio de Liebre gives the privilege of the “search” as a central action, and of the character “spect” as a figure of the action; both studied as devices of figuration of a contemporary theatre that, even committed to the representation of the recent history of the Colombian armed conflict, gives account of approaches, dialogues and relations with the social practice of memory. The above, from two aspects that influence the operation of plot configuration: first, the representation of victims as characters in memory, in the condition of “spect” given their elimination of the social plane and their demands of truth, recognition and dignity; second, the incorporation into the drama of rememor ritual, mechanisms of remembrance, reminder mechanisms, tensions or practical or artistic procedures that, being specific to the exercises of memory, are incorporated, now, as literary and theatrical resources that unfold together with the search action undertaken by the characters.
