# Leveraging the malt residue of the cerveza industry for the production of fermentable sugars as a technical platform

Much of the residual biomass produced at industrial level has a great potential for obtaining platforms of interest that allow in turn, the obtaining of different products with added value.This is the case of the brewing industry, which generates significant quantities of residues in the production process of beer, among them, the bagasse of beer which is the result of processing malta using different unitary operations.Although it has been tried to find several uses to this residue, among them the production of energy, it does not take advantage of the great potential that has this residue thanks to its attractive chemical composition.The bagasse of beer has appreciable quantities of cellulose, hemicellulose and lignin that, using the appropriate technologies allow to obtain compounds that serve as technological platforms for the production of a large amount of compounds with added value.In this sense, the fermentable sugars can be obtained from the residual biomass, the bioenergy, biomologulose and other technologies such as the fermentables. The panorama is very similar, produced about 2,26 million tons of beer per year, which generate about 445,000 tons of beer bagasse. This residue in addition to producing in large quantities, presents a chemical composition very attractive to be used as raw material in obtaining other compounds of interest.
