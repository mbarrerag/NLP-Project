# BICHOS FOR ALL: DEMOCRATISATION OF TAXONOMIC INFORMATION

Taxonomy information is vital in many entomological, agricultural, biodiversity studies, epidemiological surveillance, among others. In this talk I present an argument about why taxonomy information generated by scientists plays a predominant role, and how it can be promoted through different non-conventional means, positively impacting scientific work
