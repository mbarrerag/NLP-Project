# TOXICITY IN THE MOST ADULTATION FROM THE OCGERIATRY PERSPECTIVE AND PALIATIVE CARE PART 1

Cancer is a chronic non-transmissible disease, the prevalence of which is increasing due to population ageing. Approximately 50% of new cancer diagnoses occur in a population over 65 years of age. Age has been identified as a risk factor for cancer. Given limited evidence in relation to treatment schemes regarding chemotherapy, radiation therapy and immunotherapy in the elderly, adverse outcomes resulting from toxicity have been demonstrated in systems such as tegumentary, cardiovascular, renal or neurological, which have an impact on adherence to treatment and the quality of life of patients. In this first series of two publications for non-oncologists, the aim is to describe in a timely manner the various types of toxicity, to provide early detection tools for symptoms associated with toxicity
