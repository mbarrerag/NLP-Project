# Social Metabolism, Sustainability and Territory in the Bogotá River

This research proposes a model of interpretation of the sustainability of a territory that articulates the territorial perspective of geography and social metabolism as a focus of environmental studies, which has been conceived as the exchange of matter, energy and information in the relationship nature-society. The work provides a theoretical and methodological proposal of Territorial Metabolism, as a model of integral analysis of sustainability that is structured through four categories of analysis: the cycle of information on water and energy, the coverage of the land, the hydrosocial cycle and the main energy flows. The methodological design was mixed, with systemic approach, based on methodological pluralism and triangulation of qualitative and quantitative information was carried out according to the categories mentioned. This model of interpretation of sustainability emerged from the analysis carried out in an area of the upper basin of the Bogotá river, including the municipalities of Villapinzón, Chocontá and Susca, a scenario that was characterized by its socio-economic-territorial dynamics in historical perspective and, also, from the factors that guided the main socio-ecological transformations and territorial configurations from the second half of the 20th century to the present.
