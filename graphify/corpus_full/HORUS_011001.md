# Dynamic co-movement analysis among oil prices, green bonds, and CO2 emissions, 2014-2022.

This research addresses the gap in existing literature on how oil prices, green bonds and CO2 emissions are related to each other. In addition, the short- and long-term relationships of co-movements between these important variables are investigated in the context of the global energy transition, using an automatic learning model. Therefore, the main objective of this study is to analyze the results of short- and long-term co-movements and implications for researchers, investors and policy makers. To validate the analysis, we use daily data of oil prices, green bonds and CO2 emissions from 2014 to 2022. In addition, a scientometric analysis of the main methodologies to measure co-movements between financial markets, using techniques such as the analysis of (i) sources, (ii) authors, (iii) documents, and (iv) analysis of the concrete clusters (Wavelet) and Werctrum models are used. In addition, this research applies methodologies such as the causality test of Granger, the dynamic conditional correlation (Dynamic Conditional Correction, DCC-Garch)
