# Architectural restoration of the Santa Ana railway station in the municipality of Sasaima - Cundinamarca.

This project focused on the proposal for an intervention project at the Santa Ana railway station, in Sasaima, as an infrastructure inherited from the National Railways that were liquidated in 1995 and that left in abandonment not only this building if not all the railway stations of the country, generating a deterioration situation that has caused a state of conservation of the improvement which has caused a state of ruin in the building. In the Santa Ana station, this abandonment situation caused structural elements and carpentry to have been degraded and collapsed, among them the roof that has allowed a constant income of rain water that has caused a lot of deteriorations inside the structure. Considering the importance of this building with regard to the territory implanted, the system to which it has made part and especially to the community adjacent to this, who have adapted culturally from the property, proposes an intervention that binds and articulates a cultural endowment and an educational service to the rural area of this municipality; which has seen in the heritage built an opportunity factor to respond effectively to some needs of the population; and with the intervention of the construction, it is proposed to remedy the pathologies and to of rainwater that has caused a large amount of damage to the interior of the structure.
