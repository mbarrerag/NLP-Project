# Clinical and genomic studies of Alzheimer's and Parkinson's diseases in regions of Colombia

Modern genetics allows to advance in the knowledge of the different human pathologies in specific geographic, population and family contexts. In this sense, recognize the particularities of the main neurodegenerative diseases, Alzheimer and Parkinson, in different regions of the country, constitutes a need to advance in personalized medicine and in the establishment of prevention and management programs in accordance with the particularities of our mestizo. Very general studies indicate a prevalence per 1,000 inhabitants of 13.1 for dementias (the Alzheimer's E, the main one of them), and 4.7 for Parkinson's in our environment. The regional differences are not clear, particularly between those selected in this project, located by the UNAL headquarters participants of the alliance. In this sense, the sample sizes will be made by convenience. It is also a question of critically assimilate the developments of scientists and new clinical and genetic methodologies, contributes to the unity of the two different and particularities studies, among other aspects. It promotes also the developments of the academic community of the University, both in undergraduate and post-graduate research groups, emphasizing in the formation of researchers, in the processes of the different public health and the regions. The researchers of the collaborating consortia and researchers of the groups of the Bogotá headquarters participants, integrating those of other headquarters. The group of Telematics and Telecommunications that participates in this project will carry out analysis of usability, user design, development of virtual platform and Android iOS application and pilot test and testing of operation with the sample of study, in the different centers. As a particular aspect, the Research Group in Orinoquia Sciences seeks the consolidation of the Orinoquía Headquarters of the National University of Colombia as an entity promoter of scientific research, in an environment where knowledge about the community and biodiversity contributes to the conservation of natural resources, progress and innovation and self-sustainable development of the region. It will allow, in particular, to expand the lines of research of the Headquarters in the area of human health, important for the development of the communities of the region, among other aspects. With the variables and results obtained, different statistical analysis will be carried out, among others, significance for categorists, multivariates, main components.
