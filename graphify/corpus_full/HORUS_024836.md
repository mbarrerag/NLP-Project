# ASSESSMENT OF BLOOD PARAMETERS IN TITÍ GRIS (SAGUINUS LEUCOPUS GÜNTHER 1876) BY PORTABLE MEASUREMENT EQUIPMENT BY ELECTRIC POINT

Saguinus leucopus is an endemic neotropical primate in Colombia, in danger of extinction and of which there are few publications in the medical area, being these necessary for its correct clinical management.The objective of this study was to evaluate the values of hemoglobin, creatinine, blood ureic nitrogen, ion calcium, sodium, potassium, chlorine, glucose, total carbon dioxide and anionic gap in 31 captive animals of the species S. leucopus, using a measuring equipment by electrical potential (I-Stat Analyzer ¿ CHEM8+, Abbott, USA).The values obtained were analyzed by descriptive statistics.The normal distribution of the data was verified by means of the Shapiro Wilk test, and the difference between groups was performed by means of the t-student test for parametric variables, and the Mann Whitney test (U test) was based on the differences for non-parametric variables.The statistical differences were found in the hemoglobin and haematocrit were greater in males (hto: 49± 3,634; hb: 17,5 ± 1,228)
