# EMOTION ATLAS Emotions expressed in the territories of the lower Attrato

There is an inexorable relationship between affection, body and territory; between those who live and live in it. And a context like the Colombian one with a systematic violence that has made the territories wounded bodies that need to be healed, even more so with the Peace Agreement signed in 2016 partially, makes it a highly sensitive scenario to investigate the role of emotions in the new spatial and affective relations that are being established with the territories. An example of this is what happens in the lower Attrato, historically a forced cross of forgotten and unnamed worlds and dispossession dynamics. To address a research that analyzes the features of emotions expressed spatially in the territories, requires a qualitative methodological approach that, together with the artistic teaching methodologies, will seek through workshops, semi-structured interviews, informal conversations and observations photographic and audiovisual participants, build a pilot of the Atlas of the Emotions in the lower Attrato. That besides approaching the theoretical debate of emotions to the territory, build new interpretatil categories that read from the relationality new forms of association between the natural and cultural, the tangible and the intangible, the objective and the interpretation and the objective that they are able to explain
