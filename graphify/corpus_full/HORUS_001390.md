# Tuberculous pericarditis in a patient exposed to silicon oxide. Case report with diagnostic imaging approach

The case of a patient with tuberculose pericarditis, an uncommon form of tuberculosis with a high morbidity and mortality. This report is a contribution to the medical literature as it represents a complete review of the approach by images of this pathology. Presentation of the case. 52-year-old man with exposure to silicon oxide, who was referred to the Intensive Care Unit of a fourth level of complexity hospital in the city of Bogotá (Colombia) with hyperdensus pericardial effusion, initially considered of malignant etiology. An imagelogic follow-up with suggestive results of tuberculosa pericarditis, was performed, so that a pericardiocentesis was performed whose microbiological and molecular detection studies in pericardial fluid were negative for Mycobacterium tuberculosis and fungi; only the adenosine deaminosa test (ADA) was positive, which resulted in a suspicion of tuberculosa pericarditis.
