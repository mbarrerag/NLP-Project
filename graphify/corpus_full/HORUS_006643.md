# 35 years of fiscal decentralization in Colombia: analysis of departmental economic growth 1984-2019

The relationship between fiscal decentralization and economic growth has been a source of great discussion in economic literature, with broad evidence that supports different directions of this. This document generates significant evidence for the clarification of the aforementioned relationship in Colombia, for this purpose a model of instrumental variables is realized for departments in the period 1984-2019. From the application of this, there is a negative and significant relationship between economic growth and fiscal decentralization, measured as the proportion of tax revenues in total income, result that is robust before temporary and spatial subdivisions of the sample. In the document also it reviews the interaction between fiscal decentralization by transfers and economic growth, finding a positive and significant relationship throughout the period, but with nuances around its temporal and geographical reproduction, since it is found evidence that the transformation of transfers in the period of time has favoured the growth of regional disparities in the country .
