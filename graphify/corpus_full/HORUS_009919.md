# Analysis of aspects of the sustainability of scuba diving in San Andres island, Colombia

Recreational diving is a tourist activity with great aspirations for the island of San Andrés. The structural, functional, ecological, and aesthetic qualities of the island's coral reef ecosystems motivate visitors to engage in diving, leading to an increasing popularity of the activity. The foundation of this activity lies in the utilization of marine biodiversity. Therefore, ensuring its viability and prominence requires a sustainable approach, which involves respecting and promoting the conservation of marine ecosystems, strengthening the social and cultural values of involved stakeholders, fostering collective environmental awareness, and generating income and opportunities to enhance the local community's quality of life in line with their plans and aspirations. This research aimed to explore, understand, and characterize recreational diving on San Andrés Island. Subsequently, it was analyzed through a sustainable lens, incorporating ecological, economic, and social aspects of its development, while considering the interconnections among different stakeholders. The investigative process utilized both qualitative and quantitative methods to gather valuable information for the proper management and handling of the activity, seeking to find synergies between tourism development and ecosystem conservation
