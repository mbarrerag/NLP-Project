# Las Cruces without fear: re-signifying the urban heritage image, with safe and accessible environments

This document presents an urban project whose objective is to re-signify the deteriorated urban image of Barrio Las Cruces. This deterioration is the result of a historical spatial and social decline, which led to the stigmatization of the neighborhood. This stigmatization, together with the experiences of uncertainty and current insecurity, has led to the neighborhood being avoided or isolated from the rest of the city under a perception of fear. The urban design project was aimed at addressing the problem of spatial fear in the central urban areas and answering the question: “How to design and give a new meaning to places with perceptions of fear in a heritage context?” Although, the central position in the city of the Las Cruces neighborhood, and its set of unique physical, social and urban characteristics, rooted in its history, currently has a deteriorated urban image, in a heritage context full of values, emotions, stigma and neglect. However, this situation offers an opportunity to extract and identify design parameters or pillars of intervention, with the purpose of improving the quality of the space, changing perceptions and experiences of the place, turning it into a SAFEGURO AND TRANSFUNCIONAL LUGAR.
