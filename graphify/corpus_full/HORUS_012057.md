# CARTOGRAPH AND COLONIZATION IN THE CHICO: THE MELCHOR MAP OF SALAZAR, 1596

The objective of this article is to analyze the map of the provinces of Chocó made in 1596 by Melchor de Salazar, who served as governor of this territory between 1592 and 1594. It is a plan made in order to explain to the members to the Council of Indias the importance of a vast space, rich in gold minerals and dense in native population. With cartographic tools and the combination of different manuscript sources that rest in several archives, will examine ethnographic information (the different indigenous nations that inhabited Chocó), geographical (mountainary systems, rivers and islands) and political (cities, villas and real mines) that contains this map, and that allows to understand how the process of incorporating a border and marginal region into the Spanish empire was carried out. Key words: cartography, Indians, Chocó, urban centers
