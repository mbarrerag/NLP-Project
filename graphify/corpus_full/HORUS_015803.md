# ASSESSMENT OF MICRO DESIGN SUSPENSIONS OF MAGNETITE FE3O4 IN SOLAR RADIATION COLLECTORS

In recent years, the processes of generation and heat transfer have had significant growth in applications based on magnetic particle suspensions that interact with alternate magnetic fields. Consequently, induction heaters and ferrofluids have improved several thermal applications. Last update: March 4, 2020 CEA 2 Magazine Template Some examples of these are based on systems that use nanofluid in solar collectors and induction heaters, which compensate for the variability of radiation to improve the performance of the system, which constitutes an advantage for continuous heat generation. This work estimated the gain in internal energy of suspensions of 0.5 and 4% of volumetric concentration of micrometric magnetite (Fe 3 O 4 ) in water and coolant, using a magnetic induction heater that operates at 15A/48V. In addition, the energy associated with thermal jumps was evaluated. On the other hand, the compositional and distribution characterization of the size of magnetite particles were performed using DRX, SEM-EDS and its magnetic beam diffraction.
