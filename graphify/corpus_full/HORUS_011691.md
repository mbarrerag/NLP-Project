# Challenges for national universality of colombia in marine science

The university institution is a millennial organization that has expanded in the world since the Middle Ages to this day, its success is of such a calibre that it has very few precedents. It is recognized that university systems are essential for progress and development, there are no truly advanced countries that do not have an effective university system. The great milestones or revolutions in history have been accompanied by research that is born from education. Facts such as agricultural, industrial, technical and green revolutions, have had universities as knowledge managers for the development of the same and of society in general. The university system in Colombia is based on three mission axes: Research, Training and Extension, that is, that for an institution of higher education to be considered university, it must guarantee generation, appropriation and transfer of knowledge.These three mission axes or essential pillars support as a fundamental objective the formation of human capital (Figure 20.1). The three pillars must have the same relevance for the achievement of the final purpose of the institution
