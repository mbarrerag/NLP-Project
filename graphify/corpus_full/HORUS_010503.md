# MILENARY RUPESTRE ART. COLOMBIAN AMAZONIA

The general guideline of the museum exhibition first highlights the antiquity of the Rupestre Art as one of the first manifestations of art in the human being and cultural element considered an integral part of the genesis that characterizes our Homo Sapiens Sapiens species. Secondly, the great importance of the Amazonian Rupest Art in Colombia is highlighted thanks to the high density of drawings and the dense spatial distribution that makes it one of the most important ancient known art deposits in the world. Indeed, among the paintings existing in the Serranía de la Lindosa and the Serranía del Chiribiquete its number is calculated in more than one hundred thousand drawings painted on its walls. Likewise, the petroglyphs recently discovered in the upper part of the Itilla River are added to those already known on the margins and rapids of the Caquetá Rivers, Apaporis, Guainía and Río Negro among so many others. Thirdly, we highlight the importance of the millenary configuration of `Altos Places' considered cots of the origin of life and abode of gods and beings that control the nature and their relationship with Amazon
