# Reforms to the electricity tariff system in Colombia

This Final Master's Work presents proposals for change to the tariff system of the Colombian electricity sector, with the aim of improving the shortcomings of the current model. To this end, the functioning of the electricity market in Colombia, including its tariff scheme and subsidies, was explained in order to understand the origin of the failures in the system. In addition, a review was carried out on the literature on tariff models of other countries that present similarities in the electricity generation matrix or in the social context, characterizing the selected models. From this review, the effect of the different models on the companies of the sector and on Colombian users was evaluated. Finally, it was concluded on the feasibility and potential benefits of implementing proposals such as the Tariff Flags, the Time Rates and the complete liberalization of the retail market, adapted to the Colombian context. .
