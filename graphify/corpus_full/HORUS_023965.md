# CONVERGENCE BY COLOMBIA. IDEAS FROM UNIVERSITY FOR CONSTRUCTIVE DIALOGUES.

Convergence for Colombia is a collective initiative based on the recognition that universities can better respond to our social responsibilities if we work together and articulated with other institutions and communities. We must jointly address the current crises and their effects, understanding the particularities of our territories and the national context, in order to find solutions to the problems that afflict us as a society
