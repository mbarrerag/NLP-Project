# ISOTOPIC COMPOSITION OF 2H, 3H, 18O, 87SR/86SR, 13CDIC, 13CDOC, 14C AND CFCS OF THE MAGDALENA VALLEY.

Isotopia is a tool that allows to achieve an understanding on the knowledge of the origin of the groundwater and its flow patterns, since by identifying recharge zones, flow paths, discharge zones and estimation of transit times, it supports the conceptualization that is made on the functioning of a hydrogeological system.In this sense, approximately 250 samples were collected from different sources (rain, springs, rivers, swamps, water wells and wells with depths between 10 and 350 m) during the rainy season in November 2020 and from the stretch in March 2021 in the Middle Valley of Magdalena. 2H, 18O were analyzed and, depending on the depth of the wells, 3H, 87Sr86Sr, 13CDOC, 14C, CFC-11 and CFC-113. From the isotopic composition of 2H, 18O the rain samples collected in Aguachica, Puerto Wilches and Barrancabermeja are proposed a local meteoric line (Does:2¿, CFC-11 and CFC-113. From the rainfall, 18M allows the most economical and non-life). The 3H was measured in springs, water and wells up to 80 m, obtaining values between 0.1 and 2.3 UT, suggesting from the information of the NGPI ages between 1995 and current waters. For the analysis of CFC-11 and CFC-113, the piston flow model was selected, obtaining ages between 1972 and 1987. 14C was measured in wells with depths greater than 80 m, obtaining average ages from the samples of 26950 years to current waters. The relationship between 13CDIC and 13CDOC shows a differentiation between the samples collected in the north and south zones, probably associated with the difference in vegetation in these sectors. Concentrations in the 87Sr/86Sr ratio allow identifying flows in the east-west direction, and suggest washing of calcareous material from the cenozoic on the eastern ridge and interactions with rivers and fluvial flows towards the Magdalena river. These results show the hydrogeological complexity of the VMM, where connections can be identified depending on the analysis scale
