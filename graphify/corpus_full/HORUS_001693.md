# Effects of creatine, L-carnitine, beta-alanine or caffeine consumption as means for increased strength and endurance in trained adults. Systematic review and meta-analysis

The exercise, a planned recreational, improves physical capacities such as strength and resistance, providing well-being to its practitioners, however, Ergogenic Supplements (SE) such as Creatina, L-carnitine, Beta-alanine and Cafein are used to increase performance in these physical capabilities. This leads to recreational sportsrs using supplements in order to improve their sport performance. The aim of the study was to evaluate the effects of ergonomic supplementation with Creatine, L-carnitin, Beta-alanine and Cafein in increasing strength and resistance in trained adults, through a systematic review with meta-analysis.
