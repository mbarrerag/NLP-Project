# Coastal geomorphology in the numerical simulation of the tsunami threat and its effects on the South Pacific of Colombia

The Colombian Pacific Coast concludes on a zone of seismotonic activity in which there are events generating tsunamis – such as those of 1906 and 1979–, which can affect populations and settlements located on the coasts; mouths of rivers and esteros of the departments of Nariño and Cauca. This work focused on studying the effects that some geomorphological units may have on the reduction of the threat caused by flooding caused by a tsunami. Initially a geological, geomorphological and seismotonic characterization of the study area was carried out. Subsequently, a review of the literature was carried out to select a numerical model of tsunami simulation, the definition and subsequent simulation of a set of 132 scenarios, which allowed obtaining flood maps of the study area for different types of generating events recorded in the literature (Sánchez et al., 2020). Results similar to those reported by these authors were obtained. The most favorable scenario affecting Tuma waves of 4.7 m and flood areas greater than 80 percent of the area of the Tumaco and El Morro Islands.
