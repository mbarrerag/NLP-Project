# PINTING THE ENEMY. CHAPET: CARICATURE, DESIGN AND POLITICAL HISTORY. COLOMBIA, 1944-1958

This book deals with the work of the Colombian cartoonist Hernando Turriago Riaño, known as Chapete, framed in the period 1944 to 1958, from the appearance of his work in the newspaper El Tiempo to the establishment of the National Front. This book, written by a historian, focuses on the analysis of the artist’s incidence in the historical process pointed out and in turn of the incidence of this in the artist. Through its traces, it shows the perception and representation he had of the political daily life. His caricature is primarily political, but also abounds the social and Costumbrist themes. It is, in any case, a liberal cartoon; elaborated, it is worth saying, by a liberal man of birth. Although Chapeta moved in the world of graphic art, the newspaper El Tiempo was appropriated by him and he was also known as the journalist of El Tiem.
