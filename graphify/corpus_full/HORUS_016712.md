# PREDICTION OF HYNESTERS BASED ON BAYESIAN NETWORKS FOR ARTERIAL CORREDORS IN THE CITY OF BOGOTÁ

The research provides intensive scientific rigorous efforts to understand and predict the nature of this problem. Models for predicting accidents calibrated to study areas are used as a useful tool in the management and estimation of road safety risks in urban environments. In the last 20 years, literature has discussed a wide variety of methods used to predict the frequency of casualties, including the use of Bayesian networks. Considering this scenario, this research aims to analyze and estimate models for predicting the rates of accident in the city of Bogotá's arterial network, in the network of trunk roads of the Integrated Public Transport System SITP, and in its preferred lanes. This object is addressed from the estimation of multivariate linear models (GLM) and Bayesian probability networks (PBN), comparing the performance of these models for predicting accident rates in the city of Bogotá.
