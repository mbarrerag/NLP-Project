# INTERVENTION AND EFFECTIVENESS OF ABCDEF PACK IN THE TREATMENT OF DELIUM: STORAGE REVIEW

to describe the interventions of each component of the ABCDEF package and its effectiveness in preventing and treating delirium in patients hospitalized in intensive care. Content synthesis: a comprehensive review was carried out using the terms MeSH delirium and intensive care units, adding ABCDEF with the operator AND, in the databases Medline, ScienceDirect, Virtual Health Library and Scopus, selecting a total of 23 articles according to the inclusion criteria established. Among these, it was possible to identify interventions for pain control (A), awakening and spontaneous breathing (B), sedation of analgesia and sedation (C), delirium monitoring (D), early mobility (E) and family empowerment. The entire ABCDEF package and its components showed effectiveness in reducing the incidence and duration of delirium, as well as days of mechanical ventilation and mortality. the ABCDEF package interventions identified were pain monitoring and its control with drugs and relaxation; the implementation of the minimum wake-up and spontaneous breathing protocol, according to safety criteria; the administration of analgesia and mortality was null; the last time of the diagnosis and the initial and the initial phase of the initial phase was performed
