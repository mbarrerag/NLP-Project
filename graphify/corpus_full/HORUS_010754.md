# Measurement of VITAL SIGNATURE PARAMETERS FOR EMISSION OF MOBILE ALERTS

A concern in people is how to know their immediate health status. A key indicator for this is the parameters of vital signs: temperature, heart rate and oxygenation; wearable devices allow that monitoring by connecting with a mobile phone; however, many users do not know or fail to achieve the proper interpretation of such reports. For this reason, in addition to automatically monitoring vital signs (in specified periods of time), the objective of the proposed system is to issue alerts that are sent automatically, not only to the device user, but also to a text message companion, whenever any of these parameters are outside the normal range allowed. This is achieved by a user categorization model supported by a system of rules that describes normal ranges, establishing criteria for issuing alerts. The main benefit of the proposed system is to have specific and real-time information from a body parameter allowing to warn an altered state of health, important input, to make medical decisions
