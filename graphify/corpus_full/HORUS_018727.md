# Proposal for teaching the concept of congruence and similarity of triangles in fourth grade elementary students: reasoning, problem solving, concrete teaching material and ICT

The intention that accompanies the execution of this work was to recognize some theoretical and didactic tools that allow improving the educational practice of teaching mathematics, applying the Van Hiele theory, focused on an approach to the concept of similarity and congruence in students of the fourth grade of primary school at the Fe y alegría la Cumbre educational institution, during the period of the pandemic generated by the Covid 19 virus in 2021. Analyzing the performance of the population to intervene, a low level was found in geometric thinking, specifically in tasks that correspond to the informal deduction or order level. For this reason, this proposal seeks to design and apply learning guides through the modality of virtual work at home framed in the learning model of Van Hiele teachers, After the intervention through synchronous and asynchronous sessions mediated by ICT, it was possible to identify the level of reasoning of the students and establish recommendations for advancing to the next level
