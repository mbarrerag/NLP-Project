# IMPORTANCE OF QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT

An electron has an intrinsic binary property (the “upward” and “downward” spin) and can have binary displacements (moving to the right or to the left). Its spin may be a combination of the two mentioned, and may have movements that can be combinations of those two. Four pairs can also be formed (intrinsic property, displacement): up to the left, up to the right, down to the left, down to the right. Even combinations of these four possibilities, which is an example of quantum interlinking. Similarly, photons have an intrinsic binary property, polarization. John Clauser and Alain Aspect experimentally observed that a pair of photons may be in entwined quantum states formed with their states of polarization. The third Nobel prize of physics 2022, together with the above mentioned researchers, Anton Zeilinger, demonstrated that quantum interlinking is applicable in quantum information and communication. Such applications, along with quantum computing, quantum metrology, quantum microscopy, and other technologies, are called “second quantum revolution.”
