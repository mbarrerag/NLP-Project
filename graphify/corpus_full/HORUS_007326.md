# Public policy guidelines to prevent forced urban displacement of non-traditional inhabitants (population in vulnerable condition among them street dwellers), in urban renewal projects in Bogotá. Learning from international cases and the case of the Bronx Urban Renewal Project Creative District in Bogotá

Public policy guidelines to prevent forced urban displacement of non-traditional inhabitants (population in a vulnerable condition among them street inhabitants), in urban renewal projects in Bogotá. Learning from international cases and the case of the urban renewal project Bronx Creative District in Bogotá This research analyses the phenomenon of forced urban displacement of street inhabitants in urban renewal projects in Bogotá, taking as a case study the Bronx Creative District project. Through a comparative analysis with successful international experiences such as the Housing First model in Europe, gaps are identified in local public policies and guidelines are proposed to prevent the social and spatial exclusion of this vulnerable population. The methodology combines documentary review, case analysis and specific study of the Bronx project, showing how urban interventions can reproduce patterns of segregation when they prioritize physical transformation over social integration.
