# DIFFERENT MORPHOLOGIES OF NANOMATERIALS OBTAINED BY THE ANODIZING METHOD

DIFFERENT MORPHOLOGIES OF NANOMATERIALS OBTAINED BY THE ANODIZING METHOD RINCÓN JOYA MIRYAM 1, BARBA ORTEGA JOSÉ JOSÉ 2, BAUTISTA RUIZ JORGE HERNANDO 3, 1 Universidad Nacional de Colombia, 2 Universidad Nacional de Colombia, 3 Universidad Francisco de Paula Santander, The anodizing method is a simple and fast method for obtaining materials. Metal oxide nanostructures have been synthesized by various techniques. Anodizing is one of the simplest methods used to synthesize. In the material preparation process the sample is actually converted to its oxide form. In the syntheses of nanomaterials, various conditions are used in the electrolytic solution, another way to change these conditions is the control over the voltage applied to the anode and cathode in the process. The morphology and size of the nanostructures can be adequately controlled from all the variables of the electrolyte solution and the current. The morphologies obtained can be nanoparticles, nanotubes, nanoflowers and other forms. Depending on the type of morphology and size, it can have different applications and physical and chemical properties
