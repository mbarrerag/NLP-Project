# Economic evaluation of solar photovoltaic generation technologies for the residential and commercial sector.

The photovoltaic systems integrated in buildings (BIPV) incorporate photovoltaic generation properties in the external construction materials and therefore offer advantages in costs and appearance with respect to conventional materials. The decrease in the cost of solar photovoltaic technology and the technicalization of the production of the products BIPV has allowed to increase the number of constructions that choose to implement these technologies, mainly in European and Asian countries. In this research, 89 products are collected BIPV and its main technical characteristics. For its evaluation, the context of the tariffs in the Colombian electricity market and the characteristics of the buildings for the four cities with the greatest number of inhabitants of the country: Bogotá, Medellín, Cali and Barranquilla. From the calculation of the Equalization of the Energy Cost (LCOE) by technology is concluded that the technology with the lowest cost for energy produced is solar glass, followed by the facade, the glass ceiling and finally the solar roof. For its part, the analysis by cities confirms the great potential of the city of Barranquilla (and in general of the Caribbean region) thanks to the high cost of energy produced, followed by the glass facade and the solar roof and finally the solar roof
