# Idolatra queer design: collective and identity design practices that allow the appropriation of images of pop icons

LGBTQ+ communities have built a close relationship with images. This has been historically given thanks to actions of manipulation and appropriation with which they have given themselves the task of being exercises that build identity and community. The role of the image is that of an active entity within the communities and therefore a structural part of the community ties. This project aims to study this relationship from a design perspective, recognizing the political role that this type of creative actions has had and continues to have. Thanks to the formulation of a design laboratory, in this study I present a thorough look at the design activities and the ways in which they are interwoven with socio-cultural processes. Within these relationships, the union of design thinking and the perspective of the communities concerned create a same one when it comes to exercising creation based on the iconography of pop culture . LGBTQ+ community has built a relationship of integrity with images. This has historically realized through action and adaptation based on the iconography of pop culture .
