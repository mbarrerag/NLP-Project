# DISCLOSURE THE MOLESTIES, FOR GLOBALIZATION GROUNDS WE'RE LEARNING ENGLISH.

This text presents two re-exiions, two moments, two experiences around the learning of English as a second language and art as an excuse to navigate those moments. It proposes to land three roles, in two concrete examples that pertain to my task as a teacher of the National University of Colombia, namely: the role of teacher, the role of researcher and the role of performance artist. In turn, the text is nourished by several experiences lived in the first person and therefore narrated as such, in the first person of the singular, using as a constant between the past and the present, so that it can be read as a narrative text, autobiográ co, autoetnográ co, partial y si- tuado (Haraway, 1995, pp. 1-28)
