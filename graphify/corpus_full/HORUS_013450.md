# Do you know what a cinema club is? Spaces, technologies and practices in three Colombian cinema clubs

This study analyzes the configuration of the relations between spaces and places, the objects and technological artifacts and the cultural practices of three Colombian cinema clubs: The Cineclub Cinestar, The Cineclú Crispeta Popular and the Cine Club El Muro. These are micro social cases addressed from the studies on the spaces and cinema rooms, the social technological history of the cinema clubs and the practices in relation to the affective turn and the aesthetic dimension in the cinema. Thanks to the epistemological and methodological paths offered by the cultural studies, especially those where the realities are located from their specificity, relational character, fluid and quotas (Grossberg, 2016), it was possible to explore and establish nodes, through the ethnographic method and its application in digital scenarios (netography). I also consulted secondary sources that support the historical accounts of the cinemas in Colombia, the cinemas and the social and technological development of the objects and artifacts, as well as the cultural practices related to the use and representation of these materials around the cinema. cinema.
