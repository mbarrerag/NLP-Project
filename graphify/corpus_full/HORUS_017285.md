# CHAPTER 6: CHILD HEALTH, TRAUMA AND DEVELOPMENT DIMENSIONS: A MODEL FOR A COMBATING THE EFFECT OF FAMILY VIOLENCES IN CHILDREN AND CHILDREN FROM A PERSPECTIVE OF THE THEORY OF ADVICE, NEUROSARORALLO AND THE DEVELOPMENT PSYCOPATOLOGY

The objective of this chapter is to offer a different understanding of the traditional child psychopathology model (descriptive/atomized) and to present a evidence-based approach to child mental health, which can be articulated with a model closer to explanatory and dynamic models, which in turn allow a nonlinear understanding of the effect of violence on children during early childhood. In the second section of the chapter, practical tools will be provided to the reader to understand and identify children who have suffered chronic or complex trauma from their caregivers. These practical tools can be of great use in carrying out interventions by relational facilitators, as they will allow them to better understand the family dynamics and behaviors of the children who have benefited from the relational intervention programme
