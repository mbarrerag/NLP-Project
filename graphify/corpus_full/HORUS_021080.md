# TERRITORIES, EMOTIONS AND CONFLICT IN THE LOW ATTRACT: In search of relational cartography

After so many decades of endless violence, the territories have become wounded bodies that need to be healed, heard and visible. Because in them, conflicts, struggles, passions and affections are expressed. For this reason, the territories are carriers of emotions, which are temporarily fixed in memory and spatially in the landscape. The current process of implementation of the Peace Agreement is a highly sensitive scenario for questioning the role of emotions, and their role in the new spatial and affective relations that are established with the territories. Therefore, this proposal seeks to analyze and represent the relationship between emotions, territories and conflicts, particularly in the sub-region of Attrato, located to the north-eastern of the department of Chocó. Historically, one of the territories hit hardest by violence, as well as one of the most bioculturally diverse in Colombia. Through cartographic exercises, we will seek to read from the relationality, new forms of association between the material and symbolic, the natural and cultural, the objective and the subjective. And thus be able to explain phenomena that are not visible, and stimulate processes of territorial interpretation that include interpretatilization and other forms
