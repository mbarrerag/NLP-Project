# Impact of the Bogotá health reorganization on clinical and economic outcomes in patients with chronic pathologies affiliated to Capital Salud EPS undersidised

For the year 2015, the health system of Bogotá faced challenges related to financing, the over-occupation of services and the lack of communication between the actors involved. In an attempt to address these problems, the reorganization of the provision in the district was carried out at the end of 2016 and 2017. This study evaluates the impact of this reorganization on patients with chronic pathologies, specifically patients with arterial hypertension, affiliated to Capital Health EPS of the subsidized regime. By means of an analysis of differences in differences, the impact of the reorganization on clinical results, consumption of services, costs and mortality of patients with hypertension between 2015 and 2018 was evaluated. For this, a closed cohort was constructed in order to evaluate non-absorbentive events (consumption of services, clinical results and medical care costs) and using fixed effects. Similarly, a dynamic cohort of healthcare was constructed to evaluate mortality in the population. The results show a significant increase in the consumption of outpatient care services and a reduction in hospitalization services, the realization of myocardial revascularizations, the dialysis and total costs of medical care for the population identified with hypertension.
