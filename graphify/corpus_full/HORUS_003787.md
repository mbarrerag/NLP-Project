# INDICATOR'S PROPOSAL FOR EQUIPMENT RANKING IN FUTBOL TORNS.

Football is one of the most watched and practiced sports in the world. The proliferation of experts who analyze date after date the results of a game, teams, players etc. It is an inexhaustible source of information. Particularly a question of interest relates to speculation about future results related to the classification of teams in a particular tournament. Usually only at the end of the tournament is that we have idea of the place occupied by each team. This is almost true depending on the type of tournament and the country of reference. The place occupied by each team at the end is based mainly on the points obtained by each team at the end of the tournament, and rules are defined for a tie case in any of the places. This limited information contains variables such as winnings, tied matches, lost matches, goals in favor, goals against and points. In some cases more information appears, but it is not consistent tournament. A methodology based on main components is proposed to perform a ranking using these variables. The proposed indicator could also be used as a predictor of the position of the teams during the tournament
