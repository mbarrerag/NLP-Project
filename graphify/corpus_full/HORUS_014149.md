# "There are only mixed white students here": Racism and racial discrimination at UN Bogotá Headquarters.

This project arises from the shared concerns between the Delia Research Seed and Manuel Zapata Olivella SIZO, AfroUN student collective and the Paes-Peama Table against racism at the National University of Colombia. We seek to study racial discrimination on the campus of Bogotá headquarters and propose a toolbox for the prevention, care and action of this problem. *The project budget, the activities of the students and the description of the product of social appropriation of knowledge are in attachment*
