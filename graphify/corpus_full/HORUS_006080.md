# Assessment of the treatment of waste water generated at the National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences in an Aerobic PTAR of Bogotá headquarters and an anaerobic PTAR of Villavicencio

The National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences is a public order institution that provides forensic services to the administration of justice and the Colombian community. It has laboratories and accredited inspection bodies where samples of Probatory Material elements are processed to clarify crimes and contribute to the justice system in Colombia under criteria of scientific research, suitability, quality, impartiality and respect for human dignity. In the provision of services significant environmental aspects are generated such as waste water discharges with high organic loads due to the nature of the samples being processed, that is why in order to minimize the pollution to the water resource by the generation of this environmental aspect that have Residual Water Treatment Plants up to a tertiary level, and they comply with the efficiency of removal of certain parameters of health interest that are regulated by the current standard in Colombia.
