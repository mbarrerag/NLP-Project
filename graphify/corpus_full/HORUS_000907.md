# State and urban expansion processes related to real estate development in the municipalities of El Retiro and Rionegro between 2010 and 2020

This paper analyses the strategies of the State in the urban expansion processes related to the real estate development of the near East, specifically in the case of the municipalities of El Retiro and Rionegro, in the period 2010 – 2020 by means of the interpretation of the socio-spatial transformations in terms of housing increase, demography and soil coverage, the identification of the strategies of institutional articulation, intervention and its discourse, and the description of the relationships that are established within the State between state agents and real estate agents of the East Anti-Oqueño through the regulations related to urbanization processes in both municipalities.
