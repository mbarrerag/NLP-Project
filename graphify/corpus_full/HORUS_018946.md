# THEOREM OF THE FINISHED POINT OF BROOWER

The Brouwer fixed point theorem states that any continuous function of the disc on the disk leaves a fixed point. In the case of a dimension, this theorem coincides with the intermediate value theorem, however, in larger dimensions its demonstration involves tools of homological algebra, in particular, singular homology as a topological invariant. This theorem has had many applications in different areas of mathematics, for example, applications to game theory, in economics, in differential equations and in differential geometry
