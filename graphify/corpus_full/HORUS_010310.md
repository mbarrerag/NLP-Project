# EPIDEMIOLOGICAL PROFILE OF DEPORTIVE INDUSTRY IN NATIONAL UNIVERSITY TO LEAVE HEALTH DETERMINANTS

The participation in youth sport and young adults, as in the case of university students, has increased considerably and the injury rates have increased, making sport the main cause of injury in the young population. The benefits of physical activity are indisputable. However, sports practice is not risk-free and the most common adverse result is the appearance of a sports injury. The epidemiology of sports injuries is multifactorial, as they may be related to more than one cause, or to pre-injury aspects. Determining the true cause of injury can be a complex task. The above studies focus on the risk factors of the sportsman and some training conditions. No research has been found that have studied other conditions, other than sportsmen, that may influence the occurrence of sports injuries, such as the determinants of health, defined as factors that may positively or negatively affect the health of an individual and, therefore, of the entire population
