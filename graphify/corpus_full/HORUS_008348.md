# NITROGEN HETEROCICLES OF 5, 6 AND 7 MEMBERS: PRIVILEGED NUMBERS WITH CHEMICAL AND BIOLOGICAL PROPERTIES OF GREAT INTEREST

The heterocycles are organic compounds that have at least one heteroatom (i. e. a non-carbon atom) in the annular system, where the most common heteroatomes are N, O and S. The heterocyclic compounds as well as the composition of the heterocyclic compounds are usually natural (plants and animals); and are one of the main constituents of almost half of the known organic compounds of biological origin. The rings of 5, 6 and 7 members have been extensively studied due to their structural characteristics, both at the electronic and conformational level; where they highlight the azole, pyridine, pyriminic and thiazepin derivatives, due to their interesting chemical and biological properties.
