# Development of a mobile application for the diagnosis and treatment of H. pylori infection for health professionals.

The infection by Helicobacter pyltori (HP) has been one of the most studied in recent decades, its prevalence is about 60% of the adult population and is related to various disorders of gastrointestinal origin such as chronic gastritis, peptic ulcer, and MLL type lymphoma (Linfoid tissue lymphoma associated with mucosas) and in 1 to 3% is associated with gastric cancer, among others. The main symptoms are presented in 20% of patients, and in a large percentage can be asymptomatic 80%. The diagnosis is based on invasive tests such as high and non-invasive digestive endoscopy as fecal antigens and respiratory breath test with carbon marked urea C13 and C14. The triple standard therapy includes antibiotics such as the consultation and Metronidazole, to which the HP has high resistance rates in Latin America. Currently, quadruple therapies are used in addition to bismuth subsalicythal, with an effectiveness up to 90% when this treatment is administered by 14 days. The treatment of Hyl pylori brings multiple benefits between them. He conducted an extensive narrative review that included a search for literature in various sources of medical information such as clinical practice guides, narrative reviews and primary studies in databases such as Embase, Lilacs, Pubmed and grey literature and solved 6 very frequent questions about diagnosis and treatment in the patient who is assisted by H. pylori infection. In the second part, a mobile health application was developed on the Android platform with the MIT app inventor platform, for mobile devices and through algorithms focused on the diagnosis and treatment of H. pylori infection, and his applicability and performance in actual clinical scenarios in external consultation and internal medicine services was evaluated. A complete narrative review of the topics of interest was achieved and developed and published in the Play Store of Android, the mobile application name “PyloriApp”, in which the indications of screening of H. pylori and other indications of digestive endoscopy were presented in a simple and friendly manner and in the form of high-level diagnostics, methods that allow continuity in the approach of the patient with H. pylori infection, generating greater safety and increased in the way of infection and informed
