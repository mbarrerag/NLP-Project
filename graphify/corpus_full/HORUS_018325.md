# HUMANISM, ETHICS AND INTERCULTURE IN HEALTH

Ethics, interculturality and humanism are concepts that, one could say, are inherently related to life and health; however, they are not always reflected in the practice of professionals and staff responsible for health care, nor in the different scenarios envisaged for social coexistence and the exercise of citizenship. It is for this reason that it is necessary to deepen these three concepts, their history and relevance both in general terms, as in the areas of health and life itself. In this order of ideas, this work seeks to analyze and study each of these concepts, as well as the relations between them, in order to finally present the results of a research carried out by the authors regarding the reality of these concepts in the specific field of the National University of Colombia
