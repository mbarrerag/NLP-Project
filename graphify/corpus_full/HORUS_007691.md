# Bogotane Tempos

We propose this project as the continuation of a visual and artistic reflection around the particular forms of the temporal experience in the context of the contemporary city. In our historical moment the photography has become a sort of common language, very appropriate for the generation of that type of exploration and will now take place.We will analyze the long-term difficulties in the perception of time.The proposal focuses on generating a space for the members of the seeder to be involved in the experimentation around the photographic device.The photographic then constitutes the axis of this artistic experience.We detect that in the context of a mega urbe contemporary like Bogota, there is a great diversity of everyday phenomena that represent particular ways of living the time. We are interested in questioning the way that humanity assumes the temporary experience in the urban daily life in these first years of the 21st century, because we consider that the time of the current city, full of rituals of cronometal planning that plan the course of life, this idea is associated with a commercial vision of the time, accelerated and vertiginous ones that characterizes the city of the 21st century, since we end of the city, full of the first century. This initiative is part of the previous result obtained in the history of the research Seed - creation with its most recent projects: 1)Urbe, Time and Photography and Contemplation, city and photography.A plastic vision of the air traffic over the city of Bogota; (Hermes code 40213 see PDFs annex: Felicitation_seed, B.FA.1.00-306-21) close project 46147). 2) The Entangled Green project (Hermes code 50035 see PDFs of felicitation [B.FA.1.002-325-22] 3) The White City Times project: Semillero de investigacion creacion Tiempo, urbe y fotografía (Hermes code 56976) whose results delivered in 2023 can be consulted at https://agenciadenoticias.unal.edu.co/detalle/fotogaleria-el-arte-de-perder-el-tiempo)._
