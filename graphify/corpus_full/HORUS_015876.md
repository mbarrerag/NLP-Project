# DAY RESEARCH AND POST WEIGHT IN TERNERS CROSSED IN DIFFERENT PORTS OF BON, ANGUS AND HOLSTEIN WINTER, IN THE HIGH COLOMBIAN TRIP

The White breed Orejinegro, BON, does not have a high level of productive specialization, its greatest genetic potential is given by the great rusticity and resistance that allows it to adapt to the different thermal floors of the high and low tropics in Colombia. For this reason it is a breed widely used in cross-country programs to improve productive characteristics, given the adaptation and rusticity that it brings in the cross-country. This research evaluated the effect that the breed has on the daily weight gain (GDP) pre and post-weaning models until reaching the year of life, in cross-breedings with animals of the European breeds Holstein and Aberdeen Angus. For this evaluation the information of 64 animals bred under two different methods until the time of weaning (TC1: all milk with the mother up to the weaning model to the +/- 210 days and TC2 were used: separated from the mother, servants with bucket and weaned to the 90 days of births) all these servants and raised in the Paysandúl, located in Medellín Antioquia, each year, owned by the National University of Colombia (GON) and. The coefficient of variation for TC1 was 6.12% and 6.03% for TC2. This means a low variation for this variable In both breeding systems (TC1 and TC2) the effects of the birth time and year of birth, had no significant effect, while the breed explained as a percentage of BON had significant effect with P values varying between 0.001 and 0.1.
