# EVALUATION OF OZONE MASS TRANSFER IN A TUBULAR POROUS STAINLESS STEEL MEMBRANE CONTACTOR

Ozone-water mass transport in a novel tubular membrane contactor was investigated under continuous flow conditions. The volumetric mass transfer coefficient (KLa) was evaluated as a function of gas and liquid flow rates. The reactor is composed of an inner tubular porous membrane made of stainless steel and an outer quartz tube. Ozone-water contact occurs on the membrane shell-side, as the ozone gas stream is fed by the lumen side and permeates through the membrane pores, generating micro-sized gas bubbles uniformly delivered to the annular zone where the water to be treated flows. The KLa values were substantially improved with an increment on ozone gas and liquid flow rates, due to the enhanced turbulence on the membrane shell-side and annular zone. For the highest liquid flow tested (100 L h-1) the main resistance to ozone transfer was in the membrane side (93.4%), with a mass transfer coefficient of km = (4.20 ± 0.02)×10-5m s-1
