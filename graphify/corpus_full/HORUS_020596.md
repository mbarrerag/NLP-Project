# Geography of the land, violence, development and extractivism in the collective territories of Curvaradó and Jiguamiandó, Colombia

This research research research investigates the territorial transformations in the communities of the collective territories of Curvaradó and Jiguamiandó, located in the Lower Attrato Chocoano, from a case study between the years 1996 and 2018, which explores the intersections generated by violence and extractivisms, having as reference concepts as space, territory and territoriality; the spoils; development and memory. In this sense, the purpose of this research is to analyze how the extractivisms represented in oil palm, the monocultures of coca and mining, linked to an idea of ‘progress and development’ and to a specific vision of nature, reconfigured the territorialities of the peoples of African descent in this basin and generated socio-environmental and territorial conflicts, which in turn forged different types of resistance to return, recover and rebuild the territory with the aim to confront the imposed actions associated with capitalist and extractive developments. .
