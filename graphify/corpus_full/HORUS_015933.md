# POLICLES (3-HYDROXIBUTIRATE)-POLI (LACTIC ACID)-MICROCELLULOSE MODIFIED, AS A POLIPROPILENE SUBSTITUTE FOR FOOD PACKS

Taking into account the increasingly notaristic environmental implications caused by plastic waste, the development of sustainable alternatives is urgently needed. Therefore, a biodegradable biofilm as a replacement material for polypropylene (PP) film in food packaging was studied. This led to the development of PHA/PLA/MC films by extrusion and solvent-cast, since, although PHA (majority polymer in formulation; poly(3-hydroxybutyrate-co-3-hydroxyvalerate)) has optimal crystalinity characteristics, barrier properties and biodegradability, its thermal instability and high rigidity creates difficulties for its processing. Thus, the PLA prints it to the highest plasticity and thermal resistance mixture, and the microcellulose (MC) would improve the mechanical properties, acting as adhesive between polymers.
