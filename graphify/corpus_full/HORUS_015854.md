# Is it possible to identify MULecuLARLY SPECIES OF THE GENDER FANNIA (DIPTERA: FANNIIDAE) WITH A CORTO REGION OF THE MITOCONDRIAL DNA?: A CASE STUDY

The genus Fannia Robineau-Desvoidy, 1830 (Diptera: Fanniidae) has a wide geographical distribution and medical, health, economic, veterinary and forensic importance.The identification of its species has traditionally been based on external characters of the males, which requires technical and scientific expertise.On the other hand, the high morphological similarity in the females, in addition to the absence of keys for their immature states, represent difficulties for their precise identification.The molecular identification has emerged as an alternative by using strategies such as the bar code using a fragment of DNA of the region 5 ́ of the Citochromo Oxidase I - IOC, of approximately 650 base pairs.However, in the genus Fannia has been evidenced a greater success in the amplification of a short region located in the IOC region ́. Therefore, the following was assessed the potential of this short region for the identification of five species of the genus Fannia. Adults deposited in the Entomological Collection of Antioquia (CET) were found to be from several localities of the Colombian territory, and PTC. The first step is to identify and confirm molecularly the species of the genus Fannia analyzed. It is necessary to increase both the number of species and their sample representativeness in the geographical distribution range to validate their usefulness as marker for identification
