# Emergency accommodation in island territory: a presentation on temporary spaces for the root community in Providencia

Colombia faces numerous natural disasters and humanitarian crises, the loss of home and displacement force to quick and practical solutions that, although flexible and versatile, do not attend to the victims in their ways of inhabiting or in the way in which the settlements and occupation of the territory happen. This work investigates how it happened after the passage of Hurricane Iota in 2020 in the islands of Santa Catalina and Providencia, Hurricane that practically destroys the island and where its inhabitants still struggle for its recovery, this unfortunate fact serves as an excuse to explore and investigate not on the housing, if as the refuge can come to be, therefore the term housing is relevant for our research. The inspiration comes first from the Caribbean cabin of Gottfried Semper, who conceived “The Caribbean Hut” as an elementary structure, built with natural and simple materials, which was an idealized representation of the architecture in its most basic form and a starting point to understand the accommodation as a seed that transcends the physical structure and includes spatial, social and cultural dimensions; second, the community reflects the ancestral and knowledge of architecture. aftermath of Hurricane Iota in 2020, which devastated the islands of Santa Catalina and Providencia, and what inhabitants are still drawing to recover.
