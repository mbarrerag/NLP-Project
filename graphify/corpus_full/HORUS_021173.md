# Analysis and pre-processing of electronic language and electronic nose sensor data.

The use of sensors for monitoring environmental variables as well as in the food industry and in general in any process where liquid substances and gases are used requires monitoring and control mechanisms that guarantee the process and that can generate alerts when they change their characteristics or deviate from pre-established values. This is the case of the food industry, for example, where some processes are characterized by a certain smell or taste and are normally certified by a panel of experts who guarantee these conditions, however although this is a solution in many processes, in the case of industrial applications of high volume can be a bottleneck that can be addressed from other points of view. One solution may be the use of electrochemical sensors, which can be used for the analysis of liquid substances and gases or odours and which can provide reliable results in short periods of time aimed at being online monitoring systems. In this case, the problems with their use are derived in the development of methodologies for pre-processed and subsequent analysis of information so that this project is oriented to exploring various pre-gasecessed data systems to generate new knowledge in the semi-dilective research and to serve as a liquid development
