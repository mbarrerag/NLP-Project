# EFFECT OF THE MASSAGE ON THE PHYSIOLOGICAL AND COMPORTABLE RESPONSE OF THE PRETERM BORN RECIDE

To determine the effect of repeated practice of ki-nesthetic tactile stimulus (KET) on the weight curve of the premature newborn (NB). The study had a quantitative approach, of quasi-experimental design, performed in a sample of NPB (between =29 and =35 weeks of gestation), hemodynamically stable, hospitalized in the Neontal Care Unit of the Simón Bolívar Hospital (Bogotá, Colombia).The sample was randomly grouped; the nurse performed the NPB 3 times a day (experiment group) and 1 time a day (control group) for 5 days consecutiv-vos. The weight curve and weight/calories ratio (g.cal-1) were evaluated me-dian analysis of the variance (ANOVA), adjustment of the polynomial curve and test of the Mann-Whitney U (p < 0,05) Results: The weight was calculated as a function of time, and the percentage of weight increase compared to time curves of both groups showed no statistical differences (p < 0, < 0,0,0 = pb)
