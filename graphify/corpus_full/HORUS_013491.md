# Integrating listening as a dynamic axis of communication skills from the humanities area and PILEO-E

Within the daily exercise of the teaching-learning lithoma, communication and literature, it is proposed to work on listening as a dynamic axis integrating the other communication skills. This work should be significant, therefore, it is proposed to do so from the communicative approach, generating learning environments in the Castellan language class that enhance the design, creation and application of pedagogical strategies of comprehensive listening. The work is framed in the qualitative methodology and the design of the research participatory action in the classroom; as a route that allows to improve communication processes through the pedagogical work of comprehensive listening in a basic secondary school in the city of Bogotá.
