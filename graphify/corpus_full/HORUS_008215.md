# Toxic effects of environmental drug contamination.

To understand and identify the toxic effects in aquatic ecosystems related to exposure to environmental pollution caused by drugs, a review of scope was carried out following the methodology proposed by JBI, carrying out the search in MEDLINE, ScienceDirect, Relativity, Scopus, Scielo, Springer Link and Discoverer using a predefined search strategy in English and Spanish, selection by titles/summaries and full text by two independent reviewers, extracting the data in a pre-established form. 42 documents were collected in English that allowed to determine the toxic effects associated with environmental contamination by drugs, showing increasing interest in the last decade, through bioassays by drug and/or metabolite, their mixtures or from studies in mesocosoctalm, explaining mechanisms of oxidative stress and metabolic alterations, as well as histopathological changes, reproductive, growth and development, behavior, endocrine disruption, immobilization, genotoxicity, cytotoxicity, immunotoxicity, survival, mortality and population modifications in various species of different trophic levels, and decreases. ology proposed by JBI, searching MEDLINE, ScienceDirect, Embase, Scopus, Scielo, Springer Link and Discoverer using a predefined search strategy en English and Spanish, selection by title/abstracts and full text by two independent reviewers, extracting the data in a pre-established form.
