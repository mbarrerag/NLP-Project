# THE MESSEGRAPHICAL IMAGINATION OF CARLOS NIÑO MURCIA.

This research, which is the result of a work supported in various interviews conducted between 2016 and 2020, provides some light on the professional, academic and human trajectory Carlos Niño Murcia, professor at the National University of Colombia and outstanding architect, who can undoubtedly be considered as a master of the arts
