# Criminal justice and those who are not responsible for mental disorder in Colombia: failures that lead to injustice.

This investigation addresses the legislative omission related to judicial procedures to follow in cases involving persons unimputable due to mental disorders as active subjects of the crime. The above is based on the fact that the unimputable minors and indigenous have clear legal, jurisprudential and technical parameters that define the procedural framework for the judicial authorities to act in case of one of them committing a crime and it is necessary to follow a special procedure. However, in events in which the inability to understand and self-regulation are decisive for a subject to commit a crime, the current rule establishes that they must be investigated as unimputable and should not be prosecuted. However, few cases are in which the relevant expertise is carried out to prevent an imputable person from being punished by mental disorder. On the other hand, with the issuance of the Law 1996 2019, the need for the investigation is further illustrated. The first instance of the act is to safeguard the rights of these subjects of special protection, since this norm attempts to change the paradigm surrounding persons with mental disabilities if they are subject to a contractual matter
