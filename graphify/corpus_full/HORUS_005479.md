# 5. NUTRIENTS CONTENT IN THE CACCOA ARBOL

The production of cocoa depends mainly on factors such as edaphic co-ditions and climate, which influence the genetic material for its expression in terms of growth and production.Climatic factors, the rainfall regime and solar radiation influence the physiological mechanisms of the plant, the states of development of buds and branches and the occurrence of flowering. Soil is the system in which the cultivators find the water and minerals necessary for their metabolism and growth
