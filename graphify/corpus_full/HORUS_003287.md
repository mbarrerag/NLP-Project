# CYCLOHEXENO CO2 OXIDE CYCHOXIDE SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION OF ZN-ZR-AL CATALIZERS AND ITS APPLICATION

In this study, the synthesis was carried out, by the co-precipitation method, and the characterization of four mixed oxides of Zn-Zr-Al hydrotalcita derivatives using variable molar ratios Zn/Zr= 1, 2, 3, 4. CO2 capture experiments showed that the solid with higher Zr content (Zn/Zr=1) presented a higher CO2 capture. Likewise, the TPD-CO2 of the prepared materials showed that an increase in the quantity of Zr, increases the total number of basic sites, and in turn increases the proportion of basic sites of medium strength. The results of DRIFT-CO2 indicated that the species disorbided before 500 °C are mainly associated with bicarbonates (highly thermally labile) and fisorbid species on the surface of the oxide. Finally, these solids were used in the catalytic reaction between cyclohexene oxide and CO2 finding conversions of the substrate up to 65%. The presence of cyclic carbonate was identified as a major product although the presence of by-products was also observed
