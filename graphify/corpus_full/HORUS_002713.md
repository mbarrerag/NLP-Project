# KEY FOR TRANSITION TO TERRITORIAL AGRO-FOOD SYSTEMS

Since the end of the previous decade, challenges and challenges have been identified for the transition to more sustainable, resilient and inclusive Agrifood Systems -SATs - that ensure the availability of quality food, value food cultures, take advantage of the territory's resources, contribute to social inclusion and contribute to autonomy, sovereignty and food security in the territories where production is located. Aware that this challenge requires adequate social management of knowledge, progress in territorial governance processes around how they operate and that contribute to the systemic competitiveness of these SATs, an analytical model has been designed in networked "ANP ", incorporating as criteria and elements associated with the dimensions mentioned; some of them cross-cutting (criteria and subcriteria) that have been highlighted by previous studies and research in relation to SIAL and food systems. The model designed has allowed understanding of the importance in the elements of micro, meso, meta and macro levels, among which some stand out as being important and influence on the dimensions of knowledge management, governance and systemic competitiveness
