# DIVERSITY AND DISTRIBUTION OF BRIOPHITES IN THE CENTRE CORDILLERA OF COLOMBIA

The diversity and distribution of briophytes over the northern Andes varies with altitude in relation to different environmental factors. To evaluate this hypothesis, these characteristics of the Briophyte community will be determined in an altitude range between 1855 and 3110 msl in the natural forest of the Volcana Miserenga reserve, a co-ordination of San Sebastián de Palmitas, municipality of Medellín. In particular, it is sought to establish how various factors such as altitude variation, luminosity, substrate type and forest canopy structure influence the diversity and distribution of the Briophytes through this gradient. To do this, sampling will be carried out every 200 m of elevation and data will be taken from the proposed environmental factors. I hope with my research to understand the differential distribution of the Briophytes through a continuous altitudinal gradient and the preferences of microhabitats for different species
