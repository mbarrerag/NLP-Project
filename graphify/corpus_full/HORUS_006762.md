# Gender, Law and Bureaucracy: Study of the Path of Attention to Gender-Based Violence at the National University of Colombia (2017-2021)

In this research, a diagnosis of the functioning and implementation of the Gender Based Violence Care Route is carried out at the National University of Colombia, in cases where the alleged aggressor is a student member. To this end, a case study is carried out of the practices and daily discourses of the officials responsible for care, which account for the imaginary that exist in these persons. The diagnosis allows the identification of the scope, strategies and legal and institutional barriers that limit the actions within the Gender Based Violence Care Route at the National University of Colombia. . This research diagnoses the implementation of the assistance route for gender based violence at Universidad Nacional de Colombia in the events when the perpetrator is a student of the University. The methodology is a case study of the practices and daily discosures exerted by the responsive authority of the national assistance route, which allows to become a student of the University.
