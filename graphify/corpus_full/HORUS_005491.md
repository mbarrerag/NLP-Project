# The Terras Pretas of La Lindosa: new chronological, paleoecological and edaphological data

The ‘black soils’ or Terras Pretas show ancient human settlements; they are found in much of the Amazon basin especially in Brazil and Colombia (Araracuara, La Pedrera and La Lindosa). The aim of this research was to identify the physical chemistry composition, obtain radiocarbonic dates and perform analyses of phytoliths and archaeological seeds from two sites with black soils in the La Lindosa mountain range. Descriptions of the soil profiles, archaeological cuts with arbitrary levels of 10 centimeters were made, from which samples were extracted for phytolytes, archaeological seeds and physicochemical analysis by diagnostic horizons. A general trend of increased acidity was evident in the surface horizons and high presence of the taxonomic group of palms with at least seven taxons identified in the records of phytoliths and seeds (Attalea maripa, Bactris sp., Mauritia flexuosa, Oenocarpus bataua, Oenocarpus minor and Syagrus orinocensis).
