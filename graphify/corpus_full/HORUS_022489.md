# PREPARATION OF REFERENCE SUPERFICIENCY FOR THE MEASUREMENT OF RESIDUAL EFFORTS IN TI-6AL-4V ALLOTION, IN USE OF X-RAY DIFFRACTION TECHNICAL

The evaluation of the state of residual stress on a surface, obtained through the milling process, is important to ensure the reliability of the machine product during its life cycle. X-ray diffraction is one of the most used techniques for the evaluation of residual stress; and consists of relating the deformation of the crystalline mesh of the machined surface to that of a surface free of residual stress. The review of the specialized literature does not clearly show the way the residual stress free reference surface is established. The electropulid process was performed to a sample of the working material. Based on the diffraction pattern, the network parameters of the crystalline structure were estimated and the residual stress free state was verified. Finally, two methods are proposed to deter-mine the crystallographic planes that generate the diffraction peaks
