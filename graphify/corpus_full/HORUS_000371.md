# Distichopora yurupariensis, a New Species of Stylasteridae (Cnidaria: Hydrozoa: Anthoathecata) from the Upper Bathyal Tropical Eastern Pacific

A new species of Distichopora, D. yurupariensis, is described for the Tropical Eastern Pacific region. Distichopora yurupariensis is found at the Navigator Bank (Yuruparí Ridge) offshore in Colombian Pacific waters at a depth of 240 m. The new Distichopora differs from its congenerics by having a combination of the following characteristics: smooth linear-imbricate coenosteal texture, long and narrow coenosteal slits with lateral spines, and a short non-axial gastrostyle
