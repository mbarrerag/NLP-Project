# Integration of the Knowledge of Canoeros in the Study of Aquatic Birds in San José del Guaviare

San José del Guaviare, a municipality of great ecological richness, hosts a wide diversity of waterfowls that are essential to the balance of its river ecosystems and wetlands.This project is part of the research proposal already approved as Master's thesis in Amazonian Studies by student Luis Miguel Murcia Betancourt with Document 1015459781 entitled "The Aquatic Birds of San José del Guaviare: a participatory study in the rivers and wetlands of the municipality" which seeks to document and analyze this biodiversity, integrating local knowledge of local canoeers in the process. Research requires frequent travel through the sections of the Guaviare River and wetlands, which requires a specific budget for river transport
