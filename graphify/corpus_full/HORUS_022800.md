# INCLUSION: THE LIGHT AS A SINONIMOUS INCLUSION CREATING SOCIAL NETWORK

The article talks about the project "Light as a synonym for inclusion", born from the need to create social fabric through technology from the hands of vulnerable communities, mainly those that have been displaced in the metropolitan area of the city of Medellin
