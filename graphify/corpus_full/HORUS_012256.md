# Diagnosis of intraosseous solitary mandibular neurofibroma. A case report

Neurofibroma is a benign neoplasia of peripheral nerve sheaths, possibly associated with type I neurofibromatosis, also called Von Recklinghausen disease. Its presentation at the head and neck level is rare, so there are few reports. to present a clinical case with diagnosis of intraosseous neurofibroma and an updated review of the literature. A 46-year-old patient, treated in a oral and maxillofacial surgery service in the city of Bogotá, Colombia, with a diagnosis of intraosseous neurofibroma alone of three months of evolution, who was dismissed neurofibromatosis of Von Recklinghausen, which compromised the body and the right mandibular branch. The diagnosis was made using diagnostic images, as well as blocks and laminas for the review of initial biopsy and resection.
