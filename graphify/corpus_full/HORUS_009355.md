# STUDY OF ELECTRONIC AND TERM PROPERTIES OF AN ARTIFIC MOLECULA IN QUANTIC ANALYLS UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF EXTERNAL CAMPS

Based on images of atomic force microscopy, the energy spectrum of an electron confined in a realistic system composed of two quasi-bidimensional asymmetric rings coupled laterally is calculated. The entire system is under the combined influence of a magnetic field that crosses the perpendicular mod system and an electrical field that is located on the plane of the ring. The results obtained show that the system exhibits a behavior similar to an artifice molecule of the type H2+. Additionally, the optical response defined through the optical absorption coefficient is calculated, where it is shown that its peaks are strongly dependent on the geometry of the configuration and the external fields. In relation to the thermal response, it is shown how entropy defines the conditions of greater stability of the artificial molecular complex
