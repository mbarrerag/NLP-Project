# Effect of dose and phosphine exposure times on Hass avocado post-harvest quality (Persea Americana Mill.)

The Hass avocado in Colombia has a great export potential, although it faces trade restrictions due to quarantine pests. Magnesium phosphide fumigation has been considered as a key post-harvest strategy for pest control in chilled fruit. However, there is little information regarding its impact on the quality of Hass avocado fruits. This study evaluated the effect of phosphine treatments at different concentrations (0, 200, 400 and 800 ppm) and exposure times (36 and 72 h) on post-harvest quality in chilled avocados at 7°C. The firmness of fruit, colour of exocarp and mesocarp, weight loss and production of ethylene was analyzed by means of a multivariate analysis of the longitudinal variation. No direct damage was detected in the quality of the fruit related to the concentration of fosphine or the time of gas exposure was evaluated. Although the most significant differences in the firmness and color of the fruits were observed, these effects were attributable to variations in the concentration of gases, such as CO2, within the barrels, and to the process of maturation of fruit increased.
