# STRENGTHENING OF THE RIGHT- AND ECONOMY RESEARCH GROUP _x0096_ PROJECT: _x0093_ TOWARDS A DIGITAL STATE_x0094_

In Colombia, public policies emphasize technical improvements and massification in access to services, without deepening the possibilities that offer a more optimal citizen-machine-state feedback, that can positively impact the processes of participation and decision-making. A State that focuses on making more efficient the channels of communication between citizenship and the State and not only in increasing the automation of bureaucratic processes can be called a digital State. Thus, the group Law and Economy: Silent Commerce, within the line of research Law and Technology, raises the need to construct a theoretical and conceptual foundation on the development and implementation of more efficient communication channels, which contribute to meeting the needs of information and knowledge societies.The group aims to strengthen itself and contribute to this problem with a research proposal for the study of the problems and possible solutions in the conceptualization and construction of digital state in Colombia.To achieve this objective, several products and expected results will be proposed.The group will first be made a panel of experts, professionals, academics and representatives of public institutions and civil society organizations
