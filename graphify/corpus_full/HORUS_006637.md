# Lighting design of the public lighting on the main road of the municipality of Espinal – Tolima

In this document, we present the planimetric survey of the public lighting infrastructure currently held by the Municipality of El Espinal-Tolima, carrying out a comparative analysis of the lighting levels against the levels required by the regulations in force at the time. In addition, we propose to implement new designs applying LED technology, where they contemplate all the normative parameters of design of the new regulation of public and private lighting (RETILAP Resolution 40150 of May 3, 2024). Finally, we plan to carry out an economic analysis of the different proposals of lighting design presented in this document and thus concluding which of these is the most favorable for the project.
