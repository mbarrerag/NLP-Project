# INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATIVE ENGINEERING DOCUMENT WITH PROJECT-BASED LEARNING

The proposal for an international and multidisciplinary teaching project for engineering teaching is presented here, integrating Project-Based Learning and Collaborative Teaching (Team-teaching) with support from ICT resources and academic posters. These methodologies, implemented jointly, constitute an ideal learning environment for developing certain curricular and transversal competencies in an authentic way around the development of a real engineering project
