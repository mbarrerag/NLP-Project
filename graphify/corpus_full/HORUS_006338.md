# Free body diagrams approach and solution: a didactic proposal

The power is the central concept of mechanics and is the basis for understanding the functioning of machines and many phenomena of everyday life. On the other hand, free body diagrams are of great help in modeling the situations where forces act and predict how the movement of bodies changes. However, their correct use faces difficulties mainly related to the understanding of the concept of strength, and, to a lesser extent, with the tools of algebra and trigonometry necessary to predict from them. This work presents a didactic sequence for teaching the concept of strength and the use of free body diagrams in tenth degree.
