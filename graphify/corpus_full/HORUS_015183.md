# Accompanying the academic activities of extension and undergraduate studies of the Department of Diagnostic Images of the National University of Colombia.

The diagnostic images are a wide field of medicine and one of those that advances most quickly with technological development, therefore it is of the utmost importance that undergraduate students and future doctors know the history, indications and findings of the imaging studies. The Department of Diagnostic Images understands this need and commits itself to structure an undergraduate program that allows to achieve these objectives by structuring two subjects: Introduction to Diagnostic Images and Diagnostic Images to be dictated in VI and VII semester respectively; also assigns a resident of the specialty to support and accompany teachers and students during the development of the subjects, having in charge of carrying out evaluations, updating study material and question banks, collecting qualifications and dictating some classes. The subject of Introduction to Diagnostic Images has been based on a semipresential system that has proved effective and allowed an adequate coupling during the short-term period of the pandemic of the COVID-19 with a transition to remote education. After ending the pandemic, a survey is carried out to students, where the analysis of the success of the methodology and the success of the study was carried out
