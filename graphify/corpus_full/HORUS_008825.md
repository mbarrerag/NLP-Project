# EFFECT OF ANTIBIOPELIC PEPTIDES IN MICROBIOLOLOGICAL ISLATIONS OBTAINED FROM BOVINE CRUDE MILK

The aim of the study was to evaluate the effect of four antibiotic peptides (ACLL37-1, ACLL37-2, DLL37-1 and LL37-1) in microbiological isolates in raw bovine milk. An experimental descriptive pilot study was applied with milk samples from 14 cows in milk. In microbiological isolates, biofilm was determined by violet crystal, growth curves and the antibiofilm effect was evaluated with the peptides under study. 41 micro-organisms were isolated from 56 milk samples. 69.2% corresponded to Staphylococcus coagulase negative (SCN). Likewise, 30 strains were identified as strongly adhering biopelicle formators (73%) and 7 (17.1%) weakly adherent. In the growth curves with the peptides, an increase in the late lag phase and regarding the inhibitory effect of the peptides showed a heterogeneous behavior against each isolation.
