# Sailing with the trumpet in two seas: Music for trumpet, piano and percussion by Edgar Calvachi Revelo

This work aims to provide the reader with information on the composition entitled Litorales based on three traditional Colombian rhythms that are located on the Pacific and Caribbean coasts, Colombian trumpet works will be taken as reference with different accompanying formats. Some Colombian composers and their works for trumpet will be discussed. The recitals and master's degree works of the universities that have in their academic offer the master's programme in trumpet in which educational contributions have been made with premiere of works for this instrument. Also a description of the rhythms present in the creation of Litorales and an analysis of its structure indicating its form, harmony and elements used in its composition. In addition, information will be given on the works and composers that make part of the programme of the recital Colombian rhythms being in its entirety a new programme and for the first time interpreted in Colombia.
