# Career and reflections on care and self-care. Angela’s experience, “energy harmonization therapist”

A multitude of experiences and practices of care and healing remains invisible, since it represents jobs, knowledge and subalternized knowledge carried out by women. Therefore this research seeks to understand the path of a Bogota woman who does not identify with any ethnic people and who is dedicated to an emotional care work (physical, mental and spiritual), called by her own “energy harmonisation therapy”. To respond to this research interest, they explore their life experiences related to care, encompassing their trajectory, moments of crisis, ways of learning and exercising care, fundamental relationships, as well as the oppressions and agencies produced. This analysis is carried out both from a theoretical approach, which conceives care as a work and an ethics, as from and for the political practice of the movements of women and feminists. Thus, it proposes an approximation to and enrich the reflections in the field of care.
