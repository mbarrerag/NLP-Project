# Simon Bolivar Metropolitan Park: between places and urban imaginaries of nature

In this dispartation and exercise of an investigative character, answers to the question are elaborated.What relations are manifested between the geographical imaginarys of nature and the significant construction of place in the Metropolitan Park Simón Bolívar of Bogotá from the experiential perspective of its users? This through the elucidation that provides the theoretical scaffolding of humanistic geography, in a theoretical methodological application of the conceptual tools of the semiotic, whose interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary form is the geo-semiotic, particularly in the search for intertextual relations between three sectors of the park (Sector Central, Jardín Botánico José Celestino Mutis and Parque de Los Novios), as well as, in relation to the National Park Enrique Olaya Herrera. The methodological approach was indicated by the phenomenological influence in the humanistic geography in the search for the geographical imaginary forms.
