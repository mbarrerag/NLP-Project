# CONSENSUAL EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN: PROPOSAL FOR INCREASE OF EXPERIMENTAL CONDITIONS

The optimal designs provide the best experimental conditions - support points and the number of replicas to be performed, in order to achieve different objectives, including the estimation of parameters and/or the estimation of a linear or non-linear function in the parameters of the model under consideration, as possibly discrimination between possible candidate models. The optimal designs are the solution to a problem of optimization of a function, in most cases, of Fisher's information matrix. Under the scenario of having different optimal designs to achieve the same objective, a methodology is proposed for obtaining a hybrid design that may have a higher number of support points and may become as efficient as the optimal designs that were involved in this one. An application to a non-linear model shows the application of the proposed methodology and reports the respective efficiencies
