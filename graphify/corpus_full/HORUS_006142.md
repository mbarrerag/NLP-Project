# VIBRATION CONTROL OF STRUCTURES SISTICALLY EXCITED BY USEING AN INNERTER SYSTEM DEVICES

An important part of the structural design process is to reduce the vibration effect in buildings, particularly in those that may be subject to seismic excitement. Although research in the field of structural control has been developing since the early 1900s, the complexity and dimensions of civil structures make this problem particularly difficult to solve due to the high demand for control effort and many other associated limitations. This article analyzes the performance of a novel passive control system that can be used for vibration control in civil structures subjected to excitements at the base.This control device is called inert tuned damper (Tuned Inerter Damper, TID) which overcomes the limitation of classic passive devices that are efficient in a narrow frequency band for which they are tuned, situation which is inconvenient because earthquakes show a diverse frequency content in most occasions.This study also employs an attractive metaheuristic optimization approach based on the differential evolution method (ED), combined with a dynamic analysis elastic time-history, through which the vibration control is employed in two individual targets: first, minimizes the maximum horizontal displacement and second, minimizes the functional displacement; and minimizes the functional optimization of the functional design
