# EXPERIMENTAL AND COMPUTATION STUDY IN THE SYNTHESIS OF ORGANOCATALIZED GLYCEROL CARBONATE BY N-HETEROCCYCLIC CARBENES

Glycerol (Gly) is a by-product in biodiesel synthesis considered an industrial waste. This is why its valorization is valuable as it is a versatile molecule for obtaining value added products such as glycerol carbonate (GC). To improve the reaction it is necessary to catalysts that are able to accelerate the transesterification process. In this sense, the N-heterocyclic Carbenes (NHC) emerge which are molecules that have acquired high relevance in catalysis due to their stereoelectronic properties. That is why the objective of this work is to propose NHCs with potential application as organocatalizers in glycerol valorization. To this, seven NHCs were synthesized and their catalytic activity was evaluated in the reaction of conversion from Gly to GC.
