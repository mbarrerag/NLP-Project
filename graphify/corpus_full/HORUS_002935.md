# BIODIVERSITY, WELFARE AND HEALTH: A STRATEGIC CONNECTION IN COLOMBIAN ORINOCHY

The Orinoquia region presents a great diversity in its flora, of which we can find some studies related to the floristic richness of the region, and to a lesser proportion, studies on the biological or pharmacological properties of these plant resources. In this great biological richness stands out a great diversity of families of plants, finding native species and other species already naturalized in the region, of which, a large number of people have a variety of popular uses by the inhabitants, some of them used as medicinal plants, ornamental, food sources, for the control of some pests, like flavoring additives and colouring, for cosmetic use, to manufacture handicrafts, among others. From the National University of Colombia Orinoquia headquarters is promoting the study of the plant species of the region, to know its chemical composition, to highlight its biological or pharmacological potential and to give it an added value to the biodiversity. Within the research projects developed in the university has planned to evaluate the biological, antioxidant and pharmacological potential of some plants of the region, in addition to know the main chemical components obtained in these; equally has been considered to evaluate the potential of some plants to combat pests of agricultural, and to be of interest. This would be a valuable contribution to the few bibliographic references on this type of research in Orinoquía, and would serve to give greater added value to the great biodiversity of this region
