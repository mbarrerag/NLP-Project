# Evaluation of relational intervention based on attachment in the framework of rights restoration processes

The study evaluated the Related Intervention Based on Apego (IRBA) adapted to the Colombian context in children aged 0 to 5 years, victims of child abuse, who were in the process of reintegration in Bogotá, and their caregivers and caregivers. The effect on the Administrative Process of Restoring Rights (PARD), the re-entry into the protection system, the sensitivity of the caregiver or caregiver, the attitudes towards care and sensitive discipline, and the child problematic behaviors was analyzed. A pre-experimental design was used with a single group with pretest and post-care measures and the following instruments: Sociodemographic questionnaire, Inventory of child behavior (CBCL 1.5-5), Questionnaire of attitudes towards care and sensitive discipline, and Q-Sorts of maternal behavior for infants and preschools. After the completion of the IRBA, at 9 months, all children and girls had the closed PARD, and none had been in the protective system. periodic design with a single group, using pretest and posttest measurements, was employed, and the following instruments were used: Sociodemographic Questionnaire, Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL 1.5-5), Questionnaire on Attitudes towards Care and Sensitive Discipline, and the Q-Sorts of maternal behavior for babies and preschoolers. After completing IRBA, at 9 months, all the boys and girls had their PARD closed, and none of the those had reentered the protection system. No significant differences were found between pre and post measure in any variable. How, care sensitivity presented a positive trend with sensitive behaviour in both instances.
