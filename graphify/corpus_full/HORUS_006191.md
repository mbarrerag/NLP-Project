# Case report: complementary management with neurotherapeutic medicine for the rehabilitation of a patient with intracranial trauma sequelae

Traumatic brain lesions represent a significant global problem, which triggers a physical, emotional and psychosocial problem in the individual. Currently it is considered a chronic disease due to the development of cognitive disorders and the subsequent disability that generates in a significant percentage of patients, becoming a public health priority. Describe the effect of complementary management with Neurotherapeutic Medicine on the rehabilitation of a patient with functional, motor and cognitive deficiency secondary to an intracranial trauma caused by a fall in stairs. Descriptive study in the form of case report of a patient with functional, motor and cognitive deficiency secondary to an intracranial trauma in the rehabilitation with Neurotherapeutic Medicine, in the teaching consultation of the Hospital the Tunal of the Master of Medicine Alternative of the National University with emphasis on neural therapy. A positive effect is evident in the rehabilitation of the patient with functional, motor and cognitive impairment secondary to an intracranial traumatism in the complementary management of Neuralteraic disease. Neutral trauma medicine could have a positive therapeutic effect and be a potential to assist. patient with functional, motor and cognitive deficiency secondary to intracranial trauma in complementy management for rehabilitation with Neuraltherapy Medicine, in the teaching care consultation of the Hospital el Tunal of the master's degree in Alternative Medicine. from the National University with an emphasis on neural therapy. A positive effect is emerging in the rehabilitation of patients with functional, motor and cognitive deficiencies secondary to intracranial trauma in complementing management with Neuraltherapeutic Medicine. Neuraltherapeutic Medicine could have a positive therapeutic effect and be a potential addicted treatment in the rehabilitation of patients with functional, motor and cognitive defects secondary to intracranial trauma in the acute and chronic phase
