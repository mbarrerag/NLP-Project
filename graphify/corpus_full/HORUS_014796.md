# A DISEASE BY CATANOMY.

Cat scratch disease is an emerging zoonosis caused by the bacteria Bartonella henselae, with a variable clinical presentation that affects immunocompetent and immunocompromised patients. It presents typical manifestations 80 to 90% (antesorbing or bite, papule or pustula at the site of inoculation, adenopathy, fever, headache and rash), 5 to 20% has atypical manifestations (long-term fever, ocular compromise, hepatosplenic involvement, among others); this is an infectious disease of difficult diagnosis, which requires high clinical suspicion. The article carries out a review of the literature with emphasis on clinical presentation, methods to confirm diagnosis and provide adequate treatment, and prevention strategies
