# ASSESSMENT OF GEODIVERSITY IN THE AREA OF THE TERCIARY EARTH CARBONIFER OF EMIL GROSSE

The constitution of Colombia establishes in Decree 1353 of 2018 the importance of identifying, protecting, preserving and rehabilitating the geological heritage of the nation. Historically the Department of Antioquia has been characterized by being a region with great economic development associated in part with the extraction of minerals such as coal, gold, limestone rock for the production of cement, and construction materials from its rivers. This work presents a field guide that highlights the geological diversity of the region investigated by Grosse (1926). The aim is to highlight points of interest that, besides its geological relevance, are distinguished by its cultural, historical, anthropological and literary value.
