# STATISTICAL METHODOLOGIES USED IN EXPERIMENTATION WITH POLICICAL MATERIALS: A BIBLEGRAPHICAL REVIEW

This article provides a bibliographic review of some methodologies of the design of experiments, focusing on their role and relevance within the experiments with materials, highlighting the importance of the designs of experiments within the research using examples of methodological strategies, first of all a review was carried out that would allow the introduction of the concept of design of experiments, as this was conceived, its principles and its relation to research, including the main elements of which a design of experiments is composed, and some assumptions to be taken into account when applying an experimental design in the research. In addition, different methodologies used by some authors associated with the experiments are shown, differences are mentioned between each of them and why they are used according to the necessity of the experiment. Then, different examples of design of experiments used in different research articles focused on polymeric materials are found, in these examples the different variables of the experiment are highlighted, what was the purpose of the research and why they were chosen according to the design of experiments mentioned. Finally, after carrying out the bibliographic review, it was found that in the majority of experimental designs applied to the field of experimentation allows to optimize the resources without need to make a great deal of the design of the experiments, depending on the design of the type of experiments
