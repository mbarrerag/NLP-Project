# Implementation of problematic situations with fertilizers, pesticides and fungicides as a strategy to encourage critical thinking in the learning of stoichiometry

The present study of deepening focused on the design and implementation of a didactic unit based on problematic situations related to fertilizers, pesticides and fungicides, with the aim of encouraging critical thinking in the learning of stochiometry in students of the tenth and eleventh grades of the educational institution Alfonso Daza Aguirre of the municipality of Herveo-Tolima. In the first instance the importance of developing basic skills of critical thinking in young people, especially in the context of scientific education, where the application of theoretical concepts to real situations is fundamental. The work has a mixed approach, where various activities involving the resolution of problematic situations were designed and implemented. Interactive class sessions, debates and group work were carried out, which allowed students to explore and discuss the implications of the use of chemical products in agriculture. The findings revealed that the students improved their understanding of the stochiometry and developed basic skills of critical thinking in order to evaluate and argue different solutions to the problems raised.
