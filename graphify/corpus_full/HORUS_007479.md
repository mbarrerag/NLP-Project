# Inhabiting Ordura in a Fatphobic World: A Critical Perspective on Health Based on the Experiences of Fat Colombian Women

This research study examines the impact of fatphobia on the integral health of Colombian women, putting their life experiences in the forefront and questioning the concept of "health care" that has been built around fat people. It seeks to question traditional conceptions of health, reflect on fatphobia and propose concrete actions to confront it from psychotherapy. Using a qualitative methodology with a phenomenological and feminist approach, interviews were conducted with 17 fat women, in order to explore their experiences in the face of fatphobia and its consequences in different aspects of their life. The results show that fatphobia has a negative impact on the integral health of these women, affecting their access to rights and exposing them to various forms of discrimination.
