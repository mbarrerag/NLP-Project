# Σ -Semicommutative rings and their skew PBW extensions

In this paper, we introduce the concept of Σ -semicommutative ring for Σ a finite family of endomorphisms of a ring R. We relate this class of rings with other classes of rings such as Abelian, reduced, Σ -rigid, nil-reversible and rings satisfying the Σ -skew reflexive nilpotent property. Also, we study some ring-theoretical properties of skew PBW extensions over Σ -semicommutative rings. We prove that if a ring R is Σ -semicommutative with certain conditions of compatibility on derivations, then for every skew PBW extension A over R, R is Baer if and only if R is quasi-Baer, and equivalently, A is quasi-Baer if and only if A is Baer. Finally, we consider some topological conditions for skew PBW extensions over Σ -semicommutative rings
