# Development and innovation of single-seater electric traction vehicles based on various engineering approaches to sustainable mobility development in the country approved for national competencies

The main objective of STEOS is to develop and manufacture two electric traction vehicles (Sharky and McUN single-seat cars) with a distinctive approach to performance from different engineering techniques, with the firm conviction that promoting sustainable mobility is a fundamental task in the current context. In the research seedling capacity, STEOS is in a strategic position to contribute significantly to the advancement of sustainable mobility, a field that has gained increasing relevance both nationally, as well as internationally. Through its academic and scientific work, the project seeks to optimize and enrich this field of knowledge, and aims to demonstrate the results obtained through its active participation in the third national competition of electric traction vehicles. Thus, STEOS stands as an agent of change in the promotion and development of sustainable mobility solutions that will have a positive impact on our environment and the general world
