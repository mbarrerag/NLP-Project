# Approaching strategies for demand management from the active user on an intelligent power grid.

The main objective of the electric energy chain and all its associated systems is to meet timely and efficient the demand of the users, fulfilling the requirements of quality and reliability of the service. Factors like the economic, social and meteorological influence on the demand of electricity. It may be interesting to know the total demand that will demand a global system or study the behavior of the demand of energy from the points of consumption. It is therefore necessary to understand the behavior of the demand and evaluate indirect strategies to influence it. The consumption patterns have a high diversity and can be more stable in some users than in others, there are groups of users that present regular in the quantity of energy and the intervals of time in which they need it during the day. It can also be found groups of users with similar consumption patterns due to the final use that they give to the consumed energy, this fact gives rise to multiple studies of characterization and prediction of the demand. The chapter 5 includes the development of software in Modelica that enabled the model to be implemented. Chapter 6 presents the simulation exercises developed with the model together with the results found. Chapter 7 presents the validation of the results performed by experts in the area and Chapter 8 presents the conclusions and recommendations of the research
