# VALIDITY OF THE SOLIDAD URBAN SCALE IN CRONIC PATIENTS CAREERS IN COLOMBIA

Loneliness in family caregivers is presented as a frequent experience due to the demands that the care of a person with chronic disease involves. to determine the facial validity and content of UCLA in family caregivers of people with chronic disease. Psychometric study of facial validation and content of the UCLA scale. For the validation of the content, experts participated in the area and the items were evaluated in terms of clarity, redundancy and relevance. The Lawshe index modified by Tristan was the statistic used for its analysis. For the facial validation, family caregivers of people with chronic disease participated to the participation of those who were given a cognitive interview and the areas of understanding, recovery, judgment, adequacy of response options and content of the question were analyzed. content validity: 9 of 18 items of the UCLA scale presented adjusted IVC above 0.8. Item 8 presented adjusted IVC of 0.66, which required analysis and adjustment of semantics by the validation committee. With regard to facial validity, from the cognitive interview process it was evidenced that for all items the 86% or more of the participants reported a chronic disease
