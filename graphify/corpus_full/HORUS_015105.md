# Origin of the El Cerrito Formation in the Folded Belt of San Jacinto: Paleogeographical implications for the Colombian Caribbean

Summary In order to contribute to the knowledge of the sources of contribution and sedimentary environments in the Cinturón Plegado de San Jacinto, the sedimentary sequence of the upper Miocene recovered by the ANH-Los Pájaros-1X well was studied. This was deposited in delta systems that oscillated between prodelta-front environments of the delta and deltaic plain. The average ratio of quartz-Q, feldspato-F and lithic-L of the sandstones is Q36F24L40; the lytics identified correspond to andesites, quartzites, schistes, granites, lodolites and sandstones, indicating multiple sources of contribution.
