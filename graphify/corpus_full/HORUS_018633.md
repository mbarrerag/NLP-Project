# Implementation of a heat exchanger with phase change material (PCM-HX) in a thermoplastic injection mold cooling system.

The interest in increasing energy efficiency in processes and reducing carbon footprint and global warming are the main pillars for finding increasingly efficient systems. The use of thermal storage systems PCM allows the use of thermal energy to be exploited, stabilized and stored for subsequent use, which allows for significant improvement in a process if a correct design is carried out. This paper studied a thermoplastic injection mould cooling system, knowing the temperature oscillations of the cooling liquid analytically designed a PCM-HX phase change exchanger with persian fin geometry and flat tubes that helps reduce temperature fluctuations present in the system. An improvement in the temperature stability of the cooling liquid was found when integrating PCM-HX, reducing oscillations to 50%, further showing that the chiller operates in longer cycles of on and off, which means a reduction in energy consumption of 11%. The results found allow exploring new alternatives for implementation of thermal energy accumulators, which can have a significant decrease in electrical consumption and more stable cooling systems, contributing to the reduction of carbon footprint
