# SERIES GAME DESIGN -CHOICE OFF -TO TEACH THE DECISION-MAKING PROCESS

Decision making refers to the cognitive capacity to choose; involving: analysis, categorization, probabilistic judgments, construction of alternatives, decision and feedback. For a rational decision-making process to be considered complete, there must be conditions: present and future separation, awareness of decision-making, and motivation to eliminate the difference and determine whether the organization and the decision-maker have the resources necessary to make it.The selected methodology is to design the game for learning purposes with two characteristics, one to teach the decision-making process and, two, fun as a pedagogical contribution.Through a board designed by the authors, the participants show the ability to learn the decision-making process. Players are assigned in two groups, each group has a board and according to knowledge progress through the board following the instructions they manage to finish, the player with the most successful answers wins. Players are postgraduate students in business areas and validate the game process and generate a quantitative rating according to the result.
