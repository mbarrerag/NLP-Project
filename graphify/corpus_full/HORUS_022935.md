# Effects of the implementation of a public policy on food and nutrition security in early childhood: analysis of the case of Guajira 2009-2015.

The main objective of this research was to analyze the effects of the implementation of a public policy on food and nutrition security in early childhood: Analyse case Guajira 2009-2015; its methodology focused on the analysis of neo-institutional public policy; the results found show the need to generate socio-centric public policies that contribute to ensuring positive changes in food and nutrition security in the early childhood department of Guajira, which led to failures in the understanding of the territory that led to the implementation of public policies a process with many shortcomings. the study concluded that the negative impacts of public policies in Guajira were due to ignorance of the needs of the territory, corruption and difficulties due to the climatic and geographical situation that characterizes the region. Recommendations: it was suggested to incorporate the results of the analysis as part of the Development Plans of the Department of Guajira; greater support from decision makers is required; strengthening small producers, reducing food insecurity and improving the nutritional status of the child population
