# RECOGNITION OF VALUE STORYTELLING AND CO-CREATION IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF MICROSEGMENTS THROUGH THE DIGITAL MARKETING STRATEGY

This document seeks to evaluate the contribution of an integrative model that allows, through the Digital Marketing Strategy, to recognize the value of the generation of co-created content and the Storytelling in the microsegmentation of the market. The methodological strategy focuses on presenting the concepts from the academic developments found by searching for their relationship in a bibliometric study. Subsequently, the variables required for the construction of a conceptual model are presented, with an analytical-synthetic approach. The bibliometric analysis results in the fact that the topics have not been developed jointly. Finally, it is concluded that the adaptation of a model of integration of the 3 variables contributes to the adequate development of communication channels. This, thanks to an assertive profiling of the clients derived from a proposal of clearly defined value from storytelling and co-creation, which would be communicated through Digital Marketing
