# SUSTAINABILITY OF THE OPERATION DIRECTION

This book addresses a number of issues related to sustainability and management from different perspectives. In this regard, it seeks to provide a relevant reference to the study of sustainability from different functional areas of administration, including human management, innovation, entrepreneurship, leadership and finance. Through diverse methodologies that include states of the issue and approximations of quantitative and qualitative order, the present text is positioned as an innovative proposal that stands out for its originality and for the interdisciplinarity required for the study of administration and sustainability
