# LABORATORY GUIDE ON EXPERIMENTATION AND INSTRUMATION

This laboratory guide describes the measurement and load application procedures used in laboratory practices of the mechanical solids subject and in some usual structural engineering tests. In particular, here is the appropriate way to use the measuring instruments and equipment, including the reading and presentation of the data they provide
