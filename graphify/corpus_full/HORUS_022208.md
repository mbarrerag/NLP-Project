# Digital educational resource for teaching the concept of DNA.

The study presented below presents the experience of applying a digital educational resource for teaching the concept of DNA in the educational institution Social Promotion of Neiva-Huila. To this end, a questionnaire of 10 open questions was conducted on various cases exposed, where the students expressed their ideas. The answers given were categorized with the implementation of a rubric, the results were used as input for the design of a digital tool, organized in a didactic sequence on DNA. This work has a mixed approach, with a descriptive-interpretive scope and quasi-experimental design. Through research, it was determined that the use of different digital tools, allows teachers to teach abstract concepts in an innovative and meaningful way for students
