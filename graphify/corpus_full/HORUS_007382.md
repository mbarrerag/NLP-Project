# TiAlTaZrNbN corrosion resistance in AISI 304 steel with potential application in the food industry

The present study investigates the structural properties and the corrosion resistance of the high entropy coating TiAlTaZrNbN, deposited on a steel substrate AISI 304, commonly used material in the food industry. The technique of cathodic spraying with high-power impulse magnetron (HiPIMS) was used for the deposition of the coating. The coating characterization was performed through different analytical methods. The crystalline structure and the phases present in the coating were determined by means of the technique of X-ray diffraction (XRD), while the chemical composition was also determined by means of the dispersived energy spectroscopy (EDS). The coating morphology was examined using electron microscopy of the sweep (SEM), and the surface roughness was evaluated through the confocal microscopy. On the other hand, the composition of the coating was studied against corrosion in three electrolytic electrolytes: the acid solution of citric acid and tap water, using the techniques of potentiodynamic polarisation (PDP) and the electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (IS).
