# CORPORATE GOVERNMENT AND ETHICS: A BIBLE ANALYSIS

In recent years, the relationship between the Corporate Government and organizational ethics has taken special importance in the evolution of the competitive strategies of companies, given the necessary relationship between structures and processes for organizational direction with the socio-economic and environmental purposes of these actions. This article seeks to develop a bibliometric analysis, from scientific and statistical points of view, in order to study the structure of the recent literature on this relation; also, to examine how the diversity of contexts in administration influence the reputation of the company, directly or indirectly. As a result, it is observed that research on the impact of corporate governance on companies has helped institutions to meet transparent forma with the main needs and expectations of society. In the same context, proposals for future studies in this important area have been generated
