# Sequencing and cloning of the dihydrofolateductase (dhfr) and dihydropteroatosynthetase (dhps) genes of the RH strain and of 2 isolates of Toxoplasma gondii.

As internal combustion vehicles have a greater presence in Colombia, they generate a significant amount of hazardous waste. Lead-acid batteries (BPA) that must be changed periodically once they reach their useful life are possibly the most common hazardous wastes produced by vehicles. The inadequate removal of BPAs can be highly hazardous for the environment and the well-being of people. The recycling of Used Lead-Acido batteries (BPAU) by pyrometalurgia has been established as an alternative to recover valuable elements, mainly Pb. This thesis describes a simulation of Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) of a submerged vertical launch furnace (TSL), a technology that has not been implemented in Colombia for the recycling of BPAU but is considered promising given its high energy efficiency and production capacity. a uniform melt-inflation component was developed. The condition of operation and geometric configuration of the TSL furnace for the production of 320 kg/h secondary lead from BPAU, value that is adapted to the Colombian demand for lead batteries. The effect of the fuel flow and OFD was determined. The results indicate that the heat efficiency of the furnace is reduced by increasing the combustion power for a constant turbulence constant of 30° Gas penetration increases with CH4 flow. The proposed design considers a central well and secondary wells where the flow has a radial component equivalent to 80% of the magnitude of the speed.
