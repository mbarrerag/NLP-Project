# Entropy of black hole entanglement in the AdS/CFT duality.

In this thesis, a scalar field is studied on a Schwarzschild time space using the vacuum states of Boulware and Hartle-Hawking-Israel taking into account Mukohyama-Israel interpretation to try to explain the entropy of Bekenstein-Hawking and where it can be located. Likewise, the correspondence between an Anti-Sitter time space and a conforming quantum field theory called AdS/CFT Duality and the way it tries to explain the entropy of Bekenstein-Hawking is studied
