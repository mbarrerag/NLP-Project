# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION BY GC/MS OF THE SENTAL OIL OF BOYACANA Moldenke, RECOVERED IN SANTANDER-COLOMBIA

The essential oil of Lantana buoyana Moldenke is composed mostly of monoterpenes and bicyclic sesquiterpenes. Under the criteria of attempted identification using linear retention rates (DB 5MS and DB-WAX columns) and the comparison of mass spectra (MS, EI, 70 eV, match 90%), with those reported in spectral libraries (ADAMS, NIST), were determined as trans-ß-caryofilene and Sabinene majority compounds, with relative areas of 13.5% and 12.2%, respectively. The species Lantana buoyana Moldenke is a plant of Colombian biodiversity, whose majority compounds could provide interesting biological properties and serve as a natural source for the elaboration of various bioproducts
