# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND CITIZEN SECURITY: APPLICATIONS AND CHALLENGES OF THE GREAT MODELS OF LANGUAGE

Citizen security is one of the main concerns of the Colombian population and has become a priority in government development plans. In the framework of the Antioquia Development Plan and the Departmental Strategic Agreement on Science, Technology and Innovation, it seeks to increase scientific production and generate knowledge to address specific problems, such as citizen security. In this context, this research work is oriented to the review and analysis of emerging technologies, such as artificial intelligence, with the potential of application to identify and detect possible criminal threats or patterns through text analysis that can be used in the design of early warning systems based on textual information providing preventive solutions in the field of citizen security. In this work, research can be carried out on the current state of these large models and how they could be used in the pro-citizens' security.
