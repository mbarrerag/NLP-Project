# LOMBRICES (Annelida: Oligochaeta) AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO THE PHYSICAL PROPERTIES OF THE SOLE UNDER CAFE, FOREST AND BARBECHO

During the continuous activity of earthworms inside the soil, galleries, macropores and turricles are generated, which modify their functioning, where the aeration and storage of water is increased, this affects the development of the roots of plants and other organisms. In order to identify the relationship between the presence of worms and the physical changes occurring in the soil environment, three conditions of soil management were selected among them under coffee, forest and fallow cultivation, in the municipality of Envigado (Antioquia, Colombia).In the soil was evaluated: apparent density, hydraulic conductivity, resistance to penetration (mechanical impedance) and stability of the aggregates, the earthworms were determined the number of individuals, weight and their length, showing significant difference in each of these variables, both with the handling and in depth, is thus, in coffee in the first 10 cm of depth was accommodated the greatest number of individuals. While in the forest of 10 to -20 cm the largest number of individuals were presented, showing significant differences in each of these variables, with the majority of the species of mardibular and epidritics (Mardilus, is found in the majority of 10 cm of individuals)
