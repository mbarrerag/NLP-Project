# Transitional justice and ethnic rights

The Comprehensive System of Truth, Justice, Reparation and Guarantee of Non-Repetition includes in various objectives and initiatives the ethnic component, which responds not only to the fact that communities protected with special rights in terms of ethnic affiliation have been among the most victimized, but also to the fact that the same Peace Agreement signed between the Colombian Government and the former FARC includes an Ethnic Chapter, which establishes the modalities and forms of consideration of this subject in the implementation of this one. This project seeks to systematize and evaluate this objective of implementation from two perspectives: the recommendations of the final report of the Commission for the Clarification of Truth (CEV) and the selection of macro cases by the Special Jurisdiction for Peace (JEP)
