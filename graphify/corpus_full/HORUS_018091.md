# Gray scale of radiographic images in the assessment of moisture content in soybeans

The degree of humidity is one of the most important factors in the choice of procedures for obtaining high quality seeds. In most quality control programs, the degree of humidity is determined by laboratory tests based on maximum water removal when the seed is subjected to heat application under controlled conditions. X-rays are characterized by the generation of images with different levels of gray after the interaction of X-rays with the seed tissues. The aim of this research was to determine the gray scale values of radiographic images of soy seed (Glycine max (L.)) at different levels of hydration, seeking to establish relationships with the conventional method of moisture determination. Radiographic images of seeds of two lots with differences in seed size at different hydration periods. The images determined the values of the gray scale in different areas of the seed and compared with the humidity test by conventional method. The values of the gray scale presented variation between 120 to 187 and 134 to 215pixels for small seeds and large seeds, respectively.
