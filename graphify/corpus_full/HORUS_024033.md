# Proposal for the implementation of Lean Manufacturing tools in a company that manufactures friction materials for brake systems.

The present master's work corresponds to the results of the research carried out around the production process of the heavy band line of Incolbest S.A. The general objective was to develop a proposal for the implementation of continuous improvement tools for the increase of productivity in the process of manufacturing friction material bands for brake systems. The diagnostic stage was carried out following the mapping methodology of the value chain (Lean Manufacturing tool), from which the causes of the inefficiency of the production process were analyzed and improvement actions were established. Finally, the proposed improvements were simulated under the process approach (simulation of discrete events) in three scenarios in Flexsim software. The combination of the elimination of the final grinding operation and the implementation of a semiautomatic system for drilling machining centers improvements that would represent greater production efficiency (8.6 games/hour) and an increase of 38.4% to 47.6% of the added value in perforateded. It is intended that the company take into account the results of the performance measures as a basis for the manufacturing process
