# JOSEL LUIS SERT AND PAUL WIENER IN MEDELLIN: THE PILOT PLAN AND THE MODERN PROJECT

This article reviews the participation of the urban planners Wiener and Sert in Medellín at the end of the 1940s, with the aim of recognizing the current implications in the implementation of the Pilot Plan. The arrival of these two urban planners brought the opportunity, not only to review Medellín in terms of urban planning, but also the possibility to be equated with other Latin American cities. To achieve this purpose, a documentary research structured in two stages was carried out: the first was the existing bibliographic review on the subject and the second was the investigation of archive material and specialized serial journals of the time. Although the object of study has already been investigated in our context, this work offers, on the one hand, new unpublished documentary material resulting from rigorous archival work, and, on the other, the identification of the most important current traces of Wiener and Sert’s work in Medellín. In contribution, this article does not intend to repeat what has already been said, but to complement the academic discussions around the construction process, thinking and urban practice for the city as an ongoing issue to be discussed within the urban planning of the city
