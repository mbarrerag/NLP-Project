# Public-private partnerships: a formula for the development of strategic infrastructure through the state-of-the-art cooperation.

This paper shows the existence of robust chaos in the stroboscopic map associated with a standardized canonical form of discontinuous linear systems defined to two-dimensional pieces (PWL). The work consists of 5 chapters. In the first chapter we give a rigorous study of some concepts and essential results of discrete maps reported in the literature. In the second chapter we present the standardized canonical form for discontinuous linear systems defined to pieces, we study some properties of this system and present some mechanisms to generate limit cycles in this system reported in the literature. The third chapter presents the stroboscopic map associated with the standardized canonical form and studies some properties of this map given in the literature, such as the exponential matrix, the stability of its ́orbits and our own results on the Lyapunov exponents of this map. In the fourth chapter we use the results presented in the previous chapters to study the existence of robust chaos in the stroboscopic map for the focus case and introduce a small study of robust chaos in the case of the strozycal.
