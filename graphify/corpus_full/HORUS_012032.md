# PREDICTION OF THE AGE OF ABALONES BY TECHNIQUES OF DATA MINERY

Establishing the age of the abalons is a rather complex and important work, even more if you want to preserve and make sustainable the population of them and their lucrative business. The calculation of the age involves a complex physical - chemical process in order to visualize the number of rings and from them determine their age. In this article a methodology is established by means of which, based on 8 independent variables (Sex, length, diameter, height, total weight, descaled weight, visor weight, capazon weight) is predicted the number of rings that can have a balon. This process is carried out by means of the automatic platform of data mining and free software called Weka. Taking as a basis the different records (from a previously defined database which can be found in http:// archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/Abalone). This process is performed by means of an ARFF program which is interpreted by this platform. Initially using a statistical analysis by means of the Chi Cuadrado test, the average of the most important independent variables are ranced.
