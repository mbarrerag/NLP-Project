# Epoxic/fique composite material, as external reinforcement in concrete elements submitted to bending.

In this investigation, the influence of externally reinforcing concrete beams subjected to bending was determined using a compound made with epoxy matrix and fique fibers. For this purpose, the mechanically characterized fique fibre before and after being subjected to alkaline treatment. The alkaline treatment used was based on sodium hydroxide (NaOH), achieving with this procedure the improvement of the mechanical properties of fique fibre. Subsequently, the fique tissue was mechanically characterized, previously subjected to alkaline treatment. A epoxy matrix was manufactured, which was subjected to stress tests to determine its mechanical properties. For the manufacture of the epoxy/fique compound (EF) two types of tissues, a tupid tissue (D) and a smooth tissue (MD), were used to obtain higher mechanical performance when using the tupid fabric, with a content of fique fibres of 10 %, was added to the compound. The concrete beams were manufactured using two types of resistance to compression, one of 17 MPa and the other of 24 MPa.
