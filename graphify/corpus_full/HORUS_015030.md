# Assessment of the impact of concentration gradients on the metabolism of microbial central carbon using a multiscale modelling approach.

Modelling a bioprocess is crucial to have an understanding and predicting important operating variables in the industry. To do this it is not only pertinent to model the bioreactor with its transport phenomena but also some intracellular processes that transform the available substrate. Thus, the general objective of this thesis is to perform a multiscale modelling that includes the macro scale (reactor) and the micro scale (cell metabolism).The mathematical description of the bioreactor consisted of a compartment model, in order to calculate the concentration gradients.For the given configuration of the reactor a mixing rate of 70s was calculated if the feeding is in the middle of the tank.On the other hand, a kinetic model was constructed to predict experimental data of the central carbon metabolism of Saccharomyces cerevisiae; specifically for the glucolysis, the route of the pentose phosphate and the metabolism of trehalose and glycogen was made
