# COMPARISON OF THE EFFICIENCY OF DIFFERENT ELECTRO-FENTON METHODS, AS ALTERNATIVE TREATMENT POINTS FOR A RESIDUAL TEXTILE INDUSTRIAL WATER CONTAMINATED WITH BLACK COLORANT ACE 194

Several textile companies of the Colombian coffee coffee axis use the black colouring acid 194 (NA194) in their dyeing processes. The monitoring of the waste water of these companies, carried out by the research group PRISMA (GI-PRISMA), has been established that the use of the NA194 generates effluents (ARnD) that are highly polluting and exceed the permissible limits established by national environmental legislation (apar colour = 45850 U Pt-Co, Chemical Demand for Oxygen, DQO = 3800 mg O2/L and Biological Demand for Oxygen, DBO5 = 790 mg O2/L). The advanced electrochemical oxidation processes (PEAOs) have been reported as a potential alternative for the treatment of textile waste water. Among the PEOs, the process Electro-Fenton (EF) is of special interest due to its easy implementation and high organic load re-ups.
