# A NOVEL OZONE MEMBRANE CONTACTOR FOR THE REMOVAL OF CECS FROM SECONDARY EFFLUENT OF MUNICIPAL WASTEWATER AND REVERSE OSMOSIS CONCENTRATE.

A novel ozone membrane contactor was applied for the removal of contaminants of emerging concern (CECs) present in a secondarytreated municipal wastewater (MWW) and its resulting concentrate from reverse osmosis (ROconc.). In this system, the O3 gas permeates through the membrane pores generating O3 microbubbles that are uniformly delivered to the wastewater. Different pharmaceuticals (e.g. analgesics, antibiotics, antihypertensive, etc.) were detected in both samples. Ozonation was carried out in continuous mode with a retention time (RT) of 1.6 min and the effect of the O3 dose was investigated. Removals =80% were attained for 5 of the 13 CECs detected in the MWW (1.1 g O3 g-1 DOC) and 7 of the 23 CECs in the ROconc. (1.0 g O3 g-1 DOC)
