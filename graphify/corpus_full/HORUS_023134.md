# INSENSIBILIZATION OF EQUIDOS IN A BENEFIT PLANT IN COLOMBIA AND ANIMAL WELFARE

The methods of desensitization aim to make the animal unconscious and remain so until death, avoiding that it feels pain or discomfort when being bleeding, so in equidae it is recommended, the shot with penetrating or non penetrating bolt to generate irreversible cerebral concussion. Describe the practices of desensitization in a plant of benefit of equidae in Colombia and its relation to animal welfare. A transversal study was carried out in 160 equidae sacrificed in plant enabled by the Institute of Surveillance of Medicines and Foods (INVIMA). Variables were analyzed as method of desensitization, rhythmic respiration, corneal reflexion and attempts to re-incorporate, time of desensitization and desensitization/disacretion. It was obtained endorsement of the Committee of Ethics for the Care and Use of Animals (CICUA) of the University CES. It was found that 99.38% of the equidae were insensibilization with pointed and 0.63% was desangrated.
