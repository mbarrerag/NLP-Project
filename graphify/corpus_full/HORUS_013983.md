# Monitoring and Analysis of Hydrogeotechnic Conditions in the University City

Several buildings on the Campus of the University City have experienced problems of settlements, resulting in cracks and structural damage. Considering that the Office of Environmental Management (OGA) has data on the variation of the water table level on the campus, which may be related to the settlements of the buildings, this project will investigate and make geotechnical suggestions for the construction or reinforcement of buildings in the University City. For this reason, this project aims to enrich the information already available to assess the risk of damage due to drying and wetting of the subsoil in specific areas of the campus, using a detailed methodology in four phases. The first phase involves a thorough bibliographic review to establish a solid knowledge base by the members of the team. In the second phase, the information of 24 existing feet on the campus, related to the variation of the water level, will be collected and will be analyzed, allowing to identify temporary patterns. The third phase involves sampling of the subsoil in different seasons to assess the behavior of the surface layer of clay during cycles of wetting and drying. In the last phase, geotechnical recommendations will be made based on the building and will serve as a future design design and construction
