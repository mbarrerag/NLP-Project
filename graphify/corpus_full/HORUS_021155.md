# Institutional designs: peasantry, conflict and peace

This project aims to characterize the policies through which the State has related to the peasantry after the signing of the final agreement between the government and the FARC at the end of 2016, as well as its social and territorial effects. In particular, the project will address the following policies and institutional offers aimed at different sectors of the peasantry: restitution of land, the so-called Integral Rural Reform _x0096_contemplated in paragraph 1 of the agreement_x0096_, DDR (disarmament, demobilization and reintegration of ex-combatants), citizen participation in the context of the PDETS, and substitution of crops of illicit use.The project has thus three main purposes.First, to make an analysis of the institutional and political framework in which the above mentioned policies arise and are developed.For this purpose, a period of observation that starts from the year 2011, where the law 1448 _x0096_o Law of Victims appears, which constituted the institutional framework for these policies, until the moment of development of the research (2023-2024) is sought to observe the current application of those policies, as well as its social and territorial effects.
