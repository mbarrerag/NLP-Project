# Consolidation of the Center for Thought Medicines, Information and Power: Incidence, social reforms and essential medicines.

The Center of Thought Medicines, Information and Power (CP), created since 2016, managed to position itself as a national and global reference in the discussion and incidence of public policies of inclusive drugs and other technologies in health. Since 2019, and especially during the pandemic by COVID-19, the position was greater thanks to the production of documents, the review of evidence and the active participation in national discussions around the health emergency. The CP has published more than 200 documents, among research articles, public policy summaries, bulletins and position notes, which have facilitated not only technical recognition, academic; but the visibility in social networks and recognition among opinion leaders, mass and alternative media, and between decision makers, who consult the analyses and proposals developed to use them as an input to enrich the proposals and discussions. To strengthen the CP and continue with its positioning, this project is posed as a new stage that contemplates the implementation of an impact strategy that includes the design of a communication campaign, the definition of impact routes and the construction of new strategic alliances that allow it to continue the sustainability scheme that has allowed it to maintain. to form successful and interdisciplinary work teams, as well as to find spaces that articulate teaching, research and extension, will allow new researchers of the UNAL Network to join the strategy of impact proposed, through a territorial perspective, at the same time Latin American and the global south to contribute to the solution of concrete problems of public policy
