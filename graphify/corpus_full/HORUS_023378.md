# A low-cost lightning electric field measurement device for tropical zone. An initial result.

Currently, the different areas of engineering use design criteria that are commonly based on international standards are used that, in some cases, do not represent the geographical and meteorological conditions of each particular site. This is why different authors suggest continuing and deepening the localized study of the characteristics of atmospheric electric discharges, especially in the tropical zone. This article presents a low-cost lightning-radiated electric field measurement system implemented by the E3P research group of the National University of Colombia, Manizales campus. The integrated circuit (BUF634) is introduced, which is a commercial integrated circuit and is proposed as an alternative to the most used integrated circuits at the national level (BUF602 and LMH0033) and internationally (LMH0033 and MSK0033), which were canceled due to their manufacturers (LMH0033), or changed its characteristics (BUF602). Detailed comments are made on each stage of the implementation of the device, going through the design, calibration and results, referenced studies with similar configurations.
