# The territory of the Sabana de Bogotá from the word: an in-digenous construction and Spanish reconstruction from the onomastic, in the sixteenth century."

The present research project seeks to analyze how Muiscas groups understood and inhabited the territory of the Sabana de Bogotá from language, that is, from the way of defining and conceptualizing the geographical space in moments prior to the Spanish invasion, and how this dynamic was transformed from the territorial control that the Spaniards began to exercise since 1536, that is, giving new names to the sites, but with this also generating a new definition and significance of what that geographical space represented for them. It also seeks to establish how these two ways of understanding, appropriating and experiencing the territory were integrated creating a specific form of definition of what the Sabana de Bogotá should be and therefore how it should be occupied and inhabited from that moment on. The analysis will be made from the theoretical framework of ethnohistory and cultural history and from the conceptual framework of the named territories and of the ethnic space vs. the geographic space.
