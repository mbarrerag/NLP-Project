# THE MILL AND A FRONT

The first re-issue of the original version of La Vorágine by José Eustasio Rivera (1924), in the commemorative framework of its centenary. This volume has 1) the 1924 version, prepared and mounted by Carlos Guillermo Páramo Bonilla, Angela Zárate Díaz, Jineth Ardila Ariza and Carmen Eliza Acosta Peñaloza, 2) an extensive corpus of notes, prepared exclusively for this edition by Carlos Guillermo Páramo Bonilla and Angela Zárate Díaz, which compares this version with the manuscripts of the novel deposited in the National Library of Colombia. Is it an exercise that to date has not had precedents - and expands the information on several historical events, and 3) a collection of fifteen unpublished interdisciplinary essays that make the times of invitation and reading guide. This entry corresponds to this last point
