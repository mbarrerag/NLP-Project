# Net costs of breast cancer care in a health-promoting entity in Colombia

The estimated care costs are fundamental to implement technologies, programs and policies in cancer prevention and care. Estimate the average annual net costs of care for breast cancer by care phase and the average annual net cost per patient in each care phase in patients affiliated to a health promoter entity - EPS (contributive regime) of Colombia. Case study and retrospective paired controls performed with data from 4 278 patients attended between January 1st 2010 and December 31st 2016 in a EPS with presence throughout Colombia (713 cases with breast cancer and 3 565 controls). Net costs were estimated using the net cost method and were expressed in constant US$2023 for each care phase (initial, continuous and last year of life).
