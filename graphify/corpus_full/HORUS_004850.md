# LAND-CONSTRUCTION METHOD IN LEVEL CURVES APPLICABLE TO THE PRODUCTION OF SUSTAINABLE CULTURES FOR FAMILY AGRICULTURE.

Currently, family farming has been facing great challenges, mainly with the aim of preserving the natural resources that guarantee food security in Colombia. The development of innovative manual agricultural tools in this field of agriculture depends heavily on strategies to promote the technicalization of the rural sector and the traditional peasant communities that make up it. However, the large tracts of land with topographies in the form of hillside, in addition to the variations in climatic conditions in relation to thermal floors in these topographies, hinder the implementation of new technologies, machines and modern tools that are used in the agro-industrial sector at the global level primarily in developed countries. The ancestral agricultural practices in which the construction of terraces under the technique of level curves in these complex lands is implemented can now be presented or re-established as a comprehensive strategy applied in a variety of crops and/or polycultivatives where agronomic techniques are enabled to be friendly to the environment. However, a set of knowledge associated with soil diversity, pests and arvenses is required, as well as the effective choice in the different types of crops in sowing, vital for the construction of new sustainable agrarian reform with resilient characteristics
