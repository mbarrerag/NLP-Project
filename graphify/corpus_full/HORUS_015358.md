# Analysis of electrical behaviour of conductive textiles used in portable lightning protection devices against lightning currents.

This paper analyses the behavior of the conductive tissues used in temporary Ray Protection Shelters, against lightning type currents, in order to determine the surface densities of critical current that can cause sublimation of the different conductive materials. This is because this sublimation causes a decrease in the electrical continuity of the textile or even destruction of it, which would cause exposure to the lightning current of the occupants of the shelter. In this sense, the thermal effects are simulated by Joule effect that generates the circulation of the current in wave form 10/350 μs through the conductive tissue. For this purpose, changes of temperature for tissues in constantan material and copper according to the standard IEC 62305-1, considering the phenomenon as adiabatic, where by the speed of circulation of the current is considered that there is no heat exchange between the conductive material and its surroundings.
