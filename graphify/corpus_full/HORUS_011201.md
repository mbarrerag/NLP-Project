# A CALL FOR ACTION FOR COUPLING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE RESEARCH AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT

Social and technology agendas project strategically to connect AI and SDGs in defining societal priorities and demands. We examined the alignment between STI and SDGs through an Artificial Intelligence (AI) lens through a scientiometrics analysis of the EEE Xplore database from 200 to 2019. We address these critical questions: 1. To what extent is AI related to SDGs? 2. Which core subjects of AI are connected to an emerging interest in SD? 3. To what extent does AI stimulate collaboration between regions of the world? We identify the dominant AI topics, visible production regions, and limited intercontinental collaboration
