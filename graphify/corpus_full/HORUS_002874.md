# Entrepreneurship: Model for valorizing and regenerating businesses before or after a crisis

The business reorganiser model, presented in this book, aims to avoid the closure and liquidation of companies, designed as a working tool focused on 12 variables to facilitate to entrepreneurs, entrepreneurs, investors, consultants, academics and other agents the process of evaluating the administrative actions to be carried out, the possibility of solving the difficulties of each company that is in operation or those that are closed and will be intervened in order to strengthen or refloat them, after verification of their ability to generate wealth, after applying the necessary changes, including a new strategic approach, refinancings, variation of products and productive processes, refocusing of markets and customers, in function of preserving the companies
