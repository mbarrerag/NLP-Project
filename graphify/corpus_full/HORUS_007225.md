# Assessment of factors influencing the acceptance of solar photovoltaic technology in manufacturing organizations in Bogotá

At the global level, the use of renewable energies has grown significantly as part of the strategies to mitigate pollution that has intensified in recent years. The Paris Agreement, adopted by almost all countries of the world, established the carbon neutrality target for 2050, making it essential for all sectors to accelerate the transition from conventional energy sources to clean alternatives. In Colombia, the UPME (2015) promoted small-scale self-generation, highlighting the advantage of photovoltaic solar energy versus other forms of electrical generation, due to its modularity, competitive costs and ease of access. In this context, this research aims to identify the factors that influence the acceptance of photovoltaic solar technology in the organizations of the manufacturing industry in Bogotá, using the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT2) proposed by Venkatesh et al. (2012).
