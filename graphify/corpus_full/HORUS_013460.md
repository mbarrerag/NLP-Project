# The didactic suitability of mobile learning environments during the pandemic by COVID 19

This research paper aims to analyze the Thioneity of the Educational (ID) of the proposals made by the teachers of the course Mobile Environments of Learning in Reading, Writing and Orality (AMALEO), offered by the National University of Colombia - Headquarters Bogotá, in its programme of extension of the Faculty of Human Sciences, led by the Institute of Education during the pandemic by COVID 19. The teaching proposals are evaluated from the perspective of the theory of didactic suitability of Godino (2007) and its indicators of suitability as a pillar for the elaboration of an evaluative rubric of the Educational Environments focused on orality. This study is formulated with a descriptive – qualitative approach, based on the data derived from the proposals of the participating teachers. As an innovative result of this research, the design of an instrument or rubric of evaluation of the final projects of the Teaching Training course AMALE. Both the instrument of this research and the results offer a new look of great utility in the development of teaching processes in the orality, from the perspective of didactic suitability through the AMAs, due to the model of analysis integrated in the employment of the employment of different. approach based on data derived from the proposals of participating teachers. As an innovative result of this research, the design of an evaluation instrument or assessment rubric for the AMALEO final projects emerge. From the perspective of Didactical Suitability through the MLE, both the instrument and the results offer a new useful perception of future training processes in the development of orality, scholarship the model allows us to identify the different magnitudes of sustainability integrated in these environments agreeing to mentioned Didactical Suitability theory
