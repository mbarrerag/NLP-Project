# Communication: spaces and collective memories. Case study

Summary In the current sociopolitical situation in Colombia (2022), and in the context of the post-agreement agreement in coexistence with the internal armed conflict, the relationship between memory and space is studied from the Critical Studies of the Multimodal and Multimedia Discourse (ECDMM). This is formulated from its communicative and political nature, taking as a starting point that memory is a social construction, anchored temporarily in the daily life of communities. The aim of this article is to formulate a reflection on the sociodiscursive and political-cultural construction of memory, to address the temporality space in a place of memory in Bogotá, based on its communicative, symbolic and political dimension. At the first point, the relationship between memory and socio-communicational practices is addressed with the processes of construction of meaning. Secondly, the relationship of memory-space-place is explored and how this function as anchor for the memory of the Colombian armed conflict, whose temporality exceeds six decades. In this process of design, production and socialization, the symbolic-discursive dimension is installed
