# Multidimensional characterization and potential uses of carrot production surpluses (Daucus carota) in the eastern department of Antioquia

Carrot is a species belonging to the Apiaceae family (Umbelliferae) whose root is the main source of provitamin A (carotene) in the human diet and its production has increased during the last decades. Moreover, it is a good source of carotenoids, vitamins, dietary fiber, minerals and antioxidants. China is the largest producer of carrots in the world, with an annual production that exceeds 11 million tons, followed by the United States, Russia and Uzbekistan with a production that exceeds million tons. In Colombia, the total area sown with carrot characteristics in 2020 corresponded to 8820 hectares for a total production of 271.001 tons, with Cundinamarca the department with the highest production (38.73%), followed by Boyacá (29.84%) and Antioquia (25.84%). However, the total production of carrot is not marketed, category with the greatest degree of breakdown, with no defects, with the most acceptable form of total production in the United Kingdom. For this reason, this project aims to characterize multidimensionally the carrot surpluses produced in the eastern part of the Department of Antioquia, to construct a bioprospecting of the residual mass through technological surveillance to finally establish possible uses or vocations for the food industry and the pharmaceutical cosmetics industry for the generation of value-added products
