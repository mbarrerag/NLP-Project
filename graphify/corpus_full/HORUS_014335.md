# SIMULTANEOUS AND NON-SIMULTANEOUS BLOW-UP FOR A NON-LOCAL DIFFUSION SYSTEM

In this work, the non-local diffusion system with Neumann boundary conditions (Formula Presented) is studied, where (x, t) ∈ × (0, T), is a bounded, connected and smooth domain, f and g are continuous functions and (u0, v0) ∈ C(Ω)×C(Ω), nonnegative and real functions. Existence and uniqueness of solutions is proved. For some particular functions f, g, the simultaneous and non-simultaneous blow-up for solutions is analyzed. Finally, the blow-up rate for the solution is given
