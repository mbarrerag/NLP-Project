# DIFFUSE GLYNOMS IN ELIMINATING AREAS: DIAGNOSTICAL AND THERAPEUTICAL ADVANCES

The objective is to present the diagnostic, molecular and radiological advances, as well as the therapeutic strategies for diffuse gliomas in the last 5 years (2018-2023) at the Fundación Universitaria de Ciencias de la Salud (FUCS), Bogotá D.C., Colombia. Materials and methods are described the diagnostic and therapeutic techniques used for diffuse gliomas with illustrative cases. Results show the advances of diagnostic and therapeutic tools for the management of diffuse gliomas. Discussion in the last 5 years has advanced in the classification, diagnosis and treatment of diffuse gliomas, thanks to technological advances such as molecular markers, tractography and fusion of images for neuronavigation and cortical stimulation techniques. This has allowed the treatment of patients with such tumors to improve the morbidity rate, the quality of life free of disease and overall survival. Conclusions of diagnostic techniques such as tractography, integral fusion of intraoperative images and electrophysiological brain mapping with cortical and subcortical stimulation have improved the diagnosis and treatment of gliomas
