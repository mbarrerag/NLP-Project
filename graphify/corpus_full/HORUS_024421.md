# SPACE AND TEMPORARY VARIABILITY OF THE SOIL OF ERA5-LAND IN COLOMBIA

The ERA5-Land reanalysis has a spatial resolution of approximately 9 km and time of 1 h from 1 January 1981 to the present. ERA5-Land has soil humidity in four layers of this, between 0 cm and 7 cm, 7 cm and 28 cm, 28 cm and 100 cm and 100 cm and 289 cm. In this work we characterize the spatial and temporal variability of soil moisture expressed in volumetric content of water in these four layers in the Colombian territory. The data are characterized by its variability range, being lower in the Pacific region and Amazonia and higher in the region Orinoquia and Caribbean (north), with values close to 0.05 m3/m3 and 0.25 m3/m3 respectively. The distribution of the average values is similar in the four layers, being the maximum values in the Pacific and Northern Amazon regions and the minimum values in the Caribbean region and the Andean region (the most important part of the Magdalena river), with values of 0.5 m3/m3 and 0.3 m3/m3 respectively.
