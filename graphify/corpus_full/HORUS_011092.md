# Street transformations from the active edge: case 69th Street Ibagué, Colombia

In short, the street is a place to live the city. However, in recent years, the urban morphology has been changing and this has caused the street to lose its function. Currently, it has become a residual space in which the presence of grills or blind walls is observed that impede the interaction between the public and private environment being this crucial relationship for vitality in the street and the city. Consequently, the objective of this work is the development of urban design actions that have as result reactivating the existing streets. This initiative is carried out with the intention of generating benefits both for the city and for the owners. Through the improvement of the functioning of the street and its relation to the building typology. To this end, the research has used the deductive method. In first place, the formal causes that contribute to the inactivity of the streets, using theoretical postulates. Then, these formal conditions are analyzed through three variables in case of study, with the aim of identifying the characteristics of the street and thus to be able to design strategies and actions that allow them to be transformed.
