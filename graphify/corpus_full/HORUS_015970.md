# SPACE IN JOSEL LUIS PARDO AND REDEFINATION OF THE OBJECT OF THE ESTATE

In this article we develop the notion of space in the aesthetic proposal of José Luis Pardo. For this we base ourselves on two of his main texts in which he proposes this concept: On spaces. Painting, writing, thinking and The shapes of exteriority. There we find that Pardo proposes a redefinition of the object of aesthetics from the beautiful to the sensitive, wondering how we become sensitive by means of habit. His proposal is a subjective or presumptive, that is, tends to pay attention to the world rather than to the subject, since the same world is individualized, possesses forms of expression (aesthetic). The main thesis we propose is that space is a fundamental notion to think of a redefinition of the object of aesthetics. It is no longer studied the beautiful but the sensitive, and it is part of a non-anthropological perspective to explain how individuals arise from sensitivity and habit, and later they form their own territory by making it habitable
