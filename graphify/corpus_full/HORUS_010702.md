# Reflections on equity in intersecting higher education: Approaching the life experiences of indigenous and Afro-descendant students in special admission programmes at the National University of Colombia (Sede Bogotá)

This thesis explores the meanings of equity in higher education based on the institutional context of UNAL and the experiences of life of indigenous and Afro-descendent students who have entered through special admission programs at Headquarters Bogotá. It has a qualitative approach supported by quantitative data, from which interviews in the form of cordial conversations and participating and non-participating observation are constituted in main methodological strategies. The research is articulated from three (3) axes of analysis that are: identity, life projects and university experience; conceptions and experiences of success and failure, and spaces of participation, voice and intercultural recognition. From these, the relationships and tensions between social identities and forms of institutional oppression, stereotyped, interpersonal and experiential. It finds that equity in higher education refers to more complex senses than institutional discourse and a good part of the theory they pose.
