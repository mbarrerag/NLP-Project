# Sudden Stops and asset purchase programs in an emerging economy panel

This document examines the effect of the acquisitions of assets by central banks of a panel of emerging economies on the likelihood of the occurrence of capital shortages. Using a panel covering data on foreign investment flows for 22 emerging economies from 2004 to 2021, it is found that an increase of one billion dollars in the holdings of public debt securities in local currency by the central bank decreases the probability of the occurrence of minor capital failures by 1.36%. Furthermore, that the central bank has carried out extraordinary asset acquisition programmes during the pandemic reduces the probability of the occurrence of this type of sudden unemployment by 43,51%.
