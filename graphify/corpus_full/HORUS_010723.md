# THE ROLE OF REHABILITATION IN NEUROMUSCULAR DISEASES

Neuromuscular diseases affect functionality and quality of life; It is here where the role of rehabilitation becomes important in the best support care for this population, however, in Latin America only a small proportion access comprehensive rehabilitation programs. Additionally, objective functional assessment has been one of the most standardized measures used worldwide to monitor patients with these pathologies and define medical treatments. This module will present an update on functional assessments and rehabilitation strategies in different population groups with neuromuscular diseases, which can create a challenge for professionals related to rehabilitation both in the clinical and research fields. The proposal of this module arises from the priority need for the knowledge of all professionals related to rehabilitation in relation to the evaluation and management measures of these patients, who suffer from these chronic and disabling pathologies
