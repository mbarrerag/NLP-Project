# Factors influencing the level of adherence to treatment for diabetes in migrants living in the United States

Diabetes is a non-transmissible disease that causes 1.6 million deaths annually, the complications resulting from it are highly related to the level of adherence to treatment. It is estimated that 14% of the American population is immigrant, representing 44.9 million people in 2019. According to studies, the levels of adherence to treatment of diabetes are lower worldwide in migrant populations. Given the above, it is key to identify the factors that affect adherence to treatment of diabetes in Latino migrants living in the United States of America, in order to recognize that actions can be implemented to manage the disease, prevent complications and contribute to the quality of life
