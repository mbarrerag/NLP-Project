# THE ETHIC OF MARKETING IN COLOMBIA FROM THE LOOK OF SOME EXPERTS OF ACADEMIA

This work aims to identify the ethics implicit in marketing in Colombia based on the vision of expert professors from the best universities in the country. The methodological design was the hermeneutic analysis of the information collected in semi-directed interviews. The results identify that ethics taught in Colombian undergraduates: has a hegemonic moral stance based on utilitarian ethics; is important for the professional of marketing; becomes religious morality; is taught through codes of ethics and tends to be cross-cutting in curricula. Several universities incorporate, transversally, ethical issues in their curricula, while others eliminate or leave to the student's choice the learning of this component. The ethics approach taught in pre-graduate marketing programs in Colombia should be reviewed, to avoid phenomena of the national context that are considered inadequate, such as unfair competition or misleading advertising
