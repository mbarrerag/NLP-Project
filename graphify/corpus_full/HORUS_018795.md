# THE ASHALTIC CONCRETE VISED UNDER THE MICROSCOPY

The durability and performance of concrete asphaltic pavement rolling layers depend on multiple factors. Although conventional quality control evaluates mechanical response (portant capacity and deformability), there are characteristics that are not properly studied (chemical and mineralogical composition of the stone aggregates; internal structure of asphaltic concrete). To complement the evaluation of concrete asphalt used for road paving, unconventional techniques such as petrographic, mineralogical and chemical engineering are implemented in civil engineering. This book sets out the most relevant aspects related to sample processing, optical and electronic microscopy analysis, spectroscopy and digital imaging analysis. This methodological guide to characterize mineralologically the petreous aggregates and the internal structure of asphaltic concretes, by identifying micro and macrostructural traits that can affect their performance, addresses the potential of applying microscopy and spectroscopy techniques to evaluate and control the quality of asphaltic concretes, complementing routine production and construction practices, and even the life of the pavement
