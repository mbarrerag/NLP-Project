# MANUFACTURING OF AN ELECTRIC VEHICLE FOR PARTICIPATION IN THE NATIONAL CONCURSE OF VEHICLES TO ELECTRIC TRACTION CNVTE

This project proposes the design and manufacture of improvement elements for the electric vehicle that will participate in the National Competition for Electric Traction Vehicles (CNVTE) in which the student group has participated since 2019. In this case, using the vehicle manufactured for the edition of 2023 but presenting significant improvements in telemetry systems and reliability of the mechanisms. In this project, the integration of a group of 21 members of different careers of the National University is carried out allowing a design that integrates interdisciplinary visions of engineering
