# Effect of water deficit on photosynthesis and yield of peas (Pisum sativum L.): a systematic review

The water deficit caused by droughts is common and extreme due to climate change. The crops of arveja (Pisum sativum L.) suffer water deficit, depending on the planting season and the location. Some mechanisms of these plants in response to drought are the closing of stomas and the reduction of photosynthetic capacity. However, as a consequence, reproductive processes such as flowering and filling of pods are altered, which decreases the yield of plants. Although these mechanisms have been studied, there is no systematic review of the effect of the water deficit on photosynthesis and yield in P. sativum. This review was aimed at combining, summarizing and interpreting the current knowledge about the effects of the water deficit on photosynthesis and the yield in P. sativum. A systematic review was made using the database Scopus, Web of Science and ScienceDirect. The water deficit decreases the assimilation of CO2 by 25%.
