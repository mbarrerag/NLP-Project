# PROPOSAL FOR A RUBRIC FOR THE (AUTUM) PROJECT ASSESSMENT AND MASTER AND DOCTOR TESTING. FCE-CID DOCUMENTS SCHOOL OF ADMINISTRATION AND PUBLIC ACCOUNT No. 42, MAY 2022, NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA SEDE BOG

The aim of this document is to present a first approach to a rubric that provides elements for the evaluation of projects and thesis of master's and doctoral, mainly for the area of administration. Items are proposed for key sections in documents such as the master's thesis project, doctoral thesis project, master's thesis and doctoral thesis
