# ARTEFICTS AND LANGUAGE: A LOOK FROM THE COGNITATIVE SEMIOTICS TO UNIVERSAL PEOPLE

NUMBER 35 The artifacts and language: a look from cognitive semiotics to universals Juan Carlos Mendoza-Collos Abstract The study of the relationship between material culture and language has a long tradition, from advances in the field of the evolution of culture to the findings of cognitive sciences. The recent conclusions of these studies are inclined by linguistic diversity and evolutionary linguistics, ignoring the role of universals. This article explores the role of artifacts in the constitution of universal patterns, proposing that within the evident cultural diversity of language and is it still valid to consider universals without contradicting the new paradigms of cognitive semiotics. This idea is supported by the the thesis of a relational ontology between artifacts and language
