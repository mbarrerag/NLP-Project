# TERMS AND PROCESSING BY INJECTION OF PLASTIC PIECE IN THEIR MECHANICAL DEEMPENANCE

The degree of quality of parts manufactured by thermoplastic injection can be established by the weight of the product, appearance and minimal defects. Additionally, the conditions of the injection process can induce an effect on the mechanical performance of the injected parts, where the residual forces of the injected part generated in the processing can promote the formation of premature cracks or faults of the product during its use by applying an external load or force. The purpose of this work is to evaluate the effect that in-jection processing generates on an injected part in the mechanical performance of the latter. To this end, test pieces were injected under different process conditions and subsequently subjected to mechanical bending tests, some parts were carried out under a thermal reciding treatment ¿annealing¿, in order to obtain a molecular relaxation that allows to reduce their internal or residual forces generated during the injection process. Additionally, a qualitative evaluation of the distribution of residual forces in the injected parts using the technique of photoelasticity could be made to analyze and validate the results of the mechanical tests.
