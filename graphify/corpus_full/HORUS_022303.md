# Knowing the microscopic diversity and its utilities: educational brochure for the dissemination and recognition of microscopic beings

Microorganisms are generally recognized in a negative way because they generate serious diseases or infections in humans and other organisms, this negative conception was strengthened after the world went through a pandemic caused precisely by a microscopic organism, the SARS COV-2 virus, which affected the lives of people to such an extent that their way of living and relating to each other changed drastically. It is for this reason that we decided to contribute to the recognition of its applications through the booklet "Héroes en miniatura" aimed at the general public, which shows the applications of microorganisms in three different fields, medicine, the environment and the food industry, each of the chapters contains three different applications in which you can see how some microorganisms are used. For this purpose, it worked with a population of 20 students from Colegio La Colina, to whom we implemented a workshop and a survey to recognize their perceptions about microorganisms. The booklet "Héroes en miniatura" was socialized and finally the perceptions survey and the workshop were implemented again to identify the differences between the perceptions before and after the implementation of the booklet. The results showed significant transformations in the perceptions of the students who had a good reception of the information, the change in perceptions was positive, as it is evident that some students have clear concepts about microorganisms due to their social context, but there are still significant changes in terms of perceptions of microorganisms, going from negative to positive
