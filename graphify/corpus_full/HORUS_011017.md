# The role of orchestral leadership in the dissemination and visibility of Colombian Composers

Historically, academic musical formation has been very marked by the male presence in each of the study areas: interpretation, composition and musical direction, being an impediment for those women who wanted music to be their professional career. The following research aims to recognize that from the direction of orchestra it is possible to disseminate and make visible the works of Colombian composers. Also, identify all those initiatives of groups, concert spaces and directors (es) that highlight the work of Colombian composers in the country. This research is documentary in type and is supported under the qualitative approach in turn the research focused on the explanatory level. .
