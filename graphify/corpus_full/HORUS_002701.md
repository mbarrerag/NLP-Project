# DIGITAL REALITIES IN THE DIDACTIC OF ARCHITECTONIC REPRESENTATION

In recent years, digital realities have gained greater relevance because of their potential to generate immersive and interactive learning experiences. In architectural representation, these technologies allow the creation of virtual constructions on a real scale, superimposing information of objects in the physical world and improving the understanding of three-dimensional spatiality. This work shows the process of technological and conceptual appropriation of architecture students who used virtual reality tools, augmented and rendered 360° in the digital and 3D and 4D imaging courses, supported by the Image and Modeling and Aula Stem Laboratory of the National University of Colombia - Manizales Headquarters, during the year 2023. Students' exercises were analyzed, considering conceptualization, modelling, rendering and interaction. A co-occurrence of learning and evaluation criteria was applied with the techniques applied as didactic strategy, finding that the use of digital tools prevailed in modeling, rendering and interaction, while for the conceptualization of analog tools was used more than in complement to digital representation techniques, stimulating creativity, the use of contemporary art concepts and communication.
