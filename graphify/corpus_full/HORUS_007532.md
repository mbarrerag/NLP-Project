# Digital administrative management tool for cement chemistry laboratory

The cement chemistry laboratory seeks to consolidate itself as the main laboratory for research into building materials by 2032, but faces limitations in the day-to-day administrative management, limiting its effectiveness and scope. Therefore, the overall objective of the project is to digitize the administration and communication of the laboratory with the auxiliary students and users, by improving the pricing structure of services on the website, updating and disseminating the administration website, and evaluating and maintaining the laboratory equipment by being all centralized through the website administration tool. Thus, the laboratory internal and daily administration website will allow detailed control of the equipment, inventories, guides and services, optimizing decision-making and reporting generation. This potentially would benefit in the first instance students of the CEMATCO group, students of the courses being conducted there, students of the laboratory building the architectural faculty with whom the synergy of work would be expanded and in general all laboratories attending the workshops implementing the tool that the project intends to deliver
