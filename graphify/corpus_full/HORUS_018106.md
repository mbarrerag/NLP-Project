# SCIENCE-BUILDING, TECHNOLOGY-INNOVATION IN YOUTH TO THE LEVEL OF PREGRAPHS.

One of the functions of governments is to identify and implement policies, strategies and programmes to promote social and economic development.For several decades, countries have been making efforts to promote an early relationship between young people and research and innovation, through the articulation of efforts between universities, the State, businesses and communities, with the aim of advancing the knowledge society.In the case of Latin American countries, experiences have been varied but with low effectiveness. To have a framework of reference, this document initially presents a review of the experiences of some countries that have made greater progress in early and careful training of young people in research, technology and innovation.The following part sets out some elements of the policies followed in Latin America since the 1960s, despite which CT&I policies continue to show low scope in terms of capabilities in the region in this type of activity, as can be seen from the results of the Global Innovation Index (IGI)
