# Palliative care in Colombia: home care, access barriers and progress in the implementation of these programs during the pandemic by COVID-19

Palliative care seeks to alleviate the suffering of patients with chronic, degenerative and terminal diseases in an integral way, and thus improve their quality of life by including physical, psychosocial and spiritual aspects in care. In Colombia, the provision of palliative care services is regulated by Law 1733 of 2014; however, access to these services is limited, since the care centres where these services are provided are concentrated in departmental capitals; moreover, the general population is unaware of the existence of this type of care. Fear of contagion during the pandemic by COVID-19 has created additional barriers that make access to palliative care even more difficult; for example, access to health care staff responsible for these services has been restricted to the homes of patients and it has been evidenced that people avoid attending their consultations to health centres.
