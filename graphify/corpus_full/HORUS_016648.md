# The body that poses and the body that passes

The figure of oblivion present in a photográfia image re-activated my way of looking at and understanding cinema, which looked at and understood life, my life and my ways of operating in the field of art and affections. I asked a fundamental question for memory and traces of memory. How does the cinematographic apartment collect memory, but above all, how does the political, social and personal apparatus register memory? I wanted to destabilize my way of operating in the world and in art. I understood then that finding a device, my own device, was to find a strategy to work from desire. A kinematic device, but went beyond that, a device to activate my body anesthetized by political, social and affective practices, and by others; a device to reinvent the world, to reinvent my memories, to reinvent myself and to make this device possible through my owning and making a gesture. a device to activate my body anesthetized by political, social and affective practices of my own and others; a device to reinvent the world, to reinvent my memories, to reinvent myself. And operating this device involved a management. Like reinventing myself, fictionalizing myself from discretion detailed a management. The management that said, that silent, that unravels. The management as a way of telling and inhabiting the world, or possible worlds. The management as a space of freedom. So, through that management, make films, make memories, make fiction
