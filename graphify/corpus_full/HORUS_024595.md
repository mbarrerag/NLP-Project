# DISABILITY AND ARMED CONFLICT: APPROXIMATIONS FROM UNIVERSITY. NATIONAL COLOMBIA

A general overview is drawn from research carried out at the National University of Colombia on the production and aggravation of disability due to conflict, the state, institutional, professional and community social responses that have been deployed to address the consequences of war, and how in this framework persons with disabilities have sometimes been invisible, excluded, victimized or revictimized. Looking back has also been useful in recognizing in the interests of the university community legitimate concerns to understand and contribute to healing the wounds that as a country and as a society this long war has left us. In these bets, the professions, knowledge, education and the University take meaning
