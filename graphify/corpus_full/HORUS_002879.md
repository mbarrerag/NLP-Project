# RECITAL RAPPORTEUR: GENERAL PANORAMA, TRENDS AND MUSIC DEVELOPMENT FOR VIOLIN ONLY WRITED IN COLOMBIA DURING THE 21st CENTURY

The knowledge of the repertoires for string instruments made in Colombia in the context of academic music is scarce. Although some have been recognized and inventoryed, particularly the works composed during the first half of the twentieth century, there is still a lot of work of analysis and critical review to be done around them. If the spectrum is limited to music for violin, we will find that the panorama is even more arid. This situation has favored that in the training processes that develop at a professional level and in the artistic proposals that have as protagonists the instruments of string rubbed, particularly the violin, the music of the Colombian composers is not taken into account as didactic tool and continues to emulate the ways and tools that the European conservatories use. Likewise, it is recognized that there is an obvious and discouraging disconnection with the music and aesthetic experiences that in our region and punctually in Colombia are produced. In addition to the above, it has been recognized that in these processes of formation there is a lack of exploration of contemporary repertoires which generates important gaps in the formation of the current violinists.
