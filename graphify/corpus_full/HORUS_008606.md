# THE SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURA ON SEQUENTIAL ORDERS IN FACTORIAL EXPERIMENTS

This paper describes the results of a systematic literature review (SLR) on the generation of sequential experimentation orders for factorial designs, in which both the number of level changes and the bias associated with external factors are considered. It presents the findings, proposed algorithms and future research opportunities, as well as a taxonomic division regarding the aspects considered for the construction of sequential orders. It was found that the generalization of the properties described in the literature for factorial designs with any number of factors and levels is pending
