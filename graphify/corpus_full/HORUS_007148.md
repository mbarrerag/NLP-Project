# Discreet Event Simulation Model for Calculating CO2 Emissions in a Textile Company's Last Mile Logistics in Colombia for Different Distribution and Consolidation Policies

In this research, a simulation model is developed with a discrete event approach for the last mile supply chain of a textile company in Colombia where carbon dioxide equivalent emissions are simulated under different consolidation and distribution scenarios, in order to improve decision-making regarding the environmental performance of that supply chain evaluating the performance of delivery times. Since the geography of Colombia varies significantly in each region, the added value of this research lies in the incorporation of this heterogeneity into the model. We classify each section of the routes according to their geography (mountain, plain or undulating) to calculate fuel consumption according to the weight of the load and estimate the CO2 emissions of each truck, depending on the topography of the field.
