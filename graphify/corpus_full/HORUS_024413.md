# URBAN COLOMBIAN SYSTEM. DEVELOPMENTS AND RECENT CHANGES

The present study aims to contribute to the renewed discussion around the Colombian urban system. In this sense, based on demographic, economic and political variables, the work analyzes the evolution of the urban hierarchy and the different restructurings that the Colombian urban system has had, emphasizing the current situation, by developing a proposal for the classification of cities, both in terms of their size and their interactions at regional, national and global levels. The analysis carried out shows an urban system characterized by: the incidence of economic diversification in its configuration; the primacy of Bogotá; the national relevance and the global projection of Medellín, Cali and Barranquilla through differentiated processes; the increasing role of the medium cities and the dynamics of metropolitanization and urban agglomeration
