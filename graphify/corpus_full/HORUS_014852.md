# CULTURE AND ORGANIZATIONAL CLIMATE IN SOME EDUCATIONAL ENTITIES

Organizational culture is an important element for companies interested in understanding the interdependence and relationship of various elements associated with the environmental and external variables that make up it. In speaking of organizational culture, a series of meanings referring to variables of leadership, organizational climate, psychological contract, among others (Van Muijen et al., 1999). The study on the constructs of climate and organizational culture are diverse (Van Muijen et al.,1999; Schein, 1984; Lee, 2011). However, there are few researches that aim to describe this relationship within the model of values and competences of Cameron and Quinn (2011), in this model of values and competence it is proposed to evaluate the values specific to the organization in order to prepare them for the development of a process of change, in such a way that it is possible to diagnose the way in which the people think, act and process the information in the organization in relation to the values and fundamental deductions that allow the development of common values within the organization. The model considers two dimensions that cross between the four different climates with their respective organizational ideals of culture or climate
