# WELCOME TO THE SABBATH: CHRONIC, REVIEWS AND RELATIONS IN THE TORNEY OF THE UNDERGROUND 2001 2021

The book 'Welcome to the Sabbath' offers reflections on the appreciation of metal music and the validity of the underground that contribute to the construction of a theory of metal. In addition, it compiles unpublished essays along with an anthology of interviews, chronicles and reviews on rock & metal made by the author during the last 20 years
