# Incorporation of pseudostatic analysis into the HsQI system for geotechnical classification of slopes in road corridors.

The geotechnical classification system based on the HsQI quality index (Hillslope Quality Index) is a tool for estimating the degree of susceptibility to sliding of the slopes of road projects in a simple and practical way, since it allows the zoning of the road corridor considering its geometry, geomorphology, hydrogeology and mechanical resistance parameters of the soils of the study area. Thus, it leads to know the sectors that will present stability problems during the different phases of the project, favoring the prediction of the areas where greater ingenierial efforts must be combined, both design and construction, incurring in a decrease of the overcosts generated in this type of projects by not adequately foreseeing such stability problems.
