# Technical-economic analysis of 5G technology deployment scenarios in Colombia

This final master's degree work focused on an integrated analysis of the possible scenarios for the implementation of mobile technology 5G in Colombia, considering and integrating technical and economic aspects in a cohesive manner in an analytical framework. Through a review of the global literature, various technical-economic models related to mobile network deployments were identified prioritizing public access models in developed countries and used by mobile operators and regulatory entities worldwide in the mobile telecommunications sector. An analysis and/or reverse engineering of the chosen model was carried out in order to understand its functioning, for this purpose, initial validation and calibration tests (tests to the entry model) were carried out to corroborate and validate the understanding to the full of the original model and how to use it to apply it in the context of the country Colombia. That said, a detailed parameterization of the technical-economic model was carried out by each defined scenario, considering, analysis of the mobile traffic model, demographic or population variables of the country together with economic indicators of inflation, costs, net value and projections, frequency bands and bandwidth defined together with the other associated conditions of allocation by the Ministry of Technology and Communications (NC). use by mobile operators and regulatory bodies worldwide in the mobile telecommunications sector.
