# Implementation protocol for total skin irradiation with electrons from Stanford technique at the National Cancer Institute.

In this work a protocol was established for the implementation of total body irradiation with electrons, for which the qualification of the special mode of high dose rate in a VARIAN IX accelerator was required, meeting the requirements established by VARIAN, which require a symmetry of no more than 2 % for the inplane and crossplane of the dose profiles under reference conditions (field 36 x 36, SSD 100 cm, colimator at 0° and dose rate of 888 UM/min).
