# OPTISMATION OF MICRORRED MULTIPLES IN DISTRIBUTION SYSTEMS

This chapter examines the fundamental concepts of optimization in distribution systems with multiple microgrids coupled to the system. A review of the state of art is made and different perspectives on the subject are presented; optimization techniques addressing uncertainty are included, specifically robust distributional optimization (DRO), by means of the reformulation based on Wasserstein metrics
