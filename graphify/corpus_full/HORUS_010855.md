# DE MANIZALS (COLOMBIA)

The architecture of the nineteenth century historical eclecticism acquired in Colombia the name of "Architecture Republicana", obeying the historical moment that the country lived at the beginning of the twentieth century. As in the whole world, in Colombia this style was used to design institutional buildings and even housing in many cities of the country. In Manizales, an area enriched by the cultivation, profit and trade of coffee at the end of the nineteenth century and beginning of the twentieth century, the republican architecture was the preferred style of the economically more wealthy classes, of the government and of the merchants to make their mansions, especially in the reconstruction of the city in the 20 years after two great fires ended with about 300 real estate. Therefore the city has welcomed in its historical center the widest repertoire of this architecture made by architects from all parts of the world. From the initial buildings today survive 142, which must be preserved and protected as an important part of the heritage architecture of the city. The facades and its ornamentation are exposed to affectations and whose maintenance becomes a frequent task for the owner.
