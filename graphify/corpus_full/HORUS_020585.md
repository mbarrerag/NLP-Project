# Jewels for Knights and Dames: The Uses of Jewelry in the 16th Century Literary Chivalry

Cultural history has shown that, during the times of the Old Regimen in Spain, the courtly elites gave the jewels specific uses: in the public domain they were used to manifest the virtue of magnificence and social distinction, while in the private sphere they served for the expression of feelings and were linked with the sexuality; in the same way, it will be used as talismans and as court gifts. This work analyzes how the Spanish Romances of Chivalry of the 16th century became contaminated by these social and cultural practices
