# DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION IN THE TEACHING AND LEARNING PROCESS TOWARDS A SUBSTANTIVE UNIVERSITY

The topic of sustainability in higher education has assumed great importance in the world. Currently, in the Colombian context there is a structural problem of inequality that does not allow progress in a sustainable development of the country; problem that covers economic, social and cultural aspects, and extends to education at all levels.The country has serious deficiencies in terms of quality education from the first years of schooling; hence it is necessary to begin to inquire in depth about what should be done to improve and where this problem is most frequently evidenced.It is a challenge to strengthen itself in line with the Sustainable Development Goals (ods), specifically the ods 4 (education), addressing the needs of education for sustainability, defining the teaching-learning models that allow strengthening the institutions of higher education in-cludes, equitable and of quality, and promoting opportunities for sustainable and permanent learning for all.It is vital for the future generations that are beginning their learning process and those already in it must develop the capacity to make informed decisions and that contribute positively to higher education and sustainability: moving towards higher education for the sustainability of the emerging needs. ) This reflection is based on information from the national and international context of higher education during the covid-19 pandemic, as well as on the collection of lessons and experiences in teaching at the National University of Colombia during the period 2020-1. In this way, we will present some of the effects on sustainability in higher education in Colombia, considering aspects related to the environmental, social and pedagogical dimension of the use of digital technologies
