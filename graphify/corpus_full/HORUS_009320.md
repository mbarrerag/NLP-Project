# Revolution devours its children. Popular anticapitalist and communist experiences in New Granada (1848-1854)

This paper aims to analyze the conditions of possibility that allowed the emergence of the popular political subjects in the mid-19th century in the Republic of New Granada. To this end, it uses as a conceptual framework the category of experience proposed by Foucault, which contemplates the correlation between fields of knowledge, types of normivity and forms of subjectivity and, as a methodological framework, an analytical perspective that fits into a network of global processes of cocreation.
