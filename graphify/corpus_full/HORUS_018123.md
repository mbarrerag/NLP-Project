# Improvisation exercises for different orchestral formats through film music

This paper presents a guide to practical exercises for String Orchestra and Symphony Orchestra to address the concept of musical improvisation using as a starting point orchestral repertoire of film music, in this particular case, working on two works by the Japanese composer Joe Hisaishi, considered one of the greatest composers of orchestral music for audiovisual productions, known worldwide for his work in the films of Studio Ghibli. . This document proposes a practical exercise guide for String Orchestra and Symphonic Orchestra to address the concept of musical improvisation using as a starting point an orchestral replicaire of film scorting, in this particular case, working on two works by Japanese composer Joe Hisaishi., consider one of the great current competitors of orchestra music for audiovisual productions, known worldwide for his work on Studio Ghibli films
