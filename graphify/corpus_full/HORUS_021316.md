# sustainable processing of avocado hass and Spirulina in extracting and bioplastic under the circular bioeconomy approach

The traditional production model based on the depletion of natural resources is currently being reviewed by options in which such use is sustainable. Colombia is no stranger to environmental problems, which is why various sectors such as FAO, UN, ECLAC, DNP (National Department of Planning), the mission of sages Colombia, among others, recommend taking actions aimed at the implementation of strategies such as Bioeconomy, Circular Economy and Biorefineries. These propose economic development based on sustainable use of natural resources, in which residues or by-products are transformed and return to the productive cycle again minimizing the generation of residues, this conceptualization as a whole is grouped under the name of circular Bioecomía. Therefore, this project aims to develop sustainable processes for obtaining rich extracts with phycocycanine (blue pigment) and lipophilic extract rich in carotenoids from Spirulina and antioxidant and bioplastic extracts containing these from Hass avocado residues
