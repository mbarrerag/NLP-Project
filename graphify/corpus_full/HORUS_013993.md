# Assessment and conservation of brick architecture built in Bogotá in the early 20th century

Brick has played an important role in the construction development of the city of Bogotá. During the first half of the 20th century, it was used as an expressive resource in countless buildings that are still preserved and have a great heritage value. Currently, brick is a fundamental part of the architectural landscape of the city and has contributed to consolidating the identity of various sectors of Bogotá. Despite this, its knowledge is scarce from the ornamental point of view, being this, an important field of research that has not been addressed so far in depth. This research will be focused on the study of ornamental brick pieces used in the facades of architecture of Bogotá. These pieces are unique elements of great architectural and heritage value. Their study, as well as the architecture that employs them, will contribute to the evaluation of techniques and materials typical of our constructive tradition, will leave as a result an important basis for future research on the subject, and will promote knowledge, valuation and conservation of brick architecture in Bogotá's view
