# Effect of avocado flower on functional properties in start films from Plantain (Mussa var. AAB Simonds)

This study investigates the physical-chemical interactions of the avocado flour Hass and banana starch Dominico Hartón to develop biodegradable films with improved mechanical resistance and reduced water sensitivity for the food packaging industry. The research evaluates the impact of the chemical composition (almids, lipids and fibers) of avocado seed flour on the mechanical and hydrophobic properties of the films.
