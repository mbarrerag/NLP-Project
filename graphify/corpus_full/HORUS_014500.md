# Diagnosis and business action plan: disruptive model of diagnosis and company planning to keep it generating

This book facilitates the user to identify and evaluate the situation of a company or organization by providing the key variables essential to determine their status, in order to do so, with complete information, plan their direction and put it to generate wealth according to the resources invested in it. Diagnosis and business action plan is a model for users, operating individually or as a team, to establish the real situation of a private or public company, applying key variables in each front of the general and sectoral environments as well as in the functional areas, to define the appropriate plan of action in a comprehensive way, or on a specific front for the satisfaction of its objective clients and thus generate wealth. This text is an indispensable working tool for students of professional, technical and postgraduate careers such as Business Administration, Accounts, Economy, International Business, Marketing, Industrial Engineering and related areas. The work is fundamental for professionals engaged in business management such as employees, consultants and business consultants, entrepreneurs, board members, responsible for auditing, tax counter-loir and, naturally, for the entrepreneurs themselves, since they all are concerned with knowing the situation of the companies and their successful management.-- Contracadas
