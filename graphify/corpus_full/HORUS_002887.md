# MACROMICETES IN BIOMINERALIZATION MECHANISMS: EXPLORATION OF ITS USE IN BIOTECHNOLOGIC PROCESS

In recent years, possible biotechnological applications for fungi have been studied, including biomineralization. This consists of the formation of insoluble minerals through various metabolic processes, such as microbiologically induced carbonate precipitation (MICP), mechanism based on precipitation of carbonate compounds around their mycelium or in the environment, using ureolytic activity. This mechanism is applied mainly in construction, biotechnology, and medical applications. This research seeks to evaluate the possible application of macromycets fungi, for biomineralization through the MICP mechanism, determining the tolerance to the substrate, morphological changes and urease activity. Significant changes were observed in macroscopic characteristics and the release of coloured exudates, indicating the release of soluble metabolites. In ureolytic activity tests a range of 0.1 to 5.0 mM of urea hydrolyzed biomass urea were observed, in 14 days of cultivation, which is greater in relation to results obtained with bacterial strains.
