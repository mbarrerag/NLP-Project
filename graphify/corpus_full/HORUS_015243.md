# Political Trajectories of Women Activists of the Political Party Patriotic Union. Case study department Caquetá, municipality Cartagena del Chairá

I present in this text a reconstruction of the political trajectories of women leaders in the municipality Cartagena del Chairá – Caquetá, starting from its militancy in the political party Unión Patriotica. Through its life stories, I present the context of the municipality, population, and the emergence of organizational processes in which its work has been fundamental. To develop analysis, I base my life on the categories of agency, militancy, identity and political trajectories. I also reaffirm the importance of addressing the state and its relationship with the population from a local perspective, starting from the notion of differentiated presence of the state, in a territory in which in the midst of the armed conflict, the inhabitants and inhabitants have dedicated their life to the community.
