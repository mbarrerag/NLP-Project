# Portable fiber optic sensing platform for detection of mercury in water

The proposed project aims to design, model and manufacture a portable platform of optical fibre-sensing, which integrates a low cost sensor and which allows the rapid in situ detection of traces of mercury in water.This platform is presented as a proposal to contribute to the problem of measuring and monitoring the level of mercury pollution in the country's water sources.It is estimated that at least 80 rivers are contaminated with this highly toxic metal, mainly due to artisanal mining activities [1] [2].These activities are developed massively in Colombia and are carried out in areas of difficult access where it is difficult to carry robust technology for measuring the degree of mercury-contaminated pollution that presents only those rural populations [3].With the knowledge in fiber optic technology that has acquired the student during its research training stage, it seeks to activate an optical fibre sensor of tilted end-of-voltage, already studied and implemented as a sensor of refractive index [4], for the specific or selective detection of mercury ions in water, implementing an appropriate protocol of immobilization of a chemical recognition element.The optical sensor of the optical of the optical end-includible is evaluated for the detection of the potential. low cost, which can be a superluminescent LED diode, and a detector in the visible. This proposal of functional architecture of the platform will be optimized to make a compact and portable system potentially implementable in the field, and that has the possibility to reach rural populations allowing monitoring of the quality of the drinking water on which they depend
