# GENERAL PROVISIONS OF IMMEDIATE SCALE FRACTURS

Approximately 80% of deaths occur at the site of the accident (3), the remaining percentage occur in the first hours due to the same characteristics of trauma as hypovolemia and hypoxemia, so that during this period processes can be reversible that lead to the fatal outcome. In the United States it constitutes one of every 4 consultations to emergency rooms and generates a cost to its health system of up to 81 billion dollars per year (3). Of these patients, about 60% have fractures in extremities (4). In countries like Colombia, infant mortality has been decreasing six decades ago. This thanks to the progress in the prevention of infectious and contagious diseases that were previously fatal. However, contrary to this trend, deaths of children under 5 years of age involved in traffic accidents have been increasing. This trend is seen in several Latin American countries (5)
