# POLIMORFISMS ASSESSMENT OF GEN WNT10A AND MSX1 IN INDIVIDUALS WITH INCIVIVIOUS AGENESES.

The agenesia of previous teeth is a problem that has many aesthetic and functional implications and represents a great challenge for professionals when treating patients. It has been reported as a multifactorial condition with a genetic component of great importance and has a relative prevalence depending on the population. In the literature we find data of 1.4% to 11.3% of agenesias in previous teeth and until now in Colombian population, there is only one study in which a prevalence of dental agenesia of 10.6% in upper laterals and 5.5% in lower incisors. In Colombian population we have not carried out many researches related to this condition and their relation to genetic mutations. Previously, it has been reported that it is not associated with the PAX9 gene when studying 7 groups of Colombian families and the possible association of mutations in the MSX1 is reported in a study carried out in antioqueña population. However, in none of these studies do not make distinction by group of teeth, they do not define whether or not include patients without dromic and additionally, in the search for literature there are no studies in Colombian population that are associated mutations in the WNT10a study.. The differences in frequencies of each polymorphism between test subjects and controls will be considered statistically significant if P=8804;0.05. With these results, a possible association between polymorphisms of previously reported MSX1 and WNT10a genes and the presence of agenesis in incisors in Colombian population can be determined
