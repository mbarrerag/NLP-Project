# Digital transformation in higher education institutions. Implementation model

The institutions of higher education (institutions of higher education) have been permeated, on the one hand, to the technological advance that brings about the fourth industrial revolution, on the other hand, to the compliance of regulations embodied in the policies of state/nation, and solutions/services required by external situations so powerful that they have forced to face a digital transformation in all its dimensions. Although from the literature it is evident the growing interest in the area, there are still few researches that are developed in this respect in the field of higher education. It is necessary to clarify the path of the application of the approaches of the digital transformation to the domain institutions of higher education as an emerging field that has aroused interest during the recent past, developing a model of implementation that allows to describe complex relationships between dimensions of educational institutions with technological support. It is necessary to carry out studies of empirical character on the nature of the interrelations between the dimensions involved (organizational, socio-cultural and technological models) and its effect on the maturity of the transformation of the institutions of higher education. nce with regulations embolised in State/Nation policies, and solutions/services required by external situations so powerful as the Covid-19 pandemic that have strengthened Higher Education Institutions to face a digital transformation in all its dimensions. rough the Delphi Method, 9 expert judges validate its content.
