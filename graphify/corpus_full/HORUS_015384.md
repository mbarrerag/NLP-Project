# IRRITABLE INTESTINE SYNDROME (IIS): NEW CONCEPTS IN 2023 WILLIAM OTERO R.1, OTERO P.2 LINE, HERNANDO MARUALAND F.3

Abstract Irritable bowel syndrome, considered before as a functional disorder, is currently part of the group of disorders of the interaction of the bowel-brain axis, an organic disease that is clinically identified by the presence of recurrent abdominal pain and alteration in the frequency or consistency of the stool. It is a multifactorial pathology with negative impact on the quality of life and until now, incurable. The aim of the treatment is to control symptoms, which represents a high cost for health services worldwide. This article reviews epidemiology, physiopathology, treatment and some possible factors related such as alterations in gastrointestinal motility, visceral hypersensitivity, intestinal inflammation, alteration of intestinal microbiota or dysbiosis, bacterial overgrowth and immunological response to the diet
