# Production and circulation of textiles in the northeastern region of Granada. Socorro, San Gil and Girón 1780-1820

The aim of this thesis is to analyze the production and textile circulation of the main craft centres of the north-eastern region, and its link with the functioning of the internal market of the New Granada during the colonial period and the first years of republican life. Thus, it is sought to observe how the process of manufacturing the so-called canvases and blankets of the land –which predominated within the markets of the New Granada– was developed and their circulation from the Guanentá valley and the Girón governorate towards the main centres of consumption of the vice country. It is made special emphasis on understanding how the labour was organized, both for the production of cotton, the main raw material of the neo-Ganadian textiles during this period, and in the process of manufacturing the cloths and the impact these goods had on the regional finances of the country.
