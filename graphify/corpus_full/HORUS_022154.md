# Direct hospital costs and their predictors in the care of the Systemic Lupus Eritematoso, in a hospital in Bogotá.

The objective of this study was to define the direct costs of systemic erythematosus lupus (SLE) associated with hospital stay and its predictors. A cost-related study was conducted on the disease of patients with SLE diagnosis, quantifying the costs with a micro cost technique from below upwards. A total of 346 patients (456 hospital admissions) were included from August 2016 to April 2022. Females were predominated with a median age of 37 years and a median time of evolution of the disease of 24 months. 26.32% had admission to the intensive care unit and 21% had re-entry into hospital, with a total mortality of 4.6%. Patients entered by flare or lupus exacerbation (71.87%), infection (33.85 per cent), thrombotic events (10.55%), presence of chronic pathology due to disease damage ($14.25%) and adverse events (8.13%). The activity rates were high and very high in 61.86 per cent.
