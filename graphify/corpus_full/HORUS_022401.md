At present, it is recognized in the world wide that more than 80% of the people living in urban areas of the planet in which air quality is monitored are exposed to levels of pollutants that exceed the limits recommended by the World Health Organization, WHO (BBC Mundo, 2017).In this sense, the presence of trees in cities is vital since they extract pollutants in two main forms: they incorporate gases through the stomas in their leaves and capture particles on the surface of their leaves.
