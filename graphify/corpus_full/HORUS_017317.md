# CHAPTER 1 HYDRATY PHYSIOLOGY

Water is considered one of the most important nutrients for maintaining health, therefore, adequate hydration ensures the balance of total body water. Bernardot (2020) refers to the importance of understanding the fundamental role of water in the body, for example, it participates in the regulation of body temperature, digestion of food, transport and absorption of nutrients, joint lubrication, the elimination of metabolic residues and contributes to the balance of the immune system, since, through blood and lymph, water distributes leukocytes, minerals, vitamins and glucose to cells to perform their normal function of defending the organism and resistance to diseases (Boron and Boulpaep, 2016)
