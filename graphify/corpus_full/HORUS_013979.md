# INTEGRAL DEVELOPMENT STRATEGIES FROM THE DIMENSIONS OF Sustainability FOR THE ORINOCIA REGION.

The Orinoquia is the region of the country that has the greatest potential in the exploitation of natural resources, due to its geographical condition and biodiversity. Among these, the plants and the pharmacological activity of their compounds are a priority research line for the UNAL-Sede Orinoquia. This task force aims to evaluate the pharmacological activity of some plants native to the region that will be propagated and collected in natural environments in various localities of the region in different states of conservation. For this purpose, it will carry out extraction of specific biocomponents that will be evaluated both in vivo and in vitro, as well as the morphological description of the structures that house the molecules by electronic microscopy. Subsequently, the analyses will be made corresponding to energy sustainability and the prospect of pharmacological use. All the information generated will be analyzed with mathematical models applicable to the systems. It is expected that the National University through its headquarters, establish the technological infrastructure, and logistics to generate for the economic and social development of the region.
