# PERIOPERATORY DISEASE: A VISION FROM PUBLIC HEALTH

Report of a case study that emerged from the project of solidarity extension "Accompanying Persons and Collectives in Perioperative Processes", in which the need for perioperative health care for people living in rural areas of the country was analyzed and its impact on public health
