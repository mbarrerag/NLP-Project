# OVERVIEW OF THE EARLY CROSS LIGIANT

The reconstruction of the anterior cross ligament is a frequent procedure to restore the anteroposterior and rotational stability of the knee. To determine if there are differences in clinical and functional outcomes reported by patients in the artroscopic reconstruction of the anterior cross ligament with quadripital tendon autograft, compared with ischiotibial tendons. An analytical study of retrospective cohort type was performed in patients of the National University Hospital of Colombia. Univary, bivariate, multivariate and inferential data analysis was used by logistic regression. The difference between the techniques was determined through the Chi-square and t-Student tests. For the parametric distribution, tests of ANOVA and post hoc tendon were used; in the nonparametric distribution, the physical findings were used by the Mann-Whitney test. The statistical significance was not p = 0.05.
