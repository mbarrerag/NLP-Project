# Comparative analysis and application of marking schemes in advanced symphonic management techniques

This research focuses on the comparative analysis and application of marking schemes in advanced techniques of synphonic direction, which, based on the need for analytical references of marking schemes to deepen and refine the management technique. By which, the research is developed focusing on the main referents of the study of the art of management, which are: Sergiu Celibidache, Hans Swarowsky, Max Rudolf, Hideo Saito, Ilya Musin; and by completing the research, the personal applications of the techniques of Gennady Rozhdestvensky, Zubin Mehta and Andrés Orozco. By which, the research is developed from the biographical and historical background, to finally reach the comparative analysis of each management technique, and to conceive the technical criteria for the elaboration of marking schemes and application in the advanced techniques of synphonic direction, and branding technique (Amployment of the infonic direction), are a significant contribution to the professional improvement criteria of the orchestra directors. ical background to ultimately get into a comparative analysis of each conducting technique, and envisage technical criteria for the creation of management techniques and the application of management schemes in the synphonic-conducting advanced technologies, concluding that comparative analysis and management schemes application in the advanced technologies of synphonic conducting, established a mean contribution to professional perfecting criteria of orchestral conductors.Keywords: Musical analysis, Driving Technique, Symphonic Gesture Schemes
