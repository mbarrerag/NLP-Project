# POTENTIAL APPLICATION IN POLYMERIC PACKS OF PALMISTE OIL INCLUSION WITH ß-CYCLODEEXTRINE TO INHIBBRING THE PATOGENE COLLETOTRICHUM TAMARILLOI HONGO

With the objective of using fatty acids of natural origin as a strategy to confer functional properties, e.g. antifungal, to polymeric films, packagings were developed by means of the extrusion of low density polyethylene (LDPE) with lauric acid. However, taking into account the low thermal stability of the fatty acids under the conditions of extrusion, the use of complexes of ß-cyclodextrin (ß-CD) /palmist oil (AP) was implemented. Therefore, in this study the AP was encapsulated in ß-CD by two methods; kneading and co-precipitation, obtaining efficiencies of 78.3% and 75.9% respectively.
