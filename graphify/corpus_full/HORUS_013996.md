# Youth and politics in post-conflict. Youth struggles in Colombia (2016-2022)

The demonstrations that took place between 2019 and 2022, which were known as a social explosion, had as a protagonist the young people of the urban sector, located at the neighborhood level, grouped under the concept of Primera Linea, an initiative that emerged to confront police violence and to defend the demonstrators. The First Line was defined in this sense as a name under which various processes, claims and actions were grouped. This research aims to identify what kind of understanding of their own capacities these sectors have, as well as to place themselves within the framework of the global and national system and to design the organizational instruments necessary to influence the construction of Colombia of the future
