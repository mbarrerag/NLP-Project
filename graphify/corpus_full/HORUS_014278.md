# Towards a reconstruction of the uses of the concept of the popular by some Colombian critical sectors (1991-2016).

This thesis reconstructs the uses of the popular for the Colombian critical sectors in the period 1991-2016. To this end, a conceptual history is realized in which documentary pieces produced in the semantic strata of the intellectuals, the written press and popular organizations are analyzed and interpreted. Additionally, an exercise of theorization on social criticism is carried out from a hermeneutic perspective or approach; a state of art around ideas about the popular in the field of Colombian social studies; a social history from below up in which different processes of popular struggle are described, organized by different social sectors; and the conclusions of the study, which should be understood as the self-critical or critical balance of it
