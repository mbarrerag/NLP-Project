# Analysis of sustainability criteria in non-financial reports of companies in the agriculture sector worldwide

This research focuses on contrasting the information disclosed by the companies of the agricultural sector in their non-financial reports, with the sustainability criteria that should be followed by companies to make a transition to sustainability.From the global agendas on sustainability the agriculture sector is considered key by the importance of its activities for society (FAO, 2013, 2017, 2020).In the Sustainable Development Goals and the sectoral standards of the Global Reporting Initiative, goals and indicators are defined to account for the sustainability of the companies in this sector.From these references it is possible to identify sustainability criteria for the accountability that companies make in their non-financial reports (El Bilali et al., 2020; Hct345;ebi-o-269;ek et al., 2015).On the other hand, the agriculture sector is responsible for a series of social and environmental impacts that have led to propose actions for their transition towards sustainability (Berthet et al., 2018; Delonge et al., 2016; Hct345;ebró-o269;ek et al., 2015).
