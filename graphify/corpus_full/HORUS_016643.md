# NON-STATIONARY SPATIO-TEMPORAL POINT PROCESS MODELING OF COVID-19 DATA IN CALI - COLOMBIA

Most COVID-19 studies commonly report figures of the overall infection at a state- or county-level. This spatial aggregation tends to miss out on fine details of the propagation patterns of the virus. This paper is motivated by analyzing a high-resolution COVID-19 dataset in Cali, Colombia, that provides every confirmed case's exact location and time information, offering vital insights for the spatio-temporal interaction between individuals concerning the disease spread in a metropolis. We develop a non-stationary spatio-temporal point process, and introduce a neural network-based kernel to capture the spatially effect. The neural network-basedkernel is carefully crafted to enhance expressiveness while maintaining results interpretability. We also incorporate some exogenous influences imposed by city landmarks. The numerical results on real data demonstrate good predictive performances of our method compared to the state-of-the-art as well as its interpretable findings
