# IGYS ANTIBODIES PRODUCTION AS A TOOL FOR IDENTIFYING PETIDE ANTAGONISTS OF THE INOTROPIC N-METHYL-D-ASPARTATE (NMDA) RECEPTOR CONTAINING THE GLUN2B SUBUNIT. PRODUCTION OF IGYS ANTI-DURPS AS A TORAMIENT FOR IDENTIFICATION OF ANTAGONIST PEPTIDES OF THE N-METHYL-D-ASPARTATE INOTROPIC RECEPTOR

Three previously studied peptide antagonists of the NMDA-GluN2B receptor were used to generate hen antibodies. Immunoglobulins (IgYS) were extracted and precipitated from the egg egg, then purified by thiophilic adsorption chromatography. Activity and interactions were evaluated by dot-blot and ELISA assays. Key Words: Immunoglobulin Y, peptides, NMDA receptor. Antibodies against three antagonistic peptides of the NMDA-GluN2B receptor were produced in a chicken model. The extraction and precipitation of immunoglobulins from egg yolks were performed by means of thiophilic chromatography. Their specificity and activity were evaluated by ELISA and dot-blot trial. Key words: Immunoglobulin Y, peptides, NMDA receptor
