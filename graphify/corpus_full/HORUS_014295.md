# Computational study of copper chelating ligands coordinated by N, O and P with potential application in Alzheimer's disease.

The World Health Organization estimates that there are about 50 million people suffering from dementia in the world, cases of which 60% to 70% are Alzheimer’s disease (AD). Among the most important characteristics of their pathology is the aggregation of ß-amyloid peptides (Aβ) at the cerebral level, the deregulation of metallic ions and oxidative stress. Particularly, it has been demonstrated that the complexes formed by Aβ and extraneuronal copper are able to catalyze the production of reactive oxygen species that can trigger neuronal death. In this work the potential application of 109 chelating copper agents as possible multifunctional agents within the framework of the EA, distributed in three sets of different structures: ligands with N, O and S donor atoms, proposes the mechanism of pinza type and bindings type emerge. In this study, the evaluation of their pharmacological potential was carried out using bioinformatics tools and electronic structure following four key criteria: compliance of the five Lipinski, the reduction of the combined framework and the standard barrier, the tymbolsters are identified
