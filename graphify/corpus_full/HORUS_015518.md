# THE ART OF TEARING: DIALOGUE AND PLACE IN POLITICAL AND LAWS I

The presentation proposes an interpretation of Laws I (626a-644d) as a dialectical exhibition through which the Dorios Clinias and Megillus, biased towards militarist institutions and the primacy of courage as virtue, are progressively "woven", through dialogue, towards the legislative motivations of wisdom and moderation. Thus, they can now legislate with a view to virtue as a whole, and not as a faction within the city. Laws I would demonstrate, and indeed advance, the art of weaving described in the Political. In order to show this, the presentation argues that courage and moderation do not oppose each other as types and therefore that the art of politics can actually weave opposing factions
