# Lighting design via main entrance to the municipality of Motavita - Boyacá.

This document presents the lighting design for public lighting, guided by the Technical Regulation of Lighting and Public Lighting (RETILAP) of 2010. Taking as the beginning of this project was identified the problem that the municipality has in the access road to the Municipality of Motavita in the department of Boyacá, where we do not find public vehicle, pedestrian and cyclorutum lighting. Der there began to develop the lighting project on the access road to the municipality of Motavita where it was identified, the road, the road profile and the type of lighting it would have. As it is known that at these times the technology continues to advance, a comparison was made of a light design with photovoltaic system and a conventional system, where simulations were developed with each of these, together with an economic comparison of the cost of installing each technology valuing these costs at a given time
