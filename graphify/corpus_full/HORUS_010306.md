# The economic cycle in Colombia: an application of the Hamilton regression method to the Economic Tracking Index

This paper compares the Hamilton regression filter with the traditional methods, the Bry-Boschan algorithm and the Hodrick-Prescott filter, using the Economic Monitoring Index series to find the monthly business cycles of the Colombian economy in contrast with the result found using the quarterly real GDP. It should be noted that the Hamilton regression filter was not used to calculate the monthly business cycle. The reference periods are the Fedesarrollo contractions for Colombia and the NBER recessions for the United States. The results show that the Hamilton regression filter outperforms the other methods in measuring the monthly cycle, while the Hodrick-Prescott filter performs better at the quarterly level over the period analysed, 2005 - 2022
