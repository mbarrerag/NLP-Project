# New sailing navigators: assessment of traditional knowledge

This project seeks to develop strategies for the transgenerational valuation of living (inmaterial) heritage represented in the traditional knowledge of sailing navigation, thus promoting the recovery of ancestral knowledge in the coastal territory that conforms to the municipality of Guapi, Cauca. It aims to develop activities from a qualitative and creative approach of research, with a participatory approach to innovation in territory, applying principles such as the strengthening of sustainable territorial dynamics of knowledge management (Ceschin & Gaziulusoy, 2016). A group of experienced and young sailors from the community will participate in the project, as well as a group of students, professors and graduates from the University. As a result, the development and socialization of social cartography, which allow to recognize navigational knowledge, as well as a cultural event for the trans-generational participation of the Guapireña community, which will also be planned in conjunction with the knowledgeers, is planned to foster the appropriation of knowledge
