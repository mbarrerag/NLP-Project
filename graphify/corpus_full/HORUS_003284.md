# A new experience for the teaching of agricultural processing operations by means of active learning based on the experi­ence of agricultural processing

This paper examines the importance of agricultural mechanization and digital agriculture in the training of agricultural and agronomist engineers, highlighting their role in improving efficiency, sustainability and precision in agricultural practices. The study, carried out at the Experimental Center of the National University of Colombia - Palmira Headquarters (CEUNP), involved 74 students in an active learning experience using a 2 hectare maize crop to acquire practical skills with agricultural machinery and understand the factors that affect productivity. Using digital technologies and a virtual learning object (OVA), students learned basic concepts of machinery and geospatial data analysis. The results showed that this experimental approach and the use of digital tools improved students’ autonomy and critical reflection, and spatial variability maps provided valuable information for agronomic decision-making. The integration of theory and practice, supported by digital tools, allowed students to acquire essential technical skills and develop critical thinking. In conclusion, active learning in agricultural mechanization, complemented by digital tools and field experimentation, is crucial to prepare professionals capable of facing modern agricultural challenges, promoting sustainability and efficiency in agricultural production
