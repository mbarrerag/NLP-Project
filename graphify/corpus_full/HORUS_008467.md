# ESTIMAL MAXIMUM DAY LLUVIAS AS A SUPPORT TO INTEGRATED THREAT MANAGEMENT AND EXTERNAL FORCES IN TRANSPORT SYSTEMS

The extreme rainfall or maximum daily rainfall can detonate slip events and sudden increase in natural currents, so it is of great interest to make a reliable estimate of the magnitude and frequency of these events in the hydrocarbon transport systems (STH), since they are linear projects that have a large area of influence along their route. The case of study is the Central Pipeline S.A.S, where the estimation of the extreme daily rains is carried out using traditional methods and looking for projections to the year 2050. The daily information available in the IDEAM is used, a possible relationship between daily rain and altitude is sought in order to facilitate a possible regionalization of the rains, then analysis of trends by years and by decades for the seasons with at least 50 years of data, then the extreme rains for the period 1991-2020 are estimated, for different periods of return and the parameters of the distribution of probability that best fit to the historical series are analyzed to observe possible trends and in case of find them are proposed values of maximum events for the year 2050. For the maximum rainfall is compared with the method of the maximum rainfall (the analysis of the regional distribution and the method of the probability)
