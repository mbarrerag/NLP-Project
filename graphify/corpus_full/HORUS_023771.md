# A competitive risk model of discrete time based on regression by splines and longitudinal information.

Modeling risk survival data in competition with basal and longitudinal covariants is not appropriate to do so using classic survival models. For this purpose, Begg and Gray (1984) directly include longitudinal information within a logistic model, and Luo et al. (2016), improve the goodness of adjustment of the risk function of that proposal, with the inclusion of a time-dependent spline to the event. In this paper, an extension to the previous model is proposed through joint modelling, where the survival process corrects biases in the longitudinal model due to information withdrawals, and the longitudinal model properly estimated, is included in the logistic model to serve as a marker to the event of interest. We illustrate the application of the proposal with a credit database
