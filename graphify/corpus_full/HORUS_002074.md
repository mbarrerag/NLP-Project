# Implementation of an automated protocol for quality control in clinical linear accelerator imaging systems

This study aimed to implement an automated protocol for quality control in linear accelerator imaging systems in a public hospital of the Colombian suroccident. The procedures were structured following the recommendations contained in the publications of the TG-42 and TG-198 of the AAPM, and TECDOC-1958 of the IAEA, adapting them to the available physical simulators. The software LimageQC was developed, which automates the analysis of monthly tests on flatar images (MV and kV) and volumetric images (CBCT), using Python, Django, OpenCV and Pylinac. During the validation, more than 12,000 automated, automated measurements were performed, whose coefficients of variation and magnification errors proved to demonstrate stability and reproducibility.
