# VALIDATION TO A LATIN AMERICAN CONTEXT OF THE SCALE OF PARENTALITY AND FAMILY ADJUSTMENT (PAFAS)

to explore the psychometric properties of the Parentality and Family Adjustment Scale (PAFAS) and to make a proposal for validation in a Colombian context. cultural adaptation and validation by judges were carried out, this version was applied to a sample of 151 caregivers of children and adolescents between eight and thirteen years (M=9.2; standard deviation=341), this step was carried out to check the language changes. Subsequently, the scales were administered to sample one (n=151) to explore the scale dimensionality and then confirm the models proposed in sample two (n=130). a high-mediate reliability (alpha=0.734) was found, which increased with the final version to 0.79. Regarding the validity of the content, it could be well agreed between experts (Kappa index =0.85-0.94 and Cohen Delta = 0.53-0.60).
