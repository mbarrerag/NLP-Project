# MY FIRST ROCAS BOOK

Rocks are groups of minerals, usually of different types. For example, limestone, which we use to make cement, has a very high percentage of the calcite mineral, and very little of other minerals such as magnesite, siderite, plaster or quartz. And, what is a mineral? are the basic components of the rocks (such as legic chips) and are formed from the grouping of the chemical elements that originated the planet, and those that have been transported by asteroids and stellar dust. A mineral is a solid, inorganic substance, which is naturally formed and has an internal structure and chemical composition defined. If it does not fulfill these five properties we cannot call it mineral, as is the case of mercury (liquid), coal (organic origin) or steel (made by man)
