Recomposición Urbana: In the sector of Sans Façon sector in the Martyrs, Bogotá The process of urban development of the city of Bogotá has been influenced significantly by the railway infrastructure. Initially, the railway was in the peripheral areas of the city, but quickly was absorbed by the urban growth. However, this process of definition and integration with the urban plot was complex and slow, which generated a series of problems in the adjacent areas to the railway and has led to the generalized deterioration of its conditions. This research work has focused on the study of the physical problems of the area known as Sans Façon (comprising between race 30, street 26, the Avenida caracas and street 13) with the aim of identifying, characterizing and proposing solutions from the perspective of the urban design.
