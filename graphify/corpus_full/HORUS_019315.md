# A Sustainability Approach: Life Cycle Analysis as a Decision Making Tool in the Development of Hydroelectric Projects in Colombia

The installed capacity for hydroelectric power generation constitutes 69.2% of Colombia's energy matrix, which is due to the geographical characteristics and water resources of this country. Although this form of generation is considered renewable, hydroelectrics involve the construction of dams and water deposits (inundated areas) that, in addition to environmental impacts on communities, soil, water and the general environment, generate greenhouse gases. New hydroelectric power plants will be built in the country, so it is suggested to evaluate them from a sustainability perspective, understood as the articulation of social, economic, environmental and cultural variables in search of a balance over time. It is proposed for this purpose the Life Cycle Analysis (LCA) as a tool to determine environmental impacts of hydroelectric projects. This work reviews how the sustainability approach can be supported by tools widely used in engineering projects such as the ACV and the Analysis of Social Life Cycle (ACVs) to be used to determine the impact of hydroelectric projects in all its phases. This work is based on the economic dimension, opening the path to new projects, and to start up. It concludes to know the impact of hydroelectric projects in all phases, addressing environmental and social dimensions. The economic dimension is not taken into account, opening the path to new projects
