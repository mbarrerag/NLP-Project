# Shiny application for verosimilitud exchanges

This project presents a fundamental tool in statistical inference, the intervals of likelihood, which allow to estimate an unknown parameter and evaluate its uncertainty. An interactive application in R Shiny will be developed based on methodologies used in the literature of statistical inference, with the aim of facilitating the understanding and importance of the intervals of likelihood in the training of the students of statistics and in the decision making in the research. With this tool, students are expected to acquire practical skills in the construction and interpretation of the intervals of likelihood and its application in the selection of statistical models
