# ARBORIZATION AS

The Island of Urban Heat (ICU), caused by the increase of waterproof surfaces, high densification and lack of shade, is a common phenomenon that could intensify with climate change, especially in warm-humid climates. This research evaluated the arborization as a strategy for mitigating the island of heat in the public space of Barranquilla, Colombia. To carry out the study was selected the route "Carrera 44" due to its high exposure to solar radiation. Computational simulations of the operating temperature were carried out to compare different plant shade options for the years 2020 and 2050. The results show the percentage of mitigation of the ICUs for each proposed scenario. It is estimated that the findings of this review can be useful as guides for the design of public spaces in the Colombian Caribbean region
