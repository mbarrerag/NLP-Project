# Road accident forecast by using predictive modeling technologies.

Traffic accidents are a major global concern, as they have a significant impact on human safety, health and well-being, and thus constitute an important research field on the use of state-of-the-art techniques and algorithms to analyse and predict them. The aim of this thesis is to propose a predictive model for estimating the probability of traffic accidents in a given area by integrating information from official entities and social networks related to road accidents and road infrastructure events. The model designed was a deep learning model, composed of closed recurrent units and convolutional neural networks
