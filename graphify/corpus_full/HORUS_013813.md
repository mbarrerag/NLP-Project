# Strengthening the infrastructure of the laboratories of the Orinoquia headquarters to contribute to the missionary purposes of the National University of Colombia

At the Orinoquía headquarters, the laboratory system is integrated by the teaching laboratory where courses related to chemistry are taught, practices and internships are developed, research and knowledge transfer to the community in general, and the Soils, Waters and Foliars laboratory, accredited with the NTC 17025:2017, which provides extension services, as well as contributing to the research of the physicochemical and microbiological conditions of the soil and water of the Orinoquía region. These laboratories present needs for readjustation for their proper maintenance in infrastructure and equipment that contributes to the optimization of the useful life of existing resources, the early arrangement or replacement for those equipment that present failures in their functioning, an appropriation of the culture of quality and metrological management in the provision of services, all in order to promote the generation, appropriation and transfer of scientific, technical and humanistic knowledge, and to guarantee the processes of training, research and extension carried out with the resources existing in these laboratories. For this reason, participation in internal calls for resources to strengthen and contribute to the continuous improvement of the laboratories in the framework of the relevance of my University
