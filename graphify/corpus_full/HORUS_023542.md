# Production networks and volatility in the Colombian manufacturing industry

The volatility of the product is an indicator of the stability of growth, of the investment and of the employment in an economy. Previous works have shown that insumo-product networks composed of some products very connected and many others with few connections between them, allow the transmission of sectoral shocks to the rest of the economy, precisely by transferring the shock between central product chains that are connected to each other, amplifying the magnitude of the initial shock and generating aggregate volatility.This article studies the structure of the input-product network for Colombia between 1982 and 2012, to understand its role in generating aggregate volatility of the industrial product.To this end, a series of input-product networks at the level of goods, based on data from the Annual Manufacturer Survey at the level of establishment.The wealth of the data allows to study the structural properties of the network, by characterization of the sequences of first and second industrial product order of the centrality of the products in the network, is constructed by means of the insumo-product network at the level of establishment.The wealth of the data allows to study the structural properties of the network, by means of the first and second-generation of the main factor, and second-level, and second-level, and
