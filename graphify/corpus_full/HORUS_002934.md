# Prediction of agricultural market prices in Colombia: application of machine learning models and data mining for decision-making in agriculture

This research focuses on the application of machine learning and data mining models for decision-making in Colombian agriculture, with a particular focus on predicting agricultural market prices. In a context where agriculture faces significant challenges due to climate variability, market fluctuations and policy changes, the use of advanced analytical techniques becomes crucial to optimize production and maximize farmers' incomes. The study begins with a comprehensive review of the theoretical framework related to machine learning, including linear and non-linear regression models, as well as supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. Different algorithms of modelling learning are explored, such as neural networks, decision trees and models based on vector support. The research also addresses the importance of data exploratory analysis (EDA) and data preprocessing, essential elements to ensure the quality and relevance of the predictive models. Through rigorous methodological design, tools and technologies suitable for analysis were selected.
