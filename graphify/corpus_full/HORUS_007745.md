# Strengthening the research capacity of the Seed of Weeds Ecology

The main objective of this project is to strengthen a seedbed of research through the digitization of a part of a botanical collection within three months. The collection, which represents a valuable resource for biodiversity research, faces accessibility and conservation problems, limiting its use by the academic and scientific community. The digitalization process will allow for the preservation of botanical specimens, improve their accessibility by creating an online database and train the seedbed members in the use of specialized technological tools. This effort will also contribute to the dissemination of knowledge about local biodiversity, fostering scientific collaboration and open access to information. With an interdisciplinary approach, the project seeks not only to preserve the collection, but also to develop technical and scientific skills in young researchers, thus consolidating the seedbed as a space for academic training and growth
