# New wraveling wave cnoidal and soliton solutions to kawahara equations

In this paper we give exact analytical solutions for two Kawahara equations. We give one soliton and cnoidal wave solutions to this important equation in physics. The periodic solutions are expressed in terms of jacobian and Weierstrass elliptic functions. Soliton solutions are obtained from them as a particular case. Solutions are illustrated with 3d plots.Keywords: Kawahara equation, modified Kawahara equation, cnoidal wave solution, soliton solution, Weierstrass elliptic function,Jacobian elliptic function, Wolfram Mathematica
