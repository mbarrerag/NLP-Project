# Energy Efficiency Schemes Using Advanced Measurement Infrastructure in Colombia for Concentrated Demand

This article addresses the importance of advanced measurement infrastructure in transforming the energy sector as a solution to the limitations on the current type of energy measurement operating under a large-scale centralized model. This infrastructure requires a set of real-time data measurement, collection and transmission devices, allowing detailed and accurate monitoring of energy resources in the network. This infrastructure can be optimized by the use of concentrated measurement systems for more efficient management of energy distribution and the identification of areas of inefficiency. Advanced functionalities and deployment scenarios proposed for its massification, including demand response mechanisms through the active participation of consumers in regulating their consumption, encouraging energy efficiency and cost reduction towards a smarter and more sustainable model, will be analysed
