a model of Integral Analysis is proposed as an attempt to systematize analytical resources through its application in a specific work as a didactic tool for professional training. First, it is considered that analyzing means observing the parts of the phenomenon to understand its totality. But, later, it is necessary to synthesize the different resulting observations. Thus, the work for piano by Liszt, Il Penseroso (S.161 No.2), will be analyzed, encompassing the totality of its elements and examining the work from the theoretical-musical, expressive-artistic and extramusical point of view (programmatic and historical-contextual). Thus, the structural analysis will study the main elements as they are melody, rhythm, harmony, texture and form, and will be complemented by the analysis of gender and style to reveal the expressive-artistic idea of the work. As a result, these reflections will demonstrate a singular artistic conception of the Penserous Il that must be situated within an adequate aesthetic framework.
