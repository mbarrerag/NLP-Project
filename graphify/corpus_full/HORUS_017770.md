# PASSIPHLORA SILVE SPECIES AS GULUPA PORTAINJERT FOR A SUSARISING MANNEJE

to determine the presence of picudos (Curculionidae) in plantations of chontaduro Bactris gasipae Kunth in Villagarzón, Putumayo. in three farms were installed of 20 traps handmade of guadua barleys with pieces of sugar cane fermented for 48 hours in a solution 1:2 (melaza:agua) for 11 consecutive weeks, obtaining data of picudos/week/finca. The data were transformed to vx+1, subjecting them to a simple Anova and a Tukey test (p = 0.05), to compare means. The presence of nine species of curculionidae was determined, with an average of 41,54 adults/week and lastly the Arizona with 362 species present with an average of 32,90 adults/week.
