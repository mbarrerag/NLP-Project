# Peace initiatives by ex-combatants in Colombia: perspectives, analysis and tensions of the bets that build peace day by day

The present project aims to study the peace initiatives that have emerged in Colombia as mechanisms of resistance to the Colombian conflict and as a means of peace-building. Thus, this research commitment focuses on the population of former guerrilla and paramilitary combatants and the current initiatives that they have built based on the tensions, scopes, threats and challenges they have faced in a reality and institutionality like the Colombian one that, while it has mechanisms for their reintegration, reintegration and reintegration, still continues to problematize this group. To this, it must be added, the heterogeneity of these bets for peace whose reach is not only local but also regional and national, which becomes an essential point in their durability and stability in time
