# COMPARISON OF THE SUPERFFICIAL ERODABILITY OF A SOUND IN FIELD AND IN LABORATORY TO GET OUT OF THE INDERBITZEN TEST

In recent years, the erosion processes have increased as a response to environmental variations and anthropic actions, generating consequences on the stability of the land until its degradation. In this context, it is necessary to establish mitigation and remediation measures for areas susceptible to erosion, implementing tests that allow a direct measurement of the erodibility in the soils, as is the case with the Inderbitzen test. This work evaluates the surface erodibility of a soil using the Inderbitzen equipment, compared the laboratory measures with that equipment and the measures in a hydro-erosive plot in field. In this case, the soil loss measures were recorded with the increase of the slope of the ramp or of the flows. It is concluded that the effect of scale applied in laboratory was influenced on the value of the erodability (K) obtained in laboratory, including the slope of the plot and three variations of flow. In this case, the soil loss increased with the increase of the slope of the ramp or of the flows.
