# GOVERNANCE AND EDUCATIONAL QUALITY IN COLOMBIA: REFLECTIONS FROM THE NEW INSTITUTIONAL ECONOMY

This paper presents a reflection on the potential contribution of the New Institutional Economy to address the study of educational systems and their performance. In addition to analyzing the theoretical elements of this approach applied to the educational system and reviewing recent empirical contributions from literature, it analyses the specific problem in Colombia to arrive at a study proposal that involves these theoretical elements supported in more realistic assumptions. Neoclassical logic is questioned to explain the performance and limitations from agency theory, opening the way to the approach of governance as an alternative study according to the nature of the processes of organization and management of education systems, especially in the public sector. It concludes in favor of the potential of institutions and the contractual approach to address the study of educational systems, despite the possible problems of measurement that may be faced. Thus, the various forms of organization of education in the public sector and its hierarchical nature and the analysis of transaction costs become important beyond the orthodox analysis of production costs.
