# Ways to Inhabit: Community-based adaptations as a climate governance tool in the La Vaca wetland

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change has already decreed that the effects of climate change are irreversible, so that we only have to adapt to them to survive. Governments have been inefficient in implementing climate policies and strategies, but the communities, being the first affected, have worked on it: community-based adaptations have been the solution. However, they lack a systematization that allows them to be recognized by government entities and that prevents them from being lost in time. Since each habitat is exposed to different risks and is governed by different characteristics, it was decided to take a case of exemplary study for the development of community-based adaptations, the UPZ 80 Corabados in Bogotá, Colombia. The research was developed in a qualitative way, with participatory methods and bibliographic review, in order to determine the role of community-based adaptations in achieving climate governance of the inhabitants on their physical environment.
