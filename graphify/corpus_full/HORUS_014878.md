# EXPERIENCE OF THE CENTRE FOR ThOUGHT FOR RURAL DEVELOPMENT ON THE CONSTRUCTION OF PUBLIC POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PEACE IN COLOMBIA

The chapter presents reflections on the experiences of the Centre for Rural Development in the framework of peace and particularly of the comprehensive rural reform in Colombia. It begins with a review of historical aspects of the Centre and then deepens on some contributions in terms of public policies, referring to land, territorial approach, multifunctionality and agricultural extension
