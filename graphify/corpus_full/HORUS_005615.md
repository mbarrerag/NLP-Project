# A SITUATIONAL APPROXIMATION OF REASON AND EUGENESIA

This book analyzes the different geographical spaces, different ways of operation, technical-administrative and legal strategies, as well as objects of intervention in which the eugenic imprint is evidenced, The eugenics then becomes a 'box of toolstheoretical and practical that were available to its implementers, who, according to their interests, made use of one or the other. In the framework of the Latin American eugenics, crossed by the notion of race, the work proposes to enrich the Latin American eugenic historiography in the light of a situated perspective. As for the idea of race, the perspective situated emphasizes that its interpretation was defined by its actors according to the historical and geographical context, product of socio-cultural situations, and above all linked to the relations of alterity and alteration applied to eugenics. This allows to make a different approach: it is not to affirm or deny the existence of a Latin American eugenicity, but to highlight the availability of a box of eugenic tools that, in light of the situated methodology, allows to see the local bodies of its applications.
