# LESSONS: HOW DO THEY WORK?

This article presents and reflects on recent theoretical and empirical analyses that allow us to answer the question about the functioning of the structured dialogue strategy called Teaching Circles. Considering its theoretical bases from the andragogical approaches and experiential learning, it details its methodology of application both at the level of the macro-scheme of the strategy and the microprocess of each dialogue session. Finally, it describes the explanatory model that integrates the theoretical elements with the methodological proposal and details the results to which its application can lead in the context of basic education
