# STUDENT CHARACTERISTICS TRANSFORMATION BASED ON METRICS TO SUPPORT AUTOMATIC DETECTION OF SCHOOL DESERTION RISK SVM

The transformation of characteristics can be an alternative to improve the usefulness of predictors in a model [1]. Based on this premise, it is proposed to transform the characteristics using metrics into student data. The procedure was carried out with ́ 9 demographic characteristics and 12 academic characteristics. A statistical analysis was carried out and a metric scheme was developed in relation to each of the ́ characteristics. The transformation was evaluated in the detection of the risk of dropping out. ́ The transformation of the academic characteristics was achieved by improving the representation with PCA (achieving 90% of accumulated variance with only two components) and increasing by ́ 5% the performance of the SVM algorithm in the detection of the risk of dropping out. ́ The result shows that the transformation of characteristics improves the representation in ́ student data and can be studied to improve the performance of the algorithms of machine learning
