# MEGAFAUNA: OVERALL CHARACTERIZATION OF RESOURCES ASSOCIATED TO THE MARINE FUND IN THE PROFOUND REGION OF THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

Although much of Colombia is submerged, knowledge of the seabed and the organisms that live beyond a depth of 1,000 m is limited. The biodiversity of the deep waters is currently little known in the Caribbean region, since no systematic exploration has been carried out. The greatest limitation for this exploration and characterization is the lack of technological resources capable of reaching depths below 1,000 m. Despite this, the offshore hydrocarbon industry has managed to contribute to the exploration of ultra-deep waters (greater than 1,500 m deep). During the exploration phase, in the development of works of the hydrocarbon industry, several cruises were carried out with traps of nasa, unmanned submarines (ROV), camera systems of drag and core samplers (piston core) that allowed the first observations of bentopelagic ecosystems, of soft bottoms and structural bentic communities associated with cold filtrations, in depths between 1,274 m and 3,455 m, in the Colombian Caribbean.
