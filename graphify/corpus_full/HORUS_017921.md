# MOSCOW DOES NOT BELIEVE IN LAGRIMS OR ALSO OCCIDENT: FOUR INTERPRETATIONS ON THE RUSSIAN WAR AGAINST UKRAINE

This article presents four possible interpretations of Russia’s war against Ukraine. The first, the liberal, sees it as another episode of the “end of history,” that is, of the arrival at a stage in which there are no alternatives to the liberal political order and capitalist economy. The second emphasizes the over-emphasis (hybris) of the West by promoting an expansion of NATO in contravention of what was agreed with the former Soviet Union. The third highlights the role of humiliation in international relations as a possible cause of war. The fourth interpretation highlights the connection between the political economy of the Russian regime and its foreign policy.
