# Evaluation of physicochemical and microbiological elements in biosolids of PTAR El Salitre, Bogotá D.C.

In this study, the evaluation of physicochemical and microbiological properties of the biosolids of the PTAR El Salitre de la ciudad de Bogotá D.C. has been carried out. Statistical analysis techniques have been used to determine the distribution in the concentration of each element present in the biosolids, degree of association between the variables and of determining the most important elementary concentrations that are present in the biosolid material. For the development of these activities, the base information used for the development of the work was obtained by the technical services management of the aqueduct and sewage company of Bogotá, and contains the results on a dry basis of the analyses performed to the biosolids at the exit of the PTAR El Salitre during the period 2016 – 2020
