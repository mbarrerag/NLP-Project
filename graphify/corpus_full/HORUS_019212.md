# Is it possible to characterize sanctions in Transitional Justice? Justice and Peace, an experience to be reviewed.

This investigation will review the judicial experience of the jurisdiction of Justice and Peace created by Law 975 of 2005, in terms of the proportionality of the penalty in transitional justice scenarios. Thus, it will identify what of its experience is functional for the objectives of the Special Jurisdiction for Peace, born of the Final Agreement signed in 2016 between the Colombian government and the former FARC-EP. Thus, thanks to the innovative system of sanctions provided by the JEP normative framework, it will be found that the retributive purpose is relegated to a restorative vision of penalties. This without the State giving up the international duties to investigate, prosecute and punish the highest perpetrators of crimes committed during the period of non-international armed conflict
