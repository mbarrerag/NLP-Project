# TRENDS AND CHALLENGES IN FOOD PRODUCTION AND ITS IMPLICATIONS FOR THE GLOBAL SYSTEM

To address the growing food demand for overpopulation, humanity has resorted to methods that significantly alter planetary systems. This review article explores how energy sources, genetic modification and advanced technology impact food production and its planetary implications. 65 scientific papers published between 2000 and 2020, available in Scopus and Google Academic, were reviewed and a synthesis of the topics studied was developed to discuss with professionals from different disciplines how these could influence the environmental management of a territory. Historically, the transition from muscle strength to fossil fuels has boosted the technological development and allowed the industrialization of food production. This advance has intensified the exploitation of natural elements, causing deforestation, soil erosion, pollution, drinking water scarcity and climate changes. In addition, to increase food production, consumed flora and fauna has been genetically modified, affecting their nutritional quality. The consumption of these transgenic foods, together with exposure to pollutants, has increased the prevalence of diseases that compromise human health.
