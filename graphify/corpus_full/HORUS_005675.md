# A ANALYTICAL OF ALGORITMICAL GOVERNMENTALITY. SUBJECTIVITY AND INTERSUBJETIVITY IN THE CONTEXT OF A HISTORICAL ONTOLOGY OF THE PRESENT.

This book questions in depth all those panoramas of the new epistemic in which we exist, the technodigital epistemic, which must be thought of in a critical way. It is a look centered on a relational vitalism and an understanding of the unprecedented situations in which we enter with devices that capture our attention and shape our subjectivity
