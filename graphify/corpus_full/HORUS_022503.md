# EPIC, HEALTH AND PANDEMIA: ANALYSIS OF ENVIRONMENTS IN CHILE AND COLOMBIA

This article analyses how during the first months of the COVID-19 pandemic, discourses were generated about health professionals based on an `epic narrative.. For this purpose, a media analysis was carried out that takes as a reference the press written in Chile and Colombia. The analysis focused on the tropes that make up a discursive network that we call `the epic narrative of the health sector.. The results account for the emergence of two analytical axes: The first, `The official voice,, raises a series of justifications around the discourse construction of health professionals based on: (a) an epic war narrative and (b) an epic sports narrative. The second, `The professional voice, which responds critically to the way health personnel are represented as `heroe¿ of the pandemic. Finally, it discusses how `epic narratives are employed by governments to exalt the work of health personnel, while it cuts their rights and hides the labor precariousness of this guild
