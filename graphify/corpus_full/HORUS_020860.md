# Strengthening the productive capacity in double purpose livestock farming of the Flor de Sabana Association (Arautita), based on social innovation and social fabric generation.

Arauca to 2016 was the tenth department of Colombia in cattle production, with an estimated inventory of 1,028,500 cattle, in breeding and ceba; the livestock sector represented 20.66% of its GDP and 4.2% of the national GDP (Plan de Desarrollo Departamentoal 2020-2023). With a territorial extension of 23,818 Km2, the development of the Department has been based on an extensive livestock extraction base, characterized by low productive and reproductive indices and implementation of little technology, where the cross of Creole cattle with cebu predominates and the existence of natural pastures. The livestock of the foothill is mainly dedicated to the double purpose, seeking crosses that benefit the producer both in meat production and milk. Under this system, the main problems that affect the competitiveness of the meat and milk chain are used a little more advanced with the consequent improvement of productivity indices (Plan de Desarrollo Departamentoal 2020-2023). Of the seven municipalities that make up the department, Arauquita represents one of the most important in economic activity, however, the main problems that affect the competitiveness of the meat and milk chain in this municipality are: high production and marketing costs and marketing. continuous and ongoing education, an internship, an article of dissemination, a booklet and an event to disseminate the experiences and results of this initiative. Strategies are proposed to ensure the sustainability of the project after the end of the intervention of the National University of Colombia, with a view to transferring the results obtained in the association to other producers, through the trained people
