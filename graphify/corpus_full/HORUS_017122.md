# Level of technological adoption in avocado-producing agribusinesses (Persea Americana Mill.) and its associated factors.

The growth of avocado cultivation requires innovation to achieve the objectives of sustainability and growth. Knowledge and technology are a tool and the dissemination processes a necessity at the end of the process. Therefore, the subsector of avocado requires to know the technological supply, the technological state of the producers and those determinants of adoption. To date there is not a report that contains this information and that allows the decision making regarding the management of knowledge in this subsector. The objective of this research was to characterize the level of technological adoption and the associated factors in agribusinesses producers of avocado. Three stages were contemplated: i) to identify the technological supply, (ii) to evaluate the technological level and (iii) to identify the determinants of technological adoption in agribusinesses producers of avocado. The data were obtained from bibliographic review, exploration in the network and surveys. For the analysis, bibliometric tools, of scientific data and multivariate statistics were used.
