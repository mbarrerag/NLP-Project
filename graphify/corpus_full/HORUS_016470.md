# Number of meals per day during the course of the 19th COVID IN COLOMBIA, POVERTY AND MORTALITY FOR NUTRITIONAL DEFICIENCES AND ANEMIES

Fundamentals: During the pandemic by COVID'19 in Colombia in 2021, changes in the number of meals consumed daily in households have been reported, which warranted an analysis of the relationship between this situation with territorial socioeconomic variables and mortality due to nutritional deficiencies and anaemias. A descriptive study of multiple groups was carried out. Based on the analyses, a moderate negative and significant correlation was identified between the consumption of at least three meals per day and mortality due to nutritional deficiencies and anaemias, as well as for a higher level of multidimensional poverty at the territorial level. Population risk factors were identified as the higher levels of multidimensional poverty that can lead to the use of survival strategies such as the consumption of under-age meals per day by the population, with higher mortality rates for nutritional deficiencies and anaemias, during the pandemic by COVID- 19 in Colombia. Key words: COVID-19; Poverty; Anemia
