# DELIRUM IN CRITICAL PATIENTS SMALLERS OF 5 YEARS: INCIDENCE AND ASSOCIATED FACTORS.

Background and meaning The true prevalence of delirium in the pediatric population is not well documented, as well as the diagnosis, frequency in this population and associated factors.The purpose of this study was to apply the scale of detection PBCAM-ICU per nursing to determine the presence of delirium in pediatric critical patients and related factors and in this way identify it and/or prevent it in a timely manner. Objectives Determine the incidence and factors related to the presence of Delirium in critical patients under 5 years. Methods Observational prospective study. Through a sampling by convenience 52 patients in ages between 6 months and 5 years, hospitalized in Intensive Care Unit (ICU) pediatric, to whom the instrument Preschool Confusion Assessment Method for the Intensive Care units (PSCAM-ICU) was applied. These patients were included in their version of Spanish, with a bivariate analysis to identify the possible factors related. Results of delirium in the paediatric population under 5 years of age were 71.1%, with a mainly hypoactive typology
