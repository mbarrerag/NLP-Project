# MODELATION OF THE QUALITY OF AIR IN THE CITY OF BOGOTA USED KENENNIES OF MARKOV

Air pollution represents a significant threat to public health worldwide, requiring effective monitoring of air quality and strategies to mitigate its impact. This study focuses on the concentration of PM2.5, a highly hazardous particulate matter originating from various combustion sources, and employs a discrete Markov chain model to analyze its behavior. Using the categories of the Bogota Air Quality Index (IBOCA) as states, the transition probabilities, average times of persistence and return, and stationary distributions are determined. By emphasizing the application of Markov chains, this research provides information on the long-term probabilities of PM2.5 concentration levels, allowing for a better understanding of the dynamics of air pollution and facilitating informed decision-making to improve air quality management
