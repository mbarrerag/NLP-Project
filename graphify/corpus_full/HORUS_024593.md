# BIOCOLONIALITY, LITERATURA AND POSACUERDO

The aim of this essay is to show the potential of literature and myths of origin of the world's ontoepistemological diversity, to think differently about relations between human beings and nature. The ultimate goal is to point out the imperative need to change the imaginary of the development locomotors, if we really want a stable and lasting peace in Colombia, while the post-agreement policies in the country under that imaginary, continue to promote death (human and non-human beings)
