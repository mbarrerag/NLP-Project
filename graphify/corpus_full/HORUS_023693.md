# Non-crossing monotone paths and binary trees in edge-ordered complete geometric graphs

An edge-ordered graph is a graph with a total ordering of its edges.A path P= v1v2... vk in an edge-ordered graph is called increasing if (vivi+1) (vi+1vi+2) for all i= 1 , ... , k- 2. We say that P is monotone if it is increasing or decreasing. A rooted tree T in an edge-ordered graph is called monotone if either every path from the root to a leaf is increasing or every path from the root to a leaf is decreasing. Let G be a graph. In a straight-line drawing D of G, its vertices are drawn as different points in the plane and its edges are straight line segments. Let α ̄ (G) be the largest integer such that every edge-ordered straight-line drawing of G contains a monotone non-crossing path of length α ̄ (G). Let τ ̄ (G) be the largest integer such that every edge-ordered straight-line drawing of G contains a monotone non-crossing complete binary tree of τ ̄ (G) edges. In this paper we show that α ̄ (Kn) = Ω (log log n) , α ̄ (Kn) = O(log n) , τ ̄ (Kn) = Ω (log log log n) and τ ̄(Kn)=O(nlogn)
