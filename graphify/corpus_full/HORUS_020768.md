# Towards the musealization of furniture in the office of the directorate of the National Library of Colombia

This document contains the written results of the three components required to complete the final work to opt for the title of Master in Museumology and Heritage Management. The first section is the memory of the practice in the National Library of Colombia, which consisted in the research of the movable goods found in the office of the management of this institution. Next, is the conceptual work, as a personal continuation of this research work, which results from an analysis of the furniture as objects to be musealized, and the strategies of this process of musealization in this cultural institution. Finally, there is the memory of the collaborative work that was carried out in the Virtual Museum of the Colombian veterans of the Korean War, a university museum of the University of the Andes, in which guidelines and discussions were given to be able to plan a museological script according to the functions of this museum
