# Growth and characterization of graphene electrodes by electrochemical exfoliation.

This project proposes the manufacture of electrodes based on graphene synthesized by the electrochemical exfoliation technique, which will be deposited on common glass, silicon and bacelite using the spray and drip technique, thus generating coatings on these substrates. The study of chemical properties and their morphology will be carried out using the techniques of Raman spectroscopy, additionally the electrochemical properties of electrodes generated with baquelite and glass substrates in a solution of KOH, as well as the electrical properties in the electrode with silicon substrate and optics on the electrodes of glass substrates. This is done in order to give continuity to a future project corresponding to the generation of a battery based on a graphene-electrolyte-MnO system for the study of electrical charge and discharge
