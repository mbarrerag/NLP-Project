# Identification and impact of non-genetic and genetic factors on milk characteristics and birth weight in multiracial cattle in six municipalities of the department of Norte de Santander

The department of Norte de Santander is characterized by the presence of livestock in large part of its territory; highlighting the use of cross animals between the cebuín breeds with specialized milk breeds, as a resource to improve production, reduce costs and make efficient use of heterosis. However, not all the crosses carried out allow to express the advantages expected and the incorrect implementation of zootechnical guidelines can accelerate the presence of adaptation and production problems. Thus, to study the characteristics of productive type in multiracial cattle and the genetic and non-genetic factors that are responsible for its variation is of utmost importance to stimulate the correct use of these animals.
