# DEVELOPMENT AND EMPLOYMENT OF NANOGELS AS A STRATEGY FOR ANTAGONIZE TRPV1 RECEPTORS RELATING TO DENTAL ALTERNATIVE ADVANCE

The pain is considered the main symptom by which patients attend dental consultation and although there are therapeutic alternatives, sometimes the analgesic effect is not adequate so it is necessary the development of new assets and formulations.One of the new therapeutic targets studied for the management of dental pain is the transient potential (TRP) receptors, which in addition to participating in the pain pathway have been related to other alterations in oral cavity, so that their modulation is considered as possible therapeutic strategy. At the level of oral cavity, the challenges that are encountered in relation to local delivery systems, including low concentration and retention in the site of action.The nanogels can reach sites of difficult access, releasing the active in a sustained way and could therefore be used as delivery systems for the management of various oral pathologies. The development and characterisation of nanogels charged with capsazepina as strategy to antagonize TRV1 in OLCsa. Tukey test to determine statistical significance, using Graphpad Prism-8. Of the prototypes obtained, 71.6% had a size between 300-950 nm with a polydispersion index of 0.11±0.075. The load efficiency of the asset was 67.47%, with sustained release and no cytoxicity was evidenced. FTIR confirmed the chemical stability of CZP in nanogels. OLCs treated with CZP-laden nanogels showed a reduction in calcium inflow, similar to CZP-treated cells in solution. On the fifth day after treatment, an increase in transcribed TRPV1 cells in CZP-treated cells (p-0.001) was found (solution or charged nanogels (p-0.001)), although immunofluorescence did not show differences in channel expression until the seventh day after treatment. Nanogels developed and charged with CZP released the antagonist during a long period of time, did not induce cytotoxicity and were considered to trigger the activation of TRPV1 in dental formulation
