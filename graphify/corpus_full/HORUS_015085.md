# Development of an application to determine optical constants in non-homogeneous semiconductor thin films

Abstract This paper presents the design and development of a useful software application in the study of the optical properties of semiconductor materials, by which it is possible to know if the synthesized semiconductor thin films are suitable for the manufacture of semiconductor devices. Using the algorithm developed, based on Swanepoel methods, it is possible to estimate optical constants of non-homogeneous semiconductor films such as: absorption coefficient (= 945;), refractive index (n) and prohibited energy gap (Eg); in addition, the variation of thickness is estimated. This application has as an input the information in the spectral transmittance curves resulting from experimental procedures. It was found that the proposed application offers a high degree of reliability and that, therefore, can be used to study the properties of semiconductor thin films used in the manufacture of semiconductor devices in various applications, including clean and renewable energies such as solar cells
