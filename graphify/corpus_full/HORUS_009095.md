# On numeric solutions of topology optimization problems

This research study studies the topological optimization techniques applied to the classic heat problem and the elasticity problem. The study highlights several key aspects found during the process of finding solutions for specific problems, including the influence of initial conditions and optimization parameters. In addition, the paper explores innovative approaches and variations of fundamental methods aimed at achieving improved end solutions for each problem. These adaptations include adjustments of the minimized functional, density space representation and application of regularization methods. .
