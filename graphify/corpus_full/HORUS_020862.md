# STRENGTHENING THE LOCAL SYSTEM OF JUSTICE IN TAME ARAUCA: A OPPORTUNITY FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A JUSTICE FROM DOWN.

The communities of the municipality of Tame, Arauca gather a set of unmet needs, due to a series of problems that have not been solved. The answers traditionally offered by the institutionality to these problems have shown to be inefficient and, in many cases, have been only catalysts of the widening gap that separates the rural areas from the state coverage. The management of conflicts, corollary for the guarantee of rights, is one of these unmet needs pressing, since the State supply in this area has focused on the increase of judges and courts, located in the municipal headquarters, which present important difficulties to provide attention to the rural population. The relationship between access to justice through suitable mechanisms of conflict management and the quality of life in the communities is direct, hence its collective and territorially situated needs are of vital importance for the integral development of the local and inhabitants, who have taken advantage of their capacities, leadership and social norms to achieve the different mechanisms of managing their conflicts, with great potentials to continue strengthening their own processes of sustainable way. Therefore, the project has proposed to contribute and support these fundamental needs. civil society that have a significant impact on the conflict in the area and that make or can become part of the strategy of Local Justice Systems
