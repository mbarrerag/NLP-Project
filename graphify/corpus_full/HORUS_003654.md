# AN UPDATED ESTIMATION METHOD FOR EPIDEMIC MODELS WITH RANDOM PERTURBATIONS

In this talk, we present the updated estimation method to improve the forecasts for the population dynamics under SEIR epidemic models with random perturbations. The models for the population dynamics under SEIR epidemic models with stochastic perturbations are analyzed for the dynamics of the COVID-19 pandemic in Bogotá, Colombia. Computational experiments for the stochastic model with random perturbations were performed, and the model parameters were updated in their estimates for reported infected and recovered data
