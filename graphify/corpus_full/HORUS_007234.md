# The role of truck stops in urban freight planning using GPS data

The transport of goods is fundamental to the economy of a country, but also generates negative externalities, such as congestion caused by stops of trucks on sidewalks and roads. To address these problems and improve planning, it is necessary to have accurate and accessible data. Despite the advances in technologies such as the Global Positioning System (GPS), which allows real time tracking of the location, speed and time of vehicles, many previous studies have based their analysis on limited data or small samples. This study addresses this gap by using a complete database provided by the government’s system of charge for circulation, which includes detailed information on all trucks entering the cities of Belgium.
