# Editorial. The Pressure to Publish: Pandemic and Academia

As I write this editorial, the second week of March 2022 marks two years since the World Health Organization (WHO) declared, on March 11, 2020, the start of the global covid-19 pandemic. It is still unknown at what point it will end. It may even be that when this text is published the declaration has already passed.Much has been said about the effects of the disease, starting with the almost six million fatalities and as many millions of infected who have been left with some kind of sequel.
