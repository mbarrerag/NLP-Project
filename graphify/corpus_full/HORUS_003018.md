# Design and evaluation of a SERIES GAME to train UNIVERSITARY STUDENTS IN GENDER CRIBE ITEMS IN STEM AREAS (CIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, ENGINEERING AND MATHEMATICS)

The main objective of this research is to identify elements and concepts that intervene in the gender gap in STEM areas (science, technology, engineering and mathematics), and then design and validate a serious game that allows to train and sensitize university students. A bibliographic review of serious games focused on the formation of the concept is carried out. A three-phase game development methodology is used to design and validate the serious game. Are you right? The proposed game has a board mediated by digital tools in which participants answer questions while learning the main concepts about gender gaps in the international, national and local context. The tool designed was refined from the application to 47 participants. In conclusion, this tool allows to educate and raise awareness of students regarding existing disparities in gender issues, especially in STEM areas
