# KANKUAMA WOMEN FORMING CULTURE Tissue

The Kankuama women who form the cultural fabric, seeks to make visible the affirmative actions and the predominant role of women in the organizational, cultural and spiritual political work of the Kankuamo people, located in the southern eastern slope of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, in the Municipality of Valledupar, Department of Cesar, Caribbean region. The research seeks to systematize and visualize the processes of resistance and pervivence of these women who have had to face different types of violence. The general objective was to support a strategy of participatory research and audiovisual production, to socialize, systematize and visualize the cultural role of Kankuama women, through dialogues and productions of 5 video clips. The The Theoretical Framework takes up the concepts of recommunization and the relocation of life. The methodology is incorporated in the qualitative paradigm by reusing elements of the Research Participation (IAP) and ethnography.
