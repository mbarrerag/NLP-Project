# DO THE STATUS DREAMS WITH BRONCE SHEep? IN: MONOMENTS IN LATIN AMERICA: BETWEEN THE EPIC PATRIA AND THE INSURRECTION, CORPUS

The insurrectional flash shakes the object-statua of the subject that it has to represent. In its raw materiality, as a significant free character yoke that meant it, the monument becomes an open sign, reborn to new life. Something similar to what happens to the Nexus-6 replicators of Blade Runner, relentless androids of war, when he wakes from his first dream populated with electric sheep.1 And here I am surprised to look at the statues with some tenderness. I already find it more sympathetic, I confess. Tragic and ancient creations, Golems that have carried with the horrors of the past and now give their first sobs around the world. What are we going to do with these fallen statues in disgrace?
