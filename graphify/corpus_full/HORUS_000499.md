# I'll write my name on this stone

This research establishes tensions between certain exercises of power linked to the equestrian practices in Colombia. It reviews the contexts of the mafia of the eighties, specifically the case of Gonzalo Rodríguez Gacha, where his profile is examined as a powerful man within the regional elites. In turn, he reviews the figure of the political power instituted, as is the case of the former president and former senator, Álvaro Uribe Vélez. Through reports of press, video records, photographs, popular music and pictorial canons, among other sources of archive, a connection is proposed between them through the performativity of their bodies linked to theirs. It also reviews the concept of reproduction, as is the case of Terremoto de Manizales, Don Danilo and Patrimonio; echinos of great distinction, on those who exercised genetic experiments, economic exploitation of their pajillas, and taxidermias. This analysis establishes a relationship with some ideas of the equestrian monumentality, as a pattern of time (it reproduces), and also shows a biopolitical and a biopower on the body of the animal.
