# TIRANIES OF USE, KNOWLEDGE AND TERRITORIES: A CRITICAL TO THE NEOLIBERAL MODEL OF QUALITY IN HIGHER EDUCATION

The objective of this chapter is to expose the political character of the neoliberal quality model by taking a critical balance of its bets in the context of the reconfiguration of higher education systems. It is intended to show that the neoliberal quality model is associated with mechanisms of professionalization, accreditation and classification that are the reflection of a dispute that tends to resolve in favor of forms of conduction of the educational system inspired by the tyranny of utility, the tyranny of knowledge and the tyranny of merit, respectively. Under these formulas of conduct of the educational thing it will be possible to identify values that defend economic profitability, expert knowledge and meritocracy as constitutive of the neoliberal model of quality and evauation of the higher education systems, educational programs and pedagogical practices
