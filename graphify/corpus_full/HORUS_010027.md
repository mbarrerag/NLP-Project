# STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE PROJECT: AUTOMATIC TUTOR.

The project Automatic Tutor arises from the idea of the academic vice-deanship of the Faculty of Sciences of the National University of Colombia in grouping the undergraduate students enrolled in the curriculum programs of the faculty. The objective of this group is, initially, to use the information of all students enrolled from the first semester of 2012 to the second semester of 2022 and each semester to identify a group of students that characterize themselves by having a less academic performance than the common one, whose consequence can be the academic desertion or expulsion of the university because they have a lower academic average. In this work will be presented the statistical methodologies implemented in the first months of development of this project, which consist of the descriptive analysis carried out on the available databases, the procedures realized to choose the best technique of clustering, as the comparison of the results of the different techniques of grouping not supervised put to the test and the evaluation of the relevance of each alternative for the objectives of the project, and finally a study to the stability of the technique selected by the implementation of online botstrap clustering.
