# Associative relationship between Burning for Work Syndrome and Labor Precariety in Workers of Multiactive Cooperatives in Bogotá

This study aims to determine the association relationship between the Burnout syndrome and the job insecurity in workers of multiactive cooperatives in Bogotá, through a quantitative and correlational approach. The information collected was obtained through the application of the Inventario instruments of Oldenburg (OLBI) and the Precarious Labour Scale II (EPRES) to a sample of 200 collaborators, which by psychometric analysis confirmed their reliability and validity.In Colombia, currently the workers face two challenges, on the one hand, the Burnout syndrome, which affects approximately one in five employees and on the other the precariousness of the job, the latter is evidenced in that for 2023 more than 4,446 complaints were registered for non-employment.
