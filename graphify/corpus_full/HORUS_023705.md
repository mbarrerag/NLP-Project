# CHAPTER 9: VIOLENCE AGAINST INDIGENOUS PEOPLES IN COLOMBIA: AN ANALYSIS FOR THE PURPOSE OF INDIGENOUS PEOPLES DISPLACED IN THE CITY OF BOGOTA

Violence against the indigenous population in Colombia is a phenomenon that is complementary and historically rooted, expressed from various direct, cultural and structural manifestations, within the framework of which many people belonging to the Colombian indigenous peoples have made the decision to migrate to the big cities, in order to preserve their life. However, the conti- nuo of the violence accompanies them also during the exile of the city, in detriment of their possibilities of full and dignified existence, individual and collective. From the complementation of oral sources and epidemiological data obtained in this work with the community of six indigenous council members settled in the city of Bogotá, it is discussed based on the theoretical proposal of Johan Galtung, around the various manifestations of direct, structural and cultural violence that accompany the indigenous migration from the terri-tories original to the Colombian capital, together with the continuity of violence that settle around the indigenous life in the city. It is concluded that the violence accompanies the indigenous migration towards the cities within the framework of specific of each people, but with the generality of the indigenous deterioration that settles in the indigenous ways of the city
