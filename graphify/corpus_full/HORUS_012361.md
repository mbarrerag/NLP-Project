# PANDEMIA ORACLES: PRONOSTICS OF COLOMBIAN ECONOMIC GROWTH IN 2020

Prognosticating has allowed economic discipline to gain power and social relevance, although it has also exposed it to public discredit. Amid the uncertainty that has been set up by the COVID-19, we study the determinants of the forecasts of Colombian annual economic growth for 2020. We find that the variability of forecasts, ranging from +0.7% to -13.8%, does not depend on the evolution of the epidemiological situation, nor on the institutional characteristics of those who issue the forecasts. The variability is explained mainly by the average of the previous most recent forecasts and by the intervention rate of the central bank
