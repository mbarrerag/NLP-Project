# Methodological proposal for the adjustment of a discrete fracture network (DFN) from short-range photogrammetry.

The kinematic behavior of a rocky slope is primarily subordinated to the geometric properties of the discontinuities in low-rise rocky mass (about 100 meters high). A numerical method that is able to simulate these properties is that of discrete fracture networks (DFN). The primary parameters that are necessary for the modeling of a DDN are: direction, size, percentage of termination and intensity of discontinuities. In this research, it is explored how susceptible a model of DDN is in view of the presence of data that they may classify as discontinuous, for this purpose it was necessary to develop a code written in Matlab that allows to study these data using statistical criteria, taken from the statistical directional in spherical coordinates. Additionally, a methodology is proposed that proposes a way to analyze and interpret this type of information and in this way, to be able to evaluate the level of representation of the simulated discontinuities regarding the conditions that the same in-situ have for three cases of study, using a short-range photogramme team for the characterization of the fractures. Finally, a series of research is proposed
