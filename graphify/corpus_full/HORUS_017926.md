# Factors associated with adherence to treatment in patients with hypertension

Determine the relationship of socioeconomic factors, those inherent to the patient, the health services, the treatments and the use of ICTs, with the adherence to treatment in patients with arterial hypertension who attend a health institution in Duitama, Boyacá during the period January to August 2021. Study design: This study is of a quantitative type, descriptive cross-sectional correlation; in which it was sought to establish relationships between the variables defined in a particular context, formulated in two phases: phase 1: adaptation and validation of an instrument, phase 2: main study, in which the sample (n=200) was selected in a group of patients with arterial hypertension, aged 18 years and older, who attend a health institution in Duitama, Boyacá. The factors that manage to explain and impact on the behavior of the adherence of the study population, factors related to treatment and significant correlation between factors related to the provider, related to therapy and to the patient, with adherence to treatment in the group of participants. The sample was selected (n=200 ) in a group of patients with arterial hypertension, older than 18 years of age, who attended a health institution in Duetama, Boyacá. A high degree of correlation was found economic factors and the level of ICT usability, with adherence to treatment, and a significant correlation between provider-related, therapy-related, and patient-related factors, with adherence to treatment. treatment in the group of participants. The factors that manage to explain and impact the adherence to the study population in Boyacá are socioeconomic factors, factors related to therapy, factors related to the patient, and the level of usage of ICTs
