# DEMOCRACY WITHOUT WOMEN? DISCRIMINATING THE INEQUITIES OF GENDER REPRESENTATION IN COLOMBIAN MUNICIPAL CONCESSES.

After identifying the low level of subnational representation of women in Colombia compared to the national level and with international standards, the article analyzes variables linked to the competence and results obtained by candidates nominated for municipal councils in the 2015 and 2019 elections, identifying trends derived from the existence of nine categories of electoral constituency sizes. Through a qualitative methodology that systematizes information from the universe of municipal councils and competitors participating in the studied elections, the mechanical effects of the size of the districts on the possibilities of the candidates are examined and the way in which they affect the application of the gender quota and the type of list through which they registered their candidates
