# ENCAPSULATION OF ASTATIDE BY A WATER CAGE

A 512 cage of (H2O)20 consisting of 30 hydrogen bonds encapsulates Astatide with little geometrical distortion. The cage is marginally destabilized but the non-covalent interactions are actually strengthened. Host'cage interactions in the [At@(H2O)20]- cluster are anti-electrostatic, placing both negatively charged atoms in direct contact as in Atd-¿d-O'Hd+. An orbital interaction analysis reveals that explicit host'cage contacts are ¿inverted¿ hydrogen bonds.
