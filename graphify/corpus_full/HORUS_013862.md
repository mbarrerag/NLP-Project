# Implementation of participatory strategies for community management of plastic waste in the indigenous reserve of Santa Sofia (Amazonas)

The Indigenous Association of Amazonian Trapecio (ACITAM) has 12 indigenous reserves. In its life plan (2005), different environmental problems are identified, with the contamination of garbage and the lack of waste management being one of them. In the indigenous reserve of Santa Sofia (Leticia, Amazonas), the community, in dialogue with researchers, recognized that this is a problem, and has also expressed its interest in generating environmental management strategies, focused on the management of solid waste. This project aims to collectively generate a strategy for the management of plastic waste that allows the social appropriation of practices for the environmental conservation of the territory. Through the dialogue of knowledge and environmental education, the problem will be addressed, and a strategy for the management of plastic waste will be promoted. The main purpose of the project will be for the community to intervene and generate own and sustainable strategies in the time for the use, management and use of plastic solid waste
