# Microencapsulation of probiotic microorganisms with non-digestible polysaccharide for use in food matrices: methodological proposal for encapsulation and determination of gastrointestinal viability in vitro

Probiotics are living microorganisms that, administered in adequate quantities, contribute benefits to health by positively modulating the host’s microbiota. Due to their potential, they are considered as bioactive compounds of interest in food applications; however, their use is hampered by the sensitivity to technological processes and the need to maintain viability during gastrointestinal transit, where factors such as pH and intestinal motility may compromise their efficacy. The objective of this study was to carry out a review on the microencapsulation of probiotic micro-organs with a non-digerable polysacrid for use in food mats, which allows the approach of a methodological proposal for encapsulation and determination of the in vitro gastrointestinal viability.
