# Determine the variability of the demand for electricity that allows to evaluate the potential use of a differential tariff scheme

This study introduces a methodology for the analysis of the variability in electrical demand, with the objective of estimating the flexibility of energy consumption in Colombia. This evaluation is key for the possible implementation of differentiated tariff schemes, in particular Time-of-use (ToU). The methodology begins with a pre-processing of data, focused on the organization and cleaning of individual consumption registers. Next, a processing and classification of data is carried out using techniques of analysis of variability and clustering. The representative clusters are selected to identify time intervals with high variability in electrical energy consumption. The final step consists in analyzing the potential of energy flexibility in these intervals, both for users with high variability and for the total group of users studied. This methodology was applied using real data of intelligent meters of the Colombian electrical system, successfully identifying the time slots with potential to establish ToU rates.
