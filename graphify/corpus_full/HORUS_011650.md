# SUBE YBAJA

Sube y bajo is an illustrated book for children's readers that stimulates reading and imagination. It is the result of my experience as a teacher of the subjects Infantile Enlightenment, Read, write, draw (from the Undergraduate Degree in Graphic Design) and Scriptures for the children's public (Master of Creative Writings)
