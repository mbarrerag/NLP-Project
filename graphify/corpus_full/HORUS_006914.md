# Serious game design for learning and evaluation of building construction systems and processes

The research proposes a methodology to develop a serious online game, which aims to help improve the transfer and sharing of learning, in an educational process that is developed among groups of undergraduate students of careers related to construction, which allows the evaluation of acquired knowledge and reinforce the learning of systems and construction processes and quantities of work, of the stages of structure, enclosures and finishes in buildings, improving teamwork and analytical skills. The proposed subject matter focuses on the three main stages of construction processes such as structure, enclosures and finishes, which will allow students to interact in scenarios similar to those of building works, in relation to construction processes and systems. The use of games supports the teaching/learning process as a key factor in a scenario in which different levels of knowledge interact, offering the possibility of improving the motivation of the actors, as well as the experience of the game facilitators and, in the long run, an improvement in the results of knowledge transfer
