# Genomic characterization of a group of individuals with a Low Cognitive Deterioration (LCD) in the Atlantic population, Colombia

The mild cognitive deterioration (DCL) is an early stage of Alzheimer’s disease (EA), characterized by a decrease in cognition without becoming dementia. Genetic factors influence its development, and variants associated with its progression have been identified, highlighting the allele APOE- runner (NGS) enable the identification of key genetic factors in neurodegenerative diseases, facilitating the development of preventive and therapeutic strategies. In this context, the opportunity risks to conduct an association study and genomic characterization of MCI patients and controls from the Atlantic region, Colombia, encouraging to gain deeper insights into the genetic mechanisms involved in this population, which exhibits a high prevalence of neurodegenerative diseases
