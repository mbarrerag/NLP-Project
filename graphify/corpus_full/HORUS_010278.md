# Updating hardware and corneal surveyor software.

In the course of this work, the following is carried out: the updating of a prototype of cornea surveyor developed for the University more than a decade ago, which was in disuse because it presented failures in its functioning. This implies the deepening of physical concepts of optics such as the phenomena reflection and refraction and aberrations such as field curvature and astigmatism. From the updated team, the topographic measurement of surfaces entrusted with shaping contact lenses is taken, in order to verify the proper functioning of the topographer, draw conclusions about it and complement the documentation that will be given to the applied optical group of the University on the topographer
