# APPLICATION OF THE MEPS POLICY AND THE EUROPEAN UNION LABELLING FOR INTERNAL ILLUMINATION PRODUCTS IN THE COLOMBIAN MARKET

Currently, Colombia is in the process of updating its labelling programme and minimum energy performance standards for lighting products. Therefore, it is necessary to evaluate the proposed models with the products available on the market. This document presents a compilation of energy efficiency policies for interior lighting equipment in the United States, the European Union, Colombia, Mexico and Chile, considering the parameters and methodologies used to calculate and standardize the energy performance of LED lamps. Additionally, a sampling of the electrical, light and colour parameters of light sources marketed in the Colombian domestic market is carried out. With the results obtained from sampling, the energy performance of these equipment is evaluated taking into account the models established in Regulation (EU) 2019/2020 and Regulation (EU) 2019/2015 of the European Commission. Finally, the categorization of light sources marketed in Colombia according to the classification proposed by the European Union and adapted by the Pan American Commission of COPANT Technical Standards is presented
