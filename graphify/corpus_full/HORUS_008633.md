# Virtual social networks in contemporary families: discourses on sexuality on YouTube

The network society allows to observe that the social, environmental, economic, political and cultural networks of technology and their devices, virtual social networks, transform the daily interactions of people and their families into constitutive dimensions of humanity, as is sexuality. Given the relevance of these changes, this master's thesis analyses the circulation of discourses on sexuality issued on YouTube during 2019, in contemporary families of undergraduate and postgraduate students of Social Work of the National University of Colombia. To this, a methodological process is deployed that includes dramaturgical analysis of reality; framing and digital ethnography; inductive and deductive frameworks on sexuality; as well as studies of their circulation flows within the frameworks of interpretation of those and subjects of research. Consequently, it is detected that sexuality, as a polyphonic category of mobile work and social networks, is manifested in the musical discourses linked to the body and aesthetics; moralistic discourses with permits and prohibitions around the diverse, as well as the ethical discourses in which care and responsibility composes their teachings, reprensions and reproducciones. t the national university of colombia.
