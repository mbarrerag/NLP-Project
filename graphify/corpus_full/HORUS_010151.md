# Implementation and optimization of the synthesis of dimeric peptides derived from the LfcinB sequence (20-30) with potential anticancer activity against breast cancer.

Breast cancer is the most diagnosed type of neoplasia in the world and the first cause of death in women, so it is imperative to find new drugs that allow to combat this disease in an efficient and selective manner. The dimeric sequence LfcinB (20-30)2: (RRWWRMKKLG)2-K-Ahx has been shown to be promising in the development of new therapeutic agents, since it exerts a powerful cytotoxic effect in vitro against cell lines derived from breast cancer. In this research, the cytotoxic effect was synthesized, purified and characterized by eleven new dimeric peptides analogous to the LfcinB (20-30)2-type, in which the central position 26Met was replaced by different amino acids. For each peptide, its cytotoxic effect was evaluated in the cell lines derived from breast cancer HTB-132 and/or MCF-7, finding that the position 26 is a critical residue, since the cytotoxic effect against the cancer lines was significantly increased.
