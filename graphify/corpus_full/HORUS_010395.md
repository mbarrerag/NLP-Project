# CONFIGURATION OF FAMILY: CRYTIC REFLECTIONS TO DECOLONIZATION OF SOCIAL RESEARCH

The purpose of this article of reflection proposes to present the epistemological, ontological and methodological deliberations generated in the development of doctoral research, the configuration of practices and affective knowledge in single-parent families, which arise given the Decolonial commitment assumed in such a study and allude, on the one hand, to the questioning of the exclusion of emotions in discourses anchored to this perspective and, on the other hand, to the location of subalternization in a single population group and with this to the fragmentation of the being that maintain some understandings of colonial.
