# MULTI-TERM ANALYSIS FOR IDENTIFYING GENDER BARRIERS ASSIRANTS TO NATIONAL UNIVERSITY

Numerous projects have revealed the need for quality studies in the current situation of women at STEM. At the National University, one is not available, and it is very important to address the problem in an interdisciplinary manner and to propose solutions. The aim of this research is to be able to explain the academic future of an applicant according to his sociodemographic characteristics, that is why after carrying out a descriptive analysis for familiarization with the data, use of an ACM and having a better idea of the variables that would be most influential in this phenomenon two regression models are constructed in order to explain the fact that an applicant is admitted or not and the score he obtained in the admission examination thanks to the variables mentioned above. However, because the fact of being admitted depends not only on his characteristics but on the other people who presented to the university at that time, this model is discarded and we focus the model that explains the score.
