# Morphological, morphometric and molecular characterisation of Pratylenchus and Radopholus in Musa spp., on the coffee axis and Valle del Cauca, Colombia.

The phytoparistic nematodes cause losses between 30 and 80% of banana production (Musa AAB Simmonds - Dominico Hartón) and banana (Musa acuminata) in highly infested fields. In the areas of higher production of Musa in Colombia, its phytosanitary management has been limited by the ignorance of the species of nematodes present. In order to know the species of Pratylenchus and Radopholus associated with banana and banana crops in the departments of Caldas, Quindío, and Valle del Cauca, samples composed of roots and rhizospheric soil were collected from different production systems.
