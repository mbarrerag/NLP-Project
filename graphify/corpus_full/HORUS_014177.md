# The social appropriation of the Digital Archive of Indigenous Languages of the Amazon

This proposal for a joint extension gives continuity to a process of symmetrical dialogue between the academy and the indigenous peoples, through the Semillero de investigación Casa Hija: circles of the word of the research groups "Research of the Regional Development in the Amazon" and "Etnología y Lingüística Amazónicas" of the Sesèdes Amazonia and Bogotá. this work has led to the creation of the archive of the indigenous languages of the colombian amazon (ardilia), housed in the institutional repository of the unal, and of the laboratory of languages and symmetric dialogue in 2023. Currently, the ARDILIA Archive houses nine collections of the Carijona, Magütá (Tikuna), Miraña, Murui (Uitoto) and Wamonae (Cuiva) languages, and has received a total of 2,832 visits in the first year since its creation (according to information of the Repositivo Puerto del Carmen). The present proposal seeks to support alliances between communities and researchers to incorporate new collections in the Archive, empower the peoples represented in the Archive for the social appropriation of the materials there, contributing to the process of students of The Academy and the ancestral knowledge for the defense of the territory the construction of cultures of peace. In addition, this initiative is part of the purposes of the declaration of the Unesco of the Decade of Indigenous Languages (2022-2032) and in particular of the provisions of Law 1381 of 2010 on the protection of native languages, which assigns to public universities a role in the conservation, preservation and protection of native languages, and in the goals and projects established in the Ten-Year Plan for Native Languages 2022-2032
