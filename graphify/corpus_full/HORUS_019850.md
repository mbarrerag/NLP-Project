# Sergei IVANOVICH KORMILOV -- STIJOVED I ISTORIK LITERATURE (=SERGUEI IVANOVICH KORMILOV -- VERSOLOGY AND LITERATURA HISTORY)

The article presents the life and research of S. I. Kormilov (1951-2020), an important Russian versologist. His most important findings are analyzed, putting them in dialogue with the work of other researchers
