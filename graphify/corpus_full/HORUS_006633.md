# The Urban Lighting Master Plan as a Enhancing Cultural Urban Environment: Case Study, Park Way

Urban design is generally focused on day activities, neglecting the planning of the nightscape, which results in proposals for little integrated and monotonous lighting. An example of this is Park Way in Bogotá-Colombia, a linear park of 800 meters that connects squares and paths and which has experienced various transformations. Currently, its lighting is functional and does not highlight the expressiveness nor considers important environmental aspects. It proposes a master plan of urban lighting whose central concept is the "pulsus", which seeks to integrate a more sensitive and appropriate lighting, illuminating paths and squares to create a route full of pauses and movements. Given the environmental value of the park's tree species, strategies are suggested to minimize the environmental impact with adequate lighting levels and luminaries that do not emit excessive light up. It is also planned to highlight the facades of the heritage buildings around the park with the aim of exalting their architectural characteristics. minenating paths and squares to create a journey full of pauses and movements.
