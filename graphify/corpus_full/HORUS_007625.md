# Strengthening the Seed Research Capacities in Food Science and Innovation in Agroindustrial Processes

Currently, the seedbed of research in food science and innovation in agro-industrial processes is formed by a total of 32 undergraduate and graduate students, of programs of various faculties. This seedbed was founded and is formed by professors of the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences and the Institute of Food Science and Technology, seeking to train students from very early stages of their academic preparation in aspects related to research in agro-food science and technology, that allows them to develop with greater property in their professional life, or to engage in research aspects of this area in their post-gradual training. Although there are several areas of work inside the seedbed, the instrumental sensory analysis of food is located transversally in the research carried out in the various projects in which students and teachers participate. The instrumental sensory analysis seeks to simulate the organs of the senses, using instruments of objective measurement, to obtain more precise results, without the need to train people to act as colorists for the evaluation of a food. The training is disspendient and complex, since not all people have the same sensory abilities, and the recognition of cultural factors is highly correlated. In order to carry out special training days on the basis of colour and application of colour measurement in the processes of pigment extraction, aimed particularly at the seedbed members, the training sessions will be accompanied by graduates from the seedbed and the teachers associated with it. For the particular case of this project, support is requested in the financing of the materials required for the practical exercise, as well as the linking of an auxiliary student who supports the logistical organization of the workshop and laboratory practices. It is hoped that the seedbed members will acquire practical skills to facilitate the research activities carried out by the group
