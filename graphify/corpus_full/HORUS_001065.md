# COST OF INCAPACITIES BY MENTAL DISTORTIONS AND BEHAVIOR IN COLOMBIA, 2016-2018

Objective To determine the costs of temporary disability due to mental and behavioral disorders for the period 2016-2018 in Colombia. Methods A descriptive-correlational cross-sectional observational study, using the totality of the records of contributions to the General System of Social Security in Colombian Health between 2016-2018, reported according to the ICN-10 as mental and behavioral disorders (F00-F99) and provided by the Ministry of Health and Social Protection. Results The highest number of disabilities corresponded to anxiety disorders and the depressive episode with a participation greater than 20% each. The average number of incapacity was 2.68 days, with schizophrenia (4.09), affective bipolar disorder (3.48) and recurrent depressive disorder (3.23) those that generated a higher number of disabilities. The average daily cost per each disability was $27,178; and for each patient, taking into account the average number of days that were disabled, the cost was raised to $41,278 per person. Conclusions Women presented, with greater frequency, some type of illness and the greater number of records occurred in the center area and coffee center of Colombia; the greatest number of disorders was established
