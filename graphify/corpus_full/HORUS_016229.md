# Study of the response of xylose transforming yeasts to stress conditions in sugar cane bagasse hydrolysates

The transition towards biological base production systems involves not only the adaptation of existing technologies but also the search for efficient and sustainable processes. The use of microorganisms as biocatalysts for the transformation of residual biomass has been studied in recent decades. However, within the parameters of selection of these microorganisms the ability to tolerate and adapt to stressful conditions is one of the most limiting for their use in industrial scale.
