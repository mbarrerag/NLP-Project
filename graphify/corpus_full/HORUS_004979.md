# Model of strategic negotiations

The entrepreneurship ecosystems with the aim of promoting the generation and consolidation of companies have focused their efforts on spreading the entrepreneurial culture among proponents of business models, that is, the effort has focused on strengthening the entrepreneurial team and its product, the business model, taking less care of socializing entrepreneurship among workers and pensioners, agents that must be invited to the stage as potential contributors of capital and knowledge to achieve the three-way entrepreneurial-business-investor model that allows to create new companies, empower those that are operating and reopen closed ones. The strategy for this is to make business wheels in the classroom, in which entrepreneurs present their projects to potential investors, who qualify them valuing their ability to generate wealth, in a participatory and inclusive exercise in search of planting entrepreneurial culture, traducible in the future in companies that generate economic and social progress
