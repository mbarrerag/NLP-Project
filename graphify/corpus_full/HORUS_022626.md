# CONGELATION OF SEMINAL PLASMA COLOMBIAN ASNOSIS WITH SEMINA PLASMA AND WITHOUT USE OF PERMEABLE CRIOPROTECTORS

Artificial insemination is considered one of the most important reproductive biotechnologies to increase and accelerate the genetic progress of individuals of many species. However, one of the problems encountered in the artificial insemination of the asnal species with frozen semen is the high uterine inflammatory response to the insemination in the asnal females and is possibly due to the removal of seminal plasma at the time of freezing of the sperm cells and the presence of permeable cryoprotectors.
