# Concentration of cortisol in hair of Medical Students (National University) and time of transport to the study site (2022)

The chronic psychosocial stress has adverse consequences for mental and physical health. The increased secretion of cortisol, which is presented in chronic stress, participates in the development of pathologies such as obesity and cardiovascular diseases. The secretion of cortisol is pulsatile so the determination in blood presents difficulties for its interpretation, because of this, it has sought to measure this hormone in other biological fluids such as urine (24 hours) and saliva (various samples per day). In turn, to estimate the secretion of cortisol for prolonged periods, it will allow for longer periods of use, have been developed, in recent years, techniques to measure this hormone in the hair. The deposition of cortisol in the hair is a slow process and its quantification allows to determine retrospectively the secretion in the last three months. On the other hand, numerous studies show that prolonged transport times of the national hospital will be associated with the study of the lower amount of physical exercise, of leisure time and worse quality of sleep. In previous works, alterations of the hypothalamo-hypophysis-adrenal axis in adolescents with prolonged transport time to the site of study. Samples will be subjected to a methanol extraction process and subsequently determined by ELISA. This research aims to expand knowledge of the relationships of cortisol concentrations in hair (chronic stress biomarker) with transport time and anthropometric variables waist circumference and body mass index. This is a pilot research that could be applied, in the future, to a wider population to seek strategies that reduce chronic stress and improve the quality of life
