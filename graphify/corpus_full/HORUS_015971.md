# ECOLOGY OF THE GROWTH OF BASILISCUS BASILISCUS LINNAEUS, 1758 (SQUAMATA: CORYTOPHANIDAE) IN THE URABA CHICOANO, COLOMBIA

The growth pattern of Basiliscus basiliscus in the Chocoan Urabá was studied, based on brand data and recapture, applied to the von Bertalanffy model. The results suggest that males are more likely to grow rapidly, while females are more likely to be involved in future reproduction
