# Mobile applications as a resource for the teaching of sound qualities in secondary education

The results of a research focused on learning the qualities of sound are presented through the implementation of the methodology of Active Learning and the integration of technological elements in the classroom. Activities are developed that address pedagogical, technological and disciplinary aspects, promoting an integral educational experience. For this purpose, random working groups were organized, but with roles and tasks assigned to promote individual responsibility, exchange of ideas, decision-making, group inclusion, adaptability, collaboration and the exercise of various types of leadership.The use of technological elements such as tablets and cellulars allowed to establish a remarkable capacity of adaptation of students to integrate efficiently the technologies available in the educational process and to encourage the creation of learning environments that facilitate practical and meaningful exploration of the theoretical concepts involved in the activities.In order to establish parameters that allow the evaluation of the process, a role was taken in the classroom, the understanding of the topics, the technological literacy and the development of the students as participants of the activities. cell phones allowed for a remarkable capacity for students to efficiently integrate the available technologies into the educational processes and facilitated the creation of learning environments that allow for the practical and meaningful exploration of the theoretical concepts involved in the activities. In order to establish parameters for evaluating the process, a classroom intervention was carried out out that involved complementary teachers as observers of the implementation processes and students as participants in the activities. The results of these evaluations reflect that the learning process successfully promoted participation, collaborative work, motivation, understanding of the topics, technological literacy, and the development of skills integrated to the scientific method
