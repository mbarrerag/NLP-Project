# CHAPTER 37 - BUILDINGS AND MATERNAL LACTANCY

It is understood as puerperium at the time of birth during which physiological changes of pregnancy, labor and childbirth are resolved, the woman returns to the pregestational state and in the mammary gland there are a series of physiological changes that allow breastfeeding. Usually this period of time is 6 weeks (1.3). Chronologically divided into: Immediate puerperium: first 24 hours postpartum. Mid-term puerperium: from the second to the tenth postpartum day. Late puerperium: from the 11th to the 42nd postpartum day (4). The puerperium is a period with a large number of particularities, where serious complications can be masked by physiological events, so that one must distinguish well between the limits of normal puerperium and the signs or symptoms that should alert Breastfeeding is a way to provide an ideal food for healthy growth and development of infants. Although it is a natural act, breastfeeding is also a learned behavior, it is a reflection of the values of each culture.
