# MALFORMATIONS RELATING TO THE OVERVIEW OF AbandUM IN CIRUELA PASS: CASES REVISED REPORTS OF CASES PUBLISHED

Abdominal syndrome in plum passes through (SACP) is characterized by the absence of abdominal wall muscle, urinary system alteration and bilateral cryptocardia. Characterizing cases of patients with diagnosis of PCAS and related malformations and searching medical publications for reports in which similar cases are recorded. A series of cases were described and a systematic search was carried out limited to reports of cases that met all criteria: abdominal wall defects, urinary tract alteration and cryptorchidia, and those that reported associated malformations. Of 28,452 births 55 cases were identified, eight with inclusion criteria. All were male, two were aborted. Malformations related to renal, pulmonary, cardiac and central nervous systems were found. 1,157 articles were screened in the publications, of which 27 cases were detected in 15 reports, with a description of malformations of multiple systems. It is important to systematically evaluate possible congenital alterations in other systems, given the links with other malformations reported in this study
