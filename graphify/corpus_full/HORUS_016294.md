The advance in material growth techniques has made possible the practical realization of semiconductive quantum rings with different topologies. Thus, quantum rings with similar forms to rhombic ones have been reported. These nanostructures can be used in optoelectronic devices and in quantum computing. To carry out these applications with quantum rings of various forms it is necessary to have methods to calculate the energy spectrum of the electron.This fact has motivated to consider a single electron confined in a doped quantum ring with a donor impurity, forming a molecular complex D2+, under an orthogonal magnetic field to the surface 2D of the nanostructure and an electrical field that moves over the plane.To calculate the optical responses, the corresponding energy levels to the electron are obtained by solving the equation of Schrödinger under the effective mass approximation and using the computational methods based on finite elements.
