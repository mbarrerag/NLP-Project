# Association of Virulence of Colletatrichum Lindemuthianum with the defense response in common beans under an alternative control

Among the management alternatives, the use of compounds such as phosphites which may have an effect on the plant by inducing its resistance mechanisms as well as a fungiic effect inhibiting mycelial growth and germination of the spores of the fungus has been implemented.
