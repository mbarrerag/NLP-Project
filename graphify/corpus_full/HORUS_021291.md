# Archaeological Inventory of the Rupestre-SARS Sites of the Vides River

Its objective is to make a technical review of the archaeological site located in the Santa Teresa and Sinai area of the municipality of Villagarzón, Putumayo, together with the social analysis of what has represented and represents for the current communities SARS from approaches of feminist archaeologies. It will be sought to elaborate the third chapter of its graduate thesis from the University of Avignon while taking classes in museology and international heritage policies that help to expand the research context of the archaeological study of rock art
