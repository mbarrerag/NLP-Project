# The 1991 fiscal crisis and political constitution: a look at the limits of the construction of the free order in Colombia

This article maintains that, from the point of view of fiscal policy, the crisis that suffers the liberal order of the Political Constitution is explained by the contradiction that occurs when the State, in addition to fulfilling its function of legitimization, must also satisfy objectives of economic accumulation and maintenance of investor confidence. In the field of fiscal policy, the contradiction arises in the following terms: while the enforceability and enjoyment of rights institute a safeguard of the dignity that must be imposed on economic and political processes to ensure the legitimization of the current order, there is also a safeguard of the viability that conditions the exercise of the rights to the imperatives of accumulation and capitalist confidence. The document will show how the aforementioned contradiction has been deployed over the last thirty years in the country.
