# THE VALLENATO VALLENATO, PATRIMONIES OF THE CARIBBEAN REGION

El Cesar beyond the Vallenato: heritages of the Caribbean Region The department of Cesar, located in northern Colombia, is widely recognized for its contribution to Vallenata music. However, its heritage wealth goes far beyond this musical genre. The region houses a vast set of cultural expressions, oral traditions, artistic manifestations and ancestral knowledge that reflect the diversity of its peoples. In the material sphere, Cesar has important archaeological and architectural sites, such as the indigenous vestiges of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, witnesses of the history of the original peoples. Furthermore, the agricultural culture of the region has been fundamental in the construction of its identity, with agricultural and livestock practices dating back centuries. On the other hand, the oral traditions and Afro-indigenous rituals, folk festivals and gastronomy are essential elements of the intangible heritage. The religious celebrations and popular festivals, such as the Feast of the Saints Reyes in Curumani, are expressions that show the cultural syncretism of the region. Likewise, oral literature, transmitted from generation to generation, account of the time frame and the accounts that make up the collective heritage of the region
