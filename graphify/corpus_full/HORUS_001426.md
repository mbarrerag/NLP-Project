# SYNTHESIS, CHARACTERIZATION AND DEVELOPMENT OF WATER DISORTION BY CALENTATION OF THE COMPOSED POLIMERIC SYSTEM BY DIFFLUORURE POLYVINIL (PVDF) AND PHOSPHORICAL ACID (H3PO4)

In this work we present some studies of the effect of water desorption in polymer membranes performed with a polyvinyl difluoride (PVDF) and phosphoric acid (H3PO4) highly hygroscopic, analyzed by various techniques of calorimetry, SEM microscopy and spectroscopy of complex impedance (IS), in a range of temperatures between 20 and 100 °C approximately. The results of differential calorimetry of sweep (DSC) and thermogravimetry (TGA) show three thermal anomalies around 160 °C, 300 °C and 450 °C, corresponding to the temperature of fusion, a new transition somewhat exothermic and a breakdown of the polymeric system respectively. The results of TGA up to 100 °C show a loss of mass less than 10% attributed to the water evaporation. The energy of activation determined from the process of water molecules was about 0.30 °e/molecule of H2O.
