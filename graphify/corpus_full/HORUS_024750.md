# DYNAMIC LAND COBERTS IN THE NATURAL RESERVE OF THE SURVEY OF THE SURVEY OF THE SURVEY OF THE SURVEY, LOSSES OF MORROSQUIL, SUCRE DEPARTMENT, USED SENSORS REMOTE

The Colombian Caribbean has 74.202 ha of mangrove (Invemar, 2018), of which, about 200 ha (2019), are located within the Sanguaré Nature Reserve (Resolution 212 of the MMA, 2002). Sanguaré, is located in an area highly altered by fragmentation, loss of habitat and degradation of the soil, due to the transformation of natural cover. Sanguaré, began implementing strategies for change in the use of the soil, restoring natural plant cover and its ecosystem services of importance for the region. It was assessed the changes in the state of the cover of the land within the Reserve, through the use of remote sensors and GIS. Vegetation indices were analyzed, including the Normalized Vegetation index (NDVI) from satellite images Landsat. An increase in the spectral response was found in strategic sites of mangrove forests, forests and shrubs of firm land (NLDV) from 0.148 to 0.62 in Mangrovesat.
