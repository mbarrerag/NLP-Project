# Theoretical trends and challenges in the communication of corporate social responsibility

Abstract The article aims to characterize the academic bibliography on communication of corporate social responsibility (Corsc), as a basis for mapping theoretical trends and practical challenges of this academic and professional field. This research is of special interest to organizations that communicate their practices of social responsibility in order to convince their consumers and engage their employees. Also for interest groups such as civil society organizations, which aspire to a more dialogic construction of the meaning and purposes of corporate social responsibility. Both sides of reflection fit under an integrated approach of the Corsc (model 4I), which allows organizing and understanding how multiple theories related to what is, what serves and how this communicative process works. This utility is highlighted in the absence of systematic reviews on Corsc based on integrative approaches. Through a systematic bibliographic review from a quantitative and qualitative approach, it is found the need to advance research that studies in parallel study the four dimensions of the Corsc: identity, integration, interpretation and image. The above, based on an ethical perspective that recognizes the constructive role of dissent in the study and the practice of social responsibility
