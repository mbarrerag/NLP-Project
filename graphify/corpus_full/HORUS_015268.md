# Characterization of the Heart Dynamic in Echocardiography.

Cardiovascular diseases are the main cause of death worldwide. For 2019, it was estimated that 32% of all deaths worldwide were caused by this cause. This phenomenon is repeated at the national level, according to the National Health Institute, is the main cause of death with 23.5% of deaths in the country. For its diagnosis, echocardiography images are the first choice. The technique allows to evaluate the cardiac structures and their function in most clinical environments. Echocardiography has different modalities that taking advantage of key ultrasound principles allow obtaining modalities such as Doppler analysis and techniques of tracking speckle patterns, allowing in turn to evaluate the flow of blood at key points, as well as the movement of the heart muscle. In addition to the different modes of image, ultrasound can obtain different measures for a complete evaluation: thickness of the ventricular walls, dimensions of the cardiac chamber, volumes of the ventricles, flow rates and fractions of ejection.
