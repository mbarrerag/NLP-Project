# Diswear in doctors of the Compulsory Social Service: an experience in the Subnet Sur de Bogotá, 2023. “Compulsory social service: a Christ with his back”

In Colombia, there are few researches that have been concerned about the health of the doctors of the Compulsory Social Service (SSO). It would be worrying to show problems in the health of this group of workers. This research was mixed with qualitative predominance. The study population were the doctors who performed the SSO in the South Subnet of Bogotá in 2023. Of the total number of SSO doctors, 23 agreed to participate. 65.2% of the SSO doctors reported having presented some expression of wear. Among these: anxiety reported by 30.4%, osteomuscular diseases reported by 17.3% and headaches reported by 13.04 %. 74% reported difficulties in sleeping.
