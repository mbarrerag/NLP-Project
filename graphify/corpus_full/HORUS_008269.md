# Analysis, development and implementation of good management practices applicable to the supervision of public works projects and their relationship with those involved

In the management of projects and public works, the supervisor plays a vital role. It acts as a link between external actors, such as builders and interveners, and internal actors representing the contracting entity. It works in collaboration with various strategic, missionary, support and monitoring units of the entity to achieve the delivery of high quality and sustainable goods and services over time, thus satisfying the needs of the community. The main objective of this research work is to identify the main factors that generate problems in construction projects, analysis of the technical, administrative, legal and financial skills and knowledge of public servants in the area of supervision of works projects, to develop good management practices instruments applicable to the supervision of public works projects based on functional, predictive and adaptive approaches, in which they are articulated in a practical and systematic way of the legal regulations in force in the field of supervision, contributing efficiently in the control of the execution of public works contracts, minimizing the probability of reprocesses and the improvement of decisions by supervisors.
