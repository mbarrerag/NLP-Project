# ESTIMATES OF BIOMASS IN A KIKUYO PRADERA BY USE OF REMOTE SENSORS

Contextualisation: the direct measurement of biomass in pastures is the most reliable method, however, it is a destructive and dispensary method. Knowledge vacuum: recently researched techniques with the use of remote sensors to estimate physiological and morphological characteristics in crops from their optical properties, and to arrive at biomass estimates; so that, it is valuable to evaluate these techniques in perennial fodder such as kikuyo (Cenchrus clandestinus). the objective of this work was to develop a methodology to estimate biomass in meadows from its spectral and morphological characteristics, using different sources of remote information. a grazing strip of 1870 m2, subdivided into 27 subparcellas, where direct measurements of biomass and height were made without disturbar (ASD); the aerial photographs were taken with a UAS, and was selected for an image of the Sentinel 2B satellite that captured the scene of the study site corresponding to the date of flight of the UAS and the field measurements; by means of the model of aerial photogrammetry, and by means of the destructive and providing more accurate information in time and space
