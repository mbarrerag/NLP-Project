# Dentiger cysts Associated with Pathological Third-Molar Follicular Sacks Included

To establish the frequency and characteristics of dentigrous cysts associated with follicular bags of included third molars, extracted from oral surgery clinics of the Faculty of Dentistry of the National University of Colombia. Quantitative study, was carried out the histopathological analysis of 30 follicular bags of third molars included with haematoxylin-eosine col prayer. 30 follicular bags of superior and inferior third molars were analyzed for 21 patients aged between 17 and 36 years (mean: 25.3). Of the bags studied 25 (83.3 %) were diagnosed as dentigrous cyst and 5 (16.7 %) as follicular sac, more frequent being the diagnosis of dentigrous cyst in the mandibular zone. Follicular sac associated with the third molar included has high capacity to develop cystic odontogenic pathology, the most frequent being the dentigrous cyst with predilection to the anatomic location in the jaw
