# The current state of public veterinary health in Colombia, 2016-2021.

This research aimed to determine the situation of the Veterinary Public Health of Colombia for the period 2016-2021. It is a descriptive/transversal study that develops for the country an analysis of epidemiological indicators, extracted from the fields of action established by the Inter-American Meeting of Ministries of Health and Agriculture of the POS Program of PAHO. The fields chosen were the promotion of animal health, reflected in the livestock inventory; the protection of the supply of food of animal origin, manifested through the slaughter of the livestock inventory, the production and consumption of food of animal origin; attention to zoonoses and other animal diseases shared with man, expressed in the behavior of some zoonoses present in the country; and environmental protection, expressed with Agricultural Production Units. To achieve this, some variables were established that were described by means of frequencies, with the calculation of rates, measures of trend, among others; to know their behavior. The software used was Excel®. The information is presented in tables and graphs.
