# MANIFESTO BOGOTA. THE CONTRIBUTION OF THE MODERN ARCHITECTURE TO THE IMAGE OF THE CITY

The project consists of the development of a research work aimed at knowledge, value, protection and reuse of a selected number of works (buildings and neighborhoods) belonging to the architectural heritage of the late twentieth century in Bogotá, based on the documents that rest on the archive of the Museum of Architecture, it is proposed to build a repertoire of images that present or highlight the contribution of modern architecture to the image of the city, as well as potentiality the value of the documentary archive of the museum
