# RESIGNING GASTRONOMY FROM FOOD SOVEREIGNTY, EXPERIENCE IN BOYACA, COLOMBIA

Food has been affected by the technocratic model of rural development insofar as the commodification of food has separated the food culture from rural territories. This article presents the elements of gastronomy that are strengthened from food sovereignty in rural communities. For this, four semi-structured interviews based on categories of analysis established in the literature review are analyzed. This research demonstrated the need to claim traditional cuisines by understanding food beyond the economic category of gastronomy
