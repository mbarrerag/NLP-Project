# A MOCHE GUERRER, MINECRAFT AND THE INTERCRUZING OF THE PIXEL ART AT TIME: A EXPLORATORY EXPERIENCE FROM THE WALK AND CONFINITION

In the framework of a virtual class of history of ancient material culture and during the 2020 confinement took place the (re)creation of a digital work (in) of pixelart and the learning around this art when following the study and contemplation of a textile with a figure of a Moche warrior (i-VIII d.c.) students, digital natives, managed to make connection with the pixel in their daily experience in digital games like the minecraft. Considering this connection and the poetics of the things of Neil McGregor, it was carried out the reconstruction of this old figure in minecraft and with this, through the game, we achieved a learning of the pixel, the pixel art, geometric art, as well as the importance and relevance of the Latin American ancient art in the understanding of this; very much to the way of the constructive universalism proposed, decades ago by Joaquín Torres, only that we now integrate the new creative and life experiences of these young people for a more active and participatory learning of the art in and from the virtual classroom and the domesticity of the work.
