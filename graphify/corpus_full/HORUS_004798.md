# EMOTIONS OF THE PATIENT'S FAMILY IN THE EMERGENCY SERVICE: A SCOPING REVIEW

Family members of patients admitted to the emergency department are sometimes excluded from the care process of their relatives, which generates various emotional responses in the family.Objective: To describe the evidence related to the emotions of family members of patients in the emergency department.Materials and Methods: A descriptive scoping review was carried out, searching with the Decs and Mesh terms "Family", "Emotions", "Emergency Service".These terms were used in databases such as PubMed, EBSCO, Embase, VHL and Web of Science to describe the existing literature on the emotions of family members of patients in the emergency department.The publication period was limited to 2013 - 2023. Seventeen articles were obtained, identifying three thematic axes in the literature: Importance of family members in the emergency service, responses from family members that transcend emotions and needs of family members.It was found that family members' responses transcend emotions in the emergency service.Conclusions: The importance of the family's presence in emergencies stands out, influencing the quality of care.Specific research limitations and adapted tools are identified. Perceptions, Family approach, Family relationships, Acute illness, Emergency Services
