# The recovery of Avenida Jiménez and the Camino a Monserrate de Rogelio Salmona.

This Master’s thesis explores the project scale that lies between urban design and architecture, starting with an outstanding project, which was partially proposed and realized by the architect Rogelio Salmona. This internationally recognized architect was fundamentally highlighted by his architectural projects, but in this particular project he undertakes an urban work using all his understanding of architecture. The thesis works on the project from the reading of the context as both a place of commission, as an emblematic place of the city in which layers of change and transformation are revealed; on the other hand, the analysis of the project proposed by Salmona contrasts against this context to understand the projectual decisions that exist in the same one. This project proposes a new relationship between the city and its urban and geographical environment through the modifications that it is introducing to this public space of great value for the city
