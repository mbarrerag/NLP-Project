# Proposal for an investment portfolio that optimizes investment profitability in times of inflation, taking into account emerging countries in Asia and the Pacific through topological correlation methodologies for graphs and market entropy

This paper proposes an investment portfolio that optimizes the return on investment in times of inflation, under a perfect forecast scenario, taking into account emerging countries of Asia and the Pacific through topological correlation methodologies of graphs and entropy of the market. Thus we created the portfolio of investment using conventional ideas of reducing risk and maximizing profits.The main stock indices of the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and Singapore were used to calculate the entropy of the market of an emerging world region that had demonstrated economic growth and low inflation.After choosing the most representative assets of the emerging market of Asia Pacific, an investment portfolio was estimated.For the period 2020-2022, an optimization of the portfolio was carried out, which showed that almost 50% of the investment portfolio was based on the MMBM action of Malaysia. Meanwhile, the rest of the allocations was more evenly distributed, ranging from 4.46% to 7.73%. According to the findings, the financial markets of the developing countries of the Pacific have a potential of profits higher than those of the developed markets due to their exposure to the greatest economic expansion and inversion. had demonstrated economic growth and low inflation.
