# CAMPISINE MOBILIZATION, PUBLIC POLICY AND TRANSITIONS TO AGROECOLOGY IN COLOMBIA, 1990 - 2018

In recent years, especially since the beginning of the Peace Process between the Colombian government and the FARC guerrilla (Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia), an increase in peasant, indigenous and Afro-descendent mobilizations is visible throughout the national territory.In this context, it is worth analyzing the place that agroecology has begun to occupy as an important element of the different repertoires of popular mobilization. To analyze the nature of the relationship between peasant mobilizations and agroecology in a period high in the history of the country in which rural communities have had to deal with the economic, environmental and political crisis generated by the agrarian development model imposed within a context of violence to help understand the possibilities that agroecology brings with it today in the construction of technical, economic, political and cultural alternatives to the dominant political regime in Colombia. Hypothesis: the agroecology is based on the primary, economic and socio-cultural logics of the Colombian traditional communities, where it resurreves today as a practice and a living discourse to maintain and strengthen their historical forms of life
