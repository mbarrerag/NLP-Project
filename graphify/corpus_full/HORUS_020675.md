# Coupled model for the quantification of sand production using a new criterion based on a plastic damage intensity factor

Due to the weak relationship found in the models available between sand production and geomechanical behaviour, this work presents a numerical model to quantify sand production which starts from a sanding criterion focused on plastic deformations by shear as a base cause of sand production phenomenon. The model is developed under the hypothesis that sand production depends on the level of plastic deformations by shear and that sanding has geomechanical effects both in deformations and in efforts. Using cores obtained with 3D printing technology, a laboratory test program is developed to correctly characterize both the mechanical behavior and sand production of the same, results that were applied in numerical simulations at laboratory scale in order to validate the model. The results obtained show that a sand production criterion based on the plastic deformation by shear allows to predict the level of sand production, although, at high levels of effective confinement, the limit of pore collapse would also have a contribution to sanding level.
