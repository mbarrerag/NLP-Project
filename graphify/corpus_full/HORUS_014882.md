# Transition from school to university; a paradigm shift in teaching focused on autonomy

The change in the transition from school to university is really complex for new university students, a change that covers all aspects of their life, both social, economic, personal and academic. For this particular project, the focus is on the academic aspect, mainly on autonomy and understanding, aspects and variables that change drastically between the behavioral pedagogical paradigm (collegium) and the constructivist paradigm (university). Pointing, of course, that this relationship between paradigms and educational institutions is not entirely factual. This paradigm shift starts to generate challenges and academic difficulties in the new university student, of which they identify themselves; The management and organization of time, the academic burden sometimes excessive for the first semester, the adaptation to new models and methods of teaching-learning, comparison with peers. Challenges and difficulties for those who in most cases the new students are not prepared to face them, since they do not have a space where dialogue and reflection. It is like this in the Visual Expression subject, of the School of Graphic Design, of the National University of Colombia, develops this project..
