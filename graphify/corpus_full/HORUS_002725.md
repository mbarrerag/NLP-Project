# Green Banana hare extrusion process: Anti-oxidant and structural properties

One of the viable alternatives to take advantage of the green rejection banana that does not meet the quality parameters to be exported, consists of transforming it into flour, however, it is necessary to evaluate the changes in the physical and chemical properties, antioxidants and structural because these results are important inputs to support possible applications of each flour in different food products. The aim of this study was to evaluate the effect of hot air drying and the extrusion process on the physical and chemical properties, structural and antioxidant activity of the green rejection banana. To this, the pH, titrated acidity, water activity, total phenol content and antioxidant activity using ABTS, DPPH and ORAC both pulp, as well as flour obtained by dehydration with hot air (HBVD) and by extrusion of the higher PVD and pulse values were determined. As for the structure, HBVD and HBVE were determined by FTIR and Raman spectroscopy. No significant difference was found in the mean values of pH and acidity between the HBVD and the higher pulp and the sample of green banana (HBVE)
