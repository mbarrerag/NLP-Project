# CHARACTERIZATION OF COMPLETE GENOMES OF VIRUS DE BRONCHITIS INFECTIVE TO COLOMBIAN ISSILATION

In this study, sequences corresponding to complete genomes of Colombian isolates of IBV are reported for the first time, which are mostly phylogenetically close to the main vaccine strains. A sequence that presents a particular behavior is also reported, since it is grouped in different genotypes when analyzing its complete genome. The results obtained contribute to the knowledge of avian coronaviruses and contribute to the understanding of the evolution and variability of the strains of IBV that are currently circulating in the region
