# Financialization process in the Colombian pension system. A comparative analysis of the diversification of investments in assets of pension funds Protection and Future in the period 2001 - 2021

This paper presents patterns of financialization in the Colombian pension system through the qualitative and quantitative analysis of Protección and Porvenir, the two largest pension funds in the country. It focuses on studying the diversification of its investment portfolio in the period 2001- 2021 by observing the variation of the securities held, categorized according to different criteria, the analysis and application of financial indexes to the balance sheet, and the annual income statement by type of fund. It also summarizes the main changes to the Investment Regimen and the consequent adjustments in the investment policies of both pension funds. The results show that these institutions have grown more than the general economy and that the portfolios trend is speculative and riskier investments
