# Creation of companies and its relationship with economic growth: more is better?

This work analyzes the relationship between the number of companies created in a specific period and economic growth, aiming to distinguish between companies created out of necessity and those created out of opportunity. For the analysis, a panel of data is constructed using information from the 32 departments of Colombia and the city of Bogotá D.C. for the period 2013-2021. A fixed-effects model is estimated with economic growth as the independent variable and the number of companies created based on data from the Unified Business and Social Registry as the main variable of interest. The regression results show that the number of companies created out of opportunity has a positive and statistically significant impact on economic growth, while the number of companies created out of necessity has a positive effect but is not statistically significant for growth
