# TEXTILE MANAGEMENT: A MATERIAL APPROXIMATION TO ETNOGRAPHS, COURPS AND TIMES

A material approach to ethnographies, bodies and times is not just a book. In its lines, a series of concepts are woven, which come from a careful conversation with feminist authors and from the Studies of Sciences and Technologies, with the author’s deep and long empirical research. Here are knotted embroiderers, artists, ethnographers, daughters, mothers, with threads, scissors, needles and fabrics. The result is this book which is also a fabric, a textile text, a powerful and subtle reflection, immersed in a time also textile, a kaironic time of those that already exist, on ethnography, care, time, matter, body and thought
