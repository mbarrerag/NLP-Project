# COVID-19: A NEW EXAMPLE OF THE INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY PROTECTION MODEL TO SUPPLY PUBLIC HEALTH REQUIREMENTS.

In the book chapter, he analyses the whole problem arising from the pandemic that has plagued humanity and that has posed different challenges for both public health and intellectual property. The text highlights the difficulties and obstacles to overcome, bearing in mind how vital medicines, including vaccines, are public goods that should not be the object of patents and the need for effective technology transfer
