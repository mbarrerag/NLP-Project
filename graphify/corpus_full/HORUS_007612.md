# Chemical profiling of commercial Passifloras by-products extracts and preliminary proposal of a formulation with photoprotective activity

Agro-industry is crucial in Colombia due to its diversity in the processing of plant materials. Its importance has grown globally because it satisfies the demands of the market, which has also increased the generation of waste throughout the agro-industrial process, from the stages prior to production to the commercialization of the products. In a context where the circular economy is increasingly relevant, the development of alternative technologies that take advantage of these residues or by-products of agro-industry is needed. This raw material is interesting because of its low cost, great availability and the economic impact that has its final disposition, besides the reduction of the environmental impact that implies the proper management of these residues. A promising alternative is the use of these residues in growing sectors, such as the cosmetic sector, which in Colombia is constantly expanding and is positioned as a strategic country of production in Latin America. In addition, consumers value that the cosmetics are produced with natural and ecologically friendly materials (plaizc), characteristics that are fulfilled when exploiting by-products of agro-industry.
