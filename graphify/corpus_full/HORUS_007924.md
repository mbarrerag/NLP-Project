# ENVIRONMENTAL PROBLEM AND TERRITORIAL MANAGEMENT IN COLOMBIA. METHODOLOGICAL ASSISTANCE FROM SPACE ANALYSIS

This book offers three strategic inputs for decision-making: first, a diagnosis of the scale of hydrographic area, on the state of ecosystems and coverages, as well as the pressures that put them into tension, which, in turn, are explained by a particular historical trajectory, while also giving account of the emergence of effects and impacts with an impact on the processes of ecological deterioration. Second, it offers an index that reflects the susceptibility of environmental problems, that is to say, contradictions between economic activities and environmental determinants, to become environmental conflicts, which comes from the spatial analysis of the presence and combination of economic activities within each hydrographic area. Finally, it offers a strategy for identifying responses to the change scenarios, either to counteract driving forces, minimize pressures or reduce impacts. This insumo results from interest all time that it allows to approximate to the way in which the actors propose ways of managing in the ecological deterioration scenarios that can detonate or deepen conflicts. Its recognition can dinamize the articulation of instruments of management or environmental management, and the articulation between entities with concurrent competencies. Thus, it can promote the participation of other actors and the possibility of promoting of managing, in the positive forms of environmental situations
