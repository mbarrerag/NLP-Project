# TO BITE CITY FROM SALE SCHEDULES: EXPERIENCES IN THE LOCALITY OF SUBA, BOGOTA

Anyone who has walked through a Latin American city has encountered areas where the attendance of the diverse is a reality: streets full of people with variety of colors, shapes, sounds, goods, food and smells, in which there are many trades and economic activities, which constitute the form of work of millions of inhabitants of the city. Usually the habitation has been associated with the house, to the private. However, we follow Bachelard (2016) in his invitation to understand the inhabitation also in the movement, in the construction of spaces that happens not only in the housing, but also in the wandering in the street. The inhabitation allows to make a place in the world. Lxs salesmanxs make a place in the city through their work in the street, which allows them to occupy, integrate and fight in the urb. From the practice of street salesers they question the ways in which the public space discourse has privatized the street in favor of private actors.
