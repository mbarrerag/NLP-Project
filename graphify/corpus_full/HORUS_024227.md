# Effects of concrete cylinder residues used as a thick aggregate on concrete durability

This research allowed to evaluate the effects of the residues of concrete test cylinders used as a thick aggregate on the durability of the concrete, designing concrete mixtures that comply with a resistance, characteristic to be used as structural material, for two different percentages of replacement of natural aggregate by recycled aggregate and with a cement water ratio of 0.63. The resistance to compression, chloride penetration, carbonation and expansion of concrete sulfates with thick aggregate replaced 50% by recycled concrete aggregate - ACR, versus conventional concrete.
