# POLICE RESOURCE USED BY HALICTIDES IN A FOREST FRAGMENT OF ECO-TROPIC SECO RESERVE THE TRIBUNE

The Tropical Dry Forest (BST) is one of the ecosystems of the greatest importance within the tropical habitats and one of the most threatened. It is key due to the ecological characteristics that derive in unique interactions between the species that inhabit it, so its study is fundamental to understand its dynamics and develop strategies of management. The Eco-Reserva La Tribuna, located in the department of Huila corresponds to a fragment of BST dedicated to the restoration and research. It has elevations between 480 to 800 msnm and is characterized by pastures and forests in different successive states, have recorded species like Eucharis bonplandii which is in danger of extinction, Gustavia santanderiensis that is classified as a vulnerable species, Attalea butyracea that is a species of minor concern and American Curatella that is highlighted by its dominance in the vegetation. In the area were carried out samplings of bees and their polyhearous loads, where it can highlight the Halictidae family as one of the most abundant, which possesses a diversity of behaviors.
