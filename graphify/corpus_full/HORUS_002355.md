# Design from complexity for well-being in rurality: explorations from Arborean Experience

Addressing the global socio-environmental crisis – it also locally deals with both urban and rural areas – requires a complex approach that integrates design with other disciplines and with an ethical perspective that can be confronted in ways different from the dominant ones, whose failure is evident. Arborean experience, from such an approach, seeks to contribute to solving the conflict between dignified living needs for rural and city dwellers, eco-conscious habitability and the prevailing need for ecosystem care, based on the complex understanding of needs and perspectives of those involved in the problem and its solutions, and in particular, the role of host communities as caregivers of the territory. On the other hand, vision in design, method of research through design, part of the premise that the product-service systems obtain their meaning in interaction with people, whose suitability is determined by the context, key element of the complex and ethical approach from which the project is developed.From this approach and methodology, the inspiration for the proposal was the tree, a living system that promoted our ethical commitment and allowed to define an experience, which is embodied in a space of social, cultural and natural connection, from which the approach is the way of the project
