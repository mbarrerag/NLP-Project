# Organizational learning in the implementation of alternative development policies in the department of Nariño 2002-18

The organizational learning in Colombian drug policy has generated little academic interest, which is surprising to see that the successive state interventions for more than half a century of fighting against these illicit substances have not managed to stop the consolidation of Colombia as the world’s leading producer of cocaine. With an increasingly fractured international drug control regime and a gradual change in the position of the United States, a country that was until recently the main promoter of prohibitionism, drug policies in Colombia have been adapting to the double influence of international commitments and domestic policy, testing different formulas that have a common failure to control permanently the supply of cocaine. Considering that alternative development has until recently occupied a marginal place in the shadow of military efforts and forced eradication, the present study takes some of those experiences in the department of Nariño for the period 2002-18, with the purpose of determining whether government entities linked to alternative development in Colombia have produced functional mechanisms of organizational learning that favour the formulation and implementation of effective, comprehensive and sustainable public policies, based on experience and accumulated knowledge . account that alternative development had until recently occupied a marginal place in the shadow of military forces and forced eradication, the present study takes some experiences in the department of Nariño for the period 2002-18 with the purpose of determining if the governance institutions linked to alternative development in Colombia had functional organizational learning mechanisms that enabled the formulation and implementation of effective, comprehensive and sustainable public policies, based on experience and accumulated knowledge
