# Retention of human capital in CDA el Ruiz

The main purpose of this research work is to design strategies for the retention of human capital of the Center for Automotive Diagnostics (CDA) El Ruiz located in the city of Manizales, from the methodological point of view, is a research of mixed approach (qualitative-quantitative), of a propositional type, framed within an action research design, whose population to study are the workers of the CDA, taking as sample 11 workers of the CDA, to which they applied a survey, whose results allow to conclude that the strategies developed to favor the retention of the CH within the organization and to feed its competitive advantage are evidenced as effective. Likewise the theory found is consistent with the different analyses found inside the organization. .
