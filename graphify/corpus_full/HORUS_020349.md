# Characteristics of the interpersonal nurse/family relationship in ICU from the relational nursing needs

Design and validate an instrument of measurement of the interpersonal relationship between nurse(o) and family in the ICU from the relational needs of the nurse(o). Mixed study (Qualitative-Quantitative) exploratory derivative 3 stage analysis. First stage: Integrative review of literature (Phase 1) and narrative design through analysis of content of semistructured interviews to 21 nursing professionals in the ICU (Phase 2); Second stage: Operationalization of qualitative data through structuring of a nomological network and writing of items; Third stage: Validity of content (with panel of academic evaluators) and estimation of content validity, facial validity with quantitative/qualitative pilotages, construct validity by means of exploratory factor analysis and reliability through the Alpha de Cronbach and test-retest. For the evaluation panel, the participation of 8 researchers was counted; in the facial validity pilotages 20 nurses participated for each pilotage; for the analysis of exploratory content and estimation of Alpha de Cronbach participated 218. . Design and validated an instrument for measuring the interpersonal relationship between sexes and families in the ICU from the nurse's relative needs. Mixed (Qualitative-Quantitative) exploratory secondary study of 3 stages. First stage: Integrated literature review (Phase 1) and narrative design through content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 21 nursing professionals in the UI (Phase 2); Second stage: Operationalization of qualitative data through the structure with quantitative network and item written evaluation; Third stage: Content validity (with a panel of academic evaluation) and estimation of the content validity with The content validity allowed the reduction of 45 articles to 23 articles, with a global CVI of 0.9. In the qualitative test of facial validity, an Aiken's V index greater than 0.9 was obtained for each of the criteria evaluated, and in the quantitative test, a connection index between original elements and the mirror was obtained greater than 0.8. from a partial Cronbach's Alpha of 0.899. The factorial analysis allowed the extraction of 3 factors with an explanation of more than 60% of the total variance and the reliability analysis allowed obtaining a Cronbach's Alpha of 0.898 and an intraclass acquisition index for the test-request of 0.986. This indicates that the instrument has content, facial, construct validity and efficiency to be used
