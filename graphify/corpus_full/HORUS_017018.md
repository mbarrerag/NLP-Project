# Critical Calcium Magnesium and Sulphur Dilution Curves in Potato Cultivators (Solanum tuberosum L. Grupo Andigenum) Diacol Capire and Pastusa Suprema

ABSTRACT Diagnostic tools should be developed to optimize the management of calcium (Ca), magnesium (Mg) and sulfur (S) in potato crops. The research aimed to develop the critical dilution curves for Ca, Mg and S in potato (Solanum tuberosum L. Andigenum Group), establish their harvest indices and characterize the relationship between nutrients. Four field experiments were established in two cycles, in the localities of Facatativá (high fertility soils) and Chocontá (low fertility soils) in Colombia.
