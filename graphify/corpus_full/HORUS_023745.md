# RISK FACTORS IN DOMESSIC WORK AND THE NEED FOR CLAIMS IN COLOMBIA

From the interest of the Intersindical (which brings together the Union of Workers of the Domestic Service UTRASD, Sintraimagra, Sintrahin and the Association of Women Workers of the Bucaramanga Home), in the defense of the health of its partners, it was agreed to promote a work between the Environmental and Labour Health Research Group of the National University of Colombia, together with the Solidarity Center and DEFENS, in coordination with representatives of the unions, to recognize the risk factors in the domestic work and its effects on the health of the workers; as well as to propose trade union actions to promote the re-engineering of risks of domestic work and to protect the rights to health at work. It was based on a union perspective that recognized the experience of the workers as a basis of knowledge generation and that, in turn, recognizes that each worker's work plays a leading role in the defense of their health. From this approach, the project was carried out with a participatory approach that sought to recover the workers' own experience to recognize their labor process, the risk factors present in it and the impacts on their health; as well as to propose interventions to strengthen the preventive action from the workers. The report provides an account of the approach that this research has taken to the labour-risk situation of domestic workers in Colombia and an analysis of the inherent risk of domestic employment in order to establish a position in relation to the classification of risks of domestic work
