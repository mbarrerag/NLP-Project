# Positioning strategy in low-cost airlines in a competitive scenario: Trade off baggage by air with an extra cost or by land

Due to the current changes of the airlines that face the crisis caused by COVID, traditional airlines in Colombia (such as Avianca) pose the strategy of becoming a low -cost model, a competitive environment where the vast majority of companies are They find on the border of productivity and the only way to expand it is through a strategy that generates additional value to the user. Therefore, it is necessary to evaluate new differentiating strategies that generates value (considering Porter positioning strategies), expand the border of productivity and manage to exceed some system restrictions (defined by Goldratt). This is brought to the research question: which strategic option would generate more value to some stakeholders (airlines and users) of low -cost airlines in a competitive scenario, evaluating the trace off: send your suitcases via air with an envelope cost or cost land?
