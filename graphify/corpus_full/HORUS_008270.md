# Libraries as inclusive cultural spaces: reflections, challenges and opportunities

This document includes the final memories of the three components necessary to opt for the title of Magister in Museology and Heritage Management of the National University of Colombia. The first, the conceptual work, reflects on the right to access culture in library spaces and who actually access it. The second, are the memories of the practice carried out in the Public Library Julio Mario Santo Domingo where three scripts were built for the guided visits of children's audiences of this institution from pedagogical bases.
