# Research applied with a territorial approach to the project of integral formation of the student body of the National University of Colombia in Engineering for life and environment.

The wide diversity of teaching methodologies that have been implemented at professional level presents several problems that affect the level of appropriation of knowledge received in the learning process as identified in the thematic review at national and international level. The implementation of new teaching methodologies that adapt to the current context in an efficient way allows the knowledge to be transferred and appropriated in each of the members of the student community, including both the student body and the teacher. The methodology of learning is then proposed, which is a promising learning method that allows achieving a appropriation of knowledge through applied research practices leading the student to interaction with the real situations that are experienced in the industry and society, bringing with it benefits to the continuous learning of the teaching body as the objective and critical analysis of the different situations presented in the work activity, providing greater competitiveness and skill. With an approach in applied research and the contextualized practice, this project aims to promote the relevant learning experience in the industry and society, improving both the quality of teaching and the effectiveness of the pedagogy.
