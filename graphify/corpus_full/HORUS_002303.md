# Community agroecological experiences in the midst of armed conflict. Collective production of counter-histories. archives and repairing pedagogies in Darién.

Our project aims to create two podcasts, a community archive and a book article on agroecological experiences in the Darièn, based on a participatory research-action process between the seedbed Darien of the history department of the Bogotá-based national university and inhabitants of the region who have been involved in these same experiences. By involving the community as co-investigators and co-creators, we seek to resalate the processes that have allowed the surgient and strengthening of these agroecological experiences, enlighten the causes of political violence that communities have had to face, challenge hegemonic narratives that perpetuate divisions and stereotypes and highlight experiences of solidarity that can inspire more just and peaceful futures and challenge colonial logics in the production of knowledge
