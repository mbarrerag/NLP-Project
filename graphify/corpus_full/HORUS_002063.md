# Estimation of forested aerial biomass in rural Cáqueza Cundinamarca and its potential for linking to voluntary carbon markets

This project aims to identify areas with the greatest presence of forest ecosystems, (ii) estimate aerial biomass through machine learning models with field data and environmental variables derived from satellite images, and (iii) determine the potential for linking to voluntary carbon markets. With the development of the project objectives, we seek to increase knowledge about the benefits of conservation and restoration activities of ecosystems and natural resources, oriented towards financing projects or initiatives by individual government entities, international organizations or private companies . This project has three main objectives: (i) Identify areas with the great presence of forest ecosystems, (ii) Estimate aboveground biomass using machine learning models based on field data and environment-related variables from satellite imaging, and (iii) Determine the potential for engagement in voluntary carbon markets
