# PROGENTUAL ACTIONS IN MEDELLIN BARKPOOLS

The article describes Medellin's recent urban and social transformation that has had a significant impact on the media outside and within Colombia, and which attracts more and more visitors, curious and scholars from various parts of the world. This radical change can be compared to what Barcelona experienced in the 1980s, when Oriol Bohigas, responsible for urban planning in the Catalan capital, devised the slogan "Monumentalize the periphery": a motto that is well matched with the recent principles of urban planning in Medellín. The suburbs of the city are gradually modernising through a system of contemporary architectural works that, when they succeed in concatenating the different interests of citizens and visitors, multiply the possibilities of socialization of the inhabitants and improve the security of the districts: as former mayor Sergio Fajardo, promoter of various important projects, is a simultaneous process of change of skin, reason and heart
