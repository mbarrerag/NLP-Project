# Nationalization and State Security in the Island Caribbean. The Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina during the Liberal governments. 1930-1946.

This research proposal aims to analyze the process of incorporation of the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina to the Colombian nation in the context of the liberal governments that had power from 1930 to 1946, also seeks to examine, how this process of nationalization was carried out in parallel with an exercise of assurance of the island territories of the Colombian Caribbean, in the context of changes in the vision and discourse of security and defense used by the Colombian liberal governments to achieve a much more effective exercise of sovereignty.As a key precedent, it is assumed that this process of incorporation of the island territory into the Colombian nation began in 1912 with the creation of the model of administration known as the National Intendency of San Andrés and Providencia, when the territorial administration of the islands was adjusted and the rest of the administrative processes of the continental territories was similar to the army.From this background, the Spanish government will only investigate the transformation of an insipient model of state security, closely linked to the effort of nationalization of the island territories carried out in these years, with the intention of incorporating these border territories into the Colombians and imaginary.
