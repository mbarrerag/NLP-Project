# A system approach to support a methodology for designing cosmetic products formulated in the context of companies.

Managing a project for the design and development of a new chemical is a complex task at different levels. In addition to the technical challenges of the formulation and definition of the conditions of the process, the design teams must also take into account the requirements of the organization where the product design is carried out. Therefore, this research explores the organizational dimension and its importance in the design of chemicals. Through a bibliographic review, it was found that the methodologies for the design of chemicals that integrate the organizational context have not been thoroughly analyzed and are highly required. In this research, through a systemic analysis based on information collected from semi-structured interviews with experts in cosmetic sector design, the characteristics of the organizational context and its effects on the design process of products in that sector were studied. In addition, the information captured during these interviews was formalized in a paper-based expert recommendations to support the design process of cosmetic products. A tool was proposed to adapt those recommendations to the design process of specific companies. The tool is applied through collaborative workshops that allow the active participation of the design team in the evaluation of the most appropriate design process and implements the design process
