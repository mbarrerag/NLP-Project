# Technology for Industry 4.0 Training: Development of a Model Training Station for Engineers

The project "Technology for training in Industry 4.0: Development of a Training Station Model for Engineers" will be carried out in the Laboratory of Automation and Industrial Communications _x0096_ Mechatronics of the Department of Electrical, Electronic and Automatic Engineering (DEEA) of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín physically located in the M7-102. This has as main objective to adapt the teaching of industrial automation to the demands of the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4RI), equipping undergraduate and graduate students with advanced skills required in the current labor market. The specific objectives of the project include the identification and detailed design of technologies and modules that will form part of the training station. These modules will allow to simulate real industrial processes, using emerging technologies such as the IoT, robotics and artificial vision. In addition, the training station will be built and laboratory guides will be documented to standardize the practices of automation for academic courses. This project responds to the need to update the methodologies of engineering to close the gap between the academic skills and the demands of an industrial sector in rapid evolution
