# Innovation, technologies and barriers in the tourism sector in northern Caldas, Colombia

Abstract The objective of the article is to present the general results of the tourism sector of the north of the Department of Caldas and specifically an analysis related to innovation, the use of information technologies and the barriers that have been presented in this sector, based on qualitative research, with a type of descriptive and correlational study, a multivariate analysis was used and a response base of 68 direct and indirect entrepreneurs of the tourism sector of the municipalities of Neira and Salamina of the Department of Caldas was consolidated. The results show that, for the companies surveyed, innovation in tourism is fundamental to their growth and the creation of innovative experiences, as well as the use of social networks as the most effective tools for the dissemination of new tourism products. It is concluded that the absence of government support has a negative impact on the economic growth of both tourism operators and companies in the sector
