# DIAGNOSTIC EXACTIT, REPRODUCIALITY OF THE INFANT PACT PAIN PROFILE REVISED (PIPP-R) TO THE COLOMBIAN CLINICAL CONTEXT

The objective measurement of neonatal pain has allowed the development of more than forty instruments. The immaturity of the nervous system of the premature neonates becomes a challenge that requires the study of other responses from different perspectives. One of these, is the determination of brain waves that appear with the painful experience. The morphological patterns in the waves in the electroencephalograms are response to the nociceptive stimuli and correlate with the magnitude of the activity and intensity of the stimulus. Subject that establishes the response to the electroencephalogram as an important reference and as a possible standard of gold in the measurement of neonatal pain. To determine the diagnostic accuracy and reproducibility of the Premature Infant Pain Profile Revised test (PIPP-R) in the Colombian clinical context Method: A quantitative study of cases and controls, which was developed in the neonatal unit of the Hospital Meissen in Bogotá, during the months of March and April of the year 2021. Neonates were analyzed as cases and at another time were their own observation. 44 neonates hospitalized in the UCN 10. procedure and painful response of the heel perforation to obtain the sample Result: The traces were evaluated by an expert pediatric neurologist, who contrasted with video images the exact moment of the nociceptive or tactile stimulus, as the case or control; in search of the pattern described as characteristic wave of pain or absence of it. The data were analyzed applying diagnostic performance tests and it is found that when comparing the Premature Infant Pain Profile Revised (PIPP-R) test with the EEG gold test during the observation of the cases (PIPP-R) has a correct classification against the pain diagnosis of 78.38%, a sensitivity of 96.55% and specificity 12.5%. In both the observation of neonates as controls (PIPP-R) it has a correct classification against the diagnosis of pain of 56.76% a sensitivity of 57.14 % and specificity of 55.56%. The Premature Infant Pain Profile Revised (PIPP-R) test is a test that allows to diagnose neonatal pain, is highly sensitive and may be little specific
