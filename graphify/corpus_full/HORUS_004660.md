# NETHERLANDS: A RELATIONSHIP TO THE GEOGRAPHICAL DIMENSION OF SOCIAL PROTEST IN THE BOGOTA OF THE 21st CENTURY

In this article we want to explore the geographical dimension of social protest in light of the experience of the National Paro that took place in Bogotá in November and December 2019. We propose to make an approach to the various ways in which the act of protest relates to space and in particular to the city, starting from considering that this relationship can be addressed on two levels. The first, it has to do with the approach of social protest as a phenomenon that has a specific spatial behavior, that is, it presents distributions, locations and spatial patterns that can be followed, analyzed and mapped, thus allowing to answer questions such as: where do protests take place and why do they do it? What is the flow or sense of marches and which paths are used? Why is it protested more in some places than in others?
