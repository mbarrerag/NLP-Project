# Electrochemical detection of α-Pyrrolidinopenthiophenone (α-PVP) and 3,4-Methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA) by an electrochemical biosensor based on cytochrome P450 isoenzyme 2D6.

In the present study, the electrochemical response of α-pyrrolidinopenthiophenone (α-PVP) and 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA) to an electrochemical biosensor based on the CYP2D6 isoform of the cytochrome P450 bound via covalent bond to the carbon surface of serigraphised electrodes (Working electron: C, auxiliary electrode: C, reference electrode: Ag/AgCl). The biosensor was characterised by potassium ferro/ferricianide cup/cathodium and inhibition tests with quinidine. As a result, an analyte response to the electrochemical biosensor related to enzyme-sustrate activity was demonstrated, with a direct proportional response and with a linear tendency between analite concentration and cathodic peak current.
