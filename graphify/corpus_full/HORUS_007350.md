# Model of relationship between predictors and treatment in Colombian patients with rheumatoid arthritis

This project aims to perform an analysis of association between the variables present in the database of the Clinic of Arthritis Reumatoid of the Fundación Santa Fe de Bogotá, emphasizing in those descriptors of prediction and treatment in a cohort of patients diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis treated in this specialized clinic from the retrospective review of the information included in the clinical history. The population associated with the study has been that that which meets the criteria ACR 2010 for the diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis and that assists to consultation and treatment of rheumatology. The data were performed a process of grouping after cleaning and preprocessing. To this end the method of k-prototypes (k-prototypes) was used which generated four groups to which applied the Apriori algorithm as a method to find associations between the most important variables of each of the studies.
