# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN THE TRAINING OF RESEARCHERS: CHALLENGES, OPPORTUNITIES AND MORE TO THE MITOS.

This chapter examines the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on education, especially in research, analyzing its transformative potential and limitations. It presents a critical synthesis of current knowledge, questioning whether AI can generate work comparable to human. It highlights the role of AI as a tool that, without replacing creativity and human judgment, optimizes research and teaching, boosting educational innovation. Despite its potential, ethical and responsible use is essential to maintain academic integrity, promoting transparency policies and the participation of researchers to avoid excessive dependence. The chapter also highlights the benefits of AI in the training of researchers, such as adaptive learning and simulated environments, as well as tools that facilitate academic writing and improve project management, increasing research efficiency. However, ethical dilemmas remain, such as algorithmic bias and data management. In conclusion, AI promises a future of greater cooperation and efficiency in scientific research, which must be managed responsibly
