# Use of vocal techniques for the development of better sound in the French horn

In this work, the sound of the French horn is analyzed and understood from a vocal perspective, in turn, it grants a proposal for its production from exercises and techniques produced by experimentation and analysis of the different ways of voice production associated with both singing and using it in speech. It is proposed a timbrica experimentation from the encounter with extended vocal techniques such as the production of multiphonic in order to find a sound that is projected and rich in harmonics. .
