# Strategic model for the supply chain in the hospital sector. Case provider to health centers.

The supply chain in the hospital sector is fundamental to ensure the availability of supplies and services for medical care, being key to patient satisfaction, quality and safety of health services. However, the increase of logistic expenditure has become a challenge for the sector, which makes it necessary to seek innovative solutions to improve efficiency and reduce costs. The pandemic of COVID-19 has exposed further the vulnerabilities of the hospital supply chain, and has demonstrated the need to develop strategic models that allow informed and efficient decision making in the management of the chain. To address these challenges, a strategic model for decision making in the hospital supply chain was developed. The methodology used in this master's thesis follows a mainly exploratory and descriptive approach, and consists of three phases. In the first phase, a detailed analysis of the models presented in the review of previous literature, with the aim of achieving the efficiency of the research and barriers in the hospital logistics.
