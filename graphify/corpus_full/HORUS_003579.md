# DISOLUTIONS AND DILUTIONS

Some phrases, such as is water the universal solvent, does the similar dissolve the similar? Or are they like water and oil? Do they express everyday the ideas that are held about the dissolution process. In this practice some practical aspects will be addressed that allow us to better understand what it means that something is soluble in something and how the chemical nature of dissolved substances can be understood. Concentration will be expressed in different units of concentration and, to conclude, different dilutions will be prepared from a more concentrated dissolution. This will allow us to reflect on some aspects and calculations regarding the dilution process
