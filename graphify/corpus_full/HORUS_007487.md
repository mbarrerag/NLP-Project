# Strategies to address food and nutrition insecurity among children and adolescents in Villavicencio, Department Meta, Colombia

This research aims to analyze the strategies of coping with food and nutritional insecurity used by children and adolescents in Villavicencio, Meta, Colombia. The study analyzes how these strategies vary according to age and examines the gender differences in the management and perception of food resources management in the home from the perspective of children and adolescents. Two tools were used: one to capture coping strategies and another to measure food insecurity, both applied through semi-structured interviews to 62 students aged 6 to 17 years, belonging to an educational institution in Villavicencio, with socio-economic diversity. This allowed identifying perceptions and approaches to food insecurity and coping strategies used by them. The results indicate that children aged 6 to 9 years mainly depend on family support.
