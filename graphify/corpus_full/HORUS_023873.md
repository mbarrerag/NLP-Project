# INITIAL AND FINAL VALUE FOR THE SEMICLASSICAL EVOLUTION OF THE WIGNER FUNCTION

In recent years, numerical implementations suiting present computational architectures have fuelled a resurgence in the interest in phase space semiclassical approximations for the dynamics of quantum systems. In this work we present a careful treatment of the caustics emerging from the use of pairs of classical trajectories when the semiclassical propagator in the center-center representation, introduced by Dittrich et al [Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 070403 (2006)], is used to generate the dynamics of the Wigner function. Following this, and in order to implement numerical applications, we introduce an initial and final value representations (IVR and FVR) of the semiclassical propagation, showing that they lead to better performance with less complexity than the IVR and FVR using different criteria
