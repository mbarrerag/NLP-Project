# Random-location ubiquitous nanoperforating flaps: case series.

The Keystone flaps are colgajos of perforantes on island based on the vascular concept of angiotoma.By introducing modifications that include the introduction of concepts of pedicicular area and free design, the concept of flaps of nanoperforantes ubiquitous of random location is presented, being flaps on island pediculated from small areas of random location in any anatomical region, vascularly safe, with broad mobility arches, and technically simple. The concept of flaps of nanoperforantes ubicuquitas of random location is presented through a series of retrospective cases of 150 patients, without age restriction, nor anatomical location, treated between 2012 and 2021. A description of the surgical technique is made and the demographic profile, clinical presentation, characteristics of the flaps, additional procedures and postoperative outcomes were performed. 211 flaps were performed in 184 defects. The most common etiologies of the defects were tumors (30.4%) and trauma (23.3%). The majority of the flaps were considered to be of the lower and lower part of the neck (31%). ideal for solving all kinds of complex reconstructive problems
