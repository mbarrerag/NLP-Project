# PROPOSAL BY AN INDICATOR FOR THE ASSESSMENT OF THE SERVICE OF TRANSPORT INFRASTRUCTURE IN COLOMBIA AND THE ORGANIZATIONS PRESENTING IT.

Being inserted in a globalized economy, Colombia requires more and better infrastructure, both to sustain development, and to provide a better service to its users. Likewise, having a tool to evaluate the condition of the service of the infrastructure in the transport sector oriented to an intelligent supervision, would allow support to decision, planning and supervision processes, strengthening the activities of monitoring, control and sectoral management in terms of service and investment in infrastructure.This provision is a multidimensional and complex phenomenon, and therefore requires a composite indicator, which are frequently used to observe, analyze and evaluate complex phenomena not measurable directly, and to present them in a metric, enabling the possibility to compare between them.Since the transport infrastructure is diverse between countries and even in the same country, as the priorities of its management, the indicators must be different according to the level of decision making, security and accessibility, which grouping meets the needs of strategic operation, and the assessment of the infrastructure of transport in Colombia and the organizations that provide the service, it requires conceptualizing and defining variables, indicators and indices, pointing to the principles of quality, security and accessibility, which it meets the needs of strategic and the assessment. The second, in terms of Subjective Monitoring, is reviewed at the Corporate level of the providers. It evaluates the organization providing the service in its entirety (result management, prediction and risk management, and others), allowing for figures such as diagnosis and active information to be used to deal with each case. The indicators resulting from the application of this tool are not overlapping, but represent different information, reading the same variables, infrastructures and providers in each way so that they can interpret the phenomenon from different perspectives, depending on the level of the information sought. Their orientation towards risk-based supervision will impact both on the optimization of resources, as well as on the sectoral improvement and condition of the provision of the service of the country's infrastructure in the long term
