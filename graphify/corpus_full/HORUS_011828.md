# Multiagent Control of Autonomous Vehicles in Presence of Non-Cooperative Agents using Game Theory.

This thesis proposes a solution to the problem of autonomous driving of vehicles in a road environment, specifically in the presence of vehicles driven by agents with selfish decisions and aggressive maneuvers. The controller tries to solve the problem of optimization based on a Predictive Control of Model (MPC). Taking advantage of the previous technique of prediction of trajectory, the controller uses it to better predict the position of the neighbors and plan their path. In addition, the predictive model can solve the problem of optimal control by complying with the restrictions of safety, avoid obstacles and achieve its main objective. The optimal control problem has nonconvex restrictions due to the mixed integer variables on which it is based. By creating nonlinear MPCs that can deal with the problem of hybrid variables, we seek to solve the problem of driving vehicles against aggressive and non-cooperative decisions for the network. In addition, all the agents of the system can be controlled by means of the creation of the local controllers based on \textit{Theory of Games}. We analyze two methods to find an optimal solution: centralized and decentralized.
