# Effect of seropositiveness to bovine leukosis virus on the presence of subclinical mastitis and persistent lymphocytosis in specialized dairy herds in 3 high tropic regions of Antioquia.

Enzootic bovine leukosis (EBL) mainly affects cattle having an effect on their immune system by generating persistent lymphocytosis (PL), this disease is produced by the bovine leukosis virus (BLV), which belongs to the retroviridae family. The BLV is widely distributed around the world with eleven genotypes scattered in all geographical regions, with areas severely affected and others where eradication has been achieved. In the last study carried out in Colombia, a molecular prevalence of 63% was found in cattle, having presence of genotypes 1, 3 and 6 within the national territory. The infection by BLV is also associated with an increase in the count of somatic cells in milk, which happens especially in cows with more than four lactations, as well as the association with recurrent cases of subclinical mastitis. The increase in somatic cell counts is also an indicator of clinical and subclinical mastitis. Due to the limited information that is available in this infection in the country and its effects at both productive and sanitary level is important to establish a result of the effect of the disease BLV
