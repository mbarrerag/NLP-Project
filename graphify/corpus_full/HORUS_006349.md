# Dual structure in statistical models on warped products

This document is a geometric approach to statistical models and their structure, defined on differentiable varieties whose points are probability distributions together with a Riemannian metric (M, g) and in particular for the variety formed by the set of positive signposted measures on I=1,2,...,n} and Fisher’s metric. We highlight Fisher’s metric as a central object of study in information geometry as in [1, Amari] and [2, Jost]. The classification of statistical models, will be associated with the grouping of probability distributions in the exponential family and the mixed family, giving rise to dual and mutually free torsion connections as a dual structure. which in turn determine the Amari-Centsov tensor which defines statistical variety as a structure (M,g,T).
