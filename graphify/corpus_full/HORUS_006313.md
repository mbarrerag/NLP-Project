# 16th century Shamans: History of the Cosmopolitical Ritual in Conquest

This thesis presents the exhaustive study of shamanistic ritual manifestations in indigenous cacical societies from the documentary sources of the Hispanic conquest of New Granada in the sixteenth century. It contains an emerging definition of shamanism based on the conceptual elements that can be drawn from the documentation employed, problematizing this theoretical construction in function of the Iberian religious mentality of the sixteenth century, the influence of the popular culture under the medium-sized and the processes of removing idolatry in other regions of Hispanic America in order to approximate the chamanic mentality that came to the encounter of Iberians and that in the present is demonstrated as conditioning the social structure, political hierarchization, ecological management and the daily life of the indigenous peoples in the cacical societies during the conquest of New Granada. . This these presents an exhaustive study of the traditional ritual manifestations in indigenous rather than societies based on documenting the Hispanic conquest of New Granada in the 16th century
