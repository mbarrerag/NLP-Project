# THE ANALOGUE CRISIS TO PLAN AN ARCHITECTONIC OR URBAN PROJECT AND IDEAS ON THE PROJECTUAL PROCESS IN DESIGNING AND ARCHITECTONIC AND URBAN COMPOSITION

The analogical thought to propose an architectural or urban project Arq Camilo Augusto Muñoz Chain Faculty of Arts, School of Architecture and Urbanism Universidad Nacional de Colombia camunoz@unal.edu.co ABSTRACT The analogical thought allows to establish direct relations between the project of architecture or urbanism and other non-disciplining aspects in a direct way. These relations, seek to organize the project mind to propose and define the proposals from a spatial field oriented from the most general and structuring aspects to those of lower hierarchy. PALABRAS KEY Analogy, analogic thinking, project, spatial organization, hierarchies. 1. PROPOSED. Traditionally, in the schools of architecture and urban planning, the development of the projects has been directed from an area in which intuition has had a leading role. In general, after giving certain initial directions to the students as they can be the characteristics of the place and the program of spaces and areas required to carry out their work, it starts a process in which the student goes through a path from his own assessments to the possibilities that the right hemisphere (intuitation, perception, sensations, etc.) provide some tools to follow by the students. National of Colombia camenoz@unal.edu.co ABSTRACT The project process is the fundamental basis of the project in architecture and urban planning. However, very often this project has not been properly referenced, or has simply not been taken into account in the interest of the expectation generated by the result of the project. However, it is of paramount importance to understand the enormous importance of the process in terms of learning. KEY WORDS Project process, the process as learning, stages of the project process, organization of project ideas. 1. PROPOSAL. Generally in the schools of Architecture and urban planning, a broad interest has been placed in the result of the project. Although it is true that this result is of capital importance for the correct living of the people in the buildings we construct, it is of great importance to understand that the student's learning is not in the result of the project. On the contrary, learning is carried out when the student is aware of the project process carried out in the development of the exercise.
