# Nursing care for the person with impaired continence

This report summarizes the activities carried out during the internship at the University of Sao Paulo, Brazil, the development of this one was carried out in different services of enterostomal therapy both public, of agreement and private, in the City of Sao Paulo and Curitiba, where patients with deterioration of the continence and/or dysfunction of the pelvic floor are treated. The internship was carried out in a period of 5 weeks, beginning on 25 April 2022 and culminating on 26 May 2022. The methodology applied consisted in previous programming in the services, later assistance to these, where review of assessment formats scales and other documents applied in the care of the person with deterioration of the continence, in turn accompanying the professionals during the assessment and management, finally assistance and realization of evaluation as previously learned; the above recorded in a daily field in which the activities performed daily were more fully realized.
