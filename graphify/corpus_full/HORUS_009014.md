# EPISTEMOLOGICAL STORY

Various authors have shown that a historical approach to chemistry teaching can be complemented from a view of the philosophy of science. This allows us to open the panorama of understanding of what it means to make chemistry and thus address aspects of this discipline as a dynamic company in constant change and evolution. This is why this article presents a philosophical interpretation of the history of Organic Chemistry from the positions of Larry Laudan. To achieve this objective, an analysis of the classical models of scientific change (Kuhn, Lakatos) is carried out as a background to the model of Research Traditions (TI) of Laudan. Once the generalities of this model are exposed, the formal definition of Coordination Theory is presented as an IT that guides the discipline that is the subject of the study; in such a way that it is possible to develop an analysis at the macro-theoretical level, in terms of Laudan, of that tradition. Finally, some comments are presented in the form of conclusions and perspectives for future reflections
