# Strengthening the capacity of the Agricultural Business and Agricultural Economic Research Seed to apply qualitative research techniques

The resources financed by this call will be used to train the members of the seedbed of research in agribusiness and agrarian economy in the application and analysis of qualitative research techniques. This accompaniment will be done in a group and personalized way in each of the works carried out by the students and professors. This will enhance the competencies of the members of the seedbed in the qualitative analysis and improve the quality of the work carried out
