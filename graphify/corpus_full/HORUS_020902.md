# Proposal for the design and evaluation of complete streets. Case applied: Avenida Santander, Manizales.

A misplanning of mobility in cities has generated the proliferation of negative externalities related to the congestion of vehicles, emission of greenhouse gases and air pollution, increase in road accidents, decrease in the quality of life, decrease in the physical activity of people, social injustice, among many others.The paradigms of planning based on mobility, which are focused on the efficiency of mobility in private modes of transport (cars and motorcycles) have generated to a large extent the problems mentioned above. Currently, a change in the paradigm of planning focused on accessibility is sought, understood how the potential to reach the opportunities offered by the city in a fair and responsible way, especially from sustainable modes of transport such as public transport, walking or bicycle.The concept of complete streets are an opportunity to prioritize the active mobility, the transport and the public health through the redesign of the road profile, thus generating the decrease of negative externalities derived from the poor management of mobility.This project aims to estimate the impact of the introduction of a complete street design on the Santander Avenue social outcomes (the impact of the mobility without the contabulity of the road profile, will be considered to be based on the benefits of the negative externalities of the mobility
