# The presence of Erasmus in New Granada

Culminate the project LATINOS COLONIALES MANUSCRITES IN THE NEW GRANADA (1561-1809) I: RAZONED AND SCIENTIFIC CATALOGUE, within which the attention was drawn to the heritage books that contained works by Erasmus of Rotterdam or that were related in some way to this important figure of the Renaissance, we propose to develop a project that specifically deals with the presence of Erasmus in the bibliographical backgrounds inherited from colonial times, currently deposited in the National Library of Colombia (hereinafter BNC).This project contemplates as research activities, first, the complete inventory of erasmyan sources deposited in the BNC; second, the bibliographical and/or co-diological description of the sources; and, to conclude, the historical reconstruction of the use and circulation of those sources in the New Granada. To fulfill these purposes, we will also use the tools of modern bibliocology; and, we will guide ourselves by the guidelines of the cognitive description; finally, supported by the data that provides us with the necessary historical and historical framework, and from the perspective of the social culture
