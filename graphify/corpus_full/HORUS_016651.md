# Adoption of information and communication technologies in Colombian e-government projects: Enabling factors and inhibitors.

This research thesis identified a problem that points to the difficulty of making clear decisions and with support for the adoption of ICT in e-government projects, so the purpose of this research is to propose a group of enabling factors and inhibitors that can support the adoption of ICT in e-government projects in Colombia. To identify and propose them, an exploratory mixed sequential type methodology was used consisting of three phases 1. Literature review; 2. Validation with users; 3. Validation with experts. Giving as results in each phase: First, following a systematic review of literature 32 enabling factors and 29 inhibitory factors for the adoption of ICT in e-government projects. Second, the presence of these factors was examined, from the point of view of the needs and requirements of users in a Colombian e-government project. Third, the correct definition, relevance and impact of factors for the adoption of ICTs was verified; supported by the expert judgement in a Colombian e-government project. As a main conclusion, the factors identified allow for the adoption of information and communications technologies (ICT) taken from the e-government project
