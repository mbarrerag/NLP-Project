# UN For Colombia. Primary care in oral and communication health for indigenous children and peasants of the department of Tolima.

The mouth is an expression of social status. Among the population of Colombia, rural communities have poor conditions of oral and communicative health, situation that prevents to develop autonomously and relevant their life projects (MinSalud, 2014). From our social responsibility as University, and for two decades, the UN project for Colombia has provided care in health to peasant and indigenous communities of several regions of the country (Amazonas, Vaupés, Guaviare, Guajira, Chocó, Cundinamarca, Boyacá, Meta, Vichada and Tolima). Specifically, in this last department has interacted with indigenous Pijao del Vergel-Calarma, municipality of Ortega; and with peasants of Tierradentro, municipality of Lebanon. In this interaction, we have identified their social, discal and dislexia reality, in particular difficulties in oral and communicative health. On the first one, we have highlighted the high index of Cariados, Obturados and Perdidos teeth (COP), periodontal diseases and dental pains. In this way, we have identified their social and discalcular health problems, and difficulties, and there are learning and difficulties. This right is related to the requirement of health care to companies that manage and provide health services, which is a reason for generating routes of demand and the elimination of barriers to access to health care. In the school environment, the aim is to place this topic in the educational projects of the Rural Schools -ER-; that is, to incorporate oral and communicative health care as a daily school activity directed by teachers. the institutions are: er el vergel, in vergel-calarma (ortega), and er luis flórez, in tierradentro (lebanon). There will be oriented the strengthening of the infrastructure for oral health and hygiene (cepillers, washbasins, water filters). Likewise, care habits in vocal and auditory hygiene in classrooms will be promoted. In addition, it will be aimed to train community agents in oral and communicative health for the sustainability of the process. In the school will be carried out activities of promotion and prevention: plaque control, application of flúor, teaching of oral hygiene and prophylaxis. In addition, the strategy of teaching and school-based care will be developed for the self-care of the development of speech and prevention of these activities
