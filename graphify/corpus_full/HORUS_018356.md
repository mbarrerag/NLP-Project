# FINAL WORK AND GRADE THESIS MAESTRY IN EDUCATION ANALYTICAL SUMMITS 2018-2020

A very important feature of this third volume is that the theses advanced by teachers from the departments of Arauca, Casanare and Guaviare are summarized. The Institute of Education Research managed to attract the interest of the education secretariats of these departments to link teachers in practice in the process of master's training. For this reason, readers will find research works from institutions located in municipalities such as Arauquita ¿ Arauca, Aguazul ¿ Casanare and El Returnno ¿ Guaviare, or from institutions in rural areas, close to one another and other remote from their respective capitals. Likewise, they will find research carried out in educational institutions in Bogotá and Neiva
