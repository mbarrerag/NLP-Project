# Modeling of a conical crusher for optimisation of the operation of a stone aggregate plant.

Modeling for design and simulation is a tool that has acquired relevance in the academic and industrial field, due to the high availability of specialized computational packages. For the specific case of the modeling of the grinding process in the mineral benefit, the variables to control are those related to the mechanical configuration of the equipment and to the minerological characterization of the material. In this work, the process of conical crushing of a plant of production of pétre aggregates using artificial neural networks was modeled and simulated. An artificial neural network with topology “Feedforward” was adopted to simulate the process. In this work, different network configurations were tested with 50 laboratory tests that reported size data at the entry and output of the shredder, seeking to optimize the middle quadratic error, the dispersion of the error and the linear adjustment of the data, allowing the selection of the network with output data more adjusted to the real ones. The selected model was a combination of neurons of 70 - 40 in the hidden layers, obtaining a mean quadratic error of 6.24, a model of error and linear adjustment of the minimum data of 35.05 and a linear data was selected
