# ABECTE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF RESOLUTION 2492 OF 2022 AND RESOLUTION 810 OF 2021

The aim is to provide practical, easily understood information about the nutritional and frontal labelling requirements for food packaged or packaged for compliance with the standard, as well as processed and ultraprocessed foodstuffs. This ABC does not contain binding interpretations of the scope of Resolutions 2492 of 2022, 810 of 2021 or additional obligations than those provided for in them
