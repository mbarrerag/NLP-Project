# UNAL 2.0 Triadic Ballet.

The research-creation project Ballet Triádico UNAL consists in carrying out the reconstruction and staging of the work Das Triadisches Ballett de Oskar Schelemmer (1922) - known as the work of total art by the perfect integration of various artistic disciplines in its conception -, using it as an excuse to carry out an inquiry on pedagogy in the arts and interdisciplinarity. The intention is not the faithful reproduction of the original work but, better, its reinterpretation and adaptation to the Colombian context, trying to recognize and exalt our cultural heritage in mestizoje with the legacy of modern art of the Bauhaus and its pedagogical philosophy. It is, then, an opportunity to investigate the materials, techniques and representative elements of various rich cultural regions such as the Amazonia, the Orinoquía and the Pacific. The very nature of the work implies that the project is developed by a diverse group of students, teachers and graduates belonging to all the areas of the Faculty of Arts (Artes Plásticas; graphic design and industrial; Cinema and Television; and Music) there they found their place and nourished from the end of the process, knowledge and experience of the various stages of the different phases of as artists with the materials and materials of creation, in a critical, proactive attitude and of socio-environmental responsibility, as it is merited by the current global moment. In this second version the construction of the costume will be connected with the principles of environmental sustainability and durability, so that we can form a lasting and transformable collection with the passage of time. It will be, then, scrutinizing in our roots, writing a script, designing some characters, composing a music of its own, building a costume and a stage design, dancing, recognizing our body that appropriates the space in a staging; as we will inquire about new plastic and material possibilities, sustainable and innovative, to give life for the second time to this work. The Triádic Ballet, rather than a dance, is a critical, pedagogical and creative exercise that, without thinking too much, goes directly to experimentation and reaffirms the power of the joint and collaborative work, taking advantage of the differences, particularities, specialities and coincidences. Therefore, the project will be developed by maintaining this methodology of experimental and interdisciplinary workshops.
