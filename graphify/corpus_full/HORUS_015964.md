# Strategic-administrative model for organizational transformation of traditional university libraries into Learning and Research Resource Centers (CRAI)

The University Libraries (BU), considered as areas of support in the Higher Education Institutions (IES), are organizational units that evolve and that must be transformed at the same speed with which changes in the ecosystems of value that compose the processes of higher education are presented.The transformation of the traditional University Libraries into Resource Centers for Learning and Research (CRAI), where the users of the university community find the democratization of knowledge and learning, poses several challenges within which the strategic-administrative structure that underlies and must be fulfilled is highlighted, so that the transformation that is carried out to the goal planned for the benefit of the university community and the users of the components of the system is achieved.The problem that arises, then, is the answer to the question: How to define a strategic-administrative model that allows the organizational transformation of traditional libraries and the services for learning and research (CRAI) centers and services to be carried out? This work shows the main findings found in the theoretical and practical search of the strategic-administrative elements that should guide the transformation of the University Libraries in the Research Centers and the management.
