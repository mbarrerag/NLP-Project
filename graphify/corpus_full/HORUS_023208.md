# BIBLETICS HAVE VALUE: PERSPECTIVES FROM ECONOMIC ANALYSIS

This book describes the application of the Quotating Assessment Method (MVC) for the estimation of the economic value that citizens give to the 26 public libraries of the Medellin System for the enjoyment and use of their services, as well as for their very existence, the choice to use them and the desire to bequeath them to other generations, noting the importance that citizens attach to these cultural facilities and the recognition of the impacts that public libraries generate on the levels of well-being and quality of life of the population
