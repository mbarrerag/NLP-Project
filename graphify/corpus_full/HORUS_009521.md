# METHODOLOGIES FOR EVALUATION OF ENERGY EFFICIENCY IN UNIVERSITY BUILDINGS: LITERARY REVIEW.

Various factors, as well as the use and type of activities that are developed in buildings, have an important role in their efficiency and energy behaviors. Consideration and knowledge of these factors, uses and activities are necessary to propose actions that help improve their energy performance and apply integrated solutions to their needs. Various methodologies are used to evaluate the behavior and energy efficiency of these. However, for university buildings conventional methodologies need to be adjusted for their correct application. This contribution presents a literary review on methodologies for the evaluation of energy efficiency in university buildings, which was carried out using a methodology oriented towards bibliographic and bibliographical analysis, respectively. The review allowed to conclude that methodologies for this purpose correspond mainly to audits, modelling and simulation, comparative evaluations, and advanced measurements
