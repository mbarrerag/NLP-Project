# PROGRESS IN THE GENETICAL IMPROVEMENT OF DIPLOID PAPA OF THE PHUREJA GROUP IN COLOMBIA

The diploid potato grown in Colombia (8000 to 10000 ha/year) corresponds to the species Solanum phureja, reclassified as Solanum tuberosum Grupo Phureja, is commonly known as "papacriolla". In Bolivia, Peru and Ecuador is known as "papa chaucha". The predominant type in production and in the market of the country corresponds to the phenotype egg yolk (YH), which presents round tuber, with skin colour and intense yellow meat uniform. The program of genetic improvement of potato of the National University of Colombia (UNC), Bogotá, maintains a collection of work of 110 genotypes of this group of potatoes, which was formed in principle (1993) with materials of the Colombian Central Collection of potato that administered the Colombian Agricultural Institute and, that the time, was increased by collections in fields of farmers, this was characterized molecularly and was eliminated duplicates. The UNC improvement program has registered in 13 varieties diploid, eight of the first registered phenotype YH and, five of different phenotypes, four of phnotypes and subtypes
