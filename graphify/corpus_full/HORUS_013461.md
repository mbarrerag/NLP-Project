# A phenomenological approach to Obsessive Compulsive Disorder

Psychiatry is going through what, in current debates, has been called “the crisis of psychiatry”. One of the causes of the crisis of psychiatry has to do with the fact that it has not successfully addressed psychiatric phenomena from a first person perspective. While many approaches to psychiatric phenomena have offered third person perspectives (such as reductionist approaches, biopsychosocial approach, descriptive approaches, or valuing models), these perspectives leave aside the way in which people experience or live psychiatric phenomena. The Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder has not been alien to this crisis.
