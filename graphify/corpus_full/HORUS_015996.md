# Contribution to the standardization of a plant extract with an ethnopharmacological history of antimalarial activity, through phytochemical evaluation and biological activity.

Using the ethnopharmacological criterion, five plants were pre-selected with reports of antimalarial activity: Cecropia metensis Cuatrec, Cecropia membracea Trécul, Verbena littoralis Kunth, Ambelania duckey Mark and Curarea toxicofera Wedd Barneby & Krukoff. Plant material was collected in different departments of Colombia, conditioned and subjected to etanolic extraction by exhaustive percolation. In the first stage the etanolic extracts of the five pre-selected plants were evaluated in vitro, versus Plasmodium falciparum strain FCR-3 (resistant chlorokine).The etanolic extract of C. toxicofera presented the best antiplasmodial activity (CI50=7.6 ± 3.9 μg/mL) and the results of living activity were detected alkaloid. The antiplasmodial activity of the other evaluated species was of inactive. The results of the isolated compound were established as isochondodendrin. IC50 of the samples evaluated by the LDH method versus P. falciparum strain W2 (resistant to chloroquine) were: EtOHCt (5.25 ± 1.53 μg/mL), FrDCM1 (4.04 ± 0.83 μg/mL) and FrDCM2 (dissolved to P. falciparum strain W2 (resistant to chloroquine) and FrDCM2 (dissolved to P. ± 0.66 μg/mL).
