# Hemodynamic physiology in spatial analogues. Narrative review

The advent of a new spatial era has motivated a new era of research. In this segment, the hemodynamic physiology has been of special interest due to the potential risks of space travelers. However, the research in space presence large limitings that can be overcome by spatial analogues. This narrative review seeks to explain the main changes of cardiac output, systolic volume, arterial pressure and central venous pressure in the different spatial analogues, such as Bed Rest, Dry Immersion, Tilt Test, Parabolic Flight and short arm centrifugal. The methodology used was based on the central venous pressure and the beat volume. In general, the changes found are related to fluid dynamics, where there is a cephalization of fluids in microgravity and the distribution of fluids downwards in hypergravity. In microgravity there is an increase in cardiac output, of the central venous pressure and of the beat volume. another type of review looks to improve future research in the field of space medicine
