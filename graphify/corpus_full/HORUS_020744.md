# Relationship between technical flaws in the construction completion stage and unskilled labour

In recent years, the companies dedicated to the construction of buildings in Colombia have pointed out difficulties that the workers present, considered as unskilled labour. Through a case study, this research has managed to determine that the operational and administrative staff that supervises the activity of finishes in the light system is not previously able to execute their specific tasks; consequently, the company is granting responsibility to a human capital that has no relation to the work of the light system, leaving the development of the works in presumption of experience or empirical training that has probably not been given rise to. Based on an approach based on innovation, an analysis is proposed on the management of knowledge and technical skills from the administrative line such as the high and middle controls, to the knowledge and technical training of the operators in light system. The work was based on a qualitative approach, starting from the case study, whose context was the administrative scheme of a company dedicated to the chapter of finishes in light system. light system. It is specified that this research will not only contribute to the sector, but also to the Master in Construction of the National University of Colombia, as a reference for future research in the line of administration and management in construction
