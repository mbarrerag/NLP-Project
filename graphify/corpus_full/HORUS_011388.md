# Intimate and installed: Domestic affections at the meeting of 45 National Artists' Salon 2019

Intimate and Installed Domestic thesis effects at the rendezvous of the 45th Artists’ National Salon 2019. It is a research theses from the Masters on Social Studies at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia which is the result of the outcome objective is to place itself from the approach of the impact theory to identify how, throughout this rendezvous Fostered before the 45th Artists’ National Salon that take place in Bogota in 2019, the author performives a repetition or intensity that aim at managers belonging to the domestic space. The structure there propose only advantages, within the territory to take into account, the aim of the challenge forms of domesticity: time, places, and mine. In front of the 45 National Artist's Salon held in Bogotá during 2019, the author perceives a recurrence or intensity that points to ways of the domestic sphere. The proposed structure is composed of three sections that point to three forms of domesticity: time, spaces and myths. Each of these sections in turn seeks to develop some categories of affective theory that allow linking artistic projects installed in the salon with intimate experiences of the researcher's domestic life. In terms of research, this project also seeks to make writing a tool that allows him to review the works as much as to investigate traces or marks inscribed in his particular experience to notice, from there, threads of link with the collective experience and contemporary expressiveness
