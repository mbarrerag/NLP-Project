# Why are we still poor in Colombia?: perspective from fiscal control to public policies

This book offers a detailed analysis of the persistence of poverty in Colombia, addressing the issue from the perspective of fiscal control and public policies. It is composed of five chapters that examine various key aspects. The first chapter explores the relationship between poverty, inequality and economic development, underlining the importance of a multidimensional measurement. The second chapter analyzes the impacts of the pandemic and historical structural inequalities. In the third chapter, it addresses intergenerational poverty, with a focus on health and education. The fourth chapter examines labour informality and the role of social programs in poverty reduction. Finally, the fifth chapter discusses new forms of inequality, such as the digital divide and tertiary pathways. Each chapter offers a deep approach to these topics, combining theoretical analysis, case studies and evidence-based proposals to improve the effectiveness of public policies and promote more equitable development. -- Contracadas
