# Estimated current demand and potential for water for agriculture in a lowland tropical humid forest area

The aim of this document is to present the evolution of water demand in the agricultural sector, as well as the development of crop areas and the impact of climate change. To this end, hydrological modelling is used through Qswat software on the QGIS platform. The focus is on estimating water demand for agriculture in the study area, which partially covers the departments of Santander, Antioquia, Bolívar, Cesar and Norte de Santander in Colombia. The main purpose is to provide data on water consumption, given that there is currently insufficient evidence in the field. In this modeling, various input data are used, such as precipitation, flow, temperature, wind speed and solar radiation measurements of IDEAM. In addition, the areas of soil use obtained from Google Earth Engine through Landsat images, as well as the map of soil type and the digital elevation map.
