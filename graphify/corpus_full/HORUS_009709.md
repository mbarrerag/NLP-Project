# NEW GRANADA AND CADIZ AFTER 1810. POLICE LAW, INFRACTIONS PROCEDURE AND CONSTITUTIONAL LIMIT IN THE PANAMA PROVINCE

It is proposed that the judicial control media of the old Spanish regime in Hispanic America were incorporated into modern constitutionalism, also in Hispanic America, but in certain places where the Constitution of Cadiz was in force, through the recourse of violations of the constitution, which is, in practice, an early form of popular action or action of unconstitutionality
