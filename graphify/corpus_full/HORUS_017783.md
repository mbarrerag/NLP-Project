# THE MAIN CURVATURA THEOREM AND EUCLIDEAN COMPLETE HYPERSUPERFICIES

In this talk we will present some of the recent results obtained by the authors on the Main Curvature Theorem and its applications to the study of the curvature of full hypersurfaces oriented immersed in the Euclidean space with constant mean curvature (CCM) and, more generally with constant upper-order mean curvature.
