# THE EFFECTS OF MICROCRYSTALLINE CELLULOSE AND SODIUM BICARBONATE. TREATMENTS ON STARCH COMPOSITES

Thermoplastic starch films (TPS) reinforced with microcrystalline cellulose (MCC) emerge as one of the most promising alternatives to be used as pa kaging materials. Evaluating the effect of the addition of fibers as well as the addition of compatibilizers that improve the adhesion to the polymeric matrix is a key factor to understand the applications of these materials. This study focuses on the determination of tensile strength and elongation, water vapor permeability (WVP), and hydrophobicity by contact angle of films based on achira and plantain starches, as well as the analysis of their applicability in the industry
