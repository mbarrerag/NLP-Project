# MECHANISMS AND STRATEGIES FOR MANAGEMENT OF DANGER AND ELECTRIC RISK

The electrical safety is increasingly a concern of own and strange concepts since it is practically impossible to carry out any type of activity without the mediation of this type of energy.In Colombia this topic is especially important since the measures of prevention of electrification or electrocution are increasingly robust and are addressed from different disciplinary looks.On the one hand, there are professionals with training in electrotechnics and, on the other hand, professionals in prevention of occupational risks or of Safety and Health at Work (SST). In both cases, managing this type of risk requires a high level of identification and communication of the dangers since the electrical power is not used, unlike other types of energy (thermal, pneumatic, mechanical etc.), cannot be observed with ease. In general terms, what is evidenced in the organizations of the country are related effects of the use of the same.
