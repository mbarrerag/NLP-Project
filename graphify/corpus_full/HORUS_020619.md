# Empowerment, participation in decision-making in health and quality of life in older women with cancer

The population of older adults has increased considerably in recent years, as well as the prevalence of cancer in this population. To design and evaluate a psychological intervention that empowers older adults with cancer regarding their participation in decision-making on their health and improve quality of life indicators. Multiple case study with 3 older adults with cancer in Colombia, developed in three phases: diagnosis of empowerment constructs and quality of life through the application of two instruments and an interview, the second phase is the design and implementation of the intervention and the last phase is the evaluation of the involved constructs Results: Through the standardized instruments, an increase in the empowerment constructs (difference between pre- and post application in the three participants of 19, 22 and 26 points) and quality of life (difference between pre- and post implementation in the three participants of 4, 9 and 3 points) as well as in the semi-structure interviews conducted to the participants Conclusion: The process of empowerment contributes to the elderly with cancer obtaining control over their health, as well as on the decisions concerning their incapacity, and in the of the intervention constructs Results: Through the standardized instruments, an increase was found in the constructs of empowerment (differentity between pre and post application in the participants of 19, 22 and 26 points) and quality of life (differentity between pre and post application in the participants of 4, 9 and 3 points) as well as in the semi structured interviews made to the participants Conclusion: The empowerment processes contributions to the older women with cancer gain control over their health, as well as over the decisions concerning it, which influences the individual's quality of life
