# CITIZEN CABILDO ON EUTANSIA: SOCIAL PARTICIPATION IN HEALTH AND CITIZEN TRAINING

The article describes the implementation of the citizen lobby on euthanasia in children and adolescents in Colombia. An exploratory qualitative study of the type of research-action-participative was carried out. In the first phase, three panels were formed: trainer, involved and citizens, and two stages of deliberation were carried out: formative and public. In the analysis phase of content (oriented by questions addressed to citizens), the mini-public involved was characterized. The transformation of the opinion of the participating citizens and their deliberative capacities is highlighted as central findings, which enabled to account for the effectiveness of the methodology to facilitate citizens to formulate consensus recommendations on complex issues and contribute to training processes in democracy and social participation in health
