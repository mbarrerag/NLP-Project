# Deep vein thrombosis after bimaxillary orthognatic surgery. Case report

Venous thrombotic disease is one of the main fatal complications in inpatients and in operated patients. It has two presentations: deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and pulmonary thromboembolism (PTE). DVT is a condition in which a blood clot usually develops in the veins of the legs. The incidence of PDV in maxillofacial surgery has been poorly reported and is considered uncommon. to describe case of PDV in a young patient who underwent orthognatic surgery, in order to highlight the use of non-oral contraceptives as a possible risk factor for the development of this unusual complication following cranialmaxillofacial surgery. Clinical case: 24-year-old female patient with diagnosis of class II dental anomaly, undergoing bimaxis orthognatic surgery + menthoplastia with prior subcutaneous anti-conception with Etonogestrel 68 mg after 3 years ago.
