# Design and implementation of an IHM using the Axon Builder 3 software located in the Cabrester substation to monitor and operate reconnectors located at different geographic points through the IEC 61850 standard

This document describes the uni-functional diagrams and the communications architecture of the electrical distribution network to understand the way these equipment operate in the field, as well as the different signals of each of them, which were taken into account for the integration of equipment and the operation and importance of such equipment for the proper functioning of the network. In this implementation it was priority to present optimal solutions for the needs presented by the client, so that their automation and control requirements were met. In addition, the signaling and control tests were carried out directly with the equipment in the field; the modifications relevant to the original design were made, to ensure satisfactory behaviour of the IHM .
