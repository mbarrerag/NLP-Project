# Critical factors of success in implementing sustainability strategies in construction companies in Bogotá, Colombia

The construction industry is one of the main drivers of the current global climate crisis, being urgent to incorporate sustainability not only in construction projects but in the same companies that carry them out. In the city of Bogotá, as well as in other cities of developing countries, it is still scarce and deficient the implementation of sustainability strategies from the corporate level in construction companies. Since different external and internal factors condition the success in its implementation, the interest emerged to evaluate the critical factors of success in the implementation of sustainability strategies in construction companies in the city of Bogotá.
