# Tropical dry forest spatial dynamics linked to socioeconomic drivers in the ancestral territory of the indigenous peoples of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta (Colombia) during the period 1990-2022

For this research proposal, consolidated data from the period 1990 to 2022 will be obtained to analyse quantitatively the spatial temporal dynamics of changes in the tropical dry forest, which could be linked to events of the armed conflict as socioeconomic drivers of deforestation. Currently, these changes can be evidenced in the fragmentation of the landscape, which generates a disconnection and a tendency to reduce biodiversity, food sovereignty and water bodies at the local level. However, at the global level, the disturbances and disturbances in the tropical dry forest negatively impact the socio-economic dynamics of society in general, by widening the consequences of climate change. The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, as a study area, is not only a mountainous complex with the highest altitudinal gradient close to the sea, but is also the sacred space of four indigenous peoples that play a fundamental role in its protection.
