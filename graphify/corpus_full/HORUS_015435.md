# SOCIOECONOMIC IMPACT OF PANDEMIA SARS-COV-2 (COVID 19) ON THE PROPERTY OF EQUIPMENT OF WORK AND THE WELFARE OF THEIR ANIMALS, COLOMBIA 2021.

The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic (Covid 19) generated a large impact on the global economy and triggered one of the biggest crises in the last 100 years.In countries with low indicators of development, work equidae play an important role in the socioeconomic sector, favoring the food security of the communities.For this reason, animal welfare is a fundamental component, seeking to improve its level, including aspects that guarantee the sustainability of the system. In this research was described the socioeconomic impact of the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic (Covid 19) on owners who depended economically on the equidae of work and on the welfare of those animals. 105 structured interviews were carried out, which included general aspects of their family environment, work, economic and variables related to the welfare of their equidae. Post-field work, the job description of the needs and municipalities, based on the following variables: condition of the equidae and quality of life of the owners, including their families. The traditional work days, some behavioral changes were made. An interesting finding was that the body condition of the animals was not altered, people tried to privilege the conditions of feeding. The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic (Covid 19) impacted much of the sectors of the national and global economy, and the equine industry is not alien to that impact. However, in sectors such as cane production and milk production there were increases in income, which contradicts the above. As regards the health and welfare of the animals, the forced change of work routine that was experienced in some sectors generated impact disorders on the health of helmets and some behavioral changes. Considering the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the 2030 agenda, it is essential to propose concrete actions to the union of owners of equidae working, including strategies to favor their living condition from the perspective of "A Health and a Well-being"
