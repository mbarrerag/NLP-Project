# Surgical technique for resection of carotid body tumors implemented at the National University Hospital of Colombia and associated post-operative outcomes.

Chemodectomas or tumors of the carotid body are neuroendocrine neoplasms typically located in the bifurcation of the carotid artery. Most carotid body tumors are slow-growing, so each case should be studied, evaluating risk benefit of the surgical procedure. A series study of 64 patients is performed, carrying out a descriptive and retrospective analysis of these tumors undergoing surgical treatment by the head and neck surgery service of the National University Hospital of Colombia (HUN) between 2017 and 2022. Demographic data regarding the sex and age of presentation and other morphological cases of size, extension and classification were analyzed, evaluating the declinkages obtained. Within the group of complications diagnosed in the postoperative period, it was identified that in 18 (28.1%) patients were affected the facial nerve and in 16 (25.0%) cases were affected the hypoglosic nerve.
