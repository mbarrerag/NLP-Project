# Scientific and postmodern history: an impossible dialogue? The case of Jerzy Topolski (1973-1998)

Postmodernity emerges as a philosophical paradigm of contemporary reality that manages to trigger a series of concerns for the human sciences and their epistemological forms. For history, this impact comes from the linguistic turn that returns the gaze to language and narrative as essential components in its scientific constitution.
