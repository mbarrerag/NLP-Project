# NUTRITIONAL SUPPLEMENT IN ONCOGERIATRIA

More than half of cancer patients are elderly people. Ageing combined with oncological diseases results in a combination of high nutritional risk. Therefore, the indication of nutritional supplements is supported by the literature. However, it is a prerequisite for nutritional supplementation to contribute to the maintenance of lean mass in oncogeriatric patients that meet the energy requirements of these patients. ESPEN guidelines of 2022 recommends the use of nutritional supplement providing at least 400 kcal, 30 g of protein for at least 1 month for favorable results in older people. In the presentation we will review the meta-analysis that support this recommendation. More studies are recommended in relation to optimal contributions in oncogeriatric patients
