# CRISPR/CAS9-MEDIATED GENOME MUTAGENESIS OF DIAPHORINA CITRI VIA MANUAL AND ROBOTIC EMBRYO MICROINJECTION

The Asian Citrus Psyllid (ACP) is a major vector of the citrus disease, Huanglongbing (HLB). With HLB-infected ACP widespread in Florida and being increasingly detected through pockets of California, the ACP vector control tool kit needs to be expanded. Currently, in other pest insects including mosquitoes and fruit flies, there has been a rapid expansion of genetic-based control strategies. A major component of these strategies is the ability to generate site-specific mutations as well as integration of foreign genetic material into the organism's genome. Here we outline our methods for CRISPR/Cas9 based gene editing through both manual and robotic microinjection in ACP embryos. We saw that the ability to leave the embryos mounted to the plant and use a robotic system to orient and perform microinjections significantly enhanced survival rates while enabling gene editing efficiency
