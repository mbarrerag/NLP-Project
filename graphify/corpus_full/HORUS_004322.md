# Remote sensors in the exploration and monitoring of hydrocarbon production in offshore areas: A thematic approach and practical exercise in Baja Guajira, Colombia

The sub-basket of the Baja Guajira in Colombia presents a significant potential for hydrocarbon exploration (HCs), especially natural gas, which is why this place has been chosen as an area of interest of the present analysis. This study explores the effectiveness of the multispectral images of ASTER, Landsat 8 and Sentinel 2 as a tool to detect natural HCs filtrations in offshore areas or emissions associated with their production. A focus based on band ratios is used, using the SWIR bands of these special sensors, close to the main absorption traits of HCs (2300 and 1700 nm), to identify spectral contrasts related to their presence. Although the results do not conclusively link the differences in the reflection with the presence of HCs, they do not rule out the possibility of emissions in the study area. The detection of HCs in offshore areas is more complex due to various factors, which requires a deeper analysis to understand their impact in the observations.
