# WORK HISTORY AND WORKERS TODAY. SOME REFLECTIONS TO THE CENTENARY FOR THE FOUNDATION OF USE

In February 2023, the Workers' Union (USO) celebrated its 100th anniversary. In connection with this event, the article reflects on the history of work and workers, analyzing different perspectives of the relationship between historical knowledge, present and challenges of the current labour movement. The text is structured into two sections: the first outlines some reflections on the importance of historical knowledge to reveal issues of our time, affirming the relationship between history and present. In the second, it identifies and analyzes a number of key aspects of the particular history of USE, but whose influence goes beyond this union organization, and simultaneously projects as legacies and challenges for the current trade union movement
