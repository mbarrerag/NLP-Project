# CULTURE POLICY AS PUBLIC POLICY: DESIGN OF A MUNICIPAL CULTURE POLICY

Local culture in a globalized world is transformed, changed, gives rise to new problems, focuses and different themes, which must be thought and reflected in the framework of a municipal administrative action that is sustained and legitimized through participatory democratic processes, which brings with it cultural transformation
