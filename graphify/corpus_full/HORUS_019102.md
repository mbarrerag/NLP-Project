# GENETIC, BIOACUSTICS AND MORPHOLOGICAL ́A REVEATES A NEW OCCULT SPECIES IN PRISTIMANTIS DORSOPICTUS (ANURA: STRABOMANTIDAE

The variation in the phenotic distribution of some species at a geogramme level, makes them excellent models to evaluate the different evolutionary attributes and answer questions related to their diversity. Added to this, the constant effort of sampling in high Andean ecosystems that include the type localities of the species and the integration of a novel set of data, can lead to taxonomic findings unsuspected. In this study we provide genetic evidence, bioacoustic and morphological to support the delimitation and description of a new species of forests north of the Andes, historically confused with Prismantis dorsopictus (Rivero & Serna, 1988). This last and new species of the Punica, Punica, Punica, were not recovered as offspring in our phylogenetic analysis
