# Feasibility of the use of PP bands in the reinforcement of walls in non-reinforced masonry

The self-constructed dwellings in Bogotá with non-reinforced masonry constitute approximately 48 to 60% of the dwellings in the city. Due to the low structural performance of this system and the high probability of occurrence of a earthquake, it is necessary to strengthen these dwellings to avoid serious injuries to the inhabitants, material losses and impossibility of use of the spaces. Although there are multiple methods of reinforcement currently in use, technical and economic limitations in informal contexts require alternatives to conventional methods. Through destructive tests of axial compression and diagonal tension, the feasibility of the use of polypropylene bands, normally used for packaging in the loading sector, to strengthen walls of non-reinforced masonry, with the aim of facilitating the constructive processes and increasing the applicability of these reinforcements in self-constructed houses. Positive results were obtained especially in the performance of walls in diagonal tension, in the increase of time to occur collapse and in the containment of debris after the fragile failure of the masonry. .
