# CORRELATION BETWEEN THE DEVELOPMENT OF VIAL INFRASTRUCTURE AND THE FIRST ARMED CONFLICT TO CONSOLIDATE A STABLE AND LURABLE PEACE

Colombia is the country that possesses the oldest internal armed conflict in the world and continues without concrete negotiation that allows the complete end of the internal civil war between the different guerrilla groups, paramilitaries and the forces of the State.In 2016, the Peace Agreement between the Colombian government and the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia was signed.The Popular Army (farc-ep), the guerrilla group with the greatest influence and armed muscle during the conflict, was signed, so the nation entered into the transition towards the consolidation of a stable and lasting peace, in the period called post-conflict.The consolidation of the peace requires a multidisciplinary effort for its success in the national territory and is subject to the compliance of the agreements signed with the farc-ep. These are composed of six fundamental points: the integral rural reform, the political participation, the end of the conflict, the solution to the problem of illicit drugs, the agreement on the victims of the conflict and the implementation, verification and endorsement.The first point of the agreement has historically been listed as a priority in all the approaches to negotiating peace throughout the armed conflict because the state of the rural areas most remote was born in one of the 50th. The study focuses on deepening the phenomenon in the department of Caldas and its municipalities, since they have been one of the most affected territories. Then, key concepts of rural planning and transport models are defined, with special attention to accessibility
