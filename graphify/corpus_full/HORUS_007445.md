# Detection and molecular typification of Aviar Metapneumovirus in commercial and wild birds in Colombia

The global poultry industry, as the main producer of protein of animal origin, faces important challenges in health and production due to the high density of birds housed and the risks associated with diseases.One of the main problems is the Aviar Respiratory Complex (CRA), a multifactorial condition involving pathogens such as the Aviar Metapneumovirus (AMPV), which is frequently subdiagnosed in multiple regions of the world. The virus has the ability to affect mainly commercial birds, while wild birds are considered reservoirs and vectors of it. This study was aimed to identify the presence and subtype of aMPV in samples of commercial and wild birds. 273 samples were collected that included upper respiratory and reproductive tract hysopuses, both commercial and wild birds.In the first instance a protocol of RT-CPR conventional was used to identify the N gene of the virus in the samples.In the second phase, a protocol of RT-PCR antida addressed to the G gene was used, which allowed to detect the B subtype of aMPV in 23 samples (8.42%).
