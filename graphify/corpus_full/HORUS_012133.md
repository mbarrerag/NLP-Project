# PSYCHOLOGIC EFFECTS DERIVED FROM COVID-19 INFECTION IN MAYOR PERSONS

To carry out a bibliographic review in different databases on psychological changes in older adults due to Covid-19 causes to identify the psychological consequences and strategies of implemented interventions. A review of literature published in Spanish and English was used, taken from databases, journals and information reported by the World Health Organization registered in the last three years. The literature consulted allowed to find that older persons have been affected by Covid-19 at the physical and psychological level. They present anxiety, excessive concerns, tremors, tensions and events of depression. Likewise, the study found that the care of older adults requires to consider their mental health as a fundamental aspect because their affectation negatively affects the social behavior of the population, thus improving their quality of life.
