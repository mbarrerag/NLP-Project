# CLASSIFICATION OF MICROPLASTICS USING ELECTRONIC LANGUAGES

Microplastics have become a silent polluting agent of water bodies that are generated from mechanical interactions of plastic waste from different types of industries with the environment in which they are deposited. Unfortunately and given the massive use of plastic in daily life, the poor disposition of these wastes has generated that these elements end up in water sources and in the various ecosystems where their inhabitants in their interaction with water consume them in appreciable quantities resulting in diseases due to this contamination. In humans, studies are being carried out that demonstrate how harmful they are to health when they are ingested because of a lack of control in water treatment processes.
