# CONTRACONTABILITY AS A ASSISTANCE TO SOCIAL TRANSFORMATION: MONSANTO-BAYER AND ITS REFERRED TO IN SOCIETY AND ENVIRONMENT

This work aims to reflect critically on the operation of the multinational agrochemical Monsanto-Bayer, specifically with two of its products: the herbicide glyphosate roundup and genetically modified seeds.
