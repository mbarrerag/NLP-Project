# Code White

The thesis of Sophia is a visual account of a case of “White Code”, developed under the premise of making visible the invisible and that is asked by the notion of the image. The narrative construction, is untangled through a series of objectual approaches such as skirt, mean, knife, among others; with which different points of view of the event are related White Code and that, in some way, seeks to involve the reader-spector in the event. Likewise, the work is developed through multiple elements, tools, strategies specific to the artistic visual language as are drawing, illustration, book-art, the notion of support, symbols, footprints, indications etc. With the aim of asking for that question about what is real and what is fiction, about the representation and abstraction to decode and propose different readings of the same fact and try to dematerialize an image that is repeated and repeated. –It is a case of the White Code, it is my sister Emilia has to help the respontist of the hospital, when she had denied us the entry.
