# INFRARED TERMOGRAPHS IN MAMARIA EVALUATION: A PILOT STUDIES IN WOMEN IN FERTIL AGE

The infrared thermography has highlighted as a promising technique for the non-invasive detection of abnormalities in breast tissues, reflecting changes in heat emission and temperature patterns. This article presents a protocol proposal for the capture of mammary thermographic images in women of childbearing age, with the aim of improving the evaluation and diagnosis in this population. To develop this research, a bibliographic review was carried out in various scientific databases, considering relevant factors such as the type of study, the population of interest, the variables measured and the results obtained. A computational tool for the processing of thermographic images was developed, which were obtained following a proposed protocol, which is derived from the technological surveillance commented on in this document. The methodology used includes exclusion criteria for properly selecting patients, precise instructions for the preparation of the patient before the examination, and considerations about the environment of the examination to ensure the accuracy of the thermographic measurements. Likewise, a specific procedure for the capture of the images was established, using a support designed to position patients in front of the thermographic camera.
