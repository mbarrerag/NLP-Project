# ACCHERENCE TO

adherence to management standards in heart failure such as the Get With the Guidelines initiative (GWTG) of AHA may decrease re-entry and mortality. to describe the clinical characteristics of patients hospitalized for acute heart failure and to evaluate adherence to clinical practice standards of the program Get With The Guidelines, in a university hospital in Bogotá. observational study of the retrospective cohort HUN-ICA corresponding to 493 adult patients hospitalized between April 2016 and December 2018 due to acute heart failure, clinical variables, treatment, mortality, re-entry and adherence to GWTG quality standards were evaluated. 52.1% of the population were women with an average age 75 years and 67% with LVEF >40 % were found to be incapitulated. The most common etiology was hypertensive 58% and the greatest cause of infective decompensation (28.8%) The adherence to beta-blocker use in patients with reduced ejection fraction found to be at the end of 46% with regard to the admission and use of the lower grade of EF blockers
