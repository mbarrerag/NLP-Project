# THE DIALECTIC OF SOCIAL AND INDIVIDUAL AUTHORIZATION IN THE THEORY OF G.H. MEAD’S RECOGNIZANCE

In this article we propose to explain the double significance presented by the concept of recognition in the psycho-social theory of the self that G. H. Mead exposes in his fundamental text Espíri- tu, Yes Himself and Society (1973) The basic thesis is that this distinction points, in turn, to a double form of realization of the individual, thanks to which he comes as a recognized subject as a by-product of rights in the whole of the community to which he belongs (socialization), and as an individual who owns a peculia-rity or functional superiority that are recognized in the process of relating to others (individualization). In a final conclusion, the model of society that underlies this distinction is revealed in the Mead approach. Key words: Communication, self-conceived science, recognition, myself, self-realization
