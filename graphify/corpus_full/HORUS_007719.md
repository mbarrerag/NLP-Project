# EFFECT OF INCLUSION OF ESSENTIAL MICRO-CAPSULATED EUCALIPTO OILS AND TOMMILO IN THE DIET OF WEIGHTED LECHOTES ON PRODUCTIVE PARAMETERS AND INTESTINAL HEALTH

Essential oils are known for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties and present a promising alternative to improve intestinal health and productive performance in different species. The introduction of microencapsulated essential oils of eucalyptus and thyme in the piglet diet; the innovative and potentially beneficial strategy for responding to a global problem, such as antibiotic resistance and the need to reduce the use of antibiotics in animal production and especially in early stages of production, in this case specifically of pig production. The objective of this project is to evaluate the inclusion of two microencapsulated essential oils in a pre-initiation diet for weaned piglets, in the productive parameters and in biomarkers associated with intestinal health. The study will be carried out in the Porcine Production Unit of the Marengo Farming Centre, in Mosquera, Cundinamarca, in the case of 21 days. 216 piglets (108 females and 108 male castrated) from a commercial terminal crossing (PIC410) and the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the case of the E + EOT): CN + microparticles of (EOE + EOT) (200 mg/kg). A design in complete blocks will be used at generalized random. Three observations will be made per treatment in each block, for a total of 9 observations or repetitions, being each pen of four piglets the experimental unit. Over 21 days, parameters such as weight gain, food consumption, food conversion, diarrhea score will be evaluated. Samples will be taken for the assessment of intestinal health by analysis of intestinal morphology and gene expression of pro and anti-inflammatory cytokines, which will be analyzed by means of an ANOVA. Means of each treatment will be compared with each other using the Tukey method, maintaining the probability of type 1 error in 0.05.
