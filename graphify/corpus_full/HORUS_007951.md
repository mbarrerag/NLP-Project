# MULTIFAMILIARS IN MEXICO CITY: URBAN AND ARCHITECTONIC LABORATORIES

The multifamily buildings emerged in Mexico due to the demographic explosion and the Mexican economic miracle.. Their commitment was to focus housing in height releasing urban soil, in super-applars that also offered complementary services. The influences of modern European architecture and local traditions gave here a rich symbiosis. They were architectural and urbanistic experiments that became social transformations, until reaching the full well-being of the workers, the Revolution. Then the State abandoned the social ideario, passed the property to the tenants, who could not maintain the common areas, falling into neglect. Despite this, and the tragedies of earthquakes, the multi-family ones remain a model from which to learn about the variety of building typologies and housing for various families and economic strata, integration with the city, the mixture of housing with complementary services, or the relationship with local traditions
