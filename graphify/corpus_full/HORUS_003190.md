# EFFICIENCY ASSESSMENT OF SMALL SUDS IN WHERE MUCH DENS AND PENDENT

Urbanisation generates great pressures on the territory, increasing the flow rates of runoff and its speed, which causes the hydrograms to undergo great changes in flow rates, time of arrival at the peak and volume of runoff. Sewerage systems do not supply before this accelerated process, being habitual that floods occur when their capacity of transport is exceeded. This article presents the SWMM modeling of the drainage system and SUDS alternatives in an urbanized mountain basin, located in the city of Manizales, in which natural drainages have been modified to enable land for construction. The study area was differentiated, performing the analysis of sensitivity, calibration and validation of the model. To define if the model adequately represented the response of the basin, the indicators of volume adjustment of the hydrogram, error of the peak flow, time to the peak and NSE index were calculated to obtain the volume of the drainage. With the validated model, the design event for a 5-year TR, for which nodes with flood and ducts were presented working to overload, obtained is calculated, which is associated with the intensive process of urbanization of the basin, high-s and insufficient capacity of the SUDS
