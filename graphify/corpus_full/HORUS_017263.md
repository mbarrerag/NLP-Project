# Using linezolid in a hospital of high complexity in the city of Bogotá

the study of the prescription of last generation antibiotics helps to identify the causes and consequences of the inappropriate use of antibiotics. These include the appearance of resistance to antibacterials, a situation declared by the WHO as a global public health problem. to describe the prescription of linezolid in a hospital of high complexity of Bogotá, Colombia, taking into account the follow-up of the recommendations for use of the drug included in the clinical practice guidelines (GPC) and its indications. a descriptive observational study of longitudinal cut with retrospective collection of the information of all patients who were prescribed linezolid, at a minimum dose of 600 mg every 12 h during the period of January 1st 2017 to 31st December 2018 was performed. The characteristics of the prescription of linezolid were described. 133 prescriptions were reviewed.
