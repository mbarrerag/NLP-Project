# RESULTING VIAL INFRASTRUCTURE WITH ADVANTAGE CAMARES AND ARTIFICIAL INTELIGENCE

In this presentation, the concept of geotaging of photos and videos is central. An example of open source for its realization is presented. A comparison of this technology is made with others, applied for the lifting of road infrastructure such as horizontal, vertical signals, vehicles, traffic lights, the roads themselves, and their rolling folder. The initial concepts used for the automatic detection of these elements using artificial intelligence are outlined. Different examples are shown from videos and photos taken from a vehicle and the results are presented in a geographic information system software
