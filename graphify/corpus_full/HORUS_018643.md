# Characterization of stone aggregates for asphaltic mixtures through petrographic analysis and image processing.

The stone aggregates play a fundamental role in the behaviour of the pavements, since they contribute more to the resistance to the different efforts to which they are subjected, and in turn in volume constitute a percentage between 95% and 75% of the materials used for their manufacture. Thus, improving the techniques used for the characterization of these natural materials, it is fundamental when looking for optimal behavior, before the different levels of efforts to which they are normally subjected. In the common practice the measurement of important parameters in the behavior of the pavements, such as the form, angularity and texture are, are carried out through indirect mechanisms, or qualitatively through visual comparison with defined parameters. In order to improve the measurement of microstructural parameters and the form of aggregates, was carried out by images of aggregates taken by means of microscope and techniques of image processing, to develop procedures for the measurement of microstructural parameters and of a form susceptible of quantification
