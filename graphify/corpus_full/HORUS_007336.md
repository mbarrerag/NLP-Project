# Development and characterization of avocado oil (Persea americana ‘Lorraine’) oleogels structured with monoglycerides

The aim of this study was to develop oleogels from avocado oil (*Persea americana* ‘Lorraine’), using a commercial monoglyceride as a structural agent. The oil was obtained by two mechanical extraction methods without the use of solvents, guaranteeing its suitability for human consumption and its compliance with national and international quality standards. The mechanical extrusion and hydraulic compression methods allowed the production of oils with appropriate physicochemical characteristics. For the formation of oleogels, an experimental design of the response surface with the Box-Behnken method was used, assessing the effect of the concentration of monoglycerides, temperature and heating time on the ability to retain oil (CRA), firmness and colour of gel. The results indicated that both the concentration of the monoglyceride and the temperature showed a significant influence on the CRA and firmness, while the color was affected only by the concentration. The process was optimized to maximize the CRA and achieve an adequate firmness, obtaining optimal conditions of 7.98 % of the monoglyceride, 86 °C and 17 minutes of the heating.
