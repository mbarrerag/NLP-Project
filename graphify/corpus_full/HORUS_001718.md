# APPROXIMATION TO THE EVALUATION AND MANNEJO OF THE PEDIATRIAL TRAUMA

trauma is the most frequent cause of childhood mortality and disability, according to the centre for disease control care (CDC), it is reported that approximately 17,000 die annually from trauma-related injuries, with nonintentional injury being the first cause of death in children between 1 and 18 years of age and according to WHO in 2008 the mortality of the paediatric population oscillated by approximately 27.1%; in Europe they were 54.5% being the main cause of death above all other causes that were reported, it is necessary to take into account that 47% of the general causes of trauma, occur due to traffic accidents in this population
