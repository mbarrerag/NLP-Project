# Assessment of the adsorbing activity of an intelligent material and its operability for the removal of organic pollutants in production waters

In Colombia, approximately 1.5 million cubic metres of production water are produced daily in various oil fields. The objective of eliminating the dumping challenges the development of technologies that promote this strategy, ensuring the sustainability and preservation of the environment without compromising the production of crude. Throughout this work, the procedure for the production and characterization of a membrane equipped with an intelligent material in its matrix is studied. In the first instance, this membrane is of an oleophilic nature and allows the removal of the organic pollutants present in the production water. However, by exposing the membrane to an irradiation with UV light for 10 minutes, its affinity to the oil is reversed, transforming into oleofbic. This makes it possible to remove the pollutants from the membrane matrix and the regeneration of its removal capacity. This membrane managed to remove up to 90.6% of the pollutants associated with fats and oils from a sample of synthetic production with concentrations between 90 mg/L and 250 mg/L.
