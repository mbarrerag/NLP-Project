# The role of the university in inclusive innovation: analysis from computer modeling and simulation.

Innovation has generated expectations as a crucial factor of generating value and competitive advantage for organizations and as a means to achieve growth, well-being and development for nations. However, the results seem to be contradictory: while there is evidence of an increase in the competitiveness of innovative companies and the economic growth of certain countries, the great problems faced by the global society show worrying signs of persistence and even exacerbation. In this context, the concept of inclusive innovation arises, as an alternative proposal to competitive innovation, which seeks to direct the emphasis of innovation towards the construction of social welfare and human development.Innovation, it is produced in a systemic context, the university, as the fundamental actor of this system, is called to play a decisive role in redirection towards inclusive innovation, considering the commitment that this institution has to society by its own nature.The objective of this the the the thesis is to contribute to the understanding of the role of the university in the generation of inclusive innovation and, therefore, to generate proposals and recommendations for effective participation from the academic field.The methodology used consisted in the approach to the phenomenon with quantitative and qualitative techniques, to generate modeling and simulation of computation that allows to analyze the main results of the phenomenon
