# Coherence analysis of supercontinuum generation in multiple quantum well-on-insulator waveguides

This study explores the impact of engineering the dispersion and nonlinear coefficient of multiple quantum well-on-insulator nano-waveguides on various nonlinear phenomena. These include supercontinuum (SC) generation and dispersive wave creation, along with an examination of the intensity and the coherence behavior. The nano-waveguides are pumped at two different wavelengths in the C-band of optical telecommunications, with both negative and positive dispersion being engineered. We consider the standard one-photon-per-mode noise to analyze the coherence of the SC, and observe a high degree of stability in most regions. The MQWs are made of Al x Ga 1-x As, with three different aluminum concentration fractions (x) considered. The insulator used is hydrogen silsesquioxane (HSQ), a material related to silica, which enhances the performance in semiconductor crystal growth. This work provides valuable insights into optimizing coherence in SC generation, a critical aspect for its practical deployment in next-generation photonic technologies
