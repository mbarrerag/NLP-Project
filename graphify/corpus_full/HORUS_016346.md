# FUTURE PERSPECTIVES OF THE ART: PASSE ANCLAJES

The debate around the place that art occupies at present, its contingent and disordered character, and its permanent confusion with aesthetics, requires the historical recognition of the social practices that gave rise to a conflictive modernity.To think about how we take contact with the course of time and learn from it, opens a discussion about the temporal context and the way in which art has developed actions and events throughout these last centuries. Hence, any future perspective cannot be separated from the past. Beyond the data, the past-future conjunction encourages the birth of a temporary consciousness that takes meaning whenever its permanent work is questioned. It is surprising that after two decades of the xxi century, the metaphysics, genius and talent are still repeated as the criteria for making art. To tell it, and more serious, to teach it, shows the limited historical sense, at the same time that it does not know how the art requires to be complemented by other fields of study that allow the artist to take the challenges beyond the instruments already tested. Ontology and an epistemology of art that marks the courses of the artistic field. This conviction is accompanied by the imperative need to depart from the ideological task of legislating on art that the stetas have had, reinforcing the idea of interpreter, translator or mediator. Instead, it is demanded that it be the artists themselves who settle the foundations of the field of art and for this purpose, it is invited to open new practices that link the recognition of the capacity to understand and interpret what they themselves produce
