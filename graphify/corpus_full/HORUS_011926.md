# Child abuse: Systematic documentation

Child abuse: SISTEMATIC DOCUMENTATION Child abuse (my) is a public health problem, often unknown, minimized or omitted by the legal and social implications that its diagnosis can generate in society. Previously, explanations of symptoms and clinical findings in children suffering from IM are attributed to infectious etiologies or nutritional deficits. Today, more interventions are needed to improve the knowledge and visibility of this problem in society, as there are still many social, cultural and religious barriers that prevent the acceptance and visualization of this problem. In this chapter, real specimens of brain injuries are photographically documented, most commonly seen in the context of child abuse of INML and CF in Bogotá, and a brief explanation of their biodynamics is also made
