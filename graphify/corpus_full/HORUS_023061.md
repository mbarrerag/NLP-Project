# REMOTE WORKING MODALITY: PSYCHOLOGIC FACTORS IMPLUBING LIVING-TRAWLED BALANCE: CAP 6.

This work includes a compendium of works related to one of the most relevant organizational areas in recent decades: the Strategic Human Resources Directorate. An area that, in a context where the intensive use of knowledge and technology is becoming the norm, has become a key factor for achieving the objectives, not only at the organizational level, but also of society at large. Trends such as globalization or digitization seem to lead to greater polarization of the labour market and require new ways of addressing organizational problems. This is why it is time to address management and change processes aimed at ensuring the sustainability of organizations in all their economic, social and environmental dimensions, as well as to avoid the risk of increasing dehumanization of employee-business relations. Thus, this work aims to be useful both to researchers and professionals in the area, addressing academicly rigorously, but also in a practical sense, the study of human resources from a strategic perspective and its relationship with very current topics of business practice such as CSR and sustainability, job welfare, diversity, value management and digitalization, among others
