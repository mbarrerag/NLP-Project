# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE AFFECTS OF EXTENSIVE PALMA CULTURES TO THE HEALTH OF THE SIKUAN AND JIW COMMUNITIES OF THE MAPIRIPAN MUNICIPUM

The municipality of Mapiripán is located in the south-eastern of the department of Meta, in an area of altillanura and jungle that includes a forest of transition between the Orinoquía and the Amazonia of high environmental value. The region has been the habitat of indigenous peoples, especially of the Sikuani ethnic group, but also have settled in this territory members of the Jiw community. In the public sphere, Mapiripán has been one of the territories that was the scene of the armed conflict that occupied the pages of the media, due to the occurrence of massacres related to the conflicts inside the paramilitary groups (which have had continuity until the present of the so-called criminal bands or Bacrim), by the control of the cultivation and processing of coca. In the framework of the defense of the human rights in this territory, in the last years the Intereclesial Commission of Justice and Peace has accompanied the communities of Sikuani and Jiw palpán in this process, which has decided to carry out a study of the environmental impacts of the introduction of palm oil crops in the region, in particular those carried out by the National University of Pazani. It is requested to this process of the local communities.
