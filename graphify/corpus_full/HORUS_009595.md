# TAXONOMIC AND GEOGRAPHICAL NOTES ON LEPTOSTYLUS LECONTE, 1852 (COLEOPTERA, CERAMBYCIDAE, LAMIINAE, ACANTHOCININI) WITH THE DESCRIPTIONS OF TWO NEW SPECIES

Two new species of Leptostylus LeConte, 1852 are described: L. carinatus sp. nov., from Panama; and L. boliviensis sp. nov., from Bolivia. Two species are reported from Honduras for the first time: Leptostylus xanthopygus Bates, 1880; and Leptostylus retrorsus Bates, 1885. Notes on the variation of the elytral pubescent pattern in L. xanthopygus and L. hilaris Bates, 1872 are provided. The number of specimens used in the original description of L. retrorsus is questioned
