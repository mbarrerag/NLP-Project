# COLORIMETRIC SENSORS BASED ON GOLD NANOPARTICLES FOR THE DETECTION OF MERCURY IN WATER, OBTAINED BY THE LASTER OBLIGATION PULSED IN LIQUID

In recent decades, a wide variety of applications have been generated that use nanoparticles of gold. These applications stand out from those that take advantage of their plastic behavior, such as medical and environmental sensors. Particularly, to detect mercury in water, it is necessary that sensors measure in the order of parts per billion (ppb) since mercury is a toxic metal and small concentrations are harmful to health and the environment. These applications require colorimetric sensors free of toxic reagents and that are reproducible.
