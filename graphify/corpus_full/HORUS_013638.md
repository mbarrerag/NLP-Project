# Hybrid occupation model for the new rurality in the AETCR hills “Jaime Pardo Leal”, Guaviare, Colombia

The process of formulating housing projects for ex-combatants of the FARC-EP has been a collective effort, although the lack of clear policies for planning and regulating urban designs has generated problems in the development and maintenance of these projects; this has resulted in generic designs with minimum standards of habitability. In response to this problem, the main aim is to create a model of urban/rural hybrid occupation through the application of project tools of urban design that contribute to the planning of rural territories taking as a case of application the territory of the AETCR Las Colinas, in San José del Guaviare. The main approach is to ensure the autonomy of communities, access to services, the provision of opportunities and the efficiency of structures, contributing to the welfare of ex-combatants and neighbouring populations, as well as to the consolidation of the vision of the new rurality and urban-rural planning.
