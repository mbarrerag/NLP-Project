# COMPARISON OF PSEUDOPULCYPOPENNIS SENSU LATO (DIPTERA: CULICIDAE) OF THE AMERICAN CONTINENT BASED ON CODES OF DNA BARS

Anopheles pseudopunctipennis sensu lato is distributed from the south of the United States (EUA) to the center of Argentina and north of Chile, and is one of the main vectors of malaria in Latin America, particularly relevant in the foothills and mountain regions, which makes possible the seasonal transmission of malaria to altitudes higher than 1000 msnm. Population studies suggest differences in the feeding patterns, morphometrics, susceptibility to different strains of Plasmodium, also evidenced reproductive isolation between populations, so it has been proposed as a complex of at least three species.
