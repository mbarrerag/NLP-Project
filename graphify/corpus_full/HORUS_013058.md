# Structural model of the quality of the business relationship and its effect on technological innovation

Within the sources of external technological innovation that agribusiness can use, are the suppliers; knowledge flows that would be conditioned on the way in which commercial relations are developed. In this sense, the present research aims to evaluate empirically a theoretical model between the quality of the commercial relationship and technological innovation from a sample of 250 agricultural producers in the south-eastern part of Colombia. The methodology has an explanatory and predictive approach, mainly uses the technique of structural equation models by the partial minimum square method (PLS-SEM) and proposes for the variable `8220;The quality of the commercial relationship`8221; two models of measurement to be evaluated, one-dimensional and multidimensional, and for `8220; Technological innovation`8221; a two-dimensional model. The results allow us to conclude that there is a direct and positive relationship between the quality of the commercial relationship and technological innovation in agribusiness, contributing its results to relational marketing theory and to the concept of innovation as a system
