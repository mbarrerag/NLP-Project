# SCHOOL AGROECOLOGY: EXPERIENCES AND REFLECTIONS FROM COLOMBIA, CHILE AND CATALUNYA.

The school can and must play an important role in the changes we know urgently and in depth on our planet. In this chapter, from Chile, Colombia and Catalonia we want to present some reflections and practical proposals for teaching work with School Agroecology, inspired by Agroecology. These are based on experiences carried out in the three territories, where we are the authors of the article. In this field the school can take advantage of its central role in the communities to inspire transformations in the food system, without giving up its mission: the learning of children, young people and their communities
