# Is there tacit collusion in the Colombian film industry?: estudio para la distribución

This paper analyzes if the way in which major distributors in Colombia behaved during the 2012-2019 period resembled that of tacit collusion, acting as if they were a single major company. Using a demand windows approach, this paper examined the determinants of the temporal distance between the release dates of any pair of movies released during the same demand window, comparing among models using Bootstrapping techniques. The results suggest that although statistic evidence does not support the idea of tacit collusion among major distributors, this group of firms achieved a larger degree of coordination when choosing their release dates than the other groups of distributors
