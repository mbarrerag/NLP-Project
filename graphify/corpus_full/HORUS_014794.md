# Water governance, from the moor to the foothills: Actors and planning on the Cusiana river.

The processes of water governance are linked to four dimensions in the territory: social, economic, environmental and political. In this article the components overlap over two antagonistic scenarios: the moorland as a mountain ecosystem of the Cusiana river in the municipality of Aquitaine (Boyaca), altered by the agricultural processes of onion and potato, and the municipality of Maní (Casanare), located at the end of the Cusiana river basin, with an ecosystem of cattle plains, high oil extraction and agro-industrial processes for African palm and rice crops. Some actors are recognized and their influence on the territory, expressed in their relationship with economic activities linked to extractivism and productivism; planning tools such as Development and Territorial Ordering Plans (EOTs and POCOCA), are analyzed in light of the governance of water and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
