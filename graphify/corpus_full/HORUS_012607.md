# FUTURE BOGOTA: BETWEEN THE CITY PLANNING AND THE CITY BEAUTIFUL

Bogotá Future was a plan made by the Public Works Section of Cundinamarca under the direction of the engineer Enrique Uribe Ramírez with the aim of foreseeing the development of Bogotá at that time. The plan can be seen not only as the result of the nascent discipline of urban planning in Colombia but also of a prolonged debate between technicians, politicians and urban developers about the main ideas that should govern the city of the future. This plan inaugurates, to a certain extent, a historical moment in which the production of urban space is mediated henceforth by the plane and the task assigned to it as an instrument of control and modeling the city of the future. The richness of the sources that feed the conception of the plan and its relationship with the historical moment through which it was passing through the city of the moment make it a mandatory reference for those who approach urban studies
