# Mangroves for the protection of coastal socio-ecosystems. Case study Punta Soldado, Colombian Pacific

The mangroves are socio-ecological systems (SSE) coastal in which the biophysical and socio-economic factors interact continuously at different scales of time and space.These ecosystems play a crucial role in adapting coastal communities to climate variability, since it has been proven that mangroves contribute multiple contributions from Nature to People (NCP).Some of them are the role of mangroves as protective barriers against the action of waves and floods, the livelihood of fisheries and other marine species and the provision of essential resources and cultural values for coastal communities.The Colombian Pacific Coast (CPC) also houses extensive areas of mangroves, with significant ecological and socio-economic importance.However, these ecosystems have suffered severe degradation due to the climatic variability and human intervention.In this study, an interdisciplinary assessment of the role of the SSE and NCP is carried out (from biophysical, economic and social approaches) of the impact of climate variability phenomena in the contribution of mangroves to coastal protection and other associated NCPs. The economic assessment approach allowed understanding the role of the marine climate in the value of the CFP for coastal protection of mangroves. The model of qualitative dynamic systems showed the connections between the key elements that play a direct or indirect role in the CPN for coastal protection of mangroves in the light of the EES framework. It is a system of 106 loops that illustrates its complexity, considering how changes in one element (biophysical, economic or social) can affect other elements by building reinforcement loops and balancing them around coastal protection. This study highlights the importance of interdisciplinary approaches to strengthen the resilience of socio-ecological systems, not only in the face of climate change, but also in the face of climate variability. ed severe degradation due to climate variability and human intervention.
