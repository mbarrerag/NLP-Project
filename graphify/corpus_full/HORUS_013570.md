# Evaluation of the degradation of lambda-cyalotrin and metomill insecticides by intestinal bacteria from Spodoptera frugiperda as potential biodegraders of xenobiotic compounds

The search for micro-organisms capable of degrading insecticides is necessary in bioremediation processes. For this, pest insects are an alternative niche by sharing an evolutionary history of association with intestinal microbiota, which fulfils vital functions for the host such as nutrition, defence, development, and participates in the degradation of natural and synthetic organic molecules, including insecticides. In Colombia, the main strategies for controlling pest insects include the use of chemical synthesis insecticides, however, their application leads to the resistance of the insect, both to the impact on human health and at the environmental level.
