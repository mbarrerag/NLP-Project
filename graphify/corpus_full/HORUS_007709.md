# Improvement of the Better Campus platform user experience

The project's approach results from the analysis of students' needs regarding the enrolment of subjects at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, the main one is the visualization of possible timetables for the enrolment of subjects, where possible combinations of enrolment must be visualized simultaneously. Students use different methods and tools to be able to do that, one of these tools developed by students is Better Campus, Since the user experience (UX) it is important to think about the route, accessibility and ease of understanding of digital platforms, where an opportunity is evidenced with the Better Campus platform, improving the different functionalities that it integrates in order to attract more students for its use, besides facilitating the conformation of the schedule with its possible combinations according to the needs of users
