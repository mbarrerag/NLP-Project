Statistical analysis for cavities and gingivitis will include relative measures such as Relative Risk (RR) of the intervention and Risk Reduction group (RRR) and the absolute measures that can be calculated will be Absolute Risk Reduction (RAR) and Number needed to treat (NNT). To compare the variables between different monitoring times X2 and Fisher will be used for categorical variables and t-test for continuous variables for comparison between groups. To compare data of patients, peer data tests will be used. A survival analysis will be performed to study the follow-up time until the censorship of observations for the main outcome.
