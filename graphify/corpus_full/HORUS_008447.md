# PHYSICAL CHEMICAL ASSESSMENT OF PIANGUA CAVICHE (Tubercose and SIMILI ADARATOR) LABORATED IN TUMACO, COLOMBIA

With a view to providing alternatives to use to the Piangua that is usually captured in Tumaco, Nariño, Colombia, through a product that is consumed in the region resulting from the artisanal processing and with a view to obtaining information on its composition, the data were taken from samples of 90 portions prepared by the Association of Concheras Women Manglar Roots for the process of elaboration, the ingredients that make up it and the analysis of protein, fat, humidity, ash and heavy metals was carried out with application of AOAC methodologies. The heavy metals were determined by atomic absorption in the handcrafted oven for Cd, Ar and Pb and with graphite oven and hydrogen generator for Hg. The results in composition were Piangua 45% and spices and condiments 55% (lime juice, cylantro, onion, red pigment, salt, oil, regional spices).The results obtained were humidity 79.47%, protein 16.72 %, fat 0.26%, ash 0.89% and heavy metals (mg/kg) The variable content of Arsenic <0.02, Mercury and P0.02 are firming in relation to the product, with the highest quality of the product
