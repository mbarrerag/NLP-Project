Presentation Interdisciplinary research in the study of natural products for medicinal purposes is the cornerstone to achieve a significant advance in this area so valuable and important for our country, the second most biodiverse in the world. For many years, plants have been studied as a source of active principles, which have been isolated and subsequently synthesized as drugs for the development of medicinal products used in the treatment of various pathologies; such is the case of artemisinin, quinine and salicylic acid, among others.
