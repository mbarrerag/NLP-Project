# Is there a baseline line of information to TERRITORIALIZING SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVE: LIMITED WATER AND HEALTH FOR ALL IN COLOMBIA?

The objective of this chapter is to establish to what extent the data obtained in the National Water Survey 2018, developed by IDEAM, are sufficient for the construction of the baseline information necessary to territorialize the sustainable development goal of ensuring water availability and sustainable management and sanitation for all. (ODS 6) In addition, it seeks to explore the usefulness of the data included in the so-called Atlas of the Global Water Risk (Arga) to complement the studies carried out at the national level
