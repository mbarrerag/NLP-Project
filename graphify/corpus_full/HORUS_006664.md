# A look at tourism in the Vaupés: postpandemia approach

The Vaupés is a department located to the south-east of Colombia, which is part of the Amazon region, and in it live more than 27 ethnic groups that make up the majority of the population. Due to its cultural and environmental characteristics, it has a great potential to become a tourist destination of the country, however, it is not only an area of difficult access, but also social inequalities and environmental degradation, threatening the pervivence of indigenous peoples. This article makes an approximation to the tourism phenomenon in the region from the discourse of the press. It seeks to analyze the representation of the Vaupés as a tourist destination from two articles, one of the Revista Semana and another of the newspaper El Espectador. This approach is made from the articulation of three theoretical approaches, belonging to the geography, the critical studies of the discourse (ECD) and the political ecology (EP), positions that allow to address the complexity that implies the tourism phenomenon.
