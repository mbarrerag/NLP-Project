# PROPOSAL FOR STRENGTHENING THE LABORATORY OF MICROBIOLOGY OF WATERS AND FOOD

The Laboratory of Microbiology of Waters and Foods (LMAA) of the Faculty of Sciences-Sede Medellín performs the institutional missionary functions of research, extension and teaching. The dedications for each of these axes are 70%, 20% and 10%, respectively. The LMAA is economically supported by offering services of microbiological analysis of water, food and food, for animals to the companies and private users who require it. With the resources generated, support is provided for research projects of undergraduate, master's and doctoral students, in the purchase of inputs, maintenance and calibration of equipment and technical support of the staff of the Laboratory that is highly trained. In addition, the Laboratory supports the preparation of materials and means of cultivation, as well as the disposal of potentially infectious material for the practices of general microbiology and food from the courses offered at the headquarters. However, over time, new laboratories have emerged in the local and regional market that offer similar tests to the LMAAA, so that this has been diminished its income in recent years. It is for the above, that the Laboratory requires economic support strategies to continue performing the institutional functions, extension and extension of research
