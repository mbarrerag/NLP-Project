# Ipcua Bquyscua: After paying a penalty

The research group has been accompanying the Pospened Thought Center in the study of the processes that have to go through the persons deprived of their liberty and those who leave and must _x0093_reintegrate into society_x0094_, called post-pened, finding that not only the need arises to make visible the difficulties that these people present (individually), but the need to investigate and strengthen the academic production related to the day-to-day life of the country's prisons and the footprint that they leave in the social and family fabric of the related subjects, and society itself. This project seeks to continue to build collective and interdisciplinaryly substantive solutions that respond to the historical problems of criminal policy in our city and to continue to deepen the role of the people post-pened in it. This project is based on the articulation of three fundamental axes: i. the institutional (concentrating to the improvement of policies related to resocialization); ii. the pedagogical one (which will be strengthened by transforming the perception of the phenomenon in the citizenship through pedagogical dissemination); and iii. the popular reconstruction (visibilizing in this field the specific capacities of the post-social population)
