# Impact of metal functionalization and interlaminal space on hydrogen adsorption on graphene evaluated with Monte Carlo Gran Canon simulation (GCMC)

Hydrogen can play a significant role in the energy transition, as it transports a large amount of energy per unit of mass and can be produced using renewable energies. Therefore, it can be used as low carbon fuel for transport and mobility. However, a key bottleneck for the massive implementation of hydrogen as energy vector is its storage. A potential solution is the development of adsorbing materials for new storage systems. In particular, the low layer graphene (FLG) is a candidate to function as a basis for new adsorbing materials. In this work, the effect of the morphological characteristics and the functionalization of this material with lithium and calcium is explored in the adsorption of hydrogen by molecular simulation. The research allows to obtain information on the adsorption phenomena. The results of this work provide a systematic analysis using simulations of Monte Carlo Gran Canónico (GCMC). It is shown that the model can adequately predict the experimental isotherms and is developed to use an easy model to predict the characteristic morphology from the hydrogen isothermes.
