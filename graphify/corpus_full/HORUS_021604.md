# Diet and osteopathy: the role of feeding in the functioning of the fascial system. Systematic review.

This grade study is a systematic review that seeks to establish the relationship between osteopathic medicine and nutrition, so that, starting from it, nutritional recommendations can be provided to the patient in search of optimizing his physiology and the results of osteopathic medicine. This establishes the importance of the gastrointestinal system, the immune system associated with the intestine (GALT), migration of immune cells from GALT to the body (Homing effect), the physiopathology of acute and chronic inflammation, the functions of the fascial system, osteopathic medicine and the beneficial effects of the science of functional foods on body physiology. It is concluded from this work that adequate nutrition enhances autocure and perpetuates the results of osteopathic medicine in the patient's body
