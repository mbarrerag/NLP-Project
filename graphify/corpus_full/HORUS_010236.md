# EXPERIENCE OF THE SYNTHOMY IN THE AUDENT CORONARY SYNDROME: CHECKING A CONCEPTUAL MODEL

the acute coronary syndrome (ACS) is characterized by different symptoms, which is why the experience varies from person to person. Dodd's Conceptual Model of Symptoms Management (MCMS) addresses this aspect that needs to be tested within nursing practice. to test the postulates of the dimension of the experience of the Dodd Conceptual Model of Symptoms in people with acute coronary syndrome. theoretical verification by structural equations. 256 individuals with acute coronary syndrome diagnosis, selected by stratified probabilistic sampling, were included. Clinical, sociodemographic data were collected and the evaluation and response to symptoms were measured with five valid instruments. A statistical analysis was performed with absolute adjustment, incremental adjustment and parsimony adjustment of which derived a structural equation model. the bidirectional relationship between symptom severity (evaluation) and decision-making (response to symptom) was verified. A model was developed that explained 44 % of the variance over decision-making was made. Other variables were also included in this variable, such as cognitive evaluation and personal control
