# Forming of portfolios of investment in variable income and efficient border generation by robust methods

When selecting portfolios it is interesting to establish the portfolio in which there is a balance between yield and risk, in this sense Markowitz was the pioneer in developing a model that achieved this balance with the solution of a convex quadratic model, in which profitability and risk were measured by the standard mean and deviation respectively, model that was called median-variance (Lu, 2011). However, the existence of extreme or atypical values pollute the model and make it not behave well leading this situation to erroneous estimates so that several methods have been proposed trying to solve this problem. Among the alternatives raised this robust statistic with which methods are offered to estimate the mean vector and the covariance matrix and with which it is sought to eliminate the incidence that the extreme values have when defining the composition of the portfolio. (montaño & gaudencio zurita, 2009). The importance of the robust methods in finance is clear since it is not desired that an investment decision is affected by a small group of atypical data and above all by the impossibility of predicting if there are any atypical data that reduces and the robust ones are expected to use
