# Design of a learning object of stoichiometric calculations in I.E Buenavista, the Dorada, Caldas.

In the final master's degree work in teaching the exact and natural sciences of the National University headquarters Manizales, a Learning Object (OA) was designed as a didactic strategy to support students of grade 10 in the proper appropriation of the management of stoichiometry. The methodology used was qualitative with an interpretative – descriptive scope. A pretest was applied that reviewed the previous knowledge that was key to the design of the OA and finally a perception survey. It is concluded that it was possible to create and apply the OA. In addition, when students were asked about the perception around the OA, the young people positively valued the tool
