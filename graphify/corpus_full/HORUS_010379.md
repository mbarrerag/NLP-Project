# Decoloniality, intersectionality and culturally affirmative psychotherapy in the Colombian context

This work aimed to analyze the contributions of the colonial studies in the elaboration of culturally positive and intersecting psychotherapys in the Colombian context; it was chosen by a qualitative methodology, from an interpretive-constructive and inductive framework, divided into two phases. A systematic review of the texts that address as a central theme the culturally affirmative therapies and a qualitative conversational approach with four Colombian therapists on the potential of the colonial perspective in the creation of intersecting and affirmative therapies in the Colombian context. As results it was evidenced that these psychotherapys start from a multicultural perspective, which underpins the cultural competence, relates psychotherapy to political aspects such as the struggle for human rights and social justice, involves extrapsychic aspects in the formulation and psychotherapeutic intervention; and seeks to place in the center the voice and narrative of the consultation.
