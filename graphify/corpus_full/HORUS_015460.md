# VULNERABILITY ANALYSIS AND ADAPTATION STRATEGIES FOR CLIMATE CHANGE IN A MULTIPROPOSITE RURAL AGREEMENT.

Climate change presents important challenges in ensuring water supply in community systems that supply small bodies of water, even more so when these systems are multipurpose, that is, human consumption and the viability of agricultural and livestock production systems depend on them. This research was carried out with the association of users of the volcano’s waters, located above 2900 meters, in the municipality of Ubaté in Colombia. Engineering and social strategies were combined that allowed to assess the vulnerability of a multipurpose aqueduct to climate change. To address these challenges, adaptation strategies were identified and proposed to ensure the sustainability of the livelihoods of this rural community
