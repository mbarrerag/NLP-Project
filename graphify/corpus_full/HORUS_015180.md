# The Science, Technology and Innovation projects of the General System of Regalías. Considerations on the conjunction of SNCTI, SGR and state procurement.

This work addresses the conjunction of the National Science, Technology and Innovation System, the General System of Regalías and Colombian contract legislation, in relation to the implementation of projects approved in the SGR's Science, Technology and Innovation Fund in order to demonstrate the coherence between the legal systems and the joint scope of the objectives set by the legislator, in the period of existence of the Fund, which would allow understanding the difficulties in the implementation of projects, and the possible need for a system that responds to the complexities of the implementation of these projects. To this end, a descriptive work is presented on the evolution and characteristics of the National Science and Technology System (SNTI), the General System of Regalías (SGR) in relation to the Science, Technology and Innovation Fund (FCTEI) and the recruitment regime applicable to science and technology activities. Subsequently, considerations are presented on the combination of the different systems, in relation to projects implemented between 2013 and 2018 (until the issuance of the Law 1923 of 2018) in contrast to the rules contained in the general administrative acts of convocation, the plan of public calls taken, open and competitive from the allocation for the CTEI's (202)
