# RESISTANCE PROTECTING, PROTECTING AND RESISTANCE IN BARRANCABERMejaY IN THE DECADE OF 1970

This article analyzes the cycle of protest deli-mented by the oil strikes of 1971 and 1977 in Barrancabermeja, Colombia. It examines the relations between workers and the citizens of Barrancabermeja in the successive labor and civic conflicts of the period, investigating both the political and organizational aspects that supported the protest movements, and the social and cultural aspects that acted-alliated this "radical culture" in the context of the 1970s. It also asks for the following high level of repression displayed equally on labor protest and social mobilization. It is argued that such repression was aimed at dismantling the USO (the main oil union) and to fracture its links with the community of Barrancabermeja, pointing in full to the heart of the Barranqueña social configuration
