# Educational model for the construction of a center of science, technology and innovation from spaces of social appropriation in the municipality of Fusagasugá, Cundinamarca.

The first chapter describes the panorama Social appropriation of Science, Technology and Innovation (ASTIC) in the municipality of Fusagasugá, Cundinamarca, together with the description of the results of a perception survey of 212 inhabitants of the municipality, who chose the botanical garden as the best choice of science center for the city. Subsequently, 9 respondents are selected, based on inclusion criteria, to conduct a semi-structured interview, information that was analyzed with the Atlas.TI program. In the second chapter, the last chapter describes succinct concepts related to the neuroscience of learning, from which four basic components emerge from cognitive processes: attention, emotion, learning and memory, which, in relation to the results of the first chapter, account for three formats as a strategy for the teaching of experimental sciences. The last chapter integrates the findings of the two previous chapters to respond to the structuring of an educational model for the construction of a centre of science, technology and innovation from spaces of social appropriation in the municipality of Fusagasugá, Cundinamarca.
