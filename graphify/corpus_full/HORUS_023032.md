# The role of Community Councils in shaping local political power: The case of the Bajo Attrato Chocoano (2012-2017)

This research will carry out an analysis of the role of the Community Councils in shaping local political power in the Lower Attrato Chocoano. This territory has been the scene of disputes and violent clashes as a result of the intervention of various actors, both legal and illegal, who are dynamising public life and shaping the perceptions and realities of the population regarding the figure of the State. In this context, it is sought to question the approaches that understand the territorial dynamics in the Lower Attrato as a manifestation of the “weakness” or “absence” of the State in the territory and to generate a discussion around the relationship of the members of the Community Councils with local politicians, the institutionality and the armed actors that make a presence in the region.
