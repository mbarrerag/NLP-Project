# VII Meeting of Endodontics Research – Colombian Endodontics Association. Postgraduate Course in Endodontics Faculty of Dentistry Universidad Nacional de Colombia

On 23 and 24 September 2021, the seventh national research meeting in endodontics organized by the Colombian Endodontic Association was held remotely. On this occasion, the latter had as its headquarters the post-graduate course in endodontics of the Faculty of Dentistry of the National University of Colombia. On this occasion, 20 research papers were presented from different graduate programs of endodontics in the country, which were classified into four categories: clinical cases, basic sciences, technology and materials and clinical research. Likewise, the event had the presence as a guest lecturer with Dr. Jaime Castro Núñez who discussed related topics on how to publish in high impact indexed journals.
