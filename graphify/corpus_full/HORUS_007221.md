# Implementation and validation of an algorithm for estimating the optical thickness of aerosols on the geographical valley of the Cauca River from satellite measurements of moderate spatial resolution

The objective of this research was to implement and validate the Simplified Algorithm of Recovery of Aerosoles (SARA) to estimate the Optic Sprinkler of Aerosoles - ODA in the geographical valley of the Cauca river. The SARA algorithm was implemented using the MODIS/TERRA products: MOD02HKM (calibrated radiations at 500 m), MOD03 (geolocation at 1 km), MOD09GA (surface reflectivity at 500 m) and MOD35_L2 (cloud mask).These data were processed under three main assumptions: (i) the surface presents a Lambertian behavior, (ii) the MODERICITIM was considered simple dispersion of aerosols, and (iii) both the Palm scatter albedo and the asymmetry factor were maintained constant during the day.The results obtained were contrasted with the AERONET/UNC MOTIM/ITAR measurements. Assumptions: (i) the surface exhibits Lambertian behavior, (ii) single scattering of aerosols was considered, (iii) scattering albedo and asymmetry factor both relained constant throughout the day. The results were validated against measurements from the AERONET – UNC Palmira station and the MODIS MOD04_3K product, which provides ODA data at a spatial resolution of 3 km. After data calibration, SARA achieved a correlation of 0.94 with MODIS/MOD04_3K and 0.83 with AERONET – UNC Palmira.
