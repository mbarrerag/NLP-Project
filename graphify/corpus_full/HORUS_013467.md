# The Stateization of Ethnic Diversity, Multiculturalism, Citizenship and Cultural Policy in Bogotá

Through an analysis of the interactions between the State and ethnic groups, this paper investigates how the state entities of Bogotá address ethnic diversity. The city is constituted as a multicultural territory where various groups have presence that claim a series of rights: root, gypsy, Afro-descendant and indigenous have consolidated as political actors against statehood, and although they do not exceed 1.5% of the population, they do have an institutional scaffolding that is expressed in a set of policies, spaces of participation and affirmative actions. The institutional recognition of the ethnic groups begins thirteen years after the 1991 Constitution, and has gradually developed a relationship in institutional spaces between ethnic groups and statehood.
