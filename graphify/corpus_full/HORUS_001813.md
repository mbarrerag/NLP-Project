# Corporate Governance in Film Audiovisual Production Companies in Colombia

Following the major financial scandals at the international level, the Corporate Government (hereinafter GC) took strength and showed a significant evolution at the institutional level, especially in the present century. In Colombia, with the economic opening, circular 028 and the country code of the Financial Superintendence (hereinafter SFC), this practice was adopted to achieve its objectives that focus on improving the level of confidence of investors through the transparency of information, corporate responsibility, standardization of processes and adequate accountability, managing risks and ensuring the sustainability of the companies. One tool for the fulfilment of the objectives of GC is the control, which guarantees the adoption of processes in the execution of accounting and administrative management, in order to finally favor the corporate strategy established and supervised by the boards of directors. There are also other institutional references in the scope of the GC, such as the principles of GC of the Organization for Cooperation and Economic Development (OECD, 1999, 2004, 2016, 2023), the White Paper of GC for Latin America (OECD, 2004), the Andean Code of GC (CF, 2010), among others, which are studied in this work, the GC, GPO and the latter are the main sectors of the CPO. and, standardization of processes and adaptation accountability.
