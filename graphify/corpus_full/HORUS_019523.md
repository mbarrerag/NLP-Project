# TRANSNATIONAL PARAMILITARY CONNECTIONS RIGHT-WING VIOLENCE IN VENEZUELA AS A STRATEGY TO RESTORE POLITICAL HEGEMONY

The chapter takes a critical look at the expansion of Colombian paramilitary groups into neighbouring Venezuela in the context of the Bolivarian Revolution. They argue that paramilitary forces form part of an ensemble of violent strategies pursued by Venezuela's elites with the active support of Colombia and the United States, aimed at restoring the political hegemony and undoing socio-economic reforms implemented under Chavismo
