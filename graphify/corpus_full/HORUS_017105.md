# MOLECULARY AND BIO-INFORMATICAL TECHNICAL MANUAL FOR IDENTIFICATION OF SPECIFIC BIINDICATORS OF CARBON MINES

The mining company in the Colombian dry base produces about 90% of the coal that is extracted in the country (Unit of Planning Mining Energy - UPME, 2016; Agencia Nacional de Mineria, 2017).The environmental impact of this exploitation generates pollution of air, water and surrounding ecosystems.Among these impacts, the degradation of the soil (Singh & Singh, 2006; Maiti & Maiti, 2015; Campillo, et al., 2000). Díaz et al., (2013) determined that the quality of the soil of coal mines located in the department of Cesar presents physical degradation and low fertility.The environmental pollution by heavy metals also represents a problem in the mining of coal (Ali et al., 2013). The metals are not biodegradable and accumulate the ecological vocations, representing an environmental risk and deteriorating the quality of the soil (Bhattacharyya et al., 2008; Khan et al., 2010). The degradation and pollution of the soil has impacted negatively the populations affected by the agricultural and environmental vocation. In this socioeconomic and environmental scenario, at the moment of the final closure of the soil. Soil rehabilitation and oil extraction. Experiments have shown that the use of organic amendments in aromatic species crops contributes to increased productivity and improves soil fertility (Sabir et al., 2015; Scotti et al., 2015). This guide summarizes molecular biology techniques for sampling, extraction and analysis of mining soils impacted by coal mining in order to identify bioindicators in soils associated with the impact produced or the effect of treatments on soil improvement
