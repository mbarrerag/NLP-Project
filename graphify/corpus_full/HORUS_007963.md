# Accumulation of microplastics in mangrove forests with varying degrees of anthropic impact on the Colombian Pacific

The mangrove ecosystem has morphological characteristics and a specialized root system that alters the distribution and segregation of particles, so they have been identified as important sinks of plastic residues, including microplastics (MP). The objective of this research was to determine the density of microplastics in sediments of mangrove forests according to their degree of anthropic intervention, the environmental, spatial and temporal dynamics and their relation with the structure of the mangrove in the bays of Buenaventura and Tumaco, Colombian Pacific. For this, sampling plots were established that represented different levels of development of mangrove forest, in places with different levels of anthropogenic intervention in each bay.
