# Management of organizational change for digital transformation in the Oil pipelines Hydrocarbon Transport Industry in Colombia: Case study: administration of two pipelines with different operational characteristics

This grade work proposes an integral methodology for the management of organizational change before the digital transformation in the field of transport of hydrocarbons by pipelines in Colombia. The research is based on a comprehensive analysis of models and methodologies of organizational change existing, adapting them to the specific context of the pipeline industry. The study employed a mixed method approach, combining qualitative and quantitative analysis. In-depth interviews were conducted with experts of the sector, questionnaires were applied to assess digital maturity and a case study was carried out in two pipelines with different operational characteristics. The analysis of data included techniques such as thematic analysis, evaluation of coefficients of agreement and the use of word clouds to identify emerging topics. As a result, the Integrated Organizational Change Model (MCOI) was developed, consisting of four phases: I) diagnosis and preparation, II) collaborative design, III) agile implementation and IV) strengthening and evolution. The model incorporates elements of agile methodologies, design thinking, and sociotechnical approaches adapted to the specific needs of the pipeline sector. The evaluation of the MCOI by experts revealed a high assessment of its structure and structure, with average scores. This research significantly contributed to the field of change management and digital transformation in the hydrocarbon sector, offering an adapted and validated model for the Colombian context.
