# COMPOSITION OF HETEROPTERS OF THE FINCA PRODUCTICOL EQUALITATION IN TAURAMENA, ORINOCIA COLOMBIANA

The heteropter (Insecta: Hemiptera: Heteroptera) possess more than 40,000 species in the world, present a great variety of life stories and are found in aquatic and terrestrial environments. The productive systems in the Colombian Orinoquía have only a number of individuals and individuals, and the total number of individuals, that could have a study of the guilds and species of heteropterans present in crops and natural systems, in this area of the country.The aim of this work is to know the composition of heteropter in the productive and natural areas of the farm Guadualito, in Tauramena, Casanare.A total of 49 individuals, with 20 species, distributed in 35 genera and 12 families, where the most representative families were Coreidae, Reduviidae and Pentatomidae species where they found individuals in the rainy season and dry season, were obtained through an opportuntic collection of 12 species and 33 individuals. The management of crops using pesticides and reducing plant composition could affect the establishment of bedbugs in productive areas. This is the first study focused on morichales, gallery forests and productive systems, and therefore a list of 20 species of heteropteros in different habitats of the Colombian Orinoquia is generated
