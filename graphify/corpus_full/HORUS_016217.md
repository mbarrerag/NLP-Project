# STRUCTURE BASIS FOR A MODEL IN RURAL HEALTH IN ARAUCA

Objective This work aimed to identify some successful models of rural health care, which facilitate the construction of a model of rural health care with a differential and intercultural approach for the department of Arauca, Colombia.Methods A systematic review was carried out, based on databases such as BVS, PubMED, SciELO and LILACS. Results The main finding highlights the need to build a health system based on a primary health care model (PHC) with a community-based approach. For this, it is necessary to apply strategies related to human talent, the management of care networks and others, such as the use of technological tools; all focused on bringing more efficient and affordable care, aligned with the Colombian health system and adapted to the department of Arauca
