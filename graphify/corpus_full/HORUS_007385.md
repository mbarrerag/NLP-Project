# Social extermination in Ciudad Bolívar: a look at the resistance actions of local organizations

The phenomenon of social extermination has been a subject that has affected several communities, mainly those identities that do not comply with a social order established: people in the habitable street, those who provide sexual services payments, consumers of psychoactive substances, youth, etc. Victims of stigma, become high-risk targets of this violence, which is surrounded by a deep silence. This mutism has been one of the factors that have allowed these acts to continue to be committed, since, at the same time, it involves the impunity that brings the anonymity of the perpetrators.
