# Primary Literature Adapted as a tool to evaluate and strengthen scientific competencies in university students.

A Virtual Learning Environment (AVA) is proposed, in which Adapted Primary Literature (LPA) is used as a tool for the development of scientific competences proposed by PISA: Evaluating and proposing scientific research and Interpreting tests and data scientifically. The environment was designed according to the needs identified in students of the National University of Colombia (Headquarters Bogotá) in the 2018-2 semester, and was subsequently evaluated by external teachers and implemented in the 2020-1 semester, with students from the first semesters. The usefulness of the AVA was determined by quantitative comparison of the performance of students in an initial, intermediate and final test; as well, through qualitative analysis of the performance in the proposed open activities and the perception of students in the face of the tool and its process. An improvement in the performance of scientific competencies was observed by students after developing the AVA, particularly as regards the competence Interpreting tests and scientific data, which was statistically significant when comparing initial and intermediate tests.
