# STUDY OF PROCESS CONDITIONS FOR DEHIDRATATION OF ORGANIC CURCUUM (Long Course) VALUE-PRODUCED AND ITS APPLICATION AS NATURAL COLORANT

ABSTRACT The turmeric, through the food-processing context of Colombia, has not really been the main protagonist, however, since 2014, it has been intensifying its production, aiming at niches of market as industries, mainly in the food sector, who demand a complement of additives both natural and synthetic, seeking to provide colorization to their products, and in fact, going to supply chains with global references, in search of traceability and economy, thus moving the market to an external demand in a favorable territory geographically and with the conditions necessary to supply this input to the demand of the national industry, complying with quality regulations, as well as with possible advantages compared to the behavior of phytochemical compounds given by the geographical condition and the method of transformation, thus consolidating a series of treatments, conditions and processes, able to promote the technological development of a turmeric powder competent with the consumed today
