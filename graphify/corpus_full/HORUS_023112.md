# Determination of the three-dimensional structure of an immune peptide for MRI +$1H$ and preparation of the reference document of this protocol.

Malaria is the infectious disease that causes the highest morbidity and mortality around the world, in 2019 there was approximately $409,000$ malaria deaths worldwide according to the global malaria report 2020. Currently, $90$ countries are considered endemic for this pathology, indicating that the $40 \% $ of the population is exposed to the risk of becoming infected. According to the epidemiological week 43 of 2020, in Colombia there are approximately $65 293$ cases of malaria where infection predominates by \textit{Plasmodium falciparum} ($49.7 \%$), followed by \textit{Plasmodium vivax} with ($49.5 \%$). During the last 60 years great efforts have been made to develop an effective malaria vaccine from different perspectives.With the development of technologies such as recombinant DNA and chemical synthesis, among others, some vaccine candidates based on sporozoid proteins and merozoid, the invasive forms of the parasite that are involved in two pre- and erythrocytic stages respectively. The paper provides a reference document that facilitates the consultation and study of the -$1 technique RMN to the new generations of researchers and users of the teams in FIDIC.
