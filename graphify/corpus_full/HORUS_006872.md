# The evolution and consolidation of a thought around progressive housing: ICT in the 1960s

Between 1960 and 1972, the Institute of Territorial Credit (ICT) supported by the Inter-American Housing Centre (CINVA), carried out a research and deep reflection work on the problem of social housing in Colombia; this work was consolidated through various plans and programs to develop economic housing to a large extent, with a focus on the organization of the community for its progress. The two entities detached from the international style proper to the 1950s would seek to make architecture and urbanism, a model based on local problems, going to the formation of interdisciplinary teams capable of replicating the knowledge and experiences of the academy in each of its regions, for the particular case, in Bogotá. During this period, actors such as the academy, the industry and construction sector, as well as, the users, would achieve a close and common dialogue, supported by the technical and economic support of the State.
