# Chatbot in endodontics. Use of artificial intelligence to serve dental and dental trauma

Traumatic dentoalveolar lesions are considered an unforeseen event that can cause dental loss, so their attention should be timely and of quality and are recognized as variables directly related to the prognosis of the affected tooth. In this regard, the interactive support network for the management of traumatic dentoalveolar lesions ENtrauma, of the National University of Colombia, presented a model of teleorientation and support to users who seek agile and timely attention to solve a traumatic dental event. The developing platform, seeks to optimize the response time that a user receives when it requires immediate attention in a virtual way. To this end, it is proposed to implement within the platform a tool of exchange of information, Chatbot, as initial advice phase to users of the teleorientation service in dental trauma at the Faculty of Dentoalveolar Dentotology of the National University of Colombia.
