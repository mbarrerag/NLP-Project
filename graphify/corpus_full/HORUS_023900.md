# INFLUENCE OF THE SOCIOECONOMIC LEVEL IN THE LEARNING OF COOPERATIVE STRATEGIES

This study aims to evaluate the influence of the social class on cooperation in a job gamified from the dilemma of the iterated prisoner. 159 undergraduate university students between 18 and 30 years will participate in the study. Students will be of Colombian and Brazilian nationality. A web application will be used that will present a sociodemographic questionnaire that includes the objective and subjective social class of the participants, a gamified task based on the Dilemma of the Iterated Prisoner (DPI) in the public domain The Evolution of Trust and a questionnaire of value judgments and discrimination of strategies during the task. It is expected that the greater social class will present less cooperation in the task. A second analysis will inform if secondary measures such as social judgments, motivations when playing, discrimination of strategies, age, sex or the country influence on the rate of cooperative responses. Finally, it is sought to verify that social class indicators predict how the social class will be classified in this study
