# Collective actions against "gender ideology": the Colombian case of the family-flagged movement.

This research studies collective actions against “gender ideology”, through the analysis of the controversy generated by the implementation of the school booklets free of discrimination in Colombia in August 2016. For this, the Movement, which is the flag of the family and the journalistic production of the different national and regional media, is analyzed, based on the use of the category “framework” which allowed the identification of the different framing processes that generated the “gender ideology”, the actors involved in the joint event, the movement led by the family and the media. From this, it is found that the mobilization generated in “family defense” was carried out from the conservative and religious sectors in the name of defending Christian principles. This involved, on the one hand, the involvement of various actors, who allowed the achievement of the objectives of the movement and, on the other, an important role of the media in the dissemination of the situation, by giving voice mainly to the statements of the National Government and presenting in defense of the tablets. Finally, it exposes the importance of paying attention to this type of collective actions that fight for the maintenance of a traditional sexual order
