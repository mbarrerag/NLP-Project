# Solitude, anxiety, depression and adoption of the role of the caregiver of people with chronic illness in San Gil, Colombia

Abstract Introduction: The caregivers of people with chronic illness adopt a new role that can involve responses such as anxiety, depression and loneliness. Describe and correlate the variables of adoption of the role of the caregiver, depression, anxiety and loneliness in family caregivers of people with chronic disease in the province of Guanentina, in San Gil, Santander (Colombia), during the year 2021. A descriptive, analytical, cross-sectional study conducted with 112 family caregivers of people with chronic diseases. A profile and instruments were used: Scale adoption of the role of the caregiver ROL, scale of loneliness of UCLA (University of California at Los Angeles) and Hospitaller Scale of Anxiety and Depression HADS. The profile of the caregivers was predominantly female; 49 years on average; married, single or in free union; maximum degree of schooling; occupation and low and socioeconomic level.
