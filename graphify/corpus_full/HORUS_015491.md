# IMPLEMENTING DIGITAL CONTENT WITH VICTIMS OF ARMED CONFLICT.

The implementation of digital content with victims of armed conflict is the product of a pedagogical strategy based on e-learning in the municipality of Samaná Caldas, which is proposed as an educational solution in a post-conflict context. For this purpose, a brief review of the literature is shown mainly to generate a conceptual basis that structures and justifies the proposal of e-learning. Subsequently, digital content is elaborated in the background of application in Colombia in educational processes and related to the different effects in populations affected by the armed conflict. Finally, it is shown that the implementation of digital educational content in virtual environments is feasible when reducing the effects of armed conflict combined with the cyclical impact of external phenomena such as the covid-19 pandemic.
