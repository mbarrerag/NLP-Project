# Regional novel: garden, landscape and territory

This research thesis proposes a way of reading the regional from the concepts of garden, landscape and territory as attitudes that configure the regional in the literary space. This type of reading allows to establish a set of relations between the spatialities and temporalities that exist in the literary text and the subjectivities that constitute them. Thus, one can account for the coexistence of the multiplicity, heterogeneity and difference in the regional novel. The methodology of this research consisted in a constellation around the proposed concepts (garden, landscape and territory) in order to avoid a reading that would frame its object of study in fixed categories, on the contrary, a type of relational and speculative reading was proposed that would allow to account for the differences, multiplicity and heterogeneity of the literary space.
