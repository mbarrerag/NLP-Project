# Ground state of fermions in quasi-1D honeycomb optical lattices.

The Tight-binding models for systems of many bodies provide a good theoretical and experimental framework for studying quantum phase transitions present in various strongly correlated materials at low temperatures. In general, these phase transitions can occur due to a fine adjustment of non-thermal parameters such that each phase is determined by a fixed energy scale. In particular, Hubbard’s Ionic model allows studying a two-part crystalline network where two phases are induced due to the competition between local interaction U and geometra of the network itself given by the staggered potential
