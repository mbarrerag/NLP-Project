# Methodological route of bioeconomic business models for the recovery of organic waste generated in Vega, Cundinamarca

In the context of bioeconomy and in order to contribute to the sustainable management of organic waste, it is necessary to propose alternatives that allow the addition of value to such waste, while contributing to the generation of environmental, economic benefits and to the promotion of employment in rural areas and vulnerable communities. This project aims to formulate a methodological route for the management and development of business models that allow the use of locally available residual biomass in the municipality of La Vega (Cundinamarca), through the design and installation of an anaerobic digestion pilot plant as a technology for exploitation. The processing routes of products and by-products of the process will be evaluated for the production of value added products focused on the generation of bioenergy and agricultural production
