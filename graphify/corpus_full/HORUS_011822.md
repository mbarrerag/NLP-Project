# STRATEGIES FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF INNOVATIVE CAPACITIES IN THE SOLUTION OF ACCESS TO COMBINED MEDICAL DEVICES, A LOOK FROM PHARMACEUTICAL SCIENCES

The design of products supported by quality-based methodologies implies the appropriation of solid bases of knowledge on materials, technology, functional properties, as well as their respective correlation, to bring to the market a competitive product and adapted to the needs of the patient. In the case of combined medical devices (MCS), their access is limited both by cost, and by the complexity in the design, especially in health centers where there is no staff with high degrees of specialization for its handling. In this investigation epistaxis was identified as a frequent problem of the population, where not all patients find a solution, especially those with lower resources. Thus, the design of a MCD as an alternative system to overcome the identified deficiencies of the products of the market. For the development, it was based on knowledge about the microstructure of the system and understanding of the dynamics of a DMC, in its interaction with the surface of the nasal mucosa; at this stage, elements of the microfluid science were considered as the most suitable approximation in the understanding of the mass transport phenomena that occur during the release of an active principle and the dynamic of the functional fluid was used
