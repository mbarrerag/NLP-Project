# DIFFICULTIES FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF METHODOLOGIES FOR IDENTIFICATION OF ATIPETIC VALUES IN INTRADIARY HYDROMETEOROLOGY SERIES

The time series of hydroclimatological data serves as a basis for planning and territorial planning, as well as the analysis of extreme events and climatic trends. However, if these series have significant errors such analyses will be affected or will be biased (Crochemore et al., 2019; Ministry of Environment, 2019). Therefore, it is important to have appropriate quality controls that guarantee the consistency and homogeneity of such data for the analyses required. In addition, the incorporation of new technological developments has allowed to improve the systems of acquisition and communication of hydrometeorological data, so that nowadays it is counted with high resolution data (e.g., fiveminutal) that accumulate quickly and therefore apply quality controls becomes increasingly complex.
