# The scholastica dispute rebuilt according to handwritten and printed philosophical sources that circulated in New Granada

This article aims to reconstruct historically and methodologically the scholastic disputatio and to approach the way this type of dialectical game was exercised in the Jesuit colleges of Hispanic America. Examining handwritten and printed sources, the rules that the participants of the philosophical contests of the Baroque scholastic were obeying are listed and discussed. The logical and dialectical structure of the disputatio is examined in the light of the method of discussion abstracted and systematized by Aristotle in the Topicals. It highlights the privileged place of the ability to distinguish senses as an instrument of dialectics in the scholastic disputatio
