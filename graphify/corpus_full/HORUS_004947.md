# The language of music as a training experience in the learning processes of students of the primary acceleration course of the IED Antonio José Uribe

Music plays a fundamental role in everyday life. Nietzsche (in Safransky, 2019), said it “without music, life would be a mistake”. She accompanies us, moves us, and makes emotions and feelings of all kinds flourish.” They have “Music is capable of encouraging us; it awakens the spirit of prayer, understanding and love. It clears our minds and we know that it makes us smarter” (Campbell, 1997, p. 8). The art of music is a tool with double purpose: on the one hand, its use as a passive accompaniment and, on the other hand, its learning in the classroom with the student as an active subject. Based on this, this project seeks, with the contribution of music, to stimulate and strengthen cognitive development, and the critical and emotional thinking of students of the Primary Acceleration course, of the Back to School programme at IED Antonio José Uribe, and for this we will base our work on three very important authors of musical pedagogy: Émile Jaques-Dalcroze, Zoltan Kodaly and Karl Orff.
