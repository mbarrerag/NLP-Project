# Love of strange people

Love of rare people is a collection of short stories with themes that oscillate from everyday to fantasy with shame and disorder. Some of the stories are traversed by love, its manifestations and pains, so that in their forms they underlie searches of voices, particular characters, other common ones, but that try to open the door to play, irony and various manifestations of orality. .
