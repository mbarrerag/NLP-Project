# Study of the oncolytic potential of human rotaviral isolation Wt1-5 in gastric adenocarcinoma.

Despite advances in biomedical research, gastric cancer remains one of the main causes of morbidity and mortality in the world due to the limited effectiveness of conventional therapies.The aim of this study was to examine the oncolytic potential of rotaviral isolation Wt1-5 in samples of gastric adenocarcinomas obtained from six patients undergoing radical gastrectomy in the University Hospital of the Samaritan.To achieve this, the ability of rotavirus to spread in the tumor and the importance of expression of the corceptor proteins of the cytoplasmic membrane αVβ3, PDI, Hsc70, Hsp90, Hsp60 and Hsp40 during tumor cell infection was evaluated. It was found that these proteins are expressed differentially in the tumor cells compared to the non-tumor adjacent tissue, and that all neoplastic cells were significantly infected compared to the adjacent non-tumor tissue, which started an oncolitic effect.
