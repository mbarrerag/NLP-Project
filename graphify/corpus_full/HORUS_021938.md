# Teaching guides for work at home: Environmental education.

A didactic unit was carried out for the teaching and learning process of environmental education in the Technical Educational Institution El Placer-Marquetalia, Caldas, with different guides under the pedagogical model Escuela Nueva. The didactic guides are created thinking about the needs observed in the school environment regarding environmental education, are based on four moments and are complemented by simple activities to promote a family environment due to the confinement decreed during the health emergency by Covid-19 or SARS-Cov2. The methodological approach was qualitative, since, it was thought of a personalized follow-up through social network WhatsApp and physical means due to the isolation that was held with the rural students of the campus. Two tests were developed validated by experts, the population is composed of 16 schoolchildren and the design was quasi experimental. The results are organized in three moments: description of the previous knowledges around environmental education, creation of pedagogical guides (units) and recognition of the possible changes after the intervention. The findings are analyzed in the discussion in light of certain theoretics and their appraisals.
