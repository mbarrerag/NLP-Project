# LOGISTICAL INFRASTRUCTURE LOCATION AT REGIONAL SCALE. A PUBLIC AND PRIVATE PERSPECTIVE

The different theoretical approaches that have addressed the relationship between the logistic infrastructure and the territory have focused on analyzing the positive and negative effects of the growth and expansion of the logistic infrastructure at regional level.However, little has been deepened in the knowledge of the causes of these impacts, or in the elements that make up this relationship. The present paper, addresses the analysis of the development of the logistic infrastructure at regional level, in a spectrum that goes from the criteria of decision of the business organization that carries out logistic activities to the decisions of the public sector on the use of the land and the provision of public services. From the local planning to a system of governance and regional planning. Thus, the objective of the research is to identify and evaluate the decision variables that influence the location at regional level of the logistic infrastructure destined for the storage and storage from a public and private perspective. The development of the research involves a theoretical approach to the relationship of the logistics and the territory, the traditional models of location and its application in the decisions of location at regional level.
