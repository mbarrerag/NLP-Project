# Teaching strategy to support the teaching and learning processes of dynamics in the tenth grade.

The present research has as general objective: to analyze the perception of students in the tenth grade against the use of the GeoGebra application in the teaching and learning processes of dynamics, using as main resource simulators constructed in this application, accompanied by a didactic strategy based on the development of virtual laboratory guides that complement the proposed technological resources. This research is classified as an applied research, with mixed approach and concurrent animated design (DIAC). The work is developed with students of the tenth grade of the Educational Institution Francisco José de Caldas de Villahermosa Tolima, in four phases. In phase 1, an initial diagnostic test type test is designed and applied; in phase 2, the technological resources are developed with the GeoGebra application, the laboratory guides to guide the use of simulators, exit test, to confront the learning results and this phase is completed with the design of a test with a Likert scale; in phase 3, the plan of action structured in five moments is executed. In phase 4, the results are analyzed that show noticeable advances in the assertivity levels of the test, the analysis is carried out by the academic level of the 38.9%
