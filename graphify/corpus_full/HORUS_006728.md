# The incorporation of the doctrine of deliberate ignorance into the crime of money laundering in Colombia: Challenges and proposals

The laundering of assets is a complex crime that, due to its high impact, has been widely regulated at international and local level. As a result of the diversification and evolution that organized crime has led to, the modalities of this criminal phenomenon have mutated and are currently committed by a multiplicity of subjects within a logic of division of labour. Therefore, being faced with a crime in constant change, the State must reform the strategies designed to counteract it. As a foundational basis of criminal law, the criminal dogmatic can be an interesting place to propose changes around the treatment that has been given to the crime of money laundering. In this sense, it was sought to establish whether the foreign doctrine of deliberate ignorance could be applied in the crime of money laundering in Colombia. This purpose led to a profuso analysis of the elements and requirements for the configuration of the money laundering, and then to investigate those specific to ignorance, thus to what constitutes the regulatory authority of the Colombian criminal law, following a normative trend of the eventual. . This purpose detailed a profuse analysis of the elements and requirements for the constitution of money landing, in order to order to then investigate those of willful blindness.
