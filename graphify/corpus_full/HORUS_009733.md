# Social inclusion in the development of tourism in rural areas.

The sustainability of tourism in rural areas requires respect for cultural and natural heritage, and implies the inclusion of the local population. However, the tourism offer in rural territories is growing with products similar to those of mass tourism, is overlooked, that these activities must be alternatives that take advantage of multifunctionality and promote multiactivity to contribute to the social and productive inclusion of the local population. There is evidence, for some rural territories, that the management of the tourism destination is not optimal and has been generating on exploitation of resources in the territories that support the activity. The municipality of Villa de Leyva (Boyacá ¿Colombia), is considered the main destination of interior in the central region of the country, in which there is co-existence the development of conventional tourism modalities with that of tourism in rural spaces. The objective of this communication has been to characterize the local actors linked to the development of tourism activities in rural spaces in the territory, in order to identify the contribution that this activity is carrying out to the social inclusion of local actors.
