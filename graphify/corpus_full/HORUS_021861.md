# Interpretable machine learning model for predicting corporate credit ratings.

Corporate credit ratings are one of the most relevant financial indicators in credit risk analysis. These are generated by various rating agencies which base their methodologies on the use of different financial variables of each company and their impact on the market is of such importance that their mismanagement can trigger major financial crises such as the one that occurred in 2008. Along with this, the great importance of internal credit risk calculation models and international regulations and agreements seeks to have a level of explicability of the methods used in carrying out this risk management. In this work, the use of Machine Learning (AutoML) as a tool for the generation of a machine learning model that makes a prediction of corporate credit ratings by using data from company balance sheets, financial statements and descriptive information was proposed. Additionally, as a main contribution to this work, the inclusion, within the AutoML, of the level of interpretability of each model as a second objective to optimize, allowing the generation of models that can better explain its results
