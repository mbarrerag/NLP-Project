# ROL OF HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS FROM THE INCLUSIVE INNOVATION APPROACH

The study uses a bibliometric exercise supported by the analysis of networks to learn about the approach to higher education institutions from the inclusive innovation approach. The aim was to understand the characteristic role of universities, from the 21st century, based on the inclusive innovation approach. To this end, consultations are carried out in specialized databases that allow the identification of scenarios where these institutions play a leading role in transforming societies in terms of equity and sustainability, generating initiatives where diversity of actors, especially those located at the base of the pyramid, participate. The main findings point to the fact that the institutions of higher education of the 21st century are called to generate transformations in society, for this purpose they have relied on Innovation especially, of Inclusive Innovation as that differentiating factor, allowing them to incorporate new dynamics from teaching, research and extension to face the main problems and challenges of humanity
