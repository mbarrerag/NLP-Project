# NEW PERSONS LOST BY COLONIAL SENDERS

The research group, Literature and History, of which I have made a part, and the chair on colonial Latin American literature, have allowed me to approach a world that raises many questions about its conformation, its processes of symbolisation and creation of senses.One of them has to do with the origin of the prose of fiction.I have come to conclude that its origin is actually found in some texts and colonial figures.However, the specific conditions of the new colonial society did not allow in any way its open expression, for which they had to go to complicated processes of masking.Today we can try to run the mask and discover splendid process of fictionalization from the year of the arrival of the discoverers and conquerors until the end of the colonizing process at the end of the eighteenth century. I consider that the reassessment that have had the studies of the Baroque, the re-discovering of texts of the colony and a new conception of the genus, because they justify a study that reveals the scope of the fictional writing in the sixteenth, seventeenth and eighteenth centuries in Latin America and their contribution to the cultural project founder of our identity.
