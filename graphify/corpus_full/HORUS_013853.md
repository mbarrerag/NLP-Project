# -Living the countryside in Palmira: A parallel between agro-industry and rural living

Some rural habitats have undergone a transformation in the 21st century related to globalization and the growth of communication technologies. To study this topic, a case study will be carried out in the village of La Vuitrera located in the municipality of Palmira, Valle del Cuca. For the development of this research, a conceptual debate is proposed in which the concept of habitat is based and an approach is made to the way of understanding it in rural territories. For this, work is done from a phenomenological approach based on the experience throughout the time of different actors of the territory, the different forms of which they appropriate and their ways of living expressed from their daily life. This taking into account the rural context of the 21st century that some authors consider a new rurality. However, the discussion is opened if these transformations of the habitat that involve a change of activity in the field where food sovereignty is actually lost are a new rurality or rurality is being lost as such
