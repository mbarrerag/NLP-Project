# A UNEGRAL CLASSIFICATION OF LATIN AMERICAN UNIVERSITIES

The rankings are classifications of universities constructed with criteria that correspond to the model of elite university that defines the entity or institution that produces them. In this work, positions and scores were used in the criteria used by three well-known university rankings that publish positions of Latin American universities. Empirical evidence of inconsistencies in the positions assigned by the three rankings to universities is shown, because these only partially coincide with the first positions, while, for the rest, the positions are quite dissimilar. It is also shown that the year-to-year variations between positions assigned by the same ranking are very small, so that their annual periodicity is difficult to justify. At the end, a ranking of Latin American universities with two indices is proposed, in which the first index corresponds to each of three subgroups of universities constructed with joint criteria of the three rankings. The second index, on the other hand, classifies (order) universities within the subgroups according to the criterion in which the average score of the subgroup is greater than the average of the entire group of universities. The proposed ranking has the advantage of characterizing and classifying the universities with mixed criteria of the three original rankingss and therefore the most rich universities
