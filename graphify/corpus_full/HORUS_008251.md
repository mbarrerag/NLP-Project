# Integration of stakeholders in the software design and development process through citizen participation strategies in the context of smart cities

The transition of cities to more intelligent environments is favoured by the development and implementation of products and services of Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) co-created with the actors of the territory. Software systems are widely used as an ICT product to facilitate citizens' access to digital public services and solve perceived problems. In this context, the development of software systems presents additional challenges to those evidenced in conventional processes, due to the number and diversity of stakeholders, the absence of documented processes or the difficulty of obtaining clear requirements. The citizen participation techniques such as co-creation are an alternative to facilitate the work with different actors of the territory in the development of software-based solutions. However, there are no well structured processes that incorporate this type of strategies to facilitate the development of software in this context. Due to the above, this Theses of Mastery are proposed a method for the development of software systems in the context of smart cities focused on the citizen.
