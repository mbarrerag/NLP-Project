# Geophysical mechanisms for the formation of columnar diaclases in volcanic rocks: case studies in Colombia.

The formation of patterns is a natural property of nonlinear dynamic systems, these systems tend to self-organize into spatial-temporal patterns, whose study can give us an idea of the underlying physics. Columnar joints are one of the most fascinating examples of patterns in nature. Worldwide there are countless examples of rocky massifs and deposits with joints organized in columns and polygons. This thesis presents observations, both qualitative and quantitative, of thirteen lava outcrops with columnar disjunction in Colombia. The configuration of joints in these rocky massifs allowed identifying the faces of columnatas and entablaments, and when describing the morphological characteristics that present the columns, It was possible to infer that the mechanism of cooling predominant in the formation of the columns corresponds to the cooling assisted by fissures.
