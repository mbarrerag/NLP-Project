# LINKS-INERMERIA: INSTRUMENT FOR EVALUATING A HEALTHFUL LABOUR ENVIRONMENT*

To evaluate the psychometric properties of an instrument designed to measure the work environment for nursing care practice. 4 phase methodological study: 1) elaboration and writing of items, 2) facial and content validity by experts, 3) pilot test, 4) construct validity and reliability. ENLASA-Infermeria is an instrument consisting of 59 items distributed in 2 parts. Part A: dimension of organizational structural components and part B: dimension of organizational processes. The results obtained allowed each part to be classified as satisfactory in terms of facial validity and content; with satisfactory internal consistency and internal structure that allowed recognition of the conceptual elements of a healthy work environment that support its content. ENLASA-Infermeria is a valid and reliable instrument, promising for the investigation and evaluation of healthy work environments of clinical nurses in the hospital environment in Colombia. Key words: nursing, working conditions, methodological research in nursing
