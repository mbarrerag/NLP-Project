# METHODS OF CLASSIFICATION OF THE COMPARTIED EPITOPE AS SIGUN GREGERSEN AND VRIES allow adequate CHARACTERIZATION OF PATIENTS WITH ARTRITIS REUMATOIDE IN A COLOMBIAN POPULATION

Introduction The most important genetic association in rheumatoid arthritis (RA) was presented with some alleles of the HLA DRB1 gene that encode the shared epitope (EC). Objectives Apply the methods of classification of the EC of Gregersen et al., of Vries et al., Raychaudhuri et al., Mattey et al., and Tezenas du Montcel et al., in a group of Colombian patients with AR, and determine the most frequent alleles of the HLA DRB1 in this population. Diagnostic methods for AR, genetic study of the HLA DRB1 region by Luminex® technology of 50 subjects AR and 50 healthy. For comparative analysis of EC classifications, the exact tests of Fisher and Chi-square were applied and the contees for the AR-related alleles were performed.
