# EQUILIBRIA WORK-VIDA AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO MENTAL HEALTH IN DIFFERENT PROFESSIONAL WORKERS: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

Globalization and the search for organizational efficiency have blurred the line that balances work and personal life. It is becoming increasingly difficult to separate roles and avoid extending working hours, which can lead to overexposure of psychosocial risk factors, affecting workers’ mental health, increasing costs for organisations.The aim of the article is to characterize the relationship between work-life and work-life balance WLB, by its acronym Work-Life Balance, and the mental health of workers stress, depression, anxiety,burnout, angst and fatigue, for which a systematic review of literature was carried out,and by the analysis of 43 studies it was possible to clarify the concept of WLB. Were the general findings presented on the relationship WLB and mental health,and did it analyse the context of studies with emphasis on sectors or occupations.
