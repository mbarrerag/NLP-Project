# Peasant market of the Chaparrito village of Arauca and its contribution to the economy and food and nutrition security of the families that make up it

The peasant markets are constituted in a force that drives the economy and favors the food security of many households in Colombia both small and medium producers providing the opportunity to dynamize the economy in the agricultural sector, with products of excellent quality and at very comfortable prices for the consumer. Therefore, this research analyzed the process of the Farm Market of the Chaparrito area of the municipality of Arauca and its contribution to the economy and food and nutritional security of the families that make it. To this end, a qualitative methodology was implemented, based on a documentary design and the realization of inmersions in field such as observation and qualitative analysis of data, to identify and respond to the object of study that is based on the axis of access to the food covered by the concept of food and nutrition security defined in CONPES 113 of 2008, being this interdisciplinary concept that combines the economic, social, agrarian and health sciences elements, which has been improved, which has also occurred for the concept of peasant markets. Therefore, the instruments of collection of information have investigated on aspects related to the peasant economy, the commercialization of food and nutritional security. In general terms, the experience of the peasant markets has been and households in Colombia, both small and medium-sized producers, providing the opportunity to boost the economy in the agricultural sector, with products of excellent quality and at very low prices. comfortable for the consumer.
