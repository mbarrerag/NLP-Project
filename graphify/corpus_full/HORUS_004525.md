# IMPACT OF THE VILLAMARIA CASE CULTURAL PROJECTS (CALDAS, COLOMBIA)

In the cultural field of Colombia, a common practice is observed in the formulation of plans, programmes and projects in departments and municipalities. However, these statements are usually limited to the determination of quantitative achievements, which serve as a reference point for assessing their scope. However, the lack of a local information and cultural communication system makes it difficult to assess the impacts of cultural projects managed by various organizations and agents. Likewise, the absence of precise diagnoses does not allow for systematic information on the grassroots cultural organizations and the projects developed. This book offers an exploratory research approach with the general purpose of examining the impact of cultural projects on the territorial life of the municipality of Villamaria, Caldas, Colombia. Using as a basis the statements of governmental, private and community cultural policies, this work seeks to provide a clearer view of the influence of these projects on the local community
