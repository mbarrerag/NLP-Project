# ELECTION AT FOUR FOUR FLOWS? USE OF INTERPARTI-DISTANT PRIMARIES IN COLOMBIA'S PRISDICULATE ELECTIONS OF 2022

Objective/context: This article seeks to characterize the mechanisms of primary interpartisan elections for the selection of candidates, to construct a typology of interpartisan primary elections, and to investigate the particularities of this phenomenon based on its use and effects in Colombia's presidential election of 2022. an empirical-descriptive case study is prepared based on the analysis of the electoral results of interpartisan consultations and the presidential elections of 2022. it is emphasized that the primary interpartisan elections constitute a novel mechanism that seeks coordination between party and non-partisan organizations for the definition of a unified candidacy in presidential elections. In this process, electoral participation, ideological coherence and internal competitiveness are factors that can help explain the internal power distribution of the coalition and the subsequent electoral performance of the candidacy. Originality: this article contributes to the literature by proposing a framework of analysis for the understanding of the interpartisan primary candidates as a mechanism for the selection of presidential candidates and expands the existing knowledge about the phenomenon, whose unit of analysis has been predominantly the party organizations
