# Update and optimization of the IDF CURVAS CALCULAR FOR COLOMBIA

The hydrological design in Colombia faces the challenge of estimating flows in uninstrued basins due to the limited availability of hydrometric stations. Intensity-duration-frequency curves (IDF) are crucial for this design. Traditionally, IDF curves are built on the basis of pluviographic records, but the lack of these data drives the use of synthetic methodologies. In response, the project of upgrading IDF curves for Colombia developed the generalized IDF curves calculator (Cal IDF-G), a tool in RStudio, which estimates maximum rainfall intensities for different durations and periods of return. The original version of Cal IDF-G included four estimation methods. Recent improvements, detailed in this article, incorporate the methodology of Díaz-Granados & Puente and update the IDF curves for 26 new stations in the Caribbean region, expanding the database with updated storm station registers until 2010. The tool now allows to suggest typical storm durations and optimised its code to improve its efficiency. Updates also include the validation and comparison of methodologies in the Colombian Caribbean, with emphasis on accuracy and estimations
