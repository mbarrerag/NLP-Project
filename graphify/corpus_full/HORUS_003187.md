# ANALYSIS OF LLUVIA EVENTS IN HIGH MONTH CITY

The joint relationship between the average rainfall and the hydrological response at the seating point of a basin is what we consider a rain event. The aim of this study is to analyze the extreme events recorded in the Chinchiná river basin, Popular Forest station, located in the city of Manizales. The study is carried out by creating a database that identifies and relates the main variables of these rain events. By ease, a methodology is used that considers only the simple extreme events of a single peak whose origin is a single rain. The data collected by the database for each extreme event are: the initial level, peak level, increase of level, and times associated with different stages of rain, such as the total duration of rain, the duration of the maximum and total rainfall of the event. The average rainfall in the basin was calculated by the reverse of the distance to the square. The results indicate that the characterisation of the variables of the extreme events facilitates the identification of significant correlations between them, and emphasizes the importance of selecting variables that can be measured in real time, allowing to establish precise relationships for the development of a suitable protocol to be performed in the event
