# USE OF MATERNAL LANGUAGE IN THE CLASSES OF FRENCH AS ALIEN LANGUAGE: PERSPECTIVES OF THE STUDY

This paper examines the views of a group of uni-versity students on the use of the mother tongue in French Foreign Language (FLE) classes, by them and their teachers. First, it describes certain functions that the mother tongue can play in foreign language classes; among these figures, linguistics, communicative, sociocultural, cognitive, psychological, mental or organizational. In the same way, some of the benefits and disadvantages that the use of the mother tongue generates are taken into consideration. For this study, the data were collected through an opinion survey and then used quantitatively. The results show that about half of the students resort to the mother tongue because they have gaps in French. More than half of them are taken into consideration to communicate with the teacher and peers with greater naturalness or to understand better. A number of people consider using the mother tongue when they have difficulties in expressing in foreign language. It also shows that students at basic levels do not agree that they stop using Spanish in French classes, and conclude that they use it to use the mother tongue as a language to have difficulties in foreign language.
