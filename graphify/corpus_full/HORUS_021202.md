# Design and prototyping for the conversion of a combustion engine vehicle to a three-wheel electric vehicle

This proposal seeks to implement ingenierile processes in the students of the seedbeds having as strategic line the clean energies and sustainable development. The project promotes the strengthening of the creativity of the students and contributes to improve the livelihoods of the populations of immediate interference of the National University of Colombia in the Caribbean region. The alliance between two seedbeds of different headquarters (SACE and SINCRo) is based on the utilization of the capacities and experience of the seedbeds. In the case of SACE it is a new seedbed, while SINCRo has several years of experience with excellent results in the learning of the students1. The collaboration between the two seedbedyards takes advantage of the engineering knowledge and the national conjuncture focused on an energy transition to implement the use of clean energies in the communities adjacent to the headquarters of La Paz.
