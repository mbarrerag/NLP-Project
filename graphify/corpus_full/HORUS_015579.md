# CONCEPTUAL AND METHODOLOGICAL FUNDAMENTS FOR THE SYSTEMIC EVALUATION OF ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT - ESIA

Many developing countries such as Colombia are holders of large natural wealth in eco-systemic and anthropic goods and services, which position them in a privileged way in the international context. However, the globalization processes and market rules imposed by the current model of global economic development, make these countries (with the consent of their States) to base their development almost exclusively on the exploitation of their non-renewable natural resources (RNNR); most of the time through foreign capital and multinational companies, with very few considerations on the institutional, economic, social and environmental impacts that their activity generates. To counteract (or facilitate) the above, at least as regards environmental impacts, since 1993 many of these countries have been implementing processes of environmental licensing, such as Colombia, where the Environmental License was initially established as a mandatory requirement for the execution of works, the establishment of industries and the development of any activity, which, according to the law and regulations, can lead to serious deterioration of renewable natural resources or the environment or to introduce significant or nominating environmental changes (Art. 49, Law 99/93), and defines the authority as whether the activity is carried out in accordance with the law and regulations. Environment - SMA, the Institutes of Information and Research (IDEAM, IAVH, SINCHI, IIAP, INVEMAR), the Ministries, the Productive Sectors and the Civil Society, perhaps because of the absence of an environmental policy of a state nature, that orients, dynamizes and articulates an environmental management in the country. All the above problems identified prevent the impact assessment from being a holistic and systemic process, which ensures the incorporation of causal relations between the actions of anthropic and the environment, not only in the area of influence of the projects, but also in the sphere of influence of the policies, plans and programmes that allow them. This, together with other political and macroeconomic factors, decreases the effectiveness of the impact assessment, environmental licensing and of course, of environmental management. In order to give adequate response to the previous problem, in this chapter, synthesizes the conceptual and methodological foundations towards a systemic assessment of the environmental impact applied to policies, plans, programmes and projects, as well as the main aspects to consider for its implementation and development in Colombia
