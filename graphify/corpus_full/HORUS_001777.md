# Approaching the state of art of current technologies for green hydrogen production

Green hydrogen, produced through water electrolysis processes, uses electricity generated by renewable sources such as solar and wind, resulting in one of the most promising alternatives for decarbonisation of the global economy. Unlike grey hydrogen, green hydrogen does not generate direct greenhouse gas emissions during its production, which positions it as a fundamental element in the transition to a low carbon economy. The growing concern for climate change and the search for sustainable solutions has accelerated research and development of efficient technologies to produce green hydrogen. For this reason, the present work critically reviews technologies for green hydrogen production focused on the types of electrolyzers, in order to compare and analyze their benefits, effectiveness and limitations.
