# NUTRITION IN DEMOGRAPHICAL Aging

milena moraes postdoctoral at mcgill university, canada. doctorate in medical clinic and master's degree in human nutrition from the federal university of rio de janeiro, brazil. teacher of the area of nutrition clinic adults of the department of human nutrition, faculty of medicine of the national university of colombia. Colombia and the world have gone through a demographic aging where the older adult population is significantly increasing generating the need to strengthen policies, programs and projects for this population group, because of its biological, psychological and social changes. These changes are linked to the functional capacity and nutritional needs of this population group considered vulnerable nutritionally. In this context, Colombia due to its rapid and progressive demographic aging, has since 2007 the National Policy of Ageing and Ageing that has been updated in 2014, highlighting the importance of training professionals, through teaching on the different topics related to healthy aging and old age, with the aim of improving health care to the older adult population. Consequently, the nutritionist dietist has a fundamental role since 2014, highlighting the importance of the process of healthy aging. Additionally, the teaching and care to older people need to be improved among professionals of the greater nutritional support
