# Administrative process for strengthening the family: Organizational and systemic approach, cross-cutting to Christian spirituality

This work deals with the administrative process of the family, in which it is intended to achieve the welfare for the families of the Christian faith, by formulating an administrative process that allows planning, organizing, directing and controlling the family life, the relations and the cohabitation within the homes, that in the development or development of their lives have realized that they lack tools to obtain good and better results in each of the roles present in the family nucleus, namely: father, mother and children.
