# The failed peace agreement process in the protection of children and adolescents in the context of the Colombian armed conflict.

The permanence of the armed conflict in Colombia and the phenomenon of recruitment strongly affect the rights of children and adolescents. With the beginning of the peace process with the armed group FARC and its subsequent signature in 2016, the hope of the decoupling for the return to civilian life of the NNAC and the commitment to non-recruitment generated important points on the political agenda of the country, such as the protection of the subjects of special protection, justice, truth and non-repetition of violence, however, the failure to comply with the agreement, due to multiple socio-cultural, political, economic factors and the inability of the State to protect all its associates, resulted in a failed peace process, the continuation of violence against the NNA and the uncertainty of the decouperation of the NNAC and its subsequent return to civil life. In order to respond to this problematic situation, various factors were studied that allowed to contemplate a historical, political, economic and social landscape, which enabled the formulation of alternatives for the protection of the NNACs and NNACs.
