# --The armed conflict in two Colombian triple-borders between 2017 and 2024: Guainía and Putumayo

-In recent years the Group of International Relations and Global Affairs (RIAG) has dedicated itself to enriching academic production through collaboration and co-creation with communities and social leaders in border territories.This approach has allowed to broaden our understanding of the international border status of Amazonas and Orinoquía, critical areas due to its relative lack of knowledge in existing literature.The current call for strengthening aims to expand the capacity of the group to serve as a reference and analytical production center in border issues, focusing especially on cross-border relations and the impact of armed conflict.RIAG aims to strengthen itself as a fundamental actor in the study of borders and border dynamics.The findings of our call 2022 highlight how the conflict tensions have increased in border areas, affecting disproportionately the ethnic communities.The research is focused on two specific tri-borders, selected not only by the existing academic gap, but also by showing a marked concentration of poverty and inequality in the Pacific coasts and in the large regions of the Amazon and the Orinoquía. comparatively the challenges and prospects of border exchanges, the strengthening of academic links through participation in relevant events and the preparation of a publicity document that addresses the problems in territories inhabited mostly by differentiated ethnic communities
