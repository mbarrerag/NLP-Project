# ♪ SORKING FALS BORDA, THE SENTY FOTOGRAPH ♪

This book brings together a collection of 72 photographs that form part of the Orlando Fals Borda field diary on the sidewalk of Saucio (Cundinamarca). These photographs are part of the Central Archive of the National University of Colombia. The editors of the book carry out a task of curating, selecting and organizing the photographs that we consider most relevant when it comes to rebuilding the look of Fals. The book is accompanied by two essays that intend to guide the reader in the observation of the photographic material. The first essay of biographical cut by Marlon Celis. The second essay of analysis of the image by Veronica Salazar. The book also has a prologue of the American anthropologist Joanne Rappaport
