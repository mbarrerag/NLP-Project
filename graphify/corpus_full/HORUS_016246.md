Logistic 4.0 is the adoption of digital technologies in the different processes that span the supply chain. Currently different organizations expect an impact of these technologies in their operations and business models, however, understanding the concept Industry 4.0 is complemented and even more in the processes of selecting technologies, evaluation, implementation and improvement that comes to look for with them; therefore, this work raises the union of logistics 4.0 with the ecommerce sector due to its particular needs, exponential growth and strategic priority of the companies.
