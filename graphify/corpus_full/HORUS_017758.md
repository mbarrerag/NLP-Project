# 1821 TO THE 21st CENTURY: COMPARISON OF THE CONSTITUTIONAL LAW

The leading question is that of transforming constitutionalism according to the concepts of large-scale political-social change, interpreted as progressive in its respective epoch. In other words, the approach is not so much the guarantee for pre-existing structures, but rather that of analyzing the intention to open up to new horizons through constitutional law. Therefore, the author does not content himself with a simple textual comparison, but rather observes the paths of materialization of promises and their limitations due to socio-cultural factors. In this, the concept of constitution is understood beyond the so-called text, in the sense of what is actually constituted in a certain system of power. The starting point of the analysis is the liberal, bourgeois and constitutional revolution from 1810 onwards.
