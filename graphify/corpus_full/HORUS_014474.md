# Chemistry laboratory as a strategy for the development of critical thinking.

Critical thinking as an indispensable competence of the 21st century requires being transversalized by the different areas of knowledge, in Chemistry practical laboratories are scenarios that should have a reflexive and proactive look at the agricultural and environmental processes of the region, the chemistry laboratory allows to develop skills specific to critical thinking focused in the context of educational institutions. The aim of this proposal is to design practical strategies for the development of critical thinking through work in the Chemistry laboratory and to implement a guide built based on the attitudinal and basic skills of critical thinking, identified by diagnostic tests, in the students participating in the project. The students of the tenth grade of the La Felisa educational institution of the municipality of La Merced were the ones in charge of appropricing this work, through a methodology of mixed approach and interpretative descriptive scope. The results found show that there is a significant change in the two aspects evaluated, in the attitudinal aspects of the project.The students demonstrate greater interest in employing critical thinking in their academic and daily activities, while, in the basic skills of reading, writing and oral expression, demonstrate a favorable inclination towards having taken into account the critical thinking components that will improve these competencies
