# KARIJONEA WOMIRÍ EJORÍ. WE LEARN CARIJONE!

The book was built collectively with the community and its contents and exercises, as in its illustrated support. It consists of eight lessons with pedagogical objectives, a pronunciation and reading text, pronunciation and consultation exercises for speakers, vocabulary practice and grammatical constructions. It also has games such as crossword puzzles and letter soup." The work is part of the project to revitalize the Carijona language and culture, in which the community is engaged
