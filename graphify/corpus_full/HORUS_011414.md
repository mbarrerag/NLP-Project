# COMMUNICATION IN CARE MANAGEMENT IN INTENSIVE CARE UNIT

To characterize the scientific production related to communication as an essential element in the management of care in the Intensive Care Unit ICU. Integrative review through the search in database EBSCO, SCIELO, MEDLINE, CINAHL and scientific journals for a total of 27 articles that met the inclusion criteria. The characteristics of communication in the nursing-patient relationship are presented identifying the own skills that are carried out in the management of care in the ICU. Communication as a key element in care management is implicit in it, however, the particularities of the care scenario proper to the ICU lead to highlighting empathy and warmth as skills to develop by nursing. Patient-family and nurse communication is characterized as a joint experience: frustration where patients are aware and experience communication difficulties, as well as communication with families in which the information provided is not clear to them. key words: communication, management, nursing care, intensive care unit
