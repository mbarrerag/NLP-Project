# RETROCES AND CHALLENGES IN THE CONSTRUCTION OF PEACE: THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE INTEGRATE NATIONAL PROGRAMME OF ILLICIT CULTURES (PNIS) IN COLOMBIA

In this chapter of this book we intend to argue that, although the Peace Agreement serves as a precedent in the formulation of public policies for the construction of peace with a hybrid approach that opens the door to greater citizen participation in its elaboration, to decentralization and contextualization, to the recognition of realities and diverse communities, among other integral aspects to overcome the structural conditions of the internal armed conflict, the reality shows to be diffused in the face of the use of a model of liberal peace in the implementation of Point 4 of the Agreement on the solution to the problem of drugs Ilí- citas. This contradiction is evidenced by the way in which the items of access and participation to the eradication and substitution programmes were eluded that proved to be exclusionary, of low capacity and that they could not guarantee the security, well-being and sustenance of the farming communities through legitimizing and democratizing participation; and finally, due to the selective, pro-ductive and collective distribution linked to the aidist component and the system of transfers for the distribution of resources
