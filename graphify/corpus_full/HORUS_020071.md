# Passive dynamic system for energy returning on transtibial prosthesis

The advance in the development of lower limb prosthesis has accelerated during the last decade due to the implementation of devices and optimal materials for the lost function. Likewise, the lifestyle of the prosthesis users has changed, so that a greater comfort and yield is required for an acceptable quality of life. Unfortunately, the most demanded sectors are the most vulnerable populations of the countries, which are mostly in development. These affected populations do not have the resources necessary to acquire a prosthesis adequate to the degree of mobility required, which results in a reduction in the preferred speed to walk or run without biomechanical alterations. The main causes of an amputation of lower limb are given by diabetes mellitus, traumas (transit acid, anti-person mines, unexploded ammunition, etc.) and finally, the cancer. Although the bionic pros work, among others, it is difficult to reduce the extra metabolic cost, these are heavier and costly compared to passive pros. The GSA allowed us to implement an optimization algorithm based on a surrogate model whose purpose is to establish optimal design variables based on input parameters (walk dynamics). This optimization algorithm presents a number of design variables that do not allow it to converge (≥ 20) to an optimal solution. To solve this inconvenience, Bayesian optimization is proposed to determine the design variables that obtain the most approximate ankle dynamics to the nonpathological one. To which results were obtained very favorable to the provisions in the passive prosthetics market. With this proposal, customizable prosthetics can be configured that, through additive manufacturing, can be tangibly and adapted to amputated patients, regardless of their degree of mobility and anthropometry
