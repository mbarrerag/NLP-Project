# CHEMOINFORMATIC ANALYSIS OF NATURAL VOLATILES ORIGIN COMPOUNDS AS POSSIBLE ADDITIVES FOR FUNCTIONAL PACKAGING

The international market of agricultural products such as bananas could be at risk. Use prohibition of chlorpyrifos by multiple healthy agencies represents a significant danger. In other hand this prohibition could be a serious risk for packaging businesses because these industries use chlorpyrifos in many of them functional products. This pesticide is toxic and has a low selectivity. For this reason, agribusiness will need new effective, accessible, and safe replacement agents. Natural volatile compounds (NVC) could accomplish the previous necessity of agribusiness pesticides, these have unique scaffolds and multimodal characteristics. In this study was made research on NCV as alternatives to traditional synthetic pesticides such as chlorpyrifos. The methodology was divided into two steps. In the first step, we created and curated an NCV database and found the principal physicochemical properties related to volatility. After that COCONUT database (January 2022 version) with 407270 unique natural compounds was filtered, finding natural compounds with physicochemical properties in the volatility range. This cheminformatic study gives new entities with possible volatility for their use as pesticide agents. For the future, we pretend to do volatility assays and biological tests for a possible replacement of chlorpyrifos
