# Composition of fatty acids in fruits of Ocotea caudata (Nees) Mez (Lauraceae) from Colombia

Abstract In the family of Lauraceae, the genus Ocotea presents species as sources of wood for construction, woodwork, biofuel, disinfection, cosmetics and for the treatment of various diseases. Ocotea caudata has few phytochemical and pharmacognostic studies: this work is the first to study the fruits of this species. The fruits of O. caudata (Nees) Mez from Colombia were investigated for their oil content. The heptane extraction of the oil from the fruits of O. caudata yielded 54.9 % total lipids and the composition of fatty acids was determined. The GC-FID analysis (Gas Chromatography Flame Ionization Detector) from the oil indicated that the lauric acid is the most abundant fatty acid in the oil (51.7 %, C12. In addition to the lauric acid, the oleic acid was found (28.6 %, C18:1) and palmic acid (16.6 %, C16:0). The lauric acid is considered as a source of numerous benefits for the oil
