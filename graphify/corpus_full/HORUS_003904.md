# MOLECULATE DETECTION OF ENDOSIMBIONES IN FLEBOTOMINES (DIPTER: PSYCHODIDAE) PRESENT IN TWO DEPARTMENTS OF THE COLOMBIAN AMAZON.

Leishmaniasis are caused by parasites of the genus Leishmania, transmitted by about 14 species of phlegatominous in Colombia. These dipters are distributed in tropical areas such as the Amazon region where the opportunistic presence of the females has also favor their establishment in recently colonized areas, represents the main risk factor of contact with the pathogen.The biology of these insects and the increasing resistance to the chemicals used for their control suggest the search for sustainable alternatives such as biological control.The study of endosimbiotes with high biotechnological potential could be key for the design of these alternatives due to their influence on reproductive alterations and even on the transmission of pathogens.The aim of this study was to detect and identify the endosimbioants Wolbachia, Cardinium and Microspordia in phlebotomines, while the first study Caquetá and Amazonas from the total DNA of 9 species previously identified, the product was submitted to PCR with the WSP81F and WSP691R for Wolbachia, CLOF1 and CLOR1 was sent from Cardinium, SS18F and SS and SS14rd and SS92R. recognised as vectors and other non-vector species
