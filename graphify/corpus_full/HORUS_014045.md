# Geophysical exploration applied to groundwater exploration in the University City of Bogotá

The University City of Bogotá (CUB) receives daily a growing population that today oscillates approximately 35000 people and whose average bimonthly water consumption is 22000 m3. The source of water for the CUB is exclusively superficial, opening the possibility to alternative sources such as the underground, exploited in the city and widely in the Bogotá Sabana, but not in the CUB. In the Master Plan of Aqueduct, that the Headquarters will build for the CUB, considering long-term solutions for the supply of water, the underground sources will be evaluated within the options to consider. Despite this panorama, the aquifer levels located more than 50 m depth in the campus lack of geological and hydrogeological enough information regarding its spatial distribution, such that they allow to select those areas more promising from the exploratory point of view, making it necessary to optimize the conceptualization of the movement of the underground water or hydrogeological model conceptual (MHC) for the campus. Our proposal is based on the use of the indirect methods most used in the exploration of resources of the subsoil, the geophysical exploration, specifically through the geoelectric and seismic methods that improve the state of the knowledge of the MHC. between the company and the university for the above purposes
