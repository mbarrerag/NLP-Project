# Prototyping for NORM (U-Th-K) on-site measurements in environmental matrices.

Within the elements that make up the planet earth we find some materials that we call NORM., whose acronyms mean Natural Origin Radioactive Materials (in English Naturally Occurring Radioactive Material). This type of materials was already known by the International Atomic Energy Agency, as by the associated members included our country. In 2002 when the general regulation of radiation protection and safety was issued, Resolution 18-1434 was defined in general way the term natural exposure, as the “Exposition caused by natural sources.”, also within its articulated is related that the exposures of these natural sources will be subject to control, as specified by the Regulatory Authority. To date, on this type of materials there is no specific regulation, beyond the concentrations of Radon that must be guaranteed in homes and in the workplaces. Some years ago the attention on the exposures that can generate the NORM materials, has intensified as it has been more data; since there are places within the territory that can have concentrations of natural origin radionuclides, as the uranium, thorium and potassium; that there is a public care based on the tools of drilling for oil wells, and that have been performed; since there is no more information; since there is no specific place where there is no specific place This work was performed simulation in GEANT4 to find the most appropriate disposition of the detector set – source, so as to obtain better results in the experimentation that was carried out using a monitoring equipment of sodium iodide radiation (NaI), a radioactive source of Cesium 137 (137Cs) and different measuring materials (Air, water and phosphite); this in order to have a way to measure the concentrations of 238U, 232Th and 40K; and that through the identification of radionuclides and the dose rates that are generated, a decision-making alert is established on these formations and on the tools used in them that could be contaminated due to concentrations of natural radionuclides above the limits established by norm
