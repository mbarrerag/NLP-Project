# Continental integration in Africa and dynamics of political participation of African diasporas in a global context of migration: Equatorial Guinea, Ethiopia and Negritudes of Colombia (Work Plan Migration and Displacement Group: ACTIVITIES 2022-2023)

Within the framework of the work plan of our research group, it is proposed to carry out a series of six (6) activities that are reflected through the common title: _x0093_Continental integration in Africa and dynamics of political participation of African diasporas in a global context of migration: Equatorial Guinea, Ethiopia and Negritudes of Colombia. In this regard, the proposal includes main activities around (1) the holding of an academic event to commemorate the 60th anniversary of initiatives to seek African union through the Organization of African Unity (OAU), today the African Union (AU); (2) the inquiry into Equatorial Guinea in the era of globalization; (3) the incursion into a historical journey about the process of formation of Ethiopia (_x0093_land of gods and place where Nile is born), as a modern State; (4) the approach of an exploratory study on some cases of migration conceived as challenges to border control in Africa, Latin America and Asia; (5) the investigation on the problem of migration, childhood and adolescence, with emphasis on guaranteeing rights for migrant minors Colombia and Chile; (6) the organization of an academic event on the political participation of the Negrutus in Colombia
