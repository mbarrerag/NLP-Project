# Effect of exogenous application of brasinosteroids on growth, photosynthesis and biochemical responses of tree tomato (Solanum betaceum Cav.) under conditions of watering

With the influence of climate change, the global flood pattern has been increasing in recent years and will increase in the future, causing affectations in the agricultural sector. In agricultural crops, soil watering directly affects the photosynthetic machinery of plants and, therefore, yield. Tree tomato is an important Andean fruit tree for the Colombian region, and is considered as a species susceptible to radical hypoxia, so it requires the development of tolerant strategies. Brasinosteroids (BR) are growth regulators involved in the plant response to environmental stress, and its exogenous application has provided tolerance in several species by interacting with the antioxidant response and signaling with ethylene.
