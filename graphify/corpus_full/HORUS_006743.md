# Confrontation and adoption of the role of the primary caregiver of the person with heart failure

The heart failure not only represents a burden for the health of the sick person, but also impacts on the quality of life of the family caregiver, who assumes the role in an unexpected way, initiating an adoption of the role in which he must learn to face the changes that the disease brings with regard to his management, recognizing and developing instrumental skills. Establishing strategies that will allow him to address the situation in the best way to provide good care and reorganize his life to manage and face his other roles and adopt a healthy care role.
