# INTERACTIVE WEB-BASED TOOL FOR EVALUATING THE SPREAD OF BOVINE TUBERCULOSIS AND BRUCELLOSIS IN COLOMBIA

Bovine tuberculosis (BTB) and brucellosis are associated with devastating losses in the livestock sector in Colombia and even in developed countries. Therefore, real-time disease surveillance is a key strategy to control and eradicate infectious disease outbreaks. To design an epidemiological tool for BTB and brucellosis monitoring in Colombia. An interactive dashboard for disease mapping of BTB and brucellosis between a 2004 and 2019 as observation period was designed. Our analysis showed that the Provinces of Cundinamarca and Valle del Cauca are regions affected by BTB and brucellosis epidemics, respectively (p<0.001). Furthermore, increased case detection was reported in 2012 for BTB and during 2019 for brucellosis (p<0.001). This epidemiological dashboard can allow tracking disease hotspots, identifying trends over time and thus providing useful information to animal health authorities for designing new strategies in control programs
