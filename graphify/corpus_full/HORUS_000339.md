# Challenges and Priorities in Safety and Health at Work in Colombia, 2025

This editorial summarizes the main findings of Colombia's occupational safety and health profile, highlighting the challenges facing the country in the context of occupational safety and health and the priorities that must be addressed to improve the conditions of the Colombian working population
