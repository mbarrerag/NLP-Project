# A SMALL CORNER OF THE STARS: ASTROTORUISM FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT AT TATACOA-COLOMBIA

The small corner of the stars, a tiny house next to the local observatory at Tatacoa (Colombia) is the first astrotourism laboratory vinculated to local fauna and flora, astronomy and care of the enviroment. Through collaborative work with science clubs conformed by scholars of the local school of Tatacoa, we are conducting experiments including basic sciences, aimed to expand the current touristical offer. In this contribution we present preliminary results of this project and current challenges we are facing concerning the sustainability of this initiative
