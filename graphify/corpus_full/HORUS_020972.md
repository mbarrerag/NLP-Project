# DEVELOPMENTS IN THE CHARACTERISTICS ASSOCIATED TO THE ACTIVATIVE CAPA OF THE OCEAN IN THE OEST OF ANTI-ARTICAL PENINSULA DURING THE LAST 40 YEARS

The ocean absorbs most of the excess heat associated with global warming and the polar regions have been affected most, inducing a change in ocean and atmospheric dynamics that impacts the climate, ecosystems, sea level and global ocean currents that transport oxygen, carbon and nutrients.The polar (Artic and Antarctic) regions play a fundamental role in climate dynamics because they reflect the planet in its heat and vapour exchange with the warm regions (Ecuador, tropics).The Antarctic has experienced a warming during the last years mainly to the west of the Antarctic Peninsula (OPA).The surface layer of the sea is the first to react to atmospheric changes, with the Ocean Active Layer (CAO) being the most sensitive to the variability of the meta-marine parameters in the ocean-atmosphere interface.The evolution of the CEO will affect the processes of heat exchange, mass and momentum both to the atmosphere and to the depth of the ocean, influenced by the ecosystems.The present research aims are to analyze the evolution of the energy characteristics associated with the CAF5.The evolution of the CEO during the study region for the year 1981-year-years. The project will contribute to the advancement of knowledge of one of the regions most vulnerable to the effect of global warming, being a sector of the planet that contributes to the regulation of the climate through the general ocean circulation. It is expected to strengthen the lines of research in ocean-atmosphere interaction, physical and dynamic of the ocean of the Faculty of Sciences through the formation of a young researcher in the Master's Degree in Meteorology. Interdisciplinary work will be achieved with the intervention of 1 ing. oceanologist and 1 ing. chemical. Likewise will be achieved the national and international visibility by the dissemination of preliminary and final results in academic or scientific events, and the preparation of an article. This project is of interest to national and international organizations and institutions such as the CAP, the Colombian Ocean Commission (CCO), the Regional Study of the Phenomenon El Niño (ERFEN), Scientific Commission Antarctic Research (SCAR) among others.
