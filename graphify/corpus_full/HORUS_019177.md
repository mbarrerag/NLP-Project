# DESIGNALITY, MALTRAT OF WOMEN AND DURATION OF MATERNAL LACTANCY, COLOMBIA 2010: ECOLOGIC STUDY

ntroduction: breastfeeding reduces morbidity, mortality and promotes maternal and child health. The lower morbidity relative to non- breastfed children reduces health costs. In Colombia, the duration of breastfeeding is far from desirable and continues to decrease. the objectives were (a) to determine economic inequality based on poverty at the ecological level during exclusive breastfeeding (LME) and total breastfeeding (LMT) and (b) to establish the social determinants of the MML and LMT using the territory as unit of analysis. an ecological study was carried out based on the data collected in the 2010 National Survey of Nutritional Situation in Colombia on the median duration of exclusive breastfeeding and total breastfeeding. The data were summarized in 24 departments or geodemographic units representing the Colombian territory (n=24). Fourteen variables representing the levels of economic, social, human and structural development of the territory were related by linear regression to explain the duration of the MML and LMT.
