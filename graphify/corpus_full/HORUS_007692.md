# Prototype of community music therapy with adult caregivers: _x0093_Manzanas del Carer_x0094_ locality of San Cristobal, Centro de Desarrollo Comunitario San Blas, Bogotá, Colombia.

The prototype of Community Musicotherapy with adult caregivers, which aims to throw as a product, the seedbed of Community Musicotherapy of the Master's degree in Musicotherapy, in the call of the faculty of arts, to support seed workers, contributes to the governmental initiative "Manzanas del Cargado", which offers free services to women caregivers in the town of San Cristobal, Bogotá.With a duration of six months, the project seeks to elaborate a proposal of prototype in community music therapy, to strengthen the social fabric and promote cohesion within the community.The main objectives of the project, with respect to women participants of the care block, are: 1. facilitate communication by creating spaces where participants can express their emotions through music, thus promoting an open and honest communication, which encourages empathy and respect. 2. strengthen the work in team through group dynamics that improve cooperation and sense of belonging between the participants. 3. strengthen the community identity, connecting the participants with their cultural and personal roots, in order to strengthen their sense of community. 4. create support networks in establishing the emotional and social support through the teachers and the participants. 3. strengthen the participants's identity, the participants
