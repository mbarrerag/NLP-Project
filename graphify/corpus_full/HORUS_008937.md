# THE ROLE OF COMMUNITY OLLAS IN THE FRAMEWORK OF SOCIAL AND POPULARY STALLIDE IN COLOMBIA DURING 2021

In Colombia, 2021, a proposal of Tax Reform generated great inconsistencies in the population leading to the maximum manifestation of social organization, which came in growth from the spaces of neighborhood and popular base based on a whole history of organization and struggle, leading to the consolidation of the so-called Social and Popular Starburst. The research aimed to identify the dynamics that gave rise to the Social and Popular Starburst and its relationship with food, the construction of social fabric, food and nutrition sovereignty and security. qualitative methodology with application of methods such as participating observation, self-ethnography, ethnography of archives and focal groups with activism processes. Information was collected during the years 2021 and 2022. The Community Wave can be conceived as an organizational practice of community food, self-convoked and self-managed, organized collectively to cook a common food, under the need to supply appropriate, continuous and participatory responses, absent from the Government for the guarantee of collective well-being. The community pots are constituted as an alternative political, social and food empathy for the families affected by the structural determinants of food and nutrition.
