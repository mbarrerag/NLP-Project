# Estimation of the components of genetic variation in the vegetative phase of progeny of American palm oil (Elais oleifera).

The oleifera is a palm native of America and there is little research on genetic parameters for improvement programs. Twenty-four progeny in nursery (10 and 12 months) and 16 progenies in juvenile stage (14, 21 and 40 months); in response to fifteen vegetative traits and genetic analysis was performed with the design Carolina del Norte I to estimate genetic and phenotypic parameters in E. oleifera progeny of the Agrosavia work collection. The analysis of individual variance and combined with the GLM procedure of the statistical program SASv9.4. In nursery the source of female variation within males presented highly significant differences (P≤0.01) for stem diameter, plant height, length and width of the follicles, long of the rickys, area of the petiole section and number of leaves; significant differences (P≤0.05) for folliole numbers. The higher proportion of genetic variance was attributed to the effects of dominance.
