# GLOBAL MEDIUM ACCESSIBILITY IN IBAGUÉ

The following article will study the city of Ibagué of the department of Tolima in Colombia, carrying out an analysis of global average accessibility as a qualification measure for the offer of transport infrastructure in private vehicles, for this one will use information obtained from the city as GPS speeds the transport network represented in GIS geographic information files and also will have a geostatistical model by the Krging-Co-Kriging method, with which results were obtained such as that that that the city has average travel times between 10 and 30 minutes and as conclusions it is determined that there is a spatial inequality for the low socioeconomic strata.Keywords: Accessibility, modelling, Kriging, Offer
