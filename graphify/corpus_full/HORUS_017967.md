# The entrepreneur as consumer in dual-purpose contracts: proposal to reform the consumer statute in Colombia.

This master's work in deepening modality explores a legal basis for applying the Consumer Statute (Law 1480 of 2011) to small entrepreneurs, to micro, small and medium enterprises (SMEs) when it acts with dual purpose and in a certain way integrates the product acquired into the production chain. In Colombia, the consumer order allows a natural or legal person to satisfy their own, private and business needs that are not “intrinsically” linked to the economic activity of the entrepreneur. However, the rule does not explicitly regulate the case of the dual purpose, that is, when the entrepreneur meets private and business needs at the same time. The response provided by the courts in Colombia seems to exclude from the field of consumption the entrepreneur that integrates the product or the production chain in some way. Faced with such a lack of work proposes from a national law analysis and compared a pro-consumer interpretation that distinguishes whether it is a contract between companies (B2B) or between business and consumer (B2C), and concludes the need to reform the notion of consumer in favour of microbusinesses that in a consumption relationship act with double purpose
