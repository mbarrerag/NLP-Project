# ITINERATING DIALOGUES IN CULTURE, TERRITORY AND MANAGEMENT: PROBLEMS AND ARTICLES OF THE CULTURAL MANAGEMENT OF WATER FROM LOCAL FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OF TERRITORIES

The CP in Culture, Territory and Management has its relevance in function of the characteristics and possibilities generated by the territories in their organizational forms, of living, their identity expressions, moreover seeks to relate the concepts, methods and processes for the design and accompaniment of the relationship between public policies of water and local cultural ones with the ability to generate synergies and innovation in the socioeconomic, political and cultural development of the territories. It is a question of joining efforts and generating institutional agreements to support the component 9 UNal of: _x0093_«Excellence for the sustainable development of the Territories", the mother project of the Vice-Rector of Research "Harmonization, strengthening and visibility of the functions of research, extension and innovation for a management of collective and transformative knowledge". The CP in Culture, Territory and Management, aims to expand the spectrum of reflection, today limited of the relationship of the cultural management and the use of water in the local socioeconomic development processes, with a pilot in five (5) territories: -Teusaquillo (Bogotá), Arauca and Manizales, Villamaria (urban-rural), Sabanetaria (Antia) towards a direct integration of the local social and productive processes, with its productive and regional characteristics
