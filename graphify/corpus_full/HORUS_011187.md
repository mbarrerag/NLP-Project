# Effect of the inoculation of growth-promoting bacteria in the dynamics of edaphic phosphorus in kikuyo (Cenchrus clandestinum Hochst. ex Chiov.).

Co-inoculation with phosphorus solubilizer bacteria (PGB) in association with phosphorus sources (P) can improve the availability of P in the soil, generating more sustainable crop systems, efficient in the use of nutrients and of greater productivity. The implementation of these technologies in grazing grasses such as kikuyo established on soils Andisols with high phosphoric retention has been little explored. The aim of this research was to evaluate the productive response of the kikuyo grass and the P dynamics in the soil to the PGPB co-inoculation with different sources of P.
