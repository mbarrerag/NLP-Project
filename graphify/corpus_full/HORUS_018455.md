# Antibiotic profile of streptococcals of the oral cavity isolated viridans group in individuals with prolonged use of antibiotics

ABSTRACT The streptocoques of the viridans group (EGV) are a heterogeneous group of anaerobic optional saprophytic bacteria that form part of the normal oral cavity flora. The increase in antibiotic resistance of this group of bacteria becomes important in patients hospitalized with prolonged antibiotic therapy, in which these microorganisms can be replaced by multiresistant bacteria. To determine the percentage of strains of GHS isolated from oral cavity resistant to antibiotics used in the treatment of various infectious diseases in hospitals. Material and Methods: Descriptive observational study in which 60 patients hospitalized with history of antibiotic therapy were included for more than 3 weeks. Samples were processed following standardized protocols of collection, isolation and identification for oral cavity GHS and a protocol of antibiotic sensitivity tests. The results are shown in tables and graphs of frequency. The main indication for intrahospital antibiotic therapy was orophacial infection: 28.33%.
