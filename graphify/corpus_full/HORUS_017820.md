# Evaluation analysis of reaction mechanisms to model biomass-derived gas combustion

Abstract A fundamental part of the simulation of combustion processes is to model the chemical kinetics that takes place in the phenomenon with the greatest possible accuracy. On the other hand, in complex combustion simulations that involve the dynamics of computational fluids (CFD) of the system, the computational resource is a critical factor to take into account. Based on the above, this study evaluates the performance of four semi-detailed reaction mechanisms (DRM22, C1-C4 of Heghes, GRI 3.0 and Konnov), to model the kinetics of combustion of synthesis gases derived from biomass in CFD simulations (motors, turbines, burners, among others). The methodology consists of carrying out computational tests to obtain results related to the delay to the ignition variable. These simulations were carried out in a reactor at constant pressure varying different parameters of the combustion process. The results obtained with semi-detailed mechanisms were compared with those obtained with a detailed mechanism (Westbrook), by means of calculation of errors. It is found that the applicability of each kinetic content depends on the variables of the process
