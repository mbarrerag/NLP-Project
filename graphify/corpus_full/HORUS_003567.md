# GEOCHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF THE LEVEL IN THE PROVINCE OF Huelva, SPAIN

In recent decades, the studies of environmental geochemistry have experienced a remarkable growth and relevance due to the increase of awareness on human impacts in the environment.From the environmental point of view, characterizing geochemically a territory means knowing the origin, concentration, distribution and destination of an element that represents a risk to the health and quality of life of living beings. Recent research has evidenced the widespread presence of organic and inorganic pollutants in natural and urban environments, as well as their adverse effects on human health and the quality of the environment. One of the elements that has caused environmental pollution and health problems in many parts of the world is lead, a fundamental chemical element in the history of humanity, from its use in antiquity to its presence in all areas of the modern era.The present work shows the characterization of the lead in the province of Huelva, Spain by means of analysis of soil samples taken in three places with different geological environment and different use of the soil.
