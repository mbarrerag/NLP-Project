# Effects on educational coverage following the implementation of the School Food Programme for Indigenous Peoples in Colombia

This research project seeks to establish the effects on educational coverage following the implementation of the School Food Programme for Indigenous Peoples (PAEPI) in Colombia. In this way, a literature review and data collection will be carried out, and an econometric model will be estimated that will allow to determine whether there are differences in educational coverage between the departments that have introduced the program and those that do not, despite having indigenous territories or the majority indigenous population. Likewise, with the above, it is intended to provide inputs for the discussion of public policies with a differential approach
