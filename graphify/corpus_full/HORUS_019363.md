# INVLICATIONS OF PANDEMIA IN RADIOTERAPY FOR THE CANCER OF MAMA.

The current pandemic for the new coronavirus (SARS-CoV-2) requires that health systems quickly adopt measures to mitigate the crisis, which means redistributing economic, social and labour resources, so that those sectors of the population most affected can be treated in an optimal and timely manner. In patients with in situ and low-risk invasive breast carcinoma, postoperative radiation therapy does not offer any benefit in overall survival, making it attractive to omit this cancer resource. In this article, a review of the literature on the criteria for omitting adjuvant radiation therapy in patients with low-risk breast neoplasms was carried out. Additionally, the recommendations issued by some international scientific societies during the current contingency are summarized, and certain reasons for which doctors refuse to change clinical behaviours that offer limited advantages, often counterbalanced by the associated risks and adverse effects are analysed
