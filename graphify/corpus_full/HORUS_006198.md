# Physicochemical properties and stability of an extract of calyces from Physalis peruviana

Physalis peruviana is a plant used in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties which are attributed to its phenolic compounds. Rutin is the major flavonoid in P. peruviana calyces extract; this flavonoid has been used in the treatment of different diseases. The aim of this study was to evaluate the aqueous solubility, partition coefficient, and stability of rutin in the ethanolic extract of calyces from P. peruviana and the standard of rutin. Standard rutin is very slightly soluble in aqueous media while rutin in the extract was classified as slightly soluble. In the same way, the partition coefficient for rutin in the extract was higher than the isolated rutin. Regards to stability, isolated rutin showed higher degradation under acid, alkali, oxidation, and photolytic conditions than the rutin in the extract. The results suggested that the extract from calyces from P. peruviana is a promising rutin source with advantages for pharmaceutical formulation
