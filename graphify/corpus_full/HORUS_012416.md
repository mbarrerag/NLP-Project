# Do mesh houses influence reference evapotranspiration (ETo)?

The mesh houses are an alternative that allows the farmer to protect his cultivation from pests and diseases, and allows to reduce the application of agrochemicals. These mesh houses are usually constructed with antitrip mesh (porous screen), which prevents the passage of insects thus protecting the crop. As is expected, the mesh generates a microclimate inside, which could lead to a change in the water needs of the crop, but there are no studies in Colombia that quantify the changes produced within these mesh houses in the weather variables. This study employed two weather stations and two reference surface evapotranometers, placing a cenirometer and a station inside the mesh house and the other two outside, with the aim of observing the influence of the mesh house on the evapotranspiration of the cenirometers and the evapotranspiration of reference or ETO. The results showed that inside the mesh house the wind speed, relative humidity and solar radiation were reduced, and increased the temperature.
