# INTERPRETATION OF THE TERRITORY OVER THE AERIAN PHOTOGRAPH

In this chapter it is shown how from the analysis of photo interpretation of old images can be reconstructed a territory, specifically in the sector of Suba, where still visible the vestiges of the prehispanic agricultural systems. It is copied part of the chapter: For the specific case of the photo interpretation carried out in the current sector of Guaymaral - Las Mercedes, the study of these old vestiges had two advantages: the first, that because over the last 500 years the space was not strongly modified by labors of cattle or agriculture, product of its own historical process as it was shown in the previous chapters, the vestiges of the old agricultural system were preserved exceptionally if compared with the rest of the Sabana of Bogotá; the second advantage, although in this case it is to be placed between comillas, derived from the fact that for the time of taking the aerial photographs of the territory was not deforested, which facilitated the clear visualization of the vestiges of the old camenes. In this sense it is necessary to take into account that in Colombia, as in other regions of the continent, it was made in the last decades great discoveries. Hammen, in the 1950s and today, is evidenced as in general the Sabana was 70 years ago more devoid of forest cover, with the exception of the relicto of the forest of Las Mercedes that was larger, which shows that the campaigns carried out in recent years for the recovery of the ecological environment of Andean forest, especially by the Botanical Garden, is yielding important fruits. It is also surprising to show that, despite the countless greenhouses, the space reserved for the wetland of La Conejera is today greater than in the 1950s, since as can be seen in image 3, its basin is currently wider and is better protected by forest vegetation on its edges. On the other hand, the analysis of the vestiges of the systems of ditches and old camenes showed that during the prehispanic period the inhabitants of this area built mainly chess-type camels, destined for the management of floods by high level of the soil. Consequently, large channels for the control of high volumes of water were not identified, interpretation that is in tune with the fact of being a fluvished terrace and not in the high level of soil
