# Distributed generation based on hybrid systems case study: La Guajira, Colombia.

In this work, a proposal for a hybrid power system (HPS) with solar photovoltaic technologies and biomass gasification is presented with the possibility of being implemented in the department of La Guajira. The HPS includes a gasification system of 30 kWt and 10 kWe evaluated to function with rice husk. A model of prediction of the composition of syngas was used and a lower calorific power of 3.77 MJ/Nm3 was obtained used to determine the constructive dimensions of the gasifier and select the coupled engine. On the other hand, a photovoltaic system of 12.5 kW with hybrid type investor was dimensioned to manage the DC load of the panels and AC load of the engine. The prototype designed was evaluated from technical, financial and regulatory aspects. The regulatory evaluation indicates that the Colombian regulation has established the promotion of generation with HPS with the objective of replacing the high presence of diesel generation existing in the Non Interconnected Zones and although the connection to the National Interconnected System is not mentioned in the regulations, it is allowed and in both cases the electricity generation is obtained by 14% and the technical evaluation is carried out
