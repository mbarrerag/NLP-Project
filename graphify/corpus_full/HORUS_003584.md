# EVAPORATE OF THE SOLVENT IN HOMOGENE MESCLES

The evaporation technique is used to separate homogeneous mixtures of solids-liquids. With this process, the liquid evaporates and a solid residue remains. In addition, the liquid can be recovered, condensed, or let escape, if not of interest. In this practice, the components of a homogeneous mixture (salin solution) will be separated using an assembly for the evaporation of the solvent
