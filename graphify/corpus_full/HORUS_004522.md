# Area of action and seasonal variation in the movements of females of Podocnemis expands (Schweigger, 1812) in the middle of the river Meta Orinoquia Colombian

The study of the spatial ecology of threatened species such as the karapa turtle (Podocnemis expandsa) provides relevant information on the extent of the areas occupied by individuals and the use of resources in those areas. The aim of this study was to know and describe the areas of action or home range and the patterns of movements of P. expands in the middle basin of the Meta river in Colombia, to identify useful elements for its conservation in this region of the country. For this, during the years 2020 and 2021 we captured 16 adult females to whom they were installed as sonic and VHF transmitters. We perform active and passive monitoring for 26 continuous months for the location of the study individuals by means of the homing method. With the georeferred locations in time, we performed spatial analysis to estimate the areas of action under two methods: distribution of Kernel utilization with probability of 95 and 50% (K-UD 95% and K-UD 50% respectively) and the linear range of use (ILU; mentioned in other articles as the linear home randas). In addition, we analyzed the patterns of post- and the average of movement and utilization of Kernel with probability.
