# THE MEMORY OF VICTIMS - VICTIMS: A WORK OF JOHN HEJDUK,

This article presents a reflection on Victims: A work by John Hejduk, an exhibition held at the Rother Museum on the project that the New York architect planned for Berlin as a tribute to the victims of Nazism. This analysis allows linking the Hejduk project to the context of the victims that has left the armed conflict in Colombia. The topic discussed in Berlin opens the question raised by this exhibition and seeks to resolve this article: can architecture commemorate and at the same time contribute with its action to the victims' claim?
