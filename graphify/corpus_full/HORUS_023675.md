# GUIDELINES FOR PROPHILAXIS AND THE MANAGE OF LIVE-SILVE AND MODERN PATIENTS IN LATIN AMERICA AND THE CARIBBEAN. ABBREVIATE VERSION, AUGUST 2021

These clinical practice guidelines provide evidence-based recommendations for the prophylaxis of persons at risk of infection with SARS-COV-2; for the identification of markers and risk factors for mortality of patients with mild or moderate VOCD-19; screening of VOCID-19; home management; use of diagnostic imaging to guide management; pharmacological treatment; use of supplements; prophylactic anticoagulation, and criteria for follow-up and discharge. Recommendations are addressed to all health personnel who care for patients in the emergency and primary care services (general practitioners, specialists in emergency medicine, in pneumology, in internal medicine, in family medicine and in infectious medicine, as well as respiratory and physical therapists, nursing staff and pharmaceutical chemists, among others). Recommendations are directed at decision makers and members of government entities related to the management of patients with VOCID-19. These guidelines do not address aspects related to nutrition and the management of complications
