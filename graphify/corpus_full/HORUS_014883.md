# POLITICAL AND ELECTORAL REFORMS.

The chapter on Political Reforms is one of the 23 chapters that make up the book Building Peace and Caring for Life. It was elaborated within the framework of the Violent Conflict and Peace Project of the Nation Region Thinking Center of the National University of Colombia. It was joined by 40 researchers and researchers from five National University Headquarters (Amazonia, Orinoquía, La Paz, Manizales and Bogotá), as well as other regional universities and governmental organizations. Each article presents a package of recommendations aimed at encouraging the construction of the National Development Plan 2023-2026. Based on the fact that political representation is in crisis, political reforms are analysed rules and electoral order and proposals are made to ensure the greater democratic representation of a society in motion, increasingly diverse. It is proposed to reflect the approaches of the Special Electoral Mission report and the recommendations on Democracy that include the report of the Truth Commission, along with other measures: the institutional design of elections, the procedures for the election of representatives, the processing of a new electoral code, the examination of the system of partisan recognition, the strengthening of a democratic party system, the coalitions and the regulation of the civil servants
