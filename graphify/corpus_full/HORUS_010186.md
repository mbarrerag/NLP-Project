# INTERNATIONALIZATION AND INTERCULTURE IN THE DESIGN OF THE SUBJECT OF INTRODUCTION TO SYSTEM AND COMPUTING ENGENEERING AND INTRODUCTION TO INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING AT NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

this article describes the transformation of the subjects of introduction to systems engineering and computing, and introduction to industrial engineering of the national university of colombia. This in order to achieve the Interculturality and internationalization of the curriculum. This transformation is based on the Model of Internationalization of the Curriculum in four levels. It establishes the national guidelines in the field of accreditation and internationalization of higher education
