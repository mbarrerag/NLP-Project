# Strategy for the provision of the prioritized and required infrastructure for risk prevention in laboratories at Palmira headquarters

In laboratories for the frequent use of substances, reagents, chemicals, reference materials, instruments and other devices, it is important that they be stored in protected and orderly areas, with the aim of preventing incidents, damage and accidents to laboratory users. The objective of this project proposal is to prioritize operational controls related to the infrastructure (equipment, Personal Protection Elements and Risk Signage) for the prevention of risks, incidents and accidents. The expected results will be the acquisition of printing inputs, Printing of laboratory census Stickers, printing of labels and safety data sheets of identified chemicals, delivering printed labels with the required information of standard character of substances, reagents and chemicals to professionals in each laboratory, acquisition of special modules for the storage of substances, reagents and chemicals in prioritized laboratories and marking of furniture for the storage of reference materials instruments and other devices duly located according to the SGA classification
