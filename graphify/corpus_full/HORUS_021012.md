# Organofluorine compound synthesis

Organofluorinated chemistry has important benefits in chemical-biological interaction in biomedical applications. However, fluorination is complex due to secondary reactions, low selectivity, strong conditions of synthesis, unstable intermediaries and elimination reactions. That is why in a biomedical purpose, it will be sought to improve synthesis strategies for the fluorination of chemical compounds
