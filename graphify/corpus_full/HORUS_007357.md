# Classification of Guarinó River waters with respect to discharges by implementing the QUAL2KW water quality model

Corpocaldas, under the Decree 1076 of 2015, in order to formulate the Plan of Planning of the Water Resource (PORH) for the Guarinó river. It requires the use of simulation models of water quality like the QUAL2Kw, in order to evaluate the assimilated capacity of the river before dumpings. To this, this model was calibrated with physical and chemical data of the POMCA of the Guarinó river of 2015, to determine the quality of the water and to identify the sections of the river where it should be regulated or prohibited dumpings. The river Guarinó is crucial for the supply of drinking water in La Dorada, with an average flow of 39.55 m3/s and a sewerage system that discharges in the Magdalena river. Studies indicate that, in general, the quality of the water is acceptable for human consumption with treatment, although it presents variability. The dissolved oxygen is adequate in most of the seasons, except in the E2 station where the turbidez is regular. The quality of the river is good in the dry season until the E7 season, but deteriorate when the feconclusives occur. The analysis of the drainage scenarios showed that under current conditions (E0), the river presents quality problems with increased temperature, conductivity, DBO and decreased oxygen dissolved. With sanitation projections (E1), the quality improved partially, but problems with suspended solids and dissolved oxygen persist. It is concluded that the model QUAL2Kw provides a useful baseline, however, needs updating with more accurate and up-to-date data, with the purpose of clearly defining the classes of dumpings and quality objectives.
