# “An NIC 2+ risk according to age, in women attending the Northern Subnet screening program between 2017 and 2021.”

Risk of IAS 2+ according to age, in women attending the screening program of the Northern Subnet between 2017 and 2021" Introduction: According to the latest guidelines of the WHO for cervical cancer screening, the group of 30 to 50 years of age is prioritized, while the national guidelines include patients aged 25 years to 50 years. The objective of the study is to estimate the prevalence of presenting lesions NIC2+ according to the age group in the colposcopy unit of the Northern Subnet from the findings of cytology, colposcopy and histology, in the period from 2017 to 2021. An observational study of cross-sectional cohort with sample by convenience was performed, which included data of cytology and colposcopy carried out in the period from 2017 to 2021 in the North Subnet E.E (84996 data), to which the inclusion and exclusion criteria were applied, and elimination of follow-up data, for 49957 total data, which were treated with Microsoft Excel, SQL Server and Visual Basic. 50 to 65 years old. For our population, screening is necessary from 25 years to 50 years old, since we have a high prevalence of NCI2 + in this age group and for our screening programs it should not be left out as proposed by the last WHO guide.
