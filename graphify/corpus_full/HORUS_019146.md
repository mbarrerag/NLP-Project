# CORPORAL MOVEMENT AND COGNITIVE ROLE IN SCHOOLS THE EXPERIENCE OF PHYSIOTHERAPY PRACTICE IN THE EDUCATIONAL SECTOR OF THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY PHYSIOTHERAPY PROGRAMME

This work collects the legacies of professionals in physiotherapy from South American countries and is aimed at an audience of kinesiologists, physiotherapists, students of these professions and the rehabilitation area; who will find theorized contributions and described from a specific clinical experience ranging from the epistemological frameworks and morphology of children, to their own interaction with epilepsy, haemophilia, patients with burns, prescription of exercise and neurophysiotherapy, among others. In few cases, in Spanish speaking, a text is found that proposes evidence in the interaction of the physiotherapist/kinesiologist with, for and by the children of the region, thus making this book a space for approaching this research field
