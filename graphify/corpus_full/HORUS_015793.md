# ASSESSMENT OF THE QUALITY OF LIFE IN PATIENTS WITH CANCER OF METASTASYSICAL SIN IN NEOADYUVANT QUIMIOTERAPY IN A CLINICAL TEST ALIED DOUBLE CIEGO WITH A PHITOMEDICAMENT OBTAINED FROM UNISINOUS CAESALFINIA (CS003 - BC)

Breast cancer represents the most common neoplasm in women in Colombia (1). Pharmacological treatment generates side effects that decrease the quality of life affecting the physical, functional, emotional and social well-being of patients (2). Interventions with poly molecular drugs arise as a therapeutic alternative to reduce the side effects of chemotherapy by increasing the quality of life of patients that allows greater adherence that impacts efficacy and survival (3). In the Immunobiology and Cell Biology Group, a phytomedication enriched in polyphenolic compounds (P2Et) with antitumor, immunomodulatory and antioxidant properties was developed and has been shown to be safe in preclinical and clinical studies of phase I/phase II (4.5). Polyphenols are reported to improve the quality of life of cancer patients, according to which a randomized double blind clinical study is being conducted, in which the quality of life during neoadjuvant chemotherapy is being evaluated
