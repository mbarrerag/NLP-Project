# Historic vestiges of the 4th centenary of Bogotá: Planning the future, building the past, stopping the present

The celebration of the Fourth Centenary of the foundation of Bogotá in 1938 was an event of great importance for the city. This commemoration is inserted within a Latin American context of the celebration of foundations of sister cities such as Lima, Quito, Buenos Aires, Cali, Popayán and Tunja. This event had a strong impact in urban terms, since in the years prior to its realization it was desired that Bogotá by its century-old date would become a modern city, like other Latin American cities and thus proudly boast the title of capital of the Republic of Colombia. Parallel to this vision of the future, there was also a vision towards the past in which it reflected on its Hispanic and indigenous origin, producing an interesting historical, artistic and photographic material that together with the public works form the great legacy that as vestiges of the celebration are subject to analysis in this research.
