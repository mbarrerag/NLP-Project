# IMPLEMENTATION AND DEVELOPMENT OF RESOURCE EDUCATION PLANS AND PROGRAMMES FOR EXCOMBATIENTS OF FARC-EP IN ICONONZO TOLIMA (2018-2022)

After the signing of the _x0093_Final agreement for the end of the conflict and the construction of a stable and lasting peace_x0094_ between the former FARC-EP and the Colombian State, the process of reincorporation of the former combatants was initiated through the implementation of the three point: _x0093_end of the conflict_x0094_ (number 3.2.2). Since the signing of the Peace Agreement, a set of strategies for the demobilization, disarmament and integral reintegration of the former combatants has been developed (Final Agreement, 2016). However, multiple limitations have emerged for the implementation of the agreed social, economic and community reintegration of the former combatants, including the development of education programmes for the former combatants that have an impact on the support of the economic reintegration process in terms of the development and management of productive projects, strengthening of community and territorial logics, but also, as regards the possibilities of real access to education at all levels: basic, secondary, technical, higher, etc. In this regard, the research project is concerned with the incorporation and analysis of the economic, social and territorial dimensions of the -EP (DECRET 2026 DE 2017), being Icononzo one of which has managed to make significant progress in the process of reintegration. These achieve to be spaces of ex-combatants who _x0093_propose to reconfigure, re-signify and reinvent a new way of being in the world, from the economic, the political, the social, the educational and the familiar_x0094_ (Jaramillo, 2020, p.250). The ETCR of Icononzo-Tolima contains in its space three cooperatives, a foundation and two associations, besides having approximately 300 inhabitants among ex-combatants and relatives. That said, ETCRs can be assumed as _x0093_points that would guarantee the rights of ex-combatants and their reintegration into society, being this fundamental axis in the construction of a lasting and stable peace_x0094_ (Villarraga, 2013.
