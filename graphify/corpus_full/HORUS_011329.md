# 4.16. ASSESSMENT OF THERAPEUTIC POTENTIAL OF THE EXTRACT VEGETAL ZANTHOXYLUM MARTINICIZE ASSOCIATED TO THE AGENCY ACTIVITY OF LXR IN THE MURINO MODEL OF THE DISEASE OF ALZEIMER (3XTG-AD)

Alzheimer's disease (EA) is a neurodegenerative disorder characterized by a cognitive deterioration associated pathologically with the formation of amyloid plaques and neurofibrillary ovyls. Preliminary studies showed that the plant extract Zanthoxylum martinicense (ZM) presents agonist activity LXR and its modulating activation some biomarkers of the AE as cholesterol homeostasis, aggregation of amyloid deposits and neuroinflammation.OBJECTIVES: The objective is to evaluate the therapeutic potential of the Zanthoxylum martinicense extract with agonist activity LXR in the murine model 3xTg-AD.
