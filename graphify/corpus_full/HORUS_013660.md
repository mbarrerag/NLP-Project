# Review of suite for trumpet, stringed instruments and multipercussion by Lucas Jaramillo

This document contains the formal, harmonious and stylistic analysis of the unpublished work entitled: Suite for trumpet and instruments of rubbed string and multipercussion commissioned to the Colombian composer Lucas Jaramillo. This work contextualizes the development of the trumpet through time and exalts its most important transformations in favour of its performance during the different periods of music to this day, also emphasizes how technology has contributed significantly to these changes and how the trumpet has varied its role according to its interpretative possibilities. In synthesis, this research demonstrates the need to interpret new artistic proposals that seek to enrich the musical language through its originality, and that in turn it offers the viewer a renewed facet of the instrumentist as an artist and simultaneously gives him the opportunity to seek and develop the technical resources necessary to overcome the interpretative challenges of the new contemporary trumpet repertoire. .
