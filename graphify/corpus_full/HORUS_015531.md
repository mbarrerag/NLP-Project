# CASE OF STUDY: SUPPLY OF POTABLE WATER, COMMUNITY OF BAKURUKAR

This project analyses the basic sanitation problem and the supply of drinking water to the Bakurukar indigenous community, victims of the armed conflict in Colombia. The research showed that the community presented different health effects related to poor management of its environment and to the consumption of contaminated water. Various national regulations and other recommendations on basic sanitation and the provision of drinking water in emergency conditions were examined. The research process was carried out based on three approaches: interviews oriented using an assessment matrix; assessment of the site's health facilities and the local environment; biological-chemical sampling and assessment of water quality. The results obtained show high indicators of fecal contamination in water, not suitable for human consumption. Thus, the community is in a situation of vulnerability from the point of view of access to drinking water and basic sanitation. Interviews conducted and the assessment of the facilities reveal that the community also lacks resources to achieve its sustainable objects. The research identified specific recommendations for restructuring the elements of drinking water supply to different housing and rationalizing material resources, as well as implementing a process of chemical disinfection treatment by chlorine and treatment of wastewater based on a leach field
