# HIGHER EDUCATION.

University organizations are increasingly facing pressures of internal and external complexity in a market-oriented environment, but with weaknesses in the direction of the sector, which generates mismatches of asymmetry in service, structure and quality; and which are reflected in the positive and negative performance of users and the signaling that agents choose in labour markets, as well as in the impacts that derive directly or as externalities in other sectors of the national economy. This research presents structural elements and their relationships as patterns of the architecture of the strategy in institutions of private higher education in Colombia, supported by the concepts of value, evolutionary economic theory and governance; and for this purpose it employs the analysis of strategic groups through multivariate exploratory tools that have allowed to identify 6 strategic groups on two basic cores of speciation, based on operational performance data and perceptual approaches of university managers. Finally, three dimensions or components and seven factors of value architecture are consolidated
