# Educational citizenship, disability and social inclusion. Proposed Ideological framework for the implementation of public policies for population with disabilities

This article analyses one of the frameworks of understanding used for a research project in which it is proposed to create an urban observatory for the population with disabilities in the south of Colombia. The text assumes that any political project of urban transformation requires an ideological framework that provides support. The proposal of an educational city corresponds to that political framework required to weigh the possibilities of social inclusion of people with different forms of disability, whether motor, sensory and cognitive. This text indicates the historical and political sense to which this ideological proposal of city responds, exposes the two main models of understanding that there is about disability and from there highlights the transcendence that has an educational city to socially justify public policies in favour of people with some form of disability, given that it ideologically bases such policies not on calls of social solidarity for a population considered vulnerable, but on the enhancement of the identity difference as not central to the contemporary political structure
