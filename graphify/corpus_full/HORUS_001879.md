# CONSUMPTION OF VEGETABLE RESOURCES IN THE BOGOTA OF THE XIX AND XX SECRETARY SIGNATURES TO ANALYSIS OF PHITOLYTES IN ENTAL CALLUUM

The purpose of this article is to contribute to the reconstruction of the diet of the population of scarce resources that inhabited the city of Bogotá between the end of the 19th century and the beginning of the 20th century. An archaeobotanic analysis of silicaphytolites was carried out, present in 94 samples of dental calculation of 47 individuals from the osteological collection of the Globo B of the Central Cemetery. The conclusions of the analysis of phytolytes are based on the consultation of documentary sources of the period that address the topic of the Colombian diet of the last two centuries and the results of the bioarchaeological analyses on bone remains of the collection. It was possible to identify various species such as maize (Zea mays), rice (Oryza sativa), pumpkin (Cucurbita pepo), barley (Ordeum vulgare), wheat (Triticum aestivum), oat (Avena sativa) and a species of bean (Phaseolus sp.). The amount of morphotypes identified in female and male individuals is significantly different for some species, showing a probable sexual difference in the intake of plant resources.
