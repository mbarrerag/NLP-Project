# INSTITUTIONS, ENTERPRISES AND ACADEMICS IN THE PROMOTION AND USE OF PREFABRICATED ELEMENTS AND INDUSTRIALIZED SYSTEMS.

The first part of this article describes how two Colombian institutions with national coverage, but of a different nature, used innovations developed in the country, although they did not prove economically or functionally competitive with traditional methods in the medium term. It then notes how private companies developed industrialised systems encouraged by the demand for prefabricated elements, although with little commercial success. Finally, it describes how, from a multi-lateral body based in Bogotá, attempts were made to contribute to other technical solutions, applicable even in the Latin American context, which were made known through the construction of several prototype housing on a real scale, using local materials and techniques. The conclusions attempt to take stock of the facts and give clues about new avenues of exploration in this field
