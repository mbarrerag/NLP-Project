# Global internationalization and network of war in Ukraine

The article analyzes the war in Ukraine, which since the full-scale invasion on February 24, 2022 has unleashed a prolonged war conflict that has generated profound international and global implications. With more than 36 months of combat, it has become one of the most lasting wars since the mid-19th century to today. Various sources indicate that it has left in these first 36 months of war a high number of fatalities, prisoners and missing, as well as the largest number of European displaced persons since the Second World War, while changing the certainties and plans that the States of the region had with regard to their security and geopolitical position. The war has not only allowed Russia to conceive, plan and threaten with the use of nuclear weapons, but has also become a dynamizing of technological innovations in the military field, which has been visible in the use of drones and notorious advances in artificial intelligence. This conflict has expanded in its global influence and dynamics, because from the beginning of it has led to a realigning of international alliances that dispute its conceptions on the international order, influencing with other forms of high-voluntage conflicts
