# Study and evaluation of the reconfiguration of a 2 kW thermal steam plant using comparative diagnostic tools based on the exergetic model

This document shows the research developed of experimental type, to a thermal plant of pilot scale, located at the ECCI University, Bogotá, Colombia. The research has as precedents the studies carried out by the author and Silva, in 2016, showing imbalances in the operation and operation of the plant, which led to the approach of a design that would allow to correct those problems, starting from a complete diagnosis of the initial figuration. Subsequently, improvements were proposed to the figuration, starting from the results obtained to the energy and exergetic analysis, which allowed to decipher the equipment involved in the ineficial science of the plant, mainly the vapour condenser. Thanks to the resources provided by the ECCI University, some of the improvements proposed were implemented, showing satisfactory results in the operation of the plant's STATIONAL, and increase in the thermal inefficiencies of the cycle, total and exergetic, since the design and implementation of the overheater were decreased. the inefficiency of the plant, mainly the steam capacitor.
