# Photostability enhancement in oligo (phenylene vinyle) systems and their application as heterogeneous photocatalysts.

The first chapter reviews the most relevant aspects of the use of conjugated systems type phenylen vinylene (FV) in different photocatalytic applications. Although the photochemical activity of these systems has been described in fields ranging from photodynamic therapy in biological systems, degradation of pollutants, chemical synthesis and hydrogen production, it is clear that substantial improvements are required in terms of photostability that allow further research in this field, considering that the vinyl fragment is primarily responsible for the degradation of these materials by limiting their exploration as photocatalystizers. However, there is sufficient experimental evidence that allows proposing strategies such as the assembly of more rigid structures, the use of electroactor substitutes and the use of inorganic supports to mitigate the fragmentation of the FV system and thus, substantially improve their performance in the above mentioned applications.
