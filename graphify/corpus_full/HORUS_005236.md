# [Does the most high EFEMÉRIDES of our civil life: the centennial of the Congress of the CCUCUTA ROSSIAN AND THE NATIONAL ACADEMIA OF COLOMBIA HISTORY, 1910-1923

In order to nuance the conclusion of the invited editors of the achsc regarding the writing on the Villa del Rosario Congress between the founding republican history and the university history, we will examine the initiatives of the anh in the context of the centenary of the Congress. Thus, the text is structured in two parts. First, we will describe the way in which three number scholars characterized the Congress in two works of different scope: the book that was officially adopted for the teaching of the history of the homeland in 1911, that is, the work of Henao and Arrubla, and the fragment of a biography of former President José Ignacio de Márquez published in 1917. The second part refers to the information that circulated in the main national newspaper on the occasion of the centenary of the Congress in 1921 and the way in which the anh tried to participate in that ephemeride by publishing a new volume of the National History Library (bhn). As a context, we offer an overview of the commemorative actions that were carried out in the town located in the department of Norte de Santander.
