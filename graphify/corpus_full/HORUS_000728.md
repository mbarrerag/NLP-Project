# Applicability of ultra and extra petita faculty in the administrative labour litigation process under the principle of favorability

The aim of this investigation is to analyse the application of the power to decide extra and ultra petita in the Administrative Dispute Jurisdiction in labour matters of public employees, specifically under the principle of favorability and considering the normative framework established in Article 50 of the Code of Labour Procedure and Social Security. The above will be carried out by means of a study of the characteristics of this faculty, compiling relevant case law. It is sought to determine the scenarios in which administrative judges can apply it in accordance with the protection of fundamental labour rights. The main question is whether this power is actually being used, given the impermissible nature of justice in the Administrative Dispute and if its application is carried out in an appropriate and favourable way for the protection of labour rights. Given the above, a comprehensive study of the case law of the Administrative Dispute Jurisdiction was carried out, identifying cases in which the legal status of the State has been applied or omitted to use the power of non- and non-advertent failure in labour matters.
