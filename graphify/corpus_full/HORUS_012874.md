# Fiber options sensing platform for monitoring biological and chemical species at low-concentrations

The research work carried out for the production of this doctoral thesis has as fundamental objective the development of a platform of sensation supported in optical fiber technology that allows the detection of chemical and biological species at low concentrations. This work is presented as an alternative solution to the problem posed by the lack of portable technology of sensation in Colombia, that allows to make measurement in situ and in no more than a minute of low concentrations of substances, particularly biological samples such as the serum albumin bovine or the species Mycobacterium tuberculosis and the levels of mercury in the water. In response to that the current robust and rapid techniques of detection and analysis of substances, at low concentrations, usually require of expensive equipment, specialized personnel and are little portable, which hardly contributes to its application in field and limits the possibility of reaching the vulnerable populations.
