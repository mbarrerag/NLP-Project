# Parallel genetic operators for the solution of COMBINATORIAL problems.

Many problems combined with a combination due to its structure and complexity stand out as problems do not have a positive impact on the traditional methods of mathematical optimization. example of these are the problem of the traveling agent (tsp: travelling salesman problem, by its acronym in english) (lawler & lenstra, 1985), the problem of linear ordenamiento (garey & johnson, 1979), the problem of the maximum clique (pardalos & xue, 1994), the problem of partition of grafos (langauer, 1990), the problem of grafos packaging or the problem of quadratic assignment problem by its acronym in english) (burkard, cela, pardalos, & pitsoulis, 1998), among others. The QAP is a general case of many other problems (for example, all those mentioned above), i.e., many problems combined with QAP are applying variants appropriate to the matrices that define them. Each of the above problems corresponds to the class, i.e. A matrix formulation of QAP that takes advantage of the characteristics of the GPU as a vector calculation device. The matrices that define the QAP are housed in suitable memory spaces for the purpose of accelerating calculations. Appropriate parallel algorithms were designed to reduce computational complexity in the different calculations of the fitness function. The selection operator consists of an elitist binary tournament between the corresponding individual declared in each GPU block and another random individual housed in that same GPU block. The new individual is selected if his or her aptitude is better or equal to the ability of the random individual. The implemented crossing operator was a modified order crossover (MOX). This operator acts as follows: A crossing point that is common between the two parents is selected randomly. The genes to the left of the parent 1 crossing point are copied to the offspring and the rest of the genes of the other parent (father 2) are copied in the order in which they appear (this prevents gene repetition). MOX was also implemented at the block level in the GPU. An intermediate population of random individuals (population of couples or parents 2) is obtained as in the Voology operator of Neighbours
