# Design and local science fiction for exploration of futures

This research, carried out between 2019 and 2020, aims to explore the design of futures based on the analysis of works of Colombian science fiction, with the aim of having designers and interested in fields of future thinking, exploit their creative potential. In this research, 37 designers participated in the formation of the industrial design program of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Headquarters Bogotá, within the framework of the subject Analysis of Projectual Methods. A methodology of qualitative research-action and design, divided into three phases: 1) Recognition of science fiction stories local, 2) Workshops for the exploration of futures, and analysis of futures from Colombian science fiction, is used as a means of research tools such as documentary analysis, bibliographics, techniques of analog and digital illustration, direct observation, spaces, design workshops and qualitative research software for the analysis of data, results, and results. A qualitative research-action methodology for design is used, consisting of three phases: 1) Recognition of local future stories, 2) Workshops for the exploration of possible futures, and 3) Analysis of visualizations and design of futures from Colombian science fiction. Research tools include documentary analysis analysis, bibliographic records, illustration techniques, observation, journal, design workshops, and software for data analysis. The results show that Colombian science fiction works, through theses, conflicts, charms, and spaces, technical constructions, and software for data analysis
