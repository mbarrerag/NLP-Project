# FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO SOCIAL VULNERABILITY, AND THE CONSTRUCTION OF RESILIENCE BY COMMUNITY ARTISTIC PROCESS IN A GROUP OF YOUTH IN THE MIDDLE EAST CENTRAL SECTOR IN THE CITY OF BOGOTÁ, D.C.

Palabras Clave: Resilience, Community artistic processes, Social vulnerability Introduction: At a global level, in Latin America and the Caribbean between 10% and 15% of children (as) and adolescents, they present problems and/or mental disorders, conditioned by the presence of consumption of psychoactive substances, school dropout, criminal behaviors and other social problems (Minsalud, 2018), associated with conditions of poverty, common crime, domestic violence and few social opportunities.The sector of Centro Oriente in Bogotá is not alien to this situation.The artistic processes community respond to this problem with a fundamental contribution in the formation of children (as), young people and adults in the transformation of social contexts and life projects, art has been the engine to strengthen spaces of solidarity, respect, creation and awareness regarding the multiple situations through which the country passes.The collective artistic and cultural Aby Yala, located in the sector of Centro Oriente, generates an inclusion and participatory space through alternative training in community artistic processes, developing processes of awareness about the transformation of life projects in the community. For the above objective of the research is to know the contribution of the artistic process of the community., second and third levels of hierarchy, depending on the relationship that was established, belonging, property, determination, attribute or manifestation. Limitations: The collection of information presents disadvantages due to closures and isolation orders during the second and third waves of Covid-19 contagion in the country
