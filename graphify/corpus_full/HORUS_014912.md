# Reference values of potentials evoked visual with reverse pattern in a population over 18 years of age in an institution in Bogotá.

The potentials evoked are diagnostic techniques that, by means of sensory stimuli (visual, auditory or electrical tactile) and the record of the brain responses that these provoke, evaluate the integrity of the sentientive pathways stimulated by the disease. Thus, if the visual, auditory or electrical stimulus does not produce the expected wave in the appropriate time and place, it is possible that there is an obstacle in this nervous pathway, in this way, there can be hidden and difficult lesions to locate by means of other paraclinic methods; this makes an examination of great utility in participants with neurodegenerative diseases, multiple sclerosis, Parkinson and Alzheimer. On the other hand, it has been studied in pathologies such as the amyotrophic lateral sclerosis ALS, dementias and different ataxises. The potentials evoked provide important information to the clinical examination. The Ptanzas de conductación produce changes in the latency without further change in configuration and interocular differences are probably the most conspicuous marker of the dysfunction of the optic nerve.
