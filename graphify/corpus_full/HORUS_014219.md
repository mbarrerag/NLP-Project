# Effects of aerobic training vs. strength, hippocampus volume and osteomuscular structure, in a group of older adults.

The aging process leads to a progressive decrease in bone mineral density (BMD), muscle mass, muscle strength, muscle quality (MC) and functional capacity in older adults (AM).There is a strong relationship between physical exercise and the effects of the aging process.The physiological effects of physical exercise can improve the structure and osteomuscular quality, functional capacity, and decrease or mitigate the risk of falls, fractures, disability, as well as loss of functional independence.The study aims to identify the effects of two types of training, one in moderate intensity force and another intense aerobic, the volume of the hippocampus and osteomuscular structure in a group of older adults.For this purpose, the evaluation of the following variables will be carried out: lower limb maximum strength, muscle mass, component and bone mineral density, peak oxygen consumption and volume of the hippocampus, before and after the application of a program of 12 weeks intense aerobic exercise or strength with moderate loads, with a frequency of three times a week.The population will be constituted by 30 adults obtained randomly in experimental group and control.The study expects to bring the scientific elements to the above mentioned
