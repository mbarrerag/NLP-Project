# PO 008. PRESENCE OF ACRYLAMMEDIATE FOOD AND ITS POSSIBLE EFFECTS ON CHILD POPULATION: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LITERATURE

Acrylamide is a soluble compound resulting from the reaction between asparagina and food sugars. Studies in animal models have found that acrylamide increases the risk of developing cancer and causing DNA damage.
