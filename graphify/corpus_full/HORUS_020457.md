# Origin and evolution of gold mineralization in El Gran Porvenir mine, Lebanon, Tolima

The mineralization of El Gran Porvenir is found in the Central Cordillera of Los Andes Colombians, located in graphite schistes of the Cajamarca Complex, and consists mainly of quartz veins and sulphides such as galena, sphalrite, pyrite, calcopyrite and pyrotine. With the intention of classifying the deposit, microtermometry and isotopy of S and Pb-Pb were performed. By means of the microtermometry it was possible to establish that the formation temperature of the mineralization oscillates between 243°C and 348°C. The mineralizing fluid is composed of H2O, CO2, N2, CH4 and its saline system is of NaCl, KCl with an average salinity of 8,6 wt% NaCl.
