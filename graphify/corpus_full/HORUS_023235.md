# Development of a methodology for the analysis of hydro-dynamic economic services with the aim of environmental economic evaluation

This article describes and implements in the microbasin of the Susaguá river in Colombia a methodological proposal for the characterization of hydrological ecosystem services, specifically those of regulation and sediment retention, for the purpose of environmental economic valuation. The proposal considers the analysis of scenarios of change in plant coverage and soil use, through the rigorous implementation of mathematical models. The process starts with the selection and hydroclimate characterization of the area of interest, includes various modelling tools, depending on the availability of existing information and the experticia in hydrological subjects of the user, is supported by the analysis of scenarios of changes in plant coverage, and consolidates the results in hydrosystem properties and indices, simple to estimate, which include, among others, components of the water balance (Real Evapotranspiration - ETr, Annual Average Flow - Q), the water regulation index (IRH), the water use index (IUA), for the topic of water quantity; and the production and concentration of sediments for the water quality theme
