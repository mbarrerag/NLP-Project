# Machine learning-based method for the qualification of short essays in English from a sample of high school students

This paper addresses the challenge of the automatic qualification of English-language argumentative essays written by high school students who are learning English as a second language. The general objective is to implement an automatic method based on supervised learning that allows solving this task for 6 indicators in simultaneous: Cohesion, Syntaxis, Vocabulary, Grammar, Fraseology and Conventions in scale from 1 to 5. To achieve this, a descriptive analysis of the data is carried out, preprocessing procedures are applied and relevant characteristics are extracted; different strategies, representation techniques and models are explored from some classics to those with better performance in the present, evaluating in each iteration their performance, contrasting it with human qualifications. Then, the model with less error is based mainly on DeBERTa, to which different techniques are applied to improve their performance and is combined with a model SVR that takes as characteristics the spellings of texts concatenated in 10 models pre-trained without finishing. evaluated in each iteration, contrasting it with human ratings with a chosen measure. Then, the method with the best performance is present, it is based mainly on DeBERTa V3 Large, where different technologies are applied to improve its performance. Ultimately, and is combined with a return model SVR that takes as features the concatenated embeddings of the texts in 10 different pretrained models. With this strategy, the result is removed close to human ratings, presenting a root mean error of 0.45 over all indicators
