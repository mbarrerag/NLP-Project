# Reading of multimodal texts of a scientific nature as a mediating strategy in understanding the chemical link

This research work in the field of Science Education studies the influence of reading multi-modal texts of a scientific nature as a media strategy for understanding the Chemical Link in students of a ninth grade. The qualitative methodology, through the study cases, allows interpreting the mediating role of these strategies, generating curricular and instructional decisions to plan learning activities that integrate the content of the Chemical Link into the framework of reading multimodal texts. These pedagogical activities are characterized by the active participation of students, use of intertextuality and educational technological resources as simulators for generating representations in 3D and 2D. The implementation was in a public educational institution in Palmira, during the year 2021, a time in which this institution alternated between face-to-face and virtual education due to the sanitary constraints caused by the emergence of the COVID-19. The results showed relevant categories as the development of representative competence, markers of visual grammar, principles of multimedia learning, elements that influence the pedagogy and observed difficulties. It is concluded that the reading of scientific multimodal texts is effective for the understanding of the Chemical Link. use of intertextuality, and educational technological resources such as simulations for the generation of 3D and 2D representations.
