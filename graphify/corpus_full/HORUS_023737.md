# CARDIC FREQUENCY SENSOR PROTOCYPLE FOR THE PRONOSTIC PROBLEMS OF CARDYAC PROTOTYPICAL CREQUENCY SENSOR PROTOTYPICAL PROTOCYPICAL FOR THE PRONOSTIC OF CARDYAC PROBLEMS

This article seeks to design and develop a software tool for mobile devices that helps predict a person’s heart complications by using an Arduino sensor located on a bracelet or handle that communicates via computer networks to the object by sending real-time information. And compare the information with a database containing information on different cases of health complications in people with heart disease or blood pressure
