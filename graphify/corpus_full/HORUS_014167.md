# Yearbook XII of Comparative Constitutionalism: Between catastrophe and opportunity, the challenges of constitutionalism and democracy in the 21st century.

The CC-Constitutionalism Comparative research group continues with its permanent work plan inherent in its denomination and intends to draw up, from its successful series of yearbooks (which have published a total of 121 articles), the tenth volume in which the seven lines of research of the group are involved. CC-Concerned Constitutionalism was founded in 2006 and is since then directed by Prof. Dr. BERND MARQUART. It is a centre of historical, contemporary, theoretical, dogmatic and socio-legal constitutional studies, whose purpose is to analyze the modern constitutional state. Thus, the main object of research is the basic normivity of the State in terms of the development of its institutional organization, its values, guarantees, models of legitimization and limitation of power, taking into account the transnational context in which it is inserted and its particular social context, which has led to the work of the group integrating interdisciplinary approaches. In order to consolidate and strengthen this geopolitical approach, the group proposes this project entitled The Latin American constitutional state in a socio-cultural and transnational perspective.
