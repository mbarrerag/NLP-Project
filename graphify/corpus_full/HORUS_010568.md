# PHOCO SCIENCES OF LIFE AND HEALTH

The Vice-Rector of Research of the National University of Colombia has proposed that, in addition to carrying out the Technical Secretariat of the Focus of Basic Sciences and Space of the Mission of Sabios Colombia 2019, a group of representatives should be agreed to analyze the proposals of each of the Mission's focus areas and to build proposals on topics of training, research and innovation and entrepreneurship, to be submitted to the members of the Mission in order that they be considered for incorporation into the recommendations to the National Government. The representatives of the University are part of research groups classified in category 1A of Colciencias, have publications in indexed journals belonging to the same category and have extensive teaching and research experience
