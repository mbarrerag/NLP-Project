# Alliance for pedagogical innovation using technologies for PAZ.

To generate a strategic alliance among the participating laboratories of the National University in order to strengthen the scope of the National System of Laboratories, the current conditions for the development of their processes and projects, and in turn through the use of technologies to articulate activities that are propens for the construction of peace accompanied by the collaborative work of the citizenry and that generate a wide impact for professors, teachers, student community and society in general
