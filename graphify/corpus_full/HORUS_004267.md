# SPACE-TUMACO DYNAMIC POLLUTION BY MICROPLASTICS IN MARINE SEDIMENTS OF THE BAHIA OF TUMACO. COLOMBIAN PACIFIC

Contextualization: Tumaco Bay is potentially a recipient of domestic waste, which leads to different pollutants such as microplastics (<5mm) being accumulated in marine sediments.Vacity of knowledge: In the Colombian Pacific it has not been explored as the sanitary emergency (COVID-19) affected the patterns of contamination of microplastics in marine sediments before, during and after this. The purpose of this study was to identify the presence, characteristics and spatial temporal variation of the contamination by microplastics (MP) during the years 2020 and 2021 in the internal and external zone of the bay of Tumaco and if it had any relation to the proximity to the coastal zone, the mouth of the rivers and COVID-19. Microplastic samples were collected in the internal and external zone of the bay of Tumaco during the period of rain and dry season, and it was possible to present a decrease of 220 and 2021. MP contamination was predominant in the internal zone (721.37 ± 175 ± 100 % particles/kg)
