# SHOOT AND LOOK: THE STORY OF THE READING ART

What has been the place of national museums in the construction of the art history? What has been the role of art history as a discipline in the context of museums of a national character, such as the National Museum of Colombia? These questions are decisive for reflecting on the relationship between national art, as well as for thinking and objecting to the idea of a "national art" and beyond that, introducing additional concepts such as the "transnational" among others to enrich the debate
