# • The role of the European Union in the development of the European Union in the field of education and training;

This work is the result of discussions in a Community of Practice for Latin American and Caribbean Occupational Therapeutics working with Education (TOE Latin America). TOE Latin America arises in order to strengthen the collective work of occupational therapists in the field of education within the specifics of Latin American social organizations. Therefore, here we come to share a little of this path, presenting three topics in this essay: first, we put the concrete reasons for our existence, how we work and what the group's proposals are in reality; then, we bring a central debate on the right to education and the historical problems that involve Latin America and its populations, as well as identifying the role and significance of the Occupational Therapeutics in access to conditions of equality in education; finally, we find it pertinent to point out the emergence of the current debate that involves the COVID-19 pandemic and its impacts on the lives of individuals and populations, which directly interferes in education, to say once again on our therapeutic-occupational task in this regard
