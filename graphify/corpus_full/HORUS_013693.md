# Gambling for peace: a neighborhood experience told from rebellion and football

This document aims to systematize the process of the School of Popular Football, the Rebel Ball. This school was designed as a social, sport and political initiative, developing in the town of Kennedy, Bogotá since 2014. The purpose of this exercise of systematization is to provide recommendations for the implementation in the short and medium term of the project, starting from the recovery of the living memory that has been woven over nine years of trajectory. Likewise, to account for the contributions that these experiences make for the construction of peace on a small scale. To this end, a qualitative methodology was used and this study was based on a variety of sources, including a bibliographic review, a documentary study of the concepts and the compilation of primary sources, who, from their national and international initiatives, fed the work. Likewise, the contributions of my personal experience are presented there, being part of the process seven years ago. As we see, there is a wide range of collaborations with other organizations to strengthen the construction of peace from below, which allows coordination with other efforts at national and international level, so that in the future it is not only the transformation of the conflict, but the prevention of all forms of violence. dology was used and this study was based on a variety of sources, including a bibliographic review, a documentary study of the concepts and the compilation of primary sources, who, from their national and international initiatives, fed the work.
