# VALUATION OF ORGANOCATALIZED GLYCEROL BY N-HETEROCCYCLIC CARBENS (NHC).

The valorisation of glycerol has acquired great importance in recent years, because it is a byproduct of the synthesis of biodiesel that is obtained in large quantities accumulating as a residue of the industry. That is why its utilization turns out to be valuable since it is a versatile molecule for obtaining value added products like glycerol carbonate (CG). To achieve this, it has been demonstrated the need to use catalysts that are able to accelerate the process of transesterification of glycerol with organic carbonates, for example. In this sense, the N-heterocyclic carbenes (NHC) are molecules that have acquired high relevance in catalysis due to their unique stereoelectronic properties. These are able to stabilize metallic catalytic species as a percentage of the most important activities. Likewise, they have been shown to be the organic catalysts due to their great nucleophilic activity. However, it is important to highlight that the use of NHCs as catalysts for the valorization of glycerol is little explored. In this work, the use of the N-herols is presented as the different reaction mechanism will also be presented
