# Optimizing process design and control development for free-radical polymerization in continuous reactors

The polymer industry has been exhibiting remarkable economic growth in recent years due to the increasing demand for products. Like other processes with remarkable development, the polymer industry involves environmental and economic problems with the generation of a substantial amount of waste. An important percentage of the waste generated in the polymerization processes is the product out of specification due to lack of process control. In addition, the polymerization processes are challenging, where complex mass and energy interactions are handled, thus obtaining intricate mathematical models, whose solution may not be simple. These depend on the type of polymer and the synthesis methods used in the process, presenting in some cases a simplification compared to other polymerization systems. Among the most interesting polymers, is polystyrene, much used in the industry for its productive versatility and relevant markets, among others. The most used production route is by means of mass polymerization by free radicals.
