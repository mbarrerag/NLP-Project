# Definition of a metamodel based on usability and pedagogical knowledge for the design of educational software applications.

In the design process of educational software applications, we should consider usability criteria and pedagogical knowledge to obtain easy-to-use teaching tools. However, most of the educational software applications designed do not have such criteria. Some authors propose models and metamodels to prevent such deficiencies. Unfortunately, such deficiencies continue to be presented, as some criteria are not met, such as the presence of elements to support collaborative work and the support systems to train users without experience. For this reason, this Doctoral Thesis proposes the definition of a metamodel to design educational software applications that include usability and pedagogical knowledge criteria. For such a definition, each relevant element is conceptually described in the design of an educational software application; then, pre-conceptual schemes are selected as syntax to represent the elements and relationships identified, since these schemes can be easily exemplified by using instances, are computer-readable (they are executable) and are easy to understand; finally, the metamodel is designed and validated as syntax to represent the elements and relationships identified, since these schemes can be easily exemplified by using the use of the design of the knowledge, structural and some of the attributes and limitations of the use of the design of the design, and the design of the design
