# PROFESSIONALS IN SCIENCES OF LAND IN MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATIONS, A CONTRIBUTION TO THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE TERRITORY: COLOMBIAN CASE

The geographical location, economic vulnerability of a large part of the population, lack of education of Earth Sciences in schools, lack of planning in the planning of the territory, illegal extraction of resources and lack of public policies adapted to the reality of the territories are the perfect cropping environment for what is observed day by day in the news: loss of lives and economics due to lack of harmony between public policies-knowledge - geo-threats and environmental deterioration in the regions.In this scenario, a legislative initiative has been proposed for the availability of a necessary knowledge in Colombia that allows to have a professional in Earth sciences in the territory that can permeate the social fabric positively impacting the well-being of the population.This initiative has been accompanied by research projects in the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá with the the thesis of degree, academic practice in a municipality and short documentary, as well as the presentation in national and international events of different kinds, campaigns advanced by professional associations (Sociedad Colombiana de Geología and Asociaciónicosticos del Petróleo), communications and meetings with members of the Tierra and the legislators, news in the national and local press, social networks, among others. This draft law requires that the Colombian Geological Service be provided with a budget for the development of the project, so that municipalities are categorized according to their needs through a diagnosis that allows identifying which municipalities could be grouped together to provide them with the support and which of them, due to their characteristics, would need the presence of a professional, protected that article 15 (titled knowledge and geological cartography of the subsoil) of Law 2056 of 2020 "which regulates the organization and operation of the General System of Regalías", in the said article: "Can the Colombian Geological Service provide support to the activities of prospecting and exploration, geo-threats, exploration of underground waters, identification and plans of territorial planning in the producer municipalities, based on the information available as a result of the recognition, exploration and exploration of the national territory that is in its charge and according to the budget that of the General System of Regalías is assigned to it for that purpose?"
