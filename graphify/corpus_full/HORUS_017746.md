# VALUE AND INSTRUMENTAL RATIONALITY IN ORGANIZATIONS: AN ANALYSIS OF HUMAN MANAGEMENT PRACTICES

To analyze the self-determined humanist companies, in the light of the valorative rationality and instrumental rationality, marked by economic profitability to propose valorative rationality as a criterion of humanism in the organization. To establish this analysis, the research is based on weberian notions of valorative rationality and instrumental rationality, and examines the foundations and purposes of the decisions made by the managers. A qualitative methodology was used with an interpretative approach. The field work was supported by interviews with employees, managers and managers of human management of large organizations in the city of Medellín. It was found that there exist organizations as functionalists, paternalists or humanists according to the satisfaction, personal development, social cohesion and the capacities that are consolidated in the workplace. A level of empowerment was observed in the discourse of employees of some of the companies studied, due to the degree of integration, cohesion and stability of the employees; this empowerment is closely connected with the valorative and functionalist-valorative rational style.
