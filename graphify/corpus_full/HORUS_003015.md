# In vivo dose measurement in intraoperative radiation therapy: a measurement and dose analysis protocol for the Moberon 2000 accelerator

In this work, the dosimetry of electron beams was evaluated using 3D printed bolus in combination with EBT3 radiochrome films and TLD detectors, with the aim of optimizing the distribution of the dose in surface radiation therapy treatments. Experiments were carried out with three different configurations, using 0.5 cm and 1 cm of precision bolus and a 9 MeV electron beam. The lateral profiles and percentage of depth dose (PDD) were measured precisely, highlighting how the bolus superficialize the dose and control the distribution in the most external layers of the tissue. The results showed that the use of the 1 cm bolus reduces the dose in the depth of 2 cm in approximately 2 Gy in comparison with the 0.5 cm bolus, and in 4 Gy in relation to not using bolus, demonstrating the effectiveness of this method to protect deep tissues. The radiochrome films and TLD, previously calibrated, allowed accurate and reproducible dose measurements, validating their use in advanced studies of dosimetry.
