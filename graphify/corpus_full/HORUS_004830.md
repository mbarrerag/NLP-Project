# MANUAL TECHNICAL RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THEIR CULTURE IN THE DEPARTMENT OF CUNDINAMARCA

This document is the result of the Subproject on the Evaluation of Innovative Technologies for the Integral Management of Mango, Orange and Mandarin Crops in Production Areas of the Department of Cundinamarca, developed within the framework of the Agroindustrial Technology Corridor CTA-2, Project on Research, Development and Technology Transfer in the Agricultural and Agro-Agricultural Sector with the aim of improving the entire department, Cundinamarca, Centro Oriente, signed by the Government of Cundinamarca, through the Secretariat of Science, Technology and Innovation; the mayor of bogotá, through the district secretariat for economic development; the national university of colombia; and the Colombian Agricultural Research Corporation (agrosavia, formerly Corpoica). The Agroindustrial Technology Corridor CTA-2 is funded by resources from the Science, Technology and Innovation Fund of the General System of Regalías. It is further clarified that the results of research obtained must be understood according to the period in which the project was implemented and not with that of its publication date
