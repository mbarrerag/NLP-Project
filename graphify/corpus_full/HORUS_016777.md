# QUANTITY IN THE COMPOSITION OF METEORICAL WATER IN BARRANQUILLA,BOGOTA, TESORO AND TULENAP (COLOMBIA)

In tropical regions there is a paradigm of the effect of quantity, according to which a strong negative correlation is expected between the amount of monthly precipitation and d values of meteoric water (high d values in dry seasons and low d values in rainy seasons). However, factors such as the mixture of precipitation sources and the orographic effect could alter this pattern at local or regional scale on a permanent or seasonal basis. This paper presents a correlation analysis between monthly precipitation and its contents of 18O and dD in four World Network sites on Isotopes in Precipitation in Colombia: Barranquilla (1971-1989), Bogotá (1971-2016), El Tesoro (2002-2019), and Tulenapa (2013-2019).
