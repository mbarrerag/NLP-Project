# Become a Woman in Textile Making: Making a Feminist Genealogy

This textile research has aimed to understand the relationship between textile making and the configuration of feminist genealogies, focusing mainly on two aspects: on the one hand, how the subjectivity and the collective making of women from the same family are configured from textile practices; and, on the other hand, how textile objects allow to narrate female genealogies. For the realization of this research I worked with three women from my family who have worked or have spent part of their time in textile trades. I used a qualitative methodology that consisted of virtual conversations and epistolary communication, through which I asked about their life stories around making textiles, in order to make a recomposition of my female textile lineage that, in a collective memory exercise, was nurtured from each of their interventions.
