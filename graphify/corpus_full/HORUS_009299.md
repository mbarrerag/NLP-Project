# REFERENCE TO COLOMBIA GEOGRAPHY, HISTORIA AND EPISTOMOLOGY OF AN INCREDIBLE PROJECT

The dream of realizing a scientific geography of the territory of today Colombia was publicly expressed for the first time in the text of José Francisco de Caldas «State of the Geography of the Viceroyalty of Santafé de Bogotá, regarding the economy and the trade», published in 1808 in the numbers 1 to 7 of the Semanario del Nuevo Reyno de Granada (3, 10, 17, 24 and 31 January and 7 February).This text opened a field of studies that was nourished, over the years, of various epistemological currents of the geography, according to the paradigms in force at each historical moment.The scientific exercise is never absolutely neutral, disconnected from the societies and their contexts of production, and this affirmation has an even stronger value for the geography, that always, and in all parts of the world, is related to the projects of society in construction, either hegemonic or alternative.In Colombia, the interests that surrounded the geographical exercise were multiple: identity, in the sense of creating a national feeling; economic and commercial, with the search for the opening of roads or the promotion of foreign investments, have been developmental and focused in the country
