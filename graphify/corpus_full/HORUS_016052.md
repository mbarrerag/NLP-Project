# MUSEALIZATION OF THE MEMORY OF THE ARMED CONFLICT

The theme of this conference is directly related to my research/creation project of the Doctorate in Artistic Studies in Bogotá Colombia. The project arises as a need to reflect on the growing rise of exhibitions held in Bogotá, around the armed conflict in Colombia, within the context of a context of the presidency of Juan Manuel Santos in which the peace agreements were signed in 2016, with Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia, the People's Army (FARC'EP). Following the signing of these peace agreements made in Havana, different practices of memorialization or forms of public reminders of events of the past, as a strategy for the visibility and recovery of the memory of the armed conflict, are enhanced and extended. As part of these practices, which had already been carried out in the past, the symbolic reparation processes for the victims, some of them realized in public acts of pardon by their victims, which seek recognition of the events, as well as reconciliation between the parties as a synonym of peace, have been entrusted with sculptures and monuments for the recognition of the dignity of the victims, through which the victims, through which the construction of the various forms of the CNIMOS, have been carried out
