# Synthesis of new derivatives belonging to the isoindolo nucleus[2,1-a]quinolin-11(5)H)-one of biological interest.

The molecules belonging to the nucleus of the isoindolo[2,1-a]quinoline-11(5H)-ones have shown to have a very remarkable biological activity in relation to various pathologies. For this reason, at the research level, a great variety of molecules belonging to this nucleus has been synthesized by various methodologies, which according to the reports have quite specific characteristics, such as anti-hypoxic properties, vasodilators, antitumours, etc. Taking into account the above, this work proposes a methodology for obtaining heterocyclic systems of four members, specifically of some derivatives of the isoindolo nucleus[2,1-a]quinol-11(5H)-one. It is important to note that the low cost was taken as a principle both in the inputs (reactive, solvents, medium catalysts, etc.), as in the conditions of operation with the purpose of being callative and reproducible. Intramolecular cycling, to give as a product the respective compounds belonging to the isoindoloquinolins. The molecules obtained in this methodology, such as ftalilos and isoindoloquinolins, were characterized by spectroscopic and spectrometric techniques such as MRI, HR-MS, IR-FT, which can confirm that objective molecules have been obtained in a new methodology that does not exceed the three reaction steps
