# WE'RE HERE: CARTOGRAPHS OF DISIDENT SEXUALITIES IN BOGOTA

We are here: cartographies of dissident sexualityes in Bogotá addresses the issue of spatial practices that have been and are being carried out in Bogotá by persons who are under the age of sexual and gender-based minorities; in particular, in non-institutional areas such as ambient bars and their circuits. It is the result of a doctoral thesis on the subject of anthropology of the author, who presents in this work a cartography of these practices in the city, showing, on the one hand, the interrelations and links that are established between these spaces and the subjectivities that they promote and allow and, on the other, their importance for the social and political existence of people with dissident sexuality. The work proposes an analysis situated of these spatial practices in the field of local histories of sexuality and on a scale both local and global. Thus, the book shows the mutual effects that occur between these forms of sociability, the use of urban space of people with dissident sexualitys and the dynamics specific areas and public policies in the neighbourhood and the city. The experiences described are presented as practices that exist between urban history and their memory, and that affirm the right to the people with the sexual rights of dissidents and the future
