# COMPARISON OF SELECTION INDEXS IN BRAHMAN WIN IN COLOMBIA.

The use of selection indices (IS) allows to improve a group of characteristics simultaneously, being able to identify animals balanced in productive terms. To identify an IS that allows greater genetic progress for growth characteristics (C), channel conformation (CC) and reproduction (R) in a Brahmin herd. Production and genealogical records of 38483 animals were used. Genetic parameters and values predicted from univariate and bivariate animal models including direct and maternal genetic effects for weight at 4 months (Hm4) and weaning (HmD) using WOMBAT software were used in the index construction. 2 IS were constructed: 1.
