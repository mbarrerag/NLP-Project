# The tactics of influence of leadership used by academic coordinators with instructors in a public institution of training for the job. Case study in three training centers of the Regional Capital District of SENA.

The leadership has been a recognized topic in the business and institutional world, which has a close relationship with power, understood the latter as the ability to influence positively or negatively in a working team to achieve a purpose; to lead then is to use the appropriate influence tactics to direct efforts towards reaching goals. In this research called the tactics of influence of the leadership used by the academic coordinators with the instructors in a public institution of training for the job. Case study in three training centers of the Regional Capital District of SENA, was asked the question of research What are the tactics of influence of the leadership used by the academic coordinators with the instructors in three training centers of the Regional Capital District of SENA?, which led to identify and interpret the exercise of power through the tactics of leadership with which the academic coordinators exert influence on the instructors of the National Service of Learning SENA. To this effect, in the theoretical framework was used to address topics such as the leadership, the power and its typology, the tactics of influence in the face-to-face and virtuality, this in order to lay the theoretical bases and from the execution of what was proposed in the methodological design, the employees. The results of the research were represented through figures with their respective interpretations of each of the statements or questions formulated in the surveys to both instructors and academic coordinators, this treatment of the information collected allowed to compare the perceptions of the two populations studied and subsequently triangularized with the results of the interviews conducted to the academic coordinators. It is concluded that the perceptions on the tactics of influence of leadership used by SENA academic coordinators are perceived differently by the instructors, and by academic coordinators; the first identify tactics of influence of leadership related to formal and coercive power, and the second, that is, the academic coordinators give preponderance and consider that they use tactics of influence of leadership related mainly to the power of reference and expert power, this would lead to understanding that each one understands the power from his own context in which he develops. Based on the conclusions, recommendations are presented that result in the opening of spaces of reflection that contribute to developing effective communicative processes, seeking to close the gaps of perceptions of the two populations studied through a transformational approach to improvement
