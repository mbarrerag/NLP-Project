# INTRODUCTION: THE BUILDING OF DISCIPLINATION OF INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS IN LATIN AMERICA.

Present the chapters that compose the book and outline in an analytical way the construction of the discipline of the RRII in Latin America. Thus, we seek to place the reader in the historical and present context that has shaped the discipline in the region, as well as to point out some elements that aim to position in the global debate on the RRII the Latin American theoretical and conceptual contributions to the discipline. In this order, we then present the contributions that the various authors make in this book, which are grouped around the already classic and emerging contributions from Latin America; and to the scope and limits of the global theories and concepts to analyze the Latin American reality
