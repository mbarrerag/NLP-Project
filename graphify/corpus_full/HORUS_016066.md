# IMPLEMENTATION OF A VETERINARY FARMACOVIGILANCE SYSTEM IN COLOMBIA

Veterinary pharmacovigilance is the set of activities aimed at identifying, evaluating and preventing risks arising from the use of veterinary medicines and includes a continuous process of training, recording, analysis and evaluation of possible adverse events or lack of efficacy. Different studies around the world have shown that the underregistration of adverse events related to the administration of veterinary medicines is very high and the monitoring difficult. Therefore, in order to reduce the sub-notification, we recommend the creation of friendly, centralized and online computer tools, so that from any device with internet connection can be performed the report of possible adverse events. Thus, after a detailed analysis of the different systems of pharmacovigilance that operate at the global level, we have completed the design and development of the software, which provides the possibility of easy monitoring of adverse events both in animals and in people, lack of effectiveness, problems with withdrawal time, environmental or problems in the literature. The software has been developed in such a way that it minimizes the possibility of transcription or interpretation errors, facilitates the monitoring and carries out the timely notification with the rigorousness of the pharmaceutical work required
