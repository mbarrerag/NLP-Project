# METAGE OF ZOPHOBAS ATTRATUS UNDER EXCLUSIVE POLICE DIET

Recent research has shown that the larvae of Zophobas atratus (Tenebrioidae: Coleoptera) can be a viable polystyrene residue manager (PS), whose catalytic capacity is linked to its intestinal microbiota. Considering the socio-environmental relevance of the plastic residue management, this study used methodical data to describe the taxonomic and functional composition of the prokaryotic community associated with the intestine of Z. atratus exposed to the exclusive consumption of PS during a month. The DNA sequencing was performed using the platform Illumina NovaSeqTM 6000 System. Post-control of quality, the MG-RAST platform and the program HUMANN 3.0 were used to obtain the taxonomic composition of the microbial community and the functional profile of metabolic pathways and families of genes. From the 3,541,186 sequences that passed the quality control in MG-RAST 27,07% were mainly associated with metabolic functions, 24.8% to processing of genetic information, and 20.1% to cellular processes. Xenobiotic and recalcitrant nature, like plastics, has been tested in multiple contexts. This work constitutes an exploratory pilot study on the taxonomic and functional microbial diversity detectable by metagenomics associated with the Z. atratus intestine.
