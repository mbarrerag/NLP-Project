# Teaching unit for teaching and learning real numbers empowerment in grade eight.

This document presents a didactic proposal for the eighth grade with the aim of facilitating the learning of empowerment and establishment, in search of fostering solid conceptual structures on these knowledges, in order to promote a better conception around the management of the first topics of algebra courses proposed by the Ministry of National Education (MEN)
