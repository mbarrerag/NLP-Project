# Organizational culture and its contribution to the universal accessibility of persons with disabilities: case study in a Colombian company

The aim of this research was to analyze how the organizational culture of companies can positively affect the universal accessibility and the permanence of persons with disabilities in work environments. Factors that could contribute to ensuring accessibility, both physical and technological and communicative, for all people were examined. In addition, some of the attitudinal and communicative barriers that hinder the integration process of persons with disabilities were identified. To carry out this study, a qualitative case study methodology was used, focusing on the construction company Grupo Infinito. Visits were made to the company's facilities, in which also interviews were made to employees, both with and without disabilities, with the aim of obtaining a complete understanding of the work dynamics. . The objective of this research was to assess the organizational, organisational and administrative structure of the company, with the aim of contributing to the continuous improvement of the job linkage process in inclusive environments. Emphasis was placed on differentiating between accessibility and inclusion, enhancing potential attitude and communicative barriers that may sink total accessibility. Likewise, recommendations were formulated for this and other companies with the purpose of contributing to the continuous improvement of the integration process in including work environments
