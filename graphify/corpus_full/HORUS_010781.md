# THE PRESIDENTIAL COMMUNICATION OF GUSTAVO PETRO: BACKGROUND, RISK AND CHALLENGES

The article reviews some of the background of the presidential communication in Colombia, particularly during the government of Belisario Betancur, in the early 1980s. It also shows the current problems of fragmentation of the public sphere and the challenges that such a situation poses to public and government communication. It exemplifies this fragmentation in the situation of the National Stop in April 2021, showing the difficulty that is present today not only from the government communication, to build some basic agreements and consensuses that favor governance. Finally, the author highlights some of the problems of the communication of President Gustavo Petro, especially with his emotional and careless management of Tweet and the absence of a systematic and elaborate policy of governmental and presidential communication
