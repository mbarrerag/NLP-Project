# Technology package for the management of second MD-2 pineapple harvest, in areas of the Dagua slope, Valle del Cauca

The second harvest in MD-2 pineapple in areas of the hillside of Dagua Valle del Cauca, is a practice that is being carried out to improve the profitability of the productive system; however, due to lack of methodological information to carry out this practice in an adequate way, the best results have not been achieved.With the need to solve the problem, this research aimed to develop a technological package for the management of second harvest in MD-2 pineapple in this region. Therefore, an experimental plot was established with six treatments and three repetitions, in a completely random design; five treatments with second harvest technological practices and a control with own practices of the farmer. The variables measured were: yield, content of brix degrees, incidence of Phytopthora spp., damage by Tecla, production costs and profit margin/hectare. The data were submitted to a analysis of variance and diseases (ANOVA) in the statistical software SAS and the mean comparisons, were performed with the T3 and T5 which included practices of: plant preparation protocol, conventional management of pests and diseases, fertilis and fertilisation, fertilisation and fertilisation, fertilisation and fertilisation, fertilisation with of methodological information to carry out this practice efficiently, a good result has been obtained. The objective of this study was encouraged to develop a technical package for the management of second efforts of MD-2 pilot in those regions.
