# Cultural landscape in the West of Antioquia: transformations of the dynamics of the territory and the relation landscape-society, starting in 2006

The road connection Aburrá-Cauca, has been one of the main infrastructure projects of recent times in the subregion of West Antioquia, bringing with it changes in the structure of tenure and use of the land, in the economic activities and in the supply of goods and services associated to recreation and tourism. These transformations are reflected in the territory and in the landscape in multiple physical ways (geoforms, coverages, uses, property structure, land tenure), as well as in the demands for goods and services, as well as in the root and culture of its inhabitants. This research investigated the transformation of the cultural landscape in the municipalities of San Jerónimo, Sopetrán and Santa Fe de Antioquia between 2006 and 2018, as a product of the implementation of the connection Vial Aburrá-Cauca; analyzing the morphological changes, variations in the coverages of the soil, assessment of the visual quality, analysis of the use of the economic activity, assessment of the predial fragmentation and the perception of the landscape by its inhabitants. .
