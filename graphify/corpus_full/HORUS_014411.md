# Selecting an optimal short method for measuring interior illuminance.

The process of certification of interior lighting systems, requires a series of points of measurement of illuminance within the spaces of a project, and in this way validate its compliance with the standards and technical regulations, also as its correspondence with the specifications of lighting design or photometric calculations. However, due to problems in this field of lighting, and in the processes of certification of this type of installations, where the activities of illuminance measurement are carried out using a large quantity of measurement times and are made unviable by the excessive quantity of measuring points and by the different non uniform ways of illuminating a space, that thanks to the versatility that has developed the LED technology, this is possible. Therefore, the objective of this work was to select a method of measurement acceptablely precise from a minimum number of points.
