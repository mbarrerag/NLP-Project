# Response times for ambulance services for road traffic accidents within the town of Suba

For the management of a city, it is not only essential to provide a good coverage of public emergency services, as are paramedics and ambulances, but it is also necessary that these same services be efficient when responding to emergencies in the city, so it is important to understand the accessibility of ambulances to the site of an accident to reduce the mortality of people due to late response times in a city such as Bogotá. It is then that this research focuses on analysing the geographical coverage of ambulances within the town of Suba based on the response time from dispatch points to high road accident and then to the nearest public hospitals through local self-correlation, network analysis based on the Dijkstra model and the selection of new dispatch points to meet WHO standards in accident care, taking into account the different variables that may affect the time of response of these services during the post-pandemia period from 2021 to 2023 .
