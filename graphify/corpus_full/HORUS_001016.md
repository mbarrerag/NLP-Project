# COMPARISON OF ACCESS AND AVAILABILITY OF ESSENTIAL BIOLOGICAL MEDICINAL PRODUCTS IN LATIN AMERICA

The access and availability of medicines of biological origin (and their biosimilars) has taken on great importance at the global level, as they are essential for the treatment of some types of cancer and diseases of growing epidemiological importance. Therefore, a review was made of the availability of the biological drugs present in the list of essential drugs (SME) of the World Health Organization (WHO) in version 22 of 2021, for 6 countries of the Latin American region: Colombia, Chile, Ecuador, Peru, Mexico and El Salvador, emphasizing the new inclusions regarding the 2019 list and available biosimilars. As a result, it was found that Chile has the highest number of existing records (204) and Colombia the highest variety (availability of 27 of the 31 medicines on the list). Regarding the new inclusions, the incorporation of insulin analogues of prolonged action, glargine insulin is the highest availability (29 existing records) and biosimilar approved (7) in its category.
