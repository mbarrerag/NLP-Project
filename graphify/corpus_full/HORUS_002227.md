# PSA nadir as predictor of overall survival in hormone-sensitive metastatic prostate cancer

Androgen deprivation therapy, along with prostate-specific antigen (PSA), is used to monitor the progression of prostate cancer. This study evaluates the relationship between the PSA nadir and overall survival in patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) treated with orchiectomy. Retrospective cohort of 220 patients with mCRPC managed with orchiectomy. A PSA nadir ≥ 0.2 ng/ml and time to nadir > 6 months are significant predictors of overall survival. PSA nadir is a useful tool as a predictor of overall survival in patients with mCRPC
