# REFLECTIONS AND PROPOSALS IN THE TOOL TO SDG 11 SUSTAINABLE URBAN COMMUNITIES

The goal 11 of the 2030 Agenda, referring to "Cities and sustainable communities", tells us that more than half of the world population lives today in urban areas and that it is expected that in 2050 this figure equals two thirds of humanity. According to the National Population and Housing Census 2018 (National Administrative Department of Statistics [DANE], 2018), in Colombia we have already exceeded that average, since currently 77.1 % of the population lives in cities. In our country there are three urban centers (Medellin, Cali and Barranquilla) with more than one million inhabitants, without counting the population of its metropolitan areas and conurbations, besides Bogotá ́, which is close to eight million inhabitants.
