# Replacement of saturated fats, addition of natural preservatives and use of cryoprotective agents as a technological strategy for the improvement of leafy masses

The powder is a laminated mass commonly used in the manufacture of different bakery and pastry products, configured in layers, which are constituted by two parts of different composition, one is the filling or paste made from flour and water, and the other is the fat content.Since within its formulation a high content of saturated fat is used, it is a food conceived as unhealthy, so it generates a certain rejection in its consumption.Some studies have tried to evaluate the partial or total reduction of fat using different raw materials, some of them with unsatisfactory results in terms of sensory quality.In the search for alternatives, in this work it is proposed to use modified starch as a potential partial substitute of the fat material in the manufacture of pastry, for which different levels of inclusion and its effect on the response of reological and functional variables will be considered. In response to the current trends of consumption based on lifestyles, in this work it is proposed to use in a first stage to use modified starch as a potential partial substitute of the fat material, for which the second useful life, for which the reason of the leaf products, including the artificial origin, preservatives. The presence of these additives usually has a positive effect. The main function of this plant is to protect the structure of a food matrix against possible damage caused by the formation of ice crystals and thus to ensure the quality of the product. Finally, in a third stage, life tests will be carried out in the developed masses to establish the conservation time as a result of the modifications in the formulation. Through the generation of this knowledge, it is expected to contribute to the modification in the formulation of puff pastry masses and other products of the bakery industry, to adjust these foods to current consumption trends and increasingly rigorous policies in terms of nutrient declaration with low intake recommendation such as saturated fats, being consistent with the community lifestyle that seeks to access preferably natural ingredients linked to healthy behaviors
