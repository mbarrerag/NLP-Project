# THE EXPERIMENTATOR AND THE ENVIRONMENT: A REVIEW OF WHAT WE KNOW ABOUT MEASURES

In this activity, on the one hand, data will be collected from the environment and the human body and, on the other hand, a reflection will be made on the way in which the student uses the measuring instruments and the way in which the data are collected and organized. To begin, some activities will be carried out in order to make each student know himself and reflect on the importance of his measures. The environment and its characteristics will also have to be considered
