# ASSESSMENT OF A BIOLOGICAL AIR RESTORATION OF MOBILE LECTION FOR TREATMENT OF RESIDUAL WATERS OF SERVICE STATUTES.

According to the characteristics of the fat trap effluent of service stations, it is evident that the discharged water does not fully comply with the maximum permissible values given by Article 11 of Resolution 631 of 2015, so that not only is the construction of fat traps required as stipulated in Article 9 of Decree 1521 of 1998, but also requires the installation of additional treatment that has the capacity to tolerate fluctuations in flow changes and concentrations that are presented in these establishments. The main objective of this project is to evaluate the behavior of a suspended biomass biological reactor (SBR) and of attached biomass (MBBR) for the treatment of wastewater of a service station. To carry out the evaluation, first the characterization of the water to be treated was carried out, then the hydraulic design of the SBR reactor and MBBR was developed. With these results, the GPS-X simulator developed by the Canadian consulting company hydromantis, Inc. was used in order to construct a model for each reactor under analysis.
