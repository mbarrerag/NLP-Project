# COLOMBIAN INDIGENOUS ORGANIZATIONS TOWARDS A CULTURE AND FINANCIALLY SUSTAINABLE HEALTH SYSTEM

The Constitution of Colombia recognizes as fundamental the multi-ethnic and multicultural character of the country, currently there are 102 indigenous peoples, which are settled in 27 departments of 32 that have the country; the General System of Social Security of Health SGSSS created in 1993, after 25 years of implementation, shows inequalities in terms of cultural inclusion, the effective provision of services and financial balance, among other reasons because the epidemiological system does not take into account indigenous peoples, their knowledge systems and specific problems. In response to this situation, the Colombian indigenous organizations have advanced in the construction of the Indigenous Health System Propio SISPI, for which several indigenous peoples of the Amazonia developed a proposal for community epidemiological surveillance. a qualitative methodology was used for the documentary analysis, participating technique of observation and daily field. revaluation of the systems of own knowledge and its relevance to solve situations in health, for which they built a network of intercultural epidemiological surveillance with complete, timely and quality information; besides making visible the human resource and its importance in the comprehensive health care in their territory
