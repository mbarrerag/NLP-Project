# ADDITION OF ACETAMINOFEN IN MAIZ’S TESTING ACTIVATE BIOCARBON

In this study, new biocarbons activated by chemical treatment of maize tusa with H3PO4 and subsequent pyrolysis were obtained in the temperature range between 500 and 700 °C. The materials obtained were characterization by DRX, FTIR, N2 and SEM sortometry. In addition, the adsorption capacity of acetaminophen was evaluated in aqueous solutions simulated by adsorbing isotherms performed in batch. The results were analyzed using three models of isotherms: Freundlich, Langmuir and Dubinin- Radushkevich (model D-R). The behavior of the adsorption isotherm suggested multilayer coverage on a heterogeneous surface, which is in accordance with the Freundlich isotherm model. The maximum adsorption capacity of the acetaminofen over the materials obtained was in the range between 41.19 mg·g-1 and 400.42 mg·g-1. Finally, the kinetic process was best adjusted with the pseudo-order model, which revealed that the intraarticular phase of the process was the intraarticular diffusion
