# CHARACTERIZATION OF AGRICULTURAL SYSTEMS IN CAMPISH TERRITORIES OF BUGA, COLOMBIA: IMPORTANCE FOR AGRICULTURAL AUTONOMY

The Association of Peasant Workers of Valle del Cauca, Colombia (ASTRACAVA) is organized into agrarian committees that address peasant territories of that department, with a history of work and struggle for the defense of its territory, autonomy and food sovereignty. ASTRACAVA has faced tensions at the territorial level in the face of the productive agricultural model that is a relevant cause of the current global systemic crisis. The loss of peasant territories increases the crisis, since these spaces can pass to this monoculture and agro-industrial farming type, which tends to increase the concentration of land tenure and inequality, with negative effects on the environment, the economy, society and culture. The present study includes the conceptual and operational characterization of the agricultural systems of the peasant territory of the agrarian committees of ASTRACAVA in Cerro Rico, Buga, considering environmental, social, economic and institutional parameters. The study was carried out under the concept of participatory action, using qualitative and quantitative tools, It was found that agricultural systems are based on the production of citrus, tubercles, coffee and vegetables have important links with agrarian autonomy.
