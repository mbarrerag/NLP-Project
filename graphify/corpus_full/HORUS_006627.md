# A Deep Learning model for automatic grading of prostate cancer histopathology images

The Gleason classification is recognized as the standard method for diagnosing prostate cancer. However, it is subject to significant variability among observers due to its dependence on subjective visual assessment. Current approaches to deep learning often require exhaustive annotations at the level of pixels and are generally limited to predictions at the level of patch, which do not incorporate information at the level of foil. Recently, weakly supervised techniques have been promising when generating predictions of full-sheet labels using pathology report labels, which are more readily available.
