# Metabolic rate at rest by indirect calorimetry and prediction equations in athletes with amputation: Concordance analysis

ABSTRACT The Resting Metabolic Rate (MRR) is usually calculated using prediction equations due to its easy access and low cost. However, these equations are not validated in the amputee athlete population. To determine the concordance between the TMR measurement performed by Indirect Calorimetry (IC) and that calculated by prediction equations in athletes with lower limb amputation in Bogotá. Subjects and Methods: This study evaluated 16 adult male athletes, with lower limb amputation. The TMR was measured with IQ and calculated with the prediction equations of Harris-Benedict, Cunningham, Mifflin-St. jeor, schofield and oxford. Two different body mass variables were used: total body mass (MCT) and lean mass (MM) determined by double-energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DEXA) and by the anthropometric method of fractionation of masses into five components (5C). The concordance was determined by the intraclass correlation coefficient (CCI) and plotted by the Bland-Altman method. The TMR determined by the Cunningham equation from MM evaluated by DEXA, showed the best agreement with the CI (CCI= 0.709), followed by Harris-Benedict with MCT (CCI= 0.697) and Cunningham with MM calculated by 5C (CCI= 0.693). The Cunningham and Harris Benedict equation seem to be the most suitable for calculating the TMR, however, more studies with larger samples are required, which will allow more accurate results to be obtained
