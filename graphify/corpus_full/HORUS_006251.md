# Technical-economic typification for coffee production systems in Colombia

The cultivation of coffee in Colombia contributes to its socio-economic stability with more than 540,000 producers. 97% of these are small growers developing their productive systems with high heterogeneity. Most of these producers do not perform an adequate business management of their productive systems despite the efforts made by the rural extension plan of the Federación Nacional de Cafeteros – FNC. The FNC has databases of costs, technical productivity and socio-economic data (21 variables) of about 2,000 Agricultural Production Units – UPAs throughout the country. Using this database was carried out the technical – economic typification of the regional coffee systems, with the aim of providing guidelines that lead to the improvement of the business management program taking into account their location and particularities found. For the achievement of this objective, the functional networks were identified, using a spatial model of Durbin type error, 10 technical variables – discriminating from the different regional productive systems, with the objective of providing guidelines for the improvement of the business management program, taking into account their location and characteristics.
