# Characterization of the consulting population in traditional Chinese medicine, homeopathy and neurotherapeutic services in Colombia

The study seeks to characterize the Colombian population using the services of Traditional Medicine China, Homeopathy and Neuraltherapy between 2011 and 2019, using administrative databases of the General System of Social Security in Health (SGSSS). A quantitative retrospective observational design of cross-sectional cut, analyzing sociodemographic variables (age, gender, department, insurer) and health (comorbidities of the Charlson index, services received, frequency of use) was used. The results show that, of the 70,872 enabled health service providers, only 1.89% offer alternative medicine. Although the MATCs are included in the Plan of Benefits since 1993, the frequency of use is still low, which could be attributed to factors such as the quality of clinical records and the lack of standardized protocols. In conclusion, alternative medicine in Colombia faces challenges as to their supply, access and effectiveness.
