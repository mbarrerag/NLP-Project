# INTERACTIVE APPLICATION FOR THE LINEAL LINE RETURN MODEL

The teaching of Bayesian concepts represents a challenge due to the predominance of the Frequentist approach. Both approaches share objectives, however, their reasoning is different. Therefore, it is crucial to incorporate pedagogical strategies and tools to facilitate the study of Bayesian statistics. This work presents a Shiny application to adjust a simple Bayesian linear regression model with a priori conjugated distributions, in order to improve the understanding of this concept
