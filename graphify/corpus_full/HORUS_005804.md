# Expansion of Synbranchus marmoratus (Synbranchidae) distribution to the Central Andes of Argentina

In this work, a new record is provided that expands the western limit of the geographical distribution of Synbranchus marmoratus towards the Central Andes of Argentina, in the Department of Calingasta, Province of San Juan. In addition, an update of the map of geographical distribution of the species in the country is provided based on this record and a complete bibliographic review. We collected a specimen of this species on September 12, 2016 during a sampling of ichthiofauna in high Andean rivers. This record corresponds to the most western in its distribution in the country. This finding is interesting since this species is considered as an indicator of the ichthiofauna of the Big Rivers and Pampeana Biogeographic Provinces, while the ichthiofauna of the study area of this new registry belongs to the Andean Cuyana Province
