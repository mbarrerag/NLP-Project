# V: Border Authority 21. Border: SCENARIES AND LIMINAL TEATRALITIES. MILLIONS OF YOU ARE LIVING TOGETHER IN SOLIDITY

From the theoretical, critical and poetic reflection on the migratory phenomenon between Venezuela and Colombia during 2019 and 2020, we opened the writing and dismantling of the performative and theatrical work expanded Millions of Beings Living Together in Soledad, carried out in company with the Pitouch Company of Strasbourg, to present a state of art that is aesthetic and political in turn The dramaturgy of the work, is conceived as a space for reflection on the humanitarian crisis, while highlighting the importance of art in the critical observation of our society through the scenic language. The work embodies the documentary encounters in Tierra Muerta, located between the border Colombo Venezolana, of La Guajira. These documentary archives are interwoven with a fictional look that strongly interpels what Migrar, Moverse and the citizens in movement. It is part of the Congress in which I participated Migrations in the theater of the 21st Century, University of Valencia
