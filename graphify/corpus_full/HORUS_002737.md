# THE SOUND POLLUTED AS A BIOGEOLOGICAL RECTOR

The conceptualization of contaminated soil as a biogeochemical rector is presented, with a starting point for the first Law of Thermodynamics of the mass balance of the contaminant stoichiometry and the mass balance of the bioremediation and the pollutant dynamics. The equations design to supply oxygen, nutrients from the data obtained in laboratory and scaled to real field. The design for bioinjection for site contaminated with petroleum hydrocarbons will be shown
