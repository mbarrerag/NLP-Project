# Potential return of nutrients via leaf leaf leaf in silvopastoral systems in northern Colombia*

The cattle-raising systems of the Colombian Caribbean present problems of soil degradation, which affects the productivity and quality of the grasses. The silvopastoral systems are alternatives for sustainable soil management, since they contribute with the cyclaje of nutrients through the leaf-crop of the plant species that make up them.
