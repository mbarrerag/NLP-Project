# ROBINS Teaches the SPANISH TO CHILDREN AND CHILDREN

An interdisciplinary group of Engineering and Health Sciences of the Universidad del Rosario created the first interactive device to support minors in hearing impairment during the process of learning literacy through play. It is almost ready and is expected to be easily accessible to the target population
