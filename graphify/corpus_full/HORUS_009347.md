# POTENTIALITIES FOR THE USE OF THE INFORMATION TOOLS FOR THE OPTIMISATION OF ACCESS TO THE EDUCATIONAL OFFER OF ADULT PERSONS WITH NEUROMUSCULAR TRASTRENTS THAT ARE VOICE IN THE METROPOLITANA AREA OF THE VALLE DE ABURRÁ

Globally, around 790 million people suffer at least one type of disability, which is equivalent to approximately 10% of the world’s population. Of the previous figure, 79 million people suffer from a muscle-motor disability (10% of the total population in a disability situation) (Stucki et al., 2007). According to the Ministry of National Education (2012) 119.060 students diagnosed with special educational needs were enrolled in basic education.However, the 2005 census data indicate that there were 426,425 children under 17 years of age with some form of disability, suggesting that many of them are not studying. (OECD, 2016). Neurodegenerative diseases are a class of neurological disorders where central nervous system neurons die and/or are damaged causing serious disabilities, and ultimately causing death (Tautan et al., 2021). From the perspective of access to educational provision to people with diverse neuromuscular conditions, there is little information.
