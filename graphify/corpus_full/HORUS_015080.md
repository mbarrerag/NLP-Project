# Sick Leave in Colombia in the 2016-2018 period: A Retrospective Cross-Sectional Observational Study

Abstract Introduction: Understanding the situation of disability due to illness, causes and effects of temporary loss of working capacity strengthens policies and management in the provision of health services. To analyze incapacity due to illness in Colombia in the period 2016-2018. Cross-sectional retrospective observational study, with relative risk calculation. 12,410,837 records of formal workers between 18 and 70 years of age and with at least a temporary disability were processed, according to reports from the Ministry of Health and Social Protection. The average age of persons was 37.11 years, 53% were women. On average, 90.6% of disabilities were for dependent workers and 5.6 percent independent workers. The main causes of disability were musculoskeletal system diseases most frequent in adult men according to RR. Men compared to women and adults compared to young people are less likely to have leave due to respiratory disease. Women presented more temporary disabilities due to illness, although men had more days of disability, the median was three days in both genders
