# GLOBAL SOCIOLOGY: A THEORETICAL FRAME

the introduction of the sage handbook of global sociology. is titled "Global Sociology: A Theoretical Frame".This introductioninvites you to embark on a journey through the interconnected fabric of global society, rejecting notions of separation of civilizations in favor of a holistic perspective. The SAGE Handbook of Global Sociology adopts a lens that recognizes the fluidity of societies, influenced by continuous exchanges of people, goods, capital and ideas across temporal and spatial boundaries. This approach, rooted in the recognition of the absence of isolated and intact civilizations, including Europe, challenges the narrative of European universality. Instead, it emphasizes the varied experiences of colonialism and empire in different regions. Rather than defending claims contrary to European universality, this introduction argues for an understanding of history shaped by intricate interconnections rather than stark separations. Drawing inspiration from feminist epistemology, it highlights the situated, partial and contested nature of knowledge. The quest to recover shared histories and forge connections across imposed divisions is fraught with challenges, particularly in the face of explicit threats from governments and vested interest groups in many parts of the world. Despite these obstacles, the handbook argues for the imperative of transcending these barriers to foster a more nuanced and inclusive understanding of global sociology
