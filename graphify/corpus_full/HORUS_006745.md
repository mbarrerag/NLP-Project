# Living conditions of nurses and nursing assistants in a municipal hospital in relation to the perioperative process

This thesis addresses an ethnographic research that analyzes the living conditions of nurses and nursing assistants working in the perioperative service of a regional hospital in Cundinamarca, Colombia. The production of the information focused on three scenarios-context related to the perioperative process: geographic and historical drama, the drama of the work context and the drama of the extra-laboral context. The perspective of pensioners was included to obtain an integral understanding, encompassing both the work experiences in the hospital E.S.E and after their withdrawal. The main objective of exploring these contexts was to understand the situations surrounding the daily life of the workers and therefore their well-being. The qualitative data obtained were analyzed and organized into categories; giving rise to a final product. This product, entitled “Centinela: Conditions of life”, is based on the methodology of ethnodrama and is framed in the current called performative ethnography.
