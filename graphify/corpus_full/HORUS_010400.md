# BIOTECHNOLOGY APPLICABLE TO THE REPARATION OF FISURES IN CONCRETE: MICROORGANIMS PRECITING CARBONATES

The base-cement materials have been consolidated as the most used in the construction industry due to their affordable cost and adequate properties such as compression resistance, acoustics and aesthetics for a variety of applications. However, despite their excellent characteristics, over time and use the structures tend to have cracking problems affecting their durability. Due to this, different alternatives have been sought in the industry for repair and prevention of cracks or cracks in early stages. Within the strategies, the use of microorganisms that induce calcium carbonate precipitation has taken off in the last decade due to its great capacity for bio-cementation, allowing the repair of structures both internally and externally through a material-compatible mineral. This article presents a tour of the main advances obtained in research projects developed at the National University of Colombia, where they have isolated, characterized and evaluated the potential of application of this biotechnology for use in the prevention and repair of fissures in base-cement materials
