# Stochastic approach of lower level problems for robust design of restricted dynamic systems

In this work, the stochastic algorithm adaptive simulated annealing (ASA) is studied as an alternative to find the maxims of a function, in order to incorporate that algorithm into a reduction method (or continuous method) that solves a semi-infinite programming problem (SIP). In the development of the work the performance of the ASA algorithm versus other optimization methods such as BFGS and COBYLA was compared, with several problems. The numerical results show that the ASA algorithm is a viable option that allows to face the uncertainty that can be found in the problems of lower level derived from using the reduction method to find the approximation of an SIP. .
