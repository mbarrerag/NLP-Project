# New forms of rationalization - Industrial economy: (Work presented to the 2nd World Energy Conference)

The fact that Berlin is today the seat of several meetings in which all the peoples of the globe participate, is the most evident demonstration of the importance of a new religion proposed to all those who think and work, called Rationalization, and which consists of the more rational capture and use of all the sources of energy in the world
