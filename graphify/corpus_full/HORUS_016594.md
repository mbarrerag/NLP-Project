# 2. Status of the art of geodiversity, the geo-economic heritage and the economics: perspectives for

After analyzing the role of geodiversity in the range of natural diversity, some questions arise such as: should all elements of geodiversity be protected?, what place does geological heritage occupy within natural heritage? Next, geological heritage as a set of elements that, because of their value or special interest to humanity, deserve to be protected or promoted as representatives par excellence of geodiversity.. In recent decades, concern for the environment and nature conservation have become one of the main concerns of humanity. Currently, most people understand the concept of biodiversity and the need to protect the most threatened species, as well as the different ways in which society benefits from living nature. However, few people, including geoscientists, perceive the importance of geodiversity for the comprehensive management of nature and the services it provides, which in recent years have been called geosistemic services
