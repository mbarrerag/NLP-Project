# EXPERT RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE DIAGNOSIS, TREATMENT, AND MANAGEMENT OF ADULT B-CELL ACUTE LYMPHOBLASTIC LEUKEMIA IN LATIN AMERICA

Despite strong induction chemotherapy response rates, only 30%-40% of patients with adult B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) attain long-term remission. This study analyzes ALL in Latin America (LA) and recommends diagnosis, treatment, and management protocols
