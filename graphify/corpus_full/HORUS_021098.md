# Seed of Space Studies

The Semillero of Spatial Studies derives the same-named research group (Seminario de Estudios Espaciales) whose object of study is, in general terms, the limit of the semantic field of the concept of space and how any subject is susceptible to become a spatial problem. In this sense, the semillero proposes an open dynamic where different lines of research converge through projects proposed by undergraduate and graduate students, without thematic impositions by the teachers who direct and participate. Although the concept of space includes a complexity that would be inabridgeable in its entirety (since it is not the same as the scientific analysis of the space from the arts or the human sciences), nine (9) lines of research can be proposed as a formative strategy, so that any student, particularly from the Faculty of Architecture, can participate and know the research dynamics from his own initiatives. The proposed lines are: Architecture; plastic arts and construction; Visual Design; Spatial Design; Virtual Design lines; Spatial Heritage; Landscape; Landscape. The first three lines refer to the pregrad of the faculty (Arts, Architecture and Construction) from his case. During the first half of the year, students saw theoretical contents related to training research with the support of teachers and graduates of experts in this field, and during the current semester, the project formulation and consolidation phase is being developed. Students, individually or collectively, propose not only which areas are of their interest but also which should comply with the delivery of an academic product and dissemination of results. With more than 26 members enrolled, the research seedbed currently has research projects related to the design of a typological matrix for rural housing, the study of the urban edge of Medellín in legal and spatial terms, the relationship of habitat with water, the application of the spatial syntax theory for a comprehensive diagnosis in educational institutions in the Antioquian Urabá, the conceptualization around the heterogeneous cultural landscape in Colombia, the theoretical construction of virtual space, the cartography of airport tracks throughout the national territory, among others
