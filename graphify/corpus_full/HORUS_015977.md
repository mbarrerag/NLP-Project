# On the Diversity of Neotropical Hymenoptera

Hymenoptera is globally one of the most diverse groups of insects. There are no recent firm estimates or data on the size of fauna of this order in the Neotropical Region. Taking as a basis catalogues, printed lists or in digital form, in addition to monographs and other sources, the Neotropics comprises 26 superfamilies, 92 families, 3162 genera and a little more than 33,640 species. Hymenoptera diversity in the Neotropics is greater than in the Near, Palearctic and Australian regions. It is probably the world’s richest Neotropics region in families, genera and species of Hymenoptera, with the majority of endemic taxa in this region
