# The talk of people with Parkinson's disease after neurosurgery: follow-up of Spanish-speaking patients in Bogotá for one year.

The characteristics of the speech were described by a group of Spanish speakers with Parkinson's disease (EP) who were treated with deep cerebral stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus (STN-DBS).A longitudinal study was carried out with PRE and POST measurements implantation of the STN-DBS in 9 participants with idiopathic EP (4 women and 5 men).For each participant the deterioration of speech at the beginning and end of the follow-up was evaluated and acoustic measurements of the voice (f0, SDf0, intensity, Jitter, RAP, Shimmer, NHR, HNR, LTAS and CPPs), maximum phonation time (TMF), speech intelligibility (% of correctly identified words and degree of intelligibility), speech speed and oral diadochinesis were taken, before receiving the STN-DBS and every 3 months post-interventional status for 1 year. Although no POST group changes were found that were statistically significant in none of the speech parameters studied, nor an identifiable pattern of change over time, an analysis of frequency of changes POST intervention, as well as the individual changes of ST-TSS were observed in two times were observed
