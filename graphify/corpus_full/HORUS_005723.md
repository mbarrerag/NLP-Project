# Health experiences and practices: Covid-19 and Tuberculosis in two multi-ethnic communities in Leticia-Amazonas, Colombia

In Colombia, particularly in the department of the Amazon, the high prevalence of Tuberculosis and Covid-19 has generated great interest in the practices and experiences that indigenous communities develop around these two infectious entities. To investigate through dialogue the experiences and practices in health of traditional medicine in the face of TB and Covid-19 in the multi-ethnic communities of San Sebastián de los Lagos and Yoi with settlement in Leticia-Amazonas. Study based on ethnography, participant observation and dialogue of knowledge in the communities of San Sebastián de los Lagos and Yoi in July 2021, to deepen the meanings focused on the use of traditional medicine around respiratory diseases Tuberculosis and Covid-19. The recorded contents are the primary source of content analysis and textual analysis. There is a proactive attitude on the part of health managers to establish a complementarity between traditional and conventional medicine. Tuberculosis and Covid-19 have promoted the positioning of the use of plants as one of the fundamental pillars of health promotion in the territory
