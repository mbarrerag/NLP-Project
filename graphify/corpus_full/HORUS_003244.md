# Project: Model of simplification for the representation of the effects of different CTI public policy frameworks on sustainable development of innovation systems

The CTI policies have been positioned as a fundamental element to drive technological development and science in the world (Chaminade & Lundvall, 2019).There are different frameworks of policies of CTI that allow policymakers to justify the design, selection and use of specific instruments (Flanagan, Uyarra, & Laranja, 2011).From the perspective of Schot and Steinmueller (2018), there are three frameworks: R & D, Innovation Systems (SI) and Transformative Change and beyond this perspective also identify the Mission-oriented Policies (Mazzucato, 2018). Currently, there is a sense of urgency to redirect public science, technology and innovation policies towards the solution of the Great Global Challenges (GDG) (Chaminade & Lundvall, 2019) and to achieve this, Lundvall (2022), it is stated that it must aim towards the transformation of the Innovation Systems into an ecosystem in which also interacts the policies of other frameworks, since any form of policy proposal is unable to address the current global challenges, and how to achieve the clarity of the CITI policies.
