# Effect of microstructure on the deformation and fracture of dual-phase steels.

This research aims to evaluate the evolution of damage and microstructural fracture mechanisms associated with uniaxial traction (UTT) tests, uniaxial cyclic (CTT) limits, propagation of cracks and fracture energy, related to the fraction in volume and the distribution of martensite in a commercial steel dual phase (DP).First, through the implementation of intercritical thermal treatments different fractions in volume of martensite (MVF) were created in the material in delivery. Nanoindentations were made to evaluate the mechanical properties of ferrite and martensite. Subsequently, the mechanisms of damage using SEM micrographs obtained from samples UTT and CST. The resistance of the materials to the propagation of cracks and the energy associated with the formation of fracture surfaces are determined by the condition of the CST load. Finally, the mechanisms of the deformation and the spread of cracks in the microstructure are determined by a lower rate of stress and resistance using SEM-EBSD.
