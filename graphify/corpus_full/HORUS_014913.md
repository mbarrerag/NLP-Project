# Academic Burnout Syndrome in urology residents of Colombia.

The resident of Urology is immersed in academic and extra academic situations that can generate exhaustion, disinterest, self-abotage versus academic activities and doubts about the value of the study, which leads to Burnout syndrome in an academic context, this fact can trigger desertion, low academic performance, low self-esteem and personal frustration. To determine the prevalence of Academic Burnout Syndrome in residents of Urology in Colombia, and describe strategies that can prevent or decrease academic Burnout syndrome in residents of Urology. Transversal cut-off study, with sampling by convenience of Urology residents of different academic programs of Colombia.. The applied instrument was scale of measurement Maslach Burnout inventory-student Survey (MBI-SS). the prevalence of burnout activities academido in the 59.5% (95 % CI: 59.4-59.6%) in residents of Urologia in Colombia.
