# Evaluation of image quality in brain studies with FDG in positron emission tomography

The PET-CT analysis is essential for assessing the brain meta-bolism and detecting anomalies. The brain PET studies with 18F-FDG allow identification-tififying neurodegenerative diseases, tumors and psychiatric disorders in early stages to be achieved. The evaluation of the PET protocols allows for improvement of image quality, providing greater diagnostic accuracy, facilitating more effective and personalized treatment. This work involves the evaluation of protocols for acquiring brain PET-CT studies. For this purpose, a methodology is proposed that includes different stages from the investigation to the evaluation. Initially, a bibliographic review is carried out in databases corresponding to the current protocols, identifying that the first step requires a guide for filling the cylindrical and cerebral 3D Hoffman mannequins with a quality of the PTCi activity of the nuclear medicine systems. The study process of the activity in the brain system is equivalent and corresponds to the activity value in a real study; this guide is presented as an annex and aims to be a resource for the nuclear medicine services.
