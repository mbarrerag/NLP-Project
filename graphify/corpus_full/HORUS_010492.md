# REFLECTIONS ON THE ROLE OF THE HEALTH-PROFESSOR PROFESSIONAL IN HEALTH AREA EDUCATION

The educational process in the health professions is being transformed, integrating constructivist notions into the traditional model and changing the conception of the student. The teacher must modernize his educational practice, understanding that expertise in his or her health area (basic or clinical) is necessary, but not sufficient, and that, in turn, the teaching-learning process involves a more structured elaboration, in accordance with the curricular needs. Thus, the teaching act is dynamic, transformed with the time and experiences of the teachers, by means of clear strategies of evaluation of the educational process, to achieve the professional development of those who in the future will be in charge of the health care of the population
