# FLEXIBLE LABORATORY TO PROTECT ELECTRIC AND MICRORRED EMOLAR DEVICES

The analysis of the electrical behaviour of microgrids that include renewable sources, storage devices, nonlinear loads and traditional components is of the utmost importance for research and training new professionals in energy systems. This article presents the design and implementation of a flexible laboratory to test modern electrical devices and emulate real microgrids. The design of the laboratory begins with the design of several devices, followed by the structuring and implementation of a bus to connect the devices. The integration includes switches, contactors, copper bars, ground connection, PLC and safety connectors. The flexible laboratory is validated experimentally testing a single phase converter, charging and unloading three VRLA batteries, replicating a discharge profile and emulating a real microgrid installed in Isla Fuerte, Colombia. The results are used to evaluate the operation of the devices and to analyze the behavior of a real micronetwork, demonstrating the laboratory's usefulness in educational and research contexts
