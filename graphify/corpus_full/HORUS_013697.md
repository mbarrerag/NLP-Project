# The global agro-industrial system as the axis of agro-food geopolitics and its relationship with hunger: the case of soy (2008-2018).

The soy has spread throughout the planet as an industrial input of food interest. Its development and expansion, has been chained directly to the growth of the global agroindustrial system that has generated, in turn, negative environmental impacts and food crisis of different kinds throughout the globe. Large corporations and investors exercise a control of the entire productive system ranging from the genetically modified (transgenic) seeds to all products (processed or not) that satisfy the culinary and animal feeding demand. The review, analysis and historicity of this oil product represents a way of seeing how in the planet it affects a geopolitical model that expands rapidly and seeks permanent control of human food in the future.
