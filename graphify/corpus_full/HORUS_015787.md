# BIODEGRADATION OF FOOD FOOD AND SCIENTIFIC ARGUMMENT FOODS IN THE SCHOOL ENVIRONMENT

Thermoplastic polymers are used in the manufacture of food packaging, surpassing materials such as cardboard and glass, because of the advantages they offer such as low production cost, ease of transport and barrier properties, among others. However, these packagings at the end of their use, do not suffer an efficient absorption within the ecological system, generating irreversible conditions for the environment, related to the decrease of non-renewable natural resources, pollution of water, air and soil and contribution to global warming. Since in educational institutions, students consume a large amount of packaged food, without taking into account the environmental impact generated by the manufacture, use and waste of food wrappers, with this work seeks to create environmental awareness in middle education students through the development of scientific argumentative competition based on the discussion on biodegradation of the packagings for food, seeking to promote scientific dialogue and strengthen critical capacities within a social space. In the methodological design, first the level of argumentation of the students was identified through the scientific discourse, using the research model directed in which the students built, advised the environmental process, seeking to promote scientific understanding and strengthen critical capacities within a social space.
