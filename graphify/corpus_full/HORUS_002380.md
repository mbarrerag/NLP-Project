# EVALUATION: IDEAL TIME FOR ETHICS, LEARNING AND THE IMPLEMENTATION OF KNOWLEDGE.

This article starts from the reflection on an aspect of the teaching exercise: learning, as is evaluation. And specifically, on the ethics reflected at this moment in education, in which the citizen's ability to socialize and exercise from a profession is recognized. The context of the article is due to the field of training at the technical and university levels, in an aspect that, although it seems strictly operational, reflects the need to reflect on the way in which processes are evaluated so that their completion will account not only for an suitability or competence to work, but also for performance with integrality and collective commitment by the graduate
