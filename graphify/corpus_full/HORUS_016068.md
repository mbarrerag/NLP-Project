# PYRROLO[1,2-A]QUINOXALINAS 4-SUSTITIED WITH ANTIMYCOBACTERIAL PONTENTIAL

The resistance generated by Mycobacterium tuberculosis to first and second line drugs of the treatment represent a risk for the indigenous population of the Amazon region, since there is a high vulnerability to tuberculosis infection due to the precarious social conditions with which they live besides the difficult geographic location. This problem creates the need to contribute to the discovery and development of new antituberculosis drugs. The objective of this work was to evaluate the antimycobacterial potential of the pyrrolus[1,2-a]quinoxalins 4-substituted. It was proposed as methodology, the selection and in-silico study of 14 pyrrolo[1,2-a]quinoxalins. The synthesis of the compounds was then performed and finally tests of biological activity and cellular viability were carried out to determine the antimycobacterial potential of each molecule.
