# Knowledge management and processes in enterprises in the tourism sector of the CALDAS department (Colombia)

Abstract Knowledge management and process management are fundamental developments for today's companies; in this sense, the companies of the tourism sector of the department of Caldas (Colombia) can guarantee their permanence and success using and incorporating these concepts properly. For this case, the objective was to establish the relationship between knowledge management and process management in these companies. For this purpose, the life cycle of knowledge was studied and analyzed with the missionary, strategic and operational processes of these companies. A qualitative research was developed with a descriptive, explanatory approach with a population of 61 tourist operators. The main finding corresponds to the significant correlation between the life cycle of knowledge and the management by processes of the companies, which guarantees adequate knowledge management
