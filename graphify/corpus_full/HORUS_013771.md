# Opening Doors: The role of the school in the appropriation of traditional community knowledge and practices in the municipality of Guapi.

In this project we seek to redefine the role of the school in the territories. From a standardized and static school that deals with the "transmit of the school knowledge" to one that is contextualized and assumes its projection towards the community. The school as a pillar of knowledge can contribute to the sustainability of the communities. In the case of the municipality of Guapi this sustainability has been understood from the valuation of the traditional knowledge that have allowed the life of these communities in this territory. The same way in which the universities have as a missionary role the extension, the school must recognize in constant interaction with the communities and assume that in that interaction it learns researching and incorporating the knowledge that come from the people and that they must circulate intergenerally. _x0093_Open the doors_x0094_ as a metaphor, invites the school to rethink and see the potential of being the space in which different knowledge actors are found to build new knowledge. The experiences and practices of the traditional knowledge, of the inhabitants, the teachers and those who arrive from the university academy, to make the creative and participatory knowledge, the school will open to them
