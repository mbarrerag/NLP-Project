# Spatial modelling of supply and demand in Colombia based on financial data analysis.

This final master's degree work proposes a negocio intelligence tool that allows modeling the supply and demand for a defined geographical area through the analysis of financial data. A review of the literature on geolocation systems, geomarketing, transactional information in the Colombian field and the use that financial institutions give to the data is carried out. It also proposes a technique of geographical grouping that allows to preserve the anonymity and privacy in the data, as well as a data model and a functional prototype of the tool. Finally, three cases of use are presented in which the tool provides valuable information for the decision-making process within the entity or expert advice to a client as a strategy for retaining and deepening the client relationship – financial entity
