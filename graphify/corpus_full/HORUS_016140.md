# THE SABANA OF BOGOTÁ: FROM FOUR TO UNUNDABLE PLANTICIE. A PROCESS VITED FROM THE NEAR OF SUBA

in 2016 the distrital institute of the arts - idartes, presented the strategic line of art, science and technology for the development plan 2016-2020 is bogota better for all? of the Mayor of Bogotá. This publication, entitled mihique II suna gue II water roads, is the fifth example of a collection of specialized texts that are published annually and that are framed in the axis of research on ancestrality and technology of the Art, Science and Technology Line, which seeks to raise crosses and tensions through the different ways of seeing and understanding the world, that expand the concept of technology from perspectives such as the archaeology of media, the review of complex technologies and the generation of links with the thought of cultures and ancestral knowledge; by means of research with a transdisciplinary perspective and, from 2022, that include a transmedial component. This book collects the research of the Collective Zanjas and Camelones, coordinated by María Buenaventura.
