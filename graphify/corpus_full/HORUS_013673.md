# Complete the city of the enclave: from academic idealism to public policy at the UK University of Britain in Bogotá

This work deals with the phenomenon of residential enclave in Bogotá from the perspective of urban design as a discipline of transformation with a view to describing it, analyzing it and intervening it and thus improving its relationship with the surrounding public space. Its central reflection is tied to a broader research that began in 2018 from the academy with a number of people interested in the phenomenon of residential enclave that have produced theses and academic documents leading to the understanding of this particular phenomenon. This document deepens in the identification of a specific type of residential enclave as an example and proposes a mechanism for improving the quality of the public space of these types of enclaves: consensual architectures. Once the universe of intervention is identified, it plans an exercise as an urban laboratory in the UPL of Britain from the eyes of the previous regulations and the current one, the POT 2021 and in particular the article 311, in which the impact of implementing a methodology of intervention of these characteristics can be assessed. specific type of residential enclave as an example and proposals a mechanism for improving the quality of public space in the neighbourhood types of enclaves: consensual architectures. Eleven the universal of intervention has been identified, I propose an exercise as an urban laboratory in the British district district from the eyes of the previous and current regulations, the POT 2021 and in particular article 311, in which the impact of implementing a methodology as this one can be evaluated. The objective of this work is to advance to the real formulation of the public policies rooted in academic discussion of the movements of pedagogical utopia to executive practicality of urban regulations
