# Natural buoyancy of calcopirite under different oxide-reduction conditions

In this work microflotation tests were carried out, in Hallimond modified tube, and measures of resting potential, with platinum electrode, to study the buoyancy, without the presence of collector, of natural calcopyrite samples. It was determined that natural buoyancy depends strongly on the pH of the suspension, reaching a maximum in the range of pH 6 to 8 and falling sharply in the alkaline range. At a pH value of 11 the buoyancy is practically zero. The high natural buoyancy, at pH 8, is not suppressed by the addition of reducing agents, nor by moderate amounts of oxidizing agents.
