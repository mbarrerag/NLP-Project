# THE ROLE OF NARRATIVE PERSUATION IN EDUCATION FOR IMPROVING HEALTH IN LATIN AMERICA: REVISION OF THE LAST FIVE YEARS

An integrative review of the literature of the last five years on the research into narrative persuasion in communication for health in Latin America was carried out, the search was carried out in the WOS and Scopus databases. 87 documents of the six met the selection criteria. The results indicate that there is a great deal to be investigated in Latin America on how narratives can influence preventive behaviors and risky behaviors in health, this would help demonstrate how powerful these strategies are in generating attitudinal and behavioral changes, helping those who make decisions in the health area to create public policies based on empirical evidences that are consistent with social and cultural realities in each population
