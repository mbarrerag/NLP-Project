# ECONOMIC INCENTIVES FOR CONSERVATION AND ITS CONTRIBUTION TO THE CONFIGURATION OF NEW DYNAMICS FOR RURAL DEVELOPMENT AND MULTI-ACTOR RELATIONS: A REVIEW OF THE PAYMENT PROGRAMME FOR BANK ENVIRONMENTAL SERVICES2 IN COLOMBIA

The new dynamics of rural development involve a multiplicity of social actors and purposes that transcend the one-dimensional vision of rural as a food producer, including new visions associated with the provision of ecosystem services. This multiplicity is also immersed in development projects and in the consolidation of economic capitals that are put before the conservation of the environment and the quality of life of the inhabitants; putting in contrast the rural development, sustainability and improvement of the quality of life of the population. However, at the present time a series of institutional arrangements and multi-actor relations are formed, where the local communities contribute the environmental services, integrating with actors such as academia, public and private organizations and NGOs ́s in the search for multi-actor conservation strategies. This work presents one of these strategies implemented in Colombia under the figure of Payment for Environmental Services (PSA) and called BancO2 that has allowed to conserve 365,967 ha, integrating 21,802 rural families, 150 private companies and 325 public institutions, that allows to preserve the human communities in areas of environmental importance, generating economic income for the improvement of the quality of life.
