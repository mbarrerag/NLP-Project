# Regionalism and multipolarity: The Republic of India and its link to the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO)

This paper considers the Republic of India's link to the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) in the context of strengthening Asian regionalism and the multipolar system. For this analysis, the work takes up the diplomatic, political and economic relations of India with other member countries of the organization: China, Russia and Pakistan; showing that the historical tensions that have crossed relations between India and China and India and Pakistan prevail and represent a challenge for the strengthening of Asian regionalism, as well as for the scope of strategies and cooperation agreements established within the OCS. However, the scenario of interaction and cooperation that the OCS represents, allows the strengthening of the multipolar system by promoting diverse strategies that strengthen the political, economic and diplomatic role of the member countries, especially China, Russia and China; this favours the consolidation of other poles of power in the international system and the counterweight to US influence.
