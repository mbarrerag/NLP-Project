# Recreational Cannabis: Profile of Cannabinoids in Marijuana Samples Provided by Consumer Population

Cannabis or marijuana is one of the most widely used psychoactive substances in the world, so to know the composition and type of cannabis that is marketed in urban environments is a necessary input for the design of public health policies based on scientific evidence. This study characterized the main phytocannabinoids of marijuana samples (cigarettes or buds) obtained in urban and rural areas of the city Medellin, in October 2021. A non-probabilistic sampling was carried out in which 87 samples of marijuana donated by consumers were collected at different collection points throughout the city, applying the techniques of mass gas chromatography and flame ionization for the characterization of phytocannabinoids. Tetrahydrocannabinol was found as the main constituent of circulating marijuana in Medellin, where 67.8% of the samples presented a high or higher toxicological range for THC; the above in a context where the unregulated market limits the possibility that consumers have in the practice of calibrating or deciding the concentration of cannabinoids in their doses
