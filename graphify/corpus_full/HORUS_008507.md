# TOWARDS AN EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS OF HYDROCHEMIC IN THE CUENCA HIGH RISING OF THE CAUCA - COLOMBIA 1990-2021.

The natural dynamics and anthropic activities influence the quality of surface water at spatial time levels.The river Cauca in its passage through the department of Valle del Cauca, is vital for the economy of this department, which faces problems due to household, agricultural, industrial and mining discharges, providing mainly chemical pollutants.Despite its importance, no multivariate study of the spatial variation of hydrochemistry in the upper basin of the river Cauca has been found. Therefore, the objective of this study is to evaluate the spatial variability at multivariate level of hydrochemistry, in the stretch of ten monitoring stations in thirty-two years (1990-2021), with eight chemical parameters of water quality: Sulphates, Phosphates, Chlorus, Bicarbonates, Nitrates, Calcium, Magnesium and pH. The methodology consisted in the use of descriptive and inferential statistical techniques. The descriptive analysis was carried out through different types of graphs and tables. To quantify the correlation between the parameters studied, the Pearson coefficient was used, besides the analysis of the characteristics and the conglomeral values. natos and Nitrates in the periods analyzed
