# DESIGN OF AN ADAPTIVE IGU MODEL IN A SYSTEM LMS FROM THE REGISTRATION OF THE USER'S FACIAL EMOTIONS AND EXPRESIONS, USING MACHINE LEARNING TECHNICALS UNDER USER'S MÉTRIC EXPERIENCES.

Currently, the teaching-learning process through virtual platforms has become de facto technology. To adequately respond to this reality, a commitment between high quality courses and the creation of personalized virtual environments that have demonstrated their potential to reduce gaps in access to the educational process and that are strongly conditioned by the particularities, tastes and experiences of each student [1] is necessary. Considering that the more the LMS system adapts to the characteristics of a student, the less time it needs to perform tasks within the platform and the more you will feel comfortable interacting with it, it is necessary to design learning resources according to the characteristics of each student, beyond the static environments of most current LMS systems [2]. From this reality, the design of an adaptive model of User Graphic Interface (GUI) in a MS Moodle system based on the recording of emotions and facial expressions of the user, using techniques of Machine Learning, which promotes teaching-learning processes under metrics of User Experiences (UX), for students of virtual university programs System (PALAVE)
