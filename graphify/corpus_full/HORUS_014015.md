# Political composition of the Local Administrative Boards in the municipalities of Medellín. A comparative analysis of the elections 2015 and 2019

The Local Administrative Boards (JAL) are a tool for decentralization and were instituted in the Colombian Constitution of 1991, with the aim of strengthening citizen participation. They are voted every four years in the local elections, where mayors, governors, councils and departmental assemblies are elected. In this scenario, this research aims to compare the results of the elections to the Local Administrative Boards (JAL) in the city of Medellín in the periods 2015 and 2019. In theoretical terms, the study dialogues with the concepts of participation and political parties. To make this analysis, the electoral results will be used in each of the municipalities of the city and how these results are distributed in a general way. The research is relevant from the Political Science in that the electoral studies are focused on the single-inominal dispute for mayor of Medellín and no emphasis has been placed on the first institution popularly chosen as the JAL. The investigation will have a descriptive character and opens the possibility for further inquiries of the electoral reality in the city
