# Design and implementation of a teaching module for the teaching-learning of particle physics for grade 11.

The International Baccalaureate IB Organization (IB) is an educational foundation based in Geneva, Switzerland, and founded in 1968, which provides programs to students from 3 to 19 years of age. The Diploma Program (PD), focused on students between 16 and 18 years of age, has prescribed topics for all subjects, including physics. One of the topics required and evaluated in external and standardized examinations worldwide, is that of particle physics. The Tilatá College, which belongs to this organization since 2017, found that this topic is one of the most complex and least interesting for students in the physics subject. Because of this, a learning module was designed and implemented with a learning methodology that focuses on the student and is based on play and gamification. To apply the module a didactic material consisting of two card games, the first, the style Super Triumph, with particles, and the second, a game was designed and implemented with a learning methodology that focuses on the student and based on the game and gamification.
