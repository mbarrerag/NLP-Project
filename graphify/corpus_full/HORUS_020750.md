# The influence of music therapy on the psychological well-being of health personnel in hospital institutions in Villavicencio, during times of pandemic by COVID-19: Case studies

The psychological well-being in the health staff of hospital institutions of the city of Villavicencio, has been affected by the conditions of the pandemic, generated by COVID-19, so the objective of this research consisted in understanding the influence of the music therapy, through the four methods, in favor of therapeutic accompaniment to the staff. The qualitative research process with case design of phenomenological type, was carried out with three participants in ages between 20 and 27 years, belonging to different hospital institutions of the city of Villavicencio, that they are part of the care area - Intensive Care Unit (UCI) and Urgencias -, and administrative, in direct care of patients COVID 19. The musicotherapeutic process was carried out individually; for each case, the number of sessions was different, thus, the participants received eight, seven and five sessions respectively during three months, period in which each session had a duration of 1 hour per participant.
