# THE APPROACH TO THE THEORICAL ASIGNATURE ON IDEAS STUDY AND THE PRODUCTION OF KNOWLEDGE AT SOCIAL WORK: A STUDIES OF A PEDAGOLOGICAL EXPERIENCE

The article analyzes a curriculum development experience that has as an articulating tool for Problem-Based Learning (ABP) in a theoretical subject referring to the epistemology of Social Work. The study first proposes the formation of competencies in Social Work and explains the possible forms that the ABP assumes. Secondly, it presents the emergence of a pedagogical ecosystem in the analyzed experience that places the ABP as a tool for the formation of research competencies combined with others, as exhibition classes with questions and task-based learning. The analysis is based on the review of classroom journals, 100 diagnostic questionnaires and 59 self-evaluation questionnaires resolved by students participating in the subject in 2022, most of them, initiating their university training process. The results of the analysis present a series of tense narratives from which the experience is assessed in terms of knowledge about the theoretical influences of Social Work, skills and investigative attitudes. The study concludes with the idea that the proposal and activation of pedagogical ecosystems for theoretical subjects requires an active teaching-learning that has as a center the presence of the students and the evaluated specialists
