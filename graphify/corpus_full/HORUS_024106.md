# HUMANIZATION OF HEALTH CARE: ANALYSIS OF THE CONCEPT

The analysis of the concept of Humanization of healthcare presents a theoretical and reflective approach, which shows the principles and values that give added value to the health services provided in a changing context. To analyze the concept of Humanization of health services. The proposal by Beth L Rodgers was applied that establishes seven steps for the analysis of the concept. Documentary research was carried out with databases Springer Plus, Pubmed, EBSCO Host and Virtual Library in Health and national agency; in spanish, english and portuguese. With 33 scientific articles we can analyze the concept of humanization of health care, where ethical, epistemological and methodological attributes are described. Likewise, the assertive and effective communication in health care, in the context of health-disease, contributing to nursing conceptions about humanization in this framework. The humanization of health care allows developing strategies for the benefit of well-being and good practice.
