# Physical and metabolic changes triggered after osteopathic manipulation

This article is a systematic review of the existing literature on the physical and metabolic changes that occur after osteopathic manipulation. A thorough search of studies was carried out in the databases PubMed, Medline, PEDro and Cochrane Library, finding 11 studies that met the inclusion criteria. The studies analyzed evaluated the effects of osteopathic manipulation in different physical and metabolic parameters, including the response to pain, heart rate, blood pressure, blood pressure, glycemia and cytokines levels, among others. The results of the review showed that osteopathic manipulation can have significant beneficial effects, on muscle strength, pain reduction in different regions of the body, on heart rate, blood pressure and respiratory function. As for the metabolic effects, this systematic review of the literature suggests that osteopathic manipulation may have significant positive effects in different parameters, including inflammation and response to pain.
