# KEY KEY FOR REFORMING THE POSPANDEMIA.

The pandemic challenged humanity in every way, as it directly influenced the social, political and economic environment, having as many aspects as the social structure itself. The pandemic put us in a state of conflict, since the world paralysed to face a common problem, covid-19 co-virus. On the basis of this certainly hostile scenario, the existing systems have had to be adapted to survive, working to strengthen them and creating what is necessary to rebuild them, since the conditions of countries such as Colombia, already precarious, have been further diminished in terms of guaranteeing rights. GIADESC presents this book, a compilation of articles that seek to develop individual hypotheses that in turn respond together to the same question: what should we do in Colombia to guarantee economic, social and cultural rights in postpandemia? To respond, it is part of the fact that the pandemic is born as a historical and disruptive fact of understanding the legal systems envisaged for normality scenarios that, in the majority, are not intended to address crises of those magnitudes
