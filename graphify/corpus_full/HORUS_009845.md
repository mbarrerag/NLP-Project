# Parametric optimization and experimental validation of a prototype orange juice spray dryer designed using CFD.

There is a wide variety of dehydration techniques that are used to reduce water content in food, however, many of these techniques require long times and high temperatures that can partially degrade the nutritional properties and organoleptic characteristics of products. The spray drying or Spray Dryer, is a technique highly used to obtain powdered products. The advantage of this technique is the short residence time and low operating temperatures at which the drying process can be performed. The objective of this project was to optimize the operating parameters and experimentally validate a prototype spray drying for orange juice designed by CFD (Computational Fluid Dynamics). The project execution allowed to conclude that the proper parameters of the dryer to dry orange juice valence are: air pressure 1.8 bar; liquid flow rate 1.1 l/h; inlet-salting temperatures for the desulent 200/80 °C and in maltodextrin encapsulatant with a concentration of 10 % (p).
