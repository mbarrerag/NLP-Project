# The eclectic revelation of what exists: the proposal of Orlando Morillo Santacruz

In this reflection I try to understand certain courses of art in recent decades in Latin America, and it has its origin in the words of presentation to inaugurate the exhibition Aesthetics of Carnival of the artist from the native region Orlando Morillo Santacruz, in November 2019, and that took as stage the Water Mirror of the intercultural building Rogelio Salmona of the Faculty of Human Science of the National University of Colombia. This event was an invitation of the Institute of Studies in Communication and Culture (IECO), in the framework of the III Latin American Congress «Voces del Carnaval, Erotismos, Músicas y Danzas», whose country was invited was Mexico. This singular exhibition was complemented by the interview I had with the artist about his trajectory, aesthetic thinking and teaching in the Department of Visual Arts of the University of Nariño
