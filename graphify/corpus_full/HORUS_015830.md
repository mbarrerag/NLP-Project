# The documentation of the experience from the perspective of the design. Case study 2022.

In this article, the methodological process and the results obtained based on the model being developed by the research group TEI-D on documentation and representation of the experience lived, from the perspective of design, based on the phenomenological approach. For the case of application of the model, the research was developed within the framework of the project Expociencia Tolima 2022. The guiding question on the experience lived, has determined a position of the group TEI-D, in the approach of research projects, that allow a contribution from the discipline of the design, to describe elements that underlie or become manifest about a particular phenomenon. From that approach the instruments to be used were designed, to document from different perspectives the experience lived in the Expociencia Tolima 2022, taking into account that there, that if understood as a phenomenon to be studied, is experienced by various actors, which enable consolidating a description enriched.
