# Evaluation of the coffee erasure produced on the university campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia _x0096_ Bogotá Headquarters in the generation of high-value products for energy and environmental applications

The coffee-cleaning is a common byproduct of the coffee industry, which is generated as waste after obtaining the aqueous extract from roasted coffee beans. At the university campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia _x0096_ Bogotá headquarters this residue is currently collected in the different buildings and is subjected to composatje processes; however, this can generate environmental problems in water and soil due to the slow degradation of this residue. As an alternative and in accordance with the circular economy, it is feasible to obtain oils from this material by extraction with solvents, which can have various applications, and the resulting solid residue can be useful in obtaining carbonous materials that in combination with photoactive compounds allow synthesis of suitable catalysts for the degradation of emerging environmental pollutants. This study is essential in the search of sustainable solutions for environmental problems associated with the removal of coffee and the pollutants emerging in the water. In addition, it can contribute to the development of more responsible practices in the coffee industry and the creation of high added value products with photo-cleaning.
