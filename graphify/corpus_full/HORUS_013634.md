# Overcoming the hills and telling it: Construction of the landscape of the edge of the Bogotá Sabana from cycling

This research is an incursion towards the construction of a landscape that links the rare species of people who have forged a close relationship with a self-propelled vehicle, two wheels, and who identify themselves as cyclists, immersed in an environment of edge of Sabana that transcends the traditional limits of the urban and rural, which contains many symbolic, cultural, ecological and topographic layers to decipher. There it is possible to understand the indivisible dialectic of body/landscape that has always been maintained in the Andean context from precolonial periods.
