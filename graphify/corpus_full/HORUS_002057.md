# Discontinuous asphaltic mixtures with hot-produced granular support matrix for rolling under demanding technical conditions

Under the conditions of transit, climatic, geographical and economic characterizing Colombia, the primary road network of the country presents a low durability, where flexible pavements are predominant formed by dense asphaltic mixtures that do not satisfy the operational requirements.These mixtures, in addition to their limited useful life, present deficiencies in terms of road safety, contributing to a high rate of accidentality, especially in intense precipitation scenarios.In the international context, solutions such as asphaltic mixtures with granular support matrix, of the type Stone Mastic Asphalt (SMA), which are highlighted by their open granular structure, high stability and excellent mechanical performance.These properties make them a promising alternative to improve the durability and safety of Colombian pavements, especially in corridors subjected to high load and adverse climate conditions. This work analyzes the technical viability of the Asphaltic mixtures with the most efficient support granular matrix (MAMGS), considering three SMA granulometrys (19 mm, 12.5 mm and 9.5 mm). In the international context, alternatives such as Stone Mastic Asphalt (SMA) mixures with a granular support matrix have been developed and applied. All of this support enhanced road safety and the development of safer, more durable transportation networks
