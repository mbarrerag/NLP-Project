# The beekeeping of the Montes de María in the department of Bolívar is enhanced by designing products based on fermented bee pollen with functional and eco-friendly properties.

In Colombia, pollen production is up to five times higher than in those countries traditionally recognized for its commercialization. However, imported pollen is cheaper than the national product itself. In some departments of the country, especially in the sub-region of the Montes de María de Bolívar, the marketing of pollen does not comply with the necessary records for the marketing due to the non-compliance of the food label and the lack of knowledge of the obtaining of fresh pole by beekeepers. In addition, the community of beekeepers of Bolivar must compete with products with functional and eco-friendly characteristics required by the current market. This leads us to postulate this proposal, in which through a biotechnological process such as fermentation, the pollen produced in the Montes de María de Bolívar. To this, this proposal has a team of professionals and transdisciplinary students, with profiles in Biological Engineering, Food Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Microbiology, Good, Biology and Ecology, who will serve as a bridge between the knowledge and the value of the autoctano products of the region. As part of the proposal, we expect to use the first layer of the The research group " Borders in the movement ", " Studies of the socio-cultural diversity of the great Caribbean " (UNAL de La Paz), " Biodiversity research group for society " (UNAL de La Paz), " Bacteria acidic lactica " research group and its biotechnology and industrial applications (UNAL de Palmira), " Group for the Ecology and Conservation of Wildlife " (UNAL Medellín) and " Group for Research " " " Ensuring food quality and development of new products " (Institute of Food Science and Technology - ICTA). In addition, it is expected that the results obtained in the laboratory classroom will be transmitted to the beneficiary community, while this community of beekeepers who are victims of the conflict will participate in the exchange of knowledge that will allow the inclusion of ancestral knowledge of material and immaterial practices linked to the production of apiculture pollen
