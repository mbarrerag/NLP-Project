# Applicability of precast plastic wood in construction, as a sustainable and efficient system in Bogotá replacing standard systems

This project aims to address two significant environmental challenges affecting both the planet in general and Colombia in particular, the first refers to the pollution resulting from the manufacture of bricks based on clay while the second is related to the accumulation of plastic waste from the post-consumption of the population that remains in the environment for long periods of time, due to its slow degradation. In order to address this challenge, this research proposes to improve and optimize theoretically the structure of the prefabricated panel that has been developed by the company EcoCocon located in Bratislava, Slovakia, implementing innovative and ecological elements such as plastic wood incorporating high density polyethylene composite materials as matrix and wood and glass fibers as reinforcement, replacing the natural wood with which the panels are elaborated, offering a particular plan for the structure of the proposed panel, with the aim of finding the best in terms of the structure of the proposed panel. tislava, Slovakia, implementing innovative and ecological elements such as plastic wood that incorporates composite materials. high-density polyethylene as a matrix and wood and glass fibers as reinforcement, addressing the natural wood with which the panels are made, offering a sustainable and efficient alternative.
