# SMALL CONCRETE. TECHNICAL AND TECHNOLOGY OF THE CONCRETE REFORMED WITH VEGETAL FIBRES

The work now in its hands has an essential meaning: to invite every possible reader, beyond whether or not he possesses previous knowledge of the topics dealt with, to enter into the understanding of the aspects of life in society, within the framework of the analysis of the forms of human life, of coexistence in the urban area, of its identification and configuration in the territorial area, of aspects related to the construction and configuration of its habitat, of the historical dynamics, as well as of the cultural expressions and manifestations inherent in this coexistence. Therefore, significant reflections and experiences of academic experts who, from the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Medellín, have concentrated on the analysis of the complex and diverse problems of human life in the field of architecture, of the urban-territorial, of the material construction as a reflection of the cultural, and of human expressions in the field of the social and the arts, as a fundamental and necessary contribution to the understanding of the realities that we share
