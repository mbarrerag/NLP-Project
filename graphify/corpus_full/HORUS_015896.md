# Evaluation of the biotic integrity of the community of periphytic algae of the Shoesa swamp, department of Cesar and Magdalena, Colombia.

The microalgae community is a group of organisms belonging to the trophic step of the producers, which have been used as bioindicators since the nineteenth century. This community is characterized by its rapid reproduction, short life cycle and easy collection methods, and although taxonomically it is a complicated group to manage, other attributes have been evaluated, such as morphological measures and functional features, which allow for a rapid and efficient assessment of these organisms and give solution to the possible disadvantages due to the lack of expertise in taxonomic aspects. In this work, a tool of assessment of the ecosystem health, based on the composition and biological and ecological characteristics of the periphytic algae community, was developed to determine its biotic integrity and to evaluate the ecological status of the Cenagoso Shoesa Complex (CCZ). To this, the composition of the community of the algases and the results were analyzed, the analysis of the algaematics in the CZ was carried out
