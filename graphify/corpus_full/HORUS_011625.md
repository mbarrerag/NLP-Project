# New forms of rationalization

Here is the synthesis of this method: to investigate and foresee for a given region and for every moment of a near future, taking into account the purchasing capacity of each category of buyers; to establish on these bases a corresponding manufacturing programme, as simple, as standardized and as effective as possible; to put it into operation with the most technical and rewarding use of the means available to it; to analyze whether it is necessary to re-establish its amortization for a period that depends on the further development of the company; to harmonize the immediate manufacturing programme with the methodical forecast of this development;
