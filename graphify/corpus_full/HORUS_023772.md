# Analysis of biological relations in the hemoparasital-chelonian model in Colombia.

Some genera of parasites of the suborder Adeleorina infect usually intermediate hosts such as turtles. However, the knowledge that is available about the species of parasites that infect these hosts is scarce. In the literature, for the identification of parasites of this suborder, morphological tools, morphometric and molecular techniques are used; mainly information of the gene 18S rDNA. However, the use of this gene has several deficiencies. To increase the knowledge of parasites that infect Quelonios, as well as in molecular techniques for research in diversity, this study had as objectives: 1) to identify the genetic diversity of lineages of parasites isolated by amplification different genes, 2) to integrate lines of evidence both morphological and molecular for the identification of units of unknown evolutionary linaries and 3) to design alternative tools for the diagnosis and identification of these parasites (by implementing new molecular markers that have been poorly explored in organisms of this suborder. To achieve these objectives, blood samples were obtained and analyzed from 16 of the 29 species of continental turtles of the Colombia. All samples were analyzed by molecular methods and hepanomics. Additionally, the results also showed several processes such as heteroplasmy and/or cryptic speciation, are affecting the identification and characterization of the diversity of parasites. This study constitutes the beginning of an interesting and important area of research
