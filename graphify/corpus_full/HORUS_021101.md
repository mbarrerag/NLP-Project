# Estimation and comparison of atmospheric emissions of three propulsion technologies on a freight train. Case study Corridor Chiriguana-Santa Marta

The poor quality of air and the anthropogenic emissions of greenhouse gases in most countries are attributed mainly to the activity of transport of cargo and passengers (OECD, 2022). In Colombia, the main mode of transport for the mobilization of cargo is the roadside, however, the national and international transport policies will encourage intermodality in search of efficiency, productivity and decrease of negative impacts (CONPES 3982,2020). Some studies show that the activation of the ferret corridors and increase the volume of cargo transported by this way would imply not only a reduction of costs in the transport of goods within the country, but a decrease of the congestion, the traffic sinisterality and the atmospheric emissions (Duque, 2020).The Colombian government, in its Master Plan of Intermodal Transport bets on an intermodal infrastructure network supporting the investments in the ferrero and fluvial transport (the country has been important to connect the region with the center of the Colombian economy, 2015).In addition, some of the most important marine ports are in the Caribbean region, e.g., ports of Santa Marta, Cartagena and Barranquila, what makes the connection of this country is the country with the country's. The technology used for the operation of rail transport is diesel powered locomotives, responsible for considerable greenhouse gas emissions and pollutants. In the framework of the above, to support with scientific evidence the transformation goals of transport in Colombia, this research project proposes the evaluation of diesel trains, electric trains and hydrogen trains for cargo transport in Colombia, considering the mix of traction using the methodology of life cycle analysis in the Chiriguana-Santa Marta ferret corridor. The application of this methodology will allow the estimation of pollutant emissions in a comprehensive and systemic manner, from the obtaining of the raw material to the operation for the provision of the transport service. Once the emissions for each of the three technologies are determined, these will be compared and the advantages and disadvantages of each technology will be discussed in terms of the potential of global warming and emissions of pollutants criteria. Finally, it is expected that the methodology used in this research project will be used as input to be replicated in the ferrous corridors of the rest of the country and Latin America
