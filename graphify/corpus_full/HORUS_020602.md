# The place of housing in the realization of the dignified life of families. Approaches to the imaginary housing of a group of women victims of forced displacement through their residential life paths

This research aims to understand the social imaginarys on the housing of five women victims of forced displacement who have access to the housing subsidy 100% subsidized by the State in the residential complex Plaza de la Hoja, located in the city of Bogotá, as part of the social commitment that the State has acquired to the victims of sociopolitical violence of the country. To achieve this objective, I supported the social imaginary, the epistemological orientations of social constructionism and the methodological strategies of the qualitative approach. With this foundation and from techniques such as the in-depth interview, “the notebook of my homes” and the drawing, the trajectories of residential life of the protagonists of this thesis were recovered, placing emphasis on the most significant housing for them. In the exercise of the voice of the women interviewed for this research and with this we were able to go disentrating the imaginary housing situations and the territory., and the methodological strategies of the qualitative approach. Based on techniques such as in-depth interviews, "my Housing Notebook," and drawing, the residential life costumes of the protagonists of this these were collected, imphasizing the most significant staying for them. In the exercise, I priorised the voices of the women discussed for this research, and with this, we were able to unravel the housing imaginations. Agreeing to the analysis of the women's stories, the import of experiences and emotions in the elaboration of housing examples was identified, as well as their relationship with family dynamics and the interactions of women and their neighbouring economy
