# Alternative financial indicator for the measurement of the effectivity rate: case of banking in Colombia

This paper proposes a financial indicator that allows to measure the cash flow in an alternative way to EBITDA for the Banking Establishments of Colombia. Within the writing, theoretical positions are presented that explain the development of EBITDA, highlighting the achievement of its results in the massification of its use and the criticisms that it has received in its interpretation over time. Next, in the document, the particularities of the financial indicators are conceptualized as, the cash flows and the working capital. In addition, the research carried out the calculation of the proposed indicator (hereinafter referred to as adjusted EBITDA) from the figures reported, together with EBITDA and the net cash flow of operations during the period 2010-2016 for the Colombian financial sector, counting with a sample formed by 17 banking establishments.
