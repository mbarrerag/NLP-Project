# TRACING MAGMATIC FOOTPRINTS: INFLUENCE OF A CO2-RICH MELT ON THE MINERAL ASSEMBLAGE OF THE SAN JOSE DEL GUAVIARE SYENITE, SE COLOMBIA

Tracing the magmatic traces: Influence of CO2-rich fluids in the mineral composition of the Sienite of San José del Guaviare, SE Colombia. This contribution presents a combined study of optical and electronic microscopy (SEM, EDS and EMPA) in ten samples of rock of the Sienite unit of San José del Guaviare (SJGS), of proterozoic age, which aflora in southeastern Colombia. Accessory minerals and trace were used to interpret the magmatic evolution of these rocks subsaturated in silica. The main mineral constituents of the sienites analyzed are nefeline, clinopyraxene rich in Fe, amphibol, feldespato alkalino and biotita. The minor minerals correspond to titanite, calcite, cancrilite and sodalite. The minerals trace are also those rich in niobium or rare lands, such as pyrochlorite, echite, echnutri,, ancillite and wöhlite. In Fe, it reveals a decrease in the influence of CO2 and temperature. Possibly the transformation of orthoclase into microclin occurred simultaneously. Pertititic microclinus, as a second generation of potassium feldspar, indicates a slow cooling from high temperatures. The growth of secondary cancrinite, of Mr-Mn and ancillite-Ce carbonates, suggests a late stage of hydrothermal-metasomatic processes rich in CO2. The composition of primary calcite and apatite, crystallized early, implies that its origin is most likely related to residues of a carbonatritic cast derived from the mantle, either segregated early or formed independently, compared to an origin from the crystallization of a synic cast rich in CO2 or of molten cortical carbonates. The presence, therefore, of a carbonattytic cast at an early magmatic stage seems the most plausible alternative for the SJGS rocks. key words: colombia, amazonic cratón, sienita de san josé, si mineral del guareitas
