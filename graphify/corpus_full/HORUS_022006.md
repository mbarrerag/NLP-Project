# Characterization of the structure of two orchid communities in two Andean forests of the Alto del Tequendama region (Parque Natural Chicaque and Parque Los Tunos).

In Colombia orchids are of interest for conservation due to their beauty, diversity and endemism; many of them are threatened by the destruction of their habitat, their reduced distribution and the traffic of species for commercialization.However, their protection is not easy because of the lack of information about their distribution, their habitat and the forest communities where it is present. Likewise, the high Andean forests, where it seems to inhabit 87% of the orchids of the country, are in the most populated area of the country which is subjected to multiple pressures antropic by the expansion of the agricultural border and a continuous transformation of the natural ecosystems, which shows the conflicts of use of the soil and the degradation of the latter by the erosion that it requires to be evaluated and monitored, with a view to protecting their biodiversity and ecosystem services that support the well-being of the Colombians (in this order of ideas, the present work seeks to contrast the communities of orchids present in two near high Andean forests (Los Tunos and Chica) and to characterize their ecological distribution, with the aim of bringing to the knowledge of the communities of the orchids and to the decisions and to be studied
