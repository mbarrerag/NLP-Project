# ACTIPACK as a technological platform in active packaging for agro-idustria

In the second half of 2019 Agrobol S.A. and the National University of Colombia through the DIEB and the laboratory of Quality and Poscosecha of Agricultural Products (LCCPA) of the Faculty of Agrarian Sciences signed an agreement of cooperation framed in the project _x0093_Development of a functional packaging to preserve gulupa in fresh_x0094_ where the LCPPA is committed in the beginning of the development of the project called FASE I to the determination of kinetic models of breathing and transpiration of fruit of gulupa (Passiflora edulis Sims) of three different states of maturity in function of the ambient temperature and relative humidity. Subsequently, the aforementioned kinetic models have been obtained, fulfilling this form with the commitments of the team of polling during the FASS I., and satisfactory packaging material for the best preservation of fruit of gulupa with export purposes
