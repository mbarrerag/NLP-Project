# There is no way: mobility between the hunter-gatherers groups of the Magdalena Medio Valley in the Preceramic period. An approach from the simulation of routes. Municipalities of Barranca, Yondó and San Vicente de Chucurí

Through an interdisciplinary approach combining archaeological data and modelling techniques and spatial analysis, this study seeks to understand the configuration of mobility of hunter-collector groups that inhabited during the preceramic period the middle basin of the Magdalena river, in the current municipalities of Barrancabermeja, Yondó and San Vicente de Chucurí. Through the simulation of routes using the Optimal Displacement Accumulation Model (MADO) and the analysis of spatial autocorrelation, it was possible to visualize a network of transitability that connects archaeological sites, suggesting a planning in the choice of places for camps and a network of possible paths that structured their mobility. The results indicate a predominantly logistical mobility, with the possible combination of residential strategies, reflecting a complex interaction with the environment and a transformation of the landscape over time. .
