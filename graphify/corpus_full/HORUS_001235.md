# Alternative clinical management of fluorosis patients: Case report

The minimally invasive treatments for dental fluorosis lesions (TF3 onwards) generally use acids and oxidants that affect the structure, composition and properties of dental enamel. Thus, the objective of this case report is to present the clinical results and color changes of an alternative treatment that includes first remineralization and then microabrasion. The clinical history, visual and instrumental colouring (11 and 21) was developed with VITA Easyshade Advance 4.0 spectrophotometer and photographic recording before and after each treatment. The diagnosis was fluorosis TF3 (12, 13, 22 and 23) and TF6 (11 and 21). Subsequently, the treatment contemplated two phases: remineralization (phase 1) and microabrasion (phase 2). In the results of phase 1 there were no clinical changes in color; the brightness increased, the size of the discolorations and pores decreased, and also the color differences (phase 1) in clinical, a* and b* increased.
