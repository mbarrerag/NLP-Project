# Dance to be a native forest

Dance to be a native forest collects the process of consciousness of a teacher-daughter during her transition through the Master's Degree in Artistic Education of the National University of Colombia. She has been living for years in private schools in the city of Bogotá as a dance teacher of the preschool and primary sections. This transition through the Master's degree has proposed her to explore in her past and present, to discover and remember issues that have built her and build her as a human being and as a teacher-daughter. She reconstructs her archive and her classroom, studies them, reviews them over and over again, shares them with artists, teachers-artists and with artists-teachers, also with fellow workers, friends and family members. This dialogue with materials and people, a sustained observation and a sentipensive listening revealed issues that parted from the personal (micro) to the collective (macro). This immersion allows her to establish relationships between The Monoculture - The School and between The Native Forest - The Revelation of Being through the dance device. teacher-artists and artist-teachers, as well as co-workers, friends and family.
