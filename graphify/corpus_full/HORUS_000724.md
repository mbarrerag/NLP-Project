# Process of “replacement” of coca leaf by cardamom in the municipality of Suárez (Cauca). A case study

The fight against illicit coca cultivation has involved in Colombia large economic, social and environmental costs for the country. The strategy of substitution of coca by legal crops has not had the success that has been given in other producer countries such as Ecuador, Bolivia and Peru, where it has been achieved to replace coca by coffee, cocoa and pineapple, among others. The above mentioned countries have advanced towards legalization of coca leaf to produce derivatives such as beers, energizing beverages, biscuits, among others; something that in Colombia is still under discussion. Coca in Colombia is grown in more than 300 municipalities; and one of them is Suárez, a multi-ethnic municipality located in the north of Cauca. This municipality, autonomously, has initiated a process of substitution of coca cultivation by cardamom beginning the decade of the year 2000. The present study aims to analyze the process of “substitution” of coca cultivation by the coca cultivation line, which could be replaced by the cultivation of cardamom in the municipality of Suárez in the department of Cauca. The methodology followed was descriptive, quantitative and qualitative, which compares the processes of substitution in Bolivia, Peru, Ecuador and Colombia.
