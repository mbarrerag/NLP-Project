# POSITIONS OF SOCIAL ACTORS AND TERRITORIAL TRANSFORMATIONS OF TOURISM IN SANTA FEET OF ANTIOCIA

The territories that have been built as tourist destinations constantly undergo transformations that favor the implementation of the vision and image of this particular phenomenon. Such situation causes the social actors involved to also take different positions regarding tourism, either in their favor or against it. This article aims to analyze the positions of the social actors involved in the tourism of Santa Fe de Antioquia (Colombia), as well as their relationship and organization to oppose or adapt to these tourist dynamics. For this purpose, a qualitative methodology is used, which takes techniques such as surveys and social network analysis to identify discourses, imaginary, relations and actions of social actors in the territory. It is concluded that social actors in Santa Fe de Antioquia, despite showing various negative characteristics of tourism in the municipality, present a positive position in the face of this phenomenon, widely accept it and adapt to the new transformations that tourism creates in their territory. This can be interpreted as a sample of the incorporation of the state developmental discourse and economic actors on tourism
