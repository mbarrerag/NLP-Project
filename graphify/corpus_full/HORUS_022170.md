# Characterization of older adults in a highly complex post-acute care unit after being hospitalized by COVID-19 in the city of Bogotá.

the pandemic caused by the VOCID-19 caused by SARS-CoV-2 affected the elderly in a particular way, given their vulnerability and high disease burden, which generated a significant impact in terms of morbidity, mortality and sequelae in this population group. to characterize clinical and sociodemographically the elderly population hospitalized in a center of high post-acute complexity after being hospitalized by the VOCI-19. Patients and methods: retrospective and descriptive observational study. A descriptive analysis of the sociodemographic and clinical variables of the hospitalized patients aged 60 years or more was performed in the Clinic Society of Professional Nurses with confirmed diagnosis of VOCID-19 between January 1 2020 and December 31, 2021. a total of 41 patients aged 60 years and older was included. 65% were men, with an average age of 69.6 years. 68% had cardiovascular disease, 41% had pulmonary disease and 34% had type 2 diabetes Mellitus.
