# Chejeru: The Piaroa Woman and her Importance in the Protection of the Forest of Matavén

The project entitled "Chejeru: La Mujer Piaroa and its Importance in the Protection of the Protection of the Forest of Matavén" addresses the fundamental role of women in societies, in particular in the Piaroa indigenous community of Sarrapia, located in the Middle Orinoco region. This work highlights the crucial influence of women in various areas, such as the economy, education and culture, recognizing that their participation varies according to geographical, historical, cultural and economic factors. The term "Chejeru", which means "manager of life" in their language, emphasizes the responsibility of Piaroa women in the care of ecosystems and the protection of their environment. It is emphasized that their actions are oriented towards the collective and the common good, being essential in the current context of climate crisis. By valuing and promoting the principles of care and empathy, the Piaroa women becomes an engine of change for the protection of the environment.
