# Demolishing to densify? Chronicles of the transformation of Bogotá, 2008-2022

The city is transformed and has done it throughout its history. In fact, right now it is being transformed and will continue to do it in the future. We stop at this Bogotá and examine one of the most recent mechanisms of its mutation. The lot-to-lot transformation has been the way in which the urban form has been historically changed. House by house, building by building have been demolished to give rise to new constructions. But how this phenomenon is expressed at present, where it is presented with the most intensity, who participate and what its interests are, and what consequences it is generating. These are some of the issues that we seek to clarify with the present research. To achieve this, this thesis is appropriated from the most relevant elements of the chronic as the narrative gender of the city and its alteration. Analyzing from a relational perspective the change of the urban morphology changes through the process of batch-to-lot transformation in Bogotá, in its most recent period it is the focus of attention. To do this, we therefore, we collect the notions that the demolition and densification of the city has been elaborated and how these processes of change of the city have been related to the lot to the urban morphs, The aim is to be effective, the latter is to ensure that the results of the review are consistent with the results of the review. This work is the product of a personal experience and therefore it is an invitation to understand the change of Bogotá from the experience of moving through it. But, above all, this work is an exhortation for us to take charge of the built city, to understand its modification, and change the way it is transformed in search of a better future
