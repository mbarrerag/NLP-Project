# Determination of SAG milling kinetics considering the rheological influence of fine in pulp

The study presents the experimental results obtained at laboratory scale, where under controlled semi-autogenic molyminate SAG, a siliceous mena mono-size was milled. 1.25 cm was added to the study. A pulp prepared from the same material with different fine concentrations below 200 mesh size was added to the same material under the same controlled phase. The research found the influence on the kinetics of fracture of the coarse particles under the premise that the presence of finos in a pulp modifies the reologic behaviour of a initially Newtonian fluid as water to a non-Newtonian one as shown by the mineral pulps. The results obtained show a materially proportional relationship between the presence of finos and the specific fracture rates of the coarse particles.
