# Methodology for measuring hydration of cement added with fly ash from electrical impedance.

This research work focuses on the study of the use and behavior of the electrodes printed in 3D SPE for its implementation in the evaluation of the properties of cementing materials, being the main objective of the work to identify the different phases and processes product of the hydration of the cement pastes when they contain additions of fly ash within their composition. This has led to the fact that during the last decades different methodologies have been developed that allow their measurement mainly through somewhat complex laboratory tests. For this reason, in this document the study of the electrodes SPE is performed polypropylene base, which as it can be observed in chapters 4 and 5 have an adequate behavior that allowed to be carried out the measurements of electrical resistivity throughout its initial phase of hydration, being able to identify from the data obtained during the first 24 hours of measurement, the critical changes of the curve are produced when the main phases of the process are performed. was calibrated and verified according to an adiabatic calorimeter of the commercial reference I-CAL 8000, and obtained good results in the developed samples. Finally, from the results achieved, a methodology is defined which allows from the measurements of electrical resistivity by using electrodes SPE base polypropylene, the definition of linear models with which the initial and final forge times are identified, as well as the times where the loss of manageability of the sample begins. Further identifying the different phases of hydration of cement pastes according to the physicochemical processes developed in each of these, as well as the correlation between these measurements and the evolution of the accumulated hydration heat of the samples from bilinear models
