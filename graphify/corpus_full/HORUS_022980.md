# MAJOR ALKALOIDS FROM PHLEGMARIURUS KILLIPII (HERTER) B. OLLG.(LYCOPODIACEAE) NATIVE OF SANTANDER (COLOMBIA).

Phegmariurus killipii (Herter) B.Øllg (Basionym Urostachis killipii Herter [1]) is an specie belonging to class Equisetopsida, order Lycopodiales, family Lycopodiaceae, sub-family Huperzioideae-2012, which is growing on trees in surroundings of ¿Cascada de los Caballeros¿ (Municipio de Suaita, Departamento de Santander, Colombia S.A.)[2]. A sample of whole plants (25 g collected in 2017), dried and ground were extracted with EtOH, concentrated and separated by acid'base extraction with aqueous HCl. Then Et2O was added, washed, basified with NH4OH and the alkaloid fraction with CHCl3 was extracted. Its TLC analysis with Dragendorff's reagent showed at least five mayor bands of alkaloid derivatives. A Sample by HPLC-MS was analyzed with the following chromatographic conditions: column Terra® MSC18 (2.1 x 100 mm; 3.5 μm) flux: 0.3mL/min, T°= 20°C. Gradient: (H2O-0.1% AF: CH3CN (98%-2%) to (5%-95%). Firstly as result, search of the TICs for the following masses 243,26 and 245,15 indicate total absence of huperzine A and dihydro-huperzine A, with similar condition for licocarinatine [(m/z +H]= 476.26). On the other hand, it careful analysis led to confirm the presence of the following more abundant and known constituents: licodoline: 264.26 = [C16H26NO2 +H]+; 12 epi-licodine-N-oxide 280.22= [C16H24NO3+H]+; licoposerramine K:262.21[C16H24NO2+H]+ and phlegmariurine: 262.12 [C16H24NO2+H]+among others [3]. This work will discuss these new results and it is the first communication about this native species
