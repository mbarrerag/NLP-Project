# Spatial syntax: Methodology for the prevention of COVID 19 in educational institutions of the Urabá Antioqueño.

The concept of Spatial Syntaxis was conceived by Bernard Hillier and Julianne Hanson as a tool for architects to simulate the possible sociocultural effects of their designs and make decisions based on behavioral predictions of the population. The spatial syntax consists in the study of the forms in which they are linked and organize the spaces of an architectural group, trying to infer those aspects of the social structuring that could influence their design and also allowing to analyze the possibilities, restrictions or transformations of the space from the uses or social appropriations. In other words, the spatial syntax allows to represent the hierarchy of the spaces of a given building, characterizing the relationship between spatialities and performing simulations on the social behavior from convergence maps, accessibility graphs and the visual graphics. Taking as a starting point the spatial syntax and the spatial syntax of the given in Colombia, addressing the problem of contagion and the previous design. The application of an alternative model allowed not only the fulfilment of the objectives, but also the training in student research, the opening of classes open to all audiences, the consolidation of alliances with communities and actors in the territory, among others. This presentation then aims to expose a pedagogical process derived from the execution of a research project and extension solidarity of the National University of Colombia as an experience of teaching new methods to investigate, think and reflect on space and architecture, as well as mechanisms that allow the application of theories for the search of pragmatic solutions to complex problems in our country
