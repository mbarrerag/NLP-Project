# SOME COMBINATORIAL IDENTITIES INVOLVING q-HARMONIC NUMBERS AND GENERALIZED q-HARMONIC NUMBERS

In this note, we give a continued fraction expansion of the generating function of q-harmonic numbers. We also give new generalizations of some finite summation identities of generalized q-harmonic numbers. Then, we derive some interesting particular cases
