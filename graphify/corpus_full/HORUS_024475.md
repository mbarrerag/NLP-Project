# CONSIDERATIONS FOR THE SAFE REAPPERATION OF SCHOOLS AND COLEGES DURING PENDEMIA COVID 19.

This quick synthesis does not contain recommendations, it only mobilizes evidence of global and local research on a question presented by decision makers. Questions In this synthesis five groups of questions will be addressed: 1. What is known about the impact of the opening of schools on the transmission of SARS-CoV-2 and its variants, both in schools and in the community? How the degree of transmission and control of the infection by SARS-CoV-2 and its variants in communities impact their behavior within the school? 2. What is known about the effectiveness of the measures of opening or closing schools, adopted by governments, according to the conditions of transmission of SARS-CoV-2 and its variants? 3. What are the self-care and organizational/structural measures2 necessary in the school environment (primary and secondary) to ensure a safe face-to-face education for the school population (children, girls and adolescents, teachers, other institutional staff) in the framework of the COVID-19 pandemic? What impact does the vaccination of teachers have in the context of a safe opening of schools to avoid the transmission of SARS-CoV-2? 4.
