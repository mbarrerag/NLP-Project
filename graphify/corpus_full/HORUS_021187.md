# The evolutionary perspective applied to the debate on animal cognition and moral disagreement

The project aims to contribute to the training of students in two topics in which the research activity of the principal researcher is focused; 1. animal cognition and its impact on the discussion on animal rights; and 2. moral judgment conceived as a specifically human capacity and as susceptible to generating moral dilemmas, where disagreement is inevitable. Work with students working on theme 1. includes advice on the assimilation and understanding of relevant studies and the direction of master's thesis and final grade work. Individual meetings and group work are contemplated in the context of the seedbed "Evolution and Cognition." Given the immense thematic dispersion of animal cognition studies, in the project we focus mainly on the work that has been done on social cognition and the possible attribution of mental states in primates. With students interested in moral judgment we will study the epistemological and/or meta-ethical aspects of the recognition of the existence of moral dilemmas. We will have group meetings to discuss relevant studies and take advantage of the possible post-graduate seminars, since they all belong to the post-graduate work in philosophy.
