# METHODOLOGIES FOR THE ASSESSMENT OF MASS MOVEMENT THREAT AS PART OF BASIC THREAT STUDIES: CASE OF MunicipAL STUDIES OF ANDES, ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA

The basic studies of susceptibility and threat due to the occurrence of mass movements are a fundamental element for updating the planning plans of the municipalities of the Colombian territory. Given the above, Law 1523 of 2012 establishes the framework policy, and Decree 1807 of 2014, compiled in 1077 of 2015, establishes the technical guidelines that such studies must follow and the minimum conditions to be met. For this reason, a selection of some methodologies recognized in the literature was made, which, being adequate and validated according to the conditions specific to each municipality, can be used for the conduct of such studies, be for both the rural area and for urban and expansion soil, as for each of the factors that can detonate these events
