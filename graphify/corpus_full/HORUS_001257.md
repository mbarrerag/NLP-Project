# Integrated management and use of solid waste in the Metropolitan Areas of the Aburra Valley and Barranquilla (2006-2022)

Analysis of the critical factors in the use that allow to understand the lack of consolidation of the Integrated Management of Solid Wastes (GIRS) in both cases of study between 2006 and 2022, with a focus on sustainability and circular economy. Through a comparative methodology, the normative, cartography and planning instruments are examined, observing social, economic, environmental and institutional variables that affect their systems of GIRS. Problems were identified as the disarticulation of processes and the lack of normative integration, which hinders value chains, the circular economy and the reintegration of materials into the productive cycle. In the AMVA, the capacity of the infrastructure of exploitation and of final disposition is exploited, and the integrality of the management of RS in the planning instruments. A sustainable management requires to integrate the dimensions of the sustainability to consolidate the circular economy (in particular considering the organisation of the population, the informal management and the final disposition of the economy). In the Barranquilla Metropolitan Area only 5% to 10% of solid waste is recovered, mainly due to the low citizen awareness, weak recycling organization, and high operational transportation costs. In both cases, the capacity of recovery and dispositive infrastructure, as well as the understanding of the recovery management in planning instruments, are problematic. A sustainable ISWM requirement integrating sustainability dimensions to strengthen the circular economy, particularly by considering the organization of the recycling population, informality in the waste recovery market, the relationship between infrastructure and territory, and the coordination and competence strategy and urban development
