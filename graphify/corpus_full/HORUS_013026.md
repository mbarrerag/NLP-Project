# Speech of identity in the lyric of the son jarocho: study of six record productions representative of the Jaranero movement, made in Mexico City between 1980 and 2000

This thesis studies the lyric of son jarocho, traditional music of the state of Veracruz, Mexico. A selection of six discographic productions from the perspective of identity discourse, considering that it is in the collection of coplas where a point of union with its past is maintained, the new proposals and models that will serve as the basis for the recreating the imaginary of its poetic. The selection is from the first record productions of: Mono Blanco, Zacamandu, Los Utrera, Son de Madera, Chuchumbé, Cojolites. Groups representative of the so-called Jaranero movement, cultural phenomenon of revival that marks this musical tradition around the years 1980 and the year 2000. .
