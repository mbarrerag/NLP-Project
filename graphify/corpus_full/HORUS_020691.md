# SENTI-PENSING CITY. KNOWLEDGE AND EMOTIONS RELATED TO THE PAUTONAL ACCIDENTALITY. FORMULATION OF A PROBLEM OF URBAN PAULA-ANDREA NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY SCHOOL. MANIZALS (COLOMBIA) SCHOOL OF ARCHITECTURE AND URBANISM, ARCHITECTURE PROGRAMME __KEEP0_

To mitigate the accidentality in our Latin American cities is a key factor for urban designers and planners to include in their proposals various approaches and studies that allow us to understand the behaviors of people in the topic. To avoid the cognitive-emotional mechanisms of the different road actors in the public space contributes to the formulation of design strategies to configure safe environments that vindicate the action of walking for contemporary citizens. In order to reduce the accidentality in the city of Manizales, Colombia, a study is carried out aimed at analyzing how the emotions and knowledge of the road actors contribute to changing their interaction with the city. The methodological horizon of the study is qualitative and participatory, supported by guidelines of the design for the change of behavior, emotional design and participatory design (DP), with the aim of delineating the project route to respond to the problem of accidentiness of the city. The results show limited knowledge of pedestrians and drivers related to road standard and dissimilar emotions among these road actors. The conclusions of the study allow project to project a problem focused on developing strategies of DP that synchronizes emotions between drivers
