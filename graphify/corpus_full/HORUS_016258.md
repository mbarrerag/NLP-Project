# Contribution to the anomalous magnetic moment of the non-universal U (1)X muon extension.

The abnormal magnetic moment of the muon is a quantity that has been measured with high precision and depends on fundamental parameters of particle physics that allow testing the accuracy of the Standard Model (SM). The Muon g − 2 experiment carried out by the Fermilab laboratory found a difference of 4.2 μ from the theoretical value of the SM. This result opens the door to considering physics beyond the SM. Therefore, the non-universal model U(1)X can explain the distance between these two results. In the framework of this theory, additional diagrams of Feynman to 1-loop were studied that contribute to the g −2 of the muon. In this sense, it was taken into account that the model provides a different set of Yukawa acoples when considering the masses of neutrinos in Normal Ordamentation or Inverse Ordenation. When performing an analysis of Montecarlo's parameters related to radiative corrections, it was found that the resulting additional contribution of these diagrams adjusts the theoretical value within the uncertainty of the experimental average
