# Au) Use of the software DESWIK® in a deposit polimetalico of Cu and Au

This article seeks to represent the step by step to obtain the economic valuation and the scheduling of a mining project, based on a hypothetical polymetallic deposit of Cu and Au, by applying the concepts of equivalent law to express the presence of the different metals in terms of a single mineral, the critical and marginal cut-off grade to define the economically viable limits of mining and the implementation of the DESWIK software package
