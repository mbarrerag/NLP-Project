# EXPERIMENTAL ASSESSMENT OF THE MECHANICAL COMPORTMENT OF A CILINDRIC ADHESIVE UNION UNDER PURATIONAL TORSION USED BY THE MODEL OF RAMBERG-OSGOOD

In this work, the experimental evaluation of the mechanical behaviour of an anaerobic adhesive bond of cylindrical geometry is carried out, consisting of a bearing ring and a shaft, in a concentric assembly, which is subjected to a pure torsion load. By a 2k experiment design, the maximum and minimum condition of the geometric parameters of the bond is compared, length of overlap (between 12-16mm), adhesive thickness (between 0.1 and 0.3 mm diametric) and surface roughness of the substrates (between 0.7 and 1.5), obtaining correlations between some of the parametric variables influencing the mechanical strength of the adhesive bond. Ramberg-Osgood model was applied in the evaluation of the mechanical behavior of the adhesive bond, using the information reported in the experimental stress-formation curves obtained during the torsion tests. The results showed that the average coefficients of hardening by plastic strain (?) were higher for the maximum design condition, while the average rigidity in the elastic zone was greater for the minimum design condition of the joint, a situation that is possibly attributed to a higher degree of uniform energy intensity
