# Design of a compact drinking water treatment system of 1 liter per second to meet the demand of the company Sativa Nativa operating in the town of Curvalito located in the city of Santa Marta

The town of Curvalito faces a critical challenge in terms of water supply to make it more efficient. Contrary to the management carried out by the authorities and responsible companies, the community still faces serious deficiencies in the availability of drinking water, which negatively affects the living conditions of its inhabitants, in addition to hindering the economic development of the region. This is how this locality is characterized by a low flow rate, which prevents satisfying the demand of the local population. This limitation in the quantity of water treated not only affects the daily supply of the inhabitants, but also leads to problems of health and sanitation. In response to the problem raised, it is imperative to develop a water treatment system for human consumption that guarantees the supply of safe and sufficient water to satisfy the needs of the NATIVE SATIVE COMPANY in the rural area of Curvalito. The design of this system should consider the specificities of the local water supply, including the climatic conditions and local resources. It is also crucial to ensure the environmental, economic and social sustainability of the system to ensure its long-term viability. It is important that research is focused on evaluating the most appropriate technologies and methodologies for the water treatment in the required quantity, considering the local water supply and the safe water supply.
