# Proposal for mitigation to the variability and climate change of an urban-rural basin through analysis of extreme storms and sustainable urban drainage systems SUDS. Pilot case high and medium part of the HighVista ravine.

This project aims to make a basin-scale analysis of temporal and spatial variability of storms in a mountainous basin of tropical Andes, specifically in the area of influence of the HighVista ravine as a pilot case applicable to the entire metropolitan area as a fundamental input for understanding urban drainage in the face of the effects of variability and climate change. From the project, results are expected to be obtained on variations in intensity, duration, frequency, reliability and other characteristic aspects of storms in tropical areas such as Medellín in the face of a climate change environment. From these results strategies will be defined for mitigation of effects in the face of the increasing and flooding generated by such storms from solutions based on nature and sustainable drainage systems (SUDS, LIDS) using conceptual and numerical modelling. To validate the proposal, the results will be contrasted with the current situation and potential of the basin of the HighVista, middle and high-level slump. In this part, the participation of the community that will be represented by the Aqua Association of Direct Influence in the project area will be verified and recommended to the best actions and systems to be implemented as a tool for the increased implementation of the urban basin
