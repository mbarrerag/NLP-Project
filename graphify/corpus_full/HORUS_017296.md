# A TRANSGREEN LEGY: BELL HOOKS AND THE PEDAGOGIES OF RADICAL LOVE

The African-American pedagogue bell hooks generated a legacy of great importance for feminist and anti-racist struggles around the world. In this article I pay tribute to her life and her ideas on education. Through the documentary review of her pedagogical trilogy, elaborated between 1994 and 2010, I categorize three core concepts, namely: engaged pedagogy, love as critical emotional praxis and spirituality as the end of education. From the mapping of her contributions, I maintain that her transgressor legacy invites us to work on emotions, radical love and healing as unavoidable elements of decolonizing educational practice. Finally, I emphasize that the recovery of the bell hooks legacy constitutes an act of resistance to the practices of suppressing black feminist thinking in the educational field
