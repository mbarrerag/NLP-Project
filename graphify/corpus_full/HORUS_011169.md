# EPIDEMIOLOGICAL INDICATORS FOR THE USE OF MARIHUANA IN COLOMBIA POPULATION

In some parts of the world the prevalence of cannabis use has increased up to four times in the last 24 years, although the percentage of adolescents who perceive this drug as harmful has decreased by up to 40% despite the evidence that its use is associated with a series of health damage and other effects, especially among habitual users.In terms of the Americas, cannabis use in the general population and among secondary school students is increasing in most countries that have data on trends. Changes in legislation and regulations on marijuana have continued to be unchanged throughout the hemisphere and the decriminalization of marijuana for recreational or medicinal purposes has led to greater access for adults.
