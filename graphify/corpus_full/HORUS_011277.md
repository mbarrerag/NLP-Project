# CONVERSATION COMPETITION IN FRENCH LANGUAGE TO ANALYSATE ACTS IN INTERCULTURE COMMUNICATIONS

This research points to differences in patterns of linguistic action, and the effect of intercultural communication through lingua franca conversations. This is to analyze speech acts given in social interactions between Chinese and Colombian members. Therefore, an analytical methodology was used on conversation in which a day was selected between a Chinese teacher and a Colombian teacher, who interacted in English.The results show the overcoming of verbal obstacles between both participants, who took into account cultural dimensions, as well as signs of gestual and body language to solve any present difficulty in relation to verbal language.However, the ignorance of linguistic norms with respect to the use of language and cultural standards such as stereotypes, social distances, as well as cultural iceberg could cause certain difficulties in the pragmatic communication
