# INFANTILY APPROPRIATIONS: CLINICAL, NEUROBIOLOGICAL AND GENETIC IMPLICATIONS

The term attachment in the medical community is little known, however, it plays a role of great importance within human development as it is involved in determining the psychological and emotional characteristics of infants with respect to their personality and consolidating their interpersonal relationships. to carry out a narrative review of the types of attachment, clinical associations, genetic and neurobiological bases of the formation of a secure and unsafe attachment. a systematic search of literature was carried out in databases and academic journals where COMT genes were covered, SLC6A4 - 5 HTTLPR, DRD4, DRD2, OXTR. Insecure attachment was found to be a risk factor for the development of depressive symptoms, disorders of the spectrum of psychosis, suicidal thoughts and attempts, increased proportion of attention deficit and long-term use of psychoactive substances. the presence of documented genetic variations does not allow establishing a direct relationship with the type of attachment developed and its consequences. All genetic variations have a greater degree of influence if accompanied by the presence of negative environmental factors such as the exposure to children is little sensitive factors of mothers
