# Design of an advanced protection algorithm for AC micronetworks interconnected to a traditional distribution system

In recent decades, the increasing interest in renewable generation forms has driven the development of topologies of local electrical systems that exploit available energy resources, giving rise to the concept of microgrids. These innovative topologies have wide benefits, especially when considering current energy requirements and deficiencies in the supply of energy to the ́ectrica, as evidenced in non-interconnected areas in Colombia. However, the integration of new technologies into electrical systems carries with technical challenges that can affect their implementation. One of these challenges is associated with changes in the faulty behavior of systems with microgrid penetration, due to the inclusion of sources of generation based on investors (IBRs). These sources present a behavior in fault significantly different from traditional sources of generation. The modifications in the faulty behavior require the implementation of new protection schemes that respond to the emerging requirements, characterized mainly by their adaptability. In this the thesis of research developed to propose an innovative protection strategy.
