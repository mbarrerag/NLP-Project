# THE ROLE OF ACADEMIA IN THE PROTECTION OF THE PATRIMONY. CASE STAFF OF THE MANIZAL-MARIQUITA AERE CABLE

The academy not only this call to establish ethical values and citizens, but also to act in consonance. In relation to the recognition of the past and present realities of our societies, the role of the university institutions is transcendental, since they are identified as the intellectual lighthouse that establishes the correct act. In this recognition and valuation of the various components of the national and world heritage, it is the academy that is called to lead these processes to ensure the recognition and protection of such elements, as well as that of all the practices of transcendence that shape our cultural heritage. The contribution of the university lies, then, in which it is called to identify the practices and objects in which all kinds of conceptions, beliefs and responses to historical situations have been converted, and to proceed accordingly to recognize why ́ they are valuable and why ́ they must be preserved for the present and future of the University of Colombia. The National University of Colombia has always been aware of this responsibility and, for decades, has led research processes of national and international impact, but also of preservation, recovery and updating of buildings of a heritage character of those that the city continues today to be valued. One of these cases is the National University of the former half of the National Station of Colombia. of the need to recognize and value their conditions as a way of understanding our past, as well as the role of the National University of Colombia in this process of recognition and awareness-raising
