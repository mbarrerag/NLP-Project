# Conditional decriminalization of incest in Colombia: An analysis based on doctrine and jurisprudence

This grade work carries out an analysis on the penal type of incest in Colombia in light of the principles of the constitutional criminal law of Non bis in idem, anti-juridicity and minimal intervention. To this end, the state of the discussion is contextualized based on the important concepts that revolve around this type of criminal. Subsequently, a review is made of some of the Latin American and European penal systems, showing the discrepancy between them and the criminalization of incest as an autonomous criminal type. Next, the position of the two most important judgments of the Constitutional Court regarding the maintenance of the sanction of incest, establishing the arguments taken into account to avoid granting the unconstitutionality demanded.
