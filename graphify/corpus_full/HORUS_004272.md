# Matter between lines. Drawing as an instrument of conception of form in the House of Illustrated Guests

This master’s thesis addresses various relationships that can be established between drawing, understood as one of the main tools of the project, and the conception, development and materialization of the architectural form. The working hypothesis rests on the narrow links forged between drawing and design processes, in such a way that the detailed study of the first allows to reveal and to establish specific strategies of the second. Thus, the identification of the traits of the architectural drawing helps to understand the genesis of the project and, in turn, allows to recognize the geometries that vehicularize the conception of the form.
