# Production, characterization and corrosion resistance of TiAlZrTaNbN coatings with potential application in the Biomedical and Industrial industry

The object of study of this research are the nitride coatings of TiAlZrTaNb on the Haynes superalloy and the Ti6Al4V alloy, which were deposited by the technique of cathodic spraying with magnetron by high power impulse (HiPIMS) with a variation of the polarization of the substrate from 0 V to −75 V and a working pressure from 0.3 Pa to 0,7 Pa. The effect of the polarization of the substrate voltage Bias and the working pressure on the corrosion resistance by electrochemical methods in the films was investigated. The microstructure, morphology and chemical composition of the coatings were analyzed by diffraction of X-ray, electron microscopy of scanning and spectroscopy of X-ray of dispersive energy. The porosity of the sample and the resistance to corrosion were characterized by electrochemical methods, specifically, from the electrochemical impedance (EIS) and potentiodynamic polarization tests using a solution of NaCl to 3,5 % by weight and each polyy.
