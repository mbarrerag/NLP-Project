# Coordination of dissemination, discussion and visibility spaces for strengthening the Research Group on Territorial Development, Peace and Post-Conflict (GIDETEPP) 2022-2023

The research group Desarrollo Territorial, Paz y Posconflicto (GIDETEPP), in its work plan has defined four lines of action to promote reflections, dialogues and research between the academy, institutions, social organizations and citizens on the socio territorial conflict after the signing of the Peace Agreement and the Pandemia, which contribute to the construction of critical opinion and to the consolidation of peace scenarios from different social sectors.Our central goal is to establish spaces of discussion, participation and dissemination that are enriching after the six years of the Peace Agreement, whose balance is not satisfactory. Between advances and challenges, it is found that the latter are greater, but also important to highlight how the Peace Agreement remains in force.It is possible to advance in the peace and for this purpose it is proposed to promote in a priority and central way the debate between the academy, the social bodies and the institutions, of the central issues and problems of the Peace Agreement. In recent years GIDETEPP has focused on academic production, in the expansion of information regarding the state of territorial peace and the balance of implementation of the agreement. Therefore, the present call for strengthening seeks to change the focus the group from the peace and towards the debate. In recognizing the importance of building with others, we do not lose sight of the importance of establishing and strengthening links with other academic communities and national universities. Thus, we establish cooperative relationships with the GTI-UNAL Biosystems Management Group, in the area of Habitat, City and Territory, of the Faculty of Engineering to take research to practical areas that directly impact Colombian rurality. Our fourth proposal is framed in the recognition of the difference in the territories and the emergence of new socio-territorial conflicts in ethnically differentiated territories following the signing of the Peace Agreement. Therefore, through the organization of two panels, we intend to deepen the relations between academia and society
