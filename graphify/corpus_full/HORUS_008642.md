# BIOLOGICAL ACTIVITY AND CHEMICAL ANALYSIS OF THE LEVELS OF THE GUAYABA PAUJÍA (EUGENIA CRIBRATA MCVAUGH) PRESENT IN COLOMBIAN ORINOCIA

The guava infuriating paujía (Eugenia cribrata) is an endemic fruit of the region of the Orinoquia Colombiana, appreciated both for its cultural value and for its potential nutritional and pharmacological value. Despite its importance, it lacks scientific research that describes its chemical composition and biological properties. This study aims to evaluate the biological activity, antioxidant and chemical components of the leaves of E. cribrata. The research is based on the chemical richness of other species of the genus Eugenia and its use in popular medicine. The methodology includes the collection and drying of leaves, followed by the obtaining of etanolic extract. A preliminary chemical analysis was performed that identified the presence of various compounds, such as reducing sugars, aglycones and steroidal compounds, but a low concentration of polyphenols and flavonoids. The antioxidant activity was evaluated against the radical study DPPH, and a major anti-malarial activity Mmatónica was obtained, indicating a good antioxidant capacity. Further trials are planned with different radicals such as ABS and FRAP also, comparisons with reference compounds such as the TROLOX.
