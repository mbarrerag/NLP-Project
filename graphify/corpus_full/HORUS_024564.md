# Multiscale reconfiguring of the urban space: A counterproposal of urban design in the neighborhood Patria of the city of Bogotá

The levels of connectivity, the means of public transport and the size of the streets and platforms generate a multiscale condition of the urban plot that affects the size of the apples and properties, the occupancy rates, the height and the uses of the floor of the constructed area. From these variables three cases of study were discussed: one of local scale, one of zonal scale and one of metropolitan scale, in Bogotá, with the purpose of analyzing and characterizing the functional conditions of the urban plot and its relation with the spatial attributes of the form of the constructed space. These variables of analysis and the findings of the characterization of the study cases were used to diagnose the multiscale relationship between the urban plot and the form of the space built in the Patria neighbourhood. A comparison was made between the proposed urban renewal and urban development company of Bogotá with three proposed design scenarios (with moderate, intermediate and extreme levels of intervention). urban pattern and the built space in the Homeland needborhood. Ultimately, a comparison was made between the urban renewal proposal proposed by the Bogotá Urban Renewal and Development Company with three proposed design scenarios (with moderate, intermediate, and extreme levels of intervention). From this comparison, an urban design was developed that reconfigured the multiscale relationship of the Patria neighborhood
