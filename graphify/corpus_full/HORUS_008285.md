# PRIVATE PROPERTY CONCEPT IN THE CONTEXT OF THE SOCIAL STATE OF LAW (CAP I) AND PRIVATE PROPERTY PROTECTION MECHANISMS (CAP. II)

An analysis is made of the trends that have sought to explain private property from the individualistic thesis to the one that today pregones the functionalist conception leading to private property being considered as a fundamental right of relative character in which it has protection mechanisms that are analyzed integrally in these chapters
