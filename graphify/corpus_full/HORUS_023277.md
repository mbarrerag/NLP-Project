# ATTENTION AND MEMORY IN PRIMARY BASIC STUDENTS OF RURAL PUBLIC INSTITUTIONS OF BOYACA, COLOMBIA

The study's objective was to establish the relationship between care and memory processes in rural school children with respect to age, sex and educational level. 50 students were selected by random sampling, within a descriptive correlational study. The children's neuropsychological evaluation battery was used. The results showed significant differences with respect to age for memory and visual attention sub-tests. In the gender variable, no significant differences were found. In relation to the level of schooling, differences were for attention and memory sub-tests except in logical memory. In conclusion, a lower performance was observed when comparing them with urban population scales
