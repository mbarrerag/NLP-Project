# Identification of possible landslides on the Salamina–San Félix road by the accumulation flow procedure using GIS tools

Landslides represent one of the main causes of threat to human life and infrastructure; in the case of Colombia, it is considered a country with a high susceptibility to this phenomenon, given the mountainous topographic characteristics, climatic conditions and diversity in land use. In order to achieve an early identification of possible landslides that may occur on the Salamina - San Félix road, we propose to study them by means of the accumulation flow technique, using satellite images and the digital terrain model (DTM) as base information; This information will be processed by means of GIS programs, in order to obtain by means of pixel accumulation, the ranges with greater susceptibility to landslides, in zones denominated as geomechanics units of independent behavior (UGI), which present phenomena that favor landslides, based on the topography and existing anthropic factors. From the results obtained and the constant verification in the field, it was possible to determine the appropriate ranges for the identification of areas with high accumulation flow, from 1000 pixels, and thus delimit the threat zones for the subsequent generation of the mass movement risk map, in order to serve as input for early warning decision making and/or the projection of the necessary works in road infrastructure projects
