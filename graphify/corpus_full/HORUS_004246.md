# Behaviour of calibration curve in hole plates for different operating conditions

In this research work, an analysis of the coefficient of energy losses and the cavitation of the large diameter orifice plates in pressure ducts under a high variability of hydrodynamic conditions is presented. The focus of this study is on understanding the hydrodynamic behavior of these accessories and on using Software of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to find an optimal solution of the local energy loss coefficient of the orifice plates. To carry out the analysis, computational softwares such as ANSYS Fluent, SimScale and OpenFOAM were implemented, using the method of finite volumes. The results obtained were validated with experimental analysis carried out by several authors, which had controlled pressurization systems and some strategic monitoring points for the variables that describe their hydrodynamic background. In addition, an equation is proposed to determine the coefficient of energy loss when using plates in large-sized pipelines.
