# The playful as a strategy of learning the concept of the Cartesian plane in students of the sixth grade of secondary basic education.

The playful as a strategy of learning the concept of the Cartesian plane in students of the sixth grade of basic secondary education The general objective of the research is: To implement a didactic strategy mediated by playful environments for the learning of the Cartesian plane in students of the sixth grade of secondary basic of the rural educational institution Santa Teresa of the municipality El Paujil, Caquetá. It is an applied research, with qualitative approach and research design action that takes place in four cycles planned for the consolidation of the teaching process and learning of the object of study; Cycle I: detect the problem; Cycle II, elaborate the plan, Cycle III: implement the didactic sequence and Cycle IV: evaluate the plan. The present research makes the theoretical and didactic contribution, embodied in the design of a didactic sequence with playful activities that integrate situations specific to the context of the students who have in practice possibilities of articulation with the conceptual structures associated with the use of the Cartesian.
