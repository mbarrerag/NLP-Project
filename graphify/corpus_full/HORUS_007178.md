# Altruism and solidarity: fundamental concepts for thinking about morals in the human being

To get closer to the complexity of the human moral nature it is key to understand what motivates us to engage in the welfare of others. Generally we think that selfishness is the core of all our motivations, objectives and behaviors, including the actions of help that favor others. Different philosophical and scientific traditions have contributed to sustain this vision of the human being as essentially self-centered. With a view to questioning this vision, this work addresses the question of the human moral nature from several fundamental points. On the one hand, from an inquiry on the constitution of our psyche and the place of emotions in our understanding of the moral world. In this field, we rely on a historical perspective to rescue some philosophical approaches to the enlightened way that have estimated the human emotional sphere as central in our moral performance.
