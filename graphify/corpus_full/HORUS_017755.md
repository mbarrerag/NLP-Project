# Evaluation of materials and micro shot weighing to increase resistance to failure due to cycling fatigue in rotation endodontic files

This thesis addresses the problem of fractures of endodontic limes from two points of view; In the first instance, alternative materials to the classic alloy of Nickel-Titanium were analyzed such as Titanium-Niobio and Titanium-Molybdenum. This analysis found that Titanium-Niobio can be proposed as an alternative for the manufacture of endodontic limes. Secondly, the shot peeling procedure was proposed and analyzed to increase resistance to fatigue fracture in both the proposed alternative alloys and the Nickel-Titanium limes, finding a significant increase in fatigue fracture resistance in the files and wires subjected to the shot peeling
