# I AM AUTHORIZING 6 CHAPTERS ( PAGES 31-67, 173- 177, 296- 208, 310-319)

This book collects, organizes, formalizes and consolidates the work and experience of several professors of the Department of Chemistry, of the National University of Colombia, who dictate or have dictated, in the first semesters of the careers of the faculties of Sciences, Engineering, Medicine and Agrarian Sciences, the courses of Chemistry.In addition, the book has an instructional perspective.For that reason it contains guided activities with which it is intended to strengthen concepts, to promote the objective management of data and calculations and to develop attitudinal and procedural skills.As a result, the activities are grouped by chapters.The first chapter includes general instructions on the characteristics of the work in the chemistry laboratory and the rules of safety and behavior.From the second chapter, some usual procedures related to instrumental manipulation and taking and handling of numerical data are explained.Also includes information on the dangerousness of the chemical substances and the elaboration and use of the safety sheets of these, and the results of the results are included in the sixth chapter and the results of the results are used
