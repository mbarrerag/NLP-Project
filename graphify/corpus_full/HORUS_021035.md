# Technological proposal for obtaining a microbial inoculant and other co-products based on acid-lactic bacteria (BAL), from agro-industrial by-products.

The productivity of dairy cattle in the Colombian high tropics is low compared to that reported in European countries or the United States. The main causes of this situation are seasonal fluctuation, which is increasingly affected by climate change, and the lack of agricultural practices that guarantee the continuity of the nutritional contributions necessary for cows.One of the reasons that influence this latter cause are the losses of fodder material due to deficiency in management and conservation practices. In view of this situation, the implementation of techniques of preservation of fodder in the country is required.The elaboration of active income is one of them, but due to the lack of diffusion, the realization of deficient artisanal practices and the fact that the products used in the process are imported and of high price, this practice is very small and/or leads to losses of nutritional and microbiological quality of the ensilajes carried out in the high tropic. Because one of the alternatives for the realization of insilajes is inoculation with acid-lactic bacteria (BAL), the Colombian Corporation of Agricultural Research (AGROSAVAVA) identified this need and established in the macro-inclusion of The methodology includes the study of articles and patents using techniques of data science, optimization of the fermenting medium in laboratory scale, initial study at the bioreactor level of 5 to 10 L and the analysis of economic pre-feasibility of the process. It is then sought to establish a process that provides the active principle of bacterial inoculant, so that it is more affordable and an alternative to imported inoculants so that, together with training and technological transfer, it is implemented by the dairy farming sector in the high tropics of Colombia.
