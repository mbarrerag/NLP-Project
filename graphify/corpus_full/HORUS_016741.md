# Chemical study and cytotoxic activity of Phlegmariurus crupentus (Lycopodiaceae).

The phytochemical investigation of the etanolic extract of the aerial part of Phlegmariurus crupentus led to the isolation and purification by chromatographic techniques of four new triterpenes of the serrate type, identified as 24-acetoxyserratenediol (S5), lycophleg vexation E (S7), lycophlegmariol F (S8) and 3-acetylserratriol (S9), as well as five serrates known and named as phlegmanol C (S1), 3-serratenediol acetate (S2), 21-epi-serratendiol (S3), serratenediol (S4), and 21-episerratriol (S6). Its chemical structures and relative configurations were elucidated by the analysis of data obtained by HRESIMS, SMN in 1D, SPT 13C. and 2d (cosy, hmbc and noesy). In addition, the data obtained by the above were compared with the previously published specular spectroscopic values, SPMS, SPTS in SPT1 copodiaceae
