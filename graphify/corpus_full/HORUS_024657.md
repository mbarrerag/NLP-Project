# URBAN ECOPUNTURAS

The paper analyzes some project experiments carried out in the field of teaching and university research. Some project trials carried out in the field of the experience developed in the so-called researches Contemporary architecture in Medellín? and what about the Architecture for democracy of the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín headquarters. Based on the study of national and international references and thanks to the methodology of creating multidisciplinary groups (artists, architects, urbanists, botanists, engineers, community members, children), projects have been addressed at different scales, from the realization at 1:1 scale of small housing modules and urban furniture systems, to basic projects of self-sufficient neighborhoods
