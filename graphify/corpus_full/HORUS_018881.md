# Narrative analysis of public policy for the prevention of thyroid disease: the case of the salt iodization law in Colombia 1947-2019.

Malnutrition affects the health of human beings since pregnancy and manifests itself in different diseases. The deficit and/or excess of micronutrients is a phenomenon that goes unnoticed in populations. Iodine is an essential micronutrient that is obtained in the diet and acts as the main substrate of the thyroid gland for the synthesis of thyroid hormones, involved in the panderal and cognitive development of the human being. The public policy for the prevention of thyroid disease due to iodine deficiency in the Member States of the World Health Organization has been until today, the strengthening of salt with iodine. An insufficient intake of iodine during pregnancy increases the rate of abortions, perinatal mortality and congenital malformations; in the newborn, it is associated with low weight at birth, neonatal goitre and cretinism; in the child and adolescent it has produced mental retardation and growth, deafmude, motor abnormalities and hypothyroidism; in the adult it alters the mental function and decreases fertility.
