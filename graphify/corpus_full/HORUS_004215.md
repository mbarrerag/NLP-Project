# Industrialization and urban renewal: an analysis of residential transformations around Coltejer, Itagüí (1940-2023).

Industrialization in Colombia played a major role in the construction of the urban fabric, among these interventions; we can highlight the housing for its workers at the beginning of the nineteenth century. This research aims to show how the dynamics of industrialization and of productive restructuring of Coltejer have influenced the development and residential transformations of the neighbourhoods adjacent to their factory, in Itagüí between 1940 and 2023. To this end, a qualitative method with a socio-spatial approach of urban morphology is taken for research. The neighbourhoods adjacent emerged in different times where Coltejer has intervened in its development, for the results a historical bibliographic tracing, interviews and morphological perceptions was carried out. The research also shows the various realities of the residential transformations that emerged in these neighborhoods that its genesis was in the middle of the nineteenth century, and the change of perception of what were called labour districts, also the enhancement of the interventions of the factory of Coltejer. time, but without their growth process, Coltejer has actively intervened in their development. To trace this historical development, the research reports on bibliographic analysis, interviews, and morphological perceptions. The study revealed the diverse realities of residential transformations in these needborhoods that were originated in the mid-19th century, shelling light on the changing perception of what were eleven termed workers' needs and raising the interventions of the Coltejer factory.
