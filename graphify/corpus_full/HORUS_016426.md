# DESIGN AND PUT ON THE POINT OF A DIAGNOSTIC SYSTEM OF BOVINA TUBERCLESIS BY AMPLIFICATION ISOTERM LAMP-PCR

The diagnosis of BTB is based on intradermoreaction versus tuberculin and microbiological tests, the latter with problems of specificity, sensitivity, speed of results, as well as limited access by small and medium farmers. Therefore, this work seeks to design and standardise a specific diagnostic system of type point of care for the easy identification of M. bovis using the technique LAMP-PCR, or amplification of isotherma, which does not require specialized equipment or staff and can be easily implemented in field. To achieve this objective, a genomic region was initially sought for the specific identification of M. bovis, which was used to design a set of M. bolustrates using the technique LAMP-PCR, which allowed the amplification of fragments and their respective colorometric detection.
