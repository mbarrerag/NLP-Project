# USE OF ISOTHERMAL MICROCALORIMETRY TO STUDY THE METABOLIC ACTIVITY OF A BIOPRODUCT DEVELOPED FOR CADMIUM IMMOBILIZATION IN CACAO SOILS IN ARAUCA, COLOMBIA

In Arauca, cacao crop has been promoted for peace programs, a model that benets farmers and allows for the socioeconomic development of the region. However, high levels of Cd that exceed permissive levels can be a barrier to cacao bean export. There is a need to use techniques that allow the proper management of microorganisms, such as bacteria that tolerate high concentrations of Cadmium (Cd), avoiding the absorption and translocation of toxic elements into cacao beans. The microbiology associated with the immobilization of cadmium (CdtB) is analysed using the isothermal calorimetry technique (IMC), that, over time, shows the heat capacity produced by the metabolic activity of biological organisms found in a sample such as soil. The aim of this study was to determine the eect of enrichment of a soil with CdtB by its metabolic capacity using IMC and regards its function with Cd immobilization in cacao crops from Arauca, Colombia
