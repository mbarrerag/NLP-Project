# Identification of intra-bdominal microorganisms associated with postoperative intrabdominal collection in children with perforated appendicitis.

Introduction Acute appendicitis is the most frequent abdominal surgical urgency in children.The most commonly associated pathogens are enterobacteria and anaerobics, there are differences between bacterial sensitivity in the different hospitals.The aim of the study is to determine the association between the presence of an empirical antibiotic resistant microorganism and the development of collections in patients with perforated appendicitis. Methodology A prospective cohort study was performed in patients under 18 years of age who were taken into a laparoscopic risk with intraoperative documentation of perforated appendicitis between 1 November 2019 and 30 September 2020, and a sample of peritoneal fluid was taken for cultivation and were processed in bottles of aerobic, anaerobic and dry tube. Clinical and microbiological data were collected from all patients.
