# PEACE, JUSTICE AND STRENGTHENING OF SMALL INSTITUTIONS AS A CHALLENGE FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF A SUSTAINABLE TERRITORY.

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) can be considered as the great global policy that will improve the quality and living conditions of the population from a global and integral perspective. As the well-known principle of sustainability expresses: to think globally and act locally, or, as has also been expressed colloquially: to think globally and suffer locally?
