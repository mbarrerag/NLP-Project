# Quality of RESIDUAL WATER INDEXS AS INTEGRATED EVALUATION TOOL OF EFFUENTS. CASE OF STUDY: GOLD MINERITY

The characterization of a surface water sample implies the assessment of a large number of physicochemical parameters whose values may become difficult to interpret for the community at large. Water quality indices (WAI) emerge as tools to simplify the way in which water resource quality is evaluated. These allow to characterize water quality more compactly through a number, range, descriptive category or even a color. ICAs have become important in recent years and have become fundamental in public policy decision-making processes [1]. In addition to ACIs, the Residual Water Quality Index (RSI) has been developed, although with less deployment, in recent years. These have been applied to the evaluation of effluents from different types of industries [3, 4]
