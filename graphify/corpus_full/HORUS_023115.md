# From seed to garment and how Fashion has managed to destroy the world that feeds

I framed the process of this thesis within a perspective of undisciplined artistic practices, practices that helped me to incorporate gestures, actions, denunciations and devices. Forms developed from different disciplines that were interconnected with art, so that the method and the formation of thought were the result of the confrontation of subjectivities with which I have been bumping. I feel that this sought multiplicity was the one that helped me to build the critical and analytical framework to question what we consume, why we consume it and how we are led to do it. The content of this box is a compilation of elements that encompass all the work and testify to the transformation process that I have experienced throughout this thesis.
