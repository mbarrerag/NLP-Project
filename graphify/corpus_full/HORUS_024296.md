# THE UNITED STATES OF

The work that the reader has in his hands aims to make an approximation to the difficulties for the consolidation of property rights in the context of the Constitution of the United States of Colombia of 1863. The treatment of this topic involves understanding the role played by the conception of law that came to Latin America and its implications on private property. However, to approach this problem is to state that individual freedom, in the legal-economic sense, is the basis of property rights
