# A didactic strategy to strengthen spatial thinking in students of the eighth grade of the Sun School.

Historically, the performance of students in the Knowledge 11 and Knowledge 9 tests, of the school “Eccola Sol Nacente”, specifically the results associated with spatial thinking and geometric systems, has not been satisfactory. Some of these contents were selected to propose a didactic strategy that strengthens the spatial thinking of eighth grade students. The strategy is based on giving students, techniques and tools of representation of three-dimensional objects, instead of presenting formulas and application exercises as is done in a traditional way, also giving greater importance to the contents of spatial geometry that are usually relegated towards the end of the year. The methodology used for the didactic strategy is constructivist, since the student through activities of design, layout, geometrical constructions, playful applications, approach and problem solving in group, has an environment conducive to strengthening spatial skills and generating significant learning. First, a bibliographic review was made on the development of geometry and spatial thinking. To carry out the intervention, a diagnostic test was applied to measure the spatial skills of students, then eight sessions were taken between workshops, exercises and theoretical-practical classes, selected to strengthen the strategy
