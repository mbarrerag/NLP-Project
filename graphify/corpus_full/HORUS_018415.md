# VALIDATION OF CONVENTIONAL PCR TO DETECT ESCHERICHIA COLI O157

The main objective of this study is to determine the level of quantification necessary to detect Escherichia coli O157 enterohemorrhagic using polymerase chain reaction (PCR). Conventional PCR validation is developed by evaluating its genes of higher virulence: stx1, stx2 and rfbE. The PCR sensitivity results indicate a limit of detection between 0.011 and 0.33 ng μL-1 DNA for stx1 and stx2 and 0.0037 ng μL-1 DNA for rfbE. The lower cut limit varies between 0.033 and 0.099 ng μL-1 DNA for stx1 and stx2, for rfbE is 0.033 ng μL-1 DNA. The selectivity (inclusivity and exclusivity) and the robustness are 100% for the three genes, demonstrating the reproducibility of PCR for distinguishing E. coli O157 from other serotypes.
