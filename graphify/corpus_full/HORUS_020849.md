# PROMOTION OF A SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT CULTURE: A TRAINING PROGRAMME IN ECOTURISM-ARTROPOFAUNA IN THREE COLOMBIAN COMMUNITIES

The human communities of the municipalities Mocoa (Putumayo), Mistrató (Risaralda) and Nuquí (Chocó) present a variety of vulnerabilities, including settlements in environmentally impaired areas, incomes below a current minimum legal monthly wage and displaced or resettled population resulting from activities such as deforestation, oil extraction, extensive livestock farming and the effects of armed conflict. These communities have found in ecotourism an opportunity to take advantage of the high biodiversity of their regions and improve their quality of life; to strengthen this economic line, the conservation of their natural areas and to foster the social appropriation of traditional and scientific knowledge of the arthropofauna as one of the most common and diverse elements of their ecosystems, this project seeks to promote a culture of sustainable development through an ecotourism training programme that integrates the environmental interpretation of arthropods in independent communities or associated with Colombian natural reserves: 1. inga pakai community (mocoa, putumayo), 2. communities associated with the el amargal nature reserve: biological station & ecotourism (nuquí, chocó) and 3. Communities associated with the Mitú de Reserva avitó
