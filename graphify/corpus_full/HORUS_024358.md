# The trumpet in buoyacense music: a fusion between academic and traditional sonorities

The boyacense music has a wide melodic, harmonic, timbric and historical richness. These characteristics, combined with the tools offered by the academy, have great potential for the technical and interpretive development of the instrumentalists. In this work, it is related, from the experience of a paipano trumpeter, how a master-level recital is constructed with works by buoyan composers for trumpet, piano and typical Colombian instruments. Two of the four works that integrate the proposal are created in a collaborative way within the framework of this project, process that is described in detail throughout the text. The theoretical and technical analysis of the repertoire studied here is vital for the development of an original and quality proposal. At the end of the document, it explains how research of this type contributes drastically to the integral development of the musician.
