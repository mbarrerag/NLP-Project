# LOCAL SYSTEMS OF COLOMBIA JUSTICE: THE WAY TO A COLLABORATIVE JUSTICE PRIORIZING PERSONS

5.1 trillion people, two thirds of the world's population, lack meaningful access to justice.To achieve SDG 16+ and equal access to justice for all by 2030, we must place people at the centre of justice systems and move from words and planning to action and implementation. The Pathfinders Great Justice Challenge acts as a centre of impact, calling countries, civil society and multilateral and regional organizations to bridge the justice gap by accelerating action on people-centred justice. The Great Challenge provides a platform for the exchange of innovative practices in data collection, evidence-based policy formulation and effective use of resources to overcome fractions and transform justice systems to prevent and address people's most common justice problems.
