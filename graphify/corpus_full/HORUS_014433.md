# Analysis of violence by adolescents within their family nucleus during confinement in the city of Bogotá. Addressing domestic violence under Law 294 of 1996.

This study focused on analysing intra-family violence in adolescents during confinement in the city of Bogotá, under the framework of Law 294 of 1996. The proposed objective was to analyze Law 294 of 1996 in response to cases of domestic violence by minors in the context of the pandemic of the COVID-19. Violence has always been present in humanity and has been transformed according to the social and cultural dynamics of each society, until it becomes coexisted with violence and normalized as part of life. Thus, it is sought to develop everything related to the filioparental violence united with the pandemic COVID 19, in order to contextualize the subject matter of study that in synthesis is the violence exercised by minors and/or adolescents against their parents, grandparents or other members of the family. It is also used a socio-legal methodology with an analytical approach, and the documentary technique was used to collect information. The interpretation of the information was made by means of a content analysis, using the key words, descriptors and classification of documents
