# Wastewater treatment technologies applicable to rural sector: Bibliometric analysis

The increase of water resource pollution in rural areas, due to the dumping of untreated effluents and the intensive use of water sources by communities and industries, poses critical challenges in environmental management.With only 0.3% of the surface water available for human consumption [1], it becomes essential to explore innovative and accessible solutions for wastewater treatment (HR) in rural areas.Despite the evident need for the development of technologies for wastewater treatment (HR) in rural areas, there are few scientific publications on the subject and there is no bibliometric analysis that allow identifying trends in the disposal of these wastewater.In this Final Master's Work a detailed bibliometric analysis of scientific productivity in wastewater treatment systems applicable to the rural sector, given the knowledge gap identified in this area.The methodology adopted was based on a structured approach, using a search equation using key words, Boolean connectors, truncators and recognized data bases such as Scopus and Web of Science. This paper contains a description of the main technologies used in the water supply in rural areas and, in addition, a bibliographical analysis of the results of the study. A decision diagram was proposed, which can be used as a tool, for the correct use of each of the technologies available according to the characteristics of the waste water and available area (source taken).
