# Public Administration Observatory (OBSAP)

The Observatory of Public Administration (OBSAP) is an initiative of the line of research on Public Administration of the research group Analysis of Public Policies and Public Management (APPGP) of the Faculty of Law, Political and Social Sciences. This unit is intended to consolidate as a continuation of the Observatory of Administrative Culture (OCA) 2021-2022 of the same group, and in agreement with the Administrative Department of the Distrital Civil Service (DASCD) and the program of undergraduate of Public Administration of the University of the Valley. This Observatory will focus on the task of identifying and analyzing the trajectories around the principles and administrative fashions and the various social and technological innovations in the Public Administration through the study of its civil service, culture and administrative behavior and its institutional design at the district, territorial and national level. The above will be carried out by means of the collection, analysis, monitoring and comparison of the historical and current information of the Public Administration (APC) that allows strengthening the research and dissemination of inputs and recommendations to the public sector, the citizenship and various stakeholders on the subject. Thus, the comparative claim of the ABSAP is guided under the Public Administration (A comparison) units. these are: 1) principles and administrative moda (p-ma) (arellano gault & barreto pérez, 2016) and 2) public innovation (ip) (zubriggen & gonzález, 2014). However, this unit will be able to come out of subprojects with the APPG research group in order to advise the various entities, in particular the DAFP, and/or public institutions for their institutional improvement in relation to the different axes and dimensions of research of the OBSAP. With this, the Observatory will be strengthened as a consultative or relevant body to provide recommendations in AP in decision-making based on evidence. Finally, it is planned to consolidate the proposal in a permanent way as a unit attached to the APPGP group in order to strengthen and strengthen methodologies and approaches in the research processes for the analysis of the Public Administration within the Faculty of Law and Political Sciences and the National University of Colombia. Finally, the OBSAP is also drawn up as an inter-institutional articulate between the various entities or organizations involved and interested, around the national public administration
