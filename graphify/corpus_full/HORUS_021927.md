# Follow-up of femicide cases in Colombia through text mining techniques.

This paper addresses the problem of feminicide violence, how the maximum expression of violence against women is presented. The background to this scourge in Colombia is presented and how it has evolved in recent years. From this, it is assumed that the first way of addressing such a problem is with adequate visibility. To achieve a correct visibility, first a characterization must be made and this characterization is based on a careful process of extracting information. Thus, in this final Master's work, a text mining model is proposed that begins with the integration of sources of information around the problem of feminicide violence in Colombia between July 2017 and December 2021. Then, the information extracted is adjusted to identify key signals through techniques such as a bag of words, dendogram, recognition of entities and clustering. Based on the techniques previously mentioned, a characterization of cases of feminicide violence is carried out and the results are validated in light of the main source of information and official data from the Office of the Attorney General of the Nation.
