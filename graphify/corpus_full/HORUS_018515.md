# CONTRACTING FUNDS FOR AGREEMBERARY PROJECT MANAGEMENT

The agricultural or agro-industrial sector is one of the most important for the development of Colombia, due to the economic and social impact it generates through the use of the immense wealth in human resources that the country has. Despite the above, it is a sector where informality constitutes one of its main weaknesses. To this end, this book provides the basis for these professionals to be able to successfully face the realization of these works; presenting first some general concepts on the agro-entrepreneurial projects and different basic models of contracting that can complement according to their needs, later explains some formats of tables for the presentations of the proposals, the development and control of the contracts. It is important to bear in mind that the examples developed in the book are intended to facilitate the reader the understanding and mathematical estimation of the different models proposed, which must be adjusted by each professional before being applied to their specific reality; therefore, the quantities and monetary values presented throughout the book, can be changed by multiple factors among which stand out, the objectives and requirements of the contracting party, the technological level that is to be used and the fluctuations of prices proposed from the local, regional or national market; therefore, where the agricultural companies are developed, and the enterprise types of the enterprise are able to develop
