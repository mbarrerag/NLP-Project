# Manual for the preparation of immediate use medications for nursing professionals in a neonatal ICU

The research group Strengthening Pharmaceutical Services, has published the manual on preparation-administration of drugs for immediate use by nursing professionals in a neonatal ICU, dedicated to preventing risks associated with the handling of medicines in children in their neonatal development stage, since because of their condition they are more vulnerable to suffering injuries during their hospital stay. A large part of the health professionals who are in the care of this population recognize the need for specific safety measures to prevent them. The authors, nurse of vast experience in the field of neonatology in association with clinical pharmacists, collected the information needs of nursing professionals and through the contributions of different disciplines and professional practices, of those who carry out the activity of preparing and administering the medicines as a daily activity, propose in didactic way a set of actions, based on the International goals for the Safety of the Patient, which were compiled and presented in a manner organized in an easy-to-consultation text.
