# Genocidal rhetoric and construction of a negative otherness in the discourses of the conservative symbolic elites of Antioquia (1946-1949)

This research is an analysis of genocidal rhetoric and the construction of negative otherness in the discourses of conservative symbolic elites, in the light of the studies on genocide in the first stage of violence in Antioquia-Colombia (1946-1949). This analysis is developed from the consultation of the press of conservative tendency with wide dissemination for the period in the city of Medellín, as well as from the approach to academic discourses prior to the first stage of violence, which from medicine and humanities, have a racist and eugenic connotation that had an influence on the discourses published in the periodic publications of the period of study.
