# Strengthening laboratory capacities for the provision of outreach services

The CALAIRE laboratory requests support to strengthen its capabilities for the provision of extension services in the range of photometer calibration and verification of mass flow controllers (MFC English acronym) specifically increasing the efficiency of the generation of photometer calibration certificates through an automation process and on the other hand, acquiring the calibration of two (2) patterns in the MFC verification service with the National Center of Metrology of Mexico (CENAM), as required by our status as Designated Institute of the National Institute of Metrology of Colombia
