# Analysis of regulatory convergence in the authorization of the marketing of chemical synthesis medicinal products for the Americas region

Resumen With the purpose of studying the Latin American regulatory convergence for the authorization of the commercialization of medicinal products of chemical synthesis, regarding the reference authorities, a mixed methodology was used to determine the key factors for regulatory convergence, with which a comparison tool was generated to evaluate regulatory authorities and regulations; also a survey was applied to actors of the pharmaceutical sector to evaluate trends of harmonization, convergence and regulatory reliation. It was found that there is regional convergence in the regulatory evaluation for clinical and non-clinical studies, acceptance of pharmacopoeia, stability and validity of the registry. There is divergence in the standard of inspection of BPM, labelling content and inserts, recognition of decisions and alternate pathways for the health registry. The opinions of the respondents identify the applicability, benefits and limitations of the trends.
