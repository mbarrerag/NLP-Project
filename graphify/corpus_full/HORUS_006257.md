# Capsing of cannabis oil for use as an active ingredient in cosmetic consumer products

The cosmetic oil of cannabis due to the presence of cannabinoids, terpenoids and flavonoids presents active functions and nutritional value or treatments for epilepsy, pain management, inflammation and neurodegenerative disorders. Due to the variety of the physical properties of its compounds, this can present different challenges when integrating as an active ingredient into products. Among these are related impacts of the olfactory profile, impact on the stabilizers of the product and modifications or loss of desired metabolites. In order to present an approach to face these challenges, it is proposed to identify the encapsulation of the oil as a methodology that allows covering these fronts. For this approach, a first approach is made around the contextualization of the cannabis plant, its compounds, current market of its extract and its use in different products in the recent market.
