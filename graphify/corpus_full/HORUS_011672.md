# VAR. CAPITAL DIACOL

The plant with a low level of sugar content is a disease that has taken on importance in recent years in the potato crops in Colombia. It is caused by different species of Verticillium spp., pathogens of soil that colonize the xylem, interfering with the movement of water within the plant, causing wilt. Within the routes of dissemination of the pathogen are reported the contaminated soil and the seed tubers; however, in the latter, lack of information that specifies the level of transmission and development of the disease from tubers of infected plants. The objective of this study was to evaluate in the variety Diacol Capiro, the infection of Verticillium spp. and the pattern of browning in the vascular beams of tubers harvested from symptomatic plants in the field, with different levels of severity of plants.
