# Study of changes of leptonic taste to three bodies within the framework of a non-universal extension U(1)X to the standard model

The Standard Model assumes the conservation of the leptonic taste in the different interactions between laden leptons, quarks and bosons. However, less than six months ago, the experimental corroboration of the breakage of the leptonic taste in semi-millipton decays of the B[?] was reported. This leads to exploring the violation of the leptonic taste through interactions between laden leptons and neutral bosons. To perform this exploration, extensions to the standard model are made to explain the anomaly of the B melon, the violation of the leptonic taste, among other questions. In this order of ideas, the present project seeks to study the violation of the leptonic taste, in the light of different decaments to two and three bodies of charged leptons, where the description of these processes are framed by means of advantage and advantagelatives, and advantagen. description of these processes are framed in a non-universal extension U(1)X to the standard model
