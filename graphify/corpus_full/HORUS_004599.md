# A BACAN PSYCOANALYSIS

A Bacan psychoanalysis is to know how to do with death to support life, in Lacanian theory death is of the symbolic and the life of the real, it is as if doing something from the symbolic, symbolizing death can bear the real of life.What is a Bacan psychoanalysis ? is a psychoanalysis that does not make psychoanalytical texts dead letter, a psychoanalysis that is always recreated with each space where a subject emerges from his saying that when one hears one hears one's voice.To listen sweetly is to go to the rhythm of the words of the other, with his pauses, in order to make of those words a tone in which a subject can be located from his own history, that is the musical thing of the psychoanalytic device, that each subject encounters his own rhythm and dance with him and the others
