# ASSESSMENT OF THE EFFICIENCY OF DISCOMPOSAL OF DIFFERENT TYPES OF ORGANIC WASTE GENERATED IN THE NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA MEDELLIN DURING THE TECHNICAL OF BIODIGESTERAL PACES

The project aimed to evaluate the efficiency of decomposition of different types of organic waste generated at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Medellín, through the technique of biodigestor bales, in order to establish the coexistence of implementation on university campuses, in addition to that the resulting material can be used in the improvement of the soil conditions of its green zones. To this end the assembly of five bales of approximately 1 m3, as follows: Paca No. 0 (plant material), Paca No. 1 (plant material + composting inoculum), Paca No. 2 (vegetal material + decomposition accelerator called Genesis), Paca No. 3 (ve material + goat manure) and Paca No. 4 (plant material + coffee residues). The balas were performed through a non-probabilistic sampling by convenience, given the characteristics of the residues and the limited supply of residues in the time of the investigation (pandemia). The balas were formed with a hydraulic compaction of 1 ton/m3 and monitored by a previous delay of 6 months
