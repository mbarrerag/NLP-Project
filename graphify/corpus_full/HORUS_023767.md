# Reasonable and proportionate distribution of dynamic test loads - means of direct repair control.

Reasonable and proportionate distribution of the dynamic evidentiary burdens-direct remedy control means. This document presents a thorough review of the dynamic burden, showing the central aspects about the proof (the theory of the same and its purposes and requirements), and then to go into the application of the reader in the jurisprudence of the administrative litigation in Colombia, especially in the effects that the use of this theory has on the regime of State responsibility for alleged failure, specifying some suggestions and reflections aimed at that the use of such evidentiary burdens does not affect the procedural guarantees of the State; all this was seen from the doctrine, the jurisprudence and the analyses derived from the professional practice of the author.
