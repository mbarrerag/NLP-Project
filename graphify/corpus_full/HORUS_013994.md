# Seed of Space Studies

The Semillero of Spatial Studies derives the same-named research group (Seminario de Estudios Espaciales) whose object of study is, in general terms, the limit of the semantic field of the concept of space and how all matter is susceptible to become a spatial problem. In this sense, the semillero proposes an open dynamic where different lines of research converge through projects proposed by undergraduate and graduate students, without thematic impositions by the teachers who direct and participate. Although the concept of space includes a complexity that would be inabridgeable in its entirety (since it is not the same as the scientific analysis of the space from the arts or the human sciences), nine (9) lines of research can be proposed as a formative strategy, so that any student, particularly from the Faculty of Architecture, could participate and know the dynamics of research from his own initiatives. The proposed lines are: Architecture; plastic arts and construction; Visual Design; Spatial Design; Virtual Concepts; Spatial Heritage; Landscape; Landscape. The first three lines refer to the pregrad of the faculty (Arts, Architecture and Construction) from his case. More than four (4) projects of solidarity extension and several projects submitted to external calls to the Mayor of Medellín, the Government of Antioquia, the Ministry of Culture, among others. During the first semester of the year, students saw theoretical contents related to training research with the support of teachers and graduates experts in this field, and during the current semester, the phase of project formulation, consolidation of the seedbed, and execution of projects in which students, individually or collectively, propose not only which areas of interest but also which should comply with the delivery of an academic product and dissemination of results. With more than 26 members registered, the research seedbed has research projects related to the design of a typological matrix for rural housing, the study of the urban edge of Medellín in legal-spatial terms, the relationship of habitat with water, the conceptualization around the cultural landscape in Colombia, the theoretical construction on the virtual space, the mapping of airports tracks throughout the national territory, the execution of the project Think the Country and the formulation of multiple initiatives by the student that was incorporated as the second cohort of the 20th century23
