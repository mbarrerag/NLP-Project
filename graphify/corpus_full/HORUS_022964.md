# AMAUTA MENTORING PROGRAMME: A ASSISTANCE FOR PERMANENCE IN NEW ENGINEERS

The transition from college to university requires, in most of the students, a process of adaptation in different areas of their life: staff, affective, academic, family, social and sometimes also work. 17 months ago, that transition was carried out in a more practical way when entering the University, in a delimited physical field, could share with others the uncertainties and learnings that this new experience implies, having offices open with the possibility of acquiring that information that is needed, staying after class asking the teacher, and between courses and corridors make good friends. For newly admitted students, specifically from the semester 2019-2 due to the student unemployment and then in 2020-1 following the pandemic by the covid-19, the adaptation was otherwise shaped leading to the universal experience of higher education to rethink to not be short generating more doubts in academic, administrative and social terms. Aware of their work and the importance of promoting the adaptation of students and generating sense of belonging to the Faculty of Human and Economic Sciences of Colombia at the Medellín Headquarters, in the semester 2021-1 will be the pilot of the program of mentoring AMAUTA. The AMAUTA mentoring program has generally served as a calming program for first-in-class students, with the support of peer students from advanced courses who can serve as a guide in academic and administrative subjects.
