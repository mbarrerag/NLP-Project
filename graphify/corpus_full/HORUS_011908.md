# USE OF AUTOMATIC EXTERNAL DEfiBILATOR IN THE MASITIVE TRANSMILENIAN TRANSPORT SYSTEM IN BOGOTÁ, COLOMBIA

ABSTRACT The proposal to implement the use of Automatic External Defibrillators in public spaces emerged about 30 years ago as a tool to reduce the mortality of extrahospital cardiac arrest events. The programs of deployment of these devices worldwide have demonstrated efficiency and effectiveness, reflected in concrete and tangible figures. Faced with the global panorama, 5 years ago Colombia legislated in favour of the implementation of these devices in high influx spaces. This article seeks to expose the problem of the implementation of the DEA in the main mass transport system of Bogotá, TransMilenio, from a critical and analytical perspective, showing the deficit in primary care of cardiac arrest.
