# CONDITIONS OF LIFE QUALITY OF SAN ANDRES ISLAND: A BRIEF MULTIVATIVE DESCRIPTION

The Multiple Correspondence Analysis (MCA) allows the association and description of relationships between categorical variables of a data set and allows the visualization of the conglomerations of categories and individuals with similar characteristics among themselves through the geometric representation of individuals and categories on factorial planes. In this sense, the WMA on the data of the National Quality of Life Survey (ENCV) of the National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE) of the year 2021 of the Island of San Andrés was used to analyze the main variables that describe the living conditions on housing, households and individuals in the year after the pandemic caused by Covid 19. To this end, 13 categorical variables were taken, of which 46 categories were obtained. The main result is that Factor 1 behaves as a quality of life indicator, and is interpreted as the separation of the categories that present better quality of life conditions on the left side of Factor 1, and categories that present worse quality of life conditions on the right side
