# Diagnosis of the main factors affecting the working climate of software developers in Colombia.

The role of software development was initially considered within organizations as an operational role that could be changed within the organization as if it were a machine grane. However, through the arrival of concepts such as peopleware this role has a particular interest since it is determined that the work of software developers is part of an intellectual and creative process; and it can be affected by the different conditions in which it is found. Therefore, this work presents the identification, selection and application of a method based on a survey for the evaluation of the working climate in software developers in Colombia. Initially, the literature was searched for the surveys available for the evaluation of the working climate in general, exploring in each of its different dimensions, then the selection of the survey was carried out that had greater affinity with the peopleware postulates by applying a text mining process to finally apply the survey selected through a process of sampling of snowflake to a sample within the population of software developers in Colombia. At the end, the data were collected for their respective analysis concluding that in general the sample participants have a positive perception of the working climate as software developers
