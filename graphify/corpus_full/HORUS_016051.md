# STRATEGIES FOR THE DIAGNOSTIC OF A BACTERIAN ORIGIN DISEASE: CASE OF BLAND PUDRITION OF THE PILLS VIOLET (CYCLAMEN PERSICUM MILL.).

In Colombia, the viverismo includes the production and commercialization of live plants including ornamental plants and it is estimated that its production occupies around 1950 ha, involving more than 3000 producers and 35000 families of rural scope, that depend economically on this activity. Among the plants of nursery, is the cyclamen or violet of the Alps (Cyclamen persicum Mill.), one of the ornamental crops of pot most commercialized in the world, due to its permanent flowering. In Colombia, the production of cyclamen is mainly carried out in the department of Cundinamarca, specifically in the municipality of San Antonio del Tequendama, where 20% of its economic activity corresponds to the agricultural production, being of special importance the cultivation of ornamental plants in nursery.
