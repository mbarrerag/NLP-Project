# Effects of internal migration on intergenerational educational mobility: socio-economic analysis for Colombia

This study explores the relationship between internal migration and intergenerational educational mobility, taking into account variables such as the Departmental Competitiveness Index (DCI), gender, marital status, occupation, per capita income, among others. For this purpose, data from the National Survey of Quality of Life 2022 are used and estimates are made using a binary logistic and logistic model ordinal. The results reveal that intergenerational educational mobility is positively associated with lifelong migration, while recent migration shows negative effects.
