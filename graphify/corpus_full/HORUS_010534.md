# Urban innovation density, public space and diversity Barrio Jesús Nazareno, Medellín

The accelerated growth of the city in the Colombian context has led to large urban problems generated by the disordered expansion. In this work, different urban design strategies are explored and analyzed, which can be applied in high density contexts in the city of Medellín. These are presented in a proposal of urban design, where possible solutions are provided, with a focus on the qualitative and quantitative improvement of urban forms, to cities that are in a process of growth or transformation. A study is made of the concepts density, public space, and diversity, enriching the urban quality term. The intervention site is studied in depth and the analysis of different projects and cases of study is done that lead to the definition of urban design strategies in high density contexts. At the end, an innovative design proposal is presented, involving the combination of the different topics studied in a strategic piece for the city of Medellín, where the current conditions of the site are improved and the urban development is answered to a need and current problem, which is aimed at improving urban quality. .
