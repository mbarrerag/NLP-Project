# THE GALLINE HAND AND ITS INDUSTRIAL PROCESSING: A REVIEW* _

ABSTRACT The egg is a food with a large amount of nutrients to be used by our organism. The objective of the review is to provide general information about the chicken egg and its relationship with health; in addition, to review the state of thermal treatments and emerging technologies applied to the egg in order to generate value in products of the poultry sector. The review was carried out on databases of scientific journals attached to Science Direct, Scopus, Scielo and Redalyc, during a period from 2013 to 2020. Since the egg is considered a high risk food for health and a product with a variety of functional properties, in recent years, a tendency has been identified to apply thermal treatments that allow to obtain a safe product microbiologically for consumers and with the least effect on such properties to be used by the industry. In general, the egg is an easily processed raw material through different technologies, which allow to obtain liquid or solid products to be used in new processed products. The use of these technologies allows to reduce operating costs and improve the quality of the product
