# RESEARCH AGENCIES IN LOCAL-ACENT BIOLOGY: KEY FOR PROMOTING ACTIVE LEARNING IN BIOLOGICAL SCIENCES

The subject "Biology of development and morphophysiology" offered in the 6th semester of the Curricular Program of Biology of the Headquarters of La Paz is new in the curriculum and is immersed in an academic project focused on learning. It seeks to integrate the morphological design of the organisms with its functional purpose, recreating a body plan and its evolutionary path from the development in time and observable morphological changes to understand physiological principles. The program was built taking into account the need to learn science doing science (Luckie et al. 2012; Camacho et al. 2021). The design of the course was made from the identification of fundamental invariants and the construction of the toolbox to guide the synchronous and assynchronous activities individual and with peers, oriented towards the construction of the fundamental concepts of development and morphophysiology by each student focused on a project of territorial laboratory that seeks learning based on observation, description, measurement and experimentation with a local or regional organism. In the tool box, contents were included in Spanish and English, the platform of Peru and the «Gépóló have been reported when comparing learning perception vs. measured learning (Deslauriers L et al. 2019). It is best performed in students who carried out their year of general studies in person with those who did so remotely due to the contingency of the pandemic.
