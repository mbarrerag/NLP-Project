# ALERGY TO THE VACA MILK PROTEIN SIMULATELY DISEASE OF HIRSCHSPRUNG BY HALLAZGS IN THE ENEMA DE BARIO.

Allergy to cow’s milk protein can have multiple clinical manifestations. Some cases have inflammation of the rectosigmoid colon with secondary colonic dysmotility, similar to Hirschsprung’s disease. Previous studies have highlighted that the proportion of cow’s milk protein allergy in newborns who have symptoms similar to those of Hirschsprung’s disease is high
