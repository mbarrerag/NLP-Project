# METHODOLOGIES FROM THE SENSORIA, THE AUDIOVOUS AND THE EXPERIMENTAL

From proposals that transcend purely representative and traditional approaches, visual anthropologys have paid special attention to the uses of expressive, aesthetic, sensory and media repertoires as re-courses for the design and management of memory policies and other initiatives related to the struggle for justice and rights. In this context, the discussion on the political and cultural effectiveness of visual arts and digital production, devices of public participation and struggle, is central. It places on the table, on the one hand, questions concerning the transforming power of the visual arts, performative repertoires, digital media, and the sensory and affective dimensions of the political (Courtes Severino and Ulfe, in chapter 4)
