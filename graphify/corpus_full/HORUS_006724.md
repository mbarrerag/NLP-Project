# Characterization of the health innovation model and its expression in the technology transfer processes in Colombian universities

This document presents the results of a documentary research that seeks to characterize the stages and ideas that make up the predominant discourse around innovation in health, with the purpose of identifying how this model is expressed in the Colombian legal system, both in national public policy and in the policy of technology transfer in six institutions that research in health and that have received funding from the State for the execution of their projects. To this end, first, a historical approach is made in the construction of the various models of innovation, to give rise to a theoretical model applied to health innovation that expresses stages and ideas that underpin the predominant model, in this point the main criticisms and alternative approaches to these ideas are highlighted. Secondly, the expressions of the model are identified in the various documents and rules that make up the innovation policy in Colombia. Thirdly, the documents containing the institutional policy of the six entities referred to are analyzed with the aim of identifying concrete expressions of the model., while also highlighting key criticisms and alternative proposals to these ideas. Secondly, the study identifies expressions of this model without different documents and regulations that constitute Colombia's healthcare innovation policy. Third, it analyses the documents containing the institutional policies of the six mentioned entities to pinpoint specific expressions of the model within their frameworks. Ultimately, the research draws conclusions and proposals recommendations based on its findings. Annex 1 consist of the characterization of the institutional structure of the technical management in each of the entities, encouraging to support the interpretation of the documents in the third part of the investigation
