# The construction and development of the social function of women by Soledad Acosta de Samper (1858-1881)

So far, Soledad Acosta de Samper is the best-known writer of the Colombian nineteenth century. She began to study her work with intensity since 1980. Due to the different facets in which she entered into the world of letters, as historian, journalist, writer, translator and editor, the work of Acosta de Samper and her intellectual career still have secrets and spaces that await a critical approach to continue building an intellectual network of the nineteenth century within the Colombian intellectual field. For this reason, this research constitutes a critical approach to her life and work, especially journalistic, traductological and literary, in order to evaluate the discursive strategies created by Acosta de Samper that allowed her to position herself in the intellectual and cultural field of the country, as well as to reevaluate the situation of the woman and propose a type of agency different from that defined by the patriarchal structures. .
