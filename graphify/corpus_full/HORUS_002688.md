# STUDY OF AIR- LACTIC FERMENTATION IN POLICIAL POWER DURING THE TERMS OF BOLIVAR MARY, COLOMBIA

The apicultural pollen is a superfood rich in essential nutrients such as vitamins, minerals, proteins, carbohydrates, lipids and phenolic compounds. However, its bioavailability is limited due to the presence of the exine, an external resistant layer that makes it difficult to absorb its nutrients.This study evaluated the acid-lactic fermentation of pollen harvested in the Montes de María (Bolívar, Colombia), using three bacterial cultures: Lactobacillus plantar ATCC 8014 (LP), ChoozitTM 82 LYO 50 DCU (CZT) and Yo-mix 885 LYO 50 DCU (YM).It was experimented with different proportions of water: pollen (1:1 and 2:1) and variables were measured as the fermentation time, pH, percentage of lactic acid and antioxidant activity.The results showed that the proportion of the lactic acid, increased chemical analysis, such as the determination of moisture, total solids, ash, minerals, lipids, proteins, carbohydrate and dietary fiber
