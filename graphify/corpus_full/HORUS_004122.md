# CONJUGATE MODELS: UNDERSTANDING THE CONJUGATE MODELS IN A BAYESIAN STATISTICS UNDERGRADUATE COURSE

Conjugate models are the first models studied in an undergraduate Bayesian statistics course. However, the limited class time only allows for the analysis of a few examples, restricting a deeper understanding of the concepts. For this reason, a Shiny application is proposed, enabling students to interact with discrete conjugate models in the Poisson and binomial cases, as well as normal conjugate models in the following scenarios: 1) unknown mean and known variance, 2) known mean and unknown variance, 3) both unknown mean and variance with independent prior distributions, and 4) both unknown mean and variance with a prior distribution of the mean conditioned on the variance. In any case, students can either simulate both the data distribution and the prior distribution by setting the corresponding parameter values or load a set of data associated with the distribution of interest and simulate the prior distribution by setting the parameter values
