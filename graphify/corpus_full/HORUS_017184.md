# NAANE, K+R+GA+, JABUAIENAN: ONTOGRAPHICAL TEST ON WORD, BODY AND TERRITORY

In this paper we put in relation three native notions of different ethnic origin: We will begin this journey with the naane term of the language of the Magütá (better known in academic literature as tikuna or ticuna), from, fundamentally, the research work of Abel Santos Angarita / Wãchiaükü, Percepto tikuna de Naane and Naüne: territory and body (2013), linguist and magütá researcher from the community of Arara in the Amazonian trapeze. We will continue with the notion of kirigai from the variable dialectal uitoto minika, spoken mainly in La Chorrera, Amazonas, which we will address from the auto-ethnographic and poetic work of Anastasia Candre Yamacuri, and from the contributions of Selnich Vivas, in the field of literary studies, and from Juan Álvaro Echeverri, in the field of anthropology.
