# RELIGIOUS CHANGE IN COLOMBIA IN THE 21st CENTURY: HYPOTHESIS OF WORK

The research Panorama of religious diversity in Colombia showed several processes of transformation of the Colombian religious field. Among them is the great decrease of Catholics, the growth of evangelicals/Pentecostals and the growth of believers but not affiliated to any religion. Our study investigates the causes that originated these transformation processes. The work consists in constructing research hypotheses based on quantitative and qualitative empirical information collected during the research Panorama of religious diversity in Colombia. It is argued, therefore, that these three religious changes are closely connected and that they must be understood in a broader context that takes into account general social changes, such as urbanization, globalization, greater access of citizens to formal education and the consolidation of an individualistic culture
