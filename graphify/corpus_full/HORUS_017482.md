# Study of thermoelectric properties in nanostructured systems: possible conditions for optimising efficiency.

This work studies and extends, by means of universality arguments, the thermal behavior within the Kondo regime, of the different thermoelectric properties such as: Thermopotence, electrical conductivity and thermal conductivity, within compressed semiconductor nanostructures. We use the variable variable-valued factor to determine the variable-valued factor. We consider, by means of the impurity model of Anderson SIAM, an idealized system composed of a quantum point immersed in a ballistic conduction channel (quantum threads). For this system, a linear mapping on which, the variable-rate coefficients of thermoelectric transport can be expressed, in terms of the variable-valued condition, the variable-level coefficients of the variable-level coefficients of the variable-level mode of the variable-level mode of the variable-level distribution. Thus, when evaluating this expression on the system of a single quantum point immersed between two quantum threads, it is found that, under these conditions, it is physically impossible to obtain a phase change, which allows maximising the thermal efficiency of this type of systems. Thus, a “improved” system composed of two non-interacting quantum points coupled to each other, but with strong electronic correlation at each quantum point, is studied by means of the atomic approach method.
