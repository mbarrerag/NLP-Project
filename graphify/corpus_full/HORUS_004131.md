# SOURCES FOR THE HISTORY OF WAR AGAINST PIJAOS INDIANS, 1602-1604

This book seeks to answer old questions and raise new questions about the war against the Pijaos Indians in the New Kingdom of Granada. Historiography has focused on the military offensive against the Pijaos led by the president of the Santafé Court of Appeal, Don Juan de Borja (1605-1628). The previous period is little known, as campaigns against the natives of the Magdalena Valley and the Central Cordillera were run by private individuals and were almost always fatal for Hispanic weapons. This book compiles minutes, requirements, commissions, lists and relations on the war against the Pijaos between 1602 and 1604 that allow us to understand the different forms of war in the Hispanic Monarchy during the early Modern Age
