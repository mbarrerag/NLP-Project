# CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF THE ESSENTIAL OILS OF PERUVIAN CALEA (ATERACEAE) AND LANTANA AFF. COLOMBIANA LOPEZ-PAL. (VERBENECEAE)

The extraction yields of essential oils were 0.01% (p/p) for both species. 27 compounds were detected in the species Calea peruviana and 52 compounds in Lantana aff. Colombian in relative quantities above 0.1 %. In the AE of Calea peruviana were identified as major components Germacrene D (13%), trans-ß-caryophyllene (8.7%), ¿curcumeno (6%) and ß-selineno (4.1%). In the species Lantana aff. Colombian were identified mostly lemonone (18%), ß-elemeno (10%), sabinene (5.6%) and trans-ß-caryophylline (8%)
