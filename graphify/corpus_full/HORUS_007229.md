# Assessment of accessibility to health centres in the urban area of the city of Villavicencio

This research aims to assess accessibility to health centres located in the urban area of the city of Villavicencio, taking into account the indicators of accessibility to health services established by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD). To this end, the basic information necessary for this purpose is collected from the population census, the geolocation of health care points together with the number of beds – stretchers and doctors available in each of them, and the travel speeds of the traffic network, is then analysed from a geostatistical point of view, to finally apply the methodology of the floating catchment area improved in two steps (named E2SFCA by its acronym in English). The results of the indicators obtained show that accessibility to beds – stretchers is sufficient, however, accessibility to doctors is considerably lower than established by the OECD. Performing a comparative exercise of the results with other intermediate cities in Colombia, it is possible to conclude that the behaviour is maintained, thus it is determined that it is necessary to strengthen the medical body of the city .
