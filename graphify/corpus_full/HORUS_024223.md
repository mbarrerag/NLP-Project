# Qualitative definition, spatial and constructive quality of rural housing in Villeta and its ways of living.

At present, rural housing in Colombia is resolved with spatial and constructive characteristics of the urban house. This final master's work takes a tour of the traditional peasant house of the last century developed with constructive techniques of each site, to propose a way of interpreting the ways of inhabiting the village of Masata, using materials of the place to generate greater spatiality at low cost. Nowdays, rural warming in Colombia is resolved with spatial and constructive characteristics of the urban house. This final master's theses takes a tour of the traditional pedestrian housing of the last century developed with construction technologies of each site, to propose a way of interpreting the modes of living in the village of La Masata, using local materials to generate great spatiality at low cost
