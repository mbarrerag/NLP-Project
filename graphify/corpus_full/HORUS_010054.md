# SIMULATION, ESTIMATION, AND GOODNESS FIT FOR THE STOCHASTIC BRIDGE

This presentation aims to study a comparative analysis of different stochastic bridges used in the modeling. The advantages and disadvantages of some types of stochastic bridges will be discussed, such as the classical Brownian bridge, the fractional Brownian bridge, and a-Brownian bridge motion, which are the solutions to the stochastic differential equations. For the fractional noise, we use the Cholesky method and the case of a-Brownian bridge, a classical stochastic differential equation that can be simulated using the Euler-Maruyama method. We implemented the estimation techniques for the model parameters used and analyzed the flexibility of the proposed model and its goodness of fit on the realizations. Finally, we present some applications for the proposed models for the stochastic bridge and their statistical analyses
