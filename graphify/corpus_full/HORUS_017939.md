Public diplomacy, in turn, is inscribed in the so-called Joseph Nye, soft power. Modern States denote the need to build their power, manage it and shape their international image, and disseminate positive perceptions that, as a result, can contribute to their political, economic and cultural interests (Mélisen 2005; Szondi, 2008).
