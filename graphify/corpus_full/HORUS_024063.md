# THE MAIN AGROECHOLOGIC STRUCTURE OF AGROECOSEMS: THEORICAL AND PRACTICAL PERSPECTIVES

The book presents the theoretical bases of the Agroecological Structure Index, the main basis of agroecosystems, based on the agrarian environmental conception (ecosystem relations culture). It describes the philosophical bases, origin and evolution of the concept, while explaining its theoretical foundations in terms of dissipative structure. It details the approaches and procedures of measurement of the index, which is composed of five ecosystem indicators (connectivity of farms with landscape, extension and diversity of external connectors and extension and diversity of internal connectors) and five cultural indicators (soil uses; agricultural management practices; soil conservation, water and biodiversity practices; perception-consciousness and capacity for action). It also presents some applications of the EAP as a descriptor of the major agroecosystem and in studies of climate variability, food autonomy, phytopathology, ecology of coprophagous beetles, relationships with human appropriation of net primary productivity and studies of landscape transformation and territorial ordering. It ends with a discussion on its multiple applications in the context of agroecology and agricultural environmental thinking
