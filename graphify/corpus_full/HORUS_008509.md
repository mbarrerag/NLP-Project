# DYNAMIC MODELATION METHODOLOGY AND CASES OF STUDY IN THE KALMAN RESEARCH RESEARCH SEMILL.

This study explains in detail the methodology for the modelling of dynamic processes developed in the Kalman Dynamic Process Research Group. This methodology, which allows the construction of semiphysical models of phenomenological base (MSBF), has been used both in the courses and in the Research Seed of the Kalman Group. Additionally, two concrete examples of the application of this methodology, developed by groups of students linked to the Seed. The experience of using this methodology in the Seed has allowed to show its applicability and ease of use. The methodology for building MSBF has been used by other researchers than those of the Kalman Group. Among the most relevant examples is the physiological modeling of the human body, among which is highlighted a model that describes the dynamics of glucose homeostasis in the human body, although there are already models of this process, but not with this approach and level of detail. In addition, a model describing the growth of the endometrium during the menstrual cycle is proposed, which is completely novel, since it is the first mathematical model that gives account of how the process is developed
