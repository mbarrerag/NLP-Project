# Social and health inequalities and inequity in the perioperatory process in a Bogotá, COLOMBIA and

In the area of health, different methodologies have been used for the measurement of social inequalities. However, there is difficulty in identifying in the literature variables that reflect inequity in the perioperative process. This phenomenon refers to the different aspects that encompass the individual and collectives' expertise in the process that they live before, during and after surgery or surgical procedure [1]. This process has been extensively investigated from a biomedical perspective, but the approach from public health has been scarce [2]
