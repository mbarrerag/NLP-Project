# QUIRURGICAL DISEASE: A PRACTICE BASED ON EVIDENCE?

The nursing discipline must implement the practice based on evidence and is a key aspect to improve the efficiency in nursing care and care to the person programmed for a surgical intervention. In addition, it strengthens surgical nursing and seeks to respond to the needs of clinical practice. On the other hand, health institutions, is concerned about training and research in the disciplinary areas and improving the care processes based on scientific evidence. However, despite the advances and efforts, there remains a gap between the contribution of research and the decisions that are made in practice. The nursing methodology based on evidence (EBE) was used taking into account, the following parameters: formulation of the question based on the problem, review of literature, critically evaluate, define recommendations, evaluate and conclusions. The clinical question is structured under the acronym PICO for this, the following question was posed: what are the nursing professional interventions in the surgical area? A bibliographical search was carried out in the databases of library Cochrane, virtual library of health and pubmed. The clinical question is structured under the acronym PICO for this, the following question was analyzed: what is especially the nursing intervention in the surgical area?
