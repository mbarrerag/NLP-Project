# Design and construction of physics laboratory practices for cycles 3, 4 and 5 of education.

The understanding and apprehension of concepts, theories and physical phenomena opens the opportunity to propose and develop proposals that contribute to facilitate and/or complement their study. The academic environment is dynamic, and it is this dynamic that allows to express that not everything is proposed in question tools that contribute to such understanding and apprehension. In this work, four laboratory practices are developed that allow to have a first approach to physical phenomena framed from the experimental point of view, including the use of appropriate technological tools for this purpose, as is the programming card Arduino®, and the programming software for free use, that has been developed for the use of basic sensors coupled to Arduino®
