# Special report on verification: opportunities for implementation leading to land conversion

The Technical Secretariat (ST) of the International Component for Verification of the Implementation of the Final Peace Agreement, made up of the Center for Research and Popular Education/Peace Programme (Cinep/PPP) and the Resource Center for Conflict Analysis (CERAC), is pleased to present its latest report, which gives an account of the opportunities for improvement identified to promote implementation and compliance with the provisions of the AF, especially those that are more backward and can generate transformative actions in the implementation of each of the AF points
