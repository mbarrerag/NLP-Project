# CULTURE COMPETITION OF INSURANCE STUDENTS IN PUBLIC UNIVERSITY, BOGOTÁ - COLOMBIA 2023

Cultural competence (CC) is an essential concept throughout the history for nurses, since daily they face challenges in the care for the great cultural diversity (DC) in their patients during the development of their work (1). However, developing the CC involves varied processes, of difficult adaptation and understanding (2). Some studies propose that the training time, including the knowledge provided about DC, contact with environments and experiences with different groups increases the CC (3). This research seeks to describe the level of CC in nursing students in a public university, Bogotá, Colombia. Study in progress, employed quantitative methodology, descriptive cross-sectional, and counted with approval of the ethics committee of the Faculty of Nursing, National University of Colombia. The CC CC was carried out between October 2022 and August 2023. The sample was 259 students active of the nursing undergraduate, older, who performed the study of 104% voluntarily, the sociodemographic and the cultural characteristics of the CC. The data collection was validated in Spanish (4), with the dimensions of documentation and search of information, awareness, behavior and cultural sensitivity. The results obtained in this study showed that the male gender has a higher CC than the female and the level of CC increases proportionally to the number of enrolments (3.5). Additionally, a higher level of CC is shown in relation to a study conducted in Korea; possibly influenced by the level of cultural awareness and sensitivity (6).
