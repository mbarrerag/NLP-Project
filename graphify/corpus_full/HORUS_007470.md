# Me first: Triage without delays, complications and humanized

To understand the meaning that patients who consult the emergency department give to the triage process. qualitative study with an ethno-graphic approach, the information was collected through semi-structured interviews and a field diary was kept. the participants go to the emergency department after trying to manage their situations through the resources that they have in their environment, not finding solutions, they go to the emergency department, where they want to be attended immediately, however, they find barriers that pressure them to Using strategies to achieve the solution of their needs in emergency care, throughout this process patients expect to be "first me" in care. the meaning that the patients give to triage is that they are the axis of care and therefore, they expect it to be without delay, without complications and that it be humanized
