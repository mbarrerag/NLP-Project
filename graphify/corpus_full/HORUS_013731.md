# Handcrafted bakery: a strategy for teaching macromolecules and metabolism in middle vocational school

In recent decades, teaching natural sciences in Colombia has been a challenge for public and private schools; the transformations of middle and basic education have forced teachers and institutions to rethink teaching methods, where the interests and skills of students are more involved, without neglecting the use of technology as a tool that facilitates processes. Biochemistry is a subject that is not included in almost any curriculum of secondary vocational education, although in all schools it is studied chemistry and biology, there is no space to see the contents that are involved in it, at least the most general ones. This is why a strategy of teaching metabolism and macromolecules was proposed from the elaboration of bread with mother mass. To this end, the research methodology participatory action was used, as a possibility to assume and evaluate the learning processes in a different way in the natural sciences, where the student is the protagonist of the process, and not an object of study that is represented in figures. ught, there is no space that allows transversely see the contents involved, at least the most general ones.
