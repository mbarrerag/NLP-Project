# EPISTEMOLOGICAL, EROTIC AND POLITICAL: A POETICAL SUBVERSION OF UNKNOWN

The epistemology that we care about from psychoanalysis is that it allows us to say the history of a subject, which appears in his own saying, in his writings, in his existence, not that which others say, but that which the subjects themselves say. “What is posed is not only a singular historization of what has happened, but a subversion against that past, a commitment to rewrite a story. There is always a story that is to come, that is not written forever, but can be rewritten, because history is not immovable, it is a subjective construction. This epistemology wants to show the loopholes, the gaps of a discourse that is intended to be totalitarian or of a unique and official truth. Therefore, it is to those gaps that we are going to address, because it is for them that the subject of the unconscious emerges
