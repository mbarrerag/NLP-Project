# Improvement of the physical and chemical behaviour of clays and micacean materials present in fine aggregates of poor quality for use in the production of hydraulic concrete.

At present, the high consumption of sand in hydraulic concrete constitutes a problem of sustainability and performance for the construction, since the sources of good quality aggregates become scarcer with the passing of the years, considering that it is the material most exploited by man after the water. In order to address options of use of sands with presence of harmful minerals, in this research has proposed the improvement by the use of two molecules based on technology of polyelectric polymers called experimental 1931-1 and 1831-5. For these polymers the retention of flow in presence of sands of poor quality and has been compared its performance with a conventional polycarboxylate (EXP 3457), in doses ranging from 0.35% to 0.60% regarding the weight of the cementant. A factorial design of experiments for concrete mixtures with a content of sand of 25% to 40% and the results of the concrete added capacity (Cordobita and Cogua) with different types of harmful minerals like corrugating quartz, arcolite, micas and oxides that were characterized by the oilgraphy, absorption of concrete and FRX.
