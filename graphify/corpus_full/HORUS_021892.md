# Options for decarbonization of land transport in cities in Colombia: scenarios based on a system dynamic model.

The climate crisis and poor quality of urban air have put the decarbonization of the transport sector on the agenda of most governments alone. However, the decarbonization of transport is generally addressed from the substitution of vehicle technologies, leaving aside the potential of the modal changes in the road to the reduction of emissions. This work aims to evaluate possible ways of decarbonizing urban land transport for Bogotá D.C. and the Metropolitan Area of the Aborra Valley -two regions of Colombia, a developing country - including the analysis of the technological change in vehicles and the dynamics of the journeys.To this end, a simulation model using System Dynamics and integrating elements of the dissemination model of Bass and models of discreet choice.The results indicate that under the current conditions of around one third of the total vehicles of Bogotá and of the AMVA could be electric vehicles by 2050, percentage of the market that although it would avoid a total of 57 Mt of CO2eq and 6 t of PM between the two regions until 2050, it is low considering the increase of the number of vehicles and the slow renewal of the old fleet of vehicles of vehicles suggest internal combustion vehicles
