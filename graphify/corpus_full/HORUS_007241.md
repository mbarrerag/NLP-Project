# Development of the fused filament manufacture, debinding, and syntering processes for the acquisition of a WC-10 Co hardmetal

This research explores the application of the technology of manufacturing cast filaments (FFF) for the 3D printing of hard metals WC-10Co, focusing on the development of formulations of polymeric systems that incorporate polypropylene grafted with maleic anhydride, thermoplastic elastomers and additives as paraffin wax and stearic acid.The study adjusts the process variables through the phases of printing, thermal depolymerization and sintering.Preliminary experiments were carried out to standardize variables, including the maintenance of 48% dust volume in the raw material developed and using ready-to-press industrial grade powders.After experimenting with the final printing variables, relative densities in green up to 99.99% were achieved while controlling the microstructure of the sintered hard metals, such as the vacuum and a mixture of 75% H2 + 25% N2 hard metals, allowing effective control of the residual carbon of the polymer chains during the thermal depolymerization. eic anhydride, thermoplastic elastomers, and additive like wax and stearic acid.
