# LOSS OF VEGETAL COBERTURE TO THE CAUSES OF MINERIA TO OPEN HEIGHT IN THE NORDEST AND UNDER ANTI-QUENE CAUCA, COLOMBIA

The Subregions of the Northeast and Lower Cauca, of the Department of Antioquia, Colombia, have been characterized by extensive agricultural and mining exploitation, concentrating the latter on the exploitation of open-pit minerals, which, although of great importance for the development of the region and the country, contrasts with the poor environmental management, loss of biodiversity, social gap, violence and inequality present in the territory. Due to the above, the transformation in terms of magnitude and spatial distribution of the areas of exploitation and coverage of the region's land, should be recognized; in order to determine the transformations of this region and its implications for the socio-environmental dynamics, the present work aims to perform a temporal analysis of the main transformations generated by open-pit mining, in an area of interest, from 1980 to 2020, since this period collects important changes in the dynamics of the territory; to this end, satellite images were analyzed, CLAB indices of the built area and spatial analysis, in order to understand the spatial dynamics, the changes of these areas and the loss of plant coverage through the territory
