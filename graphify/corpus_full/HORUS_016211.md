# Massive multiplication of iraca palm (Carludovica palmata Ruiz & Pav) by an automated time dive system

Carludovica palmata is a neotropical plant with economic potential for the food, pharmaceutical, agrochemical industry and an important source of fibers for the elaboration of handicrafts. Colombia, is one of the countries with the highest production of this species, however, does not have efficient techniques for its multiplication. This research developed a biotechnological method for large scale multiplication of this plant. The method consisted in designing, constructing and implementing an Automatic Temporal Immersion System (SITA) type BIT. The technical parameters of the SITA, mean volume, immersion times, initial immersion frequencies and inoculum were evaluated. The mean MS plus 1.0 mg L-1 of BAP, 0.5 mg L-1 of ANA and 20 g L-1 of sucrose were used. The experimental design used was subdivided plots, with 2 repetitions. The variables evaluated were, number and length of shoots, number and length of roots, number and number of leaves and biomass.
