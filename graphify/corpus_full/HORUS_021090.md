# Strategic Alliance for the Strengthening of the Productive Chain of Passionflowers and Avocado in Arauca Phase I: Biological Control and Chemical Characterization

The strategic alliances interdisciplinary are the best tool to achieve tangible results that imply the interaction between basic and applied science. The project's proposing team, consisting of researchers from different disciplines such as chemistry, biochemistry, engineering and biology, will work towards the strengthening of cooperation strategies between laboratories, headquarters and faculties seeking to generate and share knowledge, and to optimize resources with the aim of generating solutions to problems of economic importance and in areas of influence of our alma mater. Arauca is one of the departments of the country with mining, livestock and agricultural wealth, with land suitable for different crops, many of which have unique organoleptic characteristics within which the avocado and the Pasifloras are highlighted, which makes them raw materials suitable for the manufacture of value-added products desired in the food and cosmetic industries among others. However, despite the quality of the fresh product of these productive chains, the quantity and quality of the fruits are diminished by the attack of pathogens in pre and post harvest.
