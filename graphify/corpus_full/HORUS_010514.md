# THE HOUSE AS THE ELEMENT OF ARCHITECTURE: A STORY OF THEIR DISPLACEMENTS

For the call Architecture and community, here is presented a review of the home as a fundamental element of architecture, as well as of the changes and displacements that has suffered in its long history. In the treatise of Vitruvio, a collective fire is the origin of architecture and its raison d’être. However, for Leon Battista Alberti the origin of architecture, and also its main element, is rather division or partition. Since then you can see a certain retreat of the home or, at least, a retreat of the social part that is housed in its original sense. Starting from the theory of Gottfried Semper in the nineteenth century, you can observe a resurgence of this element and its social sense, which is attempted to trace in projects and images of modern architecture
