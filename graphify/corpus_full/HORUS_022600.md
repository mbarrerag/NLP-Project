# Method of segmentation of images of the prostate taken by magnetic resonance imaging using artificial intelligence techniques.

The segmentation of the prostate in magnetic resonance imaging is considered an essential task for surgical planning, as well as the determination of disease stages such as prostate cancer and benign prostatic hyperplasia. However, the lack of standardization in imaging acquisition protocols and heterogeneity between individuals makes this a difficult task. In order to bring to the solution of this problem, a convolutional network architecture in the form of 3D Unet is proposed, which is characterized by greater depth, besides having a coding phase – decoding with a attention gate. This proposal unlike others implements a residual block similar to that of Resnet101 with batch standardization. In addition, it uses a loss function composed of the Dice coefficient and cross entropy to handle the problem of class imbalance. During the inference stage each image is divided into smaller images and individual predictions are generated, these are eventually joined to generate a prediction mask of the same size of the input image.
