# From waste to material. "From debris to recycled concrete"

The present Thesis gives a different look on an object already worked in the academic and professional field, as is the confection of a concrete or concrete recycled through the recycling of rubble. The difference is that this time it is not addressed from a postgraduate in Science of Materials or in Construction, but from the Human and Social Sciences, since it wants to give importance to the importance of the transition of the residue to a new material from the cultural, social and political, as areas inherent to the becoming of the human being. Therefore, in the text the physical-chemical and mechanical aspects of the material, those that speak of their optimal material and normative performance open, strengthen and give relevance to the analysis, of the properly objectual, cultural and political administrative aesthetics.
