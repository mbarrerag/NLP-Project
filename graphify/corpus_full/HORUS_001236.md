# Bibliometric analysis of the articles published in the Revista ‘Acta Odontológica Colombiana’ during the period 2012-2022

To evaluate the bibliometric profile of the Revista Acta Odontológica Colombiana based on the index h5 and the characteristics of the research articles published in the period 2012-2022. bibliometrics were performed from 190 articles, which were classified by volume, number and year. After examining their abstracts and titles were categorized by: knowledge area (18 categories), type of study (14 types) and country of origin of the researchers. The index h5 was obtained from Google Scholar analytics and results of Pubendex calls, data that were processed by the software Publish orish. Descriptive statistics were used for data analysis. the most published type of study was the cross-sectional (analytic) cut with 22.6%. The design of cohorts was only used in 0.5% of the articles and no publication corresponded to the survey types of review (wide review).
