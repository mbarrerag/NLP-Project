# POSIBILITIES AND LIMITS OF UNIVERSITY CULTURE EXTENSION, 1935-1954: UNIVERSITY REVISATIONS AND EMISOTES IN COLOMBIA

This article investigates the use of means of communication for the development of activities of cultural extension that gave the University of Antioquia and the Universidad Pontificia Bolivariana between 1935 and 1953. Through the crtica of documentary sources it is described and analyzed as both institutions, following references of the national and international ambitos, developed in these strategies such as the publication of magazines and the creation of stations that became important tools to extend and amplify the tasks carried out within their respective cloisters acad micos.
