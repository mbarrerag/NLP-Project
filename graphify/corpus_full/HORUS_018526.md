# Recent advances in the imaging diagnosis of hydrocephalus in children. Review of literature for the past six years

Abstract Hydrocephalus is a frequent health problem in pediatrics, particularly during the first month of life. The incidence in Latin America is one of the highest in the world. In Colombia there are no representative data on the real incidence of this disease. Recent findings related to the dynamics of cerebrospinal fluid allowed to propose new models on the physiopathology of hydrocephalus that, together with the findings in Magnetic Resonance, have led to a better understanding of the disease. The aim of this article is to carry out a review of the information available in the literature on the advances in the physiopathology of the disease and the findings in neuroimage, in addition to carrying out a brief review on the role of these in the diagnosis and follow-up of patients. A bibliographic review was carried out with terms MeSH, in the databases of PUBMED, OVID and SCOPUS with articles published in the last 6 years, selected a total of 30 articles that addressed the topic in an integral way.
