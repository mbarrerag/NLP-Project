# Tropical Socio-ecological Systems (SSET) of the Island and Continental Caribbean: Biocultural Diversity and Sustainability

The project is oriented to the study of the biocultural region of the Insular Caribbean and the continental Caribbean, from the integrating perspective of the socio-ecological systems with emphasis on its sustainability, based on its basic characterization, and based on the knowledge developed and the capacity installed at the Caribbean Headquarters (San Andrés, Santa Marta), in dialogue with other National Presence Headquarters of the National University of Colombia. The project starts from the budget that the regions of the country should understand in biocultural and socio-ecological terms, insofar as in each of them there is a diversity of local societies and cultures that have occupied and made a historical and contemporary use of a diversity of types of tropical ecosystems (sensu Dinnerstein et al, 1995), the totality of which (terrestrials and of continental and marine waters) are represented in the Caribbean region of Colombia (Márquez, 2008). Thus, in the island and continental Caribbean, the most diverse and integrated populations, the indigenous and peasant populations, have established a diversity of practices, knowledge and relations with ecosystems, such as coral reefs, which propose the traditional fishing, and the rural forests, very transformed by different components. They may be interested in a process of formation of this nature for their members (for example, the Armed Forces or officials of the Foreign Ministry); national and international students and academics, with emphasis on the Caribbean and Central America. Finally, the fourth component of the project is linked to the processes of social appropriation of knowledge and communication of scientific knowledge, in dialogue with other knowledge. This will be done through a research-creation exercise that aims at co-creation of a play of theatre, music and dance based on the exercises of synthesis carried out. This work is carried out as a commitment to strengthen the communication of scientific knowledge through the performing arts, which has been proposed as one of the effective and innovative strategies to bridge the gaps between the knowledge produced by the academy and other types of knowledge, contributing to the social appropriation of these. Thus, the project links, through its components, the different missionary axes of the National University of Colombia, including teaching, research-creation, extension, and internationalization, in accordance with the nature of the call, which must also generate their own resources and sustainability, and enhance the attractiveness of the existing post-graduate programmes of the country to attract other regions of the world students
