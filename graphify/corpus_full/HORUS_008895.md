# VERTEBRAL VOICE OBSERVANT OBSERVANT OBSERVER IN CANINE.

The estimated incidence of malignant bone tumors is 5.5 per 10,000 individuals, of this group, osteosarcoma represents about 85% of cases. Osteosarcomas, are classified according to their origin in central, periosteal and paraosteal, the most common frequency of presentation is in the appendical skeleton and in a lesser proportion the axial skeleton; aspects that have both orthopedic and clinical repercussions. to report the case of a periosteal osteoblastic osteosarcoma in thoracic vertebrates with medullary invasion. canine, 7 years, Pastor Belga, with signs of torticolis, progressive paresis and evidence of mass on the cervical and thoracic region. Given the poor prognosis, it is decided to perform euthanasia and necropsy studies. an organisal mass in the dorsolateral region of the musculature, arranged on the first vertebrate toracic T2-T5; approximately 18 x 10 cm; at the presence of the mecapal canaldal origin and compression of the spinal cord. This is a rare presentation, documenting these cases is necessary to contribute to the identification of their prevalence, as well as individual contribution of the biological behavior of this neoplasia when they affect the neck region in canines
