# INTELLIGENT SEMANCES WITH SOLAR PANELS IN THE CITY OF MANIZALS, COLOMBIA

This document proposes the implementation of smart traffic lights powered by solar energy in the city of Manizales analyzing its viability and benefits. The project aims to promote a sustainable energy source, reduce road accidents and generate economic savings for the city. The study is based on the collection and analysis of data from the geographic distribution in the city, the rate of the population benefited, rates of road accidents, the number of traffic lights per commune and the costs associated with implementation.
