# COLOMBIA STORY. WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

From the Indian or colonial period (16th century to the beginning of the 19th century) to the consolidation of the Republic (19th to 21st centuries), the country has experienced intense experiences of population growth, economic development, political negotiations and cultural expressions. Colombian history: what you need to know seeks to offer Colombians and foreigners, especially the younger ones, the opportunity to learn a different and playful version of the national past complex. Through short-term content based on the most recent debates on each of the topics, the book will allow them to get a general idea of the times and events that shaped the country
