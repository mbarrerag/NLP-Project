# Acquisition and processing of multispectral images focused on color reproduction and correction in controlled lighting environments

This thesis presents the validation of the operation and calibration of a new version of a multispectral imaging system using a wide spectrum camera sensor (400-1000nm), and LED-based lighting technology of narrow bandwidth within the visible spectrum (410,650nm).This version consists of a monochromatic camera of wide spectrum that was adapted to several lenses to perform different shots and tests.It has a lighting environment controlled with the form of dark and closed octagonal box.In it there are four modules, each with 8 trios of high power LEDs with narrow bandwidth in various wavelengths, which include 7 within the visible spectrum and 1 in the near infrared that takes the range of 410-850nm.The purpose of this thesis is to present a multispectral system to acquire the intrinsic data of the images in order to measure their accuracy and accuracy. This analysis presents the validation of the operation and calibration of a new version of a multi-sectoral image system using a wide spectrum (400-1000nm) camera sensor, and narrow bandwidth LED based illumination technology without the visible spectrum (410,650nm). This version consists of a broad spectrum monochrome camera that was adapted to various Lenses for different challenges and tests. It has a controlled illumination environment in the shape of dark, closed octagonal box. Inside it are four modules, each present present 8 trios of narrow to branch branch shift power LEDs of differential colour changenths (7 with the visible spectrum and 1 in the nearby) take the range 410-850nm. The purpose structure is to measure against the potential. optimized to correct errors and adjust the color with respect to the reference agreement to the Colorimetry Standard (CIE). For this purpose, linear and nonlinear regression technologies and a neural network are applied. The system's ability to reproduce color from spectral images results in a color distance of 23.74 Delta E and 40.45 Delta E when using the first version: Multispectral Crown and the second version: Dome Multispectral, respectfully.
