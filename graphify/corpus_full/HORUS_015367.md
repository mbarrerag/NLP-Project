# MODEL FOR THE SECRE-CONSOLIDATION CALULATION FOR THE LABESTRY SOUNDS OF THE CITY OF BOGOTÁ.

The secondary compression behaviour of the soft soils of the city of Bogotá is present. This was done due to the history of constructions with excessive settlements, in the soft soils of the city during the last forty years (see Figure 1). Several studies have shown that the city soil contains unusually high concentrations of diatoms (see Figure 2) [1; 2]. As results of studies there are soft soils with high ratio of voids e0, high compressibility rates CC. The stationary piston method was used to obtain samples at depths of between five and one hundred meters. Subsequently, the samples were examined in the laboratory, subjected to triaxial compression and edometer tests, and also tested the limits of Atterberg and the grain size distributions. The results of the tests for undisturbed soil showed very high liquid limit values, CC compressibility indices, C.D. coefficients of compression (see Figure 3). Some samples showed mixtures of particles of different sizes, mineralogy and diatomous concentrations.
