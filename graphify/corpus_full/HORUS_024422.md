# VALIDATION OF THE SOLAR ERARIA RADIATION OF ERA5-LAND IN COLOMBIA

This work performs a validation of solar radiation from the ERA5-Land Reanalysis of Copernicus' Climate Change Service, which has a 0.1° and 1 h time spatial resolution, and a global planetary coverage since 1981. To this end, solar radiation time series (between 6 am and 7 pm) were used from 117 IDEAM stations during the period 1997 to 2019, mostly located in the Andean and Caribbean regions. Two error metrics were used: bias (BE) and percentage error (PE), calculated for each hour and site. The results of both metrics show high dispersion and significant magnitudes. At noon, the average bias error (MBE) has a median order of 60 W/m2, and the average percentage error (MPE) of 35%. MBE and MPE show a spatial predominance of positive values at all hours of the day.
