# A bibliometric analysis of occupational health and welfare programmes: main research perspectives.

The implementation of Health and Work Welfare Programs aims to improve the health and welfare conditions of employees, reduce absenteeism and in turn positively impact productivity in organizations; however, a bibliographic analysis has not been shown that has enabled the academic community to identify and analyze the research prospects of the Health and Work Welfare Programs by applying the methodology of the Tree of Science (ToS). To cover this aspect, the articles published from 2001 to 2020 in the Web of Science database, building the ToS of the Health and Work Welfare programs, identifying and analyzing the three main research perspectives: health promotion and prevention, mental stress intervention and burnout prevention, and prevention of the effects of obesity through physical activity, are reviewed. Considering that the literature identified in this review has been generated in a greater proportion in the United States and the United Kingdom, it is recommended that future research be carried out in our country to serve as an academic reference for Colombian organizations to strengthen the management of the health and job well-being of their human talent
