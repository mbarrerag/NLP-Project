# Methodological proposal for the identification and analysis of sections of accident concentration on national roads granted: Case of corridor Los Patios - Guasca

In the first-order national corridors, there is currently an absence in updated methodological tools and that they are coupled to the reality of the projects and their requirements, that allow to identify and analyze those sectors in which siniestrality is concentrated generating human, material and economic losses for the users of these corridors. Likewise, taking into account that these projects have several governance criteria of several entities that exercise surveillance and control over the corridors, this work proposes a method that manages to satisfy those requirements. Given this work, a review is carried out in the present work to the existing methodologies at national and international level, where the relevance is reviewed and the most favorable aspects are extracted, so that a single methodology is established for identifying and analysing sections of concentration of siniestrality, complying with the contractual and control obligations to which the road managers are subject, especially focused on road dealers, proposing additionally from the source, the fying and analyzing segments with concentrated accidents, while complying with the contractual and oversight obligations imposed on road administrators, especially road concessions. Additionally, it outlines guidelines that established preliminary and actions to mitigate incidents at the identified points. For this purpose, the methodology is exemplified through its application in the Los Patios – Guasca corrador
