# -LONG-MEETRATION ARTISTIC CREATION GRUPE

The artistic training in any field always requires an inherent link with the practice and professionalization of a specific trade. It is just basic premise, it was the seed that motivated the School of Cinema and Television of the National University to seek for years models of transgressor production that would allow its students to understand the audiovisual realization from a much broader spectrum. Thanks to that, 2020 was witness to the great success of Un Tal Alonso Quijano, a fictional feature film made by more than 80 students of the school and directed by a teacher. Now, as a research group we plan to expand our creative horizons in a new film called "Coraje". This time we are proposing an academic project of research creation in which from the doctoral thesis of Libya Stella Gomez (director of the film and associate professor of the university), we carry out an audiovisual project in which from the possibilities of research we create a aesthetic proposal from various points of view of which will result a long film. The film is a reflection realized from the academy on the violent events recently occurred in the social manifestations, many of them with a wide participation of the university community.
