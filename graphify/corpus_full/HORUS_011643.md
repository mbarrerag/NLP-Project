# INFLUENCE OF PROMOTIONAL WARRANCES IN THE DECISIONS ON THE COMPANY OF CONSUMERS OF DETERGENCIES IN SHOP ONLINE: EXPERIMENTAL CONTRIBUTION IN THE COVID-19 CANAL

The covid-19 pandemic caused changes in consumption habits, one of the main ones being the digitization of the purchase, since the confinements forced the supply in online stores, as well as changes in the way of buying products and receiving promotions and incentives. For this reason, it is important to study how the most used and preferred promotional frameworks influence the time of making online purchases. Thus, two experimental exercises were carried out to analyze preferences in relation to different types of product promotions in the category laundry detergents, for which 61 people were gathered in a group of four sales promotions, analyzing their intention to buy. The results showed that promotional profit frameworks have incremental effects on the intention of buying, showing heuristics of availability in the decision process, in addition to those that included gifts or additions to the product. These findings will allow the brands to better organize their online promotional strategy and to have better agreement with the online buying habits in the pandemic by covid-19, besides expanding the theoretical knowledge in the process of buying decisions. The exercise could be extended to other types of promotion and convenience categories not covered in the present study
