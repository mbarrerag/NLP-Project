# FERRUGEM BRANCA DO GIRASSOL (HELIANTHUS ANNUUS) EM ANTIOCIA, COLÔMBIA

The sunflower (Helianthus annuus L.) is a plant belonging to the family Asteraceae, of great economic importance due to its high use in the production of oils, cosmetic and ornamental industry. One of the diseases of negative impact in the production of the sunflower is the false white rust, caused by Pustula helianthicola C. Rost & Thines (Oomycota, Albuginaceae), producing losses of up to 80% of the plants due to the rupture of the stem. The first report of this disease was made in South Africa extending itself to the other continents excepting in Antarctica. The objective of this work is to characterize morphologically the false white rust of the sunflower. The samples of Girasol parasite were collected in the corregimiento of Santa Elena, municipality of Medellín-Colombia. The sample was studied in the Microcological Museum of the National University of Colombia (MMUNM).
