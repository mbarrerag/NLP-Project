# Water as a means of learning from the BADs 5 and 7 of natural sciences in grade 4 in primary school.

Environmental problems are a key factor that must be addressed from different scenarios, especially in the field of education. There, the relationships between teachers, students and administrative bodies play a fundamental role in addressing concepts related to environmental awareness. This work adapted and implemented an educational booklet in order to strengthen the learning of DBAs 5 and 7, in which various activities focused on the preservation and conservation of the environment and especially water care are applied. The general objective of the work was to develop a booklet with the environmental management of water taking into account the DBA 5 and 7 of natural sciences of grade 4 of primary school of the San Gerardo María Mayela Educational Institution. The methodology used was qualitatively cut with a descriptive scope. Among the results found, little environmental awareness was recognized regarding water care. It was concluded that this strategy strengthened environmental awareness and progress was evident in perspective of the evidence of learning for DBAs 5 and 7 in grade 4
