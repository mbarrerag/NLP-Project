# SUSTAINABILITY OR FOOD SUstenTIBILITY: RELATIONS AND CONCEPTUAL AFFAIRS

This chapter explores historical, social, political and environmental aspects related to food sustainability, and provides elements for its conceptualization and use as a relevant approach to addressing the multiple challenges posed by the relations between humanity and the environment around food
