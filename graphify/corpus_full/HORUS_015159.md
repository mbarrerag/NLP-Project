# «Forming a field means inventing a tradition»

In this interview, Professor Olga Restrepo Forero talks about the origins, development, particularities and perspectives of social studies of science and technology (ESCT) in Colombia. According to her, this field in Colombia has been characterized by a cosmopolitan orientation; in this sense, her history in Colombia has been characterized by ambivalence in comparison to tradition. Restrepo Forero explains that the origins and the process of institutionalization of the field in the country did not necessarily imply ruptures or continuities with previous schools of thought. Key milestones in its development are addressed, in which she highlights the importance of editorial projects that have served, both to make the field known in other academic spaces, and to attract scholars with sensitivities close to science and technology studies. In reflecting on the future of the field in Colombia, she notes that, to a large extent, the new generation of students seeks, not only to understand the phenomena associated with the development of science and technology, but also to have a vocation to change the state of things in the world
