# CHARACTERISATION AND FISHICAL CHIMICAL CHARACTERISATION OF CIRCON TO PUT ON ALUVIAL AURIFERARY MINERIA RELAVES

Circon is a mineral belonging to the Nesosilicates group, frequently associated with igneous rocks. Due to its extreme hardness and chemical stability, it is also present in sedimentary deposits and is a common constituent of alluvial sands. In its natural form, the circus can be used as a gemstone, for which it must meet demanding morphological and chemical requirements. Other applications of this important mineral are found in the nuclear industry, cosmetics, paints and coatings, paper, catalysis, explosives and gas purification. In this work the results of recovery of circon from the black sands present in the tails of an alluvial auriferous exploitation in the North-East Anti-Oqueño, which offer a high potential to be exploited as a source of circon. The recovery process applied to the black sands included stages of granulometric classification, magnetic concentration of high intensity and electrostatic separation, producing a total recovery of 84% in the non-magnetic product.
