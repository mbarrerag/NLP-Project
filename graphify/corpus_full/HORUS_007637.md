# Recognition of Ethnomycology Associated with the Mitú-Cachivera Community

This project focuses on the recognition of the fungi present in the vicinity of the Cachivera indigenous community in Mitú, Vaupés. Through an ethnomycological approach, the ancestral knowledge of the community on fungi, used in its medicinal, food and cultural practices, will be documented. This research, carried out in a region little explored by the Colombian Amazon, seeks not only to preserve this valuable traditional knowledge, but also to generate scientific information that strengthens the SIPAF Research Group (Agricultural and Forestry Production Systems), expanding its field of study to innovative areas. During the development of the project, data on the fungi species present in the region are collected and analyzed, identifying their use and cultural value. To later produce audiovisual content that document this process and will serve as educational resources for the community and as tools for scientific dissemination. These materials will also facilitate future research and collaborations, contributing to the knowledge of biodiversity and enhancing the application of fungi in the development of indigenous and sustainable products at the national level.
