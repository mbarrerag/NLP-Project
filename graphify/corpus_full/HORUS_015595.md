# PRACTICE COMMUNITIES IN THE SMES IN THE TOURIST SECTOR OF THE CALL DEPARTMENT

In the context of knowledge management, it is necessary to develop intellectual capital, for which variables such as communities of practice should be considered, given their importance for the development of organizations. The results of the research presented in this article aim to establish the state of development of communities of practice related to intellectual capital in SMEs in the tourism sector of the Department of Caldas, Colombia, for which a descriptive and correlational analysis was used with an evaluation model adapted to specific communities, being possible to evaluate 87% of SMEs. The main findings show the consolidation of the communities of practice of SME operators in the tourism sector in the department of Caldas, and highlight their leadership and teamwork
