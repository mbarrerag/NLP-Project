# Drug management of Dry Eye Syndrome in post-surgical patients with refractive surgery in an ophthalmological center in Bogotá during 2019.

This is a pharmacoepidemiology study about the prescription patterns of ocular lubricants in patients undergoing refractive surgery in an ophthalmological center of Bogotá during 2019. Describe the pharmacological therapy and the prescription patterns of ocular lubricants in post-surgical patients of refractive surgery. Specific objectives: Clinical and demographic characteristics of the study population, evaluate the frequency of prescription of ocular lubricants and contrast it with clinical practice guidelines. A descriptive observational study of cross-section was performed with retrospective collection of information. This study described 3 groups of variables: Demographic, clinical and pharmacological. Demographicly, 61.2% of the study population corresponded to the female gender, while the lowest percentage of the female gender was found between 18 and 20 years. As for the occupational analysis of the population, the highest percentage of patients intervened was located in the etarian group of 24 to 36 years for female gender and 30 to 32 years for male. Clinical practice guide analyzed. There are elements such as occupation, gender, age and even the type of refractive procedure influencing the diagnosis of post-surgical dry eye. The highest percentage of individuals who underwent dry eye symptoms and who were treated corresponded to the female gender, which correlates with the literature consulted. Cellulose derivatives stand out as the most prescribed active principles of eye lubricants both in post-operative and evolutionary phases. However, in professional practice, the diagnosis of dry eye in young population tends to be underestimated. There is diversity in the prescription patterns of lubricants at the ophthalmic level, which can be based on the recommendations of scaling clinical practice guidelines; although in practice, the managements do not tend to be rigorously carried out
