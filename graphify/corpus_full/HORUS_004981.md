# EXPERIMENTAL STUDY OF THE MECHANICAL COMPORTMENT OF INTERRED TUBERS SUBJECTED TO CYCLES OF TERMS

This paper presents an experimental study on the mechanical behaviour of buried pipes subjected to thermal load cycles. First, efforts and deformations are measured experimentally on a buried pipe subjected to monotonic and cyclic thermal loads, thus reproducing the typical operating conditions of buried pipe systems. Subsequently, a numerical model is elaborated using the finite element method, which was validated using experimental results obtained. Next, this computational model was used to carry out a parametric study of the influence of the mechanical properties of the soil on the state of effort of the system.
