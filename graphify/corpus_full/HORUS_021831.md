# Model of architectural evolution of monolith to microservices for the ISys information system of the National Admissions Directorate of the National University of Colombia.

The final work presents the case study of the evolution of an information system with monolithic architecture towards microservices. The work carried out is based on the creation, implementation and validation of a model of evolution that arises from the context of the system identified by the detailed architectural analysis and in consideration of the technical and operational requirements of the National Admissions Directorate of the National University of Colombia. The evolution model allows to prioritize the modules of the system through the analysis of ascending dependencies and the expected positive impact of evolution, and presents the adaptations required to three architectural patterns used as the main strategy for evolution in an iterative and incremental way, without the need to interrupt partially or totally the operation of the system or dependence
