# Relationships between coral reef complexity and ecosystem services at Caribbean oceanic islands, Seaflower Biosphere Reserve, Colombia

The coral reef ecosystems provide ecosystem services, which are essential for the well-being of people and biodiversity in the ocean islands, such as food supply and coastal protection. However, these ecosystems have been affected by various factors, with significant declines in coral coverage and its ecological functions. This research explores the relationship between the complexity of coral reefs, their capacity to offer SE and their ecological, biological and socio-economic importance, in the Island of San Andrés-SAI, as science-based arguments to encourage investment in their restoration and protection as integral components of natural infrastructure of islands and Nature-based Solutions. It focuses on SE as a refuge for biodiversity for fish, coastal protection, ecological facilitation and tourism; and its relationship with structural complexity and health of reefs.
