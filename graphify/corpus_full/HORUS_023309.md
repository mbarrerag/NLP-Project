# OF THE PEOPLE TO URBIA, THE ROLE OF THE OSTEOLOGICAL COLLECTION OF THE CENTRAL CEMENTARY OF BOGOTA IN RECONSTRUCTION OF THE STORY OF CITY AND COLOMBIA TO THE FINAL OF THE XIXth CENTURY AND PRINCIPLES OF THE XXTH

Founded on August 6, 1538, Bogotá celebrated its fourth centenary by making efforts to position itself as the Capital of the Republic of Colombia. The end of the 19th century and the beginning of the 20th century was constituted in a period of great population growth, which just towards the end, was accompanied by an increase in infrastructure.The descriptions found in the literature on the life of the inhabitants of the city are overwhelming, by revealing marginal living conditions for the most impoverished capitals.The osteological collection resulting from the archaeological excavations that accompanied the construction of the Memory, Peace and Reconciliation Center on premises of what was part of the Central Cemetery of Bogotá has allowed to contribute to the reconstruction of the history of the most dispossessed population of that period, by corresponding to individuals who were inhumed in what was once called the Cemetery of the Poor.The analyses carried out threw out an NMI of 7103 individuals, however, the majority, 5357, correspond to individuals belonging to miscellaneous groups, sometimes represented by very few elements of bio-anatomous studies; and 1746 individuals are those individuals who have been able to preserve them
