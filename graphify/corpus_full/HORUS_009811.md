# IMPROVING PASSWORD SECURITY WITH FOUR SIMPLE QUESTIONS AND FEEDBACK

People believe that more memorable passwords are less secure. Here we tested whether a four-items questionnaire with a short explanation regarding factors affecting password security could limit the effect of such belief. Participants read passwords and rated their memorability and perceived security. Some participants answered the questionnaire before the rating task (with or without feedback), and others after. The questionnaire with feedback reduced the effect of the belief and prompted the selection of more secure and memorable passwords. In conclusion, a simple four-item questionnaire with feedback can improve password security
