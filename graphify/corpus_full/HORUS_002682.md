# SYNTHESIS OF METALORGANIC NETWORKS OF ZR AND ZR-CE BY THE METHOD OF MICROONDS

The mechanical networks (MOF, by its acronym in English) have generated great interest due to their unique structure, composed of metallic centers linked to organic bindings. [1], [2] This configuration allows to explore various combinations and design materials with specific properties, which has proven to be promising for its use, for example, as catalysts in CO2 transformation reactions.[3] Traditionally, these solids are synthesized by means of solvathermic methods that require high temperatures and pressures to form crystals, this implies long reaction times, which can be costly and impractical.
