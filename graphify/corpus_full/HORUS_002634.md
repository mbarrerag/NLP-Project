# CRITICAL REFLECTIONS ON THE STORY OF THE COLOMBIA PHONOAUDIOLOGY: LIGHTS, SUMBRATIONS, PROJECTIONS AND SIGNATURES OF THE DECALUSTRO

This chapter aims to present a historical-critical reflection of phonoaudiology, in the key of collective health, based on scientific logics that facilitated the emergence and consolidation of the professions of [re]habilitation, among them the one initially called: `Language Therapy' and that eventually acquired the denomination of phonoaudiology.
