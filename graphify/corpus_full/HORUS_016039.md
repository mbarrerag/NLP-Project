# Problem-Based Learning and Question Pedagogy: Evaluation of its joint implementation for the Teaching of Chemistry in middle-school students.

The results of the SABER tests in natural sciences from 2016 to 2020, from the IED Fabio Lozano Simonelli of the town of Usme, Bogotá, were analyzed and correlated with the institutional curriculum of the chemistry subject, the PEI, the Basic Standards of Competences and Basic Learning Rights, as well as with the characteristics of the student population of the tenth and eleventh grades of the said institution, together with their conception and vision of the sciences, in order to propose two teaching units based on the Problem-Based Learning (ABP) and enhanced with the Question Pedagogy. This strategy was designed to stimulate the competencies in which students showed deficiencies. To determine the development of the competencies in students, specific evaluation headings were designed that covered the three aspects of knowledge (Knowing, knowing how to do and know how to be), obtaining positive results related to the implementation of this proposal, opening the option of implementing this methodology on a continuous basis
