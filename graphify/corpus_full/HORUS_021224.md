# Research on the influence of water activity on the pharmacotechnical behaviour and stability of materials of pharmaceutical and cosmetic interest.

Water activity (aw) is the quantity of water weakly united or free within a product that is available to participate in physical, chemical or biochemical reactions. It is a concept that was presented in mid 1950 (Scott, 1953; 1957) and has been used in the design of medicines and cosmetics as a strategy for the assurance of their microbiological quality, the design of products that are declared free of preservatives (Enigl, 1997) or to reduce the frequency of the microbiological quality control tests as proposed by the Pharmacopoeia of the United States of America (chapter   USP, 2022). However, the aw is also useful to understand the mechanical behavior and stability of some materials and in this sense, provides unique information for the development of products (Chirife and Fontana, 2020). As a methodological strategy systematic studies will be carried out to establish the possible correlation between the aw, which is modified when working in different environments of relative humidity, and the pharmacotechnical or stability properties of the materials under study.
