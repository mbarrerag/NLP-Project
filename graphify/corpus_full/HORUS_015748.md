# STRATEGIC PLANNING - CASES OF STUDY

The internationalisation of the economy and technological advances have made organizations more competitive every day and have managed to satisfy customers better. In this process, a tool appears to define the future so-called strategic planning. Planning and strategy have been present throughout the history of humanity in different ways, without establishing a character of their own or belonging to a discipline. Only from a few years ago, these two concepts have come together to integrate and form a definition in the administrative field. It is, from this moment, when strategic planning becomes one of the most effective administrative tools in the business field, which has gradually evolved and acquired reliability among the various companies in the world. Strategic planning, besides allowing to see the current situation of the company, is a tool that allows us to predict a future, or at least, to focus the company and have a perspective of possible future scenarios in which it could be immersed, and in this way to be prepared for the changes that the external or internal environment force it to take
