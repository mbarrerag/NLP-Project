# A JOINT ENTIRE PROGRAMING MODEL FOR MANAGEMENT OF CAPACITY INSTALLATED IN A WORKER'S WORKER'S HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTION

To manage the capacity is a process that makes part of the strategic planning of an organization to define its goals in long, medium or short term. An adequate use of the available capacity allows companies to increase their efficiency and productivity. For the service companies it is not an easy task to estimate their capacity due to the variety of resources that they must contemplate and manage, the restrictions to satisfy and the uncertainty in the demand of services. Therefore, this research is focused on a laboratory of water quality, which currently does not quantify its available capacity, so it is not known if it is making an adequate allocation of the resources and the quantity of tests that could be attended in a planning horizon. For the development of the model, a characterization of its capacities is carried out, the model is executed in scenarios and the results are contrasted by occupation indicators. The results of the proposed scenarios reveal that the laboratory has a high percentage of capacity or that is used, for which, through a DOFA analysis, is proposed strategies focused to strengthen aspects that result in the laboratory has a greater demand for services and therefore the use of its capacity is increased. In the proposed scenarios, the use of the equipment is an improvement of the equipment and for the ability to be considered. The employment rate for the analyst would remain at 76 per cent and professional at 99.9 per cent. These results allow us to conclude that the proposed model is able to improve the results obtained by the organization today empirically, which means a step forward in this type of programming for this type of problem
