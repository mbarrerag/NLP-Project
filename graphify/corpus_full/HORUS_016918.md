# Distribution electrical grid planning including distributed generation, energy storage and demand management (DSM Y DR).

This paper investigates how to improve the planning of the distribution electrical networks (REDis) around their modernization, analyzing aspects such as the integration of distributed resources, energy storage systems and the possible implementation of strategies of response to demand. The work analyzes the mathematical models used to represent each of the devices and strategies indicated above, proposes models for the integration of these devices into planning, and finally describes the procedures and methods developed to execute the planning in some cases of testing. The proposed models allow the inclusion of technical, environmental and economic aspects in the optimization objectives of the problem, thus generating an interesting contribution for this area of engineering, because it allows the simultaneous evaluation (multiobjective) of the three evaluation criteria. Among the results and contributions of the research work are the developed mathematical models, indices to measure how efficient the use of the distribution system assets is, and the diagrams of flow that describe the proposed procedures for the development of planning.
