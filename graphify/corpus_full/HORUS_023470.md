# Green extract from AHUYAMA carotenoids (CUCURBITA MOSCHATA DUCH) USING VEGETAL oil for their addition as a natural coordinant in food matrices

The carotenoids are pigments naturally present in the food and give yellow-red-orange shades, being of interest both for industry and for human health due to their multiple bio-functionalities. The ahuyama Cucurbita moschata Duch, due to its agro-industrial importance and its composition, was the subject of this study as a source of carotenoids from green processes. The ahuyama was adequate by drying and grinding processes in order to improve the view to the carotenoids. In the extraction of carotenoids two technologies were compared, with the use of sunflower oil as solvent. One of them was the continuous agitation (conventional-CNV) of the matrix with the solvent, in which different times and concentrations of ahuyama were evaluated; the second was an ultrasound assisted extraction (US) with the highest percentage of peroxides and index. the potential of ahuyama pulp as a source of carotenoids-rich extracts, being these functional and useful compounds as a natural food dye. Likewise, it poses conditions for the use of conventional and non-conventional techniques for obtaining this type of compounds
