# EDUCATION IN THE GOOD USE OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS: ANTROPOLOLOGICAL PERSPECTIVE AND CHALLENGES

The medicine is a social life thing that has evolved in parallel with different advances at the cultural, scientific, technological and health level. Its generation and use responds to social dynamics of spatial-temporal and cultural contexts. In this work, the current social and cultural positioning of medicines is analyzed from an anthropological perspective. Highlighting the presence of the medicine in art, music, literature, advertising and social networks, aims to call for the need to implement policies in health education, which promote the good use of the medicine. Educating for health requires a cross-sectional reflection between knowledge and knowledge of different social actors, which leads to restoring the universality of the concept of health and to promote in the general population the awareness and prudence that demand the use of medicines and related products, such as dietary supplements and herbal products. Some elements necessary in this reflection are discussed in the final sections
