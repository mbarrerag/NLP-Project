# Approach to the Free Circulation Systems of Financial Information, its Regulatory Models and its application in Colombia.

With the introduction of disruptive technologies to the financial sector within the framework of the so-called fourth industrial revolution and the constant search for efficiency by the operators of the financial sector, the aim of the financial sector, the use of new models and structures based on the opening of the information has been promoted.This approach implies that customers, such as the owners of the information that today rests on financial institutions, can decide who and how they use it, and in this way the access to other operators and industries is facilitated.This in turn promotes the creation of new competitive financial solutions that respond to the growing and diverse needs of the consumers, increasing the inclusion and access to both financial and other products.Through comparative research, taking as a basis the leading countries in the implementation of Free Circulation Systems of Financial Information, the research project aims to use the experiences both positive and negative of jurisdictions as well as those of the European Union, United Kingdom, Singapore, Brazil, Australia, United States and Mexico, it has established the information to promote the ecosystem of the open finance 22nd and to the Mexico; to identify, analyze and evaluate the regulatory guidelines potentially useful to be considered when considering the incorporation of an Open Finance System in Colombia, which has already begun its regulation process with the creation use in Colombia
