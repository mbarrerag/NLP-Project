# TORREFACTION OF BIOMASS IN A BANCO TURN-SCALE TORTURE: EFFECT OF TEMPERATURE AND BIOMASS TYPE

The intensive use of fossil fuels contributes significantly to global warming and the growing global energy crisis, therefore, it is necessary to develop alternative energy sources that allow to relax the energy matrix and mitigate environmental impacts. One prominent option is the conversion of residual biomass to energy, since biomass constitutes a fuel with a lower impact on CO2 emissions. Therefore, this study aimed to promote the physical and chemical properties of two residual biomasses, pine sawdust and coffee-cleaning, by means of the torrefaction process. The energy valorization of biomasses was carried out in a screw reactor at bank scale (2.8 kg/h).The effect of the temperature in the range between 200 °C and 300 °C was evaluated and the torrefacted biomass was characterized by instrumental techniques: calorific power, near analysis, infrared analysis and microscopic electron removal. For both biomasss, an increase in the temperature of the process was obtained an increase in calorific power.This behavior is associated with the thermal degradation of the hemicelulose fraction, as well as the increase of the fixed carbon.
