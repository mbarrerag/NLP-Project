# PRELIMINARY SCHEME OF TRATIGRAPHICAL PALINOVENTS FOR THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN NEOGINE: TOWARDS THE DEVELOPMENT OF A PALINOLOGICAL ZONATION

The use of palinology (study of organic wall microfossils) as a biostratigraphic tool in Colombia to date sedimentary sequences Cenozoic has focused mainly on the study of continental palinomorphs (polen and spores grains) during the last half century. However, recent exploration in the coastal area outside the Colombian Caribbean during the last decade has allowed the evaluation of the use of marine palinomorphs (dinoflagelate and acritarc cysts) as biostrategic markers from the study of ripium samples from four exploration wells drilled in the Guajira Offshore Basin.
