# Formalization of informal settlements and land and housing market in Bogotá, D.C. Case study: Barrio Caracolí, Ciudad Bolívar 2000-2023

This work seeks to analyze the stages and characteristics of the process of formalization of informal settlements within the framework of the competences assigned to urban governments and their effects on land and housing prices. As a case study, the informal Caracolí neighbourhood of the city of Bogotá was analyzed. It seeks to understand the processes of occupation of the territory and the formation of an informal market of the soil and housing that frames the commercial conditions of the way of life of the settlements. In addition, to analyze the transformations in the price of the soil and housing when the settlements undergo physical, legal and social changes, as a result of public policies implemented in programmes and plans for the improvement and formalization of the informal settlements. This work has led to the identification of factors for the transformation of the price of the soil and housing in the midst of the settlements. lped to identify the factors that influence changes in land and housing prices during the process of investment and improvement in these settlements. The execution of comprehensive neighbouring improvement programs directly benefits settlements, leading to changes in land prices when accepting housing. The continued implementation of public policy has led positive results in improving the quality of life in these settlements
