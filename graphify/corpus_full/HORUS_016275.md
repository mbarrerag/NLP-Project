# STRUCTURE AND ACABISHED: 30 IDEA ON IMPROVING COLOMBIA LIVING POLICY APPLICABLE TO THE EMERGENCING WORLD

Housing policy is not just a sectoral policy because it allows the objective of equity to be effectively reconciled with those of economic growth, while it disperses its expansionary effects sectorally, as a result of the numerous productive chains that are characteristic of its activity. Colombia has recently reached the highest levels in its history in terms of buying and starting houses. Colombia, however, is an incomplete revolution, with multiple opportunities for improvement in the various instruments of supply promotion and demand in both urban and rural areas. This document proposes a methodology to describe the effectiveness of housing policy instruments and shows that, for the Colombian case, there is the possibility of further improving the depth, progressiveness, cost-effectiveness and sustainability of several of its policy instruments. Based on these findings, we propose a set of 30 ideas for improving housing policy in the country
