# Cinematics and biosignals as a basis for the development of methodology for the evaluation of postural gestures associated with the execution of toilet work at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín Headquarters.

In general, the activities associated with the cleaning of spaces have marked consequences on the performance of workers, since they are considered repetitive and in some cases extreme postures. In this project, the characterization, monitoring and study from the kinematics and the processing of biosignals for one of the gestures associated with cleaning and cleaning work at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Sede Medellín, is proposed, with the aim of creating a methodological strategy for the evaluation of gestures or jobs and that can be replicated later. All this involves an experimental study in a laboratory of motion analysis, the laboratory of Mechanical-Biomechanic Design of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Sede Medellín and in the real space of performance. The study aims to follow up the kinematics of gesture and monitoring variables such as oxygen saturation, heart rate, blood pressure and muscle activation using EEG and EMG. It is planned to make an evaluation of these variables in two temporary moments separated by a training plan and physical conditioning specifically designed to strengthen muscle groups involved in the gesture
