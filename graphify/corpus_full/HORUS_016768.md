# Techniques based on complex natural resonance extraction hardware for ground penetration radar operations.

This book presents a contribution to the remote detection of anti-personnel mines in Colombia, in terms of the analysis of radar signals of land penetration. The methods of extracting complex natural resonances for the characterization of buried objects are compared, and from this evaluation and analysis, a new method that shows an increase in the pre-pression and the implementation in hardware is proposed. Finally, this method is implemented in an ARM processor
