# Prototype of an autonomous land vehicle for mapping of spatial variability of soil based on apparent electrical conductivity

The current conditions of the agricultural sector in Colombia allow us to demonstrate different challenges in terms of the ability to increase production by cultivated area. It is particularly necessary to implement a system of soil characterization that guarantees small and medium agricultural producers to identify precisely the main physical and chemical properties of the cultivated area they possess. This project is aimed at developing an autonomous vehicle prototype that allows to map the soil, using as the main variable of measurement the apparent electrical conductivity. For the materialization of this project, the design and mechanical modelling activities are required along with computer-assisted tests and simulations through which the designs, the software implemented for the detection of obstacles based on the analysis of image and the use of sensors of electrical conductivity apparent previously calibrated in laboratory are possible. Thus, the purpose of this work is to contribute to the modernization of the Colombian agricultural sector to generate, not only research results, but also to contribute to the proper management and use of the soil, making better use of resources by implementing little invasive methods in the field, contributing to the increase of the quality and quantity of production
