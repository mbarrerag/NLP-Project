# Characterization of the hyolaryngeal complex in adults, through videofluoroscopy. Scoping Review

The purpose of this review was to examine and synthesize the existing literature on the hyolaryngeal complex, with a particular focus on its anatomical and functional characteristics. The hyolaryngeal complex is a set of structures; essential in the deglucation process, since it determines basically the safety of the deglucation. To develop the characterization of the hyolaryngeal complex in adults, a review of the literature was carried out, consulting articles of indexed journals. The main evaluation tool used in these studies was videofluoroscopy; an image technique, which allows real-time visualization of the deglucation process, which facilitates the identification of any anomalies in the dynamics of the hyolaryngeal complex. This review included a qualitative analysis of 23 articles published between the years 2020-2024. The revised contents covered the descriptive anatomy of the hyolaryngeal complex, its dynamics during deglucination. The results of this review showed the variability and lack of consensus in reference to the normal measures of the displacement of the particular hyolaryngeal complex in adults . view included a qualitative analysis of 23 articles published between 2020 and 2024.
