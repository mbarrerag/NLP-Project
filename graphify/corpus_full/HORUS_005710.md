# ALTERNATIVES OF HUMAN HEALTH BY EXPLOSIVE ARTEFICTS IMPROVED.

to review existing literature by investigating the types of injury or disease that have been identified in survivors of improvised explosive devices (IEDs), as well as individual and collective aspects related to the protection or destruction of human health faced by IEAs. systematic exploratory review of literature published in databases, such as PubMed, SciELO, LILACS, JSTOR, OpenGrey and Google Scholar, between 2001 and 2021 in any geographical location. Research, review or reflection articles, books or chapters of research book and technical reports, in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese. met the inclusion criteria and were reviewed 76 documents. Multiple injuries associated with the use of ISAs have been reported, without a characteristic pattern of injury or disease; however, the most common injury is the bilateral amputation of lower members. In survivors, it has been found that the affected military personnel usually show a greater resilience, quality of life, timely treatment and higher functional physical capacity, compared with civilians.
