# Determination of the presence and expression of geranylgeranyl enzymes synthase diphosphate, farnesyl diphosphate synthase, lanosterol synthase and squalene synthase in human crystalline with senile cataract.

The exact physiopathology by which age-related cataracts are formed is clearly unknown. Multiple relationships between the biosynthesis of lanosterol and cataract formation have been investigated. In our study a real-time polymerase chain reaction study of the enzymes Farnesyl syntase, Geranyl syntase, Lanosterol syntase and Esqualene syntase was conducted in a transparent control lens and two crystalline with senile cataract of different degrees of opacity. In the results it was possible to show a significant difference in the relative expression of the enzymes Farnesyl syntase, Geranyl syntase and Esqualene syntase being greater in samples 1 and 2 of crystalline with nuclear opacity with respect to the transparent control lens. The findings suggest a pathological modification in the functioning of the metabolic pathway of cholesterol bifurcating towards an increase in the expression of the enzyme Geranyl diphosphate syntase.
