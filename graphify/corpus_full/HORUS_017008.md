# DIGINITY IN SPACE EXPERIENCE OF THE RIGHT TO LIVING

The dignified and the appropriate in the policies and in the housing rights is a qualification of relevance that must be investigated, since it alludes to the conditions of habitability and the quality of life of the inhabitants.The right to housing is studied in texts of academic authors, from the habitability in the housing within a framework of rights, but it is not discussed in writings whose perspective is housing policy.It is also discussed from the content of legal instruments of international law in permeability with the right to housing showing the link of the housing rights with dignity, such as the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (dudh) of 1948, the Protocol of San Salvador of 1988, the conventions of the United Nations on minority groups or the International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (Pidesc) of 1991 that instituting the indicator of the right to adequate housing1 (Mejía-Escalante context, 2016).In view of the above, we guide the revision in repositories of academic documents towards the relationship of dignity and space, to find research from the ethics, bioethics and the care of the patient, in the majority, although they do not establish discussions on the content of housing, we do not refer to the The fundamental rights such as life, equality and health, but also economic and social rights such as housing. It follows an approach to dignity from its social dimension, to glimpse entities in which the damage to dignity is installed, as well as observation factors on what we name as dignity in the spatial experience, keeping in perspective the habitability seen from the subject as a condition of the habitable, which is constructed by human interaction in the act of inhabiting, not restricted to housing, nor from standards of the adequate space
