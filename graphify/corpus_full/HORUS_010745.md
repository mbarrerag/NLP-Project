# SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES IN SOCIAL WORK CROSSED FROM A RETURNING PERSPECTIVE IN COLOMBIA?

Since the third decade of the twentieth century, the intervention of Social Work has been complex in terms of the aims of development, derived from international acts signed by the Colombian governments. Currently, the introduction of the intervention of Social Work with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) is not simple either. To contribute to its understanding, in this article, the results of the research that characterizes the contributions of Social Work in the cycles of public policies directed towards the achievement of the SDGs, presented by its author at the XVI Colombian Congress of Social Work, held from 18 to 21 October 2022 in Riohacha - Guajira
