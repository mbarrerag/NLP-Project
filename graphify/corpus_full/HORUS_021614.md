# Degree of development of management facilitators: culture and organizational structure associated with innovation capacity in the footwear manufacturing sector in Colombia.

The goal developed in this final master's work was to measure the degree of development of the management facilitators: organizational culture and organizational structure associated with the innovation capacity in the footwear manufacturing sector in Colombia. For this purpose, the document is divided into 5 main chapters.The first chapter generates a whole conceptual framework of innovation, innovation capacities, models that measure innovation and generate the support under which the use of the Model for the Measurement of the Determinants of the MMDCI Innovation Capacity is defined, to apply in the footwear manufacturing sector in Colombia, by means of a systematic review of literature.In the second chapter, the selection of the sample and the form of acquisition of information is carried out by means of a questionnaire designed and in turn the characterization of the companies involved in the study is generated.In chapters three and four, all the descriptive structure of the information collected for the higher culture and organizational structure facilitators is developed.In the second chapter, the most developed form of analysis of descriptive statistical analysis and a graphical visualization of all the evaluation levels contributed by the companies is developed by the individual structure and structure and structure of the most developed by the most developed culture
