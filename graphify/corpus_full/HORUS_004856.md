# Relationship between financial inclusion and monetary poverty in Colombia

The intersection by financial inclusion and monetary poverty is a crucial issue in the socio-economic and public policies agenda of the countries. This study focuses on the relationship between financial inclusion and poverty in Colombia, recognizing the regional diversity of the country. The research takes an innovative approach, incorporating the number of bank correspondents as an accessibility measure. The detailed analysis at departmental level highlights the unique socio-economic particularities of each region. The results derived from the main estimates reveal a negative association between financial inclusion and monetary poverty at national level. Whether through the bank liability side, represented by the total savings accounts, the bank asset side, through the financial deepening of the consumption and micro-credit portfolio, or by the coverage of banking channels, using bank correspondents as proxy, it has been shown that greater access to financial services and channels is statistically linked to a decrease in the incidence of monetary poverty. The analysis at departmental level has added an additional layer of complexity, revealing unique regional trends. Departments such as Cauca, La Guajira, Quindío and Sucre stand out by showing a significant negative association between all types of financial inclusion considered in this study and the incidence of monetary poverty.
