# BEYOND HENRY¿S LAW IN THE GAS¿LIQUID EQUILIBRIUM

Gas'liquid equilibrium data are usually described with Henry's law, but this is a limited linear model with an ideal representation of the gas phase. This work describes the extension of Henry's law over pressure with the Krichevsky'Kasarnovsky and Krichevsky'Ilinskaya correlations, and the use of the van't Hoff equation to represent Henry's law constant as a temperature function. Two models not based on Henry's law are also described, the predictive Shair'Prausnitz correlation, and the application of a cubic equation of state combined with a mixing rule in a flash calculation. These models allow the estimation of gas solubility in liquids at high pressures
