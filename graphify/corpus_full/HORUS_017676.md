# THE VERDE BONO MARKET IN LATIN AMERICA AND THE CARIBBEAN: PERSPECTIVES AND CHALLENGES

The green bond market in Latin America and the Caribbean has grown rapidly in recent years. This paper shows in detail the performance of the green bond market in the region and analyses the main elements of the green bond market. Brazil, Mexico and Chile are the leading countries, while Barbados is a growing market. Regarding sectors, renewable energy stands out for the number of operations and volume issued. Finally, it is expected that this type of financial instruments will continue to be issued in the region and sustainable financing will grow
