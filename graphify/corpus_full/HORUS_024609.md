# Between air and drums: The symphonic band, as a means of recognition of the Bambuco of Chirimía Caucana

This work compiles three bambucos de chirimía caucana, exploring tools of orchestration and harmonization that allow to appropriate this musical expression optimally in the language of the symphonic band, also investigating the pedagogical and/or learning possibilities of this repertoire through the use of some tools that offers the traditional methodology with which the indigenous communities of the area carry out this process, articulated with the use of language and academic resources such as the score; all that mentioned, responds to the objective of making visible and disseminating in various municipal and urban spaces of the national and international territory, the cultural heritage that is behind the bambuco of the Nasa and Yanacona peoples in the Colombian south. The above framed in a pertinent reflection on the importance of the collective identity reinforced through the community musics from the ancestral vision, as well as from the escénica concept, the bathroom or musical group represented in the synfonic band, around the ethnic, cultural and musical mestizo in the Colombian context as amerindia and Hispanic. lesson identity reinforced through community music from the ancestral vision and also from the musical stage conception in urban-western culture represented by the concert band, around ethnic, cultural and musical miscegenation in the Colombian context as an Amerindian and Hispanic nation
