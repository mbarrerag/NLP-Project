# CHARACTERIZATION OF GENETICAL AND IMMUNOPHENOLOGICAL CHANGES ASSOCIATED WITH HEMATOPOYESS CLONAL OF POTENTIAL INDETERMINATED IN COLOMBIANS MAYOR 65 YEARS WITH AND WITHOUT NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES RELATING TO AGE

After 65 years, humans experience a large increase in the incidence of cancer, neurodegenerative and cardiovascular diseases; all of these are major causes of morbidity and disability, which imposes a considerable socio-economic impact. In Colombia, the population is ageing, hence it is important to know the biological aspects of old age diseases and to develop methods for their mitigation through prevention and early detection. Clonal Hematopoiesis of Indeterminate Potential (HCPI) is a newly discovered condition; it is known to increase with age and is a risk factor for progression to hematological cancers, cardiovascular disease and decrease in overall survival.
