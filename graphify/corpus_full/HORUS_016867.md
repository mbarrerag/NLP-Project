# Study of the physical properties of the accretion disc in AGNs, based on the analysis of the light curves.

The active nuclei, present in some galaxies, are characterized mainly by presenting variability in emissions emitted over different ranges of the spectrum. To study the properties in the variability of the light curves, in this work the CARMA model(4.1) was applied, based on analitic developments and computational models, on a photometric data base of 100 quasars, taken from the DR7 repository of the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS) in its 5 ugriz bands. In this process the carmapack package, developed in the programming language pyrthon, was used, obtaining statistical parameters describing the light curves.
