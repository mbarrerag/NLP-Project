# PERSPECTIVES OF MENTAL HEALTH IN THE LABOUR SCENEY: ANATOMY OF A ACADEMIC DISCUSSION

Mental health education in the work context has a leading role at present and responds to a problem of health and social and economic development. The increase in the prevalence of mental problems and disorders in workers with the concomitant economic burden persists. However, in the academy, the lack of consensus in its conception, derived from a plurality of competing epistemological perspectives, from which it is addressed. The aim of this article is to describe the main epistemological perspectives that underpin the conceptions of mental health at work, presented in articles and documents published between 1987 and 2017. The search for literature included consultation of regional databases taking into account terms (descriptors in Health Sciences), manual consultation, and specialized literature of international health and economic bodies. In the analysis, the findings were gathered by thematic coincidence, deriving three categories: mental health at work and the state of things; the centrality of mental health at work for development; and the lack of consensus
