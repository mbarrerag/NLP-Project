# Implementation of additive manufacturing technologies for ceramic materials as a complement to the R & D & I processes of the National University of Colombia

Currently, the country requires to adopt new manufacturing processes that are directly linked to innovation in industry 4.0. In order to be able to be with global trends in the field of engineering, it is expected that new advances will be a paradigm shift in the development of ingeniile processes in the Colombian industry.The present project seeks to create and articulate a line of production and processing of composite materials based on additive manufacturing by linking an interdisciplinary team of students who make part of the different training programs. This is why, it will be addressed and developed in a collective and collaborative way, the putting in place of equipment for the processing of materials _x0093_Made in House_x0094_, which is linked to the additive manufacturing technique that is currently a focus of attention in the field of engineering materials. It will develop the theme based on innovation through the company's production of composite materials _x0093_Made in House_x0094_, managing to generate a strategic ecosystem with the students to continue the development and disruption of new manufacturing technologies within the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, in alliance with the SENADA. D has generated new opportunities for research and technical development of materials that allow the appropriation of technologies aimed at innovation and industrial self-sustainability in the country, providing ecosystems based on production and consumption responsible for the environment
