# MOLECULAR VIRUS DETECTION IN CULTIVATIONS OF UCHUVA (PHYSALIS PERUVIANA) OF ANTIOCIA SUROEST (COLOMBIA)

Uchuva is one of the promising fruits for the Andean area of Colombia thanks to its high international demand. In Antioquia, its sowing is concentrated in the east, although other subregions such as the north and southwest have optimal conditions for its cultivation. Since diseases of viral origin are one of the emerging problems of this fruit tree, in this study was evaluated the infection of RNA virus that infects the crops of Uchuva in the southwest of Antioquia. For this, RT-qPCR tests were used in foliar samples of symptomatic and asymptomatic plants obtained in eight batches, as well as in eight samples of seeds extracted from fruits marketed in this subregion. The results indicated the presence in at least one foliar sample of the viruses PVY, PVX, PVS, CGIV-1 and PMTV, while PVV, PYV and PhyNVVV were not detected.
