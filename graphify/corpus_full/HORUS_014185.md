# Social and technological innovation strategy oriented to the productive sector of Cocoa in Tumaco, towards sustainable development and in peace

Historically agriculture has had a fundamental role in the development of settlements, means and life forms of communities. More recently, its importance has been exalted, because of its contribution in the production of food, in the obtaining of raw materials and as a boost for nutrition, health and reduction of poverty. Also towards the productive and economic growth of the actors of the productive sector and of the country. The Pacific region, by its location and tropical characteristics, has a recognized productive potential in the agricultural sector, which, from the territory and through its communities, has had to avoid a series of environmental, social, economic and political dynamics; among those that highlight: the war -civil and the armed conflict -, the narcotrafic, the poverty and social inequalities, the phenomenal extreme climates and the climate change and the diminished (in comparison with other regions) attention/compulsions for human and associative strengthening. The cocoa, which is cultivated in various areas of the national territory, is promoted as an opportunity to replace, the climate change and the decrease (in comparison with other regions) with the present proposal) 2 (of the 8) PDET pillars for the approach of transformation of the territory: (1) Economic reactivation and agricultural production and (2) Reconciliation, coexistence and construction of peace. With this of context, the present project proposes the development of a strategy of technological and social innovation that bet and allows the articulation of actors between the community and the academy, for the production chain of the Tumaco cocoa. Information will be generated on the productive, social, physical and morphological characteristics of the species, varieties and/or ecotypes of the Theobroma genere existing at agroindustrial level, besides the georeferencing and qualitative characterisation of the productive systems and actors of the sector in Tumaco; all this for the co-creation of a web tool (inmersa in a system of support to the decision making of WebGIS, which is able to achieve the possibility of the local collective and social participation for the organizations of the municipality of Tumaco), which is strengthened and strengthened through the action of this project
