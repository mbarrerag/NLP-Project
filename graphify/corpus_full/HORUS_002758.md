# POTENTIAL FITORREMEDIATOR OF THE CAMOR (CANNABIS SATIVA L.) IN AURIFER MINERIA SUELS IN COLOMBIA

The socio-economic role of gold mining in Colombia is recognized, as is the generation of negative externalities of this activity. The areas with high potential of auro-argentiferous minerals are located mainly in the Andean region, with deposits exceeding 15 g of gold/tonne. Industrialization in the use of gold in the country implied the change of techniques: from rudimentary bats to retro-excavators, dredges and amalgamation with mercury. The use of up to 35 g mercury/g of recovered gold involves the dumping of the element to the environment, and its transfer to agricultural crops. The objective of this high-level research plan is to assess the adaptation and potential phytoremediator of hemp genetic materials in soils of auriferous mining contaminated with mercury in the region of Ejeca-tnero in Colombia.
