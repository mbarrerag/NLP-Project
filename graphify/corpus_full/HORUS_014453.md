# Proposal for a multimodal transport network for exports of green coffee from Huila, Tolima and Caldas departments to the United States.

This final master's degree project aims to propose a multimodal transport network to reduce the logistical costs and export time of green coffee from the departments of Huila, Tolima and Caldas to the ports of New York, Charleston and Oakland in the United States, taking into account the existing infrastructure. To achieve this purpose, the transport network is studied from its structure, the agents involved in the processes and the problems of planning multimodal transport networks. Finally, extensive results are obtained that allow decision-making from the tactical aspect in determining the flow through the multimodal transport network, as well as the selection of suppliers and modes of transport
