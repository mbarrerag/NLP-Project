# Effect of propolis impregnation on PLA scaffolds loaded with wollastonite particles against Staphylococcus aureus, Staphylococcus epidermidis and their co-culture

Various diseases and lesions leave irreversible damage in bone tissues that require partial or total regeneration or replacement. Tissue engineering proposes the development of substitutes that contribute to the repair or regeneration process, using three-dimensional matrices (scaffolds) to create functional bone tissue. In this study, scaffolds were developed in the form of TPMS giroide PLA compounds with wollastonite particles enriched with propolis extracts from the Arauca region (Colombia) by means of deposition by molten filament (FDM). The propolistic activity extracts showed antibacterial activity against Staphylococcus aureus (ATCC 25175) and Staphylococcus epdermidis (ATCC 12228), which are responsible for ostiomelitis. The scaffolds obtained were characterized by means of electron microscopia of scanning (SEM), FTIR, angle of contact, wellness and differential degradation and calorimetria of sweeping (DSC)
