# Interpolation and spatial prediction methodologies for the analysis of the physical properties of soil in the Suarez river pit (Colombia)

Abstract In Colombia, agriculture can be developed in inclined lands that require soil conservation zonal practices. To do this, the area of interest for its determining properties is analyzed, usually with sampling at specific points. Interpolation techniques such as weighted reverse distance (IDW) and prediction as kriging for prediction and estimation of values in non-sampled places can be used. The aim of this study was to compare the methodologies of IDW and kriging for modelling the spatial distribution of the physical characteristics of the soil in the agricultural area of the Suarez river pit (HDRS).Data of five physical properties of the soil associated with erodability were used: percentage of sands, weighted average diameter, available water retention capacity, apparent density and real density, corresponding to 932 points observed on the HDRS in a grid of 700 x 700 m were displayed cross validation for each variable and the error of the techniques evaluated was compared.
