# STUDY OF REACTIONS IN THE GENERATION OF VOLATILE COMPOSITIONS WITH AROMA A PANELA

In this study, the production of volatile compounds with panel aroma was performed by means of the Maillard reaction. For this purpose, 5 amino acids and 2 sugars were evaluated, using an experimental factorial design, and analyzing the compounds obtained by GC-MS analysis. The trials carried out with mixtures of sugars and amino acids valine, glycine and aspartic acid generated products with greater similarity to the aroma of the panela. Some of the compounds obtained correspond to those olfactory active in the aroma of panel water. The reaction samples were subjected to sensory analysis with a trained panel and it was concluded that there were no significant differences when comparing them with a panel water pattern
