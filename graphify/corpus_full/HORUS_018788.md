# BIOCOLONIALITY AND ECOGENOETNOCIDIO IN GOODVENTURE.

From a new method that I define here as hermeneutic of relational ontologys, I analyze the effects of ecogenoethnocidal practices in the black communities of the Community Council of the Upper and Middle Part of the Dagua River Basin (rural zone of goodadventura-Colombia) on the transformation of their relationship with the territory and the production of the new black subjectivities, in the context of the armed conflict in Colombia. All this from the anti-racist, decolonial and biocentric perspective of biocoloniality. The purpose is to provide some analytical and methodological elements to think about the Afro-repairs and thus contribute to that field, as well as to the ecological justice and the demands of reparation of the Council
