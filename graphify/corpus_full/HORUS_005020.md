# ACCESSIBILITY TO RECREATIVE CYCLOVIES OF MANIZALS (COLOMBIA) AND SPACE EQUITY

The recreational cycle routes consist of municipal initiatives in which, for a certain period of time, the passage of vehicles in streets where they usually carry out their transit and are used to carry out recreational, wellness and sports activities by residents. These activities, commonly known as Open Street Initials (open streets initiatives) are carried out in search of the well-being of their inhabitants achieved by the recreation and recreation activities that families manage to have by possessing this ample space for their use and enjoyment. This research studies the geographical location of these cycleways in the city of Manizales, analyzes the coverage of these from parameters such as distances and times of travel, with respect to the different socioeconomic strata. `Without the results obtained it is emphasized that their location does not allow equitable access to the inhabitants of the different socioeconomic strata. Finally, from this study, it is proposed to expand the Sunday cycleways in order to reduce the inequality in the access to this recreation space
