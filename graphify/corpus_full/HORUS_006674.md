# Contributions from productive systems to livelihoods for the food sufficiency of coffee families on the El Vegon road (El Pital-Huila)

The rural communities' livelihoods have been transformed in response to the changes in the environment, these as a form of resilience and adaptation, allowing families to diversify their activities in their territory, as well as improving living conditions. The research question is thus: What are the contributions of the productive systems to the livelihoods of the coffee families in the village of El Vegon (El Pital-Huila)? The objective is to analyse the contribution of the diversity of the productive systems to the food supply and the generation of the means of life for the coffee families in the village of El Vegon (El Pital-Huila). The methodology is mixed through a semi-structured survey as an instrument to be able to characterize the productive systems and identify the resources of the agrobiodiversity of the territory that allow to analyze the contribution of the productive systems to the food supply with the means of life of the coffee families in the village.
