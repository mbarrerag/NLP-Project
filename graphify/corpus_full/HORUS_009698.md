# OF REGIONS CAY (COLOMBIA)

This paper analyzes the perception of the incidence of the Covid-19 pandemic, in the socioeconomic conditions of vulnerable populations offering goods and/or tourism services, from informality, in the cities of Cartagena (Barú Island) and San Andrés island (Johnny Cay), two tourist destinations par excellence of Colombia. The data related to the perception of socio-economic effects of Covid-19 as well as the strategies involved for its mitigation
