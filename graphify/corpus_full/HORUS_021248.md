# Strategic Interdisciplinary Alliance with the purpose of studying the diversity of mites and parasitic agents associated with Hymenoptera Apidae and formulating strategies for its conservation and sustainability in agricultural and livestock production areas in the Pacific region of Colombia.

The present research project is focused on strengthening of Scientific Alliances interdisciplinary that articulate the teaching, research and extension processes of the National University of Colombia through the study of the biodiversity of mites and parasitic agents of Hymenoptera Apidae, in the Colombian Pacific region.Our main objective is to evaluate the richness and distribution of mites and parasitic forms (bacteria and virus) in the Colombian Pacific region associated with populations of bees of the Apidae family and this way to contribute to improve the use of pesticides and polluting agents through the implementation of new designs of hives with sustainable materials and adapted to tropical conditions.The Himenoptera Apidae insects will be captured during the first and second half of the project's implementation in the departments (Nariño and Valle del Cauca).The captured insects will be preserved by means of the installation of the hives and the tropical conditions. the work of alliance among personnel of high level of knowledge will be strengthened. Training will be provided to the academic community, producers and stakeholders on the importance of preserving these insects and will be carried out a study on the diversity of Hymenoptera Apidae, mites and parasites associated with Apidae populations in the Colombian Pacific Region. Finally, strategies for social appropriation of knowledge (training and dissemination) related to the study area will be developed, highlighting the importance of parasitic disease management in Apidae, the protection of ecosystems and how science can help to solve these problems
