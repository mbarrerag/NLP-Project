# Application of the AMOS criteria for the histopathological classification of a series of cases of phylode tumor.

The phylous tumor (TF) belongs to the group of fibroepeptiteliial neoplaplaplas of the breast, together with the fibroadenoma (FA), represents between 0.3% and 1% of all primary breast tumours. The WHO classification into benign, border and malignant, presents challenges inherent to reproducability. Tools such as the Singapore nomogram have been created, using the AMOS criteria (estromal apipia, mitotic rate, stromal growth and surgical margin) to establish a more objective classification, which allows for establishing an individualised prognosis, with excellent agreement in validation in several countries.
