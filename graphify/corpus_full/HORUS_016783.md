# Design of an adhesive joint for assembly of a prototype single-seat chassis series kart.

The results obtained from an experimental investigation, related to the design and manufacture of anaerobic adhesive joints assembled in a tubular chassis of a go kart single-place vehicle, standardized according to the ICK/FIA standard, to determine the influence of some design parameters (substrate material, thickness/volumen of the adhesive, parameter of surface roughness and length of the bonding) on the mechanical resistance of the torsion. The selection of the bonding points with adhesive assembly, required the evaluation of the mechanical structural behavior of the chassis and the analysis by finite elements as a design tool was used to establish the state of stress whose maximum values of 72.2 MPa were concentrated in the areas close to the kart axes where six nodes were selected for the adhesive ensambles. A methodology for the construction and assembly of the chassis consisting of five stages was proposed, consisting of the design and manufacture of the cylindrical adhesive joint up to the assembly of the chassis elements.
