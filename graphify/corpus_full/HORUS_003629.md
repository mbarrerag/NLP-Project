# A TOOL FOR THE DIFFERENCE OF BISH SPECIES OF GENDER EUFRIESEA COCKERELL, 1908 (Apidae: EUGLOSSINI)

Geometric morphometry is an analytical method used for the identification of insect species with proven usefulness in cases where morphological variability is scarce or cryptic. In this work, the morphological discrimination of bees of the genus Eufriesea was used, (Hymenoptera: Apidae) evaluating variability in three levels: between species, between males and between females of the same species and between morphologically very similar species. Through the software tps DIG v2.31, the coordinates of 23 reference points located at the intersection of veins in the previous right wing of 197 previously determined specimens in 16 species were studied. The specimens used belong to 4 entomological museums of the country (Entomological Museum Francisco Luis Gallego, Medellín; entomological collection of the university of antioquia, medellín; museum of natural history, florence and entomological collection university of quindío, armenia.
