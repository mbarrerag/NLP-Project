# SECTION IX. SYNDROME POST COVID-19: TERRITORIAL COMPLICATIONS AND REHABILITATION

In the publication of December 20, 2020 (NG188), the NICE GUIA, after the review of 111 publications, reported persistent or emerging symptoms after SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19 infection. 19 publications were found that were sufficiently descriptive of this symptomatology, allowing the most frequent manifestations to be related as follows: discomfort and fatigue (62%), fatigue (47.19%), dyspnoea (19%), obstructive bronchos syndrome (25.8%), chest pain (unspecified) (13.8%), confusion (16.85 per cent), tachycardia (unspecified) (1.3%), exercise intolerance (14.6%), insomnia (10.1%), pain throughout the body (10.1%), cough (9 per cent dyssomia (7.6%), headache (7%) fever (unspecified) (6.74 per cent), dysgeusia (6.74 per cent), joint pain (6%) and chest pain (5.61 per cent)
