# CARLOS WALTER PORTO GONÇALVES: A Thread- GLOBAL-LOCAL ACTIVIST OF MAIN PROBLEMS/CONFLICTS/VIOLENCES/DESAFSIS OF OUR PLANET

Carlos Walter2 is a global/local thinker whose work influences fields of knowledge such as decolonial thinking, political ecology and critical geography from Abya Yala/Latin America. His contributions are essential to understand the main conflicts, problems and civilizing challenges of the present and to build a viable and dignified future. His works stand out for the global/local vision in which he described the territorial conflict over the analytical triad <territoriality-territoriality= to understand the global sures, as well as the colonialities, oppressions and r-existences within the global norths. All of this framed in the spatial arch that claims the centrality of space for understanding of the whole. Extending its legacy translates into continuing struggles for territorial existence and overcoming fragmentations at the disciplinary, temporal, interpretative, approach, case and subject level towards a sustainable society in peace, and dignified for all human beings in its diversity
