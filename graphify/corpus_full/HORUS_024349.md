# Solid phase synthesis of a cosmeceutic neuropeptide and its microencapsulation in a liposomal system

At present synthetic peptides have been constituted in a novel alternative for the treatment of aged skin. Acetylhexapeptide-3 (Ac-EEMQRR-NH2) has been used to induce reduction of expression lines in a manner analogous to botulinum toxin, but without toxic effects. to synthesize acetylhexapeptide-3 and develop a liposomal system for encapsulation and to favor its passage through a model membrane. the peptide was obtained by solid phase synthesis (SFS) using the strategy Fmoc/tBu, was purified and fully characterized. The liposomal system with acetylhexapeptide-3 encapsulated was developed by forming an emulsion with subsequent phase reversal by evaporation of the organic solvent. The systems were characterized in its size, potential zeta, efficiency of encapsulation of the neuropeptide and preliminary was performed an ex vivo permeability study. it is possible to synthesize short peptides with high degree of purity and performance, using the Fpeptic strategy
