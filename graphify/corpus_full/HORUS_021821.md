# Ethnobotanical and bibliographical study of plants marketed in market squares in Cali – Colombia to treat Diabetes Mellitus.

Diabetes Mellitus (DM) represents a worldwide public health problem, which has quadrupled its prevalence in the last three decades, it is estimated that approximately one in every eleven people in the world suffer this clinical condition. DM is a group of metabolic disorders characterized by the presence of hyperglycaemia in absence of treatment. Integrating the traditional use of medicinal plants as an alternative and complementary treatment to medical exercise can result in an innovative and effective approach for diabetic patient within its bio-psycho-social cultural context. The objective of this research was to identify medicinal plants traditionally used to treat DM in the city of Cali. To this end, an ethnobotanic study was carried out in the main market places of the city and a bibliographic review of scientific articles available in databases such as BVS, PUBMED, LILACS, SCIELO, DYNAMED and the databases of phytochemical and ethnobotanical Dr. Duke.
