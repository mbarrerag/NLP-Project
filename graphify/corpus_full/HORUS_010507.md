# Development of food products from Sacha Inchi cake as a raw material of high nutritional value

In the current situation that Colombia is experiencing, problems such as food insecurity are the most relevant despite being the second most biodiverse country in the world. In this sense, as a strategy for the conservation of flora, bioeconomic development is proposed with the use of native species to satisfy the nutritional problems present in the country. Such is the case of the deficit of protein mainly in vegan or vegetarian population, children, older adults and sportsmen. The latter being the target of this project. In this sense and as a response to the opportunity evidenced, a protein drink for post-training, based on Sacha Inchi, which contains all the essential amino acids required for the increase of muscle mass. Therefore, at the level of composition and function it can penetrate another of the niches of market, provided that, the concept of product and design is re-structured. This possibility gives to Aborigin (company) a portfolio of products with high participation in the market, offering a rapid growth in which the innovation strategy must be strong and evident in each of its products to maintain its participation in the market. .
