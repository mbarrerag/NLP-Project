# COMPUTATION SIMULATION OF ACUSTIC EFFECTS DURING TOPABOCA

The effort to return to class classrooms in different educational institutions after the contingency of the current COVID-19 pandemic, without having high risks of contagion, has boosted the development of different elements of personal protection (PPPs). Among the most common PPPs is the mouthcap, which covers mouth and nose to prevent the release and/or entry of aerosols and drips contaminated with the SARS-CoV-2 virus. However, most solutions have been developed for adult medical staff, which is logical because of their high exposure to the disease, but this makes it impossible to have specific designs for children, forcing this population to use generic mouth covers with their negative consequences such as inadequate size, inappropriate materials and unattractive designs, which discourages their use and increases the likelihood of contagion
