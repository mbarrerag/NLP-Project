# RESEARCH RESOURCE VALUE EVALUATION TO THREE CLIMATE CHANGE SCENARIES USED DYNAMIC SYSTEMS, CASE OF SUR STUDY OF VALLE OF BURRÁ

The present work is framed by the development of a Model for the Management of Water Resources, focused on the scenarios of the Supply-Demand relations of the main aqueducts and users present in the southern part of the Basin of the Borrá Valley for the municipalities of Caldas, La Estrella and Sabaneta. Through the methodology of System Dynamics, the causal relationship of the variables involved in the system is established, with the aim of being able to demonstrate once the model has been calibrated the conditions of response of the system to different resource management decisions such as, decrease of losses in the aqueduct systems, changes in the land occupation schemes, joint use of groundwater and regulatory changes in the environmental flow of surface water sources. Finally, the response of the model to Three (3) scenarios of climate change with moderate, medium and high conditions of change, with the result of statistical evaluation of the basin's responses to the management decisions implemented in the model
