# Development and evaluation of the physicochemical and antibacterial properties of latex/ zinc oxide compounds for biomedical engineering applications.

The latex of natural rubber is an morphical polymer extracted from the rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis). It is an ecological material, sustainable, non-derived from oil and low cost. It has been used in different applications in the biomedical field such as regeneration, prostheses and bone grafts, besides its use in gloves, catheters, condoms, among others. However, it does not present antibacterial properties, which could lead to bacterial colonization on the surface of the material. Zinc oxide (ZnO) is an inorganic material that within its properties has antibacterial activity, which depend on various factors, among them its morphology. ZnO can be used for modification of the latex in order to grant it antibacterial properties. However, the antibacterial properties of ZnO particles, are observed in the latex, the less than the morphic type studied.
