# SENSIBILIZATION TO THE INSURANCE PROFESSIONAL ON HUMANIZATION IN THE ATTENTION OF THE PRESS CONTROL MANAGEMENT AND THEIR PARTMENT ATTENTION. COURSE: MARKING LIFES, BEING LOVED

Nursing has a role of great importance in the care of the pregnant woman and her family in prenatal control, delivery care and postpartum care, for which there are various regulations and guidelines at national and local level that guide this care. Despite this, the lack of knowledge and lack of humanized treatment is a valid fact, reflecting the need to sensitize nursing staff working in obstetric gynaecology services. Therefore, in response to the identified need, the course was created Marking lives, taking care with love, which seeks to generate a commitment of nursing staff and institutions to build equitable humanized care scenarios for pregnant women and their families. For the execution of the course, the approval and accompaniment of the area of humanization of the Integrated Subnet of Health Services North.
