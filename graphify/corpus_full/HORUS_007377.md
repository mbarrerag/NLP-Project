# Study of the temperature and residence time of the solid phase in the physicochemical characteristics and distribution of biosolid pyrolysis products in a rotary furnace

The present grade work arises from the need to evaluate an alternative for the treatment of biosolids generated in the PTAR Salitre de Bogotá. The alternative in question is pyrolysis, a thermochemical processing characterized by the degradation of organic matter at high temperatures in the absence of oxygen.This thermochemical process was developed in a rotary furnace of indirect heating with maximum capacity of 3,5 kg/h. Thanks to the development of experimentation, biosolids could be transformed into solid, liquid and gaseous products.The influence of the central parameters of processing was studied, carrying out experiments where the temperature varied from 250 °C to 700 °C and two residence times of 30 min and 45 min. Subsequently, the influence of these variations on the distribution of the products, the distribution of the energy and the physical characteristics of the products obtained was determined. .
