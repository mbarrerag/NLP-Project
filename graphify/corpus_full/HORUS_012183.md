# PLACEED TECHNOLOGIES OF PROFOUND AMERICA

Given the condition so widespread and naturalized in our world of technologies mediated by the beautiful screens, are the following central questions of research posed: Is it possible to decolonize the technology of screens from a particular thinking/doing/feeling and transdisciplic-nar that emerges locally from the perife-ria of the Colombian Deep America? And, how would it be possible to realize this particular decolonization, from the putting in praxis of holographic screens and technologies constructed from the territory as modes of epistemic construction and creation ?
