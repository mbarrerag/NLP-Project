# STATISTICAL ANALYSIS AND COMPUTATION SIMULATION OF THE DISTRIBUTION OF TEMPERATURES OF A POLYMER INJECTION MOLD

The injection mould is one of the most important tools in the injection industry, its design and manufacture plays a fundamental role in determining its life span and product quality. A common situation in the design of injection moulds is to achieve efficient performance in terms of thermal transfer, this will allow a higher rate of production with better finished parts [1]. Since about 80% of the processing time is determined by the cooling time of the mold [2]. This work, presented results from simulations using the finite volume method (MVF) of the injection molding process, these results are compared to an analysis of experiment design (Design of Experiments -DOE) with different injection conditions, revealing study variables that are fundamental to improving the process
