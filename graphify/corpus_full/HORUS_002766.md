# Improved recovery of oil and CO2 storage by injection of CO2/N2 blend foams from nanoparticle-powered flue gas

The flue gas foams for EOR projects are a promising alternative to overcome the limitations of high mobility of the gas injection in the displacement processes, while contributing to the global energy supply and decarbonisation of industrial plants. In the context of the CCUS projects, this technology allows saving costs in the CO2 capture stage and enables its storage potential. Additionally, this technology can be enhanced with nanoparticles, improving the stability of the foam, increasing the scanning efficiency and the oil recovery factor. Therefore, the objective of this work is to evaluate, at the laboratory level, the use of CO2/N2 foams, representative of gas flow currents, enhanced with NPs in EOR processes with associated CO2 storage potential.
