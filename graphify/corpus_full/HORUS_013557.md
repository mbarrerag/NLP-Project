# Basic criteria for effective regulation of new forms of work

In Colombia, the legal conception of work has been the same since 1950; however, the social, economic and labour context has evolved and created new legal relations that merit the protection and observance of the right to work; therefore, it is necessary to change the classical perspective and its institutions, in order to better protect the new notions and working modalities that have arisen when new information and communication technologies arrive. This is the case of the labour relations that arise from the services provided through digital platforms, which, because they are in the legal limbo, have been developing in the legal context, at the expense of the labour rights of those who use them for their livelihood and that of their families. tion of criteria that allow the generation of a regulatory framework at the external of the current work context
