# A PROXIMATION OF SOCIAL VULNERABILITY IN A FOUR HYDROGRAPHY OF THE ANDINA REGION OF THE VALLE OF THE CAUCA. COLOMBIA.

Social vulnerability is the condition of a person, group of people or community in a territory that is at a disadvantage in relation to their social environment. This vulnerability may be associated with different factors, such as lack of economic resources, social exclusion, discrimination, lack of access to basic services and labour instability, among others. Social vulnerability is the result of a combination of social, economic and structural factors that generate inequalities and limit the opportunities of certain groups of the population. Evaluating social vulnerability is of the utmost importance because it allows us to understand and effectively address the social challenges faced by a particular community or group of people. A social vulnerability assessment index to natural hazards is SoVI, which was developed in 1990 mainly for the United States of America (E.U.U.) and consists of a factorial analytical approach of 42 indicators [1]. An application of this index was developed for Colombia considering municipal information [2]. However, it has not been represented at the level of watersheds and integrated with population growth.
