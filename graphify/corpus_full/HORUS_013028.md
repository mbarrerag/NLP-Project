# Monetary Transfers Conditioned in Comuna 1 of Medellín and the perception of its inhabitants on poverty reduction

The programme of conditional monetary transfers Families in Action has been implemented for more than 20 years in Colombia, supported by its objective of contributing to the prevention and overcoming of poverty; this research is aimed at understanding how the implementation of this programme is perceived in relation to its contribution to poverty reduction by the inhabitants of Comuna 1 of Medellín. For this purpose, a qualitative methodology was implemented that, through the collection and analysis of information on poverty concepts, the implementation of the programme and the relationship between its implementation and poverty reduction for the case of Comuna 1, would allow to identify the values that are attributed to a significant value in relation to its perception of the contribution of the implementation of monetary transfers programs conditional on poverty reduction, based on its points of view.
