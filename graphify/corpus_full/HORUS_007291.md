# Development and evaluation of a prototype for real-time monitoring of biological crop growth in industrial bioreactors

In the field of biotechnology, continuous and precise monitoring of cellular concentration is essential to optimize biomass growth in biological crops. Bioreactors and fermenters (devices specifically designed for the cultivation of various cells, including bacteria and yeasts) depend on the measurement of parameters such as temperature, pressure, pH and dissolved oxygen, where the estimation of the cellular concentration is equally critical to implement closed loop control systems that allow to automate the process to the maximum. The optical techniques, and in particular the measurement of optical density, is implemented commercially and allows to characterize the growth and evolution of cells in watery suspensions in the bioreactor, taking advantage of the fundamental phenomena of interaction of light with matter such as absorption and dispersion, without altering the biological properties of the system.However, for the Pymes in Colombia, the commercial optical density sensors are usually used in terms of the low-cost, and the growth of the devices is allowed to be used in industries with limited resources, such as those dedicated to the production of artisanal beers, biological inoculants and biosimilar, among others. The analysis of the results of the study is based on the results of the study. the sensor that is introduced into the bioreactor is encapsulated in a polymeric material compatible with sterilization processes in situ}, which replaces the classic stainless steel of commercial devices, and a signal conditioning system was developed to control light emission to generate constant light intensity over time, in the face of electromagnetic noise and temperature variations, obtaining a measurement resolution of 0.02, lower than 1% on the measurement scale in arbitrary units. This solution does not only seek to make the automation of production processes in the Colombian biotechnology industry more accessible, but also, to enhance the quality of the final product and optimize production time to increase its potential profit margin
