# Observatory of Colombian Politics and International Relations OPRIC

The Observatory of Politics and International Relations of Colombia OPRIC during its six consecutive periods, has developed follow-up, observations, recommendations and analysis on the foreign policy and international relations of the governments in the period 2012 - 2022. For the new validity, OPRIC will continue the consolidated work plan of the previous validities to fulfill the objective of evaluating the different initiatives and actions of foreign policy raised in the National Development Plan (2022 - 2026) Colombia: World Power of the Life of President Gustavo Petro, from the axes of migration, borders, climate change, paradigm of fight against drugs, construction of peace, foreign trade, security and defense From this, from the first analysis of the PND (2010-2014) emerged a publication of balances that today remains material of consultation for the subjects corresponding to that plan and its link with the Colombian foreign policy. For the second PND (2014-2018), the working group of the PND difused and participated in events that were key to give greater notoriety and recognition to the National University of Colombia, as the participation in the V Congress of the Colombian International Network (REDINTERCOL) Security in the period of Colombia. The project seeks, through quantitative and qualitative tools, to evaluate Colombian foreign policy from primary and secondary sources, as indicators of monitoring the NDP, taking as its main reference a work plan that has lasted in several periods and whose results will be disseminated through different digital channels and academic events. An innovative proposal in the face of the classic analyses of foreign policy that do not focus on foreign policy actions and focus on the agenda.
