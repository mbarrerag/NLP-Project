# CASE REPORT: HISTOLOGICAL DIAGNOSTIC OF LEUCOCYTOZOON SP. AND HAEMOPROTEUS SP. IN A BUTE BUTE PLATYPTERUS IN COLOMBIA.

Leucocytozoon sp. and Haemoproteus sp. are hemoparasites transmitted by hematophagous dipters that infect birds, including raptors. Infected birds can be asymptomatic or present signs such as anorexia, ataxia and tachypnea. Anamnesis: a quaresmeral gavilan (Buteo platypterus) male subadultus was found unconscious in the Bogotá savanna, so it was transferred to URRAS to be treated. Clinical findings and diagnostic aids: to clinical examination, the wild bird was found depressed, with respiratory difficulty, anorexia and signs of possible trauma craneoencephalus. Analpha of celomic cavity and upper limbs was performed. The individual is diagnosed with trauma by collision. Treatment: The ave was hospitalized and was installed medication with dexamethasone, pregabalin, meloxicam, tratadol, sulphatrimetoprim, itraconazole, nystatin and hydration. See migratory rapaz in Colombia. Suggesting to continue with research that allows to deepen in the epidemiology, epizootiology, ecology, evolution and diagnosis of both hemoparasites, their impact on the health of birds and on the regulation of the ecosystems that they inhabit
