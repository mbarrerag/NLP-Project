# Papa Chaucha (SOLANUM PHYREJA)

The International Center of the Pope (CIP) for the celebration of its 50 years, decided to publish a book dedicated to Andean crops, which are considered to be of high food potential for humanity in the future. I was invited to write about the pope known in the Andean area as Chaucha, which in Colombia is commonly known as Papa criolla (Solanum phureja) and, of which, in our territory, has the most important center of diversity. In this chapter of the book, relevant information is presented about its origin, geographical distribution in the Andean region, varietal diversity, nutritional characteristics, economic potential as an agricultural product and its importance as a cultivated species and as a genetic collection, to explore in the genetic improvement of the potato
