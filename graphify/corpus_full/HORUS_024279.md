# CRITERIA AND INDICATORS FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF FORESTIAL INCREASES IN PROTECTED AREAS.

In general, the global dynamics of fire and fire are complex and evolve to unknown proportions. However, it is known that a vicious cycle is emerging that links fire and climate change, increasing the frequency and intensity of uncontrolled fires (François-Nicolas et al., 2018). Although in the last 22 years there has been a reduction of approximately 25% of the global area burned annually. This reduction is concentrated in regions with low levels of tree cover such as tropical savannahs in South America and Africa, as well as the grasslands of the Asian steppe. This panorama is opposed in the closed canopy forests, where the trend of increase of the burned area is maintained (Andela et al., 2017)
