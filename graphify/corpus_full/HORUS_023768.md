# Water management in artisanal and small-scale gold mining in the municipality of Segovia Antioquia.

A good management of water for the benefit of gold in the (MAPE) could solve much of the water stress that occurs in the mining localities, as it is estimated according to the OECD (2015c)., that by 2050, 240 million people in the world remain without access to drinking water. Brazil, Colombia and Peru are among the ten countries that are richest in water, but paradoxically face drought, water stress and mercury pollution in their water sources (Global Water Partnership 2021). According to the Minamata Convention, the Colombian government hopes to become a mercury-free country by 2023.To do this, it is necessary to know the management of MAPE in the hydric sources and possible solutions for the use of chemicals such as mercury. Therefore, the aim of this work was to identify the state of the water sources intervened in Segovia Antioquia, and to report on some research solutions carried out by experts and governmental entities in this field
