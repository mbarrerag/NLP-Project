# SCALES IN SPANISH FOR VALUATION OF PERSONS IN PALIATIVE CARE AND THEIR FAMILIES: PSYTHEMATIC PSYCHOMETICAL REVISION

one of the bases of palliative care care is the regular and comprehensive assessment of symptoms in patients and families. In this regard, it is important to recognize the scales used in palliative care in Spanish and their psychometric properties. to identify the scales in Spanish for the evaluation of the patient in palliative care and his family, to evaluate his psychometric properties and methodological quality of his properties based on the Cosmin standards. psychometric systematic review. A search was made for literature of the scales of evaluation in palliative care in Spanish and his psychometric properties in accordance with the Prisma proposal. The standards proposed by Cosmin were used for the evaluation of psychometric properties. 21 assessment scales were evaluated. The EORTC-QLQ life quality scale was the one that most numerous properties reported. Internal consistency, construct validity, content and is the most reported. No studies reported transcultural validity tests. the results of this study indicate a variety of instruments available in Spanish to evaluate the patient and the psychometric care instruments were thus found to be the most effective
