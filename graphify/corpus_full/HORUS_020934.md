# Evaluation of the use of flour yellow worm flour (Tenebrio molitor) in the formulation of diets for rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss)

The performance of the aquaculture sector in Colombia has presented significant growth in the last 30 years, going from 9,200 tons produced in 1990 to 175,580 tons in 2020. However, one of the factors that has limited this growth, are the productive costs corresponding to the food industry, which occupies about 50 to 70% of the total productive costs; due mainly to the import of raw materials, such as fish meal that is the source of protein most used in the formulation of balanced food for fish, thanks to its high digestibility and nutritional quality. However, according to FAO in terms of environmental impact and sustainability, such ingredient has increased the overfishing of the marine resource, affecting the balance of some populations in an alarming way. It is for this reason that insect meal has been considered as an alternative protein resource, due to the comparative advantages in terms of production, low environmental impact, circular economy and nutritional characteristics for the manufacture of balanced food for animals, as well as some benefits reported in the productive parameters of fish species of economic importance at the world level.
