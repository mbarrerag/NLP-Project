# Using dynamic simulation and Forrester diagrams to describe lettuce growth (Lactuca sativa L.) in field conditions

The use of computational tools to describe some processes of crop growth has evolved in recent decades and remains an area of active research, where more and more applications are incorporated with the integration of a greater number of mathematical tools, statistics and computational calculation efficiency, simplifying the tasks of modeling and visualization of the components of the system used. The current research proposes for lettuce cultivation a dynamic growth model using Forrester diagrams, to evaluate different scenarios involving five growth functions and five varieties of lettuce in field conditions of the Bailadors region (the Venezuelan Andes, 2550 m.s.n.m.). The variety of coastal star lettuce was the one that achieved the greatest accumulation of dry matter (used as a response in each model). It was found that the logistic growth function adequately adjusted experimental data compared to the other models. The proposed diagram model can be used as a basis for the construction of more complex models incorporating other physiological variables and the cultivation environment
