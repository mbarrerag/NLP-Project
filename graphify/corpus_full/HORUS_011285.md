# ECOPHISIOLOGY OF OXG INTER-SPECTIFIC HIBRICULTIVARIES

The ecophysiology of plants is a relatively modern science, which has been described by several authors as the study of physiological adaptations of organisms to habitat or to the environment. It is also known as physiological ecolo-gy and studies, in a general way, the growth, reproduction, survival, abundance and distribution of plants; moreover, it tries to explain how these processes are conditioned by the physical and chemical environment of the environment. Both wild plants in natural conditions and domesticated plants are frequently exposed to environmental factors that generate a differential response in each organism. The different environmental conditions stimulate a better use of the potentialities of the genotype, which allows ex-presorting mechanisms that contribute to the ecophysiology of the OxG hybrid palms (Elaeis oleifera x Elaeis guinensis) and of Elaeis culpónán arásticos que desacarágenes a desarrollo a la desarrollos a nacilias a la úntar ás ácimientos ácilias áxilligos agrís
