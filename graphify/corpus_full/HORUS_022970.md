# BLENDED- FLIPPED INSTRUCTION MODEL: PERSONALIZATION, FLEXIBILIZATION AND METACOGNITION FOR LEVELATION IN ENGLISH IN HIGHER EDUCATION

This research paper presents an exploration of a hybrid and inverted classroom instructional model for learning English as a foreign language and its scope in addressing the levelling needs of a group of university students. The model was born as a strategy of academic success for university students who require to study English with an option that is adjustable to both their learning processes and their living circumstances. With a qualitative approach, data were collected and the proposed postulates were followed in the theory based on constant analysis and coding of the data. The research results indicate that students perceive that the hybrid-invested instructional model allowed them to overcome affective and time barriers, through flexibility and customization, as well as incentivizing metacognitive behaviors. The above allowed researchers to conclude that the proposed model favours English learning and that its hybrid-inverted approach demonstrates that technology is a facilitating means in the process of flexibilization and adaptation of teaching practices to the needs of language learners. Key words: Higher education; hybrid learning; computer-assisted teaching: language teaching; inverted classroom
