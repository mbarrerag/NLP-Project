# ZINC HERR OXIDE SYNTHESIS FOR EFLUENTS FROM EFLUENTS OF DEPARTMENT FROM THE GALVANISED INFDUSTRY BY IMMERATION IN HEAT

Bimetallic zinc iron nanoparticles were obtained from pickling effluents from the hot-dip galvanized industry. Most bimetallic particles are composed of Fe and Zn in similar proportions that vary in a range between 30 and 40% w/w. Furthermore, there are no statistically significant differences between the values obtained for surface areas, showing that there is no significant effect on the variation in the properties of nanoparticles obtained by the different synthesis routes
