# STUDY OF NEW LESSONS FROM THE VENENO DEL PEZ LEÓN, PTEROIS VOLITANS FROM THE COSTA DE SANTA MARTA, COLOMBIA

SLRCO-131 Introduction The study of lectins from marine organisms is very scarce, in the fish poison, they have been characterized; Nattectin of Thalassophryne nattereri, Karatoxin of Hypodytes rubripinnis and Plumieribectin of Scorpaena plumieri specific by mannose. These proteins have mitogenic, cytotoxic, insecticide and antimicrobial effects among others. Methods By different methods, which included hydrophobic and affinity chromatography, we obtained fractions of pure lectin from the active extract. In each of the isolation steps, we quantified and detected the binding activity. We carried out inhibition tests, title, cytotoxicity and determined its molecular properties. Results and conclusions In the active extract of the poison we detected for the first time the presence of lectin pure from the active extract. We found the presence of lectin using rabbit erythrocytes.
