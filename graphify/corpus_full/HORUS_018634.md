# Analysis of road accident data of the city of Bogotá from open data and data obtained from social networks.

Road accidentity is a subject that is always wanted to minimize, but technological techniques have not been applied to find and understand the road behavior according to the different variables involved. Due to this need, a reason is generated to be able to contribute to this understanding of road accidentity to support in the mitigation and in this way to save lives. In this work will be found from the architecture and techniques of extraction of the road data in the city of Bogotá, to the statistical analysis to be able to understand the behavior of this one. In order to reach the statistical analysis initially a self-sustaining architecture of collection of road data in the city of Bogotá through social networks. Having already collected the information was proceeded to be represented in a geographic tool for its visual comprehension. An initial analysis of these data is carried out. Then, a geographical statistical techniques for its statistical analysis are applied
