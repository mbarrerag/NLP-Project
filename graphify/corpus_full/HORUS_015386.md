# Leveraging coal-based cinders in the manufacture of bricks and mortars: Piloto study

In the last decades, the rapid industrial, economic and social growth has originated large quantities of solid waste, causing negative impacts on the environment and human health (Cai et al, 2018). Currently, the elimination of waste is one of the main environmental problems, since not only they pollute the environment, but they generate a load for the land (Abbas et al., 2017).The industrial activities produce large quantities of solid waste, which represent a threat to the environment.Some of these are residues of the mining, chemical process production, cast iron and combustion waste (Cai et al., 2018).On the other hand, it is estimated that the coal will continue to be the second largest source of energy in the world until 2030 and, the third from 2030 to 2040 behind the liquid fuels and natural gas (IEO, 2016).The use of the coal as a source of the marine waste generates large quantities of waste; these include the fly ash (CVC), bottom ash (CFC), the slag of the boiler and the combustion gases (Vinai et al, 2013).
