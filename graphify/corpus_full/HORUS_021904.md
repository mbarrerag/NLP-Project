# Choosing an automation system in electrical substations using a multi-criteria model.

The systems of automation of substations (SAS) are increasingly implemented in the electrical substations in Colombia due to the integration of new technologies, in these implementations different alternatives can be proposed depending on which criteria are evaluated. However, looking to evaluate the criteria and to indicate that alternative is the most appropriate to implement, this research proposes the evaluation of a multi-criteria model, because they have become a tool to support and support decision-making, which indicates what is the best alternative to implement. In this sense, a literary review of multicriteria methods and alternatives has been carried out with respect to substation automation systems. Subsequent, six experts in the development and maintenance of substation automation systems were asked to define the criteria, then workshops were held with the participation of experts to assign weights or weighting and finally the multicriteria model was evaluated with the selection of the alternative that the model gives as a result
