# NATURAL FIBRES AS A REFURED REFERENCE IN COMPOUND POLIM'ERICA MATRIZ MATRIX

Due to the environmental crisis that humanity is experiencing, various research projects are currently being carried out to find sustainable alternatives for different industrial processes.In this sense, natural fibres have become an attractive resource for different industries, including composite materials. Natural fibres are renewable resources that can be obtained from animals or plants, which have multiple advantages such as low cost and weight, biodegradability and high specific properties. This document is a review of some relevant aspects to be taken into account for the use of natural fibres in the compound industry, such as their origin, chemical composition, extraction methodologies, physical and mechanical properties, and some treatments necessary to improve adherence when used as reinforcement of polymeric matrices. In addition, some of the results obtained in a research focused on providing technical aspects for the use of Guadua angustifolia bamboo fibers as reinforcing material are highlighted. It should be noted that the review presented in this document focuses on vegetable fibers
