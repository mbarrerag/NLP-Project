# DOCUMENTATION AND REFURPING OF THE NUEVA LANGUAGE OF COLOMBIAN AMAZON

This article presents the history of the documentation process of the nonuya language of the Colombian Amazon, its subsequent incorporation into the DOBES Archives of the Max Planck Institute, highlighting the contribution of these materials to the description of the language in a comparative and typological framework. Secondly, it presents the meaning and scope of the linguistic and cultural recovery process associated with documentation work and shows that the relationship between documentation processes and linguistic recovery leads us to question the notion of what a language means in the multilingual social context of the people of the center
