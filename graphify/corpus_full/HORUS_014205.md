# Strengthening the Security and Defence Research Group 2024- 2025

The Research Group in Security and Defense (GISDE), for the years 2024 and 2025, aims to consolidate its efforts to generate new knowledge in all its lines of research, for the present project, it is intended to develop three research projects for the lines of Natural Resources, Democracy, Public Force and Society. In addition to the line of Armed Conflict and Peace, through the creation of the Observatory of Armed Conflict and Peace, being consumable in the main bets of GISDE for that validity.
