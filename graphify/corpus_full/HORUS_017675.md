# ANTROPOLOGY, VIOLENCE AND SOCIAL ACTORS IN LATIN AMERICA

In 2019, the United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime stated that Latin America was the slowest region on the planet, with the highest homi-cidio rates: 37% of the world total in a region with only 8% of the world’s population. Factors such as organized crime, drug and arms trafficking, impunity and accelerated urbanisation processes help to compose a dishonorable panorama of inequalities that mark the pattern of deadly struggles for control of territories for illicit markets, which go through practices of disappearance and domestic violence. What social and cultural factors present in the region affect these phenomena? What can be our commitment as experts, intellectuals and academics of social and human sciences?
