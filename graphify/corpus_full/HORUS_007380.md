# Detection and counting of evaders in the Transmilenian transport system

Bogota is among the twenty cities with the worst traffic in the world according to the Ranking of the Traffic Index. Moreover, it lacks a robust public transport system; the TransMilenio system, in particular, presents operational and economic deficiencies that aggravate the problems of mobility in the city. The most prominent of these deficiencies is the payment evasion, a phenomenon that has so far been difficult to quantify and therefore to mitigate. For this reason, the aim of this work is to detect and quantify the evaders in the turnstiles of the TransMilenio system. To achieve this, various techniques of image and video processing, as well as automatic learning and artificial intelligence, were explored. This work faces several challenges, including the marked imbalance in the data set (3 out of every 100 people are evaders), the subtlety in some escape events and the complex environments of the stations.
