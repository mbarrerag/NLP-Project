# Evaluation of the performance of a building with two types of seismic dissipators controlled by deformation (TADAS and arriostration restricted to the pandeo) against various seismic events.

Within the current context of the construction design, seismic control techniques each day have greater importance, since with the dissipating or insulator devices the response of the building to seismic events can be controlled. Likewise, the seismic-resistant construction codes are being projected towards an extreme performance assessment perspective, where the design is not limited to the case of the design earthquake, but, according to the objectives set for the structure, its performance is evaluated in the face of various seismic scenarios, including extreme events. In this research, the two aspects mentioned in a methodology based on the simulation of strong movement by extreme earthquakes and the evaluation of the performance of reinforced concrete buildings subject to this seismic demand were integrated into the design.
