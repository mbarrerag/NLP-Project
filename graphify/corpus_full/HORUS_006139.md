# Carl Degenhardt and some of the first fossil reported from Colombia (South America)

Carl Degenhardt’s geological knowledge helped him to arrive to Colombia with an English mining company in mid-19th century. His travels from the north coast to Marmato (central Colombia) and from there to other regions, allowed him to recognize, describe, and collect numerous sample minerals, rocks, and fossils.
