# ALTERNATIVE TOOLS FOR FACILITATING THE MUNITORY OF DATA QUALITY

The companies are managing large volumes of data, which must be stored optimally in an appropriate infrastructure and which is scalable. However, the big problem facing organizations is the control and monitoring of data quality, since quality is such a relevant topic, there are still issues to be addressed in accordance with the importance it deserves. The work presented in this article offers a tour of experiences in tools that allow companies to monitor and control the quality of their data; for which different technological platforms were evaluated. Considering that the quality of the data incorporates different dimensions that facilitate this analysis, an exploratory review of them is incorporated in this work, they define Complexity and Validity as the dimensions to work on this challenge. A data quality exploration is initiated with free tools; then the exercise is continued with an analytical tool, very powerful and easy to use as SPSS Modeler and finally work on a licensed tool DQS Data Quality Server that facilitates the exercise of definition of rules and its applicability in the organizations
