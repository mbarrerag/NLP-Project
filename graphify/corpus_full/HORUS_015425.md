# LACTOBACILLUS CASE PROBIOTICAL CEPA BY LILIZATION USING EXTRACT OF COFFEE AS MATERIAL PARED

The protection of probiotic bacteria by encapsulation for incorporation into food matrices, leads to the search for wall materials with functional potential such as coffee extract (EXT).The objective of this study was to evaluate the potential of coffee extract as a wall material in terms of wetting (HU), hygroscopicity (HI), solubility (SO) and morphology for microencapsulation of the strain Lactobacillus casei ATCC 393 by lyophilization.For this purpose, formulations were evaluated with water, maltodextrin (MD), coffee extract (EXT), milk in defatting powder (LD) and inulin (IU) at three different concentrations.The obtained microcapsultivity, hygroscopicity and solubility, microstructurality of the proscopic micros and was determined by microscopic agents probing 1, 1 and 29, g3 H. The results obtained were evaluated by analysis of variance. The microcapsulas obtained presented values of Hume 8,1 and 11,9, HI between 30, 1 and 29, g3 H2 were assessed
