# BOUNDARY CONTROLLABILITY PERSPECTIVE OF THE GEAR-GRIMSHAW SYSTEM.

We investigate the precise boundary controllability of a nonlinear coupled system comprising two Korteweg-de Vries equations on a confined interval, representing the interaction between two weakly nonlinear gravity waves in a stratified fluid. The system necessitates six boundary conditions due to its inherent nature. However, in exploring the controllability aspect, we focus on varying combinations of control inputs, considering a maximum of four. To begin, we obtain results for the linearized system employing a classical duality approach, complemented by certain concealed regularity properties within the boundary terms. This methodology transforms the controllability challenge into addressing a spectral problem, which is effectively resolved through the Paley'Wiener method introduced by Rosier in 1997. Subse- quently, the key task is to ascertain under what conditions a specific quotient of entire functions remains an entire function. This entails solving a transcendental equation, akin to factoring an entire function contingent on the control configuration. Finally, utilizing the contraction mapping theorem, we establish local controllability for the complete system
