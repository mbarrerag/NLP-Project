# -Less crimes, better justice

"Less crimes, Better Justice_x0094_ is a research group endorsed by the Faculty of Law, Political and Social Sciences of the National University of Colombia, which was born on July 25, 2019, with the aim of building proposals for the decriminalization of certain criminal types under the theories of minimum criminal law. The group has been responsible for analyzing some social phenomena, in order, based on theories, jurisprudence, doctrine and statistics, to present projects to decriminalize some behaviors, according to social problems and based on the principle of the last ratio and criminal minimalism in a Social State of Law
