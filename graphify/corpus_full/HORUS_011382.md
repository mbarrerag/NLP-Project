# SYNTHESIS AND CHARACTERIZATION OF PD(II) AND AG(I) COMPLEXES WITH PYRAZOLE-DERIVED THIOSEMICARBAZONES

The exploration of the biological activity of a variety of compounds derived from pyrazole has been of interest in recent years due to the remarkable pharmacological activity of this heterocyclic ring that can be found in commercially available drugs [1,2]. Then, in this research have been synthesized attractive coordination compounds with pharmacological application of palladium(II) and silver(I) with the Schiff base (ligand L) produced by reaction of 1,3- diphenylpyrazole-4-carboxaldehyde and 4-ethylthiosemicarbazone. The ligand L has been synthesized by reflux conditions of a methanolic solution of pyrazolic aldehyde and thiosemicarbazide in 1:1 mol ratio and catalyzed with acetic acid. The Pd(II) complex was synthesized by reaction in ethanolic solution of L with PdCl2 in 2:1 mol ratio (L:Metal), at room temperature and constant stirring for 6 h. The Ag(I) complex was synthesized by reaction of AgNO3 with 1,10-phenanthroline as coligand in ethanol for 30 min and L for an additional 2 h, in 1:1:1 mol ratio (Metal:phen:L), at room temperature and constant stirring. The ligand L and its palladium(II) and silver(I) complexes have been elucidated by IR spectroscopy, NMR 1H, mass spectrometry and elemental analysis. The results of the analytical techniques used, showed that L was obtained as a thione tautomer and a neutral ligand in the coordination sphere of the complex [Ag(phen)L]NO3. However, the ligand shows coordination to Pd(II) as a thiolate tautomer suggesting that in the reaction of L with PdCl2, deprotonation occurred in the hydrazine group of thiosemicarbazone and the consequent formation of the anionic ligand in [PdL2]. In conclusion, the results showed that L coordinates as a bidentate ligand through the azomethine and thiolate groups to Pd(II) and thiona to Ag(I)
