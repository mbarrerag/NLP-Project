# Identification of different modes of operation in fixed flow hydraulic pumps with axial pistons using machine learning techniques.

This work seeks to identify different operating conditions in the axial shaft piston pumps diverted by predictive analysis techniques; the thesis exposes the identification of various situations of operation of the equipment, which allow extracting its main operating characteristics and establishing clear decision criteria for possible failures in the equipment. The axial shaft piston pump object of this study, is part of a injector machine from a major polyurethane injection company located in the city of Medellín, where the measurements were taken during the production process of foam injection and the different product references represent their different modes of operation. The taking of operating data was given through the measurement of temporary signals such as vibrations, acoustic, ultrasonic emissions and magnetic fields, discriminating different levels of equipment requirement. This measured information will have a stage of analysis and description in the domain of time and frequency by different techniques, thus facilitating a process of classification with strategies of clustering and neural networks; and finally the performance of the classification will be examined with graphical evaluation tools.
