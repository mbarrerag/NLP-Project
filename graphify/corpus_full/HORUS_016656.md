# CAP 11 SOCIOECONOMIC STRATEGY, A IMPORTANT FACTOR FOR HEALTH AND QUALITY OF LIFE IN PERSONS WITH CARDIOVOSCULAR DISEASE IN RURAL FIELD

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) constitute disorders associated with the circulatory system that include the heart, blood vessels and blood [1]. These diseases are part of the main public health problems in Latin America [2] and occupy, together with non-communicable diseases, the first places in mortality statistics worldwide. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), CVDs are the leading cause of death in the world. 17.5 million people have died from them and represent 31 % of all deaths recorded worldwide [3]. For Colombia, the mortality rate per 100 thousand inhabitants for CVD and diabetes is 166.7 for women and 205.9 for men [4], a situation equally worrying. During 2018, a high incidence of arterial hypertension was reported. Of a thousand million people who have contracted it, 50% of people are adults between the 50 and 60 years old [5]. In Colombia, the prevalence for 2017 was 7.6 %. The disease was reported in the main causes of external consultation and cause of medical emergencies [6].
