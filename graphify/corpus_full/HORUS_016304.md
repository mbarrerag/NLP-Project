# SIMULTANIC BIOFILTRATION OF ORGANIC AND INORGANIC COMPOUNDS GENERATORS OF OFE OILS DURING A COMPOST LACT

The offensive odours affect the health of the populations next to the sources of emission of the wastewater treatment plants (PTAR), as well as their workers. The biofiltration is the most used economic technology to treat this type of contamination produced by volatile inorganic compounds (IVCs), such as H2S and NH3, and volatile organic compounds (VOCs).A biofilter is a fixed bed bioreactor with immobilized microorganisms, in which the gases are oxidized by the microbial community to salts, CO2 and water. In this work, a prototype of the biofilter with cane/pollinaza bagasse compost bed was developed, to treat simultaneously an air current that simulated concentrations of H2S, NH3 and toluene were found in the PTAR-El Salitre pretreatment area.The removal of the H2S was 100%, for the concentration range between 5 -70 ppm, due to oxidation to SO42- by sulfur oxidating bacteria (concentration of 6,7x109 UFC/g)
