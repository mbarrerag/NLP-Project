# Pulmonary gas exchange in special environments: A narrative review

The gaseous exchange alveolo-capillar is one of the primary functions of the most evolved organisms. The description of its adaptive mechanisms in view of the exposure to the various special environments (height, aviation, hyperbaric environments and microgravity) becomes relevant to the frequent exposure to them. The conceptual difficulty and the scarce information in Spanish language on respiratory physiology in special environments, specifically of the capillary alveolo exchange, makes it necessary to propose strategies that allow its knowledge. Therefore, a narrative review was carried out that reviews the physiological elements involved in gaseous exchange at the pulmonary level in normal physiological conditions and in special environments (high mountain, aviation, submarinal and space). Through a search for key words in the databases PubMed, EMBASE and SCOPUS, 126 articles were selected for their elaboration. The physiologic exchange of the alveolo-capillar basic change of each of its determinants and its variations in special environments can be described.
