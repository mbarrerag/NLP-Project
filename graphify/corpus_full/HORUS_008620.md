# MEGADIVERSITY OF BLOOD PARASITIONS IN CASANARE-COLOMBIA HERPETOPHA

The department of Casanare tax has suffered a dramatic impact on the transformation of natural coverages as a consequence of extensive livestock, oil activity and constant burning. Despite this, it remains a focus of biodiversity in our country. In two field exits of the Colombia projectBio carried out in 2021 and 2022, in transition periods between end of summer and beginning of the rainy season, 263 herpetos were sampled, belonging to 4 orders, and 49 species.
