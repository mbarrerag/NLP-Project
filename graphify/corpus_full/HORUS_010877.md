# Careers in the field of intervention to domestic violence since Social Work 2011-2020

This research work was carried out with the aim of disentangle the trajectories of Social Work in the field of intervention of intrafamily violence between the years 2011 and 2020, from the Comisarias de Familia de Bogotá to understand the routes of the professionals. To this end, a qualitative investigation was proposed exploratory type, which used as tools the in-depth interview with professionals linked to the Comisarias de Familia and the review and documentary analysis of technical line and legislation that influenced the changes and permanences. The review of the theory of Pierre Bourdieu set the pillars to understand the trajectories from the point of view of interests, positions, capitals that allow to understand the field and the habitus of the profession, all under the view of intervention and intrafamily violence as transversal axes.
