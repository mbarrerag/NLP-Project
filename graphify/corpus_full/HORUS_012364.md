# Comparative analysis of three risk assessment methodologies for landslides, on the northern slope of Pereira airport.

This work presents an analysis at calibration level and contrast of three (3) different methodologies for risk zoning before mass removal phenomena, applied in a sliding of 200 meters wide by 300 meters of length, occurred in the urbanization Portal of La Villa, to the north of the runway of Pereira Airport in June 2019. With these results we sought to obtain a tool that allows to evaluate and qualify the risk for sliding applicable to other sectors. This new evaluation methodology was applied to a corridor of 2275 meters long by 300 meters of width, in the same sector north of the Pista of Pereira Airport, which preserves similar topographic and geological conditions. The first methodology is the one proposed by the Colombian Geological Service – National University in 2016, for which the risk zoning to the landslides occurred in the urbanization Portal of the Villa in the years 2019 and 2020. The second methodology, is called HSQI (Hillslope Quality Index) proposed by the School of Mines of the University of Nancy in 1989 (France), whose main applicability is to allow risk assessment by sliding in the years 2019 and 2020.
