# ANALYSIS OF SPACE DISTRIBUTION OF SAFETY EQUIPMENT BY GEOGRAPHICAL ACCESSIBILITY IN PITALITE (COLOMBIA)

The growth and development of municipalities are affected by various social and cultural problems that may hinder their proper functioning. Among these problems, insecurity stands out as one of the most significant, so government authorities require a considerable commitment to mitigate their impact on the population. In this context, the purpose of this analysis is to carry out a coverage analysis through the use of geographical accessibility, with the aim of evaluating the current situation of the security equipment system in the municipality of Pitalito, Huila. The purpose of this analysis is to identify areas with deficiencies and propose relevant solutions. The evaluation methodology includes the use of geostatistic models, which are implemented through statistical software and GIS (GIS); these allow the generation of graphs and images that facilitate the interpretation of the results obtained. Key words: geographic accessibility, coverage, equipment, security, GIS, travel time
