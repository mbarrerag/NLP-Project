# Secondary metabolites isolated from species of the Myristicaceae family that produce enzyme inhibition

Different species of the family Myristicaceae have been used for medicinal, nutritional and industrial purposes, thus showing the importance and potential of the family in various fields. The medicinal use has been paramount by different indigenous communities and has been increasing as an alternative to the treatment of diseases, so the research is concentrating on herbal medicine and medicinal plants, being this the first step for the development and innovation of drugs. Among the types of drugs are the enzyme inhibitors, which act by regulating metabolic processes or attacking pathogens. Collect information from the herbalistery of the family Myristicaceae that includes aspects of phytochemical, ethnobotany, industrial uses, biological activity and determine possible secondary metabolites that produce enzymatic inhibition and biological activity. The search for information was made with scientific articles, specialized books and grade thesis.
