# Methodology for obtaining dynamic properties of concrete buildings from environmental vibrations

In this thesis we propose a methodology to obtain the dynamic properties of concrete buildings from the measurement of environmental vibrations. The main objective is to contribute to the reduction of seismic vulnerability in structures by implementing dynamic monitoring techniques. To this, measurements were made in the building of science classrooms "Gloria Amparo Galeano Garcés" located on the campus of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá. A high precision accelerometer (SENSR CX1) was used to capture the vibrations at different points of the building, which allowed to register accelerations in the three main axes (X, Y, Z). The data obtained were processed using the Rapid Transformation of Fourier (FFT) in order to identify the natural periods of vibration. The analysis of the results showed a good correlation between the periods obtained from the environmental vibrations and those calculated by means of a modal analysis using the software ETABS to the same. The study concludes for the first modes of vibration, which are the most relevant in terms of structural response.
