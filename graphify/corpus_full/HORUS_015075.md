# Collaborative Economy: proposed conceptual bases for its study

ABSTRACT The present document presents the conceptual bases for the study of Collaborative Economy. The differences between the modalities of economic exchange emerging from technological mediation, such as the economics of concerts and digital economy, are presented. For this purpose, a bibliographic search was carried out based on the methodology of systematic revision of literature for social and economic sciences in Scopus, WoS and Redalyc in the period 2008-2019. This exercise allowed to place the collaborative economy as a new economic institution that has four subsectors: consumption, production, education and finance. The collaborative economy is distinguished by particular solutions, the way to solve the needs of people and in the creation of community. The document concludes that (a) it is important for the academy and states to look closely at the differences between the studied modalities and (b) the collaborative economy is a mechanism for social change
