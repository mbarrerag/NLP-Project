# THEORICAL PROPOSAL FOR THE ELABORATION OF A FERMENTED BeverAGE CERTAIN AIR CERTAIN TYPE WITH A CACCOA MUCILAGO AS INCREASE

Colombia occupies fifth place as a cocoa producer in Latin America, grains that are mainly intended for the processing of chocolate; however, the treatment of this fruit generates 70L mucilage/tonne of the fresh cocoa fruit, considered as residue. However, this mucilage pulp of flavor and characteristic aroma is constituted mainly of water and sugars, making up 40-52% of the fresh weight of the seed. Mucilage can be used as an alternative and differential ingredient in the elaboration of craft beer, thus obtaining another type of sensory profiles in terms of aromas, flavors and textures. The objective of this research was to theoretically explore by means of a systematic review of the literature the feasibility of the inclusion of mucilage as an ingredient in the elaboration of craft beer, showing that it is possible to make such incorporation using a stage of conditioning constituted by the mechanical depulsate of the almonds to obtain the exudate of this fruit that will be added in the process of macerating the beer, with the aim of transforming the starches into fermentable and non-fermentable sugars.
