# Yatul: Comprehensive Approach to the Conservation and Use of Native Seeds and Creoles to Strengthen Food Sovereignty in Misak Communities in the Department of Cauca

The project "Transforming the food systems to improve the livelihoods and environmental sustainability of two indigenous territories of Colombia - Territory, Food and Life - TCV" has been carried out by the National University of Colombia and the University of Cauca and financed by the International Development Research Center-IDRC of Canada. It is a process of co-creation between the indigenous communities and the academy. It has been identified needs and aspects that require strengthening to give sustainability to the food system, among them, the strengthening of production with the use of native seeds and criollas. In the process of co-creation and within the framework of the project, it is observed that in the territory there is diversity of native seeds and criollas that are being conserved by custodians, whose work has been reinforced by the creation of networks of custodians and community houses of seeds. Today, there is a morphological characterization and security of the communities, and the extension of the communities, and the recovery of ancestral memory Misak in relation to this important Andean tuberculo (Tombe, 2023).
