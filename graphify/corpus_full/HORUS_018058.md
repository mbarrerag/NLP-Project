# INTER-AMERICAN HOUSING CENTRE (FinVA), 1951-1972PORTS TO RESEARCH, DEVELOPMENT OF CONSTRUCTIVE TECHNICALS AND OTHER CONTRIBUTIONS IN VISION OF SOCIAL INTEREST (VIS)

In the early 1950s, the Inter-American Housing Centre (Cinva) was established under the auspices of the OAS to advance programmes for teaching, research, practical advice and exchange and dissemination of knowledge in housing construction and urban planning, under interdisciplinary criteria.During the years of its activity, it was the source of a significant number of academic documents and scientific products; in addition to being a center of technical innovation for Colombia and other Latin American countries, and of impact throughout the Western Hemisphere.This book compiles the contribution of Cinva and all its documentary collection dedicated to constructive techniques applied to housing of social interest (VIS) in the period 1951-1972.The study is divided into three parts; the first covers the conceptual framework of the theories and proposals of constructive technical order formulated from Cinva; the second, it is dedicated to establishing and evaluating its technical-constructive contributions; finally, the third part gives a state of the art of research in the field of constructive techniques, through selected cases. It is also an invitation to expand critical analysis in academic spaces from the search, reconstruction, study and recovery of memory
