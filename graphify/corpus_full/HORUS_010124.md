# Public and private relationship: activities and services on the ground floor and their context

During the 20th century, cities welcomed the criteria of modern urbanism, this event provoked a regulated vision of the city, starting with the implementation of planning models by use such as closed assemblies, suburban areas, among others, resulting in single functional zones. This, has led the city to transform its spatial configuration in a discontinuing and dispersed way, having consequences such as: the increase of the long distances in the urban routes, the disconnection of scales with the city, the neighborhood and the street, in this way the size of the apples and the height of the buildings has increased by losing the proportion with the street and affecting the relationship between the public and the private of the ground floor and its profile. From the above this Final Work of Maestria, there were defined a counterproposal of urban design for the district Tintalá. Based on the identification of specific situations and the study of theories with solving ideas, the construction of theoretical variables that, through a comparative exercise of two cases of study, were defined key design criteria for the construction of the corresponding counter-position. . ground floor and its profile.
