# RETURN WREATHES: LAND CONFLICT AND FORCED DEPLOYMENT IN BOLIVAR AND ATLANTIC HOMENAGE TO ALFREDO CORREA DE ANDREIS

Professor Alfredo Correa de Andreis was assassinated by paramilitaries on 17 September 2004 in the city of Barranquilla. Prior to his murder, he was pointed out by the Attorney General’s Office of the Nation to be guerrilla, so he was unjustly deprived of his freedom between June and July 2004. This sign- lamentation was due to an order of the Administrative Department of Security (DAS), today extinct, who acted in collusion with members of the self-defendants, to initially see Professor Alfredo Correa de Andreis as subversive and then proceed to execute it. (supreme court of justice, criminal court of cassation, 2011, ruling of 14 september, p. 101). Thus, the Council of State, by judgment of 6 November 2018, declared responsible the Nation, for the anti-juridical damages caused to the human rights defender, Mr Alfredo Correa de Andreis, on the occasion of the unjust investigation of his freedom (Council of State, 2018, judgement of 6 November), and ordered, among other measures of reparation: the development and completion of a research project, whose thematic axis of the defense is the professor of the State of the State of 2018
