# Indicator of environmental impacts associated with soil use and management.

Evaluating the ecological functions of soil and the associated impacts on its use and management is fundamental for the control and monitoring of degradation or restoration of this resource. Currently the tools developed to measure soil quality are complex, expensive and difficult to access for farmers, besides having a limited approach because they are only valid at regional scale.The aim of this study is to generate a simplified indicator that allows to identify the environmental impacts associated with the use and management of the soil.From evaluations of the chemical, morphological and biological physical quality (macroinvertebrate communities) carried out in China, France, Brazil, Peru and, Colombia plus a general macrofauna database; the possibility of designing a universal formula and based on the result was constructed a general formula that could have the most representative variables of the different aspects of soil quality.The calculation of the indicator was performed through multivariate analysis and for each of the data sets (these three types: physical, morphological and macrofauna) in 9 geographic locations.In addition, it was carried out the cross-appliance of the formulas of the indicators obtained in each site
