# Identification of events associated with crimes from audio signs using Wavelet Correlation Modes

The safety of urban environments has been a cause of concern for many years for people in all cities around the world. Depending on the place, the feeling of insecurity is present in all people to a greater or lesser extent, so that, from the governance of each region, mechanisms are continuously sought to prevent criminal acts. This work introduces a method whose purpose is to contribute to the prevention of the materialization of this type of events from audio signals that can be captured in an urban environment. To this end, an emerging technique called Wavelet Correlation Modes is used to represent audio signals in a compact way, and then implement an automatic learning method to classify audio signals in two different categories: audio signal associated with violent or criminal event or audio signal associated with a common event (Nonviolent or criminal)
