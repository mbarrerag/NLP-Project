# • The case of the documental mother of mothers.

This text reflects on the various possibilities, drifts and expansions that are generated in the production of contemporary documentary. The use of animation, hybrid narrative, expanded documentary are only some devices and artifacts that have become valuable tools to construct the individual and collective memory of the historical story in documentary cinema. To this end we will focus on the analysis of the Colombian documentary "The Mother of the Mothers" by Wilson Arango, 2016. This film, considered a hybrid narrative, uses photography and animation as tools to construct the narrative of the story that tells the disappearance of the son of Teresita Gaviria, leader of the Mothers of Candelaria in Colombia. The animation, in this context, is examined as an artifact and device that contributes to the construction of memory. However, given that this project is the result of a creation research, special attention will be paid to reflect on the video installation Atlas, a museum piece derived from the film, which transforms the way in which the viewer is linked to the concept of memory and that in turn deconstructs the film space transforming the relationship with the image
