# HYDROLOGIC DESIGN IN THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN THROUGH NEW IDF CURVAS

The study addresses the acquisition of 16 new Intensity-Duration-Frequency (IDF) curves from the digitization of 4000 pluviographic charts for the Colombian Caribbean region, totaling 42 curves. The temporal and spatial variation of the intensities is analyzed, validating the prediction model by IDW. Significant differences in intensities are observed in stations in Norte de Santander and Montería due to changes in the precipitation regime. The higher intensities are recorded in Barrancabermeja, Antioquia and in northern Córdoba, while the lower ones are in Norte de Santander and southern Bolívar. The interpolation prediction shows an increase in error by expanding the analysis area and increasing the number of stations considered. IDF curves contribute significantly to hydrological design, therefore, the need to construct and update more curves for this region, and in general for all Colombia.
