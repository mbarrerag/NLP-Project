# THE ORGANIZATIONAL CLIMATE OF A UNIVERSITY IN TIMES OF THE COVID 19

to diagnose the organizational climate (CO) in times of COVID-19 of the administrative staff of the University of the Valley - Palmira Headquarters. Design/methodology: descriptive, with qualitative approach and inductive method. An eight-dimensional questionnaire and a semi-structured interview were used. The results of the Google form were processed in Excel and the interviews were categorized and analyzed through the software ATLAS.ti version 7.5.7. The sample was the totality of a population of twenty-seven people, of which twenty-four answered the questionnaire. For the interview three representative members of the University were selected. a positive CO with high favorability index (6.1 was found in a Likert scale of 1 to 7). The training dimension obtained the highest score. This result was associated with the rapid adaptation and commitment of the University to the implementation of strategies for fluid communication and an atmosphere of mutual support in all areas.
