# INSTITUTIONAL STRATEGIC PLANNING: BUILDING OF A UNIVERSITY COLLECTIVE PROJECT

In recent years, the National University of Colombia has undertaken a significant path in the construction of the main elements of strategic institutional planning; the Strategic Institutional Plan to 2034 and the Global Development Plans for the periods 2019-2021 and 2022-2024, represent the collective work between managers, teachers, administrative, students and thematic experts to imagine, formulate and implement the university transformations in the medium and long term. With the objective of permanently advising the management of the University in terms of institutional planning, the National Directorate of Planning and Statistics has called the Planning System to plan and manage the institutional development in the short, medium and long term, ensuring the fulfilment of the institutional goals in a harmonious and coherent way, thus guaranteeing the path for the development of the University (Agreement 238 of 2017).In the framework of this work it is important to say that the processes, activities and exercises of strategic planning of the University are structured around the identification and organization of its leadership, the achievement and allocation of financial resources that leverage a diversity of institutional resources and capacities (intellectual, social, relation to, etc. The main objective is to strengthen institutional transformations based on the achievement of strategic goals defined in policy documents and which allow for organized and conscious actions to transform the adverse scenarios facing the University. The results of this management are monitored and evaluated on a permanent basis, so that, in addition to having data that feed the monitoring of projects, information is also available for society. Simultaneously, the University has tools and tools that consolidate the efforts undertaken by the National Directorate of Planning and Statistics and that are closely related to the instruments of the Planning System.
