# 6. DYNAMIC WATER IN SOUND AND CACCOA ARBOLE

The world demand for cocoa has caused the cultivation of crops in different regions with conditions different from tropical humid areas where it has traditionally been cultivated. Although the market has been increasing, yields do not grow in the same proportion and it can be said that among the factors of production the water resource is limiting, both by deficit and by excess. At these times, the change of climate, especially the manifesto by the El Niño phenomenon, affects agricultural production. In this sense it was evidenced that the reduction of the rains between 30 and 60% (in 2015) and the increase of the temperature decreased the sprouting, so that the period of dormancy and affected the vegetative development, also caused an anticipated flowering, but with fewer flowers and consequent decrease of production by 30% (Urueta et al., 2017)
