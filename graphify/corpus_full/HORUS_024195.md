# Rural practices, narratives and expertise in the face of global environmental transformations in the upper Río Blanco basin, near the Chingaza Paramo, Colombia

The research is carried out in the upper basin of the Río Blanco which is part of the Chingaza system, infrastructure built and managed by the Company of Aqueduct and Sewerage of Bogotá -EAAB- for the water supply of the city of Bogotá and other municipalities, reason why it is addressed as a hydro-social territory in which multiple interests converge that are identified from the genealogy of the territory and recent relevant events, framed in the implementation of two projects, that of the Corridor of Páramo Conservation and the project Integrated National Adaptation Plan (INAP).
