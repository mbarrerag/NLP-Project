# ANALYSIS OF TECHNICAL DEBT IN SOFTWARE AGIL DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS

Agile development projects require the construction and delivery of software in short iterations. This agility implies that the developed software product accumulates errors and defects produced by the team by not applying good quality practices to meet delivery deadlines. This accumulation of errors and defects is called technical debt. The aim of this work is to describe a causal model of identifying the main variables related to the causes and consequences of technical debt in software projects that use the agile Scrum process. The results show that technical debt is an aspect that must be properly managed to ensure the successful delivery of software products achieving customer satisfaction
