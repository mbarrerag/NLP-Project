# Embedded system in FPGA for analysis of electrochemical data with artificial intelligence

Due to the need to implement strategies that allow to process data and generate results in real time with devices that, among others, present autonomy, are small size, scalable and that provide the possibility of their reproducibility, it is necessary to generate research from the seedbed to determine possible systems that provide these characteristics and that can take advantage of the advantages of producing in-situ results for processes where the use of sensors and analysis of the data is required. FPGAs as a quick prototyping element provide advantages that can be exploited in the digital design saving manufacturing times and enabling the tasks of verification and validation. In turn, considering the concept of System on Chip (SOC) it is possible to provide these devices with capacities that can exceed the capacities of simpler cards when integrating for example GPUs, CPUs, RAMs and cached as well as inputs and outputs associated with the signals to be processed or on which it will act.
