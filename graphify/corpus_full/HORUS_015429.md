# CHARACTERIZATION OF COCOA MUCILAGE AS A SOURCE OF PECTIN AND SUGARS FOR POTENTIAL USE IN THE FOOD INDUSTRY

Cocoa mucilage or pulp is a by-product obtained before the fermentation process. It has been used as a raw material for the manufacture of jams, alcoholic beverages, among others. However, although it is mainly made up of sugar and dietary fiber, few studies have been conducted on the content and characterization of pectin extracted from cocoa mucilage for use in the food industry
