# Feasibility of energy communities in Colombia's national interconnected system

This final work focuses on studying the implementation of energy communities in Colombia, addressing regulatory, technical and regulatory aspects. The real problem identified is the need to promote the transition to a more sustainable and fair energy in the country. The advances in solving the problem include the evolution of the regulatory framework to allow consumers to become prosumers, generating, storing and sharing energy within energy communities. However, there are recognized limitations as institutional barriers and financial obstacles that hinder the effective implementation of these initiatives. The general objective of the document is to evaluate from an institutional perspective the regulatory and regulatory frameworks that support the creation and operation of energy communities in Colombia. The proposed methodology implies a detailed analysis of the current regulatory framework, the identification of barriers and obstacles, the strengthening of institutional capacity, and the promotion of sustainability and energy transition. The main expected contribution of the final job is to propose strategies for communities to produce, consume and manage their own renewable energy, thus contributing to energy sustainability and the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions in Colombia. .
