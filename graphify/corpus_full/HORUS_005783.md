# CRISP-2 PROTEIN AND ANTIOXIDANT ENZYMES OF SEMINAL PLASMA ARE POSITIVELY RELATED TO THE QUALITY OF DONKEY SEMEN

Seminal plasma (SP) in equids is involved in several events that precede fertilization, such as sperm motility activation, protection, nutrition, transport and its interaction with the oocyte. In addition, SP represents the highest antioxidant defence available to spermatozoa; enzymes such as catalase (CAT), superoxide dismutase (SOD) and glutathione peroxidase (GPX) are the main enzymes with antioxidant properties, which have been related to seminal quality in stallions.
