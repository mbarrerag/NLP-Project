# PASTIZAL HAND AND PORTRIER ROTATION FOR THE WATER BUFAL'S CREATION.

Normally, herbivores do not shepherd evenly over the landscape, but repeatedly consume plants and patches of preferred vegetation, and often the structures of the plants they harvest are those of the best nutritional value. In extensive and continuous grazing plants are subjected by animals to multiple severe defoliations without sufficient recovery time, in addition, overgrazing can favor the exposure and degradation of the soil in some highly susceptible areas; causing the preferred plants to tend to disappear (die) and allowing less desirable plants, which are arvenous species or commonly called weeds, to prevail and expand by decreasing the productive potential of the meadow
