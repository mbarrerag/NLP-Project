# Proposal of didactic strategy for teaching theory and practice of control of dynamic systems integrating learning styles.

This thesis arises from the need to establish a connection between educational methodologies and the learning styles of students in order to understand concepts with a high level of abstraction as the concepts of control.For this, didactic strategies oriented to laboratory were designed, for four concepts of control of dynamic systems, integrating active educational methodologies and the learning style of Felder and Silverman. The selected concepts were: Effect of the actions of control Proportional Integral Derivative (PID), linearization and point of operation, effects of feedback and response in time. For each concept a laboratory guide was designed, a rubric of competences and a perception survey, which were implemented in the course Control: Entry-Out of the National University of Colombia Medellín, in three academic periods. As for the results of the application of the diagnostic instrument of learning styles, it was obtained that, of the 62 students who participated in the study, 74% had a sensitive style, 58% an active style, 84% a visual style and 58% a sequential style, therefore, the results of the sesssitative and visual styles were predominant among the students. and 76% were clear about the concept of feedback effects thanks to the laboratory guide. Therefore, the didactic strategies designed were relevant to the teaching of the four selected concepts
