# Human right to adequate food: relevance of food in the process of resocialization of persons deprived of their liberty in the Temporary Detention Centre- Arauca police station, Arauca

The situation of persons deprived of their liberty-PPL in the municipality of Arauca, is a reflection of what is happening in Colombia in relation to the systematic and prolonged violation of fundamental rights that are not suspended or restricted because of their relationship of special subject to the State. The Constitutional Court declared since 1998 the State of Incorporational Things-ECI in relation to this situation of persons deprived of their liberty. This research analyses the relevance of the human right to adequate food – DHAA in Persons Depripripripripri de la Libertad - PPL as one of the central axes in the process of resocialisation. This research established that the physical, psychological, cultural and social functions are reduced due to the deficiencies in the provision of service to PPLs not only in terms of quantity, quality, safety, opportunity, but in conditions of dignity. That is to say, the PPL suffer hunger, exposure to disease risks to biological pollution of food and non-dific treatment.
