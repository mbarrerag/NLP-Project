# FUNCTIONAL DIVERSITY IN COLOMBIA FORESTS

The 10-year research on dry forest is collected through a series of documents presented in an integrated manner in this publication, which begins with a synthesis of figures and data that reflect the state of ecosystems; a state of where and how these identified fragments are located; which records of plants, coprophagous beetles and amphibians are found in this ecosystem; what are the threats that fall on the dry forest and which management activities are developing. The permanent parcel monitoring network is presented in which data from functional traits and rare species allow management decisions to be made on it. It also refers to multiscale and multifunctional research and monitoring strategies, as well as their relationship with strengthening actions to conserve tropical dry forest. This compilation ends by visualizing the public availability of information resources generated by multiple entities in the country, which are the basis of a democratization of knowledge as the basis of the incidence and relevance of scientific research
