# APPROACHES IN DISEASE STUDENTS

Self-assessment in higher education programmes results in new questions given the characteristics that are identified in each stage. In nursing programs, variables are found in students that may affect learning as age, job linkage that motivates them to investigate the learning approaches that students adopt in relation to these variables. To identify learning approaches and their relationship with the age, academic average and employment situation of nursing students. Descriptive study, with a sample of 74 students from the III to VIII semesters of the nursing program, the collection of information was given with the RSPQ-2F instrument. The deep approach predominant in 38% of students, followed by 25% in superficial approach, 37% was placed in a strategic/undetermined approach, no significant statistical differences were found with age, academic average and their current work linkage. The identification of the type of learning approach adopted by each student allows teachers to project strategies to strengthen quality learning. It is pertinent to investigate other variables that influence learning approaches, since the academic average and their current work linkage are scarce
