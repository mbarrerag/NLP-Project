# Deacidification of vegetable oils used by solvent extraction method.

This research evaluates the technical feasibility of extraction with solvents of free fatty acids present in used vegetable oils. The first part of the research was carried out through a extraction process in batch operated agitated tank systems, in which ethanol was selected as the best solvent for the process and the best temperature conditions (60 °C), mass oil ratio:solvent (1:2) and water content (4%) in the solvent. At these conditions the removal was 77% acidity, 37% color, 53% polar, and oil losses in the solvent were less than 0.8%. On the other hand, it was found that properties such as peroxide index, iodine index, saponification index, anisidine index and fatty acid distribution are not affected by the extraction process. Subsequently, extraction was performed in a contactor of descending liquid film, which allowed continuous operation and the best results were obtained with a more oil ratio: ethanol of 1:2, oil flow 0.74 kg/h and a packing length of 1.07 m.
