# Assessment of the Impact of Special Permits of Permanence on the Wages of Venezuelan Migrants in Colombia (2018-2022)

This paper analyzes the impact of the Special Permits of Permanence (PEP) on the salaries of Venezuelan migrants in Colombia between 2018 and 2022. At the starting point, the main theories of migration are explored to provide a conceptual framework that allows understanding the Venezuelan migration phenomenon towards Colombia and the Latin American context in which it is developed. The study assesses the effect of the PEP on the monthly income of migrants, focusing on their ability to improve well-being and reduce poverty. The impact assessment is key to feedback and optimization of social programmes, providing valuable information for decision-making in public policies. The results show that the PEP has had a positive and significant impact on the income of migrants, with a noticeable increase after 2020.
