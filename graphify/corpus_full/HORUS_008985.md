# The role of rhetorical devices in public policy systems frameworks: Comparative case study: Process of elaboration, presentation and approval of the Bogotá Development Plan by Gustavo Petro (2012) and Enrique Peñalosa (2016)

This work is addressed from the rhetorical relationship and public policies with the aim of opening theoretical and methodological paths for rhetorical analysis. It is oriented towards the identification and interpretation of discursive trajectories that allow evidence, from the concept of rhetorical device, tensions and complementarities that trace argumentation and public deliberation. The state of art elaborated allows to show how the role of rhetoric in public policies has not been studied in depth and its theoretical and methodological approach is limited. In order to advance the consolidation of an interpretative approach to the analysis of policies from rhetoric, the question is posed by the role of rhetorical devices in policy planning. This is addressed from an empirical study comparing cases of local planning, in which it seeks to visualize retorical devices regarding the development, presentation and approval of the development plan of the city of Bogotá, before the Council of Bogotá, in the administrations of Gustavo Petro (2012) and Enrique Peñalosa (2016).
