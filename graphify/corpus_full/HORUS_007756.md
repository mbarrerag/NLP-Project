# Cross-border dynamics, sovereignty and security in the Colombian Caribbean: Strengthening national research group, region and international relations in the Caribbean and Latin America

This group aims to study the issues related to the formation of the nation-state, the region and the international relations that are presented in the nation-region construction processes in the Caribbean and Latin America. From this purpose three research agendas will be developed with central themes that have a connection to deepen and achieve the main objective
