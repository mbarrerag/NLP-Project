# IGU ADAPTIVE MODEL RSL IN MSM MODLE SYSTEMS, USING MACHINE LEARNING TECHNICALS

The document presents a systematic review of the literature on adaptability and adaptability of Graphic User Interfaces (IGU) in LMS systems using machine learning techniques. Several databases were used, including IEEExplorer, Science Direct, Google Academic, Journal Citation Reports, Scopus, and specific search criteria were applied to select relevant studies. the results indicate that there is a large amount of research on usability and user experience in LMS system user interfaces, but very few studies on adaptability and adaptivity. the discussion focuses on the importance of adaptability and adaptability in LMS systems and the need for further research in this subject. This study provides an overview of existing research on usability and user experience with user interfaces in LMS systems and highlights the need for further research on adaptability and adaptivity in this context
