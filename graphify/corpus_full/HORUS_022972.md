# Methodology for the analysis of the behaviour of wooden roof systems in heritage buildings subjected to seismic movements by scale models: Case study: Doctrinero Temple of Oicata (Boyaca), analysis of interaction with walls

This master's thesis addresses the determination of a phase of the methodology for the analysis of the behavior before seisms of systems of roofs in wood, based on the carpentry of Spanish armament in heritage buildings in the Cundiboyacense Altiplano, within the framework of the program of research on seismic behavior of the historical buildings. It aims to study these systems in their interaction with the walls carrying, by means of the development of models to scale and structural models of heritage buildings, that are carried to the fault by means of tests and tests on vibration table, in order to show the mechanisms of damage due to an earthquake and with this to define the procedure most suitable for its repair. To carry out this research, the temple of doctrine of Oicata, Boyacá, in the first place by its historical importance and clear state of conservation, in spite of the Spanish colony, has been considered one of the first churches of the indigenous population being currently one of the oldest in Latin America; and to observe, for its structural integrity and the present state of conservation, despite the fact of the first-effects of the Spanish colony, has suffered from the first- action with being walls, through the development of scale models and structural modeling of heritage buildings, which are raised to fail through trials and tests on a vibrating table, to show the damage mechanism caused by an earlierquake and then defines the most appropriate procedure for their repair.
