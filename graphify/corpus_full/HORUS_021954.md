# Development of a predictive model to determine the geographical origin of corbical pollen in Boyacá.

The production of corbical pollen is a very important livestock activity in Colombia, especially in the department of Boyacá. Obtaining a Designation of Origin seal for this product can add value and improve the economic conditions of producers. However, it is essential to have mechanisms that corroborate if a product sample comes from a given region to verify the origin of the product. Unfortunately, the current geographic verification strategies are costly and difficult to access for a producer. In this work, a new model based on color segmentation and supervised machine learning was proposed to automatically identify the geographical origin of a pollen sample from digital images acquired under controlled conditions. The proposed strategy achieved high performance, in particular F1-score=0.85, suggesting that the method can determine with a high level of certainty the producer and, consequently, the geographical origin
