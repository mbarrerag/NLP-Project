# STANDARDS FOR LEARNING STUDENTS OF THE CONTROL COURSE: ENTRY-SALIDE

This article presents the characterization of the learning styles of the students of the course Control: Entry-Exit, according to the model of learning styles of Felder and Silverman. To this end, a group of students were asked to initiate a diagnostic instrument of learning styles proposed by Felder and Silverman. With the aim of validating the premise of Felder and Silverman about learning styles for engineers, the first analysis was carried out independently of the level of preference of each style. Chi-square tests were carried out to validate whether there was a difference in portion between styles. Then, for styles in which there was such a difference, a portion test was performed to know what style the trend is. A second analysis was carried out according to the level of preference of each learning style, taking into account the scale of the model, whether it was mild, moderate or strong. The study was carried out during three academic periods and through the analyses carried out validated the premise of Felder and Silverman for only two learning styles.
