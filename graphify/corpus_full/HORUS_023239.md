# IMPORTANCE OF THE GENETIC OF BISHES IN MELIPONICULTURE

Review of the genetics of bees without sting, with emphasis on the angelite bee Tetragonisca angustula, molecular markers for the study of populations of species in the Neotropics and importance of knowledge of their biology, ecology and genetics in meliponiculture, allowing the use of their ecosystem services without detriment to their natural populations.The study of genetics contributes to the knowledge of the natural populations of bees without sting, to understand aspects of their biology, ecology and behavior that allow to recognize their biodiversity and to study the impacts that can have for the species the biotic and abiotic factors of the habitats it occupies.This is particularly important because it has been suggested that the angelite bee corresponds to a complex of species, which means that, although it is recognized morphologically as T. angustula, the genetics of its populations can be unique and differential in populations along the wide geographical range that occupies the species.Based on DNA analysis, some authors suggest that the transport of nests should be carried out only within and between genetically similar populations, so that it must be invested in studies leading to genetics, structure and structure
