# Artificial Intellience, Digital Transformation, and Education, as a Combined Contribution to Society

This book combines technology and humanism, applied to education. It focuses on digital transformation, artificial intelligence and the intangible components that link the two, such as ethics and learning, in a transversal and, at the same time, sufficiently specific way, through use cases and concrete application examples. We should not forget that technological progress, however exponential it may be, it is still a support to human development, as a collective and as individuals. If it does not serve its purpose, as much as shining that it might look, it will be useless by nature. Through a large dozen of chapters from the five continents, this edition provides a multicultural, polyhedric vision with a diversity of topics that revolve around the main concerns mentioned above. We hope that reading the book will provide some recommendations or, at least, reflections on these very current matters, which will continue to be relevant in the years to come
