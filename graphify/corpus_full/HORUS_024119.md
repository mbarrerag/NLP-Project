# Evaluation of adsorption of hexavalent chromium and heterogeneous fenton for the treatment of synthetic tanning waste water using iron nanoparticles supported in coffee husks.

The treatment of waste water in the tanning industry of the municipality of Belén, Nariño is of the utmost importance for the communities of this region, mainly because in the processes of processing leather they use sulfates of chromium, sodium and ammonium; and other reagents, which load liquid residues of pollutants of difficult degradability; hence, they are poured to the bodies of natural water, approximately 322 m3/ day of effluent, without any prior treatment. For this reason, in this work a technology was proposed adsorption and fenton heterogeneous as an alternative of treatment, in terms of efficiency of removal of pollutants, operating costs and availability of raw materials. In the document is presented the process of synthesis and characterization of an adsorbente material of coffee squee and the same impregnated area with zero-valent iron nanoparticles (nZVI) for the adsorption of hexified chromium in solution (Cr VI). In detail a slight pretreatment with citric acid to eliminate soluble compounds and improve the structure of the nelic acid, hemylylline and octillaline in the same solution of the consumin, hemoil (s) min. On the other hand, the best material impregnated with nZVI was selected for the heterogeneous fenton processes where the effects of H2O2/DQO, pH and nZVI/H2O2 were studied in the elimination of chemical demand for oxygen (DQO), total phenols (FT) and Cr VI with initial concentrations of 3277 mgO2/L, 200 mg/L and 25 mg/L, respectively, of a synthetic tanning waste water. 95.24% of COD, 97.54% of FT and 99.82% of Cr (VI) were eliminated. According to the results of this master's thesis, it is recommended to evaluate the sustainability and potential application of the processes analyzed to tanning industry currents
