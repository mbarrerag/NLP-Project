# EVALUATION OF THERMAL PARAMETERS WITH GEOTHERMAL ENERGY RECOVERY FROM A COLD CLIMATE MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE LANDFILL

This study presents finite element method (FEM) modeling with COMSOL Multiphysics to 18 estimate the thermal properties of a waste landfill through back-analysis of temperature responses 19 from buried thermistors during a full-scale multi-week active thermal response test. Field tests 20 were conducted at the Loraas MSW landfill in Saskatoon, Canada, using instrumented equipment, 21 including a vertical borehole heat exchanger and thermistor strings. The test consisted of two 22 phases: a heat injection test during the summer and a heat extraction test in the winter. The study 23 focused on a landfill 25 meters deep, observing temperature changes at various depths over time. 24 The effect of heat generation rate (HGR) on landfill temperature was investigated. Thermal 25 conductivity and volumetric heat capacity increase with depth, with close alignment between 26 summer and winter results, validating the tests. This research enhances the understanding of 27 thermal parameters in MSW landfills by combining field tests and numerical modeling, offering 28 insights into landfill behavior in cold climates. These findings hold significance for optimizing 29 geothermal energy recovery from such landfills, calibrating thermal parameters for coupled 2 30 models in landfills, and improving waste management practices
