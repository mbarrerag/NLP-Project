# Evaluation of spatial analysis algorithms for modeling future changes in plant cover and land uses.

The objective of this study is to analyze the differences in the results and execution of different algorithms for the modelling of future scenarios applied to plant cover and soil uses, in order to identify the natural and anthropic dynamics of different ecosystems in the study area, where several factors and explanatory variables are integrated such as classifications of use and soil cover monitored from satellite images, classifications of use and soil coverage emitted by government entities, soils, pathways, topography, hydrography, conservation zones, among others; which will be processed to define future scenarios types at a given time using remote sensors and geographic information systems (GIS) to determine which method produces best results, it is proposed to work with cellular automata, contained in the MOLUSCE module of QGIS software, and the EGO Dynamic software and generate a geoprocess logbook that explains how the results vary with each variable; Finally, the validation process and error statistics will be performed for each method
