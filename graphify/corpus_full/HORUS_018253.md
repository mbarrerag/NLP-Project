# Analysis of diffusion models by nuclear magnetic resonance imaging with machine learning.

This paper presents the characterization of the curves of attenuation by voxel to voxel diffusion of 4 imaging sets (one prostate, two human brain and one brain ex vivo of a benign neoplasia).This characterization includes the determination of diffusion values (D), pseudo-diffusion (D*), infusion (f) and curtosis (K) using the methods of ADC (mono-exponential) and IVIM (bi-exponential) with and without curtosis.These values are used as a reference for training and testing the validity of several machine learning algorithms (ML) which allow to decrease the time in the characterization of attenuation. To decide whether in a voxel there is diffusion, Dtra-Tree Clasifier algorithms are implemented by the precision and the test AUC.While the modality (LR) regression (L) is applied by C-Support vector (SVC), Extra-Gradient Boost (XGB) and perceptron multilayer (ML) are evaluated by the poli-Tree, and the variable test.
