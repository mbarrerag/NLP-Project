# 16. GUIDE OF CLINICAL PRACTICE FOR THE CARE OF THE FAMILY OF HOSTED CHILDREN IN THE ICU-P

In the framework of the celebration of the centenary of its foundation, the Faculty of Nursing presents in this work the most recent research developments of nine of its groups. The chapter of Gui ́a de practica ́clinica for the care of sickmeria to families of hospitalized children in the ICU-P contains a series of reliable and updated recommendations in the face of the main phenomena and situations of care that the nursing professional has to face, as well as proposals for efficient interventions, that guarantee better decision-making in the daily work of his professional practice and an optimal management of care
