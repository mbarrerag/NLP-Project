# APPLICATION OF ENZIMATIC AND OUTER PRETRATTINGS FOR THE EXTRACTION OF CAFEIN IN COFFEE PULPA

In this research, coffee pulp of the Castillo variety, cultivated in La Paz, Cesar, was used. Coffee was collected manually, selecting mature grains, and the particle size was reduced between 400-600 microns, with an average humidity of 7.43% and a density of 311.25 kg/m3. An enzyme pretreatment was applied with two formulations, Viscozyme and Cellic CTec2, to break down cellulose, hemicellulose and lignin. Different substrate/enzyme ratios were evaluated and curves were constructed to determine the optimal dose.
