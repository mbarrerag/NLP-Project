# Method for classifying vehicle type and plate number according to to toll collection classifications in Colombia, using artificial intelligence techniques

This work addresses the problem of the congestion of vehicles in the tolls of Colombia, where delays generate high logistic costs and a negative impact on the environment. Despite the advances in road infrastructure, current charging systems, based mainly on cash or the limited use of the TAG system, are insufficient to manage the increasing volume of traffic. This study proposes a method for the classification of vehicles and the recognition of registrations using artificial intelligence techniques, in order to improve the efficiency in the toll systems. A methodology based on video-analytic and optical character recognition (OCR), which allows the automatic identification of the type of vehicle and its plate, according to the current collection classifications in Colombia. Through the use of convolutional neural networks (CNN) and pre-trained models such as MobileNet and ResNet, was developed an adequate performance in the classification of vehicles, being ResNet the model with greater precision. Additionally, areas of improvement in the recognition of registrations were identified, suggesting that a greater amount of data and adjustments in the techniques used could optimize the system. This work raises the possibility of implementing charges of toll fully automatic, improving, matic identification of vehicle types and license plates according to Colombia’s toll fee classifications. Using convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and pre-trained models such as MobileNet and ResNet, an adaptation performance was achieved in vehicle classification, with ResNet showing the high accessibility. Additionally, areas for improvement were identified in license plate recognition, suggesting that a long dataset and adaptations to the applied techniques could further optimize the system. This study demonstrates the reliability of implementing fully automated toll collection systems, enhancing mobility and reducing wait times and environmental impact
