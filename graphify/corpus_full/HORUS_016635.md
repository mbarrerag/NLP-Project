# ANALYSIS OF GENDER VIOLENCE IN MEDELLIN CITY, ADJUSTING A MODEL OF MULTINOMIAL LOGISTICAL RETURN WITH A BAYESIAN APPROACH

This study presents an analysis of the different forms of gender-based violence recorded in the city of Medellín, from 2013 to 2020, and the incidence of these in the six areas that make up the city. For the respective analysis of the study, a multi-nomial logistic regression model was implemented with a Bayesian approach. The database of monitoring of gender-based violence in public health is used, of the Information and Knowledge Management Unit, which contains the registry of patients attended by health-care providers with a suspected diagnosis of gender-based violence cases in the city of Medellín, to monitor and activate a comprehensive treatment route for victims
