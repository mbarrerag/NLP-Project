# Method for classifying driving styles based on machine learning techniques, using physiological and sensory signals captured by wearable devices. Case Study: Drivers of the Metropolitan Area of the Aborra Valley.

The classification of driving styles using machine learning is a novel proposal to the public health problem and the macroeconomic impacts that represents the loss of life due to road accident. In this work the use of smart watches to monitor various aspects of the driver and for the classification tasks have been implemented algorithms of support vector machines (SVM), decision trees and naive sorter of bays. As a main conclusion is shown the correlation that sleep patterns have with the driving style, as well as the relevance of the use of these devices to the construction of a good quality data set
