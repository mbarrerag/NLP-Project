# Dependence between log-return time series and log-return time series on stock markets.

This thesis involves research based on the study of the empiric data on the existence of relations between the series of time of daily transaction volumes of shares and the series of daily price of shares for different international markets. To this end, the data of daily price achievers of 230 shares of 23 μndices different to stable Levy probability distributions are first adjusted, and it is shown that the variance of these series is related to the fraction of the parameters of scale adjustment and form of distribution, by means of a law of powers. It is then found that the data of daily volume achievers of 23 μndices conform to the loggistic distribution of prices, also shows the existence of a law of powers between the variance and the fraction of the parameters of scale adjustment and form of the distribution are adjusted to each
