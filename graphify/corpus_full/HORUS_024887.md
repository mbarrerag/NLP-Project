# Behaviour of piles loaded laterally in a medium that hardens by deformation.

This document describes the behavior of a laterally charged pile in a medium that hardens by deformation, which is evaluated through the Hardening Soil Constitutive Model and the implementation of a robust numerical method such as the incremental finite elements. The evaluation of the behavior is carried out in a short pile and a long pile, which are predicted from a carrying capacity analysis. The analyses detail the nature of the deformations and the stresses in the floor-pilot interface, the delimitation of the soil plasticization zones, as well as the cutting forces and fluctuating moments of the pilot generated by the incremental application of a lateral load in the pilot head. The behavior for both lengths of the pilot is compared for a soil with hardening by deformation and an elastic, perfectly plastic soil. As a final product, the variation of the cymation reaction modules with depth is obtained, as a function of the magnitude of the applied load.
