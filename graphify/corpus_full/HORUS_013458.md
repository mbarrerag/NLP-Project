# Representativeist disjunctivism and the explanatory role of experience

In this work we seek to defend an approach of intentionalism in the debate around the presentational character of the experience and its explanatory role that is presented among the various disjunctivist variants with respect to perceptual experience. Thus, we intend to show the possibility of articulating a response to the question about the role of experience with respect to our thinking about objects that is not only disjunctivist, but that subscribes to the representational principle and that, in turn, is compatible with a conceptualistic version of intentionalism. The general idea has to do with showing that the disjuntivist approach of McDowell possesses tools that allow him to answer the criticisms that from relationalism are posed to his exposition regarding the explanatory role of experience. .
