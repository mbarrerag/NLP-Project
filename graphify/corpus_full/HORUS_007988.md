# CHARACTERIZATION OF TERRITORIES WITH MAJOR AFFECTION OF ARMED CONFLICT IN COLOMBIA

The historical disturbance of the armed conflict in Colombia has involved the affectation of the various economic, social, environmental, cultural and political spheres. By involving a rupture and disarticulation of the different institutional synergies, which, should be oriented to guarding, strengthening and promoting the rights and duties of all the individuals of the national territory.Therefore, if the municipalities are analyzed, there is a considerable disparity between the institutions that must make a territorial presence and their institutional dynamics, which are the actions that reflect the problems of the community, presenting with greater intensity in the municipalities of greatest impact of the armed conflict (Garcia, 2013, p. 5). To attend the exercise of characterization of the population with greater impact of the armed conflict in Colombia, it seeks to contribute in an approximation to the various contexts framed in the needs, interests, expectations and preferences of the target population, as well as the adjustment of the institutional offer and the provision of information of great importance in the processes of regional intervention (dnp, 2018). For the above, the document proposes a general framework in which aspects of the natural regions of Colombia, as well as the specific orientation of municipalities subject to study (Quabdó, Ocó, 2018)
