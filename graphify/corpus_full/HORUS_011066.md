# `OMICS¿ APPROACHES FOR CROP IMPROVEMENT

The growing human population and climate change are imposing unprecedented challenges on the global food supply. To cope with these pressures, crop improvement demands enhancing important agronomical traits beyond yield, such as adaptation, resistance, and nutritional value, by pivoting direct and indirect selection approaches. The development of next-generation high-throughput screening technologies, referred to as `omics¿, promises to speed up plant trait improvement while producing more sustainable crops
