# OVERVIEW AND CHARACTERIZATION OF PARAGENTIC SECUE TO TROGRAPHICAL ANALYSIS, FLOW INCLUSIONS AND RAMAN SPECHOBIA, OF MINERALIZATION PUT IN THE TOMORROW-POMPONIO-SANTODOMING, OF THE MINA LA PAVA, MUNICIPIO DE QUAPAMA, BOJACÁ

The objective of the research was to characterize the paragenetic sequence of the esmeraldifer mineralization that is presented in the tunnel Cromo-Pomponio- Santodomingo of the mine La Pava, Municipality of Quipama, Boyacá, by studies and laboratory analysis in thin polished sections of the host rocks and of veins and gaps, as in some witnesses of the sections mentioned above. For the effect 16 thin polished sections (12 veins and gaps and 4 box rock) were selected, and 11 witnesses in their majority of the veins and gaps, of a georeferential collection that possesses the Center of Technological Development of the Colombian Esmeralda - CDTEC. Specific methodology applied: 1. Complementation of the petrographic studies (laboratory of the National University of Colombia - UN), 2. Development of Digital Images Metasystem is presented as a micro-sized, (system of robotic scanning in the UN), studies of Raman spectroscopy in solids and fluid phases (laboratory UN and CDTEC).
