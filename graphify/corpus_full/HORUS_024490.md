# CRITICAL TRANSVERSALITY IN MULTIMODAL AND MULTIMEDIA DISCOURSE STUDIES

Multimodal and multimedia discourse studies (MMDSs), including discourse analysis, have their genesis in diverse foundations that come from critical theory. The purpose is to show some of the links and contemporary tasks of critical discourse studies in conjunction with critical theory. First of all, some points about the constitutive role of critical theory in the conformation of the most recognized aspects of critical discourse studies are discussed. Second, the purpose is to recover some contemporary problems posed by critical theory and its relationship with Latin America and with multimodal and multimedia critical discourse studies. And finally, the agenda that links the actions of both perspectives is formulated preliminarilyl
