# Comparative costs of coal briquette consumption with other domestic energy sources

A comparative analysis of the costs of using electric energy, LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas), gasoline and coal briquettes is done. To this end, energy efficiency was obtained in cooking food for the above-mentioned energy sources. In terms of costs the best option is the LPG, followed by gasoline, then the two briquette stove with fire control and the most expensive option is the electrical energy regardless of the socioeconomic stratum
