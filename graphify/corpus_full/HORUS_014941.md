# STUDY OF DOPPLER SPEEDS AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO THE MAGNETIC FIELD ASSCILLATIONS IN THE PENUMBRA OF SOLAR STARS

In this work, the local oscillations of Doppler speeds in the shadow of solar stain AR 12186 were studied. To this end, images were aligned using the Enhanced Correlation Coefficient (ECC), ECC+rotation method and the method proposed by Griñón-Marín et al. (2020). The wavelet transform was used to extract wavelet powers. Analyzing the distribution of oscillation periods of wavelet powers locally and globally, no dominant period was identified in these powers. Also, a attenuation was obtained in Doppler powers of 22.1% and 41.6 % with respect to the powers obtained through the ECC+rotation method for the powers found using the ECC method and the Griñón-Marín et al. method (2020), respectively. In addition, no correlation was found between the powers corresponding to Doppler speeds and magnitude of the magnetic field in the line of view for the three methods of alignment
