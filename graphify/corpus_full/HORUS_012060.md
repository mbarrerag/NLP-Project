# Assessment of marine bacterial isolates, such as biological control agents of Microcyclus ulei, which causes the South American Mal of Rubber Leaves - (SALB).

Assessment of bacterial isolates of marine origin, as biological control agents of Microcyclus ulei, causing the South American Mal of the leaves of Cauchus - (SALB) The production of latex of the natural rubber tree (Hevea brasiliensis), is affected by phytopathogens, such as Colletotrichum gloeosporioides causing Antranosis and Microcyclus ulei (P. Henn.) v. arx (ascomycota), causing the south american malignal malignancy (south american leaf blight - salb). This last disease, endemic to Amazonia, is distributed in Central and South America, being the largest limiting for its cultivation with annual losses of 15% and 20% in production and death of highly susceptible trees. The control of the SALB is limited, based on chemical control, with preventive effects in marine livers and clonal gardens, but represents high costs and causes negative impact to the environment.
