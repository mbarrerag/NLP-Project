# Corruption in public procurement from a risk perspective. Department of Caldas case.

The present research work has as a framework for analysis corruption in public procurement from a risk perspective in the department of Caldas, for this purpose it was proposed to establish the components of risk management and its contribution in reducing it, analyzing various theories and research related to the topic, it was put in dialogue with methodologies, normative framework and the main variables that contribute to reducing corruption in the public procurement processes in the Department of Caldas. This was done under the methodology of systematic review of information, that is, in a qualitative method that describes social realities; as a result it was possible to establish that the Colombian legislation has made its greatest effort to reduce corruption, although the statistics do not show a very encouraging result, so it is recommended to make a study from the effectiveness and efficiency of the rules that regulate the topic. The final result of the research is after carrying out the thematic analysis of the literature in which it was possible to identify that national and international levels of risk perspective such as corruption are quite significant, so that methods have been implemented to mitigate it, applicable to the Department of Caldas to reduce the corruption, starting from the double-level of the internal audit
