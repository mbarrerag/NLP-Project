# SOCIAL MOBILIZATION DOES NOT FEATURE AT PANDEMIA TIMES

In the whole world, we have lived an atypical social period, the result of the circumstances generated by the pandemic by covid-19. Specifically, this imposed on society the physical distance and periods of isolation-quarantine that have greatly hindered the encounter between people and this is an element that defines social mobilization, a subject that will address this article; a condition that allows to build agreements and identities and, from there, generate organization and promote collective actions. In Colombia, in terms of social mobilization, there have been between two key moments the protest of November 21, 2019 (21N), date of beginning of the national unemployment of the end of that year, and that that that began on April 28, 2021 (28A) in response to the call for a new national strike. But, in that time, other mobilizations have occurred due to inconformity, minor, even with restrictions of grouping by confinement. On the one hand, the population has expressed demands for the survival and health derived from the situation of the pandemic; on the other, from demands prior to the pandemic that are unresolved threats.
