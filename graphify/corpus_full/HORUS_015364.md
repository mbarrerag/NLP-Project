# ASSESSMENT OF GEOMETRY OF BLOCKS IN ROCK MACE MACHINES BY FLACING NETWORKS

In this work, several fracture networks generated for the information collected in a rocky slope are presented, in order to show the implications of this variability in the geometry and number of blocks that will be generated in the different realizations of the model. The slope was modeled with the information collected in a quarry of aggregates located in Une, Cundinamarca, which exploits sandstones. The structural information was taken using the system of short-range stereoscopy ShapeMetriX3D. In the mass there are three families of discontinuity. The orientations were modelled with a Fisher distribution, with concentration parameter, while the size of the discontinuities and their separation was modeled with the log-normal and exponential distribution, respectively. Based on the information in field on the selected slopes as cases of study, the block of fracture was generated 20 networks using the tool PAS of the Siromodel software. Based on these networks the number of removable blocks and the total volume of those variables was determined.
