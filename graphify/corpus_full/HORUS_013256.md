# Monte Carlo simulation of magnetic nanoparticles of magnetite

The nanoparticles have been widely studied and used in the scientific community and industry for their innumerable applications. The computational studies reported in the literature have focused on studying isolated nanoparticles or nanoparticles systems, separating both scenarios into different models. In this master's thesis the unification of both models was proposed: from an atomistic scenario where the nanoparticles were simulated individually the results obtained as input data were used to simulate the magnetic nanoparticles systems. In addition, the magnetic properties of the nanoparticles systems were studied by the magnetic presence within the lower degree of the nanoparticles by varying their size distribution and the concentration of particles. In the atomistic scenario, the individual nanoparticles of the different sizes were modelled and simulated using the Monte Carlo method, the classical Heisenberg model and the algorithm of the Metrópolis were presented in order to study the effects of the temperature, the magnetic field and the size of the magnetic systems were used for the study of the nanoparticles systems obtained using the Monte Carlo method, the classical Heisenberg model and the algorithm of the Metrópolis and to study the influence of the magnetic systems.
