# Physiological and productive response of Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia volubilis) to the application of boron in the Meta foothill.

Plukenetia volubilis is a promising species due to the high nutritional value of its seeds. In Colombia several studies have been carried out in the transformation of products based on Sacha Inchi, however, research in the practices of crop management, post-harvest and harvest is still incipient. The present study aims to deepen the knowledge of the effect of boron on the growth, development, physiology, productivity and bromatory quality of seeds and of Sacha Inchi oil. To achieve this objective two experiments will be carried out, one of them will consist in the experiment of the missing nutrient, in which the effect of the absence of boron on the development, physiology, production and quality of fruits and seeds in semi-controlled conditions of mesh house. To validate the results obtained in the first experiment, the evaluation of three doses of boron with fractional application after the prun and in full blooming will be performed in terms of the optimal effect of the fruit and seed during an annual harvest cycle.
