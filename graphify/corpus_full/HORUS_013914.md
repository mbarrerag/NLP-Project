# Design and implementation of a programme to promote activity and physical exercise as a binational reference in clinical and community intervention in people with chronic non-communicable diseases

The Bi-Baltic Non-Transmissible Diseases (NCCDs) understood as a group of diseases of long evolution without spontaneous resolution, considered as a global public health problem impacting populations especially in the region of the Americas that relate to different lifestyles in which the populations interact, such as physical, mental, cognitive and emotional activity that when complicating reduces or coartes the functionality, autonomy and capacities of the adult/adult major. It is when, having as reference Colombia and Ecuador the perspective of a binational reference to activities and physical exercise is born in Ecuador and Colombia for the clinical and community intervention that it provides an integral program of physical activities and exercises with opportunities to enhance their independence through the various lifestyles to achieve a healthy existence framed in physiotherapy. To establish a comprehensive program of promotion of activities and physical exercise as binational reference in Ecuador and Colombia for the clinical and community intervention that impacts on the functionality and quality of life of people with chronic non-communicable diseases, methodology will be carried out the design of an integral program of activity and physical exercise through a quasi-experimental study of before and after quantitative approach (object measurements, clinical and clinical tests, clinical test and clinical tests)
