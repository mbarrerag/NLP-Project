# GENOMIC EDITION TOOLS FOR FAUNA CONSERVATION: CASE INTRODUCIDE SPECIES CONTROL.

Invasive species represent a challenge for the conservation of fauna because of their effects on different native populations. This is true for invasive species of insects such as the Philornis downsi fly in the Galapagos Islands, which has drastically affected local bird populations. According to the insect species, the mitigation of these effects has been done by control by cultural, biological methods or the use of chemical insecticides. Recently, genetic control methods based on the CRISPR/Cas9 systems have been developed for genomic editing that allow precise change of the genome of invasive species of insects, in conjunction with synthetic systems of super-Mendeliana inheritance of modified genes known as gene drive. These elements guarantee that in a crossover of a wild type individual with a heterozygous individual for a modified allele, the inheritance of said allele is dramatically increased beyond the 50% expected of a Mendelian crossover.
