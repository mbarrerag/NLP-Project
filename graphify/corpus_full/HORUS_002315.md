# National Environmental Chair

The National Environmental Chair is aimed at undergraduate students from all the headquarters of the National University of Colombia. Its purpose is to respond to the need to strengthen understanding and knowledge about the environment, addressing environmental problems and conflicts from a critical, territorial and complex approach, which contributes to the construction of sustainability. The Chair will be developed through modules that combine epistemological foundations with the analysis of practical cases, both in university contexts and in direct interaction with communities in various territories. This initiative will be led by the Institutes of Environmental Studies (IDEA) of the Bogotá, Medellín, Manizales and Palmira headquarters, and will have the participation of the headquarters of Tumaco, La Paz and Amazonas, as well as the University's Environmental Management System
