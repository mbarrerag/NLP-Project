# BLANKETS

Here I discuss blankets both as manufactured objects that embody the one who has made them, and as cobijas, which are shaped by vulnerability and care and connect with home. Be they blankets or cobijas, or both, these cozy, flexible things are intimate objects. The ways they are handmade and used'the marks they keep, the places they are kept'are examples of this intimacy. Blankets become in their use and in their making extensions of bodies and of domestic territories. For this reason, when following this material becoming, this continuity between blankets, bodies, and homes, one witnesses how life and loss are organized
