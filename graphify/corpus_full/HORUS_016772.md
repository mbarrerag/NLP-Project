# Poetics of violence in the armies of Evelio José Rosero Diago

This study presents a reading of the work The armies, by the Colombian writer Evelio José Rosero Diago. Through an exploration of social and linguistic aspects and a rhetorical study, it seeks to explore the author’s work and place it in the contemporary discursive panorama. The existence of a poetic violence by means of which Rosero seeks to signify the social phenomenon of violence constitutes the form in which the author is inserted into the Colombian literary field. First, it clarifies aspects relating to the social nature of the verbal material employed by the author. Some of the problems and common trends in the representation of violence in Colombia are reviewed in terms of the ideological construction of the discourse at the lexical, semantic and syntatic levels.
