# The evaluation of competencies tool for significant, conscious, autonomous and critical learning, which adds value to the ethical performance of engineering students. An experience with students of the subjects Topography and Digital Geomatics, of the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia.

The teachers who present this proposal have developed research in teaching in order to improve their teaching practices and thus promoting added value in the learning process of the students. They have advanced two researches in this line, as a result it was found that both teachers and students are rooted in the traditional processes in which a marked vertical relationship is presented, where the teacher gives and the student receives, hence that the students are difficult to believe that they are active agents of their learning process. It was also identified that both the and others distrust about the prevailing need for students to take the reins of their learning process, given that they have the firm belief that the teacher is the center of the process, and therefore they are difficult to identify the teacher as a companion and support. From these researches it has been derived as a challenge to find ways to convince with more arguments, both students and teachers, of the need to horizontalize the learning process and to achieve their own learning through the transformation of roles. It values its own performance through recognition of achievements, strengths, possibilities, shortcomings and limitations, and based on them establishes the changes to improve. This type of projects becomes relevant in a traditional scenario such as the Faculty of Mines, where both students and teachers are anchored in years of history and prestigious results. However, it must be understood as a factor of change that drives growth, exploration of other ways of teaching, learning and evaluating. That is why in line with the generic exit profile of the Faculty, where one of the competencies is _x0093_act with ethics, professional responsibility and social commitment_x0094_, the implementation of the joint evaluation scheme (self-evaluation, co-evaluation and heteroevaluation) will provide solid bases for obtaining skills aimed at acting prudently, truthfully and honestly in its actions; adjusted to the rules and laws that regulate the processes of which are involved in both their professional exercise and community life. Likewise, continuous and autonomous learning is promoted
