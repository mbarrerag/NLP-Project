# VIOLENCE AND MALTRAT TO COMPANY ANIMALS IN BOGOTA (COLOMBIA): AN APPROXIMATION TO THEIR EXPLANATIVE CONTEXT

The main objective is to build an explanatory approach to violence and abuse of pets in Bogotá as a social phenomenon. Methodological approach: qualitative and interpretative research of an ethnographic nature that from the Animal Human Studies (anthrozoology) in dialogue with collective health stressed the human-animal relationship of company in the domestic, economic and public spheres. As methodological tools, 17 semi-structured interviews, 3 exploratory surveys and 3 conversational ones were carried out. For the observation of practices, the following were carried out accompanying families, public health officials, animalists and representatives of the pet industry.
