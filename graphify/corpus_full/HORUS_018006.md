# Case study on the experience of an undergraduate student in instrumental music, in a music therapy process aimed at regulating academic stress

In this research, the results of a case study based on the experience of a graduate student in instrumental music are described, in a process of musical therapy aimed at regulating academic stress. Through the four methods of music therapy and additional support tools, strategies were developed that sought to strengthen the skills to address situations or stressful factors and manage the psychological, physical and behavioral reactions that derive from this type of stress.With a methodological design of descriptive type, by means of instruments for collecting information, registration and description of sessions, a group of categories was determined to intervene and implement through the activities of the musicotherapeutic, strategies for coping with stress. These categories were based on the SISCO Inventory of the Academic Stress and were: factors or stressful situations, psychological, physical and behavioral reactions. During the development of the research, the most recurring factors and reactions in the participant were defined, in order to propose intervention objectives consistent with the therapeutic needs.
