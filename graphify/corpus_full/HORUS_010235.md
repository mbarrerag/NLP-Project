# CHALLENGES IN THE ENGINEERING TEACHING IN THE 21st CENTURY.

Engineering programmes have a crucial role to play in ensuring that future engineers are trained successfully and adapted to the needs of society in the 21st century. Through bibliographical review, dialogue with academia, industry and government actors identified key topics that engineering programmes must constantly monitor to adapt their curricula, teaching methods, admission and retention processes. These topics include: rapid changes in the labour market, technological advances in education and the growing need for diversity and inclusion. To address these challenges, engineering teaching must be more adaptable, responsive and inclusive, and focus on developing the most needed skills in the labour market, more practical and project-based learning, interdisciplinary approaches, interpersonal competencies and ethical and social elements
