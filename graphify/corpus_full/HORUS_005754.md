# XPERIENCE OF MASTERED WOMEN IN THE DECISION-MAKING FOR THE RECONSTRUCTION OF THE MOTHER: INTEGRATIVE REVIEW

The decision of women to undertake a reconstruction after mastectomy allows to identify the personal difficulties and the importance of the support of the health sector. In this sense, the objective of this work was to integrate the evidence in the face of the experience of women mastectomized in the decision making for breast reconstruction, according to the theory of confined rationality of Hébert Simón. The material and method used was the integrative review of the literature, which was carried out from the five methodological steps of Robin Whittemore D.; for this, the following terms were used: breast neoplasm, experience, mastectomy, breast reconstruction, decision making; in Portuguese, English and Spanish languages, the search was made in the databases BVS, Scielo, Elsevier, PubMed, EBSCO Host, published between 2016 and 2021, with the result of twenty-eight articles.
