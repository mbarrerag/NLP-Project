# Production of hydrogen by dark fermentation from by-products of the wet benefit of coffee and other organic waste

The wet processing of coffee generates liquid and solid waste with high organic load, which constitute a significant environmental problem in producing regions such as Pitalito, Huila (Colombia). This study evaluated the production of hydrogen by dark fermentation (DF) from waters of the first coffee wash (FWCW) in co-digestion with plant residues (VW), cane juice (SCJ) and coffee pulp (CP), without thermal pretreatments or external inoculation. The tests were carried out in a 35 L batch bioreactor, under three treatments with different proportions (% v/v): L1 (18:25:5:5:14), L2 (18:48:0:0:35) and L3 (18:68:0:14), corresponding to VW, FWCW, CP, SCJ and water, respectively. The L1 treatment reached the highest cumulative production of H2 (70,03±2.65 L), as well as the best yield by volume of substrate (2,00±0.08 L2 Lustrate-1) and a H2 content (43,99±389%)
