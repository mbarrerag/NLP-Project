# Asset management of ISO 55001 standard in charging stations for electric vehicles in Colombia

Taking into account the relevance in the use of the standard ISO55001 - Asset management, this study focuses on industrial equipment and renewable energies, as well as its impact on the environmental environment of the industrial sector; allowing to establish the economic impact over time, expected profits and some options for technological improvement in 2 established scenarios for a refueling station of hybrid or electric cars in the city of Bogotá, through the use of solar energy using monocrystal panels, Ion batteries – lithium and other components necessary for its operation. To achieve the economic results, analysis was carried out in half-yearly periods and considering a six-monthly investment rate of 11.24 % corresponding to the average rate of the CDT in Colombia in 2022. This in order to perform a comparative analysis of the intention of investment of this capital. In the study, the simulation in PHYTON of the degradation over time, of the main components of the solar energy system, was carried out, and in the light of the change in the operating temperature variable is a representative factor in the useful life of these. Similarly, in the analysis also determines an alternative technological solution, which allows to monitor, monitor, control, control and avoid the change, and avoid the change of the life components of the solar system in semiannual periods and considering a semiannual investment rate of 11.24 % corresponding to the average rate of the CDT in Colombia in 2022. This in order to perform a comparative analysis of the investment intention of this capital. In the study, a PHYTON simulation of the degradation over time of the main components of the solar energy system was carried out out, identifying that the variable "operating temperature" is a representative factor in the user life of these components. In the same way, the analysis also determines an alternative technological solution that allows monitoring, controlling and avoiding the temperature change in some of the system components, which would extend their useful life
