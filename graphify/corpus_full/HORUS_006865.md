# Rehabit collective housing: a design methodology. Zarzamora Group 2, Bogotá

This TFM aims to question, diagnose and evaluate the habitability of collective housing in Bogotá with respect to the pandemic period and the post-pandemian period, where a series of problems arise in the need to seek to adapt the space from different elements, which do not always meet the objective of having the minimum conditions to develop both collective and individual activities. That is why it is decided to take as a case study, the home of the author, the group of Zarzamora CAFAM 2; and in turn, to use the term rehabit the collective housing, not without taking into account a methodology that allows to carry out a qualitative and quantitative recognition. Consequently, the above leads us to a comprehensive diagnosis in order to identify the activities, times, users and spatial conditions that allow to define the habitability patterns. These give us a starting point from the source of the projects, which will be used from the three scales, evaluating the alternatives through modulation, the multifunctional furniture, interventions in the blocks and others that allow the local regulations; in order to improve the habitability of the spaces and optimize them for the emerging activities..
