# PROPAGANDA OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP AS A CULTURE AND PSYCHOLOGY IMPERATIVE IN THE WORK OF EDWARD BERNAYS

The purpose of this text is to make a brief analysis that allows us to reflect on the proposal to simplify the conduct of citizens through propaganda, made by Edward Bernays. To recognize the theoretical horizons provided by the above-mentioned author and the elements to reinterpret the meaning, in this case, of the idea that the construction of certainties to walk the world was identified as the device par excellence that capitalism should try to operate and materialize as an integral whole of the sense of essential truth
