# Artificial vision-based method for the identification of the axle applied to the process of testing light vehicle brakes in automotive diagnostic centres

This grade work explores the application of artificial vision techniques based on deep learning to automatically monitor the process of vehicle inspection in automotive diagnostic centers (CDA) in Colombia, specifically during the brake test. The main objective is to develop a method that allows to identify the axis and vehicle to which it belongs during the inspection, in order to prevent the supplantation of axes or vehicles. The methodology used consists of three phases: preparation, implementation and documentation. In the preparation phase, videos of inspections were collected and sets of data manually labeled for the training and evaluation of models were constructed. During the implementation phase, three models of object detection (DETR-ResNet50, DETR-ResNet101 and YOLov9) were trained for the detection of axes, selecting the YOLov9 model for their best performance. In addition, a plate recognition algorithm was used to identify the vehicles and a high-level network for cases where the plate was not visible.
