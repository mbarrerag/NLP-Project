# THE Sustainability of TERRITORIES IN COLOMBIA

This chapter starts from providing a quick review of the concepts of sustainability, sustainability and sustainable development up to the considerations of the sustainable development objectives and the 2030 Agenda, identifying how the dividing lines between sustainable development, sustainability and sustainability are thinned, if they include topics specific to geography such as scale, relationships, flows and power as basic characteristics of the territories; it becomes absolutely complex to draw intraspasable dividing lines.In this context three approaches are proposed from the geographical perspective: the first from the environmental axis of the SDGs and the new agenda of the United Nations to promote sustainable development, in which an estimate of the contributions to sustainable development is proposed from the impact of public policy and quantifiable evidence.The second approach from environmental protection, the ecosystem benefits and public health requires information and development of collective knowledge versus fundamental issues such as the capacity of ecosystems, inventories of species, studies on the impact of pollutants on health, the degree of exposure to pollutants according to the activities and practices of the economic sectors.The third approach to the sustainability of the territories is a concept of sustainability and the structural nature of the situation.
