# Access, permanence and promotion schemes in public employment in Ibero-America

As an initial conceptual framework and following for this to Rodríguez (2022), a distinction is made between the two central concepts of research: meritocracy and merit.The first refers to the ideological discourse that refers to the system of beliefs that aims to achieve the consolidation of social justice through the selection of public employees according to the general interest and seeking the effectiveness in the functions of government.The second concept can be understood as the action that is worthy of a prize and, equally, as the quality that should be valued. Puntually it refers to the remuneration that relates to the proportion of merit and what is deserved.However, the second concept has been made critical from a radical perspective to the abuse that can generate the strict application of these concepts, in case of not implementing measures of distribution in the economic and educational field that can increase the possibilities of access. In order to avoid the deepening of the social inequalities that the meritático state requires in the latter, the meritático is the most important. The methodology that will guide the work plan on this occasion is qualitative in nature, considering that the identification of similarities and differences between the respective systems of access, permanence and promotion in public employment will be carried out following the initial evaluation of a series of analytical criteria at each level consisting of competencies, forms of admission, criteria of permanence in accordance with comprehensive evaluations and levels of professionalization
