# Implementation of prostate cancer planning through a multicriteria knowledge-based model in a medium- and high-complexity public hospital of the Colombian surocidente, 2023

In this work, the implementation of the planning of treatment of prostate cancer is presented using a model based on knowledge and multicriteria optimization. It is a descriptive study of retrospective. The study population was constituted by records of plans of treatment with radiation therapy for patients diagnosed with prostate cancer, during the period from January 1, 2020 to June 30, 2023, with a total of 170 records. To carry out the study, two groups were constituted: the first formed by 140 registries, which were used in training the model based on knowledge, while the remaining 30 registers were defined to perform the validation of the model. The research was established in order to determine whether the plans generated with the knowledge based model can improve or maintain the quality criteria regarding the initial plan. To respond to this concern, initially a description of demographic variables (age, stage, dose of prescription, among others) was proposed. The study was independently analyzed the variables that constitute the quality of planning category, among which are: estimation of the constraints for the at risk organs, determination of compliance with these criteria.
