# CHEMICAL CHARACTERIZATION BY GC/MS OF THE ESSENTIAL AMBROSS OIL KUNTH CUMHANSIS, RECOLECTED IN SANTANDER, COLOMBIA

The AE of Ambrosia cumanensis was obtained with a yield of 0.01% (p/p). 30 components with relative amounts greater than 0.1% were identified. The majority compounds identified in the AE were: ¿-curcumeno (24.6%), trans-ß farneseno (7.5%), trans-ß-cariofileno (6%), ß-pineno (5.2%), bicyclogermacrene (4.8%) and germacrene D (4.7%). The majority compounds have been reported in the literature by Cicció and Chaverri in 2015, and Guillen and collaborators in 2019 (1.3), but differ from those identified in this study
