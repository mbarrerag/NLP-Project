# MODELLING AND SIMULATION OF THE DIGESTION OF FISH WASTE USING ADM1: AN ECONOMIC PERSPECTIVE

The objective of this study is to perform an economic analysis of the anaerobic digestion process of fish waste for the biomethane production through the implementation and validation of the Anaerobic Digestion Model 1 (ADM1) in a Colombian context. Under steady state conditions, both adjusted and predicted biomethane output from the ADM1 model were in good agreement with experimental data. A minimum of 7 years is required to generate economic profitability in a real scale AD plant (300 Ton year -1). Costs must still be reduced in the construction of the biomethane plant and, in turn, process variables must be found that allow the process performance to be further improved
