# IGY ANTI-SPCAS9 POLICLONAL ANTI-BUILDING FOR USE IN CRISPR/CAS RESEARCH

The purpose of this work was to produce polyclonal antibodies IgY anti-SpCas9 for use in CRISPR/Cas applications. The methodology developed consisted of inocular hens HyLine Brown with the protein SpCas9-6xHis expressed and purified from Escherichia coli BL21(DE3) by precipitation by salting and chromatography of affinity to immobilized metals (IMAC). The IgY anti-SpCas9 antibodies were purified from egg yolk by standardizing a protocol of delipitation with pectin and precipitation with ammonium sulfate. The antibodies obtained were characterized by ELISA indirect, Western blot and Dotblot, while their usefulness to detect SpCas9 in biological samples were verified by Western blot on promastigo extracts from Leishmania brazillensis anti-sis.
