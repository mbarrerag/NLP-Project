# Building strategies for sustainable agriculture with a territorial focus with families reincorporated into the peace process of the former FARC-EP in the municipality of Ituango (Antioquia)

Colombia is facing a promising scenario of building a stable and lasting peace. All social actors in the country must align themselves in the search for this common goal. The National University of Colombia (UNAL) is called to be protagonist in this project of Nation and thus contribute directly to promoting sustainable development and peace building in the territories. The project will be developed in the municipality of Ituango (Antioquia), a territory where its inhabitants have suffered the armed conflict for years, the mobility is difficult and the social problems are present. Despite the above, it is also a territory with an important organizational and community working tradition. Precisely, in the process of Peace 24 Territorial Areas of Training and Re-incorporation (ETCR) were established in the country; one of them in the Santa Lucia de Ituango area.
