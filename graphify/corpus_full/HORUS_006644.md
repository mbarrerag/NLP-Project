# Estimated expected replacement rates of the higher-risk funds by pension fund managers in Colombia

The pension system in Colombia has been the subject of discussions around the need for pension reform, particularly the RAIS has been criticized due to the low replacement rates offered. From 2019 the regulation on membership was changed so that the majority of members of this regime would be transferred to the fund of greater risk with the promise of greater profitability. Thus, the following question arose: What would be the expected replacement rate of a member who has listed the majority of his pension savings in the fund of greater risk? To answer it, it was decided to use the model of optimization of Markowitz portfolio to replicate the fund of greater risk, with the aim of simulating the pension path of several workers who meet the requirements to pension and obtain their pension allowance. Finally, the replacement rate using the sex and average income as variables was estimated. As a result it was obtained that, the replacement rates for the RAIS did not vary from 23% to 39%, below the replacement rates offered by the RPM. It was decided to use Markowitz's portfolio optimization model to replicate the high risk fund, with the goal of simulating the pension path of severe workers who met the requirements to withdraw and obtain their pension allowance. Ultimately, the replacement rate was estimated using sex and average income as variable. As a result, it was obtained that the replacement rates for the RAIS range between 23% and 39%, below the replacement rates offered by the RPM. The same way, the regulation applied toAFPs generates that their portfolios are outside the efficient border.
