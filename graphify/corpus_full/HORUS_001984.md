# Visual representations and state speeches of the Colombian Amazon

To think of the Amazon from its visual representations is a powerful way to understand how a region traditionally considered peripheral or marginal has been narrated in Colombian imagination as a nation. This book gathers a set of reflections and experiences that seek to understand how the forms of visual representation and the discourses used attempt to incorporate that region into national narratives. In particular, it explores the complexity of two conjunctures in which Colombia thought of its relationship with the Amazon region, of great importance in the construction of the image of national territory: the Amazonian conflict, particularly the Colombo-Peruvian war, which took place between 1933 and 1934, and the peace process between the Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) and the Colombian State, developed after several attempts, advances and setbacks between 2012 and 2016.
