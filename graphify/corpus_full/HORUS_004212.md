# PREPARATION FOR CARE IN CRONE DISEASE CAREERS AND THEIR RELATED FACTORS

The introduction: The non-communicable chronic diseases present a burden on the health-care issues worldwide. Family caregivers assume this burden abruptly and without the necessary preparation, so they are likely to be overburdened by this and other factors. The preparation tool for care has been validated in other texts and has demonstrated reliable psychometric properties to measure preparation in family caregivers. There are other variables, besides the preparation and other factors, which influence the burden of care provider, so it is necessary to carry out studies that measure the relationship between variables. To determine the relationship between preparation for care, the socio-demographic and care variables with the burden on family caregivers of persons with non-transmissible chronic disease. The aim: To determine the relationship between the preparation for care, the socio-demographic and care variables for care, and the care variables for care with care for caregiving, the care variables for care and caregiving, the care variables for family caregivers of persons with non-transmissable chronic disease
