# Assessment and characterization of Giardia duodenalis sirtuin 2.3 (CdSir2.3)

Abstract Giardiasis is the most common gastrointestinal disease worldwide, caused by the Giardia duodenalis protozoan, for which there is no vaccine or efficient treatment. In order to seek new pharmacological targets against this parasite, enzymes of energy metabolism, such as sirtuins, deacetylases dependent on adenine dinucleotide and nicotinamide (NAD), have been studied. Previously GdSir2.1 and GdSir2.2 were identified as NAD-dependent deacetylases, with different subcellular locations.
