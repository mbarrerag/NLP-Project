# Editorial. 30 years of Innovation.

In 2021, the INNOVAR Journal celebrated 30 years of uninterrupted publication, contributing to the disciplinary development in the fields of management, finance and accounting, mainly. This document reflects on the journal’s former publishers on what has been the trajectory and dynamics, as well as on the challenges for the future of INNOVAR. It is a document structured in five parts, one for each former participant. The diverse reflections contained in this document emphasize the pluralist and international character of the Journal. Also, challenges and challenges for INNOVAR’s editorial policies and practices are being anticipated. INNOVAR readers and academic community are invited to commemorate the 30 years of the Journal
