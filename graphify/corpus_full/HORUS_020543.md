# Analysis of the behavior of lightning activity and its meteorological variables in mountain cities. Case of Manizales, Colombia

This article shows the diurnal and seasonal behavior of lightning activity and its relationship with the meteorological variables involved in the formation of storms, in a mountain city. The results show that Manizales has an average of 69 stormy days, that is, they are the days where at least one lightning is detected. Within the results, it can also be seen that the months with the most activity are March and November. The analysis was carried out for the two rainy and dry periods that occur in the Andean zone of Colombia. The results show that during rainy periods the lightning activity is more intense and that it is mainly due to local winds and the displacement of the Intertropical Confluence Zone. Within the analysis of the meteorological variables, the average diurnal cycle was used to observe the behavior of these during the days with lightning activity and those that did not have activity, the variables analyzed were surface pressure, surface air temperature, dew point temperature, mixing ratio, zonal and southern winds
