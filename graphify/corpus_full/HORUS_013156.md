# Comprehensive assessment of ecosystem services applied to the sustainability of agroecosystems in the Colombian Andes: a case study in the Pasuncha district (Pacho municipality, Cundinamarca)

The increasing environmental problems and the difficulty in achieving the objectives to halt and reverse the loss of global biodiversity have been constituted as one of the main topics in the academic, environmental and political agendas. For Colombia, it is of special relevance to have tools suitable to its socio-ecological contexts that allow to manage the territory and its biodiversity in an integral and participatory way to achieve sustainable development. This research proposes as methodological proposal and practical tool, the use of the integral valuation of ecosystem services to articulate the objectives of the conservation and restoration of biodiversity with those of its sustainable use by the communities in rural contexts of the Colombian Andes. A case study is presented framed in the correction of Pasuncha (Pacho, Cundinamarca) because it is a reflection of some national aspects: territories rich in biodiversity little explored, of cultural diversity and rural rural tradition, and with landscapes more and more simplified by the drivers of change of diversity. It is done the integration of biophysical, socio-cultural and economic approaches under a framework of innovative reference that includes the valuation of ecosystem services as part of a process inherent to the dynamics of the socio-economic systems and tion and restoration with those of its sustainable use by communities in rural contexts of the Colombian Andes. A case study is present, centered in the village of Pasuncha (Pacho, Cundinamarca), as it reflects certain aspects of the national panorama: territories rich in unexplored biodiversity, cultural diversity, and rural pedestrian tradition, yet with landscapes increasingly simplified by drivers of diversity change. The integration of biological, socio-cultural, and economic perspectives is given within an innovative framework that includes the assessment of ecosystem services as an inherent process in the dynamics of socio-ecological systems, which services as a guide in the territory planning of Pasuncha based on conservation, management, and sustainable use of the land
