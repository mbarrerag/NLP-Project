# BOUNDEDNESS OF THE HILBERT TRANSFORM ON REARRANGEMENT INVARIANT SPACES

In this self-contained review, the aspects about the applications of decreasing rearrangement techniques to the analysis of pointwise estimates for the Hilbert transform are analyzed. We make a consistent revision of these techniques in the proof of the Lp-boundedness of the Hilbert transform. This is a celebrated theorem due to M. Riesz
