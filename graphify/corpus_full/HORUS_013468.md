# Ricardo Cano Gaviria, between provincinism and literary cosmopolitanism

Aestrossecosis focuses on the analysis of four novels by the Colombian writer Ricardo Cano Gaviria: Prytaneum (1981), The 120 Days of Bouvard and Pécuchet (1982), The Passenger Walter Benjamin (1989) and A Lesson of Abyss (1991). The novels analyze the tension between provincialism and literary cosmopolitanism. In the first place, a review of the texts and opinions of the Colombian literary criticism on the writer's work is carried out to debate and question them in an alternative way from separated to doccipient to Colombian format and to discover the cosmopolitanism in his work.
