# MICROGLYA ACTION IN THE ASSOCIATED HYPOCAMPO WITH THE LESSON OF THE FACIAL NERVE

Facial nerve lesions affect long-term plasticity in the hippocampus, as well as the memory of object recognition and spatial memory, two processes dependent on this structure. Characterizing in rats the effect of unilateral facial nerve injury on the activation of microglya cells in the contralateral hippocampus. Immunohistochemistry experiments were performed to detect microglya cells in the hippocampus of rats subjected to irreversible injury to the facial nerve. Animals were sacrificed at different times after the injury, to evaluate the evolution of proliferation (cell density) and activation (cell area) of the microglya in the hippocampus tissue. Brain tissues of control animals were compared to those of injured animals slaughtered on days 1, 3, 7, 21 and 35 after the injury. Results of microglyasia cells in the hippocampus of animals with facial nerve injury could not be described.
