# Anxious Wish In Lonesome

It is a creative process that allowed me to display a series of intuitions on two impulses that surround two worlds of interest: sexual drive, and the drive of death. These two sensations led me to collect information in a series of routes that developed in Bogotá and in my city of Provenance Popayán, Cauca, connecting me with memories and anecdotes of my past. The causes that motivated this inquiry were the Observation of bodies in street condition, and bodies in display in the prostitution areas, to this, annexed the interest I had in the erotic body and the dead body that observes in the web in voyeristic way through news images of the armed conflict in the territories of Cauca and Nariño.
