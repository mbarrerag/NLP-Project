# CAUSES OF ABORT IN OVINES OF URUGAY: 100 CASES, 2015-2021

This article focuses on the identification of causes of abortion in sheep from different departments of the country, as a contribution to the reduction of reproductive losses, the improvement in animal welfare, and the diagnosis of zoonotic diseases
