# CHAPTER 7. PRO-FIT PARA-ATHLETES. VALIDATION OF A PROTOCOL FOR THE EVALUATION OF THE PHYSICAL STATUS OF PARALYMPIC ATLETATES COMPITED IN ATTHETISM IN RUEDA SILLA

The sport is more than a practice of physical skills, since it contemplates element of motivation, development environment and, more importantly, processes of inclusion of persons with disabilities, at different levels of competence, that reaches the most advanced ones, such as olimpismo. The participation of Colombians in the paralympic competitions during the last years and its important and exponential boom in disciplines such as athletics has aroused the need for an effective and precise method of evaluation of this practicing population. In response, a macroproject is constituted: a battery of evaluation of the physical condition of paralympic athletes, within the line of depth in Physical Activity and Sport, where a first phase of the project was developed, consisting of the identification, review and documentation of possible tests that can be applied in population with disabilities, wheelchair users; phase carried out during 2017.
