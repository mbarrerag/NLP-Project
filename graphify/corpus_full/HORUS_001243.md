# CALIBRATING AND VALIDATING THE AEROBIC MORTALITY RISK INDEX (IRMA): AN INDEX FOR PREDICTING MASS MORTALITY OF FISH DUE TO ANOXIA.

To generate an early warning tool for massive fish mortality due to eutrophication in tropical estuaries, we calibrated and validated the mortality risk index - IRMA. To calculate the IRMA, we used a conceptual model that relates the concentrations of PO4 (μmol/L), chlorophyll (CLA, μg/L) and dissolved oxygen (DO, mg/L) and data from 30 years of monitoring of these variables, obtained from the estuarine lagoon Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta (CGSM) - Colombian Caribbean. We calibrated the model using the fish death events recorded in CGSM and evaluated it through a confusion matrix. We calculated several metrics to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the IRMA, obtaining an optimized threshold value of 60%
