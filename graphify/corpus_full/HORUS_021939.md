# Predictive estimate of technological innovation in Colombia using Monte Carlo simulations.

Technological innovation is a process that has acquired great importance for the economic and social development of countries; for this reason, it has become necessary to create predictive models that serve as a basis for estimating the behaviour of technological innovation. However, these models are widespread worldwide and often the parameters used to estimate technological innovation in an industrialized country are not adapted to developing countries. Therefore, the present thesis seeks to explore methodologies based on Analysis of Main Components and Márkov Chain Monte Carlo simulations, in order to realize predictive models that serve as a basis for estimating technological innovation in Colombia, using as a parameter the patents registered by residents of the Super Intendencia de Industria y Comercio and using macroeconomic and social variables that are considered to have an influence on patent production.
