# Frequency of Genitourinary Trauma in Motorcycle Traffic Accidents: Scope Review

Objective Describe the frequency of genitourinary trauma (GMT) caused by traffic accidents on motorcycle. Methodology Scope review according to the methodology proposed by Arksey and O ́Malley and the extension for Prism scope reviews. Searches were carried out in PubMed and Embase. All studies that described the frequency of GMT in adult population (≥ 18 years) as a result of traffic accidents on motorcycle. Two independent reviewers were responsible for the selection by title and summary and then for the evaluation of eligibility and quality of studies in full text. Results The frequency of GMT was higher in men, with figures between 94.5 and 96.5 per cent, compared to 3.4% and 5.5% in women. Drivers were the most affected compared to passengers, with a proportion of 96.4 per cent.
