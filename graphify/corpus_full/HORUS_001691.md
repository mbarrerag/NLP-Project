# Cognitive travel inference in Parkinson's disease patients from spatiotemporal gai assessments using machine learning

Co-operational impairment is one of the most relevant complications in Parkinson’s disease (PD), which significantly affects the quality of life of patients. Early identification of these deficits is crucial to improve clinical intervention and prognosis of the disease. This study analyzes the relationship between the spatial and temporal characteristics of the march and cognitive deterioration in patients with PD at an early stage. Data were used from 48 patients in the Parkinson’s Progression Markers Initiative database (PPMI), dividing the sample into two groups: patients without cognitive impairment (PD) and those with some degree of cognitive impairment (PD-DC). Seven automatic learning algorithms were implemented for analysis, optimizing their hyperparameters and addressing class imbalance. The performance of the models was evaluated by means of metrics (recall, pre-section, F1-score and accuracy (Accuracy), prioritizing the sensitivity to evaluate the detection capacity of the minority class. Results indicated that the spatial and temporal characteristics of the march allowed significantly differentitive between PD and PD-DC groups.
