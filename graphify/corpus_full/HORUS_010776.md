# A CONJECTURE IN NON-CONMUTTATIVE GEOEMTRY

We will describe some of the central ideas in non-conmutative geometry, in particular the duality of Gelfand-Naimark, and as this leads us to consider natural generalizations of topological spaces that we will call non-conmutative spaces. Will we describe how to understand some of these non-conmutative spaces that are associated with discrete groups. Finally we will show how some of these invariants can be calculated using tools typical of algebraic topology
