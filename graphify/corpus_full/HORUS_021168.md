# Analysis of soils in mixed plantations of native species in high Andean forests and their correlation with the recruitment of woody species

Complete soil fertility analysis in 23 plots of the More Forests for Medellín project, in order to obtain information on the soil characteristics in mixed plantations of native species that can be correlated with the recruitment of woody species
