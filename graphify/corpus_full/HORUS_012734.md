# Design a Web site as virtual object of learning to develop competencies in the tissues fundamental to students of the Master's degree in Human Morphology

The National University of Colombia, within the postgraduate curricular programs of the Faculty of Medicine, offers the Master's Degree in Human Morphology; aimed at professionals in the area of human, animal and biological health sciences; allowing the student to deepen basic concepts of morphological sciences such as anatomy, histology, embryology, among others. Histology is the scientific study of the microscopic structures of the body's tissues and organs. However, its implementation differs according to the thinking of some health sciences undergraduates. This project's main objective is to offer students of the Master's Degree in Human Morphology, mainly those who do not have bases in this study; support through a virtual learning object (Website) which has theoretical bases of the general fundamental tissues (epithelial tissues, connective tissue, muscle tissue and nervous tissue) where the student will be able to observe histological slides with their respective description. The slides will be selected from the histology and cytohistotechnology laboratories belonging to the Department of Morphology of the Faculty of Medicine of the National University, and according to the established modules, evaluating their condition for their respective digitization with the help of a microscope (Motic BA300) and a camera (Moticam 2500 5.0 megapixel); so that finally, through an editing program, the important aspects are highlighted (cell morphology, the identification of organelles from their metachromatic reaction and, in turn, the physiological aspects); Important aspects for your understanding
