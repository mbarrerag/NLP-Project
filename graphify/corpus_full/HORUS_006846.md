# “Citadel of the artistic industry” urban vacuum transformation in hydrocarbon storage area, Puente Aranda, Bogotá D.C

The design of a citadel based on the concepts of diversity, permeability and renaturalization was proposed as a response to the problem of urban vacuum generated by large, inaccessible, artificial and obsolete monofunctional areas. In this work, the transformation of the urban vacuum generated by the hydrocarbon storage plant located in the industrial zone of Puente Aranda in Bogotá D.C. The reasons for which this vacuum was consolidated in a central place of the city was addressed, the urban renewal bets that have the planning instruments for this area were verified and it was found that this activity represents a threat to the territory, which makes it necessary to move it from its current location. Additionally, design elements applicable to the Citadel were identified, from projects in which industrial sectors have been transformed, from two different perspectives, through the conservation of the industrial vocation and the reuse of obsolete infrastructures.
