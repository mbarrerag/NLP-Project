# SMILE SMILE. LIFE STORY OF A COLOMBIAN WAR ODONTOLOG

The Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) was a guerrilla group that acted between 1964 and 2016 and tried to build a communist government in Colombia. It had a wide influence in the country's rural areas, where health care services are scarce. One of the political strategies of this guerrilla group was to train its own health professionals, including dentists. Characterizing the process of training dentists and dental care within the FARC. Qualitative research with an ethnographic focus, focused on the history of life. Research techniques such as in-depth interview and documentary review were used. It is the life history of a former combatant of the FARC, who was formed and exercised as a soldier and dentist inside the FARC. During the war, the FARC attended the bucal health of its soldiers and, to a lesser extent, of the civil population that lived in its areas of influence. A strategy sought to generate health autonomy through the training of its own health personnel (medicine, nursing, clinical laboratory, dental).
