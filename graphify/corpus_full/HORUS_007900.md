# Sitosterolemia caused by mutations in the ABCG8 gene. First case report in Colombia

Sitosterolemia is an autosomal recessive inherited disease caused by mutations in the genes ABCG5 or ABCG8 which is characterized by the decrease in the excretion of plant sterols and cholesterol, leading to the development of hypercholesterolemia, xantomas and premature atherosclerosis. In this article the first case of this disease registered in Colombia is reported. Presentation of the case. Male of 5 years, child of non-consanguineous parents, in active treatment with ezetimiba and cholestyramine, who was taken (April 2021) to the service of human genetics of a health institution in Bogotá, Colombia, for evaluation due to the multiple xantomas, high levels of cholesterol LDL and to which had been ruled out diagnosis of family hypercholesterolemia. By means of analysis of gene size of new generation (NGS) was detected mutation of the ABCG8 gene, so that it was diagnosed sitosterolemia and it was decided to continue the pharmacological treatment (ezetimiba and colestramine) and initiate dietary modifications.
