# Characterization of paediatric patients before and after epilepsy surgery: a quality of life approach.

Using a descriptive and relational approach based on literature and direct examination through the dissection of the forearm and the anatomical regions palmar, tenar, hypotenar of the human hand, the specific location of the intrinsic and extrinsic muscles involved in the activity of the hand is explored. Using the material and inputs for dissection of the amphitheatre laboratory at the Faculty of Medicine of the National University of Colombia, Bogotá headquarters; the basic graphic record of this work is developed. The result is a muscular inventory that provides an idea of the morphological and positional characteristics of the forearm and hand, focused from the point of view of the musculature that participates in manual activity. It is a guide for the reader and especially for students in the fields related to health. Likewise, an online consultation tool is developed in such a way that information can be accessed in a practical way from remote and asynchronous conditions
