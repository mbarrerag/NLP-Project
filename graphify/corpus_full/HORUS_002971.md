# Recognition, measurement, disclosure and presentation of ship deterioration in the Colombian Navy financial statements

This work aims to propose a procedure that facilitates the recognition, measurement, disclosure and presentation of the accounting deterioration of the ships used in naval operations for the defense in Colombia. The research, carried out in collaboration with officials of the Colombian Navy, addresses an area with little prior literature, which highlights the importance of the study for public entities that manage assets of this type. The work adopts a qualitative approach based on an inductive-analytic process that includes techniques such as documentary review, content analysis and semi-structured interviews. The results indicate that, while the organization has guidelines to deal with the deterioration in the property, plant and equipment, it lacks specific guidelines for the ships, which makes it difficult to identify and recognize the phenomenon. For this, a procedure based on technical and accounting criteria that would facilitate the work of the evaluators and financial areas in the process of determining the deterioration of the ships. equipment, it lakes specific directions for ships, making the identification and recognition of this phenomenon more difficult. Therefore, a process based on technical and accounting criteria is proposed to facilitate the work of evaluators and financial areas in the process of determining ship occupation. While the research is focused on the Colombian context, it is specified that its contributions will serve as a reference for other governance advocacy sector organizations in the region that prepare their financial statements in agreement with the International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS)
