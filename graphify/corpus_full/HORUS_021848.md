# Algorithm for the online classification of silent speech phonemes using an embedded system.

During the development of this project, methodologies for the classification of silent speech phonemes based on EMD (Empirical mode decomposition) were analyzed at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia (UNAL) headquarters Bogotá, with data from the Emotive-DB database taken with the EMOTIVE COPD+14 team, which contains information from 16 subjects, while thinking about phonemes /a/, /e/, /i/, /o/, /u/, and the syllables /fa/, /pe/, /mi/, /lo/, /ru/. In the process, the impact on results and processing time was analyzed, in relation to the overlap variables, sampling frequency, number of channels, among others; after that analysis, the number of channels, distribution of electrodes and projection vectors in the EMD decomposition was selected and treated, thus reducing the average processing time per trial from 8.73 seconds to 0.06 seconds, allowing the possibility of being implemented in a system in line
