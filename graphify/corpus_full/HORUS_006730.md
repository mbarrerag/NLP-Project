# Colombian Constitutional Court of the reflexive cusp to institutional fluctuation: considerations on the review of guardianships against judicial order based on dialogic constitutionalism

This grade paper presents some considerations regarding the dispute generated between the Colombian High Courts by virtue of the constitutional review of judicial orders -including those pronounced in terms of unification. It gives an account of the case law development that allowed the Constitutional Court to position itself as the highest court, in the reflexive cusp of the judicial system and its consequent institutional fluctuation in view of the exclusive claim of authority in the constitutional interpretation vis-à-vis the other Courts. Finally, it proposes as a dialogical mechanism the installation of an inter-jurisdictional public hearing of constitutional review of judicial judgements made by High Courts as a means of solution to stabilize such conflict . This these sets out some considerations on the disputed between the Colombian High Courts by reverse of the constitutional review of judicial rules - every review to secondary is implied in the context of unification.
