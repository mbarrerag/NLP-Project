# Impact of the four methods of music therapy on strengthening the maternal-fetal affective bond for coping with malformation mourning: case study in a pregnant mother of a baby diagnosed with bone dysplasia, severe bilateral femoral hemimelia and bilateral equinovar foot

This single case study analyzes the impact of the four methods of music therapy on the strengthening of the maternal-fetal affective bond and the coping with the malformation due to a pregnant mother. A design of mixed methods with concurrent and exploratory triangulation was used, applying the EVAP scale for quantitative analysis and qualitative analysis techniques for improvisations, interviews and bitacores. The intervention took place at the patient's home, with the occasional participation of her partner. The quantitative results showed a significant improvement in the quality of the maternal-fetal affective bond (p-value: 0.021). Qualitatively, the four methods of music therapy helped in strengthening the maternal affective bond, the coping with the grief and the integration of emotional support tools into everyday life.
