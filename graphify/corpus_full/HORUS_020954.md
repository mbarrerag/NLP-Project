# MODELS AND REFERENCE MARKS FOR RISK MANAGEMENT IN DIGITAL ENVIRONMENTS

Given the level of dependence currently presented by ICT organizations, a new field of study has been generated, such as digital uncertainty, which is materialized in adverse events affecting the technological infrastructure that any organization has. This text takes a tour of the main models and reference frameworks for digital risk management, establishing not only a description of each of them, but also highlighting a discipline associated with the governance of Information Technologies such as the management of risks of information technologies
