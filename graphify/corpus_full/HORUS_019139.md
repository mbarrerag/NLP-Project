# PREDICT OF DAY MILK PRODUCTION IN GYR BOVINOS THROUGH OVERVIEWED LEARNING METHODS.

The Colombian Association of Livestock Criators Cebu - ASOCEBU, is interested in developing a machine to predict the total daily milk production using partial production measurements in Gyr livestock and, in particular, answer two questions: 1) can a predictive reference weight be exceeded by m ́etodes developed at local level? 2) which of the two partial registers (AM or PM) has a better predictive performance? Therefore, the aim of this article was to develop a predictive machine for the daily production of milk in Gyr cattle using partial records, interval between milks, days in lactation, and number of births (n=13806), through the implementation of supervised learning methods. In addition to the predictive median baseline weight of the m ́aquina, several combinations of entry and model variables or m ́etod of learning were considered.
