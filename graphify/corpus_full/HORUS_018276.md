# Designing a methodology to diagnose and measure absorption capacity and its effect on innovation in Industrial Corona.

The purpose of this work is to propose a methodology to diagnose and measure the absorption capacity in the company CORONA INDUSTRIAL, and with this analysis the positive or negative effects in each of its dimensions and to know the impact on innovation. A questionnaire was taken that was provided by Vlačić, et al. (2019) and a semistructure interview was conducted for each of the divisions that make up the company, as follows: Surfaces, Materials and Paintings (SMP), Mesa Servida (MS), Industrial Insumos and Energy (IIE), and Baños y Cuisines (BC). The case study allowed to understand that the acquisition of external knowledge, training, training of collaborators and research and development that each division has, positively influences the results of innovation, since with the exploitation of this new strategies and products and/or processes are created; concluding as such that once the potential absorption capacity is acquired and assimilated by the participants is translated in a significant way in the obtaining of results through transformation and exploitation, and thus with these capacities and knowledge management, it becomes innovation in the great organizations of Colombia
