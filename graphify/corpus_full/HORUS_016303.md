# IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MANIFEST: ENGINEERING FOR THE LIFE OF MINES IN MICROCURRICULT OF THE PRE-AGENCY

This paper presents three examples of the application of the Manifesto: Engineering for Life, from the Faculty of Mines of the National University of Colombia, Medellín headquarters, in the microcurriculum in three subjects of three different engineering programs. The objective is to illustrate the broad spectrum of possibilities offered by the Manifesto as a driver of the introduction of concepts such as life, engineering, territory and sustainability in any subject of an engineering program. Key words: Manifesto; Engineering for life; learning results; levels of training cycle
