# Comparison of price forecasting models for Green Paddy Rice in Colombia

This work has as central axis the study of the prices to the producer of the Arroz Paddy Verde, with the objective of constructing and comparing forecast models that include those variables that are determinants and that took greater relevance both during the pandemic and in the context of the conflict between Russia and Ukraine. Taking as criteria the Analysis of Main Components and the Proof of Causality of Granger for the choice of explanatory variables, the models SARIMAX turned out to be relevant for the forecast of the prices of the cereal in its first link and more precise than the simple models SARIMA and other models used to forecast economic variables as the models of Dinamic Factors (DFM). However, following the methodology used by some authors, a model SARIMAX was constructed that includes as exogenous variable the first factor obtained through DFM (SARIMAX_F1), which obtained the most precise forecasts compared to the forecasts of the model SARIMA and the forecasts of the remaining models evaluated according to the indicators RMSE, MAE and MSE. The contrast Diebold-Mariano suggests that the forecasts of this model are not statistically more precise than those of the model SARX and the model. Moreover, SARIMAX forecasts applied to be more precise than those for simple SARIMA and other models commonly used to forecast economic variables such as Dynamic Factor Models (DFM). Nevertheless, following the methodology employed by some authors, it was built a SARIMAX model that included the first factor obtained through DFM as an exogenous variable (SARIMAX_F1). This model reached the most incurable forecast compared to the other 8 models evaluated according to the RMSE, MAE, and MSE indicators.
