# NUTRITIONAL ALTERNATIONS IN MAYOR PERSONS WITH COGNITIVE DETERIOR AND THEIR MANAGE

Nutritional alterations in elderly people with cognitive impairment are very prevalent. In this context, it is vital to understand the interactions between nutritional status and cognitive function in order to provide adequate and timely nutritional management. Nutrient intake has a direct impact on cognitva function once the consumption of antioxidants and fatty acids precursors of anti-inflammatory mediators acts in the antioxidant defense and prevention of neuroinflammation that lead to cognitive impairment. The importance of adequate consumption of nutrients such as selenium and vitamins A, D, E, C, B6, B9 and B12 has been reported. In addition, bioactive compounds such as flavonoids, carotenoids, coenzymes Q10, melatonin and alpha lipoic acid have been shown to be beneficial for cognitva function. Nutrition can also modulate cognitva function through microbiota and by contributing to the maintenance and/or increase of muscle mass
