# PROCESS IS DIFFERENT TO PROJECT: THE LEARNING OF ARCHITECTURE CONCEPTS AND TIMES OF THE PROJECTUAL PROCESS TO PUT ON ANALOGIES.

The tradition in the teaching process learning of architecture, has taken us for decades by paths in which there is not always a significant learning, this is usually due to two reasons: the first, to which almost always an absolute value has been given to the result of the projective exercise, valuing only the result obtained, that is to say reviewing only the project, even, many times we remain only in the image that the project transmits, an image that does not reveal anything in relation to the learning. The second, (linked to the first) reveals us that almost always transiting these paths is derived from actions associated with the model. Conductive where instructions are issued that when repeated represent the expected result. However, there are several possibilities for addressing the learning process by the teacher. One of them, from the possibilities of the model Constructivist but, is to understand that the learning is not in the project as such, but in the process of the process, it is a mental structure. Thus, if we understand our teaching practice on the basis of understanding the projectual process itself and the phases that make it up, and to take that understanding to our students, we will put the whole into its arrangement a mental thinking, we will be able to do architecture from analogies such as: architecture domino, architecture scores, architecture chess, architecture matrioska. The learning obtained by the student has two scenarios, the disciplinary theme and the methodological one that allows him to incorporate a systemic thinking from resources of pedagogy and didactics, in this space learn both the teacher and the student and in the classroom a collective construction of knowledge is generated. Learning architecture from analogies obtaining the understanding of the processes? allows the student to make the transition from the basic skills of thought to the skills of the higher thought where conceptualization, critical thinking and metacognition are found, fundamental tools in his formation
