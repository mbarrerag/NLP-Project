# DOCUMENTARY REVIEW OF THE DIMENSIONS OF THE SPONTANIAN ABORTE

The study promotes reflection on the importance of the psychological and social aspects of spontaneous abortion, since these influence the mental health of those women who have gone through this traumatic situation. This theoretical review was carried out based on a search in databases (Redalyc, Dialnet, Scielo and Medline). Descriptors were: abortion and psychology, spontaneous abortion and mental health. It is essential to emphasize that when spontaneous abortion is performed, physical, psychological and social disorders converge, since the individual is a biopsychosocial being. This means that the biological, that is to say the abortion itself, influences the psychological, causing the traumatic experience and inducing psychological or psychiatric disorders, but the psychological is also defined by the actions of the people surrounding the woman who is going through this situation.
