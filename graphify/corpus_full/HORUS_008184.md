# Technical guidelines for the implementation of a long-term flexible pavement performance evaluation programme using the LTPP-FHWA program database applicable to Colombia as a reference

This final master's work focuses on the development and application of a Model Evaluation of the Performance of Flexible Pavements using as a reference the extensive database of the LTPP-FHWA Program of the United States. This initiative seeks to explore the interaction of various variables, using advanced techniques such as Automatic Learning (Machine Learning) to understand and predict the behavior of pavements under different conditions. In addition, it extends to the evaluation of the performance of pavements in a specific road project in Colombia, adapting the model to the variables and characteristics specific to Colombian roads. As an integral part of this study, detailed technical guidelines are presented for the implementation of a LTPP Program in Colombia. This includes practical considerations, such as the selection of monitoring stations, the frequency of measurements and the structuring of a centralized database. In addition, fundamental aspects of interinstitutional coordination are discussed and strategies are proposed to ensure the long-term financial sustainability of the programme. This comprehensive approach ranges from the advanced modelling of data to the concrete application in the Colombian context, contributing significantly to the knowledge of the behaviour of the pavements and providing a valuable framework for the management and management of the country. represented for the implementation of an LTPP Program in Colombia.
