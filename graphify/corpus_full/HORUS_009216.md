# Methodology of prediagnosis of moisture pathologies using AI in buildings

The methodology developed aims to contribute to the conservation and protection of the buildings by means of a tool developed as an AI that allows to make prediagnosis of pathologies by humidity in vertical elements (walls of masonry) and horizontal (sloping covers, flat covers, mellows in wood, concrete or metallic structure) through an algorithm of deductive analysis that uses the method of Hamming Distance in combination with some established rules. For this purpose, a recognition was made of the methods of sealing of the buildings, the materiality of its elements and finishes, the mechanisms of damage by humidity, identification of injuries and correlations between the damages and the elements to be used with this information to construct the methodology of prediagnosis of pathologies by humidity, whose application is focused, for now, on the maintenance, the work of pathology field and aftersales of the constructions is made a test by means of the proposals of other authors, with the difference that this is focused on the automation of this process, for this purpose, rules, correlations and interactions between all variables were established.
