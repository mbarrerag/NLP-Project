# Potato starch var. White ratona (Solanum phureja) as a substitute for wheat flour in the making of cupcakes.

The objective of this research was to evaluate the starch of native potato (Solanum phureja) variety White ratona, as a substitute for wheat flour in a product of breading of beaten mass like cupcakes. To this, initially a comparison was made of the physicochemical, functional and bromatory properties of native potato starch, with commercial starches. The effect of the substitution of wheat flour by potato starch on some properties both in masses and in baked product. The proportions of wheat flour and potato starch were optimized to obtain a better characteristic pancake and were evaluated sensoryly. It was obtained that the masses with potato starch were well aired without however their viscosity was lower than the masses with higher proportion of wheat flour, the starch had an antagonistic influence on all the evaluated properties of the product (LDTF), the maltability and the resulting was greater as the substitution increased.
