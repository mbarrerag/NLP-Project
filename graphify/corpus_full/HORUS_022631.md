# Evaluation of psychosocial care for people living with HIV or AIDS in Colombia and South America: review of scope and pilot test.

To describe the process of psychosocial care for people living with HIV or AIDS in Colombia and South America, to identify the quality in terms of accessibility, opportunity, safety and effectiveness. Material and Method: 1. Scope review (scoping review) in various sources to identify the evidence available on psychosocial care/mental health care for people living with HIV or AIDS (PVV) in comprehensive care and HIV control plans in South American countries, following methodology of this type of review. 2. Pilot test of health assessment through a survey of professionals and a structured interview of people living with HIV or AIDS in an IPS in Bogotá to verify compliance with the standards established in Colombia for psychosocial care. Both methodologies included categories of analysis on minimum quality attributes, psychosocial care models in HIV or AIDS and operation of actions. The data collected were stored and processed in Excel and a descriptive analysis was advanced. Both the review of scope and the pilot test showed that the accessibility of people living with HIV or AIDS to psychosocial care is free of use according to the principles of universality and equity
