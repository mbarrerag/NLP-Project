# ASSESSMENT OF THE USE OF THE NATIONAL ACADEMIC ADVANCED TECHNOLOGY (RENATA) NETWORK IN COLOMBIA

This article models the functioning of the Internet 2, the advanced national academic network (RENATA) in Colombia, by evaluating the fundamental services it supports and its respective performance. The academic and scientific importance of the article lies in discussing the problem that NRAN networks do not take advantage of as scientific tools that can have a dominant effect by promoting the use of these tools in different educational and research centers of the highest level. It is concluded that the model of connectivity in associated networks, that is, university networks of all major cities within RENATA, do not effectively take advantage of the advantages of bandwidth capacity, proven with 0.039 Gbps
