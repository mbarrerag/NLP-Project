# Building Territories: a Feminist View of the Experiences of Inhabiting TransMilenio Stations in Bogota

This work aims to analyze the territories built in some stations of the TransMilenio, part of the integrated system of public transport of Bogotá. From a feminist approach, using participatory observation, sensory ethnography, drift and some interviews, as methods of understanding the city of Bogotá as a male construction, which makes difficult the lives of other people, such as women and racialized people. While the public spaces of the city go through privatization processes and incorporation into the capitalist logic, groups of street vendors build territories through their presence and use of the stations of the TransMilenio. The networks that foster in their territories and the social practices that pass there constitute a domestication of the public space, therefore, a resistance to the individualist logic of the city as a capitalist construction. .
