# Evaluation of the mitigation of salinity stress in radish (Raphanus sativus l.) by inoculation of halotolising fungi.

The abiotic stress by salinity is associated with the detriment of plant growth, and therefore with the productivity of the crops. Therefore, the use of microorganisms that have phytostimulant mechanisms and improve the nutritional status of plants under salinity conditions, emerges as a possible solution to the present scenario of soil degradation by salinization, due to the excess of chemical fertilizers and also as an effect of climate change. In this research, the potential activity of four halotolating fungal isolates in the mitigation of abiotic stress by salinity and promotion of plant growth in radish (Raphanus sativus L.) was assessed, with the activity of four halotolative fungal isolates in the activity of ACC desaminase. Based on the morphological characteristics, and by means of the use of taxonomic keys, the fungi were identified preliminaryly as: Aspergillus terreus, Aspergillus fumigatus, Penicillium citrinum and Rhodotorula spp. The study will consist of three phases of the in vitro evaluation and three stages of the evaluation of the saline soils
