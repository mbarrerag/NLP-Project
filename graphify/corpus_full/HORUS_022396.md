# THE PHILOSOPHIC DECADE. A BALANCE WITH EXPLICITO MATTER

The balance of the decade that has just ended is addressed in the field of Philosophy from the perspective of change that has meant the emerging role that philosophers women have been taking. They go through the different epochs of the history of philosophy in which the contributions of philosophers in Colombia have been highlighted, and they also record the way their work stands out in the reflection on the contemporary situation
