# EFFECT OF EXTRASONIDE ON PHYSICAL CHEMICAL STABILITY AND CAROTENOID COMPOSITION OF A LIPIDIUM EXTRACT EMULSION OF CHONTADURO EPICARPY

The agro-industrial fruit processing generates a significant volume of residues that can be used as a source of bioactive compounds in the food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic industry. This research aims to evaluate the effect of ultrasound on the physical and chemical properties and the concentration of carotenoids in an emulsion made with lipid extract obtained from the chontaduro epicarp (ELEC). The formulation of the emulsion (O/W) contained ELC (15 % p/p), milli-Q water (78% p/p), Tween 80 (0.25 % p/p) and glycerine (6.75 % p/p).The evaluation of the process was made from a simple randomized design, with nine treatments obtained by combining three ultrasound powers (240, 340 and 440 W) and three process times (5, 10 and 15 minutes), and the evaluated response variables were: pH, surface color, electrical conductivity, potential z, and color concentration of carotenoids.The results indicate that ultrasound treatments significantly increase the concentration of carotenoids compared to control, and the ultrasound increased at 440 minutes
