# Burnout syndrome in teachers of basic education in Bogotá: a qualitative view of its causes, consequences and possible solutions

The Burnout syndrome in teachers is a phenomenon that has been awakening the interest of researchers during the last two decades because it takes on greater strength and feeds on the dynamics and challenges presented by education today. A group especially affected by this condition is the teachers of basic education, primary and secondary, who face a number of factors that contribute to the generation of it. This is a qualitative case study of descriptive cut that investigated the perceptions of nine teachers of basic education, primary and secondary, about the factors that most influence the generation of the Burnout syndrome. Among these, we have different questions regarding the way in which education is conceived in Colombia and the teachers themselves. .
