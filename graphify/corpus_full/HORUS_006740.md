# Strengthening statistical thinking in grade 11 students from the resolution of problems with a social critical approach

This work seeks to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with statistical thinking in students of the eleventh grade, the Educational Institution Guarinocito, located in the rural area of the municipality of La Dorada, Caldas, based on the design and implementation of learning activities based on problem solving in the social context. This work has a qualitative approach with a descriptive scope, its execution follows the methodology PPDAC (Problem, Plan, Data, Analysis and Conclusion). Among the results obtained, progress is highlighted in: ability to address a problem in its context, identify relevant study objects and recognize the concepts necessary to define the problem; ability to design questions that collect relevant data, select appropriate methods of information collection and develop data collection instruments; analysis on the relevance of the data collected, use of the data collection instrument, the way in which data are organized and the continuous evaluation of the data collection strategy; ability to develop adequate graphs, use different types of graphs to present the information, identify measures of central tendency (such as media and fashion), understand the relationship between the data, tables and graphs, interpret the data and the infer from the statistical capacity of the analysis, the analysis of the data. in its context, identify relevant objects of study and collect the concepts needed to define the problem; ability to design questions that collect relevant information, select approach data collection methods and develop data collection tools; analysis on the survey of the data collected, the use of the data collection tool, the organization of the collected data, and the continuous evaluation of the information collection strategy; ability to make adopt graphs, make predictions from the data collected, use different types of graphs to present the information, identify measures of central tendency
