# Mortality and postoperative outcomes after 30 days of urological surgery in Colombia during 2016-2017.

To determine mortality and postoperative clinical outcomes at 30 days of urological surgical procedures performed in patients of the contributory regime in Colombia during 2016 and 2017. Using the base for the study of the adequacy of the UPC of the Ministry of Health in Colombia, information was collected on intrahospital outcomes and 30 days of patients belonging to the contributory regime submitted to urological procedures during 2016 and 2017. The identification of cases was performed using the codes of single classification of procedures in Health (CUPS) and the codes of the International Classification of Diseases (CIE-10) and CUPS related to each event were analyzed to estimate the rate of intrahospital mortality and 30 days, the need for transfusion of hemo-components, admission to intensive care unit and the rate of hospital admissions at 30 days. The urtic procedure was emphasized in major urology procedures including radical, radical and partial nephrectomy and radical cystectomy with and no urinary bypass.
