# Development of algorithms in R and Python for processing and analysis of data from different sources related to phytoimprovement and related areas.

Currently, progress in the sciences of computing, artificial intelligence, statistical tools for the processing of images and other types of data has been turned to many researchers of the different areas of knowledge to use these modern techniques to discover patterns that allow to classify, predict or describe variables of agronomic interest with the information available both from the environment, soil and the physiologicals of the crop. In this sense the proposal involves the use of various techniques that will be applied to the data available for analysis and discussion of results, all assisted by R, Python, Shiny and any other related tool
