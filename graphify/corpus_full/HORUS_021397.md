# Cooperation network for the production of hydrogen from glycerol and its potential application as an organic liquid hydrogen transport molecule

One of the modern energy systems that has most attracted attention, and that can replace the use of fossil fuels, is the one based on hydrogen. This is presented as a vector that facilitates a sustainable energy future with a carbon free economy. However, the use of this molecule in the energy sector is limited by the lack of infrastructure and efficient production processes. In particular, those problems related to its storage and distribution. In search of a possible solution to this problem, this research proposal raises the use of glycerol, a product derived from biomass, as an attractive platform for the production of green hydrogen and, in search of a sustainable approximation for the storage of this energy vector, its potential use as a liquid organic hydrogen transporter molecule (LOHC, by its acronym in English).
