# MAGNETIC RESONANCE AND DIAGNOSTIC IMAGINATION OF PENE PROPOSALS ALTERNATIVES.

Penis prosthesis is the third line of treatment for erectile dysfunction. It produces high patient satisfaction. Complications may occur and the urologist should be prepared to recognize and treat them. Nuclear magnetic resonance imaging (NRI) can be a valuable tool for diagnosis and decision making. We present the case of a patient with a penis prosthesis with a device-related complication, in whom magnetic resonance imaging was fundamental for its evaluation, diagnosis and treatment. In conclusion, urologists should be familiar with magnetic resonance, an alternative imaging method to diagnose complications associated with penis implants
