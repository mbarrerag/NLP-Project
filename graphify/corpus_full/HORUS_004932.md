# Analysis of the articulation of Neuraltherapeutic Medicine with primary health care in the alternative health center La Nueva Esperanza in Popayán, Cauca, 1987-2005. Case study

Through a case study, a description and analysis of an experience of providing basic health services was carried out in Popayán Colombia, developed in a post-earthquake period between 1987 and 2009, in the so-called New Hope Health Center, being the first primary health care center in Colombia with Neural Therapy and community work, managed by women community leaders. It employed a methodological model of Research Participatory Action - IAP, promoted by the non-governmental organization called Foundation for Popular Communication -FUNCOP, which lasted several years in interaction with the public health care system. .
