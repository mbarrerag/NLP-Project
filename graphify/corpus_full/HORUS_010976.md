# Due process in administrative actions of photomults in the district of Barranquilla.

The present document was intended to analyze whether the Secretariat of Transit and Transport in the District of Barranquilla has been fulfilling the right to due process and the principle of legality in the imposition of comparends product of the use of the photomulths system, in order to identify within the process the consideration of the fundamental rights of the persons involved. Therefore, the study was carried out during the establishment of the jurisprudence guidelines that have been developed around the application of photomulths as corrective and preventive measure. Additionally, the identification of the legal and constitutional scope of the right to due process in contrast to the administrative procedures of transit in the district of Barranquilla and the mechanisms of legal defense that have been developed in the result of the non-observance of the constitutional and normative legal precepts in the administrative actions carried out by the transit authorities was revised. Under the above, a qualitative methodology was used, type of descriptive where the instrument used was a matrix of documentary analysis was explained.
