# SYSTEMS DYNAMICS MODEL: A CENTRAL REVIEW

This paper presents a scientometric review of scientific production related to the topic of system dynamics model. It was based on a search for publications in the Scopus and Web of Science databases using the search equation system dynamics model by field title. A total of 956 publications were found, 946 in Scopus and 10 in Web of Science, which finally found that the latter are contained in Scopus. The algorithm of the science tree, Tree of Science, which by its acronyms is known as ToS, was used, by which root articles, trunk and leaves were identified and statistical and graphical representations were obtained. Among other aspects it is emphasized that scientific publication is in full development and that its application extends to numerous areas of knowledge
