# Expanded Chair: concert, audition, show

Through the performance of two student concerts, the premiere and recording of a piece of percussion group and the design, management, realization and evaluation of a percussion festival "week of percussion" this project seeks to create new learning spaces that expand the training offer of the percussion class and allow its members to develop the competitions associated with the production on stage: concert, hearing and show
