# HUMAN GROUPS, WATER AND LAND IN THE NOROCCIDENTE OF SABANA: A MILENARY RELATIONSHIP

Chapter dedicated to the transformation of the territory of what is today the locality of Suba. It begins by analyzing the pre-Hispanic sedentary groups of the area and their forms of agricultural production. It then analyzes how with the Spanish invasion a forced process of drying up of the territory began, considering that the "civilization" was contrary to the foundation of cities and towns in flood areas. It then stops in the process of forming the Suba shelter and the various haciendas that it had throughout the colonial and republican period. Finally, it analyzes the critical situation that the water lives in the Sabana of Bogotá in general, and in particular in the town of Suba, due to the decrease of the underground aquifers, the impact of urbanization and the contamination of the bodies of water
