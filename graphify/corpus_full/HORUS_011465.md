# A PROTOTY FOR SUSTAINABLE SOCIAL LIVING

The Solar Decathlon is an international university contest related to the construction of sustainable housing based on the use of solar energy. The contest, which originated just over a decade ago at the initiative of the Department of Energy of the United States, with the participation of North American universities, subsequently extended to Europe (Spain, France) and more recently to Asia (China).For its short career the contest has gained recognition and reputation worldwide as the most important contest on sustainable housing. At the end of 2015, in the city of Cali the first edition of the Solar Decathlon for Latin America and the Caribbean was held (SDLAC 2015), this time promoted by the organizers of the contest, the Alcaldía de Cali and the Ministries of Mines and Housing of Colombia. Undoubtedly it was an excellent opportunity for universities of Colombia and abroad to formulate housing proposals aimed at addressing energy issues lately- but also to address the topic of social housing for Latin America and the Tropica. It was a great deficit and problems in the region. The National University of Colombia participated with the prototype UNSOLAR. It was an opportunity for the Faculty of Architecture and Mines and the Autonomous Communities of Colombia to be more active in the country. Finally, the UNSOLAR prototype was an honorable fourth place, among 15 finalists of the competition, which also opened up other academic, research and extension perspectives, such as the possibility of continuing interdisciplinary work with other faculties of the University, to constitute an interdisciplinary laboratory of sustainable social housing, and the possibility of developing extension work with sponsoring companies. This book contains the memories of what were some of the most important parameters and disciplines of the proposal presented by the National University of Colombia, among which are urbanism, architecture, construction, civil and electrical engineering, and finally, the aspects that were most taken into account on sustainability. The texts are made by some of the professors and advisers who participated during the competition. We have the experience reflected here; the satisfaction of having fulfilled the expectations of the project, since the proposal and the prototype built offer concrete and feasible solutions to real problems of the community and the environment; as well as the momentum to continue to advance on this topic of sustainable social housing that is so much needed in the local and regional contexts
