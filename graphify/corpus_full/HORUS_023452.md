# ANALYSIS TO THE STATE OF EDUCATION IN ETHICS IN THE POST-PROGRAMING PROGRAMMES OF THE METROPOLITANA AREA OF THE VALLE DE ABURRA

The need to inculcate and train students and society in ethics in general is becoming more evident today, given the increasing cases of corruption and lack of ethics that occur at all levels of society around the world, with academic institutions being the first to include subjects within the curricula that facilitate such training at all educational levels and that promote the appropriation and application of ethics-specific knowledge in personal, social and organizational situations. The objective of the research is to determine the state of ethics training in graduate programs of the Metropolitan Area of the Valley of Aburrá AMVA and to determine what it is like to include these topics in the curricula of the programs, for which a simple random sampling was carried out and 271 postgraduate programs were evaluated. Of the total programs analyzed only 24.4% offer subjects related to ethical topics, and within these, the master's programmes are the most contributing programs
