# THE PRESIDENCY OF TRUMP AND THE RUPTURE OF THE INTERNATIONAL FREEDOM

ABSTRACT Donald Trump's foreign policy questioned several of the foundations of the liberal international order in which, for decades, the superpower had supported his leadership. This paper investigates the factors that explain this phenomenon, based on the analysis of the political and economic transformations that have been taking place in American society, and the way in which they were reflected in the main decisions of that administration
