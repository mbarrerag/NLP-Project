# AQUALIOT: Real-time water quality

The economic, social and cultural development of a country arises around its natural resources, either by its exploitation, production or use for the progress of growth activities. Water, is the natural resource transversal to any activity, either of an economic, social or cultural nature. In Colombia the preservation and monitoring of water resources is limited compared to other countries including Latin America, which have strict and supervised controls of the physical, microbiological and physical characteristics of water. Information on the current state and behavior of resources, in this case of water, is the first essential element that is required for any decision making made by the country. In Colombia, the online monitoring of water resources is limited and there are regions and areas of the country that its information is non-existent. The Institute of Hydrology, Meteorology and Environmental Studies (IDEAM) through the National Water Survey (ENA) is responsible for determining the current situation and possible future water scenarios in Colombia, considering the components of supply, demand, quality and hydrological risk, based on the characteristics of natural occurrence and current effects that are in the context of the natural environment and the society. The analysis of the origin and the situation of water supply, the four different water scenarios is the different components of the different components of the country. These 5 parameters must be measured online in the potabilization systems. Having the online measurement of these variables, real-time information can be transmitted through IoT communications, for subsequent storage and visualization presented through a cloud-hosted web application with a public and available open data system
