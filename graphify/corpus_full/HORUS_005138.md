# A new species of Souroubea (Marcgraviaceae, Ericales) from Colombia

A new Souroubea Aubl. species from Colombia is described and illustrated. The species is easily recognizable by its white pedicellar reproductive nectaries and perceptible laminal glands on both sides of the leaves. Only known from a small area in the Andean region of the Caquetá Department (Amazon slope), it is categorized as “critically endangered” (CR) according to the UICN criteria
