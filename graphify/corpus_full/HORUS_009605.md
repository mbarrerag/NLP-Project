# On a New Parabolic Sobolev Embedding Map

The purpose of the present article is to provide a new parabolic Sobolev embedding map between a parabolic weighted Sobolev space and the space of square-integrable functions on a cylinder. Furthermore, the embedding constant is furnished explicitly
