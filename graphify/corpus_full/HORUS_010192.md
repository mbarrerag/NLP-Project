# Influence of being operated on in a University Hospital on the survival of patients taken to a valve replacement procedure affiliated to the contributory regimen in Colombia between 2016 and 2019.

To determine the association of being intervened in a university hospital with survival in patients affiliated to the contributory regime in Colombia who were first taken to valvular replacement between 2016 and 2019. A retrospective cohort study based on administrative records. As outcomes, the time to the event of death and the cumulative incidences of death, re-entry and re-intervention are presented. An artificial sample is configured by weighing the inverse probability of treatment by propensity scores to estimate the marginal effect of exposure adjusted by sociodemographic variables, comorbidities, route and weight of intervention. From a sample of 3,690 procedures corresponding to 3,551 patients, 1,065 (30%) were operated in a university hospital. The median age was 64.9 (18.1-91.5), with 38.6% of patients ≤60 years and 6.8% ≥80 years. The cumulative incidences of death to 30, 90 days and one year were 5.9%, 8% and 10.9%, respectively. 23.7% had a re-entry to 90-days and 3.8% a re-risk per year. The patients experienced less than 30 % of death to 0, 0, 0 days and 1 year
