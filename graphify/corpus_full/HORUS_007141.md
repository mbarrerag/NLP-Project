# Supercritical CO2 extraction of secondary metabolites of chamomile flowers

The parameters affecting the extraction process of secondary metabolites of chamomile flowers (Matricaria Chamomilla), from Colombia, were studied using the technique of extraction with CO2 under supercritical conditions. An experimental central point design with unidimensional analysis was carried out, allowing the study of temperature (35 °C, 45 °C and 55 °C), pressure (150, 250 and 350 bar) and CO2 flow (5, 10 and 15 Kg/hr) as extraction parameters and developed in kinetic study over 6 hours that allowed to determine the point of highest extraction yield. The study was carried out in a system of extraction at a pilot scale of 7 L, then the best extraction conditions were applied to the extraction of manzanilla flowers from the regions of San Cayetano and Chipacán de Cundinamarca – Colombia, was characterized and compared with its composition by means of chromatography of gas coupled to mass spectrometry.
