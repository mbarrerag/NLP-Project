# RETURN MODELS FOR VARIABLES WITH ANSWER AT THE INTERVAL [0.1]: CASE OF A PREDICTION STUDY FOR ENERGY

The review aims to compare the predictive performance of statistical models for variables with response in the interval [0.1], specifically in the context of energy production in wind farms. Although there are some model comparisons for this type of variable response in the literature, these do not address all the options of statistical models and considerations included in this study. This study stands out by comparing a wide range of statistical models, from specific regression models for variables in the interval [0.1] such as quasibinomial regression and beta regression, to semiparametric and machine learning models, after a transformation of the response variable (including v-logit, probit, cauchit, cloglog, loglog) and considering a bias correction to mitigate the impact of nonlinear transformation when predicting the response variable in its original scale
