# Method for the detection of students at risk of desertion, based on a metric design and a data mining technique.

The dropout is a social problem of high impact for the educational system, hindering social mobility and the construction of equity in a country.In the last decade, with the rise of student data, the research of this phenomenon in education has been addressed from the mining of educational data and one of the main characteristics of this classification is its early detection, especially in emerging and underdeveloped countries where school dropout is increasingly frequent.The educational institutions need to detect the risk of student dropout in a timely manner and thus support the student in his or her permanence within the educational system.The above, it has led to a systematic review of literature in the computational area, demonstrating that in the last five years several methods have been developed for the early detection of the risk of school dropout, and in turn has generated new challenges in the identification of the most influential factors, the approach of new efficient and interpretable methods that can be implemented and the need for adequate representation and selection of characteristics.In addition, with this type of data, it is necessary to preproce the task of pre-processing due to the he orogeneity of the variables. However, it is not to allow only to find the causes of the abandonment and the 57% of the total number of students reached with the initial characteristics without the use of metrics. Therefore, it is recommended that the potential application of this method be used in the analysis of educational data, which allows for early prediction of the risk of desertion and the generation of strategies that will enable students to be persuaded to remain in the educational institution
