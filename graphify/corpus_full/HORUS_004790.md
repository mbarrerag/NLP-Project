# CATALAN IDENTITIES FOR GENERALIZED FIBONACCI POLYNOMIALS

This paper extends two Catalan identities, originally formulated for the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers, to polynomial sequences of the second order, which have a Binet formula similar to that of the Fibonacci and Lucas numbers. These polynomial sequences are classified as of Fibonacci type and Lucas type. As a result of this generalization, Catalan identities are obtained for a range of polynomial sequences, such as Pell, Pell-Lucas, Fermat, Fermat-Lucas, both types of Chebyshev polynomials, Jacobsthal, Jacobsthal-Lucas, and both types of Morgan-Voyce polynomials. Furthermore, we use generating functions and the Wilf-Zeilberger algorithm to derive a general expression for Catalan identities and other combinatorial identities
