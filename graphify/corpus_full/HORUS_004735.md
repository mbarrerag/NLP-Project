# Proposal for the mitigation of thermal effects on the environment due to the exploitation of stone materials for construction. Pilot experience, Santa Rita mine in the municipality of Medellín

This work was carried out in two mines located in different areas of the department of Antioquia, the first is a coal mine that is called La Margarita, in jurisdiction of the municipality of Titiribí with underground exploitation and open sky. The second is a quarries of pétre aggregates that is located in the city of Medellín. In open-air mining the surface organic layer is removed to extract the elements with which the mine benefits, leaving exposed rock, contributing, punctually, to the increase of the temperature of the zone, so it is important to find alternatives that help reduce the increase of the temperature generated by the mining. One of these alternatives is to use solar panels in areas that have already been exploited and that will take time to intervene, for this, decrease the temperature while generating energy that helps economically the mining company and that can have many other advantages.
