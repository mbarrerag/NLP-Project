# Leadership and decision-making in Manizales SMEs

The aim of this study was to establish the relationship of the type of leadership of the managers of the SMEs of Manizales, the result of which was the decision making type. For this purpose, a field work was carried out in which information was obtained from 81 managers of SMEs of the city of Manizales, through the application of the instruments General decision-making style (GDMS) and Multifactor Leadership Questionnaire (MLQ), which were analyzed statistically through the SPSS Software. The main results indicate that the behaviors are associated with the components of the transformational leadership. The study emphasizes that the intellectual stimulation and the inspiring motivation are the attitudes with which this group of leaders is most identified. While the Inspiring Motivation is configured as the least present. Regarding the way the decisions are made, it was found that these are mainly associated with the rational and dependent processes and finally it was established that there is a high correlation between the style of transformational leadership with the rational style of decision-making.
