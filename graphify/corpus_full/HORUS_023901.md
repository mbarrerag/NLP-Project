# GAYMENT, GAYMENT AND ANALYSIS OF DRIVING: THEORICAL AND PRACTICAL REVIEW

The game and gamification analysts are increasingly using games and gamification strategies in research and applied contexts, this. Games and gamification are tools that emerged in areas other than the analysis of behavior, so they use a different language and underlying theories. In this presentation, we briefly address how games, gamification, game elements, game behavior, game design and fun can be understood and worked in the analysis of behavior. The dimensions of games are explained: learning, motivation, usability and effectiveness; some underlying basic processes are explored such as strengthening, modelling, motivating operations, rule-driven behavior and stimulus control. Finally, some theoretical and methodological trends used by the analysts of behavior in the use of games and gamifications are shown. In addition, future developments that can occur within this field of study and application are presented
