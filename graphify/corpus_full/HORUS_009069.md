# ASSESSMENT OF DECADE BATTERIES FOR COMPONENTS FOR HETEROGENSIVE CATALIZED SYNTHESIS

The mass use of electronic components has resulted in the production and disposal of batteries (highly polluting). In Colombia, about 11,000 tons per year are discarded, together with the forecast of global battery production of 400,000 tons for 2025 with a mean life of 3 years [1], this results in significant environmental problems, due to the high content of heavy metals [2-3], which end mainly in landfills, together with the fact that current recycling is generally limited to mechanical processing and separation of components or direct cross-bordering of batteries, thus disutilizing the economic potential that represents the recovery of graphite and metals present for subsequent use in different applications. Generally, processes of extraction of metals based on the use of mineral acids have been implemented, which in addition to being corrosive and costly, are highly toxic to the environment, animals and man. According to literature, the use of assisted methods with sonication in obtaining carbonous supports and the extraction of metals has been vilumbrated as a more secure and environmentally friendly process, besides being simple waste, becoming a major factor of work in the process of implementation and are based on the large-scale of the use of the use of batteries
