# Collaborative design for new structures and intervention to existing structures with vernacular materials. Case study: Centralidad veredal de Mazotes, Cocorná.

The centrality of the village Mazotes consists of the school, the hall of the communal action board and a sports plaque in the open air, which is used by the children of the school and the other inhabitants of the village. However, these spaces are not suitable for the activities that are carried out there, the lack of economic resources is an impediment to being able to expand and improve the current conditions of these spaces. Also, the lack of awareness and technique for the use of vernacular materials that are abundant in the area; regarding the above, a collaborative design methodology is proposed in which, from the needs and ways of inhabiting the people of the place, more suitable solutions are proposed for this community. Through the joint work and technical orientations, it is hoped to create designs that the community can subsequently use to request support in its development from governmental entities
