# THE M-19 PEACE PROCESS AND ITS PARTICIPATION IN THE CONSTITUTIONAL REFORM OF 1991

The guerrillas of the M19, participating in a constituent assembly, achieved political and democratic spaces that allowed them to raise their political ideals and develop a thought without armed struggle, also to influence norms that benefited disarmament and reintegration into social life, also led them to participate in a majority in the assembly that promulgated the 1991 constitution. Is this process of pardon, disarmament, reintegration and peace pact possible with constitutional reform in the current negotiations?
