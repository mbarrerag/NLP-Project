# Map of descriptive risk of mass movements, by means of images, based on the geometry of the slope of a mountain on the Guaduas-Bogotá track

Land slides are the result of bottom-level movements of a mass of rock, detritus or land by gravity effects. Social losses amount to 34,198 people died and at an economic level to 600 million dollars, throughout the national territory between 1900-2018 [Herrera-Coy et al., 2023]. This, denotes the importance of risk management to prevent adverse effects of natural phenomena such as mass movements. In this research work, the problem described using artificial intelligence techniques for detecting slides through images is addressed. A dataset of 1000 images collected from the Mass Movement Information System of Colombia (SIMMA) is generated with a standardized size of 1440x1000 pixels of width and high, 96 pp (pixels per inch) and a depth of 24 bits. The photographs are of landslides in different stages of development. Subsequently, a machine training developed via the automatic learning algorithm YOLO v8, of which models with average accuracy mAP of 68 % are obtained. This allows to identify the unstable geological scenarios that can generate mass movements in different stages.
