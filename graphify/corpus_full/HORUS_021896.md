# "The right to digital labor disconnection in Colombia": conceptualization, development and perception in the Colombian national territory; a theoretical-legal approach.

This paper seeks to make a theoretical legal contribution to the tenuous approach that our legal system has presented regarding the topic of digital labor disconnection. For, although Colombia is one of the Latin American countries that pioneers in the normivization of social patterns that allow the exercise of constitutional rights, it is also one of the few that exercise the active implementation of constructive criticism and valuable academic contributions, tools that allow the concepts defined for a specific topic not to become dogmatic but to transform, arriving at legal institutions closer to the social reality and to the “physical” phenomena of the common, but not an unreal written approach. Therefore, it must be examined in those normative and constitutional limits that allow to differentiate this emerging right from others with similar characteristics, but, dissimilar in its content.
