# CAPSAICIN INDUCES TRANSCRIPTIONAL CHANGES OF PRONOCICEPTIVE AND MINERALIZATION-RELATED GENES IN HUMAN ODONTOBLAST LIKE-CELLS

To evaluate the transcriptional changes of pronociceptive and mineralization-related genes induced by capsaicin, the specific agonist of transient receptor potential vanilloid subtype 1 (TRPV1), in human odontoblast like-cells (OLCs) Methods: Dental pulp stem cells from four donors were differentiated to OLCs using an odontogenic induction medium. The odontoblastic phenotype was confirmed by RT-qPCR of dentin sialophosphoprotein (DSPP) and Dentin matrix acidic phosphoprotein 1 (DMP1) genes. Ratiometric Ca2+ imaging using Fura-2AM was performed to confirm the activation of TRPV1 by capsaicin and its inhibition by iodo-resiniferatoxin (I-RTX), a selective antagonist of TRPV1. We determined the effects of capsaicin-induced activation of TRPV1 on transcriptional changes of OLCs. We tested two pronociceptive genes, prostaglandin-endoperoxide synthase 2 (PTGS2), encoding cyclooxygenase-2, and IL1B, encoding interleukin 1-ß, using RT-qPCR in OLCs treated with capsaicin only or capsaicin plus I-RTX. We also assessed two mineralization-related genes, DSPP and DMP1. OLCs obtained from different donors showed up-regulation in the DSPP and DMP1 expression. OLCs cells stimulated with 100 μM capsaicin exhibited an increase in the Fura ratio by 72%. The treatment of OLCs with I-RTX decreased the capsaicin-induced calcium fluxes significantly. Furthermore, capsaicin treatment induced the up-regulation of PTGS2 and IL1B transcripts, which was prevented by treatment with I-RTX. The OLCs treated with CAP showed a down-regulation of DSPP and DMP1. This study shows that capsaicin treatment up-regulated the genes involved in the pronociceptive inflammatory response and the down-regulated mineralization-related genes in OLCs. Therefore, TRPV1 in OLCs could be involved in the regulatory roles of inflammatory and mineralization processes
