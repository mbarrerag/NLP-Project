# ASSESSMENT OF THE POSSIBLE IMPACT OF CLIMATE CHANGE IN PARAMO CAUDALS WITH A TEST OF MODELS

The hydrological modelling plays a fundamental role as a tool for planning and managing water resources in wasteland ecosystems. Among the objectives of the models is the assessment of the impact of climate change scenarios on the generation of flows in these basins. However, most of these exercises have been carried out by implementing a single hydrological model, which can result in errors and biases due to the structural uncertainty of the model. That is why in order to evaluate the possible impacts of climate change on the generation of flows in the Chuza river basin in the Chingaza Páramo, the construction of a model assembly has been carried out by using the Granger Ramanathan Type C (GR-C) method. For this, four hydrological models and a neural network were used to simulate climate change scenarios from two global circulation models (GCM) CMCC-ESM2 and EC-Earth3-CC.
