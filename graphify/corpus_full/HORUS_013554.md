# Impact of intradialytic nutritional support on haemodialysis patients: A systematic review

Patients undergoing haemodialysis have a higher risk of malnutrition due to loss of appetite, anorexia, restrictive diet, accumulation of metabolic waste, catabolism, among others. To carry out a systematic review of the literature of studies carried out in the last 10 years that evaluate the impact of intradialysis nutritional support in patients undergoing haemodialysis to provide a synthesis of the state of art of recommendations for its implementation and identify future research priorities. Systematic review following PRISMA-2020. The search was based on databases, selected articles, performed an independent reading of each study, applied the scale CONSORT or STROBE and ROBINS-I. Seven researches were included. With intradialysis meals, the body composition improved biochemical indicators such as albumin, IL6 and TNFα, with the SNO and NPID, improved biochemical indicators such as albumin, prealbumonia, PCR; anthropometrics such as weight, size and IMC; and body composition as the mass fat, magra and muscle mass.
