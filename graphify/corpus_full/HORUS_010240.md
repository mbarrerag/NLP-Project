# ANTROPOLOLOGICAL-PRAGMATICS CONSIDERATIONS ON PHILOSOPHY AND EDUCATION, IN ANTROPOLOLOGICAL LESSONS OF IMMANUEL KANT

The interest of Kant on philosophical reflection on Education should not be placed exclusively in its text on pedagogy, but should be explored thanks to the intense teaching activity that he carried out for more than 25 years.Although the above mentioned text is based on a compilation of the concerns on this subject, carried out by his student Friedrich Theodor Rink in 1803, starting from the four courses that on the Educational year, which Kant gave in the semesters 1776-77, 1780, 1783-84 and 1786-87, the concerns on this subject have an even greater dating that can be traced in the decades 1760 and 1770. These concerns, mainly developed in the courses taught on different subjects in the last of these decades, have an approach that few traces appear in the work of 1803. This approach is explicitly manifested in the so-called Lessons of Anthropology, which Kant in an ineantitic and innovative way introduced in the study plans of the Albertina University of Könisberg.
