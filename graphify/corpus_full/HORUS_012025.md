# Peudohypoparathyroidism: orphan disease in endocrinology. Report of a case

The pseudohypoparathyroidism (SPT) is a rare genetic disorder characterized by resistance to parathyroid hormone (PTH). In Colombia there are few reports on this disease, so the publication of this case contributes to increase interest in its search in the clinical field. Presentation of the case. 18-year-old man with history of hypothyroidism (diagnosed at 4 months of birth), growth delay, late puberal development, obesity, brachydactylia, pathological fractures, femoral osteochondroma, insomnia, paraesthesia and chronic constipation, who attended the endocrinology service of a third-level hospital in Bogotá (Colombia) referred after having been hospitalized by a convulsive episode. In this hospitalization the laboratories showed hypocalcemia, hyperphosphatemia and 25-OH vitamin D in a range of insufficiency with elevated levels of PTH, and the imaging studies demonstrated heterotopic osifications and calcifications of fatal glands of the brain.
