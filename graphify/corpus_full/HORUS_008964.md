# INNOVATIVE TECHNOLOGIES FOR THE SAFE USE OF MEDICINAL PRODUCTS.

The unsafe practices and medication errors are the main causes of injuries and avoidable damages in health care systems. These errors can be produced by failures in medication systems or by human factors affecting any stage of the process: prescription, preparation, dispensing, administration and monitoring. This problem leads to the WHO declaring as the third global challenge for Patient Safety the initiative called: Medication without harm. The strategic framework of this initiative proposes four key areas of action: patients and the public, health professionals, medicines and systems and medication practices. In relation to the latter, nursing is closely linked in the administration of medicines and patient monitoring, since it is one of the most frequent activities in the practice of disciplinary care management. As a product of research carried out in this area, currently recognize what are the most frequent errors in medication administration; the various predisponding factors and have been proposed various strategies for its prevention. Within these latter, technologies have developed technologies and different devices that demonstrate greater effectiveness and safety when they are incorporated in the medication systems. The objective of this research was to report the research and the research developed for the research. The studies available agree, however, that these technologies are very appropriate strategies for patient safety and prevention of medication error, but should never replace critical thinking, clinical judgment and the responsibility that nurses assume in the administration of medicines
