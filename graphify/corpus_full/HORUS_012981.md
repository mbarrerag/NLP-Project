# Evaluation of external vulnerability in Colombia and its main determinants

In this document, an aggregate index of external vulnerability is constructed for the Colombian economy between 1996 and 2022. Said index incorporates information from a large number of variables that are intended to reflect the exposure of the Colombian economy to shocks that may affect its macroeconomic and financial stability. The results show a deterioration of the external vulnerability index in the periods in which the country went through episodes of macroeconomic stress (1996-2000, 2008-2009, 2015-2016, and 2020)
