# Intervention project for the Old Normal building of Gachetá, (Cundinamarca)

The building of the Old Normal of Gachetá, built in different stages between 1937 and the 80s, is constituted as an architectural complex representative of the educational architecture of small municipalities, serving in its origins as promoter of the traditional and religious feminine education of the period for the region cundinamarquesa del Guavio. After passing through the abandonment and subsequent unequal use, the building arrives to this day with a state of variable conservation; what makes necessary its heritage valuation and an adequate intervention with the urban and architectural context in which it is situated, since its particular characteristics is a milestone for the municipality, allowing it to be more recognized by the institutions and inhabitants of the village, thus promoting and projecting a better destination that integrates both the parts of the complex and the buildings of its immediate context, thus restoring its educational and cultural vocation, thus creating a potential cultural project that serves the community and the region. .
