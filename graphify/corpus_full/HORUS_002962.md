# BUILDING ECOSYSTEMS CONNECTORS OF BIODIVERSITY AND PUBLIC SPACE BOGOTÁ D.C. AND THEIR ARTICLES WITH THE SABAN REGION

The conference is the presentation of three levels of intervention in different scales and instruments in the territory through plans and projects of systems of green public spaces as structural axes of the territory from the urban to the rural and regional (from the macro to the micro) consolidating a fabric of biodiversity, fundamental component of the urban and rural fabric. Civil society and its private entities must participate actively in the configuration and/or construction of this environmental mesh of biodiversity
