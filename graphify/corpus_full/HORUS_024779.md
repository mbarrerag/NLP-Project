# Instrumentation, measurement of unit deformations and calculation of the stress status of API X42 grade pipes, Codex 40, Diameter 8 inches, subjected to internal pressure and bending.

The project presents the result of modeling and laboratory tests for mechanical efforts in 8-inch API X42 pipes, 40-cardboard, subjected to bending and internal pressure, simulating ideal conditions of interaction of soils on pipelines of buried pipelines. A model was performed in the Ansys software to analyze the behavior of the pipe to bending and eight laboratory tests were carried out, applying pressures and flexion forces to generate unitary deformations in the center of the pipes, measuring with eleven strain gauges in each. The load, displacement of the tube center and applied internal pressure were also measured. As main conclusions of the research, the low incidence of internal pressure of the pipe in the resistance to bending was verified and a model of efforts of pipes tested from unitary deformations was obtained.
