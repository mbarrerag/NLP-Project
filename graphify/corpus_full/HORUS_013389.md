# Conscious prescription of antibiotics in dentistry

The emergence of a large number of antibiotic resistant infectious bacteria, associated with malpractice in its use, has triggered alarms by several health organizations, which are inviting professionals in this area to practice the conscious prescription of these drugs and to carry out epidemiological surveillance of resistant bacteria. Strategies to improve antibiotic prescription include educational ones, which seek to update knowledge and raise awareness regarding the appropriate use of these drugs. In the area of dentistry, different studies have shown frequent errors in antibiotic prescription, either during unnecessary clinical tables or as antibiotic prophylaxis in healthy people. This indicates the need to provide, in a clear and concrete way, knowledge tools for a conscious prescription of these drugs. Through a reflective and updated writing, this book will facilitate the understanding of the basic concepts, which will allow the odontologist, or future dentist, to associate the effect of antibiotics with pathogenic bacteria and the mechanisms of resistance they develop, as well as key elements for the correct prescription of these drugs use later, will include the types of infections and future odontologist, which will actually end the effect of antibiotics on the pathogenic bacteria and the mechanisms of resistance they develop, as well as the key elements for the correct prescription of these medicines
