# Digital educational resource (RED) for learning quantum numbers and electronic configuration.

This paper presents the design of a digital educational resource (RED) in open source platforms, focused on learning quantum numbers and electronic configuration in high school students; the design was based on the information obtained from the proof of identification of previous knowledge, resolved by students of the 9th grade of the educational institution GI School, focused on the topics related to atomic structure, the disposition and organization of electrons in atoms. The development of this paper allows to highlight the importance of the identification of previous knowledge in the planning process of activities of different kinds, as well as the need to build tools, simulators and other virtual instruments that allow the visualization of matter on a subatomic scale, facilitating the understanding of these concepts in the chemistry area, as well as the promotion of a dynamic and motivating learning environment for students
