# ASSESSMENT OF THE IMPLEMENTATION OF A MICP UREOLITIC BACCTERY FOR THE BIOREMEDIATION OF CDS IN CACOOTERS

The precipitation of carbonates induced microbiologically (MICP, by its acronym in English) has been used for the bioremediation of soils contaminated with metals. However, its application in agricultural soils with presence of Cd has been little studied.The objective of this study was to evaluate the application of the bacterium ureolitic Bacillus sp. 85d for the immobilization of Cd in cocoa soils by MICP.Two types of experiments were carried out, one at laboratory level using two soils with different concentrations of Cd (SA: 5.44 mg kg-1 and SB: 1.62 mg kg-1) and one at plant level of living (soil SV, with a concentration of Cd of 2.56 mg kg-1).The monitoring of pH, ureolytic activity and distribution of Cd in soils by sequential extraction was carried out over 3 months.
