# Application of valuation methodology for a firm in the fibrocement sector aimed at generating strategies for sustainability

The aim of the text is to apply a valuation methodology for a company in the fibrocement sector in order to generate strategies that ensure its sustainability and competitiveness in the long term. Through the financial analysis based on the deconverted cash flow method (FCL), it seeks to identify strengths, weaknesses, risks and opportunities, considering the economic relevance of the construction sector and its current challenges. The study proposes strategies focused on financial management, operational efficiency and the optimization of the working capital, highlighting the incorporation of models of renting, process digitization and continuous improvement as key tools to adapt to the dynamic environment and ensure the creation of future value
