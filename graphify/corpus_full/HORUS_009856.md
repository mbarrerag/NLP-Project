# GENERAL PROVISIONS ON THE QUARADURIA OF BIOLOGICAL COLLECTIONS

In this space we analyzed who or who are the persons or entities responsible for caring for the collections. The role of the researcher in proposing the project and seeking resources for obtaining suitable materials for the conservation and storage of the collections. The role of the collector and/or preparer of the specimens for the correct preservation and the association of the specimen with adequate information. The responsibility of the curator in the conservation, preservation, storage and protocols of use, among many others, of the collections. The role of the entity that supports and houses the collections and that defines who the curator is, whose profile should include the knowledge in the principles of preventive conservation, not necessarily in the taxonomy of a defined biological group. We analyzed the purpose of the museums and the importance of the associated information (field notes), with the aim of carrying out robust research. It emphasized on the type of preservation, which also depends on the use of the specimens and the type of material to be preserved and invited to reflect on the different procedures to be made about the specimen (pros and cons). It considered the purpose of what the storage furniture is (proteger, find), and its improvements in this). is, along with integrated pest monitoring, the basis of the management protocols of the collections. Finally, it was emphasized that the integrity of the copies and their data should not be compromised
