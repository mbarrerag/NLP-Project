# Estimate of the fundamental period of buildings made up of concrete porches considering fissure sections.

In structural analysis processes where force-based design methods are used, estimation of vibration periods of structures are fundamental parameters, since from these estimates the magnitude of seismic design force is determined. In particular, the method of horizontal equivalent force is one of the most used methods for the calculation of seismic force and is based on the determination of approximate periods, estimated from empirical formulations that only take into account the height of the building, the structural system and the material of the structural elements. However, these periods, in time in reinforced concrete buildings, do not necessarily reflect the conditions of rigidity when considering the possible degree of fissure that structural elements may experience due to the demands of inelastic deformations imposed for idealized seismic design conditions. Thus, through this research, an alternative is proposed to the empirical formulation to estimate the fundamental period of buildings made by concrete pórticos considering fissure sections
