# HANDLING AND OVERCOME IN CARE OF PERSONS IN HEMODIALYSIS AND PERITONEAL DIALYSIS

to determine the differences between the care ability and the overload in caregivers of patients on peritoneal dialysis and hemodialysis attended in a health center in Cúcuta-Colombia. Comparative descriptive study. 25 caregivers of persons on peritoneal dialysis and 43 of persons on haemodialysis were included. the skilled instrument for caring care of caregivers of persons with chronic disease and the interview of overload zarit was applied. Key results: A significant difference was found in two of the three dimensions of care ability. Faced with overload, higher levels of impact and expectations-competence were presented in the peritoneal dialysis group. Caregivers of patients on peritoneal dialysis require greater support and preparation that allow their ability to care in instrumental and knowledge aspects. Carers of patients on haemodialysis require greater preparation for their interpersonal relationship with the care recipient. Key words: Carer. Kidney dialysis. Kidney failure. Burden of the disease
