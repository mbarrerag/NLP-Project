# Generation of materials for applications in the production and use of hydrogen as an energy source

The production and use of hydrogen as a source of energy is a topic of scientific research of great importance worldwide. Energy demand grows day by day and climate change requires urgent solutions in the short and medium term. This project seeks to consolidate the manufacture of electrodes for the production of hydrogen (from salt water) at the headquarters of peace, the development of electrodes for combustion cells that use hydrogen as fuel to generate electricity, and the theoretical modeling that contributes to a better understanding of the behavior of the materials manufactured. The project seeks the alliance between groups and researchers of the university that leads to greater cohesion and strengthening of the university network (we are a single university)
