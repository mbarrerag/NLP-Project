# PHYSICAL CHEMICAL, BACTERIOLOGICAL AND PARASITOLOGIC WATER PERFILE FOR HOTABLES CULTIVES IN BOGOTA SABANA

The discharge of domestic and industrial waste water into channels used for irrigation of crops is a practice that many farmers from urban and peri-urban areas exercise in many parts of the world. It has been documented that in Colombia, only between 8% and 10% of domestic waste water are treated. However, the re-use of these waters for agricultural production is of great interest, because farmers obtain the nutrients necessary for the development of their crops, avoiding the use of fertilizers and/or chemicals for the vast majority. The aim of this work was to evaluate some physicochemical, bacteriological and parasitic parameters in 96 water samples used for vegetable crop irrigation, from the former irrigation district of La Ramada, which covers several municipalities of Cundinamarca, and is fed by water from the Bogotá River.
