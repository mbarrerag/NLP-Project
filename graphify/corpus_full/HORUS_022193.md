# Consent in the interaction of users with social networks and their commercial use in the framework of the protection of personal data.

Beyond being the most efficient means of communication of the present day, social networks have become a phenomenon that allows the breaking of cultural and physical barriers, allowing the entertainment, promotion of products, services and even the dissemination of ideologies. Precisely the use of potentialities of the collection and processing of personal data that are reached with these platforms deserves a system of protection that guarantees the constitutional right to know, update and rectify the information that has been collected in these networks, which for the Colombian case find regulation in Law 1581 of (2012). In this context, this work aims to illustrate the normative progress that has been achieved in the protection of personal data in the Colombian order, and as the principles that integrate the system of protection of personal data has allowed the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, as the authority in the field of data protection, to demand from the three major social networks of greater presence in Colombia; Facebook, YouTube and WhatsApp, the realization of activities aimed at the protection of fundamental rights of Colombian users
