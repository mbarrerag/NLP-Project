# Freight-Transit tour synthesis

This research presents a model of synthesis of multiclass demand for transit and load, using maximisation of entropy and diffuse logic.The model incorporates traffic data and diffuse parameters to adapt to uncertainty.The use of diffuse logic improves classic modeling by providing flexibility and addressing data uncertainty, a critical aspect in decision-making scenarios with limited resources.Final resources, such as the ability of pathways, require optimal decision-making.Flexive models are essential, since not all constraints can be fully met.Fuzzy logic is highlighted in the management of variability and uncertainty, improving the reliability of results.It helps to estimate congestion patterns, emissions levels and accidents, thus providing valuable information to decision-makers.Funtually, the flexibility of diffuse logic is crucial for real-world adaptability. Improved transport planning, benefiting urban mobility.The accuracy of results directly impacts on decisions.The diffuse logic incorporates real-world variability into models.The research focuses on triangular membership functions, a common-use approach.The models of diffuse logic demonstrate the adaptability of the diffuse logic. taken from the source)
