# INTRODUCTION: GENDER, FEMINISM AND SOCIO-AMBIENTAL, TERRITORIAL AND KNOWLEDGE DEFINES IN GLOBAL CONTEXTS

This chapter addresses the defenses, demands and resistance of indigenous women of African descent, peasants and urban areas (Women-iacr) against extractivism, institutional policies and urban expansion. Women-iacr focus on the integral defense of territories and non-humanism through various strategies, practices and actions around their knowledge, emotions and affections, and spirituality. Likewise, the daily actions they carry out around sowing, agroecology or care networks that tend to their permanence and survival in their territories based on their modes of life, which become resistance against the processes of appropriation and overlap of territorialities. Daily actions and practices that become other ways of making policies or micropolitics that tend to confront and reconfigure socio-environmental and intersectional structural inequalities
