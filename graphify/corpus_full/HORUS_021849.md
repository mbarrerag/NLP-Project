# Change of safe infrastructure paradigms for vulnerable users in the city of Bogotá.

The fatalities due to road accidents –global 1.35 million, Colombia 7000 and Bogotá more than 500 – mainly affect people between 5 and 29 years of age, worldwide.To reverse this problem, actions are required on users, vehicles, speed, infrastructure and their management.In Bogotá, the infrastructure prioritizes the vehicle, contrary to the reversed pyramid of mobility and, despite advances in normativeity and institutionality, these are not reflected in the designs, which do not systematically address road safety.The methodology included a multicriteria evaluation to choose a section of an arterial corridor (2.3 km), as a study site, in Bogotá, without trunk, based on indicators that prioritize unfavourable road safety and with studies and designs for future enlargement.A black point study identified seven sites to be evaluated, covering 79% of the sinisterality and 46% of length.The HSM method allowed to estimate the projected casualties, according to changes in design, resulting in an increase of more than 500% in total sinisterality.Sultaneously, a cross-risk assessment between the current and projected road conditions, using a high-risk, using a high-risk and a low-risk design
