# CRISTALIZATION OF BIOACTIVE VIDRICIES OF THE SYSTEM 31SIO2-11P2O5-(58-X) CAO X MGO: INFLUENCE OF THE TERMS AND CHEMICAL COMPOSITION

In this study, the influence of the chemical composition and heat treatment on the crystallization of bioglasses of the system 31SiO2-11P2O5-(58-X) CaO X MgO (X = 0, 2, 7, 16 and 32) was studied. The thermal treatment was performed at 900, 1,000, 1,100 and 1,200 °C for a period of one hour. Subsequent to this process, the samples were characterized by X-ray diffraction analysis, Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy and differential thermal analysis, finding that by increasing the content of MgO the formation of the crystalline phase hydroxyapatite is inhibited and the formation of the crystalline phase whitlockite is promoted
