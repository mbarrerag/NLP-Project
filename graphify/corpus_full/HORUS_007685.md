# Fluss

The project consists of designing a small hydrokinetic turbine for use in mountain rivers, with the objective of generating energy for individual houses, similar to solar panels.This idea arises mainly because access to electrical energy in the region is difficult for some families, due to the long legal processes for the installation of energy poles, which can take more than 2 years.In addition, difficulties are encountered in implementing conventional renewable energy systems. Photovoltaic systems lose effectiveness by variability and high frequency of rainfall and cloudiness. There are no constant winds in the area for an effective wind turbine, and mountain topography hinders their implementation. Conventional hydraulic energy systems require great water falls to function, which is a problem because the rivers of the region are found in the valleys using the different studies of floating Savonius type. Therefore, the alternative of generation is posed by a hydrokinetic turbine, which would be installed within the river channel, thus taking advantage of the abundance of rivers in the region without requiring large water falls. Initially, the project aims to design a low-cost turbine of the river, near the river surface. To date, the project has carried out conceptual tests to validate the feasibility of constructing a functional prototype of a floating turbine for turbulent rivers. In these tests, a low cost prototype was constructed to assess its behaviour in water and estimate the magnitudes of the forces to which it will be subjected in the target river, the Chorreras River. The prototype was evaluated at a water speed of 1.7 m/s. Although the test prototype does not include flotation systems or electrical systems, it was observed that, thanks to the drag of water and the anchoring by cables, the prototype is kept close to the surface. The measured speeds in the Chorreras River are in the upper range of the studied river speeds reported in the literature [3], allowing the use of existing studies to estimate the potential generation of the turbine.
