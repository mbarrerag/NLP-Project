# Hydrological analysis of maximum flows in the upper basin of the Medellín River (Colombia) with stationary and non-stationary conditions

Abstract In the upper basin of the Medellin River (Colombia), a hydrological study was carried out to analyze the rain-running relationships at the scale of events in this area, for the estimation of the maximum flow rates associated with different return periods in the river basin and tax subbasins, using hydrological modelling considering climate change. This study is composed of the characterization of the basin of the study area, as well as hydrological characterization, which includes the analysis of data measured with high temporal resolution of precipitation and river levels. Different methods are also taken into account for obtaining the information necessary for the correct execution of semidistributed hydrological modelling activities, using HEC-HMS software. The latter model was calibrated simulating 5 maximum flow events of the year 2021, and then executed with different configurations of previous humidity condition, rain-run models and the consideration of stationary and non-stationary conditions (in precipitation), the latter with the aim of evaluating potential effects due to climate change.
