# Echeverri Cortes House Conservation Project, Bogota Cultural Heritage

The present final master's work presents as a case study the house Echeverri Cortes located in the historical center of Bogotá, whose main objective is to elaborate a proposal of corrective and periodic maintenance that guarantees its conservation, since it is not in poor condition of conservation. The patrimonial significance present in the building, as a representative piece of the urban non-monumental fabric of the neighborhood La Concordia, begins with its construction as a single family house in the second decade of the last century, passes to the district administration who leaves it in abandonment by a period, until the acquisition of the Barrio La Candelaria Corporation, who has been admitted to the Association of the Exbecarios of Japan, which carries out a project of adaptation. Years later it becomes the headquarters of the local mayor of La Candelaria and currently is used as a repository of work inputs and editorial of the IDPC. The work formulates corrective actions and periodical according to the identification of early injuries to be more complex in the future, significantly reducing the costs of intervention and preventing the advanced scenarios in which the restoration is the only viable way of conservation.
