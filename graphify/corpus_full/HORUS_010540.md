# Symptomatic remission in a patient with rheumatoid arthritis treated with Neural Therapy. Case report

Rheumatoid arthritis (RA) is a chronic incapacitating disease that affects individuals mainly in productive stages of life. So far there are no studies on symptomatic remission of RA in patients treated with Neurotherapeutic Medicine (NTM), for this reason, the case of a patient with RA treated with this medical system is reported, so that it can be of assistance in future research on the management of this disease with complementary therapies described in the medical literature. Presentation of the case. 43 years old woman, who from the age of 38 began to present episodes of inflammation, pain in multiple joints, joint morning stiffness, frequent decrease of muscle strength in hands, emotional lability and difficulty to reconcile sleep, also had a positive result in the rheumatoid antibody test. It was under multidisciplinary management, including MNT, by means of injections with doses of procaine 1%. The patient presented remission of symptomatic medicine despite having suspended the pharmacological treatment formulated by the internal medicine area for the management of AR.
