# RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN LOW IMPLANATION OF THE MUSCULAR VIENTE OF THE CORRECT PERONEE AND LUXATION OF THE PERONEAL TRENDS

Abstract Introduction: The pathologies of the peroneous tendons are an important cause of pain in the lateral region of the ankle. It has been proposed in the literature that the low implantation of the muscular belly of the short peroneum occupies more space in the retromalalal groove and could cause laxity of the upper retinality associated with luxation of the tendons, tenosinovitis and/or breaks. The aim of the study is to characterize the population with low implantation of the muscular belly of the short peroneum and to study the association between the low implantation of the muscular belly of the short peroneum seen in the magnetic resonance and the luxation of the peroneum tendons. A case study and controls were developed with a sample of 103 patients. The cases with dislocation of the peroneal tendons and low implantation of the muscular belly of the short peroneum and the patient controls with luxation of the peroneum were 0,5% of the normal and temoneal implantation of the lenus.
