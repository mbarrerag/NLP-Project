# Counting Colored Tilings on Grids and Graphs

In this paper, we explore some generalizations of a counting problem related to tilings in grids of size 2×n, which was originally posed as a question on Mathematics Stack Exchange (Question 3972905). In particular, we consider this problem for the product of two graphs G and Pn, where Pn is the path graph of n vertices. We give explicit bivariate generating functions for some specific cases
