# BELIEFS AND CULTURAL VALUES IN THE NICU: MOTHERS' NARRATIVES IN THE CARE OF PRETERM INFANTS

Premature infants are vulnerable groups due to their physiological immaturity that require specialized care in the NICU. The mother must learn this care from the time of hospitalization until she returns home with the infant. This learning in NICU care, which involves nursing and family participation, constitutes a first step toward the mother's autonomy in the care. Through the observations recognized through experience, the lack of scarce participation of mothers in the care of the premature newborn was identified, which is why the research interest resided in the need to inquire about the meaning for the mother of her participation in the care of the premature newborn during hospitalization in the NICU
