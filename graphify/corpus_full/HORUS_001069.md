# B-LEARING FOR DEVELOPING PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCES FOR ADVANCEMENT OF AMBULATORY PATIENTS

A pedagogical proposal with a socio-constructivist model is presented when conceptualizing innovations in the development of the training practice of doctors resident in internal medicine, to care for outpatient patients. This proposal is exemplified by type 2 diabetes as a prevalent disease. This progress arises from the path travelled by the author, internist and educator, in particular with the research on "Construction and evaluation of a profile of professional competencies in internal medicine." The general and specific objectives, justification, the pedagogical proposal with didactic actions and strategies, the line of research in type 2 diabetes, the concept of professional competencies, the pedagogical model and B-Learning, the training practice in external consultation and B-Learning, options for improvement and final reflections. Teachers and programme coordinators are invited to reflect and review the time spent on outpatient care and service during residency, as well as the development of professional competencies by resident physicians
