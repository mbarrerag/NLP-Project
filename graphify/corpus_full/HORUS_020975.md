# Analysis of the accountability of false positives in Colombia in the first decade of the 21st century, since counter-accounting.

The research studies the process of accountability related to the _x0093_false positives_x0094_ in Colombia during the first decade of the 21st century, a painful situation that presents contradictory information, both in terms of numbers and characteristics, which is addressed from the academic perspective of counter-accountability, with the aim of constructing an alternative version of accountability, from a counter-account with the perspective of the representatives and relatives of the victims, as a way of contributing to the consolidation of peace and coexistence, within the framework of a new government that wants to ratify the peace process signed with the insurgency. To this end, the work is based on documented information from the victims and other qualitative techniques of information collection, in order to try an alternative academic interpretation of that regrettable period that was lived in the country
