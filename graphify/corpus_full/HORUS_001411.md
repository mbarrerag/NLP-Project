# ARTISANAL WORK IN COLOMBIA CULTURE INDUSTRIALIZATION PROCESS

Introduction Crafts in Colombia can be considered, from the process of cultural industrialization, as part of the heritage and popular tradition, with significant changes in recent decades; industrial/craft duality has been integrated into the process of globalization. Political commitment to the protection and cultural rescue of craft activity is confronted with the expansion and globalization of creative work, demonstrating that contradictions in the relations between labour and capital place emphasis on culture. Moreover, advances in knowledge of craft work have enabled the construction of local and regional policies and programmes. These advances are in addition to academic products such as books and magazines in several fields such as administration, cultural management, design, anthropology, history, etc
