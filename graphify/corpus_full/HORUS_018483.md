# DIGITAL PLATFORMS AND IMPACTS IN CONTAINING WORK OF THE CONCERT ECONOMICS.

The present review article discusses the different employment patterns that arise with the emergence of digital platforms. In these, there is a tendency towards job flexibility, both for the hired and the hired, which could boost jobs by allowing people to access more jobs. However, with this flexibility and with these modalities, an unwanted result can be generated, especially for the workforce. It is about labor insecurity. In this sense, for the preparation of the document a documentary review of the topics of trend on this topic was carried out. It was concluded that there is a need to legislate or regulate both platforms and the interaction to generate and thus avoid these problems
