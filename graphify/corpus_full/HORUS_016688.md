# School violence in classrooms, streets and cyberspace: inquiry at the Gabriel García Márquez Educational Institution of Soacha (2019-2021)

This research aims to establish the levels of both victimisation and aggression due to school violence, in its various physical and virtual manifestations, to which the students of the baccalaureate Gabriel García Márquez, located in the commune 4, Altos de Cazucá, Soacha are exposed. Together with these statistics, perceptions and feelings of the students regarding school violence are included. The research is of a concurrent mixed type and has as a temporary framework of analysis the period between the years 2019 to 2021. In order to obtain the data necessary for its development, a self-administered survey was applied to which 189 students of the educational institution responded and 4 focal groups were formed, where they participated between 3 and 4 people. The main results indicate that the physical violence among the students of the analyzed institution is not an instrument for the presence, but rather to diriminate conflicts, since elements specific to the harassment as threats and persecution are uncommon. On the other hand, it was discovered that the verbal violence is the most exercised by the students in all the dimensions of the analyzed institution, is to say in the physical devices and the virtual field of the Internet.
