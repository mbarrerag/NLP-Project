# The Referral of Cases or Extrajudicial Measures as an International Obligation for the System of Criminal Responsibility for Adolescents in Colombia. A proposal for implementation through Restorative and Therapeutic Justice

The referral of cases or extrajudicial measures, as an international obligation for the system of criminal responsibility for adolescents in Colombia. A proposal for implementation through the restorative and therapeutic justice. The referral of cases or extrajudicial measures, is an obligation under the jurisdiction of the States that is contained in international instruments of the human rights of children and adolescents, which are part of the constitutional block. It seeks that adolescents linked to judicial proceedings are referred preferentially to: alternative options, processes and practices other than judicialization, and is a development of the principles of dejudicialization and minimal intervention in the specific field of juvenile criminal justice systems. In Colombia, not only is there no regulation of this international obligation, but also the Constitution and the Law establish provisions that limit the discretionaryness of the exercise of criminal action in the system of criminal responsibility for adolescents, which prevent the use of alternative solutions to the criminal justice process in all cases and which are therefore contrary to the provisions of international instruments that make part of the block of constitutionality. The principle of opportunity is a distinct form of the criminal justice system, the alternative forms of justice and youth. opposite to the constitutional pad. About it, we have to say that the opportunity principle and the diversion measures come true by means of alternative justice, as the restorative justice and the therapeutic jurisdiction
