# Strengthening the Security and Defence Research Group

The Grupo de Investigación en Seguridad y Defensa (GISDE), for the year 2022 aims to consolidate its efforts in generating new knowledge in two lines of research: 1) the line of study of the Public Forces and 2) the line of illegal armed actors (and its implications in public and citizen security), through the formulation of two research projects. On the one hand, for line 1, led by Professor Alejo Vargas Velásquez, it is proposed to develop the project _x0093_The configuration of the national defense in Latin America before a new geopolitical scenario_x0094_ with which it is intended to evaluate the current defense scenarios generated in Latin America and Colombia after the health emergency by COVID 19 and the political changes in the region. As for the latter, it is important to note that the cases were selected taking into account the peculiarities of the governments, seeing continuity of the authorities and democratic changes, so that the changes that take place in a geopolitical way must be analyzed in an ideological context of each country.
