# EMPREVIATION IN POSPANDEMIA: GLOBAL AND NATIONAL SCOPE

The foundation and perspectives of entrepreneurship constitute a profound analysis of the pillars and evolutions inherent in entrepreneurship, an activity of great importance in the promotion of socioeconomic development. This work, composed of seven chapters, embraces a critical and multidisciplinary approach, in order to explore key aspects of entrepreneurship, through methodological approaches ranging from the analysis of the conceptual evolution of entrepreneurship, whose trajectory starts from classical economic school and contemporary sociological and psychological perspectives, to the conceptualization of business and management models, and the exploration of ecosystems and entrepreneurial models. It also explores fundamental areas, such as the role of emotions in the entrepreneurial process, the analysis of the post-pandemia context and its influence on entrepreneurial activity. To those that join the future perspectives for strengthening agencies and programs to support entrepreneurship. This book constitutes an essential source of knowledge for academics, researchers and students seeking a holistic and up-to-date understanding of entrepreneurship. It is also of great interest to actors involved in the entrepreneurial ecosystem and want to deepen this phenomenon, fundamental to socio-economic progress, as well as for the construction of public policies
