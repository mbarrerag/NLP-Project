# Determinants Motivationals for the Election of Informal Employment in the Island of San Andrés

This thesis aims to provide a qualitative-descriptive approach to the choice of informal employment in the island of San Andrés, by the economically active and legally established population on the island, which has voluntarily decided to use it in informal activities. This, in order to dignify and exalt the activities that the informal workers of the island carry out. Likewise, voluntary informal employment is proposed as an alternative to production, in view of the progressive environmental destruction produced by the capitalist model and, as a means of achieving harmonious coexistence between man and nature proposed by the indigenous view of the Sumak kawsay. This is achieved by collecting life stories of different workers and informal workers on the island, whose knowledge is representative, from their area or occupation and, within the island's labour market.
