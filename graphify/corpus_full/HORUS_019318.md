# HISTORICAL PERSPECTIVE OF TECHNOLOGICAL INTERVENTIONS IN MENTAL HEALTH

In recent decades, technologies have transformed most of human practices, and between it health services have not been left out (Chaudhry et al., 2006). The use of technologies to optimize health services has occurred not only as a means that allows for agile and direct communication with patients, but also have opened the doors to the development of technological tools that allow to diversify the types of interventions and that have entered to play an important role in the different levels of mental health care (Ben-Zeev, 2017). Considering the above, the first chapters of this chapter focus on the psychological care mediated by the Internet or online care with different populations, and this section will present the technological developments that have been put in the service of the interventions in health or that have been developed specifically within the field.
