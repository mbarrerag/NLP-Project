# Variation of the phosphorene electronic energy gap with the number of layers, external electric field and pressure.

This research is carried out in order to characterize multilayer phosphorene with external effects by means of the theory of density functional (DFT). This is done to find the electronic energy gap theoretically. To obtain these values of electronic gap, the SIESTA package ("Spanish Initiative for Simulations with Thousands of Atoms"). Energy gap values are calculated for the systems of multiple phosphorene stacked foil, the 3D system of black phosphorus, the multilayer system with electric field, and phosphorene with tangential pressure. The functional GGA-PBE and VDW-DRSLL were implemented to obtain the value of the gap in the multilamins. Our main results show a decrease of the gap by increasing the number of sheets, as well as a decrease applying external electric field, with the symmetrical decrease relative to the sign of this field. Involve two or more phosphorene sheets the material presents a transition from semiconducer to metal and from new to semiconducer
