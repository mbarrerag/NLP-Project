# Critical work by David Jiménez Panesso

This project consists of the meeting of manuscripts of works (some unpublished) by David Jiménez Panesso, who was professor of the Department of Literature of the National University between 1987 and 2002. Professor Jiménez is the author of several fundamental books of literary studies in Colombia, such as "History of literary criticism in Colombia", "End of the Century: Decline and Modernity", and "Poesia and Canon", as well as of diverse studies on poetry and narrative in the country
