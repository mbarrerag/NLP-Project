# Optimal distributed control for microgrid distribution systems

ABSTRACT The increasing consumption of electrical energy, as well as the progressive development of new technologies, leads to the electrical system being increasingly automated with the aim of having a more efficient and economical operation. This development guides the system to be a Smart Grid, a large-scale cyber-physical system that encompasses different technologies of power generation, storage and communications that allow exchange of information in real time and control over the parameters of the network. In this work, an approximation of the optimal control distributed based on the consensus+innovation technique is presented, where each agent of the network obtains information from its neighbours. Simulations on a micronetwork system based on a reference system IEEE 34 nodes demonstrate the effectiveness of the approach. Convergence is observed in the micronetwork system under different scenarios in the physical and communications network
