# DIVERSITY, ANCESTRY AND GENOMIC STRUCTURE OF THE BLANK WIN OREJINEGRO DE COLOMBIA

The White Cattle Orejinegro (BON), is a genetic resource of great importance, however, there are still gaps on the ancestry, mixture and global genetic diversity. To develop a genomic analysis of the patterns of genetic diversity, the population structure and ancestry of the cattle BON. Genealogical information was taken from 7799 individuals and DNA was extracted from blood or semen from 419 animals BON of 14 different herds. From the samples, DNA extraction was carried out and the genotyping of 70 individuals with the GGP-Bovine 150K chip was performed. The animals were taken with the GGP-Bovine 50K. The common information between the two chips (40k) was taken and the quality control was performed (MAF ,095 by the SNP and individual mothers). Subsequently, the purification was performed by LD (R2<0,2) and the heterozygosities, the Fis fixation index was estimated, the PCA analysis was performed and the population structure by FST with Plink v1. Finally, the analysis with other breeds showed the closeness of BON with the Iberian races. In addition, closeness to WP and recent low introgression of the Brahman race.
