# INTERCONEXION OF GENERATION SYSTEMS DISTRIBUTED: EOLIC MICROGENERATION

This chapter presents the integration of distributed generation systems such as wind-micro-generation systems. It also presents the behavior and normativeness applied to network connection of distributed generation systems, technical and data management requirements of DER for active integration into the distribution power grid. Planning of the national electricity system is fundamental to dimension the growth of the network and optimal operation ensuring the highest capacity in its transmission and distribution generation levels. Planning seeks objectives of stability, reliability, quality of the electrical energy to its population [9]. Planning of the electricity sector requires integrating new renewable energy sources to diversify the energy matrix of the country with the objective of increasing efficiency, digitization and flexibility in the operation of the network [9]. Thus, the new distributed energy resources sources (DE) use primary energy sources such as solar radiation, wind energy, diesel or natural gas. Additionally, within this category, energy storage technologies such as batteries are integrated.
