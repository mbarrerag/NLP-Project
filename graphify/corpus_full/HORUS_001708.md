# Development of tool for asset selection and portfolio optimization of the stock market

The main objective of this research is to develop an application that helps in the selection of assets for the formation of portfolios of variable income and that optimizes the portfolio formed from the selection of assets. In turn, the specific objectives of describing the tools currently used are set out, selecting the variables that will compose the tool from the initial analysis and, finally, evaluating its effectiveness and operationality to ensure its usefulness and relevance. The final product of the tool provides the user with the performance and risk of the portfolio, as well as the optimal weight of each asset, based on different optimizations according to different risk profiles. This result is useful for the investor and, together with knowledge of other strategies that study these products, allows for a better decision-making from the gap and the advantage to be taken. It concludes that the tool developed works effectively, providing a user-friendly platform and providing valuable results that investors can use to make informed decisions, applying concepts of various financial disciplines. with information on the portfolio's risk, performance, and the optimal weight of each asset, based on different optimizations according to various risks profiles. This result is beneficial for investors and, together with other strategies that analyze this type of assets, facilitates better decision-making. Include, the tool developed operators effectively within the defined parameters, offering a user-friendly platform and providing valuable insights for investors. These insights can support decision-making, leding concepts from disciplines focused on those types of financial products
