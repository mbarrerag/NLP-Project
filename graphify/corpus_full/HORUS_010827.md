# Critical success factors in the management models of improvement in the operations for companies in the pharmaceutical industry located in Bogotá and the shroud

Critical success factors in the management of improvement models in operations for companies in the pharmaceutical industry located in Bogotá and La Sabana The literature review, prior to the development of this research, suggested that the critical success factors in the implementation of improvement models are affected by the specific context in which the organization operates (Bullen & Rockart, 1981); and taking into account that there are studies related to common critical success factors between different models (Cherrafi et al., 2016; Friedli et al., 2010; Pacheco et al., 2015), as well as in the pharmaceutical industry of other countries, could be interested from the administration to identify, for the specific context of the Colombian national pharmaceutical industry, the critical success factors that project leaders should take into account in the implementation of models to improve operational efficiency. In the literature review, no articles related to the context and the variables indicated in this document were found, therefore, an inquiry tool was built based on the tool proposed by (Alhuraish et al., 2017). In his comparative study of factors for Lean and six-sigma, including factors identified in other models of continuous improvement, in a way that would allow finding in the specific context, the factors that the improvement leaders of the pharmaceutical organizations of Bogotá and La Sabana, identified as critical to the success of the implementation of improvement models. The results of this work are intended to provide valuable information for the leaders of Colombian pharmaceutical organizations on the factors that must be prioritized to promote success in the implementation of improvement models for their operations and thus facilitate the achievement of organizational objectives. From the literature review, 7 articles were selected that grouped different models of continuous improvement and 26 critical success factors from the literature review and research in various industrial and social contexts. Of the total of 26 factors, the recurrence in the different models was identified to select the 12 that presented the highest number of coincidences between the different models studied. These factors were included in the inquiry tool to be applied in the development of this research in the pharmaceutical organizations of the scope. The survey, of 36 questions, was sent through magnetic means to the leaders of the pharmaceutical companies of Bogotá and La Sabana, requesting contribution in the study, obtaining 24 for a total of 50% participation on the population updated to 2022 and from 86% of the population proposed in the project. The reliability of the tool was evaluated with the Cronbach test, ratifying the internal consistency of the survey and the validity of the construct, and the sampling was evaluated with the Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin statistics, and Bartletto's sphericity. Once the information was validated, it was identified that all the respondents consider the following to be very important factors: communication, senior management commitment, quality management, training and education, focus on strategy and focus on clients. Quality management could be determined as the only critical factor since it is for which the mode of response to the constructs that compose it is 5. Factors such as knowledge of the improvement tool, its impact on suppliers, the organizational culture and the focus on employees were valued as important, according to the proposed scale. It is coincidental for all the respondents that the factors included in the tool are important to a significant degree in the success of the implementations. The factors that obtained constructs with the highest number of assessments in the slightly important category are the focus on suppliers and the focus on employees. The results confirm the importance of the factors included in the research, in the success of the implementation of the improvement models in the context of the pharmaceutical industry of Bogotá and La Sabana, despite coming from different industrial and social contexts, giving response to the original objective of the proposal to identify the critical success factors in the management of improvement models of operations in the Colombian national pharmaceutical industry and the specific objectives of identifying from the literature, the critical success factors common to different models and operations improvement tools, with an emphasis on the pharmaceutical industry; Evaluate from the Colombian context the Critical Success Factors related to models for improving operations in the pharmaceutical industry; and Categorize the critical factors of success in the specific context of the Colombian pharmaceutical industry, according to the results of the evaluation. Future studies may consider exploring the factors through other inquiry techniques such as interviews or open questions that allow interviewees to present specific factors from the particular experience of each case and improve access to information
