# Other voices on child labour, reflections for occupational therapy.

This research is interested in promoting spaces for reflection for occupational therapy, revealing epistemological and conceptual bets from other perspectives that could broaden the look on child labour. For this process the Fundación Creciendo Unidos FCU, opened its doors allowing us to contact working children. The research work has a qualitative design, with a historical-hermeneutic approach, under phenomenological analysis, interested in making visible the voices and experiences of working children to stress hegemonic understandings that pose the eradication of child labour as the only way to address the topic, and from there expand the look of professional exercise and the reflections of Occupational Therapy on the topic. It is concluded that based on the postulates of the Southern Epistemologies, it is made possible to analyze how paradigms and understandings about children constitute positions around the theme, so that it distinguishes between abolitionist position protectionist, child and child labor, as well as differentiating between child work and child exploitation. As regards Occupational Therapy, the approach focused on the game, is stressed, to the focus on the topic, to make the objective of social participation and to act as distinct subjects
