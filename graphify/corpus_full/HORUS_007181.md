# Design of guidelines and model of application of inclusive education at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Manizales

Inclusive education is a huge challenge for society at large, at times when social changes, movements seeking the claim of rights and historically violated and excluded groups seek greater participation and insertion in the dynamics of society. Higher education is one of these areas, where it comes or tries to reach a more diverse and heterogeneous audience, why academic institutions seek to generate these inclusive spaces based on the design and implementation of an inclusive education policy. This work seeks to give light through a historical journey through education theory, the processes of exclusion and inclusion from different aspects, the multilateral and multi-organizational responses that have been built and proposed, but on, all presenting a model of both policy design in inclusive education, as a model of implementation of inclusive education policy for Manizales headquarters, within the framework of the general construction that the National University of Colombia is developing. étical responses that have been built and proposed, but above all to present both a general model of inclusive education policy design and a model of implementation of the inclusive education policy for the Manizales campus, within the framework of the general construction that the National University of Colombia has been developing. Ultimately, it is important to realize that including education itself, more than an instruction, is an opportunity to develop a new organizational culture that is based on an inclusive culture, including practices and openness of the university community in general towards this process
