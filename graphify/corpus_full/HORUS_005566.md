# METAMODEL FOR UNIFICATION OF PARTICULAR MATERIAL DATA PM 2.5 AND PUBLIC HEALTH EVENTS

This article presents the definition of a metamodel for the unification of data related to the chemical characterization of PM 2.5 particulate material and health events. The integration of data is based on the application of a model-led approach and the design of a data warehouse. Initially, conceptual models of each domain were constructed, and then defined common aspects among them, in order to identify the super-classes that make up the metamodel. Finally, a data cellar was designed for future implementation. As a main result, an abstract model was obtained that allowed the identification of concepts that integrate the defined domains
