# Preparation and methodology for the assembly and interpretation of the bambuco "María Antonia" in a symphonic band

This document constitutes one of the necessary requirements to obtain the title of Master in synphonic direction of the National University of Colombia. The document presents two fundamental aspects for the assembly of the work “María Antonia”, in a traditional Colombian rhythm, as is the bambuco, in symphonic band format. These aspects will be detailed with the purpose that the reader has a guide and can achieve an adequate interpretation within the traditional stylistics of the symphonic band and gender. On the other hand, the document presents a context about the process of synphonic bands currently in Colombia, the bambuco and the incursion in this piece of new sonorities and timbricas of the composer and arranger cundinamarques Andrey Ramos Herrera. Finally, it shows a morphological analysis, harmonic and textured, indicating in turn the process of assembly from start to end. .
