# COMMODITIES AND CORROSION OF IT-CP POROUS POROUS POROUS POWDER BLOCKS MANUFACTURED BY POWDER METALLURGY

In this research, a porous titanium base material was developed with a rigidity module and a bone-like morphology by a pulp-metallurgical processing route, which includes the stages of mixing titanium powders with foaming, uniaxial compaction and sintered, produced a titanium foam with mechanical properties similar to those of bone; It was found that with the control of the superficial characteristics of the material obtained, a set of compatibility possibilities is available that simulate the appearance and properties of bone parts which can serve as requirements to manufacture surgical implants. Titanium is the best choice by weight, resistance and biological and electrochemical compatibility, but its elastic module is comparatively very high, with respect to bone. The amount of foaming, as well as compaction pressure, were the variables that most affected mechanical and electrochemical behavior
