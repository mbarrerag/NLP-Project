# Perception on the use of databases in training research in dentistry

There are currently several strategies to consult information on the net in real time on a myriad of topics, both issues of people's everyday life and fields of knowledge that are of interest to the academic community. The databases are constituted as efficient tools for the transmission of information and their access, in general, is simple and fast; however, sometimes students and teachers do not use them as often as they would like, a phenomenon of which the causes are largely unknown. The objective of the present work was to investigate the perceptions of teachers and students of a Faculty of Dentistry in Bogotá, regarding the use of databases in formative research, using the technique of Multiple Classification of Items ¿CMI-. It could be identified that for most of the participants it is necessary to implement pedagogical strategies that promote the use of these; additionally, the participants recognize the importance of the different actors in the teaching-learning process and the inclusion of databases in different academic and research activities
