# NANOTECNOLOGY FOR THE FUNCTIONING OF TEXTILE.

Cross-contamination infections are one of the main problems faced by health-care providers. One of the strategies to reduce the impact of the problem is to use antibacterial textiles in hospital clothing, although the current textiles manufactured for this purpose have shortcomings in the durability and efficacy of the antibacterial effect. To improve these characteristics, nanotechnology presents alternatives for the functionalization of textiles with antibacterial agents. In this research, the inclusion of nanofibres loaded with chitosan and with nanoparticles of silver as antibacterial agents to a standard cotton textile. The nanofibres of chitosan were manufactured with electrospinning technique in mixture with polycaprolactone. The nanoparticles of silver were synthesized from silver nitrate and ascorbic acid as a reductive agent in aqueous solution. The adhesion of nanofibres to textile was improved with microscoponic. The images obtained by the microscopia allow the visualisation of fibers of the PCL/quito mixture of the smooth form and the nanoparticles of silver
