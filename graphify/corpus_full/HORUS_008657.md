# BEARS WITHOUT AGUIJÓN (HYMENOPTERA: APIDAE: MELIPONINI) IN THE FACILITY, PEACE AND DIVERSITY OF THE CAUCA VALLEY

The Meliponini (Apidae) are eusocial insects commonly known as sting-free bee. These are widely distributed in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide. The cemeteries are sites of peace, rest and biodiversity, which provide a refuge and are a nesting resource for some of them, which contributes to maintaining the service of ecosystem pollination in different landscapes. The main objective of this research was to recognize the biodiversity of meliponini (Apidae) in different cemeteries of the Valle del Cauca by collecting specimens by jama and direct sampling. The specimens were preserved in alcohol at 70% for their subsequent identification using taxonomic keys. In a first stage, it was recognized at the level of gender, Nannotrigona spp., and Tetragonisca spp., and two species: Nannotrigona sadtella and Tetragoniscula in cemeteries of the Valle del Cauca. thanks: universidad nacional de colombia-sede palmira, prograde quipu: 2020par4014, grupo de investigación
