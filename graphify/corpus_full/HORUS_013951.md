# Identification of compositional and quality characteristics of sheep meat with emphasis on fatty acids and potential health benefits

The production of lamb meat in the country presents a serious number of advantages related to the richness of the niches of production, the management, the social conditions and the recent opening of markets where meat is being known and desired by different properties. This meat is mostly native to small production systems, where traditional knowledge, practices and production dynamics determine the quality of meat. In this sense, recognizing aspects such as the composition of meat, exploring possible benefits derived from the presence of fats, linked to the feeding systems based on pastures, the main characteristic of production in Colombia could be reflected in the final product, with these promising aspects in terms of the development of research areas with an important impact
