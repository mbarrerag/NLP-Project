# Model for the simulation of the production of handmade male footwear in Bogotá, Colombia.

The production of hand-made male footwear is a traditional industry in Bogotá, where there are numerous factories and workshops that offer hand-made products with a focus on quality and attention to detail. This industry is known for its variety of designs and styles, distinguished by its authenticity and the skill of artisans. This industry faces several challenges, such as the competition of mass production products, the scarcity of materials and the risks associated with a manual process that is often not standardized. Considering mainly the last challenge, this work is carried out that seeks to implement policies that optimize footwear production. From this work, it can be concluded that it is possible to optimize production by reducing waiting times, with the implementation of reinforced learning models trained from a simulation by described events
