# EFFECT OF THE CALCINIATION TEMPERATURE ON TEXTUAL AND MORPHOLOGICAL PROPERTIES OF DIATOMITAS

One of the most important characteristics of silica is the ability to combine its high porosity with its thermal and chemical stability, which makes it suitable for various catalysis applications. However, its use is limited due to the cost of materials and methods for its production. In nature, silicas are found as diatomite that present good porosity and specific area, which according to recent research can be used as inert supports in the preparation of catalysts. In this research, the effect of thermal treatment on the texture and morphological properties of a commercial diatomite from the municipality of Chivata (Boyacá, Colombia) was evaluated
