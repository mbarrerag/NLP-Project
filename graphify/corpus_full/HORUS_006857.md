# Sociospatial segregation in the urban periphery of Bogotá: A look from the health and education equipments 2004 – 2022: Case study: North-West piece (Suba and Engativá) and south-western piece (Kennedy, Bosa and Ciudad Bolívar).

Social and spatial segregation is a phenomenon present in the cities of Latin America that is mainly associated with spatial imbalances expressed in the living conditions of the population, including coverage and basic social services such as health and education. This work focused on analysing how formal mechanisms and planning decisions for access to education and health services are an element that reinforces socio-spatial segregation in the urban periphery of Bogotá. For the development of this study two phases were proposed. The first was the formulation of a Socio-spatial Segregation Index -ISSE - which allowed to identify segregated and fragmented territories from six categories of analysis with emphasis on education and health equipment in the city (nor-western part formed by the town of Suba and Engativá, and south-western part with the localities of Bosa, Kennedy and Ciudad Bolívar). The second phase consisted in the formulation of ISSE and the revision of the instruments allowed to identify that the peripheral territory of the city is segregated from central areas of the same.
