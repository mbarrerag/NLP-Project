# Factors associated with the prognosis of morbidity and mortality in paediatric patients with cerebral abscess.

The cerebral abscess is an uncommon entity in the pediatric population, but with a high morbimortality load. Advances in the diagnostic and treatment process have caused modifications in the course of the infection; it is important to know the initial clinical manifestations and the local epidemiology to make an early diagnosis and a timely treatment, seeking to improve the survival and avoid sequelae. To determine the clinical and paraclinic characteristics, as well as the factors associated with morbidity and mortality of paediatric patients with cerebral abscess diagnosis attended in HOMI, Fundación Hospital Paediatrica de la Misericordia, from 2013 to 2018. Observational study, with descriptive and analytical component. The clinical histories of patients between 0 and 18 years of age were reviewed with diagnosis of cerebral abscess attended in HUMI during a period of 5 years. The possible associations with respect to the sociodemographic characterization, risk factors and variables derived from the course of the disease were considered
