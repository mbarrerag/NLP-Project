# Impact of the interactive reading model in English, foreign language, on third-degree students at the Northern Cervantes Lyceum

The development of pedagogical strategies that engage the school population in classroom projects can facilitate the achievement of learning objectives in an L.E. as well as in English. This research is aimed at the implementation of a reading model called Interactive Reading Model (MIL), which is based on research that demonstrates the importance of interaction between the reader and the text for the understanding process. It is proposed a qualitative research of an ethnographic type with a group of 26 children (between 8 and 10 years) of third grade of a private institution located in the north of Bogotá. For the analysis of the data, three categories were created that emerged in part of the theoretical framework: reading strategies, reading at the literal level and inferential and affective dimension. The research began since 2021, under the guidance of the research professor, a pedagogical process oriented to the improvement of reading comprehension in English.
