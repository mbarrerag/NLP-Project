# PUTTING THEORY AND PRACTICE IN THE DRAFT ACCOMPANYMENT

Key words: constructivism, autonomy, communication, par, learning.The Global Development Plan (PGD) 2019-2021 in its policy 1 states that the National University of Colombia has as its superior purpose to train full, responsible and autonomous citizens (Universidad Nacional de Colombia, 2019, p. 36) with the ability to transform individual and collective realities, ethically and with social commitment, and promote critical thinking, research and creation, through pedagogical innovation, [...] from an academic, collaborative and transdisciplinary construction (p. 40).From this policy, as well as from the Strategic Axis 1 `Towards an organization focused on collaborative work,' the project `Accompanying for Autonomy and Collaborative Learning in the University that Learns that, through its two programs, the National School of Guardian Pares and the Autonomous Study Groups, seeks to materialize in practice the purposes of this PGD. This accompanying project is based on the constructivist theory of learning of Piaget (1970) and Reflexsky (1978).
