# Palimpsests of inhabiting: spatial formations in the Zenú Indigenous Reserve (San Andrés de Sotavento, Córdoba-Sucre)

This research seeks to understand the habitat in the Zenú Indigenous Reserve (San Andrés de Sotavento, Córdoba-Sucre), from two moments of analysis. The first is done based on the concept of spatial formation, which establishes a form of understanding of the processes of urbanization and housing, from a historical, geographical and situated perspective. The second moment addresses the study of the ways of inhabiting in relation to the cosmovision, the types of habitats and housing. The methodological approach is qualitative, the reality is assumed as a complex construction of the social subjects. The methodological strategies are two; on the one hand, the documentary research, understood as the search, recovery, analysis and interpretation of secondary data; as techniques of information collection, the documentary review and the revision of archives. The second strategy is the Founded Theory that allows to develop theories, concepts and hypotheses based on the primary data. The techniques of collection of information used are field observation and interview. The data obtained are processed and analyzed in Atlas.ti.
