# ASSESSMENT OF THE VIABILITY OF MICRO-ENCAPSULATED AND INCORPORATED PROBIOTIC MICRO-ORGANISMS IN A MADURED MARNIC MATRIX: PROCESS OF CAPSULATION AND FOLLOW-UP OF CRITICAL VARIABLES DURING MADURATION

ABSTRACT In this study, a matured meat product was developed, with microencapsulated probiotic microorganisms. Different formulations of wall material were evaluated and the one that gave the capsules less solubility was selected. B. animalis was microencapsulated by spraying, incorporated into a meat matrix and critical variables were monitored during the maturation process. As a result, a functional matured meat product with optimal concentrations of probiotic microorganisms was obtained
