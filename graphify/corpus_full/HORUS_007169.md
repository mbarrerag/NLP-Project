# Modeling of Florida limestone Intermediate geomaterials for foundation engineering applications

This study evaluates three constituent models: Hoek-Brown, Hoek-Brown with softening, and Hardening Soil model in intermediate geomaterials (IGM) derived from Florida limestone, with the aim of predicting its main behavioral characteristics. A laboratory database was compiled, including index tests and triaxial drain tests (CID-TXC) under containment ranging from 0.35 MPa and 20.7 MPa, supplemented by field-to-real-size load tests performed in the IGMs derived from Florida limestone. Finite element models, implemented in Plaxis 2D software, were used to extrapolate the parameters obtained from laboratory calibrations to surface-based applications. Laboratory-based parameters showed greater resistance and rigidity compared with those obtained from laboratory-to-field stress simulations, which reflected a lower capacity due to the high degree of fracture in Florida limestone and the low quality of the rocky solid.
