# COLONSCOPY: TECHNIQUE OF INSERTION AND WITHDRAWAL

This is a CONCISE AND EASY READING text written for beginners and experts who wish to perfect their technique of making colonoscopy. It explains in a comprehensive way, the different maneuvers that allow colonoscopy to advance through different segments and reach the blind easily. It has multiple photographs and well designed figures. It was evaluated and corrected by multiple experts from Latin America and the prologue is done by two world leaders in Colonoscopy. Dr. jerome waye of the university of new york and dr. fabian emura of jackson memorial hospital
