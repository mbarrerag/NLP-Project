# MACHINE-LEARNING TOOLS FOR THE MOBILE OF ELECTROCHEMICAL REACTORS IN THE TREATMENT OF WASTE WATERS

This study examines the use of machine-learning tools to monitor the performance of electrochemical reactors during wastewater treatment as an integral part of Sustainable Development Goal 6: Clean Water and Sanitation. The application of classification algorithms, such as support vector machines (SVM), was carried out to detect anomalies in the operation of an Electro-Fenton process. This process, belonging to advanced oxidation processes (PAOs), is characterized by having a high efficiency in oxidation and mineralization of a wide variety of pollutants, using a low consumption of reagents. The study is based on data collected during the treatment of an industrial textile waste water. The results obtained indicated that the SVM tool achieved an accuracy of 81% in the classification of test data, indicating a huge potential for application when it was implemented and supplemented with new data
