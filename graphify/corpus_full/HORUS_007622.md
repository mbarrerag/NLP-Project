# Phenomenological modeling of agitation and aeration of plant cell cultures in bioreactors using CFD

The cell cultures in bioreactors allow the production of high added value compounds (medicine, cosmetics or nutraceutical), under controlled conditions and independent of the problems associated with land use or climate change.The phenomena of agitation and aeration are necessary to distribute nutrients and oxygen in a medium of cultivation, but they can also generate hydrodynamic stress with different effects on cell growth, viability or the same production of secondary metabolites.This project seeks to model the agitation and the aeration of plant cell cultures in bioreactors using dynamic computational fluids (CFD).A mathematical model will be constructed and will be solved by CFD - ANSYS, seeking to better understand these processes and adjusting operating parameters that can manipulate the stress and improve the cellular viability.To achieve this, the Clibertia patinoi (borodó) cells of the biological system will be cultivated in the general model of agitation and aeration of the ADPA
