# Hydrocarbon pyrolysis of natural gas and biogas as an alternative decarbonisation in hydrogen production

The project aims to carry out an exergo-economic evaluation of a process of production of hydrogen and ammonia from the use of natural gas and biogas as raw material for the reaction of methane pyrolysis, which has as products hydrogen and carbon (black carbon). The latter can be separated from hydrogen and can be valued for further use in steel production, battery manufacturing, graphene synthesis, soil treatment, among others. The hydrogen obtained, for its part, can be used directly as fuel with zero carbon emissions or transformed into ammonia to facilitate its transport. To ensure decarbonization of the process, the energy to carry out the pyrolysis will be obtained from photovoltaic energy. To complete the study, the material, energy and exergy balances, process simulations in specialized software to determine the efficiency, and an economic analysis that serves as a source of information for further development and scaling of the process. Finally, to complete the exergo-economic analysis, will be carried out tests of natural gas and biogas pyrolysis to establish the hydrogen and black cell operation (a technology)
