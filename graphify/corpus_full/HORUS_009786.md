# Non-destructive model for estimating the individual FOLIAR AREA by Alometros in Gulupa (PASSIFLORA EDULIS FO. EDULIS)

The gulupa is one of the fruits that has increased its exports from Colombia in the last 15 years, due to its organoleptic characteristics. Knowing the foliar area (AF) in this crop is important; however, there are no practical methods known that allow its calculation. The aim was to develop non-destructive models to estimate the individual AF, by means of allometric parameters in leaves of gulupa. The AF was determined in 100 sheets, using two standard methods: the laser portable meter CI-202 and the software Easy Leaf Area (ELA). The linear models with the highest determination coefficient (R2) were measured, the base width in the lobes (a) and the width between the terminal points of the lobes (b) and the total length (Lt) was calculated, as the sum of L+LD+LI.
