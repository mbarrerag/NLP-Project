# SCIENTIFIC COMPETITIONS IN SAFETY AND HEALTH SPECIALIZATION COURSES AT WORK - VIRTUAL MODALITY

This article visualized the use of a didactic sequence for the teaching of research through the design of undergraduate projects, from the regional context of students and companies where they work (students from all regions of Colombia), developed the works through synchronous training meetings with their tutoring tutor as a series of activities for the understanding of the methodology of research; based on the experience of six (6) courses of 30 professionals from various disciplines, who build their undergraduate project. To develop the systematization of experience: the recordings of the meetings were counted, the effective results of supporting the undergraduate works, the publication in journals of dissemination of a group of students such as interviews of the effectiveness of the method and recording of results of students with supporting approval records. At this moment, the new courses that will allow to be observed, for the comparison of academic periods 2021-2 and 2022-2 2, is a case study with a critical socio-center that will use information collection instruments as comparative analysis
