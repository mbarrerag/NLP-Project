# MANAGEMENT AND PLANNING TOOLS IN THE BUILDING INDUSTRY

The construction industry is an important player in the global economy with $10 trillion spent on goods and services each year, employing approximately 7% of the world’s working-age population. Iteration is an inherent aspect of any design project, which occurs in undefined cycles until the process is completed. If these iterations are not anticipated and properly planned, reprocessing and redesign can occur at random, resulting in an overload of time and costs. This document identifies and categorizes the tools used for execution design, in order to establish parameters such as: sequence of activities, relationship of dependence between activities and final duration. A systematic review of literature was carried out through the search for databases and profiles of leading researchers in this field, to identify the articles of journals that addressed the issue of management and planning tools in the execution design over the past 20 years. The results provide a basis for the proper interpretation and evaluation of approaches, methodologies and tools that can impact on improved execution design and lead to better project performance
