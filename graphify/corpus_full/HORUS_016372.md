# Behavior of the Guadua angustifolia Kunth roll during curving and its influence on compression parallel to fibers.

This study studies the behavior of the Guadua angustifolia Kunth when it is curved for later use as a structural element, in order to contribute to the engineering solution that can meet the demand for this type of architectural works. In this one, the behavior during and after its bending, were analyzed, the limitations were identified when curving a stretch of rolling bamboo, its affection in the resistance to compression parallel to the fibers (RCPF) and from the perspective of its internal structure, a comparative analysis was made regarding the percentage of fibers. This study contributes to the development of the state of art on curved bamboo elements and its mechanical behavior. Future research is necessary for the standardization of curving methodologies, also its production under quality standards where resistance and softened forms are guaranteed to provide solution to the architectural and ingenierile projects that require them
