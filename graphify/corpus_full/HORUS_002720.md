# LABORAL PRECHARIZATION OF INNOVATION, THE CASE OF RAPPI IN COLOMBIA

The revolution in technology and telecommunications has allowed innovation in business models through the use of platforms, particularly home platforms, which have generated an algorithmic management ecosystem that in many cases subjects workers to conditions of control and precariousness of their activities. Rappi’s case is an example of the use of innovation for the precariousization of workers even with the very denial of their worker character, but even in contexts such as the Colombian one with a precarious work structure and a rent class. This work analyzes the Rappi case in Colombia through the use of interviews and surveys carried out between 2021 and 2024
