# ALTERNATIVE MARKETING OF PIÑA MD2 IN THE VALLE OF THE CAUCA

As a result of the project of royalties Increase of the sustainable competitiveness in the agriculture of the hillside in the whole department of Valle del Cauca, Occidente, in which participated the National University of Colombia, Palmira headquarters, the Univer- sidad del Valle and the International Center of Tropical Agriculture (ciat), in this booklet are presented two alternatives of industrialization of pineapple variety md2: fresh and processed pineapple. It is expected that the information contained here will serve as a guide to producers, traders and processors to add value to the product and reduce the perdi- das postharves of the pineapple, by processing into minimally processed fruit or snacks
