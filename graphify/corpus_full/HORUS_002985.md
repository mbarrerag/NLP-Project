# Biopolitical analysis of the policy “Comprehensive development of the Early Childhood from Zero to Always”: The case of Riohacha and Leticia. Period 2016-2023

To analyze the implementation of the policy “integrated development of the first childhood from Cero to Always”, in the period 2016 to 2023 in the territories Leticia and Riohacha. This from a biopolitical theoretical approach, to study the policy as a biopolitical instrument. A qualitative study with an interpretative research approach, using the narrative method of public health policy: Narrative Policy Framework(NPF). The narrative was used as a methodological tool to organize and analyze the information, starting from five theoretical categories derived from the theoretical-normative framework of law 1804 of 2016 in order to know its role in implementation: 1. Crianza Guidelines, 2. Identity Construction, 3. Ser Construction, 4. Environment and hierarchies, 5. Expression of ideas and feelings. Likewise, the dialogues were used as a tool for the construction of the integrated or final Metatext, and the systematization of the information obtained with the NPF method to process the information, was realized in the software Nvivo 12. In the analysis of narratives and the integrated Metatext, a limited knowledge of rights, gender inequality and promotion of traditional roles, dynamic, together with the support of the traditional relations, was carried out in the decision.
