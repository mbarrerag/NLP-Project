# LABORATORY GUIDE OF SOLID MECHANICAL

The book "Lab Lab Guides for Solid Mechanics" describes the laboratory practices currently taught in the Mechanical Course of Solids of Civil Engineering and Agricultural Engineering at the National University of Colombia at its headquarters in Bogotá. This book seeks to contribute to the understanding of basic concepts through experimentation
