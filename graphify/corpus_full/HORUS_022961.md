# The new body of the nation: the Office of National Longitudes and Maps, 1902-1931

During the first decades of the twentieth century, the treaties of international land limits were signed and executed, which shaped the current Colombian political map. This thesis is asked for the role of national maps in the construction of a territorial image of the Colombian State between 1902 and 1931. First, a balance of the cartographic panorama of the research period is presented and it is found that the main actor, but by no means the only one, was the Office of Longitudes. After exploring its history, methodology and work, three of its maps are analyzed comparatively and the ways in which these and other maps circulated in the media, such as the press and cinema. Along the the thesis it is argued that in the modernization of the Colombian cartography led by the Office of Longitudes it was not possible to produce a single stable and iconic image of the national territory, but the scientific and diplomatic bases were established for the construction of a geographical and physical representation of the nation.
