# Narrative review of the management of fibromyalgia with traditional Chinese medicine and acupuncture.

Fibromyalgia is a chronic multi-symptomatic disease characterized by the presence of chronic pain with a widespread musculoskeletal predominance associated with somatic and psychological symptoms. It is considered that it can affect between 2 and 4% of the general population and is nine times more likely to develop in women. The World Health Organization has encouraged the use of Traditional Chinese Medicine, considered as a complex medical system within which different techniques such as phytotherapy, electroacupuncture, suction, moxibustion, acupuncture, among others. For decades, multiple studies have been conducted that have demonstrated the effectiveness for the relief of acute and chronic pain, achieving its recognition in the United States, Canada, and Europe. Traditional Chinese Medicine considers that fibromyalgia is related to the alteration in the Qi movement, which generates a stagnation of this energy that can evolve towards blood stasis thus generating pain. This review notes that Traditional Chinese Medicine can become more effective than conventional medicine in reducing intensity and areas of pain, and particularly in improving the quality of life.
