# CIRCULAR ECONOMY: REVISION AND BIBLE STUDIES

The purpose of this document is to present the result of a bibliometric study on the circular economy in order to promote dissemination and documentation on a topic that has acquired a prominent position in the context of stakeholders in organizations and their administration. The text is divided into three parts. The first section summarizes some generalities of the topic related to the problematic area at the global, regional and national levels, and shows some background research in the topic. The second part contains the basic reference to introduce the topic and to promote the approach to the results of the review. Part three presents the findings of bibliometric exploration based on, among other aspects, thematic areas, authors and institutions, funders and authors more cited
