# Ways to inhabit the public space by women. Case study: neighborhood Babylon, Usaquén locality. Bogotá.

The following research work arises from the interest of considering how women inhabit and appropriate public space in the Babylon neighborhood of the city of Bogotá. Starting from the categorization and characterization of these modes and ways of inhabiting and appropriating public space. Given the above, the research question is condensed in How women inhabit and how could they appropriate much more public space in the Babylon neighborhood of Bogotá? The general objective of the research was aimed at proposing actions to improve and appropriate public space with a gender focus based on the ways of inhabiting women between 18 and 65 years old, the aim is to implement the actions of the Babylon neighborhood of Usaquén and with the above, the specific objectives will be developed in the following way: Specific objective No. 1: Categorizing the ways in which women between 18 and 65 years old live the public space of the Babylon neighborhood. Specific objective No. 2: Characterizing the forms of appropriation of public space by women. Specific objective No.3: Formulating with women actions of improvement based on appropriation, use and enjoyment of public space (in this way, the research project is to be based on the women)
