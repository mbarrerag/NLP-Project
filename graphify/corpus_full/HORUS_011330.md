# 4.20. EXTRACTS OF PLANTS WITH AGENCY ACTIVITY POTENTIAL RESEARCH AS A THERAPEUTIC STRATEGY AGAINST ALZEIMER’S DISEASE

The extracellular accumulation of Aß and intracellular hyperphosphoridated TAU and calcium dyshomeostasis are the main histopathological characteristics of Alzheimer's disease (EA), which are associated with neurodegeneration.New therapeutic strategies for the AE have been studied, including the activation of type II nuclear receptors (LXR), which generate improvement in the performance in the Morris Aquatic Labyrinth (LAM), with decrease in histopathological markers and biochemical changes related to synapsis in a murine model of EA (3xTg-AD).OBJECTIVES: In this work, the effect of two plant extracts with agonist activity LXR, on cellular, molecular, transcriptional and behavioral changes in AE models were evaluated. MATERMS AND METHODS: Primary cultures of hypocampal neurons; intracellular registers of Morris's Laberinto Acuatic; western blotting; immunofluorescence; RNAseq RESULATED: Pre-treatment with Nectandrantural extracts (NC)
