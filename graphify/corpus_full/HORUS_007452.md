# “Petronio Álvarez” Pacific Music Festival, 1997-2017. Chirimia

The Festival de Música del Pacífico Petronio Álvarez, created in 1997 in Cali, has consolidated as a key space for the visibility of Afro-Colombian musical traditions. Initially, it emerged to promote the region’s sound diversity, but over time, its competitive structure generated tensions between traditional practices and the requirements of the competition, especially in the Chirimía Modality. On the one hand, the festival has promoted artistic careers and national recognition; on the other, it has forced adaptations in instrumental and gender conforming to award criteria. To analyze this phenomenon, this study examines documents of the historical archive (1997-2017), revealing that, despite its role in cultural preservation, the rules of the competition have influenced the transformation of authentic expressions. .
