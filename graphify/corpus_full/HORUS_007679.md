# Renewable Energy Options for Vegetable Production

The need for energy for the air conditioning of buildings is a subject that directly affects both their generation and distribution, as well as the carbon footprint produced in the process, and the products. Greenhouses are structures implemented for agricultural production with the control of climatic variables, irrigation and nutrition, and for this purpose energy is required. In this context, it is intended to implement several renewable energy options for use in the air conditioning of vegetables under greenhouse: solar photovoltaic energy, low-intalpy geothermal energy through a Canadian well, and biomass through a biodigester in a greenhouse at the Campus of Bogotá. The project is expected to contribute to a replicable model in areas where electricity supply is unstable, insufficient or non-existent, and thus contribute to rural development
