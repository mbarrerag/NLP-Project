# DIMENSIONS FOR DOCUMENTING VIOLENCES AGAINST WOMEN IN LATIN AMERICAN COUNTRIES

Chapter 8 is the fourth contribution of the book. In its pages, it presents an overview of the legal and legislative framework of Latin America around the violence experienced by the diversity of women, taking into account that this is a structural issue revealed by data, figures and narratives. The text highlights three fundamental elements for an understanding of these developments as they have incorporated aspects of ideological, conceptual and legal order. In this sense, in addition to the information on regulations in Latin American countries, the author claims the need to recognize the weight of social inequalities and power relations between women and men as a result of the dominant patriarchal logic that permeates the areas of daily life and social structures, but also reflected in legal developments. This contribution is part of a book that calls for rethinking numerical representation through analysis of figures and non-numeric representation and active listening to accounts about the phases of capture, registration and reporting of the acts of violence and gender violence. It is a call to reveal the substratums based on ideologies, conceptions, prejudices and practices that are socially installed by means of the population, and by means of the social control of various subjects of violence and violence
