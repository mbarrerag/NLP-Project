# MICRO-CAPSULATION OF ARAZÁ (EUGENIA STIPITATA) BY SPRAY-DRYING AS A SOURCE OF BIOACTIVE COMPOUNDS FOR FOOD INDUSTRY.

In this research, we managed to design solids enriched in bioactive compounds from arazá, fruit of high production in the department of Caquetá. This fruit has a high potential to be incorporated into new formulas of functional food prototypes. It is necessary to investigate the phytochemical composition, bioaccessibility and bioactivity of these novel ingredients of arazá
