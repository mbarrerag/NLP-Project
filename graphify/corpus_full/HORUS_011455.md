# STRUCTURAL DETERMINANTS OF PHYSICAL CULTURE

Therefore, this study, developed in the perspective of the line of sociology research of sport, has a special interest in making a first approach to the standardization of a definition capable of encompassing the concept of physical culture. It is expected that in upcoming research it will be possible to give an account of the magnitude and importance of this discipline, profession, culture, scope and concept within the Colombian political framework. This exercise also generates contributions to the line of research of the Universidad Santo Tomás called Sociology of Sport, since its main interest is to provide new knowledge that allows the generation and strengthening of knowledge networks around the topic, the conceptual foundation of the line in sociology of sport, the identification of problems, contexts and methodologies of intervention. Based on the previous elements and the recognition that in different countries physical culture can take different dimensions and concepts, its public policies and legislation are re-established to identify visions, conceptions and approaches of physical culture, as well as its relationship with theoretical referents and the vision of actors
