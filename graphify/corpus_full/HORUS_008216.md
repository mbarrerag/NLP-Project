# THE TERRITORIAL STRUCTURE OF COLOMBIA between 1843 and 1928, IN THE HORIZONTE OF THEIR DEMOGRAPHIC BEHAVIOR

Many studies of planetary cultures show that, in them, knowing the state and dynamics of a group of people grouped in a geographical area over time is an imperative for territorial administration, resources, culture, power. This activity in human groups, which finds in migrations its most natural metaphor, is defined as a population movement. One of the ways of analyzing this behavior is through demography and its multiple tools, such as those used here to show Colombia’s territorial configuration at the end of the 19th century and the beginning of the 20th century.
