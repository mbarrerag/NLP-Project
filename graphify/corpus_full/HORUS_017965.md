# SPACE VARIATION OF THE VEGETATIVE GROWTH OF CAULERPA SCALPELLIFORMIS (BRYOPSIDALES, CHLOROPHYTA) IN THE PENINSULA OF GUAYIRA, CARIBBEAN COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

The first reported taxon Caulerpa scappelelliformis (Bryopsidales, Chlorophylla) in the Colombian Caribbean. The algae was observed in four sampling sites (Santa Rosa, Musichi, Riohacha centro and Riohacha peripheria) in the rocky intertidal of the peninsula of La Guajira in March 2021. The length of the erect fronds of the algae was measured in three of the four sampling sites (Santa Rosa, Riohacha centro and Riohacha peripheria).The populations of Santa Rosa and Riohacha centro had an average of length of the fronda of 6,07 (n = 239) and 6,23 cm (n = 175), respectively, with maximum length of 19 cm in both sites. In Riohacha peripheria, the average was 12,09 cm (n = 321) with maximum impact of 35,1 cm, being this last population statistically different from the others.
