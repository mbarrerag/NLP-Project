# Strengthening teaching and research activities in the molecular process laboratory based on the provision of inputs and materials

The molecular process laboratory is a laboratory that serves mainly teaching and research. It is a laboratory for the training of undergraduate and graduate students, experiments associated with special academic practices (PAS), TDG and graduate thesis are carried out, however, some academic practices are carried out with the support of inputs and materials from completed research projects. In this space, micropipettes are basic input in practical work and in current conditions, they are not in sufficient quantities to allow all students to carry out their internships in the subjects. That is why, with this proposal, we want to strengthen the supply of micropipettes and micropoints so that students have sufficient inputs to carry out their academic activities and learn the necessary skills required in their professional work
