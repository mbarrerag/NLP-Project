# EXTRACTION AND QUANTIFICATION OF ANTOCIANINS FOR OENOCARPUS MAPORA EXTRACTS OBTAINED BY EXTRACTION WITH PRESURIZED LIQUID

Oenocarpus mapora (millpesillos) is a palm (Arecaceae) whose fruits are widely used in areas of the Colombian Pacific as food for its high nutritional value and is consumed in the form of desserts and juices. Mature fruits of O. mapora are sources of bioactive compounds such as anthocyanins and phenols in general. In the search for optimal conditions for the extraction of anthocyanins from mature fruits of O. mapora by dynamic pressurized liquid extraction (PLE), the effect of the solvent type (tolentol/water mixtures), concentration of citric acid (0 and 0.3%) and temperature (60 and 80 °C) using a factorial design was evaluated. The extracts obtained were also evaluated by extraction yield, total phenol content and the content of monomeric anthocyanins were evaluated as being the acidic acidic acidic, the result indicates that extraction yield increases considerably (48,5±5%).
