# SYNTHESIS AND FE-TIO2 CHARACTERIZATION TO BE USED FOR ILMENITA PROVIDING FROM MINING RESIDUES OF THE BAGE - ANTIOCIA

The ilmenite is a mineral constituted by iron oxide and titanium, FeTiO3. Moreover, it is the natural source of titanium and titanium dioxide (TiO2) most abundant on the earth [1]. The heterogeneous photocatalysis with TiO2 has been extensively investigated, as it represents a very attractive alternative for the decontamination of water effluents, given the low selectivity of this semiconductor and its innocuity. However, a disadvantage of this, corresponds to activation only with UV radiation. To activate the TiO2 in the visible range, it is necessary to include in its structure some element that reduces the prohibited band energy[2]. One of these elements corresponds to iron and therefore the ilmenita is an attractive starting material to obtain an active TiO2 in the visible. Some authors have reported the use of this mineral as starting material for obtaining materials of type Fe-TiO2 photocatalytic purposes where the obtaining and successful use of these [3], [4].
