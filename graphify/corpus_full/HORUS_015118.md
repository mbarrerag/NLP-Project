# OPHIOTHRIC SYNOECINA

Ophiothrix synoecin was classified by Borrero-Pérez et al. (2002) as Insufficient Data, due to the few population studies that existed. Subsequent research has confirmed that the species is restricted to the southern western Caribbean, mainly in northern Colombia, and with only one record in Venezuela. Studies in the Colombian Caribbean show a decrease of 52.4% in the frequency of occurrence of the species, without known causes. O. synoecine presents a forced association with the perforations carried out on the rocky coast by the Echinometra lucunter urchin (Linnaeus, 1758), which contributes to its restricted distribution; moreover, for the hedgehog the association is not obligatory. It is estimated that the area of occupation of the species is less than 330 km2, which corresponds to the extent of the rocky coastline of the Colombian Caribbean coast, considering that much of that coast does not count with the presence of E. lucunter lucunter.
