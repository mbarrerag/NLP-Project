# DEMOCRATIC STATE OF LAW - SOCIAL JUSTICE OR CONSTITUTIONAL RETORIC?

The chasm between being and duty remains a breeding ground conducive to the establishment of abusive constitutionalism, where it is necessary to shorten the realization of rights, to re-evaluate (value) the long-standing republican achievement inherent in the social and democratic state of law and to demonstrate the dangers that they pose to the constitutional control system, hence the importance of establishing an adequate level of alert and prevention against the eventual implosion of the constitution in the hands of those who claim to be supporters of constitutionalism as an appropriate and effective way to overcome the political, social and economic injustice that continues to prevail in the country
