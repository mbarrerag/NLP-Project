# A REVISION OF GENDER FANNIA ROBINEAU-DESVOIDY, 1830 IN THE NEOTROPIC

Fannia is a genus of flies found on all continents, except Antarctica, and has medical, veterinary and forensic relevance. In this review, search strategies were carried out to identify the current knowledge of Fannia in the Neotropics and DNA sequences publicly available to the genus worldwide. Searches in the databases showed 103 publications supporting a total of 113 species registered for the Neotropics. Although progress is evident in the description of the morphology of some species in the Neotropics and their presence in the countries of the region, much remains to be learned about their biology, genetics and ecology. More research is needed to improve understanding of this genus, which is fundamental to its application in the forensic, medical, veterinary and health context
