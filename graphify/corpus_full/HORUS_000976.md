# REFLECTIONS IN THE RE-PRESENTATION OF CONTEMPORARY ARCHITECTURE

To what extent have the current hegemony of the image and the representation replaced the palpable object of the architecture? A plane, a photograph or a model can be considered architecture in themselves? These are concerns that I want to raise, that more than truths seek to formulate questions, concerns that lead to reflection, even to doubt, because the doubt more than the truth is manager, motor that propitiates knowledge. I apologize to the reader for writing in first person. It is the first thing that we teach in architecture schools, especially to students of end of career or graduate of architecture when they carry out their thesis, when we propose to write them in a neutral, impartial language, because the written must look like the product of a rigorous, aseptic, almost scientific investigation, without any contamination of opinion; or when we write articles for magazines or books, wanting to make it look as if, who writes, is concerned about an objective person who does not issue judgments of value but objective findings, someone at the margin of his own personality who observes with a certain distance, without feelings, nor partiality, the object of research, which is paradoxically selected by the taste, he is always selected, he writes by the personal
