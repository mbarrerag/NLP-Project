# MANAGEMENT OF NON-RATIONALITY BY NUDE-CITIZING IN PROJECT SOCIETY I

The Project Methodology has been impacted by the intense digital transformation of the last decade, made possible not only by the evolution of microcomputers, intranets, networks and decentralized computing, but by the maturity of digital corporate spaces based on robust digital platforms. In this article, the previous phenomena are related; the product of performance mechanisms with linguistic, social and political characteristics that are impacted by constant transformations and redefinitions adjusted to a non-rationality. This is a Concective that modifies ends and modifies means according to external pressures; with the structures of projects that reflect a social imaginary or that respond to the demands socially accepted or the imposition derived from administrative fashions that make organizations and quality assurance systems emerge that help to generate management modes that are more related to non-rationality that adjust the media to permanent changes of the environment and resolve this through decision-making knots that invoke standardized mechanisms that package Lego-type decision-like routines
