# COPPER COMPLEX (II) COVALENTLY BOUND ON THE SURFACE OF TIO2 NANOTUBES AS A CATALYST IN PHOTOOXIDATION REACTIONS

In this work, a complex of Cu (II) with the ligand 4,4'-dicarboxy-2,2'-biquinoline was obtained, which was covalently bound on the surface of TiO2 nanotubes (photoactive support). Its activity in the photo-oxidation reaction of benzyl alcohol was evaluated using a 360nm lamp in the presence of O2, and variation in the mass of the catalyst. The monitoring of the reaction was carried out by gas chromatography, where it was shown that the amount of the catalyst mass influences the amount of produced mmoles of benzaldehyde obtained
