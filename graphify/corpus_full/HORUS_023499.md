# EFFECT OF DISTILATION CONDITIONS ON THE PERFORMANCE AND CHEMICAL COMPOSITION OF ESSENTIAL OILS PRODUCTED IN COLOMBIA

Key words: essential oil, steam trawl, optimization, quality. Essential oils (AE) are industrial raw materials used in food, cosmetics, cleaning products and agricultural products, among others. Their productive performance and quality depend on the agroecological conditions in which plants develop, their post-harvest treatment and variables of the distillation process, among others (1.2). By developing empirical mathematical models, the best operating conditions were established in the steam-trail distillation (AV), the AE of the thyme, rosemary, oregano of the mountain, citronel and palmarose species, which allowed the highest possible productivity according to international technical standards. The best extraction conditions of the species oregano of the mountain and palmarose, showed that the lower pressures (=50 psi) and intermediate densities (200-250 kg/m3) were achieved to obtain the highest amount of AE with high content of carvacrol and geraniol, respectively.
