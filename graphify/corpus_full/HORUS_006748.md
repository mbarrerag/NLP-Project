# Model for the detection of errors associated with the liquidation of the energy bill in Antioquia

Public service companies have the responsibility to ensure accurate billing of their services to customers. In particular, the billing of electrical energy involves various factors, such as regulations, data volume, amount of associated variables and temporary restrictions for review, which complicates the process. Companies often do not identify in a timely manner the errors of billing, generating claims and unsatisfied customers. The purpose of this study was to apply statistical and machine learning models to detect early errors in the process of electrical billing and improve customer service. Logistic regression showed a sensitivity of 94.95 %, while the ́classification trees reached a accuracy of 75.36 %. The implementation of these models can improve the billing process, identifying errors more efficiently.
