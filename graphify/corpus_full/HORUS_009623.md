# URBAN DRENAGE IN COLOMBIA

In this case, the design of the urban environment is not the most appropriate way of achieving the objective of the urban environment.In the last two centuries, it has imposed major changes to the landscape and a great challenge to the engineers, planners and computers of the territory to respond adequately to the needs of the communities, but trying not to affect the ecosystems.The pressures on the landscape have caused great changes in the hydrological cycles as increases in the volumes of runoff due to the increase of the impervious areas and changes in the materials used in the urban areas.In response to these changes, the engineering has had to adapt the traditional hydrological concepts to use in favor of the design of infrastructures more appropriate to the new needs.In this regard there is a great amount of information in the international literature, but one of the main changes that are imposed is the strict knowledge of the local conditions, so that the designed infrastructure is adapted to those characteristics rather than to the design of the foreign knowledge
