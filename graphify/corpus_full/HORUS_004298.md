# TUPARRO INSTECTS DEVORADING MURCHIES: NEW REVELATIONS ON THEIR DISTRIBUTION

The study of the new species of bats from the subfamily Phyllostominae, Glyphonycteris sylvestris and Micronycteris schmidtorum is reported on in the following areas: the new records involve the El Tuparro Biosphere Reserve located in the department of Vichada (Colombia). The detailed study of the specimens deposited in the consulted biodiversity libraries is highlighted
