# REFRIGERATION OF SORRY SEMEN IN A SUPPLEMENTED DILUENT WITH CARVACROL

The addition of antioxidants in pig semen diluents has become an alternative to mitigate the reduction of sperm quality during cooling. The aim of this research was to evaluate the capacity of carvacrol (a natural antioxidant) for the conservation of refrigerated pig semen. Fifteen ejaculates from five pigs (Sus scrofa domestica) were diluted in BTS medium supplemented with 0 (control), 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 μM carvacrol. The samples were maintained at 16°C for five days. Every 24 hours the mobility and kinetic spermatics were evaluated with a CASA IVOS system, and the functional integrity of the membrane by the hypoosmotic test (HOST).
