# Editorial

For many years, one of the main criticisms of the academic production of law was the lack of commitment of legal research to social changes. This was because legal writings focused mainly on the development of manuals aimed at deepening the dogmatic knowledge of the legal norm, these texts reduced to proposing better ways of interpreting and applying the law to legal operators and litigants. Legal investigations did not approach social reality and distance themselves from changes in economic, political or cultural structures
