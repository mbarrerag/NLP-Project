# The years of Marco Ospina (1912-1983)

The years of Marco Ospina (1912-1983) are inscribed as a reconstructive work of the life and work of the artist, Marco Ospina. This reconstruction explores from a particular life a section of the Colombian twentieth century, being pertinent this look to understand these years in the history not only of art, but also of Colombian social and political. The key questions that guided this reconstructive task relate to exploring the conditions of possibility and the tools with which the teacher of Bogota developed his work as an artist. Also was the reflection on the social existence of the artist taking the Ospina trajectory as an empirical case. .
