# Between Pan-Americanism and Mexican ‘reality’: analysis of some articles by José Luis Cuevas in the late 1950s

There it is possible to locate the way in which the artist conceives his work and his trajectory, at the national and international level. We propose a reading of his trajectory in relation to the economic and political development of Mexico during those years: Cuevas finds the way to adapt conveniently at the intersections between the artistic pan-Americanism of José Gómez Sicre, the revolutionary rhetoric in decline and the pro-American policies of the PRI governments
