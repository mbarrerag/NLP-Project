# Forming a 3D virtual archaeological museum

This proposal is framed in the complicated duality that characterizes the archaeological material, since, although it is a valuable source of information, it has a limited and fragile nature. Having this in mind, it is increasingly important to promote the use of digital technologies that allow establishing channels of approach and dissemination of the material that do not put at risk their physical integrity, so this project proposes to form a virtual museum of 3D models of the 335 complete pieces of the collection of the Archaeology-Ceramotec Laboratory of the Department of Anthropology of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá, so that it can promote research and scientific archaeological dissemination, hand in hand with the appropriation and conservation of the Colombian heritage. The aforementioned digital alternatives are all the more relevant in the specific context of the Laboratory of Archaeology-Ceramoteca since in this space is gathered a representative collection that covers much of the national territory, we believe that it is essential to increase the visibility of the archaeological material present in the University, both external and internal way, so it is proposed to complement the creation of the virtual museum with the creation of a presence of the 3D models in this space. The models allow us to characterize the material as if it were being done in a face-to-face way, the measurements and details are the same at all angles, so that the need to manipulate or compromise the physical integrity of the artifacts is reduced. For all the above reasons, the project would be supporting the National University of Colombia in promoting access and dissemination of scientific collections under custody, with applicable quality and normative parameters, thus fulfilling its missionary aims of studying and enriching cultural heritage, in order to finally contribute to its conservation and dissemination (Article 2 of Decree 2120 of 2018)
