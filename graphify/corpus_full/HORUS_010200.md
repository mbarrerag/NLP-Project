# VIOLENCE MULTIPLES IN LATIN AMERICA AND THE GENDER CARIBBEAN, DISIDENCE AND ALTERNATIVE

In recent decades, the socio-eco-logic crisis and the global climate emergency have become increasingly evident, as has the growing conviction that the current relationship between capital and nature and society is unsustainable, violent and increasingly devastated, and that it is becoming increasingly de-grass-ening, together with the social and environmental foundations of our stocks and their prospects for the future. In South America, the impacts of this crisis are so closely linked to the predominance of extractivism, the basis of economic policy and development of our countries, that it imposes a hegemonic vision of a development model, at war with nature, and against all peoples/communities that have woven a more harmonious relationship with nature
