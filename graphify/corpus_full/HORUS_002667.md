# INTELLECTUAL HISTORY OF LOVE TO INCIDES OF THE XIXth CENTURY AND ANALYSIS OF MANUELA SAÉNZ AS A FEMINITY DISDENT IN TIMES OF INDEPENDENCE

Benito Jerome Feijoo, author of the extensive work "Critical universal theatre", in volume VII, discourse XV, "Does the causes of love define that feeling as an affection, which is the first motive of all human actions, prince of all passions, monarch, whose vast empire, does not recognize on earth some limits: machine with which they are stirred, and disrupt entire kingdoms, idol, which in all religions has worshipers: in short, astro fatal, whose influence hangs the fortune of all, since according to its various aspects (I mean according to his view to different objects) some make eternally happy, others eternally unhappy. This author, foundational of the illustrated movement in Spain, one of the most translated in Europe and of the most appropriate in the Viceroyalty of the New Kingdom of Granada, classifies love in pure appetite, pure intellectual love and pathetic love. In this presentation we will make an approach to the postulates of Feijoo on love and apply them, with an intersecting perspective, to the analysis of affective and illicit relations of the neo-Grandina elites, in this century, in this context
