# IMPROVING AGRICULTURAL VALUE CHAINS FROM SUSTAINABLE USE OF THEIR WASTE IN BIORREFINERIES.

Colombia is a country that bases its economy mainly on activities related to the agricultural and livestock sector. These activities have common problems of instability of purchase prices, high intermediation in the marketing with margins that usually do not reach the primary producer, limited technical assistance services, poorly developed agro-industrial, poor infrastructure, inadequate management of waste and by-products, lack of standardization of harvest and post-harvest processes, poor soil and water management, among others.These problems cause that often the agricultural producer prefers in much of the cases to lose its harvests or to leave with surpluses that become to the detriment of its economy and living conditions.To this difficult panorama has been added the increase in the costs of energy, the difficulties and deficits of inputs imported due to logistic difficulties resulting from the pandemic and war in Ukraine that generated deficit of fertilizers in the world (by controls to Russia as one of the main suppliers of agricultural products and the integral territory) they affect further the value chains that include the production of food.
