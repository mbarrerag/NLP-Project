# Risk factors and protection for the ideation and attempted suicide in adolescents and young Latin Americans: systematic review.

Abstract Suicide behavior is a public health problem, the literature is broad in setting risk and protection factors associated with suicide attempt. Systematic reviews are required to evaluate the methodological quality of primary studies and thus guide decision-making in relation to the design of interventions from the risk approach. The objective of this systematic review was to identify risk and protection factors associated with the ideation and attempted suicide in adolescents and young people in Latin American studies and to evaluate methodological quality. The databases CUIDEN, LILACS, Pubmed, Scielo, Science Direct, SCOPUS, EBSCO and Medline were consulted, restricting the results to articles in the last 10 years. 3,642 documents were obtained, of which 19 studies were included. 52.63% (n=10) of the studies obtained an average level of internal validity and 57.89% (n=11) average level of overall quality.
