# Efficacy of osteochondral allografts in the treatment of structural joint defects of the knee in patients with osteoarthritis. Protocol of systematic review of the literature.

To know the effectiveness of the use of osteocondrale allografts in the treatment of structural joint defects in terms of clinical-radiological outcomes, functional, allograft survival and complications. Systematic review of the literature. Search was carried out with the terms MeSH (allograft osteochondral transplantation) AND (knee) AND (knee arthroscopy) in the databases PubMed, Embase, Cochrane, Clinical Trial and PROSPERO, in English published in the last 10 years. The reading of 267 titles and summaries was carried out, 57 articles were reviewed in full text and 10 studies were selected definitively that met the inclusion and exclusion criteria. Clinical and functional results of 906 patients treated with osteocondral allograft were described in the last 10 years
