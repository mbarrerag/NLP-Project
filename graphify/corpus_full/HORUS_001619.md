# Building regularized and dimensionally-reduced representations for automatically quantitative conceptual similarities between images: an application to cancer description

The spaces of alternative representation (ARS by their acronym in English) have become a crucial topic in artificial intelligence (IA), since recent advances in analysis of characteristics through deep learning have shown that small changes within such spaces of characteristics greatly affect the results of the model. These ARSs are commonly based on characteristics extracted from samples, mainly to reduce the dimension of the original sample space and improve the discriminationability of patterns. However, most of the current research of AI focuses on the performance of the model, but there is very little progress in the interpretation of the model. Particularly in the field of analysis of images, the dimension of the sample has become more important than interpretability, due to the size of these sources of information and the limitations of the computational power.
