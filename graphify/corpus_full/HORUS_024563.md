# COLOMBIA IN MIRA. PETER ALDOOR AND THE GRAPHIC ANTICOMMUNISM

This book deals with the graphic work in Colombia of the Hungarian mechanical engineer and cartoonist Péter Áldor, provides biographical information, illustrates his artistic life before his arrival in the country and familiarizes readers with his technique and drawing themes. Likewise, it shows the active role he played in the strategy of the newspaper El Tiempo to express his anticommunism much more through the graphic than by the written text, since his wit, talent and professionalism were excellent virtues used in favor of contributing to the expansion of an anti-communist consciousness among Colombians. Thanks to the cartoons of Áldor, the reader will perceive the presence in Colombia of the intensity of the Cold War in the world and the hot war in Colombia, and how from his strokes emerges a profound ethical proposal to save humanity through Christianity
