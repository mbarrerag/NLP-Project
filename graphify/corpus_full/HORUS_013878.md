# Review and update of the book A country working for banks

This research update the research results book A country working for banks (Villabona, 2015). It explores the high concentration in the Colombian banking system and its impact on competition, profitability, and socio-economic equity. Through the analysis of the financial statements of the ten main banks in the period 2000-2022, it seeks to understand how this oligopolistic structure affects the gross margin of interest and the ability of institutions to maintain their stability in the face of financial crises. In addition, it addresses the lag in the democratization of credit in Colombia, contrasting the apparent disinterest of regulators and banking institutions in promoting it, with the constitutional mandate that should govern its action. The research seeks to clarify how lack of competition and limited access to credit contribute to inequality and to a reduced economic development. The study, which adopts a quantitative approach, tries to contribute to the knowledge of the banking sector and to understand whether the current system perpetuates or aggravates socioeconomic inequalities in the country, with a view to proposing possible avenues of improvement
