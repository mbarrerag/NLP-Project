# Metabolic evaluation of the root-floor cocoa system (Theobroma cacao L.) with a view to determining possible relationships between the latter and cadmium fractions in rhizospheric soils in cacao areas of Santander

Cadmium (Cd) is a heavy metal present in soils. It does not have a biological function at human level, nor ecosystem. Cocoa cultivation in Colombia is experiencing difficulties due to the presence of this heavy metal in its soils, which has an impact on cadmium content in the final products and therefore on human health. Various researches have been reported that have addressed the previous problem, however, little is known about the metabolic dynamics of the root-soil cocoa system, especially in those systems that present different behaviors in the fractions and contents of cadmium in it. This is why this project proposes to evaluate the metabolic dynamics of the root-soil cocoa system (Theobroma cacao L.), with a view to determining possible relationships between this and the fractions and contents of cadmium in the rhizospheric soils, from cocoa areas in the Department of Santander. For this purpose, the characteristics of the physical and chemical plants, fractions and content of cadmium in the soils, as well as the content of cadmium in the respective plant tissues. Likewise, the metabolic and enzymatic activity of the respective soils will be evaluated
