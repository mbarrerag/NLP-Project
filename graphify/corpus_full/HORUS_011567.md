# Methodological proposal for planning and control for construction projects from a computer supplement

The main objective of this thesis is to disseminate a methodological proposal of planning and control for construction projects based on a computer complement that allows to identify early problems and make the necessary adjustments or corrections to maintain the project within the planned time frame. Initially, a systematic review of literature on systems, methods and planning and control tools used for time management in construction projects, its inclusion in computer tools and software of greater use, is then made a correlation of background with the findings in the documents analyzed for the development of criteria for the operativity of both the methodological complement and the software architecture. Subsequently, the application and use of the complement to give conclusions and recommendations is documented. Finally, the importance of knowing that theories, methods and tools are used to plan and control the construction projects, the combination of their variables and how they are immersed in the development of computer tools.
