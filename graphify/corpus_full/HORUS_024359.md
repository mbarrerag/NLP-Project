# Design and implementation of a virtual pedagogical process for learning Newton's laws in tenth grade students.

This paper presents a report about the follow-up process carried out to a group of 18 students of tenth grade of the Colegio Juan Rey IED (located in Bogotá), regarding the work developed by them on some concepts related to Newton's laws. Where problems in the compression of concepts prior and related to Newton's laws were identified in the students, based on the own teaching experience and in what García & Dell ́Oro (2000) points out that the Aristotelian ideas constitute difficulties in the students during the construction of concepts associated with Newton's laws. Then, to facilitate the teaching and learning processes regarding this topic, a didactic sequence was planned, taking into account that their implementation should be mediated by the use of virtual resources due to the state of pandemic in which we find ourselves. At the end of the implementation of the undergraduate work with the students, a closing test was made to carry out the study regarding the progress that each participant recorded about some concepts determined by the teacher and served as previous and related concepts to the topic of work. The theory of significant learning was used, since the students's knowledge of the students, and with this plan planning the new concepts was used.
