# USE OF PUBLIC SPACE FOR MOBILITY AS A DENAMIZER OF SOCIAL CAPITAL

The paper develops a conceptual and empirical inquiry that proposes a two-way relationship between social capital in urban spaces and the choices of people about the use and non-use of public space for mobility, taking the case of the City of Bogotá. The exercise proposes a conceptual approach to develop an analysis methodology, based on urban transport and mobility projects in Bogotá, based on theoretical reflections on the notion of social capital expressed in urban public spaces, contrasting them empirically through a review in the form of Stylized Facts, based on information from the Bogotá Mobility Survey 2019, against different examples of urban transformations in Bogotá. From this approach, there is evidence of an influence of pre-existing social capital on the use of public space for mobility, as well as on the potential change of social capital from new urban projects that affect possible mobility decisions. It is found as a result of this conceptual and empirical approach, the bidirectional influence between social capital and the choices of public space use for urban mobility, as an opportunity and a challenge to integrate them into urban projects
