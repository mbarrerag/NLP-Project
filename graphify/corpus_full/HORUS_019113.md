# Clinical and economic links associated with joint shoulder replacement in the contributory regime in Colombia.

Introduction This study aims to estimate the post-operative mortality rate at 30 days and the total costs of the medical episode, as well as to determine the prognostic factors associated with these outcomes in adult patients who have undergone shoulder arthroplasty in the contributory health system of Colombia. Method Retrospective cohort of administrative data of adult patients enrolled in the contributory health system of Colombia who underwent shoulder arthroplasty between the years 2012 and 2017. The outcomes evaluated were mortality rate, admission to ICU and requirement for transfusion during the first 30 days, mortality rate, fractures, infection and revisions at 1 year and the total cost of the medical episode incurred by the third payer during the 90 days following the procedure. Also, complications rates within the first 30 days of the associated health system were 0.36 for the results presented. Results The study included 2,471 patients, the age group between 65 and 79 years represented 55 % of the total patient, being more frequent in the female sex
