# Phytochemical characterization of a distilled oil of Cannabis iodized and pharmacological evaluation in epilepsy models in rodents.

This research wants to recognize mainly whether the anticonvulsant effect of the distilled oil of Cannabis iodized in the convulsive crisis tonic-clonic, myoclonic, and low threshold was comparable to the recognised patterns for each crisis. And determine whether the possible protective effect is analogous, superior or inferior to the isolated cannabidiol (CBD). The above because epilepsy has a high impact on the public health of the world, especially in developing countries, and that the traditional treatment lines for this pathology bring with it unintended effects for the patient that lead to a abandonment of the therapy; therefore it is necessary to increase the efforts in the investigation of therapeutic alternatives that prioritize the effectiveness and safety in the control of the convulsive crises, as can be the derivatives of Cannabis. The research was carried out through the experimental models on the model Mutilus, for the crisis induced by maximum electrochoque (MES), myoclonic induced by pentiltrazole (PTZ) and for the results of each model of electrocholes, showed the frequency, the no evaluated substance affected the motor performance of individuals, that distilled oil and CBD have an index of protection for the seizures evaluated and that the greater protection attributed in the low threshold model for distilled oil is correlated with the presence of annex molecules such as additional isoprenoid-type compounds and cannabinoids that would mediate a greater response through the entourage effect and its multiple biochemical targets related to epilepsy
