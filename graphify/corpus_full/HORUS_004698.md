# THE COLOMBIAN INSULAR CARIBBEAN BETWEEN WORLD WARS. THE DISPLACE OF STATE AND TERRITORIAL SECURITY IN THE ARCHIPIELAGE OF SAN ANDRES, PROVIDENCE AND SAINT CATALINA, 1941-1946

This paper analyzes the situation that was experienced in the archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina in the context of the world wars, in parallel with the process of nationalization implemented by the Colombian State on these island territories of the Colombian Caribbean. Since 1912 the Colombian government led by Carlos E,. Restrepo, created the National Intendency of San Andrés and Providencia, with this aim, sought to secure the Archipelago and avoid what happened with the Department of Panama in 1903, all this, with the purpose of strengthening the weak exercise of sovereignty that threatened with a new territorial dismemberment, therefore adopted speeches actions aimed at strengthening territorial security, whose first step was to adopt the political administrative model of the National Intendency. In the midst of this effort to secure and nationalize the island occurred the world wars, generating a great alert, because the United States began a process of militarization of the Caribbean to safeguard its interests in this area.
