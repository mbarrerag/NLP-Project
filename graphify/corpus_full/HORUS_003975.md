# RESEARCH OF POLIURETANUM RIGIDE SUPUM RESUMED WASTE (PUR) AS A ASSISTANCE TO SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES (SDGs): ASSESSMENT OF TWO ALTERNATIVES APPROACHED TO REINCORPOSE RESIDERING PUR RESULT TO A CONSUMPTION CYCLE RESPONSIBLE

The work covers the chemical, physical and mineralogical characterization of the rigid polyurethane foam (PUR), by means of multiple tests such as X-ray fluorescence, X-ray spectroscopy and scanning microscopy, in addition to the analysis of its performance in two alternatives of utilization, one mechanical and another thermal. These procedures were carried out in order to evaluate options of handling of PUR different from the sanitary fills, which contribute to the fulfilment of the objective of production and consumption responsible for the 2030 agenda, because it seeks the re-incorporation of the residue to a productive cycle, either by means of its use in the elaboration of construction materials or the obtaining of volatile compounds of added value. For the case of mechanical treatment, 4 different percentages of sand were replaced by PUR for the manufacture of lighted mortars, with the purpose of evaluating the mechanical properties of compression and flexion, as well as the properties of absorption of water, density and porosity, with respect to a standard mortar without addition of PUR. Among the results were obtained a good cement/PUR matrix, good relation of water/cement and positive mechanical properties.
