# -Perimeters of durability and mechanical behavior of the Compressed Land Block (BTC) in Colombia for the execution of reinforced masonry houses.

This research, developed from a quantitative approach of experimental and descriptive scope, suggests parameters of durability and mechanical behavior in compressed earth masonry with addition of cement as stabilizer (BTC) that present vertical perforations and are intended to form part of reinforced or partially reinforced masonry construction systems. These parameters are defined from the selection and execution of tests on samples from different sources, with particular stabilization conditions (mixture design)
