# PEDOGENETIC CYCLES IN A STRATIGRAPHIC PROFILE AT NATURAL NATIONAL PARK LOS NEVADOS, COLOMBIA.

The micromorphological elements of a stratigraphic profile nearby Laguna del Otún, at Natural National Park los Nevados, allowed us to define various pedogenetic cycles, or k-cycles (Butler, 1959), conformed by unstable phases of aggradation, and/or erosion, alternated with metastable periods of soil development. Through the integration of actual records of the soil forming factors during the last 10000 years (i.e, Thouret & Van der Hammen, 1981; Kuhry et al., 1983; Thouret et al., 1995) and the establishment of stratigraphic correlations by mineralogical suits and relative position, a framework for interpretation of pedofeatures was constructed. Redox patterns and overlapping of pedofeatures evidenced polygenesis controlled by the accumulation of ash fall deposits from Nevado del Ruiz and Cerro Bravo volcanoes; the hydrological properties of such materials leaded translocation of Fe. Erosion by water and wind are derived from climates more humid and colder, respectively. The physicochemical analysis indicated a moderated degree of soil development, mainly through andolization, cumulization and, organic matter and Fe translocation. Interplay of changes in volcanic activity and climate, gave rise to a composed soil profile with three buried soils, mostly andic, with paleoaquic conditions and a placic horizon
