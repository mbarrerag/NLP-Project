# Analysis of the expression of genes associated with defense in the oil palm model -thielaviopsis paradox (of seynes) höhn.

The disease of the rotor of the arrow in the palm of oil, is caused by the pathogen Thielaviopsis paradoxa. The hybrid OxG provides a valuable genetic resource to select the resistant germplasm and establish the genes associated with resistance to this pathogen. In this study, the hybrid OxG with code 517 was identified as a resistant genotype that limited the colonization of T. paradoxa, while another hybrid OxG with code 485 showed high susceptibility. To understand the transcriptional response of the hybrid to the infection by T. paradoxa, these two materials of the hybrid OxG were inoculated with this pathogen and its transcriptional profile was compared by means of sequence of RNA at six points of time (0, 24, 48, 72, 96 and 120 hpi).
