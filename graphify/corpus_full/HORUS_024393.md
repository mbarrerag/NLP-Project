# GEOCHEMICAL, MINERALOLOGICAL AND GEOPHISICAL ASPECTS FOR IDENTIFICATION AND CHARACTERIZATION OF KING. CASE OF STUDY: ALCALINO COMPLEX OF BEAR LODGE, WYOMING, UNITED STATES.

The rare earth elements (REE) are 17 chemical elements that include 15 of the Lanthanides group plus the Itrius and Scandium (Dutta, T. et al., 2016). These elements have a wide variety of industrial uses ranging from magnets, medical instruments, ceramics, glass, batteries and catalysts, to their use for the manufacture of supercomputers, weapons and defense instruments of the world powers (Humphries, M., 2011). The production and global consumption of REE have experienced a significant increase since the middle of the 19th century where economic, political and social factors make supply can be exceeded by demand (Zhou, B., et al., 2017). Additionally, more than 80% of the reserves of REE are concentrated in China leading to this lack of geographical diversity being affected by the global economy (Dushyantha, N. et al., 2020 and Eggert, R. et al., 2016)., samples of trenches and active sediments (Verplanck, P.L. et al., 2020; and Olinger, D.A. 2019). Additionally, different geophysical exploration programs have been carried out by the USGS since 1979 for the exploration of uranium deposits and through the NURE program (National Uranium Resource Evaluation), magnetometry and spectrography data of gamma rays (at different scales) were collected from all over the United States. This work aims to integrate USGS data (geochemistry and gamma-ray spectrography) with laboratory analysis of samples collected at the CBL in 2019 by UNAL-SEG. and thus to identify mineralizing events that affected the CBL, determine its geochemical characteristics and promote the identification of SRE prospects in the CBL and other places in the world
