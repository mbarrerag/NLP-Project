# Management by competencies and work performance of administrative staff of the Alfonso Jaramillo Salazar Regional Hospital of Lebanon, Tolima

The present research work aimed to determine the work performance and level of competence of the administrative staff of the “Hospital Regional Alfonso Jaramillo Salazar del Libún, Tolima, as established in the manual of processes and procedures established by the Administrative Department for the Public Service DAFP, and the internal rules established by the hospital institution. The research process carried out had an interpretative character by contrasting the hypothesis and its non-experimental design since the study variables were not submitted to an experiment; its study population was formed by 54 public servants of the administrative area assigned to the Regional Hospital of Lebanon Tolima. as an instrument, a questionnaire of the ordinary scale of Likert was used. With the objective proposed for this research, it is concluded that the performance evaluation represents a strategic tool for the administrative activity since it allows to determine the performance and commitment of the employees regarding the organizational objectives to be achieved . administrative activity since it allows to determine the performance and commitment of the employees with respect to the organizational objectives to be achieved
