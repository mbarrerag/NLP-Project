# SUSTAINABLE ENTERPRISES GENERAN RIQUEZA

The level of happiness of the inhabitants of a country depends on the quality of life they can generally enjoy, which in turn depends on the quantity and quality of companies based on their soil, since with them is generated the economic dynamics that enable positive social development. On that basis, the general social welfare depends on the national entrepreneurial capacity, materialized in the number of companies started per year and the percentage that of them remain operating, generating wealth. Believing in that principle, and after presenting statistics of percentages of closings of new companies in different countries including Colombia, this work aims to strengthen the entrepreneurial capacity in two aspects: first, to contribute to the birth of more and strong companies, and second, to minimize the closure of those operating, for what it proposes to Entrepreneurs and Investors to manage their creation and operational administration using the 16 elements set forth in the Model to strengthen entrepreneurial capacity.
