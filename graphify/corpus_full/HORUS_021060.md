# Strengthening of communication skills in Spanish and language of students belonging to the Arhuaco people of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta

The research project presented below focuses its attention on the communicative competences of students of the community Ikü de Gunnarinchikwa, Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta (Pueblo Bello, Cesar) with a view to strengthening their skills in orality, reading and writing. This project is part of the inter-institutional dialogue (University - Indigenous Cabildo) and following the call of the educational authorities of the Resguardo, who identified that the result of the differentiated competences in Spanish in their students were phenomena such as school failure and desertion; in addition, they consider that improving the competences in the languages spoken in the community constitutes a powerful tool for the development of the different cultural aspects of the people as a whole, in the measure that allows them to assume functions of defense of territorial and political autonomy. At the linguistic level, in the schools of the Resguardo can be found only the two fundamental differences, since the development of bilingualism is only bilingual and cultural in the context to which the members of the community are exposed.
