# Air gassing of oil palm leaves and stem and determination of its potential for power generation

The main objective of this research is to characterize the gasification of leaves and stems obtained as residual biomass in oil palm cultivation using air as a gasifying agent. A scanning type experimentation is developed and a downward flow free fall reactor is used. At 800°C and equivalence ratio (ER) of 0.24, the results show for the leaves a yield of 1.85 Nm3 gas/kg dry biomass and a calorific value of 4.08 MJ/Nm3. For the stem, 1.30 Nm3 dry biomass/kg and lower calorific power of 4.56 MJ/Nm3. The gas obtained has an electrical power generation potential of 1.05 kW-h/kg dry biomass for leaves and 0.90 kW-h/kg dry biomass for stem. In conclusion, the energy use of residual biomass (leaves and stems) should be explored in Colombia to economically and environmentally optimize the processes of palm oil production in a circular economy scheme
