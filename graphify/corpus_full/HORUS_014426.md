# Evaluation of fault stability with kinematic control in rocky slopes by joint probability.

In the analysis of kinematic stability in rock slopes there are several proposals that have been refined, is the case of analyses with statistical and probabilistic approach that allow the study of the uncertainty and variability of parameters inherent to stability as are the orientation of discontinuities and resistance parameters. The implementation of methodologies that present graphically the probability of failure in rock slopes has not been widely developed. In this thesis is presented a methodology for the calculation of the probability of failure with analysis of the variability of discontinuities orientation and angle of friction in a probabilistic way for different mechanisms of failure in rock slopes; with the application of the functions of probability density of variables is projected the calculation of the probability of failure by means of the joint probability and graphical representation in 2D in tools normally used in the study of kinematic stability as is the stereographic network for different mechanisms of failure. Then the kinetic analysis of reliability is presented as a complement to the methodology with which a treatment through probability of specific variables and the construction of 3D graphs or volumes of probability for each PachD is developed in this mechanism of failure
