# STOCHASTIC MODELLING OF EPIDEMIC DYNAMICS WITH RANDOM PERTURBATIONS FOR ACUTE RESPIRATORY DISEASES

The impact of air pollution on human health has become a topic of research due to its effect on the respiratory system. In this talk, we present a stochastic model for Acute respiratory infection(ARI) driven by random perturbations of particulate matter PM2.5 and PM10 on the air quality due to climate change in Bogotá. For the random perturbations in the transmission rate due to air quality, we propose a spatial interpolation methodology to analyze the particulate matter information from the air quality index(AQI) in Bogotá. We focused on the parameter estimation and data analyses of acute respiratory diseases for the actual data. The model parameters are estimated using the updated data estimation method and validate the proposed model
