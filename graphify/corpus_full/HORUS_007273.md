# Synthetic peptides quality control and assurance

Due to potential biological activity, synthetic peptides are considered a very good alternative in the ongoing search for novel active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and the development of innovative pharmaceutical products. As antimicrobial peptide-based products are produced on a large scale to meet growing global demand, it is essential to maintain high-quality standards during production. The purpose of this chapter is to address the importance of these molecules in reference to safety, effectiveness, and quality, and provide an overview to identify the critical points of the process and their control parameters, determine the quality control characteristics to measure, and outline a long-term quality assurance plan, including the risk assessment approach within the framework of the pharmaceutical quality system. The concept of pharmaceutical quality is approached from a holistic perspective throughout the entire life cycle of a pharmaceutical product, emphasizing the integration of quality from the initial stages of development using quality by design, involving a collaborative commitment with stakeholders, effective knowledge management, and ensuring a comprehensive framework through quality control, quality assurance, quality management, and quality management maturity, with a focus on safeguarding public health, guaranteeing compliance with the highest standards of safety, efficacy, and quality as main attributes of pharmaceutical products. The constant improvement is based on the accomplishment of pharmaceutical regulatory requirements. Following the corresponding GxP and recognizing opportunities for constant improvement at each process stage are important for the maturity of the quality management system. Each one of these components represents a critical aspect and stage in the maintenance and improvement of pharmaceutical quality
