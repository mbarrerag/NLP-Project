# Critical position of students in the face of texts read in the third grade of the Educational Institution Cupiagua of the municipality of Aguazul Casanare

To read is to enter a magical world of imagination and beauty of the written word (Ricoeur, 1999). It is a meanful experience of characteristics, facts and real or fictional events that invite us to travel through places full of adventure and fascination. It is a process that allows us to understand what results us. In the present research we take a look at our national and institutional context to address the implementation of learning, the educational practices in the educational institution and the role of the teacher as a mediator of them. We reflect on the review, position, and criticism of the innovation of the mimesis, phenomenological and hermeneutic, but at the same time individual, as a former who takes distance, position, and criticism s appropriation, when shaping a text as a result of his distancing as a critical reader.
