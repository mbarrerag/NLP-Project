# Six decades of the national universe biological dept.: a law of excellence and acaemic commitment

On 16 December 1965, the National University (UNAL) created the Department of Biology as one of the most important educational projects in the country in the biological sciences. The Department marked the beginning of a new academic structure at the university and established the foundations of the discipline it maintains as a legacy and a national reference to date. Making historical memory, since the creation of the Department of Biology, it is part of the Faculty of Sciences according to agreement 61 of the Higher University Council. Later, in 1971, the Higher University Council approved Agreement 16 (17 February) granted autonomy to the Institute of Natural Sciences of the Department of Biology
