# Brief overview of some artificial intelligence techniques

This article presents a brief overview of the current techniques being used in the implementation of intelligent artificial systems. The fundamental principles are mentioned and some examples are given in order to achieve a better approach to these topics. The ultimate intention is to expose how these new techniques of information processing are transforming research, teaching and application of engineering developments. In addition, an introduction is sought to upcoming articles in the area, related to intelligent control, pattern recognition, intelligent tutorial tools, etc
