# Quantum roughness as sensitivity to diffusion noise: Quantifying non-classicality of multi-mode systems

The quantumness of a physical system is context-sensitive, contingent on the particular quantum behavior selected for quantification. One such measure, roughness, recently introduced to assess nonclassicality, gauges the waviness of the Wigner function of a quantum state. This work explores some properties of roughness, generalizes its definition to include multimode systems, and presents a new geometric interpretation of this measure of nonclassicality. Furthermore, the roughness of several families of multimode states is reported, shedding light on their nonclassical features
