# Spatial Entanglement of Geometric States of Ordinary Space in Non-paraxial Inteference

An exact and exhaustive phenomenology of interference in ordinary space describes it in terms of confinement in geometric states of space. Under high prepared non-locality, such space states exhibit spatial entanglement, a behavior not reported in the standard interference formalism of optics and quantum mechanics. In this paper, the fundamental theoretical features of the spatial entanglement of geometric states of space are discussed and illustrated with numerical simulations of non-paraxial Young interference
