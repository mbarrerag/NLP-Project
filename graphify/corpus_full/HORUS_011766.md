# CHAPTER 1: A LOOK AT THE DOCTING TRAINING IN COLOMBIA

Aware that teacher training is one of the fundamental tasks in order to achieve the goal of improving the quality of education in the country, and based on the principle that the transformations in pedagogical practices must be one of the central issues in order to achieve this quality, this chapter emphasizes the historical development of teacher training in Colombia, in order to arrive at the description of the System of Teacher Training, initial, continuous or in service and advanced. This chapter shows that the Training System is particularly the teaching career, in terms of its regulation, the scenario that underpins the professional and personal projection of educators; this is borne out by the fact that in recent decades Colombia has experienced important advances in the level of training of its teachers
