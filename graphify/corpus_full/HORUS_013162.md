# Seismic threat analysis of the city of Tumaco using CAPRA and OPENQUAKE

In this work, the analysis of seismic threat of the city of Tumaco is carried out using a probabilistic approach and a comparative exercise is carried out with a deterministic analysis. The above, making use of programs with important recognition and generalized use at the global level for the evaluation of different types of threat and risk. The programs in question correspond to OpenQuake, of the Global Earthquake Model foundation and the programs that make up the CAPRA platform.
