# ARMED COLOMBIA FORCES IN PEACE MISSIONS: PERSPECTIVES AND OPPORTUNITIES FOR ARMED FORCES IN THE CONTEXT OF THE POSACUERED

The participation of the Colombian armed forces in peacekeeping operations is an instrument of the country's foreign policy that requires coordination between defence measures and military strategy, and therefore requires the structuring of objectives, purposes and means by the armed forces and their senior civil and political leaders, so that they can carry out actions outside sovereign borders. This requires the coordination of the ministries responsible for defence policy and foreign relations and means of implementation and knowledge of institutional arrangements for mission logistics, in particular in two aspects: military organization and diplomacy. The article aims to determine the opportunities for the Colombian military forces to participate in United Nations peacekeeping operations to formulate lines of action, after the signing of the peace agreement in 2016
