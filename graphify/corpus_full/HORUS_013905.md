# Exploring Blockchain's Potential: An Innovative Solution to Fight Financial Fraud and Identity Subsistence in State Subsidies in Colombia

This project seeks to study the potential of blockchain technology application in a context of public finances, especially in monetary transfer programs in the framework of social development plans. In particular, the advantages and weaknesses of the implementation of this technology will be evaluated to minimize the possibility of fraud or identity supplantation when accessing these programs by the population. This project is part of the lines of work of the seedbed, especially with the Investment Alternatives and Fintech
