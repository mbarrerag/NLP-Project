# Film Creation at School: The aesthetic experience of cinema as a strategy to think about the Colombian armed conflict

In 2021 the project "Festival Miravalle de Cortometrajes" was launched in collaboration with middle school students at the Miravalle College IED. Over three years from its inception, this project has undergone a solid consolidation in the institution thanks to the efforts of several teachers. During the period of an academic quarter, students embark on an investigation into the Colombian armed conflict, study aesthetics of cinema, develop literary and technical scripts, produce their own short films, edit them and finally present them at the Miravalle Festival of Cortometraciones. This research focuses on exploring the aesthetic experience that students experience during the process of creating their short films. Also, in the viability of projects with a pedagogy approach by projects with a commitment to transdisciplinary in a formative disciplinary context. Finally, the research seeks to identify the degree of success when incorporating cinematographic production as an integral part of the school environment. .
