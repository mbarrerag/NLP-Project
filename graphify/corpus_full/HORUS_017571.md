# Evaluation of the maturity level of IT alignment in the state sector in Colombia.

The strategic alignment of IT seeks to know that so close (or far) are the strategy and business plans of the strategy and IT plans. To measure the size of this gap, there are different models that aim to know the factors and dynamics of an adequate alignment that contributes to the improvement of the indicators of the business. Given the particularities of the Colombian State (multisectorial), as well as the orientation to the practice that should have the IT studies according to the latest studies, the model of evaluation of the maturity level of the IT alignment that is considered as the one that allows to know the dynamics and important factors in the alignment of state entities, is the model of Jerry Luftman, which consists of measuring 39 attributes grouped in 6 criteria, whose results for 23 entities evaluated are: Communications (3.27), Measurement of the Competence and Value of the IT obtained (3.22), Governance (3.56), Association (3.77), Scope & Architecture of IT (3.41) and Skills.
