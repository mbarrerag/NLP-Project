# Conceptual model of subsidence associated with hydrocarbon extraction in a Colombian non-conventional deposit

The subsidence of the soil may have different causes, some of natural type and others of anthropic type. One of the main causes of anthropic type, corresponds to the extraction of subsurface fluids, either water or hydrocarbons. This subsidence process has been analyzed by calculating the deformations in the different strata and the corresponding deformation in the surface of the ground in a certain time. Deformations in each stratum are caused by variations in the piezometric levels and by the corresponding changes in the effective efforts, which generate a process of consolidation of the materials, governed by the conditions of compressibility of these.
