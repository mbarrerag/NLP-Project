# Contemporary Kriol Challenges and Opportunities in the Archipelago of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina, Colombia

The Kriol of San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina has been the subject of a deep discussion, because it enjoys vitality in the ethnic root community of the islands and has been displayed as a symbol of identity by a prominent part of its speakers, but also because there have been trends of linguistic movement towards Spanish, a dominant language in domains such as administration and commerce. This article seeks to complement the discussion by presenting some academic reflections, research efforts and work with the community, which have been sustained by its authors from three fundamental fields of action: linguistic education, sociolinguistics and linguistic documentation. It is concluded that, although state support in language policy matters has been insufficient, the same members of the community have led and participated in actions to keep their language alive and have made significant advances on this front, although the way is still long to go
