# Experimental study of pyrolysis of biosolids in fluidized bed

This project proposes an experimental study of biosolid pyrolysis in a fluidised bed reactor in order to determine the mass and energy balances of the process and the influence of temperature on them. To carry out this objective, the biosolids and their carbonised temperature will be characterized before and after pyrolysis. Biosolids and carbonized will also be subjected to fluid dynamic characterization together with the bed material (sand) in order to confirm its proper behaviour in the reactor. A first batch of carbonised biosolids will be installed in a horizontal fixed bed reactor, which must be put into place to carry out experiments. These carbonizeds will be used to estimate the physical and fluid dynamic properties of the carbonized bed reactor, information necessary for the design of the gases in fluidized bed. Once the carbonised gases are obtained, and possible defects in the fluidisation and the bed material will be characterized as their fluidistic behaviour in a cold model of the fluidized bed reactor. The cold model allows to determine the minimum fluidisation rate of gases, and possible defects in the fluidisation. the research group on Biomass and Thermal Process Optimization _x0096_ BIOT. The group currently develops two macro projects related to the study of thermal processing of biosolids, specifically in the drying, pyrolysis and hydrothermal carbonization of biosolids. The project is carried out with the support of the Energy Technology Division of Chalmers Technological University in Sweden. Specifically, Professor Diana Carolina Guio, who is linked to this Division, will provide direct advice to the project activities throughout their duration
