# ANALYSIS OF COLOMBIAN STANDARDS FOR TERRESTERAL TRANSIT REGULATION AND VIAL SECURITY, 1990-2017

This article analyzes Colombian regulations related to traffic regulation and road safety, for the period 1990-2017. For this purpose, a documentary investigation was carried out for the observation period that, later, were ordered in a matrix containing the document's subject, year of publication and issuer. The content analysis technique was used identifying promotional or corrective policies and their active and passive safety elements. The results show that 65% of the standards issued during the period are corrective in nature, integrating greater elements of active safety. The promotional regulation is less frequent, regularly oriented to policies of development, administration and regulation of behavior. The construction standard of norms to respond to road safety is aimed at reducing road accident as a last end, leaving aside the understanding of road safety as a dynamic and complex phenomenon
