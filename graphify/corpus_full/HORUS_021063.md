# Research in dialogue: Territories in victim status. Contributions for comprehensive reparation and non-repetition for the case of two habitats associated with rivers in the departments of Antioquia and Chocó.

The concept of Territories as victims is formulated, in the Victims Law-Law 1448 of 2011- where the reparation to individuals, collectives and entire territories is recognized.In the framework of these considerations established in the Law, it is necessary to make effective the processes of management and social appropriation of knowledge, starting from the production, socialization and application of it as contribution to the construction of peace, integral reparation and non-repetition, processes in which the National University of Colombia as knowledge manager has challenges in light of the recent Havana Peace Agreement signed in 2016, which marks a milestone because it puts an end to one of the most vulnerable armed conflicts, as well as the victims of this conflict.The research aims to explore the character of a territory as a victim, in order to contribute to the options of integral reparation and non-repetition in two habitats associated with rivers in the departments of Antioquia and Chocó, taking into account some of the most vulnerable social sectors, as well as the victims of this conflict.The rivers have been part of the strategic location of the conflict that has resulted in other actions that have deteriorated their structural function, vital, symbolic and sacred, revealing, and taking into account the measures still exposed to the threats and the local circumstances. This research hopes to contribute to the inter-institutional strengthening, through the presence of the University in the Peace Networks, incentive for research in undergraduate and graduate students and where the studies of habitat, derived from the Habitat-Cehap School Group Faculty of Architecture (A-Minciencias category) in its lines of research, in particular the Environmental Relations in Habitat and the Population and Communication Studies Group of Habitat as a contribution to the clarification of truth, comprehensive reparation and non-repetition in the territories as victims
