# Validity and reliability of the English version of the Healthy Aging Brain-Care Monitor (HABC-M) instrument to detect post-intensive care syndrome

The intensive post-care syndrome (PICS) is a phenomenon of great impact and social relevance in critical patients. Generally, its detection is complex due to the great variety of affected components (cognitive, functional and psychological), in addition to the use of various scales of measurement and the absence of a specific diagnostic test. It is important to mention that, currently, the habc-M represents a clinical tool with high potential to detect the PICS in patients survivors to the Intensive Care Unit (UCI). to validate the Spanish version of the instrument Healthy Aging Brain Care-Monitor (HABC-M) as a clinical tool for the detection of the PICS. Design of the study: a quantitative instrumental approach, through which the psychometric properties of a scale of measurement in health was evaluated. The translation of the habc-M was carried out by means of a transcultural adaptation (AT).
