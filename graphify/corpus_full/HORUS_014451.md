# Analysis of superstructures. Case study: Optimizing agro-industrial waste as precursor of chemical products, an essential element for the energy transition.

This study presents an optimization tool based on superstructure analysis for the design of chemical processes and decision-making in the implementation of polygeneration schemes in Colombia. The optimization program developed in Matlab, considers the parameters of different thermochemical processes and technologies for the transformation of three possible biomasses into nine high value-added products in the national market. To obtain the necessary parameters, detailed simulation of the processes was used in AspenPlus®, which allowed to reduce the number of levels of the superstructure and also decrease the computational cost of the optimization process in Matlab. The results highlighted in this study include the generation of an optimization tool, the identification of biochar as the most viable product for the implementation of polygeneration schemes in Colombia and the identification of possible technologies for the transformation of biomass. Finally, this work shows that superstructure analysis is a useful tool for decision-making, which may be useful for government entities and potential investors in the definition of polygeneration schemes
