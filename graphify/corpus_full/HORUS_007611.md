# Female and Domestic Women in Bogotá Press: Construction of Gender Roles and Social Participation 1878-1907

The second half of the Colombian nineteenth century was dominated by entrenched patriarchal structures, where women were often relegated to domestic roles and had limited access to education and the public sphere. The literary press addressed to women constitutes a space of significant relevance in a social and cultural context marked by patriarchal norms. During this period, publications emerged that sought to communicate and connect with the female public, offering cultural and literary contents specifically designed for them. This study aims to analyze carefully the evolution of the representation and participation of women in the press of Bogotá during the period between 1878 and 1907, in which the magazines _x0093_Colombia will be examined: Revista de las Damas_x0094_, _x0093_Women: Readings for Families_x0094_ de Soledad Acosta de Samper and _x0093_La estudio_x_x0094_ administered by Ismael José Romero. At the first moment, the work seeks to identify and understand the social and cultural constraints faced by women in their participation in the period of study.
