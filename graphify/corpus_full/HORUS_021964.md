# Use of experimental design for optimisation of the extraction of phenolic compounds in an active extract of Nectandra Reticulata.

The agonist activity in LXR modulates the production of ApoE and ABCA1, decreasing the amyloid plate in murine models, thus becoming a therapeutic alternative of Alzheimer. Previous studies show that Nectandra reticulata is agonist of LXR, so it is of our interest to identify the composition of the extract, relate it to its activity and optimize the extraction of this. This is how the active extract of N. reticulata was analyzed, using RP-UHPLC-DAD and UHPLC-ESI-HRMS. The results of the analysis by EM showed that most of the compounds present correspond to flavonoid glycosilated. To confirm the identity of the aglycones, an acidic hydrolysis was performed, showing that the main compound with a chromatographic area of 84.5% to 355 nm corresponds to the quercitrin. The second compound in abundance of 8.0% of the chromatographic area was identified. In less proportion (6.9%) the μg (6'-p-cumarylgluclide) was determined
