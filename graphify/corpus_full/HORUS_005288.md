# WHOSE HEARD OF PSYCOANALYSIS? A PLACE FOR THE EXCLUDED. NO- EVERYTHING IS LOST!

Let someone choose what to say and how to say it is what makes the act of floating attention possible for a subject to emerge among significants, it is there where psychoanalytic listening is not any listening. For Roland Barthes, it is necessary to differentiate the hearing from listening. Is hearing a physiological phenomenon; to listen, a psychological action? (p.243). Lacan showed from the beginning of his teaching that the question of psychoanalytic practice was something that had to do with the singularity of a subject, of his listening, singularity that could not be separated from one history with the others, hence beyond individual boundaries. Listening to that singularity is where a subject has to create a place, affirm the singularity of his existence, and live according to his desire without being placed in the place of master or slave
