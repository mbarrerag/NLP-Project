# Computer design, synthesis and preliminary studies of biological activity of multifunctional copper ligands with controlled redox potential, for application in the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease

Alzheimer’s disease is the most common form of senile dementia that affects the adult population significantly, and in cases such as Yarumal (Antioquia) to people under 40 years of age. Given the overall increase in life expectancy, the number of cases reported is expected to increase if a cure or preventive treatment is not found. In this project we propose, through a combination of computational and experimental methods, the design, synthesis and characterization of multifunctional ligands and their respective copper complexes which, according to their chemical properties, can help prevent the damage caused by Alzheimer’s disease by regulating intra and extraneural copper levels. Ligandos who show better results in computational calculations and synthesis experiments will be performed cytotoxic tests to measure their viability in cellular media and their ability to prevent oxidative damage characteristic of Alzheimer’s disease
