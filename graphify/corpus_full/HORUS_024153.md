# PODAL DISEASE IN BOVINES: PREVALENCE AND ASSOCIATION WITH SOME SORT OF VARIABLES

The PDO is one of the most difficult syndromes to control in the dairy industry. The present study aimed to quantify the prevalence of pedal disease, evaluating its association with factors such as race, birth order and weight. It is a retrospective study using the sanitary and productive records of a dairy herd located in the high tropics of Colombia. The racial components present were F1 Holstein × White Earing, 3/4 Holstein 1/4 White Earing, 5/8 Holstein 3/8 White Earing and Holstein 100%. The prevalence of PDO was considered 6 orders of birth. The PDO presented a binomial distribution. The effect of the factors was verified using a generalized linear model, by means of a logistic regression (PROC GENMOD SAS version 9.4). In case of significant differences, the PSI procedure was applied to the PSI of SAS (version 9.4) and a PSI of the PSI was observed between levels that showed significance.
