# Calibration analysis in quantum machine learning models

The calibration analysis of machine learning models takes on great importance in different contexts such as risk assessment, diagnostics and critical systems for safety, where there are decisions influenced by model predictions. The area of quantum machine learning has received greater attention in recent years, in particular, models have been developed that obtain competitive results in classification and regression tasks compared to widely used methods. However, the properties of this type of sorters in terms of calibration have not been explored in the literature. For this reason, in this work a study is carried out of the calibration properties that have some models of quantum machine learning versus models widely used in the literature such as vector support machines, decision trees, logistic regression, among others for binary classification and multiple class tasks. In addition, an experiment is carried out to explore the effect of some quantum sorters in combination with a neural network.
