# Urban Settlements in the Protected Areas System of Bogotá, Case Study: Humedal Meandro del Say

In recent decades, the rapid evolution of cities and urban expansion in Latin America, has put at risk the natural ecosystems. Among these, the case of the Meandro del Say wetland in Bogotá is highlighted. This wetland, which is vital for biodiversity and ecosystem services, faces significant threats due to urban growth and the search for land to develop with settlements in its surroundings. This research presents an analysis of the strategies of institutional, social and territorial management, on the environmental conflict that affects the Meandro del Say, which was carried out through a qualitative approach, in order to understand the governmental practices and the interests of the actors involved, from the urban government's vision. A concurrent triangulation design was used to analyze data, which allowed a rich discussion and different perspectives on the phenomenon to be addressed by which it is obtained as findings the concrete actions for the conservation of the wetland, including ecological restoration, environmental education and participatory monitoring. which was carried out using a qualitative approach, in order to understand governance practices and the interests of the actors involved, from the vision of the urban governance.
