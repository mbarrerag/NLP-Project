# Presence of RgpA, anti-RgpA antibodies and their association with rheumatoid arthritis

Clinical and epidemiological studies have corroborated the bidirectional association between rheumatoid arthritis (AR) and periodontitis. This association is attributed to Porphyromonas gingivalis, due to the presence of the enzyme peptidyl argginginine deiminase (PPAD) and to the activity of Arg-gingipains in citrullation, linked to the break of immunological tolerance in RA. It has been confirmed that anti-P. gingivalis antibodies accompany the development of RA. However, few studies investigate the association of virulance factors specific to this bacterter with RA.
