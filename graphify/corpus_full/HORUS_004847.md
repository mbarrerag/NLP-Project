# THE PRACTICE OF DISEASE IN THE CARE OF THE CHILD WITH STOMA

This work addresses in detail the morphophysiological, psychological and social specificities in the different stages of child development. It also explores the types of stomas, their demarcation and associated surgical techniques, as well as complications and management strategies. It also includes a quick assessment guide and a detailed list of the technologies required for care, along with recommendations and corresponding levels of evidence. It is presented as a valuable tool for health professionals working in the area of pediatric stomatherapy and seek to deepen their understanding of the care of individuals with stoma in the child population
