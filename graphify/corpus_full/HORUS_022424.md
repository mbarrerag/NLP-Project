# THEMATIC AREAS — THE EFFECT OF TEMPERATURE AND THE HYDRICAL POINTS ON THE GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT OF PAPAYA (CARICA PAPAYA).

The objective was to evaluate the effect of temperature (air and soil) and the water potential of the soil on the growth and development of papaya (Carica papaya). The experiment was done in conditions of tropical dry forest in the farm of the University of Tolima. Two treatments were used one of plastic covering of the soil and one without coverage. In each treatment were installed temperature sensors (air and soil) and hydro potential of the soil (30 and 60 cm). The variables of measurement were height of the plant and the rate of foliar emission. To determine the accumulation of biomass, every two months, in four phenological moments (vegetative development, flowering, beginning of production and full production) three plants per treatment were collected randomly. The data were analyzed with a longitudinal model and Anovas.
