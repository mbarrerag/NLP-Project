# EFFECT OF CALCINATION TEMPERATURE ON SUPPORTED CUCO2O4 CATALYSTS FOR GLYCEROL OXIDATION

Three quad2O4 catalysts supported on a modified clay were prepared by the wet impregnation method to assess the effect of the calcining temperature (400, 600 and 800 °C). Catalysts were evaluated in the oxidation of glycerol in aqueous medium using hydrogen peroxide as an oxidizing agent. The results indicate that the increase in the calcination temperature improves catalytic activity. This increase is due to structural changes associated with the interaction of the spinel Cu (CuCo2O4) with Al atoms of the support by forming the CuAl2O4 structure. These interactions between particles and support induce an increase in the proportion of Co on the surface increasing catalytic activity. Catalysts were selective mainly towards dihydroxyacetone. Although, glyceric acid and glyceraldehyde were identified and quantified to a lesser proportion. Finally, these catalysts recorded activity and selectivity favorable to mild reaction conditions (80 °C and atmospheric pressure)
