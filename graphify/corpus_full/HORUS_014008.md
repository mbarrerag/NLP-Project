# Legal implications of Artificial Intelligences in the Right to Work in Colombia: the responsibility of the private employer for the substitution of human capital.

The research revolves around the research question: What should be the position and responsibility of the private sector in Colombia in the context of the substitution of human capital by artificial intelligences, in the absence of specific legislation, within contractual relations? So, it is intended to corroborate The degree of responsibility that the employer/private enterprise has, taking as a hypothesis that in the absence of regulation in Colombia on the matter, the private sector is obliged to assume a role that would normally be the responsibility of the State in carrying out the functions of legislator and judge, since the implementation of IA's that has great potential to replace the workforce in different sectors and industries is an imminent phenomenon that will not give time to adopt laws to the new requirements. Therefore, the companies will be forced to face the challenges that arise from it until the State adequately fulfills their regulatory role and they will be created as guarantors of the rights of the workers despite the absence of the required legal framework. To this end, the information will be collected and an academic article of investigation will be presented.
