# Astronomical information in the first periodical publication of the New Kingdom of Granada

The inclusion of a section titled “Astronomical Conditions of Today” in the Papel Periódico de Santafé de Bogotá, the first periodical of the Viceroyalty of The New Kingdom of Granada, is the earliest known manifestation of a significant diversity of astronomical data calculated for the local time of Santafé de Bogotá. This communication explores the motivation behind its inclusion in the newspaper, the bibliographic source used as a starting point, the possible identity of the calculator, and the data quality
