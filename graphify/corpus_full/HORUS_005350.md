# GEOGRAPHICAL ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS DECALOGY OF TRANSFORMATIONS IN AQUATIC COLOMBIA SYSTEMS

Today we face profound transformations in the natural heritage of the country that impact a high percentage of ecosystems and landscapes.From the analysis of these transformations arise the questions that gave origin to this book: What are the main impacts or environmental crimes committed against the connatural heritage of the nation? What attributes of ecosystems, both structural and functional, were affected? What have been the most significant impacts on aquatic ecosystems and other natural environments? Since the last two decades of this century, Colombia has suffered significant losses of its biodiversity and a disstructure of the natural environment without precedent. Faced with this reality, this book does not aim to be exhaustive or to cover the magnitude of the main environmental stresses on the natural heritage. What interests me is to put into evidence some of the impacts that, in my opinion, have negatively affected the natural resources, leaving different traces of this loss. It is not to mention that each of the ten environmental impacts described is exposed from the perspective of the author and in a differentiated way. They are outlined without any order of importance, because, in the end, they are few effects of the natural heritage of the country's research. And in each case of the most possible, it is explained, in a rigorous and derived case
