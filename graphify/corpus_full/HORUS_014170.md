# ADOPTION OF THE ROLE OF FAMILY CAREERS OF DISABILITIES IN ARAUCA CITY

Disability results from the interaction between people with disabilities and barriers due to attitude and environment that avoid their full and effective participation in society, on equal terms with others (1); the nature of disability, treatments, evolution and significant changes in the patient's lifestyle, description and lack of care, makes them require the accompaniment of a caregiver, who are almost always women, have a family link (2), provide unpaid, voluntary and continuous assistance in the basic and instrumental activities to their families with disabilities (3), allow for the need to reorganize their work, maintain the union of the family, make decisions (4) and face changes in the relationships, expectations and abilities (5). Family caregivers are known as hidden patients and are often ignored (6.7), feel obliged to assume this role (7.8) for which they are not prepared, manifest difficulties and limitations to balance the burden of care of their family with their daily life, generating insufficiency of the role and difficulty to progress towards a healthy transition (67), this transition involves physical, emotional and cognitive efforts and triggers various consequences such as loneliness, anxiety, stress, stress, exhaustion, stress, stresslessness, stresslessness, resilience, resilience, disability, disability, disability, disability, difficulty in their ability to move to life, and difficulty to life (5); in which the process of adopting the role of caregivers in this region of the country is taking place. For this reason, this project is proposed, whose objective is to explain the adoption of the role of family caregivers of PsD in the city of Arauca, with the purpose, on the one hand, that the local government has tools to understand the needs of this population and facilitate the delivery of programs and aids that generate commitment to them with the caregivers, with the service providers and with the PsD and, on the other, contribute to the construction of disciplinary knowledge, providing elements for an inclusive theoretical framework and specific guidelines for professional practice, research and the generation of local public policies aimed at the family caregiver of PsD. To achieve this objective, a sequential, sequential, three-phase study will be developed: a quantitative phase through a descriptive, correlational study that allows to associate the adoption of the role of caregiver with some sociodemographic variables; a qualitative phase, whose purpose is to understand the process of adopting the role of family caregiver of PsD and finally a phase in which integrates and interprets the information obtained in the previous phases of care of way that is explained with the phenomenon of adoption of Araca
