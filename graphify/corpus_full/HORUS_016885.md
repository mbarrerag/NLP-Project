# An opera without breath, an opera without lungs, a tired opera that is an email and an attached excel: An opera of opera women who work and clean and do not know where to bury their dead

A broom that has taped in the bottom part, a microphone. A hall of impolved concerts, where pigeons and ghosts live. An operator who is me, an art worker, a teacher and now a student of this master. A precarious, urgent, necessary construction. How it is done with nails, with the joy of sadness. Without the time to process all the disaster that happened to us in these years. To find a way between precariousness, humor and sweat. From feminized work, from work, from pain, comes to light opera and with it, my mother. To then disappear in the penumbra of space, where there are spectra hidden in lights and songs that sound and offer for the one who wants, a synesthetic and ghostly experience. . Abroom from from the microphone to take away from the bottom. A dusty concert hall, where Pigeons and ghosts live. A femaire and ghosts operator, me, an article and worker, a teet from from the microphone to the bottom
