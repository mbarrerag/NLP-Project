# Characterization of the graduate of bachelor's degree from the Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Manizales during the period 2018 – 2022: a Case study in Business Administration Program

This research took place among graduates of the Business Administration program at the National University of Colombia, Manizales, in the period 2018-2022. Through qualitative research and analytical description using the focus group model, and applying a consultation inspired by the Kassel method, a group of objective and subjective variables were determined for a subsequent analysis of the characteristics and behaviors of the recent graduate. This method allows concepts to be grouped into aspects such as objective measurements of the graduate's transition from their degree to their first job. Objective measurements of professional practice in your first job. Subjective measurements of the graduate's perception of their academic and work development and training. Assessments of the relationship between study and professional performance. Subjective assessments of skills and entrepreneurial capacity and finally, objective assessments of integration and professional associativity. With this presentation of the information and the analytical development of the focus group modality, we proceeded to identify traits, tendencies and perceptions of the graduates, which were structured and presented in a final characterization matrix that aims to provide valuable information not only to the headquarters, but to the decision makers of the higher education system in the country
