# Active disturbance rejection control for an energy router used in the interconnection of electric microgrids

The interconnection of electrical micro-grids (MGs) allows to improve the reliability and efficiency of power systems based on this type of local and autonomous networks. In this context, the energy router (ER) acts as a multi-port interface that controls and directs the power flow between the MGs. Effective management of energy exchange in the ER requires robust and high-performance control systems in the ER power converters. In this sense, the automatic regulation of the current and power power in each port, as well as the voltage of the internal DC bus, constitute the fundamental control needs. However, factors such as power quality problems in the RMs, uncertainty in the models, the coupling between ports and changes in the operation, introduce disturbances that affect the functioning of the ER and impose challenges for the design of the controllers.
