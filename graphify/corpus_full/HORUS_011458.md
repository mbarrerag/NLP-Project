# Validation of the Kidney Disease Quality of Life Short Form 36 (KDQOL-36) scale for quality of life assessment in Colombian patients with chronic kidney disease.

Given the importance of quality of life as a health outcome during care of patients with chronic renal disease (CRD) stage 5 in dialysis, it is necessary to have evidence on the clinimetric properties of the instruments used for the measurement of this construct. To establish the clinimetric properties of the Kidney Disease Quality of Life Short Form 36 scale (KDQOL-36) to measure quality of life in patients with chronic renal disease stage 5 in dialysis in Colombia. A scale validation study using the classical theory methodologies of the test and theory of response to the Item was carried out. Statistical analysis included the techniques of exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis, validity evaluations of concurrent criteria, internal consistency, test-retest reliability, and sensitivity to change; as well as the evaluation of the adjustment properties of the items and of the individuals, the estimation of the separation and reliability of persons and items, evaluation of redundant items or of the uncovered construct regions, and the number of the functional categories employed. It was identified that the items of the scale measure a smaller construct range than that presented in this patient sample, suggesting considering the need to incorporate items that allow the range to be extended to cover measurements with better quality of life or with less impact of CKD on the construct. The KDQOL-36 scale is an appropriate instrument to measure quality of life in Colombian patients with stage 5 CKD on dialysis
