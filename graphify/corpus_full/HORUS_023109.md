# 4D IMPRESSION: A QUICK REVISION OF TECHNOLOGY AND MATERIALS

The 4D printing part of the principles established by the different 3D printing methods and materials with the design of functional shapes, multimaterial impressions and intelligent materials (Smart materials) that respond to different internal or external stimuli to the object, that trigger a physical change (form, physical characteristics, color) or chemical (adhesion, mixing). In this article is presented in a comprehensive way the review of the 4D printing process and in particular a summary of the most used materials or a prominent use in this field; this information is provided as a tool when designing designs and applications of products with the 4D printing technique. This article seeks to make known the fundamental aspects of the 4D printing, subject that arouses much concern in different areas of knowledge. The article aims to be a reference reference for the industrial designers and engineers that are in the field of product design. Once the application of the technology for 3D printing has been known and experienced, the 4D printing appears, causing a logical concern related to its application in new products and services. The article has the following structure: key cases of the 3D printing is reported. that, from the development of intelligent materials and the characterization of stimuli to which they can respond by modi- morphology or the behavior of some of their physical properties, it contributes new conditions in a certain temporality. This allows the elaboration of designs of functional forms designed to evolve in objects that respond to internal or external stimuli predetermined changing their form in a certain time and in some cases with a return to their initial state, after the infuencia of the stimulus. 4D printing is a family of technologies and associated production techniques, which, within the academic and research field, does not count on more than six or seven years of existence. It is not visible in the successive advances of technologies of rapid prototyping by adding material. These technologies, due to their current status, apparently limit the advances of 4D printing, although any progress in these methods can and should be applicable in both models of printing, since one comes from the other and have common points in mind.
