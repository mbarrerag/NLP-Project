# Sociodemographic, clinical and radiological characterization of patients with aneurysmic subarachnoid haemorrhage in the hospital on the Tunal between 2018 and 2021.

The main origin of spontaneous subarachnoid bleeding is the rupture of intracranial aneurysms that are located mainly in the anterior cerebral circulation. Despite having a relatively low incidence, it preferentially compromises patients of working age with high morbidity and high treatment costs, which generates a high disease burden for health systems. In the world there are enough publications regarding this pathology, but unfortunately, publications in Latin America and particularly in Colombia are limited, which forces extrapolation of international information, ignoring our own characteristics. This is why the objective of this study is to describe the sociodemographic, clinical, radiographic, therapeutic and result characteristics in the adult population with spontaneous subarachnoid bleeding of aneurysmic origin in the Hospital El Tunal de la Subred Sur de la Secreta de Salud de Bogotá between 2018 and 2021. We performed an observational, retrospective study in which the clinical histories and images of patients according to the database of patients provided by El Hospital must be corroborated.
