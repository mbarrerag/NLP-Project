# Graphical design and colombianity: an analysis through the Colombian Advertising Yearbook between 2008 and 2018

The present research work has as main objective analyze and expose the narrative intentions of Colombianidad, based on precepts and ideological assumptions present in the advertising graphic ads throughout the decade between 2008 and 2018, using as object of study the Colombian Advertising Yearbook. To this, the rhetoric and the semiotic present in the advertising graphic design are explored. The specific objectives are focused on identifying the most representative iconic, symbolic and rhetorical symbolic systems in the graphic design of the advertising pieces chosen by the Yearbook, which have participated in the construction of specific narratives of the Colombian national identity. Likewise, it seeks to recognize the national identity relations expressed in the advertising pieces during the mentioned period and to describe the historical processes that link the graphic design, advertising and the Colombian national identity. To carry out this research, methods such as the Founded Theory and the analysis will be applied, which highlights for its flexibility and ability to observe, abstractly think and recognize potential biases.
