# The effect of transfer on mechanical properties in the GMAW welding process

Welding cords were deposited, using three heat inputs, in structural steel plate ASTM A36. With a ER70S-3 input metal. This to compare the microstructure and the mechanical properties at voltage of the melted and thermally affected zones, for spray and short circuit transfers, in the GMAW process*. It was not detected with optical microscopy, difference in the microstructure, when soldering with short circuit or spray at equal heat input. The difference in the strength and ductility values of the welded plate with short circuit and spray is not very marked
