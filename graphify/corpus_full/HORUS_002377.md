# South-South cooperation with cultural perspective: inter-dependence, dialogue and co-existence

The text analyses various possibilities for the realization and consolidation of initiatives that facilitate and strengthen South-South cooperation dynamics between Africa, Asia, Latin America and the Caribbean, from a cultural perspective. It also explores interdependence, dialogue and coexistence as dynamic elements of the rapprochement between peoples, states and institutions of these regions, and seeks to address the issue of South-South cooperation with specific theoretical resources and with references to intellectual and cultural collaboration. It also uses alternative epistemologies, decolonizing and critical to elucidate the history of African alterity, which is positioned as a bridge between Asian, Latin American and Caribbean initiatives. This allows for the encouragement of re-exemptions full of cultural, academic and historical imprints that seek to intensify such cooperation, and places emphasis on the role of the community of researchers in these academic collaboration initiatives. It also aims to establish favourable conditions for a better rapprochement of the peoples, States and institutions of this region. In addition, it aims for the creation of fruitful alliances and essential for a sustained dialogue, a favourable scenario for the economic interdependence and economic interdependence
