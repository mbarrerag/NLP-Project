# performance Measurement strategy in business units in companies of mass consumption. Case study Target Group – Food

This final master's degree work is developed around the general concept of strategy, giving scope to other concepts such as measurement, mass consumption and SBUs as Strategic Business Units. The case study was chosen as the research method, applying it to a Colombian company with extensive experience in the mass consumption sector, which met the parameters of the research, this company is Diana Alimentos, which is one of the SBUs of the Diana Group. It begins by describing the company, the environment of the mass consumption sector, the market variables that the customer takes into account when making the purchase decision, in addition to the internal variables that may affect the availability of the product. This is where the basic problem of the research is identified, related to the lack of a set of metrics adjusted to the business that are incorporated into a measurement framework to monitor the performance of the strategy in the company. The review of the literature in different databases continues, limiting the search to keywords and what has been published in recent years in articles, books and specialized magazines, where some methodologies and measurement frameworks that have been used in different sectors of the economy are reviewed, but which are not entirely applicable in the case study, due to the characteristics of mass consumption companies and the differentiating factors that must be used to encourage the consumption of their products that are in high demand. The main results of this work are: the identification from the theory of the methods and tools for measuring strategy performance applicable to mass consumption companies, the qualitative information collected from Diana Alimentos and the proposal of a measurement framework with the metrics selected by experts; that can allow monitoring the performance of the strategy in mass consumption companies
