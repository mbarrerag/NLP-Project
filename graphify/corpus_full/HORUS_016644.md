# OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY IN TIMES OF COVID-19. STRATEGIES AND REFLECTIONS FROM COLOMBIA

The COVID-19 crisis represented a break in the performance of our occupations, having significant consequences in the work activities. The concern raised was to determine how this situation of COVID-19 and confinements affected the work of the Colombian occupational therapist and what strategies they applied to continue their activities. Identify the strategies and interventions that some occupational therapists adopted to continue with their labor in times of COVID-19, reflecting on the issues addressed and the populations they reached with these actions. Literature review that explored the journal ¿Ocupación Humana¿ as the main national publication in OT. Likewise, content was searched on social networks and websites of the Colegio Colombiano de Terapia Ocupacional, of some universities that have the career in Colombia and in profiles of institutions and private professionals that offer the service of Occupational Therapy. In addition, it was designed and applied a Google forms questionnaire called "STRATEGIES, INTERVENTIONS AND TOPICS MANAGED BY COLOMBIAN OCCUPATIONAL THERAPISTS IN TIMES OF PANDEMIC¿. This instrument inquired about how the work of these personnel developed, what adjustments these OTs had to implement to continue providing their services, what were the preferred activities, strategies, modalities and interventions to reach individuals and communities, among other aspects. ¿ Occupational therapists began to issue recommendations focused on the populations served since the SARS-CoV-2 virus appeared in the country, mainly information for performance at home. ¿ Once the confinement was decreed, they used virtual tools and prepared documents that addressed topics such as the occupational therapist in the clinical care of COVID-19, the biosafety of professionals, guidelines for the management of the elderly at home, considerations for teleworking, carrying out sports and leisure activities, guidelines for children with sensory integration problems, etc. ¿ The results harmonize with the trends found in the scientific literature, considering the transition made during the emergency by health professionals and other experts, including occupational therapists, from face-to-face care to widespread use of a telehealth or telerehabilitation model (Dahl-Popolizio et al., 2020). ¿ Although it can be questioned to what extent most of the colleagues were prepared to carry out their work through this digital modality or had the preparation and conditions of the case, it definitely became an alternative to reach individuals and communities that required therapeutic support. 1. The professionals demonstrated the support that the services provided by the OT community have had to help carry, solve and manage the occupational problems of people and communities in times of pandemic. 2. The profession can direct its action towards all human beings and not exclusively towards those who present a dysfunction in their health and/or occupational performance. 3. It is necessary to document these experiences of Colombian occupational therapists, since these strategies could mark new fronts of attention and research that go beyond urgent responses to the pandemic
