# Calculation and experimental validation of the input surface dose from TLD dosimetry and its correlation with the dosimetric quantities delivered by conventional X-ray equipment.

This study aims to measure the Dose in the Inlet Surface (DSE) from Thermoluminescent Dosimetry (TLD) and direct dosimetry with a solid state detector to correlate it with the equipment's dose indicators. Experimentally obtained values will also serve as a reference for the validation of different methods that allow estimating the DSE as: the theoretical calculation from a generic equation, from the equipment's own performance curve and from knowing the value of the Dose Area Product (PDA). The results obtained from the TLDs had coefficients of variation between 12 and 54%, for this reason the use of these data was discarded as a reference and the results of the solid state detector measures as a reference were taken. Accuracies were found between the experimental measures and: the theoretical calculation from 0.4 to 26.8%, the team curve from 0.3 to 22.5% and from the PDA from 0.05 to 16%. The Index of Exposure (EI) was characterized and its dependence was obtained with respect to mAs, kV and DSE
