# TO TAKE THE PORTION OF NIGHT

Writing/bording pieces of life, in the midst of the imprisonment imposed by the recent pandemic, was also an exercise of freedom and escape. To lift up the record of the slowness, the tedium, the silence of the city, the uncertain wait. To let the gaze fly through the window and discover a whole throbbing world a few meters away and never before perceived. To listen otherwise to the dear neighbors, of whom we were suddenly exiled. And, above all, to suffer the agitation of the memory, with its lagoons, its vivid images, its latent conditions
