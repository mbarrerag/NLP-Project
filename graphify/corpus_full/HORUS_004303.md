# DESIGN OF A CONTACT LENS SCANNER WITH METHOD.

To measure the shape of the convex and concave sides of a rigid contact lens, an optical scanner is designed based on the triangulation principle. A ligth slit passes over the contact lens while a camera at an angle of about saves a set of images of the distorted ligth slit. The optical scanner comprises of two systems: a slit projection and an image formation systems. To design these optical systems, we use the method. For the image formation system the condition of minimum distortion is established. The results show that the design satisfies the initial requirements and that the method is a tool with great potential in optical design
