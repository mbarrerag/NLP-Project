# Factors determining agricultural management to strengthen the farmers of the Meta department in the marketing process

This research has as general objective, to identify the factors that are considered determinants or of great impact of the agricultural management, these factors can serve as guide to strengthen the peasants in the marketing process in the department of the Meta. This research is type descriptive, it has analyzed real information of 30 associations and information of the peasants that integrate these associations and its 405 Productive Units. To complement, it has analyzed information of 20 distributors of products and the perception of 20 peasants on intermediaries, the price of sale and profitability of products. The information was collected in the years 2013-2014, in the municipalities of Villavicencio, Granda. cubarral, el dorado, fuente de oro, san juan de arama, fares and acías.
