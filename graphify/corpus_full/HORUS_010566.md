# RELIGIOUS TOLERANCE VIEW BY FOREIGN TRAVELERS COLOMBIA IN THE XIXth CENTURY.

to analyze how religious tolerance was discussed in the travel literature produced by foreigners who traveled Colombia in the 19th century. textual analysis was applied to at least twenty travel accounts (diaries, memories and correspondence), disentangled in them the views that their authors had on religious tolerance. Ideas about religious tolerance were placed in the 19th century, in the political, cultural and social spheres. Originality: the article is a contribution to Colombian historiography of the 18th century because there are no published research on the way foreigners who traveled the country, most of them Protestants, saw religious tolerance. the travel accounts show that travelers' positions on religious tolerance cannot be reduced to a black and white reading. On the contrary, in the article we see the tensions between the ideas on religious tolerance carried by travellers and what the environment showed them. Following the above, we continue to investigate the religious fact focused on the history of religious tolerance and freedom
