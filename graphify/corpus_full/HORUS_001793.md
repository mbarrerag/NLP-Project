# Social representations of physical activity and active play, in children, mothers and fathers, caregivers and teachers, of preschool children of two educational institutions in the city of Cali, Colombia, during 2018-2019

With the aim of making the social representations emerge on physical activity in the participating population, to provide useful knowledge in the elaboration of healthy public policies and the realization of public health interventions in the preschool age, a study was carried out with a qualitative approach, with a case study design, from the Theory of Social Representations, using as a method of analysis the Constructivist Founded Theory. It was carried out in Cali, Colombia, in a public educational institution (Case 1) and a private educational institution (Case 2), both of the preschool level. A multimethodology was used for the collection of data, including interviews, association networks, drawings, photo voice and observation. Case 1 presented as a figurative nucleus of social representation, the category “Moving in different ways”, while Case 2 had as a figurative nucleus the category “Receiving the benefits of physical activity”.
