# Role of algae in controlling and biomonitoring pollutants in an aquatic ecosystem: Biodiesel production case

This chapter introduces alternative clean energy sources such as biofuels, biodiesel, obtained from triacylglycerol extracted from algae. First, it explains algal growth where biomass, growth rate, and composition depend on the culture conditions, such as light intensity, temperature, pH, and culture medium (micronutrients and macronutrients). It then explains the lipid extraction where the microalgae will be used as a source of triacylglycerides to produce biodiesel. By the end of the chapter, the variation in growth parameters is studied to generate as many triacylglycerides as possible, which are then used to obtain biodiesel through a catalytic process
