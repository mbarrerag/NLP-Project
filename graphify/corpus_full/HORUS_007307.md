# Weighted multiplication operator on weighted Lebesgue sequence spaces

In this work we investigate the spaces of successions of Lebesgue with weights lp,, which generalize the classical spaces lp by incorporating a succession of weights. We begin by exploring the fundamental properties of lp,, including completeness, separability, convexity and study of the dual space. We extend important inequalities such as the inequalities of Hölder and Minkowski to the context of the weights, providing essential tools for a later analysis. Then, we demonstrate Hardy's inequality in its discreet form with weights and derive the inequality of Carleman as corollary. Finally, we study the operator of multiplication acting in lp,, which generalize the classic lp spaces by incorporating a weight sequence
