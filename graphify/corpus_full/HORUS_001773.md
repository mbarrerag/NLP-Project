# Study of the electronic properties of the galium antimonium manganese interphase Mn/GaSb

The study of materials has constantly contributed to the development of new technologies, being this an attractive field for research, particularly of conductive and semiconductor materials such as manganese (Mn) and the antimoniide of gallium (GaSb), considered important in comparison to the improvement in the performance of electronic devices. In this work we seek to analyze the structural and electronic properties of these materials and of the interface (Mn/GaSb). We examine systems of multiple ideal crystals, in pure and diffusion conditions, as well as the electro-stabilisation for the interface without diffusion of -1 eV, at a distance between layers of 2.05 Å and anti-skill (DFT) implemented in the code SIESTA, in the approximation of generalized gradient GGA. We obtain as a result a minimum energy of stabilization for the interface without diffusion of -1 eV, at a distance between layers of 0.058 Å and -0.58 eV to 2.02 Å for the interface of the system with diffusion, showing a higher energy for the latter, and with a small difference in the optimal separation of stabilization between systems of the two levels of the new to the systems. tion of the layers (interface), all this by ab-initio calculations and agreement to the density functional theory (DFT) implemented in the SIESTA code, in the generalized gradient approach GGA. The result is a minimum stability energy for the diffusionfree interface of -1 eV at an interlayer distance of 2.05 -A and of -0.58 eV at 2.02 -A for the diffusion system, showing a stability at a higher energy for the latter, and with a small difference in the optimal stability separation between the two systems of 0.03 -A.
