# MINERALOGY OF PROCESSINGS OF A CONCENTRATE OF MONACITA PRODUCT OF THE GOLD MINING MINING COMPANIES S.A. (THE BAGRE, ANTIOCIA).

The benefit of alluvial gold consists, among others, of a series of processes of concentration of the fraction of heavy minerals in the accompanying sands, commonly called black sands, some of them of great economic interest (ilmenita, rutil, circus, monastic, within the most important).In these, the monace is one of the most precious minerals, since it is an orthophosphate anhydrous of elements of the rare earth (ETRs), known for possessing extraordinary catalytic, electrical, magnetic and optical properties, so its demand worldwide has increased in recent years. For this work, the gravimetric concentration processes (for cleaning of the initial sample) were used, as well as the result of its production process for alluvial gold, taken from the different dredges in a given period of time, in El Bagre (Antio).The initial sample was performed with the gravimetric concentration processes (for cleaning of the initial sample), as well as the subsequent magnetic and electrostatic separation, with the aim of obtaining a sufficiently pured concentrate of the monace. calcite, phlogopit, siderite, dolomite, quartz, ferrosilite, chromite and circum. From the results, it was concluded that the purest fraction was -140/+200. The characterization results showed, among others, that the concentration process was not efficient, since the presence of other minerals, especially in the thickest fractions, could have negative effects on the processes of extraction of ETRs, resulting in impure products.
