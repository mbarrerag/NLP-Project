# Paleomagnetism in the Province of Volcano-Tectonics San Diego – Cerro Machín

A study was carried out on the north-west of the Nevado del Ruiz Volcano, covering 17 sites, representing domes and lava flows. Thermal and alternal field desmagnetization analyses were performed, magnetic susceptibility vs temperature, histéresis curves, isothermal residual magnetisation and reverse field, and magnetic susceptibility anisotropy (AMS). The results of the directional analysis were also found for 13 sites, showing 7 with normal polarity and 4 with reverse polarity, in accordance with the ages assigned in previous studies (<45 ka to 1.97 Ma) related to the Brunhes cron (7 sites) and Matuyama (4 sites).In addition, 2 intermediate polarities were also found.
