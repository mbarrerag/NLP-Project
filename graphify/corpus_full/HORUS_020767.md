# Applications in the construction of architectural concrete precasts with recycled rubber grain aggregates (GCR)

The waste of out-of-use tyres generates environmental and social problems due to its poor disposition, this research proposes to include aggregate of recycled rubber grain (CGR) as a replacement of 10% in volume of the coarse aggregate for a mixture of precast in architectural concrete. The properties of the concrete in fresh state and in hardened state were studied in control mixtures without aggregate of recycled rubber and in mixtures with addition of aggregate of recycled rubber, the workingability of the material decreases with the addition of GCR by 92.59% and the use of additives becomes necessary. The properties of the concrete in fresh state and in a state of hardened were maintained in water/cement of 0.35 with the use of a plasticizer and mechanical tools for the mixing and vibrating of the elements, thanks to this, the workingability of the material decreased with the addition of water/cement of 0.35 with the use of a plastic and mechanical tool for the mixing and vibrating of the elements was achieved by the results of compression resistance to the 28 days of 57.6 Mpa (8.354 Psi), which opens a world of opportunities for its application, however, represented a loss of 30.35% with the inclusion of concrete with the inclusion to the inclusion of the inclusion of with the addition of GCR by 92.59% and the use of additives becomes necessary. It was possible to maintain a water/cement ratio of 0.35 with the use of a plasticizer and mechanical tools for mixing and vibrating the elements, by using them it was achieved comprehensive strengths at 28 days of 57.6 Mpa (8,354 Psi) which opened a world of opportunities for its application, howver, it represented a those of 30.35% with the inclusion of rubber. Regarding resistance to abrasion and impact, very similar results were obtained between the control mix and the modified mix.
