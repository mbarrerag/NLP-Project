# Development of spatial thinking in students from grade 6 to 9 through problem solving using chess as a motivational source of learning.

This work aims to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with spatial thinking in students from grade 6 to 9 of the Antonio Nariño Educational Institution located in the rural area of the municipality of Philadelphia (Caldas), based on the design and implementation of learning activities based on the approach of problem solving in the context of chess. The work is framed in the naturalistic paradigm, with a qualitative and descriptive approach, whose methodological design is framed in the theory based on design that is dynamized from the application of 3 types of workshops called: familiarization, diagnostic, advance and final. Among the results obtained are highlighted the following: Allow students to obtain conclusions based on the presentation of objects, their relationships and/or transformations by identifying relationships and properties of differentiation between a drawing and geometric object, recognize conditions and properties of the figures in the solution of problems, develop the ability to argue with well-founded reasons about a property, relationship or situation giving meaning to the concepts and procedures, communicate the results of their research, identify the drag as a tool of validation of the procedures performed in the construction of the characteristics, and describe the geometrical design of other properties
