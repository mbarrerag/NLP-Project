# PHOTOCATALYTIC OXIDATION OF GLYCEROL TO PRODUCE DIHYDROXYACETONE AND OTHER PRODUCTS OF HIGHER INDUSTRIAL VALUE

Within the catalytic transformation processes, photocatalysis emerges as a promising way to valorized crude glycerol, a waste by-product of the biodiesel production industry. In this work, catalysts based on Ag and Cu nanoparticles (NPs) supported on TiO2 (two monometallic and one bimetallic) were synthesized, which proved to be active in the oxidation of glycerol under white light irradiation with the external assistance of hydrogen peroxide. After 8h of reaction, the Ag/TiO2 catalyst showed the highest glycerol conversion (74%) with selectivity to dihydroxyacetone of 75%. However, the best selectivity to this product, which is valuable in the cosmetic industry, was achieved with the Cu/TiO2 catalyst (86%) with a glycerol conversion of 55%.
