# INTITIONS ON THE COMMON TERRITORY OF ARTISTIC CREATION AND SPIRITUAL EXPERIENCE.

Self-ethnography as a research strategy makes an effort to bring science and art closer together to reveal complex human, universal, and unique evocative narratives. Seven artists from various disciplines and multiple spiritual paths conducted a reflexive writing exercise in autoethnographic journals during the ten months of the Art and Spirituality Laboratory. This was the central methodological strategy of the research project -creation The hermeneutic function of art in spiritual experience., conducted at the School of Human Sciences of the University of the Rosario, with funding from Templeton Religion Trust
