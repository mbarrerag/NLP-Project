# PSYCOMETRIC PROPERTIES: SEXUAL AUTHOCONCEPT INVENTORY IN ADOLESSENT WOMEN IN EARLY STAGE

to establish the validity and reliability of the self-concept sexual instrument in early-stage adolescent women (SSCI) Spanish version in Colombian population, more precisely in Santander. Material and methods: methodological study. Tests were used for the validity of the theoretical construct of its dimensions and internal consistency with a sample of 308 adolescent women, aged 10 to 14 years, from two educational institutions in Girón, Santander, Colombia. inventory of 34 items with three dimensions; through an exploratory factor analysis, the three factors were confirmed with a total variance of 43,261 %. The instrument reported an internal consistency of a=0.89 for the dimensions of sexual excitability (0,908), sexual agency (0,812) and negative sexual effect (0.572). the inventory of sexual self-concept (SSCI) in adolescent women enrolled in early school. Spanish version is reliable for assessing behaviors, sexual feelings about oneself and understanding adolescent sexual behavior. Key words (Source DeCS) Adolescent; women; psychometry; sexual health; reproducibility of results; reliability and validity; academic institutions
