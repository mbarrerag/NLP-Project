# Magnetic nanoparticles flow into a Newtonian vascular fluid.

The directed transport of medicinal products linked to particles, whose trajectories are affected by magnetic fields external to the bloodstream, as an alternative to traditional methods of disease treatment, is a field of research in which different theoretical, analytical and computational elements are used. In order to describe and obtain information on the dynamic and kinetic behavior of the particles as well as the effects of the magnetic force on them. Therefore, this work seeks to describe and establish which are the most relevant theoretical elements to study the dynamic evolution of these particles when they are immersed in a Newtonian vascular fluid whose trajectories are affected by a permanent magnetic field. Under these conditions, a series of simulations were performed with the help of the software specialized in finite elements COMSOL Multiphysics, in which the initial conditions of three models containing the working space called “world”, the channel through which the fluid and particles were displaced and the permanent magnet in charge of generating the magnetic field were obtained. As a result of the simulations, data were obtained from the average magnetic force acting on the particles and, as well as data from their capture efficiency.
