# Methodological approximation for the integration of DataOps and MLOps methodologies applied to automatic trading.

The traditional methodologies used for the development of data science applications accommodate a large technical debt in its development process that ends up causing the failure of many projects. To mitigate this debt, an integration is proposed between the approaches of DataOps and MLOps, where the benefits, steps and principles to follow are defined in the construction of an application framed under this proposed integration. Subsequently, two automatic trading applications are compared using Deep learning, one of which is developed under traditional methodologies and another under the integration of MLOps and DataOps approaches. It is concluded that the integration of both approaches is highly beneficial, allowing to fill the gaps of traditional methodologies and the deficiencies of each of the approaches used independently
