# WHY MARX IN THE 21st CENTURY

I have not known an intellectual mourning more prolonged than that which the Marxists and capitalists keep keeping for Marx. Each book or event is a supposed reanimation. However, it calls attention that it is the most desired for the resurrection, and the most buried daily by those who celebrate the life and success of capitalism. To try to bury Marx is like trying to kick out a boomerang. This makes me think, more about the deaths that are intended to constantly propel him the capitalists than by the reanimations of the Marxists, that something is going on, something is left of him. And it is about this that it seems that there can be, pass and stay that I intend to inquire.
