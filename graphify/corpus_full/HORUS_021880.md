# Enhanced job stability of workers in disability: A study on the development of justiciability of abuses arising from the employment relationship.

This work, which has been built with the compilation of extensive jurisprudence, doctrine, comparative law and field work (surveys), shows how constant abuses in the employment relationship by the worker in a situation of disability are presented, where the application of Law 361 of 1997, is not a guarantee for the employer and how the jurisprudence of the high courts counteracts each other, generating legal uncertainty for the management of the situations they press, in different sectors of production
