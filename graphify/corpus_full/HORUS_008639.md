# RESULT RESULT RESULT ACTIVITY STUDY IN SOUND SAMPLING IN BOGOTA

The enzymes are a special type of proteins that combine with a specific substrate and act to catalyze a biochemical reaction. Due to their protein nature can be affected by environmental factors such as temperature and pH, among others. The enzymes that are obtained in the soil and that are produced by plants, animals, microorganisms and even fungi, are essential for the transformation of energy and the nutrient cycle. Among them. urease is an enzyme that accelerates by one hundred trillion times the rate of hydrolysis of the urea in ammonia (NH3) and carbon dioxide (CO2). The urea of the soil can be harmful for the production of crops, especially when the technique of fertilisation of coverage is used. In fact, more than 50% of the N-urea applied in the soil surface can be lost, mainly by volatization of the NH3, reducing its fertilization efficiency and the development of the plants. Considering the importance of urease in agricultural and biomedical applications, it was proposed as a laboratory project to determine the urease activity present in the soil from three soil in Bogotá, Florida, a company, and a near to a living-life. 42 mg·L-1. Overall, the results allowed the inclusion of this work as a laboratory guide for biochemical practices
