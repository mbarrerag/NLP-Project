# GENETIC VARIABILITY OF MALIFORMIS L. (CHOLUPA) PASSIPHLORA (CHOLUPA) IN THE DEPARTMENT OF HUILA, COLOMBIA

The cholupa (Passiflora maliformis L.) is one of the main cultivated species of the genus Passiflora L. due to its nutritional properties of the fruit and the different uses of the plant. In the department of Huila in Colombia this species is cultivated between the 600 and 1,000 m s.n.m. and occupies an important line in the peasant economy. However, there are no studies on the degree of genetic variability present in the commercial crops in Huila, which limits the processes of selection and improvement of the crop. Establish the genetic and morphological variability of the gremoplasma of cholupa cultivated in the main municipalities of the department of Huila. The morphological characterization was carried out following a list of the descriptors (stallo, leaf, flower and fruit) and the molecular program with markers based on the ribosomal DNA (ITS4 and ITS5, internal transcription spacers) and compared with the analysis of 19 species of the vabosssicada (VA) and comparison with the analysis of the analysis of the analysis of the The average of 0.19.
