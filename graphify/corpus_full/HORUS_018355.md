# WOMEN: EXPERIENCES OF RESEARCHERS ACCOUNTED IN LATIN AMERICA

The role of women in society is fundamental to development, but historically it has been discriminated against and marginalized in different aspects, to a greater or lesser extent and in different contexts. This has led to the formation and strengthening of movements in favour of women's rights and has encouraged women to gain access to the same rights as men, from such fundamental aspects as health or education to more contemporary guarantees such as property, wage and voting rights.The results of the questionnaire applied to women accounting researchers from Mexico, Colombia, Chile and Argentina, countries relevant to the inquiry into feminist trends, according to a previous review, are analysed through qualitative research.In this way, the chosen information collection tool was a questionnaire designed by the group with the aim of establishing the personal and academic characteristics of the accounting researchers, research interest derived from a first stage of theoretical reflection that revealed the need to demonstrate the participation of women in accounting research.It is worth mentioning that a total of twenty-six researchers responded to the instrument, explaining from their experiences, geographical and academic context their vision of the research that they develop
