# Books on Medicinal Indian Herbs

From the collection of the Colombian National Herbarium and from artistic research as a platform and laboratory located, this project proposes to establish other ways of representing the territory where the Royal Botanical Expedition of the New Kingdom of Granada took place (1783-1813). To this end will be critically reviewed, from artistic practices, the paradigms of representation of nature that since José Celestino Mutis have been superimposed on the cultural construction of the territories. In fact, the critical discussion on this matter has been rather scarce and, however, to the eyes of today would seem to be unplaced the review of, for example, the botanical gardens and the herbals as historically determined places and bearers of conflicting territorial memories. Bearing in mind that the Royal Botanical Expedition of the New Kingdom of Granada is given in a territory clearly determined by the real paths from Bogotá to Honda-Maricita, Ibague, Valle de San Juan, La Mesa and again Bogotá, this project implies the realization of a biography of territory by means of the journey.
