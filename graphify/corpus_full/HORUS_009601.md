# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND NEW TECHNOLOGIES APPLICABLE TO MINORITY SECURITY AND THE PREVENTION OF ACCIDENTS AND EMERGENCY

The mining industry generates a large amount of data that are digitally found. Examples are the concentrations of gases from personal detectors. Often, these data have very limited use and, in most cases, are discarded. Industries such as transportation, manufacturing, agriculture and medicine have begun to use all of this data through new technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (IA). This article illustrates how data from personal gas detectors could be used by an artificial intelligence algorithm, specifically an artificial neural network to alert about a methane explosion. Exercise takes as an example the explosion of methane and coal dust that took place in Topaga in 2021. After demonstrating by means of physical modelling tools the low ventilation and possible stratification of methane in the mine, a database is generated that feeds the artificial neural network, which manages to alert about an imminent explosion in 971,9 times
