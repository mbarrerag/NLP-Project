# DEVELOPING EMULATOR SOLAR WITH XENON TYPE FLASH LAMP.

This article develops the stages of design, construction and testing of a solar emulator with flash type xenon bulb used to measure the feature Current vs. Voltage, or I-V curve, used in the quality control of photovoltaic modules. The solar emulator, and within them the one that uses a Xenon bulb, is one of the most practical ways to perform this type of testing. To be accredited this type of equipment must meet the requirements defined in the international technical standard IEC 60904-9. To meet these requirements with the current standard photovoltaic modules, Xenon Flash bulbs with an energy close to 4000 J. Therefore, the power of this solar emulator must ensure the energy necessary for the uniformity and duration of the flash pulse of the bulb. It is for this reason that a DC power source of 24 pulses is developed, taking as a reference its operating characteristics with low harmonic content and low undulation in the output voltage.
