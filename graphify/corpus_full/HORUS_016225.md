# CHALLENGES AND CASES IN THE TORNEY TO PATRIMONIAL ACTIVATIONS, THE METROPOLITIAN AREA OF VALLEDUPING AS A STUDY: SUSTAINABLE EXPERIENCE IN THE PATRIMONY.

Challenges and cases surrounding the patrimonial activations in the metropolitan area of Valledupar: Sustainable experiences in heritage The patrimonial activation in the metropolitan area of Valledupar faces multiple challenges, arising from the need to balance cultural preservation with sustainable development. In a context of increasing urbanization and socio-economic transformation, the management of the material and intangible heritage must address tensions between modernization and local identity. One of the main challenges is the integration of communities into the patrimonialization processes, ensuring that their participation is not merely symbolic but effective in decision-making. Likewise, gentrification and commercialization of the cultural heritage may distort its original meaning, affecting groups that have historically supported it. Specific cases in Valledupar, such as the revitalization of Vallenata music and the protection of the Historical Center, show attempts to balance conservation and economic use of the heritage.
