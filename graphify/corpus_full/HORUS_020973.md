# Search for bioactive compounds of native Andean species with allelopatic and antifungal potential

In tropical forests the adaptation of plants to a competitive environment is very dynamic so they influence the production of chemical agents.The abundance of some plant species in an ecosystem has been considered as a physiological predictor of their chemical composition.This means that plants can be more successful in the competition with other plant species.The above has resulted in significant contributions in agriculture where they have been reported by their herbicide, fungicide, bactericide, insecticide activity, with a lower impact on the environment.In this study, it is proposed to select native tree species from permanent forest monitoring plots in Antioquia, studied by the research group of "Conservation, use and biodiversity" and which have been previously evaluated or reported with bioactive potential, to evaluate the biological activity and chemical characterization of the selected species' work.On one hand, the search for potential herbicides based on the ecological relations that generate allelopathy in the forest will be addressed. Thus, preliminary allelopathic trials will be carried out by means of the in vitro evaluation of the inhibition of the seed of lettuce with the plant extracts as potentials will be considered as potential for the potentials to be considered. The bioautographic tests of the fractions will be carried out until the majority nuclei are characterization in the active fractions by means of chromatographic and spectroscopic techniques. Considering the above, this study is based on the biodiversity of species that may be a source of bioactive alternatives. The proposed biological and chemical characterization is the basis for enhancing the sustainable use of natural resources from sustainable use. Thus, proposals for formulations of natural products that are compatible with the environment and safe use, giving an added value to native species and an argument for the conservation and protection of the habitats of these species as a source of solutions with lower environmental impact, can be generated
