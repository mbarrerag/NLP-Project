# A INFLUENCE OF THE XIXth CENTURY ON TV OF THE XXIst CENTURY. THE SUTIL ORIGIN OF REVOLUTIONS, EXPERIENCE OF HISTORICAL DISCLOSURE WITH GENDER PERSPECTIVE

The Subtle Origin of Revolutions is a series produced by Vista Priore in partnership with Canal Thirteen. It creatively tells the story of how women in Colombia have fought for their rights and have opened their way in different spaces such as politics and education. The protagonist, Teresa Cortés de León, a woman who influenced the 19th century, finds a door that allows her to travel through time in different times, thus, she manages to meet the heroines who hide behind the great revolutions of women and women who are currently in important scenarios such as the country’s legislation. The series recreates those small moments that forged a great history, and every moment was orchestrated by the action, feeling, emotion or thought of a woman. It is called the Subtle Origin of Revolutions because it shows that perhaps everyday, perhaps simple, time when a woman dares to break the schemes of her generation to claim a right that we Colombians enjoy today in the 21st century.
