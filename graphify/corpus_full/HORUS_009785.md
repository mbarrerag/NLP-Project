# CONTEMPORARY RURAL RECOMPOSITIONS: TERRITORIAL CONFIGURATION OF THE RIEST DISTRIBUTION OF HIGH CHICAMOCHA, BOYACA, COLOMBIA, 1989-20191

The changes in rural areas based on different development strategies have marked the way in which rural areas are expressed in the territory. From documentary sources, socio-economic information, property situation, changes in land cover, territorial planning plans, and their contrast with oral sources, do you have interviews with the community and a landscape analysis?The rurality of the irrigation district of Alto Chicamocha for the period 1989-2019, marked by the implementation of this infrastructure work, was addressed. The productive activities and uses of the land were identified, on which rural areas are established that reflect the urban-rural contemporary relationship. It is concluded that the role of local communities is central in the reconfiguration of a rurality framed by the advance of tourism and urbanisation
