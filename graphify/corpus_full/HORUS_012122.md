# Open innovation and ownership in the manufacturing sector of Colombia

Companies often need to be open to collaboration with various actors to carry out their innovation projects and, at the same time, they need mechanisms to prevent potential unwanted knowledge leaks that would make it difficult to take advantage of the benefits in the marketing phase. Although recent results show that different appropriation mechanisms (formal or strategic) can have a differentiated effect on the decision to cooperate with external partners, most of these researches circumvent the importance of the context and focus on companies located in developed economies. This paper analyses the behaviour of this relationship in the context of emerging economies and, in particular, on Colombian manufacturing companies. The results show that, while the use of the two types of mechanisms positively affects the likelihood of cooperation of the companies studied, the size of the effect differs between the types of cooperation and also when cooperation is limited to the specific objective of i+d. The implications of the analysis are of interest both for understanding of the business strategy and for innovation policies
