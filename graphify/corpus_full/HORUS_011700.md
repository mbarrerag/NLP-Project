# Economic technical feasibility study of electricity generation from photovoltaic solar energy in the island of San Andrés.

Given the presence of extensive coral reefs, seagrass meadows, mangroves, beaches, open sea and tropical dry forests, which house high endemism points, the region of the San Andrés Archipelago, Providencia and Santa Catalina was declared as a reserve of Seaflower Biosphere in 2000 by UNESCO (Seeflower Foundation, 2019). However, at present, there is not adequate conservation of the key species and strategic ecosystems stated above, by factors among which is the generation of electricity that is made 100% from fossil fuels with an obsolete model that already exceeds 60 years. Through the use of computational tools such as Neplan, Homer Pro and MATLAB, a model is developed for the optimization approach of the diversification of the energy matrix of the island of San Andrés, by means of the implementation of SSFV based on technical restrictions of the current electricity network. It is found that the penetration of SSFV can be up to 28 MW.
