# Letter to Readers

In this first edition of the year 2025 of the journal, a series of articles is compiled that, from different themes and methods according to the expertise of their authors and authors, coincide in the use of the political exercise to investigate, reflect and propose solutions to the discomforts within contemporary problems in the social, economic, environmental and political contexts that transversalize the entire Latin American region. To this end, analysis of public policies, environmental movements, armed conflict, integral reparation and political instability are proposed
