# STRATEGIES AND TACTICS TOWARDS POLICY ON THE LEGISLATION AND REGULARIZATION OF INFORMATION SETTLEMENTS

The logic behind the policies of legalization and regularization of informal settlements proposes that, by granting them legal status, these lands and buildings acquire fiscal responsibilities, without making clear what are the rights they acquire when making part of the formal logic of the city. However, this perspective ignores the origin of these settlements as an alternative arising due to the limitations of a housing market that focuses on a specific sector of the population, ignoring the needs inherent to these places, which generally have to do with places with poor or non-existent public services, lands with threats of risk and a precarization of the ways of inhabiting these places, generating a gap of social injustice with these places.The legalization processes are usually prolonged and inefficient, without achieving improvement of the urban conditions previous of the neighborhood due to the restrictions imposed by the use of the land. Also, these initiatives do not go further to facilitate the management of public services with the corresponding entities, without addressing the conditions of life in an integral way. As a result, the families that do not allow the processes to afford and face the informal responsibilities end generating new ones
