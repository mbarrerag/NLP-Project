# INTRODUCTION TO THE STUDY OF ¿RACIAL ISSUES¿ IN SOCIAL SERVICES IN BRAZIL: STRUGGLES, NEGOTIATIONS AND INVISIBILITIES

Analyzing the production of an area of ​​knowledge is possible through the social and historical, political and economic environment or context, observing the "styles of thought" in which collectives or groups articulated around explanations about the reality of the social world and/or the natural world are configured.The objective of this work is to present how, especially, the struggle between styles of thought in Social Work influences the dynamism of the insertion of studies on ¿racial issues¿ in this area.The chapter is based on a bibliographical research of academic literature on Social Work published in magazines, undergraduate and postgraduate coursework in Social Service programs in Brazil.It appears that the slow dynamism of the study of ¿racial issues¿ in this area is marked by the preponderance of the ¿hegemonic¿ style of thinking in Social Work.The development of the theme "racial issues" is signed by the subjects identified with it, who, at the same time, build complex connections with the "hegemonic" style of thought
