# SYNTHESIS AND OXIDATION STUDIES WITH AMBIENTAL OXYGEN OF 1.3.5-TRIARIL-4,5-DIHYDRO1H- PIRAZOLS

Polysubstituted pyrazole-type compounds are interesting for medicinal chemistry given their potential applications, especially as antioxidant agents1 and anticancer agents,2,3 in addition to their interesting optical characteristics of luminescence at 254 and 365nm.4 For this reason, the search for new synthetic methodologies framed within the precepts of green chemistry for obtaining these heterocyclic nuclei is of interest in order to expand the chemical space. With this in mind, the synthesis of 7 compounds 1.3,5-triaryl-4,5-dihydro-1Hpirazols was carried out by means of a strategy of cyclocondensation of aril hydrazins with chalcona-type compounds with good yields. These compounds were subjected to oxidation processes by various conditions, finding that by means of the air bubble in a weak acid medium, the aromatization of the substrates with yields greater than 43% can be achieved and whose kinetics depends on the electronic nature of the substitutes of the benzene systems linked to the central heterocycle.
