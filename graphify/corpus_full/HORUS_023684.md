# GUIDE FOR THE CRITICAL CARE OF SERIOUS ADULT PATIENTS WITH CORONAVIRUS (COVID-19) IN AMERICAS (LONG VERSION)

This clinical practice guide was developed with the aim of providing recommendations for the management of critical adult patients with CIVID-19 treated in intensive care units (ICUs). It provides recommendations informed by the evidence for the identification of markers and risk factors for mortality of critical patients, control of infection, sample collection, support care (ventilatory and hemodynamic), pharmacological treatment, early rehabilitation, use of diagnostic images, prevention of complications and discharge criteria. The recommendations are addressed to all health personnel who care for patients in the emergency and emergency services and the intensive care unit. It is also developed for use by decision makers and members of government entities related to the management of patients with COVID-19 in the ICUs of the Region of the Americas
