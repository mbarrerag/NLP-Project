# RELATING DYNAMICS FOR THE ACTIVATIVE MANAGEMENT OF TOURISTIC ACTIVITY IN Rural TERRITORIES: THE CASE OF VILLA DE LAWVA - BOYACÁ

Rural tourism as an alternative activity is developed mainly in rural environments, and these activities employ cultural, heritage and natural elements for their development. When the management of this activity is not adequate, undesirable effects are generated in the territories, impacting on the resources, societies and local institutions. To ensure the sustainability of tourism activity in rural areas, the various actors and interest groups must be involved in their planning in order to achieve proper management. The objective of this research is to analyze the relational dynamics between actors and interest groups linked to the development of tourism activities in rural areas in Villa de Leyva -Boyacá, to propose guidelines that guide the active management of the tourist destination. The methodology used in this research is mixed in nature, the mapping of actors will be used, the analysis of social networks, which, together with the triangulation of information, will allow us to deepen in the analysis of the relational dynamics and understand how the social capital is structured among the actors and propose guidelines for the design of strategic actions that contribute to the sustainability of rural tourism activities in the territory
