# COVID-19 and public transport: a review and recommendations applicable to Latin America

Abstract The article presents a review of the transmission of COVID-19 in the context of public transport, with particular application for decision-making in Latin America. Based on recent findings on Covid-19 transmission and the relative importance of each factor (goticles, fomites and aerosol routes) in such transmission, we seek to update the discussion on the topic that has generally been based on `8220; social distance `8221; as a unique parameter of reduction of risk of transmission and expand this vision to integrate ventilation, user behavior (use of mask, eye protection, silence), and travel distance. Recommendations to improve mobility conditions in general are indicated at the end without increasing the risk of Covid-19 contagion
