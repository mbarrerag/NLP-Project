# Strengthening the infrastructure of the Laboratory of Mechanical Design of Physical Engineering through the acquisition of materials and tools for supporting applications for the manufacture of professors and researchers from the University.

The mechanical design laboratory of Physical Engineering, attached to the Faculty of Sciences of the Medellín headquarters of the National University of Colombia; provides services of design, construction, assembly, testing and evaluation of mechanical systems, parts and prototypes, in support of the teaching and research activities of the headquarters. Currently, the laboratory serves as space for the development of the practices and construction of the projects of the students of the subjects Workshop I (Dibujo de Ingeniería) and Workshop II (Mechanical Design). Likewise, it supports research activities of different groups of the Headquarters, especially with the research groups of the Faculty of Sciences, among which are the groups of Opt-Digital and Ceramic and Vitreous Optics Processing. Additionally, the laboratory has been supporting the development of the projects that advance the Semillero de Investigación de Ingenión y Desarrollo de Prototypes. All these projects, works and maintenances that are requested daily from the laboratory, demand the use of tools and the expense of available materials that often implies that these resources are exhausted.
