# I don't want to be another brick in the wall: Transformation of everyday school due to standardized tests: The case KNOW 11°

This paper seeks to describe the transformation of the school reality that is lived in schools as strong auditing mechanisms are made under the figure of standardized tests, specifically the case of the SABER 11th test, in private institutions. I intend to show, from ethnographic observations, texts of teachers, a survey and a free story, that this type of examination is far from being purely technical devices for evaluating and measuring results, but that it brings with it a political and ethical burden that mobilizes discourses and practices at institutional level that transform school daily life under the rhetoric of educational quality. This occurs in a context in which rationality is mostly accepted, and therefore, education is subordinate to the needs of the market, forming homogeneous, competitive, measurable and classifiable subjects. .
