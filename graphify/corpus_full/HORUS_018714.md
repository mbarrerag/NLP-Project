# RIGHT

One of the areas of life most affected by the appalling amount of contagion in such a short time, and above all by the state measures of isolation and confinement that were taken to try to mitigate the effects of the virus itself, was undoubtedly the legal one. The status and mobility of persons, goods and obligations were dramatically modified and restricted. In all of this, the imperative need to try to comply with private agreements, suspend them, modify them, attach them to the extraordinary situation, or even, in the worst case, and subsidiaryly, reinterpret them, terminate them, rescind them or resolve them, in order not to complicate the precarious situation of the majority of citizens, occupied a leading role in the legal and economic sphere
