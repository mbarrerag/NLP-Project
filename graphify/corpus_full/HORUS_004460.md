# Social marketing: evolution and trends

ABSTRACT Social marketing allows organizations to promote behaviours beneficial to society, addressing issues such as health, education, social inclusion and the environment. In addition, it is a tool used to strengthen relationships between companies and their interest groups, especially with their clients. Due to the rise of the topic among researchers, academics and experts, this study contributes to social marketing by examining their evolution and trends. For this purpose, publications recorded between 2000 and 2022 in the Scopus and WoS databases are used. Data are analyzed through tools such as Bibliometrix, Tree of Science and Gephi, and the results are organized through the metaphorical scheme of "the tree of science." This analysis facilitates the identification of classical and structural works, as well as the main trends in the field. Of these, three approaches stand out: the first, the theoretical development of social marketing, the second, the use of strategies in public health and prevention, and the third, the application in nutrition and nutritional behavior programs. Also, a route is proposed for future research in this field
