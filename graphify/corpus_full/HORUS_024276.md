# Paragraphs and Parisians in the New Granada Kingdom of the 18th Century: Between Economic Holgura and the Differences of Self-Suspension.

The chapter shows various ways in which priests, throughout the New Kingdom of Granada, negotiated and solved their economic sustenance. In some cases the situation was comfortable or economic ease. In others, almost the majority, it was very difficult to obtain self-sustaining. It is intended to break the image that the entire clergy lived comfortably and that there was no economic difficulty. By the contario, there are many cases that prove the opposite
