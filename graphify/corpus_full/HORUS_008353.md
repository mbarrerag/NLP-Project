# COMMUNICATION BETWEEN ACTORS AS KEY TO EARLY WARNING SYSTEMS (SAT).

The results of the study were mostly collected and processed by researchers after the agreement between the institutions, and the results of the study ended in 2021. The aim was to qualitatively evaluate the SATs implemented or installed in eight municipalities of Antioquia during the implementation of the agreement, with the aim of answering the question about the functioning of community SATs once the institutions cease to accompany the communities. The results of the research show how the problems of operation begin with the prevalence given to the instrumental components of the system on the communication practices of the inhabitants, the pre-existing organizations and the practices agreed between them. The role of the inhabitants and the SATs are identified as a technological factor that allows for the success of the SATs
