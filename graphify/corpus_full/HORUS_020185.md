# Improvement of the image reconstruction in digital lensless holographic microscopy by scanning of the sample plane

A method to improve the quality of reconstructed images in digital lensless holographic microscopy is presented. The sample plane is scanned to record multiple holograms whose coordinated addition produces an enhanced reconstructed image
