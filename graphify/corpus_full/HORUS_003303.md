# HOMOORIFISMS BETWEEN UNABLE FOUR-FOUR-FOUR-INJECTIVE CASES

In this work, a duality was used between the category of representations of a partially ordered set and the corresponding category of its opposite, to obtain homomorphism spaces between the types of incompatible representations belonging to the pre-injective component of the Auslander-Reiten carcaj associated with the problem of the four subspaces
