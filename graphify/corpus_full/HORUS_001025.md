# Corals as a methodological resource in symphonic band format in training processes

This paper compiles some methodologies based on coral tools and procedures that can be implemented in the form of synphonic bands in formation, starting from the background of the coral texture, its basic foundations in tonal harmony, and the analysis of the original repertoire for band of the twentieth century with choral components. The paper presents the perspectives of international directors on the use of the coral tool in band, and details mechanisms and resources used by teachers and pedagogues close to the Colombian context, revealing various strategies and recommendations that suggest musical process trainers. Subsequently, a proposal is presented based on the instrumentation of 5 chorales by Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach to be implemented in symphonic band. This research concludes by suggesting the interpretation of choral repertoire and the possible positive results in benefits of musical aspects such as soundness, tuning, conductación de las líneas, phrasic and occupational structure of the internals, and ultimately, to generate action plans and communication in the different synphonic programs so that they can know these tools and enrich the knowledge of directors/trainers. .
