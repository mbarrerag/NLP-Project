# Numerical study of the interdepedence of the nusselt, Reynolds and Prandtl dimension numbers.

A numerical study was carried out on the interrelation between the adimensioanle numbers of Reynolds and Prandtl in situations of heat transfer by forced convection in external flow. For each geometry two different flow conditions were explored with the aim of recreating a wide set of separation scenarios. A parametric study was carried out, using five values of the number of Prandtl, between 0.1 < Pr < 500, and four values of the number of Reynolds, between 10 < Re < 10000. The number of local and average Nusselt, together with speed profiles, pressure coefficients and coefficients of friction were analyzed. From the results it was observed that the values of the number of Nusselt averaged on the faces have a maximum value pra Pr = 50 between 10 < Re < 1000.
