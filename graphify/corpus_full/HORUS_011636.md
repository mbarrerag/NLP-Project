# RESISTANCE, GIVING DIGITY: THE AUTONOMOUS INDIGENOUS INTERCULTURE IN THE FIELD OF HIGHER EDUCATION IN COLOMBIA

The book is composed of three sections.The first will explain how the so-called indigenous question has been reconfigured historically according to the horizons of struggle defined by the indigenous peoples. It will highlight the way that the indigenous peoples have managed to provide emancipating content to initially colonizing institutions such as the Castilian language, human rights, reservations and indigenous lobbying. This first approach will show why the disputes of the indigenous peoples of Cauca can be characterized as a process that has assumed autonomy as an objective of their demands, but also, as a means to achieve them, and that today it poses interculturality as an objective of their demands and as a means to achieve them.The second section will present the inequalities, exclusions and injustices suffered by the indigenous peoples in the field of higher education in Colombia. It will show the governmental actions that in terms of ethnoeducation and affirmative actions have attempted to alleviate such situations, and the response of the communities associated with the constitution of the SEIP and the UAIIN. It will be emphasized that the responses offered by the State always go back to the horizons of autonomy and interculturality, that have been generated by the third point of the process
