# Path Loss Measurements in the 60 GHz Frequency Band in a Greenhouse

This study characterizes path loss in the 60 GHz band in a tomato greenhouse and reveals significant losses due to ambient and vegetation. The experimental results are compared to applicable propagation models, showing varying levels of correspondence and achieving a satisfactory theoretical description. The findings improve the understanding of millimeter wave propagation in agricultural environments, which can support 5G network deployments
