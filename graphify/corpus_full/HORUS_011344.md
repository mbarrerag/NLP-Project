# LABORAL OPPORTUNITIES AND RURAL-UBANA YOUTH MIGRATION.

Rural youth have high levels of vulnerability in dealing with migratory phenomena in search of better living conditions. In Colombia, these migration processes are mainly framed by the lack of job opportunities. In the territorial sphere, youth unemployment is very representative. Through participatory diagnoses and the methodology of public policy analysis proposed by Peter Knoepfel et al. (2006), which allows analysis of interactions between actors, resources and institutional rules, the relevance of the 2015 Ordinance to address-mitigating the lack of job opportunities and reducing migration rates is addressed. It is concluded that public policy requires a codification that involves conceptual, substantial and operational reconstruction to increase the job supply and thus reduce youth migration; on the other hand, young people require greater participation in local political, social and economic spaces
