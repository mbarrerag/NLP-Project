# Environmental sustainability of the franchise supply chain: an agent-based model for analysing the influence of governance mechanisms on green practices

This research finds in the study of franchise networks an enabling field to contribute to the improvement of the environmental effects of production and human consumption. The analysis focuses on the effect of governance mechanisms present in the franchising-franchised relationship on environmental sustainability and a simulation model based on agents to understand the phenomenon is carried out. This model simulates a fitness landscape determined by the interdependencies (K) of environmental practices (N) for different types of franchises, classified according to the degree of implementation of these practices. Thus, franchisees straggled, followers and leaders, configure each one of a fitness landscape, where in a framework defined by the configuration of formal and informal governance mechanisms, four modes of governance are determined (confinfidence, relational network, leadership and formal governance).
