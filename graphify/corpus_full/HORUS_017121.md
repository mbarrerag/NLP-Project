# SCHEMATIC PROPOSAL FOR GENERATION OF MULTIPLES DESIGNS OF KEY STANDARDS BASED ON ASSOCIATION RULES

Part of a store’s success depends on its ability to understand the customer’s behavior and its ability to react to changes in consumer habits. Data analysis of the purchases made by customers is crucial to search for new sales opportunities. Therefore, it is important to find a suitable solution for the design of a store. In this work, the problem approach and a schematic proposal are presented to generate multiple designs for a store, which includes (i) the mining of partnership rules, (ii) the total number of units purchased from products, (iii) the selection of interesting partnership rules (those that exceed certain thresholds for some of the measures that characterize them) and (iv) a set of restrictions that state that certain products or product categories should not be close (according to the sections of a store)
