# INSURANCE CARE IN THE TRANSITION PLAN OF THE HOSPITALIZED PATIENT IN THE MENTAL HEALTH UNIT: SCOPING REVIEW

At present, mental illness represents one of the main challenges in the health agenda, mainly due to the gap in access to health services and the high burden of global morbidity, also, is the phenomenon of hospital reentry caused mainly by the dismissal of mental illness, which is related to a worse prognosis and has an impact on the family, social and work environment, so that interventions based on evidence and cost-effective, as well as interventions focused on rehabilitation and reintegration into the community, being the transition from hospitalization to home care.Sequrent strategy that contributes to reducing the gaps in access to services, favoring continuity in care and promoting individual and family self-care behaviors.
