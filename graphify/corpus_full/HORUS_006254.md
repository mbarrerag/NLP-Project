# Development of a Colombian start based Starbon® type material for its use as catalyst in fatty acid sterification

In the heterogenous analysis, mesoporous materials are attractive supports to facilitate access and mobility of large-sized molecules, thus improving yields in various reactions. Among different materials, Starbon® materials are mesoporous carbons that can be synthesized advantageously from bio-based sources, and then activated with acid to be used as catalysts in sterification reactions. Conveniently, Starbons® are produced using starch with high amylose content, which guarantees a high volume of mesopores due to the availability of the amylose molecules during the synthesis process. In search of the valorization of residual currents, it is interesting to study the use of starch derived from roots and tubers common in the synthesis of these materials.
