# THE INTER-AMERICAN CENTRE FOR LIVING AND PLANNING - FIVE- 1952-1972.

The notion of social housing in Latin America in the mid-20th century was determined by rightful situations. Among them, the intervention of the United States in the political and economic landscape of Latin American countries, the urbanization of the population, and the institutionalization of housing. In this measure, urban planning and housing became pressing more state-owned that led governments to assume a role as direct promoters and implementers of housing policies, in search of solutions to urban poverty and misery. This context, which determined social housing as a serious Latin American problem, framed the multidisciplinary activities of the Inter-American Center for Housing and Planning CINVA- with great diffusion in the region
