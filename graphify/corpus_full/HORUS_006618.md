# Multimodal model for oil well production forecasting

This research paper presents a contribution in two areas of time series study in the context of the production of oil wells, with these classifications to identify faults in wells, and production forecasts. The data set used corresponds to the production of oil wells, including multimodal information such as numerical data, images and text for each time point. In the classification of time series, the prediction of faults in the next time step is addressed, achieving an accuracy of 61.3% with a multimodal model connected to an LSTM layer. In time series forecasts, multimodal models with LSTM layers stand out, surpassing non-modal models and ARIMA implementations in quarterly and bi-annual predictions, presenting an average absolute percentage error of 8% reaching 2% in specific cases.
