# ASSESSMENT OF THE EFFECTIVENESS OF UCHUVA CAPACITY FOR THE REMOTION OF TARTRAZIN

At present, water contamination by synthetic dyes represents a significant environmental challenge. Tartrazine (TA), an anionic dye derived from oil widely used in the food industry, is associated with human health risks such as asthma, dermatitis, hyperactivity and possible carcinogenic effects. In search of sustainable solutions, this study evaluates the efficacy of the uchuva layer (CU), an agricultural byproduct, as adsorbent to remove TA from aqueous solutions
