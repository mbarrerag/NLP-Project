# ANTI-QUEKE CACHO, SELECTING FINAL MATERIALS WITH SAFE AND AROMA FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CULTI-CUT

the booklet entitled "Antioqueño Cocoa, the Selection of fine materials with flavor and aroma for the benefit of the grower", prepared in the framework of the research project entitled: "Auntar efforts to identify, characterize and evaluate the genetic resource of Colombian cocoa End of Aroma, to form the CECE (Elite Collection of Special Cocoa) for the Department of Antioquia", of the Special Cooperation Agreement No. 4600003348 concluded between the department of antioquia secretary of agriculture and rural development, university of antioquia, corporación universitaria lasalista and fedecacao. This project was financed by the Department of Antioquia through the Regalías General System, was managed and administered by the University of Antioquia. The purpose of the booklet is to socialize the results of the research project to the growers and technicians of the field of Cacao of Antioquia, Colombia and the world, researchers and genetic improvers of Cacao, university students and companies interested in the cultivation of Cacao
