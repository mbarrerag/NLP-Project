# Nonlinear analysis applied to mixed problems in Partial Differential Equations

Mixed problems in dispersive equations are problems involving initial and frontier conditions simultaneously, so this is useful for modelling physical phenomena that not only depend on temporal evolution, but also on conditions at the spatial boundary of the domain. We focus on the specific case of the fifth order Korteweg-de Vries equation (KdV), a relevant extension of the well-known third order KdV equation, which has been studied in literature. On the other hand, nonlinear elliptical problems with values at the border, serve to model stationary phenomena. In this project we focus on the study of existence and multiplicity of solutions to elliptical problems involving the phi-Laplatian operator, which has been generating interest in the area due to its presence in several applications. Thirdly, we propose to develop an algorithm to approximate the transformation of the medial axis of open, narrow and related regions in the complex plane
