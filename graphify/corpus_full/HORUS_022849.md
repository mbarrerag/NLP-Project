# SOFTWARE AND HARDWARE TOOLS TO IMPLEMENT THE INTERNET OF THINGS IN AGRICULTURAL APPLICATIONS

This book emerged as an initiative of the University Corporation of Asturias and the SUMMA RED, with the last idea of establishing reflections and dialogues between the social, economic and administrative sciences, that allow the reader to delve into different aspects of the disciplines that are contained in the title of the work, and, contributing to the development of his critical thinking through arguments, studies and analysis. Thus, the present one is structured into two main sections: the first one, focuses on social sciences, addressing such important and exciting contents as the gamification in virtual learning environments, perspectives on virtual education in times of COVID-19, factors that influence student permanence, inclusion and the right to education, emotional design as a factor of educational quality, among other topics that will allow the reader to understand various problems from different edges; and the second, it relates to economic and administrative sciences, in which issues such as business intelligence, emotional finance and selection of portfolios of investment, research around financial and accounting management, model of business of the social enterprise, among others, generate in this way, a constant invitation to deepen on them and reflect on the XXI century
