# Inclusions Between Spaces of Interpolating Sequences for Banach Bargmann-Fock Spaces

The purpose of the present short article is to provide inclusions between generalized Banach Bargmann-Fock spaces in several complex variables and their associated interpolating sequences. Also, we provide results on the invariance of these spaces under restrictions and extensions
