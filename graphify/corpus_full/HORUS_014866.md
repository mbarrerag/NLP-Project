# OVER THREAT YEARS OF PORTS FROM NATIONAL UNIVERSITY OF COLOMBIA TO THE KNOWLEDGE OF LIVING CONDITIONS OF OUR ANTI-RESOURCES FROM BIOARCHEOLOGY

A critical tour is conducted on the contributions, advances and perspectives of biological anthropology in general and of bioarchaeology in particular in the Department of Anthropology of the National University of Colombia, emphasizing the contributions that teachers, graduates, graduates and students have made to the discipline in the country
