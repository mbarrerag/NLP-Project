# WAYUU WOMEN, WATER AND WATER RIGHTS IN MINING CONTEXT, GUajIRA, COLOMBIA

The chapter is structured as follows: in the first part I present conceptual discussions based on the perspectives of feminist political ecologys of water, relational ontologys and environmental justice. The second part will focus on territorial, cultural and environmental transformations of water caused by the Cerrejón mine. The third part will focus on the resistance and dynamics of fmw to defend the territories of water and its ways of life. Finally, I will discuss the implications of a relational perspective of environmental and territorial justice that allows the continuity of spirituality and the materiality of life and guarantees the existence of humans and non-humans
