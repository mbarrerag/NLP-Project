# A MODEL OF QUALITY MANAGEMENT CENTRED IN THE PATIENT AND HIS FAMILY IN CARE

This paper focuses on the development of a proposal to implement a quality management model, with the patient and family-centred care process in the intensive care unit as the central focus. It reflects the expert recommendations and best practices that have been formulated by different certification initiatives to achieve high quality standards, which allow users to meet their needs, taking into account their preferences and service expectations
