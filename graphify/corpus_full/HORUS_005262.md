# FUTURE CONSTRUCTION INCLUSIVE: DIALOGUE ON HISTORY CATEDS WITH GENDER PERSPECTIVE IN PUBLIC UNIVERSITIES

This dialogue with colleague Nayeli Andrade Fajardo related to the experience of the chairs of History with a gender perspective in Colombia. A comparison was established between the University of Magdalena and the National University of Colombia-Sede Bogotá. The dialogue deepened in the agencies that allowed the establishment of spaces in the two universities, the topics they deal with and the need to extend this type of chairs to all departments of history of the country
