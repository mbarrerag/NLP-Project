# “Move well, dancer, and let joy overtake you.” Dance, affections and knowledge at Son Academy in Havana

This research studies closely the affections that students shape during their dance practices and their recurring encounters in a dance academy focused on “Latinos” rhythms, located in Bogotá, Colombia. Through somatic attention and through an ethnographic and autoethnographic perspective, it approaches what people do with dance, from and with their bodies, in the interaction with music, knowledge, conditions of participation and certain narratives, imaginary and discourses about race, gender, sexuality, age, social class, among others. In this particular situation – the academy as a pedagogical setting for formalization of a popular practice and craft workshop of the movement – the dance is complex and multiform, and asks to be understood in its affective, historical, socio-cultural and commercialized dimension.
