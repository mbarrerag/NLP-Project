# Crystallographic, electrical and magnetic analysis of double perovskit type material Yb2FeMnO6

The synthesis and characterization of the type material perovskita YbFe0.5Mn0.5O3 has been performed, produced by two methods: solid state reaction and modified Pechini. Through techniques of X-ray diffraction (DRX) and Rietveld refinement, it was determined that the material crystallizes in two phases: a majority hexagonal phase, representing 93.9% in the case of solid reaction synthesis and 96.1% in the case of Pechini modified with P63/m spatial group (#185), and a minority orthorombic phase, which constitutes 6.1% and 3.9%, respectively, in the Pnma spatial group (#62). The hexagonal phase exhibits a bipyramidal configuration, with inclinations of the mean beam Fe and Mn with respect to the O3 and O4 oxygens of approximately 119°, while the orthorombic phase, with a modified segment, has an octaedral configuration. The electron scanning microscopia (SEM) revealed that the sizes of the mean grain were 0.746 μm for solid reaction and 150 nm for Pechini, which were used. . The synthesis and characterisation of the perovskite-type material YbFe0.5Mn0.5O3 were carried out out, using two methods: solid-state reaction and modified Pechini. Through X-ray diffaction (XRD) technologies and Rietveld refining, it was determined that the material crystallizes into two phases: a majority hexagonal phase, representing 93.9% in the case of the solid-state synthesis and 96.1% for the modified Pechini method with space group P63/m (#185), and a minority alternative phase, constituting 6.1% and 3.9%, respectively, in the space group Pnma (#62). modified Pechini, which are typical values for a semiconductor material. Finally, magnetic characterisation shown that the material exhibits weak ferromagnetic behavior, competing with an antiferromagnetic orderring, and displaying a magnetically frustrated response due to the disorder in the magnetic moments, evidenced by the irreversible exporter of magnetization in the ZFC compared to the FC curves. It was also observed that the samples produced via the Pechini method present features not observed in those synthesized by the solid-state reaction, such as a superparametic tendency and an exchange beias effect at low temperatures, attributed to the interaction between ferromagnetic and antiferromatic nanoparticles
