# Falling of leaf litter and nutrient return in different vegetable communities of Northeast Mexico

Summary The leaf litter that accumulates on the forest floor and its subsequent decay play an important role in maintaining the productivity of the different terrestrial ecosystems by constituting the main flow of nutrients to the soil.
