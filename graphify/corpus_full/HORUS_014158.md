# Barrancabermeja: the refinery and the city. Forms and mechanisms of construction of environmental and spatial injustices

In Barrancabermeja, the refinery is as old as the city. Opened in 1922, when it was just beginning the oil exploitation in the fields adjacent to Magdalena Medio, it is a milestone of the oil map of the country and also the main source of employment for the inhabitants of the city. The refinery has attracted several generations of oil workers in the Magdalena Medio, but in turn, it constitutes today for the inhabitants an important source of pollution and a generator of technological and sanitary risks, because of its location within the urban area. The environmental and territorial regulation imposes certain restrictions and a follow-up to the environmental impacts generated, in particular the dumping of waters, sludges and contaminated gases.
