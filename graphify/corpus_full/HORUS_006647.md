# Estimation of the economic growth impacts of tax reform for equality and social justice: analysis through a model of deterministic dynamic general equilibrium for Colombia

This paper updates the dynamic general equilibrium model of Fedevelopment1 with the purpose of analyzing the possible impacts on economic growth resulting from the tax reform approved in 2022. The results show that the growth impacts depend on the use given to the new company. An exclusive use to increase investment in public infrastructure could increase the level of aggregate production by 0.035 pps during 2023-2026. On the other hand, it is only used to increase current government expenditure or to finance direct transfers to non- Rican households could reduce the level of aggregate production by 0.057 pps and 0.092 pps, respectively. In turn, an exercise of robustness is carried out with the general computable equilibrium model of Fedevelopment.
