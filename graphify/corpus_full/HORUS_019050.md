# EPISTEMOLOGICAL APTENTS IN GILBERT SIMONDON PHILOSOPHY

In this article we propose that Gilbert Simondon's philosophy contains some sketches of an epistemology, which is characterized by moving away from substantialism, positivism and also relativism to place itself in a different place. The author proposes a redefinition of human sciences based on the alllagmatic concept, which defines it as a theory of operations, since he considers that science has focused on studying structures and not operations, thus falling into substantialisms. Alllagmatics is not then a new science equal to others, it is a methodological approach to knowledge based on the operations applicable in different domains such as the physico-chemical, the psycho-physiological and the psychosocial. An initial approach to epistemology and criticisms of positivism is presented, and then to develop the definition of alllagmatics and their roots in cybernetics. By way of conclusion, the aspects found on epistemology in this author are highlighted
