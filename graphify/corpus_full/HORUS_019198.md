# THE MUSIC FOR FRANCISCO GONZALEZ GUITAR

When making a list of Colombian composers who wrote concert music for guitar during the previous decades, the name of Francisco González inevitably emerged (1954-). In most of his work it is the traditional Colombian Andean music that has marked his starting point for the composition. In order to achieve a close approach to the life and music of this Colombian composer we highlight several interviews with him between the months of February and March 2021. In this article they synthesize first, aspects of his biography provided in these dialogues and, in a second section, aspects of some of his works for guitar, which exemplify his original personal language.
