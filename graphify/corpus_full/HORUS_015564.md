# VALIDITY AND CONFIABILITY OF THE INSTRUMENT ADOPTION OF THE FAMILY CARE ROLE OF THE CRONE PATIENT IN THE USE OF AROL-M MEDICINAL PRODUCTS

ABSTRACT People with chronic health conditions are one of the greatest challenges facing the health system due to its magnitude, morbidity and mortality, premature disability and, due to the complexity and high cost of their treatment (Castañeda-Porras 2018).These patients, have an impact of the physical, emotional, intellectual, social and spiritual functioning, which is projected to the family caregiver.It is necessary to recognize that the family caregiver is involved in the process of using medicines implicitly when assuming a responsibility with being cared for and explicitly when accepting a treatment involving the pharmacological therapeutic regime.Among the instrumental responsibilities of great impact is the use of medications, process seen as an activity but that is not only that, but that it demands a series of knowledge and abilities to generate beneficial results in the person with chronic disease or major adult polymedicated. to perform validation of construct, convergent, 0.5% and evaluate the internal consistency of the instrument of Adoption of the caregiver role in the use of medications (ROL-M). psychometric validation study conducted between August 2020 and February 2022. Sample of 321 family caregivers with chronic disorders
