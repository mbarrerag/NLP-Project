# EFFECT OF THERMAL AND NON-THERMAL PRETREATMENTS ON THE PHYSICOCHEMICAL, SENSORY, MICROBIOLOGICAL, AND BIOACTIVE FEATURES OF AN ANTHOCYANIN-RICH BEVERAGE CONTAINING A PREBIOTIC FIBER

The aim of this work was to evaluate the performance of different pretreatments on an anthocyanin-rich fruit beverage containing berries, banana, and a prebiotic fiber (Orafti P95), in terms of the quality and bioactive composition of the product. After formulation, the beverage was subjected to three pretreatments: pasteurization (85°C x 5 min), commercial sterilization (121°C x 15 min), and ultrasound treatment (60%, 80%, and 100% x 15 min, 3.5) which allows the addition of beneficial probiotic culture
