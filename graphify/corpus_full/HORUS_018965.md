# OLISTIC TERRITORIES

The text is a partial synthesis of the result of the research \"Arquitectura y ciudad: análisis, proyectos, transformaciones¿, carried out in Medellin, in the Faculty of Architecture of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia and describes an educational project carried out with colleagues of the research group \"Transepto¿ and with students of the Faculty, which claims to generate a system of reflections on the future of our cities and the intense cultural and ecological responsibility that our generation of professionals and teachers must transmit to the youngest, with the hope of transforming our cities into harmonious, healthy, ecological and self-sufficient spaces
