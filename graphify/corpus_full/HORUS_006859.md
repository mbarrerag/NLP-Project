# Urban management of the area of influence of the Regiotram of the West. The case of the municipalities of Funza, Mosquera, Madrid and Facatativá

The development of transport infrastructures generates impacts on the use and occupation of the land, so it is necessary to coordinate with the planning and planning of the territory, to promote and encourage urban developments under an idea of territorial balance. In the Colombian context, there is a limited urban management that implements instruments of territorial planning in areas of influence of the transport infrastructures, despite the potential to implement territorial models such as the Transport-oriented Development – DOT. This TFM aims to propose the application of instruments of management and financing of the territory, in view of the possible impacts of the proximity train of the West Region in its area of influence on the municipalities of Funza, Mosquera, Madrid and Facatativá. The document starts from a conceptual framework and the state of art, to diagnose the occupation of the land, to identify the trends of territorial impact from the application of the Nodo model - place and with it, to determine the potential of development oriented to the transport., Madrid and Facatativá. The document contains a conceptual framework and the state of the art, to diagnose land occupation, identity territorial impact trends from the application of the Node - Place model and thereby determine the potential for transportation-oriented development. Obtaining as a result a proposal for the implementation of management and financing instruments, with the prioritization of urban planning actions that take advantage of and guide the impacts returning from the execution of the commuter train according to its territorial and regulatory characteristics
