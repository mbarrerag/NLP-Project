# The labour productivity of the knowledge-intensive teleworker in the service sector and its determinants

Changes in labour productivity can strongly impact organizational performance and, consequently, affect the profits of companies. Therefore, improving understanding of the factors that promote or hinder workers' productivity has been a subject of great interest among professionals, managers and academics for many years. Currently, new challenges have arisen, such as the need to determine the impact of telework on work performance in the area of knowledge work. The theoretical framework used for its study also brings together different thought streams, such as human resource management, industrial and organizational psychology, industrial engineering, economics, among others. Consequently, the methodology employed in this research is based on an empirical, cross-sectional and mixed-focus study, contemplating both qualitative and quantitative strategies for information collection and analysis. Thus, this work assumes an associative-predictive strategy, since its purpose was to explore the functional relationship between labour productivity and job satisfaction and a set of potential predictive variables.-- Contracadas
