# Design and construction of a secure system for the implementation of drivers in unmanned aerial vehicles

The unmanned aerial vehicles, UAV, as a new component of the aeronautical system, have begun to have an important popularity in the performance of exploration tasks, aerial search, precision spraying in agriculture, aerial photography for topographic studies and many other applications. Therefore, a great research interest in this type of technology has been generated thanks to the advantages offered by this type of robots compared to conventional helicopters or aircraft in terms of manoability, portability, control and costs.
