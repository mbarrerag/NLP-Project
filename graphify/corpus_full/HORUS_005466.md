# SOLUTION OF INTEGRAL EQUIPMENT OF FIRST-SHIFT FREDHOLM LINE PROGRAMMING

The resolution of first-rate Fredholm equations is considered fundamental in various areas of applied sciences. It is widely recognized that this type of equations poses a "badly conditioned" problem, which implies that minimal changes in data can cause significant alterations in results. Therefore, standard methods such as Wiener's parametric filtering method and Tikhonov's regularization method are used to address them. In this work, a different approach is taken when considering the resolution of the integral equation as a linear optimization problem. As a result, solutions are less sensitive to atypical disturbances. In addition, the introduction of penalty terms in the objective function of the optimization problem to improve the accuracy and stability of the solution. The effectiveness of the proposed method in restoring distorted images is demonstrated. Finally, our approach is compared to others by several examples
