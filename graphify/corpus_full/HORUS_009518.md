# Proposal of an alternative for the management of deviations in an intraday dispatch in Colombia.

Currently, the Colombian energy market operates with an economic dispatch carried out since the day before the operation, this means that since the previous day the Generators acquire a generation commitment for the following day, however, these commitments are sometimes not met, these generation failures are known as deviations and may have criminalization. Progress is being made in regulatory studies for the implementation of an intraday dispatch in Colombia, which emits supply and demand signals in a short time, and allows the supply of generation in several auctions during the day of the operation. However, the deviations will continue to exist due to equipment failures, delays to enter into operation, among others. This is why this work proposes an alternative for the management of deviations in an intraday dispatch in Colombia. To achieve this, a review of the operation of deviations in some countries was carried out, selecting a group of deviation schemes under criteria of interest, strategies for mitigation of deviations by a Generator Agent were evaluated, and with these elements a proposal for alternative of deviation management in an intraday dispatch in Colombia was consolidated
