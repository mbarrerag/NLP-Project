# TYPE-I SUPERCONDUCTIVTY: SPIKE VORTEX STATE

The spike vortex-state is a meta-stable state that is found lowering the magnetic fields near to the normal-superconducting transition in conventional type I samples for a determined Ginzburg-Landau parameter ¿ range. We can say that the spike state is originated by the expulsion of vortex-bubbles originated by deformation of a conventional giant-vortex, this process takes some nanoseconds, and occurs at an upper single field H3. The spike state is analyzed solving the time-dependent-Ginzburg-Landau equations numerically en FC and ZFC processes. We show the extrusion process for several geometries and selected a ¿ for a genuine type-I superconductor, we believe that the sharp edges play an important role in the formation and stability of the spike state because increasing the number of edges in the sample, will be easier the transition to the superconducting state
