# Tomographic and endoscopic analysis of the measurements of the lower cornets and their relationship with the perception of nasal obstruction in the pediatric population attending the otolaryngology consultation at the Hospital de la Misericordia Foundation between 2017 and 2021.

A retrospective study of cases and controls that seeks the relationship between the percentage of occupancy of the head of the lower corneas by endoscopic analysis and the subjective measure of nasal obstruction using the analogous visual scale (EVA) in the paediatric population aged 6 to 17 years evaluated at the Hospital de la Misericordia Foundation in Bogotá, Colombia. A sample of 50 cases and 50 controls was collected. Among the cases, 90% of patients had a score of EVA between 5 and 7, with 5 being the most frequent score (68%). The average occupancy of the lower corneas in the control group was 47.3% (DE +/- 15.8) for the right cornet and 46.4% (DE +/- 12.3) for the left cornet. For the case group, the average occupation was 49.4 (DE +/- 19.9) for the right cornet and 49.6% (SD +/- 14.9) for the left cornet. There was no significant impact of the nasal cycle in any of the patients.
