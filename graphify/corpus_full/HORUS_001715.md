# Measures of dissimilarity of dynamic processes type Lotka-Volterra in complex systems

This paper examines dynamic processes like Lotka-Volterra between species with interactions described by networks. In the first part, an introduction to the basic concepts of network theory is presented, as well as examples of dynamic processes such as diffusive transport or synchronisation, to introduce the mathematical model of Lotka-Volterra for two interacting species and its numerical solution. Subsequently, the characterization of a parameter that determines the evolution of the populations of the interacting species and how the changes of that parameter account for different dynamic processes. With this, a description of the dissimilarity metric is proposed and explored to quantify the effects of the changes that define a second process, when a reference process is established to compare. Also, the application of that measure is analyzed for a series of dynamic processes modeled by networks with three interacting species comparing both structural differences and different types of interspecific relationships is studied in the final part, with the analysis of the application of that measure for a series of dynamic processes modelled by networks (the proposed disimple changes when the process is introduced by the differentintegrating processes or by the evolution of the differentiforms., the classification of a parameter that determines the evolution of the population of the interaction categories and how changes in this parameter account for dynamic processes is explored. With this in mind, a dissimilarity metric is proposed and explored to quantitative the effects of changes that defines a second process, by establishing a reference process with which to compare. The application of such a measure is also analyzed for a series of dynamic processes modeled by networks with three interaction sectors comparing both structural differences and different types of interspecific relationships.
