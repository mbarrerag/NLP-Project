# THE THESIS OF INTERTHEORICAL INCONMENSURABILITY: CHALLENGES AND IMPLICATIONS

This work is born of my interest in the central problems of the philosophy of science, especially those related to the explanation of the social aspects of science in conjunction with the ideas of progress and eleccio? of theories. As is well known, Kuhn is recognized as a brilliant expositor of the first and a mediocre theotheotheology of the second. Such a judgment has never seemed appropriate to me, so I decided to find out if it was possible to find in the theorie of the science of Kuhn elements that would allow to rethink such a dramatic verdict.
