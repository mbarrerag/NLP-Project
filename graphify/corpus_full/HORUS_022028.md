# Equivalence classes of skew dyck paths modulo some patterns

For any pattern (formula presented), we enumerate the equivalence classes of skew Dyck paths, where two skew Dyck paths of the same semilength are p-equivalent whenever the positions of the occurrences of the pattern p are the same. In this paper we use generating functions, bijective arguments, and recurrence relations to obtain the main results
