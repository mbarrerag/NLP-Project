# Effect of citric acid and calcium on the quality and service life of the minimally processed banana (Paradisiacal Musa L.)

The aim of this study was to evaluate the behaviour of quality attributes and respiratory activity of banana slices stored at 13 ± 2 °C and 92 ± 2 % relative humidity (HR), simulating marketing conditions. Bananas were selected, washed, hygienised and sliced, then divided into four groups to which different treatments were applied: 1 % (p/v) calcium chloride (CaCl2) + 0.92 % (p/v) citric acid; 1 % (p/v) CaCl2 alone; 0.92 % (p/v) citric acid alone; and fruit without citric acid or CaCl2 called control. Fisicochemical analysis methods were used under established methodologies such as ICONTEC standards. Values of pH, titrible acidity, color, total soluble solids, weight loss and respiratory intensity were significantly reduced, every two days and until day 17.
