# Effects of pulmonary rehabilitation programmes on aerobic capacity and peripheral muscle strength in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis: systematic review

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a chronic disease with limited medical treatment. In advanced stages of the disease an impact on the patient's quality of life is observed. Pulmonary rehabilitation (RP), through physical training, is a non-pharmacologic strategy that helps to decrease symptoms and improve the tolerance to exercise in patients with chronic lung disease. However, evidence and research on the effects of rehabilitation in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis are insufficient. Due to the above, this review seeks to know what are the effects of the programs on the improvement of aerobic capacity and muscle strength in patients with IPF. following the recommendations of Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISE) patients was performed the search for clinical trials in the following databases: Medline/PubMed.Excerpta Medica data BASE (EMBASE 32. clinical trial cinahl (ebsco) lilacs (breme), google scholar and plinical trails.gov.
