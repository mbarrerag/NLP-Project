# Venezuelan mixed flow in times of pandemic and uncertainty and the Colombian Temporary Protection Statute as a better alternative to international protection

The Temporary Protection Statute for Venezuelan Migrants, under temporary protection regime, (Decreto 216 of 2021 and resolution 091 of 2021) constitutes one of the fundamental components of the Colombian government's public migration policy, given the arrival in Colombia of a mixed Venezuelan flow of more than two million people in less than 5 years, as a result of a systemic failure of their country of origin.
