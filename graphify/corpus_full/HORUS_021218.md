# It contributes to the value chain of the fruit of Physalis peruviana by developing pharmaceutical and cosmetic products with the potential to be transferred to the national industry.

In search of new treatments and applications of our biodiversity, this proposal seeks to give added value to the fruit of uchuva (Physalis peruviana), through the development of products based on the sustainable use of our natural resources. On the one hand, the formulation in a polymeric nanoparticle system, of which it is expected to maintain or even improve the antidiabetic activity of the extract, due to the therapeutic potential of these systems to reach the site of action and control the speed of release of the asset, the biological activity will be evaluated by in vitro tests of the inhibition of the enzyme amylase. This development of the phytotherapeutic is carried out in the framework of a doctoral thesis.
