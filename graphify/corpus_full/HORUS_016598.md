# MOTHER-CHANGE METHOD WITH DOMICILARY OXYGENOTHERAPY: MATERNAL CULTURAL CARE, A LOOK FROM DISEASE

40% of premature children are discharged from neonatal units and families are faced with an unexpected situation. Raising a premature child and low birth weight in the kangaroo position in the home with oxygen is not an easy task for mothers is an extreme care, it means a challenge that will be compensated with child growth. to describe the meaning of the cultural maternal care of the premature and/or low birth weight infant with home oxygen. qualitative study with ethnographic approach, carried out in an outpatient Kangaro programme of a Bogotá hospital Colombia. Prior informed consent and institutional approval, 8 mothers participated, the dense description or saturation of the sample was given with 21 interviews recorded in the homes and transcribed textually. For the collection and analysis of the information was used the theory of the diversity and universality of the cultural care of Madeleine Leininger and the in-depth interview of James Spradley.
