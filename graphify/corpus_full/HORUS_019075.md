# Selection and analysis of MiPymes business variables that influence ‘technical efficiency’ levels: Application of an estimation methodology to a sample of construction and civil engineering companies in Boyacá

The MiPymes (Micro, small and medium-sized business) Colombians compete for having a space in the market, but to reach this objective, one of the aspects that must evaluate and improve is the level of efficiency in their corporate processes so it is vital to have agile tools to use for their diagnosis. In this sense, the present research explores the different methodologies that allow to carry out analysis of efficiency and, after deepening conceptually and carrying out a careful bibliographic review on this topic, it was identified that the methodology DEA (Data Envelopment Analysis) is suitable to use in the present research due to its flexibility in terms of the use of data and versatility in the definition of the variables of entry and exit. It is necessary to specify that the DEA methodology is an optimal tool for carrying out the evaluation of efficiency ‘technical’ between a group of companies through the generation of an optimal border with the data, and thus to be able to make comparisons to the companies in the decision making of the evaluated scenarios. In order to validate this methodology, it was collected information from 14 engineering companies located in the department of Boyacá (Colombia) during the year 2019, and in which it was dedicated to the construction of 2 companies. and, after deepening conceptually and driving a careful literature review on this topic, it was identified that the DEA (Data Development Analysis) methodology is acceptable for use in this research due to its flexibility in the use of data and versatility in the definition of input and output variables. It is necessary to specify that the DEA methodology is an alternative tool to perform the evaluation of technical' efficiency as a set of companies throughout the generation of an optimal frontier with the data, and then be able to make compatibilitys that allow guidance the company in making decisions on the evaluated scenarios. In order to validate this methodology, information was collected from the 14 engineering companies located in the department and agencies located in the province and management of decisions on the evaluated scenarios
