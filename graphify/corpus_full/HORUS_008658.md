# IDENTIFICATION OF PARASITIONS ASSOCIATED TO MELLIFERA APIS IN THE CAUCA VALLEY

Apis mellifera is a species of Hymenoptera with sting, one of the largest pollinators and producer of different commercial products such as propolis, wax, honey, apitoxin, pollen, among others. The objective of this research was to analyze 496 bees from an apiary of three beehives for the identification of associated parasites. The collected specimens were deposited in bottles of wide mouth with alcohol of 70%, the mites were fixed in plate with medium hole and were classified using taxonomic keys reported in the literature.
