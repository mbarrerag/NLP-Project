# CHANGES IN THE QUALITY ATKINS ́ FRESH TREATMENTS OF THE MANGO ́TOMMY ATTINS ́ FRONT ́ DEBT DEBT TO DIFFERENT TREATMENTS OF INFECTION.

The minimum mango processing accelerates its perecibility and generates environmental impact. In this work, the effect of individual and combined treatments of alternative disinfectants such as: short wavelength ultraviolet (UV) light, edible coating based on aloe vera (RC), ultrasound (US) and a traditional disinfectant (sodium hypochlorite, CL), on the post-harvest quality of 'Tommy Atkins' mango freshly cut, stored under refrigeration (5 °C and 70% HR), packed in PET boxes, for 15 days. Every 3 days, its quality attributes were analyzed. It was found that there is a significant effect of the type of disinfection treatment and the storage time on the physical chemical attributes, color, native microbial population and sensory acceptance by the fruit consumer. The application of ultraviolet light, in combination with the coating of aloe vera, maintained the firmness of the fruit, produced minor variations of color, delayed the maturation process and allowed to obtain a better sensory acceptance compared to the other treatments.
