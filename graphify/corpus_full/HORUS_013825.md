# Shards of the field: the estetization of nature in Henry David Thoreau's El Diario (1837-1862)

-The present research proposes the realization of a series of analysis and reflections on the forms of literary and philosophical creation associated with the estetization of nature in The Diary of Henry David Thoreau (1817-1862). To this end, the appraisals, perceptions and conceptions of nature in relation to the different literary expressions of the self present in this work are studied, through philosophical and critical-literary investigations, with the aim of establishing the lines of aesthetic thought that make the thoreaudian postulates effective. The Diario de Thoreau is the result of 24 years of periodic writing, carried out between October 1837 and November 1861; it covers an extension of more than two million words, in which different literary series converge in function of its communicative intentions, such as the autobiography _x0097_personal or intimate writing_x0097_, more autoficcion _x0097_narración de viviciones daily_x0097_, poetic exploration _x0097_diario literaria_x0097_, investigation of the scientific _x0097_, more naturalistic_x97_bitca_x 94_ (2013: 16). With these elements, the present research and the thesis will reflect on Thoreau’s ethical and philosophical principles that underpin the process of estetization of nature, his understanding of the relationship between the human and its surrounding world, and finally, he will warn how the author’s metaphorical and analogical system of thought operates in his exercise of sensitive observation. The results of this research will be presented through the writing of the thesis, composed as essayical academic text; in a scientific or dissemination article, submitted for evaluation for publication in an indexed journal and, finally, through a series of academic-cultural events or open chairs as a strategy of dissemination for the social appropriation of knowledge
