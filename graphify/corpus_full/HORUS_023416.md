# RADIAL SOLUTIONS TO SEMILINAL EDP PROBLEMS

In this presentation we explain how differential geometry allows us to study the existence of solutions of a problem of a semilinear EDP defined on a Riemanian M variety. Using classic results due mainly to Helgason in [J DIFFER GEOM,6(3), 411-419] we managed to reduce the EDP to a defined EDO on a subvariety with appropriate conditions which allows us to look for solutions that are constant on the orbits of an action of a compact group of M isometrys. Our method applies to a lot of examples and interesting particular situations that can be found separately in the literature, but that can be shown, make part of the same general theory. Thanks to our method, the problem of finding positive solutions of the original EDP is translated into a more manageable one where it is possible to prove the existence of positive solutions and in turn involving the existence of solutions of the original problem
