# Computer tool for designing and optimizing squeeze inlay inhibition treatments

Physical-chemical water studies show that there is currently an incrusting trend in 24 oil fields in Colombia. This implies that water tends to precipitate different scales, mainly calcium carbonate and barium sulphate, and that the need to implement tools to prevent or delay precipitation and depositation, through the design and optimization of squeeze treatments was emerging. Therefore, a mathematical model was selected applicable to sandstone formations, which was validated for linear and radial flow through literature, laboratory and field, allowing to estimate the distribution of the inhibitor along the plug during adsorption, and its subsequent release related to the desorption phenomenon. This methodology was validated under pressure and field flow conditions, allowing to estimate the prediction of the useful life of the treatment, and the effect of the porous volumes injected is analyzed, and the production flow over the duration of the treatment is concluded that the high flow rates present a negative effect on the duration of the squeeze treatments.
