# EFFECT OF PROPOSAL SUPPLEMENTATION ON GROWTH AND INTESTINAL INTEGRITY IN GOVERNMENT POOLS

Intestinal health is an essential component in the overall health status of birds, because the integrity of the intestine cells and their proper functioning allow optimal use of nutrients and increase resistance to diseases. Antibiotics have been used as growth promoters to maintain intestinal health in broilers. However, their regulation and subsequent prohibition in different countries led to the search for alternatives. Despite their emergence, little is known about the interaction that may exist with intestinal mucosa in addition to the possible effect that they may have on epithelial cell functions.
