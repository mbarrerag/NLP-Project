# ENVIRONMENTAL CHALLENGES IN THE BUILDING OF PEACE

The conflicts over natural resources in Colombia have a violent history, causing ecological, social and economic problems.Considering the environmental challenges of the post-agreement between the Colombian government and the demobilized FARC-EP guerrilla, the relationship between the environment and armed conflicts, the environmental implications of points 1 and 4 of the Final Peace Agreement and statistics are analyzed, the incidences of the armed conflict in the deforestation processes in Colombia are described and the evolution of the main drivers of deforestation after the signing of the Agreement is analyzed
