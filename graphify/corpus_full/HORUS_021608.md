# Prevalence of gastrointestinal manifestations of COVID-19 in patients from a hospital in Bogotá, Colombia.

Prevalence of gastrointestinal manifestations of COVID-19 in patients from a hospital institution in Bogotá, Colombia Introduction: Gastrointestinal involvement in COVID-19 is presented in 20% of patients approximately and may include nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhoea or alteration of the liver profile. In our country, the characteristics of gastrointestinal involvement in patients with COVID-19 have not been studied. To determine the prevalence of gastrointestinal and hepatic involvement in patients with COVID-19 attended in two hospitals in Bogotá, Colombia. To determine the association of gastrointestinal involvement by COVID-19 with hospital stay, severity and mortality. Transverse breakdown study, performed in two hospitals of a hospital network in Bogotá, Colombia, between February 2020 and March 2021. 1176 patients with positive respiratory RT-PCR were included. Gastrointestinal manifestations were presented in 50% (CI95% 47-52%), the most frequent diarrhoea in 18.4%, odinophagia 17.6%, anorexia 14.7% and abdominal pain in 8.8%.
