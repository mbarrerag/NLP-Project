# Contributions to the stay of young people in rural territories through the conservation of native seeds and creoles in agroecological systems

The rural sector in Colombia has been the scene of multiple unresolved conflicts and a contradictory governmental attention to the needs generated by farmers and peasants on a small scale, and even more, by the rural youths who have been historically invisible.The current agrarian crisis, based on the conventional model of green revolution, has generated agroecological movements for alternative agricultural production, in favour of the conservation of cultures, species and knowledge in favor of life in both rural and urban territories. Such agroecological strategies deserve to be inherited by the rural youths, who are largely migrating to the cities. The migration of rural youth to urban sectors implies that traditional knowledge about their environment tends to disappear, and therefore, programs and projects are required to disseminate and preserve practices and knowledge, which reduce the progressive loss of cultural wealth in relation to uses and meanings around plants, the diversification, propagation and protection of native seeds, artistic and culinary practices, as well as those related to the historical memory and oral traditions (Pedico UNAL 2018). strategy to contribute to the permanence of the young people of the School of Agroecological Thoughts - EPA in the rural territories, by using appropriate methodological tools for field work and social appropriation of knowledge, such as popular education and the mingas of thought and action. This will allow to obtain as a result of collective work: young people formed as custodians of native seeds and creoles, varieties of native seeds and creoles guarded, and seed houses strengthened by the supply of native seeds and creoles
