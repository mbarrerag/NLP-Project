# ORGANIZATIONAL AND LEADERAL LEARNING AS A VARIABLES RELATING TO ORGANIZATIONAL CHANGE.

The objective of this study was to find the relationship between organizational learning and leadership in the context of organizational change in 264 workers from nine companies in the tertiary economic sector of Bogotá. It was carried out under a cross-sectional/correlational design, using the structural equation model, by applying the multifactorial leadership questionnaire (MLQ) form 5x short adapted (82 items), leader form and form of the classifier, adapted by Vega & Zabala (2004); Castañeda & Rios (2007) instrument of organizational learning, and the organizational change instrument (IMC) of García & Forero (2010). The results suggest relationships between organizational change and organizational learning. Key words: organizational learning, leadership, organizational change, structural equation model, leadership styles
