# THE UNSPECTED VISIT OF THE SPANISH DAMA: A LOOK AT THE CONDITIONS IN WHICH THE 1918 GROW WAS DEVELOPED IN BOGOTÁ.

The chapter analyzes the socio-economic, cultural, demographic and institutional conditions in which the flu epidemic developed in Bogotá, emphasizing the understanding of the pandemic as a social phenomenon of great complexity. The approach is of social history of the disease, prepares a small account of the studies that have been made to deal with the influenza epidemic of 1918 and concentrates on locating the epidemic phenomenon in the context of Bogotá at the beginning of the twentieth century
