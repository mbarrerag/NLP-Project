# Molecular characteristics, functions and mutations of the Collagens type I, III and V, of Tenascin XB, and of the Similar Protein to the Aortic Carboxipeptidase (ACLP), in the extracellular matrix: the phenotype-genotype relationship in the Classic Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome, similar to the classic and vascular. A Scope Review.

Describe the genotype-phenotype relationship in the Ehlers-Danlos vascular syndrome (vEDS), the Ehlers-Danlos classic syndrome and the Ehlers-Danlos syndrome similar to the Classic (clEDS).Methodology: A review of scope developed under methodology of the Joanna Briggs Institute (The University Adelaide, South Australia). The databases Pubmed, Embase, OVID, LILACS, Cochrene Library and Open Gray were consulted. We included descriptive and analytical observational studies describing the clinical manifestations and mutations associated with described syndromes. We also included review articles describing the characteristics of proteins associated with the syndromes: vEDS(Type III collagen), cEDS(type V and type I collagens). We selected studies in English, German, Spanish. cleds(tenas xb and aclp). The results were exported to Rayyan® software, for the screening of the references.
