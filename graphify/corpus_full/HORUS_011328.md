# POLICE BRIEF: RECOMMENDATIONS FOR PUBLIC POLICY FORMATION ON THE OCCUPATION EXPOSURE TO BIOLOGICAL POLLUENTS AND IONIZING RADIOATION IN HEALTH WORKERS

The Center for Decent Work Thought, Occupational Health and Quality of Life, was born from debate and reflection on the improvement of working conditions of the working population. In the period 2019-2021 an increase in the incidence of occupational disease and the death associated with diseases in health workers was shown, mainly by COVID-19. Description of the problem: Health sector workers are occupationally exposed to different factors of occupational risk, among which is highlighted the occupational exposure to ionizing radiation and biological agents.The qualification of origin of a disease is a process that includes technical-scientific, legal and performed aspects when a occupational disease is not considered direct. Policy options and analysis: It is proposed to include the occupational diseases described in Decree 1477 of 2014 caused by occupational exposure to ionizing radiation as direct occupational diseases and diseases caused by occupational exposure to biological agents due to presumption of legality in health workers who perform their work activities in the care and community, provided there is direct contact with patients.The above, taking into account the scientific evidence of current work and for the effects of eliminating administrative barriers due to the disease's qualification The disease is based on the disease
