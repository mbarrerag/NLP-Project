# Strategies for strengthening and teaching-learning the languages of the Yaigojé Apaporis Park Reserve

This project aims to continue the work designed for teaching and learning native languages of communities in the Yaigojé-Apaporis National Natural Park-Resguardo-Parque, in the Colombian Amazon. It seeks to test, enrich and publish a didactic material previously developed from research strategies critical participatory action and collaborative ethnography, as well as principles for acquiring additional languages in the context of linguistic revitalization processes and from an ecological perspective of educational linguistics. From the methodological point of view, spaces for collective deliberation will be promoted in which the didactic material can be piloted, fed and adjusted to the linguistic and pedagogical/deachtical needs of local languages. Likewise, metallinguistic reflection will be motivated, along with collaborative selection and linguistic documentation. With this, it is desired to impact socially by generating reflections that allow participants to feed their organizational process in terms of linguistic and pedagogical policies and their relationship with language education and strengthening. Likewise, it seeks to achieve a scientific-academic impact, feeding the field of knowledge, teaching and learning of native languages, can be applied in other countries, and can be applied to the development of teaching and learning methods
