# Low-Cost System for the Manual Measurement of the Electric Field over 3-D Domains

We present a system to measure the electric field distribution of radiating systems over domains swept manually by a human operator which provides significant time, cost, and complexity savings with respect to conventional acquisition methods. The system is divided into four main blocks: (1) generation of the signal used to excite the antenna under test, (2) position tracking, to evaluate the location of the field sensor during the sweep, (3) acquisition, which determines the field amplitude during the sweep, and (4) control and data integration, which coordinates the operation of the previous three blocks. The experimental validation of the system demonstrates very good accuracy against reference data using a fast and simple acquisition process
