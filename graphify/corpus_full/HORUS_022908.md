# Changes in the role of the Colombian State in relation to hydroenergy production

Through the use of documentary review techniques, this research identified the changes in the role of the State in the production of hydroenergy. In the first part, the role of the State of a fomentist type (1936-1994) is characterized and contrasts with the regulatory role of the State (1994 onwards). Historical conflicts in relation to electricity are identified and due to them, the institutional schemes with which the State attempted to resolve them are outlined. It is concluded that under the regulatory role the Colombian State has generated an institutionality that favors the production of hydroenergy with important business returns, managing the territory with productivity criteria. However, the conflict due to the impacts of the construction of hydroelectric plants is increasing in the country without the emergence of an institutionality through which the State seeks to solve these conflicts at low social and environmental costs
