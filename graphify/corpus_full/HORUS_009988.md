# Collaborative networks and interaction of dynamic supply chain capacities for collaborative risk management in the Avocado Hass production chain in the Department of Antioquia.

Changing, dynamic and uncertain environments such as the current ones represent an important challenge for supply chains which should be strengthened with a view to managing risk and avoiding possible disruptions in their productive processes. Collaborative risk management is presented as a strategic tool through which companies integrate with third parties to work together together to reduce the risks to which they are exposed, combining resources and interacting to achieve common objectives. Horizontal collaborative networks (HCN) allow interaction between actors that develop in the same productive sector, suggesting the need to take advantage of the dynamic supply chain capacities (SCDC) of each of the actors to enhance collaboration and thus mitigate the risk in a collaborative way. With a view to contributing to the development of more resilient supply chains and articulated to an associative context, the present study aims to develop a horizontal collaboration model that allows the use of the SCDCs of each of the involved actors to facilitate the risk management in a collaborative way of their productive chains.
