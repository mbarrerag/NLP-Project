# Community tools and resources for living well: Approaches to issues of coexistence based on processes of sovereignty and knowledge management

The project will be carried out in four communities in the suburban area of Mitú. A protocol will be developed in collaboration with the aim of carrying out own and intercultural research with peoples and communities that are part of the association of traditional AATIAM authorities, and reflections will be given on the procedures required for the formulation of own research from local resources
