# USE OF AMINO ACIDS AND ENZYMES TO REDUCTION ACRYLAMIDE IN RAPADURA IN COLOMBIA.

Introduction, Rapadura is a traditional and basic product of the family food basket, with consumption per capita and production in Colombia highest in the world, elaborated through of an evaporation process and high temperatures of sugar cane, which gives rise to acrylamide formation in the Maillard reaction;with acrylamide being studied as a possible carcinogenic compound in humans.Regarding the indicative values of acrylamide in rapadura, specific limits have not yet been established for this product. However, EFSA has defined an indicative value for acrylamide in other foods, which is 500 micrograms per kilogram (μg/kg) of food.Objective(s), carry out the chemical characterization regarding acrylamide in rapadura in the department of Cundinamarca and its reduction through recommended practices.Methods, samples were collected from a honey center in the municipality of Utica, Cundinamarca, these were analyzed using liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry (HPLC-MS), where: 1. the amount of acrylamide in honey and brown sugar was quantified, 2. a study was carried out stability of acrylamide in rapadura over time, once a month for three months 3. Tests were carried out to show the effects of using amino acids and the enzyme asparaginase at laboratory level;Results, the amount of acrylamide in the honey and pulverized brown sugar of 16.4±1.9 and 1221.0±189.9 μg/Kg.In the stability study reduction percentages of 32.2 and 36.8 were obtained for the first and second month respectively, for a total of 69.0% reduction.Lastly, with the use of glycine, cysteine and an enzyme reduction percentages of 64.0, 72.9 and 80.2 were obtained, but for the amino acid glutamine the amount of acrylamide was greater by 96%. Conclusion, the partial results obtained from the investigation show that it is possible say that there is a reduction in acrylamide in the process of making rapadura through two techniques: during the storage period of the rapadura and with the use of additives.Where the best additive is the addition of the amino acid cysteine and the enzyme, achieving reductions significant, it is also not an option to use the amino acid glutamine as it helps Maillard reaction.These results are a possibility for safer production of rapadura
