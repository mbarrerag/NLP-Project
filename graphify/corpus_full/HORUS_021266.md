# Development of a method for automatic detection of the meiotic spindle to establish the relationship with maturation and in vitro fertilization in porcine oocytes based on the Stokes vector and strategies artificial vision

In meat production, the pig industry is an important line in the agricultural economy, which will be considered as an initial step in the production of organic products. In order to achieve high productive standards, it must make use of various assisted reproduction techniques such as: in vitro fertilization and intracytoplasmic sperm injection, with its acronym in ICSI. Both techniques are susceptible to the development of new procedures that favor the increase of the maturation and fertilization rate. Finally, it should be emphasized that advances in this field not only favor the production of pigs, but have the potential to be applied in human reproduction protocols. By reviewing the literature it is found that traditional techniques based on fluorescence do not allow the evaluation of all the ovoids, since being invasive processes compromise the viability of the embryos. It is like this way in the last years, the microscopy of polarized light has been increasing as a tool to evaluate the maturity of the egg by identifying the presence of the meiotic spindle. In HSI space where intensity is the average of the polarisation states analyzed, the degree of linear polarization will be the saturation channel and the angle of polarization will be the tone channel (Hue). Secondly, on the previous images, segmentation algorithms such as Otsu will be applied and different texture descriptors will be evaluated oriented to the identification and description of the characteristics of the meiotic spindle. As a third item, a strategy of convolutional networks based on learning transferred to establish the presence of the meiotic spindle will be explored from the input images. Finally, from the previous steps, the presence and characteristics of the spindle will be integrated with the maturation and fertilization rates, which will be evaluated with the standard procedures in the evaluation of in vitro processes. This project is expected that when selecting oocytes presenting the meiotic spindle, it will be possible to increase the rates of maturation and fertilization, and therefore the efficiency of the in vitro production procedure of porcine embryos. In addition, the development of the hardware and software based on the proposed artificial vision stages will allow to have an automatic tool for inspection of the in vitro process
