# Access to justice for persons with disabilities understood as a human right in the Colombian context: a look since the ratification of the CDPD.

This grade work aims to answer the question: is access to justice for persons with disabilities violated, when the State does not ensure its effective implementation, by not carrying out concrete actions, in particular, aimed at “removal of barriers” that this population group faces in the process of accessing justice?
