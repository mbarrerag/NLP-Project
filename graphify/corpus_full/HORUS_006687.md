# Acupuncture in the treatment of Osteoporosis

Osteoporosis is a metabolic disease, of the skeletal system characterized by decreased bone density and deterioration of the bone microarchitecture, which increases the probability of presenting fractures, most frequently located at the vertebral, hip and distal region of the radius.(1) It is a silent disease, the first manifestation may be the presence of fractures before low impact lesions. It affects both sexes. Its prevalence is increasing worldwide; the incidence is associated with age, the decline of estrogen production by the onset of menopause, nutritional deficiencies, sedentaryism, use of medicines, chronic diseases among others. Current treatments are effective but with low adherence by the side effects.
