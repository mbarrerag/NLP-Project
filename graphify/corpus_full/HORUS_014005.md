# Low cost 3D printing platform for developing optical devices.

With recent advances in 3D printing technologies using polymers, such as Volutric and Melt Electro Writing (MEW)[1], new possibilities are opening up for creating innovative high-resolution structures in a cost-effective way. In photonics, for example, these new additive manufacturing techniques prepare the ground for the construction of new optical systems by taking advantage of the compact size of the fibers generated. Recently, an article was published showing how to build a new affordable open source MEW printing platform [2] with which a high print resolution with fiber filaments of 0.5 to 50 micrometers can be achieved. Based on this new technology, this project seeks to implement a 3D printer inspired by the low-cost MEW platform, to print optical devices and investigate possible applications for them
