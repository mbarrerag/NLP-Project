# Sedimentology and stratigraphy of the Guaduas Formation in the Sincline of Sueva and its influence on the content of Methane Gas associated with Coal (GMAC), Macheta sector. Cundinamarca, Colombia

This work presents information corresponding to the drilling well Machetá 1-SGC made in the eastern flank of Sinclinal of Sueva in the municipality of Machetá, which is carried out vertically and with recovery of nucleus, starting in the roof of the Guaduas Formation and reaching the upper part of the Labor – Tierca Arenisca Formation, reaching a depth of 526,6 m. In this well, 19 carbons present in the Guaduas Formation can be recovered and sampled, obtaining thicknesses that are between 0,30 and 1,21) m. Likewise, measurements of the contents of Methane Gas associated with these coals are carried out, registering values that vary between 2,29 ft3/ton and 315, 23 ft3/ton.
