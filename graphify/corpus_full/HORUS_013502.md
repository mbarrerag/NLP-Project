# Mass movements in the municipality of Soacha with thunderous rain

A study has been carried out using Geographic Information Systems (GIS) with the aim of mapping the susceptibility to landslides in specific areas using frequency ratio (RF) models. During the study, a total of 151 mass movement events were identified. The four factors that condition land slides, such as slope, cover, soil types and precipitation, were integrated with mass movements. This allowed to determine the weights associated with each factor conditioning mass movements, as well as the classes of factors, by applying models of frequency relation. The maps of susceptibility to landslides were generated by superposing the weights corresponding to all conditioning factors by means of the calculator of the spatial analysis tool in ArcGIS.
