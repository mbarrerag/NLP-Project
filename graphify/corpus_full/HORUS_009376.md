# National initiative for the development of sustainable agricultural systems facing climate change, proposed EMBLEMATICAL PROJECT IN EDUCATION, SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION FOR COLOMBIA, IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE 2019 SABS MISSION

The productive agricultural systems in Colombia are very vulnerable to environmental changes such as the alteration of the regimes of rains, floods and droughts associated with the phenomena of La Niña and El Niño, which bring with them the exacerbation of health problems that, in turn, demand plant protection controls that can have a strong impact on the environment.These strong variations in the climate are called climate change.In general, in Colombia there is little knowledge about the impacts of climate change in the different regions of the country and how they impact on activities important for the life and well-being of Colombians, especially in the agricultural systems, without regard to the type of economy that sustains them.In Colombia, there is little knowledge about the limited management of production, low rates of yield, little use of available technology and volatility in prices in the market.This is due, in part, to the limited support of the State to farmers, especially small and peasant economy, to the high technological deficiencies, institutional improvisation and the lack of intersectoral articulation that does not allow the development of programmes in the medium and long term, supported by the country's agrobiodiversity, their territorial characteristics and their territorial characteristics and their cultural diversity
