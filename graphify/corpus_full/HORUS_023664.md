# HEALTH REQUIREMENTS IN RENAL CRONE DISEASE IN DIALYSIS: A QUALITATIVE SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

Chronic renal disease (CRD) in 2017 affected 14.5% of the U.S. population. Health needs are defined as "capability to benefit" and incorporate determinants of health, knowing that this is an important opportunity to generate a current and evidence-based picture of current deficiencies that really affect the patient's health status. This study aims to identify the health needs present in patients with CKD in dialysis from the experiences and perspectives of patients, family caregivers and health professionals. Studies were considered in peritoneal contexts of haemodialysis or dialysis; disabilities, immigrants and kidney transplantation were excluded. The protocol was registered in PROQuest; a thorough search was carried out in MEDLINE, Embase, Web of Science, Lilacs, PsycARTICLES, Sociology Database, Nursing & Allied Health Database (through ProQuest), and gray literature; the selection and extraction of data were performed by two independent reviewers.
