# CORPOLAB: EXPERIMENTED COURSES

In this space of the Third National Meeting of Research on the Body, called CorpoLab, in its days of April 21 and 23, the bodies participate and resist the agencies possible in the virtual happening. In each corpolaboratory, we become virtual body and experience, create and contribute together to the reflection, fragmentary, incipient and ephemeral around methodological perspectives linked to the virtual experience with dance, smell, touch, graphic gesture and voice. That is how CorpoLab invited us to go through paths between the smell, the tongue, the bones of a body and its ways of becoming present from breathing; routes that, ultimately, we could say that all are body, although some of them, like dance, believe us ideas of a body without organs or functionally common organs
