# Molecular detection of viruses and viroids in native potato plants and commercial varieties using qPCR technique

The main focus of our study was the implementation of six systems for the detection of five viruses and a native and commercial potato viroid using the qPCR technique. The standardization of individual reactions and the use of known concentration dilutions from synthetic positive controls allowed the development of calibration curves to characterize the dynamic ranges of the systems evaluated. The adequate selection of primers, together with a comprehensive computer analysis and the evaluation of reaction variables, led to the successful design of two RT-PCR Multiplex detection systems with resolution in agarose gels.
