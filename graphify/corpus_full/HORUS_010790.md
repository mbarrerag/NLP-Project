# Challenges and strategies in education processes during the pandemic at the Ofelia Uribe district educational institution in Acosta

This paper aims to raise awareness of the challenges faced by teachers and students of the Distrital Educational Institution Ofelia Uribe de Acosta during 2020, because of the health emergency unleashed by Covid 19, since, due to extensive quarantine days to prevent the spread of the virus, a model of distance education mediated through different digital tools was implemented. Likewise, the strategies carried out by teachers to address this new model of remote education that brought with the pandemic will be presented; this work is committed to a proposal of exploratory research whose qualitative methodological approach will help generate a reflection of the academic experiences faced by teachers and students during this period.
