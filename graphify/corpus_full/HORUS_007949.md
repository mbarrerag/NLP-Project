# Perception and construction in relation to the feeling of the principle of beneficence.

to understand the perception of the principle of charity constructed by patients from an integral adult clinic, from the Faculty of Dentistry at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia. qualitative research; three data collection tools were used: participant observation, the field diary and ten semistructured interviews, recorded and transcribed. Content analysis was carried out on the information obtained. five categories were established on which the perception and construction of common sense revolves with respect to the bioethical principle of charity in the participants: experience during care, evaluation of the quality of treatment, needs in oral health, communication and balance risks/benefits, and benefits obtained. the perception and construction of common sense with respect to the bioethical principle of charity, according to the participants, beyond technical and therapeutic benefits, is mediated by the objective experience of the care process and is assessed through the resolution of their needs and the benefits obtained in therapeutic terms, aesthetics and self-care, as well as economic, accessibility, professionalism and intersubjectivity, in the student-patient triad, through the consolidation of trust
