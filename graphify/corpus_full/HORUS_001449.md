# RAMIRO ISAZA MEJIA MASTER OF GUITARRA IN COLOMBIA. I REMEMBER OF HIS WORK VEINTION YEARS OF HIS FALLING

After twenty-one years of the death of guitarist and pedagogue Ramiro Isaza (Medellín 31 December 1947-Bogotá 13 May 2003), who was a committed promoter of the guitar career at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia and founder of the first guitar quartet, the quartet Espiral, is intended in the following article to study aspects of his journey and his work that opened a great door to the development of classical guitar in Colombia
