# MATERIALS FOR THE COMPRENSION OF A GOVERNMENTALITY

The present work aims to show some characteristics of algorithmic governmentality in the contemporary world. In methodological terms, we are located in a critical ontology of the present, which aims to reflect on the futures of the present world. Such governmentality has been configured in recent years by the hand of informational and digital technologies. We start from the idea of governmentality in Foucault and show how its work allows us to understand the different forms of governmentality and its transformations. Subsequently, we address two central characteristics of algorithmic governmentality that we call the data as reality and the regime of opacity. The data as reality allows us to understand the place that the notion of data occupies in computational or algorithmic systems and, the regime of opacity, points out how such governmentality operates without making explicit its regimes of truth. Finally, we conclude that algorithmic governmentality erodes the idea of a regime of truth as a support of truthfulness and of the intelligibility of individuals with the world
