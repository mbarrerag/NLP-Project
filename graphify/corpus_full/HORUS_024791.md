# SCHOOLS OF THOUGHTS IN PUBLIC HEALTH TEACHING IN DENTISTRY

To identify the schools of thoughts that serve as basis for public health teaching in some dental schools in Latin America. Mixed methods study with Design: ¿Comparative Case Study¿. The sampling of dental schools in Latin America was intentional, non-probabilistic. The following websites: OFEDO/UDAL, Latin American dentistry training portals and academic associations from Brazil Mexico and Colombia, were accessed periodically during one year to identify and extract data from the programmatic content of courses related to public health and assign the school of thought their teaching is based on. These were typified as: preventive and social dentistry, family and/or community dentistry and social medicine and/or collective health. A content analysis was carried out for each program employing 15 paragraph marks, where any of the three schools becomes apparent. The marks of each school were counted and if any exceeded 50%, the program was linked to it. The process was repeated using the country as unit of analysis. 107 academic programs were obtained from 246 dental schools studied, mostly from Brazil, Mexico, Peru and Colombia. Preventive dentistry was predominant in 55% of the cases, compared with family and/or community dentistry in 18% and social medicine and/or collective health in 10%. Public health teaching in Latin America is heterogeneous, it does not adhere to the discourse and the guidelines disclosed by the WHO/PAHO on the minimum content and objectives that courses related to the field of public health should have. National and regional workshops on disciplinary skills in public health are recommended for general dentists, serving the specific contexts of every country, in order to fulfill the essential functions of public health proposed by the PAHO, the GOHIRN recommendations (¿La cascada¿ declaration) and the Sustainable Development Objectives proposed by the WHO for 2030
