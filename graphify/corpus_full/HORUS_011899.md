# REFORM TO COLOMBIA HEALTH

Medical medicines and technologies are a good cross-sectional and longitudinal tracer of the fundamental right to health and the functioning of the health system. Therefore, it is striking that the bill that reforms health in Colombia, which is based by the government in Congress, includes some elements on the drug policy described in the Statutory Law -and some of the advances in pharmaceutical policy (Conpes 155 of 2012) - but does not deal judiciously with the management and regulation of public expenditure on medicines and health technologies to guarantee access to medicines
