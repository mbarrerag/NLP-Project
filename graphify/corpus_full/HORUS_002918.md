# A LOOK AT THE POLICK (ANNELIDA, POLYCHAETA) VENINE MARINES OF THE COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

Aneleids (philum Annelida) are segmented worms that cover organisms such as earthworms, leeches and polychaetes (Class Polychaeta), the latter being the most abundant and diverse group of phylum Annelida. Within the Polychaeta class there are several families with records of poisonous/toxic species (e.g. acoetidae, amphinomidae, glyceriidae, sigilionidae, syllidae); however, toxicity studies have focused mainly on some species of Amphinomidae and Glyceriidae. For the Colombian Caribbean there are nine species of polychaete families reported as poisonous, however, accident records with them are almost non-existent; however, identification of these organisms is fundamental for those visitors to the sea and avoid unfortunate accidents
