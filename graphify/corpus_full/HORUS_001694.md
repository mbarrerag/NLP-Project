# Crimes and criminals in Rionegro Antioquia during 1930 and 1948

This thesis arises from the interest to understand the reasons that lead the subject to participate in criminal activities, taking into consideration social, cultural, political and religious factors. To this, it is part of the premise that a criminal is not only a offender of the law: it is also a neighbour, a citizen with whom it interacts. The condition of criminal does not define exclusively the identity, because this label is assigned through legal regulations and, in some cases, through stigmatization. The object of the study is to analyze the duality between the norm that defines the subject as a criminal and his status as a social subject that tries to justify his actions with his statements. To this, it is necessary to focus on the judicial processes and underlying resuppressable aspects, since it is assumed that criminal behavior does not occur because it is, but often it is a response to adverse conditions that individuals are confronted with their search for survival.
