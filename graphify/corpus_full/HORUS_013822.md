# The environment as a problem: relations, processes, conflicts and actuators in Colombia

The consolidation of ecology as a field of knowledge and the recognition of global environmental change denatured nature. This ceased to be a given fact and transformed into the environment, that is, a complex assembly of non-human and human actants with socio-natural processes and biogeochemical cycles.The environment presents five main characteristics that differentiate it from nature: 1) it supposes a form of government much broader than the exploitation of nature, which is deployed on various temporal and spatial scales, and involves a great diversity of actants, processes, relations and conflicts? 2) that form is crossed by the idea of environmental crisis of anthropic character; 3) that government is impossible without the close participation of scientific knowledge that expands the boundaries of environmental appropriation; 4) the environment becomes a field of battle in which the accumulation of capital and social conflict in the present environment is played out; 5) that capitalism begins to be understood as a socio-ecological system in which the environment is internalized within the circulation and accumulation of capital, while the environment becomes internalized within the biogeochemical and ecological cycles of the Earth.
