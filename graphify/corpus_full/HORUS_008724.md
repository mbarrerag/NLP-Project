# Massivity and weightlessness: diaphanous architectures in Brazil 1954 - 1995

This research explores the apparent antagonism between modern architecture in 20th century Brazil, where there is a contradiction between the tradition of a previous architecture highlighted by its masivity and a modern architecture that seems to be contrasted with planes and lines that manifest the lightnessness, spatial flow and the absence of links with traditional decorative or figurative elements, the research explores the apparent link between these two positions that seem to be linked between the past and present of the period in powerful buildings that are works where technical and structural development has a protagonical character in volumes with predominant use of concrete as a raw material, a devotion to the condition of masivity and a minimum resistance to spatial continuity, explores whether in this coexistence a new interstial space could have been generated at the ground level of the soil through a process by ex-horation or material subtraction of the building, opposing both positions, and in that sense, it is intended to consider whether this space formulated within the Brazilian architecture has roots with a Latin American character or part of a transposition of European inherited features. .
