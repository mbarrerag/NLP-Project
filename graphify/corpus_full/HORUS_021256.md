# Biodiversity and City: Decrypting Local Patterns to Understand Global Problems

The establishment of cities and their expansion in tropical regions has generated changes in landscape and environmental conditions whose effects on biodiversity are little known, which limits the scope and effectiveness of planning and conservation policies in this type of ecosystems where conflicts between biodiversity and development are increasing. This study will assess the effects of urbanization in three Colombian cities of different region, climate and altitude (Medellin, Valledupar and Bogotá), using birds as a bioindicator group, based on bird censuses under standardized protocols that will allow to analyze responses of the taxonomic diversity of the whole community in different cities and the responses of functional diversity in one of the cities, using bird groups such as insectivores of underforest and nectarine, whose ecology will allow to determine the effect of other more local anthropic pressures related to the management of vegetation in different cities and the practices of ornamental gardening and the use of artificial drinkers.
