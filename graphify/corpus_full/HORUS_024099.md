# Loss of land dispossession and forced displacement: a loss of object or subject?

This research paper aims to understand the way in which peasants assume or signify the loss of their land in the context of the armed conflict and the acts of dispossession that led to forced displacement. Therefore, what happens when there is a breach in the relationship of the subject with the object, when it is taken away, when it is stolen?, what effects does this generate on the subject?, will the relationship of the subject with the object earth change? The above, in light of the testimonies of the victims of dispossession and forced displacement of land? as well as, of the conceptual tools provided by psychoanalysis in relation to the structural elements of the encounter with the real thing that is derived from dispossession, the effects of the subject on the subject and the object, and in the relationship of the subject with the object earth
