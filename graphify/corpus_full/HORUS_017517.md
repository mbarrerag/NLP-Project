# A RECOMMENDATION MODEL OF EXPERIMENTAL PROTOCOLS BASED ON THE USE CONTEXT OF USE

In this study, we propose a model of recommendation of experimental protocols based on the user's context of use. As a case study, we analyze 107 experiments related to 5 experimental protocols of laboratory tests of soil mechanics in the field of Civil Engineering, according to ASTM (American Society for Testing and Materials) standards. Our approach starts from the formal specification of the experimental protocol and proposes a representation model that describes the entities involved in the scientific product. This model of representation contributes to the formalization of the experimental protocol in a knowledge domain that has not been explored until now. Based on the model of representation, we present a model of recommendation that takes advantage of the context of use to identify the entities that characterize the context in which a user performs a new experiment, reproduces, repeats or audits experiments performed previously. This also addresses the currently limited visibility of recommendations for this type of products in the explored repositories of experimental protocols. Using the two models, we develop a prototype of software and validates it through test cases.
