# ASSESSMENT OF THE ECOSYSTEM SERVICES: CHALLENGES, SCOPE AND PERSPECTIVES (CHAPTER 2)

The chapter develops the approach to environmental economic valuation from the perspective of ecosystem services, initially presenting conceptual aspects about the structure and functionality of ecosystems and their relations with human well-being. It then introduces the concept of socio-ecological systems and finally presents the dilemmas and interpretations around the approach of ecosystem services
