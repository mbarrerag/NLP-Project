# 60 years of the Business Administration Program of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá Campus: Trajectory, Innovation and Projection by INNOVAR Journal

As part of the 60th anniversary of the Business Administration Program (PAE, by this spanish initials) at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá Campus, this 96th issue of INNOVAR pays tribute to an institutional trajectory that has linked academic training, research, and the dissemination of knowledge relevant to the country and the region. The editorial note traces the shared origins of the PAE and INNOVAR journal, highlighting their role in the professionalization of management in Colombia, the consolidation of a rigorous editorial policy, and their international reach. Throughout its pages, it reflects on the contributions of special issues to the articulation between academia and practice, interaction with Ibero-American scientific networks, and the impact on curriculum content and researcher training. This anniversary invites us to renew our commitment to open, socially relevant, and epistemically plural science, reaffirming the editorial and academic legacy that has characterized INNOVAR and PAE in recent decades
