# ♪ At the beginning of the day ♪ ♪ Does my lyceo rickard ♪ ♪ Give it, get it, get it ♪

The unfinished fragment of Pushkin in tercetos chained "At the beginning of my life I remember the school..." has been called more than once "mysterious" and intriguing. Initiated, apparently, in the autumn of Boldino, during the life of the poet was not published. VA Zhukovsky, who published it for the first time in 1841, included the text in the number of "Imitations of Dante", obviously seeing a connection with Dante's theme in the form of a stanza of the passage. But the key "Dante" suggested by Zhukovsky did not answer many questions. The poem has provoked a wide variety of readings. Its genre, form of stanza, lyric hero, the characters and realities mentioned became subject of consideration and discussion. We managed to establish a hitherto unknown source of the text of Pushkin and the English attention, which allowed us to clarify some of the dark passages "At the beginning of life...."The form of Dante seems to have appeared in the unfinished passage thanks to the influence of Sainte-Beuve-Delome.
