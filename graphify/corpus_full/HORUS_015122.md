# INFANCE AND IN-COMMON AFFECTS BETWEEN THE MISE-IN-EXPERIENCE AND THE MUSE-IN-SITUATION-ECOLE. SOME NOTES ON CINEMATOGRAPHYC MODES OF EXPERIENCE IN A CHILD SCHOOL IN CAMPINES (BRASIL)

We want to think of the powers of cinematography in the context of the school as affective powers that expand and intensify the experience of bodies in in-common directions. Written at six hands and as a deployment of an experimental film workshop held in 2019 with children up to six years of age in a school in the city of Campinas (Brazil), the text proposes a series of kaleidoscopic notes from three points of view: the school teacher (Sandra Amaral), the researcher in education (Wenceslao Oliveira Jr.) and the filmmaker-philosopher (Sebastián Wiedemann) looking to outline lines of strength of the modes of film experience that emerged on that occasion.
