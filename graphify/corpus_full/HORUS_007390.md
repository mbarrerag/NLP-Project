# Self-management and State: Right to the City and Integral Improvement in the Barial Case study: Barrio Bello Horizono, Pereira

This document aims to reflect on the right to the city and urban informality, including the differences in the forms of self-management and the subsequent action of the State through the programs of Integral Improvement Barrial (MIB). In this sense, the main objective is to analyze the relationship between the process of self-management of the Barrio Bello Horizonte (Pereira, Colombia), within the framework of the right to the city, and the effects of the program of MIB.
