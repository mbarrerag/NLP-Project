# Treatment of a textile waste water contaminated with acid black colouring 194 by an Electro-Fenton process

The textile industry in Colombia has been crucial for the economy and society, providing about 8% of manufacturing GDP and 0.9% of national GDP. Although the role of this industry is relevant for the economy, this generates different types of waste during the textile transformation process, where the liquid effluents generated in the dyeing stage are highlighted. These effluents present a high polluting load (DQO > 400 mg/L and 41330 Pt-Co units), mainly by the presence of colorant not fixed to the textile fibre and some compounds associated with it (chrome and chlorides).
