# Trading method for irrigation under low water availability conditions

This document contains the experimental procedures, results and conclusions of a project of multiagent systems applied to the estimation, management and decision-making for irrigation of crops in the districts present in Boyacá. For the construction of this project, the objectives were set out to Model an agent for the reservoir, obtain a model for the crops, simulate the yield of crops with specialized software, construct an agent of estimation of water requirement, and finally implement a negotiation algorithm based on agents for the management of irrigation. To meet these objectives, it was proposed to create agents with various techniques of artificial intelligence as convolutional networks and random forests to perform the tasks of: estimating the amount of water in the reservoir The Cup from which the resource is extracted for the surrounding farms, estimating the water requirement according to the environmental and cultivation conditions, calculating the validity of renegotiation and implementing it using a consensus algorithm and, finally, simulating the behaviour of the cultivation with the soil and irrigation conditions estimated to show the effect of the system's decisions in the farms . hear from which the water resource is obtained, estimate the water requirement according to the viromental and crop conditions, estimate the validity of negotiation and execution it and, ultimately, simulate the crop behaviour with the soil conditions and the calculated ted irrigation profile to show and analyze the effect of the system decisions in the crops
