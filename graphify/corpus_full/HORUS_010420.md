# Evaluation of spectral responses as a basis for estimating the nutritional status of manganese in cultivated rose sp. var. Freedom plants.

This research was carried out in rose cultivated under greenhouses located in the municipality of Tocancipá department of Cundinamarca, with the objective of evaluating the relationship between reflectance and manganese content compared to chemical analysis of foliar tissue, to focus on plant nutrition in the cultivation of pink variety Freedom. An experimental design of complete blocks was used at random, with five treatments of different doses of manganese and five repetitions. Five samplings were carried out, for each sampling 10 plants were analyzed per treatment for a total of 50 plants per sampling and of each plant were taken spectral responses to 10 leaflets with the fieldSpect spectroradiometer.4 In each sample, images were captured with three Nikon chambers in different bands (red, blue, red, and infrared) adapted to a mobile platform, and the best foliar content analysis was performed in laboratory; the results showed that at lower concentrations of manganese in foliar tissue were higher, the vegetation indices that presented the best correlations were GNDVI, DATT4, ND2, and DLT2, and D1, the results were performed
