# Experimental study of the capacity of a hydrocyclone with large cone angles

The conclusions obtained in an experimental study in hydrocyclones of large cone angles (close to 180°) are reported and compared with those obtained in a traditional hydrocyclone or small cone angle, in which reference is made to the mass flow or capacity that feeds the apparatus, with respect to the design and operational variables
