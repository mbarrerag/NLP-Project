# SPACE AND STRUCTURE IN RELIGIOUS ARCHITECTURE. A VI TO VIII KINGDOM OF GRANADA

In this book one of the factors analyzed in the main research, corresponding to the usefulness: space and structure and spatial organization, is presented bi-conceived space and structure in the religious architecture. Male conventual temples in the New Kingdom of Granada, where the univocal relationship of space with materiality is demonstrated, and how it defines that. It explores how the current spatiality is the result of technological decisions; it addresses the concept of spatial efficiency and structural efficiency of the constructive system used, establishing relations and differences between religious communities and within them, and how this is directly defined with the technical and socio-economic context. In the first three chapters, a contextualization and the state of the subject matter is presented. In chapter four, the constructive process is presented, as one of the technological aspects of the buildings studied. It is important to highlight that, in order to reach the analysis, it is necessary the knowledge of another technological aspect: the constructive systems and socio-economic analysis; the research result is not presented in this book, and it will be reserved for a subsequent publication, but it is necessary to take into account the topic of space and structure, and thus to achieve the analysis of the first stage of the research. and that, for this case, it is a general scale; later, the aspects or factors to be evaluated are defined with their variables and indicators, which were based on one of the principles that should govern the architecture according to Vitruvio, and all its spatial elements or materials based on the vision of González, Casals and Falcones. For the definition of some indicators, and its application, it is used parameters defined in the construction treaties of the time. Chapter six and seven present the result of the application of the method developed for the analysis of each of the buildings evaluated. As already stated, this research phase starts with the information obtained, complemented by other instruments such as calculation of execution times and application of geometric rules, for which 3D plans and models were used
