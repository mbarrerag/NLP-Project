# ANALYSIS AND DEFINITION OF TRAINING OBJECTIVES

The quantitative model of professional profiles presented in Chapter 5 can be used to analyze the training objectives of a curriculum and therefore also contribute to its definition. The purpose of this chapter is to illustrate, by way of an example, the analytical potential offered by the quantitative model. The selected example is widely documented and is intended to help determine whether the newly graduated professionals of a specific curriculum are able to perform in a specific professional area. The selected program is the Electronic Engineering program at the National University of Colombia, and the area is the metrology one. The analysis is particularly interesting, because in the conception of the program metrology was not explicitly considered as one of the areas of performance. In fact, this is an area that in Colombia only recently has been opened up to professionals of electronic engineering, undoubtedly due to advances in electronic sensors and the systemization of specialized laboratories. The analysis question can also be formulated in a proactive way, for example: what should be the training objectives of a curriculum offered to electronic engineers (perhaps a diploma), so that they can be performed in the area of metrology?
