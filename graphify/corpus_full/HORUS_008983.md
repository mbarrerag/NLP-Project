# THE ECOCIDIUM AS THE 5TH CATEGORY OF THE COMPETITION CRIME OF THE INTERNATIONAL CRIMINAL COURT.

The following paper aims to analyze, not only the inclusion of ecocide as the fifth category of crime of imputation of the International Criminal Court, but also to analyze the possible paradigm shift of extending the criminal sanctions that are today individual, towards the responsibility of societies like companies and national and international multinationals. That is, it seeks to move from an individual criminal sanction for those who cause serious damage to the environment inefficient until now, to the penal linkage of societies and corporations both at the national and international levels
