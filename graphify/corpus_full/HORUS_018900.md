# OPTIONAL JUSTICE IN CHILDREN, THE RIGHT TO HUMAN OCCUPATION AND SOCIAL PARTICIPATION

Introdution. Childhood as a stage in the life of every human being has been recognized from different disciplines as essential for the proper development of the person and society. Thus it has been thought that being attentive to it will help to overcome inequalities in the world. Reflecting on Occupational Justice in Childhood within the practice in education of the program of Occupational Therapy, in the family of the children cared for and in society as precursors of a change in the health and social conditions of the Childhood. Study with descriptive qualitative approach in which it reflects on the practice in education, the family and society related to the occupations of the caregivers-infants and the childhood as alternatives for the construction of the Occupational Justice. It is found that the preliminary categories of caregivers-infant and childhood from the perspective of Occupational Therapy help to reason about the concept of Occupational Justice due to the fact that they recognize the aspects of articulation on the occupations of the families, their roles and the opportunities provided by society for them.
