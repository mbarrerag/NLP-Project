# Treatment of gold ores associated with arsenic minerals.

This paper presents the results of a bibliographical research and the subsequent analysis of the metallurgical extraction of gold from minerals associated with arsenic. It details the chemical-mineral characteristics of these ores and possible metallurgical treatments to improve the extraction conditions in the presence of arsenic minerals that has relative capacity to complex under the physical-chemical conditions of the complexion of gold in cyanide medium. It is concluded that mineralurgic alternatives, associated with mineralological characteristics of the ores, are present and this issue is not yet definitively closed, which implies a research challenge for the exploitation of auriferous deposits associated with arsenic minerals
