# CHAPTER 1 - PRECONCEPTIONAL CARE

The Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology of the Faculty of Medicine of the National University of Colombia presents, after 10 years of the first virtual edition, the second edition of the text Obstetrics Integral XXI century. It was an obligation to update this academic heritage for teaching and dissemination of the knowledge of a specialty that has been modernized with ultrasound, molecular biology and epidemiology, and has become science and art of maternal and fetal care. The number of chapters has been reduced, but the idea of preserving semiology and physiopathology is maintained. It has been written by experts and presents topics of general obstetrics, obstetric syndromes and medical complications of pregnancy. It contains scientific evidence of the state of art. This edition is complementary to the previous one. It has been added topicals of laparoscopic during pregnancy, breast cancer in gestation and fetal physiology. It is intended for undergraduate and postgraduate students, and professionals who seek up-to-date information
