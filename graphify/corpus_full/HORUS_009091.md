# INFLUENCE OF DESIGN THINKING IN THE INNOVATION OF CIRCULAR ECONOMY FOR WASTE MANAGEMENT

The production of uchuva is part of the most important markets of the exotic fruit sector in Colombia. These fruits are usually marketed without capacho, therefore, this material becomes a waste without any value. The circular economy (EC) seeks to close the loop where the waste becomes resources. However, it goes through a transition process where barriers arise that limit it. In this process there are design tools that pave the way. The design for a biological cycle uses materials that degrade easily, and the industrial symbiosis converts the waste into raw material. For example, a biofilm with capacho is adapted to the mentioned design strategies. But it is not only to develop EC practices, but also to manage to adapt them to the context of the companies and the industrial field. In this sense, it is important to use a design focused on the user to guarantee the success of the circular strategies. The design of the design and the design (DT) allows to create innovative products based on a complete understanding of the needs. This work has as a goal to describe the influence of the DT in the innovation of EC. In addition, this influence is verified in a real case with the elaboration of the design of the design of the design. The development of EC from an innovation perspective. Companies need to determine whether their circular actions are viable in the future. DT offers a picture to alleviate uncertainty because it is designed with all possible risks in mind. This can be achieved by an exhaustive view of the context and requirements of all stakeholders. Likewise, innovation is a key lever for achieving a more sustainable circular economy, and DT involves understanding the barriers and trying to eliminate them as much as possible thanks to the user-centred approach. Finally, thanks to the DT process within the EC, a biomaterial based on uchuva layer with favourable properties was achieved
