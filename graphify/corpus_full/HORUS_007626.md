# Mathematical modeling for prediction of the degree of homogenization in granulated solids and coffee mixers

This project aims to develop an innovative mathematical model to predict the degree of homogenization in granulated solid mixers in coffee processing. Currently, coffee processing plants face critical challenges in producing homogeneous mixtures efficiently, due to the lack of a quantitative approach that allows to determine the optimal time of homogenization. Dependence on empirical methods by operators causes operational inefficiencies, high energy consumption, accelerated wear of equipment and a remarkable variability in the quality of the final product. To address these problems, it is proposed to develop a mathematical model based on the phenomenology of the mixing process, using the Discreet Elements Method (DEM). This model will allow for accurate evaluation and prediction of the degree of homogeneity in mixtures, optimizing the operational conditions for improving energy efficiency and ensuring a uniform quality of the product. Implementation of this model will reduce the dependence on empirical experience, allowing for more rigorous and scientific control of the mixing process, resulting in products of higher quality and lower production cost, thus strengthening the competitiveness and sustainability of coffee processing plants
