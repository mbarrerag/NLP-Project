# Plan for strengthening and scientific visibility of the Biogenesis Research Group.

The resources financed by this call will be used to pay for the review of scientific writing, the writing in English of those articles that require it or to translate the manuscripts of students that do not yet have the competence for writing in English. It is expected to have academic productivity of written quality and submitted for publication in English language, which will contribute to maintaining the classification of the research group before Minciencias in the current category (A1)
