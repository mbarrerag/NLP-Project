# Visualities of Extractivism: Comments on Three Works of Contemporary Art

This research is interested in the interaction between image, territory and extractivism in three audiovisual works of contemporary Latin American art: Journey to a previously told land (2011) by Laura Huertas Millán, Suspension I (2019) by Adrián Balseca, and El Oriente is a myth. chapter 01: oil (2021) by sofía acosta. From an intersectual approach the work weaves a dialogue between political ecology, colonial and visual studies. The three works allow us to delve into different forms of violence and coloniality, as well as articulate a series of annotations around the elaboration of the visualities of extractivism from art. The visualities of extractivism are characterized, among others, by a vision without soil, the conversion of the other into resource, and the hypervisibility of certain images. These visualities embody aesthetic, material and thought manifestations, which affect human and non-human bodies .
