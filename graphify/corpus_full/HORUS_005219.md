# Geological and petrographic characterization of units that include an archaeological site in the La Palma estate located in La Nueva Unión, Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta

In this study, the detailed geological context of a region to the west of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta (SNSM) was studied, where the lytic structures associated with pre-Hispanic indigenous communities that inhabited the area were identified.These structures correspond to settlement areas and housing elements, such as walls, terraces, roads and stairs, which have lasted over time and were developed using rock blocks. Currently, no clear relationship has been established between the lytic structures and the geology of the area. To address this, several geological analytical techniques were applied, including detail mapping at 1:5,000 scale, mineral paragenesis, petrography studies, analysis of mineral chemical composition and dating.These methods allowed establishing links between the structures and regional geology. Thus, the presence of two main rock units that correspond to Metagranodiorita de la Palma, a name suggested in this work, was considered a meta-organorite body of meta-gnegrederas of Pérmic age (269,71±3,92 and 268,96±366), and a meta-vulcanotype sequence of these events.
