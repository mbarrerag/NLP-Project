# Radical nephrectomy in renal cell carcinoma with venous tumor thrombus: Long-term deslinks and overall survival

ABSTRACT Objective: Describe the overall survival (OS) at age five and perioperative morbidity of patients with renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with venous tumor thrombus (VTT) treated by radical nephrectomy and thrombectomy. A cohort of 530 patients with CCR diagnosis was evaluated between January 2009 and December 2019, and TTV was found in 42 of them; these 42 patients composed the sample of this study. The patients were stratified according to the classification of Neves thrombus (NCT). The baseline and perioperative characteristics of the patients were described, as well as the follow-up. The Kaplan-Meier curve and its respective COX regression were applied to present the OS at 5 years and the patients were stratified by CPN. The average age of the sample was 63,19 ± 10.7%, at the level of the OST was present in the 7.9% of the patients.
