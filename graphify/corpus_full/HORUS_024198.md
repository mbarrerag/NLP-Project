# ENVIRONMENTAL CONFLICT BY WATER

The identification of social links in tension, determined by the inequitable distribution among human beings of the elements of nature, shows the presence of environmental conflicts. Below, a mapping of the history of systematization of environmental conflicts is carried out and arguments are offered to understand conceptually an environmental conflict, its meaning and dynamics around water in the Latin American and Colombian context. In addition, some benefits of the collective water management model are explained as a sustainable alternative, invisible by the State and the market
