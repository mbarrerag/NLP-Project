# Chanos chanos (Gonorhynchiformes, Chanidae) also arrived in the Caribbean: first record of Colombian waters

Chanos chanos is a marine and estuarine bonefish, distributed in the tropical portions of the Indian and Pacific oceans. In November 2021, a specimen of this species was fished about 85 cm long fork on the Caribbean coast opposite San Bernardo del Viento (Colombia). There is no clear explanation for its presence in the Caribbean, but it is important to be attentive to future sightings in the Caribbean waters of this exotic species
