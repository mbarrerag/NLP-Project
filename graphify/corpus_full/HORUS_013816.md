# Singing and dancing harmonizes the territory: the rituals of the People of Center as a tool of peace building

This proposal for a solidarity extension gives continuity to a process of symmetrical dialogue between the academy and the indigenous peoples, through the Semillero de investigación Casa Hija: circles of the word of the research group Ethnology and Linguistics Amazonian of the Headquarters Amazonia and Headquarters Bogotá. This work of dialogue has led to the creation of the Archive of the Indigenous Languages of the Colombian Amazon (ARDILIA) in 2022 and the Laboratory of Languages and Symmetric Dialogue in 2023. In the present proposal is sought to strengthen one of the collections of the Archive ARDILIA, the collection Neeba Gwajko of ritual songs of the Miranha people. The proposal seeks to contribute to the strengthening of the Miraña materials of the Archive (transcriptions, translations and metadata of the existing sonorous objects and the generation of new sonorous documents); generate dynamics of intergenerational dialogue in the Miranha people from the transmission and recreation of knowledge about the songs and ritual dances, and contribute to the process of formation of students of the National University of Colombia, all this as a tool of peace building.
