# Chonta-Cao: Promoting inter-institutional collaboration and knowledge dialogue for agro-ecological sustainability of chontaduro and cocoa in the coastal regions of Ecuador and Colombia

The project Chonta-Cao responds to the initiative of cooperation UNAL-UNEMI in which two research groups agree on a proposal with the general objective of promoting binational cooperation and the exchange of knowledge to strengthen the sustainable production of chontaduro and cocoa in the coastal regions of Ecuador and Colombia. It has only three specific objectives, two of applied research and one of inter-institutional cooperation. The first objective includes the work to be developed in cocoa in Ecuador, focused on phytosanitary activities (plagas and diseases) in commercial nurseries and in endomicrobioma associated with certain symptoms. In this study, the experiences in the entomology of UNAL for the work with pests in the liveries. It is intended to obtain a fungal endophytic complex that is able to colonize the tissue of seedlings with cocoa in Colombia. The planned activities of the disease and the disease in future development will involve the patient disease in Colombia. The integration of our experiences in research and academia during the implementation of this first project will allow the participating research groups to set us more ambitious goals in the future.
