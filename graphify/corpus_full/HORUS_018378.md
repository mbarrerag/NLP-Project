# SAFE AND HEALTHFUL COMMITMENTS FROM THE PRACTICAL AND METHODOLOGICAL IMPLEMENTATION OF THE DPCC

The design is commonly focused on improving people's lives or meeting their needs, but also the spaces and devices of Design that are generated from this intention, can influence human behavior generating transformations and adaptations. However, only recently has a field specialty been defined called design for behavior change (DPCC), whose objective is to produce desirable human practices (Niedderer et al., 2014). This theory applies to ecological sustainability, health and welfare, safety and social design; however, the change of behavior is also applied for commercial purposes. Next, two cases of study are presented in research projects in DPCC, the first related to health problems such as overweight and obesity and the second focused on pedestrian safety in public space. The writing focuses on the importance of understanding and studying the behaviors, emotions and knowledge of people affected by these complex situations; and how from the theories of Design, cognition and behavioral economy, proposals can be generated that contribute to the solution or improvement of these problems. In this sense, as part of the methodological analysis of these proposals, the participatory process is presented, taking into account the different actors involved
