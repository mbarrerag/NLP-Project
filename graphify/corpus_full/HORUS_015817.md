# DEVELOPMENT OF A RULE OF PROCESSING FOR DISUNCTION OF WASTES AND REGULAR MINERAL PESED IN GOLD ALUVIAL MINERIA

This paper presents a possible route of benefit for rare earth minerals (REE) and other heavy minerals associated with the tailings of the exploitation of a gold alluvial pleasure deposit located in the Lower Antioquian Cauca, Colombia. The mineral studied corresponds to a residual current of the gold exploitation process by dredging, and presents a concentration of potentially exploitable heavy minerals exceeding 50% by weight, distributed in different ranges of sizes and densities making its benefit especially complex. The process includes stages of separation by sizes, magnetic, gravimetric and electrostatic concentration, initially producing concentrates of magnetite, ilmenite and rutile. A high intensity magnetic separation final stage also produces a product with appreciable contents of rare and circon lands. The chemical and mineralogical characterization of the products was performed by Diffraction of X-ray, petrographic analysis and SEM-EDS, and evidenced the potentiality of exploitation of main iron minerals (like magnetite), titanium (ilmenite), monacita and zircons, with various mineral associations
