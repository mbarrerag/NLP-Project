# Replacement Direct Soil Cutting Equipment

-In the field of civil engineering, particularly in the area of the Mechanic of Sue-los, there are a series of parameters of the terrain that must be known for the design of any construction work. The majority of these parameters are measured from laboratory tests. Among the most relevant, the parameters of resistance to the cutting stress of a sample of soil subjected to loads and/or of-formations equivalent to those presented in the field are measured from the test of Direct Cut. Among the equipment used to measure the resistance to the cutting effort of the sue-los in the laboratory, those that have reached greater importance in the development of the practical engineering as well as in the research, are basically the apparatus of direct cutting and the triaxial compression. The apparatus of direct cutting, most typical is the equipment for the displacement of the moving half of the case, the equipment is separated horizontally, the sample is placed in it. The equipment proposed is: The cutting machine 27-WF21A60, is an automatic machine for direct/residual testing of cor-te.
