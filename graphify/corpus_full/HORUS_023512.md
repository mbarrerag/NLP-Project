# VALIDATION OF A METHOD BY CRIMATOGRAPHY OF GAS FOR ANALYSIS OF NITROSAMINES IN PLASTIC EMPHASIS MIGRATION TESTS.

N-nitrosamines are carcinogenic compounds that can migrate from plastic packaging to food during storage. In Colombia, elastomeric materials intended to come into contact with food and drink should not yield N-nitrosamines in quantities exceeding 0.01mg/kg of simulant. This requires a metrologically traceable method of analysis. Global migration test with saline simulant (artificial saliva). N-nitrosamines extraction. Gas Chromatography Analysis (FID and MSD). Through GC-FID, it was established that the limits of quantification for 5 regulated nitrosamines are between 6 and 12mg/L (ppm), value much higher than established by normivity. The method of analysis was then implemented and validated by GC-MSD. Teteran migration tests were performed with salinant simulant (artifical saliva)
