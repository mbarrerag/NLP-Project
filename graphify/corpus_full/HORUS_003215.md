# SELECTING A BPM PLATFORM: BEYOND TRADITIONAL EVALUATION CRITERIA

Resumen. In this study, we present the methodology that we used to find a free and open-source platform ¿ for making our forthcoming research more accessible and impactful ¿ with the biggest worldwide presence and the most active community around it. Our contribution to academia and industry is the combination, through a standardization table, of comparison criteria with very different scales. On the one hand, traditional criteria such as portability and languages, which are internal to each platform. On the other hand, and the real challenge of this study, aspects such as global presence and community around the platform. The latter, using (i) specialized social networks such as LinkedIn, StackOverflow and Google Trends, and (ii) expert opinion reports, such as Forrester and Gartner. Therefore, our selection goes beyond traditional evaluation criteria
