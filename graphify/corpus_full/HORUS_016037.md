# BIOLOGICAL BEHAVIOR OF TRANSMISSIBLE VENERAL TUMOR BY CYTOPATHOLOGICAL EXAMINATION

Tumors control surrounding environments to promote growth, survival and propagation via the secretion of different cytokines, which interfere with both neoplastic biological behavior and the immunological response to cancer cells.Understanding the interaction of cytokines and tumors stimulates the emergence of new anti-tumor therapies such as immunotherapy.In this sense, the canine transmissible venereal tumor (CTVT), one of the only contagious neoplasms observed in nature, has been studied with the aim of better elucidating the mechanisms of escape from immunological detection based on the interaction of the tumor microenvironment (TME) with cytokines;Therefore, the study seeks to understand the interaction of the cytokines IL-6, TGF-B1 and CD8+ cells, responsible for mechanisms of tumor regression and progression, with tumor biological behavior, therapeutic resistance, clinical, cytopathological and histopathological characteristics of CTVT
