# Development and study of physical properties of new materials applicable to devices based on polarized spin current generation

Complex perovskit type compounds are one of the most exciting material families due to the wide range of technological applications that emanate from their physical properties attuned to compositively. Among them, the possibility of generating new spin transport channels, in addition to the transport of electrical load, opens new perspectives within the spintronic industry.
