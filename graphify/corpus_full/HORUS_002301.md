# BOX BLACK

Black box is a poetic gesture resulting from the two-year research process in the Interdisciplinary Mastery in Theatre and Living Arts that articulates techno-poetic devices in dialogue with stories of native peoples in Colombia, mainly the Awá, Barí, Nasa and Wiwa. The assembly is constructed from visual relations with the geography associated with these peoples, interrupted listening, noise, interference and loss in transmission as structural elements. In the scenic space ephemeral drawings emerge _x0097_sensitive cartographies of symbolic routes through these territories, made with breath, body, water, fire and ink_x0097_, light devices of precinematographic character, epistemological hacks applied to imaging technologies, and a narrative pulsion that overflows the borders between the human, the mountain and what cannot be fully named.The project proposes a friction between documentary languages starting from the sound: voices, narratives_x0097_explored in the framework of processes of historical memory_x0097_, appropriate recordings, sound synthesis and artistic practice
