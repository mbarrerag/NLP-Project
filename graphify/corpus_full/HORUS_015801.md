# DISEASE SERVICES IN THE FIRST WATER LEVEL IN COLOMBIA. A ANALYSIS OF THE 2002-2020 OFFER

Given the current primary health care approach proposed by the Astana declaration and implemented in the operational framework developed by the WHO, it is necessary to identify the resources and capacities of national health systems in the primary care component. Nursing participation in these services contributes to improving quality of life, greater adherence to treatment, higher level of knowledge and higher patient satisfaction rate
