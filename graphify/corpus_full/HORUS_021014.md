# Simulation in digital learning environments by reinforcement in autonomous robots

The programming of an autonomous exploration robot requires the ability to respond to obstacles and events, that its sensors and peripherals can capture from the environment. One of the tools that is counted on for this purpose is the learning by reinforcement, which allows the robot to respond with its actuators, in an optimal way and learn from the decisions that it has made in the past. However, to test these algorithms, it is necessary to perform tests, that ensure that its decision-making has been efficient during the time of the mission. It is important to emphasize that field tests are usually expensive, and require a lot of logistics. Simulation tools, such as engines for the creation of video games, can be a useful tool for simulation of physical variables, to test the making of deditions, as well as the proper use of its electronic and mechanical components. Artificial intelligence application and learning by reinforcement, are increasingly applicable resources all branches of engineering science. For the specific case of robotics, it is necessary to optimize decision-making processes, for the proper use of sensors and actuators, for process efficiency and saving of electrical, electronic and physical resources.
