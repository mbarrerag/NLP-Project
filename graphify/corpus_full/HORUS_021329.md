# ACUAPANAL - Alliance for the strengthening of the subsistence aquaculture and fisheries sector of the departments of Valle del Cauca and Cesar.

The project of research that is presented entitled _x0093_Circular economy: a commitment to the aquaponic systems that contribute to the food sovereignty of subsistence acuculturers and artisanal fishermen in the departments of Valle del Cauca and Cesar_x0094_, is aimed at helping to solve the problems of sustainable food of two regions of the country (the department of Valle del Cauca and the department of Cesar). It is a project directed towards the population of small artisanal acuculturers and subsistence fishermen, in which it seeks to develop a process of aquaculture production, under the methodology of the acuapical, which allows simultaneously to produce the ictictic resource and the vegetable food resources, using rationally the recirculation of the water. In addition, the project aims to develop food products of fishing origin, seeking to give greater added value to the primary production and using the resources of the lower quality to be commercialized directly and the by-products resulting from the filleting of the same, and the production of the following products. 0093_Aguadesonso_x0094_. As results, it is expected to obtain guidelines for the installation and implementation of aquaponic systems in the already annotated regions of the country, establish a database with technical indicators of the applied production system, strengthen the fish associations that support the project with contributions in science, technology, research and innovation, establish methodologies for the processing and transformation of products and by-products resulting from the aquaponic systems, lay the foundations for the implementation of a circular economy system and strengthen the intersidence seedbeds formed by students from the headquarters participating in the project. Finally, the project has a clear purpose, such as to promote sustainable development according to the regional and particular conditions of each of the two places where it will be implemented. It will be carried out, as already noted, with the direct assistance of the communities of artisanal and subsistence fishermen, which must result in better living conditions of these communities, in improving the food of these communities, in the creation of sustainable employment sources, in the improvement of the welfare of the fishermen and their families, and in general the development of the general, the general development of the general
