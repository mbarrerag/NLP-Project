# Transition to a pro-environmental habitation: a comprehensive look at normalized attitudes and habits in household waste management

It is widely recognized that the predominant linear scheme in the management of the garbage should be aligned with a circular approach, however, this is unthinkable without considering a change in the habitation. With the aim of inferring criteria to favour a pro-environmental habitation, this research proposes a comprehensive look at the relations between the normalized management of domestic waste (GNRD) and the attitudes and habits of urban inhabitants-users linked to this. According to a qualitative methodology, this exercise began with a comprehensive state of the art of the issue and a section of contextualization that places the relevance of the topic and the approach adopted in a scenario dominated by technocratic visions and the ingenieril practice.
