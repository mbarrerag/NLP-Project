# Litho-geophysical model of the Amazon Craton in the Colombian East based on the integration of geophysical information.

The Amazonian Cratón is one of the largest cratonic areas in the world. The geological model of the Cratón in Colombia is little known, mainly because a large portion of the rocks associated with this craton is covered by sedimentary sequences and recent deposits, added to little accessibility and dense jungle coverage.The most accepted models for the Amazonian Cratón indicate that this evolution by multiple accretion of belts/terrenes around an ancient nucleus, but recent studies suggest that the evolution of the northwestern portion may have been different from the areas where those models were formulated. In this work we used geophysical data (magnetic/gravimetric), applying multi-scale detection methodologies of edges and 3D modeling that allowed identifying and delineating the main cortical discontinuities for the Amazonian Cratón in Colombian territory, five proposed for other geological characteristics
