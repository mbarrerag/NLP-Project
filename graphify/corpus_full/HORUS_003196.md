# USE OF NIR SPECTROSCOPY IN THE EVALUATION OF SOILS AFFECTED BY SALTS.

The presence of Soelos Affected by Sales (SAS) limits banana production in the Department of Magdalena. The expansion of the agricultural border and the use of groundwater for irrigation are the main causes of the increase of SAS. The costs of analysis for salinity and the time for the elaboration of cartography make it difficult to evaluate; for this reason the near infrared spectroscopy (NIRS) as being a fast, efficient and low cost alternative, is an option for the evaluation and monitoring of SAS. The main objective of this work is to evaluate the properties of the soil associated with the involvement by salts by the combination of NIRS, and the landscape soil relation. This research was carried out in the Municipality of Banana Zone, Department of Magdalena. The sampling area is delimited from the soil-landscape relationship, then using the presence of the conditioned latin hypercubo (PLSR) and the main components were designed.
