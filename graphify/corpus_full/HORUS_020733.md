# Laundered Death. Brief Atlas Mnemosyne on ways to tame and hide death in Colombia (1730 – 1937)

This thesis studies different images of corpses in Colombia, made between 1730 and 1937, starting from the question: the image of death in Colombia has been outside the various social conflicts? To address this question, I use the method of the Atlas Mnemósovyne, made by the historian of art and culture Aby Warburg (1866 – 1929), enriched by three conceptual tools: on the one hand, microscopy and telescopy, shaped by the Colombian mathematician and philosopher Fernando Zalamea (1959); on the other hand, the pathoscopy, fruit of my own work. The hypothesis I present from the previous question seeks to show how the image of death has not been given in the air and in an ahistorical or apolitical way, but has responded to concrete social bases, which represent a precise series of moral, religious, political and cultural values, developed historically.
