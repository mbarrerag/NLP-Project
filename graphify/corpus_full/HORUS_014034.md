# Analysis of compliance with concrete specifications for infrastructure works from the use of concrete with recycled plastic fibre additions.

The concrete is a material highlighted by its compressive strength but that requires reinforcement to increase its resistance to tension and/or bending, this has commonly been achieved by means of the use of steel and electro-welded meshes. However, steel bars are not the only alternative to strengthen the concrete and in the industry there are other proposals such as the use of fibers, these have been used for a long time when it was added straw to the adobe in order to improve its structural performance by providing greater tensile strength (Toxement, 2018); recently they have also been generated in the industry proposals of metallic fibres and synthetic fibres, which, they have other advantages as they do not require additional labour for their placement and that generate a reinforcement in three dimensions when they are homogeneously mixed with the concrete.The technical feasibility in the use of fibers for concrete and the high levels of pollution generated by the plastic industry, where their accumulation of the hand of its slow degradation and its harmful chemical composition represents a problem for future generations, open the door to the construction industry to seek alternatives in order to reduce, reuse or recycle this material.The present research, the present research project proposes to perform, and the concrete
