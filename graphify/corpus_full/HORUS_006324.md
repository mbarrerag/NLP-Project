# Cutlery. Colombian university fables and fablers

My experience in oral narrative and the performance of Social Work generated the interest in asking how the articulation occurs between the circumstances of the context or specific and artistic practice. Is it possible that the work of art is devoid of political purposes? Or, scholarship it is an expression of human sensitivity, does it imply taking a position towards the world? Storytelling or scenic oral narrative – NOE – is proposed at universities in Colombia to take advantage of the experience and knowledge of the artistic field for the benefit of research. The initial focus is on the emergence of the phenomenon and outcome of the event. The context of the emergence is defined by consulting texts, location of the first stories and repertoires of the participants to produce information that is susceptible to cultural analysis. The approach is qualitative with the use of the in-depth interview and autobiography
