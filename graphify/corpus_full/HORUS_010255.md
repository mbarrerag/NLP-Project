# A CHARACTERIZATION OF VIRTUAL DOODLES.

In this talk we present a short characterization of virtual doodles in terms of the solution of the signed Gauss word problem by considering a restriction of the graded matrices introduced by V. Turaev in [3] and later studied in [1] and [2]
