# Characterization of Millimeter Wave Propagation in Agricultural Environments

This study describes the characterization of path losses in a tomato greenhouse at 60.48 GHz. The results show considerable losses due to the presence of vegetative strata and are compared with established propagation models, successfully describing the path loss behavior. These findings aim to support the deployment of 5G networks in agriculture, promoting fields like agro-IoT
