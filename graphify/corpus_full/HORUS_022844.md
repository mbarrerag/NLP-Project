# SIMONDON AND TECHNICAL OBJECTS IN THE DIGITAL ERA

The present study provides a perspective that allows the updating of the Simondonian theory, in terms of digital technology and its relationship with people. To this end, the analog objects as understood by Simondon are first analyzed, which were the technical objects of their time. Is this exhibition enriched by the interpretations of some scholars of his work; to this is added the work of other thinkers who have reflected on analog and digital objects. This is postulated in order to understand the material conditions surrounding the life of digital objects, with what is above the notion of digital device. Finally, the value of analog objects in the digital age is rescued, since these still have something to teach us even in a world that the more digitized is more alien to them
