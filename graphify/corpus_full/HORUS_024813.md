# EFFECT OF MYRICETIN ON FLEXURAL STRENGTH OF DEMINERALIZED DENTIN

The chemical structure as flavonoid of Myricetin (MYR) makes it a powerful antioxidant, an inhibitor of matrix metalloproteinase activity, and a collagen cross-linker. The aim of this study is to evaluate the effect of MYR on the flexural strength and the apparent elastic modulus (E) of demineralized dentin Methods: Dentin beams (n = 90) were obtained from healthy human third molars, conditioned with phosphoric acid (10%) for 5 h, and randomly divided according to dentin treatment. Control group: mineralized dentin (MD) and experimental groups: demineralized dentin (DD), 100% ethanol as a vehicle control (EtOH), 1% glutaraldehyde (GA), 6.5% proanthocyanidin (PAC) and 600 μM Myricetin (MYR). The samples were tested to the three-point bending test on the Shimadzu A-GIS 5kN® universal testing machine (Shimadzu, Tokyo, Japan) (0.5mm/min). Data were statistically analyzed through ANOVA and a post-hoc Tukey test (p<0.05). The flexural strength and E of MD was significantly decreased (p<0.0001) by acid conditioning. MYR was effective in improving the flexural strength and E of DD. MYR-treated dentin showed the highest flexural strength values (30,58 ± 1,78 MPa) compared with DD and EtOH (p < 0.05). No difference was observed between the dentin treatments with MYR and GA or PAC (p = 0.073). GA-treated dentin presented the highest E values followed by MYR-treated dentin, without any significant difference between the groups (p = 0.065). Stress'strain curves show that GA and PAC presented an elastic behavior, meanwhile; MYR demonstrated a viscoelastic behavior, suitable for the strain under flexural loads Conclusions: The MYR application as a natural collagen cross-linker contributed to enhance the mechanical properties of dentin. MYR exhibited a dentin biomodification potential and enhanced these properties without adhesive system infiltration. Although more studies are required, data suggest that MYR, could be improving the stability of the dentin-resin interface and durability of composite resins
