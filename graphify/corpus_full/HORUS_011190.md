# ASSISTANCE OF ASSISTANCE FOR THE TRAINING OF DOCUMENTS IN SCIENCES,

This paper presents the results of the Pedagogical Innovation Project developed with teachers of post-gradual training of the Master's Degree in Teaching Natural Sciences from the Faculty of Sciences, Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Bogotá; particularly those enrolled in the subject "Teaching Electromagnetism and Ondas." With the advent of the Covid-19 pandemic, the question arose, how to carry out academic activities in theoretical modality practical for teaching physical concepts related to electromagnetism and waves?. With this in mind, it was proposed to carry out theoretical activities through telepresence through the Moodle platform, complemented by 4 experimental activities based on the methodology of Active Learning, for this purpose, low cost and easy achievement materials sent to the home of each of the students in order to be reused or reproduced, thus impacting their immediate environment and their own classroom practice
