# STRUCTURAL AND MECHANICAL CHARACTERIZATION OF POROUS ZINC WITH COMPLETELY CONTROLLED STRUCTURE

The structural and mechanical characterization of Zn-porous with 100% interwoven pores is presented. The samples present poro spherical, homogeneous poro diameter of 5mm, 6mm and 7mm, interconnecting pores of 3mm diameter and a porous structure of simple cubic shape; and dimensions L/D=0.8 (4mm-4mm-3.2mm).The densities of 2.29, 2.67 and 2.82 g/cm3, with pores of 68, 62 and 60 %, for the samples with pores diameter of 5mm, 6mm and 7mm respectively, were calculated from the stress vs deformation curve of 10.47±3.5 MJ/cm3 for pores diameters of 6mm. The massive presence of pores in the metallic foams gives these materials multifunctional properties, such as lightness, high rigidity, excellent energy absorption, etc. [1,2]. Due to these unique properties, they are candidate materials for the formation of parts in the spatial stations such as radiation shielding, damping of vibrations and resistance to impacts. Therefore, the structure is very important for the aerospace industry. In each porous size, 5 pieces with a ratio L/D = 0.8 as indicated by ASTM standard, with dimensions of 4 cm x 4 cm x 3.2 cm, were manufactured for each porous size.The structural characterisation of the samples was performed by measuring density and % of porosity.In addition to characterizing the foams mechanically doing quasi static compression tests to determine the energy absorbed during the deformation.The quasi-static compression tests were performed at a compression rate of 1 mm/min.In Figure 1, one of the samples obtained for each pores size is observed.It is important to note that the samples present a regularity in their shape, pore diameter and porous structure.On the other hand, the structural and mechanical characterization of the porous zinc with different pores diameter is observed in Table 1, from this point we can see that when increasing the poreus diameter the material density increases and the porosity decreases.This is expected already when the density of the metallic material within the sample is increased and the result is reduced
