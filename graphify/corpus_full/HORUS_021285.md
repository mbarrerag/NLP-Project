# STRENGTHENING OF THE ENVIRONMENTAL RESEARCH SEMILLIER. ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY FOR ADJUSTMENT TO CLIMATE CHANGE.

The proposal seeks to strengthen the Environmental Research Seed, which began work since 2017, with academic and economic resources that allow support and continuity to research initiatives. The multidisciplinary group also has traveling professors who accompany their initiatives in the different projects that it develops. The project advances in the research of environmental sustainability initiatives that favor the adaptation to climate change with urban and rural communities. Through hybrid methodologies (qualitative-quantitative, descriptive-analytical and inductive-deductive), depending on the research initiative, the study is addressed in two lines of work that are feedback and complement: the vertical garden UN of the university campus (Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Palmira) with its university community, and the high mountain area of the Valle del Cauca (Parque Nacional Natural las Hermosas (PNNH) and the co-organisation of the community, municipality of Palmira) with the rural population. The Semillero expects, with the support of this project, to continue with the implementation of bets around the awareness and environmental education, on the ecosistmic services granted by the vertical garden, together with the inhabitants and the inhabitants of the rural areas
