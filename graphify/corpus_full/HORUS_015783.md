# Weaving Gender Violence: Cases in Families of Colombian Military and Police

In this research work, we analyze cases of gender violence and violence against women in families of Colombian police and military, in order to understand what is the relationship of these violences with the type of masculinity that is demanded and reinforced in the military and police training.
