# Criteria and strategies in urban logistics to constitute a “Urban Logistic Corridor”, case study Av. Calle 13 of the city of Bogotá D.C.

As a result of the final master's work, it was determined that the city of Bogotá D.C. presents a level of average maturity in topics of Urban Transport of Cargo (TUC) according to the methodology of Strategic Planning of Cities (Kiba-Janiak, 2017) applied in this research. The study was carried out on Av. Calle 13, which mobilizes the largest number of vehicles of the city of Bogotá with 17,032 of 80,444 vehicles in working days and 14,742 of 62,383 vehicles in non-working days (Secrería Distrital de Movabilidad de Bogotá D.C., 2015a). The above has marked the need to define strategies and provide specific criteria in urban logistics to constitute a “Logistic Urban Corridor” in Av. Calle 13 of the city of Bogotá D.C. as a solution to the congestion of artery roads and the use of land in Colombia. In this grade work, the urban transport of cargo of Av. Calle 13 was studied, its territorial system and its socio-economic characterization, as well, the city of the normative and policy framework of transport and the results of the urban tools in Colombia
