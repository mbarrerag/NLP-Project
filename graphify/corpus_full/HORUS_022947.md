# Optimization of the lyophilization process and comparison with Russian stragon convection drying (Artemisia dracunculus L.).

Dehydration is a conservation process that can negatively affect the nutritional and organoleptic characteristics of the treated product. This study was developed in the Laboratory of Quality Control of Foods, at the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, Medellín headquarters, with the objective of optimizing the process of lyophilization (LIO) of Russian tarragon leaves (Artemisia drancunculus L.) to conserve, in the best possible way, its characteristics. A tray lyophilizer was used, where the plant material was cooled from 25 °C to -40 °C, at a speed of 0.22 °C/min. The optimization was carried out using the surface response methodology with an optimal design, in function of the independent variable heating speeds of the plate (0.03. – 0.06 °C/min) during the total heating content of the product was obtained from -40 °C to 35 °C. The product was compared with the optimal color temperature of the plate during 1 h to 35 °C.
