# COVID-19 is in the air: why do we keep ignoring the importance of ventilation?

Abstract The main mechanism for transmitting the SARS-CoV-2 virus is by air, particularly in poorly ventilated interior environments. Recognizing the importance of this mechanism has taken a long time, despite the evidence generated by scientists of aerosols from an early stage of the pandemic. Therefore, the measures applied more rigorously by the population have focused on disinfection of surfaces, often in an exaggerated way, while measures focused on reducing the concentration of aerosols in indoor environments, such as adequate ventilation and air filtration, have been promoted very timidly. In addition to the advances in the National Plan of Vaccines, it is necessary to intensify the measures of prevention of contagion for a reopening of the safer economy. It is therefore urgent to educate and generate clear guidelines for the evaluation and improvement of ventilation in interior spaces
