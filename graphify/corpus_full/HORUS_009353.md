# EFFECTS OF EXTERNAL PROBES ON THE ELECTRO-OPTICAL PROPERTIES OF A SINGLE-ELECTRON WAVY QUANTUM DOT

The recent development of novel semiconductor growth techniques has enabled the fabrication of exotic nanostructures such as graphene nanoflakes, nanoflowers, and graphene nanospindles, among others. In this contribution, we have undertaken computations of the energy and linear as well nonlinear absorption spectra of a wavy single-electron quantum dots utilizing the effective maass approximation and the density matrix formalism, employing the Canonical Equation
