# INFRASTRUCTURE DESIGN IN A BIM ENVIRONMENT WITH INFRAWORKS

The following is a guide for students and for those who make use of the basic tools of InfraWorks software, integrating different types of information quite common in the design and administration of the road infrastructure and coming from multiple data sources, such as geographic information systems (GIS), CAD, orthophotos, point clouds, among others, aimed at integration and generation of design proposals at conceptual level and, subsequently, in the definitive design of road infrastructure projects within a local environment or in the cloud. With the use of this tool it is possible to analyze and evaluate in a simpler way proposals of design and implementation of road infrastructure in environments that allow to realistically represent the proposals proposed, making use of primary and/or secondary information, superplacing them and creating a definitive alternative of implementation. This interoperability and conceptual design software allows professionals not only to model and analyze the current situations of a road infrastructure project, but also to visualize the design elements once they are endorsed by technical and/or regulatory parameters, and the modifications that the project presents during their life cycle, to later, recreate the final conditions at each stage of the same, in a realistic environment and even more rapid design
