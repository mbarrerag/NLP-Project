# Proposal of a business model for subscription with the strategic approach of blue ocean in the market of products for the pet care in Bogotá

This document develops a review on different business models related pet marketing and makes a business model proposal through the blue ocean strategy. The goal is to conduce a quality jump based on client ́s value and for the subscription products market on pet care in Bogotá, looking in a unique and different service. This document uses an exploratory method with expert validation and survey application due this is product innovation in a market that doesn ́t exist (Hernández Sampieri et al., 2014). Some patterns were defined to create blue oceans based in four (4) out of (6) main basic approaches or paths that rejects the markets boundaries. Those are: path to the industry, path to a strategic group, path to a buyer’s group and path to functional-emotional orientation. As a result of utility test with pet’s owners and expert guidance, there is an market opportunity for a gifts business through product subscription based on pet care products with an monthly or bimonthly subscription in Bogotá for people who lives on 3 or 4 stratum that have a pet like a dog or a cat
