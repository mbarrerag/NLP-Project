# Characterization of the morphology, genetic diversity and pathogenicity of Ceratocystis sp. in cocoa producing areas.

Validating control strategies that allow the management of limiting pathogens of Colombian cocoa culture are an alternative to offer the agricultural sector technologies that aim at an economically and environmentally sustainable production. Colombia has managed to position itself within the high quality cocoa producing countries and is recognized internationally for its finesse, flavor and excellent aroma, which is very important, since in this ranking classify only five percent of the total cocoa produced and marketed on the planet. However, the production has been limited by the presence and proliferation of diseases that can reach losses up to 50%. Pathogens like Phytophthora sp. and Moniliopthora roreri have been studied in the country given their epidemiological importance achieving favorable advances for the cocoa guilder. The aim of this work is to contribute to the knowledge of other pathologies that affect the cultivation of cocoa and of which there is little information as is the case of machete disease caused by the fungus Ceratocystis fimbriata Ellis & Halsted.
