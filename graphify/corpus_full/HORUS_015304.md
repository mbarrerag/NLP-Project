# immersive virtual reality in the design of urban illumination: state of the art, challenges and opportunities

This article presents the state of the art of immersive virtual reality (iVR) in the field of urban lighting design, as well as its challenges and opportunities. Since a well-lit city maintains a balance between decorative and functional aspects, such as public and road lighting, facade lighting, monuments and landscapes. Lighting design is one of the most important inputs in the process of urban modernization. However, traditional design methods limit user interaction and can create false expectations in the end result. In this sense, advanced lighting design methods such as virtual reality have become essential for assessing lighting systems, especially in aspects linked to user expectations. However, the review of the state of art presents that iVR is used without objective evaluation between virtual and real environments. Therefore, inmersive virtual reality in order to be used effectively in quantitative and qualitative analysis in lighting design must ensure a correct reproduction of light behavior from photometric and colorimetric points of view
