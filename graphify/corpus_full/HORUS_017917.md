# PROPOSAL FOR A RUBRIC FOR (AUTUM) PROJECT ASSESSMENT AND MAESTRY AND DOCTORATION THESIS

The aim of this document is to present a first approach to a rubric that provides elements for the evaluation of projects and thesis of master's and doctoral, mainly for the area of administration. Items are proposed for key sections in documents such as the project of thesis of master's, doctoral thesis project, thesis of master's and doctoral thesis
