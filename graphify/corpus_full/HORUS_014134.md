# Proposal for the delimitation and spatial and conceptual characterization of the functional metropolitan area of Bogotá

The project refers to a constant concern in terms of urban management in Bogotá, and to a central scientific concern in urban studies at a global level.In the first aspect, the discussion of the dynamics of metropolitanisation has been present at least since the 1950s when the generalization of the North American model of urbanization was opposed, in our environment, to the local narratives of planning that demanded a dense city and saw metropolitan expansion as a danger to avoid.This attitude against metropolitanisation has been maintained in urban politics with few modifications, however, this has not been an obstacle to a real process of metropolitanization marked by economic transformations.As a consequence, we have a metropolitanization _x0091_indeseada_x0092_ that has been implemented in a fragmented and inconsistent way, marked by the role of the economic actors: real estate, industrial and services; but far from the public intervention and an institutional and organized planning.In the second aspect, although the metropolitanization is a phenomenon whose development can be reversed, at least, in the 1920s with the expansion of the large North American cities, driven by the industrialization, is also a phenomenon. is clearly allocated to different activities in different places, from real estate activity for groups of different incomes, to industrial corridors such as those on the North Highway, 13th Street and 80th Street, as well as the concentration of different industrial and agro-industrial activity spaces. These changes will be analysed considering the evolution in land use, urban expansion, location of economic activity, comparative analysis of demographic dynamics, mobility aspects and also the assessment of the challenges imposed by the urbanization processes in terms of land impact, demand and water supply, disposal of waste and historically implemented governance strategies. All based on the analysis of the metropolization dynamics, using mainly information derived from the interpretation of satellite images and historical databases with a temporary window of just over 20 years (2000-2023).
