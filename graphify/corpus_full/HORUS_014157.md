# Uninadvertent Footprints

On the eve of the commemoration of the 65 years of the foundation of the Department of Sociology of the National University of Colombia and bearing in mind that next year the 100 years of the birth of Orlando Fals Borda are celebrated, we believe it is pertinent that academic works and artistic pieces are encouraged that investigate the history of the department and generate reflections of the importance that it has had in the university and the country. By creating a documentary audiovisual piece, our group hopes to promote reflection and debate on the relevance and incidence of sociology in the life of those studying, exercising it, or in some way its experience is crossed by this discipline, its scope and involvement in the National University of Colombia and in the country. From this, this documentary is made thanks to the recovery that our group achieved from an audiovisual archive of the year 2000. It is a recording of 3 hours and 25 minutes of duration, in format VHS, that registers the celebration of the 40 years of the Department of Sociology. From this methio we show the images of Sociology and students, professors and administrative (as) interacturismo in several spaces of this, as the coffee shop, and the main entrance. and the foundation of the Sociology department and it exposes its main approaches to Participatory Action Research. The documentary will use as a primary source of information and footage the aforementioned archive, but it intends to make new interviews with different people related to the department, including students, teachers, administrative and other people who are part of the life and daily life of the department. This audiovisual piece aims to use predominantly a wide range of film and artistic resources, seeing them as the perfect opportunity to take sociology to a sensitive and emotional field that can achieve a strong informative impact. The use of tools of design, photography, lighting, editing and film editing, will be key pieces to articulate the creation of a piece that not only highlights its content, but also for being a valuable display of novel narrative and aesthetic forms
