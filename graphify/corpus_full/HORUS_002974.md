# Assessment of adaptation to climate change in the Upper Suarez River basin 2016 – 2023

Given the evidence that indicates the presence of climate change and its likely impact on physical, biotic and socio-economic aspects in various parts of the world, different regions and communities are looking for options and implementing measures to reduce risks arising from this progressive transformation of the environment. Adaptation, which implies adjusting to the changing conditions imposed by climate change, is promoted in various territories by institutions, civil organizations and NGOs. Institutions such as the Cundinamarca Regional Autonomous Corporation (CAR), have been working on the assessment of the risks associated with climate change and have proposed and implemented adaptation measures in their area of jurisdiction for several years. In this process, the evaluation is based on the results of the adaptation actions implemented in the period 2016-2023. In addition, this work is aimed at assessing the adaptation to climate change in the Upper River basin, which forms part of the territory of the Cundinamarca Regional Autonomous Corporation (CAR). l years. In this process, the need has concretely examine the results of the implemented adaptation actions. Therefore, this work tools to evaluate climate change adaptation in the Alto Suárez River Basin, which is part of the territory of the Regional Autonomous Corporation of Cundinamarca (CAR). This evaluation is based on the actions implemented from 2016 to 2023. Additionally, impact/result indicators will be explored, and a diagnosis of the adaptation measures implemented by various actors in the base during the mentioned period will be conducted
