# Tax benefits and the effective tax rate – case international air transport company

The effective tax rate (TIE) reflects the percentage that represents the fiscal pressure in the determination of the income tax in relation to the accounting profit. Accounting as an essential component in the formulas for the determination of the effective tax rate, has been studied through different accounting theories, generating diverse results and interpretations. As a factor to mitigate fiscal pressure, the companies review and applied the benefits that the tax regulations provide. In Colombia, the international air transport sector has some peculiarities of the business, among others, because it deals with the provision of the service in different jurisdictions. The case study of the determination of the TIE is carried out in a Colombian company in the air transport sector (Colombian Holding), has two companies in Colombia, one in the air cargo transport sector and the other with several lines of business related to air transport international. This paper analyzes the main tax benefits used by the case study company, which impact the determination of the effective tax rate; especially, the legal stability contract, which includes the benefit of the deduction of the investment in real productive assets. Additionally, the main reconciliation items are identified, generated by the differential accounting and tax treatment.
