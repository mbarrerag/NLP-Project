# DETERMINANTS OF VIRTUAL PAYMENT CHANNELS IN THE PURCHASE OF GOODS AND SERVICES: EVIDENCE FROM A DEVELOPING COUNTRY

E-commerce has experienced strong growth both in volume of users and commercial websites, but also has had to face several challenges such as security, buyer confidence, payment methods to be offered, among others. We sought to identify the main determinants that influence consumers when making purchases of goods and services through electronic channels, based on the review of twelve constructs. The method scope was descriptive- correlational, for which an online structured survey was applied to 906 participants. The data analysis was carried out through a structural equation model, using the partial least squares-PLS method. Results showed that three independent variables: the use of credit cards, the ease of use of web pages and the development and evolution of e-commerce, influence the use of virtual payment methods, explaining 26.4% of the variance
