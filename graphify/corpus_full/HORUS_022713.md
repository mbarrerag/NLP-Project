# A So-called Occupation for the Humanities

ABSTRACT In this article we seek to provide a reflection based on philosophy and sociology around the occupation, if any, of the humanities. According to the hermeneutics of founding texts on the question of what is man and on different perspectives on it, we set up a reflection that aims to support the one that, according to the author's view, corresponds to the basic and determining occupation of the humanities. To this end, five sections will be developed in which we present an introduction to some basic ideas about humanities, foundations of a philosophical anthropology, what is man from the study of sociology, what is man from the study of biology and neurosciences, and the humanities as a whole. The article aims to contribute to the understanding that cultivating the humanities and developing them requires an intention to make these a conceptual and unfinished representation of the world? Not a piece of ductile clay to image and likeness by those who see in them the scenario of praxis by excellence.
