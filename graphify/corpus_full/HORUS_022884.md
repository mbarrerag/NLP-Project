# ANALYSIS TO THE STATE OF EDUCATION IN ETHICS IN THE POSGRADING PROGRAMMES OF THE METROPOLITIAN AREA OF THE VALLE DE ABURRÁ, COLOMBIA

The need to train students and society in ethics in general is becoming more evident in the growing cases of corruption and lack of ethics presented at all levels of society around the world. That is why educational institutions should be the first to include subjects within the curricula that facilitate such training at all levels, and to promote the appropriation and application of ethics-specific knowledge in personal, social and organizational situations. The aim of this research is to determine the state of ethics training in graduate programs of the Metropolitan Area of the Aburra Valley AMVA, Colombia, and to determine what it is like to include these topics in the curricula of the programs, for which a simple random sampling was carried out and 271 postgraduate programs were evaluated.
