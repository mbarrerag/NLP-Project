# Analysis of the constitution, developments and appropriation of digital communication in the metamorphosis of the contemporary world

The work is configured from four bets: the first explores a conceptualization of communication according to four approaches that identify it as social mediation (Martín Barbero, 2010), representation (Hall, 2010), generation of meaning (Pardo, 2006) and structural part of the process of individuation (Simondon, 2019). The above to analyze digital communication as a sociocultural, technological and ontological process, for which it is proposed an explanatory model based on four concepts: connection, interaction, personalization and relocation, which serves to illustrate the mode of individuation experienced by the contemporary subject in the digital world. The second bet, establishes a review of each of the paradigms of the epistemology of communication, indicates its validity and relevance to find explanations, possibilities of interpretation and methodologies that, although many decades ago were thought, facilitate the understanding of the communicative and sociocultural dimension of digital communication.
