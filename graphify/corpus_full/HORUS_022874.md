# PRELIMINARY RESULTS OF N-Butanol OBTAINATION FROM ETANOL DURING USE OF MIXTO DERIVED HYDROTALCITES OXIDES AS CATALIZED

The n-butanol is an important product in the industry given its wide range of applications and its high commercial value. In this project, preliminary results associated with the transformation of ethanol into n-butanol using mixed oxides derived from hydrotalcitas as catalysts are presented. The effect of the change in the molar relation Mg:To the catalytic activity.The catalysts subject to this study are mixed oxides derived from hydrotalcitas Mg:To the molar ratios 1, 3 and 5, synthesized by the coprecipitation method and subsequent calcination at 500°C. The materials were characterized by XRD, TGA and BET area.The catalytic activity was evaluated using a Parr® type batch reactor using 10 mL of ethanol and 0,250 g of catalyst.The reaction was carried out by the autonomic pressure in N2 atmosphere, at a temperature of 623 K for 6 hours.The reaction products were analyzed using a different gas chromatographer of Shimadzu GC-17A.
