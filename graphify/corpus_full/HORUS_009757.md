# PROPERTIES BY TRACTION AND TERMS DEGRADATION OF NANOPOPHOBIE EPOXI PREPARED BY MASS POLIMERIZATION

The mechanical properties of traction and thermal degradation of nanocompounds with natural and organically modified clay quantities were determined between 1 and 3% p/p. It was found that the values of the Young module were independent of the amount of clay. But the dispersion of the inorganic clay Cloisite®Na+ gave improvements in tensile properties up to 50% in effort and maximum force relative to the base polymer. The incorporation of polyethylene glycol gave plasticity and reduced hardness to the thermofix polymer. Through thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) clays were determined to slightly reduce the stability of the conventional polymer. X-ray diffraction (XRD) was used as a technique of analysis in nanocompounds prepared by polymerization in mass with polyethylene glycol (PEG). Nanocomposites, pure clays and the intercrossing reaction of conventional polymer were characterized by infrared spectroscopy with Fourier transform (FTIR)
