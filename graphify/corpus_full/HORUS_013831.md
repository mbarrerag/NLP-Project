# Biodiversity and defense of the Amazon. Social appropriation of knowledge of biodiversity by indigenous socio-environmental organizations of the Amazonian trapeze.

In Leticia there are several indigenous socio-environmental organizations (TIKA, TIWA, KAURE, among others) with origin in Amazonian territories (Leticia, Amazonas) that have the objective of defending water, forests, biodiversity and social welfare.Each of them was born in response to conflicts in their communities.Some of the most important are associated with overexploitation of fishing and timber resources, indiscriminate hunting, increase of mass tourism, use of pollutants, among others.These conflicts that were characterized in 2017 by a group of researchers denote the effects on the biodiversity and social welfare of the local population (Duque et al., 2018).The main causes of them are the increase of the population due to the displacement of indigenous families from other areas of the department and the country in search of education, health, employment and safety services from different places of Colombia. The following pillars are being identified by the following prototype: the positioning of indigenous socio-environmental organizations and their actions in the territory, the definition of a group of species with high social, environmental and cultural value for the organizations and the region, and the social appropriation of biodiversity knowledge in their families and neighbours. The defense of Amazonian biodiversity, water and forests will be possible through the strengthening of local organizations.
