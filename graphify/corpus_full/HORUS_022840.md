# THE SCHOOL OF SCIENCE

The National University of Colombia is founded in 1867. Between the years of 1868 and 1875, Francisco Bayón gave courses in botany, Liborio Zerda a course in geology and paleontology and Fidel Pombo several courses in zoology; additionally, the students and professors prepared speeches.They are all subsequent to the publication of the origin of the species of Charles Darwin (1859), so we ask ourselves the following question: did these courses follow the paradigm of natural history inherited from the New Kingdom of Granada Botanical Expedition, or did they settle in the modern biological tradition, interpreting nature in the background of the evolutionary theories arising during the nineteenth century, or even in the theory of evolution by natural selection proposed by Darwin and Wallace? Historical sources were consulted to determine whether between 1868 and 1875 the courses had a creative or evolutionary transfondo meaning.
