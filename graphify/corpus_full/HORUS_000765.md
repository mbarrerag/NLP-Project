# EQUIDS AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES (SDGs)

Thinking about the well-being of equidae necessarily forces us to look beyond, observe carefully for the sides, and understand that behind an animal there is an owner, a family, a community that connects in a close way. Additionally, these communities are influenced by the climate, the availability of drinking water, winds, among many other factors, generating a beautiful alliance between animals, humans and environment. Sustainable development invites us to analyze how we live today, seeking to guarantee a better future for the next generations, that is for the children that will be born in the coming years
