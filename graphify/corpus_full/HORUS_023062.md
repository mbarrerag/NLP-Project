# DENSITY OF PLAINTING, PRODUCTIVITY AND QUALITY OF FRUTA IN WATER WATER WATER CV. HASS IN THE DEPARTMENT OF ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA

In Colombia, for avocado cv. Hass, there is limited information on how certain agricultural planning practices influence the production and quality of fruit. In two lands of the municipalities of Rionegro and El Peñol, in the department of Antioquia and with the purpose of evaluating the effect of different planting densities in commercial avocado gardens cv. Hass on the production and quality of fruit, 9-year-old trees were selected, in full production, in good agronomic and sanitary condition, according to the certifications required for the export of fruit. A completely random design was used with three repetitions and six treatments (plant densities). According to the results significant differences were found in terms of the equatorial and polar diameters of the fruit in the different planting distances. No effect was observed on the fruit components with respect to planting densities. The average weight of the fruit, as well as tree production, also showed statistically significant difference according to the planting distance
