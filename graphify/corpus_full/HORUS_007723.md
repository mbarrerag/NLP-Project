# MANUFACTURING OF An UNTRACTED MONOCACO EQUIPMENT FOR PARTICIPATION IN A NAVAL ENGINEERING CONCOURSE

In this project, the design and construction of a single-hull type boat of 2 meters in length is carried out, powered by an electric motor of 1.4 kW. This project responds to the invitation for participation in the Naval Challenge: Promoting Sustainable Innovation in Colombia with which it is intended to contribute to the development of sustainable solutions for the country's river and maritime mobility
