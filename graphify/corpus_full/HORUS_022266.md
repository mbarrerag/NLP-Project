# Influence of environmental quality on the dynamics of the assembly of fish of commercial interest in the Bay of Tumaco, Colombian Pacific.

The estuaries are highly productive ecosystems, essential to support activities such as fishing. However, the anthropo-salpical activities drive changes on these ecosystems, which can affect the environmental quality of estuaries. Consequently, this may have effects on the availability of fish of commercial interest. The objective of this research was to determine the environmental quality of the Bay of Tumaco and to establish its influence on the biomass and density of fish of commercial interest in different climatic periods and estuary areas. For this, samples of fish were collected by means of artisanal trawling. In addition, the concentration of the physical and chemical variables was measured, and the granulometric composition and percentage of organic matter in 2 areas of the estuary, representing the spatial variability of the system, during the rainy and dry seasons to represent the temporal variability. Multivariate and univariate methods were used to evaluate the spatial variations in environmental quality, the biomass and density of 6 species selected from the biological value index (IVB): Ariopsis simnsi (35 points), Spirust. Biomass and density showed significant differences according to the interaction period and zone, were greater in the dry season in the inner zone of the bay. Biomass and density of fish of commercial interest were higher in conditions of low water quality, mainly due to the dominance of Ariopsis simonsi, Stellifer typicus and Achirus macatlanus. This could favor tolerant species that feed from the bottom and enter the area to feed on invertebrates that are stressed and exposed on sediment by the decrease of oxygen.
