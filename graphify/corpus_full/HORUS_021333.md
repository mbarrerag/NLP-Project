# Strategic partnership for strengthening institutional research for the development of magnetic nanofluid applicable in hyperthermia, as an alternative for the treatment of tumours in the national context.

Cancer is one of the diseases with the highest incidence worldwide and the main cause of death [1]. Within typical treatments for different types of cancer, there are chemotherapy, radiation therapy and surgery; however, there are multiple studies associated with adverse effects on the well-being of patients [2]. Due to this, the use of hyperthermia as complementary treatment to conventional ones has been considered, showing a reduction in the requirement of radiation therapy cycles, which improves the life expectancy of the patients treated [3]. However, techniques such as ultrasound, radiofrequency, infusion, microwave and phototherapy have been limited due to the fact that adequate distribution of heat in all cases, especially in large-sized tumors, has not been achieved.The use of magnetic nanofluids in interaction with high frequency AC magnetic fields, called magnetic hyperthermia, has been shown as an alternative of generation of localized heat, more precise than conventional hyperthermia, which has improved the prospects of use of this technique for cancer care. In addition, the Alliance will strengthen the University’s infrastructure through the purchase of equipment and the development of assemblies in which nanoparticles suspensions can be evaluated, which will be valued in the commercial potential of the allied company and by the animal husbandry and veterinary advisers who support and are part of the alliance
