# Design of a prototype information system based on real estate crowdfunding for financial leverage of construction projects in the city of Manizales.

In Colombia-Manizales 95% of the construction companies surveyed would be interested in participating in alternative financing methods such as real estate crowdfunding. For this research, the inductive method was used from the particular to the general, non-experimental research design was carried out, the paradigm of research is interpretivism, the approach of research is qualitative. The traditional means of financing of construction companies in Manizales was analyzed. As a research technique a survey was carried out to 20 builders of the city of Manizales where I throw that 55% of these builders do not have traditional methods of financing. The real estate crowdfunding method based on financial technology was analyzed to raise capital in construction projects with the systematic review methodology of literature “SLR” with some aspects of the method “PRISTA”, supporting that the real estate crowdfunding software is an efficient alternative for builders who are outside the banking sector or traditional alternatives and complementing it with the tokenization of real estate assets and blockchain. A business model and a prototype of information system were designed, which allows to manage the construction model
