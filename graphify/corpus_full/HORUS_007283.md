# VISION TO REALITY: BUILDING COMMUNITY THROUGH A WET WEATHER TREATMENT FACILITY

More and more utilities are constructing wet weather treatment facilities to help reduce combined sewer overflows and sanitary sewer overflows. While most wet weather treatment facilities have been located at publicly owned treatment works (POTWs), that option is not feasible in all circumstances. Utilities are increasingly locating facilities in the collection systems away from the POTW. These remote facilities offer a unique opportunity to help build community. This paper will present the approach King County implemented and results to realize the vision of incorporating community needs and goals in the design of a remote wet weather treatment station located in a heavily populated, urban environment
