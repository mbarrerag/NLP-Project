# ECUADOR: SMALL COMMUNITY WORKS GROWTH IN THE DAY OF ARCHITECTURE

The article highlights the current research of young Ecuadorian architects. Conceiving flexible, well ventilated spaces that adapt to the tropical climate, using simple, natural and economic materials, reusing, making extensive wooden roofings, are some of the themes of the new architecture realized in Ecuador. Thinking in community is another fundamental objective. Intellectuals, architects, builders, grown in a often rural environment, with common interests, manage to tighten strong ties. Works made with native workforce, local techniques and materials that promote cooperation. Projects promote pedagogy and creativity. Simple, economic, reinterpret ancient and popular teachings. A conscious approach that allows the active participation of the community in the different stages of construction, use and maintenance of works
