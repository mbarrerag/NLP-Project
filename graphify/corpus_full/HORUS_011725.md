# DEVELOPMENT AND ANALYSIS OF A Meteorological DATA BASE DEPURING FROM THE GEOGRAPHICAL VALLEY OF THE CAUCA RIUM

Continuous monitoring of weather variables is of the utmost importance for understanding weather phenomena at local and regional levels, as well as for understanding the role of meteorology in air quality in a region, and monitoring extreme events such as floods. Therefore, protocols for the assurance and quality control of meteorological data should ensure the proper functioning of monitoring equipment (US EPA, 2017), which allow for reliable information to be obtained for monitoring, climatology and simulation purposes. However, as part of meteorological information analysis it is common to find data that are considered atypical, produced by failures in measurement equipment and associated various problems, including failures in electrical fluid and insufficient maintenance and verification procedures, often related to the chronic scarcity of human and technical resources needed in monitoring networks. In this study, the processing and analysis of meteorological information for the Cauca river valley (VGRC) was carried out, resulting in the geographical dispersion of air pollutants.
