# RESEARCH IN LATIN AMERICA. RESEARCH IN LATIN AMERICA

These results show a significant increase in research published in this database over the last decade, which may be related to the increase of researchers in the field of anesthesiology in LAC, and to the strengthening of relations with public and private universities, national and international alliances to research in the specialty and above all, in protecting the environment (Green anesthesia, low flows, patient safety, etc.)
