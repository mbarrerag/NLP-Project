# Design and production of ceramic scaffolds through stereolithography with potential application in fabric engineering

It is estimated that in Colombia currently around 3 million women suffer from osteopenia and 1.5 million suffer from osteoporosis. Additionally, the increasing increase of traffic accidents proper to large and medium cities, results in a high percentage of fractures that require permanent attention by health systems. Most of these cases require surgery for their repair, demanding the use of implantable devices of sizes and personalized shapes, with a latent risk of bacterial infections, main cause of the failure of bone implants. The use of autografts can cause painful procedures and compromise the functions of the organ or healthy tissue. The grafts of materials manufactured synthetically based on engineering arise as promising alternatives using structures called scaffolds to replace the affected tissue or organ. These mimic the characteristics of the extracellular matrix and allow incorporation into the host body.
