# STRUCTURAL LIGIUM ON THE COLOMBIA PEACE AGREEMENT: REFLECTIONS ON THE ROOMS OF POLITICAL AND JUDICIAL INSTITUTIONS

Based on the analysis of the structural litigation scenarios regarding the implementation of the Final Peace Agreement in Colombia (AFP), it is argued that political and judicial institutions have incorporated practices (habits) typical of the routine follow-up of the Constitutional Court to the unconstitutional States of Things (ECI). While judicial institutions reproduce these practices, doubling even the follow-up, political institutions reiterate the answers without a substantive response to comply with failing. As a result, structural problems remain in the area of legal discourse and the materialization of rights is deferred and extended indefinitely
