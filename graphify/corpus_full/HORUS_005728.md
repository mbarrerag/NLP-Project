# CHARACTERIZATION, IDENTIFICATION AND QUANTIFICATION OF THE DIAGNOSIS BARRIERS IN THE COLOMBIAN PORCINO SECTOR

The early detection and diagnosis of swine diseases is essential for timely health intervention. For example, it can help reduce antimicrobial resistance (RAM), which occurs when microorganisms undergo changes when exposed to antimicrobials; and this increases the risk of disease spread, which is considered a public health problem worldwide. The inadequate diagnosis of diseases in farm animals is one of the main causes of the emergence of the ADR. For this reason, the timely and adequate diagnosis of diseases is one of the main strategies for their prevention. Recent evidence suggests that in Colombia pig producers do not access the infrastructure or diagnostic services available in the country. This article characterizes and identifies the main barriers to access to the network of diagnostic services and quantifyes their importance.
