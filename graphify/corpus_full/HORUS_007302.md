# Pharmaceutical intervention in older adults in a fourth-level health service provider institution in Bogotá: Identification of risk factors related to polypharmacy in the care of the elderly hospitalized from the pharmaceutical intervention

The ageing of the population is one of the main challenges in public health of the 21st century, particularly due to the high prevalence of multimorbidity and polypharmacy in the elderly. This situation increases the risk of adverse events related to medicines, hospitalizations and inappropriate prescription. In this context, this study evaluates the impact of pharmaceutical intervention on elderly hospitalized patients, by applying the Drug Adjustment Index (MAI) and identifying drug related problems (PRM). A comparative study was conducted between an intervention group, which received pharmacotherapeutic review and conciliation of medicinal products by the hospital pharmacist, and a control group without intervention. The results showed a significant reduction of 26.4% in the final MAI score in the intervention group, as well as a greater identification of PRM (426 versus 326 in the control group). In addition, a high degree of acceptance (67.3% of the pharmaceutical recommendations were observed by the medical team. The findings reinforce the need to implement interdisciplinary pharmaceutical care strategies, focused on optimisation of pharmatherapy, prevention of adverse events and the improvement of clinical outcomes in the population.
