# gastrointestinal manifestations of the COVID-19 in a Colombian population Cross-sectional study

Summary Introduction: gastrointestinal involvement in COVID-19 is presented in 20% of patients approximately and may include nauseous, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhoea or liver profile alteration. In our country, the characteristics of gastrointestinal involvement in patients with COVID-19 have not been studied. to determine the prevalence of gastrointestinal and hepatic involvement in patients with COVID-19 attended in two hospitals in Bogotá (Colombia). To determine the association of gastrointestinal involvement by COVID-19 with hospital stay, severity and mortality. cross-sectional study, carried out in two hospitals of a hospital subnet in Bogotá (Colombia), between February 2020 and March 2021. 1176 patients with RT-PCR (reverse transcript polymerase chain reaction) positive. Gastrointestinal manifestations were presented in 50% (part 95% 47-52%), the most frequent were diarrhoea in 18.4%, or more ODID 17.6%, anorexia 14.7% and abdominal pain in 8.8%
