# Effect of ractopamine and Cr-yeast supplementation on leptin levels, haematological variables and fatty acid profile in pigs

The aim of this study was to determine the effect of ractopamine (RAC) and chromium-yeast (Cr-Lev) on the fatty acid profile, haematological parameters and serum leptin levels in finishing pigs. For this, 20 pigs of 63.2 ± 7.6 kg and 118 days of age were assigned to four treatments during the last 30 days of fattening. The treatments were T1: control group with basic food; T2: addition of 10 ppm/kg of RAC, and T3 and T4 with 0.2 and 0.4 ppm/kg of Cr-Lev without addition of RAC, respectively. Serum leptin levels were quantified at days 15 and 30, backfat thickness at day 30, and haematological analysis at days 0, 15, and 30 of the study. In addition, the profile of fatty acids in the meat was determined by gas chromatography. A completely randomized design with five replicates was used.
