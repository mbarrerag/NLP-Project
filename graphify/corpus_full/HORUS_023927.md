# REDAP ONE: A TOOL FOR THE ANALYSIS OF PRICIPTATION DATA CAPTURED IN THE URBAN MICROCUENCA OF THE CAMPUS OF NATIONAL COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY

In Colombia, the use of precipitation data continues to be the main input for hydrometeorological analysis and studies of various kinds, such as the construction of design storms, the obtaining of intensity-duration-frequency curves, and, in general, to support hydrological modelling. In this regard, the development of a tool that allows updating, consulting, visualizing, downloading and analyzing storm data is of wide utility. To this end, the application "Repository of rainfall data National University (REDAP UN) has been developed. This article describes in detail the tools developed in the application and its functionalities. RedAP UN was designed to store and process about 42 million precipitation data with minute accuracy, taken in 14 dual pluviographers located within the urban micro-basin of the university campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Bogotá between the years 2007 and 2012. The implementation of the REDAP UN allows users to interact openly, optimally and efficiently with the pluviographic information registered at the University, saving time in the manual processing of this information. REDAP UN is the first analysis of the hydrologic data that allows for users to have a very high-level environment
