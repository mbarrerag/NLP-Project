# SUSTAINABLE RURAL ELECTRIFICATION TO PARTICIPATE LOCAL ENERGY POOTENTIALS A LOOK AT COLOMBIAN NON- INTERNET AREAS

The electrification of rural areas, especially in remote and difficult to reach areas, poses a significant challenge in terms of reliable and sustainable energy supply. This article carries out a comprehensive review of electrification options through renewable energy, analyzing their economic, environmental and social benefits and limitations. International studies are highlighted that demonstrate the successful implementation of various forms of energy generation in isolated areas to meet the electrification needs. These technologies seek to improve the quality of life of families in non-interconnected areas by exploiting local energy potentials. In addition, a diagnosis of the current system used in Colombia to supply electricity to isolated areas is carried out using data from the Single Information System (SUI) and the Institute for Planning and Promotion of Energy Solutions (IPSE). This diagnosis allows identifying the type of generation present in different locations in the country's non-interconnected areas, as well as its characteristics. In conclusion, the article provides an integral view of the challenges and opportunities related to rural electrification, stressing the importance of considering sustainable approaches and solutions based on local resources to achieve an efficient, reliable and sustainable energy supply in these areas
