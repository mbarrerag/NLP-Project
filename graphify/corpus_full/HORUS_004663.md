# SERIOUS GAME DESIGN FOR THE TEACHING AND IMPLEMENTATION OF FINANCIAL PROJECT ASSESSMENT

The ability to plan, analyze and make informed decisions on projects is crucial for success in any work environment; in engineering projects have various applications and constitute a field of study relevant to future engineers. Specifically, project evaluation tries to establish the financial viability of initiatives and allows to make decisions in a relevant and accurate way. Concepts such as flow of funds, revenues and costs, depreciation, depreciation, value of money over time, financial indicators, among others, are fundamental to make such evaluation, however, constitutes a little known area for some engineering external to topics related to financial environments. Thus, it is identified the need to create tools that allow future engineers to train in financial evaluation of projects, through teaching, practice and application of the main concepts associated with this, for subsequent decision making successful. Serious games are tools designed with a formative purpose, in which there is a game of participant in a controlled environment that allows to teach and apply the desired concepts, through entertaining and practical elements. As a future work, the test phase of the game will begin, and work will continue on consolidating the final version of the proposed tool, which will later be used with engineering students, for their training in financial evaluation of projects
