# Functional, physicochemical and sensory properties of a fermented milk beverage with addition of auyama (Cucurbita maxima) and corn starch (Zea mays)

The fermented dairy drinks (BLF) have responded to the growing demand for products that are characterized by their functional value, pleasant taste and benefits for consumer health. The aim of this research was to evaluate the effect of the concentration of auyama seed flour (HSA, 1.5; 2.0 and 2.5 %), auyama pulp (PA, between 6.0 and 8.0%) and modified maize starch (MMA, between 0.4 and 0.7 %), on the functional, physical and sensory properties of a sweet whey BLF. Each beverage was evaluated by pH, acidity, total soluble solids (SST), CIEL*a*b* coordinates, syneresis, zeta potential (-), viscosity and sensory attributes. The results showed that with the addition of 1.5 % HSA in the BLF, the highest score in flavor and general acceptability was obtained by consumers. The increase in PA concentration caused a significant effect on o and L* values, obtained the agglomeration threshold and favouring the perception of yellow colour of the BLF.
