# Assessment of the Dynamic Water Balance hydrological model on a daily scale in tropical basins.

This final master's work presents the results of the evaluation of the Dynamic Water Balance model (DWB) at daily scale to represent runoff in tropical basins. The present project used as input different data sets generated in the framework of the National Water Survey 2018 (ENA2018) developed by the IDEAM. The information provided corresponds to a total of 497 basins, of which 30 were selected for this study. The selection of basins was made through the algorithm of unsupervised classification k-means that considered morphometric, hydroclimatological, demographic and geographical variables and its relation to a variable that quantified the change of coverages. Thus, the evaluation of the uncertainty given by the parameters of the model was identified.
