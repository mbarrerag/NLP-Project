# Incidence of reporting events with serious unwanted effects associated with health care in hospitals of high complexity. Systematic review and meta-analysis.

It is estimated that between 10% and 36% of hospitalized patients suffer some type of involuntary damage, the figures vary according to the type of hospital and report, without there being a grouped estimate of serious events in hospitals of high complexity.The objective of this study was to determine the incidence of reported events with serious undesirable effects in hospitals of high complexity described in the literature and to make a grouped estimate, by subgroups of risk and methodology used. Systematic review and metaanalysis of primary studies carried out in institutions of high complexity including university hospitals in patients who have presented reportsable events with serious unwanted effects during their hospitalization. Search by two reviewers in electronic and manual databases in English and Spanish languages without date limit, evaluating bias with tools for incidence studies. A meta-analysis of proportions with and without transformation, with evaluation of heterogeneity of studies and estimating the predictive value for proportions. PROSPERO registry: CRD42020181786. 19 studies were chosen, a patient incidence of serious events was found between 0.3% and 29%, in 6 studies carried out meta-analysis of events leading to 7% of events, with a breakdown of incidence of events of serious events and 7%
