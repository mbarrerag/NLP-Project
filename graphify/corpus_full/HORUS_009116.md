# INDIRECT REDUCTIVE MINATION OF AROMATIC ALDEHYD WITH HYDROXIDE OF HONEY

In this study, the indirect reductional alumination of aromatic aldehides with ammonium hydroxide was studied as an accessible method for the synthesis of benzylamines. The results allowed to establish that the condensation of aromatic aldehides with ammonium hydroxide produces compounds known as hydrobenzamides, and that its reaction with boronhydrone produces equimolar mixtures of primary and secondary benzylamines. This study analyses this behavior and proposes a possible mechanism of reaction
