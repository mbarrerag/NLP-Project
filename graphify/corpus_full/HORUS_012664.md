# Management as a catalyst for the relationship between innovation and bioeconomy

The bioeconomy or bio-based economy has gained increasing relevance in the context of the Sustainable Development Agenda and in the face of crises such as those generated by the Covid-19 pandemic, climate change and the growing population. Understood as the production of goods from biological resources such as biomass, it requires specialized knowledge and innovation dynamics to achieve its mission, so it is essential to efficiently manage processes for the generation and implementation of innovations. Therefore, the objective of this article is to analyze the role of management to revitalize the relationship between innovation and bioeconomy, for which a review of scientific literature was carried out through a scientometric exercise. It was found that the number of publications on the subject is still very low, which represents an opportunity to contribute from various fields. The identified articles have contributed to the conceptualization of innovation, the study of the processes to generate innovative solutions, the key actors, the methodologies for the development of such solutions and the identification of challenges of innovation management within the framework of the bioeconomy, regarding the complex technological and knowledge base, the development and use of convergent technologies and the commercialization of bioproducts
