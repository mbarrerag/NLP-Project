# Inhibitory effect of substances from species of the genus Piper on Quorum Sensing of Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

Pseudomonas aeruginosa is an opportunistic bacterium classified as a priority critical by the WHO and is associated with a wide range of infections mainly in intensive care units (ICU).The pathogenicity of this bacterium is regulated mainly by the communication system called Quorum Sensing (QS) that allows it to control its virulence factors.This system has been related to resistance to antibiotics and therefore has been proposed to be inhibited from substances from plants as a strategy for the control of resistant microorganisms.The plants of the genus Piper have been characterized by having antibacterial, antifungal, insecticide, antioxidant and cytotoxic properties, however, few studies focused on the inhibition of QS. Therefore, in the present study the potential of the substances from the species of the genus Piper, and the formation of the chemical (Pypluminaria) was determined by the plant's production as well as the main inhibitors of P. aeruginosa. The methodology carried out included the identification of some species belonging to the genus Piper with potential for the inhibition of QS and the formation of the biopelicle. The identified compounds are reported for the first time for the species and its structures are in accordance with the chemotaxonomy of the genus Piper. Finally, this study highlights the inhibitory potential of virulence factors and biofilm formation of compounds with multidiana potential that corresponded to amidas (C9 and C10), hydroquinones (C8, C18 and C19) and benzoic acid derivatives (C16). present in species of the genus Piper. Additionally, with the results obtained, some preliminary structure-activity relationships were established on the most active compounds. This research contributes to the search for natural origin inhibitors that can serve as an alternative to control QS in P. aeruginosa to reduce bacterial resistance, by characterizing the inhibitory potential of substances from some species of the genus Piper
