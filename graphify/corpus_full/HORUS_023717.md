# ENDOGNE DEVELOPMENT A OPPORTUNITY IN THE CHACO AND CAQUETA MINOR EXTRACTIVISM, IN THE COLOMBIAN POSSULTURE.

Colombia is one of the mega-diverse, mega-water and mega-miner countries, a potential that strategically places it in the global framework; but mining is the fundamental axis that allows a rapprochement to the different socio-economic and environmental processes in the post-conflict framework, as an important activity in generating economic resources for the different activities planned in the Havana Final Agreement; it recognizes the inability of the state institutions to reach the territories far from the cities and to make the controls and controls that are required by law, leaving a vacuum for the development of traditional and artisanal mining alternatives in reserve areas and with foreign investment
