# Work system for the management of research data. Case Universidad Nacional de Colombia

The National University of Colombia, as the main institution of higher education, requires a working system (information system) that allows to optimize the management of research data. This study aims to propose essential elements for the design of an information system that responds to the needs of the various actors in the research processes. This study was developed under the multimethodology of Mingers, which included a review of the literature, the application of a semi-structured interview aimed at researchers teachers and the formation of an expert panel to evaluate the different elements of the dimensions of the work system. The proposal articulates nine dimensions: clients, products and services, processes and activities, participants, information, technology, environment and strategy, which identify key elements in each of them. The importance of products and services that respond directly to the needs of the clients, as well as the dimension of the processes and activities, aimed at establishing clear procedures for their implementation by the participants. The results suggest that the infrastructure must be robust enough to support the technological tools required, while the organizational environment should encourage ethical behaviors that guarantee the transparency of the research data management of the institutions. esses nine dimensions: clients, products and services, processes and activities, participants, information, technology, infrastructure, environment, and strategy, each outlining critical elements.
