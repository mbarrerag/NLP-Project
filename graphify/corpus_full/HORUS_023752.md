# INTERNAL(S): Modes of relating (me) (us) (se)

The following text (written, sound and visual) contains the path and transformation that was (and is still given) given by encounters, disagreements, losses, coincidences, reflections and revisions of what constitutes relating; relating to us; relating to me — with me, ourselves and the others. The body, skin, breathing, voice, look, movement, these are the modes: my modes, our modes. . The following text (written, audible and visual) contains the route and the transformation that takes place (and continues) thanks to the accountants, sameches,losses, coincidences, thoughts, and revisions of what constitutes to relate: we relate, I relate —withself, between us and with others — The body, skin, break, voice, gaze, movement; these are ways: my ways, my ways, others
