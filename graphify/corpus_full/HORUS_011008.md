# DAY CONSUMPTION OF FUNCTIONING YOGURES WITH COLOMBIAN OILS SOURCE OF ALPHALINENIC AND OLEAN ACIDS: EFFECTS ON LIPIDIUM PROFILE AND APOB/APOA1 RELATIONSHIP IN SUCH HANDLES

Cardiovascular disease (ECV) is the main cause of global morbidity and mortality; in Colombia for 2019, 130.4 deaths per 100,000 inhabitants were by ECV (OPS,2021). In addition, the recent health emergency caused by the COVID-19 has worsened the clinical results in people with pre-existing CVD (Voulalasetal.,2021). Within the strategies of prevention of CVD, is the development of lactic beverages with modified fatty acid profile (Villamiletal.,2023). Therefore, in this study two Colombian oils were vehiculated in yogurts. Sacha Inchi oil (ASI) and the palm hybrid oil (AHP) are sources of alpha-linolenic acids (ALA) and oleic data.
