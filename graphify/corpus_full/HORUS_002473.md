# TOWARDS THE TRANSFORMATION OF THE COMMUNICATING AND HUMANITARIAN EMPROACH

In Colombia, different governmental initiatives and public policy instruments supporting entrepreneurship emerged in 1980, with the aim of materializing business ideas and fostering the entrepreneurial culture of the country, considering the particularities and needs of the territory and its entrepreneurs. Public policy instruments and their respective support programmes, which make up the national entrepreneurship ecosystem, provide support and financing through the provision of seed capital, training in entrepreneurial, innovation, managerial and productive competencies, as well as in the generation of alliances and value connections. Given their significant relevance at the national level, these mechanisms are called to improve and consolidate their support systems, adjusting to the needs of the entrepreneur and the challenges of the country's political, economic and social context
