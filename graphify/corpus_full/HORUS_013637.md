# Study of vernacular architecture in the hill of Yuca in the municipality of Sutamarchán, with a view to its conservation and protection

The loma de Yuca is a well defined sector geographically that is part of the vereda Santo Ecce Homo, located in the municipality of Sutamarchán, in the department of Boyacá. It is a place of great historical, archaeological and natural richness framed in a rural landscape that has evidenced a continuous occupation since the prehispanic period. Its landscape has experienced various transformations, especially due to the impact of the Spanish colonization and the implementation of different extensive agricultural practices. In its territory is imbued a vernacular architecture very particular works related to what used to be a small village called Yuca. This place has passed from being a Muisca indigenous settlement to become a town of Indians and later in parish, to finally be dismantled at the beginning of the 20th century, although some of its inhabitants continued occupying the territory. Studies were carried out in thirteen rural dispersive housings with the objective of understanding and characterizing the constructive techniques, the materials used, the state of conservation and the relationship with the landscape.
