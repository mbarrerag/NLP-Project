# University governance analysis from the perspective of New Public Governance: recommendations for the National University of Colombia

The present paper aims to analyze the model of university governance in the light of the theoretical perspective of the New Public Governance. The need to reflect on university governance occurs in the context of promoting a more democratic participation of the university community in the decision-making of the National University of Colombia. This is at the same time that it faces the demands of interested external actors such as the State, the productive sector or the graduates themselves. Through a documentary review, examples of the implementation of university governances are presented that combine innovative elements of theory with the social, political and economic realities of universities. Finally, some pertinent recommendations are provided for strengthening governance in the National University of Colombia
