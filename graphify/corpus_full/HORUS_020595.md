# The open book of the territory. Spaces of thought, memory and speech in the Sierra Nevada

Why do the peoples of the Sierra Nevada consider the territory to be an open book? What is the origin of this metaphor?, what meanings and meanings do this symbolism contain? and what message do the native and original peoples seek to convey by suggesting that their conception of ancestral territory is like that of a book for civilizations centered on alphabetic writing? They are some of the concerns that the present essay attempts to address from the establishment in relation to traditions with different and sometimes opposite systems of knowledge and communication such as orality and writing, but that also allow opening spaces of contact and intercultural dialogue.
