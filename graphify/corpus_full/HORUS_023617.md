# The semiotic representations (decimal numerical and one-dimensional figurative registration) in additive operations with integers through the ThatQuiz site.

In the seventh grade of secondary basic education, it is common to identify difficulties in additive processes with integer numbers, this presumably arises by making mathematics in the educational context, a purely operational action, that is, the treatment of mathematical terms is given more importance than to conversion processes, thus preventing their understanding. Instead, the use of semiotic representations (decimal numerical representation and one-dimensional figurative) is necessary in the development of additive operations. In this sense, there is the need to design a system of ten (10) activities that merge the treatment and conversion through the website ThatQuiz. These activities are grouped into a set of four activities: Exploration or recognition; Foundation: Operations of additive order; Evaluation and Projection. And the methodological strategy is addressed from the interactive (where the technology is involved), the meta-cognitive (where the understanding of mathematical content is promoted through the relationship between treatment and conversion of mathematical terms) and reflection (validate the importance of technology in the educational process and mathematics in the context)
