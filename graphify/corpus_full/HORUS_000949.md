# Innovation and competitive advantage: theoretical review based on multiple correspondence analysis

The globalizing phenomenon that represents the greatest challenges for the global productive sector has been that of significant differentiations in its products and services. On this front, industry specializes in changes in demand and the criterion of its clients. As a result, the creation of value in the face of competition takes a privileged place in the implementation of strategies, especially in productive contexts characterized by intempsual inflections of the market. Therefore, innovation is considered as a tool for the effectiveness of strategies in organizations. This review of 134 publications establishes the relationship between innovation (inn) and competitive advantage (vc); the methodological framework integrates text mining, analysis of multiple and clusters correspondences, and finds a tendency towards the study of inn as an interactive process, while the vc maintains a vision in resources, knowledge and links with the company sector. They are associated by internal capacities, resource allocation to research projects, identification of market opportunities and establishment of links with the institutional innovation system (State). A central limitation of the inquiry is the scarcity of previous studies in the service sector
