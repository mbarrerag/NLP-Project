# Integrated methodology for product design including sustainability criteria and palm oil value chain requirements

The decision-making process for the design of chemicals is a key activity to increase its acceptance in the market and improve its performance in the field of sustainability. To this, it is important to take into account not only the preferences of consumers, but also the requirements of the supply chain, which are not taken into account in the existing design methodologies. Therefore, this study presents a methodology to support the product design process taking into account the requirements of the supply chain. The methodology is implemented in 2 phases: a phase of diagnosis of the supply chain, in which the different stakeholders are interviewed to know its limitations; and a phase of design of the product, in which a workshop including the limitations identified in the design problem. The methodology was tested in a case study: the design of the chemicals from the supply chain of the palm oil. The palm oil is the most productive plant oil, but is in the point of view of its implications in environmental and social issues.
