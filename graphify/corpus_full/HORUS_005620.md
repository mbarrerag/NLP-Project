# ANALYSIS OF THE VARIABLES OF CLIMATE IN THE VALLEY OF THE RIUM CAUCA

Biomes are regions of the planet that share similar geological and climatic conditions (Strakhov, 1967), whose interaction is initially translated into characteristic soils on which typical vegetation develops. In the sugar cane producing region of the Cauca River valley, three biomes predominate: the Orobioma Azonal, the Helobioma of the Cauca Valley and the Alternohigric Zoom Tropical of the Cauca Valley (Valderrama, 2013). The Zonobioma Azonal, located on the foothills of the Western mountain range, is characterized by being very dry. The Helobioma of the Cauca Valley, located in the flood areas of the Cauca River (flood plains), is characterized by its dry climate. The Alternohigric Zonobioma, located on the alluvial fan, is also characterized by being dry in the flat area, but is more humid near the Central Valley. This means that in the valley of the Cauca River there are different ecosystems, all warm, from very dry to humid areas.
