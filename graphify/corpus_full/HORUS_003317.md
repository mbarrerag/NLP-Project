# EFFECTS OF THE INGESTION OF MICROPLASTICS ON THE CONDITION FACTOR OF TWO FAMILIES OF FISHES IN THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC

The presence of microplastics (MP) in aquatic ecosystems has been identified as a problem due to its potential effects, not only on fishes, but on humans. For this reason, this research aimed to estimate the incidence of MP in fishes from the Ariidae and Sciaenidae families, in the Colombian Pacific. On the other hand, the influence of the trophic level (TL) of the fish on MP consumption and its possible effect on their condition factor (CF) was determined. According to this, fishes were collected in different climatic periods during 2020 and 2021 using a trawling net. A total of 758 individuals from Buenaventura Bay were captured and their gastrointestinal content was analyzed. The results showed that 18% of the individuals of Ariidae and 8% of Sciaenidae presented MP. Furthermore, the species with higher TL, presented higher MP consumption (p(Per)=0.039), and a decrease in de CF (p=0.00002- rs. =-0.43). The Red sea catfish (Bagre pinnimaculatus) and the Silver drum (Larimus argenteus) were the species affected the most, since their CF diminished 21% and 25% respectively. These results suggested a decrease in the well-being of individuals due to the ingestion of this pollutant, which are consistent with studies in different coastal areas around the world
