# Changes in fluidity in oral presentations through awareness and emotional regulation skills in A1 level English students at the Colombo American Center in Bogotá

In the English language learning students generate emotional states that facilitate or block the development of oral skill. For this reason, the present mixed-cutting research was intended to analyze the possible changes or transformations in the fluidity in oral presentations of students of the A1 level of English who were part of a program designed to strengthen emotional competencies of consciousness and regulation. It was carried out for eight weeks, during which a strategy was proposed to develop the competencies of consciousness and emotional regulation in 24 students of a binational English center located in the city of Bogotá, Colombia. Initially, two groups were randomly chosen from their classes, one experimental and one control; both took an entry test to compare their level of fluid before the beginning of the process. Then, a series of workshops were designed in which the students of the experimental group were instructed on how to work the competencies of consciousness and emotional regulation, while the group took control from their classes of regular form. After implementing the workshops, an exit test was carried out in both groups with the aim of comparing their results. In addition, there were collected daily field, videograms and interviews that qualitatively validated the results of the process. two participation groups were random chosen, one experimental and one control; both groups took a fluency pretest to compare their level of fluency before the beginning of the research process. After the test, several workshops were designed and participants of the experimental group were instructed on how to work on awareness and emotional regulation competitions, while the control group took their regular language classes. After implementing the workshops, a fluency post test was carried out out in the two groups in order to compare their results. Additionally, students’ journal, class video records from and interview were equipped to validate the results of the processes qualitatively
