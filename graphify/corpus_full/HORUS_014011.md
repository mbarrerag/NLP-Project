# Study of hydrogen storage in new porous materials

The aim of this project is to study one of the current global energy problems related to the storage of hydrogen for fuel use. In particular, it is intended to study new materials for adsorption, such as graphene, doped graphene and decorated graphene. For the project it is intended to synthesize and characterize the graphene by means of electrochemical exfoliation partition of raw materials such as graphite in leaves, platinum electrodes, sulfuric acid, organic solvents (DMF, NMP or cyrene). The general process consists of two stages: the first production of graphene praistine or graphene oxide, in which an electrochemical expansion is carried out with the help of a platinum electrode in an aqueous acid medium (which the concentration changes in function of how oxidated the graphene oxide or graphene of the prístine is required), and a second stage in which, by means of thermal treatment, the modification of the nanomaterial obtained to obtain its various variants, thus, with the help of an autoclav and furnace it will be possible to carry out decoration of the different derivatives of the graphene, while a controlled by a thermal or by a thermal treatment
