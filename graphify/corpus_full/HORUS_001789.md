# Spatial prediction of sugar cane crop yield (Saccharum officinarum) by machine learning

The cultivation of sugar cane is fundamental for the agricultural economy of Colombia, particularly in Valle del Río Cauca, a region that faces challenges such as climate change and efficient resource management.In this context, the use of machine learning (AA) and geospatial data technologies is presented as an innovative solution to accurately predict crop yield, optimizing water use and other key inputs.This study proposes a methodology that integrates historical climate, soil and agricultural management data with AA algorithms such as Random Forest, XGBoost, and Catboost, in order to develop robust predictive models that provide spatial estimates of sugarcane yield.The results show that AA-based models exceed traditional data (how linear and penalised regressions) methods, providing more precise predictions that can be implemented for agricultural planning and resource management in the Valle del Río Cauca, more specifically in the municipality of La Candelaria.The research highlights the potential of AA-based techniques to improve productive efficiency and contribute to a more sustainable agriculture (the context of the Cataca valley).
