# Spanish-Speaking South America: Lutheranism in a Catholic Culture

The objective of the study is to characterize the Lutherans of this region in four dimensions: the demographic dimension, the provenance dimension, the moral dimension, and the ecumenical dimension. To do this, we rely on quantitative data from Lutheran organizations, such as the Lutheran World Federation, and on in-depth interviews with Lutheran experts in the main countries of the region. We find that Lutheranism reveals a pattern of stagnation and decline in numbers. By tracing the origins of Lutheran churches in this region, we find that those South American churches started by particular denominations from the north still wield influence among these daughter churches in either liberal or conservative directions
