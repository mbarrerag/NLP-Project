# Geomorphology and Tectono-stratigraphy of the Neogene of the northwest sector of the Vaupes-Amazonas Basin

The use of geophysical methods in geosciences for surface and subsurface studies in the Amazon region can contribute to knowledge of the geometry and three-dimensional arrangement of rock layers located hundreds of meters deep. The integration of surface and subsurface data includes analyses of geomorphological structures not yet described in the area. This study used seismic sections and well profiles to generate a deep geological model. The geomorphological and tectono-stratigraphic evidence in the area was obtained from the evaluation of surface and subsurface information
