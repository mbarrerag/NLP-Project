# ASSESSMENT OF OPPORTUNITIES FOR USE OF ELECTRIC TAXIS IN VILLAVICENCIO

In Villavicencio, Colombia, with a population that surrounds the 500,000 inhabitants, there is an intention by the municipal administration to modernize the public transport fleets: buses and taxis. It is recognized the operational disorganization, problems of quality of service, congestion, pollution and improper occupation of public space. As a proposal is identified the possibility of incorporating electric taxis with urban operation in the city seeking to reduce the polluting emissions, position the city as a national reference and generate changes in the operating schemes of the taxi type service. Result of a survey, 42.86% of the current taxi drivers are interested and 28.57% fully interested in acquiring an electric taxi. The previous experience in Bogotá shows the economic competitiveness of the technology versus gasoline solutions. In Villavicencio it is essential to use electric vehicles of moderate cost and to appropriate all the existing tax benefits. Likewise, the participation of companies of the electrical sector and the support of the local administration are key to driving and strengthening the initiative recognizing that it will be a long-term process. From the city, it requires a master plan of mobility that generates the public parkings in strategic sites, new operational concepts of service and electric services are used
