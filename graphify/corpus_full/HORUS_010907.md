# The Niche Group beyond legend: History and analytical characterization of discography between 1979 and 1999

This thesis proposes an investigation into the Colombian salsa orchestra: The Niche Group and its work during the 1980s and 1990s, based on the different interpretive possibilities offered by the discography; this document addresses the historical, the style of the grouping, the process of creation and production of the LPs, the covers, as well as the identity construction of the group in the discourses of its musicians, of which the qualifier of ‘Pacific salt’ is highlighted, and its subsequent adherence to the romantic salsa. .
