# Perceptions and emotions present from the experience of being a teacher in inclusive early childhood education

This research seeks to highlight the experiences, subjectivities and emotions of early childhood teachers in relation to inclusive education, highlighting the fundamental role they have in guaranteeing the rights of diverse students. The study recognizes and dignifies the efforts of teachers who, often without specific training, implement inclusive practices that improve educational quality. The methodology used is qualitative, with a phenomenological approach, in which a little explored aspect is reflected in previous research: the emotions and perceptions of 8 early childhood teachers about the inclusion processes in Bogotá, Colombia. To do this, two instruments of information collection, the questionnaire and the focal group where the narrative booklet ̈counts of aula ̈ is proposed. .
