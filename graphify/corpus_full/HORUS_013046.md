# Urban growth in the Toronto region between 2011 and 2016: population and housing

The Toronto region in Canada is one of the fastest growing regions in North America. It is also the centre attractor of the largest number of immigrants in the country and its main economic and financial center. The region has been the focus of study of numerous research; however, given the magnitude and speed of its growth, it requires constant monitoring. This research aims to interpret urban growth in the Toronto region from a population and housing approach between 2011 and 2016. To this end, a qualitative method is used for the analysis of key variables such as natural growth, immigration, migration, housing, housing typology, population density and housing density.
