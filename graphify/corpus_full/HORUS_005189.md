# ANALYSIS OF THE OVERCOME OF CHARTER-INSTITUCED LINCHEVAS CAREERS

Longevas carers may experience overload during care, representing a risk factor of irritation for both them and the person cared for. The aim of this study was to assess the characteristics and factors associated with overload in longevos carers in the city of Cartagena. Cross-sectional study, which included carers of longeval and centennial persons. Through a structured survey and using the scales of Zarit redu cida and Katz, sociodemographic characteristics and caregiver overload were evaluated. A total of 101 persons with longevovovovocar role, with average age of 54.3 (±16.2) years, being 83.5 % female sex. The average age of longevo was 84.8 (±12.6) years, represented both female sex and functional. 87% of carers reported to be family longevocar. Mild or absent dependence was identified in 52.4 % of longevos; while the average age of longevos was overburdened in 16.8% of the female association.
