# Patients assessed by high blood pressure teleconsultation during the pandemic by COVID-19 in Tucumán, Argentina

During the pandemic by COVID-19 the use of telemedicine increased considerably as an alternative to provide care in health.In this context emerged the Virtual Consultorio de Cardiología del Sistema Provincial de Salud de Tucumán, Argentina (SIPROSA). Describe the characteristics of patients with or without previous diagnosis of arterial hypertension (HTA and non-HTA, respectively) who were evaluated by teleconsultation at the Virtual Consultorio de Cardiología del SIPROSA during the pandemic by COVID-19 due to high blood pressure (PA). Transverse descriptive study performed in 275 patients (HTA=167; no-HTA=108) attended by the Virtual Consultorio de Cardiología del SIPROSA between July and September 2021. Bivariate analyses were performed (ANOVA, T-Stoden and blood pressure test χ2) to determine differences between groups (HTA vs. No-HTA) according to sex.
