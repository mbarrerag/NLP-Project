# Geomechanical and petrographic characterization of humic coals of the Sinifana basin sector Amaga Titiribí Antioquia.

Coal is a fragile and heterogeneous material, and its composition varies according to its genesis, which creates a challenge when addressing its analysis as an engineering material. For this reason, this research addresses the study of its main characteristics from the point of view of quality and resistance. For this, the analysis of 7 mantles of humic type coal with high degree bituminous in volatile type C in mines in operation, which belong to the Sinifana basin in the municipalities of Titiribí, Angelópolis and Venice. The tests executed include a close analysis of quality (wetness, ash, volatile matter, fixed carbon, total sulfur and calorific power), a maceral count using computer-based segmentation procedure and laboratory resistance tests (point load, uniaxial compression and triaxial).From the point of view of quality, the results showed characteristics common to the coals of this area. Additionally, the vitricite group was found as the predominant maceral, which shows linear tendency inverse to the resistance. The above is an effect related to the Maceral-Colotetura group
