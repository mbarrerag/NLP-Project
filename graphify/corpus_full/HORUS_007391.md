# Corporate governance and gender equity in Latin American companies

Gender equity is a foundation of great importance in the social construction of a prosperous and sustainable world. However, a gender inequality problem persists at the global level that leads to stagnation and causes difficulties to social development. This paper analyses the influence of corporate governance on the promotion of gender equity within Latin American organizations, especially from the composition of its boards through a descriptive and exploratory study. It is structured into five chapters, the first of which raises the study problem, the second contains the initial review of literature applying the deductive method, compiling the main concepts and ideas raised in theories related to gender equity and corporate governance, as well as the factors that link them. The third chapter brings together the analysis carried out to the regulatory frameworks related to this topic, the fourth chapter presents the results of interviews conducted to men and women who occupy managerial positions in large companies in Latin America and, on the other hand, a panel of experts on these issues around the world, and then, to carry out a contrast and analysis of the information collected, and finally in the fifth and last chapter, presents the results and conclusions that show that the corporate government is an influence of gender (a. ries related to gender equity and corporate governance, as well as the factors that link them.
