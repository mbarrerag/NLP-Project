# Intervention of the Cultural Interests of the San Pedro de Usme Church

The study of the Church San Pedro de Usme located in 137 C street 14 7 in the city of Bogotá, Colombia, presents the possibility of understanding the historical, social and religious development surrounding the only 16th century doctriner temple within the urban perimeter of the city, which still retains the rural vocation. During the development of this work, formal, spatial, technical and material analysis of the building is carried out, as well as the sector in which it is located, recognizing four representative moments in which the most powerful intervention was made, accompanied by an architectural survey and a diagnosis of the state of conservation in which the damage mechanisms present in the building and its context are identified. This process culminates in the attribution of values and the approach to criteria and guidelines of intervention from the urban and architectural scale, in order to generate a proposal that this is in accordance with what the building represents and respects the development that the church has had for more than four centuries. .
