# Strategies and tactics for a policy of legalization and regularization of informal settlements. Case study Communes 4 and 6, Soacha

This research study focuses on formulating strategies and tactics related to the legalization and regularization of informal settlements in Colombia, taking into account their evolution during the period 1989-2023. It was carried out through a co-investigative methodological framework, involving active collaboration with community leaders of communes 4 and 6, of the municipality of Soacha. The resulting diagnosis identified physical-spatial problems affecting the quality of life and revealed limitations in the effectiveness of legalization policies. These guidelines, based on the vision and community organization, address land tenure through a community land trust, the progressive provision of public housing services, the adequacy of urban regulations through risk studies, public space and equipment through alternative solutions, the hierarchicalization of the road infrastructure and the strengthening of community leadership. The construction of these guidelines was carried out in collaboration with community leaders, thereby validating the relevance of the proposals.
