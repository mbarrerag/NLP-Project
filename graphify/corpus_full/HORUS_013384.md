# Articulation of music schools with a symphonic vocation in Santiago de Cali: An assessment from the management of stakeholders in their value chains

The present master's thesis focuses on the municipal music schools with synphonic orientation located in the municipality of Santiago de Cali. In this context, the identification of the interest groups (stakeholders) linked to the various links of the value chain has been carried out, encompassing the formation, creation, circulation, exhibition and consumption of cultural products. These products include both educational services and the execution of musical performances, both generated by these schools. The central purpose of this project is to evaluate the possibility of creating articulation scenarios, allowing the construction of institutional links not only among the participating music schools themselves, but also with cultural and academic entities of the municipality. Thus, we seek to promote an integral collaboration that benefits all the parties involved and contributes to the development and promotion of symphonic music in the community of Santiago de Cali.
