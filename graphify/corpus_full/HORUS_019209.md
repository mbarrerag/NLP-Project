# Expert consensus on the management of thyroid cancer in children.

Thyroid cancer is the most frequent endocrine neoplasia in the pediatric population, it has a more aggressive behavior because the rate of malignancy of thyroid nodules is higher in children compared to the adult population and cursa with local metastasis at the time of diagnosis. In our country there are no protocols that guide the treatment of thyroid cancer in pediatric population. In the Fundación Hospital Pediátrico la Misericordia (HOMI) we have identified an increase in the number of cases with the passage of years however we do not have an institutional consensus for the management of this pathology. We proposed the elaboration of recommendations using the GRADE methodology focused on achieving an early diagnosis and adequate treatment. Three questions were formulated according to the PICO methodology, systematic reviews of the literature were carried out for each question, following the methodology of the Cochrane manual and taking into account the importance of the outcomes. The quality of the studies and the recommendations were written as a central ultography (US) were classified according to the criteria drawn by GRADE and were validated by consensus of the patients
