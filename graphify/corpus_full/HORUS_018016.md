# Assessment of the effect of biochar application on agricultural soils based on nutrient migration.

Soil is a complex ecosystem involving chemical, physical and biological phenomena that interact and influence among other phenomena in the availability of nutrients. This work evaluated with a mathematical model, the effect of application of biochar in soils. Transport phenomena coupled to a chemical equilibrium model were involved, offering a panorama of the interaction of nutrients in the soil, involving phases of the soil, effect of roots, reactions of oxidorreduction, balance, transport, release of nutrients, effect of biochar, leaching and saturation of the soil; all the above solved by finite differences. An adjustment was found between the model and the results reported in the literature. The transport model is coupled by the method of Godunov to Phreeqc that solves the chemical balance, which was compared experimentally finding that it is coupled to the soil dynamics after day 16 with a percentage of difference of 0.48% for K, 3% for NO3, 0.02% for NH4+ and 7.2% for P. It was identified with the application of biochar, an increase in the pH of a soil for sandy soils, increased in the soil with a percentage of humidity between 24-29% and more than in all soils
