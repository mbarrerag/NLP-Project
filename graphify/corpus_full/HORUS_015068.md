# methodological Proposal for the implementation of the digital transformation for the activities of storage and transport: case study logistics operators in Bogotá

The national logistics survey identified that storage and transportation activities represent more than 81.7% of the logistics cost. Due to this, it is important to look for tools that allow reducing the impact on the cost structure of companies. The appearance and use of new technologies is affecting the way in which society interacts with organizations, this process, which is known as DT, represents an opportunity to reduce logistics costs. However, more than 50% of the companies are not implementing DT processes, this can be evidenced in the results of the national logistics survey. For this reason it is important to build a methodology that helps companies to implement digital transformation processes, the methodology that was used in this research work is a case study in logistics operators located in the city of Bogotá and obtained are the following: through a literature review it was possible to identify the success factors for the implementation of the DT, in a later phase a characterization of the logistics operators was carried out where the main result was the identification of governance and organizational structure as main barriers to the implementation of the DT. Finally, a methodology based on Business Process Management is proposed, taking into consideration that this methodology seeks to improve processes within the organization and is highly related to the implementation of new technologies within the organization
