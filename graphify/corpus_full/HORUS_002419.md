# Evaluation of the production of lactic acid from a cluster of microorganisms native to a Colombian biorefinery

In this investigation, a total of sixteen strains of native lactic acid bacteria were isolated from a Colombian biorefinery, from musts of alcoholic fermentation (10) and sugarcane honey (6) This cluster was evaluated for the production of lactic acid in the medium of Man, Rogosa and Sharpe (MRS) (100 mL) under anaerobic conditions, at 150 rpm and without pH control. The effects of temperature (37, 39.5, 42 °C) and fermentation time (0, 8 and 24 hours) were evaluated on the production of lactic acid (BAL) which achieved a production of the metabolite of interest, similar to the pattern strain Lactobacillus delbrueckii ATCC 9649 were Pediococccus acidicic and Pediococuspentosaceus 1 with concentrations of 12 gL-1 and kinetic parameters, 0.61 Yp/s and 0.51 gL-1 medium-to-inctitus (42 °C)
