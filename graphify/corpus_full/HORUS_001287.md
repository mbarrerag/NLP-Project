# The establishment of Free Zones in the Province of Sabana Centre: Economic and territorial transformations in the municipalities of Tocancipá and Gachancipá 2005-2022

The purpose of this research is to identify and explain the main economic, demographic, regulatory and territorial transformations result from the implementation of Free Trade Zones in the municipalities of Toncipá and Gachancipá within the framework of the metropolitan process processes that occurred in the period 2005-2022. To this end, the study examined the relationship between the economic development processes, the location of the economic activities, and the role of the State in regulating the land market; the regulatory evolution of the Free Trade Zone system in Colombia, the competence of municipalities in territorial planning as well as the economic impact of the economy produced by the implementation of this figure of the specific economic situation in the municipalities reviewed., the informality or quality of life of the inhabitants of these municipalities
