# Public actions of unconstitutionality in Colombia (2013-2023) analysis in a socio-cultural and transnational perspective.

The public action of unconstitutionality in Colombia is a particularly interesting legal tool. It is not only one of the oldest constitutional control mechanisms in the world, it is also an action at the disposal of citizens in general, contrary to what happens in most countries of the world (Mendieta, 2010). In addition to the above, within the democratic exercise, the control of constitutionality by the citizenry plays an essential role in the defense of fundamental rights (Romero, 2016) and the progress of them. In view of the above, this project is questioned about the traceability that has been had in the country during the last 10 years.
