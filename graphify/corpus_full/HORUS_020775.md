# NOTES ON THE NATURE OF ENVIRONMENTAL AND DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH IN COLOMBIA UNIVERSITY EDUCATION INSTITUTIONS

The text analyzes, from Latin American environmental thinking, some theoretical-practical aspects of research in the environment-development twinning in higher education institutions in Colombia, starting from accepting the complexity of their objects or fields of study. It examines the role of personal efforts in these approaches, reveals some characteristics of what could be considered or not environmental research and proposes that these may be considered, in an operational taxonomy, as interdisciplinary context research. It discusses some particularities of this type of research in higher education institutions, in particular the obligatory theoretical approach of some graduate programs, the demands and difficulties of leveling knowledge, the contributions of consultancy and the difficulties in establishing lines of research. Finally, it addresses the necessary institutional and personal tensions that generate these interdisciplinary approaches
