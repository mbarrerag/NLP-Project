# Fireflies and hugs

They re-create community stories like LUCIÉRNAGAS AND ABRAZOS, nesting narratives and collective realities that through objects, sonorities and urban images enable the aesthetic transcendence of the theatre of objects at interdisciplinary experimental levels. The paper summons a poetic that re-sizes from the symbolic the sense of community, inherent bond of the home - bonfire - house - neighborhood. That area near the urb, the flood plain of the Cauca river in the east of Cali, is the place where “the homeless” raise their ranches with what they have at hand, “light and temporary materials.” On the nights of the district Yira Castro cocuyos de luziernagas titilantes que menguan su intensión a que se lost again in the darkness. .
