# DENTAL DEVELOPMENT OF ADJACENT TEETH TO NONSYNDROMIC CLEFTS: RADIOGRAPHIC EVALUATION.

dental development of adjacent teeth to nonsyndromic clefts: radiographic evaluation. The objective of this study was to determine the differences in dental development of teeth near to nonsyndromic cleft lip and palate (NSCLP) in Colombian children using the Nolla (MN) and Haavikko (MH) methods. A Case control study was carried out, dental age was evaluated in panoramic radiographs of 146 NSCLP patients and 171 healthy controls by Nolla and Havvico methods; The sample size was calculated with 95% confidence and 80% power, and it was selected paired by age and sex. The statistics were performed in the STATA program (v16) using paired t tests. A statistically significant underestimation of the dental age of LPHNS subjects (-0.29 ±0.83 years) was found with the Haavikko method, and a significant overestimation for the healthy controls (0.16 ±0.87 years) with the Nola method. In the comparison of LPHNS subjects with matched healthy controls, the longest delay in the development was observed on the left lateral incisor: 0.68 years for (MN) and 1.04 years for (MH).
