# The integral urban articulation, a solution to the problems of fragmentation of the city in the Park San Cristóbal and May Day

This work arose from the concern that exists for the isolation between natural processes and urban processes, which have weakened the mechanisms of spatial integration in cities. The interventions in the urban space individually, without including the total context generate processes of segregation and fragmentation. The intervention area is located in the city of Bogotá, to the south east, where the parks San Cristobal and Primero de Mayo are located of metropolitan scale and of recreational character. Its natural environment is important because of its proximity to the eastern hills and the Fucha River, body of water that crosses the city. However, the analysis of the site presented three types of fragmentation: a functional fragmentation that arose from the inadequate evolution of the urban plot that subdivided the area of the parks until achieving a deficient and disintegrated reading between itself and with the environment, especially with the urban image Fucha, which lacks environmental and landscape network due to having been projected as a barrier and not as a strategic point for mobility and communication within the neighborhood. This disintegrated reading generated an environmental fragmentation in which it was evidenced that the parks do not relate as an environmental network and landscape through the structure of the structure of the structure and the structure of the rural area.
