# OVERVIEW OF THE SYSTEMIC RESPONSE BY THE SINTENIZED MASS AMORTIGATOR INNERTER (TMDI) EMPLOYING HEURISTIC AND DIFFUSALOLOGICAL METHODS

One of the fundamental tasks of seismic-resistant engineering, is to develop new methodologies and devices that help to efficiently protect structures when subjected to dynamic actions such as seismic loads. Within the field of passive dampers, is the inertor or TMDI tuned mass damper (tuned mass Damper Inert). TMDI is a shock absorber that connects the classical tuned mass damper TMD (tuned mass Damper) with an inertor type element, mechanism that is able to withstand a large axial load with very little mass due to its internal configuration. The inertor is basically defined as a device of two nodes, with the property that equal and opposite forces applied in nodes, are proportional to the relative acceleration between nodes. The concept of inertor was born with the idea of completing analogies between mechanical and electrical devices. This work focuses on performing numerical performance analysis of TMDI devices in multiple levels, subjected to lateral loads by means of the known acelerograms at national and international level
