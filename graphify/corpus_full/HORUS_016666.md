# In-silico evaluation and microwave-assisted synthesis of heterocyclic compounds with pyrazolopyridinic nucleus as potential allosteric modulators of GABA-A receptors.

The present study aims to provide new methodologies for the synthesis of pyrazolopyridinic nuclei, tetrahydropyrazoloquinoline and tetrahydropyrazoloisoquinoline, through multicomponent strategies involving 5-aminopyrazoles, cyclic ketones and electron-rich olefins, using conventional and microwave-induced heating, where synthetic exploration led to an innovative methodology that allows the regioselective obtaining of the studied isomeric structures. On the other hand, this research seeks to provide bioactive molecules that can be used for the treatment of some central nervous system disorders involving the GABA-A receptor, therefore, an in-silicon evaluation of the proposed prototypes was carried out that includes an inquiry of the pharmacokinetic, pharmacodynamic and affinity properties by the receptor, exhibiting a promissory behavior as potential allosteric modulators of the GABA-A receptor
