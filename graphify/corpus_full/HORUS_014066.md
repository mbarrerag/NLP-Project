# Habeas Data of Big Data in the Financial Law of Consumption

This research focuses on studying the risks associated with the use of Big Data in the financial sector with respect to the violation of the protection of Habeas Data of financial consumers. It asks whether the regulation with which Colombia has in terms of data protection is sufficient regarding the risks involved in technological progress in data science, because the detailed profile of consumers allows to persuade, restrict or of plan to prevent their decision making seriously affecting their free choice and the materialization of their fundamental rights without the latter having full awareness of the processing of their data. In this section will address the following items: I) Concept of big data, II) Applications of big data in the financial sector, III) Concept and scope of the right of habeas data and last IV) Identification and evaluation of the risks of big data in the financial sector with relation to the right of habeas data of financial consumers. The Big Data is a discipline of the computational science that focuses on the collection, management and analysis of large data sets, the extraction of conclusions from this analysis and the application of these conclusions to specific cases and the data. (ii) Identification Discrimination or financial exclusion of financial consumers, due to the use of predictive algorithms or models that may generate biases or errors in the assessment of credit risk, negatively affecting the rating or access to financial products or services. III) Loss of control or transparency over the personal data of financial consumers due to the complexity or opacity of Big Data analysis processes, which may hinder the effective exercise of the right to have data, i.e. knowledge, update and rectification of information contained in databases or risk centres
