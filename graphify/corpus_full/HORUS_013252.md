# Participatory evaluation of the food sustainability of chontaduro (bactris gasipae kunth) in the department of Guaviare, 2023.

The study focused on assessing the sustainability of the agri-food system of the peasant, family and community-based chontaduro based farming, with the aim of identifying strengths and weaknesses of the current system and proposing sustainable alternatives. The FoodSAT methodology was used to provide scientific knowledge based on evidence and to promote promotion strategies that improve agri-food sustainability. The farmers’ families of chontaduro were the objective of the study. The results revealed the complexity of the system and highlighted the importance of collaboration between communities, universities and other entities to achieve sustainable development. Furthermore, it was found that the cultivation of chontaduro contributed positively to reducing violence in the territory. In conclusion, it is recommended to promote sustainable agricultural practices and policies that promote a fair and sustainable agri-food system in the Guaviare region. .
