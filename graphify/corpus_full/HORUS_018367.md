# IMPORTANCE OF THE GENETIC RESOURCES OF THE PIÑA (COMOSUS ANANAS [L.] MERR. VAR. COMOSUS) IN COLOMBIA. IMPORTANCE OF GENETIC RESURCES OF PINEAPPLE (COMOSUS ANANAS [L.] MERR. VAR. COMOSUS) IN COLOMBIA

The pineapple (Ananas comosus [L.] Merr. var. comosus) represents one of the productive systems with the greatest participation in the fruit and vegetable sector in Colombia. Plant genetic resources are the biological basis of global food security and are also the raw material indispensable for the genetic improvement of crops. In Colombia, planting an improved variety of pineapples is a major problem, because it displaces the use of native cultivars, which have a cultural roots in production and consumption at each site. This problem could be solved by knowledge, characterisation, conservation and sustainable use of genetic resources, which are sources of genes of interest and which could be used to start a breeding programme for this species.
