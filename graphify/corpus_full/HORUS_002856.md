# ADVANCEMENTS IN FRQI ON SUPERCONDUCTING PROCESSORS: AN OVERVIEW FROM RECENT RESEARCH

Quantum computing is an alternative for executing processes traditionally handled by classical machines, such as image processing and time series analysis, specifically, in the treatment of images of large size. Classical computing continually strives to enhance its processing capabilities for these tasks, situations such as these show some advantages of quantum computing over classical computing and motivate the research in this field. Flexible Representation of Quantum Images (FRQI) addresses the challenges and limitations of implementing quantum image processing techniques on current quantum hardware. Several improvements of the implementations and results are provided on the FRQI approach, reducing its computational depth and optimizing time and computational resources on real quantum computer backends The study attempts to bridge the gap between theoretical quantum image processing methods and their practical application on real quantum processors, particularly in the noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) era. The results show that although FRQI is theoretically valid, its practical implementation on current quantum hardware is constrained by noise and gate errors
