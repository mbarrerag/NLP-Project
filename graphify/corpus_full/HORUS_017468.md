# Manual for mounting post-ensed woodboard for use in vehicle bridges in low traffic roads in Colombia

In the development of this grade work, the most appropriate procedure is presented for carrying out the construction of a post-stressed wooden board for use in vehicular bridges, taking into account methodological and technical aspects necessary for its ideal implementation, operation and maintenance. This type of structure is proposed to address in an agile manner access problems in tertiary roads, generating with its implementation advantages in the environmental area, sustainable, construction times, easy procedures to execute by local labour and without special preparation, low investment costs, simple maintenance, promoting the use and responsible commercialization of wood species in Colombia, specifying types of wood with potential use in heavy construction. . In the development of this paper, the most acceptable process is presented for the construction of a post-stressed wood deck for use in vehicles bridge, taking into account mechanical use and technical aspects necessary for its ideal implementation, operation and management
