# Diagnosis of the state of mental health in schools as a basis for the implementation of health promotion, prevention and care programmes in a school in Gachancipá, Cundinamarca

The comprehensive care of mental health in students faces challenges by ignoring the consideration of social factors, contextualities, background of current problems and needs of children and adolescents (NNA). The lack of precise identification of these needs hinders the effectiveness of programs of promotion and prevention of mental health in schools, with significant consequences not only in the educational field, but also in the social, health, economic and development of the person. This study starts from the premise that recognizing the precursors and demands placed on students can guide interventions or actions that respond to their real needs, thus contributing to their well-being. Currently, there is a lack of scientific support for the inclusion of programs of prevention and promotion based on the evaluation of these factors. As a result, the programs implemented tend to focus more on the attention of the problem itself rather than addressing the underlying elements that could trigger it and, therefore, prevent it. The importance of schools as allies is highlighted, given the insufficient overall investment in mental health and restrictions in access to adequate services. This urges the implementation of effective actions aimed at preventing the mental illness and/or associated school problems. The law of the precise identification of these needs means the effectiveness of mental health promotion and prevention programmes in schools, with significant consequences not only in the educational real but also in social, health, economic, and personal development. This study is based on the priority that is collected by the precursor and situated demands of students can guide interventions or actions that address their real needs., serving as a basis for future studies, and guiding the creation of effective programs from a systemic and contextual perspective. Specific objectives include reviewing existing experiences, characterizing mental health needs through surveys, and applying the Human Development Scale matrix to understand the emotional and psychological demands of students. Inclusion, the study recognitions the importance of identifying the needs of NNA prior to the development of disease prevention and health promotion programs in educational institutions
