# Projecting place in the city from social function: spatial alternatives for intervention of the sector of the Public Market of Barranquilla

The cities on a daily basis are growing and in many places the regeneration of urban areas that have fallen into obsolescence is necessary. Usually, when considering a process of this scope, the essential elements that guide the design decisions to promote real places, that contribute jointly to the development of the person and highlight the social function that entails the definition of the city are lost. This document develops a proposal of qualification for the sector of the Public Market of Barranquilla based on the social function of the city as a guide of the urban change, succeeding in solving the urban obsolescence that it currently presents. Hence, with the revision of concepts, an analysis of the sector, and the identification of project strategies, it is possible to realize the meaning of the social function for the city, as well as to formulate strategies that allow to qualify the area, highlighting this purpose and arriving at defining spatial alternatives for its qualification. . making of its social function for the city, as well as formulating strategies to quality the area by highlighting this purpose
