# Alternatives to cash payment in the digital age, for permanent betting companies in Colombia

For permanent betting companies in Colombia, cash is the preferred payment instrument, although recently a incursion has been found in other channels such as web pages and applications for transactions, consolidating the use of electronic payment online means, which generates the research guide question: What are the alternatives to cash in the digital age, for permanent betting companies in Colombia?. This paper addresses the research question based on a qualitative methodology focused on interviewing managers and decision makers of these companies and analyzed through content analysis, where cash is found to be the main means of payment in the sector, due to the preference of older customers of low socioeconomic strata. The trend towards digital media presents advantages in the prevention of asset laundering and security, but also disadvantages such as the reduction of income and barriers such as lack of connectivity and financial inclusion. The diversification of physical payments where digital payment and the implementation is faced as high costs of implementation. The most mentioned means that could be adapted to the sector are the QR code, virtual wallets, transfers and payment links. Finally, the proposed scenarios are the strengthening of the transactional payment system and financial inclusion (the digital sector is the most effective).
