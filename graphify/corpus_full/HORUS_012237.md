# Predicting demand for drugs in hospitals through stochastic approaches.

The health sector is changing rapidly today. Hospitals are facing increasingly limited budgets and high costs. The logistics activities of hospitals in France (stock management, delivery, etc.) represent one of the most expensive components. Logistics costs can be reduced by an optimized inventory management system. The optimization of inventory depends to a large extent on the accuracy of the prediction of drug demand. The first objective is to make a state of the art of existing methods to predict drug demand in health centres. Many factors influence this demand, such as seasonality, size and location of the hospital. Consequently, a stochastic method may be relevant to capture fluctuations in demand. A second objective is to use historical data from a French hospital to predict drug consumption through a Markov chain. An analysis of experimental results is proposed to assess the effectiveness of the method. The result could contribute to the management and dimensioning of hospital inventories
