# Institutional performance and challenges PANIAT: Reflections on policy evaluation.

The research project is based on the participatory action research methodology (IAP) approach, with the development of focal groups, workshops and semi-structured interviews conducted with key actors to support the process of Evaluation of the food and nutrition security policy implemented in Tumaco over the past years (PANIAT 2012-2022). This will allow us to answer the question: What was the degree of institutional progress achieved in Tumaco following the implementation of PANIAT 2012-2022? This way, contributing to the achievement of the phases of the public policy cycle and the strengthening of the processes that impact the fight against hunger and food insecurity in the municipality
