# VALUE OF RISK, NUTRITIONAL ASSESSMENT AND TREATMENT WITH NUTRITIONAL SUPPORT SPECIALIZED IN THE NATIONAL UNIVERSITY HOSPITAL OF COLOMBIA

Malnutrition is defined as a state that results from the absence or decrease of the intake and the insufficient absorption of nutrients that leads to an alteration of the body composition with subsequent clinical, physical and mental deterioration. This condition may be due to disease, starvation, or advanced aging, alone or in combination(1). Malnutrition related to diseases in hospitalized adult patients is a syndrome associated with a substantial increase in morbidity, disability, short-term and long-term mortality, poor recovery of the disease, hospital re-entry and the cost of care(2). This condition affects between 20-50 % of patients in hospitalised patients(3). About 66% of patients entering with nutritional deficit have a deterioration of their condition, and 33% of patients with adequate nutritional status develop malnutrition(3). However, their prevalence is higher in intensive care units, where the prevalence of the patients in hospital has been reported 38 - 78% (4). In Colombia, the most patients entering with nutritional deficit are not different from those found worldwide.
