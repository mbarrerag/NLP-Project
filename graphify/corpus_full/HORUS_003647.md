# Double purpose bovine agroecosystems typification in Tuluá, Valle del Cauca, Colombia

The slopes of the department of Valle del Cauca, Colombia, concentrate double-purpose cattle agroecosystems (AGESBDP), which are characterized by high complexity and intraregional variability, as well as low technification and negative impacts on biodiversity.The aim of this study was to typify AGESBDP located in the middle area of the San Marcos sub-basin (Tuluá, Valle del Cauca), from the economic, productive, ecological and environmental dimensions.To this end, a representative random sample of 30 AGESBDP was selected.The information was obtained from registers and from the application of a structured survey; the data were analyzed by means of descriptive statistics, analysis of main components (ACP) and clustering of clusters.The two types of AGESBDP (T1 and T2 were identified; the topographic, productive and income/ha characteristics of the properties were determined by means of the AGESBDP.
