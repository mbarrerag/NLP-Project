# Improvement in the post-harvest phase of amaranth cultivation in ASOGRANG from traditional knowledge and a technological proposal

The amaranth is an ancestral cultivation that is being recovered by the Association of Farmers of Guatiquia (ASOGRANG) after being eradicated in the colonial period in Mesoamerica. Its recovery process has been difficult due to the absence of technology for the threshing process of the post-harvest stage (separation of the seed of its coating), so the association resorts to artisanal methods that are not efficient. Consequently, it has been proposed to determine, in conjunction with ASOGRANG, the appropriate technology to improve the post-harvest process of the cultivation Amaranthus caudatus taking into account the ancestral knowledge that the community has around it.
