# OBTAINATION OF COLLAGEN TO PARTING FROM ANIMAL Woven Woven fabrics SUBPRODUCTS OF THE CARNIUM INDUSTRY

Collagen is the most abundant structural protein in the human body, for this reason it is widely used in tissue engineering. When good mechanical performance of the final product is required, it is intended to maintain with the highest content in long fiber. For its characterization it is used to techniques such as electrophoresis and infrared spectroscopy by Fourier transform, which allow to determine the purity and protein content of the same. Characterize collagen obtained from chicken and pork for subsequent use in cardiovascular tissue engineering. The dissolution of the collagen source was performed in acetic acid at 5%. Collagen was characterized using electrophoresis tests using the SDS-page method and the infrared spectrometry by Fourier transform using the method of analysis by reflection with the ATR SPECT accessory and range from 600 to 4000 1/cm, to determine purity. The results provided collagen type I and III in the mixture of legs plus crest of chicken. Also collagen type I in the samples of legs, chicken crests and pork skin.
