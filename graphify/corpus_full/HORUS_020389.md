# Study of labour performance and consumption in foundation activities in the construction of single-family housing in the municipality of Tame, department of Arauca

This research project is the result of the study of the yields and consumption of the labour force in foundational activities in the construction of single-family housing in the municipality of Tame, department of Arauca, in which observations and data were made to be analyzed statistically. It was supported by 2 construction companies in the area for data collection, of which 8 housing projects were developed and 10 activities in the foundation component were analyzed that presented dimensional and structural similarities. This project aims to address the lack of databases of performance and consumption of the labour force in the construction activities of the municipality of Tame and thus to be employed in similar projects taking into account the importance of good planning and estimation of costs and the duration of a project. The result of the project is the performance and consumption of the labour force of the 10 activities selected for study, to achieve this 619 measurements were carried out for about 4 months. .
