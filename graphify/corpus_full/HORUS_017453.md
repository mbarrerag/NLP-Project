# MAXONOMIC OVERVIEW OF THE ALCHORNEA GENERUS (EUPHORBIACEAE) FOR COLOMBIA

The genus Alchornea belongs to the family Euphorbiaceae, and possesses about 60 tropical species. In the neotropics, Colombia is the country with the highest number of species, which are widely distributed in all natural regions of the country, but with a greater concentration in the Andean area and from the level of the sea to 3150 m of altitude, generally growing in disturbed areas. In Colombia, the species are characterized by being trees or dioic shrubs without exudation, and are covered by star hairs; the leaves are simple, with glandular maculas and of alternate disposition, but sometimes they are opposite or verticilated; the venation is triplinervia, but can be pinnada. The female flowers have ovaria, usually bicarpelar and with two elongated species; the seeds are very determined by being covered with a bright red aril.
