# RENAL AGUDA

Internists are specialists who apply scientific knowledge and clinical experience for the diagnosis, treatment and care of adults in all aspects, from health to complex disease. They are especially well trained in the diagnosis of puzzling medical problems, in the continuous care of chronic diseases, and in the care of patients with more than one disease
