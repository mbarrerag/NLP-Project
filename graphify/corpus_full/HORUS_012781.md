# Transforming Colombia: Sustainable Development Goals

Inequality weakens social cohesion and the confidence of the population in governments, institutions and their peers, and generates a world of profound contrasts that, in recent decades, has gone back a hundred years in the area of wealth distribution. In this context, the international community in 2015 presents the SDGs as a universal agenda to fight inequality, and the consolidation of peaceful and inclusive societies based on economic and environmental sustainability, with explicit recognition of international collective action as the only way to achieve it. Thus, the National University of Colombia, as an actor of the 2030 Agenda, promotes the principle of integrality by collecting, in this work, sectoral analyses and territorial initiatives that have contributed to the implementation of SDGs in Colombia.-- Contracadas
