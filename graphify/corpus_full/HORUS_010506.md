# CRISIS AND SOCIAL STATULIDE IN COLOMBIA

This article presents reflections and analyses on the political and social situation in Colombia, following the national strike carried out by social and trade union organizations in 2021. Social and political situations experienced during this period are addressed, such as the participation of different social sectors, the management given by the government of President Duque to the actions of the national police, the behavior and relationship between State and civil society and the international context, with the pronouncement of the Inter-American Commission on Human Rights. On the other hand, the role of women in this socio-political movement is emphasized to show the significance of new leadership and citizen commitments
