# Adoption by homosexual couples who must regulate it: law, constitution or constitutional jurisprudence

The purpose of this research is to explore and analyze the legal context regarding adoption by homosexual couples in Colombia. This approach is framed in the need to determine whether Colombian legislation and jurisprudence provide equal protection for these couples in comparison with other forms of family organizations, or whether there is a deficiency in this aspect. The study is developed within a socio-historical context that covers from the beginnings of the legislation related to adoption by homosexual couples to the current situation. The dynamics and significance of this topic in Colombian society and culture is recognized, with a central focus on the protection of the rights of children and adolescents (NNA). One of the main objectives is to define and examine whether homosexual couples have the right to adopt in Colombia and what types of adoption are allowed according to national regulations. This analysis is carried out taking into account the contributions and impact of equal marriage on the conception and evolution of adoption by same sex couples. From a dual perspective, research addresses two interrelated problems. On the one hand, research is carried out how adoption by homosexual couples affects the integral protection of the children and the children.
