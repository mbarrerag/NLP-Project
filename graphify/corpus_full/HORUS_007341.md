# Environmental governance of the ecosystem water supply service. Case study: Río Negro Hydrographic Basin, 0ientiente antioqueño, Colombia

This research analyzes the relationship between environmental governance, ecosystemic services (SSEE) and territorial planning (OT) from a systemic and complex thinking approach, focusing on the supply of drinking water through the assessment of territorial elements and landscape arrangements associated with the case of study of the Río Negro Basin, in the Antioquian East, Colombia. It explores the biophysical characteristics of the basin from GIS data such as climate, soil-drainage, coverage and topography, highlighting its interrelation with the anthropic activities. Spatial hierarchy tools of actors are fundamental to understand how these factors interact at different scales and affect the basin’s ability to provide water. In addition, key actors involved in water management are identified and characterized, highlighting the multiplicity of roles and power dynamics in the governance of the basin.
