# A CRITICAL APPROACH TO THE

To investigate the potential of the rights approach to confront capitalist barbarism requires, in the first instance, to define what capitalism is, to identify the social relations that support it and to study its human, collective and ecological consequences. This will be the purpose of the first part of the document. There will be shown how the force of capital colonizes the world of life through exploitation processes that are embedded with patriarchal, racist and ecological extremist relations. From different mechanisms of dispossession, capitalism deploys forms of domination and command over resources and economic results, but also over the life and activities of people. However, these processes are objected to and blocked by social processes that oppose the force of capital and the relations of domination with which such force is imbued. There will arise antagonisms that interpel the relations of exploitation and that have the potential to overcome them. The anticapitalist force of such antagonisms will depend on the way they incorporate the horizons of the common.
