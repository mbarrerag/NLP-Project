# Reconfiguration of the urban structure of 22nd Street transformed by the light train of Regiotram®, Fontibón

The proposal arises as a response to the challenge of implementing a railway system similar to a tramway in the Fontibon area. In this context, it is considered to maintain steps at level and two stations, which generates an impact on the urban structure and structure. The lack of a design that harmoniously integrates the environment into the train project creates an obvious conflict. Therefore, a reconfiguration of the urban structure and forms is necessary to adapt adequately to the system proposed by the Regiotram. If these structures do not conform to these needs, there is a risk of compromising the efficiency and the appropriate scale of the area in the future. .
