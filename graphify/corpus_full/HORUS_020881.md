# Visual and graphic arts as a virtual framework for FLE teaching and learning, focusing on students at the basic level (A1) of the National University of Colombia

The following project aims to make use of some web applications (ICT tools) in order to propose a first approach to the development of ludicure activities that provide greater interaction between the members of a basic French course, level A1, of the career of philology and languages; french, within the national university of colombia, bogotá. These activities are suggested to be used in virtual environment. The project starts from a brief reflection on the teaching-learning of a foreign language and its implications in the globalized and technological era of the 21st century. Where, not only the ways of imparting knowledge, the contents and contexts are continuously modified, but also, from the global impact that COVID had 19, a greater displacement towards distance learning was generated, adapting it to a completely virtual or hybrid space, triggering new challenges.The activities that are intended to develop will be addressed from two main axes.The first aspect is the lighting, which is proposed as a main dinamizing resource.The concept of the game will be integrated as part of the cognitive development from where the present the contents of the interactive one is achieved
