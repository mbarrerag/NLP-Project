# THE GENERALIZED CHEN-LEE-LIU MODEL WITH HIGHER ORDER NONLINERITY: OPTICAL SOLITONS

A special type of equation which have variable coefficients and contains terms with higher order nonlinearity is studied. We give conditions under which exact solutions for it can be obtained using a special computational method. From the solutions presented here, novel traveling wave solutions for the Chen-Lee-Liu (CLLE) are presented. Finally, we give some conclusions
