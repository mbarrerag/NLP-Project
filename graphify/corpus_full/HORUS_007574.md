# Careers, development and career diasporas of graduates of Administration from the Faculty of Economic Sciences of the National University of Colombia.

This research project aims primarily to explore the career paths, work development and career diasporas of graduates of business administration from the Faculty of Economic Sciences of the National University of Colombia. It seeks to understand what happens after the training received in the faculty in terms of career choices, work experiences, geographical location and connection (diaspora) with the university and with Colombia of graduates in different contexts and geographies
