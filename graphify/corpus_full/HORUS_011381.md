# TERRITORIAL ECOSYSTEMS.

This document analyses the difficulties facing the region in terms of economic and social development and welfare, which are due to the many factors that the context has had in recent years. It notes how poverty has grown in the region and, with it, the difficulties of sufficient food and quality. It proposes as a central argument of the document to think about development from the territory, using the circular economy, based on the model of ecosystem metaphor. It considers the concept of territory as the possibility of development from the local level, but that integrates mega-chains for the total use of products, by-products and food waste. The document becomes an invitation to reflect on productive models to improve the use of biomass, rethinking processes from the vision of circularity so that fewer people suffer hunger
