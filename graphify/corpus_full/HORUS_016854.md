# CULTURE MANAGEMENT: A BRICULATION BETWEEN BRONTERS

It is a reflection that is born of the absence, of relationships constructed and defined by disciplines with their objects and solid subjects, rigid methodologies, neutral epistemes, to overcome the limits of the immediate, and to assume in the irrevocable, the unforeseen, and thus enter into a process circle whose dynamics cannot be planned
