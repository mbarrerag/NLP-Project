# 5E6 - CHARACTERIZATION OF FOOD SECURITY AND THE NUTRITIONAL STATE OF MATERNAS OF A SONG MOTHER PROGRAMME IN BOGOTA, COLOMBIA

Food insecurity occurs when people lack safe access to sufficient quantities of safe and nutritious food, for normal growth and development and an active and healthy life. Characterizing the level of food security and nutritional status of mothers of the Mother Baby Programme in a hospital in Bogotá, Colombia. An observational cross-sectional study was carried out. Mothers attending the programme were studied between September and November 2019. Food security was determined with the Latin American and Caribbean Food Safety Scale and nutritional status, based on the assessment by nutritionist at three times: pre-gestational, gestational and post-natal. 200 mothers, with average age of 28.9 ± 5.8 years, were included, in their majority of socio-economic stratum two, from families categorized as complete nuclear, who were assigned between USD 90 and USD 150 for food. The average gestational age was 35.6 ± 2.2 weeks and 63.9 % of births were pre-term.
