# Prototype of monitoring system based on a simulated wireless sensor network, as support for planning of solid urban waste collection routes.

The mass production and the increase of the population in the urban settlements has generated a proportional increase in the generation of urban waste, the efficient treatment of these waste has become a challenge for the cleaning agencies, which continue to carry out this operation using periodic fixed routes in which the streets of the neighborhoods are comprehensively traveled in search of the waste, in these operations the usable material is not separated from that which is not, causing a negative environmental impact. The proposal in this work consists of a system that calculates the routes of collection considering the levels of filling reported by the garbage containers, for this purpose the containers are endowed with a level measuring device, the information is transmitted by means of a network that supports long distance communication, the data reported by the containers are used to decide which of the containers should be included or excluded from the route that is being planned according to the level of filling that they report.
