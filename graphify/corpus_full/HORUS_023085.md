# THE ETHICS IN MARX

With the presentation on ethical conception in Karl Marx, it is sought to provide an interpretation and vindication of the youth works of the German thinker where it is evident that it provided an ethical conception to carry out political and economic analyses, as well as, this conception presents a clear and solid foundation in the philosophy of the German thinker Hegel
