# Proposal for Dyakonov surface wave excitement using fiber options

The study of the interaction of light with matter has very important connotations in the applications of sensation, being one of them the surface waves generated for the propagation of light in the interface of two materials. Recent research has discovered possibilities to detect surface waves. A successful investigation on this topic is the polariton method of surface plasmon, which is widely used for the purposes of sensation. The surface wave of Dyakonov (DSW) is a new possibility to expand the possibilities of sensating using dielectric materials. DSW is a phenomenon that depends both on the refractive indices of the interface materials and the surrounding medium, changing its properties of propagation, or not excited when the angle of propagation with respect to the optical axis of the anisotropic material is greater than the maximum angle of the domain of angular existence. This sensitive behavior of the DSW waves opens the door to new alternatives to improve the technological application of surface waves in detection and scientific research.
