# Strengthening in the area of computational topology

Currently in the department of mathematics we have a working group made up of teachers and undergraduate and graduate students. Within this group we have three students of Master's degree in Applied Mathematics Sciences, one of PhD in Mathematics and a seedbed with undergraduate and graduate students of the department. In this project we present several work plans related to the areas of mathematics associated with the group
