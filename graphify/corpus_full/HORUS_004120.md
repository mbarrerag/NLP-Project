# PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS AS SUPPORT IN PUBLIC HEALTH: MODELS OF PRONOSTIC WITH TIME SERIES APPLICED TO SUICTED DRIVING

Mental health has been identified by the World Health Organization (WHO) as a matter of priority interest at the international level. In the particular case of Colombia, the importance of mental health care including the phenomenon of suicide has been highlighted. In this sense, regulatory bodies have sought alternatives that solve or contribute to the mitigation of this problem. In this context, it is relevant to make academic contributions around the development of tools that facilitate the decision-making of government entities, for example, through the formulation of forecasting models that allow identifying trends and behavior patterns of suicide attempts. The aim of the study is to formulate and select a regional prognosis model for suicide attempts through predictive analytics. This article contributes to the gap in literature for the case of the city of Medellín, Colombia. The adjustment, validation and comparison of three different models of temporary additives, under criteria of minimal prognosis error. The comparative models include parametric approximations, Holt-Winters and Box-Jenkins.
