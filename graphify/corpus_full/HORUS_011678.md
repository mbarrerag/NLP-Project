# RECOVERY OF OCCLUDED OBJECTS IN DIGITAL LENSLESS HOLOGRAPHIC MICROSCOPY

A method for recovering the information of occluded objects in digital lensless holographic microscopy (DLHM) [1] is presented. Different DLHM recordings of a sample volume which includes an occluded sample are done by displacing the sample across its plane. A normalized cross-correlation [2] is calculated to determine the relative displacement of the set of recordings. The coordinated addition of the recordings allows the production of a new composed hologram from which the occlusion is removed. The occluded object is retrieved by the calculation of the diffraction that a numerical converging spherical wavefront undergoes in new composed hologram. The method allows the recovering of different occluded samples that placed at varied distances along the optical axis; for each sample plane a new composed holograms must be generated. The method has been tested by imaging a phase-resolution USAF test occluded by a drosophila melanogaster fly
