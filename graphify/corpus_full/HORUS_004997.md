# Partially obtaining and characterizing the mutant human protein α-synuclein G51D and producing its polyclonal antibody

The α-sinuclein (SNCA) is a protein that participates in the formation of aggregates, whose presence is distinctive for all α-sinucleinopathy and some addictions. The alterations in the gene that encodes SNCA and in the synthesis of this protein have been related to aggregation processes that alter its conformation and acquire autoaggregate capacity, which is related to its presence in dopaminergic neurons and could be a significant physiopathological factor in disease progression. One of the most common gene variants of SNCA is G51D, which could be an an anatomopathological indicator for neuronal diseases and long-lasting addictions. This study presents the techniques of expression, purification and characterization of recombinant protein SNCA G51D, in addition to aggregation tests. Likewise, the circumstances for obtaining the antibody of polyclonal type directed towards SNCA G51D are detailed.
