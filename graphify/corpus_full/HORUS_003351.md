# ASSESSMENT OF THE ECOSYSTEM SERVICES AND THE BIODIVERSITY OF THE CORAL ARRANGEMENTS

The coral reefs of the Seaflower Biosphere Reserve play a fundamental role in the well-being and economy of the inhabitants of the San Andrés, Providencia and Santa Catalina Archipelago and protect the emerging areas from strong waves, allowing the very existence of islands, cays and other ecosystems such as seagrass, beaches and sand dunes. Although the ecosystem services provided by these formations are recognized for their importance for biodiversity and human well-being, in island territories such as the archipelago are essential for survival, food security and the protection of human life, as well as the infrastructure, economy and well-being of the populations that inhabit it
