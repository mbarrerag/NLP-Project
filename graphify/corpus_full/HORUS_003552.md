# ANALYSIS OF THE ACHIOTE AGROCADENNE (ORELLANA BIXA) IN THE BAUDO-CHOCÓ MUNIIPIO

The production chains are made up of links that are integrated to develop goods and services to meet human needs. Achiote is a plant originating in tropical America and world production (14.500 tons), 60% are represented by the countries of Peru, Brazil and Colombia; and from national production Chocó contributes 95.6%, being a crop with decades of production and marketing; however, there is little technical knowledge of its value chain, so it is important to carry out an integral analysis of the chain structure to know the links that make up it, its interrelation and main limiting factors of the agrochain in the region of Medi Baudó, department of Choco-Colombia. For the development of the research, a non-experimental descriptive type was applied, which allowed identifying and characterizing the links of the productive chain of the achiote, knowing its relation between links and the management of knowledge of the actors to improve its competitiveness, through the implementation of research strategies of the mixed approach, which includes quantitative and qualitative research.
