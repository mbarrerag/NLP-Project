# XXII CLATPU - LATIN AMERICAN CONGRESO OF PUBLIC AND URBAN TRANSPORT

The XXII Latin American Congress of Public and Urban Transport (CLATPU 2024) has been carried out for the first time in the city of Santo Domingo of the Dominican Republic. This, after 21 editions carried out in 18 Latin American cities, since 1984. Fifteen of these events have been held in cities of South America, three in Central America and three in the north. The periodicity of the CLATPU has been variable in time, between 1 and two years, although in the times of Covid19, it has distanced 3 years.
