# On the performance of Kernel Density Estimation using Density Matrices.

The methods of estimating density can be applied to different problems in statistics and machine learning. They can be used to solve tasks such as anomaly detection, generative models, semi-supervised learning, compression, text-to-voice conversion, among others. A popular technique for finding density estimates of new samples in a nonparametric configuration is done through Kernel Density Estimation, a method that suffers from costly assessments, especially for large data sets and high dimensions. In this thesis we want to compare the performance of the novel Density Estimation method using Density Matrices introduced by González et al. [9] with other fast state-of-art procedures to estimate the probability density function in different sets of complex synthetic scenarios. Our experimental results show that this new method is a competitive strategy of calculating density estimates among its competitors and also shows advantages when working with large sets of data and high dimensions.
