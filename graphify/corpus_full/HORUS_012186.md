# CRITERIA FOR IDENTIFICATION OF HEREDITARY SINDER-MANDER SYNDROMS. REVIEW OF LITERATURA AND RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE NATIONAL COLOMBIA CANCEROLOGY INSTITUTE

The three inherited breast cancer syndromes (SCMH) correspond to 5% to 10% of all cases of the disease, mostly explained by mutations in the BRCA1 and BRCA2 genes. Multiple guidelines and updated international recommendations have been published, with the aim of providing guidelines for selecting cases with suspicion of a SCMH. As a local background, the National Cancer Institute of Colombia has an institutional program for the identification and management of families with suspicion of hereditary cancer.For the purposes of care, within which breast cancer is the most referred pathology to the genetic service (55%; 540/986). In 21% of cases with breast cancer that met NCCN criteria, a broad SCMH was diagnosed, the majority associated with mutations in the BRCA1 and BRCA2 genes (12.3%) and in a lesser proportion to other genes of susceptibility to breast cancer (8.6%).Objective: Identifying the most successful selection criteria for breast cancer cases was diagnosed through a review of the literature, and making an institutional consensus on the indications of reference to the genetic cancer and systemic disease. genetics and application for genetic studies to identify hereditary breast cancer, and the criteria of the Olympia study for the application for genetic studies are established in order to guide systemic treatment with iPARP at the National Cancer Institute. This will allow our institution to provide this service to the Colombian population adequately
