# Hypostomomus (Siluriformes: Loricariidae): native species or introduced in the middle and under Cauca?

Loricariidae, the most diverse family in the order Siluriformes, houses the subfamily Hypostominae, within which is Hypostomus, one of the most diverse genera with 161 valid species and a wide geographical distribution, mainly in the Amazon-Orinoco ecoregion. Hypostomus presents high intraspecific morphological variability and similar color patterns, which has generated taxonomic uncertainty and cases of synonymy. In Colombia, 15 species of Hypostomus have been registered, distributed in the basins of the Pacific, Caribbean, Amazon and Orinoco. In addition, the introduction of exotic and transplanted species, such as Pterygoplichys pardalis, due to negligence in its trade and management, suggests a risk of introduction of more species of lotricarids to the natural environment. With the aim of dilucting the taxonomic identity of loricarides species in the low and middle basin of the Cauca, a taxonomic review of the genus Hypostomus will be proposed.
