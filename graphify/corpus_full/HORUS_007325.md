# Application of Lean methodology in the pre-sales process of a company

The construction industry in Colombia faces important challenges in the elaboration of technical-economic offers for tenders, highlighting the lack of standardization in the processes, the poor communication between teams and the long response times as some of the main difficulties. These inefficiencies in the area of presales negatively affect the competitiveness of the companies and their ability to participate in new projects.To address these barriers, the implementation of the Len Construction methodology was proposed, which sought to optimize the processes by eliminating activities that do not add value, continuous improvement and reduction of waste.The main objective of the project was to improve the process of developing technical-economic offers through the use of Lean Construction tools, in order to increase the effectiveness in the presales stage.The specific objectives included a comprehensive analysis of the current problems, the selection and implementation of Lean tools and the most suitable results in the implementation of the project.The results showed an insituation of the comparative case study of Lean practices, focusing on the identification of bottle neck and improvement opportunities. the implementation of the Lean Construction methodology was proposed, which was to optimize processes by eliminating activities that do not add value, continuous improvement, and waste reduction. The methodology was based on a comparison case study that examined traditional methods versus application Lean tools, and the evaluation of their impact on reduction timeline and costs
