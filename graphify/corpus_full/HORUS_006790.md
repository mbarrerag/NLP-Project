# Characterization of pathological lesions of the porcine respiratory complex as a diagnostic tool in the pig farms production line

The porcine respiratory complex (CRP) is a multi-etiological, polyleural and multifactorial clinical syndrome of difficult focus for its management, prevention and control in the productive stages of pigs, which lead to severe economic losses. The aim of this work was to characterize the macroscopic pathological lesions and their correlation with the pneumonic patterns observed in pre-bae and brood pigs in an intensive farm of three sites in the country. 50 necropsy, 25 pigs from each pre-baeba stage respectively and sampling of pulmonary tissue for histopathology were observed. At least one neumonic pattern evident to macroscopic evaluation in all pigs.
