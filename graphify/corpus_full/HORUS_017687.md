# Determination of the condition of oscillating flap type anti-return valves by analysing time signals and artificial intelligence algorithms.

The anti-return valves are very useful complements within the industrial coffee extraction lines. These valves can present faults during their operation due to the detachment of the disk, wear of the seat or fatigue of the spring, generating faults and time losses in the processes. Currently, deep learning through the convolutional neural networks has allowed to investigate and process great amount of data, achieving excellent results in the prediction of the state of industrial components. Thus, this work focuses on techniques of predictive maintenance of vibrations, ultrasound and acoustic emission by means of the capture of temporal signals, analysis of temporary descriptors, strategies of reduction of dimensionality like PCA, tSNE and UMAP, in addition to the grouping with training Kmeans. Additionally, the SFS method was used for the selection of the most significant characteristics of the temporal descriptors and in this way the analysis of the analysis of the data is used anti-return.
