# DISEASES IN THE COMMUNITY: ACTIVE LEARNING DURING THE ELABORATION OF A PODCAST

The professorship of physiopathology addressed to the students of Pharmacy of the National University of Colombia offered since 2008, has been mainly taught through master classes, the student being a recipient of knowledge. This subject, which explains how the human organism is sick, is part of the biomedical line in the formation of the pharmaceutical chemist and is a requirement to understand how drugs work. The pandemic was an opportunity to generate changes in the traditional model, part of the strategies implemented is the elaboration of a podcast in which students explain the physiopathology of a disease of interest to people in their environment by using a simple language aimed at an audience without academic education in health. During 4 academic semesters 43 podcasts have been presented to which 34 people have been invited to the students. This activity enhances the role of students in society through the dissemination of knowledge in a local context, making use of the learning-service resource; it is also an opportunity to encourage creativity, significant learning and soft skills
