# BELIEVE CALLS FROM THE BLACK BOX BLACK BOX BLACK AS A REALITY TABLE

This essay discusses the relationship between the image as a means of representing reality and the value of truth attached to such representation. To this end, the scientific and artistic uses possessed by the image are assumed, on the one hand, and, on the other, how the importance in representation has shifted from the image to the data, turning the image into a mere layer of visualization of algorithmic processes destined for human understanding. This change is reflected in the growing credibility in algorithmic manipulations on large volumes of data, both in scientific and everyday processes, to the detriment of a digital image and in a easily false network. For this purpose, it is part of the concept of technical image of Vilém Flusser and offers a critical adaptation in line with the current situation within the so-called software culture. Thus, this shift is represented metaphorically with the passage of the dark camera of optical devices from past to the cybernetic black box, to emphasize the particularities of algorithmic processes as an index of reality.
