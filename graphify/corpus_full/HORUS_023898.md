# DIALOGUE OF KNOWERS: EXCHANGE OF EXPERIENCES IN PANDEMIA BY COVID-19 IN UNIVERSITARY STUDENTS, YEAR 2020

Description of problem or need: The pandemic by the COVID-19 has generated crisis and radical changes in society and the economy around the measures adopted for its management, which have an important impact on the health of people and society (1). University students consider themselves vulnerable to the events experienced in the pandemic and the economy, and the measures adopted by governments to address this situation (2).
