# Repertoires of strategic actions in the configuration of the territorial conflict in the Isla de la Paz and La Cima neighbourhoods, Buenaventura (1990-2020)

The research analyzed the impact of the repertoires of black communities on the reconfiguration of the territorial conflict in two neighborhoods, which are the case study of the present research: Isla de la Paz and La Cima, of the Commune 6 in the City # 2 ‘El Pailón’ of the urban area of Buenaventura. Control and dispute is over corridors and strategic territories in the urban-rural area of the neighborhoods in the period, addressed in the study from the decade of 1990 to 2020. In the territory are configured mechanisms of legal and illegal dispossession to force the communities to abandon the lands, and consolidate forms of territorial control hegemonic. The actors defined as hegemonics include illegal, state and private armed groups, of which strategic alliances are established in function of certain economic interests, both legal and illegal, on territories in Buenaventura. Consequently, the black communities that inhabit the territories are (re-) victimized in the development of the port economy and the drug trade.
