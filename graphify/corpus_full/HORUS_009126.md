# FUNDS FOR INTEGRAL CONTROL OF TUBBERCLESIS

The book integrates knowledge and experiences acquired by health professionals who have dedicated their professional and research life to the study of tuberculosis. Throughout nine chapters it addresses relevant topics on one of the oldest infectious diseases of humanity and that prevails in the current global and national epidemiological scenario. The aim of this publication is to consolidate the necessary bases for the comprehensive control of tuberculosis in Colombia, because it is a priority of research and intervention in the Ten-Year Public Health Plan of the country and to which it is part of the development of the End Tuberculosis Strategy of the World Health Organization. From the academy it is considered essential to support the training of qualified human talent for the care of patients diagnosed with tuberculosis. Thanks to research results obtained by our research groups, gaps in the knowledge of health workers have been identified, in relation to the diagnosis, control and treatment of tuberculosis of adults and children. Finally, this document also highlights the importance of the general theme of tuberculosis infection control measures for health institutions and includes a chapter on childhood tuberculosis, thus constituting a fundamental tool for all tuberculosis professionals
