# Construction of a technological tool for the detection and care of students with specific learning difficulties.

The diversity of characteristics, styles and learning rhythms is evident in the classroom. Some students face specific learning difficulties, manifested in difficulties in acquiring and developing basic instrumental skills such as reading, writing, mathematics, among others. Children with learning problems do not respond to traditional teaching methods but do learn with other methods and at another pace. Most students with learning difficulties can achieve academic activities with good results, if their specific difficulty is detected in time and supported by appropriate methods. But it is almost never possible to access inclusive educational environments that allow to detect the types of learning difficulties and from there carry out a programme of activities suitable for each specific learning difficulty identified, improving the learning process of these students. ICTs have shown great possibilities in educational processes that recognize the particularities of students and offer personalized activities. This proposal seeks to take advantage of the previous experience of the GAIA Group in adaptive and inclusive systems and aims to build a technological tool for the detection and care of students with specific learning difficulties, in addition to the monitoring of the evolution of each student
