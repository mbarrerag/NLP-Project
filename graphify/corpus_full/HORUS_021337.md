# Phenomelogical-based model of direct blue 2 adsorption on corncob in a fixed-bed.

This work presents a phenomenologically based semi-physical model (PBSM) for the liquid-solid adsorption process, which has been experimentally validated. Using the PBSM, the two modes of operation for adsorption of direct blue 2 dye (DB2) on corn cobs in a fixed bed are discussed. The first mode of operation is the typical con- tinuous liquid flow, whereas the second is a semi-continuous mode with the recycling of liquid leaving the column. The equilibrium distribution curve (EDC) for low DB2 concentration was obtained. The PBSM deduction led to the evaluation of the local mass transfer coefficient for the solid phase. Following the deduction of this coef- ficient, the diffusivity of the surface solid was experimentally evaluated, but using the PBSM as a point of comparison. It was shown that regardless of the mode of operation used, surface diffusion is associated only with the nature of the adsorbent material and not with the concentration of the liquid phase. In addition, the PBSM showed liquid and solid concentration changes at different points in the column. This fact allows the construction of the different operating curves of the process, which were validated, and showed similar results when comparing the predictions of the model and the experimental data
