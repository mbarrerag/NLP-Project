# The Complete Genome Sequences of 3 Species of Fishes (Labrisomidae, Blenniiformes).

We present the complete genome sequences of Malacoctenus gilli, Malacoctenus tetranemus, Malacoctenus triangulates. Illumina sequencing was performed on genetic material from museum specimens. The reads were assembled using a de novo method followed by a finishing step. The raw and assembled data are available via Genbank
