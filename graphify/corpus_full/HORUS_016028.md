# Spectral analysis of ergodic Schrödinger operators.

In this final master's work we study the spectral types of Schrödinger's discrete one-dimensional operator families {H in which H potential is given by V{n) = f(T n}), for n Z, where f: → R is a continuous function and T is an ergonomic homeomorphism in a compact space. Based on the research of Boshernitzan and Damanik (2008), we define the topological and metric repetition properties in the dynamic system, T} and we demonstrate in detail that each of these properties is a sufficient condition for the purely continuous spectrum to be a generic property of {H. The main tool of the work is Gordon's motto, of which we propose a step by step demonstration and analyze its implications. We also expose and demonstrate two own results that generalize the central theorem of the research. cited and discuss examples of application
