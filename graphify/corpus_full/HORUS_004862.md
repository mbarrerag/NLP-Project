# ENERGY BALANCE BY SANKEY DIAGRAMS OF A COUNTER WITH R600-A

In the world, between 1500 and 1800 million refrigerators are used; there are estimated to be about 14.6 million in Colombia, 3.8 million low efficiency (over 10 years of use); they represent 22% of the total consumption of domestic electricity and generate about 20.7 million tons of CO2 equivalent annually. Semi-industrial refrigeration benefits the productivity of small and medium-scale agro-industry by reducing environmental pollution and promoting the well-being of communities. A high-efficiency cooling system with R-134 A powered by photovoltaic solar energy was built, instrumented and monitored remotely from the city of Bogotá to be replicated in municipalities of the country. The research conducted a study of energy balances presented in Sankey diagrams of a freezer that uses as a source of energy the photovoltaic solar voltaic variable thermal load and daily radiation conditions
