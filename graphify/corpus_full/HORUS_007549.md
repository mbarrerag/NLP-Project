# Palinological and physicochemical analysis of honeys produced by Apis mellifera bees in the Piedemonte Llanero of the Department of Meta.

Honey produced by bees is a mixture of mineral carbohydrates, proteins, phenolic compounds, enzymes, vitamins, organic acids, being an excellent source of energy. There are few reports of the quality of this product in the Piedemonte Llanero of the department of the Meta, so it is proposed to carry out an evaluation of this product that comprise two main characteristics as follows: i) analysis, which will allow to know the composition (%) of pollen grains and thus determine their botanical and geographical origin; ii) physicochemical analysis and bioactive compounds, which include pH, free acidity, lactonic acidity, total acidity, humidity, diastase activity, hydroxymethylfurfural, water activity, electrical conductivity, minerals, ash, antioxidant capacity (DPPH, TEAC), total phenol content, reducing sugars, total flavonoids, instrumental color in scale CIE Lab and Pfund, this information will be related. The results aim to establish the characteristics of honey of Apis mellifera bees in this region, allowing beekeepers to demonstrate the origin of their product, besides the calendar and to increase in the production of the honey sector
