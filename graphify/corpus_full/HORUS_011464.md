# RADIOGRAPH OF THE CURRENT TIME OF THE GOVERNMENT

The article deals with the initial tendencies of Gustavo Petro's government; it exposes the features of the progressive project, addresses its supports and limitations; it deals with resistance to reforms and analysis of the opisitor forces, as well as the immediate perspectives of the government
