# Design of didactic booklet for understanding the interpretation of the concept of fraction in students of grade four of primary.

The document addresses the conceptualization necessary for the design of a didactic booklet, taking into account the difficulties documented in the learning of students from different institutions and the theoretical approach of this situation, expressed by different authors experts in the subject. In addition, its importance was taken into account as fundamental content in the area of mathematics. The central objective of this document is to design a didactic proposal based on the interpretations of the concept of fraction that is evidenced in students of grade four of primary, that allows the development of skills focused on solving situations of problem of fractional type. The development of this booklet represented a contribution to the teaching practice and the performance of students. The prepared booklet can be considered as a motivating, dynamic and innovative tool, favoring the teaching and learning process
