# Social transformations in the peasant families of Cota from their relationship with the land

To the north-offident of the city of Bogotá, in the department of Cundinamarca, is found Cota, a peasant and farmer municipality. This research is carried out that aims to investigate the social transformations within peasant families based on their relationship with land, the interests for it and the practices that are carried out therein. The researcher uses the case study as methodological strategy and through in-depth interviews and observations with participants from two families in the municipality, she manages to give rise to thoughts, emotions, knowledge and feelings that in dialogue with theory generate new knowledge and enable the formulation or restructuring of municipal public policies related to families and agriculture. .
