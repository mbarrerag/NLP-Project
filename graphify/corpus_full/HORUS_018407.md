# ASSISTANCE LEVEL OF THE FAMILY CAREER OF RARA DISEASE PERSONS

To describe the level of adoption of the role of the caregiver of people with rare disease in a cohort in the municipality of Chía-Colombia. A cross-sectional descriptive study conducted with 30 main family caregivers of people with rare diseases in Chía-Colombia in 2020. Variables were collected for the characterization of caregivers and their caregivers, as well as the instrument for adopting the role of the caregiver-ROL. Descriptive statistics were used for the analysis of variables. 93.3% of caregivers were women, with a low socioeconomic stratum of 67.7%, with a high school study of 46.7% and with ages of between 37.5 and 45.1 (95% CI). Persons with rare diseases were 66.7% men aged between 17.3 and 30 years (95% CI) and with a wide variety of rare diseases, mostly one case by type of disease. A basic role was reported in 60% of caregivers, with expressions of exclusive dedication to the role, isolation, limited knowledge, preparation and support. Caregillers of rare persons in Chía-Colombia require interventions for the promotion of the role, satisfactory adoption
