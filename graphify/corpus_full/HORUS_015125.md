# COLOMBIAN VERSION OF THE PERSON OF PALIATIVE CARE: ADAPTION AND VALIDATION

Abstract Introduction: the Palliative Care Quiz for Nursing (PQCN) is a questionnaire used internationally to evaluate the level of basic knowledge in palliative care in nursing. There are validations of the PCQN in France, Iran, Korea and Spain, validation is required in Colombia. The objective of this study was to adapt and validate the PCQN to the Colombian context in nursing students. psychometric validation study conducted in the second semester of 2022. The process of content validity was developed with 13 experts who evaluated the clarity, understanding, accuracy, relevance and consistency; the agreement between the judges with the modified Lawshe index was calculated. The facial validity process was developed with 17 nursing students, who were asked about the understanding, recovery, judgment and adequacy of the questionnaire through a cognitive interview. the 20 items of the PCQN had a PCC equal to or higher than 0.61, 13 items had semantic adjustments, 4 had content adjustments and 3 items were not adjusted. In the cognitive interview with the students of valid nursing, it was determined that the questionnaire's 20 items had a useful understanding, recovery and was used to be considered as a clinical outcome
