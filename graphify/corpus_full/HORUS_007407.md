# Productivity in public toilet service

This research aims to carry out a productivity measurement in the activity of collection and transport of solid urban waste of the public toilet service, taking into consideration the recommendations and theoretical conclusions regarding the construction of the capital factor, the labor factor and the added value in the economy in general. Generally the productivity measurements are made for the economy in general and for the aggregate sectors that bring together industries according to the international uniform industrial classification, ISIC, but there are few research works with proposals for the measurement of productivity in a given production of a good or in the provision of a service. This document shows that it is possible to make a productivity measurement for a specific industry and with data typical of that industry, in the light of theoretical considerations. To this purpose, the capital stocks are constructed in chains volumes and the growth of the two factors and the added value is calculated from the average rate of the labour resources obtained in the sector studied. ivity in the production of a specific good or in the provision of a service.
