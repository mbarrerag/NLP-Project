# Lighting design of the El Porvenir Gibraltar Park in the city of Bogotá

This work aims to show the lighting design of four sports scenarios of the park El Porvenir Gibraltar, located in the city of Bogotá, Colombia: BMX track, beach volleyball court, track athletics and archery field. The objective of this park is to provide a leisure space for the community, promoting the practice of physical exercise, relaxation, contact with nature, recreation and entertainment. Since this park will be the most important in the south of the city, its lighting must meet the highest standards of quality and efficiency, adapting to the specific use of each space. Among the challenges of the design were the diversity of uses, since each sport scenario requires a specific lighting that guarantees visibility and safety during its use; energy efficiency, using state-of-the-art LED technology to reduce electrical consumption; compliance with international regulations for sporting events, designing lighting that is functional and aesthetic, creating a pleasant and safe atmosphere for users during the night.
