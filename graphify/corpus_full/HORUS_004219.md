# GEOMETRIA FOCUS AND ITS APPLICATIONS, 26

It is known that several physical systems can be modeled by ordinary di-ferential equations. But there is a particular class of these systems, the systems of control, in which there is explicitly a function of control (or feedback, in some texts) that imposes more information on the system. Since the beginning of the study of these systems, several methods have been established, mostly analytical methods; however, these can be re-examined by means of techniques of differential geometry and thus explore new tools in these models. The workshop presented here is divided into two parts: 1) classic approach of some control systems; 2) basic elements of geometry necessary for geometric models. The workshop is an invitation to investigate in modern methods of study of both physical and geometric systems, as the ex-position has as a culmen a description of a common geometry for all models described in the two sessions
