# Correctional measure in military criminal proceedings, as an exception to the principle of unity of jurisdiction

In this document, the reader will find an analysis of the coherence or internal and external validity of article 305 of the Colombian Military Criminal Code (CPM) concerning the corrective power by the judge of exception and it will be noted that it lacks such power with respect to some subjects and participants - civil - who escape the wording of the aforementioned article. As a result of the internal and external analysis of article 305 of the CPM, it will be noted that the prohibition of the trial of civilians may explain this limitation of the corrective power, but it raises the problem of the protection of the jurisdictional function and the inconvenience of the exercise of a military authority which is structurally owned by the executive branch of public power, which is why its independence and autonomy cannot be fully affirmed. Thus, the reader is asked whether a system of justice as conceived, between the being and the opinion (text of the source, and the opinion). judicial branch in agreement with the organizational part of the national constitution, its independence and autonomy cannot be affiliated. Thus, the reader is asked when a jurisdiction can really be considered a jurisdiction that falls the coercive power on which the judicial function exercised by judges is based and with respect to which their independence cannot be fully assured, while at the same time when a system of justice granted in this way, between being and applying, is not too costly for modern constitutional democracy
