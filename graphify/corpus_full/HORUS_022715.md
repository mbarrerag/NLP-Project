# Seroprevalence of HTLV-1 and 2 in organs donors and kidney transplant recipients Colombia 2010-2017

Summary In Colombia, since 2004 there is a recommendation to detect human type 1 and 2 lymphotropic T virus (HTLV-1-2) in donors and organ recipients. Prevention of infection by HTLV-1 virus and 2 in receptors is important given its relationship with lymphoproliferative, inflammatory and opportunistic diseases. The aim of the study was to describe the seroprevalence of HTLV-1 and 2 between kidney organ and receptor donors, between 2010 and 2017 in Colombia. descriptive study, which included 1979 organ donors and 3311 kidney transplant recipients from the 2010 to 2017 transmission network. Seroprevalence was calculated for HTLV-1 and 2. Serological and demographic variables were described. 1979 donors, antibody detection against HTLV-1 was performed in 92% (1820) with a seroprevalence of HT-2; 50% of cases were derived from the Pacific area (endemic area in Colombia). 90% of donors were examined for HTLV-2, with a similar seroprevalence of 0.2%; of both cases of HTLV-1 were reported for 2010 and 2017
