# Corporate Social Responsibility Management in Non-profit Sports Organizations: A Focus on the Caldense Athletics League

This research addresses the management of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), in non-profit sports organizations, focusing on the Caldense Athletics League. Through a theoretical and practical analysis, models, challenges and opportunities are identified to integrate CSR as a strategic tool in the sports field. The methodology used is qualitative cut; semi-structured surveys aimed at stakeholders of the Caldense Athletics League and other sports organizations of the same line in Colombia were conducted. The results highlight the importance of structuring a formal CSR model, which is adaptable and applicable to other similar sports organizations in Colombia, and which allows maximizing social, economic and environmental impact, promoting inclusion, sustainability and transparency in sports management. This research adds the management of Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) in non-profit sports organizations, focusing on the Caldense Atletismo League
