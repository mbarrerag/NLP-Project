# Resilience of vegetation from the paramos to different fire regimes

The moors are the main source of water for most of the major cities of the north of South America. In addition, they are ecospot with high levels of endemism and contain large amounts of carbon in their soils. Currently there is concern and the effects that human activities produce are unknown and how they can enhance the effects of climate change in these ecosystems. Some of the disturbances associated with human activity and influenced by climate change are fires. Although it is known that fires have a significant impact on the structure of vegetation and on the capacity of carbon capture of the moors, the data set of _x0093_Paramo Fire atlas_x0094_ will be used to help better understand how the fires affect the structure and functioning of the moors, which can contribute to the informed decision of their conservation and management.
