# Assessment of decision variables that influence the regional location of logistics infrastructure for storage and storage from a public and private perspective.

The different theoretical approaches that have addressed the relationship between the logistic infrastructure and the territory have focused on analyzing the positive and negative effects that the growth and expansion of the logistic infrastructure has on a regional scale, however, has been deepened little in the knowledge of the causes, or in the elements that make up this relationship. The grade work tries to deepen in the analysis of the regionalization of the logistic infrastructure, from the logistic organization towards the use of the land and from the local planning to a system of governance and regional planning more wide; therefore it is proposed to identify and evaluate the variables of decision that influence the regional planning variables of the logistic infrastructure destined for the storage and the hold.
