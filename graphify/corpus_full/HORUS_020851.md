# Laboratory of located projects: a capacity building strategy for the appropriation and management of the CTeL

Laboratory of Projects Located is a proposal derived from the winning project of the national call for solidarity extension 2020 entitled: The research located as strategy of social inclusion, that aimed to develop a methodology of learning of the research for students of secondary basic, based on the concept of Localized Research, that raises the possibility of a collaborative work model between educational actors through the development of three mediation processes: the first are the research tutories, in which students from different undergraduates visit the educational institutions linked to the project, to share information on the academic offer of the university, the objects of study of the different disciplines and their research methodologies; the second are the research seeders, in which four undergraduate researchers will develop their research project accompanied by six (6) students from the baccalaureate, inserting themselves into the rudiments of their disciplines; and, finally, the Practices of Localized Learning, in which the students collect everything they have learned in the other processes to propose their own research, accompanied by the research team that composes the laboratory. In this project two Educational Institutions participated: The Sierra and Ramón Giral Ceballos, selected under the criteria of vulnerability established (2018 ) The project was presented in two (2) pedagogy congresses, and was the beneficiary of an internal call by UNAL to develop research on infection vectors in Antioquia with one of its tutors. In addition, the project was the beneficiary of the Explora audiovisual innovation laboratory, in which two incentives were given: a (1) set of Oculus Meta 2 virtual reality glasses and a (1) play to develop strategies with an immersive approach. In the feedback of the 2021 process, the participating institutions insisted on the need to continue accompanying, both students who were able to enter the university, and those who were unable to pass the exam, but who are interested in maintaining a link with the research processes. Following this evaluation, we propose the creation of the Laboratory of Located Projects, which will be the vertex of the mediation processes that have been taking place since 2020
