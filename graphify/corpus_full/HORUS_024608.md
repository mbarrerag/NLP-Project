# PRACTICE VISIBILITY, ALTERNATIVE IN THE TRAINING OF YOUTH FOR IMAGE REFLEXION AND PRODUCTION

The young people are part of a generation of visual content with the aim of finding a purpose and a message that is consenting to be transferred through practice in the writing of the image, the challenge is to recognize from experience and everyday experience new dynamics for the formation of young people, as regards the new social dynamics and challenges for these digital nomads. The research-creation that takes place in this document proposes through processes of creating photographic image and visuality, to reflect on new changes such as: social networks and its massification, the expansion of communicational tissues and the production and high technology in the new mobile devices
