# EcoAulas de Paz: Co-creation and participatory implementation of living classrooms from circular agriculture with native insects in the Arhuaco Learning Center - SWAKA and the National University of Colombia - Headquarters La Paz

In this proposal, the UNAL headquarters of La Paz (UNP) makes a commitment to respond to territorial demands of the department of Cesar related to guaranteeing food sovereignty and the right to food and the sustainable use of biodiversity. It is proposed to lead in territory a project that integrates, provides continuity and expands two initiatives financed in previous calls of solidarity extension that supported the socioeconomic development of diverse vulnerable communities, led by two professors of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and Zootecnia, Bogotá and that have demonstrated a high level of sustainability: 1. The strategy of management of organic residues for animal food generation through the production of black soldier fly (MSN) carried out in the project _x0093_Insectos por la Paz_x0094_ (IPP) in Tolima (Prof. Karol Barragan ) and 2. The development and implementation of fish production in the center of Aruaco Apprenticeship in Agro-acultura Integrated SWAKA in Pueblo Bello, Cesar (Prof. Adriana Muñoz) The articulation of the two initiatives is made through the design and participatory implementation of the concept of _x0093_EAuco. 94_(AVP) on the campus UNP. The AVP includes a demonstrative EAP of MSN with components that can be transported for pedagogical purposes to schools, associations, etc.; and a plan of articulation of that EAP with other agroproductive strategies of _x0093_campus sustainable_x0094_ (CS) as nurseries, beekeeping, etc. This articulator plan will be co-constructed with the integrated management experience of productive systems of the Arhuaco SWAKA center, the experience of IPP and the Environmental Management System of UNP, and will result in _x0093_paths of learning circular agriculture_x0094_ (RAAC) using the elements of the CS as permanent environmental interpretation spaces, open to visitors and programmed, that will facilitate the processes of extension of the headquarters and that will be oriented and supported by students or passers of different geographical, cultural and ethnic origins linked to the visitor community and/or to productive processes that allow to develop the circular skills of the academic model UNP.
