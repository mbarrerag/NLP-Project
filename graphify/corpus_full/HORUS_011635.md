# The teacher is dead, see her memory

on 16 february 2023, the illustrated teacher maria teresa peralta aello died. Honoring her memory one of her students describes the national trajectory of the teacher. Her importance as a female leader of the specialty, her national and international recognitions and her long teaching career in the Faculty of Medicine. The Teacher was a very significant figure of the National Obstetrics and at the last biennial Congress of the Specialty, this was dedicated to her memory and the Colombian Federation named her Maria Teresa Peralta Abelo
