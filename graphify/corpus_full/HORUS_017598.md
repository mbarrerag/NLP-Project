# Dimensioning and optimal localization of large-scale energy storage systems to reduce the cost of network restrictions in the Colombian electricity system.

This document presents a methodology that seeks to locate and size battery-based energy storage systems (SAEB) to reduce the cost of restrictions in the Colombian electrical system. The methodology is proposed as a mixed whole linear programming model (MILP), initially with a deterministic approach to be improved using a scenario-based approach, with the aim of taking into account the uncertainty of some parameters of the model. The optimization models are written in the Python programming language and making use of the Pyomo library. The different stages of methodology development are tested and validated with different power systems such as the 6 node test system, the Colombian equivalent 15 node system and the Colombian national transmission system (STN); real information of the Colombian electrical system is used in the last two systems. The results of the methodology are evaluated by comparing the cost by restriction with and without SAEB under different conditions, identifying additional benefits of integration of SAEB into the systems studied
