# CONSTITUTIVE EXPRESSION OF CASE9 IN PROMASTIGOTES OF LEISHMANIA Braziliensis

Protozoan of the genus Leishmania are the agents that cause leishmaniasis, parasitic disease for which effective medicines, treatments or vaccines are not available. Consequently, it is essential to find therapeutic alternatives based on the understanding of the biology of the parasite. The CRISPR/Cas9 edition system is promising to identify essential genes and generate attenuated lines with vaccine potential. To obtain transfectant lines of Leishmania braziliensis that constitutively express the endonuclease Cas9, as a starting point for the editing of its genome through the CRISPR/Cas9 system. Promastigates of L. braziliensis were transfected with the plasmid pTB007-Viannia, previously analyzed by enzyme digestion and PCR.
