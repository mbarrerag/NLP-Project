# CARD OF CONTROL FOR NON-LINEAL PROFILE BASED ON THE APPENDIX OF THE MODIFIED EPIGRAPH AND THE PROFUNDITY BASED ON AMENDED BANKES

Due to the need to monitor nonlinear profile processes, this paper proposes a control letter for functional data based on the graphical method of detecting functional outliers, the Outliergram. Specifically, the chart identifies changes in the form of functional processes with a control limit calculated by Bootstrap from a sample of curves under control. The chart monitoring capability is tested on a simulation scenario made up of four models and good ARL values are obtained for all
