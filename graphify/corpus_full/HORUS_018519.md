# SOUND IN THE TORNEY TO SCHOOL CONVIVISION, INTERCULTURE AND REDUCTION OF XENOPHOBIA

In the framework of the project of methodological transfer of the booklet “Let us be Panarceros! Ways for peaceful coexistence between Colombian and Venezuelan students” by Deutsche Gesellshaft für Internationale Zusammerarbeit (GIZ), the result of a quantitative investigation on the phenomenon of xenophobia and discrimination in Cúcuta and its metropolitan area is presented. With the aim of analyzing the interpretations of the population of the Colombian-Venezuelan border against xenophobia, discrimination and Venezuelan migration in the school environment, 1681 surveys were compiled during the month of October and November 2020; thanks to the process of training in interculturality and coexistence to public educational institutions in Cúcuta and its metropolitan area (El Zulia, Los Patios, Puerto Santander and Villa del Rosario). As reported in this document, the exponential increase of Venezuelan migrants and refugees has affected the perceptions of residents in border areas, who remain with a positive but selective reception of the arrival of immigrants in the area, even though they understand that the mobility situation is one of the consequences of the prolonged economic, political and social crisis in the neighbouring country
