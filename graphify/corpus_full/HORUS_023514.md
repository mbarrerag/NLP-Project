# MODELATION AND MOLECULAR DOCKING OF PEGTIDES DERIVED FROM TITUS METROCHIRUS (BUTHIDAE) TOXIN TORPHONE TORCHES WITH HIGH AFFINDITY FRONT TO THE CANAL RECEPTOR VOLTAGE DEPENDENT TO POTASIO KV10.1

SLOC2-233 INTRODUCTION A target of therapeutic interest has been the Kv10.1 channel that is expressed in cancerous tissues, and where the blockers of this channel have been used as antiproliferative agents. In this study was modeled and performed molecular docking, for obtaining 4 candidate peptides. METHODS Modeling was performed by the I-TASSER and SWISS-MODEL servers. Files of the solved models were prepared using AudockTools 1.5.6. The molecular docking was performed with AutodockVina defining as a receptor the Kv10.1 channel and as ligands the 65 aa and 35 aa peptides (Tma1 and FV2-16). RESULTS AND CONCLUSIONS The model obtained by SWISS-MODEL for the recipient (Tsitus) showed 99.63% of identity against a structure reported in the PDB (5K7L). The main model used as a template by SWISS MODEL for the construction of the model of Tma1 was the neurotoxin VII (Ts1) model of Tsup) of Tity Serpéis (Concepts)
