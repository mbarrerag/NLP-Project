# THE COLOMBIAN ORIGIN OR THE INCERTIDUMBRES OF AN OPEN FRONT

The Colombian Orinoquía overlaps with what is currently the departments of Casanare, Arauca, Vichada and Meta, a territory that sinks its deepest historical roots until the very moment of discovery and conquest, a period from which the macroregion formed by the Orinoquía, Amazonia and Guayanas began to be seen in a oscillation between wild geography and economic potential, in which it was considered, there was only an empty space in which there were only a few indigenous people who, until very late in the 19th century, were denied recognition of their social character.From this oscillation, the Colombian Orinoquía has been characterized, by a discontinuant occupation, both in time and space (González, 2015), which means that the historical processes developed there have not been homogeneous in the forms, the time, nor the space, so the region, or some of its subregions, is visible or invisible, temporarily, from the framework of the national experience.
