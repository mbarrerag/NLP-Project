# RESEARCH IN COLOMBIA IMMUNOLOGY AND TRANSLATION MEDICINE, IN PERSPECTIVE: TRANSSLATION MEDICINE: BIOMARKERS AND VACUNES FOR CANCER HANDLING IN COLOMBIA

The research group in Immunology and Translation Medicine (I_KE) of the Faculty of Medicine of the National University has been working for several years to articulate the knowledge generated in basic research in immunology with clinical practice. The group is pioneer in immuno-monitoring studies of patients with cancer and in the implementation of strategies of immunotherapy and personalized medicine for the management of cancer in Colombia. As part of the work of social appropriation of knowledge, framed in the project of young researchers is Diagnostic, evaluation and proposed tools to dynamize the development of translational medicine in Colombia.The research group carried out on 17 and 18 November 2021 the virtual event "The future of Translational Medicine in Colombia: international and national advances". The activity allowed to discuss the state of translational medicine in Colombia and to know international experiences that can nourish the activities of national researchers (the national research group).The 17th day participated, in the first part, three leading professors and researchers in translation medicine and immunology of the Washington University and a Colombian physician and researcher of the Department of Fundamental Oncology of the Ludwig Cancer University, the first day of Colombia
