# Design lighting sports areas of the San Andrés zonal park

The zone park San Andrés, currently presents deficiencies in lighting in some of the sports areas that make it, which discourages its use in night hours, this condition was ratified by the user community, by means of a survey of perception of lighting. Therefore, it was considered pertinent to advance an lighting design for the skating rink, football field, multiple court section, trotting track and pedestrian trail of the park. This design was made to guarantee a functional lighting with attenuating luminaires to generate no affectations on the flora and fauna, co-existing in the park. The lighting design was carried out under the parameters set out in the Technical Regulation of Lighting and Lighting Public RETILAP and the international standard IES-R-P-6-15, guaranteeing a saving of energy and generating an attractive space for visitors and users of the park
