# Orientalism in Medellín: The Egyptian Palace and the Mausoleum of the Londoño in the first half of the 20th century

Through a critical analysis of the construction and realization of the Egyptian Palace (Barrio Prado-Centro) and the Londoño mausoleum (Cementario San Pedro) in Medellín during the first half of the 20th century, this project is asked for the attraction that Egypt generated in part of the elite of the city, in specific families such as the Estrada and the Londoño. On the other hand, we are interested in the different interpretations that the inhabitants of the city will generate about these works. Egyptomania is a topic that is not addressed in our Colombian context and this project aims to understand the reasons for its use in Medellín and in the last analysis of Orientalism in the middle of the Colombian Andes
