# Implementation of radiosurgery treatments in a clinical linear accelerator of a medium and high complexity public hospital in Valle del Cauca, 2021.

In this final master's work, the implementation of intracranial radiosurgery (SRS) treatments was carried out in a medium- and high-complexity hospital. The first part was the commissioning of CLINAC TrueBeam, for the validation of the commissioning, the TG 119 protocol was applied using the octavius 4D fantoma, Ruby and the Dosimtry Portal. For the VMAT technique and the gamma index evaluations using the Octavius 4D, values of the confidence limit of 4.5, 4.7, 4.06, 5.30 and 4.5 were found for the power beams 6, FFF, 10, 10 FFF and 16 MV, respectively, for this same technique and the dose-to-point evaluation, confidence limits of 0.019, 0.026, 0.016, 0.026 and 0.018 for the power beams 6, 6 FFF, 10, 10 FFF and 16 MV were used, respectively. Subsequently, a protocol for the implementation of intracranial radiosurgery was developed; from the recommendations for the simulation to the evaluation of the treatment plans were found. For the quality controls using the gamma index, the average amounts of the Tvig and 16 MV were used
