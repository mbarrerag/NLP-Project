# Pump potatoes

Cross-contamination is a global concern, it is the focus of food-borne diseases, serious diseases that can cause even death. On the other hand, conventional packaging used for itinerant consumption and fast meals has presented problems with materials (a chemical that adheres to food and causes health problems) and the preservation of food (does not retain liquids or fats, does not preserve the fresh product). That is why it is proposed to develop a new compound material with scientific and research base in the design of composite materials with technological function: insulation and thermal to minimize the consequences of cross-contamination and preserve the safety of the food
