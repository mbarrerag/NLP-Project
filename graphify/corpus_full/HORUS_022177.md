# Deep learning for temperature prediction on the refractory walls of an electric arc furnace.

This document details the workflow carried out for the development of deep learning models for the estimation of average wall temperature in two electric arc furnaces belonging to Cerro Matoso S.A. The document begins with an introduction to the context under which the final master's work was developed, giving way to the theoretical description of all relevant aspects and generalities about the operation of the plant, the time series and deep learning required during the development of the project. The workflow is divided into a 3 step methodology, beginning with the study and preparation of the data set provided by CMSA, followed by the development, training and selection of various models of deep learning used in predictions with data from a test set obtaining RMSE errors between 1-2 °C and ending with a validation stage that studies the performance of the various models obtained in the face to various variations in the conditions of training parameters
