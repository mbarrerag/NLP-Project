# Relationship dynamics for the development of tourism activity in rural territories: the case of Villa de Leyva, Boyacá

Tourism in rural areas employs the cultural, heritage and natural resources of the environment where it is developed. When its planning and management is not adequate, its sustainability is put at risk, generating unwanted effects that impact the base of resources, societies and local institutions of the territories. The objective of this research has been to analyze the relational dynamics between actors linked to the development of the activities of tourism in rural spaces in Villa de Leyva -Boyacá, to propose guidelines that guide the active management of the tourist destination. The methodology of mixed character used the mapping of actors, interviews with key informants, the analysis of social networks and the triangulation of information. It was deepened in the analysis of the relational dynamics achieving understanding how the social capital is structured among the actors and interest groups involved, prevaluating a social capital type linking with a configuration of actors in function of power that restricts the effective participation of the local society in the decisions that concern them.
