# BASIC INFILTRATION OF CAUCA,

The basic infiltration speed (Vib) is an important parameter for planning irrigation systems; however, this parameter is unknown in most soils of the hillside of Valle del Cauca, Colombia. For this reason, the objective of this research was to determine the Vib in three areas of the hillside using a new adaptation of Mariotte type droppers of low workload, from the wet area and the flow of the dripper designed. The experimentation was developed in three areas of the hillside of Roldanillo, Dagua and Pradera in the Valley of Cauca, in Hass avocado, MD-2 pineapple and blackberry crops, respectively. The soils were classified into: Frank-limous, Arclinus and Arcolimous, respectively. Water sheets of 2, 3, 3.5 and 4 litres were applied using the Mariotte drip principle and were distributed from a complete design to random in each farm. The wet area (Am) of each of the sheets applied using an image processing software were obtained.
