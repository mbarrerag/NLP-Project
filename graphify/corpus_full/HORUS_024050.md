# Feelings in the Colombian equity market: an extension to the four-factor model of Fama-French-Carhart

This document implements a model of asset valuation (CAPM) of four Fama-French-Carhart factors, extended by three alternative variables of feeling. The model is made on the monthly returns of the shares belonging to the COLCAP index of the Colombian Stock Exchange, for the period July-2013/June-2020. The exercise seeks to verify the effect that the feeling, approximated in different ways, has on the transversal cut of the return of the shares, as stated in Baker and Wurgler (2006). The variables of feeling that are proposed are inspired by the preceding literature and the theoretical precepts of the current of the finances of the behavior. Of the empirical exercise is found, among others, evidence in favor of the existence of a positive feeling effect on the actions of low capitalization and an effect of efficiency in the classical sense
