# Family photography, gender and memory. A material exploration.

The project proposes a critical-creative exploration of my family's photographic album to identify the practices of gender that with greater force build the social relationships and places of its members. In my thesis, I explore the way in which the gender is created in my family, and with this the way in which the order of prevailing gender is reproduced and re-created. The thesis is located from the critical perspective of feminist visual studies, feminist materialism and research based on the arts. The methodological approach of the the thesis is inspired by the embroidery of data, the intervention of embroidery on photography for the activation of photographic files, and the proposal of the contra-archive, and proposes the creation of a counter-album, in which this development and in turn materializes the analysis of the photographic album family. The text is divided into three chapters. The first develops the theoretical framework and state of art that frame the critical-creative analysis of the album, the second analysis and the metabolic proposal of the contra-album, the third and the ability to be used to be used in the contra-album
