# EXPERIMENTAL AND THEORETICAL STUDY OF FERROMAGNETIC ORDERING AND SEMICONDUCTOR RESPONSE IN THE LA2COFEO6 CERAMIC MATERIAL

Structural, magnetic and electric measurements of the La2CoFeO6 perovskite produced by the Pechini technique are reported. Ab initio calculations of the electronic and thermodynamic properties for these oxide ferrocobaltite were carried out by means of first principles calculations, using the formalism of the Functional Density Theory and the Plane Wave and Pseudopotential method through the VASP code. Exchange and correlation energy was described using the Generalized Gradient Approximation, including spin polarization and Hubbard potential correction due to the presence of Fe-3d and Co-3d orbitals. The ferromagnetic semiconductor behavior of the material was established by obtaining a band gap of 2.35 eV. Strong hybridizations between the 2p oxygen orbitals in the valence band with Fe2+-3d and Co4+-3d states allow us to explain the ferromagnetic nature through the superexchange mechanism between high-spin states of Fe2+ with low-spin states of Co4+ mediated by O2- orbitals. The theoretical results are in agreement with the values obtained from experimental measurement
