# THE WORLD AS COLLECTION: PHOTOGRAPHICAL SERVICES, CYCLOPEDIES AND SERIALITY

In 1975 the Salvat group published and produced the Encyclopaedia Salvat del Arte Colombiano, in which the production model initiated with the Encyclopaedia Salvat de Arte of 1914 and reprinted, with adaptations, in 1970. In that model the Colombian word could be replaced by Mexican or Ecuadorian, countries in which it also published their respective stories of art. In some cases art could be replaced by kitchen or sports. In all of them visual information, produced with technologies considered new at the time, was present both in the printed pages and in the strategies of persuasion that used the group editorial for wide and specialized audiences, among them the organization of thematic exhibitions in different museums, the design of catalogues and periodic advertising in printed, radial and television media. This model of production of visual information for encyclopaedias, periodic and updated by definition, it linked the Salvat group, simultaneously, to visual markets and international, in which the images, like other goods, formed part of a serial art, at large scale, adapted and adapted to the short-term inventory of the works
