# Ethnomedical and ethnobotanical study as a contribution to the formulation of an intercultural health model with the Senú de Caracolí indigenous community in Necoclí, Antioquia

The project focuses on understanding traditional medicine and the use of medicinal plants in the Senú community of Caracolí, Necoclí, Antioquia, with the aim of contributing to the formulation of an intercultural health model. The objectives include researching fundamental concepts of traditional medicine and health care in the Senú community, characterizing the use of medicinal plants from the cosmovision and ethnomedicine Senú, and proposing intercultural strategies for the promotion of health and the treatment of specific diseases. The methodology consists of a qualitative ethnomedical and ethnobotanical study with a research approach participation, through ethnobotanical and conversational tours with traditional physicians. The use and traditional classification of medicinal plants were characterized and various cultural diseases and traditional medicine practitioners Senú were analyzed. The data obtained were compared with planned publications of studies carried out with Senú communities. The strategy of intercultural health uses are proposed for the Senú indigenous people. The importance and value of medicinal plants in the culture of the Senú people, where the knowledge ethnomedicine is integral to their daily practices. search approach, through ethnobotanical walks and conversations with local traditional doctors. The traditional use and classification of 72 medical plants was characterized and varied cultural diseases and practices of traditional Senú medicine were analyzed. The data obtained was compared with previous publications of studies carried out with Senú communities. Intercultural health strategies are proposed for the Senú indigenous people. The import and value of medical plants in the culture of the Senú people is highlighted, where ethnomedical knowledge is integral to their daily practices. It is recommended to strengthen and culturally sensitive medical practices, looking to integrate traditional medicine with modern care approaches as proposed by the Indigenous and Intercultural System
