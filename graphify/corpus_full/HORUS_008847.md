# RELIENCE OF WOMEN UNDERTRACT, ANTIOCIA - CHICO, COLOMBIA

This work is based on the results of three years of research carried out in communities affected by the Bajo Atrato in the northwestern Colombia. The research focuses on the resilience of women who are victims of sexual violence in the case of the armed conflict in Colombia. It also focuses on the participation of writing on youth and citizenship as a contribution to the document "Aportes de la Universidad de Antioquia al diálogo Nacional", of 2022, collective work that was carried out with other colleagues from the University of Antioquia in Medellín, Colombia. In participatory research, the analysis was used in context, conducted to interviews and focus groups with women and young people from the populations of El Roto, Marriaga and Bocas del Atrato, located in the jurisdiction of the departments of Antioquia and Chocó, in Colombia
