# PEFICA 2.0: A TOOL OF LEARNING OF THE METHOD OF FINITE ELEMENTS FOR ALL

Many undergraduate curriculum programmes in Civil Engineering and postgraduate courses in Structural Engineering have included subjects describing basic concepts and applications of the finite elements method. Three limitations have been observed in the courses taught at the National University of Colombia on the subject: (i) the difficulty in solving problems with a moderate amount of degrees of freedom without the use of a computer program; (ii) the low appropriation of knowledge achieved when a problem with a computer program is simulated with closed code or with little documentation; and (iii) the low availability of free and open source computational tools that contribute to learning the method. The finite elements open source program PEFiCA 2.0 has sought to overcome these limitations, through a free and multiplatform computational tool, learning the finite elements method applied to solid mechanics. This tool calculates displacement, deformation and effort of a solid, allowing observing, understanding and modifying the lines of code of its procedures. The formulation of the method, the implementation of routines, validation through application problems and the use of the PEFiCA 2.0 program are documented and available
