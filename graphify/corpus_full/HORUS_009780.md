# GUIDE OF GEOLOGY INTEREST SITES IN THE CENTRAL CORDILLERA OF ANTI-IQUEN AND THE VALLEY OF THE RIUM CAUCA

The geotourism is considered a tool of conservation of the geological heritage, that can include geological, geographical and cultural elements. This concept has been developed recently in Colombia, in order to make known places of geological interest that promote the appropriation of the territory by the society. We present a field guide for a circular route of approximately 200 km of length, that unites Medellín with Santa Fe of Antioquia, Bolombolo and again Medellín. The study area covers an area of 2,500 km2 and is characterized by its geological diversity, geomorphological, archaeological and historical, comparable to other territories that promote the memory of the Earth in the world.
