# LETTERS FROM THE NIBLE

The article describes the experience in the production of the film Cartas desde la Niebla, Marta Isabel Hincapie. Both in the article, and in the film, references are made to what it involves for a group of immigrants to leave their own countries to inhabit the strange world that is the fog of Vic, a city in northern Spain
