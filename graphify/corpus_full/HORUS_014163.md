# Remote monitoring and semi-automatic generation of treatments for insulin-dependent people in the departments of Antioquia and Casanare

In the age of the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4RI), health benefits from technological advances such as digital systems and data analysis.The convergence between the health and engineering sciences drives research to improve treatment and provide the medical staff with the cutting edge technology that revolutionizes aspects of health service to the communities.In the case of diabetes mellitus (DM), the technology advances with continuous monitors of glucose and developments such as the artificial pancreas.However, challenges persist, especially in the treatment with insulin. It identifies the need to integrate digital components and expert knowledge in a friendly software to advance medical care, especially in areas far from urban cities that do not count with qualified staff or medical experts endocrinologists able to analyze trace of continuous monitoring of glucose and generate insulinotherapy suitable for people with type 1 or 2 insulin-dependent diabetes.The advances of research that the group has developed after several years and their interaction with experts indicates that the current process of medical assistance for DM, based on the experience of endocrinologists, can be partially automated and therefore build medical assistance software that can be used to collect data, analyze them and use them
