# EXCEPTIONAL CLAUSES IN STATE CONTRACTING

The book deals with one of the most important topics of public procurement. it is a clear difference with the private contract. the exceptional clauses are placed within the execution of the state contract and are a protection of public money and is an obligation for the public administration to finish the state contracts successfully and with this the public needs are met. through these clauses the administration directs, monitors the execution of the contract and through them can punish, interpret or terminate unilaterally and abnormally the contractual link with the contractor. it must be emphasized that for its application the rights as compensation, compensation and guarantees to the contractor, such as due process and when it is not their act that gave rise to the sanction clauses
