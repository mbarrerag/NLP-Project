# METATAXONOMIC AND METABOLIC ASSESSMENT OF THREE MICROBIOTS OF KEFIR CULTURED WATER IN SUGAR WATER SUNCH

The Sugarcane juice (JCA) is known as a carbon source in the growth of the spránulos de kéfir de agua (GKA).This study evaluated metataxonomic and metabolically the microorganisms present in three microbial consortia of GKA from Cali (KC), Bogotá (KB) and Medellin (KM) using as a medium of JCA. A molecular characterization and analysis of microbial diversity was carried out by sequencing in Illumina Miseq and the Mothur V1.4 platform.
