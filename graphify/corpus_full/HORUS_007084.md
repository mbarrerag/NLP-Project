# Technical Paper: Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia volubilis) as a potential ingredient in the development of plant-based protein drinks

The plant drinks (BV) are part of a growing market, encouraged by consumers interested in nutritious and sustainable food, as well as those with health conditions related to the consumption of milk or its components, who have adopted them as an alternative to milk drinks. The nutritional profile of the VBs is varied, depending on the plant sources used, being in several cases unable to match the supply of nutrients from milk. In addition, in some markets, more than 50% of commercial drinks are produced from a small number of vegetable sources, such as oats, soya and almonds. Therefore, there is the need to evaluate new plant sources to allow the development of BV options with high nutritional and sensory quality. The Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia volubilis) (SI), has aroused interest in the food industry thanks to its high percentage of oil rich in poly- fatty acids, its protein content, and the presence of vitamins, minerals and bioactive compounds, considering itself a matrix with a high potential from the functional and nutritional point of view (SI), despite the fact that it is used in terms of its use. monds.
