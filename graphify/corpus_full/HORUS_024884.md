# Transmical, the re-signification of a territory. A proposal to articulate the urban structure of the Manitas and Juan Pablo II neighborhoods to the Transmical transport system in Bogotá 2019 and 2020

The implementation of a new system of mass urban transport in a sector of informal origin represents a series of challenges and opportunities for the territory and its community, by generating a change in the socio-economic dynamics and in its relationship with the city. The Transmicable project is the first of its kind in the city of Bogotá. It is located in the town of Ciudad Bolívar, specifically in the Barrios Manitas and Juan Pablo II, developed from an informal occupation, having as consequence the physical and social vulnerability of the territory that is represented in the lack of urban services and in the multidimensional poverty. In view of the above, this TFM investigates the transformations that a project of this magnitude can cause in the urban structure of an informal neighborhood, and it is evidenced the need to generate a physical structure that supports the new dynamics that it produces, giving rise to an opportunity for improvement for the quality of life in the sector. territory with virtues and the potential to achieve a different way of referring to peripheral areas of the city. Taking as an instrument the integral improvement of neighborhoods to achieve the integration of the formality of the transmicable and the informality of these neighborhoods in a project developed with the community. Through urban circuits they manage to link different situations, activities and uses in a single integral urban structure
