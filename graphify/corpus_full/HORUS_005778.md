# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE PRESIDENCY DISCUSSION AND IDENTIFICATION OF POLITICAL COMMUNITIES IN COLOMBIA

The statistical analysis of social networks is a predominant methodology in political science research. In this article we propose to implement methods of networks to characterize the presidential discourse and identify political communities in Colombia. We propose an empirical approach to the discursive structure of heads of State and to the configuration of alliance and work relations among prominent figures of Colombian politics. Thus, we implement methods of networks from two perspectives: words and political actors. We conclude on the relevance of social media statistics to identify frequent and important terms in the communicative action of the rulers, and to examine cohesion in the discourses. Finally, we distinguish notable actors in the consolidation of working relationships and alliances, and political communities
