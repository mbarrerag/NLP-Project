# Contribution of bending by cutting in the total deflection of laminated guadua elements requested for bending.

In this work, the total deflection of laminated guadua specimens requested for flexion, oriented in radial and tangential direction, and the deflections produced by the cutting forces, were determined in order to establish their contribution to the total deflection, in the linear elastic range of the material. To this end, the modulus of elasticity to sharpness G and the modulus of elasticity E parallel to the fibres were determined experimentally, the latter was determined in two ways, the first of which from the displacement in the centre of the light, for this case was identified as the modulus of elasticity E_a, and the second based on the longitudinal deformations to compression _supp and voltage From the investigation, it was concluded that the L/h ratio for beams with vertical sheets should be greater than 16 in order to make the contribution of deflection per shear less than 5%. In the case of horizontal sheets the ratio should be greater than 20 in order to obtain a contribution of deflection per shear less than 5%
