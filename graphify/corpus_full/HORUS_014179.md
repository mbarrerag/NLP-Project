# Consolidation of the investigation processes of the Royal Justice Group, two proposals: I) Research on Neutra Clauses and their impact on women in three different areas: i) as mothers in the processing of leave permits for their children; ii) as land claimants; and iii) as women victims of VBG in disciplinary proceedings. And, II) the publication of the research results of the I-Imbricate Justice project: A localized analysis of justice in Latin America.

The Research Group - JURE- Real Justice emerged in 2017 (at the beginning of the National Observatory of Real Justice - ONJURE-) under the female leadership in partnership with external researchers, as well as law and political science students. From the beginning, our intention has been to produce empirical constitutional research, which uses both qualitative and quantitative research techniques, with a gender focus and the interdisciplinarity that reflects more accurately the barriers or obstacles in access to prompt, timely and effective justice by historically marginalized groups. Thus, we have addressed critically the follow-up to the compliance of judicial orders by the high courts and then, the anchoring of constitutional justice in Latin America in a wide spectrum with the study of 6 countries in the region, in both cases questioning the existing methodologies and proposing a new one that would enable us to achieve the purposes of the research. In the spirit of consolidating -JURE-, we propose two main lines of work for this call: 1. To update the findings obtained in the research already completed and financed by Hermes entitled Imbe-I (2019-2021) in which we analyze the political systems of Bolivia. Ecuador and Mexico-based. We will embroider a new research project called "neutral clauses and three impacts on women as land claimants, on the right of mothers not to be separated from their children on international trips and as victims of VBG", in this new commitment we will deepen our experience with empirical and intersectional constitutional research that goes through constitutional law, legal sociology and political science. In this second part we persist on research issues that have marked the difference of JURE with the production of critical constitutional research, which goes beyond normative analysis to verify the justice of the law in its daily application, in which the claim of neutrality of the law hides forms of indirect discrimination that is only verifiable when determining the normative effectiveness.
