# Nanotechnological base alternatives in the control of ethylene and/or antimicrobial activity to improve the post-harvest life of Hass avocado

The maturation of avocado and many of its quality parameters are influenced by the production of ethylene and pathologies such as the anthracnosis, whose management represents a challenge for the industry as they affect the quality of the fruit and with this its commercialization. An alternative in the control of the production of ethylene is the use of nanoparticles with photocatalytic behavior that by means of reactions inhibit the production of gas. It is thus that in this work were synthesized particles of TiO2 doped with different concentrations of Ag by the technique of self-combustion in solution. The particles obtained were characterized morphologically using techniques like DRX and SEM. The aggregates of particles of TiO2 in anatase phase were obtained, with 0.75%, 1.5% and 3.5% mol Ag of approximately 109.5 nm and in mixed phase of anatase-rutilus with 0% and 4.5% mol Ag with average sizes of 184,03 and 205,06 nm.
