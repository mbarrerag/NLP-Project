# Science for cocoa farming

-The cocoa grain obtained from the species Theobroma cacao L. is dynamizing the world economy, result of the increasing demand in the international market and the employment in various products of the food industry and the pharmaceutical industry, which are factors that have stimulated the sowing of the cocoa tree.The increase in the demand for cocoa butter and consumption of chocolates are aspects that motivate the production of cocoa.The demands of the buyers of cocoa grain that request stable volumes and quality are arguments that in the last decade have encouraged the research in this crop.The technology for the cultivation of cocoa in the last 20 years at the world level has been aimed at issues related to the propagation methods, the conditioning of seeds and the techniques of grafting and the management of nurseries, although the scientific publications, around 4930, mainly in the field of agricultural and biological sciences, they present different topics from genetics, cultivation methods and plant health, socio-economic aspects and biological control.The dissemination of results and technology is led by the United States, Brazil and France, thanks to the work of institutions like the Agricultural Research Service of the USDA (Department of Agriculture of the United States), the University of the University of the University of the European Union of the European Union of
