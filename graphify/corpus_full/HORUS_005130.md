# Modeling of cargo travel for shopping centres in cities in developing countries.

The commercial centres are establishments that allow the various activities within the same physical space. Therefore, they need to be supplied with various products and services that generate daily travel and freight transport, a subject that will be addressed in this research. The commercial centres are being constructed and remodeled around the world as the dynamics of society are changing, and it is becoming more and more common to search for spaces that offer different types of activities and services within the same site (bank, health, supermarket, entertainment, etc.). The research seeks to analyze and propose mechanisms that allow us to evaluate this behavior to find solutions to many problems that arise during supply and logistics; some of them are the vehicular congestion that is generated around these establishments, a condition that harms the daily mobility of the surrounding areas. This research was developed through surveys, interviews and data collection to define variables that allow us to perform models that are closest to reality and thus provide conclusions and valuable observations.
