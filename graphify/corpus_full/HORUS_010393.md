# ADDITIVE MODELS GENERALIZED TO OPTIME THE HYDROPHOBICITY PROCESS OF CAOLINITE

The extensive industrial use of kaolinite requires that extraction processes be modeled from to determine the appropriate conditions of benefit. Although classical linear regression models have been used, these have not been appropriate due to the non-compliance of normal distribution for the response variable. The data analyzed in this study correspond to a process of extraction of kaolinite by physicochemical surface surfaces performed in La Unión, Antioquia, Colombia. The response variable was the zeta potential and the explanatory variables were type of collecting solution, concentration and pH. In this article, the recovery of kaolinite through generalized additive models is modeled, which allow to choose the statistical distribution and model all parameters according to explanatory variables. Five distributions were selected for the response variable according to the information criterion of Akaike (AIC). The model with generalized distribution Beta 2 was the model that presented the best performance according to the metrics used. From this model it was found that the best operating conditions obtained from the analysis of the response surfaces are type of oleic acid collector, concentration 10 units and pH of 6
