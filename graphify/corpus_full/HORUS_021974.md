# U(1)' models with and without leptonic universality.

A phenomenological analysis is performed in the framework of two abelian extensions of the group of the Standard Model of elementary particles, one universal in the lepton families and the other non-universal that distinguishes these families. In particular, this work proposes an extension of the universal model with spontaneous CP violation, resulting in a viable model that allows adjusting the experimental limits of the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon and electrical dipolar moment of the electron, finding regions of the space of parameters simultaneously compatible with these two observations. Within the framework of the non-universal leptonic model, a detailed study is made for the calculation of the decay of the B-meson in leptonic final states, finding solutions that conform to the deviations found in the CERN-LHCb experiment
