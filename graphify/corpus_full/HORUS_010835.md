# PERCEPTION OF TOURISM STAKEHOLDERS DUE TO THE EFFECTS OF COVID-19. CASE OF MUNICIPAL TERRITORIES IN ANTIHOQUIA - COLOMBIA

Summary The measures taken to contain the spread of the Covid-19 virus affected the world economy and the tourism sector in particular. The paralysis of activities resulted in a drastic fall in its operations, also impacting on the perception of tourism actors in the economic recovery process. The research sought to identify the perception of the actors of the tourism sector chain about the impacts generated by Covid-19 in the municipalities Jericho and Garden, located in southwestern Medellín-Colombia, with a qualitative and interpretative approach which tended to understand, interpret and perceive the realities of tourism service providers. The design was of a cross-sectional, non-experimental type and the phenomenological method was used. Direct effects were evidenced mainly on the sustainability and permanence of companies in the sector, financial and income weaknesses, which led to unemployment in the region. Finally, tourism companies located in these two municipalities do not have action plans and response to these external threats, which leads to decisions such as reducing staff, closing and liquidating companies in a short time and feeling of not having the support of public institutions.
