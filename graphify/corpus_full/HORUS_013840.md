# Mathematics Olympics of the UNAL Orinoquia Headquarters: towards the formation of critical thinking and the study of mathematics

Within the framework of the National Call for Solidarity Extension 2023 _x0093_ Harmonization of missionary functions to promote sustainable development and peace-building in the territories_x0094_, the first version of Mathematics Olympics in the city of Arauca is proposed, accompanied by training sessions for students of the main educational institutions at secondary level in the city of Arauca, in order to promote the development of critical thinking and the resolution of mathematical problems in young people. Likewise, and in the hope that this activity will generate a greater impact, it is proposed to hold some workshops in relation to mathematics Olympics with teachers in the mathematics field of participating educational institutions. For this organization, activities are proposed for the development and execution of the proposal as well as a schedule of activities of the same
