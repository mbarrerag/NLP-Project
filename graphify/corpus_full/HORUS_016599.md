# MANAGEMENT OF EQUIPMENT AND ELEMENTS OF PERSONAL AND COLLECTIVE PROTECTION

The use of personal protection elements (PPE) and collective protection elements (PCPs) is a mechanism for managing the risks present in the workplace. In Colombia, their provision is the responsibility of employers, taking into account that, according to the hierarchy of controls, this is the last resort and will be taken into account as a complementary and non-exclusive measure in relation to exposure to risks. Their selection is defined according to the worker’s protection needs, which depend on the types of linkages whether they are employees, temporary or contractors. This document raises legal and technical aspects that it is necessary to be known always applying the hierarchy of controls. The document is presented in three units: the first presents the concept of the hierarchy of controls; the second, it indicates the steps to build a management programme for EPP/PC. Finally, the document presents a generic, non-specific or exhaustive matrix on EPP/PCs
