# Standardization and systematization of the internal and external user requests management process for the provision of services in laboratories

The Directorate of Laboratories at Medellín Headquarters, in the framework of the investment project 2019-2021, defined as one of its goals the Definition of a management model for laboratories at Headquarters (docencia, Investigación, Extension), where with the participation of the laboratory staff who have accredited tests or have worked on the implementation of management systems in the laboratory, documentation and validation of the processes and procedures for the management of laboratories was achieved, however, it was identified that in order to achieve its agile implementation in the other laboratories at the headquarters, it is necessary to systematize these processes. In this sense, this proposal proposes the systematization of the process of handling requests from internal and external users of laboratories under the BPM methodology, for which the university has acquired the software Process Maker for the systematization of processes for some years, starting from the schematization of the same processes through a flow chart where the tasks are clearly defined and that also allows to control the execution of tasks and obtain statistics.
