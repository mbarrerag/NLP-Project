# Kotaiiki 2 -A pilot for active, collaborative and researchive learning of Bioclimatic Architecture on the University Campus

Bioclimatic is the study of the thermodynamics of the buildings. The bioclimatic architecture is an improved architecture with the knowledge on the transformations of the energy in the matter and on the mechanisms of transfer of the heat. The global warming has awakened again in the guild of the architecture and the urgency of reducing the emissions and the energy consumption. The Bioclimatic is an opportunity for these urgent needs. The School of Architecture and Urbanism is understanding this need and attending to the external evaluations??? has increased the hours of class on the topic. Moreover, throughout the experience in teaching the Bioclimatic in the school of Architecture I have found that the best learning results have been given when I have been able to use the elements of measurement and laboratories. The present research aims to contribute in the formation of the Architect this knowledge in a dynamic way through the experimental observation in the spaces of the university. Practice environmental measurements in the school of Architecture, modeling the spaces and surfaces understanding its thermodynamics, analyzing the results, proposing, simulating and/or experiencing possible solutions to the thermal problems found, generating these results of the knowledge of the students and the theoretical knowledge process
