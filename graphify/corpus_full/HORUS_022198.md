# Causality analysis for functional multivariate time series.

The causality of Granger is a test created almost half a century ago that allows to know if a temporary series helps in the prediction of another. For the case of temporary fun-tional series the topic has been explored by authors such as Saumard and Hadjadji (2021) or Sen et al. (2022), however the topic still has many open lines of research that have been little explored. This work concentrates on studying an extension of the Granger causality tests for multivariate functional time series of dimensions greater than 2 (specifically 3 and 4), based on the procedures proposed by Saumard and Hadjadji (2021). For this purpose bivariate, tri-variate and tetra-variate processes are simulated from models FAR(1) and FARX(1). The causality tests of Granger are performed through three procedures (DFPCA, F-cauality and G-cautality).
