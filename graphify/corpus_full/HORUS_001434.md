# BIBLE ANALYSIS OF PUBLICATIONS IN SCOPUS ON THE EVALUATION OF THE PATIENT'S SAFETY CULTURE

The Culture of Patient Safety is a key element in improving quality in health systems, since it directly influences the prevention of adverse events. To identify the main researches that evaluate the Culture of Patient Safety between 2014 and the first quarter of 2024. A descriptive bibliometric analysis was carried out using data from the Scopus database and VOS viewer 1.6.20 software to analyze scientific production, co-authorship networks and institutional leadership. The results indicate a significant increase in the publication of studies since 2020, coinciding with the COVID-19 pandemic. 93.5% of the research uses a quantitative approach, being the Hospital Survey on Patient Safety Culture instrument the most used to measure the Culture of Patient Safety. United States, China and Spain lead the scientific production in this field, while the participation of Latin American countries is limited. The analysis also emphasizes the importance of organizational processes and the continuous improvement of quality in the evaluation of the culture of safety.
