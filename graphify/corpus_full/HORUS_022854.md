# TROTAMIND CULTURES: A CONTRIBUTION OF ICA AND BIOVERSITY-CIT ALLIANCE FOR AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH

Scientific research and technological development is the creative work as a result of the collaboration of multiple institutions, and this has existed for decades in a great synergy between the Colombian Agropecuario Institute (ICA) and the Bioversity-CIT Alliance. This collaboration has contributed significantly to the distribution and promotion around the world of genetic materials of rice, fodder, frijole and yuca with better agronomic characteristics, and at the same time in the maintenance and conservation of the diversity of these crops. The records of the genetic improvement programs and the geneplasm bank of the Alliance show that in collaboration both institutions have distributed about 400,000 genetic materials containing base germplasm, advanced lines, or improved varieties to various countries around the world, following the international normivity and phytosanitary certification issued by the ICA. The results show that the distributed materials have been requested by a great diversity of institutions in different countries and with multiple purposes in the research and agronomic engineering. There are several achievements achieved by the ICA and the Alliance (e.g., development of varieties, recovery of genetic diversity, etc.), which have contributed to the scientific research and scientific research. The work carried out by the ICA and the Bioversity-CIT Alliance is essential to ensure the quality of the material stored by CGIAR and kept at the disposal of the international community, protecting diversity through the preservation of seeds and materials vital to this end
