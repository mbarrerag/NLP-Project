# SATISFACTION OF THE FAMILY'S INFORMATION NEED: A DISEASE INTERVENTION IN THE UNIT OF INTENSIVE CARE.

The entry of a person to the intensive care unit (ICU) causes the family to be confronted with situations that break its functional structure, with consequences such as: high levels of anxiety, depression and stress (1); derived from factors associated with the critical situation of the patient. The ICU environment generates in the family a series of information needs that have been studied using different instruments such as the CCFNI (Critical Care Family Needs Inventory) and the CCFSS (Critical Care Family Satisfaction Survey) (2, 3). The needs identified in the family have been grouped into cognitive, emotional, social and practical needs. These needs can be met through nursing intervention proposals to the patient's family at the ICU. This research seeks to implement a nursing pilot intervention to meet the need of patient family information at the ICU
