# Sense and context, tools for designing marketing strategies

The challenge of teaching must be taken up with commitment, effort, responsibility, reflection and discipline. This is why a pedagogical project is developed that seeks to build tools for meaningful learning in students of the last semester of university, who are practically a step away from becoming involved in the real world and facing the reality of work. The commitment is not only to students, it is with society. The project seeks that students have a period of reflection of their professional work, who have doubts of their passion can have a close approach, explore, who have clarity deepen in their interest.
