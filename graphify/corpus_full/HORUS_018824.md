# SIMULATION OF METHANOL PRODUCTION FROM RESIDUAL BIOMASSES IN A CU/ZNO/AL2O3 PACKED BED REACTOR

The objective of this article is to simulate an algorithm built in MATLAB to represent the catalytic conversion of SYNGAS into methanol in a packaged bed reactor, based on chemical kinetics for a heterogeneous system with a Cu/ZnO/Al2O3 as a catalyst, and in mathematical and phenomenological models, such as pressure drop and catalyst deactivation. A validation of the model is performed, comparing the reference results and results obtained when executing in the MATLAB algorithm the SYNGAS reference composition. In addition, the model constructed considers a catalyst deactivation by sintering and pressure drop along the reactor. Several parameters were evaluated to identify the conditions for the production of metyl alcohol; These parameters include the selection of the gasifying agent, the effect of the biomass and steam ratio and the origin of biomass
