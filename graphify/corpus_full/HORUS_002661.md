# GERMANY IN THE REPRODUCTIVE TRACT IN A YEGUA.

Hemangiosarcoma is a malignant tumor of endothelial origin rarely described in the reproductive tract of the horses, there are no reports in Colombia. to describe a hemangiosarcoma in the reproductive tract of a mare. Anamnesis: a mare of 41 months old horse-bred Colombian Creole, coming from Chia, Colombia, referred to the Clinic of Great Animals of the National University of Colombia for persistent vaginal haemorrhage with history of surgical intervention by post-uterine wash-infusion, lesion near the urinary meato. Clinical findings: in the vaginoscopy cells two masses were observed, one approximately 5 cm cranial of the external uretral or other hole, and the other in the roof of the same area, both of a trabeculated appearance with clots on the surface, the yegua was referred for surgery. Surgical abordage: under the neurolepto analgesia in constant infusion rate, epidural and local anesthesia, surgical removal of the masses of the vagina was performed with a margin of healthy tissue of approximately 1cm.
