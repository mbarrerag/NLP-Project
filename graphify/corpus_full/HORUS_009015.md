# EXPERIMENTATION BODIES FOR FACTORIAL DESIGN 2K

When planning a factorial design, the usual practice is to suggest that experiments are conducted in random order. This randomization is intended to safeguard the possible influence of unknown factors, since it is expected that this influence will be dissipated between all effects so that none is substantially affected and no errors are made when estimating their statistical significance. But this way of acting has two major drawbacks: that the number of level changes in the factors required by randomization may be high and difficult to implement, which complicates and encourages experimentation. Furthermore, it is not possible to control the influence of unknown factors, since there are clearly better orders than others to minimize the influence of those factors beyond experimentation. This proposal aims to present a procedure that allows obtaining experimental orders with the minimum number of changes in the factors and that also minimizes the linear influence of unknown factors in the estimation of effects, for any factorial design at 2 levels
