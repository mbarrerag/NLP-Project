# Evaluation of the extensional tectonic of the Lower Cretaceous in the central segment of the Colombian Andes

A continental margin characterized by continuous subduction as the Andes of the North, includes the alternation of phases in which the extension, understanding or the tectonic neutrality alternates (Horton and Fuentes, 2016).Each of these phases gives rise to a structural, sedimentary, magmatic and thermal particular register.During the extension phases, the processes of generating magmas and the thermal regime are modified by the approach of the astenosphere and cortical slimming. This situation also modifies the filling history of the sedimentary basins and their diagenetic conditions. For the Colombian Andes, there is still a wide ignorance of the nature of the magmatic and sedimentary register in its initiation. The spatial and temporal distribution of the deformative phases is ambiguous; as well as the influence of the thermal flow in the processes of the Intland and the central Credipenta are significantly advanced. The results of the study are presented and are intercalated with pyroclastic rocks, presenting also concordant relations with younger Cretaceous rocks that accumulated under neutral tectonic conditions. This project aims to evaluate in an integrated way the compositional characteristics of the plutonic register of the Central Cordillera (Stock de Mariquita and Complex Ígneo de Samana) by geochemical analysis of total rock and analysis of Hf isotopia in circus, in conjunction with the analysis of integrated provenance (petrography, analysis of heavy minerals and geochronology of U-Pb in circus) in the formations Yavi and Horses in the Upper Valley of Magdalena, as well as evaluating the diagenetic conditions of the units of the Cretaceous Inferio to Superior (modifications in the mining model of clays), in order to construct an integrated tectonic model that contributes to answer three key questions. 1) As is the relationship between the magmatic and extension history, especially if this event controls the multi-stage of the plutonism and the vulcanism present
