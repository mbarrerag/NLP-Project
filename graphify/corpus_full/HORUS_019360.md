# SINTONEED MASS AMOUNTS: A GENERAL REVIEW

The loads generated by seismic events and strong winds are forces of nature that subject civil works to extreme situations, which eventually causes the failure of structures and often the loss of human lives. To face these forces of random character and difficult prediction, structural engineering poses regulations of design and construction of obligatory compliance in most countries of the world, which allow structures to adequately withstand the forces imposed. And as history has shown, sometimes good design is not enough, so that seismic engineering develops new methodologies and devices that help to protect even more structures when they are subjected to actions such as earthquakes and winds. To address these challenges, mechanisms such as shock absorbers and controllers, grouped as passive, active, semi-active and hybrid devices, with innovative designs that contribute greatly to giving greater security and confidence to our civil works, appear in this article an overview of the tuned mass dampers, their historical development, mechanical and analytical models, reach, strengths and weaknesses
