# Sodium-reducing alternatives in food: a review of study cases that do not use sodium substitution by other cations

The reduction of sodium in processed foods has become one of the most important bets of the world public health, due to its impact on the quality of life and the index of death. However, reducing salt in a food carries different consequences at the level of taste, useful life and texture. It is emphasized the use of substitutes, enhancers and modified salts. In this review cases of sodium reduction using aromatic compounds as a strategy of reformulation and NaCl salts modified in its morphology and crystalline structure. During the review it was possible to identify some aromatic compounds capable of inducing the salty taste, useful to adjust the sensory acceptance after a considerable reduction in the sodium content.
