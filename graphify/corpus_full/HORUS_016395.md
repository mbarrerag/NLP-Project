# An information architecture for cohort studies in environmental epidemiology.

The cohorte studies in environmental epidemiology are widely used to determine the occurrence of a specific event in a group of individuals. These studies experience numerous challenges because the information required to be carried out requires a wide temporary range of observation and constant follow-up of the participants. The development of concepts and techniques to carry out the research of the relation with the environment and health events is the focal point of these challenges. The causes of these challenges include several factors. The first factor is an inefficient process in the recovery of the medical information of each participant. The second factor is the inexact data. The second factor is the design to manage the data that results in the architecture of the information. The third factor is the lack of information that researchers require to plan a strategy to solve the research problem. This work involves an information integration solution based on logical models and physical models using technological solutions. This study focuses on a design to help manage the data that is based on the architecture of environmental epidemiology. The work consists in developing an information architecture for the studies of environmental epidemiology that are associated with the problems mentioned above to develop concepts and techniques based on the design of the design of the design of environmental architecture
