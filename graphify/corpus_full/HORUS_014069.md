# Limitations of gender parity in parties: a comparative study of the case of the Colombian Liberal Party, the Central Democratic Party and the party coalition Historical Pact in the legislative elections of 2022

The legislative elections of 2022 in Colombia represented a milestone in the democratic history of the country, since the congress was formed with the highest percentage of women elected in the recent history of the country, when a total of 86 legislators were elected, representing 29.15% of the legislature for the period 2022-2026 (Corporación Sisma Mujer, 2022). However, the debate on equity within political parties, far from being settled, was one of the most important issues within the political contest of the election. This was visible when it evidenced the presence of political parties and movements that tended to seek the construction of parity lists for the corporation, the emergence of feminist political bets that positioned the issue of parity within the parties, as well as the debates around the relevance and effectiveness of the electoral legislation in force when it came to achieving substantive equity between men and women within the positions of popular election in Colombia.
