# Assessment of methodologies to estimate aquifer fee in a Tropical Climate Zone

In the present research, evaluations of empirical methodology was carried out out with the aim of calculating the potential aquifer aquifer recharge in the Lebrilla river basin, located in Santander, Colombia, a region characterized by its tropical climate. Aquifer recharge referers to the flow of water that percolates the soil into the groundwater. Empirical methodology were used to determine this value, taking into account different variables such as precipitatation, temperature, land cover and land use, current evacuation and topographic information. These aspects are fundamental as they directly affect the environment. The calculation and analysis result result of the results was implemented using the Rprogramme. The analysis and calculation of results were carried out using the R programming language, chosen for their potential as an innovative alternative to traditional Geographic Information (GIS) systems used in the management of geospatial data in research of this type. The results obtained highlight the importance of taking into account multiple parameters when estimating the potential recharge of aquifers. Likewise, it was observed how adverse climatic phenomena, such as La Niña and El Niño, significantly affect estimates of recharge in tropical areas
