# Educational sequences in ethnobotany and taxonomic classification as support for the strengthening of scientific competences.

The current challenges in education are focused on the search to provide a solid training for young people, this challenge focuses especially on the development of skills and competencies that allow them to have a greater understanding of their environment and in this way to ensure that their actions are aligned with the demands and challenges of today's society. For this reason it is necessary to design instruments as didactic sequences to support the strengthening of scientific competencies. In this work, didactic sequences in ethnobotany, taxonomic classification and medicinal plants, based on significant learning and developed within a target group made up of 38 students, 19 women and 19 men aged between 14 and 16 years old were designed and implemented between strata 1 and 3. In this sense, the type of research is applied, with a mixed approach and simple correlational scope that under a quasi experimental design allowed the application of a pretest, a postest and surveys of perception of students before and after the implementation of the pedagogical strategy. As results of the implementation of the didactic sequences it is possible to conclude that students strengthened scientific competencies such as research, communication, the team work and the willingness to recognize the social dimension
