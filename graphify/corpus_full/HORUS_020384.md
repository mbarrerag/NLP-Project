# To dwell at the expense of the environment. A reflection on the dynamics of self-construction and of living in the popular city

Morar a costo del medio is an artistic inquiry on gentrification, occupation and logic of appropriation processes in the city of Medellin, from the case of the informal settlement of the community Sion City of God in the lower areas of the Puente Madre Laura Montoya. The research-creation process groups a series of visual narratives ranging from sculptural experimentation and intervention in public space, to the use of audiovisual and documentary tools that enunciate the power of social resistance through self-management and self-construction processes. In addition, it launches a critical look at the phenomena of urban development that transform the landscape and the dynamics of life of citizens. .
