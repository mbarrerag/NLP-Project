# Evaluation of neutralising capacity of a multivalent anti-coral antivenom in neuromuscular preparation.

The poison of coral snakes, of the genus Micrurus, is known to generate a neurotoxic effect. This effect is similar among the different species of this genus, however, the efficacy of cross neutralization of antivenoms is not yet fully known. In this study we evaluated the effects of the poisons of three Colombian coral snakes, of the species M. lemniscatus, M, medemi and M, sangilensis. Determining the ability to inhibit neurotransmission, in a preparation of frenic nerve and diaphragm of Wistar rats. The effects of the poison were evaluated at doses of 1, 10 and 50 μg/ml and the neutralizing capacity of the antivenom anti-collus polyvalent (AAP) of the National Institute of Health (INS), in front of the dose of 10 μg/ml of each species. The 3 poisons generated neuromuscular blockades dependent on the doses and the controls were evaluated.
