# Study of the intestinal microbiome of Zophobas died (Coleoptera: Tenebrionidae) as an approach to the potential use for polystyrene biodegradation.

The Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) is a synthetic polymer of common use in different industries, mainly in packaging and construction. Due to its chemical composition based on long chains of hydrocarbons, the EPS has a molecular activity that allows it to remain immodifiable for hundreds of years, which generates a negative impact on the environment, since it is not a substrate that is nutritious for any microorganism or is unknown for which it is considered. Currently, it has been reported that larvs of coleopter larvers of the family Tenebrionidae can biodegradable it thanks to enzymes produced by their intestinal microbiota. Considering the above, the objective of this research was to characterize the intestinal microbiota of the larvs of Zophobas morio as an approximation to the potential use for the biodegradation of the Polyestirene (PS), using metabarcoding. For this purpose, the analysis was performed Illululumina Misetrostero Piseq of the V3-4 region of the 16s of the ARNr of the complete lar of Z. morio-car to feed with two types of diets, EPS and Avn and Avna.
