# IN PANDEMIA, GENDER-BASED DISIGUALITY GROWTH IS INVISIBLE.

The pandemic has deepened the conditions of inequality and inequality between population groups and between territories.The case of women is dramatic.Before the pandemic, it was already recognized that women in Colombia contribute three quarters of unpaid care work in households, which is reflected in twice the hours in time compared to that dedicated by men to those tasks.During the pandemic and with the definitions of social confinement and distancing what have meant to concentrate activities in the houses, is an indisputable fact that women find themselves with an overload of physical and mental work, by combining care, domestic and work activities, whether formal or informal.In the basis of this differentiation there is a patriarchal culture that reinforces in these times previous patterns of the role of women in care.It is mainly about them the response of institutional care to women, who are sick and with disabilities who need it and the need has increased in the stage of confinement.With an additional overload resulting from the deepening of the food, economic and civil insecurity; even, political.To this point, the response of institutional care to women, and the presence of women workers, as well as the greater social services and the need is increased in the context of the social services
