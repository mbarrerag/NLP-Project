# ASSESSMENT OF THE POTENTIAL EXPLORATORY OF HYDROCARBOURS OF THE CRETACY SUCCESSION OF THE ORIENTAL PLANS (PROVIANCE OF CASANARE) TO PARTICIPATE STRATIGRAFT AND PETROPHISIC CHARACTERIZATION

The Ante-Country Basin of the Eastern Plains is the largest oil province in Colombia; however, most of the known fields are associated with structural traps in river sandstone deposits of the Cenozoic. The aim of the study is to evaluate the exploratory potential of the marine succession of the Cretaceous from the definition of changes of characteristics, petrophysical properties of potentially storage units and identification of seals
