# AGROECOLOGY IN A PLATO: AGROBIODIVERSITY AS A FOOD AND NUTRITIONAL EDUCATION TOOL

The experience to be shared consisted in the development of a tool for ethnically and territorially adapted food and nutrition education, drawing on the knowledge of the food and culinary systems that reflect the agrobiodiversity of fragile ecosystems such as the tropical dry forest. The main results of the process of constructing these food guides are the participation of communities in the recognition of their food potentials and how from these can be achieved a varied, sustainable and nutritionally adequate food from food customs. The novel thing about this research exercise is the inclusion of a geographical and historical approach based on the dialogue of knowledge in the planning of food guides from inter and transdisciplinary exercise. This tool is also an input that strengthens the local public procurement processes that dinamize family and community peasant agriculture. food cartography; territory; indigenous; nutrition; agrobiodiversity
