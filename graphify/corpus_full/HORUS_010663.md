# ASSESSMENT, VOICES OF EDUCATIONAL ACTORS IN THE UNIVERSITY CONTEXT

The importance of the implementation of quality management systems in the university context lies in the establishment of strategies that allow the participation of the actors of the educational community (teachers, managers and students), since, on these, the improvement of different processes such as the evaluation of learning depends. Consequently, this research considered what is stated in Decree 1330 (2019) in relation to the evaluation of learning results. These are conceived as the express statements of what a student is expected to know and demonstrate when completing his academic process MEN (2019). This qualitative approach study, it was proposed to know the different perspectives of the evaluation, through interviews directed by the actors of a higher education community. For the analysis of data, the transcripts of the interviews were passed by the Altas Ti software, where the information was organized in the categories: individual factors, collective factors, teaching and learning process. The results managed to show that evaluation is multifactorial, is a powerful tool and its effectiveness depends on how the systems, types of evaluation and the people involved in the process are integrated
