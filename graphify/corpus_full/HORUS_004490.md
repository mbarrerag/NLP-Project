# THE MULTI-FAMILIARS. EXPERIMENTAL SETTINGS OF MODERN SOCIAL LIVING IN MEXICO CITY

The housing buildings, known as multifamily buildings, originated in Mexico as a result of the population explosion and the economic boom that took place in this country in the mid-20th century. Their main feature consisted of concentrating the house on high buildings to liberate the ground and generate public parks. This arrangement was made in apples larger than usual, in super-apples, which additionally contained complementary services, such as schools, health centres, sports facilities or commerce. These housing blocks were greatly influenced by the housing packages of modern European architecture and also by local traditions, which led to an extraordinary mix of foreign influences with local traditions. Multi-family housing can be considered as experiments in the social, architectural and urban areas, which reached a high degree of excellence, until achieving a full well-being of the State workers, to whom these homes were intended
