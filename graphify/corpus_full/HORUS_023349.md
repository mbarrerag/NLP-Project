# FACTORS ASSOCIATED TO THE USE OF RESHIBITOR N95 IN WORKERS OF A HEALTH INSTITUTION

tuberculosis is a disease of interest in safety and health at work, since it affects health workers more frequently than the general population as a result of exposure to occupational risk factors and the lack of implementation of the measures of control of tuberculosis infection [1,2]. to determine the relationship of sociodemographic, occupational, knowledge and practices on tuberculosis with the proper use of the N95 respirator, in health workers of a specialized health service unit, 2020 - 2021. Methodological: quantitative type study and cross-design. A simple random sample stratified by occupation and service was performed. Participants virtually applied a data collection tool with sociodemographic, occupational, knowledge and practices variables on tuberculosis, as well as a tool with the steps to use the N95 respirator (0,001). The ethical aspects were safeguarded. 250 healthcare workers participated. The educational levels of university and post-gradual (P<0.001), the occupations of nurse, specialist physician, general physician and bacteriology (P0,001), as well as the services of hospital (0,001), as the intensil and intensil (0,001). Significantly with the proper use of the respirator N95. It is necessary that health institutions guarantee the permanent supply of respirators N95 to health workers in the care of patients with tuberculosis and develop constant educational processes on the measures to control tuberculosis infection, especially in the proper use of respirators N95
