# SMIELD TECHNOLOGY APPLICATION IN THE RED TYLAPIA INTENSIVE CULTIVE AND WHITE CACHAMA.

This project is part of the new trends in aquaculture production, symbiotic technology, promises great benefits in the production of aquatic species of zootechnical interest for human consumption. This technology based on the fermentation of by-products obtained from the processing of low-cost raw materials such as cereals, legumes and grasses that contain important sources of carbon, that when integrated into the aquatic system and assimilated by probiotic bacteria, substantially improves the chemical parameters suitable to produce excellent quality animal protein, decreasing the rate of diseases and mortality, positively shooting the zootechnical parameters, increasing the weight gain, obtaining lower values of the food conversion factor (FA), higher seed density and decrease in the production cycle. The fermentation of the obtained by-products (rice flour, soy cake and melaza) is carried out by a certain type of specific bacteria acidic acid, which degrade these by-products and generate fatty and sugar-reducing acids important for the development of aquatic organisms, at the same time that serve as a source of food for the growth of zooplankton and the same bacteria, which find a rich means to achieve the productive substances that are also able to be assimilated
