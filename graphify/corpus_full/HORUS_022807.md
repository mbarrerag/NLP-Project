# Inventory and diagnosis of infrastructure for the treatment of domestic waste water in the southern municipalities of the Huila department.

Inventing and diagnosing the infrastructure for the treatment of domestic waste water in the municipalities of the south of the department of Huila In this work, activities were advanced to obtain information that allows to prepare an inventory and a real diagnosis (evaluation) of the domestic waste water treatment facilities (municipal) existing in the municipalities of the south of the department of Huila. In the development of this one it was possible to find that only five (5) of the nine (9) municipalities of the study area have a plant of treatment of waste water – PTAR but one (1) of them is out of operation. Coincidenceally, the latter corresponds to the municipality with the greater population in the region (66%). In terms of coverage the four (4) operating facilities would be treating 13.5% of the population served, which means that 86.5% of the generated dumping would be discharged, without prior treatment, to the bodies of water receiving, impacting negatively the quality of the water resource in those sources. The final receiver is the Magdalena river and these municipalities are the first contributors of the polluting load that receives the same related to the region of the area of the country's geography. In addition, a subutilization of the physical and installed capacity was found
