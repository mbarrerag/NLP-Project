# Constructive viability of ECOPANEL in Bahareque with scale model analysis

Habitat Sin Fronteras, a foundation led by the graduate of the EAU Mauricio Castaño Giraldo, has extensive experience in the development of structures with guadua and research in this field. Together with this foundation and the research seedling TACSO, the aim is to carry out the manufacture of a prototype of a modular of 18.00m2 implementing an ECOPANEL system based on the construction system of the Bahareque. To approach the constructive viability of this, tests of prototypes will be carried out on scale, manufacturing and assembly of prototype 1:1 scale and testing materials. Out of all the processes will be carried out logbooks to document the research and with these inputs, in addition to the final report to present technical and manual files of installation of the prototype. For the University this pioneering research project will allow the training of students in ancestral systems of construction relevant to the study area, as well as revitalization and adaptation of these knowledge to the current needs with technological development, in addition to supporting sustainable projects of entrepreneurship of graduates, which seek to mitigate climate change
