# The approach of formulating and solving problems in the development of general processes of mathematical activity, in students of seventh grade.

The present work aims to contribute to the strengthening of processes associated with mathematical activity in students of the seventh grade through the design and implementation of learning activities that contemplate problem solving as a didactic mediating strategy of the process. It is a work with qualitative approach of descriptive scope, which develops in three phases: diagnostic phase, where an identification of the initial state of the students is made; intervention phase, in which work is done with the proposed learning activities and validation phase, where a verification of the level of appropriation of mathematical processes is carried out. Among the results obtained the following advances are highlighted: with regard to the resolution and approach of problems, the strengthening of skills for decision-making, understanding of concepts, establishing relationships and analysis of information available. In relation to reasoning, to give account of the reason for the process that they follow to reach the objective, ease to justify with clear and convincing arguments the route that follow to establish the model, responding consciously and logically to what is requested. As regards the communication of ideas, to establish proposals of ways to represent ideas through images and concrete materials.
