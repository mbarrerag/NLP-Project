# Ritual practices and daily life. Expressions of collective agency through the construction of symbols in the local community of Nueva Esperanza, 6th century BC to 15th century AD.

The aim of this work was to explore the relationship between the construction of symbols and expressions of collective agency in the domestic groups of the local community of Nueva Esperanza during the Herrera periods (600 BC to 200 AD), Muisca Temprano (200 AD to 1000 AD) and Muisca Tardío (1000 AD to 1600 AD). To this end, I assessed the spatial distribution and characteristics of architecture, tombs, deposits, ceramics and bony deer remains, as routes of approach to a wide range of ritual practices and daily life. From spatial statistics analysis I found that there were four long-term groupings and some of them used different material symbols to express sense of belonging to a group. The results of this research show that to visualize the human experience of actors and social groups of the past it is necessary to contemplate several scales and various lines of evidence. .
