# Intellectual structure of academic production on “research in administration”

The intellectual structure of academic production encompasses the documents, authors and bibliographic relations most representative in a field of knowledge. In agreement, this work examines the academic production on “research in administration” to determine its intellectual structure, themes and trends in the development of knowledge. To achieve this, the main bibliometric characteristics of academic production on “research in administration” are described, the main themes addressed in the hegemonic, structural and current documents of this field are analyzed and their attendance is analyzed against the four axes of knowledge in administration presented by Salazar, Gentilin and Miranda (2016): Administrative Theory (TA), Organizational Theory (TO), Organizational Studies (EO) and, Research and Education in Administration and Organizations (I&E). As a methodology, bibliometric analysis is used using the analogy TOS (Tree of Science) and Gephi software, identifying an intellectual structure composed of knowledge, Management, Management of Business, 9) and Business Management (in the area of the area, its respective themes and its distribution among the 16 themes identified: 1) International Business, 2) Business Management, 3) Academic Production in Administration, 4) Management of the Supply Chain, 5 Methods of Research in Administration, 9) and 9) on "management research" to determine its intellectual structure, topics and trends in the development of knowledge.
