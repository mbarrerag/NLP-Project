# Technical-economic feasibility of generating electricity from photovoltaic solar energy on the island of San Andrés

The generation of electricity on the island of San Andres is made from fossil fuels with diesel generation units, affecting the sustainability of electricity supply. This island is projected in the future as a self-sustaining region incorporating renewable energy sources, that is why by using computational tools such as Neplan, Homer Pro and MATLAB, cases of study are developed for the approach of diversification of the energy matrix of the island of San Andrés, through the implementation of SSFV considering the technical restrictions of the current electricity network. It is found that penetration of the SSFV can be up to 28 MW. The saving of a single year in fuel and subsidies, added to the monetization of carbon bonds per tons of CO2 not emitted, allows the full financing of departmental policies oriented to the entire vulnerable population of the Department Archipelago for the four year 2020 – 2023. The return of the investment with the result of the proposed optimization model is 3.18 years, which determines its viability
