# Towards a Bogotá region: proposal for regional delimitation based on spatial analysis

In the context of accelerated urbanization and globalization, the precise delimitation of the city-region of Bogota is set as a crucial challenge for effective territorial planning. This study uses advanced techniques of Spatial Data Exploration Analysis (ESDA) and the method of Analytic Hierarchies (JA) to identify functional interaction patterns, prioritizing the analysis of flows between municipalities and capital. This approach, which favours dynamic variables over statics, allows the capture of the complexity of territorial interrelations and overcome the limitations of traditional departmental administrative boundaries. The results obtained constitute a city-region of 79 municipalities, with a central nucleus that integrates Bogota and its first contiguity rings (10 municipalities). This proposal for spatial delimitation, based on functional and objective criteria, transcends the current boundaries of the Metropolitan Region of Bogotá-Cundinamarca, providing a more granular representation of the interdependence and dynamicalities that characterize the region. .
