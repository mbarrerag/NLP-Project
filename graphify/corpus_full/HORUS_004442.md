# Reduction of the distances of electrical insulation in the air in conventional electrical substations in Alto Voltage in the range of 115 kV at the height of the city of Bogotá and surroundings

This paper provides the results of experiments carried out by several researchers and from practical experiences in the operation of electrical power systems, the aim is to support recommendations on changes in the application of international technical standards. In particular, it addresses the aspect of the application of electrical engineering in the specific field of electrical insulation, which is concerned, among others, with the study of safety in the light of the consequences of the approaches between energyed parts to electrical potentials, on the one hand, and of the living beings and objects in general connected to the earth potential. This aspect constitutes the fundamental basis for establishing parameters that guarantee the quality and safety of the electrical system and the safety of living beings, of the installations of electrical power systems and of electrical services at the current level. . This document presents the results of experiences performed by other researchers, to support and suggest changes in the electrical innovation Standard used as an alternative alternative
