# “The healthy popular organization” Pilot process of community music therapy for collective rethinking of the concept of masculinity, based on the work of emotional expression, of the group of men belonging to the Maipú Health Committee.

The present degree work was carried out in Santiago de Chile, with a group of men between 35 and 50 years belonging to the Maipú Health Committee, social organization of residents who struggle for a dignified health. It aimed to facilitate the collective rethinking of the concept of masculinity based on the work of the emotional expression. The methodological design was based on qualitative research, mainly from research-action, making use of tools of ethnography and the theory based with the technological tool Atlas.ti8 and others like the participant observation, interviews, matrices and triangulation of the data, within a continuous process of reflection, planning, action, rigorous follow-up and constant analysis. The musicotherapeutic process was carried out from a community approach, through the 4 main experiences of the musicotherapy (improvisation, re-creation, composition and receptivity), between May 2022 and August 2023 and was divided into four stages: approach to the community, implementation, closure and follow-up.
