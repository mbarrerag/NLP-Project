# SELF-MATIZED DETECTION OF INDUSTRIAL CHIMENEAS TO PUT IMAGES WITH DRONES

The detection and location of fixed sources of pollution is of the utmost importance in air quality management, as it represents a significant advance in environmental governance, especially in developing countries, where there is informality and outdated statistics that hinder the control and monitoring of polluting discharges to air by industrial source. This research proposes a solution according to adverse conditions that make it difficult to advance in activities of identification and location of the sources of air, which will allow to analyze the real impact on air quality of different industrial activities. This proposal is based on the recognition and georeferencing of industrial chimneys using high resolution images through techniques of intelligence and artificial vision in an automated way. It aims to determine the efficiency and applicability of these technologies, in such a way that it allows to construct a tool for the detection of industrial chimneys that can support the environmental authorities responsible for the processes of control and monitoring emissions by fixed industrial sources in the country
