# IMPLEMENTATION OF GEOMATICS IN THE OBTAINATION OF PARAMETERS FOR TRANSIT ANALYSIS.

Geomatics is a discipline that encompasses Geosciences with the integration and application of information and communication technologies (ICT). This sum of Geosciences + ICT makes possible the capture, processing, analysis, interpretation, storage, modelling, application and dissemination of digital geospatial or localized information, applicable in the fields of engineering, territory and society (Geomatics, 2022).
