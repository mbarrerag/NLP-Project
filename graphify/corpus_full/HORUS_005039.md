# Quantum error correction via quantum convolutional neural networks

As a subclass of quantum variable algorithms (VQAs), the quantum quantum convolutional neural network (QCNN) has emerged as an efficient algorithm of quantum error correction (QEC) and a complete quantum error correction code. Through the quantum-classical hybrid optimization of a QCNN architecture for a particular error model, it is possible to "train" a neural network to reduce logical error rates for specific error models. Entering the era of noise-based intermediate-scale quantum technology (NISQ), effective quantum error correction is necessary for precise quantum computing with noisy neural network (QCNN), and the QQAs can promote reliable quantum computing in the short term and on the intermediate scale. .
