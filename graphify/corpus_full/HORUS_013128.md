# Transition of the organizations of the social sector of the Coffee Area of Colombia towards sustainability, approximation from dynamic capacities and circular economy

This research seeks to integrate into the discourse of the circular economy approach to a crucial actor for sustainable development as is the social sector or non-profit sector, considering itself an important means that promotes the participation of citizens in associative activities and in civic, social and environmental issues to offer powerfully positive solutions to the political and social life of nations.However, on this sector is found voids in the literature of the circular economy to a large extent by the neglect of the biodiverse ecosystem of the organizations that integrate it; focusing overwhelmingly on the state and business sector, so that the social sector organizations also known as non-profit entities (ESAL), have presented challenges to integrate into circular trade. In this sense, the circularity approach in relation to the theoretical vision of resources, competences and dynamic capacities, as a way to improve their management for a transition to sustainability, perspectives that are framed as a solution for them to reduce to the maximum use of their resources and surpluses through a restorative or regenerative system, and also as a way to take an innovative path towards the sustainability in terms of their eco-efficiency and efficiency. The contribution of the research is aimed at complementing the theoretical corpus on the conceptual relationship between the vision of the theory of resources, competences and dynamic capacities with the approach of the circular economy, and therefore it is proposed guidelines of a sustainable management system from the theoretical model validated in the development of dynamic capacities for the transition to a circulating economy of the EES qualified in the TEN . For this reason, a theoretical model is proposed between the vision of resources, competition and dynamic capacities with the principles of the circular economy and its contribution is evaluated in terms of the development of the meritorious activity of the NPO, but those that, in the case of Colombia, are qualified in the Special Tax Regime (STR) and are located in the geographic area of the Coffee Axis. For the validity of the model, a sample of 180 entities is empirically evaluated and measured by means of the Partial Least Squares Structural Equations Modeling economically evaluated (PLS-SEM), approach, and therefore, guidelines for a sustainable management system are proposed from the theoretical model validated in the development of dynamic capacities for the transition to a circulating economy of the NPO qualified in the STR
