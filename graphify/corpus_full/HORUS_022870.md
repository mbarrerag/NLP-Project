# PHOTOCATICAL DECOMPOSITION OF NURSE CIA in WATER USED MATERIALS SINTED TO BE DEFINED FROM ILMENITA

In this work, the synthesis of iron-doped anatase (Fe-TiO2) was performed, starting from an ilmenite from mining residues of gold alluvial extraction in Antioquia. The synthesis method used was a modification to the sulfate process. The variation in the synthesis parameters allowed the obtaining of several solids, which were evaluated in the photocatalytic decomposition of cyanide dissolved in water under different pH conditions and catalyst charge. The cyanide quantification was performed by argentometric titration. The photocatalytic reactions were also developed in a semi-temperatured glass batt (septalized (23°C), open to the atmosphere, with a coolant jacket and a UVC lamp of 13 W. The experiments were performed by triplicate. The optimal conditions for the removal of cyanide were pH 10 and 1.0 g L-1, with the solid called S4, reaching an 82% decomposition (18% of residual cyanide) in 4 hours of reaction.
