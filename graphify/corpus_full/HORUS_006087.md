# LATTICE PATHS IN CORRIDORS AND CYCLIC CORRIDORS

In this paper we use discrete Fourier transform and generating functions to count families of paths of a given length in a corridor. For example, we count Motzkin paths, colored Motzkin paths, Dyck paths, and Schröder paths
