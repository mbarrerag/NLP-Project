# A notable Colombian species of Prioninae (Coleoptera: Cerambycidae: Prioninae): description of the male of Calocomus kreuchelyi Buquet

The genus Calocomus Audinet-Serville, 1832 is a South American group that is currently composed of five species. Of the known species, only C. kreuchelyi Buquet, 1840 is known only by one of the sexes, the females. In this work, the unknown male of Calocomus kreuchelyi Buquet, 1840 is described for the first time, including its terminalia and the species is reported for the first time for the department of Casanare. In addition, a map of the geographical distribution of C. kreuchelyi and an updated key of the species of Calocumus is provided. The validity of the “kreuchelyi” spelling is discussed
