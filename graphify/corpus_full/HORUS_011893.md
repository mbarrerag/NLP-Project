# LINKS BETWEEN STRATEGY AND INNOVATION IN THE FIELD OF ORGANIZATIONS*

It is common to find the term strategy by referring to a large number of issues and referring to issues pertaining to the most varied areas of organizational interests. The strategic definition sets out the framework and purposes that define the fate of the organizations and the courses of action that they must follow in order to achieve them. Likewise, the changing conditions of the environment have generated the need to incorporate ideas of uncertainty and adaptation into the study of the organizations, which has resulted in the demand made to them to innovate, even in the instruments used to characterize their situation and determine the paths to be followed
