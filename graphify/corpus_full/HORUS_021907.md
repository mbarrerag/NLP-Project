# Application of a simulation model to evaluate the diffusion of electric taxis in Colombia.

The growing concern about climate change, the characteristics of the passenger transport sector and its evolution, have made this sector relevant in the analysis of strategies for the diffusion of clean technologies. Although this problem has been studied by the academic community, little is known about the processes of diffusion of these technologies in developing countries such as Colombia. This work presents the use of an agent-based simulation model for the case of the diffusion of electric taxis in Colombia. The methodology used consists of: Definition of the problem, Parametrization of the simulation model, Analysis of results, and Evaluation of strategies. The development of this methodology allows understanding and comparison of the results obtained for the different strategies evaluated in this work. The results of this research are oriented to the development of new knowledge, to a greater understanding of the phenomenon of diffusion of electric taxis and to the application of the simulation model based on agents in the case of the Colombian market
