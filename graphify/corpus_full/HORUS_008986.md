# PAPAYA DRYING KINETICS BY DIFFERENT METHODS

Drying is one of the most traditional methods for preserving products biological.Its principle is to reduce product moisture to levels suitable for storage.The study of drying kinetics is essential for adequate control and management of the process.Therefore, this work aimed to evaluate the drying kinetics of papaya subjected to different drying methods.A refractance window dryer (RW) was used, with 70, 80 and 90°C, and a convective dryer (C), at 50, 60 and 70°C and 1.5 m.s-1.The drying rates of each test were analyzed, for subsequent mathematical regression of the drying curves using the Fick and Page models.According to the results, the rates decreased as a function of time and followed the increase in drying temperature, with the RW90 treatment showing the highest rates and C50 the lowest.The two models presented good fits, according to their regression metrics.The effective diffusivities Def of the tests followed the increase in temperature, with RW90 presenting a value of 6.183E-10 m2.s-1, while C50 was the lowest, with a value of 2.651E-10 m2.s-1
