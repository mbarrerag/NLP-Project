# TRANSFER OF KNOWLEDGE IN THE VALLEY OF THE CAUCA. EXPERIENCES, NETWORKS AND ARTICLES

From the project on Strengthening the System of Competitiveness, Science, Technology and Innovation (C&CTI) of Valle del Cauca: Towards a Knowledge Economy, a project funded by resources of the General System of Regalías, formulated by the Network of Universities for Innovation of Valle del Cauca - rupIV, and supported by the Government of Valle del Cauca (Rupiv, 2017), the present document was developed from component 3: Network of dissemination and transfer of knowledge that aims to explore alternatives of formation of scientific technological and academic networks among researchers and research groups with the company, the State and civil society, in order to favor the processes of diffusion and transfer of knowledge among the actors of the C&CTI System of Valle del Cauca and to strengthen the relationship academia, enterprise, State and civil society (p.126), built from product 3.2 Alternatives of diffusion and exchange of knowledge between researchers and research groups with different propellers (p.128), which allowed to implement the objectives and indicators planned within the framework of the project of Un Valle del Sabimiento
