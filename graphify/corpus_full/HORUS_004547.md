# “The Salvation of the Fatherland”: The Patriotic Political Language in Cundinamarca in the War and Battle of St Victorine (1808-1813)

“Wednesday 6 [January]. Good day. There were no parties in Egypt, because of the war.” The testimony recorded by José María Caballero in his diary presents in a few words the consequences of a civil war that reconfigured the social, political, economic and daily practices in Santafé at the beginning of the second decade of the nineteenth century. The confrontation between two political communities born of the New Granada was unthinkable years ago, therefore “The defense of the homeland”: The patriotic political language in Cundinamarca in the midst of the war and battle of San Victorino (1808-1813) analyzes the political transformations of the imprisonment of Fernando VII and the problems of establishing a new order in the New Granada associated with a homeland. What country did you have? The power vacuum, the retroversion of sovereignty in the peoples, the territorial fragmentation and the proclamation of territorial autonomys constituted a new time in the New Granada. The construction of a new order constituted the expectations of the political actors in order to advocate for “salty of the homeland”, which was the fundamental concept of the republican political language.
