# Synthesis and characterization of Fe/Ni oxyhydroxide-based electrocatalysts, incorporating Nb or Co, for efficient production of green hydrogen

In this project it is proposed to obtain materials with high electrocatalytic activity, based on transition metals, for the efficient generation of green hydrogen by electrolysis of water using renewable energies. The work will focus on the synthesis of Fe/Ni oxyhydroxides on nickel foam, incorporating other transition metals such as Nb or Co, for the obtaining of economic materials, with improved catalytic activity and by simple procedures. This will allow to replace the scarce and expensive noble metals in the production of electrocatalysts, at a lower price, for the efficient conversion of electrical energy into chemical energy through the obtaining of hydrogen. Thus, it is intended to provide solutions for the storage and proper management of energy from renewable sources such as wind or sun, helping to reduce the increasing problems of energy demand and global environmental pollution. The activities of this project will be carried out in the research laboratory in fuels and energy (LICE), in stages ranging from the conditioning of the nickel substrate, obtaining reference materials formed by bimetallic oxyhydroxides of Fe/Ni, obtaining of electro-hydroxides materials in the Femical and Feacry analysis
