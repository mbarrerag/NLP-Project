# THE STATUS IN THE LAW OF COLOMBIAN FAMILY: TWO LEGAL PRONOUNCES THAT ARE TRANSFORMING THE LAW

The regulation of family relations in Colombia in recent decades has been altered by numerous and frequent cases of jurisprudence of the High Courts, in response to the State’s commitment to constitutionalize family law. It has been a slow process but the important thing is that it has been profound, because it has touched the roots of the most classic institutions of family law. The Constitutional Court has been initiated and continues to lead this process, however, it has been joined by the Council of State and, more importantly, the Supreme Court of Justice. This latter Court, which has been characterized by being the guardian of the most conservative jurisprudential lines of the clási- cas institutions of family law, we see it now as a very advanced judicial corporation that has decided to update that old, retardary and patriarchal family law, the one that the legislator wrote focused on the needs of the fathers, as laid out in the Civil Code and whose updating and modification has always met with great opposition by some civilist authors
