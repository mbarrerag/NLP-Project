# Implementation of a butterfly garden to teach, conserve and encourage ecotourism in the Peñas Blancas ecopark, Quindío, Colombia.

A butterfly garden was established in the Ecopark Peñas Blancas department of Quindío, located in the municipality of Calarcá during the months of April to June 2022. Observations and monitoring recorded the sighting of 178 adult individuals of butterflies of the superfamily (Papilionoidea), which corresponded to five families, 18 subfamily 47 genera, 52 species and four morphospecies, as well as five families of nutritive and nec-price plants in the area. Based on this information, 290 plants were planted in different states of development in random sites in the Ecopark, to ensure their proliferation, converting several areas into natural nurseries. Participatory monitoring was carried out with the community to postulate and enhance the most emblematic butterfly species in the area. Finally, five pieces of information were designed and exposed to the public, which played an important role in the visitor experience, generating an eye-catching message related to the world of butterflies
