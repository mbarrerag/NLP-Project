# Perceptions about the risk of using smartphones during anesthesia practice in surgery rooms, in specialists and graduate students in Anesthesiology at the National University Hospital of Colombia.

Recently, we have witnessed a growth and advancement of mobile technologies and the internet, it must be emphasized the rapid evolution of smartphones or smartphones. In the field of anesthesiology these devices allow communication between the different members of the team, the acquisition of knowledge, the implementation of telemedicine and/or remote monitoring, among others. However, it has been suggested that these devices can represent a threat to the safety of the patient, since they are a source of distraction during cognitive tasks, being able to provoke adverse events. Globally, some studies have focused mainly on the benefits of using smartphones in the education and training of health professionals, however, there is a gap in the research about perceptions of the risk represented by the use of smart phones during clinical practice. Describe the perceptions about the risk involved in the use of smart phones during the anesthetic practice in surgery rooms, in specialists and graduate students in Anesthesiology of the National University Hospital of Colombia.
