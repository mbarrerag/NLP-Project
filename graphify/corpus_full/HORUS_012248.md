# Utility of diagnostic scales in acute appendicitis adjusted to the normal values of its variables according to age in paediatric population.

The use of diagnostic scales in acute appendicitis adjusted to the normal values of its variables according to age in pediatric population Acute appendicitis is the most common surgical pathology in the pediatric population. The diagnosis of this disease could become a challenge for the emergency physician; therefore, the use of diagnostic scales that have in common the use of inflammatory markers such as leukocytosis and neutropophilia has been proposed. However, the normal ranges for these variables change with the patient's age, variable that is not considered in the scales currently used and that could represent significant changes in the management of these patients. The aim is to evaluate the diagnostic usefulness of the scales when adjusting leukocytosis and neutropy to the normal values according to the patient's age. A cohort study was performed between December 2021 and March 2022 in the HOMI Foundation. The patients included were those evaluated by pediatric surgery with suspected acute appendicitis.
