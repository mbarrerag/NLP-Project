# Design of a circular economy strategy in solid waste management for the municipality of Cachipay (Cundinamarca)

The case study of the municipality of Cachipay (Cundinamarca) shows that in Colombia, the territories different from the big cities face increasing challenges in the management of their solid waste at urban level and especially in rural communities. Although from 2019 in Colombia there exists the National Strategy for Circular Economy (ENEC) as part of the set of policies for the management of the waste generated by the population, at municipal level the potential that collaborative governance can offer between the different actors of a specific geographical space for the creation, promotion and joint execution of circular economy initiatives that are sustainable and reflect national policy is wasted. As a step forward to the implementation of practices of circular economy in a territory, a preliminary inquiry is proposed on beliefs, cultural values and knowledge about the use of resources and management of waste by the inhabitants of the territory, which serves as a basis for the promotion of changes of behavior in people and, starting from the literature on national and international experiences as well as from the information of the municipality, a set of actions is built for the deployment of the ENEC for solid waste in the municipality of Cachipay.
