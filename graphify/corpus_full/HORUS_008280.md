# Comparison of Deep Learning algorithms for forecasts in Cryptocurrencies prices

Abstract Due to the high attractiveness of cryptocurrencies, investors and researchers have paid more attention to the forecasting of their prices. With the methodological development of Deep Learning, the forecast of cryptocurrencies has been more important in recent years. In this article, four models of Deep Learning are evaluated: RNN, LSTM, GRU and CNN-LSTM with the aim of evaluating the performance in the daily closing price forecast of the two most important cryptocurrencies: Bitcoin and Ethereum. Performance analysis metrics such as MAE, RMSE, MSE and MAPE were used and as adjustment metrics, the R2. Each Deep Learning model was optimized from a set of hyperparameters and for different time windows. Experimental results showed that the RNN algorithm had a higher performance in the prediction of the price of Bitcoin and the LSTM algorithm at the price of Ethereum. Even, both methods presented better performance with two evaluated literature models.
