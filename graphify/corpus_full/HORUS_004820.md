# OVERVIEW OF LIFE. DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES FOR LIFE

The artistic creative processes with biological computers, understood as undetermined forms of life or bio-di-gypal symbiotic organisms, in turn emerge from- and at the same time shape the bio-centric turn in the development of these digital technologies. Biological computing, developed with living organisms or molecules derived from the biological, is an emerging medium that requires another understanding of our- after relations with biological entities included in the processing and storage of information, and with biocomputers themselves as living organisms. Together with them, artistic creation, or even more am- plio, the shared aesthetic experience of metabolization, generates a new form of life that dilutes many taxonomic edges and that, welcoming the uncontrollable nature of the living, becomes evidence of biocentric digital technologies
