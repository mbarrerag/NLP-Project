# EFFECT OF A PRIMARY ATTENTION PROGRAMME IN FAMILY FUNCTIONALITY DIRIGIDED TO THE LEADERS OF THE COLOMBIAN PACIFIC

The statistically significant effect of the implementation of a primary care program on the family functionality of leaders of the Colombian Pacific in the municipality of Guapi, Cauca, is presented. Through it, the development of skills and capacities was promoted, aimed at improving their life project and the generation of resources in order to enable them to meet their basic needs, the main motivation for achieving stability translated into happiness and well-being of their children
