# Electromagnetic shielding design for a wireless power transmission system of electric vehicles

In this research work, a bibliographic review was carried out on the technologies available for wireless energy transfer in electric vehicles. Based on this review, a methodology was established for the design of a wireless energy transfer system that focuses mainly on the design of the electromagnetic shielding system by using finite element simulation software. As a result of the evaluation of different combinations of coil dimensions and the shielding system, an optimal design was identified that maximized the coefficient of coupling between coils, reduced total size and ensured compliance with the electromagnetic safety standards. After making modifications to the screening system, different materials and forms were evaluated to improve its efficiency, which allowed identifying the most appropriate ones to reduce losses obtained by the shielding
