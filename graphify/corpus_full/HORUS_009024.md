# Child abuse: Sit-down horizonte and Odontolog actions

The most frequent physical lesions, related to child abuse, are the craniofacial ones; they can be fractures of bones of the skull, face or neck, contusions, burns, lacerations in face, neck or oral cavity. Because these lesions affect the stomatognatic system, the dentist plays an important role in its detection. It is common to observe in the consultation cases of orofacial trauma of suspicious nature, but are ignored or passed by high, this decreases the rate detection and management of child abuse. The statistics show a low notification of suspicious cases by dentists. Understand the horizon of meaning and the actions of a group of dentists of Bogotá in cases of suspected child abuse.
