# CHARACTERIZATION OF THE ELECTRONIC AND DEMERSAL FAUNA OF THE NATURAL RESERVE CORDILLER SUBMARINE BEATA

The Beata underwater cordillera is located in the north-eastern end of the maritime territory of Colombia, dividing the Caribbean into the basins of Colombia and Venezuela, with depths ranging from 1500 to 4400 m etros. The seamounts like the Cordillera Beata, are characterized by steep slopes with hard and soft substrates, also because they are zones of geographical isolation presenting high degrees of endemism; it is for this reason that it was selected as an area with potential for conservation for biodiversity. The objective of the study, was to characterize the megafauna bentónica and demersal, in order to complete the biological inventories of the area. From the bathymetry previously acquired by the Maritime General Directorate (DIMAR), 14 anomalies of interest were chosen according to its geomorphological characteristics, to be explored using a vehicle of remote operation (R.O.V) with a range of 122 m depth. The seven transects obtained, were analyzed later for the interpretation of photograms and identification of the main groups present and visible with this technology. The results of the study are important contributions to the knowledge and conservation of deep-sea systems in the Colombian Caribbean
