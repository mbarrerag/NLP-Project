# SILICE OBTAINED FROM CANDICE CANDICE AS A PONTIAL ASSISTANCE IN CATALIZER SYNTHESIS

Rice husk (C.A) is a by-product obtained from the industrialization of rice; for each kg of green paddy rice 0.16 -0.25 kg of this residue is produced [1].Considering the production of 2022 in Colombia, of 2,9 Mt 145,907 is generated, 227,980 tons of rice husk [2] should be emphasized. The unique characteristics of rice husk compared to other agricultural residues: high silica content in ash (87-97 %), high porosity and surface area, reactivity associated with silanol groups (hydrogen, isolated, geminal, neighbourhood and terminal) on the surface considered activity sites (anchoring), which makes it a valuable biomass for obtaining efficient catalysts in water-phase oxidation reactions [3]. Additionally, few work is found on the use of C.A or rice husk ash in obtaining heterogenous catalysts for the treatment of water contaminated with recalcitrant substances [4]
