# The structure of the habitat influences the diversity of day butterflies (Lepidoptera: Papilionoidea) in high mountain ecosystems in Colombia

Abstract Introduction: Recent research has shown positive correlations between habitat structure and the animal communities that inhabit them. Generally, these studies evaluate the hypothesis of a greater diversity of animals in complex and heterogeneous habitats, since these can offer a greater number of potential niches unlike those structurally simpler ones.
