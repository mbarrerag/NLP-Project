# Leadership styles and capabilities in telecommuting environments in the administrative and accounting areas of a business holding company in Colombia

The teleworking has gained relevance as a response to the technological changes and demands of the labor market, especially during the pandemic of COVID-19 in Colombia. Despite the return to the face-to-face modality in 2022, the crisis highlighted the importance of effective leadership in telecommuting environments. This study focuses on the relationship between leadership styles and leadership capacities in telecommuting contexts, in this way it seeks to answer the question: What is the relationship between leadership styles and leadership capacities in this modality? To address this issue, virtual surveys were conducted. The results indicate that the transformational leadership is the most predominant, with 65% of the collaborators recognizing this style in their leaders.
