# The quality of the commercial relationship: scale of measure for agribusiness (Colombia)

Summary The commercial relations between agricultural producers and suppliers require to be studied in order to identify the diadic dynamics that occur in the territories between two actors that are closely linked in terms of being able to build competitive advantages from the perspective of the supply chain. Therefore, the purpose of this research is to propose a scale of measurement of the quality of the commercial relationship between suppliers and agricultural producers applicable to the Spanish-speaking contexts. To this end, various operational concepts were selected that are part of the construct of the quality of the commercial relationship, which are framed within the relational marketing paradigm. Based on the theory, new scales were established that were adapted and validated in their content and at the statistical level through a confirmatory factor analysis, which allowed to select the models of measurement that best represent the commercial relational quality construct. These analyses were carried out through structural equations by the partial least square method (SEM-PLS) and with the help of SmartPLS software 3.
