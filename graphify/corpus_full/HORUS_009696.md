# Is it possible to overcome the labor-related pre-career and worker health-care workers?

Various researches have shown that the result of the structural reforms of a privatization nature implan­tized during the eighties and nineties of the twentieth century in the world and with great force in Latin America, which led, among others, to labor removals, social security and health systems, has extended a process of easing and intermediation that has generated a process of precariousness of the work for the staff of the health sector.This labor phenomenon has been expressed by means of temporary hiring methods for multiple activities, including the missionary ones, mainly through figures such as the orders of provision of services, labor contracts, labor, or by associated labor cooperatives, that have led to an occupational precarisation faceted by means of intermediated and unstable hiring, diminished wages, increase of the working hours, increase of the labor burden, lack of protection of the health in the work and to the possibility of organizing or collective bargaining. Such situation in the health sector has led, as a way to compensate the income, to the workers to have several different branches at the same time, what leads to the job load and to the greater possibility of health in the work and to the overall life
