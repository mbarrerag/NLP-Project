# NETWORK CIRCULAR ECONOMY OUTSIDE

The NFUs are categorized as post-consumption waste, these wastes have generated an environmental and social problem worldwide due to their inadequate disposition. For several decades different routes of use or recycling of the NFUs have been proposed, being the processes of mechanical, thermal and chemical recycling some of the most researched. The thermochemical process of pyrolysis of NFU allows to obtain several by-products that can be used in different industries, thus contributing to the circular economy of the tires. The products that are obtained in greater proportion of the process of pyrolysis of NFU are the TPO and the rCB; the performance of each fraction and its quality depend on the type of reactor employed, the conditions of pyrolysis and the mass flow, among other factors.
