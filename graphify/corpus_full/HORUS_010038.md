# CONJUGED MODELS

The models conjugated in Bayesian Statistics focus on quantifying the belief about the occurrence of an event through the concept of subjective probability. Below, a Shiny application is presented that facilitates the study of discrete binomial and Poisson conjugated models, allowing users to interact in two scenarios: simulating data distributions and a priori distributions, either entering parameters or entering real data. This allows to reduce the gap between theory and practice, improving the understanding of key concepts, allowing to analyze how the observed data and a priori distribution affect the subsequent distribution and optimizing the teaching and learning of Bayesian Statistics
