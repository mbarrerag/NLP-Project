# Urban control in Bogotá: historical and legal context of its background in the transition from urban planning to urban planning 1950 - 2020

Urban control in Bogotá: historical and legal context in the transition from urban planning to urban planning 1950 - 2020 Urban control is a component of urban development policy designed to ensure the fulfilment of the objectives of strategic planning in cities. In this work, the main characteristics of the norms and institutions of urban control created in Bogotá between 1950 and 2020 were identified. The methodology used was the documentary analysis of three types of text: research that examines urban control from legal, historical and social perspectives; Colombian and Bogotan legislation around the topic, and secondary sources of social and historiographic studies. The research was divided into two stages: the first one that goes from 1958 to 1978, when Bogotá enters into a process of modernization, and another that begins in 1980 and goes until 2020, which corresponds to the years of incorporation of the neoliberal current in the country and its capital. It was possible to identify that in the first stage of urban planning in Bogotá the institutions and the rules of urban control respond to a centralized model and statistic regulation.
