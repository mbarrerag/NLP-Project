# BRONTERES WITHOUT MURSE AND HEGEMONIES: FINDINGS BETWEEN AMAZON, AMERICA AND EUROPE

The book discusses current trends in border research in the Amazon region, AAmerica and Europe, based on the theoretical, methodological and casuistic discussion of several authors whose works were compiled and edited by the book's editors
