# The project of life in the Missionary Church Rock Strong: pedagogy and education

This study focused on the collection, analysis and colletization of experiences of participants in formation processes in the Roca Fuerte Missionary Church. A qualitative research process was carried out that would provide a firm basis for the research procedures and thus a deep understanding of the phenomenon to be investigated. As a methodological strategy, a systematisation approach was chosen, in order to understand a more in-depth perspective of the experiences experienced by the participants, a series of tools were used for this purpose to collect research data: interview, observation, and interview analysis. The findings were given from the processing and analysis of the data, carried out from the selected methodological perspective.
