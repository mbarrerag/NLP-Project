# Relationship between the motor unit index (MUIX) and manual function in patients with Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (AMIS) in a hospital in Bogotá.

The MUNIX is a claniological hand tool to make a quantitative estimate of motor units. It has been used in the characterization and follow-up of patients with motor neuronal diseases and the association between the MUNIX of hand muscles and functional tests of manual dexterity, ALSFRS-R scale and levels of functional dependence in patients with ALS has not been described in the literature. To establish the relationship between the number of motor units calculated with the MUNIX and the manual function of patients with ALS. Descriptive study, cross-sectional, with prospective data collection. Two functional tests were performed for each muscle (AB, ADM and IDF) not consecutive by the same evaluator following the updated protocol of registry of MUNIX. A patient level according to the scale of the patients was established, progression index, classification of the ALS-MITOS and Ortiz et al. We performed functional tests of manual dexer on the same day of realization. An intraclass correlation coefficient between the intakes of each muscle was established.
