# “We are the Impulse Committee” The role of emotions in the history of the women’s community organization of the Hinche Alto - Hinche Bajo Street Impulse Committee

In this research project I have designed the objective of understanding the role of emotions in the history of the Hinche Alto - Hinche Bajo Impulsation Committee, a community organization of rural women who have worked for the recovery of their territory after having been victims of mass displacement. To fulfill this objective I have drawn a methodological commitment from a qualitative inductive approach of ethnographic cut, from which I initially reconstructed together with the Committee, the different moments that were key in their configuration, understanding that behind them emotional meanings were built. In the exploration of these moments it is emphasized mainly their participation in the Strategy Interlacing and their passage through the psychosocial care program PAPSIVI.
