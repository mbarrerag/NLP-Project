# Estimated water supply for crop planning in a river basin in the Colombian Orinoquía

The variations in yield in agricultural systems for the Orinoquia region associated with extreme precipitation events, make it necessary to study water supply for crops. For this purpose a hydrographic basin was instrumented with equipment for the measurement of soil humidity, flow rates, precipitation and evapotranspiration, and identify physical and chemical properties of the soil that determine water dynamics. The basin is identified as Quenane pipe, located in the foothill of the Colombian eastern mountain. Additionally, the conditions of climatic variability in the water supply were analyzed. With the information generated in the instrumentation, the hydrological model HBV is cashed. The results identify little amount of usable water given in the soil, generating that soil moisture is frequently close to the values of permanent March point. It shows a change in the distribution of rainfalls with a decrease in the number of rainy days per year, especially in the second half, which together with well drained reliefs would favour dry soil conditions, identified as Ustic.
