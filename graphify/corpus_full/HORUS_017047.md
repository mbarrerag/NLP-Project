# Traditional medical therapies in androgenic alopecia.

The alopecia androgenic (AGA) is a multifactorial dermatological condition and is the most common form of alopecia, its incidence increases with age, affects up to 80% of men and 50% of women in the course of their lives. The aim of this study is to carry out a bibliographic review of the therapies of traditional medicines that are currently counted for the treatment of AGA, reviewing the physiopathological mechanisms according to traditional medical systems and compiling their different therapeutic approaches to propose management recommendations. A search was made in databases of SCIENCE DIRECT, PUBMED, MEDLINE, DYNAMED, direct results and references product of cross searches, all in texts that are recognized in the area of medicine. The method used was qualitative analysis, with the aim of searching, identifying, organizing and reporting the traditional therapies registered in the databases with the corresponding mechanism of action diluted, to give recommendations according to the availability in Colombia.
