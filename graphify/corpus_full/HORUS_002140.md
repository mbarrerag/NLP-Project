# Evaluation of excitation modes of wheelsets and track in a commercial metro through dynamic modelling and field measurements

Railway systems have resonant frequencies that may cause damage to the wheel-rail interface and the substructure. In this work, several excitation modes of the wheelsets and the track in a commercial Metro were evaluated by dynamic modelling and field measurements. A multibody dynamic model was developed aimed at identifying the system's characteristic frequencies. The model was built with the system’s design data and the results were compared with acceleration measurements on the track as well as hammer tests on the wheelsets and the track. The model was successfully validated using force distribution at the contact patch and comparison with acceleration measurements. Several representative frequencies were detected with values of 80, 200, 450, and 1280 Hz, which are related to resonance phenomena of the railway system and may generate corrugation in the track
