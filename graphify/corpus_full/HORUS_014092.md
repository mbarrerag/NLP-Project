# Study of the production of dark fermentation biohydrogen using banana pulp (Paradisiacal Musa) and pretreated shell in alkaline medium, assisted by ultrasound

The depletion of fuels and the associated environmental problems, such as global warming, have driven the search for alternative and sustainable sources of energy. Currently, biohydrogen is committed to becoming one of the most viable options due to its high energy efficiency and clean characteristics during combustion (Sriyod et al., 2021; Li et al., 2022). In order to keep biohydrogen production viable, it is necessary to use abundant, low-cost and environmentally friendly raw materials. Under this view, in the last decade bioenergy research from biomass has gained popularity, so that organic waste from industry and agriculture has not only been used to generate bioenergy, but also contributed to bioremediation (Bhatia et al., 2021; Patel et al., 2021; Saravanan et al., 2021).
