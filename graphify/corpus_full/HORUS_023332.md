# USE OF ARCHECILLA AS PRIMA MATERIAL IN ARCHEOLOGY

The earth as a constructive element is one of the most original and powerful expressions of human capacity to create a constructed environment that includes a great variety of structures in prehistory. Due to its availability and durability, it was a material of preferred construction of the human groups that established the first settlements. Archaeological excavations continually prove this indication that clay has accompanied the life of humanity without interruption from the Upper Paleolithic. In fact, clay represents the most important raw material in archaeology to produce objects of artistic and architectural-structural purpose that have had a very wide distribution and history. This study aims to provide an archaeological perspective on the interaction between humans and the earth in the past in order to obtain conclusions on the role of clay in prehistoric architecture. In this study we will examine archaeological constructions that were made with material based on soil, giving examples of prehistoric. It will be presented results of various archaeological research that have studied structural aspects of prehistoric buildings
