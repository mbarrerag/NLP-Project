# Evaluation of three conservation techniques on post-harvest quality of lulo fruits (solanum quitoense lam)

The lulo (Solanum Quitoense Lam) is a tropical fruit whose center of origin has been established in the forests of the Andean region between Peru, Ecuador and Colombia. The fruit of lulo is desired by the national market and has potential of export for its characteristics organoleptic and benefits to health. Due to its process of maturation, being considered a climateric fruit, it presents a reduced useful life time, which generates significant losses for producers and marketers either for the use of the product in fresh or as an input for other agro-industrial processes. The objective of this research was to evaluate the effect of the application of low conservation temperatures, 1-Methylcyclopropene (1-MCP) and modified atmospheres (AM) on the post-harvest quality of the fruit of lulo (Solanum quitoense Lam).
