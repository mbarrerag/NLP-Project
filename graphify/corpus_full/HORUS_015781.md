# WORKING WITH THE COMMUNITY FROM RESEARCH ACTION PARTICIPATION

This research identified the risk situations for emergencies and disasters, based on the experiences and perceptions of the Tausa community, through analysis and reflection on the risks and possible emergencies that the community lives in, in order to generate knowledge and the capacity to respond to the presence of these events
