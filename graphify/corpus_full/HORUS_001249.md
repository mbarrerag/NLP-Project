# PERCEPTION OF TRAINING IN PEDIATERIC ANESTEIOLOGICAL ANATESIOLOGY IN COLOMBIA: QUALITATIVE STUDY

The development of pediatric anesthesiology in the world recognizes the clinical challenges of the infant population, as well as their differences with the adult, increasing access to training opportunities. In Colombia, it has not been possible to recognize training in this discipline, since it is considered not relevant and limiting the exercise of anesthesiology. To describe the perception of a group of health professionals related to anesthesia during child surgical care in Bogotá, regarding training in pediatric anesthesiology in Colombia. A descriptive qualitative approach, in which semi-structured interviews were conducted to a group of health professionals related to pediatric anesthesia, selected under the technique of maximum variation search, whose recruitment in the form of snowball was stopped when saturation was found in interviews. A thematic analysis of networks was developed with the support of the software MAXQDA 24.1.0. Three global topics were identified: limitations of the exercise of pediatric anesthesia in Colombia, strengthening of competences in pediatric anesthesiology and strategies to optimize and encourage training in pediatric anesthesia
