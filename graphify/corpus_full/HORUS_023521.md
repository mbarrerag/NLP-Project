# Standardisation of a tissue microarrangement design for the immunohistochemical detection of NTRK gene fusion proteins in a series of solid tumors.

NTRK genes participate in the expression of tyrosine kinase receptors, which participate in the differentiation, development and regulation of the nervous system. Mergers in these genes constitute the main mechanism of oncogenic activation and are found in variable frequencies in various tumours in both paediatric and adult populations. Their detection is relevant since there is currently targeted therapy with selective TRK receptor inhibitors with favorable response. There are multiple ways to identify these mergers, each with advantages and limitations, including immunohistochemistry as a screening tool. This work aims at the realization and standardization of a technique of immunohistochemistry through microarreglos of tissue, in order to detect proteins that could indicate possible mergers of NTRK genes. The marker VENTANA pan-TRK (EPR17341) was evaluated in 93 cases that include gliomas, thyroid carcinomas, adenocarcinomas of colon and lung, squamical carcinoma and cancer secretor of breast, which was distributed in 14 microarregments. The suggested positive control, ccalal appendix, was expressed in all microregulas
