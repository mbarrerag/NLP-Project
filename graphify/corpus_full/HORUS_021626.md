# Learning about water care in primary school students.

In this work, a didactic strategy was designed and applied regarding the conservation and good use of water, in order to solve the problem presented by the waste this vital resource, by the students of the Retiro de la sede derejo de la institución educativo Real Campestre La Sagrada Familia de Fresno Tolima. It was carried out with a group of 11 students from the first to fifth grades of primary school under the pedagogical model Escuela Nueva. The methodology of this work was participatory action with a mixed approach that allowed to know in a qualitative and quantitative way, where the student was leaving and with what preestablished ideas came, and from there the work was organized, taking into account that he was the protagonist of his own learning, not only in the school, but that, at the same time, it was multiplier in his home of these learnings.
