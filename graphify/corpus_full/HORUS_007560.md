# Landscape structure and pollinating insects in the agroecosystems of Sibaté-Cundinamarca

The present proposal aims to estimate relations between floral visiting insects (pollinators) and landscape structure in nine strawberry crops located in three ecological zones of Sibaté, Cundinamarca. The information obtained will strengthen the specific objectives 1, 2 and 3 of Alexander García’s thesis, a doctoral candidate attached to the Agroecology program of the Faculty of Agrarian Sciences in Bogotá. It has signed informed consent for the visit and inspection of lands that allow the sampling and observation of insects in plants with strawberry crops with a view to recognizing their biodiversity by study area. Regional and each crop will be obtained maps that allow together with pollinators biodiversity, estimating relationships of connectivity and functionality of ecosystem services that can benefit strawberry crops. This proposal also contributes to the comprehensive formation of the student from the strengthening of inter-institutional alliances, of continuous improvement of verbal and written communication; systemic and transdisciplinary research in its disciplinary emphasis
