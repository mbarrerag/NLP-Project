# Sustainability studies of biomass recovery stages and processes from products and by-products from the agricultural and fish sectors

The main objective of the project is to strengthen collaboration between the group of New Food Processing Technologies of the University of Zaragoza (UZ) and the Food-Fruit Group of the National University of Colombia-Manizales (UN), focusing on sustainability and environmental impact management in the food industry of Spain and Colombia. Specific objectives include the comparative assessment of the environmental and sustainability implications of freezing and the PEAV technique to produce fish meat without parasites, propose sustainable alternatives for the PEAV technique, implement similar exercises to increase the value of biomass in the UN processes chain (biorefinery of rice, dairy industry and dehydration of fruits), and examine regulations and propose road sheets per company to obtain certifications that inform on the sustainability implications of the techniques used. Both universities, the National University of Colombia Manizales and the University of Zaragoza will participate in the proposal through their institutes, which have solid installed capacity and an equipped physical infrastructure to carry out processes in food and agro-industry. Both universities, the National University of Colombia will be responsible for the development of biomass and the University of Zaragoza, will participate in the project
