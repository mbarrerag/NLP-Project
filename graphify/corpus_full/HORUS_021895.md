# Effectiveness of emergency precautionary measures in relation to the protection action in Colombian jurisprudence: an assessment from the judicial guarantee.

This work develops a doctrinal diachronic review of the concept of precautionary measures, until reaching the dogmatic and practical review of the study of precautionary emergency measures in Colombian legislation, on the occasion of the issuance of Law 1437 of 2011, which would incorporate them for the first time. From the normative review of this tool, the case law positions within the Council of State, endorsed by the Constitutional Court, are analyzed, which led to support the majority the theory of effectiveness of emergency precautionary measures, in contrast to those who defend their ineffectiveness, with which, in passing, discussions arise regarding the exceptional origin of the protection action. Finally, considering the importance of protecting fundamental constitutional rights such as access to the administration of justice and due process, the State Council's files are reviewed to verify the announced benefits of emergency precautionary measures regarding the protection action, to validate in light of that review the ineffectiveness of precautionary measures, supported in other techniques of data collection that corroborate this thesis as interviews, surveys and work with focal group, adhering the author to minority the theses that claim the exceptional origin of the cases (Vate of the protection against the cases)
