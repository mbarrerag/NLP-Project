# Material culture in the habitat: reading and interpretation of the landscape. Puerto Garza case in San Carlos - Antioquia and Tutunedo in Quibdó - Chocó

The thesis Material culture in the habitat: reading and interpretation of the landscape. Case study of Puerto Garza in San Carlos - Antioquia & Tutunendo en Quibdó - Chocó, is research that contributions to a methodological construction and interpretation of the landscape to address the landscape. The first, a theoretical orthological one, deals with material culture in the habitat. We start from a complex approach to habitat based on three issues. The first, a theoretical orthopecal one, deals with the material culture in the habitat. Second, a methodological contribution is the result of the construction of the landscape, the interpretation of the material Puerto of the habitat. And third, the approach of the theoretical perspective with the project and methodological contribution to the project. The third is the application of theoretical concerns with the proposal and methodological contribution, for the case of study of the material culture of the communities of Puerto Garza in San Carlos - Antioquia and Tutunendo in Quibdó - Chocó, where the methodological contribution was consolidated, which recognizes the relationships, daily actions and the approach of reading and interpretation that the researcher and the inhabitants have for the productive trades and practices that they develop in the territory
