# Open Digital Educational Resource for Spanish Teaching to Deaf University Students

A research is presented that contributes to the literacy processes in Spanish as a second language (L2) of deaf students from a Colombian university with the support of digital tools. A collaborative research-action perspective was chosen aimed at promoting collective construction and reflection among deaf and hearing students and professionals. Four deaf students from different undergraduate programs of the university and a team of professionals accompanied by a deaf teacher. The results are: the construction of an open digital educational resource (REDA) of literacy and the creation of a test to evaluate the A1 level of Spanish as L2 of these students. It is concluded that the heterogeneity of the participants allowed to recognize different cultural ways of constructing inclusion and that in digital creation processes a harmonious relationship between technology and classical methods/savers of the humanities must be established. In future, a piloting of the REDA and testing with a wider group of deaf students is needed
