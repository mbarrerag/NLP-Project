# ON THE GROUP COHOMOLOGY AND K-THEORY OF GROUPS ZN ? Z/M

Let $\rho:\mathbb{Z}/m\to GL(n,\Mathbb{Z})$ be a homomorphism, it defines a semidirect product $\Gamma=\mathbb{Z}^n\rtimes\mathbb{Z}/m$, when $m$ is free of squares we provide explicit computations of the group cohomology of $\Gamma$, we also provide a technique to compute the cohomology of the associated toroidal orbifold. For this, we proof that when we tensor with $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$, every $\mathbb{Z}/m$-lattice can be decomposed in a unique way (up to isomorphism) as direct sum of $\mathbb{Z}/m$-lattices with constant isotropy and then use the Lyndon-Hoschild-Serre spectral sequence associated to a certain extension. On the other hand, using the Baum-Connes conjecture we prove that the topological K-theory of the reduced group C*-algebra of $\Gamma$ is torsion free. This generalizes results of Adem-Gomez-Pan-Petrosyan and Davis-Luck-Langer. This is joint work with Luis Jorge Sánchez (UNAM)
