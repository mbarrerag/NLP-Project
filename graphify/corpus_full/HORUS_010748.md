# Design and construction of passenger or cabin fortifications and some examples of high-profile examples in the new GRAND KINGDOM

In the first part of this article, we take stock of the characteristics of the passenger or campaign fortification based on the explanations given by some of the writers of the fortification that published their works in Europe between the sixteenth and eighteenth centuries, highlighting those related to its layout and construction and that undoubtedly, as is later to be shown from the study of particular cases, constituted the conceptual foundations that guided several military engineers in the service of the Spanish Crown in the design of this type of works for the territories of the New Kingdom of Granada
