# ANALYSIS OF THE PERSONNEL IN THE FIELD OF COLOMBIA

The objective of this research is to characterize the personnel of the manufacturing sector, starting with the Annual Manufacturing Survey of 2021 in Colombia. The analysis of the personnel takes into account the type of linkage, position and area of performance, for the national sector and the departments where it is relevant. The results highlight the generation of employment of the sector in manufacturing industry and the participation of women mainly in the area of production as workers or workers. Differences are identified by departments in the average labor costs and expenses per employee, and in labour productivity. The Department of Antioquia is highlighted for its high labour productivity, the superiority of the average costs and expenses per employee, the presence of large companies, the high linkage of permanent staff and the recruitment of professionals, technicians and technologists. It is concluded that the generation of added value is favored by offering better conditions to employees
