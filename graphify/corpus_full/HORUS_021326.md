# HACAL IV - Environmental History of Colombia and Latin America IV

A new cohort of young researchers of the Research Line in Environmental History, which has 17 years of activity, has been formed during the health emergency by COVID19 and the escalation of the global environmental emergency, in a context of post-agreement and recent change of national political relationship, which in turn represent important changes of the Colombian scenario. At this juncture, new interests have been opened to regions as well as to perspectives that were not part of the consolidated agenda of the environmental history in the country. This project wants to accompany national bets among them, but that translate a deep concern to expand the spectrum of understanding of the relationship between human societies and the rest of nature in time. The research to be developed investigates 5 topics: birds as vectors of a different and less terrestrial history of the Pacific at the beginning of the twentieth century; the forests as actors - and not only victims - of an internal armed conflict in Colombia that must also be interpreted in the context of the Cold War in Latin America and the experiences of armed insurgents during the second half of the twentieth century; the relationship with agricultural water, seeking to understand the case of a hydraulic intervention as the district of the most of Coello irrigation (To93th)
