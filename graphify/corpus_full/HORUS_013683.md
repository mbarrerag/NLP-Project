# TRUMPETS, GOLD, MINSTRELS AND DEATH: MUSIC AND THE ARMADA OF PEDRO ARIAS DÁVILA IN CASTILLA DE ORO (EASTERN PANAMA- NORTHWEST COLOMBIA), 1513-31

The armada that sailed from Sanlúcar de Barrameda in April 1514 commanded by Pedro Arias Dávila, was the largest and most richly supplied ever sent to America, as King Fernando el Católico himself proclaimed. However, hunger, social disorder and depopulation quickly en- sued its arrival in the Gulf of Urabá on the border of present-day Colombia and Panama
