# E-learning systems accessible instructional design model for people with hearing impairment

The technology has allowed people to access massively several services among those that highlight education, however, millions of users cannot use these resources satisfactorily because they do not meet the minimum inclusion criteria. In view of this reality, the present Doctoral Thesis proposes an accessible educational design model consisting of five phases. The study was carried out in the Specialized Educational Unit Deaf of Chimborazo, using surveys, observation and interviews as techniques to collect data, were determined and characterized the problems presented by students when using technology. Applying the methodology of creation of the model ADDIE very competitive, the model is generated by the Instructional Design Accessible MDIA and is implemented in Moodle a prototype of the mathematics subject considering three models of design WCAG 2.1, DUA and PACIE, the accessibility of the prototype using specialized tools is 79.25% with OAW and 81% with the qualitative qualification of Satisfactory.
