# Institutional Practices that have guided National Oral Morbidity Studies – Oral Health in Colombia (1965-2014): An Approach from Neoinstitutionalism

Throughout Colombia’s history, four national studies of oral morbidity – oral health have been carried out: the first one between 1965 and 1966 called National Oral Morbidity Research, as part of the Human Resources Study for Health and Medical Education in Colombia; the second one, carried out between 1977 and 1980, was carried out in the framework of the National Health Survey, which included a component of Oral Morbidity; the third one was the National Oral Health Survey or ENSAB III, carried out between 1998 and 1999; and the fourth one, carried out in two phases, the first one of design in 2009 and the second one of implementation implemented between 2013 and 2014, the latter was called the Fourth National Oral Health Survey or ENSAB IV. This research focused on the study of the practices of the health institution in Colombia and the way these studies affect the way of doing the oral morbidity - oral health studies in the country.
