# The Integral Trace Form as a Complete Invariant for Real S n Number Fields

In this paper we show that the integral trace is a complete invariant for degree n, Sn real number fields that satisfy certain ramification bound. Among the fields that our results cover, there are those of square free different ideal; for such fields we find an explicit description of the isometry group of the integral trace
