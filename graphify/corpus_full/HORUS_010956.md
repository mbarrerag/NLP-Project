# EDUCATION IN MILENnium DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES AND SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT OBJECTIVES: AWARDS A DEVELOPMENT PERSPECTIVE

In September 2000, in New York City, 147 heads of State and Government and 42 delegates, for a total of 189 world dignitaries, signed a commitment to make joint efforts to break development imbalances between countries. In that declaration, the leaders stated: “We recognize that, in addition to the responsibilities that we all have towards our societies, it is our collective responsibility to respect and uphold the principles of human dignity, equality and equity at the global level.” As leaders, therefore, we have a duty to fulfil towards all the inhabitants of the planet, especially the most vulnerable, and in particular the children of the world, to whom the future belongs.” (Millennium Declaration, 2000)
