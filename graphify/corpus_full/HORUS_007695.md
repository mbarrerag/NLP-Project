# Technological strengthening of the Biogenesis Research Group for data management

The resources financed by this call will be used to acquire one or two tablets that will ensure the correct and agile data collection for market research and studies of intent carried out continuously by the research group Biogenesis. It is expected to increase the capacities for the taking of large volumes of data supported by technological devices. This data collection of greater efficiency will favor the academic productivity of the group and therefore allow to maintain its classification in A1 in the following calls for measuring groups Minciencias
