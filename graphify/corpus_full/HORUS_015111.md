# Fear of Crime in a Colombian Rural Context: An Exploratory Study in the Southern Province of Tolima

This work aimed to explore the phenomenon of fear of crime in a rural Colombian area, specifically the province of the south of Tolima. Information was collected through a mixed approach; which consisted of the conduct of semi-structured interviews constructed based on the delimitation of fear of the crime of Kessler (2013) and the application of a format that included the following instruments: Sociodemographic questionnaire, General Health Questionnaire of twelve items of Goldberg and collaborators (1997), Test of fear of the crime of Reyes and Molina (2018), Scale of diffuse fear of Ruiz (2007), Scale of concrete fear and its subsequent scale of victimization also elaborated by Ruiz (2007). A total sample of 145 people whose ages ranged between 18 and 64 years old (M = 33.8 / DE = 138). The results indicate that, the inhabitants of urban areas appear to be presenting greater psychological symptomatology and fear of crime compared to the inhabitants of rural areas, however, the latter are those who are affected by the greatest variety of crimes. its subsequent escalation of victimization also elaborated by Ruiz (2007).
