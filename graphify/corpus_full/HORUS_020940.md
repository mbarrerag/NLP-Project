# Study of the diversity of bees without sting (Apidae: Meliponini) and its relationship with the floral resource in the Eco-reserva La Tribuna

The Tropical Dry Forest (BST) is a highly degraded ecosystem and represents for Colombia a strategic interest due to the species that are usually found there, of which there is still a great lack of knowledge, even on ecosystem services from which humans receive a direct benefit, therefore it is important to study the diversity of the biota that inhabits it. The Eco-Reserva La Tribuna, in the department of Huila, is a clear example of this problem, so it is valuable to concentrate efforts in studying a group of organisms that have a bioindicator role, as is the case of pollinizing insects, in this sense the study of interactions between bees and plants is fundamental for the maintenance of communities in ecosystems. Consequently, understanding how these interactions occur in the BST is of vital importance for its conservation. In this work, the study of the bees without aguinón (Hymenoptera: Meliponiini) and its relationship with the floral resource is fundamental for analyzing different coverages, weavest grasses, herbazales.
