The musical pieces have been composed with the intention of exploring a new form of listening and have been written in rhythms of Sanjuanito, Albazo, Pasacalle and Sonsureño, which are the most common and performed in the Carnival of Blacks and Whites of the department of Nariño and have a structure that allows experimentation of effects and a staging focused on an enveloping sound and interaction with the public. This project seeks to be a guide for Euphony interpreters and composers who will investigate how to address extended techniques with the Euphony, also for those interested in composing new music for the instrument and want to deepen their different interpretative possibilities.
