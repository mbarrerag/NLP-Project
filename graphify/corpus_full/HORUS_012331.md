# THE ORLICZ INEQUALITY FOR MULTILINEAR FORMS ON SEQUENCE SPACES.

Orlicz’s inequality states that the norm $ left( ell_{2}, ell_{1} right)$ of the coefficients in a continuous bilinear form over $c_{0} times c_{0}$, is equivalent to the norm of the supreme form. In this inequality, the parameters $ ell_{2}$ and $ ell_{1}$ are great in a certain sense. In this lecture we will present a great and general version of the result of Orlicz, for multilinear shapes about the sequence spaces $c_{0}$ and $ ell_{p}$
