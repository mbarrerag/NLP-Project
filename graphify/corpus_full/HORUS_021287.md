# URAMBA Network. Project: "Treaning stories in the river that brings life: dynamics of creation of value oriented by the associativeness for sustainability" in the artisanal fishing process in the municipality of Guapi-Cauca

In this project, an interdisciplinary group of professors from different headquarters of the National University of Colombia, graduates and students, with different academic and life paths, will work together with an educational institution and community associations of the municipality of Guapi, Cauca, in order to cement a model of value creation in the artisanal fishing process from a complex perspective that starts from the dialogue of academic and community knowledge. This proposal seeks to continue the work based on one of the results of a previous project in Guapi and to focus it from the perspective of the associativeness, so that the community continues to expand this proposal, assuming and recognizing its roles in the model according to its abilities and motivations, as well as enhancing the relationships between different actors of the community and values that have traditionally characterized them. The project seeks to create sustainable dynamics in a network of diverse actors, which from research, extension and university training, the values, knowledge, experiences and practices of people related to artisanal fishing in the municipality of Guapi, encourages the creation of a model of value that allows the understanding of artisanal fishing not only as a commercial activity, but in the real dimension that it has the ability to continue to create a territorial activity
