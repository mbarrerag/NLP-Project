# Ecosystem analysis of innovative entrepreneur within the National University of Colombia at Bogotá headquarters to strengthen their soft skills

The research project takes into consideration two of the themes of the seedbed of the Faculty of Economic Sciences Novus Risk, since it addresses a topic that has taken on relevance in recent years: innovative entrepreneurship with emphasis on the analysis of the characteristics of the innovative entrepreneur in the context of the National University of Colombia during the second semester of 2022. The proposal will be based on a mixed (quantitative/qualitative) non-experimental approach in data collection and analysis, where the target population will be the campus of Bogotá Headquarters at the National University with the application of different methods such as triangulation of experiences with spaces for discussion. For the development of the project there will be a joint articulation with the knowledge of the founding groups of the seedbed GEM+i and the Unit of Entrepreneurship and Innovation. It is expected that the results obtained will be presented to the 26 International Congress of Administrative Sciences of the National Autonomous University of Mexico.
