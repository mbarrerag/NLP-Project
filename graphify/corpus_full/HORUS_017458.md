# ON THE G-DIGRUPO CONCEPT

The structure of the group was proposed as a generalization of the structure of groups whose tangent space is governed by a Loday or Lebniz algebra. In this talk we studied an additional generalization of the group in which we do not demand that the inverses with respect to any given unit bar be necessarily bilateral, this genralization is called g-digroup, see [7], [4], [5] and [6]
