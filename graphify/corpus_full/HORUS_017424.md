# CHAPTER 1: CAPITUNIUM HYDRATIATION PHYSIOLOGY 2: HYDRATIATION RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CHILDREN AND ADULTS

The majority of our bodies is composed of water. Various conditions lead to the modification of the body water balance and, therefore, the concentration of electrolytes, which in turn generates an important modification of the bodily functions. One of the aspects that must be monitored when practicing regular physical activity and sport (amateur or high performance) is the level of body hydration, since when this is in optimal conditions and the level of training is adequate, physiological performance and performance is promoted in the face of the demands that demand the practice of physical activity and sport.During the practice of physical activity and sport, different symptoms associated with dehydration can be experienced, such as fatigue, muscle cramps, dizziness, among others, which are to be translated into deficiencies in the performance and can predispose to the presentation of accidents and sports injuries. Although to perform processes of replacement to water losses in the body, it is a task entrusted to the professional in nutrition and dietetics, on various occasions professionals of the teams of sciences applied to the sport and physical activity, as well as trainers and the same sports, develop these processes without considering the particular conditions involved in the body. for the development of field-based measurement processes, which allow for the execution of hydration levels estimation processes in ways closer to the sport gesture and the environment in which the practice is carried out. This book, although a research text, is thought of all those interested in deepening the topic of hydration, the processes involved in this and the best ways to apply what has been learned in the practice of physical activity and sport. Therefore, we hope that it will be useful for health professionals, coaches, athletes and people who practice physical activity in a regular way, who, in one way or another, are committed to the care of their own health
