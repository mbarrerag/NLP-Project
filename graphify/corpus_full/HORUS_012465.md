# History of the physiology in Colombia

Colombia is considered a megadiverse country (WWF-Colombia, 2017), and hosts immense biological diversity. The country has a large maritime territory, which corresponds to approximately 892.118 km2, almost 45% of the national extension (CCO, 2014), being the only country on the South American continent to have coasts in both oceans, the Pacific and the Atlantic. However, knowledge of Colombia’s marine and coastal ecosystems is still incipient: it is estimated that only 0.51% has been mapped at 1:100,000 scale. According to these studies, the marine ecosystems of the Colombian Caribbean are constituted by calcareous algae on sediments (32.2%), associations of algae (17.9%), seagrasssins (14.9%) and coral reefs (35%). Submerged sandy bottoms, intertidal bottoms and mud bottoms have been identified in the Pacific. Corals and algae associations have coverage below 0.01% (WWF-Colombia, 2017)
