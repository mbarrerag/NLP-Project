# THE MORAL THEOLOGY AND THE ETHIC THESISM OF KANT

In Kant there is a convivial moral theology and an ethical atheism whose purpose is to emphasize the very Kantian principle of autonomy. We are in the crux of the second and third Kantian questions. Kantian thought allows us to make a double reading on his reflections on religion, but the ethical perspective imposed by his practical primacy can never be abandoned. Hence, some collaborations speak of Kantian moral theology and others place the emphasis on reading between the lines, according to the spirit rather than the letter of Kantian texts, to underline that within ethical formalism morals mutis by the forum as soon as one or another divinity appears
