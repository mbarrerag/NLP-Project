# ASSOCIATION OF ANTROPOMETRIC AND SARCOPENIA MEASURES IN MAJOR ADULT WOMEN IN BOGOTÁ

The book deals with research results of members of the Food and Human Nutrition Research Group of the Faculty of Medicine. This chapter is the result of an investigation with the objective of evaluating the association between anthropometric measures and sarcopenia in older adult women in Bogotá of strata 3,4,5 and 6
