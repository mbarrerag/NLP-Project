# KRIGING PREDICTION FOR STATIONARY AND NONSTATIONARY RANDOM FIELDS

This work concerns with geostatistical analysis of functional data, that is, spatial prediction of curves when we dispose of a sample of curves collected at sites of a region with spatial continuity. Several works has been proposed to fulfill this task. Ordinary kriging for function value spatial data and continuous time varying kriging for functional data, among others, can be used for this purpose. These methods assume stationarity. Here we propose two methodologies to the case where the stationarity assumption is not appropriated because the mean function is not constant through the region of interest. A real data set corresponding to temperature curves measured at 35 Canadian weather stations is used to illustrate the proposals.
