# DIGITAL ENVIRONMENTAL STORY: COMMENTS FROM LATIN AMERICA

In times of digital revolution and consolidation of a field of practices and knowledge that has been autorecognosing in the term "Digital Humanities", who practices the environmental history faces challenges, difficulties and possibilities that derive from the encounter of the culture and digital technologies with the office of historian. It is not only a question of learning to use digital tools that multiply and change the possibilities of producing historical-environmental knowledge, but also - of imagining and experiencing new means that promote the social appropriation of knowledge, or even its collaborative construction with other actors and especially with citizenship. On another front, the increasing availability of data and information (including bibliographical and primary sources through the policies of digitalization of several institutions of managing documentary heritage) and the relative ease to produce and share new data useful to the historical-environmental research improves the possibility of finding evidence of the relationship society-environment, overcoming the documentary silence that often confronts research in this field. So much digital evolution does not, however, also leave to the discovery of several weak flancos of the work of the environmental historian of Latin America, which they propose from the weak and open to document and to put in common research platforms. useful to the historical-environmental inquiry, (3) the collaborative participation to the existing initiatives of thematic libraries and digital archives to make visible and make possible the investigation and historical-environmental knowledge about and from Latin America.
