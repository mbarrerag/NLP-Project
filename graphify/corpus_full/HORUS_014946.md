# Methodology for the implementation of energy efficiency and water conservation strategies in resolution 0549 of 2015 for the rehabilitation of heritage buildings in Colombia: Case study: Central Building - CHSJD

Recognizing the environmental impact of the buildings built in the consumption of water and energy, this research was developed with the purpose of establishing a methodology for the implementation of energy efficiency and water conservation strategies in resolution 0549 of 2015 for the rehabilitation of heritage buildings in Colombia. Considering that these buildings, have a special management, it is necessary to map the route from the methodological and normative framework in order that, like the other buildings, they contribute to the conservation of natural resources, preserving their heritage value. The referents studied in countries such as Mexico, Canada, Spain and Germany among others allowed to see that such integration has been possible through practice, however, it is still necessary at the normative and methodological level, tools that allow to carry out this type of interventions where sustainability and heritage complement each other and contribute to the conservation of the heritage.
