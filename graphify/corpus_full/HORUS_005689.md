# THE "POVERTY CIMETIER" IN BOGOTÁ (COLOMBIA): A CHANTAGE OF KNOWLEDGE ON THE DEFAVOURED PEOPLES OF THE 19TH AND 20TH

The archaeology of rescue work in a part of Bogotá's central cemetery, called the Globe B, and for some time called "the cemetery of the poor", allowed to form a large osteological collection of individuals buried between 1850 and 1950 (NMI=7103). During this period, the Colombian capital sought modernity, but the testimonies on the living conditions of the citizens were far from what was desirable. Bioarcheological and paleopathological analysis provides a good example of "osteological paradox". Analysis of the information from the necrological registers helped to interpret the results better. All these results contribute to a reflection on the discipline, by questioning the status of collections for which no documentary evidence exists
