# Design, construction and evaluation of an INDIO-SET-TABLE CELL CALL AS A TERMM-ETRIC PATRON FOR CALIBRATE OF CONTACT TERMOMTERS

A closed Indian fixed point cell was designed, constructed and evaluated to be used as a temperature standard in the Temperature and Moisture Laboratory of the National Institute of Metrology (INM). The results obtained were compared with the open Indian fixed point cell currently held by the INM through the standard error method
