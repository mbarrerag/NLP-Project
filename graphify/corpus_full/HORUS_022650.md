# IMMUNIZATION WITH NEUMOCOCO CAULT VACUNS AND OTHER RECOMMENDED VACUNS IN HIGH RISK PATIENT PEDIATRICS. CONSENSATION OF MEXICAN PEDIATRIC INFECTOLOGY ASSOCIATION 2021

Immunocompromised patients are the most affected group and with the highest mortality rates associated with invasive infections, so the vaccination of the immunocompromised patient plays a fundamental role in improving their survival and quality of life. Since the safety and efficacy of these immunogenes will depend on the type and degree of individual immunosuppression, it is important to know what are the vaccines indicated, and which contraindicates that may have a risk for these patients. The purpose of this consensus was to analyze and discuss the impact of vaccination against pneumococcal and other vaccines recommended today for the pediatric patient of high risk. The academic consensus model was used, where the available scientific literature was discussed. To this end, the guide Linements for the elaboration of consensuses and the recommendations and useful criteria for the critical reading of the selected articles of the European initiative AGREE. For the conformation of the participating group, mixed and representative teams were integrated in which the paediatricians infectologists, members and paediatricians with speciality in immunology and allergy as well as pediatricians of the private sector were used.
