# CHAPTER 4 DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION IN THE MEDICINE FACULTY OF THE UNAL IN THE FRAMEWORK OF PANDEMIA

This chapter presents the experience related to Digital Transformation that the Faculty of Medicine implemented in the period of health emergency. It describes the articulated strategy of organization through committees to address the pandemic and the main results grouped in the scope of Digital Transformation in crisis management, risk management, dissemination and communications, assistance, teaching and pedagogical strategies. It lists the reflections and challenges in the future in the face of the Digital Transformation process
