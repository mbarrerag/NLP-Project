# NON-TECHNIQUE GERENTIAL OBSERVATIONS NEEDED FOR DIRECTIVE Entrepreneurship

The objective of the study was to identify the non-technical managerial skills that promote better performance of managers with technical training of small and medium-sized enterprises in Bogotá and Medellín (Colombia). Design/methodology: The methodology was descriptive, with a non-experimental quantitative design. 142 surveys were applied to managers and a focal group was carried out to classify, select and propose the necessary non-technical skills. The research showed a relationship between the characteristics of managers, their work needs and their living conditions with the development of non-technical skills. Hence, interpersonal skills appear as the most important for managers with technical training to start their career. Therefore, business schools should rethink the way of teaching non-technical managerial skills, so that emotional intelligence is established as a basis for the training of competent managers. It is concluded that a managerial with non-technical training should focus on the development of four non-technical managerial skills: training of work teams, assertive communication, delegation and self-knowledge. Originality: This article differs from other studies related to non-technical management skills studied in Bogotán, Colombia
