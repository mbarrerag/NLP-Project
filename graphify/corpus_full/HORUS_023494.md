# VOLUNTARY CITIZENS AS ASSORBENTS UNDER COST FOR THE RETENTION OF ATRAZIN IN SOUND

Introduction The fly ash (CV) is a by-product of energy generation in Colombia that represent an environmental problem and could be used for the remediation of soils contaminated with pesticides [1,2]. In this study the potential of CVs in the immobilization of atrazine in soil was evaluated. Methods The isotherms of adsorption-dissorption were obtained by the technique batch-equilibrium [3] with mixtures at 0, 5 and 10% w/w of CV and a soil of Tinjacá, Colombia. Six doses of technical atrazine were used (0.0523 - 0.6008 mg/L) plus 14Catrazin. The analyses were performed in Centelleo Líquido counter. Results and Conclusions The percentages of adsorcid were 99.73±0.07% for CV and 75.21±2.29% for soil increment. The data were adjusted to the isoterm of Freundlich x/m = KfCeqna. The values of Kf were 1697.06±37.12, 4.35±0.48, 24.16±3.0% and 65.0% for soil increase. Science, 2009, 19(10), 1173-1186.
