# Synthesis of nanoparticle amorphous aluminosilicates from mining residues for application as adsorbents of asphaltenes.

In this study, aluminosilicates of fine quarrying (mining waste) were synthesized and used for adsorption and removal of asphaltenes. The conversion of the residue was performed by hydrothermal alkaline hydrolysis for the production of the donor silicate. Subsequently, a synthesis of the precipitates of amorphous aluminosilicates was made by copolymerization with microwave. These were characterized before and after by techniques such as X-ray diffraction, X-ray fluorescence and Infrared. Asphaltens were obtained from heavy-Colombian crudes using the IP 43 precipitation method. Once obtained, the adsorption isotherms were constructed, for this purpose.
