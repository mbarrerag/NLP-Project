# VALIDATION OF THE SINTETICAL CURVAS VALUE IN COLOMBIAN CARIBBEAN

This article describes the results of the validation of synthetic IDF curves in the Colombian Caribbean, estimated by Chen, Vargas & Díaz-Granados methods, of precipitation scaling and methodology proposed by Díaz-Granados & Puente, included in the calculator developed to estimate synthetic or generalized IDF curves in Colombia, in areas without rainfall information, but with rainfall information (Villamizar et al., 2018).For this purpose, the values of maximum intensities obtained from the use of the calculator and the use of the four methods, for durations of 15, 30, 60, 120 and 360 minutes, with the values of intensities of nine IDF curves constructed in a traditional way (using flood registers), reported by Torres et al., 2021. In order to compare the results obtained by using the calculator and the IDF curves derived from traditional intensity, two metrics of performance (mseu) and BIAS are used.
