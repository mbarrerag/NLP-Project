# ̈GREENIUM ̈ IN COLOMBIA: CASE STUDY OF THE VERDE BONES MARKET TO A STRUCTURAL MODEL OF TWO FACTORS

The overall temperature has increased by an average value of 1,1?C compared to pre-industrial levels (1880-1900) whose consequences have been reflected in natural disasters, change in climate patterns, and affected ecosystems.In view of these circumstances, the Paris Agreement signed in 2015 led different countries to limit this increase to 1,5?C, however, despite good intentions, the world is on the way to increases of the order of 2,1?C whose consequences would be catastrophic and irreversible (World Wide Fund for Nature, 2022). This increase consists of the requirements of metals such as lithium, copper, cobalt, nickel, tantalum, among others that, according to the International Energy Agency, would be necessary to multiply by 6 to reach the expected environmental goal in 2050. In this sense, it will be essential to identify suitable mechanisms to optimize the financing costs associated with leverage of green and non-green projects. As a contribution to the knowledge of sovereign green bonds and with the aim of seeking mechanisms that contribute to environmental sustainability, this research consists in estimating the value of the unobservable variables that determine the Grennium in Colombia from the price series of the country’s first green sovereign bond. Based on these estimates, the sensitivity of some macroeconomic variables is analyzed to determine different values of Greenium and consequently financing scenarios favorable to the country.
