# THE CHILDREN OF SILENCE

The cinema has tried to shape the most painful facts of our historical past, but what happens when the protagonist of the film that recalls is a boy or a girl? If the infant is conceived as the one that has no tongue, if childhood does not speak for itself, who does it in its place and in what way? Undeclarable, as the sublime, childhood, the reference to the past establishes conflictive relations with him. the films with girls are a crossroads and a test of the narrative forms of cinema and memory, and childhood is a rhetorical figure that questions both in the films seen in depth in this book, that address the trauma left by the dictatorships and the revolution wars of the second half of the twentieth century in Latin America
