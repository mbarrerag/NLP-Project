# MODEL TO FOLLOW AND EARLY INTENTION IN A RURAL CONTEXT

The present study seeks to identify the mediating effect of the norms subject to the study, the perceived behavioral control and the entrepreneurial attitudes with respect to the relationship between the models to be followed and the entrepreneurial intentions. It was carried out in the context of rural Colombia, where 1,037 quaes were analyzed, filled by people in tertiary education programs, through the University in the Field program. A model of structural equations was developed and the method of sampling bootstrapping was used to determine the robust parameters of the structural model. The results show that the norms subjective and behavioral control perceived mediate the relationship between the models to be followed and the business intentions. The main contribution of this study is the analysis of the entrepreneurial intentions in the context of the theory of planned behavior and in the Colombian rural context, characterized by poverty, marginality and having been affected by the civil war
