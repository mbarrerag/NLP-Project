# The Legal Field in the State Margins

The project is concerned with the analysis of the production of legal fields at the margins of the state based on its structural (internal logic) conditions and contingent (external logic) factors in contexts of legal pluralism. For this purpose, the emergency conditions of the practices of administration of justice, the institutional and community actors that produce social regulation in the territory and the regulatory structures that allow the existence and transformation of conflicts will be taken into account. The research will be carried out from a case study in the municipality of Puerto Libertador in the department of Cordoba. The determination of the municipality considered the confluence of the following elements: a.Territories in which there is no centralized control by the State, even it does not have the monopoly of the use of force; b. Diversity of organized social actors that have regulatory structures that regulate some sectors of the population and that are disputed the hegemony by the determination of the social order as Juntas de Acción Comunal, modalities of administration of community justice, indigenous peoples; c. Presence of armed actors of different ideologies that have historically been in the territory
