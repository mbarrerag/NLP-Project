# COLOMBIAN TABLE OF FOOD EXCHANGE FOR PEDIATRIAL USE (TIAP): PRE-SCHOOL AND SCHOOL

The table of Food Exchanges for Pediatric Use (TAP) is designed as a tool for nutrition and dietetic professionals, since it allows to quantify quickly, practically and reliably the intake of nutrients ingested by the pediatric population, as well as to carry out the planning of a varied and healthy diet according to nutritional needs and according to the recommendations of energy and nutrients intake (RIEN) for the Colombian population. The size of the portion and the home measures used for the design of the TIP are based on recommended and validated amounts for the child population. Its use facilitates the realization of food and nutrition education activities. TIPA groups the food according to its nutritional value, taking into account the food groups suggested by Food Guides Based on Food for Colombian population over two years old.
