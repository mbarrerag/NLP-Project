# SUBJECT EFFECTS OF THE PLACE OF THE VICTIM IN CONTEMPORARY

It can be said that the existing literature on the victim's place in contemporaneity is quite prolific. However, these analyses have not deepened the subjective effects that trigger the new victim's status. In this connection, it is evident that the new victim's place produces subjects framed in traumatic experiences, which do not allow spaces for their transformation or for the whole of social relations. Thus, an analysis is necessary to allow to assimilate the complexity that the new victim's status implies in contemporaneity
