# Strategic guidelines for the construction of a rural agricultural land use planning plan for the municipality of Sibaté

In land use planning processes, mainly where rural areas predominate, there is a weakness in incorporating the temporality of changes in land use. An alternative to improve the design of POTs may be to incorporate biophysical data and images from remote sensors that make evident the temporary changes in land use in the territory. Identifying and analyzing the logics used by agrarian decision makers in the orientation of one use or another on rural land, becomes a key element to understand the uses that are developed and dynamize the rural environment in which different media that inhabit it converge. . The objective of this work has been to propose some strategic guidelines for the construction of a rural agricultural land use plan; the case of analysis is the municipality of Sibaté - Cundinamarca. Documentary research, digital image processing for multi-temporal analysis and information gathering with key actors were used. The articulation of the analysis and processing of the data that can be incorporated, the current legal regulations and the logic of the agrarian decision-makers, allowed the generation of guidelines as guidelines that should be considered in the elaboration of a rural agricultural planning instrument for the municipality
