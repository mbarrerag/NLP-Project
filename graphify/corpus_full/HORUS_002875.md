# INDIGO SUGAR ADVOTION ON UCHUVA CAPACH

Water, a fundamental resource for life, has been seriously affected by industrial and technological growth. In particular, pollution by dyes is a persistent problem, since several industries generate large volumes of coloured water, representing a significant risk to both human health and the environment. The textile industry, for example, produces 21 to 377 m3 of waste water per ton of textile generated, which are usually poured into water sources, where even concentrations of 1 mg/L trigger visible colouring and levels dangerous to consumption. Indigo Blue (AI), is an an anionic dye, classified as a carcinogenic, genotoxic and neurotoxic substance, which can cause hypertension, as well as cardiovascular and respiratory effects. In this context, adsortion has been consolidated as an efficient alternative for its treatment, where the use of non-conventional adsorbents increases its attractiveness due to its favourable cost-benefit ratio.
