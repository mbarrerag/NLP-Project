# Frequency of prescription of medicines in patients attending external geriatrics consultation in a University Hospital.

The polypharmacy has been associated with an increase in morbidity and mortality and adverse outcomes. The geriatric in this scenario systematically performs the analysis to define the relevance of continuing, modifying the dose or replacing a drug, taking into account clinical, pharmacological factors and also the opinions and preferences of the patient; this process is called deprescription.The objective of this study was to identify the frequency of drug deprescription in the geriatric consultation of the HUN Methods: Descriptive, observational, retrospective study. Clinical histories of patients over 60 years of age who attended the external geriatric consultation for the first time during a period of 6 months were evaluated. 426 clinical histories were analyzed. The average age was 83.4 years (DE 7.1). 79.5% lived with a family caregiver.
