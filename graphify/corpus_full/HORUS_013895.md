# Strengthening of research capacities in sensory analysis for seedbed members in food science and innovation in agro-industrial processes

Currently, the seedbed of research in food science and innovation in agro-industrial processes is composed of a total of 34 undergraduate and postgraduate students, of programs of various faculties. This seedbed was founded and is formed by professors of the Faculty of Agricultural Sciences and the Institute of Food Science and Technology, seeking to train students from very early stages of their academic preparation in aspects related to research in agro-food science and technology, which allows them to develop with greater ownership in their professional life, or to engage in research aspects of this area in their post-gradual training. Although there are several areas of work inside the seedbed, the sensory analysis of food is located transversally in the research carried out in the various projects in which students and teachers participate.The sensory analysis is a work area that involves the organs of the senses as a tool of work, which as such must be trained and validated to obtain reliable results.The training is dispensive and complex, since not all people have the same sensory skills, and the recognition of the aroma and flavor is highly correlated with social factors. In the spirit of replicating this skill in the development of their researches under objective and homogeneous measurement principles, the training sessions in instrumental methods will be accompanied by graduates from the seedbed and the teachers associated with it. Finally, the course will be closed with a session in multivariate data analysis techniques
