# Design of a hybrid photovoltaic solar system for the municipality of Barrancominas ZNI of the Department of Guainía

This grade work focuses on the design of a solar system photovoltaic hybrid located in the locality ZNI of Barrancominas, applying a sustainability approach PESTAR, identifying its problems associated with the lack of electrical infrastructure and geographical isolation affecting its economic development. It analyses the existing electricity supply, based on a diesel generator which has an average supply of energy service for 16 hours, identifying the energy requirement and fuel consumption. It is proposed the implementation of solar technology photovoltaic hybrid and energy efficiency strategies to be implemented in the locality to reduce by 10% average monthly fuel consumption. It is carried out an analysis of the solar and wind resource in the area to determine the best combination of renewable energy sources that meets the objective of reduction of consumption Diesel, then the optimal alternative of the solar system is selected using the hybrid and is carried out with its design. The design of the selected alternative (SSFH-D) considers technical and economic aspects as well as the compliance of the regulatory requirements. is key challenge related to the scourge of electrical infrastructure and geographical isolation, which is effectively impacted the region’s economic development. The current electrical supply, based on a diesel generator provided energy for an overview of 16 hours daily, was analyzed, including the community’s energy demand and fuel consumption. A hybrid solar photovoltaic system, combined with energy efficiency strategy to determine the best renewable energy mix that was proposed to reduce diesel fuel consumption by an average of 10% per month.
