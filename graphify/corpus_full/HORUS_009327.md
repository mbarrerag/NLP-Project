# EFFECT OF THE PHOTOVOLTAIC GENERATORS AND STORAGE SYSTEMS PENETRATION INTO THE VOLTAGE STABILITY OF A DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM

To achieve the energy transition, the electric system must change into a power system with high penetration of distributed energy resources (DERs); among them, photovoltaic systems (PVSs) are attractive due to the constant decrease of their cost. Unfortunately, their grid connection has an impact on the voltage stability, on operation of protections and on losses. In this paper, the voltage stability of a Colombian electric system based distribution system (DS) is studied by means of the ¿¿¿¿¿¿® software. A 34 nodes DS is selected from those proposed by IEEE. Three indicators are used to evaluate the voltage stability: eigenvalues of the modal analysis, sensibility value and active power margin. The effect of the connection of a battery energy storage system (BESS) on the voltage stability is also analysed
