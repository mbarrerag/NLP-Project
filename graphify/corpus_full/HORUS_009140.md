# Implementation of tariff zoning in Integrated Mass Transport Systems and its effects on social welfare.

The decisions on the tariff policies of the SITM are important for the whole of society, because they impact the welfare of the agents that make up the market, according to this the institutions responsible for the SITM and the people that are beneficiaries of this system, are negatively affected if the decisions made on the rates do not take into account the welfare of these agents. The present research proposes a methodology to evaluate the social welfare of the implementation of a tariff scheme for tariff zones in SITM with an application for the line A of the AMVA Metro. The data used correspond to a matrix of origin/destination of the SITVA and to 720 surveys carried out to users of the SITVA, with which rates were estimated for zones and models of discreet choice with the inclusion of a variable of travel in one, two and three zones that affect the value of the tariffs. The models were used for predictive purposes and analysis of welfare to know the changes in demand, surplus of the consumer and producer and compensatory variations and equivalent.
