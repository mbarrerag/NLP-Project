# Programme for promoting voice health and low stress in the workplace among university teachers

The health promotion programs in the workplace are an effective approximation for the health care of university teachers, because through these they aim to promote healthy habits. This project consisted of the implementation of a program of promotion of health in the workplace (PSLT) aimed at university teachers in virtual mode. The implementation of the program in virtual mode was necessary due to the situation of pandemic that we experienced since 2020 at the global level. The objective was to determine the effect of the implementation of a program of Promotion of vocal health and reduction of stress levels in the workplaces aimed at teachers of the Bogotá headquarters of the National University of Colombia, for which the participants carried out a questionnaire and recorded voice samples before and after the implementation of the program. The questionnaires included questions on the vocal functioning, psychosocial factors, quality of life and health conditions of the teachers, and work of the group. The methodological design of the group was quasi-experimental type with a group that participated in the program and a group that did not participate in the program (during the development of this the the thesis. Once we finished the research, the group of non-intervention program had access to all the materials. The implementation of the online programme was due to the pandemic COVID-19, and the restrictions for in-person meeting. The objective present to determine the effect of the implementation of a workplace health promotion program on demand and reduction of stress levels combined remuneration professionals at the Bogotá campus of the National University of Colombia. Participants completed out a questionnaire and recorded voids before and after the implementation of the programme. The questionnaires included questions about the voice functioning, psychological social factors, quality of life, and health and work conditions of teachers. The methodology design was of a quantitative study with a group that participated in the programme and a group group group that did not involve the on the stress levels of the participants.
