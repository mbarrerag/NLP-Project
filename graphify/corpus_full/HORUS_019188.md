# ILLEGAL ECONOMICS IN THE MINICRIPTION OF SAN VICENTE DEL CAGUAN IN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE POSACuerDO (2016-2020)

This article is the result of an investigation carried out on San Vicente del Caguán, in which it is intended to expose the dynamics of illegal economies during the implementation of the Final Peace Agreement between 2016 and 2020. The research consists in making a case study in the municipality, based on an approach to the concepts of state and economy, from the configuration and development of dynamics in the territory. Thus, a marked institutional inadequacy was identified in the municipality, which was attempted to solve through the Peace Agreement, but since it was not effective, other armed groups co-opted the power vacuum left by FARC-EP after its demobilization, complementing the configuration of illegal economies and the effect of these on the population, transforming the post-conflict scenario into a neo-conflict
