# Parameters of additive manufacturing and post-processing surface finishing in cobalt-chromium alloy joint implants bearing surfaces manufactured using Selective Laser Melting

The surfaces of joint implants, known as friction pairs surfaces, are crucial for the mobility and proper functioning of implants. These surfaces must have a specific finish to ensure optimal functioning and avoid premature failures. Manufacturing by means of Selective Laser Melting (SLM) allows the creation of individualized implants of great complexity and geometries in a time and cost less than traditional techniques. However, due to the nature of the process, post-processes are required to obtain the necessary friction pairs surfaces. This research evaluated the manufacturing parameters for SLM and three different post-processes: micro-mechanical, micro-granalized and laser-polying. The post-processing parameters that allow the proper manufacture of the surfaces of friction pairs were determined. It was found that the best way to manufacture these surfaces is to use manufacturing parameters in SLM that provide a homogeneous roughness throughout the surface, followed by a post-processing surface by laser-polishing. With this combination of techniques, the recommended surface roughness values were achieved to guarantee the quality and functionality of the surfaces taken in pairs of joint implants (less). conductive to proper bearing surface manufacturing were identified. The optimal approach involves using SLM manufacturing parameters to achieve homogeneous roughness across the entire surface, followed by laser policing with parameters of 600 W laser power, 0.05 mm hatch distance, and 5.5 /min scanning speed. This method effectively ensures the quality and functionality of friction pair surfaces in joint implants produced via SLM
