# ADVISORY ISSUES FOR COBRE AND LEVEL REMOTION OF HEALTH REST LIXIVATED

The objective of this study is to know the efficiency levels in the treatment of heavy metals such as copper and lead of leachate, since these are found in most of the occasions above the permissible limits established in the normivity. To determine the removal rates and adsorption capacity of copper (Cu) and lead (Pb) of leachate of sanitary fillers, the sorption was used with processed coffee pulp. Adsorption isotherms were performed in individual solutions with Langmuir and Freundlich models. Statistically, an experimental design was performed 2 3 and response surfaces. In addition, thermodynamic parameters were determined to know the type of sorption. It was found that the Langmuir model represented the sorption of copper and lead. Maximum adsorption capabilities for copper in individual solutions for doses of 1.3 and 5 g of adsorption material were 0.29, 0.12 and 0.59 mg/g, respectively and for lead were 0.05, 0.02 and 0.02 mg/g, respectively.
