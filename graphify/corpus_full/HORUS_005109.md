# Estimation of susceptibility to surface mass movements through local spatial regression analysis. Application for a stretch of the average Chicamocha river basin

In this research work, the estimation of susceptibility to surface mass movements is presented by means of a local spatial regression analysis, specifically a geographically weighted logistic regression model (GWLR) that takes into account the non-stationary relationships of factors that influence the occurrence of mass movements in a zone. This method was applied in a section of the average basin of the Chicamocha river, located in the department of Boyacá, which was selected due to its geological and geomorphological characteristics, as well as to the evident instability observed in the region. In addition, a model of global regression (conventional logistic regression, LR) was calibrated to determine the advantages and disadvantages of each method in studies of susceptibility. Independent variables such as lithology, proximity to faults, slope, curvature, roughness of the ground, TWI, SPI, proximity and density of drainage, precipitation, proximity to roads, coverage of the land, NDVI and areas with predominance of erosion processes were considered.
