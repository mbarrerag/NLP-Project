# THE INFORMATION SEVEN. THE CASE OF HOBBBES THHOMAS

In "The hijacked information: the case of Thomas Hobbes", the influence of Hobbesian thought is studied in the configuration of authoritarian regimes and its impact on social organization. It is analyzed how the political theory of Hobbes, based on the need for an absolute power to avoid chaos, has served as a basis for centralized control models that persist in various modern institutions. An innovative contribution is made from the concept of *abducted information*, which introduces an innovative methodology by establishing a logical link between epistemology, anthropology and the institutional proposal in Hobbes. From this notion, it is explained how the author of the *Leviathan* conceives knowledge as a resource that must be monopolized by sovereign power, which allows justifying the suppression of the individual sociability of the human being and the imposition of a centralized order.This methodological analysis allows to articulate the Hobbesian view of knowledge with its conception of the individual and its institutional structure.
