# SYNTHESIS OF NANOPARTICLES OF COBRE AND PLATA BY CHEMICAL REDUCTION AND ASSESSMENT OF THEIR ANTI-ACTIVITY AGAINST RALSTONIA SOLANACEARUM

Ralstonia solanacearum is a phytopathogenic bacteria that affects a wide range of hosts of agronomic importance among those that is found banana (Musa x paradisiaca), causing the disease called Moko that affects a wide range of musaceae worldwide, being this a horticultural product of importance in the food and in the Colombian economy. For the control of this disease different pesticides and antibiotics have been used, but the inappropriate and irrational application of these products do not show satisfactory results. In this study two different sizes of nanoparticles of silver [1-AgNPs] and [2-AgNPs] were synthesized by chemical reduction using silver nitrate, sodium bolorhydride and sodium citrate, and for the synthesis of copper nanoparticles [1-CuNPs] and [2-CuNPs], copper chloride and anti-ascómic acid were identified.
