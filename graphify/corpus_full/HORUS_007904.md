# SOCIO-AMBIENTAL ASSISTANCE OF SMALL COMMUNITY ASSETS IN CUNDINAMARK.

This article presents the results of an interdisciplinary participatory research, led by social workers, that contributes to the production of knowledge and generation of methodological tools for the recognition of conditions of access to water among the Colombian rural population. The diagnosis of natural, socio-ganizing, infrastructure and cost-cutting aspects, carried out with three social organizations of community aqueducts in the municipalities of Tena and Zipacón, department of Cundinamarca, Colombia, recognized potentialities and fragility in these systems of water distribution for human consumption. Among the most significant aspects are recognized the cultural meaning of water, the family and solidarity identity of the human group, through non-commercial exchanges and self-managed effort for the transformation of infrastructure of community aqueducts. Likewise, barter and an organized administration of family quotas, with strategies of environmental education, are the main actions that enhance organizational membership among the associated members
