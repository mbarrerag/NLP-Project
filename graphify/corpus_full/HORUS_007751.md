# Strengthening the capacity of the BIOFOOD Research Group in the line of dehydration of vegetable foods

The BIOFOOD research group works on characterisation, in vitro functionality, conservation and processing of natural foods, produced in environmentally friendly conditions, with interest in bioactive compounds that generate functional characteristics, use of bioprocesses and application of bioconservative techniques. It currently has 13 members and there is interest to improve the ability to control the dehydration process, to develop research work using renewable energies with solar dehydration of vegetable foods, a sustainable technology of great potential, which can contribute significantly to food safety, the use of waste, the reduction of post-harvest losses and the development of new alternatives of natural ingredients to value and transform indigenous foods. This proposal will study strategies and formulate proposals for the use of solar dehydration of vegetable foods, for this purpose it requires control instruments that allow obtaining reliable experimental data in the dehydration equipment
