# SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION IN MILITARY INSTITUTIONS

This chapter addresses key elements for the analysis of technological management in the military forces, particularly in the Colombian National Navy (arc), which will be the subject of further study. It begins with the review of the Colombian institutional framework for activities and articulation of actors around the issues of science, technology and innovation, and their relationship with competitiveness. Based on this framework, in the second section, it deepens the concept of management oriented to these issues, in particular, in technological management, to highlight its importance in the fulfilment of organizational objectives and strategies. Finally, it analyses the role of technological management in the military field, in which the development of science, technology and innovation can represent the victory in war and the protection of the sovereignty of a nation, among other key aspects that concern this type of organizational management
