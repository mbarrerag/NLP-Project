# On the controlability of a free border problem for the heat equation

The master's thesis addresses the problem of controlability in free border problems, introducing an approach that includes the presence of internal control and explores an additional variant that implies a delay in time within the model. Divided into five chapters, it begins by establishing the fundamental principles of control theory, especially applied to parabolic equations. Then, it reviews the current state of the research regarding internal controlability in free border problems for the heat equation in one dimension. Subsequently, it presents new findings and results related to this problem, but considering the influence of an internal delay in time. In the last chapter, significant conclusions are derived from the research carried out and possible directions are proposed for future work. In addition, an appendix is included that provides relevant complementary information.
