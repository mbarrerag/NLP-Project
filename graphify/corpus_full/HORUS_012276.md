# Contribution to the development of a topical panela formulation with possible healing activity.

The wound healing is a highly regulated physiological process for the recovery of the functionality of the skin after damage or injury to its structure by trauma or disease. In traditional medicine, the panela has been used empirically for the topical treatment of wounds with satisfactory results, for this reason, the present work consisted in developing a topical formulation based on panela with the aim of evaluating its possible healing activity. To this, the qualitative composition and definitive operational conditions of the cream were obtained by applying experimental statistical designs of exploratory and optimization type. The healing activity of the final formulation developed was evaluated by determining the progressive change of wounds by excision performed on rat skin during 12 days of treatment. The percentage of oil, the percentage of emulsifying and the speed of homogenization of the cream were statistically significant factors for the size of drop D4,3 and the span.
