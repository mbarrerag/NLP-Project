# Inter-laboratory testing programme for the physical characterisation of Guamo sand

In soil testing, the assessment of physical properties is essential to characterize sands accurately. However, the results of the tests may vary according to the experimental procedures used and their implementation. A round-robin exercise facilitates simultaneous analysis of reproducibility and replicability of standard procedures used to characterize the properties of a specific material. This article presents the results of the first inter-laboratory test initiative (i.e., a round-robin exercise) whose objective was to evaluate the variability of results in the physical characterisation of a sandy soil. Guamo sand, widely used in research and engineering projects in Colombia, was the focus of this study. 11 national academic laboratories participated in the programme, carrying out seven replicas of grain size distribution tests, specific gravity of solids and maximum and minimum void ratio. Data provided by all participants were analyzed and interpreted using statistical techniques. The results revealed significant differences between the data collected for each physical parameter, attributable to the intrinsic variability of the natural base of this sand and to the use of various test procedures.
