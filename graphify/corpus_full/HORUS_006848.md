# Technological characterization of the Colored house of the early twentieth century, with a view to its valuation, restoration and conservation

To carry out the intervention of a property it is necessary to know what to do, but it is essential to understand first the know-how to do in the light of the context of the building itself and particular, which will avoid erroneous actions that can generate more damage due to negligence or ignorance. With this work we seek to propose criteria of intervention for a building from the beginning of the twentieth century, through the technological characterization of the building chosen as a case of study, where through the research and particular analysis focused on its form, function and structure, we can deepen in the area of knowledge of the constructive technique of the cultural heritage building of the republican period, with a view to its valuation, restoration and conservation. The case of study is taken from the building called Colored House, owned by the Cultural Heritage District Institute (IDPC), and from the results obtained with its technological characterization, we also hope to contribute to strengthen and enrich the critical thinking that allows the elaboration of criteria to be reasoned and coherent with the current needs of the building that has been present in the city for more than 100 years. Considering, we can generate the necessary co-generation technique and materiality of a well-being of a good. where through research and particular analysis focused on its form, function and structure, it will be possible to develop in the area of knowledge of the construction technique of the immovable cultural heritage of the Republican era, with a view to its valuation, restoration and conservation. The case study is taken from the building street Casa Colorada, owned by the District Institute of Cultural Heritage (IDPC), and from the results obtained with its technological characterisation, it is also applied to contribute to strengtheningthen and enrich the critical thinking that allows the development of resettlement and consistent criticism with the current necessity of the construction, it is also presented to contribute to further 100 years.
