# Apego, Parental Competences and Moral Development representations between 4 and 9 years

From the perspective of Dominions of moral development (Turiel, 1983) it is proposed that people build judgments on justice, equality and rights. Although it has been observed that children distinguish this knowledge from other aspects of the social world from early ages is not considered that this knowledge is innate (Turiel, 1893; Smetana et al., 2018), on the contrary, it is postulated that this is built through reciprocal interaction with the environment. Therefore, it can be inferred that, since the caregivers play a significant role in the interactions of children throughout their development and are constituted as the main figures of attachment, they exercise an influence on the social development of children. In this framework, this project seeks to describe and analyze the possible relationships between the representations of attachment, parental competences and moral development (moral reasoning and construction of moral agency) in children between 4 and 9 years old. To respond to the general objective, two studies will be carried out using a mixed design of aluminated quantitative predominance. The sample will be selected by means of non-probabilistic criteria, using methods of convenience and snow. The two studies will use a socio-economic questionnaire to characterize the participating families. Additionally, for the first study, an interview will be conducted, both with the children and their parents, to characterize situations of interpersonal conflict and moral agency indicators. For the second study, cartoons will be used that illustrate hypothetical situations of harm, two related to physical damage and two with psychological harm, varying between intentional and accidental damage. For each of the situations, moral judgments and psychological attributions will be evaluated (Smetana and Ball, 2017). A content analysis will be carried out (with the Nvivo program) that allows the codification of qualitative information derived from the interviews. Next, quantitative analysis will be carried out (descriptives, multivariate analysis and multiple regression models) according to the characteristics of the data. As results, a detailed characterization of the representations of attachment of children between the 4 and 9 years and the related competencies of their parents will be carried out, as well as the characteristics of conflict between parents and children in this age range, of moral reasoning and of the precursors of the moral agency's sense of moral agency can provide evidence
