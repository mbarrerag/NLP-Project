# CATALITICAL ASSESSMENT OF THE HIBRITED SYSTEM 0.1%PT/1CO2MNOX/AL2O3-3DOM IN TOXIDATION IN DILLUTED GASEOUS PHASE

The volatile organic compounds have a boiling point of less than or equal to 25°C at standard pressure are highly toxic to human health and the environment. Catalytic oxidation is an alternative to the elimination of these toxic pollutants, where they are completely oxidized in the presence of a catalyst, and active and selective materials are required. It is well known that bimetallic catalysts can sometimes exhibit higher activity, selectivity and resistance to deactivation, compared to the corresponding monometallic ones. Additionally, noble metals can promote the reduction of transition metals and stabilize them against a possible sintering. Therefore, attempts have been made recently to modify the catalysts Co, Mn, Ce and Ni by adding a second metal such as Pt or Au [1] [2] giving way to hybrid systems, composed of transition metals and noble metals.
