# ASSESSMENT OF THE HYDROLOGIC RESPONSE OF THE DYLE DWB IN THE CAUTION CAUTION CAUTION CAUTION CAUGHTER ON THE LEVEL OF A CUENCE AND MICROCOUNCE, CASE OF STUDY THE SONG-MOMENT RIUM, COLOMBIA

The flow duration curves (CDC) reflect the effects of the characteristics of a basin on the flows, this without taking into account the temporality of occurrence. Traditionally, they are constructed from historical records recorded by stations and are input for the calculation of water resource management indices such as the Water Regulation Index (IRH). Due to the scarcity of hydrological records, in practice, strategies of regionalization of CDCs are used to study those uninstrued basins. This work seeks to evaluate the performance of the DWB model from daily inputs in the simulation of CDC in two sub-basins of the Sogamoso river that have different characteristics (The Tabletazo and Colonial Bridge). A methodology is developed that uses the aridity index to identify 4 hydrologically contrasting periods, 2 humid periods and 2 dry periods. Subsequently, 8 calibration experiments were implemented which validate from the combination of previously identified periods. Finally, from the qualitative and quantitative review with signatures of sesquego in the medium, high and low flows, the model was evaluated
