# Estimate of resilience capacity of basement excavation systems in urban sectors. Case study: Green Office Building Failure at Carrera Avenue 11 N° 98-07 – Bogotá D.C.

The concept of resilience is currently applied to different areas of knowledge, and in engineering it is necessary to provide definitions and analysis that allow the consolidation of the criteria for the calculation of the resilience of a given system, with the purpose of being an indicator in the management of risk and mitigation of threatening events. In order to contribute to the definition of resilience in the field of geotechnical engineering and methodologies that allow the calculation of the parameters that make up it, in this work is made a methodological proposal for the estimation of the index of resilience of an excavation in urban environment. By means of a review of the main concepts of resilience, the criteria that make up the resilient parameters that are Robustez, Redundance, Management Capacity and Recovery in a geotechnical framework. For the aggregation of the criteria it is proposed the theory of diffuse sets that is based on the diffuse logic that allows to manage the imprecision and the processing of the information.
