# SENT-UP PROVOCATIONS FROM COLOMBIA TO QUESTION THE `DIFFERENTIAL APPROACH'

This article has two fundamental objectives: to question the institutional discourse of the `differential approach, and the forms it has taken in the field of public policies in Colombia; and to propose alternatives that allow to rethink and resent other panoramas for that field, especially from intersecting perspectives. The text explores in a critical way how and why this particular strategy of managing the difference reproduces binarisms and spurious essentialisms and, on that basis, we expose its ways of producing fences in the institutional sphere. Finally, we present some reflections in favor of intersectional alternatives on which, we consider, can establish public agendas much more plural and expansionary in Colombia
