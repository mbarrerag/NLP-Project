# Between Miramar and Miravalle. The gardens of the Second Mexican Empire through literary and historical sources

In this research, we study the historical and fictional gardens of and on the Second Mexican Empire, their representation in a diversity of literary and historical texts, from a perspective of cultural history, supported by the semiotic of culture and comparative literature. I analyze the types of description of the garden and its historical dimension as a cultural problem, the historical background preceding the installation of gardens in the nineteenth century, both of pre-Hispanic Nahua culture and of the European garden culture of the Renaissance and modernity. The events of the Second Mexican Empire are the center of gravity around which the theoretical reflections on the garden, concretely concretified in texts of different periods that include codices, chronicles, letters of relation, travel diaries, memories, official discourses, among others, as well as literary texts of the twentieth century. These latter, in which there is a critical reflection on the concept of history through the representation of the garden, are specifically “Tlactocatzine, of the garden of Flanders” and Aura, by Carlos Fuentes, and the novel Noticias of the Empire, by Fernando del Paso.
