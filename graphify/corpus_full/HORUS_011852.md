# Clinical characterization of aeromedically certified aeronautical personnel with infection confirmed by Sars-cov-2/Covid-19 reported to the Special Administrative Unit of Civil Aeronautics of Colombia, in 2020 and 2021.

The burden of the pandemic of the Acute Acute Respiratory Syndrome Severo Acute Acute Respiratory Respiratory Syndrome Severo Coronavirus-2 (SARS-CoV-2/COVVV-2/COVID-19) in the working population is still unknown. The critical safety populations, such as the holders of aeromedical certificates, must be monitored to assess the type, severity and sequelae following infection by SARS-CoV-CoV-2/COVID-19. The study describes the clinical characteristics of the holders of medical certificates with infection confirmed by SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19; they must be monitored to assess the type, severity and sequel to the following after infection by SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19. This study describes the clinical characteristics of the holders of medical certificates with infection confirmed by SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19 in holders of aeromedical certificates during the years 2020 and 2021. Individual and grouped frequencies were observed and reported for demographic data, symptoms and severity of infection and were analyzed using a consolidated database of the ACACC. RES, RES: The ACACC has been registered, the following: The ACACC has been counted with 14, ACC Hyposmia/anosmia 83 (25.00%) and dysgeusia/ageusia 64 (19.28%). Finally, most complications were respiratory 38 (11.45%), including mild and severe pneumonia 29 (8.73%) and 9 (2.71%) and infectious complications 10 (3.01%), such as bacterial overinfection, sepsis, and septic shock (2.11, 1.51% and 1.20%, respectively). The clinical characteristics of SARS-CoV-2/COVID-19 infections in Colombian aeromedical certificate holders are similar to those reported in the literature for general population, the long-term effects of this involvement are still being evaluated. Knowing the clinical characteristics of the infection in this high-impact population will contribute to the construction of future actions, programs and policies to ensure the best human performance in its critical roles for safety and to improve decision-making processes in aerobic certification
