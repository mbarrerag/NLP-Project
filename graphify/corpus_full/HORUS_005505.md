# STRATEGIES FOR THE FRONT AND RESILENCE OF PROFESSIONALS IN DISEASE DURING CIVID-19 PANDEMIA

during the pandemic by COVID -19, nursing professionals in charge of the direct and indirect care of these patients were exposed to highly stressful personal, family and work situations.Objective: to determine the relationship between the level of resilience and the coping strategies of nurses who provide direct and indirect care to patients with confirmed or suspected COVID-19 diagnosis in a four-to-four-level institution in the city of Bogotá.Methods: quantitative descriptive study; the sample was composed of 50 nursing professionals who were in the critical care, emergency and hospitalization services.The data collection instruments used were the Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale and the Inventory of Confrontation Strategies form.Resulted: regarding high resilience, this study showed a statistically significant relationship with postgradual training (p = 0.017), a statistically significant negative relationship (p = 0.027) with the strategy of self-critical and a positive relationship (p = 0.007) with the strategy of problem resolution.Conclusions: Exest relationship between the stress and the stress adaptation (p = 0.027), with the strategy of positive;
