# Genetic anomalies of good and poor prognosis in LLA-B and its influence on cells of the medullary stromal in an in vitro model of leuchaemic niche.

The role played by the mesenchymal stem cells (MSC) present in bone marrow (MO) in the establishment, development and progression of LLA-B and in the relapse of these patients has taken on importance in recent years. In particular, it is known that interactions between the MSC and the leukemic cells are dynamic, altering in the MSC the stem properties, secretoma and gene expression, and in turn, promoting proliferation and chemoresistance of LLA-B cells. Thus, a leucemic niche (NL) is established that favors the growth of malignant cells to the detriment of normal hematopoietic cells in the MO.
