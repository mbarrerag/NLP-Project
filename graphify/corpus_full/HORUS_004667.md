# Evaluation of PD-L1 expression, CD31 in canine breast tumors and their relationship to malignancy criteria

The objective of this study was to evaluate the immunoexpression of PDL-1 and CD31 in normal canine mammary glands and mammary tumors, determining its relationship with histopathological malignancy criteria, for this purpose, 30 tissues of tumours of mammary glands diagnosed as carcinomas, adenomas and hyperplasias and belonging to the laboratory of Veterinary Pathology at the National University of Colombia years 2019-2023 were analyzed; in each case variables were included as age, race, reproductive status, tumor size, neoplastic location according to its anatomical location. The results did not show a significant association between the type of neoplasia and the reproductive state, race or location of the tumor. However, a significant relationship was found between the age and size of the tumor with the type of neoplasia.
