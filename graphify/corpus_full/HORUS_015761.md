# THE COMMUNITY CRISIS, NARRATION AND EXPERIENCE IN THE FLIGHT OF SANGRED AND SIRGA

This article focuses on Colombian films The turn of crab and La Sirga. Its protagonists arrive in unknown places after episodes of intense suffering. These places function as false thresholds that separate the characters from their communities of origin but that cannot offer them a new one. Suspended in a liminal state, they find themselves unable to make an experience of what they have experienced, since, as Walter Benjamin reminded us, this requires a community that tells stories and listens to them. The crises of the community, of the narrative and of the experience are the result of the modernization that penetrates the geographic and economic periphery of the country. We postulate that these films constitute an aesthetic of the slowness that allows them to express and respond to this triple crisis
