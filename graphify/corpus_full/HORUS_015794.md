# Medicinal products with off label indication or condition - UNIRS USED IN THE TREATMENT OF AGREED AND CHRONIC INSTRUMENTS

The care of patients with complex chronic wounds carries multiple burdens for patients, family members, caregivers and the medical care system (1). The goal of the administration of medicines is to obtain a beneficial effect for those who take it (2) and for the professional the commitment to intervene in the problem, in circumstances that are not evident in the improvement of the health condition, it is necessary to use the medicines in indications and conditions outside those established in the technical file or registration of use, being known these as off-label (unlabeled use included)(3) or in Colombia as Use Not Indicated in the Health Registry (UNIRS) (4). This type of use of medications has been frequently used for the care of people with complex wounds (2) that due to various factors such as economic, social, cultural among others, have become difficult to manage and the devices used and approved have not shown better results. Thus, an adequate assessment and follow-up by wound clinic nursing allows to establish the most appropriate treatment (approved) to contribute to the healing process.
