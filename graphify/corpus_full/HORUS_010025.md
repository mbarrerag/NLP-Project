# Characterization of the factors associated with the digital transformation that influence financial inclusion in the banking traditional colombian: assessment by experts [[- C302 -]] THE RISK OF OTHERS (NON -) EXISTING: BUILDING ON the FIELD, CREATING POSSIBLE BRONTERS[[C 303]]Analysis of the distribution and habitat risk of hammerhead sharks in the area of Malpelo using Hidden Markov Models

This final master's degree project is based on the following research question: ¿Which factors associated with digital transformation affect financial inclusion in the traditional Colombian bank? To answer the question of investigation and determine the factors associated with the digital transformation that affects the financial inclusion in the traditional Colombian bank, an investigation of an exploratory type was carried out which was based on the multi-methodology of Mingers through the application of three methods; in the first section the construction of the conceptual framework was carried out through a literature review, in which the concepts of digital transformation, financial inclusion, and traditional banking were identified, which are considered the foundation of the investigation; based on which the identification of factors was carried out. In the second phase, an analysis of the previously identified factors previews using the PESTEL method. In the third section, the previously classified factors were evaluated through a panel of experts, following the Delphi method with the purpose of giving the corresponding conclusions and/or recommendations. As a result of this work, the following results were obtained: 1) Identifying based on the literature, the different factors associated with the digital transformation that affect financial inclusion in the traditional Colombian bank. 2) Classify the factors determined by means of the political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal aspects. 3) Evaluate the factors associated with the digital transformation that affect financial inclusion in the traditional Colombian bank. This final work presents a contribution to the financial and governmental sector by conducting academic research that characterizes the factors associated with the digital transformation that affects financial inclusion in traditional Colombian banking. This research has a significant scope since based on it, services can be built that leverage the strategic objectives of the National Government in terms the financial inclusion, in the same way this research contributes to the fulfillment of the sustainable development objectives that have been proposed by the United Nations (ONU)
