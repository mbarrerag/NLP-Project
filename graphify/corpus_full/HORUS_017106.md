# The principle of the most beneficial condition: tension in its application to survivors' pensions, between constitutional and ordinary jurisprudence, since the reform introduced by Law 797 of 2003.

Survivors' pensions are a right of high social and constitutional relevance, which has been subject to numerous reforms in terms of the requirements for their causation, amount and beneficiaries. This legal succession has brought enormous challenges to legal operators, since in the face of this type of periodic provision no transitional regime was created.The principle of the most beneficial condition was of great use for this purpose, which allowed for the granting of a kind of ultra-active effect to the repealed rule in order to apply it to a case, in principle, governed by the new regulations. However, since the reform introduced by Law 797 of 2003, strong tensions have been presented and presented today between the jurisprudence of the Constitutional and Supreme Courts of Justice on the scope of the principle of the most beneficial condition, as they have given different solutions to cases with identical factual assumptions, which have led to a violation of the principle of equality, institutional crisis and legal insecurity, aspects of undoubted academic relevance
