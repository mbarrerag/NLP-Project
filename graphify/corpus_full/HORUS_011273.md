# RURAL CATATUMB AND URBAN CUSTOMS FRONT: A COMPARISON IN THE FRAMEWORK OF VENEZOLAN MIGRATION

This is an analytical and comparative work on the behavior of the rural border of Catatumbo and the urban border of Cúcuta in the face of the Venezuelan migration phenomenon. It is evident that the border of Catatumbo presents its own practices due to the effect of the armed conflict, the rural economy based on agricultural production and the informality of its border crossings; which facilitates the arrival of Venezuelan migrants. In contrast, the border of Cúcuta is inserted in the logic of commercial capitalism, with an industrial export and a local offer of services that, among others, are used by Venezuelan migrants
