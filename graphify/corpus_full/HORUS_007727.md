# --Becoming aesthetic of human reason: from classical pragmatism to the aesthetics of Mark Johnson's understanding

This work proposes a theoretical research that proceeds through the review of primary academic and literary sources to, through critical analysis, generate an argumentative text (doctoral theory) and a derivative academic product (article) in which ideas are developed that constitute a novel contribution to the field of aesthetic studies. In the doctoral thesis three looks will be related that give rise to a perspective of thought in which it is possible to recognize an aesthetic becoming of human reason. First, it will be shown how inside the classical pragmatist philosophy of the early twentieth century an aesthetic thought was generated that recognizes the importance of the sensitive aspects of the experience in the generation of meaning and the human cognitive processes.
