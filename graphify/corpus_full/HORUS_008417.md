# MARKET STRATEGY AND PRODUCTION COSTS OF ENTRYED CODORNIZ HANDS WITH OMEGA-3 GRASS ACIDS

In the local market there is no offer of egg enriched with omega 3 fatty acids (AG n-3) and there is recorded a low consumption per capita of codorniz egg, which justifies incorporating innovation to this system.The objective of this study was to identify a niche and market strategy for the successful sale of quail eggs enriched with omega-3 fatty acids in Bogotá and to evaluate the economic viability of this innovation.A survey was applied to 105 people, 14 questions were made associated with the characterization of the population, purchase, consumption of quail eggs and perception on quail eggs enriched with AG n-3, with the results three classification levels were made: age, sex and socioeconomic stratum.For the economic analysis the overcost of the diet of birds was determined, as per egg tray n-3 as well as the economic sensitivity with variations in the percentage of posture and annual value of egg per linear regression = 2433
