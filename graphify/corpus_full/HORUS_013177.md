# Comparative analysis of the biomechanical behaviour of the implant-pillar interface in dental implants with conical and hexagonal connection

Currently, dental implant treatment represents the best alternative for the replacement of static and functional dental parts that have been lost. However, the combination of the design of the implant-pilar connection can compromise the ́excessiveness of the treatment. This is why the aim of this study was to carry out a full analysis of the biomechanical behaviour of dental implants with a chronic and hexagonal connection, evaluating the simulti­mal form of microgap and remodelling. Two three­dimensional CAD models were built (one for each type of connection) in the solidworks software and exported to the ABAQUAS finite elements software, where the in­siliconal studies of the microgap formation were carried out in the implant-pilar interface, when the implant was subjected to c clicked loads.
