# Design and optimization of an energy integration system with renewable energy sources for fruit drying processes in the La Mesa region in Cundinamarca, Colombia

The association ASOMEFRUT that is located in La Mesa, Cundinamarca realizes the production of handle, which presents great losses in the post-harvest and commercial stages. The drying of this handle is presented as an alternative to reduce losses and generate greater income to the members of the association. This document presents the conceptual design and computational modeling of a solar dryer, which integrates different renewable energy sources available in the region to perform a continuous drying of the handle, independent of the environmental conditions of the region. The dryer has an integrating system of the different available sources, which will change from main source of thermal energy depending on the climatic conditions. Likewise, it is foreseen the inclusion of a photovoltaic generation system which will meet the electrical energy requirement of the system and will give it autonomy to the drying. For the evaluation of the model, the integration of renewable energies, which includes a standard day, the warmer day and the coldest day of the year, with which the computational model is evaluated. ly, a photovoltaic generation system is planned to provide the electrical energy requirements of the system, providing autonomy for the drying process. The model evaluation considerations five scenarios in the region, including a standard day, the hottest day, and the coldest day of the year, to assist the computational model. The minimum dry temperature is set at 50 °C. The renewable energy integration system regulated the heat provided by the varying subsystems to ensure this minimum temperature is maintained
