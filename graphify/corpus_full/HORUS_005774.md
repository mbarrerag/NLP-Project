# EFFECT OF CLIMATE CONDITIONS ON SOME PHYSICAL CHEMICAL AND PHYSIOOLOGICAL CHARACTERISTICS IN GROWTH OF SCIRED FRUITS

Contextualization: the climatic conditions are determinant factors of the quality of the fruits at the time of harvest and are defined by the physical and chemical and physiological characteristics of the fruit. Void of knowledge: know the relationship between different variables that can affect the production of plum variety Horvin. to observe the effect of the climatic conditions on some physical and chemical characteristics of the fruits of plum variety Horvin from the quay to the harvest. thermohygrometers were installed in each of the localities located at four different altitudes, in order to obtain the temperature and relative humidity during the observation period; on the other hand, the precipitation was measured with pluviometers installed in each of the localities. The fruits were removed in different branches of the middle part of the trees, in each of the days of study. the climatic conditions had a great influence on the growth and development of the fruit of plum; changes were observed in terms of weight and size of one harvest to another in the four localities studied.
