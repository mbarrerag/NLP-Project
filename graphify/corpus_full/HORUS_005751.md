# EFFECTS OF THE PARTICULAR RESPLAZ OF Wheat HARINA WITH GREEN BANNHARINE ON REOLOGICAL MASS PROPERTIES AND PAN QUALITY PROPERTIES

Evaluating the partial substitution of wheat flour with green banana flour by verifying the quality properties of the bread mold. The green banana flour was characterized in its physicochemical, emplacement and compositional properties. The rheological and pharinographic properties of the masses were evaluated, and the physical, chemical, textureal, color and macrostructural properties were determined by analysis of the images of the breads with substitution of 5%, 10% and 20% with green banana flour. Green banana flour showed a high content of starch and dietary fiber with values of 85.1 g/100 g and 8.6 g/ 100g, respectively. The increase in the percentage of substitution with banana flour of rejection resulted in higher values of G¿ and G¿, and less stability in the masses with respect to the control. No significant differences were observed in the physicochemical properties of the breads with different treatments. To the greater substitution of wheat flour with banana flour (20%) the firmness of the miga, the hardness and the masmaturability of the flour was increased
