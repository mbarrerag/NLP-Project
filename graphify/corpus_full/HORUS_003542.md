# COMPARISON OF SOME KENDALL TAU ESTIMATES FOR BIVARIATED DATA WITH INTERVAL CENSURE.

Bivariate fault data are common in reliability and survival studies, where the estimation of the dependency force is often an important step in data analysis. In literature, correlation coefficients have been established to measure the linear relationship between two variables, but strong non-linear relationships may also exist. Kendall's tau concordance coefficient has become a useful tool for bivariate data analysis, which is used in nonparametric independence tests and as a complementary measure of association. In the analysis of reliability data, there is a phenomenon that occurs when the value of observations is partially known, which is known as censorship. In this work, two methods of estimating Kendall's tau are compared via simulation, one of which is assuming normality in marginal distributions and adjusting them individually, and the other one based on copules (Gaussiana and Clayton), where bivariate data are censorized at interval.
