# THE MAGIC OF INDIGENOUS TOURISM PURACÉ

It is the result of an investigation process in response to the invitation made by the Research Directorate of the National University of Colombia - Manizales Headquarters (dima), and the Research Group Finance and Marketing, in the framework of the project bpin 2014000100061. Is development of a thermocolor technology environment in the Puracé Indigenous Reserve for research and innovation in the use of thermal waters and natural sulfur, aimed at enhancing tourism and well-being, seeking to integrate the diagnosis, conceptualization and the respective recommendations for the implementation of land bridge and strengthening of the tourist attractions of Puracé. In summary, the book The Magic of Indigenous Tourism Puracé shows the result of a consultancy process carried out with the aim of determining the tourism development plan and marketing plan that the municipality of Puracé should have with the tourism product Living the indigenous culture vci-Quiliparzá, to position the indigenous culture coconuco and its natural environment. The reader will be able to find in the first four chapters a theoretical framework in which the development of the strategic planning and planning of the development of markets is concentrated
