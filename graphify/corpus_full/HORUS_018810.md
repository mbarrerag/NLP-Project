# REASON AND DESIGN OF THE CONFERENCE

This book presents the professional and bureaucratic trajectory of Vallecaucano doctor Jorge Bejarano Martínez (1888-1966), Prime Minister ofHigiene in 1947. In order to support Bejarano’s historical character in politics and his role in the process of hygiene in Colombia during the first half of the 20th century, he deepens the political traits of this intellectual based on the analysis of his thinking and his participation in public life for more than thirty years. After consulting multiple archives and national and regional press, a life marked by the effort and, why not, good luck was rebuilt. Bejarano’s participation in national politics was thanks to an an anecdotal situation, various academic and social figures and the willingness to ascend within the Liberal Party. It also helped the existence of a common ideario among the national class of the time: the hygiene of the country. The situation that arose in 2020 with the pandemic allowed the survival of the struggles and of several old enemies of Jorge Bejarano, who fought the factors that made up the Colombian people, according to hundreds of its generation
