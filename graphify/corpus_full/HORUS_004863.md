# FOLD-FOLD SINGULARITY IN A MARKET MODEL

After providing a brief introduction of the theory that frames the smooth, piece-less dynamic systems (PWS PieceWise-Smooth), some of the main bifurcations presented in a mathematical model of the dynamics of a stockless market are shown. PWS systems, although known for some time, have not been extensively studied in the community of differential equations, given the lack of regularity results. Only Filippov, in the previous century, in his book in Russian, translated years later by Springer [1], gives an appropriate theoretical framework. In it, it deals with issues of existence of solutions. However, in the applications to engineering, it is more significant to see the evolution of trajectories, and possible bifurcations versus variation of parameters. The energy market is described through a system of ordinary differential equations defined with smooth-to-part functions, using supply, the ability to produce in development and demand as state variables.
