# Application of EPANET 2 toolkit tool for the modelling of the destination and transport of residual chlorine in water distribution networks as an operational improvement tool.

The EPANET 2 toolkit is a dynamic bookcase that customizes the computational engine of EPANET, adjusting the hydraulic and water quality programming. This work presents the application of the tool EPANET 2 toolkit for the modelling of the destination and transport of residual chlorine under different operating scenarios in the AUACACT network of Ciudad Bolívar in the city of Bogotá. The critical points of residual chlorine were determined, improved scenarios were generated and the minimum application dose of chlorine at the entry of the network was obtained to obtain a residual chlorine of 0.3 mg/L in all nodes. In the scenarios with minor kb and kw were found 5% of critical points and 21% where the maximum amount of chlorine and kw reached the “coal”. The hydraulic improvement did not affect the amount of residual chlorine for the compliance with the standard, the reinjection nor the minimum doses of chlorine application in all operating scenarios. The reinjection did not contribute to the minimal amount of chlorine nodes located in the kcola and kw reached the maximum amount of chlorine. The improvement did not affect the amount of residual chlorine at the same level of the same level of chlorine for compliance with the norm, nor the minimum chlorine application in all the minimum doses in all operating scenarios
