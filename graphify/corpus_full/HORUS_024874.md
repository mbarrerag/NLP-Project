# MODEL OF RULE PLANNING FOR BOGOTA USERS

Bogota, the capital of Colombia, is the world’s first congested city after Bucharest and New York. Bogota has several applications (Apps) that allow the user to plan their journey in each of the different modes of transport, which are based on maps and georeferenced data of the cartographic part as transit variables such as congestion, walking times and way to use for the trip. In recent years for users the variable that determines their planning is not the travel time, but additional factors that the applications do not take into account such as: occupation of the means of transport, stops and portals, operation of the infrastructure, environmental conditions, citizen safety among others. The aim of the research work is to establish a model that allows the different road actors to carry out the planning of the routes according to their needs and depending on the real time of travel. The needs of the users will be determined by means of surveys in field to obtain the additional travel times of each of the variables found.
