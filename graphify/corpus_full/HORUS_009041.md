# NICO HYDROTALCITES WASHCOATING PROCESS (HT) FOR FECRALLOY MONOLITS MANUFACTURING

Within the processes of scaling and formulation of technical catalysts, the use of systems shaped by catalytic reactions contributes to: i) overcoming the limitations in heat and mass transfer presented by the catalysts in powder, ii) avoiding the drop of pressure in the reactors by their high geometric surface area per unit of volume and its large open front area, iii) increasing their mechanical stability and iv) increasing thermal inertia within the industrial reactors [1]. Currently the monoliths type ¿honeycombs are the most used support to overcome these problems [2,3]. These monoliths are structures of panal type with many small channels that extend axially through the structure. Monoliths can be ceramic extrusions or sets of corrugated metal sheets, coated with an inorganic oxide layer, called the powder layer of the solvent [1]. The monoliths metal monoliths are widely used by requiring less heat to heat themselves, presenting less specific heat capacity and greater thermal conductivity than the optimal systems.
