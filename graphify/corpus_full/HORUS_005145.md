# Factors associated with adequate levels of knowledge about type 2 diabetes mellitus in patients from health service networks in Bogotá, D.C., Colombia

One of the factors that influence the clinical evolution of patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (DMT2) is their level of knowledge about their condition. Little is known about the level of knowledge about DMT2 in Colombian patients and about the factors associated with that level. Identifying the factors associated with having an adequate level of knowledge about DMT2 in patients with this diagnosis attended in outpatient health services in Bogotá, D.C., Colombia. Cross-sectional study performed in 1068 adults with DMT2 attended in 2021 in outpatient services of 53 health institutions belonging to four health networks in Bogotá, D.C. From one case randomly selected in each institution, consecutive patients were chosen to conduct a telephone survey. Participants were applied a questionnaire with 9 questions about DMT2 (6 questions were validated due to their relationship with the age of diagnosis (in years) or the levels of HbA1c). The participants were defined as appropriate level of knowledge to respond correctly to 4 of the 6 validated questions.
