# Teaching of the concept "matter" from experimentation, for students of the fifth grade of primary basic.

In this paper, a series of experimental guides were designed and implemented, following the five moments of the active learning model, with students from the fifth grade at the Tzyon school in Neiva (Huila). The objective of the research is to contribute to the concept of matter, counteracting the traditional teaching of Chemistry that still persists in the classroom, reduced to learning formulas, leading students to lose interest in science, which is undoubtedly a fundamental aspect for the development of society. The research begins with exploratory activity (pretest), in order to identify the previous knowledge of the students; then, experimental activities, designed with materials from the environment, are planned to put into practice the phenomena of the subject. Finally, the questionnaire (postest), was applied to compare the learnings acquired by students in this way measure the impact and effectiveness of the experimental guides. It is important to emphasize that the implementation of the applied strategy contributes to better results in the classroom, maintaining a greater motivation in the learning of the student
