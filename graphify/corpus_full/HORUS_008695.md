# PHITOCHEMICALS IN DIETHOTHERAPEUTIC PRECRIPTION: WE TAKE A TEA

There are records of tea consumption between 4,000 and 5,000 years ago in China. Historically, its consumption is associated with the promotion of health, prevention and treatment of diseases and cerimonies (social, religious and healing). Tea is one of the three main drinks in the world (along with coffee and coconut) and low cost. Plants used to prepare teas for therapeutic purposes are considered phytocomplex, because they contain several active compounds that generate different beneficial effects for human health, dentre they are the properties of ant inflammatory, antioxidant, antitumor, antidiabetic, antiviral, antimicrobial, among others. These effects are generally conferred by the presence of apigenin, catechenes, quercitin.
