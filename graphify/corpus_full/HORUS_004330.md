# Resist the Limit. Repertoires of the skills of the societies Cuiba, Sikuani and Piapoco of the Easter Reserve in the Plains of Orinoco

This research carries out a historical-ethnographic reconstruction of the indigenous societies of the eastern Colombian Llans from the study of the process of formation of the indigenous reserve La Pascua, located in the Department of the Vichada in the Llanos of the middle Orinoco basin. From a collaboration of more than 10 years with the communities of the Resguardo, from a long field work and from the construction of an inter-ethnic and regional approach, the strategies that the societies Cuiba, Sikuani and Piapoco (families Guahibo and Arawak) used to resist the different social and ecological limits imposed by the extension of the borders of colonization. The use of cartographic tools and spatial analysis, combined with the study of the oral history, has made it possible to see that the indigenous families that currently make up the Resguardo La Pascua used a wide repertoire of adaptation skills within the nomadism-sedentarism spectrum.
