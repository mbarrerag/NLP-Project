# ALTERNATIVE FUELS AND THEIR INFLUENCE ON PHASE FORMATION IN CLINKER

The challenge facing the cement industry to achieve carbon neutrality in its operations by 2050 is closer and closer, and with it, efforts to find impact measures to achieve this goal are intensifying. Two of the main alternatives to achieve this goal are the reduction of the clinker factor and the use of alternative fuels. Municipal solid waste, hydrogen, biochar, plasma, among other alternative fuels for their implementation, however, there is one factor within their implementation that has not been detail and that is of utmost importance: the effect of the use of alternative fuels on the development cementitious phases in the clinker. Variations in the thermodynamics of the clinkering kilns resulting implementation of these alternative fuels can affect the reaction kinetics of the clinker resulting in formation of polymorphs of the alite and the belite of different reactivity, affecting the quality of the thus possibly compromising the final performance of the cement if measures are not taken during thus disturbing the desired reduction of the carbon footprint. The following paper engages in a discussion based on research reported in the literature about the effect of the implementation of fuels in the clinker kiln, their effect on the development of phases in the clinker and their impact on CO2 reduction based on the decrease in the clinker factor that can be affected
