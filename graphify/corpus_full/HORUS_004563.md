# Multifunctionality of agriculture and common goods in the field of property

The research focuses on the multifunctionality of agriculture, a key theme in the rural and socio-economic field of the country. This concept highlights the ability of agriculture to perform various functions beyond food production, addressing environmental aspects, rural development and cultural diversity. The relationship between multifunctionality, water management and knowledge becomes crucial in the context of post-conflict in Colombia, where agriculture plays a fundamental role in peacebuilding and sustainable development. The research was developed in six areas in the co-ordination of Puerto Venus, Nariño and raised the identification of the multifunctionality perceived by the family unit, as well as the recognition of environmental and socio-economic functions.
