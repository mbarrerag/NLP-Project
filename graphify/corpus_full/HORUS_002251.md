# Atelopus marinkellei Cochran and Goin, 1970 (Amphibia, Anura, Bufonidae): after 20 years, the rediscovery of a Critically Endangered species in the páramo of Siscunsí, a Neotropical high-mountain ecosystem

We report a new record of the harlequin toad Atelopus marinkellei Cochran & Goin, 1970, a Critically Endangered species endemic to Colombia. A single individual was observed in the Siscunsí páramo, after two decades without confirmed sightings. The finding was made by community park rangers during rou-tine monitoring. This observation reinforces the importance of community-based conservation and system-atic habitat monitoring to protect this species, which may persist in a highly localized remnant population
