# EFFECTS ON THE HEALTH OF LATIN AMERICAN AGRICULTURERS EXPOSED TO PLAGUICIDES: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW 19912018

Pesticides are widely used by humans, in order to improve crop productivity, minimize the possibility of pests and supply food to a population; their deliberate use by workers has favoured the increase of poisonings and diseases, especially in the working environment. The objective of this research was to identify the effects on the health of Latin American farmers exposed to pesticides. A search was carried out for scientific literature in the period 1991 to 2018, in databases such as SciELO.org, the Virtual Library in Health and Google Scholar. It was evidenced that exposure to pesticides causes multisystemic affectation, with predominance in genetic susceptibility and DNA damage (chromosomal aberrations, micronuclees, carirexis, cariolisis, condensation of chromatin and even alterations in proinflammatory markers), psychiatric disorders, neuroconductual syndromes, lower limb poll Europeia, headache, decreased vision and hearing, alteration in thyroid function among others, related to long-term exposures.
