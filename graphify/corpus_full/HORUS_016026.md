# Assembly of the genome of Leuconostoc mesenteroides IBUN 91.2.98. by means of bioinformatics tools.

Despite the importance of dextrane in several industrial applications and the need to massify its production, the mechanism of expression and regulation of dextransacarases in producing strains such as Leuconostoc mesenteroides strain IBUN 91.2.98 is not well documented. The development of second generation sequencing methods and the use of bioinformatics tools will allow the sequencing, assembly and evaluation of complete genomes at a relatively low cost guided by a reference genome of L. mesenteroides subsp. mesenteroids ATCC 8293, to assist the assembly process. Sequencing produced a total of 1.47 Gb of raw data, which after trimming and quality control generated 1.40 Gb (7.78 X depth) that were used for assembly. An assembly was obtained for Leuconostoc mesenteroides strain IBUN 91.2.98 of 2,064 Mpb length. The length of the assembly represented 85% of the estimated size of the reference genome of L. mesenterosteroids subsp. 82 sensenoides ATsp.senteroids
