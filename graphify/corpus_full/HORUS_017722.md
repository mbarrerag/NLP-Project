# AUTOMATIC STRUCTURE OF MAINTENANCE REGISTERS

The lack of relevant information for decision-making is one of the major problems faced by the maintenance departments. This article proposes a new automatic method to contribute to the solution of this problem by extracting relevant information from the records that are taken from the maintenance activities carried out to the teams. Given the nature of the data, unstructured text with technical language, it is proposed to implement different representations (word bag, reverse document frequency-term frequency, Fasttext and Doc2vec) for machine learning models that perform the structuring of the relevant information contained in the documents. In the search for the model of better performance, the models of support vectors machine, random forest, Gaussian naïve bays and gradient enhancers trees were compared. The models were applied to data of a yellow machinery sales and rental company. 15 mount loaders of 3 different models and four independent variables were considered in which information is extracted: typology, fault, final state and system.
