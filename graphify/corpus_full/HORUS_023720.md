# THE FRENCH INFLUENCE OF THE COLOMBIAN CONSTITUTIONAL SYSTEM FOR THE TWENTY-FIRST CENTURY AND IN THE CONSTRUCTIONAL NATIONAL ASSEMBLY DEBATE OF 1991

It is now accepted that the Colombian legal system has been influenced by other systems belonging to the two great families or systems of law in the West: the Roman-German or civil law system and the Anglo-Saxon or common law system. However, the traceable influence of these two families in Colombia is not uniform. In certain periods of history some ideas typical of the Roman-Germanic system have been predominant. Specifically, some elements of French constitutionalism dominated the national panorama well into the twentieth century. However, at the end of that century and beginning of the twenty-first century some common law ideas and institutions (specifically of American law) were strongly installed in the Colombian legal system. Given this context, the following question arises: what was the influence of French constitutionalism in the debates prior to the issuance of the 1991 Constitution?
