# Selection of the most appropriate CRM information system, in a financial sector company in Colombia, using multicriteria analysis methodologies. Case study

The objective of this study is to propose a methodological framework for the optimal selection of systems of Customer Relations Management (CRM) that is in line with the specific needs and requirements of an entity belonging to the financial sector in Colombia. This proposal is based on the comprehensive review of the relevant literature and on the precise identification of the requirements and particularities of the company in question. The proposed model is based on a multi-criteria analysis approach, which allows systematic structuring of the criteria relevant to the evaluation of the different alternatives of CRM available in the current market. This approach guarantees a comprehensive assessment that covers both technical and operational, financial and strategic aspects, thus ensuring the selection of the CRM system that is best suited to the specific needs and objectives of the company. By integrating the findings obtained in the review of the literature with the particular needs and expectations of the financial entity in question, the proposed model provides a solid and objective guide for the decision-making of the CRM system selection, thus contributing to the efficiency and effectiveness of the management activities of relations with clients in the context of the Colombian financial sector. The methodology used in this academic study focuses on the structure and application of the CRM system and the analysis. This identification is carried out in an environment characterized by regulatory and market changes that demand a redefinition of the focus in the management of customer relations. The result of this analysis is the obtaining of a hierarchical model that facilitates the evaluation of CRM alternatives, allowing the choice of the most suitable system for the company concerned, considering both its particularities and the specific market conditions.
