# SAFE NUTRITION IN THE INTERAL DOMICILIARY NUTRITION SAFE MANAGEMENT OF HOME INTERAL NUTRITION

ABSTRACTIntroduction: An analysis of patient safety practices was carried out in nutritional care in home health institutions (Homecare).Methods: a systematic review of the literature was carried out. To this end, a search was made for articles through the databases PubMed®, ScienceDirect® and SciELO®, published in the last 10 years (June 1, 2010 – June 1, 2020) in English, Spanish and Portuguese that included topics related to patient safety. Conclusions and recommendations:Integer nutrition in the home should aim to maintain or improve the nutritional status, and contribute to an adequate clinical evolution of patients.The identified recommendations correspond to indications related to processes, technology management, microbiological quality and nutritional composition of commercial nutritional formulas versus diets made at home as criteria to ensure patient safety
