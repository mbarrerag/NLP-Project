# Characterization of thoracic radiological findings in patients diagnosed with COVID-19 in the paediatric population of the Fundación Hospital de la Misericordia during the period March to December 2020.

On December 12, 2019, in the city of Wuhan (People's Republic of China), a series of cases of viral pneumonia in humans began to present, some of which evolved to critical conditions, due to a new viral strain of Coronavirus called (SARS-CoV2) that comes from a zoonotic pathogen. Radiological findings may be useful in early screening in patients with suspected COVID-19, and may also assist in the assessment of severity and extent of the disease. Chest imaging plays a very important role in the evaluation in paediatric patients with COVID19. Chest X-ray is highlighted, which is a simple, affordable and used test for the approach of patients with respiratory symptoms. The objective of this study is to characterize and determine whether there is a relationship between radiological findings in chest imaging in paediatric patients with diagnosis of Covid-19 and clinical outcomes such as hospitalization, prolonged hospital stay and mortality. An analytical retrospective observational study was performed where patients with positive evidence for COVID-19 were found and that they had a combined respiratory picture of COVID in March and December 19 was performed
