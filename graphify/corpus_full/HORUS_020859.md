# ♪ Spinning Paths ♪

The Hilando Caminos project, part of the second phase of the project Technical Skills in Electricity and Electronics -Gypses of Hope- which was the beneficiary of the resources of Solidary Extension in 2017. Originally, the project was born as an initiative promoted by the young people of the Comuna Ciudadela del Norte, seeking to bet on the transformation of the territory through the generation of spaces for the teaching of science, facing situations of inequality present in the commune. Faced with these social inequalities, it moved the fact of seeing children and young people who at an early age consume psychoactive substances. Starting from this situation, the corporation began to seek alternatives to this problem and began to develop activities with the population in order to occupy their free time, putting it in place to build a different commune and to mobilize actions and thoughts of change. During the course of execution of the initiative, since 2016, the project MES Electronics has also been supported by the support and support of several public and private entities, as are the Tec Group (since 2020), the Universidad Nacional de Colombia (through the Convocatoria Nacional de insolaria Ciudad 2017, the project of the Spanish Chis, the technical skills in the family. between the social and scientific aspects, in order to contribute to the reduction of inequalities that these populations live and to produce knowledge from the needs of them. It is important to emphasize that in this new phase the project is intended to socially innovate through the articulation between the STEAM methodology with a focus on Electricity and Electronics, Critical Thinking and Popular Education, and so from Hilando Caminos to be able to contribute to the development of communities and their social inclusion from strategic alliances between the population, the academic community and private enterprise. To conclude it is necessary to mention that the project is aligned with the Sustainable Development Goals 2015-2030 in terms of quality education and reduction of inequalities, since this is a population vulnerable to education and also with the PLEI of the National University, re-establishing the idea of Benavides (2020), when Colombia is facing a major delay in terms of science, technology and research, so to achieve that social well-being we must work from the communities through diverse strategies that promote the social inclusion of populations at risk of vulnerability and place for their sustainable development
