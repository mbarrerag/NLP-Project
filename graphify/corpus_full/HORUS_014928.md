# Exposure to atmospheric pollutants at the street level: comparison in different modes of transport and with reduction strategies.

The study of air quality in the microscale is fundamental since it allows to evaluate the exposure to pollutants in addition to the quantification of inventories of emissions. Among the most important pollutants known as criterion are the material particulate less than 2,5 micrometers (PM2.5, English acronym), gases: oxides of nitrogen (NOx) and carbon monoxide (CO) to which this work seeks to compare the levels of exposure of users at the street level of different modes of transport. Simulation was used in a software of computational dynamics of fluids (CFD) where concentrations of pollutants in the domain were obtained, then these concentrations were compared with the levels of exposure of users at the street level of different modes of transport and the process was repeated with strategies of reduction of pollution. In emissions, the articulated and biarticulated vehicle grouping of buses (transMilenium fast transit (TM) were found to be the highest contribution of PM2.5 and the light modes of transport was the most of contribution of NOx and CO.
