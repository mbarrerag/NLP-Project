# LESSONS IN AULA: RUPTURAS, DIALOGUES AND WORLDS

In this first volume we are facing a compendium of proposals that have succeeded in disrupting the strict rigor of the school. We will see the role of memories and experiences in learning to read and write, the presence of deaf literature as an act of communication, the reading that includes who is different, or has other forms of learning and teaching for teachers. There is also present, the infinite richness of the versions of a classic or poetic text, the role of the question in writing, critical thinking and the autonomy of existing in the face of the ways in which the teachers read, the refiguration of critical thinking in reading, the games with Divine Comedy in the contemporary context, the role of social conditions in the reading and writing process, the mediation of literature to stimulate writing, the passage from contemplation to expression in the acts of reading and writing
