# Participation of families in education of students with disabilities: challenges and opportunities for inclusion and equity

In Colombia, Decree 1421 of 2017 recognizes the family as an essential actor in the effective participation of students with disabilities. It offers a reflection on the barriers that families face in trying to comply with the obligations set out in this regulation, and proposes strategies to increase their involvement. Attitudinal, physical, communication and social barriers faced by families hinder the success of children and adolescents with disabilities in school. Likewise, the low recognition of linguistic diversity, geography and armed conflict minimize the presence of students with disabilities and their families in school environments in some regions of the country. Moreover, the one-way approach focused on student limitations makes it difficult to participate and fulfil school obligations in some educational establishments. The generation of policies with a territorial approach that visualize regional needs and promote linguistic and cultural differences, as well as awareness-raising, psychosocial support, collaborative work and the creation of empowerment spaces, from a rights perspective, can foster the role of families in achieving inclusion and equity in education
