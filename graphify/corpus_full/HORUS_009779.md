# STRUCTURAL CONFIGURATION OF BASAMENTS IN THE OCCIDENT OF ANTIOCIA AND ITS INFLUENCE IN THE DEPOSITION OF AMAGA TRAINING

The Amagá Formation is a continental silicyclastic sequence that was deposited in river environments during the cenozoic (Eocene-Oligocene) on a igneous metamorphic cretaceous and pre-creatic base deeply deformed by the Romeral fault system. The complex tectonic structuring controlled the deposition of the Amagá Formation in regimes of subsequently reactivated traction basins. The Amagá Formation itself is deformed as the Combia Mio-Pliocene Formation was demonstrating extensive tectonic activity throughout the Cenozoic. By the time Emile Grosse published the Antioquia Carboniferous Tertiary, in the decade of the 20th century last, the models of geological evolution were fundamentally different from what is understood today. On the other hand, modern techniques in istopia and geochemistry were only incipiently developed.
