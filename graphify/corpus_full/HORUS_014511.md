# Design of information systems support for the treatment of fertilization in tropical crops.

This research aims to design an information system for the interpretation of physical and chemical soil analysis, with interaction to various databases, such that, allowing to assign a plan of fertilization in tropical crops; thus, and based on its use, give agility to the diagnosis and directive of action. Therefore, the cassava cultivation was used as a basis, in which its phenology, yield and nutrient requirements are specified in the soil. The support software interacts with various databases: soil-water plant. The algorithm, flow diagram and pseudocode, supported by different programming languages: HTML, PHP and JavaScript, hosted on an application server, with a MySQL database system; developed in a web environment and hosted on an online application server. The system is divided into four modules: 1. Soil analysis; 2. Need for fertilization; 3. Crop database (in progress) and 4. Tools and reports. Tests: functional and non-functional: the first one is reviewed that the software works smoothly and the second provides reliability, security, ambitability and ease of use.
