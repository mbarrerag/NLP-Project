# Factors associated with admission to ICU in patients with IRAG by SARS CoV-2 during the pandemic in Colombian hospitals.

Patients with COVID-19 present a variable clinical spectrum and their severity is influenced by individual risk factors. Identifying them in a timely manner allows for predicting serious forms of disease. To determine the factors associated with admission to the intensive care unit (ICU) COVI with a variable clinical spectrum and its severity is influenced by individual risk factors. Identifying them in a timely manner allows for predicting serious forms of the disease. To determine the factors associated with admission to the intensive care unit (ICU) in patients hospitalized by COVID-19 during the first 2 waves of the epidemic in Colombia. Multicentric, retrospective cohort study in patients hospitalized by COVID-19 in institutions providing health care services in Bogotá and Girardot, Colombia. Adults with confirmed diagnosis from March 2020 to January 2021 were included.
