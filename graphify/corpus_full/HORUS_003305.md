# CHAPTER 2: PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION AND TERRITORIAL PEACE FROM GOVERNANCE IN TERRITORIES

In order to contribute to inclusive rural development and to propose recommendations of public policies in this regard, this chapter proposes a proposal for productive inclusion and territorial peace from governance, considering the decisions made by producers and peasants, and their practices of agrotechnological management. This proposal is addressed from the systemic and territorial approaches, based on the concept of territory, understood as a geographical space that constitutes a cultural container, differentiated from the technological geographic space (Fals Borda, 2000). This approach to the territory from culture, allows to raise governance based on the factual characteristics of the inhabitants in their territories, which is based on their knowledge-making that originates from their experience in the nature that they live, in the social fabric, in the production and economic relations that they establish, this is the interaction culture-nature as a complex adaptive system. Thus, the governance is proportional to the domain of their means of production land, work, capital and knowledge that underpins this knowledge, that allows them to establish autonomy and negotiating relations, not of submetimiento, with the markets, with other territories
