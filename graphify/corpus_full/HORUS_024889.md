# ¿PHOTON­MEDIATED DIPOLE­DIPOLE INTERACTIONS IN DIRAC CONE PHOTONIC CRYSTAL SLABS¿

Dirac energy dispersions in photonic crystals have shown to lead to many exciting phenomena. Some of these are the possibility of obtaining power-law, decoherence-free, photon-mediated interactions between quantum emitters through the photon interaction in such a photonic structure. However, the prediction of these phenomena has been made restricting the emitter position to highsymmetry points of the unit cell in realistic structures. Here, we develop a theory of dipole radiation near photonic Dirac points in photonic crystal slabs using a combination between the Guided-Mode Expansion method and the k · p approximation. This approach allows us to compute the effective photon-mediated dipole-dipole interactions inside the whole unit cell. We find several results which escaped previous studies, such as finding positions that: first, maximize the interactions between emitters; second, increase the range interactions, displaying a trade-off between strength and range; and third, change their nature from being purely coherent to incoherent interactions. Our results significantly improve the knowledge of Dirac light-matter interfaces and can serve as guidance for future experimental designs
