# WATER BACCTERIES: EXPLORATION OF THEIR RELEVANCE FOR PUBLIC HEALTH AND BIOTECHNOLOGY POINT.

In Colombia, some cities have waste water treatment systems that include primary treatment with desarination and screening, followed by anaerobic and optional lagoons in a second phase, and tertiary treatment in maturation gaps. However, reports indicate deficiencies in the operation and maintenance of these plants, such as the accumulation of floating material, the poor state of the sludge drying beds and the lack of laboratory analysis to monitor essential physicochemical and microbiological variables. These deficiencies affect the quality of partially treated discharges, representing a risk for communities that supply these resources. The aim of this research was to identify bacterial communities present in treated wastewater that are discharged into the Cesar river. This longitudinal qualitative study collected samples of a treatment plant in Valledupar during different seasons. The bacterial communities were identified by cultivation and isolation in various media. The aim of this research was to identify the bacterial communities present in the treated wastewater that are being converted to the Cesar river.
