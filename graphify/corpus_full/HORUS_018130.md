# Development of a teaching box as a pedagogical tool for learning bacterial morphology.

The main objective of this research was to develop a didactic box as a pedagogical tool for learning bacterial morphology. To this end, the concept of micro-organism cells and their associations in colonies was identified in the classroom, field outputs and laboratory practices were implemented for the collection, isolation and morphological identification of bacteria. In addition, a CIDACTIC CAB was designed for the sampling of bacteria in the field and the relevance of the didactic box was evaluated in strengthening the learning of bacterial morphology. The design of this pedagogical proposal was framed in a qualitative research methodology, using research-action as a tool, a proposal that was implemented with 17 students from the District Educational Institution Colegio Nuevo Horizon headquarters Torca. From the results, it was possible to determine that the applicability of the Educational Fund not only allowed the sampling of bacteria, but also made social interaction possible, motivating the attention of the students to develop more significant learning processes appropriate to microbiological contents and studying bacteria through knowledge in context, strengthening the previous learning with those acquired during each session
