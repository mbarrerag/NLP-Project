# On the way out, we see each other. An analysis of practices associated with school violations.

This research has as main objective to analyze the practices of riñas, surproductos and consumption in the school using the category of school contraventions from a sociopedagogical framework. The work was developed in a public educational institution of the municipality of Zipaquirá which implements the flexible educational model Walk Secondary. Using a qualitative ethnographic approach and through surveys, observation, semistructured interviews and interviews at depth show as in the reproduction of practices associated with school contraventions influence various contexts as family, neighborhood, school and friendship groups; also that these actions relate factors such as emotion, fear, response to situations of impotence and boredom; I state how this phenomenon works for the achievement of status among students and as a defense mechanism, sometimes mixing elements of school contracultura and youth subjetivity. At the same time, evidencia como las redes sociales y lo virtual acquire relevance in the study of the phenomenon of school violence.
