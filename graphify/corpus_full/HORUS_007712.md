# Bito Hut

This project aims to design and build the prototype of a modular automated self-cultivation system for edible fungi, in response to the need for access to fresh and healthy foods. Fungal autoculture is an emerging market; however, in its traditional model of crop bags presents difficulty in the performance, durability and conditioning of an adequate microclimate
