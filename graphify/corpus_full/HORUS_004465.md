# Is it a mythic heresy?

The mystical dimension of Heracliteal philosophy has always been the object of fascination not only because it is considered an interesting terrain for exploring the links that the Ephesians philosopher had with the rites and religions of his time, but also because it is interesting when it comes to exploring the points of confluence that exist between philosophical and religious thought in general. However, the definition of what is mysticism and who is fit the appellation of "mystical" has always been at stake. In the present lines I propose to explore a chapter of Heraclitus reception in India where, precisely, the topic of debate is whether Heraclitus is or not a mystic, what can be understood by it and what are the implications for his thought
