# Causal agent of powdery mildew on Spathodea campanulata P. Beauv. in Antioquia, Colombia

The powdery mildiú is a disease caused by plant-bound pathogens (Erysiphaceae), with a cosmopolitan distribution, affect more than 10,000 plant species. The study of these pathogens is incipient in Colombia and in species such as Spathodea campanulata P. Beauv (Bignoniaceae), native of Africa and introduced in Colombia as ornamental, for sombre, close alive and as food for the fauna, is affected by this group of fungi and the agent causing this disease is unknown. The objective of this work has been to identify morphologically and molecularly the species of milliú powderso that affects S. campanulata in the campus the Volador of the National University of Colombia headquarters Medellín, for this purpose, samples of leaves with signs and symptoms of the disease, the sample was deposited in the Micrological Museum of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia headquarters Medellín (MMUNM), the micropreparades of the software ZEN, and the micro-organisms were made in the micropreparades in the lactoglycerina and blue of the lactolycerina for morphological analysis, using a stereomycros, the micro The morphological and molecular results coincide with the species such as Phyllactinia obclavata (Wakef.) U. Braun & S. Takam. This is a new record of this pathogen for Colombia that increases the geographical distribution of the fungus and molecularly confirms the morphological findings obtained on the same host in Panama
