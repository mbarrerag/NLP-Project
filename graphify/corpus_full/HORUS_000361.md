# Dissociating the City with Childhood: A Proposal from the Pentaletics of Space in Manizales, Colombia

In the context of the right to the city, this article proposes a new urban theory, the pentalectic of space, together with an applied methodology: the laboratory of landscape and territorial futuristicization. This theoretical-methodological proposal, used in a case of research with children, seeks that they explore, imagine, design and transform the urban spaces they inhabit, as a community exercise to put into practice their right to participate in the collective construction of the city. In this sense, the implementation of the analytical proposal with children of the San José commune in Manizales (Colombia), affected by a failed macro-project of urban renewal initiated in 2008, is presented. Among the results are the openness of children to share their memories, experiences, feelings and proposals, managing to disown other future community-based futures that include the kindergartens in the commune. In conclusion, the article contributes to the territorial studies a theory on urban spatiality, also highlights the relevance of incorporating the visions and expectations of childhood in the demands for the right to the city and spatial justice
