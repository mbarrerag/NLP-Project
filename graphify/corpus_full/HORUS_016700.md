# Landscape X-ray: temporary spatial reconfigurations of the landscape of El Peñol and Guatapé

This work explores hidden forms of the landscape of the El Peñol-Guatapé dam, which are revealed from a multiplicity of practices associated with tourism and mass consumption.From artistic practices and through the operational metaphor of radiography the landscape, some cultural dynamics are investigated that pose the conflict between the ideal of landscape and the landscape practiced, appropriate and modified by the economic dynamics that radically reconfigure the lifestyles of the root inhabitants. The research/creation process that is supported by the main postulates of the non-representational theory is ordered conceptually by the hermeneutic route, organized in three chapters: the first, a context in which it is analyzed....The second, resources with which the changes and the tactics of appropriation that the root make of the landscape are interpreted and that result in what we have called the “pasajes de oportunidad”. of research/creation that leads on the postulated principles of the non representative theory, is organized conceptually by the hermeneutical route, derived in three chapters: the first, a context that is analyzed....as well as the tactics of acceptance of the locals and give way to what we have streetd “landsscapes of opportunity”.
