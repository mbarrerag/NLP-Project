# Strengthening capacity for PAULONTOLOGIC AND PALEOCEANOGRAPHY RESEARCH AND PALEOCEANOGRAPHY IN THE LABCAT

This proposal aims to promote teaching and research in the laboratory of Environmental and Earth Sciences through the proper management of paleontology collections and microfossils of foraminifers. It tries to take advantage of the capabilities acquired over 20 years of working with this group of microfossils, to lay the foundations of collections that can be registered at the national level. And promote the knowledge of this group of microfossils through an extension course
