# BLENDED LEARNING: A CENTRONIC REVIEW

There are many tools in education today and new strategies such as blended learning are needed to support new educational contexts. However, there is a lack of scientific literature that brings together the different contributions. This study shows in a chronological way the conceptual evolution of blended learning. The most relevant articles were identified using the metaphor of the science tree from a consultation in Scopus. The results show an improvement in academic results in the different areas. In the pandemic caused by COVID 19, the need was generated in the educational field to seek new teaching alternatives using available tools and technologies, including the blend learning.
