# Framework for the implementation of autonomous passenger vehicles in cities and metropolitan areas of Colombia.

The Autonomous Vehicles represent a very important technological advance for the development of transport in the big cities. To characterize this technology, the Society of Automotive Engineers (The Society of Automotive Engineers ~ SAE) has defined levels of autonomy, where level 0 refers to a fully manual vehicle, level 1 a vehicle that has certain automatic assistance, and autonomy increases at each level to reach the 5 that describes a fully autonomous vehicle. Several countries have made efforts to incorporate these innovations in their territory, through the creation of laws and the realization of several pilots. With these experiences, the main challenges that a country like Colombia could have to carry out a plan of implementation of autonomous vehicles, among them the deficiencies in the road infrastructure and the difficulty to generate confidence by users. In this work of addressing different relevant aspects to take into account to promote the use of autonomous vehicles in Colombia, mainly in public transport. It starts with a normative count compared between some pioneer countries in the subject, then generalities on the autonomous vehicles are displayed and several problematic around their future incorporation in the country, to the end to investigate by the acceptance of the autonomous vehicles
