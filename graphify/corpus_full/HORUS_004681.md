# Formal and informal territorial occupation and access to water as a public service in Soacha 2000-2022

This document seeks to explain how the increase of the territorial occupation with residential use of formal and informal type implies dynamics of differentiated access to water and in turn generates difficulties for the management of the water resource in the urban area of the municipality of Soacha. Within the framework of the definition of UN-Habitat (2021) on adequate housing, one of the characteristics to consider adequate is the availability of services, in this way the housing conditions mediated by the features of a formal or informal development, point out that this feature at the beginning of the processes of informal occupation is precarious or nonexistent. In this sense, the form of access to water as a public service, varies from the access to housing of formal or informal type.
