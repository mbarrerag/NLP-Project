# The role of capability and professional marketing control in business results

--Management nowadays is increasingly demanding proof that marketing decisions affect business results. This paper explores the link between formal and informal marketing controls and their effects on non-financial and financial results. A cross-sectional survey was sent to 301 marketing managers to analyse the relationship between the constructs based on a partial least squares structural equation model, supported by the Smart PLS 4 software. This research revealed the mediating role of professional controls to explain how capability controls have effects on financial results. Further, it exists evidence to probe the positive influence of capability control on non-financial results
