# DETERMINATION OF XENOBIOTICS COMPOSES AND POSSIBLE METABOLISATION PRODUCTS IN PIÑAS COMMERCIALIZED IN THE CITY OF CALI SANTIAGO

Agricultural toxic products derived from the use of pesticides to prevent, eliminate or reduce unwanted pests, both plant and animal, may affect the production, processing, storage, transport or marketing of food, wood or feed. National and international legislation requires food safety requirements for the marketing of fruit products, considering the possible presence of pesticide residues, which represent risks to animal and human health, causing poisoning and damage to the nervous system, among others. Pineapple is one of the main export and consumption products in the country, prioritized as one of the productive chains of the fruit subsector in the Valle del Cauca in the General Regalías System (SGR) between 2017 and 2019.
