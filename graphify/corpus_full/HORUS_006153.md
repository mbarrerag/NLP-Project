# BIODIGISTERIAL VEGETAL RESIDUE TRANSFORMATION IN A UNIVERSITY HIGHER EDUCATION INSTITUTION

The results of the research related to the transformation of organic residues, through biodigestoral bales, were presented below. 5 bales of approximately 1m3 were assembled with the following residues: No0 (plant material), No1 (plant material+composting inoculum), No2 (vegetal material+genesis®), No3 (vegetal material+ goat's estiércol), and No4 (vegetal material+ coffee residues). These were performed through non-probabilistic sampling for convenience, due to the limited supply of residues in the time of the research (Pandemia). They were constructed with a compaction system of 1t/m3 and were monitored for 20 weeks, measuring height and temperature; samples were taken to determine the physicochemical composition and organisms of the resulting material. It was found that the No0 bale was the one that presented the best behavior in terms of height followed by the Papaa 1, 2, 3 and 4, respectively. Papa No4 presented better C/N ratio, due to the high concentrations of organic carbon of the coffee. It was found that the resulting material was not organic fertilizer for the organic elements, according to NTC 5167
