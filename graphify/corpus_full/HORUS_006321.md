# : Wisdom and Enigma in the Doctrine of Heraclitus of Ephesus

The wisdom of Heraclitus is taxed by the mystical cults of Greek antiquity, but it is strained in the philosophical future regarding the cultural and religious tradition. The contents and principles of the mystical cults transmitted through enigmas, as well as the mythological representations, serve the sage of Ephesus as the foundation for formulating his own doctrine. The logos or the truth that Heraclitus discovers as “universal reason”, no longer represents an exclusivity in revelation as it occurred in the initiatic cults (orphics and eleusinos), but the logos are the common and available to all men who want to understand the nature of all things. . The wisdom of Heraclitus is unknown to the mystery cultures, but it stretches for in philosophical anticipation, divergent tradition of Greece.
