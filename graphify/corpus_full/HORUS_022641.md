# POPULARY WOMEN AND SOCIAL REPRODUCTION: KEY TO RESPENSE WORK IN LATIN AMERICA

In the current situation, social mobilizations with a strong role for women are more and more frecuent. Marches throughout the globe denounce the extermination of life, while the interests of capital are defended by governments. Women and their children, who suffer firsthand the effects of neoliberal policies, but it is also they who, based on the study of the logic of the system that exploits, oppresses and violates them, offer us elements of analysis to transform society. Women claim their role as political subjects and indispensable workers, despite the historical invisibility of their work both for the functioning of the capitalist system, and for social reproduction. They have in their hands the strongest and most radical power of transformation of society, because on them depends the production and reproduction of the most precious good for the capitalist system: the labor force (Fortunati, 2019).Now more than ever they are aware that they move the world and shout it this way
