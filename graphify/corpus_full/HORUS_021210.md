# REDVAR- Waste recovery network

The Network of Valorization of Wastes _x0096_ REDVAR is composed of teachers of 7 headquarters (Amazonia, Bogotá, La Paz, Medellín, Manizales, Orinoquia, Palmira), three international institutions (Universidad de Sao Paulo de Brasil, Universidad de Guanajuato de México, Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María de Chile), two national institutions ( Politecnico Colombiano Jaime Isaza Cadavid and Tecnológico de Antioquia Institución Universitaria), two environmental corporations (CORANTIOQUIA AND CORNARE), three companies (Biomasnesest, ASEI and Faro Tecnológico), 11 research groups of the participating headquarters, together with the research groups of the participating external institutions. It also has the participation of the Environmental Management Systems of some headquarters and the idea is that in future the other headquarters and that join other groups that work with the valorization of waste will be firm in the formation of a network of researchers and companies of 9 countries of Ibero-American. a diagnosis of the problems surrounding waste recovery is expected to be carried out in order to generate proposals for solutions to these problems. A web page is expected to be created to publish the reports, teaching materials, starting from meetings, to prepare reports on each of the meetings, workshops, protocols for the recovery of waste, document of the statutes of the network, promotion of courses, undergraduate and postgraduate graduates and chairs on waste recovery. Additionally, it is expected that research activities, scientific exchange and dissemination of the results achieved will be promoted, which will contribute to national and international knowledge. It is also expected to formulate research proposals at national and international levels that will contribute to the sustainability of the network
