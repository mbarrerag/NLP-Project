# FOOD IN THE PAST OF PRECONCEPTION

Present the international and national recommendations currently accepted in the area of nutrition and nutrition during pregnancy, which constitutes a great help for the entire health team. The topics are analyzed within the framework of national regulations, international agreements that have proposed goals and objectives in health, and taking into account the approach of food and nutrition security, as well as sexual and reproductive rights
