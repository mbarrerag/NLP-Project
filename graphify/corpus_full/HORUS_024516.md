# ILLICIT USE CULTURES AND CAMPESIN TRANSFORMATION PROCESS IN MACARENA (META, COLOMBIA)

This work addresses the peasant economy as the subject of transformation processes in function of the market economy. The objective was to determine the relationship between productive systems and peasant transformations in the configuration of Alternative Development Plans (PDA) in La Macarena, a municipality prioritized in the plans for integral rural reform proposed by the Peace Agreement in Colombia. A mixed methodology was used, combining a systemic methodology with a dialectic, and finding that the peasant resorts to illicitly used crops immersed in the subject-structure dialectic conditioned by the unresolved agrarian problem. There are four variables that can be managed by the peasantry in the productive substitution systems, emerged from the PDA of the National Program for Substitution of Illicit Crops (PNIS): Energy Efficiency, Cost Benefit Relationship, Environmental Risk and Organization; their indices would improve by establishing synergies with the endogenous development plans of the Losada and Guayabero Peasant Reserve Zone (ZRC-LG)
