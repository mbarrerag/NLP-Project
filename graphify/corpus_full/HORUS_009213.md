# BISH DIVERSITY (HYMENOPTERA: SUPPORT) AND THEIR RELATIONSHIP TO THE FLORAL RESOURCE IN THE ECO-RESERVELATION OF TRIBUNE, NEIVA, COLOMBIA

Tropical forests represent the most diverse ecosystems of the planet, particularly, the Dry Tropical Forest (BST) presents great diversity of flora and fauna resulting in high rates of endemisms, threatened to a large extent by human activity. In general, it is estimated that BST coverage loss of more than 94%, which, together with a climate change scenario, leads to the small remnants of this type of forest being confronted with desertification. The study of species that inhabit the BST is indispensable to derive strategies that lead to the conservation of it. The use of bees as bioindicators from morphological and molecular perspectives, such as DNA bar codes, can yield useful information about the quality of the ecosystem facilitating the decision-making on the management of the forests. In order to study the diversity of bees and their relationship with the floral resource in a fragment of BST. Methodology was carried out for this type of insects in the Eco Reserva La Tribuna, located in the municipality of Neiva, Huila, Colombia, during the months of October 2021 to September 2022, starting from the use of different methods, collecting and collecting from the total. the morphotypes of bees showing the greatest presence of pollen samples for all sampling, in order to define their dietary preferences at pollen level. The identification of pollen has been made from traditional techniques of palinology, as well as the development of a protocol derived from what Ivanova proposed, for the extraction and amplification of pollen DNA for sequencing purposes for obtaining DNA bar codes with the use of the Internal Transcribed Spacer marker (ITS)
