# Analysis of Asset Management Models for a Transformer Fleet in the National Laboratory of Smart Grids (LAB+i)

This paper presents the methodology classification of a set of 12 Asset Management Models related to Power Transformers Monitoring and the application of three of them in 3 substations of the National Laboratory of Smart Grids (LAB+i) located in the University Campus of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia, using the results to identify the main challenges concerning the Asset Management Application in Transformer Fleets related to data availability and precision. Finally, it was identified that there is a need to develop an Asset Management model that can use non-invasive real-time measurements for continuous monitoring of power systems and diagnosis.
