# THE SCHOOL FOOD PROGRAMME (PAE): A USED PUBLIC PLATO

The objective of the article is to make a historical account of the transformations of the normative frameworks of the School Food Program (SAP), identifying the effects of its commercialization and pointing out some recommendations of public policy. This work used qualitative-quantitative methodologies based on semi-structured interviews. The results indicate that the commercialization of public food has favored the transition of the SEAP from the public sphere to the private sphere, while the State has been negligent to exploit the potentialities that public food grants for the development of the countryside and the city with social justice
