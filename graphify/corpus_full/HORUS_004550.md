# DEVELOPMENT OF A DIDACTIC STRATEGY IN THE ACID-BASE ENTITLED ENTITLED EARNING PROCESS

398543_b8adbfa4eda14930b91f83c9344cc90a.pdf The Chemistry Department of the Faculty of Sciences of the National University of Colombia, offers the Basic Chemistry Laboratory course for students of Nutrition and Agronomy as part of their disciplinary training. This academic space designed for students who did not choose to study chemistry, but whose training process or professional performance requires it, constitutes a challenge for both students and teachers as it is available 3 hours a week, therefore strategies are developed that achieve optimization of the times in addition to showing the applicability in context. Based on the matrix of syllabus and the matrix of resources of module 3 of the Basic Chemistry Laboratory course, analysis of the solutions, the subject of titration Acid was selected. Basis for developing didactic strategies using the methodology of Instructional Design. One of the results with greater impact was the development of a video showing how chemical species interact during the titration process and, what happens in the behavior of the titration curve.
