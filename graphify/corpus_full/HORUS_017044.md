# REPRESENTATION AND SUNS

The amorphous volumetry implanted on sinuous planes are a singular physical aspect derived from the ways of inserting itself into rough territory, correlated with the study of the forms composed by broken planes, staggered masses in implausible ensambles, with high irreverence towards classical geometry. The physical analysis applied to the grouping of precarious popular housing treats the subject as a receiving agent of concepts from theoretical principles, applicable to the morphological message in key with the meaningful-significant relationship, as a path of knowledge to the study of this phenomenon (Hessen, 2004), in accordance with particular articulated levels of perception with the support of theories such as morphology, autopoiesis, the sustainable, chaos; popular architecture, as a dimension of study, focused on the study of the physical forms involved in the interpretation of the phenomenon, superimposed on the emotional and subjective distortions applied in the way of interpreting it
