# Evidence-based clinical standard for diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of adults with obesity and mass weight loss

Obesity is an ENT that is associated with higher mortality and morbidity rates and has a great economic impact, particularly the increase in healthcare costs. In addition, the mass weight loss after treatment is an aspect to always take into account due to the consequences that can cause in this population. Identify the clinical indications for the diagnosis, treatment and follow-up of adult patients with obesity and for the surgical treatment of those with mass weight loss by developing a clinical standard based on evidence (ECBE) in a university hospital of national reference in Colombia. Once the “discharged” group was formed and the scope and objectives of ECBE were defined, in April 2021 systematic searches were carried out in MEDLINE, EMBASE and LILACS instrument and in the selected patient groups, a preliminary proposal of EBCE (clinical algorithm and recommendations) was developed to identify “PCS published in the last 10 years that responded to these objectives and scope. The quality of the GPCs was evaluated with the AGREE II instrument.
