# SPANISH: MODEL FOR SIMULATION OF A ENGLISH LINE OF TESTING: ASSEMBLY LINE SIMULATION MODEL

The aim of this paper is to show a simulation model of a assembly line. To do this, the methodology of developing the basic ideas of the CDIO initiative and the universally accepted methodology for developing simulation models by the discrete event method are proposed. When applying the methodology, a model is developed in a hypothetical assembly line of refrigerators
