# Methodological proposal for the analysis of animal thermal comfort in remote areas of Colombia

Abstract Over time, several authors have investigated the relationship between climatic conditions and animal production, demonstrating that the state of comfort positively influences production. This research aimed to make a methodological proposal for the analysis of animal thermal comfort in isolated areas, taking as a case study three Territorial Areas of Training and Reintegration, where productive projects are carried out within the framework of the Final Agreement for the Termination of Conflict and the Construction of a Stable and Lasting Peace, using climatic data of temperature and relative humidity, obtained from time series from 2002 to 2019 with remote sensors. The methodology proposed from the use of digital platforms of free access showed to be applicable and, in the case of study, found that the regions that may present minor effects on birds, pigs and cattle are Tierra Grata and Miravalle, while, in Caño Indio, it is suggested to adopt climate control strategies to improve the animal thermal environment
