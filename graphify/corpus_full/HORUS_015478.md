# VALIDATION AND CULTURE ADOPTION OF THE IBERAMERIAN CLINICAL CONTEXT PROFILE REVISED PIFILE POWER (PIP-R).

Neonates requiring hospitalization can receive up to 12 painful procedures every day. After decades of research, it is known that in fact, they are able to perceive pain from very early gestational ages, but the immaturity of their nervous system makes it difficult to express with clear signs the painful experience. Their difficulty in verbalization makes professionals little worry about diagnosing and treating it. The consequences of these nociceptive experiences can be devastating for neurological development that according to their intensity and duration can cause neuronal apoptosis and decrease of synaptic connections. To measure pain, there are more than 40 scales, some of them have physiological and behavioral components; some have been reevaluated and redesigned considering the gestational age to obtain better performance, in the clinical practice. The Premature Scale Infant Profile Revised (PIPP-R) scale was one of the best available tools, but the Spanish version does not have validity tests. The aim of this research is to perform translation, cultural adaptation and determine facial validity of the Premature Infant Pain-Proed. Spanish language.
