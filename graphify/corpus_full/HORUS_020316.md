# Seven decades of Ecology and Forest Sciences at the FCA

The first documented reference of knowledge in Colombia on teaching ecology, as a regular subject in a undergraduate university program, dates from 1954. This does not know that, since the end of the eighteenth century Mutis with its “Catedra de Mathematics”, and then in the nineteenth and twentieth centuries several naturalists, botanists and geographers national and foreign, have offered courses and palestres that gravitate in the environment of what is now known as the “Science of Ecology”. In mid 1954 the professor of the University of the State of Michigan (MSU) Forrest Dean Freeland arrived to Medellín, who had recently presented his doctoral thesis on a topic of Forest Hydrology in the MSU itself; he was part of a cooperation program between that university and the National University of Colombia that developed for the headquarters Medellín and Palmira in the areas of agriculture and forest development, derived from the Four point of the program of government of the president of the United States Harry S. Truman, which was concerned with assistance to countries little developed after the Great War. Freeland arrived to the newly created Forest Institute of the National Faculty of Agronomy and Palmira in Medellín, to support the first. The first as a pioneering unit in the study of forest sciences in Colombia, and ecology as already mentioned from the Chair of Freeland. In 2021 it was then the remarkable celebration of the 70 Year Ephemeride of the Department of Forest Sciences and the Forest Engineering Career; so many decades guiding academic efforts, research and undergraduate and postgraduate training in forest sciences, fill with pride this Faculty and all its professors, students and graduates. The occasion was also the reason to reiterate that the Faculty and its Department of Forest Sciences will continue in its purpose to contribute to the development of the forest and ecological culture of the country
