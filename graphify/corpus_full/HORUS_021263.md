# Water balance simulation for the middle Miocene in the Tatacoa desert

Studies of paleosuelos in the desert of La Tatacoa, Colombia, show significant climatic transitions between the optimal climate of the Middle Miocene (~17-15 Ma) and the M4 glaciation of the Middle Miocene (~13.1 Ma), related to changes in soil moisture content. This study aims to investigate, with the help of a model of soil water balance, possible climatic conditions consistent with these changes
