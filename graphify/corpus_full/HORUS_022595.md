# SPANISH SPANISH MIELY REPORT OF CASE

Myelolipoma is a benign tumor composed of variable proportions of adipose tissue and haematopoietic elements, is a neoplasia of rare presentation in veterinary medicine, when observed, its main location is in the adrenal gland and to a lesser proportion is described in the gastric, hepatic, lymph node, skull or splenic region. Anamnesis: It was referred to the Pathology service of the National University of Colombia headquarters Bogotá a spleen with multiple masses of a canine patient of labrador race, 13 years old; the tissue was processed by routine techniques for microscopic analysis. Histopathological findings: it was evidenced proliferation of mature adipose tissue intermixed with hematopoietic precursors of different distinct cellular lines, the pieces observed were histologically similar to the bone marrow, the tissue was diffused and infiltrative, not encapsulated, in multiple focis comprimonia or substituated the structures of the baczo, additionally it was found to be considered to be abundant pigments derived from the hemoglobin, the mild hemoglometic, the tissue and the tissue was not included
