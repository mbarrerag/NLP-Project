# NEGRANDI NEUSTRAN MANUSCRITES OF THE BRICEÑO MARTÍNEZ FUND. BIBLIOTECA JOSÉ MANUEL RIVAS SACCONI. INSTITUTO CARO Y CUERVO MSS 1-16.

The core of this book is the codicological description sheets of sixteen Neo-Granadin manuscripts currently preserved in the José Manuel Rivas Sacconi Library of the Caro and Cuervo Institute. It is preceded by an introduction in which the history of these pieces is reconstructed, to the extent possible, to their current location and status. The elaboration of this catalogue is part of the initiatives and actions of conservation, research and dissemination of the Colombian bibliographic heritage. In addition, photographic images of single sheets, as well as reproductions of the filigree or watermarks that display the manuscripts under examination, complement this work
