# FUSAROIDS AND MARINE TORTURES: A NEW THREAT FOR COLOMBIA CONSERVATION.

A pathology called STEF (Sea Turtle Egg Fusariaryosis) is affecting the eggs of sea turtles. The Cellular and Molecular research group of Pathogen Microorganisms (CeMoP), from our Department of Biological Sciences, collected samples of hatched turtle eggs to determine what agents cause fusariosis in sea turtles in Colombia. In the study also participated our Laboratory of Molecular Ecology of Aquatic Vertebrates (LEMVA). The results showed that several pathogenic species, of the genus Neocosmospora, are etiological agents of fusariosis in sea turtles. This was one of the first studies to isolate this type of Neocosmospora in gortorge and caguama. The results show the urgency of continuing to study STEF
