# Prediction of university quality of life through data mining

Abstract The objective of this article is to measure through intelligent techniques the university standard of living of a university population. In this research, a dependent variable called quality of university life is taken into account together with 10 independent variables: academic burden, economic resources, relation with classmates, relation with teachers, curriculum, extracurricular activities, current housing, family relations, emotional state and university environment. 127 surveys were carried out for sampling of these variables by students of a public university located in the central region of the country. Subsequently, the most relevant variables were selected through statistical techniques in order to establish an analysis file from the J48 decision tree classification algorithm of the Weka platform. The result shows, with an effectiveness of more than 80% that the most influential variables in the quality of university life are: university environment, current housing, emotional status and relationship with teachers, finding, several times, that the quality of life can also depend on external variables to the university, such as: current housing and emotional state. These results are of great importance in the design of university policies to come
