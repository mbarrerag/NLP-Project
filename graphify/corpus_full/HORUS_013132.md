# Art condition: homeopathy and bronchial asthma in pediatrics

bronchial asthma is the most frequent chronic childhood disease and in the last five decades it has been reported an increase in its prevalence in many countries of the world, predominantly affecting ethnic minorities and low-income groups. Homeopathy has been widely used for the treatment of bronchial asthma and this trend is also increasing, explained by the greater affordability of alternative medicine and complementary therapies and the comprehensive approach that this complex medical system proposes to the individual. In order to know what is the contribution of homeopathy in the treatment of asthmatic children and adolescents, a state of art was proposed that describes the current approach to conceptual level both from the point of view of conventional medicine and homeopathic medicine. In addition, the various studies and new perspectives that guide the current homeopathic management of asthma towards a medicine of useful precision in the treatment of children and adolescents with this diagnosis in its different therapeutic moments are presented. .
