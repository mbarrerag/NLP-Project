# Beyond “Heaven”: Portrait of an Ethnographic Experience with an Indigenous Family in Pandemic Times.

Beyond “In Heaven” is an ethnography of the particular that portrays the author’s experience together with an indigenous family of the Tikuna ethnicity of the municipality of Puerto Nariño in the Colombian Amazonian trapeze during the pandemic of COVID-19 that struck the region in 2020. This family is made up of Robert Ahué Coello, Nexi Gómez Laulate and his daughter Cristal. This ethnographic portrait in phenomenological key tells the life story of the family before the pandemic, when they lived in the city of Medellín, their return to Leticia and their subsequent return to their territory in the community Doce de Octubre of the River Loretoyacú, forced by the quarantine in the department of the Amazon. The show also the situation of health and economic emergency in the city of Leticia, the campaigns of humanitarian help and social mobilization around this emergency. Subsequently, the experience with the family of the Beretoyacún is recounted in its process of adapting to this new life in its territory during the pandemic, together with the realization of the documentary “The Sky”. This ethnographic text, in its wake, reflects on the social context and in the region to Leticia and their subsequent return to their territory in the Twelve of October community of the Loretoyacú River forced by the quarantine in the Amazon Department. It also portraits the health and economic emergency in the city of Leticia, the humanitarian aid campaigns and the social mobility surrounding this crisis. In addition, it relates the experience with the family at the Twelve of October community in their adaptation processes to this new life in their territory during the pandemic, reviewed alongside the making of the documentary "Heaven". This ethnographic text also reflects on the sociocultural context of the Amazon during this juncture.ly, it offers some reflections form about the ethnographic experience and its writing, as upon the ethnic implication of the Amazon territory
