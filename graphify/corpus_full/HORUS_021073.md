# Yuma infinite cultural diversity: An analysis of the Magdalena region from the cultural and environmental heritage. South of Cesar, Bolívar and Magdalena- Phase I

Culture, environment and heritage are categories and dimensions of exploration that acquire a singular importance in the lower basin of the Magdalena River. The expectation of forming a new department, environmental changes, the climate crisis, combined with the few works around inventories and lists of cultural heritage, makes it urgent to deepen in the cultural and nature relations of the northern areas of the departments of Bolívar and Cesar, and the Middle Magdalena region of Santander and Antioquia. This project addresses the dimension of water as a historical river Yuma-Magdalena, which includes ecosystems invisible by war as the large marshes, as well as the socio-cultural constructions that in millennia of interaction with these ecosystems have left a diverse cultural heritage that is still to be described and analyzed in context of the heritage activations, cultural legacies and interactions society- nature. This research aims at a first stage to address the documentation of the local environmental and cultural heritage, as well as the development of inputs that contribute to the analysis of the cultural, environmental and social diversity of the lake basins associated with the Magdalena River because of their passage in the zone of interaction of the middle basin with their final part of the river basin, will be able to construct the cultural heritage
