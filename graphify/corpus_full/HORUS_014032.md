# Recirculation and automation of the hydraulic network of the hydraulic laboratory of the Universidad Nacional de Colombia

The hydraulic network built by the members of the water seedling serves as a complement to the activities learned in the subjects of the hydraulic line of the civil engineering program; it allows to carry the concepts learned to the practice in order to strengthen this learning in a more approximate way to the practice. The present project seeks to automate the measurements of the hydraulic network constructed so that students of undergraduate and master's degree can have modern elements of measurement that allow to make the most of the multiple combinations that the constructed hydraulic network. It allows, besides reducing the uncertainty in the manual measurements linked to the human being. Reviewing the aspect of recirculation, is sought the maximum utilization of the water resource to reduce the waste water when using the network. Currently the four outputs with which the hydraulic network is available are at free discharge due to the lack of adequate drainage systems in the place
