# The Jordan River as the spatial axis of the city of Tunja: the university promenade

The present urban design project aims to recover the Jordan river as a spatial axis of the city of Tunja, by means of the design of an urban walk of university character. The study sector is part of the upper basin of the Chicamocha river, which is born to the south of Tunja, in the parame of the river Runta. The Jordan river is part of one of the most important rivers of the center-orient of Colombia, the Chicamocha. The section of river that is studied, is located to the north of the city, sector with a fragmented growth scenario, in which a new commercial area is concentrated, with large areas and university spaces, starting from the development in the road infrastructure of the University Avenue. This has given the sector a greater urban growth in the city, with new avenues, residential areas, government buildings, medical centers and university centers. However, this urban development has happened around the river, ignoring it. It is not until the decade of 2000 that the first projects that form an integration with the river: the Unicentro river (2005) and the Buvar commercial sector. The selection of the final alternative is proposed from the following objectives: ● Respect the normatively established round areas, increasing the green area around the round as an environmental public space and enabling new green areas as extension of the same. To this end, a sequence of public spaces linked by the river, identified with institutional, administrative, cultural, educational, recreational and sports vocations, is proposed, doubling the complementary green area of the round. ● Set up a road and pedestrian network hierarchical for the sector, which guarantees the increase in connectivity and integration of the neighborhoods with the proposed urban structure of the river. ● Establish a structure of equipments to increase the dotatisation area of the study polygon, organized by institutional, administrative, public and cultural services, which allows for the joint use of the river-peate and the unit to increase the pedestrian paths and road mesh. ● Establish a structure of equipments to increase the area of the study, organized by institutional, administrative types, public and cultural services, which will be integrated with the urban mesh. river section under study is located to the northeast of the city, a sector with a scenario of fragmented growth, in which a new commercial zone is being concentrated, with large commercial surfaces and university spaces, based on the development of the road infrastructure of University Avenue.
