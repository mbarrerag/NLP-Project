# Method of objective assessment of the vulnerability of buildings exposed to Geoamenazas in Colombia

The present study was proposed as a compilation based on elements of the methodology of quantitative research, focused on obtaining reliable and replicable information, primarily referring to the techniques applied by areas of knowledge in the field of risk assessment and in particular, in the assessment of the vulnerability of buildings exposed to it, characterized by their location in medium and high-risk environments by mass removal, whose assessment was carried out an analysis of the specific seismic vulnerability. These results were evaluated and related to elements components of the pillars of sustainability (ambiental, social and economic), which were weighted by methods of multicriterial analysis (MCA), as well as to the risk managers. . formulation of a matrix for the characterization of buildings. more necessary for environments exposed to threats and in particular those also located in hillside areas, in order to define parameters that offer more adapt responses to community decision makers, as well as risk managers
