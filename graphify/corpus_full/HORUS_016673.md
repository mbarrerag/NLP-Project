# HISTORIA AND COMPARISONAL CONSTITUTIONAL THEORY.

This work aims to present in an integral way the comparative history and constitutional theory in a global and transnational perspective, based on the thought of the research group CC - Comparative Constitutionalism. It is divided the material into three volumes, according to the key concepts of protoconstitutionalism, modern constitutionalism and unconstitutionalism. The first part of the first volume that the reader has in his hands will develop the socio-cultural, historical and transnational school of public law and the constitutional state
