# PATHOLOGICAL STUDY IN TIMES OF BUILLING INFORMATION MODELING: FROM THEORY TO PRACTICE

The Building Information Modeling (BIM) has generated important changes in architecture and pathology studies are not foreign to it, but its immersion involves challenges such as harmonizing conventional and contemporary digital languages by making appropriate use of what the BIM offers. This project proposed a methodology based on the digital-conventional used today, with a view to linking it with the BIM, integrating graphical, qualitative and quantitative data in all its dimensions for diagnoses and interventions, knowing that the expert look should be part of it in any case. In a first phase, the integral conventional methodology was structured using the concept of damage mechanism and the diagnostic matrix with the data collected in field, to be parameterized in a BIM model promoting its proper use and analysis, since it is possible to integrate all the disciplines and phases involved in the process. This article presents the methodological formulation phase for the characterization of buildings in its pathology component, as an instrument of information collection in a qualitative and quantitative way that can be managed from the BIM model defining a structure and a route for the elaboration of the protocol in its three phases: recognition, diagnosis and intervention, and integrating only the hypothetical method of information that can be used by means of the method of analysis
