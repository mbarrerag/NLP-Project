# INTRODUCTION: THE ROLE OF PANDEMIA, TEACHINGS AND CHALLENGES

If we do not save ourselves all, no one is saved. Many warn that this will not be the only pandemic of the century, but the first of several that will threaten the whole society. That is why the pandemic should help us to think about the foundations of human civilization. The commodification of life, bodies, knowledge and nature backs the basic principle of co-dependency of the species. Treating nature as a set of goods to exploit, as a veto, deposit, deposit, or resource, violent its systemic functioning on which human life depends. An increasing number of scientists warn that pandemics originate in this disorder on a large scale from environmental cycles and circuits. That is why economic systems, institutions and the organization of society must fully assume the consequences of being part of socio-environmental systems. It is not a question of thinking of mere limits to the predation of natural balances. Beyond what are the rights of nature, must we convert all social, economic and institutional relations into sustainable social and environmental relations for the enriched reproduction of life
