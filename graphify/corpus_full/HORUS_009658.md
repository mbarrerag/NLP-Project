# Historical aspects of co-authorship networks in a Latin American journal of natural sciences: Caldasia 1940-2021

to analyze the scientific structure of the collaboration in the journal Caldasia throughout its history. It uses the co-authoring network analysis and uses the Gephi software. The network of the period from 1940 to 1995 is formed by five communities, where an author to communicate with another author requires crossing three authors to interact with other researchers, while the network of the period from 1996 to 2002 is formed by three communities; in this network an author only requires crossing two authors to interact with other authors. The network from 2003 to 2015 is made up of ten communities, in this network an author requires to cross an average of author and a half to interact with other researchers. The network of the period from 2016 to 2021 is formed by nine communities and to interact with other authors you have the same distance from the previous network to communicate. The intermediation in the co-author networks in each of the periods shows that only 20 out of 89 authors are information intermediaries. The number of collaborative articles ranges from one to six and the clusters of co-authoring networks consist of one to nine authors. The co-authorship networks of the four periods are not cohesive and are formed by conglomerates that respond to different research interests, or to different periods or to different institutional affiliations
