# HPV VACCINE: GENECHOLOGICAL, ETHICAL AND BIOETHICAL CONCEPTUAL ELEMENTS

Based on the latest meta-analysis on the efficacy and safety of the human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine, its vicissitudes in the gynaecological, ethical and bioethical fields are explored. A review of the literature on the subject is made. As one of the justifications of the vaccine is the prevention of endocervical cancer, viable alternatives are shown and commented for its early detection and treatment. Some ethical limitations shown by this vaccine are analyzed. It is concluded that there is a need to rethink the management of certain diseases with vaccines, to achieve greater transparency in the pharmaceutical industry, to resize the medicine based on evidence and to design educational strategies that promote behaviors that are in accordance with the nature of human sexuality
