# Description and characterization of clinical research in Latin America between 2011 and 2021

To describe and characterize clinical research of drugs in Latin American countries for the periods 2011-2021 based on global and local databases. Descriptive and retrospective analysis of clinical trials carried out in 19 Latin American countries and Latin Caribbean in the ICTRP and Clinical Trials.gov. A description of the regulatory framework for implementation and transparency and the selection of case studies to analyze the relevance of these research in decision-making was included. A total of 8516 clinical trials records were included in the analysis. They predominated the phase 3 clinical trials sponsored by the pharmaceutical industry for cancer. The transparency of clinical trials presents important advances in the previous record of clinical trials. Regarding the current regulatory framework, the regulations for the implementation of clinical research are based on the BPC. However, the regulation of transparency on clinical research and the use of its results for decision-making is still incipient.
