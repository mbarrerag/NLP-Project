# Visual narrative: Pedagogical proposal to stimulate reading in Colombian adult EFL learning using The Four-Phase Model of Interest Development

The lack of interest of Colombian adults who learn English as a foreign language in English reading represents a significant challenge for educators. This study aims to address this problem through the development of a pedagogical proposal. Following the methodological framework proposed by Jolly and Bolitho (2011), the design of the material and activities advances through stages that include identification, exploration, contextual understanding and pedagogical application. Throughout these stages, the key principles underlying the proposal were the constructivist perspective, various reading approaches, visual narratives and situational interest. The study involved six Colombian adults learning English. They participated in a group interview and nine workshop sessions. The Model Development of Interest in Four Phases was used to observe the interest during the sessions, then the pedagogical implications of the situational interest were observed. The findings of the group interview revealed that the apprentices did not consider themselves to be avid readers in English, being the barriers of the language, in particular the unknown vocabulary, a significant challenge. Despite this, they recognized the importance of reading in English to improve their skills and achieve personal and professional goals.
