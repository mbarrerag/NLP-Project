# A PARALYLE OF OPTISMATION BASED ON A METAHEURISTICS OF POPULATION AND TRAINED TO REsolVE THE MAXIMUM CLICK PROBLEM ON GPUS

In the study we consider the Maximum Clique Problem (MCP: Maximum Clique Problem, by its acronym in English), a representative optimization problem. It attacks it through a modeling in parallel computing by means of an evolutionary algorithm improved by a local search heuristic, for problems of significant size. The problem has different possibilities to analyze it and transform it into a problem of equivalent parallel nature, to such degree that it can be implemented its algorithmic solution in a parallel processing device such as a GPGPU (Gggwww: General Purpose Graphics Processing Unit, by its acronym in English).This study aims to implement a metaheuristic population based as a parallel algorithm of coarse grain and fine grain and improved with a trajectorial metaheuristics based on a local search as a heuristical to find an optimal solution or close to the optimal unit of the MCP (Taillard, 1995).The fine grain model combines the most representative characteristics of the population in the evolutionary algorithm (Tomassini, 1995), the coarse grain model combines the most representative characteristics of spatially distributed populations in the domain of the MCP (Taillard, 1995).: Single Instruction, Multiple Data Is the algorithm performance measured by running some instances of different sizes BHOSLIB (Benchmarks with Hidden Optimum Solutions for Graph Problems, 2021)
