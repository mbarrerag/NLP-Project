# Petrogenesis of Early Miocene Plutonism in the Western Cordillera of Colombia, and its relationship with Neogene subduction re-organization

The lower Miocene pluts exposed in the Western Cordillera of Colombia record the re-initiation of magmatism of the continental arc in the Andes of the North after a period of magmatic inactivity between the Late Eocene and the Early Miocene. The petrography, the U-Pb geochronology in circus, the geochemistry of total rock, the mineral chemistry and the data of the Hf isotopes in circus of these plutons are used to reconstruct the magmatic evolution of the Miocene in the Colombian Andes and to understand its relationship with the reorganization of tectonic plates experienced by the northwestern margin of South America during the Inferior Miocene. We examine a set of gabros, gradiorites and tonalites of the plutons The Danube, Pance and Tatamá processes, formed between 21 Ma and 15 Ma.
