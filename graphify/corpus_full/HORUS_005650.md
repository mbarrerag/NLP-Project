# Promotion and strengthening of the design sector in Colombia based on analysis of the documentary series “Towards success along the path of design”

In the contemporary landscape, public policies and government strategies focused on consolidation, innovation and design regulation play a decisive role in promoting and strengthening their practice. Interest in public design policies has grown due to debates on how to obtain greater competitive advantages in the global market, which has led to generating some interest in tools that help improve competitiveness, innovation and design (Gómez Gonzáles, 2017)These policies not only enhance cultural identity (Calvera & Veciana, 2005) but are also a key driver for economic progress and social welfare of the environments. Promotion strategies play a crucial role that must be defined from the conception of the policy, not only as a communication component, but as a pillar that guarantees the dissemination of good practices, the visibility of the strategic axes of design and the strengthening of the sector. Through effective promotion strategies, it can also generate a significant impact to the perception and adoption of innovative practices, as well as in the consolidation of a design culture in society. Therefore, understanding the strategic value of promotion in public design policies is crucial not only for the advancement of Colombia..
