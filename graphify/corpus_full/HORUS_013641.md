# Teaching strategies for the prevention of stage fear in violin students

In this document, structured from the essay format, through my experience as a musician-violinist, through the analysis of theoretical references, the application of qualitative measurement instruments and autoethnographic records, I will seek to show an overview of the problem of the scenic fear experienced by the violin student, by confronting an interpretative audience, an instrument examination situation, an audition and other possible scenarios in which the musician wants to show the final result of an individual or collective process of a musical piece; it is also intended to provide pedagogical tools that allow to face that fear, through physical and technical exercises, to achieve a better result in examinations, auditions and concerts. This fear is presented with some symptoms that may be physical or psychological, harming the musical result in a negative way and generating technical errors and other difficulties. .
