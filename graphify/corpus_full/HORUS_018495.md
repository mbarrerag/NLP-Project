# MODEL OF MEASUREMENT OF INTELIGENT ECOSYSTEMS DEVELOPED UNDER THE PARADIGMA OF THE INTERNET OF THINGS (IoT)

To propose a measurement model within an IoT architecture, in order to establish which quality characteristics are the most convenient and necessary for the network of objects using the ISO/IEC 25000 hierarchical model of software quality as a reference. For the design of the model, a systematic review of literature was carried out that allowed obtaining evidence from scientific articles stored in digital repositories in order to quantify quality attributes and determine ideal metrics. As a starting point, a series of research questions and a survey were carried out to a group of experts with knowledge of IoT to determine which characteristics and sub-characteristics of quality meet the necessary requirements. It is evidenced that the results and resolution of research questions raised as well as the survey carried out by the expert group for the validation of the model respond to the criteria necessary for a quality model. In addition, in order to facilitate the understanding of the model that is proposed here, a graphic representation is made through preconceptual schemes. Finally, this model includes external metrics to measure quality attributes in any area of the IoT
