# Dynamic frequency stability analysis in power systems with variable generation integration.

This paper presents the dynamic analysis of the stability of power systems with variable integration, taking as primary focus, inertia as the main impact variable against the energy transition. The work carries out a review of the literature of the different methods of dynamic analysis. Finally, work is done with the nonlinear method of analysis to evaluate the frequency impact on electrical power systems by mathematical and graphical analysis
