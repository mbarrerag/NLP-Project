# WUHAN SOPA: between the criticism of the CAPITALIST SYSTEM, OPTIMISM AND CHANGE OPPORTUNITY

This review aims to highlight the theoretical and practical scopes to which, from various disciplines of the Social and Human Sciences, the anthologized authors have arrived in Wuhan's Sopa, compiled by Pablo Amadeo (2020). To do this, they review their conceptual foundations and follow the argumentative line they present there. Thus, the present paper is elaborated from some of the texts that are compiled in the above book; that is, only those that tend to critical analysis on the capitalist system or warn of the possibility of being optimistic in the face of the global difficulty that is being shaped by the pandemic by covid-19
