# Design of a functional baking product including Sacha inchi flour and oil (Plukenetia volubilis)

The development of foods fortified and/ or respectively enriched with bioactive compounds is currently of great interest to industry and research institutes, given the evidence that associates the intake of bioactive molecules with lower risks of contracting chronic degenerative diseases, considered high cost for public health. Added to this, the need to implement more sustainable production processes, turned the look towards agro-industrial waste, to establish strategies for valorization, by using them as sources of ingredients with functional characteristics, due to their important content of compounds with biological activity, such as proteins, essential fatty acids, vitamins, polyphenols, carotenoids, etc. The Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia ssp.) is a shrub that grows wildly in the Amazon and has been introduced as an emerging crop in Central America, South America and in some countries of Southeast Asia, due to its large agro-industrial potential, and to the characteristics of the crop. The plant produces a fruit made by seeds or nuts (36-60 % p/p) of oil rich in essential fatty acids omega-3 (al-mega-) (alpha-alfa-alpha-Lolin,alpha-alpha-alical,alpha-alical This project contributes to the generation of new knowledge related to the production process of a baked product with a higher concentration of omega-3 and omega-6 protein and fatty acids than conventional similar products, and on the other hand, to the promotion of more sustainable processes, by valorizing the main by-product of the process of extracting Sacha Inchi oil (press cake), by using it as a functional ingredient in baked products
