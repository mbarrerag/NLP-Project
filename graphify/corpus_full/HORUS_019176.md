# CONCORDANCE BETWEEN OBSERVERS IN THE DIAGNOSTIC OF EYPITELIAL ATIPE PLANA. SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

To review the agreement between pathologists in the diagnosis of flat epithelial breast atipia. Systematic review of articles registered in the database of Pubmed, Scopus and Embase with the terms ‘pathologists’ and ‘flat epithelial atypia’, ‘flat epithelial atypia’ and ‘interobserver variability’, ‘interobserver agreement in flat epithelial atypia’ (Scopus and Embassy) and in the database Lilacs with the terms in Spanish ‘pathologists’ and ‘flat epithelial atypia’, looking for articles with agreement assessment between observers in the diagnosis of flat epithelial atypia using the kappa coefficient. Review articles, summaries of congresses and commentaries may be excluded. With the terms of search 140 publications were found. Of these, 121 were excluded in the screening by title and summary. From the remaining review of complete articles, 5 were selected for qualitative analysis. The kappa values varied from a regular agreement, kappa and comments. With the terms of interest were found 140, 121 publications were not included
