# NUTRITIONAL RELATIONS IN BANNUM PEUEDOTALL UNDER NITROGEN DOSE IN TWO PRODUCTION CYCLES

The export banana (Musa AAA Simmonds cv. Williams) represents for the Urabá region an important source of income which is also a source of employment for the whole community. The present work was aimed at evaluating the nutritional relationships existing in pseudo-talls of a commercial crop of sixth generation bananas in three phenological stages (vegetative, flowering, harvesting) under different doses of nitrogen in two consecutive production cycle (2011-2012). For this, variance analysis, linear regression models and analysis of main components (ACP) were used in a repeated design of measures. Synergy was found between Mg:Ca; Zn:Fe; Mg:N; K:Ca; Mn:B; K:P. The correlation between these nutrient associations and the production variables was 0.20, so it was not possible to apply a model of prediction that contains these nutritional relations and production parameters.
