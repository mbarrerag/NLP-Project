# EVALUATION OF DYNAMIC EFFORTS CAMP USING DIGITAL PHOTO-PLASSICITY IN AN EPOXIC PROBLEMENT WITH METAL INSERTS UNDER MAGNETICAL INDUSTRY

Photoelasticity is a technique that allows to analyze the field of efforts in birrefrigent materials, especially in the study of polymeric materials. On the other hand, the composite materials have a wide application in the development of actuators and sensors, whose design and manufacture depend on their dynamic behavior. This work evaluates by means of photoelasticity, the dynamics of the field of forces of prismatic samples of epoxy matrix with inserts of high magnetic susceptibility (cylinder, thin plate and conglomerate of microparticles of magnetite Fe3O4) heated by magnetic induction. To this, a circular polariscope and a source of white light was used to obtain the patterns of strips associated with the state of forces of the probeta and a welder-inducer adapted to generate the magnetic field oscillating by incorporating a solenoid coil. The evaluation of the variations in the RGB space of the temporal patterns of the strips of color evidenced that the samples presented residual forces around the inserts.
