# Performance evaluation of electric vehicles in San Andrés Isla

This study aims to evaluate the energy performance, economic impact and environmental benefits of electric vehicles (VE) compared to conventional vehicles (VC) in San Andrés Isla. The importance of this research lies in the urgency of implementing sustainable mobility solutions in an island environment, where energy efficiency and emission reduction are essential to preserve the local ecosystem and mitigate the effects of climate change. In a global context marked by the concern for climate change and pollution, this analysis provides fundamental information to support strategic decisions in transport and energy policies. The adoption of electric vehicles represents a decisive step towards more efficient and sustainable mobility, contributing to the environmental and social well-being of the island. The overall objective of this research is to determine how the infrastructure and the use of electric vehicles can influence the energy, economic and environmental sustainability of San Andrés. To do this, specific objectives were set: to assess the energy efficiency of the vehicles versus the VCs, to analyze the economic impact of their adoption through operating and maintenance indicators, and to quantify the reduction of polluting emissions derived from their use. The methodology used was quantitative, based on the direct data collection of the actual results of the vehicles in both vehicles and the study. The implementation of infrastructure for electric vehicles in San Andrés emerges as a highly effective strategy from the economic, energy and environmental perspectives. VEs not only stand out for their efficiency and capacity to reduce emissions, but also represent a step towards a more resilient mobility model and committed to the sustainability of the island. It is recommended to continue with the expansion of the charging infrastructure, to encourage policies that encourage the adoption of electric vehicles and to carry out additional research that address the entire life cycle of the VE, including the recycling of batteries. These actions will contribute to the sustainable development of San Andrés and to the consolidation of the benefits associated with the transition to electric mobility. .
