# Coordinates and trajectories of a judicialization process. An ethnographic reading of the experience of the Arhuaco indigenous people's search for justice in Colombia

The article is the result of an ethnographic research developed with the people Arhuaco indigenous people of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta in Colombia, between 2017 and 2020, a starting from my participation as an expert before the Supreme Court of Justice (highest instance of criminal and penal matters in the country), in the process of seeking justice for the arrest, torture and murder of three indigenous authorities in 1990. On the basis of this experience I intend to analyze in the article the complex journey of the Arhuaco people to confront this fact of violence, by undertaking a socio-legal mobilization that he invoked the presence of the Colombian state with the judicialization of what happened at three authorities
