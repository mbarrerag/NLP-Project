# Finding out tumor microenvironment characteristics associated with the progression of ovaran cancer

The micro-environment of tumour (TME) covers dynamic interactions between a tumor and surrounding tissues, playing a crucial role in progressing towards the CTM. This methodlog suggests that the organisation TME may serve as a predictive marker in serous ovarian carcinoma, providing valuable information on the role of the tumor microenvironment in the development of the disease. The tumor microenvironment (TME) encompasses the dynamic interactions between a tumor and surrounding tissue, playing a crucial role in cancer progression. Historical images provide valuable information about the characteristics of the TME. Computational pathology techniques have significant advantages in the identification and classification of these interactions. The cases were categorized into high and low-risk groups based on survival time. The results demonstrated a significant correlation using hand-crafted features extracted from the TME, the Cox regression exhibited a remarkable hazard ratio of 2, 59 (95% CI: 1, 06 − 6, 3, p = 0.03), indicating a statistically significant impact between TME and survival rate. This methodology suggested that TME organization could serve as a predictive marker in seros carcinoma of the ovary, providing valuable insights into the role of the tumor microenvironment in development
