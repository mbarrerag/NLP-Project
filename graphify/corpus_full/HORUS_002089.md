# Areas of cohesion and expansion: subverting the zero limit for the multiplication of the social meeting. Project: SENA Training Centre Suba

The Final Work of Maestría explores the zero level as an expanded system that articulates the relationship between architecture and city, overcoming its traditional conception as a simple level of access. Based on Ravetllat, Ruby and the notion of Erdgeschos, it is understood as an intermediate space, where urban and architectural dynamics converge, functioning as a programmatic and spatial threshold. Faced with the fragmentation and privatization of the lower floor in contemporary architecture, the work proposes its vertical multiplication, allowing the public spaces to expand at different levels. From the analysis of the cases of study – Gabriel García Márquez Cultural Center, Carpenter Center, SuperCADE Manitas and Harajuku Quest – key strategies are identified as the stratification of surfaces, the programmatic displacement and the spatial simultaneity. SENA Suba applies these strategies to structure its program in spaces served, activatings and servers, differentiating routes and strengthening spatial continuity. Through the peripheral displacement and through traversation, the project generates urban catalysts and establishes urban address, connecting the architecture with the city.
