# VALUATION PERSPECTIVES FOR FRUIT WASTE FROM THEIR PHYSICAL CHARACTERISTICS

This article seeks to establish the prospects for valorization of 9 seeds and 12 fruit shells from their physical properties. These residues were characterized with respect to: waste generation index (IGR), particle size, thickness, sphericity, density (particle and bulk), total humidity, hardness and maturity (color scale). When analyzing the results of these physical characteristics, the possibilities for use for obtaining edible foods, flours, pigments, composting, extraction of oils and activated charcoal were established. It was found that the IGR of the shells and seeds is between 11,91-55,61 % and 2,08-13,95 %, respectively. The seeds presented particle sizes of 0,38-4.21 cm, sphericities between 0,42-0,90, particle density of 0,91-1,43 g/mL, density of the gases and bulk use of the other oils and total humidity of 31,42-84,71 %
