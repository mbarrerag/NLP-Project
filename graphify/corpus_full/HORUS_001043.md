# Nutraceutic and toxicological study of polyphenolic extracts of Hyeronima macrocarpa fruit supported by lignocellulosic aerogels

In this study, the functional and toxicological properties of a nutraceutical product were evaluated, formed by immobilization of extracts rich in natural antioxidants on aerogels. The extracts were obtained from the pulp and the peel of mature fruits of Hyeronima macrocarpa, while the seeds were the raw material for the synthesis of nanocellulose aerogeles (AGNC) and surface derivatives of acetate (AGNC-A) and sulphate (AGNC-S). The antioxidant capacity, the release profile under conditions of simulated digestion, the ability to inhibit the oxidation of lipids and proteins were determined, and the cytotoxicity and genotoxicity was evaluated.
