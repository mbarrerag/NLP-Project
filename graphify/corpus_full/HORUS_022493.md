# PRAXIS AND KNOWING EXPERIENTIAL OF CULTURE MANAGEMENT. HERMENEUTIC OF A PRACTICE

The book takes on the reflection on the praxis of cultural management from the experiential knowledge that characterizes it. It is posed in the recognition of a sensitive reflection in which a aesthetic of knowledge and experience predominates. It also addresses the problem of alterity from the senti-thinking place in the interstitials of the disciplines that affect the work of cultural management. It is also placed in a critical sense regarding the epistemic references that have determined the social sciences
