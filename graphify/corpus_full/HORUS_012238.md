# Utility of the Pediatric Appendicitis Score (PAS) scale to distinguish between acute and non-perforated appendicitis in children.

Abdominal pain is one of the most frequent causes of emergency consultations in paediatric patients and acute appendicitis is the most frequent surgical urgency in children. Perforated appendicitis is related to increased complications and longer hospital stays. The aim of the study was to determine the utility of the SBP scale to differentiate between perforated and non-perforated appendicitis in children. Retrospective cohort study between December 2021 and April 2022 at the HOMI Foundation. Data were collected from patients undergoing acute appendicectomy: age, gender, variables included in the SBP scale and other variables associated with perforation. The SBP scale score was calculated for each patient and the information was collected from the surgical findings. A total of 282 patients were included in the study.
