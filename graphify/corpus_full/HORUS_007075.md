# Estimated direct net medical costs of breast cancer care in Colombia's general health social security system

To estimate the annual net medical direct costs per patient, in women with breast cancer diagnosis and obtain a predictor cost model; based on information from an insurer belonging to the General System of Social Security in Health of Colombia. Patients with breast cancer were associated with cancer-free controls (by a case study and peer-by-effect control). The costs were estimated by stages of care, between 1 January 2010 and 31 December 2016, and a cost-predictor model was established, by means of supervised learning algorithms. The average annual net costs of care per patient were higher in the initial care phases (USD 32,223,12) as the final (USD 92,700,40) and lower in the continuous care phases (29,77,02) and before the diagnosis respectively (USD 13,233,40).
