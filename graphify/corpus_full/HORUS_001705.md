# Priority of Circular Supply Chain Management Practices for Sustainability of Health Service Provider Institutions (IPS) in Colombia

This research aims to develop and validate a framework of prioritization of circular supply chain management practices (CSCM) to support decision-making oriented to the sustainability of the institutions providing health services (IPS) in Colombia. It seeks to facilitate the implementation of circular philosophy in the health industry, providing tools that allow decision-makers to identify, select and implement CSCM practices. This study was developed in four phases: first, a characterization of the supply chain of Colombian IPS was carried out; then, circular practices applicable to the Colombian health sector were defined and refined; then, the framework was validated through a focal group of health sector representatives. The results highlight the importance of management practices oriented to the production and delivery of cleaner services, as well as the health initiative and administrative will in the transition to circularity.
