# Impact of the addition of low-range coal on thermal conductivity of sodium saline soil

This study focuses on assessing the impact of the addition of low-range coal on thermal conductivity, soil moisture and maize growth (Zea mays) in sodium saline soil collected in the Department of Cesar, Colombia. Samples (2 kg) of soil are prepared with proportions of 0, 2 and 4 t/ha of low-range coal and maize variety ICA 109 is sown from Colombia. The thermal conductivity of the soil is measured in situ 10 days after the experiment is established. The results show that the addition of low-range coal causes a significant decrease in the thermal conductivity of the soil and a survival of maize plants of 36%. It is concluded that the average dose (2 t/ha) of low-range coal is the most efficient in promoting plant growth
