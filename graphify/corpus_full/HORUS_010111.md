# Potential neuroprotective effect of LXR agonists in the activation of autophagy of glial and neural cells subjected to glucose and oxygen deprivation.

Cerebrovascular disease is one of the most important causes of morbimortality in the world. Therefore, it is of great importance the understanding of the physiopathological mechanisms of this disease, which allow to find therapeutic targets to improve the neuronal survival that continues to the ischemic lesion. It was usually talked about 2 types of cellular death related to this type of lesion: necrosis and apoptosis; however, recently a great morphological variety of additional types of cell death has been found, among these, the associated autophagia has taken great importance in the last decade. There is recent evidence of the neuroprotective effect of the agonists of X receptors of the liver (LXR: by its acronym in English, Liver X receptors) in scenarios of cerebral ischemia and its ability to modulate the activation of autophagia. It has also described the role of the glial cells in the neuronal damage that follows ischemic lesion lines. Due to this, it was proposed as general objective to analyze the effect of the autophagia in the glical cells and neuronal cells
