# Evaluation of the implementation of mass electrical mobility in Colombia’s electricity distribution network

This document assesses the impact of mass electrical mobility on the electricity distribution network, using the distribution circuit of the island of San Andrés as a case study. Through different simulation methods, the analysis of operating scenarios and massification of the use of electric vehicles, both with uncontrolled and controlled recharge, highlights three key premises related to demand management, its influence on the indicators of network reliability, and the proposal of a methodology for the design and planning of distribution networks with high penetration of electric vehicles. It underlines the importance of the relationship between the installed power of the user and the charge of the electric vehicle, as well as the identification of new design factors. Finally, it emphasizes the need to design and build modular and interoperable distribution networks, which adapt to the growing penetration of mass electrical mobility, and implement advanced measurement technologies to monitor the state of demand, and adjust design models . electric mobility, and to implement advanced metering technologies to monitor demand status and adjust design models, is stressed
