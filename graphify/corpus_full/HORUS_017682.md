# HYDROSOCIAL SUSTTENSTABILITY OF THE ROSSIAN VERED COMMUNITY, CHOACHI-CUNDINAMARK

There is sufficient information in the world regarding conflicts generated around water management, in the vast majority of these conflicts are determined by the social, economic and political dynamics internal and external of the region. Addressing these conflicts from the hydrosocial perspective allows considering not only the natural aspects of water flows but the social processes that modify these flows, besides recognizing the complexity and heterogeneity of these phenomena, generating a more integral vision of the space. The El Rosario valley, located in Choachí Cundinamarca, is part of the direct buffering area of the PNN Chingaza and part of its territory is within the jurisdiction of this. This protected area is of great importance for the country due to the diversity of ecosystem services provided, one of the most important is the provision of water for the city of Bogotá, the capital of the country, to identify the existing structural elements, through the Chingaza system.
