# TABLE 2: APPROPRIATION, AUTHONOMY AND GOVERNANCE OBSERVED ITEMS AND LESSONS LEARNED

The Alternative Food Networks (RAA) and Short Marketing Channels (CCC) have been consolidating as alternatives that contribute to strengthening collective actions that enhance relational capital and territorial roots of the actors that link to the implementation of these initiatives. The experiences that have gradually evolved as alternatives that contribute to social and economic inclusion, mainly of farmers, family and community farming -ACFC-, have become scenarios for encounters that strengthen ties with consumers, with those who find social and spatial proximity. The search is for the experiences of RAA and agro-food CCC in which the effective participation and self-management of the actors linked to experiences will show fundamental learnings resulting from synergy among actors, particularly those linked to links that have less power in the food system.
