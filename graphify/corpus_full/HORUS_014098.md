# Comparative evaluation of some parameters of production and quality of meat at high-tropic crosses in Colombia

-The livestock production in Colombia is segmented according to the tropics where the region or department is located. There are high tropics (cold regions) where dairy breeds such as Holstein and Jersey predominate and the specialized dairy intensive production systems. While in the low-term (hot regions) the yielding of beef predominates, the yielding of beef, the yielding of beef and veal and its cross-breeding with commercial cebu, which are destined for meat production. The dairy sector has recently been highly affected by the increase in the cost of raw materials for the processing of concentrated food, thus increasing the cost of feeding. In the case of fertilizers where the milk production is highly dependent, a higher than 100% increase in relation to last year's prices has been evidenced, increasing the cost of meat and the production of beef. Although at the end of 2021 and beginning of 2022 the companies collecting milk have increased the value of the milk liter this does not reach to meet the high costs of the current food and fertilizers, thus leading the producer to think as being more efficient in the production and production of the production. zootechnical and quality of meat in the high Colombian tropics using hybrid vigor and heterosis as genetic system. After the crosses are carried out, monthly measurements will be made on some productive parameters such as daily gains of pre-weaning weight and post-weaning, and on the quality of meat from the measurements of the area of the loin eye (AOL), fat thickness (EG) and the percentage of intramuscular fat on the Longissimus dorsi muscle. Then, with the information collected, several generalized linear models will be implemented and some of repeated measures over time will be to determine the factors that affect the characteristics in question and determine the adjusted averages for each of the reproductive, productive and quality of meat parameters proposed in the investigation.
