# “Rowing, rowing between struggle and suffering”: mental and emotional health of Afrochocoanas elderly women

To evidence the relationship between the economic factor and the mental and emotional health of Afrochocoanas elderly women, from a life course, intersectional and cultural perspective. Qualitative study of renewed ethno-nursing, with participant observation and ethnographic interview to 18 Afrochocoanas elderly women. The Creswell and Poth analysis method was used, following Leininger's stages. The results are presented under the theme "Rowing, rowing, I move forward amidst struggle and suffering" and two main categories: (1) Life is about struggle; you have to fight your way through; (2) Everything is carried inside the heart; it is a suffering. The mental and emotional health of older Afro-Colombian women is closely related to the type of work and working conditions they experience during the course of their lives. However, their workand struggle also dignifies them, being an important element to create care strategies for these women
