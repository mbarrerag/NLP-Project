# EVALUATION OF TWO METHODOLOGIES FOR THE ANALYSIS OF THE MANCOZEB AND PROPINEB FUNGICIDE RESIDUES IN FRUITS AND VEGETABLES

Dithiocarbamates (DTC), specifically mancozeb and propineb, are the best-selling fungicides in Colombia, these are applied in a wide variety of crops due to their broad-spectrum fungal activity, and have also been reported in recent years with great frequency in monitoring of pesticide residues carried out by the European Commission. DTCs are analyzed by indirect methods due to their chemical instability and low solubility. This study consists of evaluating two methodologies for the analysis of dithiocarbamate residues, specifically for mancozeb and propineb in fruits and vegetables with the objective of providing a tool to evaluate the quality and safety of food
