# Inhabiting Violence: Escapes from the Theatre Tradition in Three Colombian Dramaturgy

The following work proposes the concept of escape as a tool to interpret the aesthetic bets of contemporary Colombian dramaturgia, which approaches the phenomena of violence in the country. This study starts from the conviction that the works in question are in dialogue with the theatrical tradition that precedes them, particularly, with the postulates of the New Theatre, understood as a representative practice of modern theater in Colombia. Therefore, the concept of escape (used in analogy of the musical genre) has as its function to characterize the relationship that exists between these forms of understanding the theatrical work. This analysis is made from the contrast of “The dream” by Enrique Buenaventura, thought as an example of the modern Colombian drama, with Yellow taxi by Victor Viviescas and Kilele by Felipe Vergara, considered as two representative samples of the current dramaturgical practice in the country. The detailed interpretation of these three works allows the transfer of modern theatre to the contemporary theatre, as a musical piece of three escapes: one referring to the structure, another to the relationship with the real world and, one last, to the link with the public. . a, considering as two representative samples of dramatic contemporary practice in the country.The detailed interpretation of these three plays allows me to postulate the transition from modern to contemporary theater as a musical piece composed of three fugues: the first one referring to the structure, another about the relationship with the real world, and the last one related to the link with the viewers
