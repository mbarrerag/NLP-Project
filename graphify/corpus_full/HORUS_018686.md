# Between Ergonomy and EthnogRAPHY: BOTTLES OF USE OF MEDICAL DEVICES

A medical device (DM) is not only technology in itself. When a DM is used, it interacts with other DMs, users, physical spaces, institutions, among others, becoming part of a more complex technology that emerges from the execution of specific medical procedures or from the development of activities associated with the team, such as its installation, maintenance, etc. When the interactions between this network are obtained positive results are called compatibility, while when difficulties, incidents or adverse events are generated, there is talk of incompatibility. This book gives an account of the results of the research carried out around the use of medical devices in orthopedic surgeries and traumatology in a second level hospital in Bogotá. It presents the compatibility and incompatibility in the use of medical devices as part of the technology of the operating room, as sociotechnical systems and as sociomaterial practices, that is to say, as a result of interactions between elements of the systems as well as of the deduction of routines.
