# Is the survival of tree species in the tropical dry forest explored by functional drain resistance routes?

To determine the general susceptibility to drought of the species of the tropical dry forest is not easy, since all possess different characteristics and face drought in various forms (elevating or facing).To evaluate the ability of the trees to survive the drought it is not enough to evaluate traits at different levels of the plant, but it is necessary to understand more deeply the traits and how they are associated with the mechanisms of resistance to drought of each species. In this study we evaluate structural features (such as specific foliar area: SLA, leaf thickness: LT); anatomical (such as area of vessels: MVA; density of vessels: RV) and mechanisms of resistance to drought as hydro potential to loss of turgency (WPLT), resistance to cavitation (P50), density of wood (WD) and loss of electrolytes (EL), of 12 species with contrasting growth rates, used in the ecological restoration of the project Hydroelectric Ituango, where the survival of the trees sown between 2019 and 2021 was measured. We studied the intra and inter-specific variation of the variables of the evaluated traits, and the relationships between them and the main components.
