# THE WORK OF SCIENCES AND THEIR MEASURES: RECOGNIZING THE WORKPLACE

The chemistry laboratory is a space for experimentation, creativity and research, but it can become a dangerous place if the people working in it do not know certain basic standards regarding the handling of substances, safety implements, materials and techniques used there. This first practice shows the need to have prior knowledge about the activities to be developed. Likewise, it is necessary that the laboratory work is carried out according to safety, prevention, common sense and responsible attitude guidelines
