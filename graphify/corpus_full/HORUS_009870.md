# Material and press engineering for efficient entangled photon pairs generation

In this master's thesis, the engineering of material and pulse for the improvement of light-matter interaction with a view to increasing efficiency in the generation of photons interlaced in chip is proposed. To do this, a careful review of the state of the art of common procedures for the generation of interlaced photons in chip was carried out, finding that there are great efforts to find new material platforms to be used as integrated quantum photonic platforms. In addition, photonic devices and excitation pulses are also being strongly investigated. Bearing this in mind, the proposal of new materials and the engineering of the excitation pulse is being carried out. Its feasibility was tested with numerical simulations and experimental approaches.
