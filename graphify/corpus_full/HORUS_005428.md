# USE OF INDUSTRY 4.0 TECHNOLOGIES IN SMART PORTS

Smart Ports (Smart Ports) focus on developing more competitive processes under cost, time and information criteria around the entire supply chain with the help of industry 4.0 technologies. This research aims to analyze the use of emerging technologies used in Smart Ports through bibliometric analysis queries and literature review of the Scopus database, where they were categorized by common themes. The boom and development of the adoption of emerging technologies in ports, mainly the Internet of Things (IoT), Big Data, Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence are concluded; these technologies have helped to make port logistics processes safe and efficient. An emphasis is also identified for contributing to environmental sustainability processes, ensuring through improved technologies at port terminals. The main examples that are using industry technologies 4.0 in ports are located in Shanghai, Huanghua, Rotterdam, Hamburg, Barcelona, Salerno, Ravena, Vancouver and Los Angeles
