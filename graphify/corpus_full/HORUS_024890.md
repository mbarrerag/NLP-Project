# RELATIONSHIPS BETWEEN BRAUER CONFIGURATION ALGEBRAS AND SOME DIOPHANTINE EQUATIONS

In 2017, Green and Schroll [2] introduced Brauer configuration algebras as a generalization of Brauer Graph algebras all these algebras have the property that its theory of representation is based on some combinatorial data. In this talk, we describe how to use mutations in Brauer configuration algebras and suitable automata, in order to give solutions to generalizations of the Chicken McNugget Problem [1; 3]. Introduced tools allow for obtaining an algebraic description of the key schedule of AES (Advanced Encryption Standard). [1] Chapman, S.T.; o'neill, c., factoring in the chicken mcnugget monoid. Mathematics Magazine 2015, 91, 323-336. [2] Green, E.L.; Schroll, S. Brauer configuration algebras: A generalization of Brauer graph algebras. Bull. Sci. Math. 2017, 141, 539-572. [3] Ramirez-Alfonsin, J.L. Complexity of the Frobenius problem. Combinatorica 1996, 16, 143-147
