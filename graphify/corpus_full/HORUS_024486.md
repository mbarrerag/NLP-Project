# FALLS

The attitude of the physician to the elderly pacinete who has presented a fall cannot be of indifference since this, generally, is a warning signal that reveals many times serious clinical situations. The fall is related to increased mortality and morbidity, higher health expenses, increased need for hospitalization and indirect cost due to loss of the productivity of the caregivers; although in low proprtion, it may have unfavorable clinical outcomes such as fractures, bruises, lacerations, or conductual as we are afraid to fall, immobilization, social isolation and even institutionalization
