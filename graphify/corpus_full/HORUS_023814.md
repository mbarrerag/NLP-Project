# LEARNING A FOREIGN LANGUAGE: A RE-CREATIVAITY CASE

As a result of the absence of local language policies on teaching and using foreign language, language skills outside the needs of the apprentice have been developed through the anchoring of learning models conceived in leading countries in scientific and humanistic knowledge. In opposition to this procedure, this article aims to give the right value to the specificity of the insertion of a foreign language in view of the variability of socio-cultural contexts and institutional objectives. For the purpose of defining socio-linguistic and foreign language policies, this proposal presupposes the deployment of creativity in foreign language that favours the expression of the apprentice, which highlights the uncomfortable condition of cultural decontextualization of the foreign language, as it is the subject of study in an environment of uprooting, which in turn makes it possible to reaffirm the innovative identity of the institution where the processes of his teaching and learning are carried out
