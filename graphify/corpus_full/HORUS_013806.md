# Betting for the personal and professional development of young people and adolescents of San Andrés de Tumaco (2023)

Education is a fundamental right in Colombia and ensuring that it is equitable and inclusive is one of the UNESCO Sustainable Development Goals. Access to free and quality education allows reducing the gap in social inequalities by guaranteeing access to those with fewer resources, especially if they are in a vulnerable situation. Young people in the municipality of San Andrés de Tumaco have performance results under the Knowledge 11 tests and few are admitted to the undergraduate programs of the National University of Colombia, which reduces the possibility of accessing higher education. In addition, they develop in a context with multiple barriers, including access to the Internet, high rates of multidimensional poverty, unmet basic needs, shortage of educational provision of higher education programmes, geographical distance from higher education institutions and social violence situation. The aim of this research is to provide tools to young people of the last grade of the municipality's baccalaureate that strengthen their knowledge in areas of foundation such as natural sciences, social sciences, mathematics, literacy and English to improve performance in the state tests and the examination of admission to the National University of Colombia is an invaluable opportunity to reduce the inequalities of professional development and each participant in Colombia
