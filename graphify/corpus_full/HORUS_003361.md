# AID ATTENTION TO THE MAJOR ADULT INSTITUTIONALIZED WITH A MODEL OF AUTHORITY.

To determine the mental and social functional capacity of older adults under a self-care model Methodology: quantitative descriptive study, the selection of participants was by intentional sampling, 80 institutionalized elderly persons Results: Older adults of female gender 52%, male 48%, with Functional Autonomy in 45%, activities engaged in bathing and dressing 20%, dependencies in 17% Conclusion: Ensure nursing interventions based on self-care capabilities. Establish new lines of research based on the elderly's self-care capabilities. Promote nursing interventions based on conceptual nursing frameworks (Orem Theory). Key words: Older person; Functional capacity, Self-care
