# Uncovering the richness of Cerambycidae (Coleoptera) in the Amazon region: new species, notes, and new records

Three new species from Brazil are described: Mionochroma complanatumn. sp. (Cerambycinae, Callichromatini), from Roraima; Plistonax albicansn. sp. (Lamiinae, Acanthoderini) from Acre; and Scythropopsis roraimensisn. sp. (Lamiinae, Acanthoderini) from Roraima. Three species are newly recorded for Brazil: Nealcidion elegans Monné & Monné, 2009 (Lamiinae, Acanthocinini), Paranyssicus quadriguttatus (Buquet, 1860) (Cerambycinae, Elaphidiini) and Penaherreraus pradosiae (Tavakilian & Peñaherrera-Leiva, 2003) (Lamiinae, Acanthoderini). Furthermore, the geographical distribution for 12 additional species is expanded in Brazil to include the Roraima State and one to the Acre State. Mecometopus cauaburi Martins & Galileo, 2011 (Cerambycinae, Clytini) is proposed as a junior-synonym of Mecometopus wallacei (White, 1855) (Cerambycinae, Clytini). Mionochroma vittatum (Fabricius, 1775) (Cerambycinae, Callichromatini) is redescribed and some morphological variations are pointed out
