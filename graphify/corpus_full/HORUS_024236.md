# MUSIC FOR QUAARTET OF BLAS EMILY ATEHORTUA STUDY: QUAARTET FOR STUDY #5, OPUS 198

This critical edition revolves around the music for string quartet of the Colombian composer Blas Emilio Atehortúa; important figure of academic music in Colombia of the 20th century, whose professional and creative activity had significant international relevance. The publication seeks to contribute to the transcendence and visibility of his work: creative product that belongs to the musical memory of the country. The catalogue of work of Atehortúa is significant, reaches opus 260. The compositions that integrate it are examples of various aesthetics and styles of composition of the 20th century. The majority, contributions from ideas defended by the composer around Latin Americanism and the creation of syncretic.
