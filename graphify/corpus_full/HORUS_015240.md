# Vulnerability situations in the San Luis neighborhood of Chapinero in Bogotá, D.C., which explain the persistence of chronic malnutrition in children under five years of age.

Chronic malnutrition in children under five years of age is defined as the delay in growth that occurs when children do not reach the expected size for their age. This delay is the result of nutritional deficiencies, but is also determined by a series of specific situations and material and symbolic resources, divided in an unequal way, of social, economic, cultural and political nature. In this sense this research aims to characterize situations of vulnerability, which contribute to explain the persistence of chronic malnutrition in the San Luis neighbourhood of the town of Chapinero in Bogotá D.C., this based on the vulnerability approach.
