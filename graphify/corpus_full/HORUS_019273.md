# Demographic, clinical and cerebrospinal fluid findings in Central Nervous System infections of patients with immunosuppression associated with solid tumours and hematolynoid neoplasms at the National Cancer Institute in the period 2010 - 2020. A descriptive and retrospective study.

The infections of the Central Nerve System in cancer patients have not been evaluated in Latin America, however, they are of great importance given their morbidity and mortality. Describe the demographic, clinical and microbiological characteristics of patients with diagnosis of hematolyintuitive cancer or solid organ with infection of the central nervous system. Patients: Patients were selected in all age ranges with confirmed diagnosis of solid or hematolyintuitive cancer, with positive cerebrospinal fluid for some microorganism. Patients were excluded in those who were not sufficient or reliable data. Descriptive, cross-sectional and retrospective study of the last 11 years at the National Cancer Institute-ESE (2010-2020). Positive results of the cerebrospinal fluid were taken in order to seek the variables of interest, the patients were considered to be the most frequent patient (Cunmarkintravenous disease)
