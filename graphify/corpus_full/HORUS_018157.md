# Nanobioconjugated with silica nanoparticles and/or fullerenol, Buforin II and OmpA protein, with potential use as cell penetration vehicles.

Different biological barriers are generally responsible for the limited release of charges at the cellular level, as a therapeutic strategy. Previously, a new family of nanobioconjugated with capacity for cellular penetration and exhaust to the endosomal route was developed, immobilizing the Buforin II peptide (BUF-II) and the OmpA protein in nanoparticles (NPs) of magnetite. Here, we propose to extend this approach to nanoparticles of silica (SNPs) and fullerenol (F) as nanostructured cell support for conjugation of these powerful cellular penetration agents (OmpA-SNPs) and as well as the functionally-based (F) of the nanoplying of the nanoparticles (F) and the functionally-supplied (F) of the nanoplying of the nanoplyts
