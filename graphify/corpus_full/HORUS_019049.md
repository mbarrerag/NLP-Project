# Clinical and demographic characteristics of the geriatric population in the oncology service of a university hospital in Bogotá over a period of 12 months.

The world population ages accelerated. Cancer is one of the main causes of death in the world and by different genetic, environmental and physiological mechanisms, the elderly are more susceptible to suffering from this disease, which explains that in the whole world between 50-60% of the diagnoses with cancer are made in the elderly. The multidimensional characterization of the elderly with cancer is a useful tool for the best decision making in these patients. Characterizing the population aged over 60 hospitalized by Oncology of a third level University Hospital and identifying the main diagnoses, comorbidities, mortality, identifying which of them were evaluated by the geriatric service and which were their main geriatric syndromes. Patients and methods: Descriptive study with information collection retrospectively. Patients aged over 60 years hospitalized by the Oncology service of the National University Hospital with neoplasia confirmed during the period February 2019 - February 2020 were the patients of the elderly. The patients were reviewed by the patients evaluated by the hospital of geriatric service of the elderly.
