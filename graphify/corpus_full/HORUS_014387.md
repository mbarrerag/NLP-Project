# Social effects of the processes of implementation of public policies in Cartagena in the period 2012-2019.

The Cartagena district during the years 2012 to 2019, had a total of 7 mayors; 3 of them were elected popularly and four as commission this atypical situation caused serious public health problems in the executions of the PIC programs carried out in the period 2012-2019 described in each of the development plans. This research seeks to Analyze the implementation process and its relationship with the financial execution of the public health programs in the Cartagena district during the above mentioned atypical period to determine the impact on the population that is the subject of these interventions. For this purpose, the reference method of analysis of public police proposed by Pierre Muller and the data collected both from primary sources (in-depth interviews) and second-hand sources in the documentary reviews issued by official entities and authorized bodies for its dissemination, taken from the database of the public health surveillance system (SIVIGILA) Administrative Department of Health DADIS, etc. All this served to discover a series of problems within the Cartagena district related to apathy and little participation of actors at the time of assuming functions and taking decisions regarding the efficiency of public policies, omission of public knowledge
