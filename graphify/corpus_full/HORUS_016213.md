# POLICIES FOR THE DISPUSSION OF ELECTRIC MOTOCYCLETS

New technologies of electric mobility could contribute to the improvement of the environmental problems of the big cities. In order for these technologies to become more widespread, it is necessary to identify the factors that affect the adoption of an electric transport medium. Although research in this sense for light vehicles is extensive, little knowledge is available about electric motorcycles. The aim of this work is to present a systemic vision that integrates the main factors of adoption of electric motorcycles, in order to promote a discussion on possible policies useful for the diffusion of this technology. The dynamic hypothesis constructed presents two technologies in competition for the same market (electric motorcycles and internal combustion motorcycles), each of which generates 2 cycles of balance. Electric motorcycles, in addition, generate two cycles of reinforcement related to the learning curve and the resistance to its adoption
