# USE OF PROMISING AMAZON SPECIES IN FOOD APPLICATIONS UNDER A CIRCULAR ECONOMIC APPROACH: CASE SACE SEARCH SEARCH SEARCH

The Sacha Inchi (Plukenetia volubilis) is a plant of Amazonian origin with great expansion due to the possibilities of transformation of its fruit (almonds with a high oil content (35-50%), high biological value proteins (20-25%) and dietary fibre (10-15%)). Due to its high content in oil rich in polyunsaturated fatty acids omega-3 and omega-6, the Almonds of Sacha Inchi are mainly used for the production of oil. The process of obtaining the oil is based on the two main by-products are obtained from the oil production process: the pressing cake and the shell that envels the almond, which represent about 75% of the processed material. In view to achieve sustainable development of the Sacha Inchi, the agro-industry should aim to propose a full use of the fruit, the objective of this work was to propose innovative alternatives for the diversification and use of the main products and the by-products of the oil industry of Sacha Inchi.
