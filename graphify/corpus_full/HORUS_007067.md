# Relations between Agroforestry Systems and the Main Agroecological Structure in agroecosystems coffee. Case study: Finca BuenaVista (Santander) and Los Angeles (Cundinamarca)

The Main Agroecological Structure (EAP) is understood as that quality related to agrobiodiversity of the agroecosystem (finca) and refers to the spatial configuration and the connectivity of its biotic elements, historically constituted. For this reason, from agroecology and environmental thinking, this research aimed to take a case study: Finca BuenaVista (Santander) and Los Angeles (Cundinamarca) in Colombia, constituted in time as agroforestry systems (SAF) of production of coffee under shade to, deepen the understanding of the ecosistemic and cultural factors that affect the use of agrobiodiversity at farm scale. This, by means of the use of a mixed methodology containing materials and qualitative and quantitative methods and that it considered for each farm, the use of the EAP as an index of agrobiodiversity, spatial analysis, an agroforestry of semistructure interviews and revision of secondary sources. The results, showed the interconnection between the agroecosystems and their ecological environment by means of different plant coverages and bodies. The results showed the interconnection between agroecosystems and their ecosystem environment through different schemes and bodies of water in internal and external sectors. Likewise, it was clear that the implementation is a result of the import of confectioning management practices
