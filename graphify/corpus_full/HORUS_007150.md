# Evaluation of the rheology of cement pastes added with calcined clays and limestones in the presence of superplasticizing additives

In the development of this work experimental tests were carried out in order to determine the fresh state behavior of cement pastes added with calcined clay (LC3 cement) by means of reology tests. Moreover, characterization tests were carried out such as: density, fineness, normal consistency, times of fructuring, minislump, fluidity and resistance to compression in order to have a better knowledge of the material studied and to describe in detail its physical behavior during the first hour of fructuring. The addition of calcined clay and limestone in cement pastes generates an increase in the demand for water of the mixture, increases the effort of fluence and decreases viscosity. In this sense, superplastifying additives available in the national market (Viscocrete 3100 and Sikaplast 5500) in order to evaluate its contribution to improve the properties in fresh state.
