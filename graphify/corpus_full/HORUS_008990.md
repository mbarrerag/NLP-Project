# CHAPTER 9. SAMPLING TECHNIQUES AND SAMPLE PREPARATION

Geotechnical cartography, by providing information regarding phenomena associated with the earth's surface, constitutes an important instrument for planning and implementing civil works, especially in the pre-project phase.Among the attributes related to the geotechnical behavior of tropical soils, humidity conditions are capable of identification, recognition and measurement on the surface and subsurface, thus enabling their spatial and temporal mapping.In this context, this chapter initially briefly addresses the pedological, geomorphological (chapter 3, chapter 4 and chapter 6) and climatic (chapter 14) characteristics that influence soil moisture conditions.The various methods for recognizing, measuring and mapping soil moisture are discussed below, mainly those applied to large areas, which generally make use of current technologies.At the end of this chapter, examples and suggestions for representing the properties in question are presented, using geotechnical charts and maps and three-dimensional models
