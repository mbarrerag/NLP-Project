# IMPACT OF THE HEALTH MODEL AND THE INCLUSION OF TECHNOLOGIES IN THE PBS.

From 2022 onwards, the Ministry of Health of Colombia will make a change to update and modify the medicines and health technologies to be included in the unit of payment per capita, by resolutions 2273 and 2292 of 2021, being one of the most ambitious modifications carried out in recent years (1-4). This means that out of 459 active principles were included 1059, which opens up the use of a wide range of drugs (5). The list of medicines previously included in dermatology was limited in options, which, moreover, were little used by dermatologists because they were not updated. It would be used to formulate topical drugs for the patient to buy them or high-cost medicines through the MIPRES/Sispro platform (6). This situation has been criticized in different scenarios by the indifferent view of the policy in this problem, although it seems that this resolution would seek to provide a positive response to improve access to this class of medicines
