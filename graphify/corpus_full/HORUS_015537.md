# Proposal of a programming and routing model for a company in Bogotá that provides home care services.

The companies that provide home care services have seen an increase in the demand for patients over the last few years, mainly as a result of the ageing population. In this context, the inefficiencies in the service delivery processes have become evident, therefore, it is necessary that the organizations improve their operating performance to remain competitive in an increasingly demanding market. In view of the above, this work proposes a programming and routing model for a company in Bogotá that offers this type of services, in order to reduce the costs of the routes. First, the characterization of the programming and routing process is carried out, identifying the policies under which the process is carried out and several additional aspects related to its operation. Then, a decomposition approach is used to relax the complexity of the problem, which results in three interconnected mathematical formulations that together represent the problem. Finally, the results of the company are compared with the solutions obtained from the model, demonstrating statistically that the proposed solution offers a better performance.
