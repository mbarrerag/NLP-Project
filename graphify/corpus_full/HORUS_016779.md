# SMALL INTERPRETATION AND APPLICATION OF ATTRIBUTS FOR PROSPECTING GAS IN PORK TRAINING IN VIM, MAGANGUE AREA

The Magangue arch in the Lower Valley of Magdalena is a structure produced by an extensive tectonic, which generated and controlled the development of sub-basins, in which, on a basis of continental affinity, a thick sedimentary sequence of marine rocks was deposited known as the Porous Formation, a unit in which recent discoveries of economically profitable gas accumulation have been reported north of the basin. These discoveries motivated the application of a methodology to identify anomalies associated with the accumulation of gas in the sandy levels of the Porero Formation, based on information from wells and seismic 3D. Based on the analysis of fluid substitution in the well we have called "Pozo A" added to the generation and analysis of synthetic gothers, AVO Class IV anomalies were identified at the top of the interest interval, and class I at the base of this. Taking into account the stratigraphy and structural geology, a structural map was developed in time, by which the extension of the AVO anomalies, associated with stratigraphic and structural traps in the northern part of the area was determined as anomatographical presence of the AVO
