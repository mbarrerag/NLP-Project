# ESTIMATES OF MARKOV CHANGES IN TIME-CONTINUED WITH DISCRETE COMMENTS: APPLICABLE TO THE PROBLEM OF TRANSITION OF CREDITIC QUALITY

The estimation of the probability of transition of credit rating is made for an arbitrary period of time through the implementation of a genetic algorithm assuming that credit rating transitions follow a Markov process. Markov’s chain is estimated in time-continuing using discrete data from the maximum likelihood function based on the absolute frequency matrix. The methodology is applied in two cases, the first, for global corporate bonds, and the second, for Colombian corporate bonds. The results of the application of the developed methodology are evaluated by comparing the estimation of the probability matrix of transition to one year obtained by the genetic algorithm with the methods of hope maximization and the quasi optimization algorithm. It was evidenced that the results obtained through the application of the proposed genetic algorithm, are computational efficient and competitive against the methods with which it is compared
