# Documentary management: the basis for the management of Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta

The Cienaga Grande de Santa Marta (CGSM) is one of the most studied socio-ecological systems in Colombia and, at the same time, one of the most threatened. Its deterioration has intensified since the 1960s by infrastructure interventions that altered its water connectivity. Despite multiple restoration efforts, such as the Pro-Cienaga Project, the lack of continuity in the management and lack of knowledge of accumulated technical information has limited the effectiveness of the interventions. This article presents a documented repository systematized with more than 1800 references about the GCSM, available online through the CEMarin portal. The collection includes technical reports, theses and scientific articles of difficult access, recovered from institutional and personal archives. The platform not only facilitates access to historical documents, but also categorizes them by abiotic, biotic and anthropic dimensions, integrating modern navigation tools and links to digital copies. It is proposed that the strategic use of this historical information is essential for more effective, proactive and sustainable environmental management. Instead of prioritizing the constant generation of new data, is advocated for integrating the “environmental memory” and the use of SM in order to improve the quality of information
