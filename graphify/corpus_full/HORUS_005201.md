# RADIOTERApia INTRAOPERATORIA WITH ELECTRONES (RIOE) IN PATIENTS WITH MOM CANCER: REVISION OF THERAPEUTIC EVIDENCE AND ADVORDATION IN THE NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF CANCEROLOGY, COLOMBIA

Breast cancer is the neoplasm with the highest incidence and mortality in women in Colombia. Although its early stage treatment pillar is conservative surgery and the administration of adjuvant radiation therapy, new radiation therapy techniques and equipment have recently been developed that shorten the time of such treatment and improve adherence and accessibility to it. Among them, intraoperative electron radiation therapy (RIOe) has been shown to be clinically safe and useful in patients with early breast cancer in the low-risk group A luminals. Since the National Cancer Institute is the only institution in the country that has a linear accelerator of mobile electrons that allows the administration of RIOe, the evidence available was reviewed to define the selection criteria for the administration of this technique
