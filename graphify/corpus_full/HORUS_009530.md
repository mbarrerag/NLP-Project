# JENOPHANES PARADRE DE SCEPTICISMO?

There is an ancient tradition that makes Jenophanes of Colophon the father of scepticism. This idea, transmitted by various sources, links the collophon not only with some theses that were appreciated by the ancient skeptics but also with an attitude towards knowledge in general. In this text I will study that skeptical tradition in order to make explicit the reasons that make it possible to attribute to him such paternity and the implications that articulate such a tradition have when it comes to in-terpreting his thought
