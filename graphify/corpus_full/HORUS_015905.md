# CHAPTER 13 CONSIDERATIONS ON THE RESUTILIZATION OF THE ARCHITECTURE FROM DIECISEIS

As part of a series of approaches to the notion of transformation in architecture this research was oriented to the understanding-of and the experimentation-with the principles that govern the reuse of architecture and was realized in the conjunction between the analysis of works of architecture of the past, understood as the main source of knowledge on the topic, and the concreteness of new spatial organizations using the conceptual instruments learned and apprehended, the proper knowledge of the trade extracted from the study of the examples.
