# ♪ I'm not gonna be a woman ♪

Casadentro is a research + creation that seeks to recognize the daily practices in voice of women who have predicted the identity of the ururbanity of the northwestern of Caldas, focusing their gaze on the multiplicity of territorialities of the municipality of Riosucio, through the co-creation of expanded accounts that make visible the traditional and practical knowledge of everyday life, of what could be named as a cultural landscape of clay that shares with other Colombian regions, the particularity of transferring various processes of territorialization silent and silenced. To the land undermined and, at the same time, united by ancestors extracting from the delirious gold, that has left in the ceed an undeniable trail of disputes and contrasts, today it comes from the most diverse collectivities, indigenous and black that confluence in the social and cultural processes more important since the role of women. Our purpose is to re-narr the knowledge that has been rooted in them and their descendants, perceiving in the edge of the glocal pressures and agents, as well as to know the social agency
