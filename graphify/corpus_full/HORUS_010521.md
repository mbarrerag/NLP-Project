# COLOMBIA, COUNTRY OF CITIZENS

The "city" is often defined as a permanent grouping of a certain number of people in one place, giving priority to the number of inhabitants as a criterion for delimitation. This criterion is widely used by national statistical institutes; however, the threshold that allows defining from what figure a grouping of people is a city can vary greatly from country to country: it is a relative and also reducing definition, because it does not account for the multidimensionality of the city. In fact, the city is both an organized space, a functional economic system, a political entity, a social body endowed with its own identity (Zambrano and Bernard, 1993) and a product of the dynamics of the urban land market. For each of these dimensions, different expressions of the tension evidenced between the uniqueness of the city and the indifference of the generalized urban thing are observed.
