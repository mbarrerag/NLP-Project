# Adolescent citizen participation: An analysis of youth networks in Cundinamarca

During adolescence, a sense of citizenship is constructed through civic participation in a range of settings that facilitate social interactions such as community, family, institutions, and culture. This arti-cle describes young people's networks and relationships in contexts that generate psychosocial de-velopment through adolescent civic participation in the municipalities of Facatativá, Ubaté, and Fu-sagasugá, in Cundinamarca. This qualitative study used the network mapping technique with the participation of 18 adolescents. Results highlighted the multifunctional role of the family, characteri-zed by an increased number of contacts and interactions. Young people also develop denser com-munity networks that are characterized by close relationships and increased civic engagement. This is manifested through cultural engagement such as artistic and sporting activities, social expression and collective and community actions. These relational contexts enhance the psychosocial processes that are generated through participation
