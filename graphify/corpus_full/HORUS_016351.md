# Design and simulation of LINEAL AND NON-LINEAL ESTIMATORS IN A DC/DC POTENCE CONVERTING MODEL

The state estimate for electronic power converters is an interesting alternative for indirectly obtaining some variables that are difficult to measure, due to sensor costs or limitations imposed by measuring noises. Therefore, this article is designed to design three linear and three nonlinear state estimators for a DC/DC boot converter model, in order to verify the convenience of using only nonlinear estimators as a solution to the converter current estimation problem. Additionally, the behavior of these estimators in Matlab software is tested by simulation
