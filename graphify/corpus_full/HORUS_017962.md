# POSTRAMATIC CYCLOLISIS, DIAGNOSTIC AND THERAPEUTIC DISAFFISTANCE. CASE REPORT AND LITERATURA REVIEW

We present the case of a patient with a difficult posttraumatic cyclodialysis difficult to locate, even with repeated ultrabiomicroscopy (UMB). The aim is to report the diagnosis of a small, difficult-to-visión cyclodialysis with atypical presentation. Patient with chronic 1 hour posttraumatic cyclodialysis with difficult visualization both by clinical examination and by image (UBM), despite repeated evaluations. Initially, medical treatment was performed, however, spontaneous closure and no exact location were achieved. Strict follow-up based on clinical suspicion finally allowed its location and successful surgical repair. Cyclodialysis is a rare condition and most ophthalmologists treat only a few cases, there is no standardized protocol and both diagnosis and management can be difficult. Gonioscopia remains the diagnostic method of choice, and certain diagnostic aids such as BUBM and possibly optical coherence tomography can improve detection sensitivity, although in some cases only clinical suspicion and rigorous monitoring can lead to accurate diagnosis and adequate treatment
