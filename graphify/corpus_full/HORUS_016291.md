# EFFECT OF IRON INTRODUCTION IN THE BUILDING STRUCTURE OF ZINC OXIDE: STUDY BY ELECTRONIC PARAGNETIC RESONANCE

Results associated with the effect of the introduction of iron atoms into the wurtzite structure of zinc oxide (ZnO) are presented. The material was synthesized by the sol-gel method, with a molar ratio of doped x varying from 1 to 5% at. The samples were studied by electron paramagnetic resonance (EPR), measured to 10 K. The identification of the oxidation state of the Faith and the local symmetry of the Faith in the ZnO matrix was possible to establish them from the construction of a Hamiltonian spin composed by Zeeman electron isotropic interaction and interactions due to the second-order field expansion (ZFS) in axial symmetry. The presence of ZFS causes the widening of experimental PR signals. This behavior has been associated with the difference of zinc (II) and iron (III) ionic rays
