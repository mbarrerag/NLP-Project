# Deaf Voices: Contributions from Co-Research. Journey through Leadership and School Empowerment Actions, in Bogotá

Given the ethical and political impacts on the deaf community, this research recognizes the importance of highlighting the notions and experiences about leadership and empowerment of deaf community students in some schools in Bogotá, Colombia. In this research, the spaces of deliberation were oriented from interviews and focal groups from a qualitative study perspective, which allowed identifying and interpreting characteristic features of the partners, based on an analysis from the perspective of the theory of ecological systems, applied to human development. The results allowed to show, in some cases, that the students have a perception of leadership as a driver of ideals and social transformation. Related considerations were highlighted about who exercises leadership and how it should act in the face of society. However, regarding the deaf person as a leader in a community, the comments found showed a distancing about the representation of themselves in these social roles from the school level, including a delegation of colleagues who consider with greater communication and representativeness within a school population, in this case.
