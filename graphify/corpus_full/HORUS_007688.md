# Digital evaporator bench twin from the chemical engineering laboratory (LIQ)

The evaporators bank of the chemical engineering laboratory (LIQ) is a fundamental team in the training of chemical engineering students, reinforcing key theoretical concepts and providing practical experience with pilot scale equipment and good safety practices. However, this team, with more than 10 years of use, needs a technological update to align with current, emerging industrial needs and allow the development of projects involving digital twins which are in high demand. The UNAL Engineering Sustainable Processes Seed (UNAL-ESP) seeks to align the academy and industry to address problems involving energy transition, emission reduction and process modelling and optimization. Modernisation of the evaporator bank will enable students to familiarize themselves with technologies and tools of high industrial demand, and develop innovative solutions. This modernization includes updating the programmable logic controller (PLC) of the bank with a latest generation equipment donated by West Engineering, improving the collection of experimental data and facilitating the creation and validation of a digital twin.
