# COLOMBIAN PALINOLOGICAL. METHODS, APPLICATIONS AND STATE OF KNOWLEDGE

The main interest of this book is to make available to students and professionals of the natural sciences updated information about the basic principles of palinology, and the palinological research that have been carried out in Colombia. It is divided into five chapters: in the first one the palinology is defined identifying the different types of microfossils that it studies and its applications. In the second one the concepts regarding production, dispersion and preservation of pollen and spores are posed. The third one describes the forms of collection and treatment of samples for palinological analyses, and mention is made in general way, some methods of dating rocks and sediments. The fourth one addresses the bases for the morphological study of pollen and spores; it is worth noting that to illustrate some of the mentioned aspects have been used examples of studies that the Institute of Research in Stratigraphy (IIES) of the University of Caldas is carrying out in Colombia.
