# Corporate social responsibility and corporate governance – Influence on sustainability reporting.

Corporate social responsibility (CSR) and its relationship with governance is a topic of increasing importance, given the responsibility of corporations to contribute to the solution of social and environmental problems; although the submission of sustainability reports is a practice that more and more organizations are doing it, even at the research level there are gaps of knowledge regarding this phenomenon. This document proposes a review of literature through the use of the ToS methodology (Tree of Science); which revised the co-citations of articles published on Web of Science (WoS) between 2001 and 2019, thus tracing a chronological evolution of the documents to determine through clusters the three perspectives of greater relevance based on the topics of greater publication. The results show that the three perspectives are oriented to the presentation of sustainability reports and the formation of the boards, effect of gender diversity in the areas of corporate governance, in which they identify that there is positive relationship between women directors, transparency and cooperation in the presentation of CSR reports. However, in countries of emerging economies it is not usual to guarantee spaces of participation of women. Finally, the corporate approach is taken into account
