# WEIGHT OF CROSS TERNERSHIPS BETWEEN GENETIC GROUPS ON, ANGUS AND HOLSTEIN, IN ANTIOCIA, COLOMBIA PRODUCTION SYSTEM

In Colombia, the White breed Orejinegro is used for meat and milk production with high rusticity and adaptability to the tropics. Its inclusion in cross-breeding programs seeks to have rusticity in the cross product without losing the virtues of the specialized European breeds.This research was aimed at evaluating the effect that different proportions of the breed BON has on the weight to weaning (PD), in cross-breeding with animals of the breeds specialized in meat and milk production, Aberdeen Angus and Holstein respectively.The registration of 64 individuals bred under two different methods (TC2: separated from the mother, bred with bucket and weaned at 90 days of birth; SS1: all milk with the mother until weaning at +/- 210 days) was taken into account for this evaluation.The calves were born each year 2017 and 2022 in the Paysandu Farm Station, located in Medellín Antioquia, owned by the National University of Colombia.The fixed effects of the genetic group (G), including 8 crosses with different proportions of BON; the gender (S), female and male; the year of birth (EN) were used in the year 2017; the year of birth (TC1 and SS2) the effects of the birth and year of birth, as well as the covariable PN, had significant effect with values of P varying between 0.001 and 0.1. The PD averages for the different crosses in the group TC1 were: Pure BON 203.1Kg, for group F1 1/2 Bon x 1/2 Angus 218.9Kg and for group 3/4 Agus x 1/4 Bon the PD was 214.6Kg and for pure Angus 216.01. For group TC2 the mean PD were: F1 1/2 Bon x 1/2 Holstein 114.3Kg, for group 3/4 Holstein x 1/4 Bon 115.2Kg, for group 7/8 Holstein x 1/8 Bon 116.9Kg, and for groups 13/16 Holstein x 3/16 Bon and 31/32 Holstein x 1/32 BON the PD was adaptation.
