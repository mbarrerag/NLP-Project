# Formulation of a model based on optimization techniques for scheduling class schedules in a university educational institution in the city of Girardot.

The scheduling of schedules is a problem derived from the planning of resources that responds to a set of desirable objectives subject to resources that require to be placed in a certain space-time. In the academy, the problem is addressed from the scheduling of class schedules or the scheduling of examination schedules, with application in different educational institutions (schools, colleges, universities); for which the use of operational research tools such as optimization techniques have served as a support for its solution. In context, the purpose of this document is to formulate a model through optimization techniques to solve the problem of scheduling of classes at the university level that maximizes the use of classrooms, especially those of the main headquarters and secondary headquarters that consequently reduce the use of classrooms of the auxiliary headquarters, which are subcontracted. For the development of the formulation a characterization of the university studied through the cybernetic tool of the third order was carried out, transforming the process of programming of classes schedules into a system, in addition to the structure of mathematical modeling that leads to the validation of the current system against the proposed one through performance indicators.
