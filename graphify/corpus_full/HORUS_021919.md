# Structure of a business entrepreneurship model within the framework of circular economy from fisheries by-products.

The changing and increasingly high demands of the global market have led to the restructuring of the productive processes of companies worldwide, including practices responsible for the planet and society. On the other hand, it is a fact that productive projects are large generators of value from which a large number of families can benefit, such as San Andrés de Tumaco, a municipality with high levels of poverty and therefore a focus of enormous social problems, where much of its inhabitants are supported by artisanal fishing, whether for their own consumption or for commercialization. However, an opportunity has been observed to make the activity much more profitable, based on the implementation of processes that achieve a better use of parts that are normally discarded. Thus, by means of the formulation of a model of entrepreneurial entrepreneurship within the framework of the circular economy and taking into account the ancestral knowledge of the region, based on by-products of fishing, it is intended to increase the benefits for the community of San Andrés de Tumaco
