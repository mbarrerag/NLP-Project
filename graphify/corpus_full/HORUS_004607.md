# ON G-DIGROUP REPRESENTATION

The concept of g-digroup was introduced by Salazar, Velásquez and Wills in the paper [1]. Many results in this theory have been given, for example free g-digroups, Sylow-type theorems, Cayley theorem, etc, see [2] and [3]. Nowadays, we are exploring the idea of a good definition of representation of g-digroups. Thus, in this talk we present some importants results in this line. Referencias [1] O. P. Salazar-Díaz, R.
