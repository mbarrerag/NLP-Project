# POLICE ATLAS OF SOME SPECIES OF THE PART UNDER THE CALL-RlION VALLEY OF THE CAUCA

The Biogeographic Chocó presents unique environmental conditions that configure it as one of the regions of the world with great richness of vascular plants and high rates of endemism, but this region has been studied little in spite of its high diversity. This atlas seeks to describe the polynic morphology of some species in the forests of low land of the Colombian peaceful platform, because, there are no species of plants represented widely in the ecosystems of the region. In this work 51 species were described from floral buttons taken from fertile specimens of the herbarium MEDEL, which were collected in the lower part of the basin of the river Calima and its surroundings. These samples were acetylized, mounted in permanent plates and analyzed in microscopy of visible light and electronic sweeping climate. It was found that the families best represented in this sampling were Rubiaceae, with 9 species, Cyperaceae and Fabaceae, with 4 species each.
