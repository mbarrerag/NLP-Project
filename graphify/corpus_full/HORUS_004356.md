# ELEMENTS FOR ADVANCEMENT IN PRODUCTIVE INCLUSION CONCEPTS AND TERRITORIAL PEACE

The chapter is structured as follows: initially, the themes of productive inclusion and territorial peace are introduced. Subsequently, contextual aspects of poverty are presented, the main backdrop of these problems. Following this, with the aim of recognizing relevant contributions, a bibliometric analysis is carried out, whose purpose was to identify the key words and main lines of work around the aforementioned concepts. Finally, relevant aspects of the 2016 final peace agreement and the Comprehensive Rural Reform (RRI) are analyzed. The objective of the chapter is to analyze fundamental elements to be able to advance in understanding the concepts of productive inclusion and territorial peace, as a contribution of importance, from the academy, in the definition and comprehensive evaluation of agricultural production systems that contribute to improving the quality of life of Colombian rural inhabitants and to propose recommendations of public policies in this regard
