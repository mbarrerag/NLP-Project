# IMPLEMENTATION OF A METHODOLOGICAL STRATEGY FOR CONSOLIDATION OF ACADEMIC-ADMINISTRATIVE MANAGEMENT OF A MANUFACTURE PROCESS LABORATORY

This research focused on the implementation of the consolidated methodological strategy between 2016-2019 on the academic and administrative processes of the Laboratory of Manufacturing Processes LPM, attached to the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Universidad Nacional de Colombia Sede Medellín, which was nominated and elected within the bank of successful projects for the National High Management Award 2020, which organizes the Administrative Department of Public Service of the National Government, in the Equation category of Equity, with emphasis on institutional performance management of knowledge and innovation. This strategy is based on the mandatory regulations that the Colombian Ministry of Education has guided according to a series of laws and decrees that lead to higher education institutions becoming self-sustainable agents, for which it has been necessary to implement strategic plans for improvement, flexible and adaptable to the variable situations in time. This situation allows to consider the IES as organizations and the teachers managers as managers of them, given that much of their working time is dedicated to the implementation of the academic-administrative processes, which in most cases led to a fragile relationship between the processes of professionals, for the implementation of the administrative processes and for the administrative management of the implementation of the LPM
