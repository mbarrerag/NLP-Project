# INFORMATION TECHNOLOGIES AND COMMUNICATION FOR HEALTH EDUCATION: SYSTEMATIC REVIEW.

the use of information and communication technologies (ICT) is gaining more and more relevance due to technological advances in the different areas of knowledge. ICTs are considered as a way of learning, where the subjects involved, students and teachers mediate and communicate, despite being separated by a physical distance. to synthesize the existing scientific literature about information and communication technology tools as a means or strategy for the education of health personnel. a systematic review of literature was prepared under the PRISMA methodology. The search was carried out in the databases of PubMed, ScienceDirect, MEDLINE, SciELO and ProQuest; articles published in the last 5 years in English, Portuguese and Spanish. a total of 1756 articles were filtered, of which 42 were reviewed in depth, already they met the inclusion criteria. It was found that the most used ICT tool is the website or website, used in the 30% of the investigations.
