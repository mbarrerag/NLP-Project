# RISK FACTORS AND SCENARIOS IN A RURAL COMMUNITY

This article presents the results of a qualitative study of participatory action research corresponding to the first diagnostic stage of the project "Participatory action of the community of Tausa Cundinamarca in the construction of the emergency and disasters plan." Twenty-seven video talks and twenty-two interviews were collected and analyzed through a content analysis using Atlas.Ti 8 software. That to learn about the experiences and perceptions of the community regarding risk and emergencies in the municipality. As a result of the perception of the inhabitants of the municipality of Tausa, three main categories emerged: "risk factors" (composed of the subcategories "threat" and "vulnerability"), "risk scenarios," and "disasters." This research made it possible to identify the situations that generate concern among the inhabitants; to understand the state of alert and participation in risk management in the community; and confirm the need to complement and implement, with the help of the community, the municipal risk management plan, and response strategies (promoting joint action with municipal entities)
