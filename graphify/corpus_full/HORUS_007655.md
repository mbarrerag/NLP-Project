# Development of community strategies for water monitoring and management.

Currently Colombia suffers an accelerated environmental deterioration characterized mainly by the crisis in natural resources, especially in water and soil. Water resources present alterations in quantity and quality required in the different territories of the country. Access to water by communities for their supply is at high risk, a situation that is exacerbated by the effects of climate variability and change. According to the above, it is necessary to implement strategies that integrate popular knowledge and academic knowledge, in order to facilitate new empowerments in communities towards defense and permanence in their territories, in which water is the most important good that makes life, production, and in general human activities and nature cycles possible. In this project, the objective is to evaluate the quality of water from physiochemical, microbiological and bioassaysys, standardized ecotoxicological at different levels of trophic work with the community, and then to guide the governance model that, according to the experiences of the community, allows for a clear and participatory social dynamic in the decision-making for the protection, care and conservation of the resource
