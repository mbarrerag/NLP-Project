# EFFECT OF THE APPLICATION OF MAGNETIC CAMPS ON THE ELECTRIC PROPERTIES OF THE PVOH+CO3O4 SYSTEM, BY THE USE OF COMPLETE IMPEDANCE SPECTROSCOPY (IS) TO HIGH EMPERATURES.

PVOH + Co 3O4 samples were prepared, with concentrations of 0.02 and 0.01 cobalt oxide. The electrical characterization studies of the samples were performed using the complex impedance spectroscopy technique (IS) applying a constant B magnetic field of 250 Gauss, perpendicular to the electric field E of the IS technique, with different temperatures between 80 and 180 C. The results show that there is a weak bonding energy between the Co 3+ ions and their environment, which decreased with the application of the B field favoring ionic mobility in the membrane and finding higher values in conductivity of the order of 10-3 10-2 S/cm. The Arrhenius model showed that there are weak activation energies of the order of 1 eV for the cobalt ion (Co 3 +). Sweeping in frequency of 42 Hz to 1 MHz were used in the IS technique and two semicircles were observed in the Nyquist diagram allowing the adjustment and analysis of data using two R-CPE circuits thus finding poly-capacitance values of the order of 1 nF
