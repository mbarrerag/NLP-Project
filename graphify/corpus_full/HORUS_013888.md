# Research Observatory: Analysis of the processes of political opposition and social movements carried out during the periods of government of Iván Duque and Gustavo Petro.

The research topic of this observatory is the analysis of the political opposition processes at the level of political parties and those carried out by social movements, during the presidential periods of Iván Duque and Gustavo Petro. This research proposal is based on the interest of carrying out a process of observation, interpretation, analysis, description and comparison regarding how the political opposition has developed from a dimension of political parties and social movements, consolidating critical and propositional readings regarding the current state of Colombian democracy.This research has as its beginning the government of former president Iván Duque, since, in this presidential period it is possible to show how the political opposition is presented as a dilemma for the national government, regarding whether it tolerates or suppresses it.This dilemma we can understand from the theoretical of the political problem Robert Dahl, who exposes, that the governments of a state of affairs, it is possible to put forward a probability and to analyze the political opposition as a whole
