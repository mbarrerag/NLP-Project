# INSURANCE CARE FOR THE NEED FOR OXIGENATION IN ADULTS IN CIVID-IN-CIVED DISEASE-19: INTEGRATIVE REVIEW

the disease by COVID-19 generates acute respiratory distress syndrome, affects the need for oxygenation and demands nursing care to maintain hemodynamic stability and prevent respiratory complications. to analyze scientific evidence on nursing care in response to the need for oxygenation in adults hospitalized with disease by COVID-19. integrative review with a universe of 518 scientific articles from 2020 and 2021 of the databases: PubMed, Ebsco, Cuiden and ScienceDirect. Six studies were analyzed (n= 6) that address: what are nursing care in response to the need for oxygenation in adults with disease by COVID-19? nursing care includes oxygen therapy strategies, recommendations on care in the first line of care, use of high flow oxygen, early awake prono positioning, and control of saturation and vital signs through a nursing care process. nursing care satisfies the need for oxygenation through oxygen therapy interventions, control of comorbidities and prevention of hospital risks
