# Nursing intervention to reduce incidence and duration of delirium in people in intensive care

to determine the effectiveness of nursing intervention based on the Model of Dynamic Symptoms (MSD) and on scientific evidence, versus daily care, for the reduction of the incidence and duration of delirium in people in intensive care. two phases were used, design and evaluation of the intervention. The design followed Sidani's recommendations from the theoretical approach with application of the MSD and from the empirical approach with a systematic review. The evaluation of the intervention was carried out with a randomized clinical trial of double blind parallel groups. The sample was 213 persons in ICU. The analysis employed analytical statistics, parametric and nonparametric. the intervention called Dynamic Delirium (DyDel) was designed with 10 care and 33 activities. The care was based on the physiological, psychological, spiritual and social components, environmental, experience and path of the MSD. The activities of each care also in the scientific evidence.
