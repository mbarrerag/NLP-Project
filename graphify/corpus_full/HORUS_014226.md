# Strengthening the Botanical Garden Environmental Reserve of the municipality of Tabio, Cundinamarca as a center of social, cultural and ecological inclusion

In the configuration of the landscape in Bio there still exist remnants of high Andean forest, classified as areas with greater biodiversity of the world and as one of the most threatened areas by human activities (Gómez Mora et al., 2005). In addition, few municipalities at national level have the fortune to own a specific area to establish an institution that functions as a Botanical Garden. Tabio has a space named according to the EOT as _x0093_Reserva Ambiental Jardín Botánico_x0094_, which is part of the urban ecological structure of the municipality and was established for the protection of the ecological heritage of the municipality (agreements 002 of 28 May 1994, 005 of 7 June 2001). However, it does not yet fulfill the purposes that according to the regulation of the botanical gardens in Colombia (law 299 of 26 July 1996). In this sense, have a reservation of _x0093_Reservación Ambiental Jardón Botánico_x0094_ in the municipality, allows consolidating as a _x0093_municipio verdeñnes_ and additionally provide to the local population a space of leisure, recreation and teaching.
