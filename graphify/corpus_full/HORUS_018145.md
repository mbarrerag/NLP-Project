# BASIC FOUNDAMENTS OF PHYSIOLOGY

Comprehensive health care for the enteral food therapy of newborns is essential: it is a goal of particular importance in the context of timely, safe and quality care. This comprehensive care should include the provision of a nutritional medical therapy that provides the tools and supports required to care for premature newborns. Thus, this text aims to respond to challenges that arise in clinical practice and are analyzed in the context of clinical care and academia in order to meet the nutritional needs of premature newborns. In 10 chapters, this book elaborates on key aspects of clinical practice and responds to the academic and research needs that arise in the Neonatal Intensive Care Units. In short, this work constitutes a rapid consultation document for health professionals, teachers and students who want to complement what has been learned in the book Neonatal Parental Nutritional Therapeutics. It presents the foundations of the newborn's physiology based on the changes that arise in the final period of pregnancy and that go up to the first months of life, which allow for an adequate adaptation to the extrauterine environment, as well as to the health and well as to the well-being of the newborn
