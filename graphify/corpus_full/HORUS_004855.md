# Organ donation in Colombia: an analysis of the effect of law 1805 of 2016

At present there is a great shortage of bodies worldwide, so the way to reduce the imbalance between supply and demand has been sought using strategies such as the presumed donation system, which leverages the behavioral economy and its postulates. This study assesses the impact that law 1805 of 2016 has had on organ donation in Colombia, for which descriptive and normative analyses of the context were carried out, as well as the development of a difference in differences assessment. Thus, since the law seeks to increase the rate of individual donation in the country through the implementation of a “hard” mechanism, the impact of the law on the number of effective donors was assessed. As a result, although effective donors have increased after the implementation of the law, this increase does not prove to be significantly different from zero, showing that the law has not been able to reduce the family refusal to the levels expected. . different from zero.
