# Prevalence and coprevalence of immunogenetic factors in patients with idiopathic inflammatory myopathy in a third level complex center

The idiopathic inflammatory myopathy constitute a heterogeneous group of diseases mediated immunologically with different physiopathological mechanisms, but most share as main characteristic the muscular destruction, although they also have multiorgan commitments; thanks to the clinical characteristics, serological and histopathological they are classified dermatomyositis (DM), polymyositis (PM), myositis of inclusion bodies (MCI), myositis necrotizing autoimmune (MNA) syndrome antisynthetase (SA) and myositis _x0093_overlap_x0094_. Although its etiology to date is not found clarified genetic, immunological and environmental factors have been linked; within the genetic factors described the greater complex of Histocompatibility (CMH) of the HLA I and II have been found to be highly related to autoimmune pathologies
