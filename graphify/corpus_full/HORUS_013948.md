# Addressing Inequalities in Rural Basic Education: A Binational and Multidimensional View.

This research includes the educational aspects that allow, free access to basic training for children in rural sectors in the countries of Ecuador and Colombia in two towns (Ecuador) and two municipalities (Colombia). This research process will focus on basic education who receive a unified or multi-grade education. From a world view, the impact of rural education on the various actors involved will be visualized by recognizing the different markers of indigenous differences, Afro-descendants, immigrants, persons with disabilities, women achieving inclusive education for all, in the key of equity. Visualizing rural education issues from a holistic perspective, strengthened by the commitment of both universities and the participation of the main actors: children, teachers, government entities and others that strengthen the improvement of educational action in these sectors. The State University of Miracle and the State University of Colombia - Bogotá headquarters will join efforts in the areas of research, methodological, logistical, technological, etc. through their research groups represented by the director of each group and the associated researchers and a budget allocated to achieve the objectives of this project
