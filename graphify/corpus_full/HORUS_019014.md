# Chronic illness and disability narrative points of encounter.

The research recorded in this document aims to narration of experiences of teaching-learning of the plastic arts as an educational process that contributes to integral training, cognitive and motor development and to strengthening the capacities and abilities of children with some condition that limit their learning potential, they and they are students of the Atabanzha IED. Their design was framed in qualitative research with the interpretive hermeneutic method, using observation technique and conversational interactionism. The analysis model was concentrated on the exchanges of sensitive aesthetic relations of communication in the pedagogical process of teaching-learning the plastic arts that occurs in the daily life of girls, boys in the school space and the information achieved in field work was analyzed with the matrixes of the Prosaica proposed by Katya Mandoki in the articulation of rhetoric with their registers: lexicon, acoustic, quinesic and iconic and the dramatic in its modalities: proxémic, kinetic, tonic, pulse. The conclusions indicate that the plastic arts are constituted in the integral process of the pupils, using the cognitive and the ability to strengthen their skills
