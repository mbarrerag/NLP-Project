# RESEARCH IN LATIN AMERICA

This is an editorial, in which, based on an exercise in research on literature published in Pubmed/Medline, in Latin American countries, the strengths and gaps in research in this region are highlighted. An exercise of what is expected for the future is then proposed, in the research on anesthesiology in Latin America
