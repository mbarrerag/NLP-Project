# AND CRONIC CAPACITY

To analyze the association between cognitive capacity and social participation of older adults with chronic disease. Observational study, sample of 1110 elderly people who re-present to the 12 provinces of the La Libertad region (Peru). cognitive evaluation instrument, minimental state examination modified (mmse) and the instrument of social participation. Cognitive evaluation suggests deterioration of 6.4% and cognitive normality values of 93.6%. A significant relationship was found between cognitive state with gender and age P < 0. 001. The main diseases found were: Hypertension arterial (55.3%), arthritis (38.1%) and diabetes mellitus (16 %). Social participation was mostly regular (62.2 %), good (19.5%) and deficient (18.5%). Statistical significance was found between social participation and cognition. Social participation and cognitive status supports positive health, brin-da commitment or flow of experiences that are intrinsically satisfactory, well transmitted an objective sense of purpose and the opportunity of positive social relations, thus decrease a social-cognitive function
