# Design of a morphologically variable aerodynamic profile.

The concept of morphology applied to aircraft wings is related to the ability of a structure to change its geometry, to adapt to different flight conditions. This in order to increase performance, reducing the amount of fuel and increasing its operating time. This work is intended to describe the procedures carried out for the generation and subsequent evaluation of the conceptual and detailed design of a variable morphology aerodynamic profile. Designs created earlier by different authors are taken as the starting point and the development of own design concepts is carried out. After this, a decision process is carried out, using different engineering requirements, the dominant global concept is determined; which is based on a flexible mechanism to deform the leakage edge of the aerodynamic profile, for subsequent analysis by numerical means. A two-level optimization methodology is generated for the development of the flexible mechanism. At the first level, the best form of the aerodynamic profile is obtained by means of a multi-objective optimization process. At the second level, the best structural configuration is obtained by means of topological optimization. Finally, several analyses are carried out by means of dynamic of computative fluidss using the software (SST)
