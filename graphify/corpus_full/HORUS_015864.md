# ADVERSE REACTIONS TO MEDICINAL PRODUCTS IN PATIENTS INCOME IN THE UNAL HOSPITALIZATION AREA

The pharmacovigilance is a relatively new discipline in veterinary medicine and in Colombia, recently, a system of pharmacovigilance was introduced, so the objective of this study was to use the hospitalization area of the small animals clinic of the Faculty of Veterinary Medicine and Zootechnics of the National University of Colombia as a monitoring post to detect and record possible adverse drug reactions in patients admitted to the hospitalization area, following the procedures established by the European Medicines Agency and the Committee of Medicinal Products for Veterinary Medicine for the establishment of a system of pharmacovigilance that serves as a model for the implementation of a system of pharmacovigilance at national level. During a period of 6 months, using the ABON system, adverse event reports were used to record all cases in which ADR was suspected as a reason for hospitalization or that the ADR was presented at the time of hospitalization of patients canine or felineated. During the 6 months, using the ABON system, each case was categorized to carry out a causality assessment.
