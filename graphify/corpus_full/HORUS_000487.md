# PEACE URDIMBRES FROM THE FAMILY PEDAGOGIES

This small article explores the contributions of feminist pedagogy to think about the dialogue in school on the results of the report of the commission for truth in Colombia. In this sense, three pedagogical actions are promoted to question the pedagogy of memory and to promote a culture of peace: 1. the fight against gender binarism in the stories of memory, 2. to think of peace on a body scale and 3. a pedagogy of care and political recognition of vulnerability
