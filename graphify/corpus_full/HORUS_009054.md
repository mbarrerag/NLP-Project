# Analysis of the degradation of a drug using a one-compartment stochastic model

We consider a pharmacokinetic (PK) model with first-order drug absorption and first-order elimination representing the drug concentration in the body, including both the absorption and elimination parts, and we also add a random factor to describe the variability between patients and the environment
