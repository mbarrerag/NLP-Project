# The Zapatista Movement: A Response to the Utopia Crisis

After the fall of the Berlin wall and the collapse of Soviet socialism, Victor Flores Olea has proposed that the world has entered into a period of crisis of utopias. On the one hand, the capitalist and liberal paradigm, which in the name of freedom ensured well-being and progress for all, has created a gap of social inequality in which only a small minority benefits from the system, while the vast majority is subjected to exploitation and misery. On the other hand, the declared Marxist states became into authoritarian regimes where despotic bureaucracies were consolidated whose successes in social policies did not compensate for their negative aspects. Following the above, the world has been shaken by a strong wave of pessimism and skepticism in which to conceive a political theory that presents a new utopian horizon seems somewhat impossible. In the midst of this despair, and as a response to the utopian crisis, the Zapatista movement emerges in 1994 to the yardstick of the cry is enough! It is a rebel organization that ensures not to register within the traditional ideological canons, but to bet at anything new. The objective of this research is to identify the contributions that the Zapatistas have provided in 1994 small minority benefits from the system, while the vast majority is subject to exploitation and mystery.
