# DENAMIC SOCIAL JUSTICE, UNIVERSITY LIFE AND TRAINING PROCESS IN STUDENTS OF DONTOLOGY

Social injustices are posed in three levels intertwined with each other. A general one, related to poverty and social inequalities; a second, related to inequalities in oral health, access to care services and the negative impact of neliberalism on the most vulnerable layers of society; another related to the role of the university and the analysis of social problems during the training processes. To contribute to the realization of social justice, education takes a transcendental role, including in dentistry. Social justice and health equity are frequently identified as "fundamental values" for the field of health and dentistry. However, it is not clear what these values consist in the practice. Interpreting relationship between notion of social justice, university life and training process in students of dentistry of a Colombian public university. qualitative research with ethnographic approach. The tools used were: workshops of research based on art IBA, field diary and semi-structured interview. IBA workshops 182 students and 20 needed in the interviews: 6 men and 6 men. The Campus as territory, the multidiversity in the University, the otherness, the professional autonomy, and the ethical-political pedagogical space, hidden curriculum? When university life allows interaction with human diversity, the connection with the environment and other forms of life, and the process of critical and ethical formation, does politics leave the classroom to know and understand social reality, a dynamic social justice idea is forged in students. Campus as territory, alterity and vision of the future promote reflections leading to the construction of the notion of dynamic social justice, allowing students of the present to understand current and future challenges, and the construction of a collective subject that investigates how to transform reality to have a better society
