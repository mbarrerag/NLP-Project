# OPTITUTIONAL POINT OF OXG INTER-SPECTIFIC HIBRICULTIVES

The cultivars of the interspecific hybrid OxG, derived from cross-sections between American palm (Elaeis oleifera) and African palm (Elaeis guineensis) have been gaining importance in the agro-industry of the oil palm in America, mainly because of their resistance to the rotting of bud. These cultivars are of recent development and, in that sense, many aspects of their agro-nomi-co and sanitary management are still unknown, since they present very marked differences from the African palm, which is the one traditionally cultivated. It is thus one of the most relevant aspects that differentiates African palm cultivars from African palm cultivars is the optimal point of harvest. Indeed, the traditional standards of determining the point of ripening of the clusters that are applied for the African palm cannot be applied directly in the cultivars of the organically grown segments at the stage.
