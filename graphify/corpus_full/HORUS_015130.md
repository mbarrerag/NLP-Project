# CAREFUL CULTURAL PRACTICES IN THE BINOMI MOTHER-SON DURING THE PURPERY: INTEGRATIVE REVIEW

to identify in the literature the cultural practices of postnatal care performed by women and their families during the post-natal period. an integrative review was carried out through the search in the databases: Gale Cengage Learning, Scielo, Redalyc, Dialnet, EBSCO, Proquest, Science Direct, PubMed and Medigraphic in English, Spanish and Portuguese languages between 2014 and 2019, using the terms: cultural care practices, care in the puerperium, newborn care and perinatal maternal nursing care; the data were analyzed from the construction of a matrix in Excel. 70 articles were obtained, from which four thematic categories of cultural care practices emerged: care of women with themselves, care of newborns by mothers/families, institutional care of the puerna and newborns, with maternal breastfeeding being the common factor among them. the identification of various postnatal care practices in the hospital and outpatient environment vary by social, economic and cultural aspects, but the majority of them favor the link with the newborns, the difficult cultural practices among them
