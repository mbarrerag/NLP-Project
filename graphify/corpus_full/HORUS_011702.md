# PALACE AND PALUMA ENTIRE AUNTS ON THE GENESIS OF COLONIAL ELITES IN THE NEW GRANADA KINGDOM

The concept of elite was not part of the vocabulary of what we call today a colonial period. Will its appearance only take place in the nineteenth century, when the consolidation of the modern state, founded on the emergence of democratic republic, left behind in a definitive way? The Ancient Régime. However, the classical theory of elites, initially outlined by sociologists such as Vilfredo Pareto (1848-1923) or Gaetano Mosca (1858-1941), could be applied to the sociopolitical structures in force between the xvi and xviii centuries, which shows that these had also been assembled on the division between the elected ruling ones and a social group that is governed (Pareto, 1966, pp. 67-70).
