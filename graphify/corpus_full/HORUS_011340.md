# THE JEDRECIST STATE AND THE CREATION OF THE META DEPARTMENT, COLOMBIA

The idea that the Colombian State acted for more than a hundred years as a chess State in the management of the national territories is evidence of the lack of a State policy appropriate for the defense of the territory, the consolidation of the nation and the construction of the national space. This chapter outlines an initial outline of the configuration of the Meta department and shows how a series of troops reinforced from various sectors of society, which spoke of infinite spaces, uncontrollable savagery, loneliness and death, covered the ignorance of the State with respect to the territory and its role in the construction of the national society, its inability to hold it and, above all, the lack of value it gave to the border space and its inhabitants; tropes that made the Meta see it, as a part of the country whose merit resided solely in its abundance of natural resources and its possibility to be dispensable to meet the needs of the center of the country and foreign companies, so the department seems to have emerged for Bogotá, not for the Métenses.
