# CIVIL CONSTITUTIONAL LAW STRATEGIES FOR INTEGRATIVE DEVELOPMENT OF THE AUTHONOMY IN THE CONSTITUTIONAL AND DEMOCRATIC STATE OF LAW

this book is the second installment of the research group for the coordination of civil law and economic, social and cultural rights (giadesc) of the national university of colombia. It contains various research articles designed by several members of the group in their different lines of research, but with a common denominator that constitutes the central nucleus of the group's research: the purpose of reflecting on how to achieve the proper constitutionalization of private law in Colombia, a country marked by the transition from war to peace that it tries in recent years. Topics are presented in various areas of constitutional law, especially private law, such as goods, contracts and the right of consumption, always with a social approach adaptable to Colombian social reality, in order to maximize the guarantee of the economic, social and cultural rights of the population
