# Determination of Helicobacter spp. in glandular gastric mucosa of mules through the urease activity and histopathology test

Information on the presentation and predisposing factors of gastric ulcer syndrome in mules (SUGM) is scarce compared to gastric ulcer syndrome in equines (SUGE) and asnals. Due to the multifactorial nature of this syndrome, helicobacteriosis has been studied in other species. The aim was to establish the presence of Helicobacter spp. in gastric mucosa of mules through rapid urease testing (PRU) and histopathological analysis. Less than 27% of the samples reacted to PRU, with prolonged reaction times, and to Agar Urea (gold test), with a lower percentage of positivity. Histopathology revealed chronic inflammatory processes, without the presence of curvoespirated bacteria. The PRU were not conclusive in the determination of Helicobacter spp., similar behavior reported in horses. Specific diagnostic examinations and complementary procedures are required to explore regions of the stomach in consideration of the number of representative samples
