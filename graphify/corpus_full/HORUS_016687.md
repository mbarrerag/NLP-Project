# Dark Sounds in the Bay - Mapping Landscape of Memory

Dark sounds in the Bay. Cartographic landscape of memory, is a research project that is configured through a series of autobiographical contemplations and narratives of events that revolve around the question about the identity of a group of metalmakers inscribed inside a socio-cultural situation in the city of Santa Marta between the 80s and about 2000.
