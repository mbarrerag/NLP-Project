# Integration of the physical-chemical parameters of polymer nanofluid based carbon quantum points (CQDs) for viscosity prediction using machine learning models

The improved recovery processes in Colombia take on relevance to maintain the energy sovereignty of the country through the implementation of new technologies. This work allowed to identify the variables relevant for the evaluation of polymer nanofluid based on carbon quantum points (CQDs) and partially hydrolyzed polyacrylamide (HPAM) to implement machine learning models that can predict the viscosity of the resulting solution as monitoring strategy. The data set was generated experimentally under different salinity conditions, ageing time, addition order, type of CQD and cutting rate as viscosity predictors, maintaining the temperature, concentration of CQD and fixed polymer. The implementation of four (4) models of supervised learning regression, allowed establishing the predictive capacity of the models before a highly unbalanced data set and low linear correlation between variables using the model of random forests with better performance with a R2 close to 87%. The experimental results of the regression systems allowed to establish new approaches to investigate the synergic effect CQD-HPAM in polymer injection processes (to be used by the modeling of polymers to recover the R2 in Colombia). tors, keeping the temperature, polymer, and CQD concentration fixed. The implementation of 4 regression models allowed the establishment of the predictive capacity of the methods in the face of a high level unbalanced dataset and low linear correlation between the variables, with the random forest model being the algorithm with the best performance with an R2 near 87%. The experience highlighted novel approaches to investigate the CQD-HPAM synergistic effect in polymer injection processes for enhanced oil recovery using machine learning techniques as an effective tool to generalize the better of these physical systems
