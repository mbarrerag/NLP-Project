# Safe light in marine oil operations with LED lighting for classified areas

The project focuses on the design of an integrated LED lighting system for areas classified in a marine oil reservoir, with the objective of creating a safe and efficient environment for workers. This system will address critical areas, including internal circulations, heliport and emergency lighting systems. The specific objectives include a detailed study of the lighting requirements for internal circulation, considering visibility, orientation and safety of the staff. A specific lighting design will be developed for the heliport, considering maritime conditions, landing and take-off protocols, and night visibility for safe operations. An emergency lighting system will be designed to ensure visibility and safe evacuation in critical situations, complying with safety standards. The implementation of a LED lighting design in a marine oil reservoir responds to the need to ensure safe and efficient working conditions in highly specialized industrial environments. The adequate lighting is essential to prevent occupational risks, improve visibility and effectively respond to emergencies. The LED technology offers significant advantages, such as increased energy efficiency, extended useful life and lower environmental impact, contributing to the sustainability and compliance of regulations.
