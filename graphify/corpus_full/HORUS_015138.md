# DESIGN OF AN INTEGRAL FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM THROUGH INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGIES (ICT) FOR ROAR SOACH ROOMS HABITAT REFERRING TO ELECTIVE PERIODIC PROCESS

The study consisted of the development of an intelligent system of comprehensive follow-up for people who require perioperative-elective processes, in rural areas of Soacha, Cun-dinamarca, Colombia. The perioperative process was the problem on which the software was constructed. This process includes the events that occur from the moment it is noticed that a person requires a procedure or surgery, until a person returns from the best possible way to the activities of daily life [1]. Our project proposes a systematic framework for the comprehensive follow-up of patients during the perioperative process, starting from the development of an intelligent system of monitoring and accompaniment to the people who live rural areas and the monitoring of perioperative variables [2, 3]. Among the results obtained are the construction of software susceptible to be distributed in the municipality through institutions and actors1.
