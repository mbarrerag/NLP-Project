# BIOLOGICAL METAPHORS APPLICABLE TO ORGANIZATIONS III

We are pleased to present the book Metaphoras Biológicas III, as an effort to allow the analysis of organizations from the point of view of the biological sciences. On this occasion, we have focused on the contribution they can offer to marketing. The book is divided into three parts: the first presents the marketing seen from the perspective of the development of the web. This presents opportunities for marketing, through a historical tour of its different forms. The second part shows the opportunities of collaborative marketing, through organizational symbiosis processes, and the third part proposes a general model of understanding, from sociobiology to management sciences. The book proposes an invitation to the analysis of organizations from a systemic approach, according to which marketing is seen as an organizational function that permeates the entire structure, to find mechanisms of cooperation and collaboration. The reader is invited to imagine his own organizational metaphors, based on the teachings that nature leaves us in terms of evolutionary strategies for the utilization of capacities and forms of adaptation in turbulent environments
