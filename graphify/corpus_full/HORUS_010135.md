# MODEL OUTSIDE - DEFLEXION OF VIGAS I MANUFACTURED WITH POLISTER RESIDENCY AND ROCK HAIR AND PUTUAL VERTICAL BORGES

The polyester resin reinforced with rice husk has been proposed as composite material with potential applications in structural elements. To date, there are few experimental and numerical validations of structural elements with this composite material.This article describes the strength-deflexion behavior of I beams manufactured with polyester resin and rice husk and subjected to vertical charges point. 13 I beams were tested with point vertical loads in the thirds of the light and 3 prismatic specimens of the same material of the beams were tested to compression.The results show that the force-deflexion curve of the I beams is trilinear and that the three areas of this curve correspond to the cases: beam with first fissures, beam with fissures stabilized and beam in the fault.The mechanism of the I beams is attributed to the flaw of the compression mechanism or to a combination of the flaw and the flectable moment.The mechanism of I beams started with fissures in the lower fin tension; these fisuras can spread through the soul of the vigage until they reach the mechanism of the V-comple resulting from the failure of the V-d
