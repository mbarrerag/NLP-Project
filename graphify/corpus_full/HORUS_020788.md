# Incidences of the current reform of fiscal control in Colombia for the exercise of the protection of public resources and goods (2019)

Fiscal control is configured as the mechanism by which the use of public resources is monitored, however, there is a multiplicity of concepts that coincide with its characterization, hence the need, first, to signify the concept of fiscal control. Defined fiscal control is included a historical review of the regulation that regulates its scope taking as a turning point the powers brought about by the 1991 constitution. It is included an analysis of the reform to fiscal control of 2019 identifying the main changes, and analyzing how these contribute to effective fiscal control. The above is countered by a brief regulatory review of fiscal control in four countries of the region in order to identify best and worst practices in the exercise of the surveillance of public resources. It is concluded that the 2019 constitutional reform includes modifications on which it is not clear how it contributes to the strengthening of the fiscal control, such as the prevalence of the Comptroller General of the Republic over territorial counter-control, the powers of judicial police and compliance with constitutional principles such as efficiency.
