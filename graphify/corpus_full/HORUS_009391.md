# FROM ZERO TO HERO: PRACTICAL MATLAB/SIMULINK ANALYSIS AND PROCESS CONTROL FUNCTIONALITIES

The automatic control of processes is a vital area for chemical engineers as it facilitates the operation of industrial processes in a stable and optimal way. Currently, thanks to the strategies of digitization driven by the Fourth Industrial Revolution (technologies 4.0), the automatic control of processes has been profiled with greater potential to give solutions to the industrial challenges that will be faced by future chemical engineers. Then, the optimal operation of complex processes, besides seeking maximum productivity and lower energy consumption also, will consider less environmental impact and greater safety in the processes. Due to the interdisciplinary nature of automatic control of processes, as well as the relative complexity of mathematical formulations and degree of abstraction of concepts, this is a demanding subject and its learning is not a simple task.On the other hand, the simulation tools have shown to be an important complement in the training of chemical engineers, since they allow the compression of the theory through the implementation of complex study cases that require numerical solution. Thus, the book of dynamic processes is vital for the learning of automatic control of processes.
