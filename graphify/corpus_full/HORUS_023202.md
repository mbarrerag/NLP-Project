# Nest Traits

The journey that aims to be the project Tramas del Nido seeks to exhibit and verify an essential sense of home. From different experiences, and after having contact with textile traditions of different cultures, we seek to speak of the nest, understanding it as a natural place of the function of inhabiting, in its vulnerable and ephemeral character that takes place within the wandering human life. By definition, a nest is an original place, a stage of birth and break when left. Its roots are not material, but it is destined to be a memory, a memory-tissue that teaches a decentivized culture when the moment comes, where the same providence decides the point where each person should find, if not compose, his own refuge in the midst of incessant flights.
